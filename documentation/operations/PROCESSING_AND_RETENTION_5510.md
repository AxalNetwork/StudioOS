## 1. Evidence standard and boundaries

Each entry records the *source-backed observed collection*, not everything a notice says might be collected. Status is one of **CODE VERIFIED** (source and safeguards inspected), **CONFIGURED** (live Worker binding metadata seen), **FLOW UNVERIFIED** (no synthetic end-to-end execution), **VENDOR UNVERIFIED** (no approved vendor contract/region/deletion proof) or **LEGAL REVIEW PENDING** (controller role, lawful basis, retention/hold not signed off). Several statuses can apply to one entry.

**Read-only Cloudflare check (2026-11-11):** `studioos` Worker settings returned 124 bindings: 46 plain-text, 49 secret-text, 4 R2, 3 KV, 2 Vectorize, 2 Queues, 10 Durable Object, 1 D1, 1 Workflow, 1 Workers AI, 1 Analytics Engine, 1 assets, 1 browser, 1 Images, 1 Stream. This confirms configured classes of bindings, **not** the current effective values of secrets, which vendors receive a payload, contract approval, storage region or successful invocation. No secret names, values or sensitive customer settings are published in this record. The Worker has both frontend assets and the production D1 binding configured; compare [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md), `wrangler.toml` and production deploy metadata before sign-off.

The existing [deck retention inventory](DATA_RETENTION.md) remains authoritative for deck stores; its known `no cascade` rows must not be silently called deleted. #4675 owns the DSR execution and audit evidence; this document owns what should be examined, not the implementation of an erasure engine. #5509 owns opt-in referral handling. #5511 owns the effective public notice text. #4683 owns the Trust publication transaction and subprocessor notice lifecycle.

## 3. Processor and recipient evidence — proposed Trust review subset

**Classification test:** a package, configured binding or API import is evidence of potential processing only. A company becomes a confirmed external **processor** when Axal has actually sent it covered data for an approved service under the relevant contractual/controller terms. Do not publish a vendor as active merely because code, a secret binding, an SDK or a dashboard integration exists. The public Trust list is a **sanitized approved subset** of the restricted vendor register, never a blind export of account configuration.

## 5. Operating update and verification protocol

1. **Detect a new field/vendor:** the PR's Help/privacy impact decision identifies changed collection, processor integration, AI provider, tracking, role or storage. Link its route/schema and data class to a row here before publication. No editor should hand-type a new retention date without approved evidence.
2. **Route to owners:** Privacy/Legal owns purpose and legal basis; Engineering names live calls and stores; Security checks access/scope/region; Product explains the customer workflow; Finance/GP reviews investor/financial obligations; Trust owner prepares the public subset and notice date.
3. **Verify the flow with synthetic data in an approved environment:** sign up, consent and revoke, contact, recording and delete audio, optional connector grant/revoke, paid test checkout, KYC synthetic case, DSR request and manual fulfillment. Capture a redacted request trace, D1 store names, R2 key class, any processor transmission, expiry/hold result and replay/backup behavior **in a restricted evidence location**. No production-user payloads or actual identity documents in GitHub.
4. **Review vendor record:** service, real legal counterparty, data categories and roles, purpose, geography, transfer basis, contract/DPA/version, approved access, subcontractors, retention, deletion API/procedure, incident notice, owner and review date. Status may be **proposed**, **configured**, **active-verified**, **off**, **retired** or **unknown**.
5. **Publish through the existing Trust workflow:** owner-approved subset, contract-confirmed fields only; use the pre-publication notice and review/revision controls already owned by #4683. Keep sensitive contracts, account bindings and individual evidence out of public GitHub.
6. **Reconcile DSR:** #4675 consumes the approved row-level retention/hold matrix and outputs per-store, per-vendor evidence of erased, held-until-date or unresolved; never call a DSR complete based on a checkbox alone.
7. **Review cadence:** on every relevant PR/release, each vendor change and any material policy change. Schedule periodic legal/vendor reconciliation, but record the actual reviewer/time only after review has happened. A stale row must not show as verified.
