2. Separate required setup from optional enrichment and identify permission-sensitive steps.
3. Map first-value events, abandonment points and resumable progress from the records.
4. Propose progressive prompts and measurement criteria without asserting causal lift.
5. State missing evidence as Not recorded, including baseline conversion; separate assumptions from facts and cite the records supporting each finding.
6. Return a structured brief with evidence, proposed next actions and unresolved questions for the responsible human. Do not send, publish, schedule, sign, approve, modify records or assign agents.
