### Deck v2 D12: per-slide investor analytics (D1573, #3820)

- **`deck_slide_analytics.ts`** (mounts itself, #4105, no `before`): `GET /api/decks/:id/investor-links/:shareId/analytics` answers one investor link's views, its "Time per slide" consent, seconds per card by card uid in the link's own order (null for a card no heartbeat counted; `cards: null` with the consent off), the most-read card and the visit timeline (`opened`, `nda_accepted`, `reached_ask`, `revisited`). `PUT /api/decks/:id/investor-links/:shareId/time-per-slide` `{ on }` sets the consent and answers `{ ok, link }`. Gate: `projectOwned`, as `GET /:id/engagement`; the consent with `write: true`. A link that is not one of the deck's investor links is 404. No migration.
- **The consent** is the link's own `include_json` key `time_per_slide` (on unless `false`), written by the one link writer (`createDeckLink`, body `time_per_slide`) and the switch above. With it off the share heartbeat (`decks.ts` `POST /share/:token/heartbeat`) stores nothing past the view row: no read seconds, no seconds per card. `GET /:id/investor-links` rows carry `time_per_slide`, and `analytics.read_seconds` is null when off or untimed.
- **Frontend:** `frontend/src/lib/api/deckSlideAnalytics.js`; `deckV2Adapter.slideAnalytics` through it (contract `deckSlideAnalytics.schema.json`); `ViewerAnalyticsSheet.jsx` (pd37) from the Analytics cell of `InvestorLinksTable.jsx` (pd35), its row switch, and the new link's switch in `ShareDialog.jsx`.
