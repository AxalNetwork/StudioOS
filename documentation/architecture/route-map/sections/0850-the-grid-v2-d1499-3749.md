### The Grid v2 (D1499, #3749)

`routes/deck_grid.ts`, mounted at `/api/decks` beside `deck_check.ts`. Geometry and catalogue in `services/decks/structures.ts`, mirrored (geometry only) in `frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/gridModel.js`. Client: `lib/api/deckGrid.js` (`deckGridApi.grid`), read through `deckV2Adapter.grid` (part 2, a `SWAPPED` row of the seam, with D1555's `consistency`). The editor's stage (`GridStage.jsx`), Present and the print page (`BlockGrid.jsx`) place every area with `gridModel.placeOf`, so they draw this route's boxes; ⋯ › Slide size writes `theme.aspect` with the next version save, so no route is added for it.

| Route | Who | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/decks/:id/grid` | whoever may read the deck (`projectOwned`) | `{ deck_id, version, aspect: '16:9' \| '4:3', slide, frame, cell, baseline: 8, grid: { cols: 12, rows: 6 }, structures: [17 × { id, label, use, areas, background?, recommended? }], cards: [{ index, structure, derived, areas: [{ id, c1, c2, r1, r2, order, inset, align, role?, box }] }] }`. A card saved before v2 reads `structure: 'custom', derived: true`, its areas derived from its blocks. Every `box` is `boxOfAt(area, aspect)` in slide pixels. D1554 (#3801): `?aspect=16:9\|4:3` adds `reflow: { from, to, count, moved: [{ card, block, kind: 'trimmed' \| 'removed', text }] }`, which is what a switch to that aspect would move from the saved version into the speaker notes (`services/decks/reflow.ts`), and `reflow_map` (each structure's areas at both aspects). Any other aspect is 400 `invalid_aspect`. D1571 (#3818): the reply also names `lang` (the theme's, or null), `dir` (`ltr` \| `rtl`) and `script` (`latin` \| `arabic` \| `hebrew` \| `cjk`), and the reflow measures at that script. 403 / 404. |

Saves: `PUT`/`POST` of a version keep `card.structure` (one of the 17, or `custom`) and `card.areas` (≤ 12, whole cells, no overlap) and refuse a bad one with its sentence (`modelProblems`); `theme.aspect` keeps `4:3`, and 16:9 is the default left out.
