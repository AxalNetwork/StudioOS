### Deck v2 D3 follow-up: a round's securities exemption (D4086, #4086, #4087)

- **Migration `202611022116_raise_rounds_exemption.sql`:** `raise_rounds.exemption`, nullable, no default, CHECK `506b`, `506c`, `reg_cf`, `reg_s` or `other` (a proposal for counsel). NULL reads "Not recorded"; existing rounds stay NULL.
- **`contacts.ts` `PUT /api/contacts/raise-round`:** takes `exemption` (one of the five, or null to clear; left out, it is kept; anything else is 400). Activity logs the field name `exemption`. Recording anything but 506(c) over a different value unpublishes every published link of a deck for this round (`services/decks/roundExemption.ts`), logs `deck_link_unpublished` on Pitch deck, notifies the venture's founders in-app, and the reply carries `unpublished_links`.
- **`deck_publish.ts`:** `PUT /api/decks/:id/investor-links/:shareId/publish` reads the exemption from the deck's round (`pitch_decks.round_ref`) and ignores any `exemption` in the request. Refusals: 409 `round_not_linked`, 409 `exemption_not_recorded` ("Record this round's exemption in Raise first."), 409 `exemption_506b`, 409 `exemption_not_publishable` (Reg CF, Reg S, other), 503 `round_unreadable`. `GET` adds `round: { uid, name, exemption } | null`, `round_readable` and `refusal: { code, message } | null`.
- **Existing links:** a link published before its round recorded an exemption keeps working; the share read, the email gate, the embed and the preview card each re-check the round (`linkStands`), and a round recorded under anything but 506(c) leaves the link a private link.
- **Frontend:** Raise › Capital relationships › Commitments › Round settings (`CapRelCommitments.jsx` `RoundSettingsDialog`) records it, with a warning before a change that takes links down; ShareDialog's `PublishPanel.jsx` shows the round and its exemption read-only and links to Round settings; the per-link picker is gone. The client methods are unchanged (`api.raiseRoundSave`, `deckPublishApi`).
