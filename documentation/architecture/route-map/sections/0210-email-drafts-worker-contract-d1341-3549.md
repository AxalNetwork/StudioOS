### Email drafts Worker contract (D1341, #3549)

Worker routes in `routes/mail_drafts.ts`, mounted at `/api/mail`, backed by `services/mail/` and migration 648 (`mail_drafts`). The client is `lib/api/mail.js`. There is no page yet: the composer is #3552. Every route needs sign-in, and a support session is refused 403 `support_session_refused`. A person is a ref, `kind:id`: `interviewee`, `prospect`, `cofounder` (`h-<n>`), `growth_partner`, `advisor` (an engagement the member is the client of) or `partner` (an engagement on the member's venture). `member` is part 3's (#3550). A ref the member cannot read is 404 "Record not found.", the same body as one that does not exist.

| Route | Who | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/mail/context?person=[&project=]` | signed in | `{ person, intents, items }`. `items[0]` is the person's record, `selected`, read through its module's record gate, store check and section gates. The company's one-liner and KPI board follow, not selected, only when each has something to say. `project` names the company for an advisor, who has none. **UPDATE 2026-10-28 (D1342, #3550):** a person may be `member:<share uid>`, a Network match (relationship `network`, `email_ready` once both have shared); its record never carries an address. The matches themselves are `routes/mail_shares.ts` (migration 649): `GET /api/mail/shares` (each pair's state on both sides, and the other member's address only while both have shared and neither revoked or declined), `POST /api/mail/shares/:uid` `{ share: true|false }` (409 `not_matched` without a mutual accept) and `DELETE /api/mail/shares/:uid` (hidden again at once; 409 `not_shared`). A pair opens when the second side of an introduction accepts (`POST /api/introductions/propositions/:uid/accept`), which asks both "Share your email address with ‹name›?", linking `/email`. **UPDATE 2026-10-28 (D1348, #3555):** `GET /api/mail/people` (`routes/mail_people.ts`) answers `{ profile, groups: [{ key, label, people: [{ ref, name, role, source, last_contact, marker, marker_label }], empty, error }], note, empty }`, grouped per profile (investor: deal flow, portfolio, co-investors, Network matches; advisor: clients, cohort founders, Network matches; operating partner: clients, leads, Network matches; admin: the applications and tickets they hold, partner firms; Explorer and founder: Network matches). Markers are derived, never stored. A person may now also be `deal:`, `holding:`, `coinvestor:`, `client:`, `cohort:`, `engagement:`, `lead:`, `application:` or `ticket:`, each under its module's own rule and only for its profile. An admin's pack is the case only: no other kind, no company item, and `POST /api/mail/blocks` is 403 `case_only`. **UPDATE 2026-10-28 (D1344, #3552):** `person.address` is the address the person's own module row records (a capital relationship's, a co-founder holder's, a growth partner's contact), null for an interview and always null for a Network match, whose address only `GET /api/mail/shares` serves. |
| `POST /api/mail/blocks` | signed in | Takes `{ kind, ref?, project? }`, where `kind` is `kpis`, `raise_status`, `project_progress`, `interview_insights` or `one_liner`. Answers `{ block: { kind, text, as_of, link } }`, computed now under the module's own gate, or `{ block: null }` when nothing is recorded or readable. An unknown kind is 400 `block_invalid`; no venture is 400 `project_required`. |
| `POST /api/mail/drafts` | signed in | Takes `{ person, intent, tone, length, refs[], blocks[], project?, estimate? }`. With `estimate: true` it answers `{ estimate: { hold_microusd, model }, sources, blocks }` and runs nothing. Otherwise it runs one `mail_draft` run, charged in AI tokens by the router, and answers 201 `{ draft }`, with the draft's sources and its `cost.charged_microusd`. A short balance is 402 in the top-up sentence. An answer that is not an email is 503 `draft_unreadable`. |
| `POST /api/mail/drafts/:uid/revise` | the draft's author | Takes `{ instruction: 'shorter' \| 'warmer' \| free text, project?, estimate? }`. It reads the parent's records again and answers 201 with a new row whose `parent_uid` is the parent. |
| `GET /api/mail/drafts[?person=]`, `GET /api/mail/drafts/:uid`, `DELETE /api/mail/drafts/:uid` | the draft's author | The member's own drafts, newest first. Another member's draft is 404. |
