### Ranking an area's variants (D1525, #3783)

`routes/deck_judge.ts`, self-mounted at `/api/decks` (`export const mount`). One Clef decision (`module_decision`, a `choice` question, `services/ai/decide.ts`) ranks the two or three variants of one of #3764's `deck_area_proposals`, blind: each variant is "Variant A", "B" or "C" in an order drawn from the proposal's uid, and no model name or variant number reaches the judge (`services/decks/judge.ts`). The judge is the first of the `decide` kind's models in #3721's table whose family is none of the writers' (read from the run's own steps); a writer whose family cannot be read refuses it. Clef is billed on input tokens only, and the hold says so (`holdQuoteMicro`). Ranking accepts nothing. The run is under the `deck_judge` rate bucket (30 a minute); the quote runs nothing. No migration. Client: `lib/api/deckJudge.js` (`deckJudgeApi`), read through `deckV2Adapter.judge`; drawn as pd15's "Ranked n of m" in `AreaResults.jsx`. AUTO-5 calls `judgeArea` inside its run's one quote.

| Route | Who | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /api/decks/areas/:uid/judge/quote` | the deck's writers | `{ ai: true, hold_microusd, model_label, family, writer_families, of }`, or `{ ai: false, reason, message }`: `same_family`, `generator_unknown`, `nothing_to_rank`, or the router's own refusal. Runs nothing. |
| `POST /api/decks/areas/:uid/judge` | the deck's writers | `{ proposal_uid, ranked: true, model_label, family, writer_families, ranking: [{ n, rank, probability, line }], best, of, score, clears, panel: { best, agrees, line }, charged_microusd }`. A probability Clef did not send is null; `charged_microusd` is null when not recorded. `ranked: false` with the reason for the family rule, nothing to rank, an answer from a writer's family (charged) or none readable. 404 for a proposal that does not exist or whose deck the member may not write; 409 `not_open` once decided. |
