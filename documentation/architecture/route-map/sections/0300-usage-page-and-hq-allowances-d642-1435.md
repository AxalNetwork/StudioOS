### Usage page and HQ allowances (D642, #1435)

`GET /api/usage/storage` (`usage.ts`, `services/storageUsage.ts`) reads the caller's own stored files across all their companies, whatever company the sidebar has selected. It returns `{ total_bytes, files, unknown_size_files, areas }`, with one area per store: `data_room`, `diligence_files`, `research_library`, `message_attachments` and `interview_recordings`. A recording counts toward the member who owns its project. A NULL size counts as unknown, never as 0 bytes, and a failed read marks only that area `recorded: false`. **UPDATE 2026-10-26 (D1169, #3082, Storage part 3):** the read grows to thirteen areas, adding `data_room_versions`, `session_audio`, `deck_reviews`, `validate_evidence`, `build_proofs`, `grow_minutes`, `brand_files` and `csv_imports`, and (D1411, #3894) `articles`, each article's objects under `articles/<id>/` read from R2 and counted to its author, each with its `kind` and `measured_bytes`. It also returns `measured_bytes`, `plan_key`, `allowance_bytes`, `allowance_reason`, `plan_recorded`, `state` (`ok`, `near_80`, `near_95`, `full`, `over`, `no_allowance`), `kinds`, `companies` (with ventures) and the ten `largest` files with `largest_complete`. Company files now count to the company owner, not the uploader (#2449 owner question 3, A). Signed copies, fund, identity, 83(b) and HQ stores are never read. `/account/usage/storage` lists the thirteen places until part 5 (#3084) draws the rest. **UPDATE 2026-11-02 (D1580 part 2a, #3827):** a fourteenth area, `deck_rehearsals` (kind `recordings`), counts a rehearsal's audio while it is kept and still stored, at its size in `deck_rehearsal_extras`. **UPDATE 2026-11-03 (D1590, #3837):** a fifteenth area, `deck_recordings` (kind `recordings`), counts every stored walkthrough take at its `media_bytes`; a take is kept until its maker deletes it.

Both screens are built to the Usage canvas (`Ops · Boards.dc.html`, set `usage`; `us-*` and `hq-allowances` in `Ops Page.dc.html` and `ops-data.js`, committed by #1456). Their figures come from D640 (`ai_budget.ts`), D641 (`ai.ts`: `by_model`, `days`, task labels, `GET /ai/rates`) and the storage read above, never from the canvas's bracketed placeholders.
