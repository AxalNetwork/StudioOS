### Product testing Worker contract (D812, #2258)

These are Worker routes in `routes/testing.ts`, backed by `services/testing.ts` and migration 516. Three routers share one set of rules. Every change writes one `testing_events` row, in the same batch and only when the change happened. The bell is told in-app. A partner's programme is draft, then submitted, approved by HQ, opened by HQ, and closed. HQ's own programme opens from draft. Picks never exceed `places`: the limit is checked in the same `UPDATE`. The frontend methods are `productTestingApi`, `partnerTestingApi` and `adminTestingApi` in `frontend/src/lib/api/productTesting.js`. D812 added no UI. The owner retired D814's member page, Studio cards and Account menu entries in #2314 (2026-10-21). The old `/product-testing` address, including historical My applications notification links, redirects signed-in members to Studio. New status notifications open Inbox. The member API and stored programmes/applications remain; their future member intake/withdrawal flow is tracked in #2318; the partner's and HQ's management screens are D815's.

| Route | Who | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/testing/programmes` | signed in | `items`: open programmes, between `opens_at` and `closes_at`, whose `eligible_roles` hold the caller's role and with a place left. Each has `places_left` and `my_status`. Also `consent`: the sentence a member agrees to and its version. **UPDATE 2026-10-27 (D1336, #3541):** this read and `POST /api/testing/programmes/:uid/apply` are an active Product tester's only; anyone else gets 403 `not_active_tester` with the sentence for their pool status, and `GET /api/testing/summary` counts nothing for them. The member's own place in the pool (`services/testerPool.ts`, D1042's store): `GET /api/testing/tester` (the row, the consent text and version, `can_apply`), `POST /api/testing/tester/apply` (`{ consent_version, availability? }`; refused while applied, active or paused, for a declined member before `reapply_after`, and for one HQ removed) and `POST /api/testing/tester/leave` (`removed`, "Left by the member"; picked places carry on); an Admin is refused each. `GET /api/testing/applications/mine` rows carry `finished`, `decides_by` and `withdrawn_by`; closing a programme tells each picked tester (`testing_programme_closed`, linking `/inbox`). |
| `GET /api/testing/applications/mine` | signed in | `items`: the caller's applications, with the programme's name, dates and status. |
| `POST /api/testing/programmes/:uid/apply` | signed in | `{ answers, availability?, consent_email? }`, one answer per question. Answers 201 `{ application }`. Refusals: 409 `not_open` (not open, or past its close), 403 `not_eligible` (the caller's role), 409 `programme_full`, 409 `already_applied`, and 400 for missing answers. `consent_email: true` records the time and `consent_version`. The member is notified (`testing_applied`). |
| `POST /api/testing/applications/:uid/withdraw` | the applicant | An applied or picked application becomes `withdrawn`, which frees a pick's place. Another member's is 404; one already withdrawn or declined is 409 `not_withdrawable`. |
| `GET /api/testing/summary` | signed in | Member summary (the D814 Studio card is retired): `open_count`, what the caller could apply to now, and `picked`, the programmes they were picked for. |
| `GET /api/partner/testing/programmes` | a partner | `items`: their firm's programmes (`owner_partner_id`), each with `places_left`, `picked_count` and `applicant_count`. |
| `POST /api/partner/testing/programmes` | a partner | A draft for their firm (201): product name, logo key, summary, tester tasks, time needed, the four dates, `places` (1 to 10,000), `eligible_roles` (one or more of founder, partner, investor, exploring, advisor) and up to 5 `questions`. Bad input is 400 `invalid_request`. |
| `PATCH /api/partner/testing/programmes/:uid` | a partner | The same fields, on a draft or on one HQ sent back, which becomes a draft again. Any other status is 409 `not_editable`. |
| `POST /api/partner/testing/programmes/:uid/submit` | a partner | A draft is sent to HQ (`submitted`). Anything else is 409 `not_submittable`. |
| `GET /api/partner/testing/programmes/:uid/applicants` | a partner | `{ programme, items, tester_pool_readable }`: each applicant's name, role, answers, availability, `consented`, status and dates, without the withdrawn ones, and `tester` (D1200: in the tester pool, active or paused; null when the pool could not be read). Never an email address. |
| `POST /api/partner/testing/applications/:uid/pick`, `…/decline` | a partner | Allowed while the programme is open, testing or closed. Pick takes an applied or declined application; past the places it is 409 `programme_full`. Decline takes an applied or picked one. The member is notified (`testing_picked`, `testing_not_picked`); nobody is emailed. |
| `GET /api/admin/testing/programmes?status=` | Super Admin | Every programme, with `owner_name` (Axal VC, or the partner firm), optionally one status. An unknown status is 400. |
| `POST /api/admin/testing/programmes/:uid/approve`, `…/reject` | Super Admin | `{ note }`, required to reject. A partner's submitted programme becomes `approved` or `rejected`, with `review_note`. Anything else is 409 `not_reviewable`. Whoever created it is notified (`testing_programme_approved`, `testing_programme_sent_back`). |
| `POST /api/admin/testing/programmes/:uid/open` | Super Admin | HQ's from draft, a partner's only once approved; otherwise 409 `not_openable`. D1198 (#3148): the firm's active partner accounts are told (`testing_programme_opened`); the answer is `{ programme, told: { owner } }`, `owner` null for HQ's own. |
| `POST /api/admin/testing/programmes/:uid/close` | Super Admin | An open or testing programme becomes `closed`; otherwise 409 `not_closable`. D1198: the firm is told (`testing_programme_closed`), with `told` as for open. |
| `POST /api/admin/testing/programmes/:uid/testing` | Super Admin | D1198 (#3148): an open or closed programme becomes `testing`; otherwise 409 `not_testable`. The firm and the picked testers on active accounts are told by the bell (`testing_started`). `{ programme, told: { owner, testers, mail_reach } }`: counts only, `mail_reach` being how many Mail's `testers:<uid>` rule would write to. StudioOS sends no email. |
| `POST /api/admin/testing/applications/:uid/withdraw` | Super Admin | D1198: `{ reason }`, 10 to 500 characters (else 400). An applied or picked application becomes `withdrawn` (else 409 `not_withdrawable`), freeing a picked place; the member is told with the reason (`testing_withdrawn`); the event is `withdrawn_by_hq` under the Super Admin. `{ application, freed_place, told: { member } }`. |
| `POST /api/admin/testing/programmes`, `PATCH …/:uid`, `POST /api/admin/testing/applications/:uid/pick`, `…/decline` | Super Admin | The partner's routes, on HQ's own programmes (`owner_kind = 'hq'`). A partner's programme answers 404 here. |
| `GET /api/admin/testing/programmes/:uid/applicants` | Super Admin | D1197 (#3147): any programme's applicants, as the partner's read, with `can_decide`: true on HQ's own, false on a partner's, which HQ reads only (#2382 owner question 2, read as yes). Pick and decline above still reach HQ's own only. Never an email. |
| `GET /api/admin/testing/programmes/:uid/events?page=&page_size=` | Super Admin | D1197 (TST5's Activity): `{ programme, events: { available, reason, page, page_size, total, items } }`, newest first, 25 a page (100 at most). Each item has `action`, `at`, `actor` (`{ name }`, or null), `application_uid`, `applicant_name` and `detail`. Never an email. A missing programme is 404; a failed read is 503 `testing_unreadable`. |
| `GET /api/admin/testing/applications?programme=&owner=&status=&role=&consented=&tester=&q=&sort=&page=&page_size=` | Super Admin | D1197 (TST8): every application across programmes, `{ applications: { available, reason, page, page_size, total, sort, items } }`. Each item has name, role, status, `consented`, `in_pool` (an active tester in D1042's pool), answers, availability, dates and its programme (uid, name, owner). Filters: `owner` is `hq`, `partner` or `partner:<firm id>`; `status` left out means everyone but the withdrawn, `all` means everyone; `consented` and `tester` are `yes` or `no`; `q` searches the name (at least two characters, wildcards as themselves). `sort` is `applied` (newest first) or `name`. A bad filter is 400; a failed read is 503. Never an email. |

**The product tester pool (D1042, #2890; migration 576).** Under `/api/admin/product-testers` (`admin_product_testers.ts`), every route `requireAdmin`.

| Route | Who | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/admin/product-testers?status=&role=&availability_given=&in_programme=&page=&page_size=` | Admin | `rows`, oldest application first, 25 a page (100 at most), each with `member` (name, email, role, `email_verified`, `kyc_status`), `decided_by_name` and `programmes: { applied, picked }` (D1200), and, for a declined one, `reapply_after`; `totals` per status; `matching`, what every filter keeps. `availability_given` and `in_programme` (a picked place in a programme not closed or rejected) are `yes` or `no`. An unknown status is 400 `unknown_status`, a bad role 400 `unknown_role`, a bad yes/no 400 `bad_filter`. The email stays for Approvals; the Product testing page never draws it. |
| `GET /api/admin/product-testers/:id` | Admin | `{ tester, programs, events }`: the testing programs they applied to and their history. 404 `tester_not_found`. |
| `POST /api/admin/product-testers/:id/approve`, `…/decline`, `…/pause`, `…/resume`, `…/remove` | Admin | applied → active; applied → declined (`reason` of 10 or more characters, reapply 90 days on); active → paused (`reason`); paused → active; active or paused → removed (`reason`). `note` is HQ's alone. The member is told by email and inbox (`product_tester_<event>`). Refusals: 409 `email_unverified`, `admins_cannot_test`, `wrong_status`, `tester_changed`; 400 `reason_required`. |

Mail's testers list is `testers:<uid>` on D811's link, read by `testerRecipients` (`services/mailLinkTesters.ts`): picked testers who consented and have not turned programme emails off, on a programme that has opened, at an address neither suppressed nor unsubscribed. `testersAudience(env, uid)` reads the same rule. Mail's two Product testing reads are under the Mail link contract below (D812 part 2b).

**Referral partners and their custom terms (D958, #2791; migration 563).** Under `/api/admin/referral-partners` (`admin_referral_partners.ts`), every route `requireHqAdmin`. Money (accrued, paid) is the Super Admin's; an Admin's read carries `{ available: false, reason: 'hq_reads' }` in its place.

| Route | Who | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/admin/referral-partners?segment=&terms=&status=&q=&sort=&limit=&offset=` | HQ admin | `items` (name, segment, terms state, code, introductions, converted, money, last activity), `total`, `facts` (Who, Join, Terms). |
| `POST /api/admin/referral-partners`, `PATCH /api/admin/referral-partners/:userId` | HQ admin | HQ adds a member as a referral partner with a segment (OD3 A), or changes it. 409 `admins_cannot_refer`, `already_referral_partner`. |
| `GET /api/admin/referral-partners/summary?quarter=`, `GET /api/admin/referral-partners/series` | HQ admin | The quarter's partners, signed and waiting terms, introductions and money by currency; introductions by week, outcome and segment, and money by month. |
| `GET /api/admin/referral-partners/terms?status=`, `GET /api/admin/referral-partners/:userId/terms` | HQ admin | The terms in one state, oldest first (submitted: the Queue); one partner's terms, newest first. Each row carries its state today (`active` and `expired` read from its dates) and its triggers. |
| `POST /api/admin/referral-partners/terms`, `PATCH …/terms/:uid`, `POST …/terms/:uid/submit` | HQ admin | A draft, its edits, and draft → submitted. Refusals `trigger_unknown`, `trigger_amount_ambiguous`, `invalid_dates`, `terms_not_draft`, `terms_overlap`. |
| `POST /api/admin/referral-partners/terms/:uid/approve` | Super Admin, step-up | submitted → approved; a plain Admin gets 403 `super_admin_required` before any code (O-TERMS A). |
| `POST /api/admin/referral-partners/terms/:uid/preview`, `…/send`, `…/end` | HQ admin | The contract through today's envelope preview and send to the partner on record (approved → sent, `envelope_id` stored); signed or active → ended with a reason. E-sign completion signs the row by its envelope. |

`fireCommissionEvent` pays a referrer's active signed terms before the standard rule, within their caps and with no L2 or L3 bonus, and writes `commissions.terms_id`. `GET /api/network/admin/commission-rules` gains `fires` (only `referral_kyc_approved` today; OD4 open).
