### Annual licence-fee invoices Worker contract (D802, #2238)

HQ invoices a territory licensee's annual fee, one invoice per licence year, the year running from the licence's anniversary (`starts_on`). Backed by `services/licenceFeeInvoices.ts` and migration 513 (`licence_fee_invoices`). The PDF is the generated-document type `territory_licence_fee_invoice`, drawn from the figures frozen at issue; Holdings is the sender, and the payment block is the `territory_licence_fee` purpose, masked.

| Route | Who | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/admin/licences/:uid/fee-invoices` | Super Admin | `{ invoices, annual_fee_cents, currency, starts_on }`: the licence's invoices, newest year first, each with its period, amount, currency, reference (`AXAL-LIC-<licence_ref>-<yyyy-yy>`), issue and due dates, status (`issued`, `paid`, `void`), paid total, outstanding, notes and `pdf_url` (null when void). |
| `POST /api/admin/licences/:uid/fee-invoices` | Super Admin with a TOTP session and a recent step-up | Issues the invoice for the licence year that holds today (the first year before the licence starts), at the ledger's `annual_fee_cents` and `currency`, due after D797's `payment_terms_days` (30 days when not recorded). A licence whose terms name another IP licensor than Holdings is refused (`licensor_not_payee`). 409 `invoice_exists` (with its uid) while that year is invoiced, a void one included; 409 `no_annual_fee`, `no_start_date`, `licence_not_billable` (not active or suspended), or the payment refusal when no Holdings account carries the purpose. Audited (`licence_fee_invoice_issued`). |
| `PATCH /api/admin/licences/:uid/fee-invoices/:invoiceUid` | Super Admin with a TOTP session and a recent step-up | `{ paid_cents, paid_note? }` records the running total received (400 `bad_amount`, 409 `over_amount`); the status is `paid` at the full amount, else `issued`. `{ status: 'void', void_note }` voids an invoice with nothing paid (409 `paid_not_void`; a reason of 10 characters or more). A void invoice takes nothing more (409 `invoice_void`). Audited. |
| `GET /api/admin/licences/:uid/fee-invoices/:invoiceUid/pdf` | Super Admin | The invoice as issued, `private, no-store`. A void invoice is not printed (409). |
| `GET /api/licence/mine/statements` (D798) | the licence's Administrator | Gains `fee_invoices` (newest year first, without HQ's notes, each with its own `pdf_url`) and `fee_invoices_reason` when the read failed. |
| `POST /api/admin/statements/draw` (D111, amended) | Super Admin | Each stream is owed at D797's share for it (`share_subscriptions_bps`, `share_licence_fees_bps`, `share_token_margin_bps`), else the ledger's `revenue_share_bps`; each stream's row records `share_bps`, `share_source` and `owed_cents`, and a fallback row says so. A fifth row, `platform_licence_royalties`, is the licence fees' gross at `royalty_platform_licence_bps`, Not recorded and owing nothing when unset. Terms that cannot be read refuse the draw (503 `terms_unreadable`); a re-draw below what was recorded is still 409 `recorded_above_owed`. |
| `GET /api/licence/mine/fee-invoices/:uid/pdf` | the licence's Administrator | One of their own licence's invoices, as issued. Another licence's or an unknown uid is 404; a void one 409; on a branch 501 `invoice_hq_only`. |
