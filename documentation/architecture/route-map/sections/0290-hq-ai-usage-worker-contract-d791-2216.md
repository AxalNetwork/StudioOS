### HQ › AI usage Worker contract (D791, #2216)

`routes/admin_ai_usage.ts`, mounted at `/api/admin/ai-usage`, on HQ only. Every route needs a recent step-up (`requireStepUp`) and the Super Admin elevation (`requireSuperAdmin`); anyone else gets 403, nothing changed. Each write is audited in `activity_logs` with its reason, in the same batch as the change (`services/aiUsageAdmin.ts`). A member is `{id, name, email}` on these routes alone: the owner's ruling of 2026-10-20 (S1 on #2227), so a Super Admin can find and reach the member behind a figure. No member-facing answer gains an email, the audit lines keep the user id, and no answer carries a prompt or an output. Part 2 is the page, `/admin/platform/ai-usage` (board B5 of the "Usage & Billing console" canvas; its row is under "Usage page and HQ allowances" below). For it the read also answers `by_day_model` (each day's cost and charge by model, for the daily chart), `topups_microusd` (every member's packs credited in the range, bonus left out), each member row's `role` and `plan`, the detail's `role`, `plan` and `joined`, and each history line's `actor_name`.

| Route | Who | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/admin/ai-usage?range=7d\|30d\|mtd` | Super Admin, step-up | Runs, refused runs, cost, charged and margin (the charge less the cost of the rows that recorded one), and `not_recorded`, totalled and by day, model, role and plan (the AI licence); the top 20 members by charged (each with runs, cost, charged, margin and `topups_microusd`, the AI tokens bought in the range) and by velocity (today over the 7-day daily average, the average counted as at least $1); the flags (`spike`, `refusals`, `held`, `refund_after_spend`, `new_heavy`), each with why and since; `thresholds` (`AI_USAGE_FLAGS`); and who is paused or capped. A member is `{id, name, email}`. Another range is 400 `invalid_range`; an unreadable store 503. |
| `GET /api/admin/ai-usage/members?q=&range=` | Super Admin, step-up | Find members: `q` all digits is an exact user ID, else a case-insensitive email prefix (`%` and `_` match themselves). At most 50 rows, ordered by email, each with the same sums as a top member; a member with no runs reads zeros. An empty `q`, or one over 254 characters, is 400 `invalid_query`. |
| `GET /api/admin/ai-usage/members/:id?range=` | Super Admin, step-up | One member over the range: `totals` (equal to their top-members row), `by_model` and `by_day` with Cloudflare's cost beside the charge; `balance`, `allowance` (`resolveAiCaps`) and `spend_limit` (D788), each `{state: 'ok', …}` or `{state: 'unreadable'}`, never a zero; `pause` and `cap` (or null) with who set them; and `history`, the member's pause and cap audit lines, newest first (at most 100). An unknown ID is 404 `ai_member_not_found`. |
| `POST /api/admin/ai-usage/members/:id/pause` | Super Admin, step-up | `{reason}` (10–500 characters): pauses the member's AI (migration 510's `member_ai_pauses`) and tells them once, in the app. From then the hold refuses their runs as `ai_paused`, 403, "AI is paused on this account. Contact support.", and nothing is charged. Already paused is 409 `already_paused`; an unknown member 404. |
| `DELETE /api/admin/ai-usage/members/:id/pause` | Super Admin, step-up | `{reason}`: restores the member's AI; the audit line names the pause it ends. Not paused is 404 `not_paused`. |
| `PUT /api/admin/ai-usage/members/:id/cap` | Super Admin, step-up | `{monthly_usd, reason}`: a monthly cap for one member, $0 to $100,000, or null to clear it (`member_ai_caps`). `resolveAiCaps` reads it after the licence's cap and the lower one wins, so it only ever narrows an allowance. The audit line has the cap before and after. |
