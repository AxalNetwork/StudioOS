### Generate into areas (D1512, #3764)

`routes/deck_areas.ts`, mounted at `/api/decks` beside `deck_batches.ts`. "Generate into N areas" asks for one quote, admits one run under one parent hold (`admitDeckRun`, D1502) and makes one proposal per area in `deck_area_proposals` (migration `202611022246_deck_area_proposals.sql`). Nothing reaches a card from here: the editor places an accepted variant and Save writes the version. The admission path is under the `deck_generate` rate bucket. Client: `lib/api/deckAreas.js` (`deckAreasApi`), read through `deckV2Adapter.areasQuote` and `areasGenerate`.

| Route | Who | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /api/decks/:id/areas/quote` | the deck's writers | `{ class, quote_uid, expires_at, hold_microusd, runs, refused, areas: [{ key, card_uid, area_id, element, kind, model, model_label, variants, gates, refusal, lines, hold_microusd }] }`. Each area's lines sum to its hold. An area the server refuses (its gate, its plan, nothing to write from) carries its sentence and holds nothing. The model is resolved here per element; the client never sends one. |
| `POST /api/decks/:id/areas` | the deck's writers, with an `Idempotency-Key` | One run for every area that can run: `{ batch_uid, class, hold_microusd, replayed, proposals, refused }`. 409 `quote_stale` when the quote's price, areas or 10 minutes changed; 422 `nothing_to_run` when no area can run; the run's own refusals (`deck_busy`, an allowance) as `admitDeckRun` answers them. The same key twice is the same run. |
| `GET /api/decks/:id/areas/proposals?batch=<uid>` | the deck's writers | `{ batch: { uid, state, held_microusd, charged_microusd }, done, proposals }`, each proposal settled from its steps as they end, with one variant per seed: model name, seed, cost, seconds, its own error, and the free critique (Fits, Contrast AA, Density OK). Without `batch`, the deck's open proposals. |
| `POST /api/decks/:id/areas/:card_uid/:area_id/accept` | the deck's writers | `{ proposal_uid, variant, all? }` → `{ proposal, variant }`. Accept is by the area: the area's other open proposals are superseded, and an image variant's deck image is kept (the others discarded). 409 `variant_not_ready` for a failed variant, `not_open` for a decided one. |
| `POST /api/decks/:id/areas/proposals/:uid/discard` | the deck's writers | `{ proposal }`, outcome `discard`; its proposed images are discarded. A proposal nobody decided expires after 14 days (A15's daily cleanup). |
