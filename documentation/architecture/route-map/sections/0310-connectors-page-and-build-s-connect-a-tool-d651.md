### Connectors page and Build's "Connect a tool" (D651, #1463)

Connectors replace Integrations (D649). `/integrations` and `/account/integrations` (retired D2319) redirect to `/account/connectors` with their query and hash, so a provider's sign-in, which still returns to `/integrations?…` (`integrations.ts`, `calendar.ts`, `linkedin.ts`), opens that connector's own page on what happened (D753, #1876: `/account/connectors/:key`; a Mercury return goes to the hub, stripped). The page reads `/api/integrations` until #1462 (D650) lands `/api/connectors`; the fields only D650 carries read "Not recorded". `IntegrationsPage.jsx` is retired. Canvases: `Connectors Page.dc.html`, `Cycle Action.dc.html` and `Connectors · Boards.dc.html`, in S1's intake for #1461.

**AI assistants (D665, D666, #1496).** StudioOS answers as a remote MCP server at `/mcp` (D784, #2204; `/api/mcp` and every path under it answer 308 to the same path under `/mcp`), with its OAuth 2.1 endpoints at `/api/oauth/authorize`, `/api/oauth/token` and `/api/oauth/register`, and discovery at `/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server` and `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource/mcp`. These are not Hono routes: the Worker's `fetch` sends them to `cloudflare-worker/src/mcp/oauth.ts` first. The SPA's half is `GET /api/mcp/consent/:id`, `POST …/approve` and `POST …/deny`, `GET /api/connectors/assistants` and `DELETE /api/connectors/assistants/:id` (`routes/mcp_consent.ts`). The consent page is `/connect/assistant?request=:id` (D668, #1498): `pages/connect/ConnectAssistantPage.jsx`, mounted signed-in outside the shell's guard. The grants are Connectors' "AI assistants" group (`pages/account/AssistantsGroup.jsx`, `#ai-assistants`), and Record a session offers "From an AI assistant". All of it answers 404 while `MCP_SERVER_ENABLED` is not "true" or `OAUTH_KV` is not bound; live in production since 2026-10-14 (D681).
