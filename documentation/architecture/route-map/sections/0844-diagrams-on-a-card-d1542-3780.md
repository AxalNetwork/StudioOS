### Diagrams on a card (D1542, #3780)

A Flow, Cycle or Matrix is generated by the `grid_diagram` job through Generate into areas (D1512, above): the element's model is the rail's Diagrams group's (`resolveElementModel`), the model writes structure only (`{ type, nodes, edges }`), and code strips every number, draws it and refuses it when `fitProblems` or a node's own box says it overflows. It arrives as a proposal; nothing is saved before Accept. Funnel, Hierarchy, Hub and spoke and Positioning 2×2 are refused "coming later". `routes/deck_diagrams.ts`, mounted at `/api/decks` before `decks.ts`, checks a diagram the founder edits on the canvas. Client: `lib/api/deckDiagrams.js` (`deckDiagramsApi`), read through `deckV2Adapter.diagrams`.

| Route | Who | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /api/decks/:id/diagrams/check` | whoever may read the deck | `{ diagram, grid, card? }` → `{ diagram, type, fits, problems: [{ kind, message }], figures, steps }`. Free: no model, nothing saved. `diagram` is the stored diagram as it would be saved, or null with a problem; `problems` are `fitProblems` and every node's box at the deck's aspect; `figures` are the numbers the founder's own labels name, shown and never removed. 400 `invalid_grid`. |
