### An instruction on the selection (D1521, #3773)

`routes/deck_instruct.ts`, mounted at `/api/decks` beside `deck_areas.ts`. An instruction (or one action) on up to six areas of one card is quoted, admitted as **one** run under one parent hold (`admitDeckRun`, D1502) and answered as one proposal per selected area in #3764's `deck_area_proposals`, which also records the selection, the hash of the words and what was asked (migration `202611031458_deck_area_instructions.sql`). Nothing outside the selection is quoted, run or proposed, and nothing reaches a card from here: the proposals are read, accepted by the area and discarded through the areas routes. The run path is under the `deck_generate` rate bucket; the quote is not. Client: `lib/api/deckInstruct.js` (`deckInstructApi`), read through `deckV2Adapter.instruct`.

| Route | Who | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /api/decks/:id/areas/instruct/quote` | the deck's writers | `{ card, selection: [{ area_id, card_uid?, instruction? }], instruction? \| action?, variants? \| mode? }` → #3764's areas quote: per-area lines that sum to the hold, each area's gates and refusal (`not_text` for a figure, chart or picture, `no_text`, `area_not_found`, the model gates), the total, `quote_uid` and `expires_at`. 422 `names_figure` ("Figures come from your records. Pick a recorded figure instead. Nothing was charged.") before anything is quoted; 400 for a malformed body (`too_many_areas`, `one_card`, `one_change`, `no_instruction`, `instruction_too_long`). |
| `POST /api/decks/:id/areas/instruct` | the deck's writers, with an `Idempotency-Key` | The same body (+ `quote_uid`) → `{ batch_uid, class, hold_microusd, replayed, selection, action, proposals, refused }`. 409 `quote_stale` when the price, the areas or the words changed or 10 minutes passed; 422 `names_figure` or `nothing_to_run`; the run's own refusals as `admitDeckRun` answers them. The same key twice is the same run, charged once. |
