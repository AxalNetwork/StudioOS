### Bank accounts Worker contract (D708, #1773)

`/api/admin/bank-accounts` (`admin_bank_accounts.ts`) is the Super Admin's registry of each Axal entity's bank accounts, on HQ only; a branch refuses. `GET /` needs `requireSuperAdmin` and returns every account masked (`••••NNNN`), `coverage` (the resolver's answer per payment purpose), `fund_i` (the fund carrying the `spinout-fund-i` slug, or null) and `refusals` (D717, #1856: `{ code, message }` for the four refusals the console explains, each in the resolver's own sentence). Each write and the reveal first refuse an admin without the elevation with 403 `super_admin_required`, then take `requireSuperAdminWriteBar`: `POST /` adds (201), `PATCH /:uid` takes `label` and `purposes` only (anything else is 409 `instructions_immutable`), `POST /:uid/retire` takes a reason, and `POST /:uid/reveal` takes a reason, writes `entity_bank_account_reveals` first and answers one number with `Cache-Control: no-store`. Migration 472 holds the number only as `v1.` ciphertext, and its triggers refuse any change in place.

The resolver is `services/payInstructions.ts`. `payInstructionsFor` gives masked instructions and the warning; `revealPayInstructions` records a reveal and then decrypts. Its refusals are `payee_not_entitled`, `fund_account_missing`, `no_active_account`, `holder_not_payee`, `savings_account`, `ambiguous_account` and `account_unreadable`. A capital call on Fund I is refused with that code (409, nothing written) on `POST /api/funds/:id/capital-call`, `POST /api/funds/:id/lps` with `first_call_cents`, both legacy `/api/capital` call routes, and the `capital_call_notice` job, which writes `capital_call_held` instead. `POST /api/funds/:id/capital-calls/preview` adds `payment_instructions`. Moving the slug onto or off a fund through `PATCH /api/funds/:id` is the Super Admin's. `GET /api/funds/lp-portal` adds `pay_instructions` to each unpaid Fund I line (masked, with the reference `AXAL-CC-<fund>-L<lp>-<line>`). `GET /api/funds/:id/capital-calls/lines/:lineId/pay-instructions` gives the LP whose line it is the full instructions, recorded first; a support session gets 403, anyone else 404, and a paid line 409. Writes, the reveals and the LP's GET share the fail-closed `bank_accounts` rate-limit bucket (10 a minute per user).
