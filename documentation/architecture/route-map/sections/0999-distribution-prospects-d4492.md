### Distribution Prospects (D4492, #4492; part of #3722)

`/grow/distribution/prospects` mounts the venture's existing customer pipeline
and individual prospect sheets. The Grow form-options reader supplies write
permission; false, failed and malformed responses disable write controls.
`?import=1` opens the consent-aware CSV import for writers.

The campaign wizard uses one venture/segment consent-summary reading in
Audience and Schedule. Unrecorded segment or imported-list membership remains
Not recorded. Marketing consent and venture suppression remain the stores
introduced by #4511. This integration adds no sending or migration.
