### Formation partner assignments (D6710)

HQ offers a formation case to a partner firm at `POST /api/admin/formation/cases/:uid/offer`. It moves the case to another firm at `POST /api/admin/formation/cases/:uid/reassign`, which needs a step-up and a reason. `GET /api/admin/formation/cases/:uid/eligible-partners` ranks the firms that cover the case's form. Each ineligible firm comes with the reason it cannot be offered the case: verification, coverage, licence, data agreement or restricted places. All three routes are limited to the case's HQ owner or a Super Admin, and both writes re-screen the case's people first. A reassignment ends the old firm's access in the same write and records a deletion or return request to that firm. The request is not sent until counsel approves its wording. `canAccessFormationCase` in `services/formationAssignments.ts` is the partner-side check that #6711's routes will call. An offer that expires returns the case to the queue's `unassigned` view.
