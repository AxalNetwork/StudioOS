### Capital relationships part 2 (D1190, #3124)

`cycle_raise.ts`, mounted at `/api/cycles/raise`; the reads are `services/capRelReads.ts`, under the module's gate (the venture's founder or an admin). Migration 607 adds cents and a currency to prospects, rounds, pro-rata and closes, and a start to a prospect's step.
- `GET /capital-relationships/commitments?project=` → `{ currency, round, totals, investors: [{ key, prospect_id, name, chosen, lines }], sources, words, basis }`. It reads typed prospects at Committed, answered round calls, deal-room commitments and (the founder alone) Mercury's matched credits. Closings say "not recorded". Received, Signed, Wired, Confirmed and Pending count; Indicated, Soft and Status not recorded never do. Each investor counts once, by their strongest line. A missing amount is null, never 0, and another currency is never summed. A failed store is `unreadable` and the rest answer.
- `GET /capital-relationships/match?project=&q=&hq_country=&focus=&size_band=&fit=&warm=1&sort=&limit=&offset=` → the fund directory's answer, each fund with `fit`, `warm_paths` (the caller's own network), `in_pipeline` and `stage: null`; `verified_investors: { count }`, never names. `stage`, a bad `fit` or `warm` is 400.
- A meeting or call logged with `starts_at` (the record's Log action, `logProspectEvent`) becomes an `investor_meeting` item on the venture founder's `/calendar`, its `.ics` and Google and Outlook sync, with no attendees.
- Client: `capitalRelationshipsApi` in `frontend/src/lib/api/capitalRelationships.js`. Overview draws the commitments read's totals (D1192); Commitments and Match draw them (D1193).
