Distinct from `/marketplace` (services) and `/plans-and-pricing` (Stripe storefront). No perk or credit-ledger table exists. **Wave 4 shipped** it: migration 186 (`perks`, `perk_claims`, `perk_credit_ledger`, `perk_views`), `routes/perks.ts`, and `/perks` role-branched inside one route — founders browse and claim, partners submit and see their own listing stats, admins review. A perk is priced one of three ways: `credits` debits the ledger, `tier` is gated by the SAME `userMeetsTier` the rest of the product uses rather than a second notion of entitlement, and `money` quotes `price_cents` the partner invoices offline. Balance is `SUM(delta)` and is never stored; a claim and its debit go in one `DB.batch`, and two unique indexes (`perk_claims(perk_id, user_id)`, `perk_credit_ledger(user_id, kind, source_ref)`) make a double-spend unreachable rather than unlikely. **A second credit ledger, deliberately:** `intro_credit_ledger` (migration 150) is a different unit — one intro credit buys one warm introduction, where the canvas prices perks at 150–3,200 — so sharing the pool would impose an exchange rate nobody set; and its `bucket`/`kind` CHECK clauses cannot be extended without rebuilding a live table, which an additive migration must not do. **Not built, and why:** no monthly plan allowance. How many credits Growth or Studio includes is a commercial term nobody has set, and inventing one would put a made-up price in the product — the same reason Pricing is blocked in Wave 3. Credits enter only through an admin grant today, and the response carries `allowance_configured` so the page can say "no allowance is set up" instead of "not enough credits", which are very different things to tell someone with a zero balance. The catalogue also starts empty: the partners named in the canvas (Northlake, Sablefin, Oakbridge…) are placeholders, and listing them would invent commercial relationships. |
