### 3. Custom terms (PG8) and the other writes

Every write is a dialog. A refusal is one sentence in place, keyed on part 5's code (`PARTNER_REFUSALS`, with the shared ones through `refusalSentence`).

- **Custom terms** opens the partner's newest draft to edit. Live terms open read only, with what their step allows. With neither, it opens a new draft. It has:
  - the contract (Partner Custom Deal, or Finder's Fee / Intro Agreement), with Preview (the template store's PDF);
  - What pays, one row per trigger, a flat amount or a share and never both;
  - the caps, Starts → Ends, the currency and the sectors;
  - the live sentence, built in the browser from the fields;
  - the five steps, from the terms' state;
  - the foot's three sentences.
- **Only the triggers an event fires today are offered** (`FIRING_TRIGGERS`, held equal to the Worker's `FIRING_RULES` by the test). That is a referred member's identity check, which carries no amount, so it pays a flat amount only.
- **Save draft** and **Send to HQ for approval** are an Admin's. The send saves the draft, then submits it.
- **Approve** is the Super Admin's (O-TERMS A), behind the step-up bar: `request()` opens the shared authenticator prompt and retries once.
- **Send contract** previews the contract (`POST /terms/:uid/preview`), then sends what was previewed (`preview_sha256`, native signing).
- **End terms** needs a reason of 10 characters or more.
- **Not drawn:** Studio, because terms are HQ's until #2376 and part 3; and Payout method, because the partner picks it on each payout request, and credits wait for OD6.
