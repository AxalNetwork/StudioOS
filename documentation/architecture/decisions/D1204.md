### 1. The Tester pool (TST9)

`TestersTab` in `frontend/src/pages/admin/productTesting/ProductTestingTabs.jsx`, with its derivations in `productTestingModel.js` (pure).

- **The read is the whole pool.** `readPool` reads `GET /api/admin/product-testers` with no status, 100 a page, at most 10 pages, as Approvals does. The chips, the chart and the table all draw from that one answer.
  - When the pool holds more than 1,000, the tab says that search, filters and sort cover the first 1,000 of the total.
  - **In a program now** is the read's own `in_programme=yes` filter. It is a second read, made only while the filter is on. If that read fails, the filter shows nobody and says so, rather than guess.
- **Chips:** Active, Paused, Declined and Removed, each with the canvas's mark and the read's `totals`. Declined and Removed say "· not in the pool". The amber "applications waiting · Decide in Approvals" link stays.
- **Testers by profile:** active testers only, one bar per profile in the canvas's order, plus "Profile not recorded" when there is one. It has View as table and the canvas's foot.
- **Search, filters, sort and pages are drawn in the page,** because the read takes no search or sort.
  - **Search:** by name only, from two characters. The email is never searched.
  - **Filters:** Profile (and "Profile not recorded"), Availability given, and In a program now.
  - **Sort:** Tester since, newest (the default), oldest, or Name.
  - **Pages:** Rows per page 25, 50 or 100, with "1–25 of 57".
- **Columns:** Name, Profile, Tester since, Approved by (Decided by on a status other than Active), Availability, Programs (applied / picked, from D1200), Email consent, Status, and ⋯.
- **The ⋯ menu** (`ActionMenu.jsx`) offers what the status allows (`poolMenu`):
  - Active: Open, Pause…, Remove…;
  - Paused: Open, Resume…, Remove…;
  - Declined and Removed: Open only.

  Pause, Resume and Remove open D1042's own `TesterDialog` (now exported from `approvals/TesterBoard.jsx`). Its POST, note and reason rules are unchanged. After a change, the pool is read again and the dialog's line is shown.

### 2. The states (TST12–TST15)

- **TST12b, empty:** when the read's `totals.total` is 0, the tab is one card: "No testers yet." and "Open Approvals". A failed or unread total is never drawn as empty.
- **TST13, loading:** a "Reading…" card with skeleton lines. Overview's loading tiles are D1201's.
- **TST14, failed:** "We could not load the tester pool." and "The rest of Platform still works. This is not a claim that there are none.", with Try again. The partial tile (TST14a) and the Programs failed card (TST14b) are D1201's and D1202's.
- **TST15a, HQ only:** a studio admin on any Product testing address reads "HQ runs product testing. You can approve and manage testers on Accounts, and decide tester applications in Approvals." The card offers Go to Accounts and Go to Approvals. These are a path-keyed entry in `pages/hq/hqNoticeModel.js`, after Payments' precedent (D1012).
- **TST15b:** the existing AdminOnlyNotice, unchanged.

### 3. The phone (TST16)

- **One program's applicants are cards below `md`.** Each card shows the name, "Profile · applied date", the status pill and the Tester badge, with a 44px ⋯.
- **The ⋯ is TST16f's bottom sheet:** the eyebrow "⋯ · name · applied date", "Actions", one 48px row per step (Pick…, Decline…, Withdraw for the member…), and Cancel. Each step is D1203's dialog. The sheet offers only the steps the desktop row offers.
- **Answers open full screen** (TST16d): a Lightbox with back "‹ Applicants", the name, the pills, the screening questions, and the availability and email foot.
- **Review's Approve and Send back** are full width on a phone.
- **New program's bar is pinned** to the foot on a phone: Cancel first (flex 1, 44px), then Save draft (flex 1.4). `ProgrammeForm` takes the bar's classes from its page, so the partner's form is unchanged.
- The Tester pool's ⋯ uses the same sheet on a phone. Its fields and pager are 44px tall.

### 4. Deviations from the canvas

- **"Open account" is "Open tester record".** Accounts has no per-account address to open. The menu instead opens the tester's record in Approvals (`/admin/approvals/product-testers/:id`), where D1042 decides it.
- **Persona segment** is a dashed "Not read here yet" chip. The tester read does not carry persona segments, so the pool cannot be filtered by one (`WAITS.personaSegment`).
- **Search, filters and sort run in the page** over the whole pool read (section 1). The server's own filters are used only for In a program now, which the page cannot work out from the row.

### 5. A fix on the way

`testerRow` read `member_name` and `member_role`. The tester read has never sent those: it nests them as `member.name` and `member.role` (`services/productTesters.ts` `present()`). So on `main`, every pool row said "Name not recorded". The row now reads the nested member. The D1195 test's fixture was flat and has been changed to the read's own shape.
