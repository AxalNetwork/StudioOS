## D8249 — Pitch analytics: per-visit card reads on an investor link (#8249)

A follow-up of D6062 (#6062, PR #8151). D6062 left one departure from the canvas's PS-08 B journey: it did not say "Read 9 cards · longest on Traction" per visit, because the link read summed seconds over all visits. This decision closes that departure.

### What is built

- **Worker: `visit_reads` on the per-link read.** `GET /api/decks/:id/investor-links/:shareId/analytics` gains `visit_reads` and `visit_reads_limit`. `visit_reads` lists the link's visits newest first, at most 50 (`VISIT_READS_LIMIT`). Each is `{ view_id, at, cards_read, seconds, longest, cards }`, where `cards` lists only the cards a heartbeat counted on that visit, in the link's order, each `{ card_uid, index, title, seconds }`. The pure read is `services/decks/visitReads.ts`. The route reads it in its own helper, `linkVisitReads`, inside `linkSlideAnalytics`, and changes no other read. The daily read (`/analytics/daily`) is untouched.
- **No migration.** D663's `deck_view_slides` already keeps one running total per view and slide place, named by card uid since D1500 (migration 714). That is seconds per card per visit, so the store answers the question as it is. A row written before 714 is named by place, and is read against the cards as the link sends them, as the link's summed read does (`secondsByCard`).
- **Bounded on its own.** The summed read takes the link's heartbeat rows oldest first, up to 20,000. The per-visit read instead takes the 50 newest views (`ORDER BY created_at DESC, id DESC LIMIT 50`), then only their rows. A link with many visits still answers its latest ones.
- **Missing is null, never 0.** A visit no heartbeat timed answers `cards_read`, `seconds`, `longest` and `cards` all null. That covers a reader who closed the tab at once, a visit made while Time per slide was off, and a heartbeat that counted 0 s. With Time per slide off now, `visit_reads` is null, as `cards` is. A link nobody opened answers `[]`.
- **Frontend: one line per visit on the journey.** In `ViewerAnalyticsSheet.jsx`, each Opened and Revisited step carries "Read 3 cards · longest on Traction · 40 s" (`slideAnalyticsModel.visitReadOf`). The line is drawn only from measured seconds: the count is of cards with counted seconds, and the longest card's own seconds follow it. A visit with no timed card reads "Not recorded · No card was timed on this visit." Steps that are not visits (the NDA, the ask, created, withdrawn) carry no line.
- **D6062's states are unchanged.** With tracking off, no visit line is drawn. The tiles and Time per card keep Tracking off, and the visits stay on the journey. A failed read stays Unavailable with Try again, and no journey is drawn. A visit older than the 50 the read covers has no line, not a made-up Not recorded. When the link has more visits than that, a note under the journey says so.

### Departures

- "longest on X" names the card and adds its counted seconds. The canvas names only the card.
- A tie for the longest card goes to the first card in the link's order, as the link's most-read card does (D1573).

### Rollback

Revert the PR. The read only adds two fields and writes nothing, and there is no migration. A sheet without `visit_reads` draws no visit line (`visitReadOf` answers null).

### Tests

- `cloudflare-worker/test/deck_visit_reads_d8249.test.ts` (5 tests): a link with several visits (each visit's own cards and longest, newest first, summing to the link's cards, and one link's visits never in another's); a visit with no heartbeat and a 0 s heartbeat (null); tracking off (null), including off and back on; the 50-visit bound; and the pure read (rows by place, dropped uids, ties).
- `frontend/test/pitch_visit_reads_8249.test.mjs` (6 tests): the same three cases on the model and on the mounted sheet, plus the bound's note.
- Mutation check: 10 of 10 frontend mutations caught and 8 of 9 Worker mutations caught. The one that lived removed a `> 0` guard that `secondsByCard` already applies, so the guard was dropped from the code.
