## D569 — Partner earnings: four partner screens and an admin commissions console, built on routes that had no screen

**The Ops canvas's Partner earnings area (`design/incoming/Ops Page.dc.html`,
blocks `is.pe_earnings`, `is.pe_referrals`, `is.pe_log`, `is.pe_board` and
`is.pea_commissions`, with `ops-data.js`) is built, to the app's own styling,
from client methods `api.js` already had and no screen called (#1165).** Slot
S01, issue #1174, 2026-10-06. No client method was added and no Worker code
changed.

**What was true on main.** The Worker has served commissions, payouts,
referral codes, sign-ups, intro credits, a relationship's events and
interactions, a leaderboard, the commission rules, the payout queue and the
take rate for a long time. Fourteen of the methods these screens call had no
caller: #1165's list for this area, less `logActivity`, which they do not use.
A fifteenth, `payoutsMe`, had one caller, which reads the wrong shape (see
"Found on the way").

### The screens, and what each reads

| Route | Canvas | Reads | Writes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/partner/earnings` | `pe-earnings` | `commissionsMe` (`GET /api/network/commissions/me`), `payoutsMe` (`GET /api/network/payouts/me`) | `payoutRequest` (`POST /api/network/payout/request`), after a confirmation |
| `/partner/referrals` | `pe-referrals` | `referralCode`, `referralList` (`/api/network/referral/*`), `introCredits` (`GET /api/introductions/credits`), `commissionsMe` for each sign-up's commission | none (copy and share are the browser's) |
| `/partner/relationships/:id` | `pe-log` | `partnerRelationships` for the header, `relationshipEvents`, `partnerInteractionLog` (`/api/partnernet/relationships/:id/*`) | `partnerInteractionAdd` (`POST …/interactions`) |
| `/partner/leaderboard` | `pe-board` | `partnerLeaderboard` (`/api/partnernet/leaderboard`, falling back to `/leaderboard/public`) | none |
| `/admin/commissions` | `pea-commissions` | `adminCommissionRules`, `adminCommissions`, `adminPayouts` (`/api/network/admin/*`), `hqTakeRate` (`GET /api/admin/platform/take-rate`) | `adminProcessPayout` (`PATCH …/payouts/:id/process`) and `setHqTakeRate` (`PUT …/take-rate`), each after a confirmation |

Every read has its own loading, empty, "Not recorded" and "Unreadable" states,
and one failing read never blanks another. A failed read says what it is not
evidence of ("This is not a claim that you have none"). A write shows the
route's own refusal sentence, or what it did.

**Where they mount.** No partner bucket holds them: `shellConfig.js` has no
`/partner` prefix (D4 keeps persona roots out of the URL, not the namespace),
and adding zones to Pipeline or to the shared Network bucket would have put
earnings pills on four licences' shells. So they sit at the canvas's own
routes, in `App.jsx` beside `/partner/insights` and `/admin/refer-earn` (D528:
an area that already lives in `App.jsx` adds its routes there). Earnings,
Referrals and Leaderboard draw one `ZoneNav` row between them
(`EARNINGS_AREA`); the log has a back link to Network · Relationships.

**Guards.** The partner routes are `guard(['admin', 'partner'])`: every route
they call is `requireAuth` scoped to the caller, or a party-or-admin check, so
the persona's guard is the narrower one. They are not wrapped in
`partnerPrivateWorkspace`, for the reason `/referrals` gives: an admin reading
them reads their own rows.

`/admin/commissions` is `guard(['admin'], hqOnly(…))`, the Super Admin's. It
lists every partner's commissions and payouts and processes payouts, so the
narrowest role that needs it is HQ's. Its routes are still `requireAdmin` on
either tier, so another admin could see a colleague's earnings and settle
their payouts. #1183 asks for `requireSuperAdmin` on those routes. This
followed Codex's review of #1191; the console was first built for any admin,
to match its routes. The take-rate card's route is the one that checks the
elevation itself. The card asks only when `/me` carries it, and otherwise
says "Super admin only".

**Doors.** `/admin/commissions` is linked from the referral review console
(`ReferralReview.jsx`, Approvals lane 2), so `admin_route_reachability` walks
to it. Each accepted relationship on the partner's Network · Relationships
zone (`RelationshipRequests`, given `logPath` by `NetworkWorkspace` on the
partner shell only) links to its log. The three earnings screens have no
sidebar row yet: #1164 owns `sidebarConfig.js`.

### What the routes force on the screens

- **Totals are per currency.** `commissions.currency` is a column, and a
  purchase commission carries the payment's currency. The route's
  `balance_cents` and `lifetime_cents` add cents across currencies, so the page
  sums the rows itself, per status and per currency, and prints every amount
  with its code.
- **The payout form offers only what the route accepts.** The request claims
  whole accrued commissions, oldest first, and refuses an amount that is not
  the sum of the first N of them, or is under 1000 cents. So the amount is a
  select of those sums (`payoutOptions`), the whole balance by default, and it
  stops where the currency changes. The method is the route's three (`stripe`,
  `wire`, `crypto`), with none preselected. An optional note goes in
  `payout_details`.
- **"Paid" means claimed by a request.** The route marks commissions `paid`,
  with `paid_at`, the moment a payout is requested. The page says so, and the
  payout's own status says whether it settled.
- **"Process payout" moves no money.** `action: 'complete'` sets the status and
  records the admin. The confirmation says to pay it by the partner's method
  first. Actions are offered by status, since the route checks none: requested
  → Process payout, Mark processing, Mark failed; processing → Process payout,
  Mark failed; completed → Mark failed (a bank return); failed → nothing. Mark
  failed needs a reason, which the partner reads.
- **A voided touch stays in the log, marked.** The interactions route returns
  a touch its recorder voided, with `voided_at`. The timeline strikes it
  through, labels it Voided with the day, and leaves it out of the entry count
  and the rail's count. The relationship's last interaction already skips it,
  since the route computes that with `voided_at IS NULL`. The first build drew
  it as live; Codex's review of #1191 caught it.
- **The log writes to the interaction log, not `logActivity`.**
  `POST /partnernet/activity/log` takes a closed list of product actions and
  writes nothing about a relationship (and D553 found it records nothing).
  `partnerInteractionAdd` is the route that logs a touch, as the investor book
  uses it. Type, date, optional time and a note; a date alone is noon UTC.
- **A rank is read, not made.** `rankBoard` ranks the returned rows by
  `network_score`, equal scores sharing a rank. A viewer outside the returned
  rows is "Not ranked". Nobody but the viewer is named, even when the admin
  arm of the route returns names, emails and earnings.

### Departures from the canvas

- Routes: the canvas's own. No existing page held these screens. `/referrals`
  is Refer & Earn, a different programme with its own store, and is unchanged.
- Earnings: the "Requested" total is the sum of payouts still requested or
  processing, because no commission status is "requested". The method select
  offers the route's Stripe, Wire transfer and Crypto, not Bank transfer and
  PayPal. The amount is a select, not a free figure. The confirmation shows the
  partner's optional note, not "IBAN ending 1234", because no destination is
  stored.
- Referrals: the credits are monthly ("of 3 from this month's allowance",
  "renews on Nov 1"), not quarterly. The statuses are the route's "Signed up"
  and "Converted", not Active and Churned. A KYC column is added because the
  route returns it.
- Relationship log: no Outcome field. Commission and introduction events are
  not on the timeline: nothing links them to a relationship.
- Leaderboard: one measure, network score. The Introductions, Referrals and
  Commissions chips and the period are drawn disabled with the reason. Other
  rows show rank and role, not "[Partner]", because the board holds every
  member with a score.
- Admin: "New rule", "Edit" and the rule toggles are not working controls
  (New rule is drawn disabled, the toggles read Active or Off). "Retry" on a
  failed payout is not offered. The take rate is described as what it is, the
  platform's take on advisory sessions, not "applied to every commission".
- Rails: Worker AI rails on four screens, none on the leaderboard
  (`rail: false`). The canvas's proposals (statement, share text, next step,
  queue note) are listed as unavailable: no draft surface reads these stores.

### Gaps — "Not recorded" on screen, with its route

1. **A payout's destination.** `POST /network/payout/request` stores a method
   and free `payout_details`, and no route stores where a partner is paid.
2. **A commission's payout** (closed by D572, below). No column links a commission to the payout that
   claimed it (`commissions`, `payouts`), so a payout cannot list its
   commissions.
3. **A failed payout's commissions** (closed by D572). `PATCH /network/admin/payouts/:id/process`
   with `fail` leaves them `paid`; its own comment says it resets them and the
   code does not. No route retries a failed payout.
4. **A failed payout's date** (closed by D572). `payouts` has `completed_at` only; a failure
   writes no date.
5. **A payout's currency** (closed by D572 for payouts requested since). The request route never writes `currency`, so every
   payout row carries the column default, USD, whatever its commissions were.
   So a payout's amount reads "currency not recorded" in the history and the
   queue, and the payouts in flight are counted, never added up. The first
   build printed the default as the payout's currency; Codex's review of #1191
   caught it. The test reads the route's INSERT, so storing a currency there
   fails it until the page shows it.
6. **Who processed a payout** (closed by D572). `GET /network/admin/payouts` returns
   `processed_by` as an id, not a name ("account #7").
7. **A sign-up's role** (closed by D572). `GET /network/referral/list` returns name, email,
   status and KYC, and no role.
8. **A relationship's company.** `partner_relationships` has no company, and no
   writer sets `metadata.organization_name`.
9. **An interaction's outcome** (closed by D572). `partner_interactions` has `kind`, `note` and
   `interacted_at`, and no outcome.
10. **Leaderboard by metric, by period, and change.** `/partnernet/leaderboard*`
    ranks by `network_score` only, has no period and keeps no history.
11. **Editing a commission rule** (editing closed by D572; creating is not).
    No route creates, edits or switches a rule
    (`/network/admin/commission-rules` is GET only).

### After D572: the screens read what it added (#1196)

D572 (#1183, part 1) added the fields behind gaps 2–7, 9 and 11. The screens
read them as follows.

- **Payout currency.** A payout requested since migration 393 has a `uid` and
  the currency it claimed, and its amount shows that currency in the history,
  the queue and the confirmations (`payoutCurrency`). A payout from before has
  no uid. Its row still holds the column default, so it still reads "currency
  not recorded".
- **Payouts in flight.** They are added up per currency. One with no recorded
  currency is counted and left out of the sums ("1 not added up").
- **The payout request.** It names its currency (`payoutRequestBody`), because
  the route refuses a balance held in two currencies without one. The form
  offers each currency's runs, oldest first within the currency, grouped by
  currency. The first currency's whole balance is the default. The old stop at
  the first change of currency is gone.
- **A failed payout.**
  - **Its day:** read from `failed_at`.
  - **The confirmation:** says how many commissions go back to Accrued, from
    `commission_ids`. For a payout from before 393, it says none do.
  - **After the write:** the note repeats the route's `commissions_reopened`,
    or `unlinked`.
  - **The partner's history:** says the same.
- **Completed is final.** "Mark failed" is offered on requested and processing
  only. The test reads each action's statuses off the route's own `UPDATE …
  WHERE status` clauses.
- **The processor.** It is a name, from `processed_by_name`, with the day from
  `processed_at`.
- **A sign-up's role.** It comes from `referred_role` (the account's role now),
  with a role filter.
- **An interaction's outcome.** The log form takes an optional outcome of at
  most `OUTCOME_MAX` (200) characters, the route's bound, and the timeline
  shows it. One logged before outcomes were kept has none, and none is
  inferred from the note.
- **Commission rules.**
  - **Editing:** each rule has Edit, opening an editor for a flat amount in
    USD (a rule stores no currency) or a share in percent, the description,
    and Active.
  - **Checks before sending:** the route's bounds (`RULE_LIMITS`, read back out
    of `network.ts`). A rule pays exactly one way, so the body sets one and
    clears the other. A figure must be above zero: a rule that should stop
    paying is switched off.
  - **The confirmation:** lists only what changes, from what to what.
  - **Each row:** says who last edited it and when.
  - **New rule** stays disabled, because no route creates one.

Still open: the payout destination (gap 1, owner question Q1 on #1183), a
relationship's company (gap 8, Q2) and the leaderboard by metric and period
(gap 10, D572's next PR).

paid" always read "Not recorded". The figures exist on `commissionsMe`. Left for
