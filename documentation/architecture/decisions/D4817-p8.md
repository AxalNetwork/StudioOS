## D4817 part 8 — PF-08 Admin Investor preview: one banner, "Private in preview" chips, no write (#4817)

Part of #5311 (PF-08): the Admin Investor preview board of the Portfolio v2 canvas (`design/incoming/Portfolio v2 Page.dc.html`, board PF-08 and the "Preview privacy" pattern on PF-11; `Portfolio v2 · Boards.dc.html` row PF-08), with the addendum's line for it (`Portfolio v2 · Addendum.dc.html`, D4817 "Frontend route and metadata handoff": "Route-aware title adds `Investor preview` before `Axal VC`; one shared banner and compact withheld sections, without private reads"). Frontend only. No route, Worker or schema change.

**What a preview is today.** An administrator who picks Investor in the shell's Preview shell menu browses the investor shell with their own session (`lib/previewShells.js`, D288: "data and permissions unchanged, every write still the viewer's own"). Before this part, each Portfolio address drew the real investor board for them: it read `/api/positions`, analytics, health, updates, support, reporting and chases as the administrator, so the page showed the administrator's book (or none) under an investor's heading, and the Reports chase and the Value-add Log support / Deliver / Withdraw were live and went out as the administrator. The only preview chrome was the shell's strip ("Previewing · Investor shell", "Every write still goes out as …", Back to HQ). No Portfolio page carried a preview banner of its own.

**What is built.**
- `frontend/src/pages/portfolio/previewModel.js`: who is previewing (`isInvestorPreview`: an administrator, not in a support session, browsing as `investor`), which board an address draws (`previewBoardOf`: `/portfolio`, `/portfolio/health`, `/portfolio/positions`, `/portfolio/updates`, `/portfolio/performance`, `/portfolio/value-add`), the banner's words, the way back's label (`previewExitLabel`, the strip's own: Back to HQ for the holder, Back to HQ’s own accounts otherwise), the title (`previewDocTitle`), and each board's withheld figures, sections and writes (`PREVIEW_BOARDS`, headed with the real boards' own section names).
- `PortfolioPreview.jsx`: `PortfolioWorkspace` draws it in place of the investor board for those six addresses when `isInvestorPreview` holds. It draws the shared frame (crumb, heading, the local navigation with no counts), then, per board: the performance summary with NAV and each multiple and flow as a "Private in preview" chip beside its definition (Overview and Performance); the period tiles as chips (Reports, Value-add); each section with its heading and a chip; and the board's writes (Reports: Chase, Chase all overdue; Value-add: Log support, Deliver, Withdraw) drawn **disabled**, each `aria-describedby` the reason beside them. It reads nothing and draws no rail (Eadwyn would read the same records). The title is `<tab> · Portfolio · Investor preview · Axal VC`.
- `PreviewBanner.jsx`: the one banner, "Previewing Investor · private investor data withheld", a second line saying it is read-only and why (the administrator's own session, so no investor's positions, reports or support are read and nothing can be changed), and the way back; and `PrivateChip`, the compact lock-and-words chip. `preview.css` holds their look on the frame's tokens; the banner is the canvas's dark band in both modes.
- `PortfolioFrame.jsx` gains one optional prop, `banner`, drawn at the top of the named section, above the crumb. No existing prop is renamed or reordered.
- `App.jsx`, two additive edits: the shell's `ViewModeContext` value also carries `superAdmin` and `exitPreview` (the strip's own choice of home: `onViewModeChange('admin', { hq: superAdmin })`), so the banner's way back is the strip's; and `PortalSwitcher` stands its strip down where the page draws its own banner (`pageOwnsPreviewBanner`: Investor selected, one of the six addresses). App.jsx's imports are frozen (D5615, `hub_freeze_5615`), so App.jsx holds its own copy of that rule (`PREVIEW_BANNER_BOARDS`) rather than importing `previewModel.js`; the PF-08 test holds the two to the same answers. Everywhere else the strip is unchanged.

**One banner, not two.** On those six addresses the viewer had the shell strip, which says writes still go out, and the canvas asks for a page banner that says the page is read-only. Drawing both would be two banners that disagree. The page banner carries the same way back, so the strip stands down there and only there.

**Departures from the canvas, and why.**
- **Every board, not only the Overview.** PF-08 draws the Overview; the five other boards follow the same rule with their own section headings, since the preview reaches all of them from the local navigation.
- **The banner sits in the frame, above the crumb**, not above the shell's top bar: the shell's chrome is not Portfolio's to redraw.
- **A second line in the banner.** The canvas banner is one line; the task asks the banner to say it is read-only, so a short line under it does.
- **Writes are shown off, with their reason**, rather than only the canvas's footnote "Writes are disabled in preview", so the administrator sees what the investor can do.
- **No rail in preview.** The real boards' rails are Eadwyn reading the same records.
- **"Who is being previewed" is the Investor shell**, not a person: a shell preview has no investor identity. Acting as a person is View As (a support session), which is not a preview here: the session is the member's own and their Portfolio draws as theirs, under the impersonation bar.

**Not covered.** The company workspace (`/portfolio/companies/:uid`, `…/reports`, `…/terms`) is PF-07's, built in parallel, and is untouched: in preview it keeps today's behaviour and the shell strip (no page banner there, so the strip still draws; `pageOwnsPreviewBanner` answers false). `/portfolio/waterfall` is likewise unchanged. Both need the same treatment once PF-07 lands.

**Tests.** `frontend/test/portfolio_preview_d4817.test.mjs` mounts the whole App against a stubbed `fetch` as an administrator previewing Investor on every Portfolio address: exactly one banner, which says who, read-only and the way back, and the strip only off Portfolio; a chip on every withheld figure and section, no `$0` and no empty book, and no investor read; no enabled button but the way back, every write disabled with its reason; the way back returns to `/hq` with `viewMode` `admin` and the banner gone; a real investor and a support session get their own board and no banner; and the model. Eleven mutations are listed in its header, each failing and then passing again when reverted.
