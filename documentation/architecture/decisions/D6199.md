## D6199 — The investor's closing room: deadline, what is left, and after Closed a summary with a handoff to Portfolio (#6199)

- **Status:** built by S17, 2026-11-14 (#6199, under #4815).
- **Builds on:** D1215 (the closings store), D1216 (staged signing), D1218 (the room), D1058 (reporting duties), D1056 (the Portfolio company page).
- **Migration:** none.
- **Worker:** `GET /api/closings/:uid` returns `for_investor` when the caller reads the closing through a line of their own (`reach: 'own'`); it is `null` for an Admin and the founder. Built in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/closingInvestorRoom.ts`, called from `readClosing`.
- **Frontend:** `frontend/src/pages/closing/RecordRoom.jsx` (the investor's line, the closed summary, the reporting card), `roomModel.js` (`investorLeft`, `investorSummary`, `investorDuties`, `portfolioCompanyHref`, `ROOM_WAITS`), `frontend/src/lib/api/closings.js` (`signedCopyBlob`).

### 1. What was already there

The investor's read already carried `deadline` (D1215 returns it for every reach), and `roomTiles` already turned it into a day and days left. The investor's room did not draw it. That part is frontend only.

### 2. `for_investor`, scoped to the viewing investor

Every query is narrowed to the caller again, not only by the route's reach:

| Field | What it holds | Narrowed by |
|---|---|---|
| `project_uid` | The company's uid, for its Portfolio address | the closing's project, not deleted |
| `conditions.waiting_on_you` | `{line_uid, step}`: `sign` when the caller's own stage is sent to them (not void, not declined); `send_payment` when their line is open and its paper is fully signed (or it has no paper) | the caller's own lines; the stage's `role = 'investor'` and `user_id` = the caller |
| `conditions.rest` | Counts only: `your_lines` (open steps on the caller's lines that wait on someone else or on an earlier step), `other_lines` (other lines not received or withdrawn), `round` (the founder's cap-table decision when proposed, and Mark Closed) | a `COUNT(*)`; no name, amount, route or uid of another line |
| `conditions.rest_count` | Their sum; 0 once the closing is not open | |
| `summary[]` | Per own line not withdrawn: amount, currency, `signed_at` (the last stage's day once every stage is signed), `received_at` (the first receiving-side evidence not withdrawn), `signed_copy.envelope_id` | the caller's own lines; the copy is the caller's own stage envelope, `completed` with a stored signed file |
| `reporting` | `not_closed`; `ok` with the duties; or `unreadable` when that read fails (the rest of the read still answers) | `closing_id`, `holder_user_id` = the caller, and `closing_line_id` in the caller's own line ids |

A line's five steps are counted as: route, paper, signing, payment, receipt. A prospect line has no paper here, so it has three.

**Not counted for an investor.** The deal's closing checklist and IC conditions are the studio team's and counsel's. No row, label or count of either reaches the investor; the source test fails if the service reads either table.

### 3. The room

- **Open:** under the line, "Closing deadline" shows the day and "in N days" or "N days past", or Not recorded with the reason in words.
- **What is left** lists the steps that wait on the investor, each with its action. Sign opens the signing order on that line. Send opens the payment instructions.
  - A step tied to a line that is not among the viewer's rows is dropped. The room never draws a line that is not theirs.
  - Below the list, the count of the rest and its parts are shown.
  - When the read has no part, it says Not recorded, never "Nothing waits on you".
- **After Closed:** "Your closing in sum" shows the amount, the signed day, the received day and Download your signed copy.
  - Each value shows Not recorded with its reason when it is absent.
  - The download goes through `GET /api/legal/esign/:id/document`, which is already scoped to the caller's own envelopes. If it fails, the room says so.
  - The card ends with **Open [company] in Portfolio**, linking to `/portfolio/companies/:uid` (D1056, on `main`). When the read names no company, the link is left out and the card says why.
- **Reporting owed to you** lists each duty on the investor's line. It shows the cadence, the due rule, the clause, and whether the term comes from the signed paper or the template. It also marks a term the paper left blank as the default.
  - It says so when no duty is recorded, and Unreadable when the duties read failed.
  - It replaces D1218's "Reporting · Not read here yet" card.
- **Phone:** the same blocks, in one column, with actions at full width.
