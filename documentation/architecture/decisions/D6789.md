### The rules

1. **The mark is who and when.** `PUT /checklist/:key` with `{ not_applicable: true }` records the time and the user. The row reads "Not applicable · by {name} · {date}". `{ not_applicable: false }` clears both, and the item is open again. Anything but a boolean is a 400. The write is logged as `grow_checklist_not_applicable` or `grow_checklist_applies`.
2. **A Not applicable item is neither done nor open.** It is left out of the score. The page's header reads "{done} of {items that apply} done · {n} not applicable". The Company card's series carries `done`, `total` (the items that apply), `not_applicable` and `pct`, so the card's "Checklist · {done} of {total}" is the same count as the page's. When every item is Not applicable, `pct` is null, never a division by zero. The card's next step skips an item that does not apply.
3. **A Lab tick says the item applies.** An item the Spin-Out Lab ticked cannot be marked Not applicable: the route answers 409, and the page offers no button. If the Lab ticks an item after it was marked, the Lab's tick wins and the item counts as done.
4. **A hand tick and the mark exclude each other.** Marking an item Not applicable clears a hand tick. Ticking it by hand clears the mark. An item that Mercury ticks can still be marked Not applicable. A Mercury item marked Not applicable stays listed even for a founder without Mercury, so the mark can be cleared.
