### 1. Review (TST2)

- **Waiting for HQ** shows each partner programme sent for review as a full card:
  - the logo square (D1199's image, else the product's first letter);
  - who sent it and when;
  - who can apply;
  - the summary, what testers do, the time needed, the screening questions, the places and both date spans;
  - **Approve…**, **Send back…** and the ⋯ menu.
  A field nobody filled says "Not recorded", never a blank or a zero.
- **The earlier programmes line** reads "Partner firm’s earlier programs: 2 closed, 1 open." (`earlierLine`).
  - It comes from the programme list itself. HQ's read carries every firm's programmes, so this is a count, not a guess, and part 2's read is not needed for it.
  - A firm with none reads "none yet".
- **The last send-back** is read from the programme's own history (D1197's `GET …/programmes/:uid/events`), one read per waiting card. It reads "Last sent back: 18 Oct 2026." or "never".
  - A history that cannot be read says so ("Its history could not be read, …").
  - It never says "never" on a failed read (`sentBackLine`).
- **Approved, not yet open** carries **Open…**.
- **Sent back** quotes HQ's note.
- Each of the three lists says when it is empty.

### 2. Programs (TST4, TST4m)

- **One table** of every programme. Its columns are:
  - the logo;
  - the product, which links to the program, with its owner;
  - status;
  - places: "n of N" with a bar once it has opened, "N places" before;
  - applicants: "Not open yet" before it opens;
  - the applications and testing spans;
  - who can apply;
  - the last change;
  - the ⋯ menu.
- **Over it:**
  - the status chips, in the canvas's order, each with its count;
  - search by product or partner firm;
  - the filters: owner (Axal VC, partner firms, or one firm by name), who can apply, places (full, not full), and the closing window (next 7 days, next 30 days, past);
  - the sort.
  - A programme with no closing date is in no date window.
- **The pager** shows 25 a page, with "1–25 of 40" (`PAGE_SIZE`, `programsView`). Filters that match nothing say so; an empty list says how to start one.
- **The ⋯ menu** (`menuFor`) holds only the steps the Worker allows. The rest are left out, never drawn disabled:

| Programme | ⋯ |
|---|---|
| HQ's draft | Edit…, Open…, View history |
| A partner's draft | View history (the firm edits it) |
| Waiting for HQ (a partner's) | Approve…, Send back…, View history |
| Approved (a partner's) | Open…, View history |
| Open | Open program, Close…, Move to testing…, View history |
| Closed | Open program, Move to testing…, View history |
| Testing | Open program, Close…, View history |
| Sent back | View history |

  Move to testing is open or closed only (D1198 `not_testable`). Close takes an open or testing programme. View history is the program's `?tab=activity`.

### 3. The dialogs (TST3)

Every step opens an in-page dialog (`TestingDialog`, a `Lightbox` that is a sheet on a phone). Nothing is sent until its button; Cancel sends nothing. No page here calls `window.confirm`, `alert` or `prompt`, and the tests fail if one is called.

Each dialog says what the step changes, who is told and what is recorded (`dialogFor`). It marks **New** what D1198 made new.

| | Step | Field | Request |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Approve | a note, optional | `approve(uid, note)` |
| B | Send back | what should change, required | `reject(uid, note)` |
| C | Open to applications | — | `open(uid)` |
| D | Close | — | `close(uid)` |
| E | Move to testing | — | `startTesting(uid)` |
| F | Pick an applicant | — | `pick(appUid)` |
| G | Decline an applicant | — | `decline(appUid)` |
| H | Withdraw an application (HQ) | a reason, 10 to 500 characters | `withdraw(appUid, reason)` |

- **Who is told.** On HQ's own programme, C and D say "No one else is told: it is Axal VC’s own program."
- **After the Worker answers yes,** the page says what changed and, from the answer's `told`, who was told, in **counts only** (`toldLine`): "1 at the firm told · 2 picked testers told · Axal VC Mail can reach 1."
  - A null count is left out, never shown as zero.
  - Nothing but those counts is drawn from `told`.
- **I is not its own dialog.** A refusal is drawn inside the dialog that asked, in the Worker's own sentence, and the dialog stays open; its code is never shown.
- **A full programme.** Pick knows the programme is full before asking, so dialog F shows the Worker's sentence for that case at once ("Every place is taken. Decline someone first.", `services/testing.ts`) and its button is off.
- **H is HQ's only.** `ProgrammeApplicants` draws Withdraw… when its `api` has `withdraw`. HQ's one-program page passes it; the partner's zone does not.

### 4. New program (TST7)

- **New program is the page header's action** on every tab of `/admin/platform/product-testing`. It links to `/programs?new=1`, which opens the form as a lightbox over Programs. Closing the lightbox drops the `?new=1`; Review never opens it.
- **The form takes the logo:** a PNG or an SVG of at most 256 KB.
  - The type and size are checked in the browser, in the Worker's own words (`logoProblem`).
  - Whether it is square, and safe, is the Worker's to decide (D1199).
- **The logo is uploaded once the programme is saved,** because D1199's route needs its uid. Edit can replace the logo or remove it.
- **A refused logo** leaves the programme saved, and the notice says why the logo was not saved.
