## D6092 — What changed on Grow Overview

GR-02 shows up to five dated changes from the last 30 days. The feed uses
project-scoped Grow activity and sent-update timestamps. It links to the
corresponding area and never exposes update subjects or recipients. Readers
get area-level descriptions without free-form log details. Writers can see
the recorded summary.

MRR and customer deltas use the same snapshot source in each period. NRR's
period comparison and the active roster's count of recent starts are
excluded because they do not measure the same 30-day change. No new store is
required. The activity log does not reconstruct events that were never
logged. An unreadable feed leaves the other Overview sections available.
On phones the feed follows Needs your attention.
