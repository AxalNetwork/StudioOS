### 1. One program (TST5, TST6)

`ProgramTab` in `frontend/src/pages/admin/productTesting/ProductTestingTabs.jsx`, with its derivations in `programModel.js` (pure).

- **Where it stands: the lifecycle stepper.**
  - **Steps:** Axal VC's own pass Draft, Open, Testing and Closed. A partner's add Waiting for HQ and Approved.
  - **Dates:** each step reached is dated from its event in the history (`created`, `submitted`, `approved`, `opened`, `testing_started`, `closed`). One whose event is not in the history says "Date not recorded".
  - **Testing, while next,** says the day its dates give.
  - **A program HQ sent back** stands at Waiting for HQ, "Sent back".
  - **An open program whose testing dates have begun** carries the canvas's foot: "It stays Open until someone moves it to Testing."
- **The history is read whole** for the stepper and Activity: pages of 100, newest first, at most 10 pages. When there is more, Activity says how many of how many were read.
- **The four stats:**
  - **Places filled:** picked of places, and how many are left. Places never recorded read "Not recorded".
  - **Applicants:** the applicants read's count, with the withdrawn counted apart from the applications read (`status=withdrawn`).
  - **Agreed to email:** of the applicants.
  - **Reachable by Mail:** from D1196's `mail_reach`, reachable of picked. A program the block does not list (not open) is "Not recorded", never 0.
  - A read that failed makes its stat "Unreadable", with a sentence.
- **Details:** summary, what testers do, time needed, who can apply, the screening questions, places, and the application and testing dates. A partner's program adds the Review card (sent, approved or sent back, opened, each with who and when, from the history).
- **Recent activity:** the history's newest five, then "See all in Activity".

### 2. The applicants (TST5a, TST6)

### 4. The Applicants tab (TST8)

`ApplicantsTab`, over D1197's applications read, 25 to a page:
- **Show:** Open programs (the default) or All programs.
- **Status chips:** All, Applied, Picked, Not this time, and "Withdrawn · not in All", each with its count.
- **Filters:** program, owner (Axal VC or partner firms), profile, agreed to email, and tester in the pool; search by name from two characters; sort by applied or name; a pager.
- **Each row:** name with the Tester badge, profile, the program (linking to its applicants), owner ("Axal VC" or the firm), applied, status, and agreed to email.
- **Actions:** Pick… and Decline… appear only on Axal VC's own programs. Withdraw for the member… is on any application that is applied or picked.

**Two Worker additions** (`services/testing.ts` `allApplications`, D1197's read):
- **`scope`:** `open` keeps programmes that are open or in testing; `all` (the default, so D1197's callers are unchanged) keeps every programme; anything else is 400.
- **`counts`:** one count per status under every other filter (status bound as `all`), so the chips add up to what each status lists, the withdrawn included. A measured 0 is a 0.

Both are fixed, bound SQL: one more `(? IS NULL OR …)` clause, and a `GROUP BY` over the same WHERE. The client passes `scope`.

### 5. The sub-tabs stay `?tab=`

`admin_route_reachability` must find a literal door to every routed path. The program's sub-tabs are built from its uid (`programHref`), so a path below `/programs/:uid` would have no literal door. `/programs/:uid?tab=applicants` and `?tab=activity` are kept, as D1195 drew them.
