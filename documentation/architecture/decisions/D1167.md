## D1167 — One storage allowance catalogue and public plans projection

#3080, S28. Part 1 of #2449. No migration, new route, binding or Stripe change.

### Catalogue and readers

`cloudflare-worker/src/services/storagePlans.ts` owns the fourteen storage plan
keys, their `allowance_bytes` and the owner's exact `best_suited` lines. Its unit
is binary GB. The price-to-plan mapping is the owner's answer on #2449;
allowances do not follow the current Stripe price or an AI allowance. Negotiated
Institutional and staff values are null with distinct reasons, never zero.
Market Intelligence adds no storage to a profile plan.

The existing D1050 `planCatalogue.ts` projects this table into `storage` and
`best_suited_for`, replacing the obsolete “not decided” placeholders. The public
`GET /api/public/plans-and-packs` keeps its prices, AI allowances, five-minute
cache and anonymous read, and adds `storage_plan_key`, `storage_bytes`,
`storage_reason`, `best_suited`, and `best_suited_reason` to each plan row.
Free Advisor and Partner rows and LP Access are present without Stripe
products. Investor Plus has its storage figure, no price/product, and D1050's
existing not-sold reason. `planOf` still does not map a Plus product to a
sellable plan; Checkout #2451 owns selling it. The whole catalogue continues to
include plans without products. No person's field is returned.

Checkout already consumes the nested catalogue storage field. Its existing
formatter now uses the same binary unit; otherwise a newly recorded allowance
would display a different GB figure. No payment or sheet interaction changes.

### Whose allowance

`storagePlanKey(env, user)` consumes an authenticated member, starts with
`aiLicenceKey`, and reads only that member's `account_subscriptions` and fund
admissions. Membership comes from the existing `LPs.listByUser`/`lpSelfScope`
reader, paired with `claimLpRowsByEmail` exactly as other LP self-views are;
no predicate or SQL-interpolation baseline is duplicated. A matching Advisor/Partner Pro record uses the existing access
status/grace rule (`inGrace`); an ended, mismatched or another member's
subscription grants nothing. A valid dated admission in the LP's own fund
grants LP Access, including an unclaimed LP matching the member's verified email.
A linked LP never matches another account's email, and an empty email never
matches. A legacy email match is permanently linked to the account by that
canonical claim; a later holder of the address cannot inherit it. If that
best-effort link cannot persist, storage returns Unreadable rather than granting
a standing email entitlement. Future
admissions are not yet effective. The scoped LP ids/fund ids reach the admission
query as one bound JSON value, with no new SQL interpolation.

The largest measured allowance wins; ties keep the profile plan. Nothing is
added. An Institutional plan stays negotiated rather than being replaced with
the smaller measured LP figure. Staff return `admin` without reading personal
entitlements. Null negotiated does **not** mean a measured unlimited allowance;
the later guard must distinguish it from staff. An invalid member or failed
entitlement read throws fixed `Storage plan unreadable` words, never raw data
or a guessed Free plan. The only possible write is the existing LP account-link
claim; resolution changes no plan, billing or file row and makes no external call.
