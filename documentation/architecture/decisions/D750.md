## D750 — The AI top-up says what is wrong, fetches a missing price once, and the rail shows money to the cent

Issue #1841, slot S07, the follow-up to D642 (#1435). On 2026-10-18 the AI rail's Usage card told members **"The AI top-up could not be read. Retry to read the purchase option."**, and Retry never cleared it. The same card printed **"This month $0.029536 of $50.00"**.

### Why it said "could not be read"

`readAiTopupProduct` (`cloudflare-worker/src/services/aiTopups.ts`) threw whenever `STRIPE_PRICE_AI_TOPUP` was set but unusable, and `GET /api/ai-budget/top-up` turned every throw into 503 `ai_topup_unreadable`. Four different configuration problems reached the member as the same line:

- the D1 mirror `stripe_products` lacked the price. `getCatalog` syncs from Stripe only when the table is empty, so a price created in the dashboard after the last **Sync from Stripe** stayed invisible;
- the price or its product was inactive, or the price was recurring, not USD, or had no whole positive amount;
- the variable did not look like `price_…`;
- the price came from the other Stripe mode than `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`.

None of these is fixed by retrying, so Retry repeated the same failure.

### Decisions

1. **A configuration problem is a state, not an error.** `readAiTopupProduct` returns:
   - `{ configured: false }` when the variable is unset;
   - `{ configured: true, state: 'ready', product }`;
   - `{ configured: true, state: 'not_ready', reason }`, with `reason` one of `missing_from_catalog`, `inactive`, `not_one_time`, `not_usd`, `bad_amount`, `bad_config`, `mode_mismatch`.

   It throws only when D1 or Stripe cannot be read, and only that is 503 `ai_topup_unreadable`.
2. **A price missing from the mirror is fetched once.** `fetchAndMirrorPrice(env, id)` (`services/catalog.ts`, beside `syncCatalog`) reads `GET /v1/prices/:id?expand[]=product` with the existing key and `stripeCall`. A price Stripe returns is written into `stripe_products` through the same `upsertCatalogRow` and `normalisePrice` that `syncCatalog` uses, keeping the product's other mirrored prices, and is then judged ready or not. A 404 is `missing_from_catalog`; a 404 naming "a similar object exists in test/live mode", or a price whose `livemode` disagrees with the key, is `mode_mismatch` and is never mirrored. Each price ID is asked of Stripe at most once per 10 minutes per isolate (`MIRROR_FETCH_WINDOW_MS`), so a bad ID cannot hammer Stripe. Nothing in Stripe is created or changed.
3. **The member sees the state, never the reason.** `GET /api/ai-budget/top-up` answers `{configured:false}`, `{configured:true,state:'ready',product}` or `{configured:true,state:'not_ready'}`. The rail (`WorkerRail.jsx`), the compact card (`AiBudgetUsage.jsx`), `/account/usage`, the phone pricing screen and checkout draw:
   - `ready`: **Add tokens**;
   - `not_ready` or unset: **"Top-up isn't available yet."** with no button and no Retry (the account pages keep their existing "Top-ups are not available yet." line);
   - 503: the existing Unreadable line with Retry.

   `aiTopupState` in `AiBudgetUsage.jsx` is the one reader the rail and the card share; `usageModel.topUpState` maps the same shape for the account pages.

   **Amended by D755 (#1870):** one sentence everywhere, "Top-up isn't available yet." (D755 §10), and `GET /api/ai-budget/top-up` adds the packs on sale, `on_hold` and the latest purchase beside `configured` and `product`, which stay deprecated until the D755 screens ship. The new `state` (`unset`, `not_ready`, `ready`) is the packs' state.

4. **HQ sees why.** `GET /api/ai-budget/top-up/status` (Super Admin only) returns `{ state, reason, price_id_suffix, mode }`: `state` is `unset`, `ready` or `not_ready`; `price_id_suffix` is the last six characters of a well-formed price ID and `null` otherwise, since a malformed value may be a key pasted into the wrong secret; `mode` is `stripeMode()`. No key and no full ID is ever returned. The AI allowances screen (`/admin/platform/allowances`, D642) draws it as one plain sentence per reason, for example "STRIPE_PRICE_AI_TOPUP names a recurring price. The top-up needs a one-time USD price."

   **Amended by D755 (#1870):** `GET /api/ai-budget/top-up/status` adds `packs`, one line per token pack with its state and reason (D750's reasons, plus `off_sale`, `price_mismatch` and `product_mismatch`), the last six characters of its price ID and, for the fallback, `adoptable`. The single line above stays until HQ draws `packs`.

5. **Spend is shown to the cent on the rail and the compact card.** `usageMoney(micro)` rounds half up to the cent and reads "<$0.01" for a spend above zero under half a cent; the exact figure is the element's `title`. It is used for today's and this month's spend and for what runs hold. Caps and the top-up balance keep their exact formatter, and `/account/usage` keeps its exact figures in the detail table.
6. **No new payment path.** The top-up is still a PaymentIntent from the existing checkout, credited only by the verified `payment_intent.succeeded` webhook (D629, D707). No Checkout Session, no card data, nothing written to Stripe.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/ai_topup_ready_d750.test.ts` covers each reason, the one fetch across two reads and its mirror row, a 404 and the window, both mode mismatches, 503 on Stripe and on D1 failure, the member shape, `/top-up/status` refused for members and plain admins and never carrying the full ID or `sk_`/`rk_`, and one order through `POST /api/orders/intent` credited once by two deliveries of the signed webhook with Stripe stubbed. `frontend/test/ai_topup_ready_d750.test.mjs` covers the rail and the card in each case and `usageMoney`.
