## D536 — The Research overview: one read of the totals, demand against supply, the competition and where the money is

**`GET /api/research/overview` gathers, in one signed-in read, everything the
`/research` overview shows. Each section reports its own failure, an empty
store reads "not recorded" with the reason rather than a zero, and no stub row
from market intelligence is read.** Issue #1082, slot S09 (moved from S05),
part of the Research rebuild (#1077). This PR is the route, its tests, the
client module and the founder desk's two count fixes. The shared
`ResearchOverviewPanel` waits for the owner's approval of the design canvas
(`needs-design`). No migration.

**What was true.** `/research` was a different page per role and none of them
showed a directory total, the market split or a chart. The founder desk's
"Company profiles" card counted the platform's own member companies
(`/api/companies?limit=12`) and said they came from "the company directory",
and its fund card printed "0 researched" when the count was missing (`?? 0`).

**The route** (`cloudflare-worker/src/routes/research_overview.ts`, mounted
before `/api/research` in `index.ts`). Each section answers
`status: 'ok' | 'not_recorded' | 'unreadable'`, with `reason` when not ok:
- **totals:** companies, markets and funds, each with its source and as-of
  date, plus the distinct countries and the distinct raw sector labels in the
  company directory. A total whose store cannot be read says "Unreadable" on
  its own line while the other totals still count. The private, public and
  government split is not recorded anywhere yet, so it says so and names
  #1083.
- **heat:** the 12-by-6 grid from `market_intel_indexes`, live rows only
  (`liveIndexSql`, D538), the current month, marked month to date, with each
  cell's change on last month. A cell with no live row is null with "No live
  source yet for this sector". When the month has no live index row but live
  observations exist inside the scoring window (the hours before the first
  day's 04:00 recompute, or after a missed cron), the read computes the index
  and reads it again, as `/market-intel/sector-compass` does; if that fails
  or writes nothing, heat and demand_vs_supply say "Live observations are
  recorded, but this month's index has not been computed yet", never "No
  live source yet".
- **demand_vs_supply:** the sectors where both demand and supply are recorded
  live this month with a change on last month, and demand's change is the
  larger, ranked by the gap. A sector missing either is left out rather than
  ranked on a guess.
- **competition:** companies per raw sector label and per country, the top
  `top` of each. Raw labels until #1080's crosswalk merges; the response says
  so.
- **money:** funds per HQ and per focus, size bands (empty bands left out),
  and the vintages of the last five years. The directory records no currency,
  so the bands carry none, and the response says sizes are reported sizes
  only.
- **signals:** the latest active signals, labelled as the hand-written theses
  they are. `signals.advisor_note` is never selected (voice rule).

`top` (default 10) is clamped to 1 to 25 and bound; the size-band edges
interpolated into one query are constants of the file. `liveIndexSql()` is a
literal predicate, recorded in `scripts/sql-prepare-baseline.json` like
D538's.

**The founder desk** (`FounderResearchDesk.jsx`, item 3 of the issue):
- the fund card's count goes through `fundCountMeta`: a missing count reads
  "Not recorded"; a recorded zero still reads "0 researched", because an empty
  store is a fact about the store;
- "Company profiles" keeps its count, "N returned", zero included
  (`research_zone_states.test.mjs` pins that), and its text now says these are
  the platform's companies, not the research directory, and points at
  Companies for the directory.

**The client module** `frontend/src/lib/api/researchOverview.js`
(`researchOverviewApi.get(top)`), re-exported by one line in `api.js` (D527).
Nothing calls it until the panel lands.

**Guards.** `cloudflare-worker/test/research_overview_d536.test.ts`, 16 tests
on real SQLite built from the migrations' CREATE statements: the sign-in gate;
the totals with their as-of dates; every section "not recorded" with a reason
on empty stores; competition and money groupings; `top` clamped and bound;
signals active only and no advisor note; heat reading live rows only; a
pending index computed on the read, or reported as pending when that fails or
writes nothing, while stub or out-of-window rows compute nothing;
demand against supply; the ranking helper; each section's failure isolated
(dropping a table breaks only the sections that read it); a total's failure
isolated within totals. `frontend/test/research_overview_d536.test.mjs`, 3
tests: the module and its re-export and mount order, the fund count, and the
companies card.

### Follow-up — the overview additions and `ResearchOverviewPanel`

The owner approved `design/incoming/Research · Main.dc.html` (D546), because
A7 of the Founder Workspaces canvas draws none of this issue's zones. S1 asked
for the additions below in a follow-up PR stacked on this one, not in it. The

every section still answers on its own.

**The route.**
- **totals:** `supplied_companies` is `SUM(research_market_directory.company_count)`,
  labelled as supplied by Axal and not verified; `fund_sources` is the number
  of fund lists in `research_fund_directory`, with each list's name and fund
  count; `sectors_tracked` is the twelve market-intelligence sectors, each
  with its last `computed_at` over live index rows (a sector with none says
  "No live source yet for this sector", an unreadable store says "Unreadable"
  on every sector). `sectors`, the raw-label count, stays for its readers; the
  overview shows `sectors_tracked`.
- **market_split:** `private` is the company directory, with the note that
  listing status is not recorded per company; `public` is `signal_companies`;
  `government` is `value: null` with "No source lists government-owned
  companies yet", because no store holds ownership type. #1083 Q3 decides only
  the wording. Each is its own total, so an unreadable `signal_companies`
  leaves the private count standing.
- **competition:** `other_sectors` and `other_countries` (the labels or
  countries past the top N and the companies under them), `no_sector`,
  `no_country` and `total_companies`. Every company is in exactly one of a top
  row, the remainder or the missing count; the test adds them up.
- **money:** HQ and focus go through the D533 crosswalk
  (`services/researchTaxonomy.ts`, migration 376, on `main` since #1093):
  `by_hq` is countries (`hq` is the country's name, `code` its ISO code) and
  `by_focus` is markets (`focus` the market's name, `slug`). A fund naming two
  countries or two markets counts under each; `other_hq` / `other_focus` and
  `hq_unmapped` / `focus_unmapped` count each fund once, so raw text the
  crosswalk cannot place is counted apart, never dropped or shown as a bar.
  Sizes are banded only where the currency is known — the `EUR_SOURCE` rule
  of `services/researchFundDirectory.ts` (D535) — and the bands now say €;
  the other list's sizes are never banded or summed with them. `vintages` is
  every recorded year with `vintage_range`; `recent_vintages` stays.
  `missing` counts the funds with no HQ, no focus, no size in a known
  currency, and no vintage. An unreadable crosswalk table makes `money`
  unreadable, and only it.
- **signals:** each item adds `why_now`, `build_opportunity` (its `headline`,
  `wedge`, `icp`, `gtm`, `moat` and `risks` text, nothing else from the JSON),
  `region` and `confidence_score`, and the section adds `active_count`. The
  stored defaults are not values: an empty `why_now`, a `{}` build opportunity
  and a `confidence_score` of 0 (what the ingest stores when no evidence was
  scored) read as null with "Not recorded". `advisor_note` is still never
  selected; a test reads the query.
- One more `${liveIndexSql()}` in a prepared statement; its baseline count in
  `scripts/sql-prepare-baseline.json` moves from 1 to 2. Every other value is
  bound, `EUR_SOURCE` included.

### The Public count is the Public tab's, through one read (#1077, 2026-10-07)

**What was true.** The overview's Public count read `signal_companies` alone.
`/research/markets`' Public tab lists those rows and SEC EDGAR's
(`research_public_companies`, migration 404, D595). Both printed 21 only
because EDGAR's table is still empty. Once migration 412 lands its rows, the
two pages would print different totals for the same thing. S09's audit of
#1077 found it (gap 2), and S1 confirmed option A.

**Now.**
- **One statement:** `services/listedCompanies.ts` `readListedTotals`.
  - It counts both stores and dates each one.
  - It counts how many EDGAR tickers are also a signal company's symbol.
  - Its `total` counts one row per source, as the Public tab lists them.
- **The overview** prints that `total`, dated by the newer store. Its note gives
  the split, and the overlap when there is one: "21 signal companies with
  their bands, and N from SEC EDGAR with ticker and exchange only". The card's
  source names both stores, and no longer claims every row has bands.
- **`GET /api/research/listed-companies`** reads its store figures from the
  same statement. EDGAR's `store_total` and overlap come from it, and so do the
  new top-level `store_total` and the signal companies' `store_total`.
- **Unreadable stays honest.** If either store cannot be read, the overview's
  count reads Unreadable. It never falls back to the signal companies alone.

**Tests.** `research_overview_d536.test.ts` builds migration 404's table and
gained two tests:
- One seeds a signal company, then an EDGAR row, then a ticker in both, and
  shows the overview's count and the tab's totals move together: 2, then 3,
  then 4. It also checks the note and the overlap.
- The other checks that an unreadable EDGAR store reads Unreadable.

`research_overview_d536.test.mjs` checks the card prints the server's split
and names both stores.

Mutations: 8 of 8 caught on the Worker, 2 of 2 on the page.
