### Investor (PRM20, PRM21)

**Request a mark.** It opens from the Stale chip's "Request a mark…", or from the mark-history Sheet, and goes through four steps in that Sheet.

- **Prepare.** The company is fixed, and the latest mark shows with its age, or "No mark recorded". The investor picks the kind and writes a required reason. Evidence is optional:
  - a link, which must be http or https with no credentials, at most 2,000 characters;
  - or one file: a PDF, PNG, JPEG or WebP of at most 10 MB.

  A request carries a link or a file, never both. These are the Worker's own checks (`routes/mark_requests.ts`), so the sheet never offers a request the Worker would refuse. Review stays closed until the draft passes them.
- **Review.** What HQ will see; nobody else sees it.
- **Confirm.** What changes now (a request is stored and HQ is told). What does not: the investor's mark, NAV and Positions figures, until HQ records a mark. A refused or failed send keeps the reason and the evidence on screen, and the button reads "Try again".
- **Receipt.** It is read from the stored row the Worker returned: "Requested <day>", the kind, the state and the stored evidence.

**The requests list.** Under the panel, "Your requests · newest first" lists the company's requests. Each reads one of:
- Requested;
- Answered · mark recorded, when it links a mark; the mark is in the history above;
- Answered, for a follow-on answered by a note;
- Declined, with HQ's reason in quotes.

An open request is never followed by a second one: opening the Sheet from the chip does not open the steps over it. A list that could not be read is never "no requests".

**Words.** "Resolved" now reads "Answered", as PRM21 draws it. The D6205 test pins were updated to the new words.

### HQ (PRM22, PRM23)

**Where the queue sits.** "Open mark requests" sits above the register on the admin Positions page. It covers every company, oldest first, as `GET /api/mark-requests/queue` returns it. Investors never see it.

**Each row.** It shows:
- the company, with its latest mark or "none recorded";
- the investor, or "Investor (name not recorded)", and the kind;
- the reason;
- the evidence. A file downloads through `GET /api/mark-requests/:uid/evidence`. A link is followed only when it passes the same http(s) check, and anything else is shown as text;
- the day it was requested, and its age in days.

**Record a mark….** It opens the company's position sheet. There HQ records the mark through PF-07's write flow and answers the request with it (D6205 part 2). Recording a mark stays an HQ admin act.

**Decline….** It goes Scope → Review → Result, with a required reason the investor sees. Review says that declining records no mark and changes no figure. A failed decline keeps the request open and the reason on screen.

**When the queue is read again.** After every decline and every answer given on the sheet.

**States.**
- Loading.
- Unavailable: "This is not 'No open requests'", with Try again. The register below still shows.
- Empty: "No open requests. The queue read succeeded."

### Not built here

- **Prefilled values.** Record a mark opens the company's sheet, but the request's as-of day, basis and source are not yet filled into the write flow's fields.
- **The Requested chip in the mark-history header.** The register's chip and the panel carry it already.
