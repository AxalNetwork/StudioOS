### What the canvas decides

- Fees shows an "Initial invoice · one-off" card above the unchanged annual list: the initial fee plus the regulatory surcharge for the frozen class, with reference, issued, due, paid so far, PDF, Record payment and Void.
- There is one initial invoice per licence, ever. A voided one is struck, its PDF hidden, and no second invoice can be issued.
- Money follows Prepare → Review → Confirm → Authenticate → Receipt. Nothing issues, sends or reprices itself.
- The Issue review names the amounts, the source pricing decision, the payee, the due date, what happens and how to undo.
- A missing initial fee or surcharge reads Not recorded on one line, never €0. Unavailable and €0 are drawn apart.
- With no accepted pricing, Fees offers one action: Open Pricing & Market.
- The licensee sees the initial invoice read-only on its Statements tab.
- The Apply review lists each changed value before → after with where it goes. Its receipt links to "Initial invoice: issue it from Fees".
- Eadwyn proposes an overdue reminder note, a one-sentence Apply summary and a reason. HQ accepts, edits or discards; Eadwyn sends nothing.
