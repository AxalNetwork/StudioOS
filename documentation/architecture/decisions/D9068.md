The routes are `frontend/src/routes/networkIntroRequests.jsx`, open to every licence that has Network. The client is
`frontend/src/lib/api/introRequests.js`. A founder's `Request an intro` and an advisor's `New introduction` on
Network › Introductions now open the request; an investor's keeps its own sheet (D6257).

- **Readiness is the server's.** `GET /readiness` answers each requirement's status and `ready`. The page groups the
  rows (Needs you, With a reviewer, Done) and draws one aggregate, worst first (`aggregateReadiness`). Ready needs
  both the server's `ready` and every row Approved; a failed read, an unknown status or a disagreement reads
  Unreadable, which is never ready and keeps Submit off.
- **The draft survives.** Leaving step 1 creates the draft with one idempotency key kept for the sheet, so a retry
  returns the same draft. Text saves send the revision they read; a newer save elsewhere is shown beside what was
  typed (Keep this tab's text, Use the newer text). A submit the server answers with `readiness_needed`, a refused
  submit and a failed submit all keep the draft. A lost answer is checked by reading the request back, never by a
  second submit.
- **Nothing contacts anybody.** The pages call no `/operator/…` route, and submitting, answering and withdrawing only
  change the member's own request.

### The one Worker change

`GET /api/intro-requests` takes `next_actor` (requester, operator, target or none) and answers `lanes`, each
lane's count from one grouped read over the member's own requests, whatever the filter. A lane with nothing in it
is a counted zero. `next_actor_invalid` refuses any other value. Tested on SQLite in
`cloudflare-worker/test/intro_requests_member_9068.test.ts`.

### Departures from the canvases

The platform is the data; where the canvas draws something the Worker does not record, the page says what is
recorded or leaves it out.

- **Policy.** The active policy (version 1) has two required items, `professional_profile` and `capacity`, for every
  capacity. The illustrative v0.3 set (seven items, RQ- ids, an Optional group, per-capacity applicability, NRD-02's
  three cards and NRD-03's switch preview) is not drawn. Row statuses are the Worker's five: Not started, Submitted,
  Declined, Expired, Approved, plus Unreadable for any other. Incomplete, Complete, In review, More information
  needed and Not applicable have no store.
- **Facts.** Identity verification reads Not applicable (the policy does not ask for it; Q1's Trust Center reuse is
  not in the policy). Due-diligence review reads as reviewed counts. Eligibility is the server's `ready`.
- **Profile (NRD-04).** The form holds what the Worker stores: a headline and an optional https link. Name,
  location, role, affiliation, description, contact method, the title conflict and Eadwyn's description draft are
  not built. A readiness save has no revision, so NRD-04 B's "saved in another tab" is not drawn.
- **Capacity.** Founder, investor or advisor, and a represented organisation as text, on the readiness record. A
  request does not record its own capacity, so the sheet shows the readiness record's capacity with a Change link,
  and the desk has no "As" column.
- **Evidence (NRD-05, NRD-05 B, NRD-07 C).** No evidence store, upload, reuse or reviewer question on readiness; a
  row shows only that a private evidence reference exists. Expiry after submit is the operator's (#9069).
- **Desk (NIE01).** Through Axal is its own page beside Network › Introductions, which keeps team requests, curated
  matches and the quarterly ask. Lanes follow the Worker's `next_actor`, so declined, unavailable, agreed and
  introduction-made requests sit in Awaiting Axal until an operator closes them, not in History. No Service or Owner
  column: the requester projection names no operator.
- **Choosing the person (NIE03).** The teammate check is not drawn. A person not in the directory takes a name,
  organisation and public page; title and "how you know them" have no store. Directory rows show place, source and
  date only. Person Sheet and organisation-row entry points pass `?person=` or `?org=`; wiring them is #9025's and
  #9071's.
- **Review and receipt (NIE06, NIE08).** Steps are a page, not a sheet over the desk. Cost reads "No charge · this
  service spends no credit" (D7673); no price policy read exists. The receipt shows the policy version checked at
  submit, not a snapshot id. "What happens next" is the activity list and the Next line.
- **Status (NIE10, NIE10B).** Contact is "Contact recorded" from the requester-visible event, without channel or
  delivery detail. Follow-up date, brief versions and "Suggest an edit" after contact are not built. Answering Axal
  edits the purpose or brief and returns the request to review; there is no separate answer field.
- **Links and states (NIE11, NIE18 B).** The deep link is `/network/introductions/requests/:uid`, opened as a page.
  A duplicate open request, stale readiness and delivery-unknown states have no store. A support session sees the
  Worker's refusal on a write rather than a banner first.
- **Shell.** The pages draw no Network tab row and no Eadwyn rail; a breadcrumb leads back. Tablet 768 is the
  responsive layout between the phone and desktop frames.
