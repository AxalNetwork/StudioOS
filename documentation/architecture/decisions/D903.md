### 2. The store: migration 543

`deck_images` holds one row per image, generated or uploaded:

- the bytes in R2 (`FILES`, `deck-images/<project>/<uid>.<ext>`), with their MIME type and size;
- `use`: a content image, a card background or the deck background;
- `overlay`: none, light or strong, for a background;
- `state`: proposed, kept or discarded;
- `ai_usage_log_id`, `charged_microusd` and `model`: the run that drew it and what it was
  charged;
- `shows_person`, `person_consent_at` and `person_consent_by`.

Two CHECKs hold the people rule in the table itself:

- a row that shows a person needs a recorded agreement;
- a generated image never shows a person.

### 3. The routes (`routes/deck_images.ts`)

Mounted before `decks`, with the other `/api/decks` routers.

- `POST /api/decks/:id/images/quote` returns the model and its price per image (`quoteRun`'s
  hold), the total for the count asked, and whether the member's AI tokens cover it. The balance
  is checked before any hold (`balanceRefusal`, the meeting bot's rule).
- `POST /api/decks/:id/images` makes one `run()` per image, on the rail option `deck:image`.
  Each image is stored in R2 and kept as a proposed row naming its run and its charge. If the
  model stops part-way, what it drew is returned with the reason.
- `POST /api/decks/:id/images/upload` takes a PNG, JPEG or WebP image of at most 5 MB, read from
  the bytes themselves. An upload showing a person is refused without the agreement
  (`person_consent_required`).
- `GET /api/decks/:id/images` lists the venture's kept images: PD12d's Design list.
- `POST /api/decks/images/:uid/keep` keeps an image, with its use, overlay and alt text.
- `POST /api/decks/images/:uid/discard` discards a proposal. A kept image stays, because a saved
  version may show it.
- `GET /api/decks/images/:uid/file` serves the bytes at an https app URL that passes
  `sanitizeImageUrl`, never a `data:` URL.
  - A kept image is in shares and exports, so it is served to anyone holding its unguessable
    URL, cached immutably.
  - A proposal is served only to the venture's writers.
  - A discarded image is served to nobody.

**Refused before the quote or the hold, so they cost nothing:**

- a real person (422 `real_person`), in the canvas's words: "Eadwyn does not draw real people.
  Upload a photo the person agreed to share." That means:
  - a face, a portrait, a headshot or a selfie;
  - "our team" or "the founder";
  - a full name of one of the venture's own people (its founders and `project_members`), or
    their surname written as a name;
- a figure (`figure_in_prompt`, D897's rule);
- a chart or a graph (`not_illustrative`).

Then, before the hold:

- a balance that cannot cover every image asked for (402);
- a storage plan with no room (413, `assertStorageRoom`).

**The prompt** ends "An illustration only: no identifiable people, no faces, no text, no
letters, no numbers, no charts or graphs." A data point's label shapes the description; its value
never does.

**Every image run, Keep and upload** writes one `raise.pitch-deck` cycle row (D691 decision 12),
never a figure.

### 4. The overlay's contrast (PD12b, PD12c)

- A background's text is light (`#fbfaf7`) on an overlay in the brand ink. Light is 65% and Strong
  is 80%.
- The image under the text could be anything, so the check is the worst case: the overlay over a
  white pixel, by WCAG 2.x relative luminance.
- "Keep as background" is refused until that reaches 4.5:1. So with no overlay it is always
  refused (PD12c).
- On the default ink `#0f2a3d`, Light reads 4.58:1 and Strong 7.46:1. A light brand ink fails
  even on Strong, and the lightbox says so.
- `frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/imageContrast.js` is the same rule in the page, and the Worker
  test holds the two equal, figure for figure.

### 5. The slide model

- **An image block** keeps `image_id`, `fit` (cover or contain) and `crop`, the point that stays in
  view, in percent.
- **A background**, the card's or the theme's, keeps `image_id` and `overlay`.
- `GET /api/decks/model` lists `image_fits` and `overlays`.

### 10. Amendment for #3660 (S22, 2026-10-29): visuals that carry the story, from records and Cloudflare only

S33's ruling on #3660 for the owner, 2026-10-29: extend D903 for imagery and visual assets. This builds on D898 §8's unchanged block contract (#3658) and D897 §9's composer (#3659), and touches neither `slideModel.ts` nor the renderer owned by #3656. No migration.

**10.1 Diagrams placed by records** (`services/decks/visuals.ts`). Each is a D898 §8.4 diagram: editable labelled steps, each item bound to the record that placed it.
- **The roadmap timeline** (`roadmapDiagram`):
  - Build's open objectives (`roadmap_okrs`) in working order: now, next, later, then the founder's own order.
  - Each item's detail is its column and its quarter when recorded.
  - Done objectives are left off, and there are at most six.
- **Use of funds** (`useOfFundsDiagram`): the founder's recorded sections as a flow, largest share first, **by name only**. A diagram carries no percentage; the shares stay figures.
- **The competitor matrix** (`competitorMatrix`):
  - Each competitor is placed from what the founder recorded in Validate › Competitors: price (lower or higher than ours) and depth (narrower or broader).
  - A competitor recorded as "about the same", or with no position, is left off rather than guessed.
  - It needs two placed competitors. The empty quadrants say "None recorded here".
- **Reads that fail or come up short:** a failed read places no diagram, and fewer than two items is none. Either way the card falls back to a layout without the visual (D898 §8.3), never a made-up one.

**10.2 Charts.** `chartTypeFor(series)` picks how a bound series reads best:
- four points or more is a line, and fewer is columns;
- a weekly count is columns;
- one point, or an unrecorded series, is no chart.

The composer uses it for the traction and validation charts. Numbers are only ever the bound series; no model writes a series or a point.

**10.3 The composer draws them** (`newDeck.ts`, D897 §9):
- **Where the diagrams go:** `recordDiagrams` feeds `CardVisuals.diagrams`, plus `rows` for the matrix. Roadmap and use of funds take the `timeline` layout, and market and competition take `comparison_table`, with the typed diagram in its range.
- **The deck route** reads them once per build, beside the chart series. A seed fund's full deck from the synthetic full records then uses seven or more layouts, every card within budget.

**10.4 Artwork with Workers AI.**
- **Capability, checked rather than promised** (`imageCapability(env)`): it is available only with the Worker's `AI` binding and FLUX.1 Schnell (`@cf/black-forest-labs/flux-1-schnell`, D903's model) listed as generally available in the committed Workers AI catalogue (`services/ai/catalogue.ts`, read 2026-10-19).
  - Without it, the deck takes its no-image layouts, and no other provider is ever called.
  - This session could not run the model against the live account. The live check stays the owner's (§9).
- **One art direction per deck** (`artDirection(theme, kit)`): one style, a palette from the theme's legible colours, and one prompt suffix ("No text, no numbers, no charts, no logos, no people."). Every illustration in a deck therefore belongs together, and none can bake in a figure or a slide's words.
- **A plan, never a generation** (`imagePlan`, `GET /api/decks/:id/images/plan`):
  - It lists the words-only cards an illustration helps (title, solution, product, why now) that have no image yet.
  - Each comes with a starting description from the card's heading, figures stripped.
  - It proposes nothing when the capability is missing.
- **Generating:** this goes through D903's existing image dialog (PD12): the quote first, the description's refusals (real people, figures, charts), the preview, crop, alt text, and keep or discard. The kept image is stored in R2 as before.
