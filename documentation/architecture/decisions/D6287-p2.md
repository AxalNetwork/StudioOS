## D6287 part 2 — Firm Settings status blocks, deal fit after save and Team seats (#6287)

Part 1 is `D6287.md`: the investor branch at `/account/portfolio/settings` and
the one editor with a partial save (#7569). This part builds the rest of the
Investor Firm Settings canvas that `D6286.md` marks frontend-only, with the
owner's answers in `D6286-p2.md` (option A on every question). No Worker
route, store or migration changes.

**Built (boards CS-17, CS-18, CS-18B, CS-19B, CS-20A/B, CS-21, CS-24, CS-24B, CS-25, CS-26).**

- Four read-only status blocks beside the form, stacked above it below
  1024px: Identity and accreditation (`GET /api/kyc/status`; accreditation
  from the profile, labelled self-declared), Licences
  (`GET /api/account/licences`, each held licence with its plan or Not
  recorded), Funds (`GET /api/funds`, the count and how the caller stands in
  each) and Team seats (`GET /api/investor-seats`). Each reads alone, with its
  own skeleton and Unavailable with Try again; they still read when the
  profile read fails, and that page still draws no form.
- The head says Complete or "Needs: …" from the saved profile (investor type,
  a sector, a stage, a cheque size), never from `completed_at`.
- "In your deal flow now" appears after a save: the deals in the firm's flow
  (`GET /api/deals/flow`, the first 50) with Match, Outside, Not set or Not
  recorded for sector, stage, region and cheque (`GET /api/deals/fit`). A
  whole failed read is Unavailable with Try again; a 402 says deal fit comes
  with the Professional plan and links to Billing; a 403 is a refusal with no
  Try again. Both deal reads go through `dealFlowApi.flowInline` and
  `dealViewsApi.fitInline` (the same routes with `inlinePaywall`), so the
  paywall modal stays shut on this page.
- The compare is this session's only. The fit read that stands for "before
  Save" starts on the first edit, and Save waits for it at most three seconds
  before it writes; past that the round has no compare. After the save the
  panel lists each criterion that moved ("Stage moved Match → Outside") or
  says none did, and each good read becomes the next round's "before". A
  reload starts with no compare: the platform keeps no fit history.
- Team seats: the list with Joined, Invited or Revoked and the date; Send
  invite, apart from Save; the invite link shown once (D632); Revoke as Scope
  → Review → Result, the review naming the seat, what stops and what stays.
  A refused invite says why in the page's own sentence, never the server's
  text. On an investor plan other than Institutional with no seats, the
  section says seats come with the Institutional plan and links to Billing.

**Departs from the canvas, and why.**

- The fit panel has no Eadwyn rail. The before/after explanation is the
  panel's own list of moved criteria, worked out on the page; no assistant
  call writes it.
- "Preview before saving" is not drawn: fit is read only for the saved
  thesis (D6286, backend required).
- A deal row shows its venture and sector; the canvas's stage, region and
  minimum cheque line is not in the flow read.
- The revoke review opens inline under the list, not as a modal.
- Team seats is a list at every width, not a table, so 390 needs no sideways
  scroll; the fit table becomes cards below 768px as CS-25B draws.
- A seat colleague still reads their own (usually empty) seat list and their
  own profile; the owner's read-only firm (CS-20D) needs the shared firm
  profile that D6286 lists as backend required. Their invite is refused with
  "Ask the account owner to add seats."
- Not built here and not in this issue's scope: the "On this page" nav, the
  Eadwyn thesis draft (CS-17B), the leave dialog (CS-20F), the conflict state
  (CS-20E, backend required), and the Country, LP interest, LP target and
  private notes fields and the read-only keywords, which the profile already
  stores.
