## D628 — An operational fund ledger with explicit fee terms

Issue #1371 implements the owner's 4.A answer on #1138. Migration 418 is additive. The historical `journal_entries` and live investment decision journal do not record a fund's double-entry books and are not reused.

### Books and double entry

Each fund has its own chart of accounts (asset, liability, equity, income, expense), non-overlapping accounting periods, entries and lines. All amounts are safe integer USD cents. General entries have two to fifty one-sided lines and positive, equal debits and credits. Expenses debit an expense account and credit cash or a liability. No opening balance, receipt, cash holding, valuation or past expense is inferred.

Posting inserts the header and all lines, recomputes debit/credit totals and line count from those inserted lines, and publishes the entry under a database CHECK, together with the actor audit in one D1 batch. Audit failure rolls the transaction back. Composite foreign keys prevent a line or period from belonging to a different fund. An explicitly recorded zero fee has two zero lines and is a recorded zero, not an inferred absence. There are no runtime schema bootstraps or hand-created triggers.

### Close and reconciliation

Every post rechecks the open period and its version inside the transaction. Close and reopen increment that version; this also detects a close/reopen that occurred while a post was prepared. A close cannot admit an unpublished or unbalanced entry. Reopening requires a nonblank reason, recorded with its actor. Period state changes use the previously read version and state as a compare-and-set. Posted entries have no update or deletion endpoint; corrections are new balanced entries in an open period.

A reconciliation mark contains a statement reference, unique statement-line reference within the fund, statement date, signed integer cents, actor and timestamp. Its amount must match debit minus credit on that line. A statement line cannot reconcile a second ledger line. Reconciliation cannot change a closed period, and a mark has no replacement or deletion endpoint. The entry and statement references remain visible to authorized ledger readers.

### Recorded fee terms and arithmetic

Pick A from the issue's STATUS: append explicit annual basis points, basis cents, effective date and source. The existing `management_fee` default of 0.02 is a fund setting, not evidence of an agreed contract, and is never used for accrual. Terms effective at the start of a period must remain the latest terms throughout that period; a later term change requires splitting the period. A concurrent term change refuses the prepared accrual. Each period permits one management-fee accrual.

Fee cents = round(basis cents × annual basis points × inclusive calendar days / (10000 × 365)). BigInt arithmetic rounds once, half up, to the nearest cent and refuses an unsafe result. The recorded basis is explicit rather than inferred from commitments or NAV. A $1,000,000 basis at 200 bps for 2024-01-01 through 2024-03-31 has 91 days and accrues 498,630 cents ($4,986.30). Missing terms post nothing and say “Not recorded”; a zero rate is recorded and accrues zero.

### Access and actor

Pick A from STATUS: `vc_funds.fund_admin_user_id` is an explicit account assignment made only by an admin, to an active investor account, or removed with null. The existing administrator name and GP identity do not grant ledger authority. Authenticated admins and the assigned fund administrator can create accounts and periods, record terms, post entries/expenses/fees, reconcile, close and reopen. The transaction rechecks the administrator assignment, so a revoked assignment cannot authorize a prepared post. Every write commits its `activity_logs` actor and complete provenance with its data.

An admin sees all funds; the assigned administrator sees their funds and full journal/trial balance; an investor LP sees only summaries for funds in their canonical LP scope. Legacy email-based LP access claims an unclaimed row without replacing an existing user assignment. Other funds and roles receive 404. Business refusals use a runtime allowlist of authored sentences; provider/database exceptions remain sanitized Unreadable. LP responses contain no account chart, entry descriptions, actors, statement references or journal lines. Journal lines are restricted to the header IDs already read, so a concurrently posted entry cannot enter the trial balance alone. Trial balances and journals are explicitly per fund and selected period; absent periods or posted entries are Not recorded, and failed reads are sanitized Unreadable rather than partial totals.

### Read surfaces and boundary

Both `/funds/accounting` and `/funds/ledger` read `/api/fund-ledger` independently of legacy capital analytics. The shared read panel uses IF3's Line / Amount / State / Note table and Fund Administration's fee-by-period controls. It displays fund and period selections, summary fees/expenses, and authorized journal/trial-balance reads. Loading hides stale figures; Unreadable has a retry; missing records preserve the Worker reason; explicit cents, including zero, are displayed without dollar rounding.

The Fund Administration canvas has a fee ledger and statements, and IF3 names journal/reconcile/close controls, but neither draws a posting, reconciliation or close editor. This issue asks to wire reads, so these pages remain read-only; no new editor is invented. Journal export, collected fees, carry accrual and ledger-derived NAV are not recorded by this change. Existing capital accounts and issued reporting periods retain their existing stores. Ledger fees and expenses are posted amounts, not automatically paid or statutory disclosures. The operational ledger is **not the fund's statutory books**.
