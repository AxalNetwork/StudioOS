## D4374 — A daily sweep stops a stuck generation run and settles its hold (#4374)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-04 (#4374, follow-up to D1502 and D1536).
- **Number:** D4374 is the issue's number (D4106).
- **Migration:** `202611040743_deck_batch_stop_reason.sql` adds `deck_area_batches.stop_reason`.

### Why

A deck generation run whose driver died (the `DeckGenRoom` lost it, its queue
slice went to the DLQ, or it hit the slice cap) stayed `running` for ever. It
blocked its deck from a new run, its read said "Running", and its hold was left
to expire, so steps that had charged were never settled. DECK_GRID.md §3 had
only a by-hand SQL path and said "A sweeper is not built".

### What changed

- **The rule.** `services/decks/batchSweep.ts` `sweepStuckBatches` finds runs
  still `queued`, `running` or `cancelling` with no step started or ended (and,
  before the first step, not started or created) for `STUCK_AFTER_MS`, 30
  minutes. The constant's comment gives the reason: longer than anything a live
  run waits through between two step events (a step's attempts, the governor's
  10-minute wait, a 10-minute DO slice).
- **Steps end by rebuild's rule.** A `running` step whose usage row exists under
  the run's hold is `done` at what it charged, clamped to its quote; any other
  `running` or `waiting` step is `stopped` with `error_code = 'timed_out'`. A
  charge is never lost and never invented.
- **Closed once.** The run becomes `cancelled` with `stop_reason = 'timed_out'`
  in one compare-and-set on its state. Only the sweep that closed it settles the
  hold, through `settleAiReservation`, at what the steps charged; the rest is
  released. A hold no longer pending is counted `already_released`, not settled.
- **Audit.** One `activity_logs` row per sweep, `deck.batch_sweep`, actor
  `system`, with its counts.
- **The read.** The batch read (and its contract) gains `stop_reason` and
  `state_line`; a swept run reads "Stopped · timed out".
- **Where it runs.** The daily cleanup in `models/jobs.ts`, in its own
  try/catch, so its failure stops nothing else.

### Not built

An HQ list of stuck runs, and a lease on a queue slice (D1536).
