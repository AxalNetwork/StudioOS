## D4853 — Raise's Pitch entries open the one Pitch Studio, `/raise/pitch` (#4853)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-07 (#4853, a live-browser finding of the same day).
- **Number:** D4853 is the issue's number (D4106).
- **Builds on:** D4254 (the Studio's address family and the first redirect), D2319 (retained redirects), D688 and D695 (the module cycle's Add forms).
- **Migration:** none.

### Why

The redesigned Pitch Studio lives at `/raise/pitch`: Overview at the root, then `/decks`, `/script`, `/share`, `/edit`, `/new`. The Raise module cycle was the studio's first home, and its addresses were still the ones a founder was handed from Raise:

- the overview's **Pitch** tab and card named `/raise/pitch-deck?project_id=12`;
- **Share the deck with an investor** and the module's **New link** named `/raise/pitch-deck/new?project=12`, the module's own Add form: a second, older sharing flow beside the Studio's Share tab;
- the readiness next step and the overview's jump list named `/raise/pitch-deck?project=…`.

Which Pitch experience a founder reached therefore depended on where they started. `/raise/pitch-deck` already redirected to the Studio (D4254), but that redirect kept the query as written, and the Studio reads `?project_id=` only. The module cycle's links said `?project=`, so the founder's chosen venture was dropped on the way.

### What changed

- **One address per entry, written at the source.** `services/raiseCycle.ts` and `services/cycleFocus.ts` give the Studio's own address:
  - the Pitch module and its readiness step: `/raise/pitch?project_id=<id>`;
  - **Share the deck with an investor** and **New link**: `/raise/pitch/share?project_id=<id>`.
  The integration label (`integrations/moduleLabels.ts`) and the calls model name `/raise/pitch`.
- **The client says it too.** `pages/raise/pitch/pitchRoutes.js` maps the two legacy addresses to the Studio, with the venture carried as `project_id`, every other parameter and the hash kept. `cycleTabs`, the overview's module link and `safeHref` use it, so even an older read, or a Worker that has not deployed yet, links the Studio.
- **The legacy addresses redirect, by replacement.** `/raise/pitch-deck` → `/raise/pitch`, and the new `/raise/pitch-deck/new` → `/raise/pitch/share`, both through `KeepQueryRedirect`, which now carries the venture as `project_id`. They replace the entry, so the Back button does not bounce between the families. Both stay in `REDIRECT_ROUTES` (kept and counted), because a bookmark or a link already sent can still carry either.
- **Retained on purpose: `/raise/pitch-deck/<id>`.** One investor link's own record page (views, slides, revoke) has no Studio equivalent yet: the Share tab lists links but has no per-link page. It keeps its address and its purpose until the Studio draws one.

### Not changed

- The module cycle's data address, `/api/cycles/raise/modules/pitch-deck`, and the `pitch-deck` module slug: they are the Worker's record keys, not founder-facing addresses.
- Documentation anchors named `build/pitch-deck` (the explainers and the advisor banks): they are help-page anchors, not routes.
- Historical decisions (D688, D695, D894, D1223, D2319, D4254) keep the addresses they recorded.
- The Pitch Studio itself: the issue asks for route integrity, not a redesign (#4839).
