## D1484 — Calendar as the one timeline: the source registry, plain names, and the readers wired

- **Status:** part 1 (#3977) built by S19 from S1's specification on #3723 (sections "The source registry", "Tests" and "Task breakdown 1").
  - Part 2, the source settings store and the CAL4 panel (#3978), built by S17. It takes migration 708; see its section below.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1205–D1212 (the Calendar page and its readers);
  - D1208 (the four personal source readers);
  - D1384 (the Compliance layer and `PAGE_ONLY_KINDS`).
- **Migration:** none for part 1.

### The question

The calendar had no single list of its sources. Each kind's family, label and pushability lived in four places: `READERS`, `FAMILY_OF`, the route's `PUSHABLE_KINDS`, and the page's `KINDS`. The page's legend showed table names (`ic_meetings`, `raise_prospect_events`). Three readers D1208 built (interview calls, cohort dates, deal closings) were read by nothing, and the events a member adds with "New event" (`POST /api/calendar/events`, stored as `calendar_events` kind `other`) were written but never shown.

### What was decided

**One registry** (`cloudflare-worker/src/services/calendar/registry.ts`)
- `SOURCES: Record<CalendarEvent['kind'], SourceDef>`, where `SourceDef` is `{ family, module, label, page_only, pushable }`.
- `family` is one of `meetings | events | validate | grow | deadlines | raise | tasks | external`.
- `module` and `label` are plain words, for example "Raise › Capital relationships" and "Investor meeting". They are never a table name.
- The other lists either come from the registry or are tested against it:
  - `PAGE_ONLY_KINDS` is derived from it, and `FEED_KINDS` follows.
  - `READERS`, `FAMILY_OF`, `COPYABLE_KINDS` and the route's literal `PUSHABLE_KINDS` are asserted equal to it (`calendar_registry_d1484.test.ts`).
  - `Record<CalendarEvent['kind'], …>` makes tsc refuse a kind without an entry.

**Every item names its source.**
- Each item from `GET /api/calendar/events` carries `source: { family, module, label, account: null }`.
- `account` is for part 3's connected calendars.
- Each `sources[]` entry also names its family, module and label, so the legend can name a source that returned nothing in the range.

**Four kinds read** (fourteen in all). Each is page only: never in an `.ics` file, a feed or a provider copy. Each is the owner's rows alone.

| Kind | Reader | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| `interview_call` | D1208 `interviewCallEvents` | the call's recorded owner |
| `cohort_date` | D1208 `cohortDateEvents` (all day) | the member's own Lab cohort, or an advisor's assignment |
| `deal_closing` | D1208 `dealClosingEvents` (all day) | an investor's deal-room memberships |
| `manual` | new `manualEvents`: `calendar_events WHERE kind = 'other' AND user_id = ?`, bound | the member who added it |

- None of the four enters a staff scope (`PERSONAL_ONLY_KINDS`): there is no projection of them for anyone but their owner.

**The page**
- `CalendarPage.jsx`'s `KINDS` no longer carries `src`. The legend shows each source's `module` from the read.
- The four kinds join `KINDS` and the families in `calendarViews.js`:
  - Meetings gains `interview_call`;
  - Deadlines gains `cohort_date` and `deal_closing`;
  - Events gains `manual`.
- Each new kind has its own light and dark hue and dot shape in `calendarPage.css`.

**Not in part 1:** the source settings store and panel (part 2, migration 708), Google and Outlook read-only (part 3), `copy_target` (part 4), and the further sources (part 5).

### Verified

**`cloudflare-worker/test/calendar_registry_d1484.test.ts`** has 5 tests:
- the registry's keys equal `READERS`' keys and the union, and the derived lists agree;
- no label or module is a table name;
- the four kinds reach their owner only, and a second synthetic user sees none;
- none of them is in `.ics` or a feed;
- each item carries `source`, and a deadline stays all day.

**Updated for the new kinds:**
- `calendar_compliance_d1384` (the page-only set);
- `calendar_d1206` (`sources[]` names its module, and the scope compares against the kinds it reads);
- `calendar_page_c1` (no `src`, fourteen kinds, the legend reads the Worker's module, each new mark distinct);
- `calendar_views_d1205` and `calendar_grids_d1210` (the families and counts).

**Mutations**, each applied alone and caught:
- `user_id = ?` dropped from the manual reader;
- a table name rendered as a label again;
- `manual` not page only;
- `source` left off the items.

### Part 2 (#3978, 2026-11-01): hide and colour sources per member, and the CAL4 panel

Built by S17 from S1's spec on #3723 (Settings store, Routes, Frontend and Tests).

**Migration 708** (`708_calendar_source_prefs.sql`) is additive and `IF NOT EXISTS`. It adds two tables:
- `calendar_source_prefs (user_id, source_key, shown, colour, updated_at)`, keyed on `(user_id, source_key)`;
- `calendar_settings (user_id, copy_target, updated_at)`, where `copy_target` is `all`, `google`, `microsoft` or `none` and defaults to `all`.

How the store behaves:
- **Colours are palette tokens**: violet, blue, teal, green, amber, orange, red, pink and slate. A CHECK refuses anything else, so a hex never reaches D1. Each token has a light and a dark value (`--pal-*` in `calendarPage.css`).
- **A missing row means "shown, in its family's colour".** No read writes a default row.
- **Every read and write is the member's own row**, `WHERE user_id = ?` bound (`services/calendar/prefs.ts`). Nothing widens to staff.

**Routes** are all `requireAuth`, in `routes/calendar.ts`:
- `GET /api/calendar/sources` answers `{ families: [{ key, label }], sources: [{ key, family, module, label, shown, colour, page_only }], copy_target }`. The families are the registry's nine, in its order. Their labels live in `FAMILY_LABELS`, so the external family reads "From other calendars".
- `PUT /api/calendar/sources/:key` takes `{ shown?, colour? }` and changes only the fields given. `colour: null` clears the token. It refuses with 400:
  - `unknown_source`: not a registry kind, so a table name is refused too;
  - `invalid_colour`;
  - `empty_body`: neither field given, or `shown` is not a boolean.
- `PUT /api/calendar/settings` takes `{ copy_target }`. It refuses with 400 `invalid_copy_target`, or with 409 `provider_not_connected` for `google` or `microsoft` when that calendar is not connected. Part 4 reads the setting.
- `GET /api/calendar/events` changes only when `kinds` is absent:
  - the sources the member hid are not read at all, so they get no item and no source status;
  - they are named in a new `hidden: [key]`;
  - with every source hidden, nothing is read and the read still answers;
  - an explicit `kinds` reads what it names, hidden or not.
  - If the store cannot be read, nothing is hidden: the page shows every source rather than failing.
- **`.ics`, the subscription feeds and the push never read the settings.**

**Frontend**
- **Client:** `lib/api/calendarSources.js` (`calendarSourcesApi`), with one line in `api.js` (D527).
- **CAL4** is `CalendarSourcesPanel.jsx` plus `calendarSourcesModel.js` (pure), at `/calendar/sources`. It opens over the view it came from, with the same guard as `/calendar/connected`.
  - Sources are grouped by family. Each has a Show toggle and a colour ("Family colour" or one of the nine tokens), and each change saves at once and re-reads the page.
  - Each source's count in the page's range comes from the page read, never stored. A source that did not answer shows "—" with its reason, never 0. A hidden source reads "Hidden".
- **The rail:** its "What is on this calendar" card draws each source's dot in the member's colour, and links to the panel.

**Verified**
- `cloudflare-worker/test/calendar_prefs_708.test.ts` (6 tests) runs real SQL, with 708 applied twice on the baseline. It covers:
  - the PUT/GET round trip;
  - each refusal code;
  - user B can neither read nor change A's settings;
  - a hidden source is absent from `/events` without `kinds` and present with it;
  - hiding everything;
  - `.ics` and the subscription feed never read the settings, and `.ics` is unchanged.
- `frontend/test/calendar_sources_d1484.test.mjs` (3 tests) covers:
  - the model: "—" with why, never 0;
  - the palette matching the migration and the CSS, both themes;
  - the panel at its address: families, a toggle and a colour that save, and the page re-reading;
  - the rail's dots and the link.
- **Mutations**, each caught:
  - the owner predicate dropped from the prefs read;
  - `/events` ignoring `hidden`;
  - `.ics` reading the settings;
  - a colour left unchecked;
  - a failed count mapped to 0;
  - the toggle saving the wrong field;
  - the page not re-read.
- `calendar_item_d1211`'s pin of the page's `behind` line now names the panel too.
