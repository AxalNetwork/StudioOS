## D6039 — Filing reminder dates and eligible recipients (#6039)

An open formation step or entity obligation is eligible on its UTC due date
and on the calendar day given by the configured lead time. Undated, filed
and waived records are excluded. Eligible recipients are the venture’s
active owner and accepted active co-founders. An entity shared by multiple
ventures contributes one candidate per recipient and window.

The delivery service takes a lead time and uses the existing notification
service. A database key claims each filing, due date, reminder day and
recipient before notification. The delivery record has a sent timestamp
only when the notification service records an inbox entry. Concurrent
runs and reruns do not repeat an attempt.

The owner chose 14 days before the due date and on the day. The Worker
runs the sweep at 05:35 UTC with the scheduled UTC date. The filing record
shows sent timestamps and the open filing’s upcoming reminder dates.
The Add filing form explains the schedule. This supersedes D690’s
decision 8 on filing reminders.
