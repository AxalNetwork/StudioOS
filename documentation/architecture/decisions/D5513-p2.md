## D5513 part 2 — Public claims: unsourced outcome figures and testimonials come down (#5513)

- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-17, slot S21. The owner answered questions 3–5 on #5513. Questions 1 and 2 shipped in #6929 under D5513.
- **Record:** `documentation/product/CLAIMS_5513.md`, now marked approved, with an owner and review date per claim.

### Decision

- **No fixed program track record.** `PROGRAM` (37 graduates, 86% incorporated on time, $8.6M raised by alumni) is removed from `frontend/src/lib/spinoutFundModel.js`. No source was ever recorded for it.
  - `programFigures()` in `spinoutFundFigures.js` reads only the `GET /api/spinout-lab/fund-metrics` program block. A figure the block does not carry reads "Not recorded", and "Unreadable" when the read failed. This is the absent-state rule of #5129 and D6137.
  - The LP workspace hero, the investor page hero and the fund brief PDF all draw these figures from `programFigures`, so the three print the same strings.
- **The investor page's studio-proof tiles carry no values.** `PROOF_STUDIO_STATIC` held 92%, "34 of 37 graduates", 89%, 62%, 24 days, 30% and "11 of 37 graduates backed". It is replaced by `PROOF_STUDIO`, which says what each tile measures and which program field fills it.
  - These figures were not named in the owner's answer, but they are the same kind of unsourced outcome figure, and two cite the same 37. The owner's rule (the live read, or nothing until a source is recorded) applies to them as written.
  - The provenance captions no longer say "operator-maintained". They say a figure appears once platform records hold it.
- **The Demo Day testimonials are taken down.** The three named "Past Attendees" quotes on `/lp/spinout-demo-day` had no recorded consent or source. They return only through the partner proof flow's consent record.
- **Owner and review.** Each claim in the record is owned by the owner and reviewed at the next quarterly review. The re-review triggers are listed per claim.

### Not changed

- The quarterly report view model never printed the three program figures. It still prints fixed fund-reporting tables from `spinoutFundModel.js`: `COHORT_HISTORY`, `FUND_PROGRESSION` (NAV and IRR), `SECTOR_EXPOSURE` and `DEPLOYMENT_BY_QUARTER`. Those are fund facts for committed LPs, outside #5513's two public claims. They are raised for the owner separately.
- The network counts (38 matched advisors, 6 jurisdictions, 19 tools) describe the product, not an outcome. The cohort snapshot is captioned as an illustrative composite and is pinned by `lp_spinout_lab_nav.test.mjs`.
