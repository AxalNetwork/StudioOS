### What the canvas decides

- The directory lists companies from the advisor's accepted relationships only, with the source "Linked on their account". Pending requests, public companies and guesses stay out.
- Known people counts distinct accounts (`other.id`), not names. Two people named the same get two rows and count as 2.
- Each row shows the people known, relationship types and the latest recorded interaction.
- People with no linked company have their own group, each linked to their relationship record.
- A note says affiliation is not referral credit; the typed `referred_org` on a referral is not read.
- A name opens the Person Sheet; "Open relationship record" goes to that card in the Relationship book (`?rel=<id>`) with a way back. Back restores search, sort, scroll and focus, and is announced.
- States: none (View relationships), companies unreadable (relationships still listed, no total), relationships unreadable (Try again, no counts), loading, accepted with none linked, no matches, and two profiles sharing a name.
- The Relationship book also shows `last_interaction_at`.
