## D677 — The Firm, Platform and Administrators pages, built to the owner's website canvas

Issue: #1551. Part of #1546 (D673). Source: `design/canvases/integrated/Axal VC Website.dc.html`, views FIRM, PLATFORM and ADMINISTRATORS, with their content from the canvas's logic script (`philosophy`, `criteria`, `platformLayers`, `dataFlows`, `adminBlocks`, `adminLicences`, `adminFaq`).

### What was built

- **`/about` becomes the Firm page (`pages/site/FirmPage.jsx`).** It follows FIRM, in its order:
  1. the hero, "We are early because that is where the outcome is decided.", with a "Meet the team →" link to `/team`;
  2. the mission beside the investment philosophy;
  3. what we look for, the four weighted criteria;
  4. the Fund I band.
- **The team page moves to `/team`.** `TeamPage.jsx` is unchanged. The `/team → /about` redirect is replaced by the page itself.
- **`/platform` is new (`pages/site/PlatformPage.jsx`).** It follows PLATFORM:
  1. the hero;
  2. the four layers, each with its module cards;
  3. "Why it compounds / The data model is the product", with the data flows.
- **`/administrators` is new (`pages/site/AdministratorsPage.jsx`).** It follows ADMINISTRATORS:
  1. the hero, with "Request a licence" (to `/contact?topic=licence`) and "See the console" (to `#admin-console`);
  2. the console's eight places: the Studio with its sketch (`aria-hidden`, as in the canvas), the six blocks, Settings with the DNS records, and the Eadwyn note;
  3. the two licences;
  4. the questions;
  5. the closing.
- **The copy lives in `lib/siteFirm.js`,** and the three pages share `pages/site/firm.css`. Each page renders in `SitePage`.
- **Three more paths are public in `isPublicPath` (`lib/api.js`):** `/team`, `/platform` and `/administrators`. A background 401 on them does not bounce a logged-out visitor to `/login`.
- **The slide editor's "Team" source link (`SpinoutSlideEditor.jsx`) now points at `/team`.** It pointed at `/about` because the team page lived there.

### The decisions

- **Fund I ships as option B, pending the owner's answer on #1551.**
  - The band keeps "Fund I is open to qualified LPs." and its "Investor overview →" link to `/for-investors`.
  - The line "$20M target · $25M hard cap · pre-seed · vintage 2026" is left out.
  - Philosophy item 04, "Concentration with reserves", is left out whole: its check sizes and reserve share are Fund I terms. The other four are numbered 01–04.
  - Option A would restore the band line and item 04.
- **Each Platform module links the Spin-Out Lab page of its tool.**
  - The canvas's module copy was drawn from those pages, and each route is one `App.jsx` serves today.
  - The routes:
    - `/spinout-lab/profiling`, `/spinout-lab/scoring` and `/spinout-lab/startup`;
    - `/spinout-lab/discovery`, `/spinout-lab/market` and `/spinout-lab/revenue`;
    - `/spinout-lab/roadmap`, `/spinout-lab/brand` and `/spinout-lab/pitch-deck`;
    - `/spinout-lab/incorporate`, `/spinout-lab/captable` and `/spinout-lab/capital`.
  - They are guarded by `labRoles(['admin'])`, and Brand by `labRoles(['admin', 'founder'])`. A logged-out visitor signs in first. A signed-in account outside a cohort is sent to its home by `RoleGuard`.
  - The Lab intro chose to show its arsenal without links for this reason (`spinoutLabArsenal.js`). #1551 asks for links, so the cards link. Whether to send outsiders to the Lab intro instead is S1's call.
- **The counts in the module copy are read from the code.**
  - The Scoring Engine's count is `DIMENSIONS.length`, six today.
  - The Pitch Deck Builder's count is `SLIDE_META.length`, eleven today.
  - The canvas's "Nine" for the scoring engine was wrong, so neither count is typed.
- **The DNS table is the Worker's own records.** It shows what `cloudflare-worker/src/services/licenceDomain.ts` asks a white-label to publish and then checks (D197, D449):
  - `CNAME app.yourterritory.com → cname.os.axal.vc` (`CNAME_TARGET`);
  - `TXT _axal-challenge.app.yourterritory.com → axal-verify=` and the licence's token (`TXT_PREFIX`, `recordsFor`).
  - The token is minted per licence, so the page names it rather than printing one.
  - "Two records. Your nameservers stay where they are." is true and stays.
  - A host that verifies does not serve until Cloudflare for SaaS is configured on `os.axal.vc` (see `licenceDomain.ts`). The page does not claim that it serves.
- **"See the console" is an anchor to `#admin-console`.** It smooth-scrolls when motion is allowed, and the section keeps clear of the fixed nav with `scroll-margin-top`.

### What was dropped, and why

Each drop below is a claim the code does not make true. It was removed, not reworded.

- **Platform, in the module and layer copy:**
  - Layer 01's "measured rather than claimed": the skills radar is the mean of the founder's own self-ratings (`SpinoutLabProfilingPage.jsx`).
  - Profiling's "blend with confidence bands": one archetype is stored, with no runner-up or blend, and the per-axis band is evidence coverage.
  - The Scoring Engine's "Nine … with evidence provenance per score":
    - the engine has six weighted dimensions;
    - its run integrity is run provenance, which the page refuses to call evidence.
  - Capital's "warm-intro routing" and "SAFE generation":
    - the Capital tool omits warm intros;
    - a SAFE is a record of terms until counsel's text is registered (D578).
- **Platform, in the data flows.** Three flows are kept, each traced in the code:
  - discovery interviews feed the deck's problem slide (`services/decks/axalSpinoutDemoDay.ts`);
  - the Use of Funds allocation feeds the deck's use-of-funds block (`services/decks/spinoutDeckData.ts`);
  - profiling skill gaps feed advisor and co-founder matching (`skillComplementarity` in `routes/advisors.ts` and `routes/cofounder.ts`).
- **Platform, the flows and sentences dropped:**
  - "Cap table stock issuance → 83(b) filing deadline", and "Their cap table drives their 83(b) deadline":
    - the deadline is computed from the transfer date the founder types on the 83(b) page (`routes/legal_83b.ts`, the only `electionDeadline` call);
    - the cap table supplies the share count only.
  - "+ LP report" on the use-of-funds flow: no LP report reads the allocation.
  - "Revenue proof grading → Scoring Engine traction dimension":
    - the engine has no traction dimension;
    - it does not read the revenue ledger.
  - "Their scoring dimensions select their advisor": the advisor match is domain overlap, values alignment and skill complement.
- **Administrators:**
  - Contracts' "KYB, and conflicts": the Contracts place lists agreements and holds no KYB or conflict record.
  - Settings' "sets its own mark, colours, and powered-by", the Mark, Colours and Powered-by chips, and the same clause in "Who approves our brand?":
    - the administrator binds the hostname (`DomainWizard`);
    - the mark and colours are captured by Super Admin at issue (`routes/admin_licences.ts`, D198), and the branch-side editor is filed, not built;
    - no powered-by setting exists.
  - "The territory binds its own hostname" and "Axal VC does not approve a white-label's brand" stay, because both are true.
- **Option B's figures:** the Fund I line and philosophy item 04, as above.
