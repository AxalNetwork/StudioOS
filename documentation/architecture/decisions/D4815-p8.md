### What is drawn

`/deals/closing` (`frontend/src/pages/investor/deals/ClosingZone.jsx`) is a list by deal, not by document.

- **The strip is DE08's five figures:** Deals at closing, Paper executed, Signatures outstanding, Blocking conditions, Transfers recorded. Each figure has a state: recorded, not recorded, unreadable or loading. A figure whose read failed says Unreadable and names the read (for example "the signature archive could not be read"). A read still in flight says Reading…. Neither prints 0.
- **One row per deal at closing.** Each row shows the investment, closing progress ("6 of 8"), signatures outstanding, open conditions, a teal-edged transfer tag ("Transfer recorded" or "Transfer not recorded"), and Open. Each part of a row keeps its own state, so a failed paper read leaves the conditions count readable.
- **Empty:** "No deals are currently in closing." with "Closing tracks documents, conditions and recorded transfer status."
- **Paper reads in the agreement lifecycle's words.** The Documents view's state pill, its column and the CSV export say Executed for a completed envelope, "Sent · awaiting signature" for a sent one, Prepared and Voided. These are the `AGREEMENT_STATES` labels. Declined keeps its own word, as Closings does.

### Which read feeds what

| Figure or column | Read | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deals at closing | `GET /api/deals?scope=mine` | `dealStage(d) === 'closing'` |
| Paper executed | the deals and `GET /api/legal/esign` | completed envelopes of all envelopes on those deals |
| Signatures outstanding (strip and row) | the deals and the e-sign list | recipients less signatures on paper that is `sent`. An envelope with no recipient recorded is not counted, and the note says how many there are |
| Blocking conditions / open conditions | the deals and `GET /api/ic/conditions?status=open` | open conditions on those deals |
| Transfers recorded / transfer state | the deals and `GET /api/deals/transfers` | recorded transfers on those deals |
| Investment | the deal list's `capital_committed` and `currency` | the round's confirmed commitments, through `money.js` (`majorToMinor`, `formatMinor`). With none confirmed, or no currency, it reads Not recorded |
| Closing progress | `GET /api/deals/:id/closing-checklist`, one read per deal at closing | items done of items not skipped. With no checklist applied, or one with no items, it reads Not recorded |

### Departures

- **Investment is the round's confirmed commitments, not the reader's own.** The deal list carries `capital_committed` and no per-investor amount. `GET /api/deals/:id/commitments` would be a second read per deal, so this part does not add it.
- **Open goes to the deal page, `/deals/:id`.** The closing workspace (DE08b, `/deals/:id/closing`) is the next part of DX8 and has no route yet.
- **The checklist editor stays under the list** ("Closing checklists"), with its apply, add, done and block controls. It belongs to the DE08b workspace, and it stays here until that workspace exists, so the redraw removes no capability.
- **The chips and the ops row stay.** This close is the deal list and counts deals. Documents is the paper table and counts envelopes. Blocking and Wires are unchanged. Each chip drops its count when its read failed. The Funds moved tile is replaced by Transfers recorded, and the recorded amounts stay in the Wires view.
