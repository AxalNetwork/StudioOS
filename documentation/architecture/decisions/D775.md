## D775 — The Super Admin's boards draw the new rail: a rail on every board, and every option each board offers listed for D764

- **Status:** decided by S1 for the owner, 2026-10-19 (#2101; rollout M5 of D720 §11, #1874). PR 1 wrote §1. The per-section PRs (page keys, crumbs and `useRailOption` calls, stacked on D768, #2092) add §2 onwards.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `4f7816e8`. Frontend paths are relative to `frontend/src/`.
- **The boards:** the canvas's `Super Admin` profile (`design/incoming/airail-every-page-data/canvas.json`), 10 sections and 27 modules, numbered H1–H37 in the canvas's own order. D720 §11's table pins the numbering: H11 is Users and H12–H17 are Revenue and its five modules.

### 1. The missing rails and the options inventory (PR 1)

1. **The inventory is `lib/railOptions/superAdmin.js`,** under the folder's rules (`lib/railOptions/README.md`). It has two lists.
   - **`SUPER_ADMIN_BOARDS`:** one row per board:
     - `id`, `section` and `module`, the canvas's;
     - `route`, where the board is drawn today;
     - `files`, the components it renders;
     - `rail`, the file that mounts its `<WorkerRail>`, a parent's where the board is a tab. When there is none yet, `rail` is null and `railPending` gives the reason.
   - **`SUPER_ADMIN_RAIL_OPTIONS`:** one row per control that runs a model, in the siblings' shape: `{page, key, label, kind, handler, source: {file, match}}`.
     - `page` is the canvas board name.
     - `kind` is one of the canvas's fourteen kinds.
2. **One control on the 37 boards runs a model.** It is H20 Publications' "Draft with AI" (`draft:publication-summary`, kind `write`, handler `draft`). It calls `POST /api/admin/publications/draft` → `draftSummary` → the router.
   - **The uploads only store a file**, so under the folder's rule they are not rows:
     - H1's brand mark;
     - the photos on H23 and H25;
     - H34's post media;
     - H37's headshot;
     - H37's LinkedIn PDF, whose import is a deterministic parser.
   - **Monitoring's anomaly panel (H33) is not an option.** It calls `env.AI.run` from `GET /api/monitoring/anomalies` on mount and every 60 s, outside the router, with no budget row. Moving it onto the run path is D780's (#2105); this PR does not touch it.
3. **The missing rails.** 16 boards drew no rail.
   - **PR 1 adds 7**, each in `RailFrame` and named for its board:
     - H1 Licences;
     - H19 Content queue;
     - H20 Publications;
     - H25 Public team page;
     - H31 Credit rules;
     - H33 Monitoring;
     - H34 Telegram channels.
   - **H20 is one board on three routes.** `/admin/publications`, `/new` and `/:id` share `pages/admin/PublicationsFrame.jsx`, so the board draws one rail under one name.
   - **The tier.** Where a plain admin also reaches the page (H19, H20, H25, H33 and H34), the rail takes the viewer's tier, `super_admin` or `admin`, as `ContractsDesk` does. H1 and H31 are `hqOnly`, so their rail is HQ's.
4. **Nine boards wait, with the reason in `railPending`.**
   - **Eight are `/admin` tabs:** H11 Users, H22 Personas, H23 Advisors and partners, H24 Directory, H27 Integration keys, H28 GitHub sync, H29 Payments catalogue and H30 Promo codes.
     - `pages/AdminPage.jsx` also hosts the Admin profile's boards (D774, #2100), and neither issue owns it.
     - A frame added there twice would conflict, so S1 is asked on #2101 which PR adds the one tab-keyed frame.
   - **H21 Assessment Studio** shares `/admin/assessment` with the Admin profile's Assessment runs. Under D720 §11, the board the canvas lists first owns the route, and that is the Admin profile's.
5. **The guard is `frontend/test/rail_options_super_admin_d775.test.mjs`.** It checks:
   - the boards against the canvas: H1–H37, in its order and under its names;
   - every board file exists, and every rail file mounts a rail;
   - a pending board really mounts none;
   - each added rail sits in `RailFrame` with its board's name and a tier;
   - each row's shape and kind, and that its `match` appears exactly once in one of its board's files;
   - **parity:** every draft band or draft call in a board's files is a row, and every row matches one.

   Seven mutations each fail it:
   - dropping the row;
   - a kind that is not one of the fourteen;
   - two boards swapped;
   - a new unlisted draft call on a board;
   - a rail taken out of its frame;
   - a Publications route without the frame;
   - a row pointing at a file the control is not in.

**Rollback:** revert the PR. The seven pages lose their rail, and nothing else reads the inventory yet.

### 2. The Content section (PR 2, H18–H25)

D768's shell provider (#2133) gives every rail its page key from the route, so each Content board's key already comes from `lib/railPages.js`. Publications' `/new` and `/:id` answer `Super Admin · Content · Publications`, as its list does. PR 2 adds the two things the provider cannot know.

1. **Record crumbs.** A record view names its record as the last crumb and as the run's `record`, through `pages/admin/useRecordCrumb.js`. That hook is CycleRoute's `RecordCrumb` contract, written as a hook. Leaving the record, or the page, drops it. Three views use it:
   - the article under review on H19 (`article`);
   - a publication on H20 (`publication`);
   - a team member being edited on H25 (`team-member`). A new member has no record yet.
2. **H20's option.** `/admin/publications/new` is the Draft with AI form, so the page opens `draft:publication-summary` for as long as it is shown (`useRailOption`). A pick on the rail then applies before the press. Carrying the rail context on `POST /api/admin/publications/draft` is D780's (#2105).
3. **No other Content option.** H21 Assessment Studio is S09's route. H22–H24 are `/admin` tabs, which wait on S1's answer about `AdminPage.jsx`. H18 Content draws no record view.
4. **The guard is `frontend/test/rail_content_super_admin_d775.test.mjs`.** It checks:
   - each board's route answers its own key;
   - each record view's call and record type;
   - each Content row is opened once, with the inventory's key and label;
   - a render under the provider: the record is the last crumb and the run's record, and it is dropped on leaving.

   Six mutations each fail it:
   - the option call dropped;
   - its label changed;
   - the article crumb dropped;
   - the cleanup dropped;
   - the run's record dropped;
   - a crumb for a member not yet saved.
