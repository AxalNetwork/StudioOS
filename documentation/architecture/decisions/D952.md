## D952 — Firm links move to Advisors & Partners › Firms, and the old address redirects (#2728)

- **Status:** S23, 2026-10-24. Part 9 of Partner invitations (#2370). Renumbered from D932, which S21's Community work took.
- **Canvas:** "Partner invitations" (`design/incoming/`). Its DO NOT DRAW list takes the firm-link tool off the desk. Its Overview keeps one Needs-you row about unattached partner accounts. Advisors & Partners' Firms tab (D972, #2369) is where firms live.
- **Migration:** none. No Worker route changes: `GET` and `POST /api/admin/partners/links` are the same calls from a new page.
- **Builds on:** D923 (the desk, and Firm links at its own address with a confirm), D972 (Advisors & Partners and its Firms tab), #2301 (the redirect registry).

### What changed

1. **The tool moved.** `frontend/src/pages/admin/partners/FirmLinks.jsx` is now `frontend/src/pages/admin/advisors/FirmLinks.jsx`, drawn on the Firms tab below the firms table as "Attach accounts to firms".
   - Its behaviour is D923's, unchanged. A select only asks. The confirm says what the change does. The save is the confirm's button, and its result is a line on the page, never a toast. A refusal stays in the dialog.
   - The "first 500" wording comes with it: "Counted over the first 500 partner accounts." Nothing pages the list yet.
   - Its copy and helpers (`FIRM_LINKS_NOTE`, `firmChangeCopy`, `firmCounts`, `firmSavedLine`, `refusalText`) and its read states stay in `partners/partnersDeskModel.js` and `partners/parts.jsx`, imported from there. One rule, one place.
   - It reads the Firms tab's existing `links` read. The tab still makes exactly its three reads.
2. **The Firms strip points at the section below.** "Partner accounts with no firm: n" now links `#ap-firm-links` ("Attach below"), not the old address.
3. **The old address redirects.** `/admin/partner-invitations/firm-links` forwards to `/admin/advisors-partners/firms`, keeping the query and hash and adding `?moved=firm-links`.
   - The Firms tab then shows "Firm links moved here: attach a partner account to its firm under Firms."
   - The forward is done by `advisors/FirmLinksRedirect.jsx`, unguarded like every redirect; the page it lands on is guarded.
   - It is listed in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/redirectRoutes.ts`, so #2301 counts it.
4. **Partner invitations stops drawing it.** `PartnerInvitations.jsx` and `partnersDeskModel.js` drop the `firm-links` tab. `FIRM_LINKS_PATH` becomes `FIRMS_TAB_PATH`.
   - The Overview's Needs-you row about unattached accounts now reads "Open Firms" and links the Firms tab, written out as a literal door.
   - Advisors & Partners' own Needs-you row does the same.
5. **The partner-facing sentences name the new place.**
   - `partner/kit.jsx`: "An admin can attach it under Advisors & Partners → Firms."
   - `partner/PartnerFirmProfileCard.jsx`: the same correction to the same stale sentence, which #2728 did not list.

### A choice to review

Advisors & Partners' README said no email reaches the page. The attach table names an account by its email when the account has no name, as it did on Partner invitations. Without it, a nameless account could not be told apart from another. Those emails come from the `links` read the Firms tab already made, admin-only as before. The README now records this as the one exception. The page file and `apModel.js` still never touch an email (`admin_advisors_partners_d972.test.mjs`).
