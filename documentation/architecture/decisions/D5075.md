## D5075 — Raise on a phone: Cap table and Use of funds without sideways tables (#5075)

- **Status:** Part 1 built, 2026-11-23 (frontend only). No route, no Worker change, no migration. The Data Room part waits for its phone frame.
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Raise v2 Page.dc.html` and `Raise v2 · Boards.dc.html` (D4863), board RA-26 "Mobile, per module": B (Cap table: Recorded ownership, instruments and a Modelled scenario as cards, with View table) and C (Use of funds: what the round buys and runway, Recorded against Modelled).
- **Builds on:** D1028 (the Cap table on a phone: holder cards, a holder as a page, scenarios one a card, the 44px root), D1023 (Instruments), D6083 (the section and hire sheets), D5081 (Home owns the Use of funds page, Runway recorded against modelled).
- **Released by:** the bounded release on #5075 of 2026-11-23, after #5074 closed.

### What was missing on a phone

D1028 and D5081 had already drawn most of RA-26 B and C. Three places still showed a desktop table as the phone's main view:

1. **Cap table › Instruments.** The record's SAFEs and notes were an 11-column table (`RecordTable`). Convert at's Modelled result was a 4-column table, and its as-converted bars used a 240px + 120px grid that left no room for the bar at 390px.
2. **Cap table › Overview.** The holder cards had no way to reach the holders' table, which carries Source and Last change. The cards leave those out.
3. **Use of funds › Home.** The section table (`min-w-[720px]`) was hidden on a phone, and nothing replaced it. The stack bar's legend gave share and amount but not the monthly figure, milestone or hires, and no section could be opened from Home.

### What a phone draws now

- **Instruments:** one card per instrument on record. The holder comes first and opens the instrument sheet. Then the type and status. Every table column is a labelled line, in the table's order: amount, cap, discount, MFN, basis with its Check note, interest and maturity (notes only), signed, document or "! No document". The source and the On record tag come last. Empty and filtered results keep the table's own sentences.
- **Convert at (Modelled):** one card per instrument, with conversion price, which price won, shares, and % after the round. An instrument that did not convert says why, as the table does. The bars stack: the label and value sit on one line, and the bar spans the line below.
- **View table:** under the instrument cards, under the Modelled cards and under the holder cards. It is a real button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. It opens the detailed table below the cards, and that table may scroll sideways inside its own box. On Overview, the opened table does not draw the Count and Group by switches again.
- **Use of funds Home:** "What the raise buys" draws one card per section, below the stack bar. Each card shows the share, then labelled lines for amount, a month over the target, the first milestone and its date, and hires. The section's name is a 44px link to its sheet (D6083). Runway recorded against modelled stays below (D5081).

Values come from the same model functions as the tables (`recordInstruments`, `convertView`, `holderCards`/`whoOwns`, `sectionRows`, `money`), so no figure is reformatted or rounded for the phone. Not recorded and Unreadable stay words, never 0. Long names and figures wrap (`overflow-wrap:anywhere`) instead of widening the page. Desktop layouts are unchanged.

### Accessibility

Cards are lists (`ul`/`li` with an `aria-label`), and their figures are `dl` pairs, so a screen reader hears each term before its value, in the table's column order. On the Cap table, targets are 44px from the D1028 root (`captable-phone`). On Use of funds, the section link carries `min-h-[44px]`. The focus ring is the global `:focus-visible` one.
