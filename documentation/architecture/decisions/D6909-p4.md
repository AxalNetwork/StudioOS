## D6909 part 4 — Eadwyn's drafts on a mark request: the investor's reason and HQ's basis and source, never a value (#6909)

- **Status:** built by S10, 2026-11-18. Part 4 of #6909, after `D6909.md`, `D6909-p2.md` and `D6909-p3.md`. No migration.
- **Canvas:** PRM20 (the reason draft in Prepare) and PRM22 (the proposal above Record a mark) of `design/incoming/Portfolio v2 Rights and Request a Mark Page.dc.html` (#6228).

### What Eadwyn does here

Eadwyn drafts and the member decides. Nothing a draft does sends a request or records a mark.

- **The investor's reason (PRM20).** In the request's Prepare step, *Ask Eadwyn to draft the reason* reads the company's latest mark (its day, basis, event and age) and the latest report the investor is granted. Eadwyn drafts one or two sentences and lists the sources it read. The sources come from the data, not from the model.
  - *Accept into reason* fills the reason field.
  - *Edit*, then *Keep my edit*, fills it with the investor's words.
  - *Discard* leaves the field as it was.
  - Eadwyn never sends a request: the investor still reviews and sends it.
- **HQ's basis and source (PRM22).** On a request HQ opened from the queue (D6909 part 3), a panel above the mark's write flow asks Eadwyn for a basis, a source and an as-of day. It never asks for a value.
  - *Accept into the mark* reopens Prepare with those three fields filled, and the value is left blank for HQ to enter. HQ still reviews, confirms and records the mark.
  - *Discard* leaves Prepare as it was.
  - A mark opened from the company's sheet has no panel and no seed.

### What Eadwyn reads

Never a figure: no fair value, no post-money, no invested amount, no KPI, and no report body.

- **The reason draft.**
  - The latest mark's day, basis and event.
  - The latest submitted report the investor is granted, using the same grant as the updates history (`INVESTOR_GRANT`, written out as literal SQL): its period and day only.
  - The request kind and today's date.
- **The basis draft.**
  - The latest mark's day, basis, event and source.
  - Up to three submitted reports' periods and days.
  - Up to five rounds' names and days.
  - The request's kind and reason. These are the investor's words, so they go in the untrusted block and the prompt says never to follow them.

### The run and its checks

- **Quoted first.** Each draft is one `rail_reason` run, quoted beforehand. The press carries `confirm_quote`, a digest of the member, the record, the input, the model and the hold. A missing or stale one gets 402 `quote_required`, and the model is not called.
- **A reason is kept only if it passes these checks:**
  - plain words of at most 400 characters;
  - no money (a currency symbol or word, `million`, or a thousands-separated number);
  - no email, no link, and not the words the product never uses.
- **A basis is kept only if all of these hold:**
  - the basis is one a mark may carry (`round_price`, `secondary`, `gp_estimate`, `write_down` or `cost`, as `positions.ts`);
  - the source passes the same word checks;
  - the as-of day is one of the facts' own days and not after today.

  A draft that names `fmv`, `value` or `post_money` is dropped whole.
- **Accept.**
  - A reason accept takes an optional edit of up to the request's own 2,000 characters, with no email or link.
  - A basis accept takes an optional basis, source or day, each checked the same way. A value sent with Accept is ignored.
  - What was kept is stored on the proposal as `accepted` or, when changed, `edited`.
- **One draft per kind and record.** A new draft withdraws the member's open draft of the same kind on the same record.

### Store and gates

- **Store.** Rows go in `ai_rail_proposals` with page `Portfolio · Positions · Mark requests` and option `markreq:reason` or `markreq:basis`. The record is `mark-request-draft:<projectId>` or `mark-request:<requestUid>`. The surface is `AI_SURFACE.portfolioPositions` (`/portfolio/positions`, new, listed in D552).
- **Reason gate.** An investor with the licence, not an admin, for a company they hold (`investorHeldProjectIds`, the request route's own scope). Not held and not found answer alike, with 403 `not_held`.
- **Basis gate.** HQ (`requireAdmin`), on an open request. An answered request gets 409 `not_open_request`.
- **Ownership.** A draft is its proposer's own: another member's, even another admin's, is 409 `not_open`.
- **Generic route.** The generic `/api/ai/rail/proposals/:uid/decide` can only discard a `markreq:` row, as with `dataroom:` and `share:` rows.

### Reads and writes

- `POST /api/mark-requests/drafts/quote`: takes `{ kind: 'reason', project_uid, request_kind }` or `{ kind: 'basis', request_uid }`.
- `POST /api/mark-requests/drafts/run`: takes the same body plus `confirm_quote`, and answers `{ draft | null, cost }`.
- `POST /api/mark-requests/drafts/:uid/accept`: takes the same scope fields plus `{ reason? }` or `{ basis?, source?, as_of_date? }`, and answers `{ draft, kept }`.
- `POST /api/mark-requests/drafts/:uid/discard`.
- Route: `routes/mark_request_drafts.ts`. Service: `services/markRequestDrafts.ts`.
- Frontend:
  - `pages/portfolio/MarkDrafts.jsx` (`ReasonDraft`, `BasisProposal`) and `markDraftsModel.js`;
  - `WriteFlow` takes an optional `seed` that starts Prepare filled and counts as a draft;
  - wiring in `MarkRequestPanel.jsx` and `PortfolioPositionsPage.jsx`.

### Where this departs from the canvas

- **No *Edit* on HQ's proposal.** PRM22 draws Accept, Edit and Discard. Accept only fills Prepare, where every field is HQ's to change before Review, so Prepare is where the edit happens.
