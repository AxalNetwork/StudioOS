## D6269 — The investor's room list drawn to the Investor Research v2 Rooms canvas (#6269)

- **Status:** built. `/research/diligence` follows boards IRV-02 (tiles, rows, Requests block), IRV-05 (states) and IRV-06 (phone) of `design/incoming/Investor Research v2 Rooms Page.dc.html` (D6268, owner answers A in `D6268-p2.md`).
- **Builds on:** `GET /api/research/diligence` with `requests`, `requests_unreadable` and each room's `nda` (D6267, #6510) and `deals` (D6266, #6379). No Worker change, no migration and no new API method.
- **Owner answer kept:** A on D6268 and #3701: rooms stay in Research and in Deals. There is no Ask over a room.

### What the page shows

- **Tiles.** Rooms granted, Partly staged and Requests open. A measured 0 is a figure. A failed request read makes the Requests open tile Unavailable; it never shows 0.
- **Each room row.** It shows:
  - open to you, in the room and behind an NDA, as separate numbers, never a ratio;
  - the NDA with the founder: NDA signed (with its end date), NDA out for signature or No NDA, and Not recorded for a status the read does not carry;
  - the deal's stage with Open the deal (`RoomDeals`), and Unavailable when the deal join failed;
  - when the room was granted;
  - when you last opened it, or Never opened;
  - the expiry, marked "Expires in N days" within 14 calendar days (UTC), or No end date.
- **Requests block.** Your open requests and the declined ones, each with the date sent. A declined request shows the date decided and the founder's reason, or Not recorded when the founder gave none. A request whose company you could not already see is not named (D6267).
- **Phone.** Below `lg` each row stacks into a two-column card with labelled cells. The header row is desktop-only, and Open the room is a 44px target.

### The chips select real states

`All`, `Partly staged` (rooms with files behind an NDA), `Requested` (your open requests) and `Declined` (the ones a founder declined), each with its count.

- `Granted` is no chip any more. Every room listed is a live grant, so it is the Rooms granted tile's count.
- `Not staged` is gone too. A company that never opened a room leaves no row to select.
- Both labels stay on the old IR2 artboard, and `profile_zone_filters.test.mjs` records the swap.

### New request is withheld

A request is made on its deal (Documents, then Ask for access), where the founder can answer it. This list has no one deal to ask on, and the v2 canvas draws no such control, so it is not drawn rather than drawn disabled. `profile_zone_actions.test.mjs` lists it under `withheld`. Attach to deal stays as it was (D6266); it cites no issue.

### States are #5129's

#5129 is built (D5129, D5129-p2). The page uses `DataState` and `StateChip` from `ui/Honesty.jsx`:

| State | What the page draws |
|---|---|
| Loading | The block skeleton |
| Empty | None, in a sentence |
| A value the store does not hold | Not recorded |
| A failed read | Unavailable, with Retry, the default word for a new surface |
| A 403 or 404 | Refused, with Go to your deals |

The page no longer uses the expertise kit's `ZoneBody`, `NothingYet` or `Unrecorded`.

### Export

The Export columns are Company, Open to you, In the room, Behind an NDA, NDA with founder, NDA valid until, Deal stage, Granted, You last opened and Access expires (`diligenceZoneRead.js`). A missing figure exports as its state word, never 0.
