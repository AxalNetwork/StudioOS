### 1. The Inbox (APR1) is HQ's inbox read

Before this part the Inbox was one row per lane from the Studio glance. Now it lists every waiting item from `GET /api/admin/approvals`.

- **The table.**
  - **Columns:** a checkbox, Waiting (the age with the band pill), Lane (with its group), Who, What, Assigned, Decided on, and ⋯.
  - **Who** links to the item. **Decided on** is the lane's door, `LaneDoor` (kept literal) or `HqDoor` for a Super Admin's HQ lanes.
  - **Assigned** reads "Unassigned" only when the Worker says nobody. When the assignment record was not read, the Worker leaves `assignee` out and the cell says "Not read".
  - **The ⋯ menu:** Open, Assign… and Copy link.
- **The bulk bar.** "3 selected", Assign… and Clear, over the checked rows.
- **Filters live in the address.** Group chips with counts, Age (Any, Under 24 hours, 24 to 72 hours, Over 72 hours), Assigned (Anyone, Me, Unassigned, then each admin), Sort (Waiting longest, Newest, Lane, Who) and search.
  - The page sends exactly what the Worker reads (D1132). Search is sent from two characters. A value the Worker would refuse is dropped rather than sent.
  - A new filter starts at page 1.
- **Pages.** "1–25 of 133". When a lane failed it reads "of 123 +". When the total is withheld it reads "of —".
- **The four tiles.**
  - Waiting, from `unfiltered_total`, with "across 20 lanes" (Super Admin: "· 22 for HQ").
  - Over 72 hours, with the oldest lane from the glance.
  - Assigned to you.
  - Decided this week, from the weekly read, with the median wait.

  A tile the Worker withheld reads "Could not be read" with its reason, never 0.
- **Waiting by lane.** One stacked bar per lane, split into the age bands: sky under 24 hours, amber 24 to 72, violet over 72, and age not recorded. The worst lane comes first. A lane that failed, or was cut at the read's cap, is a row that says so, never an empty bar.
- **Arrived and decided, last 8 weeks.** Two lines, with this week drawn as to date. Its foot names the lanes Decided leaves out ("Wellbeing experts, Exploring do not record when they were decided…").
- **Charts.** Each chart has "View as table" and a one-sentence summary as its `role="img"` label.
- **The partial note.** "LP applications could not be read, so its items are missing below. The other 19 lanes are complete." with Try again.
- **Waiting on HQ.** This studio's open requests to HQ, each with when it was sent and how long ago.
  - An account that administers no licence (404 `no_licence`) "asks HQ nothing here", never "0".
- **HQ decides band.** For a Super Admin, from the glance's `hq_decides`: "HQ decides · 22 waiting · only Super Admins see this group". The HQ group chip is added too.

### 2. The item (APR3) has its own address

- **The address** is `/admin/approvals/items/<lane>-<id>`, drawn as a Lightbox over the Inbox.
- **Why one segment.** The reachability walk resolves a literal template only one segment past its static head. Lane keys never hold a hyphen, so the id is what follows the last one.
- **Every way in is a literal template.** For example: ``<Link to={`/admin/approvals/items/${r.lane}-${r.id}${loc.search}`}>``. The filters ride along, so closing returns to the same list without reading it again.
- **What the drawer shows.**
  - The person's name, and pills for the lane and the wait.
  - Submitted, Profile, Item ID and Lane.
  - Assigned, with Assign….
  - History ("Assigned to Admin A by Admin B · 21 Oct 2026").
  - Close, and "Open in [the lane's page]".
  - The foot: "This lane is decided on its own page, where its rules and messages live."
- **When the item cannot be shown.**
  - 404 `item_not_waiting`: the Worker's sentence, with "Back to the inbox".
  - 404 `item_not_found`: "That item is not on this inbox."
  - Anything else: "This item could not be read.", with Try again and "Details · HTTP 503 · lane_unreadable".
- **Focus.** Escape and Close navigate back to the Inbox. The Lightbox gives focus back to the row link that opened it.

### 3. Assign (APR3a)

- **The dialog.**
  - Title: "Assign [X]’s identity check", or "Assign 3 items".
  - "They see it under Assigned to me. Assigning decides nothing."
  - One radio per active admin: "[Admin A] · you · 14 now".
  - Cancel and Assign. Assign is disabled until someone is chosen.
- **What it sends.** `POST /api/admin/approvals/assignments` with `{ assignee_user_id, items: [{ lane, item_id }] }`.
- **No note.** The canvas shows a note field; the Worker has none, so none is drawn.
- **After it answers.** Either way the list is read again. Success says "Assigned to [name]."
- **A refusal is a toast (APR13c).** It carries the Worker's own sentence and "The list is up to date now." Nothing on the page uses `alert` or `confirm`.

### 4. Lanes (APR2, APR9)

- **Notifies.** Each lane gets a Notifies column from the glance (D1041). "Nobody yet" is an amber pill. A glance without the field reads "Not read here yet".
- **HQ decides.** For a Super Admin, the HQ group now has Waiting and Oldest from `hq_decides`. A lane that failed reads "Could not be read". A lane off the glance reads "Not read here yet".
- **The tab count.** Lanes shows 20, or 29 for a Super Admin.

### 5. Decided (APR10)

- **The read.** `GET /api/admin/approvals/decided`, filtered in the address by lane, outcome, from and to, newest first, 25 a page, "1–25 of 357".
- **The table.** Decided, Lane, Who, the store's own outcome word, Decided by, Wait, and Note. A note the person is shown carries "◉ They see this".
- **What the log cannot say, above the table.**
  - A lane that records only from a day: "Wellbeing experts: decisions were not recorded before 21 Oct 2026."
  - Each lane that does not record, with why.
  - Each lane's caveat.
  - Each lane that failed.
- **The tab count** is the total when the read answered. Otherwise it is a dash with why, never 0.

### 6. States (APR11–APR14)

- **APR11a:** "Nothing is waiting on you." with "See what was decided". A filtered empty list says "Nothing matches these filters." with Clear filters.
- **APR12:** a skeleton for each tile, chart and table while it reads.
- **APR13b:** "We could not load Approvals." with Try again and "Details · HTTP 503 · assigned_unreadable", never an empty inbox.
- **APR13c:** the refusal toast in §3.
- **APR14a:** an Admin on an HQ lane's link meets "This lane belongs to the Super Admin." with "Show my inbox".
- **APR14b:** a non-admin on any `/admin` path meets RoleGuard's "This page is for studio admins" (D940). It is pinned here, not rebuilt.
