### What the canvas decides

- Markets has two tabs, Market directory and Listed companies. Government ownership becomes a coverage note that says why it is not available.
- A labelled "Search markets or sectors" field, then Country, Source freshness, Source type, Sort and View.
- Each row shows the market, its tracked sector, the taxonomy company count (labelled "not market size"), countries represented, distinct sources and the latest source with its age. Sector-level sources and sources naming the market are told apart.
- A missing value reads Not recorded with the Worker's reason. Unmapped markets stay in the list.
- Table, Cards and Analysis carry the same columns. Analysis is dated coverage of all markets, with no size or growth.
- `tab`, `view`, `page` and `open` live in the URL. Back from a sector page names the view, reopens the same row and puts focus on it.
- Investors see their profile thesis beside the results (a compact section above them on phone). "Matches your thesis sectors" appears only on a real sector match; no thesis and an unavailable thesis are drawn, and the directory stays usable. Readings are hidden for investors (RS-17).
- Export view and New market research replace the unbuilt toolbar actions.
