## D656 — The four cycle pages and Validate's interview session, wired to real stores

**Issue:** #1469 (tracking, S1). **Decided by:** the owner, 2026-10-09.
**Canvases:** `design/incoming/Cycle Overview.dc.html` (+ `pro-cycles.js`), its three boards, `Interview Session.dc.html`, `Recording View.dc.html` (+ `ring-viz.js`) and its board, committed by #1468.

### What was decided

**Validate, Build, Raise and Grow each get a new overview, and each module gets its own page.**
- **The overview** shows the cycle's question, four headline figures with sparklines, then one chart card per module in tab order. It has no module list, no status pills and no Pro strip. The old overviews listed every module twice, once as a list and once as cards.
- **A module page** shows the module's chart at full size, its records as a working table, then its further parts.

**Validate's "Record a session" gets voice recognition and speech-to-text on Cloudflare Workers AI.**
- The channels are in person, video call, phone call, text chat and manual entry.
- The rail picks the live model, the final model and the structuring model.
- The output feeds every Validate module, through proposals the founder accepts, edits or discards.

**The owner's standing rules for this work:**
- the backend and the wiring are built around these UIs;
- **the Spin-Out Lab's work carries over to these modules.**

### The read every cycle shares

```
GET /api/cycles/:cycle/overview?project=:pid
GET /api/cycles/:cycle/modules/:slug?project=:pid&q=&filter=&sort=&page=
Figure = { key, label, value|null, unit, delta_30d|null, spark, state, source, as_of }
Card   = { slug, name, state, range, caption, series, next|null, reason|null }
state  ∈ ok | empty | too_little | not_recorded | unreadable
```

**How the read behaves:**
- **Each card is computed on its own.** A failing source makes that card `unreadable` and leaves the others alone.
- **A value nothing records is `null` with `not_recorded`, never 0.**
- **Fewer than three points is `too_little`,** and no line is drawn.
- **`services/cycleCards.ts`** holds the states and the types.
- **Each cycle's reads** live in their own `routes/cycle_<cycle>.ts`, mounted at `/api/cycles/<cycle>`, so no two slots edit the same route file.

### Why the Spin-Out Lab carries over

A Lab venture is an ordinary `projects` row, with no venture entity of its own.

**These modules read the Lab's project-keyed stores directly, with no migration:**
- interviews and pain;
- the ICP definition;
- market sizing;
- MVP scope and the roadmap;
- the revenue ledger, use of funds and the raise pipeline;
- the cap table, and scores.

**What the Lab computes only in the browser becomes stored,** so a founder and a Lab member see the same scores:
- the discovery funnel;
- the market-readiness checklist;
- the data-room readiness score.

**The IP and tech-transfer answers** are keyed by user. #1475 moves them onto the project.

**The Lab's pitch-deck builder** stamps every deck DRAFT for a founder outside the Lab. A missing Lab start date reads as programme day 0. #1476 fixes this.

### Speech-to-text and structuring

Every model is a Workers AI model:
- **The live transcript:** `@cf/deepgram/nova-3` or `@cf/deepgram/flux`, over a WebSocket the Worker opens with `env.AI.run(model, …, { websocket: true })`.
- **The final transcript:** `@cf/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo` by default, with Nova-3 batch, Whisper and Whisper Tiny EN as alternates.
- **Structuring:** `@cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct-fp8-fast` by default, with DeepSeek V4 Flash and Llama 3.1 8B.

**The rules on cost and consent:**
- Every run goes through `services/aiRouter.ts`, and every cost through the D640 budget.
- No audio is recorded until consent is recorded. The Worker refuses it with a 409.
- Nothing an interview produces enters a module without the founder's click. Proposals extend `validate_proposals` (migration 214); no second proposals table is added.
