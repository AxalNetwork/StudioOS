### 1. Versions and the executed agreement

**Agreement version** is a contract's place in instantiation order: the first is v1. The template's own version is a different number and is shown as the template's ("Template subsidiary-licence v7").

**The executed agreement** is the newest contract whose status is `signed`. Status follows the envelope when one exists (D451).
- A re-issue sets `superseded_at` on every earlier row, the executed one included (`POST /:uid/contract`).
- Until the new version is executed, the old one is still what the parties signed. L09 says so: "Until v5 is signed, v4 stays the executed agreement".
- `superseded_at` alone therefore does not end an executed agreement. A newer executed contract does.

Each version's state is one of:
- **Executed:** the newest executed contract.
- **Superseded:** an older executed contract, or a draft or sent one that a re-issue replaced before it was executed.
- **Void:** a voided contract.
- **Sent for signature:** a current contract out for signature.
- **Draft:** a current contract not sent yet.
