### 1. Migration 612: four stores, cents and the links

- **`closings`:** one closing of a deal. It holds:
  - its venture, round label, instrument, template slug and currency;
  - its status (`open`, `closed`, `cancelled`), owner and deadline;
  - the founder's cap-table decision, with who made it and when;
  - who closed it, and when.

  A partial unique index keeps **at most one open closing per deal**.
- **`closing_lines`:** one line per commitment, or per Raise prospect.
  - A CHECK requires one of the two.
  - Each line has its capital route (`direct`, `syndicate`, `axal_fund`, `third_party_fund`) and its amount in **integer cents above zero**, with a currency.
  - Each line has its envelope, `safe_issuances` uid, vehicle reference and status (`open`, `sent`, `received`, `withdrawn`).
  - One line per commitment or prospect per closing.
- **`closing_evidence`:** a piece of evidence on a line. It holds:
  - its kind and its side, receiving or sending;
  - its source reference and file key;
  - who recorded it and who accepted it;
  - its withdrawal: by whom, when, and the reason.
- **`closing_events`:** append-only. Two triggers refuse an UPDATE or a DELETE. The columns are id, closing, line, actor, event, source reference and time. **There is no column for an amount, a name or an account number.**
- **`commitments.amount_cents`:**
  - It is copied once by `CAST(ROUND(amount * 100) AS INTEGER)`, for an amount from 0 to ten trillion dollars.
  - Two triggers keep it in step on every insert and every change of `amount`, by the same rule. This is migration 607's pattern, so no writer in `deals.ts` changes.
  - A missing or out-of-range amount stays NULL, never 0.
  - `amount` is kept.
- **The links:** `closing_line_uid` on `fund_capital_calls`, `portfolio_positions`, `safe_issuances` and `deal_transfers`, each nullable and indexed. Existing rows keep NULL.

### 2. The rules (`services/closings.ts`)

- **Who reads what** (owner question 3):
  - an admin reads every closing;
  - the venture's founder (`projectOwnerScope`) reads every line and the events;
  - anyone else reads a closing only through a line of their own. They then read their own lines and the round's total (`total_cents`, every line not withdrawn), and no other line, name or amount, and no events;
  - any other caller gets the same 404 as a closing that does not exist.
- **Received** (owner question 4): a line reads `received` only on receiving-side evidence that is not withdrawn:
  - the company's Mercury (`company_mercury`) or an Axal entity's (`axal_mercury`);
  - an Admin's phone-verified record (`phone_verified_record`, a `deal_transfers` row on this deal with `phone_verified = 1`);
  - a bank confirmation file (`bank_confirmation`).

  The investor's own Mercury (`investor_mercury`) is the sending side. Alone it reads `sent`, "Sent, not yet confirmed". The side comes from the kind, never from the body. The status is recomputed from the evidence in the same batch as every evidence change.
- **A source names a stored record by its id:** `mercury:<id>`, `deal_transfer:<uid>` or `file:<key>`. Free text (a memo, an account number, a name) is refused, and the refusal echoes no input.
- **Every write appends exactly one event, in the same batch as the write.** A refused write appends none. An evidence event's source is `closing_evidence:<uid>`, never the bank reference.
- **The cap-table proposal:**
  - it is `not_ready` until every line is received or withdrawn, with at least one received;
  - it is then `proposed` until the venture's founder accepts or dismisses it. An admin or an investor is told "The founder decides the cap-table change.";
  - accepting records the decision only. The holder is written by the cap table's own path, which is part of #2432.
- **Complete** needs every line settled and the proposal decided. After that, every write on the closing is 409 `closing_not_open`.
- **The admin desk** (`GET /api/admin/closings`): every open closing, deadline first. Each row has its lines, lines waiting, total and received cents, the cap-table state, the owner, and the next actor:
  - `admin` to add lines, choose a route, confirm a sent line or close;
  - `investor` while a line has no evidence;
  - `founder` for the proposal.

### 4. The money gates, and the suites that moved

- **`POST /api/positions`** keeps `requireAdmin` and now asserts `position.record`. It accepts the `closing_line_uid` it records, which must be a line of a closing on that venture, else 404 `closing_line_not_found`.
- **`POST /api/deals/:id/transfers`:**
  - It refuses an Operating Partner with 403 `admin_records_transfers`: "An Admin records transfers. An Operating Partner ticks the closing checklist." Partners keep the checklist (owner question 6).
  - It asserts `closing.evidence`.
  - It accepts the `closing_line_uid` it serves, which must be a line of an open closing on that deal.
- **Three suites recorded transfers as a partner.** They record as an Admin now, and each gains a case refusing the partner:
  - `deal_closing_money`;
  - `deal_rooms_d630`;
  - `deal_transfer_firm_d467`.
- **D467's firm stamping is unchanged for an Admin in a firm.** Its cross-firm wire-gate cases changed meaning:
  - An Admin is unscoped in `icDecisionScope`, so every firm's open condition is counted in the refusal. The test now says so, and still checks that no condition's text crosses.
  - The gate's "a decision you cannot see" branch has no caller on this route any more. It stays in the code, because it is correct for any scoped caller.

### 5. Decided here: the admin page keeps its deal-id address

#3212 asked whether `/admin/contracts/closings/:id` becomes a closing uid.

**It does not, for now.** A deal has at most one open closing, so the deal id names the closing the desk works on. The desk and `GET /api/closings` give each closing's `deal_id`, so the page (D1213, #3210) reads the deal's open closing, and any closed one, by its deal.

No redirect is needed until a deal holds two closings the page must tell apart. A later part can move to the uid then, with the deal-id address redirecting.
