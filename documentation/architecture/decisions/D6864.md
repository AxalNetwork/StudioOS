### What was built

- **The pack is the founder's own preview projection.** `outsidePackView` (`services/raiseEvidence.ts`) is the reader the founder already previews as: invited, every folder, not committed. A pack therefore never shows more than an invited investor on Axal would see at the same point:
  - no Private item;
  - no unconsented quote;
  - no file the data room refuses;
  - no bank line, and no one investor's name or money.

  Before an NDA, the pack is coverage only. After an NDA, it carries summaries, a figure only for items shared After NDA, and Value withheld with its reason. An optional selection narrows the pack to the items the founder ticked, and never widens it.
- **An NDA outside Axal is the founder's statement.** An after-NDA pack needs `nda_confirmed: true` and a day that is not in the future. Both are stored, along with who confirmed.
- **A Mismatch carries the founder's note (Q7 A).** The note shows under a Mismatch only while it was written against the figure still stated (`note_value IS stated_value`), the same rule as D900. A Mismatch is never hidden.
- **The pack is frozen.** `snapshot_json` is what the reader sees, and it never changes. `pack_hash` is compared with the current pack for live links, so Shared links says "Changed since sent" and offers Make a new pack. The reader's copy does not change.
- **The link is a capability.**
  - The token is 32 random bytes; the store keeps only its SHA-256, as `captable_share_tokens` (migration 170) does.
  - Opening a link consumes one view in a single conditional UPDATE. The UPDATE checks that the link is not revoked, not expired, under its limit, and that its venture still exists.
  - An unknown or malformed link refuses with 404. An expired, used-up or revoked link refuses with 410, and each has its own page.
  - A refusal names neither the venture nor any evidence, and consumes nothing.
  - The read is rate-limited per IP (`evidence_share_read`, 30 a minute) and never cached.
- **Shared links (RA-29).** Every link the founder made is listed, newest first. Expired, used-up and revoked rows stay, in grey with a word and a glyph. Revoke is scope, review, result; the row stays.
- **Nothing is sent.** Making a link returns its address to the founder, who copies it. The composer hands off to Data room access for files.
- **Add evidence by hand (RA-32).** `POST /evidence/items` writes `kind = stated-by-founder` from `raise_evidence_typed`, with source Typed, strength Anecdote and sharing Private. Such an item is its own source:
  - `currentShared`, `live` and the founder's list take the stored row as current;
  - sharing it later is the founder's ordinary sharing decision;
  - a claim it backs stays Founder's figure.
- **Who can act.** Founders only. An admin, a Super Admin and capital staff get no Share action, and the routes refuse them with 403.

### Reads and writes

- `GET /api/cycles/raise/evidence/share-links`: the founder's list, with state, views, `changed_since_sent`, and the defaults and bounds.
- `POST /api/cycles/raise/evidence/share-links`: takes `{ label, reader, nda_confirmed?, nda_signed_on?, expires_in_days (1–90, 14), view_limit (1–100, 10), items? }`.
- `POST /api/cycles/raise/evidence/share-links/:uid/revoke`: refuses with 409 if the link is already stopped.
- `POST /api/cycles/raise/evidence/items`: takes `{ area, summary, value_num?, value_unit?, currency?, observed_on? }`.
- `GET /api/share/evidence/:token`: public.
- Store: `raise_evidence_share_links`, created by migration `202611161128_6864_raise_evidence_share_links.sql`.
