### 1. The metrics Sheet (`services/google/growFeeds.ts`)

A Sheet chosen for `revenue_traction` is mapped to columns, then each dated row lands in `project_metrics` with `source = 'google_sheets'`.

- **The mapping** names a column letter (A to Z) for `date` and for at least one figure. It may name a `tab`, and `header` (default yes). `PUT /api/connectors/google-workspace/files/:uid/mapping` checks a `revenue_traction` file against it.
  - The figures are `new_users`, `cac`, `ltv`, `headcount` and `nrr_pct`.
  - A column Stripe or Mercury already writes (`mrr`, `arr`, `monthly_churn_pct`, `active_users`, `paying_accounts`, `cash_in_cents`, `net_burn`, `cash_balance`) is refused with `mapping_column_not_allowed`.
  - Other refusals: `invalid_mapping`, `mapping_column_twice` and `mapping_needs_a_figure`.
- **Days:** `YYYY-MM-DD`, `YYYY/MM/DD`, or a month `YYYY-MM`, which lands on its last day (where Mercury's month rows sit). `10/03/2026` is not read, because which part is the month would be a guess.
- **Figures:**
  - A currency sign, thousands commas and a trailing % on `nrr_pct` are read.
  - `1.234,5` is not read.
  - Counts are whole, and nothing is negative.
- **Skipped rows:** every row not landed is named with its row number and reason: `not_a_date`, `date_repeated`, `no_figures`, `not_a_number` (with the field), or `date_in_another_sheet`. At most 500 rows are read, and `partial` says when there were more.
- **One row per day:** one source keeps one row per day. A second Sheet's row for a day an earlier Sheet wrote in the same sync is skipped.
- **The ledger:** each day's ledger id is `<drive_file_id>:<project_id>:<day>`, under module `revenue_and_traction`, kind `metric_day`.
  - Stop using (now `module IN ('revenue_and_traction', 'durable_company_building')`) unlinks it.
  - A row the founder deleted is never written again: the ledger still knows the day and no row is left.

### 2. Sheet rows next to typed and connected rows (`routes/progress.ts`)

- **Edits:** a Sheet row is changed in the Sheet, never in StudioOS. An edit answers 409 `sheet_figure_locked`. The team deletes a Sheet row with `DELETE /api/cycles/grow/google/facts/:id`.
- **`CONNECTED_METRIC_SOURCES`** gains `google_sheets`.
- **Latest-row reads:** a Sheet row does not shadow a typed one in the reads that take the latest row. These are:
  - progress's series, summary and lifecycle;
  - `research.ts`'s venture read;
  - the Grow records' runway history.

  Each excludes it with `COALESCE(source, '') <> 'google_sheets'`, written in the statement itself, so `schema_guards` counts no new interpolation.

### 3. Tagged Calendar events

- **Tags:** `#board` and `#filing`, each as its own word in an event's title, propose a board meeting or a filing deadline. The title is kept without the tag. A title with both tags, or neither, is not proposed.
- **The read:** the member's primary Calendar, on the grant they already gave (D1091's one Google store), from today for 365 days, at most four pages.
  - It is GET only, with no redirect and a ten-second timeout.
  - Only `id`, `status`, `summary` and `start` are asked for.
  - Cancelled events are left out.
  - Failures answer with a code only: `google_not_connected`, `calendar_not_allowed`, `calendar_reconnect`, `google_rate_limited` or `calendar_unreadable`.
- **Proposals:** each tagged event becomes one row in `google_workspace_proposals` (`calendar:<event_id>`).
  - A pending proposal follows its event when the event's title or day changes.
  - A decided proposal is never proposed again.
  - When the read was whole, a pending proposal whose event is gone or no longer tagged is withdrawn.
- **Accept** writes the obligation through `addObligation` with `source = 'google_calendar'`. A board meeting becomes `governance` and a filing deadline becomes `compliance`. It is refused as a typed obligation would be, then linked in the ledger as `calendar:<event_id>:<project_id>`.
- **Dismiss** marks the proposal decided and writes nothing else.

### 4. Routes (`routes/cycle_grow.ts`, under `/api/cycles/grow`)

- `POST /google/sync` with `project` and `part` (`sheets`, `calendar`, or both when left out). Each part answers on its own, so one failure never blanks the other.
- `GET /google/:slug?project=`, where the slug is `revenue-and-traction` or `durable-company-building`, returns what that module's From Google section draws:
  - for Revenue and traction: the venture's Sheets (with `mine`) and their rows;
  - for Durable company-building: the reader's own pending Calendar proposals, and the decided counts.
- `POST /google/proposals/:id/accept` and `POST /google/proposals/:id/dismiss`. An unknown proposal answers 404, and a decided one 409.
- `DELETE /google/facts/:id?project=`, for the team only.

**Checks:** sync and decide check what D1091's routes check: no support session, the member's own active Workspace connection, venture write access and company enablement (`workspaceMember`, `workspaceContext`). Every write logs a Grow write.

**What is logged:** counts and outcomes only. No title, figure, token or event text is logged, returned in an error or thrown.

### 5. Departures from the issue

- **`cash_balance` is not a Sheet figure.** The issue lists it among the Sheet's columns while also saying a Sheet never overwrites a connected figure. Mercury writes `cash_balance` (D752), so the second rule wins.
- **The tag rule:** a whole-word `#board` or `#filing`. A title holding both tags is not proposed, rather than guessing which one was meant.
- **The latest-row exclusions** in §2 are not in the issue. Without them, a Sheet row for a later day would become the venture's "latest" figures and hide the typed ones.

### 7. The page part (2026-10-27)

`frontend/src/pages/cycles/GrowFromGoogle.jsx`, mounted by Grow's `parts` map in `CycleRoute.jsx` after From Mercury. It reads `GET /api/cycles/grow/google/:slug` itself.

- **Revenue and traction** draws the shared section (`FromGoogle.jsx`, GW3 and GW7):
  - **The rows:** each day the Sheet landed, with Date, New users, CAC, LTV, Headcount and NRR (%). A blank cell is "—" with its reason, never 0.
  - **Kept-deleted days** are counted.
  - **Sync** reads `part: 'sheets'`. It shows only to the member who chose the Sheet, since the Worker reads that member's own file, and it reports counts only.
  - **Delete** goes through the section's own dialog.
- **Map columns (GW4)** is the shared dialog with `SHEET_FIELDS`: Date and the five allowed fields, and nothing Stripe or Mercury writes.
  - The dialog gains two optional props, `encode` and `decode`. Grow's turn the dialog's column-to-field map into the field-to-letter map `metricsMapping` checks (`{ date: 'A', new_users: 'B' }`), and back.
  - Without them the dialog saves what it shows, as before.
- **Durable company-building** draws From Google Calendar:
  - how to tag an event (`#board`, `#filing`);
  - the pending proposals, each with its kind, title and date;
  - **Accept**, through a dialog: "Board meeting on … becomes a governance obligation in Durable company-building, due that day. The event in your Google Calendar is not changed."
  - **Dismiss**, through a dialog: not proposed again;
  - how many were accepted and dismissed before;
  - **Sync**, `part: 'calendar'`.
  - A refusal keeps the dialog open with the Worker's word.
- **States:**
  - a read refused (403 or 404) draws nothing;
  - a failed read is "Unreadable … not the same as having none" with Try again, for that part only.
- **Words:** `growRefusal` maps the Worker's Grow codes (the mapping codes, `not_found`, `decided`), then falls back to Google Workspace's own words. Sync sentences are counts only.
