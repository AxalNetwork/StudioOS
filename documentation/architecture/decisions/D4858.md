## D4858 — Platform reads report their state in one shape, and the catalogue owns its sync facts (#4858)

- **Status:** Built, 2026-11-07 (Worker and frontend). No migration, no new route. Stacked on D4859's readers.
- **Builds on:** D4859 (one reader per panel), D213 (H16's four consoles), D1011 (a failed catalogue sync is audited as `catalog_sync_failed` and shown in Payments' change log), D4856 (the catalogue counts current-mode rows only).

### The problem

Platform's panels each said whether they could be read in their own way, with different mixes of `available`, `complete`, `reason`, `read_at`, `last_written_at` and hand-written caveats. That had already let Platform and Payments disagree. Platform said, twice, that a failed catalogue sync leaves no record: in `CATALOG_SYNC_BASIS` and in the rail's "Catalog schedule" line. Its Sync row also said "whether a sync is running or failed is not stored". Meanwhile `POST /api/admin/catalog/sync` has written `catalog_sync_failed` since D1011, and Payments' change log shows those rows.

### One read contract

`cloudflare-worker/src/util/operationalRead.ts`:

- **`OperationalReadMeta`** has six fields: `status` (`ready` | `partial` | `unavailable`), `read_at`, `source_updated_at`, `complete`, `completeness_reason` and `source`.
- **`OperationalRead<T>`** adds `data`, which is null exactly when the read is unavailable.
- Each status follows one rule:
  - `ready` is complete and has no reason;
  - `partial` and `unavailable` are never complete and always carry a reason (a blank reason is filled with a sentence).
- `source_updated_at` is null where the source keeps no such time. It is read as Not recorded, never as "now".

The summary's blocks keep their own fields, because the page reads them. `GET /api/admin/platform/summary` gains `reads`, one meta per reader, keyed the same way the readers are:

- **Status.** Each meta's status comes from its block. A reader that failed or timed out is `unavailable`, with its block's own reason.
- **Composite panels.** GitHub sync (window and recent) and payments (catalogue and webhook) are `partial` when one part is down, and `unavailable` when both are. Payments is also `partial` when the catalogue's price lists did not all parse.
- **Integration keys** are `partial` when the key table or the console audit could not be read.
- **Source times.** These are given where the source keeps one:
  - the catalogue mirror's last write;
  - the latest cron run across the declared triggers;
  - the latest Telegram send.
- **Sources.** Each panel's source is named in `PLATFORM_READ_SOURCES`.

The page draws each summary-backed zone's read as one faint line under it, such as "Read in full at … UTC · source last changed … UTC · from …". A partial or unavailable read adds its reason.

### The catalogue owns its sync facts

`CATALOG_SYNC_CAPABILITY` in `services/catalog.ts` holds the facts:

- the sync is on demand, and nothing schedules it;
- failed syncs are recorded;
- the actions are `catalog_sync` and `catalog_sync_failed`, recorded in `admin_audit_log`;
- its source.

`CATALOG_SYNC_BASIS` is built from these facts. It says:

- a manual sync is recorded whether it succeeds or fails;
- a re-sync after an edit is recorded as that edit;
- a sync started by an empty read is not recorded.

`readCatalogSyncHistory` reads the last manual sync of each outcome from the audit log. It never creates the table. An unreadable log gives `history_readable: false` and both times null. That reads as unknown, never as "never failed".

`readCatalogMirrorSummary` now carries `sync` (that history) and a real `sync_state`:

- `succeeded` or `failed`, from whichever recorded outcome is later;
- `not_recorded` when there is no recorded sync, or when the log is unreadable.

The Payments catalog panel's row is now "Last manual sync". It shows Succeeded, Failed (in red) or Not recorded, then the last success and the last failure. The rail's "Catalog schedule" line is corrected.

### Drift guards

`cloudflare-worker/test/platform_read_contract_4858.test.ts` checks:

- the contract's rules;
- `reads` for every panel, including the failed, partial and composite cases;
- that the capability's two actions are the ones `routes/catalog.ts` writes, that `paymentsChangeLog.ts` maps both to `sync`, and that `payModel.js` names the failure;
- that no code in `catalog.ts`, `platformSummary.ts`, `PlatformPage.jsx` or `payModel.js` says a failed sync is unrecorded while the capability says it is;
- the sync history against a real schema, including ISO and SQL stamps and a dropped audit table.

`frontend/test/platform_read_contract_4858.test.mjs` checks `readLine`, the sync row's three states and an unreadable log, and that each summary-backed zone passes its read.

Eight mutants were each killed:

- the state order;
- `failed_syncs_recorded`;
- the `complete` rule;
- the composite partial;
- the `datetime()` normalisation;
- the ready-reason rule;
- the source-time guard;
- a dropped zone read.

The new drift test also caught the rail line, which no one had listed.
