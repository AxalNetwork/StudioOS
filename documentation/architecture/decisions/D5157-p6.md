## D5157 part 6 — Research Project context and comparison sets (#5157)

The project workspace's context rail reads the active project from its URL
and shares the existing owner-scoped evidence, findings and comparisons
reads. It opens as a bottom sheet on a phone (RS-10D). Saved comparison sets
carry their recorded titles and members with links to the Comparisons tab.
Each failed read has its own Retry. Open questions remain Not recorded, and
AI settings starts collapsed with a link to Account Usage.
