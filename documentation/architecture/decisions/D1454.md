### 2. One record header (IV-D1), every cycle

`CycleHeader` in record mode (`pages/cycles/CycleOverview.jsx`) draws:
- "‹ <Module>" (`data-testid="record-back"`), above the eyebrow, linking to the module;
- the eyebrow, then the title with the record's pills beside it;
- one action row at top right;
- the module tabs, as navigation only. No tab says Back, and none is `aria-current="page"` on a record.

The pills and the row are the record page's, not the header's: each record keeps its own handlers (the interview's Edit details, Delete and Open recording are its own). So the header holds two empty slots, and `RecordHeaderRow` (`pages/cycles/RecordHeader.jsx`) portals the record's pills and row into them. `CycleRoute` owns the slots (`headerSlots`) and passes, through `RecordHeaderProvider`, the Email action and Ask.

The row is built by `headerRow` (`pages/cycles/recordHeaderModel.js`), in this order:
- the first action the read gives the `primary` tone (Open recording on an interview; Edit on most records);
- Ask (`PageAsk`, D1443; nothing when the page has no Ask);
- Email (D1344's `EmailAction`, on a record with a person);
- the read's other actions, in its order;
- ⋯ holding the danger actions (Delete) and any extra item the record names (the interview's "Open the session").

**No action sits on a row of its own.** The lone Email row above every record (`CycleRoute`'s `flex justify-end` div) is gone.

A record drawn outside the route (a test, a preview) has no slots, so the same row draws inline where the record put it.

### 3. Facts (IV-D2)

`RecordFacts` draws the facts in one even row (`auto-rows-fr`, `h-full`). A fact the read leaves null shows "Not recorded". When the read names one of the record's declared actions for it (`fact.action`, a key), that one action replaces the reason, on desktop and as the phone row's action. A key the record did not declare draws the reason, never a guessed button.

The keys come from D1456 (W1): `judge-fit` on a null ICP fit and `judge-need` on a null Need-to-have. Consent has none, because it cannot be added after recording. Until W1 lands, no fact names an action and nothing changes on screen.

### 4. The interview record on a phone and a tablet (IV-M1 to IV-M4, IV-M7)

Below 1024px (D703), under `CycleBar`:
- **Head:** the pills, then the primary at full width, Ask and ⋯. ⋯ holds Email, the other actions, "Open the session" and Delete. The old "Actions" group at the foot is gone on this record; other records keep D703's group, with Email added to it.
- **At a glance:** the facts, as before, with the fact action as the row's action.
- **Sections:** Summary · Transcript · Findings · Notes, a sticky control, kept in `?tab=` beside `?session=`; anything else in `?tab=` reads as Summary. A section this reader has nothing in is not offered (a partner's read has no Transcript or Findings). The parts are placed by `sectionTab`:
  - Summary: the session's summary card, Cost, Session, Activity;
  - Transcript: the transcript card;
  - Findings: Eadwyn's placements;
  - Notes: What it fed, From this conversation, the interview's tools.
- **Transcript:** each passage with a start offers "▶ Play from here". `useSessionPlayer`'s new `seek(ms)` plays the part whose `start_ms` holds it (`seekTarget`), at the offset inside it. A passage or a part with no start plays nothing; `null` is no start, never 0. Marks (`marks` on `GET /sessions/:id`) are drawn on the passage whose time holds them.
- **Player:** sticky at the foot below 1024px, with 1×, 1.5× and 2× (`setRate`, kept across parts).
- **From 768px (IV-M7, Q1 A):** the section control hides and every section shows, in the record's two columns. This amends D703 decision 1 for this record only.

Every touch target on the phone record is at least 44px.
