## D6164 — The deal's Evidence tab, built to the Deals v2 Evidence addendum (#6164)

- **Status:** part 1 of #6164, built 2026-11-18. It builds the Evidence boards of D6163's canvas (`design/incoming/Deals v2 Deal Page Evidence Page.dc.html`): DE19 before the NDA, DE20 after it, DE21 one item, DE22 the states, DE30a on a phone and DE31a in dark. Team, Market and Traction (DE23 to DE26, and DE30b to DE30d) are the issue's later parts.
- **Where it lives:** `frontend/src/pages/cycles/evidence/DealEvidence.jsx`, with its rules in `dealEvidenceModel.js`. `EvidenceSummary` in `InvestorEvidence.jsx` draws it on the deal page. The round call and the diligence room keep D1268's block, so #6174's deal-only filters and hand-offs moved out of that block into this one.
- **The read:** `GET /api/deals/:id/evidence` (`investorEvidence`), unchanged. No Worker route is added.

### What it draws

- **Before the NDA:** counts only. The coverage table (Area, Kind, Source, Items, Newest) with its groups and items in the footer, the lock line from the read, and Sign the NDA for an investor. The footer's item total is drawn only when every group's count was read.
- **After the NDA:**
  - The claims line ("5 of 8 shared claims are backed") with a count for each claim state, and the list of claims behind "See the N claims".
  - Filters by area, source and claim state, built from the returned items only.
  - A sort by when a claim was last checked, or by the observed date.
  - Items grouped by area, the first area open, three rows before "Show N more in this area".
  - A claim counts only when it backs an item this reader received; one behind a private item is never counted or offered as a filter.
- **One item:** a side sheet (`ui/Sheet`) with its place in the area, its figure or the read's reason, its facts, the claims it backs with their checked dates, and four hand-offs for an investor: Data room, Diligence, a prefilled firm note ("Re: kind · area") and the Scorecard tab.
- **States:**
  - Empty offers "Send a request to the founder" from Diligence.
  - Partial is a banner above the claims line counting only what was shown.
  - A failed read, item block or claims block says what could not be read, that it is not the same as none, and offers Try again. None of these draws a zero.
  - Loading is a skeleton.
- **Phone and dark:** below 1024px items and coverage are cards with 44px targets, filters fold behind "Filters · N", and no table is drawn, so no first column needs sideways scrolling. Every light surface has its dark pair.

- **Claim words** on the deal page follow the canvas ("The founder’s own figure", "Does not match"); the founder's own Raise › Evidence keeps D1261's words.
- **Eadwyn's proposals** in the rail (Summarise what is backed) are #5918's, not built here.
