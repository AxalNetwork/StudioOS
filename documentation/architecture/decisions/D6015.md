## D6015 — Explain competitor signal filters (#6015)

Competitor records show Latest signal, Funding to date and Source. The
permanently empty Headcount fact is removed.

The record's Signal filters and both Add/Edit forms explain that the
selected types govern what a re-run keeps and what the record displays.
Axal does not monitor competitors automatically. D690 §8 still governs
persistence, visibility and manual recording.
