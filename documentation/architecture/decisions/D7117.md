### What the canvas decides

- `/wellbeing` stays the one authenticated destination, for founder and admin as today. The page opens with "Take a moment to check in", then Today, This week and This month, then Recommendations and My bookings.
- Each period has its own date and its own state (○ Not yet, ✓ Saved, couldn't be read). There are no streaks, no "you missed" copy and no score of anyone's mental or spiritual health.
- The header heart opens Today directly, with a second link to the full home. Its failed read says "Couldn't load your check-in" with Retry. The phone Account sheet row is renamed Wellness and opens Today.
- Daily, Weekly and Monthly views each show a short private history and Edit reflection. Days without an entry are left out, never drawn as 0. A failed save keeps what was written.
- Clinical support, Nutrition, Mindfulness and grounding, and Spiritual guidance are four opt-in practice switches. The journal works with all four off. Using check-in topics for suggestions has its own consent.
- Recommendations carry Choose interests, Why this?, Dismiss with undo and Browse all. Every card shows credentials, scope, format, language, time zone, price and the next opening. All providers drawn are labelled [Sample].
- 1:1 and group booking decisions say what the provider learns when the member reserves (name and email, never check-in content), and for a group what other members learn.
- The content area uses a warm off-white and sage palette inside the unchanged Axal VC shell; actions stay violet. The invitation sits on the owner's dawn image under a dark scrim. Nothing moves.
