at zero / 20 / 100 USD; Investor Basic / Plus / Pro at zero / 50 / 150 USD;
Advisor Basic / Pro and Operating Partner Basic / Pro at zero / 20 USD each.

sold or shown on /pricing.

204 USD, in the six paid plans' order. Advisory Firm, Partner Firm and persona

recorded price until the member changes plan; no subscription is repriced.

### One runtime source, one offline target table

Stripe prices mirrored in D1 `stripe_products`, read through
`GET /api/public/plans-and-packs`, remain the runtime source. No page types a
paid price. No schema migration is needed.

`scripts/lib/ownerPlanPrices.mjs` holds the six owner targets in cents, plus
Institutional/LP/Admin unpriced rules, for offline planning and the live check.
The Founder and Investor exports derive from it; the new persona planner
matches `plan_group=advisor|partner`. Missing products are reported, not
invented or priced on another product.

The planners take a private export of `GET /api/admin/catalog/products` and
make no network call. Each request carries the reason required by HQ Payments.
One wrong active USD price at an interval produces one existing `/change`
request with a stable `own3702-<tier>-<interval>` key. No current price produces
create; several produce create if needed and archive after verification.
Unrelated products, currencies and multi-interval paid-plan prices are left
alone. Active Institutional offer prices are listed for archival; existing
subscriptions retain archived prices. The owner verifies creates before any
separate archive action, then exports again and replans after interruption.

### Custom means contact on every offer surface

Pricing, the paywall, the checkout sheet and Billing say Contact Axal VC.
Institutional draws Custom without a mirror-derived price or purchase button,
even when an old priced product is still mirrored. Checkout identifies this
state by its catalogue key rather than matching a sentence. The legacy hosted
Investor checkout refuses both Institutional plan keys with 409 `not_sellable`
before any Stripe call. Its existing identity and TOTP gates remain.

The unmounted old Investor pricing page, which typed obsolete prices, is
removed. The no-typed-price guard also covers PricingPage and the billing
Worker. The old Annual “save 2 mo” claim is removed; savings elsewhere continue
to be computed from the two mirrored prices.

### Drift and verification

The existing anonymous AI credits live check also compares all six monthly
and annual prices, currency and offer state with the owner targets. Missing
plans/prices are Not recorded, never zero. Institutional must have no public
offer price, LP must remain free, and Admin must have no public plan row.
The check accepts the current D783 public allowance shape and LP family,
while retaining the earlier CR2 shape. No person, credential or Stripe call
is part of this check. The existing post-deploy warning step reports drift.

Tests pin the table, metadata/storage parity, profile ladders, accepted planner
bodies, missing/ambiguous products, live drift findings and CLI exit status.
Mounted tests exercise the paywall, Billing and LP/contact actions against a
mirror containing an old Institutional price. Worker tests refuse legacy
Institutional checkout with both active monthly and annual prices present.
Mutation evidence and final validation are recorded in the PR.
