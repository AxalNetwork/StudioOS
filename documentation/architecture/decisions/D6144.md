Status: built 2026-11-22 by S04, on Deals v2 addendum B (D6142, owner's answers D6142-p2: option A throughout), the voting roster (D6143) and the decision record (D6154).

### What was already done when this was built

D6154 (#6154, merged 2026-11-21) had already done two parts of this issue's scope. All decisions is the `?view=decisions` view of `/deals/commit`. One decision opens at `/deals/commit/:uid`, and `/ic` and `/ic/:uid` forward there. The owner's answer A on #6154 (2026-11-20) chose `/deals/commit/:uid` for one decision over this issue's `?decision=<uid>`. That answer stands. `?decision=<uid>` now forwards to `/deals/commit/<uid>`, so the address this issue named does not dead-end. `frontend/src/App.jsx` is not edited. Its `/ic` and `/ic/:uid` routes still admit admin, partner and investor, and they render the existing pages, which forward. No new hub entry was needed.

### What this builds (CommitZone and `pages/investor/deals/commit/`)

- **Open a decision** (DE19/DE20). Opens from the current view (`+ Open a decision` above the card, and on the empty states) with the same dialog All decisions uses. `OpenDecisionSheet` is extracted from `ICDecisionsPage.jsx` and calls `api.icCreate`. From the current view the page stays on `/deals/commit`: the new Draft is the newest decision not yet decided, so it is the one the card shows. All decisions still opens the record.
- **Your vote** (DE20/DE21). A panel under the decision card offers Cast my vote or Change my vote, plus Recuse myself. Each opens a `Sheet` dialog through `api.icVote` (`POST /api/ic/:uid/vote`):
  - The vote is yes, no or abstain, with a required reason. Changing a vote starts from the stored vote and reason.
  - A recusal is `recused` with the declaration as its rationale. It needs a non-blank declaration (`recusalBody`) and the "I understand a recusal is final" acknowledgement. Recusing after voting says that it replaces the vote.
  - Once a reader has recused, no vote control is drawn.
- **Close the vote and Add condition** (DE22/DE23) move from inline panels to `Sheet` dialogs.
  - Close restates the tally with Recused beside it. It warns about open conditions on this decision. It writes `{ decision }` only after Close vote confirms a chosen outcome.
  - Add condition calls `api.icAddCondition`.
- **Dialogs.** Every dialog is the shared `Sheet`. It traps focus, returns focus to the opener and closes on Escape. A refused or failed write keeps the dialog open with the reader's edits. The dialog shows the refusal in the Worker's own message (`refusalWords`), for example `recusal_is_final`, `rationale_required`, `voting_closed`, `not_on_roster` and the preview refusal "Return to HQ to make this change."
- **Unreadable never offers a vote** (`voteOffer`). If the read did not carry the decision's votes or its roster, or a roster arrived without members, the panel says Unavailable and draws no control. A decided record, a member who is not on the roster, and a recused member also get no control, and each gets its reason.
- **Recused vs abstain.** Recused keeps its own word, the ⊘ glyph, a dashed mark and a muted row. It is counted beside the tally ("⊘ N · beside the tally") and never in it. Abstain is a vote cast.
- **Eligible / To vote** come from the decision's roster (D6143). With no roster set, Eligible voters reads Not recorded. If the roster was not read, it reads Unavailable. Invitees are never counted as voters.

### Kept where it was

- **Record position.** The admin's "Record position" action stays on the decision record, `/deals/commit/:uid` (`ICDecisionPage.jsx`), under Decision after an invest outcome. The Commit card's title links there, and `/ic/:uid` forwards there.
- **Partner and admin.** Partners keep Commit-only access (D6154). The `/ic` pages stay for every role as forwarding entries only.

### Policy preserved

No Worker change and no new `/api` client method: every call is an existing mounted route in `routes/ic.ts`. The private voting and access rules are the Worker's (the scope, the roster, the author gate on `PUT /:uid`). The page only stops offering a control the Worker would refuse. Preview policy is unchanged. The Worker's `investorPreviewRefusal` still refuses votes and decision writes in the Investor preview, and the dialog shows that refusal. No preview write is widened.

### Departure from the canvas

- **Vote words.** The canvas draws Invest / Pass / Abstain as vote words (Q1 A). The stored values are yes / no / abstain, and the decision card, the record page (D6154) and `icDecisionModel` all print Yes / No. So the dialog prints Yes / No / Abstain, to match the card it sits under. Renaming the words is a one-place change across the card, this dialog, the record page and `tallyText`. It is left to a follow-up so that the three screens never disagree.
- **Not built here.** The meeting line in Open a decision, quorum, vote history, condition Reopen and Owner picker, and Eadwyn's condition wording are not built. They remain the backend items D6142 lists.
