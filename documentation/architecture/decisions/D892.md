## D892 — A plan bought through the payments intent is granted, once, and a failed renewal is told

- **Status:** decided and built by S1, 2026-10-24 (#2453, assigned to S01 by the owner on 2026-10-23).
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `edffa49`.
- **Migration:** none.
- **Why:** members could pay for a plan and not get it. An upgrade started a second subscription. A renewal that failed told nobody.

### 1. The plan travels with the subscription

- **The bug.** `/pricing`'s Upgrade confirms through `POST /api/payments/intent`. For a recurring price, the intent made a subscription stamped only `user_id`, `uid` and `price_id`. The webhook grants a plan from `metadata.kind`, so the card was charged and the plan never moved.
- **One mapping for both paths.** `services/planSubscription.ts` reads the plan from the product, with the metadata key the Billing checkouts already look up through `priceForPlanMetadata`:

  | Product metadata | Values | Kind |
  |---|---|---|
  | `tier` | growth, studio | `tier` |
  | `investor_tier` | professional, institutional | `investor` |
  | `plan_group` | any persona plan group | `plan` |
  | `plan` | mi_pro (the interval picks monthly or annual) | `mi` |

  The intent stamps those fields on the subscription (`planMetaParams`).
- **The investor customer.** An investor plan is made on the investor customer, because the investor webhook matches on `investor_stripe_customer_id`. When the member has none yet, one is made for them (§6; this said "the general customer becomes it" until §6).
- **A recurring price that is not a plan** is charged as before, with no plan stamped.

### 2. Subscriptions made before this fix

A `customer.subscription.*` event with no `kind` takes its plan from its price's catalog product (`subscriptionMetadata`), so subscriptions created the old way are granted on their next event.
- **An event that names its kind keeps it.**
- **A price that is no plan, or a catalog that cannot be read, leaves the metadata as it came.** No plan is ever guessed.

### 3. One subscription per plan family

- **The guard.** The intent refuses a second subscription when the member already holds one of the same family. It answers 409 `subscription_exists`, with the subscription id and `swap: /api/billing/subscription/swap/preview`, the route that prorates.
- **What counts as held:** `active`, `trialing` or `past_due` (a renewal Stripe is still retrying).
- **What does not:** a partner-granted tier with no Stripe subscription, a cancelled plan, or a plan of another family.

### 4. A failed renewal is told

- **The notice.** `invoice.payment_failed` with `billing_reason = subscription_cycle` sends the member one in-app and email notice per failed attempt, "Your plan's renewal payment failed", with the next retry date when Stripe gives one and a link to Billing.
- **Redelivery.** A redelivered event sends nothing new.
- **What sends nothing:**
  - a failed first payment, which the card form shows;
  - a subscription no member owns.

### 5. Not changed here

- **Access while Stripe retries was #2451's owner question 4.** When this was written, `requireTier`, `requireInvestorTier` and the other `past_due` checks dropped the plan at `past_due`. D1052 answered the question (a `past_due` plan keeps its access), and D1100 bounds that to period end plus 21 days. §6 keeps both as they are.
- **The proof.** `cloudflare-worker/test/billing_plan_grants_d892.test.ts` runs the real intent payload through the real webhook, with Stripe stubbed. The Stripe test-mode run comes after the merge: the owner runs it, or S1 does once the Stripe connector is authorized for its session.

### 6. After review (#2673, 2026-10-25)

- **Basis:** `main` at `996f076`, which has D1049, D1052 and D1100. No migration and no new decision number.
- **Why:** two reviews of the merged change found plan events and intent inputs handled more loosely than §1 to §4 intend. Everything they confirmed is fixed here.

#### What is now true

- **An unpaid subscription grants nothing.** Stripe makes the intent's subscription `incomplete` until its first invoice is paid, and `incomplete_expired` if nobody pays.
  - Neither status writes a founder or investor plan in `customer.subscription.created` or `.updated` (`isUnpaidStatus`). The `active` event that follows payment grants it.
  - One that expires unpaid takes back what such an event gave, only on the row that still points at it.
  - Every gate refuses both statuses: `userMeetsTier`, `effectiveInvestorTier`, the introductions allowance (investor and founder, `monthlyAllowanceFor`) and investor seats (`primaryCover`).
- **The quantity** is a whole number of at least one, or the intent answers 400 `invalid_quantity`. A fraction is no longer floored. A plan is always one.
- **The investor customer** is the member's own Stripe customer, made with `metadata[scope]=investor` (`ensureInvestorCustomer`), never the general one. The intent makes it only once nothing is held:
  - it reads the stored investor customer without making one (`storedInvestorCustomer`);
  - it checks D1, then Stripe on the general customer and on the investor customer when there is one;
  - a refusal, 409 or 503, makes no customer.

  When the held plan is on the general customer and the member has no investor customer, the general customer becomes their investor customer there and then (`adoptInvestorCustomer`, only `WHERE investor_stripe_customer_id IS NULL`). The plan's next event then grants it, and the 409's swap pointer finds it in the investor scope.
- **A swap acts on its own scope's family only** (`subscriptionInScope`). A founder-scope swap of an investor subscription, or the other way round, answers 400 `invalid_scope`.
  - A subscription made before D892, with no kind, has its family read from its price.
  - A subscription of no known family (its price is outside the mirrored catalog) keeps today's behaviour: the swap goes through.
  - A destination price outside the mirrored catalog is refused with 400 `invalid_price`, before Stripe is read, as before.
- **An event for another subscription never takes over a plan the member still holds**, unless that one is live too (an upgrade made through a checkout). This applies to the founder, investor, MI Pro and persona rows.
- **A subscription whose plan was read from the catalog** (made before D892) never takes over a held plan at all, so a duplicate stays inert and its own end drops nothing.
- **Pre-D892 investor plans** on the general customer are granted on their next event. The member their metadata names adopts that customer as their investor customer, if they have none, in the webhook or in the intent (above). A member with an investor customer of their own keeps it.
- **One subscription per family, also from Stripe.** When D1 holds none, the intent reads the member's live subscriptions in Stripe (`heldPlanInStripe`). One of the same family is refused with 409.
  - Stripe's `resource_missing` for the general customer means nothing is held there: the id is unknown under the active key, which `/billing/overview` heals.
  - Every other failed read answers 503 `plan_check_unavailable` and charges nothing. So does `resource_missing` for the investor customer, which is the one the plan would be sold on.
- **The 409's swap pointer** is given only for founder and investor plans. The swap route takes no other family's price.
- **The last failed renewal attempt** says Stripe will not try again. A dated one names the day in UTC.

#### Rows left at `incomplete`

Before §6 each event wrote its status as it came. A `created` event (`incomplete`) delivered after the `updated` one (`active`) could leave a paid row at `incomplete`, which the gates now refuse. Two repairs read Stripe for such a row. Each touches only the row that still holds that subscription at `incomplete`, and only when the subscription is on that row's own customer and of its family (`settleIncompleteRow`, `services/planSubscription.ts`).

- **The webhook's.** An `incomplete` event, or `invoice.paid`, that names the subscription a founder or investor row holds at `incomplete` reads it from Stripe.
  - When Stripe says it is live (`active`, `trialing` or `past_due`), the row takes its plan, status and period end, and accepted seat colleagues follow.
  - Anything else is left to its own event, or to the owner's reconciliation.
  - Stripe is not read when no row holds the subscription at `incomplete`. A failed read never fails the webhook.
- **The owner's reconciliation.** `POST /api/admin/billing/subscriptions/reconcile-incomplete` (`routes/admin_billing.ts`, `reconcileIncompleteRows`) walks every such row, a page at a time by user id, and reads each subscription from Stripe:

  | Stripe says | The row | Outcome |
  |---|---|---|
  | `active`, `trialing`, `past_due` | its plan, Stripe's status and period end; seat colleagues follow | `granted` |
  | `incomplete_expired`, `canceled` | free, and the subscription let go | `ended` |
  | `unpaid` | `unpaid` | `unpaid` |
  | `incomplete`, or any other status | unchanged | `unchanged` |
  | on another customer, or of another family | unchanged | `mismatch` |
  | no such subscription | unchanged | `missing` |
  | a read that failed | unchanged | `unreadable` |

  - **A dry run unless the body says `"dry_run": false`.** A dry run only reads Stripe and needs a Super Admin.
  - **Writing** takes the write bar (a TOTP session, a recent step-up, the holder) and a `reason` of 1 to 500 characters, as D1009's payment writes do.
  - **Every run is audited** once through `logAdminAction`, as `billing_incomplete_reconcile_dry_run` or `billing_incomplete_reconcile`, with its counts and reason.
  - **Paging:** `after` (a user id, default 0) and `limit` (1 to 100, default 25). The answer carries `next_after` while rows may remain.
  - **The owner runs it against production.** No deploy, cron or agent session runs it. Run a dry run first and read its results. Then run the same page with `"dry_run": false` and a reason, and repeat with `after` set to `next_after` until it is null.

#### With D1052 and D1100

- **Grace while Stripe retries is unchanged.** D1052's `past_due` grace, bounded by D1100 to period end plus 21 days, stays exactly as `main` has it. The unpaid statuses are a separate check beside it in each gate.
- **Seat colleagues carry the primary's period end** (D1100), in the webhook's cascade and in both repairs.
- **`HELD_STATUSES`** (`active`, `trialing`, `past_due`) is what counts as held for the duplicate check, and what counts as live for the repairs.

#### The proof

`billing_plan_grants_d892.test.ts` runs the intent, the webhook, the swap routes and the reconciliation through the real routes, with Stripe stubbed. Its "§6" tests cover each rule above, and the finding-5 test holds both rules in every gate: `incomplete` and `incomplete_expired` refused, `past_due` inside D1100's window kept.

Each assertion was mutation-checked: 43 mutants. 41 are killed. The other two are the two halves of the adoption guard, the intent's own check and the update's `IS NULL`. Each covers the other, so each survives alone, and together they are killed.
