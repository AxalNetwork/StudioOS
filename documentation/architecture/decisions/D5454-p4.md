### What is decided

The same guard and the same SQL fragment as parts 2 and 3: `hiddenDealIds(env, mode)`, bound as `d.id NOT IN (SELECT CAST(value AS INTEGER) FROM json_each(?))` before any `LIMIT`, or `dealHiddenFrom` for one id. Legacy deals with no preparation (Q1 = A), an unreleased `legacy_attach` and a published release are unchanged everywhere.

- **CRM push exports read as the venture's owner (`transactional`).** Every deal a push, a preview or a sync sends belongs to a venture of the company that holds the connection (`projectEnabled`; Salesforce also checks the venture's founder), so the export goes to the owner's own CRM, the audience D5454 §3 keeps for an unlisted release, as `D5454-p3.md` reasoned for the Eadwyn CRM readers. A private or unknown preparation is refused before any request is made, with the error a missing deal gives (`deal_not_found`). The id is coerced with `Number(...)` first, because SQLite matches the text `'7'` to deal 7 and the guard would otherwise skip it. `sync` filters its outbound batch in SQL, with one statement per run and no lookup per deal. A guard failure fails the push, the preview or the whole sync, and nothing is sent.
- **Diligence is a relationship reader (`transactional`).** Every deal read in `routes/dd.ts` already narrows to staff by licence, an investor invited or committed, or the venture's founder, so a withdrawn or archived release stays for them and a private or unknown one is a deal that does not exist, for an admin too:
  - Open a case's subject search and deal list (`GET /subjects`, `withDeals`);
  - an investor's right to open a case (`investorOnVisibleProjectDeal`, one statement replacing the per-deal `investorHasProjectDeal` loop here; `services/dealAccess.ts` is unchanged, since open PR #6510 owns it);
  - a case's linked deal on create and on change (`caseContextFrom`, 404);
  - the asking team's name on a founder's requests, which is never read off a hidden deal.

  A guard failure is a 500.
- **The scorecard switch: the transactional reading (recorded choice).** D5454 §3 allows two readings of `investorsMaySee`'s "on once the venture is listed (it has a deal)":
  - **A, transactional.** "Listed" counts deals less `hiddenDealIds('transactional')`.
  - **B, discovery.** It also drops withdrawn and archived releases.

  **Pick: A.** The switch is never an audience on its own. Every caller asks it only for an investor already on one of the venture's deals:
  - `GET /api/venture-scores/deal/:id` and the deal-list batch, after `investorOnDeal` or `userReadableDealIds`;
  - `deal_case` and `dealSummary`, after `visibleDeal`;
  - `dealCompareNote`, after `requireComparableDeals`.

  Under A, a venture whose only deal is a withdrawn release keeps its scorecard for the investors on that release, which is the audience §3 keeps for it, and nobody else gains anything. B would switch the scorecard off for exactly those investors, a narrowing §3 does not ask for. Under either reading a private or unknown preparation counts for nothing. `dealProject` (a deal's venture, by id) reads the same way: a hidden deal has no venture, and the route answers 404.
- **Raise progress is a discovery sum.** The collector's figure is stored and shown to every investor on any of the venture's deals, not only the people on each deal. So it sums deals and commitment lines less `hiddenDealIds('discovery')`, and a withdrawn or archived release's target and committed capital never reach an investor who is not on it. A guard failure makes raise progress alone unreadable (`unreadable: true`, "Could not be read"), the way Mercury's failure does for runway. It is never summed unfiltered.
- **Failure.** A database that has not applied the migration hides nothing (`isMissingPreparationTable`). Any other guard failure, including the `HIDDEN_SET_MAX` bound, fails the reader closed, as listed above.
- **Cost.** One indexed statement per request or per sync run. A push or preview by id is one `dealHiddenFrom` read, and no reader makes a lookup per row.
