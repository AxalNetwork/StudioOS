### 1. Frame 1: the Overview at full scroll (`accounts/AccountsOverview.jsx`)

- **One column.** Every block grid already stacks below `sm` (`sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-4`). The test pins that no Overview grid has columns without a breakpoint.
- **44px targets.** Each link and button below `lg` is at least 44px tall: `TAP` (`inline-flex min-h-[44px] items-center lg:min-h-0`) on Try again, the tile and widget links, View as table, View all and each newest account's date. The readiness segments, Open and Invite an Operating Partner keep their desktop heights from `lg` up.

### 2. Frame 2: All accounts as cards (`accounts/AllAccounts.jsx`)

- **The toolbar** is the search (44px), **Filters and sort** with a count, and the count line. The desktop's profile chips, Views, Filters, Sort and Columns are not drawn.
  - The count is each chip (`chipsOf`, the profile among them), plus one for a sort that is not the default (`phoneFilterCount`).
- **Each account is a card** (`cardView`, over D1305's `rowView`):
  - initials, then the name, the email, the profile and its sub-account pills;
  - one line: the identity, then the status only when it is not active, the email only when it is not verified, then the joined date. What is ordinary is left out.
  - The initials take letters and digits only, so "[Ada Example]" reads "AE".
- **Opening a card** opens the record, as a row does. **⋯** is a 44px button.
- **Paging.** On the first page with more to come, **Show 25 more** (the next page size, `moreOf`). Past the first page, or at the largest size, **Previous** and **Next** at 44px.
- **No table at 390.** The table and its columns are not drawn on a phone.

### 3. Frames 3 and 4: the two sheets

- **Filters and sort** (`PhoneFiltersSheet`, `Lightbox phone="sheet"`) holds:
  - Profile with each profile's count;
  - Sub-accounts;
  - every filter (D1305's `FiltersPanel`, without its own buttons);
  - Sort, as radios.

  The footer has **Clear all** and **Show N accounts**. Each choice sends its query as it does on the desktop; Show closes the sheet.
- **The row menu** (`PhoneRowMenu`) is D1305's `rowMenu`, word for word, as a sheet. Each item acts and closes it. **Cancel** only closes. An administrator's note and its Licences link are kept.
- **Targets.** Every chip, radio, item and footer button is at least 44px tall.

### 5. A gap left as it is

The × in both sheets is the shared `Lightbox`'s, 36px (`h-9 w-9`). `Lightbox` serves every dialog in the product, and this issue owns none of it. Each sheet has a 44px way out of its own (Cancel, or Show N accounts). Making the shared × 44px on a phone is a one-class change for whoever owns `Lightbox`.
