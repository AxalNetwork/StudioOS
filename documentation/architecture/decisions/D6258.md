## D6258 — Investor Network: Organizations reads the counterpart's company the Worker already returns (#6258)

- **Status:** built, 2026-11-14 (#6258). No migration. **Worker:** no change.
- **Deciding record:** D572 part 3, in `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/partnernet.ts` (`companiesOf`). D551 is the founder side's `contacts.organization` and does not decide this.
- **Code:** `frontend/src/pages/investor/InvestorNetworkWorkspace.jsx`; `frontend/src/workspaces/investorZoneFilters.js` and `investorZoneActions.js` (the Organizations row); comments in `NetworkWorkspace.jsx` and `noStoreCopy.js`.
- **Test:** `frontend/test/investor_network_organizations_6258.test.mjs` renders the page with its reads stubbed. `investor_network_d497.test.mjs` and `profile_zone_actions.test.mjs` follow the new reasons.

### Why

`/network/organizations` was empty for every investor. `orgIdentity` read six fields (`organization_name`, `organization.name` and their `other.` and `metadata.` forms), and nothing writes any of them. Since D572 part 3, `GET /api/partnernet/relationships` has returned each counterpart's own company on every row: `other.company`, the company that person links on their account (`user_company_links`), primary admin first, else the oldest link. It is read at query time and is a name or null. When the company read fails, every row carries `companies_unreadable: true` and `other.company` is null.

### What changed

- **The roll-up** groups ties by `other.company`. A tie whose counterpart links no company is not grouped. Nothing is read from a name or an email address.
- **The Person row** in the relationship book shows the company under the name, or "Not recorded". The book's export writes the same value in its Organization column.
- **A failed company read** is said as one. The Organizations section says "Your counterparts' companies could not be read. This is not a claim that none are recorded." with Try again, which reads the book again. Its heading says "Companies unreadable". The Person row says "Company could not be read". The "none recorded" empty state is kept for a readable book in which no counterpart links a company.
- **The chip row.** `All` is a live chip: it is every company in the list, so it narrows nothing. `Portfolio` and `Co-investors` stay held, with new reasons. A company here is a name from another person's account, nothing ties it to a deal you invested in, and a tie's type describes the person rather than their company. `LPs` and `Passed` keep `KEPT_IN_ANOTHER_STORE`. The old shared reason said nothing names a firm and cited the closed #1023. It is gone.
- **Add org and Merge duplicates** stay held. Their reasons no longer say the list is empty on every account. A company belongs to the other person's account, so this page cannot add one. Ties are grouped by the company name, so one name is already one entry.
- **The footnote** names the source and still says names and email domains are never used to infer a firm.

### Left as it is

- No Worker change. The shape is D572 part 3's.
- No firm type. Nothing records whether a company is in your portfolio or is a co-investing firm, so `Portfolio` and `Co-investors` stay prose.
- `my_company`, the viewer's own company, is not drawn here.
