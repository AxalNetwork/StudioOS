## D1283 — Cap table › Scenarios: "Before" price per share from the record (#3341)

- **Status:** built by S12 for #3341, part of #2432, 2026-10-26.
- **Basis:** `main` at `bf0b0e12ff`.
- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** one field on the today read.
- **What it closes:** D1024 §4 left Today's price per share as "Not read here yet" because no read carried the record's last price. That gap is gone.

### 1. The Worker: `today.price`

`GET /api/captable/scenarios/by-project/:projectId/today` now carries, on `today`:
- `price`, from `capTableToday.ts` `priceOnRecord`;
- `price_sentence`: "No priced round is on record." when `price` is null, else null.

Both are present whether or not the record can start a scenario.

**Where the price comes from:**
1. The last recorded `priced` issuance with a price above 0.
   - "Last" is by issue date, then by when it was recorded.
   - Its class's name is the instrument, or "Priced round" when its class is unknown.
   - Its issue date is `as_of`.
2. Else the latest share class with a price above 0, with no date. An option pool's class is never a price.
3. Else `null`. Never 0.

A void issuance, a transfer, a SAFE or a note is never a price. `price` is `{ micros, as_of, instrument, source }`, with `source` either `issuance` or `class`.

### 2. The page

`scenariosModel.js` `todayPrice(read, onRecord)` gives Before's price:

| Today is… | Before reads |
|---|---|
| on record, with a price | `$2.5000`, with "On record · [instrument] · [date]" under it |
| on record, nothing priced | Not recorded, with "No priced round is on record." |
| the typed model | Not recorded. The block's foot already says "The start is your typed model, not the record." |
| still being read | Reading… |
| a failed read | Could not be read |

**Where it is drawn:**
- Before and after (`beforeAfter`, through `ResultsBlocks`).
- The compare table's Today column (`compareRows`, through `CompareBody`), which on the phone feeds the phone compare too.

`NOT_READ` and its "Not read here yet" are gone from `scenariosModel.js`.

### 3. UNVERIFIED until the owner's live check

- A real venture's record: that its last priced issuance is the price a founder expects as "today". A later priced issuance at a lower price, as in a down round, is still the last.
- A venture whose classes carry a price while no priced issuance does.
