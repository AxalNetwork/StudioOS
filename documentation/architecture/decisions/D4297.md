### 1. Nothing priced is typed

- **Rates and durations** come from `GET /api/influencer/tiers`, HQ's tier settings (D4289 §1.7). A tier the settings leave to terms reads "Set per agreement". The hero's "20–30%" and "12 invoices" are computed from the same read. When the read fails, the table says so ("not the same as having none") and the hero says "a share of each subscription" without a number.
- **Plan prices** on the @handle page come from `GET /api/public/plans-and-packs` through `lib/planPrices.js` (D1050). A plan the read does not price reads "Price not read", never a zero.
- **Bonus and payout amounts** are each influencer's terms', which a public page never reads. The page names the milestones (10, 25, 50, 100 paid members, P3's `BONUS_MILESTONES`) and says "your terms set each amount". The canvas's `$[25]`… and `$[50]` are samples; they are not drawn.
- A test reads the five page files and fails on any `$`/`€`/`£` amount or `NN%` rate written in code.

### 2. The disclosure goes with every handle and offer

- The @handle page draws the Worker's sentence ("<name> earns a commission when you subscribe. Axal memberships are software, not an investment.") beside Join and the offer. If a body ever arrives without it, `disclosureOf` builds the same sentence from the name.
- A paused or ended influencer's page shows no name, no offer and no handle, so it owes no disclosure; it credits no sign-up.
- The Refer & Earn card and /influencers show no handle and no offer, so they carry none.

### 3. The featured offer on the handle (Worker)

P4 exported `featuredOffer` for this read but P2's read still sent `offer: null`. `publicHandle` now calls it for a live influencer: the featured, active offer whose Promos code is live, in P4's words (`discount`, `who_for`, `plans`, `ends_at`), never its budget. An offer store that cannot be read logs a warning and shows no offer; the page still answers.

### 4. The application (IN9, IN9r)

- **Three steps:** where you publish (1 to 10 channels, each with an http or https link and an optional whole-number audience); your audience (regions, topics, why Axal, up to 2,000 characters); the terms (each of the Worker's three `CONSENT_RULES` ticked). Each step refuses what the Worker would, in words, before it moves on, and Submit is refused until every rule is ticked.
- **The version** sent is `consent_version` from `GET /api/influencer/me`, so a member who opened the sheet before the terms changed gets the Worker's `terms_version` sentence. Every refusal reads in the Worker's own words.
- **States** (`standing(me)`): admitted (a live membership, active or paused); in review; more needed (HQ's question, and "Add the details" opens the sheet from the answers sent, which P1 takes as the answer); declined until `reapply_after` (HQ's reason, "Apply again" disabled until then); otherwise free to apply, which includes a decline past its wait and an ended membership.

### 5. Where Apply goes

- Signed out, Apply on /influencers is `/register?program=influencer&next=%2Finfluencers%3Fapply%3D1`. Register keeps a same-origin `next` across sign-up (`sanitizeNextPath`), and `?apply=1` opens the sheet once the member can apply. It opens once, so it does not reopen after sending.
- Signed in, Apply opens the sheet. An applicant with an open application sees its state instead. An admitted influencer sees "Open your Influencer section" (`/influencer`, P7).
- The Refer & Earn card (standalone `/referrals` only, not the embedded copy) opens the same sheet in place.
