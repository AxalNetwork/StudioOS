### What the canvas decides

- My funds pages and searches a shortlist of any size (1,284 saved, page 1 of 26, illustrative). The All funds filter stays, and every count says what it counts.
- Each row shows the vehicle, the manager, an identity chip (linked catalogue record, made by hand, or legacy) and the uid. Stage fit, route in and status are three separate columns.
- The dossier keeps the public record read-only and apart from the founder's thesis, note, stage, route and pass reason, with Open catalogue vehicle →.
- The cheque card shows two tests: the whole round against US$3m, which works today, and an intended allocation against US$250k, which needs a new field. Examples compare only in the same currency.
- A legacy entry is linked through a chooser Sheet with evidence about the entry and each vehicle. A vehicle already linked to another entry is refused, and Keep as my own record is offered.
- Saving one field never discards another unsaved field. The canvas draws dirty, saving, saved, save failed with the draft kept, offline, the leave guard, and a conflict Sheet comparing both versions.
- Write into the note writes the private note only and sends the fund nothing. It waits while the note is unsaved, then moves focus to the added text; note_full, failed, nothing to draft, 404 and out of tokens are drawn.
- Phone at 390, dark mode, focus return and announcements have their own boards.
