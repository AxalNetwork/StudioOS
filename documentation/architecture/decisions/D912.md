### 1. Where the states come from

From D1264's claim check (`raise_claims`, `surface = 'narrative'`), and nowhere else. The page re-derives no state.

**`services/raiseClaims.ts`:**
- The narrative surface takes a version. `checkNarrativeVersion` checks one version and writes it with the same `writeSurfaces` the deck's check uses (D900). Other surfaces and other versions are untouched.
- **A fix inside the check.** Solution, Business model and Team keep their words in `narrative_sections.body` (D908), and the check never read them. A figure typed there was never a claim. It now is, under field key `section:<key>`. This also applies to the newest version on every Evidence refresh.

### 2. The reads and the write

| Route | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/cycles/raise/narrative/:version/claims` | The narrative's reader, then Evidence's gate | Each claim with its section, its figure, the stated value, its state, the best source (label, provider, date), the founder's note when it applies, and when it was checked. Only the founder also gets the record's figure, and for a Not-backed claim the pinned records whose kind could back it (D1263's `backs`). |
| `POST /api/cycles/raise/narrative/:version/check` | The founder | "Run the check" on this version. It logs one `raise.investor-narrative` cycle row, with no figure. |

- **Gates.**
  - The narrative's own gate runs first: an Admin gives a reason and only reads (D914).
  - Evidence's gate runs second, because claims are evidence. Only the founder sees a record's figure. A Super Admin who has given a reason reads states, sections and sources. An Admin who is not a Super Admin reads no claim, as on Evidence (D1263). A support session reads none.
- **Version.** A version is one of the venture's saved versions. Version 0 is the live deck, not a version, and answers 404.
