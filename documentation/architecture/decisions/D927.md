## D927 — Partner invitations part 4: the overview read, one invitation's read, the offer preview, and whether a resend renewed the signing link

Issue #2723; part 4 of Partner invitations (#2370, D923 §6). Worker reads and
their clients only; parts 6 and 7 draw them. No migration.

Code:

- `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/admin_partners_reads.ts`, mounted at
  `/api/admin/partners` before `admin_partners.ts`;
- `services/partnerInvitationReads.ts`;
- the resend reply in `routes/admin_partners.ts`.

The clients are `adminPartners.overview`, `getInvitation` and `previewOffers`
in `frontend/src/lib/api.js`. The tests are in
`cloudflare-worker/test/partner_invitation_reads_d927.test.ts`.

### 1. The rules

- **Read only.** No read here writes a row, logs a view, or calls the
  invitee's token routes, which move Sent to Opened. A test compares every
  table's row count before and after the reads.
- **Every route is `requireAdmin`.**
- **The words are D923 §2's:**
  - **open** is a status of sent, viewed, profiled, proposed, selected or
    finalized, with the link not lapsed;
  - **lapsed** is one of those statuses with `expires_at` passed;
  - **a stored `expired`** keeps its own meaning: signed after the link
    lapsed, deal voided. It is neither open nor lapsed.
- **D1's zone-less stamps are UTC.**
- **Each block answers alone in D893's shape:** a failed block is
  `{ available: false, unreadable: true, reason }`, never zeros.
- **The agreement-ready rule now has a Worker copy,** `agreementReadiness`.
  It is the same rule as the page's `agreementReady`. The agreement is
  `not_ready` when:
  - the Partner Services body cannot be read (`template_unreadable`);
  - it has no body (`template_has_no_body`);
  - it holds a field finalize leaves empty: a sender field outside the five
    finalize fills, or any `counterparty.*` field (`fields`).

### 2. `GET /api/admin/partners/overview`

The response is `{ as_of, tiles, needs_you, c1, c2, c3, c4 }`.

- **`tiles`**, each its own block:

  | Tile | Returns |
  |---|---|
  | `open` | `count` and `oldest_first_sent_at` (`created_at`, which a resend does not move) |
  | `lapsed` | `count` |
  | `signed_90d` | `count` by `signed_at` |
  | `days_to_sign` | `median_days` from first send to signed over the last 10 signed, and `over_signed`. `null` when none is signed, never 0. |
  | `active_deals` | `count`, `ending_within_30d`, and `term_over_not_closed` (active past its end, before the daily sweep) |
  | `awaiting_signature` | `count`, and `invitation_lapsed` (its signature would be voided at activation) |
  | `redemptions_90d` | `count` |
  | `failed_sends_30d` | invitations whose latest send, in the last 30 days, did not go, from part 1's columns, with `by_reason`. Part 1 stores only the latest send per invitation. |

- **`needs_you`** is `{ kinds, rows, unreadable_kinds }`.
  - **`kinds`** holds each kind as its own block.
  - **`rows`** is every readable kind's rows, each `{ kind, id, … }`.
  - **`unreadable_kinds`** names any kind that failed, so the total is never
    taken as complete.

  The kinds:

  | Kind | Its rows |
  |---|---|
  | `lapsed` | `{ id, stage, lapsed_at }` |
  | `agreement_blocked` | open invitations at Offer chosen while the agreement is `not_ready`. The block also carries `agreement`, the rule's answer. |
  | `failed_send` | open invitations whose latest send did not go: `{ id, reason, last_sent_at }` |
  | `deal_ending` | active deals ending within 30 days: `{ id, ends_at }` |
  | `unattached_account` | partner accounts with no firm: `{ id }` (`users.id`) |
  | `due_diligence_open` | active deals whose partner has an open or in-review case: `{ id, case_uid, case_status }` |
  | `revshare_closing` | rev-share deals with an attribution window closing within 30 days: `{ id, windows }` |

- **`c1`** has, for each open stage, `count`, `oldest_entered_at`,
  `oldest_days` and `entered_not_recorded`. It also has `closed`: `signed`,
  `lapsed`, `revoked` and `signed_after_lapse`.
  - **When a stage was entered:**
    - Sent: `created_at`.
    - Opened: `viewed_at`, or else its activity row.
    - Profile done, Offers shown, Offer chosen and Agreement out: the latest
      onboarding activity row for that step.
  - **An invitation with no such row** is counted in `entered_not_recorded`,
    never given a time of zero.
- **`c2`** has 12 Monday weeks of `sent` (by first send) and `signed`. The
  current week carries `to_date`.
- **`c3`** has deals by type and state: proposed, awaiting signature, active,
  `term_over` (active past its end), terminated, expired, voided and other.
- **`c4`** has 12 weeks of redemptions by `attribution_kind`: `referral`,
  `deal_sourcing_revshare` and other.

### 3. `GET /api/admin/partners/invitations/:id`

The response is
`{ invitation, steps, offers_seen, chosen_deal, agreement_refusals, history }`.
404 when there is no such invitation.

- **`invitation`:** the row with `personal_message`, the send columns, the
  inviter's name and `is_lapsed`. It carries no token.
- **`steps`:** `sent`, `opened`, `profile_done`, `offers_shown`,
  `offer_chosen_first`, `offer_chosen_latest`, `agreement_out` and `signed`.
  - They come from the onboarding `activity_logs` rows, which carry the
    invitation id. `opened` prefers `viewed_at`.
  - Each date is `null` when its row is absent.
- **`offers_seen`:** `buildProposals(allowed, profile)` over the stored
  profile, computed and never stored.
  - It is shown only once the invitee asked for offers, that is, once a
    `partner_proposals_generated` row exists.
  - The profile's answers are not returned; they stay a separate, logged read
    (D923 §3). A custom offer quotes the invitee's motivation, because that is
    the offer they saw.
- **`chosen_deal`:** the latest deal with its proposal, and its envelope's
  status, sent date and the partner's pending signing-link expiry. The signing
  token is never returned.
- **`agreement_refusals`:** the `partner_envelope_refused` rows, as `{ at,
  code, fields }`.
- **`history`:**
  - `items` are created, resent and revoked, from `admin_audit_log`, each with
    `at` and `by` (the admin's name):
    - created and resent carry `email_sent`;
    - a resend made after this decision carries `signing_link`;
    - revoked carries `reason`.
  - `signing_link_renewals` are D924's renewals, from the envelope's
    `esign_audit_events`, each with `renewed` and `previous_link_restored`.

### 4. `GET /api/admin/partners/offers/preview?types=`

The response is `buildProposals`' text for those types over a neutral, empty
profile, with `source: 'worker'`. Whose text is canonical is O-OFFER (D923 §5);
this read serves the Worker's.

- **The phrases that depend on the profile are marked** in each offer's
  `profile_dependent`, as `{ field, what }`:
  - equity hours;
  - the capital commitment;
  - the capital tier;
  - the custom text.
- **Only the first three types are ever offered.** Any after the third are
  listed in `not_offered`.
- **Refusals:** an unknown type is 400 `unknown_deal_type`, and no types is 400
  `types_required`.

### 5. Resend says whether it renewed the signing link

`POST /api/admin/partners/invitations/:id/resend` keeps every field it had and
adds `signing_link`:

- `'renewed'`: the pending partner signing link was renewed and mailed;
- `'not_renewed'`: the renewal email failed and the prior link was restored;
- `null`: no partner signature was pending, so the onboarding email went
  instead.

The audit row `partner_invitation_resent` records the same value, so the
history (§3) can show it for every resend from now on.
