## D927 — Partner invitations' reads: the Overview, one invitation, the offer preview, and whether a resend renewed the signing link

- **Status:** built by S10 in slot S05 for #2723 (part 4 of #2370).
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `6daf1a3173`, which carries D923's desk and D924's send columns (#2735). Paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Canvas:** "Partner invitations", boards PI1, PI3 and PI4. S1's map is `documentation/audits/PARTNER_INVITATIONS_MAP_2026-10-24.md`: Part A's PI1 and PI3 rows, and Part B §3 and §6.
- **Migration:** none.

### 1. The decision

Three reads and one new reply field. The reads live in `routes/admin_partners_reads.ts`; their routes are in `routes/admin_partners.ts`, under the same `requireAdmin` as every route there.

| Route | Answers |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/admin/partners/overview` | `{ as_of, tiles, needs_you, c1, c2, c3, c4 }` |
| `GET /api/admin/partners/invitations/:id` | `{ invitation, steps, offers_seen, chosen_deal, refusals, history }`; an unknown id answers 404 |
| `GET /api/admin/partners/offers/preview?types=a,b` | `{ types, profile: 'neutral', offers }`; no type, an unknown type or more than three answers 400 |
| `POST /api/admin/partners/invitations/:id/resend` | today's reply, plus `signing_link` |

The clients are `api.adminPartners.overview()`, `getInvitation(id)` and `previewOffers(types)` in `frontend/src/lib/api.js`.

### 2. Nothing here writes

- Every statement in the three reads is a SELECT. The tests check SQLite's own `total_changes()` before and after each read.
- The invitee's token routes are never called. They flip Sent to Opened and write an activity row.
- **The profile's answers are not in these reads.** They stay the separate, logged read (D923 §3).
  - The offers an invitee saw are `buildProposals` over their stored profile, and some offers quote an answer: equity's hours, the capital amount, and the custom deal's text.
  - Each quoted answer is replaced by "From their answers", and the field is listed in `profile_dependent`. The capital tier the invitee was offered is kept, because it is an offer term, not an answer.

### 3. Each block fails on its own

D893's pattern: `{ available: true, … }`, or `{ available: false, reason }`. The reason is a sentence ending "That is not the same as none.", never the database's text and never a zero.

- **Each tile fails on its own,** and so does each Needs you kind. A kind that fails is listed in `needs_you.unreadable`, and the total counts only the kinds that answered.
- **Each store is read once and shared.** A store that fails costs only the blocks that read it.
- **The overview reads every row,** not a capped list: invitations, deals and redemptions, then cut in the Worker. This is the scale D918 chose for the Events desk. It ends D923's Guard L and Guard F for the figures here.

### 4. What each block counts

The words are D923 §2's.
- **Open:** a stored stage from `sent` to `finalized` that is not Lapsed.
- **Lapsed:** `expires_at` has passed and the invitation is not signed, revoked or stored `expired`.
- **Signed after the link lapsed:** a stored `expired`, kept apart from Lapsed.
- **Dates:** D1's zone-less stamps are UTC. Every comparison is made in the Worker on epoch milliseconds, by one rule.

**Tiles.**
- **Open invitations,** with the oldest first send (`created_at`).
- **Lapsed, not closed.**
- **Signed in the last 90 days.**
- **The median days from first send to signed,** over the last 10 signed, with how many it was taken over. None signed is `null`, never 0.
- **Active deals,** with how many end within 30 days. An active deal past its end, before the daily sweep, counts as `term_over`, never negative days.
- **Awaiting signature,** with how many belong to a lapsed invitation. Activation voids a signature on those.
- **Redemptions in the last 90 days.**
- **Failed sends in the last 30 days.** D924 stores only the last send per invitation, so this counts invitations whose last send, within 30 days, was not sent.

**Needs you.** Each row has its `kind` and its record's `id`:
- `lapsed`: the invitation, its stage and when it lapsed;
- `agreement_blocked`: an open invitation at Offer chosen while the Partner Services agreement cannot be sent (§5), with why and the fields;
- `send_failed`: an invitation whose last send failed and that is not closed, with D924's reason;
- `deal_ending`: an active deal ending within 30 days;
- `unattached_account`: a partner account with no firm;
- `dd_open`: an active deal whose partner has an open or in-review due-diligence case, with the case's uid;
- `revshare_closing`: a deal-sourcing redemption whose 365-day window closes within 30 days.

**C1.** Each open stage carries:
- its count;
- the oldest time an invitation there entered it;
- `undated`, the ones whose entry has no activity row, which are never dated as now.

The entry times come from:
- Sent: the first send;
- Opened: the `partner_invitation_viewed` row, else `viewed_at`;
- Profile done, Offers shown and Agreement out: their own rows;
- Offer chosen: the first `partner_proposal_selected` row.

Closed counts are Signed, Lapsed, Revoked, and signed after the link lapsed.

**C2.** Invitations sent (first send) and signed per week, over 12 weeks starting on a Monday, UTC. The current week is marked.

**C3.** Deals by type and state. An active deal past its end is `term_over`.

**C4.** Redemptions per week by attribution (`referral` or `deal_sourcing_revshare`), over 12 weeks.

### 5. The agreement-ready rule, in the Worker

D923 §2's rule, as the page applies it, is the same here (`agreementReady`).

The Partner Services agreement cannot be sent when:
- it has no body; or
- it holds a field finalize leaves empty: a sender field outside the five finalize fills, or any `counterparty.*` field.

A template store that cannot be read fails only the `agreement_blocked` kind, and nothing is said either way. With no library row, the bundled `partner_msa_v2.md` has no such field, so Offer chosen is not blocked.

### 6. One invitation

- **`invitation`:** the row, now with `personal_message`. It carries the same link and lapsed flag as the list.
- **`steps`:** sent, opened, profile done, offers shown, offer chosen (first and latest), agreement out and signed.
  - Each comes from that step's onboarding `activity_logs` row, matched on the invitation id in its details.
  - Each is `null` when its row is absent, never the creation time. Opened falls back to `viewed_at`.
- **`offers_seen`:** `{ seen: false, offers: [] }` until a `partner_proposals_generated` row exists. After that, the offers over the stored profile, with no answer verbatim (§2).
- **`chosen_deal`:** the latest deal for the invitation, or its resulting deal, with its stored label and summary.
  - It includes its envelope's status and created time.
  - It includes the pending partner signature's link expiry, never the link or its token.
- **`refusals`:** each `partner_envelope_refused` row, with its code and the fields it names.
- **`history`:** created, resent and revoked from `admin_audit_log`, each with who, when, whether the email was sent, and the revoke reason.
  - It also includes D924's signing-link renewals from `esign_audit_events`: renewed, or not renewed with whether the prior link was restored.

### 7. The preview

`buildProposals`' text for the types, over a neutral profile, with each phrase that depends on the profile listed in `profile_dependent`:
- equity: `terms.time_commitment` and the excerpt;
- capital: `terms.capital_commitment_usd`, `granted_tier_investor` and the excerpt;
- custom: `terms.description` and the excerpt.

Whose text is canonical, the canvas's or the Worker's, is owner decision O-OFFER (D923 §5). This read serves the Worker's.

### 8. Resend's `signing_link`

D924's resend renews the pending partner signing link at Agreement out, but its reply did not say so. It now carries `signing_link`:
- `renewed`;
- `not_renewed`: the renewal email failed and the prior link was restored;
- `null`: no pending partner signature, so the onboarding email went instead.

Every field the reply had is kept. Part 7 reads this in the Resend result instead of inferring it.

`frontend/test/partner_reads_client_d927.test.mjs` (1) checks the three clients' requests. D924's 34 tests still pass.

  - ending soon counting past ends;
  - Term over dropped;

  - offers invented before they were shown;
