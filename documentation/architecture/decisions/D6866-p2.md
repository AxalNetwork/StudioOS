### 1. After close: four figures (DX23 A)

These figures appear once the closing reads closed, above the cap-table card
and the Record in Portfolio table. They are laid out two by two on a phone and
four across from `lg`.

| Figure | Read | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lines received | the closing read (`GET /closings/:uid`) | Received lines out of the lines not withdrawn. Withdrawn lines are named, up to two, otherwise counted, and they are not counted in the figure. |
| Total received | `GET /deals/:id/transfers` | Every recorded transfer on the deal, all time. This is the Transfer tab's total, with "n transfers recorded, all time" under it, in the closing's currency. |
| Executed paper | `GET /legal/esign`, filtered to the deal | Envelopes that are completed, and nothing else. |
| Reporting duties | `GET /reporting/desk?project=` (D1058) | The venture's duties whose own `closing_line_uid` is one of this closing's lines. The figure says whether they came from the signed paper, the closing template, or both. |

- **Who reads the duties.** Only an Admin, and only after close. The desk
  admits an Admin (`canAccessProject`), and the founder too, but the founder
  does not reach `/deals/closing`. For anyone else the figure is not drawn.
  The page never shows a 0 it did not read.
- **A failed read.** Any of the four says Unavailable with Try again
  (D5129). A read still in flight says Reading…. Neither is ever 0. Zero
  duties from a read that succeeded is a real 0, and it reads "None recorded
  on this closing's lines".
- **No guessed link.** A duty is counted only by its own `closing_line_uid`.
  It is never matched to a line by holder, venture or amount.

### 2. The cap-table card (DX23 A)

Only the founder decides the proposal (`decideCaptable`). The closing read
carries `captable.state` and `captable.decided_at`. The card shows the
decision as a word and a glyph: Accepted ✓ or Dismissed –, followed by
"Accepted by the founder on <date>." When the read has no date, it says
"· date not recorded" and never shows today's date. Proposed reads "Proposed ·
Waiting on the founder."; a closed closing cannot carry that state, but the
model draws it honestly.

### Where this departs from the canvas

- **Total received** is the recorded transfers' total, as the canvas's own
  caption says ("n transfers recorded, all time"). It is not the sum of the
  received lines. The two can differ, and the caption says which one it is.
- **Withdrawn lines** are named only up to two. Past that, they are counted.
