## D638 — The Super Admin's page for credit rules and grants

**Issue #1384, slot S08, 2026-10-07.** Frontend only. No Worker change, no
migration, and no Claude Design pass (S1, on #1384).

### Why

#1101 says every credit amount "lives in one table that the Super Admin can
edit, never in code". The table (`credit_reward_rules`, migration 381) and its
routes (`routes/admin_credit_rules.ts`, D540) existed, but no screen called
them. D540 listed the page under "Not built here". So the only way to change
an amount, switch a rule off or take a grant back was to call the API by hand.

### What it is

**`/admin/credit-rules`** (`frontend/src/pages/hq/CreditRulesRegistryPage.jsx`) is behind `hqOnly`,
so an admin without the elevation sees the Super Admin notice. The Worker
checks `requireSuperAdmin` on every call regardless. The page is drawn to the
HQ canvas's registry pattern, Q1 and Q2 (`hq-contracts` and `hq-credits` in
`design/canvases/integrated/ops-data.js`).

- **Reward rules.** Each rule shows:
  - its key, its kind (bounty, Lab milestone, reward or monthly cap) and its
    amount in credits and dollars;
  - its state;
  - who last changed it. A rule nobody has changed reads Not recorded, with
    the reason: the migration set it.

  The monthly bounty cap is listed with the others. "Change" opens a reason
  card holding the credits and the on/off switch.
  - **The dollar value is never an input.** 1 credit = US$1 (D540's
    `usd_value_fixed`).
  - **Only what changed is sent.** An edit that changes nothing, or credits
    outside 0 to 100,000, is refused on the page and never sent.
- **Grants.** The list from `GET /grants`: rule grants and D608's manual
  lines, newest first, the latest 200.
  - **Each row shows** the member, the change (a deduction is negative), the
    source (the rule, or "Manual adjustment") and the state, "Standing" or
    "Taken back <date>" with the reason on hover. A member with no email on
    record reads Not recorded, with the account id.
  - **"Take back"** is offered on a standing grant of credits. It opens a
    reason card. A revoke of credits already spent is refused by the Worker
    (`would_overdraw`), and the page shows that refusal as a sentence.
- **Each registry** has search, filters, a count line ("1–25 of N") and pages
  of 25.
  - Rules filter by kind.
  - Grants filter by source and state, and search by member, rule, source or
    exact account id.
- **The reason card** has the 10-character floor every Super Admin credit act
  shares (`CREDIT_REASON_MIN`). It says how many characters there are so far,
  and keeps its submit disabled until the floor is met. The Worker's refusals
  are shown as its own sentences.
- **Four states per list:**
  - loading;
  - empty, with "Clear filters" when the filters hide everything;
  - Not recorded, with its reason, on a cell nothing holds;
  - Unreadable, with "Try again". A failed read is never drawn as an empty
    table.

**Placement.** The page is on the HQ shell for the Super Admin. Its door is
the review queue's header (`/hq/review`), where an approved task already shows
what it pays. Q2's member credits view (#1391, D633) will link to it as well.
It is not a strip item, because the H36 strips are held to their verbatim
order.

**Beside it.** #1399 already shipped HQ · Credits at `/hq/credits`
(`frontend/src/pages/hq/CreditRulesPage.jsx`), reached from Platform. Both
pages stay. They call the same Worker routes. Their pure helpers stay in two
modules (`frontend/src/lib/creditRulesRegistry.js` and
`frontend/src/lib/creditRules.js`) because each defines `refusalSentence` and
the two definitions disagree.

### #1103's open questions

These were built on S08's picks and never answered by the owner. S1 settles
them as built, until the owner says otherwise:
1. **Staff never earn.** That means admin and partner accounts.
2. **A bounty that would cross the monthly cap** is refused whole.
3. **Revoking credits that are already spent** is refused. The page shows the
   Worker's refusal.

### Verified

`frontend/test/credit_rules_page_d638.test.mjs` has 8 tests, and the file
fails on main. They cover:
- the filters, search and paging;
- that a rule edit sends only what changed, with a trimmed reason;
- the reason floor;
- refusals as sentences, the spent-credits revoke among them;
- each list's states, rendered;
- which rows offer "Take back";
- the reason card's disabled submit;
- the `hqOnly` route, the review queue's link, and the client's calls.

35 of 35 mutations were caught.
