### The material is the record, not a summary of it

`services/portfolioNotes.ts` builds the lines the model sees. The draft route already scopes the read through `investorProjectIds` and refuses empty material before any model call.

- **Quarter-end reads.** Each completed quarter-end (counted from the Worker's date) is read the way `GET /api/positions/analytics?as_of=` reads it: paid-in and distributed counted on or before the date; NAV as the latest usable mark per company on or before it, a company with no usable mark carried at cost and counted apart; TVPI and DPI from those.
- **Events.** Every mark between two quarter-ends is listed with its date and its basis. A missing basis is shown as `UNRECORDED`. Distributions and new cheques are listed with their dates too.
- **Gaps are named.** A quarter-end with no recorded book is "a gap, not a zero". A quarter that holds a book but has no recorded mark, distribution or cheque is "a gap in the record", which the model is told never to call flat.
- **Nothing recorded, no tokens.** If no completed quarter-end holds a book, the material is `[]`, and `POST /api/research/drafts` answers `nothing_to_draft` (409) before any model call. The band then shows "No position is recorded at a completed quarter-end, so there is nothing to explain."
- **Instruction.** Cite each figure by its date, keep marks apart from cash, say the figures are gross of fees and carry, and do not forecast or tell the reader what to do.

### Nothing is kept or sent without Accept

This is the existing `ZoneDraft` contract. A run stores its draft only as a pending row in `research_zone_drafts`, which only this member's band reads. Accept stamps `accepted_at` and writes no other record. Neither surface has an `accept` hook, and nothing is sent anywhere.
