### What the canvas decides

- The profile starts with the vehicle, its manager, vintage and a dated size in its own currency. Dawn Capital Opp (€75m EUR) and Afore IV (US$185m USD) are drawn; amounts are never converted.
- Every figure carries three dates kept apart: Reported, Imported on and Source use reviewed. The same publisher and reported date follow one figure from the Sheet to the profile to the comparison.
- Two vehicles of one manager are two Sheets and two profiles. The manager history lists each vehicle by its label, and the size chart uses only that vehicle's observations.
- Save to shortlist is drawn in six states, including the link to the founder's dossier.
- The project picker shows an existing membership and an archived project read-only, with the outcomes and refusals.
- A comparison shows EUR and USD in their own cells with sources, and one unknown value as Not recorded. Compare in project adds to an existing comparison and refuses a seventh fund.
- States: loading, Unavailable with Retry, a missing record (404) and a withdrawn source (410), including a source withdrawn while the Sheet is open or inside a comparison.
- Phone at 390 has the Sheet, the profile, the picker and a six-fund comparison by attribute and by fund. Dark mode, focus order and announcements have their own boards.
