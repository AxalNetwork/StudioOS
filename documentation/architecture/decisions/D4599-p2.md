## D4599 part 2 — The recorded walkthrough's UI: Present › Record and pd35's Walkthrough column (#4599)

Pitch audit 5/5 (#4599), its third part. Part 1 (`D4599.md`, PR #5727) added the
import entry and cut the Overview's reads. This part connects the walkthrough
the Worker already keeps (`routes/deck_recordings.ts`, D1590, #3837) to the
screens D1590 §"Still to come" names: the pd36 Record screen, the adapter swap
and pd35's Walkthrough column. The share viewer playing the attached walkthrough
is not in it (below).

### The swap

`deckV2Adapter.walkthrough` answers through its client:
`through(() => deckRecordingsApi.list(deckId), SURFACES.walkthrough.issue)`.
`SWAPPED` is derived (D4134), so no list changed. The `deckRecordings.js` import
sits in file-name order. `pitch_v2_adapter_d1604` used `walkthrough` as its
example of a row still off. It now uses `outlineReorder`, which is still off.

### The switch is HQ's, not the UI's

The UI writes no flag and turns nothing on. Every route reads
`deck:walkthrough` (`flagsFor(...).on`). While it is off they answer 404
`not_switched_on`, and the adapter reports that as "Not switched on yet.":

- **Present › Record** draws that sentence. It asks for no camera, reads no
  quote and no links, and sends nothing.
- **pd35's table** leaves out the Walkthrough column.

**For the owner.** A deck flag with no row is on (D1509: "a flag is a switch HQ
turns off"). So founders see the walkthrough after this deploys unless HQ ›
Decks has a `deck:walkthrough` row set to off. This PR does not change that
default or write a row. If the walkthrough must stay off until the share
viewer plays it, switch it off in HQ before this merges.

### Present › Record (pd36)

`RecordPanel.jsx`, opened by Record beside Rehearse, or by `?view=record`
(pd35's "Record ›" link). While it is open the arrow keys and the phone remote
are Record's, as they are Rehearse's.

- **One take a card, as the Worker stores them.** Start asks for the camera and
  microphone, then records the card on the stage. ← → (or Back / Next) ends
  this card's take and starts the next card's on the same stream. Stop ends the
  last. Each finished take is sent at once: `POST /recordings` with the video,
  and its own sound as a 32 kbps audio track for the captions. The Worker does
  not caption a video file.
- **The card number is the Worker's.** It is the card's place among all the
  version's slides, hidden ones included (`slideNumbers`). Present shows only
  the cards that are not hidden, so the second shown card of `[A, hidden, B]` is
  sent as card 3.
- **The chips:** "Slide 1 · Take 2 ✓" (with Play, Retake and Delete),
  "Recording", "Not recorded", "Sending…", or "Not sent" with the reason.
- **Retake** records the card again. The Worker removes the earlier take.
- **Delete** asks first ("Delete this take? The take and its file are removed
  for good."). A 503 (the file could not be deleted, so the Worker keeps the
  row) says so and offers Try again.
- **Playback** plays the take from `/media` (cookie auth, `private, no-store`).
  Under it are the Listen job's cues at the playhead, or "Captions not recorded
  for this take." when the Worker stored `null`.
- **Attach** is "Attach to [Fund]'s link" over the version's live investor
  links (`deckV2Adapter.investorLinks`), with a picker when there are several.
  Once attached it reads "Remove from [Fund]'s link". It is off until a card is
  recorded, with the Worker's own reason.
- **The price.** "Captions · Holds up to $X a take · you pay what it uses" comes
  from `POST /recordings/quote` for a full take. A refused quote, or a balance
  short of the hold, disables Start before the camera is asked for.
- **Retention** is pd36's own line, "Stored with your deck; delete it any time."
  (owner decision W = A).

### Truthful states

| State | What is drawn |
|---|---|
| Reading | "Reading your walkthrough…" |
| Switched off | "Not switched on yet.", no recorder |
| Read failed | "Your walkthrough could not be read." with Try again. It is never drawn as an empty walkthrough. |
| Camera or microphone refused (`NotAllowedError`, `SecurityError`) | "Camera and microphone access was refused. Allow both for this site in your browser's settings, then try again." with Try again |
| No device, or device busy | Their own sentences, with Try again |
| No MediaRecorder | "This browser cannot record video here." Nothing is asked for. |
| Under one second, or over 40 MB | Not sent, with the reason. Record again. |
| Interrupted upload (no Worker answer: a network drop or the request's timeout) | "The upload was interrupted. The take is still here: send it again." Send again sends the same take. |
| Refused upload (413 full plan, 402 budget, 415 format) | The Worker's own sentence, with its top-up link when it gives one. Send again, or Record again. |
| Links unreadable | "Your investor links could not be read." with Try again |

### pd35's Walkthrough column

`InvestorLinksTable.jsx` reads `deckV2Adapter.walkthrough` beside the links.
Each row shows one of:

- "Attached · 3 slides" with Remove;
- "Attach · 3 slides" on a live link, once a card is recorded;
- "Record ›", which opens Present on Record;
- "Not attached", for a link that is no longer live.

A failed read says "Could not be read" with Try again, never "Record ›".

### Where the build departs from the boards

The boards are `design/incoming/Pitch Deck Studio v2 Page.dc.html` (`FR.pd36`,
`FR.pd35`) and `Pitch Deck Studio v2 · Boards.dc.html` (PD-8).

- **No caption bar while recording.** pd36 draws one under the slide. Captions
  come from the Listen job after a take is sent, and nothing transcribes live
  here. So the bar is drawn in playback, from the stored cues.
- **Camera and Microphone are pickers that show after access is given.** The
  browser names devices only once access is allowed. Before that the line reads
  "The camera and microphone are asked for when you start."
- **Remove and Attach in the table.** pd35 draws only "Attached · [3] slides"
  and "Record ›". Removing a walkthrough from a link needs a control, and the
  row is where the founder sees which links have it.
- **Delete and Play on the chips.** pd36 draws only Retake. Delete is the
  owner's W = A requirement, and Play is the issue's playback criterion.
- **Desktop only,** as Rehearse is. Present on a phone is the viewer and the
  remote. pd36 is drawn at 1280.

### Not in this part

- **The share viewer playing the attached walkthrough** (D1590's last
  follow-up). It needs a public read of an attached link's takes, which is a
  Worker route that does not exist. No client method is added for it (D527).
- **The share page's "Walkthrough recording" switch** in the Pitch Studio v2
  canvas (PS-06, drawn as Later). That page is not built. The table column and
  Present › Record are the approved places to attach.
- **A rail option for the captions model.** The Worker reads
  `transcribe:walkthrough` on the Listen job's own choice (DK11C). That option
  is not in `railOptions/founder.js` or the Worker's option catalogue, so the
  member cannot pick its model yet.

### Tests

`frontend/test/pitch_walkthrough_d4599.test.mjs` has 14 cases. They mount
`RecordPanel`, `PitchPresent` (`?view=record`) and `InvestorLinksTable` on a
stubbed `fetch` with the real clients. The camera, recorder and clock are the
test's own:

- the swap and the switch;
- the words and numbering;
- the switch off, with no camera asked for and nothing sent;
- a failed read with Try again;
- record, move on and stop sending each card under the Worker's number;
- a take that is too short;
- camera access refused, then Try again;
- no recorder;
- an interrupted upload, then a refusal, then Send again;
- playback with captions, retake, delete behind its confirm (a failed delete
  kept), and attach and remove;
- an unpayable quote disabling Start;
- Present opening on Record, on and off;
- the column on, off and unreadable.

**Mutation checks.** 26 source mutations were each applied on their own, and a
case failed every time. Among them:

- the swap left off;
- the shown index sent as the card;
- moving on without sending;
- the short-take check skipped;
- the refusal words bypassed;
- the failed take not kept;
- delete without its confirm;
- attach sending `false`;
- Start not blocked by the price;
- the column drawn while off;
- each Try again unwired.
