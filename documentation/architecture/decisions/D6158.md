### What the canvas decides

- `/deals/closing` becomes tabs: Paper, Conditions, Transfer and Audit, each with its count.
- Paper lists every envelope with its state as a word and a glyph, and signatures counted per envelope.
- Conditions is read only: open first, then Met, then Waived, with "2 open conditions hold every transfer on this deal" and Open in Investment committee as the one action (the dialogs are #6142's). An Admin hold from a decision the Admin cannot see shows no count.
- Transfer shows the disclaimer once and the all-time total. A held transfer disables Record transfer with its reason beside it; a hold that could not be read never reads as "nothing holds it".
- A checklist item opens in a side sheet with status, owner, due date and note; Overdue, Blocked and Skipped each have a word and a glyph.
- The investor sees their own line and the round total, the checklist read only, their own paper, no Audit tab, and Open my closing room (#6188's).
- After close: lines received, executed paper, the cap-table decision, reporting duties and Record in Portfolio per line. Before close, each unmet rule is written beside the unavailable Close action.
- Failed reads use the D5129 word Unavailable. Phone 390 for Closing and Commit, and dark, are drawn.
