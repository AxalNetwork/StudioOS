## D557 — The Learning center's core: content, courses, lessons, progress and a federated catalog (migration 388)

**The base the Learning epic (#1157) builds on. It adds the store, the member
routes and the Super Admin's authoring, and a client module for each so the
screens (#1163) can call them.** Slot S08, issue #1158, 2026-10-06. The canvas
is `design/incoming/Learning.dc.html` (D566).

### Migration 388

| Table | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `learning_items` | One video or reading. `kind`, `title`, `summary`, `body` (markdown, readings), `video_source` / `video_url` / `r2_key`, `duration_seconds`, `thumbnail_url`, `transcript`, `topic`, `level`, `visibility`, `status`, `created_by`, timestamps and `published_at`. |

| `learning_course_modules` | A course's modules, ordered by `position`. |
| `learning_lessons` | A module's lessons. `kind` is `video`, `reading`, `exercise`, `exam` or `webinar`. `ref_uid` is what the lesson opens, and `required` marks whether it counts. |
| `learning_progress` | A member's own progress on an item, lesson or course: `status` (started or completed), `percent`, `position_seconds`, `started_at`, `completed_at`. Unique on (`user_id`, `subject_kind`, `subject_uid`). |

Every closed set is a CHECK.

**The canvas added two fields**, at S1's request on #1158:
- `learning_items.transcript`. It is nullable, and the player's transcript
  panel reads "Not recorded" when it is null.
- `learning_courses.resources_json`. It is a list of `{title, url}`, and
  every url must be https.

**Null is not recorded, never a guess.** A video with no duration has none,
not 0. A progress row with no percent was opened, not measured at 0%.

### Who sees what: `canSeeLearning`

One function, `services/learningAccess.ts`, decides visibility for every
member read: the catalog, a course, an item, and the subject of a progress
write.
- Draft and archived content is the Super Admin's only.
- `program` content stays hidden from everyone but the Super Admin until #1159
  extends this same check to enrolled members.
- Published, `open` content is every signed-in member's.

The catalog's SQL narrows with the same predicate, so its totals and pages
agree with the rows it returns. A hidden subject answers 404, never 403, so a
draft's slug is not confirmed to exist.

### Member routes: `routes/learning.ts`, at `/api/learning`

- **`GET /catalog?kind=&q=&topic=&level=&limit=&offset=`** returns one list
  over six kinds from four stores, read-only:
  - courses, videos and readings, from this store;
  - published articles (`articles`);
  - published, non-internal publications (`admin_publications`, the public
    /insights filter);
  - upcoming public, published, admin-approved events (`events`).

  **The row shape.** Each row carries `kind`, `id` (uid or slug), `title`,
  `summary`, `href`, `date` and `source`.
  - **Article rows** also carry the author's `name` and `role` and
    `read_minutes`, as the canvas draws them. A read time that was never
    computed (0) is `null`, not "0 min read".
  - **Publication rows** say `file.recorded: false`. The canvas draws a PDF
    and its size, but the publication store holds no file.

  **`sources`.** It carries one state per kind:
  - `ready`, with its `total`;
  - `unreadable`, with the reason. A store that cannot be read is never an
    empty list, and the others still answer;
  - `filtered_out`, when a `topic` or `level` filter names a field that
    source does not have. Only videos and readings have a topic, and only they
    and courses have a level.

  **Paging.** Without `kind`, the catalog is the newest `limit` rows across
  every source. `offset` pages one kind at a time.
- **`GET /courses/:slug`** returns the course, its resources, its modules and
  lessons, and the caller's progress on each. The course percent is measured
  over required lessons. A course with none has no percentage.
- **`GET /items/:uid`** returns one item, with `transcript_recorded` and the
  caller's progress.
- **`POST /progress`** records the caller's own progress.
  - The user is the session's, never the body's.
  - The subject must exist and be visible to the caller.
  - A completed subject stays completed, and the percent never goes
    backwards.
- **`GET /me`** returns the caller's started and completed subjects, with
  titles.

### Super Admin routes: `routes/admin_learning.ts`, at `/api/admin/learning`

- **The gate.** Every route calls `requireSuperAdmin`. A plain admin, HQ or
  branch, authors nothing and cannot list drafts.
- **Items and courses** can be created (as drafts), edited, published,
  unpublished and archived.
  - Publishing a video needs its link, and a reading needs its text.
  - Publishing a course needs at least one lesson.
  - A course slug is lowercase, digits and hyphens, and unique (409).
- **Modules and lessons** can be added, edited, removed and reordered.
  - Positions are assigned inside the insert.
  - A reorder must list every child exactly once.
  - Only an empty module can be deleted, decided in the delete itself.
- **What a lesson opens is checked.**
  - A video or reading lesson names an item of its own kind.
  - A webinar lesson names an event slug.
  - Exercise and exam lessons are refused until #1160 builds them.
- **A video is a link on the allow-list.** It must be https from YouTube,
  Vimeo, `videodelivery.net` or a `*.cloudflarestream.com` customer host, with
  no credentials and no port. An upload (`video_source = 'r2'`) has no upload
  path yet and is refused, rather than stored without a file.
- **Every write is recorded** with `logAdminAction`: the actor, the action
  (`learning.item.create`, `learning.course.publish` and so on) and the uid.
- **Updates name only columns from a fixed list**, never a key from the body.

### Clients

- `frontend/src/lib/api/learning.js` (`learningApi`) for the member routes.
- `frontend/src/lib/api/adminLearning.js` (`adminLearningApi`) for authoring.
- Each is re-exported by one line in `api.js` (D527). `check-api-drift` maps

### Not built

### Verified

`cloudflare-worker/test/learning_core_d557.test.ts` has 11 tests, run on a
database built from the baseline and every migration through 388. It covers:
- the migration and its CHECKs;
- the three gates, with the audit row;
- the allow-list, with lookalike hosts, http, credentials, ports and
  `javascript:` refused;
- drafts, archived and program content hidden from members and from totals;
- the publish preconditions;
- lesson references and reordering;
- progress ownership, completion and percent;
- the federated catalog, with the article row's author and reading time;
- an unreadable source answering beside the others;
- a `%` search term bound and escaped.

28 of 28 mutations were caught, each file restored and checked by sha256.
