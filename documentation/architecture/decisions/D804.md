## D804 — The Licences page, part 2: the Contract, Statements and Requests tabs, and the "Request a change" sheet

- **Status:** decided by S1 for the owner, 2026-10-20 (#2241; part of D795, #2228). It is the second half of the Licences page, split from D800 (#2233).
- **Basis:** `main` after D798 (#2239, #2254) and D799 (#2249). Frontend paths are relative to `frontend/src/`.
- **Design:** `design/incoming/Licences Page.dc.html`, boards L3, L4 and L5, and the sheet on L5 and L8.

### Where it sits

The tabs are four components under `pages/subsidiary/licence/`, as S1 laid out on #2241:
- `ContractTab.jsx`, `StatementsTab.jsx` and `RequestsTab.jsx`, the three tabs;
- `RequestChangeSheet.jsx`, the sheet;
- `licenceTabsModel.js`, their pure functions, shared with the tests.

**None of them draws a rail.** D800 mounts them in its tab frame.

**Until then, each tab is its own route** behind the same `guard(['admin'])` as `/admin/my-licence`: `/admin/my-licence/contract`, `/statements` and `/requests`.
- `LicenceTabRoute.jsx` wraps each tab in `AdminBoardFrame`, the Licences board's own frame, so every tab carries the Admin rail.
- The page map (`lib/railPages.js`) names the three paths as aliases of `Admin · Licences`, so D781's render test draws each one. No `RENDER_NOT_YET` line is added.
- Whichever of D800 and this PR merges second resolves the three route lines in `App.jsx`.
- **Resolved (2026-10-20):** D800 (#2283) merged second. The three paths mount `MyLicencePage`, which draws these tabs in its frame. `LicenceTabRoute.jsx` and `LicenceTabsNav` are retired.

**No new `/api/*` method.** The tabs read through D798's `licenceAccountApi`, D799's `licenceRequestsApi` and `api.myLicence`. D798's contract view gains one field, `drafted_at` (the row's `created_at`), for the Drafted step.

### Contract (L3)

- **The status line** runs Drafted → Sent → Signed, each with its date. A step not reached reads "Not recorded".
- **"In force" and "Pending" show together,** as the Summary card shows them: "Sent 12 Oct · waiting for your signature / In force: v6, signed 2 Mar 2025".
  - The body drawn is the pending contract, or the one in force when nothing is pending.
- **The body is the stored text, never re-rendered.**
  - The section index comes from its own `## N. Title` headings: §1–§8, §19 and §22 on D803's template.
  - Every merge field the licence has not filled stays in the stored body as `{{name}}` (D451) and is drawn "[not filled]". A merge token is never printed as written.
- **Buttons:**
  - "Download PDF" saves D798's PDF.
  - "Sign" is drawn only when D798 gives this caller a `sign_url`. Otherwise its `sign_reason` is shown.
- **Earlier versions** are listed read-only, each with its PDF.
- **With nothing sent,** the tab is the "Waiting for HQ" card. On a branch, it shows D798's reason.

### Statements (L4)

- **The table** has Period, Gross, Share, Owed, Paid, Disputed and Status. The first row is open on its streams.
- **Streams:**
  - The four the canvas draws are always listed: Subscriptions, Licence fees, Token margin, and Platform licence royalties, which D802 stores.
  - A stream not stored reads "Not reported", and an estimated one carries "Estimated".
  - `streams_json` is read as stored, so a further stream follows under its key.
  - Each statement shows its payment reference.
- **"How to pay HQ"** is D798's masked block (method, payee, the bank lines and the warning).
  - With no account for the fee, it is the refusal's sentence.
  - It never shows a full account number, and nothing here marks a statement paid.
- **"Annual licence fee invoices"** are D802's `fee_invoices`. They are listed with their PDFs when the answer carries them. Until D802 merges, the card says they are not on this page yet.
- **"Usage reports":** each period is sent (with its date), late, due, or not sent, as D798 reports it.
  - "Send now" is drawn only when the answer's `send_now` is true, which is on a branch (D808 limits it).
  - On HQ, the Worker's reason is shown instead.
- **"Dispute a line"** is offered on an issued or paid statement.
  - It takes an amount in the statement's currency, as whole cents with at most two decimals and no more than owed, and a reason of at least 10 characters.
  - It is D798's request to HQ, and it never writes the statement.

### Requests (L5)

- **The five buttons:** Request renewal, Request a change, Request seats, Request a delegate, and Give notice of non-renewal.
  - Change and seats open the sheet (on its Seats chip for seats).
  - Renewal, a delegate (email, optionally a name) and the notice are short forms.
  - A kind this licence does not offer (`kinds_hidden`, D206) is disabled, with the Worker's reason.
- **The renewal timeline** runs Today → Notice deadline → Renewal. The deadline is the renewal date less D797's `non_renewal_notice_days`, read from `/mine`'s terms. It reads "Not recorded" until D797 (#2242) puts the terms there.
- **"Your requests"** shows each request's kind ("Seats · Founder +20", "Change · Fee"), the date sent, HQ's response time (D799's `response_hours`), its status and HQ's answer inline.
  - The status is Open; Past response time, for an open request past its band; or the decided status.
  - An open request without an answer says so.

### The "Request a change" sheet (L5, L8)

- **Layout:** a 460px panel on the right from `sm` up, and the whole screen on a phone (`SHEET_CLASS`).
- **The chips map to D799's kinds:**
  - Seats is a `seat_increase` (seat type and count).
  - Territory, Fee and Term are a `terms_change` of that term.
  - "A deal term" is a `terms_change` of a term the Worker accepts beyond those four (`requestable_terms`). D797's keys join that list when it merges. Until then the chip says no deal term can be requested yet.
- **"As it stands"** is read-only, from `/mine`'s licence. It shows the contract section when the terms carry one, and a `terms_change` sends it as `current`.
- **The rest of the sheet:** what is asked and why, the response sentence ("HQ answers seat requests within 72 hours. Nothing changes until HQ does."), Send and Cancel.
- **Prefilled from the Terms tab (D800):** `/admin/my-licence/requests?change=<term>` (`requestChangeHref`) opens the sheet on that term's chip.

### Tests

`frontend/test/licences_tabs_d804.test.mjs` has 17 tests. Each tab is rendered from fixture answers shaped as D798 and D799 send them. The tests cover:

- in force and pending together;
- the status line;
- the body, its index and "[not filled]";
- Sign only with `sign_url`;
- the Waiting for HQ card and the branch reason;
- the PDF download;
- the streams with the royalty and the chips;
- the masked payment and the refusal's sentence;
- Send now hidden on HQ and shown on a branch;
- the fee invoices, both present and missing;
- the dispute's amount and its offer;
- the five buttons, the timeline and the requests table;
- a hidden kind;
- the simple requests' bodies;
- the chips' kinds and the requests they send;
- the prefill from `?change=`;
- the full-screen phone sheet;
- the routes, the rail frame and the page-map aliases;
- that no tab calls the API directly.

36 mutations were made, each breaking one of these rules, and every one was caught. The Admin render test (`airail_render_admin_d781`) draws the three routes with their rail. `licence_account_d798` pins `drafted_at`.
