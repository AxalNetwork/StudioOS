## D559 — Learning: exercises and exams, graded on the server with no answer leaked (migration 390)

**Exercises a reviewer marks, and exams the server grades, with the correct
answers kept off every member payload until the exam allows them.** Slot S08,
issue #1160, 2026-10-06. It builds on D557 (#1158). The canvas is
`design/incoming/Learning.dc.html` (D566).

### Migration 390

| Table | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `learning_exercises` | `title` and `instructions`. `submission_kind` is `text` or `checklist`; a checklist has its items in `checklist_json`. Also `visibility`, `status`, `created_by` and timestamps. |
| `learning_exercise_submissions` | One row per submission. `body` is the text, or for a checklist the indexes checked. `status` is submitted, accepted or needs_work. Also `reviewer_id`, `feedback`, `submitted_at` and `reviewed_at`. Resubmitting adds a row, so earlier attempts stay (the canvas). |
| `learning_exams` | `title` and `intro`. `pass_mark_percent` defaults to 70. `time_limit_minutes` is null when untimed and `max_attempts` is null when unlimited. Also `shuffle`, `reveal` (`score` or `score_and_answers`), `visibility`, `status` and `created_by`. |
| `learning_exam_questions` | `prompt`. `kind` is `single` or `multiple`. `options_json` holds 2 to 10 options and `correct_json` the correct indexes. |
| `learning_exam_attempts` | `started_at` and `question_order_json`, the questions in the order this attempt was shown them. Then `submitted_at`, `answers_json`, `score_percent`, `passed` and `timed_out`. |

**Exercises and exams carry `visibility`.** It is not in #1160's column list.
#1159's programs hold exercises and exams as contents, and `canSeeLearning`
decides their visibility the same way as everything else.

### No answer leaks

- **`memberQuestion`** builds a member's copy of a question from an allow-list:
  `uid`, `prompt`, `kind` and `options`. A column added later is not served by
  accident.
- **The correct answers** leave the server only through `revealed`, on a
  submitted attempt of an exam whose `reveal` is `score_and_answers`.
- **The intro** carries no question at all, only the count. Questions arrive
  when an attempt starts, so the timer starts before anyone reads them.
- **The Super Admin's authoring view** (`GET /api/admin/learning/exams/:uid`)
  is the one place `correct` appears before reveal.

### Grading, attempts and time

- **Grading is exact.**
  - A single-choice question takes one option.
  - A multiple-choice question is right only when the chosen set equals the
    right set, with no partial credit.
  - An unanswered question is wrong.
  - A malformed submission (an out-of-range option, two options on a single
    question) is refused, not graded as blanks.
  - The score is the share right, rounded. `passed` means the score is at or
    above the pass mark.
- **Every attempt counts.** Every attempt started counts against
  `max_attempts`, checked inside the insert so two starts at once cannot both
  pass it. An open attempt is resumed, not doubled. With `shuffle`, the
  question order is drawn from the CSPRNG and stored with the attempt.
- **The clock is the server's.** The deadline is `started_at` plus the limit.
  A submission more than 30 seconds (`SUBMIT_GRACE_SECONDS`) after the deadline
  is not graded: the attempt closes as `timed_out`, scored 0 and not passed. An
  abandoned attempt past its deadline closes the same way the next time the
  member starts or lists.
- **Questions freeze at the first attempt.** Adding, editing or deleting a
  question is refused once anyone has started the exam. The check is in the
  statement itself, so a stored score always means what it meant when it was
  given. Make a new exam to change it.

### Routes

**Members, at `/api/learning` (`routes/learning_assessment.ts`):**
- `GET /exercises/:uid` returns the exercise and the caller's own
  submissions.
- `POST /exercises/:uid/submissions` submits or resubmits. An accepted
  exercise takes no more.
- `GET /exams/:uid` returns the intro.
- `POST /exams/:uid/attempts` starts or resumes an attempt and returns the
  deadline and the questions.
- `POST /exams/:uid/attempts/:attempt/submit` grades the attempt.
- `GET /exams/:uid/attempts` returns the caller's attempts and the attempts
  left.

Another member's attempt answers 404, and their attempts and submissions are
never listed.

**The Super Admin, at `/api/admin/learning`
(`routes/admin_learning_assessment.ts`):**
- exercises and exams can be created, edited, published, unpublished and
  archived. An exam needs a question, and a checklist needs its items;
- questions can be added, edited and deleted until the first attempt;
- `POST /submissions/:uid/review` marks a submission accepted or needs_work.
  Needs work requires feedback.

Every write is recorded with `logAdminAction`. A review names the learner as
`target_user_id`.

**Lessons.** D557's lesson check now accepts an exercise or exam lesson that
names one of its own kind.

### The read-only review: item 4

- **The routes.**
  - `GET /api/learning/review/exercises/:uid/submissions`
  - `GET /api/learning/review/exams/:uid/attempts`
- **Two gates in `services/learningAccess.ts`.**
  - `canReviewLearning` decides whether the viewer may open the review.
  - `canReadLearnerWork` filters the rows, per learner.
- **Today both admit the Super Admin only.** The epic (call 1A) gives this
  read to the admins of an assigned program, for that program's members. That
  assignment is #1159's (S07), not built yet. #1159 adds its condition to these
  two functions, and the routes and tests already ask them.
- **Learners are named and nothing more.** The review returns a learner's name
  and uid, never an email or an account id. Exam reviews return the score, not
  the answers.

### Clients

`learningApi` gains `exercise`, `submitExercise`, `exam`, `startExam`,
`submitExam`, `examAttempts`, `reviewSubmissions` and `reviewAttempts`.
`adminLearningApi` gains the exercise, exam, question and review methods.

### Follow-up (#1249): an exercise or exam lesson completes itself

**Before this, a course with a required exercise or exam lesson never
reached 100%.** A video or reading lesson is completed by the member's own
"Mark complete". An exercise is complete when a reviewer accepts it, and an
exam when an attempt passes, and neither route wrote a progress row.

- **`services/learningCompletion.ts` `completeAssessmentLessons`.** It
  writes a `completed` lesson row on every lesson that names the exercise or
  exam (`kind` and `ref_uid`), in whichever courses hold it. Then it brings
  each of those courses' own row up to date, with the measure
  `GET /courses/:slug` serves:
  - completed required lessons over required lessons, rounded;
  - `completed` at 100, and `started` below it;
  - no percent when the course has no required lesson.

  That is what the page's own "Mark complete" writes, so the course page and
  My learning agree.
- **It only ever adds.** Both writes use `PROGRESS_UPSERT`, which follows
  the progress route's own upsert rules: a completed row stays completed and
  keeps its first completion time, and a percent never goes backwards.
- **Where it is called.**
  - `POST /api/admin/learning/submissions/:uid/review`, on `accepted`, for
    the submission's member.
  - `POST /api/learning/exams/:uid/attempts/:attempt/submit`, on a passing
    attempt, for the caller.
- **What records nothing.** "Needs work", a failed attempt and a timed-out
  attempt record nothing. A later acceptance or pass records as above, and a
  second pass changes nothing.
- **A failure to record is said.** The review or the graded attempt stands,
  and the answer's `lesson_progress` reads `{ unrecorded: true }` instead of
  what was written. Every value is bound.
- **The course builder's warning** ("a required exercise or exam keeps a
  course below 100%") is part of Learning stage 3 (D562, #1163), which is not
  on `main` yet. It is to be removed when stage 3 lands. This change makes
  the warning untrue.

**Verified.** `cloudflare-worker/test/learning_assessment_completion_d559.test.ts`
has 5 tests. It covers:
- acceptance completing the exercise in two courses, for that member only,
  with the course page agreeing;
- needs work, a fail and a timeout writing nothing;
- a second pass changing nothing;
- a completed course not being downgraded;
- the measure, with every value bound.

13 of 13 mutations were caught.
