## D7826 — Research organizations with roles and sourced relationships, linking company and fund records (#7826)

**Context.** The company directory and the fund catalogue were unrelated stores, and a vehicle's manager was only a
text fact. Nothing could say that a record is an adviser, that a manager runs a vehicle, or that a company record and
a fund record are the same organization. Built as option A: adviser records stay in the company directory and carry
the role (Q1); the role set below (Q3).

**Decision.**

- **Tables** (migration `202611241148_7826_research_organizations.sql`, all empty at first; the first records are
  #7827):
  - `research_organizations`: a stable uid and a name.
  - `research_organization_roles`: one role per organization from a closed set (`operating_company`,
    `registered_investment_adviser`, `fund_manager`, `fund_vehicle`, `accelerator`, `venture_capital`,
    `corporate_venture`, `growth_private_equity`, `family_office`, `lender`, `corporate_group`, `other`), each with
    its source and the date the source states it.
  - `research_organization_record_links`: an organization's company or fund record, with an origin (`source_record`
    or `reviewed`) and a state (`proposed`, `confirmed`, `withdrawn`). A partial unique index allows one link that is
    not withdrawn per record, so a record belongs to at most one organization. Only confirmed links are read. A fund
    record moved by D7166 is stored under its current uid.
  - `research_organization_relationships`: `manages`, `advises`, `vehicle_of`, `member_of_group` or `invested_in`,
    each with its source link, where in the source it is stated, and the date. The table and the write both refuse a
    relationship with no source. Both ends are organizations, so no person is ever at either end. `invested_in` alone
    may carry the investor's role (`lead`, `co_lead`, `participant`), its own amount with its currency, and a
    `company_funding_rounds` row for the round's facts; it never copies a round total.
- **Kind and role.** A fund row's kind is #7744's `record_kind` and is not restated. Role `fund_manager` corresponds to
  kind `manager`, `fund_vehicle` to `vehicle`, `accelerator` to `accelerator`. A link to a fund record whose kind is
  `resource` is refused (`record_kind_not_organization`); a link whose kind is none of the kinds the organization's
  roles name is refused (`role_contradicts_record_kind`), and so is a new role that would contradict a fund record
  the organization already owns. An organization with no such role, or a row with no kind recorded, is allowed.
  **#7744's column is not on main** when this lands, so every row reads as not recorded: the service reads the
  served row's own columns (`f.*`) and names no kind column in SQL, and the refusal is tested on a fixture that adds
  the column.
- **Clues never make a link.** No code here reads `research_fund_vehicle_facts`, #7743's statement store or a fund
  row's name as a clue. A manager name equal to an organization's name gains no link. `invested_in` from staged round
  records waits on #7740 Q5; nothing from the private store is read. Network's previous-investments text never becomes
  a relationship except through the admin write with its source.
- **Admin writes** (`routes/research_organizations.ts`, `requireAdmin`, each through `logAdminAction`): create an
  organization, add a role, confirm a link, add a relationship. None inserts into `research_fund_directory`. A firm
  row #7751 promotes gets its organization through the confirm write here. Deciding what a staged source record is
  stays in #7746's table.
- **Reads** at `/api/research/organizations`: `GET /:uid` (an organization with its roles, relationships and linked
  records) and `GET /record/:type/:uid` (the organization of a company or fund record, by the record's uid; the reader
  #7674 and #7680 use for View in Research).
- **One access decision.**
  - A company link, and anything derived from a company record, is shown only to an account the Companies rule admits
    (D7178, `admitsCompanies`) and only while D7819's served rows serve that record. An organization with a company
    link the caller may not see is not shown at all, because its name and roles are derived from that record.
  - A fund link is shown only while `SERVED_FUNDS` serves its row, following a moved uid one hop. A linked fund
    answers as its uid, name and, where recorded, kind. No read returns a fund statement or a vehicle fact.
  - An organization with no link the caller can see is not shown. A hidden organization answers `404 Not found`
    exactly as a missing one, and `{ organization: null }` from the record read; a relationship whose other end is
    hidden is left out. A company record asked of an account the Companies rule does not admit gets that rule's
    refusal, decided before anything is read.
- **Registration with #7824.** #7824 has not merged, so these two reads are not yet in its enumeration test or access
  matrix; that issue registers them when it lands.
- **Network boundary.** Nothing here reads or writes a `network_directory_*` table, and neither store holds the other's
  id. A Research organization and a Network directory organisation meet only through a Research record both name.
  Mapping of the #7672 bridge (`network_directory_research_links`, merged with PR #7687):
  - `target_kind = 'company'` is a record link here to that company record;
  - `fund_manager` is a record link to that fund row, on an organization with the role `fund_manager`;
  - `managed_fund_vehicle` is a `manages` or `vehicle_of` relationship here.
  Network's entity type and a source's type label set no Research role, and a bridge row never creates a Research
  organization. The test over the real bridge table shows two Research rows of one organization, bridged from one
  Network organisation, resolving to that organization, and a bridge insert or withdrawal changing nothing in
  `research_*`.

**Not here.** The manager import and the adviser role action (#7827), any fund statement or vehicle fact in a read
(#7748), any screen.
