## D5030 — HQ Support: queue counts from the whole board, and a ticket keeps the queue it was filed in (#5030)

- **Status:** Built, 2026-11-08 (Worker, migration and frontend). HQ Support audit 1 of 6.
- **Migration:** `202611082048_ticket_filing_snapshot.sql` adds `tickets.filed_persona`, `filed_role`, `filed_licence_uid` and `filed_branch_code`. Nothing is backfilled.
- **Builds on:** D204 (the three queues and `personaOf`), D485 (branch-host tickets), D801 (the kind filter), D131 (a cut count is not a total).

### Per-kind counts

`GET /api/admin/hq-support` listed the oldest 25 escalations, and the page counted a kind filter from those rows (`filterByKind(items, kind).length`). Past 25 open escalations, a kind whose items were all newer read 0.

- The route now sends `escalations.kinds`: every kind on the board, plus D799's four request kinds, each with its count from the whole read and its own oldest 25 rows.
- Past the read ceiling (2,000) a kind's count is `null` and the pill says "not counted". The rows stay exact, because the read is ordered oldest first.
- The pills and a filtered card read the same measurement. A filtered card no longer shows the whole board's SLA bands as its own.
- Against a Worker that sends no `kinds`, the page counts from the listed rows only when they are the whole board. Otherwise every per-kind count is null.

### Filing-time classification

A ticket's queue was derived from its requester's standing now. A licence Administrator who was demoted, closed or detached after filing moved their open tickets to another queue.

- `filingSnapshot` runs `personaOf`'s rules at filing time. `POST /api/tickets` writes the result once, onto the new row, while that row has no record. It is never updated.
- On a branch host, an active admin files as `admin_product` and anyone else as `branch_member`, with the branch code.
- `queuePersonaOf` decides the queue: the filing record wins. A ticket with no record (filed before this) is queued from current standing and marked `basis: 'current_standing'`. The record is never filled in from today's standing.
- Each ticket item carries `basis`, `filed`, `current` and `standing_changed`. The row says "Filed as licence administrator (AXL-002) · now HQ-held user" where the two differ. Each card counts the tickets it queued from current standing.
- The tenant matrix attributes About-Admin tickets to the licence they were filed under.
- A branch's `readBranchAdminTickets` sends tickets filed as `admin_product`, plus legacy tickets by a current active admin.
- A database without the columns is read the old way, and every row is flagged as current standing. The snapshot write is best-effort, so a missing column never stops a ticket being filed.
