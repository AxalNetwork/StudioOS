### 1. The Worker first: the roster answers what FND5 and FND6 draw

`GET /api/fund-admin/lps` (D1158) keeps its gate: `requireFundAdminReader`, HQ only, no support session. It also keeps its fail-closed `fund_lps_read` audit row, written before any row is read. This decision adds three things:

- **`?lp=<id>`:** one LP's row, for the drawer.
  - It is a positive integer, else 400 `invalid_lp_query`.
  - The audit row records the `lp_id`.
  - An LP on a fund the console does not administer is not in the roster, so it is not answered.
  - The fund filter still applies.
- **`booklet.uid`:** the live booklet's uid, chosen by the same rule as its state (not withdrawn first, then the newest). It is null before the applicant opens one. With it, the drawer's Booklet tab opens that booklet.
- **`tally`:** what the tiles and the chart count, over every row the filters keep across all pages, as `totals` already does:
  - `lpa_pending`;
  - `identity_pending`, a recorded check that is neither approved nor rejected;
  - `identity_not_recorded`;
  - `overdue_lines`, null when no row holds a ledger figure;
  - `by_type`, `[{ lp_type, lps, commitment_cents }]`:
    - a type no row records is `lp_type: null`, drawn as "Not recorded";
    - a type whose rows hold no commitment has `commitment_cents: null`, never 0;
    - it is ordered by commitment, with the unrecorded type last.

  Counting this on one page of rows would have been wrong on page two. That is why it is on the Worker.

The Worker tests seed a store and check:

- the tally across pages;
- that `lpa=signed` reads no LPA pending;
- that a recorded `pending` check is pending;
- `?lp=`, including an outside fund's LP, the fund filter, and three bad ids;
- the audited `lp_id`;
- the booklet's uid, and a null uid before one is opened.

Mutation checks:

- Breaking any of the filter, the uid, the audit field or the id check fails a test.
- Breaking any of the five tally rules fails a test. The LPA rule needed the `?fund=3` case: the fixture is split two signed, two unsigned, so the first assertion alone could not tell the rule from its inverse.

### 2. FND5: `/admin/fund-admin/lps`

It replaces D1157's `fund-admin-wait-lps` card. It keeps `fund-admin-panel-lps` and the tab's address.

- **The tiles:**
  - LPs, "in N funds";
  - Total committed;
  - Called;
  - Outstanding, amber with "N lines overdue";
  - LPAs pending;
  - Identity checks pending, with how many have no check recorded.

  A sum no row holds reads "Not recorded", never $0. A sum some rows lack says how many.
- **Commitment by LP type:** one bar per type, its width against the largest.
  - "Not recorded" is a dashed outline, and its legend says so.
  - A one-line reading names the type that holds the most.
  - The chart's `aria-label` is the full summary.
  - Chart/Table switches to the same figures as a table.
- **LP applications:** "LP applications are decided in Approvals.", with a link to `/admin/lp-applications`.
- **The table:** LP (name and email), Fund, Type, Commitment, Called, Received, Outstanding, Distributed, LPA, Identity, Booklet, Last notice, and the menu.
  - A figure the Worker returns null is "Not recorded", with the Worker's reason as its title, or "Unreadable" when its store failed. **An LP with no ledger line reads Not recorded under Called, Received and Outstanding** (tested).
  - It scrolls in its card on a desktop and stacks on a phone (`parts.jsx`'s `Table wide`).
- **Search, the filters, the toggles, the sort, the direction and the page** live in the address, and the Worker applies them:
  - the filters are LPA, Identity and Booklet;
  - the toggles are Has outstanding and Overdue.

  The header's `?fund=` is the Fund filter. A filter change goes back to page 1. Anything malformed in the address is left out, so the Worker never refuses an address the page would not write.
- **The row menu:** Open (the drawer), Set LP type (`PUT /api/funds/:id/lps/:lpId/type`) and Log a contact (`POST /api/funds/:id/comms`). Both writes are `fund.write`, an Admin's act. Each opens an in-page dialog that says what it changes.
- **Add LP:**
  - It is the tab's button, and also the header's primary, which now has an act page. It links to `?add=1`, keeping the roster's filters.
  - It writes through `POST /api/funds/:id/lps` (`fund.write`), then sets the type through the type route. If the type write fails, the dialog says the LP was added and the type was not.
  - A first call issues a call (`capital_call.issue`, a Super Admin's act). So the first-call field and its due date are drawn through `FundActControl`. **An Admin sees "A Super Admin issues capital calls." and "Add the LP without a first call; the Super Admin calls it later.", never a disabled field, and the body sent carries no first call even if one was typed** (tested).
  - A Super Admin's first call needs its due date, never filled in for you, and cannot exceed the commitment.

### 3. FND6: the drawer at `/admin/fund-admin/lps/:id`

It is the shared `Lightbox` over the roster: Esc and Close go back to `/lps` with the roster's filters. Its row is the roster's own `?lp=` read, so it is audited as the roster is. Each tab reads its own store only when it is opened; the Summary reads nothing more. A tab whose read fails says Unreadable with Try again, and the drawer stays.

- **Summary:** commitment, called, received, outstanding, distributed, LPA signed, identity and booklet. A missing figure says "Not recorded: <the Worker's reason>" (tested).
- **Calls & receipts:**
  - the LP's lines on the fund's call ledger (`GET /api/funds/:id/capital-calls`), newest call first, each with owed, received, outstanding, its state and the days overdue;
  - Record receipt (`capital_call.receipt`) on a line with something outstanding, in a dialog: the full outstanding or another amount, the date the wire landed, and a reference;
  - "This records money already received. It moves no money." More than is outstanding is refused in the dialog, and by the Worker.
- **Distributions:** the fund's distributions to this LP (`GET /api/funds/distributions?fund_id=`).
- **Notices:** D716's notices to this LP (`GET /api/fund-notices?fund_id=&lp_id=`), with Superseded marked. PDF reads the kept file (`GET /api/fund-notices/:uid/pdf`) and saves it.
- **Booklet:** the row names the booklet, and the tab reads nothing until Open.
  - **Open** reads `GET /api/fund-booklets/:uid`. The Worker writes the `fund_booklet.read` audit row before it answers (`fund_booklet_d716`).
  - The tab says "Opening a booklet's answers is recorded in the audit log.", and after Open "This view is recorded in the audit log.", with the ticked count per part and the signing state. **Opening a booklet records a view** (tested in the drawer, and audited on the Worker).
  - **Withdraw** (`fund_booklet.withdraw`) opens "Withdraw this booklet?" with a reason of 10 to 200 characters; Withdraw stays disabled until it has one.
  - **Send to the General Partner** (`fund_booklet.accept`) is a Super Admin's act, behind the write bar; the global step-up prompt asks for the code. **An Admin sees "A Super Admin sends a booklet to the General Partner for acceptance."** (tested).
  - With no booklet, the tab says why, in the Worker's words.
- **Contact log:** what was sent to this LP and when (`GET /api/funds/:id/comms?lp=`). Log a contact (`fund.write`) is the same dialog as the row menu's.

### 4. The clients (D527)

- **`fundNoticesApi`** gains an Admin's `list({ fundId, lpId })`, `one(uid)` and `pdfBlob(uid)`. The LP's `minePdfBlob` and the Admin's `pdfBlob` share one binary helper, which is not typed drift, as before.
- **`fundBookletsApi`** gains `list()`, `one(uid)`, `withdraw(uid, reason)` and `sendToGeneralPartner(uid)`.
- **Every path is a Worker route** in `routes/fund_notices.ts` or `routes/fund_booklets.ts`. A mounted test checks each method's path, method and body.

### 5. Not built, or built differently from the canvas

- **"Open statement"** in the canvas's row menu is not drawn. An LP's statement is produced by the GP report desk in the LP workspace, and the console has no page for one LP's statement yet. A link that went somewhere else would be a claim.
- **The canvas's commitment date field** is not drawn. `LPs.create` records `datetime('now')` and ignores a date sent, so the dialog says "The commitment date is recorded as the day the LP is added."
- **Six tiles draw in the console's four-up grid** (two rows on a desktop, two up on a phone), not the canvas's three-up. `Tiles` is shared by every tab.
