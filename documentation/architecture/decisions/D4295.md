## D4295 — Influencer analytics: totals only, "Fewer than 5", and every figure from its store or said not to be recorded (#4295)

- **Status:** D4289 P6, 2026-11-03. Part of #4289. S27 built the read before P1 to P3 merged (#4311); S24 wired the figures those parts' stores hold (§5), which closes #4295.
- **Migration:** none.

### 1. The reads

- **`GET /api/influencer/me/analytics?from&to&channel&grain=week`** is the influencer's own read. It returns every figure IN1, IN2, IN4 and IN5 draw, and `line`, IN1's Eadwyn line.
- **`GET /api/admin/influencers/:uid/analytics`** returns the same figures to HQ.
  - Money (`commission`, `revenue` and `bonuses`) is the Super Admin's.
  - Any other admin reads `{ available: false, reason: 'hq_reads' }` (O-MONEY A).
  - The counts are any admin's.
- **The range** defaults to the 12 weeks to today. Weeks start on Monday, UTC. A range over a year, or one with `from` after `to`, is refused.

### 2. Where each figure comes from

| Figure | Source |
|---|---|
| Sign-ups, total and by week | `referrals` the influencer referred, in the range |
| Visits, total and by week | `handle_visits` (P2) for the influencer's `influencers.id`, by `day` |
| Channels, ranked by visits | visits by `handle_visits.channel`; sign-ups by the member's handle attribution (`referral_attributions`, source `handle`); paid members by the channel on their first paid invoice |
| The `channel` filter | the same three columns. It narrows visits, sign-ups, paid members, revenue, commission, the audience, retention and visit → paid |
| Paid members, total and by week | the member's first paid invoice (`influencer_commission_invoices`, `invoice_number` 1, not cancelled), paid in the range |
| Revenue influenced, per currency | those invoices' `net_cents`, paid in the range, never summed across currencies |
| Commission: pending, payable, in a payout, paid, reversed, per currency | `commissions` by status. **Paid** means claimed by a payout that completed. A commission a failed payout gave back and a later payout claimed counts once, by its latest payout. |
| Commission earned by week, per currency | `commissions` not reversed |
| Bonuses | `influencer_milestones` (kind `bonus`) with their commission, not reversed; the next milestone from D4292's count of paid customers |
| Audience by plan family | referred members' current subscriptions (`member_subscription_amounts`: active, trialing or past due) |
| Audience by region | referred members' `users.country`; a member with none is its own group |
| Audience by month joined | `referrals.created_at` |
| Retention | a cohort is the members whose first paid invoice fell in a month of the range; in each later month that has ended, a member counts while their subscription had not ended by that month's end |
| Visit → paid | median days from the handle's first touch (`referral_attributions.first_touch_at`, source `handle`) to the first paid invoice |

- **Sign-ups** count every referral attributed to the influencer, by code or by handle. Only a handle attribution has a channel.
- **The handle itself** is a channel of its own: `channel: null` in `channels`. A sign-up or invoice by code is on no channel and is left out of `channels`.
- **Bonuses** are paid on the influencer's whole count (D4292), so a range or a channel leaves them as they are.
- **Money** is per currency and is never summed across currencies.

### 3. Privacy and honest values

- **Totals only.** Every statement aggregates, and no reply carries a referred member's name, email or id. The test holds both the influencer's reply and HQ's to that.
- **Small groups.** A group of 1 to 4 reads `{ fewer_than: 5 }`: a week's visits, sign-ups or paid members, a channel's counts, a region, a plan family, a retention cell or the total.
  - The median from visit to paid reads only over five members or more; under that its `days` is null.
  - The next milestone's `needs` would give away a count of 1 to 4, so it then reads `{ more_than: milestone − 5 }`.
- **Zero.** A counted 0 is 0, because each store is written on every visit, sign-up, invoice and commission. An influencer with no handle has no visits, a counted 0.
- **Missing data.** Every figure's store is on `main`, so none reads Not recorded. A read that fails reads `{ available: false, reason: 'unreadable' }`, and the other figures still return. It is never 0.

### 4. The Eadwyn line

`summaryLine` restates the figures with their date ("From your link records, as of …"). It names only figures the read returned, and a small group reads "fewer than 5". It is built here, never by a model, so it cannot promise anything (D4289 §1.5).
