## D1527 — Deck v2 B19: editing by instruction, version 2 — the box anchored to the selection, on several areas (#3795)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-03 by S09 for #3795, part of #3716 (deck builder v2, Phase B). Spec: the issue's "Final canvas revision (2026-11-01)".
- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** none. It uses D1521's instruct routes (#3773) and D1512's area proposals (#3764).
- **Decision AB is settled: version 2.** This is the instruction box anchored to the selection (pd19, pd20). Versions 1 and 3 are not built. There is no chat, no docked panel and no Ask panel.

### 1. What changed

`InstructionBox.jsx` and `instructionModel.js` (D1604, S1) are extended. No new UI file was added. One area still runs through D899's card tools, as before. Several areas now run end to end:

1. **Send** quotes the whole selection once: `deckInstructApi.quote` with `{ card, selection: [{ area_id }], instruction, variants }`.
   - The box shows the hold, how many areas run and how many cannot.
   - Each area that cannot run shows its own sentence. For example, a figure tile "changes from its own tools".
   - A variants picker (1, 2 or 3) sits beside Send. Changing it drops the quote, so the price shown is always the one that runs.
2. **Run** admits the quoted run: `deckInstructApi.run` with the `quote_uid`, keyed once per press.
   - If an area is still generating, the box reads the batch again (`deckAreasApi.proposals`, every 1.5 s for up to 3 minutes) until it settles.
   - An area still running after that says so. It stays in the deck's proposals.
3. **The proposals** are D1512's `areaRunResults`, drawn by `AreaResults`. That gives one card per area and variant, named by its area chip ("c1–7 × r2–6 · Body"), with:
   - before and after;
   - the model label, read-only, from the AIRail's pick;
   - the cost;
   - Accept and Discard.
4. **Accept all** places one variant per area as **one undo step**. It is `AreaResults`' Accept all, whose placements share one D1515 `group`. One Undo takes every area back. Accept is by the area (`/areas/:card_uid/:area_id/accept`), so the area's other variants are superseded.
5. **Retry** sends the same words again for a new quote.

The figure refusal stays before any charge, on one area or several: "Figures come from your records. Pick a recorded figure instead. Nothing was charged." The Worker refuses it too (422 `names_figure`, D1521 §3).

`deckV2Adapter.instruct` stays for any other caller. The box calls `deckInstructApi` directly, as #3795 step 4 asks.

### 2. Not here

- **Timing.** Editing task 13 (§7.4) is to be timed against Genspark and Dokie. That is a timed session against two outside products. It is reported on the issue, not tested in CI.
- **The variant pager.** pd19's "variant 1 of 2 ‹ ›" is drawn as one card per variant, as D1512 draws Generate into areas. A pager on one card is a visual change to `AreaResults`, shared by both surfaces.
