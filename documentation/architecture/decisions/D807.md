## D807 — The Limits page, `/account/usage/limits`: the spend limit and its notices, with the plan allowance, auto-reload and today's pack orders beside it

- **Status:** filed by S1 for the owner (2026-10-20) as #2246, part of D785 (#2210). Built by S26.
- **Canvas:** board B4 of the "Usage & Billing console" (`design/incoming/Usage Page.dc.html`, `screen: 'limits'`, desktop and phone). The canvas is in PR #2247's intake, not yet on `main`.
- **Stacks on:**
  - D788 (the monthly spend limit, on `main`);
  - D794 (auto-reload, PR #2236), whose branch this one starts from;
  - D805 (#2243, today's pack orders), which has no PR yet.
- **Split from** D792 (#2217), which keeps the Billing page.

### 1. The decision

Usage gets a Limits page that says what stops spend, and when it resets. It has one editable card, the monthly spend limit. The member has one limit: runs and auto-reloads both stop at it. Beside it are three read-only cards:
- the plan allowance;
- auto-reload, which is changed on Billing;
- today's token-pack orders against the five a day.

No `/api/*` method is added.

### 2. As built

1. **The address.**
   - `/account/usage/limits` renders inside `SettingsPage.jsx`, as `/account/connectors/:key` does.
   - `usagePageFor` (`pages/account/limitsModel.js`) reads which Usage page the path names: `limits`, or `overview` for anything else.
   - `accountPathFor({ section: 'usage', usagePage })` writes it back.
   - The canonicalising effect passes the page, so `/limits` is kept. An unknown `/account/usage/<x>` lands on the overview.
2. **The account navigation.**
   - `AccountSectionNav` draws a section's pages (`subpages`) indented under it, always, as the canvas's `acNav` does.
   - On Limits, Usage is drawn in bold and marked `data-active`. Limits is drawn with the background and `aria-current="page"`.
   - **Usage's pages are Overview and Limits.** The canvas also draws Cost, which is D789's (#2214) and has no page yet. It joins `USAGE_PAGES` when that page lands, rather than leading to a page that is not there.
3. **"Monthly spend limit"** (D788's `GET` and `PUT /api/ai-budget/limit`):
   - **The field and its buttons.**
     - A dollar field, with Save and, when a limit is set, Remove.
     - Save checks the amount the way D788 does: $0 to $100,000, two decimals at most. An amount it refuses is never sent.
     - Remove sends `null`.
     - A refusal that names `monthly_limit_usd` is shown under the field, and any other refusal under the card.
     - Save asks for a recent sign-in: `lib/api.js` prompts on `step_up_required` and retries once, as for every money setting.
   - **With a limit set,** the card draws:
     - the bar, "[$135.22] spent this month · [27]%";
     - the three switches "Notify me at 50%", "80%" and "100%";
     - "Notices arrive in your bell and by email.";
     - "Runs, and auto-reloads, that would pass it are stopped until [1 Nov]."

     A switch saves the limit with that one notice changed, and is drawn from what the Worker answers.
   - **With no limit,** the canvas's empty state: "No limit set. Runs continue while your plan allowance and token balance last.", with "Set a limit", which focuses the field.
4. **"Plan allowance · read-only"** (D640's `GET /api/ai-budget/me`):
   - "[$200.00] a month", "[$135.61] used · resets [1 Nov]", with its bar.
   - With no plan it is Not recorded: "Plan allowance: there is no plan, so there is no allowance."
5. **"Auto-reload · read-only"** (D794's `GET /api/ai-budget/auto-reload`, with the pack's label from D755's `GET /api/ai-budget/top-up`):
   - On or Off, and "When on, buys the [$25] pack below [$5]." With no pack chosen: "No pack chosen yet. Choose one on Billing."
   - "The spend limit above is also auto-reload's limit."
   - "Open billing →", which goes to `/account/billing#auto-reload`.
6. **"Token pack orders today":**
   - "[1] of [5]", with its bar, and "Auto-reloads count toward it." This reads D805's `pack_orders` from the limit read.
   - Until D805 adds it, the card is Not recorded: "Today's count is not read yet." It never shows 0.
7. **States.** Each card loads on its own, and one failed read costs only that card. A card is loading, Not recorded with its reason, or Unreadable with Retry (the shared `Unreadable`).
8. **Phone.** The page draws its own bar, "‹ Usage", titled "Limits" with the page's line, as Usage's A2m does. The cards stack in one column.

### 3. Departures, for review

- **Cost is not in the navigation yet.** Its page is D789's (#2214), and it joins `USAGE_PAGES` with that page.
- **Today's pack orders are Not recorded** until D805 (#2243) adds `pack_orders` to `GET /api/ai-budget/limit`. Once it lands, this card draws the count with no change here, and this PR stacks on D805's PR.
- **Reading auto-reload asks for a recent sign-in.** D794 made all three of its routes need a recent TOTP sign-in, the read included. Opening this page outside the step-up window prompts for it, as Billing's Auto-reload card already does. Cancelling leaves that one card Unreadable, and the rest of the page draws. **For S1's review:** whether D794's read should need only a signed-in session. The read shows the pack, the threshold and the card's brand and last four, and changes nothing.
- **Five source pins widened.** Five existing tests pinned lines of `SettingsPage.jsx` that this page extends:
  - `account_canvas_coverage`, `account_phone_d700`, `connector_page_d753` and `telegram_page_d756` pin the canonicalising call and the section click, which now carry the Usage page;
  - `usage_page_d642` pins the Usage pane, which now chooses between Overview and Limits.

  Each pin still holds what it guarded.
- **`ROUTE_MAP.md`.** The Account row names the address. The canvas's own row is #2214's, as PR #2247 records.
