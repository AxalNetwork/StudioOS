## D4672 — Offers › Proof: an approval belongs to one revision, only the client gives it, and a withdrawal stays (#4672)

- **Status:** Built for #4672 (Offers audit 1 of 6), 2026-11-09. It covers the Worker, the Proof zone's consent labels, the client's page, and request telemetry.
- **Builds on:**
  - migration 209 (proof items and consents; published is derived, never stored);
  - D4670, the same model for advisors. Its code, hash and mask helpers are reused, not copied.
- **Migration:** `202611092206_partner_proof_consent_integrity.sql`. It is additive:
  - columns on `partner_proof_items` (`revision`, `claim_sha256`);
  - columns on `partner_proof_consents` (`claim_sha256`, `claim_json`, `revision`, `expires_at`, `closed_at`);
  - two new tables, the one-time codes and an append-only answer history.
- **Public record only.** The repository is public. Specifics of the issues this closes are not recorded here (SECURITY.md).

### What was decided

1. **An approval is bound to one revision of the claim.**
   - A proof item's claim is its kind, title, detail, outcome and the engagement it is about. The item carries a revision number and the SHA-256 of that claim.
   - A material edit moves the item to the next revision. Saving the same words does not.
   - Each request pins the claim exactly as it read, with its revision and hash, and the client is shown that text.
   - A confirmation counts only while the item still reads as pinned. After an edit it stays attached to the revision it was given for and is shown as **superseded**. The new revision is self-stated until the client confirms it.
   - Each consent that was live or open when the claim moved gets a `superseded` event.
2. **Only the client named can answer.**
   - The firm still hands over a link (`/attest/partner/:token`), and may read it back while the request is open. The link shows the claim; it cannot answer it.
   - Every answer (confirm, decline, withdraw) needs a six-digit code. Axal emails it, on the client's own request, to the address the firm named.
   - The address is required. It cannot be the firm's profile address or any of its members' (422 `consenter_is_requester`).
   - A signed-in member of the firm is refused even with a code (403 `requester_cannot_confirm`).
3. **Credentials are short-lived and single-use.**
   - A code lasts 15 minutes, allows five wrong tries, is spent by the answer it makes, and is stored only as a hash bound to its request.
   - A request sends at most five codes an hour.
   - A link stops answering after 30 days (**expired**).
4. **A confirmation names the claim it confirms.** The client's answer carries the hash of the claim they were shown. It is recorded only when that is the claim this request pinned and the item still reads that way; otherwise 409 `claim_changed`, and nothing is written.
5. **Closed stays closed.**
   - Declining, or withdrawing (by the client with a code, or recorded by the firm), closes the request.
   - No code, old or new, reopens it, and the page offers nothing that could.
   - Asking again is a new request, on the record.
   - The firm can record a withdrawal only of an agreement; an unanswered request has nothing to take back.
   - Every ask and answer is appended to `partner_proof_consent_events`, which two triggers make append-only. The history survives the item's deletion and holds no name or address.
6. **States, worded on both sides:** requested, confirmed, declined, withdrawn, superseded, expired.
   - On the firm's side, each consent row has its own pill and says which version it was about.
   - An item whose asks are all superseded or expired reads **Ask again**, never "Nobody asked". It has no chip of its own: the chip row is the artboard's.
7. **Confirmations recorded before this decision** were never bound to a revision. They are kept and shown, and no longer publish (superseded), which is the call the owner made for advisors on #4670.
8. **No credential carried in a path reaches telemetry** (`util/pathRedaction.ts`).
   - Before writing, the observability middleware replaces every route parameter named as a credential (`token`, `…Token`, `secret`, `signature`, `sig`, `code`) with its name. This covers `system_metrics`, Analytics Engine, `activity_logs` and `error_logs`.
   - It reads the matched route pattern, so a new `/:token` route is covered when it is mounted, including the advisor twin and the share, signing and download links.
   - A path no route matched, and the rate limiter's log (written before routing), redact by shape: any opaque segment of 20 or more URL-safe characters.
9. **`research.ts`** counts a consent as live only when its pinned hash is the item's current one, and an ask as pending only while it is open and about that same revision.

### Not changed

- Ownership: every firm route is scoped to the firm's own items, and a non-owner gets 404.
- Recording an ask sends nothing on the firm's behalf. The only mail is the client's own code.
- The Offers layout: only the consent labels and the ask form changed, because the layout is being redesigned under #4872.
