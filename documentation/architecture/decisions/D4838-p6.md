### Built

- **One rule set for both pages.** `commandCentre.js` now exports `retainerActive`, `retainerUtilPct`, `isOverServiced`, `isRenewingSoon` and `isLowUse`, built on its `LOW_USE_PCT` (50) and `RENEWAL_WINDOW_DAYS` (30). Its own Needs attention and Risks call them.
  - `retainersModel.js` imports them, and the band, the Utilization risk tile and the ledger filter all read the same sets.
  - Before this part the zone's under-consuming filter used "below 60". An active retainer at 55% was therefore an exception on the zone and not on the root.
  - The reviewer's boundary fixture is now a test, rendered: active rows at 0, 49.99, 50, 55, 60 and null, plus an ended row at 49.99.
    - Only 0 and 49.99 are low use.
    - The band item, the filtered ledger rows, the phone cards and the root's attention keys name the same two ids.
- **The attention band** sits above the strip and the ledger. Pressing an item narrows the ledger to that item's own array, and pressing it again shows every retainer.
  - The toolbar's chips use the same keys (`over`, `renewing`, `under`), so the two controls are one view.
  - An item with no rows is not drawn. A band with nothing in it says so.
  - When active retainers have no utilization recorded, the band says how many, and that they are in neither utilization item.
- **The KPI strip:**
  - Current recurring revenue is the Worker's `mrr_cents`, with its `mrr_note`. `mrr_basis` is printed under the strip.
  - Active retainers is the Worker's `active_count`, broken down by shape (`shape_counts`), with ended retainers named as not counted.
  - Renewal value next 30d sums the monthly equivalents of the renewing rows. Unpriced rows are named, not added as $0.
  - Utilization risk counts over + low over the rows read. It is Not recorded when no active retainer has utilization recorded.
- **The ledger** has the board's six columns as a real `<table>`.
  - Monthly equivalent is the Worker's `monthly_amount_cents` (quarterly ÷ 3). A quarterly row says how it was reached ("$7,800 a quarter ÷ 3").
  - An ended retainer reads Not recorded with "ended". No column is headed "Monthly".
  - Utilization reads "▲ 118% · over scope", "▼ 35% · low use" or "● 76% · healthy". The bar is `aria-hidden`. Not recorded carries the Worker's own `utilisation_note`.
  - The total row is the response's `mrr_cents`. It is drawn only when no filter is narrowing the rows.
- **Phone (PP06m):** below `md` the ledger is a list of cards. Each leads with the client and its health, then utilization, then "$X / mo equivalent · renews <date>". The opener is at least 44px.
- **The sheet:** a row opens a dialog with the tabs Summary, Usage, Terms and History. A tab appears only when something is recorded behind it:
  - Summary is always shown.
  - Usage appears once a retainer is recorded, because hours are logged against a retainer.
  - Terms shows the recorded terms, or the form that records the first one.
  - History appears only when a period is logged or the retainer has ended. It lists exactly those, and says that changes to the terms are not kept with a date.
- **A refresh keeps the editor and the draft.** The sheet holds an engagement id and looks its row up on every render. Each form keeps a draft that only an accepted save (or Discard changes) clears, and a refresh with changed stored terms leaves the draft as typed. Every tab panel stays mounted and hidden, so switching tabs keeps a draft too.
- **Engagements with no retainer** are listed under the ledger, each with a "Record as a retainer" button that opens the sheet on Terms.

### Where part 6 departs from the board

- **The toolbar chip row stays** (All, Renewing 30d, Under-consuming, Over scope, from the integrated canvas). PP06 draws only the band.
  - The toolbar row is bound to the band's view, so it is not a second filter. It also keeps the partner filter census and the export row in place.
  - Its Under-consuming chip now narrows at the root's 50%, not 60%.
- **"No exception", not "Healthy", when utilization is not recorded.** The board's sample shows a quarterly retainer with no logged usage as Healthy. A period nobody has logged is not a healthy one.
- **The Worker's `under_consuming_count` and `at_risk_mrr_cents` are not drawn.** Both are counted below 60%, and the Worker is outside this issue. Aligning the Worker to `LOW_USE_PCT` is left for a Worker change.
- **Ended retainers stay in the unfiltered ledger** with Health "Ended". Bare engagements are listed below the ledger, so the first retainer can still be recorded from the row it belongs to.
- **The total row and the export stay.** Both are from the earlier ledger. The export gains a monthly-equivalent column.
- **The sheet is this zone's own.** The shared opportunity sheet (PP08) is not built, and no lifecycle feed exists.
- **The zone nav keeps the shell's `ZoneNav` without counts,** as in part 1.
- **The AI band (`ZoneDraft`, "Proposal · renewal risk") stays.** It sends nothing.
- **Spelling:** the page copy says "utilization", as the boards do. Field names keep `utilisation_pct`.
