## D8536 part 4 — Recovery notices mail once, and the claim email carries its link (#8536)

**Context.** D8536 part 3 left two items under **Not moved** for the owner. The owner answered on 2026-11-27:
**1B** and **2A**.

1. **1B: each recovery notice is mailed once.** `notifyAllChannels` called `notify()` with
   `channels: ['in_app', 'email', 'slack']`. Beside it, the referenced templated send also mailed the account
   holder. `notify()`'s email (`sendNotificationEmail`) has a free recipient and no reference, so every notice
   arrived twice.
2. **2A: the claim email carries the claim link.** At the end of a trusted-contact or admin-cosign recovery, the
   claim email (`auth_recovery_resolved`) said the request was resolved but carried no link. The link (`ctaUrl`)
   reached only the bell, which a locked-out person cannot open.

**Decision.**

1. **1B.** `notify()` keeps `in_app` and `slack` and drops `email`. The notice's one email is the referenced
   templated send (`recovery_notice`, part 3). The in-app row and the Slack DM are unchanged.
   `sendNotificationEmail` itself stays as it is: its other callers are outside the critical flows (D8536 inventory).
2. **2A.** A new registry template, `auth_recovery_claim` (category `security`, `alwaysSend`), is used by both
   claim sends.
   - It carries the claim link in its text and HTML parts, says it expires in 15 minutes and is single use, and
     tells anyone who did not ask for it to reply to security@axal.vc.
   - Both sends pass `skipMirror` and no `ctaUrl`. As with the recovery magic link (part 3), no bell row is
     written: a row there would hold a usable link that its owner cannot reach.
3. **Each claim issuance is its own attempt.** The attempts are `claim.admin.<n>` and `claim.trusted.<n>`, where
   `n` is the number of approvals that issued the link.
   - Before this change the attempt was fixed (`claim.admin`). A third co-signature issues a new token and
     replaces the stored hash. Its email was then answered from the ledger as already sent, so the person held only
     a link that no longer worked. With no link in the email this did not matter; with 2A it did.
   - Now the replacement is mailed and the earlier link is refused (`invalid_or_used`). A retry of the same
     issuance still reconciles.
   - The trusted-contact flow cannot reissue: after two attestations the ticket moves to `awaiting_admin`, and a
     third attestation is refused with `ticket_not_open`.

**Inventory change** (the table in `D8536.md`):

| Sender | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `send.ts` `sendBound` (`auth_recovery_resolved`) for the claim emails | Yes, but no link in the body | **Yes**, `auth_recovery_claim` under `recovery_notice`, attempt `claim.<path>.<n>`, link in the body, no bell row |
| `email.ts` `sendNotificationEmail` from `notifyAllChannels` | No (a second, unreferenced copy of each notice) | **Not called**: the fan-out no longer names `email` |

**Rollback.** Revert the two routes' edits and the template. Each notice is then mailed twice again, and the claim
email again carries no link.

**Verified.** `cloudflare-worker/test/email_delivery_contract_8536_part4.test.ts`, 8 tests, over the migrated
schema with Gmail stubbed at fetch.
- **1B:** a recovery mails the holder exactly one started notice and one resolved notice, and `notify()`'s in-app
  row stays.
- **The template:** the link is in both its text and its HTML parts.
- **Admin cosign:**
  - one co-signature mails nothing;
  - the second mails the holder a link on the sending host that `/claim` accepts;
  - an expired link answers `expired`;
  - a third co-signature mails a replacement and the earlier link answers `invalid_or_used`;
  - refused, then timed out after accepting, is one email under one reference;
  - no link reaches a log line or a stored row, and no bell row is written.
- **Trusted contact:**
  - the second attestation mails the holder, and no contact, a link that `/claim` accepts, with nothing logged or
    stored;
  - a third attestation is refused and mails nothing.

Mutation check: 12 mutations, 11 caught. The one not caught logs the link in the existing `catch` of the admin
send, which runs only when the send throws; no test injects that fault.
