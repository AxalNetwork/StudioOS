## D4715 — Advisor reads show another member only what is theirs to see: part 1, the directory and slot projections (#4715)

- **Status:** Built for #4715, 2026-11-07. Worker and frontend.
- **Part:** 1 of #4715. The access policy for the whole advisor domain, which defines one coded refusal and who may read and write each advisor record, comes in a later part.
- **No migration.**

### What was decided

1. **The directory and the match list carry no account email.**
   - `GET /api/advisors` and `GET /api/advisors/match` build each advisor with `directoryAdvisorDto`, which is `advisorDto` without `email`. The public profile (`GET /:uid`) never carried it.
   - `id` and `user_id` stay. The pages key advisors by `id`, and the trust badge and the booking button read `user_id`.
2. **A slot's meeting link and notes belong to its advisor.** `GET /api/advisors/:uid/slots` returns the slot whole only to:
   - the advisor;
   - an admin;
   - a founder holding a `confirmed` or `completed` booking on that slot.

   Everyone else gets `publicSlotDto`, with no `meeting_url` and no `notes`. It adds `has_meeting_link` instead. A pending or cancelled booking does not open the link.
3. **The page reads the flag.** `slotView` gains `hasMeetingLink`, taken from `has_meeting_link` when present and otherwise from the slot's own link. The directory's video badge reads `hasMeetingLink`, so it still shows on a slot that has a link.

### Partner office-hour slots

`GET /api/partner-office-hours/partners/:uid/slots` handed every signed-in member each slot's meeting link and the partner's slot notes. It now follows the same rule as the advisor slots:

- the slot is whole for the partner who owns it (`users.partner_id`), an admin, and a founder holding a `confirmed` or `completed` booking on it;
- everyone else, another partner included, gets `publicSlotDto`: no `meeting_url`, no `notes`, plus `has_meeting_link`.

The founder booking page (`SpinoutLabOfficeHoursPage`) reads neither field, so no page changes. `cloudflare-worker/test/partner_public_slots_4715.test.ts` covers it with six tests on the schema baseline; seven source mutations were checked, and each one fails a test.

### A booking's meeting link waits for the confirmation

A founder held a session's meeting link the moment they asked for it, not when the host agreed. Two places did this:

- **Partner bookings list.** `GET /api/partner-office-hours/bookings/me` joined the slot's `meeting_url` onto every row, whatever its status. It now goes through `founderBookingDto`: `meeting_url` only on a `confirmed` or `completed` booking, and `has_meeting_link` on every row. The partner's own list (`/me/bookings`) is unchanged. The advisor booking lists never carried the link.
- **Tentative calendar copy.** When a founder books, the tentative calendar event (advisor and partner, D4703) went out with the slot's link as its location. It now goes out with none. The confirmation's push (`deferCalendarConfirm`) still carries the link, so the founder's calendar gains it when the host confirms.

The Office Hours list says "link on confirmation" from `has_meeting_link`, and Join still needs a confirmed booking that carries its link. `cloudflare-worker/test/booking_meeting_link_4715.test.ts` (five tests, six mutations checked) and `frontend/test/booking_meeting_link_4715.test.mjs` (two tests, two mutations checked) cover it.

### The advisor access policy

Every advisor route now asks the same three questions, in this order. `cloudflare-worker/src/services/advisorAccess.ts` holds them.

1. **Eligibility.** Does the account hold the Advisor licence today? It counts as the primary role, or as an additional licence that has not been revoked (D4472). `requireAuth` reloads the account on every request, so a demotion or a revoked licence takes effect on the next request. An admin also passes for the profile and practice routes. Cohort reads stay licence-only (`mayHoldCohortAssignment`).
2. **Ownership.** Is the advisor row the request acts on the account's own? That is the `advisors` row whose `user_id` is the account. The stored `users.advisor_id` is a shortcut to that row and never a grant on its own.
3. **The record.** Is the booking, engagement, deliverable or slot on that row? Routes filter by the owned row's id, never by a path id.

A founder's side of a booking (the founder who made it) needs no licence.

What changed:

- **Who may create a profile.** `POST /me` and `GET /me` use `mayActAsAdvisor`, which is the same rule as before (D4670). It is now one export, and its refusal is the coded `advisor_licence_required`.
- **Booking transitions and reviews.** Confirm, complete, no-show, cancel and the advisor's review decided "the advisor" by comparing `users.advisor_id` with the booking's advisor. They checked neither the licence nor that the row was the account's own. They now use `actsForAdvisor`, which is eligibility plus ownership. An admin and the booking's founder keep the access they had.
- **Cohort list and guidance edits.** `GET /me/cohort` lists no cohort for an account without the licence. Its assignment rows stay, so a restored licence brings them back. `PATCH /me/guidance/:uid` needs the licence, as the other cohort writes already did through `requireOwnCohort`.
- **Every `/me/*` practice route** already resolved its row through `myAdvisor`. That is now `actingAdvisor`, so those routes follow the same rule with no change in behaviour.

What a demotion does:

- The account keeps its advisor row, its bookings and its cohort assignments. Nothing is deleted.
- It can no longer read or write anything as the advisor.
- Its founders keep their side: cancelling, paying and reviewing still work.
- An admin can still act on every booking.

`cloudflare-worker/test/advisor_access_policy_4715.test.ts` has six tests on the migrated schema. They cover a former advisor, a live advisor, an admin, a pointer to another account's row, an additional licence before and after it is revoked, reviews, the cohort list and a guidance edit. Six source mutations were checked, and each one fails a test.
