### 1. The tabs, each at its own address

| Tab | Address | Profiles |
|---|---|---|
| Companies | `/account/companies` | everyone |
| Projects | `/account/companies/projects` | founder, Operating Partner |
| Funds | `/account/companies/funds` | investor |
| Engagements | `/account/companies/engagements` | advisor |
| Invitations | `/account/companies/invitations` | everyone |

- **Which tabs a member sees is their profile's** (`profileOf`, `tabsFor`). A Super Admin is HQ, an Operating Partner is `partner`, an Explorer is `exploring`.
- **An address for a tab the profile does not have** opens Companies.
- **The tabs are `HeaderTab`s with their counts** (D936's shapes): a grey bar while reading, a dash when the read failed, the number when read.
- **`accountViewFor` already resolves each address to the section.** `accountPathFor({ section: 'companies', companiesTab })` gives it back, so the canonicalising effect keeps the tab instead of rewriting it to `/account/companies`.
- **Archived (the canvas's fourth tab) is not added.** It has no read until part 3 (#3017) lands archive and restore; a tab is not added before its feature.

### 2. The reads, each its own block

| Read | Drawn as |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/account/companies` (D1136) | the cards, and the Companies count |
| `GET /api/account/projects` (D1136), for Projects | the ventures, and the Projects count |
| `GET /api/account/invitations` (D1136) | Invitations to you, and its count |
| `GET /api/funds`, for an investor | Funds by the firm that runs them |
| `GET /api/advisor-grants/shared/list`, for an advisor | Engagements |

- **The every-project read (`GET /api/projects`) is never made.** It answers every venture to an investor or an Operating Partner.
- **The companies read is part 2's summary, not the memberships read.** The switcher's context (`ActiveCompanyContext`) gives the active company (this device's, by uid) and the numeric ids the funds read keys on.
- **Each D1136 figure is a block.** One that could not be read says so ("Members: could not be read", "Cycles: could not be read", "offers: could not be read"), never 0 and never "none".
- **A failed read is its own Unreadable with Try again,** and never blanks another tab. A failed summary says "That is not the same as having none", and the switcher in the sidebar reads Unavailable until it reads (CMP16b).

### 3. Each profile's board

- **Founder (CMP1, CMP3, CMP5):**
  - Companies: the active note, search, a role filter and active first. Each card has the role and authority, primary admin, stage and title, members with the editors' pending invitations, the cycles, last opened and the ventures count.
  - Projects: grouped by company, then "Not in a company" (the founder's own), then "Seats on other ventures". Each row shows the stage, Lab week, members, the seat and the cycles, with search and a company filter.
  - Invitations: both kinds, newest first, then "Invitations you sent" as each company's pending count, with Company Settings for resending and revoking.
- **Investor (CMP9):**
  - each card lists the funds its firm runs, with Manage where `can_manage`, or "Switch to this firm to manage";
  - the Funds tab groups them by firm, then "A fund with no firm";
  - LP positions link to `/funds`.
- **Operating Partner (CMP10):** each card's "In this firm" shows the engagements, quotes and offers counts.
- **Advisor (CMP11):**
  - Engagements: each founder's grant with what it shares (Project, Data room, Sessions), since when and until when, with Open brief (`/research/client-prep`);
  - the note "Practice and Expertise are not yet separated by company."
- **Explorer (CMP12):**
  - no New company, in the header or in the empty state;
  - "Creating a company opens once your profile is set." with Set up your profile;
  - Companies and Invitations only.
- **Admin and Super Admin (CMP13):**
  - the reach line with Open Licences (`/admin/my-licence`, or `/admin/licences` for HQ);
  - a Super Admin with no company reads "You belong to no company. HQ work does not need one."

### 4. The two writes, each an in-page dialog

- **Answering an invitation:** Accept (both kinds) and Decline (a venture seat).
  - The dialog says what it changes before it does it. Joining a company does not make it active.
  - It calls D1136's answer routes.
  - A refusal is said inside the dialog in words (`already_answered`, `expired`, `investor`, `not_found`).
  - After a company is joined, the switcher's list is read again.
  - A company invitation offers no Decline: D1136 §4 has no route until its migration lands, and the tab says so.
  - An investor's venture invitation says an investor account cannot take a seat.
- **New company** (the header's button, never an Explorer's):
  - the canvas's words: "You become its primary admin. It becomes your active company on this device.";
  - the switcher's own `create`, so the switcher and the page agree;
  - the Worker's refusals (D1135's name rules) are said inside the dialog.

  Part 5 (#3019) draws CMP6's name-clash board over it.

### 5. The states

- **Empty (CMP14):** "You don't belong to a company yet." with New company (not for an Explorer) and See invitations; "No ventures yet."; "No invitations waiting for you."
- **Loading (CMP15):** the header and tabs at once, the tab counts grey, and three reading cards.
- **Error and partial (CMP16):** each read's Unreadable; one failed invitation block is named while the other lists.
- **Phone (CMP18):** the tabs scroll in their own row, the cards stack, and New company is full width. Company Settings is not drawn on the card.
- **Dark:** every colour has its `dark:` pair.
