### What the canvas decides

- Ordinary HQ sees its own book read-only. The banner and header say whose book is open; search, view filters and the Person Sheet work; there is no Add, Import, Invite, Remove, Accept or Decline.
- Ties and contacts are two groups with two counts. Search announces its result, and Back returns to the results with focus on the row.
- A role preview (View as Advisor) draws the advisor layout over HQ's own book with every write off and End preview.
- A comparison matrix sets ordinary HQ, a role preview and a genuine advisor side by side, with the server permission behind each action.
- An advisor's requests (to you and from you, saying who must accept), accepted ties with last touch, and referrals with Axal's status are three records with their own glyph, word, status and refresh.
- Each section reads and retries on its own: a failed ties read never blanks loaded referrals.
- Remove tie goes Scope → Review → Result. The review names the person and the tie, what changes (both books, both scores) and what does not (referrals), and covers failure and "already ended".
- One change updates Overview, list and Organizations; referrals are not re-read.
