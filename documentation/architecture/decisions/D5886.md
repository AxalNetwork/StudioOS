## D5886 — Best-Fit consultation: the member is told in app on confirm and cancel (#5886)

**Decided by the owner, 2026-11-15: option B, in app only.** When an admin
moves a Best-Fit consultation:

- **confirmed:** the member gets one in-app notice, with the slot time in UTC
  when one is set, and the topic.
- **cancelled:** the member gets one in-app notice saying it will not take
  place and that another can be requested from their Studio home.
- **completed:** nothing is sent.
- Nothing is emailed for any status.

**How.** `POST /api/admin/consultations/:id/status` (`routes/consultations.ts`)
commits the status and its `admin_consultation_booking_events` row in one batch,
as before. Only after that commit does `noticeMember` call `notify` with
`channels: ['in_app']` and category `calendar`, linking to `/studio`. Following
D5300 §3, delivery is best effort. A failed notice never undoes or fails the
committed status: the route answers 200 with `notified: false`. `notified` is
`null` for a status the member is not told about. A refused transition
(version conflict, illegal move) commits nothing and sends nothing. The
statuses are one-way, so a retry cannot announce the same transition twice.
The notice carries no email address.

The Approvals lane's "Notifies" for Best-Fit now reads "The member, in app"
(`services/approvalSources.ts`).

**Verified.** `cloudflare-worker/test/consultations_5301.test.ts`:
- one notice each on confirm (naming the slot time) and cancel;
- none on completion or a refused transition;
- a failing inbox write leaves the status committed and reports
  `notified: false`.

`approval_lanes_d1041.test.ts` pins the lane's new "Notifies". Adding
`completed` to the noticed statuses, or dropping the notice call, fails these
tests.

Part of #4716 (package 2, member notification semantics).
