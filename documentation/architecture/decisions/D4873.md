## D4873 — Programs V2, part 1: a single choice reads as one, and no internal identifier in member copy (#4873)

- **Status:** Built for #4873 part 1, 2026-11-07. Frontend, plus one Worker sentence.
- **Builds on:** D953 (the Programs pages' parts), D961 (the catalogue's period and charts).
- **No migration. No new route.**
- **Part 1 of #4873.** The items that need the Worker (a member-access field, proposal revisions, capability flags) and the card layout that waits on them are later parts.

### What was decided

1. **A single choice is a radio group.**
   - The catalogue's period (4, 12 or 52 weeks) and every chart's Chart or Table were toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`). A screen reader announced them as independent switches, so nothing said which period was selected.
   - They are now one `ChoiceGroup` (`pages/admin/programs/parts.jsx`): a `radiogroup` with a label, whose options are `radio`s with `aria-checked`.
   - The selected option is the group's one tab stop. The arrow keys move the choice and the focus, and wrap at the ends, as a radio group's do.
   - A click on the selected option sends nothing, so the period is not read again.
   - The look is unchanged: the chips keep D953's lavender for the selected option.
2. **No internal identifier in copy a member reads.**
   - The People tile's left-out reason was "The Lab is left out until O-FND and #2374 say who a cohort's founders are." It now reads "The Lab is left out until a cohort's founders are defined.", the wording "How is this counted?" already used.
   - A sweep of the Programs pages and the Worker's program services found no other identifier in rendered copy. Comments and READMEs keep theirs.

### Tests

`frontend/test/programs_v2_4873.test.mjs` mounts `PeriodControl`, `ChartBlock` and `ChartCard`:
- the group's role and label, each option's `aria-checked` and tab stop, no `aria-pressed`;
- the arrows move the choice and the focus and wrap; other keys change nothing;
- a click on the checked option sends nothing;
- the table follows the choice;
- the Lab reason carries no identifier.

Eight mutants (the roles, the tab stop, the wrap, the focus, the repeat click, the key map, the old sentence) were each killed. The catalogue fixture takes the new sentence.
