### Part 1, built: a single choice reads as one, and no internal identifier in member copy

1. **A single choice is a radio group.**
   - The catalogue's period (4, 12 or 52 weeks) and every chart's Chart or Table were toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`). A screen reader announced them as independent switches, so nothing said which period was selected.
   - They are now one `ChoiceGroup` (`pages/admin/programs/parts.jsx`): a `radiogroup` with a label, whose options are `radio`s with `aria-checked`.
   - The selected option is the group's one tab stop. The arrow keys move the choice and the focus, and wrap at the ends, as a radio group's do.
   - A click on the selected option sends nothing, so the period is not read again.
   - The look is unchanged: the chips keep D953's lavender for the selected option.
2. **No internal identifier in copy a member reads.**
   - The People tile's left-out reason was "The Lab is left out until O-FND and #2374 say who a cohort's founders are." It now reads "The Lab is left out until a cohort's founders are defined.", the wording "How is this counted?" already used.
   - A sweep of the Programs pages and the Worker's program services found no other identifier in rendered copy. Comments and READMEs keep theirs.

`frontend/test/programs_v2_4873.test.mjs` mounts `PeriodControl`, `ChartBlock` and `ChartCard`:

**Worker.** Migration `202611082300_programs_member_access_modules_4873.sql` adds two columns to `programs`.

- **`member_access`.** One of `open`, `apply`, `request`, `invite`, `assigned`, `not_open` or `closed`, or NULL ("Not recorded").
  - The backfill derives it only from what the registry already records:
    - Learning's `who` already said "Not open to members yet".
    - A `not_built` or `proposed` program is `not_open`.
    - Otherwise the join method names it.
    - Anything else stays NULL.
  - New program and an approval write a `proposed` row as `not_open`.
  - `PATCH /api/admin/programs/:key` sets it (Super Admin only, audited as `program.registry.update`). It refuses any other word with 400 `bad_member_access`.
  - It is a record, like `join_method`: each program's own gate still decides what a member reaches.
- **`modules`.** A JSON list of capability modules (applications, hosts, cohorts, referral_queue, perk_queue, learners, influencers, offers, nda_signature).
  - The seed names only the modules that already have an operating screen. A new program has none.
  - Nothing writes it yet: adding a module needs that module's screen first (part 4).
- **Reads.** `GET /api/admin/programs` and `GET /api/admin/programs/:key` carry both fields on each row.
  - They come from their own guarded read (`registryExtras`).
  - A database the migration has not reached answers `extras: { available: false }` and the row still reads. The page says "Could not be read", never "Not recorded".
  - A read with no field at all (an older branch's) says "Not read here yet".

### Built (part 3), 2026-11-09: proposal revisions, and the review drawer (PG04)

Part of #4873. This builds the third Worker item ("proposal revision numbers and an immutable snapshot per send; the decision names it") and PG04. The canvas files are unchanged.

**Worker.** Migration `202611090830_program_proposal_revisions_4873.sql`.

- **`program_proposals.revision`.** The number of the latest send; NULL for a proposal never sent.
- **`program_proposal_revisions`.** One row per send.
  - It holds the proposal's own columns as sent (`spec_json`), and when and by whom it was sent.
  - Once HQ decides that revision, it also holds the outcome (`approved_studio`, `approved_catalogue`, `sent_back` or `declined`), HQ's note, and when and by whom.
  - It is append-only: the triggers refuse a DELETE, any change to the send's half, and a second decision.
- **Send** (`sendProposal`). The UPDATE takes the next revision number. In the same batch, an INSERT … SELECT freezes the fields from the row that UPDATE just locked. What HQ reads is what was sent, whatever the studio edits after a send-back.
- **Decide** (`decide`). The request names the revision it decides (`expected_revision`).
  - None is 400 `expected_revision_required`.
  - A proposal sent again since is 409 `revision_changed`, and nothing is written.
  - The decision's UPDATE repeats the check (`AND revision = ?`).
  - The decision is recorded on its revision once, gated on having won (D4806's gate, plus the revision).
  - The approved spec (`program_approved_specs.spec_json.revision`) and the audit line name the revision.
- **Reads.**
  - `GET /api/admin/programs/proposals/:uid` and the decision's answer carry `revision` and `revisions` (oldest first: the frozen fields, or `spec_recorded: false`, and the decision on each).
  - HQ's queue rows carry `revision` and `decided_at`. Your proposals' rows carry `revision`.
- **The backfill records what can be known, and no more:**
  - Every proposal ever sent becomes revision 1. A send before this migration left no trace, so the history starts here.
  - Sent, approved and declined proposals: their fields are what was sent, since a sent proposal cannot be edited.
  - A sent-back proposal: its fields are frozen only if nobody edited it after the send-back (`updated_at <= decided_at`). Otherwise `spec_json` stays NULL, and the page says it is not recorded.
  - Sending is still closed (`PROPOSALS_CLOSED`, D955), so production likely holds few or none of these.

**Frontend (PG04).**

- **The queue** (`/admin/programs/proposals`).
  - Columns: Proposal, Studio, Submitted ("Sent 4 Nov · 3 days" while waiting, "Decided 21 Oct" once decided), Revision, Status and Review.
  - The status filter is a radio group with its counts ("Waiting for HQ 1 · Sent back 1 · Approved 1 · Declined 0"). A count that has not read shows the word alone, never 0.
  - Every row's Review opens the drawer.
- **The review drawer** (`ProposalReviewSheet.jsx`). A `Sheet` (D1317) over the list, at `/admin/programs/proposals/:uid`; closing it returns to the list.
  - It shows "Revision 2 · submitted 4 Nov 2026, 10:12 UTC by Studio North · read-only", the full submitted specification of that revision, and the history ("12 Oct · Revision 1 sent · sent back by HQ: “Add eligibility”").
  - A radio group switches between revisions. An earlier revision is shown as it was sent, and is never decided.
  - A revision whose fields were not recorded says "Not recorded" with the reason, never the current fields.
- **The decision** goes Scope → Review → Result.
  - Decline, Send back, or Approve revision N (with its scope: this studio only, or the catalogue) picks it.
  - A review step says what it does to which revision, and quotes the note.
  - Confirm sends it with `expected_revision`, and the result stays in the drawer.
  - The note is required for Send back and Decline before anything is sent.
  - `revision_changed` is shown in words and the drawer reads the proposal again.
- **Retired:** D964's Decide dialog (`DecideDialog.jsx`), which the drawer replaces. `ProposalReviewPage.jsx` now draws the list with the drawer.

**Departures from the canvas:**

- **Approve keeps its scope.** The canvas draws one Approve; the Worker has two approvals (this studio only, or the catalogue, D955), so the drawer asks which with a radio group above "Approve revision N".
- **A review step before every decision**, not only Decline, as the brief asks for consequential actions. An approval creates a program.
- **No "Studio ▾ / Oldest first ▾" chips.** The studio filter and the sort stay the existing selects.
