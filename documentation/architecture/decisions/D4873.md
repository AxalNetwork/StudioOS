## D4873 — HQ › Programs v2: an operating console whose numbers reconcile (#4873)

- **Status:** canvas landed 2026-11-07 (`design/incoming/Programs v2 Page.dc.html`, `design/incoming/Programs v2 · Boards.dc.html`, boards PG01 to PG08). Not built.
- **Builds on:** `programRegistry.ts` (statuses live · partly_built · not_built · proposed · paused; six groups), `programProposals.ts` (draft · sent · approved · sent_back · declined), `admin_program_grants.ts` (granted · paused · ended), `PUT /api/admin/programs/:key/grantable`. Related: #4807, #4808.

### What the canvas decides

- **Header:** neutral Scope selector (HQ-held or a studio), reporting period as a radio group, last updated, Refresh, New program; Catalogue / Proposals below.
- **Four headline cards** that reconcile with the queues: Actions requiring HQ 13 = 12 Perk listings + 1 Strategic referral access. A partial figure is drawn dashed, marked Incomplete and states its coverage (“9 of 10 programmes counted”); an unread queue makes a headline “12+”.
- **Five separate dimensions** per programme, never one badge: build readiness, operational state, member access, studio offering policy, existing studio grants.
- **Catalogue** as compact rows grouped by category (cards on narrow screens). A custom programme uses the same row, card and detail shell.
- **Generic programme shell:** Overview, Playbook, Availability & grants, Participation, History, Settings, plus optional capability modules (Perk queue, Cohorts, Referral queue, …). It works with none.
- **Proposals:** a summary on the overview; review on its own route, with the full read-only specification of one revision and a primary action that names the revision approved.
- **Eadwyn:** recommendations, or “No recommendations for Programs right now.” No model lists or pricing. No internal identifiers anywhere.

### Backend required

- A member-access field (“not open to members yet” is not stored).
- Proposal revision numbers and an immutable snapshot per send (a sent-back proposal is edited in place today).
- Capability-module flags on the registry.
