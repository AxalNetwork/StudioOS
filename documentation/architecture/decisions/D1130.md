### Part 2 — Ask for access, stored and answered (#3010, CT15n, CT22b)

The second PR, on `main` after #3062, by S22 (the S21 session). It closes #3010.

**The store.** Migration `202611031740_cap_table_access_requests.sql` adds `cap_table_access_requests`:
- one row per ask: the venture, who asked, the level asked for (Own position, Summary, Investor or Full; never None), and an optional note of at most 500 characters;
- `status` goes from `pending` to `approved` or `declined` once, with `resolved_by` and `resolved_at`. An approval records the level the founder granted, which may differ from the ask, and the grant row it created;
- a partial unique index holds at most one pending ask per person and venture, in the database and not only in code;
- there is no email, phone or IP column.

**The routes** (`routes/captable_access.ts`):
- `POST /:projectId/ask` takes `{ level?, note? }`. D1027's rule for who may ask is unchanged: an investor introduced to the venture whom the founder has not shared with. It now stores a pending request (the level defaults to Investor). A second ask while one is pending, or within a day of a decline, is 409 `already_asked`. The founder is told, as before, and the `captable_access_asked` audit row stays.
- `GET /:projectId/requests`: the owner and co-founders read the pending asks, with name and role and never an email. `can_change` is the owner's alone.
- `POST /:projectId/requests/:uid/approve` takes `{ level? }`, the asked level when none is named. Owner only. One batch resolves the request, revokes any live grant for that person, inserts the new grant (`source_ref = request:<uid>`), writes the `captable_access_granted` audit row and links the grant to the request. Every statement after the first acts only on a request the same batch has just resolved, so an ask answered twice grants once. The asker is told.
- `POST /:projectId/requests/:uid/decline`. Owner only. Nothing is granted, one `captable_access_declined` audit row is written, the asker is told, and they can ask again after a day. An answered request is 409 `already_answered`; an unknown one is 404.

**The pages:**
- **Access tab:** a new "Asked for access" section above "Who sees what" lists the pending asks. The owner answers each in a dialog: Approve, with the asked level preselected and every askable level offered with the Worker's own sentence, or Decline. A co-founder reads the list and changes nothing. An unreadable queue says so with Try again; it never reads as empty.
- **CT15n and CT22b:** the investor's panel (CT15n) and the other company's refusal page (CT22b) use one `AskForAccess` button and dialog. When the Worker answers that this person cannot ask (`investors_only`, or a venture they were not introduced to), the page says "Only an investor introduced to this venture can ask its founder for access." instead of an error.

**Tests:** `captable_ask_d1027` gains four D1130 tests: the stored ask with its level and note, the founders' read, approve, and decline. The new `frontend/test/captable_requests_d1130` covers the rows, the client, the Access tab and CT22b. Nine mutations were each seen red and then restored:
- the approve ignoring the founder's level;
- the decline's day being dropped;
- the pending index not unique;
- answered asks listed as pending;
- a co-founder approving;
- the owner-only buttons drawn for everyone;
- the asked level not preselected;
- the CT22b refusal not explained;
- an email used as the name.
