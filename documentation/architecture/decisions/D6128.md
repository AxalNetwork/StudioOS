### What the canvas decides

- `/onboarding/investor` keeps the live frame: one card per step, "Step n of 7" with a progress bar, Back / Skip for now / Continue. Steps 1 to 4 are required.
- The "What matters most" sliders are dropped.
- Accreditation and country are labelled "You told us · not verified".
- Sectors use the NFX list with search and a pick count. A thesis sector shows as unavailable under exclusions, marked "In your thesis".
- Eadwyn drafts the thesis on the shared rail from steps 1 to 5 only, with Accept, Edit or Discard. Nothing is saved until Continue.
- Review groups answers by step, with Edit and return. Skipped, None picked and Not recorded are written out in words.
- Finish saves the profile, then marks onboarding complete, then shows one of three endings: the fund application prefilled, the next steps, or an exploring member.
- A failed progress read is a state of its own, not a first visit. A failed save or finish is shown, not swallowed (IO-8).
