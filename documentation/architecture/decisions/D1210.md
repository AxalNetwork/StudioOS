## D1210 — Calendar part 5: the month and week grids, the agenda, the Kinds filter, the day popover, the states, dark, and the Explorer (CAL1–CAL3, CAL6, CAL12–CAL17, CAL20)

- **Status:** built by S20, 2026-10-26 (#3176, part 5 of #2420). Not deployed or live verified.
- **Basis:** `main` at `c85d3d21`, after D1205's views, D1206 and D1207 (parts 1 and 2), and D1211's item drawer (#3319).
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Calendar Page.dc.html` and `Calendar · Boards.dc.html`.
- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** no change. The page draws the same `GET /api/calendar/events` read, in each view's window.

### 1. The views

- **Month (CAL1):**
  - six Monday-first weeks;
  - days outside the month muted, and today ringed;
  - three items a day in time order (all-day first), then "+N more".
  - **Opening a day:** "+N more", or the day's number, opens the day popover (CAL12) with every item that day.
  - **Opening an item:** each opens its D1211 drawer at `/calendar/item/:kind/:id`.
  - **An item with no record** to open draws as text.
- **Week (CAL2, CAL20):** 07:00 to 21:00, with an all-day row on top.
  - **The all-day row:** an item that runs a whole day or more, or that the Worker marks all-day.
  - **Overlapping items** sit side by side: greedy lanes per cluster of overlaps, so a later lone item keeps the whole width.
  - **The now line** shows on today within the hours, in the canvas's violet, which lifts to #a78bfa in the dark (`--cal-accent`).
  - **An item with no end** is drawn 30 minutes tall and says "End not recorded".
  - **An item running past the hours** is clipped and says so. One wholly outside the hours is counted under its day ("N outside 07:00–21:00 · open the day"), never dropped.
- **Agenda (CAL3):** the list grouped by day, which every view drew before.
- **The anchor:** `?date=YYYY-MM-DD` anchors every view.
  - A day that does not exist (31 February) is ignored, and the anchor is today.
  - Earlier and Later step a month on Month and a week on Week and Agenda; Today drops the parameter.
  - `windowFor(view, anchor)` asks the Worker for that window.

### 2. The Kinds filter (CAL6)

It replaces the chip row. A button reads "Kinds · N on", or "needs me", and opens a panel:
- **the three quick sets:** Everything, Only what needs me, Hide deadlines;
- **the families with their kinds:** Events, Meetings, Sessions, Deadlines, From other calendars;
- **counts from the one list** (`counts`, still `chipCount` over `inRange`);
- **Reset;**
- **"Show N items"**, which writes `?kinds=` with every other parameter kept. Every kind chosen drops it.

**The panel never invents a count:**
- A kind whose source did not answer reads "— · Could not be read", and its family says "and 1 unread".
- With no list read, it shows no count at all, and the button reads "Show these kinds".
- Deadlines carry no kind yet, so they say "Not read here yet". Hide deadlines therefore shows every kind there is.

**"Only what needs me"** picks items, not kinds: `?needs=1` keeps the items whose attendee row for the viewer is invited or unanswered. Choosing it by kind would have brought in every item of those kinds.

When the choice leaves nothing in range, the page says so (CAL15 B), and names how many items of other kinds are there, with Show everything.

### 3. Keyboard and screen readers

- **One tab stop:** each grid has one, on today, or on the 1st when today is not in the month.
- **Movement:** the arrows move a day, up and down a week, and Home and End to the week's ends, clamped to the grid.
- **Day labels:** each day reads as "Thursday 12 November, 3 items" (or "1 item", or "no items"), with no count when the list was not read.
- **Today** carries `aria-current="date"`.

### 4. The states (CAL15–CAL17)

- **Empty:** "Nothing scheduled" says which sources answered. New check-in is not offered to an Explorer.
- **Loading:** the skeleton.
- **Errors:**
  - one failed source is a dash in the Kinds panel and the degraded strip;
  - when nothing answered, no grid is drawn, so a month of empty days never stands in for an unread calendar.

### 5. The Explorer (CAL14)

- **The routes:** `/calendar`, `/calendar/week`, `/calendar/agenda` and `/calendar/item/:kind/:id` admit `exploring`. The Worker's `GET /api/calendar/events` has no role gate, and an Explorer's sources are their event tickets and Calendly.
- **What they see:**
  - the Kinds filter and the legend list only Events and From other calendars (`familiesFor`);
  - no New menu and no IC strip;
  - no New check-in in the empty state.
- **The account menu** marks Calendar "Current" on every calendar address, as CAL1 draws it.

### 6. Departures and not built

- **The canvas's drag on Saturday** (CAL2), which shows the slot a drag would book, is not built. New opens D1211's dialogs.
- **Deadline flags first in a day (CAL1)** wait for a deadline read (D1205's WAITS).
- **The hover quick look on Mon 23 (CAL12)** is not built. The day popover and each item's drawer carry the same facts.
- **CAL13's Studio scope switch** is part 1's read (`scope=`). This part draws the viewer's own calendar only.
- **The phone (CAL21)** keeps D700's phone page over the same views. Its one-week strip and dots-only month are a later part.
