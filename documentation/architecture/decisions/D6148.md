## D6148 — Screening: the firm's own pass memory, and passed deals that come back on their date (#6148)

- **Status:** Built for #6148, 2026-11-14. Worker and frontend.
- **Builds on:** D1238 (an investor firm's own deal flow, migration 618), D1241 (New for you, the Pass dialog), D1260 (Screening reads the scorecard), D4632 (a flow row's version guard).
- **No migration.** `deal_flow_entries` already stores `pass_reason`, `passed_at`, `revisit` and `revisit_at`.
- **Under #4815 DX6.** #5909 redraws the rest of Screening; this decision changes only the Pass reasons view and its strip tile.

### What was decided

1. **Pass reasons on Screening is the firm's own record.**
   - It reads `GET /api/deals/flow/passes` (`routes/deal_flow.ts`, `services/dealFlow.ts` `readPasses`). The read is scoped the way the flow is: the firm selected (`c:<company_id>`), or the investor's own (`u:<user_id>`). It never reads another firm's rows, and it never reads `deals.status`.
   - It counts each of the five reasons and lists each passed deal with its reason, its date, its return rule (`revisit`, `revisit_at`) and whether that date has arrived (`due`).
   - It reads the same rows as `GET /api/deals/flow` counts in `by_stage.passed`, a deleted venture left out of both. A Worker test asserts the two numbers agree.
   - When there are no passes, the share is null rather than 0%.
   - `/passes` is a literal GET registered with `/inbox` and `/compare-note`, before every `/:dealId` route. Only PATCH and POST take a bare `/:dealId` today, so a GET literal could not be shadowed anyway.
2. **The studio's aggregate leaves the investor page (owner's call, default built: option A on #6148).** Screening used to read `GET /api/deals/pass-analytics`, the studio's round-level passes, under the title "the fund's memory". Nothing on Screening reads it now. The studio's aggregate stays on the studio's own Deal flow ("Why we passed" in `StudioLens.jsx`), for an admin or an Operating Partner. The question of whether investors should read it at all (`pass-analytics` admits holders of the investor licence today) belongs to #4631. It is raised there through the PR, not changed here.
3. **Each reason opens its deals.** A row with passes has Show its deals, which lists them with Passed on and Comes back: "Back on 12 Mar 2027", "Next round", or Not recorded. A deal the reader may open has Open deal room.
4. **Three states, three sentences.** A readable, empty record says there are no passes in the flow yet. An account with no investor licence (`investor_required`) is told that Pass reasons is an investor firm's own. An unreadable read is Not recorded, and the tile shows no figure. None of these is 0.
5. **A pass with a date comes back in New for you on that date.**
   - `inboxItems` has a `revisit` source: rows of this flow at Passed with `revisit = 'date'` and `revisit_at` on or before today (UTC).
   - Unlike every other source, its deal is already in the flow. The item carries `return: { reason, passed_at, revisit_at }` and is labelled "Back from your pass".
   - It offers **Back to Screening**, which is the flow's own `PATCH` move off Passed (it clears the pass from the row and keeps it in the events). It also offers Not for me and, where DEAL's rule holds, Open. It never offers Add or Pass.
   - The key is `revisit:<entry id>:<YYYYMMDD>`. Not for me hides that return. A later pass with a later date brings the deal back again.
6. **Next round is not detected.** A deal row records no round of its own: no round name or number. A second deal on the same venture can also be a CRM import or a refiled application. So nothing in stored data tells a new round apart.
   - The inbox read says so (`sources.next_round = { detected: false, reason }`), and so do New for you and the Pass reasons note.
   - A pass marked Next round never comes back by itself.
   - The taxonomy's resurfacing promises (`passReasonRevisit`) are no longer printed on Screening, since the product does not act on them.
7. **The return date shows on a passed deal.** It appears on the peek (Passed for, Comes back) and on the board card, from the flow row's `pass`.

### Options put to the owner on #6148

- **A (built):** Screening shows only the firm's passes. The studio's aggregate stays on the studio's own Deal flow.
- **B:** Screening also keeps the studio's aggregate, as a second view clearly named "Studio round passes (not your firm's)", for readers `pass-analytics` admits.
- **C:** As B, but for an admin or an Operating Partner only, after #4631 settles who may read studio pass data.
