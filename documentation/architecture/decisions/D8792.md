## D8792 — Diligence Templates: the blocking switch's hidden name escaped the table scroller and widened the phone page (#8792)

- **Status:** browser retest and the layout-free fix built by S21 for #8792, part of the browser-audit handoff #8795. `needs-design` stays: the phone layout, hit areas and focus outline wait on the canvas.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `d26a93a25`, SPA built locally from `frontend/src` and served from `docs/`. `/api/*` was answered in the browser by fictional fixtures: a Super Admin and an Investor with `example.invalid` addresses, and built-in Lite, Standard and Deep templates of six sections each. No production, no real account.
- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** unchanged (`GET /api/dd/templates` already says `can_edit`).

### 1. Retest (2026-11-27)

Chromium 1243 (Playwright 1.64.1) at 1440, 768, 390 and 320 px, light and dark, on `/due-diligence/templates`. Two readers:
- **Super Admin:** `can_edit: true`, so each row draws a weight input and a blocking switch;
- **restricted reader:** an Investor, `can_edit: false`, so the same table with no control.

The section names were short (Team … Traction) or long (up to 73 characters).

| Settled document width | 1440 | 768 | 390 | 320 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Super Admin, short names | 1440 | 768 | 390 | **325** |
| Super Admin, long names | 1440 | 768 | **448** | **448** |
| Restricted reader | 1440 | 768 | 390 | 320 |
| My reviews, same session | 1440 | 768 | 390 | 320 |

Dark measured the same as light at every width.

- **Reproduced:** the document grew wider than the phone for a Super Admin only.
  - The six switches are 13 × 13 px, and each `label` hit area is the same 13 × 13.
  - They sat at x = 325 at both 390 and 320 with short names, and further right with long ones, the "six inputs at x = 402" the audit saw in the Investor shell.
- **Cause:** found by hiding elements one at a time.
  - Each switch was named by `<span className="sr-only">{title} blocks</span>`.
  - `.sr-only` is `position: absolute`, and its offset parent was `body`: no ancestor inside the table's `overflow-x-auto` is positioned.
  - So the six spans escaped the scroller. They stayed at the switch's unscrolled x even after the table was scrolled, and they widened the document.
  - Hiding them alone brought 320 px back from 325 to 320.
- **The table itself** scrolls locally, as intended: the scroller is 324 of 720 px at 390, 254 of 720 at 320 and 686 of 720 at 768.
  - The scroller has no `role`, label or `tabindex`, so a keyboard user scrolls it only by tabbing into a control.
- **Keyboard:** the first switch is the 32nd Tab stop at 390 and 320 (33rd at 1440). Focus scrolled the table locally to bring it into view (`scrollLeft` 260 at 390 with long names), and Space toggled it.
- **Cards and Save:**
  - the three depth cards stack at 358 × 98 (390) and 288 × 98 (320);
  - "Save as new version" sits below the table at x = 33, about 1 300 px down the page.
- **Seen in passing:** the shell header's right-hand group overflows its own box by 1 px (light) and 17 px (dark) at 390. The header clips it, so the document does not grow. That is the account-menu work in #8682, not this issue.

### 2. What changed (no layout)

- `frontend/src/pages/duediligence/DdTemplates.jsx`: the switch is named by `aria-label="{title} blocks"`, and the `sr-only` span is gone.
  - The accessible name is the same and the visible layout is unchanged.
  - Nothing absolutely positioned is left inside the scroller.
- Rechecked on the rebuilt SPA: the Super Admin's page settles at 1440, 768, 390 and 320 with short and with long names.
  - Tab still reaches the first switch as stop 32 and scrolls the table locally.
  - Space toggles it, and the page never scrolls sideways (`scrollX` 0).
- Tests: `frontend/test/dd_templates_phone_8792.test.mjs` (3).
  - It mounts the page for a Super Admin and for a restricted reader, and pins the switch names and the absence of any `sr-only` node in the table.
  - Mutation-checked: restoring the span fails two of the three.

### 3. What waits on the canvas

- The 13 × 13 switch and its hit area. The issue asks whether a label supplies a larger one: today it does not.
- How the seven-column table reads on a phone: kept as a scrolling region, or rows reflowed into cards.
- Whether the scroller becomes a labelled, focusable region.
- Where Save sits relative to the table.
- The focus outline at 320 and 390.

### 4. questions:

- **Route:** `/due-diligence/templates`. The Admin board's copy, `/admin/due-diligence/templates`, draws the same `DdTemplates`. A founder is sent to `/due-diligence/requests`.
- **Who sees it:** admins, partners, investors and advisors (`App.jsx` guard). Only a Super Admin on HQ edits (`can_edit`). Everyone else reads the same table under the line "HQ edits templates. You choose one when you open a case."
- **The parts and their stores:** all from `GET /api/dd/templates` (D1249's `dd_templates` in D1, falling back to the built-in catalog per depth).
  - **Depth cards (Lite, Standard, Deep):** each with its version (`v<n>` or Built-in), section and item counts, and a one-line blurb.
  - **The chosen depth's table:** each section's title, weight (an input for the Super Admin), share of the total, blocking (a switch for the Super Admin, a "Blocking" chip otherwise) and items per depth, then a Total row.
  - **Save as new version:** Super Admin only (`POST /api/dd/templates`), through a dialog listing the changes.
  - **Versions line:** the depth's versions, each with who saved it.
- **States to draw:**
  - loading ("Reading the templates…");
  - **Unreadable:** "The templates could not be read." with Try again, which is not an empty store;
  - a depth with no `current` row: the card's counts say Unreadable;
  - **Not recorded:** a version saved with no recorded author shows no "by" name;
  - the restricted reader;
  - a Super Admin with a draft, a refused save and a saved version;
  - a long section name (73 characters);
  - the selected depth card (`aria-pressed`).
- **Questions for the owner:**
  1. On a phone, does the table stay one horizontally scrolling region, or does each section become a card with its weight, share, blocking and item counts stacked?
  2. Should the blocking switch get a full-row or at least 44 × 44 px hit area, with its name visible rather than only announced?
  3. Should Save stay below the table, or stick to the bottom of the viewport while a draft is open?
  4. How does a reader get back to My reviews from here on a phone: the tab strip above, or a back link?
