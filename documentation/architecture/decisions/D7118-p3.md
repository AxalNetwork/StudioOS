### What is built

- **The read.** This week (`/wellbeing?period=week`) makes one more read: `GET /api/wellbeing/reflections?cadence=week&limit=8`. That is the member's own saved weeks, newest first, through the existing author-scoped route.
- **The table** (`FourWeeks`, `FourWeeksTable` in `pages/wellbeing/Reflections.jsx`; `fourWeekTable` in `reflectionsModel.js`).
  - **Columns:** the four week starts ending with this week, oldest first ("26 Oct", "2 Nov", "9 Nov", "16 Nov"). They are worked out from this week's `period_start` by calendar arithmetic, never from the device clock, so the member's own week start and time zone (D7118) decide them.
  - **Rows:** each weekly question the member answered in at least one of the four weeks, in the canvas's order (Stress, Sleep, Support, Decisions, Energy). The canvas draws Sleep and Energy because those are the questions its sample member answered.
  - **Cells:** the member's own number.
    - A week with no reflection, or a question left blank, reads "— no entry", never 0.
    - A reflection that could not be read reads "couldn’t be read" rather than looking blank.
    - A day or month reflection never fills a week.
  - **The note under the table:** "A week or a question you left blank reads “no entry”, never 0. Your own numbers, not a score."
  - **When nothing was answered in the four weeks** it says "Nothing answered in the last four weeks."
  - **A failed read** says so, with Retry: "Your last four weeks couldn’t be read. They are unchanged." It is never an empty table.
- **Privacy is unchanged.**
  - The numbers are the member's own, read from their own reflections.
  - Nothing is scored, compared or sent to a model.
  - The table refreshes after a save or a delete on the page (`version`).

### Where this departs from the canvas

- **The note is generic.** The canvas's note names the blank week ("In the week of 9 Nov you left Sleep and Energy blank"). The built note states the rule for every blank, so it never has to list several weeks.
