## D1591 — Slug availability while typing: `GET /api/account/slug-check`

- **Status:** S11, 2026-10-31 (#3858; Portfolio canvas #3696, boards PF4a, PF4b and PF6). No migration.
- **Basis:** migration 685's slug columns and `slug_history`, and `services/slugs.ts` (`checkSlug`, `slugHolder`, `companyPath`), from D1415 (#3697 part 1, #3853).

### 1. Why a read

The create sheets and the slug rows draw a live answer under the address field ("✓ Available", or "That address is taken in [Northwind Labs]. Try [ledgerline-2]."). No route could give it. `PUT /api/company/:uid/slug` answers only after a write, and `GET /api/account/resolve` (#3697 part 2) answers 404 for both "free" and "not yours", by design.

### 2. The route

`GET /api/account/slug-check?kind=company|project&slug=…[&company=<uid>][&target=<company uid | project id>]`, in `routes/slug_check.ts`, mounted at `/api` beside `account_companies.ts`. Signed in; it writes nothing.

| Answer field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `slug` | the value trimmed and lower-cased |
| `available` | free in its scope, or already held by `target` |
| `reason` | `null`, `slug_invalid`, `slug_reserved` or `slug_taken` |
| `suggestion` | for `slug_taken` only: the first of `<slug>-2` … `<slug>-20` with a valid shape that is free in the same scope, else `null` |
| `path` | `/c/<slug>`, `/c/<company slug or uid>/p/<slug>` or `/p/<slug>`; `null` for an invalid or reserved slug |

- **Scopes** are 685's: a company slug is global; a project slug is unique within its company (`company`) or among standalone projects (no `company`). A slug in `slug_history` stays taken for everyone but its target (#3697 question C, answer a).
- **A bad shape or a reserved word** is a 200 with `available: false` and its reason, so the field can say why. Neither gets a suggestion.
- **Refusals:** 400 `kind_invalid`; 401 signed out; 404 `not_found` when `company` is not a company the caller belongs to. An unknown uid reads the same 404 body. A platform admin counts as a member, as `viewerIsMember` does.

### 3. It never names the holder

The answer carries no name, uid, id or count of whoever holds a slug. Two rules keep it so:

- **`company`** answers only inside the caller's own company.
- **`target`** counts only when the caller may reach it: a company they belong to, or a project in the scope being checked that they found, hold an accepted seat on, or that sits in their own company. Any other target is ignored, not refused, so it can neither confirm nor deny that some other row holds a slug. A project that left a company keeps its retired slug there, and a member of that company cannot claim it through `target`, because the target's scope is compared.

### 4. The suggestion and create

`nextFreeSlug(env, kind, base, scopeCompanyId)` in `services/slugs.ts` is the loop. #3697 part 2 merged first with its own `generateSlug(env, kind, name, scopeCompanyId)`, which slugifies a name and then tries `-2`, `-3`… up to `-50`, trimming the base to fit. For a slug the member typed (already a valid slug, without repeated hyphens, short enough for its suffix), both give the same next address, so the field and the save agree. The check keeps its own loop because its input is a slug, not a name, and the issue fixed its range at `-2` to `-20`.

### 5. Client

`slugsApi.check({ kind, slug, company, target })` in `frontend/src/lib/api/slugs.js`, re-exported by one line in `api.js` (D527). S1's `CreateSheets.jsx` (PF4) and the slug rows in company and project settings (PF6) call it, debounced.
