## D1412 — The Portfolio card's legal line, location and status, on `GET /api/account/companies`

- **Status:** built by S17, 2026-10-31 (#3857). The issue was filed for S09; the owner moved it to S17.
- **Number:** #3696's spec reserved D1412 for the Portfolio Worker model. #3697 moved that model to D1415, so D1412 was free, on `main` and in every open PR (S1 checked, 2026-10-31).
- **Canvas:** the Portfolio canvas (#3696): PF1, PF5 and PF7. Each company card draws a legal line ("legal name · entity type · jurisdiction · incorporated [date]"), a status pill (Active, amber "In formation", Archived), the location ("[Berlin HQ] +[1]") and the team count.
- **Builds on:** D1136 (`GET /api/account/companies`), D1137 (archiving), D1415 (migration 685: the legal columns, `company_locations` and `incorporations.company_id`) and D601 (the formation order's states).
- **Migration:** none.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `ce9e885b33`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/`.

### 1. The problem

The cards' read carried none of the legal, location or status facts. The only way to draw them was one `GET /api/company/:uid/legal` per card (N+1).

### 2. What each company now carries

`routes/account_companies.ts` adds these to each company. Each block is read **once for every company** (a `json_each(?)` statement, like the existing counts) and fails on its own as `{ available: false, reason }`, never as 0.

| Field | Shape | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `archived_at` | string or null | `company_profiles.archived_at`, in the links read |
| `legal` | `Block<{ legal_name, legal_form, jurisdiction, incorporation_date }>`; each field null when not recorded, never `""` | `company_profiles` |
| `primary_location` | `Block<{ label, kind, city, country } \| null>`: the open `headquarters` row first, else the open `registered` row, else the first open row by id | `company_locations` where `closed_on IS NULL` |
| `location_count` | `Block<number>`: the open locations | the same read |
| `formation` | `Block<{ order_id, order_status, jurisdiction_id } \| null>`: the latest order in `paid`, `packet_processing` or `packet_ready` | `incorporations` joined to `projects`; the company is `COALESCE(i.company_id, p.company_id)` |
| `status` | `Block<'active' \| 'in_formation' \| 'archived'>` | derived (below) |

**Status:**
- `archived` when `archived_at` is set;
- otherwise `in_formation` when `formation` holds an order;
- otherwise `active`;
- unavailable when the orders could not be read and the company is not archived.

**An order's company** is the one it was linked to at filing (D1415 Part 3), else its venture's company now. An order linked to a company keeps counting there after its venture moves away, and does not count for the venture's new company.

**What it never carries:**
- the tax ID, the registration number, officers or full addresses (the card draws none of them, and `GET /api/company/:uid/legal` is the place for them);
- an order's amount, payer or any Stripe field.

**The gate is unchanged:** only the caller's own links.

### 3. Screens

S1 integrates them, with no new client method. `accountCompaniesApi.companies()` already calls this route. Until this merges, the cards draw the legal line from nothing and say so; they never draw a zero.

### 4. Verified

`cloudflare-worker/test/account_companies_d1136.test.ts`:
- Its fixture now adds the baseline's `incorporations` and migrations 597 and 685, and its 7 tests stay green.
- Three tests are new:
  1. The legal block: the four fields, and four nulls inside an available block. There is one statement per block for every company. No tax ID, registration number, amount or Stripe field appears. A failed legal read costs only its block, and the counts still answer.
  2. The primary location: headquarters beats registered, and a closed headquarters is skipped. With no open location it is null with a count of 0. A failed read makes both blocks unavailable, not 0.
  3. The status: a `paid` order means in formation, with its order id. `pending_payment` is ignored, and `filed` means active. An order linked by `incorporations.company_id` counts after its venture moves, and not for the new company. Archived wins over in formation. A failed orders read makes `status` and `formation` unavailable for a live company, while an archived company stays archived.

5 mutations were run and all 5 caught:
- the legal record read per company (N+1);
- `location_count` returning 0 when the read failed;
- `pending_payment` counted as in formation;
- the company taken from `p.company_id` only;
- `registered` ranked above `headquarters`.
