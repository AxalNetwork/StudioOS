### 1. The read

`GET /api/admin/testing/overview`, on `routes/testing.ts`'s HQ router, `requireSuperAdmin`.
- **Where it is built:** `services/testingOverview.ts`.
- **The client:** `adminTestingApi.overview()` in `frontend/src/lib/api/productTesting.js`.
- **Every statement is bound.** No row names a member, and **no field carries an email address**, or a name.
- **Each block answers alone:** `{ available: true, … }` or `{ available: false, unreadable: true, reason }`. A failed block carries no figures, never zeros, and the others still answer. The log holds the block's name only.

**An open programme** is one whose status is `open` or `testing`: it takes applications, or its testers are at work. That is the set TST1's places bars draw (D1195's `LIVE`). The reply says so in `open_means`.

### 2. The blocks

| Block | What it counts |
|---|---|
| `weekly` | Twelve Monday weeks (UTC), the current one `to_date`. Each week counts `testing_events` rows with action `applied` and `picked`, by the day they were written. |
| `by_profile.pool` | Active testers (`product_testers.status = 'active'`) on active accounts, by account role, with `total` |
| `by_profile.applicants` | Members with a non-withdrawn application on an open programme, each once however many programmes, by account role, with `total` |
| `withdrawn` | Withdrawn applications on open programmes: `total` and per programme (`uid`, `product_name`, `status`, `withdrawn`) |
| `mail_reach` | Per open programme: `picked` → `consented` → `emails_on` → `reachable`, counts only |

**The weekly block's two kinds of empty week:**
- **Not recorded:** a week that ends before the first programme opened. The first opening is the earliest `opened` event in `testing_events`. Such a week is `recorded: false` with `applications` and `picks` null, because nothing could be applied to then.
- **A measured 0:** a quiet week after the first opening.

If no programme ever opened, every week is not recorded. `first_opened` gives the day.

**Profiles:** founder, investor, advisor, partner and exploring. Any other role counts as `other`, which is how an admin who joined the pool shows. Admins cannot apply.

**Mail reach is `testerRecipients`' rule, step by step.** Each step narrows the one before it:
1. picked;
2. consented: agreed to programme email;
3. emails on: the programme's `mail_enabled`;
4. reachable: an active account with an address, not in `email_suppressions` and not unsubscribed from the newsletter.

The last step is exactly the WHERE that `mailLinkTesters.testerRecipients` sends to. The test checks the two agree, programme by programme, on the same rows. Delivery figures stay in Axal VC Mail.

### 4. Tests

**`cloudflare-worker/test/testing_overview_d1196.test.ts`** has 4 cases. They run on node:sqlite with the baseline's tables and migrations 515, 516, 517 and 576 as they ship, and every figure is counted by hand.
- **Weekly:**
  - the twelve Monday weeks;
  - two weeks before the first opening not recorded;
  - quiet weeks after it a measured 0;
  - the current week to date;
  - an application before the window and a withdrawal counted nowhere;
  - no opening at all leaves every week not recorded.
- **By profile and withdrawn:**
  - the pool leaves out a closed account and a paused tester, and counts an admin as other;
  - the applicants count a member on two open programmes once, and leave out a closed programme and the withdrawn;
  - withdrawn per open programme.
- **Mail reach:**
  - each step on a fixture with no consent, emails off, a closed account, a suppressed address and an unsubscribe;
  - `reachable` equals `testerRecipients`' count for each programme.
- **The route:**
  - a plain Admin is 403;
  - the Super Admin's reply carries no `@` and no name;
  - dropping the pool's table fails only the pool;
  - dropping the history fails only weekly.

**15 of 15 mutations killed.** The first try, dropping the SQL action filter, was equivalent: the sum filters by action too. It was replaced by counting picks from applications, which is caught.
