### What the canvas decides

- Compare is its own page, linked from SK-10's new "Based on Axal … · Compare with Axal" line.
- With an update it shows three columns: Axal's base version, your version, Axal's current version. Up to date shows two.
- Conflicting parts come first. Every marker is a word and a glyph (Conflict, Changed by you, Changed by Axal, Same as Axal), and added and removed lines carry + and −.
- The one primary action is "Extend Axal vN again". It is disabled with its reason when Axal's newest version is suspended. A retired base shows no update.
- Draft edits are not in the comparison, and the page says so.
- When Axal's version cannot be read, there is no comparison and never "up to date"; the reason, your version and history still show.
- Members and HQ admins see it read-only. HQ is never a writer.
- Eadwyn proposes a version summary and a way forward per conflict. It never proposes a tool outside the base.
