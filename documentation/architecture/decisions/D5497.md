## D5497 — Keep whole major units in the cap-table model (#5497)

The owner selected option A. Saved model amounts use whole US dollars.
The holder writer refuses a newly entered amount or cap that includes a
fractional dollar. D966's historical matcher still reads the fractional
dollars an earlier writer could save.

Recorded instruments carry minor units and their own currency. The Today
model, instrument explanation, and access projection convert at their
read boundaries using that currency. The access projection continues to
expose numeric major units, including existing fractional amounts. Native
legacy issuances without a code keep the USD interpretation. Linked SAFEs
need their own currency.

Importing a saved USD model into the record can propose instruments only
when the record uses USD. Founding share proposals have no currency.
The five cap-table paths leave the D5373 pending list. This change adds
no migration and requires no backfill.
