### 1. HQ's inbox read

D1038 part 7 builds the Inbox table, its filters, the band chart and `/admin/approvals/[id]` on D1040's `GET /api/admin/approvals`. That read had none of these. They are added in `services/approvalInbox.ts` and `approvalAssignments.ts`, and the route is `routes/admin_approvals.ts`.

**Query parameters**, beside `?page=`:

| Parameter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `q` | Search on who, what and the lane's name, from two characters. One character does not filter, as the page's search box does. |
| `lane` | A lane the reader sees. An Admin who names an HQ lane gets 400, not an empty answer. |
| `group` | The canvas's chips: `people`, `programs`, `community`, `deals`, and `hq` for a Super Admin's lanes. |
| `age` | `ok`, `due_soon`, `past` or `unknown`. |
| `assigned` | `me`, `none`, `any` or one admin's id. |
| `sort` | `oldest` (the default), `newest`, `lane` or `who`. An unknown age sorts last in every order, and within a lane or a person the oldest still comes first. |

**How the query runs.**
- **In memory only.** It runs on the rows the board already read. Nothing the caller sends reaches SQL, so there is no new statement.
- **A bad value** gets 400 `bad_filter`.
- **An `assigned` filter when the assignment record cannot be read** gets 503 `assigned_unreadable`. Answering it would read every item as unassigned.

**The total.**
- `total` counts the rows that match.
- `unfiltered_total` keeps the whole inbox.
- When a lane is cut at the cap, a narrowed total is withheld with its reason rather than shown short.
- The tiles still cover the whole inbox.

**Each lane** now carries its `group` and `bands` (`ok`, `due_soon`, `past` and `unknown` counts) for APR1's chart. A lane that failed, or was cut at the cap, has `bands: null` and a reason.

**`LANE_GROUP`** is the page model's grouping. `approvals_gaps_d1132.test.ts` holds the two equal.

**One item: `GET /api/admin/approvals/items/:lane/:id`.** It returns:
- the row as the inbox shows it;
- its assignee;
- its lane: group, who a decision tells, the page that decides it, and whether it is HQ's;
- its assignment history, with `decides: false`.

It refuses in three ways:
- An item that is no longer waiting gets 404 `item_not_waiting`.
- A lane the reader cannot see, or a malformed id, gets 404 `item_not_found`. That tells an Admin nothing about what HQ holds.
- A lane that cannot be read gets 503 `lane_unreadable`, so an outage never reads as "gone".

**Client.** `adminApprovalsApi.inbox(page, filters)` and `adminApprovalsApi.item(lane, id)`.

### 3. A removed tester's places

APR5c's dialog and the member's message both say removal ends their open program places. It did not.

**What a removal now does.** `applyTransition` adds two statements to the removal's own batch. Each runs only once the tester row carries this removal's stamp:
1. one `testing_events` row per open place: `place_ended`, the acting admin, and `{ reason: 'tester_removed', tester_id }`;
2. each open place becomes `withdrawn`, the only ended state migration 516 allows.

**What counts as an open place.** An `applied` or `picked` application in a programme that is not `closed` or `rejected`. A closed programme's picks are its record and stay as they were.

**What does not end places:**
- A removal that loses a race (`tester_changed`) ends nothing.
- A pause ends nothing: its message says the programs carry on.

The route answers `places_ended`.

### 4. The co-marketing item

- **`GET /api/comarketing/admin/pitches/:uid`** (`requireAdmin`) reads one pitch as the queue shows it, with its partner, its attribution and `decided`: status, who decided (`{ id, name }`) and when. `decided` is null while the pitch is proposed.
- **A repeat or illegal decision** now answers 409 `pitch_decided`. It carries the status and `decided`, and a sentence such as "Bea Admin approved it on 2026-10-20". It used to answer a bare `{ detail }`.
- **The decision's UPDATE** now holds only while the pitch is still in the status it read. If a second admin decides in between, the first gets that 409 naming the second, and the second's decision stands.
- **Client:** `api.adminCoMarketingPitch(uid)`.
