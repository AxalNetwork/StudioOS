### Built

- **`form-options` sends each option's `direction`** (with `strength` and `kind`) — `services/records/verdict.ts`; test 14 of `verdict_d693.test.ts`.
- **The evidence read (V17, V18).** #5130's shell-wide rail task system is not on `main` (only references to it), so the read is a Worker endpoint the page's task card calls: `POST /api/cycles/validate/decision-summary` and `/quote` (`routes/verdict_summary.ts`, `services/verdictSummary.ts`). The server counts the checked rows with D4586's rule (`offeredCoverage` in `services/verdict.ts`, the same count a decision uses) and sorts them into Contradicts, Supports and Checked but not counted; the model only writes one line per row against a token (`E1`…) the server gave it. A line citing no offered row is dropped, and a missing line or one that reads as a choice ("proceed", "pivot", "stop", "recommend", "should"…) falls back to the row's own label. What would change it is the hypothesis's own disproof, success bar and deadline. Founder in person only (no admin, no support session); nothing stored. Run under the page `Founder · Validate · Verdict`, option `summary:verdict-evidence` (not registered in `railMatrix.json`, so the page's own job runs), surface `/validate/verdict`. Tests: `verdict_summary_6862.test.ts`.
- **Decision shares (V19, V20).** `validation_decision_shares`; candidates, list, grant and revoke in `services/verdict.ts`; routes in `routes/verdict_shares.ts`. Candidates are the venture's active Operating Partners (accepted `project_members` row and the partner licence) and investors (a deal-room seat on one of its deals or an introduction to it, and the investor licence), never the venture's own. Only the founder in person grants and revokes. A Withheld decision is refused at the grant (409 `withheld_not_shareable`) and again at the read. The recipient's read needs an unrevoked share of that one decision, a given decision, and the recipient still a candidate; every refusal is the same 403 `not_shared`, so it tells nobody which decisions exist. A superseded decision answers its `superseded_at` day and nothing about the later decision. Grants, revokes and each recipient's first open are logged under `validate.verdict` on the decision. Tests: `verdict_shares_6862.test.ts`, each refusal named.
- **Frontend.** `VerdictEvidencePanel.jsx` (the E-numbered table with Strength, Date, Direction and Toward the bar; the coverage sentence; the task card in its seven states with Use as reasoning, Copy and Discard), mounted in Record a decision through `formAssistants.js`; `DecisionShares.jsx` under the record's Memo section through `recordSectionExtras.js` (Share with…, the sheet, Shared with, Revoke, Copy link, Withheld and Unavailable states); `SharedDecisionPage.jsx` at `/shared/decisions/:id` (V20 A–D). Pure rules in `verdictEvidenceModel.js`; test `frontend/test/verdict_evidence_6862.test.mjs`.
