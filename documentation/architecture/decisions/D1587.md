## D1587 — The investor-side deck view, read-only on the deal page (deck v2 F8, part B)

- **Status:** part B of #3834, built by S09. **Owner decision R, 2026-11-01:** B, then C. B is read-only on the deal page. C (notes, the investor's own rubric, partner sharing) follows once B ships. HQ absorbs the cost of `investor_summarise`, metered under HQ in the consumption rail, never charged to the founder.
- **Builds on:**
  - D902, what a deck link includes (`services/decks/shareInclude.ts`);
  - D898, the public deck fields (`publicDeckOf`);
  - D1150, the investor's own deal access (`deal_context.ts` `context()`).
- **Migration:** `745_deck_grants.sql`.

### The question

An investor on a deal had no way to open the venture's deck on their own deal page. Deck versions reached outsiders only through share-token links. `deal_context.ts`' "What's new" said so: "this read has no caller-specific deck grant".

### What was decided

**A grant is the founder's choice of one frozen version for one investor on one deal** (`deck_grants`).
- There is one live grant per deal, investor and firm (`company_id`, taken from the investor's invitation or commitment on the deal).
- A new grant replaces the live one. Withdrawing sets `revoked_at`, and no row is deleted.
- `include_json` is what the founder ticked: the four switches of D902 (figures, sources, notes, bands).

**The investor's read, `GET /api/deals/:id/deck`.**
- Access is D1150's `context()`: an investor with an invitation or a commitment on the deal, for the firm they act for. A grant must match that deal, that investor and that firm exactly. A support session is refused.
- **What it answers:**
  - `granted: false`, with a sentence, when there is no grant or the granted version is gone;
  - otherwise the granted version: its title, theme, frozen version and date, and its slides.
- **The cards go through the share link's rules:**
  - `publicDeckOf` drops the audience, round, author, content hash and speaker notes;
  - `shareSlides` with the founder's ticks never sends an internal score, a bank field or a quote without consent, and it removes a field the founder did not tick rather than blanking it. So nothing unticked reaches the investor, not even a "Not recorded".
- **No provenance (pd24):** every `binding` and provenance key is cut at any depth. Badges show only in the founder's editor.

**The founder's grants, `/api/deals/:id/deck-grants`.**
- Only the venture's founder, through `canAccessFounderResource` (an admin passes, a support session does not).
- **The routes:**
  - `GET` lists the deal's grants (investor name, firm, version, ticks, granted and withdrawn dates) and the investors on the deal who may receive one;
  - `POST` takes `{ deck_id, investor_user_id, include? }`. The version must be this venture's, and the investor must be on the deal;
  - `DELETE …/:uid` withdraws a grant.
- Grants and withdrawals are written to `activity_logs`.
- **The founder never sees an investor's notes, scores, weightings or decisions.** No founder answer here reads `deal_notes`, IC decisions or venture-score weights, and the founder is refused on the investor's reads.

**What's new** counts the live grant as the deal's deck source. Its "unavailable" deck line appears only while there is no grant.

### Not yet, and why

- **The deal-page panel:**
  - the final canvas draws no investor-side view (dk-boards §4);
  - the client `deckGrantsApi` (`frontend/src/lib/api/deckGrants.js`) is in place;
  - the panel, and the founder's grant control, wait on a design, or on the owner confirming a plain read-only render.
- **Per-investor links and versions (#3812) and per-slide consent (#3820):** neither is on main. When they land, a grant's version and the slides an investor sees follow them. This read records no view and no time per slide.
- **`investor_summarise`:** its model comes from the per-job table (#3934, the model use-case plan's task 0) through `catalogue.ts`, metered under HQ. It is built after #3934.
- **C:** notes, the investor's rubric weights (`venture_scores.ts` `/weights` already holds an investor's own weighting) and "Share with a partner". It follows B.
