### Where part 1 departs from the canvas

- **No Eadwyn prompt buttons.** The canvas's suggestions ("Help me define our services", "What needs my attention today?") are questions put to Eadwyn. The rail's page-configurable block is `steps`, which are links to where the work is. The rail keeps its Ask.
- **The rail's sentence is not the canvas's.** The canvas says Eadwyn "can explain your pipeline and draft work for review". Pipeline drafts nothing today: the rail's own unavailable list says no proposal is drafted. So the step block says "Nothing in Pipeline is sent or changed without your click."
- **"No reply recorded", not "Proposal unanswered".** Nothing records a reply other than a decision or a negotiation, so the card says what the record shows.
- **The zone pills keep the shell's `ZoneNav`, without counts.** The lifecycle strip carries the counts instead.
- **Attention reads the first page of each zone,** as the zones do. The strip's counts are the Worker's whole-book totals.
- **"Draft proposal" is not built.** The owner removed bidding from Leads (#2338), and no store holds a proposal draft for a lead, so it waits for a decision (part 2).
- **#4685 (editors) is already merged** (`usePipelineZone`). Part 1 opens no editor. **#4693 (wording)** stays with S20; part 1 reuses its fields (`open`, `label`, `current_monthly_cents`, one win rate). **#4705 (accessibility and phone)** covers the zones' controls; part 1 covers only the root's.

### Built, Leads (2026-11-14, S23, #5935): boards PP03, PP03b and PP03m

`/pipeline/leads` is redrawn to the Pipeline v2 canvas. The rules are in `pages/partner/pipeline/leadsModel.js`, so the page and `frontend/test/pipeline_leads_pp03_5935.test.mjs` share them. It reads `GET /partner/pipeline/leads` and writes `POST /leads/:needId/pass` as they are; no Worker change.

- **Toolbar.** The views are the canvas's: All, Strong fit, Unscored, Excluded, Recent. Recent means posted in the last 7 days, newest first. The p1 row's Passed view left, because the passes are their own table below the list on every view. Warm intros left too: it had always been prose. The header action is **Edit capability rules**, which links to Offers › Audience fit, where the fit rules are edited.
- **Strip.** The tiles are Open leads, Strong fit, Unscored and Passed this period. Every figure prints beside its population, and a zero over an empty population is words ("None open", "No open leads", "None yet").
  - Strong fit is Not recorded when nothing can be scored (`strong_fit_count: null`).
  - The Worker sends no unscored count, so Unscored is counted over the loaded rows. It reads Not recorded unless every open lead loaded.
  - The period is the calendar month (UTC), since its 1st, counted from `passed_at`. It reads Not recorded unless every pass loaded.
- **Cards** follow the canvas's order: name, provenance, the fit explanation, budget, need, action.
  - The fit explanation puts the met and missed rules first (✓/✕), then the fit line ("Strong fit · 86", "Partial fit · 61").
  - An excluded lead reads "Excluded · no score" with the firm's own sentence. An unscored lead reads "Unscored" with the Worker's `score_note`. Neither shows a number.
  - **Why this score?** opens every receipt, including the gaps a scored lead was not measured on, and the sum ("1 of 2 rules measured are met").
- **Pass** opens a dialog (PP03b): "Pass on {name}?", a required reason as a radio group, and an optional note. A failed write keeps the dialog, the reason and the note. The dialog closes only once the server took the pass.
- **Phone (PP03m).** Cards stack, the fit reasons stay visible, and the dialog's and card's actions are full width and at least 44px tall.

**The pass reasons match.** The brief's six (Below floor, No capability, Scope mismatch, Timing, Price, Other) are the Worker's `PASS_REASONS` (`below_floor`, `no_capability`, `scope_mismatch`, `timing`, `price`, `other`), in the same order. There is nothing to reconcile, and the test pins both lists.
