### Built, part 1 (2026-11-09, S09): the root is a setup path or a command centre

The issue's own order puts the first-run state and zero semantics first, then the populated root and the rail. This part builds those. The issue stays open for the zones (Leads, Proposals, Negotiations, Retainers, Analytics), the shared opportunity sheet and the remaining required states.

- **`/pipeline` renders `PipelineCommandCentre`** in place of the five-section board. The rules are in `pages/partner/pipeline/commandCentre.js`, so the page and its tests share them. The board stays registered (`boards/partnerPipeline.js`) for the crumb anchors and its own tests; no route is added.
- **The reads are the zones' own:** `/partner/pipeline/{leads,proposals,negotiations,retainers}`, `/quotes/analytics`, `/partner/offers/fit-rules` and `/services/offerings?mine=1`. Each settles on its own. A failed read is Unreadable where it would have been drawn, and "no partner profile" is said once.
- **First run (PP01, PP01m)** is when leads report nothing to score against (`scoring: 'none'`) and no quote, tracked negotiation or retainer has ever existed. Every one of those reads must have succeeded: an unreadable read is not an empty pipeline.
  - The shell title becomes "Set up your pipeline". The card carries the canvas's supporting copy and five steps derived from the record, a lifecycle preview and Explore demand.
  - **Add services** links to Offers › Catalog and **Set fit rules** to Offers › Audience fit, the editors that exist. Nothing is edited here.
  - "Add services" is done when the caller's catalogue has an active offering, or when leads are scored with no active fit rule. It is not done only when leads report nothing to score against. Otherwise it reads "Not confirmed", because `?mine=1` lists the caller's offerings while scoring reads every member's.
  - "How lead matching works" discloses the Worker's own `scoring_note`.
  - No outcome metric is drawn on first run.
- **Zero needs a population.** Open pipeline with no open quote is "No open quotes"; win rate and median cycle with nothing decided are "No decided quotes"; the weighted pipeline with nothing open is "Nothing open to forecast". A real zero over a real population still prints (three decided, all lost, is "0%" with "0 of 3 decided"). Each metric has its basis line and a How calculated disclosure carrying the Worker's own basis where it sends one. The same rule fixed three zone tiles: Analytics' weighted forecast (the Worker always sends a forecast object, so it printed "$0 open"), Proposals' "$0 in play" and Negotiations' "$0 carried".
- **The populated root (PP02, PP02m)** follows the canvas's order: Needs attention, four metrics, the lifecycle strip, Recent movement, Risks, Explore demand.
  - **Attention** is facts on recorded fields, most urgent first: a negotiation whose ball is ours, a retainer over its retained hours, a renewal within 30 days, a quote with no recorded reply after 7 days (not one in open terms), a retainer under 50% use, and a strong-fit lead (80+, not excluded). Ended retainers ask for nothing.
  - **Recent movement reads Not recorded.** No lifecycle feed exists, which is backend-required item 3.
  - **Phone** gives a 2×2 metric grid, a lifecycle row that scrolls sideways, full-width 44px actions, and attention cards stacked.
- **The Eadwyn rail** gets the page's next steps (`steps`, D4873) in place of the model list: setup links on first run, the top attention items when populated. Model, usage and top-up go behind Account › AI tokens (`usageAt`, D4811).

### Where part 1 departs from the canvas

- **No Eadwyn prompt buttons.** The canvas's suggestions ("Help me define our services", "What needs my attention today?") are questions put to Eadwyn. The rail's page-configurable block is `steps`, which are links to where the work is. The rail keeps its Ask.
- **The rail's sentence is not the canvas's.** The canvas says Eadwyn "can explain your pipeline and draft work for review". Pipeline drafts nothing today: the rail's own unavailable list says no proposal is drafted. So the step block says "Nothing in Pipeline is sent or changed without your click."
- **"No reply recorded", not "Proposal unanswered".** Nothing records a reply other than a decision or a negotiation, so the card says what the record shows.
- **The zone pills keep the shell's `ZoneNav`, without counts.** The lifecycle strip carries the counts instead.
- **Attention reads the first page of each zone,** as the zones do. The strip's counts are the Worker's whole-book totals.
- **"Draft proposal" is not built.** The owner removed bidding from Leads (#2338), and no store holds a proposal draft for a lead, so it waits for a decision (part 2).
- **#4685 (editors) is already merged** (`usePipelineZone`). Part 1 opens no editor. **#4693 (wording)** stays with S20; part 1 reuses its fields (`open`, `label`, `current_monthly_cents`, one win rate). **#4705 (accessibility and phone)** covers the zones' controls; part 1 covers only the root's.
