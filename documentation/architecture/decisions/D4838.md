### Built, PP04 (2026-11-14, #5937): Proposals is a follow-up list

`/pipeline/proposals` is redrawn to PP04 and PP04m. Frontend only: it reads `GET /partner/pipeline/proposals` and writes `POST /proposals/:quoteId/versions`, `PUT /proposals/:quoteId/outcome` and the quote withdraw as before. The rules are in `pages/partner/pipeline/proposalsFollowUp.js`.

- **The list has five columns:** Client, Value, State, Last activity and Next action. The version number is not a column. It is under Versions in the row's sheet.
  - **Last activity** is the newest thing the proposal's own record holds: its decision (`decided_at`), its latest version (`created_at`), or the send. The read gives only the send's age (`days_since_sent`), so the send reads "Sent 3 days ago". A row with none of these reads Not recorded.
  - **State** is the read's own word. A sent proposal adds "no decision recorded". A lost one adds its reason, or a "No reason recorded" mark and a tint, as before.
  - **Next action** depends on the state. A sent proposal gets Follow up, which opens the sheet's Overview: nothing here sends mail, and the sheet says so. A lost proposal with no reason gets "Record why it was lost", and one with a reason gets Review; both open Outcome. A won proposal with an engagement links to Delivery. A withdrawn proposal reads "Nothing to do".
- **Opened reads Not recorded.** The Worker sends `read_receipts: 'none'`, and its `read_receipts_note` is printed under the strip after "Opens aren't recorded." No "Opened 4×" and no "Unopened" count is drawn anywhere.
- **The strip uses only figures the read supports.**
  - Open proposals and their value are the Worker's whole-book counts. With none live the value reads "nothing in play", as before.
  - Opened is Not recorded.
  - "No decision · 7+ days" counts sent, undecided proposals over the rows read. It is a floor ("n+") when a later page did not load.
  - Win rate is `commandCentre.winRateMetric`, so the root and this zone print one figure: the rate with "won of decided", or "No decided quotes" when nothing is decided.
- **Bulk nudge and the CSV export are under More.** The zone's action row is unchanged and drawn as a menu. The export runs as before. The nudge stays disabled, and its reason is printed in the menu. Ask sits beside More, where the shared action row put it.
- **Phone (PP04m):** one card per proposal with the client, value, state, last activity and the next action as a full-width 44px button. The table is hidden below `md`.
- **The row opens a sheet.** No shared opportunity sheet (PP08) exists yet, so the zone draws its own. Its tabs are the issue's, as the row's data allows:
  - Overview, Versions and Outcome always.
  - Proposal when the quote has a price or a timeline.
  - Activity when the record holds an event.
  - Versions shows the trail. Its "Record a version" button aims the version form at that proposal. Outcome holds Withdraw (submitted only, as `needs.ts` allows) and the loss-reason picker.
  - When PP08 lands, the row should hand it the same row and tab.
- **Kept as they were:** the chip row, the version form with its per-proposal drafts (#4685), the loss-reason chart with unstated losses counted apart, the win/loss draft band and the stated limits.

### Where PP04 departs from the canvas

- **"No decision" rather than "no reply".** The canvas draws "Sent · no reply" and "No reply > 5 days". The quote records only a decision, and this zone does not read negotiations, so a reply short of a decision is not known here. The tile also uses the command centre's 7 days (`NO_REPLY_DAYS`) rather than the canvas's 5, so the root's attention card and this tile agree on what "quiet" means.
- **There is no Draft state and no "Finish and send" action.** No store holds a proposal draft (part 1, "Draft proposal" is not built).
- **"Open Delivery", not "Open engagement".** The proposals read returns `engagement_id` but not the engagement's `uid`, and Delivery's `?engagement=` matches the uid when one exists. A link that silently focuses nothing would be worse than a link to the board.
- **The sheet is zone-local, with no stage tracker.** The cross-lifecycle identity and its activity feed are backend-required item 3. The Activity tab holds only this quote's own stamps.
- **The version form stays below the list,** with its Proposal picker, instead of moving into the sheet. #4685's tests drive it there. The sheet's Versions tab aims it rather than holding a second copy.
