### The assessment fallback is retired

Decision a (`PROFILING_V2.md` §2) kept the gamified assessment as a read-only
fallback until "Session 15 … the card page stops reading it". It now has:
- `ArchetypeCardPage` no longer calls `GET /assessment/results/me`.
- The archetype is `cardArchetype(GET /best-fit/me)`.
- The locked state (D594) is read from that one reply.

A person with no conversational signal sees the existing empty state ("Answer
a few archetype questions with Eadwyn …"), never an assessment result standing
in for it. The `/studio` preview card (`ProfileFitSection`) is not this page
and is not changed here.

### Published-member matches: `GET /api/profile/published-members`

Decision c: **archetype types always, plus real members only if they
published their archetype.** The new route takes `archetypes=slug,slug`. The
page sends the slugs its copy's "Who you match with" names (`matchSlugs`).

**Who is listed.** Every condition must hold:
- `profile_archetype_publish.published = 1`;
- the account is active;
- the member is not the caller;
- the archetype that member's *latest* snapshot, per persona, displays is one
  of those asked for. An archetype a member used to display is not listed.

**What a row carries:** `user_id`, `name`, `role`, `persona`, `slug` and
`label`, and nothing else. No trait vector, confidence, history, skill or
value leaves the route (§7.9). A member is identified by name and role
because publishing is that member's consent to be seen with the archetype,
and a match nobody can identify is not a match. The email is never sent.

**Guards:**
- The list is 1 to 10 known slugs (the engine's `ARCHETYPES`), checked
  before any read. Anything else is `400 invalid_archetypes`.
- The slugs reach SQL as one bound JSON array.
- A caller whose own archetype is locked (D594) is answered `locked: true`
  with the plan and no members, because the matches are part of the card.
- At most 50 rows, with `more: true` when there are others.
- A failed read is `503 published_members_unreadable`, never an empty list.

`matchGroups` puts the members under the match they answer, in the copy's
order. A match nobody has published keeps its type and reason, with no
members.

### The radar and the timeline (data)

- **Radar** (`radarLayers`, from `GET /skills/me/evidence`, D318). For each
  of the eight axes it keeps the self layer (0–5, or null), the evidence
  layer (weight, or null), the lifetime count and the §5.2 state. Nothing
  recorded stays null and is drawn as Not recorded, never as 0. An
  `evidence_only` axis shows its count, not a level.
- **Timeline** (`archetypeTimeline`, from `GET /profile/history`, D357). One
  point per change of the *displayed* archetype, oldest first, from the
  first classification, per persona. Snapshots that moved only traits,
  skills or values are not points (§7.7).
