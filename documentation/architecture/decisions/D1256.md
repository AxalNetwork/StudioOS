### 1. The routes (`routes/venture_scores.ts`, mounted at `/api/venture-scores`)

| Route | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /models` | any signed-in member | Model v1 as data: the eleven scopes, their inputs and weights, the floor, cut-offs and bands, and the versions stored so far. |
| `GET`, `PUT /weights` | investor, Operating Partner, Admin | "Your weighting": eleven whole numbers 0–5 by scope key, or `null` for equal. It changes the caller's sort only. |
| `POST /batch` | investor, Operating Partner, Admin | The deal list's strip: up to 200 deal ids. |
| `GET /fairness` | Super Admin | The latest quarterly fairness check. |
| `GET /deal/:dealId` | investor on the deal; staff, founder or shared advisor as below | An investor's read, by the visibility rule, logged. |
| `GET /:projectId` | founder, Operating Partner, Admin, shared advisor | The full read. |
| `POST /:projectId/recompute` | founder, Operating Partner, Admin | Re-score (trigger `rescore`). |
| `PUT /:projectId/scopes/:scope/review` | Operating Partner, Admin | A note, or an adjustment within 15 points with a reason. |
| `POST /:projectId/corrections` | the venture's founders | Ask to correct one input. |
| `GET`, `PUT /:projectId/sharing` | the venture's founders | The investors' switch and the advisors shared with. |

**What the batch returns:**
- for each deal the caller may read: each scope's state, band and confidence word, never a number;
- a locked row when the founder's switch is off and the caller is an investor;
- a count of the ids the caller may not read, which are left out.

**What the full read returns:**
- **For a founder or staff:** a recompute on read (trigger `read`), each scope's latest review, and the corrections. A founder also gets their sharing, and "What investors see" as the investor's view before and after an NDA.
- **For a shared advisor:** the stored scorecard, read-only.

`POST /models/:id/publish` is not built: model v1 is code, frozen per scope on its first stored row (D1255). Publishing a later version waits for HQ's page (part 6).

### 2. Who sees what (`services/ventureScoring/access.ts`)

- **Founder:** the venture's founder (`users.founder_id = projects.founder_id`) and its accepted owners and co-founders, in full. Their own reads are not logged.
  - An advisor on the roster is not a founder: `canAccessProject` would let them in, so the scorecard has its own check.
- **Operating Partner (`partner`) and Admin:** in full, with reviews. This matches the deal page's rule, which lets staff read every deal. Every read is logged.
- **Advisor:** read-only, only when the founder has shared with them by name (owner question 10 A). Logged.
- **Investor:** never by venture id. They read through a deal only.
  - **Off the deal:** nothing. "On the deal" is invited to it or committed on it, the deal page's one rule.
  - **The founder's switch:** if it is off, the investor gets a locked answer with its reason.
  - **Before an NDA:** each scope's state, band and confidence word, and any review in force. Nothing else.
  - **After an NDA:** values, coverage and inputs too. The NDA must be an active pairwise NDA with a founder of the venture that has not expired; a pending or expired one is not an NDA.
  - **Personal inputs** (D1255's `personal`, the team-only ones) stay "Counted, not shown": a band, never their figure, words or grade.
  - Every read is logged, a locked one included.
- **Explorer, and anyone else:** nothing.

**The founder's switch (owner question 2 A):**
- With no sharing row, it is on once the venture is listed (it has a deal), and off before.
- The founder can turn it off or on. Each change is audited.
- Staff are not held by it.

**A read log** is one `activity_logs` row (action `venture_score_read`, entity `venture.scorecard`). It carries:
- the viewer kind, the scope count, the deal, and whether the read was masked or locked;
- never a value, band, input or name.

### 3. Reviews, corrections, weighting

- **Reviews (owner question 9 A, migration 626 `venture_score_reviews`):**
  - **Two kinds:** a note, or an adjustment of up to 15 points with a reason of at least 10 characters.
  - **Refusals:**
    - over the cap: 400 `adjustment_too_large`, never clamped, and nothing is written;
    - an adjustment to a scope that is not scored: 409 (a note is still allowed).
  - **What is stored with it:** the scorecard row it was made against and that row's computed value. The computed score is never overwritten.
  - **What readers see:** the latest review per scope, beside the computed value. It is "current" only while its row is still the latest; investors see a review only while it is current.
  - **Telling the founders:** every founder and co-founder is told in their inbox, with the scope, the points and the reason. A notice that fails never undoes the review.
  - **The audit** carries the scope and the kind.
- **Corrections (`venture_score_corrections`):**
  - The founder names one input of one scope by its model key, with what is wrong (10–2000 characters).
  - One correction per input may be open at a time.
  - The audit carries the scope and the input key.
  - Answering a correction is part 5's page; the table holds its decision.
- **Weighting (`investor_scope_weights`):** one row per person. It is all eleven keys or nothing, each a whole number 0–5. No row means equal. It never touches a score.

### 4. Sharing (`venture_score_sharing`)

The table has one row per (project, audience, user):
- `investors` with user 0 is the switch;
- `advisor` with a user is one advisor shared with.

Only an active advisor account can be shared with. A share that is removed is kept with `enabled = 0`, so its history stays.

### 5. The jobs (`services/ventureScoring/jobs.ts`, from the per-minute cron in `index.ts`)

- **After a connector sync, every 15 minutes:**
  - It recomputes, with trigger `sync`, any scored venture where someone on the venture has an active connector that synced after its latest row.
  - Each provider already stamps `integrations.last_synced_at` when its sync ends, and the sweep reads that stamp, so no provider's code changes and none is missed.
  - At most 20 ventures a sweep.
- **Nightly at 04:20 UTC:** the listed ventures and those already scored, the longest unscored first, at most 50 a night. A venture whose inputs did not change writes nothing (D1255's change-only rows).
- **Quarterly, at 04:30 UTC on 1 January, April, July and October:** the fairness check for the quarter just ended (bias guard 9).
  - **What it computes:** each scope's mean value and confidence, over the latest row of each venture, by stage, sector, solo or team, and connector use.
  - **Small cells:** a cell of fewer than five ventures is suppressed.
  - **Gaps:** a group whose mean differs from all ventures' by more than 15 points, or 0.2 confidence, is listed as a gap that opens a model review.
  - **One run per quarter:** a second run in the same quarter writes nothing.
  - **Studio:** "by studio" is one studio per deployment, since each branch scores its own ventures.

**The jobs carry no actor.** `scoreVenture` now takes `actorId: null` for `nightly` and `sync` only (D1255's `store.ts`):
- the rows' `computed_by` is NULL, and the audit names `system:nightly` or `system:sync`;
- a person's trigger with no actor, or a job's trigger with one, is refused;
- a job leaves Product-market fit to its own writer (`recomputePmf` needs a person) and answers its stored row.

`storedScorecard` now returns each scope's confidence word and row uid. With `{ inputs: true }` it adds the stored inputs, read from the signed row.

### 6. Rules kept

- **Scores are derived, never typed.** No route accepts a value, band, confidence or grade, and none sets a deal or venture status.
- **Eadwyn never writes a review, a weighting or a sharing choice.** The three tables join `venture_scorecards` in the never-fill registry's `score_rating` kind (`services/fills/neverFill.ts`).
- **Never an input.** No statement reads a name, email, photo, gender, age, nationality, school or employer, or `kyc_data`, and the test scans every one. The founder's sharing list returns the advisors' names only to the founder.
- **Every statement is bound.** They are static strings, scanned by the test. People and deal lists are bound as one JSON array.
- **Errors and logs:** a failed read is 503 `scorecard_unreadable` with our sentence, never the error's text. Logs carry counts and outcomes only.

### 7. Tests

**`cloudflare-worker/test/venture_scores_d1256.test.ts`** has 12 tests, over the full schema (the baseline and every migration, 626 included), real JWTs and D1255's synthetic seed:
- **Investors:**
  - an investor off the deal reads nothing;
  - on the deal, before an NDA (a pending or an expired one), bands and confidence only, logged with no score;
  - after an NDA, values and inputs, with personal inputs counted, not shown, and none of the seeded private values.
- **The switch:** on once listed; off locks investors and their strip; staff are not held; unlisted is off.
- **Readers by venture id:** founder and co-founder in full and unlogged; staff logged; a roster advisor refused; a shared advisor read-only and logged; an Explorer refused.
- **Reviews:**
  - over 15 refused with nothing written, and an adjustment with no reason refused;
  - the founder and investors may not review;
  - within the cap: the computed value kept, both founders told, the audit by kind, the investor seeing it beside the computed value;
  - a note on an unscored scope is allowed, an adjustment is not.
- **Ask to correct:** a valid ask; one open per input; a bad input, a bad scope or a short message refused; non-founders refused.
- **Weighting:** 0–5 by eleven keys only, reset to equal, founders refused.
- **The strip:** no number; unreadable deals left out and counted; founders refused; more than 200 ids refused.
- **Re-score and models:** Re-score for the founder, not an investor; models for any member.
- **The jobs:**
  - nightly with no actor, and a quiet second night writes nothing;
  - the sync sweep picks up a newer sync;
  - the context checks on the actor;
  - the fairness check: written once a quarter, small cells suppressed, a Super Admin's read only.
- **The fairness aggregate:** a cell of five shows its means; a group far from all opens a review.
- **Bias guards:** every new statement bound, with no never-input; the new tables are never-fill.
