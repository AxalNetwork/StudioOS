### What was built

- **The sheet.** Activate, reinstate, renew, suspend and terminate each open one sheet, built on `ui/Sheet`: a labelled dialog that takes focus, keeps Tab inside, closes on Escape, Cancel or ×, and returns focus to the control that opened it (More's "⋯", or the Activate button). Its four headings are the canvas's: 1 · What will happen, 2 · What else it affects, 3 · Reason recorded, 4 · Can it be undone? Only the confirm button carries the action's tone. Only Terminate is red. No browser prompt or confirm is used.
- **Terminate** says the licence ends, which countries are released for another licence, that its administrators lose their access (with the count from the Overview's read), and that each one is demoted, detached and deactivated, a Super Admin being skipped with the reason. It also says the branch is sent the terminated state and unbinds its administrators, that open compliance notices are withdrawn, and that agreements, fee invoices and history are kept (with the open invoices from the Overview's read). Last, it says the termination cannot be undone.
- **Suspend** says the licence still holds its countries. The rule sentence moved here from the reason dialog: "A suspended licence still holds its countries, so no other licence can be issued over them. Releasing them is a termination, not a suspension." The suspended banner on the licence now states only the fact (it still holds its countries). `LICENCE_REASONS` keeps demote, accept and reject, and no longer has `suspendLicence` or `terminateLicence`.
- **A read that has not answered or failed** is said as such ("Still reading…", "could not be read"), never counted as none.
- **More.** Today's four header buttons (Reinstate or Suspend, Renew, Terminate) are now items in the shared More menu (`MoreMenu`, SHL1). Activate is there too. Items follow the Worker's state machine (`services/licenceTransitions.ts` `FROM`): Activate for a draft or pending licence with no blockers, Reinstate for a suspended one, Suspend for an active one, and Renew and Terminate for anything not terminated. A terminated licence draws no More. The body's Activate button, beside the blockers list, uses the same rule. Before, it was drawn on a suspended licence too, which the Worker refuses.

### Departures

- **A reason where the write records one.** Suspend and terminate require a reason: the Worker refuses an empty note, and the confirm button stays disabled until one is typed. Activate, reinstate and renew take no note in the Worker. Their sheets say "No reason is recorded. History records who … and when." This is better than asking for a reason the write would throw away. A recorded reason for them needs a Worker change, which this task left out of scope ("writes as they are").
- **Renew** gets the sheet as well. It was one of the four header actions, and committing it from a menu item with no sheet would be the only unconfirmed action in More.
- **"⋯", not "More ▾".** The trigger is the shared More menu's "⋯" button, named More, so the licence header and every other admin header use one menu.
- **Record a request** is not added. HQ has no write for it, and D4819 part 3 places it in the studio's lane (`/licence/mine/requests`).
- **Mutation outcomes (L18)** are unchanged: the existing `OutcomeNotice` reports how each write ended. Extending the sheet to administrators, invoices, send, deploy and sync is the L18 issue.
