### 1. Three tables (migration 755)

| Table | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `posthog_tracked_metrics` | A saved insight the founder chose to track for one venture. It carries the role they gave it (active users, new users, activation, retention, conversion, sessions or other; never guessed from event names) and the module it shows in. One row per venture, insight and module. Its `uid` names the KPI `posthog-<uid>`. |
| `posthog_metric_facts` | Small aggregates a section sync reads: one row per fact and period. Kinds: metric, web overview, experiment result, flag state, survey stats. Each row has its `connector_fed_items` row. |
| `posthog_proposals` | What waits for the founder's Accept, as #2409 lists it: experiment evidence, survey signal, survey feedback, flag link and flag shipped. One row per kind and source. |

Each table goes with its connection (`integration_id` cascades). A record accepted into a module stays in that module's own store.

### 2. The shared service (`services/posthog/metrics.ts`)

- **`track` and `untrack`.** The role and module are checked against fixed lists before anything is written. Untracking removes the metric's facts with it.
- **`landFact`** writes one fact and its ledger row; a re-sync updates the same fact and period in place.
  - **Deleted stays deleted.** A fact the team deleted keeps its ledger row with no `local_id`, and `landFact` answers `kept_deleted` without writing.
- **`proposeOnce`** keeps a pending proposal fresh. A dismissed or accepted one answers `decided` and is never proposed again.
- **Accept and Dismiss:**
  - Accept runs the kind's acceptor (`registerAcceptor`). Parts 3 to 6 register theirs, each writing through its module's own writer. Until then Accept answers `not_ready` and writes nothing.
  - Dismiss marks the proposal decided.
- **`fromPosthog`** is a module's read: the link, its tracked metrics read, its other facts and its pending proposals. The connection's `uid` is returned only to the member it belongs to.
- **Never a 0.**
  - A tracked metric with no fact reads "Not recorded".
  - With its connection broken, gone or unlinked, it reads "Not recorded: PostHog is disconnected", even if facts are still stored.

### 3. The `posthog-<uid>` KPI family (`services/growKpis.ts`)

- **Keys.** `kpiKeys` lists the seven fixed KPIs, then the custom ones, then each tracked PostHog metric.
- **Ownership.** `isKpiKey` accepts a `posthog-<uid>` for its own venture only, so roadmap items, KPI alerts and metric evidence can name it.
- **Reads.** `kpiRows` reads each tracked metric from PostHog facts alone. The row carries the latest value, its change over 30 days, `source: 'posthog'` and the as-of date, or its reason when nothing was read.
- **No hand entry.** `enterKpi` refuses a hand entry for a `posthog-` key with "This figure comes from PostHog." A typed figure claiming the source `posthog` was already refused (D969).
- **Never written.** PostHog writes no row to `project_metrics` or `custom_kpi_values` (decision G).
- **Unreadable stores.** When the PostHog tables cannot be read, the KPI table still answers with its other rows. The failure is logged without a value, and no PostHog row is shown.

### 4. Connected and definition sources (`routes/progress.ts`)

- **`CONNECTED_METRIC_SOURCES`** gains `posthog`, so a typed snapshot cannot claim it. `google_workspace_gw6.test.ts` pins the list and is updated.
- **`DEFINITION_SOURCES`** gains `posthog`, so a founder can declare a metric as PostHog's. A disagreement then shows as a finding (migration 251).

### 5. Routes (`routes/posthog.ts`)

**The insight picker:**
- `GET /api/connectors/:uid/posthog-insights?project=&q=` lists the venture's linked project's saved insights (`insights-list`).
- It is for the connection's own member only, outside a support session, and needs a link through that connection.

**`posthogCycleRoutes`**, mounted on `/api/cycles` ahead of the cycle routers:

| Route | Who |
|---|---|
| `GET /:section/posthog?project=&module=` | The venture's team only (decision C, `isTeamViewer`). An admin, partner or another founder gets `team_only`. |
| `POST /:section/posthog/tracked` | The member whose PostHog serves the venture, on a venture they may write, outside a support session. The insight must be on their project's list. |
| `DELETE /:section/posthog/tracked/:uid` | Any team member who may write the venture. |
| `DELETE /:section/posthog/facts/:id` | Any team member who may write the venture. The fact stays deleted. |
| `POST /:section/posthog/proposals/:id/accept`, `/dismiss` | The member whose PostHog serves the venture. |

- **Modules.** A module must belong to its section (`POSTHOG_MODULES`).
- **Logging.** Every write logs ids only to `activity_logs`.
- **Syncs.** The sync routes are parts 3 to 6.

### 6. The SPA

- **Client.** `lib/api/posthog.js` gains `insights`, `fromPosthog`, `track`, `untrack`, `deleteFact`, `accept` and `dismiss`.
- **The part.** `pages/cycles/FromPosthog.jsx` is a module's "From PostHog", which parts 3 to 6 mount. `fromPosthogModel.js` holds its words and figures. It draws:
  - each tracked metric as a figure "From PostHog, as of <date>", with its 30-day change, or a dash with its reason;
  - Track a metric (PH3): pick a saved insight, then what it measures, in an in-page dialog. Nothing is sent before Track, and a refusal stays in the dialog;
  - Stop tracking and Delete, each asked first in a dialog that says what changes;
  - pending proposals with Accept and Dismiss;
  - Sync from PostHog, when a part passes `onSync`;
  - the PH8 states: loading, unreadable with Try again, not linked, disconnected, and nothing tracked yet.
- **Who acts.** Only the member whose PostHog serves the venture acts; the rest of the team reads.

### 7. Departures from the plan

- **Tracking is the connection's member, not any team member with write access.** Only that member can list the project's insights, and the route checks that the insight is on that list. Untracking stays open to any team member who may write.
- **Accept and Dismiss are generic here, with no acceptor yet.** Each part registers its kind's acceptor with its module writer.
- **`MODULE_LABELS` is unchanged.** `product_improvement` is a key in `POSTHOG_MODULES` only. Part 6 adds it to `MODULE_LABELS` as #3731 says.
