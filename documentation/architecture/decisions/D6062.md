## D6062 — Pitch Studio v2 PS-08: analytics with four kinds of nothing and one investor's journey (#6062)

Build 8 of 9 of D4839 (`design/incoming/Pitch Studio v2 Page.dc.html`, board PS-08: frames PS07, PS07B, PS07C). After D6061 (#6061, the Share slice, PR #7100). No Worker change and no migration: every figure comes from `deck_slide_analytics.ts` as it is (`GET /api/decks/:id/investor-links/:shareId/analytics` and `GET /api/decks/:id/analytics/daily`), the links read and the changes-since read the links table already makes.

### What is built

- **Four kinds of nothing, one vocabulary.** `slideAnalyticsModel.ABSENCE` holds one word and one sentence for each, and `AnalyticsFigure.jsx` draws each differently, with its word as text (no state relies on colour):
  - **Tracking off**: the link's Time per slide is off. The Worker stored visit counts only and answers `cards: null`, `read_seconds: null`. Its own chip (pause bars), "Turn it on for future visits; past visits stay untimed." No figure.
  - **Unavailable** (the unreadable state): the read failed. The platform's failed-read chip (D5129; the screen word is "Unavailable", owner's answer A to #5129's Question 2), "Views could not be read. Not zero: the read failed.", and Try again. No figure.
  - **Measured zero**: the count was made and it is 0: a link nobody opened, a period with no views, a timed link whose ask card never counted time ("Not yet"). The only state that shows a 0, in ink, with what was measured.
  - **Not recorded**: nothing stores the value: no reading time counted on the visits, a version with no card read as the ask, no timed card in the period, no link that sends an ask card and counts time per slide.
- **PS-08 A (overview).** `ShareBoard.jsx` gains three tiles over its chart from the daily read (`shareBoardModel.overviewFigures`): Visits with first views and revisits, the most-read card on the version shown with its counted seconds, and Reached the ask of the opened links. The period the read answered heads them. A counted day with no views draws a grey line: a measured zero, not missing data. Views per day, the funnels and drop-off are D4272's and unchanged.
- **PS-08 B (one investor).** `ViewerAnalyticsSheet.jsx` becomes the journey: Version and the link's expiry, Visits (revisits), Time on deck and Reached the ask ("Yes, by" the ask card's last counted heartbeat, which is all the store keeps); Time per card as horizontal bars, where "Not read" is never 0 s; the journey from Link created through opened, the NDA, the ask and each revisit to Link withdrawn; and, when the deck has a newer version than the link opens, "vN differs" with the changes-since line the links table already reads. Draft follow-up and Open in Capital relationships are #4272's and unchanged.
- **No live activity is inferred.** Every line is a counted visit with the time it was counted, and the journey says so: "It does not say anyone is reading now."

### Departures from the canvas

- **Reactions & feedback are left out.** The canvas's fourth overview tile and the journey's "Reaction or comment" row wait for proposal 9. D6064's `ShareFeedback.jsx` under the board is unchanged.
- The overview tiles cover the daily read's own period (30 days by default), not "Last 14 days", and do not name the read time.
- "N investors" under Visits is not drawn: the daily read counts views and first views, not readers.
- The most-read card shows its total counted seconds on the version, not an average: the read sends no per-reader count.
- The journey does not say "Read 9 cards · longest on Traction" per visit: the link read sends seconds per card over all visits, not per visit.
- "v7 differs" names the version and the changes-since line, not the changed card names; "Send v7 to Ines…" is not drawn (a new link is Share's, D6061).
- The journey opens from the Analytics cell of Share's Investor links table, as before; Share & analytics' "By investor" table does not open it (its rows come from another read).
- The version and NDA tile shows the link's version and expiry; the NDA date stays on the pill beside the name.
- The phone frame (PS08h) is #6063's: the sheet's tiles fold to two columns and the bars narrow their labels below 768px, without a 390 viewport test here.
