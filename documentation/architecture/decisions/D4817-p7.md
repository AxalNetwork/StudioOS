## D4817 part 7 — PF-07 money actions: Prepare → Review → Confirm → Receipt around the mark and distribution writes (#4817)

Part of #5311 (PF-07): the canvas's financial action boards (`design/incoming/Portfolio v2 Page.dc.html`, PF07a Prepare, PF07b Review, PF07c Conflict, PF07d Receipt, PF07e Save failed; the board list in `Portfolio v2 · Boards.dc.html`), with the addendum's validation and failed-write rules (PFA08 / PFA12: labelled fields, retained values, error association, retry, the original company kept). Built around the two writes that exist today. One Worker change: both routes now audit the act.

**The writes it wraps.** The only screen that records a mark or a distribution is the admin position sheet on the Cap Table tab (`frontend/src/pages/PortfolioPositionsPage.jsx`, mounted by `PortfolioWorkspace`), through `api.positionMarkCreate` and `api.positionDistributionCreate`, on `POST /api/positions/:projectUid/marks` and `POST /api/positions/:projectUid/distributions`. Both routes are `requireAdmin` (a frozen admin gets the 423, an admin without required MFA the MFA refusal); neither asks for step-up or a reason, and `request()` would carry a step-up if one were added. The page, the sheet, the routes, the admin gate and the histories it reads back are unchanged; the two inline forms are replaced by the flow.

**What is built.**
- `frontend/src/pages/portfolio/WriteFlow.jsx`: one write in four steps, inside the position sheet, with the company and its position ("Company · Northwind Labs · 1 round · invested $1,500,000") named in every step and the steps listed with the current one marked (`aria-current="step"`).
  - **Prepare**: each field has its label; an error is drawn under its field and tied to it (`aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`), with a count above. The rules are the Worker's (FMV ≥ 0, amount > 0, a real `YYYY-MM-DD` date, a basis or kind from its set) plus its length limits (event 120, source 200, note 2,000), which it would otherwise apply by cutting the text short. The mark gains the Source and Note the route already accepted. A draft holding anything asks "Discard this draft …?" before Cancel or closing the sheet discards it.
  - **Review**: exactly what will be written (the company, the store `portfolio_marks` / `portfolio_distributions`, "No fund named: recorded at firm level", each field, a blank one as Not recorded) and the effect on what the company read supplies, before → after: the carrying value (the latest mark, or cost when unmarked, said so) and MOIC for a mark; distributed and DPI for a distribution. A mark dated before the latest one joins the history and leaves the carrying value unchanged, and the row says so; one on the same date as the latest reads **Not determined**, because the Worker does not say which of two same-day marks is latest. MOIC or DPI without a recorded invested amount reads Not recorded with that reason. The book's NAV, TVPI and DPI read **Not projected**: the Worker recomputes the book on its next read and the page does not estimate it.
  - **Confirm**: the act on a button that names it ("Record $2,100,000 mark for Northwind Labs"), with what it means (an admin act, audited, every holder sees it on their next read).
  - **Receipt**: the stored row as the Worker returned it: its id (`uid`), each field, "Saved by the Worker at 14 Nov 2026, 14:44:09 UTC" from `created_at`, the audit action, and **Revision: Not recorded**, because neither table keeps one (a correction is a new record). The sheet's histories are read again behind it.
- **A failed save keeps every entry.** A refusal with a status shows the Worker's own sentence and "Nothing was recorded; your entries are kept", and the button becomes Try again. A 400 that names a field (`fmv is required…`, `basis must be…`) goes back to Prepare with the sentence on that field. No answer at all (a network failure) is **not** called "nothing recorded": the outcome is not known, and the next try re-reads first, so a write that did land shows as a change.
- **A conflict re-reads.** The routes carry no revision and no "what I read", so before sending, the flow reads the company again (`GET /api/positions/:projectUid`). If the history the write lands in moved since the draft was prepared (or the Worker answers 409, which it does not today), nothing is sent, the draft is kept, and the canvas's table shows "When you started / Now / Your draft", with Discard my draft and Review my draft against the new mark (Review is then measured against what is there now). This narrows the window; it does not close it, and the decision says so rather than claiming atomic conflict detection (#4642 holds that).
- `writeFlowModel.js` holds the pure parts: `WRITES` (fields, nouns, store, audit action), `draftOf`, `isDirty`, `parseMoney`, `isIsoDate`, `validate`, `payloadOf`, `fieldOfError`, `baseOf`, `changedSince`, `markAfter`, `reviewOf`, `actLabel`, `stamp`, `receiptOf`, `failureOf`. No clock: the page passes today's date for the draft's default. Formatting (`money`, `multiple`, `day`, `basisLabel`) and `NOT_RECORDED` (from `lib/absence`) are the shared ones.
- `writeFlow.css`: its own tokens on `.pfw` (the sheet is outside `.pf-shell`), redefined under `.dark`; one column, full-width actions and the effect table as labelled rows below 600px.

**The Worker change: an audit row for each act.** A mark or a distribution moves the figures every holder of the company reads, and is recorded by an admin, so it is a write on their behalf; before this part neither route recorded it anywhere but the row's `created_by`. Both now call `logAdminAction` after the insert (`activity_logs` and `admin_audit_log`), as `portfolio.mark_recorded` (project uid, mark uid, date, FMV, basis, fund) and `portfolio.distribution_recorded` (project uid, distribution uid, date, amount, kind, fund). A refused write is not audited. `logAdminAction` never throws, so the audit cannot undo the act. Test: `cloudflare-worker/test/positions_write_audit_d4817_p7.test.ts` (real schema, real route), three mutations listed in its header.

**Departures from the canvas, and why.**
- **The flow is drawn inside the position sheet**, not a separate 600px dialog over it: the addendum says confirmations are Sheet content, and a second dialog over a sheet would not work on a phone.
- **No portfolio NAV / TVPI / DPI before → after.** The canvas projects $11.1m → $10.8m; the company read does not supply the book, and an estimate here could disagree with `/api/positions/analytics`. Those rows read Not projected, with the reason.
- **The conflict is found by a read just before the write**, not by the write stating what it read (backend required, #4642). The "Now" column shows the mark and its date; who recorded it and when is not in the read, and the board says so instead of the canvas's "M. Okafor · 14:41".
- **The receipt's audit line names the action, not a link.** No screen reads one audit row by id; HQ's governance feed lists them.
- **Revision reads Not recorded**: marks and distributions are not revised.
- **"Record another"** is not offered: the receipt closes back to the company, whose sheet now shows the new row, and its Record a mark opens a fresh draft against the new history.

**Not changed.** The admin Record Round (`POST /api/positions`), the fund valuation write (`/api/fund-performance/positions/:id/valuations`), and the company Reports' Ask for this report and Waive this period (a later part rebuilds them).

**Tests.** `frontend/test/portfolio_write_flow_d4817.test.mjs` mounts the real `PortfolioPositionsPage` with the real `WriteFlow`, inside `AuthProvider`, against a stubbed `fetch`, in five tests, plus one model test: every step and the receipt from the stored row (the re-read before the POST, the exact body); labelled, associated validation and a Worker 400 back on its field; a failed distribution save keeping every entry, and no answer not called "nothing recorded"; a conflict sending nothing and re-reviewing against the new mark, a 409 re-reading, an earlier-dated mark leaving the carrying value; discard asking from Cancel and the sheet's close, and an investor seeing no write. Eleven mutations are listed in its header, each failing and then passing again when reverted. `investor_portfolio_alerts_chase` and `sheet_panels_d1317` pass unchanged.
