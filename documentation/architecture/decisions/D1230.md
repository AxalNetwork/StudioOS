### 1. Screening on submission

Before this part, `POST /api/trust/sanctions/screen/:user_id` ran only when an admin pressed it. Now `POST /api/kyc/submit` screens the person as they submit.

- **When it runs:** after the record is written, when the submission is `pending`.
- **What it screens:** the legal first and last name, the date of birth, and the nationality (or the country when no nationality is given), through the same `screenUser`.
- **The submission stands either way.** A screening that cannot finish never fails or delays the member's submission.
- **The member is not told** anything about the screening.
- **Every attempt is recorded** in `kyc_screening_attempts`:
  - `screened`, with its `sanctions_screenings` id;
  - or `unreadable`, with a code: `lists_unavailable` (a list was down or empty, #2444), `not_recorded` (the result row could not be written), or `error`.

  Without this table a failed run would leave no row at all, and the case could not tell "could not be read" from "never screened".
- **A match** sends the case to HQ on its own (reason `sanctions_match`), carrying the screening's id.
- **A self-disclosed PEP** sends it too (reason `pep`).

### 2. The case read says what the screening found, and whether the case is with HQ

`GET /api/kyc/admin/:userId` gains two blocks.

- **`screening`** takes one of four states:
  - `match`: the latest run found a possible match;
  - `clear`: the latest run found none;
  - `unreadable`: the latest attempt could not run, and no run came after it; or the store could not be read (`read_failed`);
  - `not_run`: the person was never screened.

  `match` and `clear` carry the run's id, time, severity and match count. They also carry `reviewed`: who reviewed it, when, and their notes, or null.

  **An unreadable screening is never `clear`.** An older clear run does not cover a later attempt that failed.
- **`hq`** is `{ state: 'ready', with_hq, open, decided }`, or `{ state: 'unreadable' }`.
  - Each referral carries its reason, note, screening id, who raised it and when, and who decided it, when and how.
  - A referral the Worker raised has `raised_by: null`.
  - A failed read is never "not with HQ".

### 3. The review write for a match

`POST /api/trust/sanctions/:id/review` records who reviewed a possible match, when, and their notes (5 to 2,000 characters). It writes `reviewed_by`, `reviewed_at` and `review_notes`, which no route wrote before.

- **Gates:** an admin, with the verdicts' gates (a branch not suspended, a recent TOTP step-up).
- **Only a match is reviewed:** a clear run answers 409 `not_a_match`.
- **Only once:** the write is `UPDATE … WHERE reviewed_at IS NULL`, so a second review, or two admins at once, answers 409 `already_reviewed`, naming who reviewed it and when.
- **Other refusals:** 404 `screening_not_found`, and 400 `notes_required`.
- **The log** (`sanctions_match_reviewed`) names the screening and the member's id, never the notes or the match.
- **A review does not close a referral.** HQ still decides a case it holds.

### 4. Send to HQ (migration 615)

`kyc_hq_referrals` holds one row per reason a case went to HQ: `pep`, `sanctions_match` or `admin`. Each row has a note, the screening id, who raised it and when, and who decided it, when, how (`approved` or `rejected`) and with what note.

- **One undecided row per person and reason.** A partial unique index enforces it, and a second raise of the same reason is a no-op.
- **An admin sends a case: `POST /api/kyc/admin/:userId/hq`.**
  - A note is required (5 to 1,000 characters).
  - Gates: an admin, and a branch not suspended. It decides nothing, so it asks for no step-up.
  - Refusals: 404 `user_not_found`; 409 `already_sent_to_hq` while an admin referral is undecided.
  - The log (`kyc_sent_to_hq`) holds no note.
- **While a case is with HQ, the studio's approve and reject refuse it,** with 409 `sent_to_hq` and the open reasons. A Super Admin is refused on those routes too, so HQ's decisions go through one route.
- **If the referrals cannot be read,** approve and reject answer 503 `hq_unreadable`. A case that might be with HQ is not decided by the studio.
- **HQ decides: `POST /api/kyc/admin/:userId/hq/decide`.**
  - Gates: `requireSuperAdmin`, a branch not suspended, and a recent TOTP step-up.
  - The body is `{ verdict: 'approved' | 'rejected', note }`. A rejection needs a note, which becomes the member's rejection reason.
  - The verdict runs through the same helper as the studio's routes (`approveCase` / `rejectCase`, now shared): the document purge on reject, the commission event on approve, and both activity rows.
  - Every undecided referral then closes with the verdict, and `kyc_hq_decided` is logged.
  - Refusals: 409 `not_sent_to_hq` when nothing is open; 503 `hq_unreadable`; 400 `bad_verdict` or `note_required`.
