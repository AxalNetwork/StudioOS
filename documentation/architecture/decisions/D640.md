## D640 — Held AI runs can be read and settled within their reservation

Issue #1433, part of the Usage page work in #1430. Migration 430 follows D629's AI allowance and top-up tables. S18 owns the Worker contract; S09 owns the screens.

### Review hold

When a recorded run costs more than its reservation, the Worker changes that reservation from `pending` to `review` and writes `recorded_cost_microusd` in the same update. It keeps the included and paid amounts reserved. The cost is evidence of the run, not a new charge: the member is never charged more than the amount held. Migration 430 adds that checked integer column and an index for the review queue. It does not alter migration 419 or invent earlier review rows; production had no reservations when the issue was written.

`GET /api/ai-budget/me` still reports the member's allowance, spend and top-up balance. It adds `held_for_review` with each reservation's UID, creation time, held amount and recorded cost. A held run no longer makes that read Unreadable. The Worker still refuses new reservations for that member until every review hold is settled, including when another request creates a review hold between the budget read and the reservation write. A failed store read remains Unreadable.

### Super Admin review

`GET /api/ai-budget/reviews` lists held runs across members with their names, times and amounts. `POST /api/ai-budget/reviews/:uid/charge` settles at exactly the held amount; the platform absorbs any provider overage. `POST /api/ai-budget/reviews/:uid/refund` settles at zero and restores the held allowance and paid balance. Both require the Super Admin and a reason of at least 10 non-whitespace characters. A reservation in another state is refused. Repeating the same completed decision reports that it was already settled without writing again; a conflicting decision is refused.

The reservation status, included spend adjustment, paid balance adjustment, paid-draw ledger and `activity_logs` entry share one D1 batch. A settlement token gates each downstream write, so concurrent calls can settle only once. The audit names the actor, member, held amount, recorded cost, amount charged, platform-absorbed difference, included and paid portions, and the reason. Store constraints reject negative or fractional amounts. An audit failure rolls back the money updates and leaves the hold for retry.

`GET /api/ai-budget/caps` is also Super Admin only. It lists all ten `AI_LICENCE_KEYS` in their canonical order, with daily and monthly integer micro-USD values. A stored override has source `recorded`, its actor and update time; otherwise the effective deployment fallback has source `environment`. An explicit stored zero remains zero. A failed read reports Unreadable, not an invented zero.

The Worker routes are mounted under `/api/ai-budget` in `index.ts`; this change adds no new frontend API method or production binding. Deploy migration 430 before the Worker. The owner handles merge, deployment and production verification.
