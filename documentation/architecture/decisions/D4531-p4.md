## D4531 part 4 — A claim can carry its reason, and a required claim can be held conditional; #4588's two staged expectations are enforced (#4531)

- **Status:** built by S17, 2026-11-13. Part 4 of #4531 (slices 1 to 3 are in `D4531.md`). No migration, no route.
- **Code:** `cloudflare-worker/src/services/agents/skillAssertions.ts` (`claims`), judge version 4.
- **Content:** two scenarios under `services/agents/skills/scenarios/`, each now revision 2.
- **Test:** `cloudflare-worker/test/skill_judge_claims_4531_p4.test.ts`.

### Why

#4588 (D4588 §6) left two expectations `staged`, because no deterministic assertion could check them:

1. `board-meeting-brief-missing-burn`: the runway statement should name burn as the reason runway is missing. No
   assertion related two fields within one statement.
2. `safe-convertible-instrument-review-conditional`: a conversion calculation on assumed inputs should stay
   conditional. The number and claim checks skip conditional statements, but nothing required one.

### The rule: two optional fields on a claim

Both are fields of the existing `claims` assertion, judged by the same `judge()` on the draft and published paths. No
second runner, no new `expect` key: `ASSERTION_KEYS`, the CI key list and the HQ case editor's key list are unchanged.
A claim without the new fields is judged exactly as under version 3.

- **`because: "<reason>"`** (required or forbidden claims). A statement states the claim only when it also gives the
  reason: a cause marker (`CAUSE_MARKS`: because, since, due to, owing to, given that, as a result of, for lack of,
  for want of), then the reason as a whole word, either after the object ("Runway: Not recorded, because monthly burn
  is not recorded") or wholly before the subject ("Because burn is not recorded, runway is Not recorded"). A negation
  inside that leading reason clause belongs to the reason and does not negate the claim; a negation between the
  subject and the object still does. The subject and object without the reason do not state the claim, so a required
  one fails "did not state"; the reason in another statement, with no marker, or between the subject and object does
  not count. The reason is part of the claim's identity, so the same claim may be required with one reason and
  forbidden with another.
- **`conditional: true`** (required claims only; refused on a forbidden claim, where its meaning would be unclear). The
  claim must be affirmed in at least one conditional statement and in no other. "Conditional" is the same list the
  number and claim checks skip (`if`, `would`, `could`, `assume`, `assuming`, `suppose`, `hypothetical`), so the
  conditional claim is exactly the complement of what they read. Three misses, in this order: stated as fact ("the
  SAFE will convert at the cap"), negated in a conditional statement (contradicted), or not stated conditionally at
  all.

Each miss names the claim (`"runway … not recorded" because "burn"`, `"SAFE … convert" as conditional`), never the
output. `checkClaims` refuses an empty, overlong or non-string reason, a reason equal to the subject or object, a
non-boolean `conditional`, and `conditional` on a forbidden claim. `conditional: false` is dropped when kept.

### Judge version 4

`JUDGE_VERSION` is 4, because version 3 refuses a claim with either field (it rejects unknown keys). The version is
recorded on each result row and bound into a draft's release evidence (D4533, slice 3), so the effects are the
documented ones of an evaluator change: a draft's evidence names the new evaluator and needs new results before it
publishes, and `compare` reports pairs across the change as `evaluator_changed`, never as regressions. No result is
re-judged.

### The two scenarios

| Scenario | Added | Targeted incorrect outputs added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `board-meeting-brief-missing-burn` r2 | `claims.required: [{ subject: "runway", object: "not recorded", because: "burn" }]` | the reason dropped; another reason (churn); the reason in a separate statement |
| `safe-convertible-instrument-review-conditional` r2 | `claims.required += { subject: "SAFE", object: "convert", conditional: true }` | the calculation stated as fact; the calculation left out |

Each revision is bumped because the case text changed (D5336): the stored case's identity is `<case_id>:r2`, imported
beside r1, never an edit of it. Both `staged` lists are removed. The known-correct outputs are unchanged and pass. The
test also shows each targeted output passes without the new field, so the field is what catches it.
