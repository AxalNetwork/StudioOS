### 2. What is read, and from where

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/admin/events?status&limit=200&offset` (R1), paged until a short page | every event's own fields: the queue, the table, the status counts, the dates |
| `GET /api/admin/events/analytics` (R2), per-event rows joined by `id` | seats taken, attended, waitlisted |
| `GET /api/admin/events/:id` (R3) | the event page |
| `GET /api/events/:id/agenda` (R5) | the event page's agenda |

- **R2's `summary` is not read.**
  - Its counts are all-time, across every event.
  - Its status buckets have no `rejected`.
  - A failed query returns zeros with status 200.

  The status counts come from R1 instead.
- **Guard G makes R2's rows safe to draw** without a Worker change:
  - R1 is truthful: a D1 error is a 5xx.
  - R2's rows are one query, which on failure returns `[]`.

  So when R1 has events and R2 has none, the per-event counts **could not be read**, and every count says so. An R1 event missing from R2's 200 rows reads "Not in this read" for its counts, never 0. Part 3's paged list retires the guard.
- **The roster read (`GET /api/events/:id/roster`) is not used here.** It carries every registrant's name and email.
  - The desk draws counts, and the canvas puts no name on any board.
  - So the event page's payment, free-seat, invitation and source counts wait for part 3's counts-only summary.
- **The words are the canvas's:**
  - Status: Draft · Waiting for review · Published · Sent back · Cancelled. The stored `rejected` reads "Sent back".
  - Seats taken = Registered + Confirmed + Attended. That is exactly R2's `registrations`.
  - Fill = seats taken ÷ capacity. An event with no cap reads "No cap".
  - Show-up = Attended ÷ seats taken, for events that have ended. Before that it reads "Not held yet", never 0% or a dash. R2's `conversion` is never drawn, because it gives 0 for every upcoming event.
  - **An event has ended** once its `ends_at` has passed. With no `ends_at`, it has ended a day after its start.
  - A missing value is "Not recorded". A value that is stored but not summed by any read yet says "Not read here yet". A failed read says what could not be read.
- **Rules the parts share**, so that the overview read, the calendar and the review queue agree:
  - **Overdue** means waiting longer than 72 hours since `submitted_at`. That is the Approvals lane's `SLA_PAST_HOURS` (`approvalSources.ts`), which #2390 shares. The canvas's "two days" yields to it.
  - **Two events clash** when neither is cancelled and their times overlap. An event with no `ends_at` lasts one hour for this rule.
  - **Low sign-ups** means an event with a cap that starts within 7 days with fewer than a quarter of its seats taken.
  - **How a seat was got**, in this order:
    - a registration with an invitation is Invitation;
    - otherwise a comp seat is Free seat;
    - otherwise it is Signed up.
    - "Added by an admin" is "Not recorded": nothing writes it.

### 3. What each tab draws today

- **Overview.**
  - Tiles: Waiting for review (the count; waiting time is "Not recorded yet" until part 2's `submitted_at`), Next 30 days, On waitlists (registrations waitlisted on upcoming events) and Show-up rate (events that have ended).
  - The status strip from R1. Each chip opens All events with that status.
  - Page views and Feedback as "Not recorded", with the canvas's reasons.
- **Review.**
  - The queue is R1 with `status=pending_review`, every page of it, searched by title and sorted by start or by creation.
  - Each row has its start, plus the Paid and Free seats flags.
  - The selected event's facts come from its R1 row: visibility, place, start and end with the event's timezone label, capacity and waitlist, registration, price, who may register, free seats for, created and description. Submitted is "Not recorded yet".
  - "What approving does" has the canvas's first two lines.
    - Its third line has no counts until part 3's preview. It names the two sources approve invites: official partners and invested LPs.
    - The other three free-seat flags give a seat at sign-up and send no invitation, and the line says so.
  - The action bar has Send back, Open as host and Approve and publish.
  - Approve (EV4) has "Also feature it". It sends `{ featured: true }` after the approve, and says so if that second call fails.
  - Send back (EV5) needs a reason in the page, with the quick reasons and the 1000-character counter.
  - After an approve, a line appears over the list. No toast.
- **All events.**
  - R1 joined to R2's counts under Guard G.
  - Columns: Event (with Featured), Starts, Status, Visibility, Capacity, Taken, Waitlisted, Attended, Fill, Show-up, Price.
  - Search by title, a status filter (from the address), the canvas's default sort, and 25 rows a page with "1–25 of [N]".
  - Each title opens the event page.
- **One event.**
  - Header: the title; the status, visibility and Featured pills; the time with the event's timezone label; the place.
  - The publish gate (EV7g).
  - Tiles from R2's row: Capacity, Seats taken, On the waitlist, Attended and Show-up.
  - The agenda from R5, read only, with "Edit as host".
  - "Names and emails are on the host's roster." with "Open roster as host".
  - History has one line: "Created · [date]". Everything else is "Not read here yet".
  - Actions, each through a dialog (`Lightbox`), never a browser pop-up: Change capacity, Feature or Stop featuring, Unpublish (Published only) and Cancel event (not when cancelled).
  - The menu: Open as host, Download roster (CSV), Open public page (public, published and approved only) and Add to calendar.
- **The dialogs say only what the Worker does** (the map's §2, row 12):
  - Capacity:
    - raising says "[n] move off the waitlist, oldest first. Each is told in StudioOS." There is no email.
    - lowering says new sign-ups join the waitlist only when the waitlist is on.
  - Cancel says "It leaves the public calendar." It does not say "every calendar". It also does not say "cannot be undone", because approve revives a cancelled event until part 2.
    - It names who is told: people with an account who hold a seat or wait for one, in StudioOS.
    - A paid event adds the refund box and its checkbox.
    - It has no note field until part 2.
  - Unpublish says "The host is not told automatically." It has no note until part 2.
- **Choices the build made where the canvas left room:**
  - The event page keeps the tab strip, with no tab selected; the canvas hides it there.
  - A refusal sits under the dialog's buttons, and the dialog stays open, so a typed reason is not lost. The canvas draws it under the page's action bar.
  - "Next 30 days" and "On waitlists" count only events that are published or waiting for review.
  - A cancelled event's attended and show-up read "Not held".
  - A sum with no events to cover is a real 0.
- **Refusals are drawn as one sentence under the action:**
  - 423 `branch_suspended`: the licence is suspended, and approving and featuring are paused;
  - 403 `admin_frozen`;
  - 404: the event no longer exists.
- **Not drawn now, and why.** Each waits for the part that builds it (§6):
  - the period control, the weekly chart and its table (EV12);
  - Registrations, Paid tickets, Free seats given and Cancelled with paid tickets;
  - Who comes, Events by type, Needs attention, Next up and Show-up by event;
  - the review preview, clashes, history, host and venture;
  - the table's other filters, views, sorts, CSV and phone cards;
  - Calendar;
  - the event page's charts and its count cards.

  Type and format are not drawn anywhere until part 1. Four of the twelve types are stored as Meetup, and In person is stored as Online.
