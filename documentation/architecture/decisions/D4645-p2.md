## D4645 part 2 — An "Ask again" reminder is a recorded round with one delivery per recipient, reconciled and never re-sent blind (#4645)

- **Status:** Built for #4645, 2026-11-18. Worker, migration and the existing
  sent-update page. Part 2 of the reminder lifecycle that D4645 §5 began.
- **Builds on:** D4645 §5 (the cooldown claimed in SQL before sending); D4636
  (`services/updateLifecycle.ts`: one logical delivery per recipient per
  version, a claim token per attempt, `ambiguous` looked for by its own uid and
  re-sent only on request); D124/D125 (stored times compared through
  `datetime()` on both sides).
- **Migration:** `202611182124_4645_ask_reminder_deliveries.sql`, additive
  (two new tables).

### What was still missing after part 1

Part 1 made one press per window send, and stopped reporting the recipient
list's length as delivery. Three things were still not recorded:

- **How far a reminder got.** The claim was durable; the deliveries were not.
  A Worker stopped half-way through the loop left a claimed window and no
  record of who had been reminded, so the author could neither finish it nor
  tell who was missed.
- **A lost response.** A press whose answer never reached the browser was
  indistinguishable from one that never ran. Pressing again answered 409
  ("asked again a moment ago"), with nothing about what the first did.
- **Records that change mid-send.** Recipients were read once after the claim
  and every one was sent to, so a reply, a corrected version of the update,
  or a recipient removed while the loop ran did not stop it. Recipients were
  also read across **every** version (and including declined deliveries),
  not the current version's.

### The round

`POST /portfolio-updates/asks/:askUid/ask-again` now opens a **round**
(`update_ask_reminders`). The cooldown claim, the round row and one
reservation per current recipient (`update_ask_reminder_deliveries`,
UNIQUE (reminder_id, user_id)) are written in **one batch** — one D1
transaction — so a claimed window always has its round and its
reservations, or none of them stands. The round exists only where this
request's claim does (the INSERT is conditional on `asked_again_at` being the
value this request wrote), so of two presses at once one opens a round.

"Current recipient" is read from the records, not from the request: a person
with an account whose delivery of the update's **current published version**
was made (`delivery_state = 'delivered'`), or, for an update sent before D4636
recorded versions, a recipient row with no version. A recipient of an older
version only, or one whose delivery was declined, is not reserved.

The logical reservation is therefore one per ask round, per update version
(the round names it, `version_id`), per recipient.

### Each delivery

Each reservation is claimed with a fresh token, conditional on the state it
was read in (D4636's `claim`), and then checked against the records as they
stand at that moment:

| Check | When it fails | Recorded as |
|---|---|---|
| the ask has no reply | a reply landed | `skipped`, `replied` |
| the update's current version is still the round's | a correction was published | `skipped`, `version_changed` |
| the person is still a current recipient of it | their row went | `skipped`, `not_current_recipient` |
| the check could be read | it could not | `failed` (nothing was sent; resuming retries it) |

Only then is it sent. The outcome is written only where the claim still
stands: `delivered` with the inbox row id; or, when the notification path
answers null, the inbox is looked for by the delivery's uid first — `notify`
answers null both when it wrote nothing and when it failed after writing the
row — and it is `delivered` if found, `declined` if not, `ambiguous` if the
look-up itself failed. A throw from the path is `ambiguous`.

### Reconciliation and lost responses

- An attempt that never recorded its outcome reads as `ambiguous` once its
  claim is older than D4636's `STALE_ATTEMPT_MS` (15 minutes); a fresher one
  is someone else's and is left alone.
- An `ambiguous` delivery is looked for in `notifications_inbox` by the
  `reminder_delivery_uid` its notification carries. Found, it is `delivered`
  and nothing is sent. Not found, it **stays ambiguous** and is sent again
  only when the author asks for that explicitly (`resend_ambiguous: true`,
  the page's "Send the unconfirmed again"). An inbox that cannot be read
  leaves it ambiguous.
- A `reserved` delivery (never attempted) and a `failed` one (stopped before
  sending) are attempted on resume, after the same checks.
- **A lost response.** The page sends a `request_key` with each press
  (UNIQUE per ask). When the answer is lost — no status, or a server error —
  it repeats the request once with the same key, and the Worker answers with
  the **original round**, resumed and reconciled as above, `recovered: true`.
  A key that collides inside the batch rolls the whole batch back, and the
  other request's round answers. A refused press (409 `ask_not_due` or
  `ask_claimed`) carries the latest round's progress in `reminder`.
- "Check delivery" sends `reminder_uid` and does the same for that round.

Nothing runs on a schedule; a round moves only inside a request its author made.

### What is guaranteed, and what is not

Stated in the response's `delivery`, on the page, and here, and no wider:
one round per ask per window; one reservation per recipient per round (and
so per version); before anything is sent again the inbox is checked for the
earlier attempt. That deduplication is **logical**. The notification path's
email and push go out by each person's settings and are **not** exactly-once,
and a re-send the author asks for may reach someone twice — the button says so.

### Cooldown times

`canAskAgain` used a bare `Date.parse`, which reads SQLite's zone-less
`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS` as **local** time, moving the window by the runtime's
offset. `stampMs` reads that form, an ISO time with its zone, and a bare date
as UTC. The SQL claim applies the same rule with `datetime()` on both sides
and now includes the "never asked again, counted from the send" arm, which
part 1 checked only in JS. Policy: ASK_AGAIN_DAYS (14) whole 24-hour days in
UTC after the last ask, or after the send when it was never asked again, and
no reply.

### Response and page

The response keeps `recipients`, `notified` and `failed` (now `declined` plus
`failed`) and adds `pending`, `unconfirmed` (ambiguous plus stale attempts),
`skipped` with `skipped_reasons`, `states`, `settled`, `reminder_uid`,
`version_id` and `recovered`. `GET /:uid/page` gives the author each ask's
latest round as `reminder` (null when never asked again) and
`reminders_readable`, false when the rounds could not be read — the page then
says "Reminder progress: Unreadable" rather than drawing none.

The existing `/updates/:uid` page (no new screen) shows the round under its
ask — "Asked again 18 Nov · 4 recipients · 1 in their inbox · 1 not confirmed
· 1 no longer due" — with "Check delivery" while the round has work left.

### Tests

- `cloudflare-worker/test/portfolio_ask_reminders_4645p2.test.ts` — reservations
  are the current version's delivered recipients only; a repeat with the same
  key answers the original round and sends nothing; a reply, a new version and a
  removed recipient mid-round each skip the rest with their reason; an
  interrupted round is reconciled (found → delivered with no second row, not
  found → unconfirmed and unsent, never attempted → checked then sent, a fresh
  attempt left alone) and only `resend_ambiguous` re-sends; the page shows the
  latest round; a zone-less time is UTC in JS and SQL, run under a non-UTC zone.
- `cloudflare-worker/test/portfolio_ask_again_4645.test.ts` (part 1's) loads the
  migration and counts an unreadable inbox as unconfirmed, not failed.
- `frontend/test/sent_update_reminders_4645.test.mjs` — the round's line, when
  "Check delivery" is offered, the outcome sentence, and a lost answer repeated
  once with the same key while a refusal is not.
