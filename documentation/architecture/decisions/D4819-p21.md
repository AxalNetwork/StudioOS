### What was built

- **Five states, each drawn differently.** Pending, Completed, Partially completed, Refused and Outcome unknown each have a word, a shape (◌ ✓ ◐ ✕ ?) and a look of their own. Colour is never the only signal. Refused and unknown are announced (`role="alert"`); the others are polite.
  - **Pending** names the act ("Suspending…") and waits for HQ.
  - **Completed** says HQ and the branch confirmed the revision, or that there is no branch to sync.
  - **Partially completed** is never green. It is amber and dashed, and it says three things: what HQ holds ("Licence suspended at HQ"); that the branch has not yet acknowledged the revision; and that retrying the act itself is not necessary. It adds what D5925's branch read reports, what the copy left out, and that HQ retries on its own (`licence_push_pending`). Its one button is **Retry branch sync**.
  - **Outcome unknown** (no answer: a timeout, a 502 with no code, a 503 or 504) says to re-read the licence before trying again, because the act may or may not have been made. Its one button is **Re-read licence**.
  - **Refused** shows the Worker's own sentence (`message`, or an `error` that is a sentence), never a bare code.
- **Where it draws.** The lifecycle five (activate, reinstate, renew, suspend, terminate) and Retry branch sync report in the licence's card. That card reads the licence's `sync` as last read, so a partial outcome says what the branch reports now. Administrators, fee invoices and the agreement each draw their own card.
- **Retry branch sync is real.**
  - `POST /api/admin/licences/:uid/sync/retry` (Super Admin) pushes the current copy now, through the same `pushLicenceToBranch` every write uses. It changes nothing at HQ, and it answers with the push result. A draft is refused (`not_issued`).
  - The Branch sync strip's Retry sync, which D5924 drew disabled because nothing served it, now sends.
  - The retry is recorded in the admin audit log (`licence_sync_retried`, with the revision and how it ended), not in `licence_events`. That table's CHECK lists the events that change a licence, and widening it is an append-only table rebuild (migration 266) for an act that changes nothing.
- **The one sheet.** The nine writes L18 names all ask in the consequence sheet, with its four questions: add administrator, remove administrator, issue invoice, void invoice, send agreement, deploy branch, retry deployment, reconcile deployment and retry branch sync.
  - The reason is asked where the Worker records one, at its own minimum: ten characters for add, remove and void. Elsewhere the sheet says what is recorded instead.
  - The reason dialog's add and remove asks (`addAdminAsk`, `removeAdminAsk`) and the inline Void form are gone. A delegate request's reason still arrives pre-filled and editable.

### Departures

- **Deploy, retry deployment and reconcile keep D4706's notice.** They ask in the sheet, but their outcome stays the deployment's own notice. That notice already says pending, done, refused or unknown, and it follows the provisioning run until it settles, which the card does not. The quiet re-check while an attempt is open asks nothing.
- **"Has not yet acknowledged"** is said from what the push recorded and from D5925's read of the branch, where there is one. Nothing more is claimed.
- **Detach** (the second step of removing an administrator) takes no reason and is not one of the nine. It reports in the card, without a sheet.
