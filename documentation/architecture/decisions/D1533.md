## D1533 — Deck v2 B1: direct manipulation (guides, align, distribute, group, lock, layers)

- **Status:** built in slot S25, 2026-11-02 (#3777, deck builder v2 Phase B, part of #3716). Not deployed or live verified.
- **Scope:** S1's final-canvas revision of #3777 (2026-11-01), which wins over the original body.
- **Boards:** PD-3 pd26 "Align and distribute" and pd3b "The Grid" (its "DROP REFUSED" inset).
- **Builds on:** D1604 (#3885: `GridStage.jsx`, `gridSelection.js`, `BlockTools.jsx`), D1513 (#3765, A4: dividers, reading order), D1499 (#3749: `boxOf`, the 8 px `BASELINE`) and D1500 (#3750: a card's `decor`).
- **Migration:** none. Everything here lives in the version's `slides` JSON.
- **Owner decision:** none. S21's STATUS on the issue: build the model fields now, and key the layers list to #3779's decor kinds.

### Why

HEAD's `sanitizeBlocks` kept only known fields, so a group, a lock and a layer order would not survive a save, and nothing on The Grid could align, distribute, group or nudge. pd26 draws the Arrange set and the decor layers; pd3b draws the refused drop.

### What was built

**1. The model (Worker first; `services/decks/arrange.ts`, read by `slideModel.ts`).**

- **Group.** A block and a decor item keep `group`, a plain id (letters, digits, `-`, `_`, at most 40). A malformed one is dropped on read and refused on save.
- **Lock.** The Arrange set's Lock is `pinned: true` on a block or a decor item: the item keeps its place and size. It is deliberately not `locked`. `locked` is #3819's brand-kit lock, which makes an area unselectable (`gridSelection.selectable`); a pinned item stays selectable, so it can be unlocked. Brand-kit locks stay with #3819.
- **Layer order.** Decor only ("LAYERS · DECOR ONLY"): areas never overlap, so they have none. A decor item keeps `layer`; `sanitizeModel` stores the decor list in layer order, bottom first, each item renumbered 1…n. A group's members share one place in the order, where its lowest member stood (`gathered`), so a group is one shape in PowerPoint and stacks the same in the PDF.
- **Decor on 8 px.** A decor item may carry `at: { x, y, w, h }`, slide pixels, every value a multiple of 8, inside 1920 × 1080. It is never rounded into place: an off-grid box is dropped on read and refused on save ("a decor item's box sits on the 8 px baseline, inside the slide."). An item without `at` stays on its cell range (`grid`), as D1500 and #3779 place it.
- **Decor is never content.** `modelProblems` refuses a decor item that carries `text`, `paras`, `value`, `series`, `points`, `rows` or `binding`, or claims a block's kind: "Card N: decor never holds a figure or body text." `sanitizeModel` drops an item that claims a block kind. Decor may overlap areas because it is not an area: it is drawn under them.
- **Fit and contracts.** `fitProblems` reads blocks only and is unchanged; a test holds that a card's fit report is the same with and without the arrange fields. No contract describes a card's fields (`deckSaveCard`'s `card` is an open object), so none changed.

**2. The exports honour all four.**

- **Editable PowerPoint** (`pptxEditable.ts`, the Google Slides export). Decor is drawn first, under every block, in layer order. Each item is a native shape: a circle, a square, a rounded square (`ellipse`, `rect`, `roundRect`) or a divider line (a connector), filled with its tone. A pinned shape carries `noMove` and `noResize` locks (`spLocks`, `picLocks`, `cxnSpLocks`). Each group of two or more becomes one `p:grpSp`; a group whose members are all pinned is locked as a whole. Decor and areas are grouped apart, so a group never lifts decor over an area.
- **PDF** (the print page, `PrintExtras.jsx`'s `PrintDecor`). The same plan, the same shapes, under the blocks, each tagged with its layer, group and lock. The PowerPoint download photographs this page, so it carries the same decor.
- **One plan.** Both read `decorPlan` (the Worker's, mirrored in `frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/arrangeModel.js`). `EXPORT_GEOMETRY` (the shape for a `kind:ref`) and `TONE_COLOURS` (the print card's ink, accent, muted and surface) are each one table on each side, and a test holds them equal.

**3. The Grid** (S1's files; no new editor).

- **`gridSelection.js`: multi-select and group.** A decor item joins the selection as `decor:<id>`, beside the areas' ids. `pickGroup`: a plain click on a grouped area or decor item selects the whole group (`groupSelection`, one layer), and Shift still adds or takes away one.
- **`GridStage.jsx`: the handle, nudge, snap, guides and markers.**
  - A selected decor item gets a violet handle over the areas.
  - Arrow keys nudge it 8 px ("Moved 8 px"; Shift, 24 px), and the stage says "Arrow keys nudge by 8 px."
  - A pointer drags it, snapped to 8 px and kept on the slide.
  - While it is selected, the stage draws violet guides where its edges or centre meet another item's, or the slide's centre, and spacing markers ("24 px") to the nearest item each way.
  - A pinned item does not move: "Locked items keep their place. Unlock to move them."
- **`BlockTools.jsx`: `ArrangeSet` and `DecorLayers`.**
  - The Arrange set, in pd26's order: align left, centre, right, top, middle and bottom; distribute horizontally and vertically; Group (Ungroup when the selection is one group); Lock (Unlock when all are pinned); Bring forward; Send back; Copy style.
  - It appears only while an area or decor item is selected (`PitchEditor.jsx`), never as a permanent toolbar.
  - A tool that cannot run is greyed and says why on hover. Examples: "Arrange decor with decor, or areas with areas."; "Areas never overlap, so only decor has layers."; "Select 3 or more items to distribute."
  - A press is one edit through `onCard`, with its line ("Align bottom: 1 moved."). A refusal is grey, never red, and ends "Nothing changed."
  - `DecorLayers` is "LAYERS · DECOR ONLY", top layer first, with "Decor never holds a figure or body text." A row selects its item.
- **Areas stay on cells.** Aligning or distributing areas moves whole cells (a centre between two cells rounds to one). An overlap is refused with pd3b's sentence: "That range overlaps the 7 × 5 text. Pick a free range."
- **Decor stays on 8 px.** Aligning or distributing decor writes `at` on the 8 px baseline.
- **Copy style.** The first item selected is the source. A decor item's tone, a text block's style and an image's fit go to the selected items of the same kind.
- **The refused drop (pd3b).** It already drew a grey ghost and "That range overlaps [X]. Pick a free range." (D1604). A test now holds that the ghost is grey and that nothing on the stage is red.

**4. The adapter.** `deckV2Adapter.arrange` needs no route. Align, distribute, group, lock and layers edit the card document, and the editor's existing save and autosave already write it (`PATCH /api/decks/:id/cards/:uid`, `POST /api/decks/:id/versions`). So `arrange` is a local implementation: it answers `{ available: true, value }` from `arrangeModel.arrange`, with no `through()`, and therefore no contract. It joins `SWAPPED`. The contracts test (`deck_contracts_d1511`, case 3) still pairs every `through()` with a contract.

### Departures

- **Where decor lives.** The coordinator's brief said "`sanitizeBlocks` must keep decor blocks". Decor stays in D1500's one list, `card.decor`, which #3779 extends with the library's kinds. A decor block kind would be a second decor list, which the brief and S21 both rule out.
  - `sanitizeBlocks` keeps the areas' group and lock.
  - `sanitizeModel`, through `decorOf`, keeps decor's group, lock, layer and box.
  - The required test runs the card through both.
- **The decor kinds are #3779's.** #3779 (B3, D1541) has not merged; its branch `agent/claude-s18/3779-deck-elements` has no PR yet. This file adds no decor kind.
  - **The layers list** names each item by the library's label when `deckV2Adapter.elementsLibrary` answers. On main that is "Not switched on yet", so the list falls back to the item's kind and ref ("Shape · circle"). It needs no change when #3779 lands.
  - **The exports' shapes** are keyed by #3779's refs (`shape:circle`, `shape:rect`, `shape:rounded`, `divider:line`), the four pd26's list draws. On main, a save keeps no `ref` or `tone`, because #3779's `decorOf` is what keeps them. Until it merges, a saved card's decor is placed in neither export. The render test therefore adds `ref` and `tone` back to the saved card the way #3779 stores them, and says so.
  - **Not drawn by either export:** icons, textures and the other shapes. Their art is #3779's SVG, which its D1541 §7 leaves out of the exports for now.
- **No decor art in the editor.** B1 draws only the selected decor's handle, guides and markers. #3779's `GridStage.jsx` draws the decor itself.
- **Not covered:** the HTML file (`offlineHtml.ts`) and the .odp export (`odp.ts`). They lay a card out on a CSS or ODF grid, not on slide pixels, and do not draw decor. The issue names PDF and PPTX only.
- **The original tests.**
  - "3 testers place 10 elements with no off-grid edge" is a usability test with people, which CI cannot run. Its automated stand-in is a seeded property test: 400 cards, ten random Arrange operations or decor drags each. Every operation either leaves every area on whole cells with no two sharing a cell, every decor box on 8 px inside the slide and every pinned item in place, or is refused and changes nothing.
  - "Editing tasks 2 and 5 timed against Canva" is also a usability test, and is not run here.

### Mutation check

31 guarded lines were broken one at a time by script, each run against its test file and restored: 30 caught, 1 survived.

- **Worker side (15, all caught):**
  - sanitize keeping the block and decor fields, the layer order and the 8 px box;
  - the three save refusals, and decor claiming a block kind;
  - group gathering;
  - decor under the blocks, the block lock, the decor lock and the group shape;
  - the geometry and tone mirrors.
- **Frontend (15 of 16 caught):**
  - the drag's snap, area rounding and the area overlap check;
  - the mixed refusal, Lock as `pinned`, and tools on selection only;
  - the nudge key, print order, layers order and group selection;
  - a pinned item staying put, guides and the nearest spacing marker;
  - the local adapter and the grey ghost.
- **The survivor** is `decorTargets`' clamp to the slide. Aligning or distributing boxes that already sit on the slide cannot leave it, so no operation reaches the clamp. It is kept as a guard.
