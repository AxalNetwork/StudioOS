## D570 — Wellbeing built to the Ops canvas: the member's week, booking an expert, the expert's practice and the resource library

**Issue #1174, area Wellbeing, slot S01, 2026-10-06.** Built to
`design/incoming/Ops Page.dc.html` and its `ops-data.js` (D566): the screens
`wb-home`, `wb-book`, `wbx-practice` and `wba-library`. The routes existed.
Nine of the client methods behind them were called by no screen.

**What was true on main.**
- `/wellbeing` was a daily form of six sliders. Every slider the member did
  not touch was saved as 3. The page also held a 30-day chart, the expert
  directory with a booking modal that asked for a note, and the resource list.
  No screen anywhere showed a member their own bookings.
- `/wellbeing/expert-dashboard` listed services, availability windows and
  bookings as plain rows, and showed the booker's note. Its profile form
  offered `chat` and `insurance`, which the route drops without a word, and it
  sent `null` for an empty hourly rate, which the route stores as 0.
- No screen created or deleted a resource.

**What the Worker stores, read in `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/wellbeing.ts`,
where the brief differs from it.**
- `POST /api/wellbeing/checkins` (`wellbeingSubmit`) validates with
  `validateDailyBody` and upserts **one row per user per day** into
  `wellbeing_daily_pulses`. A row holds six optional 1–5 scores (`mood`,
  `stress`, `sleep`, `energy`, `focus`, `social`), `free_text` and up to eight
  `tags`, and needs at least one score. There is no `week_anchor`, and
  nothing refuses a second save: on the same day it replaces the first. The
  brief's five weekly scores (stress, sleep, support, decisions and energy) are
  the legacy `wellbeing_checkins` keys, which nothing has written since Task
  #33. "One per week" is the dev FastAPI's `week_anchor`
  (`backend/app/api/routes/wellbeing.py`), and that is an upsert too.
- `wellbeingExpertRate` is `POST /api/wellbeing/experts/:uid/rate`: a member's
  1–5 star rating of an expert. It is not the expert's price.

**The screens.** The pure half (the check-in body, weeks, slots, the grid, the
library's filters, the rails' coverage) is
`frontend/src/pages/wellbeing/wellbeingModel.js`.

1. **`/wellbeing`, the member home** (`WellbeingPage.jsx`, extended in place).
   It reads `wellbeingMyCheckins`, `wellbeingMyBookings` and
   `wellbeingResources`, and writes `wellbeingSubmit` and
   `wellbeingExpertRate`. Each of its four cards reads on its own and has its
   own loading, empty and unreadable state.
   - **This week's check-in** shows the canvas's five rows. Stress, sleep and
     energy are saved. Support and decisions read "Not recorded" with the
     reason, and there is no input for either. Mood, focus, connection and
     the topics are still asked, under "More", because the route stores them.
     The private note is `free_text`.
     - An unanswered score is left out of the body. It is never sent as 3.
     - **One per week is this page's rule.** If the week (Monday to Sunday,
       in UTC days, as the route writes `day`) already has an entry, the form
       starts from it and the save sends that entry's `day`. The route's
       per-day upsert then replaces it. The card says "Saving again replaces
       that entry"; nothing claims a refusal.
     - The upsert overwrites every column, so the save sends back every
       stored value. Otherwise it would erase them.
     - If the history cannot be read, the form does not save, since it could
       not tell a new entry from a replacement.
   - **My past weeks** draws eight weeks as sparklines (weekly means). A week
     without a check-in breaks the line and is never drawn as zero. Support
     and decisions read "Not recorded". `GET /checkins` returns 30 rows unless
     asked for more, so when a full page comes back, the weeks it may not have
     reached are left blank and the card says why.
   - **My bookings** has Upcoming and Past tabs: the expert, "service not
     recorded", the time in the viewer's zone, the length, the status as
     stored, and the call link (http(s) only). A past session that happened
     can be rated with stars through `wellbeingExpertRate`. No row shows a
     note.
   - **Resources for you** lists the library in its own order, crisis lines
     first, three at a time. Links are http(s) only. Admins also get a link to
     the library.
   - **Rail:** `WorkerRail` (`founder`). Its coverage is counts of the
     resource library and nothing else (`homeCoverage`).
   - The admin view's anonymised pulse now reads the six keys `/aggregate`
     returns. It used to read `support` and `decisions`, which the route never
     sends.
2. **`/wellbeing/book`** (`pages/wellbeing/WellbeingBookPage.jsx`, new). It
   reads `wellbeingExperts`, `wellbeingExpertCategories`,
   `wellbeingExpertSlots` and `wellbeingMyCheckins` (past topics, to rank the
   directory as the old page did), and writes `wellbeingExpertBook`.
   - The four steps sit side by side, as drawn, and stack on a phone.
   - Expert cards show specialties (named from the catalogue), the
     directory rate, ratings and the next opening. The next opening comes
     from each listed expert's `/slots`, so no profile view is spent.
   - Search and the old directory's four filters stay above the steps. The
     list shows "1–N of M".
   - The booking body is the slot and `duration_minutes: 30`, nothing else.
     There is no note (D331). The route's 409 for a taken slot is shown, and
     the slots are read again.
   - An expert with their own scheduler is booked through it, as before.
   - No rail: the canvas sets `rail: false`.
3. **`/wellbeing/practice`** (`ExpertEditorPage.jsx`, extended; the old
   `/wellbeing/expert-dashboard` redirects here, so notification links and the
   Stripe return URL still land). The zones are in
   `pages/wellbeing/PracticeZones.jsx`. It reads `wellbeingExpertMe`,
   `wellbeingExpertMyServices`, `wellbeingExpertMyAvailability` and
   `wellbeingExpertMyBookings`. It writes `wellbeingExpertServiceCreate`,
   `wellbeingExpertServiceUpdate`, `wellbeingExpertServiceDelete`,
   `wellbeingExpertAvailabilityCreate`, `wellbeingExpertAvailabilityDelete`,
   `wellbeingExpertBookingPatch` and `wellbeingExpertMeUpdate`.
   - **Services:** name, length, rate (zero reads "Free", as the route means
     it) and an active switch, with inline edit. A delete asks first. The rate
     is typed in the currency's main unit and sent in cents.
   - **Weekly availability** is the canvas's grid: Monday to Friday, 08 to
     17, widened for any window outside it, in the windows' own zone.
     - A closed hour opens as a one-hour window.
     - An open one-hour window closes.
     - An hour inside a longer window asks to remove that window. Splitting
       it would take writes that cannot be undone as one.
     - A booked hour, or a window that holds one, cannot be closed.
     - Windows in another zone are listed, not guessed onto the grid.
   - **Upcoming bookings** show names and times only. The booker's note is in
     the response and is never rendered. Confirm, cancel (asks first) and
     mark completed are kept from the old dashboard. Earlier bookings sit under
     a fold.
   - **Profile and payouts** follow under their own label. The option lists
     are now the route's own (`VALID_MODALITIES`, `VALID_PRICING_MODELS`), and
     an empty hourly rate is left out instead of being sent as `null`.
   - **Rail:** `WorkerRail` (`advisor`). It reads counts only.
4. **`/admin/wellbeing/resources`** (`pages/wellbeing/WellbeingResourceLibrary.jsx`,
   new). It reads `wellbeingResources` and writes `wellbeingResourceCreate` and
   `wellbeingResourceDelete`.
   - The list has search, a kind filter, a sort, "1–25 of N" and pages.
   - Rows show the kind, the title (http(s) links only), topic "Not
     recorded", Published, and the date added.
   - Delete asks first and says members stop seeing the resource at once.
   - The editor checks the title, the kind, the link and the order before
     publishing, and shows the route's refusal otherwise.
   - The door is "Resource library →" on the Admin console's Wellbeing tab.
   - **Rail:** `WorkerRail` (`admin`).

**Departures.**
- The check-in is weekly by re-saving onto the week's day, not by a route
  rule. Support and decisions are drawn, not asked.
- Mood, focus, connection and topics are kept under "More", and past weeks
  shows them under "Also recorded" when present. An artboard that leaves out a
  working field is not an instruction to stop collecting it.
- `wellbeingExpertRate` is wired where a member has had a session (Past
  bookings), not on the practice page. An expert's prices are each service's
  rate. The directory rate is the profile's hourly rate.
- The practice page is the old dashboard, extended. Its booking actions are
  kept. The note it used to show is gone.
- Booking rows carry a status pill and a call link the canvas does not draw.
  Expert cards carry ratings.
- The library's kind is the route's category: crisis line, therapy, peer
  group, coaching or reading. The canvas's article, audio, video and link do
  not exist. "Updated" is "Added". There is no "Save draft" and no upload.
- The library's six columns need a card at least 42rem wide. Beside the
  editor, under the app's sidebar and rail, a laptop card is narrower than
  that, so each row stacks (a container query, keyed to the card and not the
  screen). The services list does the same at 32rem.
- The rails' canvas proposals are not built. See GAPS.
- The booking page keeps search and filters the artboard does not draw. The
  prompt's own rule gives every list them.
