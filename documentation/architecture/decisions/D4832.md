## D4832 — Practice Sessions puts the day's sessions first and scheduling setup below them (#4832)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-16 by slot S31 (#4832, Practice UX 4/6, P1).
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Practice v2 Page.dc.html`, board PX-05 A (Sessions and setup) and B (the editor while loading) (D4874).
- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** unchanged. Every read and write uses a mounted route: `GET /me/bookings`, `/me/slots`, `/me/availability`, `/me/session-types`, `/me/booking-links`, and `PATCH /me/bookings/:id/billing`.
- **Owner's answer (2026-11-09):** Practice Sessions owns booking configuration. Session types, durations, prices and booking links are edited here. The Expertise service card (#4824) links to a session type and does not edit it.
- **Builds on:** D571 (the session types and booking links cards), D1424 and D1596 (the Block form), D4687, D4692 and D4712 (the lifecycle), D4702 (the past window of the hours grid), D4713 (rules are replaced whole, so they are edited only after a read), D4715 (a booking carries the offer it was made on), D5316 (Included), D5358 (the confirmed reprice step), D5364 (the Ask hand-over in `sessionsBlockHandoff.js`).

### What the page shows

- **Header:** Sessions, with **Block time** as the primary action and **Share booking link** as the secondary one. Export to calendar stays as a third action.
  - **Block time** opens the same Block form as before, with the same handler. The Ask hand-over (D5364) is unchanged.
  - **Share booking link** copies the link when exactly one booking link is active. With several active links, it sends the reader to the Booking links card to choose one. With no active link, or links that could not be read, it says so. It never chooses a link for the reader (`shareTarget`).
- **Chip row: Upcoming · Past · Cancelled.** Each chip has a count taken from every booking. A failed read shows no counts at all, never zeros. This replaces PR4's `Two weeks · Month · Past sessions · Unpaid held`. `profile_zone_filters.test.mjs` reads the row from the v2 canvas.
  - **Past means time that has passed.** A booking is Past once its end, or its start when no end is recorded, is at or before now. It is also Past once it was closed as held or no-show. Past is never a wider forward window. A started session that has not ended stays Upcoming.
  - **Cancelled** holds every cancelled booking, whatever its date. A request the user declined reads "Declined".
  - A booking with no time stays Upcoming until it is closed, so it cannot drop out of sight.
- **Session card:** client · topic, then "Today · 14:00–15:00", then a status pill.
  - The terms line shows the offer the booking was made on ("Strategy Session · 60 min"), or "Session type not recorded" for an older booking.
  - The price is taken from what is recorded on the booking:
    - **Priced** shows "$250 recorded price".
    - **Free** is a recorded $0.
    - **Included** is D5316's state, "Included in an engagement · not billed".
    - **Unpriced** shows "No price recorded". A missing price is never Free.
  - Billed, Collected and Written off are a separate money pill.
  - Actions: **Open** on every card. A confirmed card also has **Complete**, **No-show** and **Cancel session**. A completed card has **Review it**.
- **Open** shows the client, the terms the session was booked on and what it was worth.
  - On a confirmed or completed session it also shows the price record: `BillingEditor`, with #5358's confirmed reprice and clear steps unchanged.
  - **New:** an **Included in an engagement** control, offered only on a line that has no price. It sends `{ billing_state: 'included' }`. The refusals `included_has_no_price` (400), `priced_line` (409) and `included_line` are shown as fixed sentences.
- **Your hours**, below the feed: the published slot grid, its four tiles and the publish card.
  - The grid has its own window control: Two weeks, Month, **Past hours** (PR4's "Past sessions", renamed because Past is now the sessions' tab) and Unpaid held.
  - A blocked hour still shows its private reason on the grid.
- **Scheduling setup**, a separate section with its own heading, containing three cards:
  - **Availability** lists Weekly schedule, Minimum notice, Buffers, Blackouts and Session limits. Weekly schedule reads "Not recorded", because no weekly pattern is stored: hours are the slots that are published.
  - **Session types** and **Booking links** are the D571 cards. The booking links card shows opens and attributed bookings, with "Not recorded" where the Worker has none.
- **Availability editor (PX-05 B):** Edit availability can open before the rules have been read.
  - Until they arrive, the editor shows a skeleton and "Loading your current schedule… Save stays disabled until it has loaded, so defaults can't replace it", with Save availability disabled.
  - Once the rules are read, the form holds them, and saving sends the revision it read (D4713).
  - When the read fails there is no editor, and the card says why.
- **390px:** cards stack, actions wrap, every control this page draws is at least 44px tall below `sm`, and nothing scrolls sideways. Dark mode uses the existing tokens.

### The lifecycle did not change

`SessionLifecyclePanel` now draws the session cards for one tab. Its write path is the same code as before: only a confirmed session can be closed, a cancellation needs a reason because the founder is told, a 409 means the session moved and offers a reload, and any other failure says the session is unchanged. The rules themselves stay with #4692, #4712 and #4713. The only change is a label: "Session held" is now **Complete**, as on the canvas. The resulting status is still shown as "Held".

### Departures from the canvas

- **The current advisor shell is kept, not a Practice v2 shell**, as in D4833 and D4834.
- **Cancel and No-show are buttons on the card**, not a "⋯" menu. That gives three taps instead of four on a phone, and no hidden menu.
- **Export to calendar, the four hours tiles, the slot grid and the publish card stay**, below the feed. The v2 canvas moves health figures to the Overview cockpit, which is not built yet. They are kept rather than lost.
- **Weekly schedule is "Not recorded"**, and the canvas's "Mon–Thu · 10:00–17:00" is not drawn: nothing stores it.
- **The session types card keeps its New type and Edit controls, and the booking links card keeps Copy, the enable switch and New link.** The canvas draws Edit on a link, but the Worker can only toggle a link, not rename it.
