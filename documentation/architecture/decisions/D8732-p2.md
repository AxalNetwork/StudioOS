### What is built

- **A booked row is one link** to `/calendar/item/advisor_booking/<id>`. Its state carries `from` (Studio's own address), `focus` (`today-row-<id>`), `scroll` (taken at the click) and `today` (who, time, day, date, read time, status, as Today read them). Its accessible name gives the time, person, topic and status: "Open session: 10:00 to 10:45, Maya Ruiz, Founder clinic, Confirmed". A click with a modifier key is left to the browser, so a new tab still works. Rows lead with time and person, so two sessions with the same topic on one day stay distinct.
- **Row words** follow SAV04: Confirmed (✓), Awaiting your answer (◷, for `pending` and `requested`), In progress · until 10:45 (◉), Cancelled (✕, action View). The detail line is topic · Advisor session · place. The client is named from the bookings read, or from the calendar copy's founder when only the calendar answered.
- **An open slot** is not a link. It is a group labelled "Open slot, 16:00 to 16:45, not booked" with the chip ◌ Not booked and one link, Manage availability, to `/practice/sessions`.
- **The day line** reads "Thu 26 Nov 2026 · From 09:10 · Berlin time (UTC+1)" in the viewer's zone, named once. Times use the 24-hour form of the drawer's `whenLine`, so a row and the drawer it opens give the same time.
- **States.** Partial keeps the rows that answered and names the gap, with the Partial chip and Retry after the rows, and "Reading slots…" while slots are still being read. Empty shows the day line, the sentence that every source answered, "Your next booking is on Tue 1 Dec." from the same bookings read, and Open calendar. When nothing came back and a source failed, the card shows Unavailable with Try again and "Open Thu 26 Nov in Calendar ›", never an empty day. Stale appears once the read is 30 minutes old (the clock ticks each minute and when the tab becomes visible). It shows "Stale · read 2 h 5 min ago", with Refresh before the rows. Refresh reads all three sources again and keeps the rows while it reads. The read line, "Read 09:10 · bookings, calendar and slots", closes the card.
- **Focus return (SAV03).** Close returns to `from` with `{ focus, scroll }`. Studio reads Today again on mount, as on main. Once the bookings read has settled, it restores the scroll and focuses the row, or the Today heading if the row has gone. This happens once per mount. Only a `today-row-<digits>` key is ever queried.
- **The drawer.** Opened from Today, it adds "See Thu 26 Nov in Calendar ›" under When (`/calendar/agenda?date=`). It adds "What <founder> wrote when booking. Nothing from their venture is shown here." under the notes. When the status has moved since Today's read, it shows "Changed since Studio read it" ("Studio showed this as Confirmed at 09:10. This is the current record."). On a 503 it keeps Try again and adds the dated fallback; on a 404 it keeps the not-party refusal and adds the same fallback. The fallback reads "From your Today list: Maya Ruiz · 10:00–10:45 · Thu 26 Nov", "What Studio read at 09:10. The session itself is not shown here.", then "Open Thu 26 Nov in Calendar ›" and Back to Studio. Opened from Calendar itself, the drawer is as it was.
- **Open booking** on an advisor's own session goes to `/practice/sessions?record=session&id=<id>`. The Practice focus target is now `session-card-<id>`, the node `SessionActions.jsx` draws, so the focus lands.

### Departures from the canvas

- **Loading** stays the tile's own skeleton with "Reading…", not "Reading your bookings, calendar and slots…" (SAV06A). A built Today in its loading state would draw Open calendar beside a list that is not there yet.
- **Partial chip.** SAV06B draws a Partial chip in the card header as well as in the note. The header holds the count note ("2 sessions · slots still reading"), and the chip appears once, in the note.
- **Place words** need the calendar copy, the only read that carries the meeting link (`GET /advisors/me/bookings` does not). When the calendar is unread, the row leaves the place out rather than claiming one.
- **The day link** is `/calendar/agenda?date=<day>` both under When and in the fallback. SAV02 annotates only `?date=`; SAV05A names Agenda.
- **The phone sheet** is the drawer's existing `Sheet` on a phone. No new Back control was added: Close is the way back, and it returns focus the same way. Where focus starts inside the drawer is the `Sheet`'s own behaviour, unchanged.
- **Words the canvas does not draw:** Completed and Status not recorded for a booked row; "Cancelled by you", "Cancelled by an administrator" and "Cancelled with its slot" beside the drawn "Cancelled by the founder". The `slot_cancelled` reason code is never quoted. Unavailable has a second sentence for when only some sources failed and the rest returned nothing.
- **The count note** is the same at 390 px as on the desktop ("1 open slot", where SAV07A has "1 slot"). The notes line keeps its second sentence on the phone.
- **Open slots:** every bookable slot left today is shown and counted. Main showed at most two.
- **Not checked in a browser.** The 1440 and 390 layouts, dark mode and the focus ring were built from the boards and tested through rendered markup, not looked at in a browser. No visual baseline covers Advisor Studio or the advisor drawer, so none changed.
