## D5511 — Review referral-cookie disclosures before publication (#5511)

The existing Privacy Policy v2 draft is the review copy for the cookie section. Its redline describes the live controls in the terms visitors see: Essential, Analytics and Marketing. The effective date stays pending owner and counsel approval.

- Essential storage supports sign-in, security and chosen settings, including cookie preferences.
- Analytics is optional. It measures signup steps and incoming links and uses a separate browser-tab ID for visits to active referral handles.
- Marketing is optional. The first referral cookie lasts up to 30 days for a code or 90 days for a handle from the first qualifying use. Withdrawal is available in the footer's Cookie preferences. It clears the relevant browser identifier, and signup remains available without either optional category.

Evidence: [D5509](./D5509.md), [the live Cookie preferences implementation](../../../frontend/src/lib/cookieConsent.js), and [the referral browser regression tests](../../../tests/e2e/referral-consent.spec.ts). The periods in this draft describe browser storage. The server-side inventory and retention evidence belong to [#5510](https://github.com/AxalNetwork/StudioOS/issues/5510).

The owner and counsel still need to approve the wording and effective date.
