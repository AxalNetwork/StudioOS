## D798 — The licence Administrator reads their contract, statements and payment instructions, signs a sent contract, and disputes a statement

- **Status:** decided by S1 for the owner, 2026-10-20 (#2231; part of D795, #2228, item D-c). It shows payment instructions, so S1 reviews it for security before the owner merges.
- **Basis:** `main` at `8902ea2c`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Builds on:**
  - D451: a licence contract's status follows its envelope.
  - D708: one resolver of payment instructions, masked.
  - D716: `paymentBlock`, the masked payment lines a fund notice prints.
  - Migrations 259 and 260: licence contracts, HQ escalations, statements and usage reports.

### What was there

A territory licence's Administrator could read the licence itself (`GET /api/licence/mine`), answer HQ's notices, and bind a host. HQ held their contract (`licence_contracts`) and their statements (`subsidiary_statements`), and only HQ could read either. Nothing told the Administrator what they owed, where to pay it, or how to sign the contract HQ had sent them.

### 1. Scope: the caller's own licence, always

Every route sits under `/api/licence/mine/*` in `routes/licence.ts`, and the work is in `services/licenceAccount.ts`.

- **The licence comes from `licence_admins`:** the caller's own row, as a principal or a delegate. No route takes a licence uid from the request.
- **A dispute's statement is looked up within that licence** (`uid = ? AND licence_uid = ?`). Another licence's statement therefore answers 404, exactly as an unknown one does.
- **A caller who administers nothing** gets 404 `no_licence`, as `/mine` does.

### 2. `GET /mine/contract` and its PDF

This follows the Licences canvas, boards L1 and L3, which S1 added to this PR on 2026-10-20.

- **The stored body.** `body_md` is the contract as it was instantiated, and it is never re-rendered: re-rendering from today's licence would show a contract nobody signed.
- **The status follows the envelope.** It is read through `enrichContractFromEnvelope`, now exported from `routes/admin_licences.ts`, so the Administrator reads the same status as HQ's console.
- **A draft is never shown.** This covers a draft HQ has drawn and not sent, and an envelope not yet sent, which reads as a draft. It is HQ's work in progress, like a draft statement.
- **`in_force`** is the latest signed contract.
  - HQ's draft supersedes every earlier row the moment it is drawn (`admin_licences.ts`). A signed contract therefore stays in force until a later one is signed, whether or not it is marked superseded.
  - Read literally, "the latest signed contract that is not superseded" would leave `in_force` empty in exactly the canvas's case: "Sent v6.1 · waiting for your signature / In force: v6.0".
- **`pending`** is the latest contract that is sent and not superseded. A sent contract that HQ has since redrawn is not waiting.
- **`earlier`** lists every other contract HQ has sent, each with its own stored body and `superseded_at`.
- **`sign_url`** is the caller's own signing link: the envelope's recipient row whose `user_id` is the caller. It is given only for `pending`, only while that row is pending, and only while its link has not expired. Otherwise it is null, and `sign_reason` says why:
  - "Not sent yet";
  - "HQ sent it to <the recipient's name>";
  - "Signed";
  - "The signing link has expired. Ask HQ to send it again.";
  - "HQ has sent no signing link for it. Ask HQ." (a contract marked sent before envelopes carried it);
  - "HQ has not drawn a contract for this licence yet".
- **A delegate never receives the principal's token.**
- **`GET /mine/contract/:uid/pdf`** prints one visible contract from its stored body through the legal paper (`paperModel` with `preserveBody`, then `renderLegalPdf`).
  - The pdf-lib fallback draws it when Browser Run is unbound.
  - A superseded contract prints as superseded by the current version.
  - A draft, another licence's contract or an unknown uid answers 404. A failed render answers 503. On a branch it answers 501.

### 3. `GET /mine/statements`

- **Which statements:** only `issued`, `paid` and `void` ones. A `draft` is HQ's work in progress and is never sent.
- **What is never sent:** `drawn_by_user_id`, `paid_by_user_id`, `paid_note` and `dispute_note`.
- **Streams pass through as stored** (`streams_json`), so the fourth stream D802 (#2238) draws, `platform_licence_royalties`, arrives as HQ wrote it.
- **Usage reports** for this licence are grouped by period.
- **The payment reference** is `AXAL-LIC-<licence_ref>-<period>`, given on each statement as `payment_reference`. It is computed, never stored.
- **`usage`** gives each period's report status. The periods are every period a statement covers or a report was sent for, newest first.
  - A period with a report is `sent`, with the date HQ last received it.
  - Otherwise, once D797's `usage_report_day` of the month after the period has passed, it is `late`. Before then it is `due`, with `due_on`.
  - With no report day recorded, an unsent period is `not_sent`, with `late: null` and `report_day_reason`. A missing report is never guessed to be late.
  - `periodEnds` reads both `YYYY-Qn` and `YYYY-MM`. A branch pushes quarters today (`services/usageReport.ts`, D266), while the canvas draws months, so the status follows whichever grain is stored.
- **`send_now`** is false on HQ, with `send_now_reason`: only a branch reports.
  - On a branch, the statements read answers `available: false` with `usage.send_now: true`.
  - `POST /mine/usage/send` runs the branch's own push (`pushUsageReport`) on demand, and answers 502 with its reason when the push cannot be sent. On HQ it answers 501 `usage_branch_only`.
- **The annual fee invoices** (`fee_invoices`) are D802's (#2238). Whichever PR merges second adds them to this read.

### 4. The payment instructions

- **A new purpose, `territory_licence_fee`,** paid to Holdings (`PAYMENT_PURPOSES` in `services/payInstructions.ts`). It needs no migration, because purposes are stored as JSON on `entity_bank_accounts`. It has no #1772 money kind yet.
  - The bank-account console offers it for Holdings.
  - D717's fund structure now lists it among what Holdings receives. No flow draws it, because no money kind is paid under it.
- **The block is `paymentBlock`:** the beneficiary, the bank, the routing number, SWIFT and "Account ending 1234", with `PAY_WARNING`. The full number is never decrypted on this path.
- **When no active Holdings checking account carries the purpose,** `payment` is the resolver's refusal sentence and its code. There are no lines.
- **Adding that account is a Super Admin step,** on the bank-account registry. Until it is done, every Administrator reads the refusal. S1 adds the step to #1008.
- **The block names the payee:** `method` "Bank transfer", `payee_entity` and `payee_name`. The canvas draws "[IBAN] · [BIC]", but the registry carries no IBAN. The block shows the account fields an account does carry, masked, and requires no IBAN.
- **The payee is the licence's IP licensor:** D797's `licensor_ip_entity`, which is Holdings until D797 records one. The purpose has one payee, Holdings. A licence whose terms name another IP licensor gets a refusal sentence (`licensor_not_payee`) rather than an account that is not its licensor's. The check is `licensorRefusal` in `services/licenceAccount.ts`.
  - **Not wired yet.** D797 (#2230, migration 512, `licence_terms`) is not on `main`. Reading a table nothing creates would fail `check-sqlite-tables`. `licenseeStatements` therefore takes `{ licensorIpEntity, usageReportDay }` as an argument, and the route passes none today.
  - **Tested by injection.** The tests inject both values.
  - **Wiring is one read in the route,** once D797 merges.

### 5. `GET /mine/needs`

This is the Summary's "Needs you" list, and the count pill on the sidebar row, from one read. Items come in a fixed order, each with `kind`, `title`, `link`, `ref` and `due`:

1. **A contract waiting for the caller's own signature.** It appears only when `sign_url` would be given, so a delegate who is not the recipient is not asked.
2. **HQ's notices waiting for the caller's answer** (`admin_notices`, status `issued` or `overdue`), soonest deadline first. A store that cannot be read gives a reason, not an empty list.
3. **Issued statements not yet paid** (`paid_cents < owed_cents`).
4. **On a branch only:** each seat type at 90% or more of its seats licensed. The count is the branch's own (D127), through `branchLicencePayload`. On HQ, where seats used is not counted, `reasons.seats` says so. On a branch, the contract and statements give their reason instead.

### 6. Disputing a statement

`POST /mine/statements/:uid/dispute` takes `{ cents, reason }`.

- **Validation:**
  - `cents` must be a positive whole number, no more than the statement's `owed_cents`.
  - `reason` must be 10 to 2,000 characters.
  - Anything else is 400 `invalid_dispute`, naming the `field`.
- **Which statements:** an `issued` or `paid` statement may be disputed. A `void` one answers 409 `not_disputable`. A `draft` answers 404, because the licensee cannot see it.
- **What it records:** a request to HQ, as an `hq_escalations` row with these fields:
  - `branch_code` `hq` (D795 D-d: an HQ-hosted Administrator writes HQ's own lane);
  - kind `other`;
  - subject "Statement dispute <period>";
  - `subject_ref` set to the licence uid;
  - the statement, the amount and the reason in `detail`;
  - due in 72 hours (`SLA_HOURS.other`).
- **`raised_by_branch_user_id`** is the caller's id. With `branch_code = 'hq'`, that id is in HQ's own user space.
- **Notices:** every Super Admin gets a `statement_dispute` notice (category `compliance`, linking to `/admin/licences`).
- **It never writes `disputed_cents` or any other statement field.** Those stay HQ's to record. A test compares the whole row before and after.
- **When D799 (#2232) merges,** its own escalation kind replaces `other`.

### 7. On a branch

Contracts and statements live on HQ, and `rpc/hqOps.ts` has no read for either. No branch is deployed today, so the branch path gives the reason only:

- **A non-admin** is refused (403) first, as `/mine` refuses.
- **An admin's reads** answer `{ available: false, reason: "Read on HQ; this branch has no copy yet" }`, with no figures. The statements read adds `usage.send_now: true`.
- **The contract PDF** answers 501 `contract_hq_only`.
- **A dispute** answers 501 `statement_hq_only`.
- **`needs`** gives the branch's own notices and seat types, as §5 says.

### Frontend

The methods are `licenceAccountApi.contract()`, `.contractPdfBlob(uid)`, `.statements()`, `.needs()`, `.sendUsage()` and `.disputeStatement(uid, cents, reason)`, in `frontend/src/lib/api/licenceAccount.js`, re-exported from `api.js`. This task adds no UI.

`cloudflare-worker/test/licence_account_d798.test.ts` has 17 tests on node:sqlite. They use the baseline's own DDL and migrations 256, 257, 259, 260, 264, 265, 284 and 472, read from their files. The bank account's number is drawn at run time, and the people are placeholders. The tests cover:

- a principal and a delegate reading their own licence and never another's;

- in force, pending and earlier through a redraft, a signature and a new draft, with no draft ever shown;
- `sign_url` given only to the recipient, only while the contract is sent and the link is unexpired;
- the contract PDF, and its 404s;
- a hidden draft statement, HQ-only fields never sent, and streams passed through as stored;
- the masked payment block, its payee and warning, and the full number never sent;
- the refusal sentence when no account carries the purpose;
- the licensor refusal;
- usage report status, both without a report day and with an injected one, by month and by quarter;
- Send now on HQ and on a branch;
- needs on HQ and on a branch, including seats at 90% and an unreadable notice store;
- a dispute writing an escalation and a notice and leaving the statement unchanged;
- dispute refusals;
- the branch path.

41 mutations were made, each breaking one of these rules, and every one was caught. `pay_instructions_d708` and `fund_structure_d717` now expect the new purpose.
