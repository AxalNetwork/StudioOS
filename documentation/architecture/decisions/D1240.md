### 1. What is read

| Block | Read |
|---|---|
| Mine: the board and the table | `GET /api/deals/flow`: the firm's own rows, each with its stage, owner, how it entered, days in stage and `can_open` |
| All deals | `GET /api/deals`: every listed round, joined to the flow row when the firm has one. The flow carries no currency or instrument, so each row takes them from this list. |
| Scores | `POST /api/venture-scores/batch`, in chunks of 200 deal ids |
| Fit to my thesis | `GET /api/deals/fit?ids=`, in chunks of 50 |
| Your weighting | `GET /api/venture-scores/weights` |
| Saved views | `GET`, `POST`, `PATCH` and `DELETE` on `/api/deals/views` |

**Each read answers alone.**
- A failed scores read leaves names, stages and money as they are. It says "Scores could not be read. Names, stages and money are current." with Try again, and every score cell reads "—".
- A failed fit read makes each fit cell read "Unreadable".
- A failed views read is said inside the Saved views menu.

### 2. A score is a band, never a number

**The batch.** D1256's batch gives, per scope, its state, a band (Strong, Fair, Weak) and a confidence word, and never a value.
- **The canvas departs from this.** DLS1 and DLS2 draw figures ("Team 78"). Here a cell says the band with its confidence ("Strong · High"), and Risk reads as a risk word ("Low risk").
- **The scorecard strip** draws a band's height, not a figure.
- **A test checks** that no score cell carries a digit.

**Every missing score is a word:**
- Not scored (the batch returned no row for the scope);
- Not enough recorded;
- Not expected yet;
- Unreadable;
- Locked (the founder's switch is off, with its reason);
- Not shared (the deal is one the investor may not read, so the batch left it out, and a line under the table says why);
- Reading… while the read is out.

**There is no Overall.** The eleven scopes are SCORING's, in its order.

**Sort by a scope** ranks the band, then its confidence. "Sort by confidence" ranks confidence first. Unscored and locked rows sort last whichever way the direction points (the issue's test).

**"Your weighting"** is the weighted mean of the scored scopes' band ranks, using the investor's 0–5 weights (equal when none are set). It orders rows and never makes a score.

**Fit to my thesis** is the investor's own comparison (D1239), not one of SCORING's scopes, so it keeps its number with its confidence ("86 · High").
- A measured 0 stays 0.
- **No thesis:** "Set your thesis", linking to the account page. No page edits a thesis yet; see §6.
- **A deal the investor may not read:** "—", with the reason as its tooltip.

### 3. The board (DLS1, DLS15, DLS18b)

**The columns** are the firm's stages, Sourced to Closing. Each shows its count and its asks:
- in one currency;
- or "Asks in n currencies";
- and "n with none recorded".

A currency is never guessed. **Passed** is a column only when the filters ask for it.

**A card** carries:
- the venture, linked to the deal only where `can_open` says so;
- the round's word and how it entered;
- the ask and instrument ("Ask not recorded" when there is none);
- the three card scopes and the fit;
- days in stage and the owner's initials;
- a Compare tick and Move to.

**Moving a card.**
- **How:** drag it onto another column, or use Move to, which is also the keyboard's way and the phone's.
- **The write:** `PATCH /api/deals/flow/:dealId { stage }`. The card takes the stage the Worker answers with.
- **What the page says:** "Moved [venture] to [stage]." with Undo. Undo moves it back once.
- **A refusal** stays on the page in the Worker's words, and nothing moves. For example: "Closing follows a commitment".
- **Passed is never a target.** A pass takes its reason (D1238 §3); that is part 5's dialog.

**Under All deals** the board holds only the rounds in the flow. A line says the others are in the Table.

**Today's zone board and deal rooms** stay, folded under the board: "The studio's rounds by status, and your deal rooms". Nothing an investor used is lost.

**Loading (DLS15):** the five columns with "Reading…", the toolbar drawn at once.

**Phone (DLS18b):** below 1024 px (`usePhoneWidth`), one stage at a time with its count on each tab, no dragging, and Move to.

### 4. The table (DLS2, DLS2b, DLS18a)

**The columns:**
- the compare tick and Venture, which stay put while the rest scrolls sideways;
- My stage, Round, Ask and Scorecard (the strip);
- each of the eleven scopes;
- Fit, with the thin sky edge;
- Days in stage, Owner, and Entered (how it entered, and when).

**The head:**
- every scope head, and Fit, Days, Ask and Venture, sorts on a press;
- a scope's ≥ steps its minimum from Any to Fair or better to Strong, and back.

**Paging and confidence:** pages of 25. "Show confidence as words" turns the confidence word into a dot that keeps its label.

**A round not in the flow** reads "Not on your deals" with **Add to my deal flow**. That opens a dialog that asks for the stage and posts `POST /api/deals/flow { deal_id, stage }`. The dialog says three things:
- Closing follows a commitment;
- the round itself does not change;
- adding a round does not open it, which needs an invitation or a commitment.

**Phone (DLS18a):** the table as cards, with the card scopes.

### 5. Filters, sort, columns, card scopes and saved views (DLS3–DLS5)

**Everything lives in the address**, so a link reopens the view:

| Address | What |
|---|---|
| `?v=` | The saved view |
| `q` | The search |
| `stage`, `round`, `via`, `sector`, `owner` | The filters |
| `min_<scope>` | Fair or Strong |
| `conf` | Medium or High |
| `unscored=1` | Include deals not scored on those scopes |
| `sort`, `dir`, `mode` | The sort |
| `cols`, `cards` | The table's columns and the card scopes |

The defaults are left out, so `/deals` is still Board, Mine, sorted by Team. Unknown values are dropped, never guessed.

**The Filters drawer (DLS4),** 480 wide:
- **What it filters:** My stage (Passed included), Round status, How it entered, Sector (from the rows read), Owner (Anyone, Me, Unassigned), each scope's minimum band, a minimum confidence, and "Include deals not scored on these scopes".
- **Show** counts what it would show.
- **The chips** under the toolbar each remove one filter, and there is Clear all.

**Sort, Columns and Card scopes (DLS3)** are popovers:
- Columns has Reset to default;
- Card scopes allows three, in SCORING's order.

**Saved views (DLS5):**
- **The menu** lists Mine and My firm.
- **A view's spec** is D1239's: `version`, `view`, `scope`, `filters`, `sort` and `columns` (the card scopes ride in `columns.cards`), with lower-case keys only, as its validator requires.
- **Picking a view** writes its settings and uid into the address. A change after that shows "• edited", with Save changes (`PATCH`, with the version the page holds) and Save as new.
- **Save view** asks for a name. It lists what the view keeps and who sees it; My firm is offered only when the read says `can_share_with_firm`. It also offers "Make it my default".
- **A saved default** applies only when the address names no setting of its own.
- **Delete** is the author's only, in a dialog. It says that no deal, stage or score changes.
