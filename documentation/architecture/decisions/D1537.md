### 2. Grid health: per element and model

**Source:** the run steps (`deck_area_batch_steps`), over 7, 30 or 90 days. Only finished steps count: done or failed.

| Column | From |
|---|---|
| Steps, error rate | failed over done plus failed |
| p95 | the 95th percentile (nearest rank) of done steps' seconds from start to end, against §5.9.6's target: text 5 s, image 10 s |
| Cost | the steps' charged microdollars |
| Fallbacks | steps that ran on a fallback model |
| Drift | the routed model is no longer in the catalogue, or no longer marked as served (`ga` or `partner`) |
| Eval score | **not recorded.** No store holds an evaluation score yet, so it reads Not recorded rather than a made-up figure. |

The kill switch stays on AI models (D4251). The page also shows today's ceiling from D1510's read: "Not set" until the owner sets it.

### 3. The error budget and its alert

- **The target:** §5.9.6 is "99% of batches succeed over 28 days".
- **What counts:** a batch counts once it has finished, done or failed. A cancelled batch is the founder's choice, not a failure.
- **The budget** is 1% of the window's finished batches. It is computed in whole percent, so 1% of 400 is 4 and not 3.999….
- **The read** gives the success rate, the share of the budget the window used, and the last hour's failures as a share of the budget.

**The alert.** At :17 each hour on HQ, `noteErrorBudgetAlert` runs. When the last hour burned a quarter of the budget or more (at least one failure), each active HQ admin gets one in-app notice and one email (`deck_error_budget_alert`), linking to Grid health.
- **Once per UTC hour:** the hour is claimed in `job_idempotency` (`deck-error-budget:<hour>`), as D1510's ceiling alerts are.
- **Category:** `system`, so quiet hours and the digest apply.
- **Never fails the tick.**
- **The cron's import** is `services/deckErrorBudgetCron.ts`, a re-export. `mercury_member_privacy_d752` counts every word in the scheduled handler that names one of `index.ts`'s imports, so a `decks/` path there would read as the decks router, which reaches member bank data. The re-export's own graph reaches none.

**Departure from §5.9.6.** The plan said the alert would go "through the `studioos-tail` consumer". That Worker only writes logs to R2: it has no database and cannot notify anyone. The burn is counted from the batches in D1 instead, on the main Worker's existing cron, with no new binding. The tail consumer is unchanged.

### 4. The funnel

| KPI (§5.9.6) | From |
|---|---|
| Decks with at least 3 area generations | `deck_area_proposals` in the window, per deck |
| Quote conversion by price band | `deck_run_quotes` in the window: shown is a quote, confirmed is a quote used. The bands: under $0.10, $0.10 to $0.50, $0.50 to $2, $2 and over. |
| Accept rate per element and model | D1510's `acceptRates`, with each model's label |
| Time from draft to first share | per venture whose first deck was made in the window: the median hours from that first deck to its first share link |
| 4-week retention | ventures whose first area generation was 4 to 8 weeks ago, and of them those that generated again 28 days or more after it |

Each KPI is read on its own, so one that fails leaves the others. The plan's Analytics Engine funnel points (D1510 §3) are not read here: reading them needs the account's SQL API, and every KPI above is in D1.

### 5. Not built here

- **An eval score store.** The evaluation harness that would write one is not on `main`.
- **Per-surface funnel steps** that only Analytics Engine records: area selection, lightbox open, compare, undo. These wait on a reader of the `studioos_metrics` dataset.
