### 1. Status and assignee are fields of the card

`services/decks/slideModel.ts` keeps two more fields on a card, through `sanitizeModel` (which `sanitizeSlides` calls for every card):
- `status`: `todo`, `in_progress` or `done` (To do, In progress, Done);
- `assignee`: a person's user id.

Each is kept only when set, so a card saved before E6 hashes as it did. `modelProblems` refuses a save that carries an unknown status ("Card n: the status is To do, In progress or Done.") or an assignee that is not a positive id ("Card n: the assignee must be a person on the deck."), rather than trimming it.

They are the venture's working state. `publicDeckOf` drops both, so the share link, the print page and a deal's deck grant never carry them.

**Why no table.** A status is a fact about one card of one version, and a version is already the document that holds its cards. Columns would need their own migration, a second writer and a rule for which version a status belongs to. In the card document, Set status and Assign are edits like any other: held on screen until Save, and written with the version.

### 2. Tracked decks

A deck no card of which carries a status has never been tracked. None of its cards is "not Done", the strip draws no pill and no filter card, and Share opens no warning. Once any card has a status, the deck is tracked, and a card without one reads To do.

Without this rule, every deck saved before E6 would warn before every share and show "[n] cards not Done" on the home card. The same rule is in `services/decks/cardStatus.ts` (`cardStates`) and in `frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/cardStatusModel.js` (`statusOf`).

### 3. Who can be assigned

D1570's people rule, unchanged: the venture's founder users, its accepted owner and cofounder members, and an advisor or Operating Partner with a live `scope_deck` grant. Never an Admin.

`peopleOf` moved from `routes/deck_comments.ts` to `services/decks/deckPeople.ts`, word for word, so the comments route and the card assignee read one rule.

`PUT /api/decks/:id` checks each **new** assignment against it before anything is written: 400 `assignee_not_on_deck`, "Card n: assign someone who can work on this deck. Nothing was saved." An assignee kept from the version before is not checked again, so a person whose grant ended stays on their card and never blocks a save.

### 4. One notice per assignment

`newAssignments(before, after)` names, by card uid, each card whose assignee is set and differs from the same card's on the version the save replaces. A card moved, or saved again with the same assignee, is not new.

After `insertVersion` succeeds, `notifyAssignments` sends one notice per such card through `notify()`, the path D1570's mentions use:
- type `deck_card_assigned`, category `mentions`;
- "[name] assigned you card [n]." with "Open the deck to work on the card.";
- like the mention notice, no words of the deck;
- never to the person who made the assignment;
- a failed notice never fails the save.

**What makes it once.** `insertVersion` is a conditional batch on the version the editor read, so of two saves from the same version only one writes, and only that one notifies. A second save compares against the version the first wrote, where the assignee is no longer new. There is no claim row, as D1570's mentions have, because the version itself is the record.

### 5. The read

`GET /api/decks/:id/card-status?status=all|todo|in_progress|done&assignee=anyone|none|<user id>` (`routes/deck_card_status.ts`, mounted after `deck_comments.ts` and before `decks.ts`) answers:

| Key | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `deck_id`, `version` | The version read. |
| `tracked` | Whether any card carries a status. |
| `filter` | The filter applied. |
| `total` | Every card on the version. |
| `cards` | Exactly the cards matching both the status and the assignee, each with its place, uid, title, status and assignee `{ id, name }`. |
| `not_done` | `{ count, cards }`: the cards To do or In progress, whatever the filter. |
| `people` | Who a card can be assigned to. |

An assignee who is no longer among the people keeps their name from their account. A bad filter is 400 `invalid_filter`. Who reads: whoever may read the deck (`projectOwned`, as `GET /:id`). It writes nothing.

### 7. The screens

All on S1's files, with no new screen.

**`CardsStrip.jsx`**
- Each card carries its status pill and its assignee's avatar: Done positive (emerald), In progress a violet outline, To do neutral. The avatar shows initials and is named in full on hover and to a screen reader.
- The filter card sits above the cards on a tracked deck, or one with an assignee: the segment All · To do · In progress · Done, "Assignee: anyone ▾" (anyone, unassigned, each person) and "[3] of [12] cards".
- The filter runs `cardStatusModel.filterCards`, the Worker's rule, over the cards on screen, so an unsaved status filters at once. Each card keeps its number from its place on the deck.

**The card menu**
- It leads with Set status › and Assign ›, as pd34 draws. Each opens its list in place: the three statuses, or Unassigned and each person, with the current one checked, and ‹ Back.
- A pick is a card edit held until Save. The save dialog says it on its own line ("the status becomes Done", "assigned to someone new, who is told when you save"), never "its words change".
- Assign › is greyed, with its reason, until the people are read.

**`StudioHome.jsx`** draws the deck card's amber "[n] cards not Done" beside Current, from the version's card-status read. It draws nothing while reading, at 0 or on an untracked deck. A failed read says "Card status could not be read." with Try again, and never shows a count.

**`ShareDialog.jsx`** reads the card status beside D1570's approval, for its one pre-share confirm (`commentsModel.preShareGate(approval, cards)`):
- With cards not Done, the confirm shows the amber inset "[n] cards are not Done. Share anyway?", listing each card's number, title and status.
- **Review cards** closes the dialog and selects the first of them in the editor.
- **Share** passes the warning.
- An approver who has not signed off still holds Share in the same confirm. The not-Done line never does.
- An unread card status says "Which cards are not Done could not be read. You can still share." and lets Share through.

### 8. Departures

- **The confirm's button reads "Share", not "Share anyway".** pd34 draws "Share anyway?" as the inset's question and **Share** as the button. D1570's button was labelled "Share anyway". It keeps its test id (`pitch-share-anyway`) and its hold.
- **The strip's filter runs on the screen, the route's on the saved version.** The issue asks for a filter read, and the route is it. The strip filters the cards on screen, so that a status set but not yet saved is filtered at once. Both run the same rule, and each has its own test with the same six cards.
- **Status and assignee are written by saving a version.** There is no write route. A status change makes a new version, so an approver's sign-off (D1570, which is of one version) waits again. That follows from the card document, and the issue keeps them there ("migration: none").
- **Duplicate copies a card's status and assignee.** The duplicate is a new card (its own uid), so saving it tells the assignee once, about the new card.
- **Pd34's 260 px strip.** The strip stays S1's 150 px column (and 120 px row on a tablet). The filter card's segment wraps to two rows in it.
