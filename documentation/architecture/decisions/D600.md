## D600 — The ticket mirror names nobody

**Issue #1106, slot S01, part of #1101 (the support-ticket epic),
2026-10-06.** No migration and no new route. This builds the owner's answer
to D540's first question.

### What was true on main

- **D540 Q1 B** (the owner, on #1102): tickets stay in this public
  repository, and the mirror strips the submitter's identity. The answer was
  to land in #1105 and #1106. Neither did it.
- **Three places sent GitHub a member's identity,** all in
  `routes/tickets.ts`:
  - a new ticket's issue body ended "Submitted by: <name>";
  - an admin's backfill of a saved ticket ended "Submitted by:
    <submitted_by>". That column holds the member's name or, when the name is
    empty, the email address;
  - a reply posted from StudioOS was signed "Comment by <name or email>".
- **Nothing had leaked.** Five issues carry the old line: the owner's own
  tickets and test accounts. No reply had been signed.

### The change

- **Two builders in `services/githubSync.ts`** are the only text the ticket
  routes add to what the member wrote. Neither takes a name or an email:
  - `mirrorIssueBody(ticket, source)` writes the description, then
    Priority, Type, Source and the sync marker;
  - `mirrorReplyBody(body, from, ticketId)` signs a reply "Reply from the
    member who filed this ticket" or "Reply from the Axal team", then adds the
    marker.
- **The create route, the backfill and the reply route use them.** A reply's
  side is whose ticket it is: the member who filed it, or anyone else, which
  the route's own 403 limits to an admin. An admin replying on a ticket they
  filed is its member.
- **The platform still knows who filed each ticket.** `submitted_by` and
  `user_id` are unchanged, and so is the Slack notice to the team's own
  workspace, which is not public.

### What the public issue still carries

The member's own title, description and replies. D540 Q1 B kept tickets in
this repository, so what a member writes is public by that decision. A
security-related ticket goes to a private security advisory, never to an
issue (D543).

### Verified

`cloudflare-worker/test/ticket_mirror_identity_d600.test.ts` captures every
request the routes send GitHub. It checks that none carries the name or the
email of any of three accounts: a member, a member with no name (whose
`submitted_by` is the email), and an admin. It covers:
- a new ticket;
- an admin's backfill;
- four replies: two members, an admin on a member's ticket, and an admin on
  their own ticket;
- the two builders' exact output and their arguments.

Mutation checks: 9 of 9 caught. Each one put back a single old behaviour:
- a name in the create body;
- `submitted_by` in the backfill;
- a name or an email in a reply;
- every reply signed as the member;
- the side decided by role instead of by owner;
- the old "Comment by" line;
- the Source line dropped;
- an identity argument on a builder;
- the old create line itself.
