## D4607 — A Research detail editor answers only the record its route names (#4607)

#4607 (Research audit 2/5, slot S07, taken at the owner's request).

### The problem

A detail page reads its record when the route opens it. On a move from record A to record B (another analysis, or another company inside the same analysis), A's read was still in flight. Nothing dropped it, so a late A could be drawn on B's route, and Save then sent A's fields to B. A late failed A could replace B with an error. Existing press guards (D778 part 9) covered writes, not the initial reads.

### The rule

- **Every read carries a ticket** (`frontend/src/pages/research/recordGate.js`, `createRecordGate()`). A newer read, or leaving the page, makes every older ticket stale, and a stale read's success, failure and `finally` are all dropped.
- **The loaded record carries its identity** (`recordKey` of the route parameters that name it). A page draws its editor, and writes, only while that identity is the one the route names (`sameRecord`), and a write is sent to the loaded identity.
- **The previous record's editor goes with it.** On a route change, the record, the draft fields, the notice, the preview and any half-filled add form are cleared before the next record loads, so nothing typed on A can reach B.
- Writes still pass the same per-press guards as before; an unrelated refresh of the same record does not clear a draft.

### Where

`CompanyAnalysis` and `CompanyCandidate` (the audit's reproduction), and the same read pattern in `FundDossier` and `BenchmarkDetail` (editors that also gain a write guard), `MarketReading` (create and attach refuse a reading the route no longer names) and the two diligence pages (read-only).

Tests: `frontend/test/research_record_identity_4607.test.mjs` mounts the real pages and answers reads out of order.
