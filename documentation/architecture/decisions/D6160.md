## D6160 — Investor › Deals › Round calls: the list built to its canvas, with company and own-response fields (#6160)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-15 to the Deals v2 Round Calls canvas (D6159, `design/incoming/Deals v2 Round Calls Page.dc.html` and its `· Boards` file): DCA16 (the list), DCA17 (empty), DCA18 (the Other deal activity entry on `/deals`), DCA20's list read states and DCA21a/DCA21d (phone). Under #4815. One call (DCA19, DCA21b, DCA21c), Needs your attention items, Eadwyn's nudge and the dark board's token pass are not part of this change.
- **Worker:** `GET /api/round-calls` for `users.role = 'investor'` now returns `services/roundCalls.ts` `investorList`: per call the uid, name, instrument, target (`amount_cents`), minimum cheque, target close, recorded state, `effective_state`, `sent_at`, `closed_at`, the company's name only (as `investorPage` reads it), and `mine` — the caller's own `opened_at`, `commitment_cents`, `data_room_requested_at`, `question_count` and `answered_count`. Every `mine` field is read from the caller's recipient row and the caller's questions only. Drafts are never listed. `cloudflare-worker/test/round_calls_investor_list_d6160.test.ts` seeds a second recipient with an indication, a data-room request and questions on the same calls and asserts none of it reaches the first investor's reply.
- **Effective state** (`roundCallPolicy.ts` `effectiveCallState`): `awaiting_counsel` reads Paused; `sent` and not past its close date reads Open (the rule `writableCall` already applies to writes); everything else, a sent call past its close date included, reads Closed. That is D6159's Q4 as drawn (option A); the one-call page already read an expired call as Closed.
- **Frontend:** `pages/investor/RoundCallIndex.jsx` (the list) and `pages/investor/roundCallsModel.js` (its words and rules, pure). `App.jsx` renders `InvestorDealsRoutes` at `/round-calls` for an investor; that shell titles the page Round calls, lights no pill and passes `bucketPath="/deals"` to `WorkspaceShell` so the crumb reads Deals ‹ Round calls. A founder's `?project_id` list keeps `founder/RoundCallPage.jsx` `RoundCallIndex`, unchanged in behaviour. `/round-calls/:uid` keeps its current chrome.
- **List:** filters Open, Paused, Closed, All and Needs my answer (Open with no indication), kept in the address as `?filter=`; Closed calls sort last, most recently closed first, the rest by close date soonest first. Each row names the company and shows the call, instrument, target, a chip with a word and a glyph (● Open, ‖ Paused, ■ Closed), the close-date words ("Closes in 4 days · 18 Nov", "Closed on 2 Nov", "Closed early by the founder"), the investor's own response — Indicated · amount or Not indicated, questions asked and answered, the data-room request — or the single line "No response recorded", and Open the call. New marks an Open call not yet opened. From 768px it is a table; below, whole-card links and a sideways-scrolling filter row, every target at least 44px.
- **States:** loading (aria-busy); 503 `round_calls_disabled` → Coming soon with Browse Deal flow; 403 `verified_investor_required` → Round calls are for verified investors, with Finish verification (`/kyc`); a failure or any 5xx → "Your round calls could not be read. That is not the same as having none." with Try again; any other refusal says it was refused; no calls → DCA17's three lines and Browse Deal flow.
- **`/deals`:** `otherActivityModel.js` `roundCallsItem` and `OtherDealActivity.jsx` add "Round calls · N open" (Open calls only) after the four zones, read only for an account whose role is investor. A failed read is Unreadable for that entry alone and joins the line's Try again. While the read is out, while calls are switched off (503 `round_calls_disabled`) and for an unverified investor (403 `verified_investor_required`) the entry is absent — never 0 and never Unreadable.
