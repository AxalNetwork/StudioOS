## D6172 — Source the deal page's Team blocks (#6172)

The owner's source-or-drop answers on #6172 govern the Team tab.

A roster start date does not record when a person became full time, and the
roster has no publication choice for that date. The fixed full-time field
and its line are removed.

Founder-market fit points to shared Team evidence. The response uses the
Evidence tab's current-source, audience, sharing and NDA rules, returning
counts, sources and dates for kinds that back Team. It assigns no individual
founder a fit claim. The tab links to Evidence.

Advisors come from active accounts' accepted, non-removed project memberships.
The read gives the count and membership roles. Advisor names and profiles
are excluded from the Team people projection.

A values band remains withheld until every founder has consent on record.
The response explains that condition. The response before the NDA is signed
keeps its existing public projection.
