### 1. Where it lives

| Address | What |
|---|---|
| `/deals` | Deal flow for an Investor: the Board view (DLS1), Mine |
| `/deals?view=table` | The table (DLS2) |
| `/deals?scope=all`, `?scope=new` | All deals; New for you (DLS6) |
| `/deals/compare?ids=` | Compare, up to four ventures (DLS8) |
| `/deals/screening`, `/commit`, `/closing`, `/pipeline` | Unchanged zone pages |
| `/deals` for an Admin or Operating Partner | Unchanged: `DealsPage`, until the Studio lens (part 6) |

- **The address carries the view.** The view and scope are query words, so a link reopens the same view. Defaults (Board, Mine) stay out of the address. Saved views and sort join the address in part 4.
- **One shell.** `InvestorDealsRoutes` renders `pages/investor/dealflow/DealFlow.jsx` at the root, titled "Deal flow" with the canvas's line. No zone pill is lit on the root or on Compare.
- **Today's board stays.** The Board view shows the investor's own stages as "Not read here yet", with today's zone board and deal rooms under it. Nothing an investor uses today is lost.

### 2. What is read, and from where

| Block (boards) | Read |
|---|---|
| The table's rows (DLS2) | `deals.ts` `GET /api/deals`, all or `scope=mine` |

| Compare's ventures (DLS8) | `GET /api/deals?scope=mine` for the picked ids |
| The investor's own stages, moves, passes (DLS1, DLS10) | none |
| Scores by scope with confidence (DLS1, DLS2, DLS8) | none: SCORING (#2428) |
| Fit to my thesis | none |
| Saved views, filters and sort by score (DLS3–DLS5) | none |
| Thesis matches, introductions, syndicates, watchlist (DLS6) | none wired to deals |

**The round's words are the canvas's.** `applied` In intake, `scored` Screened, `active` Raising, `funded` Funded, `rejected` Not raising. An intake "rejection" is therefore never drawn as a pass (owner question 7).

**A row opens its deal only where the investor may read it.** Under Mine (invited or committed, `deals.ts` canReadDeal) a row links to `/deals/:id`. Under All deals it does not. This is #2423's broken item 2 on the new table.

### 3. What is drawn today

- The toolbar: Mine, All deals, New for you; Board, Table. Each is an address.
- **Table:**
  - venture and sector, the round's word, the ask in its currency, days in stage;
  - a missing ask or start says Not recorded;
  - the Scorecard column says Not read here yet, under the scopes' names in SCORING's order;
  - ticking two to four rows opens Compare.
- **New for you:** the studio's pending invitations, each with its message and its door, then the other ways in waiting.
- **Compare:** the picked ventures on the list; fewer than two asks for more; one not on the list is counted; the chart waits.
- Saved views and fit wait below every view.
- **Departures, each a part's:**
  - the board's own stages, drag and the move dialogs;
  - filters, sort and columns;
  - the peek drawer;
  - the Studio lens;
  - the phone layout.

### 4. Who sees what

- Unchanged on the Worker: an Investor needs Professional for `/api/deals` (`requireInvestorTier`), and reads a deal only when invited or committed.
- An Admin or Operating Partner keeps `DealsPage` until part 6. An admin in Investor view sees this page.
- The new route `/deals/compare` admits `admin` and `investor`, as the zone routes do.

### 5. Owner decisions

#2423's eight questions take their recommended answers until the owner says otherwise:
1. Each investor firm keeps its own stage per deal. The round's status shows beside it, and an investor's pass is theirs alone.
2. All deals holds only ventures whose founder chose to raise (PIPE-RAISE), plus rounds the studio publishes.
3. Operating Partners get the Studio lens read-only, with moves on the rounds they lead.
4. `/deals` is Deal flow. `/deals/pipeline` will redirect to `/deals?view=table` (part 7). Screening, Commit and Closing stay.
5. Views are shared with Only me or My firm.
6. There is no Overall. An investor sorts by one scope, or by "Your weighting".
7. Intake rows read Not raising, never a pass.
8. Joined rooms follow DEAL's rule: invited or committed. A join becomes a request to be invited.
