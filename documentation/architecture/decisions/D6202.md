## D6202 — The support ledger's lifecycle: due dates, in progress and cancelled, and an introduction's meeting (#6202)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-15. Worker and migration only; the page is #5845 (PF-05).
- **Builds on:** the support ledger (migration 237), its revision guard and history (D4642, migration 202611092215), and its totals over the whole scope (D4647). D4817 marked these fields "backend required"; #4642 closed without them.

### What changes

- **States.** An entry is `promised`, `in_progress`, `delivered`, `withdrawn` or `cancelled`.
  - `promised` and `in_progress` are open. `delivered`, `withdrawn` and `cancelled` are terminal, as delivered and withdrawn already were.
  - A promise moves promised → in_progress → delivered, or from either open state to cancelled (or withdrawn, which stays for the rows that already use it).
  - In progress never moves back to promised: work that started is not un-started (409 `cannot_reopen`).
  - An entry may be logged as promised, in progress or delivered. It is never logged as already withdrawn or cancelled.
- **A due date.** `due_date` (YYYY-MM-DD) on POST and PATCH. `null` clears it. A malformed date is refused with a sentence, never dropped: a due date the store silently lost would read as "no due date".
- **An introduction's meeting.** `meeting_outcome` (`held` or `not_held`) and `meeting_at` (YYYY-MM-DD), on an `intro` entry only. Any other kind is refused 400. Recording the meeting moves no state.
- **Cancelling stamps its date** (`cancelled_at`), as delivering and withdrawing stamp theirs.
- **History.** Every move is one `portfolio_support_entry_events` row with its actor, in the same batch and under the same revision guard as D4642. Its `action` is the state moved to (`in_progress`, `delivered`, `withdrawn`, `cancelled`), or `amended`. A change to the due date or the meeting is an `amended` row naming those fields.
- **Totals.** Over the whole scope, as D4647 requires:
  - `in_progress`, `cancelled`, and `outstanding` (promised plus in progress).
  - `overdue`: open entries whose due date is before the Worker's own date, UTC, said in `overdue_basis`. An entry with no due date is never overdue, and neither is a delivered, withdrawn or cancelled one.
  - `by_kind`: for every kind, `entries`, `delivered`, `outstanding` and `meetings_held`, so "Introductions made" is a counted figure (`by_kind.intro.delivered`). A kind with no entries reads zeros.
  - A failed per-kind read makes the figures Unreadable, never zero.
  - `promised` now counts only `state = 'promised'`. Before, it counted everything not delivered or withdrawn, which was the same rows until this change.
  - The `state=` filter reaches each new state, and its `matching_total` follows.

### The migration

`202611150140_6202_support_lifecycle.sql`. Migration 237 put `CHECK (state IN ('promised','delivered','withdrawn'))` on the entries, and 202611092215 put the same vocabulary on the events. An ADD COLUMN cannot widen a CHECK, so both tables are rebuilt. The rebuild follows GOTCHAS:
- no transaction statements and no `foreign_keys` toggle (`PRAGMA defer_foreign_keys = TRUE` only);
- each table is recreated under its own name before the rows are copied back;
- the events table, which references the entries `ON DELETE CASCADE`, is copied aside and dropped first, so dropping the entries cannot cascade-delete the history.

Existing rows read unchanged: their states are all in the wider vocabulary, and every new column is NULL on them. The rehearsal on node:sqlite with foreign keys on kept every entry and event, found no foreign-key violation, refused an unknown state and a malformed date, and kept the cascade working.

### Tests

- `cloudflare-worker/test/support_lifecycle_6202.test.ts` covers each transition and its history row with the actor, the terminal rule, `cannot_reopen`, a stale revision, logging with a due date and the five refusals (nothing written), the introduction's meeting and its refusal on another kind, every new total, and that existing rows read unchanged. Mutation-checked: dropping `in_progress`, treating `cancelled` as open, removing `cannot_reopen`, dropping a malformed date, removing the intro-only rule, and counting overdue regardless of state each fail at least one test.
- The ledger's other suites (`portfolio_support_scope`, `support_entry_integrity_4642`, `portfolio_periods_pages_4647`) now build its tables from the real migrations through `cloudflare-worker/test/_support_ledger_schema.mjs`, instead of a copy of their DDL, so the next change to these tables reaches them too.
