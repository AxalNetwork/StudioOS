## Context

The Fees tab could list terms, confirm a draft, preview a quarter and issue
it, but nothing on screen recorded the inputs a fee period is computed from.
A fund with no terms showed "No terms recorded" and every Preview was
refused. The four `fee.write` routes (`POST /terms`, `PUT /lps/:id/admission`,
`POST /net-invested`, `POST /offsets` under `/api/fund-fees/:fundId`) and
their client methods (`frontend/src/lib/api/fundFees.js`) were on main with
no caller.

## Decision

1. **Four recording dialogs on the Fees tab** (`pages/admin/fundAdmin/feeInputs.jsx`,
   pure rules in `feeInputsModel.js`): Record fee terms (a new draft
   version), Record an admission date for one `LP #id`, Record net invested
   capital, and Record an offset. Each is a `Lightbox` with `phone="sheet"`,
   each button sits in `FundActControl act="fee.write"`. `fee.write` is tier
   `admin` with no sentence, so the control shows for every HQ Admin; the
   Worker's refusal (for a support session, its 403 sentence) is shown inside
   the dialog.
2. **Only what cannot be sent is checked in the browser** — a rate or an
   amount that does not parse, no company chosen. Every other rule is the
   Worker's, and its sentences (the body's `refusals`, which `request()`
   keeps on `e.data`) are shown one per line in the open dialog, with the
   typed values kept.
3. **A record reloads the fee read, closes the dialog and drops any preview
   on screen**, whose digest the new input changes.
4. **Rates are typed as a percent and sent as basis points** through a new
   string-based `percentToBps` in `lib/bps.js` (`'1.50'` → 150, `'2.005'` →
   null), with `bpsToPercentInput` for the prefill. **Amounts are typed in
   dollars and sent as whole cents** through the existing
   `lib/moneyInput.js` `amountToCents` (`'1,200.50'` → 120050); no second
   dollars parser was written.
5. **The terms form** prefills rate, offset share and basis from the read's
   `defaults`. With no confirmed version "Takes effect" is hidden and
   `effective_from` is not sent (the Worker takes the fee start date); with
   one, the fee start date is the first confirmed version's (read-only) and
   "Takes effect" is asked, hinted "The first day of a quarter after every
   issued period." A later version with a lower rate than the confirmed one
   before it reads "Step-down to X% from <date>". Confirm and Issue keep
   their `ActFlow` and stay the Super Admin's.
6. **Offsets get their own panel**, listing what the read already returned
   (company, Axal entity, amount, received, description, source, and
   "Counted in 2026Q3" or "Not counted yet"). The offset dialog reads the
   fund's positions (`GET /api/fund-performance/:id/positions`) when it
   opens; positions with no `project_id` are left out, one option per
   company, and a failed or empty read says so and keeps Record disabled.

## Departures from FND13

- **The four forms are not drawn on any board.** They follow FND12's Record
  a mark: labelled fields, a required source of up to 500 characters, an info
  line, Cancel and a primary button.
- **LPs are `LP #id`, not names** (D1165): the fee read carries no name.
- **Net invested is fund-level**, one line and a dated list, not the
  canvas's per-LP column; the read does not attribute it to LPs.
- **Offsets are their own list, not a per-LP Offset column**: the per-LP
  share is known only after a preview, in the period's lines.
- **An offset's company reads `Company #id`** in the list: the fee read
  returns `project_id` only, and the positions are read only when the offset
  dialog opens.
- **"Drafted by [Name] · Admin · [date]" is not drawn** under a draft: the
  fee read returns no recorder or recorded date.
- **"Funding call link" is not added to the terms card**; linking stays on
  the period, as built.
