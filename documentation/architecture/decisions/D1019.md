## D1019 — Cap table part 4: who sees what (migration 575): per-venture levels, an investor's read through a grant, an admin's support read with a reason, the access log, share links by level (#2856)

- **Status:** S11, 2026-10-25. Part 4 of Cap table (#2432, D1015 §6). The owner moved the task from S10 to S11.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `154cd86cf5`. D1015 and its canvas are on S21's integration branch (#2852), not yet on `main`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Migration:** 575, `575_cap_table_access.sql`.
- **Why:** #2651 (#2436) closed the open doors: investors are refused, and co-founders read and write through accepted membership. The canvas (CT13, CT15–CT18) draws who sees what, at which level. D1015 §5 reads owner questions 3 and 4 as answered A:
  - nothing is seen until the founder grants a level;
  - Operating Partners read only when granted;
  - Admins see their own studio's ventures, and the full table only with a logged reason;
  - the Super Admin reads only.

### 1. Migration 575

- **`cap_table_access_grants`** holds one row per person, or cap table holder, and venture. Its columns:
  - `project_id`;
  - `user_id` or `holder_ref`, or both;
  - `level`: `none`, `own_position`, `summary`, `investor` or `full`;
  - `source` (`founder` or `side_letter`) and `source_ref`, the side letter's envelope uuid;
  - `granted_by`, `since`, `revoked_at` and `revoked_by`.

  At most one live row per person and venture, enforced by two partial unique indexes. A changed level revokes the old row and inserts a new one, so who could see what stays on file.
- **`cap_table_access_log`** records each open: `project_id`, `user_id`, `share_token_id`, `via` (§3), `level`, `reason` (an admin's support read) and `opened_at`. **No IP address is kept, and the table has no column for one.**
- **`captable_share_tokens`** gains `project_id`, `level`, `holders_attested_at` and `holders_attested_by` for venture links (§6). Old scenario links keep working until they expire. `routes/captable.ts`'s runtime bootstrap adds the same four columns, so its self-heal and the migration declare one shape (`check-schema-pair-drift`).

### 2. The levels (CT13)

Each level is a read that returns only what it shows. Every view is built by copying allowed fields, never by deleting from the whole (`services/captableShare.ts`):

| Level | Returns |
|---|---|
| Own position | the holder's own final-ledger rows (shares and %), their own SAFEs and notes, the SAFEs sent through StudioOS in their name, and the group split. No other holder is named. |
| Summary | % by group and the number of rounds. No names, no counts. |
| Investor | every investor and founder position, rounds and pricing, the pool as one line, and the waterfall. Not who holds options, and none of the rounds' free-text events. |
| Full | everything the founder sees. |

- **Summary, Investor and Full are the three link audiences of `redactForAudience`, unchanged,** so a grant and a link at one level show the same thing.
- **The views come from the saved model.** Every view is built from the venture's canonical saved model, the only cap table on file until part 2's record. Each answer says so: `source: 'model'`, with "These figures come from the venture's saved model, not from a record of what was issued." Part 2 or 3 switches the source to the record.
- **No model saved** is `{ recorded: false, reason }`, never an empty level.

### 3. Who reads at which level (`resolveAccess`, `services/captableAccess.ts`)

| Caller | Level | `via` |
|---|---|---|
| The venture's owner | Full, and the only one who changes access | `owner` |
| An accepted co-founder (`project_members`) | Full, read only here; writes stay #2651's | `member` |
| An accepted advisor | Summary by default; a grant widens or narrows it | `member` or `grant` |
| An investor | the grant's level, and only with a relationship to the venture (`investorProjectIds`: dealroom, introduction, converted watchlist), never `canReadProject` | `grant` |
| An Operating Partner, or anyone else | the grant's level | `grant` |
| A plain admin | Summary (the group summary) | `studio` |
| A plain admin, opened for support with a reason | Full, read only | `support` |
| The Super Admin | Full, read only | `hq` |

- **A grant that names a holder** sees at least that holder's own position, even at None.
- **No level is a refusal:** None, no grant, or no relationship is a 403 `not_shared` with CT15's sentence, "The founder has not shared the cap table with you."
- **A broken grant or model store is a 5xx,** never "not shared".
- **"Their studio's ventures."** A branch's database holds only its own studio's ventures, so an admin reads this deployment's ventures. On HQ that is the HQ-held ventures.

### 4. Routes (`routes/captable_access.ts`, mounted at `/api/captable/access` before `/api/captable`)

| Route | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /:projectId` | anyone with a level | the view at the caller's level, logged |
| `POST /:projectId/support` `{ reason }` | an admin | the full table, read only, logged with the reason (10 to 500 characters). The founder reads the reason in the log. A correction goes through part 2's correction, with its reason. |
| `GET /:projectId/grants` | owner, co-founders | who sees what, with each grant's level, source, granted by, since and last viewed. `can_change` is true for the owner only. |
| `PUT /:projectId/grants` | owner | `{ user_uid? \| holder_ref? \| from_envelope?, level? }` |
| `DELETE /:projectId/grants/:id` | owner | revoke |
| `GET /:projectId/log` | owner, co-founders | who opened it, when, at which level, and a support read's reason; newest first, paged |
| `GET /log` | Super Admin | the access log across companies |
| `GET`, `POST /:projectId/links`; `DELETE /:projectId/links/:id` | owner (co-founders list them) | share links by level (§6) |
| `GET /shared/:token` | public | a venture link's view, logged |

- **Every open is logged, or the read is refused.** A view is returned only after its log row is written. A log that cannot be written is a 503 `access_log_unwritable`, with no view. A refusal is not an open.
- **Grants, revokes and links are the owner's only,** each with one `activity_logs` row: `captable_access_granted`, `captable_access_revoked`, `captable_link_created` and `captable_link_revoked`. A grant's revoke, insert and audit row go in one batch. A co-founder reads access and changes nothing.
- **The Super Admin edits none:** every write route answers the Super Admin 403, like any non-owner.
- **Refusals** go through `refuse` with a `code`.

### 5. A grant prefilled from a signed side letter

`PUT /:projectId/grants` with `from_envelope` takes:
- an `esign_envelopes` row whose `document_type` is `pro_rata_information_rights_side_letter` and whose status is `completed`;
- on a deal of this venture;
- and the investor who signed it (`esign_recipients` with status `signed`).

It grants Investor unless the owner names another level, with `source: 'side_letter'` and the envelope's uuid. Anything else is 400 `not_a_signed_side_letter`.

### 6. Share links by level

- **The existing links stay as they are.** They belong to one scenario and one of three audiences (`POST /api/captable/scenarios/:uid/share`). Old ones keep working until they expire.
- **A venture link** names the venture and a level: Summary, Investor or Full. A link has no holder, so no Own position.
- **Investor and Full need the founder's attestation** (`holders_agreed: true`) that the named holders agreed, recorded with who and when. Without it the answer is 400 `holders_not_attested`.
- **Each link** has:
  - an expiry, 1 to 2,160 hours (default 168);
  - a view cap, 1 to 500 (default 25);
  - Revoke.
- **Opening a link** reads the model when it is opened, at the link's level. The signed token and the stored row must agree on the venture and the level. Each view is logged with the link and no user, and no IP.
- **The token is shown once** and never listed.
- **The share page.** `share_path` is `/share/captable/venture/<token>`. Part 11 (#2863) draws that page and the Access tab.

### Not in this part, and one call for S21

- **Drawing any of it.** That is part 11 (Access, CT17 and CT18) and part 12 (the investor's panel).
- **Admins on the old routes.** `routes/captable.ts` still gives an admin read and write through `computeProjectPermissions` (#2651), and the Admin consoles open cap tables through it. This part does not change those gates, because doing so would break today's admin pages before part 11 moves them to this read. **Proposal:** when part 11 lands, the old routes' admin path narrows to the group summary, and the full table is read only through `POST /support`.
