### 1. The problem

An empty Problem discovery, Customer research or Ideal customer profile drew the full D661 table: search,
filters, sort, an empty column header, "No records yet." and "0 of 0", with the cycle's Record a session in
the header and the card's step beside the table's Add. Build's modules already show one guided first action
(D5076).

### 2. The rule

D5076's three stages, unchanged, now also on these three modules (`PROGRESSIVE.validate`):

| Module | Heading | Dominant action (the module's existing Add) | Count noun |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem discovery | Name the problem you are solving | Create your first pain theme (Create theme, `FORMS.validate`) | pain theme(s) |
| Customer research | Hear it from customers first | Record your first session (Record a session: the record lightbox through `onOpen`, same href) | interview(s) |
| Ideal customer profile | Decide who you are building for | Create your first segment (Create segment, `FORMS.validate`) | segment(s) |

- **Zero** is only the read's own `total` of 0 with no search or filter; a read with no total keeps the full
  table ("Not recorded"), an unreadable read keeps Unreadable with Retry (D5076's honesty rule).
- **Record a session.** `dominantAdd` spreads the Add it relabels, so the lightbox's `onOpen` and its
  `/validate/record` href survive; `ZeroState` (desktop) and the phone's `Cta` already open it on a plain click.
- **The stat strips.** Validate has no Sources region (`sourcesRegion` is Build's), so its parts (the
  Interviews strip, the Pain map, From Google, From Notion) and Eadwyn's fill bands are the page's children; at
  zero `CycleModulePage` and `PhoneModulePage` already put children in the closed "Sources and Eadwyn drafts"
  region under the first action. From the first record they draw in place, as before.
- **One Add at few.** With 1 to 9 records the header's action gives way when it is the table's own Add (only
  Customer research's Record a session is both). A different header action stays. At many nothing changes.
- **Scope.** Market intelligence, Demand and urgency signals, Experiments and evidence and Verdict keep D661's
  table; the issue's part B can add them to `PROGRESSIVE.validate` the same way.
