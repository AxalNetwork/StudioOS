### 1. The item read (Worker)

`GET /api/calendar/item/:kind/:id` (`routes/calendar.ts`, `services/calendar/itemRead.ts`).

- **The page read's own item.** The route runs the same owner-only reader as `GET /calendar/events` (`fetchUserEventsWithSources`, cancelled rows included) for the one kind, and describes the item with `describeItems`. The drawer therefore never shows more than the list did, and never to anyone the list would not show it to.
- **Where it looks.** An IC meeting or a check-in is looked for within a day either side of its own start, so a deep link works however far out it is. Any other kind is looked for within 400 days of today.
- **People by name.** The attendees leave as `people`, each with:
  - a name;
  - a profile in words (Investor, Admin, Founder…);
  - where they stand (Organiser, Invited, Founder, With the founder);
  - their answer (Going, Maybe, Not going, Not answered yet).

  The item carries no `attendees` and no `organizer_email`, so never an email and never a user number. The reader's own answer is `my_rsvp`. A check-in records no answers, so none is invented.
- **CAL18.** A meeting the reader is not part of answers 404 `{ error: 'not_party', detail: "Only the people invited and studio admins can open this meeting." }`, exactly as one that does not exist, so the refusal says nothing about the meeting.
- **Other refusals.** An unknown kind or id is 400 `invalid_item`. A source that does not answer is 503 `unreadable`, never a refusal.

### 2. Meet on create (Worker)

`POST /ic-meetings` and `POST /founder-checkins` take `add_meet: true`.

- **When the link is made.** After every existing gate, and before anything is saved, `googleMeet.createMeeting` makes a Calendar event with a Meet conference on the creator's own Google account. Its join link becomes the item's `location_uri`. If the save then fails, the event is deleted again.
- **The Meet event is the creator's copy.** It is recorded in `calendar_sync_records` for the creator. So the copy hook that runs next PATCHes it in place rather than writing a second event beside it, a Reschedule moves it (PATCH keeps the conference), and a Cancel removes it. Nobody else's calendar is touched by the Meet step; their copies come from the existing sync, as before.
- **Refused with nothing saved,** each with `MEETING_ERRORS`' sentence and the statuses `founder_validate_meetings` uses:
  - 409 `meet_not_connected` (no Google connection; Google is not asked) or `meet_reconnect`;
  - 502 `meet_create_failed` or `meet_link_unavailable`;
  - 503 `meet_unreachable`.
- **New check-in from a founder** names only who it is with (`counterpart_uid`). The founder who names no founder is the founder, so the page never sends the reader's own user number.

### 3. The drawer (CAL4, CAL5, CAL18)

`/calendar/item/:kind/:id`, under the same guard as `/calendar`, opens `CalendarItemDrawer.jsx` over the view the link came from (`state.from`), or over Month on a direct link. Esc or × returns to that view. Each row's title links to its item.

- **The header:** the kind, the reader's role, their answer, and "Waiting for confirmation" on a pending booking.
- **When.** For example "Thu 12 Nov · 14:00–15:00 · 60 min · Lisbon time (UTC+0)." It is in the reader's zone. Where the row records another zone (an advisor's), its time there follows. An end nobody recorded says so.
- **Where.** The link, Copy, and Join, which is violet from 15 minutes before the start until the end. An item with no place says "No place recorded", and Calendly's says Calendly sent none.
- **Your answer.** Going, Maybe and Not going, only where `can.rsvp`. The answer is re-read, and the view behind re-reads too.
- **People:** "[n] of [m] going" where answers are recorded. An expert session shows "Only you see this" and no people list.
- **Deal or Venture,** with its page (`href`). For other kinds the page link reads Open booking, Show ticket, and so on.
- **Agenda or Notes.**
- **Copies:** Google and Outlook, each "Copied" or "Not copied", with "Copy to my calendar" when a calendar is connected, else the reason.
- **Actions.** Reschedule and Cancel each follow their own `can`. Where none is offered, the canvas's line says why:
  - "No actions on a cancelled meeting.";
  - "No cancel here. The booking page handles it.";
  - "StudioOS cannot change or cancel this booking."
- **A cancelled meeting** shows its reason, or "No reason was recorded."
- **A failed read** is Unreadable with Try again, never the CAL18 refusal.

### 4. The dialogs (CAL7, CAL8, CAL19)

Every one is a `Lightbox` dialog (a bottom sheet on a phone), and never a browser pop-up. The row's Cancel, which used `window.confirm`, now opens the Cancel dialog.

- **New menu (CAL8 A).** IC meeting appears only for admins and investors, the rule `canScheduleIc` applies. Then Check-in. A founder also gets Interview call ↗ and Book an advisor session ↗. Everyone gets Event ↗. Items that leave the page carry ↗ and say where they go.
- **New IC meeting (CAL7):**
  - title;
  - start, in the reader's own time and zone, with "Choose a time from now on." under a start that is not ahead;
  - length (30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes);
  - the deal, found by name among the deals the reader can open;
  - then its people, found by name (D1207's pickers);
  - Place: "Add a Google Meet link" (on by default where Google is connected, and not drawn otherwise), or a pasted link;
  - agenda.

  A 402 reads CAL18's plan line, "Creating IC meetings is part of Investor Professional.", with See plans. No paywall opens over it.
- **New check-in (CAL8 B):**
  - With: a founder picks anyone on their ventures, and anyone else picks the founder;
  - the venture, from the person picked (chosen when they share one);
  - title;
  - start;
  - length (15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes);
  - place;
  - notes.
- **Reschedule (CAL19):** a new start and length, with the current length chosen. A refusal shows the Worker's sentence in the dialog.
- **Cancel (CAL19):** "[when] · [n] invited". An IC meeting takes an optional reason. A check-in keeps none, so none is asked.
- **What each dialog says it does is what the Worker does.** No create, move or cancel sends a message from StudioOS. What changes for the people on it is their own calendar copy, where they connected Google or Outlook. So the dialogs say that, and the canvas's "Everyone invited is told" is not used. The test holds that no dialog's words claim a message.
- **Removed.** The old modals that asked for a deal id and attendee user ids (`IcMeetingModal`, `CheckinModal`, `ModalShell`, `Field`) are gone. Nobody types a user number or a deal id.

### 5. Not drawn here, and why

- **Download .ics on one item.** No per-item file read exists. The whole-calendar file and subscription stay in the header (part 7 redraws them).
- **"Copied to Google · 9 Nov 09:12".** The item carries where it was copied, not when.
- **Cancelled by [name] on [date].** The store keeps the reason, not who cancelled or when.
- **Book office hours ↗** in the New menu. Its page is not open to every founder.
- **Drag on an empty slot to start New IC meeting (CAL2)** belongs to part 5's grid.
