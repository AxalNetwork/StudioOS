### What the canvas decides

- Advisory refunds get their own block inside Billing exceptions, under the existing table, with a third tile: "Advisory refunds due · As of now · N open".
- Open rows come first. Resolved rows sit in a collapsed list labelled "most recently recorded first", because that is the route's order.
- Marking a row records a fact. It moves no money and calls no provider. The sheet says "Make the refund in Stripe first."
- "Mark refunded…" runs Scope → Review → Step-up → Result. The reason is 12 to 500 characters. Step-up reuses RefundReviewFlow's.
- 409 and 404 each offer Refresh the list. 503 keeps the reason and offers Try again.
- The Stripe payment id is copyable mono text and does not link out.
- A missing amount reads Not recorded, never blank or €0. An unreadable store reads Unavailable, never an empty list.
- Eadwyn drafts a reason from the row's values, with blanks for the refund id and date.
