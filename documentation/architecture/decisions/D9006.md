## D9006 — the fund workspace's standing chip and fund brief use the canvas words (#9006)

A follow-up to #6130 part 2 (`D6130-p2.md`, PR #9005), on the owner's choice
there ("A: keep part 1's words in #9005 and switch the chip and the fund brief
PDF to the canvas wording in a small follow-up issue"). The canvas is
`Axal VC Fund v2 and LP Workspace` (D6127), whose `STAND` map in
`design/incoming/Axal VC Fund v2 and LP Workspace Page.dc.html` draws the chip
for every standing.

### The words

| `lpAccessState()` | Before | Now (canvas) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| visitor | Invited guest | Visitor · viewing by invitation |
| pending | Applicant · under review | Applicant · application in review |
| approved | Approved LP | Approved · nothing committed yet |
| committed | Committed LP (and "$150K committed" beside it) | Committed LP · $150,000.00 |
| voting | Committed LP (and "$300K committed" beside it) | Full rights · $300,000.00 |

The canvas's "applicant" is the ladder's `pending` (all three cases the ladder
reads as pending, as board VF-02 says), and its "full" is `voting`. The issue's
table grouped committed and voting under "Committed LP"; the canvas draws the
voting standing as "Full rights", and the issue asks for the canvas words for
every standing the canvas draws, so voting reads "Full rights". The loading
chip ("Reading your standing…") already matched the canvas. The canvas draws
every standing the ladder has, so no standing keeps its old words.

### The amount

- It is the commitment from the viewer's own `limited_partners` rows
  (`GET /api/funds/lp-portal`, `performance[].commitment`), summed per row in
  whole cents by `lpAccessState()` (`commitmentCents`), so a dollar figure with
  cents is not truncated by float error.
- It is formatted with `formatCents()`, as My commitment and the first screen
  print money ("$150,000.00"); the canvas's "[$150,000]" is a placeholder.
- Only committed and full-rights standings carry it. With no commitment
  recorded (`commitmentCents` null), the chip prints the words alone, never $0;
  an admin previewing "committed" with no position of their own sees
  "Committed LP".

### One string, printed once

`standingWords(state, commitmentCents)` in
`frontend/src/pages/investor/fund/FundWorkspaceSupport.jsx` builds the string.
The header chip, the "Your access" box under Participation rights and the fund
brief's `recipient.standing` all print it. The "$150K committed" text that sat
beside the chip and inside "Your access" is gone, and the brief no longer
appends "· $<n>K committed", so the amount appears once. The brief still reads
the viewer's own derived standing, never an admin preview.

### Not changed

The chip tones stay as they were (visitor gray, applicant amber, approved and
committed violet, full rights green). The canvas draws the visitor and
applicant chips neutral and the three LP standings violet; the issue asks for
the words only.
