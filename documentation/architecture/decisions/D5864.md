### 1. When the proposal is raised

A sync raises "Add TikTok as a channel" (`raiseChannelProposal`, `services/tiktok/grow.ts`) when the venture has no `growth_channels` row whose `utm_source` is `tiktok`.
- It is one `INSERT … SELECT … WHERE NOT EXISTS (…) ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, with every value bound.
- `UNIQUE (project_id, kind, subject)` holds one row per venture (`kind` `add_channel`, `subject` `tiktok`). A row already there is left as it is, in any state, so **a dismissed proposal never returns** on a re-sync.
- The sync's counts carry `proposals` (1 when raised), and its activity line says how many.
- It is computed from local data and calls nothing at TikTok. So it is a built entry in `TIKTOK_FEED_LIST` with no page tool, as Beehiiv's channel proposal is (a D753 page tool must name a call).

### 2. What Distribution shows

`parts.from_tiktok.proposal` on Distribution, whether or not a video has landed:
- **pending**, while no TikTok channel exists: the sentence, and `can_decide` (true only for the venture's founder, writing, outside a support session);
- **accepted**: the channel it created (`uid`, `name`), or `null` if that channel was later removed;
- **`null`**: dismissed, never raised, or a TikTok channel the founder added another way.

### 3. Accept and Dismiss

`POST /api/cycles/grow/tiktok/proposals/:id/accept` and `/dismiss`, body `{ project }`.
- **Founder only.** A support session is refused first (`support_session_refused`); a writer who is not the venture's founder (`isVentureFounder`) is refused `not_founder`. This follows Mercury's partner proposals; the founder decides even when a teammate's TikTok serves the venture.
- **Accept** calls the existing channel writer, `addChannel` (`services/growWriters.ts`, the one `POST /channels` calls), with name "TikTok", `utm_source` `tiktok`, kind `organic`. There is no second writer of `growth_channels`. Then the proposal records the channel. A TikTok channel already on the venture answers `channel_exists` and creates nothing. `growth_channels` is unique on (project, utm_source), so two Accepts at once still leave exactly one row.
- **Dismiss** is final.
- Either verb on a decided proposal answers `proposal_decided`; an id not on the venture answers `proposal_not_found`.
- Each writes one `logGrowWrite` line with counts only ("TikTok channel proposal accepted: 1 channel added", "TikTok channel proposal dismissed"). `addChannel` writes its own `grow_channel_added` line, as it does for every channel.

### 4. Disconnect

`purgeStatements` and `purgeByOpenIdStatements` delete the connection's `tiktok_proposals` rows with its facts (decision E), on disconnect and on the deauthorization webhook. A channel the founder accepted is the venture's and stays.

A proposal deleted by a disconnect can be raised again after the member reconnects and syncs, if the venture still has no TikTok channel. That follows from decision E. A dismissal holds for as long as the connection does.
