### 1. What Review reads

| Read | For | When |

### 2. The queue (EV3, left; EV17b)

- **Search** covers the title, the host's name and the venture's name, D918's `q` rule (`allEventsModel.matchesSearch`).
- **Filters:**
  - Type: the types in the queue, in the registry's order, then any stored type the registry does not know.
  - Host role: the roles in the queue, and "Role not recorded" for a host with none (or no host).
  - Free or Paid.
  - Starts: the next 7 days, the next 30 days, later than 30 days, or already started.
  - On a phone the filters fold behind "Filters · [n]".
- **Sorts:**
  - Waiting longest, the default: the oldest wait first, then the soonest start.
  - Starts soonest.
  - Newest (created).
  - A missing value sorts last either way (D918 §4).
- **Each row** shows:
  - the title;
  - "[host] · [role] · [venture]" (`allEventsModel.hostLine`; "Host not recorded" with no host);
  - the type pill and the start;
  - the flags;
  - "waiting [n days]", with an amber dot past 72 hours.
- **The wait** counts from `submitted_at`. An event queued before part 2 has none, so it counts from `created_at`. That is D918's `waitingSince` and the Approvals lane's rule, and the row's tooltip says so. With neither time it reads "Waiting time not recorded", never 0. Overdue is more than 72 hours (`SLA_PAST_HOURS`).
- **The flags** are:
  - Paid, and Free seats (D915);
  - Clash, from D918's `clash`;
  - the send-back flag. A send-back stores its reason in `review_note` and nothing clears it (D917), so a waiting event with a `review_note` reads "Sent back before". How many times is only in the history. The selected event's history is read, so its row says "Sent back once" (or "twice", or "[n] times"), and a history with no send-back drops the flag.
- **Moving the selection:** J and K move it anywhere on the tab; the arrow keys move it while focus is in the queue. Neither works while typing or with a dialog open.

### 3. The selected event (EV3, right)

- **The preview on the public calendar** shows:
  - the cover, drawn only from an `http(s)` or same-site address, otherwise a plain panel;
  - the date block, in the event's own zone;
  - the title, the type pill, and "[format] · [place]";
  - the summary and "Hosted by [name]." ("Host not recorded." with no host).
- **The facts are D915's, with these changes:**
  - Type comes first.
  - Place becomes "Format and place". The format comes from `location_kind`: Online, In person (`physical` and `in_person`) or Hybrid.
  - Venture is added: its name, "No venture" when the event has no project, or "Not recorded" when it names a venture whose name is gone.
  - Submitted follows Created: the date from `submitted_at`, or "Not recorded" for an event queued before part 2, with the reason.
- **What approving does** keeps D915's first two lines; the third is counted (§4).
- **Clashes.** The events the selected one overlaps, by D915 §2's rule over every event (`calendarModel.clashMap`):
  - Each is listed with how its start sits against this one ("starts 30 minutes earlier"), its start and status, and a link to its page.
  - With none, the card reads "No other event overlaps it."
  - A failed list says it could not be read, never "no clash".
- **History**, from D918 §6, oldest first. Each item is in words, such as "Sent back on 9 Oct 2026 by an admin: ‘…’", "Submitted again on 11 Oct 2026." or "Edited by the host on 10 Oct 2026: place and start.".
  - The actor is "an admin" or "the host", never a name.
  - An edit names the fields, never their values.
  - With no earlier send-back, the card leads with "First time in review.".
  - A failed read, or a history block that says it could not be read, is unreadable. It never reads as a first time.

### 4. The approve dialog (EV4)

- **The third line counts only from the free-seat preview** (`would_invite`, D918 §7): "Emails [n] free-seat invitations: [a] official partners, [b] invested LPs."
  - Only the sources whose flag is on are named.
  - When nobody is left to invite, it reads "Sends no invitations.".
  - Under it: "Each invitation arrives in the member’s StudioOS inbox, and by email where Gmail is set up." (map B).
- **While the preview reads, or when it failed,** the line names the sources without a number. A failure adds "The number could not be read." A count that is not a number is not read as one.
- **An event with no inviting flag** reads "Sends no invitations." whatever the preview says, followed by D915's sentence for the seat-at-sign-up flags.
- The page's card and the dialog state the same lines.

### 5. Refusals (EV15c)

- **D917's 409 `not_waiting`** means another admin decided the event, or the host changed it, after the page read it.
  - It closes the dialog.
  - Under the action bar it reads "This event changed while you were reading it. Reload to see the latest.", with Reload, which reads the desk again.
- **D917's 400 `reason_required`** is drawn under the reason field, which is marked invalid, and the dialog stays open. The page already requires a reason, so this is the Worker's rule showing through.
- **D915's other refusals** (suspended licence, frozen account, gone event) stay one sentence under the dialog's buttons.

### 6. The phone (EV17b, EV17c)

- Below the `lg` breakpoint the queue stands alone. Tapping a row opens the selected event as a full-screen sheet with a role of `dialog`.

- The action bar keeps "Send back" and "Approve and publish" in view.
