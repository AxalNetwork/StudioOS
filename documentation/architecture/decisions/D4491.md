## D4491 — Distribution Results: read-only attribution with a reporting window (#4491)

Part of #3722. Designs DS9 (primary) and MK9 (funnel cards, the Campaigns
table and the Revenue note). Depends on #4447, #4483 and #4484, all merged.

### What it reads

`GET /api/grow/results?project=&from=&to=` (`routes/grow_results.ts`,
self-mounted) is behind the venture reader gate the campaign routes use:
401 without a session, 400 without a project, 404 for a missing or deleted
venture, 403 without read access. Every statement is bound and scoped to
that venture's `project_id` (contacts, channels, campaigns, Mercury facts);
manual `channel_spend` rows are reached only through the venture's own
channels. Unexpected failures answer 503 with a fixed sentence; no raw
error text reaches the response or the screen.

### Attribution rules

- **Campaigns.** Results reuses #4483's shared read
  (`campaigns(env, pid, opts)` in `services/growCampaigns.ts`) rather than
  recomputing. A lead is a contact of the venture whose `utm_json` carries
  the campaign's `utm_campaign` (trimmed, lower-cased); malformed JSON is
  not a match. Demos are contacts whose current status is `qualified` or
  `active` (qualified through active in `CONTACT_STATUSES`); customers are
  `active`. A campaign with no UTM tag has leads, demos, customers and cost
  per lead null with `Not recorded: no UTM tag`.
- **Sources.** Each contact's `utm_source` is mapped to the venture's
  channel with that `growth_channels.utm_source`; a tagged source with no
  channel is its own row, named by the tag. These rows carry figure source
  `attributed`. Contacts added by hand (`source = 'manual'`, no UTM) form an
  `Added by hand` row with figure source `manual`. Malformed or untagged
  `utm_json` is skipped. A channel scanned with no contacts is a counted 0.
- **Spend.** Per channel and per campaign, Mercury's `channel_spend` fact
  wins a month that also has a typed `channel_spend` row (#4483's decision);
  a month with no rows is skipped, never 0. A campaign's spend is the sum of
  its channels' recorded months; none recorded is
  `Not recorded: no spend entered`. Each figure carries its source:
  `mercury`, `manual` or `mixed` (both chips).
- **Cost per lead** is spend over leads in whole cents, rounded half up
  (`$620 / 18 = $34.44`). It is null with the leads reason when leads are
  missing, with the spend reason when spend is missing, and
  `Not recorded: no leads counted` at 0 leads. It shows two source chips:
  the spend source and the leads source (UTM attribution).

### Reporting window (S1's decision A, 2026-11-06)

`campaigns()` takes an optional `{ from, to }` (YYYY-MM-DD, both or neither).
Absent, it behaves exactly as before, so the #4483 and #4447 tests pass
unchanged. Given:

- leads, demos and customers count contacts whose `created_at` day is in
  [from, to]; status is the current status;
- spend counts the channel-months where the campaign's start/end months
  intersect the window's months; a campaign with no shared month is
  `Not recorded: no campaign months in this window`;
- cost per lead uses that same window on both sides;
- per-source counts and per-channel spend use the same window (channel
  spend over the window's months).

Without a window Results returns the lifetime/campaign-window figures and
labels them (`scope.kind = 'lifetime'`; on screen "No window chosen:
lifetime figures"). `from > to`, a malformed or impossible date, or only one
of the two is a 400 with our own sentence and the offending field. The tab
checks the same before reading.

### No write to MRR

Results is read only. It writes nothing to revenue, MRR, Raise figures,
`project_metrics` or any other store; a test asserts the row counts and the
MRR value are unchanged. DS9's note ("Customers here feed Revenue and
traction…") is overruled by the spec until the owner says otherwise; the tab
shows MK9's line instead: "Revenue stays on Revenue and traction. Nothing
here changes MRR." with "Open Revenue and traction ›" to
`/grow/revenue-and-traction`.

### Screen

`ResultsTab.jsx` / `resultsModel.js`: MK9's three funnel cards (the three
campaigns with the most counted leads; bars relative to leads), the
Campaigns table, DS9's Attribution and Spend per channel tables (1.3fr | 1fr
on desktop, one column on tablet and phone through `useDistributionLayout`),
numeric columns right-aligned in mono, each missing figure as its named
"Not recorded" reason, a loading skeleton, and the shared read-error card
with Try again. DS9 labels UTM figures "Connector"; they are labelled
"UTM attribution" here, because no live connector delivered them.
