### 2. What a scenario is

A scenario is a `cap_table_scenarios` row with `is_variant = 1` (D632). It has one of two starts.

**It starts from Today** when its stored inputs carry D1018's `start` (the record's holders). New scenario makes these whenever the record holds shares.
- Every run goes through `POST /scenarios/by-project/:id/simulate`, with the scenario's rounds, exits and the instruments it lets convert. So the scenario always starts from what is recorded now, never from a copy taken when it was made.
- Save writes the run's own inputs (the record's holders at that moment) through `PUT /scenarios/:uid`.

**It starts from the model** when it is typed by hand: founders, a pool, SAFEs and rounds, as the variants page edited them.
- It runs through `POST /captable/simulate` and saves its own inputs.
- New scenario makes one only when nothing is recorded, as a copy of the saved model, as the variants page did.

**The saved model** is "Cap table · model" (CT19b). It is the main cap table, so Scenarios reads it and never renames, deletes, edits or attaches to it. It can be duplicated into a scenario. Its own inputs are still edited in the simulator until part 14.

### 3. The cards (CT6)

- **Each card** shows:
  - its start: "Starts from Today", "Starts from your model", or "Your saved model";
  - its round in one line, with the pool target;
  - founders after, as last saved;
  - when it changed;
  - the Modelled tag;
  - a Compare tick.

  The card's name opens the scenario.
- **The head line** says how many scenarios there are and where a new one starts.
  - "Compare selected (n)" appears once one is ticked.
  - Up to three can be ticked, in the order ticked; a fourth tick is refused.
  - "Open the simulator" stays a link.
- **No owner stamp.** The canvas's "changed 2 d ago by Founder B" is drawn as the date only: the row stores who owns it, not who changed it last.
- **New scenario** opens a dialog. It names the start ("It starts from Today: the holders and instruments on record now, with your model's round to change") and says who sees the scenario and that the main cap table does not change. The name is "Scenario n" until changed.
  - From Today, the inputs are the record's holders and instruments with the model's rounds and exit.
  - With nothing recorded, they are a copy of the model.
  - With neither, the button is not drawn.

### 4. One scenario (CT6)

**The inputs of a scenario from Today:**
- each round's name, pre-money, amount raised and pool target, and who pays for the top-up: "Pre-money (founders)" or "Post-money (everyone)";
- Add a round;
- "Instruments that convert": the record's SAFEs and notes, all ticked by default;
- up to four exit values.

**Two inputs are not built as the canvas draws them:**
- **New investors.** The engine takes one amount per round and names the buyer "<round> Investors". The page says the money is not split by investor, rather than listing names the engine would ignore.
- **Who pays, with no pool target.** A round with no pool target sends no top-up and no payer.

**The run:**
- A blank field is never sent as 0.
- A value that is not a number, a negative one, a pool target of 100% or more, or a fifth exit stops the run with a sentence, and nothing is run.
- The Worker's refusal (an instrument named to convert that is no longer on the record, for instance) is shown in its words.

**A scenario from the model** edits in place with D632's form: founders, pool, SAFEs, rounds and an exit. Its name field is left out, since renaming is a dialog here, and its button reads "Save scenario".

**The results**, all from the run:
- **Before and after,** by group (Founders, Investors, Employees and advisors, Pool unallocated) and by founder, with the change in points.
  - For a scenario from Today, "before" is Today and is tagged On record. For a typed one it is "Start", and the foot says it is not the record.
  - A group nobody holds is left out. A side with no holder is "—", never 0%.
  - Price per share before the round is "Not read here yet": no read carries the record's last price. D1283 (#3341) closes this: Today's price per share is read from the record.
- **What happens in each round,** in words from the engine's numbers:
  - the pool top-up and who paid;
  - each conversion, at the cap, the discount or the round price, with its shares and price;
  - the new money.
- **The engine's notes:**
  - the over-100% check first;
  - its warnings;
  - each instrument held out;
  - each instrument the record does not let it model (D1018's `not_modelled`), in D1018's sentence.

**Save** edits in place through `PUT /scenarios/:uid` and says "Saved. The main cap table did not change.", as the variants page did. It is the one change made without a dialog.

**Duplicate, Rename and Delete** each open a dialog that says what changes and who sees it.
- Duplicate copies the inputs and opens the copy.
- Rename changes the name only.
- Delete says it cannot be undone, and that the record and the main cap table do not change. It returns to Scenarios.
- A refusal stays in its dialog, in the Worker's words.

### 5. The exit waterfall (CT9)

- **The table** at a chosen exit shows each holder's group, preference ("1× · $X", "None · common", "None · options"), whether it takes the preference or converts, and its payout. The words for "takes or converts" are mapped from the engine's `source`; an option holder exercises and takes common.
  - The unallocated pool is its own row, "Takes no proceeds".
  - The foot says the payouts add up to the exit, or to how much of it when they do not.
  - An exit the engine did not calculate says so with the engine's reason; it is never a table of zeros.
- **The step chart, "Share of the exit, by group",** draws one 100% column per exit value on a log axis, with dotted lines at "Preferences paid in full" and at each investor's conversion point. It is built as `ExitSteps` in `ui/Charts.jsx`, in D893's palette. A column the engine did not calculate is a dashed outline.
  - **Each conversion point is the engine's own test,** `preference > exit × shares ÷ total`, solved for the exit from the shares and preferences its waterfall returned. Nothing else is derived.
  - **The canvas draws a curve sampled at ninety exits.** The engine takes four per run, so the columns are drawn at the scenario's own exits, with the points marked, rather than interpolating a curve the engine never computed.
  - Before a round, a SAFE chooses cash back or conversion, so no point is drawn.
- **The Source column** (signed, document, Mercury) is not drawn: a run's rows carry no document.

### 6. Compare (CT7, CT8)

- **Columns:** Today (dotted, on record, from D1018's Today read), then each scenario.
  - Every scenario is run again at the same exits (all of theirs together, at most four), so the columns compare like with like.
  - A run that failed says "Could not be read" down its column.
- **Rows:**
  - groups and founders;
  - price per share (Today's from the record since D1283);
  - post-money;
  - each instrument's shares ("Not converted" where it did not convert).

  A founder a scenario does not hold reads "Not in this scenario".
- **Founder ownership as small multiples:** one panel per scenario, through `WeekLines`. Today is the hollow dotted point, and only for a scenario that starts from Today; a typed one starts at "Start".
- **The exit waterfall:** one panel per exit, with a stacked bar per scenario through `Columns`. "F $x" over each bar is what founders take; a bar not calculated is a dashed outline.
- **Each chart has its Table toggle,** with the same rows.
- **The assumptions line** is the Worker's own, from the Today read, with three fixed sentences when that read has not answered.
- **"Change the selection"** returns to Scenarios.

### 7. The read-backs: what is Eadwyn's and what is not

The canvas draws "Read the round back" (CT7) and "Explain the order of payment" (CT9) as Eadwyn's bands. Eadwyn's own bands, ZoneDraft's `raise/capital` and `raise/liquidity`, are on Overview (D661). Their `gather` reads the saved model only, never a chosen scenario.

So these two are read back from the engine's numbers, every step shown, and the band says so: "Read back from the engine's numbers, every step shown. Eadwyn's own read of your saved model is on Overview." No figure is written that the engine did not return, which keeps #2431's rule that Eadwyn fills no amount, price or share count.

- **The round:** the price over the shares before the round, the new money and what it buys, the top-up and who carries it, each conversion, and founders before and after.
- **The order of payment:** the preferences in all, the first and last conversion points, who takes the preference at the chosen exit, and that nothing records a seniority order. Before a round, it is who takes cash back.
- **Attach or Discard.**
  - Discard hides the band and writes nothing.
  - Attach opens a dialog, then saves the read with the scenario's inputs as `notes`, one per kind.
  - The canvas's "Attach to term sheet" is "Attach to scenario" here: this tool holds no term sheet.
  - Saving changed inputs takes an attached read off, since it would describe the old ones. The dialog says so.
  - Nothing is attached to the main cap table.

Making Eadwyn read a chosen scenario needs ZoneDraft's `gather` to take a scenario's uid. That is a follow-up, not this part.
