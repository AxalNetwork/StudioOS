### The read

`GET /api/decks/:id/analytics/daily?from&to&version&nda=all|yes|no` (`services/decks/shareDaily.ts`, mounted in `routes/deck_slide_analytics.ts`) reads the deck's venture across all its versions. Every count is over **investor links**: a share link with a prospect or an investor label.

- **`days`:** every UTC date in the range, oldest first, `{ date, nda, open, first_nda, first_open }`.
  - The two series split views by the link's `nda_required`. A first view is a link's earliest view.
  - A day with no view is a counted 0, because views are recorded, not sampled.
  - `nda=yes` or `nda=no` leaves the other class at 0 views.
- **`funnels`:** `{ nda, open }`, over the investor links sent in the range, with what their readers did at any time since.
  - **sent:** the links.
  - **opened:** links with a view.
  - **NDA shown:** equal to opened on an NDA link, because its gate shows on every open. Null on an open link, which has no NDA step.
  - **accepted:** links with an `nda_signed` conversion. Null on an open link.
  - **reached the ask:** links whose furthest counted card is the ask card or past it.
    - It is counted only over links that both send an ask card and count time per slide.
    - When no link of the class does both, it is null, never 0.
- **`dropoff`:** `{ current, previous }`, the chosen version (or the newest) and the one before it.
  - Each card gives its seconds and `drop_pct`: of the views that read the card, the share that stopped there.
  - Seconds nobody counted are null. The last card, and a card nobody reached, have no drop.
  - A row named by card uid counts for that card. A row written before migration 714 is read by place.
- **`versions`:** the venture's versions, for the filter.

### The rules

- **Bound:** the range is at most 90 days (`range_too_long`), and defaults to the 30 days ending today, UTC.
  - A bad date, a reversed range, an unknown `nda` or a bad `version` is 422.
  - There is no row cap: the date range is the bound.
- **Who:** the venture's writers only (`deckFor(…, write: true)`), as the link's time-per-slide consent. Another founder gets 403, as every deck read answers them.
  - Another venture's links, views and signatures never enter the read: every query joins through `pitch_decks.project_id`.
- **Counts only:** no hash, email or reader identity leaves the read.
- **A failed read** answers 500 `{ state: 'unreadable', detail: "Views couldn't be read." }`, which is ps7x's state.
