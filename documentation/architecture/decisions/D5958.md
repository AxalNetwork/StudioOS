### What is built

- **Placement.** "What a paid plan produces" sits on the Founder tab, between
  the AI band and Compare plans. It is static copy, so it renders while the
  plan read is loading or unreadable.
- **Founder only.** The Investor, Advisor, Operating Partner and Models tabs
  draw nothing in its place. The Investor research example stays out until
  Research v2 (#3701).
- **The two examples.**

  | Example | Plan |
  |---|---|
  | Evidence synthesis and company planning | Validate + Build |
  | A connected fundraising workflow | Raise + Grow |

- **Each example's anatomy:**
  - the label **Demonstration**, written out on a dotted outline (never colour
    alone);
  - the plan, by its card name;
  - Source facts, Eadwyn's interpretation (marked Generated) and Limitations,
    each under its own heading;
  - "See [plan]".
- **Sample data only.** Every name, quote, count and date is bracketed. The
  test fails on any digit outside brackets, and on any price, percentage,
  logo, testimonial or compliance word.
- **The "Real evidence, shared with permission" variant is not built.** Nothing
  records which customer evidence Axal VC may show.
- **How it connects:** four steps, Customer evidence → Fundraising narrative →
  Controlled sharing → Follow-up, each naming where it lives in the product.
- **After the raise:** lists exactly the four Grow rows that the comparison
  already gives Raise + Grow alone. A test holds the two lists together, so
  the section states no new entitlement.
- **The in-plan line.** A signed-in founder sees In your plan or Not in your
  plan, from `heldCard`. A visitor, or a plan the page does not know, sees
  nothing.
- **"See [plan]"** scrolls the plan card into view and focuses it. Cards take
  `tabIndex={-1}` and show a focus ring. It never opens checkout. If the cards
  are not drawn (plans unreadable), it does nothing.
- **The phone comparison:**
  - each cell says **Included** or **Not included** in words, with its glyph
    hidden from assistive technology;
  - the plan chips wrap instead of scrolling, are at least 44 px tall, and
    show a check on the chosen chip and "· Your plan" on the member's own;
  - the examples and the four steps stack.

### Departure from the canvas

PR-C1 draws the plan chips as a radio group. They stay the tabs #4655 built,
with the same arrow, Home and End behaviour and the same tab panel. The issue
requires #4655's keyboard behaviour to keep passing, and the tab pattern
already says which plan is shown.

### Not changed

No price, plan, limit or entitlement text, no route, and no Worker read.
