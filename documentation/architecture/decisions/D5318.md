### H8

**`GET /api/funds` no longer writes. The LP claim runs at sign-in and when an LP row is written; the read's ownership check is unchanged.**

**Before and after, on the #4573 fixture** (`funds.list`, account 4, second request, `main` at `d16c59081c`):

| | Total statements | Auth | Telemetry | Route | Writes (all) | Route writes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before | 8 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 1 (`UPDATE limited_partners SET user_id = ? WHERE user_id IS NULL AND LOWER(email) = LOWER(?)`) |
| After | 7 | 4 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 0 |

The `funds.list` budget in `perf_budgets_4573.test.ts` goes from route 2 to route 1.

**What changed.**

- The list handler (`funds.get('/')`) no longer calls `claimLpRowsByEmail`. Its scope is unchanged: `visibleFundsScope` still ORs the GP arm with `lpMembershipScope`, whose arm `user_id IS NULL AND LOWER(email) = LOWER(?)` shows a fund for an LP row nobody has claimed yet.
- `claimLpRowsAtSignIn(env, user)` in `services/lpClaim.ts` is the one helper every sign-in calls. It is `claimLpRowsByEmail` with the account's own address, so the existing rules hold at every sign-in: an owned row is never re-pointed, an empty address claims nothing, and a failed claim never fails the sign-in. It runs at every route that mints a session for the account's holder:
  - `routes/auth.ts`: `/confirm-verify-email` (the password sign-up's first session), `/login` (TOTP or recovery code) and `/magic/verify` (the magic link, sign-up included);
  - `routes/auth_google.ts` (`mintGoogleSession`, sign-in and sign-up);
  - `routes/auth_passkey.ts`;
  - `routes/auth_sms.ts`;
  - `routes/auth_recover.ts` (`mintRecoverySession`, behind every recovery path).
- `claimLpRowsForAddress(env, email, fundId)` runs when an LP row is written: `POST /api/funds/:id/lps` (only when the row has no `user_id`) and `POST /api/capital/investors`. It links the row only when exactly one account holds the address, and that account is verified and active. A typo that matches nobody, or an address held by two accounts that differ only by case, links nothing.

**There is no LP invite to accept.** An LP row is written by a GP (`POST /api/funds/:id/lps`) or an investor admin (`POST /api/capital/investors`). Neither route sends an invitation that an LP later accepts. The investor invitations in `deals.ts` are deal-room invites, not `limited_partners` rows. So "at acceptance" is the row's creation, and the two creation routes claim.

**Why nobody who has access today loses it.**

1. **The read is unchanged.** Visibility never depended on the claim. `lpMembershipScope`'s email arm grants an unclaimed row by the account's address. The list, and every LP surface that shares that predicate, still shows the row whether or not it has been claimed. The claim only turns that email grant into an account link.
2. **A long session when the row is created later.** Suppose an account is signed in on a 7-day session and a GP then records it as an LP. `claimLpRowsForAddress` links the row at the moment it is written. If it does not (the address is unverified, ambiguous or unknown), the email arm still shows the fund on this session, and the next sign-in claims the row.
3. **Rows that were unclaimed when this ships.** Rows that the list would have claimed on the next GET are still reachable through the email arm. They are claimed at the account's next sign-in, or by the LP surfaces that still claim on their own request (`/api/funds/lp-portal`, the fund LPA routes through `mayReadLpa`, `capital.ts`, `lp_reports.ts`, `liquidity.ts`, `fund_ledger.ts`, `fund_performance.ts`, `spinout_lab.ts`, `storagePlans.ts`, `capitalAttestations.ts`, `fundNoticeStore.ts`). H8 removes only the list's claim. The others are outside its scope and stay as they were.
4. **A claim can only ever be added.** Both new helpers go through the same `WHERE user_id IS NULL` UPDATE. Claiming earlier can never move a row away from an account that already owns it.

**What does not claim, and why.**

- Impersonation (`routes/admin.ts`) and support hand-offs (`rpc/branchOps.ts`, `POST /api/auth/support/redeem`) act as the target account. A link written on such a session would name an operator as the actor. These paths never claimed at sign-in, and a list read by an impersonator no longer claims either.
- The deck-share sign-up (`routes/deck_share_actions.ts`) creates a session for an address nobody has verified. A claim is permanent, so it waits for a verified sign-in. Until then the read is the same as before.
- An email change (`routes/settings.ts`, `/email-change/confirm`) does not claim. The change can be revoked within a window because it may be a takeover, so a permanent link should not be written while it is still open. The new address's unclaimed rows are reachable through the email arm, and the next sign-in claims them.

**Tests.** `cloudflare-worker/test/lp_claim_signin_d5318.test.ts`, with each assertion mutation-checked:

- The list runs no `UPDATE limited_partners` and leaves an unclaimed row as it found it.
- The list's LP arm still grants that row's fund.
- The sign-in helper links unclaimed rows and never re-points an owned one.
- The creation helper refuses unverified, inactive, unknown and ambiguous addresses.
- Every sign-in mint has its claim beside it, and the operator and unverified mints have none.
- Both creation routes call the creation helper.
