### Why these tests

#4615, #4616 and #4617 pinned their repairs on the pure helpers, such as `SerialSaver`, the savers,
`sessionRowState` and `buildCatalogue`. For the page wiring, they pinned the source text of `SettingsPage.jsx`.
The Worker tests sent requests one after another.

Each repair was taken back out of the code, one at a time, and the earlier suites were run against it:

- the four #4615/#4616 route and helper suites;
- `billing_page_d792`, `billing_customer_scope_d792` and `auto_reload_card_d794`;
- `connectors_page_d651` and `account_center_d568`.

The earlier suites missed 15 of the 20 mutations in the table below: F1, F3–F5, F7, F7b, F9, F10, F12–F15 and
W2–W4. The new tests catch all 20. With every repair in place, both new files pass: frontend 13/13, Worker 4/4.

The Worker's public-profile race is made certain rather than left to timing. A read named by the test (the route's
`SELECT privacy_prefs, role …`) waits until both requests have made it. Both saves therefore pass the route's own
revision check, and the compare-and-swap decides.

### Found while testing, not changed here

When the compare-and-swap refuses a public-profile save, the 409 carries the revision and effective switches that the
losing request read before the race, not the ones now stored. The client's next save then gets a second 409 that
carries the stored values, so it recovers. The cw test does not pin either body. A follow-up can re-read before
answering.

### Not changed

- No source file. The tests use synthetic accounts.
- The existing tests, including the source-text pins. They stay as cheap early warnings.
