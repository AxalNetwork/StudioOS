### 1. Stripe connection, complete (PAY5)

- **Deliveries per hour** come from part 3's read, `paymentsApi.webhookDeliveries()`. It is a fifth read on the Stripe tab and answers on its own.
- **The chart.** It is drawn with the shared `Columns` chart (D893/D929). Accepted deliveries are violet and stacked under the not-accepted ones in amber. Violet is `#a78bfa` in the dark.
- **An hour with no log.** If the request log holds nothing for an hour, the read returns null for it. That hour is drawn as `Columns`' dashed open bar, never as a zero bar, and the line under the chart names those hours ("No log was kept for 03:00 to 05:00.").
- **An empty hour that was logged.** An hour the log wrote in with no delivery is 0, and is drawn as 0.
- **Half-recorded hours.** An hour counts as logged only when both of its counts are numbers.
- **The table form.** "Table" shows the hours newest first: six at a time, then all 24. The current hour reads "· so far" and a silent hour reads "Not logged". On a phone the table is the only form (PAY12d).
- **The day's totals and last delivery** come from the same read. A count the read did not carry reads "Not recorded", never NaN or 0.
- **If the read fails,** the block says "Could not be read… This is not a claim that no delivery came" with Try again. The tab's other blocks still draw.
- **The mirror block** now says "Could not be read" with Try again when the summary fails, like the account and the key.

### 3. The Change log tab (PAY7) at `/admin/platform/payments/log`

- **Route and door.** The route sits in `App.jsx` behind `hqOnly`, like the other two. The door is a literal `HeaderTab` (D936), so the reachability walk finds it. `tabOf`, `TABS` and the page's reads know the tab.
- **The read.** `paymentsApi.changeLog` (D1011) is read by the tab itself, because its filters drive it. The page's only other read on this tab is the catalogue, which supplies product names.
- **If the catalogue fails,** a row names its product by its ID and the tab says so. Nothing else is lost.
- **The rows.** Each row shows When (UTC), Who, What, Product and Detail, newest first.
  - **What** is the action in the canvas's words ("Added a price", "Replaced the publishable key"). An action this page does not know is shown by its code, never hidden. A failed sync gets a red "✕ Sync failed" pill.
  - **Detail** is a short sentence built from what the writer recorded (for example "24 products synced", "Stripe answered 503" or "Now Live"), plus the admin's reason on its own line.
  - **Codes** (the action, the product and price IDs and the raw details) appear only under Technical details.
  - **Who** is the account's name. When the name was not recorded it reads "Account #n · name not recorded", never an email.
- **The filters:**
  - **What** offers the kinds the read returns.
  - **Who** offers the people the log has named so far in this visit. The read has no list of admins, and the page does not invent one.
  - **Product** offers the catalogue's products.
  - **From and To** are dates.
  - Changing any filter goes back to the newest page, and Clear filters resets them all.
  - The canvas's free-text search ("Search product or detail") is not drawn, because the read has no text filter.
- **Pages** hold 25 rows (the canvas's "1–25 of 142"), with Newer and Older buttons.
- **The export.** "Export CSV" opens an in-page dialog that asks why. The reason must be 10 to 500 characters, the same rule the Worker applies, and the button stays disabled until the reason meets it. The current filters go with the export.
  - If the Worker refuses (for example, `export_not_recorded` fails closed), its sentence is shown in the dialog and no file is saved.
  - If the export goes through, the dialog closes and the page says how many rows were exported and that the export and its reason were recorded.
- **Empty and failed states.** A failed read says "Could not be read. Nothing is shown rather than an empty log." with Try again. An empty log says nothing has been recorded yet and lists what will appear there. An empty result under filters says that no change matches them.

### 4. Dark mode (PAY13)

- **Shared classes.** The page's classes now live in `payUi.jsx`, and each one carries its dark colour. A source test reads every `className` in the four files: a `bg-white` must have a `dark:bg-` and a `text-axal-ink` must have a `dark:text-` on the same element.
- **The dashed hour.** `Columns`' dashed no-log bar gained `dark:stroke-gray-400`, so it stays visible on the dark card. That is the line PAY13 asks for, and it is the only change to the shared chart.

### 5. The phone layout (PAY12), below `md` (767px)

- **PAY12a, the catalogue.** It stacks into one card per product inside its group.
- **PAY12b, the product.** Tapping a card opens the product as a sheet. The sheet is read only: prices (archived ones struck through), audiences, and IDs under Technical details. Changing a product waits for part 4's lightbox.
- **PAY12c, dialogs.** Every Payments dialog is a bottom sheet. `ui/Lightbox.jsx` gains an opt-in `phone="sheet"` that pins the dialog to the bottom, sizes it to its content up to 92% of the viewport and rounds the top. The default is unchanged, so no other lightbox moves.
- **PAY12d, the chart.** On a phone the deliveries chart is the table.
- **The change log** is a list on a phone.
