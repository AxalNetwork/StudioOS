### What the canvas decides

- A directory company carries an outlined ▣ badge and an analysis candidate a violet ◎ badge on every board, so the two are never confused.
- The profile leads with identity, sector, country, official site when known, record source and the actions this member may use. Unknown optional fields are grouped into one Not recorded line.
- Each metric card shows value, currency, publisher, Observed and Updated. An unknown observation date reads "Observed: Not recorded" and never borrows the update date.
- Funding rounds, employment, financial statements and field citations are record tables, each with its own state and Retry. A recorded zero is shown as 0. Inspect opens a drawer (a sheet on phone) that separates Observed from Updated.
- "Competitors" becomes "Sector peers", and the Worker's rule is printed: same label, same country first, then by name. Direct and Adjacent appear only in an analysis with a subject, a rationale and sources; without them a category reads Unsupported.
- The CTA reads "Set up competitor analysis →" and opens setup with the same company as subject (D7041).
- The candidate page shows Signals on phone too, and keeps company signals apart from sector and country context.
- Phone 390, tablet 768, a states board (including four absences side by side), dark and a prototype board are drawn.
