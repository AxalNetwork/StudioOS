## D637 — Delivery edits, co-founder matching controls, and editing a proposed co-marketing pitch

**A partner's delivery record can be corrected without losing what it records: Clear keeps a blocker on record and Delete removes it, a live seat's scope is edited in place, and a deleted hours entry reads "Not recorded", not 0. A founder can unlist themselves and withdraw interest they sent, and sees each connection's NDA state, with the other person's email hidden until both have signed. A partner edits a co-marketing pitch while it is Proposed, sees it locked after, and sees results that read "Not recorded" until something is recorded.** Issue #1395, for the Ops canvas's area 8 (#1165): screens `sp-delivery`, `sp-cofounder`, `sp-comarketing`. No migration: every store already existed.

### Every write records its actor

`services/actorLog.ts` (`logActorAct`) writes one `activity_logs` line for a member's own write: the hashed email, the user id, the action, the project when there is one, and the ids the write touched. It never carries an address or text the person typed. It is best effort, like `logAdminAction`: a failed line warns, and never undoes a write that has already happened. The three routes below call it on every write these screens make.

### M7 — Delivery edits (`sp-delivery`)

**Where they live.** On the pages that already serve the canvas's addresses: blockers on `/delivery/health` (`HealthZone.jsx`), seats and hours on `/delivery/capacity` (`CapacityZone.jsx`). The edit pieces are `pages/partner/delivery/deliveryEdits.jsx`. No address changes, so no `ROUTE_MAP.md` row changes for these.

**Blockers.**
- **Clear** stamps `cleared_at` and keeps the blocker, struck through, with "Cleared [date] · kept on record". **Reopen** takes the stamp off.
- **Delete** is a separate button. It removes a blocker raised by mistake, after a confirm that says it is removed, not cleared.
- Before, there was one Clear/Reopen toggle, and `DELETE /blockers/:id` had no caller.
- The log line says which act it was: `delivery_blocker_cleared`, `…_reopened`, `…_edited` or `…_deleted` (with `was_cleared`).
- A failed blocker read shows its sentence and Try again.

**Seats.**
- A live seat's scope is edited in place through `PATCH /seats/:id`, which had no caller.
- The scope stays free text, the client's own words. Board, KPIs, Roadmap and Data room are toggles that add or remove those words.
- A revoked seat's scope is history: the route now refuses the edit (409, as a sentence), and the page shows no editor.

**Hours.**
- `/capacity` sums hours per person, so no single entry could be found there to delete. `GET /partner/delivery/capacity/hours?period=` (new) lists each client × person pair with an entry in the month asked or the two before it. Each month holds its hours, or `null`.
- The page draws `null` as "Not recorded" ("No entry for this month. Not the same as 0."). Each entry has Delete, which calls the existing `DELETE` route. No screen called that route before.
- **Periods are monthly.** The canvas draws weeks (W41, W40), but hours have always been logged per `YYYY-MM` period (migration 208, `parsePeriod`), so the page shows months.
- **The delete route is checked like the save.** The period must be a real month (400 otherwise). The person id must be a positive integer. Deleting an entry that does not exist is a 404 with a sentence, not a silent success.
- The person is not required to still be in the firm: the engagement is the firm's, and an entry logged for someone who has since left can still be a mistake to remove.

### M8 — Co-founder matching (`sp-cofounder`)

**Where it lives.** A new `cofounders` tab on `/build/team` (`FounderTeamPage.jsx`), behind the same Studio tier gate as Co-founder Match. It renders `CofounderMatchingPanel.jsx`, laid out for a 390-wide phone. The legacy `?tab=cofounder` still lands on Hiring, where the browse and profile form remain.

**The panel.**
- **Your listing:** Listed or Unlisted.
  - "Unlist me" calls `DELETE /cofounder/me`, which already set `listed = 0` but had no caller.
  - "List me again" saves the profile with `listed: true`.
  - Unlisting keeps the profile: browse stops showing it, and new interest is refused. Interest already sent and connections already made stay.
- **Interest you sent:** `GET /cofounder/interests/sent` (new) lists the interest not withdrawn, newest first. Each row has:
  - the person's name;
  - what they are looking for;
  - whether they are still listed;
  - the connection uid once the interest was mutual.

  It never carries an email. "Withdraw interest" calls the existing `DELETE /interest/:user_uid`, which had no caller.
- **Connections:** each shows its status (Waiting for both NDAs, Active or Closed), who has signed and when, and the other person's name. Their email is shown only when the Worker returns it, which D610 allows once both have signed. Until then the line reads "Email: shown once both NDAs are signed."; for a connection closed before both signed, it says the email was not shared.

**Refusals.** Every refusal on the interest path, and the closed-connection refusal on signing, now goes through `refuse`, with its code and a sentence. Unlisting with no profile is a 404 with a sentence.

**Logs.** These writes are logged: unlist (only when it was listed), interest sent, interest withdrawn, NDA signed (with `both_signed`), and connection closed. The close reason is not copied into the log.

### M9 — A proposed co-marketing pitch (`sp-comarketing`)

**Where it lives.** Nothing served the canvas's `/comarketing/[pitch]`, so it is a new address: `/comarketing/:pitch` (`CoMarketingPitchPage.jsx`), for admin and partner, inside the partner's Offers tabs. "My pitches" links each pitch to it, as Edit while Proposed and Open after.

**The pitch.**
- `GET /comarketing/me/pitches/:uid` (new) returns one pitch to the partner who owns it, or to an admin. Anyone else gets a 404, so a uid tells nobody whether the pitch exists.
- **While Proposed,** the form is prefilled with type, title, angle, audience, what you bring and summary. It saves through the existing PATCH, which no screen called.
  - The PATCH now enforces create's floors: a title of at least 3 characters and a summary of at least 10.
  - Its refusals are sentences.
- **Approved, published, rejected or withdrawn:** every field is disabled, with a note.
  - An approved pitch's note reads "Approved on [date]. The form is locked; to change the pitch, withdraw it and propose again."
  - The Worker refuses the edit too, as `pitch_locked` with `lockedPitchSentence(status)`.

**Results.**
- Visits, sign-ups, leads and conversions come from `comarketing_attributions`.
- A kind with no event reads "Not recorded", never 0. A count of 0 there only ever means nothing was stored.
- **Visits are now recorded.** The Published feed's View link calls `trackCoMarketing` (`recordFeedVisit`) when clicked. The call is fire and forget: the link opens either way. Nothing called it before, so every result read 0.
- Nothing records sign-ups, leads or conversions yet, and the page says so.

**A visit is not a lead.** `POST /comarketing/track` stored the caller's email as `lead_email` for every event. Feed visits now reach it from any signed-in reader, so a visit stores no address unless the caller names one. A sign-up, lead or conversion still records it. Track's refusals are sentences.

**Logs.** These writes are logged: an edit (with the field names, never their values) and a withdrawal (with the status it left).
