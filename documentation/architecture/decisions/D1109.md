## D1109 — Data room part 8: the old pages retire, their addresses forward with DR24's notice (#2989)

- **Status:** built by S14, 2026-10-26 (#2989, part 8 of #2456, under D1101).
- **Basis:** `origin/main`, which carries parts 1 to 7 (D1101 to D1108) and D1284.
- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** links only. No route and no read changes.
- **Canvas:** boards DR24, DR24a (Files) and DR24b (Access). With this part, `Data Room · Boards.dc.html` and `Raise Data Room Page.dc.html` graduate to `design/canvases/integrated/`, unedited. D374's backlog canvas `Data Room.dc.html` moves to `scraps/`.

### 1. What is deleted

- `frontend/src/pages/raise/DataRoomPage.jsx` is deleted. It was the files tool at `/raise/data-room?mode=workspace`.
- `frontend/src/pages/founder/FounderRaiseDataRoom.jsx` and its stylesheet are deleted. They were the read-only access view at `?mode=access`.
- A file's record page at `/raise/data-room/<id>` is no longer drawn. CycleRoute does not read the record.
- `dataRoomRead.js` keeps only the readings other pages still use:
  - the investor access rule (`accessNow`, `noAccessReason`, `tierOf`, `expiryMs`, `grantIsActive`);
  - the queue's refusal;
  - `tierRefusal`;
  - `grantAttribution`.

  The page's own helpers go with the page: `roomStats`, the tree, the activity filters and the download label.

### 2. What moved, so nothing is lost

The old page held three things the tool had not drawn yet:

- **The advisor grant.** `AdvisorGrantSection` is now on Access, for the owner only.
- **The scoring report's shares.** They are read-only on Access, in `ReportShares.jsx`.
  - Every share is listed in every state: active, expired, every open used, revoked. The old page showed active shares only, so a revoked link could not be seen.
  - An unreadable end date says "a date not recorded".
  - An unread list is Unreadable, never "shared with no one".
- **The admin summary (D1102).** It moved verbatim to `DataRoomSummary.jsx`.

Share's success line carries the old page's hedge: "If <email> has a StudioOS account, <investor> can open the room now. Nobody is invited by email." A grant to an address with no account opens nothing, so the line never claims access the Worker did not give.

### 3. The forwards (DR24)

| Old address | Lands on | Notice |
|---|---|---|
| `/raise/data-room?mode=workspace` | `/raise/data-room/files` | The data room workspace is now Data room › Files. |
| `/raise/data-room?mode=access` | `/raise/data-room/access` | The access view is now Data room › Access. |
| `/raise/data-room/<id>` (digits) | `/raise/data-room/file/<id>` | A file’s page is now its window over Data room › Files. |

How the forwards work:

- **The address.** Each forward is `Navigate replace`. It keeps the rest of the query (`project_id`) and the hash, drops `mode`, and sets `moved=<kind>`.
- **The notice.** The landing tab shows the one-line notice under its header (`useMovedNotice`). Dismiss drops `moved` and keeps the rest.
- **An investor** still goes to `/research/diligence` first, as before.
- **Counting (#2319).** The tool's own tab words share `/raise/data-room/:id` with these forwards. The address-bar beacon cannot tell them apart, so each forward counts itself once under its own key, `redirectKey('/raise/data-room?mode=workspace')` and so on.
  - The keys are `FORWARD_REDIRECTS` in `redirectRoutes.ts`, marked `forward: true`.
  - The beacon skips them, so nothing is counted twice.
  - The #2301 guard leaves them out of its App comparison.
