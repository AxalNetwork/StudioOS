## D578 — A startup's SAFEs are recorded per investor, and sent only once counsel's text is in

**The Lab Capital page records the terms of each SAFE in the round: the
investor, the amount and the post-money valuation cap.** Each SAFE's status
is read from the e-sign envelope it was sent in. Nothing can be sent until a
counsel-approved SAFE text is registered. Slot S08, issue #1205, 2026-10-06.
Migration 410.

### The decisions

- **Owner, on #1205: A.** Counsel supplies or approves the SAFE text. The
  text has not been supplied.
- **S1, for the owner (2026-10-06): Q1 A.** The form is a post-money SAFE
  with a valuation cap and no discount.
- **S1, for the owner (2026-10-06): Q2 A.** The terms go in a
  `safe_issuances` table, linked to the SAFE's envelope.

### The store (migration 410)

Each `safe_issuances` row holds:
- `project_id`;
- an optional `prospect_id` (the round's `raise_prospects` row);
- `investor_name`;
- `form` (always `post_money_cap`, held by a CHECK);
- `amount_cents` and `valuation_cap_cents` (positive integers);
- `currency`;
- `envelope_id`, which is NULL until the SAFE is sent;
- `created_by`.

### The route (`routes/safe_issuances.ts`, `/api/safe-issuances`)

- **`GET /project/:projectId`** returns the form, `sendable`, and each SAFE
  with `discount: null` and its status.
- **`POST /project/:projectId`** records terms. It refuses:
  - a blank name;
  - an amount or a cap that is not whole positive cents;
  - any discount;
  - a prospect from another round.
- **`POST /:uid/send`** answers 409 `safe_text_not_approved` while no SAFE
  text is registered. `safeSendable()` is true only once
  `templateKeyForDocType('safe')` resolves, which is the same registry the
  e-sign routes use.
- **Who.** A founder or company editor records (`canAccessProject` with
  `write`). An advisor member and an admin read. An investor, another
  founder, and anyone else get 404.

### Status comes only from the envelope

- **No envelope:** "Draft · not sent".
- `sent`: "Sent".
- `partially_signed`: "Signed by some parties".
- `completed`: "Countersigned".
- `rejected`: "Declined".
- `void`: "Voided".

An `envelope_id` with no envelope behind it is still a draft. This is the
issue's "no row reads Sent or Countersigned without an envelope behind it".

### The card (`SafeIssuancesCard.jsx`)

- **What it lists.** The SAFEs, with investor, amount, cap, Discount ("None in
  this form") and status.
- **What it can do.** It records new terms. A round's prospect can fill in the
  investor's name.
- **What it cannot do.** It offers no send button. It says that sending waits
  on the counsel-approved text.
- **When the read fails,** it says Unreadable.

### Not built (waits on the text)

These pieces wait on the counsel-approved text:
- registering the body and its merge fields;
- refusing a send with any field unfilled (D411);
- drafting one SAFE per investor and sending it through the e-sign routes.

`LEGAL_REVIEW.md` records the request. This issue stays open for it.
