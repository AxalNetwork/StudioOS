## D5879 — One registry of approval-lane facts (#5879)

Part 6/8 (a) of #4716. Before this change, an approval lane's facts were written in four places, and nothing held those places to each other:
- **The inbox sources.** Label, who a decision notifies, and the page that decides the lane, in `services/approvalSources.ts` and `services/hqApprovalSources.ts`.
- **The inbox groups.** The lane's group, in `services/approvalInbox.ts`'s `LANE_GROUP`.
- **The decision log.** Whether the lane records its decisions, from when, with what caveat or reason, and its scope, in `services/approvalDecisions.ts`'s `DECISION_LANES`.
- **The decision log's labels.** A second label map, built from the two source lists.

### The decision

`cloudflare-worker/src/services/approvalLaneRegistry.ts` holds every lane's facts once, as `APPROVAL_LANES`, in the canvas's order: every studio's lanes, then HQ's. Each entry states:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `label` | the lane's name on the board, and in the decision log |
| `group` | its chip on APR1 (`INBOX_GROUPS`, which moved here too) |
| `scope` | `studio`, or `hq` for HQ's own queue, which only a Super Admin sees (D1040) |
| `notifies` | who a decision tells today (D1041) |
| `decidedOn` | the page that decides it. `null` with an `undecidedReason` when nothing decides it yet |
| `decisionPath` | the lane's own address, only when it has one |
| `decisions` | the decision log's capability (D1044): `{ recorded: true, since, caveat? }` or `{ recorded: false, reason }` |

The services read these facts from the registry, and each keeps only its own queries:
- **Inbox sources.** `APPROVAL_SOURCES` and `HQ_APPROVAL_SOURCES` spread `studioSource(key)` or `hqSource(key)`, then add their count and rows SQL.
- **`LANE_GROUP`.** Derived from the registry. `approvalInbox.ts` re-exports it and `INBOX_GROUPS`, so every existing import still works.
- **`DECISION_LANES`.** Spreads `recordedLane(key)` or `unrecordedLane(key)`, then adds its SQL. `laneLabel` reads the registry.

Each helper throws when the module loads if the registry disagrees with how it is used:
- a key with no entry;
- `studioSource` on an HQ lane, or `hqSource` on a studio lane;
- `recordedLane` on a lane that does not record its decisions, or `unrecordedLane` on one that does.

So a lane cannot be recorded on Decided while the registry says it is unrecorded, or the other way round.

### No behaviour change

Every value was moved from where it was written on main, unchanged.
- **Same objects.** Before the change, every source object, decision lane, label and group was serialised (keys sorted, functions named). After it, the serialisation is byte-identical.
- **Same shapes.** Studio lanes still carry no `scope` field, and the decision lanes keep their exact fields.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/approval_lane_registry_5879.test.ts` has six tests:
- every lane key has a label, a group, a scope, a notice, a destination or an undecided reason, and a decision-history entry;
- HQ scope, the `hq` group and the HQ source list name the same lanes;
- the inbox sources, `LANE_GROUP` and the decision log carry the registry's values, in its order;
- a recorded lane is never unrecorded on Decided, and a recorded lane has a query;
- the helpers refuse a lane the registry does not describe.

The existing suites pass unchanged. That includes the three the issue names, `approval_lanes_d1041`, `approval_history_5302` and `admin_approvals_d1040`. In all, 278 tests across the 29 Worker test files that import the approval services pass.

**Mutation checks.** Nine mutants, all killed:
- `best_fit` with no group;
- `kyc` with no scope;
- `articles` with no decision entry;
- a new lane key with no facts;
- `best_fit` marked unrecorded;
- `grants` moved out of the `hq` group;
- the `score_flags` destination dropped;
- a decision lane overriding the registry's `since`;
- a source overriding the registry's label.

### Not in this change

The frontend's lane constants (`approvalsModel.js`, `LANES` and `HQ_LANES`) stay as they are. Holding them to this registry is the 6/8 (b) task, which follows this one.
