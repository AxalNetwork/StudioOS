## D6865 — Grow › Feedback v2, part 1: the customer link, origin, counts, a theme's weight and Close the loop (#6865)

**Date:** 2026-11-16 · **Issue:** #6865 (the build of #6099's canvas, D6099) · **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Grow v2 Feedback Page.dc.html` (GR-21 to GR-31) · **Follow-up:** #6891

### Built

**Worker**
- **Migration `202611161128_6865_feedback_contact_origin.sql`.** It adds two columns to `product_feedback`:
  - `contact_uid` names the asker's `contacts` record in the same venture. The typed name, email and company stay as the fallback and are never cleared by a link.
  - `origin` records where the item came from: `inbox_reply`, `interview`, `notion`, `posthog_survey` or `typed`. Rows written before this read Not recorded.
- **Writes.**
  - `POST /feedback` and `PATCH /feedback/:uid` take `contact_uid` (a contact of this venture, else 404; `''` or `null` unlinks) and `origin`.
  - A body with no origin is `typed`, and a PostHog proposal is `posthog_survey`.
  - The Notion importer writes `notion`.
- **The list read (`GET /modules/product-improvement`).**
  - Writers now also get `from_email`, `from_company`, `notify_on_ship`, `told_at`, `told_via` and the linked record (`contact`).
  - Everyone gets `origin` and `shipped_on`, plus `parts.counts`: open items, themes, and shipped and not yet told. The counts name nobody. A failed count read is `null`, never zeros.
- **`GET /feedback/weight?project&theme`, one theme's weight.** It is a writer's read because it names people.
  - **Who asked:** distinct people, keyed by linked record, then email (any case), then typed name. An item that names nobody is counted apart.
  - **Revenue:** recurring revenue on the ledger (`revenue_entries`, `revenue_type = 'recurring'`) received in the last 60 days under each asker's company name. This is the rule the record view's Plan row already used. Each currency is summed apart. An asker with no company, or whose company has no such revenue, is listed Not recorded with the reason, never as $0.
  - **Roadmap items:** those that answer the theme.
  - **Close the loop:** the theme's rows (`loop`), each Told, Due, or Cannot with its reason.
  - No impact, rank or value number is computed.
- **`POST /feedback/told {uids, via}`, Mark told for several.**
  - It tells only rows that are shipped, asked to be told and not told yet. The SQL guards the same three conditions again, so a row told meanwhile is not told twice.
  - Every other row comes back named with its reason: Not shipped yet, Did not ask to be told, Already told, or Not found. Another venture's item reads as Not found.
  - Each told row with an email returns a mailto draft. Axal sends nothing.
  - It writes one activity row with the count.
- **The record view (`services/records/grow.ts`).**
  - An Origin fact.
  - A Record row in the Customer panel: the linked record's name, audience and status, or Not linked.
  - Edit's "Customer or prospect record" chooser, listing up to 200 of the venture's contacts.

**Frontend (`pages/cycles/FeedbackLoop.jsx`, `feedbackLoopModel.js`)**, on `/grow/product-improvement`:
- **Counts strip:** three tiles with what they count. An unreadable read says so and draws no 0.
- **Themes:** ranked by item count, as a column on desktop and a scrolling chip row on a phone.
- **The selected theme's weight:** people with their record or Not linked, revenue per currency or Not recorded, roadmap items, and an Unreadable card with Try again.
- **Close the loop:**
  - A checkbox appears on Due rows only; every other row says why.
  - "How you told them", then Mark told, in a bar that stays visible.
  - The result names who had no email and every refused row, and gives Write email links.
- **Advisors** (no `parts.themes`) see the counts only.
- **Client methods:** `cyclesWrite.grow.feedbackWeight` and `cyclesWrite.grow.feedbackTold`.
- Touch targets are 44px on a phone, and dark tokens are used throughout.
