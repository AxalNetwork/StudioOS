## D4817 part 9 — PF-09 Ask for this report and Waive this period, with Review, confirm and Receipt (#4817)

Part of #5311 (PF-09): the approved addendum's reporting actions on the company workspace's Reports (`design/incoming/Portfolio v2 · Addendum.dc.html`, frames PFA03, PFA04, PFA05 and PFA10, drawn in `Portfolio v2 Page.dc.html`). PF-06 (D4817 part 6) left the two actions exactly as they were; this part rebuilds them. A small Worker change, no migration.

**What was there.** The Reports timeline (`CompanyReports.jsx`, RPT11, D1061) had an "Ask for this report" and a "Waive this period" button per period, each opening a one-step dialog that posted `POST /api/reporting/periods/:uid/ask` or `/waive` and closed. The Worker answered `{ ok, asked, chase_uid }` and `{ ok, waived }`. The company read said only `asked: true|false` per period, and `waived_at` without who. Nothing told the page whether this session could act.

**What is built.**
- **The obligation card** (PFA03 desktop, PFA10 phone), new: `frontend/src/pages/portfolio/ReportAsk.jsx` (`ReportObligation`) over the pure `reportAsk.js` (`obligationOf`). It shows the most pressing open period (missing, then late, then due, then upcoming; then the earliest due). Its facts are:
  - company and period, with the period's dates;
  - the report the duty asks for, and its due day;
  - received (or "Not received");
  - what is missing: the duty's required figures and documents;
  - your last ask, from the record;
  - the source document and clause;
  - the holding route.
  The two actions are drawn only where the Worker would accept them: Ask while late or missing and not yet asked by you, Waive while not sent or waived. With no route or source recorded, each says Not recorded with its reason.
- **Review** (PFA04, PFA05): one Sheet per act (`ReportActionSheet`). It names who acts (you, the holder), what is recorded, who is told (the founder team of the company, in the app and by email; no other investor) or who sees it (the founders, on their desk), and the company, period, holding route and source. "Step 1 of 2 · Review. Nothing is sent until you confirm." The Waive Review says the waiver stores no reason, so it offers no note field (as the addendum decided), and that it does not waive future periods or change the signed duty.
- **An explicit confirm naming the act:** "Ask Northwind Labs · Q3 2026", "Waive Northwind Labs · Q3 2026".
- **Receipt**, from the Worker's answer only (`receiptOf`). For an ask: the stored time, by you, the company, the period, how many in-app notices were stored for the founder team, that email delivery is not confirmed here, and the chase reference. For a waive: the stored time, by you, and "this period only". A field the answer lacks reads Not recorded with its reason; nothing is filled in from the browser.
- **A failed send keeps the draft.** The Review stays open with the company and period still chosen. The Worker's own sentence and code show in an alert ("Not sent. Unreadable: reporting could not be read or saved. Try again. · reporting_unreadable"), and the same confirm can be pressed again. A conflict (`already_asked`, `already_waived`, `not_late`, `period_sent`) is shown the same way, never guessed at.
- **The state afterwards** comes from the record. Closing the Receipt reads the company again (`retry('owed')`). The period then reads, on the card and on its timeline row:
  - "You asked on 14 Nov 2026, 09:12 UTC";
  - "Waived by you on …";
  - "Waived on … · who waived it is not recorded".
  The press alone never marks a period asked or waived.
- **Who can act.** The company read says so (`can_act`). A support session sees the obligation and the timeline with no Ask or Waive anywhere (card, timeline row, or the phone's period sheet), and the Worker's sentence in their place. Its write was already refused 403, and still is.
- **The timeline** keeps Read and its phone list. Its Ask and Waive buttons (and the phone sheet's pinned ones) now open this same Review through `onAct`. The old one-step `AskDialog` and `WaiveDialog` are removed. Each row gains the record line. A waived row's sub-line no longer says "You waived this period" when the record does not say who.

**The Worker (minimal, `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/reporting.ts`; no migration, the columns exist since migration 579).**
- `GET /portfolio` and `GET /companies/:projectUid` add `can_act` (false for a support session) and `act_refusal` (the sentence the write would refuse with).
- Each period adds two fields:
  - `asked_at`: the caller's own ask, from the `reporting_events` audit row;
  - `waived_by_you`: true, false, or null where the row records no author.
  Another investor's ask is never read.
- `POST /periods/:uid/ask` answers with the stored audit row read back (`asked_at`), the period (`uid`, `label`), `by_you`, and `told: { team, in_app }`. That is the founder team's size, and how many in-app notices `notify` actually stored. A notice the member switched off is not counted.
- `POST /periods/:uid/waive` answers with the period's stored `waived_at` and `waived_by_you`.
- Writes are the investor's own and were already audited in `reporting_events`. Nothing new is written, and every statement is bound.

**Departures from the addendum, and why.**
- **"Last chase · by Morgan":** the investor reads only their own asks (D1058: an investor never sees another investor), so the card says "Your last ask" with its time, or "You have not asked for this period". Another holder's chase is not shown.
- **"Missing: Q3 report · 6 required KPIs"** is drawn from the duty's required figures and documents. It is not a comparison against a received report, because none was received for an open period.
- **Delivery outcomes** ("request recorded, not sent, unconfirmed, cooldown or conflict"). The Worker records the ask and stores in-app notices. It has no cooldown other than once per period, and it does not record email delivery. So the Receipt shows the stored ask, the in-app notices stored, and "Email · Delivery is not confirmed here". A conflict is the Worker's 409 sentence.
- **The obligations table at the top of PFA03** (every company) is PF-04's inbox (`/portfolio/updates`). On a company's own Reports, the card shows the most pressing open period, and the timeline below lists the rest, each with its own actions.
- **The holding note for a syndicate** (RPT13, "only the SPV's lead can waive a period") is unchanged. The Worker accepts a waive from the holder of the duty whatever the route, as before; this part does not change who may waive.
