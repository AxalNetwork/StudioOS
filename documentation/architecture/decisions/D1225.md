### 1. What is built

`frontend/src/pages/founder/RaiseCallPage.jsx` at `/raise/calls/:uid`. It keeps its route, the Raise shell, the rail and D686's redirects, and draws from these new files:
- **`raiseCallModel.js`:** pure. The state, the line, the strip, the funnel, the rows, the timeline, the extension rule and the refusals.
- **`CallDialogs.jsx`:** Extend, Close and Answer.
- **`CallRecipientDrawer.jsx`:** one recipient.

**CLS8, an open call.**
- **The head:** "‹ Calls", the call's name, the state chip, and the line. The line reads like "Sent 1 Oct 2026 · closes 15 Nov 2026 · SAFE · cap $10,000,000 · minimum $25,000", showing the instrument's own terms only.
- **The strip:**
  - Indicated of the target, "indicative", with a bar;
  - Recipients, as "[n] chosen · [n] matching";
  - Opened;
  - Data room ("asked for access");
  - Questions waiting;
  - Not told, with "See who", which filters the responses to those not told. A draft's not-told is "Not recorded", never 0 (D1222's `null`).
- **The funnel:** "From recipients to amounts", with its sentence and "View as table".
- **Responses:**
  - **Columns:** Investor (with "Chosen from your pipeline" or "Matching · named once they responded"), Furthest response, Amount indicated ("Recorded"), Last activity, and On Axal ("Told on Axal" or an amber "Not told").
  - **Tools:** search, the filters All, Indicated, Data room, Opened, Not opened and Not told, Sort (Furthest along by default, or Latest), "1–25 of n" and pages.
  - **Opening:** a row opens its recipient.
- **Questions:** "[n] waiting" in amber; each question with who asked and when.
  - An answered question shows "Answered [day]" and the answer.
  - An unanswered one shows Answer, which opens CLS10's dialog.
  - A reply from an update links to its source update, as before.
- **The foot:** "Investors keep what they already sent.", Extend the date, and Close the call.

**CLS9, the other states.**
- **Draft:** "Not sent." and Continue draft, which opens D653's sheet on the draft.
- **Awaiting counsel:** "Sent … · asked to extend to … on …" and "Responses are paused until counsel approves the extended terms."
  - Close stays, because the Worker takes a close while counsel has it.
  - Extend and Answer are not offered.
- **Expired:** "This call closed on [close] with $X indicated. It is read-only." with Open a new call.
- **Closed:** the same sentence, with no button.

**CLS10, every change in a dialog.**
- **Extend:** a new closing date, after the current one and not past, which is the Worker's rule. It also says "Extending pauses responses until counsel approves the new terms. Investors keep what they already sent." The button is "Extend and ask counsel".
- **Close:** "Investors can no longer respond. Nothing already indicated is lost.", with Keep it open or Close the call.
- **Answer:** the question, the answer field, and "Investors who asked see your answer.".
- **A refusal** stays in its dialog, keyed on `code`, with Reload where the call changed (CLS12's "This call changed since you opened it.") and "Nothing you entered was saved.".

**CLS11, one recipient.**
- **A chosen investor, or a matching one who has responded:**
  - their email;
  - "Told on Axal" or "Not told", from `delivery_state`;
  - their furthest response;
  - the amount indicated, with "Indicative. It is an intention, not a payment and not a signed investment.";
  - their timeline (opened, asked for the data room, indicated);
  - their own questions, matched on their address;
  - "Open in Capital relationships".
- **A matching investor who has not responded:**
  - "Matching investor", "Name hidden until they respond";
  - why they match (`reasons`);
  - Opened as "Not shown until they respond";
  - whether they were told;
  - "Their name, firm and email appear here once they ask for the data room, ask a question or indicate an amount."

**CLS14.**
- **A co-founder** (D1222's `relation: 'member'`) drafts and answers. Extend and Close are not drawn, and the foot says "Only the venture's owner can send, extend or close a call." The buttons follow `can`.
- **An admin the Worker refuses** reads "Calls belong to the venture's founders. Studio admins do not open them." with Go to Studio. A Super Admin is counsel and is still sent to `/round-calls/:uid`, where counsel's view is.

**Indicated, never committed.** The page never says "Committed". D653's wire field stays `commitment_cents`, and the action stays `commit`.

**A matching investor is nameless until they respond.**
- The Worker sends no name, id or email for such a recipient.
- The page also draws no opened time or last activity for them, though the read carries `opened_at`.
- They take no search, and every filter but All and Not told leaves them out.

### 2. Departures, for review

- **Delete draft (CLS9)** is not drawn. No route deletes a draft, and the Worker-first rule keeps a door off the page until one does.
- **Eadwyn's proposed answer and "Draft with Eadwyn" (CLS8)** are not drawn. No route proposes an answer, and the AI never fills a money term.
- **Their firm and role** ("Chosen · [Name], Partner") are not drawn. The read carries a recipient's name and email, not a firm. The investor column says chosen or matching.
- **"Told on Axal [date]"** is drawn without a date. The read carries `delivery_state`, not `notified_at`.
- **"You granted access on 9 Oct"** in the timeline is not drawn. The read carries no grant date.
- **CLS14's Explorer card** is not drawn here. `/raise/calls/:uid` is guarded to founders and admins, so an Explorer never reaches the page.
- **"Only [Founder A] can send or close a call"** reads "Only the venture's owner…": the read does not name the owner.
- **"‹ Calls"** goes to `/raise/calls`, which redirects to wherever the Calls list lives (D1221's `/raise/capital-relationships/calls` once #3210 lands).

- "committed" in a state line;
- Extend without its dialog;
- a refusal closing its dialog;
- a co-founder offered Extend;
