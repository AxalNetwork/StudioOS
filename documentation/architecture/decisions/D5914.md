### One shell

Every consequential deal dialog is drawn in `DealActionDialog`, which is the shared `Lightbox` with three promises added:

- **Focus.** Focus moves into the dialog, Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside, Escape and the backdrop close it, and focus returns to the control that opened it. On a phone it is a bottom sheet with 44px buttons.
- **Refusals.** A refusal stays in the dialog in the Worker's own sentence, followed by "Nothing was recorded." when the sentence does not already say so. A failure with no sentence (the network) reads as the dialog's own fallback, never as a raw error. A check made before anything is sent (a missing reason, an unticked confirmation) is said the same way and sends nothing. Commit keeps D6191's `commitRefusal` wording.
- **Never a browser prompt or confirm.**

### What each dialog asks

| Dialog | Where | Asks | Sends |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pass | Deal flow, New for you, Screening, the peek | A reason (required), when it comes back, a private note (optional) | `POST /api/deals/flow/:dealId/pass` |
| Move to diligence | Screening's Move to diligence | Nothing: it states what the move does | `PATCH /api/deals/flow/:dealId`, or `POST /api/deals/flow` for a deal not yet in the flow |
| Commit | The deal page's Commit and Change amount… | Amount in the deal's currency (with the minimum), the firm, the route preference, a note (optional), the confirmation | `POST /api/deals/:id/commitments` |
| Withdraw | The Investment tab's Withdraw… | A reason (required) | `POST /api/deals/:id/commitments/:uid/withdrawal` |
| Record transfer | The deal page's Transfers tab (an Admin) | Amount, reference, phone-verified and who confirmed it (an Admin, or Not recorded), a note | `POST /api/deals/:id/transfers` |

### Departures from DE14

| Board element | Built | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Move to diligence: "opens a diligence checklist" | "It does not open a diligence case: open one on the deal's Diligence tab." | The flow move records the firm's stage and nothing else; a diligence case is its own step (D1153). The founder is not notified, as drawn. |
| Move to diligence on the deal flow board | Stays immediate with Undo | D1241 makes a forward move immediate with Undo; the dialog is where the canvas draws Move to diligence as a button (Screening). |
| Pass: reasons and note only | Also "When it comes back" | The Worker records it with every pass (D6148). |
| Commit: "Commit €100K" | "Commit €100,000"; a change says "Save the change" and starts from the live row | Whole amounts in the deal currency; D6191. The route preference (D6195) stays in the dialog. |
| Commit: Firm and Minimum ✓ | The firm from the company switcher, or "None chosen…"; the minimum as a line under the amount | The Worker takes the firm from the switcher or the only firm held; no firm is guessed. |
| Withdraw: "the round shows €520K confirmed after this" | Not drawn: "It stops counting toward the round." | No read gives the round's total after the withdrawal; a computed figure from the round read and this commitment would be a guess while another investor's commitment can change. |
| Record transfer: "closing condition is still open (cap table)" | "N IC conditions are still open on this deal. The transfer is refused until …" | The Worker's gate is open IC conditions (`transfer-gate`, D1155), which it enforces; the closing checklist does not hold a wire. A condition on a decision the recorder cannot see is said without a count. |
| Record transfer: "Phone verified ✓ with Name A, 14:05" | A checkbox and an optional Admin verifier | The Worker stores who confirmed it (D6889), not the call's time. |
| Preview: "Commit as Super Admin" | Return to HQ only | D5915, the owner's answer B. |
| Phone and dark | Bottom sheet at 390 with 44px targets; every surface has its dark pair | DE14 draws desktop and light only. |

The closing workspace's own record-a-wire form (`ClosingZone.jsx`, D6889) is not moved into the shell by this change.
