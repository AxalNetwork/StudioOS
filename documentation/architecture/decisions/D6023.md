## D6023 — Edit, close and reopen a feature's risk from its record (#6023)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-15 by S05. Filed under #4846.
- **No migration.** `venture_risks.mitigation`, `state` and `updated_at` already exist.

### What changed

- **Route.** The inline `PATCH /api/cycles/build/risks/:id` handler in `routes/cycle_build.ts` is now an exported `patchRisk`, as `postRisk` is. The move changes nothing it does: the same reads, refusals, update and `build_risk_changed` row on the risk's feature (or `project`).
- **Read.** The Solution design record's Risks section reads each risk's id, state and mitigation. A row is the title, then `Owner: … · State: … · Mitigation: …`, each `Not recorded` when empty, with the severity as its pill.
- **Actions.** The record declares three, each through `viaRoute(…, patchRisk, …)`, each naming its risk in `references` (`venture_risks`, proven within the venture), and each refusing with a 404 a risk tied to another feature:
  - `edit-risk`, "Edit a risk": a risk, then title, severity and mitigation. A blank field keeps what is stored, and an edit that changes nothing is a 400.
  - `close-risk`, "Close a risk": offers the risks not closed; sets `closed`. Closing a closed risk is a 409.
  - `reopen-risk`, "Reopen a risk": offers the closed risks; sets `open`. Reopening one that is not closed is a 409.
  - A choice with a single risk opens with it chosen. None of the three is declared on a feature with no risks, and Close and Reopen only when they have a risk to offer.
- **Rows.** The records contract gives a row one `action`, and a row's form opens with no values (D6020). Each risk row's action is Edit a risk, its body naming the risk. Close and Reopen sit among the record's own actions. The renderer is not changed.
- **Readers.** The route already strips row actions and record actions below the write gate. A reader sees the rows and runs nothing (403).

### Choices

1. **A blank field keeps the stored value.** Edit opens blank, so a mitigation that a blank could clear would be wiped by an edit of the severity alone. A mitigation is therefore not cleared from this form. `PATCH /risks/:id` with `mitigation: null` still clears it.
2. **A closed risk stays in the module chart's counts.** The Risks stack counts every risk by severity, whatever its state. This is the issue's default. The owner may decide instead that a closed risk leaves the count, which is one `AND state <> 'closed'` in `solution()`'s count.
3. **`mitigating` is not offered.** The route accepts it, and a risk already in that state reads Mitigating and is offered by Close. Nothing on the record sets it.

### Later

When #6329 (D6113) lands `Row.actions`, row `values` and `rowOnly`, a follow-up can put Close or Reopen on the row beside Edit, open Edit on that risk's values, and let a mitigation be cleared there.
