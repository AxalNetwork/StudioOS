## D1100 — Bound subscription retry grace to period end plus 21 days

#2978, part of #2451, corrects D1052's unbounded `past_due` entitlement.
`middleware/planSubscription.ts` provides `inGrace(status, renewsAt, now)` and
`GRACE_DAYS = 21`. Twenty-one days covers Stripe's default smart-retry window
without delegating indefinite access to an account's dashboard configuration.
Active and trialing subscriptions pass. Past-due subscriptions pass through the
inclusive cutoff (`now <= period end + 21 UTC days`); missing, malformed or
unreadable dates fail closed. Unpaid and both canceled/cancelled spellings fail.
ISO timestamps and SQLite's offset-less UTC timestamps are interpreted in UTC.

The founder tier gate, investor tier gate, MI Pro, investor seat primary-cover
check, introduction allowances (founder and investor), Eadwyn's subscription-tier
read and the investor-thesis write gate all enforce the same helper. Each family
uses its own period end: founder `subscription_renews_at`, investor
`investor_subscription_renews_at`, and MI's auth-hydrated
`mi_subscription_period_end` from its side table. The introduction quota's user
projection now loads the investor date. AI licence and MCP gates inherit the
founder/investor checks. Existing role bypasses, free access and partner grant
and referral expiry rules retain their policies.

Accepted seats inherit the primary's period end alongside its status, both on
acceptance and on the existing subscription-update cascade. Otherwise an
inside-window colleague could be refused solely because its date was omitted.

The optional Confirm safeguard is included: the shared `RATE_LIMITS` middleware
budgets all `POST /api/billing/alerts/:id/confirm` IDs together per user at ten
requests per minute, and refuses when KV is unavailable. Other methods and
neighboring paths do not consume this bucket. It is the existing KV limiter's
best-effort window, not a newly claimed atomic counter.

The owner setup is `documentation/operations/STRIPE_RENEWAL_GRACE.md` and is
reported on #1008: after all Stripe retries fail, cancel or mark unpaid. The
actual billing webhook destination API version remains **UNVERIFIED** until the
owner checks Stripe; D1052 records the payload-contract assumption rather than
inventing a configured version. The live retry setting, payload version and
post-deploy access/recovery are all **UNVERIFIED**. No production data or settings
were read or written, and all Stripe calls in tests are stubbed.

Verification covers real SQLite projections and writes, all seven gate families,
the exact inclusive cutoff, inside/outside windows, missing/invalid dates,
terminal statuses, independent plan-family dates, existing bypass/grant rules,
seat date propagation and a real middleware/KV fixture for Confirm. New
assertions are mutation-checked with failing mutants and passing restorations.
