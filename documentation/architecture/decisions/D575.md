## D575 — Phones: one lens at a time, switched without a route change (canvas M2)

**On a phone, a zone pill opens its zone in place, with no route change and
no history entry. On a desktop the pills still navigate.** Slot S08, issue
#1188, 2026-10-06, from the owner's answer on #977: "B, adopt mobile canvas
M2". No migration and no Worker change.

### What changed

D425 left M2 open. Its canvas (`design/canvases/backlog/Mobile Canvas.dc.html`)
draws a workspace hub that shows one lens at a time, where the zone chips swap
the lens with "no route change". SHELL_MIGRATION rule 2 and
`founder_shell.test.mjs` held that a pill navigates. Both rules now hold,
split by width:
- **Desktop:** a pill navigates to its zone's route, as before.
- **Phone:** a pill opens that route's page in place as a lens.

"Phone" is below 1024px (`PHONE_QUERY`), the phone tab bar's breakpoint
(D425, `lg:hidden`), so the tab bar and the lenses switch on together.

### How it works

`frontend/src/workspaces/lens.jsx`:
- **`LensProvider`** wraps `AppInner` and holds a lens location: a pathname,
  search, hash and state.
- **App's one `<Routes>`** renders `lensLocation || location`. A lens renders
  the zone's own route element, with its own guard, while the browser's URL
  and history stay where they were.
- **`LensNavLink` and `LensLink`** are the pills. A plain tap on a phone
  cancels the link's own navigation and opens the lens. A desktop click, a
  modified or middle click, or a link with another target behaves as a plain
  `NavLink` or `Link`. Outside a provider they are plain links.
- **The current pill follows the lens.** Inside `<Routes>` the location is
  the lens, so `ZoneNav`'s `zoneForPath` and NavLink's active state read it
  with no extra code.

### What the routes still give

- **A deep link opens its lens.** With no lens open, `<Routes>` renders the
  URL, so `/deals/closing` opens Closing on a phone.
- **Back leaves the workspace.** Switching pushes nothing, so Back returns to
  the page before the workspace, as it would from a single page.
- **A lens belongs to the history entry it opened on.** Any real navigation
  (a link, Back, Forward) gives a new entry and clears the lens. Tapping the
  pill the URL already names closes the lens.
- **Widening clears the mismatch.** If the window widens past the phone
  breakpoint with a lens open, the URL is replaced with the lens's route. It
  is replaced, not pushed, so no history entry is added.

### How a phone user shares a lens

They copy the lens's route. Every pill is still a real `<a href>` to its
zone's route, so long-pressing it and choosing Copy link copies that lens's
address. Whoever opens it gets that lens, by the deep-link rule above. The
address bar still shows the URL the workspace was entered on, and a refresh
reopens that one.

### Where it is built

- **The shared shell.** `ZoneNav` renders `LensNavLink`, so every licence's
  workspace that uses `WorkspaceShell` or `ZoneNav` gets lenses: founder
  Validate and Research, investor, advisor and partner.
- **The founder desks and zone pages.** These build their own pill rows, so
  those rows now use the same two components. That is 6 desks and 21 zone
  pages, 27 rows in all. A row that kept a bare link would navigate (and push
  history) the moment it was tapped inside a lens.
- **SHELL_MIGRATION rule 2** says this. `ROUTE_MAP`'s Mobile Canvas row
  records M2 as built, and D425's note points here.

### Not built

- The rest of the Mobile Canvas: M3 and M4's reflows, and the gestures.
- The other three licences' tab-bar allocations (D425).
- A lens for rows outside the workspaces, such as the public `NetworkSubNav`.
  Those are separate pages, not one workspace's lenses.

### Verified

`frontend/test/phone_lens_d575.test.mjs` has 10 tests. It renders without a
DOM: a switch is seeded through `LensProvider`'s `initial`, and a probe
outside `<Routes>` reads the URL. It covers:
- a phone switch: one lens shown, the URL unchanged, and the current pill
  following the lens;
- a plain tap cancelling the navigation and opening the lens;
- modified, middle and other-target clicks left to the link;
- the lens cleared by any real navigation, and no lens for the URL's own
  route;
- the breakpoint;
- desktop clicks left to the NavLink, with the pill's own `onClick` still run;
- every pill still a link to its route;
- a deep link opening its lens;
- App's and ZoneNav's wiring.

`founder_shell.test.mjs` rewrites the pill rule: each desk's row is the lens
switcher, and so is every founder zone page's section row (27).
`zone_pill_active_state.test.mjs` now expects `LensNavLink` with the shared
class rule.

**Six tests found App's `<Routes>` by the literal `<Routes>`.** Once it took a
`location` prop, that literal was gone, and those tests would have sliced
App.jsx at index -1 and passed on the wrong text. They now find `<Routes`, as
`impersonation_bar_h25_d290` already did.

25 of 25 mutations were caught, each restored from a sha256-checked copy. Two
first-run survivors were fixed: a ZoneNav that aliased `NavLink` as
`LensNavLink` (the wiring test now checks the import), and a ZoneNav accent
fixed to one zone (the current-pill check now reads the accent as well as
`aria-current`). `npm run test:drift` passes.
