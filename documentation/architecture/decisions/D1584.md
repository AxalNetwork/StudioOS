## D1584 — Deck v2 F5: investor-update reuse

Issue #3831 (deck builder v2, F5). No migration.

### What was decided

A founder's latest **investor update** feeds the deck in two ways:

1. **Refresh from the latest update** (pd6), beside Refresh from records on
   the card notice. `GET /api/decks/:id/derive-update` reads the latest update
   the venture **sent** (`portfolio_updates.sent_at`, the newest; a draft is
   never read) and proposes, for each metric tile bound to a slot the update
   reports, the update's words for it. The tile's words stay as they are
   until the founder presses **Accept**. Accept places the words on the card,
   and nothing is saved until a version is saved, as with Refresh from
   records. **Discard** drops the proposal. The read writes nothing.
2. **Changes since vN** (pd35), under each per-investor row's Version.
   `GET /api/decks/:id/changes-since` compares the version the link froze
   with the deck's current version: the metric tiles whose words changed
   (matched by slot, else by label), the tiles added or removed, and the
   cards added or removed. Nothing is drawn on the current version.

### The rules

- **Which tiles.** An update carries D654's five figures (`METRIC_KEYS`). Each
  maps to the one deck slot that holds the same figure (`UPDATE_SLOTS` in
  `services/decks/updateReuse.ts`, mirrored in `figuresModel.js`):
  - MRR → `grow.mrr`
  - Growth → `grow.mercury_growth`
  - Customers → `grow.paying_customers`
  - Burn → `grow.net_burn`
  - Runway → `grow.runway`

  A typed tile, a chart series, free text, or a tile bound to another slot is
  never touched.
- **Never a zero.** A figure the update did not record is listed with the
  update's reason ("Not recorded: …"). It is never proposed as 0 or blank.
  A tile that already shows the update's words is counted as unchanged.
- **Source and grade stay on the tile** (plan §0 row 10). The record is the
  update (`portfolio_updates:<uid>`, the figure's own as of). The grade
  follows `sourceAndGrade`: a figure the update took from Stripe or Mercury is
  Measured (Connector); one the founder typed is Stated.
- **No false "changed" line.** On Accept, a tile whose store already holds the
  same words keeps the store's own snapshot binding. Otherwise it would read
  as "changed in its store" at once.
- **No update yet.** `update: null`, no proposal, and the reason: "No
  investor update has been sent yet. Send one from Grow › Investor updates,
  then refresh from it."
- **Who.** derive-update needs write access to the venture (it proposes an
  edit); changes-since needs read access, like the investor-links table.

### Files

- Worker:
  - `routes/deck_update_reuse.ts`, mounted through the route registry (#4105: its `mount` export, with no `before`, since no `decks` route answers its two paths);
  - `services/decks/updateReuse.ts` (`deriveFromUpdate`, `changesSince`, `updateText`).

  - `figuresModel.js` (`UPDATE_SLOTS`, `acceptUpdate`, `cardHasUpdateSlot`, `updateForCard`, the words);
  - `FigureCard.jsx` (`UpdateRefresh`), drawn by `GridStage.jsx` after `ChangedNotice`;
  - `PitchEditor.jsx` wires the read and Accept;
  - `InvestorLinksTable.jsx` (`changesWords`).
