## D6168 — Team, Market and Traction draw the live scorecard rows; a scope's drawer has an address (#6168)

**The problem.** `dealInsightsModel.js` drew four grey slots on the deal
page's Team, Market and Traction tabs. Each said a read "has not landed" and
named an issue (#3280, #3291). Both reads have been live for weeks, on the
Scorecard and Evidence tabs.

**The scorecard rows.** `Scorecard.jsx` exports `ScopeRows`. It takes scope
keys and reads `GET /api/venture-scores/deal/:dealId` once per tab
(`useDealScorecard`). `SCORECARD_ROWS` in `dealInsightsModel.js` holds the
keys, as the v1 canvas places them:
- Team (DL4): Team.
- Market (DL5): Market, Product-market fit, Geographic, Political and
  regulatory.
- Traction (DL6): Traction and Financial.

Each row is drawn as the Scorecard tab draws it:
- the shared score (`ScopeScore`): band and confidence, or the state's own
  word (Not enough recorded, Not expected yet, Not scored yet);
- the value only after the NDA, Locked before it;
- coverage;
- Open, to that scope's drawer.

A scope that could not be read says so, with Try again, and has no Open. The
whole read's states use the Scorecard tab's words: locked by the founder, not
on the deal, no access, not found and unreadable. Before the NDA the rows
keep band and confidence, show no Open, and point to the NDA. Nothing is
drawn as 0.

**The drawer's address.** `/deals/:dealId/scorecard?scope=team` opens the
Team drawer (`scopeHref` in `dealPageModel.js`, `scopeParam` in
`scorecardModel.js`). The panel takes `addressed` from `DealRoomPage` only.
- Open on a row, and Open full detail on the tab, push the address, so Back
  closes the drawer.
- The drawer's own close replaces the address, so Back does not reopen it.
- Deal flow's peek keeps the drawer as the panel's own state, so `?scope=`
  never touches Deal flow's address.
- An address opens no drawer before the NDA (no inputs are sent), for a scope
  that could not be read, or for a word that is not a scope key.

**The evidence: a link, no count.** Market and Traction draw "Evidence the
founder shared" with a link to the Evidence tab. They show no count, because
the only per-deal read, `GET /api/deals/:id/evidence`, logs every read as an
open of the evidence: `open_evidence`, or `open_evidence_staff` for staff
(D1265, D6167). Counting on Market and Traction would log a second open for
a tab visit that is not one. The tab therefore reads nothing for it.
`deal_insights.ts` still sends an `evidence` block on the market and
traction reads. No page draws it now.

**Removed.** `SLOTS`, `slotOf`, the `SlotFrame` wrapper, and `WAITS` in
`dealPageModel.js`. No page imported `WAITS`. Scorecard and Evidence draw
their reads (D1257, D1268). The stale `source` of "Score and evidence" in
`dealAudienceModel.js` now names the two reads.
