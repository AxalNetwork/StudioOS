### 1. The middle block (CRP1, CRP13)

Every figure is `availableView`'s (D1277) over `/me`. Nothing is computed beyond formatting.

| Who | Eyebrow | Plan | Lines |
|---|---|---|---|
| A member with an allowance | Included with your plan | the plan card's name (`PLAN_CARDS.match`) | "[$left] of [$monthly] left this month", "Resets on [1 Nov]" |
| A founder or investor tier with none | Your plan | e.g. Investor Basic | "Investor Basic includes no AI allowance. Runs use your tokens." |
| An advisor or partner with none | Your plan | (not named) | "Your plan includes no AI allowance. Runs use your tokens." |
| Explorer (`other`) | Your plan | No plan | "No plan. Runs use your tokens." |
| Staff (`admin`) | Your plan | Staff account | "Staff account. Runs use your tokens." |

- **The plan is named only where the licence says which plan it is.** `advisor` and `partner` do not tell a paid plan from a free one (`aiLicenceKey`), so the band names Advisor Pro or Operating Partner Pro only once an allowance shows the paid plan. Naming it on the licence alone could call a Basic member "Advisor Pro".
- **A Super Admin's staff account** adds "Super Admin: token packs are set on HQ › AI allowances ›" (`/admin/platform/allowances`, CRP18).
- **A `/me` that is not whole** says "Your plan's allowance couldn't be read. That's not the same as having none." The balance above it keeps D1285's own states.

### 2. The storage line

- From D1166's read: "Your plan also includes [10 GB] storage." with "See storage ›" (`/account/usage/storage`).
- With no allowance, the line is "Storage: [the read's reason]." (or "Not recorded"). **Zero bytes is never drawn as 0 GB.**
- A failed read says "Your plan's storage couldn't be read."

### 3. The bar

- **Allowance left (violet), then tokens (sky).** Each part is labelled in words in the legend ("Plan allowance left $3.10", "AI tokens $3.40"), and the bar's `aria-label` is "Plan allowance left $3.10, then AI tokens $3.40", so colour never carries the meaning alone.
- With no allowance, the bar is tokens alone.
- A part at `$0.00` draws no segment and keeps its label, because a measured zero is still said.

### 4. Auto-reload

- **The line comes from D794's read:**
  - "Auto-reload: off · Set up ›";
  - "Auto-reload: on, below [$5.00] · Manage ›" (only a `true` `enabled` is on);
  - "Auto-reload: paused · Manage ›" when auto-reload turned itself off;
  - "Auto-reload couldn't be read · Manage ›".
- **Every link goes to Billing's `#auto-reload` card.** The band changes nothing.
- D794's rules stand.

### 5. Add tokens (CRP10)

- **One primary button.**
  - While `/me` reads, it is disabled with "Reading…".
  - When the pack read answers with no pack on sale, it is disabled with its visible line, "Top-up isn't available yet." (`TOPUP_UNAVAILABLE`), tied by `aria-describedby`.
  - A pack read that failed leaves it on, because the packs below say so themselves.
- It still goes to `#token-packs` until D1287 opens the sheet's pack mode in place.
