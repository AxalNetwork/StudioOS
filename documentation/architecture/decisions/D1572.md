### 1. Brand locks

- **Stored** in `deck_brand_locks`, one row per locked part: `fonts`, `palette` or `logo`. Each row has who locked the part and when.
- **Who locks.** "Only the venture's admins can lock." That is the venture's owner, the founder whose venture it is (`canLockBrand`). A cofounder, an advisor or a platform admin is refused 403 `admins_only` with that sentence.
  - Owner decision I = C (per deck, plus an HQ default and lock) is about model choices, not brand. An HQ-level brand lock is not in this task.
- **Routes:**
  - `GET /api/decks/brand-kit/locks?project_id=` returns `{ locks, can_lock, admins_only }`, for the deck's readers;
  - `PUT /api/decks/brand-kit/locks?project_id=` with `{ part, locked }` sets one lock, and is audited (`deck_brand_locked` or `deck_brand_unlocked`).
- **A kit save** (`PUT /api/decks/brand-kit`) that changes a locked part is refused 409 `brand_locked`, with "This colour is locked in the brand kit by [name]. Nothing changed." For fonts the sentence begins "These fonts are", and for the logo "This logo is". Nothing is written.
- **A card save:**
  - Both save paths run the shared gate (`saveGates.ts`): the working copy's `PATCH /api/decks/:id/cards/:uid` and `PUT /api/decks/:id`.
  - While the palette is locked, the gate refuses a card colour that is new to the save. A colour is any `color`, `accent` or `fill` the card holds.
  - A colour passes when it is one of the kit's palette roles. A colour already on the card being saved over (its working copy, else its version) also passes, so a card made before the lock never blocks a save.
  - The test pd31 names: a locked colour cannot be changed on a card.
- **A store with no lock table holds no lock.** Any other failed read refuses, because a gate that cannot be checked does not pass.

### 2. The .pptx template (`services/decks/pptxTemplate.ts`, one module that #3810 reuses)

- **What is read.** Only what maps the layouts:
  - the slide size;
  - the first theme's colour scheme and major and minor Latin fonts;
  - each slide layout's name and placeholder boxes. A placeholder the layout leaves unplaced takes its master's box of the same type.
- No slide is opened and no slide text is read: "Your file's text is read only to map the layouts." A layout's name is the only text kept. If a prompt ever names a saved layout, it goes in through `layoutPromptLine`, framed by `untrustedBlock('deck_import', …)`.
- **Mapping.**
  - Each box is scaled to 1920 × 1080, and each edge is snapped to The Grid's nearest line (`boxOf`'s 12 × 6 cells).
  - An edge more than 0.3 of a cell pitch from every line means "A box sits between columns" (or rows). Two areas on one cell means "Two boxes share a cell". A layout with no placeable box means "The layout has no boxes to place".
  - A box reaching into a margin snaps to the outer cell.
  - Date, footer, slide-number and header placeholders are not places for content and are left out.
- **Theme.**
  - The palette roles are: ground = lt1, ink = dk1, accent = accent1, secondary = accent2, highlight = accent3.
  - A font is kept when it is one of `BRAND_FONTS` (owner decision K = A, OFL only). Otherwise the nearest stand-in is used: a serif or a sans face for display or body. The report marks it `fallback: true`, which is pd30's "fonts found with a fallback".
- **Limits:**
  - at most 50 MB per file;
  - every inflated part is capped at 4 MB, so a zip bomb stops at the cap;
  - at most 60 layouts.

### 3. Routes (`routes/deck_layouts.ts`, before `decks.ts`)

- `POST /api/decks/templates/import?project_id=` takes a multipart `file` or the raw bytes with `X-File-Name`. It returns pd30's mapping report and stores nothing. It is switched by `deck:pptx_import`; when switched off it answers 404 `not_switched_on`.
- `GET /api/decks/layouts?project_id=` lists the venture's layouts, oldest first.
- `POST /api/decks/layouts?project_id=` with `{ source_name, layouts: [{ name, areas }] }` is "Save as your layouts".
  - Every layout is checked again on the Worker: it must be whole cells with no two areas sharing a cell. The client is never trusted, and anything else is refused 422 `not_whole_cells`, with nothing saved.
  - A venture keeps at most 60 layouts.
  - The save is audited (`deck_layouts_saved`).
- `DELETE /api/decks/layouts/:uid?project_id=` removes one layout.
- **Access:** the deck's readers read, and its writers import, save and remove.
