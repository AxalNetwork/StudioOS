## D6123 — Grow's roles, partners, markets and obligations open on one first action (#6123)

Part of the 2026-11-14 coordinator audit (#4864). Grow's four other record modules showed, with nothing recorded, an empty chart sentence, an empty table and connector blocks. Build's six (D5076), Validate's first three (D6004) and Grow's Feedback and KPIs (D6100) already open on one first action.

### The rule

- **The modules.** Talent's roles (`team-expansion`), Partners (`partner-network`), Markets (`market-expansion`) and Obligations (`durable-company-building`) join `PROGRESSIVE.grow` in `frontend/src/pages/cycles/progressiveList.js`.
- **At zero records.** Each one draws D5076's zero state on desktop and phone:
  - one heading;
  - one outcome line;
  - one action: the module's existing form button, unrelabelled (Open a role, Add partner, Add market, Add obligation).

  The connector parts (Mercury, and Calendar for obligations) sit in the closed "Bring it in from your tools" region (D6100's `fold`).
- **Zero only.** The issue asks that a page with one or more records stays unchanged, so these four carry `zeroOnly`. The new `moduleStage(records, query, copy)` turns D5076's `few` stage into `many` for them: one record or more keeps the full table with its search, filters and sort.
  - Build's six, Validate's three and D6100's two have no `zeroOnly`, so their three stages are unchanged.
  - `CycleModulePage.jsx` and `PhoneModulePage.jsx` call `moduleStage` where they called `listStage`.
- **What is never a zero.** An unreadable read, or a read with no total, is still `many` (D6100), so the page keeps its Unreadable and Retry.
- **The wording.** Each outcome line is plain and states the founder outcome. No state word is introduced, so #5129's vocabulary (`frontend/src/ui/dataState.js`) is untouched.

### Not changed

- Distribution keeps the full table.
- The Grow overview cards (#4864).
- The module forms and the records reads.
