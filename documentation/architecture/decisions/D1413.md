### 5. Rules kept

- A missing value is "Not recorded" (muted, `Unrecorded`), never a 0, blank or dash; a failed read is "[Block] could not be read. That is not the same as having none." with Try again, alone in its block (`portfolioUi.jsx`'s `Failed`). A failed `GET /api/account/projects` reads "Its projects could not be read" under each card while the cards draw (pf7c2).
- Never `GET /api/projects`, the every-venture read.
- People by name, never by email: the company page's Team lists names and roles; invitations are Team's.
- A standalone project has no Legal anywhere: `scopeFor` gives it Team and Project settings › General, and its page says it is not a legal entity, with "Make it a company" (Incorporate, prefilled) and "Move under a company".
- The switcher: a project picked makes its company active (`selectProject`), is kept on this device in try/catch, and is what the sidebar's block follows when no page publishes a scope. A failed projects read is "Projects could not be read · Try again" while the companies still list.

### 6. Deviations from the plan and the canvas

- **The Worker was further along than the plan said.** Parts 1, 2 and 4 of #3697 and D1412, D1591, D1592, D1593 merged after the plan was written, so the legal record, slug check, documents, mailing address and the card's status are wired, not stubbed. The plan's per-company legal reads (N+1) were not needed: D1412 carries the card's facts.
- **Projects nest from `GET /api/account/projects`**, with addresses from the switcher. The switcher's nesting is used in the switcher itself. A company's Projects settings tab lists the company's projects from the settings scope, so a member sees projects they do not sit on.
- **The project page is `/account/portfolio/projects/<id>`**, inside Account, and `/c/…` and `/p/…` resolve to it (and to the company page) through `GET /api/account/resolve` rather than drawing pages of their own.
- **Team's routes keep `/account/companies/:uid/settings/team`** (D1497 owns them). Company settings' General, Legal and Projects are under `/account/portfolio`.
- **A project row under Company settings › Projects** now opens that project's settings (PF6c), not its Team; the project in view still shows Team and Settings.
- **The investor's and partner's card extras** (funds run by the firm; engagements, quotes and offers) are kept as a line under the card's facts, so D1138's boards are not lost.
- **Not built:** the PF4c and PF8b Ask insets (Q8), and the tax ID's "Show" (the number is never shown on a page; Replace in the legal sheet writes a new one). Officers and locations are added on New company and shown on the company page; ceasing an officer or closing a location stays with their routes until a sheet is drawn for them.
