## D626 — Help Center guides are grouped by task, with guarded surface links

Issue #1369 · S18 · Codex. Frontend only; no migration.

One placement table covers all 96 existing guide anchors. The ordered areas are
Getting started; The Spin-Out Lab; Building your startup; Incorporation & legal;
Raising & cap table; Funds & portfolio; Data rooms & due diligence;
Advisors & office hours; Partners & the network; Connecting your tools;
Billing & account; Running the platform. The last area remains admin-only.

The canvas's Capital calls & distributions becomes Funds & portfolio, because
the corpus covers reserves, portfolios and market intelligence too. Spin-Out Lab,
Building your startup, Partners & the network, Connecting your tools and Running
the platform are added to the canvas's seven areas so those guides are not
misfiled. The home shows four guides per card, an All N control, each guide's
surface label and a badge only when every guide applies to one common role.

Addresses do not move: section/sub anchors, old hashes and /help/section/sub
paths still identify the same guides. Areas change presentation, not routing.
The breadcrumb, tag, area rail, search results, popular cards and admin answers
name the task area. Existing roles filtering remains the visibility rule.
The descriptive appliesTo field never hides a guide.

Surface metadata copies mounted route guards, including Account's dynamic
section guard and Lab guards. Open the surface is shown only when the viewer's
role is admitted or the route is a Lab route and spinout_lab_active is exactly
1. Admin view-as links follow the active role; Lab routes also preserve the
router's admin-preview exception. Impersonation uses the target account.
Public routes have no role restriction; article authoring also requires a
signed-in viewer. General and multi-screen guides have no single surface and
show no jump. This avoids inventing a token, project ID or screen address.

Guide text was reviewed against routes: signature recipients have token-specific
addresses, NDA flows span several records, and Lab weeks 2–4 span multiple tools,
so those guides have no single screen. Office-hours and expert-profile guides
also span different role-specific destinations and individual profiles. Broad
admin guides point to Admin Console; HQ-only switches and host-specific events
operations have no generic admin jump. No guide text or role guard is changed.

The metadata appears in the article column so phone readers can see it; the
canvas's right rail is hidden below desktop width. The desktop area rail still
lists the area's guides. Tests render cards and articles, verify all placements
and route guards, preserve addresses and check role and active-Lab link gates.
Every added assertion is checked with failing mutations and passing restorations.
