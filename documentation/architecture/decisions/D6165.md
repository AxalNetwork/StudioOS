### What was decided

- **The card** (canvas DL1 `deckK`) replaces the grey "Deck" slot on Overview and on the investor's Documents tab, whose words ("once a per-deal deck grant exists") were out of date. It reads once and shows:
  - `granted: true`: the deck's title, then `venture · v<version> · frozen as of <day, UTC>`, and Open the deck. A missing title, version or date reads "Not recorded", never a blank, a 0 or a guessed date;
  - `granted: false`: the Worker's own sentence (`NOT_GRANTED` or `NO_LONGER_AVAILABLE`), and no button: never an empty viewer;
  - a failed read: "The deck could not be read." with Try again. The rest of the page stands.
- **Investors only.** Overview mounts the card only for an investor; the Documents view that carries it is already the investor's. A founder or staff member sees no card and the read is never sent for them (the Worker would refuse it anyway).
- **The viewer** is `DealDeck`, in the shared `Lightbox` (`size="tall"`, full screen on a phone), drawing `DeckCardView` with the Worker's `deck` object as it came. The Worker already cut what the founder did not tick and every provenance key (pd24); the viewer adds nothing back and rebuilds nothing. The card view is phone-first (one column, 16 px gutter), so it holds at 390 px; every colour has its `dark:` pair.
- **No write.** Opening the viewer sends no request; the only request is the one GET.
