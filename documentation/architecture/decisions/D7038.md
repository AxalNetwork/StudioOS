### What the canvas decides

- Rank sets the order. Confidence is a separate 0–100% score for how well the recorded evidence backs a signal, never a probability of success.
- The collapsed card shows rank, title, sector and geography, the signal's update date, confidence and View evidence.
- View evidence opens a side sheet (a bottom sheet on phone): Why now, then evidence newest first with source name, observation date and Open source, or the recorded citation when no URL exists.
- Signal updated and Newest evidence observed sit on separate lines. A live signal with no evidence says so.
- Open sector profile appears only when a tracked sector matches the signal's sector by exact name.
- Confidence formats: 82 reads 82%; a measured 0 is a value; null reads Not recorded; a failed evidence read reads Unavailable.
- Five card states stay distinct: live, live but empty, labelled illustrative examples, loading, and "Could not load signals" with Retry, with the other Markets panels still usable.
- `?signal=` sits beside the directory's URL state, and closing the sheet returns focus to the same View evidence.
