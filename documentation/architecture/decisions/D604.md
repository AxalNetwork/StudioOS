## D604 — The ticket screens, built to their canvas

**Issue #1280, slot S01, 2026-10-06.** Worker first. There is no migration, and one new
route: `POST /api/tickets/:id/mirror`. This is step 4 of #1280, and the PR says
`Part of #1280`.

The owner, 2026-10-06: "where are the tickets users submitted?" D599 put a
member's open tickets on the Help Center home. This builds the screens behind
them, H3 to H7 of `design/canvases/integrated/Help Ticket Screen.dc.html`.

### What was true on main

- **`/help/tickets` was one page** with a toggled inline form and one table
  for both roles.
- **The form preset the type to `task`.** D544 (#1293) made the Worker refuse a
  ticket without a type, because a preset filed ordinary support requests into
  the Super Admin's suggested-task queue. The form still sent `task` for
  anyone who did not change it.
- **The form required a category.** D482 says a ticket may have none, and none
  is never counted as Other.
- **The comment list named the GitHub token's owner as the author of every
  reply.** Every reply posted through StudioOS is posted by one GitHub
  account, so its login said nothing about who wrote the reply. A member saw
  their own words under someone else's name.
- **Anyone on GitHub could appear in a member's thread.** The mirrored issues
  are in a public repository. Nothing told a stranger's comment from the
  team's beyond a login the member could not check.
- **A thread GitHub would not give read as "No comments yet."**
- **The list lost its bounty chips every minute.** The page refreshed from
  `POST /api/tickets/sync`, whose rows carried no `bounty`.
- **The ticket list sent no review.** A feature request's review (D544,
  `feature_request_reviews`) was visible to the Super Admin only.

### The change: the Worker

- **Every row of `GET /api/tickets` carries `review`** for a feature request:
  - `awaiting` when there is no decision yet;
  - `approved`;
  - `declined`, with the reason the member was sent;
  - `unreadable` when the review could not be read, which is never shown as
    awaiting.

  Other types carry `null`.
- **An admin's rows also carry `sla`**: the tier from priority, HQ's hours for
  it (D482's store), and the band.
  - The clock stops at resolved or closed.
  - A store that cannot be read bands nothing (`clock: 'unreadable'`), as the
    HQ Support panel does.
  - A member's rows carry no SLA at all.
- **`POST /api/tickets/sync` sends the same rows** as the list, so a page that
  takes them keeps every chip.
- **`GET /api/tickets/:id` returns `thread`**, by side, instead of `comments`
  by login. It also returns `thread_unreadable` when GitHub could not be read.
  `services/ticketScreens.ts` reads each comment:
  - **The team**, if it comes from the account that opened the mirrored issue,
    or from the repository's owner, members or collaborators.
  - **The member**, if it is such a comment that ends with the member
    signature D600 appends. Only the very end counts, where the Worker puts
    it. A member can type the team's signature into their own reply, but not
    after the one the Worker appends.
  - **`outside`, with its login**, if it comes from anyone else. It is never
    shown as the Axal team.

  The signature and the source marker are dropped from the body.
- **`POST /api/tickets/:id/mirror`** (admin) retries the mirror of one saved
  ticket.
  - It takes the same lease as the backfill, and the backfill now shares its
    code (`mirrorSavedTicket`).
  - A mirrored ticket is a 409 (`already_mirrored`), and so is a lease someone
    else holds (`mirror_in_progress`).
  - A GitHub failure is a 502 that names GitHub's reason and records it on the
    row.

  The backfill would also have opened issues for up to 24 other tickets.

### The change: the screens

- **H3, a member's tickets:**
  - tabs by status with counts;
  - Type and Category filters, with "No category" as its own choice;
  - each row's type, category, priority, status, bounty and review chips, and
    its last activity;
  - three states: loading, "You have not opened a ticket.", and "Your tickets
    could not be read." with Try again.
- **H4, the form** (`/help/tickets?new=1`):
  - **Title and Type are required.** Type has no default, and each error names
    its field.
  - **Choices.** The three types and the four priorities are cards, each
    saying what it is for. Category may be none.
  - **The bug note:** a confirmed bug earns credits when the fix ships.
  - **"These guides may answer it":** up to three guides from the reader's own
    index, matched on the title, each opening in a new tab so the form keeps
    what was typed.
  - **After submit:** the outcome is shown on the new ticket, keeping D171's
    split. A failed mirror is said to everyone. Not configured is said only to
    an admin, and so is the pointer to Admin Console → GitHub Sync.
- **H5, one ticket:**
  - the description;
  - the thread by side ("You", "Axal team", or "@login · Outside the team");
  - the reply box, open only once the ticket is on the tracker;
  - a rail with who and when, the tracker link, and the bounty (a bug) or the
    review (a feature request, H5b, with its reason).
  - A ticket the reader cannot open is "not found" whether it is missing or
    someone else's. One that could not be read says so, with Try again.
- **H6, the admin queue:**
  - every member's tickets in the canvas's eight columns: requester, category,
    priority with its tier and hours, SLA, status, bounty or review, and
    tracker;
  - a title search, and Priority and SLA filters;
  - the missing-issue banner, in the canvas's words.
- **H7, one ticket as an admin sees it:**
  - status buttons;
  - Assign to me;
  - the SLA in the header;
  - the mirror state with GitHub's error and a Retry for that ticket.
- **The Help Center's "Your tickets" card (D599)** now shows the review beside
  the bounty. D599 left the review out because the list did not carry one.
  Both use the ticket screens' status chip, so every ticket surface has one
  palette (`lib/ticketScreens.js`).

### Where the build departs from the canvas, and why

- **"It is retried until it succeeds" and "The team retries" are not said.**
  No job retries a failed mirror on its own. The admin backfill and H7's
  Retry are both manual. The screens say an admin can retry it, which is
  true, and the test bans the promise.
- **The assignee is "Assign to me", not a picker.** `assigned_to` is free text
  mapped to a GitHub login through `ADMIN_GITHUB_LOGINS`. No roster of support
  staff exists to fill a picker.
- **No "Feature review queue →" link on H7.** The queue's page waits on its
  design (#1107, `needs-design`). The review itself is shown.
- **No logo bar.** The app shell carries the brand, as on H1.
- **The "After submit" legend is not drawn.** The outcome it describes is shown
  on the ticket after submitting.

### What is not changed

- The addresses: `/help/tickets`, `/help/tickets/<id>`, and `?new=1`.
- D171's honesty about the mirror.
- D600's identity-free mirror.
- The 25-ticket backfill.
