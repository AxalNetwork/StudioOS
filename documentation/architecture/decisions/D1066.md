### 1. Accept

`POST /api/cycles/build/plan/items/:uid/accept`, with an optional `{ payload }` edit.

- **The claim comes first.** The proposal's own `ai_rail_proposals` row moves off `proposed` (`WHERE status = 'proposed'`, and only while its plan is `draft` or `open`). Two presses, or a press racing a newer plan's withdrawal, decide it once; the loser answers `decided`.
- **Then one D1 batch writes:**
  - the module's row through its statement builder;
  - the item's `landed_table` and `landed_id`;
  - one `build_evidence_links` row for each piece of evidence the item carries, with its label as a snapshot;
  - one `fill_provenance` row for each carried field (§4);
  - the activity line under the item's module.
- **A failed batch writes none of it, and the claim is handed back.** The proposal returns to `proposed` with the founder's saved edit, if there was one.
- **The writers:**

| Kind | Lands in | Through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feature | `mvp_features` | `featureCreateStatements` (D1064) |
| Must-have | `mvp_features` with `is_must_have = 1` | `mustHaveCreateStatements` (D1064) |
| Roadmap item | `roadmap_okrs` | `roadmapCreateStatements` (D1064) |
| Risk | `venture_risks` | `riskCreateStatements` (new) |
| Formation step | `formation_steps` | `formationStepCreateStatements` (new) |
| Prototype test | `hypothesis_experiments`, method "Prototype test" | `experimentCreateStatements` (new): `addExperiment`'s row, as #2426's mapping says |
| Connect door | nothing: a door, never a system write | the activity line only |

- **No owner or date is ever written.** A must-have lands in Backlog. A roadmap item has no owner and no target date. A commitment's item, which carries no lane, lands in Next. A formation step starts at to-do.
- **Every statement after the writer names the row through the item.** The landed update reads the new id: the venture's newest row, which is exact inside a batch (D1064 §3). A prototype test is read by its own uid, because its INSERT writes nothing when the claim has since been retired. The links and the provenance read `landed_id` back from the item. A writer that wrote no row leaves `landed_id` NULL, the provenance insert's NOT NULL fails the batch, and the claim goes back.
- **Every carried field passes the never-fill registry (D777).**

### 2. Edit and accept, Dismiss

- **`PATCH /items/:uid`** saves the founder's version in `decided_payload_json`. The item stays proposed, so D1065's plan read already shows the edit. Accept lands the saved edit, plus any edit sent with the press.
- **What a founder may change** is each kind's own fields. A key the kind does not take, or a value it does not take, is refused (`invalid_edit`):
  - a feature: title, description, impact and effort;
  - a test: title, method and target;
  - a risk: title, severity and mitigation;
  - a roadmap item: title and lane;
  - the rest: the title only.
- **The proposal reads `edited`** when any editable field differs from what the plan proposed, and `accepted` otherwise.
- **`POST /items/:uid/dismiss`** moves the proposal to `discarded` and writes an activity line. D1065's preparation never proposes a key whose earlier item was discarded, accepted or edited, so a dismissed item never returns, and a dismissed item cannot be accepted (`decided`).

### 3. Accept all in a module

`POST /modules/:module/accept?project=&scope=` runs Accept on each proposed item of the current plan's module, in key order, at most 50 (`MODULE_ACCEPT_LIMIT`, AUTOFILL owner question 3).

- Each item goes through its own claim and its own batch. One item's refusal does not stop the others.
- The answer reports each outcome and `more: true` when items are still waiting.
- Founding team is not a plan module, so it answers 404.

### 4. Provenance in today's columns

`fill_provenance` does not have AUTOFILL's source columns yet (#2431 owns them). The owner chose, on 2026-10-25, to write the rows now in the columns that exist, rather than add a migration here or wait. Each carried field's row keeps:

- its address: the target table, row and column;
- `fill_class = 'sourced'`;
- the value the plan proposed, the value written, and `edited` derived from the two;
- the proposal (`ai_proposal_id`), who decided (`decided_by`), and `target_row_ref = build_plan_item:<uid>`;
- `citation_json`: `source_kind: 'module'`, `source_ref`, `source_label`, `prior_value` and the item's uid and marker.

`prior_value` is null, because Accept creates the row: undo is the row's removal. When AUTOFILL adds its columns, they can be filled from `citation_json`.

### 5. Who

- **Deciding is for the venture's founders only:** an owner or an admitted Lab founder on their own venture (`ownsVenture`, #2426 owner question 3).
- An admin, another founder or a partner is refused `not_this_project` (404), as D1065's prepare refuses them.
- **Refusals go through `refuse()` (D278)**, with the code on `code` and `error` and our sentence on `message` and `detail`. `PlanItemError` is ledgered in `scripts/check-refusal-bodies.mjs` as our own sentence:
  - `decided` (409);
  - `writer_refused` (409 for an item no writer can land; 502 for a batch that failed);
  - `not_this_project` (404);
  - `plan_paused` (409);
  - `invalid_edit` (400).
- A store failure answers `unreadable` (503), never as a refusal.

### 6. The evidence trail

`GET /api/cycles/build/evidence?project=&table=&id=` (`buildEvidenceRoutes`, mounted before the Build router). `table` is one of the five tables in §1. PIPE-RAISE (#2430) reads this.

- **Readers are the plan's readers** (D1065): the venture's own, an admin, and a partner of the venture's company.
- **A row that is not this venture's answers 404.**
- **The trail holds:**
  - `evidence`: each linked piece with its label snapshot, its source label and the date it was linked;
  - `accepted`: the plan item, its status and when it was decided;
  - `decision`: the plan's decision with its state and date, read through `decisionById` in `services/verdict.ts` (the only file that reads the Verdict's tables, D693), present only for `canReadDecision`. For a partner the key is absent, and decision and commitment evidence are left out, as D1065's partner read leaves them out;
  - `sent`: `{ recorded: false, reason }`. Sending to a tool is part 5 (D1068), so nothing is recorded as sent;
  - `came_back`: each `connector_fed_items` row fed onto this Build row, with its provider and dates.
- **An unreadable store answers 503,** never an empty trail.
