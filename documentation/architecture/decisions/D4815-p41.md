### What is drawn

An investor's Overview is DE04's grid: Scorecard beside What's new, then Investment case beside Your position. Below it, About this deal, Terms at a glance, The round and the deck's slot stay as part 4 left them.

| Block | From | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Scorecard | the batch strip for this deal | the eleven-scope strip; Strongest; Needs attention; Changed; Open scorecard. No overall score. |
| What's new | `whats-new` | each change the read returns, dated, newest first, with Since |
| Investment case, Why this deal | the deal read; the fit read; a link | the deal's summary; Fit to my thesis with the criteria it matched or missed; Main evidence, which opens the Evidence tab |
| Investment case, Open questions · risks | the notes read, tags Question and Risk; the batch strip | the firm's three newest of each, first line only, and how many more; Missing: the scopes with not enough recorded |
| Your position | the commitments read (part 4) | Your commitment, Firm, Route, Open investment |

**Strongest and Needs attention** come from bands and confidence, the investor read's words, never a figure.
- **Strongest** is the highest band, then the higher confidence, then scope order.
- **Needs attention** is the lowest band, then the higher confidence (the surer weakness), then scope order.
- It is drawn only when it is a lower band than Strongest. With every scored scope in one band, the card says none stands apart.
- Risk reads in its risk words (Low risk for Strong).

**Each block fails on its own.**
- **Scorecard:** a failed batch read is the Scorecard's Unreadable. It does not take the other blocks with it.
- **Missing:** says it could not be read, with its own Try again.
- **Fit and notes:** a failed fit read and a failed notes read each say so in their half of the investment case, with Try again. That Try again reads only what failed.
- **What's new and Your position:** keep their own Unreadable, which neither says nothing changed nor that there is no commitment.

**A founder and studio staff** keep the Scorecard and the deal's details. What's new, the investment case and Your position are the investor's, and none of their reads is made for anyone else.

### What no read carries, so it reads Not recorded

- **Changed** ("Market ▲4 since your last visit"). The investor's scorecard read sends no change, and the score history (`trajectory`) is the founder's and staff's. #6173 adds a per-scope change against the investor's `deal_seen.last_seen_at`. Until then the card reads Not recorded and says why.
- **Route** ("Direct"). The commitments read carries no route (part 4). The row reads Not recorded.
- **Open, as in an unanswered question.** A note has no open or answered state. So the block lists the firm's notes tagged Question or Risk, newest first, and calls them that. #6173's case read is the place to decide which are open.
- **Top risks from the venture.** No read holds a ranked list of a venture's risks. The Risk scope's band is on the Scorecard, and the firm's Risk notes are listed here.

### Main evidence is a link, not a read

The investor evidence read (`GET /api/deals/:id/evidence`, D1265) logs an `open_evidence` row each time it answers. Founders see those rows as who opened their evidence (D1266). Reading it on every Overview visit would record an open the investor never made. So Main evidence links to the Evidence tab, where the open is real. When #6173's case read lands, it can carry the main evidence without logging an open.

### What's new is material changes

The read compares the latest stored version of each source the investor may read against their last visit. Those sources are:
- the deal's status, the granted deck, documents and the firm's notes;
- other investors' commitments, counted and never named;
- diligence answers;
- the investor's own invitation, room membership, case, IC record, commitment, envelope and funding.

It reads no audit log, so it carries no audit noise, and every row it returns is drawn. The sentence for each type is `CHANGE_WORDS`; #6170 gives the rest of the types their own sentence.
