## D4472 — One account, several licences: the sidebar and access are their union (#4472)

The owner's rule, verbatim:

1. There is no licence switcher anywhere. Remove the one at the top-left of the left sidebar. The top of the sidebar is for companies and projects only. Anything about licences lives in the Account menu.
2. A member holding several licences (Founder, Investor, Advisor, Operating Partner) sees the sections of **all** of them in the one left sidebar, in licence order Founder, Investor, Advisor, Operating Partner.
3. A section two licences share (Studio, Spin-Out Lab) appears **once**. Its page shows each held licence's part as its own block.
4. Portfolio and Companies never appear in the left sidebar, for any licence. They live in the Account menu.
5. The bottom of the sidebar is always Trust, then Network, then Research, whatever the number of licences.
6. The two switcher design artifacts shared for #3704 are rejected and must not be built.

This supersedes the proposed D1436, D1437 and D1438 and their active-profile session; none were merged. No `account_profiles`, active-profile session, switch route, switcher or rejected switcher design is introduced.

### Model and scope

`users.role` remains the primary licence and the only source of Admin. The additional `account_licences` rows are never backfilled from it. Held licences are the primary (when licensed) plus active grants, ordered Founder, Investor, Advisor, Operating Partner, without duplicates. Revoked rows remain recorded and grant nothing. The append-only `account_licence_events` records each actual grant/revoke exactly once in the same batch; retries are no-ops. Role correction and the partner-to-investor boot correction do not create or rewrite grants.

The users table stays at its existing column count. Auth attaches the grant union once per request, keyed to the token subject (including support/impersonation). A grant cannot elevate Admin. Legacy role casing is normalized for licence recognition without overwriting the returned primary role. A missing grant table has no possible grants; other failed reads never manufacture additional access. The account licence route reports an unreadable store as a fixed 503 rather than inventing an empty list.

Granting creates the licence's profile idempotently and seeds the union of Trust obligations. In particular, a Founder grant cannot waive a held Investor's KYC. Existing company, project-membership, lead, NDA, KYC, active-company and subscription checks remain in force. A pure-role exclusion must not remove an independently held licence's rights; having Investor plus Advisor is not Admin. Permission scopes continue to require their existing relationships, and an investor grant cannot turn a diligence subject list into an unscoped read.

Only the API client changes on the frontend. Part 2 builds the Account menu mapping: `/account/portfolio` is labelled "Portfolio", or "Companies" when Investor is held. `/portfolio` is labelled "Portfolio" and shown to Investor holders only. Self-service purchase of a second licence is deferred; every addable licence says `how: 'contact'`.

### Atomic Admin appointment

[S1's retention clarification](https://github.com/AxalNetwork/StudioOS/issues/4472#issuecomment-6638567535) permits `retainPrimary: true` only with `source: 'admin_appointment'`; any other source is refused with `invalid_retention`. An ordinary grant of the current primary remains `already_held` and writes nothing.

The task originally named `routes/admin.ts`, but that route explicitly refuses Admin promotion. S1 approved using the real `routes/admin_licences.ts` appointment instead. Its role change, territory binding, existing licence audit, existing activity audit, retained grant and single grant event commit in one `db.batch`. A failed statement rolls all of them back. The service builds the retention/role statements; the route supplies its existing appointment statements to that same batch. `admin.ts` and demotion remain unchanged.

### Verification

Synthetic users only. Real SQLite via the D1 transaction shim tests no backfill, CHECK constraints, ordering/de-duplication, profiles, Trust union, request hydration and impersonation, revoked access, fixed refusals, idempotent event counts, primary corrections and the actual appointment route's rollback. The client contract is checked against the mounted paths. The PR records final build, full-suite, mutation and review results.
