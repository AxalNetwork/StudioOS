### 1. Typed figures are the venture's, and always Stated

`deck_typed_values` holds one figure per venture and key: label, value (a number or words, never both), unit (`count`, `usd`, `percent`, `months` or `text`), who typed it last and when.

- **Keyed by the venture, not one version.** Every version of the deck reads the same figures; `deck_id` records the version a figure was typed on.
- **Graded Stated.** Nothing measured backs a typed figure. The reply says `grade: 'Stated'` and carries `typed_by: { id, name }`, which is what pd24's hover line needs: "Typed by [Avery Stone] · not linked to a record".
- **Absent until typed.** A key nobody typed has no row, and readers show "Not recorded", never 0.

**Routes** (`routes/deck_typed_values.ts`, mounted at `/api/decks` beside `deck_grid.ts`):
- `GET /:id/typed-values`: whoever may read the deck (`projectOwned`, as the Grid read).
- `PUT /:id/typed-values/:key` `{ label, value, unit }`: write access to the venture. A support session is refused, 403 `support_session_refused`. Refusals are sentences: `invalid_key`, `label_required`, `invalid_unit`, `invalid_value` (a number, and a percent within ±1,000), `value_required`; at most 200 figures (409 `too_many_values`).
- `DELETE /:id/typed-values/:key`: as `PUT`; 404 `not_found` when nothing is typed under the key.

Every statement is a bound literal.

### 2. The table block

`slideModel.ts` gains `table` among the block kinds:
- at most **8 columns × 12 rows**; rows past 12 and cells past 8 are cut, and every row is squared to the widest, with empty cells;
- each cell is `{ text }` (Stated), `{ binding }` (a D896 binding, the record's own grade) or `{ check }` (✓ or ✗); anything else is an empty cell, drawn blank and never as 0;
- `header` (default true) makes the first row the head;
- a table binds per cell, so it carries no block binding;
- `modelProblems` refuses a table with no cell, or one over 8 × 12, with a sentence.

`designSystem.fitProblems` reports `too_many_rows` when a table holds more than two rows per grid row of its block, so a one-click deck with an overflowing table fails its zero-fit gate.

### 3. The market_circles and projection chart types

Both join `CHART_TYPES` (14 now). They are drawn from typed points, not from a dated series, so:
- **the library keeps its twelve tiles** (pd6). `LIBRARY_CHART_TYPES` excludes them, `chartFits` reads the library only, and `GET /api/decks/model` keeps `chart_types` at 12 and adds `typed_chart_types`;
- **market circles** need exactly TAM, SAM and SOM, each positive, with SOM ≤ SAM ≤ TAM. Each circle's area is its share of TAM, and the three rest on one bottom. A market that does not nest is said in words, never drawn;
- **the projection line** is solid up to today's month and dashed after it; the two parts meet at the last point not past today.

**One Projection label.** The label, footnote and Projected grade are S1's `projectionOf` and `PROJECTION_NOTE` (`figuresModel.js`). `BlockGrid.jsx` draws them beside both typed charts in the editor, Present and print; no second label exists. On the Worker side, the exports name the two types ("Market circles chart", "Projection chart"). The forward-looking footnote in the server-side exports is **#3790's** (B14, D1546, in progress by S24): this issue adds no footnote there, so only one task owns it.

### 4. The one-click rules (`visuals.chartTypeFor`)

`chartTypeFor` picks the chart from the data shape alone, with no user edit:
1. TAM, SAM and SOM that nest → `market_circles`;
2. a series with a month after today's → `projection`, so a zero-edit deck never shows a forecast as a result;
3. otherwise, as before: a weekly count is columns, four points or more a line, fewer columns; one point or an unrecorded series is null.

The rules use the frontend's month rule (`projectionOf`'s `monthOf`), so the generated chart and the label agree. Fixtures in the Worker test cover each rule and its boundary (this month is not a projection).

### 5. A typed number is counted by the evidence check

`raiseClaims.deck` (the D1264 deck surface, run by D900's check) now reads:
- **each table row** as its label and figures in both orders the stated-figure patterns read (`figureLines`: "MRR: $12,000" and "$12,000 MRR"), rather than as loose cells (`cardText`);
- **each typed figure** of the venture, under `typed:<key>`, in the same two orders, dollars with "$", a percent with "%", months as "n months".

So "Paying customers · 38" in a table, or a typed "paying customers" figure of 42, becomes a claim the check grades against the pinned evidence.
