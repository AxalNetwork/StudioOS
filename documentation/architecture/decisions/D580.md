## D580 — 83(b) operator review: an Axal admin per startup checks the filing record

**An Axal admin is assigned to each startup as its 83(b) operator, and records
reviews of the startup's 83(b) filing record. A review does not gate
graduation.** Slot S08, issue #1207, 2026-10-06, from the owner's answers on
#1207: 1A, 2A, 3A. Migration 398.

### What the owner chose

1. **Who operates: A.** An Axal admin, assigned per startup (`projects.id`).
2. **What is reviewed: A.** The 83(b) filing record (D361). The Compliance
   page's Week 4 categories are not reviewed.
3. **Does a review gate graduation: A.** No.
4. **The role's wording** was not answered. S08 proposed this line on #1207,
   and the owner can change it in review: "Operator review is a check that
   the filing record is complete. It is not tax or legal advice." The page,
   HQ and the Worker all show that one line.

### The store (migration 398)

There are two append-only tables, and no row is ever updated:
- **`section_83b_operator_assignments`.** The newest row for a project is its
  current operator. A NULL operator is an unassignment, so the history stays.
- **`section_83b_operator_reviews`.** Each row records the operator, the
  outcome (`complete` or `incomplete`), a note, a follow-up date, the tracker
  reviewed when there is one, and who recorded it. The newest row is the last
  review, and its follow-up date is the next one. A CHECK holds `item` to
  `section_83b_filing_record` (2A).

### Who writes and who reads

- **Only an admin writes**, through `routes/admin_83b_review.ts` at
  `/api/admin/83b-review`, which `requireAdmin` gates:
  - `GET /` lists every startup with a tracker and the active admins.
  - `PUT /projects/:id/operator` assigns an operator. The operator must be
    an active admin.
  - `POST /projects/:id/reviews` records a review. It is refused with no
    operator assigned (409), for an unknown outcome, for a follow-up date
    that is not a calendar date or is in the past, and for another startup's
    tracker.
- **Every write is recorded through `logAdminAction`**, with the acting
  admin. `section_83b_operator_assigned` and
  `section_83b_operator_review_recorded` are written to both `activity_logs`
  and `admin_audit_log`.
- **The review is stamped with the assigned operator.** The admin who typed
  it is stored as `reviewed_by`.
- **The founder reads their own startup's** through
  `GET /api/legal/83b/operator-review?project_id=`. That is the owning
  founder, or an accepted owner or co-founder member (`canAccessProject` with
  `write`). An admin reads any startup's. Anyone else (another founder, an
  advisor member, a partner, an investor) and an unknown project all get 404.

### What the founder sees

The 83(b) page's Operator assist row (`OperatorAssistRow`) shows the
operator, the last review (its outcome and date) and the next follow-up.
- A value not on record says Not recorded, with its reason: no operator, no
  review yet, or no follow-up set.
- A failed read says Unreadable, as does a failed project read.
- Deadline reminders still have no store and still say Not recorded.

### Not built

- Reviews of the Compliance page's Week 4 categories (2B).
- A graduation gate (3B).
- Follow-up reminders: the follow-up date is shown, and nothing sends it.
