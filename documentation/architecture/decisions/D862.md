### 1. Where it lives

- **The address is D1309's.** `/validate/record/whatsapp` is Inbox, and each other tab is a path under it:
  - `/sessions` and `/sessions/:id`;

### 2. The boards

- **WA1 Connect.**
  - The six steps from D856's read, each with its state.
  - The first step not done is the only one to press: "Resume from this step" when it failed, "Run this step" otherwise.
  - Each press opens a dialog with the Worker's own confirm sentence, then sends `confirm: true`.
  - While the guided setup is off (the read answers 404), the tab offers Embedded Signup on the connector page.
- **WA2 Inbox.**
  - WhatsApp chats by default, newest first, with the All · WhatsApp · Telegram filter Telegram shares.
  - Each row shows the contact, the consent state in the canvas's words (Asked, Given, Declined…) and the message count.
  - Actions:
    - Assign to interview: a dialog picking one of the venture's interviews; creating one stays in the inbox;
    - Ask for consent;
    - Ignore.

    Each opens a dialog that says what it changes.
  - A contact name that is a phone number is shown as "•••• 1234".
- **WA3 Transcript.**
  - The session's final segments as turns, with Original · Translation · Both.
  - A translation is shown only when the Worker sent one; without one the original stands in every view.
  - Voice notes are marked.
  - A pending segment reads "Translation pending".
  - The consent banner is the session read's `channel_consent`.
- **WA4 and WA5 Analytics.**
  - D858's figures as tiles, messages by side, pain themes, objections, willingness to pay, competitors and quotes per theme.
  - Each figure carries its Source chips, Recorded or Typed.
  - A figure the store does not hold reads Not recorded with the Worker's reason, never 0.
  - The venture view reads the last 30 or 90 days, with `channel: whatsapp`.
- **The trend.** Eight Monday-to-Sunday weeks, one venture read each, so a week that cannot be read is a dashed hairline marked Not recorded.
- **WA6 Proposals.**
  - The session's pending placements, grouped as the canvas groups them: Pricing, Market intelligence, Problem, Customer and Follow-ups.
  - Each item shows its quote and its confidence (High, Medium or Low from the model's score; Not recorded without one).
  - A prototype ask carries "For Build".
  - Accept, Dismiss and Accept all (at most 50, proposed-only items left out) each confirm first.
  - Edit opens the session page, where a placement's payload is edited before Accept.
- **WA7 Out of AI tokens.** Covered by the pending notice (§3).
- **WA8–WA11 States:**
  - **Empty:** no WhatsApp connection; it offers Connect.
  - **Loading.**
  - **Error:** "could not be read. That is not the same as having none.", with Try again.
  - **No permission:** a 401 or 403, or a member offered no channel.
- **WA12 Phone.** Every target is at least 44px tall, rows stack, and dialogs are bottom sheets.
- **WA13 Dark.** Every colour has its dark pair, and `check-dark-mode` passes.

### 3. Departures

- **The inbox row's last message, language chip and voice-note count are not shown.** `ThreadView` carries none of them, and reading every thread to show them would decrypt every waiting chat for a list.
- **WA7 does not name the reason.** The Worker keeps no reason for a pending translation. So the transcript says how many messages wait and that the originals are kept, names running out of AI tokens as one cause, and links Add tokens. It never claims tokens ran out.
- **Proposal segment links are not drawn.** The placements read carries a quote and a span, not a segment's link.
- **The venture range** is the last 30 or 90 days. There is no custom range picker.
- **The trend counts sessions per week**, from one venture read per week, because no trend read exists.
- **Voice notes** are recognised by their speech-to-text model on the segment, since a chat's segments carry no kind.

### 4. Security and privacy

- **Phone numbers:** a name that is a phone number is masked, and the setup's number is the Worker's masked one.
- **Quotes:** they appear only where the analytics read sends them (D858's quote consent). The venture view shows counts only.
- **Scope:** every read is the Worker's, scoped to the venture's founder. Nothing here widens it.
