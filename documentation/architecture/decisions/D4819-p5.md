## D4819 part 5 — New licence steps 1 and 3 to 6 (L41 to L47) (#4819, #5931)

Built for #5931, after parts 1 to 3 in `D4819.md`, to the boards D5930 landed (`design/incoming/Licences v2 New Licence Steps Page.dc.html` and its boards file, L41 to L49). It is frontend only: no Worker route, no migration. Every write is a route that already exists, and each step's call was checked against it.

### What was built

- **`licences/NewLicenceSteps.jsx`.** The five step cards: Legal entity (L41, L41B), Commercial terms (L42), Administrators (L43), Agreement (L44) and Issue (L45). Each card has its board's title, fields, primary action and footer line. `NewLicenceStep` is the piece L21's frame places in its card slot. Its props are the step index, the draft (null before step 1), `onLicence` and `onStep`. It keeps every step's typed values while the person moves between steps. So Back, Continue, Edit from the review, a blocker's link, a failed save and a refusal on Issue never clear what was typed.
- **`licences/newLicenceModel.js`.** The rules, as pure functions. They cover what each step sends, which field a refusal belongs to, the review's five groups and the Issue step's four questions.
- **Reused, not copied.** Step 3's seats are `SeatFields`, now exported from `CommercialTerms.jsx`; `SeatEditor` draws the same component. Step 3's money terms go through `buildCoreSave` (`lib/licenceCoreTermsForm.js`), the Terms section's own function, so only changed fields travel, with `expected_revision`. Steps 4 and 6 read the administrators through `useLicenceAdmins`, now exported from `AdminsEditor.jsx`. Steps 5 and 6 read the agreement through `useLicenceContract`, now exported from `ContractStep.jsx`, and `currentContract`. Both editors use the same hooks.
- **The consequence sheet (L17, D4819 part 4) is on main and is used.** Its four headings are now one constant, `CONSEQUENCE_QUESTIONS` in `licenceConsequenceModel.js`. The sheet and the Issue step both read it. The Issue step also takes its branch sentence and its reason statement from `licenceConsequence('activate', …)`, so the review and the sheet cannot say different things.

### The routes, step by step

| Step | Call | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 create | `POST /admin/licences` | Now also sends registered address, signatory name and title. A 409 "`<REF>` is already in use" sits on the reference field. |
| 1 after the draft exists | `PATCH /:uid/entity`, then `PUT /:uid/brand` (white-label) | Only changed keys are sent. A recorded value cleared is refused on its field as "`<key>` cannot be blank", the Worker's words. If the entity saves and the kit does not, the page says so (partial). |
| 3 | `PUT /:uid/seats`, then `PATCH /:uid/terms` | Two writes, in that order. If the second fails: "Seats were saved. The money terms were not." `errors[]` land on their fields as `<field> <message>`. `terms_conflict` shows who changed the terms and when, with Reload terms; typed values stay. |
| 4 | `GET` / `POST /:uid/admins` | 404 and the 409 "already administers" sit on the email field. A reason under 10 characters sits on the reason field. |
| 5 | `GET` / `POST /:uid/contract` | Restricted and empty templates cannot be chosen. The text appears only right after Generate, because GET returns no body. Each unfilled field says where it is set, with "Go to step N". |
| 6 | `GET /:uid/activation`, `POST /:uid/activate` | `blocked` lists its blockers, each linked to its step. `bad_transition` and `licence_changed` show in the Worker's words with Reload review. No answer reads Outcome unknown, with Check status. `pushed` decides the L18 outcome; a partial one never looks like full success. |

The write bar (TOTP session plus a step-up) asks for its code through the shell's one step-up sheet (`components/StepUpModal.jsx`). Cancelling the sheet keeps every value. A 403 "TOTP required" reads "Sign in again with your authenticator app to make this change." A 403 "Super admin required" reads "Only HQ Super Admins can issue licences; ask a Super Admin."

Every existing create and update route can carry its step. Nothing needed a new route.

### Choices a reviewer should check

- **Step 3's reason needs 10 characters.** That is how L42 and L46 draw it. `PATCH /:uid/terms` itself only needs a reason that is not blank.
- **Percent to basis points is decimal-text arithmetic** (`percentToBps`), so "12.5" is exactly 1250. A value outside 0 to 100 is the field's own check. A value that is not whole basis points ("12.505") is sent as typed, and the Worker refuses it in its own words, as L46 draws.
- **The token split field is labelled `TOKEN_SPLIT_HQ_LABEL`** ("Token split to the Licensee", D4671), not the board's bare "Token split".
- **The undo answer follows L45:** "No. It can be suspended or terminated later; termination releases the territory." The activate sheet's own answer is different, so the review takes its undo text from the board rather than from the sheet.
- **The countries are shown as their codes.** The board says "Switzerland", but the licence stores codes and no name lookup was added.

So New licence and Issue first licence still open today's inline form. `NewLicenceStep` is ready for the frame to place, and `stepLine` gives the frame its step line.
- **The Eadwyn rail panels.** These are the step 5 list of unfilled fields and the step 6 plain-words summary; both belong to the frame's rail. The step 5 document card already lists each unfilled field and where it is set. Step 3's renewal proposal is drawn inside the card, with Accept, Edit and Discard, because no frame rail exists yet.
- **The outcome banner on Overview (L47D).** The outcome is shown on the Issue step, with links to the licence's Overview and to Operations.
- **The phone (L48) and dark (L49) boards.** The cards stack at phone width, use 44px targets below `md` and keep the primary action at the bottom. Every colour has its dark pair. The Steps sheet and the full-height verification sheet belong to the frame.
