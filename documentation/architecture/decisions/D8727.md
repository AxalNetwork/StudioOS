### What the canvas decides

- The h1 becomes **HQ home**. The page opens on the rose scope bar and **What needs my attention?**; escalations and scope come before Platform tiles, subsidiary cards and the licensing feed, which are kept as shipped (SHQ00, SHQ20).
- Escalations get their own read beside the overview. Each source has its own Reading, value, All clear and Unavailable state with its own Try again; retrying one card leaves every loaded card usable (SHQ01 to SHQ04, SHQ06 to SHQ06D).
- Pending is loading, a confirmed zero is All clear, and only a failed read is Unavailable. Slow is shown at 8 s, and a read with no answer at 20 s counts as failed (SHQ05, SHQ05B).
- A failure on first load is announced once through one polite live region; a failed retry is an alert because the person pressed Try again (SHQ04, SHQ06C, SHQ20).
- If the overview fails while escalations are current, rows show the branch code, since the subsidiary name comes from the overview (SHQ06D).
- An escalation row opens HQ Support's existing Queue: row → Answer on Support → Review (SHQ03 → SHQ07), linking `/admin/hq-support`, optionally `?tab=queue&escalation=<uid>`.
- My tasks moves last with one sentence, "Work is not switched on for HQ yet", and no Show or Due filters; controls that cannot change the result are not drawn. Copy carries no internal references.
