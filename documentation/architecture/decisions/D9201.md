## D9201 — The product tour waits for the cookie choice (#9201)

**Decided.** The product tour does not open until the visitor's cookie choice
is settled: a choice is stored (`isDecided()` in `frontend/src/lib/cookieConsent.js`,
the record the banner itself reads) and the cookie chooser, the first-visit
banner or the "More choices" dialog, is not drawn. Once both hold it opens
after its usual delay.

**Why.** On a first signed-in visit the tour opened over the banner. Its
backdrop (`z-[240]`) dimmed the banner (`z-50`) and the tour made the rest of
the page inert, so 'Reject all' could not be reached until the tour was ended.
The choice has to be reachable first. Waiting for a stored choice alone was
not enough: "Save preferences" stores the choice but leaves the dialog open,
and the tour would have made that open dialog inert. The tour therefore also
waits for the chooser to close.

**What it changed.**
- `lib/cookieConsent.js` gains the one shared signal: `setChooserShown()`
  (CookieConsent reports whether it draws the chooser), `isChoiceSettled()`
  and `subscribeChoiceSettled()`, which re-reads on a consent change, a
  chooser change or a write from another tab. `hooks/useCookieChoiceSettled.js`
  is its React hook. #9175 (the banner over the sign-in sheet at 390px) can
  read the same signal instead of adding its own.
- `CookieConsent.jsx` gains only the two effects that report its open state.
- `ProductTour.jsx` opens only when `useCookieChoiceSettled()` is true.

**Not built.** The banner's own stacking and placement are unchanged (#9175
owns the phone case). If the banner fails to render (its SafeMount), no choice
can be made and the tour stays closed; it can still be run from the account
settings once a choice exists.

**Verified.** `frontend/test/product_tour_cookie_choice_9201.test.mjs` mounts
the tour and the banner together: with no stored choice the tour is not drawn
and 'Reject all' is drawn and not inert; 'Reject all' or 'Accept all' opens
the tour; after "Save preferences" it waits until the dialog closes; with a
stored choice there is no banner and the tour opens as before; and with no
banner mounted it still waits for a stored choice. Mutation check: 8
mutations, 8 caught, 0 equivalent.
