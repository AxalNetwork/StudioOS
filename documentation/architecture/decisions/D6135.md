## D6135 — Honest investor profile completion (#6135)

The owner chose to remove the unused value-slider step. The earlier step
indexes stay unchanged; the old slider and final LP indexes both clamp to the
remaining LP step. Saved answers are retained in onboarding progress, including
old fields, and unused weights are no longer copied into the investor profile.
Copy describes the thesis fit shown on deals and consented anonymised Investor
Signals; it promises no matching engine.

The investor flow requires its final progress save to succeed before marking
onboarding complete. OnboardingWizard gains optional final-save and completion
rendering hooks; other flows keep their defaults. Its completion event still
retires the shell's stale resume gate.

The investor completion panel reports the actual profile-copy result. Failure
says Your profile was not saved, offers Try again and Continue anyway, and
keeps the person on the page. No handoff is offered during a pending save.
After success or explicit continuation, See deal flow uses investorLanding;
only admin/investor roles with yes_now/maybe intent also get the fund route.
An Exploring member goes to the holding page and gets no fund door.

Reopening a completed wizard never automatically overwrites a potentially
newer profile. It retains the saved answers, offers an explicit Save profile,
or lets the person continue. There is no persisted claim that a failed or
unconfirmed copy succeeded.

and retry, explicit continuation, final progress failure, legacy step resume,
completed-wizard reopening, pending saves, consent, payload and role-safe
handoffs. Mutations and final build/drift results accompany the batch review.
