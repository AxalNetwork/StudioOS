## D4964 — Company settings: one company read, a joined roster, structured matching fields, accessible navigation (#4964)

- **Status:** Built, 2026-11-07 (Worker, migration and frontend). Stacked on D4963.
- **Migration:** `202611072325_company_region_codes.sql` adds `company_profiles.region_codes`.
- **Builds on:**
  - D4152 (the General form and the settings head);
  - D4963 (`profile_version`);
  - D431 (carry is served to the member and editors only);
  - #3841 (a member's email goes to that member only);
  - D1413 (the Projects tab).

### Reads

**One company read per General page.** `CompanySettingsAt` read `GET /api/company/:uid` for the heading and `CompanyGeneralForm` read it again for the form. Both now subscribe to `useCompanyDetail(uid)`:

- the first subscriber of a visit starts the read, and every subscriber mounted meanwhile shares it;
- `reload()` is one refetch for all of them;
- a write's answer (save, logo, conflict) is published to every subscriber, so the head's name, the form, the preview and the edit capability read one response.

**A joined roster.** `detailDto` loaded the links, then each member's account in a loop. It also made separate reads for membership and the edit rule. It now does one joined read: `user_company_links JOIN users`, ordered by link id.

- **Membership and editing** are decided from the same rows. `canEdit` takes the viewer's link from the roster (`{ link }`) and makes no query of its own. Its literal role list is unchanged, and the guard tests that pin it still read it.
- **Measured:** a company of 60 members is answered with the same number of statements as one of 2. That is three from the company read on: the company, the route's member gate, and the roster.
- **`viewer_can_edit`** is now served, and the form uses it when present.
- **FastAPI:** the dev serializer gets the same join (dev parity only).

### The address names the record

In Settings › Projects, an id the company does not hold fell back to the first project. The URL and the record could disagree, and its settings and its Move out could be acted on under another project's address. `chooseProject` decides it now:

- **no id:** an explicit replace-redirect to the first project's own address;
- **an unknown id:** a "Project not found" state;
- **after a move-out:** the tab navigates back to the index.

### Matching fields

`services/companyProfileVocab.ts` holds the vocabulary. The frontend mirrors it, and a test holds the codes equal.

**Regions are canonical codes** in `region_codes`, a JSON array of eleven codes (`global`, `north_america`, `latin_america`, `uk_ireland`, `europe`, `middle_east_north_africa`, `sub_saharan_africa`, `south_asia`, `east_asia`, `southeast_asia`, `oceania`).

- `international_presence` stays as optional prose, labelled "Notes on where you operate".
- The form edits regions as a labelled group of checkboxes.
- A PATCH takes only codes from the list.

**The migration strategy is lazy and lossless:**

- A row with no codes is served codes **derived** from its legacy text by an alias table: case and punctuation are folded, and "UK" and "United Kingdom" are one region.
- It is marked `regions.source: 'derived'`, with every entry no alias covers listed as `unmapped`. The form shows these for the editor to place.
- The first committed General save stores the derived codes, and they are named in the `profile_update` audit.
- The legacy text is never rewritten. Clearing the codes falls back to it.

**The other fields.** Where this overlaps D4962, D4962 is the write contract (`services/companyProfileFields.ts`): revenue is bounded text, not an enum; a website and a LinkedIn address are canonical http(s); the count is a whole number from 0 to 1,000,000; prose is bounded at that contract's lengths. A refusal of those is **400** with `code`. `region_codes` stays **422** `region_codes_invalid`.

- **The form** still offers the revenue bands (`pre_revenue`, `under_100k`, `100k_1m`, `1m_5m`, `5m_20m`, `20m_plus`) as a select. A stored value off the list stays as its own option until someone changes it. Website and LinkedIn are `type="url"`. Employee count is `type="number"`, min 0, step 1. Prose the editor is typing is capped at 500 characters.
- **Each check applies only to a value that changes**, on both the Worker and the form. A value written before these rules (a free-text revenue, a scheme-less website) is shown and kept until someone edits it.
- `settings_no_fill` reasons the new column into its refusal list.

### Accessibility

- **The settings head** used `role="tablist"` and `role="tab"` without the tabs pattern (no arrow keys, no panels). It is now a labelled `<nav>` of links, the current page marked `aria-current="page"`.
- **Every field's label names its control.**
- **The logo tile** looked clickable (pointer cursor) but was not a control. Editable, it is a real button that opens the picker and takes a drop. Read-only, it is the mark alone, with no pointer and no handler.
