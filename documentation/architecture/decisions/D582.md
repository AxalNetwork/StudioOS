## D582 — Neither /build/brand nor /incorporate retires; Lab members reach the Lab's Incorporate page

**Both routes stay. A Lab member who follows an "Incorporate" link from a Lab
page reaches `/spinout-lab/incorporate`, and everyone else reaches the generic
`/incorporate` wizard.** Slot S08, issue #1209, 2026-10-06. S1 decided **A**,
the stated default, after the owner's "Yes" on #1009 could also have been
read as "retire". No migration.

### Why both stay

- **`/build/brand`** (BrandBuilderPage, any founder, in the Grow workspace)
  holds the layout and media editing that the Lab Brand page lacks. The Lab
  page links to it for that work (D360).
- **`/incorporate`** is the generic wizard: Delaware C-Corp and LLC, UK,
  Singapore and Estonia. It serves founders, partners and investors, and the
  Legal Engine's Incorporation card embeds it. The Lab Incorporate page is a
  different surface for Lab members, and ROUTE_MAP says not to collapse
  them.

### What B or C would take

- **B (retire `/build/brand`).** Port the layout and media editing into
  `/spinout-lab/brand` first. Decide where founders outside the Lab edit their
  brand (`/grow/brand` or the Lab page). Then redirect, as D361 did for
  `/incorporate/83b`.
- **C (redirect Lab members inside `/incorporate`).** Add a member check to
  the wizard route and keep the wizard for everyone else.
- **Either one** is a rescope by Session 1 before it is built.

### What changed

- **The helper.** `incorporateRoute(user)` (`SpinoutLabStartupPage.jsx`, beside
  D352's `cofounderAgreementRoute`) sends an admin or an account with
  `spinout_lab_active === 1` to `/spinout-lab/incorporate`. That route's
  `labRoles(['admin'])` guard admits exactly those accounts, and everyone else
  goes to `/incorporate`. Before `/me` loads, the helper answers
  `/incorporate`, so nobody is sent to a page that would lock them out.
- **The three links that used `/incorporate`** now go through the helper:
  - the Office Hours page's milestone tools (`milestoneTool`);
  - its "Related tools" row;
  - the Startup page's "Legal entity" readiness row.

### Verified

`frontend/test/incorporate_routes_d582.test.mjs` has 5 tests. They check:
- both routes, and the Lab route, keep their guards;
- the Lab Brand page links to `/build/brand`;
- the helper's answer for each viewer;
- the three links use the helper;
- no Lab page or Lab component links to `/incorporate` directly, apart from
  the helper's own fallback.

Removing either route, the Brand page's link or the helper fails the test.
