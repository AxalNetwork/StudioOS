## D6278 — A benchmark's "Ours" from the fund's recorded TVPI or DPI (#6278)

Canvas RS-14 (Investor Research v2 Benchmarking, D6273) draws "Use our figure
from [fund]" under a benchmark's Ours. The owner answered on #6278
(2026-11-15): a benchmark may carry an optional fund reference, and using the
recorded figure fills Ours without overwriting a typed value.

**Schema.** Migration `202611180253_6278_research_benchmark_fund.sql` adds
three nullable columns to `research_benchmarks`: `fund_id` (references
`vc_funds`, set null when the fund goes), `our_value_metric` (`tvpi` or `dpi`)
and `our_value_as_of`. A row with no `fund_id` reads "As entered on this row".
Ours stays TEXT: the copied figure is written as the multiple the row shows,
for example `1.31×`, so a typed row and a copied row read the same way.

**One fund list, one formula.** `readableFunds` moved from
`routes/fund_performance.ts` to `services/readableFunds.ts`, unchanged. Fund
performance and the benchmark offer read the same list (admin: every fund;
investor: their LP funds; the GP of record: the funds they run), and the
figure is `readFundPerformance`'s TVPI or DPI as of today. No second formula.

**Routes** (`routes/research_benchmark_funds.ts`, mounted ahead of
`research.ts` so `fund-figures` is never read as a uid):

- `GET /api/research/benchmarks/fund-figures` lists the readable funds with
  each figure as `{ value, display, reason }`. A figure the fund does not
  record carries the fund-performance reason and no value. A failed read is
  503 `fund_figures_unreadable`, never an empty list.
- `POST /api/research/benchmarks/:uid/ours-from-fund` `{ fund_id, metric,
  replace? }` on the caller's own benchmark. Another owner's benchmark is 404
  `benchmark_not_found`. A fund the caller cannot read is 404 `fund_not_found`,
  the same body as a fund that does not exist. A figure that is not recorded
  is 409 `figure_not_recorded` with "Not recorded: <reason>", and nothing is
  written. A typed Ours is 409 `typed_value` until `replace: true`. A figure
  copied earlier is not typed, so swapping it needs no confirmation.

**A hand edit drops the link.** `PATCH /benchmarks/:uid` that changes
`our_value` clears the three columns, because the typed figure is no longer
the fund's. A PATCH that leaves Ours as it was keeps them.

**The fund name follows access.** The detail read's `item.our_from` is
`{ fund_id, fund_name, metric, as_of }`. `fund_name` is null once the reader
can no longer open the fund; the figure stays as copied, and the page says
"a fund you can no longer open".

**Page.** Under Ours, `BenchmarkDetail` shows "As entered on this row" or
"Recorded TVPI from <fund> as of <date>", and `OursFromFund` offers the fund
and the figure, shows the figure or "Not recorded: …", and asks before
replacing a typed figure ("Replace my figure" / "Keep mine").
