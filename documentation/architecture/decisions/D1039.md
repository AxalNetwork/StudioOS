## D1039 — Approvals part 1: the Perks queue without drafts, co-marketing decisions behind requireAdmin, and the Exploring lane counting signed Explorers

- **Status:** decided by S21 for the owner, 2026-10-25 (#2887; part 1 of #2390, from D1038's integration in #2852). Built by the S08 agent in slot S29, at the owner's request.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `daf7624bd2`. Paths are under `cloudflare-worker/src/` unless they say otherwise.
- **Migration:** none.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1038's map, `documentation/audits/APPROVALS_MAP_2026-10-25.md` §1 items 5, 6 and 8 (in #2852);
  - D130 and D215, the approval lanes and their one read model;
  - D135, the HQ compliance freeze.

### 1. The Perks queue holds only listings in review

- **The change.** `GET /api/perks/admin/queue` (`routes/perks.ts`) listed `in_review` and `draft`. It now lists `in_review` only.
- **Why.** A partner's submission is created `in_review`, so a `draft` is nobody's to review and is not reviewer backlog. This is the rule D130 states for referrals.
- **Listings is unchanged.** It still shows a draft under its own status (`?status=draft`). Review still accepts a draft, so nothing that could be decided before is refused now.
- **No lane count reads this queue on `main`.** The glance's Perk listings lane is part 3's. The Programs page's comment that named `draft` is corrected (`frontend/src/pages/admin/programs/programsModel.js`).

### 2. Co-marketing decisions are behind `requireAdmin`, and a decline needs a reason

- **The gate.** `GET /api/comarketing/admin/queue` and `POST /admin/pitches/:uid/approve|reject|publish` checked `requireAuth` plus `isAdmin`. They now call `requireAdmin`, as every other admin surface does.
  - The HQ compliance freeze and its 423 refusal therefore apply to the three decisions.
  - A read is never frozen (D135), so the queue still opens for a frozen admin.
  - The decisions now run inside `mapError`, so the gate's refusals answer in the house's words.
- **The decline.**
  - `reject` requires `notes` of at least `DECLINE_REASON_MIN` (10) characters after trimming.
  - A shorter or missing reason is refused through `refuse`, with 400 and code `reason_required`: "A decline needs a reason of at least 10 characters. The partner sees it." Nothing changes.
  - The reason is stored trimmed in `review_notes`, which the partner reads on their own pitch.
  - Approve and publish keep their optional note.
- **The old page.** The co-marketing page's notes field (`frontend/src/pages/CoMarketingPage.jsx`) now says a decline needs a reason, instead of "optional". Part 8 brings the dialogs.

### 3. The Exploring lane counts Explorers who signed

- **The rule.** An admin can give an Explorer a role only once their binding agreement is signed (`routes/admin_exploring.ts`, POST /users/:id/assign-role).
- **The lane** (`services/approvalSources.ts`, `exploring`) used to count every account in the holding role. It now counts an Explorer only when `user_role_review.binding_envelope_id` names an envelope on that same account whose status is `completed`, ignoring case. That is the route's own gate, written as subqueries so the count still has no join (D130).
  - The rows read the same predicate, so the board and the count agree.
- **The sub-count.** `ApprovalSource` gains an optional `subSql`: one more literal query whose columns are counts.
  - `laneCounts` reports them as `sub`, and `queuePressure` passes them to the branch home and HQ's glance.
  - If that read fails, `sub` is null and the lane's own count stands. It is never read as zeros.
  - Exploring's `sub` has two figures:
    - `holding`: every account in the role;
    - `no_agreement_sent`: those with no binding envelope.
- **HQ's Accounts tile keeps its meaning.** It read the lane's count as "N exploring". It now reads `sub.holding`, so the tile still counts every account in the holding role while the lane counts those ready for one. If the lane cannot be read, the tile reads null, as before.
- **D1038's note.** Its lane note ("Counts every Explorer, signed or not, until part 1…") is in `frontend/src/pages/admin/approvals/approvalsModel.js`, which is in #2852 and not on `main`. Whichever of #2852 and this PR lands second drops the note, and can also show `no_agreement_sent` as the lane's sub-count.

### 4. Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/approvals_fixes_d1039.test.ts` (new, 6) uses `node:sqlite` with the baseline and the whole migration chain. It covers:

1. The Perks queue holds in-review listings, oldest first, with no draft, and Listings still shows the draft. The queue is admin-only.
2. Co-marketing: a frozen admin reads the queue but cannot decide (423), and nothing changes. A partner and a founder are refused 403. An admin approves.
3. A decline refuses no reason, an empty one, a blank one and two short ones with `reason_required`, leaving the pitch unchanged. A reason is stored trimmed, the partner reads it, and approve still takes no note.
4. Exploring:
   - The lane counts only signed Explorers, in any case, and not one who was sent but has not signed, has no agreement, signed someone else's envelope, or already has a role.
   - Its oldest is the oldest of the signed, and its rows match the count.
   - The sub-count reaches `queuePressure`, and only Exploring has one.
5. HQ's Accounts tile counts every holding-role account, while the lane counts the signed.
6. A failed sub-count is null with the lane still counted. A failed lane is null, never 0.

Fixtures that moved to the new rule: `_approval_s16_stores.mjs` gains `esign_envelopes`, `binding_envelope_id` and `signBinding()`. Each fixture's explorer now signs, so these tests keep meaning what they meant:

- `analytics_d210`;
- `branch_approvals_s16_d215` (with an unsigned explorer added who must not count);
- `studio_glance_hq_d555`.

**Mutation check.** 13 mutations were tried, and all 13 were caught:

- the queue counting drafts;
- the decisions skipping the freeze;
- the queue open to any account;
- the reason minimum lowered to 1;
- the reason not trimmed;
- the reason not required;
- an unsigned agreement counted;
- another account's envelope counted;
- the status compared by case;
- every Explorer counted as unsent;
- the Accounts tile reading the lane;
- a failed sub-count read as zeros;
- a failed sub-count taking the lane down.

### Rollback

Revert the PR. The queue counts drafts again, the decisions skip the freeze, a decline takes no reason, and the lane counts every Explorer. Nothing is stored or migrated.
