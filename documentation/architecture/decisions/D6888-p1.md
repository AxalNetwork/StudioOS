## D6888 part 1 — Eadwyn's covering-note proposal mounted in the Statement sheet's rail (#6888)

**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Slot:** S09 · **Status:** built. Part 1 of #6888 (the Worker surface, rail option and card are `D6888.md`, PR #6914). Frontend only: no Worker, route or schema change.

### What

`StatementSheet.jsx` (#6867) mounts `StatementCoverNote` for the sheet's chosen quarter-end, in a rail the canvas draws two ways:

- **Desktop (PF-13a).** Eadwyn's column in Portfolio's rail slot: a 320px column beside the sheet (`.pst-rail`, `position: fixed` at the right; the sheet moves left by the same width through `.pst-layer.has-rail`). It stays inside the dialog's DOM, so `aria-modal` still holds and Escape still closes both.
- **390 (PF-13h).** A collapsible card, "Eadwyn · covering note proposal", between the preview and what the PDF holds; its toggle is a 44px button with `aria-expanded`.

It is one mount at both widths (CSS decides the placement), so the proposal is the same at either width. It sits in the date's body, so a new date mounts it afresh and the card already forgets a proposal from another date.

### The wiring

- **Nothing runs on mount.** The investor presses Propose; the run is for the chosen date.
- **Copy** is the card's own (the clipboard).
- **Edit** ticks "Include as a cover note" and puts the proposal in that box, then focuses it, so the investor edits it there. The PDF still takes a note only from the ticked box: edited words go in, and unticking leaves it out. Edit replaces what the box held.
- **Discard** removes the proposal and returns the card to its press. It calls nothing and leaves the box as it was.
- **Eadwyn off or unavailable:** "Eadwyn is unavailable. The statement does not need it." (`coverNoteModel.js`), and the statement is made as before.

### Departures from the canvas

- The rail head draws "E · Eadwyn · Portfolio" without the canvas's close button. Closing the sheet closes it.
- The canvas's "Ask about this statement" box is not built. Ask has no statement surface, and #6888 does not ask for one.

- Discard calling nothing and leaving the box unticked;

- the 390 toggle and the two layout rules.
