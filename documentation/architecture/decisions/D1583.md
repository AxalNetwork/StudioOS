## D1583 — Deck v2 F4: a teaser, a one-pager and a memo from one deck version

Issue #3830 (deck builder v2, F4). Migration 744.

### What was decided

A founder makes three outputs from one saved deck version: a **teaser** (the
cover and the sections an investor reads first, at most six cards), a
**one-pager** (one 4:3 card with up to four metric tiles and a line per
section) and an **investment memo**. Each can be **blind**: no venture,
company or founder name, no logo or photo, no team card, and no link, video
or audio block (so no data room and no NDA link travels with it).

- **Entry.** ⋯ › This deck › "Teaser, one-pager or memo…" in `MORE_MENU`
  (`deckV2Model.js`), a write item, opening `DeriveSheet.jsx`. No toolbar.
- **A proposal first.** `POST /api/decks/:id/derive` files one
  `ai_rail_proposals` row on "Founder · Raise · Pitch deck" with the option
  `derive:teaser`, `derive:one_pager` or `derive:memo`. Nothing is stored
  until Accept. Ask (`DeckAsk.jsx`) lists these beside the card edits, under
  "Teasers, one-pagers and memos", with Accept, Accept all and Discard, and
  they count in its waiting badge.
- **Accept is its own route.** `POST /api/decks/:id/derive/:uid/accept`
  inserts the derivative and accepts the proposal in one D1 batch: the insert
  runs only `WHERE EXISTS` a still-proposed proposal, so the derivative exists
  exactly when its proposal is accepted. `decideRail` refuses to accept a
  `derive:*` proposal through the generic decide route (only discard passes),
  on the pattern of D1263's `raise:evidence` guard. A closed proposal is 409
  `not_open`.
- **The teaser and the one-pager run no model and cost nothing.** Their
  proposal holds the card titles and a sha256 of the cards, not the cards
  (the proposal payload limit is 16,000 characters). Accept derives them
  again from the frozen version and refuses with 409 `derivative_changed`
  when the hash differs (for example, the venture was renamed between propose
  and accept on a blind output).
- **The memo is one `memo_write` run** (new task class; GPT-OSS 120B, falling
  back to Nemotron 3 120B; option kind `memo` in the rail matrix, which reads
  the job table's IC memo step IN1 through `RAIL_KIND_JOBS`). Its model is the
  AIRail's only: the member's pick for `derive:memo` in `ai_rail_preferences`,
  or the row's default. The route passes no `model`, because D720 §5 lets a
  caller's model past the row whenever it can run; the client sends none.
  `POST /api/decks/:id/derive/quote` returns the hold, and the sheet shows
  "Holds up to $x · you pay what it uses" before Propose is offered. With AI
  paused or out of tokens the memo is greyed; the teaser and the one-pager
  still work and say "No model · free".
- **Memo figures equal the deck's.** Every number the cards state becomes a
  `{{Fn}}` placeholder before the model reads them; the system prompt forbids
  writing a digit; `memoFrom` puts the deck's own value back and drops any
  sentence that holds a bare digit or an unknown placeholder. A memo with no
  sentence left is 422 `memo_empty`; a memo whose figures are not the deck's
  is 422 `figure_in_output`. A teaser or one-pager with a stray figure is 500
  `derive_unsound` (it cannot happen by construction; the check is the proof).
- **No provenance on derived outputs.** `cleanCard` rebuilds every card from a
  whitelist: no binding, no notes, no source chip; a chart series that was
  only bound is dropped. The source cards come through `shareSlides` with
  sources, notes and bands off, and hidden cards removed.
- **Blind redaction** (`redact`) replaces the project's name, slug and company
  name with "the company" and the founder's and accepted members' names (and
  surnames of three letters or more) with "a founder", whole-word and in any
  case, longest first, in every string of a card but `image_url` and `url`.

### Deviation from the issue's wording (owner's choice)

The issue says `pitch_decks.format`. Asked whether a derived output should be
a `pitch_decks` row of another format or its own table, the owner chose **its
own table**: `deck_derivatives` (migration 744) points at the frozen master
version (`deck_id`, ON DELETE CASCADE), as D1565's `deck_variants` does.
`pitch_decks.format` exists, but only as `'deck'` (`CHECK (format = 'deck')`),
so no reader of `pitch_decks` changes and no version number is skipped. The
runtime bootstrap adds the column where 744 has not run
(`services/decks/deckVersionColumns.ts`).

### Not built

- **Sharing a derivative by link.** `GET /api/decks/:id/derivatives` and
  `GET /api/decks/derivatives/:uid` read it back (project read access), but the
  share link, print and export paths still send a `pitch_decks` version and
  the venture's name with it. A blind derivative is redacted and clean in
  storage; sending it is a follow-up.
- **Seeding the IC memo from a shared memo**, which the plan mentions.
- **Viewing an accepted derivative in the editor.** It is stored and readable
  through the routes above; no page draws it yet.
