## D1545 — Deck v2 B12: media and structure (#3788)

- **Status:** built by S15 on `agent/claude-s15/3788-media-structure`. Not merged, deployed or live verified.
- **Builds on:** D898 (the slide model and the editor), D902 (share links), D903 (images and their store), D1500 (card uids, `hidden` and `section`), D1604 (the v2 studio and its adapter seam).
- **Migration:** none. A video lives in R2 alone; a button press is a row in the existing `deck_share_conversions`.

### 1. Hidden cards and sections

D1500 already sanitizes `hidden` and `section` (at most 60 characters). B12 makes the Worker honour them.

A hidden card is left out of every surface a reader sees:
- every share link, publish, derive and grant (`shareSlides`);
- Present, the share viewer and the print page the PDF and PowerPoint are photographed from (`publicDeckOf`);
- PowerPoint, .odp and Google Slides (`exportDeckOf`);
- the HTML file (`deck_exports.ts`);
- the frontend's Present (`presentExportModel.cardsOf`).

The editor keeps the card, dimmed and tagged "Hidden" in the Cards strip.

Two readers that paired cards by place now pair them by uid:
- **`printDeckOf`'s speaker notes.** A hidden card would otherwise shift its notes onto the next card.
- **The heartbeat (`recordSlideCardUids`).** It names a seen slide by the card at that place in what the link sent (`shareSlides` over the whole deck, with the link's own `include_json`), not by the deck's raw index. This also fixes an older misalignment: a link without the Sources appendix sent fewer cards than the deck holds.

A section is a card's label. The card ⋯ offers "Move to section", an in-page dialog with the deck's existing sections, a new name or "No section"; a card in a section shows its name in the strip. Hide / Show opens its own in-page dialog saying where the card will and will not appear. Both are local edits, undoable, held until a version is saved.

### 2. Video: Loom and an uploaded MP4

- **Loom.** `videoOf` reads `loom.com/share/<32 hex>` and `/embed/`, and keeps `https://www.loom.com/share/<id>`. `checkVideoLink` asks Loom's oEmbed (`https://www.loom.com/v1/oembed`) with redirects refused, and keeps a poster only from `cdn.loom.com`.
- **An MP4.** `POST /api/decks/:id/videos/upload` takes a multipart `file`:
  - it needs write access to the deck's venture;
  - the file is at most 50 MB (413 `video_too_large`) and starts with an `ftyp` box (415 `not_mp4`);
  - it is stored at `deck-videos/<uid>.mp4` with `projectId`, `deckId` and `userId` in the object's metadata;
  - the answer is `{ video: { provider: 'mp4', video_id, url } }`.

  The block keeps `provider: 'mp4'`, `video_id`, `url`, `poster_url`, `title`, `ratio` and `loop` (an MP4 only; a provider's player decides for itself). A save keeps an MP4 only when its `url`'s path is exactly `/api/decks/videos/<video_id>/file`, so a block cannot point at another file or another host.
- **Reading the file.** `GET /api/decks/videos/:uid/file` serves the bytes with `Range` (206 with `content-range`), `nosniff` and `default-src 'none'`.
  - Once a saved version of one of the venture's decks names the uid, the file is public and cached immutably, as a kept image is.
  - Before that, only someone who may read the venture's project gets it, with `no-store`; anyone else gets 404.
- **The editor.** Add block › Video offers "A link" (YouTube, Vimeo or Loom, checked by the Worker) or "Upload an MP4", with an optional title, poster link and loop. It goes through `deckV2Adapter.media`. The picker's "Recorded video" opens the dialog on the upload.

Why no row: `deck_images` has a mime CHECK that allows images only, and a video has no proposal, no charge and no usage row to keep. A separate table would hold only what the object's metadata already holds.

### 3. Button

`button` is a new block kind: `{ url, label, style }`.
- `url` must be https on a public host (the slide-image rule).
- `label` is 1 to 60 characters.
- `style` is `primary` or `secondary` (anything else reads `primary`).

A refused button is named: "a button needs its words (at most 60 characters) and a link that is https on a public host." Its share gate is `none`, so it goes on every link.

On a share link, a press opens the link in a new tab and calls `POST /api/decks/share/:token/click { card_uid, block_id, view_id? }`:
- **The token.** It is verified as every share action verifies it (signature and the live-link predicate).
- **The button.** It must be on a card in the link's own `shareSlides`, so a hidden card's button or a block that is not a button answers 404 `button_not_found`.
- **The view.** A `view_id` is kept only when it is a view of this deck.
- **The row.** One `deck_share_conversions` row of type `button_click`, with `ref_id = <card_uid>:<block_id>`, at most once per view per button.

The founder reads `GET /api/decks/:id/button-clicks` → `{ buttons: [{ card_uid, block_id, clicks, viewers }] }`. A failed read is 503 `clicks_unreadable`, never an empty list. The adapter's `buttonClicks` (#3788) carries it.

### 4. Cross-deck paste

`POST /api/decks/:id/paste { from_deck_id, card_uids }` needs write access on the target and read access on the source. It answers `{ cards, missing }` and saves nothing: the editor places the cards and the founder saves the version.
- **Every card is sanitized as a save would be (`sanitizeSlides`).** A block a save would refuse is not pasted.
- **The source's working state stays behind.** The card arrives with no `uid` (the editor gives it one), no `status`, no `assignee` and no `notes`.
- **Across ventures, an image keeps its address and loses its `image_id`.** The id names the other venture's store, and D903's background and fit rules key on it.

The adapter's `pasteCards` (#3788) carries it. The editor's paste UI is not part of this slot.

### 5. The studio

- `deckV2Adapter.media` is swapped, with two new rows, `pasteCards` and `buttonClicks`, both keyed to #3788.
- The card ⋯'s Hide and Move to section no longer read the seam.
- The picker's Recorded video and Button are on main; Embeds stays "Coming later".
- **The Cards strip** virtualises past 24 cards on screen (`VIRTUALIZE_AT`), with `VirtualList`'s new `threshold` prop and a per-card height. The row layout below 1024 px stays one scrolling row.
- **Thumbnails** draw an image block's picture with `loading="lazy"`. The slide's own images are not lazy: the print page is photographed for the PDF and PowerPoint, and a lazy image there could be blank.
- A test renders 40 cards in under 2.5 s.
