### 1. One read behind every investor-facing score

No investor page reads a score outside `/api/venture-scores`. `frontend/test/scorecard_reads_d1260.test.mjs` holds it for:
- every file under `pages/investor/` and `components/scoring/`;

None of them may name the Lab scorer's reads (`getScores`, `getCohortBenchmark`, `/scoring/…`, `total_score`, `score_snapshots`), venture risk's automatic read, or founder risk.

### 2. Screening (`/deals/screening`, ID2)

- **`GET /api/deals/screening` sends the desk only.** Those are the deals the reader may see, by the rule it already had: Admins and Operating Partners see every deal; an investor sees deals they are invited to or committed on (D1254). There is no score, tier, six-dimension rubric or anomaly flag in the reply. The Lab scorer's snapshots come from the founder's own sliders, and they are practice.
- **The page draws each deal's eleven-scope strip** from `POST /api/venture-scores/batch`. The batch applies the deal relationship, the founder's switch and the NDA itself. A scorecard closed to the reader, or not shared, is an outline, never a low score.
- **The tiles:**
  - Deals;
  - Scorecards: how many have a scored scope open to the reader. It reads Not recorded while unread, never 0;
  - Pass taxonomy.
- **Chips and actions:**
  - **Chips:** Scored (now "Scorecards") and Pass reasons are live. Rubric and Red flags stay `unbuilt`, with the reason that they were the Lab scorer's.
  - **Actions:** New batch run and Edit rubric stay `unbuilt` for the scorecard's reasons. It recomputes on its own, and each investor's weighting is chosen in Deal flow's Your weighting dialog.
- **The Screening memo (`draft:screening-memo`) is retired.**
  - **Why:** its surface handed the Lab scorer's newest snapshot for the twelve top-scoring deals on the whole platform to Eadwyn, for any investor who opened the band. So it cited scores the scorecard does not hold, for deals the reader may not be on.
  - **What was removed:** the Worker surface (`research.ts`), the investor rail option and the rail matrix row (`scripts/build-rail-matrix.mjs` regenerated).
- **The investor Deals board's Screening section** lists the desk's deals with their sector and status, with no score.

### 3. Venture risk: the ten layers as Risk's sub-rows (SC5)

- **`services/ventureScoring/riskLayers.ts`** (new) reads each layer's analyst review from `venture_risk_overrides`, in the ten layers' one order.
  - A layer no analyst scored is "Not reviewed", never 0. A note with no score is not a review.
  - The band is the analyst's stored band, else derived from the score.
- **The layers are read with the scorecard, never stored with it.** Scoring and model v1 are untouched. They already feed Risk's "Analyst layer reviews" input as a mean (D1255).
- **Who sees them:**
  - Studio staff read the analyst's note.
  - The founder, a shared advisor, and the founder's "What investors see" preview after the NDA read the score and band only.
  - An investor reads them on the deal read after the NDA, never the note. Before the NDA, nothing.
- **The old automatic layer score is not read anywhere an investor looks.** That is `ventureRisk.ts`, built from the Lab scorer's sub-scores and the projects row; it scored a no-data layer as 0 and averaged it into an "overall derisking" figure.
- **`VentureRiskPanel.jsx` is rewritten on `GET /api/venture-scores/:projectId`.**
  - It shows the Risk scope (value, band in risk words, confidence, or why it has none) and the ten layers. The radar plots reviewed layers only.
  - An Admin or Operating Partner reviews a layer in an in-page dialog that says what it changes. It writes through the existing `PUT /api/venture-risk/:id/layers/:key` (and `DELETE`), then reads the scorecard again.
  - An investor, who reads scorecards through a deal, gets the Worker's sentence and a link to their deals.
  - `RiskLayerCard.jsx` and the client's `ventureRiskByProject` and `ventureRiskRecompute` are removed; nothing called them any more.
- **The project page (`ProjectDetail.jsx`)** no longer reads or shows the Lab scorer's Latest Score to an investor. Staff and the founder still see the practice score.
- **Risk's drawer** (`Scorecard.jsx` `ScopeDrawer`) draws the layers under its inputs, with Not reviewed and, for staff, the note.

### 4. The method page cites the model in force

- **`GET /api/venture-scores/models`** now describes the live model (`liveModel`), not always v1.0. It gains `live: { version, published_at, default_live }`. The publisher is never named.
- **`/scoring/method`** says which model is in force:
  - v1.0, which has scored every venture since the scorecard shipped;
  - or the version HQ published, with its day.

  It then lists each scope's inputs with weight, what it reads, and the store it reads, or "no store yet". The old line said v1 was "not published yet, so no venture carries a score from it today". That was untrue once D1255 began scoring.

- **Founder risk (`FounderRiskBadge.jsx`)** is unused and untouched. #2428 keeps founder risk out of the scorecard.
- **The Lab scorer,** its page and its routes are unchanged: practice.
