## D649 — Connectors replace Integrations, and three cycles get their primary action

**Issue:** #1461 (tracking, S1). **Decided by:** the owner, 2026-10-09.
**Canvases:** `design/incoming/Connectors Page.dc.html`,
`design/incoming/Cycle Action.dc.html`, `design/incoming/Connectors · Boards.dc.html`,
committed by #1468.

### What was decided

**Connectors replace Integrations.**
- A connector pulls information into StudioOS, or pushes it out to another tool, the way Connectors work in Claude and ChatGPT.
- The page moves from `/account/integrations` to `/account/connectors`, and gets an entry in the user menu.
- The old URL redirects to the new one.

**The same canvases draw the primary action of three founder cycles:**
- **Build:** "Connect a tool".
- **Raise:** "Call for Capital".
- **Grow:** "Share an update".

Validate's "Record a session" came later, under D656.

### Why

**Integrations was an account page that nobody reached from the work it fed.**
- A founder connecting Stripe, HubSpot or a calendar was in Build or Grow, not in account settings.
- The owner's reference is the connector model of Claude and ChatGPT:
  - each tool's state and scopes are visible in one place;
  - the AI rail reads from a connector only when it is switched on for that conversation;
  - nothing is written to a connected tool without the member's Send.

### The rules every task keeps

- **Credentials are encrypted at rest** (`integrations/secrets.ts`). None appears in a log, a response, an issue or a PR.
- **Data belongs to its companies.** A connector belongs to the member, like their licence and their Usage. Its data lands only in the companies the member ticks.
- **Eadwyn never writes to a connected tool on its own.** It shows the exact payload, and only a click on Send sends it. The Worker refuses any other path.
- **A Pro connector is refused on Free** by the Worker, not only hidden by the page.
- **Call for Capital never shows a round publicly.** Only verified investors on the platform see a call. It is stored as `round_calls`, because `capital_calls` is already a GP's call on its LPs. It ships behind `ROUND_CALLS_ENABLED`, off in production, until counsel answers the general-solicitation question on #1008.
- **A missing value is "Not recorded", never 0.**
