## D5940 — Pipeline analytics periods and calculation basis (#5940)

Analytics offers This quarter, Last quarter, Year to date and All time. The filter applies to decisions. The forecast covers all open quotes, and the win-rate series covers all recorded quarters. The existing analytics response returns a median per selected period and shape, but no quarterly cycle-time series or count of qualifying dates. Cycle time shows the selected period’s median and names that limit. A loss with no recorded reason has its own row.

Issue: #5940.
