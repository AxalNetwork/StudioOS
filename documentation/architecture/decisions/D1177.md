### 2. Home (UF1, UF16, UF17, UF22)

- **The four tiles:**
  - **Raise:** the round source D1176 chose, with its own source (a counsel-approved round call is Document).
  - **Runway today:** the measured source D1176 chose, with its source and date. It is never the forecast.
  - **Runway after the raise:** the plan's model, labelled Modelled, against the target. "At least N months" when cash lasts the whole horizon.
  - **Committed:** Not recorded. No read carries a round's commitments yet.
- **Where the money goes:** the stacked bar, and the section table (share, amount, a month over the target, first milestone, hires).
- **Cash over time:** the model's cash path, with a table view. The raise is counted in the month it is recorded to arrive. "Without the raise" is not modelled, and the foot says so.
- **Where this plan is used:**
  - the round, the cap-table scenario and the deck;
  - Open shows the round's sources with the one the plan uses (UF13);
  - Choose the round size offers only recorded sizes. It changes only the plan's raise (`PATCH /scenarios/:base`).
- **Needs attention:** D1176's `needs_you`, each in words, with the step that fixes it.
- **Empty (UF17):** tiles read Not recorded, and one card offers Edit split.

### 3. Allocation and Edit split (UF2, UF3)

- **The split:** the bar, with a table view.
- **The allocation table:** each section's milestones, hires and hires' cost against its amount. Its actual share over the last 3 months comes from the module read's Mercury part, which is the founder's alone; an admin reads "Founder only".
- **A section whose hires cost more than it holds** carries UF2w's warning. The split still saves.
- **Edit split:**
  - It takes the raise, five whole shares and the target (18, 24 or a custom 1–120 months).
  - The total turns red, and Save waits, until the shares add to 100.
  - It saves through D1176's `PUT` with the shares as typed.

### 4. Milestones and hires (UF5, UF6)

- **Summary:** counts and the modelled cash-out month.
- **Payroll from planned hires:** each step as the model sums it.
- **Milestones table:** search by name and filter by section. State comes from the model (Funded, At risk, Not costed) and the source from the record.
- **Hires table:** each hire, with a month's share of its yearly cost.
- **Add milestone:** an empty cost is sent as null and stays Not costed, never 0.
- **Add a hire** stays on the section's page. D1176 offers no hire create route here.

### 5. Runway (UF7)

- **Runway today:** from its most direct source. **Other runways we found** lists every measured candidate, with the one used marked. The **forecast** is listed apart, with no figure: "A model you set. Never runway today."
- **The cash path:** as on Home.
- **Planned burn:** by month, with a table by section.
- **Runway after the raise:** against the target. The forecast link keeps the venture (D1173).

### 6. Plan vs actual (UF12)

- **Window:** 3, 6 or 12 months.
- **Planned against actual share:** actual share is the section's spend over all mapped spend, from Mercury's accepted mappings. A measured 0% stays 0%; no mapping is Not recorded, never a zero.
- **Categories:** Mercury's categories and their pending mappings are the existing `MercuryRaiseFeed`. A suggestion changes nothing until it is accepted.
- **Without Mercury:** the tab says "Connect Mercury to see actual spend." An admin reads that it is the founder's.

### 7. History (UF15) and the one Worker read

- **`GET /api/cycles/raise/use-of-funds/history?project&page`** (`routes/cycle_use_of_funds.ts`):
  - the plan's `raise.use-of-funds` activity, newest first, 20 a page;
  - each row carries who made it and whether they are an admin;
  - it goes through the router's own scope, so founder or admin, active company, read only for support.
- **Mercury rows** (`mercury%`) are left out for anyone but the founder whose Mercury it is.
- **The tab:** search, the Admin tag, and the pager. The client is `useOfFundsApi.history`.
