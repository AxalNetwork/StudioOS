### 1. Access (DR6, DR6m)

- **By investor.** Each grant shows:
  - when it was granted and by whom;
  - its end, with "in N days" inside fourteen days;
  - its folders: All folders, the names, or "Could not be read", never drawn as every folder;
  - the NDA: Signed, Waiting for signatures or Not sent;
  - Committed, from Capital relationships;
  - what it opens now ("3 of 5");
  - its last event among the room's last fifty, or "None in the last fifty events", never "not opened".

  The list opens on Active grants, as the canvas's "Grant: Active". It can be searched and filtered by NDA, committed and grant state.
- **The matrix.** Each file is drawn against each grant in force: up to six investors side by side, then "+N more".
  - **Every cell goes through `filesModel.js`'s `grantOn`.** That is `accessNow` (the tier, the end date, the NDA), then the grant's folders.
  - So **a cell never says an investor opens what `accessNow` refuses**. The test holds every cell against `accessNow` and `grantOn`.
  - A closed cell names the first rule that shuts the investor out: the grant, its folders, the file's tier, its end date, then the NDA.
- **Scoring report shared with** is read-only, from the room's `report_shares`. It reads Unreadable when the shares could not be read.

### 2. Share (DR7) and one investor (DR7i, DR7r)

- **Share picks one of this venture's prospects in Capital relationships (#2457), and nothing else.**
  - **The candidates** come from `GET …/share-candidates`, the owner's only (403 `owner_only`), and only this venture's `raise_prospects` rows.
  - **What cannot be picked:** a prospect whose address already holds a grant in force says "already has access", and one with no email says so. Neither can be picked.
  - **What is never said:** whether an address has a StudioOS account, as `POST /grants` never says it.
- **The address never comes from the page.**
  - **What is sent:** `POST /grants { prospect: <uid>, expires_at, folders }`. The Worker reads the email from `raise_prospects WHERE uid = ? AND project_id = ?`.
  - **What is refused:**
    - another venture's prospect is 404;
    - one with no email is 400 `no_email`, with a sentence.
  - **The email form stays:** `email` still works for today's files tool.
- **The dialog says what the grant opens before it is sent:** "They will be able to open N files. M more need the NDA.", from `grantOn` on the grant it would write.
  - The end date is a day, today or later.
  - "Choose folders" with none chosen is refused here, because the Worker would refuse an empty list.
- **One investor's lightbox** shows:
  - the grant, its folders and committed state;
  - the NDA, with Send the NDA when none is sent;
  - what they open now, and how many more open once the NDA is signed;
  - their events among the room's last fifty;
  - Change and Revoke access.
- **Change** is D1103's PATCH. It says how many files they open after the change, against now.
- **Revoke** says "They lose the room now. Their past events stay in Activity." before the DELETE.

### 3. The NDA (DR8)

- **Send the NDA** is D1104's `POST …/grants/:uid/nda`, offered only when the NDA is Not sent.
  - **The dialog** names the three signers and how many NDA files the NDA would unlock for them.
  - **After it is sent:** the founder's own signing link is the only one returned, and the page offers it as "Sign your part".
  - **The refusals** (`nda_active`, `nda_pending`, `template_fields_unfilled`, `envelope_creation_failed`, `founder_only`) are said inside the dialog.
- **The founder read now carries `nda_pending`.** It is true while a pairwise NDA for the pair is pending, so a sent NDA reads "Waiting for signatures" and is never offered twice.
- **The NDA strip** shows each grant in force with its state, then "What the NDA unlocks · named for you only": the room's NDA files, by name, for the founder.

### 4. Requests (DR9, DR9d)

- **Access requests** come from D1104's queue: open first, each showing where it came from (Round call · <call>, or Deal page), the date and the investor's words.
  - A decided request says who decided and when. A backfilled answer says "who and when were not recorded" and never invents a decider.
  - **Share the room** opens the end date and folders, then calls `acceptRequest`.
  - **Decline** needs a note, because D1104's Worker requires a reason (1 to 500 characters). The canvas drew the note as optional; the Worker's rule stands, and the dialog says the note is sent to them.
  - Only the owner answers. A co-founder reads the queue and is told so.
- **Diligence requests** are the venture's `dd_requests`, read through `GET …/requests`'s new `diligence`.
  - **What is read:** the founder-visible columns only (migration 167's invariant) and the room's files matched to each request.
  - **Its state:** a request a file answers reads Answered; Reviewed is the diligence team's.
  - **Answer with a file** is D1106's file PATCH with `dd_request_id`. A co-founder may do this (D1103).
  - **A failure is its own block:** `diligence: null` with `diligence_unreadable`, and the access requests still answer.

### 5. Who sees what (DR16–DR18)

- **The owner** sees everything above.
- **A co-founder** (DR16b) reads Access with "Only the venture's owner shares the room…". There is no Share, Revoke, Change, Send the NDA or access-request answer for them, and they still answer diligence with a file.
- **An admin** (DR17) still gets D1102's summary on every tab: counts and grants, no file names, and Open for support with a reason.
- **A refusal** (DR18) is the canvas's board for who the caller is: an investor goes to their deals, a partner or advisor to their home, an Explorer to set up their profile. Build's guard keeps most of them off `/raise` already.

**Every change opens an in-page dialog that says what it changes before it is sent** (`DataRoomDialog.jsx`): Share, Change, Revoke, Send the NDA, Share the room, Decline and Answer with a file. There is never a browser pop-up.

### 6. Not built here, and why

- **"Send a reminder" for a pending NDA (DR7i, DR8):** no route resends an envelope, as D1104 §5 leaves for the owner.
- **"Advisors with a brief" (DR6):** the founder read carries no advisor grants. They are managed in each advisor's client brief.
- **Typing an address in Share:** the issue asks Share to pick from Capital relationships, so an address not in it is added there first. The canvas's "No StudioOS account uses this address" would say who has an account, which `POST /grants` never says.
- **The canvas's "Sort: Last event" control:** the list keeps the read's order, newest grant first.
