## D4817 part 6 — PF-06 Company workspace: one portfolio company's Overview, Financials, Reports, Support and Activity (#4817)

Part of #5311 (PF-06): the company workspace of the Portfolio v2 canvas (`design/incoming/Portfolio v2 Page.dc.html`, boards PF-06a to PF-06e), with the approved addendum's company specimens (`Portfolio v2 · Addendum.dc.html`: PFA01, PFA02, PFA09, PFA11, PFA16). Drawn in the frame PF-01 built (D4817 part 1). Frontend only, against today's Worker reads. No Worker or schema change; three frontend routes are added.

**What is built.** `frontend/src/pages/investor/PortfolioCompanyPage.jsx` is still the page at `/portfolio/companies/:uid` (admin and investor, as before). It now draws in `PortfolioFrame`:
- **The frame** gained four optional props, so a board below Portfolio can say where it is: `title` (the company's name as the heading), `crumb` (Account › Portfolio › the name, with Portfolio a link back), `nav` (the company's own tabs in place of Portfolio's) and `docTitle`. Every other board passes none of them and is unchanged.
- **The header:** the name; the sector line (stage and your holding, as before); the holding route and recorded ownership as the frame's context (addendum PFA01, PFA11); the company's health (`HealthStatus`); and a refresh.
- **Six key facts** (canvas PF-06): invested, ownership, latest FMV, multiple, latest report and open support. Each is a `FinancialMetric`. A failed source shows Unreadable with a Retry for that source alone.
- **Five tabs**, each its own address, as links with `aria-current` (addendum PFA16):
  - **Overview** (`/portfolio/companies/:uid`): why the company reads as it does, with dated evidence from health, the mark and reporting; next actions, each a door to the tab that holds it; and the company-reported performance. That last is the old Figures (`FiguresTab`, RPT10), unchanged, kept separate from the investment's own figures (PFA01).
  - **Financials** (`…/financials`, new). From `GET /api/positions/:uid`: investment history with its total; return (multiple, distributions, IRR, marks); valuation marks with event, basis and source; distributions. Then **Ownership & rights** (PFA02): the old Terms (`TermsCard`) and the read-only cap table (`InvestorCapTablePanel`).
  - **Reports** (`…/reports`): the holding route line, then the timeline (`Timeline`) and the updates received. The timeline's Read, Ask and Waive are unchanged.
  - **Support** (`…/support`, new): this company's entries on the support ledger, their counts from the ledger's own rollup, and each entry's recorded history on request (`/api/portfolio-support/:uid/history`). "Record support…" goes to Value-add, where the write lives.
  - **Activity** (`…/activity`, new): each source's own history, side by side: financial, reports, support. A two-state switch per source (`aria-pressed`) shows one or all.
- **The old `…/terms` address** is replaced by `…/financials#ownership-rights`, keeping the query. This is the addendum's handoff table, and every link to `/terms` still lands.
- **The document title** is `<name> · Portfolio · Axal VC` on Overview and `<name> · <Tab> · Portfolio · Axal VC` on the others. It is `Company · Portfolio · Axal VC` while the identity is read, and for a company that is not held or could not be read. The name is drawn only after an authorised read has named it (a position, or a duty owed to you).
- **The model** is new and pure: `frontend/src/pages/portfolio/companyModel.js`. It imports from `portfolioModel.js` and adds nothing to it. The tabs' components live in `CompanyPanels.jsx` (Financials, Support, Activity). The styles are in `company.css`, on the frame's tokens, which `.dark` redefines.

**The reads, each on its own** (`usePortfolioReads`):
- `GET /api/positions`: the position;
- `GET /api/reporting/companies/:uid`: duties, periods and the holding route. A 404 means it owes you nothing;
- `GET /api/portfolio/health/:uid`;
- `GET /api/portfolio-support`.

Financials and Activity each read `GET /api/positions/:uid` on their own. Reports reads the updates, as before. The Overview reads no round or mark history.

**Who the company is** (addendum PFA09), in four states:
- **Held:** a position or a duty names it.
- **Not held:** both reads answered and neither names it. The page says so and links back to Portfolio.
- **Unreadable:** a read that could name it failed and the other does not. The page says which read failed and offers its Retry, never "not held".
- **Loading:** a read that decides it has not answered.

**How each figure reads.**
- No mark is "Not recorded · carried at cost for NAV", never the cost written as a value.
- No ownership, no support row and no report owed each say Not recorded with the reason.
- A distributions read that answered with none is a measured $0 ("None recorded").
- The per-company IRR reads Not recorded: the platform computes no per-company IRR.
- A failed source reads Unreadable with its own Retry. The page names it in one partial-failure line under the tabs.

**The holding route (#5311 asked).** The addendum is approved (owner, 2026-11-13) and draws the holding route on the company's Overview, Financials and Reports (PFA01, PFA03, PFA10, PFA11). So the build shows it:
- in the frame's context on every tab: "Holding route: Direct · 6.2% ownership as recorded";
- above the Reports timeline.

The route is what the company's reporting duties record (`routeOf`). With no duty it reads Not recorded, and with a failed reporting read, Unreadable. `HoldingNote` (RPT13) is unchanged.

**Departures from the canvas, and why.**
- **No Stale chip on the latest FMV.** The canvas has "as of 31 Mar 2026 · stale". Mark age needs a Worker date to measure against (PF-02 uses `kpi-compliance`'s `as_of`). This page reads no reporting-population read, and the browser clock is not used. The date is shown; the age is not.
  - **Superseded by D6226 (#6226):** `GET /api/positions` now carries the Worker's own `today`, so the latest FMV draws its Stale chip ("Stale · 7 months old") measured at that date. A positions read without `today` still shows the date and no age.
- **"Chase report…" and "Record ▾" are not in the header.** Ask lives on the Reports timeline, unchanged here. A later part rebuilds Ask and Waive to the addendum. Recording marks and support stays in its authorised workflow and on Value-add.
- **"Ask HQ for a new mark"** is backend work (request-a-mark), so Next actions says it is not available yet.
- **Columns the reads do not carry:**
  - Investment history has no Source column; the rounds read carries no document.
  - Valuation marks have no Recorded by; the marks read carries no recorder.
  - Support has no Owner, due date or linked meeting.
  - Each table says so in its foot, rather than drawing an empty column.
- **Support's "Overdue":** the ledger records only Promised, Delivered and Withdrawn, with no due date (D4642). So nothing reads overdue, and the Support tab says which fields are not recorded.
- **Activity is per source, not one timeline.** D4817 keeps the joined company timeline as backend work ("do not fabricate a joined feed"). Activity lists each source's own history and says the joined one is not recorded. Chases are not in these reads.
- **A financial action history** (drafts, edits, who changed what) is backend work (#4642, #4647), and Financials says it is not recorded.
- **The support entries shown** are this company's rows on the ledger page the Worker returns. The ledger is paged across the whole book. When the company's rollup counts more entries than the page holds, the tab says how many are on later pages, in Value-add.
- **Recent activity on the Overview** is a door to Activity, not three joined lines. Joining them would be the feed D4817 withholds.

**Not drawn, because no read supplies it.** D4817's "Backend required" items stay with #4642, #4644, #4645 and #4647:
- a per-company IRR;
- a mark's recorder;
- a round's source document;
- support owners, due dates, In progress and Cancelled, and meeting outcomes;
- request-a-mark;
- the joined timeline.

Each reads as absent here, with its reason; none is estimated.
