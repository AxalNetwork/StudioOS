### What the walkthrough found

- At 390 × 844 on signed-in HQ Contracts the document was 390 px wide, and the account button spanned x=312–417, so its last 27 px were outside the viewport. The workspace menu and notifications competed for the same row.
- `App.jsx`'s header (now L1469) is one `h-14` row with no wrap and no overflow menu. Below 640 px only Email and Refer & Earn hide; the HQ or "Investor View" chip, the Wellness heart (founder and admin), the bell and `UserDropdown` stay. The account button shows the name (up to 160 px) and a chevron; ☰ is 30 px and the bell and heart 34 px.
- `UserDropdown` (L852) closes on Escape but does not return focus and has no arrow keys. The drawer in `SidebarNav` has no Escape handler and does not return focus to ☰.
- The founder band already has 44 px targets, a modal sheet, Escape and focus return. Computed from its classes, not measured, its centred company pill overlaps the Studio target by about 27 px at 390 and 71 px at 320.
- `NotificationBell`'s `refreshCount` keeps 0 when the read fails, so an unread count that could not be read looks like none.

### What the canvas decides

- Below 1024 px the row has four jobs, each in one place: workspace (☰), shell shortcuts (**⋯ Shortcuts**), notifications (the bell) and account (initials). Every control is a 44 px target; the row budget leaves 70 px free at 320, 52 at 360 and 82 at 390 (AMN10, AMN15, AMN20).
- The name, email, role and release tag move into the account menu, where a long name wraps. Mode and preview indicators stay in the admin bar, and page actions stay on the page (AMN12, AMN21).
- The route at 320 on HQ Contracts: closed with target boundaries and keyboard order, the workspace menu with HQ selected and Contracts current, the account menu, Shortcuts, and dismissal with focus back on the trigger (AMN10 to AMN14; AMN22 at 390).
- Desktop keeps main's row with initials and the full accessible name, and the dropdown gains its header and keyboard focus (AMN30, AMN31).
- Viewing a role and acting as a person stay distinct: Preview shell as Investor says the account menu holds the operator's own settings, and a support session shows an "acting as" line (AMN40 to AMN43).
- Six roles (Super Admin, Admin, Founder, Investor, Advisor, Operating partner) at 320 and 390 with each role's account rows, and the founder band with the pill fixed (AMN50 to AMN52).
- States: account loading, no display name, 99+, unread count unavailable, beta, branch suspended, focus and open (AMN60); dark (AMN70, AMN71); accessibility (AMN80); handoff (AMNH). AMN00 is the overview and AMN01 main today.
