- **Status:** decided by S1, 2026-10-24 (#2371), and built in draft PR #2706.
- **Canvas:** the owner's "Programs", boards PG1–PG20 in 40 frames (artifacts `QWsj2LjALVRBd1rsTg8bW3`, `8GEfQTVW1ebBpZsWbG8CG6`). It is committed as `design/incoming/Programs Page.dc.html` and `Programs · Boards.dc.html`.

- **Map:** `documentation/audits/PROGRAMS_MAP_2026-10-24.md`, the boards read against the code.
- **Migration:** none for the integration.
- **Why:** the owner asked for one home for every program the studio runs, a page per program that says how it runs and holds the tools to run it, and a way for a studio to propose one. The canvas draws a catalogue, a page per program, a proposal flow and HQ's decisions, across 40 frames. Most of its figures need a program registry, reads or columns the Worker does not have. Some of what today's landing shows is also wrong:
  - a card says Advisor cohort access is "Also an Approvals lane", and the Worker says it is not;
  - two cards open one console, and one of them counts nothing;
  - a failed glance shows on cards that read nothing;
  - Referral review says "Nothing in this queue." under its own error, and prints a submission's id as its referrer.

  This decision is the integration: the addresses, the shell, and the blocks whose reads exist, each drawn truthfully. The rest is twelve slot tasks, listed in §6.

### 2. What is read, and from where

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/admin/studio/glance` (R1): the HQ lanes `cohort` (`cohort_applicants` pending) and `referrals` (submitted, under review, more info needed), each with `count`, `oldest_age_hours` and `sla`. A failed lane is `count: null`. | Waiting for you; the Lab and Refer & Earn cards |

| `GET /api/advisors/admin/cohort-assignments/assignable` (R6): users with the Advisor role, at most 500 | People's Assign |
| `GET /api/admin/cohort/timeline` (R7): the 12 newest cycles, with `status`, `app_status`, `start_at`, `end_at` and the week windows | coverage, the no-mentor row, the cycle strip, People's cohort names |
| `GET /api/refer-earn/admin/submissions?status=&limit=200` (R8): one status per call, newest first, at most 200, no total. It serves the referrer's name and email, not their id. | Refer & Earn's Overview (one call per open status) and Queue |
| `GET /api/learning/programs/mine` (R9): each program's status, active enrolments and contents, at most 500. An Admin gets the programs assigned to them; a Super Admin gets every one. | the Learning card, Overview and programs |
| `GET /api/learning/programs/:uid/learners` (R10): one program's active learners, by name and uid, never an email. It answers 403 `not_assigned` outside an Admin's programs. | Learning's People |
| `GET /api/admin/testing/programmes` (R11): Super Admin only | Product testing's figures, for a Super Admin |
| `GET /api/admin/insights/scopes` (R12): Super Admin on HQ only; 403 `hq_only` otherwise | the scope chip, for a Super Admin |

The writes are Assign and End access, the referral review and the perk review. Their clients all exist, so the drift test needs no change.

- **Every one of these reads is truthful:** a D1 error is a 5xx, or `count: null` on an R1 lane.
- **Guard H — reads that hide a failure are not drawn.** `GET /api/admin/spinout-applications` and `GET /api/admin/spinout-participants` answer `[]` with 200 on any error. Their figures are never drawn as a count, and never as 0. Lab applications read "Not read here yet" until part 7 makes those reads truthful.
- **A failed read says so.** Each block draws `Unreadable` with Try again, and the rest of the page stands. A failed read marks only the blocks it feeds. Today's landing pins a glance failure on every card; this page does not.
- **Guard L, as D923's — lists that stop without saying so.** A list that returns exactly its cap is incomplete.
  - The caps are R2 500 items (its `counts` are uncapped), R3 200, R4 200, R5 500, R6 500, each R8 call 200, and R9 500.
  - A figure from a list at its cap says what it covers: "200+", "within the newest 500". Below the cap, counts are exact.
  - R8 is newest first, so a full call drops the oldest rows. That is why Refer & Earn counts each status on its own.
- **Waiting for you** is the labelled total of the four admin queues that can be read:
  - cohort applications (R1 `cohort`);
  - Refer & Earn reviews (R1 `referrals`), which include referrals waiting on the member;
  - office-hours hosts (R2 `counts.pending`);
  - perk listings (R3's `in_review` rows; a draft is not reviewer backlog).

  It reads "Could not be read" when any of the four cannot be read, because a total that misses a queue is smaller than the truth. It reads "at least [n]" when R3 is at its cap. Strategic referral access has no admin read, and the tile says "Strategic referral access is not read here yet."
- **Ages and bands (S-AGE, S1-BANDS).** The bands are under 24 hours, 24 to 72 hours, and 72 hours or more. Overdue starts at 72 hours. These are `slaWindow.ts` and `approvalSources.ts`'s `slaFor`, which the Approvals lanes and #2390 share. The canvas's two and seven days yield to them, as in D915.
  - A referral waits from its submission ("since submitted"). The open statuses are the lane's: `submitted`, `under_review` and `more_info_needed`, so the page and the Approvals lane agree. `more_info_needed` is shown within that count as "waiting on the member".
  - A perk listing waits from `updated_at`, "since last edit", everywhere. A queued listing's `updated_at` is its creation or its partner's last edit, because every review moves a row out of `in_review`.
  - Cohort applications and hosts use their lane's or item's stamp.
- **Mentor counts, one rule everywhere** (card, Overview and Needs you):
  - **Active mentors (S-CNT)** counts distinct people: R5's `advisor_user_id` on active rows whose current role, lower-cased, is `advisor`. A demoted holder already reads nothing, so they are not counted. A deactivated account still counts until part 7 serves account state, and a visible line says "Deactivated accounts are not left out yet."
  - **A cohort that needs a mentor (S-COV)** is a cycle that is scheduled or running and not postponed: R7's `status` is `scheduled` or `active`, and its `app_status` is not `postponed`. A postponed cycle still turns `active` at its first unlock, so it is left out by name. Scheduled cycles count because PG5 asks for a mentor before a cohort starts. "Covered" means at least one active row held by a current Advisor.
- **Learning counts enrolments, not people.** R9's per-program `learners` are summed and labelled "active enrolments". A person in two programs is two enrolments, and the label says so.
- **Dates.** D1's zone-less stamps are parsed as UTC, with D923's shared `parseStamp`. A cohort deadline is 00:00 ET and inclusive, so it reads "[8 Nov] 00:00 ET", never "[7 Nov] 23:59".
- **The words are D915's**, and every placeholder shows its reason as a visible line, never only in `title=`:
  - **Not recorded:** no store holds it.
  - **Not read here yet:** it is stored, but no read this page may call serves or sums it.
  - **Could not be read:** the read failed.

  `Unrecorded` keeps its reason in `title=` only, so the page draws the reason beside it as a line.
- **Credits.** No referral pays perk credits. A referred member's sign-up earns the referrer one introduction credit, and it is named as such. Perk credits and introduction credits are never summed together.
- **Money (O-MONEY).** Every commission and cross-member credit read is Super Admin only. Until O-MONEY is answered, such a figure reads "Not read here yet" for an Admin, with "HQ reads these figures."
