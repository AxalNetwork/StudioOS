- **Status:** decided by S1, 2026-10-24 (#2371). The decision merged with #2706 and its §6 follow-ups with #2736; the integration is built in draft PR #2784.
- **Canvas:** the owner's "Programs", boards PG1–PG20 in 40 frames (artifacts `QWsj2LjALVRBd1rsTg8bW3`, `8GEfQTVW1ebBpZsWbG8CG6`). It is committed as `design/incoming/Programs Page.dc.html` and `Programs · Boards.dc.html`.

  - a failed glance shows on cards that read nothing;

The writes are Assign and End access, the referral review and the perk review. Their clients all exist, so the drift test needs no change.

- **Every one of these reads is truthful, with one exception, R12.** For the others, a D1 error is a 5xx, or `count: null` on an R1 lane.
  - R12 does not fail with a 5xx when the deployment registry cannot be read. It answers 200 with HQ-held alone and a `reason` (`insightsScope.ts:120-128`, `admin_insights_scopes.ts:36`).
  - The page reads that `reason`. When it is present, the chip's `title=` gives the reason in place of `SCOPE_TITLE`, because the read has not established that no subsidiary is deployed.
- **Guard H — reads that hide a failure are not drawn.** `GET /api/admin/spinout-applications` and `GET /api/admin/spinout-participants` answer `[]` with 200 on any error. Their figures are never drawn as a count, and never as 0. Lab applications read "Not read here yet" until part 7 makes those reads truthful; part 8 then draws the figure.
- **A failed read says so.** Each block draws `Unreadable` with Try again, and the rest of the page stands. A failed read marks only the blocks it feeds. Today's landing pins a glance failure on every card; this page does not.
- **Guard L, as D923's — lists that stop without saying so.** A list that returns exactly its cap is incomplete.
  - The caps are R2 500 items (its `counts` are uncapped), R3 200, R4 200, R5 500, R6 500, each R8 call 200, and R9 500.
  - A figure from a list at its cap says what it covers: "200+", "within the newest 500". Below the cap, counts are exact. R3 is the exception: its cap counts drafts too, so its `in_review` figures read "at least [n]".
  - R8 is newest first, so a full call drops the oldest rows. That is why Refer & Earn counts each status on its own, and takes its oldest from R1.
- **Waiting for you** is the labelled total of the four admin queues that can be read:
  - cohort applications (R1 `cohort`);
  - Refer & Earn reviews (R1 `referrals`), which include referrals waiting on the member;
  - office-hours hosts (R2 `counts.pending`);
  - perk listings (R3's `in_review` rows; a draft is not reviewer backlog).

  It reads "Could not be read" when any of the four cannot be read, because a total that misses a queue is smaller than the truth. It reads "at least [n]" when R3 is at its cap. Strategic referral access has no admin read, and the tile says "Strategic referral access is not read here yet."
- **Ages and bands (S-AGE, S1-BANDS).** The bands are under 24 hours, 24 to 72 hours, and 72 hours or more. Overdue starts at 72 hours. These are `slaWindow.ts` and `approvalSources.ts`'s `slaFor`, which the Approvals lanes and #2390 share. The canvas's two and seven days yield to them, as in D915.
  - A referral waits from its submission ("since submitted"). The open statuses are the lane's: `submitted`, `under_review` and `more_info_needed`, so the page and the Approvals lane agree. `more_info_needed` is shown within that count as "waiting on the member".
  - A perk listing waits from `updated_at`, "since last edit", everywhere. A queued listing's `updated_at` is its creation or its partner's last edit, because every review moves a row out of `in_review`.
  - Cohort applications and hosts use their lane's or item's stamp.
- **Mentor counts, one rule everywhere** (card, Overview and Needs you):
  - **Active mentors (S-CNT)** counts distinct people: R5's `advisor_user_id` on active rows whose current role, lower-cased, is `advisor`. A demoted holder already reads nothing, so they are not counted. A deactivated account still counts until part 7 serves account state, and a visible line says "Deactivated accounts are not left out yet."
  - **A cohort that needs a mentor (S-COV)** is a cycle that is scheduled or running and not postponed: R7's `status` is `scheduled` or `active`, and its `app_status` is not `postponed`. A postponed cycle still turns `active` at its first unlock (`cohortTiming.ts`'s Week-1 unlock does not read `app_status`), so it is left out by name. Scheduled cycles count because PG5 asks for a mentor before a cohort starts. "Covered" means at least one active row held by a current Advisor.
    - **The denominator [b]** is every cycle among R7's twelve newest that meets that rule. R7 is newest by month, so a future cycle is always among them.
    - **[b] includes cohorts nobody has staffed yet.** The Worker keeps the current month's cycle and the next two months' in being: each cohort-applications tick calls `ensureCycleWithWindow` for all three (`cohortApplications.ts`), and a new cycle starts `scheduled`. So next month's and the following month's cohorts count from the day they are created, and each reads uncovered until a mentor is assigned to it.
    - **A scheduled cycle with no week windows yet still counts.** S-COV reads only `status` and `app_status`; only the cycle strip reads the windows.
- **Learning counts enrolments, not people, in published programs only.** R9 returns draft and archived programs too, and a draft or archived program can still hold active enrolments.
  - So the card's and Overview's program count and enrolment sum both take only R9's rows whose `status` is `published`. The sum of those rows' `learners` is labelled "active enrolments".
  - A person in two programs is two enrolments, and the label says so.
  - The Overview table still lists every row with its own status and its own count.
- **Dates.** D1's zone-less stamps are parsed as UTC, with D923's shared `parseStamp`. A cohort deadline is 00:00 ET and inclusive, so it reads "[8 Nov] 00:00 ET", never "[7 Nov] 23:59".
- **The words are D915's**, and every placeholder shows its reason as a visible line, never only in `title=`:
  - **Not recorded:** no store holds it.
  - **Not read here yet:** it is stored, but no read this page may call serves or sums it.
  - **Could not be read:** the read failed.

  `Unrecorded` keeps its reason in `title=` only, so the page draws the reason beside it as a line.
- **Credits.** No referral pays perk credits. A referred member's sign-up earns the referrer one introduction credit, and it is named as such. Perk credits and introduction credits are never summed together.
- **Money (O-MONEY).** Every commission and cross-member credit read is Super Admin only. Until O-MONEY is answered, such a figure reads "Not read here yet" for an Admin, with "HQ reads these figures."
