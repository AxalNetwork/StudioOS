## D8912 — A company administrator's read of their own company's skill analytics (#8912)

Parent: #4536 · tracker #4528

- **Status:** built. Worker only: no migration, no new table, no client method, no screen.
- **Code:** `routes/team_skill_analytics.ts` (`GET /api/teams/skills/analytics`, self-mounting ahead of `agent_engine.ts`), `services/agents/skillCoverage.ts` and `services/agents/skillExecutions.ts` (a `subject` on the existing measures, and the company reads' SQL beside the HQ reads'), `routes/admin_hq_skills.ts` (reads the shared HQ coverage and pin SQL; its output is unchanged).
- **Dictionary:** `documentation/architecture/SKILL_ANALYTICS_METRICS.md`, § Where it is served, § Execution and usage and § Filters.
- **Test:** `cloudflare-worker/test/company_skill_analytics_8912.test.ts`.

### What was found, on main

- Only the Super Admin read (`GET /api/admin/ai-agents/skills`) served analytics. It counts a company's skills by scope and never names one (D4161). D4536.md, D4536-p4.md and the dictionary listed the company read as not built, behind `canEditAsMember`.
- The measures (`catalogueCoverage`, `adoptionOf`, `matchesSkill`, `skillExecutions` and the outcome rates) detailed HQ skills by a literal `'hq'`. The company read needed the same definitions over another population, not new ones.
- The published history (`agent_skill_version_evals`) is written by `evaluatePublished`, which reads published HQ skills only. A company skill has no golden-case requirement on publish, and release evidence for company versions follows #4533.

### What is decided

1. **The gate is `canEditAsMember`** (`services/companyAccess.ts`): an Owner, Admin or Founder seat, or the primary admin, in the named company. It is the gate that already decides who writes a company's skills. The query's `company=<uid>` only says which company is meant; the seat is read from `user_company_links`.
   - No seat, another company or an unknown uid: 404 `not_found`. Another company's existence is never confirmed.
   - A seat without editor rights: 403 `not_company_admin`.
   - A platform admin with no seat is not let in. HQ reads its own route.
   - A missing or repeated `company`: 400 `company_required`.
2. **One definition, a subject.** Each measure takes `subject: 'hq' | 'company'` (default `hq`), which says whose skills it details. The HQ read passes nothing and is unchanged. In the company read, a row of any other scope is dropped, never counted.
3. **Narrowed in SQL.** `COMPANY_COVERAGE_SQL`, `COMPANY_LINKS_SQL`, `COMPANY_EXECUTIONS_SQL` and `COMPANY_OUTCOMES_SQL` share their text with the HQ reads (`HQ_COVERAGE_SQL`, `HQ_LINKS_SQL`, `EXECUTIONS_SQL`, `OUTCOMES_SQL`) and add `s.company_id = ?`. The runs are narrowed inside `runs`, before any per-run subquery. No HQ skill, no HQ draft and no other company's uid, slug or count leaves SQL. The latency read takes the company's own skill ids.
4. **Same window, filters, charges and availability.** The 30-day default window, the validated filters (`scope` takes `company`), the integer micro-USD charge with `runs_unrecorded` and `runs_incomplete`, and `{ available: false }` where a measure has no row, as on the HQ read. A company with no skills reads observed zeros for its counts.
5. **What the company read does not serve.**
   - `published_evaluation` is `{ available: false, reason }`: no evaluation runs on a company version (see above). It is never served as zeros.
   - `coverage.sources` (Axal's library files), `coverage.drafts.without_current_evaluation` (HQ release evidence) and `coverage.overlap` (Axal's published skills) are `{ available: false }`.
   - `executions.workflows` stays unavailable until #4535.
   - The paged skill list: the company's library read (`GET /api/teams/skills?company=`) already lists its skills. `filters` carries no `skills` entry.
6. **No ledger.** The read writes nothing: no analytics table, no audit row. #3851's proposal board is not read.
7. **No client method yet.** No screen draws the read, so none is added. The page that draws it adds one under `frontend/src/lib/api/`; the route is mounted, so the API drift check will accept it.
