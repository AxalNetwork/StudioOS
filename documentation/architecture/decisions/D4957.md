### What the canvas decides

- Home puts search directly under the title, then Popular (views, last 7 days), Your tickets, the areas with real guide counts, and Still stuck? with the status line.
- Signed out, Home shows the six public guides with Account recovery first, and the signed-out ways to get help.
- Search says "First 25 results" with refine, area chips and arrow keys. Nothing found offers broader terms, the areas and a ticket that carries the search.
- Each guide keeps one page at `/help/<section>/<guide>`, with Go to as the one primary action and its owner. Every guide reads "Verification pending" until a first verified date exists.
- After "Did this answer it? No", the page offers next steps.
- The ticket sheet asks first whether it is an ordinary ticket or a security concern, then uses the same endpoints as today.
- A directory lists every guide, with a role filter that dims guides for other roles and hides none.
- Admin review of answers and content shows no names.
