# D6013 — Demand signal terms and confirmed value

The Worker accepts a named price on a Would pay signal. It records willingness to pay and stays out of committed
value. Confirmed letters of intent and pre-sales count toward committed value,
alongside verified deposits and pilots in the revenue ledger. Totals keep
currencies and billing intervals separate, including an unrecorded interval.

A successful read can show a count of zero. A failed read supplies no counts
or totals. The waitlist chart still requires enough observed weeks.
