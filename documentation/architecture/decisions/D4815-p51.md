### The table

| Column | From the read | When there is none |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | the eleven in SCORING's order | no Overall row, no total |
| Score | the shared score: the band and confidence the read sent (Risk in its risk words) | the state's own word and mark |

### The five states, each its own

| State | Score | Value | Expands into |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scored | Fair · Medium | 62 | its inputs, grades, dates and points |
| Not enough recorded | Not enough recorded (dashed) | — | what is missing and why |
| Not expected yet | Not expected yet (dotted) | — | the reason |
| Locked (before the NDA) | the band and confidence | Locked (the shared score's lock) | "open after you sign the NDA", with Sign NDA |
| Unreadable | Unreadable (the shared score's mark) and "could not be read. The other scopes are current." | — | does not expand; Try again reads the scorecard again |

**Two more states on this tab:**
- A scope never scored reads "Not scored yet", as before.
- A scorecard the founder has not shared, a venture not in the deal flow, and a failed read stay the panel's own whole-card states (D1257).

### Each scope expands in place

**The row is a native button.** It carries `aria-expanded` and an `aria-controls` that names the region under it. Click, Enter and Space all toggle it.

**What the region holds:**
- the scope's evidence: the review in force, the inputs (PMF in its four groups), Risk's ten layers, and the line that a personal input is counted and never shown;
- Open full detail, which is the drawer of D1257, with the model and the computed time;
- for Team, Market and Traction, a link to that view on the Evidence tab.

**More than one scope can be open at once.** Before the NDA a row expands into the sentence that its inputs open after signing, and no drawer is offered.

**Team, Market and Traction's context is reachable.** A line under the table links all three, and each of those scopes links its own view when it is open. Nothing on the Evidence tab is removed.

### What no read carries

- **Change** ("▲4"). The investor's scorecard read sends no change, and the score history (`trajectory`) is the founder's and staff's. #6173 adds a per-scope change against the investor's last visit. Until then the column reads Not recorded, and its hover says why.
- **The evidence sentence DE05 draws under a row** ("2 founder CVs (verified) · 3 reference calls · advisor note"). The read carries inputs with grades, not a summary, so the expansion lists the inputs themselves.
