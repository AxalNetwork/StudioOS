### What is built

Given a deal, the Syndicates list on the investor's Investment tab reads two lists: the syndicates the investor leads or belongs to (`?project_id=`, as before) and the open ones that their invitation to that exact deal shows (`?deal_id=`). A row from the second list that the investor neither leads nor belongs to reads "Lead · members · Minimum · You are not in this syndicate". It never shows a committed total, because the count-only read does not carry one. A failed second read says so with Try again under the first list. It is never drawn as "no syndicates".

Open on such a row opens the DL13 lightbox, which is a bottom sheet on a phone. It shows the status, Lead, Target, Minimum and Members ("Counted, not named"), then "You are not in this syndicate." It offers Close and Join this syndicate. No member read is made. Join this syndicate turns the same dialog into a second step. That step says what a pledge records: it moves no money, it does not mark the syndicate funded, the lead sees the name and amount, and other members see only the count. The step takes the pledge in dollars. The join call is made only from that step's button, with the deal's ID, so the Worker binds it to that invitation. An amount below the minimum is stopped before anything is sent. Each Worker refusal (403 identity check, 404 not open to you on this deal, 409 already in or no longer open, 400 minimum, not open or own syndicate) is drawn as a sentence that says nothing was recorded. After Done, both lists are read again.

The drawer for a syndicate the investor is in follows the Worker's `show_member_names`. A member reads "N members. Others are counted, not named; only the lead sees members by name.", and the lead keeps the named roster and Close the syndicate.

The legacy deal room (`DealRoomPage`) passes no deal and is unchanged.

### Departures from DL13

- **Committed bar for a non-member:** not drawn. The count-only read carries no committed total, and part 1 keeps it that way.
- **"A member sees only their own line":** replaced with "Only the lead sees members by name. Members see the count." The detail read does not return a member's own pledge, so the member's view (DL13l) draws the count without "Your line" and its Signed pill.
- **The join step:** DL13 draws only the Join this syndicate button. The step after it uses the canvas's own dialog shape (title, sentence, Back and the action). Its wording is this build's.
- **Phone and dark:** DL13 has no 390 or dark frame. The lightbox is a bottom sheet below 640px with 44px targets, and every light class carries its `dark:` pair.
