Census establishments cover businesses with paid employees, not every business or an
automatically eligible customer population. BLS spending is per consumer unit with its
published uncertainty. SEC company revenue is not market size. These observation
importers use the shared writer, never the market-intelligence table or an index.
The BLS unemployment and EDGAR filings-count index connectors, ticker identity file,
and generic Eurostat macro source are distinct from the chosen commercial imports.

Each search candidate retains its original publisher, link, supporting passage and date.
The search service's answer is not a source; repeated articles about one estimate count
as one source. No search result automatically enters observations, an index or sizing.
The research run must show cost before running, and no key belongs in Git or an issue.

These tasks retain their implementation/design dependencies and #5719 backpressure.
The owner permits initial draft PRs while that alert is open; this decision does not
permit branch updates, workflow dispatch, a re-run or production activation.
