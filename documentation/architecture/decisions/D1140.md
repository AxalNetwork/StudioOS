### 1. The owner's choice: a new sub-address

`/company-settings` drew the whole company editor: Public profile, Growth details, Members and access, Workspace and lifecycle. It also carried a partner's Firm Settings and an advisor's Practice Settings. #3020 forwards the old address to the company page, and the lightbox there (D1139) is not that editor. The owner chose (2026-10-27, in the session that built this) to move the editor, not drop it:

- **`/account/companies/<uid>/settings`** draws `CompanySettingsPage`, unchanged, for the company active on this device. That page reads the active company. Another company's address says so and links to its page, where it can be made active. It is never drawn as the active company's settings.
- **`/account/companies/settings`** forwards to the active company's address, with the query and hash kept. With no company, it draws the page as before: a partner's Firm Settings, an advisor's Practice Settings, and the way to create or join a company.
- **Nothing is decided while the memberships are still being read:** the page renders nothing until then.
- **`settings` is reserved.** `companyUidFor` and `settingsUidFor` never read it as a company, and its two routes come before `/account/companies/:tab`.

### 2. `/company-settings` forwards (CMP19b)

- **Where it goes:** `/account/companies/<uid>`, the company page of the company active on this device. That is the saved `active_company_id` when it is still a membership, else the first membership, as the switcher restores it. With no company, it goes to `/account/companies`.
- **What it keeps:** the query and hash, plus `?moved=company-settings`. A stale `moved` is replaced, never doubled.
- **It reads the memberships itself**, because the switcher may not have restored the active company yet. A failed read still forwards, to the Companies tab, so an old link never strands anyone.
- **Who reaches it:** the route keeps its five-role guard.
- **The notice on arrival:** "Company Settings has a new address. Update your bookmark." (`useMovedNotice`). Dismiss drops `moved` and keeps the rest of the query.
- **Counted for #2319:** it is a path-only `REDIRECT_ROUTES` entry, and its route only forwards (`redirect_hits_2301`). It is not `keep`: no mail the Worker sends carries `/company-settings`, so its count decides when it goes.
- **`/partner/operations` and `/partner/operations/overview`** (D395) now go straight to `/account/companies/settings`.
