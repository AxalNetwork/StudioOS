### What the walkthrough found

- A Legal agent's Add skills drawer said "No other skill of this role yet" with Add disabled. `attachCheck` still refuses with `role_mismatch`, against D4116; `/available` keeps published skills and silently drops every refusal except `tool_not_approved`; and Legal holds 20 listed skills, none published. `SkillSheets.jsx` prints that sentence whenever the list is empty.
- The new-agent wizard reached Model and cap with zero suggestions. `SkillsStep` filters on the client by role and publication with no tool check, and `AddAgentSheet.jsx` treats that step as always ready.
- The Finance skill inspected was Draft with no assign action. `GET /skills` lists drafts to writers, so the Super Admin preview sees every Axal draft; `SkillsTab.jsx` offers Assign only on a published skill and shows "Not published yet." with no reason and no alternative. Its assign sheet says "Any agent can take it" while `/assignable` lists only agents of the skill's roles.
- Several descriptions in `services/agents/skills/*.md` end in a "when …" routing clause. The truncated roadmap and infrastructure text seen on 26 Nov is not in those files, so its source on main is not yet found.

### What the canvas decides

- One rule, read from the Worker, gives every skill one of six states: Listed, Published, Can attach, Attached, Blocked by policy or Couldn't be read, each with its own word, glyph and border. The checks run in `attachCheck`'s order without the role gate (TMS00).
- Natural fit sorts the list and adds a label but never removes a skill (D4116). The gates stay: a published version, Axal's golden-case bar and the sponsor's tool approval. Nothing attaches, publishes or grants by itself.
- Every surface shows counts and a recovery. The Finance directory reads 7 listed · 6 published · 1 draft of yours · 14 Axal drafts (count only) · 0 unreadable, with `/` search over job, inputs and output (TMS01).
- The skill page shows the job, required inputs, output, steps and every agent's state; a Blocked agent names the tool (`mercury.read`) with her sponsor and Settings as the recovery. Assign to agents uses the same states (TMS02, TMS17).
- A draft says why it cannot be attached (`version_not_published`, the golden-case bar) and makes the published alternative the primary action, with no Assign (TMS03).
- The existing agent's drawer lists every published skill she can take, natural fit first. A draft-only area says so and offers Finance as the way on, and picks survive filters and a trip to the details (TMS04 to TMS06).
- The wizard applies the same rule to an agent not yet added and lists the tools step 3 will ask for. When nothing is eligible it says so, with counts and a recovery, and Next reads "Next: model and cap, with no skills" (TMS07, TMS08).
- Cards show the job, Needs, You get and Run cost "Not recorded", with routing clauses removed from the job line (TMS15). States (TMS09), phone 390 for all four routes (TMS10 to TMS14) and the handoff (TMS16) are drawn.
