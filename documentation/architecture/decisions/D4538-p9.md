## D4538 part 9 — SK-20: the rebase sheet on Compare with Axal (#4538)

Part 5 (`D4538-p5.md`, #5965) built `POST /api/teams/skills/:uid/rebase` and `teamAgentsApi.rebaseSkill`. #5964 (`D4538-p7.md`) built Compare with Axal and the Extend dialog, but left "Rebase onto vN" drawn disabled ("Not built yet · #5965"). This part builds SK-20 of the approved D5963 canvas on that route, as S1 scoped it on 2026-11-25. No Worker, route or schema change is made.

### What this decides

1. **Where it opens.** On Compare with Axal, an editor (the card's `can_write`) sees "Rebase onto vN…" beside "Extend Axal vN again…".
   - It opens a right-side sheet, the shared `Sheet` that the Extend dialog uses. On a phone the sheet is full height.
   - The canvas draws SK-20 as a panel under a one-part close-up page. The sheet keeps the panel's content and order, and puts it where the page's other write already lives.
2. **Who sees what.**
   - Members and HQ admins see no Rebase, as before.
   - With no update, or a retired base, there is no Rebase.
   - With a suspended newest version, Rebase is disabled, with "Axal has paused vN, so it cannot be rebased onto right now."
   - A card with no `draft_hash` (or a malformed one) shows Rebase disabled with "Reload the page to rebase", because the route refuses without it.
3. **The choices.** Each conflicting part, as the Worker's `threeWay()` flags it, is a fieldset with three native radio inputs: Keep mine, Take Axal vN, Edit by hand.
   - Native radios give arrow keys within a part and Tab between parts.
   - Nothing is pre-chosen.
   - Edit by hand starts from the company's side as the comparison shows it:
     - Name is a text field.
     - Purpose is a text box.
     - Steps take one per line; blank lines are dropped.
     - Tools are checkboxes of Axal vN's tools only.
4. **Output, Skill tags and Roles are not written by hand here.** For these, Edit by hand is disabled with "Keep yours or take Axal's here; then change … in the new draft." The canvas draws Edit by hand only on Steps and Tools. These parts' values need the skill editor's pickers, and the new draft opens in that editor.
5. **The other parts' result lines** follow the Worker's merge rule (D4538 part 5):
   - changed only by Axal: "Taken from Axal vN";
   - changed by the company, including the same change made on both sides: "Kept from yours";
   - changed by neither: "Unchanged".
6. **Tools.**
   - The Tools part says the draft can use Axal vN's tools or fewer, and names the tools new in vN that only Take Axal or Edit by hand can carry.
   - Keep mine with a tool vN lacks shows a warning before sending.
   - The Worker still decides: its `tool_not_in_base` refusal appears on the Tools part.
7. **The request.** Create draft sends `{ version, expected_draft_hash, resolutions }`:
   - `version` is the exact newest version the page shows.
   - `expected_draft_hash` is the card's `draft_hash`.
   - `resolutions` has one entry per conflict; only `edit` carries a `value`.

   A conflict left without a choice is refused in place and nothing is sent.
8. **Refusals.** A refusal keeps every choice.
   - One the Worker ties to a `part` shows on that part.
   - `draft_conflict`, `rebase_conflict`, `no_base_update`, `base_unreadable` and `company_version_unreadable` say that nothing was saved, and offer "Reload the comparison". Reload reads the page again and closes the sheet, so the person chooses again on the new comparison. Nothing is retried for them.
   - The remaining refusals show their own words in the dialog.
9. **What it writes.** It writes only the rebase. On success the new draft's skill page opens in Team › Skills.
   - The sheet says that it creates a draft on Axal vN.
   - It says that the published version and the pinned agents stay as they are, and that nothing is published.
   - When the card's unpublished draft differs from what is published, it says the rebase replaces those draft edits.
   - No publish, attach or assign call is made.
