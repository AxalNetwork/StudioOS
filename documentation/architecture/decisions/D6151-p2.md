## D6151 part 2 — Screening and Compare on a phone, boards DE26 and DE29 (#6151)

Part 2 of #6151. Part 1 (`D6151.md`, PR #6893) built Deal rooms to DE19 to DE24. This part builds two phone boards of the same Deals v2 addendum canvas (`design/incoming/Deals v2 Deal Rooms and Phone Page.dc.html`, D6141):
- **DE26:** Screening at 390;
- **DE29:** Compare at 390, with two ventures and with four.

Each sits on the desktop redraw it extends: #5909 for Screening, #6493 (#5913) for Compare, both merged. No read, route or Worker change.

The IC phone board (DE27) waits for the desktop Commit page (#5910, PR #6876) and is part 3.

### Screening (DE26)

- **Cards on a phone, the table from 768px up.** Below 768px the decision table (960px wide, which scrolled sideways) gives way to one card per venture, from the same reads. Each card carries:
  - the venture and its sector and status;
  - the scorecard strip and its link;
  - Fit, Confidence and Material risks (Not recorded, as in the table);
  - the decision.
- **Move to diligence and Pass… run the width of the card, at 44px.**
  - Pass opens the same reason dialog (`PassDialog`).
  - An unread flow offers only Try again, never Pass, as in the table.
- **Separate test ids.** The card's ids carry `-card`, so the table's ids (which tests and the Playwright specs use) never match twice.
- **Pass reasons** stays the disclosure under the list, as on desktop.

### Compare (DE29)

- **On a phone, a stacked list in place of the chart and table.** Below 768px, "Scores by scope" keeps its text summary. The bar chart and the table give way to:
  - a sticky legend of the ventures in their colours;
  - each section of `compareSections` (the list the table and the CSV use) stacked, each row naming itself once, then every venture's value beside its colour.
- **Missing values are words.** A scope without a score is its word ("Not open to you", Not recorded), never a bar. A scored scope draws its band bar, its word and its confidence.
- **Sections open:**
  - with two ventures, every section is open (DE29 A);
  - with three or four, one section at a time, each a 44px toggle (DE29 B).
- **Print** still prints the table at any width (`print:block`); the phone layout never prints.
