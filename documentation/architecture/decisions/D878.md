### 1. What the Worker gains: two routes

The boards needed two reads that did not exist. Both are in `routes/founder_validate_sessions.ts` and are scoped as every session route is (`canWrite`; 404 for anyone else).

**`GET /api/founder/validate/meetings/calls?project_id=`** (`services/channels/callList.ts`)
- **Why it is needed:**
  - Until now a page could list calls only one interview at a time: `GET /interviews/:id/meetings` for refs, `/interviews/:id/sessions` for uploads.
  - MC3 and MC7 list them across the venture.
  - MC7 needs each call's "audio kept until", which no route computed.
- **What counts as a call:** what D877 keeps audio for — a session with a Zoom or Meet ref, an upload, or the notetaker's.
- **Order and limit:** newest first, at most 100; `truncated` says when there are more.
- **Each call carries:**
  - its first ref, as `meetingView` draws it (a restarted Zoom call's later instances are hidden, as the meetings route hides them);
  - the session's state and consent method;
  - the interviewee's name and company;
  - its pending proposals;
  - its audio.
- **The audio states:**
  - **held,** with `kept_until` = the newest chunk + its founder's retention, counted as `purgeExpiredCallAudio` counts it; null when kept until deleted;
  - **removed,** with when and why;
  - **none,** for a call whose provider sent text only. A chunk time that cannot be read is none, never a date.
- **Never sent:** a transcript line, a passcode or an attendee.

**`PATCH /sessions/:id/speakers` `{ from, to }`**
- MC5's Rename speaker renames one speaker in every passage of the session at once.
- Before, the only route was the per-segment `PATCH /sessions/:id/segments/:segId`. A 45-minute call has hundreds of segments.
- `400 speaker_label_required` when either name is empty; `404` when no passage has that name. The name is trimmed and cut to 80 characters, as the per-segment route does.

### 3. The boards

**MC1 Connection: Google Meet and Zoom, each the Worker's three steps (D875).**
- Each step reads Done, To do or Waiting, with the Worker's own sentence.
- Each tool's plan requirement is a line marked "Not checked by Axal".
- **Zoom's audience pill:**
  - `beta` reads "Zoom beta: open to invited founders";
  - `own_account` says Zoom is Axal's own account only, for now.
- **A sign-in Google refused** (`sync_error.code` `connection_refused` on `GET /calendar/google/status`) reads "Sign-in expired", its step Failed, with **Reconnect**.
- **What is pressed here:**
  - Google's own pages: Connect, Reconnect (`POST /calendar/google/connect?return_to=record`) and Allow transcript import (`allowMeetTranscripts('record')`). Each goes through an in-page dialog first, and only a Google sign-in URL is followed.
  - **Zoom connects on its connector page** (`/account/connectors/zoom`). Its OAuth start carries the company, the paywall and PKCE, all of which that page already handles.
- **The notetaker:** "Eadwyn joins the call" is drawn as **Not available yet**. The live join waits on #2581, #2582 and #2587, so nothing here starts a bot.

**MC2 Upcoming: one list by start time.**
- **Calls already on an interview:** the venture's scheduled refs in the next 14 days, each linking to its call.
- **The calendar's own calls** (`GET /meetings/upcoming`):
  - **A Meet call the founder organizes** has **Attach to interview**. Its dialog asks for the interview and how the interviewee agreed (out loud, in writing, beforehand). Nothing is sent without both. It goes through D871's session-less attach.
  - **A Zoom call** says it is attached when it starts, from Record a session. The Worker attaches a Zoom link only to the video session recording it (D685's `session_required`), so offering Attach here would only meet that refusal (§5).
  - **A call that cannot be attached** shows the Worker's reason.
- **States:**
  - **not connected** — neither tool, and the calendar unread;
  - **empty** — "No calls in the next 14 days";
  - **reconnect** — a 409 from Google;
  - **error,** with Try again.

**MC3 Calls: each call's status timeline and its source badge.**
- **The badges:** Meet transcript, Zoom transcript, Transcribed by Axal, Uploaded recording, or No transcript yet.
- **A Zoom or Meet call** reads Scheduled → Ended → Transcript ready → Imported. Where it stopped, it reads the Worker's own sentence.
- **An upload** reads Uploaded → Transcribing → Transcript ready.
- **A time the store does not hold** reads Not recorded.
- **The next steps follow the ref's error code:**
  - **Try again,** after the provider did not answer;
  - **Transcribe with Axal,** for D870's audio run (out of tokens, paused, failed), with its estimate "about $0.02 in AI tokens". The estimate is the call's minutes × the default final model's price from `GET /sessions/models`. The dialog says it is paid from AI tokens before anything runs, and it runs the existing `POST …/meetings/:refUid/import`.
  - **Open Connection,** for a reconnect;
  - **Upload the recording,** when the call has no transcript. It opens Upload with the interview chosen.
  - **Find the recording in Drive,** for D871's Meet recording.

**MC4 Upload, on D872's `uploadRecording` (resumable by `client_key`; a part already stored is skipped).**
- **Before the upload:**
  - the interview;
  - a drop zone;
  - the file read on the device, giving its length and the AI tokens estimate;
  - the attestation, with none chosen at first. **The upload button stays off until it is answered.**
- **During:** the parts as the canvas draws them: stored, in flight, to come.
- **A stopped upload** says to drop the same file again, and that it carries on from the part it stopped at.
- **When done,** it links to the call.

**MC5 Transcript.**
- **Each turn** shows its speaker and its time (00:05:02). The time is a button that plays the audio part holding it (`GET /sessions/:id/audio`). Audio already deleted says the text is kept.
- **Speakers:** each one once, with its turn count. A provider's "Speaker 3" is marked Rename.
- **Rename speaker** is an in-page dialog. It refuses an empty name or the same name, sends `PATCH /sessions/:id/speakers` and reads the transcript again. A failure keeps the dialog and says nothing changed.
- **The language switch** is Original · Translation · Both, by the chat boards' rule. A pending translation shows the original, marked.
- **The consent line** says how everyone recorded agreed, or that it is not recorded.

**MC6 Proposals.**
- **The summary's lines,** from the session's digest:
  - **Accept** keeps it (`POST /sessions/:id/digest/keep`, through a dialog);
  - **Edit** is the session page.
- **The pending proposals,** in the canvas's groups:
  - **Decisions** (`decision`, D873);
  - **Action items** (`follow_up`, `prototype_ask`);
  - **Data points,** each naming the Validate module it lands in. `ChannelProposals` now shows an item's module when it has one.
- **Accept, Edit, Dismiss and Accept all** are D862's, each through its dialog. Nothing lands before Accept.

**MC7 Retention (D877).**
- **The setting:** 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, or until deleted. The founder's own value is kept if it is another. It is saved through a dialog that says older audio goes in the next daily run and transcripts stay.
- **Each call's audio:**
  - kept until a day;
  - kept until deleted;
  - deleted on a day, by retention or by the founder, with its text kept;
  - text only.
- **Delete audio** (`DELETE /sessions/:id/audio`) is offered only where audio is held, through a dialog.

- Rows wrap, and the dialogs are bottom sheets, as on the chat boards.

### 4. The issue's rules, where they live

- **Authorized audio only:**
  - the upload's attestation is required by the button and by `uploadRecording`, and again by the Worker;
  - attaching a Meet call needs the interviewee's consent method.
- **The call's own transcript first, then speech-to-text:** Axal transcribes only where the provider saved none (D870), and the badge says which.
- **Eadwyn proposes, the founder accepts:** every proposal and the summary need the founder's press.
- **"AI tokens":** the estimate and the dialog use those words. A missing price is "the price is not recorded here", never $0.00.
- **Mock data:** the tests use "[Participant …]".

### 5. Where this departs from the canvas

- **Zoom's "Attach to interview" in MC2.**
  - The canvas draws it for Zoom calls too. The Worker attaches a Zoom link only to a recording session (D685), so the row says when and where it is attached.
  - A session-less Zoom attach (the Worker checking the host with `verifyHostedMeeting` and opening the session, as D871 does for Meet) would make the row's button real. That is a Worker task for #2516's owner.
- **"Transcribe with Axal" in MC3.**
  - The canvas offers it after "Zoom saved no transcript".
  - On the Worker, D870 transcribes the audio-only recording on its own when Zoom sends it. The button is offered where that run stopped (no tokens, paused, failed) and re-runs the import.
  - "No transcript" with no audio file offers Upload the recording, as the canvas's second button does.
- **"Dismiss" on the summary.** The digest has Keep and Edit routes but no dismiss, so the summary card has Accept and Edit only.
- **The "Signed in until 18 Oct" line.** No route says when a Google grant expires, only that Google refused it, so the card says "Sign-in expired" from the refusal alone.
