## D6264 — Research › Ask renders while its history is pending or unreadable (#6264)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-14 by S08. Filed from the 2026-11-14 coordinator audit under #3701.
- **Builds on:** D4610 (every `totals` tile goes through `UNREADABLE`), #5281 (`totals` is `thread.payload?.totals || null`).
- **Code:** `frontend/src/pages/research/AskZone.jsx`. Test: `frontend/test/research_ask_null_totals_6264.test.mjs`.
- **No migration, no new route, no new client method.**

### What was wrong

The metered banner draws as soon as `GET /api/ai/pricing` answers, and read
`totals.cost_usd` directly. When pricing answered before
`GET /api/research/ask/sessions`, or that read failed, `totals` was null and
the whole page threw, for all four licences.

### What it does now

- **History pending:** the banner shows the rate and no spend figure. The strip
  keeps its skeleton tiles.
- **History failed, or answered without totals:** the banner says
  `this session: Unreadable`, never `$0.00`. The strip now counts a failed read
  as answered (`ready` is the library plus either the history or its error), so
  its `totals` tiles say Unreadable with the D4610 note instead of sitting on
  "not read yet" for good.

The test mounts Ask for founder, investor, advisor and partner, with the
sessions read held open and with it rejected. It fails on the previous code
with `Cannot read properties of null (reading 'cost_usd')`.
