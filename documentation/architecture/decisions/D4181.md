### 1. What a view tells us

The player keeps one `learning_progress` row per member per video (`subject_kind = 'item'`). The row holds the status, a percent that never goes backwards, and the last position it saved.

- **How far a viewer got** is the furthest of the position and the percent of the length. A completed view reached the end.
- **Never past the end:** a viewer's distance is never more than the length.
- **A view with none of these** is still a view. It is left out of the curve, and the Sheet says how many were ("Not recorded").

### 2. The figures

| Figure | How it is worked out |
| --- | --- |
| Views | Members who started the video. |
| Completion rate | Completed views over views. |
| Median watch time | The median of how far each viewer got. |
| Drop-off | The video in 20 equal spans. For each span, the share of viewers who got past its start; everyone counts at 0. |
| Biggest drop | The largest fall between two spans, named with the chapter it falls in (D4176's chapters). |

The board says "at each minute". The issue asks for 20 buckets, which keeps a two-hour video and a three-minute one at the same resolution, so the build follows the issue.

The Sheet draws the curve as 20 rows, one per span, each a bar whose width is the share still watching. The Learning pages style nothing inline but a bar's width (`learning_ui_d562`), so the board's vertical chart becomes rows, and the chapters are listed under them with their start times.

### 3. No small cohorts, and no names

- **Under five viewers,** the reply carries no figure: not the count, the rate or the curve. It says only "Not enough views to show". The curve and the median have the same floor over the viewers whose position is known.
- **No names:** the read selects no user id. The reply is counts, a rate, a median and shares; nothing per viewer leaves the Worker.

### 4. Not recorded, never 0

- A video with no length has no curve ("Not recorded"). Its median still comes from the positions, in seconds.
- A median with no known position reads "Not recorded".

### 5. Who

The Super Admin only, through the studio's existing `gate`, like every other `/api/admin/learning` route.
