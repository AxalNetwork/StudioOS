## D1350 — LinkedIn part 1: the connector's infrastructure

S07, 2026-10-28, for #3565 (part 1 of #3564). Migration **651**. Basis: `main` at `daed664fd1`.

### 1. What already existed, and stays

- **`routes/linkedin.ts`** is OpenID Connect sign-in.
  - The state is HMAC-signed and single-use in KV.
  - The access token is thrown away after userinfo, and still is.
  - The identity sits on `users.linkedin_*`.
- **`services/providerOauthKeys.ts`** holds the keys.
- **`network_connections`** is the member's own book.

### 2. Reused, and added

| Need | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The member's book | **Reused** `network_connections` | It is the book. `contact_sources` points at its rows rather than copying contacts. |
| The identity | **Copied** `users.linkedin_*` into `linkedin_connections` | The user columns stay, read-only, for Refer & Earn's badge, and the callback still writes them. |
| Import jobs | **Added** `contact_import_jobs` | `data_imports` is the spreadsheet importer. It counts against the member's monthly import quota and keeps one success and one failure count, with no uid. A LinkedIn import needs created, matched and skipped counts, and must not spend the spreadsheet quota. |
| The audit log | **Added** `integration_audit_log` | `integration_logs` needs an `integrations` row, and a LinkedIn sign-in is not one. `admin_audit_log` records staff actions. |

### 3. Migration 651

- **`linkedin_connections`**, one row per member:
  - the LinkedIn `sub`;
  - the scopes LinkedIn granted;
  - the status: `active`, `revoked` or `expired`;
  - the connected and disconnected times;
  - `token_enc`, null while only OpenID Connect is used. Part 5 fills it, encrypted, if LinkedIn approves the API.
- **`contact_import_jobs`:**
  - its uid and owner;
  - the source: `linkedin_csv`, `linkedin_api`, `linkedin_dma` or `csv`;
  - the status: running, done or failed;
  - the counts: rows, created, matched, skipped and errors;
  - the error sentence and the times.
- **`contact_sources`:**
  - which row of `network_connections` came from which source and job;
  - the source's own reference;
  - `owner_user_id`;
  - `created_row`: 1 when that source made the row, 0 when it matched a row that was already there.
- **`integration_audit_log`:**
  - the actor and the owner;
  - the provider;
  - the event: connect, disconnect, import, delete, share or access;
  - a subject, which is a job's uid, a count or the scopes. It is never a token and never a contact's email.
- **The identity already on `users`** (one production row) is copied into `linkedin_connections` with the scopes OpenID Connect asks for. A blank `linkedin_sub` is not copied. The copy is `INSERT OR IGNORE`, so applying it again changes nothing.

### 4. The routes

- **The callback** also upserts the member's connection, with the scopes LinkedIn's token answer granted, and audits `connect`.
- **`GET /api/linkedin/status`** also returns `status`, `scopes` and `last_import`, the member's latest import job.
- **`POST /api/linkedin/disconnect`** also:
  - marks the connection `revoked`, clearing any token;
  - audits `disconnect`;
  - with `{ delete_imported: true }`, deletes every row of the member's book that a LinkedIn source made and no other source has touched, then audits `delete` with the count. A row the member typed and LinkedIn later matched (`created_row = 0`) stays, as does a row that also came from a spreadsheet. Their provenance survives.
- **`GET /api/linkedin/imports`** and **`GET /api/linkedin/imports/:uid`** return the member's own import jobs.
- **`services/linkedinConnections.ts`** carries the writes later parts use: `startImportJob`, `finishImportJob` (which audits the import by count) and `recordSource` (which records only on the owner's own row).

**Every read binds the member's id:** `user_id = ?` on the connection and `owner_user_id = ?` everywhere else. Another member's job reads as 404.

### 5. Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/linkedin_connector_d1350.test.ts`, 5 tests, on the migrated schema in memory:

- **The state is single-use:** a replayed callback is refused before the token exchange, and a forged one too.
- **No token anywhere:** not in the redirect, the status reply, any log line, the connection row, the audit log or the activity log. No email in the audit log.
- **Disconnect, without and with `delete_imported`:** only the LinkedIn-only row is deleted, and provenance survives for the typed row and the two-source row.
- **Another member:** 404 on the job, an empty list, a null `last_import`, and their delete touches nobody else's book. A source can't be recorded on another member's row.
- **The migration:** it copies the existing identity once, skips a blank one, and applying it again copies nothing twice. Its CHECKs refuse an unknown status or event.

**Mutation check:** 13 mutations, all caught.
