### How the PDF was produced

- `frontend/src/pages/wellbeing/wellnessDeck.js` draws the five slides as HTML. The slide words are the canvas's `SL` (PGW04A to E) verbatim; the cover's purpose and audience and slide 3's four steps come from `wellnessPresentation.js`, so the page and the PDF tell one story. It uses the kit's slide master (`frontend/src/pages/programs/deck.js`: its class names, `DECK_CSS` and `SITE_HOST`) and adds `WELLNESS_DECK_CSS` for what only Wellness draws: the "Private to you" bracket over steps 1 and 2, "Seen by" on each step, the dashed planned box, the dawn line and the cover image under a dark scrim.
- `scripts/build-wellness-brief.mjs` prints it the way `scripts/build-program-briefs.mjs` (D6966) prints the kit's decks: Playwright's Chromium, 13.333 × 7.5 in at scale 2/3, one slide per page, text as text, the legal paper's faces embedded and subset, live links, tagged, with an outline. `--gray --out DIR` prints the PGW05 print sheet elsewhere; `--html DIR` writes the HTML to look at.
- Why a second script rather than a catalog entry: Wellness's page is its own (`pages/wellbeing/`, D7121 part 1), not the kit's `ProgramKeyPage`. Its slides 3 and 4 need blocks the kit's `slideBlock` has no type for. `deck.js`, `build-program-briefs.mjs`, `collect.js` and `scripts/lib/programCatalog.mjs` are also edited by open PRs #7795, #7801, #7802 and #7804. Nothing shared is edited here.
- **The file:** `frontend/public/program-briefs/axal-vc-wellness-v1.pdf`, 5 pages, 16:9, 365,024 bytes (356 KB). That is under the 500 KB `scripts/lfs-size-gate.mjs` limit, so it is a plain blob, not LFS. Most of it is the cover image. `frontend/public/axal-vc-future.png` is re-encoded as a JPEG at its own width of 1536 px, quality 0.7, because Chromium would embed the PNG as a raw Flate image. The document title is "Wellness · Axal VC · Edition 1 · November 2026", and there is no author.
- Workers static assets serve a `.pdf` under `frontend/public/` (built into `docs/`) as `application/pdf`, and `_headers` overrides no type there. The test pins both, and that `pdfPublished` agrees with the file on disk.
