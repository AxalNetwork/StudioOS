## D879 — Mercury is a member connector only: Axal VC's own accounts leave the registry row, the connector page and /connect; Grow and Raise declared

- **Status:** built by S07 for #2552 (C1), part of #2551.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `784a1bed`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Supersedes:** D710's entity audience on the member connector (decision 5: an admin on HQ saw Mercury as an `entity` row) and D753 decision 15's `entity` page.
- **Keeps:** D710's HQ console and store (`/api/admin/mercury`, `services/mercury/`, `mercury_entity_connections`) and D713, D752 and D840's personal connections.
- **Migration:** none.

### 1. The owner's ruling (2026-10-22)

> Mercury connector is for users, you put the Mercury Axal VC accounts, which has nothing to do there.

- `/account/connectors/mercury` and the Connectors catalogue are about the member's **own** Mercury, read through Mercury's MCP server and used in Grow and Raise.
- Axal VC's own entity accounts never appear there for anyone, the owner included.
- They are managed only in HQ › Fund administration › Bank accounts, which is unchanged.

### 2. What changed

**The registry row (`integrations/registry.ts`).**
- `entity_roles` is gone. A new `preview_roles: ['admin']` takes its place (§3).
- The `description` now speaks to a member: their own Mercury, read-only, through Mercury's sign-in, for Grow and Raise.
- `CONNECTOR_METADATA.mercury`:
  - `pulls` and `feeds` are the founder's Grow and Raise modules, built from `MERCURY_FEEDS` (`integrations/mercuryFeeds.ts`, D840 parts 2 and 3). They are read, not retyped.
  - `used_by` is Grow (`/grow`) and Raise (`/raise`).
  - `permissions` is one sentence naming the member's MCP reads, never "Axal entity accounts".
- A partner's and an investor's card keep their own variant. They carry empty `pulls` and `used_by`, so neither claims Grow or Raise: the partner page stays as it is, and C6 (#2557) adds the investor's Deals and Pipeline.
- `connection_source` is `integrations` for every Mercury viewer. `mercury_entity_connections` is never named on a member surface.

**The pages (`integrations/connectorPages.ts`).**
- `mercuryPage('entity')` and the `entity` key of `CONNECTOR_PAGES.mercury` are gone, and so is `SignInKind 'entity'`, which nothing else used.
- `pageFor`, `trustFor` and `pageDescriptor` lose their entity branch.
- The founder, partner and investor pages are unchanged.

**`/connect` (`routes/integrations.ts`).**
- The `entity_connection_only` 409 is gone, and so is the entity `already_connected` branch.
- An admin's `/connect` for Mercury reaches Mercury's own connect, which answers 403 `purpose_not_allowed`: "Your role does not hold a personal Mercury connection." This is the answer any role without a personal connection already got. The sentence is now `noPersonalConnectionSentence` in the registry.

### 3. An admin on HQ: a preview of the founder's page

`audienceFor` answers `preview` for a `preview_roles` role on HQ, and `none` for it on a branch.

A previewing admin:
- reads the founder's card and page (the same tagline, tools and trust notice);
- gets `has_implementation: false`, so there is no connect button;
- gets `already_connected: false`, so no connection is read, not even a Mercury row in their own name;
- gets `preview: true` and `hq_accounts`: "Axal VC's own accounts are managed in HQ › Fund administration › Bank accounts.", linking to `/admin?tab=bank-accounts`.

No member's card carries `preview` or `hq_accounts`.

C2 (#2553) renders the line on the member pages and moves the entity UI under HQ. Until then, the SPA's entity branches (`isEntityRow`) simply never match: no entry carries `mercury_entity_connections`.

### 4. Not changed

- `/api/admin/mercury` and its tests;
- the fund-admin services;
- `mercury_entity_connections` and every stored connection, entity or member token.

Nothing moves money, and no Mercury scope or tool is added.

### 5. Verified

`cloudflare-worker/test/mercury_member_only_d879.test.ts` covers eight viewers: a founder, a partner and an investor on HQ and on a branch, and an admin on HQ and on a branch. For each it checks:

- that no response names "Axal entity", the entity store or an entity key (`holdings`, `management`, `gp`, `fund`) as a key or a value;
- that no request reads `mercury_entity_connections`.

It also checks:
- the admin's preview, its line to HQ, and no connection read;
- the founder's Grow and Raise in `used_by`, `feeds` and `pulls`;
- the partner's and investor's empty claims.
