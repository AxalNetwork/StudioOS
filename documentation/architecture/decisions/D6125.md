## D6125 — A signed partner's renewal date reminds the founder (#6125)

Under #4861. `growth_partners.renewal_on` was stored and shown on Partner
network, but nothing acted on it: Grow's daily step (`services/growDaily.ts`,
D691) read only obligations and KPI alerts.

### What is sent

- **Who and when.** Each run of the daily step (05:35 UTC) reads every partner
  at stage `signed` with a `renewal_on` that is today or later. At 30 days and
  at 7 days before that date (`RENEWAL_DAYS`), the venture's founder
  (`founderUser()`, the same pick obligations use) gets one in-app
  notification, type `grow_partner_renewal_reminder`, category
  `proactive_nudges`, linked to `/grow/partner-network/<uid>?project_id=<id>`.
  No email.
- **Not sent.** A partner that is `talking`, `terms`, `paused` or `ended`; a
  renewal date that has passed; a partner with no date. A paused partner signed
  again before its date is reminded from then on.
- **Several due at once.** A date entered 5 days out finds both thresholds due
  in one run. It sends ONE notification (for the nearest, 7) and records both,
  so the founder is not told twice the same morning. A threshold the step
  missed (a skipped cron) is caught by the next run while the date is ahead.

### What is stored

Migration `202611141525_6125_partner_renewal_reminders.sql` adds
`growth_partners.renewal_reminders_sent_json`, `{"on": "<date>", "sent": [30, 7]}`.
It is keyed to the renewal date it was sent for: when `renewal_on` changes the
stored `on` no longer matches, `renewalSent()` reads it as nothing sent, and
both thresholds are armed again. No writer clears the column, so the partner
PATCH writer is unchanged. (Obligations reset `reminders_sent_json` in their
writer instead; keying the record avoids a second writer rule.)

The claim is made before the send, as obligations do: the `UPDATE` matches only
the row as it was read (same stored record, same `renewal_on`, still
`signed`), so an overlapping run, a date change or a pause between the read and
the claim sends nothing.

### Needs your attention

`services/growAttention.ts` adds a `partner_renewals` signal and, for a writer,
a `partner_renewal` item (priority 55, between saved proposals and offers):
signed partners of this venture renewing between today and 30 days from now.
One partner links to its record ("Prepare for <name>'s renewal"); several link
to Partner network ("Review partner renewals"). A failed read is
`unreadable` (the overview turns `partial`), never zero.
