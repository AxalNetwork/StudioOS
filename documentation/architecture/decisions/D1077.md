- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Trust center · Boards.dc.html` and `Trust center Page.dc.html`.

### 1. The page reads the published store

`pages/site/TrustCenter.jsx` reads three things once per page, each settling on its own:
- `GET /api/public/trust`: the blocks, the subprocessors and the latest changes (D1075);
- `GET /api/public/status`: the live state and the 90-day history (D1076);
- `GET /api/public/trust/matrix`: "What we check, and for whom".

Each block (`blockOf`, `trustCenterModel.js`) is in one of four states:

| State | When | What the page draws |
| --- | --- | --- |
| loading | the read has not answered | skeleton cards (TRU16) |
| unreadable | the read failed, or its blocks part is `{ unreadable: true }` | the part's sentence and Try again; never the page's own lines in its place |
| fallback | the block was never published (`null`) | D1072's lines, each "Checked: Not recorded" |
| ready | published | the block's groups; each fact with "Checked [day]", or "Checked: Not recorded" when the line has no date; each gap with Not recorded and its reason |

- A stored line with no text is dropped. A gap with no reason shows only Not recorded.
- The header's "Last reviewed" is the newest publish of any block. When none is published it reads "Last reviewed: Not recorded".

### 2. The tabs

- **Overview (TRU1):**
  - **Five tiles.**
    - Status, from the probes.
    - Certifications: "None held", or each certification with its auditor.
    - The security contact.
    - Subprocessors: "[n] listed" with the last change, or Not recorded while the list is with counsel.
    - Your data.
  - **"How we protect you"** from the At a glance block, each card with Read more.
  - **The Status strip (TRU1s).** One cell per day, the worst across monitored services. A day before records began is dashed and never green. The count reads "Days with no incident reported: [n] of [m] days recorded"; no percentage appears.
  - **The matrix.** Each cell reads Required, "Renew every [n] months" or "When it applies". A dash means the requirement is not asked of that profile.
  - **"Changes to this page"**, with a date block per change and All changes when the log is longer.
- **Security (TRU2) and Data and privacy (TRU3):** the blocks.
  - Data and privacy adds Your rights. Signed out, Download and Delete read "Sign in to do this"; signed in they open the account. Ask a question copies privacy@axal.vc.
- **Subprocessors (TRU4, TRU4v):**
  - The listed companies. One that has not started yet reads "From [day]".
  - None listed is counsel's line (owner question 3, A).
  - Connectors are listed apart.
  - The subscribe step posts the address, Turnstile and the honeypot once, then says to check the inbox. A refusal is the Worker's own sentence.
- **Documents (TRU5):**
  - The legal pages with their own dates. A version nobody recorded says so.
  - The Data Processing Addendum reads "Not published yet".
  - Cookie preferences opens the cookie dialog.
  - security.txt opens the file.
  - A published Documents block adds its lines below the table.
- **Disclosure (TRU6, TRU6d):**
  - The three ways to report:
    - the private advisory;
    - email, with Copy address;
    - signed in, Report a security issue.
  - The report dialog sends what is affected, what happened and, optionally, how to reproduce (`POST /api/help/security-reports`, D1075). Signed out, the third way is "Sign in to report".
  - Then the response times, safe harbour, coordinated disclosure, and the block's gaps (the PGP key, researchers credited).
  - "Report a security issue in the app" was a Not recorded gap. It is now the dialog, and `SECURITY.md` says so.
