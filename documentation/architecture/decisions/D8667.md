### What the audit found

- Start exam stayed live beside "Could not be read" because `ExamFlow.jsx` disables it only on `!onStart || busy || spent`, so a failed attempts read leaves it enabled.
- The title appeared twice because it is the `h1` in `LearningHeader` and again an `h2` in `ExamIntro`; the untimed policy was repeated three times and the draft warning came after the facts.
- The Exams tab said "No exams published yet" to a Super Admin with only drafts because `GET /exams` in `routes/learning_lists.ts` lists published exams only, while `services/learningAccess.ts` lets a Super Admin open the draft.

### What the canvas decides

- Before you start has one `h1`, one card with four facts (8 questions, untimed, 75% pass mark, 3 attempts left), one sentence on what starting does, and one action; an open attempt shows Resume, and no attempts left says so (LEX02, LEX02B, LEX02C).
- On a draft, a Super Admin sees who can see it and the rule that questions freeze at the first attempt above the action; Preview is primary and the counted start is secondary (LEX03).
- The attempts read goes reading → failed → retrying → read; until it succeeds Start stays visible but switched off with its reason and Retry is primary. A failure never reads as 0 or as unlimited, and a real "No limit" is drawn separately (LEX04, LEX04B).
- Author preview is not an attempt, records nothing and shows keyed answers without a score (LEX05, proposed).
- The Exams tab keeps the published-only empty state apart from the failed read and from a search with no match; Super Admins also see a line about drafts (LEX06, LEX06B).
- Synthetic boards extend the flow: start confirmation, question, review, submit confirmation, not passed with answers revealed, passed on a score-only variant, try again and start errors; after a pass, Back to the course replaces Try again (LEX07 to LEX13).
- Phone 390 × 844 on LEX20 to LEX27; variants, focus order, accessible names and wrapping on LEX30 and LEX31.
