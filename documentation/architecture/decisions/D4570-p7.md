## D4570 part 7 — A share is only as live as its grant (#4570)

#4570 slice 7, after slices 1 to 4 in `D4570.md` and slices 5 and 6 in `D4570-p5.md` and `D4570-p6.md`. D4570-p5 kept one download route back: "a revocation must take effect on every read path that the revoked access opened". This part is that route and its fix. The owner chose to land it as an ordinary PR, because on 2026-11-13 production held no document shares and no advisor grants. A read-only count on `studioos-db` found 0 active shares, 0 revoked grants and 0 grants past their expiry.

### What was wrong

A founder grants an advisor access to a project (`advisor_client_grants`, migration 218) and can push research documents to that advisor (`advisor_client_document_shares`). The research library read the share alone:
- `readableDoc()` in `routes/research.ts`, behind `GET /api/research/documents/:uid/download`;
- the shared half of `GET /api/research/documents`.

Both checked `s.status = 'active'` on the share, and nothing on the grant. The grant appeared only in a subquery that named the project for the "From <project>" chip.

So, after a founder revoked the grant, or after it passed `expires_at`, the advisor still saw each shared document listed and could mint its signed download. Revoking a grant does not revoke the shares made under it (`advisor_grants.ts`).

Two smaller faults sat beside it:
- **The chip's subquery matched the wrong ids.** It matched `projects.founder_id` (a `founders` row) against `research_documents.owner_user_id` (a `users` row). The label and the access-log project id were right only where those two ids happened to be equal.
- **Brief notes ignored expiry.** Brief notes (`routes/research_client_prep.ts` since D4574-p8) and the brief draft's client context checked the grant's status but not its `expires_at`.

### The rule

A share reads only through the newest **live** grant that the share's advisor holds over a project of the document's owner.
- **Live** means `status = 'active'` and `expires_at` empty or still ahead: the same predicate as `activeGrant()` in `advisor_grants.ts`.
- **The owner** is matched through `users.founder_id = projects.founder_id`, as the advisor brief already does.
- **Refusal:** a share with no live grant is neither listed nor readable. It gets the same 404 as a document that does not exist, so the refusal says nothing about whose document it is.

The subquery is written out in each query, not built from a shared string, so the SQL stays literal for `check-sql-prepare`.

Brief notes and the brief draft add the same expiry predicate to their grant lookups.

### Not changed

- **Grant revocation still leaves the shares in place.** The read side now ignores them, which also covers expiry, a case a cascade could not.
- **Re-granting revives the earlier shares.** Revoking the shares together with the grant would change that, and would stop the founder's "shared documents" list showing shares that no longer open. It is a product decision and is left open.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/research_share_live_grant.test.ts` has 7 tests. It drives the real routers: `research_client_prep.ts` mounted ahead of `research.ts`.
- **A live grant opens:** with no expiry, and with an expiry still ahead. The chip names the project even though the founders row id differs from the owner's user id.
- **Refused, and the share is not listed,** when:
  - the grant was revoked;
  - the grant has expired;
  - there never was a grant;
  - the only live grant is over another founder's project.

  Each refusal body equals a missing document's, and the owner's own read still opens.
- **Brief notes** open while the grant is live and are refused once it has expired.

On `main` before this part, 6 of the 7 fail.

Each of these mutations is caught:
- dropping the expiry predicate;
- dropping the status predicate;
- dropping the read gate;
- dropping the list gate;
- dropping the brief-notes expiry predicate.

The 903 research and advisor worker tests pass.
