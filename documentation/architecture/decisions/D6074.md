## D6074 — Data room Upload shows storage used and left, warns near the limit, and refuses an over-limit queue before sending (#6074)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-14. Filed under #4863 from the 2026-11-14 coordinator audit.
- **Boards:** DR4s ("Storage left") and DR23 ("Storage near the limit") of `design/canvases/integrated/Raise Data Room Page.dc.html` (listed in `Data Room · Boards.dc.html`). There is no copy under `design/incoming/`.
- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** unchanged. No `/api` route or client method is added.
- **Code:** `frontend/src/pages/raise/dataroom/filesModel.js` (`uploadStorage`, `queueStorage`, `storageLine`, `queueEntry` keeps the file's bytes) and `DataRoomUpload.jsx`.

### 1. The figure: Usage › Storage's own read

Upload reads `usageApi.storage()` (`GET /api/usage/storage`, D642 as D1169 grew it), the read `StoragePage.jsx` and `UsageOverview.jsx` draw, and passes it through that page's `allowanceView` (`storagePageModel.js`). The two pages cannot disagree.

A data room file counts to the venture's owner (D1170 §2). The read is the caller's own plan. Its `companies[].ventures` lists the ventures whose files count to the caller, so:

| The read | Upload says | Blocks? |
|---|---|---|
| A measured figure against an allowance, and this room is among the caller's ventures | `Storage: <used> of <allowance> used · <left> left.` (or `None left`, `<n> over`) | only a queue that would pass the limit (§3) |
| Failed, a place unreadable (`measured_bytes` null), plan unreadable (`plan_recorded: false`), or `companies` null | `Storage left: Unreadable. Your storage could not be read. That is not the same as having none. You can still add files.`, with Try again | no |
| A plan with no allowance figure (negotiated) | `Storage left: Not recorded.` and the reason | no |
| Staff | `Storage left: Not recorded.` and Usage's staff line | no |
| The room counts to another member's plan (a co-founder's upload) | that the files count to the company owner's storage, which is not shown | no |

Nothing is ever drawn as 0. After a send that landed a file, the figure is read again.

### 2. The near-limit line (DR23)

**Threshold: the Worker's own `state`, `near_80` or `near_95`**, that is 80% of the allowance (`storageState` in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/storageUsage.ts`, D1169/D1170). It is the threshold Usage › Storage already uses for its "Near your limit" pill and its 80% notice, and the one the Worker's in-app notices fire at (D1170). No new threshold was chosen.

The line is the canvas's, amber, with its one door: "Your files use 4.6 GB of 5 GB. Versions count too." and **Open Storage** (`/account/usage/storage`). DR23 draws it under the tool's chips on Home; this change draws it on Upload, which is the issue's scope. Home's chip is not built here.

### 3. The pre-send refusal (DR4s)

The waiting files' total (files already refused for size, or already in the room, are not counted) is weighed by the Worker's own rule in `assertStorageRoom`: refused when used plus the total passes the allowance, or when nothing is left and the file is empty. Exactly what is left still fits.

- The refusal is drawn with the shared `StorageRefusal` (D1171), testid `dataroom-upload-over`: "These 2 files need 4 MB. You have 3 MB left on your plan. Nothing was sent; remove some or make room first." (one file: "This file needs …"; nothing left: the shared "Storage full." sentence). Its doors are **Open Storage** (Usage › Storage) and **Compare plans**.
- Add to the room is disabled, and `send` returns before anything is sent.
- A waiting file can now be taken out of the queue (**Remove**), so a refused queue can be trimmed without closing the sheet.
- Only a measured figure refuses. The Worker's refusal at upload (D1170/D1171, `StorageRefusal` under the file) stays and answers for every case the browser cannot weigh.

### 4. Also

- `storageLine` keeps the room's own count ("This room's current files: …") and drops the sentence that sent the reader to Usage for what is left. The stale comment ("no store totals a plan's limit") is gone.
- D1106's "Storage left: not recorded" is superseded by this decision.
