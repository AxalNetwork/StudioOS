## D3876 — The Gallery is the template store, with covers, PREVIEW and Yours (#3876)

- **Status:** Built by S21 (integration), 2026-11-04. Frontend only. The content work and the cover fields are handed to S25 (see below).
- **Builds on:**
  - D1535 (#3782: the store, its routes and its Q5 answer);
  - D1604 (#3716: the gallery screen, pd29).
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Pitch Deck Studio v2 Page.dc.html`, frames `pd29`, `pd29c`, `pd29s`, `pd29y` and `pd29strip`. Also `design/incoming/Pitch Deck Templates Page.dc.html` and its boards (TPL-01 to TPL-24).
- **No migration, no Worker route.**

### What was decided

1. **The Gallery's tiles are the store's templates** (`deckV2Adapter.templates`, `GET /api/decks/templates`), as D1535 Q5 answered.
   - v1's 13 deck methods stay on New deck as its method start. The gallery no longer reads `deckMethods` or `deckRecommend`.
   - The `form:deck-template` rail option no longer lists the gallery. The store's apply runs no model, so the gallery presses none.
2. **A cover is the template's title card on its own swatch** (pd29c).
   - It shows "[Venture name]" and "[One-line pitch]" only.
   - The swatch's ground, ink and muted colours are H10's palette (`templatesModel.SWATCHES`, #3876 §2).
   - The template's own accent and display face are drawn only when the list returns them (`meta.cover.accent`, a hex; `meta.cover.display_font`, a family name). Until then the cover uses the swatch and Space Grotesk.
3. **Filters ask the Worker**, one chip per group, from the store's own `filters` list: Stage, New deck's seven audiences, and Style. Pressing the chosen chip clears it. A filter with no match says "No template matches these filters. Clear one to see more."
4. **PREVIEW** reads the selected template (`GET /decks/templates/:uid`) and lists every card in order (pd29s).
5. **Use this template** writes the store's proposal (`POST /decks/templates/:uid/apply`) and lands on the shared review, so nothing is saved before Accept. Its dialog says what really happens: a placeholder proposal, figures from records or "Not recorded", and no model.
6. **Yours** (pd29y) lists the caller's own templates, used the same way.
   - **Save as my template** names the venture's current version (at most 40 characters). It reads the list again and says "Saved to Yours."
   - A venture with no deck cannot save, and says why.
   - A reader sees Yours and has no Save.

### Handed to the build sessions

- **Cover fields** on the list's Meta: `cover: { accent, accent_ink, display_font }`, from the template's `theme_json`.
- **TPL-01 to TPL-12 for AI-native companies:** the new canvas's `HD1`, `SUP1` and `TL1`, reworded to pass `numberIn`. This includes new metric tiles that may need record slots.
- **A re-seed when a seeded template's content changes:** `seedHq` is `INSERT OR IGNORE`, so a changed library never reaches a database that already holds the row.

All three are on #3876 (S25).
