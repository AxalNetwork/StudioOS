## D7907 — GitHub-hosted 8-core included compute as a third pool, and its bounded canary (#7907)

**Scope.** The owner authorized the GitHub-hosted 8-core runner `studioos-bench-linux-8` (runner group 6, "StudioOS Included Compute") on included compute only, with no overage. This supersedes the exclusion of "paid larger labels" in D7887 and `RUNNER_CAPACITY.md` for this one runner. Every other larger label stays excluded.

**A third pool, not an alias.** `github-8` is its own profile in `profiles.json` (`pool: github-hosted-included`, group ID 6, one label), in the coordinator's `CiProfile`, in admission's `executionTarget`, and in `runner-executor.yml`. It is never mapped to `linux-x64` or to the self-hosted group. Bootstrap validates the row but refuses it as a registration target. Recording group 6 approves nothing for the self-hosted profiles, whose numeric group stays unrecorded (D7906).

**Selection.** `selectPools` delegates to `github-8` only when the policy's `maxIncludedWorkerShards` (0 or 1; 0 is the default and disables the pool) has room. Included compute has no inventory the integration can read, so every unfinished `github-8` attempt holds its slot, including running ones. A missing or stale self-hosted inventory closes only the self-hosted pools. The Mac keeps `worker-1-of-6`. `github-8` comes before the persistent Linux host, which still needs #7905's isolation proof. Self-hosted shards stay capped by `maxExternalWorkerShards`. All delegated pools together leave `minimumHostedWorkerShards` on standard hosted, which stays the floor and the fallback.

**Fallback.** A job queued on the label never moves to `ubuntu-latest` by itself. An attempt not picked up by `includedStartMs`, or past `includedExecutionMs`, is fenced and cancelled. Only an observed termination starts a hosted attempt under a new generation, which revokes the old receipt. A verified test failure on `github-8` is a real failure, never a fallback. The budgets (3 and 28 minutes) are provisional. `validateCapacityPolicy` refuses any value that would not leave a full hosted fallback inside the 90-minute request deadline. The coordinator and admission budgets must stay equal, and a test reads both.

**Canary.** `.github/workflows/included-compute-canary.yml` is dispatched from `main` by an operator with one commit already on `main` and one Worker shard (default `worker-3-of-6`). It writes one equal-weights plan from that commit, records its digest, and runs the same shard once on `github-8` and once on standard hosted. Each job and the test step have their own time limit. The report compares queue, job and test time, and refuses to compare lanes whose source, part or plan differ. Overage is not readable with the job token, so the report says "Not recorded" and the operator reads it from the billing page. The canary changes no routing, required check or variable.

**Still the owner's.** Group 6 must allow exactly `AxalNetwork/StudioOS/.github/workflows/included-compute-canary.yml@refs/heads/main` for the canary. For routing later, it must also allow `runner-executor.yml@refs/heads/main`, and nothing broader. The branch-only smoke workflow stays readiness evidence only. Enabling routing (`maxIncludedWorkerShards: 1` in a deployed policy), the isolation adapter for GitHub-hosted profiles and the required-check cutover stay with #7909, after the canary's numbers are in.
