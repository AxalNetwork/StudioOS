### Decisions

1. **No migration.** The issue asked for one adding a team-member assignee to `compliance_events`. Migration 443 already added `owner_user_id INTEGER REFERENCES users(id)` beside `owner_name`, and nothing wrote it: `addObligation` bound `null`. This change only starts writing and reading that column, so there is no new migration file and no schema ledger change.
2. **The company team.** This is the `user_company_links` rows for the venture's `projects.company_id`, which is the set Build's owner picker uses (`memberOwner` in `records/build.ts`). A venture with no company has an empty team, so only No one yet can be chosen. Pick owner then says that no one is on the team yet.
3. **Writes.** `owner_user_id` is refused (400, field `owner_user_id`, "Choose someone on the company team") for anyone outside the team, and for anything that is not a positive whole id. A refused write changes nothing.
   - Picking a member clears `owner_name`.
   - Picking No one yet (`''` or null) clears a picked member but keeps a name typed before #6790.
   - An Edit that does not send `owner_user_id` leaves the owner alone.
   - `owner_name` is still accepted from older callers when no `owner_user_id` is sent.
4. **One owner on every surface.** `ownerShown(member name, typed name)` gives the picked member's name, else the typed name, else null. The following all read it, so they all show the same owner:
   - the record's sub and Owner fact;
   - the module rows (`owner`, beside the raw `owner_name` and `owner_user_id`);
   - the cycle card's calendar and its "Give X an owner" step;
   - Calendar → Compliance (both the founder read and the staff scope);
   - `GET /api/compliance/events` (`owner`).
5. **Picker (GR-23).** The Add and Edit owner field is a listbox (`CH`): No one yet first, then the team by name.
   - The record's own Edit carries a note naming a typed name, and says it stays until someone is picked.
   - The frontend Edit keeps a typed name as its own choice, "Sam (typed, not on the team)". That choice sends nothing.
   - A picked member who has left the team stays as a choice under their name.
6. **Pick owner.** On the record, an ownerless obligation's Owner fact offers Pick owner (`pick-owner`, `row_only`). It opens a listbox of the team and saves through `updateObligation`. On the module table, an ownerless row's control is Pick owner in place of Edit (`pickOwnerAction`, as BD7's Link evidence). It opens the same Edit focused on `owner_user_id`. A row with a typed name is not ownerless, and its Edit offers the listbox.
7. **Reminders go to the assignee.** The daily step sends to `owner_user_id` while that person is still on the company team. Otherwise the reminder goes to the founder, as before. Before this change it sent to any `owner_user_id`, but nothing wrote one.
   - The body names who the reminder went to and why: "Sent to Cai Moreno, its owner on the company team"; "Sent to you as the founder: it names Sam, who was typed in rather than picked from the company team"; "…: its owner is no longer on the company team"; "…: no one on the company team is its owner yet".
   - The payload carries `assignee_user_id`.
   - The record's Reminders section ends with a Goes to row that applies the same rule.
8. **Not in scope.** Agenda items' `owner_name` (`obligation_agenda_items`) and market entry steps' owner stay free text.
