## D5811 — Record a session: Microsoft Teams is selectable, and the setup survives a trip to Account (#5811)

**Decided.** The Call's Microsoft Teams chip is no longer "Coming soon". Its
word, its "Create the meeting for me" and its connect link come from the
member's own `GET /founder/validate/meetings/capabilities` Teams row (D5810),
read when the lightbox opens and again whenever the tab comes back into view;
an answer older than the newest is dropped, and a typed link is never reset.
While the read is out the chip says "Checking"; when it failed, "Unreadable",
with Try again. It is never disabled: a pasted Teams link needs no Microsoft
sign-in, and the three Teams hosts stay the only links taken.

**Creating is not recording.** For Teams, "Create the meeting for me" is its own
button, shown to a connected member on a deployment that can create, and it
only fills the link from `POST /meetings/instant`. It makes no session and
sends no bot. Start no longer creates a Teams meeting. A refusal (a personal
account, a refused grant) prints the Worker's sentence and keeps every field,
so the member pastes the link from Teams. Eadwyn AI is sent only by Start,
only when the Worker says it may join this member's Teams calls, with a valid
link and consent recorded. When nothing can capture the call, the lightbox
says so without promising an import, and offers "Upload the recording"
(`/validate/record/calls/upload`) and "Paste the transcript".

**The round trip.** "Connect Microsoft Teams in Account" keeps the setup in this
tab's `sessionStorage` (`recordDraft.js`): the venture, the interview, Who's
name and company, the mode, the provider, the link and the length, under the
member's id, for an hour at most. Never consent, never a URL or a log. The
Teams page then offers "← Back to Record a session", whose only possible
target is `/validate/record?project_id=<n>[&interview=<n>]`
(`safeRecordReturn`). Record a session restores the setup once, for the same
member and venture, and forgets it; Cancel and a Start that lands forget it
too, and another member's read removes it. Coming back, connecting or
creating a link never starts anything.

**Known limit.** `sessionStorage` is per origin. Microsoft's callback returns to
`APP_URL`; a member who started on another host finds the setup again on the
host they started from, not on `APP_URL`.

**Verified.** `frontend/test/record_teams_d5811.test.mjs` (chip and offer
states, the draft's member, venture, expiry and allowlist, create fills only
the link, personal-account refusal, no capture, no consent, unreadable and
retry, focus refresh with a late reply, the same-tab round trip, Cancel and
Start forgetting it), plus the D1346 and D685 suites updated for Teams.
Rendered at desktop and phone widths in the PR.
