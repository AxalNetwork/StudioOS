## D5585 part 19 — WorkGraph releases: the public site record stays uncached, so there is nothing to purge (#5585)

- **Status:** Part 19 of #5585, 2026-11-28. This part is a decision only: no code, route, migration, binding, secret or
  flag changes. The owner chose it on 2026-11-28 ("keep no-store, skip it") over adding an edge cache with a
  token-backed purge.
- **Builds on:**
  - D5585-p3 §2: the Worker holds no token that can purge a cache (`CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN` is scoped to Workers
    Scripts).
  - D5585-p9: the public site-record route, `Cache-Control: no-store`, and its `release_site_read` rate limit.
  - D5585-p18: the cross-origin reads.
  - D5585-p7 §6, D5585-p9 §6 and later: "serving the site record at the edge, with cache headers and a purge, which
    needs a public bucket or a CDN token".

### 1. The decision

The public site record (`GET /api/public/release-sites/:tenant/:company/:site`) keeps `Cache-Control: no-store`. No
cache holds a copy, so a takedown or a new publication shows on the very next request, and there is nothing to purge.
"Edge purge" is closed for #5585. It is not left open as owner-gated.

### 2. Why

- **Nothing is cached at the edge today.** Cloudflare does not cache a Worker's own answers unless the Worker writes
  them to the Cache API or a cacheable fetch goes through it, and this route does neither. A purge would only be
  needed after adding a cache.
- **A cache would trade correctness for little.** A takedown has to stop the record at once (#5585's acceptance: "do
  not continue to serve disallowed content"). With a cache, a purge that failed or was delayed would keep serving a
  taken-down record until the cache time ran out. With `no-store` that window does not exist, and it needs no token,
  secret or audit of purge failures.
- **The read is cheap and bounded.** Each request costs a few indexed D1 statements and one R2 get (D5585-p9). The
  `release_site_read` bucket limits it to 60 a minute per IP. A customer site that needs to absorb heavy traffic
  should keep its own copy of the record (ids and digests only) and refresh it; D5585-p18 lets its browser read the
  record from a listed origin.

### 3. What would reopen it

Revisit this only on evidence that the read cost matters: the route's D1 and R2 cost, or its rate limit, showing in
production for a real site. Then the design to evaluate is:

- the Cache API with a short life (tens of seconds);
- a purge of the exact URL on every publish, re-pin and takedown;
- a token scoped to Zone › Cache Purge only, as a Worker secret the owner sets;
- an audited purge failure, whose stale window is bounded by that life.

### 4. Tests

There is nothing new to test. `work_graph_release_sites_5585.test.ts` already pins `no-store` on a served record, on a
refusal and on a withdrawn record.
