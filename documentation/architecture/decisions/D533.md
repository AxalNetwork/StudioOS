## D533 — one sector and country crosswalk for Research

**Research's four sector vocabularies and its free-text geography meet in one
service, `cloudflare-worker/src/services/researchTaxonomy.ts`, backed by
migration 376.** Issue #1080 (step 1 of 4), slot S03, part of the Research
rebuild #1077. No route, no page and no `api.js` change in this step; #1079,
#1081 and #1082 read through it once it merges.

**Why.** On production (read-only aggregates, 2026-10-04) the 300 directory
companies carried 154 distinct `sector` labels and 49 `country` names, and the
2,032 directory funds carried 620 distinct `sector_focus` values and 250 `hq`
strings. None of these met the 82 market-directory slugs or the 12
market-intelligence sectors (`services/market_intel/aggregator.ts`), so a
sector page could not list its companies or funds and there was no geography
at all.

**What migration 376 holds — the facts, not the rules.**
- `research_countries`: 66 countries, ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 `code`, `name`,
  `region` (Europe, North America, Latin America, Asia, Middle East, Africa,
  Oceania). Every country the directories name is seeded.
- `research_market_sectors`: each of the 82 market slugs and its
  market-intelligence sector, chosen by hand (`method = 'manual'`), or NULL
  where none fits closely enough to borrow its index (cloud infrastructure is
  not AI Infrastructure; security is not only cybersecurity). Fourteen are
  mapped; a NULL market's charts say "Not recorded".
- `research_taxonomy_overrides`: a hand correction for one raw value
  (`company_sector`, `fund_focus`, `company_country`, `fund_hq`). It wins over
  the rule; a row with `target = ''` pins a value unmapped. `method` can only
  be `'manual'`.

**How a raw value is mapped.** At read time, by exact match after
`normalizeLabel` (lower case, hyphens to spaces): `COMPANY_SECTOR_RULES`
(label → one market), `FUND_FOCUS_RULES` (each token of the list, split on " · " or ", " → markets),
`COUNTRY_ALIASES` (place → ISO code; a fund HQ is split on commas, "and", "/"
and parentheses, and maps to every country it names). Every answer is
`{raw, targets, method, rule, unmatched}`.

**Never guess.** No substring or similarity matching. A value no dictionary
names stays unmapped and its pieces are reported in `unmatched`. Places that
name no single country (`Global`, `Nordic countries`, `Cambridge`,
`Not recorded`) are listed as such; ambiguous cities (Durham, Richmond,
Manchester) are left out, and "Manchester, NH" resolves only as a whole. A
company country that names two countries is not resolved. Fund focus tokens
that are not markets (`Generalist`, `B2B`, `Consumer`) are listed as such so
they are not reported as unknown.

**Why rules in code and not rows.** Production holds funds the repo's seed
(365) does not, so a migration cannot list every raw value; rules applied at
read time cover whatever is there now and later, and the overrides table is
where a reviewer pins the exceptions without a deploy of new code.

**Verified.** `cloudflare-worker/test/research_taxonomy_d533.test.ts`, 16
tests, runs migration 376 on SQLite: every rule target is a real market slug,
every alias code a seeded country, every dictionary key already normalised;
the 82 slugs appear once with only the 12 sectors; each mapping rule, the
unknown-stays-unmapped cases, multi-country HQs, overrides winning and
pinning, the table checks, and re-running the migration. Mutation-checked:
substring matching, ignoring overrides, resolving a two-country company,
reporting `Generalist` as unknown, dropping normalisation, mapping a
not-a-country place, keeping an empty override target, and dropping
`unmatched` — eight mutants, all caught.

## Step 2 — the market and geography routes

`cloudflare-worker/src/routes/research_markets.ts` is mounted on `/api/research`
before `research.ts`, and its paths don't overlap with `research.ts`'s. Every
route is signed-in only and reads through the crosswalk; nothing is written.

- `GET /markets?q&sort=name|company_count|supplied_count&dir&limit&offset`:
  the 82 markets with their market-intelligence sector, `company_count` (the
  company directory in D1, mapped) and `supplied_count` (the directory's own
  figure), each labelled apart. Also returns `total` and `companies_unmapped`.
- `GET /markets/:slug`: companies, companies by country, funds focused on the
  market (largest first), signals whose tags map to it, and sub-markets (the
  raw company sector labels and fund focus tokens that land here, with
  counts).
- `GET /geographies?q&region&sort=name|company_count|fund_count&dir&limit&offset&with_data`:
  countries with company and fund counts. A fund whose HQ names two countries
  counts in both. Also returns `companies_unmapped` and `funds_unmapped`.
- `GET /geographies/:code`: companies by market, companies, funds
  headquartered there, and signals.

**Signals.** `signals.sector` is a broad stock-market sector ("Technology"),
so it is never matched to a market. A signal's `tags` go through the fund
focus dictionary (`mapSignalTags`). `advisor_note` is never selected
(S1, #1080). Archived signals are left out.

**The index series and the source mix** read live data only, through
`services/market_intel/liveRows.ts` (D538, #1078).
- `index.series` has one entry per dimension (demand, supply, capital, talent,
  research, sentiment), with up to six monthly points. The current month is
  marked `month_to_date`.
- A point's `change` is the written `delta_pct` when there is one. Otherwise
  it is the difference from the previous calendar month, and never across a
  missing month. `change_basis` says which.
- A point with no source (the aggregator's neutral 50) is not a reading.
- A dimension with no live point is `not_recorded` with its reason.
- A market with no market-intelligence sector says so.
- `index.source_mix` counts live rows by source. Stub rows and uncited rows
  are left out.

**Verified.** `cloudflare-worker/test/research_markets_d533.test.ts` has 10
tests on SQLite, with the tables cut from migrations 136, 228, 229 and 365
and 376 applied whole. They cover the sign-in gate on all four routes,
counts, unmapped totals, search through a bound parameter (an injection
string returns nothing), sorting, paging and `total`, clamped parameters,
404s, multi-country HQs, the region filter, and that `advisor_note` never
appears. Three more tests cover the index: live rows only, no neutral 50,
the change rules, the six-month window, month to date, and the December to
January wrap. All nine index mutants were caught. Across the route checks,
thirteen mutants were tried. Twelve were caught. The survivor was a
two-letter code guard that the bound lookup already made redundant, so the
guard was removed.

## The pages, after the canvas (#1080, 2026-10-05)

S1 posted the owner's approval of the canvas on 2026-10-05 ("wire the
wireframes"). The canvases are committed as `design/incoming/Research ·
Markets.dc.html` and `Research · Geography.dc.html` (D546). The sector page
moved to #1139 (S18). This part builds the rest.

**The API, completed.**
- **`GET /api/research/listed-companies`** reads `signal_companies` (migration
  136). It takes `q` (name, or a symbol prefix), `sector`, `country` (an ISO
  code, placed through the crosswalk), `sort` (`name`, `-name`, `market_cap`,
  `-market_cap`), `limit` and `offset`. Each row carries the symbol, name,
  exchange, country, region, sector, cap band, employee band and `updated_at`,
  and the response carries `total` and `as_of`. A company with no market cap
  sorts last in both directions. A failed read is a 503
  `listed_companies_unreadable`, never an empty list.
- **The currency rule** (`EUR_SOURCE`, as `researchFundDirectory.presentFund`
  applies it). Only the EuroTech list records a size in a known currency, so
  only its sizes are printed, as euros. Any other list's number is null with
  `size_reason`, and it ranks after every euro size, however large it is.
  EIF and EIFO are true, false (only when the fund names its LPs), or null.
  This applies in `/markets/:slug` and in `/geographies/:code`.
- **`GET /api/research/geographies/:code`, complete.**
  - `totals` and `as_of`: the whole directory's counts, so a page can print
    "2 of 300".
  - Companies by sector label, with the unlabelled counted.
  - The largest companies by recorded headcount, and those with none after,
    by name. Each carries migration 375's headcount and last round.
  - `fund_aggregates`:
    - how many funds have a size, a vintage, and a stated focus;
    - euro size bands;
    - how many have no size in a known currency;
    - the top focus keywords.
  - Signals that name the country, then those that name only its region.
    Each is marked with `match`, and each carries its build headline.
  - `demand` is `not_recorded`, with the reason.

**The pages.**

### Top movers (2026-10-06)

The card waited on #1092's Research overview. That PR closed unmerged, and
the overview reached main through #1151 (D536). The card now reads it.
- **What it reads:** the `heat` section of `GET /api/research/overview`.
  That is the live index rows only (D538), this month to date, each cell
  with its change on last month (`delta_pct`). The page asks with `top=1`,
  because it uses none of the overview's top-N lists.
- **What it shows,** to the Markets canvas: the four largest rises and the
  four largest falls across the 12 sectors and six dimensions. Each row
  links to its sector page, and the caption reads
  `market_intel_indexes · global · <month> against <month before>`.
- **What it leaves out:** a cell with no live row, or with no change
  recorded, is not ranked. It is not a change of 0. If nothing changed, the
  card reads Not recorded with that reason. One empty side says "No live
  index rose (or fell) on last month".
- **Its states:**
  - a failed read, or a heat section answering `unreadable`, reads
    Unreadable;
  - a `not_recorded` heat section prints the Worker's own reason, such as
    the month's index not being computed yet.
- **No colour on the change.** The ▲ or ▼ carries the sign, as on the
  canvas, so the page test's guard against a red index line still holds.

`frontend/test/research_top_movers_d533.test.mjs` has 6 tests and fails on
