### What the canvas decides

- The Feedback tab shows three counts with their period (open items, themes, shipped and not yet told), filters and sorts, themes ranked by item count, and the selected theme's items. Add feedback is the one primary action.
- One item shows its facts with Not recorded words, its theme counted by status, a Customer panel with a linked record and Plan, and Closing the loop. A typed name that matches no record reads Not linked, with Link to a customer or prospect.
- A theme's weight is who asked (distinct people), recurring revenue in the last 60 days from the askers' companies, askers with no record as Not recorded, and the roadmap items. No impact, rank or value score.
- Save as feedback is offered from an Inbox reply and from a Validate interview passage (with quote consent beside it).
- Close the loop lists each person's told state, with checkboxes only where usable, Mark told for several, and Write email per row. A person with no email, not shipped yet, or who did not ask to be told is named, not hidden.
- Eadwyn's theme placements open the GR-11 review (Use this theme or Discard), and only into themes already named.
- States follow GR-12 and Platform States: loading, Not recorded, list unreadable while Pipeline and Accounts work, one theme's weight unreadable, saving, save failed with edits kept, Mark told refused, and the advisor's read-only view. Phone 390 and dark are drawn.
