- **Why:** the owner asked for one professional tool in place of three surfaces (#2456). This decision is the integration: the addresses, the tabs, the blocks today's founder read carries, and where writes stay until the tool takes them over. The rest is eight slot tasks (§6).

### 1. Where it lives

- **Home** is `/raise/data-room`, the module page as it is: coverage, opens and the Files records. `DataRoomHomeNav` adds the tab row, and a line linking today's files tool.
- **The tabs** are `/raise/data-room/files`, `/access`, `/requests`, `/activity`, `/checklist` and `/preview`.
  - They arrive on the module's record route, `/raise/:slug/:id`, and `isDataRoomTab` makes CycleRoute draw `DataRoomTabPage` instead of a record. This is the pattern D906 and D1015 use.
  - A file's page is still its number, and `/new` is still Upload.
  - No route is added.
- **Today's two other surfaces stay** until part 8: `?mode=workspace` (the files tool, where every write still happens) and `?mode=access`.

### 2. What is read, and from where

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/cycles/raise/modules/data-room` | Home, as CycleRoute draws it |
| `GET /api/projects`, then `GET /api/data-room/:projectUid` | Files, Access and Activity. The venture's uid is taken from the founder's own list; a venture not in it is refused, never shown as an empty room |

Who can open a file is `dataRoomRead.js`'s `accessNow`, the predicate every investor read applies.

### 3. What is drawn today

- **Files:** every file with its folder, gate, tier, end date and links issued, plus who can open it now, or why nobody can. A link goes to the files tool to upload and manage.
- **Access:** every grant, in force, ended or revoked, with since, until, NDA, committed state and who granted it.
- **Activity:** the week's room opens, links issued and refusals as three figures, and the last fifty events, each as itself. A link issued is not a read, and a refusal is never an open.
- **Requests, Checklist and Preview:** "Not read here yet", each with its reason.
- **Not drawn now, and why:**
  - every write (parts 5 and 6, on parts 2 and 3);
  - versions and the founder's own download (part 2);
  - the matrix, Share and the NDA (part 6);
  - Activity's period chart and export (part 4, then 7);
  - Eadwyn's proposals (part 7, after #2431);
  - the moved notices (part 8).
