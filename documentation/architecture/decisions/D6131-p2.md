## D6131 part 2 — the investor profile keeps NFX sectors and exclusions (#6131)

Part 1 is `D6131.md`: the investor onboarding v2 frontend, which offers the
NFX taxonomy (`frontend/src/lib/nfxSectors.js`) for both the thesis sectors
and the excluded sectors. The owner answered D6128 Q2 with option A: the
server accepts the NFX list (`D6128-p2.md`).

**What was wrong.** `PUT /api/investor-profile/me`
(`cloudflare-worker/src/routes/investor_signals.ts`) kept only the eleven
labels of the old Settings vocabulary (`AI/ML`, `Fintech`, …) and dropped
anything else without a word. Every sector and exclusion an investor picked
in onboarding was therefore discarded, `sectors_json` stayed `[]`, and
`completed_at`, which needs at least one sector, was never set.

**The contract.**
- The Worker holds the NFX list verbatim and in the source's order, and a
  test holds it equal to the frontend's list, so the two cannot drift.
- Sectors and excluded sectors accept one vocabulary: the NFX list plus the
  eleven legacy labels. The legacy labels stay because rows saved before this
  hold them and the Settings cards ("My thesis", the thesis editor, Firm
  settings) still offer them and send the stored arrays back whole. Dropping
  them would erase a stored thesis on an unrelated Settings save.
- Matching stays exact (`fintech` is not `FinTech`); blanks, repeats and
  values outside the vocabulary are still dropped, as for stages and regions.
- A closed vocabulary caps an array at the vocabulary's own size instead of
  25, since onboarding sets no pick limit and the NFX list has 82 entries.
  Open lists keep the cap of 25.
- Unchanged: omitted fields keep their stored value and a field sent blank or
  empty is cleared (D6136, #6287); an omitted `contribute_to_signals` leaves
  consent as recorded (#6392); the completion rule is the same.

**Not done here.** The server does not refuse an excluded sector that is also
a thesis sector; the onboarding disables those chips and D6131 lets a legacy
overlap be removed, so refusing it would block Settings saves for such rows.
The Settings cards still offer the legacy labels rather than the NFX list; a
stored NFX sector is kept by their saves but is not drawn as a chip there.
The Research markets directory's owner-approved crosswalk (`THESIS_CROSSWALK`,
D7171 Q1 A) maps the legacy labels only. It is not extended here, so an NFX
sector (other than Robotics, which both lists share) is kept on the profile
and draws the thesis but matches no market until the crosswalk maps it;
`research_markets_directory_7171.test.mjs` pins that.
Remaining gates of #6131: the Eadwyn thesis-draft endpoint, profile-first
atomic completion and read-back, fund-application prefill and target-range
validation, and the final composed UI, phone and server acceptance.

No migration, binding, endpoint or configuration changes. Roll back the commit
to restore the old filter.
