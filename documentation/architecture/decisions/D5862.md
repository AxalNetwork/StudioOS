## D5862 — Grow: "From Facebook" on Distribution and Revenue and traction (#5862)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-22. Part 2 (screen) of #2399. Builds to the From Facebook canvas (D5861, boards GR-21 to GR-26, `design/incoming/Grow v2 From Facebook Page.dc.html`), with the owner's answers in D5861 part 2 (option A on each).
- **Reads and writes:** the Worker of D5859 (`parts.from_facebook`, sync, delete) and D5860 (the channel proposal). No Worker route, migration or read changed here.

### What is built

**`frontend/src/pages/cycles/GrowFromFacebook.jsx`.** The section is `id="from-facebook"`, mounted after From TikTok on `/grow/distribution` and `/grow/revenue-and-traction` (`defs/grow.jsx`). Its words live in `growFromFacebook.js`, which is pure.

**Header:**
- the Facebook mark;
- a source line: the Page's name, who serves it, and "From Facebook, as of <date>";
- Sync. One primary per screen (D5861 part 2, option A): Sync is violet unless a proposal waits on Accept.

**Distribution:**
- **Three figures**, as label and number:
  - Views: the days the read carries, with how many of 28 were recorded;
  - Unique viewers: on the newest day, because D5859 counts them per day and they never add up;
  - Post engagements: the same days as Views.
- **Daily rows, newest first:** a table on a desktop and day cards on a phone. Each recorded figure has a Delete.
- **The proposal:** "Link your Facebook Page to a Distribution channel?", with Accept and Dismiss. Dismiss shows "Dismissed. It will not be proposed again." A dismissed proposal is not drawn: the read leaves it out (D5860).

**Revenue and traction:** "Audience, not revenue". Followers now, with the day, its change since the snapshot before, and a Delete. It is never revenue, never a KPI and never part of a money figure.

**Delete:** a small dialog (GR-21), a bottom sheet on a phone. Focus starts on Cancel. It reads "<figure> · <day> · <value>. It stays deleted after the next sync." The Worker keeps it deleted (D5859 §3).

### Who sees what

The read decides, through `can_sync`, `can_delete`, `can_link` and `proposal.can_decide`, never a role read here.
- A reader sees the figures and their source, and "Reads only": no Sync, Delete, Accept or Dismiss.
- A support session gets none of them, because the read sets them false.
- Connect Facebook is shown only to a member who may link.

### States

| State | Drawn |
|---|---|
| Loading | "Loading From Facebook…", while the module read has not arrived (`loading={!data}`). |
| Empty | nothing, once the read has no part. |
| Not connected (`not_linked`) | "Connect Facebook and choose a Page to see its followers, views and engagement here.", and Connect Facebook for a member who may link. The link stays in the app. |
| Not recorded | the read's reason, "Nothing has been read from Facebook yet." |
| Partly synced | "Some months were not read yet. Sync again to finish." |
| Unreadable | "Unreadable: the Facebook figures could not be read", Try again, and the rest of the module loads. |
| Sync failed | "Facebook could not be read. Nothing changed." Sync becomes Try again, and the last good figures stay. |
| Reconnect | `sign_in_again` or `facebook_page_not_granted`: "Facebook needs to be reconnected. Reconnect with Facebook to continue.", with the link. |
| Rate limited | "Facebook is limiting requests. Try again later. Nothing changed." |
| Disconnected | the connection that served the venture is gone; the last figures read stay. |

A figure Facebook did not give reads **Not recorded** with its reason. A recorded 0 stays 0. Nothing is ever 0 for a missing figure.

### API

`frontend/src/lib/api/cycleWrites.js` (`cyclesWrite.grow`) adds four methods, each to a mounted route in `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/cycle_grow.ts`:
- `syncFacebook` → `POST /facebook/sync`;
- `deleteFacebookFact` → `DELETE /facebook/facts/:id?project=`;
- `acceptFacebookProposal` → `POST /facebook/proposals/:id/accept`;
- `dismissFacebookProposal` → `POST /facebook/proposals/:id/dismiss`.

### Departures from the canvas

| Canvas | Here | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Distribution's four figures include Followers | Three: Views, Unique viewers, Post engagements | Distribution's read carries no follower count (D5859 §2); followers are Revenue and traction's. |
| "October 2026, to 21 Oct": the month so far | The days the read carries, up to 28, ending at the newest recorded day | D5859 serves 28 days, not a calendar month. |
| A Delete on each of the four headline figures | A Delete on each daily figure; the totals have none | The store holds daily facts; a total is a sum, not a fact to delete. |
| Revenue and traction's 12-month followers line, with month rows | Followers now, with its change since the snapshot before | The read carries the latest two follower snapshots, not a series. A 12-month line needs a read that #2399 does not have yet. |
| Choose a Page in the part | Connect Facebook, to the connector page | Linking a Page (`PUT /facebook/page`) needs the member's chosen Pages, which this read does not carry. The connector page holds that choice (D5292). |
