## D1382 — Start Call opens the meeting at once on a computer, and saves the call while the founder joins

- **Status:** asked for by the owner on 2026-10-29 ("very slow … until the call starts"). Built by S21.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1362: one Start for a call;
  - D1377: the meeting app on a phone;
  - D1378: the bot's start is off the save.
- **Migration:** none.

### Why

Start Call opened a blank tab inside the press, so the browser allowed it. The tab was pointed at the meeting only after every save had answered:
- the interview and its stage;
- the Zoom or Calendar read;
- the session;
- Eadwyn AI's dispatch.

The founder watched a blank tab for all of that. For Zoom the wait also delayed the bot, because Zoom issues Eadwyn AI's pass only once the founder is in the meeting (D886).

### What changed

- **On a computer, the meeting opens first** (`RecordLightbox.jsx`, `startCall`). As soon as the meeting link is known (pasted, or just created), the tab Start opened is pointed at it. The interview, session and Eadwyn AI are then saved while the founder joins.
- **A refused save leaves the meeting open,** because it is the founder's own call. The dialog says what was not saved. A tab that was never pointed at a meeting (for example, when creating the meeting failed) is still closed.
- **A phone is unchanged:** the Zoom or Meet app opens only once the call is saved. Handing the page to the app would stop the save.

### Verified

- **New test** (`record_call_create_meeting_d1346`): with the Zoom save still in flight, the tab already shows the meeting. It is pointed once, and stays open after the save answers.
- **Updated test:** a refused Meet attach that may not fall back to the bot shows the refusal and leaves the founder's meeting open. Before, the tab was closed.
- **Mutation check:** removing the early open fails two tests.
- **All 284 Record a Session frontend tests pass.**
