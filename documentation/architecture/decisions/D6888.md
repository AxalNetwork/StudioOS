### The material is the statement, not a reading of the book

`scope_key` is the statement's date. Only a completed quarter-end (a quarter-end before the Worker's date) is accepted, as the sheet's chooser lists only those.

The gather does not compute a figure. It calls the two reads the sheet itself makes for that date, in-process with the caller's own headers, the way `admin_insights_export.ts` calls its page's read:

- `GET /api/positions/analytics?as_of=<date>`: page 1's summary (NAV, paid-in, distributed, total value, TVPI, DPI, RVPI, IRR), its mark coverage and its positions at cost (with no mark, and with a mark that cannot be used).
- `GET /api/positions?as_of=<date>`: page 2's rows that the statement marks "Funded after", using the statement's own rule (`statementModel.js` `fundedAfter`), so the note never names a company page 2 does not mark.

So the session, the position scope (`positionScope`, the active company included) and the arithmetic are the statement's own. `statementCoverMaterial` formats each figure as the sheet does (`money`, `multiple`, `rate`, `day` from `portfolioModel.js`) and names the page it is printed on. An IRR the read cannot define is "Unavailable", never a number.

Nothing else reaches the model:

- no other quarter-end;
- no mark history;
- no fee or carry (the statement is gross, and net returns are Not recorded);
- no forecast input.

The instruction tells the model to compute nothing, mention no other date, not forecast, not grade performance and not advise. A date that is not a completed quarter-end, a read that failed or a date with no book gathers [], and the route answers `nothing_to_draft` (409) before any model call.

### Nothing is sent and nothing is kept

The canvas says nothing is kept, so the surface sets `keep: false`. The route returns the text (`item.uid` null, `kept: false`) and writes no `research_zone_drafts` row: there is nothing to list, accept or discard on the Worker.

The run's usage receipt (`ai_usage_logs`: cost and tokens, not the text) is written as for every run. In the card:

- Copy puts the text on the clipboard.
- Edit hands it to the sheet's cover-note box through `onEdit`.
- Discard forgets it in the browser.

None of the three calls the Worker.

### When Eadwyn is off

`assist_off` (403), `ai_paused`, `draft_unavailable` and a failed request all read as "Eadwyn is unavailable. The statement does not need it.", with the canvas's "Write your own note in your email." beneath. Some answers keep their own words instead:

- a guard that withheld the draft (D770);
- an empty allowance (402);
- `nothing_to_draft` ("No position is recorded on or before this date, so there is no statement to cover.").

### The rail option is a card, not a band

Every other investor draft option is a `<ZoneDraft>` band, which stores a pending draft and has Accept. This one keeps nothing, so it cannot be a band. The card names its surface and option on its root (`data-draft-surface`, `data-rail-option`). The rail inventories read it from there:

- `rail_options_investor_d772`;
- `rail_options_run_d779` and part 3;
- `rail_pages_investor_d772`.

`rail_options_run_d779` leaves it out of the band-run loop. Its run is `statement_cover_note_6888.test.mjs`'s to test.
