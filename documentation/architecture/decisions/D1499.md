## D1499 — The Grid v2: one geometry, the 17 structures, areas on a card, and `GET /api/decks/:id/grid`

- **Status:** Built by the S14 session (#3749, deck v2 A2, part of #3716; 2026-11-01), in two parts. Part 1 (#3925) is the Worker, the catalogue mirror and the client. Part 2, after S1's #3885 merged, moves the editor's stage, Present and print onto the one geometry, swaps `deckV2Adapter.grid` and adds ⋯ › Slide size.
- **Builds on:** D898 (the 12 × 6 grid, `LAYOUTS`, `sanitizeModel`, `modelProblems`), `designSystem.ts` (`SLIDE`, `FRAME`, `boxOf`) and D896 (bindings). It amends the D1472 spec reserved on #3716, which never reached main.
- **Migration:** none. Structures and areas live in the version's `slides` JSON, and the aspect lives in `theme_json`.
- **Owner decision:** A · Card view versus slides is still pending. S1's pick B stands: the 16:9 slide is canonical, and a read-only responsive card view stacks areas in `order`.

### Why

Every area must be drawn from one geometry on screen, in Present and in print, so that text capacity, image sizes, PPTX mapping and the fidelity tests agree. Today the editor lays blocks out with a CSS grid, `gap-2 p-[3%]` on screen and `gap-6` in print (`BlockGrid.jsx`). Neither matches `FRAME`, so boxes are tens of pixels off at 1920. Deck v2 also needs a card to have a **structure**, which divides the grid into **areas** that the founder selects and generates into.

### What was decided

1. **The geometry is `boxOfAt(range, aspect)`** (`services/decks/structures.ts`).
   - At 16:9 (1920 × 1080) it is `designSystem.boxOf`, exactly, for every one of the 1,638 ranges on the grid.
   - At 4:3 (1440 × 1080) it keeps the same 12 × 6 grid and the same margins (112 / 96 / 88, 24 px gutter), with narrower cells.
   - The baseline is 8 px. An area's inset is 0 to 4 baseline steps.
   - The frontend's `gridModel.js` has the same `boxOf`, `cellAt` and `FRAME`. It adds `cellAtPoint`, the inverse used for clicks, and `stageScale`, which scales the fixed stage to fit its box.
2. **The 17 structures of the final canvas (pd10)**, in its order:
   - Full, Title band + body, Halves, Split 7/5, Split 5/7;
   - Sidebar 4/8, Sidebar 8/4, Thirds, Hero + 3, Quarters, 2×2;
   - Big number, Team, Timeline band, Z-pattern, Overlay, Appendix table.

   The issue's title says 15; plan §6.2 and the canvas say 17. Each structure has an id, a name, a use and its areas, in reading order. **Hero + 3** and **Big number** carry `recommended: ['seed']` ("Recommended for Seed").

   Two readings of plan §5.1's table:
   - **Overlay:** its photo is the card's background (`background: 'image'`), not an area. Its one text area (c1–6 × r4–6) therefore overlaps nothing, and the no-overlap rule holds for every structure.
   - **Appendix table:** it has a 12×1 title band above its 12×5 table, so the slide still has a title.
3. **An area** is `{ id, c1, c2, r1, r2, order, inset, align, role? }`.
   - It covers whole cells of the 12 × 6 grid.
   - Areas never overlap, and a card holds at most 12.
   - `areasProblem` names the first fault in one sentence: a half cell, a range off the grid, an overlap, a thirteenth area, a repeated id, an inset out of range or an unknown alignment.
4. **A card keeps `structure` and `areas`.**
   - `sanitizeModel` keeps a known structure id, or `custom`, and keeps the areas only when they are sound.
   - `modelProblems` refuses a save with an unknown structure or unsound areas, with the sentence, rather than trimming them silently.
   - `block.grid` stays the stored truth for blocks.
5. **Old decks open unchanged.**
   - A card without areas reads `areasOf(card)`, which uses `deriveAreas(blocks)`: one area per block, in reading order (top to bottom, then left to right). An overlapping block gets no area, and an empty card is one Full area.
   - `deriveAreas` round-trips: the areas derived from a layout's blocks derive the same areas again.
   - Nothing is backfilled.
6. **The aspect is the deck theme's.** `sanitizeTheme` keeps `aspect: '4:3'` and leaves 16:9, the default, out, so a theme saved before v2, and its content hash, are unchanged.
7. **`GET /api/decks/:id/grid`** (`routes/deck_grid.ts`, mounted with the other deck routes) is read by whoever may read the deck, through `projectOwned`, the rule `GET /:id` uses. It answers:
   - the deck's aspect, slide, frame, cell, baseline and grid;
   - the 17 structures;
   - each card's `{ index, structure, derived, areas }`.

   Every area carries its `box` from `boxOfAt`. The route writes nothing.
8. **The client** is `frontend/src/lib/api/deckGrid.js` (`deckGridApi.grid(deckId)`), with one line in `api.js` (D527).

### Part 2: the page on one geometry

9. **Every surface places areas with `gridModel.placeOf(range, aspect)`.** It is `boxOf`'s box as a share of the slide (`left`, `top`, `width` and `height` in %).
   - **What it covers:**
     - The editor's stage (`GridStage.jsx`): blocks, free areas, the marquee, the drag ghost and "Show the Grid" (its safe frame, 72 cells and 8 px baseline).
     - Present and the print page (`BlockGrid.jsx`).
   - **What is gone:** `GridStage`'s `LAYER` / `TRACKS` / `FRAME` CSS grid (`gap-2 p-[3%]`) and `BlockGrid`'s `gap-2 p-[3%]` on screen and `gap-6 p-[3%]` in print.
   - **Why percentages and not a CSS transform:** a box at a share of the slide is exactly the fixed 1920 × 1080 (or 1440 × 1080) stage's box scaled by `stageScale`. Each surface's type keeps the size it already sets, instead of shrinking with a transform.
   - **Measured:** in Chromium (Playwright), 360 ranges at each of 16:9 at 1000 and 733 px and 4:3 at 900 and 517 px landed within 0.22 px of `boxOf × scale`.
10. **The face is the deck's slide.** Its aspect ratio is `ratioOf(theme.aspect)` in the editor and Present. A studio deck at 4:3 prints on a 1440 × 1080 page (`StudioPrintCard`, and `@page` in `PitchDeckPrintPage.jsx`). Every other deck stays at 1920 × 1080.
11. **The pointer is read back onto the slide.** `gridSelection.cellAt(x, y, faceBox, model, { aspect })` maps the point into slide pixels and uses `gridModel.nearestCell`, the inverse of `boxOf`:
    - a point in a gutter belongs to the nearer track;
    - a point in a margin belongs to the edge track.

    The Playwright check found the centre of every cell, clicked, read back as that cell: 72 of 72 at both aspects.
12. **The structure is the area source** (plan §4.1).
    - `gridSelection.areasOf` offers as free areas the structure's areas that no block touches, in the structure's order. A card's own `areas` win over the catalogue's.
    - A card with no structure, `custom` without areas, or an unknown id keeps the row-by-row free ranges, so old decks open unchanged.
    - `structureAreas(card)` is the read.
13. **`deckV2Adapter.grid` is swapped.** It is `through(() => deckGridApi.grid(id), 3749)`, and `SWAPPED = ['grid']`.
    - The row stays in `SURFACES`, so the plan's table still reads whole.
    - `availability('grid')` greys nothing.
    - `deckGrid.js` is the one `lib/api` client the seam imports. The seam test pins this, and drops `/grid` and `deckGrid.js` from its "not on main" list.

    Part 1's client on main had tripped that list once #3885 merged. This swap is what clears it.
14. **⋯ › Slide size** (`SizeSheet` in `DeckSheets.jsx`) is an in-page sheet with two choices: Widescreen 16:9 (1920 × 1080) and Standard 4:3 (1440 × 1080).
    - **How it saves:** it writes the theme's `aspect` through `withAspect`, the Worker's `sanitizeTheme` rule. 4:3 is written; 16:9 is left out; an untouched brand theme stays `null`. Like a theme, the change is held until a version is saved, and the save summary says "The slide size becomes Standard 4:3 (1440 × 1080)."
    - **The Theme sheet keeps the aspect.**
    - **No block moves to the notes.** pd3c's "[2] blocks no longer fit" cannot happen, because 4:3 keeps the 12 × 6 grid and its margins, and only the columns narrow.
    - **Amended by D1554 (#3801):** no block loses a cell, but a narrower column holds fewer characters. So a text block that fits at 16:9 can overflow at 4:3, and the switch now moves what no longer fits to the speaker notes, with pd3c's notice and one Undo.

The structure picker and the divider drag stay A4's (#3765).
