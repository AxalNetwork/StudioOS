### Decisions

- **The page owns its layer.** A cycle def may now carry `homes` (`slug → (data, ctx) → { sub, body, Bands }`). `CycleModulePage` and `PhoneModulePage` take `home` and `sub`: with a home they draw `home({ card, records, recordsStatus, recordsMessage, add, rowHref, … })` in place of the generic chart, One next thing (phone) and the records table, and the header's line is the home's. The refused and unreadable module states are the pages' own, unchanged. `CycleRoute` gains no import (D5615): it reads `def.homes` through `fnOf` and wraps the bands in the home's `Bands` (`PlainBands`, a fragment, elsewhere). Only Fundraising readiness has a home; #5081 can give the other mature tools one the same way.
- **Product-market fit is not an area.** Its row (`id: 'pmf'`) is left out of the table, the priorities, the coverage and the gain. The raise kit tile still reads the card's bars as the Worker sends them.
- **Areas are the Worker's seven, not the board's eight.** RA-16's sample lists Market; the Worker scores no Market area (`READINESS_ITEMS`), so the table shows the seven it reads, and its count says seven.
- **No target per area, so no Worker change.** RA-16 draws no founder-set target for any area: its one "Not recorded · no target set" is the Waitlist inside Traction's evidence. No store holds an area target, so every area reads "Target: Not recorded · no target set", true for each. The module read does not carry the Waitlist bar (it is Validate's `metric_targets`), so the Traction row does not claim one either way. No write is added next to `PUT /readiness/:key`, and no migration.
- **Gap routing is D5071's.** Every action is the row's `fix_href`, accepted only as an address inside the app (`appPath`). A raise kit gap is now its area's action, so the PMF section's gaps are its own and the All / PMF / Raise kit filter is gone.
- **Where the two Eadwyn bands live.** RA-16 is not silent: it draws "AI gap analysis · Eadwyn’s reading; proposals only" collapsed under Detail on this page, and the room band is labelled "Gap analysis". So both bands stay in the `fundraising-readiness` drafts table and draw inside that disclosure (`ReadinessBands`), with the PMF reading. They were not moved to Data room and Legal: moving them would also re-key their rail page in the Worker (`FOUNDER_DRAFT_PAGES` in `routes/research.ts`, `railMatrix.json`), which this issue keeps out of scope. "Explain the clauses" is the one RA-16 does not name; it stays here with the room band rather than move without its rail page.
