### 1. One address per call, and the old one counted

- **The forward.** A sent call lives at `/raise/capital-relationships/calls/:uid` (D1221, CLS8). `/raise/calls/:uid` now forwards there through `ParamRedirect`, which keeps the uid, the query and the hash. It sits behind the same guard as the page it replaces (`labRoles(['admin', 'founder'])`).
- **The count.** `/raise/calls/:uid` is in `REDIRECT_ROUTES` (`services/redirectRoutes.ts`), after `/raise/calls`, so the address-bar beacon counts it as `redirect:/raise/calls/:uid` until #2319 retires it on its count. It is not `keep`: no email carries it after this change.
- **Every link that built the old address now builds the new one:**
  - the counsel-approval notice (`services/roundCalls.ts`, `round_call_counsel_approved`);
  - the author's link on a sent update (`SentUpdatePage.jsx`);
  - the founder's list of round calls (`RoundCallPage.jsx`).

  Only old links and bookmarks reach the redirect.

### 2. The Calls history left Overview

`pages/cycles/CallsHistory.jsx` drew a "Calls for Capital" section on Capital relationships' Overview, with a row line reading "committed $100,000 of $1,500,000". Calls for Capital is the Calls tab now (D1221), and a call's amounts are Indicated, never committed (D653). The component and its mount are removed, and Overview begins with the receipt matches (D752).

The Worker still sends `parts.calls` on the module read, because the Calls tab reads it (D1223).

### 3. Indicated everywhere

No founder view of a call puts "committed" on screen any more. The words in `callsModel.js`, `CallsTab.jsx`, `RaiseCallPage.jsx`, `RoundCallPage.jsx`, `RoundCallSheet.jsx` and `SentUpdatePage.jsx` are checked outside comments. A field name such as `committed_at` is not a word on screen, and passes.
