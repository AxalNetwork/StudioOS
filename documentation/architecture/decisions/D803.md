## D803 — The Subsidiary Licence Agreement master template: sections 1–8, 19 and 22, merging the ledger and D797's terms, a draft subject to counsel

- **Status:** filed by S1 for the owner on 2026-10-20 (#2240; part of D795, #2228). Built by S25. **The text is a draft, subject to counsel.**
- **Basis:** D797's branch (#2242) on `main` at `a44b65242a`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Depends on:** D797 (#2230): its terms (`services/licenceTerms.ts`, migration 512).

### What was there

- **HQ instantiates a licence's contract from any master template** (`POST /api/admin/licences/:uid/contract`, D110). It stores the rendered body in `licence_contracts` (migration 259), leaves a field the licence cannot fill visible, and lists it in `unfilled_fields`.
- **No master template was a territory licence agreement.** The Licences canvas's "Subsidiary licence agreement" (board L3, `design/incoming/Licences Page.dc.html`, on #2228's branch) had no source.

### What was decided

1. **The template is `templates/legal/subsidiary_licence_agreement_v1.md`,** with the section numbers the canvas's Contract index and the Terms tab's chips cite.
   - The sections are §1 Parties, §2 Territory and exclusivity, §3 Term and renewal, §4 Fees, §5 Deal terms, §6 Seats, §7 Brand and platform, §8 Compliance and reporting, §19 Termination and §22 Governing law.
   - Its clauses are plain and standard. Where a choice is the owner's or counsel's, the text holds a visible bracket instead: the royalty rate (§4.7), the promotions ceiling (§4.5), whether each revenue stream takes its own share (§4.2), the statement dispute window and the audit notice (§8), and the forum (§22.2).
   - The two licensors are those the canvas draws: Axal VC Holdings LLC for the platform IP, and Axal VC Management LLC for the trademark and brand.
   - The first line says it is a draft subject to counsel and not for signature.
2. **Its merge fields are `licence.<name>` and `terms.<key>`.**
   - `licence.<name>` is the ledger (migration 187), as `services/licenceContract.ts` already formats it, for example `licence.annual_fee`.
   - `terms.<key>` is D797's terms, under their own keys, for example `terms.carry_share_bps`. The template states all 22 of them.
   - The flat names (`annual_fee`) stay for the templates that use them.
3. **The instantiate route reads D797's terms into the merge** (`termValues`). Each kind reads as the issue asks:
   - money in the licence's currency;
   - basis points as a percentage, to two decimals (25 → "0.25%");
   - days as "[n] days", and business days and months the same way;
   - the usage report day as "the 5th day";
   - each enum and yes/no term in the words its sentence needs;
   - the licensor entity by its legal name.

   A term HQ has not recorded stays `{{terms.<key>}}` in the stored body and is listed in `unfilled_fields`, so the canvas draws it as "[not filled]". So does every term when the store cannot be read, or when migration 512 is not applied.
4. **The seed is migration 514,** the next free number after 513.
   - It inserts the file's text verbatim, its sorted merge fields, version 1, `gp`, and `doc_kind` `agreement`, as migration 471 inserts catalog rows.
   - It inserts only when the slug is absent, so a re-run, or an admin's row under the slug, is left as it is.
   - The slug is in no earlier migration and not in the baseline.
5. **It is a draft subject to counsel.**
   - `LEGAL_REVIEW.md` lists it under "Needed (D803)", in §D. Counsel must confirm the two-licensor structure, the royalty clause, whether the non-compete is enforceable per territory, the governing law and forum, and the data-processing roles.
   - HQ's library marks it "Draft — subject to counsel", desktop and phone. A new set, `COUNSEL_REVIEW_SLUGS` (`services/templateFormat.ts`), marks templates that have a body counsel has not reviewed, beside `COUNSEL_DRAFT_SLUGS`, which marks stubs.
   - The licence contract picker carries the same flag. The template leaves the set when counsel signs it off.

### Not changed

- The instantiate route's other rules: the template version is read from the library, the prior contract is superseded, and an event is logged.
- The send route. Nothing stops HQ sending a draft subject to counsel; `LEGAL_REVIEW.md` and the library's mark say it must not be sent before sign-off.
- No other template's text.
- The other machine-drafted templates (`dpa_v1.md` and the rest marked **NEW DRAFT** in `LEGAL_REVIEW.md`) are not added to `COUNSEL_REVIEW_SLUGS` here. S1 may add them.
