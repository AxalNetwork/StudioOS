### 1. Authors (ART9, ART10)

- **The tiles:** the read's summary:
  - authors with a published article;
  - new authors in the last 90 days;
  - articles waiting from first-time authors;
  - authors at the weekly limit, with the cap as its sub-line ("3 submissions in 7 days").

  A tile whose figure is missing says Not recorded.
- **The toolbar:** Search an author, Profile, "Has articles waiting" and the sort (most published, most waiting, most views, most followers, last submitted, name), with the range ("1–25 of 37"). There are 25, 50 or 100 rows a page.
- **The columns:** Author, Profile, Published, Waiting, Rejected, Approval rate, Views all time, Followers, Last submitted, and This week ("2 of 3", or "3 of 3 · at the limit"), then ⋯.
- **Never a 0 for something unknown:**
  - Followers whose read failed say "Couldn’t be read", and they sort last, not as 0.
  - An author with no decision yet reads "No decisions yet", not 0%.
  - A count the read does not carry is Not recorded.
- **The row menu:** Open author, Open account (Accounts) at `/admin/accounts/all/:id`, and Open public author page at `/authors/:id`.
  - **"Show their articles in the Pipeline" is left out.** The Pipeline has no author filter with its own address yet, and that filter is D1295's. Once it exists, the item is one link.
- **The author drawer (ART10):** an in-page Lightbox titled "[name], author". Its facts come from the authors read:
  - writing since;
  - published, waiting and rejected;
  - approval rate;
  - median time to a decision;
  - followers;
  - this week;
  - views all time.

  **Published per month and the review history are not drawn.** The authors read gives each author's totals, not each article. The drawer says so in a line rather than drawing an empty chart. Drawing them needs a per-author read of articles by month, which is a later slot.
- **The states:** reading; couldn't be read, with Try again ("not the same as there being none"); no author yet; and no author matching the filters, with Clear all.

### 2. Performance (ART11, ART12)

- **The period:** "Last 12 weeks" sends no range, so the Worker's own default applies: the twelve Monday weeks to today. "Last 30 days", "Last 90 days" and "Last 12 months" send `from` and `to`, ending today (UTC).
  - **"All time" is left out.** D1294 refuses a range over 400 days.
- **The tiles:**
  - **Published**, over the period.
  - **Views:** the sum of the weeks the Worker sent. Before the first daily row it says Not recorded, with the Worker's `views_reason`. From the week the count started, it says "Counted from [day]".
  - **Median time to a decision**, with the number of decisions timed. It says Not recorded when none was timed.
  - **Followers told**, with the publishes that recorded a reach.

  "Median read time" and "Unique readers" are not tiles. The read has no per-read time, and unique readers are in its `not_recorded` list, so they are listed with the rest of that list (below).
- **The charts:** recharts bars, each with a sentence that says it and "View as table":
  - Published per week;
  - Submitted and decided per week (Submitted, Approved, Changes requested, Rejected);
  - Views per week;
  - Views by sector.

  In the table, the week the count started in reads "(counted from mid-week)".
- **Before the daily store,** Views per week, Views by sector and Top articles each say Not recorded with the Worker's reason, never an empty chart or a zero. After it, a week with no read is a real 0, as D1294 sends it.
- **Top articles:** Rank, Title, Author, Published, Views and Read time, from the daily count. "Views per day" and "Followers told" per article are not columns: the read carries neither per article.
- **Not recorded yet:** unique readers, how far people read, where readers came from, and newsletter clicks, each with the Worker's line, under "These need new tracking. Until then, they are left out rather than shown as zero."
- **The states:** a failed read says "Could not be read. This is not a claim that nothing happened." with Try again, and the period stays usable.

### 3. The phone (ART18)

ART18 has four frames. This part builds the phone for its own two tabs:
- **Authors:** below `md`, cards replace the table, and the filters and sort sit in a bottom sheet (`Lightbox phone="sheet"`) behind "Filters and sort", with "Show N authors".
- **Performance:** one column, and the top articles become a numbered list.
- **Targets:** every control is at least 44px below `md`.

**Not here:** the phone's Pipeline, Filters sheet and Review frames are D1295's (#3391), and its Calendar agenda is D1296's (#3392). Both were in progress when this was built and own those files.
