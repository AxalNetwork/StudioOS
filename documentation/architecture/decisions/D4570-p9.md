## D4570 part 9 — Partner workspace: the 43 `/api/partner` ledger routes, tested and verified (#4570)

#4570 slice 9, after `D4570-p8.md`. It covers every `unreviewed` ledger route under `/api/partner`, the largest
family left: delivery (`partner_delivery.ts`), offers (`partner_offers.ts`) and product testing (`testing.ts`). No
product code changes: every gate was already right, and this slice proves it.

### How the firm is decided

The caller's firm is read from their own account, never from the request: `requireAuth`, then
`requirePartnerRole` (partner or admin), then `requirePartnerProfile`, which loads `partners` by the account's
`users.partner_id`. A record is then reached only through one of these, each comparing the row's firm with the
caller's:

| Record | Gate |
| --- | --- |
| an engagement (`/engagements/:engagementId/...`) | `requireOwnEngagement` (`engagements.partner_id`) |
| a milestone, blocker, deliverable, seat or status report (`/:id`) | `ownChild`, which joins the row to its engagement |
| a seat holder or an hours row's person | `requireOwnHolder` (`users.partner_id`), 400 otherwise |
| a surface, proof item or fit rule | `ownSurface`, `ownProofItem`, `ownFitRule` (`partner_id`); a consent must also belong to its item |
| a testing programme, its logo, applicants or applications | `ownProgramme` (`owner_kind = 'partner'` and `owner_partner_id`) |

The three `/offers/proof-consents/:token/...` routes are the client's (D4672). They need no account: the link's
random token (`newUid`) is the capability. The firm cannot answer its own request (`requester_cannot_confirm`).

### What the suite proves

`cloudflare-worker/test/authz_partner_d4570.test.ts` mounts the three routers on the full schema. Every record
belongs to firm A, and the suite sends each of the 40 firm-owned routes a body its owner could send (a real PNG for
the logo upload), so the gate is what stops a refusal, not the body.

- **Another firm's partner** gets 404 on every route, naming nothing (no title, summary, claim or firm name).
- **A founder, investor or advisor** gets 403.
- **A partner or admin account with no firm** gets 400 ("No partner profile attached to your account").
- **No credentials or a disabled account** gets 401.
- **No refusal changes anything**: the 20 tables these routes write, and the logo store, are compared before and after.
- **The firm's own partner passes every gate**, and its reads carry the record.
- **A seat or hours row** naming someone of another firm is refused with 400 and writes nothing.
- **A consent link**: an unknown, oversized (101 characters) or altered token answers 404 `consent_not_found` on all three
  routes, whoever calls, and writes nothing. The valid link's claim page shows the firm's name and a masked address,
  never the full address or any other record of the firm. Codes being single-use, short-lived, limited to five tries and
  rate-limited per request stays proven by `partner_proof_consent_integrity_d4672.test.ts`.
- **Completeness**: the first test reads the ledger and fails if a route under `/api/partner` is missing from the
  suite or is not marked verified by it.

Mutation check: 10 gate mutations, 8 caught, 2 equivalent. Caught: the firm comparison in `ownChild`,
`requireOwnEngagement`, `ownSurface`, `ownFitRule`, `ownProofItem`, `requireOwnHolder`, `ownerWhere`'s
`owner_partner_id`, and `requester_cannot_confirm`. Equivalent:
- removing `requirePartnerRole`, because `requirePartnerProfile` makes the same partner-or-admin check, so a founder
  still gets 403;
- removing the token length bound, because a 101-character token matches no row anyway. The bound only saves a
  query.

### Hardening, not findings

Neither is exploitable as the code stands.

- The logo upload reads the file before the ownership check, so another firm sending no file gets 400 `empty_file`
  rather than 404. It names nothing. With a file it gets 404, like every other route.
- A partner account with no firm gets 400, not 403. The error is the generic one `requirePartnerProfile` throws for
  every route that uses it, so changing it belongs with that helper, not this slice.
