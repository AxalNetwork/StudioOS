## D4812 — HQ › Fund administration v2: one fund scope, six workspaces, attention first (#4812)

- **Status:** canvas landed 2026-11-07 (`design/incoming/Fund Admin v2 Page.dc.html`, `design/incoming/Fund Admin v2 · Boards.dc.html`, boards FA01 to FA14). Part 1 built 2026-11-08 and part 2 (the money-action flow) 2026-11-09 (below); the remaining boards are later parts.
- **Builds on:** `fund_admin.ts` (`/access`, `/overview`, `/lps`), `fundAdminAccess.ts` (`FUND_ACTS` tiers), `fundAdminOverview.ts`, `funds.ts` (capital-call preview and issue, ledger), `fund_ledger.ts`, `fund_reporting.ts`, `fund_fees.ts`, `fund_notices.ts`, `fund_booklets.ts`, `fund_performance.ts`.

### What the canvas decides

- **One fund-scope control** replaces the segmented links and the second select. A one-fund workflow asks for a fund explicitly.
- **Six workspaces** replace nine peer tabs: Overview, Investors (LP roster, commitments, applications), Capital (calls, receipts, distributions), Portfolio (NAV, positions, marks), Reporting (reports, fees, notices, statements), Banking & Books (transactions, reconciliation, accounts, books). Funds become context plus a fund page.
- **Overview:** Needs attention (fund, issue, date, amount, next action) → fund position (NAV with its own as-of date) → fund health rows → recent activity → banking, collapsed to one line when no entity is connected.
- **Context-aware primary action:** never “New capital call” on a fund with $0 committed.
- **$0, Not recorded and Unreadable** are distinct treatments.
- **Every money action** goes Prepare → Review → Confirm → Authenticate → Receipt, shows its exact effect, and the button names the act and amount. Admin vs Super Admin refusals are sentences; unverifiable permissions disable money actions.
- **Person / entity picker** with eligibility reasons, for GP of record and ledger administrator.
- **Eadwyn** prepares, explains and navigates; it never moves money.

### Backend required

- A recent-activity feed (no endpoint; it could read `admin_audit_log` scoped to fund acts).
- CSV export of the current LP view.

### Built — part 1, 2026-11-08 (read-only views)

**Worker.** `GET /api/fund-admin/activity` on the existing `fund_admin.ts`
router (`services/fundAdminActivity.ts`), behind `requireFundAdminReader` (an
HQ admin in their own session, as `/overview`). It reads the `fund_admin.<act>`
rows `auditFundAct` already writes to `admin_audit_log`, newest first, and
leaves out reads and previews (`fund_booklet.read`, `capital_call.preview`,
`distribution.preview`, `fund_notice.preview`). Each row is whitelisted: the
fund, an amount in cents, a call number, the LP and the actor, each by name;
never `filters_json`, a reason, a route or an email. `?fund=`, `?before=` and
`?limit=` are digits or a 400, and bound. A store that cannot be read is a
503, never an empty list. No migration. Test:
`cloudflare-worker/test/fund_admin_activity_d4812.test.ts`.

**Frontend.** `workspaceModel.js` (pure) and a rewritten `OverviewTab.jsx`; the
console's header in `FundAdminConsole.jsx`; `HeaderTab` gains an optional
`active` for a tab that owns several addresses. Six workspaces over D1157's
addresses, so every old link opens where it did; one fund-scope control (a
disclosure of links, `?fund=`); amber flags only; "Updated HH:MM UTC ·
Refresh"; the state-driven primary; the Overview in the canvas's order. Test:
`frontend/test/fund_admin_overview_v2_d4812.test.mjs`, plus the D714, D1157,
D1160, D1161 and D1164 tests moved to the six workspaces.

**Where the build departs from the canvas, and why.**

1. **Workspace parts follow the views that exist.** Capital has no Receipts
   part: a receipt is recorded on a call line, on Capital calls. Investors has
   no Commitments or Applications part: commitment is a roster column, and LP
   applications stay in Approvals, linked from More. Banking & Books has no
   separate Accounts part: accounts and transactions are one view, labelled
   Transactions. Reporting's Notices and Statements stay under Reports' own
   sub-addresses.
2. **Funds is in More** ("Funds and fund settings") and on each Funds row of
   the Overview; a fund's page carries a crumb back to Fund administration.
3. **The scope control is not drawn on Books, Banking or Reconciliation.**
   Books keeps the fund ledger's own selector, because the ledger decides which
   funds this viewer may read (D1165). Banking and Reconciliation are read per
   Axal entity, so they say "Scope: Axal entities · banking is read per entity"
   instead of offering a fund that would change nothing.
4. **Needs attention shows no amount for overdue lines.** The overview read
   counts overdue lines but does not total them, and a fund's outstanding
   includes lines not yet due; the amount is left out rather than invented.
   "Fund setup incomplete" is derived from nothing committed only (not from
   economics), and its action is Add LP, the concrete next step.
5. **NAV's date is "latest marks to <date>"**, the performance read's as-of.
   Each mark's own date is on Portfolio.
6. **Recent activity lists HQ admins' acts only**, because `auditFundAct`
   writes only for `role = 'admin'`; a non-admin GP of record's acts on their
   own fund are not in that store. It is read by an Admin and the Super Admin
   alike, on the overview's gate: it is the funds' operating record, each
   person named, with no detail beyond the whitelist.
7. **Unverified permissions:** the header's primary offers no money act while
   the act table is unread. The in-tab controls still follow D714 (a failed
   access read keeps the controls; the Worker refuses) until part 2 builds the
   money-action frame, which is where "Financial actions are temporarily
   unavailable" belongs.
8. **Eadwyn** is the shared header's Ask, drawn when the rail serves it; the
   canvas's rail answer is not built.
9. **Typography and card levels** are v2's on the Overview and the header
   (12–14px text, 22px figures, a definition list for Fund position). The
   other workspaces keep D1157's parts until their own part.

### Built — part 2, 2026-11-09 (the money-action flow)

**Frontend only; no Worker change and no migration.** `MoneyFlow.jsx` and
`moneyFlowModel.js` hold the flow, Prepare → Review → Confirm → Authenticate →
Receipt, and three acts use it:

- **New capital call** (`callDialogs.jsx`): the terms; Review with the exact
  effect (fund, amount, LPs affected, due date, current outstanding,
  outstanding after issue, documents) and the split folded under "See the N
  lines"; Confirm and Authenticate on one screen, as FA-05b draws it; the
  receipt. The button reads "Issue $X capital call".
- **Record receipt**: Review shows the line's outstanding before → after.
  "Record $X receipt" is an Admin's act, so Authenticate reads "not required".
- **Execute distribution** (`DistributionsTab.jsx`): Execute opens Review (the
  job's split as an effect), then Confirm and Authenticate ("Execute $X
  distribution"), then the receipt. It no longer acts on one click.

**Authenticate.** For a Super Admin act the flow asks for the authenticator
code and calls `POST /api/auth/step-up` (BLOCK-AUTH-03) before the act. A code
that is refused stops there and names the error on the field. The global
`StepUpModal` stays the fallback: if the Worker still answers
`step_up_required`, the request helper prompts as before.

**Not offered (FA-05d).** An Admin reviews and reads the act table's sentence
where Continue would be. While the act table is unread, the flow stops at
Review with "Permissions could not be verified. Financial actions are
temporarily unavailable." This replaces, for these three acts, D714's rule
that a failed access read keeps the control. A fund with nothing committed
gets "… has no committed capital. A capital call needs commitments. Add LPs
first." instead of the form.

**Departures.**

1. **Documents.** The canvas says "28 notices will be generated". Issuing a
   call here generates no notice: notices are issued from Call notices, after
   the call exists. Review and Receipt say so.
2. **The receipt's time and audit line.** "Confirmed by the server at" is when
   the server's success reached the page. The audit line names the act key
   (`capital_call.issue`), because the issue response carries no audit row id.
3. **Recording a receipt** gets no Authenticate step: it is an Admin act
   without the step-up.

**Left for later parts.** The same flow for fee periods, report issue,
reconciliation matches, economics changes and valuation marks; then FA-02,
FA-04 (the fund page and the person picker), FA-05 to FA-07 and the phone
boards.
