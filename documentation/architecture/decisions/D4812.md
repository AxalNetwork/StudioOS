## D4812 — HQ › Fund administration v2: one fund scope, six workspaces, attention first (#4812)

- **Status:** canvas landed 2026-11-07 (`design/incoming/Fund Admin v2 Page.dc.html`, `design/incoming/Fund Admin v2 · Boards.dc.html`, boards FA01 to FA14). Part 1 built 2026-11-08 parts 2 to 4 (the money-action flow, the fund page and person picker, the LP roster and its export) 2026-11-09 (below); the remaining boards are later parts.
- **Builds on:** `fund_admin.ts` (`/access`, `/overview`, `/lps`), `fundAdminAccess.ts` (`FUND_ACTS` tiers), `fundAdminOverview.ts`, `funds.ts` (capital-call preview and issue, ledger), `fund_ledger.ts`, `fund_reporting.ts`, `fund_fees.ts`, `fund_notices.ts`, `fund_booklets.ts`, `fund_performance.ts`.

### What the canvas decides

- **One fund-scope control** replaces the segmented links and the second select. A one-fund workflow asks for a fund explicitly.
- **Six workspaces** replace nine peer tabs: Overview, Investors (LP roster, commitments, applications), Capital (calls, receipts, distributions), Portfolio (NAV, positions, marks), Reporting (reports, fees, notices, statements), Banking & Books (transactions, reconciliation, accounts, books). Funds become context plus a fund page.
- **Overview:** Needs attention (fund, issue, date, amount, next action) → fund position (NAV with its own as-of date) → fund health rows → recent activity → banking, collapsed to one line when no entity is connected.
- **Context-aware primary action:** never “New capital call” on a fund with $0 committed.
- **$0, Not recorded and Unreadable** are distinct treatments.
- **Every money action** goes Prepare → Review → Confirm → Authenticate → Receipt, shows its exact effect, and the button names the act and amount. Admin vs Super Admin refusals are sentences; unverifiable permissions disable money actions.
- **Person / entity picker** with eligibility reasons, for GP of record and ledger administrator.
- **Eadwyn** prepares, explains and navigates; it never moves money.

### Backend required

- A recent-activity feed (no endpoint; it could read `admin_audit_log` scoped to fund acts).
- CSV export of the current LP view.

### Built — part 1, 2026-11-08 (read-only views)

**Worker.** `GET /api/fund-admin/activity` on the existing `fund_admin.ts`
router (`services/fundAdminActivity.ts`), behind `requireFundAdminReader` (an
HQ admin in their own session, as `/overview`). It reads the `fund_admin.<act>`
rows `auditFundAct` already writes to `admin_audit_log`, newest first, and
leaves out reads and previews (`fund_booklet.read`, `capital_call.preview`,
`distribution.preview`, `fund_notice.preview`). Each row is whitelisted: the
fund, an amount in cents, a call number, the LP and the actor, each by name;
never `filters_json`, a reason, a route or an email. `?fund=`, `?before=` and
`?limit=` are digits or a 400, and bound. A store that cannot be read is a
503, never an empty list. No migration. Test:
`cloudflare-worker/test/fund_admin_activity_d4812.test.ts`.

**Frontend.** `workspaceModel.js` (pure) and a rewritten `OverviewTab.jsx`; the
console's header in `FundAdminConsole.jsx`; `HeaderTab` gains an optional
`active` for a tab that owns several addresses. Six workspaces over D1157's
addresses, so every old link opens where it did; one fund-scope control (a
disclosure of links, `?fund=`); amber flags only; "Updated HH:MM UTC ·
Refresh"; the state-driven primary; the Overview in the canvas's order. Test:
`frontend/test/fund_admin_overview_v2_d4812.test.mjs`, plus the D714, D1157,
D1160, D1161 and D1164 tests moved to the six workspaces.

**Where the build departs from the canvas, and why.**

1. **Workspace parts follow the views that exist.** Capital has no Receipts
   part: a receipt is recorded on a call line, on Capital calls. Investors has
   no Commitments or Applications part: commitment is a roster column, and LP
   applications stay in Approvals, linked from More. Banking & Books has no
   separate Accounts part: accounts and transactions are one view, labelled
   Transactions. Reporting's Notices and Statements stay under Reports' own
   sub-addresses.
2. **Funds is in More** ("Funds and fund settings") and on each Funds row of
   the Overview; a fund's page carries a crumb back to Fund administration.
3. **The scope control is not drawn on Books, Banking or Reconciliation.**
   Books keeps the fund ledger's own selector, because the ledger decides which
   funds this viewer may read (D1165). Banking and Reconciliation are read per
   Axal entity, so they say "Scope: Axal entities · banking is read per entity"
   instead of offering a fund that would change nothing.
4. **Needs attention shows no amount for overdue lines.** The overview read
   counts overdue lines but does not total them, and a fund's outstanding
   includes lines not yet due; the amount is left out rather than invented.
   "Fund setup incomplete" is derived from nothing committed only (not from
   economics), and its action is Add LP, the concrete next step.
5. **NAV's date is "latest marks to <date>"**, the performance read's as-of.
   Each mark's own date is on Portfolio.
6. **Recent activity lists HQ admins' acts only**, because `auditFundAct`
   writes only for `role = 'admin'`; a non-admin GP of record's acts on their
   own fund are not in that store. It is read by an Admin and the Super Admin
   alike, on the overview's gate: it is the funds' operating record, each
   person named, with no detail beyond the whitelist.
7. **Unverified permissions:** the header's primary offers no money act while
   the act table is unread. The in-tab controls still follow D714 (a failed
   access read keeps the controls; the Worker refuses) until part 2 builds the
   money-action frame, which is where "Financial actions are temporarily
   unavailable" belongs.
8. **Eadwyn** is the shared header's Ask, drawn when the rail serves it; the
   canvas's rail answer is not built.
9. **Typography and card levels** are v2's on the Overview and the header
   (12–14px text, 22px figures, a definition list for Fund position). The
   other workspaces keep D1157's parts until their own part.

**Left for later parts.** FA-02's roster chips, Export view (the CSV route is
backend-required) and the LP drawer's restyle; FA-03's five-step money frame
(Prepare → Review → Confirm → Authenticate → Receipt) for calls, receipts,
distributions, fees, report issue, reconciliation, economics and marks; FA-04's
fund page and the person/entity picker; FA-05 to FA-07's restyles; FA-09's LP,
match and confirm phone boards; and FA-08's patterns as shared components.

### Built — part 2, 2026-11-09 (the money-action flow)

**Frontend only; no Worker change and no migration.** `MoneyFlow.jsx` and
`moneyFlowModel.js` hold the flow, Prepare → Review → Confirm → Authenticate →
Receipt, and three acts use it:

- **New capital call** (`callDialogs.jsx`): the terms; Review with the exact
  effect (fund, amount, LPs affected, due date, current outstanding,
  outstanding after issue, documents) and the split folded under "See the N
  lines"; Confirm and Authenticate on one screen, as FA-05b draws it; the
  receipt. The button reads "Issue $X capital call".
- **Record receipt**: Review shows the line's outstanding before → after.
  "Record $X receipt" is an Admin's act, so Authenticate reads "not required".
- **Execute distribution** (`DistributionsTab.jsx`): Execute opens Review (the
  job's split as an effect), then Confirm and Authenticate ("Execute $X
  distribution"), then the receipt. It no longer acts on one click.

**Authenticate.** For a Super Admin act the flow asks for the authenticator
code and calls `POST /api/auth/step-up` (BLOCK-AUTH-03) before the act. A code
that is refused stops there and names the error on the field. The global
`StepUpModal` stays the fallback: if the Worker still answers
`step_up_required`, the request helper prompts as before.

**Not offered (FA-05d).** An Admin reviews and reads the act table's sentence
where Continue would be. While the act table is unread, the flow stops at
Review with "Permissions could not be verified. Financial actions are
temporarily unavailable." This replaces, for these three acts, D714's rule
that a failed access read keeps the control. A fund with nothing committed
gets "… has no committed capital. A capital call needs commitments. Add LPs
first." instead of the form.

**Departures.**

1. **Documents.** The canvas says "28 notices will be generated". Issuing a
   call here generates no notice: notices are issued from Call notices, after
   the call exists. Review and Receipt say so.
2. **The receipt's time and audit line.** "Confirmed by the server at" is when
   the server's success reached the page. The audit line names the act key
   (`capital_call.issue`), because the issue response carries no audit row id.
3. **Recording a receipt** gets no Authenticate step: it is an Admin act
   without the step-up.

**Left for later parts.** The same flow for fee periods, report issue,
reconciliation matches, economics changes and valuation marks; then FA-02,
FA-04 (the fund page and the person picker), FA-05 to FA-07 and the phone
boards.

### Built — part 3, 2026-11-09 (the fund page and the person picker)

**Worker:** `GET /api/fund-admin/funds/:id/people?role=gp|ledger&q=`, on the
`fund_admin.ts` router (`services/fundAdminPeople.ts`). This is the read the
canvas's picker needs, and nothing provided it before.
- Any HQ admin reads who holds the role, by name and account kind, never by
  email. The page no longer says "their name is not read on this page".
- Only the Super Admin is shown candidates, because only the Super Admin can
  change either role. Candidates are active admin and investor accounts
  matching `q` (trimmed, at most 60 characters, LIKE wildcards escaped,
  bound), at most 20.
- Each candidate carries the Worker's own rule as its eligibility, with the
  reason when not eligible.
- A fund not administered here is a 404. No migration. The writes are the
  existing ones: `PATCH /api/funds/:id` with `gp_user_id` and a reason, and
  `PUT /api/fund-ledger/:id/administrator`.

**Frontend:**
- **Fund page** (`FundPage.jsx`, `fundPageModel.js`):
  - Identity first: the name, then "Vintage · Status · GP: <name>".
  - Six key figures: target size, committed, called, NAV and distributed
    (from `GET /api/fund-performance/:id`, NAV with its date), and LPs.
  - Local navigation: Overview, then Investors, Capital and Portfolio with
    this fund in scope, then Documents (the LPA) and Settings.
  - Economics (with the waterfall line) beside Roles, which names the GP of
    record and the ledger administrator.
  - Service providers and the GP's document fields fold into "Service
    providers and GP documents".
- **Person picker** (`PersonPicker.jsx`):
  - Search, then each account by name and kind, marked Current, Eligible or
    Not eligible with its reason. An ineligible person or the current holder
    cannot be chosen.
  - The confirmation reads "Replace A with B?" and says what the change does.
    For the GP it asks a reason (10–500 characters, kept on the audit record).
  - Then the authenticator code (both are Super Admin acts), then "Make B …".
  - The ledger administrator can be removed ("No one"). An Admin reads the act
    table's sentence where Change would be.
- The placeholder "Choosing the person is not drawn on this page yet." is
  gone.

**Departures.**

1. **Eligibility follows the Worker, not the canvas.**
   - The canvas's ledger-administrator example says "must be an HQ admin".
     The Worker's PUT writes only an active investor account (HQ admins
     already keep every fund's books), so the picker offers investor accounts.
   - For the GP of record the picker offers HQ admins only, because a fund
     whose GP of record is not an admin leaves administration. An outside
     GP's fund is run from its own fund page.
2. **The Worker does not check the GP of record.** `PATCH /api/funds/:id`
   accepts any `gp_user_id` without checking that the account exists or is an
   admin. The picker only offers eligible accounts. A Worker-side check on
   that write is a follow-up, because it would change a write the GP's own
   page also uses.
3. **The local navigation** opens the console's workspaces with this fund in
   scope, rather than tabs inside the fund page. Documents and Settings are
   sections of this page.
4. **"M. Okafor keeps read access"** (the canvas's example) is not said: the
   ledger read follows the same rule as the write, so a removed investor
   administrator loses both. The confirmation says what the Worker actually
   does.

**Left for later parts.** The flow for fee periods, report issue,
reconciliation, economics and marks; FA-02's roster chips and Export view (its
CSV route is backend-required); FA-05 to FA-07's restyles; FA-09's phone
boards.

### Built — part 4, 2026-11-09 (the LP roster and Export view)

**Worker:** `GET /api/fund-admin/lps/export`, on the `fund_admin.ts` router.
This is the canvas's backend-required "Export view".
- **Same rows as the table.** It uses `/lps`'s own filters, search and sort,
  and covers every page: `fundAdminLps` gains an `all` mode, and `page` and
  `per_page` are ignored.
- **Audited first.** The read is audited under the roster's own read action,
  with surface `fund_admin.lps.export` and the filters used; a failed audit
  sends nothing.
- **The file** (`services/fundAdminLpsCsv.ts`) is CSV:
  - money in dollars with two decimals; a figure no store holds is written
    "Not recorded" and one a store could not read "Unreadable", never 0;
  - **no email** — the file leaves the console, so an LP is named, as
    everywhere in fund administration;
  - a cell a spreadsheet would run as a formula is prefixed with an
    apostrophe.
- `Cache-Control: no-store`. No migration.

**Frontend** (`LpsTab.jsx`, `lpsModel.js`):
- The section is "LP roster", inside the Investors workspace.
- Each active filter is a chip with ✕ that removes only itself. The fund scope
  is the header's, so it is not a chip.
- The result line reads "N LPs · $X committed · showing a–b", over every row
  the filters keep; LPs with no commitment recorded are counted, never
  summed as 0.
- The LP's name opens the record, and Open is gone from the ⋯ menu.
- An identity check the store could not read says "! Unreadable · Retry",
  distinct from Not recorded.
- Export view downloads the Worker's file and says what it wrote, or the
  Worker's refusal.

**Departures.**

1. **No email in the export,** though the table shows one. A file that
   leaves the console carries less personal data than the screen.
2. **Column visibility, sticky headers and a quick-preview drawer** are not
   added. The roster's drawer (`/lps/:id`) already opens over the table, and
   the shared `Table` stacks to labelled records on a phone.
3. **"Clear all"** is not offered: each chip removes itself.

**Left for later parts.** The money flow for fee periods, report issue,
reconciliation, economics and marks; FA-05 to FA-07's restyles; FA-09's phone
boards.


### Follow-up, 2026-11-09: the GP of record is an existing, active account

Part 3 noted that `PATCH /api/funds/:id` accepted any `gp_user_id`. The owner
chose a Worker check on any active account.

- A change of the GP of record (`fund.gp_of_record`) now refuses an id that is
  not an existing, active account: `400 gp_account_invalid`.
- The check runs after the act's gate. A caller who may not change the GP is
  refused as before (`super_admin_required`) and learns nothing about which
  accounts exist.
- Any role may still be named: a non-admin GP moves the fund to "run by an
  outside GP", as it did before.
- Null (no GP named) stays allowed.

The person picker shows the Worker's sentence if it ever refuses.

### Built — part 5, 2026-11-09 (the money flow for the remaining acts)

**Frontend only; no Worker change and no migration.** `ActFlow`
(`MoneyFlow.jsx`) runs any act as Review → Confirm → Authenticate → Receipt.
The page's own form is Prepare, and the act runs only from Confirm. Five more
acts use it:

| Act | Where | Tier | The button |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Confirm fee terms (`fee_terms.confirm`) | Fees | Super Admin | "Confirm 2% fee terms" |
| Issue a fee period (`fee_period.issue`) | Fees | Super Admin | "Issue $X 2026Q4 fee period" |
| Issue a report pack (`fund_report.issue`) | Reports, an opened pack | Super Admin | "Issue Q3 2026 report pack" |
| Confirm a bank match (`mercury.reconcile`) | Reconciliation | Admin | "Confirm $X match" |
| Change the economics (`fund.economics`) | Fund page | Super Admin | "Change the fund's economics" |
| Record a valuation mark (`fund.valuation`) | Portfolio | Admin | "Record $X mark" |

- **Review** shows each act's exact effect (`moneyFlowModel.js`):
  - the fee period's gross, catch-up, offset and net fee;
  - the terms' rate, start date and LPA clause;
  - the report pack's period and its frozen digest;
  - the match's transaction and what it pays;
  - each rate before → after, with the reason;
  - the mark's value and date beside the previous mark.
  A figure the screen does not hold is Not recorded.
- **Authenticate:** the code is asked only for a Super Admin act. The tier
  comes from the act table (`tier`, or a refusal sentence, which only Super
  Admin rows carry). An act the table does not list is not refused here, as
  `FundActControl` already does; the Worker decides and its step-up stays the
  fallback.
- **When the act table is unread,** the flow stops at Review with FA-05d's
  sentence.

**Departures.**

1. **The fee period is issued by its preview's digest,** as before. The
   Review figures are the preview's, so the figures cannot change between
   Review and issue.
2. **Lists reload after the receipt closes.** The reconciliation matches and
   the report packs are read again when the receipt closes, not when the act
   answers. A reload remounts the row that holds the flow, which would hide
   the receipt.

**Left for later parts.** FA-05 to FA-07's restyles (Portfolio, Reporting,
Banking & Books) and FA-09's phone boards.

### Built — part 6, 2026-11-09 (Portfolio, Reporting, Banking & Books: FA-05 to FA-07)

**One Worker change and no migration.** `GET /api/admin/mercury/matches` now
returns each match's ledger side (`ledger`, from the new `ledgerSides` in
`services/mercury/match.ts`):

- a capital-call line's LP, call number, due date and what it still owes;
- a fee period's quarter and net fee.

A target no longer on the ledger is `null` with `ledger_unavailable`. If the
ledger read fails, every match still reads, each saying that its ledger side
could not be read. The audit row is written before the ledger is read, as
before.

- **FA-05 Portfolio.**
  - Each tile says its own date. NAV, RVPI and TVPI stand on the marks, so
    each reads "marks to" the newest mark's date. Paid-in, Distributed and DPI
    are cash, "as of" the read.
  - IRR stays Not recorded, with the Worker's reason, when it has no figure.
  - The positions table gains a State column: Current, or "Stale · older than
    1 quarter" for a mark more than three calendar months before the read. A
    position with no mark has no state; it still reads No mark.
  - Every chart already had View as table.
- **FA-06 Reporting.**
  - The queue's count line says how many prepared packs wait to be issued.
    Capital statements are issued with their report, so they are not counted
    on their own.
  - The issue confirmation names the recipients: one statement per LP in the
    pack, counted from the queue's own rows. The button names them too:
    "Issue Q3 2026 report to 31 LPs".
- **FA-07 A, Transactions.** One account's balance and its read time ("Balance
  $412,380.00 · as of …"). With every account chosen, the line asks for one:
  no single balance covers them all.
- **FA-07 B, Reconciliation.**
  - An open match shows Bank and Ledger side by side, then its effect: "Records
    a $50,000.00 receipt on call 3 · outstanding $125,000.00 → $75,000.00".
  - The confirm button says the same: "Confirm match and record $50,000.00
    receipt".
  - A fee-period match says the period then reads as collected.
- **FA-07 C, Books.** The admin journal gains a Source column (Manual journal,
  Expense, or Fee accrual with its fee terms). Below it, the period's totals
  read Balanced, or Out of balance by the difference. A line with no amount
  makes the totals Not recorded, never 0.

**Departures.**

1. **Recipients are LP portal only.** The canvas reads "31 LPs · LP portal +
   email", but issuing a pack sends no email (`issueDraft` writes the drafts,
   the period and an activity row only). The confirmation says so rather than
   promise an email.
2. **Part 5's match effect was wrong, and is corrected.** It said "it moves no
   money and records a match". Confirming a capital-call match also records a
   receipt on the line (`confirmMatch` → `recordReceipt`). The effect, the
   consequences and the button now say so.
3. **Books sources are the ledger's own kinds.** The canvas shows "Bank
   match" and "Fee period". The fund ledger records `journal`, `expense` and
   `fee_accrual` entries, and no bank match posts there. Each entry is named
   by its kind; nothing is inferred.
4. **"Not this one" and "Split…" are not added.** Reject (with a reason) is
   "Not this one". A split has no Worker route, and none is drawn.
5. **Close period is not added to Books.** The periods and their Open or
   Closed state are already read beside the journal; closing one stays where
   the ledger already does it.

**Left for later parts.** FA-09's phone boards.
