### What was there

Wellbeing had only 1:1 bookings (`expert_bookings`). A "peer group" was a resource link that reserved nothing. `routes/events.ts` holds the platform's only capacity model, but it is built for public events: discoverability, approvals and a wait-list that are visible to organisers. It is not reused here, so attendance is never exposed through it. Its idea is borrowed instead: a seat taken in the same statement that checks capacity.

### The model

`wellbeing_group_sessions` is one occurrence. It records:
- the facilitator, an expert;
- the kind of support (`SUPPORT_TYPES`) and a title;
- the language, the time zone, the start (stored in UTC) and the duration;
- capacity, from 2 to 50;
- `cancellation_hours`;
- `scheduled` or `cancelled`, with when and why.

`price_cents` is CHECKed to 0.

`wellbeing_group_reservations` is one seat. It records the member, `reserved` or `cancelled`, and the member's calendar choice. It is UNIQUE per session and member.

### Seats never oversell

A reservation is one `INSERT … SELECT`. Its WHERE requires all of these in the same statement:
- the session is scheduled;
- its start is in the future;
- the facilitator is still eligible;
- the count of reserved seats is below capacity.

D1 runs a statement whole, so of two members after the last seat exactly one gets it. The other gets 409 `session_full`, which names no one.

Its `ON CONFLICT … DO UPDATE … WHERE status = 'cancelled'` re-opens a member's own cancelled seat, and it does so only when the capacity check passed. A repeated request from a member who already holds a seat returns that seat (`already_reserved`) and takes no second one. This is guarded twice: a short-circuit before the statement, and the conflict clause.

Just before the statement, the route also checks status, start and eligibility, so each failure gets its own code: `session_cancelled`, `session_started` or `facilitator_unavailable`.

### Cancelling

**By the member.** Allowed until `cancellation_hours` before the start. The cutoff is computed in UTC from the stored start, so a member's change of zone never moves it. After the cutoff the answer is 409 `cancellation_window_closed`, with `cutoff_at`. Cancelling releases the seat, and the member may reserve again if one is free. The session is free, so `refund.applicable` is false. Paid groups and refunds are ⚑ Q4 on #7117.

**By the facilitator, or by HQ.** A reason is required. Each member who holds a seat gets their own notice, which names no other member. The session leaves the list and refuses new seats. In the members' own "My bookings" it reads `cancelled`, with the reason.

### Confidential attendance

- **Members** see a seat count and `seats_left`, never a name. Every session states what the facilitator learns ("Your name and email, so they can let you in…") and what other members learn ("Nothing…").
- **Cancelled or hidden sessions** are shown only to a member who reserved them.
- **The calendar entry** (`.ics`) is the member's own and lists no attendee and no organiser.
  - `discreet` (the default) is titled "Private appointment", with no description.
  - `named` shows the title.
  - `none` gives no file.
- **Facilitator and HQ.** Only the facilitator, for their own sessions, sees attendees' name, email and the time they reserved. HQ may cancel a session. Another expert answers 404.

### Eligibility

Only an approved expert can schedule: active, verified, not hidden, not a sample, with a complete profile. The session's kind must be within the categories the expert lists, and its start must be at least an hour ahead and at most a year away.

If the facilitator stops being eligible, their sessions leave the list and a reservation is refused at commit.

### Suggestions

With group in the member's formats, or no format chosen, upcoming sessions that fit are suggested after the experts. A session fits when its kind is one the member chose, or one a consented topic may suggest (D7119), it is in the member's language, and it has not been dismissed. Each has "Why this?".

A full session is kept, marked unavailable, so "no seats" is said rather than hidden. `group_sessions.offered` is now true. The suggestion's uid is the uid a member reserves with.

### Unchanged

The 1:1 path keeps its own routes, availability, payment and cancellation: `POST /experts/:uid/book` and `POST /bookings/:uid/cancel`. Nothing here writes `expert_bookings`.

### Not built here

- **A wait-list, paid groups and refunds:** ⚑ Q4 on #7117. A full session says it is full.
- **Rescheduling:** a facilitator cancels the occurrence and schedules a new one, and members reserve again. Moving reservations across is not built.
- **Calendar invitations sent by the platform:** none are sent for groups. The member downloads their own entry.
