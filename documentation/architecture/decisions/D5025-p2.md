## D5025 part 2 — Package changes take effect at the next term, scheduled by HQ (#5025)

Part 2 of #5025, on top of D5025 (part 1). The owner answered the three
questions part 1 left open (2026-11-24, on #5025):

1. A mid-term upgrade or change takes effect **at the next term only, with no
   proration**.
2. A proration credit or debit, if one is ever made, is **an adjustment row on
   the licence fee invoices** (D4694).
3. A downgrade or removed program needs **no notice period**.

### What this part does

- **The rule is a constant.** `packageLifecycle.ts` exports
  `FINANCIAL_RULE = 'next_term_no_proration'`. Part 1's change review reads
  it in `financial_rule` (it read `not_decided`) and adds
  `proration_applies: false`. The `effective_on` quote stays as an estimate
  for HQ, but nothing charges or credits it.
- **A schedule records HQ's decision.** `licence_package_changes` (migration
  `202611240011_5025_package_changes.sql`) holds one row per schedule: the
  current term, the next-term offer, the kind, both annual fees and
  currencies frozen at the time, the rule, the reason and who scheduled it.
  `POST …/package-offers/:offer/schedule-change` writes one, with an
  idempotency key.
- **Next term only, enforced.** A schedule is refused unless the offer is
  `offered` and starts exactly the day after the current term ends:
  `change_not_next_term` for an offer that starts inside the term (its last
  day included), `change_leaves_gap` for one that starts later, and
  `no_current_term` for a licence with no accepted term, whose first offer is
  accepted directly. Two partial unique indexes allow one live schedule per
  current term and one per offer (`change_already_scheduled`).
- **No notice.** Any schedule can be made up to the last day of the term.
- **Acceptance is still D5024's.** On or after `effective_on`, HQ accepts the
  offer through `POST …/package-offers/:offer/accept`, which re-checks grants,
  legal policy and the review hash and already refuses a package that starts
  early or overlaps a term. This part does not accept anything by itself: a
  change of grants and legal holdings stays a reviewed human action.
- **Status is read, not stored.** `GET …/package-changes` answers each
  schedule as `scheduled` (before its day), `due` (on or after it, offer still
  offered), `applied` (offer accepted), `lapsed` (offer declined, expired or
  superseded) or `cancelled`. Only `scheduled` and `cancelled` are stored, so
  the status cannot disagree with the offer.
- **Cancel.** `POST …/package-changes/:change/cancel` with a reason, refused
  once applied (`change_already_applied`) or already cancelled.
- **Audit.** Both writes call `logAdminAction`
  (`licence_package_change_scheduled`, `licence_package_change_cancelled`).

### Money

With no proration, a change writes no credit or debit and no invoice row. The
next term's invoices use its own accepted fee. Answer 2 is recorded here so a
later proration change knows where its adjustment goes: a new adjustment row
on the D4694 licence fee invoices, not a separate ledger.

### Not in this part

- The operator screen for schedules: #5026, with its canvas.
- Automatic acceptance on the day. It would bypass D5024's reviewed acceptance;
  the `due` status is what HQ acts on.
- The open cohorts, claims and workflows of an ending program still read
  `not_recorded` (part 1).
