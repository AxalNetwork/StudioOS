## D5929 — New licence as a stepped issuance: L21's frame and its territory step (#5929)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-27. Part of #4819 (Licences v2). Frontend only: no Worker route, no migration.
- **Canvas:** board L21 of `design/incoming/Licences v2 Page.dc.html` ("New licence · territory step"), with the
  frame details the New Licence Steps canvas (D5930) draws around its cards: the crumb and header (LNS41 to LNS45),
  the step list's Current and done marks, "Leave New licence?" (LNS47E) and the phone's Steps sheet (LNS48D).
- **Builds on:** #5931's step cards (`NewLicenceSteps.jsx`, D4819 part 5), `GET /admin/licences/territories` and
  `PUT /admin/licences/:uid/territories` (`routes/admin_licences_ledger.ts`, `routes/admin_licences_terms.ts`).

### What was built

- **`licences/IssueLicenceFlow.jsx`, the frame.** New licence (populated ledger) and Issue first licence (zero state)
  open it in place of the ledger, at `/admin/licences`, in the same rail frame. It draws the crumb (Licences / New
  licence), "Issue a licence" with "Step n of 6 · <step>", what is saved so far ("Nothing is saved until you create
  the draft", then the draft's reference, entity and Draft), Leave, and the six steps of the canvas as a 240px list.
  The current step's `li` carries `aria-current="step"`; a step left forwards is marked done (✓); a step already
  reached can be opened from the list. Below `md` the list is a Steps sheet (`ui/Lightbox`, `phone="sheet"`).
- **The card slot.** Step 2 is `TerritoryStep`. Steps 1 and 3 to 6 are #5931's `NewLicenceStep`, placed unchanged.
  It stays mounted on step 2 (it draws nothing there), so its typed values survive a visit to Territory; the
  countries chosen are held by the frame for the same reason.
- **`TerritoryStep` in `licences/TerritoryEditor.jsx`, step 2.** "Which countries does <legal entity> hold?" Chips
  for the 27 EU states (`role="checkbox"`, `aria-checked`), and any other ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code added by hand. A
  country another licence holds carries its holder and the coverage grid's taken tone (`COVERAGE_TONE`, `heldState`,
  now exported from `Coverage.jsx`, so the step and the grid cannot disagree). Choosing one shows the conflict at once,
  as a `role="alert"` notice: "Austria is already held · <holder> holds Austria (suspended licences still hold their
  countries). Overlapping territories are refused; remove Austria or terminate the other licence first.", with Remove.
  Continue to commercial terms stays `aria-disabled` while a conflict stands or no country is chosen, and the footer
  says why. The existing editor on the licence page reads the same holder map (`heldByOthers`).
- **The Worker's refusal in its words.** Continue sends `PUT /:uid/territories`. A 409 `territory_conflict` (a
  country taken since the holder read) is shown as the Worker's own sentence ("IT is held by AXL-007 (active)"), every
  chosen country stays chosen, and who holds what is read again so the chips and conflicts catch up. No answer reads
  Outcome unknown, never refused.
- **Leaving.** Leave and the crumb ask first when a draft exists or anything was typed, in a dialog: "Leave New
  licence?" with LNS47E's sentence (the draft stays in the ledger as Draft; values typed on this step and not saved
  are discarded), Leave and Stay, focus on Stay, Escape stays. With nothing typed and no draft it closes at once; once
  the licence is issued it is no longer a draft and closes at once. Closing the tab with something to lose asks
  through the browser (`beforeunload`). On leaving with a draft the ledger is read again, so the draft is listed.
- **`licences/issueLicenceFlowModel.js`.** The rules as pure functions: `stepMarks`, `canOpenStep`, `leavePlan`,
  `territoryConflicts`, `conflictTitle`, `conflictSentence`, `territoryPlan`, `readCountryCode`, `territoryRefusal`
  and `KIND_HOSTS`.

### The issue's open question: one call, or create then fill?

The licence is **created first and filled in step by step**. Step 1 is `POST /admin/licences` (reference, legal entity,
brand, kind, and the address and signatory when given); it answers with the draft's uid. Step 2 saves the countries
to that draft (`PUT /:uid/territories`), step 3 the seats then the money terms, step 4 appoints, step 5 generates the
contract, and Issue activates (the routes are listed in D4819 part 5). No route takes every field at once, and none
was added. The inline form this replaces did the same first call and left the rest to the licence's own page.

### Nothing removed

The inline form's fields are step 1's (reference, legal entity, brand, kind, plus the address and signatory #5931
added). Its two kind sentences (where members land, where the host is bound, Super Admin's host; D196, D197) and its
line "A new licence starts as a draft holding no territory…" are kept under step 1 until the draft exists, as "Where
each kind's members land" (`KIND_HOSTS`). The Cancel toggle became Leave.
