## D6060 — Pitch Studio: Review of one saved version, and the qualitative mark (#6060)

Pitch Studio v2 build 6/9 (D4839, tracker #3716): boards PS-06 A and B, with
PS-09 F (phone) and the dark rules of PS-10. It also builds D6066's
"intentionally qualitative" card mark, which the owner decided on 2026-11-15.

**Review of one exact saved version.** `/raise/pitch/review` now opens with
Review of the saved version the studio has selected (`?deck=`, else the
current one). The page is `VersionReview.jsx`, mounted through `PitchReview.jsx`'s
`PitchVersionReview`, and the rules live in `versionReviewModel.js`. The page
names the version it checked ("v7 · saved 8 Nov, 16:40"). It shows counts by
status and the line "No readiness score". Then come six groups:

- **Completeness:** cards with nothing on them, cards not Done, and placeholders the preflight found.
- **Evidence & freshness:** each figure from `GET /api/decks/:id/figures` with the check's claim (`GET /api/decks/:id/check`, D900) and its consistency rules (D1555). Bound figures that moved in their store come from `POST /api/decks/:id/refresh` (D896). Cards with no figure, and cards marked qualitative, are listed here too.
- **Readability & layout** and **Technical preflight:** D1370's preflight, run on the saved version's own cards and measured on the page at export size (`usePreflight`, `PreflightMeasure`, read only).
- **Disclosure & legal:** the forward-looking footnote and Confidential (`complianceModel.js`, D1546).
- **Approvals:** the approvals D1570 stores.

Every read is for that version's id. There is no score and no verdict.

**Statuses.** Each row has one status: To fix, Worth a look, Couldn't check,
Stale, Needs approval, Qualitative by choice or Passed. "Checking…" shows while
the preflight measures. A read that failed, a check never run on the version,
and a card the page could not draw at export size are all "Couldn't check".
They are counted on their own and never as Passed. A failed read or a failed
card offers Try again. A version never checked offers Run the check to its
founder (`can_check`). Every finding links to its card in the Editor
(`?card=N`).

**The qualitative mark (D6066).** `qualitative: true` sits on the card in the
version's saved slides. When a card is not marked, the field is absent.
`sanitizeModel` keeps the field only when it is `true`, so an unmarked card
hashes as it did. `publicDeckOf` leaves it out of every outside reader. The
founders set and clear it from the Editor's card ⋯ ("Mark as intentionally
qualitative" / "Clear the mark"). It is a local edit, held until a version is
saved, and the strip tags the card "Qualitative". Review reads a marked card as
"Qualitative by choice". That row is not evidence, not a pass, and not counted
in the linked-figures line. It only stops Review from asking the card for a
figure. Every figure the card shows is still checked like any other. No
migration is needed and no route is added.

**Not changed.** The forward-looking wording stays with counsel (owner
decision T, #3716). Review reads `FLS_FOOTNOTE`'s rule and never restates its
words.
