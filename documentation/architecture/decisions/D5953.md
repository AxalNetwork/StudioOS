## D5953 — Country pricing: the apply writes the accepted minimum royalty into the schedule's minimum guarantee (#5953)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-14 by S07 for #5953. The owner's answer to Q9 was A: the accepted minimum royalty becomes
  the licence's minimum guarantee. Amends D5017 §3 and §6.
- **Number:** D5953 is this issue's number (D4106). No migration: `licence_terms.minimum_guarantee_cents` (migration
  512) and `licence_terms.revision` (`202611071531_licence_revisions.sql`) already exist.
- **Code:**
  - `cloudflare-worker/src/services/licencePricingApply.ts` (`CONTRACTUAL`, `scheduleOf`, `planApply`,
    `applyStatements`, `driftOf`);
  - `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/admin_licence_pricing_apply.ts` (the read, the apply's replies, the licence event);
  - `frontend/src/pages/admin/licences/countryPricingModel.js` (the panel's "Current licence" column and
    `changesContract`).
- **Tests:** `cloudflare-worker/test/licence_pricing_apply_5017.test.ts` (the D5953 cases),
  `frontend/test/licence_country_pricing_5017.test.mjs`.

### 1. The mapping: same unit, same period

| Pricing figure | Schedule term | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `minimum_royalty_cents` (recommended and accepted) | `licence_terms.minimum_guarantee_cents` (§4.4) | whole cents a year |

The two figures share their unit, so the value is copied as it is, with no conversion.

- **Cents.** The engine computes the minimum royalty in integer cents. It takes the model's ratio of the rounded
  annual fee, then rounds to the commercial increment (D5015). The schedule stores integer cents, `>= 0` by CHECK.
- **Per year.** The minimum royalty is a ratio of the annual fee. The agreement's §4.4 reads "at least
  {{terms.minimum_guarantee_cents}} for each year of the Term". Both figures are annual. `fee_invoiced` (annually or
  quarterly) governs how the fee is invoiced, not the guarantee's period, so it does not change the figure.
- **Currency.** `licence_terms` has no currency column. Its amounts are in the licence's currency
  (`territory_licences.currency`). The apply refuses a licence held in another currency (`409 currency_mismatch`, D5017
  §3) and sets the currency to the recommendation's in the same batch. The guarantee and its unit therefore move
  together.

### 2. One batch, one revision guard

After the decision insert and its `decisionGuard`, the apply's batch now holds up to three writes:

1. the core-terms `UPDATE`;
2. the schedule write, when the accepted minimum royalty is recorded;
3. the review close, when a review is named.

The decision insert names two things, and both must still hold at commit:

- `expected_terms_revision`, as before (D4694);
- the schedule row exactly as the route read it: absent, or at the revision it read. This check is
  `(SELECT revision FROM licence_terms WHERE licence_uid = ?) IS ?`, and it is bound only when the schedule is
  written.

A stale terms revision, or a schedule edited between the read and the commit, makes the decision insert change no row.
The guard then rolls the whole batch back. Nothing changes: no terms, no schedule and no decision. The answer is
`409 terms_conflict`, and its `current` carries `minimum_guarantee_cents` and `schedule_revision`. The client does not
send a schedule revision. The apply sets the guarantee HQ accepted; the guard only keeps the plan's "before" value and
its contractual-change verdict true at commit.

### 3. The schedule write reuses `PUT /:uid/terms`'s rule

`admin_licences_terms.ts` creates a missing schedule row at revision 1 and moves an existing row by exactly one
revision. The apply does the same, for the one key:

- **No `licence_terms` row: the apply creates it.** The new row is at revision 1 and holds only
  `minimum_guarantee_cents`. Every other term is null ("not recorded"). `sections_json` is `'{}'`, so the read gives the
  canvas's default sections, the same as a first `PUT` that overrides none.
- **A row exists:** only `minimum_guarantee_cents`, `updated_by_user_id`, `updated_at` and `revision + 1` change. A
  schedule editor holding the old revision then gets the `PUT`'s own `409 terms_conflict`, and D4819's
  `scheduleChangedBy` names the apply's actor.

The write is one fixed upsert: `INSERT … ON CONFLICT(licence_uid) DO UPDATE`.

### 4. Null is "not recorded", never 0

An accepted minimum royalty of `null` writes nothing to the schedule. This covers two cases: HQ sends
`accepted.minimum_royalty_cents: null` (an override, which needs its reason), or the recommendation has none. An
existing guarantee keeps its value and revision, and a licence with no schedule row gets none. A missing minimum
royalty never blocks an apply the way a missing annual fee or royalty does (`accepted_value_required`). The decision
row still records both the recommended and the accepted figure, `null` included.

### 5. A changed guarantee is a contractual change

`CONTRACTUAL` now names three figures, each with its store:

- the annual fee and the royalty, in `territory_licences` (`core`);
- the minimum royalty, in `licence_terms` (`schedule`).

A recorded minimum royalty that differs from the schedule's current guarantee is a contractual change. That includes a
guarantee where none was recorded. It lands in `changed_terms` as `minimum_guarantee_cents`. D5017 §6's rule then
applies unchanged:

- with a current contract, the apply needs `acknowledge_agreement_mismatch: true` (otherwise
  `409 agreement_ack_required`);
- afterwards the pricing read reports `agreement.state: 'mismatch'` until HQ instantiates the replacement.

The read's `terms` adds `minimum_guarantee_cents` and `schedule_revision`. Its `drift` adds `minimum_royalty_cents`
against the guarantee, and the panel's "Current licence" column shows the guarantee.

### 6. What does not change

- A refresh, a snapshot, opening a review and keep/postpone still write nothing to the licence. The schedule is
  included, and no schedule row is created.
- Issued `licence_fee_invoices` are never read or written. The test compares the rows before and after an apply. No
  invoice path reads `minimum_guarantee_cents` today. A shortfall invoice under §4.4 is not built here.
- The branch push is the existing one, after the commit (D272). It allocates the next `hq_revision`, and its copy's
  `terms_json` is read from `licence_terms` at push time, so it carries the new guarantee. The `terms_changed` event
  records the guarantee's before and after, and the schedule revision before and after.
- The decision table is unchanged. It already held `recommended_minimum_royalty_cents` and
  `accepted_minimum_royalty_cents`.
