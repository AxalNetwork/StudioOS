## D859 — Conversation items: what customers say in a chat, as placements the founder accepts

- **Status:** filed by S1 for the owner on 2026-10-22 as #2504 (W4 of #2500). Built for slot S10.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `3704a16a62`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Stacks on:** D658 and D757 (the interview engine, its placements and two passes), D754 (consent first), D629 and D844 (the AI token ledger), D765 (a chat's digest).
- **No migration.** Every item is a row in `validate_proposals` (migration 214/439) or, once accepted, in `interview_findings` (migration 489). Both tables already hold any kind, and `interview_findings`' module CHECK already admits `customer-research`.

### 1. The owner's ruling (option A, 2026-10-22)

The issue asked for a new store of "conversation items". The code already had one: the interview engine's placements. The owner chose A:

- every item is a Validate placement in `validate_proposals`, stored by `storePlacements`;
- the founder's Accept writes it through the module's own writer (`/placements/:id/accept`), and Discard is final;
- items use the kinds that already exist, plus two new finding kinds, `follow_up` and `prototype_ask`.

There is no second store, and no second accept path.

### 2. The mapping

| The customer said | Kind | Module |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a price they would pay | `demand_signal` (`would_pay`), with the amount | Demand signals |
| what they spend today | `budget`, with the amount | Demand signals |
| the tool they use today | `competitor_mention` | Market intelligence |
| their team size | `firmographic` (`company_size`) | Ideal customer profile |
| how often they do it | `job_to_be_done`, "what · how often" | Ideal customer profile |
| a pain | `pain_severity` | Problem discovery |
| a follow-up, an intro offered, a beta asked for | `follow_up` (`kind`: `follow_up`, `intro_offer`, `beta_signup`) | the interview (Customer research) |
| a feature asked for, a workflow described | `prototype_ask`, with `build_candidate: true` | the interview (Customer research) |

The two new kinds (`services/interviewFindings.ts`):
- **`follow_up`:** `{ kind, detail ≤ 200, due_hint? ≤ 80 }`, keyed `follow_up:<kind>:<detail>`.
- **`prototype_ask`:** `{ feature ≤ 200, workflow? ≤ 300, pain_ref? ≤ 120, build_candidate: true }`, keyed `prototype:<feature>`.

Both are in `PLACEMENT_KINDS` and in pass B, so the structure step and the MCP `propose_placements` tool know them too. Nothing here writes to Build. PIPE-BUILD (#2426) reads accepted `prototype_ask` findings by their `build_candidate` flag.

Once accepted, both show on the interview's Customer research record, under "From this conversation" (`services/records/validate.ts`).

### 3. As built

**`services/conversationItems.ts`:**
- **`extractConversationItems(env, { sessionId, userId, model?, rail?, maxChars? })`** runs in this order:
  1. the member and the venture are re-checked (`writableSession`);
  2. consent (`sessionDigestConsentBlocks`): a blocked session is not read;
  3. the final transcript (`finalSegments`), split without cutting a segment (`chunkSegments`, `transcriptLimit`);
  4. one model call per part, task `extract_conversation_items`, through `aiRouter.run`;
  5. `storePlacements(…, dedupe: true, task: 'extract_conversation_items')`.
- A refused call stops the run, stores nothing and returns `{ ok: false, reason: 'refused' }`.

**The engine** (`services/validateEngine.ts`): after a successful structure stage, a session submitted through a messaging provider (`MESSAGING_PROVIDERS`) runs the extraction too. A refusal adds a note ("Axal did not yet list the actions and data points in this conversation: …") and the structure stands. A recording runs no extraction. (A call or an upload did not either, until D873 §6, #2520.)

**The router:** `extract_conversation_items` routes as `structure_interview` does, and its catalogue line is "Find actions and data points in a conversation".

### 4. The rules

- **Consent first (D754).** A chat whose consent gate blocks a digest is never read, and nothing is called or charged.
- **Every item cites its passage:** a verbatim quote of at most three segments whose span matches the cite, as `parsePlacements` requires. An item with no passage is dropped.
- **Money is only a quoted figure (`money_term`, #2431).** An amount is kept only when that figure is in the quote. A price or a spend whose figure the customer did not say is dropped, never estimated. A tool's figure that was not said is removed, and the tool is kept.
- **Confidence.** A data point below `MIN_CONFIDENCE` (0.5) is dropped.
- **Idempotent.** A re-run proposes nothing the interview already has, in any status. An accepted or discarded item never comes back.
- **Metered.** Each call holds and settles the member's AI tokens (D629, D844). With none left, nothing runs.
- **Chat text is data, never instructions.** The prompt says so, and the transcript is delimited per call (`transcriptData`).
- **Unknown is NULL.** An optional field the customer did not give is absent, not guessed.

### 5. Not here

- Translation (W2, #2502, D857): until it lands, segments are read as stored. When it does, `finalSegments` reads its text and nothing here changes.
- Telegram (#2512, D866) and Meeting Capture (#2521, D874), which stack on this.
- Build's own read of `prototype_ask` (PIPE-BUILD, #2426).
