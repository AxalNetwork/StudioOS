- **Route:** `/deals/:dealId/closing`, the Closing sub-page of `DealRoomPage.jsx`. No new route. #8838's "Open my closing room" (PR #8922) links here and is unchanged.
- **Files:** `frontend/src/pages/closing/InvestorRoom.jsx` (the investor's room, and `InvestorNotOpened` for DE38 and DE39), `investorRoomModel.js` (pure rules), `roomShared.jsx` (the read hook, paper reads, no-access card and cells, moved unchanged out of `RecordRoom.jsx`). `RecordRoom.jsx` hands an investor to `InvestorRoom`; the founder's and Admin's room is unchanged. `ClosingRoom.jsx` draws DE38 for an investor until the studio opens a closing. `RoomDialogs.jsx` `PaymentDrawer` gains the change notice and Mask now.

### What is built

The header (back to the deal, "Your closing room", company and round with the closing's chip, instrument, round total, who opened it and when, "your line only"); the seven steps with the one that is now (`aria-current="step"`) and the sentence under them; the line card (chip, amount, route, held for, money words, round total); the paper with its signers in order and who signs next; the deadline tile; the firm's committee conditions (open, met, waived, each with its day); the evidence's receiving and sending sides kept apart; the one primary action by step (Review and sign → See payment instructions → "Waiting for the company's bank" shown disabled); the payment sheet's eyebrow, change notice and Mask now; after Closed the chip with its day, the receipt day, what you hold, reporting duties and Open in Portfolio; before a closing, the investor's own commitment and Back to Investment, or Refused when they have none; the states; the phone's step card, folded conditions and evidence rows and sticky primary; dark.
