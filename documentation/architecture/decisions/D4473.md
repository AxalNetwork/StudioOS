## D4473 — One sidebar that is the union of held licences (#4473)

Part 2 of #3704, on the Worker contract of #4472 (D4472, merged as #4504). The owner's rule: no licence switcher anywhere; a member holding several licences sees all of their sections in one left sidebar; Portfolio and Companies are never sidebar rows.

### What was decided

**The builder.** `frontend/src/lib/licenceSidebar.js` exports `LICENCE_ORDER` (Founder, Investor, Advisor, Operating Partner) and a pure `buildLicenceSidebar(licences, { primaryRole })`. It returns one flat group in the `SIDEBAR_GROUPS` shape (`key: 'home'`, `label: ''`, plus `licenceShell: true`):
- the shared head once: Studio, Spin-Out Lab;
- each held licence's own rows, in licence order whatever the input order;
- the fixed foot: Trust, Network, Research.

Rows are taken by `to` from the per-role blocks in `sidebarConfig.js`, which stay the row source and are not changed. A shared row comes from the primary licence's block first. A repeated `to` is dropped; Portfolio and Companies (`/portfolio`, `/account/portfolio`, `/account/companies`) are never emitted.

**The held set.** `resolveHeldLicences` sits beside `resolveActiveRole` in `lib/activeRole.js`, so the shell, the routes and the menus read one rule:
- an admin's "View as" preview is `[viewMode]`;
- an impersonation is the impersonated member's `licences`;
- everyone else is `user.licences`, falling back to `[user.role]`.

`effectiveRole` keeps its meaning: the primary role, or the previewed or impersonated one.

**The shell.** `getSidebarGroups` builds the licence sidebar whenever `shellRoleFor` gives a licence. Admin, super_admin, branch_admin and exploring keep their own blocks, and the persona group still goes after group 0. In a licence shell, `withDiligenceRow` and `withInfluencerRow` place their rows after the last licence row and before Trust, Diligence first. A staff shell keeps the old positions. Diligence is asked whenever any held licence is a diligence licence.

**Routes.**
- `RoleGuard`, and through it `guard` and `labRoles`, admits a route when `effectiveRole` or any held licence is in its list.
- A branch that picks the page of a licence-owned section uses `holds(licence)`:
  - Founder: Raise, Grow, Build, the execution editor, `/execution`, the data room's investor redirect, Pitch's boundary, `/build/competitors`;
  - Investor: `/deals`, `/funds`, `/funds/performance`;
  - Operating Partner: `/pipeline` (then the investor redirect, then the fallback) and `/comarketing`.
- Shared routes stay on `effectiveRole`, so they render the primary licence's view: Network, Research, Trust, Perks, Profile, Studio, Spin-Out Lab (until part 3).

**Account menu.** D4416 already moved the account Portfolio out of the sidebar, its foot and the HQ shells, into the Account menu above Billing. This keeps that place. Account Portfolio reads "Companies" when Investor is held; the investment holdings "Portfolio" (`/portfolio`) follow it when Investor is held; then "Licences" (`/account/licences`) for every member account but an admin's. "Your profile" and Perks follow the held set. The phone account sheet carries the same three rows (`accountRowsFor`). The phone company sheet's Portfolio row is removed.

**Account › Licences.** `routes/account_licences.jsx` with `pages/account/AccountLicences.jsx` reads `GET /api/account/licences`. It lists each held licence with its label, "Primary", when and how it was granted and its plan; a value not recorded reads "Not recorded". It also lists the licences that can be added, each a link to `/help`: "Contact Axal VC to add this licence". A failed read is a fixed sentence. The page has no switch.

**Admin grants.** The account drawer's Profile tab (`/admin/accounts/all/:id`) gains a Licences block (`AccountLicencesBlock`). It lists the member's licences through the admin GET and grants or revokes through the POST and DELETE. Refusals map to fixed sentences by code, `primary_licence` included.

**Phone.** A member holding Founder gets the founder tab plan over the built sidebar, `mobilePlan('founder', { founder: builtGroups })`. The four cycle tabs stay, and every other row lands in More in sidebar order. Members without Founder get no bar, as before.

**Guard.** `frontend/test/no_licence_switcher_3704.test.mjs` pins three things:
- no profile or licence switcher, active-licence state or control mapping over `licences` in the sidebar, the company switcher, the phone company sheet or `App.jsx`; the admin `PortalSwitcher` is the named exception;
- no Portfolio or Companies row for any of the 15 licence sets;
- no `/account/portfolio` in `SidebarNav.jsx`.

### Departures from the issue text, and why

- **Account Portfolio's position.** The issue asks for the three items right after "Account". D4416, the owner's decision of 2026-11-04 on this same menu, places account Portfolio immediately above Billing. The new items follow it there, in the issue's order.
- **Step 4 was largely done on `main`.** D4416 had already removed `withPortfolioRow`, `PORTFOLIO_ROW` and the sidebar foot link; the issue's line numbers predate it. What remained was the phone company sheet's row.
- **`lib/sidebarConfig.js` is `frontend/src/sidebarConfig.js`** on current `main`.

### Not decided here
