### What the canvas decides

- Search by capability: a "What it does" fieldset with one of the twelve attributes and an optional whole-word term. Only companies with a supporting sourced fact are listed, in name order, each with the fact, its statement type, source, Published and Retrieved; the search is kept in the address (RCQ-01).
- Two counts sit beside the results, each in its own words: companies with a recorded fact that does not support the search, and companies with nothing recorded (Needs research). Neither is a rank or a score.
- A result found on another field says so in one line, "Matched on other name: … · source …", with a fixed precedence; case and accent folding finds "Müster" from "muster", and a register number is searchable (RCQ-02).
- Saved searches live in a menu beside "Copy link to these results": save with a name, open, rename, delete with a review step, a cap of 20. A saved search keeps criteria, never results; a criterion that no longer exists is named and nothing runs until the member chooses (RCQ-03).
- The Shortlist panel sits on the project Overview beside the Progress card. Each subject (company or fund) has a rationale, a note marked "Only you see this", a status, a next action and a due date, edited in place; On hold, Withdrawn and no longer in the directory are shown with their words (RCQ-04).
- States include three empties, Unavailable for the fact search and for notes while the subjects stay, Refused for an Advisor with who can grant access, an archived project, saving, save failed with text kept, and a note changed elsewhere refused (RCQ-05).
