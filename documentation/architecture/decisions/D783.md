## D783 — a plan includes 25% of its price as AI allowance each month; no plan, no allowance; no daily cap

- **Status:** decided by the owner on 2026-10-19 and filed by S1 as #2124. Built by S24.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `98295a23`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Stacks on:** D629 (caps, top-ups and the hold before every run), D640 (review holds), D755 (token packs and the public plans-and-packs read), D760 and D762 (cost and billed price), and D769 (Add tokens).

### 1. The decision

The owner, 2026-10-19: "Customers get 25% in token credits of the total subscription cost, 20$ subscription would offer 5$ in tokens. … if you don't want to get a plan, you need to top up."

The owner then answered S1's four questions:

- **No paid plan, no allowance.** Every run uses top-up tokens. This covers free founders, free investors, advisors and partners without a plan.
- **A yearly plan gets a monthly share:** 25% of the yearly price ÷ 12, reset each month.
- **No daily limit.** The month's allowance is the only limit a member has.
- **Billing at × 1.25** is #2123's, not this decision's.

On #2124:

- **Q1, which price counts:** answer A. A new table records the member's own price from the webhook. A subscriber with no recorded row falls back to the catalogue's price, labelled as such. Nothing is backfilled.
- **Q2, Registered Agent:** it is a company service and gives no allowance.

### 2. What was built

**The figure.** `services/planAllowance.ts` holds the rule:

- `PLAN_AI_SHARE = 0.25` is the share.
- `allowanceFromCharge` gives `floor(cents × 2500 ÷ months)` micro-USD, where a month is 1 and a year is 12, times `interval_count`.
- `planAllowanceFor` sums the member's active member plans.

**Which plans count.** A member plan counts only when it is in Stripe's `active` status, for one of five families: founder tier, investor tier (the primary account; a seat colleague gets nothing), Market Intelligence Pro, and the advisor and partner persona plans. A trial and `past_due` do not count. Registered Agent never counts.

**The store.** Migration 505 adds `member_subscription_amounts`, one row per Stripe subscription with the member's own amount, interval and status.

- **Writes.** `routes/billing.ts` writes the row on `customer.subscription.created` and `updated` (`recordSubscriptionAmount`), and marks it canceled on `deleted`.
  - It uses the price Stripe already sends: Σ `unit_amount × quantity` over the items, at the first item's interval.
  - A canceled row is never reactivated.
  - A failed write is logged without the member's data and never fails the webhook.
- **Fallback.** A member holding a plan with no recorded row (subscribed before 505) gets the catalogue's current price from `stripe_products` (`source: 'catalogue'`). A family with any recorded row never falls back.
- **No plan** gives `source: 'none'` and $0.

**The caps.** `resolveAiCaps` (`services/aiBudget.ts`):

- **A member** gets `monthly_microusd` from their plans, and `daily_capped: false`. `daily_microusd` equals the month's figure, so the existing hold SQL, which takes the smaller of the two, never stops on a day.
- **Refusals.** `reserveAiBudget` refuses a member with `budget_user_month` only.
- **The admin licence** keeps its typed caps (`ai_licence_caps`, then the vars, then the defaults) and its day.
- **Kept as they were:** the organisation cap and the kill switch.

**Typed member caps are retired.**

- `PUT /api/ai-budget/caps/:licence` answers 409 `ai_allowance_computed` for every licence but `admin`.
- `GET /api/ai-budget/caps` lists member licences as `source: 'computed'`, read-only, with `ignored_recorded` set when a row typed before D783 exists. Such a row stays in the table and is no longer read. On production the table was empty when #2124 was filed.
- The same read adds `share` and `catalogue` (each plan's price, share and allowance).

**The reads.**

- **`GET /api/ai-budget/me`** carries `daily_capped` and `plan`: the share, the month, and each plan's family, source, interval and allowance. It carries no amounts paid.
- **The Super Admin's member read** (`GET /api/ai-budget/members/:id`) shows the same projection (`planView` in `aiBudget.ts`), so no read names what a member pays.
- **`GET /api/public/plans-and-packs`** gives each paid plan `{share, monthly_microusd, source: 'plan'}` from its headline price (monthly first, else yearly), and each price its `allowance_microusd`. A free plan gets $0 with `source: 'none'`. Its `resets` line now says "Resets on the 1st of each month at 00:00 UTC."
- **`services/aiSpend.ts` and the Validate session receipt** report no day cap for a member (`null`).

**The words.** `frontend/src/lib/planAllowance.js`:

- **A plan card** reads "Includes $19.75 of AI each month: 25% of the price. Past it, runs use your tokens." Market Intelligence Pro gives its share like any other member plan.
- **With no plan** the line reads "No plan: every run uses your tokens."
- **Where the Today row is dropped:** the rail's Usage box, the usage card and `/account/usage` draw no Today row when `daily_capped` is false. With no plan, the rail shows the no-plan line and Add tokens.
- **HQ › AI allowances** shows member licences as computed (no day, 25%, no Edit) and adds a card of the catalogue's plans with price, share and allowance.

### 3. Not built here

- **Billing in billed dollars** (Build step 4). `billingMode` still returns `'cost'` on this base, although `AI_BILLING = "charged"` is set in `wrangler.toml`; D762's reader is not merged. The allowance is spent at whatever `billingMode` settles at. The billed-dollar test lands with D762.
- **No backfill** of `member_subscription_amounts` (Q1, answer A). The catalogue fallback covers existing subscribers until their next `customer.subscription.updated`.
