- **Status:** built by S39 (Claude Code), 2026-11-19. Part 2 of #4653; part 1 is `D4653.md` (#6646). No migration, no route, no price or limit change.
- **Source:** Session 1's register note on #4653 (2026-11-16, from the public-site plan, tracker #6976, row E17).

### What changed

The Operating Partner plans advertised two features that do not run:

- **"Verification badges"** (Partner Basic). D676 dropped them from `/for-service-partners` because `kyb_verified` is never set and no KYB or conflict check runs.
- **"Payouts through Stripe Connect"** (Operating Partner Pro). D676 dropped them because Stripe Connect is not configured for partners.

Both are removed wherever a plan is presented to a buyer or member:

| Where | File |
| --- | --- |
| The catalogue's `included` lines, which the public `plans-and-packs` read and Checkout's summary project | `cloudflare-worker/src/services/planCatalogue.ts` |
| Pricing's Operating Partner cards and their Compare rows | `frontend/src/lib/sitePricing.js` |
| Billing's partner plan lists (as "Stripe Connect payouts" and "Verified partner badge") | `frontend/src/pages/account/BillingSection.jsx` |

Nothing was substituted for them: D676's rule is to drop an unbacked claim, not to reword it. The Compare table keeps one cell per card. `frontend/test/pricing_partner_claims_4653.test.mjs` fails if either claim returns to the catalogue, the public read, any Pricing tab's cards or comparison, or Billing's partner lists.

This settles what #6985 (X3) waited on: the audience pages may take plan identity and order from the catalogue, and Pricing's partner bullets now carry no claim D676 removed.
