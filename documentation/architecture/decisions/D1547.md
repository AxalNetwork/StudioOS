### What it is

`documentation/operations/DECK_GRID.md` holds the four procedures HQ runs on the deck builder's generation path: switch off a model, refund a batch, unstick a batch, and purge an image, including a DMCA takedown. Each procedure starts with the route that exists today, then the SQL for the step no route covers.

The page says where a route does exist, including the batch refund (`POST /api/ai-budget/deck-batches/:uid/refund`), the daily stuck-run sweep, and the HQ image takedown. It does not settle a hold by hand: settling is `settleAiReservation`.
