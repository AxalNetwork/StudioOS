## D590 — The incorporate co-founder agreement route stays

**`/incorporate/cofounder-agreement` stays.** The owner's answer on #1217 (from #1009, D352), moved from S07 to S06, is A: keep the route. D352 retired nothing and left retirement on the deferred list. Session 1 confirmed A, the same way #1209 kept `/incorporate`.

The Lab page at `/spinout-lab/cofounder-agreement` admits admins and active Lab members (`labRoles(['admin'])`). The incorporate wizard admits admins, founders and partners (`labRoles(['admin', 'founder', 'partner'])`), and an active Lab member's own role through that same helper. Partners reach the wizard only through this guard. `cofounderAgreementRoute` sends an admin or an active Lab account to the Lab page and everyone else to the wizard, so a founder outside the Lab is not sent to a page that would turn them away.

The Legal Engine card Founders & Agreements still embeds `CofounderAgreementPage` behind the Studio gate. Send for Signature's not-offered Co-founder Agreement entry still links to `/incorporate/cofounder-agreement`. The placement list still names that path.

Retiring the wizard, as D361 retired the 83(b) page, would drop partners and would make the Lab page serve founders outside the Lab. That is not this decision. No route changes. No migration.
