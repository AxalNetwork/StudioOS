## D590 — Spin-Out Lab: /incorporate/cofounder-agreement is retained (D352's default)

**Issue:** #1217, from #1009 (Session 7's line "Whether to retire /incorporate/cofounder-agreement"; D352). **No migration.**

### What was decided

Option **A** was confirmed by Session 1: `/incorporate/cofounder-agreement` is kept as D352's stated default, matching the retention of `/incorporate` (#1209).

### Why the route stays

1. **Role admission integrity:** `/incorporate/cofounder-agreement` admits founders, partners, and admins (`['admin', 'founder', 'partner']`). The Lab counterpart `/spinout-lab/cofounder-agreement` is scoped strictly to active Lab cohort members and admins. Retiring the route would lock out partners from co-founder drafting.
2. **Access for non-Lab founders:** Founders outside the Spin-Out Lab programme need a drafting wizard without requiring Lab enrollment or membership state.
3. **Studio gate preservation:** The Legal Engine's Founders & Agreements card (`LegalEnginePage.jsx`) embeds `CofounderAgreementPage` behind the Studio tier gate. Retiring the standalone route would complicate the tier gating structure.
4. **Active inbound links remain valid:**
   - `cofounderAgreementRoute(user)` in `SpinoutLabStartupPage.jsx` directs active Lab members and admins to `/spinout-lab/cofounder-agreement` and all other founders/partners to `/incorporate/cofounder-agreement`.
   - The Legal Engine's Founders & Agreements card renders `CofounderAgreementPage`.
   - `services/esignOriginators.ts` (`NOT_OFFERED`) points to `/incorporate/cofounder-agreement`.
   - `lib/adminPlacement.js` lists the standalone path in the admin catalog.
