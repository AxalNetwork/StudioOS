## D6020 — Change an evidence link's strength from the hypothesis record (#6020)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-14 by S15. Filed under #4837.
- **No migration.** `hypothesis_evidence_links.updated_at` and its `strength` check already exist (migration 460).

### What changed

- **Writer.** `setLinkStrength` (`services/validateWriters.ts`) takes a claim, a link (`pain-<id>` or `evidence-<id>`, the ids `evidenceFor` puts in each item's `link`) and a strength (`anecdote`, `pattern` or `measured`).
  - A pain link must be this claim's; it is then changed by the existing `setEvidenceStrength`, which logs `evidence_strength_set` on the claim and on its theme, as the `PUT /evidence-links/:id/strength` route always did.
  - An evidence link must be this claim's and this venture's. It had no update writer before. It is updated in one batch with an `evidence_strength_set` row on the claim (`validate.experiments`).
  - Anything else answers 404 and writes nothing. A malformed link or strength answers 400. Summaries hold the claim's code and the strength word, never the evidence's label.
- **Record action.** The hypothesis record declares `evidence-strength`, "Change strength", a form with the link and the strength. It names its link in `references`, as Unlink does, so the route proves the link before the writer runs.
- **Row action.** The records contract gives a row one `action`. Each Evidence row's action is now Change strength, with its own link in the body. Unlink evidence stays among the record's own actions, where its form already names the link. So the Evidence rows lose their Unlink button, and nothing in the renderer changes.
  - When a contract for more than one action per row lands (#6113's `Row.actions`), Unlink can go back on the row beside Change strength.
- **Readers.** The route already strips row actions and record actions for anyone below the write gate. A reader sees the strength and has no control.
- **Client.** Six `cycleValidateApi` methods that no page called and the records' actions and forms make redundant are removed: `addSegment`, `setInterviewSegment`, `addDemandSignal`, `setDemandSignalStatus`, `addCompetitorMention`, `setHypothesisPlan`. Their Worker routes stay. `setEvidenceStrength` and `setWaitlistTarget` stay.

### What did not change

The experiments list's Strength filter, its Strength column and the record's Strength fact read the claim's strongest **pain link** (`claimsOf`). A pain link's new strength reaches them on the next read. An evidence link's strength shows on its Evidence row only, as it did when the link was added.

### Owner decisions (2026-11-14)

1. **Strength stays pain-links-only** (D4585). The claim's Strength (list filter, column, record fact) counts pain links only. An evidence link's strength shows on its own Evidence row only. This limit is deliberate: the acceptance line "the list's Strength filter reflects it" holds for pain links.
2. **Ship as built.** Change strength is each Evidence row's action, and Unlink evidence moves to the record's header actions. Once #6329 merges (`Row.actions`), a follow-up puts Unlink back on the row beside Change strength.
