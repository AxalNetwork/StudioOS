## D4631 part 4 — Investor plan tiering on deals follows the held Investor licence (#4631)

The owner answered **A** on #4631 (2026-11-16): a member who holds Investor on their own Investor plan is tiered as an investor, whatever their primary role. The deal-room allowance and the Free-tier deal projection follow the held licence. This matches the owner's answer A on #4472, which [D4729](./D4729.md) built for the investor plan gates. It extends [D4631](./D4631.md) and [part 3](./D4631-p3.md), which had left this site on the primary role as the owner's call.

| Surface (`routes/deals.ts`) | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/deals/:id`, Free-tier projection | Applies when the caller reads this deal as an investor (the `dealAudience` relationship) and their own Investor plan reads Free. Deal staff and the venture's own founder keep their projections, whatever Investor plan they also hold. |
| Deal-room allowance: `GET /dealroom/quota` and `POST /:id/dealroom/join` | The admin is never capped. A caller who holds Investor is capped by their Investor plan, including in the write that checks the allowance. A partner or advisor primary without Investor stays uncapped, as before. |
| `GET /dealroom/quota`, rooms listed | Narrowed by the active firm for anyone who holds Investor, as the join and the deal list already were. |

The plan is read by the existing `effectiveInvestorTier` (`services/licencePlans.ts`, D4729): a held Investor licence with no plan of its own reads Free, and an ended trial reads Free. A single-licence investor sees no change. A revoked Investor licence is not held, so the caller falls back to their primary role's access. This supersedes the `routes/deals.ts` entries that D4472's retained-check inventory and D4729's "left on the primary" list record for this projection, the allowance and the quota's firm narrowing.

Verification: `cloudflare-worker/test/deal_investor_tiering_4631.test.ts` exercises the real router behind the production investor plan gate on SQLite with synthetic users. It compares a combined-licence account with a single-licence investor on Free and paid plans, and covers an ended trial, a revoked licence, staff and the venture's founder, the firm-narrowed quota, concurrent joins and uncapped staff. Each new rule was mutation-checked.
