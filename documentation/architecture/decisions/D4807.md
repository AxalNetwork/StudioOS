## D4807 — one entitlement predicate for "is this account a mentor today?" (#4807)

**Status:** part 1 of #4807. The remaining items on the issue (five-dimension status, KPI reconciliation, historical participation episodes, read-state fixes) follow in later parts.

## Decision

An account is an eligible mentor when `users.is_active = 1` (D960) AND it holds the Advisor licence, as the primary role or as an additional `account_licences` row that is not revoked (D4472). That rule is written once, in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/mentorEligibility.ts`, and every SQL read that needs it writes `{ELIGIBLE}` and passes the statement through `withMentorEligible`.

## Why

Three answers existed. The assignable list and the access gate honoured an additional licence; the catalogue's mentor count, cohort coverage, the "no longer an Advisor" warning and the opt-out total tested the primary role only. A valid secondary Advisor could be assigned and read a cohort's founders, yet was not counted, left the cohort reading "no mentor", and was flagged as having lost the role.

## Consequences

- The assignment list carries `advisor_eligible`; the Programs pages read it and fall back to the primary role for an older read.
- A revoked licence, a changed role and a deactivated account each remove eligibility at once, because the rule reads the live rows.
- A database without `account_licences` falls back to the primary role.
- Tests: `cloudflare-worker/test/mentor_eligibility_4807.test.ts`, `frontend/test/mentor_eligible_rows_4807.test.mjs`.

## Part 5 — five dimensions, honest loading and failure, scope, title

- **Five dimensions, never one status word.** Each program shows **Build state**, **Operation**, **Member access**, **Studio offering** and **Studio grants** as five labelled rows on its card and in its page header (`programDimensions` in `programsModel.js`). Learning can be Live and running yet "Not open to members yet"; a program can be held by HQ (the offering policy) while no studio holds it (the grants, from D956's per-viewer grant field). A paused program records no build state here rather than a guessed one; a grant field that was not read says "Not read here yet", and one that could not be read says "Could not be read".
- **A registry that has not answered is not a fact.** While the catalogue's registry read is loading or has failed, the cards keep their doors, but their status and grant are "Reading…" / "Could not be read" (and the dimensions say the same), never the constant's "Live" or "HQ-held" standing in for what was read.
- **Stale is its own state.** When a card list is the last answer for the same scope carried while the next period reads (part 2 sets `cards.stale`), the catalogue says so in a status line.
- **"Scope", not "Studio".** The selector offers HQ-held accounts as well as studios, so it is called Scope. The scope's identity is also part of every read's cache key (`catalogue:<weeks>:<scope>`; part 2 binds the answer to it).
- **A tab title that names the page.** Programs sets `Programs · HQ — Axal VC StudioOS` (or the studio's name), and each program page `<Program> · Programs — Axal VC StudioOS`, through `useProgramsTitle`, which restores the previous title on leaving and fires no public pageview.
- Already on main and not repeated here: the period and Chart/Table controls expose a selected state (`radiogroup` with `aria-checked`, #4873).
