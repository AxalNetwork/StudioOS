### 3. What is wired today

- **People.** Company: the members of `GET /company/:uid`, with Change role (`PATCH …/members/:userId`) and Remove (`DELETE`) for an editor (`companyPowers(…).editor`, or a platform admin). Project: the members of `GET /projects/:id/members`, with Remove for its manager. A title or authority the store does not hold is "Not recorded" (a project seat has neither).
- **Another member's email is never drawn.** The Worker still sends them (#3841 stops it); the model keeps an email on your own row only. Invitation addresses are masked, as the canvas draws them (owner question T3).
- **Invitations.** Company: every status with Resend and Revoke by state (TM2c). Resend issues a new token, so the old link dies at once: when the email does not send, the new link is handed to the editor in the same copy-link toast a new invitation uses, and the row stays Pending with "email not sent", never "Resent". "Resent" is read from the invitation (its live link is newer than the first: the invitation DTO carries no `updated_at`, and resend restarts the 14 days, so the live link went out 14 days before `expires_at`), so it survives a reload; the line says "resent <that day>". A member who joined through an invitation accepted this week is highlighted with "Joined <day>". Project: the pending list with its source; Revoke is wired; Resend is off with its reason until #3841; a link invitation says its link is shown once.
- **Invite people.** Company with role, title and authority, the authority's meaning and the ladder's suggestion read from `team-vocabulary` (the title hint names the vocabulary's own functions). Project by email or by co-founder match, as a radio group; "Link an existing Axal account" is off until #3841's name search, its reason read with that option only. Both list the invitations already pending (TM2b).
- **Change role and Remove** offer only what the Worker allows: Change role opens on the current role and Save stays off until another listed role is chosen; Remove is not offered on a project's Owner or a company's only primary admin.
- **Project settings › General** and **Move under / out of a company** on `POST /api/projects/:id/move`.
- **The sidebar's bottom block** by scope (§5).

### 4. Behind the seam, and what waits for which issue

`frontend/src/pages/team/agentsAdapter.js` is the only door to agents. Every function answers `{ available: false, reason: "AI agents aren't switched on yet." }` on this base, and each screen draws that honestly: "… That is not the same as having none." ### 5. Authority by scope and the sidebar block

`lib/settingsScope.js` decides the scope in one pure function, `scopeFor`, from data that exists: the device's active company, and a project's own `company_id` (with its company's uid and name from `GET /api/account/projects` or the switcher's list). Three kinds: `company`, `company_project` and `project`.

- **The block reads "Company settings"** in a company or a project inside one: Team · Company settings (General, Legal) · Projects, with the project in view nested under Projects (Team, Settings). Legal is drawn without an address and says it is not open yet: the company legal page is #3697 part 1 (D1415), not on this base.
- **The block reads "Project settings"** in a standalone project: Team · Project settings (General). **No Legal entry, anywhere.**
- Billing is a link under the block, not in it. "Companies and projects" stays the last row (D1140's pin).
- The Team page, Project settings and the project page (`ProjectDetail`) publish the scope they draw (`usePublishScope`), once their read is ready and never for a refusal, so the sidebar names the same scope as the page: a standalone project reads "Project settings" on `/projects/:id` too. With none in view the block follows the active company, and with no company it is absent.
- **Projects lists every project of the company** (from `GET /api/account/projects`) on the company's and its projects' pages, each linking to its Team; the project in view also shows Team and Settings. Without that read (the sidebar on any other page) it lists none, and invents no "none".
- **One page lights one row.** A parent whose own page is one of its children ("Company settings" and "General") is drawn as their label. "Company settings" and "General" open only the company active on this device (`CompanySettingsAt`), so for another company they are drawn off with that reason.
- **The company's authority over a project's team is not live.** The owner's rule says a company's editors manage every project under it; the Worker's `isProjectManager` gains that branch in #3697 part 2. Until then `COMPANY_AUTHORITY_LIVE` is false and the page says only what is true: "This project sits under …. Its own Owner manages this team today." The move copy promises only what `POST /move` does. "Company editors with access" (TM1f) is drawn only when a read supplies `company_editors` (#3697 part 2), and "You manage this project through …" (TM9a4) waits for the same branch.

### 6. Deviations from the canvas, and why

- **No AIRail and no Ask panel on these routes.** The company form is under Account, which has no rail by the owner's standing rule (`NO_RAIL_ROUTES`); the project forms are not in a rail shell (owner question T1). The rail itself is unchanged. `AskButton` is "Ask" with the accessible name "Ask Eadwyn" and draws only where a panel serves the page, so it is absent here today. The rail's states (TM9c2, TM9c3, TM9c5, TM9c7) are not drawn.
- **No invitation message field** (TM2a, TM2b): no store keeps one, and its field trigger waits for #3693.
- **No "Copy link" on an old link invitation:** only the token's hash is stored; the button says so.
- **Every agent named carries the "AI agent" pill,** including the places the canvas drops it (TM4a "Held by", TM7a's cards); role cards keep the tile alone.
- **The owner's full list of editors** replaces the canvas's "Owner, Admin, Founder" (TM8a, TM9a3): the primary admin and an Axal VC admin are editors too.
- **Project people show "Not recorded"** for Title and Authority, which the canvas fills: no project store holds them (owner Q4 open).
- **The tablet sidebar's collapsed block** reads "Team" and "Set." with the full label as the tooltip and the accessible name, not the canvas's colliding initials (TM9e).
- **No Ask button yet (TMA).** Ask opens #3706's Ask panel, which is not on this base. The only Eadwyn panel that exists is D698's phone panel, which shows the rail's body and closes at 1024px, so `AskButton` renders nothing and registers nothing; `AskButtonView` waits for #3706 or #3693.
- **Billing under the block** is a link to Account › Billing, marked "pending owner" as the authority addendum leaves it.
- **No "Invited" rows in People (TM1a).** An invited person is listed once, in Invitations inside the same card, with its state pill; a second, Invited row in the People table would list the same person twice. People's Status is "Active" for every member, which is what a member row is.
- **TM9c3 and TM9c5 are not built.** TM9c3's page part ("Eadwyn is paused for this account…" and "Runs are off while Eadwyn is paused. Nothing is lost.") waits for the agents' run path (#3842) to read the account's pause; TM9c5's read-only HQ view ("Read only. Changes are off while HQ reads this record.", the agents footnote, no head actions) waits for an HQ read of a member's Team, which has no route.
- **TM9a5 draws the lock line only.** The locked "Models allowed for this project's agents" and "Monthly cap per agent · set by the company" fields have no store (no per-company model list or cap, §4), so they are not drawn as values.
- **TM9c6 says "You can't see this team."** A non-member's read is a 404 that does not name the company, and the switcher lists only memberships, so "You're not a member of <name>" has no name to say.
- **The project forms admit what `/projects/:id` admits** (admin, founder, partner, investor), not the advisor profile; the company forms admit what Company settings admits, advisor included.
- **The Spin-Out Lab gate** shows only when the Worker locks invites, in its own words; there is no "Gate met" count to read.
