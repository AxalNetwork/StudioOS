### V7, the list (`/validate/experiments`)

- **Columns:** Code, Claim, Evidence state, Relationships, Distinct sources, Sprint, Owner, Source. The read names them (`LISTS.experiments.cols` in `services/cycleValidate.ts`, so D661's rule holds: the read owns every column label and the adapter keys each one); `adaptRecords` honours a module's `mapFilters` for the methodology's filter words. D4585's interview-only support is the Relationships cell.
- **Code and Claim:** split from the read's `hypothesis` ("H2 · claim") at its first separator.
- **Relationships:** supporting, contradicting and unknown fit together, each a word with a glyph (`↑ 1 supports · ↓ 1 contradicts · ? 2 fit unknown`), never colour alone. They count interview relationships, not sources.
- **Distinct sources:** the distinct interviews, the organizations behind them (left out when not recorded, never 0), and the other linked evidence by kind.
- Nothing read is "Not recorded", never 0. Rows still open the hypothesis.
- **Source:** D1327's Fill-with-Eadwyn mark stays as the last column.

### V8, one hypothesis (`/validate/experiments/:id`)

- **Computed state and decision apart:** `HypothesisRecord.jsx` draws "Computed evidence state · Mixed · from linked evidence, not proof" apart from "Founder decision · recorded in Verdict · Record a decision →". The record read sends no decision for a hypothesis, so the page never shows one; the state pill leaves the header so the two are never side by side.
- **Evidence by direction:** the Evidence section is split into "↑ Supporting · n" and "↓ Contradictory · n" (`hypothesisRecordView`). The split's key is the Worker's own tone: a contradicting row is `bad`, pinned in the test.
- **Everything else as read:** facts (Proves it, Disproves it, Interview support with breadth, Strength), Experiments, Pain themes, What would prove this wrong, activity, and every action. Add evidence, unlink, edit, retire, change strength, record a decision and Lovable keep working.

### Departures from the canvas, and the fields the boards need that no read sends (follow-ups)

- **V7 Experiments column:** not drawn. The list read sends `linked_evidence.experiment` (experiments linked as evidence), not the hypothesis's experiments with their result state ("1 · result recorded").
- **V7 Deadline:** the list read sends `sprint`, not the hypothesis's `deadline`, so the column is Sprint. The record shows the deadline.
- **V7 Action ("Open"):** the row itself opens the record, as every module table does.
- **V8 founder decision value:** the record read does not send the latest decision for the hypothesis, so it is named as kept in Verdict.
- **V8 Unknown / incomplete column:** no item-level unknowns are sent. Unknown fit stays inside the Interview support fact ("2 fit not recorded").
- **V8 Experiment results "Result needed":** the experiments section sends status (Planned, Running, Done) and the result text joined into one line, not whether a done experiment has a result.
- **V8 "each evidence row opens its source":** the evidence section rows carry no link to their interview, signal, metric or experiment.
- **V8 breadth line** ("2 relationships from 2 sources — not 2 studies"): the Interview support fact carries support and breadth together. The sentence's exact form needs the record read to send the counts apart.
- **V8 split on desktop:** the Supporting and Contradictory sections stack in the record's main column rather than sitting side by side.
