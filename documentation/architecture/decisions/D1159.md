## D1159 — Fund admin names, acts and booklet queue

Built for #3066, part 2 of #2388. No migration or new route.

The distribution preview includes `lp_name` on every line, from the LP record;
an absent name stays null. `splitDistribution`'s arithmetic is unchanged, as
is the job's use of that split. Named lines reconcile to distributable cents
with the existing explicit unallocated-cents adjustment, including rounding.

`fund.create` and `fund.valuation` join `FUND_ACTS` at the Admin tier and are
returned by the existing access read. POST funds, POST position valuations and
PUT position fund attribution enforce their act and record a `fund_admin` audit
row after a successful change. Existing position activity remains. New audit
details include identifiers and recorded mark amounts/dates, never source text
or fund names. Support sessions and paused admins cannot write.

The owner clarified directly in S29 on 2026-10-25: keep outside institutional
GPs' existing rights; apply Admin-tier rules to platform admins. The existing
GP ownership, subscription-tier and company scope remain, including the
existing helper's role behavior. No extra role restriction is introduced. A
member who fails the existing tier or fund-ownership gate remains refused.

The overview adds `booklets: {state, items:[{fund_id, waiting}]}` for administered
funds. A waiting booklet is submitted, has a completed applicant envelope with
a signed recipient and recorded signing time, and has no GP stage sent. It is
matched through its application and fund slug. Withdrawn, unsigned, incomplete,
and already-sent booklets do not count. An empty successful read is zero;
a failed signing/booklet read is Unreadable independently. An unreadable
administered-fund scope also makes this block Unreadable. No booklet answers,
names, signature tokens or documents leave this aggregate.

No Mercury test calls a provider. Tests use actual schema/migrations, refuse
network access, and exercise both allowed and refused writes with unchanged
stores on refusal, one admin audit per successful act, arithmetic, missing
names, signing states, fund scope and independent failures.
