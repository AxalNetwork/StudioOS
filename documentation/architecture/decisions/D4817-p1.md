## D4817 part 1 — PF-01 Portfolio Overview and the one Portfolio frame (#4817)

Part of #5311 (PF-01): the first board of the Portfolio v2 canvas (`design/incoming/Portfolio v2 Page.dc.html`, boards PF-01, PF-01b, PF-01c, PF-11), built against today's Worker reads only. Frontend only; no route, Worker or schema change. The board list follows D4817 where it differs from #5311.

**What is built.**
- **The frame**, `frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioFrame.jsx`, which PF-02 to PF-08 draw in: a visible-on-focus skip link; the content as a named `<section>` (the app owns the one `<main>`, addendum PFA10; the old Overview drew a second `<main>`); the crumb Account › Portfolio; the heading "Portfolio" and its line; the basis context; the one local navigation, Overview · Positions · Reports · Performance · Value-add; the route-aware document title (`Portfolio · Axal VC`, `Positions · Portfolio · Axal VC`, …, from the addendum's handoff table); and the rail slot.
- **The local navigation** is the shell's own zone row (`ZoneNav`, targets from `shellConfig`). Performance is a route of the bucket and not a sidebar zone, so `ZoneNav` takes an additive `row.extra` (`{ slug, label, to }`) placed by `row.order`. Nothing else passes it. Before, Performance had no door inside Portfolio (audit F-03).
- **The shared parts**, `PortfolioParts.jsx` (canvas PF-11): `FinancialMetric` and `MetricValue`, `BasisStrip`, `HealthStatus` (Healthy, Watch, Attention, Not enough evidence, Not recorded, Unreadable, each a word and a shape), `ObligationStatus` (Received, Due, Not received, Not recorded, Unreadable), `SourceFailure`, `SummaryTile` and `AttentionItem`. #4818 hardens these; it does not make a second set.
- **The model**, `portfolioModel.js`, pure and clock-free: a read is loading, ok or unreadable (`readOf`, `SHAPES`). A failed read or one that answers without its expected shape is Unreadable. A read that answers with nothing is Not recorded, with its reason. A measured zero stays 0. `usePortfolioReads.js` reads, commits and retries each source on its own, so one failure leaves the panels beside it standing (PFA09).
- **The Overview**, `frontend/src/pages/investor/InvestorPortfolioCanvas.jsx`, still at `/portfolio` and `/portfolio/health` through `PortfolioWorkspace` (no route changed). It is in the canvas's decision order:
  - Performance: NAV with mark coverage and the at-cost count; TVPI, DPI, RVPI and IRR, each with its definition; invested, unrealised, realised and companies. Then the basis strip: gross, the as-of, the newest mark date, the mark rule, the at-cost rule, and Methodology.
  - Needs attention, backed by evidence.
  - The Reporting and Value-add summaries.
  - The company table: search, a Health filter, the name as the link to `/portfolio/companies/:uid`, and labelled record cards on a phone.
- **How each figure reads:**
  - An unavailable return reads **Unavailable** with D4644's reason, in words (PF-01c).
  - A failed analytics read reads **Unreadable**, with a Retry for that source alone.
  - An empty book reads **Not recorded** ("No position is recorded in this book"), never $0.

**Needs attention: the evidence rules** (addendum PFA06). Each line names its source and date:
- health Attention or Watch in the latest snapshot, with the intervention flag;
- a stale snapshot (D4645's `stale`);
- no health snapshot (Not recorded);
- the founder's latest reported runway under six months;
- the last completed period's report not received (`kpi-compliance`, D4647);
- the latest mark below cost;
- a mark older than two quarters. This is measured against the Worker's own `as_of` on `kpi-compliance`, never the browser clock;
- no mark, or a mark that cannot be used, carried at cost.

Most urgent comes first, five are shown, and "Show all" lists the rest. A failed health, reporting or updates read adds no line and the card says which source is missing. An empty queue claims "nothing needs attention" only when every source answered.

**Departures from the canvas, and why.**
- The local navigation is the platform's zone-row pills, not underlined tabs. It carries no counts ("6", "2 overdue", "4 open"): each count is another board's read, and PF-08 drops them in preview anyway.
- No Export ▾ and no "read 14:30 UTC": the Overview has no export route, and no read returns its read time.
- **The as-of date.** `/api/positions/analytics` without `?as_of=` returns `as_of: null` (audit F-02). So the basis says "As of the latest recorded marks", and adds "Newest mark <date>" from `/api/positions`. No quarter-end is pinned, and "Up $X since 30 Jun" is not drawn: both need dated reads, which PF-03's quarter-end series owns.
- **MOIC is not on the Overview.** It is TVPI's figure on the gross basis (audit F-14).
- The basis strip's "#4644 confirms the policy" is dropped: no issue number goes in member copy (#5365 F-09).
- **Health states.** "Not enough evidence" is drawn only where the Worker labels a company that way. Today's `/api/portfolio/health` names an unscored company `not_recorded`, so it reads **Not recorded**.
- **Needs attention leaves out:**
  - "chased 2 Nov": the chase log is not read on the Overview;
  - "health turned red within 90 days": that needs each company's history, one read per company.
- **The Reporting card** shows Received, Not received (Outstanding while the period is in progress) and the Period:
  - Overdue and due dates are not drawn: `kpi-compliance` records no due date.
  - Missing KPIs per company stay on Reports (PF-04).
  - An empty reporting population says so, rather than "0 of 0".
- **The Value-add card:**
  - Open commitments and Delivered are over every recorded entry, not "this quarter", as the ledger's totals are.
  - Introductions made is omitted: the totals are not split by kind.
  - Meetings resulting reads **Not recorded**, with the reason (backend, #4642).
- **The company table** has no Sector, Stage or Reporting filter menus, no pagination footer and no saved views. Saved views need the backend, and the whole book arrives in one read. "Last update" is the last report date from `kpi-compliance`.
- **The rail** is the existing `WorkerRail`, placed by the frame; its structure is #5130's. The Account-menu state (PF-01b) is #4473's. The admin preview banner is PF-08.
