- **Status:** built by S17, 2026-10-27 (#3402, part 6 of #2377 under D1299). Not deployed or live verified.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `1035eca9`.
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Accounts · Boards.dc.html` and `Accounts Page.dc.html`, boards ACC2, ACC3, ACC9b, ACC10b, ACC11b, ACC11c and ACC12, integrated by D1299.
- **Builds on:**

  - D1303's saved views, bulk resend and export (`/api/admin/accounts`);
  - D1299's addresses and console props;

- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** no change.

### 1. Where it lives

- **The table:** `pages/admin/accounts/AllAccounts.jsx`. Its rules are pure, in `allAccountsModel.js`.
- **The console:** `consoles/UsersConsole.jsx` mounts the table in place of its tiles, search and table. The console keeps the record (opening at `/admin/accounts/all/<id>`), the dialogs and the handlers.
  - A row's click opens the record; each ⋯ item runs the console's handler.
  - The table hands the console the rows it read, so an address's record opens from its row.
- **Removed:** the console's batched trust scores, `UserTrustCell` and the KYC badges. D1300's rows carry `trust`.
- **HQ › Team** mounts the same console, so ACC12 is the same table. What a Super Admin sees beyond an Admin follows from `drawsAccountControls` (§5).

### 2. The query (ACC2, ACC3)

Every filter, the sort and the page go to D1300's read. Nothing is filtered or counted over the rows on screen.

- **Chips:**
  - the seven profile chips (All, Founders, Investors, Advisors, Operating Partners, Explorers, Admins), each with its count over the whole table (D128's `total` and `by_role`), and no number until the read answers;
  - the two sub-accounts, LPs and Product testers, without a count, since no read gives one per sub-account.
- **Search:** name, email or account ID, from two characters after a pause (D128's gate).
- **Filters:**
  - status, email, identity check, limited access and plan;
  - country, searched and added, up to 20;
  - joined, with a custom range;
  - last active;
  - persona segment;
  - programs;
  - trust.

  Plan and persona offer the values the read answered. Each choice is sent at once, and the footer reads "Show N accounts" from `matched`.
- **Sort:** D1300's seven sorts, in the canvas's words.
- **Columns:** the canvas's eleven. Persona segment and Profile completeness are off at first. Reset brings back the default set.
- **The count** is "1–25 of 61" over the filtered set, or "0 of 1,284" when nothing matches.
- **Paging:** 25, 50 or 100 a page, with Previous and Next. Next is off when `next_offset` is null.
- **Chips under the toolbar** name what narrows the table. Each removes itself; Clear all keeps the search, sort and columns.

### 3. Saved views

- **The Views menu** lists All accounts, then the reader's views and the studio's, from `GET /api/admin/accounts/views`.
- **"Save this view…"** opens a dialog: a name, Only me or Every admin of this studio, and "It keeps the profile, filters, sort and columns. Nobody is told."
- **The query is stored under D1303's `VIEW_KEYS`,** mapped from the list's names (`sub` → `sub_account`, `limited` → `access`, `active` → `last_active`, `program` → `programme`). A test reads the Worker's list and fails on any key it does not hold.
- **Applying a view:** gives a fresh table with that query, keeping the search. A key or value the table does not know is left out, never guessed.
- **Deleting:** a view the reader may change has a delete control, which opens a dialog first.

### 4. The bulk bar (ACC2)

Checking rows replaces the toolbar with "N selected · Resend verification email · Export selected · Clear selection".

- **Resend** opens its dialog (ACC7 J) on D1303's dry run: who is sent to and who is skipped, and why. The button reads "Send N emails". At most 50 accounts at a time.
- **Export selected** (ACC7 K):
  - the selected accounts, the columns, CSV, and a required reason of 10 to 500 characters;
  - "The file holds names and emails. Each export is recorded under your name.";
  - the Worker records it before it makes the file.

### 5. The row menu, and ACC12

**Each row's ⋯, in the canvas's words:**
- Open account;
- Change profile…;
- Grant full access without an identity check…;
- Grant or Revoke limited access…;
- Admit to Spin-Out Lab…;
- Start a support session… and Deactivate (or Reactivate) account….

**Only where `drawsAccountControls` allows,** the support session and Deactivate are drawn. So:
- a Super Admin's administrator row offers both (ACC12);
- a plain Admin's does not;
- an administrator's row offers no profile, access or Lab item, and says "Administrators are removed on their licence (Licences › Administrators)."

**Change profile** opens an in-page dialog that names the change and holds the existing role picker. An Explorer seen by a plain Admin is pointed to the Exploring board, since leaving Explorer needs a signed binding agreement. A Super Admin's goes through the existing override dialog.

### 6. States

- **ACC10:** "Reading…".
- **ACC9b:** "No accounts match these filters.", with Clear filters and Search every profile. The chips stay.
- **ACC11b:** "Trust scores could not be read. The accounts are shown without them.", with Try again, when D1300 names `trust` unreadable.
- **ACC11c:** "We could not load the accounts. The rest of Accounts still works.", with Try again. **A failed read is never an empty table.**
- **Nothing recorded** reads "Not recorded": a country, a completeness, a column this database lacks. A plan is "Free" only when the read said so, and a last active is "Never" only when the column was read.

### 7. Departures from the canvas

- **Export covers the selected accounts only.** The canvas also offers "The 61 that match your filters". D1303's export reads the selection or a search, not D1300's filters (D1303 §6), so it is not offered.
- **"Approve as product tester…" is not on the row menu.** Its write acts on a tester record (D1042's `/admin/product-testers/:id`), which belongs to the drawer's Sub-accounts tab (D1306).
- **The dialogs behind Grant full access, limited access and Deactivate** are D1306's in-page dialogs (ACC7), which the row menu opens through the console's handlers. Nothing on the page opens a browser pop-up.
- **No sub-account counts on the chips:** no read counts them per sub-account.
