## D6183 — Reference calls: notes and Eadwyn's summary of them, no audio (#6183)

**Decided by the owner, 2026-11-15: option B.** A reference call keeps the
investor's typed notes, and Eadwyn summarises those notes on press. There is
no recording, no transcript and no audio storage.

**Worker** (`routes/references.ts`):
- **The stubs are gone.** The three 501 stubs (`POST /:id/recording`,
  `GET /:id/recording-url`, `POST /:id/transcribe`) are removed, and their
  auth-ledger entries with them.
- **`POST /api/references/:id/summarize`:**
  - **Who.** The same scope as every reference route (D609: the deal must be
    one the caller may open, else 404). Then the same author rule as PATCH:
    the reference's author or an admin, else 403.
  - **Short notes.** Notes shorter than 40 characters are refused
    (`409 notes_required`) before any run.
  - **The run.** One run of the router's `workspace_explain` task, charged to
    the caller in AI tokens, on the new surface `AI_SURFACE.dealReferences`
    (`/deals`).
  - **What the model sees.** The notes only, never the reference's email.
  - **What is stored.** The answer must parse to
    `{ summary, strengths[], concerns[] }`. Otherwise `503 summary_unreadable`
    and nothing is stored. Otherwise it is stored in `summary_json` with the
    model and a fingerprint of the notes.
  - **Status.** `summarized_at` is set, and the status becomes Summarised (a
    cancelled check stays cancelled).
  - **Audit.** The deal-room log records `reference_summarized`.
- **Stale summaries.** The reference DTO carries `summary_stale` when the
  notes changed after the summary.

**Frontend** (`components/deals/DealReferencesTab.jsx`):
- The Recording, Transcript and Summary "Not available yet" rows are
  replaced by one Summary row:
  - Not recorded, with why;
  - "Written <date>";
  - or "The notes changed after this summary".
- Eadwyn's summary is drawn under the check, labelled as a summary of your
  notes, with strengths and concerns.
- "Summarise notes" (or "Summarise again" when stale) appears once the notes
  are long enough.
- `api.uploadReferenceRecording`, `getReferenceRecordingUrl` and
  `transcribeReference` are removed. So is the never-mounted
  `components/ReferenceChecksPanel.jsx`.

**Privacy.** The notes the investor typed about a third person go to the
router's model for this run. They are customer content: they are not cached,
and the summary is stored on the same row, under the same deal scope. The
consent text is unchanged.

**Not built.** Call recording, transcription and audio retention (option C).
The meeting bot (`meeting-bot/`) is not used for reference calls.
