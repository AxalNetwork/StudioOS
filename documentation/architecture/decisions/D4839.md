### What the canvas decides

- **One navigation model:** the StudioOS sidebar, six Pitch tabs (Overview, Decks, Script & rehearsal, Review, Share & analytics, Notes), and Editor and Present as full-screen modes. Draft and saved version never share a word or a look.
- **Studio home** shows one Next step chosen from state, the deck's draft beside its last version, readiness, evidence coverage and investor activity; first use offers generate, build step by step and import.
- **New deck** is one stepper (audience and round → sources → outline → price → generate → review) with five price states: reading, unavailable, confirmed with no charge, confirmed metered, expired. Unknown price is never "free".
- **Editor:** three columns (cards by section, The Grid, Design · Data · Eadwyn); document actions only in the toolbar (Undo, Redo, draft status, Save version, Present, Review, Share, Export); unsaved, offline, recovery, read-only, save failed and conflict states.
- **Eadwyn on an area:** source, model and price before the run, progress, a proposal preview with Accept, Reject or Regenerate; authored content is never overwritten silently.
- **Review** checks one exact saved version (completeness, evidence and freshness, readability, preflight, disclosure, approvals). No readiness score; "Couldn't check" is never a pass.
- **Share** works one investor at a time (version or variant, included content, NDA, expiry, download, forwarding, watermark, time per slide, walkthrough) with a preview of exactly what that investor receives.
- **Analytics** keeps tracking off, unreadable, measured zero and not recorded apart.
- Phone flows at 390 (overview, new deck, light edit, recovery, conflict, review, share) rather than the desktop editor compressed; dark boards; deviations from the approved boards are listed on PS-12, and nothing approved is removed.
