## D4815 part 2 — Deal flow's table: two fixed columns, a quick preview and numbered pages (#4815, #5901)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-16. Canvas section DX2, board DE02 (`design/incoming/Deals v2 Page.dc.html`, `Deals v2 · Boards.dc.html`). Frontend only: no Worker route, migration or API module changed.
- **Numbering:** this part file takes its number from its canvas section (DX2), as parts 3 and 8 do, so the parallel DX parts of #4815 never collide.
- **Builds on:** the table of D1240, the shared score (`ScopeScore`, D5900 / #5900), and the reads the table already makes (the firm's flow, the deal list, the score batch, the fit).

### What is drawn

`/deals?view=table` (`frontend/src/pages/investor/dealflow/FlowTable.jsx`):

- **Fixed columns.** Venture and My stage stay fixed while the other columns scroll: the compare tick (32px), Venture (200px), then My stage. A member who hides My stage in View settings keeps only Venture fixed.
- **Scrolling columns,** in the canvas's order: Round, Ask, Scorecard, the eleven scopes, Fit to my thesis, Days in stage, Owner, Entered via. The column is renamed from "Entered" to "Entered via", as the board and the issue name it.
- **Scope cells.** Every scope cell is the shared score (`FlowCells.ScopeCell` → `ScopeScore`). A band shows with its confidence; a missing score shows as its word (Not scored, Not enough recorded, Not expected yet, Unreadable, Locked, Not shared), never as 0.
- **Quick preview.** Each row has a Preview toggle under the venture's name (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`). The preview opens as a full-width row under the deal.
  - Only one preview is open at a time.
  - A new page, a new sort or a change in the rows closes it.
  - Its lines come from `flowModel.previewOf` (listed below).
  - Its actions are Open deal, only where the deal may be opened (`can_open`; otherwise "Opens once you are invited or commit."), and Details, which opens the existing peek.
- **Footer.** The footer keeps "1–25 of 80" and ‹ ›, and adds numbered pages: every page up to seven, otherwise the first, the last, and the current page with its neighbours, with a gap between (`flowModel.pageNumbers`). The current page carries `aria-current="page"`. The page size stays 25.
- **Sort state.** Every sorted head carries `aria-sort`: the scopes, Fit, Days and Ask (as before), and now Venture, which also shows its arrow. Only one head is sorted at a time.

### The preview says only what the reads carry

DE02's preview reads "Founder uploaded a financial model 2 h ago · 3 open diligence requests · next: Continue diligence". None of those three is in a read the table holds:
- what changed since the viewer's last visit is #6173's read, and does not exist yet;
- open diligence requests are the deal page's;
- the next action is the deal page's progress (DX4).

So the preview shows:

| Line | From | When absent |
|---|---|---|
| My stage | the flow's stage and `days_in_stage` | "the stage's start is not recorded"; a round not on the deals reads "Not on your deals" |
| Owner | the flow's owner | Unassigned (muted) |
| Entered via | `entered_via` and `entered_at` | the line is left out |
| Your note | the firm's note on the row | the line is left out |
| What changed | none | "Not recorded yet: Deal flow does not read what changed since your last visit." |

When #6173 lands, What changed takes its line from that read.

### Departures from the canvas

| Board | Departs from the canvas | Why |
|---|---|---|
| DE02 | The preview has no "what changed", diligence count or next step | No read the table holds carries them (above). |
| DE02 | Scores read as a band and confidence, not 0–100 | The investor batch sends no number (D1240, D5900). |
| DE02 | The preview opens from a Preview toggle, not a click on the row | A row holds several controls (compare, Add to my deal flow, Details), so a row-wide click would collide with them. |
| DE02 | The board draws six scopes in view; all eleven are columns | Every scope stays a column, and the extra ones scroll. |
