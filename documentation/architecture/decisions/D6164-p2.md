### What it draws

- **Each tab's head:** the scope rows (D6168) come first, as the canvas's scope head does: Team; Market, Product-market fit, Geographic, Political and regulatory; Traction and Financial.
- **Team (DE23, DE30b):**
  - "People, as they published themselves", with the count and "anyone with a private profile is not listed".
  - Each person: archetype or "No archetype published"; prior roles or "No prior roles published"; Full-time since and Founder–market fit as a list, each its value or Not recorded with the read's reason.
  - "Team coverage by skill area", a band per axis, never a number.
  - "Team values band" and "Advisors" (how many and their roles), each Not recorded with its reason.
  - "Next: Diligence, the next step on this deal."
- **Market (DE24, DE30c):**
  - Counts on Market sources, Who pays (segments) and Competitors. A segment with no source says Not recorded.
  - "Company markets and readiness" is a table from 768px (Market, State, Decided, Demand, Regulation, Partners, Cost), each reading its score out of 10 with its source, or Not recorded; a recorded 0 is 0/10. Below 768px each market is a card with its readiness line.
  - "Customer geography and geographic risk" is its own block, Not recorded with the read's reason.
  - Competitors are searched (name and why it matters) and sorted (Name A–Z, Name Z–A) in the browser. The table from 768px and the cards below it show the same rows; a search with no match says so.
- **Traction (DE25, DE30d):**
  - "MRR, last 12 months" lists each month newest first: MRR, paying accounts, growth and source, with "5 months reported, 1 not reported. A month not reported is a gap, never 0."
  - A month not reported says Not reported and "No update shared for this month", with no figure. A reported month that lacks a figure says "Not in this update". A recorded 0 stays 0.
  - Below 768px the months are a list, so no table needs sideways scrolling.
  - The tiles, the three charts with their tables, the CSV and Ask the founder (D6177, D6187) stay.
- **States (DE26, DE29):** masked before the NDA, unreadable with Try again, one failed block leaving the rest, and loading are D1154's, unchanged. Nothing draws a zero for a missing value.
