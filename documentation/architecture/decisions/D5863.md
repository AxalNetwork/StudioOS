### 1. One account per venture, and how it is enforced

`tiktok_account_links` holds the venture a connection serves. `project_id` and `integration_id` are **each unique**: a venture has one TikTok, and a TikTok feeds one venture.
- **The first sync makes the link.** No separate link route is needed, because a TikTok connection holds exactly one account.
- **A venture another member's TikTok already serves** is refused with `venture_served`. This is Beehiiv's precedent (`beehiiv_publication_links`), which the issue names.
- **A TikTok that already serves another venture** is refused with `serves_other_venture`.
- **The link goes with its connection** (`ON DELETE CASCADE`, and the disconnect purge below) and with its venture.

#2401's owner decision F words the rule as "enabling a second member's TikTok for the same company is refused". Enabling stays per company here: a company can hold several ventures (migration 189), so the venture is the unit that is enforced. The company switch is still required: a sync whose company is switched off is refused with `company_not_enabled`.

### 2. What lands

| Store | Module | Figures | Ledger kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tiktok_account_facts` | Revenue and traction | `followers`, `following`, `likes_total`, `video_count`, one row per venture per sync day (`UNIQUE (project_id, snapshot_date)`) | `tiktok_account_snapshot` |
| `tiktok_video_facts` | Distribution | each public video's `views`, `likes`, `comments`, `shares`, `posted_on`, `duration_s`, and the first 150 characters of its `title` (`UNIQUE (project_id, video_id)`) | `tiktok_video` |

**Never stored:** a cover, an embed URL, comment text or the `open_id`.

**Departure from #2401's Backend section:** video rows are unique per venture (`project_id, video_id`), not per connection. The ledger is read by venture and external id, so a deleted video stays deleted, whichever connection reads it again.

**Not written:** no figure goes into `project_metrics` or `custom_kpi_values`. The funnel's Visit row is computed in `services/growCycle.ts`, which never reads TikTok, because a view is not a visit.

**`parts.from_tiktok`** is on the `distribution` and `revenue-and-traction` reads.
- **Revenue and traction:** followers, total likes and video count, each with its change since the previous snapshot, and the note "shown beside revenue and never as revenue".
- **Distribution:** the latest 20 videos.
- **States:**
  - `not_linked` (no account serves the venture);
  - `not_recorded` (linked, not synced, or a scope not granted, with that reason);
  - `ok`;
  - `unreadable`, in which case the rest of the module still loads.
- `can_sync` is true for the member whose TikTok serves the venture, or anyone before a link, when not in a support session and with write access. `can_delete` is true for any writer.

### 3. The sync and the delete

**`POST /api/cycles/grow/tiktok/sync { project }`** reads the account first.
- **The video list:** it reads from where the last sync stopped, newest first, at most `VIDEO_PAGE_BUDGET` (5) pages of 20.
- **Past the budget**, or on a failed or rate-limited page, it stops "Partly synced" and keeps the cursor in `tiktok_account_links.video_cursor`. It clears the cursor once it reads to the end. The module part shows `partly_synced`.
- **One failed read** leaves the other's landing intact. **Both failing** answers the read's refusal (`tiktok_unreadable`, `rate_limited`, `sign_in_again`) and lands nothing.

**Refusals:**
- a support session (403 `support_session_refused`);
- no active TikTok (409 `tiktok_not_connected`);
- `company_not_enabled` (403).

**Logging.** It logs through `logGrowWrite` with counts only: "Grow synced from TikTok: 1 snapshot, 3 videos, 0 kept deleted".

**Scopes.** A scope the member did not grant (`user.info.stats`, `video.list`, read from `scopes_json`) is not asked for, and is reported as `scopes_missing`. Its figures read "Not recorded: you did not let Axal VC read …". A count TikTok did not give, or one that is not a non-negative integer, is `null` with its reason. Never 0.

**`DELETE /api/cycles/grow/tiktok/facts/:id?project=&kind=account|video`** is open to any writer.
- The figure's ledger row keeps no `local_id`, so it stays deleted through every later sync.
- One route serves both stores; the ids are per table, so `kind` says which.

**The rules of every call** (`services/tiktok/client.ts`, the only caller of the Display API):
- one host;
- `redirect: 'error'`;
- a 10-second deadline;
- the token in the header only;
- errors as codes, never TikTok's body;
- answers parsed field by field.

The token comes from the provider's `tiktokAccessToken` (the refresh lease, exported from `providers/tiktok.ts`).

### 4. Disconnect and the deauthorization webhook (decision E)

- **Disconnect** (`providers/tiktok.ts` `disconnect`): after TikTok revokes the grant, `purgeTikTokConnection` deletes the connection's facts, their ledger rows and its link. A failed revoke throws first, the route answers 503 `tiktok_revocation_failed`, and nothing is deleted.
- **`authorization.removed`** (`routes/tiktok_webhook.ts`). **What I found:** the webhook does not mark the row revoked. It deletes the connection row and its `integration_companies`. The facts would then be left keyed to a connection that no longer exists, so the same batch now deletes them first (`purgeByOpenIdStatements`, the same open_id and cutoff, with the subquery written out in each statement).

### 5. UNVERIFIED facts

#2401 marks TikTok's video list method and field names as UNVERIFIED. This session could not reach `developers.tiktok.com`: the egress proxy refused it (403 on CONNECT, and WebFetch could not resolve it) on 2026-11-15. No page was re-read, so each name below is #2401's reading of 2026-10-21. Every one is a constant in `services/tiktok/client.ts`, and nowhere else.
- `GET /v2/user/info/?fields=follower_count,following_count,likes_count,video_count`, with the answer under `data.user`.
- `POST /v2/video/list/?fields=id,create_time,title,duration,view_count,like_count,comment_count,share_count`, with body `{ max_count: 20, cursor }`, and the answer under `data.videos`, `data.cursor`, `data.has_more`.
- The error envelope `error.code`: `ok`, `rate_limit_exceeded`, `access_token_invalid`, `scope_not_authorized`.

The owner's first live sync is the check. The connector page's `verified_on` stays null.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/tiktok_grow_5863.test.ts` (8), on node:sqlite through the mounted routes, with TikTok stubbed:
1. A sync lands the snapshot and videos through the ledger and links the venture. No cover, embed URL or open_id is stored, and no metric or KPI is written.

7. Disconnect purges after revoke, and a failed revoke purges nothing. The webhook's purge runs in its batch.
8. Logs and the activity log hold counts only. The feeds, the registry and the page agree. Only the client calls the API.
