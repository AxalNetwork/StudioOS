## D654 — Grow › Share an update, built on portfolio_updates

**Issue:** #1466, part of #1461 (D649). **Slot:** S02. **Migration:** 437.
**Canvas:** `design/incoming/Cycle Action.dc.html`, `action=update`, read from
#1468's branch, where it is committed.

One update for investors, the team and the Axal network, written once. It is
still a `portfolio_updates` row (`routes/portfolio_updates.ts`). This decision
adds no second updates store.

### What was true

- `portfolio_updates` held a founder's KPIs and narrative, listed, edited and
  submitted, and notified the company's followers. It had no period dates, no
  sourced metrics, no asks and no audience.
- Grow's desk (`FounderGrowDesk.jsx`) had no action button.
- Raise's "investor updates" list read `raise_investor_updates`, a separate
  table written from the pipeline.

### What is true now

**Migration 437** adds the following:
- On the update: `period_start`/`period_end`, `metrics_json`,
  `highlights_json`/`lowlights_json`, `audience_json`,
  `network_includes_metrics` and `sent_at`.
- `update_asks`: the kind is one of hire, customer, partner, market or capital,
  by CHECK. Each ask records the module and the item it links to.
- `update_ask_replies`: each reply, and the module item it became (or why it
  became none).
- `update_recipients`: who received the update, through which path, and when
  they opened it.

**The rules** live in `services/updateShare.ts`:
- **Metrics** come from Revenue and traction (`project_metrics`): MRR,
  customers (`paying_accounts`) and burn, each from the latest snapshot in the
  period that records it, with its source and date.
  - Growth is derived only from two recorded MRR figures.
  - Runway is derived only from recorded cash and a burn above zero.
  - **A figure nothing records is `null` and reads "Not recorded", never 0.** A
    recorded 0 stays 0.
  - A kept figure with no source is dropped.
  - With no metrics, the update still sends, with the metrics left out.
- **Highlights and lowlights** each carry a source.
  - The Worker refuses a line with no source, whoever wrote it.
  - Eadwyn drafts them through `aiRouter` (task `publication`, surface
    `AI_SURFACE.growUpdate`) from the period's stored facts: the metrics, the
    open roles, and customer contacts captured. Each line must name the fact it
    draws on, and a line that names none, or a fact that does not exist, is
    dropped.
  - A period with no fact makes no model call. The sheet then says "Nothing new
    this month" and offers "Skip this month" or "Write by hand".
- **Audience:**
  - Investors are the Raise pipeline (`raise_prospects`, not passed).
  - The team is the company's accepted members (`project_members`).
  - The Axal network is the company's followers.
  - Investors and the team receive the whole update. **The network receives the
    asks only, unless the founder includes the metrics; it never receives the
    highlights.** The Worker shapes what each recipient may read
    (`payloadFor`); the page only draws it.
- **Send** goes once, through `notify()`, the notification path that also mails
  people by their preferences.
  - An investor in the pipeline with no Axal account is recorded as not
    delivered, with that reason, and is never counted as sent.
  - A recipient whose notification settings refuse it is recorded the same way.
- **Replies** are made in place by a recipient, and each lands in the module
  that asked:
  - **a hire:** a candidate (`job_applications`) on the open role the ask links
    to. A hire may link only to one of the company's own open roles.
  - **a customer:** a contact through `ingestContact`, audience `customer`.
  - **a partner:** a contact with audience `partner`.
  - **capital:** a contact with audience `investor`, which Raise reads.
  - **a market contact:** stays on the ask, with "Not recorded: there is no
    Market expansion store yet". See question A below.
- **"Ask again"** is offered on an ask with no reply 14 days after the update
  was sent, or 14 days after it was last asked.
- **Raise:** `GET /api/contacts/raise-updates` adds `shared_updates`. They are
  read from the same `portfolio_updates` rows, with the investor count and how
  many were delivered, never copied.

**The routes** (`/api/portfolio-updates`), each for the project's owner unless
noted:
- `GET /share/context`
- `POST /share/draft-lines`
- `PUT /:uid/share`
- `POST /:uid/send`
- `GET /:uid/page`: the author, or a recipient, who sees their audience's view.
  Opening it records the open.
- `POST /asks/:askUid/replies`: a recipient only.
- `POST /asks/:askUid/ask-again`: the author.

Every request value is bound.

**The pages:**
- **`ShareUpdateSheet.jsx`**, opened by Grow's header button, has the six steps
  and the `nometrics` and `nothing` states.
- **`SentUpdatePage.jsx`** at `/updates/:uid` is the `page` view and the
  `noreply` view. Each reply links "Open in Talent", "Customers",
  "Partnerships" or "Capital relationships". It shows each recipient's opens
  and non-delivery, and the reply form for recipients.
- **The client** is `lib/api/shareUpdate.js` (D527).

### Names that differ from the issue

The issue names modules from the cycle canvases that are not on `main` under
those names. They map as follows:
- **Team expansion** is Talent (`/grow/talent`, `job_postings`).
- **Distribution** is Customers (`contacts`).
- **Partner network** is Partnerships.
- **Revenue and traction** is `project_metrics`.

#1461's connectors do not feed metrics yet. When they land, they write
`project_metrics` like Stripe's import does, and this update reads them with
their source.
