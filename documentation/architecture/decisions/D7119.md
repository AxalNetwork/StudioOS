### What was wrong

The booking page read the member's own check-ins (`GET /checkins`). It took their most-used tags and sent them to `GET /experts` as `want_category`, with no consent step. Every visit to the directory read the journal.

The tags were expert-category keys, so the topic "Anxiety" ranked psychiatrists first. That prescribed a specialty from an ordinary word.

### Choices, and the kinds of support

`wellbeing_preferences` holds one row per member, written only by that member. It records:
- the kinds of support they chose, from `SUPPORT_TYPES`;
- languages, time zone and a price ceiling;
- formats: 1:1, group, or both;
- whether suggestions are on;
- topic consent and its sources.

No row means nothing has been chosen and consent is off. Edits carry `base_revision`, and a stale copy gets 409 `preferences_changed`. Reset deletes the row and every dismissal.

The kinds of support are separate choices:

| Kind | Expert categories | A topic may suggest it |
|---|---|---|
| Licensed therapy | therapist, psychiatrist, performance psychologist, relationship counsellor | no |
| Registered dietetics | dietitian | no |
| General nutrition support | nutritionist | yes |
| Coaching | executive, leadership, burnout, stress, sleep, productivity, habits | yes |
| Mindfulness and grounding | mindfulness, breathwork, yoga | yes |
| Spiritual guidance | spiritual / philosophical advisor | no |

### Topic consent

- **Off by default.** While it is off, `consentedTopics` returns before any query. Neither the recommendations nor the directory reads a reflection or a check-in, and the tests assert that no SELECT names either table.
- **Scope.** Turning consent on requires choosing its sources: day, week, month or earlier check-ins. Only those records are read, only the member's own, and only from the last 90 days.
- **What is read.** Only the topics column is decrypted. The text the member wrote, their answers and their bookings are never read.
- **Revoking.** Revoking takes effect on the next read, because nothing derived from topics is stored. `topic_consent_at` records when consent was last turned on, and is cleared when it is off.
- **What a topic may do.** It may suggest one of the three kinds marked "yes" above. It never adds therapy, dietetics or spiritual guidance, which come only from the member's own choice. Within a kind the member chose, a topic may rank one expert above another.

### Recommendations

`GET /api/wellbeing/recommendations` answers one of four states:
- `off`: the member turned suggestions off.
- `choose_interests`: nothing chosen, and no usable topic.
- `no_match`: nothing fits.
- `ok`

Every answer also carries `excluded` counts (dismissed, language, price, format) and `group_sessions: { offered: false }` until #7120.

**Who can be suggested.** The candidates are the experts the directory lists: active, verified, not a sample, not hidden, and with a complete profile. Each suggestion carries:
- its kinds of support;
- languages and time zones;
- active services and prices;
- whether it offers times.

An expert with no times is still shown, marked unavailable and listed last.

**"Why this?"** Each suggestion says what was used: the interest chosen, the topic, the language, the 1:1 format and the price ceiling. It also says what was not used: what the member wrote, their answers and their bookings. When consent is off, it adds that topics were not used either.

**Dismiss.** Dismissing is per member and idempotent, and it can be undone.

### The directory

`GET /experts?personalise=1` reads the member's choices, plus their topics only when consent covers them. It ranks by those, uses the member's languages when they set any, and reports `personalised: { interests, topics_used, topic_consent }`.

`WellbeingBookPage` sends `personalise` and no longer calls `GET /checkins`. `want_category` is still accepted from any caller.
