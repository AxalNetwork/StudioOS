## D620 — The archetype card's compact /studio card and page top, built to the #1006 board (A1–A23)

**Issue:** #1323. **No migration.** The board is the owner's #1006 canvas,
committed by D619 as `design/canvases/integrated/Studio · Archetype card.dc.html`.
D619 built that board's A24–A30. This builds the earlier artboards, which draw
the compact `/studio` card and the top of `/studio/archetype`. Where the board
and `Studio · Archetype preview` (D325, D594) disagree on the compact card,
the newer board wins (S1's recommendation 4). The older canvas stays committed
as the record of those two decisions.

### The compact card (`ArchetypeCard` in `components/profile/ProfileFitSection.jsx`)

- **A1.** The "Preview" chip is gone. "View full card →" sits in a footer row
  under a hairline, with the illustration setting on the right
  (`illustration · f`, `· m` or `· both`). The card is still one link with one
  label (D325).
- **A2 and A2b.** Hover now adds `0 6px 18px rgba(24,24,27,.08)` to the 2px
  lift and the accent border. The focus ring's offset is 3px.
- **A9, on a phone.** Below `md`, the sprite is a 96px tile beside the name,
  and the description is left to the page. From `md` up the sprite sits above
  the name, as before.
- **A10, locked.** The lock tile keeps D594's line, which names the plan from
  `required_plan` and already says the answers are kept (S1's recommendation
  2). Under it are the board's "Primary archetype", "Locked" and "The reading
  unlocks with the plan." The board's own line repeats "your answers are
  kept", so that clause is dropped rather than said twice. The card is still
  not a link and carries no archetype text.

### The top of `/studio/archetype` (`pages/ArchetypeCardPage.jsx`)

- **A5's crumb row.** "‹ Studio" and "Profile & Fit · Archetype" sit above
  every state, so the empty and locked pages have a way back too. At the
  card's foot are "‹ Back to studio" and the slug.

  - The licence chip sits beside "Primary archetype", with a 32px title.

  - **Left:** Core approach (with the lean under it), Description, then

    - Confidence, as a figure and a bar. With no reading it shows "N/A" and

### Where the board was not followed

- **`both` keeps the 50/50 split** (S1's recommendation 1). The board resolves
  `both` to the man's still. An unset illustration also reads `both`, so that
  would show a man to every member who never chose.
- **The read is "from your conversation with Eadwyn"** (D322). The board says
  "advisor conversation".
- **The complements caption** says "Archetypes whose strengths sit where this
  one's blind spots are." The board says "Sibling archetypes in the same
  licence". `ARCHETYPES[slug].complements` crosses licences, so that sentence
  would be false.
- **The failed read and the empty state** keep D325's copy and its retry.
