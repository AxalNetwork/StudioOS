## D4579 — Grow keeps its destination through sign-in and setup, and says where sign-in leads (#4579)

- **Status:** Built for #4579 (Grow audit, first phase, 3 of 3), 2026-11-09. Frontend only. No Worker change, no migration.
- **Builds on:**
  - `lib/pendingNext.js`, the one intended-destination mechanism: #4913's return-after-login areas (D4651, #4646) and Task #1's pending `next`. Nothing here is a second mechanism.
  - D914's route boundaries in `RoleGuard`.
  - D4472's Licences page.

### What was decided

1. **Grow keeps its destination through sign-in.**
   - `/grow` joins `RETURN_AFTER_LOGIN_PREFIXES`, so the route guard sends a signed-out visitor to `/login?next=<the Grow page>`.
   - Signed out, `/grow` itself now goes through the guard instead of first forwarding to `/grow/focus`, so `next` is the page the visitor asked for.
   - The existing validation (`sanitizeNextPath`, `sameOriginNextPath`) is the only gate on what is carried. Every sign-in method (email link, Google, passkey, authenticator) already lands on a validated `next`.
2. **Sign-in says where it leads** (`signInContextFor`).
   - With a Grow destination, `/login` reads "Sign in to continue to Grow". It adds one line saying what Grow is and what account it needs: a founder account, or for the reporting desk a founder, advisor or operating partner account (App.jsx's routes).
   - Its "Create an account" link keeps the destination.
   - `/register` with the same `next` says it continues to Grow, and its "Sign in" link keeps it.
   - A direct `/login`, or an unsafe `next`, keeps the generic page.
3. **The destination survives required account setup.**
   - An invitation's pending target is exempt from the setup gates (chat, licence picker, wizard resume, terms). A Grow destination is not exempt, because Grow needs the founder account that setup creates.
   - Each setup gate keeps the address it redirects from (`keepThroughSetup`): only a Grow path, only a safe one, for 24 hours (the verification email's life).
   - Once onboarding is complete, `RequireAuth` returns the visitor there once, and the kept value is cleared after commit.
4. **An account without the Founder licence is told why.**
   - Before, a signed-in investor, advisor, operating partner or explorer on a Grow address was sent home without a word.
   - Now `RoleGuard` draws `GrowBoundary` there: Grow is the founder workspace, this account does not hold the Founder licence, and here is how to add it (Licences) or go home.
   - A support session is not offered a licence on someone else's behalf.
   - The guard and every Worker check are unchanged.

### Not changed

- The generic `/login` and `/register` pages for visitors with no destination.
- What any role may open. Only what an account that cannot open Grow is told.
- `/learning`'s behaviour. It keeps its destination as before and is not held through setup.
