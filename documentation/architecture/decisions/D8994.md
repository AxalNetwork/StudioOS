### What changed

- **A new guide, `capital/share-evidence`** ("Raise › Evidence: share evidence outside Axal", `frontend/src/pages/docs/sections/capital.js`). No Help guide covered Raise › Evidence before, so the section is a new guide rather than a section of an old one. It is placed in Raising & cap table with Raise · Evidence (`/raise/evidence`) as its page (`guidePlacement.js`: one new placement and one new surface whose roles and lab flag are the route's own guard). It is readable by founders and admins, the roles that route admits; it is not a public guide (D4637 approves those one by one).
- **What it describes, each from the code that does it:**
  - **Who can share.** Only the venture's founders; an admin or Super Admin gets no Share outside Axal action and Shared links refuses them (`RaiseEvidence.jsx`, `founderOnly` in `services/raiseEvidenceShare.ts`).
  - **The evidence pack (RA-27).** Output Link or PDF; Before an NDA: coverage only, or An NDA is signed outside Axal with the founder's confirmation and a day not in the future; a label of up to 80 characters; expiry 1 to 90 days (14 by default) and 1 to 100 views (10 by default); The reader sees per row; Create pack sends nothing (`ShareOutside.jsx`, `shareOutsideModel.js`, `LINK_DEFAULTS` and `LINK_BOUNDS`).
  - **The readiness snapshot (RA-30).** From Raise › Fundraising readiness: who it is for, an optional covering line of at most 200 characters, Link or PDF; the reader sees seven areas, Not scored yet never 0, Team is assessed by a person, no overall score and no Product-market fit (`ShareReadiness.jsx`, `ReadinessSnapshotPage.jsx`, `readinessBody`).
  - **Revoking a link (RA-29).** Shared links at `/raise/evidence/shared`, row states Live, Expired, Used up and Revoked, Revoke as review then Revoke link, the row kept in grey, Changed since sent with Make a new pack, and PDF copies that cannot be stopped or counted.
  - **What the recipient sees (RA-28, RA-36).** At `/share/evidence/<link>`, no sign-in and no Eadwyn; dated and saying it does not update; before an NDA, What is on record; after one, each item with its source, day and strength, a figure only where shared After NDA, Value withheld with its reason, and Claims these items back with a Mismatch never hidden and the founder's note under it; the expiry day in the footer. A refused link shows the Worker's own title (`REFUSALS`), names neither the venture nor any evidence, and uses no view; every open, a reload included, uses one view (`openPackLink`).
- **Eadwyn** appears only as what it does here: it proposes a selection or drafts a covering line, each run quoted and confirmed first, and the founder still makes the pack (`ShareDrafts.jsx`).
