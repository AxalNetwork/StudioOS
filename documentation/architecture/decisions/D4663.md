## D4663 — Spin-Out Lab: the corrected guarantees as services, tested through the routes; public reports bounded in SQL (#4663)

- **Status:** built by S09 on `claude/funny-bohr-ttghqt-4663-lab-services`. Not merged.
- **Audit:** Spin-Out Lab audit 5 of 5, revision `d85951c`. Builds on D4657, D4658, D4659 and D4661, all on `main`.
- **Migration:** none. The bounded reads use the existing index on `spinout_lab_enrollments (user_id, …)` and `ix_spinout_applications_user`.

### Decision

1. **Three services own the Lab's rules. The handlers parse the request and return the outcome.**
   - **`services/labLifecycle.ts` (enrollment transitions).**
     - `getLabState`, `startLab`, `recordMilestone` (the week advance and week 4's close) and `exitLab` moved here from `routes/spinout_lab.ts`. Their statements are unchanged.
     - `beginLab` is `POST /start` whole: the eligibility policy, the cohort's start instant, the guarded switch-on and the Week 1 plan.
     - `leaveLab` is `POST /exit`.
     - Services answer the route with a `LabOutcome`, a `{ status, body }` pair.
   - **`services/labProgress.ts` (evidence completion).**
     - `completeLabMilestone` is `POST /milestone` whole. `incorporation_completed` goes to the paid-order sync (D585); every other key goes to `recordLabMilestone`.
     - The recorder now imports the week advance from the lifecycle module. Before, a service imported it back from a route.
   - **`services/labGraduation.ts` (graduation eligibility).**
     - `graduationOf` is the one read of D4659's view for one founder, and returns `graduated` and `by_rule`.
     - `/state` and the credential trigger (`issueIfGraduated`) ask it. Before, each wrote its own SELECT.
   - **`services/labReporting.ts`** holds the public outcome reads (item 2).
   - **`SPRINT_DAYS`** moved to `spinoutLabCatalog.ts`, next to the four weeks it counts. `founder_lp_duration_consistency.test.mjs` now pins it there.
   - `admin_exploring.ts` starts the Lab through the lifecycle module.

2. **The public reports use the enrolled venture and are bounded in SQL.** These are `/graduates`, `/stats` and `/cohort`.
   - **Before:**
     - every graduate (and, for `/cohort`, every project of every active founder) came back to the Worker;
     - the Worker folded those rows to one per founder in memory;
     - each company with no project name had its own application query.
   - **Now:**
     - The application fallback is a correlated subquery. A founder with no name anywhere is filtered out in SQL. Ordering, the page size (`LIMIT ?`, bound) and the totals (`COUNT`, `SUM`) are SQL's.
     - `/graduates` is one statement returning at most 12 rows.
     - `/stats` is one statement returning one row.
     - `/cohort` is two statements, returning at most 24 rows and at most 32 rows. The second read covers the 8 graduates plus up to 24 already shown as active.
   - **The 503 rule from D4661 is kept.** A failed statement still answers `503 lab_outcomes_unreadable` with our own sentence, never `[]` or zero. The application fallback is now part of the same statement, so a failure there fails the whole read. Before, the read could succeed and leave out rows.
   - **The public fields are unchanged.** The same JSON keys are returned, and no founder field is added.

3. **What the reports answer: unchanged, apart from three stated points.** On the measurement fixture, `/graduates` and `/stats` return byte-identical bodies before and after (same SHA-256 at three sizes).
   - **`/cohort`'s active founders are named by their enrolled venture.** That is D4659's identity, and the graduate column, `/graduates`, `/stats` and the certificate already used it.
     - Before, this half still joined every project the founder owned and showed the one with the lowest id.
     - A founder with no venture bound, or none live, falls back to their latest application's name, as before.
     - This is scope item 5 of the issue.
   - **The graduate column of `/cohort` counts only graduates it can name.**
     - Before, a nameless recent graduate used up one of the eight places and was then not drawn, so the column could show seven or fewer while named graduates were left out.
     - Now it holds up to eight named graduates, the same way `/graduates` counts its twelve.
     - The fixture's one nameless recent graduate shows the difference: 7 graduates before, 8 now.
   - **Ties are ordered by user id.** Before, two graduates with the same `graduated_at` (or two founders with the same start) came back in whatever order SQLite chose. Now ties are broken by user id.

### Measurements (local only)

- **Method.**
  - The real route ran over the migrated schema in `node:sqlite`, through a counting D1 binding (`test/_lab_reporting_world.ts`).
  - The fixture gives every founder three projects. Every fifth founder has no venture bound, and every seventh has no application. One credential holder predates the rule.
  - "Before" is `origin/main`'s route (4c24f3d6bf) run beside this branch's route on the same database.
  - Latency is the median of 7 runs on a shared development machine. It is relative, and it is not a D1 figure: D1 adds a network hop per statement, which this does not measure.
- **No production saving is claimed.**

What the tables show:
- **Rows reaching the Worker stop growing with the population.** Before, they grew linearly.
- **Statements no longer depend on how many names fall back to an application.**
  - Before, `/graduates` made one statement plus one per named-by-application card shown (3 here).
  - Before, `/cohort` made two statements plus the same per-row reads.
- **Local latency does not improve for `/graduates` and `/stats`.** The view `spinout_lab_graduates` aggregates every milestone row on each read, and that dominates both before and after. The name subquery also runs for every graduate before the filter.
- **`/cohort` is faster locally.** It no longer joins every active founder to every project they own.

### Tests

`test/lab_services_4663.test.ts` runs the real routes and imports the service modules.

- **A whole programme through `POST /milestone`,** run twice: once with incorporation paid last, once with it paid before weeks 1–3. After every step, these must agree:
  - the milestone rows (evidence only from the venture or the person);
  - the week, and the close exactly when week 4 is met;
  - the grants (one per recorded paying step, plus the bonus once graduated, never twice);
  - the credential (issued exactly at graduation, naming the venture);
  - `graduationOf`, `/state`, `/graduates`, `/stats` and `/cohort` (never the side project).
- **Concurrency:**
  - the same step sent twice at once records one row and pays one grant;
  - the last two required steps sent together issue one credential and one bonus.
- **Recovery:**
  - a retried step records nothing twice;
  - a lost grant is paid once by the reconciler;
  - a lost credential and its bonus are restored once by the certificate backfill.
- **A failed evidence read is a 503 with nothing written,** and the retry records once.
- **An Explorer with two projects:**
  - is asked to name the venture, and the side project proves nothing;
  - graduates;
  - after the Lab closes, keeps a readable `/state` (`graduated`, not active) and their own credential at `GET /certificates/mine`, while new steps are refused.
- **The services answer directly:** `completeLabMilestone`, `getLabState`, and `graduationOf` for a credential holder from before the rule.
- **The public reports are compared with an independent row-by-row reading** of the same tables, on a 60/40 population.
- **The reports are bounded:**
  - statements, and the row ceiling, are the same at 30/10 and 400/150;
  - a graduate's unrelated project adds nothing to the money raised.

**Mutation-checked: 12 mutations, each caught by a named test.**
- the name filter dropped;
- the graduates read unbounded;
- `/cohort` and `/stats` joining projects by founder;
- the graduate column uncapped;
- a credit paid on a replay;
- an unreadable evidence read answered 409;
- `by_rule` read as true for any row;
- "graduated" with no row;
- `/start` skipping the policy (`lab_admission_evidence_4657`);
- the week written without `MAX` (`spinout_lab.test.mjs`);
- no week-4 close.

**Existing tests that changed:**
- `spinout_lab.test.mjs` imports `spinoutLabCatalog.ts` and `labLifecycle.ts` instead of slicing functions out of the route's source by brace counting.
- `lab_lifecycle_4658.test.ts` imports `startLab` and `recordMilestone` from the service.
- Two cases in `lab_outcome_reads_4661.test.ts` followed the fallback into the statement:
  - "a failed fallback read is unreadable" now fails the one statement;
  - "a named company never reads the application" became "a venture's name always wins over the application". That guarantee was about a separate per-row query, which no longer exists.

### Not done here

- **`/shipped` and `/fund-metrics` are unchanged.**
  - `/shipped` already reads through the venture and caps its milestone fetch at 2000 rows. It still reads application names per company.
  - `/fund-metrics` aggregates in `services/spinoutFundMetrics.ts` over one row per graduate.
- **The graduation view is not materialised.** Every read of `spinout_lab_graduates` still aggregates every milestone row. Materialising it is a migration and a write path, and nothing measured here needs it yet.
- **A credential lost after graduation is not restored automatically** (owner decision below).
  - Graduation closes the Lab (`spinout_lab_active = 0`), and the reconciler and its cron walk only founders with the Lab on.
  - So if the request that recorded the last step dies after the insert and before the credential is issued, only the admin certificate backfill (`POST /certificates/backfill`) restores the credential and the bonus.
  - The recovery test proves that path. It is not wired to a schedule.
