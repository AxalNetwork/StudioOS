### The modes

A text area is written one of three ways (`services/decks/ensembles.ts`):
- **`single`:** one call per variant, as D1512 runs today. It is the default for an areas run.
- **`draft_refine`:** each variant is drafted, then refined by the same model #3754 routes the element to. The refine is a second call that reads the draft, with the card's words and the draft both as data. It is the default for a whole-deck build (`class: 'deck_build'`, AUTO-1).
- **`best_of_n`:** three drafts, then B7's judge (`judge.ts`, D1525) ranks them blind under the family rule (the first `decide` model outside the drafts' family) and names the best.

A request may ask for a mode (`ensemble`); an unknown one is the class default. Only text areas take a mode. Charts, diagrams, images and records keep their own path.

### Why plan lines, not a second call inside `run()`

The issue named `aiRouter.ts`, where a run is one call. The ensemble is built one level up instead, as lines of the run's plan:
- **One quote and one hold (AUTO-6).** Every refine and judge line is priced at its run's maximum when the plan is quoted. A refine is quoted with a draft as long as the area's limit, and a judge with every draft at that length. So the quote, and the run's one parent hold, already holds the second calls, and nothing an ensemble does takes a hold of its own.
- **Unchanged guarantees.** Each call is still one `run()` under the parent hold, with its own usage row, guard, idempotency key and record ref. Stop, the crash-safe rebuild, retries and the per-step charge clamp all work as before.
- **Progress for free.** Each pass is a row of the run's progress (pd2b), with its model, state, seconds and cost.

`run()` itself is unchanged.

### Order

- **`after`.** A refine line names its draft, and a judge line its drafts (`after`, the run's sequence numbers, resolved from each line's offset within its area by `resolveAfter`). The runtime claims a line only once every line it is after has ended (`genRuntime.ts` `READY_STEP`), so six drivers racing never start a refine before its draft.
- **Drafts first.** Every ready draft is claimed before any ready second pass, so the deck's first full draft lands as early as the drivers allow.
- **Failures.**
  - A refine whose draft wrote nothing runs nothing and is free (`draft_missing`); the draft's own failure stands.
  - A refine that fails leaves its draft as the variant, which says so (`ensemble.refined: false`).
  - A judge with fewer than two drafts to compare, or one refused by its rules, ranks nothing, and every draft stays acceptable.

### What a run leaves

- **One variant per draft.** A refine is no variant of its own: the variant is the refined text, or the draft, at the cost of both calls. Each variant carries `ensemble: { mode, refined?, draft_model_label? }`.
- **The judge's pick.** In best-of-n, the variant the judge named carries `best: true`. The judge is no variant.
- **Progress rows.** `GET /api/decks/areas/batches/:uid` names each step's `role` (`draft`, `refine`, `judge` or `variant`) beside its element, model label, state, seconds and cost, so `BuildProgress.jsx` can label pd2b's rows through the adapter with no new client code. The SSE events carry the same fields, as D1502 already did.

### The load test

`cloudflare-worker/test/deck_ensembles_load_d1551.test.ts` runs the gate on the fake binding:
- 12 cards × 5 founders at once, each card with three text areas, each area drafted and refined;
- each run with its six drivers under its one hold.

The gate is the first full draft (every area has its first words) at p50 ≤ 45 s and p95 ≤ 90 s.
- **Result:** p50 26.2 s and p95 29.5 s.
- **What it assumes:**
  - Each fake call takes a seeded latency from 1.5 s to 6 s, skewed low, with a median of about 2.6 s.
  - The runtime's own D1 work is counted at the same time scale, so the figures read high, never low.
  - The AiGovernor's pacing has its own tests and is stubbed open here.
- **What it does not show:** real model latency. That is the capped staging run, which waits on owner decision N (the test spend cap).

### Tests

`deck_ensembles_d1551.test.ts` (10 cases):
- the modes and their defaults;
- draft-refine's and best-of-n's lines summing to the hold, the refine quoted at its most, and the judge outside the drafts' family;
- `resolveAfter`;
- the `after` order with six drivers;
- drafts before second passes;
- the refine reading its draft under the one hold;
- a refine that does not land;
- the judge naming the best inside the hold;
- the progress roles.

Mutations, each caught:
- the claim without its `after` clause;
- the claim ordered by sequence alone;
- the refine left out of the hold;
- the refine not reading the draft;
- the judge's pick ignored;
- a failed refine treated as landed.

`familyOf` stays in `critic.ts`, and the runtime's judge imports it from there. The stage-plan warning shape is declared beside it, the same fields as `narrative/arcs.ts` `Warning`, and not imported, so that module graph does not reach the record sources (D752). The cron drives this runtime.
