## D1551 — Text ensembles on the generation runtime, and the load test (#3798)

- **Status:** Built for #3798 (deck v2 C3, part of #3716), 2026-11-03, to the issue's final canvas revision (2026-11-01). Worker only; no UI.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1502 (A3a: the runtime, one quote and one parent hold, the batch payload and its Server-Sent Events);
  - D1503 (#3754: the element routing that names each element's model);
  - D1525 (B7: the judge and its family rule);
  - D1516 (A5: the one-click build's `deck_build` run).
- **Migration:** none.
- **Owner decision N** (the evaluation and test spend cap, #3716) is still open. It gates only the capped staging load run, which is this PR's follow-up step.

### What was decided

1. **Two ensemble modes, in `aiRouter.ts`.** Until now a run was one call. `runEnsemble` makes an ensemble of ordinary `run` calls, so each call is guarded, metered, recorded and settled like any other:
   - **`draft-refine`:** one draft, then one refine of it by the routed model. The refine is asked to tighten the draft and add no number, name or claim, at a temperature of at most 0.3. A refine that fails or comes back empty keeps the draft: an ensemble never loses an answer it had.
   - **`best-of-n`:** two or three drafts at rising temperatures (+0.2 each), and the caller's judge picks one. With no judge, a judge that refuses, a pick that names no answer, or only one answer, the first answered draft stands.

   `onDraft` hears the first answered draft before the refine or the judge runs.
2. **Which element runs which, from the routing.** A whole-deck run (`deck_build`, one variant per area) writes each text area through its element's ensemble, on the model #3754 routes it to:
   - **best of three, judged:** a title, a kicker and a callout, the short lines that carry a card;
   - **draft and refine:** a body, bullets and a caption.

   The lightbox (`areas`) keeps one call per variant, because there the founder asked for variants to choose from. No model id is written anywhere new.
3. **The judge keeps the family rule.** For a best-of-n line, the planner picks the judge with `judgeModelFor` (another family than the writer's, from the `decide` list). It runs through `judge.ts`'s `judgeArea`, blind, with its decision going through the step's own `run`. When no judge of another family is offered, nothing is quoted or run for it, and the quote line says the first answer is kept.
4. **One quote, one hold, never above it.** An area's line is the sum of every call's own quote (`quoteEnsemble`), plus the judge's one decision for best-of-n. The refine is quoted with the draft's longest answer in its input. Every call, the judge's included, runs under the run's parent hold and the step's one record ref, each with its own Idempotency-Key. The step charges the sum of its calls, and the runtime clamps that to its line. No ensemble takes a second hold.
5. **Busy models within an ensemble.** Each call keeps D1600's rules: same-tier fallback, and busy retries counted across the whole step (at most three).
6. **Per-element progress.** The batch payload already carries, per step, the element, model label, state, seconds and cost (D1502); `BuildProgress.jsx` reads them through the adapter with no new client code. An ensemble step adds to them in two places:
   - **its result** carries `ensemble`: the mode, each call's role, model label and outcome, whether it was refined, and the judge's pick, probability and model label;
   - **the stream** shows the first draft as `variant_ready`, with `variant.draft: true`, while the step is still `running`. Its money is filled from the run, so pd2b's row shows words before the refine or the judge.
7. **The load test is the Gamma gate (`deck_ensembles_load_d1551.test.ts`).**
   - **The set-up:** five founders build a 12-card deck at once on the fake binding: 28 text areas each (12 titles, 12 bodies, 4 callouts). Six drivers run each deck, under the real AiGovernor's caps, on a simulated clock.
   - **Simulated latencies:** a draft 3–8 s, a refine 2–5 s, a judge 1–2 s, from a seeded generator. The Worker's own time between calls is counted in.
   - **Results:** first draft p50 is about 41 s and p95 about 43 s, against the gate of p50 ≤ 45 s and p95 ≤ 90 s.
   - **The money:** each run has one hold, settled once at the sum of its calls, never above its quote.

### Not in this decision

- **The capped staging run** on Workers AI (12 cards × 5 founders), which waits on owner decision N.
- **Ensembles for charts, diagrams and images**, which keep their own steps.
