### 1. The decision

A workflow that calls several connectors, such as Validate → Build → Vercel → back, runs as a durable run on JOB_QUEUE, with its state in D1.
- Each step holds and settles its own AI tokens, under the run's one execution id.
- A retry, a redelivery or a crash recovery never charges twice.
- A cancel releases every hold the run still has.
- Nothing is published without the founder's accept.
- There is no new balance, ledger or rate: every hold is `reserveAiBudget`'s, through `meteredCall`.

### 2. As built

**Migration 526** (`526_connector_flows.sql`): two new tables, additive.
- **`connector_flow_runs`:** `uid`, `user_id`, `company_id` (NULL for a flow with no company), `recipe_key`, `recipe_version`, `status`, `execution_id` (unique), `current_step`, `input_json`, `result_json`, `error_class`, and timestamps. `status` is one of `queued`, `running`, `waiting_accept`, `done`, `failed`, `cancelled`, held by a CHECK.
- **`connector_flow_steps`:** one row per step **attempt**: `run_uid`, `step_key`, `attempt` (1 to 3, by CHECK), `status` (`pending`, `running`, `ran`, `done`), `outcome` (`ok`, `failed`, `cancelled`), `error_class`, `idempotency_key` (unique), `reservation_uid`, `quote_microusd`, `cost_microusd`, `charged_microusd`, `output_json` and the lease.
  - `UNIQUE (run_uid, step_key, attempt)`.
- Unknown is NULL: `charged_microusd` stays NULL until a step settles, and while a cost above its hold is in review.

**`services/flows.ts`:**
- **Recipes are code constants** (`RECIPES`). A recipe is `{ key, version, steps }`, and each step is `{ key, kind, operation, connector?, run(ctx) }`, with the kinds trigger, read, map, proposal, publish and result.
- **`assertRecipe`** refuses a recipe that:
  - has a malformed or repeated step key;
  - has a step whose operation `FLOW_POLICY` does not price;
  - has a publish step that does not come after a proposal.
- **Prices** come from `FLOW_POLICY`: D844's `METERING_POLICY`, plus the demo recipe's stub operations (`flow.demo.*`). The real recipes add their prices to `METERING_POLICY` when they ship.
- **`startFlow(env, { userId, companyId?, recipeKey, input? })`:**
  - It checks scope (§4), with every connection the recipe names.
  - It writes the run and its first step in one batch, then queues the first step.
  - Without the Cloudflare queue it fails closed: the run is marked `failed` (`queue`) and the call throws.
- **`runFlowStep(env, message)`** runs one step attempt (§3). It returns `{ kind: 'ack' | 'retry', delaySeconds?, note }` and throws only when D1 or the queue cannot be used.
- **`acceptFlow(env, userId, runUid)`:** only the run's own member, and only at `waiting_accept`. It moves the run to `queued` and writes and queues the step after the proposal, in one conditional batch.
- **`cancelFlow(env, userId, runUid)`:** only the run's own member.
  - It closes the run and its open steps. Cancelling a cancelled run again only retries the releases.
  - It releases every pending hold filed under the run's execution (`settleAiReservation(…, 0, refund)`).
  - A finished run answers with its status and changes nothing.
- **The demo recipe** (`demo.stub_round_trip`, version 1) is trigger → read (github) → map (priced) → proposal → publish (github, priced) → result, all on stubs that call nothing. **No route starts a flow yet:** the founder flows come with #2426 (PIPE-BUILD) and #2430 (PIPE-RAISE), and the publish flow with #2400.

**`queue-consumer.ts`:**
- A message with `job_type: 'connector_flow_step'` goes to `runFlowStep` before the generic `job_idempotency` claim. A flow step dedupes on its own row's lease and schedules its own retries.
- The consumer acks, or redelivers after the runner's delay. If the runner throws, the consumer has the message retried.

### 3. One step, one hold, and the crash table

Each attempt's key is `flow:<run uid>:<step key>:<attempt>`. Its hold is filed under `holdKey(execution id, that key)`, so the issue's key is the hold's component.

The runner:
1. **Leases** the step with a conditional update: `pending`, or `running` past its lease (60 s).
2. **Re-checks scope** (§4).
3. **Quotes** the step from the policy.
4. **Runs** it through `meteredCall`.

Inside the metered run, the step's output is written to its row (`status = 'ran'`) **before** the hold settles.

| Where it stops | What the row and the hold say | What the next delivery does |
|---|---|---|
| A second delivery while the step runs | `running`, lease not ended | Redelivered once the lease ends; nothing runs |
| The worker dies mid-step | `running`, hold `pending` | After the lease: runs the step again under the **same key**; `reserveAiBudget` returns the same hold; it settles once |
| The output is written, the settle fails | `ran`, hold `pending` | Settles the pending hold at the recorded cost, then moves on; the step does not run again. A hold that expired meanwhile is `refunded`: nothing is charged, and the step records a known 0 |
| The hold settles, the close fails | `ran`, hold `settled` | Closes the step and moves on |
| A delivery of a finished step | `done` | Nothing runs. A pending next step whose message was lost is sent again |
| The old worker wakes after its step was resumed | its row is no longer `running` | Its output write changes nothing, it records nothing, and its release of the already-settled hold changes nothing |

**Retries.**
- A step's own failure (it throws, or times out after 30 s) releases that attempt's hold.
- The next attempt is a new row with a new key, queued after 30 s, then 120 s. Three attempts at most.
- A `FlowStepError('permanent')` is not retried.

**Refusals fail the run at once,** with no retry and nothing held for the step:
- a refusal at the hold: `insufficient_tokens`, `monthly_limit`, `ai_paused` or `budget_review`;
- a scope refusal: `forbidden`.

Earlier steps stay charged once.

**A proposal step** ends with the run at `waiting_accept`. The step after it is not even written until `acceptFlow`.

**Cancel during a step:**
- The step's pending hold is released at once.
- When the step tries to record its output, the row is already closed, so it records nothing and queues nothing.
- `meteredCall`'s settle then finds the hold not pending and changes nothing.

### 4. Security

- **At every step**, before the hold, the runner re-reads:
  - **the member:** an inactive account, a deletion request, or a `jwt_min_iat` later than the run's start ("sign out everywhere") ends the run;
  - **the company:** `canWrite`, as an MCP write tool;
  - **the step's connection:** an `integrations` row of the member's own, `active`, for the step's provider.

  Any miss fails the run as `forbidden`, with nothing held for the step.
- **The run is the server's.** Step input and output are data. They never change the run's member, company, recipe, step order or prices.
- **No secrets.** An error is stored only as a class from `ERROR_CLASSES`, never as a message or a provider's reply. The one log line names the recipe, the step and the attempt. A test asserts that a thrown error's text reaches neither the rows nor the log.
- **Bounded inputs.** A run's input is at most 16 KiB of JSON, and a step's output at most 64 KiB. A larger output fails the step as `permanent`.
- **SQL.** Every statement is bound, with no interpolation. `cancelFlow`'s `LIKE` prefix is escaped.

### 5. Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/connector_flows_d853.test.ts`, 10 tests, on the AI budget fixture with migrations 510, 525 and 526, the real holds and a fake JOB_QUEUE:
- **The migration:** its CHECKs and unique keys.
- **Recipes:** the demo is valid; a publish before a proposal, an unpriced step and a repeated key are refused.
- **A run end to end:** one hold per priced step under one execution; `waiting_accept` until the owner's accept; another member can neither accept nor cancel it.
- **A duplicate message is a no-op:**
  - two concurrent deliveries run the step once;
  - a redelivery runs nothing and re-sends a lost next-step message;
  - a malformed or stale message is acked.
- **A crashed step resumes without a second charge,** at the three crash points in §3, and records a known 0 when its hold expired before the redelivery.
- **A cancelled run releases its holds:**
  - the running step is not charged and queues nothing;
  - a cancelled proposal cannot be accepted;
  - a hold whose release failed is released by the next cancel;
  - what already ran stays charged.
- **Not enough AI tokens mid-run:** the run fails at the publish step, nothing is held for it, and there is no retry.
- **Retries:** the backoff delays, released holds and new keys; three failures fail the run; a permanent failure is not retried; no error text is stored or logged.
- **Scope at every step:** losing the company, the connection, the session or the account stops the run before any hold, and `startFlow` refuses each of these up front.
- **The queue consumer** dispatches, acks, and redelivers while leased.

**Mutation checks: 20 of 20 killed,** each both ways. Each is named in the comment of the test that kills it.

### 6. Known limitations

- **No route and no UI.** Starting, accepting and cancelling are service calls. The routes come with the first real recipe (#2426, #2430, #2400) and its UI.
- **A step whose message is lost before it is ever delivered** is re-sent only when another message of the run arrives. No sweep re-queues runs that have gone quiet. This is a small cron job (stale `pending` and `running` rows), to add with the first real recipe.
- **A provider call that repeats** after a crash in the middle of a step is deduped only if the provider honours the attempt's `idempotencyKey`, which `FlowStepContext` passes to every step. The connector contract (D848) is where a provider's dedupe is declared.
