### 1. Two Worker reads (`routes/admin_partner_firms.ts`)

Both are requireAdmin and read only. They are mounted at `/api/admin/partners`, before `admin_partners.ts`. The writes the tab makes already existed and are unchanged:
- `POST /api/admin/partners/:id/directory`, the directory switches;
- `POST /api/admin/partners/links`, attach and detach (D952).

**A firm's deals are its accounts' deals.** A deal belongs to the partner account that signed it, and the account's firm is `users.partner_id` (D57). D972's table and D973's overview read them the same way. A deal on an account with no firm is no firm's.

**Each block is in D973's shape:**
- `{ available: true, recorded: true, value }`: the store was read;
- `{ available: true, recorded: false, reason }`: no store holds the figure;
- `{ available: false, reason }`: the read failed.

Each store has its own statement and catch, so one failed store costs only its column. No email, motivation, prior deals, chat, proposal JSON, memo or referral code is selected; the test holds that no statement names `email`.

**`GET /firms`** (AP5) lists every firm, with no first-500 cut.
- **Search** (`q`, at most 200 characters) runs over the name, company and specialization.
- **Filters:**
  - `directory`: `listed`, `featured` or `not_listed`;
  - `deal_type`: one of the five;
  - `ends_within`: 30, 60 or 90 days, on the firm's soonest active end;
  - `no_accounts`: `true` or `false`.
- **Sorts:** `name_asc` (the default, by company, else name), `name_desc`, `accounts_desc`, `deal_ends_asc` and `rating_desc`. Missing values sort last, with the firm id as the tie-break.
- **Pages:** `limit` from 1 to 100 (default 25) and `offset`. Filtering and sorting happen before paging, and `total` is exact.
- **Refusals:** a value outside its list is a 400 through `refuse`. The firms failing is a 503 (`firms_unreadable`). A filter or sort that needs a failed store is a 503 (`filter_unreadable`, naming the `source`), never a false empty list.

Each item carries the nine columns:
- `accounts`;
- `deal`: count, types, `soonest_end` (null when no end is stored, never a guessed term) and `ending_soon` (within 30 days);
- `redemptions`, on active deals;
- `directory`;
- `rating`: count and average of 1–5 ratings, with an average of null when there are none;
- `settlements_due`: issued settlements due from today through 30 days on the Lab's clock (ET), each direction summed alone;
- and the firm's name, company and specialization.

**`GET /firms/:id`** (AP6) answers 404 for no such firm and 503 when the firm row itself could not be read. It returns:
- `accounts`: the attached accounts' names and headlines;
- `directory`, with when it was last decided;
- `deals`: every state, newest 50, each with its redemption count;
- `settlements`: every state, latest 50 by due date;
- `profiles`: each attached account's partner profile. Each fact is a value or Not recorded, the lists are de-duplicated, and capital is in cents;
- `office_hours`: the firm's three guidance fields, its rating and its latest Lab host status.

**Settlements are HQ's** (D973). On a branch both reads answer `recorded: false`, "Settlements are kept by HQ.", and no settlement statement runs.
