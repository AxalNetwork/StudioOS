### 1. The nine builders (`DRAFT_BUILDERS`)

**Each place reads only the fields `PUBLISH_SOURCES` lists for its stores.** A test holds every `SELECT` in `services/publishSources.ts` to that list.

**Every line keeps its source and its date** on the draft's `source_refs`: `{ ref, store, source, as_of, team_only, text }`. The chip reads, for example, "From Product improvement · 1 shipped item · Oct 2026".

| Place | A line | From |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | "MRR: $18,400" | `growFigures`: each recorded headline figure. Unrecorded or unreadable ones are left out; `too_little` (under three points) still has a value and is quoted |
| Updates | the title; "We are looking for: …" per ask; then "MRR: $18,400" | the newest **sent** `portfolio_updates` row, or one named by `portfolio_updates:<uid>`. This is the network's version: the asks, plus the metrics only when the founder included them for the network. Never highlights, lowlights, the body or the audience |
| Product improvement | "Shipped: Bulk CSV import" | `product_feedback` that is `shipped` in the last 90 days, once per item. Never the person, their email, their company or their words |
| Distribution | "Now on LinkedIn"; "Tried: Founder video series" | active `growth_channels`, and `channel_experiments` that ran. Never spend, CAC or UTM |
| Revenue and traction | "MRR: $18,400", "Paying customers: 42", "New users: 0" | the newest recorded value of each `project_metrics` figure. A recorded 0 is 0; an unrecorded one is left out |
| Team expansion | "Hiring: Founding engineer · Lisbon — … /jobs/founding-engineer" | `job_postings` that are `published` **and** `admin_published`. Never pay, equity, the description, review notes or applicants |
| Partner network | "Signed an integration partnership" | signed `growth_partners`: the type only (§2) |
| Market expansion | "Now serving Portugal", "Entering Spain", "Done: …" | markets `served`, `entering` or decided `enter`, and done `market_entry_steps`. Never scores, their sources, size or owners |
| Durable company-building | "Tax ID (EIN) received: complete" | done `durability_checklist` items, by `DURABILITY_ITEMS`' labels |

`source_refs` narrows a draft to the records chosen. Text of the founder's own makes a draft with no pre-fill, as part 1 did.

### 2. The never-list

- **No customer's, applicant's or interviewee's name or contact.** None of those fields is selected. The test seeds a PRIVATE value into each and finds none in any draft.
- **No partner's name.** #2400 asks for "The partner agreed to be named" to be recorded, but `growth_partners` has no such column, and this part has no migration. So a signed partner's line names its type, never the partner. `dropped.unnamed` counts it. A consent column, with the name gated on it, is the follow-up.
- **No team-only figure unless ticked** (#2400 decision F, (b)). A figure is team-only when its source is the founder's Mercury, or when it is a bank figure: net burn, cash, cash in, runway, or the "Twelve months of runway" checklist item.
  - **Not ticked** (`team_only_included` absent or false): the line is left out and counted in `dropped.team_only`.
  - **Ticked:** the line goes in, and its source ref is marked `team_only` with the figure's own text. If the founder later takes the tick back, part 1's `publishLint` blocks that figure again on every preview and confirm.
- **Never a 0 for a missing figure.** An unrecorded figure is left out and counted in `dropped.not_recorded`; `figureText` answers `null`, never "0". A recorded 0 stays 0.

### 3. Publish to… and "Published from here" (`publishPart`)

The overview read carries `publish`, and each Grow module read carries `parts.publish`. A sent update's `GET /api/portfolio-updates/:uid/publish` carries the same, for `updates`.

- **`can_publish`** is true only when both hold:
  - `canPublish` passes: not a support session, never an admin, partner, advisor or investor, and only the venture's founder (D1319 §3);
  - at least one of the founder's own places is switched on for the company, through a provider with a `PUBLISH_TARGETS` row.
- **PUB8's empty states:**
  - **`connect`:** "Connect a place to publish", linking to `/account/connectors`, for the founder with nothing switched on;
  - **`founder_only`:** "Only the founder publishes for [Company].", for anyone else who reads the page;
  - **`support`:** for a support session.
- **`recent`:** the page's last three sends, newest first (`publish_sends` by `module`), for the venture's team only (`isTeamViewer`; `null` for anyone else).
  - Stranded rows are released first, as part 1's history read does.
  - No post body is in the gate.
- **Failures:** a gate that cannot be read is `unreadable` with its sentence. The rest of the page draws.

### 4. A sent update

`POST /api/portfolio-updates/:uid/publish-draft` makes a `updates` draft from that update (§1), with `{ team_only_included? }`.
- Only the venture's founder can make one (`canPublish`; anyone else gets `publish_not_allowed`).
- Only a sent update qualifies; one not sent gets 409 `not_sent`.
- A report (`kind = 'report'`) gets 404.

Nothing is sent here: the founder previews and confirms through `/api/publish`.

### 5. Checks

**`cloudflare-worker/test/publish_sources_d1333.test.ts`** has 8 tests, on the migrated schema with the routes on a bare Hono and `_publish_fake.mjs`:
- **The sources:** nine places with a source and a builder; no field both quoted and never; the plan's never fields listed; every `SELECT` reading listed fields only; no interpolated SQL.
- **Never-list fields are dropped:** every place's draft from records seeded with PRIVATE values carries none, and every line keeps its source and date. Each builder's text is pinned:
  - shipped once, and recently;
  - active channels only;
  - published and admin-published roles;
  - the partner's type, not the name;
  - served and entering markets and done steps;
  - the runway item held back;
  - the update's network version.
- **Team-only figures are dropped unless ticked:**
  - a Mercury figure, a bank figure typed by hand, and the runway item are all held back;
  - ticked, they go in, marked;
  - through the routes, taking the tick back makes the check block the figure.
- **An Overview figure from Mercury** is team-only too.
- **Nothing missing becomes 0:**
  - an empty venture drafts no zero;
  - unrecorded figures are counted;
  - `figureText`.
- **The founder's own text is kept;** chosen refs narrow the draft; an unknown place is 400.
- **The gate is false for every refused role:**
  - connect, then on;
  - the co-founder, the admin and the partner read "Only the founder publishes for Northwind Data.";
  - a support session;
  - the last three sends, for the team only, from the route and from the gate;
  - the overview's gate.
- **A sent update:**
  - its network draft, with no highlight, bank figure or unrecorded one;
  - a co-founder refused;
  - the gate for the team, and 404 for anyone else;
  - an update not sent: 409, and it drafts nothing;
  - metrics only when included.
