# D6287 — Investor firm profile and thesis settings

The investor's unscoped Company Settings address opens Firm Settings. When a
company is selected, it appears as a separate link to its scoped settings.
Explicit company addresses and the existing partner and advisor branches retain
their previous behavior. Investor Home, Deal flow and Security & Privacy point
to the new editor.

The editor waits for the profile read, distinguishes unreadable from empty
values, and submits changed fields with the recorded Signals preference in one
save. The Worker upsert preserves omitted fields atomically and clears explicit
nulls or empty selections. Ticket amounts and thesis keywords follow their
parent fields. Completion is calculated from the saved row in the same batch.

The status blocks, deal-fit comparison and seat flow shown in D6286 remain
follow-up work. The editor uses the existing personal profile rather than
assuming a shared firm profile or changing the Signals policy.
