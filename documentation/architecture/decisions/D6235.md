## D6235 — The GP of record creates their fund and edits its terms without the admin console (#6235)

- **Status:** built on `claude/trusting-fermi-7zjy71-6235`. Canvas: `design/incoming/Investor Fund v2 Page.dc.html`, board IFD01B (FV1b), from D6229.
- **Builds on:** `POST /api/funds` and `PATCH /api/funds/:id` in `routes/funds.ts`, unchanged; `services/fundAdminAccess.ts`; the admin console's parsers in `frontend/src/pages/admin/fundAdmin/fundsModel.js`.

### What was checked first (the issue's "confirm first" list)

- **`FUND_PATCHABLE`** (`models/funds.ts`) takes name, vintage_year, total_commitment, deployed_capital, lp_count, status, lpa_doc_id, fund_size_cents, carried_interest, management_fee, the GP-of-record and provider fields, and slug. Edit terms sends only name, vintage_year, fund_size_cents, status, management_fee and carried_interest, and only those that changed.
- **`assertAdminAct(c, creator, 'fund.create')`** asks nothing of a non-admin creator beyond refusing a support session. `requireFundCreator` is the tier gate (Institutional, or an admin). The creator becomes the GP of record and an LPA draft is queued.
- **Step-up for a non-admin GP:** none. A fee or carry change is the `fund.economics` act, a Super Admin act for an admin (step-up applies there), but for a non-admin GP of record the gate only refuses a support session and applies the recovery cool-off. The PATCH `reason` is optional on the Worker, 10 to 500 characters when sent, and kept on the activity and audit rows.

### Decisions

1. **Create** sits on the no-fund state of `/funds` as the canvas draws it: name, vintage, size, management fee, carry, plus status (the issue asks for it; the board omits it). After the Worker returns the fund, the overview selects it with `?fund=` and reloads.
2. **Both rates are required.** The board's line "Left blank, the platform uses 2% and 20%" is not built: a blank stored as the Worker's default would read as a recorded rate everywhere, which the fund pages promise never to show. This matches the admin console's New fund.
3. **Edit terms** is on `/funds/:id` for a manager who is not an admin, in place of the old "Edit fund facts: Not available" line. An admin keeps the console's Edit fund facts link, where the step-up that admins need is already drawn. The form reads `GET /api/funds/:id` first and sends one PATCH of what changed; a changed fee or carry requires the reason in the form, though the Worker does not.
4. **Nothing is assumed saved.** A Worker refusal is shown as its own sentence followed by "Nothing was saved." A request that got no answer says the outcome is not known and asks for a refresh before trying again.

### Not built here

The canvas has no Edit terms board, so the form follows the create board's fields and the console's wording. Changing the GP of record, the slug and the service providers stays in the admin console.
