## D6234 — Fund overview to canvas IF0, and a fund's quarterly history (IF5) (#6234)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-15, frontend only. Builds the parts of IF0 and IF5 (`design/canvases/integrated/Pages · Funds and fund research.dc.html`, boards `if0`, `if0e`, `if5`, `if5e`) that D6229 lists as frontend only: the overview tiles, the gap list, and the chart's gap and zero rules, with FV12's states and FV14's phone layout from `design/incoming/Investor Fund v2 Page.dc.html`.
- **Reads (both on main):** `GET /api/funds/analytics` for the tiles; `GET /api/funds/:id/report-periods` for the chart, the history and the gaps. The list read returns `snapshot_json` raw and parsed as `snapshot` (`shapePeriod` in `routes/funds.ts`), and `narrative` parsed from the notes.

### What is built

- **Tiles** (`overviewTiles`): Committed, Called, Deployed, Dry powder (collected less deployed). A tile whose source is absent reads Not recorded with its reason; a recorded 0 is a figure.
- **Chart** (`chartGroups`, `FundQuarterChart.jsx`): issued periods only, oldest first; a draft never plots. Four bars per quarter (Committed, Called, Collected, Deployed). A figure the snapshot does not carry is a short hatched stub with a dashed outline, never a zero bar; a measured 0 sits on the baseline. "Show as table" lists the same figures.
- **Gap list** (`packGaps`): four rules for the quarter the next pack reports on, which is the last calendar quarter that has ended. (1) an issued snapshot for that quarter, (2) NAV present in it, (3) the letter written in that quarter's period, (4) delivery recorded on it (`delivery_count`, `delivered_count`, `delivery_status` or `delivered_at`, the fields the Reporting zone already reads; none exists on main, so this row stays open: issued is not delivered). Each open row links to where it is fixed: the period editor (`/funds/:fundId/reporting/:periodId`), the Ledger or Reporting zone with `?fund=`. An unreadable archive claims no row either way.
- **Filters** Overview / This quarter / Gaps: tiles, chart and gaps; tiles, the quarter's periods and gaps; open gaps only.
- **History** at `/funds/history?fund=<id>` (`FundHistoryPage.jsx`, route module `frontend/src/routes/fundHistory.jsx`): tiles, the full-width chart, every period including drafts with its snapshot figures (cards on a phone), the gap list, and Export series (CSV: exact dollars from integer cents, an empty cell where a figure is not recorded).

### Route

IF5 drew `/funds/:id`; `FundPage` holds `/funds/:fundId` (D6244) and `/funds/performance` is the cross-fund summary (D6244 Q1). Neither D6229 nor D6244 names IF5's route, so the history is `/funds/history`, a child of Overview that takes the fund from `?fund=` like every Fund zone (D6229). The static segment outranks `/funds/:fundId`.

### Snapshot fields the chart lacks (Worker follow-up)

- A snapshot issued by the GP of record (`readFundPage`) carries committed and invested only: no called-to-date, receipts-to-date or NAV.
- A pack issued through the reporting queue (`lp_quarterly_report/1`) carries no deployed (cost) figure.
- Neither carries a sector mix.
These draw hatched until the Worker freezes them.
