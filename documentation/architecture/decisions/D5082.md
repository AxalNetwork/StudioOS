## D5082 — Revenue's four ledger positions read the licence-fee invoice ledger, per currency (#5082)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-09 (#5082). Frontend only. No route, no migration.
- **Number:** D5082 is the issue's number (D4106).
- **Builds on:** D5039 part 1 (#5111: the cockpit, the eight value states, temporal labels, the Data coverage drawer), D5036 (#5132: `licence_invoices` on `GET /api/admin/revenue/summary`), D5029 (money in native minor units).

### What part 1 left open

#5111 met most of #5082's acceptance criteria: the executive strip, one Data coverage surface, Coverage in place of Read, reusable value states, temporal labels and no cross-currency total. The four ledger positions (Invoiced, Collected, Outstanding and Overdue) were drawn once as Not recorded, because no store backed them. D5036 adds that store for licence fees. This change draws it.

### What the summary now does

`ledgerPosition(summary)` in `pages/hq/revenue/revenueModel.js` decides the four tiles:

| `summary.licence_invoices` | The four tiles |
|---|---|
| absent (a Worker without D5036) | one row of **Not recorded**, as part 1 drew them |
| `{ available: false }` | one row of **Unavailable**; the reason is in the drawer |
| the summary is still loading, or failed | one row of **Loading**, or Unavailable |
| read | one row **per native currency** |

When the ledger is read:

- **Invoiced and Collected** come from `by_currency`, the invoices issued in the selected period, and carry the period label.
- **Outstanding and Overdue** come from `receivable_by_currency`, every open invoice as at today, and carry "As of now". So a past period never hides today's receivable, and the period label never implies it.
- A currency the readable ledger holds nothing in, beside one it does, is a measured **Zero** in its own currency. A ledger with no invoice in any currency draws **None**, never a currency it never held.
- Overdue is marked for attention only when there is an overdue amount.
- The block is headed "Licence fees · invoice ledger", with one "Why?" link.
- Licence years started without an invoice, and billable licences that cannot be invoiced, are counted under the block. The names stay in the Worker's reply.
- Currencies are never summed or converted.

### The coverage drawer

The `ledger` source goes from Not recorded to **Partial** once the ledger is read. Its one reason, `ledgerReason`, says the ledger covers licence-fee invoices only: subscription revenue and revenue-share statements are outside it, Collected is HQ-entered and not reconciled against a bank, and no monthly series exists. An unreadable ledger is Unavailable, with the Worker's reason. Before the field exists, the drawer keeps part 1's `LEDGER_REASON`.
