## D1342 — Email part 3: Network members email each other after a mutual accept, each sharing their own address

- **Status:** built by S17, 2026-10-28 (#3550, part 3 of Email, #3547).
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `daed664fd1`. Paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Map:** D1347. The owner's canvas draws option A of #3547 Q2 (§1): each member shares their own address. EM5's first card reads "You're matched. Email needs both of you to share an address." (§3).
- **Migration:** 649 (`649_mail_address_shares.sql`).

### 1. The store (migration 649)

`mail_address_shares` holds one row per pair of members whose introduction both accepted:
- `user_low` is the lower user id and `user_high` the higher, so a pair is one row whichever side opens it (`UNIQUE (user_low, user_high)`, `CHECK (user_low < user_high)`);
- `source_kind` is `introduction` or `match`, and `source_ref` names it. Today only a mutually accepted introduction opens a row; `source_ref` is the proposition uid whose accept completed it;
- each side's `shared_at`, `declined_at` and `revoked_at`.

A pair is named by its `uid` everywhere, never by a user id.

### 2. When a pair opens

- **On the second accept.** `POST /api/introductions/propositions/:uid/accept` already noticed when both sides had accepted (the `intro_connected` notices). In that branch it now calls `askBoth` (`services/mail/shares.ts`). That opens the pair (`INSERT OR IGNORE`) and asks each member, linking `/email`: "Share your email address with ‹name›?", with the body "You're matched. Email needs both of you to share an address." It is best-effort, inside the existing notice block.
- **Lazily, for pairs accepted before 649.** Listing one's matches opens a row for each mutual accept the member is in that has none. No notice is sent for those.
- **Never otherwise.** A pair needs both of the members' propositions for each other to be `accepted`.

### 3. The routes (`routes/mail_shares.ts`, mounted at `/api/mail`)

| Route | What it does |
|---|---|
| `GET /shares` | The member's Network matches, newest first. Each row has the uid, the person ref (`member:<uid>`), the other's name and role, `mine` and `theirs` (`pending`, `shared`, `declined` or `revoked`), `ready`, `address`, `line` and `matched_at`. |
| `POST /shares/:uid` | `{ share: true }` shares the member's own address and clears their decline or revoke. `{ share: false }` declines. Anything else is 400 `share_required`. The pair must still be a mutual accept, else 409 `not_matched`. |
| `DELETE /shares/:uid` | Takes a share back. Only a side that shared can: 409 `not_shared` otherwise. |

**The address.** It is the other member's account address, lower-cased. `GET /shares` serves it only while both sides are `shared`: neither has revoked or declined since. A revoke or a decline hides it from the other at once, on their next read. This is the only route that serves another member's address. D414's rule stands everywhere else: a match's context record, the notices and every other read carry none.

**Telling the other member.** When a member shares, the other is told, linking `/email`: "‹name› shared their email address with you", or "You can now email ‹name›" once both have. A decline or a revoke tells no one, as an introduction's decline tells no one.

Another member's pair, or one that does not exist, is the same 404, "Match not found.", and changes nothing. A support session cannot share, decline or revoke (403 `support_session_refused`); it may read the list.

### 4. The People list gains Network matches

D1341's context pack (`services/mail/contextPack.ts`) left the person kind `member` to this part. `member:<share uid>` is a Network match:
- its relationship is `network`, so its intent is "Introduce myself";
- its record is the match itself: the other's name, role and when the introduction was accepted;
- `person.email_ready` is true only when both have shared, so the Email action exists only then (#3552 draws it from this flag and from `GET /shares`);
- its link is `/email`.

A member outside the pair reads the same 404 as a ref that does not exist. #3555 (D1348) groups the matches into each profile's People pane. An Explorer's pane is their matches only, and the pack already refuses an Explorer every founder module's record (the record pages' own gates).

### 5. Verified

`cloudflare-worker/test/mail_shares_d1342.test.ts` has 6 tests, on the real migrated schema with the introductions, drafts and shares routes mounted:
1. each side's state (a revoke wins, then a share, then a decline) and `ready`;
2. one accept opens nothing; the second accept, through the real route, opens the pair and asks both, in D1347 §3's words, linking `/email`;
3. one side sharing reveals nothing; both reveal both; a revoke hides the address at once, and sharing again shows it; a decline from either side hides it; a high-side revoke and re-share;
4. one-sided accepts list nothing; a forged pair without the mutual accept cannot share; another member's pair is 404 for share, decline and revoke and is left unchanged; a bad body is 400; a support session is refused;
5. an Explorer reads only their match; the match's pack is `network`, with `email_ready` false then true and no address; a founder module's record is 404 to them; no notice carries an address;
6. a pair accepted before 649 opens once, whichever side lists it, without a notice.
