### What the canvas decides

- The desk opens on a "Needs you now" strip. Asked of you and Follow-ups due sit above the history and are read on their own, so the Team and Status filters never hide them. When the history is filtered they say "not filtered".
- The history pages 20 at a time with Load 20 more, and says "20 shown of 201" only where the Worker returns a whole-list total.
- The investor sees three groups, each with its own count and unit: From your team (free), Curated matches (Accept uses 1 credit) and Your asks (each uses 1 quarterly ask). Founder, advisor and operating partner see only the groups their licence grants. Asks stay investor only.
- A notification link opens its exact record as the record Sheet over the unchanged desk, even when the record is outside the loaded page or the filter. The page looks it up directly and says "not available" only after a 404.
- `kind=request` opens a free teammate request; `kind=proposition` opens a curated match. A table shows where each lands for each licence.
- A signed-out link keeps its target through sign-in. On return the desk restores filters, scroll, loaded pages and focus.
- In a support session From your team is refused without disclosing anything; Curated matches and Your asks are read-only.
- Request an introduction is the one primary and opens the existing request sheet. Offer one stays a separate feature (#6637). Eadwyn quotes the cost of a follow-up draft before writing it.
