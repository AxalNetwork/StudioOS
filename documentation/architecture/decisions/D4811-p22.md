2. **Closings on the agreement lifecycle.** A closing line's paper is D1216's staged envelope (`GET /api/legal/esign/staged/line/:id`, `services/stagedSigning.ts` `readStaged`). One pure rule, `closingPaperLifecycle` in `lib/contractLifecycle.js`, maps what that read already returns. It draws the same pill objects as an agreement:

   | The staged paper | Lifecycle | Detail |
   |---|---|---|
   | `complete` (the Worker's own reading: every stage, Axal's included, has a signature) | ● Executed | "countersigned for Axal" when Axal signed last |
   | a stage sent or signed, Axal's countersign waiting | ➤ Sent · awaiting signature | "waiting for Axal's countersign" |
   | a stage sent or signed | ➤ Sent · awaiting signature | "1 of 2 signed" |
   | stages, none sent yet (its text and digest are stored) | ◔ Ready for signature | "not sent yet" |
   | only voided papers | ⊘ Voided | none |
   | no paper | no state | "No paper sent" |

   The newest paper that is not voided is the one read. A paper is never Executed without the Worker's `complete`, even when every stage reads signed. Prepared is never drawn: no closing store records a paper before it is generated.

   `deskModel.paperWord` (the admin desk, the route dialog, the third-party drawer) and `roomModel.paperPhrase` (the room) return this rule's words. The route dialog and the third-party drawer label that row "Paper", not "Signed paper", since it now also reads Sent or Ready. The room's tile that counts lines with executed paper reads Executed, not Signed; its count is unchanged (a paper the Worker reads `complete`). The admin desk's Paper cell and the room's line table, phone card and "Your line" tile draw the pill through `closing/PaperState.jsx`, which uses the shared `LifecycleBadge` with its light and dark pairs. A prospect line, an unread paper and a failed read keep their own sentences.

**Not mapped, and why (D4811 question D, posted on #4811; the owner answered A on 2026-11-13: keep their own words, no pill):**
- **Declined:** a signer declined their stage. The staged paper is not voided (`voided_at` is unset), so Voided would claim a void the record does not hold. No other lifecycle word fits. It reads "Declined", with no pill.
- **A signer's envelope voided:** one stage's esign envelope is void while the staged paper is not. This is the same case as Declined. It reads "A signer's envelope was voided", with no pill. Before this part, it read "Signing · n of m signed".
- **The closing's own Open, Closed and Cancelled** is the state of the round, not of a document. Closed follows money received and the cap-table decision, not executed paper. It stays as it is and is not drawn as a lifecycle pill. The money words (Waiting for money, Sent, not yet confirmed, Received, Withdrawn) are not legal states either, and they stay.
