## D6700 — Country-specific counsel review and second-person catalogue publication (#6700)

HQ reads a version's proposed content digest and required countries, then submits that digest, a reason and a map from each required country to its assigned counsel member. Each rule-set review covers the canonical rules **and** scenarios. A change to either needs approval for every country with an orderable form. Enabling an additional form also requires admission in its country. Fee, source and fact changes alone require a reason and a second person. The publisher uses a TOTP session with recent step-up. The last successful D6699 row edit identifies the editor, with the draft creator as the fallback. Submission and withdrawal do not change this identity.

The editor checks a draft's base and the absence of another open version inside its copy transaction. For a draft created before the migration, its creation audit supplies the base. Saving identical canonical content keeps the last editor. The existing editor field `rules_sha256` covers rules alone. The submission field `review_rules_sha256` covers rules and scenarios.

The draft's monotonically increasing content revision closes the race between reading content across tables and storing the submission. All eight versioned tables increment it on insert, update and delete. Submitted rows are frozen until HQ withdraws the round to correct the draft or change its reviewer. Old decisions remain attached to their original round. D6713's nullable obligation metadata survives cloning and contributes to the canonical catalogue digest.

Counsel can inspect the rules and scenarios behind their assigned hash, and decide once after step-up. The store binds the reviewer, jurisdiction, hash, object and review round, and prevents deletion or replacement. It stores a decision with its time. A country admission requires existing counsel membership. HQ Super Admins grant and revoke admission with a reason and the same write bar as publication. Publication rechecks current admission and membership for every required approval. A successful guarded update retires the specific current base and promotes its successor in the same transaction as the audit. A losing publish leaves the current version in place.

Version 1 keeps its seed publication. The D6693 order gate checks actual template bytes against the existing template reviews in the form's jurisdiction. D6700 does not change template review ownership or enable any form, appoint counsel, publish content, or change decision 15's launch restrictions. H6 (#6702) owns the screens.
