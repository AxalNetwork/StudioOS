### What Eadwyn does here

Eadwyn proposes; the founder decides. Nothing a draft does reaches a reader.

- **Pack selection (RA-27 A).** In the pack composer, with a reader after an NDA (the only reader for whom the founder ticks items), the founder says who the pack is for in one line. Eadwyn proposes items for that reader, one plain reason each, and one line on what it left out.
  - *Accept selection* ticks those rows in the composer and nothing else. The founder can still change any row, and still presses Create pack or Download PDF.
  - *Discard* drops the draft.
  - An item made Private since the proposal is not ticked, and the result says how many were left unticked.
- **Covering line (RA-30).** Under the snapshot's covering line, *Use Eadwyn's draft* asks for one sentence: where preparation stands, the largest gap, and what comes next.
  - *Accept* puts the sentence in the field. *Edit* lets the founder change it first. *Discard* drops it.
  - The kept line is the founder's, in the field they can still change. The Worker checks an edit like any covering line: one sentence, at most 200 characters, no email or link.

### What Eadwyn is sent

- **Pack.** The items a pack may hold now, read through `outsidePackView`, the founder's own preview reader after an NDA. Each item goes as uid, area, kind, source, strength, day and the founder's summary (at most 200 characters). At most 120 items are sent, newest first. Never a Private item, never a figure (`value_num` is not sent), never a file, nothing from the data room.
- **Covering line.** The seven snapshot areas as `readinessBody` builds them: score words, evidence kind, what is missing. There is no overall score and no Product-market fit row, as on the snapshot.

Both go inside an untrusted block.

### The run

- **One `rail_reason` run per press, quoted first.**
  - The press must carry the quote's `confirm_quote`: a digest of the member, the venture, the input, the model and the hold.
  - A missing or stale one gets 402 `quote_required` with the new quote, and the model is not called.
  - A quote for one audience does not run another.
- **Surfaces.** The pack draft runs on `AI_SURFACE.raiseEvidence` (`/raise/evidence`, new). The line runs on `AI_SURFACE.raiseReadiness`. D552's table lists both.
- **The output is checked before anything is stored.**
  - A pack draft keeps only uids the pack may hold now, each once, at most 30, with a reason of at most 200 characters. A reason with an email, a link, or the words the product never uses is dropped.
  - A line must pass `coveringLine` and the same word check.
  - When nothing survives, there is no draft and the page says so.
- **One draft of each kind.** A new draft withdraws the member's open draft of the same kind.

### Store and gate

- **Store.** Drafts are `ai_rail_proposals` rows on page `Founder · Raise · Evidence`, option `share:pack` or `share:covering-line`, record `raise-share:<projectId>`.
  - Accept writes the row as `accepted`, or `edited` for a changed line, with what was kept and who pressed.
  - Discard writes it as `discarded`.
  - No pack, link, snapshot, sharing or evidence item is written.
  - One cycle-activity row records each ask and each press.
- **The generic decide route can only discard a `share:` row.** It goes through `/api/ai/rail/proposals/:uid/decide`, the same rule as `dataroom:` (D4170), so every keep reads the founders' gate.
- **Who.** The venture's founders, through `evidenceScope(c, true)`, exactly as the other Share outside Axal routes. A draft is its proposer's own: another founder's is 409 `not_open`.

### Reads and writes

- `GET /api/cycles/raise/share-drafts`: the member's latest draft of each kind.
- `POST /api/cycles/raise/share-drafts/quote`: takes `{ kind: 'pack', audience }` or `{ kind: 'covering-line' }`.
- `POST /api/cycles/raise/share-drafts/run`: takes the same body plus `confirm_quote`, and answers `{ draft | null, cost }`.
- `POST /api/cycles/raise/share-drafts/:uid/accept`:
  - a pack, with no body, answers `{ tick, dropped }`;
  - a line takes an optional `{ line }` (the founder's edit) and answers `{ line }`.
- `POST /api/cycles/raise/share-drafts/:uid/discard`.
- Route file: `routes/raise_share_drafts.ts`, mounted by the registry under `/api/cycles/raise`. Service: `services/raiseShareProposals.ts`.

### Frontend

- `pages/cycles/evidence/ShareDrafts.jsx`: `PackProposal` and `LineDraft`. Each reads the cost first (Holds up to … · you pay what it uses) and runs only on Confirm.
- `pages/cycles/evidence/shareDraftsModel.js`: the canvas's words, the audience check, the proposed rows against the composer's rows, and one sentence per refusal.
- When the model cannot be asked, each panel says the sheet works without it.

### Where this departs from the canvas

- **No *Edit* button on the pack proposal.** RA-27 A draws Accept selection, Edit and Discard. Accepting only ticks rows, and every row stays the founder's to change, so the composer's own checkboxes do the editing. The panel says "You can still change any row."
- **Pack proposals only for a reader after an NDA.** A pack before an NDA is coverage only, and the composer offers no rows to tick there, so the proposal is offered only for a reader after an NDA.
