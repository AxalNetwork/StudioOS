## D6225 — An investor asks the company about a received report, and the founders answer (#6225)

**Date:** 2026-11-27 · **Issue:** #6225 · **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Grow v2 Report Questions Page.dc.html` (D6096, GRQ22 to GRQ27)

### The owner's answer

The issue asked how an investor asks about a report they received: (A) one question thread per investor per report, private between that investor and the founder team; (B) a link into the existing messaging with the report attached; (C) keep it off the platform. **The owner answered A on 2026-11-27**, matching the Report Questions canvas's questions with Reply. It agrees with the owner's answers on #6096 (D6096 part 2): reports carry questions, private to the asker and the venture's founders, in a new store tied to the report row, and an admin, advisor or partner reads the threads without replying.

The word is **Question**. The older "Ask for this report" (`POST /api/reporting/periods/:uid/ask`, `routes/reporting.ts`) chases a late period and is a different thing.

### What is built

- **Store.** Migration `202611272118_6225_report_questions.sql`: `report_questions`, one row per question, tied to the revision it was asked on (`update_id`), the asker, an optional figure key, the text (1 to 600 characters), the founders' first read (`read_at`), a withdrawal, and one answer (1 to 1,200 characters, who and when). A `CHECK` keeps the answer's three columns together and a question from being both answered and withdrawn; a trigger refuses any edit of an answer.
- **Routes.** `routes/report_questions.ts`, mounted at `/api/report-questions`: `GET` and `POST /reports/:uid`, `POST /questions/:uid/reply`, `POST /questions/:uid/withdraw`.
  - An investor recipient of that revision (`update_recipients`, audience investor) asks, reads only their own questions, and withdraws one not yet answered. No response to an investor names another investor or the asker.
  - The founders (`founderTeam`: the owner and accepted co-founders, never an admin) read every thread on the report and answer each question once. Their first read marks the unread ones read; the response still shows them as Unread.
  - An admin or an advisor of the venture (`canAccessProject`) reads the threads (`view: 'read_only'`) and is refused 403 `founders_only` on a reply.
  - Anyone else, a draft, and another company's report are 404 `not_found` (a question: `question_not_found`). Another investor's question is the same 404 as none.
  - A support session reads what the member would read and every write is 403 `support_session_refused`; its read marks nothing read.
  - Fifty questions per investor per report, checked inside the `INSERT`. A reply to a withdrawn question is 409 `question_withdrawn`; a second reply is 409 `already_answered`.
- **Notifications** through `services/notify.ts`, category `portfolio`, in-app and email: `reporting_question_asked` to each founder (link `/grow/reporting/:uid?project_id=…#questions`), `reporting_question_answered` to the asker (link `/portfolio/companies/:uid/reports`). A failed notification never undoes the write.
- **Client.** `frontend/src/lib/api/reportQuestions.js` (`reportQuestionsApi`).
- **Investor.** The reader (`CompanyReports.jsx`'s `ReportReader`) ends with "Your questions" in place of "A report carries no asks to reply to.": each question with what it is about, when, its pill (◷ Sent · not yet read, ● Read, ✓ Answered, – Withdrawn), the founder's answer, and Withdraw… reviewed in the page. "Ask about the whole report" and each figure row's "Question about this figure" open the composer (600 characters, counter, the privacy line, Cancel and Send question); a figure row carries its live question count.
- **Founders.** The sent report (`ReportPage.jsx`) draws Questions above the report as frozen: the counts line, one card per question with the investor, what it is about, the pill (● Unread, ◷ Open, ✓ Answered, – Withdrawn) and Reply as the primary press, which opens a 1,200-character box with Send reply. "No investor has asked about this report." when there are none; Read only for an admin or advisor.
- Both sides: loading ("Reading…"), Unreadable with Try again ("This is not a claim that nobody asked."), a failed send that keeps the text and gives the Worker's sentence, phone width and dark tokens.

### Where the build departs from the canvas

- **One answer per question.** The issue's store is "body, answer, times"; the canvas's "Add a reply" on an answered card (a later reply adding to the thread) is not built. A second reply is refused 409 `already_answered`.
- **The composer opens in "Your questions"**, under the thread, for a figure as for the whole report, rather than inline under the figure row; the figure link focuses it.
- **Withdraw is reviewed inline** in the question's card (what changes, what stays, no undo), not in an overlay dialog over the reader, which is itself a dialog.
- **Phone.** The composer and the reply box are drawn in place at 390 with 44px presses and the actions side by side, not as full-screen sheets with the action pinned (GRQ25s, GRQ25r).
- **Not built:** Eadwyn's rewording for the investor and draft reply for the founder (GRQ22, GRQ23); question rows in the desk's Needs you (GRQ23a); figure chips on the founder's frozen report; "n questions on revision 1" on a corrected report (a question does stay on its revision); the reply box's "Reply as [name]" (it reads "Your reply"); new `reporting_events` values (the question row is its own record); `lintForSend` over the question and reply text (the reply goes only to the asker).
