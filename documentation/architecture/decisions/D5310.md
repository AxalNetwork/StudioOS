## D5310 — Network workspace v2: the founder overview built to the #4799 canvas, NW1, NW1.2 and NW5 (#5310)

Part 1 of #5310. It builds boards NW1 (Overview, populated), NW1.2 (first run) and NW5 (data states) of the approved Network workspace canvas (`design/incoming/network-workspace/`, D4799), the first slice D4799's **Build order** names. The owner's answers on #4799 apply:
- **A1**: Made is the delivered state.
- **B1**: needs attention means overdue follow-ups only.
- **C1**: HQ gets a read-only view of its own book, which is not in this part.

The canvas mints no route. A founder's `/network` renders `FounderNetworkDesk`, rebuilt in place, so its mount in `App.jsx`, its Worker AI rail, its header Ask, its zone pills and the D551 draft bands stay where every existing contract expects them.

### What each figure reads

Each source is its own read, so one failure never blanks the page.

**The new Worker read.** `GET /api/contacts/book/follow-ups-due` is in `routes/contact_imports.ts`, backed by `followUpsDueOf` in `services/linkedinCsvImport.ts`.
- It returns the caller's open follow-ups whose due date is before today, oldest due first. Each row has its contact, the last note's time (null when none is recorded) and the contact's sources.
- `total` counts every overdue follow-up, not the page.
- A follow-up with no due date is never overdue.
- Every statement binds the owner, and the contact must be in the owner's own book.

### States (NW5)

- **Loading**: a skeleton in the card's shape.
- **Could not be read**: a failed read says so, with Retry, "This is not zero."
  - A tile never draws 0 for a read that failed.
  - A summary sub-read that reports `recorded: false` is unreadable too, even when it carries a 0.
- **Stale**: a refresh that fails after an earlier read keeps that read. The tile adds "out of date", and a banner says what could not be refreshed and when the shown read was made.
- **Not recorded**: relationship strength (no store) and the activity feed (no read yet).
- **Empty**: a read that came back with nothing says so in the canvas's words.
- **Credits**:
  - an unread balance turns Accept off, "never accepted against an unknown balance";
  - a balance of 0 turns it off with "Accepting needs 1 credit".
- **First run (NW1.2)** shows only when the book, the three intro reads and the matches have all been read and are all empty. An unread book is not an empty one.
