## D4690 — Delivery measurements: one period basis, one retainer rule, observable evidence only, one seat state (#4690)

- **Status:** built by S24 on #4690 (Delivery audit 3/6, of 6 November 2026). S1 assigned the shared retainer rule to this issue on 2026-11-07; #4693 (Pipeline audit 4/6) calls it and does not define its own.
- **Builds on:** D208 (the stores), D395 (the board), D637 (capacity edits), D4673.
- **Migration:** none. Every rule is computed at read time; nothing new is stored.

### The question

Five Delivery figures compared things that were not the same thing, or counted a missing measurement as a finding:

1. Capacity compared hours logged **per month** (`engagement_hours.period` is `YYYY-MM`) with a cap stated **per week** (`partner_capacity.weekly_hours`). Its chips said "This week" and "Next week" while reading this and next month.
2. Pipeline · Retainers measured a quarterly retainer in its quarter (`2026-Q4`); Delivery · Health and Board looked up the *calendar month* for every retainer, so the same quarterly retainer showed a different utilisation on each page. Pipeline's MRR also summed ended retainers.
3. The Board's "Embedded / month" tile summed `engagements.price`, the accepted one-off quote, instead of the recurring contract.
4. Health rated an engagement "at risk" for "N deliverables sent and not opened", and the deliverables page and the report draft said "never opened" / "not opened". Nothing in the product records an open (`opened_at` is the client's to set and no client surface sets it), so every sent deliverable read as ignored.
5. The Board, Health and Capacity each took *one* seat row (first, or newest, or newest-not-revoked) and called that the engagement. Health decided Embedded from `seat_scope` being set, so an empty-scope seat was a Project.

### What was decided

**Capacity time basis.** Hours are per calendar month. The stated cap stays per week and is returned as stated (`cap_hours`, now with `cap_unit: "hours_per_week"`), and the comparison uses the cap scaled to the days of the month the hours cover: `weekly_hours × days_in_month ÷ 7`, rounded to two places in `periodCapHours`, so the figure on the page is the figure compared. February is 28 or 29 days, so 40 h/week is 160 h in February 2026 and 177.14 h in December. `GET /capacity` adds `period_cap_hours`, `period_days`, `period_weeks`, `hours_unit: "hours_per_month"` (and `period_cap_hours` per person), and `over_committed` compares `total_hours` with it. `?period=` must be a month (`YYYY-MM`) or the read is a 400. The chips are labelled **This month** and **Next month**; the table row reads "N of C h" with C the month's cap, and the cap form says how a week becomes a month. The artboard's own strings stay as `canvas` (provenance).

**One retainer rule, in `_partner_workspace_helpers.ts`; Delivery and Pipeline both call it.**
- `retainerProjection(row, usage, now)` is the full projection: cadence, current period, usage, retained hours, utilisation, recurring amount, monthly amount, active flag. It is the only place utilisation is computed from a retainer row.
- **Period.** `retainerPeriodFor(cadence, now)`: `YYYY-MM` for monthly, `YYYY-Qn` for quarterly. Utilisation is the hours logged in that period over the hours sold for that period. A usage row labelled with some other period is not used. The Board counts a quarterly embedded retainer's logged hours over the quarter's three months (`periodMonths`), against the quarter's retained hours.
- **Active.** `isRetainerActive`: no `ended_at` is active; a future `ended_at` is still active today; a past, current-instant or unreadable `ended_at` is ended. An ended retainer is not current recurring revenue, has no current utilisation (null with a note, never a finding), and is not counted in "unpriced".
- **Normalisation.** `monthlyAmountCents`: `amount_cents ÷ CADENCE_MONTHS[cadence]` (monthly 1, quarterly 3, semiannual 6, annual and yearly 12), rounded to the cent; an unknown cadence or a missing amount is **null**, never a guess and never 0. `currentMonthlyCents` is that for an active retainer and null otherwise. Sum the non-nulls.
- Pipeline's `GET /retainers` keeps its shape and adds `active_retainer_count` and, inside `retainer`, `active`, `monthly_amount_cents`, `current_monthly_cents`. Its `mrr_cents` is the sum of `current_monthly_cents`. **#4693 must call these exports** (`retainerProjection`, `isRetainerActive`, `monthlyAmountCents`, `currentMonthlyCents`, `retainerPeriodFor`) for its recurring-revenue figures and labels, and must not re-divide or re-filter.

**Accepted value and recurring revenue are different numbers.** Board `price` and `project_value` stay the accepted engagement value. `embedded_monthly` (dollars) is now the sum of `current_monthly_cents` over embedded engagements with a live seat, not cancelled, and an active priced retainer; `embedded_monthly_cents` is the same in cents. When none is recorded it is **null** with `embedded_monthly_note` ("Not recorded…"), and the tile draws Not recorded, not $0 and not the quote. `embedded_unpriced_count` says how many were left out. Each row carries `recurring_monthly_cents`, `retainer_cadence` and `retainer_active`.

**Read tracking is explicit, and an unavailable input is not judged.** `readTrackingFor` answers one question for the book: opens are observable only if the build is instrumented (`DELIVERABLE_READ_INSTRUMENTED`, false today) or some deliverable already carries an `opened_at`. Where they are not, `read_tracking: { available: false, reason: "Not recorded: …" }` is returned by `/deliverables`, `/health`, `/board` and the report draft; `unopened_count` and each row's `deliverables_unopened` are **null**; a deliverable's `is_unopened` is null and its new `read_state` is `not_recorded` (else `not_sent`, `opened`, `unopened`); the draft's `shipped_from_log[].opened` is null. `healthFor` takes `readTracking` and `sentDeliverables`: with reads unavailable it adds no read reason, does not move the rating, still counts "a deliverable was sent" as something recorded, and lists what it left out in the new `health_unavailable_inputs`. Every remaining Health reason is backed by an available input: milestones (due dates), blockers, and utilisation from an active retainer in its own period.

**Seat state is derived from the whole collection.** `seatStateFor(seats)` is the one definition, called by Board, Health and Capacity:
- `mode`: **embedded** if the engagement has ever had a seat row (revoked or not; the board keeps it, struck through), else **project**. Scope is optional and plays no part.
- `state`: **active** if any seat is unrevoked (partial revocation leaves it active, whichever row is newest), **revoked** if seats exist and all are revoked, **none** otherwise. Regranting adds an unrevoked row and returns it to active.
- `lead` (grant, holder, scope shown on a row) is the newest active seat, else the newest revoked one; `revoked_at` is null while any seat is active.
- Retainer cadence and ended retainers have no bearing on seat state, only on revenue and utilisation.
- Board and Health rows add `seat_state`, `active_seat_count`, `revoked_seat_count`; Health adds `mode`; `GET /capacity` adds `engagements[]` with the same fields and `has_active_seats`. Board `revoked_seats` now counts seat rows (the unit Capacity counts); `fully_revoked_engagements` is the old engagement count. Live board totals exclude only engagements whose state is revoked.

### What was not done

- Health still flags utilisation under 60% at any point in the period. Pro-rating by how much of the period has elapsed is a judgement the audit did not ask for; Pipeline uses the same threshold.
- Per-person capacity is not weighted by public holidays or part-month starts; a week is seven days of the month.
- The `Monthly amount` wording on the retainer form and the Pipeline heading are #4693's.
- No client-side surface was added to record opens; the instrument flag flips when one exists.
