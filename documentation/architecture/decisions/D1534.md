## D1534 — Image editing, phase 1: crop, focus and filters, the same everywhere; uploads described and screened (#3778)

- **Status:** phase 1 built 2026-11-02. Deck v2 B2, part of #3716.
- **Spec:** the issue's final canvas revision (S1, 2026-11-01) and S1's release note (2026-11-02): pd27 "Image crop, focus and filters" and pd4 "The lightbox".
- **Builds on:** D903 (#2682: an image block's fit and focus point), D1505 (#3756: the image screen), D1506 (#3757: upload hardening), D1604's `ImageLightbox.jsx`, `ImageToolbar.jsx` and the adapter.
- **Migration:** none.
- **Phase 2**, upscale and background removal, waits on owner decision M (third-party models through AI Gateway). Until then both stay greyed under "Coming later", on their own adapter row (`imageEditAi`). Erase, Expand and Vary stay with B8 (#3784).

### What was decided

**One rule, every renderer** (`cloudflare-worker/src/services/decks/imageLook.ts`, and the same table in `frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/imageLook.js`; a test fails when the two differ).

- **The crop** is D903's focus point (`crop.x`, `crop.y`), plus an optional zoom window (`crop.rect: { x, y, w, h }`, in percent of the block's own frame).
  - The window keeps the block's aspect, so `h` is always `w` ("Aspect locked"). The picture is never stretched.
  - `w` runs from 10 to 100. At 100 there is no window.
  - The window stays inside the frame.
  - A block shown whole (`fit: 'contain'`) has no window.
- **The filter** is one of eight presets: Original, Warm, Cool, Mono, Soft, Vivid, Fade and Brand tint.
  - Each preset is the same numbers for every renderer: saturation, brightness and contrast (as a change from 1), and grayscale.
  - Some presets add a colour wash over the picture at an alpha. Brand tint washes in the deck's brand ink, D903's `DEFAULT_INK`.
  - Original stores no key.
- **The browser** (`BlockGrid`: the editor, Present and the print page) draws the window by scaling and offsetting the cover-fitted image inside a clipped frame. It draws the filter as a CSS `filter` and the wash as an overlay.
  - The PDF and the PowerPoint download are taken from the print page, so they draw the same.
- **The editable PowerPoint** (`pptxEditable.ts`, the Google Slides export) draws:
  - the cover fit at the focus point and the window as the picture's own `<a:srcRect>`, from the image's pixel size;
  - the filter as the picture's `<a:grayscl>`, `<a:hsl>` and `<a:lum>`;
  - the wash as a filled rectangle over the picture.

**The slide model** (`slideModel.ts`). `cropOf` keeps the window, made whole by `rectOf`, and `filterOf` keeps one preset.

**Replace keeps the crop** (`imageModel.placeImage` with `keepLook`). A replaced image takes the old block's place, id, crop, filter and fit. It keeps its own alt text, else the old block's. The lightbox says "Replace image keeps the crop."

**Uploads.**
- `POST /api/decks/:id/images/upload` and `/import` need alt text: 422 `alt_required`, "Alt text is needed before this image can be inserted." Nothing is stored. The lightbox's Upload waits for it.
- Each stored upload is screened once with #3756's image screen (`uploadScreen.ts`), through `run()` on the pitch page and charged to the uploader, whose upload is the press.
- The screen flags and never blocks:
  - `image.screen` is `{ flagged: true }` with a line the toolbar shows;
  - `false` when it is clear;
  - `null` with "This upload could not be screened" when it did not answer, never "passed".

**The adapter.** `deckV2Adapter.imageEdit` is on and local, because crop and filters are the block's own fields, saved with the deck. No new route runs, so no client module was added. `imageEditAi` (upscale and background removal) stays off.

## Phase 2 (2026-11-03): background removal through AI Gateway (#3778)

Owner decision M = **B**, 2026-11-03: Bria only, through Cloudflare AI Gateway, at about $0.018 an image, quoted and charged to the founder.

### What was decided

- **The model.** `bria/remove-background` (Bria RMBG 2.0) runs on the Workers AI binding as one of AI Gateway's unified models. Cloudflare bills it per image against the account's AI Gateway credits, at the $0.018 its model page prints.
  - It is a row of `GATEWAY_IMAGE_MODELS` in `services/ai/catalogue.ts`, with the page and the date it was read.
  - It is not in `CATALOGUE`, which is Workers AI's neuron-priced list.
- **Upscale has no model.** Cloudflare lists no Bria upscale model; its upscalers are Pruna's `p-image-upscale` and Topaz. M = B is Bria only, so Upscale has no row and stays "Coming later". Choosing an upscaler is the owner's call (below).
- **Off by default.** A tool runs only while `AI_PROVIDER_BRIA` is exactly `'on'`, like the provider rows (D851). The owner turns it on after the two steps on #1008: buying AI Gateway prepaid credits, and sending Bria's 10-day sub-processor notice (DPA §6.1).
  - Until then, `GET /api/decks/images/tools` says why.
  - The quote answers `ai: false`.
  - A press is 503 `image_tool_off`.
  - Nothing is held, called or recorded.
- **Quoted before it runs, charged through the ledger.** The quote is the printed price at the member's billed multiplier. The run takes `meteredCall`'s hold, keyed by the press's `Idempotency-Key`.
  - A ledger refusal (no tokens, AI paused, a cap) is 402 and calls nothing.
  - A failed call, an answer with no `https` result, or a result that is not a PNG within 20 MB releases the hold. Nothing is charged, and one usage row records `all_models_failed`.
  - A success settles at the quote, with one `ai_usage_logs` row: task `rail_edit_image`, surface `/raise/pitch`, `est_cost_usd` 0.018, units `{ images: 1, provider: 'bria' }`.
- **The result is a child image, as an edit's is (D1544).** Bria hosts the cutout for three days. The Worker fetches it once and stores it as a proposed `deck_images` row with `parent_uid` and `image_tool = 'remove_background'` (migration 766). The parent is never changed, and Revert discards the child.
  - A child carries exactly one of `edit_op` (735) or `image_tool` (766). 735's CHECK cannot be widened in SQLite, hence a column of its own.
  - Unlike B8's generative edits, a photo of a person is not refused: removing a background does not change the person. The child keeps the parent's alt text.
- **Routes** (`routes/deck_image_edits.ts`):
  - `GET /api/decks/images/tools`;
  - `POST /api/decks/images/:uid/tools/:tool/quote`;
  - `POST /api/decks/images/:uid/tools/:tool`.
- **The toolbar** (pd27, no new panel).
  - `ImageToolbarLive` reads `deckV2Adapter.imageEditAi`, now swapped to `deckImageEditsApi.liveTools`. It is "Coming later" with the Worker's own sentence while every tool is off.
  - A tool that is on is a live button under Filters. Its press prints the quote first ("Bria RMBG 2.0 · 1 image · …"), runs only from it, keeps the child and places it in the block's own place. The block keeps its crop, filter and fit.
  - Upscale, Erase, Expand and Vary stay greyed. Erase, Expand and Vary are B8's.

### Spend (owner)

- Background removal costs $0.018 an image, charged to the founder at their billed multiplier.
- The account pays $0.018 an image from AI Gateway credits.
- The monthly figure is removals × $0.018. Nothing is spent while the switch is off.
