### 1. The Evidence part inside each Raise module (VR6)

`EvidencePart.jsx` is mounted by `CycleRoute.jsx`'s Raise `parts`, after From Mercury, on all seven modules.

| Module | Claims it shows | Pinned evidence it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Fundraising readiness | readiness (Traction proof, Team, Product-market fit), capital model | Fundraising readiness, Legal, Capital model |
| Investor narrative | narrative, newest version | Investor narrative and Pitch deck |
| Pitch deck | the deck, newest version | none |
| Data room | none | Data room |
| Cap table | founder holdings | Cap table |
| Use of funds | each section's share | Use of funds |
| Capital relationships | none | Capital relationships |

- **The newest version.** Versions are compared as numbers (`v10` after `v3`). An older version's claims stay in the Evidence view, not in the module.
- **Each claim and item opens the Evidence view's own drawer** at its address (`/raise/evidence/claim/:id`, `/item/:id`), keeping the venture. There is one drawer, not a copy per module.
- **A reader the read refuses sees no part.** That covers an advisor or a partner on the module page, since the evidence is the founders' and their admins'.
- **A failed read is Unreadable with Try again.** A block the read could not answer is Unreadable too, never empty.

### 2. The deck editor's claim chips (VR7)

`SlideEvidence` sits beside the editor's right panel at `/raise/pitch/edit`:
- **The open slide's claims:** the deck claims of this version whose `field_key` is `slide:<n>#…` (slide 1 is not slide 12). Each shows its state and its record's source, and opens its claim drawer.
- **A version the check has not read** says so. The check reads the current deck when Evidence is opened (D1264 §2).
- **The note:** "People who open this deck by link see the slides only. Evidence shows in the deal, the round call and the data room."
- **The optional "Sources on Axal" card:**
  - A preview lists one line per figure the deck states: the figure, its record's source and provider ("which differs" for a mismatch), and its date. "No record on StudioOS" when there is none. Each figure is listed once.
  - "Add a 'Sources on Axal' slide" opens a dialog first. It says the card is added at the end, that anyone with the link sees it as they see every card, and that nothing is saved until Save.
  - The card is the statement layout, titled, with its text block a list (`sourcesCard`). It is an ordinary card, so it is kept only by the editor's own Save, which says what it changes.
  - The words are built from the claims' records by code, never by Eadwyn.
- **A reader of the editor** (`readOnly`) sees the chips and cannot add the card.

### 3. A share-link deck carries no evidence

- **The Worker's share reader** (`GET /api/decks/share/:token`) reads no evidence store.
- **The share page, its viewer and the presenter** (`PitchDeckPrintPage.jsx`, `ShareViewerParts.jsx`, `PitchPresent.jsx`) import nothing from `pages/cycles/evidence/`.
- **The chips live in the editor only.**
- **The Sources on Axal card is the one exception, and it is the founder's.** When added and saved, it is a card like any other.
