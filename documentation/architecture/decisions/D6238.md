## D6238 — Fund · Calls: a list of calls and a page for one call, with correction and chase (#6238)

Under #5994, built to the Investor Fund v2 boards FV5–FV7 and FV14 (D6229, `design/incoming/Investor Fund v2 Page.dc.html`). `/funds/calls` (`InvestorFundCalls.jsx`) treats a capital call as a thing a GP opens, corrects and chases.

### What the page does

- **All calls** lists every numbered call, one row each: number, issued, due, amount, collected, outstanding and lines behind ("lines that still owe part or all of their share"). A withdrawn call reads Withdrawn in those columns, not 0. On a phone the rows are cards.
- **A call's page** is `?call=<id>` on the same route, so `?fund=` travels with it. It shows the header as corrected (purpose, due, issued on and by, residual), the call's figures, its lines (cards on a phone, with Record receipt and More…), its receipts, the comms logged on its lines, and the notices Axal issued on its lines. Receipts, comms and notices are read apart: one that fails reads Unreadable and the rest still show.
- **Correct due date / Correct purpose** and **Withdraw call** sit on the call page, behind `FundActControl` for the two new acts. Withdraw is offered only while no line has a receipt or is paid; otherwise the page says why. Before confirming, the form says how many Axal notices exist on the call and that they are not reissued or recalled.
- **Send reminders** (the zone op, previously stated as unbuilt) opens the chase on the call in view, or the current call: one Eadwyn draft per line behind (the existing `funds/calls` surface, scoped to the line), each with its own Log as sent. A reminder is logged (`fund_lp_communications`, kind `call_reminder`, linked to the line) only when the GP presses Log as sent for that line. Nothing is sent by the product.
- **New call preview:** the KYC column already existed (`kycLabel`). The issue's item is reworded to "a pending KYC is called out as a warning": the preview now carries a ⚠ sentence naming each billed LP whose account's KYC is not approved, and flags that line. An LP with no platform account is named apart ("no KYC record"), never counted as pending. Issuing is not blocked, as the board draws.
- **The intro** is "Numbered capital calls, each LP’s line, the receipts recorded against it and what was logged to each LP." The old one named a schedule builder, letters and delinquency, which do not exist. The heading stays "Capital calls": the board's "Calls and distributions" names distributions, which this page does not hold.
- The **current call** is the newest call that is not withdrawn.

### Worker

- **Migration `202611152336_6238_capital_call_amendments.sql`:** `fund_capital_call_amendments`, append-only and sealed by triggers like migration 312's tables. One row per correction (`due_date` or `purpose`, old and new value, optional reason) or withdrawal, each with `recorded_by` and `created_at`. A partial unique index allows one withdrawal per call. The 312 header stays sealed and keeps what was issued. A call's effective purpose and due date are its latest correction of each, else the header's.
- **`GET /api/funds/:id/capital-calls/:callId`:** one call, its lines, receipts, comms and notices. `requireFundGp`; a call on another fund is 404.
- **`POST …/capital-calls/:callId/correct`** `{ field, value, reason? }` and **`POST …/capital-calls/:callId/withdraw`** `{ reason? }`: `requireFundGp`, then `assertFundAct` with the new acts `capital_call.correct` and `capital_call.withdraw`. Both are **Super Admin** tier, like `capital_call.issue`, because they change what the call's LPs were told. Under D714 rule 6 the fund's own GP of record passes for their own fund. A plain Admin gets `super_admin_required`. Each write is logged with `logActivity` (`capital_call_corrected`, `capital_call_withdrawn`) and, for an admin, `auditFundAct` (`logAdminAction`). The audit row holds no purpose or reason text.
- **A due-date correction** also moves the date onto the call's lines (`capital_calls.due_date`) in the same batch, because every line reader uses the line's date (overdue, the LP's own view, the reminder). A date before the call's issue day is refused. The reminder draft (`callReminderMaterial`) reads the purpose as last corrected.
- **The withdraw rule** is checked when the call is read and again inside the write: no line has a receipt or is paid, and no management-fee line is linked to one (the D709 link cannot be undone). The withdrawal writes the amendment, with `lines_json` holding each line's share as issued, and sets every line to `status = 'withdrawn'` with `amount = 0, amount_cents = 0`, in one batch. **Why zero the line rather than only flag it:** about twenty readers sum `capital_calls` amounts (rollups, report builds, attestations, performance cash flows, Mercury matching). A zero amount keeps all of them right with no change to any of them. The issued amount is not lost: it stays on the sealed header and in the amendment's snapshot, and the call page shows each line's issued share. A receipt cannot land on a withdrawn line, because it must fit what the line still owes; the receipts route also refuses one with `call_withdrawn`. `lineState` reads `withdrawn`.
- **Notices.** The call page does not keep a second notice record. It reads `legal_notices`, the store behind `routes/fund_notices.ts`, by the source each call-line notice is built from (`call_line:<id>`), through `callNoticeSummaries` in `fundNoticeStore.ts`. The GP gets summaries only (type, reference, amount, date, superseded); the figures and the PDF stay with the admin and the LP. This answers D6229's backend item "letting the GP read the call notices Axal HQ issued on their fund" for the call page. Previewing or issuing notices for a withdrawn call is refused (`call_withdrawn`).

### Pending the owner (D6229), not built here

- (a) whether the GP runs distributions. This page draws no distributions, and the heading does not name them.
- D6229's Authenticate step (step-up) for a non-admin GP on money acts. Correct and withdraw use today's gate (a Super Admin is stepped up; a GP of record is not), the same as issue.
- (b), (c) and (d) do not touch this page.

- The admin console's Calls tab (`/admin/fund-admin`) does not show corrections or withdrawals.
