Every chart has a Table view (`aria-pressed` on the switch) and an alt text.
On a phone (SL20) the timeline and the wide tables scroll sideways inside
their card; the page does not.

### 2. States (SL15–SL18)

Each block is one of `loading` ("Reading…" and two skeleton lines),
`unreadable` (its reason, in a `role="alert"` box, with Try again), `empty`
(its own sentence) or `ready`. A block the read marks `available: false`
fails alone with the Worker's reason; the overview read failing fails its
five blocks and leaves the tiles and Needs you, which are other reads. Dark
mode is drawn on every new element. A metric is its number, Not recorded with
the Worker's reason, or Unreadable — never a zero.

### 3. Rulings made here

- **No share in the heat table.** D944 records no reached-week ledger, so a
  cell says how many passed, never "of those who reached it". The note under
  the block says so.
- **A Week 4 still running is "Not decided yet" in the funnel,** not the
  Worker's `0`. The funnel computes its steps from the cohort item, the same
  mapping as `blocks.funnel.steps`, so the switch can show any cohort without
  a second read.
- **SL6's messages and history are "Not read here yet":** no admin read
  returns the messages the Lab sent a cohort or the Lab clock's events. The
  capacity check's own result is not read either; capacity shows Settings and
  the approved count only.
- **Grace and overrides stay today's `AdminCohortTiming`** on SL6, acting on
  every cohort, until part 7 (#2754) redraws them for one cohort.
- **Places and the minimum are drawn only when Settings is read.** When it is
  not, no line is drawn and the block says why; SL6's capacity is Unreadable.
- `NOT_RECORDED` is `lib/absence.js`'s, re-exported by the model (D268).
- D937's Overview cohort table (`buildCohorts`) and its placeholders are gone;
  the calendar replaces them.
