### 1. What is drawn (AP7)

`AdminNetworkProfiles.jsx`, at `/admin/advisors-partners/deck`, reads `GET /api/admin/network-profiles?limit=500` (shown and hidden rows).

- **The line:** "This list feeds the Team & Network slide… The first 6 shown people are drawn with photo, name and role. Everyone shown counts in the network total."
- **Four tiles:** Shown, Hidden and Archived from the read's `counts`, and On the slide, the shown rows whose reach is "On the slide". A failed read makes each one Unreadable, never a zero.
- **Search and Kind:** search over name, role and company, and a Kind filter, both over the loaded rows. While either is on, rows cannot move; the list says "Clear the search and the Kind filter to reorder." No match says so, with "Clear the search".
- **The shown people, in the deck's order:**
  - each row's number, photo (or initials), name, role and company, skills, kind, reach pill and linked-account chip ("Linked to Ada's account" or "Not linked");
  - **Up** and **Down**; **drag**; and **Alt+Arrow** on a focused row. Each move is saved at once (`POST /reorder`) and announced in a polite live region: "Dee Example moved to position 7 of 9. Name only." A failed save puts the saved order back and says so.
  - the dashed line after the sixth: "Below this line, people are not drawn on the slide."
- **Hidden** and **Archived**, collapsed. Archived is its own read (`?state=archived`), made when it is opened, so a failure there costs that section only.
- **The slide preview:** the six people drawn, with photo or initials, name and the slide's role (their own, or "Advisor", as `deckAdvisorRole` prints it), and "+ N more counted · M in the network total". It follows the order as rows move.
- **Phone (AP13d):** the tiles and list stack; Up and Down are on every row.

**Reach** is D214's rule (`services/decks/deckRoster.ts`): the first six shown are on the slide; later shown people with a name, up to eight names in all, are "Name only"; the rest are "Counted only". The Worker sends each row's reach; the page works it out again after a move, with the same rule, so the pills and the preview follow the order before the next read. `admin_deck_roster_d980.test.mjs` holds the two to each other by importing the Worker's function.

### 2. The card dialog (AP8)

One dialog, one Save, for a new card or an edit:

- Name (required), Kind (Advisor, Partner, Investor; a stored `mentor` is an Advisor, D975), role, company, bio with its count to 2,000, LinkedIn URL, the twelve skills, the photo, a member-account link, and "Show on the deck".
- **Errors are inline**, under their field, and nothing is sent while one stands. The LinkedIn rule is the Worker's own: it keeps a URL only when it starts `http://` or `https://`, and drops anything else silently. So the dialog says "Use a full LinkedIn address, starting https://" before the save.
- **The photo** is chosen and previewed in the dialog. For a new card it is held until the card is created, then uploaded with the new id, so a new person gets a photo in one Save. If the card saves and the photo does not, the dialog stays open on the saved card and says so. A second Save then updates that card; it never creates it twice.
- **The member link:** a search of member accounts by name (two characters or more, eight results, active accounts only, as the Worker allows). Picking one sends `linked_user_id`; "Remove the link" sends null.
- **What the canvas draws and this does not:** AP8b draws a linked card's name, role, company and photo as read only, "from the account". No read or write does that today: the Worker stores the link and nothing else. So the card keeps its own fields, and the dialog says the link records whose account this is.

### 3. The confirmations (AP9a, AP9b)

Every change but a move opens an in-page dialog that says what it changes, from the row's ⋯ menu:

| Act | Says | Writes |
|---|---|---|
| Hide | "The next deck built leaves them out. Their card stays here under Hidden." | `PUT /:id { is_active: false }` |
| Show | "The next deck built includes them, in their saved place in the order." | `PUT /:id { is_active: true }` |
| Archive | "They leave this list and the deck. The record and photo are kept, and you can restore them from Archived." | `POST /:id/archive` |
| Restore | "They come back under Hidden, not on the deck." | `POST /:id/restore` |

Each takes one submission, keeps a failed write in its dialog with the reason and Try again, and reloads the list after it succeeds. D975's Archive copy said the profile stayed in the list; with D974's archived state it no longer does, so the copy is the canvas's.

### 4. Rulings

- **A move is saved without a dialog.** The canvas draws Up, Down and drag as direct, and the announcement says what happened. Reordering is refused while a filter is on, while the list is at its cap (500), and while a save is in flight, because a partial order would be saved as the whole one.
- **Photos use the admin photo read** (`/api/admin/network-profiles/:id/photo`, D974) in every state, so a hidden or archived row shows its photo here. The public proxy still serves shown rows only.
- **The Worker sends each row's reach, and the page works it out again** only to follow a move before the next read.
- The toast stays for "Profile created" and each act's result line. Refusals are in their dialog.
