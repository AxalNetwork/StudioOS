## D4659 — Spin-Out Lab: one graduation rule, one enrolled venture (#4659)

- **Status:** built by S15 on `agent/claude-s15/4659-lab-graduation`, stacked on D4658 and D4657. Not merged.
- **Audit:** Spin-Out Lab audit 3 of 5 (findings 6–7), revision `d85951c`.
- **Migration:** `202611071800_lab_graduation_venture.sql`. It is additive: one view, one event table, and the venture backfill. Issued credentials are not touched.

### Decision

1. **One graduation rule.** A founder graduates when every required step of weeks 1 to 4 is recorded (the catalog's `MILESTONES`, including one of week 3's pair). `incorporation_completed` is week 4's step, and nothing more. It can be recorded, behind a paid order (D585), before weeks 1–3 are met; it no longer makes a graduate on its own.
   - **Where the rule lives.** The view `spinout_lab_graduates` is the rule in SQL, and `isLabGraduate` is the same rule in code. The tests pin the view's key lists to the catalog.
   - **`graduated_at`** is when the last required step landed.
2. **Every reader uses it:**
   - certificate issuance and its backfill;
   - the issuance trigger, which fires on whichever step completes the rule, not only on incorporation;
   - `/graduates`, `/stats`, `/cohort`, `/fund-metrics` and `/shipped`;
   - the certificate console's waiting list and count;
   - the admin participant reads;
   - `/state`'s new `graduated`, which the workspace's standing now reads.
   - **Not changed:** the admin "incorporated" count measures incorporation, and keeps it.
3. **An issued credential is a recorded decision.** Its holder stays a graduate (`by_rule = 0`) until an admin revokes it. Revocation is the existing, audited route, and is the only repair for a credential issued before this rule. No snapshot is rewritten.
4. **One enrolled venture.** `spinout_lab_enrollments.project_id` names the company the enrollment is about.
   - **How it is set:** automatically only when the founder owns exactly one live project; otherwise by the founder (`POST /spinout-lab/venture`), choosing among their own live projects.
   - **Changes:** a live venture is never switched by the founder, and a deleted one asks again. Every change is a `spinout_lab_enrollment_events` row.
   - **Who reads it:** milestone evidence counts only the venture's project work. With several projects and none chosen, no project counts and the founder is asked; person-level steps still count. The same venture names the company for the certificate and the public lists, for Eadwyn's answers (which no longer land on a guessed project) and for every Lab tool page (through `pickLabProject` and `lib/labVenture.js`).
5. **Existing records.** The migration:
   - binds the venture from an issued credential's project;
   - otherwise binds the only owned project;
   - otherwise records `needs_choice`, which the founder sees as a chooser in the workspace.

   Nothing is guessed.

### Why

The audit found two things:
- the workspace, the credential, the public list and the rewards each had their own idea of "done";
- each had its own idea of which company.

One rule (the view), one identity (the enrollment's project) and one place each reader asks remove the disagreement, without rewriting what was already issued.
