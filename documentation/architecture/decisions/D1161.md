### 1. The Funds tab (FND3)

`/admin/fund-admin/funds` reads `GET /api/funds`, which lists every fund to an admin.

- **Who runs it.** The overview's funds block names the funds the console administers (`administeredFunds`: no GP of record, or an admin as GP of record). Those fill the table. The rest are run by an outside GP: they sit in a collapsed card, "Run by an outside GP · n", each with **Open fund page**. When the overview did not read, every fund stays in the table and the page says it could not tell which an outside GP runs.
- **The table:** Fund, Status, Vintage, Fund size, LPs, Carry, Management fee, GP of record, LPA (✓ On file or ◷ Pending), Slug and Open. Search reads the name, slug and GP; status filters; sort by vintage, name or size, with a fund that recorded none last. Between `sm` and a wide screen it scrolls inside its card; a phone stacks each row.
- **Never a rate the fund did not record.** A carry or fee no read gives reads "Not recorded" (titled "No carry recorded for this fund."), never 20% or 2%. A recorded 0% is a rate and reads 0.00%. A size of 0 is how the store writes "none given", so it reads Not recorded too.
- **The states.** Loading; a failed read is "Unreadable: The funds could not be read. This is not a claim that there are none." with Try again, never "No funds yet"; an empty read says "No fund is recorded yet. New fund creates the first."
- **No fund filter on Funds.** The tab lists every fund, so the header's pills are not drawn there. `?fund=` is still kept for the next tab.

### 2. New fund

The header's primary on Funds is **New fund** (`?new=1`), opening an in-page dialog:

- the fields are name, vintage year, target size ($), status, carry % and management fee %;
- the canvas's note: "An LPA draft is generated for the fund. The fund has no general partner of record until a Super Admin sets one."

**Both rates are required.** `Funds.create` stores 0.20 and 0.02 when a rate is missing, and the columns are `NOT NULL DEFAULT 0.20` / `0.02`. A blank in the form is refused ("… It is never filled in for you."), so the default is never taken silently. A percent is sent as a fraction (20 → 0.2) and dollars as integer cents. Vintage and size are sent only when given.

The act is `fund.create` (an Admin's), drawn through `FundActControl`. On success the new fund's page opens.

### 3. One fund (FND4), and the Worker's reason

`/admin/fund-admin/funds/:id` comes through `/:tab/:sub` in `App.jsx`. `viewFrom` keeps it under Funds, and `fundIdFrom` picks the id. It reads `GET /api/funds/:id`, and the overview's row for the fund gives the call figures.

**The cards:**
- **Tiles:**
  - Vintage (with the status) and Fund size;
  - Committed, Called, Received and Outstanding (with the overdue lines).
  - A fund the overview does not list says these are not read here, never zeros.
- **Economics:** carry and management fee, each Not recorded when none is recorded.
  - The Super Admin sees "A change asks a reason, then your authenticator code." and **Change**. The dialog takes both rates and a reason of 10 to 500 characters; nothing changed is refused.
  - An Admin reads "A Super Admin changes a fund’s carry or management fee." where Change would be (`fund.economics`), never a disabled button.
- **Waterfall:** "Not recorded: the distribution job has no hurdle or tiers." There is no editor.
- **General partner of record:** the account of record (Named or Not recorded) and the four document facts. With none, the card says no LPA is drafted until one is named.
- **Service providers:** the five facts of migration 163, with **Edit** (`fund.write`). It sends only what changed; a cleared fact is saved as null and reads Not recorded.
- **LPA:**
  - The version and status of the linked document.
  - With none, either "No LPA is drafted until the fund has a general partner of record." or "A draft is generated in the background."
  - **View LPA** downloads the body through `api.downloadFundLpa`.
  - **Regenerate LPA** asks first: "A new LPA draft replaces the current draft. Signed LPAs are kept." The Worker's 409 `no_gp_of_record` is said in our words.
- **Books:** whether a ledger administrator is assigned.
- **Edit fund facts** is the header's primary on this page (`?edit=facts`): name, vintage, target size and status (`fund.write`). A blank size is left as stored, since the column takes no empty value. The rates change only under Economics.

**The Worker's reason.** `PATCH /api/funds/:id` takes an optional `reason`:
- **It is optional**, so a GP's own edit and every earlier caller (the D714 suite PATCHes the carry without one) keep working.
- **When it is sent,** it must be a string of 10 to 500 characters once trimmed, else 400 `reason_invalid`.
- **It is checked before the act gate,** so a bad reason never costs the Super Admin a step-up, and a plain Admin with a bad reason is told about the reason.
- **Where it is kept:** on the `fund_admin.<act>` audit row and the `fund_updated` activity row. `FUND_PATCHABLE` never writes it to the fund.

The console always sends one with Economics.

### 4. What this part does not draw

- **The people acts' pickers.** Changing the GP of record (`fund.gp_of_record`) and assigning the ledger administrator (`fund_ledger.administrator`) choose a person. The console has no person picker yet, and guessing an account would be worse than saying so.
  - An Admin reads each act's sentence.
  - The Super Admin reads "Choosing the person is not drawn on this page yet."
  - The routes are unchanged: `PATCH /api/funds/:id { gp_user_id }` and `PUT /api/fund-ledger/:id/administrator`.
- **The administrator's name.** `GET /api/funds/:id` carries `fund_admin_user_id` only. The card says one is assigned and that the name is not read here, never a user number.
- **The Fund I bank account card** (`fund.slug_fund_i`, the canvas's "Move") and the tiles Distributed and NAV. They read elsewhere: on Distributions and NAV & performance.

### 5. Owner question: the stored default rates

The canvas says a fund with no carry or fee reads Not recorded. `vc_funds.carried_interest` and `management_fee` are `NOT NULL DEFAULT 0.20` / `0.02`, and `Funds.create` fills those defaults in. A fund created by any other path therefore stores 20% and 2% as though recorded. The console never sends a blank (§2) and draws a null as Not recorded, but it cannot tell a default from a choice.

- **A.** Keep the columns. Only the console's own creates are known to be recorded.
- **B (recommended).** A migration makes both columns nullable with no default, and `Funds.create` stops filling them in. Existing rows stay as they are, since they cannot be told apart.
