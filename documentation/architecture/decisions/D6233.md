## D6233 — Fund locked state: one surface for /funds and every zone, with what is included and Talk to Axal (#6233)

- **Status:** built by S12 (Claude Code), 2026-11-22. Builds board FV2 (IFD02, IFD02P) of `design/incoming/Investor Fund v2 Page.dc.html` (D6229; owner answer (c) A in `D6229-p2.md`). No route, migration, price or entitlement change. `App.jsx` (a frozen hub) is not edited: the routes reach the surface through the frames it already has.
- **Sources:** the S1 blocker reconciliation on #6233 (2026-11-22); D4653's approved entitlement policies and words (#6646, #7781, #7883); D6245 for `/funds/performance`.

### What was wrong

`/funds` drew a blurred outline and a card with no control. `/funds/lps`, `/calls`, `/ledger` and `/reporting` drew a different notice inside generic "Fund operations" chrome, whose only control revealed one more sentence. Neither said what the add-on includes or what to do next.

### The rule

- **One component.** `frontend/src/pages/investor/FundLocked.jsx` draws the locked state on every Fund route: `InvestorFundLanding` (`/funds`, zone Overview), `InvestorWorkspacePage` with `page="fund"` (`/funds/lps`, `/funds/calls`, `/funds/ledger`, `/funds/reporting`, and `/funds/history`, which `routes/fundHistory.jsx` wraps the same way) and `FundPerformanceShell` (`/funds/performance`). The zone's name is the heading's variable: "{zone} is part of the Institutional add-on". The zone label is the zone row's own (`shellConfig.js`), so `/funds/ledger` reads Accounting, as its pill does.
- **What is included comes from the Fund bucket, not a second catalogue.** The list is Overview plus the zones of the `requiredInvestorTier: 'institutional'` bucket, each with one line saying what that zone does on main today (`fundLockedModel.js`). The test fails if a zone has no line or a line names no zone. The catalogue's Institutional `included` list is not used: it describes research, integrations and negotiated support, which the scope excludes.
- **Talk to Axal** is a link to the Contact form, `/contact?topic=invest`, the topic the Institutional plan door already uses (`siteDoors.js`). Its caption says it opens the form and that nothing changes on the account until Axal replies. Under it: Checkout's own "Pricing agreed with Axal VC. Nothing is bought here." and D4653's availability sentence.
- **`/funds/performance` asks the licence, not the tier (D6245).** An LP below the Institutional tier still reads it, with no zone row and no lock. Only a member without the Investor licence (`<FundPerformancePage locked />`) gets the surface, reading "Performance is part of the Investor licence", the page's existing sentence, the one thing the licence gives here and Talk to Axal to `/contact?topic=licence`. The chrome, rail and refresh are not drawn, as before no read is made.
- **No preview.** FV2 draws none, and a blurred preview could only hold invented figures. The zone row is not drawn while locked (FV2), so Talk to Axal is the one focusable control. The surface has no digit in it, and the test checks that.
