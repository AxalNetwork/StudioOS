- **Status:** built by Claude Code for slot S26, 2026-11-03. Frontend only. Not deployed and not checked live.
- **Part of:** #4116 (Roster R3). It builds on R1 (#4117, D4117: the personas and `GET /api/teams/personas`).
- **Waits on:** R2 (#4118, the portraits) and R5 (#4121, the cost figure). Both are drawn as described in §5.
- **Canvas:** boards AG1–AG7 of `Agents Skills v2 · Boards.dc.html`, from draft PR #4226 (D4116). D4116 §2 and the owner's revisions on #4119 win over the canvas where they disagree (§6).
- **Migration:** none. No new route; no new client method.

### 1. The agent card (AG1)

`pages/team/AgentCard.jsx`, in three sizes, used wherever an agent is drawn by this PR (the gallery, Compare, the Add sheet's later steps and the profile):

- **Full:**
  - the portrait;
  - the name with the "AI agent" pill;
  - the pronouns, from the persona's gender;
  - the tagline in quotes;
  - "✓ On your team" or "Chosen";
  - Core values: the top 3 as chips;
  - Risk: Cautious ··· Bold, with the persona's point marked;
  - Natural fit: the two areas;
  - an optional footer.
- **Compact:** the portrait, the name, the pill, and the tagline or a given subline.
- **Chip:** a 20px portrait, the name and the spark.

The risk words are D4117's (Most cautious … Boldest), not the canvas's.

### 2. Add an AI agent (AG2–AG4)

Three steps: **Who**, **Skills**, **Model and cap**. They replace D1497's Role → Agent → Skills → Review.

- **Who.** The 16 personas as full cards.
  - **Finding one:** search by name (the tagline, goal and personality are searched too), the filters Top core value, Natural fit and Risk (Cautious 1–2, Balanced 3, Bold 4–5), and the count ("16 agents", "3 of 16 agents").
  - **On the team:** a persona live on this team is kept in the list and marked "On your team". It cannot be chosen: the radio is disabled, and the click handler refuses it too.
  - **Compare** holds up to three. Its dialog shows each persona's six value bars (top 3 highlighted), risk, natural fit and story, with "Choose [Name]".
- **Skills.** D1612's step (SK6) is kept.
  - Under it: "Coverage with these skills", the areas and levels the picks reach, or "Not recorded" with none.
  - The cost line: "With N skills · cost per run Not recorded", until R5 serves its figure.
- **Model and cap.** The platform model and read-only tools, as D1497 had them, then:
  - the area of work (§3) and the rule book it starts from;
  - the sponsor;
  - the monthly cap above $0;
  - "Everything [Name] makes is a proposal."
- **The post.** "Add [Name] to [scope]" sends `POST …/agents` with:
  - `persona_key` and `role_key`;
  - `model_id`, `tool_grants` and `monthly_cap_microusd`;
  - `skills` only when some were picked.

  The first name and surname are no longer asked: the persona is the agent's name.

### 3. The area of work, not a job title

D4117 §5 keeps `role_key` required on add, because the rule book is still keyed by role. The Worker's role names are job titles ("CTO", "CFO", "Head of People"), and D4117 §6 and D4116 §4 forbid drawing those. So:

- **The labels.** `personaModel.js` `WORK_LABEL` names each role key by the work it covers:
  - Company direction, Engineering, Product, Finance;
  - Marketing and growth, Sales and partnerships, Operations;
  - Legal, People, Research, Investor relations.

  A key outside the catalogue (a custom role) reads its own name.
- **The step.** The Model and cap step has an "Area of work" select:
  - it is suggested by `rolesFor`: the persona's own area first (D4117's `ROLE_PERSONA`), then the areas of its natural fit, then the rest;
  - an area that already has an agent on this team is not offered, since one agent per role per team still holds in the Worker;
  - natural fit only sorts, and any skill can still go to any agent.
- **Elsewhere.** The rule-books tab and the skills screens use the same labels. Their full rebuild is #4224's.

### 4. The profile (AG5)

The sheet opens on the persona:
- **The card:** the full card, with a status footer (Active or Paused, and the sponsor).
- **Story:** the story, and "Goal · …".
- **Values:** the six Axal values, with the top 3 highlighted.
  - The caption is "Fixed · part of who [Name] is", and the risk scale sits under it.
  - The labels and meanings come from the personas read's legend, shown on hover and to screen readers.
- **The rest:** the existing sections (permissions, budget, proposals and activity), then D1612's Skills card.
- **Gone:** the name fields, since the persona is the name, and "the CFO agent" in the confirm titles.

**One values component for people and agents** (owner revision of 2026-11-02 on #4119):
- `components/profile/AxalValuesBars.jsx` is extracted from `ProfileFitSection`'s "Axal behavioral values". People keep their score out of 5, under the heading "N Axal behavioral values".
- Agents use the same labels, bars and colours, plus the highlight and the caption, and no confidence state.
- A value with no score reads "Not recorded", never a zero-width bar.

### 5. What waits

- **Portraits (R2, #4118).** The card draws the agent mark, a rounded square (never a circle), with the persona's initials and the spark. Its alt text is "[Name], [tagline] (portrait not added yet)". When the PNGs land, `AgentAvatar` draws `persona.avatar`, and `avatarAlt(p, { portrait: true })` drops the suffix.
- **Cost (R5, #4121).** Every cost line reads "Not recorded", never a figure the screen made up.
- **The skill library read.** It is still off in `agentsAdapter.js`, so the Skills step says skills aren't open yet.

### 6. Where the build departs from the canvas

- **Values:** the six Axal values (D4117 §1), not the canvas's eight. There are no philosophy, Schwartz bars, outlooks or trade-offs; the canvas's unused trade-off data is not carried.
- **Alt text:** "[Name], [tagline]", not "[Name], [philosophy]".
- **The area of work** (§3) is not on the canvas. It exists because the Worker still needs a role.
- **The Skills step** keeps D1612's list and adds the coverage lines. The canvas's large labelled radar and the generalist-or-specialists explainer are #4224's, with the cost figure (R5).

### 7. Replaced role labels

`role_name` is no longer sent (D4117 §6). Every place that drew it now draws the persona's tagline, or the area of work:
- Team › AI agents rows and the phone line (`teamModel.agentRow`);
- Approvals, whose Role filter becomes an Agent filter by name;
- Settings and Working here;
- the profile's Skills card;
- the Add-skills sheet's subtitle.
