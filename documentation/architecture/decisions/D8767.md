### What the walkthrough found

- Two agents read Active, yet nothing could be assigned to them. The roster needs only `TEAM_AGENTS_ENABLED`, while tasks, runs and the engine also need `AGENT_ENGINE` and answer 404 `agent_engine_off` without it. The status pill reads the roster, not the engine, and `AgentProfileSheet.jsx` offers Change sponsor, Edit cap, Pause and Remove, with no assignment action.
- The company Work URL from #3973 returned 404: `routes/team.jsx` has no `/account/companies/:uid/work`, and Work exists only at `/projects/:id/work` (D1614).
- My tasks reported Work unavailable. `MyTasks.jsx` lists project tasks and company compliance tasks only, and with the engine off it shows `WORK_OFF`, which cites #3969.
- The pieces of a company task already exist but nothing on screen uses them. `POST /:scope/:id/tasks` takes a company scope and a person or agent assignee, and `GET /tasks/:uid/quote` and `POST /tasks/:uid/start` answer refusals in words, but no page calls `taskQuote` or `startTask`. There is no field for skill, deliverable, reviewer or per-task cap, and Request changes exists for project tasks only; company proposals go through the Approvals queue.

### What the canvas decides

- Assign a task sits on every agent row and on the profile. The profile gains "Can Orrin take work now?", built from the engine, status, sponsor, cap and skill readiness instead of "Active". Six reasons a run cannot start are drawn, each naming who resolves it (TMA01, TMA02B, TMA13B).
- Configured but unavailable (agents on the roster, engine off) says who switches the engine on, what can be prepared now and what happens to a draft, and keeps the task distinct from a support ticket and from a suggestion awaiting acceptance (TMA02, TMA13, TMA20C, TMA23).
- The Assign sheet asks for agent, skill, expected deliverable, named reviewer, due date and cap (TMA10). A human-only assignment shows seat refusals in the picker (TMA11), and a failed save keeps every answer with Try again (TMA12).
- No eligible skill (Solen, TMA03), a read-only investor seat (TMA04) and the Super Admin preview with writes off (TMA04B) each have their own board.
- A new Team › Tasks tab lists the company's tasks in five states: populated, loading, confirmed empty, Unavailable with retry, and switched off with the device draft (TMA20 to TMA20E).
- The task sheet runs Start run, running, the proposal for review, Request changes, round 2 and accepted, with owner, reviewer, deliverable, cost and next action on every step (TMA21 to TMA25).
- The way back from My tasks keeps filters and focus in the address (TMA30 to TMA31B), and My tasks says what it shows while tasks are off (TMA32).
- Phone 390 (TMA40 to TMA40J), dark (TMA50), and headings, keyboard order, exact labels and announcements (TMA60) are drawn.
