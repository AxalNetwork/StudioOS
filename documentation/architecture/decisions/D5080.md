# D5080 — Derived Raise summaries keep source uncertainty

## Decision

A summary derived from multiple reads carries its value, completeness, every contributing source state, and the keys of sources that could not answer. Source states use the platform vocabulary in `services/sourceState.ts`; the derived answer may additionally be `partial` when at least one required source answered and at least one did not.

`recorded` and `not_recorded` are answered source states. `unavailable` and `unreadable` cannot authorize a measured zero or categorical words such as “nothing”, “none”, “all”, or “every”. A refused browser read is likewise incomplete because the viewer cannot use it to prove an empty or complete result.

## Shape

```text
{
  state: recorded | partial | unreadable | not_recorded,
  value,
  complete,
  sources: [{ key, state, required? }],
  unreadable_sources: [key]
}
```

The Worker owns the contract in `derivedSummaryState.ts`; `frontend/src/lib/derivedSummary.js` is its browser projection for independently loaded cards.

## Raise application

Raise summary surfaces must gate categorical copy on `complete`. Existing cards already isolate unreadable reads in Raise Overview, Readiness, Data Room, Use of Funds, Evidence, Cap Table, and Pitch; they remain compatible. Capital relationships was the mixed-source exception: its “Needs you” card used the prospects read while asserting that every commitment had a status, even when the separate commitments read failed. It now derives the card from both reads. A failed commitments read produces a partial explanation and preserves any known prospect actions, never “Nothing needs you”.

## Compatibility

No endpoint or stored schema changes. Existing source-state DTOs remain unchanged. Callers can adopt the derived shape incrementally; a complete answered empty array remains a real empty result.
