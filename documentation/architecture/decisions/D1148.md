## D1148 — Deals › the deal page: each tab at its own address, today's reads where they exist, and "Not read here yet" where they do not (#2421)

- **Status:** decided and built by S21, 2026-10-25 (#2421, under #2711).
- **Canvas:** the owner's "Investor Deal Page", boards DL1–DL24 with lettered sub-boards. The two artifacts are `SG1gbgTjsbqyatvYDyhj1Z` (the board grid) and `7ji9JjHGCXCFHbJPjM6T3s` (the page). Both were decoded with `scripts/read-canvas.mjs` and committed as `design/incoming/Deal page · Boards.dc.html` and `Investor Deal Page.dc.html`.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `a265fb2561`. The map is `documentation/audits/DEAL_PAGE_MAP_2026-10-25.md`.
- **Migration:** none here. Part 1 takes 599 and part 2 takes 600.
- **Why:** the owner called the deal page "poor and sloppy" and asked for scoring, team, market and traction feeding the investor's deal flow through to signing and deploying capital (#2421). This decision is the integration: the page's tabs as addresses, and the blocks today's reads carry. The rest is seven slot tasks (§6).

### 1. Where it lives

- `/deals/:dealId` is Overview, and `/deals/:dealId/<tab>` is every other tab, as the canvas's `/deals/[id]/<tab>`. The new route sits beside the old one in `App.jsx`, with the same guard and the same investor workspace. No address moves.
- The tabs come from `pages/dealPageModel.js`:
  - an investor draws the canvas's eleven (DL1): Overview, Scorecard, Evidence, Team, Market, Traction, Diligence, Documents, Terms, Commitment, Notes;
  - a founder draws five (DL16): Overview, Documents, Terms, Commitment, Activity;
  - an admin draws the investor's tabs without Notes, plus Activity, Invitations and Transfers (DL17a);
  - an Operating Partner draws the same without Diligence or Invitations (DL17b; D609 keeps reference calls from them).
- A tab word the viewer's role does not draw opens Overview, never a blank page. A recorded commitment opens Commitment at its address.
