## D1336 — Product testing member flow part 1, the Worker: join and leave the tester pool, programmes for active testers, Programme closed

- **Status:** built by S17, 2026-10-27 (#3541, part 1 of the member flow, #3517 and #2318). Part 2, the frontend, is D1337 (#3542).
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `9dab27cdea`. Paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Map:** D1335 (#3543), §2 and §3: the owner's canvas, boards PT-M1 to PT-M5.
- **Migration:** none. The pool is D1042's store (migration 576); programmes and applications are D812's (516).

### 1. The member's own place in the pool (`services/testerPool.ts`)

Three routes on the `testing` app (`routes/testing.ts`), signed in. Each refuses an Admin or Super Admin with 403 `admin_account`: Admins cannot be testers (migration 576), and the canvas draws them no card.

| Route | What it does |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/testing/tester` | The member's own row: `status`, `applied_at`, `decided_at`, `reason` (HQ's sentence, or "Left by the member"), `reapply_after` (a declined member's, from `reapplyAfter`), `left_by_member`, `availability`, `consent_version` and `consent_current`. With no row, `tester: { status: null }`. Also the consent text and version, `can_apply`, and `cannot_apply: { code, message }`. |
| `POST /api/testing/tester/apply` | `{ consent_version, availability? }`. Answers 201 with the same shape. |
| `POST /api/testing/tester/leave` | The member's own row becomes `removed`. Answers the same shape. |

**Joining.**
- Consent is required. `consent_version` must be the current one, `POOL_CONSENT_VERSION` (`d1336-1`), else 400 `consent_required`. The row stores `consent_email_at` and `consent_version`.
- The consent text is the canvas's, word for word: "I agree that the firm running a programme I join, and Axal VC, may email me about it, and that my answers go to that firm. I can withdraw from a programme, or leave the tester pool, at any time." Its version reads "[v2]" on the canvas, a placeholder; the Worker's is `d1336-1`.
- `availability` is optional, at most 1,000 characters (the canvas's one field).
- Refused (409):
  - `already_applied` while the row is `applied`;
  - `already_tester` while it is `active` or `paused`;
  - `reapply_later` for a declined member before `reapply_after`, with the date in the sentence. From that date the join is taken.
  - `removed_by_hq` for a member HQ removed. The canvas's Removed card offers "Open a ticket" with Support, and no Apply. **This is the build's reading, not the issue's list; the owner can open it up (§5).**
- A member who left the pool themselves may join again.
- With no row the join inserts one. With a row (declined from its date, or left) it returns the row to `applied`, clears the decision and the reason, and keeps HQ's `note`.
- Either way one `product_tester_events` row, `applied`, with the member as actor, in the same batch. It is written only when the write stamped this join.
- HQ is told through #2390's Product testers lane, where an `applied` row already appears. Nothing more is sent.

**Leaving.**
- From `applied`, `active` or `paused`; otherwise 409 `not_in_pool`.
- The row becomes `removed`, with reason "Left by the member" and `decided_by` the member. One `product_tester_events` row, `removed`, with the member as actor.
- Picked places carry on (D1335 §3). Leaving is not HQ's removal, which ends open places (D1132).

### 2. Active testers only

`memberProgrammes` and `apply` (`services/testing.ts`) call `requireActiveTester` first. Anyone not `active` gets 403 `not_active_tester` and no list, with the sentence for their status:

| Status | Sentence |
|---|---|
| none | Open programmes are for Product testers. Apply to join the tester pool first. |
| `applied` | Open programmes are for Product testers. An Axal VC admin is deciding your application. |
| `paused` | Your place in the tester pool is paused, so you can't apply to programmes. |
| `declined` | Open programmes are for Product testers, and your application was not approved. |
| `removed` | You're no longer in the tester pool, so you can't apply to programmes. |

`GET /api/testing/summary` counts the same set, so its `open_count` is 0 for anyone not active. Withdrawing an application is not gated: a paused or departed tester still withdraws.

### 3. My applications

Each row of `GET /api/testing/applications/mine` carries three new fields:
- `finished`: picked, on a programme now `closed`;
- `decides_by`: the programme's `closes_at`;
- `withdrawn_by`: `member` or `hq`, or null when the row is not withdrawn. It is read from the newest `testing_events` row that ended it: the member's `withdrawn`, HQ's `withdrawn_by_hq`, or HQ's removal ending the place (`place_ended`, D1132). The actor is the member, or it is HQ.

### 4. Programme closed

Closing a programme now tells each picked tester on an active account: `testing_programme_closed`, "<product> is closed", linking `/inbox`. It is best-effort, as `tell` is. The firm's notice of the same type keeps no member link. `told` gains `testers`, a count.

### 5. Open for the owner

- **A member HQ removed cannot rejoin by themselves.** The issue lists the refusals as applied, active, paused and declined-before-the-date. The canvas's Removed state offers Support and no Apply, so the build refuses HQ's removals too, and lets a member who left rejoin. Say so on #3541 to change it.
- **Apply again after withdrawing.** The canvas's PT-M3 offers "Apply again" while the programme is open. `testing_applications` holds one row per member and programme, so a second apply is refused (`already_applied`). That is D812's rule and is not changed here; D1337 (#3542) should not draw the button until it is.
