## D7651 — Research › Markets: the Eadwyn summary of a market reading, to the cent, from the stored row (#7651)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-19 on #7651 (Part of #3701), the follow-up the owner asked for when answering A on #7174. Builds on D7039 (canvas, board RMP-12 and the rail's "Summaries" line), D7174 (every other amount to the cent through `lib/money.js`) and D6285 (which removed the `research/market` draft surface).
- **Route:** `GET /api/research/market-readings/:uid/summary`, a new handler in `routes/research_client_prep.ts` (the market-readings area's own file, which mounts itself). No migration. No other route changes.

### What D6285 removed, and why that shapes this build

`36c583be` (#6487, D6285) deleted the `research/market` entry from `DRAFT_SURFACES` in `routes/research.ts`. That entry gathered up to 40 of the caller's readings as lines (`metric (scope): $low – $high, from N comparables, run DATE`) and sent them, with a three-sentence instruction, to a model through the zone-draft machinery. It was removed because no mounted page could start it: its only `ZoneDraft` was in the unmounted `MarketZone.jsx`, and `research_ask_session.test.mjs` requires every allowed surface to have a mounted band. Its `money()` helper also rounded to whole dollars (`Math.round(cents / 100)`), which is the defect D7039 drew the ◇ against.

Bringing the model draft back would need a mounted band, a rail option per licence in `lib/railOptions/*.js` and the Worker's job and page maps (D7174 lists them), and would call a model over pricing records. RMP-12 does not draw a model's prose: it draws one line, "Investor-update edit · per update (One investor update…): **$99.50 – $99.99**, from 7 comparables, run 2026-11-16", which is exactly the evidence line the old gather wrote, to the cent. So this build writes that line **from the stored row, with no model call**, and keeps the rail's stated limit ("Nothing here calls a model to price a service line") true.

### What was built

- **Worker.** `services/marketReadingRead.ts` `readingSummary(row)` over the reading joined to its own service line, the same join `GET /market-readings/:uid` uses. It returns `metric`, `scope` (null when blank), `service_line`, `range` (`{ status: 'recorded', low_cents, high_cents, low_usd, high_usd }`, or `{ status: 'not_recorded', reason }` when the stored range does not read low to high), `comparable_count`, `ran_at`, `catalog_price` (`recorded` with `cents` and `usd`, `not_recorded` for an unpriced service line, `not_applicable` without one), `line` (as RMP-12 draws it, null without a range) and `basis: 'stored_reading'`. Amounts are cents in and two-decimal text out through `toMajorString` and `formatMinor` from `lib/money.js`; nothing divides by 100 or rounds. 99.50 is "99.50", 4500.00 is "4500.00", and the line writes $4,500.00 – $6,000.00.
- **Gate.** `requireAuth`, then an investor-only account (`holdsOnlyLicence(user, 'investor')`) is refused with 403 `readings_hidden_for_investors` before anything is read (D6273 Q2, as `readingsHiddenFor` hides readings on the page). A reading that is not the caller's is 404 `not_found`, the same as a missing one (`WHERE r.uid = ? AND r.owner_user_id = ?`). Both refusals go through `refuse()`.
- **Client.** `frontend/src/lib/api/researchMarketReadings.js` (`researchMarketReadingsApi.summary(uid)`), because `api.js` is a frozen hub (D5615).
- **Page.** `MarketReading.jsx` reads the summary beside the reading, on its own record gate, and never for an investor. `SummaryLine` draws "Eadwyn summary of this reading" with "From the stored reading only. No model is called and nothing is rounded.", then the line, with the range in mono and kept on one line at 390, and the catalog price beside it on a service line ("Catalog price $120.00 · your offer, beside the range and never added into it.", or Catalog price Not recorded). States: loading (a one-line skeleton, `aria-busy`), Unavailable with Try again (`DataState`), Not recorded. `marketReadingRead.js` `summaryView` reads the Worker's two-decimal text back through `parseMinor` and draws a figure only when it equals the cents sent beside it, so a rounded text can never be shown.
- **Rail.** The "Summaries" line ("Summaries: when Eadwyn summarises this reading it quotes $99.50 – $99.99, to the cent.") joins the rail's `note`, the one page-specific sentence `WorkerRail` still draws; nothing is added while the summary loads.
- **Dark and phone.** The summary sits on the page's violet band skin with its dark pair; every muted line carries `dark:text-gray-400`; the line wraps and the range does not split at 390.

### Departures from the canvas

| Canvas | Built | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RMP-12 "Eadwyn summary of your readings", under the CSV export | "Eadwyn summary of this reading" on `/research/markets/:uid`, under "What this reading says" | The issue puts it on the reading page; one reading's line is what the page can state from its own row. A summary across all readings is not built (below). |
| The summary as Eadwyn's (model) output, ◇ on `research.ts` | Computed from the stored row; no model call | D6285 removed the model surface for want of a mounted band; restoring it needs rail-option and job-map rows outside this issue, and RMP-12 draws only the stored facts. |
| Rail "Summaries" as its own labelled block | A sentence appended to the rail note | `WorkerRail` no longer draws per-page blocks (`RETIRED_RAIL_PROPS`: coverage, unavailable); `note` is the one it draws, and only while AI is on. |
| Failed read "Unavailable" / "Retry" | "Unavailable" chip with "Try again" | House rule (D7174); the canvas handoff accepts either. |
| Scope truncated with an ellipsis | Full scope, wrapped | Truncating hides part of a stored fact; the line wraps instead. |
