## D4490 — Distribution Ads plans and recorded spend (#4490)

Ads are manual plans. Each platform charges the founder's own account; Axal never holds ad money. The banner stays present while loading, empty and unreadable.

The mounted Worker read `/api/grow/ads?project=&campaign=` checks venture access before looking up an unarchived campaign scoped to that venture. It reads existing campaign/channel links and budgets, and returns no creatives. Creative reads and writes use D4483's existing campaign endpoints and table; there is no second campaign or creative store and no migration.

A paid channel is one with an explicit daily/lifetime advertising budget, or a known paid-platform UTM source (google, google_ads, google_search, youtube, meta, facebook, instagram, x, twitter, linkedin_ads). The store has no paid-channel flag; unrelated channels are omitted. This source classification does not imply a connected account.

Within campaign date months, measured Mercury spend wins over manual spend for a channel-month. Missing months remain absent from the API and chart blank rows contain no values and draw no bars. A recorded zero remains a recorded zero. Mercury figures retain the existing `isTeamViewer` gate; non-team readers see manual entries only. Failed spend reads are Unreadable, never partial totals.

Seven-day leads come from customer contacts' UTM source, using real timestamp comparisons and ignoring malformed attribution. Spend is only monthly: no seven-day allocation is invented, so seven-day spend and cost per lead remain null with a reason. A monthly lifetime total can flag a reached lifetime budget, but monthly data cannot establish a reached daily limit. Alerts never claim that an ad was paused. Planned bars show the lifetime amount for comparison, explicitly labelled rather than implying a monthly allocation. Daily plans omit a monthly planned bar.

Google Ads, YouTube through Google Ads, Meta and X remain Not offered yet until owner question C on #3722 is answered. Clicks, estimated reach and ad suspension have no source. No create, pause, rule, connector or ad-money API is added. Later copy states what is unavailable.

Proposed environment names for future access only: `GOOGLE_ADS_DEVELOPER_TOKEN`, `GOOGLE_ADS_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_ADS_CLIENT_SECRET`, `META_APP_ID`, `META_APP_SECRET`, `X_CLIENT_ID`, `X_CLIENT_SECRET`. No values or new bindings are introduced.
