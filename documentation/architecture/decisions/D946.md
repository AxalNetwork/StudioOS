- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `fd02f60e59`, with D960 (#2793, merged as #2842), which made `GET /api/admin/spinout-participants` answer a failure truthfully. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Migration:** none.
- **Why:** SL7's table and SL8's drawer need, for each founder:
  - the week and day;
  - progress;
  - this week's outcome and its deadline;
  - the missing items;

  They also need filters and sorts that are not done in the browser. Today's `GET /api/admin/spinout-participants` returns 400 rows with milestones and nothing else. ### 1. Who is a participant

`LAB_PARTICIPANT_WHERE` (`services/labParticipants.ts`) is the rule `GET /api/admin/spinout-participants` has always used:
- admitted (`user_spinout_flags.spinout_lab_admitted`);
- or active (`users.spinout_lab_active`);
- or graduated (the `incorporation_completed` milestone).

The participants read and both Lab-only filters use it, so "Lab ventures" on Formation are the founders the Participants tab lists.

### 2. `GET /api/admin/cohort/participants`

It takes `requireAdmin` and is read-only. `routes/admin_lab_participants.ts` serves it, mounted by `admin_cohort.ts` beside D944's reads, and `services/labParticipants.ts` computes it.

- **Query:**
  - `cohort` (`YYYY-MM`) and `status` (`active`, `admitted` or `graduated`);
  - `week` (1–4) and `this_week` (§3);
  - `incorporated` (`yes` or `no`), and `needs=1` for SL7's "Needs a decision";
  - `q`, which searches the founder's name and the company;
  - `sort`: `days_left` (fewest first), `progress` (least done first), `cohort` (newest first) or `name`. Ties break by name;
  - `limit` (1–100, 25 by default) and `offset`.

  Any other value is a 400 with its own code (`bad_cohort`, `bad_week`, `bad_sort` and so on), never a quietly wider list. Nothing in the query reaches SQL.
- **Filtering, sorting and paging all happen in the Worker,** over every participant. The read considers at most 5,000 participants and says `truncated` when it reaches that.
- **Each row:**
  - the founder: `uid`, `name`, `company`, `sector`;
  - their place: `status`, `cohort`, `started_at`;
  - the clock: `week`, `day` of 28, `days_left`, `paused`;
  - `progress: { done, required }`;
  - the outcome: `this_week`, `failed_week`, `deadline_at`, `grace_until`, `missing`;
  - `incorporated` and `moderation`.
- **The answer also carries:**
  - `total`;
  - `standing`: the counts by outcome over the rows every other filter keeps, plus `everyone` and `needs_decision`. That is SL7's "Where [cohort] stands" and its two toggles;
  - `definitions`.
- **No email address leaves the read.** The canvas names a founder by name.

### 3. This week's outcome, one rule with the founder's workspace

- **The founder's week is their gate.** Their cycle is the one whose window holds their start, as `getCohortGate` finds it. The week, the deadline, grace and the freeze come from `gateFromRows`. That is `getCohortGate`'s arithmetic, extracted to a pure function in `services/cohortTiming.ts`, and `getCohortGate` now calls it. So the admin row and the founder's own workspace cannot disagree about a week, and a test holds them equal for every founder in the fixture.
- **The outcome**, first match wins:
  - `graduated`: the incorporation milestone is recorded;
  - `failed`: a week failed and the founder is paused at it;
  - `grace`: a week is in grace, until `grace_until`;
  - `on_track` or `at_risk`: the current week's required items are all done, or not yet. This is `evaluateWeekOutcome` with now as the cutoff, the cohort review's `at_risk` rule;
  - `not_in_cohort`: not active, or a start no cycle holds (a legacy founder).
- **"Needs a decision"** is `at_risk`, `grace` and `failed`.
- **Missing items** are the current week's, as milestone keys. `any_of:a|b` means one of them. The Worker has no labels for the keys; the page names them, as it does for the cohort review.
- **Progress** counts required items done across the four weeks, of `REQUIRED_ITEMS`, which is 10 by today's catalog. An "any of" counts once. The canvas's "[9] / [27]" is a sample.
- **Days and deadline.** The day and days left count from `spinout_lab_started_at` for an active founder only. The deadline is the deadline of the week the founder is held at, which reads at 00:00 ET, inclusive (D953).
- **Moderation** is the founder's newest open case, `under_review`, `suspended` or `ejected` with no `resolved_at`, as `{ state, since }`, or `null` for none.

### 4. A read that failed says so

- **The participants list failing** is a 503 `lab_participants_unreadable`, as D960 made the old route answer.
- **Each inner read** (the cohort calendar, the week statuses, the milestones and the moderation cases) that fails nulls only the fields it feeds, and the answer names it in `unreadable`.
  - Moderation unread is `undefined`, never `null`, because `null` means no case.
  - Milestones unread leaves a founder who is not active with no `status`, because admitted and graduated cannot be told apart.
- **A filter or a sort on an unread field** is refused with a 503, rather than answered from nothing:
  - `this_week`, `needs` and `week` when the calendar, the week statuses or the milestones failed;
  - `progress` when the milestones failed.

### 5. `GET /api/admin/cohort/participants/:uid`: SL8's drawer

It returns:
- the row;
- each week's window, unlocked state, status and its required items, done or not;
- the tools unlocked of every tool the catalog unlocks (20 today; the canvas's "[8] of [17]" is a sample);
- the open case;
- the history of week decisions, newest first. Each is by `clock` or `admin`, from `decided_by`, never a name.

A uid that is not a participant is 404 `not_a_participant`.

### 6. The Lab-only filter on 83(b) and formation (SL10)

- **The two reads.** `GET /api/admin/83b-review?scope=lab|all` and `GET /api/admin/incorporations?scope=lab|all`.
  - `all`, or no scope, is today's list, unchanged.
  - `lab` keeps only ventures whose founder is a participant: the project's founder for 83(b), and the order's user or its project's founder for formation.
  - Anything else is 400 `bad_scope`.
  - Each answer echoes its `scope`.
- **How they filter.** Each scope is its own fixed query string, `LAB_STARTUPS_WITH_TRACKERS` and `LAB_PAID_ORDERS`, so no SQL is assembled from the request.

### 7. The support-sessions client (SL14s)

The clients are:

- `section83bReviewApi.adminList(scope)` and `adminIncorporationsApi.list(scope)`, where today's callers, which pass nothing, read every startup as before.
