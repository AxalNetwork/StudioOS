### V1 (desktop)

The page runs top to bottom:
- the header: tabs, the PMF chip and Record a session;
- "Trying to learn:";
- the next action and its meter;
- Evidence;
- Needs attention beside Recent changes (7/5);
- Active hypotheses.

- **One primary.** The next action is `ValidateLead` exactly as it was: #4589's policy, #5999's meter beside it, D5997's objective above it. Record a session moves from the header's primary button to a secondary one beside the tabs (`data-testid="cycle-action"` is kept), so the page has one primary. The canvas draws it as a plain button.
- **Every count names its population and window.** Each Evidence figure draws its value ("4", "1 of 2"), the read's `detail`, and "Counts <population> · <window>" from the read. A figure with no population is Not recorded with the read's reason, never 0. A counted zero stays 0.
- **Needs attention** shows the read's items (at most four) with V13's tag and the read's own action and href, and "N of M · most useful first" when the read counted more.
- **Active hypotheses** show:
  - the code, the claim and the sources line;
  - the computed status in V7's words (Testing, Mixed, Supported, Refuted, Not started, Inconclusive);
  - + Supports / − Contradicts with their counts, or No evidence linked;
  - the newest experiment, or Result needed;
  - one action. A writer's action is Record the result, Link evidence or Review evidence; a reader's is always Open.
- **The module cards are gone.** The header's tabs are the module navigation, as on Build (D6028).

### V13 (states)

- Each part fails on its own. The shared Unavailable (`DataState`, #5129) carries the read's reason and one Retry, which re-reads the overview. The others draw as they are.
- A part an older read does not send draws nothing.
- **Loading:** a skeleton, announced as "Loading Validate overview".
- **Refused:** the Worker's sentence.
- **Unreadable read:** Unavailable with Retry.
- **Empty venture:** when every module card (Verdict aside) is `empty`, one Still to establish list draws under the next action. Each card's own name and reason, Not recorded, and its next link. It replaces the evidence, attention and hypothesis sections. A card that failed means the venture is not shown as empty.
- **Read only:** a read whose `can_write` is `false` (a partner or advisor) draws the Locked tag and "Read only". A read-only page (support, a narrative admin) draws the same and no Record a session; its read is drawn as a reader's.

### Departures from the canvas

- **How these are counted:** no separate link. The definitions are on each figure instead, which the issue's "every count shows its population and window" asks for.
- **The 768 module selector:** the canvas draws "Module: Overview ▾". Below 1024px the page keeps the phone root's module list, the one navigation every cycle shares there (D703).
- **The decision line in the header** ("Decision · not recorded"): not drawn. The overview read carries no verdict data (D5996 § Decisions); the PMF chip stays.
- **The Eadwyn rail open (VA01b):** the rail is the page shell's (D5077), not this page's.
- **VA01b's "record a decision anyway":** drawn when the Worker's focus sends it as a secondary link, as `ValidateNextAction` already does. No second policy.
