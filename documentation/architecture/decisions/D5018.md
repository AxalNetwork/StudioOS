### 1. Two stores, separate from the grant terms

- `program_pricing_defaults` holds each program's global reference price. It is versioned: publishing names the version
  read (`expected_version`) and retires the active version in the same batch. A partial unique index allows one active
  version per program.
  - A trigger freezes a published version's figures; only active → retired may move. Nothing is ever deleted.
  - Each version holds a base annual fee, an optional base initial fee, and optional floor and ceiling multipliers.
  - An optional rounding increment can be set; without one, the pricing model's own increment (`fee_rounding_cents`) applies.
  - A base of 0 is refused: a price is never "free" by default.
- `program_pricing_decisions` is append-only. Each row records:
  - the reference-price version, the licence snapshot (D5017), its model version and the country index;
  - the base, the recommended and accepted fees, and the trace before rounding;
  - `accepted` or `overridden`, the override reason and the write's reason;
  - the effective period;
  - the grant terms revision the decision produced;
  - the actor.

  A per-program `sequence`, computed inside the insert, orders a licence's history.

`program_grant_defaults` (D982) and the grant's own terms are not repurposed. The grant's `fee_cents`/`currency` stay
its accepted price, so program enforcement (D982/D984), the schedule (D1120/D4805) and the branch copy are unchanged.

### 2. The calculation

`recommended = base × country index`. The result is clamped to `[base × floor, base × ceiling]` when those are set,
then rounded to the program's increment, or the model's when the program sets none. The arithmetic is exact integer
micro-cents (the D5015 helpers), and the value before rounding is kept in `trace_json`.

The index is the licence's own: the country index of its D5017 licence snapshot. For a basket, it is the indexes of
the frozen country snapshots, weighted by the basis' weights. A licence and its programs are therefore priced from the
same immutable snapshot. A grant write may name the snapshot (`pricing.licence_snapshot_uid`); otherwise it takes the
licence's latest snapshot. A newer recommendation exists only when HQ refreshes it.

There is no recommendation when any of these holds:

- there is no reference price;
- there is no licence snapshot;
- a country index is missing;
- the reference price's currency differs from the model's.

Each case says why. The figure reads **Not recorded**, never 0.

### 3. Setting the price is a grant write

The grant (`POST …/licences/:uid/:program`) and change-terms (`PATCH`) writes accept `pricing`:
`{ licence_snapshot_uid?, accepted_annual_fee_cents?, accepted_initial_fee_cents?, override_reason?, effective_from?, effective_to? }`.

- **Accept the recommendation:** `pricing` without an accepted fee. The grant's `fee_cents` and `currency` become the
  recommended ones.
- **Override:** an accepted fee that differs, or any fee where there is no recommendation. This needs
  `override_reason`; without it the answer is `400 override_reason_required`, and nothing is granted.
- A fee in both `terms` and `pricing` is refused (`fee_from_pricing`).
- **No `pricing` at all.** A write that sets or changes `fee_cents`/`currency` still records a decision. It is
  `overridden`, and its reason is the write's own (at least 10 characters, D982). An unchanged fee records nothing.
  So the history holds every price a grant carried, and the existing grant flow needs no change.

The decision insert sits in the grant write's own batch, after its `decisionGuard` (D4805). It lands only when the
grant write wins, and a stale write leaves neither.

### 4. Freeze

- A market refresh or a newer licence snapshot never writes a grant or a decision; the test refreshes after granting.
- Pause and resume do not reprice: they never touch `fee_cents`, and they record no decision.
- A change of terms that leaves the fee alone records no decision.
- Renewal or a change of price is a new decision with the next `sequence`.
- The branch copy still carries only the grant's accepted `fee_cents`, `currency` and its dates (D984), not the model.

### 5. Reads and export (Super Admin)

- `GET /api/admin/program-pricing/defaults`: every program's active and retired versions, with
  "Not recorded … not free" for a program that has no price.
- `POST /api/admin/program-pricing/defaults/:program`: publish the next version. Needs the write bar, a reason and
  `expected_version`.
- `GET /api/admin/program-pricing/decisions[.csv]?licence=&program=`: the history, as JSON or a CSV export.
- `GET /api/admin/licences/:uid/program-pricing`: per program, the base, the country index and model version, the
  recommendation, the accepted price with an override flag and period, and the grant.

`/api/admin/program-pricing` joins the recovery cool-off prefixes, beside `/api/admin/licence-pricing` (D5014).

### 6. HQ screens

- **Studio view** (`/admin/licences/programs/studio/:uid`): a Program pricing table showing base, country-adjusted
  price, accepted price, an Override chip with its reason, and the period. It downloads the pricing history as CSV.
- **Default terms tab:** Reference prices, with Publish a new version. It shows "Not recorded" for a program with none.
- **Grant sheet:** a Price block with three choices: keep the fee in the terms, accept the recommended price, or
  override with a reason. Dates are optional. The sheet keeps every other grant term as it is.
