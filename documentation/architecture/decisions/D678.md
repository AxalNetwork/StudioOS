### The decisions

- **Methodology reads nothing live.** The page states a method: no figure, person, date or price is on it. Its words are the owner's, in `lib/siteMethod.js`, and a test holds every string there to the canvas.
- **The blueprints scroll inside their frames, and the page never does.**
  - Below 900px each image keeps the canvas's 1000px width inside `.ax-m-bp-scroll`, and "Scroll sideways to read the blueprint" shows under Fig. 01, as on the canvas.
  - The scroll box is focusable and named after its figure, so a keyboard can pan it.
  - Every flexible grid track is `minmax(0, …)`, so a wide figure cannot widen its column, and the page clips sideways overflow (`.ax-m { overflow-x: clip }`).
  - Each image carries its file's width and height, so the page holds its place while it loads.
- **The dashed return path stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`,** and so do the hover lifts.
- **The canvas's faces are not set.** Its Space Grotesk labels take the site's text face, and its Bricolage display lines that are not headings use `.ax-display` (`app_typography.test.mjs`). The SVG's text inherits.
- **The Intelligence page ships only the claims the code makes true.** `lib/siteIntelligence.js` holds the ledger (`INTEL_CLAIMS`): each claim the canvas makes, whether it shipped, and either the file and text that make it true or what the code does instead.
- **The model table reads the registry.**
  - It reads `GET /api/public/ai-models` and names no model in its source.
  - While the read is out, after it fails, or when it returns nothing, one sentence stands where the table would be.
  - The canvas's nine rows (GLM, Kimi, Gemma, GPT-OSS and others) are not models the router runs, so none ships.
  - The canvas's "Why" column has no source and is not drawn.
- **The Models heading is the one line that is not the canvas's.**
  - The canvas's "Pick the model behind each feature page." is false as written, so its verb is dropped.
  - The page says "The model behind each feature.", which the table shows.
  - **The owner may replace it.**
- **The registry endpoint caches like `/stats`, without the KV step.**
  - `/stats` caches in KV because its origin is D1.
  - This one's origin is a constant in the bundle, so it keeps a per-isolate copy and sends `Cache-Control: public, max-age=300, s-maxage=300`, the five minutes `/stats` keeps.
  - It is on the anonymous-read allowlist in `index.ts`, so it never waits on a role-schema repair.
- **The Lab took one difference.**
  - The board is the `Spin-Out Lab · Landing` canvas the live page was built to (D385), redrawn at 1440px with annotations.
  - Its hero picture is the same image the live page draws from `/axal-vc-future.png`, as WebP (193 KB against 688 KB), with a second scrim. Both were taken.
  - Every other difference is one D385 settled the other way, and they stay as they are:
    - per-track gates;
    - the "Yours" tag;
    - founder-to-founder asks;
    - the sample cohort;
    - a `?track=` on the apply link that the register page would drop.
  - The cohort name, start, end and deadline still come from the cohort record (`useCohortRecord`, D141, D385), and the places from `useCohortPlaces`.

### What was dropped, and why

Each claim below is in `INTEL_CLAIMS` with its reason. None was softened.

- **The Beta badge** and "the beta can be withdrawn": nothing in the code names or versions a release.
- **The intro line.** Two of its three parts are false; see the next two items.
- **01 · Backlog.**
  - Studio's Eadwyn panel does have Proposed, Pending and Completed buckets (`AdvisorProgressWidget`).
  - But they hold Eadwyn's interview questions from `GET /api/advisor/queue`, not open work from every feature.
  - They are ranked by the state machine (focus page, Lab week, milestones, recent activity), not oldest and most blocking first.
- **The rail paragraph and "Pick the model behind each feature page".**
  - The rail lets a member pick a model only for Validate's two fill bands (`pick: true` in `ui/railModels.js`).
  - The rail sits on the workspace pages, not on every feature page.
  - "Nothing swaps silently" could not be shown: the router falls back on a failure and records it, but not every surface was shown to say so.
- **"A safety screen (Llama Guard) runs before any answer is shown."**
  - Llama Guard screens the member's message on the Eadwyn chat (`classifyInput` in `routes/advisor.ts`, `ai.ts` and `eadwyn.ts`).
  - The feature pages' runs are not screened: Validate's fills, Research Ask and interview structuring.
  - The screen reads the question, not the answer.
- **03 · Language.** Nothing adapts Eadwyn's wording to a member's language, terms or level of detail. Voice notes are transcribed, but the sentence pairs that with read-back aloud, and there is no speech output.
- **04 · Persona.**
  - The archetype card (`/studio/archetype`, D602, D619) is real.
  - The section's other claims were not matched to code in this task: values feeding values alignment, activity raising or lowering confidence, each part saying what would make it surer, and a persona the member can correct.
  - An unproven claim does not ship. **Restore it once each sentence is checked.**
- **Three Research sentences:**
  - "Use it to size a market…": market sizing lives on the project, not in Research;
  - "Every figure carries its source and date": a fund directory row has no date;
  - "Eadwyn cites the rows it read": Ask cites the member's documents.
- **Two research card sentences:**
  - Markets' "Sector and stage data with sources and run dates": not shown to hold for stage;
  - Companies' "Compare positioning and pricing side by side": a competitor analysis has no pricing field.
- **06 · Architecture.** Every model runs on Workers AI. Nothing trains locally or merges updates.
- **The canvas's sample rows:** the five profiles, the task list, the rail mock-up, the French exchange, the persona chips and the five layers.
