A read that answers after another company was opened, or after the sheet closed, is dropped. The investor-requests list beside it follows the same rule.
2. **Value-add: a Deliver or Withdraw that saved said nothing.** The confirmation closed and the ledger was read again, but no message said the move had been recorded. A screen-reader user heard nothing. The status region that part 3 put in the page now carries the outcome, naming the company and the entry: "Northwind Labs …: "Intro to two climate-fund partners" is marked delivered." (or "is withdrawn", "is started", "is cancelled"). The row's new state still comes from the read. A saved notice does not mark the row as contested.
3. **Reports: an Ask with no answer was reported as refused.** A 504, a gateway error or a dropped connection showed "Not sent." and offered the same confirm again. The ask may have been stored and the founders told, so a second press could ask twice. The Review now uses `lib/mutationOutcome.js`'s rule (D4706: unknown is not refused). It says that no answer came back and that the request for that company and period may or may not have been recorded. Its only action is **Check the period**, which closes the sheet and reads the company again. The period's own record then says whether you asked, and focus lands on the obligation card. Waive follows the same path.

This covers a 503 that carries the Worker's own `reporting_unreadable` code. `routes/reporting.ts` answers that from any thrown error, including the read-back after the ask was already stored and the founders told. The node suite `portfolio_report_ask_d4817` had used that 503 as its refused-then-retried example. Its test 4 now uses the route's 403 `reporting_refused`, a real refusal. A new test 4b pins the 503 as "may or may not".
4. **Computed contrast: the AI draft band's button was 3.2:1.** White 11px text on `amber-600` in `workspaces/ZoneDraft.jsx` appears on every Portfolio board, in light and dark. The amber accent now uses `amber-800`, which gives 7.1:1, and hovers to `amber-900`. Not `amber-700`: that is the partner zones' one filled primary, and a first push on `amber-700` made Offers' Proof zone draw two primaries (`offers_interaction_4844` caught it). The band is still amber. This is a shared component, so every page with an amber ZoneDraft band changes the same way. No committed screenshot baseline shows one (the baselines are the editor's).
5. **Computed contrast: "Also in Portfolio".** In `workspaces/AlsoIn.jsx`, the label was `axal-faint` on white (2.6:1). It is now `axal-muted`. axe's `color-contrast` rule had passed every board with 4 and 5 present. Part 2's and part 3's runs reported no serious violation there, so the computed check found what axe did not.

**Harness fix, not a product change:** part 3's "Mark delivered … focus is not dropped" check read focus once, straight after the conflict notice. ### What the computed contrast check proves, and what it does not

**What it does.** For every visible element in the Portfolio frame that has text of its own (a sheet or a confirmation, where named), it reads the browser's computed `color`. A 1px canvas resolves the colour to sRGB, whatever its syntax. The check composites the element's and its ancestors' background colours, each translucent layer over the next, down to the first opaque one. It applies the element's and its ancestors' `opacity` to the text. The ratio is WCAG's: relative luminance with the sRGB transfer function, `(L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05)`. Text needs 4.5:1, or 3:1 at 24px, or at 18.66px bold. Disabled controls are exempt, as 1.4.3 allows. The keyboard focus ring of the local navigation is measured against the background behind the control and needs 3:1 (1.4.11). An element over a background image or gradient is listed and not passed. None was found on these pages.

**What it proves.** In the states the fixture draws, at 1440 wide, every measured text in those frames meets AA in both themes. It replaces the "measure by hand" part of D4818's contrast item for those states.

**What it does not prove:**
- Hover, active and visited colours, and focus rings other than the local navigation's.
- States the fixture does not draw: errors on other boards, every chip tone, and the phone layout's own colours.
- Text drawn over something that is not its DOM ancestor: an absolutely positioned overlay, `mix-blend-mode`, or a backdrop filter.
- Text shadows and anti-aliasing.
- Non-text contrast of borders, icons and chart marks, beyond the one focus ring.
- The app shell outside the frame.

It is a measurement of computed styles, not of pixels, and it is not a conformance claim on its own.

### Mutation check

Each new assertion was checked both ways. The product code was broken, the SPA rebuilt and only that check run; then the file was restored from a backup copy and its SHA-256 compared. Twelve mutations were run, and all twelve were caught:

- **The money sheet's latest-read guard** (fix 1): the late Northwind read replaced the open sheet.
- **The investor mark-history drawer's own guard**, already there: Northwind painted into Lumen's sheet.
- **The saved Deliver or Withdraw notice** (fix 2), in two ways:
  - removed: both moves failed;
  - drawn in a status region added with its text: "the saved move" failed, `before: false`.
- **The delivery-unknown branch** (fix 3), in three ways:
  - removed: both variants read as a refusal;
  - its footer: the confirm was offered again;
  - its re-read on close: the period never read as asked.

  In the node suite, removing the branch fails test 4b alone.
- **The ZoneDraft button**, back to `amber-600` (fix 4).
- **"Also in"**, back to `axal-faint`, and its dark link back to `violet-600` (fix 5).
- **Part 2's dark primary ink:** white again on the dark violet button. The computed check caught it on the company Reports' Ask button.
- **Without the company workspace's `key={uid}`**, the late-answer check fails, but on the address change: Lumen is never read. It does not fail on a late paint. With the key, `usePortfolioReads`' own sequence guard never gets to act, because the workspace unmounts first.

A first version of the dark-ink mutation also replaced the dark theme's `--pf-bg`. It ran against Positions, whose only primary button is disabled there and so exempt, and it survived. It was rewritten to break the one rule, and it was run where an enabled primary is drawn.
