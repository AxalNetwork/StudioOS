### 1. Migration 579: four stores

**`reporting_duties`.** It is the one table #2424 and #2427 share. CAPITAL names it and its close creates the rows. #2424 never created it, so this part adds it, with every column #2427 lists:
- the closing, the closing line (id and uid), the venture and the holder;
- the kind: `monthly_financials`, `quarterly_kpi`, `quarterly_update`, `annual_audited` or `annual_budget`;
- the cadence and the due rule: after the period ends, or before its year starts, for a budget;
- `due_days`, an integer from 0 to 366;
- the required figures and documents, and the recipients, each a JSON array;
- the source: the envelope, the document, the clause, `body_sha256`, and whether it came from a signed envelope or the closing's template;
- `defaulted_json`: the terms the paper left blank;
- the start date and a status.

One duty per line and kind (a unique index).

**`reporting_periods`.** It holds:
- `period_label` (`2026-10`, `2026-Q3`, `FY2026`, `B2027` for a budget), `period_start`, `period_end` and `due_on`;
- the founders' `expected_on`, its note (280 characters at most) and who set it;
- `waived_at` and `waived_by`;
- `met_update_id` (the `portfolio_updates` report that met it) and `met_at`.

It is unique on duty and label. **Late and missing are never stored.**

**`reporting_events`.** The audit, append-only: two triggers refuse an UPDATE or a DELETE.
- **Columns:** venture, duty, period, actor, event, reference and time. There is no amount or figure column.
- **Events:** the fourteen #2427 lists, plus `reminders_set`.
- **Two partial unique indexes:** one reminder per period and offset, and one ask per period and investor.

**`reporting_reminder_settings`.** One row per venture: the offsets, the channels and the team recipients (RPT7). A venture with no row takes the defaults.

### 2. Duties come from signed paper (`createReportingDuties`)

**When it runs.**
- `completeClosing` (D1215) calls it after the close. A failure there never undoes the close: the answer says `reporting_duties_reason: 'retried_daily'`.
- The daily step runs it again for any closing closed in the last 45 days that has no duty.

**What it reads.**
- **Lines:** only `received` lines. A withdrawn line, or one only sent, is owed nothing.
- **The source of a line's terms:**
  - its envelope, when that envelope's status is `completed`, which means signed;
  - otherwise the closing's `template_slug`, recorded as `closing_template` with no `body_sha256`.

**The registry.** `INFORMATION_RIGHTS` quotes the clauses in Axal VC's templates:

| Document | Rights |
|---|---|
| Investors' Rights Agreement (`voting_rights`) | §1.1(a) monthly unaudited statements in 30 days; (b) quarterly KPI report with no deadline; (c) audited annual statements in 120 days; (d) the budget 30 days before the year |
| Term Sheet (`term_sheet`) | §2.2 monthly reports, annual audited financials and the budget, with no deadlines |
| The deal pack's term sheet | A quarterly financial summary |
| The deal pack's side letter | A quarterly investor update |

When two documents give the same kind, the Investors' Rights Agreement wins.

**Blank terms.** A blank deadline takes owner question 4's default and is named in `defaulted_json`: monthly 30 days, quarterly 45, annual audited 120, budget 30 before the year.

**No clause, no duty.** A SAFE and the stub Pro Rata and Information Rights Side Letter carry no clause, so they create no duty. That is owner question 7's path: Eadwyn proposes the terms and both parties confirm. It is not a guess made here.

**Not recorded.**
- **The major-investor threshold:** the clause leaves it as "___%", so every received line is owed its rights. The owner can narrow this.
- **Required figures:** no clause names one. They stay empty until part 3's `REPORT_FIGURES` gives a KPI report its keys.

**Idempotent.** The insert is `INSERT OR IGNORE` on the unique index. A `duty_created` event is written only for a duty the batch wrote, so a second run writes nothing.

### 3. The daily step (05:40 UTC, `runReportingDaily`)

1. **Duties a closing missed** (§2).
2. **Periods.**
   - Each period opens the day after it ends. A budget opens 30 days before it is due.
   - The first period is the one containing the close. For a budget it is the next year's, once this year's is already past.
   - Each opening writes one `period_opened` event, and only for a period the run wrote. A run catches up at most 36 periods per duty.
3. **Reminders.**
   - **Defaults:** 14 and 3 days before, on the day, and 7 days late; in the app and by email; to the founder team. The team is the owner and accepted co-founders, never an advisor or an investor.
   - **Once:** each reminder is claimed by its event row first, and only a claimed one is sent.
   - **No stale burst:** a reminder whose day is more than two days past is skipped, never sent late in a burst.
   - **Never for a closed period:** a period that was sent or waived gets none.
   - A recipient saved earlier who has since left the team is dropped.

### 4. State on read

| State | When |
|---|---|
| Sent | `met_update_id` is set (part 3 sets it on send) |
| Waived | `waived_at` is set |
| Upcoming | More than 14 days before the due day |
| Due | From 14 days before the due day to the due day itself, with the days left |
| Late | After the due day, with the days late |
| Missing | Late, and the next period is due too. It stays in the record |

**No zero.** A missing figure or state is never drawn as zero.

### 5. Routes (`/api/reporting`)

**The founder's desk.**
- **`GET /desk?project=`:** for the venture's team; an advisor and an admin read it too, and an investor or another venture gets 404. It returns:
  - every duty, with its holder's name and its record (sent, late, missing, waived);
  - every period with its state;
  - the investors' asks;
  - the reminder settings and the team;
  - `can_write`, which is true only for the owner or a co-founder, never an admin (owner question 6).
- **`POST /periods/:uid/expected`:** `{ expected_on, note }`. The owner or a co-founder only. The date is from today on, and the note is 280 characters at most. A sent or waived period is 409.
- **`PUT /reminders`:** `{ project, offsets, channels, recipient_user_ids }`. The owner or a co-founder. At most six offsets, from 60 days before to 30 after; `in_app` and/or `email`; recipients from the team only.

**The investor.** Every read and write is scoped by `holder_user_id = caller`.
- **`GET /portfolio`:** each company that owes the caller, with its duties and periods.
- **`GET /companies/:projectUid`:** one company, or 404 when it owes the caller nothing.
- **`POST /periods/:uid/ask`:** while the period is late or missing only (otherwise 409 `not_late`), and once per period (409 `already_asked`).
  - It writes D464's `portfolio_update_chases` row and an `asked` event in one batch.
  - It tells the founder team, linking `/grow/reporting`.
- **`POST /periods/:uid/waive`:** once. A sent period is 409.

**An investor never sees another investor.**
- Another investor's period is the same 404 as one that does not exist.
- The investor's rows carry no holder, recipient, closing line or any name.
- A support session writes nothing.

**The client:** `frontend/src/lib/api/reporting.js` (`reportingApi`): `desk`, `setExpected`, `setReminders`, `portfolio`, `company`, `ask` and `waive`.
