- **No Worker change.** The route was mounted and live; the drawer still said "not mounted yet (#3214)". That wait (`ROOM_WAITS.instructions`) is removed.

### 1. What the drawer shows

The warning comes first, as before (CP9). Then the account as the Worker reads it, always masked, in this order: payee, bank, account (`••••4821`), routing (ABA), SWIFT, currency, reference, then the line's amount. A field the account does not carry, such as an empty SWIFT, reads Not recorded and is never left blank. The canvas puts the warning under the rows; the issue's acceptance puts it first, and the room has always put it first, so it stays first.

### 2. Revealing the full number

- **It asks first.** "Reveal the full account number?" says the reveal is recorded without the number. Nothing is written before the press.
- **It names this line's commitment.** The reveal takes a commitment uid, and a closing line carries only `commitment_id`. The drawer reads the investor's own commitments on the deal (`GET /api/deals/:id/commitments`, which returns `id` and `uid` for the caller's own) at the press and picks the one whose `id` is the line's. A line with no commitment of the investor's shows no Reveal. If the list cannot be read, the drawer says so and offers Try again. This avoids adding `commitment_uid` to the closing read, which would be a Worker change.
- **It shows the number for the Worker's seconds.** The reply's `expires_in_seconds` drives a countdown ("Hides in 30s"). At zero the row goes back to the masked number. A reply with no seconds or no number shows nothing and reads as unreadable.

### 3. States, each in the Worker's sentence

| State | When | Drawn |
|---|---|---|
| No account set yet | `recorded: false` | The Worker's `reason`, then "Nothing here tells you where to pay yet." No rows, no Reveal |
| Unreadable | 5xx, a network failure, or an answer of neither shape | The Worker's sentence, "That is not a sign the company has no account.", and Try again, which reads again |
| Support session | `support_session_refused` | The Worker's sentence. No Try again |
| Not signed yet | the reveal's `signed_line_required` (403) | The Worker's sentence under the rows. No Try again |
| Reveal not recorded | the reveal's 503 | The Worker's sentence and Try again |
| Currency differs | the reveal's 409 | The Worker's sentence. No Try again |

A refusal's sentence is `e.message`; the words in `roomModel.js`'s `PAY_WORDS` are only fallbacks for an answer that carried none.
