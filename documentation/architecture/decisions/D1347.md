### 2. What the canvas adds beyond the prompt

1. **One People pane per profile (EM2i, EM2a, EM2o, EM2x, EM2e).** Each profile has its own groups and its own "What Eadwyn knows" sources:
   - **Investor:** deal flow, portfolio, co-investors, Network matches.
   - **Advisor:** clients from Practice sessions, Cohort founders, Network matches. Its sources are session notes, the next session booked, the cohort week plan, and their own Expertise service.
   - **Operating Partner:** clients from Delivery engagements, leads from Pipeline, Network matches. Its sources are status reports, milestones, proposals and Offers.
   - **Administrator:** "only the cases they hold": Spin-Out Lab applicants, open support tickets, and partner firms. The canvas's rule: "Eadwyn reads that case, never a member's own records." The admin's mailbox is the Google Workspace `@axal.vc` address, send only.
   - **Explorer:** Network matches only.

   #3549 builds the founder's sources only, so §4 files the rest as D1348 (#3555).
2. **The five "Insert a live update" chips** are KPIs, Raise status, Project progress, Interview insights and Company one-liner. These are #3549's five block kinds.
3. **EM6's numbers are a contract.** One draft per person, charged per draft, with the total estimate up front ("8 drafts · about 9,600 tokens in total"). It shows "3 of 8 sent". Nothing goes in bulk or by blind copy.
4. **EM7's sent log** pages "1–4 of 23" and shows the records each email quoted. That is `mail_sent.quoted_refs` (#3548).
5. **EM8's send failure names the provider's reason**, for example "Gmail refused it: the attachment is larger than 25 MB." So #3548's adapters map each provider's refusal to a sentence.

### 3. Two places the canvas goes past what may be built

1. **"Save drafts to your Gmail drafts"** (EM1b, EM1c, EM7). Writing to Gmail drafts needs `gmail.compose`. That scope is **restricted**, the same CASA tier as reading (D855 §84). Option A gives `gmail.send` only.
   - So the build drops that line and its "Copy to Gmail drafts" action.
   - The composer's "Save" keeps the draft in Axal (`mail_drafts`).
   - Outlook may save to Drafts, because Graph's `Mail.ReadWrite` is not restricted. #3548 asks for it only when the member chooses it.
2. **EM5's first card says "You can now talk on Axal VC."** After #3553 there is nowhere on Axal to talk. The build reads "You're matched. Email needs both of you to share an address."
