### 1. What a close is, read from the code

- **CAPITAL (#2424) is still a planning issue.** The Closing room and its CP11 are not built.
- **The close that exists today** is the raise pipeline's tranche: `raise_closes` (migration 169), `planned`, `open` or `closed`, with its `closed_date`.
- **Its investors** are the round's prospects assigned to it (`raise_prospects.close_id`), each with:
  - `amount` and `instrument` (safe, note or equity);
  - `commit_status`: soft, signed or wired. No status reads as signed, as migration 169 says.

The writer builds on these and on part 2's row. The Closing room reads the same row when it is built (S1, #2858).

### 2. Proposals from a close (CT11b): `services/capTableClose.ts`

**When:** a close in state `closed` proposes when a person asks, through `POST /record/:projectId/closes/:closeUid/proposals`. The raise pipeline's close routes (`routes/contacts.ts`) are unchanged.

**Who it proposes:** one issuance per firm investor of that close: `stage = 'committed'` and signed or wired. A soft commitment is not in the close's issuances.

**The key:** each row is part 2's `cap_table_issuances` row with `origin = 'close'` and `origin_ref = 'raise_prospect:<uid>'`.
- **Accepted once, here or there.** The key is the proposal's identity, so the Closing room decides the same row. Accept and Dismiss are part 2's routes, so deciding it on either page decides it on both.
- **A key is written once.** Running the writer again skips every investor with a row under its key, whatever that row's status. A dismissed investor is not proposed again.
- **Another writer first.** If a person or the Closing room recorded the same SAFE, part 2's unique index answers `already_on_record`, and the investor is listed under `already`.

**What each row carries:**

| Field | From |
|---|---|
| holder | the prospect's firm, else their name |
| kind | the instrument: `safe` → safe, `note` → note, `equity` → priced |
| amount | the commitment, in the venture's currency's smallest unit (part 2's `currency`; the ISO exponent, so JPY has none) |
| class and price | a priced close's, given by the person in the request (`class_uid`, `price_per_share_micros`) |
| shares | the amount over that price, rounded down, when both are known |
| date | the close's `closed_date` |
| document | below |

**Nothing is guessed:**
- an instrument not recorded is skipped (`instrument_not_recorded`), and so is a prospect with no name (`holder_not_recorded`);
- a missing amount stays empty, and so do a SAFE's cap, basis and discount and a note's terms: the person enters them on Accept (#2431).

**Refusals:**
- a close not closed: 409 `close_not_closed`;
- another venture's close: 404 `unknown_close`;
- a class that is not this venture's: 400 `unknown_class`, checked before anything is written;
- an option pool as the class: 400 `bad_class_uid`;
- a price that is not a whole number above 0: 400 `bad_price_per_share_micros`.

**Gates:** part 2's.
- Anyone who may propose runs the writer: the owner, a co-founder, or an admin with a reason.
- Only the owner, or an admin, accepts.
- The read takes the venture's read gate.
- One `captable.proposal.created` audit row per proposal, in the same batch (`proposeFromSource`, beside part 2's `recordFromSource`).

### 3. Signed, and "Not signed yet"

`signatureOf` (`services/capTableRecord.ts`) says whether the document behind a row is signed:

| Document | Signed when |
|---|---|
| A SAFE sent through StudioOS (`safe_issuances.prospect_id` is the investor) | its envelope is `completed` (countersigned) |
| `legal_documents:<id>` | the venture's legal document is marked `signed` |
| `data_room_files:<id>` | a person attached it from the venture's data room as the signed copy |

**What the writer does with it:**
- A countersigned SAFE is linked by its uid (`source: 'signed'`), and its terms are read from it, never copied (D1017 §5).
- Every other investor's row reads **"Not signed yet"** until a person attaches their document on Accept (`document_ref`, `source: 'document'`).

**The Accept rule:** part 2's Accept on an `origin = 'close'` row refuses with 409 `not_signed` ("Not signed yet: …") unless `signatureOf` says signed. Other proposals keep part 2's rule.

**The read:** `GET /record/:projectId/closes/:closeUid` is CT11b's. For each investor it gives:
- the holder, kind, amount and currency;
- the signature: `{ signed, via, label: 'Signed' | 'Not signed yet' }`;
- the proposal's uid and status;
- why it was skipped, if it was.

### 4. Eadwyn's read of a signed document (CT11c): `services/capTableDocumentRead.ts`

`POST /record/:projectId/document-read` with `{ document_ref: 'data_room_files:<id>' }`, from anyone who may propose.

**The text, page by page:**
- The file is read from the data room (`env.FILES`) and converted by `env.AI.toMarkdown` with PDF metadata off. The converter heads each page "### Page N", and `pagesOf` splits on those headings.
- Nothing in the Worker read a PDF's pages before. `routes/imports.ts`'s reader does not inflate streams, and `services/linkedinImport.ts`'s keeps no page breaks.
- A legal document StudioOS generated is not read here. A SAFE sent through StudioOS is linked with its terms (§3).

**One metered run:** `aiRouter.run` with `task: 'rail_legal'`.
- **Rail:** the Cap table page, option `draft:raise/document-terms`, record `project:<id>`, surface `/raise/cap-table` (new in `services/aiSurfaces.ts`).
- **What that gives it:** the page's Manual switch, the budget hold, the guard (the upload is the member's own text, `guardOutput: 'mark'`) and the charge to the member who pressed. This is how #1874's metering applies to a run.
- **Models:** part 10 adds the option to D771's founder inventory and draws it with `useRailOption`. Until then the run takes the page's row in the matrix.

**What the draft holds:**

| Group | Fields | Each carries |
|---|---|---|
| Terms | holder, instrument type (safe, note, priced, grant), basis (post- or pre-money), MFN, the date signed (`executed_on`) | the value and the page it came from |
| Money | amount, valuation cap, discount, price per share, shares | **the page only, never the figure** |

A term or money field the run did not find reads **"Not found in the document"**.

**The screen (`screenRead`)** builds the draft from an allow-list, so a figure the model writes has no key to land in. A term is kept only when:
- it cites a page the document has;
- it passes its own check: an instrument or basis from its list, MFN a boolean, a real calendar date;
- for the holder, the name is on the page it cites ("Eadwyn never states a figure a document does not hold"), and it has no currency sign, no percent and no run of three digits.

**Where it lands:**
- The draft is kept in D780's rail proposals (`proposeRail`, kind `draft`). A new read replaces the member's open one.
- It lands only on a person's Accept (D778's proposal path): the page decides the draft, and the person's own amounts go to part 2's `POST …/proposals` or `…/issuances` with `source: 'document'` and the `document_ref`.
- The read writes nothing on the record.

**Refusals:**
- a ref that is not a data-room file: 400 `bad_document_ref`;
- another venture's file: 400 `unknown_document`;
- no storage or converter: 503 `document_read_unavailable`;
- no text: 422 `nothing_to_read`;
- the router's own refusals (Manual, budget, guard) through `refuseRun`; otherwise 503 `ai_unavailable`.

The product copy names Eadwyn and never calls it advice.

- the amount in the smallest unit (USD, JPY) and the shares rounded down ($600,000 at $1.03 is 582,524);

- **a close's proposal decided on one page is decided on the other:** accepted and dismissed on the record, the close's read shows each decided, a second decision is 409, and a second run proposes nobody twice;
- **"Not signed yet":** no document, or a legal document not signed, is 409 `not_signed`; a signed legal document, or a countersigned SAFE, records; a typed proposal keeps part 2's rule;
- the page split;
- **an extraction never returns a money figure:** a model answer with figures in the money keys, in made-up keys and in a term comes out with none, and a money field has no value key;
- terms not on their page, on a page the document lacks, or failing their check, read "Not found in the document";
- the read: one run on the Cap table's rail, charged to the member who pressed, a draft kept and replaced, nothing recorded; refusals with nothing run.
