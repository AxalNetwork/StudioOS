### What is built

**The Recommendations section (WLM-05, WLM-05C).** It sits on the home below Today, This week and This month.
- It shows what the member chose ("From what you chose: … Spiritual guidance is off.").
- It has **Choose interests** and **Browse all →**, which goes to `/wellbeing/book`.
- Each card shows the provider's kinds of support, format, languages, time zones, lowest active price and whether they offer times. A group session card shows its format, language, time, "Free" and the seats left.
- **Why this?** lists what the suggestion used, then what it never uses: what you wrote, your answers, your bookings, and your reflection topics unless you allowed them. The Worker's `why` arrives with the card and is shown word for word.
- **Dismiss** hides a suggestion and shows **Undo** in its place.
- **View** opens an expert's profile.
- A line below the cards says how many suggestions were not shown, and why.
- The section answers in six states:
  - loading;
  - a failed read: "This is not a claim that none match", with Retry;
  - suggestions off;
  - nothing chosen yet;
  - no match;
  - the cards.

**Choose interests (WLM-04, WLM-05B).** The panel holds:
- the kinds of support, each off until chosen;
- formats (1:1, group; none chosen means both);
- languages, as short codes;
- a price limit in dollars, or blank for no limit;
- suggestions on or off;
- separately, **Use topics I tick in my reflections**, with the Worker's own scope sentence. When on, it needs at least one source: daily, weekly or monthly reflections, or earlier check-ins.

Save sends only what changed, with `base_revision`. A copy changed elsewhere (409 `preferences_changed`) offers Reload. **Reset all** asks first, then clears every choice, the permission and every dismissal.

**What the page never reads.** The section calls only the preferences and recommendations routes. It never reads a reflection or a check-in. Reading topics, when allowed, is the Worker's, inside D7119's consent rules.
