## D4656 — Measure pricing views and plan selection with the signup funnel (#4656)

Use the existing consent-aware funnel store for pricing intent. Capture the
audience tab and billing toggle when a pricing view is shown, and the stable
plan key and displayed interval when a card starts checkout or registration.
Free plans have no billing interval. Keep the pricing payload limited to
controlled dimensions. The event dictionary and cohort interpretation are in
[Signup Funnel Analytics](../ANALYTICS_FUNNEL.md).
