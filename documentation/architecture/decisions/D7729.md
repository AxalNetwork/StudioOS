### Scope (the definition half of Scope and questions)

- The fields #7719 holds and no others: product, product boundary, business model, the decision to be made, horizon in months; segments with buyer, user, budget holder, purchasing unit, what is counted, industry scheme and codes, size band, customer countries, exclusions, beachhead, sample note and limits. The study PUT sends every field with the version read; a stale save (409 `study_changed`) shows "Someone saved this scope first" with Reload and keep my edits, and nothing is overwritten. Every other refusal is shown in the Worker's words, edits kept.
- Customer geography is a segment's own customer countries: it starts empty and is never filled from the Markets filters. Owner answer Q5 A: a study started with a country filter offers "Use [countries] as customer geography for [segment]?" for one segment with none (the beachhead first); only Use writes it; No hides it.
- One beachhead: marking another clears the current one first, then marks the new one (the Worker's partial unique index refuses two).
- The venture shows what #7719 records: linked (its name), none, linked but no longer readable (Refused, "Cannot read", no name), or unreadable (Unavailable). Linking uses the venture open in the company switcher (`useActiveProject`); there is no picker.
- **A link that cannot be read is never dropped by accident.** The study PUT replaces every field, and a venture the caller cannot read comes back with no id, so a save would clear it (and every segment's Validate link). Save scope is therefore blocked with that reason until the member presses Unlink; a segment whose Validate link cannot be read cannot be edited from here for the same reason.

### New market research carries the market (changes D7171-p2's hand-over)

D7171 part 2 handed only the market's name over, as the question text (`?new=1&question=`), and created nothing by itself. That stays the plain link. **Now, with a row open, the link also carries an origin** after the question: `market` (the row's slug), `sector` (its tracked sector, absent when none) and, from the Market directory tab only, `country` (repeatable), `fresh` and `src`. `newFromSearch` reads it back through the checks `readMarketQuery` applies (a slug of at most 64 characters as the Worker allows, two-letter codes, at most 20, the freshness and source lists) and drops anything else; no valid slug is no origin. The form then shows a "Started from" block (the market's name read from the market directory, its tracked sector or None, the filters labelled as where listed companies are based, and "Customer geography is chosen on Scope") and Create project and study sends `POST …/with-study` with `{ project, origin: { market_slug, country, fresh, src }, request_id }`: the sector is shown, never sent (the Worker reads its own). A failure creates nothing, keeps what was typed, and Create again reuses the form's request id. Without an origin, including the form inside Add to research, the form calls `researchProjectsApi.create` exactly as before. The action stays disabled for advisor and partner.
