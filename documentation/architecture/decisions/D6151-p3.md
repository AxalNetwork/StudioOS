## D6151 part 3 — the Investment committee on a phone, board DE27 (#6151)

Part 3 of #6151. Parts 1 (`D6151.md`, Deal rooms DE19 to DE24) and 2 (`D6151-p2.md`, Screening DE26 and Compare DE29) are on main. This part builds the last phone board of the Deals v2 addendum canvas (`design/incoming/Deals v2 Deal Rooms and Phone Page.dc.html`, D6141):
- **DE27:** the Investment committee at 390, from DX19:
  - A and B, the decision;
  - C, the Vote sheet;
  - D, the Eadwyn sheet;
  - B to E of the states board (empty, unreadable, loading, refused).

It sits on the desktop Commit page from #5910 (PR #6876, merged), `CommitZone.jsx`, and reads what that page reads: `GET /api/ic/commit-room` and `GET /api/ic/conditions`. The one write is `POST /api/ic/:uid/vote`, the route the decision record (`ICDecisionPage.jsx`) already uses. There is no Worker change, no new route and no migration.

### The decision on a phone (DE27 A and B)

- **One column below 768px, from `CommitPhone.jsx`.** The desktop strip, the decision card and the conditions table are the view from 768px up.
- **Title and stage.** The phone shows the title with its stage: "Voting", or "Opened · no vote cast yet". Beside them it says "Outcome not decided yet".
- **The memo is a link** to the decision record, where the memo and terms are. The committee read does not carry the memo.
- **The tally.**
  - Yes, No and Abstain are counted.
  - Recused sits beside them, dashed, as "Recused · not in the tally" (D461).
  - A count the read did not carry is Not recorded, never 0.
- **Who has voted.**
  - With a roster (D6143), "N of M eligible have voted". A recusal counts as voted against the roster, as `rosterSummary` counts it.
  - With no roster, the line says no roster is set, so eligible voters are not recorded.
  - Then whether you have voted, and how.
- **One card per vote.**
  - A rationale, or "Rationale not recorded · cast before a rationale was required".
  - A recusal reads "Declared: …", never as a rationale.
- **Conditions.**
  - An open condition links to its deal's Closing: "! Open · blocks Closing ›", `/deals/closing?deal=<id>`.
  - Met and waived show their date.
  - The decision's author or an admin still gets Mark met and Waive.
- **Minutes.** These come from the desktop page's own `MinutesCard`, with its Record the minutes control.

### Vote (DE27 C)

- **Vote is pinned to the foot of the screen.** It opens a sheet with four choices: Yes, No, Abstain, or "Recuse · declare a conflict".
- **The reason is required.**
  - For a vote, the field reads "Rationale · required".
  - For a recusal, it reads "Declare the conflict · required".
  - Before it sends, the sheet refuses a blank one in the Worker's own words.
- **A Worker refusal is shown in its words**, in the sheet: not on the roster, voting closed, or a recusal that is final. A recorded vote closes the sheet and re-reads the page.
- **Vote is offered on the Worker's rules.** Where they rule it out, the bar says why in place of the button. The rules:
  - a role that may vote (admin, partner, investor);
  - a decision not decided;
  - no standing recusal of yours;
  - your name on the roster, if one is set.

### States (DE27 B to E)

- **Empty:** `NoOpenDecision`, as on desktop.
- **Loading:** `ZoneBody`'s skeleton.
- **Unreadable:** `ZoneBody`'s error with Try again.
- **Refused (new, both widths):** a 403 from the committee read is a refusal, not a failure. It reads "Investment committee is for investors, partners and admins. An admin can grant the licence.", with no Try again. Before this, a 403 read "The committee record could not be read".

### Departures from the board

| Board | Departs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| DE27 A | The memo is a link to the decision record, not a disclosure with "2 pages · author · date". | `commit-room` does not carry the memo, and adding it is a Worker change. The record already draws it. |
| DE27 A | "N of M eligible" only when the decision has a roster; otherwise it says none is set. | No roster means nobody is recorded as eligible (D6143). The desktop card still says Not recorded either way, as it did. |
| DE27 B | Minutes are the desktop's `MinutesCard` (a card, with its record control), not a bare line. | One component for one record on both widths. |
| DE27 C | No Eadwyn sheet (DE27 D). | The board marks it "IC task · no Eadwyn surface yet". The page's IC memo draft (`ZoneDraft`, `draft:ic-memo`) stays below, as on desktop. |
| DE27 C unreadable | The unreadable state keeps the page's sentence, "The committee record could not be read." | It is pinned by `ic_decision_pages_6155`. Try again stays. |
