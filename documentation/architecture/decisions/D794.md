## D794 — Opt-in auto-reload of AI tokens: a saved card charged off-session for the member's pack when their balance drops below their threshold

- **Status:** filed by S1 for the owner (2026-10-20) as #2219, part of D785 (#2210). Built by S26. **A money path: S1 reviews it for security before the owner merges.**
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `84f6e33b63`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Stacks on:**
  - D755 (token packs, the `ai_topup_purchases` row and the webhook credit, refunds and disputes);
  - D629 and D640 (the hold and its settlement);
  - D783 (the allowance drawn before tokens);
  - D707 (which modules may reach Stripe code).
- **D788** (#2213, the monthly spend limit) merged during this work (`cbb88dd4a8`). Its check is this PR's last commit, as the issue asks (§2.5, §2.6).
- **Waits on:** D791 (#2216, HQ's Pause AI). Its check is in `reloadBlocked` since PR #2227: a paused member's reload answers `paused`.

### 1. The decision

The owner, verbatim: "Opt-in auto-reload, but enable the user to choose a limit, amounts are auto charged automatically in advance for consumption limit, until consumption limit is reached. If there is not limit, the platform will keep recharging automatically."

- **Off by default.** The member turns it on and chooses two things:
  - a **pack**: what is charged each time;
  - a **threshold**: "reload when my token balance drops below $X", from $0 to $500.
- **The limit is D788's monthly spend limit, so the member has one limit, not two.**
  - With a limit, a reload is made only while it keeps this month's consumption within it.
  - With no limit, reloads continue each time the balance crosses the threshold.
- **The platform's safeguards stay:**
  - the five orders a day (`TOKEN_PACK_LIMITS.ordersPerDay`) count reloads;
  - the $1,000 balance ceiling and the dispute and shortfall holds apply;
  - HQ's Pause AI stops reloads.
- **A card that fails turns it off.** A declined card, a bank asking to authenticate, or a saved card Stripe will not charge turns auto-reload off. The member is told once, in the app and by email: "Auto-reload is off: your card was declined. Update it on Billing."
- **Every reload is an ordinary pack purchase.** It is an order with its PDF invoice, credited by the webhook as D755 does, and refunded or disputed the same way.

### 2. As built

1. **Migration 511** (`511_ai_auto_reload.sql`), additive. I checked `schema_baseline.sql`, every migration and the open branches first.
   - `member_ai_auto_reload` holds:
     - `enabled`, `pack_uid` and `threshold_microusd`;
     - `stripe_payment_method`: the card's id only;
     - `last_attempt_at`, `last_outcome` and `disabled_reason`;
     - `last_crossing` (see §3).
   - `member_ai_reload_locks (user_id, locked_until)`: one reload at a time per member.
2. **Saving a card.** `POST /api/ai-budget/auto-reload/setup` makes a SetupIntent on the member's own Stripe customer:
   - `usage = off_session`, card only, `metadata[purpose] = ai_auto_reload`;
   - only the client secret leaves the Worker;
   - the card is entered on Stripe's `PaymentElement` and confirmed in the browser;
   - when the member saves, `PUT` reads the SetupIntent back from Stripe. It accepts the card only if the SetupIntent:
     - succeeded;
     - is on this member's customer, `off_session`;
     - carries this member's id and the auto-reload purpose.

     The Worker never stores a payment method id the browser names.
3. **Routes**, each a money setting: a TOTP session with a recent step-up, as billing's checkout is (`requireFactor('totp')` and `requireStepUp`).
   - `GET /api/ai-budget/auto-reload`: the settings, and the saved card's brand and last four from Stripe. The card is shown only if it is on the member's own customer.
   - `PUT /api/ai-budget/auto-reload { enabled, pack_uid, threshold_usd, setup_intent_id? }`:
     - refuses an archived pack, and a pack not on sale when turning it on;
     - refuses a threshold below $0 or above $500, or with more than two decimals;
     - refuses turning it on with no card (400, with the field);
     - keeps the saved card when none is sent;
     - writes `ai_auto_reload_saved` to `activity_logs`, without the card.
   - `POST /api/ai-budget/auto-reload/setup`.
   - Both writes answer 423 during an account recovery's cool-off (D248), so a recovered account cannot set up charges. Reading stays open.
   - `step_up_required` keeps its code through `ai_budget.ts`'s error handler, so the SPA can prompt for a fresh TOTP.
4. **The trigger.** A settled run that drew on tokens calls `queueAutoReloadIfDue` (`services/aiAutoReloadDue.ts`, from `aiBudget.settleAiReservation`). It reads D1 only and, when a reload is due, queues the `ai_auto_reload` job. A run never waits on Stripe.
5. **The job** (`services/aiAutoReload.ts`, `runAutoReload`, on the minutely queue drain or the Cloudflare Queue):
   1. Take the member's lock.
   2. Check the reload is still due, and check `reloadBlocked`.
   3. Price the pack through the catalogue and judge it with `tokenPackQuote`: on sale, five a day, the ceiling, the holds.
   4. Check D788's limit (`limitStop`): this month's charge plus the pack's tokens (credit and bonus) must stay within it. This is D788's own rule for a hold (`limitRefusal`), with the pack as the estimate.
   5. Claim the crossing.
   6. Create the PaymentIntent **unconfirmed** (`metadata[kind] = cart_order`, `metadata[auto_reload] = 1`).
   7. Write the pending order and its `ai_topup_purchases` row.
   8. Confirm it off-session.

   The webhook credits it as any pack and renders its invoice. Confirming on create could let the webhook arrive before the purchase row, and a payment with no purchase row credits nothing.
6. **Failures.**
   - A card failure (any Stripe 4xx on the charge, or a confirm that needs authentication):
     - sets `enabled = 0` with `disabled_reason`;
     - hands the crossing back (nothing was charged);
     - notifies once, in the app and by email, through the `billing` category.

     The reasons are `card_declined`, `authentication_required`, `card_unusable` and `pack_unavailable`.
   - A charge that fails after it was confirmed does the same through the verified `payment_intent.payment_failed` webhook. Only a payment marked `auto_reload` is touched.
   - A Stripe 5xx, a 429 or no answer leaves auto-reload on, hands the crossing back, and the next settlement tries again with the same idempotency key.
   - A refusal (five a day, the ceiling, a hold, a pack off sale) is recorded in `last_outcome` and charges nothing.
   - **The spend limit** stops a reload that would pass it. Nothing is charged, the crossing stays unclaimed, and auto-reload stays on, so it resumes when the month resets or the member raises the limit. The member is told once, in the app and by email, by the write that first records `spend_limit` in `last_outcome`. They are told again only after a reload has since been made or refused for another reason. With no limit, reloads continue.
7. **The card** on Billing (`pages/account/AutoReloadCard.jsx`, words in `autoReloadModel.js`, Stripe's form in `AutoReloadCardSetup.jsx`, loaded only when a card is added) sits beside the orders on today's `/account/billing` until D792 lands. It has:
   - the switch, the pack and the threshold;
   - the saved card;
   - the state line, and why the last reload was skipped.

### 3. One crossing, one charge

- **The crossing** is the payment intent of the member's latest credited pack (`crossingOf`; `'none'` before the first). It changes only when a new pack is credited.
- **Due** means:
  - auto-reload is on with a saved card;
  - the balance is below the threshold;
  - the crossing is not `last_crossing`.

  So runs settling below the line while a reload is pending queue nothing.
- **Three guards** stop a crossing being charged twice, each tested with the others removed:
  - the lock (a held lock refuses; an expired one is taken);
  - the conditional claim of `last_crossing` before Stripe is called;
  - Stripe's idempotency key.
- **The key** is `reload:<user>:<crossing>:<settings version>`. The version is the settings' `updated_at`, which only the member's own save moves. After a declined card is updated and auto-reload turned back on, the attempt is new rather than a replay of the cached decline. Within one attempt every retry reuses the key.

### 4. Departures, for review

- **The trigger is the job queue, not `waitUntil`.** The settle point (`settleAiReservation`) has no request context, and a run is reached from many routes. Threading `waitUntil` through every caller, or adding the Worker's first `AsyncLocalStorage`, is more than this needs. The queue job meets the issue's goal: the run never waits on Stripe. It is also durable: a crashed isolate does not lose a reload.
- **`last_crossing`** is a column the issue did not list. It keeps one crossing to one charge after Stripe's 24-hour idempotency window.
- **The idempotency key** carries the settings' version as well as the crossing (§3).
- **VAT.** A reload is charged at the VAT rate of the member's latest paid order's billing country. Today that is 5% for the UAE, otherwise none, as the checkout computes it. A member with no paid order pays none.
- **D790's offers** (first-purchase-only packs) are not on `main`. The pack rule is "a pack HQ sells and has not archived, on sale to turn it on". The first-purchase-only refusal joins when D790 does.
- **The limit counts the pack's tokens, bonus included,** against this month's charge, because those are the tokens the reload lets the member consume. It does not count the balance already held, so the last reload of a month may be refused with some headroom left.
- **D791.** `reloadBlocked` answers `paused` while `member_ai_pauses` has the member (PR #2227): no Stripe call, the crossing unclaimed, auto-reload left on. A pause that cannot be read throws, so the job charges nothing.
