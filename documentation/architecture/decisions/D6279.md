## D6279 — A founder or investor's market reading has no proposal card (#6279)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-15 by S22 for #6279 (tracker #5994).
- **Number:** D6279 is this issue's number (D4106). No Worker or schema change.
- **Code:** `frontend/src/pages/research/marketReadingRead.js` (`OWN_RECORD_LINE`,
  `attachesNowhere`, `DRAFT_PILL`), `frontend/src/pages/research/MarketReading.jsx`
  (`MarketReadingView`).
- **Tests:** `frontend/test/research_market_reading_licences_6279.test.mjs`, one
  behaviour test per licence; `frontend/test/research_market_reading_d593.test.mjs`
  now pins the "No proposal on this account" line on advisor and partner.

### 1. No proposal card where a proposal cannot exist

Proposals are the quotes a partner profile owns, so a founder or an investor
reads none. When the payload carries no proposal and no attachment, their page
draws no Proposal tile, no attach pill in the header and no Proposal attachment
card. One line says what a reading is on that licence instead, and a stale
reading keeps its "Record a fresh reading" control beside it. The stated limit
and the age footnote stop saying "travel onto any proposal" and "still
attachable". A founder or investor read that does carry a proposal or an
attachment draws the card as before. An admin reaches the page as a founder
(D593), so the same applies there.

Partner and advisor keep the card, the tile, the pill and the "No proposal on
this account" sentence unchanged.

### 2. The Service line picker

The "Record a reading" form on `/research/markets` already offers the Service
line picker only when the caller's readings name a catalog offering, and falls
back to the free-text "What it prices" otherwise. `MarketDirectoryZone.jsx` is
not changed; the new test pins that behaviour.

### 3. The draft pill

The pill above the reading's draft said `ZONEDRAFT`, the canvas's token. It now
says "Draft, not saved", because Accept writes nothing. The other research pages
that print the token are outside this issue.
