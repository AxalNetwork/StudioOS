## D4817 part 2 — PF-02 Positions register and mark history (#4817)

Part of #5311 (PF-02): the second board of the Portfolio v2 canvas (`design/incoming/Portfolio v2 Page.dc.html`, boards PF-02 and PF-02b, with addendum PFA06 and the PF-10c phone layout), drawn in the frame PF-01 built (D4817 part 1). Frontend only, against today's Worker reads. No route, Worker or schema change.

**What is built.** `frontend/src/pages/investor/InvestorPortfolioPositions.jsx` is rebuilt in place. It keeps its file, its mount through `PortfolioWorkspace` and its address, `/portfolio/positions`, so every link to it still resolves. It draws in `PortfolioFrame` with `tab="positions"`, which gives it the document title `Positions · Portfolio · Axal VC`. The page holds:
- **Read the book as of** (PFA06): a date, Apply, a one-click "last quarter-end" from the reporting read's own `period_until`, and Latest marks.
  - The date goes to `GET /api/positions?as_of=`, which D4644 already honours. `api.positionsList` gains an optional `asOf`; every other caller is unchanged.
  - Health and reporting stay current reads with their own dates, and the page says so.
- **Three counts, kept apart:** mark coverage, stale marks (older than two quarters) and marks that cannot be used.
- **The investment register.** Its columns, in the board's order: Company (a link to `/portfolio/companies/:uid`), Invested, Ownership, Latest FMV, Multiple, Mark date, Basis, Reporting and Health.
  - A Stale chip sits under a mark older than two quarters.
  - Every position has a row, sorted by cheque size, then name.
  - The total row gives Σ invested, Σ carrying value and the multiple, and names each position it includes at cost.
  - The filters, Export, Mark history and Add follow-on come from the existing `portfolio/positions` zone tables. They open on All, never on Needs attention (audit F-18).
- **Mark history for one company** (PF-02b): the mark date opens the shared `Sheet` over `GET /api/positions/:uid`.
  - Marks are listed newest first, with FMV, post-money, event, and basis and source.
  - It shows the latest mark's age and what "stale" means.
  - It says that recording a mark is an HQ admin act. No write is offered to an investor.
- **The book-wide mark history** (`GET /api/positions/marks`) is still the toolbar's Mark history action, in the frame's styles.
- **Attention rules and evidence** (PFA06): runway under 6 months, update older than 30 days, health turned red within 90 days, latest mark below cost, and no positive scoring evidence.
  - Each rule shows its queue reason and each company's evidence with its date.
  - Each rule says whether it also feeds the Overview's queue.
  - A rule whose source failed reads Unreadable, says it is not a claim that nothing fired, and offers Retry.
- **Kept from the old page:** the Eadwyn narrative band (`ZoneDraft`, `draft:multiple-narrative`), the governed-writes card, From Mercury and the one rail (`WorkerRail`, #5130's).

**What the shared pieces gained** (no second set):
- `portfolioModel.js`:
  - `markOf`, which tells a marked position, one with no mark and one with a recorded but unusable mark (D4644's `mark_unavailable_reason`) apart;
  - `basisLabel`: one basis vocabulary (audit F-24), and null for a missing basis, never the column default;
  - `markReference`: the date a mark's age is measured at. That is the chosen as-of date, else `kpi-compliance`'s `as_of`, never the browser clock;
  - `isStale`, `registerRowsOf`, `markCountsOf`, `registerTotalOf`, `filterRegister`, `markHistoryOf`, `ageWords` and `attentionRulesOf`;
  - `STALE_MARK_DAYS`, `RUNWAY_FLOOR` and `runwayFor`, now exported.
- **A fix to PF-01:** the Overview's evidence and company table now use `markOf`. The Worker sets `mark_unavailable` for a position with no mark too (`no_mark`), so the Overview used to label every unmarked position "Mark not usable". It now reads "No mark recorded".
- `PortfolioParts.jsx`: `ObligationStatus` takes `compact`, which leaves the period out of the word when the column header names it.
- `portfolio.css`: the as-of bar, the counts, the register and its total row, the rules table and the Sheet body, with tablet and phone layouts. Colours come from the frame's tokens, which `.dark` redefines; the only new dark rule sets the date picker's colour scheme.
- `investorPortfolioPositions.css` is deleted: nothing else imported it.

**How each figure reads.**
- **A failed positions read** replaces the counts and the register with "Positions could not be read. No empty book or zero is shown in its place." and a Retry for that source alone. A Worker `detail` never reaches the page.
- **An empty book** says no position is recorded. Each count reads Not recorded with that reason, and no total is drawn.
- **No mark recorded** reads as such, with "At cost for NAV". A recorded mark that cannot be used reads "Mark not usable" with the Worker's reason. In both cases the multiple says "At cost".
- **Stale marks** need a reference date. If the reporting read failed and no as-of date is chosen, the count reads Unreadable and says why; it never falls back to the browser's date. *Superseded in part by D6226 (#6226): the positions read's own `today` is the reference when no as-of date is chosen, so a failed reporting read no longer leaves the count Unreadable. The reporting read's `as_of` is the fallback only for a positions read without `today`.*
- **A failed health, reporting or founder-updates read** labels its own column or rule Unreadable. The others stay current.

**Departures from the canvas, and why.**
- **"Unreadable mark sources: 0, measured zero · read 14:30"** is drawn as "Marks that cannot be used". No read reports per-source mark failures or a read time. What the Worker does report is the marks it could not use (two funds disagree, or the value is negative), so that is what is counted.
- **The register is sorted by cheque size.** The canvas's sample is in that order, and the Overview already sorts by health.
- **The Reporting column** names the period in its header ("Reporting · 2026-Q3") when every row shares it, and each chip says Received, Due or Not received. The canvas's "11 days overdue" is not drawn: `kpi-compliance` records no due date.
- **The Multiple column of an unmarked position** shows the Worker's 1.00× labelled "At cost", where the canvas has a dash. A bare dash is not a state.
- **The total's multiple** is Σ carrying ÷ Σ invested over the rows, labelled as such. The canvas's 1.59× is the same arithmetic. TVPI, DPI and IRR stay on the Overview and Performance.
- **The mark-history Sheet** offers no "Record a mark…" button. The write is admin-only (`requireAdmin`) and this is the investor's book; Prepare → Review → Confirm is PF-07's. Distributions and rounds are not drawn in the Sheet: they belong to PF-06's company Financials.
- **Two rules are not in the Overview queue:** "Update older than 30 days" (from the Worker's `days_since_report`) and "Health turned red within 90 days" (a lazy 90-day history read for each Attention company only). Adding them to the Overview would change PF-01's queue, so the rules table says which rules feed the queue.
- **The phone layout** keeps the toolbar chips and the search box, not PF-10c's "Filters (1) / Sort" sheet. Each row becomes a labelled record card.
- **The local navigation** carries no counts, as in PF-01.
