## D813 — The website's newsletter signup, its confirm and preferences pages, and Account › Notifications › Newsletters

- **Status:** decided by S1 for the owner, 2026-10-20 (#2259; part of D809, #2255). Built by S17's session on S10's task. The owner ruled at 20:12Z to build from prompt 2's written boards (W1, W2, W3, D1) without waiting for the "StudioOS · Newsletter and Product testing" canvas; S1 files a restyle follow-up when it lands.
- **Basis:** D810's routes and client (#2273, `claude/beautiful-pasteur-3mghp7`). This is stacked on that branch and adds no `/api/*` method: every call goes through `frontend/src/lib/api/newsletter.js`.

### What is built

1. **W1, the signup** (`frontend/src/components/site/NewsletterSignup.jsx`, `newsletter.css`).
   - **The block** above the footer of every `SitePage`: "The Axal VC Brief", "Monthly: essays, portfolio news, open roles and events.", an email field and Subscribe, then "We'll email you to confirm. Unsubscribe in one click from any email." and the privacy policy link. It subscribes to `brief`.
   - **`/newsletter`** (`pages/site/NewsletterPage.jsx`): the same form with the six public topics as switches, the Brief on.
   - **The states:** sending (field and button locked, "Sending…"), sent, and an error in the route's words (invalid address, Turnstile, 429, not configured). An empty topic list and a malformed address are refused before the request.
   - **Turnstile and the honeypot** as on Contact. The block mounts the widget only once its field is focused, so the script does not load on every public page; `/newsletter` mounts it at once.
2. **W2, `/newsletter/confirm?token=`** (`NewsletterConfirmPage.jsx`): confirmed, "This link has expired." with Subscribe again, an already-used or bad link, not configured, and an unexplained failure with Try again ("Your link has not been used"). The single-use token is read once per mount.
3. **W3, `/newsletter/preferences?token=`** (`NewsletterPreferencesPage.jsx`), no sign-in:
   - the topics the read returns as switches, in migration 515's order, with the masked address;
   - Save, which sends only the switches that changed;
   - "Unsubscribe from everything", and "Unsubscribe from [topic]" when the link names one (`&topic=`);
   - the unsubscribed page, "You're unsubscribed from [topic]." or "…from every Axal VC newsletter.", with Resubscribe, which turns back on what was on;
   - a bad or expired token, an unreadable read with Try again, and a write refused for its token, which goes to the bad-link page.
4. **D1, Account › Notifications › Newsletters** (`pages/account/AccountNewsletters.jsx`, mounted in `SettingsPage.jsx`'s Notifications section): the member's topics from `GET /api/me/newsletters`, the website's six then the ones a role adds (marked "For your role"), each switch saved on its own with `PUT /api/me/newsletters` and redrawn from the answer; a refusal (`topic_not_allowed`) leaves the switch where the Worker holds it and says why. "Sent to [email] by Axal VC Mail." is the card's footer. Loading, empty and Unreadable with Retry. Account's kit (`PanelCard`, `Switch`, `useAccountRead`), so dark mode and the 44px phone targets come with it.
5. **The weekly-insights toggle is gone** from `PartnerInsightsPage.jsx`. Its place is a "Newsletters in Account" link to `/account/notifications`; D810 keeps `insight_subscriptions` in step with the `insights` topic. The old routes stay for now; nothing in the UI calls their subscribe, unsubscribe or status any more.
6. **Registered:** the three routes in `App.jsx`, `isPublicPath` in `lib/api.js`, `/newsletter` in the OG registry and the sitemap (the token pages are not for crawlers), and all three in `scripts/check-spa-live.mjs`, which asserts the shell and its security headers live after each deploy.

### Never saying whether an address is subscribed

D810's subscribe route answers the same for every address. The page awaits that answer and reads nothing from it, so there is one sent state: "Check your inbox to confirm." and "If that email is already subscribed, we've sent a link to manage your topics instead." That is the board's "already subscribed" state, worded so it never asserts it. No refusal sentence mentions an address's state either.

### The topics

There is no public read of the topic list, so `pages/site/newsletterModel.js` holds migration 515's eight topics, and the test holds them equal to the seed rows and to the Worker's `NEWSLETTER_TOPICS`. The member's card reads its labels and descriptions from its own route.

### What is not built, and why

- **W2's "You're subscribed to [n] newsletters" and its list.** D810's JSON confirm answer is `{ ok, status }`, with no topics, so the page says "You're subscribed." and the welcome email carries the topics. When the answer carries `topics`, the page draws the count and the list from it with no change here (`confirmView`). No count is invented.
- **The confirmation email's link** points at the Worker's own HTML page (`/api/newsletter/confirm?token=`), not at this page. Pointing `confirmUrl` at `/newsletter/confirm?token=` is a one-line change in D810's route, for S08 or S1; this page is ready for it.
- **The look** follows the written boards in the site's and Account's existing styles, as S1 directed; the canvas restyle is S1's follow-up.

### Security headers

The three pages are SPA shell paths, served by the Worker's assets binding, so `frontend/public/_headers`'s `/*` rule (D529) gives them HSTS, `nosniff`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY` and the Referrer-Policy. The test holds that the `/*` rule carries them, that no rule of their own detaches one, and that no `run_worker_first` glob in either `wrangler.toml` table takes these paths from the assets binding. `check-spa-live.mjs` checks the live headers.
