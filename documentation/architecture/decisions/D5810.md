## D5810 — Microsoft Teams in Account › Connectors, on the shared Microsoft connection (#5810)

**Decided.** Microsoft Teams is a first-party connector with its own card and
page (`/account/connectors/teams`), and no grant of its own. Its connection is
the member's one row in `microsoft_oauth_tokens`, the same row Microsoft 365's
page manages: the same status read (`GET /api/calendar/microsoft/status`), the
same OAuth start and callback, the same encrypted refresh, reconnect and
disconnect. An existing Microsoft grant fills both pages at once, and either
page's disconnect ends both, which the confirm dialog says before the click
(`SHARED_DISCONNECT`).

**Why one grant.** Creating a Teams meeting is a calendar write with
`Calendars.ReadWrite` (D1359, `services/channels/teamsMeet.ts`). A second grant
would ask the member for the same permission twice and could disagree with the
first.

**What changed.**
- Worker: `FIRST_PARTY_CARDS.teams` and `FIRST_PARTY_PAGES.teams`
  (`integrations/connectorPages.ts`) with `connection_source:
  'microsoft_oauth_tokens'` and `shares_connection: 'microsoft'`;
  `/api/connectors/available` sends it in `first_party` after LinkedIn's, and
  `/api/connectors/directory/teams` answers its page. Flags and our sentences
  only: no token, tenant or meeting link. Its tools name the two Graph writes
  `teamsMeet.ts` makes (the event with its Teams meeting, and its removal for a
  personal account), checked by `connector_pages_d753.test.ts`.
- `GET /api/founder/validate/meetings/capabilities`' Teams row: `connected` is
  the member's own grant, read whether or not this deployment can create with
  it, and null when the read fails for anything but a missing table
  (`microsoftConnected`). `create_available` is the deployment on its own;
  `can_create_meeting` is both. A failed read can no longer say "not
  connected".
- SPA: `FIRST_PARTY` lists Teams; `SHARED_CONNECTION` reads its status from
  Microsoft's, and the row is drawn only when the Worker sends its card.
  Search finds it as "Teams" or "Microsoft Teams". A Microsoft refusal of the
  saved grant (`sync_error: 'connection_refused'`) is "Reconnect required" on
  both pages. A sign-in started on the Teams page leaves a tab-only marker
  (`teamsConnector.js`), so Microsoft's return lands on the Teams page
  (`connectorsRedirect`, `pageReturn`). `TeamsSection.jsx` draws three
  separate rows: the Microsoft connection, creating a work or school Teams
  meeting, and Eadwyn AI joining Teams calls, each with loading and
  Unreadable states, re-read when the tab comes back. The card and the page
  draw the owner-requested local Teams mark (`/microsoft-teams.ico`) through
  `ConnectorMark.jsx`; every other connector keeps `BrandIcon`.

**Not built.** No Teams transcript or recording import. No Teams-only grant or
scope.

**Verified.** `frontend/test/teams_connector_d5810.test.mjs` (existing grant,
connect and return, cancel, reconnect, shared disconnect, unreadable, search,
deep link, the section's rows and Back link per member);
`cloudflare-worker/test/connector_directory_d753.test.ts` (the card and its
page read, for every role); `teams_create_meeting_d1359.test.ts` (another
member's grant, an unreadable read, the deployment flag). Rendered at desktop
and phone widths in the PR.
