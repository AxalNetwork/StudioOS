## D6683 — Incorporate truth pass: no uncited country claims, and the lowest-cost answer does not pick Estonia (#6683)

- **Status:** built by S04 for #6683, 2026-11-15. Part of #6680 (plan key E3, Phase 0).
- **Basis:** D4357 says no figure appears without a source and a day. D4421 says no new legal wording. D4677 removed the Atlas call.
- **Migration:** none.
- **Paths:** Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`; frontend paths are relative to `frontend/src/`.

### 1. `JURISDICTIONS` (`routes/legal.ts`)

Before this change, the six entries carried tax, fee and time claims with no source or date. Examples:
- "Stripe Atlas one-click"
- "£50 same-day filing"
- "0% tax on retained earnings"
- "Startup tax exemption ~75%"
- "Corporation tax 25%"
- "DE franchise tax $400–$1,750"
- cost ranges and day ranges

**What changed:**
- **Fields set to null:** `est_cost_usd`, `time_to_form_days` and `tax_summary` are now `null` on every entry. The frontend already treats `null` as "nothing recorded", and the compare step shows "Not recorded", never 0.
- **Strings removed:** every pro, con and summary phrase that stated a tax, a fee, a time or Atlas is gone. Nothing new was written in their place.
  - Two summaries lost only their claim: "Fast and credible…" became "Credible…", and "Fully remote, 0% tax…" became "Fully remote."
  - Wyoming's summary, which was itself a tax claim, now names only where the company is formed.
- **Atlas:** `atlas_supported` is `false` on every entry. The Stripe Atlas handoff branch of the incorporate route is removed, so the handoff is always documents only plus each country's next steps.
- **The Estonian form:** its form displays as "OÜ" (`entity_type`), and its id stays `ee_oy`.
- **What stays:** ids, labels, structure, templates and the qualitative pros and cons that state no figure.

### 2. The recommendation (`pages/incorporate/incorporateModel.js`)

- **What was removed.** `recommend()` loses the `minimalCost === 'yes'` → `ee_oy` branch. That branch sent lowest-cost founders to Estonia, against the owner's playbook, and there is no recorded cost to rank by.
- **What stays.** Estonia can still be chosen by hand. The goal question stays in the wizard (`IncorporateFlow.jsx` is outside this issue's files), and its answer no longer changes the recommendation.

### 3. The packet (`services/incorporationPacket.ts`)

- **Singapore.** The statement drops "(Cap. 50)" and now reads "under the Companies Act of Singapore".
- **Estonia.** It drops the "EUR 2,500" share-capital sentence and names the form "OÜ".
- No other wording changed.

### 4. Not done here

- New country facts with sources: they arrive with the catalogue.
- Any new wording about law or tax.
- The order and checkout routes in `legal.ts`, which this issue does not touch.
