### The system, in `pages/partner/kit.jsx`

The advisor kit's `ZoneHeading`, `NothingYet` and `Field` also serve every Expertise, Pipeline and Delivery zone, so the Offers scale is opt-in names beside the partner's amber pair rather than an edit to every licence's surfaces:

- **Type (OF-10):** `OfferHeading` (section heading 18px, its explanation 14px), `OfferEmpty` (18px outcome, 14px reason), `OfferField` (13px semibold label, never tiny uppercase; help 12px; error 12.5px). Inputs are 14px (`offerInputClass`), buttons 13px. Metadata is 12px minimum; uppercase is kept only for table headers and the Consent record group label (12px). The edited Offers files draw nothing below 12px, and the test reads Catalog and Proof for it.
- **Actions:** `offerButtonClass` is the one filled amber primary, `offerGhostButtonClass` every other action, `offerDangerButtonClass` a destructive one. All have a visible focus ring and a 44px target below 640px.
- **Fields:** `OfferField` wraps its control in a visible `<label>`, puts the help and the field's own error outside it (so the control's name is the label's words) and ties both with `aria-describedby`, with `aria-invalid` while the error stands. The error leads with a cross.
- **Status:** `OfferStatus` / `OFFER_STATUS` is OF-10's chip, an icon and a word (Live ✓, Draft ◌, Unlisted ‖, pending …, Rejected ✕), so no state depends on colour alone.

- **Catalog:** New service, in the toolbar (already the primary). The heading carries none, so it is not drawn twice. The empty state's "Add your first service" is the one exception the issue allows.
- **Proof:** "+ Add proof" in the heading. **Ask for consent is no longer marked `primary`** in `partnerZoneActions.js`: OF-03 draws Add proof alone in amber, and asking is about a result that already exists. `partner_shell.test.mjs` and `profile_zone_actions.test.mjs` now count three partner primaries.
- **Audience fit:** "+ Add fit rule". A row with no reply keeps its small filled "Add reply", which OF-04 draws that way (`btn p bsm`); it is a row action, not the zone's.
- **Product testing:** "+ New programme". **The toolbar's "New programme" is removed**: it was the same action drawn a second time, outlined, beside the filled one and above the empty state's "Create a testing programme". Export stays secondary. A programme's next step keeps OF-06's filled "Review applicants" / "Submit for approval".
- The Eadwyn draft band (`ZoneDraft`) keeps its own amber run button; it is a shared component outside this pass.

### Editors on the shared Sheet

- **Catalog `OfferingSheet`** (was `OfferingFormModal` in a bespoke `fixed inset-0` modal, now deleted): OF-02b's slide-over. Eyebrow "Catalog · new/edit service", the service's name as the title. Focus starts on Service name. Client checks put "Give the service a name." and a price that is not a non-negative number on their fields before anything is sent. A failed save keeps every entry and says "Couldn’t save. Everything you typed is still here." with `writeFailureText`'s fixed sentence (the raw response text is no longer shown) and Try again, which sends the same payload. A 400/422 naming one of the form's fields puts the sentence on that field (`writeFailureField`). "Saving…" is on the button while in flight; on success the sheet closes and "{name} is saved." is announced (`role="status"`). Delete sits at the left of the footer, Cancel and Save on the right. Escape, the × or the scrim on a sheet with unsaved entries asks first, because the entries live in the sheet; Cancel closes at once. Row-menu List/Unlist/Delete failures are their own fixed-sentence alert instead of borrowing the read's error box.
- **Proof `ProofSheet`** for a new result and for a card's Edit (both were inline panels), and **the Ask for consent `AskModal`** (was a bespoke fixed overlay). Fields: Title (required, checked before sending), Kind, What the work was, Result claimed (now also on a new result: the store and the card headline already read it). The entries live with the zone or the card, so closing the sheet throws nothing away and needs no confirmation. Delete is on the left of the edit sheet's footer. The card's inline "Request client approval" panel stays inline: it is a short form about the card it sits on, and #4842's tests drive it there.
- Every moved editor keeps `Sheet`'s contract: `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, named by its visible title, focus moved in (to the first field), Tab and Shift+Tab kept inside, Escape (top dialog only), the × and the scrim close, the page behind inert, focus returned to the opener, a bottom sheet on a phone (`phone="sheet"`).
- `FitRuleSheet` (#4842's) keeps its Sheet and gains `OfferField`: the minimum-engagement error is now an error tied to its field rather than replacing the help text, and a refusal naming a field lands on it.

### Saving, saved, not saved

`useGuardedWrite`'s `WriteNote` leads its sentence with ✓ (saved), ! (saved, list not refreshed) or ✕ (not saved), at 13px, and the failure note now carries the `field` a validation refusal names. Its sentences and Retry are #4688's and D4836's, unchanged.

### State wording (#5129, not a new Offers set)

Proof, Audience fit and Product testing pass `what` to `ZoneBody`, so loading and a failed read are `SectionState` (D4828 on D5129): a skeleton announced as "Loading your proof…", and Unavailable with the zone's sentence and Try again, never the empty state. Catalog draws `DataState` directly: "Your catalog could not be read. Nothing here is out of date; it just isn’t shown." with Retry (OF-08's words). Visibility, outside this issue's list, still draws the older card.

### Empty states

Each names the commercial outcome and offers one action: Catalog "Show founders what your firm sells" (new copy; OF-02 draws no empty catalogue), Proof "Show founders why they should hire you" (OF03b), Audience fit "Tell Pipeline who your firm is for" (D4842's wording kept), Product testing "Validate an offer before launch" (OF06b).

### Departures from the canvas

- The service sheet asks "Service name" first; OF-02b shows the name only as the sheet title. A new service needs a name field.
- OF-02b's "Unlist service…" in the footer is "Delete service…": listing is already the row menu's List/Unlist, and the footer's destructive slot is the one place Delete was missing.
- "Who is it for?" (OF-02b) is not drawn: `service_offerings` has no such column.
- Audience fit's empty title stays D4842's "Tell Pipeline who your firm is for" rather than OF06b's "Define your ideal client", which the issue quotes as an example; #4842 settled it and its tests pin it.
