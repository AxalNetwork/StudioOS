### 5. Never automatic

These are #2400's rules, and the tests hold every part to them:

- Publishing is `publish`, never `push`, so neither `autopush.ts` nor Eadwyn's
  `SEND_TARGETS` can reach it.
- A publish target has no `default_mode`.
- Every send is pressed by the founder in the confirm dialog.
- An outcome that is not known is never re-sent.
