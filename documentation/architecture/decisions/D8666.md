### What the audit found

- The rubric rendered as one paragraph of pipes and a separator row because `ExercisePanel.jsx` uses `<ReactMarkdown>` with no plugins; `remark-gfm ^4.0.1` is in `frontend/package.json` but nothing in `frontend/src` imports it.
- At 390 Submit, Not submitted yet and No review yet sat above the instructions because the Status card is `order-first` below `lg`, and `ExerciseSubmit` lives in the header.
- The textarea uses `maxLength`, so a paste over 2,000 characters is cut without a word; drafts live only on this device (`useLearningDraft`).
- Migration 390 has no deliverable, time, rubric or score column, and the learner read sends no reviewer name and keeps no server draft.

### What the canvas decides

- The Pitch decision memo (lesson 7 of 10) opens with the deliverable band: a decision memo, up to 2,000 characters, about 30 minutes, and one primary action chosen by draft state, Start your answer or Submit for review.
- On desktop the task, case, five requested headings and marking criteria sit in tabs beside a fixed answer column; on phones they open as sheets over the editor.
- The marking criteria are a semantic matrix (5 criteria × scores 0–3, caption, row and column headers, "◆ Must score 2 or 3" on R3, pass at 11 of 15) at 640px and wider, and one section per criterion below.
- The counter reads "750 / 2,000" with no `maxLength`; an empty memo or one over the limit blocks submission, a missing heading only advises, and 400 `bad_answer`, 409 `already_accepted` and a timeout (which reads the submissions before offering a retry) each have their own words.
- First attempt, draft, submitting, submitted, needs work, accepted and save failure are distinct states; only a persisted action claims saved or submitted, and a device draft says "Saved on this device. It is not sent until you submit."
- Review feedback is drawn as option A (written feedback and status, which main records) beside option B (per-criterion scores, proposed).
- The rubric wording on the boards is a sample. The production rubric text must be copied verbatim from the exercise's instructions before build.
