## D5996 — Validate's overview read gains V2's parts: evidence, needs attention, hypotheses, recent changes (#5996)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-14 by S12. Filed under #4837. Builds to V1 (VA01, VA01b), V13 (states) and V16 (developer handoff) of `design/incoming/Validate v2 · Boards.dc.html`.
- **No migration.** No frontend change: the V2 page is a separate issue.
- **Additive:** `GET /api/cycles/validate/overview` keeps every key it sent (`cycle`, `question`, `figures`, `cards`, `focus`, `meter`, `index`, `can_read_decisions`) and adds `evidence`, `attention`, `hypotheses` and `changes`. The existing overview tests pass unchanged.
- **Code:** `services/validateOverviewParts.ts`, called once from the route.

### States

Each part is `{ state: 'ok', … }`, or `{ state: 'unavailable', reason }` when a read it needs failed. One part failing leaves the others, and today's keys, as they were (#5129).
- **Not recorded:** a figure whose population is empty (no held interview, no open hypothesis, no done experiment) is `value: null` with a `reason`, never 0.
- **Counted zero:** a count over a population that exists stays a zero.

### `evidence`: the six figures of V1's Evidence row

Each figure carries `label`, `value`, `of` (for "N of M"), `population` (what it counts, in words), `window` ("everything recorded to date") and `detail`.

| key | counts | null when |
|---|---|---|
| `distinct_sources` | held interviews + confirmed demand signals (paid deposits and pilots included) + done experiments with a result | none of them |
| `supporting` / `contradicting` | evidence relationships on open hypotheses, from `claimsOf`; the population says "not distinct sources" (V8) | no open hypothesis |
| `unknown_fit` | held interviews with no `icp_fit`, of all held interviews | no held interview |
| `experiment_results` | done experiments with an observed result, of done experiments, on open hypotheses | no done experiment |
| `demand_signals` | demand signals plus paid deposits and pilots; detail "N confirmed · N to confirm" | none |

### `attention`: Needs attention

At most four items (`ATTENTION_SHOWN`), most useful first; `total` counts every candidate. Each item has a `kind`, a V13 `state`, a `label`, a `detail`, an `action` and a venture-aware `href`.

1. **`reviews`** (writers): interviews awaiting review, from `validateFocus.ts`'s own queue. That loop is now `reviewQueue`, and rule 2 reads its first item, so the item and the focus point at the same session. The queue is walked to 10 sessions (`REVIEW_SCAN`); past that the label says "10 or more". It stops before reading an eleventh session, so the focus's statements are unchanged.
2. **`suggestions`** (writers): Eadwyn's pending placements (`run_id` set). Verdict contributions are left out (below). The href is the module of the first one.
3. **`results`:** done experiments with no result, on open hypotheses, oldest first. The href is the first one's hypothesis.
4. **`mixed`:** hypotheses not decided that have both supporting and contradicting relationships. This is the next-action policy's own test, so there is no second policy.
5. **`fit`:** held interviews with no customer fit. The href is Customer research filtered to `fit:not_recorded`.

A reader who cannot write gets no review or suggestion item, and the actions are "Open experiment" and "Open interviews", never a write.

### `hypotheses`

The open claims in board order, at most 20 (`total` counts all). Each carries:
- `code`, `claim` and its computed `status` (the evidence board's, not the founder's decision);
- `supporting`, `contradicting`, and `sources` (distinct interviews, D4586's coverage, with `interviewees` and `organizations`);
- its newest experiment, with `result_needed` when it is done without a result;
- its href.

### `changes`

The latest 8 `activity_logs` rows across the six modules (`entity_type` `validate.<slug>`): `at`, `module`, `module_name`, `action`, `summary`. They are never `validate.verdict`, and never another venture's or another cycle's rows.

**Coverage, which the issue asked to confirm.** Every Validate write logs through the cycle activity convention (migration 459). Today that covers:
- Problem discovery, Customer research, Market intelligence (fills), Demand signals and Experiments.
- **Not covered:** Ideal customer profile's segment writes, which log no row. A session's transcription is not logged either: the canvas's sample line "session was transcribed" has no row to come from. The feed lists what is logged; adding those rows is a follow-up, not this read.

### Decisions

No new part carries verdict data:
- a claim's status is the board's computation;
- `changes` never reads `validate.verdict`;
- verdict-contribution suggestions are not counted.

A partner (who reads the board but not decisions, D693) and a founder see the same verdict-free parts.
