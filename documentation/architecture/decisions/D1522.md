### Why

Genspark's bar is investor-standard slide order per stage. Ours is a structured stage (Pre-seed, Seed, Series A, Series B+), with:
- a "why this order" line on each card;
- warnings in code when a stage's proof is missing.

The one-click run (#3768, dk-auto AUTO-1) also needs the order and the missing-data cut as a function it can call with no one pressing anything.

### What was decided

1. **Arcs are keyed by stage × investor type** (`services/narrative/arcs.ts`). Each stage has:
   - a card order, title first, from the New deck catalogue;
   - a why line per card, with no figure in it;
   - a weight per card;
   - the cards it requires.

   | Stage | Order | Requires |
   |---|---|---|
   | Pre-seed | title, problem, why now, solution, team, validation, market, product, round, use of funds | problem, team |
   | Seed | title, problem, solution, traction, validation, product, market, why now, team, unit economics, round, use of funds | problem, traction, team |
   | Series A | title, traction, unit economics, market, product, solution, problem, team, competition, roadmap, round, use of funds | traction, unit economics, market, team |
   | Series B+ | title, traction, unit economics, market, competition, product, roadmap, team, cap table, round, use of funds | traction, unit economics, market, team |

   The investor type lifts its `AUDIENCE_EMPHASIS` lead cards to just after the title, as New deck already does. A lifted card's why line names its reader ("Angels read team first."). A lead card the stage does not read is not added.
2. **Proof is records.** A card has proof when one of its deck sources is recorded (`readSources`), or when a pinned evidence item backs it (`EVIDENCE_RULES[].backs` through `CARD_BACKS`). The title and Why now need none.
   - A source that is withheld (bank data for someone other than the founder) or could not be read makes the proof **unchecked**. It is never called missing.
   - If the source read fails entirely, every proof is unchecked.
3. **Warnings, for required cards only:**

   | Code | Example message | Action offered |
   |---|---|---|
   | `missing_card` | "Seed deck with no traction slide" | "Add a traction slide" |
   | `missing_proof` | "Traction has no recorded evidence" | "Add a source" |
   | `unchecked_proof` | "Its records could not be checked here." | "Open its sources" |

   No warning compares the venture with others, so none needs a stage norm (D1589).
4. **`deck.stage` is read in this order:**
   1. the stage asked (`?stage=`);
   2. else the stage the deck's audience implies (angel and pre-seed fund → Pre-seed, seed fund → Seed, Series A fund → Series A);
   3. else the venture's `projects.stage` (idea and validate → Pre-seed, any later series → Series B+);
   4. else Seed, the standard deck.

   `stage_source` says which one decided.
5. **The plan is a service.** `stagePlan(env, …)` in `services/decks/stagePlan.ts` is what the route answers with. The one-click run calls the same function, and then `cutUnproven(plan)`:
   - every card with missing proof is left out with "Left out · add data to include", required cards included;
   - an unchecked card is kept, because the run cannot say its proof is missing;
   - so a deck shown with zero edits never holds a stage-required card with no evidence.
6. **The plan also lists objections:** up to three the venture's investors raised and nobody has answered yet (`objections.ts`). They do not change the order.
7. **The route** is `GET /api/decks/:id/stage-plan?stage=&investor_type=` (`routes/deck_stage.ts`).
   - Who may read it: whoever may read the deck (`projectOwned`).
   - Bank-derived sources are read only for the founder (`mercuryFor`).
   - It returns 400 for a stage or investor type not on the lists.
   - It runs no model, charges nothing and writes nothing.
8. **The client and the swap.**
   - `frontend/src/lib/api/deckStage.js` (`deckStageApi.stagePlan`) is re-exported by one line in `api.js` (D527).
   - `deckV2Adapter.stagePlan` now answers through it, and `SWAPPED` lists `stagePlan`.
   - New deck's Stage chips no longer read "Coming later". No screen changed otherwise.
   - ⋯ › This deck › Stage stays greyed, now on the `deckActions` adapter (#3808): the editor has no stage sheet yet, so the plan is not offered from the menu. The pd23 sheet is #3808's.
   - Per the revision, the stage chips in the Grid header are dropped, and the "one undo step" reorder test moves to #3808.
