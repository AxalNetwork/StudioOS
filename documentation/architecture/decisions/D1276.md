### 1. The range

- **Chips:** Last 12 weeks (the Worker's default, 84 days with today), Last 30 days, This quarter, and Custom.
- **The address carries it:** `/admin/platform/promo-codes/usage`, `?range=30d`, `?range=quarter`, or `?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD`. A reversed range, a day that does not exist or one over 366 days falls back to the 12 weeks, so the page never sends a range the Worker refuses with `invalid_range`.
- **Custom:** a first and a last day, then Apply. A bad range is said in the page ("The first day comes after the last.", "A range is at most 366 days.") and nothing is read.
- Whole days in UTC, both included; weeks start on Monday, as the Worker groups them.

### 2. The four tiles

- **Redemptions:** the range's total, "every source".
- **Discount given:** each currency on its own, joined by "and", never added. Redemptions with no amount are counted in the line under it.
- **Members who used a code:** each member counted once.
- **Average saving:** the first currency's average over the redemptions with a recorded discount, and each other currency's average in the line under it.
- **A missing figure is never 0.** A tile the read did not give says "Not recorded" and "Could not be read."; a period with no redemptions says 0 for the counts, "None given" for the discount and "No redemptions to average." for the average.

### 3. The five blocks

Each block is drawn, said empty in the canvas's words, or failed on its own with Try again. One failed block never blanks the tab. Each chart has View as table, which draws the same rows.

- **Redemptions per week:** stacked columns by source (Cart orders, One-time payments, Free unlocks, Subscriptions, Not recorded) over every week the Worker lists, so a week with none is a measured 0. The table names the week holding today "This week, to date". Under it, from `detail_recorded_from`: "Source is recorded on each redemption from …; N redemptions before that are Not recorded."
- **Discount given, per currency:** each currency's total, redemptions and average, never added. See §5 for the canvas's chart by week.
- **Top codes:** bars of the top ten, each "44 of 100" or "9, no cap", and "WEBSUMMIT was redeemed most: 44 of its 100." A code the mirror no longer holds is named by its Stripe id.
- **By plan and product:** bars of the top twenty, "name · interval", and a dashed "Not recorded" row for a cart of several products and every redemption from before migration 631.
- **Codes near their end:** at their cap first, then soonest, each "N of M" with "ends in X days" or "At its cap", and Open to the code's drawer at its address (`codeHref`). Every active code, whatever the range; paused codes are left out, as the Worker reads them.

**Empty, in the canvas's words:** "Nothing was redeemed in this period.", "No discount was given in this period.", "No code was redeemed in this period.", "Nothing to break down in this period.", "No redemptions to average.". A block whose `reason` is `not_recorded` says that the redemptions carry no amount or product. A block whose `reason` is `unreadable` is a failed read with Try again, never an empty period.

**Export CSV** downloads the range's redemptions (`GET /export.csv`, audited by the Worker). A refused export is said beside the button.

### 4. Dark and the phone (PRM8, PRM13)

- **Dark:** violet is `#a78bfa` on the chips, links and the charts (the one palette, D915 §6); the Not recorded marks are the muted dashed stroke, which stays visible on the dark card.
- **Phone:** tiles two a row, then one block per screen width (`grid-cols-1`, two columns from `lg`). The charts size to their box (`useBoxWidth`).

### 5. What is not built

- **Discount given per week, per currency (PRM7's second chart).** The usage read gives each currency's total for the period, not by week. The block says so with "Not read here yet" (`WAITS.discountWeeks`) rather than drawing a chart from figures the read does not carry. Drawing it needs the Worker to group discount by week and currency.
- **The Code and Applies to filters** on the canvas's range bar. The Worker takes `promo=` (one code) but no scope filter; neither is offered yet.
