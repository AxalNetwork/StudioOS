## D5347 — Noindex routes do not belong in the sitemap (#5347)

`/login` is deliberately `noindex, follow` under D4582, but the sitemap still submitted it to search engines. The sitemap and the indexing directive therefore described incompatible intent and produced a Search Console warning.

The sitemap no longer lists `/login`; `/register` remains listed and indexable. The rule is registry-wide rather than specific to one URL: every route whose `robots` value contains `noindex` must be absent from `frontend/public/sitemap.xml`. `frontend/public/robots.txt` continues to allow `/login`, because crawlers must be able to fetch the page to observe its meta and header directives.

`frontend/test/auth_route_meta_4582.test.mjs` reads the exported registry and sitemap, proves the rule has at least one live example, checks every noindex route, preserves `/register`, and verifies that robots.txt does not block `/login`. Restoring the `/login` sitemap entry makes the test fail. No Worker behavior, route, migration or production record changes.
