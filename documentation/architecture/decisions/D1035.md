### 1. Two addresses, one drawer over its tab

`/my/events/ticket/:id` draws Upcoming with the ticket drawer over it; `/my/events/hosting/:id` draws Hosting with the hosted-event drawer. Both use `Lightbox`, so a drawer traps focus, closes on Escape or Close back to its tab, and returns focus to what opened it. A bad id draws the tab with no drawer.

### 2. The ticket (MEV3)

- **The place** comes from the list: Upcoming's `attending`, then Past's first page. A place older than that page falls back to `GET /api/events/:id`'s own registration. With neither, the drawer says "You hold no place at this event." and offers the event page.
- **A held seat** shows the QR, its code in mono, and "Show this at the door. The host scans it to check you in."; when and where, with "Open in maps" for a venue or the join link (Copy, Join) for an online event, for seat holders only; Add to calendar (Google, Outlook, `.ics`) from D1033's read; the seat ("Paid ticket · $40 · Paid.", "Free seat · invitation", and so on); the agenda from `GET /api/events/:id`; the host; and "Cancel my place".
- **Four states with no code:**
  - Pay to confirm · €15, with Pay now. It goes to the event page `/events/:id`, where the existing checkout lives; the drawer opens no checkout of its own.
  - Waitlist · #n: "You’re third on the waitlist. You’ll be told if a seat opens.", with Leave the waitlist.
  - Waiting for the host.
  - Declined by the host, with the host's reason when one was given. A declined or cancelled place says "It is in Past."
- **The host's name is in no read,** so the host section says "The host’s name is not read here yet." rather than inventing one.
- A failed calendar or agenda read fails alone and says so; the code and the `.ics` still stand.

### 3. The hosted event (MEV5)

- **The gate line** is D1036's, with lines for a draft and a sent-back event.
- **What to do next,** one block per status: a draft (Edit details, and Send for review or Publish, opening MEV6); a past draft ("Its start date has passed…", Edit details); sent back ("Sent back by an admin." and the review note; no date, because none is stored); waiting for review ("Submitted 5 Nov."); published ahead (Invite people, Registrations), on the day (Check-in is open, with progress, Check in), or ended ("31 of 42 came.", Download roster); cancelled.
- **Four tiles** from part 2's counts: seats ("42 of 60"), waitlist, to approve, and checked in, which is a dash and "Not held yet" until the day. Counts the list does not carry say "Not read here yet", never 0.
- **Who may register** in words from `audience_rules`: everyone, selected profiles, or invite only; free seats; approval.
- **The four host pages:** Details, Who may register, Registrations, Agenda (and Check-in on the day), each a literal link; and Cancel event.

### 4. The dialogs (MEV6, MEV7)

Every change opens a dialog that says what it changes and who is told. A refusal shows the Worker's message and the dialog stays open; a success closes the drawer, re-reads the list and says what happened in a toast.

| Dialog | Route | Who is told |
|---|---|---|
| Send X for review? (public) | `POST /api/events/:id/submit-review` | "Admins see it in their review queue. No one else is told." |
| Publish X? (unlisted, private) | the same | Publishing sends no notice; a private event names its open invitations |
| Cancel your place? | `DELETE /api/events/:id/registration` | the next waitlisted person; the host sees it in Registrations |
| Leave the waitlist? | the same | the host sees it in Registrations; no notice |
| Cancel X? | `POST /api/events/:id/cancel {note}` | "N people registered and M people waitlisted get a notice.", with an optional note |

- **The paid-seat box** shows on cancel my place only for a paid seat (`payment_status = 'paid'` and an amount): StudioOS does not refund a place you cancel.
- **The paid-ticket box** shows on cancel event for a priced event; after cancelling, the toast gives the reply's count and amount and says the refunds went to the Events desk.
- A Hosting row whose action is Send for review or Publish opens the same dialog in place.

### 5. MEV17

- A hosted-event address for an event the viewer does not host draws "{title} is not an event you host." and "Only the host and studio admins manage this event." An admin manages any event; a 403 or 404 read draws the card with no title.
- **The Explorer card** is built and kept behind `MY_EVENTS_OPEN_TO_EXPLORERS = true`, for the day the owner closes My events to Explorers. Today an Explorer reaches My events, as D1030 decided.

### 6. The rows

Upcoming's next event and Going rows have "Show ticket"; a Hosting row's title opens its drawer; Discover's held place reads "Show ticket"; and the notices from D1031 now open the drawers instead of the tabs.
