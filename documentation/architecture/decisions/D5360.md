## D5360 — Spin-Out Lab: a lost graduation bonus is restored on schedule, and every issuing path asks one hold (#5360)

- **Status:** built by S09. Follow-up to D4663 part 2 (#5257), whose "Not done" list named both items.
- **Migration:** `202611100844_lab_graduation_bonus_backfill.sql` (additive: one new table, four nullable columns on the tick row).

### Decision

1. **One hold for every path that issues a Lab credential or its bonus.**
   - `labIssuanceHold(env, nowMs?)` in `services/certificateIssuance.ts` answers "may this studio issue now?". These paths call it:
     - `POST /certificates` (the admin's Issue);
     - `POST /certificates/backfill` ("Issue all eligible");
     - the every-minute `runCertificateBackfillTick`;
     - `issueIfGraduated` in `services/labProgress.ts`, reached from the final milestone and from the progress reconciler.
   - **The rule:**
     - a suspended branch (D511) holds as `frozen`;
     - a licence that does not hold the Spin-Out Lab (D985) holds as `not_granted`;
     - grants that cannot be read hold as `grants_unreadable`.
     - HQ (no `BRANCH_CODE`) is never held.
   - **On a held branch, the final milestone is still recorded.** The step, its week advance and its own milestone credit are the recorder's, and they are unchanged. Only the credential and the graduation bonus wait.
   - **When the hold lifts, the scheduled backfill issues both.** It picks a graduate by the rule with no certificate, issues once, and pays the bonus once.
   - **The admin routes still run `requireBranchNotSuspended` first.** Its 423 body carries the suspension's date and reason, which the shell draws. The hold then answers the program refusal's 403 or 503 unchanged, so the refusal bodies are the same as before.
   - Before this, `issueIfGraduated` checked none of the three, so a suspended or ungranted branch issued a credential and paid its bonus at the final step.

2. **The same tick pays a lost graduation bonus.**
   - **Who:** a graduate holding a certificate with `status = 'issued'` and no ledger grant with source ref `lab:<user>:graduation`.
     - This is the case where the credential was issued and the request (or a tick) died before `creditLabGraduation` ran. The certificate step never picks such a graduate, because they hold a certificate.
     - **A revoked credential is never paid.** Only `issued` counts.
   - **How:** each graduate goes through `creditLabGraduation`, the same call the live path and D541's admin credit backfill make. Its own rules still apply: the payee must be an admitted member whose incorporation step was paid.
   - **Bounded:** at most `CERT_BACKFILL_TICK_LIMIT` (3) per tick. This is its own step, so a tick touches at most three graduates for the certificate and three for the bonus.
     - The `LIMIT` is bound as limit + 1; the extra row answers "more are due".
     - Untried graduates go first.
     - A graduate whose last try paid nothing waits `CERT_BACKFILL_RETRY_MS` (six hours), so one who cannot be paid never holds the queue.
   - **Idempotent:** the ledger's unique `(user_id, kind, source_ref)` key, plus the candidate read's `NOT EXISTS`. A second tick finds nobody.
   - **Held with the tick.** A held tick runs neither step.
   - **Recorded:**
     - `spinout_graduation_bonus_backfill_attempts` has one row per graduate tried: attempts, last outcome, first and last try, and when it paid.
     - The tick row gains `bonus_scanned`, `bonus_paid`, `bonus_skipped` and `bonus_more_waiting`.
     - A failed candidate read throws, as the certificate step's does, so the cron row reads `partial`.
   - **Why a sibling table, not columns on `spinout_certificate_backfill_attempts`.** That table's row means "the certificate backfill tried this graduate", and its `last_outcome` is `NOT NULL`. There is no honest value for that column when another path issued the certificate. SQLite cannot relax a `NOT NULL` without rebuilding the table.

3. **The Graduation desk says what the bonus step paid.**
   - `GET /certificates` → `scheduled_backfill.last_tick.bonus` is `{ scanned, paid, skipped, more_waiting }`.
     - It is `null` on a tick recorded before this migration. The line then says nothing about bonuses, never "0 paid".
   - `scheduled_backfill.bonus_not_paid` counts the holders still owed the bonus, by their last outcome.
   - The desk line adds two things:
     - "lost graduation bonuses: N paid of M tried";
     - who is still owed, and why.

### Tests

`test/lab_certificate_backfill_tick_4663.test.ts` (6 new; the existing 8 now expect the `bonus` field):
- a credential with no bonus row is paid once, a second tick pays nothing, and the tick row carries the count;
- a holder whose credential was revoked is not paid;
- the bound holds in SQL (limit + 1 rows read), says more are due, and drains;
- a holder who cannot be paid (not admitted) is not retried within six hours, is counted in `bonus_not_paid`, and is paid once the cause clears;
- a frozen tick pays no bonus, and the first tick after the hold lifts pays it;
- a tick recorded before the bonus columns existed reads its bonus as `null`.

`test/lab_services_4663.test.ts` (4 new):
- **The final milestone through `POST /milestone`,** in three cases: a suspended branch, a licence without the Lab, and grants that cannot be read. In each case:
  - every step is recorded, and no credential or bonus is issued;
  - `issueIfGraduated` (the reconciler's path) issues nothing;
  - the tick reports the hold;
  - once the hold lifts, one tick issues the credential (naming the venture) and its bonus;
  - a second tick does nothing.
- **Both admin routes refuse unreadable grants through the same hold** (503 `program_grants_unreadable`).

`frontend/test/admin_lab_desks_d970.test.mjs` (1 new): the desk line's bonus part, its `null` case and the owed reasons.

`spinout_certificates_admin_d382.test.ts` applies the new migration and builds `perk_credit_ledger` in its hand-built schema.

### Not done

- **The milestone credits themselves are not held on a frozen or ungranted branch.** Only the credential and the graduation bonus are. The issue scoped the hold to those two.
- **The progress reconciler is unchanged.** It still walks only founders whose Lab is on. After graduation, the scheduled backfill is what issues on a branch whose hold has lifted.
