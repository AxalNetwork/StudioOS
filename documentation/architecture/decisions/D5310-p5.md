## D5310 part 5 — Recent network activity on the founder overview, from the recorded-events read (#5310)

Part 5 of #5310. Part 1 (`D5310.md`) drew NW1's **Recent network activity** as Not recorded, because no single read assembled it, and filed the read as Backend required (#6557). D6557 built it: `GET /api/relationships/activity?limit=` returns `{ events, sources, complete }` from the caller's own follow-ups, introductions, LinkedIn imports and duplicate reviews. This part draws the card from that read. No Worker change.

### What the card shows

- **The read:** `relationshipsApi.activity(5)`, the overview's ninth source (`SOURCES.activity`), loaded and retried on its own like the others.
- **Each row:** the recorded action, an import's count ("Contacts imported · 118 added"), and its time ("2026-10-19 16:20"), newest first, at most five. A glyph marks the kind: ✓ done or accepted, ✓✓ made, → requested, × declined, ⇩ imported, ⇄ duplicate review.
- **A source the read could not reach** is named above the list ("Introductions could not be read"), with Retry. With rows: the list may be missing that source's events. With none: "This is not an empty history", and the empty state is not drawn.
- **Nothing recorded, every source read:** "Nothing recorded yet", with what appears here.
- **The whole read failing:** Could not be read with Retry, "This is not an empty history".
- **A refresh that fails** keeps the earlier list and says it is out of date; the page's stale banner names Recent activity.
