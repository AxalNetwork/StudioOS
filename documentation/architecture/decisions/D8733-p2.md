## D8733 part 2 — The firm feed built: every alert opens its item, the overflow is disclosed by kind, and the way back keeps the view (#8733)

- **Status:** built for #8733 from the landed canvas (`design/incoming/Studio Home Operating Partner Page.dc.html`, boards SOP00 to SOP13) and D8733. The owner accepted option A for Q1, Q2 and Q3 on 2026-11-27: the full feed expands in the module, the phone gets a jump link under the firm line, and the summary reserves one row per kind present. Frontend, plus the one Worker field the canvas marked ◇. No migration, no new route, no `api.js` method.
- **Builds on:** D8733 (the canvas and the open questions), D394 (the feed's reads and receipts), D4620 (the partial-read qualifier and retry), D4822 (`?engagement=` focus on Health and Capacity, the Engagement Sheet on Board), #4709 (the board's 200-row pages), D5615 (no hub file touched).

### What was built

- **The model** is `frontend/src/pages/partner/firmFeedModel.js`, pure over the three reads and a `now`. `homeFeed` now keeps every alert (it was `out.slice(0, 6)`), each with its kind (`blk`, `risk`, `due`, `cap`, `ren`, `seat`), its client first, its receipt, an action that names the destination and an accessible name with the client ("Open engagement: Kestrel Freight"). Within a kind the most pressing come first: blocked and at risk by overdue milestones, then open blockers, then name; due by the next milestone's date; over cap by how far over; renewing by date.
- **The summary (Q3 A).** `feedSummary` keeps six rows, reserves the most pressing row of each kind present, fills the rest in feed order, and returns them in feed order. With the audit's test (six blocked, a milestone due tomorrow, a renewal) the summary is four blocked, the due milestone and the renewal, and the disclosure reads "2 more not shown · 2 blocked: Marlow Bio, Tidewater Robotics".
- **Kind chips** above the rows count every alert as toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`); kinds with none say "At risk 0 · Over cap 0" in words. Pressing a chip opens the list filtered to that kind.
- **Show all (Q1 A)** expands the module in place, grouped by kind under `h4` headings, with an All chip, Show fewer (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) and a polite count line ("Showing 1 of 8 alerts · Renewing"). The view is kept in `/studio?feed=all|blk|risk|due|cap|ren|seat`. Show all moves focus to the first row it revealed; Show fewer returns it to the button. The module link "Open the feed" (to Delivery · Health) is removed, and the module note reads "8 alerts · 6 shown".
- **Destinations.** Blocked and at risk open `/delivery/board?engagement=<uid>` (the Engagement Sheet); due opens `/delivery/health?engagement=<uid>` (D4822's focus bar); over cap opens `/delivery/capacity?person=<user_id>` (new: `usePersonFocus`, `focusPeople`, `PersonFocusBar` in `delivery/engagementFocus.jsx`); a renewal opens `/pipeline/retainers?engagement=<uid>`, which now opens that retainer's sheet from the URL; a seat opens `/delivery/capacity?engagement=<uid>`. Each link carries `from=studio`.
- **The way back.** `from=studio` draws "Back to Studio · Firm feed" above every partner zone (`FromStudioBack.jsx`, mounted once in `PartnerBucketRoutes.jsx` for zones, not bucket roots) and inside the Engagement Sheet and the retainer sheet, after Close. Opening an alert first replaces the Studio entry with `/studio?feed=<view>&alert=<key>#firm-feed` and keeps a return record (`feedReturn.js`, sessionStorage), so the browser's Back and the link return to the same view. On return the feed focuses the action used and says "Back from Kestrel Freight. Your view of the feed was kept." If the item has left the feed, focus goes to the alert now in its place and the line says why ("Kestrel Freight is no longer blocked, so it left the feed. Focus is on the next alert.").
- **Fallbacks (SOP08).** Board no longer opens nothing silently for a key outside the loaded rows: it reads on through the book's pages until the key turns up or every page is read, then says "<client> is not on your firm’s board … Nothing was opened. It may have been removed since your Studio loaded, or it is not one of this firm’s engagements", with Back to Studio and Show all engagements. A removed engagement and another firm's read the same, so the copy does not say which. Capacity with an unknown `?person=` narrows to nothing: "Showing a person this roster has no record for · 0 of N people". A removed retainer record keeps main's sheet ("This engagement is not recorded as a retainer"). An engagement missing from the retainer read draws the same notice as Board.
- **Partial data (SOP09).** A kind whose read failed draws "<Kind> · Unavailable" (`aria-disabled`), never 0; the module note adds "Partial · 2 of 3 reads"; D4620's qualifier and retry stay, and its sentence is fixed: "The delivery board could not be read" (main built "The feed's the delivery board").
- **Confirmed empty (SOP10 C)** adds the read time and the zeros in words: "All three reads answered at 09:12 today. Blocked 0 · At risk 0 · Due this week 0 · Over cap 0 · Renewing 0." `usePartnerHome` records `readAt`. Loading, unavailable, withheld and not recorded are unchanged.
- **Phone (Q2 A, SOP11A).** `FeedJumpLink` sits under the firm line, below `md` only, while `firm_feed` is placed and a read answered: "Firm feed · 8 alerts / 6 blocked · 1 due this week · 1 renewing", jumping to `#firm-feed`. Row actions and chips are 44 px on a phone and 36 px from `sm` up.
- **Worker (the canvas's ◇).** `GET /partner/delivery/board` rows carry `next_milestone_title` and `next_milestone_due_at`: the earliest open milestone dated today or later (`nextOpenMilestone` in `routes/partner_delivery.ts`; the board's milestone read now selects `m.title`, still bound to the acting firm). The due alert reads "1 milestone due this week" and, under it, "Pricing workshop · due Fri 27 Nov, tomorrow".

### Departures from the canvas

- **Embedded seats are a sixth kind.** Main's feed lists embedded seats and the canvas does not draw them. They stay, as "Embedded" with a chip shown only when one exists, their own reserved summary row and the action "Open seats" (Capacity narrowed to the engagement, as SOP13's handoff routes them).
- **No printed destination paths.** SOP03 prints each row's route "for review". The build does not print URLs to the partner; the action label names the destination, the accessible name adds the client, and the link carries the item.
- **Partial · 2 of 3 reads** is part of the module note, not a separate tag: the Studio module frame has one note.
- **The phone chips** are the same chips as desktop (full names, no All chip until the list is expanded), not SOP11B's shortened "Due" with All.
- **Kind glyphs** are lucide icons (square-minus, triangle, clock, gauge, refresh, circle; a crossed circle for Unavailable), always with a word.
- **The jump link** shows below 768 px (`md`), so a narrow tablet gets it too, not only the 390 frame.
- **The return status line** clears on the next chip or Show all press, not on any action.
- **The retainer list's missing-engagement notice** is not drawn in the canvas; it reuses SOP08 A's words with "retainer list".
