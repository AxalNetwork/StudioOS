## D9112 — Licensee initial invoice on Statements (#9112)

D5955 board L46 places the one-off initial invoice above annual licence-fee invoices and royalty statements on `/admin/my-licence/statements`. The invoice amounts, status, reference, dates and PDF come from the existing licence-scoped `GET /api/licence/mine/statements` response. The licensee uses the same issued-state and currency formatting rules as HQ Billing. The Worker keeps a failed invoice read separate from the rest of the response. The page shows its reason and lets the administrator retry.

The licensee response has no regulatory class. The surcharge line uses the recorded amount and the label “Regulatory surcharge”. The card uses a compact item and amount list with metadata alongside it on desktop and stacked on phones. The existing royalty detail, payment instructions and usage reports follow the invoice sections.
