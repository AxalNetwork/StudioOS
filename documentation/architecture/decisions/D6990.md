### The two scoring models keep their own counts

**Six and nine are two models.**
- The venture score has six dimensions (`DIMENSIONS`, `lib/scoringViewModel.js`).
- The founder profile has nine (`FOUNDER_DIMENSIONS`, `lib/founderDimensions.js`).

**Where each count comes from.** Every line reads its count from the module that defines its model, so neither count is typed. No line calls the venture score nine-dimensional.

**Advisor matching.** It reads the founder profile, so the Support row counts that model.

### The printed brief

**What is printed.** The brief prints through its print stylesheet (D141), from the same `BriefDocument`. So the printed brief carries the same corrected lines, with no separate printed copy to keep in step.

**The Programme length tile.** It is drawn by the page wrapper around `BriefDocument`, and it prints with it.

### What stays

- **"Revenue proof", capital R,** is the name of the Revenue tool in the arsenal. It is a tool, not a claim, so the case-sensitive scan of "revenue proof" leaves it.
- **"Warm intro prep with partner network"** is an activity in the Pitch week. It promises no count, and the week names and their content are #6991 (X9).
