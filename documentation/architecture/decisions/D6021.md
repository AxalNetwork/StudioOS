### What the canvas decides

- On Record a decision (V17), a rail task (#5130), not a second panel, reads only the evidence offered for this decision.
- It sorts that evidence into Contradicts (listed first), Supports, Checked but not counted (with why), and What would change this (the disproof, success bar and deadline).
- Every line cites one evidence row (E1…). It never picks Proceed, Pivot or Stop; the picker stays the page's primary action.
- A coverage sentence says how many distinct sources count, and that a decision below the bar is kept as Withheld.
- Use as reasoning copies the text into the Reasoning field, where the founder edits it. Copy and Discard sit beside it.
- The summary has seven states (V18), including Eadwyn off, not available with nothing charged, and out of date.
- Under option B, a given decision is shared with named Operating Partners and investors (V19). They read a read-only page with no edit and no Eadwyn actions (V20). A Withheld decision cannot be shared; a superseded one shows a banner and never the later decision.
