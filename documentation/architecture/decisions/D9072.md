## D9072 — Deals › Closing: /deals/closing opens to the round's lead Operating Partner (#9072)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-27. Part of #6866. Builds D6158 Q1, answered A on 2026-11-16 (`D6158-p2.md`): the round's lead Operating Partner opens `/deals/closing` for the rounds they lead. D6866 left this as the one route change it did not make.
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Deals v2 Closing Workspace Page.dc.html`, the Checklist board ("You are the round's lead", Q1 · A) and its footnote: "Anyone other than the round's lead or an Admin sees 'Only the round's lead or an Admin changes the checklist.'"

### Who opens the workspace

| Reader | List at `/deals/closing` | `?deal=<id>` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admin | every round at closing, as before | the workspace, as before |
| Investor | the rounds they are related to (`scope=mine`), as before | the read-only workspace, as before |
| Operating Partner who leads the round (`deals.lead_partner_id`) | the rounds they lead, and no other | the workspace |
| Any other Operating Partner, including the lead of a different round | — | "Only the round's lead Operating Partner or an Admin opens this closing workspace." and no workspace |

A round with no lead is led by no one, so no partner opens it. While the list is read the answer waits (Reading…); a failed read is Unavailable with Try again, never a refusal and never an admission. A partner with no round at closing reads "You lead no round at closing."

### The route guard and the Worker agree

- **Frontend.** The `/deals/closing` guard in `App.jsx` is `admin`, `partner`, `investor`. `InvestorDealsRoutes` already pins the investor shell for a partner (as for `/deals/commit`, D6154), and Closing joins Commit in the partner's Deals nav. `ClosingZone.jsx` narrows the partner's list and refuses any other round through `closingSeatModel.js` (`closingDealsFor`, `closingSeat`), the one place the rule is written for the browser.
- **Worker.** `services/dealClosingAccess.ts` (`closingWorkspaceRefusal`) sits on top of `loadDealForRead`, the narrowest existing gate for a deal read, and adds `canMoveRound` (D1242), the same lead check the checklist writes use. It narrows only the `staff` audience held through the partner licence: an Admin passes; the lead passes in their own session; a support session acting as the lead is refused `support_session_refused`; a partner who is not the lead but is the venture's founder or a related investor reads through that relationship, as before; anyone else holding the partner licence is refused 403 `not_your_closing`. `dealAudience` is unchanged, so the Studio lens and the deal page are untouched.

### Which read the Worker narrows, and why only that one

The Worker rule is applied to the workspace's own read: `GET /api/deals/:id/closing-checklist/events`, the history behind the Audit tab, which nothing but the workspace calls. Before this change every Operating Partner read it on every deal.

The workspace's other reads are shared with surfaces that already admit every Operating Partner, and keep their rule:

- `GET /deals/:id/closing-checklist` and `GET /deals/:id/transfers` are read by the deal page's closing room, which #2424 question 3 opens to every Operating Partner (D1213: "the checklist, the transfers and the confirmed total"). Narrowing them would take the checklist and transfers out of that room for every partner who does not lead the round, which is a different owner decision.
- `GET /legal/esign` is scoped at its route to the caller's own paper; `GET /ic/conditions` is decision-scoped (D461); `GET /deals/:id/transfer-gate` is an Admin's; `GET /closings` gives a partner no closing record.

So a partner who does not lead the round gets nothing from the workspace that the closing room does not already give them, and the one read only the workspace serves refuses them.

### What the lead sees and does

Following the canvas, the lead:

- edits the checklist: status, owner, due date and note in the side sheet, and applies a closing template from the list (the routes' `canMoveRound`, unchanged);
- reads Paper (the paper they sent or sign, as the signing archive scopes it), Conditions (read only, with Open in Investment committee), Transfer and Audit;
- does not record a transfer ("An Admin records transfers.", D1215) and does not close.

An Operating Partner reads no closing record (D1213, CP19), so the workspace does not ask for one. The deadline and total say "Not read here", and the close panel says the closing record is the studio team's and its deadline, total, lines and close rules are not shown. Before, a partner would have read "No closing record is open on this deal", which would be false when one is open.

### Unchanged

- The investor's view: the read-only workspace, no Audit tab, their own line. #8838's Open my closing room (PR #8922, open) touches the investor's line only and does not overlap.
- The Admin's view, the transfer gate and Record transfer.
- `dealAudience`, the deal list (`GET /api/deals`) and the closing room.

### Where this departs from the canvas

- **The Audit tab.** The canvas's Audit footnote reads "Admins read this tab". D6866 drew the tab for everyone but investors, and the lead keeps it here, since it is the history of the checklist they keep. Owner question below.

### Owner questions

1. **Does the lead Operating Partner keep the Audit tab?** A: yes, as built (the history of the checklist they keep). B: Admins only, as the canvas footnote reads; the lead's view drops the tab and the Worker refuses the events read to every partner. Recommended: A.

B: the closing room also opens the checklist and transfers only to the round's lead, and the Worker narrows `GET /deals/:id/closing-checklist` and `/transfers` for partners. Recommended: A.
