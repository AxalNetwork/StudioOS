## D6274 — The investor's Markets card states the owner's thesis answer and stops citing closed #1023 (#6274)

**Status:** built (2026-11-27). Copy only; no behaviour changes.

### Background

`/research/markets` shows an investor the `markets:investor` store-gap card ("Market readings are stored. Theses are not, yet."). Its reason said whether a thesis belongs to the investor or the firm was "the owner's decision, asked on #1023". #1023 closed on 2026-10-06 with that question (question 5) unanswered, so the card pointed at a closed issue for a decision nobody had taken.

#6274 asked questions 4 and 5 of #1023 again. The owner answered on 2026-11-15:

- **Q4: B.** Clear history hides rather than deletes. That was built by D6272 (#6272, PR #6640).
- **Q5: A.** A thesis is the investor's own (`research_theses` plus `research_thesis_deals`); a firm share can come later.

### What changed

- **The `markets:investor` card** (`frontend/src/workspaces/noStoreCopy.js`):
  - The eyebrow is now `Blocked on a store`, not `Blocked on a decision`. The decision is taken; the store is what is missing.
  - The reason states the answer and cites #6274: "The owner has decided that a thesis is yours, not your firm's (#6274); the store that would hold one is not built yet, and nothing here invents one."
  - The heading, `blocks` and `what` are unchanged.
- **A guard** (`frontend/test/investor_research_d495.test.mjs`): every string exported by `noStoreCopy.js`, `INVESTOR_ZONE_ACTIONS` and `INVESTOR_ZONE_FILTERS` is checked, and the test fails if any cites `#1023`. Code comments are not checked; several keep #1023 as history.

### Checked, nothing to change

- `investorZoneActions.js`: Clear history is a live handler since D6272 and has no note. The Network rows that cited #1023 (questions 7 and 8) no longer carry it on main.
- `investorZoneFilters.js`: no exported string cites #1023.

### Out of scope

The thesis store itself (Q5 A: the two tables, the deal link, the Active, Parked, Retired and Builder filters) is a separate build issue. Until it ships, the card stays.
