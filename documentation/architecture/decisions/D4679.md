## D4679 — A priced line keeps its price, its rate and its cut; earnings say what happened to the money (#4679)

- **Status:** built by S13, 2026-11-07 (#4679, Practice audit 1/6). No migration.
- **Already on main:** the sent-deliverable recipient and the client receipts are D4684 (#4745). They are not touched here.

### The defects

1. `PATCH /api/advisors/me/bookings/:id/billing` recomputed `platform_cut_cents` and `take_rate_bps` from today's global rate on every call. Marking a line priced at 15% as collected after the rate moved to 12% rewrote its rate and its cut.
2. A new price rode along on any status update. Nothing said "reprice".
3. `POST /api/advisors/bookings/:id/pay` took the application fee from today's rate, not the line's.
4. `/me/ledger` summed every booking, including cancelled and missed sessions nobody collected on, and the page called the net of everything priced "You received".
5. A period note could be saved under a quarter other than the one it was drafted for, and a slow ledger, note or model response for an earlier quarter could land under the newer one.

### The rule

1. **Price, rate and cut are a snapshot.** They are written in exactly two cases: the line had no price and now has one, or the advisor sent `reprice: true`. A status-only update keeps all three.
2. **Repricing is on purpose.** A different `amount_cents` on a priced line without `reprice: true` is 409 `reprice_required`. `reprice` needs `amount_cents` and must be a boolean (400). Taking a price away is a repricing. A `collected` or `written_off` line cannot be repriced at all (409 `settled_price`). A repricing restamps the rate and cut at the rate in force then.
3. **The write is a compare-and-swap** on the state and amount the request read; a changed row is 409 `booking_changed`.
4. **Payment uses the stored fee.** `chargeSession` takes the line's `take_rate_bps` and `platform_cut_cents` and never reads the setting. A missing or impossible snapshot (not whole numbers, a cut above the gross) is `FeeSnapshotMissing`. The pay route checks it before a Stripe customer is created and answers 409 `fee_snapshot_missing`. The advisor fixes it by confirming the price (`reprice: true`).
5. **Earnings say where the money stands.** `/me/ledger` has `states`: `billed`, `collected`, `written_off`, `unpriced`, `refunds_owed`, `refunded`, `paid_out_cents` and `void_sessions`. `totals.gross_cents` is what was priced; `totals.received_cents` is the collected net and nothing else. A cancelled or missed session that was never collected leaves every total and is counted in `void_sessions`; one that was collected stays (the money is real) and shows in `refunds_owed` until an operator resolves the row, then in `refunded`. `/me/earnings` and `/me/tax-summary` leave the same sessions out. The page labels only the collected net "You received".
6. **A note belongs to its period.** `PUT /me/period-notes/:key` with a `period_key` other than `:key` is 409 `period_mismatch` and writes nothing. The page sends it always, and files text only against the period it was drafted or loaded for.
7. **The newest request wins.** The ledger, note and model-draft requests take a ticket; a response whose ticket is no longer current is dropped. A period change cancels any draft still in flight and clears the old figures.

### What it does not do

- Nothing here moves money. `settlement` is unchanged.
- A line billed before the rate columns existed has no snapshot. It stays `null` (shown as not recorded); it is not stamped from today's rate.
- A payout is not tied to the bookings it paid, so `paid_out_cents` is the window's paid payouts, not a per-line state.
