## D5037 — A refund is looked up and reviewed before money moves, and an unreadable policy needs an audited override (#5037)

#5037 (slot S17). Revenue and the Billing console each had a raw refund form: paste an id, an amount and a reason, press Refund, pass the step-up. The Worker's gates were strong, but the operator never saw the payment before money moved, and the policy lookup failed open: a Stripe or D1 read that failed, or an incorporation or booking that could not be found, returned "allowed, kind unknown".

### Decision

1. **Lookup → Review → Confirm → Step-up → Refund.** `POST /api/admin/billing/refund/review` (Super Admin; a read, so no step-up) resolves the PaymentIntent or Charge in Stripe, GET only, and answers the identifiers, the customer and the local account it maps to, gross, refunded to date, what is left, the amount asked and whether it is all of it, the product kind, the policy decision with its note and whether an override is needed, and the referral clawback it would cause (`previewReferralClawback`, read-only). It moves no money and writes no row.
2. **The refund needs the review.** The review returns a `review_token`: an HMAC (keyed from `JWT_SECRET`, domain-separated) over the target, the amount, what was left, the currency and the policy state, for the reviewing admin, for ten minutes. `POST /refund` refuses a request without one (`review_required`), a token another admin made or that was altered (`review_invalid`), an expired one (`review_expired`), and a body that names its own target or amount (`review_fixed`): the operator confirms what they reviewed, or reviews again.
3. **The write re-checks with the review's code.** Before Stripe is asked to refund, the write re-reads the payment and re-decides the policy through the same functions (`services/refundReview.ts`), and refuses with `review_stale` when what was left, the currency or the policy state moved. A partial refund cannot exceed the authoritative remaining (refused at review with `over_remaining`, and re-checked at write). A full refund sends exactly the reviewed remaining.
4. **Three policy states.** `decideRefundPolicy` answers allowed, blocked or **unavailable**. Unavailable is a context it could not read or find (an incorporation or booking row missing or unreadable, a booking with no session time); it is never read as allowed. A charge with no product kind is allowed by the default rule and says so. Blocked and unavailable both need `override_policy: true` with a written `override_reason` (at least `REFUND_REASON_MIN` characters); both are recorded in Stripe metadata (`policy_state`, `policy_override`, `policy_unavailable`, `override_reason`) and on the audit row with the review's figures.
5. **Idempotency.** The Stripe idempotency key is the caller's, or derived from the review's nonce, so a double click or retry of one review is one refund; a second refund of the same charge needs a second review. The UI disables Confirm while the request runs and spends the review on success.
6. **One component.** `frontend/src/components/RefundReviewFlow.jsx` is the refund form on Revenue's Billing exceptions and on the Billing console (with the Stripe category and account fields). The console's old form multiplied the typed amount by 100, the bug D5029 removed from Revenue; it is gone with the form.

### Kept

TOTP session, recent step-up and the Super Admin elevation (`requireSuperAdminWriteBar`), the written reason, the `logAdminAction` audit row, and the referral clawback after the refund, audited with its outcome.

### Verified

`billing_refund_review_5037` (12): the lookup moves nothing and shows every figure; a raw identifier is refused with Stripe never asked; another admin's, a tampered and an expired review are refused; a stale step-up cannot confirm; partial over the remaining refused, partial and full sent exactly; a refund in between makes the review stale; one review, one idempotency key; blocked needs override and its reason, recorded; unavailable is never allowed and needs an audited override; a failed lookup refuses; the clawback is audited; the evaluator's booking window and default rule. `billing_refund_governed_d224` and `billing_refund_currency_5029` now go through the review. `refund_review_flow_5037` (5) holds the UI.
