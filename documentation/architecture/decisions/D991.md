### 1. What a profile now records (migration 557)

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `reviewed_at` | When the current verdict was given (ISO text) |
| `reviewed_by` | The admin who gave it (`users.id`) |
| `reject_reason` | A rejection's reason, which the member reads. Null unless rejected |
| `changed_after_review_at` | Set when a writer saves answers over a verdict |

- **A verdict given before 557 has no date or reviewer stored anywhere.** Those stay null and read "Not recorded". Nothing is backfilled or guessed.
- **`routes/profiling.ts`'s bootstrap** adds the four columns and the history table in 557's shape, as it does for 275's and 556's (D187); `schema_pair_drift_d191` pins the loop at 29 entries.

### 2. The history

`partner_profile_events` holds one row per capture and per decision. Nothing updates or deletes a row.

- **Kinds:** `captured`, `verified`, `rejected`, `reopened`.
- **`source`** names the writer, and is set only on a capture (a CHECK holds it): `chat` (the onboarding chat's `/save`), `invitation` (a partner invitation's acceptance) or `eadwyn` (Eadwyn's partner questions, `writeRouter.ts`).
- **`admin_user_id`** is set on a decision; `reason` carries a rejection's or a Reopen's reason.
- **Keyed by the profile's reference,** never by its email.
- **It starts at 557.** Nothing before recorded these events, so none is invented.
- **A capture is best effort:** the writer's own save has landed, so a failure to record it is logged by reference and never fails the save.

### 3. The decision path

`POST /api/profiling/admin/:email/verify` stays the decision path. Part 3 (D992, #2810) changed the same route first; this part adds the reason check after D992's status check and one call after the write:
- **A rejection needs a reason** of 10 to 2000 characters. Without one it is refused with 400 `reject_reason_required`, before anything is written.
- **A verdict** writes `reviewed_at`, `reviewed_by`, the reason (only on a rejection; a verification clears it), clears the changed-after flag, and adds the history row, in one batch.
- **`pending` is refused on this route** (400 `invalid_status`, part 3's D992): Reopen (§4) is the one way back to Waiting.
- **The answer adds** `reviewed_at` and `member_notified`.
- **A failure to record the review** is logged by reference; the verdict the route already wrote stands.

### 4. Reopen and the history read, by reference

On part 1's router:
- **`POST /api/admin/partner-profiles/:ref/reopen`** (`requireAdmin`). It is not frozen on a suspended branch: the verdicts it undoes are not either (D260's Approvals row in `lib/branchFreeze.js`). An optional `reason` of up to 2000 characters (400 `reason_too_long`). 404 `profile_not_found`, and an email in place of a reference is a 404 too. 409 `not_reviewed` when the profile has no verdict. The profile reads pending with no reviewer, date, reason or flag; the verdict stays in the history. **Nobody is told** (PRF5d). Logged as `partner_profile_reopened` by reference.
- **`GET /api/admin/partner-profiles/:ref/history`** (`requireAdmin`): newest first, `limit` 1–100 (default 50) and `offset`, with `total`. A decision names its admin (`by: {user_id, name}`); a capture names its source; no row carries an email. A D1 error is 503 `partner_profile_history_unreadable`, "That is not the same as none.", never `[]`.

### 5. The changed-after flag (owner decision 3)

D989 §5 decided that a profile changed after its verdict keeps the verdict and is flagged. **Any of the three writers** sets the flag when it saves over a verified or rejected profile, not only the chat: a partner's answers changed whoever wrote them. The next verdict or a Reopen clears it. The list row carries it as `changed_after_review_at`; PRF2's dot and sentence are part 5's to draw.

### 6. The member's notice

- **With an account** (the profile's `user_id`, or a user with its email): `notify()` on `in_app` and `email`, type `partner_profile_reviewed`, link `/inbox`, with no category so quiet hours and the digest do not hold it. The member's own email settings decide the email, as for every notice, so the answer says `{in_app: true, email: 'by_settings'}`.
- **With none** (an invitee who has not signed up): an email alone, `{in_app: false, email: 'sent' | 'not_sent'}`.
- **Titles:** "Your Axal partner profile is verified" and "Your Axal partner profile was not verified"; a rejection's body carries "Their reason: …".
- **A failed notice** answers `member_notified: null`; the verdict stands.
- **Today's review dialog** (`PartnerProfilesConsole.jsx`, outside #2802's file list) gains "Reason if you reject (the member reads it)"; Reject stays disabled until it has 10 characters. Without it the required reason would have broken Reject. Part 6 replaces the dialog.

### 7. What the list reads now

- Each row: `reviewed_at`, `reviewed_by: {user_id, name} | null`, `changed_after_review_at`.
- **`sort=reviewed`:** newest verdict first, never-reviewed last. Part 1 refused it as `not_recorded`.
- **`reviewed_by=<admin id>`**, or `reviewed_by=not_recorded` for a verdict with no reviewer (given before 557). Anything else is `bad_filter`.
- **The counts do not change:** Verified in 30 days and the weekly verified and rejected stay `available: false`, now saying why: a verdict given before 24 Oct 2026 has no date, so a count would be short. Reading them from `reviewed_at` is for the owner to decide once 557 has 30 days behind it.
