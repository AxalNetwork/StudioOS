## D7585 — Extend worldwide fund-manager coverage without overlapping pending imports (#7585)

### Context

The next worldwide research pass found 73 manager candidates after D7326.
Sixty-one are also present in the separate 605-row research snapshot proposed
by #7337. Importing the entire research pass again would obscure ownership and
create avoidable duplicate risk. Twelve candidates remain additional to both
the merged catalogue and that pending snapshot. Further primary-source
research adds 17 more managers/platforms across Asia, Africa and Latin America.

### Decision and evidence

Add 29 independently verified discovery records through
`202611190015_7585_research_global_funds.sql`, generated from
`attached_assets/research-global-funds-2026-11-19.json`. The snapshot retains
71 distinct evidence URLs, identity notes, qualified manager/vehicle
relationships, field-level source references, and exclusions requiring review
or already covered elsewhere. Twelve records retain their actual 18 November
verification date; 17 were checked on 19 November. The compilation review date
does not overwrite the individual evidence dates.

Examples include Indus Valley Capital and i2i Ventures in Pakistan, MCS
Ventures in Mongolia, Dolma Fund Management and One to Watch with Nepal
mandates, and Mekong Strategic Capital in the Greater Mekong region. The
additional emerging-market managers include ShEquity Partners, Beta.Ventures,
Kayyak Ventures, Impacta VC, BuenTrip Ventures, Kamay Ventures, Epakon Capital
and Rally Cap. Coverage descriptions are research evidence, not headquarters
claims or promises that a manager is accepting investments or fundraising.

The directory import stores manager names, official links, supported HQ
countries, source citations and verification dates. It does not invent a
specific vehicle, size, ticket, vintage, sector, stage, investment-country or
LP commitment. Those facts remain unrecorded; no manager summary is inserted
into `research_fund_vehicle_facts`. Existing zero EIF/EIFO storage flags with
no supporting LP evidence continue to read as unknown through the service.

Two records have supported HQ geography: Bicycle Capital's dated dual-base
evidence supports the United States and Brazil, while Mekong Strategic
Capital explicitly describes its Cambodian headquarters. The other 27 HQs
remain unknown. Add Cambodia's country label and a `fund_hq` mapping only
where missing. Existing manual mappings, including explicit unmapped pins,
continue to win. Investment markets, contact addresses and legal domiciles
are not substituted for headquarters.

### Manager identity and preservation

The compilation has its own source name and committed evidence URL. It stays
`cite only`: this records independently checked factual metadata and does not
assert permission to republish website content. The prior D7326 source,
manifest, generator and migration remain immutable.

The new generator retains the additive D7326 identity predicates: exact
official hosts, recorded name/UID aliases, and vehicle suffixes supported by
an explicit `fund_number`. The migration preserves existing rows, source
verdicts, reports, vehicle facts, moved identities and saved/member references.
Withheld-source rows keep their restrictions; independently verified new
records cannot take over their primary keys or revive retired UIDs.

Singapore's nVentures has a qualified display name, legal-name keys and its
own `.sg` host. The ambiguous bare `nventures` brand and unqualified numbered
fund keys are excluded from matching because NVIDIA has a different manager
with the same short name. Its related vehicles remain documented under the
Singapore platform. OBOR's vehicles and Mekong Strategic Ventures/SADIF are
likewise retained as relationships, not extra manager records.

The evidence snapshot records the exact main and #7337 heads used for
comparison. This import does not depend on #7337 landing first and does not
modify that PR's files. Both application orders are checked against the
committed catalogue and the pending migration; repeated application must
preserve every original row and add each new identity at most once. These
repository replay counts describe migration state, not a live-production
population or a guarantee about a later concurrent import.

### Verification and release

`scripts/import-global-research-funds-7585.py --check` verifies byte-for-byte
reproducibility. `research_global_funds_7585.test.ts` uses the real service and
SQLite schema to check historical preservation, repeat application, competing
identities, ambiguous names, source restrictions, original evidence dates,
unknown financial facts, country filters and manual HQ pins. Deliberately
weakened SQL predicates are exercised only in memory to establish that the
preservation assertions detect regressions.

Before landing, run the root build, required drift suite, focused import
tests, generator checks, both-order pending-import replay and a local D1
execution. Record the results and any inherited main failures in the PR.

Production application uses the normal gated migration/release workflow.
Opening or merging the PR alone is not evidence that the database has been
updated. Verify the migration ledger and source-level aggregate counts after
release. Do not apply ad hoc production SQL. If publication needs to be
withdrawn later, use a new reviewed migration to set this compilation's source
verdict to `not allowed`; preserve its identities, member references and
historical migration instead of deleting or editing applied data.

No new route, Help interaction or frontend design is introduced. The existing
directory search, profile, source citation and country-filter behavior serves
these additions. Landing remains subject to current-head CI and the T2 owner
landing policy.
