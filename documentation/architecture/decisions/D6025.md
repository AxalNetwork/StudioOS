## D6025 — Prototype / MVP's burn-up draws inside one month (#6025)

**Why.** The burn-up grouped Done must-haves by month of completion and drew
only with three months. A team that shipped its must-haves in a few weeks only
ever read "Fewer than three completion dates are recorded."

**Decided.** The period is chosen from what is recorded, coarsest first:

- **Month** when completions fall in three months or more: unchanged, labels
  `2026-07`, each point dated the 1st.
- **ISO week** when they fall in fewer than three months but three weeks or
  more: labels `W45`, each point dated its Monday.
- **Day** when they fall in fewer than three weeks but on three days or more:
  labels `12 Nov`, each point dated that day.
- Otherwise the card stays `too_little` with the same sentence. A line still
  needs three real points and none is invented. Only real calendar days are
  grouped by week or day; a malformed `done_at` counts toward its month only,
  as before.

Each point's value stays the cumulative count of Done must-haves, and the Ship
point is sorted in by date as before, so the mark lands in date order at every
grain. Week labels carry no year: a line by week spans fewer than three
calendar months, and the Ship mark carries its full date.

**Query shape.** #4603 part 3's one grouped scan stays one statement. Its
completion key is now `substr(done_at, 1, 10)` (the day) instead of the month,
and `burnupPoints` in `routes/cycle_build.ts` folds the days into months, weeks
or days in the Worker. No statement is added to the overview or the module
page. The covering index `idx_mvp_features_must_groups` already holds
`done_at`, so the scan reads the same index rows: rows read are unchanged.
What grows is rows returned by that scan, one per (state, hypothesis, day of
completion) instead of per month; it is bounded by the number of dated Done
must-haves.

**Frontend.** `CycleCharts.jsx`'s line draws `labels` as given and assumes no
month; nothing there changes. Week and day labels are shorter than month
labels.

**Verified.** `cloudflare-worker/test/build_burnup_weekly_6025.test.ts`:
three weeks of one month draw `W45, W46, Ship, W48`; two weeks on three days
draw by day; two days, three completions on one day, and undated Done rows read
the too-little sentence; three months still draw by month; the overview and the
module page run the same statements at every grain with one grouped scan; ISO
weeks across 2026's 53rd week. Rows read re-measured on a local D1 with the
`build_rows_read_4603` harness (numbers in the PR).
