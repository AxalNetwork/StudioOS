## D5664 part 2 — Stable terminal CI identity across endpoints (#5664)

The run-list and attempt REST endpoints can report updated timestamps one
second apart for the same finished attempt. For a known Actions run/job attempt, terminal identity excludes the
occurrence timestamp and its confidence; the immutable record retains the
first observed source timestamp. Run/job IDs, attempt, conclusion, refs and
other metadata still distinguish records. Nonterminal observations, external checks and records without known attempt
identity retain their timestamps. A completed Actions attempt takes precedence
over a nonterminal observation even when its timestamp is slightly later; a
new attempt remains independent and can be pending.

The six-hour census found this before capture activation. The committed
capture configurations remain disabled. Any ledger or outbox populated with
the earlier identity must be reconciled before activation; do not replay old
keys alongside corrected keys into an active publisher.

Validation uses run and job endpoint differences, immutable timestamp and
delivery-receipt counts, distinct attempts/conclusions, and nonterminal
observations. The real metadata census is replayed with a fake publisher only;
this is not a live Slack canary or a proof of intermediate-event recovery.
