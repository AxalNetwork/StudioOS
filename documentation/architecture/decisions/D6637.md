## D6637 — Offers between accepted ties (#6637)

The owner chose D6263 Q8=A: Offer one chooses two different active people who are both accepted ties of the proposer. The existing intro_propositions store receives mirrored pending propositions. One D1 batch checks both current ties, inserts both rows and records the actor audit; a failure cannot leave a half offer. An existing pending proposition in either direction blocks the offer. Expired or declined counterparts close the obsolete pending side before a new offer.

Closed offers retain separate UIDs and consent history. The migration keeps matching's existing ordered-pair uniqueness while limiting offered pairs to one pending row per direction. Counterpart status is correlated by the offer group so later offers cannot rewrite historical consent. Existing accept/decline and recipient credit charging are reused; offering itself spends no credits. Recipients receive an in-app link to their own proposition. Member offers have no recorded match score and are never presented as platform matches.

The investor toolbar opens the existing Sheet pattern with two names-only paginated accepted-tie pickers. The selected counterpart is excluded, duplicate selections cannot submit, and loading, insufficient-tie, read failure, send failure and success states remain distinct.

Verification uses synthetic members and fully migrated SQLite databases. No production member data or live writes are required.
