### 2. What is read, and from where

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/advisors/admin/cohort-assignments/assignable` (R1): accounts with the Advisor role, `has_advisor_profile`; the first 500 by name | Advisors; Needs you |
| `GET /api/advisors/admin/cohort-assignments` (R2): assignments, `ended_at` null while active; the newest 500 | Cohort assignments |
| `GET /api/admin/partners/links` (R3): partner accounts with their firm (first 500, unattached first), firms with their account counts (first 500), `attached` and `unattached` | Operating partners, Firms; Needs you; the firms table |
| `GET /api/admin/partners/directory` (R4): every firm's specialization and directory flags, first 500. It carries emails; none reaches the page | Firms' listed count; the table's specialization and directory |
| `GET /api/admin/partners/deals?status=active` (R5): the newest 200 active deals, with `deal_type`, `expires_at` and the partner account | Active partner deals; Needs you; the table's deal column |
| `GET /api/admin/lab-hosts?status=pending` (R6): all-time counts by status, every capacity | Lab office-hours hosts; Needs you |
| `GET /api/admin/network-profiles` (R7): every roster row | Needs you (shown cards with no photo); the Deck roster |

- **Every one is truthful:** a D1 error is a 5xx. Each answers alone, and a failed one marks only its blocks with "Unreadable" and Try again. When all seven fail on Overview, the page says "The network's figures could not be read. Nothing here is a zero."
- **Guard L.** A list at its cap says what it covers: "500+" advisors or partner accounts, "the newest 200" deals, "The first 500 firms by name."
- **A deal belongs to a firm through its partner account** (R5's `user_id` → R3's `partner_id`). A deal whose account has no firm is counted on the tile and in Needs you, and drawn on no firm row.
- **Ending soon** is a deal whose term ends from now to 30 days on; a past date is not "soon".
- **The words.** "Advisor" is always a human advisor. A legacy roster kind `mentor` is not relabelled here; part 3 folds it into Advisor, as the deck already does.

### 3. What each tab draws today

- **Overview (AP1).**
  - Eight tiles:

A queue that could not be read says so in its own row.
  - **C1–C4 and People:** "Not read here yet", each saying what it waits for.
- **Firms (AP5).**
  - One row per firm: name and company, accounts, specialization, the active deal (type and end date, amber when within 30 days) and directory (Featured, Listed or Not listed).
  - A strip "Partner accounts with no firm: [n]" with "Attach on Partner invitations".
  - A column whose read failed says "Could not be read".
  - Search, filters, sort, pages, the drawer and the directory switches are part 7.
- **Deck roster (AP7):** today's roster, unchanged but for its title. Its defects are part 3; its redesign is part 8.
