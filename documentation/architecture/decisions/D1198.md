### 1. Move to testing

`POST /api/admin/testing/programmes/:uid/testing` is Super Admin only.
- **The step:** an `open` or `closed` programme becomes `testing`. Any other status is 409 `not_testable`, "Only an open or closed programme can move to testing." A missing uid is 404.
- **The event:** `testing_started`, written in the same batch as the update. An update that changed nothing writes no event.
- **Who is told, by the bell (`testing_started`):**
  - the firm, for a partner's programme (§3);
  - the picked testers on active accounts.

  An applicant who was not picked is not told.
- **Mail:** StudioOS sends no email. Mail writes to testers only through `testers:<uid>`, D811's rule (picked, consenting, `mail_enabled`, not suppressed or unsubscribed).
- **The answer:** `{ programme, told: { owner, testers, mail_reach } }`.
  - It holds counts only, never a name or an address.
  - `mail_reach` is how many people that rule would write to.
  - A count is of notices that reached an inbox, so a member who turned the notice off is not counted.

### 2. Open and close tell the owner

- **What is new:** `open` and `close` used to tell no one. Now the firm is told (`testing_programme_opened`, `testing_programme_closed`). Both still answer `programme`, and now also `told: { owner }`.
- **HQ's own programme** has no owner to tell, because HQ is the one acting. Its `told.owner` is `null`, not 0.

### 3. Who "the owner" is

**A partner's programme belongs to its firm:** every active account with `role = 'partner'` and `users.partner_id` set to the programme's `owner_partner_id`.
- Another firm is never told.
- A deactivated account is never told.
- Approve and send back still tell the programme's creator, as D812 had it. This part does not change them.

### 4. HQ withdraws an application

`POST /api/admin/testing/applications/:uid/withdraw` takes `{ reason }`.
- **The reason** is trimmed and must be 10 to 500 characters. Otherwise it is 400 `invalid_request`, "Give the reason they will read, 10 to 500 characters." A number is not a reason.
- **The step:** an `applied` or `picked` application becomes `withdrawn`.
  - Anything else, including a declined application or one already withdrawn, is 409 `not_withdrawable`.
  - A missing uid is 404.
- **A picked place is freed.** Places count `picked` applications, so `places_left` rises at once.
- **The event:** `withdrawn_by_hq`, under the Super Admin as `actor_user_id`, with `{ reason, from }` as its detail. It is written in the same batch as the update.
- **The member** is told by the bell (`testing_withdrawn`, "HQ withdrew your application: <reason>"), linked to Inbox like the other member notices.
- **The answer:** `{ application, freed_place, told: { member } }`.
- **Unchanged:** the member's own withdraw (`POST /api/testing/applications/:uid/withdraw`) takes no reason.

### 5. Clients

`adminTestingApi.startTesting(uid)` and `adminTestingApi.withdraw(uid, reason)` are in `frontend/src/lib/api/productTesting.js`, which `api.js` already re-exports.
