### 1. The problem

The 8 November audit found Build's module pages reading as integration dashboards: Connect a tool as every module's header action, connector states drawn at full weight even on an empty module, and an Eadwyn rail that led with model names, model descriptions, per-token prices, the safety model, the month's allowance, held usage and the top-up balance, under one desk-wide sentence about Monday plans, retros, roadmap ordering and metric annotations on every module.

### 2. What changed

| Board | Change |
|---|---|
| BD7, BD8 | On a Build module, `primaryForModule` returns no header action (`modulePrimary: false`); the overview keeps Connect a tool. The module's connector parts draw in a **Sources** region under the records ("Sources · what your tools report"), whose one action, "Connect or manage tools →", opens the Connect a tool lightbox over the page. Eadwyn's bands (Product roadmap, Operating systems) stay where they were, above Sources. |
| BD18 A | At zero, Sources is one closed line ("What your tools report appears here once the first record exists"), so no connector state is drawn; it is still one press away. Under the first action, **Ask Eadwyn** with the module's asks: the box opens Ask empty, each ask opens it with the question filled in. |
| BD21 A | On a Build module the rail takes `prompts` (the module's asks) and `foldSettings`: the asks are the rail's first block, and the model block, the safety line and Usage (allowance, held, top-up, Add tokens) move into one closed **AI settings** at the rail's foot. Nothing is removed, so model choice and billing stay reachable in the rail. The rail's sentence under its switch is the module's own (`BUILD_RAIL_NOTES`): the Monday plan, retro and annotation sentence only on Operating systems, roadmap ordering only on Product roadmap, none on the other four. |
| BD21 B | Each module's asks (`BUILD_ASKS`), in the board's order. They fill Ask's composer and send nothing; they never run on their own and offer no model. |
| BD24 A | On the phone at zero, Ask is one row, "Ask Eadwyn · N suggestions for this module ›", which opens the Ask sheet with the asks first. Past zero, Ask is the bar's Eadwyn button. Sources follows the rows on the phone, as on the desktop. |

- **One panel.** An ask is `openAskPanel(label, draft)`: the store carries the words (`draft`), and `AskPanel` puts them in its composer. The rail, the page block and the phone row all open the same Ask panel.
- **Honesty.** Each part keeps its own `PartBoundary`, so one failing tool reads Unreadable with its own Retry and the rest stay drawn. The Ask block and the phone row draw nothing where Ask does not serve the viewer (a support session, an Explorer), so no control leads to a panel that cannot open. The rail fold hides nothing it could not show before: it is closed, not removed.
- **Scope.** `foldSettings` and `prompts` are passed only on the six Build modules (and their records); every other rail is unchanged. `sourcesRegion` is Build's alone; the other cycles keep their parts as children.
