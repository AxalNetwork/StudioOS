## D7650 — A chase asks only a company whose report is due and unreported (#7650)

Split from #4645 by the owner's answer **A** (2026-11-19). `POST /api/portfolio/chase` accepted any company the investor could see. A founder could be chased with no reporting obligation, before the period's due date, or for a period already reported. The only guard was a one-hour repeat window.

### The rule

Before writing a chase row or notifying anyone, the Worker reads the company's reporting obligation for the caller (`services/chaseEligibility.ts`). It is the obligation `GET /api/positions/kpi-compliance` measures (D6204, D4647):

- **It applies** when the company is held in the caller's book: a position the caller holds, one in a fund they run, or any position for an admin.
- **Its period** is the last completed one, at the request's `cadence` (quarterly unless `monthly` is sent; the Reports page reads quarterly).
- **Its due date** is the period end plus the earliest active `after_end` duty term at that cadence in the caller's book, else the firm default of 30 days.
- **It is reported** when a submitted update counts for the period (`periodCounts`, D1057).

A company is chased only when the obligation applies and the report is due or overdue and unreported. "Due" includes the due day itself: the report is owed that day, though D6204 calls it late only from the next day. So Chase all overdue (the Worker's overdue set) is always eligible, and a per-row Chase on the due day is too.

### Skips

Every other target is skipped before anything is written, with one of four reasons:

| `reason` | When |
|---|---|
| `no_obligation` | The company is outside the caller's book, does not exist, or holds no position in it. These read the same, so a refusal says nothing about another tenant's company. |
| `not_due` | The due date is still ahead. The skip carries `period` and `due_date`. |
| `already_reported` | A submitted update counts for the period. The skip carries `period`. |
| `unreadable` | Any read behind the obligation failed: the book, the positions, the duties, the updates or the clock. A failed read is never treated as eligible, and the firm default is never filled in for it. |

The response keeps `chased` and `skipped` (the skipped ids) as they were. It adds `skips`, one `{ project_id, reason, message, period, due_date }` per skipped id. Each `chased` item also carries `period` and `due_date`.

### Times

- The due date is compared with SQLite's clock, `datetime(?) <= datetime('now')`.
- The repeat window now reads `datetime(created_at) > datetime('now', '-1 hour')`. Chase rows are written as ISO text (`2026-11-19T10:00:00.000Z`). Compared as text against `datetime('now', '-1 hour')`, the `T` sorted after the space, so any earlier chase that day held back a new one, not just one inside the hour.

### The page

Reports (`/portfolio/updates`) counts a company as chased only when it is in `chased`. A per-row Chase the Worker skipped reads, for example, "Fjord Data was not chased: not due until 14 Nov 2026". Chase all overdue lists each skipped company with its reason. A company missing from both lists is "not confirmed as chased", never "asked". The words are in `chaseSkipText` and `chaseOutcomeOf` (`pages/portfolio/portfolioModel.js`).
