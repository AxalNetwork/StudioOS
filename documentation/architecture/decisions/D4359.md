## D4359 — The Pitch deck studio unlocks at Raise + Grow, the same as the Worker (#4359)

- **Status:** built by S21, 2026-11-04 (#4359).
- **Number:** D4359 is this issue's number (D4106).
- **Answers:** #2434 question 8, answered **A** by the owner on 2026-11-03: the studio is a Raise + Grow plan feature. This closes D1111 §4 (bug 2).
- **Builds on:** D683 (the founder plans, `growth` and `studio`), D894 (the studio), D905 (its boundary for other roles), D1050 (the plan named by its catalogue key).

### 1. What was wrong

The Worker gates the studio on `studio`: New deck and the old `/generate` (`deck_new.ts` `planRefusal`), Share (`POST /api/decks/:id/share`) and the one link writer (`raiseWriters.ts` `createDeckLink`, `updateDeckLink`) answer 402 below it.

The UI checked no plan at all. The audit's `PitchWorkspacePage.jsx` `hasTier(user, 'growth')` has since been removed, so on main every founder, Free and Growth included, saw an open studio. They then met the Worker's refusals.

### 2. One rule, read from the Worker

- **The rule:** `services/decks/studioAccess.ts` holds `DECK_STUDIO_TIER = 'studio'` and `deckStudioAccess(user)`.
  - The answer is open exactly when `ensureTier(user, DECK_STUDIO_TIER)` passes. It goes through `userMeetsTier`, so subscription status, grace, partner grants and the bypass roles count as the Worker counts them.
  - Every studio gate above now names `DECK_STUDIO_TIER`, never the literal.
- **The read:** `GET /api/decks/access` (`routes/deck_access.ts`, mounted before `decks.ts`) → `{ open, required, plan: { tier, name, plan_key }, message }`.
  - `message` is the 402's own words (`TIER_COPY.studio`, now exported), and null when open.
  - It reads only the signed-in member.
- **The UI:** every studio route in `App.jsx` is drawn inside `StudioGate` (`studioGate(...)`). That covers the Overview and its moved notice, Decks, Script, Share, Review, Notes, the editor, New deck, Templates and Present.
  - The gate reads `deckAccessApi.get()` and shows one of three states:
    - **Open:** the page.
    - **Locked:** "The Pitch deck studio is on the Raise + Grow plan", the plan's line, **See Raise + Grow** (opens the paywall on `studio`) and **Compare plans ›** (`/pricing`).
    - **Unread:** "Your plan could not be checked", with **Try again**, when the read fails or answers anything but a boolean `open`.
  - A studio that will refuse is never drawn.
  - `studioAccessModel.js` keeps no plan list. No file in the studio calls `hasTier` or reads `subscription_tier`.
- The Advisor, Operating Partner and Explorer boundary (D905) is unchanged: it is drawn before the gate is reached.

### 3. What changes for whom

- **Growth (Validate + Build) and Free founders:** the studio is locked, with the upgrade path.
- **Raise + Grow founders:** the studio is open, as before. That holds while the subscription is good, including a live partner grant or a payment within its grace.
- **Admins, partners, investors, advisors:** unchanged; they pass the Worker's gate as a bypass role.
- **A Growth founder on the Spin-Out Lab:** locked.
  - The frontend's `hasTier` lifts a Lab member to Growth tooling, but the Worker's `userMeetsTier` has never done so for `studio`. These members were already refused at New deck and Share.
  - The Lab's own deck page, `/spinout-lab/pitch-deck`, is a different route and does not use this gate.
  - Opening the studio to Lab members would be a Worker change to `deckStudioAccess` first. The gate would then follow it without a frontend edit.

### 4. What this does not change

- **Some deck writes still have no plan gate on the Worker.** These are saving a version, applying a non-premium template, autofill and export (audit #2434, bug 2's other direction). The UI no longer leads a member below the plan to them, but the routes themselves are unchanged. Gating them is a Worker change for its own issue.
- **Premium templates and the watermark** keep their own `studio` checks (D683). They are not the studio's entry gate.

### 5. Tests

- `cloudflare-worker/test/deck_studio_access_d4359.test.ts` (3 tests, real mounted routes on SQLite) covers:
  - eleven kinds of member: Free, Growth, Raise + Grow, cancelled, past due beyond grace, incomplete, expired and live partner grants, Growth on the Lab, admin and partner. For each, the read is open exactly when `POST /api/decks/outline` and `POST /api/decks/:id/share` do not answer 402. This is the parity test: it fails if the UI's answer and the Worker's gates differ;
  - the locked answer's plan and words, matching the 402's;
  - every gate naming `DECK_STUDIO_TIER`;
  - the read mounted ahead of `GET /:id`.
- `frontend/test/pitch_studio_gate_d4359.test.mjs` (5 tests) covers:
  - the state from the answer, never open by default;
  - a Growth answer drawing the locked card and opening the paywall on `studio`;
  - a Raise + Grow answer drawing the studio;
  - a failed read and Try again;
  - every studio route inside the gate, with no second plan check in the studio.
- Route pins in `founder_shell`, `pitch_notes_d905`, `pitch_overview_d4254` and `pitch_studio_d894` now expect the gated form.
