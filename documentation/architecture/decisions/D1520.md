## D1520 — Deck record sources for market size, the financial projection, competition, round terms, use of funds by section and the narrative's words

- **Status:** Built by the S14 session (#3772, deck v2 A21, part of #3716; 2026-11-01). Worker only. Not deployed.
- **Builds on:** D896 (`DECK_SOURCES`, `sourceAndGrade`, `GET /api/decks/sources`) and D908 (the Investor narrative's stores). It changes no route and no page.
- **Migration:** none.
- **Owner decision it rests on:** T · Compliance defaults is still pending. S1's pick stands: counsel's wording to come; until then the forward-looking footnote is on and public views are off. The footnote here is a placeholder sentence the owner or counsel replaces in one constant (`PROJECTION_FOOTNOTE`).

### Why

The one-click deck (#3768) plans a whole deck from the venture's records, and it cuts a card as missing data when no record backs it. D896 gave it seventeen adapters, but none for market size, the financial model or competitor detail. The narrative was only a count, the round's valuation terms were not read, and use of funds was one line of text. Without these, the market, round-terms and competition cards would be cut, or a model would be asked for figures it must never invent.

### What was decided

1. **Six new adapters, in their own file.** They live in `services/decks/recordSources.ts` as `RECORD_SOURCES`, and `DECK_SOURCES` lists them after D896's.
   - `sources.ts` changes only:
     - the new grade;
     - the projection rule (point 3);
     - the exported adapter types;
     - the registry line.
   - Every value is read from the venture's own records, never from a model.
   - A value that is not recorded is left out, so `readSources` reports it `not_recorded`, never 0.
2. **Market size: `validate.market_tam`, `validate.market_sam` and `validate.market_som`.**
   - The values come from `projects.tam`, `sam` and `som`. A figure that is 0 or missing is not recorded.
   - The grade is **Sourced** (source Document) when the venture has recorded a market source (`market_sources`), and **Stated** (Typed) otherwise.
   - **The figures must nest, SOM ≤ SAM ≤ TAM** (`nestedMarket`). A figure larger than the one above it is left out, together with everything below it, rather than shown as a market. The page then asks for it in Validate › Market intelligence.
3. **A projection is graded Projected, and only a projection is.**
   - `Grade` gains **`Projected`**, the exact string, appended last to `GRADES` as the weakest. `coverage.by_grade` gains `Projected`, and a binding may keep the grade.
   - An adapter marked `projection: true` has every filled slot graded Projected by `readSources`, whatever origin the adapter reports. A projection can therefore never pass for Measured, even if an adapter is later written wrong; the test pins this with a probe adapter that reports a connector.
   - Each such item also carries `projection: { label: 'Projection', footnote }`. A binding cannot drop it, because it comes from the adapter, not from the deck.
   - `sourceAndGrade` never answers Projected; only the projection rule does.
4. **The financial projection** (`financials`, from `financial_models.computed_json`, the model Use of funds › Runway › Forecast saves):
   - `raise.projection_revenue_12m`: the sum of months 1 to 12;
   - `raise.projection_runway`;
   - `raise.projection_breakeven`: not recorded when the model never breaks even, never 0;
   - `raise.projection_ending_cash`.

   The table is created on the model's first save, so a database without it reads as not recorded, not unreadable.
5. **The competition table** (`competitors_detail`): `validate.competitors_direct`, `validate.competitors_adjacent` and `validate.competitors_names`.
   - The names are the direct ones first, then the adjacent, up to `COMPETITOR_NAMES_MAX` (8), then "+N more".
   - The source is `cycleValidate`'s `loadTracked`, now exported, so a name the venture stopped tracking leaves the table here exactly as it leaves the Market intelligence card.
   - The grade is Sourced only when every listed competitor has a source.
6. **Round terms** (`round_terms`):
   - `raise.pre_money` comes from the latest round call's counsel terms (`round_calls.pre_money_cents`), or else the active round's `raise_rounds.pre_money`.
   - `raise.post_money` and `raise.dilution_pct` are read only when both the pre-money and the round's target are recorded: post = pre + target, and dilution = target ÷ post, to one decimal.
   - They are graded Reviewed while counsel's approval covers the round call, and Stated otherwise, the same rule as D896's `round`.
7. **Use of funds by section: `raise.use_of_funds.<section>`.**
   - There is one percent per `FUND_SECTIONS` entry, read from the same allocation as D896's `raise.use_of_funds` text slot, which stays.
   - A recorded allocation states every section's share, so a 0% section is a recorded 0.
   - A key segment is `[a-z0-9_]` (`sanitizeBinding`), so `go-to-market` becomes `go_to_market`.
8. **The narrative's words: `raise.narrative.<section>` and `raise.positioning.<kind>`.**
   - These are text slots for the ten narrative sections and the seven positioning kinds.
   - They read the latest saved version (a `spinout_deck_overrides` version, as `ownVersion` defines one), or version 0, the live deck, when none is saved.
   - Positioning reads a version's own field where it has one, and the core otherwise. Several lines of one kind are joined by line breaks.
   - The words are Sourced when the section's source is a document, and Stated otherwise.
   - The section and kind lists are written in `recordSources.ts`, because importing `narrative/store` would be a cycle through `raiseWriters` → `routes/decks` → this registry. The store is loaded inside the read, and a test checks that both lists are the store's.
   - D896's `raise.narrative` count slot stays.

### What changes for readers

`GET /api/decks/sources` answers more items, and `DataReady.jsx` shows them with no client change. The frontend's `sourcesModel.js` `GRADES` and `GradeDot` do not yet know **`Projected`**. S1 adds it on #3885, as the issue says.

### Tests

- `cloudflare-worker/test/deck_record_sources_d1520.test.ts` (7 tests) has a fixture for each source:
  - nothing recorded means `not_recorded`, never 0, and every key passes `sanitizeBinding`;
  - market nesting and its grades;
  - the projection grade, label and footnote, including for a probe adapter that reports a connector;
  - the competition table and stopped names;
  - the round terms, only when recorded;
  - the use of funds sections;
  - the narrative and positioning versions, and the parity with the store's lists.
- `deck_sources_d896.test.ts`: `by_grade` gains `Projected: 0`.

Mutation checks, each red when applied:

- a projection graded by its origin;
- the projection label dropped;
- the market source ignored;
- the nesting dropped;
- a missing model table read as unreadable;
- a never-breaks-even model read as 0;
- post-money without a target;
- the dilution inverted;
- one sourced competitor counting as all;
- the narrative version read from `narrative_version_meta`;
- a 0 market figure counted as recorded.
