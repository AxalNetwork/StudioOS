### What each board became

| Board | Built in | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| DE03a, the table as cards | `FlowTable.jsx` `PhoneCards` | A row on the member's deals is the Deals v2 card (`DealCard`), the same one the board draws. It shows the venture, the three status families (my stage among them), the round, the ask, the score strip, the chosen scopes, fit to my thesis, days in stage, the owner, Open, Move to…, Assign to me and Compare. A round not on the member's deals keeps the short row with Add to my deal flow, and its Compare tick is now a 44 × 44 target. Paging is unchanged. |
| DE03b, one stage at a time | `FlowBoard.jsx` `PhoneBoard` | The stage tabs are 44px tall and keep their width (`flex-none`), so the strip scrolls sideways inside its own `max-w-full` box and the page never does. Passed is a tab when the filter asks for it, as before. Cards are never dragged; Move to… is on every card. |
| DE03c, Filters | `FlowControls.jsx` `FiltersDrawer` | Already the shared `Sheet`: full screen below 640px, focus held inside and returned to the opener. Its chips and fields are now 44px tall below 640px. |
| DE03d, Compare | `DealFlow.jsx` compare bar | Picks come from each card's Compare tick, already 44px. The bar's Compare link and Clear button are now 44px on a phone. |

### Departures from the canvas

- **Filters.** DE03c draws a short list of disclosure rows (My stage, Sector, Location, Round, Owner) and an "Include passed" box. The sheet keeps the groups the read supports, as the desktop drawer does: My stage with Passed among its chips, Round status, How it entered, Sector, Owner, and the score minimums. Location is not on the deal list's read, and the sheet already says so.
- **Sort.** DE03a's toolbar shows Filters and Sort. The page's own toolbar is unchanged.
- **The Compare page on a phone is not here.** DE03d's two-column phone Compare is the same frame as #6151's DE29. #6151 part 2 built it as `ComparePhone` in `Compare.jsx` (PR #6927). But #6927's base was #6893's branch (`agent/claude/6151-deal-rooms-v2`), and it merged there on 2026-11-16 after #6893 had already landed. So it never reached `main`, and no open PR carries it. It stays #6151's to land again; this PR does not copy another slot's work. Until then, Compare on a phone is the desktop page's grid (`grid-cols-1` below 640px).
