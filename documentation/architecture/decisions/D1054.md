### 1. One way to open the sheet: `openCheckout`

`openCheckout({ plan_key, interval, from, unlocks })` asks for D1048's sheet over the current page, with no route change:
- `plan_key` is a catalogue key (`founder:studio`, `advisor:pro`, …);
- `from` is `{ label, href }`, the page it opened over. Only an address inside the app (`/…`) is kept;
- `unlocks` names the feature asked for.

**The host.** `CheckoutHost` is mounted once, beside the paywall (`GlobalCheckoutMount` in `App.jsx`), for the paywall's reason: the sheet is asked for from many pages, so it cannot live on one. It renders nothing for a visitor, and nothing until a plan is asked for. Then it:
- reads the plans, once per page load (`loadPlans`, D1050), and finds the plan by key. A plan with no product (Investor Plus, Institutional) is drawn from the read's catalogue entry with no price;
- for an Advisor or Operating Partner, reads their plan status (D1049), so a change is told from a new plan, with Try again;
- for a Super Admin only, reads `GET /api/payments/config`. A `pk_test_` key is test mode (§5).

**A plans read that fails** says "This plan couldn't be read just now. That doesn't mean it's free.", with Try again. No sheet opens on a guessed price.

**The role** is the shell's (`shellRole`) where the shell's provider reaches. Outside it, it is the session's own (`shellRoleFor`). An admin viewing as another role therefore still holds no plan: the host sits outside the shell.

### 3. PAYX10: the renewal banner

`RenewalBanner` sits in the shell, after `BranchNotDeployedBar`. It reads `GET /api/billing/alerts` (D1052). It draws one banner at a time: the bank's check first, then a failed renewal, then an ended plan.

| Alert | Words | Buttons |
|---|---|---|
| `payment_failed` with a retry date | "Your payment for [plan] on [21 Nov] didn't go through. We'll try again on [24 Nov]." | **Update card**, Pay now |
| `needs_confirmation` | "Your bank needs you to confirm the payment for [plan]." | **Confirm payment** |
| `payment_failed` with no retry date (Stripe stopped; the subscription is unpaid) | "[Plan] ended because the payment didn't go through. You're on Free. Nothing was deleted." | **Pay now**, Open Billing |

- **Each button opens a small sheet over the page:**
  - Pay now and Update card ask D1133's routes for the intent (`/alerts/:id/pay`, `/alerts/:id/update-card` with a nonce). Both need a fresh authenticator code, and the request helper's step-up prompt opens on the 403.
  - Each is confirmed on `RenewalPayForm`, a Payment Element on that client secret. A new card is then made the default (`POST /billing/payment-method/default`) for the next retry.
  - Confirm payment hands the bank's check to Stripe.js with no card form (`/alerts/:id/confirm`).
- **What it says:**
  - A decline is one of D1053's five families of words.
  - A refusal is words, never a code: settled already, the alert closed, payments not set up, or sign in with the authenticator app. Each says nothing was charged.
  - After a payment, the alerts are read again. The sheet stays on its Done words while the banner goes.
- **Dismiss** hides the banner for today only. The key names the alert, its state and the UTC day, so a new state, or the next day, shows it again. The alert stays open on Billing.
- **Who sees none:** staff (Admin, licence administrator, Super Admin). A failed read draws nothing either: Billing is where an unread alert says so.
- **Two departures from the canvas:**
  - **"Restart [plan]" is Pay now.** An unpaid subscription still holds its family (D892), so a new checkout would be refused as a second one. Paying the open invoice is what turns it back on, and D1133's route already prepares that.
  - **"Your plan stays on until [28 Nov]" is not drawn.** The alerts read sends no such date, and none is guessed.
- **Billing's pills:**
  - `past_due` now reads **Payment failed**, in red;
  - `unpaid` reads **Ended**, neutral (PAYX10c).

### 4. PAYX11 and PAYX12

**Billing's plan card (PAYX11):**
- **Cancel plan** opens the page's own dialog, "Cancel [plan]?": "It stays on until [date]. After that you're on Free, and the plan's limits apply. Your files stay." Its buttons are **Cancel at period end** and **Keep plan**. This replaces the `window.confirm` it had.
- **A cancelled plan** shows an "Ends [date]" pill and "Cancelled on [date]. You're on Free from [date]. Files stay.", with **Resume plan**.
- A date not read is left out, never guessed.
- The invoice table is unchanged.

**Usage › Limits (PAYX12):** a card titled "Included with your plan", headed "Account › Usage › Limits".
- With no allowance: "Free plan: no AI allowance. AI runs from AI tokens you top up."
- With one: "Your plan includes [$] of AI a month."
- **Compare plans** goes to the profile's Pricing tab. **Upgrade** opens the sheet on the next plan up, which is absent above the top plan.
- An Advisor's or Partner's plan is read from their plan status; a Founder's or Investor's from the session.

### 5. PAYX13–16: the sheet per family

- **PAYX13, a plan the catalogue does not sell.** A new mode, `notsold`, is checked before any price, so a Stripe price never makes such a plan sold here.
  - Investor Plus reads **Not offered yet**, with the catalogue's reason (D786).
  - Institutional reads **Contact sales**, linking `/contact`.
  - Neither is quoted, and neither shows a price or Pay. The summary still shows the plan's name, line and inclusions.
- **PAYX14, Advisor Pro and Operating Partner Pro:** the same sheet. A paying member's change is told from their plan status (D1049).
- **PAYX15, the Explorer:** D1048's words, unchanged: "Choose your profile first", with Choose a profile and the AI tokens line.
- **PAYX16, staff:**
  - "Staff account · no plan needed". A licence administrator (`branch_admin`) is staff now too; they could reach Pay before.
  - For a Super Admin while Stripe is in test mode, the header shows a **Test mode** pill, "No real money moves.", and links to **Payments ›** (`/admin/platform/payments`) and **Promo codes ›** (`/admin/platform/promo-codes`).
- **PAYX7b:** Accepted and Scheduled show Close, Open Billing and **Back to [label]** when the sheet was opened with `from`.
