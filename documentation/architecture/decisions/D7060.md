### What the canvas decides

- The Person Sheet looks a person up by the LinkedIn profile or email you recorded, never by name; the identifier is not shown to teammates.
- The lookup has distinct states, each with its own next action and none drawn as 0: Not recorded (name only), None, Unavailable with Try again, loading, one teammate and several.
- Two contacts with the same name are matched by their own identifiers; a teammate's same-name contact with a different profile never appears.
- Request introduction goes to teammate selection and then the Ask sheet, which shows target, recipient, cost and reason before Send.
- The Ask sheet stacks over the Person Sheet, which keeps its unsaved note; leaving for Introductions is guarded.
- After the send the request reads Asked. Asked, Accepted and Made are separate steps, and nothing reaches the person.
- The sharing summary covers Off, one team, several teams and unreadable, with the fields a team can discover and those never shared.
- Errors are drawn: send failed, teammate no longer sharing, the 20-a-day limit, support session, the Eadwyn quote and a missing reason. Investor credit flows stay out.
