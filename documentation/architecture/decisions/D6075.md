## D6075 — Data room: add files from Google Drive on Upload (#6075)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-22 (#6075, under #4863). Built to board DR30 of `design/incoming/Raise v2 Data Room · Boards.dc.html` (canvas D6071, owner's answers D6071-p2: option A throughout). Frontend only: no Worker route, store or migration.

### What it reuses

All of these are on main (D1091, D1094):
- `GET /api/connectors/google-workspace/mine` gives the member's connection state and the files they chose.
- The Data room module read, `GET /api/cycles/raise/modules/data-room`, carries `parts.google`: the chosen files, the copies already in the room, and how many copies the founder deleted. It is served only to the member's own connection, behind part 2's gates: a writer of the venture, with Google files enabled for its company and no support session.
- `POST /api/cycles/raise/google/sync` (`services/google/raise.ts`) copies each chosen Drive file into the room once, through the `connector_fed_items` ledger:
  - it replaces a copy only when Google's `modifiedTime` changed;
  - it never copies a deleted copy again;
  - it reads at most 20 MB a copy (`MAX_COPY_BYTES`) and checks storage first (`assertStorageRoom`);
  - each copy lands behind the NDA with access `nobody` (`COPY_ACCESS`) until the founder opens it.
- The shared section `pages/cycles/FromGoogle.jsx` and Google's own picker (`lib/googleWorkspacePicker.js`, a browser-only `drive.file` token). Validate, Build and Grow already mount these.

### Built

`pages/raise/dataroom/DataRoomFromGoogle.jsx` and its pure rules `dataRoomGoogleModel.js` are mounted on Upload (`DataRoomUpload.jsx`) once the room is read. They replace the stale sentence "Files from Google Drive or Notion are not added here yet".

- **Only a connected account sees the option.** If the member is not connected (or Google files are not allowed) and chose nothing for this venture, nothing is drawn and the module is never read. If they are connected but this venture serves them no Google part (company not enabled, support session), nothing is drawn either.
- **Connected.** The shared section shows the chosen file and Choose from Google Drive. It lists every copy in the room as "From Google Drive, as of <date>", using Google's `modifiedTime`; with no date it says "date not recorded" and never invents one. It also shows "N rows you deleted stay deleted". One line under it says what the Worker does: behind the NDA and private (Nobody) until opened, up to 20 MB a copy, counted in storage.
- **Sync.** Sync calls the existing route and says what happened in counts only: copied, replaced, unchanged, kept deleted, could not be read. A sync that added or replaced a copy refreshes Upload's storage line and marks the room changed.
- **Partly synced.** A sync stopped by Google's read limit, a full plan or unset storage reads GW7's Partly synced, with the reason.
- **Revoked.** If the member chose files here and Google is no longer connected or allowed, the section reads that: the files are not read again, the copies already in the room stay, and a link opens the Google page.
- **Unreadable.** A failed read of the connection or of the module's Google part reads "That is not the same as having none.", with Retry, and never "No Google files chosen".

Not drawn here: removing a copy. It is a room file, and it leaves through Files, which sends it to Trash like any other file. The section therefore passes no Delete.
