## D5315 part 2 — Explicit publication of confirmed proof (#5315)

Publishing a claim links it to one current confirmation. The public advisor profile returns the claim fields only while that confirmation still matches the current text. It leaves out the attester, their address, statement and consent history. The former private `is_public` field cannot expose an item.

Confirming a claim never publishes it. Withdrawing the confirmation that backed publication clears the link in the withdrawal's database statement. Later reconfirmation requires a new publish action, even when another confirmation exists. The advisor may also unpublish explicitly.
