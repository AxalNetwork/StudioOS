## D1568 — Follow-up signals from an investor's views (deck v2 D7)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-02 (#3815, deck v2 D7, part of #3716).
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `10c1c931d7`.
- **Scope:** S1's final canvas revision on #3815 (2026-11-01). D4's per-investor links (D1565, #3812) are what a signal is about.
- **Migration:** 740 (`740_raise_prospect_signals.sql`).

### Why

A founder who sent a deck link to an investor learns that they read it only by opening the analytics. The canvas draws the moments that matter as signals: on the investor row (pd35, "Revisited after 48 h") and in the visit timeline (pd37, "Opened", "Reached the ask", "Revisited"). Each one is a reason to follow up, and the follow-up should be waiting, not invented each time.

### 1. The three signals

`services/raiseSignals.ts` `recordViewSignals` runs from the share heartbeat (`POST /api/decks/share/:token/heartbeat`), after the view's read time and slide totals are stored. It looks only at links made for a prospect of the deck's own venture: a typed name (D902) or another venture's prospect has no one to signal.

| Signal | When | How often |
|---|---|---|
| `first_view` | the link's first view that was read | once per link |
| `revisit` | a read view that opened 48 hours or more after the link's previous read view | once per view |
| `reached_ask` | a read view whose cards include the version's ask card or a later one (`shareAnalytics.askCardIndex`) | once per link |

A view that was opened and never read (no heartbeat) is not a visit: it neither counts as the first nor starts the 48 hours. The unique indexes of migration 740 make a repeated heartbeat a no-op, so one revisit is one signal and one notification.

### 2. What each signal leaves

None of these changes a record.
- **A notice** in the app to the founder who made the link (else the venture's founder account): "[Name]: revisited after 48 h", and "A follow-up draft is ready for you to edit and send." Category `deals`.
- **An `after_view` draft** in `mail_drafts` (D1341), for `prospect:<id>`, written from a template: no model, no charge. Its words hold no read seconds and no figure; they greet the investor by first name and offer a call. The founder edits and sends it, or not.
- **A stage proposal**, only when the prospect's stage is behind: on the rail (`ai_rail_proposals`, page "Founder · Raise · Capital relationships", option `signal:stage`, record `prospect:<id>`, kind `proposal`). It only moves forward: a first view of someone `to_contact` proposes `contacted`; a revisit or the ask reached by someone `to_contact` or `contacted` proposes `meeting`. Nothing later is ever proposed. One proposal waits per prospect at a time. Its button is **Accept** (plan §0 row 5), and the stage changes only when the founder accepts and the record's own route saves it: this code never writes `raise_prospects.stage`.

### 3. Where it shows

- `GET /api/decks/:id/investor-links` gives each link `signals: [{ kind, at, words }]`, oldest first, through `signalsForLinks`.
- `InvestorLinksTable.jsx` draws pd35's TAG "Revisited after 48 h" on the Analytics cell once a revisit is recorded. D7 adds no screen and no chat.
- The visit timeline (pd37) is #3820's `slideAnalytics` read, not on main yet. When it lands, it reads the same `signalsForLinks` for its entries.
