### What was decided

- **The draft extends the deal note's own fill scope, not a new route.** The Worker-side scope is `deal_notes` (`services/fills/scopes.ts`), and its access rule is the notes route's own (`noteFirm`, `visibleDeal`). The card runs `POST /api/ai/rail/run` with `option: form:deal-note`, `parent: <deal>` and `input.draft: 'deal_summary'`, so the quote, the hold, the guard, the charge and the stored proposal are the fill's own accounting. Any other `draft` value is ignored. Without it, Ask on New note reads exactly what it read before.
- **What a summary reads**, each only as this investor may read it:
  - **The deal's terms:** instrument, target raise, currency and closing date, plus the company's sector. These are the fields `GET /api/deals/:id` shows even a Free reader. A summary never reads another investor's commitment, the round's total, a vote or an IC decision.
  - **Shared evidence**, through `investorEvidence` (D1265). It applies the audience, the NDA, each item's sharing, the file gate and quote consent. Before the NDA, only coverage (area, kind, count) is read, and the source says "Not shared". After it, the items the founder shared are read, with a figure only where the item shares one. The read logs `open_evidence` as any investor open does.
  - **The caller's own diligence case** on the company (owner or reviewer, as the deal's stage reads it). It reads the status, the risk band, checklist counts, and the titles and severities of open findings. Notes, details and excerpts are never read: they are encrypted and private.
  - **The firm's own notes**, read as before. A second firm's notes and diligence never enter, and someone else's firm-less note never does either.
- **Every source is named, with its state:** Used, None on record, Not shared or Unreadable, each with a sentence. The `done` event carries them as `sources`. A source the page does not know reads Unreadable, never Used.
- **A summary may be longer than a title.** A composition keeps 300 characters. A deal summary keeps up to 1,500 (`DEAL_SUMMARY_MAX`), well inside a note's 10,000.
- **The card.** It drafts only on Draft a deal summary, never on load. The draft is marked "Eadwyn's draft · Not saved" and shows its sources. Save as my note posts the note through the notes route (untagged) and then records the kept proposal against the new id (`POST /api/fills/record`). Discard writes no note and marks the proposal discarded (`decide … discard`). A refusal says why (the fill's own `refusalView`) and offers Try again. Some cases draw the button disabled, with the reason: AI off on the page, a support session, a reader who may not fill, and an unreadable read.

### Departures

- **The draft is shown, not edited, on the card.** To change the words, save it and edit the note, or use Ask on New note, which places a draft in the form to edit before Save.
- **Coverage before the NDA.** The issue says nothing behind an unsigned NDA is used. Coverage (what kinds of evidence exist and how many) is what D1265 shows an investor before the NDA, so the draft may say that evidence exists. No figure, file, quote or summary is read before the NDA.
- **The NDA test** injects the evidence read (`dealSummarySources`'s last argument). `investorEvidence`'s own NDA and sharing rules are tested in `raise_evidence_investor_d1265.test.ts`. The HTTP tests use the real read for an investor with no NDA.
