## D7167 — Research › Funds profile: one dated provenance object from Sheet to comparison, a distinct `source_withdrawn`, proposed fields and a vehicle history (#7167)

Builds part of the canvas D7032 decided (`design/incoming/Research Funds Fund Profile and Comparison Page.dc.html`, boards FND-P00 to FND-P11). Part of #3701. Two items D7032 lists, vehicle ids (one row and one uid per vehicle) and native currency (`size_currency` per list), are built by #7166, not here; this PR reads `presentFund` as it is, so a USD size appears wherever #7166 serves one.

### What the Worker now serves

- **One provenance object** (`services/researchFundProfile.ts`, `provenanceOf`): `{ list_name, list_title, publisher, published_url, reported_on, imported_on, reviewed_on, verdict }`. `GET /api/research/fund-directory/:uid` returns it as `item.provenance`, and every recorded fund cell of `GET /api/research/projects/:uid/comparisons` carries the same object. The three dates come from three columns and are never merged: `reported_on` is the row's `fund_date` (when the list recorded the figure), `imported_on` is `as_of` (when the row was copied), `reviewed_on` is `research_fund_sources.reviewed_on` (D597). The older `source` and `as_of` fields stay, so nothing that reads them breaks.
- **Four proposed fields**, each with its own source and dates: manager name, investment stage, investment geography and initial cheque range. They live in a new table, `research_fund_vehicle_facts` (migration `202611180053_7167_research_fund_vehicle_facts.sql`), one row per vehicle and field, with CHECKs that a text field carries text and the cheque carries a range in EUR, USD or GBP. The read answers `item.proposed` with each field `recorded` (value and provenance), `not_recorded` (with the canvas's reason), or `unavailable` when the table cannot be read. A fact read from a list whose terms forbid republishing is not served. The migration writes no rows: no committed list states these fields, and the canvas's figures for them are sample data.
- **A vehicle-labelled history** (`history` on the fund read). The manager's vehicles are the served rows whose `manager_name` fact names the same manager, each by the label its list gives it, with its own size and provenance. Without a recorded manager the list reads Not recorded with the reason. The size observations are this vehicle's alone: its own row, and report rows whose new `vehicle_label` column matches its label. The report rows written before this have no label and mixed several vehicles of one manager (Dawn Capital's Opp, IV and III under one uid), so they are counted (`unlabelled_reports`) and never charted.
- **`source_withdrawn`.** A fund still held in the directory but withheld for its list's terms is refused by name, 410 `source_withdrawn`, on `POST /api/research/funds` (Save to shortlist, when `catalogue_uid` names it), `POST …/subjects`, `POST …/comparisons` and `POST …/comparisons/:uid/members`, where it was `not_in_directory` before. A uid the directory never held is still `not_in_directory`. The fund read's 410 gains `error: 'source_withdrawn'`. A failed check on the save does not block it; the facts are gated on every read.
- **The comparison's fund rows** follow FND-P06: Manager, Reported size, Vintage, Manager HQ, Stated focus, Label in the list, Investment stage, Source use, then the two rows the table already held (Notable LPs, EIF / EIFO), kept rather than dropped. Source use is the list's verdict with its reviewed and imported dates.

### What the pages now do

- The Sheet (`FundDirectory.jsx`'s drawer) shows only what its own read answered: no facts and no action while loading, Unavailable with Retry when the read fails (it used to keep the list row's facts), and the Withdrawn panel on a 410. Its facts start with the dated reported size and its source chip, then vintage, manager HQ (where the manager is based), stated focus and the proposed fields; the old per-manager report list is gone from it.
- The profile (`FundCatalogProfile.jsx`) starts with the vehicle, its manager and vintage, the fact strip, How this vehicle invests, the source card with its three dates, the manager's vehicles by label (two that share a label carry their vintage, option A) and this vehicle's observations. The chart that plotted the mixed report rows (`EuroTechFundHistoryChart.jsx`) is removed.
- The three action names: Save to shortlist (Open dossier → once saved), Add to research project, Compare in project. The profile matches the shortlist on `catalogue_uid`, never on the normalised name.
- Save, Add and Compare re-read the fund first (`rereadBeforeAction`). A 410 clears the Sheet's or the page's facts, removes the actions and says that nothing was saved.
- Back to results returns to the directory address the Sheet was opened from (its search, filters, sort and page, D4612); a direct visit goes to the directory tab.
- "Unavailable" replaces "Unreadable" on the funds screens and in comparison cells (Platform States, D5129).

### Not built here, and why

The project picker showing membership and archived projects read-only, Compare in project into an existing comparison with n of 6, restoring the selected row and focus on Back to results, and the phone comparison by fund and by attribute are frontend-only and remain on #7167. Owner option A for a withdrawn fund's dossier (keep the founder's notes, drop the directory facts) is the dossier's (#7168). A withdrawn fund keeps its comparison column until removed, as main already does.
