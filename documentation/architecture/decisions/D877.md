## D877 — A call's audio kept apart from its text: per-member retention, the founder's delete controls, Meeting Capture's readiness check and runbook

- **Status:** filed by S21 for S1 as #2524 (M8), part of #2516; built by S15. Migration 530.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `e19ff787`, then `784a1bed` (after D870, D857 and D875 merged). Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Builds on:** D657 (sessions, their R2 chunks and `deleteSession`), D684 and D757 (Zoom and Meet transcripts), D872 (an uploaded recording, #2519), D754 and D756 (consent and the recording notice), D757 §2.13 (an accepted finding outlives its session).
- **Covers M1 (#2517, D870, merged as #2543):** Zoom audio transcribed by Workers AI. Its audio lands as session chunks under the call's session (`meetingAudio.ts`, `putChunk`), so this retention and the audio-only delete cover it unchanged. Its `recording.completed` event is on the readiness test's tenancy gate.

### 1. What a "call" is

A session with a Zoom or Google Meet ref (`interview_channel_refs`), or an uploaded recording (`submitted_via = 'upload'`). Its audio is the R2 chunks in `interview_session_chunks`; its text is the segments.

The in-person recorder's audio is not a call. It is kept until the founder deletes it, as before. The issue names "call audio", and a recording made in the room is a different promise to the interviewee; a retention for it is a separate choice for the owner.

### 2. Retention (migration 530)

- **`member_call_audio_settings`:** one row per member who chose. No row means the default. `call_audio_retention_days` NULL means keep until deleted. Whole days, 1 to 3650.
- **The default is 30 days** (owner question 1 on #2524, answer A, the issue's recommendation). It is one constant, `DEFAULT_CALL_AUDIO_RETENTION_DAYS`. Answer B (90) or C (keep) is a one-line change.
- **The routes,** on the session router (`routes/founder_validate_sessions.ts`), the caller's own setting:
  - `GET /api/founder/validate/sessions/audio-retention` returns `{ days, chosen, default_days, min_days, max_days }`;
  - `PUT` takes `{ days: 1..3650 | null }`; anything else is 400 `invalid_retention` and changes nothing.
- **The purge** (`services/callAudioRetention.ts`, `purgeExpiredCallAudio`) runs in the daily cron at 03:50 UTC, beside the channel purge, in bounded batches (10 of 100).
  - A call's audio goes when its newest chunk is older than its founder's retention. Counting from the newest chunk means a recording is never cut while it is still arriving.
  - A session whose processing is still moving is left for the next run, so audio is never taken from under a transcription. A failed run's audio goes at retention, like any other.
  - **The text stays.** The segments, marks, receipts, digest and accepted items are untouched.
  - The session records `audio_removed_at` and `audio_removed_reason` (`retention`), and one `activity_logs` row names the session, the reason and the count, never a word of the call.

### 3. Delete controls

- **The audio only:** `DELETE /api/founder/validate/sessions/:id/audio`. The issue writes `/api/validate/…`; the session routes are mounted at `/api/founder/validate`, so it sits beside `DELETE /sessions/:id`.
  - It calls `deleteSessionAudio` in `services/interviewSessions.ts`: the R2 objects first, under the feature's prefix only, then the chunk rows, then the marker (`deleted`) and the audit row.
  - It is behind `scopeSession` like every session route, and waits, 409 `transcribing`, while a run is moving.
  - The first reason stands: a later purge does not overwrite "deleted" with "retention".
  - Playback then answers 404 `audio_removed` with the reason and a sentence, instead of "no recorded audio".
- **The whole session:** `DELETE /sessions/:id`, unchanged, removes both.
- **An accepted item stays.** `findingsFor` (`services/interviewSuggestions.ts`) now returns `source_deleted`: true when the finding's session no longer exists. The module record's line reads "<interviewee> · Source deleted", next to its interview link. Removing only the audio is not a deleted source.
- **Nothing is restored from a backup** into a deleted session or a removed recording. The R2 delete is immediate.

### 4. The readiness check

`test/meeting_readiness_d877.test.ts`, in D861's shape (#2506): each gate is a scenario run against the real modules, then against an isolated source mutation (read, changed in memory, imported from a `data:` URL; no tracked file changes) that must fail it.

| Gate | What it asserts | Mutations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signature | Zoom's HMAC: ok, mismatch, missing, stale | a mismatch accepted; the clock ignored |
| Tenancy | an unscheduled meeting is ignored with nothing claimed or queued, for `recording.transcript_completed` and M1's `recording.completed`; a known one's import is queued by the ref alone | an unscheduled meeting taken; the payload riding on the job |
| Scope | a Zoom grant wider than the registry's is overreach; Meet transcripts need `meetings.space.readonly`, which is read-only | a wider grant accepted; Meet without the scope |
| Consent and notice | no audio work without recording consent; the notice at `/privacy/interviews`; an upload refused without the founder's attestation | consent bypassed; the notice moved; attestation dropped |
| Metering | a minute of upload audio is charged on the AI token ledger; with no AI tokens it is refused, says so, and calls no model | minutes zeroed; the budget refusal hidden |
| Purge | a call's audio past retention goes and its text stays; a young call and the recorder's audio stay | retention never reached; the recorder taken; a young call taken |

The `mutate` helper is D861's, copied; #2535 moves it to a shared harness, and this copy follows it there.
