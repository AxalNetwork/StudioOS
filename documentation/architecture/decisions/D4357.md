### 1. The read

`GET /api/legal/incorporation/itemised-quote?jurisdiction_id=<id>` comes from `routes/legal_incorporation_quote.ts`, mounted before `legal.ts`, over `services/incorporationQuote.ts` `quoteOf`. It answers `{ jurisdiction_id, label, lines, total }`. Every line carries:
- `key` and `label`;
- `amount_cents`, null when not known and never 0;
- `currency` and `interval` (`year` for a subscription);
- `optional`;
- `paid`: `at_filing`, `with_order` or `after_you_pay`;
- `source`, `source_url` and `checked_on` (YYYY-MM-DD, or null).

It reads only. Who: anyone signed in, as the one-price quote. An unknown jurisdiction is refused with 400 `unknown_jurisdiction`.

### 2. Where each fee comes from

| Line | Source | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| **State filing fee** (paid to the state at filing) | `STATE_FILING_FEES`: one reviewed entry per jurisdiction the Worker forms, each naming the official schedule. Delaware Division of Corporations (both Delaware entities), Wyoming Secretary of State, Companies House, ACRA, Estonian e-Business Register. | The day someone read that schedule and wrote the figure. **No figure is recorded today:** each entry is null and reads "Not recorded". |
| **Axal VC service fee** (charged with the order) | The Stripe catalog's incorporation SKU for the jurisdiction (`resolveIncorporationPrice`): the same price `GET /incorporation/quote` states and `POST /incorporation/order` charges. | The catalog mirror's `synced_at` for that product. |
| **Registered agent** (optional, a year at a time, added after you pay) | The catalog's registered-agent subscription (`resolveRegisteredAgentOffer`). | Its product's `synced_at`. |
| **Optional add-ons** | Any other active catalog product tagged `metadata.category = 'incorporation_addon'`. None is configured today. | Its product's `synced_at`. |

**Why the state fees are empty.** A state fee is a public figure that changes, and the founder relies on it before paying, so it is written only with the day it was read from the named schedule. A figure written without that day is not shown (`quoteOf` drops it), and `incorporation_quote_d4357` test 4 fails on one.

This session could not reach the official schedules (corp.delaware.gov, sos.wyo.gov, gov.uk and the others are outside its network), so no figure is guessed. Recording one is a one-line change to `STATE_FILING_FEES`: the amount, its currency and the day it was read.

The wizard's `est_cost_usd` ranges and the old `JURISDICTION_COSTS` stay what D362 says they are: estimates, not a price, and never a line here.

### 3. The total

The total is the sum of the known lines that make up the filing, the state fee and the service fee, in the quote's currency (the service fee's).
- It is `complete` only when both are known in that currency, and `unknown` names the ones that are not.
- A fee in another currency (a UK fee in GBP beside a USD service fee) is not added in. It counts as not known for the total.
- Optional lines are listed apart and never added in.
- The total is null when no line in it is known.

### 4. The view

On Incorporate's review step (step 4 of 6), beside the one-price order card, **What it costs** lists each line in the total:
- its amount, or "Not recorded";
- when it is paid ("Paid to the state at filing", "Charged with your order");
- its source, a link when it is an https address, and "checked 4 Nov 2026" or "not checked yet".

Then:
- **Total**, with "The sum of the known lines only. Not recorded: State filing fee. The full cost is not known yet." whenever a line is unknown;
- "Optional, not in the total" and the optional lines.

A failed read says "The itemised quote could not be read." with Try again, and the step still works. The pay step and the order card are unchanged: the order still charges the one catalog price (Q5).
