## D6006 part 2 — Problem discovery v2: the ranked list, the selection bar and the theme record, drawn (#6006)

Part of #6006. Part 1 (D6006, #6786) is the Worker read; this part draws it to boards V17 to V20 of the Validate v2 Discovery and ICP canvas (D5995). It adds no route and changes no Worker file.

### The ranked list (V17)

- **Rank and reason.** The theme cell reads "Why rank 1: Most mentions · 6 from ICP · mostly Severe", from `rank` and `rank_reason` (`themeNotes`, `adapters/validate.js`).
- **Not grouped.** A phrase in no theme reads "#4 · Not grouped · no record to open". Its `id` is null, so its row links nowhere.
- **Sources beside mentions.** "7 interviews" has "7 people · 7 organisations" under it (`sourcesLine`). A count no interview recorded says "not recorded", never 0. A loose phrase has no sources line.
- **Severity.** Each severity carries a word and a glyph (●●● Severe, ●●○ Real, ●○○ Mild, ○ Not judged; `severityPill`).
- **Hypotheses.** They read "+ H1 · − H2" with "1 supporting · 0 contradicting" under them (`hypothesesCell`). A theme with no link reads "No hypothesis linked".
- **Cell notes.** These lines are a generic `note` on a table cell (`cellView`, `Cell`). On a phone, the title cell's first note follows the second cell (`phoneRow`).
- **The stats strip** has the canvas's six tiles, each naming what it counts and over which period: Themes, Interviews, Phrases in no theme, Need-to-have, ICP mention and Fit not recorded.

### The selection bar (V17)

- **Choosing quotes.** A writer chooses unthemed quotes by box. The bar then says "N selected". Assign to theme or New theme from selected sends one `painThemesApi.assignMany` write (part 1's `assign-many`).
- **Saving.** A saved assign clears the selection. A refused one keeps it and shows the Worker's sentence as an alert.
- **The per-quote select.** It stays for a caller that passes no `onAssignMany`.
- **The note** says the panel counts each phrase once per interview, which is not the same as Phrases in no theme.

### The theme record (V19)

- **Problem statement.** The section, and its Write the problem statement form, are the Worker's (part 1). The generic record page draws both, with no frontend form spec.
- **Under the sections,** `ThemeRecordParts` draws:
  - the sources;
  - the links in each direction;
  - Used on slide as **Unavailable** with the Worker's reason, then Open the Problem slide;
  - the handoffs: See who has it (Ideal customer profile) for every reader, and Write a hypothesis from this (New hypothesis) for a writer only.

  Every link carries `project_id`.

### Departures from the canvas

- **Write a hypothesis from this** opens New hypothesis without the theme already chosen. The Add form takes no prefill from its address.
- **See who has it** opens Ideal customer profile unfiltered. Its list has no "names this theme" filter.
- **Theme · mentions** draws no bar of "N of 12 interviews". The table's cells are text and pills.
- **Rank column.** The rank sits in the theme's note rather than its own `#` column, because the columns are the Worker's (`LISTS`).
- **Each row's ⋯ menu** (Open the record, Write a hypothesis, See who has it) is not drawn. The row opens its record, and the record carries the two handoffs.
- **Share link and PDF** of the pain map are not drawn. The canvas draws them disabled, and they need a public read and a renderer (a later part).
- **Eadwyn's grouping proposals** in the rail, accepted as a set, are not drawn. No suggestion kind proposes a grouping yet. When one does, accepting the set will call `assign-many`.
- **Eadwyn's draft problem statement** (V19's draft state) is not drawn, because nothing drafts it.

### Unchanged

- **Merge, Split, Edit, Delete, Attach a quote and Link** are the Worker's record actions, drawn as before.
- **Quote consent.** A reader without write access sees no unthemed panel, no boxes and no quote.
- **A failed read** says Unavailable (D5129).
