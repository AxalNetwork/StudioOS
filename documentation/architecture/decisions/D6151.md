### Rules

- **Access** keeps its three states apart (D4815):
  - open: Open deal;
  - Invitation needed: Request an invitation, or Requested · Leave;
  - Access unreadable: Try again, never a request.
- **Request an invitation and Leave are Review → Result.**
  - The review names the room and says what the action records and what it does not.
  - Nothing is written before it is confirmed.
  - The result line names the room.
- **With the allowance full,** Request stays on a refused row and opens the full message in place of the review (DE20): "You are in 2 of 2 rooms on the Plus plan. Leave a room, or move to Professional for 5." Professional names Institutional as unlimited.
- **Refusals and failures stay on the row,** which keeps its state, and each names the room (DE22):
  - the 402, re-reading the allowance and naming the next plan;
  - the 409 from another firm;
  - a request, leave or add that failed, with Try again.
- **Add to my deal flow** is in a row's More menu, and lands the deal in Sourcing ("Added to Sourcing"). A deal already in the flow says so.
- **Each view has its own empty board and action** (DE21):
  - All live deals: Go to Deal flow;
  - You can open: Show all live deals;
  - Requested: Show all live deals.
- **On a phone (below 768px)** the rows are cards: venture, chips, round, invitation, access, and one action at full width. The views are a segmented control with short labels, and nothing scrolls sideways (DE23).
- **Dark** reads the platform's dark theme (DE24).
