## D759 — A submitted Subscription Booklet is signed electronically: the applicant on Parts 3 to 8, then the General Partner on Part 9, each over the frozen copy under the booklet's digest

Status: Decided. Date: 2026-10-18. Slot: S10. Issue: #1858. Decided by S1 under the owner's standing rule.

D716 (#1781) stopped the booklet at Submit: `POST /api/fund-booklets/mine/submit` froze it (`frozen_json` and its SHA-256 `digest`), and nothing sent it for signature. Decision 9 there deferred the send, and D716.md item 15 said "the e-signature send S1 files next signs the frozen copy". This records that send.

### Decisions

1. **What is signed: the frozen copy, under the booklet's digest.**
   - Each envelope's `document_body` is the booklet's `frozen_json`, sent through `createAndSendEnvelope({ renderedBody })`, so its `body_sha256` is the booklet's `digest`, with no change to `createAndSendEnvelope`. The send checks the stored hash; a mismatch records nothing, voids that envelope and answers 409 `booklet_text_changed`.
   - Every page prints the parts at the versions frozen at submit, with the frozen ticks (`bodyAtVersion`, `frozenBodies`): the live row when its version is the frozen one, else the `legal_template_versions` snapshot an edit took. Its SHA-256 must equal the frozen hash; a missing version or a different hash is 409 `booklet_text_changed`, naming the parts.
   - The recorded format is `{ kind: 'booklet', party: 'fund' }`; `document_type` is `subscription_booklet`. Native provider only.
2. **Two stages, two envelopes, one link table** (migration 485, `fund_booklet_envelopes`): one row per stage, `applicant` or `gp`, with the booklet's digest. Append-only, as 478's tables are. The booklet table does not change.
3. **Who sends and who signs.**
   - **The applicant stage:** `POST /api/fund-booklets/mine/sign`. The sender, the subject and the one recipient are the applicant's own account; the recipient name is their legal name, else their account name (`applicantName`). The caller is that recipient, signed in, so the route answers the signing token and the page opens `/esign/:token` at once; the email still goes out. The token is never logged or audited.
   - **The GP stage** is sent when the applicant's envelope completes (decision 6). The recipient is the HQ Super Admin whose email is `AXAL_COUNTERSIGNER_EMAIL` (else `legal@axal.vc`), so only that person, signed in, can sign (`signer_identity_mismatch` otherwise); the name is `AXAL_SIGNATORY_NAME`. With no Super Admin at that address the stage is not sent (`fund_signer_missing`): a bearer link is not enough to bind the Fund. The sender stays the applicant, so the accepted copy lands in their Documents & agreements and the D410 sender notice tells them.
   - Both stages send as "Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP". Titles: "Subscription Booklet · v[N] · Parts 3 to 8" and "Subscription Booklet · v[N] · Part 9", where v[N] is the booklet's edition for its application (the count of its booklets with `id <= booklet.id`; booklets are never deleted).
4. **What each page prints.**
   - The nine parts in order, from the frozen text.
   - The applicant's adopted signature on every applicant line (Part 3's counterparty and Parts 4 to 8); the Fund signatory's on every Fund line (Part 3's Fund party and Part 9). `PaperPartInput.signed` places each by the party's `role`; with no `signed` the paper is unchanged.
   - Each signature keeps its own copy (D711 decision 7): the GP's copy prints the applicant's lines as signed, with their time and envelope, and no image, because none is stored. Each copy ends with its own certificate, the digest as its SHA-256.
   - **The map's audience** (`bookletAudience`): every booklet paper passes `issued` to `structureForPaper` only once the GP stage is completed, else `preview`. "Issued" for the booklet means accepted; the booklet says "Send no money until you receive a Capital Call Notice." The stamp is unchanged.
   - A submitted booklet's own paper (`/mine/paper`, `/:uid/pdf`) prints the frozen text too; an open booklet keeps today's current-body paper.
5. **Refusals of `POST /mine/sign`**, in order, each through `refuse()`: 404 `booklet_not_found`; 403 `support_session_refused`; 403 `hq_only`; 409 `booklet_not_submitted`; 409 `booklet_already_sent` (`extra: { state }`); 409 `booklet_part_missing` (a part is a stub in the store now; `extra: { parts }`); 409 `booklet_text_changed`; 503 `booklet_send_failed`. On `already_pending` from a concurrent send, the other envelope is recorded as the stage when it carries the digest and no row exists, then the answer is `booklet_already_sent`.
6. **After the applicant signs**, the winner of the completion UPDATE calls `onBookletEnvelopeCompleted` (imported on demand, as the other hooks are): an applicant row sends the GP stage; a gp row writes nothing. It never fails the signature and logs only a code (`booklet_gp_stage_failed` or `fund_signer_missing`). The applicant stage skips the two generic `contract_signed` notices, which would say "fully signed" before the Fund has signed; the GP stage keeps them.
7. **A Super Admin can send the GP stage again:** `POST /api/fund-booklets/:uid/acceptance`, HQ only, D714 act `fund_booklet.accept` (tier `super_admin`, owner #1858, "A Super Admin sends a booklet to the General Partner for acceptance."), asserted after the `hq_only` refusal. Refusals: 404 `booklet_not_found` (also a withdrawn booklet), 409 `booklet_not_signed`, 409 `booklet_already_sent`, 409 `fund_signer_missing`, 409 `booklet_text_changed`. It answers 201 `{ uid, stage: 'gp', envelope_uuid }` with no token and is audited through `auditFundAct` with uids only.
8. **Withdrawal and envelopes.** Withdrawing voids the booklet's pending envelopes (`sent` or `partially_signed` to `void`) in the same `DB.batch` as the withdrawal, so the next booklet's send does not meet D410's one-pending key. A completed envelope is never touched. An accepted booklet cannot be withdrawn (409 `booklet_accepted`), checked before the batch and again inside the withdrawal's own UPDATE, so a GP signature landing meanwhile wins.
9. **The booklet view gains `edition` and `signing: { state, signed_at, accepted_at }`**, where `state` is `not_sent`, `sent`, `awaiting_gp`, `accepted` or `void`. The Admin's read also answers `gp_sent`. `GET /sign/:token` gains `booklet: { stage, edition }` for a booklet envelope only.
10. **What the screens say** is PR 2's (issue #1858, decision 10).
11. **What this never does:** it writes no legal text and changes no template body; rewrites no envelope; sends nothing through DocuSign, Stripe or Mercury; puts no signing token in a log, an audit row or an Admin response; stores no signature image outside the signed PDF.

### As built (PR 1)

- `cloudflare-worker/sql/migrations/485_fund_booklet_envelopes.sql`: the table; `BEFORE INSERT` triggers (a submitted booklet under its own digest; a `gp` row only after the `applicant` row); `BEFORE UPDATE` and `BEFORE DELETE` raise "fund_booklet_envelopes is append-only (D759)".
- `services/bookletSigning.ts`: `stageTitle`, `sendApplicantStage`, `sendGpStage`, `bookletLink`, `bookletSignInfo`, `onBookletEnvelopeCompleted` and `bookletEnvelopeModel`. Not in `FUND_MODULES` (D707's pattern does not match it); the Stripe walk from `routes/fund_booklets.ts` still finds no Stripe code.
- **Departure from the issue's file plan:** `bodyAtVersion`, `bookletSigning`, `bookletEdition` and the frozen-paper assembly live in `services/subscriptionBooklet.ts`, beside the store, rather than in `bookletSigning.ts`, so the imports run one way (`bookletSigning.ts` imports the store; the store imports nothing back). `frozenBookletPaperInput` is `bookletPaperInput(env, booklet, signed)`, whose frozen branch is the one part assembly (`bookletParts`) both papers share.
- **Departure: the pdf-lib fallback draws a composite paper's parts** (`services/pdf.ts`, `drawParts`). It drew only the cover and the top-level body, which for the booklet is empty, so a signed copy drawn while Browser Run was down would have carried none of the parts it was signed over. Each part now prints on its own page with its block, the Structure of the Fund list after Part 2.
- `routes/esign.ts`: the three signer routes and the completion block take a booklet branch for `subscription_booklet` only; the booklet's model is built before the claim, so a refusal changes nothing. Every other envelope takes exactly today's path.
