## D1136 — Companies and projects part 2, the Worker: my companies in one light read, my ventures for every profile, and the invitations addressed to me, answered while signed in

- **Status:** built by S24 (claimed for slot S16's queue at the owner's request), 2026-10-25, for #3016, part 2 of #2422.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `a265fb2561`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`. D1134 drew the page; this is its Worker.
- **Migration:** one is needed, for company decline only (§4). Its number is asked on #3016. Nothing else here changes the schema.

All of it is in `routes/account_companies.ts`, mounted at `/api` with its paths written out, before `projects` and `companyRoutes`. Every route needs a session.

**Each block is one of two shapes:**
- `{ available: true, value }`;
- `{ available: false, reason }` when its store could not be read. That is never a zero.

**What is never returned:** no member email, invitation address, token or token hash. Logs hold the block's name only.

### 1. `GET /api/account/companies`

One entry per company the caller belongs to, in the switcher's order: primary admin first, then the oldest link. Each entry carries:
- **from the link and the company:** `uid`, `company_name`, `stage` and `logo_url`, with `my_role`, `title` and `authority` (null is "not recorded", migration 191) and `is_primary_admin`;
- **blocks:**
  - `member_count`;
  - `cycles_active`, from `services/companySheet.ts`, so the rule is the switcher's;
  - `last_opened`;
  - `counts`, per company: `projects` (not trashed), `funds` (`vc_funds`), `engagements`, `quotes` and `offers` (`service_offerings`). A profile's page reads the counts it shows (the canvas: an investor's funds, a partner's engagements).
- **`pending_invitations`, for an editor only.** That is an Owner, an Admin, a Founder or the primary admin, which is `company.ts`'s `canEdit`. An invitation names someone's address, so even its count is the editors'. For anyone else the field is absent, not null. Lapsed invitations are not counted.

**Statements:** one for the links, then one grouped statement per store over the company ids (`json_each`), instead of the memberships read's per-company loop (#2422 item 11). The links failing is a 503 (`companies_unreadable`).

### 2. `GET /api/account/projects`

**What is listed:** the caller's own ventures (`projects.founder_id`) and accepted seats (`project_members`, accepted and not removed), for every profile. Trashed ventures are left out.
- **Check: this is never the privileged arm of `GET /api/projects`.** An investor with no seat gets `[]`, not every venture.

**Each entry carries:**
- `my_seat`: `owner`, `cofounder` or `advisor`;
- the company: `company_id`, `company_uid` and `company_name`;
- `stage` and `playbook_week`;
- `member_count`: the accepted seats plus the owner, counted whether or not `projects.ts` has yet seeded the owner's row;
- `cycles_active`, per venture.

**The per-venture cycles statement** is written out over CYCLE_STORES' seventeen tables, because the SQL guard allows no composed statement. A test holds it to `CYCLE_STORES`, so a store added there and not here fails the build.

A failed venture list is a 503 (`projects_unreadable`).

### 3. `GET /api/account/invitations`

Two blocks, `company` and `project`, each listing pending invitations that are open and on a live company or venture.

- **Company invitations:** to the caller's address, matched case-insensitively.
- **Venture invitations:** to the caller's account, or to their address when the invitation names no account. An invitation that names an account belongs to that account, whatever address it also carries.
  - **A bare link names no one,** so it is never listed: only its token answers it.
- **Investors** are never project members (`projects.ts`). Their venture invitations are listed with `can_accept: false`.

Each carries who sent it (their name, never their address) and when it was sent and when it lapses.

### 4. Answering while signed in

**The binding is the token routes'.** A company invitation is answered only by its address; a venture invitation only by the account it names, or else by its address. An invitation that is not the caller's reads as 404, so its existence is not learned. Refusals go through `refuse` with a code:

| Answer | Refusals |
|---|---|
| any | `not_found`; a closed invitation is 410 with its status; a lapsed one is 410 `expired`, and is marked expired |
| accept, venture | `investor` (403) |
| any, raced | `already_answered` (409) |

- **`POST /api/company/invitations/:uid/accept`** joins the company with the invitation's role, title and authority, never as primary admin. Someone already a member stays so, with `already_member: true`.
- **`POST /api/projects/invitations/:id/accept`:**
  - takes the invitation's seat (`cofounder` or `advisor`);
  - seeds the owner's row as `projects.ts` does;
  - the owner accepting one to their own venture takes no seat.
- **`POST /api/projects/invitations/:id/decline`** marks it declined.
- **Check: each accept is one batch.** The seat rows are written only while the invitation is still pending, and the answer is claimed in the same transaction. So two answers at once cannot both land, and a failed write leaves the invitation open. A test races each accept.

**Company decline is not built yet.** `company_invitations.status` is CHECK-limited to pending, accepted, revoked and expired (migration 236), so `declined` cannot be stored.
- **What the owner chose:** a migration rebuilding the table with `declined` allowed and a `declined_at` column. Its number is asked on #3016.
- **What lands with it:** the route, its client and its tests. Its client is not in `accountCompanies.js` until the route exists, so the drift test stays true.

**The token routes are unchanged.** That is `POST /api/company/invitations/accept` and `POST /api/projects/invitations/accept`.
