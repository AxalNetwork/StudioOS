### Built (#8776)

Built to option A for all three owner questions, as Session 1 asked on the issue (2026-11-27).

- **Product roadmap** (`RoadmapBoard.jsx`): the link above the board reads "Quarters, dependencies and scenarios ›" with one line, "The same N items placed by quarter, with what waits on what and saved what-ifs." It opens `/build/roadmap?project_id=…&view=quarters` and hands the board's own address over in history state. Board or Timeline (`?view=`), the phone column (`?column=`) and a linked item (`?item=`, which opens its column and focuses its card) are in the address, each written with `replace`, so the Back link and the browser's Back both restore them; Back from the landing puts focus on the link that left.
- **The item sheet** (`services/records/build.ts`): Quarter sits beside Column (a record carries at most five facts); a side section, "Quarter, links and scenarios", gives the key results (Not recorded when none) and links "See its quarter, links and scenarios" to `/build/roadmap?project_id=…&view=quarters&item=<id>`. It is a section row, not an action, so a reader who cannot write still has it. Edit carries a Quarter field (placeholder `Q1 2027`).
- **The quarter write** (◇, `cycle_build.ts` `patchRoadmap`): `quarter` is accepted on `PATCH /api/cycles/build/roadmap/:id` and through the item's Edit. `quarterValue` keeps one spelling, `Q1 2027` (`2027-Q1`, `q1 2027` and `Q1-2027` are the same quarter); anything else is a 400, "Write the quarter as Q1 2027", and nothing is written. Empty or null clears it; a body that names no quarter keeps it. The activity row says "Quarter changed".
- **`can_write`** (◇, `founder_roadmap.ts`): the read returns `can_write` from the same `canWrite` the writes use; each item also carries `target_date` and `owner`, for the By quarter rows.
- **The landing** (`FounderBuildRoadmap.jsx`, rules in `pages/cycles/roadmapShortcut.js`): h1 "Quarters, dependencies and scenarios" (focused on arrival), the sub from BLR02, a crumb Build / Product roadmap / this page, and "‹ Back to Product roadmap" (to the board as it was left) or "‹ Back to <item>" (to that item's sheet). A `from` address is followed only when it is Product roadmap's own path. The venture card says it was kept from the link, and the card "How this relates to Now, Next and Later" sits above the counts (a `<details>`, open, so it folds on a phone). Views By quarter (default), Dependencies and Scenarios are in `?view=`; the retired `board` and `timeline` read as By quarter. By quarter groups by the stored quarter in calendar order, then any quarter stored another way, then No quarter recorded with a link to set it on each item. Arriving with `?item=`, that row carries `aria-current` and the words "Opened from Product roadmap" and takes focus; an item not on the roadmap is said. Dependencies' one primary is Edit dependency links; Scenarios' is New scenario.
- **States:** zero items show one action, "Add the first item" → `/build/product-roadmap?project_id=…&add=1`, and no relation card, counts or views; the `/execution` editor is no longer linked (Q2). A reader (`can_write: false`) gets a Read only line and no edit control. A `project_id` that is not the reader's is said ("Project N isn't one you can open · Showing …") rather than swapped silently. A failed read says "Unavailable", never 0, with Retry; the Back link still works. No venture reads "No venture is available" with Back to Build.
- **Q3:** the zone row (This week · Board · Roadmap · Cadence · KPI entry) and the Board and Timeline views are gone from `/build/roadmap`; `founderZoneFilters`' `build/roadmap` entry is By quarter · Dependencies · Scenarios.
