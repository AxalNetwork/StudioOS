### What was decided

1. **Three tabs, one address each:**
   - `/admin/workgraph/in-flight` (Work in flight);
   - `/admin/workgraph/pairs` (PR pairs);
   - `/admin/workgraph/evidence` (PR evidence).

   They are in `frontend/src/routes/adminWorkgraph.jsx`, HQ only. The page is linked from `CONSOLE_GROUPS` › Infrastructure on the Platform overview, and has a WorkGraph row in Platform's strip (`HQ_STRIPS`, `App.jsx`), after Meeting bots, so the strip still mirrors the console groups (D4860). The row lights on the Work in flight tab only, because a strip item owns its own address and the paths below it. The three tabs share one rail key, off the AIRail canvas (`railPages.js`).
2. **One read, one page state:** loading; refused (the read's 403, for anyone but a Super Admin; nothing about the scan is shown); unreadable; none posted yet; stored scan unreadable; or read. No state is drawn as zero or as an empty board.
3. **Every count names its scan:** for example, "2 to serialize in the 14:31 UTC scan". A partial scan prints its notes and every count reads "≥ n". A scan past the read's stale line gets a Stale banner with its time and age.
4. **Work in flight (WG-01):**
   - **Summary strip:** claims held, each action, expired-but-still-held, and refused.
   - **Claims table:** sorted by expiry, as the scan sorts it. Each row has issue and holder, registration, base, PRs/paths/depends, and action and risk. A disclosure lists the reasons, highest rule first.
   - **Below the table:** Refused, Notes and Scan notes.
   - **When claims were not read or are Unreadable:** the page says so ("not 'no work'") and draws no counts. The other two tabs still show.
5. **PR pairs (WG-02):**
   - The risk-class legend ("a risk signal, not a merge verdict").
   - Pairs grouped by top folder, each group with Integration owner Not recorded.
   - Per pair: risk class, Conflict Yes/No/Not checked, and shared migration stamps.
   - A disclosure per pair with its hunk proof.
6. **PR evidence (WG-03):** merge status, required checks, CI queue delay and base, as four separate cells, each carrying the read's reason when it is Unknown or Not measured. Review delay is Not recorded on every row.
7. **Not recorded is printed with its reason**, not left on hover: lease, owner override, estimated lifespan, integration owner, review delay, and a label or STATUS claim's missing times and base. This uses the shared `Unrecorded`.
8. **Phone and dark:** below `md`, the tables become cards and every target is at least 44px. Every light surface carries its dark pair (`check-dark-mode`).
9. **GitHub links** open in a new tab and are built only from the snapshot's `owner/repo`.

| Board | Built | Departs | Why |
