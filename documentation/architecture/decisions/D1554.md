## D1554 — Magic Switch: 16:9 ↔ 4:3, with what no longer fits moved to the speaker notes

- **Status:** Built by the S17 session (#3801, deck v2 C6, part of #3716; 2026-11-01).
- **Builds on:** D1499 (#3749: The Grid, `boxOfAt` at both aspects, ⋯ › Slide size writing `theme.aspect`) and D898 §8 (`textCapacity`, `fitProblems`).
- **Migration:** none.
- **Scope** is S1's revision on #3801 (2026-11-01): **16:9 ↔ 4:3 only**, because pd13's ⋯ › THIS DECK › "Slide size 16:9 · 4:3" is the only format control on the final canvas. The other formats are not built here: 1:1, 4:5, 1.91:1, 9:16 and A4/Letter portrait wait on an owner question, and the one-pager is #3830 F4's.

### Why

At 4:3, D1499 keeps the 12 × 6 grid, its rows and its margins, so no block loses a cell. But a 4:3 cell is narrower: 1440 wide against 1920, with the same margins and gutters. So a line holds fewer characters, and a text block that fits at 16:9 can overflow at 4:3. A metric tile's figure has the same problem. Before this decision, `fitProblems` measured at 16:9 only, so a 4:3 deck could overflow and nothing would say so. D1499 §14 said "No block moves to the notes", which is true of cells but not of characters.

### What was decided

1. **Capacity reads the aspect.** `textCapacity(g, role, aspect)` and `fitProblems(slides, aspect)` measure with `boxOfAt`, as does the new `metricValueChars(g, aspect)`. Each defaults to 16:9, where every result is what it was (a test checks every range and role). A problem found at 4:3 says "at 4:3". A chart's series and points, a diagram's columns and a tile's rows do not depend on the aspect.
2. **The reflow** is `reflowSlides(slides, to, from)` (`services/decks/reflow.ts`). It is pure.
   - **A text block** keeps the words that fit at the target aspect, cut at the last word boundary. The rest goes to the card's speaker notes. A block where no word fits leaves the slide, and all of its text goes to the notes.
   - **A metric** whose figure no longer fits its tile leaves the slide, and "label: figure" goes to the notes.
   - **Where the notes go.** Moved text is filed under "Moved from the slide at 4:3:", after the founder's own notes, within the 4,000-character notes limit.
   - **What does not change:** blocks keep their cells, and areas and structure are untouched.
   - **The guarantee:** the result has zero `fitProblems` at the target aspect for anything the aspect decides, and no word is lost (the kept words, then the moved words, are the original). Back to 16:9, nothing moves.
   - **The count:** `count` is the number of blocks that moved, in whole or in part. It is pd3c's "[N]".
3. **The reflow map** (`reflowMap()`) gives each structure's areas at both aspects, and the share of width 4:3 keeps (between 0.7 and 1). Heights and rows never change.
4. **Served through the grid route, not a new route family.** `GET /api/decks/:id/grid?aspect=16:9|4:3` adds two things for the saved version:
   - `reflow: { from, to, count, moved: [{ card, block, kind: 'trimmed' | 'removed', text }] }`;
   - `reflow_map`.

   Any other aspect is 400 `invalid_aspect`. The read writes nothing. The aspect is written with the version's theme, as D1499 §14 decided.
5. **The editor reflows its draft.** In ⋯ › Slide size (`SizeSheet`), choosing a size calls `changeSize`, which runs `reflowCards` (`frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/deckReflow.js`, the Worker's reflow mirrored; `deck_reflow_d1554.test.ts` holds the capacities and the reflow equal on every range and fixture).
   - **The notice.** When anything moved, pd3c's amber notice reads "[N] blocks no longer fit and moved to the speaker notes." ("1 block no longer fits…" for one), with **one Undo**. Undo puts back the cards and the size as they were before the switch.
   - **Undo lasts while the sheet is open.** Closing the sheet keeps the change and drops the Undo.
   - **Saving.** Like every edit, the change is held until a version is saved.
