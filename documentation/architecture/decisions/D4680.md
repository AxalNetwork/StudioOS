## D4680 — Preserve catalogue permissions and request history (#4680)

Authenticated members can browse live offerings. Draft, unlisted and archived
offerings all use the existing inactive state. They appear only in the owner's
catalogue and detail reads, scoped to the selected company when one is present.
An offering with no company stays in the owner's catalogue across companies.

Removal unlists an offering with requests or market evidence. Only an unused
offering is deleted. The removal checks both dependencies in one D1 batch.
The request insert checks availability again in its write statement, and the
request foreign key restricts catalogue deletion as a second line of protection.

New requests capture the offered USD cents at the moment of insertion. Old
requests retain a null amount. The API's historical `booked_value_cents` key
reports the sum of recorded quotes on the returned catalogue rows, alongside
the count of unpriced requests. A quote does not establish a booking or payment.
