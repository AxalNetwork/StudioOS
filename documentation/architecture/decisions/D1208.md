## D1208 — Four owner-scoped Calendar sources (#3174)

Part of #2420. No migration, binding, secret or provider request.

### Source rules

`services/calendar/sourceReaders.ts` holds one bound-SQL reader per kind:

- `interview_call`: the call reference's recorded owner, while its interview
  and undeleted venture still exist. Cancelled calls stay out. Starts and ends
  normalize legacy SQLite and ISO timestamps to UTC; an absent end remains the start, without an
  invented duration. The join URL is visible only to that owner.
- `cohort_date`: the member's active Lab cohort, using the gate's
  `spinout_lab_started_at` interval, or a current advisor assignment while
  their role remains Advisor. Week openings, week deadlines and the cycle's
  end are separate, stable items in the Lab's canonical New York time zone;
  a cycle end appears once.
- `compliance_due`: outstanding filings and board meetings on the founder's
  own undeleted ventures. Staff-wide access and team access never widen Mine.
- `deal_closing`: an investor's actual deal-room memberships. A demoted
  investor or a deleted venture grants no access. Funded and rejected deals
  no longer contribute an outstanding closing deadline.

Every reader excludes inactive or deletion-requested accounts. The existing
source reads span the member's own records, rather than an admin's studio or
All studios view. SQL normalizes timestamps before comparison and uses an
exclusive upper boundary. All-day obligations use a date-only start and the
following date as their exclusive end. Invalid query results or unreadable
stores fail visibly; they never become an invented empty source.

### Integration prepared

The intended wiring adds these four kinds to Calendar's registry, puts
interview calls in Meetings and the other three in Deadlines, and removes
the two obsolete Not read here yet notices. The page keeps all-day records
on their written date in the viewer's time zone and says All day. Downloaded
ICS uses VALUE=DATE with an exclusive end. None is pushable and the bulk sync
excludes all four before reading/copying sources.
