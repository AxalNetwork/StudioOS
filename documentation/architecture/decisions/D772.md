### 2. The options inventory

`lib/railOptions/investor.js` lists every control on an investor board that runs a model: 15 rows, each with its canvas `page`, its `key` (`<verb>:<object>`, unique on its page), its `label`, one of the canvas's fourteen `kind`s, its `handler` and its `source`. D764 PR 3 (#2089) reads it to give each option the models that fit its work.

- **Drafts (11):** the zone draft bands of Pipeline, Screening, Commit, Closing, Positions, Updates, Value-add, Capital calls (per LP line), Ask, a Diligence room and Library. All run `POST /api/research/drafts`.
- **Forms (2):** Research's "Find evidence" and Ask's "Ask", `POST /api/research/ask`.
- **Uploads (2):** Library's "Add to library" and "Re-index", which embed the document.
- **Not listed:** CRUD writes, the CSV exports (all client-side) and the Network's propositions (a heuristic in the Worker). No investor board offers a fill band or a transcription.
- **The kind is the option's work,** not its board's: Positions' narrative is `numbers`, a memo `reason`, a note or reminder `write`, a read or brief `synth`, a library action `search`.

`frontend/test/rail_options_investor_d772.test.mjs` holds the list to the canvas (every page a board, every kind one of the fourteen) and to the code: every `<ZoneDraft surface=…>` and every `api.research.ask|upload|reindex(` in the files the investor boards render is a row, and every row's control exists. It also renders the two new rails, and an admin's Performance and the Trust chrome without one. 12 mutations were checked, all caught.

### 3. Page keys and options (per section)

D768 (#2092, PR #2133) gives every board its page key from the map at the shell, so no board passes one. Each section's PR names a record view's record as the last crumb, and calls `useRailOption(key, label)` for each row of §2 while the member has it open.

**The draft bands.** D770 (#2095, PR #2163) makes every `ZoneDraft` band name the registry's key, `draft:<surface>`, by its `label`, while a draft runs or is shown. `workspaces/ZoneDraft.jsx` gains `railOption`, the row's key, which takes that key's place under the same rule. The investor's keys (§2) name the work, not the surface, so each investor band passes its own; a band with no `railOption` keeps D770's key.

**Deals (I1–I5).** `/deals` and its four boards answer `Investor · Deals[ · <board>]` from the map. They draw one rail between them, `InvestorDealsRoutes.jsx`'s shell's; the zone pages draw none. The four bands pass `draft:stale-deal-notes`, `draft:screening-memo`, `draft:ic-memo` and `draft:closing-cover-note`. `frontend/test/rail_pages_investor_d772.test.mjs` holds the keys, the one rail and each band's key, and mounts a band under the shell's provider: it names nothing while closed, names its `railOption` while it runs and while its draft is shown, and names `draft:<surface>` without one. 4 mutations on the hook-up, all caught. Deals has no record view.

**Portfolio (I6–I10).** `/portfolio` (and `/portfolio/health`) answers `Investor · Portfolio`, and its four boards answer their own keys; the legacy `/portfolio/growth` answers Value-add's. Each board draws exactly one rail: Portfolio, Positions, Updates and Value-add their own, Performance the shared frame PR 1 added. The three bands pass `draft:multiple-narrative`, `draft:updates-read` and `draft:support-summary`. Portfolio has no record view. 3 mutations were checked, all caught.

**Fund (I12–I17).** `/funds` answers `Investor · Fund`, which I13 Overview aliases; `/funds/lps`, `/funds/calls` and `/funds/reporting` (also `/lp-reports`) answer their boards, and `/funds/ledger` answers I16 Distributions (D720 §11). Each page draws exactly one rail of its own; the ledger panel sits inside Accounting's. Capital calls' per-line band passes `draft:lp-reminder`. Fund has no record view. 1 mutation was checked, caught.

**Research (I22–I27).** `/research` answers `Investor · Research`; Ask, Diligence, Benchmarking, Markets (and `/research/geographies`) and Library answer their boards, and each record view answers its board's key (a room and its files Diligence's, a benchmark Benchmarking's). The landing (`InvestorResearchWorkspace.jsx`) and the zones' shell (`ResearchWorkspace.jsx`) draw one rail each, the zones none, and each record view (a Diligence room, a Diligence file, a benchmark, a market reading) its own.

- **Shared zones look their key up.** `AskZone`, `LibraryZone` and `DiligenceRoom` render on more than one profile's Research, and each profile's inventory keys the same control its own way (the investor's re-index is `reindex:document`, the founder's `upload:reindex`). S1 chose option A (#2098, 2026-10-19): one helper, `railOptionFor(pageKey, { file, control })` in `lib/railOptions/index.js`, returns `{ key, label }` from the inventory of the member's page (D768's `useRailPage()`). A zone passes its own path (`ZONE_FILE`) and its control: a band's `surface`, or `ask`, `upload` or `reindex` for the call it makes. The control is read from each row's own source marker, so no inventory changes. With no row, a band keeps D770's `draft:<surface>` and any other control names nothing.
- **What that changes.** A founder's keys are the literals the zones passed before. An investor's are `ask:question`, `draft:session-brief`, `draft:thin-evidence`, `upload:document`, `reindex:document` and `draft:index-gaps`. The advisor's and the partner's Ask band and Library controls now name their own rows (`draft:session-brief`, `reindex:document`, `draft:index-gaps`) instead of the founder's. S14 (#2099) uses the same helper for the advisor's and partner's Research boards.
- **The landing's "Find evidence"** names `ask:evidence` while its question is at the model.
- **Record crumbs.** A Diligence room names its project (`diligence-room`), a Diligence file its file name (`diligence-file`), a benchmark and a market reading their metric (`benchmark`, `market-reading`), through `pages/admin/useRecordCrumb.js`.
- **Tests.** `frontend/test/rail_pages_investor_d772.test.mjs` holds the keys, the rails, each option where it lives (a shared zone's lookup reaching `useRailOption` or the band's `railOption`), the helper's answer for all four member profiles, and the four record crumbs. `railOptions_founder_d771.test.mjs` accepts a shared zone's lookup as naming the founder's key. 10 mutations were checked, all caught.

Network (I18–I21) and Axal VC Fund (I11) offer no model-running option, and their page keys and rails were already in place, so they need no PR.
