## D6686 part 2 — stage records for every stage but draft through quoted, and one log per filing step (#6686)

- **Status:** Built for #6686, 2026-11-16, after part 1 (`D6686.md`, PR #6729) merged.
- **The owner's answers (2026-11-16):** "log once for stages, all eleven except draft through quoted". These settle part 1's two open points.
- **Migration:** `202611161045_6686_formation_case_stage_records.sql`.

### What was decided

1. **Which stages keep a record.** Every stage except draft, options_shown, chosen, structure_review, details and quoted. That leaves eight: paid, preparing, ready_to_file, submitted, registry_query, registered, activating and handed_off. The answer says "eleven", but the exclusion as stated leaves eight. The owner confirmed eight (see below).
2. **When a record is written.** Writes happen inside the transition's own batch, after the case update and guarded on the case now being at the new stage and revision. A transition that loses its race therefore writes no stage record.
   - Entering a recorded stage opens its `formation_case_stages` row: case, stage, and the responsible role.
   - Leaving a recorded stage completes that row with `completed_by` (the actor) and `completed_at`.
3. **Leaving the same stage twice.** If a case leaves a recorded stage a second time (for example submitted → registry_query → submitted → registry_query), the first completion stands. Each later pass is in the event timeline (`formation_case_events`).
4. **The responsible role:**
   - hq for paid, preparing, ready_to_file, registered and activating;
   - registry for submitted and registry_query;
   - founder for handed_off.

   A named owner and evidence stay empty until the playbook fills them.
5. **Cases on record.** `ensureCaseForOrder` opens the row for a case it creates. The migration opens the current-stage row for each case already on record. Stages those cases passed before it ran are in their timeline, not in this table.
6. **One log per filing step.**
   - `POST /api/admin/incorporations/:id/packet-ready` and `/filed` keep their own `incorporation_packet_ready` and `incorporation_filed` entries.
   - They no longer hand the admin's email to the service, so the service writes no second `formation_case_transition` entry for them.
   - The service still logs any other admin transition whose caller passes the email.
