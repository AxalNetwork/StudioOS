## D6139 — Accreditation evidence: upload by the investor, review by the fund team (#6139)

The accreditation requirement on `/trust/me` (`legal_obligations.accreditation_v1`, required of every investor) can be met on the platform. Before this, its only action was a link to the Help Center and the page said no route accepted the document.

### Owner answer A (2026-11-27)

- **What may be uploaded.** Only a verification letter, from a CPA, an attorney, a broker-dealer or a registered adviser. Not tax returns, statements or other financial documents (option B), and not "no upload" (option C).
- **How long it is kept.** For the 12-month accreditation period plus the retention period #5510 sets for investor records.

Answer A is the least sensitive data that still meets the requirement.

### The two parts

1. **Part 1 (`D6139-p1.md`): the Worker and the member side.** The upload, the member's read, the review route (the only writer of `accreditation_v1 = 'satisfied'`), the migration, the `/trust/me` states and the LP workspace's onboarding row.
2. **Part 2: the HQ review surface** in the KYC desk (`frontend/src/pages/admin/kyc/`), over part 1's review routes.
