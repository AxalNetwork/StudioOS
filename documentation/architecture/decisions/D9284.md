## D9284 — Eadwyn's panel names three actions: Suggest a community task, Assign project work, Open a support ticket (#9284)

- **Status:** built by S21 on #9284, 2026-11-28.
- **Chosen by:** the #9271 audit, as filed on the issue, with the owner's supplemental audit reconciled there.

### The problem

The Tasks tab of Eadwyn's panel had one button, **Suggest a task**, which opened the support-ticket form with Type = Task. That is a community suggestion awaiting Axal's review, not project work, and nothing on the panel opened a support ticket of another type. One label stood for two different outcomes and a third had no button.

### The decision

The panel (`frontend/src/components/advisor/EadwynPanels.jsx`) names three actions, each with one line under it saying what happens next:

| Tab | Action | Goes to | Its line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tasks | **Suggest a community task** | the chat's ticket form on Type = Task, unchanged (D606; on a founder's Studio, `/studio/tickets/new?type=task`, D701) | `suggestNote`: a Help Center ticket that Axal reviews; an approved one joins Community tasks, and the credits it earns when the rule pays one |
| Tasks | **Assign project work** | `/account/companies/:uid/work?tab=tasks[&project=<id>]`, the company Work path #8767 restores | where the work gets an owner, a status and a next action |
| My tickets | **Open a support ticket** | `/help/tickets?new=1`, the Help Center's form (D599) | nothing from the conversation is copied into it |

- **Assign project work is hidden, not broken.** #8767's path is in open integration PR #8968, not on main: `frontend/src/routes/team.jsx` has no `/account/companies/:uid/work` route, so a link there would 404. `ASSIGN_WORK_PATH_LIVE` is `false`, `assignWorkHref` returns `null`, and with `null` the button is not drawn. A test fails if the switch is on while the route is missing. To turn it on once #8968 merges: flip `ASSIGN_WORK_PATH_LIVE` and pass `workScope={{ companyUid, projectId }}` to `TasksPanel` from the chat.
- **The three actions stay apart.** A community suggestion keeps the ticket form and its reward semantics. Existing community task records are never read as project work. The support ticket opens the Help Center form on no type, so a private ticket is never turned into a team record and no transcript is copied into it.
- The `/task` and `/ticket` shortcuts are #9276's. When they land, they open these same three.

### Not changed

- The chat's ticket form and its heading (`PersonalAdvisor.jsx`), and the transcript line after a ticket is filed.
- The tab bar (#9289) and the Teams tab (#9282).
- No Worker route, no API method, no migration.
