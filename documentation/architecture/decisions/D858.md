## D858 — Conversation analytics for Record a Session: each chat's and each venture's figures, read from the stores already kept

- **Status:** S13, 2026-10-22 (#2503; part of #2500). No migration. Read only: nothing here calls a model, a provider or a writer.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at the branch point of #2503's PR; the D684, D754 and D756 channel code in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/channels/`, D657/D685 sessions, D658 placements, D757's engine (`sessionAutoStructure.ts`).

### 1. What was decided

1. **One service, two reads.** `services/conversationAnalytics.ts` computes every figure; `routes/validate_conversations.ts` checks the caller and the query.
   - `GET /api/validate/sessions/:id/analytics` — one session, any kind (a chat, a recording, a typed paste).
   - `GET /api/validate/conversations/analytics?project_id&from&to` — a venture's chats (`channel = 'text'` with a WhatsApp or Telegram `submitted_via`), `from` and `to` as `YYYY-MM-DD`, both inclusive of their day, at most `VENTURE_SESSIONS_MAX` (500) sessions, newest first.
2. **Who reads.** Signed in, and `canReadBoard` on the session's venture: the venture's founder, or HQ staff. Anyone else gets 404, the answer the session routes give since D685, so another member learns nothing. A malformed id or date, or `from` after `to`, is 400. `canReadBoard` has no co-founder concept today; when it gains one, these reads follow it unchanged.
3. **Every figure is `{ value, sources, reason }`.** `sources` is the #2431 label set: `Recorded` (a recording, a voice note) and `Typed` (chat text, a paste). A figure no store holds is `value: null` with a `reason` that says why, never 0. A store this build lacks (`no such table|column`) reads null with a reason naming it, and the other figures still read.
4. **The figures.**
   - **Messages by side and source.** A segment labelled `Me` is the founder's; any other speaker is the customer's. A voice note's segment is moved from Typed to Recorded by its speaker.
   - **Voice notes** (a chat's `interview_chunk_transcripts`) and **voice minutes** (a recording's `active_ms`).
   - **Languages**: what speech-to-text detected on each voice note, or the recording's own `language`. **Text languages** are null until W2 (#2502) records them.
   - **Median reply time**: null (§2).
   - **Suggestion reach**: the share of a session's lines that some proposal cites, a cite overlapping a line's span (`toSegments`, exclusive end). A discarded proposal still reached its line; a proposal without both `cite_from` and `cite_to` reaches none.
   - **Pain themes**: live (`pending` or `accepted`) `pain_severity` proposals by the venture's `pain_groups`, matched by a named `pain_group_id` of this venture, else by `pain_group_aliases` on `normPhrase`; the rest are one unthemed row. Suggested and accepted counts.
   - **Objections** by `about`; **willingness to pay** (`demand_signal` with `would_pay`, `loi` or `pre_sale`, and `budget`); **competitor mentions** by name. Live proposals only.
   - **Quotes per theme.** In the session read, each theme's quotes (`citation_json.quote`, at most `QUOTES_PER_THEME`), only when the interview allows quoting (`quote_consent = 1`, D685 §5.1.4) and the chat's consent is not `declined`; otherwise `QUOTE_WITHHELD`. In the venture read, counts only: `quotable` and `withheld` per theme.
5. **Aggregates only in the venture read.** It carries counts, shares and theme titles, never a line, a quote or a customer's name.

### 2. What the stores do not hold (filed on #2500)

- **Reply time.** A chat message's send time and direction are not kept once the thread moves into a session (`interview_transcript_segments` has no time for a chat line), so the median reply time is null with `REASONS.replyTime`.
- **Voice-note length.** WhatsApp and Telegram notes are stored without their duration, so a chat's voice minutes are null.
- **Text language.** Null until W2 (#2502).
- **Token cost per conversation.** #2465's usage rows are not on `main`; nothing here reads them.

### 3. Coverage audit

`conversation_analytics_d858.test.ts` drives an assigned WhatsApp text chat and a consented WhatsApp voice note through the real webhook, assign, consent and voice job, then runs the queued `session_structure` job (`chat_text_added`, `voice_note_added`): each reaches `structureSession` and leaves a pending `pain_severity` proposal for the founder to accept, the voice note's citing the note's own line.

### 4. Verified

`cloudflare-worker/test/conversation_analytics_d858.test.ts` (8 tests): hand-counted figures for one session and for a venture over a range; null with a reason, and a dropped store named; 404 for another founder's session and venture, 200 for HQ, 400 and 401; quote consent and the per-theme cap; the coverage path for text and voice. Every new assertion was mutation-checked: 25 mutations of the service and the route, each killed.
