### 1. The read (Worker)

`routes/cycle_build.ts` `company()` keeps every fact it had (`ip`, `incorporation`, `filings`, `next_filing_due`, `next_filing`, which now also names its row `id`) and, on the module page only (`opts.pageFacts`), adds three blocks from `services/buildCompanyCreation.ts`. The overview reads none of them, so #4603's statement budget and the overview card are unchanged. Each block catches its own failure and says `unreadable`; the filings and the other blocks still read (BD29).

| Fact | Holds | From |
|---|---|---|
| `entity` | `has_company`; `name`, `jurisdiction`, `registered_agent`, `formed_on`, each `{ value, source, reason }`; `order` (status, its label, track, the three dated steps); `ein` (Issued, Not issued yet or not recorded; the number is never stored) | the order already read, `formation_case_orders` → `formation_cases.track`, `company_profiles`, `entities`, the `ein` formation step |
| `elections` | one row per founder on the cap table: grant date, deadline, days left, mailed on, status, proof, the 83(b) Build filing's id, whether a tracker exists, and why a value is missing | `cap_table_holders` founders, their newest `section_83b_trackers` row, their `kind = '83b'` formation step |
| `papers` | the company's four named papers, Stored, Missing or Unreadable, with the day and uploader | `routes/company_documents.ts` `readSlots`, for a member of the company |

**Formed on (D6694).** It is the order's `filed_on` once the order is `filed`. On a `v2` case that is the registry's registration date and the source line says so; on a `legacy_v1` order, or an order no case was opened for, it is the submission day an admin typed (D601) and the source says that. Without a filed order it falls back to `company_profiles.incorporation_date`, then `entities.incorporation_date`, each named. `incorporations.status` keeps its five values; while a filing is with the registry the status reads `packet_ready`, so the order line says Packet ready and draws no Filed with state date.

**Who reads the 83(b) rows (Q1, answered A on 2026-11-16).** Every reader of the board reads every founder's row: the venture's founders by the owner's answer, and an admin or partner, who already read every tracker through `GET /api/legal/83b/trackers`. No wider than before for anyone. A founder with neither a tracker nor a filing is Not recorded with its reason, and gets no deadline. A pending election whose deadline passed with nothing mailed is Missed.

**Papers.** A venture with no company is `not_linked`. A board reader who is not a member of the company (a partner) is `refused` and gets no rows and no counts. The 83(b) proofs are the 83(b) rows' own proof state, so they are not read twice. No new store.

**Where a filing is changed.** The filing sheet of an Axal obligation (`services/records/build.ts`) gains a Linked row, "Set by Axal · Changed only by Axal: ask Axal to change it", to `/help/tickets`. A Lab step's sheet already links the Legal engine.
