#### 2. One catalogue of every Workers AI model (D760)

- **The store** is `cloudflare-worker/src/services/ai/catalogue.ts`.
  - It holds one row per model in the verified catalogue: 94 rows, read on 2026-10-17 from Cloudflare's model pages and its pricing page (updated 2026-10-15).
  - The rows are written by hand. A test checks each id, status and price against the committed `catalogue.json`.
  - Each row has: id, name, kind, capabilities, context window, status (GA, partner, beta, deprecated or not found), paid-only flag, partner flag, raw unit prices, canvas group, NEW flag and docs URL.
- **Which models are offered.** A model is offered when its status is GA or partner and it has a price: 59 models.
  - The rest stay as rows but are never offered: 18 deprecated, 12 beta models with no price, 3 not found, and 2 GA models with no price (`llama-3.1-8b-instruct-fast`, `dreamshaper-8-lcm`).
  - `run()` refuses them as `model_not_offered` or `model_unpriced`. The $0 path in `estimateCostUsd` becomes the `model_unpriced` refusal.
- **Units:**
  - tokens: input, cached input and output, per 1M;
  - embeddings: input per 1M;
  - audio minutes, batch and websocket;
  - per 1k characters;
  - per 512² tile, input and output;
  - per tile per step;
  - per megapixel: the first, each further one, and each input-image megapixel;
  - per image, for ResNet-50 ($2.51 per million images).

  The Worker measures every unit other than tokens from the request itself, the way `audioMinutesFromBytes` does (`aiRouter.ts:308`). It never takes the figure from the browser.
- **Precision.**
  - Raw unit prices are stored as decimal strings, exactly as Cloudflare prints them. FLUX.1 Schnell's tile is 0.0000528, which is below a micro-dollar.
  - A run's cost and its charge are computed in exact decimal arithmetic over its measured units. Each is rounded up to a whole micro-USD, once per run.

#### 3. The price a member pays: Cloudflare × 1.25, once the owner turns it on (D760, D762)

- **One constant.** `AI_MARKUP = 1.25` lives in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/ai/pricing.ts`, the only place the number is written. Its comment names its source: the owner's canvas `usd()` (`rail_logic.js:74`) and this decision.
- **The frontend never multiplies.**
  - The Worker serves each billed unit price as `parseFloat(raw) × multiplier`.
  - The rail formats it with the canvas's own `Number(x.toPrecision(4))`, so the canvas's strings come out exactly, float artifacts included (0.293 shows as $0.3662).
- **The billing switch.** The production variable `AI_BILLING` decides what members pay.
  - Unset or `cost`: members pay Cloudflare's cost, as today (D629).
  - `charged`: members pay cost × `AI_MARKUP`.
  - D760 serves `billing: "cost"` always. D762 makes the field read `AI_BILLING`, in the same PR that settles at it. So no screen ever draws a price the Worker is not charging.
  - The owner sets `charged` after confirming the pricing line (owner step 4). Until then every price the rail, the Usage page and the receipts draw is the cost price, and no copy mentions the 25%.
  - The quote, the hold, the settlement, the receipts, `/api/ai/me/spend`, `/rates`, `/pricing`, `/catalogue` and the rail all use the billed figure. The price a member is quoted is the price drawn.
- **Who pays the margin.** The `admin` licence always pays cost. It covers Admin and Super Admin runs (`aiLicenceKey`, `aiBudget.ts:29-33`), and staff runs are not revenue.
- **Cost and charge are both recorded.**
  - `ai_usage_logs.est_cost_usd` stays Cloudflare's cost. Migration 481 adds `charged_microusd` and `reservation_uid` beside it.
  - The organisation cap and the kill switch stay on cost (`killSwitchOn`, `aiRouter.ts:632`; the organisation check at `:1077`), because cost is what Cloudflare bills Axal.
- **The margin HQ sees.** HQ Revenue's token line (`admin_revenue.ts:118-139`) counts margin on top-up draws only.
  - The margin is the members' token spend (`ai_budget_reservations.actual_topup_microusd`) less that spend's share of Cloudflare's cost.
  - Allowance draws are shown apart, at member prices, as "Included in plans", because the plan's price already covers them.
  - Admin-licence runs are left out.
  - Rows from before migration 481 read "Not recorded".
- **Top-ups do not change.**
  - One dollar paid still buys one dollar of balance (D629.md:15). With charging on, a run costs more, and a dollar of balance stays a dollar.
  - The doors' "each dollar you add is a dollar of AI use" stays true.
  - Once the owner sets `charged`, `/pricing` and the doors add "Model prices are Cloudflare's published rates plus 25%."
- **What this reverses**, from the day charging turns on:
  - D629.md:15's "not a new price surcharge";
  - D698 decision 10: prices return to the rail.

#### 6. Picks, the switch, and when a run may start (D764, D765, D769)

- **Storage.** Picks and the switch reuse migration 465's `ai_rail_preferences`, with no new migration:
  - `section` is the page key, lower-cased;
  - `task` is the option key, or `page` for the page's own pick;
  - the `''` row holds the page's mode.

  D698's rows keyed by section are left in place and no longer read. The cap rises from 200 to 1,000 rows per member (`RAIL_PREFERENCE_CAP`, `routes/ai.ts:177`).
- **Scope.** A page pick applies to every option on the page that has no pick of its own. A stale pick is refused as `model_not_offered`, and the rail clears it.
- **The switch.** "Turn off AI assistance on this page" is drawn on every page. With AI off, the rail says "Eadwyn won't suggest or draft on this page. Your work saves as usual."
- **AI is on by default** (`rail_logic.js:59`), which reverses D698.md:90 and D46. That is safe because no member run starts without the member's press:
  - No run starts on page load, on mount, on a GET, or in the background. The syndicate read becomes a press (§9).
  - A recording's Stop no longer summarises or places by itself.

#### 7. One run path (D765)

- **The route.** `POST /api/ai/rail/run`, signed in, answers as server-sent events.
  - The body is `{page, option, record?: {type, id}, model?, input?}`.
  - The events:
    - `meta`: the model, the chain and the quote;
    - `delta`: checked text only (§8);
    - `guard`: pending, safe or blocked, for each slice;
    - `done`: the proposal or draft ids, the usage, the charge and the caller's fresh `/ai-budget/me` snapshot.
- **The steps.** Resolve the row, check the mode, find the handler, gather, reserve, guard the input, run the model, guard the output slice by slice, settle, store, and answer `done`.
  - The reservation comes before the guard, so a budget refusal is answered before `guard_unavailable` can be.
- **Handlers.** Each matrix option names a handler kind in `services/ai/railOptions.ts`.
  - Eight generic handlers serve most option rows with no code of their own: explain, ask, draft, summarise, rank, translate, transcribe and extract.
  - Bespoke handlers are needed only for:
    - fills: the five `FILL_KINDS`, registered unchanged;
    - forms and uploads (D777);
    - the 47 `DRAFT_SURFACES` (`research.ts:1321`), registered unchanged.
- **Scope lives in the gather.** A generic handler gathers only two things:
  - the text the page sends, which is what the member sees. It is treated as the member's own input and checked as input;
  - a record, through the one-record reads in `services/records/`, which authorise the caller.

  The rules for every handler:
  - A bespoke handler reads only the caller's own rows.
  - `/api/research/drafts` authorises on `requireAuth` alone (`research.ts:3651-3652`), so its handler is where scoping happens.
  - Every gather refuses another member's record.
  - An Admin's gather of a record that is not their own writes `logAdminAction`.
  - No gather reads a member's Mercury personal data (#1778's rule).
- **Branch audit.** A run on a branch page keeps the audit row that `/workspace/explain` writes today (`routes/ai.ts:380-453`; D150, D153).
- **Aliases.** `POST /api/founder/validate/propose/:projectId` (`founder_validate.ts:611`) and `POST /api/research/drafts` (`research.ts:3651`) call the same service.
  - Their responses do not change.
  - The client now sends the page and the option.
- **Store.**
  - Validate's proposals stay in `validate_proposals`, and zone drafts stay in `research_zone_drafts`.
  - Every other draft, proposal or note goes in migration 482's `ai_rail_proposals`. Its columns: uid, user, page, option, record_ref, kind (draft, proposal or note), payload, model, usage-log id, status (proposed, accepted, edited, discarded or withdrawn) and timestamps.
- **Streaming settlement (D762).** A streamed run settles on the provider's usage where the final chunk carries it, otherwise on the counted output. This replaces the 0 completion tokens recorded today (`aiRouter.ts:910`).
- **What each run records.** `ai_usage_logs` gains `rail_page`, `rail_option` and `record_ref` (migration 481), beside D552's `surface`.
- **Refusal copy.** Every budget refusal uses `budgetRefusalMessage` (`aiRouter.ts:961`), replacing "The model could not be reached" (`routes/ai.ts:482`; `routes/eadwyn.ts:537`).
- **Timeouts (D761)** are set per class of model: chat 8 seconds; reasoning and long context 30 seconds; images 60 seconds. This replaces `PRIMARY_TIMEOUT_MS` (`aiRouter.ts:541`).

#### 8. Llama Guard checks every run (D763)

- **Coverage.** `run()` calls `services/ai/guard.ts` on every run except two: the guard's own run, and system embeddings for the search index and its queries (userId 0).
  - **Input:** the text the member typed or supplied for the run, in full: typed text, the page's text, an upload's text. It is checked in slices of at most 2,000 characters. Rows the handler gathers from the member's own store are not checked again.
  - **Output:** every text output, in full, in slices of at most 2,000 characters, before it is returned, streamed or stored.
  - **Images:** the text prompt, and any text output. A text guard cannot read an image, and D763.md says so.
  - **The member's own material.** A transcript, or text extracted from an upload, belongs to the member. An unsafe verdict marks it and is logged, and the text is kept. Any run that uses it is checked again on its input.
- **Category policy.** One table in `guard.ts`, tested:
  - S1–S5 and S8–S14 block.
  - S6 (specialised advice) and S7 (privacy) are logged and do not block, because legitimate legal, cap-table and diligence drafts trip them.
  - HQ sees the counts per page (`loadGuardrailCounters`, `aiRouter.ts:1377`).
- **Fails closed.**

  | What happens | Refusal | What is charged |
  |---|---|---|
  | The guard can't be reached | `guard_unavailable`: nothing runs, and the hold is released | Any guard call that did complete |
  | The input is unsafe | `guard_blocked` | The guard |
  | The output is unsafe | `guard_blocked_output`: the output is withheld | The guard and the model, which both ran |

  This replaces the fail-open returns at `guardrails.ts:138` and `:146`.
- **One hold.**
  - The guard runs inside the run's reservation (`runGuard`). It never takes a hold of its own, as `classifyInput` does today (`guardrails.ts:132`).
  - The four routes that call the guard themselves drop that call, so each run is checked once.
- **The cost is counted.**
  - The quote covers the guard on every input slice and on every output slice the run's maximum output allows, at the billed price.
  - Each guard row is logged with `guard_for` pointing at the run's own row, and the same page and option.
  - It is settled under the same hold.
- **Streaming.** The Worker releases text one checked slice at a time: at most 2,000 characters, cut at a paragraph break where it can. Unchecked text never reaches the browser, so "withheld" is true.
  - While a slice waits, the rail shows "Eadwyn is checking this draft."
  - Accept is refused until the last slice passes.
- **The rail's sentence.** "Llama Guard 3 8B checks every run ($0.605 in · $0.0375 out per 1M tokens)." takes its prices from the catalogue, at the billed rate.
  - It is drawn only when `GET /api/ai/catalogue` reports `guard.every_run: true`, a constant D763 sets. So the copy cannot ship before the code.
  - Until then the rail draws only "Eadwyn drafts; you send." (`SAFETY_LINE`, `frontend/src/ui/WorkerRail.jsx:302`).

#### 9. "Eadwyn drafts; you send": what auto-fill may do (D766, D767, D770, D777–D780)
