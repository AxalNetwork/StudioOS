## D6242 — Eadwyn in the Fund zones: an LP summary and a first draft of the quarterly letter (#6242)

**Status:** Built. Part of #5130's Fund work; no new shared rail UI (S1, 2026-11-21: "use the existing approved
LP/Reporting draft controls and registries; any new shared rail UI must wait for #5130").

Eadwyn helped in one place in the Fund section (D553's reminder for one call line, the `funds/calls` surface). This
adds the two proposals a GP uses most, each as a draft the GP accepts or discards, through the same machinery.

### 1. Two more zone-draft surfaces, mirroring `funds/calls`

Both are entries in `DRAFT_SURFACES` (`cloudflare-worker/src/routes/research.ts`) beside `funds/calls`, so they run,
store, accept and discard exactly as the reminder does, through `POST /api/research/drafts` and its `PATCH`/`DELETE`.
No new route and no new client method. The builders live in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/fundZoneDrafts.ts`.

| Surface | Control | Scope | Reads |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `funds/lps` | "Summarise this LP", on the LP record drawer (D6236) | `<fund id>:<LP id>` | that LP's register row, ledger lines and receipts (`readFundLedger(fund, lp)`), comms log (`listCommunications(fund, lp)`) |
| `funds/reporting` | "Draft the letter", on a draft period's editor (`FundReportPeriodPage`) | `<fund id>:<period id>` | the period's recorded capital calls and paid distributions (D6252's `periodMovements` and `letterMaterial`) and its saved Key developments |

- **The gate is the drawer's and the editor's own.** `/research/drafts` authorises on sign-in alone and leaves the
  scoping to each surface, so each gather runs `requireFundGp`: the institutional tier, the GP of record, the active
  company, or an admin. Anything it refuses, a scope that is not two ids, an LP or period of another fund, or an
  issued period gives nothing to draft and is answered `409 nothing_to_draft` before any model runs — the same
  answer as a record with nothing on it, so the route is no oracle.
- **No `accept` hook on either.** Accept stamps the GP's own `research_zone_drafts` row and writes no fund record.
  The run writes only that row. Nothing is sent.

### 2. Every figure in the LP summary is one the drawer shows

- The material is formatted by the drawer's own functions. `lpCommitmentDollars`, `lpPaidDollars`, `registerMoney`
  and `lpFigures` moved from `InvestorFundLPs.jsx` into `fundLpRecordModel.js` (beside `callLines`, `calledToDate`
  and `moneyText`), and the Worker imports that file, as it imports `lib/money.js`. One function prints each figure
  on both sides. `fundLpRecordModel.d.ts` and `fundLpCommsModel.d.ts` give the Worker's typecheck their types.
- It carries only the LP's commitment, paid to date, committed on, called to date, each line (owed, received,
  outstanding, state, its receipts' dates and references), receipts not on a listed line, and each comms log entry.
  The share of fund is left out: it is a figure against the whole register, which is other LPs' commitments. The
  LP's email is left out: it adds nothing a summary uses.
- `finish` holds the draft to its material (`holdToMaterial`): a draft that quotes any number the material does not
  carry is not stored and is answered `503 draft_unfaithful`. So a kept summary can only repeat figures on the page.
  The letter's draft is held the same way to its movements and developments.
- `frontend/test/fund_zone_drafts_6242.test.mjs` runs the Worker's builder over the fixture the drawer renders and
  asserts every amount, date and number in the material is in the drawer's markup.

### 3. With nothing to read, the control says so and does not run

- **LP.** The band is drawn inside the drawer (`LpRecordView`'s `proposal`, called with the drawer's ledger and
  comms reads), and `lpSummaryBlocked` disables the run, with its sentence beside the button, while either read is
  out, when either failed ("a summary would quote figures this page does not show"), or when the LP has no
  commitment above zero, no call line, no receipt and no logged message. A commitment of 0 is the column's default,
  not an agreed figure.
- **Letter.** A period not yet saved, or with unsaved dates or Key developments, says "Save the draft first": the
  draft reads the saved period. A period with no movement between its dates and no saved development is refused by
  the Worker and the band shows its own sentence. An issued period, or a reader who cannot manage it, has no band.

### 4. The letter fills the Letter section; nothing saves until Save draft

Accept calls the band's `onAccepted`, which adds the draft's paragraphs (one per blank-line block) after the
Letter section's own, in the unsaved editor (`fillLetter`). It never passes the editor's 50 paragraphs: the overflow
joins the last paragraph, and a full letter holds Accept with its sentence. Save draft and Issue stay the page's own
buttons; the leave guard covers the unsaved change. The only stored thing before Save draft is the GP's private
draft row.

**Beside D6252's opening, not instead of it.** D6252 (#6252, merged after this issue was filed) drafts only the
letter's *opening paragraph*, from the capital movements, and puts it first. This drafts the whole Letter section
from the movements *and* the developments and adds it after what is there. Both stay; whether the opening band is
retired now that the whole letter is drafted is the owner's call (see the PR).

### 5. Refused and failed runs use the platform's words (D5129)

ZoneDraft takes an opt-in `stateWords`. With it, a refused run reads `Refused · <sentence>` (Eadwyn switched off,
a 402/403/404, the guard withholding the draft) and a failed one `Unavailable · <sentence>` (the model unreachable,
a guard that could not check, a draft held back as unfaithful), the words from `ui/dataState.js`'s `STATE_WORDS`.
Nothing to read is neither: it shows the band's own sentence. Every other ZoneDraft mount is unchanged.

### 6. Rail options

`lib/railOptions/investor.js` gains `draft:lp-summary` (Investor · Fund · LPs, kind `numbers`, job IN5) and
`draft:quarterly-letter` (Investor · Fund, kind `write`, job RA10). A period's page, `/funds/:id/reporting/:periodId`,
answers the Fund board's key in `lib/railPages.js`, so the letter's row is on that page;
`pages/FundReportPeriodPage.jsx` joins `INVESTOR_BOARD_FILES`. `railMatrix.json` is regenerated.
