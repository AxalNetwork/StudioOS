## D1588 — Eadwyn and MCP deck tools, proposals only (#3835)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-03. Deck v2 F9, part of #3716.
- **Spec:** the issue's final canvas revision (S1, 2026-11-01) and S21's release note (2026-11-03). Worker only; no board draws it.
- **Builds on:**
  - D666, D846 and D854: the MCP registry, and Eadwyn using that same registry in the member's own access (`services/eadwynTools.ts`);
  - D1604 (#3885): DeckAsk's "Waiting for you", which lists `ai_rail_proposals` rows on `DECK_ASK_OPTIONS` with record `deck:<id>`, and the whole-deck `pitch_deck_proposals` row;
  - D767: a deck proposal is never a version until Keep;

  - D1341: Email's drafts (`mail_drafts`); D913 and D1582: the objections and their answers.
- **Migration:** none.

### What was decided

- **Six tools, one registry.** They are MCP tools (`mcp/tools/decks.ts`), so an assistant connected over MCP, Eadwyn, and #3742's agents all reach deck work the same way. The logic is in `services/decks/eadwynDeckTools.ts`.
  - `decks.read_outline` (`raise:read`): the current version (or a named one of the venture's), each card with its number, uid and title, and each area as a block range on the 12 × 6 grid, with its kind and, for text, its words (cut at 400 characters).
  - `decks.propose_area` (`raise:write`): new words for one text area.
  - `decks.propose_variant` (`raise:write`): a variant of the whole deck, as new words in up to 20 text areas and or a new card order.
  - `raise.prospect_brief` (`raise:read`): D1585's brief, unchanged.
  - `raise.qa_prep` (`raise:read`): the objections the venture logged, the ones this investor raised first, then by how many investors raised them, each with the founder's recorded answer or null, and how many are unanswered. At most 12.
  - `raise.draft_follow_up` (`raise:write`): a follow-up the caller wrote, kept as their draft in Email.
- **No chat or thread tool** (plan §0 rows 1–2, §4.4–4.5).
- **Every write tool proposes; none saves.** Each answers `saved: false` and the proposal's id, and says where it waits:
  - `decks.propose_area` leaves one `ai_rail_proposals` row on `deck:<action>` (`rewrite` by default; `shorten`, `expand` or `tone`), record `deck:<id>`, page `Founder · Raise · Pitch deck`, with the payload a card's Rewrite leaves (`deck_id`, the card's place and uid, the block, `before`, `after`). DeckAsk's `cardEdit` reads it, so it appears in "Waiting for you" with Accept and Discard and needs no new UI.
  - `decks.propose_variant` leaves one `pitch_deck_proposals` row over the version it read. **One already waiting is never replaced** (409 `proposal_waiting`): the founder's own Fill or template proposal is theirs to decide first. This differs from Fill from project, which replaces its own earlier proposal.
  - `raise.draft_follow_up` leaves one `mail_drafts` row (`person_ref = prospect:<id>`, intent `follow_up`, no model, no charge). Nothing is sent.
- **Areas are grid block ranges** (`{ c1, c2, r1, r2 }`, checked by `gridOf`). The card's blocks are read through `sanitizeBlocks`, and the area must hold exactly one text block (`area_empty`, `area_ambiguous`). The new words must still be kept by `sanitizeBlocks`. A card is named by its uid or its number.
- **Typed figures are refused** (plan §4.3), with `figure_in_text`, by `numberIn`: the rule the deck editor, the new-deck prompt and `promptWarning` use. It covers area words, a variant's words and title, and the follow-up's subject and body. `numberIn` moved, unchanged, from `newDeck.ts` to `services/decks/numbers.ts`; `newDeck.ts` re-exports it. Reason: D752's guard forbids any MCP tool from reaching member bank data, and `newDeck.ts` reaches it through the deck sources.
- **Who:** only the venture's own founders and co-founder editors (`canWritePitchDeck`), for reads too. An admin or an advisor is refused (`not_owned`), as D1191 keeps Capital relationships the founder's. The grant must cover the company, and a `raise:write` tool needs the company writable (`WRITE_SCOPES` in `mcp/server.ts`).
- **New scope `raise:write`:** "Propose deck changes and keep follow-up drafts for you to accept. Nothing is saved to your deck or sent without your press." It is offered for API keys and consent, and counted as a write.
- **Eadwyn's routes** in `routes/eadwyn.ts`:
  - `GET /api/eadwyn/deck-tools?project=` lists the six for a venture under the member's active company;
  - `POST /api/eadwyn/deck-tools/:name` `{ project, args }` runs one through `runEadwynToolCalls`.
  - A support session and an Explorer are refused, as D1442 refuses them on Ask.
  - Only these six are reachable there.
- **A tool marked `proposes`** (a new `McpTool` field) runs through `callTool` as itself in Eadwyn, instead of being wrapped as a generic `kind: 'proposal'` row on the caller's option. That is how its proposal lands in the store the founder decides from.
- **Price:** each tool is listed at 0 in the metering policy, and none runs a model.
- **The AI switch:** no tool runs a model, so none checks the studio's AI switch. A proposal left while AI is paused still waits, and DeckAsk says what is waiting "can still be accepted or discarded".
