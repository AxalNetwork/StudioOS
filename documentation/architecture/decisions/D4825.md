### What a proof card says (EX-08)

- **One state, from the Worker.** `proofCardState` reads `row.attested` and each consent's `state` (`consentState` in the Worker) in a fixed order: Confirmed, Awaiting confirmation, Needs reconfirmation, Declined or Withdrawn, Self-stated. Nothing on the page computes a confirmation.
- **Who, when, public.** Each card names who was asked ("Asked K. Osei · CEO, Lumen") or who confirmed ("Confirmed by Jane Smith · Partner, Acme"), dates it from `requested_at`, `consent_given_at` or `withdrawn_at` (a missing date is said, never invented), and says whether it is public on the profile.
- **Never "Verified".** Axal verifies no one's identity, so the strongest word is "Confirmed by <name>".
- **Publishing is the advisor's.** Publish proof appears only on a confirmed card and calls `PATCH /me/proof/:id/publication` (#5315 part 2); a published card offers Make private. Nothing publishes on confirmation, and the Worker's refusal reads as a fixed sentence.
- **Cancel request** sits beside each pending link and calls `POST /me/proof/:id/consents/:consentId/cancel` (#5315 part 1).
- **Delete…** still asks first and names what goes with the claim (D4681).
- **Tiles count the whole list (D4689):** Confirmed, Awaiting an answer and Not confirmed come from the Worker's `counts`. The Worker does not count claims needing reconfirmation, so that tile has a figure only while every claim is on screen and reads Not recorded otherwise.

### The request flow (EX-09)

Claim → Attester → Request → Confirmation, with the claim shown exactly as the attester will read it, pinned when sent. The fields are the canvas's: their name, their role and company as it will appear, their email (required; the Worker refuses the advisor's own address). Nothing is emailed: "Request created" shows the link with Copy invitation link and Cancel request.

### The attester's page (EX-10)

A · Arrive: who asked, the exact statement, how they will appear, what confirming means (true as written, not an identity check), how it is used (private until the advisor publishes it), how to withdraw, and Email me a code to the masked address, or I already have a code. B · Code: the six digits, then Confirm this statement or I can’t confirm this. C · Confirmed: the date, how the advisor sees it, the history, and Withdraw my confirmation…, which needs a new code like any answer.

### Where the build departs from the canvas

- **Declined offers "Ask someone else", not "Edit claim".** The Worker has no route to edit a proof claim.
- **Confirmed offers Publish proof, not "View history".** The advisor's list carries no answer history (only the attester's read does), and publishing is the action #5315 added.
- **Every card keeps an ask button** ("Ask another person" on Awaiting and Confirmed), because a claim takes several attesters and D4711's focus return lands on it. The canvas draws one action per card.
- **Tries left are not counted** on the code step. The Worker does not return attempts; the page says "5 tries each".
- **"You’ll appear as" shows the name only.** The attester's read does not return the role the advisor typed.
- **The checkbox is gone.** The canvas draws none; "Confirm this statement" sends the same consent text, which is printed above the buttons. The optional public sentence stays.
- **"Not you? … the request expires unanswered"** is not said: requests do not expire. The page says nothing is recorded unless someone answers with the code.
- **Resend by email** stays unbuilt (◇ on the canvas).
- **The card grid is D4828's** (EX-04a, #7113): one card a row on a phone and on a desktop, two at 768px. The canvas's EX-08 draws cards side by side on a wide screen; D4828 landed first and its test pins the row.
