## D4819 part 11 — Access: administrators and licensed seats (L11) (#4819)

Built for #5922, after parts 1 to 3 in `D4819.md` and part 12. The part number is the board number (L11), as part 12's is, so it cannot collide with a sibling slice. It is frontend only, with no Worker change and no migration.

### Backend check

Each value was checked against the read it comes from, on main as it stands:

- **Administrators:** `GET /api/admin/licences/:uid/admins` (`routes/admin_licences_admins.ts`, migration 190, D134). Each row has `name`, `email`, `admin_role` (principal or delegate), `users.role` and `users.is_active`.
- **Add administrator:** `POST /:uid/admins`, behind the write bar, with a reason of at least 10 characters. It grants the admin role and binds the account in one batch, writes the licence history and the audit log, and tells the account holder. A licence whose status is not in `APPOINTABLE` (`services/licenceTransitions.ts`: draft, pending activation, active, suspended) is refused with 409.
- **Remove:** `POST /api/admin/users/:userId/demote-admin`, with a reason of at least 10 characters, then **Detach** (`DELETE /:uid/admins/:userId`). Detach takes no reason and refuses while the account still holds the role.
- **Licensed seats:** `seats` on `GET /api/admin/licences/:uid` (`licence_seats`, one row per profile). `seats_used` is always null on HQ (`seatsUsed()` in `services/licenceLedgerCore.ts`): HQ does not attribute accounts to a licence.
- **Seat changes:** `PUT /:uid/seats`. Its writes carry `status <> 'terminated'`, so on a terminated licence they change nothing.

### What was built

- **The Access section** opens on Administrators. It shows board L11's two cards side by side: Administrators (7 parts) and Licensed seats (5 parts). The side panel steps aside here, as it does on part 12's Requests and compliance. On a phone the cards stack, and each administrator row stacks with labelled cells.
- **Administrators** (`AdminsEditor.jsx`):
  - Each row gives the name and email, the role (Principal or Delegate) and the account state. The account state is Active, Deactivated (the account holds the admin role but is switched off), No longer an admin (detach to finish), or Not recorded where the read carries no role or no active flag.
  - Add administrator sits in the card's header. It opens the email and role fields, and Continue… opens the labelled reason dialog. The dialog says what will happen before it asks why.
  - Remove… (the demote) also opens the reason dialog with its consequence first: the role goes now, and the binding stays until Detach.
  - Detach is offered only on a row that is no longer an admin, and the row says what Detach records.
  - An action the row cannot take is not drawn, where before it was a disabled button.
- **Licensed seats** (`LicensedSeats` in `CommercialTerms.jsx`) has columns Profile, Licensed and In use.
  - Licensed is the ledger's count. A recorded 0 shows as 0, and a profile with no seat row shows as Not recorded.
  - In use is Not recorded on every row. The foot gives the reason in operator words, with no internal identifier.
  - Change seats opens the Seats tab.
- **Invalid actions are not offered.**
  - On a terminated licence (or any status the Worker does not appoint on), Add administrator is not drawn, and the card says why.
  - On a terminated licence, Change seats and the Seats tab's editor are not drawn either. The counts stand as read.
- **Three absences, three treatments.**
  - **None:** the read succeeded and nobody is named ("None. No administrator is named on this licence.").
  - **Not recorded:** grey italic, on seat cells and an account state.
  - **Unreadable:** the administrators read failed. `ui/Honesty`'s `Unreadable`, in red, with Retry, saying it is not the same as having none.
- **The reason dialog** (`LicenceReasonDialog.jsx`) accepts an `initial` reason. A delegate request's "Grant admin" still arrives with the reason it wrote, which the appointment form used to hold in its own textarea.

### Departures from the canvas

| Board | Built in | Departs from the canvas | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L11 | `AdminsEditor.jsx`, `CommercialTerms.jsx` (`LicensedSeats`), `LicenceDetail.jsx`, `licenceAccessModel.js` | No "Invite pending" state | Appointment needs an account that already exists, and there is no invite store. The states drawn are the ones `users.role` and `users.is_active` give. |
| L11 | same | Role reads Principal or Delegate | Those are the roles `licence_admins.admin_role` records. The canvas's "Studio lead" and similar roles are sample content. |
| L11 | same | Add administrator is still offered on a suspended licence, and its consequence says the licence is suspended | The canvas's note hides it there, but the Worker appoints on a suspended licence (`APPOINTABLE`, #4678). |
| L11 | same | The canvas's standing note is not drawn. The sentence appears only where an action is withheld. | A note about an action the licence can take would be noise. |
| L11 | same | Remove is two steps, Remove… then Detach | The Worker refuses to detach while the role is held (D134). |
| L11 | same | Consequences are in the labelled reason dialog, not the consequence sheet | The sheet is #5927 (L17). |
| L11 | same | The Seats tab keeps the seat editor | The canvas draws seats read-only. The editor stays on its own tab, so Terms' "edited on Seats" links and a request's Open terms still land. |
| L11 | same | Phone and dark layouts | The canvas draws neither. Built to the house rules: rows stack below `sm`, targets are 44px, and every light utility has its `dark:` pair. |
