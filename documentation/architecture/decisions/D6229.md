- The zone row is Overview · LPs · Calls · Ledger · Reporting, and `?fund=` travels across zones.
- Committed, called, collected, outstanding, deployed, distributed and dry powder are separate figures, each with what it counts and its as-of date.
- NAV, RVPI, TVPI and IRR read Not recorded with their reasons, never 0.
- Money actions (new call, receipt, distribution) follow Prepare → Review → Confirm → Authenticate → Receipt. Receipts are append-only.
- The locked state is one surface for every zone, saying what the add-on includes, with Talk to Axal (FV2).
- The ledger offers a read-only journal for the GP of record (FV9, option A).
- Issued is not delivered: the reporting archive shows Delivered as Not recorded (FV10).
- States follow Platform States (D5129), with Unavailable as the failed-read word. Eadwyn is the shared rail (#5130).
