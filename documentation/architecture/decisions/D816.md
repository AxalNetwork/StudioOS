## D816 — Every paid plan sells monthly or yearly: the founder and investor checkouts take an interval, the status reads say which is held, and the public plans read gives /pricing both prices

- **Status:** filed by S1 for the owner on 2026-10-20 (#2262; part of D785, #2210, for D786 part 2, #2211). Built by S25 for slot S22, at the owner's request. **A money path: S1 reviews it for security before the owner merges.**
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `9a4eb61e94`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`; frontend paths to `frontend/src/`.
- **The owner's rulings (2026-10-20):**
  - Advisor Pro and Operating Partner Pro cost $20 a month.
  - Every paid plan has a yearly price at 15% off: Validate + Build, Raise + Grow, Investor Plus and Pro, Advisor Pro and Operating Partner Pro.
  - Existing yearly subscribers keep their price.
- **Not here:** the prices themselves. They are created in the Stripe catalogue from HQ's catalogue console (`routes/catalog.ts`), the owner's step on #1008. This decision makes the code sell and show them.

### What was there

- **The persona plans' checkout** (`POST /api/billing/plan/checkout`) already took `{ interval: 'month' | 'year' }` and chose the price with `priceForPlanMetadata(env, 'plan_group', group, interval)`.
- **The founder checkout** (`POST /api/billing/tier/checkout`) sold the tier's first recurring price. Its comment read "interval not encoded in the tier string".
- **The investor checkout** (`POST /api/billing/investor/checkout`) already sold yearly, through its plan key (`investor_pro_yearly`, `investor_inst_yearly`), but took no `interval`.
- **No status read said how often the held plan is charged.** D783's webhook already records each member plan's charge, with its interval, in `member_subscription_amounts` (migration 505).
- **The public plans read** (`GET /api/public/plans-and-packs`, D755 §11) gave every plan its active prices and allowance, cached 60 seconds. #2226 (D786 part 1) made it the role tabs' read; there is no `/api/public/plans`.

### What was decided

1. **One rule for the interval a checkout names** (`checkoutInterval`, `routes/billing.ts`).
   - Absent is `month`, so a caller that names none buys what it always bought.
   - `month` and `year` are the only values. Anything else is 400 `invalid_interval`, never read as monthly: on a money path a typo must not quietly buy the other price.
   - The persona checkout keeps its own reading (anything but `year` is monthly). It is unchanged here, but for the refusal below.
2. **The founder checkout takes `interval`.**
   - It chooses `priceForPlanMetadata(env, 'tier', tier, interval)`.
   - The checkout session's metadata records `interval` beside `tier`.
3. **The investor checkout takes `interval`.**
   - An explicit `interval` chooses the price over the plan key's own; absent, the key's stands, so every caller today is unchanged.
   - The plan key Stripe records (`metadata[plan]`, `subscription_data[metadata][plan]`) is rebuilt from the tier and the interval sold (`investorPlanFor`). It names what was bought, never the key the caller sent.
4. **`catalog_price_missing` names the interval** on all three checkouts: `{ error, interval, detail }`. The page reads it to show "Yearly not offered yet". The status code stays 502, as before.
5. **The status reads return `interval`** (`/tier/status`, `/investor/status`, `/plan/status`).
   - It comes from the charge the webhook recorded (`heldInterval`, `services/planAllowance.ts`): the member's newest row for the family that is not canceled.
   - It is null when no charge is recorded (a free member, one who subscribed before migration 505), when the subscription is canceled, or when the table is missing.
   - The page marks "Current plan · yearly" from it. It never reads Stripe.
6. **The public plans read is extended, not duplicated.**
   - The issue asked for `GET /api/public/plans` unless #2226 already served the catalogue publicly. It does, at `GET /api/public/plans-and-packs`, which `lib/pricingModel.js` reads for the role tabs. A second read of the same mirror would be two answers to keep in step.
   - Each plan now carries `monthly` and `yearly`, each `{ unit_amount, currency, allowance_microusd }` or null, and `yearly_saving_percent` (`intervalPrices`, `services/aiCredits.ts`). Free cards carry nulls.
   - A price counts when it is charged every one month or every one year. The yearly price counts only in the monthly price's currency.
   - **The saving is computed, never stored or hard-coded:** 1 − yearly ÷ (12 × monthly), a whole percent, rounded to the nearest. From $20 and $204 it is 15. It is null without both prices, and null when the yearly price saves nothing.
   - The allowance each price gives is D783's: 25% of the monthly equivalent (a yearly price gives 25% of it ÷ 12).
   - **Only active prices of active products,** as before. **No new id:** `price_id` and `product_id` were already there, and the catalogue page builds its visitor cart from them (`lib/plansPricing.js`). No metadata, licence keys beyond the plan key, or person.
   - **Cached five minutes at the edge** (`public, max-age=300, s-maxage=300`, was 60 seconds), with the existing per-IP `public_plans` bucket.
7. **"Operating Partner Pro".**
   - No page hard-codes "Partner Pro": the persona plans' names come from the catalogue product's name, which the owner renames there.
   - Nothing in the code changed for it.
8. **The API methods** `api.tierCheckout(tier, interval?)` and `api.investorCheckout(plan, interval?)` send `interval` only when given, so today's bodies are unchanged. `lib/api/publicPricing.js` documents the new fields, for #2211 part 2's interval switch.

### Not changed

- **The webhook and D783's allowance.** A yearly charge is already recorded and already gives 25% ÷ 12.
- **The persona checkout's price choice.**
- **Market Intelligence's checkout,** which encodes its interval in its plan.
- **No migration.** No `wrangler.toml` change.
- **Existing yearly subscribers** are not touched: nothing here re-prices a subscription.
