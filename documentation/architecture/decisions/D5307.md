## D5307 — Deal NDA exemptions follow venture ownership (#5307)

D4631 uses staff-first precedence for deal response fields. The NDA exemption answers a separate question: is this caller the platform admin or the venture's founder? The same account can hold both the founder and Operating Partner licences. The documents and insight routes now check ownership directly for the exemption, so the founder can read their own venture and remains subject to the NDA on other ventures.

The existing deal admission and response projections still govern both routes. Route tests exercise the combined licences on documents and all three insight sections.
