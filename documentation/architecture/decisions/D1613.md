### 1. What a session is

An **AI agent session** is one run of one agent, on one of its role's skills:
- optionally on one record (a meeting, deal, deck, module or document), with a short instruction;
- at a time (`once`) or on a repeat (daily, weekly, monthly).

Its work comes back as **proposals in Approvals**, and nothing is applied until a named person accepts it. Before the run, a hold is shown, within the agent's monthly cap, paid from the sponsor's AI tokens.

A session is skipped, with its reason and **without a charge**, in four cases:
- the agent is paused;
- the sponsor is out of tokens;
- a tool the skill reads is disconnected;
- the skill was retired.

These are #3957's terms on #3849's `agent_duties`, not a second scheduler.
