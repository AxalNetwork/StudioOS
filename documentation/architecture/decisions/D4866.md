## D4866 — Grow v2: Reporting as a utility, and related links for the founder's task (#4866)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-27 on #4866, Grow UX V2 part 4 of 5. Boards: GR-02's "For this job" row,
  GR-10 (Reporting as a utility) and GR-16 (phone) of `design/incoming/Grow v2 Page.dc.html` (D4861), with
  GR-04's finding P2 ("'Also in Grow' → Perks only" → task-based contextual links).
- **Subtracted:** D4862 part 2 already put Reporting outside the area tabs, as a header utility on the Grow
  overview and module pages and on the Focus, Customers, Talent and Launch desks (`nav aria-label="Grow
  utilities"`). This part does not redraw that; it closes the gaps and builds what was left.
- **Mints no route.** Every link opens a route already registered; `/grow/reporting`, its Duties, report
  and edit routes, `/perks` and every Grow deep link are unchanged. The Eadwyn rail (#4865, #5130) is not
  touched.

### What changed

- **"For this job" replaces "Also in Grow" (GR-02).** The static list (`workspaces/AlsoIn.jsx` fed by
  `alsoInFor('founder', 'Grow')`) named only Perks, and since D4862 part 2 gave Grow its own `def.Overview`
  it was drawn nowhere, so Perks had no door on Grow at all. The overview now ends in a `nav` landmark headed
  "For this job · <task>" (`GrowRelated.jsx`, `growRelatedModel.js`). The task is the first item of the
  overview's own ranked attention, by its area (Focus, Customers, Distribution, Talent, Partnerships,
  Reporting; an unknown area falls back to the item's route); with nothing ranked, a venture with no focus
  recorded is on Focus, any other gets the canvas's general set. Each task names at most three existing
  destinations, chosen from the issue's candidates (connections, calendar, outreach and email, research,
  reporting) plus Network for Talent, Partnerships and Markets:

  | Task | Links, in order |
  | --- | --- |
  | General (nothing ranked) | Reporting · Calendar · Outreach inbox · Research |
  | Reporting | Reporting · Connections · Calendar |
  | Focus | Reporting · Connections · Research |
  | Customers | Outreach inbox · Email · Research |
  | Distribution | Calendar · Outreach inbox · Research |
  | Talent | Network · Calendar |
  | Partnerships | Network · Email · Outreach inbox |
  | Markets | Research · Network |
  | Company | Reporting · Calendar (compliance layer) |

  Perks closes every list as a plain link with no detail line, once on the page: findable from Grow, never
  ranked or promoted. `PAGE_HOMES['/perks']` is now `['founder', 'Grow', 'related']`, a fourth door that
  `workspaces_retired_d563` checks against the model; the sidebar's Grow row still lights `/perks`.
  A kept stale read (D4864's refresh failure) names no task; loading, refused and failed overviews draw no
  list. Read-only viewers keep the links: they are navigation, not operating actions.
- **Reporting from every Grow surface (GR-10).** `GrowReportingLink` is the one Reporting utility link
  (`aria-label="Reporting utility"`, the venture kept, `aria-current="page"` on Reporting itself). Besides
  the places D4862 part 2 drew it, it is now on a desktop record's header, on Distribution's own header (all
  nine tabs), and on Grow's phone module pages beside the module's own controls. Another cycle draws none.
- **Readiness for the next update (GR-10).** For a founder who writes the desk, the Reporting desk gains
  "Readiness for the next update" above the History (`reporting/ReportReadiness.jsx`,
  `readinessModel.js`): one read of `GET /api/cycles/grow/overview`, each figure exactly as the overview's
  card says it through D4864's `growKpi` (a current value, Stale with its age, a measured zero as a value,
  Not recorded with its reason, Unavailable), a Highlights row counted from What changed, "N of M figures
  current", and Focus linked with the venture for fixing a stale or missing figure. Loading, Unavailable
  with Retry, and Refused say so and never claim a figure is missing.
- **Keyboard and screen readers.** Every new control is a native link in tab order with a visible focus
  ring; "For this job" is a labelled navigation landmark with a heading; the readiness card is a labelled
  region whose chips use the shared D5129 words.
- **Phone (GR-16).** Below 640px "For this job" is one 44px link per row, full width, read top to bottom;
  from 640px the links wrap as chips. Nothing scrolls sideways. The readiness rows stack label, source and
  state on a phone. Distribution's header actions now wrap.

### Departures from the canvas

- **"For this job" on the sparse overview too.** GR-01 does not draw it; GR-02 does. It is drawn on both,
  because the sparse overview is where a founder's task is clearest (Focus) and Perks would otherwise have
  no door on a new venture's Grow.
- **Detail lines are purpose words, not counts.** GR-02 draws "November due 3 Dec", "2 launch dates",
  "5 replies", "UK market". The overview read carries none of these except a due report, so Reporting
  shows the Worker's own "Due …" / "Late since …" when a report is due and a fixed purpose line otherwise;
  the others show a fixed purpose line ("Replies to your outreach"). No count is invented.
- **No sidebar "Utilities" group.** GR-10's shell nests the eight areas and a Utilities separator under
  Grow in the sidebar. The shell sidebar is shared across profiles; D4862 part 2 kept Reporting as the
  header utility instead, and this part keeps that.
- **GR-10's own page is the existing desk.** Its crumb ("Back to Grow keeps the period and venture") is
  the desk's Grow tabs, which keep the venture (no period is in the address to keep); its Duties and Draft
  buttons are the desk's Duties tab and Prepare action; its Sent table is the History. Only the readiness
  card is new. GR-10's "Synced 14:05" is drawn as "as of <date>" (D4864: no sync time is stored).
- **The canvas footnote is not repeated.** GR-10 says Share an update becomes the Overview's primary only
  when an update is due and the record is current; the Worker makes it primary when a report is due
  (`growAttention.ts`) and does not read freshness. The card says only what it reads.
- **Phone records.** A Grow record on a phone keeps its bar (‹ Module, title, Eadwyn); Reporting is one
  step away on the module page it returns to. Adding it to the record bar would crowd the bar the Eadwyn
  button shares, which #4865 owns.
