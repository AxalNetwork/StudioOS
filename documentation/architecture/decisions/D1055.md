### 1. PAYX20, the phone at 390

The sheet was already full screen below `sm` (640px). Below `sm` it now has:
- **The top bar: "‹ Back" and "Checkout".** ‹ Back closes the sheet, as × does. The × is the desktop's only, so the phone has one way out in the bar.
- **The summary as one row:** "[Plan] · [$20]/month · Details ⌄" (`summaryRow`).
  - The row is the interval's own price line, cut to its monthly figure, so a yearly plan reads "$17/month" and its billed total stays in Details.
  - With no price it names the plan.
  - Details opens the full summary (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) and reads "Details ⌃" while open. From `sm` up the summary is always drawn and the row is not.
- **Pay held at the bottom with the total.** The Pay row is `sticky bottom-0` inside the sheet's scroll, on the card's ground (with its dark pair). Above Pay, "Due today [$21.00]", only when the Worker sent the total (`payTotal`); a missing figure is never drawn as $0.00. From `sm` up the row is in place, as before.
- **Accepted and Refused in one column.** The grid was one column already; Accepted's Close, Open Billing and Back to [page] now stack too.
- **The renewal banner** already wraps (`flex-wrap`). Its buttons, and its sheet's links, are 44px.
- **Every touch target is at least 44 × 44px.** That covers:
  - the links (`LINK`);
  - the Monthly and Yearly switch, 36px from `sm`;
  - the promo field and Apply, 40px from `sm`;
  - both Pay with rows;
  - the banner's buttons (`BTN`), 36px from `sm`.

  The test also checks that no target under 44px is left outside an `sm:` size.

### 2. PAYX21, dark

- **The sheet** had its dark pairs already, and `check-dark-mode` stays green. The held Pay row adds `dark:bg-gray-900` and `dark:border-gray-700`.
- **The Payment Element.** The shared appearance (`lib/stripe.js`, `buildAppearance`) serves six forms and is not changed. In dark it draws a selected tab's label and icon in the deep brand violet, which does not read on the dark card. The sheet's form now uses `sheetAppearance(buildAppearance(isDark), isDark)`, which adds `SHEET_DARK_RULES` in dark only:
  - the tabs on gray-900 with a gray-700 hairline, gray-600 on hover;
  - the selected tab with a violet-400 edge;
  - labels and icons in gray-200, and the selected one in violet-300.

  Light is unchanged, and so is the form the pack sheet (D1287) shares, apart from these dark rules.
- **Apple Pay stays black.** It stays first in the Payment Element (`paymentMethodOrder`). Stripe draws Apple Pay's own mark and button inside the Element. The appearance API styles the tab around it, which gets the dark hairline above, but cannot restyle Apple's button. A white hairline round a black Apple Pay button would need the Express Checkout Element (`buttonTheme`). That is a different element and a different confirm flow, and is not built here (§4).
- **Status colours** keep their dark pairs: the declines' red, the test-mode pill's amber and Accepted's positive check are D1053's and D1054's, unchanged.
