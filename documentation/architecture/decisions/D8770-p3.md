## D8770 part 3 — Pitch › template previews: the template's chrome and decor, Example unavailable, Try again, and the preview as a phone sheet (#8770)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-27, against `main` at `1accc5c64`. Continues D8770-p2. The owner accepted option A for every open question of D8770 (issue comment, 2026-11-27); this part builds Q1 (Example first) and Q2 (one shared example company) as p2 did, and touches neither Q3 (already built) nor Q4 (brand kit, which only matters once the theme survives Keep).
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Pitch Template Composition Page.dc.html`, boards PTP08, PTP11, PTP16 and PTP20 to PTP25.
- **Scope kept:** slide composition in the template previews and at small sizes. Nothing here touches export (`ExportDialog.jsx`, D8771) or the preflight panel (D8772).

### Built

- **The template's chrome (PTP08, PTP11).** `templatePreviewModel.templateChrome` reads the store's `theme.chrome` (`templateLibrary.compose`): the name top left, bottom left or nowhere, the slide number bottom right, a hairline over the footer and the card's title as a section label. It travels on the face (`previewFace(...).chrome`), so every preview card draws it: Kitchen Table's traction card reads "[Venture name] · Traction" top left, with the footer rule and "03". A theme with no template chrome (a saved version's, in Yours) keeps p2's plain chrome. `chromeLines` puts a bottom-left name before the example's source line, so the two never overlap.
- **The template's decor (PTP08).** `decorPlan` places each card's decor at export size. A heading's divider is a short rule (the canvas's 56 px at 960, `RULE_PX` = 112 at 1920) in the gutter below the heading's area, so it never sits over the words; Kitchen Table's is the double rule. A divider in a one-column area spanning rows (Nib and Trace's gutter) is a vertical rule. Icons, shapes and textures are the elements library's own drawings (`GET /decks/elements` and `/elements/icons?refs=…`, read once per preview through `deckV2Adapter.elementsLibrary`), drawn in the item's tone (`decorArtSrc`); Kitchen Table's title and Why now carry the grain, Why now the lightbulb. When the library, or the Worker's icon set, cannot be read, that ornament is left out, never a broken image. Rules need no read. At most 16 items, as the slide model holds.
- **Example unavailable (PTP16).** A template the example has words for on none of its cards (one of Yours whose cards were renamed) opens on its blank structure: `viewFor` falls back, Example is disabled, and the line under the switch says "Example unavailable · The example has no card for this template’s layouts, so the blank structure is shown." Placeholders are never shown as if they were the example.
- **Preview cards failed (PTP16, PTP25).** The preview's error now has Try again, which reads the cards again (`previewRead` drops a failed read from its cache), and Use this template stays off until the cards have read.
- **The phone (PTP20 to PTP25).** Below 1024 px (`usePhoneWidth`, the app's phone rule) selecting a cover opens the preview as a sheet over the covers (`Lightbox phone="sheet"`, a bottom sheet under 640 px) instead of an aside under all 24 covers. The sheet shows what the template sets, the switch and its note, one card (the first with a chart: Kitchen Table's traction, `sheetCard`), "Preview all 13 slides", and Use this template pinned to its foot. Full size on a phone is titled "3 of 13 · Traction" (`phoneHeading`) and says "Turn the phone for a larger slide · pinch to zoom" in place of the arrow-key line. The switch, Open full size, Previous, Next, Try again and Use this template are 44 px tall under 640 px.
- **Tests.** `frontend/test/template_preview_8770_p3.test.mjs` (7 tests: chrome, decor placement, library art and tones, the server-rendered card, Example unavailable, the phone full size, the gallery's wiring). `frontend/tests/visual/template_composition_8770.spec.mjs` gains three browser tests (the 390 px sheet with its chrome, double rule, grain and pinned Use this template; Try again; Example unavailable) and its 390 px pass now reads the sheet; `template_previews_5218.spec.mjs`'s phone pass reads the sheet too. Both specs ran locally against the real Worker in Chromium: 12 of 12.

### Departures from the canvas

- The canvas's numbered step badges (`STEP(n)`, `aria-hidden`) annotate the route between boards; they are not drawn in the product.
- "Example unavailable" says the example has no card for the template's layouts, not that it "could not be read": the example store is a frontend module that is always read, so the only real case is a template it does not cover.
- The heading rule sits in the gutter below the heading's area, not directly under the last line of text: the preview places decor on the grid, as the store records it, and does not measure the words.
- Between 640 and 1023 px the sheet is the centred dialog `Lightbox` draws there, not a bottom sheet.
- The gallery covers draw the chrome and rules but not the library's art: a cover reads only its own template, and the art waits for a selected preview.
