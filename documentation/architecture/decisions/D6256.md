### What the canvas decides

- The investor Overview (NW12) shows four figures, each saying what it counts: ties on Axal, going cold (no touch in over 60 days), reminders due, curated matches awaiting a decision. A line below says "N of M asks left this quarter".
- Going cold means a last touch more than 60 days ago. A tie with no touch is Not recorded, never cold. Cold is a word and a glyph, never colour alone.
- Relationships (NW12.2) has two labelled groups, "On Axal" and "Contacts you keep", each with its own count and pager. They are never merged into one count.
- The Tie Sheet holds touch history with Void (struck through, not removed), open and done reminders, the private note and Request introduction.
- Introductions (NW3) has three groups: From your team, Curated matches, and Your asks. Accepting a match costs 1 credit; declining is free. An ask shows Pending only.
- Request introduction (NW3.1) has three steps. When no teammate knows the target and it is a founder or startup on Axal, the investor can spend one quarterly ask. Anyone else is told plainly that an ask cannot be used.
- Made is recorded only by a send from the Email composer. There is no Made button. NW3.5 covers a send whose delivery could not be confirmed.
- Organizations come only from a recorded company name, never an email domain. Portfolio, Passed and LPs views are drawn disabled.
- Eadwyn proposes drafts with the cost quoted first. It never sends, spends a credit or an ask, or edits a note.
