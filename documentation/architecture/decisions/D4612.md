## D4612 — Research watches its indexing, reports batches, and links only where the reader is admitted (#4612)

#4612 (Research audit 4/5, slot S19, taken at the owner's request). It builds on D4605's index states (`partial`) and does not take #4610's shared-document contract, which is not in this change.

### Background indexing

- While an own document is `pending`, the library re-reads itself on a bounded schedule (`pollDelay`: 2, 3, 5, 8, 13 s, then every 15 s, at most `MAX_POLLS` = 20), quietly, and stops once nothing is pending, the schedule runs out (the page says so and offers Check again), or the page leaves. A re-read that answers after a newer one, or after unmount, is dropped (`recordGate.js`, D4607).
- A failed or partly indexed row shows its recorded `index_note` and offers Re-index; a file nothing could be read from offers replacing it, because reading it again reads nothing again.
- Re-index targets own documents that are not `indexed` and not already `pending` (the route refuses those with `already_queued`), runs two at a time, keeps the page busy until every one has answered, and reports how many went, how many were already being read, and which could not, by title.
- The Ask history read beside the library has its own state. A failed read is Unreadable with a retry, never "0 questions asked".
- The worker counts a `partial` document as answerable (`in_ask`, `indexed`) and returns `pending`.

### Navigation

- `frontend/src/lib/researchAccess.js` restates `App.jsx`'s Research admissions in one table; `research_links_4612.test.mjs` fails if the two disagree, so the routes stay where they are. `canOpenResearch` honours `labRoles`'s two widenings (an active Spin-Out Lab admits any role; an admin is admitted everywhere). Nothing is widened.
- Sector, market-sector, country and company pages link a company or fund profile only for a reader admitted to it, and name it otherwise. A company profile's fund lists keep their filter on "See all" (`directoryHref({ focus })`, `directoryHref({ hq_country })`), so the total the link names is the list it opens.
- The fund directory keeps search, filters, sort, page size and offset in the address (`queryFromSearch` / `searchFromQuery`), replacing the history entry on each change, so a direct link, a reload, opening a profile and Back all land on the same list. Other parameters (`view`) are kept.
