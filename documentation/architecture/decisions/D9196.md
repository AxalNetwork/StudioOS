## D9196 — Public site accessibility: door text accents, the team photo link's name, and focusable Trust tables (#9196)

- **Status:** built for #9196 (coordinator audit of 2026-11-27, under #6976).
- **Out of scope:** the logo link's name (UXP-14, `PublicNav.jsx`) is fixed by PR #9156 (#9037) and is not touched here.
- **Migration:** none.

### 1. Each door has a text accent

The canvas's door accents are light: emerald-600 `#059669` on white is about 3.8:1 and amber-600 `#d97706` about 3.2:1, under WCAG AA's 4.5:1 for small text. Each door in `frontend/src/lib/siteDoors.js` now carries `accentText` beside `accent`:

| Door | `accent` (decoration) | `accentText` (words, and fills under white words) |
|---|---|---|
| founders | `#6d28d9` | `#6d28d9` (already 7.1:1) |
| investors | `#059669` | `#047857` (emerald-700) |
| partners | `#2563eb` | `#1d4ed8` (blue-700; `#2563eb` was 4.55:1 on its soft tint, too close) |
| advisors | `#d97706` | `#b45309` (amber-700) |

`DoorPage.jsx` sets it as `--door-text`. In `pages/site/doors.css` every rule that colours words with the door (label, fact and FAQ numbers, chapter numbers, the status pill on `--door-soft`, plan step, tick, tokens link) and every coloured fill that carries white words (hero badge, last flow chip, plan tag, the highlighted plan's button) reads `--door-text`. `--door-accent` stays only on decoration: the dots and the highlighted plan's border.

Each `accentText` reaches 4.5:1 on white, on the door's `soft` tint, on the plans band (`#faf8ff`), and under white words. The public site is light only (`SitePage` calls `useForcedLightTheme`), so dark mode draws the same pairs; the test checks that framing and the pairs for both.

### 2. The team photo link is named

On `/team` the photo is a LinkedIn link. With no photo recorded, the member unread, or the image failed, its only child is an empty box, so it now carries `aria-label="<name> on LinkedIn"` (`Managing Partner` when no name was read). While the photo shows, the image's `alt` names the link and no label overrides it.

### 3. Trust tables scroll by keyboard

The four wide tables on the Trust Center (What we check, Subprocessors, Documents, How fast we answer) sit in a shared `TableScroll` in `TrustCenter.jsx`: the `overflow-x-auto` scroller with `tabIndex={0}`, `role="region"` and an `aria-label` naming its table, so at 390px the hidden columns can be reached with the arrow keys (axe's scrollable-region-focusable).

### 4. Tests

`frontend/test/public_a11y_9196.test.mjs`: the contrast of every door's text accent on each background, the CSS rules that may not pair words with `--door-accent`, the page setting `--door-text`, the team link's name with no photo, a failed photo and an unread member, and each Trust scroller's focus, role and name, mounted. Once #9156 lands, `/for-investors`, `/for-advisors`, `/team` and `/trust` belong in its axe route spec, which checks color-contrast and scrollable-region-focusable in a browser.
