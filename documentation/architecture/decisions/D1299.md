### 1. Addresses

| Address | What it opens |
|---|---|
| `/admin/accounts` | Overview (a Super Admin is still forwarded to Team, D941) |
| `/admin/accounts/all` | All accounts |
| `/admin/accounts/all/:id` | All accounts with that account's record open |

- **The model.** `pages/admin/accountsAddress.js` holds `ACCOUNTS_TABS`, `accountRecordHref`, `recordIdOf`, `PROFILE_WIDGETS` and `widgetCount`.
  - A record id is a positive whole number.
- **The record belongs to the address.** `UsersConsole` takes `openId` and `onOpenChange`: a row's click and the record's Close move the address.
  - HQ › Team embeds the same console without them, so its record stays in state.
- **The controls rule.** A record opened by id before its row is read has no role yet. `drawsAccountControls` draws no View As or Disable until the role is known (`lib/accountControls.js`), because nothing yet says the account is not an administrator's.
  - The Worker refuses those actions on an administrator anyway (`admin.ts`).
- **The link.** "Open member record" in #2369 and #2370 targets `/admin/accounts/all/:id`, which now resolves.

### 2. The Overview (ACC1)

- **Header.** `AdminPageHeader` with the title "Accounts", the line "Every account this studio holds, by profile: who joined, who is still exploring, and who needs you.", and the two tabs.
- **Totals.** Active accounts, new in 7 days, new in 30 days and deactivated, from the glance.
- **By profile:** Founders, Investors, Advisors, Operating Partners and Explorers, each from the glance's `by_role`.
  - The glance groups every active account by role, so a profile it does not list is a measured 0.
  - A failed glance is Unreadable, never 0.
- **LPs and Product testers** have no count on any account read yet. Each says why: an LP is an approved application, not a role (D1301 counts it); there is no tester sub-account yet (D1302 adds it).
- **"Also in Accounts"** keeps the four doors: the Users table, the Exploring board, Persona segments and Trash.

### 3. All accounts (ACC2)

- The Admin shell's page carries the two tabs.
- A Super Admin's carries none, since their `/admin/accounts` is Team's (D941).
- The table is today's `UsersConsole`, until D1305.

### 4. Open decisions

#2377 asks no numbered questions, but its plan names two calls. The integration takes what the canvas draws, and the owner can override either.
1. **What counts as an LP:** an approved LP application. Whether an LP record in a fund (`limited_partners.user_id`) also counts is D1301's to state.
2. **Product testers:** any account with a profile may apply, Explorers included and Admins not. Any Admin or Super Admin decides. The statuses are applied, active, paused, declined and removed. The member's apply flow still waits on #2318.
