## D709 — The 2% management fee: fund terms, a tested calculator per LP and period, and fee periods the GP issues without moving money

Issue #1774, on the owner's instruction of 2026-10-17. Mercury carries the 2% management fee, which the Fund pays to Axal VC GP LLC, the General Partner. The Fund's documents set the fee at 2% a year, on commitments during the investment period and on net invested capital after it, payable quarterly in advance (`templates/legal/subscription_booklet_lpa_v1.md:15`). The `lpa` seed says "invested capital" with no "net" (`085_seed_legal_templates.sql:158-162`); the signed LPA settles which, so the GP records the figure (decision 3).

Before this, nothing worked out an LP's fee. D628's `fund_fee_terms` holds one fund-level basis for the books; `vc_funds.management_fee` is never used for accrual (D628). D709 adds its own tables (migration 473) and never reads or writes D628's tables or that column.

### Decisions

1. **Fee terms per fund, versioned, confirmed by the GP** (`fund_mgmt_fee_terms`). Defaults, which the GP confirms against the LPA:
   - `annual_bps` 200 (0 to 10000);
   - `basis_during` `committed`, and `basis_after` `net_invested` or `committed`;
   - `frequency` `quarterly_in_advance` and `payee` `gp`, each the only value;
   - `offset_bps` 10000;
   - a required fee start date and investment-period end, `effective_from`, and an `lpa_reference` of 1 to 500 characters.

   A row is written as `draft`, and only `POST …/confirm` makes it `confirmed`, the only state that bills. The first confirmed version takes effect on its fee start date. A later one takes effect on the first day of a quarter after every issued period, checked again at confirm, so nothing confirms into the past. Confirmed versions never share an `effective_from` and all keep the first fee start date. A quarter bills under the version in force on its window's first day.
2. **Admission dates are recorded, never inferred** (`fund_lp_admissions`). `commitment_date` is never read. A committed or active LP with a positive commitment and no admission date blocks the quarter. The date is fixed once that LP has a fee line that is not a draft.
3. **The basis.** Up to and including the investment-period end, the LP's commitment (`commitmentCents` of `commitment_amount`). After it, the LP's share of net invested capital: `net_invested_cents × commitment ÷ the quarter's billable commitments`. The latest record on or before that segment's first day applies; with none, the quarter is refused as Not recorded. Billable LPs are the call preview's (committed or active, a positive commitment), admitted on or before the earlier of the quarter's last day and today (UTC).
4. **The arithmetic** (`services/fundFeeCalc.ts`, pure: no D1, no `fetch`, no clock).
   - Days are actual, inclusive, over 365, as in D628.
   - Each amount is one BigInt fraction rounded once, half to even, to whole cents. A quarter straddling the investment-period end is one sum of its two segments, rounded once.
   - Anything above `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` cents is refused.
5. **Partial periods and catch-up.**
   - A period is a calendar quarter (`2026Q4`), whose window runs from the later of its first day and the fee start date to its last day.
   - An LP with no issued line in an earlier period, first billed in a window that starts after the fee start, owes catch-up: the fee on its commitment from the fee start to the day before the window, with no interest.
   - A catch-up span that crosses the investment-period end or a change of terms version is refused. A later closing after the investment period is not modelled.
6. **Offsets** (`fund_fee_offsets`).
   - The GP records a fee a portfolio company of this fund (a `portfolio_positions` row) paid to an Axal entity: Holdings, Management or the GP.
   - An offset counts once, in the first period issued after it is recorded whose window starts after its received date, at `amount × offset_bps / 10000` (half to even). That issue sets `counted_in_period_id`.
   - The pool is the previous period's carry plus the newly counted offsets. The amount applied is the smaller of the pool and the period's gross plus catch-up; the rest carries.
   - It splits in proportion to each line's gross plus catch-up, shares floored, with the residual to the largest line (lowest LP id on a tie) as D371 splits a call. No line's offset passes its base; a cent that would goes to the next line in that order.
   - Which payments are offsettable is the LPA's question, so the GP records them; the Spin-Out Lab conflict (Management charging founders whose companies may be Fund I's) is why the rule exists.
7. **Periods and lines move from `draft` to `issued` to `paid`.**
   - **Preview** recomputes and, with no refusal, replaces the quarter's draft and answers a `digest`: SHA-256 of canonical JSON (sorted keys, lines by LP id) of the window, the lines and every input id (terms version, admissions, net invested record, offsets, previous period). A refusal saves nothing.
   - **Issue** recomputes, and a digest that differs is 409. One D1 batch holds the compare-and-set (`WHERE status = 'draft' AND digest = ?`), its audit row, the lines' freeze and the offsets' count, each guarded on that compare-and-set having landed.
   - **Order.** A quarter issues only after the quarter before it, unless that one ends before the fee start. A quarter after the coming one is refused, since the fee is in advance.
   - **Twice.** Issuing an issued quarter with its digest answers `already_issued: true` and writes nothing; any other digest is 409.
   - **References.** Lines get `AXAL-MF-<quarter>-L<limited_partners.id>`, and the period `AXAL-MF-<quarter>-F<fund id>` for the Fund's remittance to the GP. Both are unique. #1775 matches by amount and date window, never by reference.
   - **Seals.** Triggers keep an issued row's amounts fixed, its status moving forward only, its call link set once, and DELETE to drafts.
8. **Funding and paid.**
   - A line's `paid` means the LP's share reached the Fund, not that the Fund paid the GP.
   - A line links, at issue or later, to its LP's line in a capital call of the same fund. Net fees linked to one call line may not pass what it owes; a line that would, or whose LP has no line in that call, stays unlinked with its reason.
   - `recordReceipt` pays a linked line in the batch that pays its call line in full, with the receipt as evidence. That covers the receipts route and mark-paid. Linking to an already-paid call line pays the line at once.
   - A period is `paid` when all its lines are.
9. **Statements move no money.** A statement gives the window, days, rate, basis segments, gross, catch-up, offset, net, reference code, funding call line, status and `lpa_reference`. The LP funds its share by a capital contribution, so `pay_to` is `{ due: false, sentence: 'Nothing is due from you against this statement.' }`. The period read and each statement carry the period's `remittance`: the Fund's payment of the fee to the GP, under the period's `AXAL-MF-<quarter>-F<fund>` reference. Only when #1773's `fundPayeeOf(env, fundId)` answers `'fund'` (Fund I) does it read `payInstructionsFor('gp', 'management_fee')`: the GP's masked account with #1773's warning, or #1773's code and sentence (for example `no_active_account` until the GP's checking is entered). For any other fund it is `{ available: false, code: 'not_an_axal_fund', message: 'Axal holds no payment instructions for this fund.' }`, the registry is never read, and nothing names Axal VC GP LLC as payee. Issuing needs no account.
10. **Not here:**
    - the GP screen and the LP's view (#1779's PR 3 adds `fundFeesApi`), and the Investment Management Agreement's recipient (#1772);
    - per-LP terms (a waiver on the GP's own commitment, or a side letter's rate or offset), so every billable LP pays the fund's terms;
    - a call billing each LP its own fee line, equalisation and carry;
    - posting to D628's books, and replacing the LP report's `FUND_COSTS`;
    - printing or sending statements;
    - the remittance's arrival at the GP, which is #1775's period match.

### Rounding note

D709 rounds half to even because half up drifts in the GP's favour across many lines and quarters. D628's `feeForPeriod` keeps half up for the books, so the two can differ by one cent on a line, as the tie example shows.
