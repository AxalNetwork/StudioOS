## D7661 — The company switcher's trigger is named by the words it shows (#7661)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-19. Filed from D7517's follow-ups (#7517, #7544) on the owner's answer of 2026-11-19.
- **Builds on:** D7517 (the axe scan that found it), D699 (the switcher's honest states: "…", "Unavailable", "No company"), PF5 / D1413 (the Company and Project selection and its badge).
- **No migration, no Worker route, no client method, no new dependency.**

### The question

The expanded `switcher-trigger` in `ui/CompanySwitcher.jsx` had `aria-label="Switch company or project"` when nothing was selected. The button shows "NC No company" (or "U Unavailable"), so its name did not contain its visible text: WCAG 2.5.3 Label in Name, axe `label-content-name-mismatch` (serious). A speech-input user who says "click No company" cannot reach it. The selected case, "Switch company or project. Current: Example Ventures, Company", failed the same rule in a browser too, because the initials mark "EV" is visible text that the label lacked.

### The decision

The button has no `aria-label`. The new `SwitcherTriggerFace` draws its content in two parts:

- one screen-reader-only sentence, `switcherTriggerName(selection)`. It is the visible text in the order drawn, then the action: name, type, the project's "in …" line, then `switch company or project`, joined with commas;
- the drawn parts (the initials mark and the name, badge and sub-line), `aria-hidden`. They look exactly as before.

Names, as Chromium computes them:

| State | Visible | Accessible name |
|---|---|---|
| No company | NC · No company | No company, switch company or project |
| Read failed | U · Unavailable | Unavailable, switch company or project |
| Company | EV · Example Ventures · Company | Example Ventures, Company, switch company or project |
| Project | AP · Atlas Pilot · Project · in Example Ventures | Atlas Pilot, Project, in Example Ventures, switch company or project |

Every name starts with its visible words, so Label in Name holds. The initials mark only repeats the name, so it is not read out. The selected case keeps its meaning: the current choice is named, then what the button does. "Current:" goes, because the name now leads with the choice itself.

**Why one sentence and not commas between the drawn parts.** Commas in their own `sr-only` spans each became a separate block in Chromium's name computation, which read "No company , switch company or project". One sentence gives an exact name.

Because there is no `aria-label`, axe's `label-content-name-mismatch` no longer applies to the button. The name comes from its content, and that content starts with what is on screen. The collapsed trigger (initials only, with `title`) is unchanged.

### Tests

- `frontend/test/switcher_name_7661.test.mjs` (5 tests) pins the shape of the content (the sentence, then the hidden mark, then the hidden drawn text) and the name and the visible text with no company, after a failed read, with a company and with a project. It also checks that the trigger carries no `aria-label`. Mutation-checked: putting the `aria-label` back, moving the action first, dropping the type, un-hiding the drawn text, un-hiding the mark, or showing the sentence on screen each fails at least one test (6 of 6 caught).
- In Chromium (the D7517 fixture pattern at 1440 px, light and dark, with no company, a failed read and a selected company), axe under the WCAG 2.0 to 2.2 A and AA tags reports nothing on `switcher-trigger`. Before the change all six states reported `label-content-name-mismatch`. That check was a local scratch spec and is not committed. The shell is still outside `expertise_axe_7517.spec.js`'s scope.
