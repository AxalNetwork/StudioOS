### 1. The desk (RPT1, RPT7)

The desk reads `GET /api/reporting/desk` once. The History still reads the Durable company-building module's `parts.updates`, which lists Share an update's updates beside the reports. Each read answers alone, so a failed desk never hides the History.

| Block | What it draws, from the desk read |
|---|---|
| **Due next** | The earliest open period not yet late, with every period of the same window, grouped by duty with their holders. Part 3's draft meets them all with one report (D1059 §4). With nothing ahead it is the earliest late one; with nothing open it says so. |
| **Readiness** | Not drawn as a number. The desk read carries no figure; readiness is the report's own read, so the block says Prepare reads every figure with its source. |
| **Needs you** | Each late or missing period, with its expected date if one was given, then each ask. The asker is the period's holder (only a holder may ask, D1058). The founders only (`can_write`). |
| **The calendar** | One row per duty, twelve months (five before today's, today's, six after), each period in the month it comes due, today's column lit. The grid holds one tab stop; arrow keys, Home and End move it. A press selects a period: its due date and lateness, the expected date its holder sees, and Prepare it and Give an expected date for the founders. View as table lists the same periods by due date. |
| **History** | As D1056 drew it. |

**The header.** The primary is "Prepare [period] report" for Due next's period. It opens the draft (`POST /periods/:uid/draft`, so preparing twice opens the same one) and goes to its builder. Share an update and Reminder settings sit in the More menu (`CycleHeader`'s new `more` slot). A refusal from Prepare stays under the header in the Worker's words.

**Lateness is never red.** Late and missing are amber, due is violet, sent is green and waived is grey; a refusal is the only red. A test reads `STATE_TONE` and `DeskBlocks.jsx` for it.

**The dialogs (RPT7a, RPT7b)** are in the page:
- **Give an expected date** posts `{ expected_on, note }`, a day from today on and a note within 280 characters, and says the period's holder sees them.
- **Reminder settings** offers the defaults (14 and 3 days before, the day, 7 days late) and any saved offset, the two channels and the founder team from the read. It says when nothing is saved yet. Reminders go to the team only.

Both keep a refusal in the dialog and read the desk again on success.

### 2. Duties and the drawer (RPT2, RPT8)

- **Duties** is one row per duty: holder, kind, cadence, due rule, required figures, documents, source and record (sent, late, missing, waived), and the next open period.
  - **Grouping:** by investor or by kind.
  - **Search:** by holder or kind.
  - **Filters:** kind, cadence, and state (late or missing, due within 14 days, nothing open late).
- **The required figures** are the duty's own when its document names them. Otherwise they follow `requiredFiguresOf`'s rule (D1059 §2): migration 168's required KPI rules for a monthly financial or quarterly KPI duty, said as "the studio's KPI rules; the document names none".
  - The page's copy of those rules (`KPI_RULES_REQUIRED`) and of the registry's labels (`FIGURE_LABELS`, keys and aliases) is held to `reportFigures.ts` by a test that reads its source.
- **The drawer** shows:
  - whether the duty came from a signed envelope or from the closing's template;
  - the clause's reference (`source_clause`, e.g. "Investors' Rights Agreement §1.1(a)"), with a door to Raise;
  - the template default, when the document named no deadline;
  - the terms, the record strip of every period in its state words, and the record line.

**Departure: the clause's words.** RPT8 quotes the clause's text. No store holds it: a duty keeps the document, the clause's reference and the signed text's fingerprint (D1058). The drawer quotes the reference and says the words stay in the signed document.

### 3. The builder (RPT3–RPT5, RPT19a)

**The route.** `/grow/reporting/:id/edit` opens a draft for the founders. `/grow/reporting/:id` is the sent report. A draft at the sent address moves to its builder, and a sent report at the builder's address moves to its own.

**Opening re-reads.** Opening the builder saves the draft once with no change (`PUT /reports/:uid {}`), because each save re-reads every Grow store (D1059 §4). The figures are then today's, and `unreadable` names a module that could not be read. If that save is refused (a support session, say), the figures last saved are drawn and the page says so.

| Step | What it does |
|---|---|
| **1 · Figures** | Every figure by Grow module, in tab order, then the founders' typed figures. Each figure shows:<br>• its value or a dash with the Worker's reason (a measured 0 stays 0);<br>• its source chip: "Connector · Stripe · 31 Oct", "Connector · Mercury · computed", "Typed · entered by…", or "◐ Team only · from your Mercury";<br>• Required or Optional.<br>"In the report" is disabled for a figure with no value. "Share from my Mercury" appears for a team-only figure. A required figure left out asks for the reason the investor reads, saved when the field is left; while empty, it says Send refuses it.<br>A typed figure has a name, a value, a unit and a date, at most eight. An unreadable module heads the step ("Unreadable: [module] could not be read. The rest is ready."). The readiness line counts the required figures in, left out with a reason, left out with none, and the modules not read. |
| **2 · Narrative and documents** | The title. Eadwyn's lines, from Share an update's `POST /share/draft-lines` (D1059 §5 names this part to wire it). Each proposal sits on lavender with its source and may be edited before Accept.<br>**The Accept rule (#2431):** a line lands only when accepted, and only with text and a cited source; one with no source shows why and its Accept is disabled. The lines in the report can be removed. The founders' own lines cite "Written by the founders", as Share an update's do (D654).<br>"In your words" is optional, 4,000 characters. Statements: PDFs, at most ten, 6 MB each, each downloadable. |
| **3 · Recipients** | Each holder owed a covered period, with the duties it meets for them; the team as a choice. There is no network option. |
| **4 · Preview** | As an investor or as the team (`GET /preview?as=`). A stale preview says why, offers to save again, and holds Send. |

**Send (RPT5d)** is an in-page dialog. It lists:
- that figures freeze now (or, for a correction, who is told and with what reason);
- what it meets, per duty and holder;
- what it is sent without, and for whom, with the reason, or that Send refuses while a reason is missing.

It posts the preview's digest. A privacy finding (422 `pii_found`) stays in the dialog with its kind and where, never the words it caught.

**The page sends choices, never values.** A figure row sends `{ key, include, reason, share }`, and only the founders' typed figures carry a value. A test checks no save body carries `"value"` for a sourced figure.

### 4. The sent report (RPT6)

The sent report shows:
- the Sent chip (and Corrected), the sent line with its revision, and the revision strip: the reason of this revision, and the one that corrected it;
- for the founders, the recipients with Delivered or the Worker's reason it was not delivered, and the date each opened;
- the report as frozen, and its statements.

**Correct this report** is the founders' while no later revision exists. It asks for a reason of 3 to 280 characters and opens the new revision's draft in the builder. There, the correction's reason is editable and Send's dialog says everyone the last revision reached is told.

### 5. Who reads (RPT14, RPT15)

**The guard widens only as the Worker reads.**
- `GET /desk` and `GET /reports/:uid` admit an admin, and an advisor or Operating Partner who is a member of the venture (`canAccessProject`).
- So `/grow/reporting`, `/duties` and `/:id` now admit `advisor` and `partner` too.
- The builder (`/:id/edit`) stays `admin`, `founder`: the Worker refuses an admin's draft, and a draft is 404 to anyone outside the founder team.

**What a reader gets.** Whoever the Worker gives `can_write: false` reads the desk with "Reporting · [venture]", a "◉ Read only" pill and their line, and without Grow's module tabs, which their role cannot open.
- **The admin's line:** "Read only: admins never prepare or send a company's report."
- **Everyone else's:** "The founders prepare and send reports."
- **Drawn:** the calendar, a selected period and the History.
- **Not drawn:** Due next, Needs you, Prepare, the expected date and the More menu.

A sent report read by them is the reader view: the frozen report, "Read only", no recipients and no Correct.
