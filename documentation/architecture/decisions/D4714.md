## D4714 — Practice says what it read: decision times, unread sources and unpriced hours (#4714)

- **Status:** Part 1 built for #4714 (Practice audit 5/6), 2026-11-08. Worker and frontend.
- **Builds on:** D4702 (root sections from their own stores, `advisor_decision` and `advisor_decided_at`), D4712, D4713.
- **No migration.**

### What was decided

1. **A row is dated by its decision only when the decision's time is recorded.**
   - The Opportunities log's time column and sort read `advisor_decided_at` (D4702) and say "Decided". A row without it says "Last changed" from `updated_at`, which Sessions moves on every billing edit.
   - The CSV carries both columns, "Decided at" and "Last changed".
   - The "Median decision" tile is computed from `created_at` to `advisor_decided_at` over the answers that have it. It says how many it covers ("Over 3 of 4 answers with a recorded time"). With none recorded it says "Not recorded", never zero.
2. **A source that did not answer is not "none".** Each secondary read keeps its own read flag, and a failure is drawn as `Unreadable` with a scoped Try again:
   - **Earnings, payout account:** no state card is marked "Yours", and no Connect is offered.
   - **Earnings, payout history:** it does not say "No payout has been recorded".
   - **Earnings, period note:** nothing can be drafted, typed or filed over a note that was never read, so Accept cannot overwrite a stored note.
   - **Delivery:** the create form's engagement picker says the engagements could not be read.
   - **Engagements:** the link picker says the bookings could not be read, instead of "Nobody has booked you yet".
   The other sources on each page stay drawn.
3. **An hour with no recorded price is never counted free.**
   - `GET /api/advisors/me/slots` carries `booked_pricing` per slot. It comes from the active bookings' `amount_cents` (migration 205), in one grouped read:
     - `priced`: a positive amount;
     - `free`: an explicit 0;
     - `unpriced`: NULL;
     - `mixed`: bookings that disagree;
     - `null`: a slot nobody holds.
   - `slotCounts` counts paid, free and unpriced separately. Mixed or unread pricing is unpriced. The Booked tile adds "N with no price recorded".
4. **The board's header comment is current.** It no longer says renewal-due and opened are never drawn: D4702 draws both from their stores.

### Part 2: the lists page, and their totals are the whole set's

5. **Every Practice list reaches every row.** `GET /api/advisors/me/bookings` (200 per page), `/me/engagements` and `/me/deliverables` (400 per page) take `?limit=&offset=` and answer `page: { offset, limit, total, next_offset }`, as #4689's lists do.
   - **Bookings** page in SQL, `ORDER BY created_at DESC, id DESC`, and keep `total` and `limit`.
   - **Engagements and deliverables** compute `totals` over every row. A deliverable's state and median come from its versions, which were already read unbounded, and the list is a page of those rows. Before this, LIMIT 400 hid row 401 and the totals described only the 400 kept.
6. **Pages read every page; the board reads one.**
   - `readEveryPage` (`pages/advisor/practice/everyPage.js`) follows `next_offset` in order. It stops at a bound (25 pages), on a page that returns nothing, or on one that does not move forward.
   - Opportunities, Sessions, Engagements and Delivery use it, so their rates, lanes and counts cover every row.
   - A walk that stopped short says "Showing the N most recent of M. The figures above are over all M."
   - The `/practice` board keeps its bounded preview page and counts from `page.total` (`wholeCount`), never from the rows it drew.
7. **Owner slots are unchanged.** `/me/slots` keeps its 500-row window, which already answers `truncated` and shows a notice. The grid is a window of days, not a list to page.
