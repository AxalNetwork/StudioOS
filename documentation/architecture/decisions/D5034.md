- **Migration:** none for the frontend-only parts; see Backend required.

### What the canvas decides

- **Four workspaces:** Queue, Branches, Delivery, Policy.
- **Queue, all clear** is the production state: one “All clear” block covering escalations and HQ tickets, with subsidiaries “No support sources connected” on one line. No grid of zero cards.
- **Queue, active:** Action required (Past SLA, Due soon, Open, Oldest); escalations banded Past SLA / Due soon / Open with kind filters; HQ-hosted tickets oldest risk first; subsidiaries folded to one line linking to Branches. Due soon follows the backend window, `min(24h, limit/2)`.
- **Decisions** open in a sheet over the queue (scroll kept): Record answer or Decline. A decline needs a reason; escalations are never withdrawn. Outcomes: saving, recorded and delivered, recorded but not delivered (Resend), refused, outcome unknown. Recorded at HQ and delivered to the branch are never merged.
- **Branches:** per-subsidiary cards and a queues-by-subsidiary matrix. An unavailable branch reads Unreadable, never zero; its escalations are held at HQ and stay visible.
- **Delivery:** decisions recorded at HQ and their delivery to each branch, with failed deliveries and Resend.
- **Policy:** ticket SLA P1 24h · P2 48h · P3 96h (1–720 whole hours), filing priority urgent→P1, high→P2, medium and low→P3, and escalation deadlines by kind. “SLA policy is not distributed to subsidiaries” stays on the page until #5032.
- **Incomplete totals** read “18+” with their reason. Phone boards at 390; the policy is read-only on phone.

### Built, part 1 (2026-11-08): the four workspaces and the read views

Frontend only, on `main` after #5099 (#5030 to #5033). No Worker change and no migration: every read this slice draws was already served by `GET /api/admin/hq-support` and `/local`. The pieces are in `frontend/src/pages/hq/support/` (`supportWorkspaceModel.js`, pure; `SupportWorkspaces.jsx`), composed by `HqSupportPage.jsx`, which keeps every component it exported before.

**What was built.**

- **Workspaces.** Queue (the default), Branches, Delivery and Policy are `?tab=` on `/admin/hq-support`. They are a `nav` of links with `aria-current="page"`, never ARIA tabs, so Back and Forward move between them, and an unknown `tab` is the Queue. A workspace carries a mark when it holds work: "N past SLA", "N not delivered", "Not on every branch".
- **Header.** HQ · Support, Support and "Manage support workload across Axal VC." come first. The scope follows in words: "Platform-wide support · HQ + all available subsidiaries", the sources ("2 of 3 subsidiary sources responding", or none connected, asking, or unreadable), "Updated HH:MM UTC" and Refresh. The "All subsidiaries" bar is gone. Refresh is quiet: the last read stays drawn, and the line reads "Refreshing… showing the HH:MM UTC read" in a polite live region. Review feature requests is a header action beside Ask.
- **Queue, all clear (SU01a).** One block: All clear, the measured time, "Escalations 0 open", "HQ tickets 0 open" and the subsidiaries in one line. It shows no filters, no SLA breakdowns, no matrix, no mirror and no policy.
- **Queue, active (SU01b).** Action required shows Past SLA, Due soon, Open (escalations and HQ-hosted tickets) and Oldest.
  - **HQ escalations** are full width, in Past SLA, Due soon and Open bands. Each row shows `branch · subject` (and the kind when the subject is not the kind), the request's first line or "No detail recorded", "Raised by" or Not recorded, the SLA as a word with a shape ("Past SLA · 38h"), and the deadline ("72h SLA · late by 2h", from `due_at` against the read's own time). Review is named for its row.
  - **The kind filter** shows All and the kinds with something open; every other kind waits behind More filters. The counts are #5030's whole-board measurement.
  - **HQ-hosted tickets** are one table, oldest risk first, with Ticket, Requester, Context (customer ticket on HQ or subsidiary admin ticket), Priority with its tier, Age and SLA with time left or late. On a phone the table is a stacked list. The footing that ties the total to HQ Home is behind "How this total is calculated".
  - **The subsidiaries** are one line linking to Branches.
- **Branches (SU04a, SU04b).** Each subsidiary is a card: Connected with Open tickets, Past SLA, Oldest and the host/axal.vc split, or Unavailable with the branch's reason, "Its branch-hosted tickets are not counted; its escalations are held at HQ and still shown" and Retry. The matrix is drawn only when a licence row sits beside HQ's. Each branch's own tickets follow. With no subsidiary, the canvas's empty state is shown.
- **Delivery (SU05a, SU05b).** Decisions recorded on this page are listed, the undelivered first, each with Send again (#5033's states). The GitHub mirror is one line while quiet or healthy ("No ticket activity in the last 24h"), and the full strip when anything failed, no token is set, or the read failed.
- **Policy (SU06).** A banner says whether HQ's ticket SLA is on every subsidiary, then the existing policy panel follows (ticket SLA, version and branch coverage, escalation deadlines, filing-priority mapping, the fixed taxonomy). On a phone the policy reads only; Edit hours is drawn from `md` up, with a line saying so.
- **Text.** Section headings are 16 px, body text 13–14 px, and new metadata at least 11.5 px. SLA badges reuse Security v2's glyphs (`security/StatusBadge.jsx`'s `StateShape`), so no state is colour alone.

**Departures from the canvas.**

- **The decision sheet is not built.** Review opens the existing D205 decision panel under its row, so the queue keeps its place and answering, declining and the outcome states (#5033) work as before. The sheet over the queue (SU02, SU09) and its outcome board (SU03) are part 2.
- **Policy states what #5032 measures.** The canvas keeps "SLA policy is not distributed to subsidiaries" until #5032. #5032 shipped with #5099, so the banner reads the measured distribution instead:
  - no subsidiary deployed (nothing to reach);
  - applied on all N;
  - "SLA policy is not on every subsidiary — applied on X of Y", each branch named in the panel;
  - not known yet, while branches are asked or could not be read. This is never "complete".
- **Delivery lists this session only.** HQ keeps no delivery history to read back (#5035), so the list says it covers decisions recorded on this page, and the Delivery mark counts only those.
- **All clear needs every source.** The canvas draws All clear with the subsidiaries line. This build calls it All clear only when HQ's parts are measured at zero AND every subsidiary answered with nothing open (or none is connected). HQ at zero while a branch is unread, still being asked, or has tickets reads "Nothing waiting at HQ", with the subsidiaries line saying what is not known.
- **A floor reads "N+".** When a part is unread or cut, Action required adds what was listed and reads "N+" with each reason under it (SU11's "18+"). Oldest is the oldest that was read.
- **The matrix keeps H22's names.** "Tenant × queue", "HQ-held" and "About Admin" stay, because `hq_support_h22_d204` pins them to the H22 canvas. The operator labels ("Queues by subsidiary", "Customer tickets on HQ", "Subsidiary admin tickets") belong to the same change that retires that pin.
- **Not built:** the stale-branch state (no per-branch read time is served), the offline state, Eadwyn's support prompts with model and usage folded away (an AIRail change), and the phone decision sheet.
- **HQ-hosted tickets** list the ten most at risk, then say how many are listed.

### Built, part 2 (2026-11-09): the decision sheet

Frontend only. The PATCH and resend routes, what they answer, and the answer and decline semantics are unchanged. The sheet is `frontend/src/pages/hq/support/DecisionSheet.jsx`, drawn by `EscalationDecision` with `variant="sheet"`, so one component still owns the rail's record (D775) and there is still one write. The licence page's requests panel keeps the inline panel.

**What was built (SU02, SU03, SU09).**

- **Opening.** Review on a queue row opens the shared `Sheet` (D1317) over the queue, which keeps its place. The sheet is 560 px beside the rail on desktop and a bottom sheet on a phone. It is `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal`, named by its title. Focus moves to the first outcome, Tab stays inside, and Escape or × closes it. Review is named for its row ("Review fr · Seat increase") and carries `aria-haspopup="dialog"`.
- **Header.** "Escalation · <subsidiary>", the kind (and subject when it differs), the SLA as a word and shape ("Past SLA · 38h"), and "72h SLA · late by 2h · raised Fri 6 Nov 00:30 UTC". The subsidiary's name comes from the reads (branch hosts, then the matrix), or the code when neither names it.
- **Facts.** Subsidiary, Raised by, Subject, About (the record and whether the request localises or changes it), and Request. Each missing fact reads Not recorded or No detail recorded.
- **Decision.** Record answer ("HQ agrees or replies") or Decline ("HQ refuses, with a reason"), as a radio group. Escalations are never withdrawn, and the footer says so.
  - The page's own sentence says what the branch will see.
  - The text is required, up to 4,000 characters, and labelled "Answer · required · stored at HQ and sent to <subsidiary>" (or "Reason for declining").
  - An empty submit is refused in words tied to the field: `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`, `role="alert"`, "A decline needs its reason.". Nothing is sent.
- **Recording.** The form is read-only, and the button reads "Recording at HQ…" with `aria-busy`.
- **Recorded.** The sheet stays open on two facts that stay two:
  - "Answered · Recorded at HQ · 14:03 UTC";
  - "<subsidiary> received it", "Not delivered to <subsidiary> · <reason>" with Send again, or "Whether it reached <subsidiary> was not reported".
  - Send again is the D5033 resend: "Sending…" while in flight, the delivery line updated in place, and a failure or unknown said as an alert.
  - "Back to the queue" closes the sheet and moves focus to the next row's Review. Escape or Cancel returns focus to the row that opened it.
- **Outcome unknown** (no status or a 5xx): "The decision may or may not be recorded at HQ. Re-read the queue before trying again: a second answer replaces the first." It never says nothing changed. Re-read the queue closes the sheet and reads quietly.
- **No longer on the board** (404): "Nothing was recorded here. It was answered elsewhere or removed.", with Back to the queue.
- **Refused** (any other 4xx): the server's sentence as an alert on the form, which stays filled in.
- **The session list** ("Recorded this session" on the Queue, and Delivery) still records every decision, so a delivery failure stays visible after the sheet closes.

**Departures from the canvas.**

- **Focus on open** lands on the first outcome, not the title. The shared `Sheet` focuses a control, and the title is not one.
- **"Re-read escalation"** is "Re-read the queue". There is no single-escalation read, and the quiet queue read is what shows whether the decision landed.
- **The outcome defaults to Record answer**, as drawn. The text is still required, so nothing is sent by default.
- **Recent decisions with durable history** (SU03's last card) still waits on #5035.

### Built, part 3 (2026-11-09): Eadwyn's steps, operator names, stale and offline

Frontend only, stacked on part 2.

**What was built.**

- **Eadwyn on Support.** The rail gets the page's own next steps through D4873's `steps`, drawn in place of the model list and prices. The block is titled "For Support", and its steps are:
  - "N past SLA", "N due soon" (a floor keeps its "+"), "N decisions recorded at HQ but not delivered";
  - "HQ's ticket SLA is applied on X of Y subsidiaries";
  - "Not read: <subsidiary>".

  Each step links to its workspace (`/admin/hq-support?tab=…`), and the block ends "Eadwyn reads this page and changes nothing. You record every decision." Nothing in it acts. With nothing to do, the block reads "Nothing is waiting at HQ, and every source answered." only when that is measured, and otherwise "Nothing on this page needs a step from you.". A failed read reads "unknown, not none".
- **Queues by subsidiary.** H22's "Tenant × queue" takes the v2 canvas's names (SU04a): Subsidiary, Escalations, Customer tickets on HQ, Subsidiary admin tickets. The table has a caption and `th scope`, and its numbered reasons sit behind "What each cell means". The cells and their words (Does not apply, On the branch, Unreadable, Not counted) are unchanged. `hq_support_h22_d204` now pins the columns to the v2 canvas file instead of H22's.
- **Stale subsidiary figures (SU11 "Stale branch").** `mergeSupport` now returns which read the branch parts came from. When a quiet refresh brings HQ's read back before the branches answer, or the branch re-read fails, the subsidiary figures are older than HQ's. Branches and the subsidiaries line then say "Subsidiary figures are from the 08:30 UTC read; the latest subsidiary read did not complete." with Retry, or "…asking each subsidiary again…" while the re-read is in flight. Each branch is read live, so this is the only way figures can go stale; no per-branch age is invented.
- **Offline (SU11).** The page follows the browser's `online` and `offline` events.
  - Offline, a banner says "You're offline. Showing the 14:30 UTC read. Decisions can't be recorded until you reconnect." and Refresh is disabled.
  - In the decision sheet the submit is disabled, a line says the decision can't be recorded until reconnecting, and the typed text stays. The write itself refuses to send while offline.
  - Back online, the same sheet submits.

**Departures from the canvas.**

- **Eadwyn's four free-text prompts** ("Summarize current support workload", "Draft a response to this escalation", …) are not built. `WorkerRail` takes no prompts. The shared mechanism that exists (D4873 `steps`) gives Support's facts instead, and still takes the model list and prices off the page. A draft-reply prompt needs a rail change of its own.
- **The stale state is dated by read, not by branch.** Branch reads are live per request, so there is no per-branch "last read 2h ago" to show.

### Built, part 4 (2026-11-09): Eadwyn's Support prompts

Frontend only. This is a small shared change, stacked on part 3, that answers part 3's open departure.

**What was built (SU13).**

- `WorkerRail` takes an optional `prompts` list and hands it to the Ask panel. `AskPanel` draws those prompts first among its chips (`promptOptions` in `ui/askPanelModel.js`), keyed `prompt:<n>` so they never collide with an option or a run chip. They are never run chips: pressing one fills the composer with its words, and the member reads, edits and sends. The six-chip cap still applies.
- Support passes the canvas's four prompts: "Summarize current support workload", "Explain this SLA status", "Draft a response to this escalation" and "Explain why data is unavailable". With the decision sheet open, the escalation is the rail's record (D775), so "Draft a response to this escalation" is asked about that escalation.
- Eadwyn drafts and explains; it never answers, declines, resends or changes policy. A prompt sends nothing, and every decision is recorded by the member in the sheet.
- A page that passes no prompts is unchanged: no new chip row, and the same options in the same order.

**Why not the rail-option registry.** `lib/railOptions/*` lists one row per control that runs a model, and a parity test holds it to the code. A prompt runs nothing until the member sends it, so it is the page's own words, passed as a prop. The server's rail matrix is untouched.

**Departures.** The canvas draws the prompts in the open rail itself. They are drawn in the Ask panel, which the rail and the header's Ask open, because that is where a prompt can fill a composer. The rail's own block shows Support's next steps (part 3).

**Tests.** `frontend/test/hq_support_prompts_d5034.test.mjs` adds 4 tests:

- the rail and Support pass them.

Six mutations were each caught. The Ask panel's existing tests and the Support tests pass: 158 of 158.
