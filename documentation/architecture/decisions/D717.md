## D717 — The "Legal Documents · Templates" screens: each template's kind and Axal party stored on its row, one-template publication, page-1 thumbnails, the entity and money map, the bank-account registry and the phone screens

Issue: #1782. S1 decided every open point on 2026-10-17, under the owner's standing rule. The canvas is "Legal Documents · Templates" (boards R1, R2 and L0 to L12); this decision covers L8's thumbnails and L9 to L12. The paper boards (L0 to L7) belong to #1776 and #1781.

The work ships in parts, each `Part of #1782` until PR 3 closes it. **PR 1a**, the Worker steps that need neither #1776 nor #1773, is the first. This file grows with each part; each section says which part shipped it.

### Why

- **Kind and Axal party were never stored.** Every render derived them from the slug (`classifyDocument` and `axalEntityKeyForDoc`, `services/legalDocFormat.ts`), so a slug that routed to the wrong party could only be fixed by a code change.
- **Publish was all or nothing, and tied to the branch push.** `publishContractTemplates` snapshots every row with a body in one batch, and the same route fans the catalogue out to every branch. The canvas publishes one template at a time and pushes separately.
- **Five documents go out under a key that is not their catalog row** (decision 6), and no screen showed their text.

### What was decided

1. **Scope.** L8's thumbnails and L9 to L12. Where the final export differs from the issue, the export wins on layout and copy; the issue wins on data, routes and who may act.
2. **Kind and Axal party live on the catalog row** (PR 1a).
   - Migration 479 adds `doc_kind` and `axal_party` to `legal_templates`, `legal_template_versions`, `legal_template_publications` and `esign_envelopes`. Both are nullable, and NULL means "from the slug", which is the behaviour before this decision. The runtime bootstraps (`ensureLegalTemplatesSchema`, `esign.ts`'s schema list) add the same columns, and the `superset:esign_envelopes` sentence of `scripts/schema-pair-drift-baseline.json` names them.
   - **Kinds:** every `DocumentKind` on `main` (`TEMPLATE_KINDS`, with #1781's `notice` and `booklet`). A row may store only the five the paper draws from a body (`STORABLE_KINDS`); storing `notice` or `booklet` is `kind_not_drawn`.
   - **#1781's notice and booklet slugs take no stored override.** Their sender comes from #1781's notice types. A write that sets either column on a slug whose `classifyDocument` is `notice` or `booklet` answers 409 `format_fixed`; a write that sends neither, or resets both to `null`, goes through. The reads mark them `format_fixed: true`.
   - **Parties:** `management`, `holdings`, `gp`, `fund` and `none`: five, not the canvas's four, because D705 made the GP and the Fund different parties.
   - **Pairing:** `unilateral`, `corporate` and `policy` take `none`, and every other kind takes an entity. The pair is checked on the effective values, stored or derived (`kind_party_mismatch`), so a party alone is checked against the slug's kind.
   - **A change is an edit.** It snapshots the prior row, both columns included, and bumps the version. An absent key keeps the stored value, and `null` returns it to the slug.
   - **A publication copies both columns**, the library publish included.
   - **A send records the pair it printed on the envelope**: the contract send records its publication's effective pair, and every other caller of `createAndSendEnvelope` records the active row's (`templateFormatFor`). Signing and the DocuSign source read the envelope's values, so a template edited after a send never changes what that envelope prints. An envelope with NULL columns, sent before migration 479, renders from the slug as before.
3. **The catalog (L9).** Layer sections in today's order with their counts. Each card shows the page-1 thumbnail, the title, the effective kind and party (marked "from the slug" when derived), the version, the "N uses" chip and its state: "Stub", "Has a body", "Published v[N]" and "Changes not published". The state filter offers all, has a body, stub, unpublished and published. The canvas's "New draft (counsel review)" state is **dropped**: no record holds it, and `LEGAL_REVIEW.md` is prose.
4. **Stubs.** A stub reads "No body in the store yet." The 31 catalog entries created with no body anywhere add "Stub — counsel to draft.": D706's 28 (migration 468), D707's two platform licences (471) and D716's `securities_legend` (478). `COUNSEL_DRAFT_SLUGS` lists them, and `template_format_d717.test.ts` reads them out of those migrations. Add body imports no file.
5. **Thumbnails are images the Worker draws once per version** (PR 1b), through Browser Run's screenshot, stored in `FILES` under a key of the slug's hash, the version and the paper's hash. A thumbnail is **the one stored preview**: #1776 stores none. About 100 cards each holding a live paper frame would parse the inlined faces 100 times; an image costs one render per version.
6. **"Not in the catalog" (L8).** Five bundled keys of `LEGAL_TEMPLATE_CATALOG` send text that is not the store body of the row they count under:

   | Key | Counted under |
   | --- | --- |
   | `investor_nda_v1` | `investor_nda_axal` |
   | `mentor_nda_v1` | `mentor_nda_axal` |
   | `mentor_disclaimer_v1` | `mentor_engagement_disclaimer` |
   | `partner_msa_v1` | `partner_services` |
   | `nda_3way_founder_investor_axal_v1` | `nda_3way_founder_investor_axal` |

   `GET /templates/outside` lists them, built from the catalog where `key` differs from `doc_type`, with the title of the row each counts under (PR 1a; their thumbnails come with PR 1b).
7. **The Fund I documents panel** (PR 2), kept in `frontend/src/lib/fundIDocuments.js`, in signing order. "Ready" means a recorded publication.
8. **The editor** (PR 2): highlighted `{{tokens}}`, who fills each field, scroll sync, a "Pages" view of the real PDF, and two selects with "From the slug (…)" first. The PDF is drawn with `pdfjs-dist`, pinned to an exact version of 4.10.38 or later: CVE-2024-4367 (arbitrary script through a crafted font) is fixed from 4.2.67. It is loaded only when the view opens, with `isEvalSupported: false`, and draws to a canvas only.
9. **Version compare** (PR 2), word by word in a pure `textDiff.js`; above 20,000 words a side, line by line.
10. **Publish one template; push separately** (PR 1a's routes).
    - `POST /published-templates/:slug/publish` `{ version }` snapshots that one row with both columns and writes `contract_template_published`, in one batch. The snapshot's SELECT is guarded on the version inside the batch, so an edit after the check publishes nothing. A stale version answers 409 `published_version_changed`, and a stub or empty body 409 `template_has_no_body`.
    - `POST /published-templates/:slug/withdraw` deletes that publication and writes `contract_template_withdrawn`; with none, 404 `not_published`.
    - `POST /published-templates/push` sends the recorded publications to every branch as they stand, in the payload shape of the library publish, and writes no publication. Its rows carry `services/branches.ts`'s states.
    - All three take `requireHqAuthoring`, so a branch Worker refuses them.
    - **The library-wide `POST /published-templates/publish` keeps working with no screen caller.** It still snapshots every row with a body and pushes in the same call, and now copies both format columns.
11. **The entity and money map (L10)**, one model and two drawings (PR 1c, after #1773's resolver, and #1776 for the paper page).
12. **The registry (L11)** is #1773's console, restyled in place (PR 3).
13. **Phone (L12)** (PR 2 and PR 3).

### PR 1a: what shipped

- `cloudflare-worker/sql/migrations/479_template_kind_party.sql`: the eight ALTERs, no seed, no `BEGIN`/`COMMIT`.
- `services/templateFormat.ts`: `TEMPLATE_KINDS`, `STORABLE_KINDS`, `TEMPLATE_PARTIES`, `PARTYLESS_KINDS`, `COUNSEL_DRAFT_SLUGS`, `STRUCTURE_MAP_SLUGS`, `derivedFormat`, `formatFixed`, `effectiveFormat`, `validateFormat`, `templateFormatFor`, `renderFormat`, `formatView` and `formatOptions`. It imports `legalDocFormat.ts` and does not edit it.
- The store, the store routes' format fields (`doc_kind`, `axal_party`, `kind`, `party`, `format_source`, `format_fixed`, and `format_options` on the single read), the three publication routes, `/templates/outside`, `preview.pdf` and the envelope preview printing the effective pair, and the envelope's recorded pair through send, signing and DocuSign.
- **The DocuSign source now passes `documentType`.** It used to classify the document by its title; it now renders the envelope's recorded kind and party, as signing does.

### Consequences

- An admin who sees the wrong party fixes it on the row, and the signed copy names the party the sender saw.
- A new envelope always stores its pair, so a later change to the slug rules does not change what it prints.
- A D1 without migration 479 cannot publish: the snapshot names the new columns. The deploy applies migrations before the Worker ships (CLAUDE.md fact 1).
