## D717 — The "Legal Documents · Templates" screens: each template's kind and Axal party stored on its row, one-template publication, page-1 thumbnails, the entity and money map, the bank-account registry and the phone screens

Issue: #1782. S1 decided every open point on 2026-10-17, under the owner's standing rule. The canvas is "Legal Documents · Templates" (boards R1, R2 and L0 to L12); this decision covers L8's thumbnails and L9 to L12. The paper boards (L0 to L7) belong to #1776 and #1781.

The work ships in parts, each `Part of #1782` until PR 3 closes it. **PR 1a**, the Worker steps that need neither #1776 nor #1773, is the first. This file grows with each part; each section says which part shipped it.

### Why

- **Kind and Axal party were never stored.** Every render derived them from the slug (`classifyDocument` and `axalEntityKeyForDoc`, `services/legalDocFormat.ts`), so a slug that routed to the wrong party could only be fixed by a code change.
- **Publish was all or nothing, and tied to the branch push.** `publishContractTemplates` snapshots every row with a body in one batch, and the same route fans the catalogue out to every branch. The canvas publishes one template at a time and pushes separately.
- **Five documents go out under a key that is not their catalog row** (decision 6), and no screen showed their text.

### What was decided

1. **Scope.** L8's thumbnails and L9 to L12. Where the final export differs from the issue, the export wins on layout and copy; the issue wins on data, routes and who may act.
2. **Kind and Axal party live on the catalog row** (PR 1a).
   - Migration 479 adds `doc_kind` and `axal_party` to `legal_templates`, `legal_template_versions`, `legal_template_publications` and `esign_envelopes`. Both are nullable, and NULL means "from the slug", which is the behaviour before this decision. The runtime bootstraps (`ensureLegalTemplatesSchema`, `esign.ts`'s schema list) add the same columns, and the `superset:esign_envelopes` sentence of `scripts/schema-pair-drift-baseline.json` names them.
   - **Kinds:** every `DocumentKind` on `main` (`TEMPLATE_KINDS`, with #1781's `notice` and `booklet`). A row may store only the five the paper draws from a body (`STORABLE_KINDS`); storing `notice` or `booklet` is `kind_not_drawn`.
   - **#1781's notice and booklet slugs take no stored override.** Their sender comes from #1781's notice types. A write that sets either column on a slug whose `classifyDocument` is `notice` or `booklet` answers 409 `format_fixed`; a write that sends neither, or resets both to `null`, goes through. The reads mark them `format_fixed: true`.
   - **Parties:** `management`, `holdings`, `gp`, `fund` and `none`: five, not the canvas's four, because D705 made the GP and the Fund different parties.
   - **Pairing:** `unilateral`, `corporate` and `policy` take `none`, and every other kind takes an entity. The pair is checked on the effective values, stored or derived (`kind_party_mismatch`), so a party alone is checked against the slug's kind.
   - **A change is an edit.** It snapshots the prior row, both columns included, and bumps the version. An absent key keeps the stored value, and `null` returns it to the slug.
   - **A publication copies both columns**, the library publish included.
   - **A send records the pair it printed on the envelope**: the contract send records its publication's effective pair, and every other caller of `createAndSendEnvelope` records the active row's (`templateFormatFor`). Signing and the DocuSign source read the envelope's values, so a template edited after a send never changes what that envelope prints. An envelope with NULL columns, sent before migration 479, renders from the slug as before.
