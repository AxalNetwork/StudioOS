### 1. What the sheet reads now

| Route | For |
|---|---|
| `POST /api/billing/checkout/quote` | On open, and again for a new interval or code: the subtotal, discount, VAT and due today, or for a change its direction, proration and date |
| `POST /api/billing/checkout/start` | On Pay: a client secret to confirm, `free`, `paid`, or `scheduled` |
| `GET /api/billing/checkout/status?ref=` | After Pay, polled every two seconds: `activating`, `active`, `failed` or `scheduled` |
| `GET /api/payments/methods` | "Pay with": the saved cards |

**Every figure is one the Worker sent.** A total the quote did not send shows its reason (`total_reason`, `vat.reason`), and the button reads "Pay", never "Pay $0.00". `moneyOf(null)` is null, never `$0.00`.

**`AxalCheckout` is no longer used for a plan.** The sheet neither imports nor mounts it. Token packs keep it until #2452.

### 2. The boards

- **PAYX2 and PAYX3: a new plan.**
  - The totals are the quote's rows, with "Pay [$total]" as the one primary button.
  - The card form (`PlanCardForm`) is a Payment Element on a deferred intent (`mode: 'subscription'`), with Apple Pay and Google Pay first where the device offers them. Nothing is made in Stripe until Pay.
  - On Pay, the form is submitted, then `start` makes the subscription, and its client secret is confirmed in place.
  - **"Pay with" lists the saved cards.** The first is the default, and "A new card" shows the form. A saved card asks for the authenticator code (D1051 §3); the request helper's step-up prompt opens on the Worker's 403, and the press retries.
  - **An investor plan reads no saved cards.** `/payments/methods` lists the general customer's cards, but an investor plan is paid on the member's own investor customer (D892 §6), where those cards would be refused.
- **PAYX4: a change in place.**
  - An upgrade shows the plan, its prorated difference, VAT and due today, with "Confirm change · [$]". Its invoice is confirmed in place when the bank asks.
  - A downgrade shows when it applies, "Nothing is charged today", and the storage note: the new plan's storage applies from that day, nothing is deleted, and new uploads wait while over it.
  - Both say "Plan changes need a fresh code from your authenticator app." The code itself is asked by the global step-up prompt on the Worker's 403, not by a field in the sheet.
  - **A change takes no code**, so the promo field is not drawn. Until D1053, a change sent the member to Billing.
- **PAYX5: the promo field.** Its states:
  - applying;
  - valid, with Remove;
  - covers the first payment: "Your code covers the first payment. Your card is saved for renewals." Pay reads "Start my plan", and no card form is drawn;
  - invalid, in the canvas's words ("That code isn't valid.", "This code has expired.", …);
  - not for this plan: "This code doesn't apply to [plan]."

  A reason the map does not know reads "That code can't be applied to this purchase.", and a raw reason is never shown.
- **PAYX6: Processing.** The form locks, Pay reads "Processing…", and the sheet says "Don't close this window." 3-D Secure opens in the bank's own window.
- **PAYX7 and PAYX8: after Pay.**
  - **Activating** polls the status route, and after a minute says the plan is still being switched on.
  - **Accepted** shows "What you get now" from the status result, and the plan before and after when both were recorded. A figure not recorded says so, with its reason.
  - **Scheduled** (a downgrade) gives its date.
  - **Failed** says the member was not charged, and offers Try again with a new nonce. Each press of Pay sends one nonce; a retry after a decline reuses it, so one form never makes two subscriptions (D1051 §3).
  - **A status read that fails** says "That is not the same as a failed payment." and the poll goes on.
- **PAYX9: the decline map** (`declineFamily`). Stripe's `code` and `decline_code` become one of five families:
  - Card declined;
  - Insufficient funds;
  - Expired card;
  - Authentication failed;
  - Payment didn't go through.

  Fraud codes and any code not listed read "Card declined". A raw code is never shown, and every line says "You were not charged." The form stays filled in, with Try again and "Use another method".
- **PAYX17: loading.** The sheet's frame draws at once, with skeletons where the totals and the payment go. No Pay button shows until the quote answers.
- **PAYX18: refusals** (`refusalView`). Each says whether money moved, and none shows a Pay button or a price it does not have:
  - Payments aren't set up here;
  - the price couldn't be read ("That doesn't mean it's free."), with Try again;
  - you already pay for a plan of this kind, with Open Billing;
  - Checkout couldn't start, with the Worker's own sentence when it says what happened to the money.

  A card form that fails to load (Stripe.js blocked, or offline) says so and that nothing was charged.
- **PAYX19: sign in first, the plan kept.**
  - A visitor's sheet (no user and no role) reads nothing and links to `/login?next=/pricing?checkout=<plan key>&interval=…`.
  - Back on Pricing, the sheet opens on that plan and interval once. The request then leaves the address, so Back does not reopen it.
  - Another profile's plan is still told why (D1048).

### 3. Choices for the reviewer

- **The deferred intent's amount** is the quote's `total_due_today`, else the plan's price, and at least 50 cents, because Stripe needs one above zero. The charge itself is the Worker's subscription invoice. Whether Stripe accepts a different amount at confirmation, for a wallet, is listed in §5.
- **`bridge`** is a prop with the Stripe side as its default: the card form, a saved-card confirm and an intent confirm. The tests stand in for it; nothing in the sheet imports Stripe at load.
- **`pollMs`** is a prop defaulting to `POLL_MS` (2 seconds), so a test can follow a poll to its end.

**Mutation checks: every non-equivalent mutant is caught.** They were run against the suite and cover the model's rules, the sheet's order (submit, start, confirm, status), the nonce, saved cards, the free first payment, the polling, the change box and step-up line, and Pricing's reopen and address.

- the saved card only for a new plan, because a change never selects one and the Worker ignores it there;
- Pricing's reopen only when signed in, because a visitor's cards never open the sheet.
