### 1. Who can get `r_1st_connections` (Connections API)

**VERIFIED — partner-approved only, not self-serve (A).**
- "The use of this API is restricted to those developers approved by LinkedIn and subject to applicable data restrictions in their agreements."
- It returns only the 1st-degree connections of the member who granted access. Second-degree connections are "not available from LinkedIn", and "you cannot browse connections".
- Results are subject to each member's privacy settings.

### 2. DMA Member Data Portability (3rd Party), `r_dma_portability_3rd_party`

**Regions — VERIFIED: EU/EEA and Switzerland members only (B, D).**
- Page B: "Only LinkedIn users from the European Economic Area are allowed to consent to share their LinkedIn data with 3rd party developer applications."
- Page D: "only members located in the EU/EEA or Switzerland (as determined by the location provided by the member in their LinkedIn profile)". Other members get an error when they try to consent.

**Whether it includes connections — VERIFIED: yes (C).** The snapshot domain `CONNECTIONS` is "Name, position, company, and connection date of 1st degree connections of the member".

**Other data — VERIFIED (B).** After consent, LinkedIn archives the member's activity as changelog events, which can only be queried for the past 28 days. The Snapshot API returns the member's data at the time of the call.

**What access requires — VERIFIED (B).**
- an app on the LinkedIn Developers Platform, which needs a LinkedIn Company Page;
- the Company Page association verified by that Page's super admin;
- an access request from the app's Products tab, reviewed by LinkedIn;
- business verification:
  - a verified business email address (personal addresses fail);
  - the organization's legal name, registered address, website and privacy policy.

### 3. The brief's questions (#3564), for each route

LinkedIn's API Terms of Use (E) govern both routes. Any product-specific agreement LinkedIn signs with an approved developer may add to them; those agreements are not public, so anything they might change is marked UNVERIFIED.

**3a. Storage in D1, and for how long — VERIFIED as a default prohibition (E §4.1–4.3).**
- "You may not store any Content, except in the specific circumstances outlined."
  - Where storage is permitted, Content must be stored so it can be identified, segregated and selectively deleted.
  - It may be kept "only for the duration necessary" to provide the service.
  - It must not sit "in a data repository that would enable any third party access (other than the customer or Member to which it relates)".
- Tokens and member IDs may be stored (§4.2).
- §4.3 lets stored Profile Data be kept with consent, but it "excludes data such as a Member's network". So connections fall under §4.1's prohibition unless an Additional Terms agreement permits them.
- Whether the Connections API partner agreement or the DMA product permits storing connections: **UNVERIFIED** (not public).

**3b. Matching across users — VERIFIED as prohibited by default (E §3).** The Terms forbid allowing "any third party, including other Members, to see information obtained from another Member's network". They also forbid combining Content "with any other LinkedIn content" and commingling Content "with third party data". Matching one member's connections against another member's data would breach all three unless an Additional Terms agreement says otherwise.

**3c. Showing relationship paths to teammates — VERIFIED as prohibited by default (E §3).** This is the same clause: teammates are "other Members" seeing information "obtained from another Member's network". The team relationship layer must not show API-sourced connections to anyone but the member they came from, so `may_share_with_team` stays false for both API sources. Whether a partner agreement could permit it: **UNVERIFIED**.

**3d. Automatic sync — partly VERIFIED.**
- §4.3 lets stored Profile Data be refreshed "only … when the Member is actually using your Application and not on an automated schedule". A scheduled background sync of profile data is therefore not allowed.
- Changelog events can only be queried for the past 28 days (B). Our inference, not LinkedIn's words: a DMA sync that misses 28 days loses events.
- How often connections may be re-fetched: **UNVERIFIED**.

**3e. Deletion and revocation duties — VERIFIED (E §4.4–4.5).**
- Delete "all Content collected through the APIs on behalf of a User, including the Member Token and the OAuth Access Token" when the user asks, or when they close their account.
- Delete all stored LinkedIn data if LinkedIn notifies a breach, or if the application stops using the Content.
- The duty does not cover data the user "separately entered or uploaded", such as the CSV import (#3566).

### What this means if the adapters are reopened

Both routes are LinkedIn-gated:
- **Connections API:** partner approval.
- **DMA:** a verified Company Page, business verification, a review, and EEA/Swiss members only.

Under the default API Terms, neither may store connections or show them to teammates. Before any adapter is built, the approved agreement must say otherwise in writing, and its terms recorded here.

Until then:
- the CSV export the member uploads stays the only route that can power the team relationship layer. It is the member's own upload, outside §4.4's scope;
- the two switches named in #3569 (`LINKEDIN_CONNECTIONS_API`, `LINKEDIN_DMA`) are not added.
