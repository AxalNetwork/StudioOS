### What is built

**The seat decision (WLM-07, group side).** **See seats** on a group suggestion opens the session under its card. The panel shows:
- when it runs, in the member's zone;
- the facilitator;
- the format, as group size and language;
- the seats, as a count: "3 of 8 open". It never names anyone.
- **Free**;
- the Worker's own release rule;
- the Worker's two sentences: what the facilitator learns, and what other members learn.

The member then chooses a calendar entry: a discreet title (the default), the session title, or no entry. **Reserve a seat** sends that choice and nothing else.

**The outcomes (WLM-07B, WLM-08).**
- **Reserved:** "Your seat is reserved… Nothing was charged."
- **The last seat was taken first** (409 `session_full`): "Someone reserved the last seat first. Nothing was reserved for you, and nothing was charged." The panel re-reads and shows Full.
- **Each other state says why seats can't be reserved:** Full (no wait-list yet), cancelled by the facilitator with the reason, started, and facilitator unavailable.

**My group seats.** This section sits on the home beside My bookings and lists the member's own seats:
- **Seat reserved**, with **Release seat**, which asks first and is offered only before the cut-off.
- **Seat released**.
- **Cancelled by the facilitator**, with the reason.
- **Held**, once the session has started.

A release refused after the cut-off (409 `cancellation_window_closed`) says so and that the seat is still reserved. A failed read says it couldn't be read, never that there are no seats.
