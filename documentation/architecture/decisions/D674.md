## D674 — The public front page, nav and footer, built to the owner's website canvas

Issue: #1548. Part of #1546 (D673). Source: `design/canvases/integrated/Axal VC Website.dc.html`, views NAV, HOME and FOOTER.

### What was built

- **`/` (`LandingPage.jsx`)** follows HOME, in its order:
  1. the hero, with the image, the eyebrow, "Where we **build** the rails for the next generation of ventures", two CTAs and the figures;
  2. Build. Collaborate. Scale.;
  3. the four doors;
  4. how it works;
  5. the Spin-Out Lab band, with its cohort strip;
  6. the platform's tool cards;
  7. the network teaser, with articles and the calendar;
  8. the closing CTA.
- **`PublicNav.jsx`** follows NAV:
  - the mark;
  - the four doors;
  - a Network menu, with each link's icon and one line;
  - LP login and Apply →;
  - a burger menu at 1060px or less.
- **`PublicFooter.jsx`** follows FOOTER:
  - the For, Network and Build With Us columns;
  - the social row;
  - the legal row, where "Cookie preferences" opens the existing consent dialog;
  - `OWNERSHIP_NOTICE`;
  - the disclosure.
- **The styles live in `components/site/site.css`:**
  - scoped to `.ax-site` on pages and to `.ax-site-chrome` on the nav and footer;
  - every colour is explicit, because the public site is light only.

### The decisions

- **One list of links, `lib/siteLinks.js`, feeds the nav, the burger menu and the footer.**
  - It holds only routes that exist.
  - The canvas's Overview, Intelligence, Methodology and Administrators links are added by their own tasks (D677, D678, D679) when their routes land.
  - A test fails on any path `App.jsx` does not route.
- **No figure is typed.**
  - The hero draws the stats endpoint's `tools`, `users`, `companies` and `funds`, in that order. It draws only those the endpoint returns as positive numbers. D675 (#1549) adds the first three; until then the hero shows what is there.
  - The invented fallback numbers (200, 4, 1200, 38) are deleted.
  - The cohort strip reads the Lab's own cohort rule (`openCohortCopy`) and the cohort record (`useCohortPlaces`). A value it cannot read is left out.
  - The articles and events are the live lists. An empty list says so in one line.
- **The nav stays `position: fixed` and 64px tall,** not the canvas's 66px sticky bar.
  - Twenty-odd older public pages render it and pad their tops by `pt-16`.
  - A taller or sticky bar would have shifted every one of them.
- **The faces:**
  - Public Sans for text and Bricolage Grotesque for headings, as the canvas's CSS names them;
  - they are tokens in `index.css` (`--font-site`, `--font-site-display`), and `site.css` is the one stylesheet allowed to use them (`app_typography.test.mjs`);
  - the canvas's preview never loaded Bricolage and fell back to the system font. If the owner prefers that look, `--font-site-display` changes in one line.
- **What links where:**
  - the lanes link to the four door pages;
  - LP login goes to `/lp-portal`, the existing door into the LP workspace;
  - Apply and Join go to `/register`;
  - the cohort CTA goes to `/spinout-lab/apply`.
- **The social links keep the live footer's addresses,** not the canvas's. The live ones are the accounts in use.
- **The tool cards are not links yet.** The canvas points them at the Platform page, which D677 builds.

### Guards

`frontend/test/landing_home_d674.test.mjs` pins:
- the section order;
- the hero drawing only real, positive figures;
- the canvas's sample numbers and cohort staying out of source;
- every shell link being a route;
- the nav and the footer drawing the same lists;
- every image shipping in `frontend/public/site/`.

Seven mutants were killed, each by its own test:
- a zero drawn;
- a typed cohort;
- an unrouted link;
- the sections reordered;
- a footer link dropped;
- a missing image;
- the stats not fetched.
