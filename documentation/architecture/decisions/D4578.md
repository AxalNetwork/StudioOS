## D4578 — The entry journey says what each action does: an account first, membership after a review (#4578)

- **Status:** Built for #4578, 2026-11-09. Frontend wording plus one Worker email choice.
- **Builds on:** D4651 (#4651, the plan-continuity slice of /register), the D258 rule that the page keys on `email_already_registered`, not a sentence.
- **No migration. No policy change:** account creation, the review, the agreement and the role gates are exactly as they were.

### The rules, from source (current `main`)

1. **The account is created at once.** `POST /api/auth/register` (`cloudflare-worker/src/routes/auth.ts`, `/register`) inserts the user with role `exploring` before any link is clicked; the lane chosen on the page is stored only as `suggested_role`. Confirming the email (the link) verifies it and signs the person in; the role stays `exploring`. The bare magic link (`/magic/verify`) find-or-creates an `exploring` account the same way.
2. **Membership is a reviewed step, for every lane.** Only an admin turns `exploring` into a role (`routes/admin_exploring.ts`): it sends the binding (membership) agreement, and `assign-role` is refused until that agreement is signed. Nothing promotes an account automatically. Partners have a second gate, `partner_profiles.admin_status`. While waiting, the person sees `/exploring` ("Membership under review …").
3. **Grow is opened by role, not by plan.** The `/grow*` routes are guarded by role (founder, with reporting also open to advisor and partner); `/api/cycles/grow` is not behind the Studio paywall prefixes and no tier check applies to it. So a free account can open Grow **once it is a founder member**; an `exploring` account cannot.

### What changed

- **Homepage nav and the sign-in card:** "Apply →" and "Not a member yet? Apply to Axal VC →" became "Create account →" and "New to Axal VC? Create an account →", with "Free. Your membership is reviewed after you sign up." They create an account; they never submitted an application. The Spin-Out Lab's "Apply to Cohort …" is a real application and is unchanged.
- **/register:** "Free to join" (which reads as membership) became "Creating an account is free", and every lane, including the default and investor, names the review. The founder lane says the workspace (Validate, Build, Raise and Grow) opens after the review and the signed agreement. A line under the heading states the steps (`MEMBERSHIP_STEPS`). No review time is promised.
- **The new-user email is not a sign-in email.** The page used to create the account and then send the returning member's "Your Axal sign-in link / Sign in". It now asks `/magic/start` for `purpose: 'signup'`, which sends `auth_signup_link` ("Finish creating your Axal VC account", button "Finish creating my account", with the review named). An address that already has an account (409 `email_already_registered`) still gets the sign-in link, and the page says it already has an account. The page's own button, confirmation and resend text follow the same split. `/magic/start`'s answer is identical either way, so nothing is enumerated; every other caller (sign-in, recovery, the production probe) keeps `auth_magic_link`.

### Walkthrough

Traced from source for a first-time founder: homepage "Create account →" → /register (account free; review and agreement named) → "Email me a link to create my account" → the "Finish creating your Axal VC account" email → the link verifies the address and signs in → `/exploring` ("Membership under review") until an admin assigns the role after the signed agreement → the founder workspace, Grow included. Each step's wording now matches what that step does. A live run against production was not done.
