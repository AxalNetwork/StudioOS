## Authority and recovery

Compact status is read-only aggregate history under the parent fence. Compact
receipt retrieval still refuses execution. An authenticated historical outcome
must match the original receipt and independently verified job, and may carry
only a genuine test failure. The transport checks ownership again after awaited
job verification. Result receipt/job association is immutable, including the
A/B/B conflict sequence that the store alone cannot reject.

A result transaction persists sticky negative truth and its pending application
journal together. Coordinator application precedes acknowledgment; marking the
result applied and clearing the journal is another synchronous transaction.
A fault between these transactions retains negative status and blocks both full
and compact expiry. Alarm replay is idempotent, visits at most 64 journal rows,
and checkpoints past an unresolved row so other failures can progress. Legacy
negative results are fenced immediately by indexed owner reads and recovered by
a separate 64-row cursor scan. Unknown or unreadable evidence is retained.

The runtime supplies the consumer only with a validated publication mode from
the actual configuration. Invalid or unavailable configuration refuses handoff
and expiry. Pending failure application fences its retention guard. Duplicate
full/compact states never publish positive authority; verified negatives update
both matching representations, while incompatible evidence remains unresolved.

## Bounded publication and dependent evidence

The publisher retains intents and job-bound results while either full or compact
authority exists, or a negative application remains owed. Orphan collection
visits at most 64 rows per durable cursor page. Invalid JSON, unacknowledged
publication and unapplied negative evidence are retained, including legacy rows.

Each publication turn selects at most four records across independent durable
cursors: full active priority, full settled history and compact history. Each
nonempty full lane receives a place and compact history receives up to two;
unused full places may be filled. Compact reads are capped at 100 per page.
An original publication mode is never reinterpreted in another mode, and a
missing/malformed compact intent cannot create a replacement check. Failed
PATCHes retain their exact intent for restart. Parent and full-suite evidence
checks remain in force. The existing API request budget is not increased.

Alarms are prearmed before work, replay pending negatives, and remain scheduled
when only compact history or bounded garbage collection remains. Being disabled
does not erase authenticated negative/cleanup reporting or owed reconciliation.
All admission, caller and execution controls, resource limits and ordinary hosted
CI remain unchanged.

native resource/timing and production throughput qualification are not established

result associations per request; its total cost still needs runtime qualification.
