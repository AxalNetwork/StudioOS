## D671 — the daily flagged-score digest email is retired

Issue: #1540. The owner's call (2026-10-11): stop the "[Axal] N flagged scores awaiting review" email.

### What it was

Every day at 14:00 UTC, the cron enqueued a `flagged_score_digest` job.
- The job listed every non-sandbox score snapshot still flagged for review more than a day after it was created.
- It wrote a bell notification for each admin and emailed them the same list.
- Nothing ages an item out of that list. So the same snapshots, some months old, went out every day until someone reviewed them.

### What changed

- **The cron** no longer enqueues the job (`index.ts`).
- **The job type stays.** Its handler in `services/queueWorker.ts` is a no-op, so a job enqueued just before the deploy drains without an error and sends nothing.
- **Deleted:**
  - `digestUnreviewedFlaggedScores` (`services/notifications.ts`);
  - `sendFlaggedScoreDigestEmail` (`services/email.ts`).

### What stays

- **Flagging, and the review queue in Monitoring › Integrity.** A flagged score is still hidden from LPs and partners until an admin approves or rejects it.
- **The one-time alert** when a score is first flagged: `notifyAdminsOfFlaggedScore` and `sendFlaggedScoreEmail`. It is one email per event, deduplicated per admin.
- **The nightly integrity audit** (`score_hash_audit`, 03:30 UTC).
- **Bell notifications the digest already wrote.** They stay where they are.

### Guard

`cloudflare-worker/test/flagged_score_digest_retired_d671.test.ts` pins four things. Each was mutation-checked: putting the old behaviour back fails its own test.
- Nothing enqueues the digest.
- A leftover job touches neither D1 nor the network.
- The builder and its email sender are gone.
- The first-flag alert and the nightly audit remain.
