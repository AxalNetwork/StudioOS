## D8521 — One customer interview, invitation to summary, under test (#8521)

`cloudflare-worker/test/interview_journey_8521.test.ts` runs one Customer Research interview through the real routes and the real `interview_process` job on the whole production schema (`freshSchema`), with the inference stubbed. Fixtures (`_interview_journey_8521.mjs`): one study ("Ledger Study", three questions), two invited interviewees (Rowan Example, Sasha Example), scripted answers, `.invalid` addresses and a 555-01xx number. No model, mailer or network runs; the assertions are on rows written and on the fields the founder's session read returns, never on model wording.

Paths and what each pins:

1. **Complete.** Invited, booked on open, three questions ticked, three answers stored in order, summary and digest written. The stub also offers a quote nobody said and a sentence citing a span nobody spoke in; the product keeps neither, and every quote the founder can read is a substring of the transcript.
2. **Declined.** Consent declined at setup: start, chunk upload, finish, summarise and process are all 409 `consent_not_recorded`; no audio, answers, receipts, job or model run. Consent withdrawn afterwards clears the summary and digest and blocks any new run.
3. **Abandoned.** A last part that never arrives is not summarised as complete: the founder's read carries `processing.error` naming the missing part, and `points_covered` is 2 of 3. A recording left open has no summary, is not marked held, and Summarise is 409 `no_final_transcript`.
4. **Contact details.** See owner decisions.

### For the owner

- **Emails and phone numbers in answers are not redacted today.** Nothing between the transcript and the summary removes them: the answer, the key quote, the overview citation, the stored `digest_json`, `summary_citations_json` and `discovery_interviews.transcript` all carry the address and number verbatim. Only the assistant-submitted interview path refuses contact data, and only in the interviewee's label, role and company fields (`parseInterviewee`), not in answers. Test 4 pins today's behaviour; adding redaction is a product change, so the test must change with it. No product behaviour was changed here.
- **No "partial" state exists.** A summary of an incomplete interview is stored and shown like any other. The only signals are the session's `processing.error` ("Part N of the recording never arrived...", set by the upload sweeper only) and the questions ticked against the questions planned. The summary and digest carry no marker, and a recording stopped early by the founder (all parts arrive, fewer questions asked) has no marker at all. Test 3 pins the sweeper's marker.
- **Withdrawing consent keeps the answers.** `withdrawDigest` clears the summary, digest and pending suggestions, but the transcript stays until the founder deletes the session (stated in its own comment). "Declines consent leaves no answers" holds only for a decline before recording.
- A non-writer reading a kept digest sees quotes withheld unless the interviewee agreed to be quoted (`quote_consent`); the founder's own read, which these tests use, is unredacted.

### Steps the journey could not reach without a model, for a later task

- **Invitation.** There is no route that invites a customer interviewee: the interview sits at stage `invited` on the board, and a founder contacts them outside the product. The fixture seeds the stage; the invitation itself, and any message sent for it, is not exercised.
- **Questions asked by an AI interviewer.** Questions are talking points the founder ticks; nothing generates or asks them. The AI-drafted guide (`interview_guide.ts`, `/draft`) needs a model and is not run.
- **The interviewee's actual speech.** The speech-to-text stub returns the scripted answer for each part; real transcription, speaker labelling (`Speaker ?` here) and the live socket are not run.
- **Structuring.** The `structure` stage runs with a stub that returns no placements, so findings, pain groups and suggestions from an interview are not asserted.
- **Themes.** The journey ends at the summary and digest sections. Themes across interviews (the pain groups and the Validate overview) are a later step and are not reached here.
- **Summary wording.** Only that the stub's text is kept or dropped is asserted; whether a real model's summary is faithful to the answers needs a model-graded check.
