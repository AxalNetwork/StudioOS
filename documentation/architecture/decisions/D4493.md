## D4493 — Distribution sequence builder: per-step consent, drafts the founder presses (#4493)

Part of #3722. Designs DS7 (right column) and MK7b.

### The owner's answers

The owner answered questions D, E and F on 2026-11-06, recorded on #3722:

- **D = (a): email now.** Email steps, and the founder's own voice note sent
  as an email attachment, are the only steps that can be built. WhatsApp and
  Telegram wait for counsel's review; until counsel answers they are drawn as
  blocked with the fixed sentence "Only to contacts who wrote first or opted in
  · after counsel’s review." LinkedIn has no messaging API (D1350 §3) and is
  drawn blocked: "Needs LinkedIn’s approval." There is no SMS step, and the
  builder says so. The Worker refuses each of these channels with the same
  sentence; the schema keeps the channel values only so a blocked step can be
  drawn and read.
- **E = (a): a due step becomes a draft the founder presses Send on.** This
  amends nothing: D1340 stands. `POST /api/grow/sequences/due/:enrolmentId/draft`
  writes one `mail_drafts` row and never calls a mail adapter (the test spies
  on every adapter). The send is the existing composer press:
  `POST /api/mail/send/confirm` issues a one-use nonce and `POST /api/mail/send`
  spends it, one recipient, from the member's own mailbox, at 20 an hour. There
  is no background sender and no scheduler that sends.
- **F = (a): the founder's own recording only.** The browser records it
  (MediaRecorder), it is stored in the venture's R2 files under
  `grow-voice/<project>/` behind the existing storage guard
  (`assertStorageRoom`), and it can be previewed. There is no text-to-speech
  path; the builder shows "AI voice · disclosed" greyed with "Not offered". If
  an AI voice is ever added, its message must say so (the Ask's hold line
  carries that promise).

### Stores

Migration `202611070812_outreach_sequences.sql` adds `outreach_sequences`
(venture, optional campaign from #4447's `grow_campaigns`, name,
draft|active|paused), `outreach_steps` (position 1–10, channel, wait in days,
subject, body, recording) and `outreach_enrolments` (contact, current step,
active|stopped_replied|stopped_optout|done, next due time and the recipient's
time zone). Two nullable columns on `mail_drafts` carry a sequence draft's
voice-note key and the opt-out line its send must keep. No second campaign or
consent store is added.

### When a step is due

`services/distribution/sequences.ts` `dueSteps(project)` reads, per enrolment,
in this order: a recorded stop; the venture's HMAC suppression list from #4492
(an `opted_out` entry stops the sequence, any other entry is never due); an
inbound `contact_replies` row since enrolment (stops it); then, from the
current step on, the channel (blocked channels are skipped with their reason),
recorded consent in `contact_marketing_consents` (email needs `opted_in` or
`wrote_first` for email; a voice note needs that and the same for `voice`),
whether the sequence is active and the step written or recorded, the wait, and
quiet hours. A step without consent is skipped for that person with
"Not sent: <name> has no consent recorded for email. The step was skipped."

Quiet hours are #4492's `venture_outreach_settings`, read in the recipient's
time zone, which is recorded on the enrolment. Inside the window the step says
"Due after 08:00 their time". Without saved quiet hours, or without the
recipient's time zone, a step is held, never sent in someone's night: the
platform has no other recipient-zone store. A suppression list written with a
different key makes nothing due until it is checked (as D4492's reachability).

### The send boundary

For a draft whose intent is `sequence_step`, `/api/mail/send` checks, before
the nonce is spent, that the step is still due for that person, that the
recipient is the prospect's recorded address, and that the body still holds
the opt-out line. It attaches the voice note (Outlook, Gmail and SMTP build the
attachment; a member's MCP mail server takes text only and is refused before
the nonce). After the email has gone, the enrolment moves to its next step;
the send stays accepted if that record fails.

### The opt-out

Every sequence draft ends with "To stop these emails, open:
<base>/api/grow/optout/<token>". The token is the contact's uid and an HMAC of
it under `columnCipher.indexHmac`'s key (the existing `KEK_PII` secret,
domain-separated), so it names no address and cannot be forged. The public
`GET /api/grow/optout/:token`, mounted at its own path so no signed-in route
takes it, records an `opted_out` suppression (hash only), stops the contact's
active enrolments and answers one fixed sentence that names no person,
address or venture. A forged or unknown token is 404. Like the newsletter's
unsubscribe it has no rate-limit bucket of its own (a capped opt-out is an
opt-out lost); the general per-IP cap applies. It records on GET, as the issue
asks for a one-click link; a mail scanner opening the link can only stop
messages, never start them.

### Ask

"Ask Eadwyn for a sequence" runs D4488's drafting task (`draft:distribution`)
with the new kind `sequence`: one to five email steps, each a subject, a body
and a wait, quoting a recorded pain or saying none fits. It is quoted first;
the hold line reads "Holds up to $… · you pay what it uses · an AI voice is
always disclosed in the message". Accept or Edit saves a **draft** sequence in
the decision's transaction while the proposal is open; it is never activated,
enrolled or sent, and a sequence is never accepted in bulk. The old
"Proposal · outreach sequence" band (ZoneDraft `grow/customers`, "Draft a
sequence" / "Accept sequence") is retired from the Distribution page. Its
rail row moved with it, key, models and job kept: `draft:grow/customers`,
labelled "Ask Eadwyn for a sequence", now names the builder, and a `sequence`
run takes that row's models (GR2) while its proposal is filed under
`draft:distribution` like every Distribution proposal. The Worker's
`grow/customers` draft surface stays, because team agents still propose
through that option (`services/agents/catalogue.ts`); this page no longer
mounts it.

### Screens

The builder sits under the Prospects pipeline: the sequence card, numbered
steps with wait conditions and consent pills, blocked steps with their
reasons, the due count "N steps due · Review and send" with the composer, the
Consent-by-step table (Opted in / Wrote first / No consent) and enrolment with
the recipient's time zone. Overview gains the Needs-you SEQUENCE row and This
week's STEP items from the same due read. Counts come from data; an unreadable
read is never shown as zero. Writes and Ask follow the shared Distribution
states (D4496).
