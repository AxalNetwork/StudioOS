## D1496 — Team agents: the role catalogue, the rule book and its floor, the permission model, and the registry routes

- **Status:** Part 1 built by the S14 session (#3840, part 1 of #3727, 2026-10-31). Behind `TEAM_AGENTS_ENABLED`, off in both `wrangler.toml` blocks. Not deployed.
- **Builds on:** D435 (the company roster), D760 and D1369 (the offered model catalogue), D764 (the rail matrix and its option keys), D780 (`ai_rail_proposals`, migration 506), D762 (`ai_usage_logs.charged_microusd`, migration 493), D852 (custom MCP servers and their plan rule), D889 (`isProjectManager`).
- **Migration:** 712 (`712_team_agents.sql`). D1497 is the canvas intake (#3727 part 6). D1498 is unused and released.
- **Owner answers it rests on:** Q1 = A (nothing automatic in parts 1 to 3), Q2 = B (the sponsor pays, with a per-agent monthly cap), Q5 (no seat limit beyond one live agent per role per scope), Q6 = A (paid Founder plans only). Q3 (what "MCP agent" means) and Q4 (people roles) stay open and do not block part 1.

### Why

#3727 asks for AI agents in defined roles (CTO, CFO and the rest) on a company's or a project's team, each governed by a rule book. Nothing on main could hold one. A company's team is `user_company_links` and a project's is `project_members`; neither has a row for something that is not a person. `ai_rail_proposals` has one owner and no actor, and there is no per-agent spend. Part 1 is the store that the run (part 2), the approval queue (part 3), tools (part 5) and the screens (part 6) all stand on.

### What was decided

1. **An agent is a record on one scope.** `team_agents` holds exactly one of `company_id` or `project_id`, enforced by a CHECK. It has:
   - a role from the catalogue in code;
   - an optional first name and surname (each trimmed, at most 40 characters; the canvas TM3c);
   - a model from the offered catalogue;
   - a sponsor (the editor who added it);
   - a monthly cap in micro-USD, above zero;
   - a status (`active`, `paused` or `removed`) with `paused_reason`. The reason is `manual` here; `cap`, `sponsor_left` and `tool_disconnected` come from the parts that detect them. It has no CHECK, because #3742 E3 extends it.

   A removed agent keeps its row, its events and its proposals.
2. **One live agent per role per scope.** This is a partial unique index over `(scope_kind, IFNULL(company_id, 0), IFNULL(project_id, 0), role_key)` where `status <> 'removed'`.
   - The `IFNULL` is deliberate. SQLite treats NULLs as distinct in a unique index, and one of the two scope columns is always NULL. An index on the raw columns would therefore never collide. The test's mutation check proves it.
   - Removing an agent frees its role.
   - `team_rulebooks`' version index uses the same form.
3. **The role catalogue is code.** `services/agents/catalogue.ts` holds the eleven roles of #3727's table, each with a default rule book that S1 drafted and the owner reviews:
   - CEO / Chief of staff, CTO, CPO / Product, CFO, CMO / Growth;
   - Head of Sales / BD, COO / Operations, General counsel (assistant);
   - Head of People, Research analyst, Investor relations.

   General counsel carries `disclaimer_key: 'agents.general_counsel.disclaimer'`, a key and never wording. Its text is the owner's or counsel's.
4. **The keys come from what exists.**
   - **Module keys** are the cycles' own module slugs: Validate's seven (with Verdict), Build's six, Raise's seven and Grow's seven. Three founder pages that are not a cycle are added: `studio`, `research` and `network`. The test checks the cycle lists against `cycleValidate.ts`, `raiseCycle.ts` and `growCycle.ts`.
   - **Option keys** are the rail options founder pages register (`ai/railMatrix.json`, D764). A module's options are those on its own page and on its cycle's page: `draft:update` on "Founder · Grow" belongs to every Grow module.
   - **Read keys** are owned here, as #3742 correction 9 asks: `READ_KEYS`, sixteen of them. Most have no reader yet. Part 2 gives the model only the reads that exist and that the rule book lists.
5. **The rule book is a closed shape:** `{ mission, works_in, reads, may_propose, may_do_automatically, always_asks_human, escalates_when, kpis, tone }`.
   - `rulebook.ts`'s `checkRules` refuses an unknown section, a missing one, an unknown key, a duplicate, an over-long line, a control character, and a `may_propose` option that no `works_in` module offers.
   - Each section is one entry of `SECTIONS`, so #3742 E4's `duties` and `hands_off_to` are two more entries.
   - A scope's rule book is versioned. Rows are never updated: the highest version wins, and no row means the catalogue's default (version 0).
6. **The floor is code and only grows.** `always_asks_human` always holds `payment`, `sign`, `file`, `cap_table_write`, `send_email`, `send_post` and `invite_person`. `may_do_automatically` is always empty (Q1 = A).
   - A rule book that breaks either is 422 `rule_floor`.
   - The floor is checked before the shape, so an edit that does both is told about the floor.
   - A scope may add `share_outside`, `delete_record` or `change_plan` to what its agent asks.
7. **The permission model is sponsor ∩ role.** An agent will run only as its sponsor (part 2), so it can never reach further than its sponsor can. Its rule book narrows that further.
   - **Read:** a company's `user_company_links` row (or HQ's admin), or a project's `canAccessProject`. A project advisor reads.
   - **Manage:** a company's `canEdit` (`services/companyAccess.ts`, the one rule every company route uses), or a project's `isProjectManager`. The latter is called and never reimplemented. The owner's 2026-10-31 rule that a company's editors manage the projects inside it therefore reaches agents as soon as #3697 part 2 gives `isProjectManager` its company branch. The test holds that case as `todo`, with the issue link.
   - **Plan (Q6 = A):** adding an agent needs a founder on a paid plan, `userMeetsTier(user, 'growth')`, as D852 does for custom MCP. HQ's admin passes. Anyone else is 403 `plan_required`, and the list says `plan_ok`.
8. **Approvals stay with people.** An agent's output is always an `ai_rail_proposals` row, and migration 712 adds `agent_id` and `decided_by_user_id` to it for part 3's shared queue.
   - Removing an agent withdraws its open proposals.
   - `ai_usage_logs.agent_id` is the column part 2 writes. An agent's month is the sum of `charged_microusd` over its runs since the first of the month (UTC).
9. **"Not recorded" is never zero.** `month_spend` is `{ available: false }` when the read throws, or when any of the month's runs has a NULL charge, because that sum is unknown, not smaller. It is `{ available: true, value_microusd: 0 }` only when the read succeeded and the agent has no runs. `last_activity_at` is the newest event, or `null`.
10. **No email, no numeric id.** A sponsor, an event's actor and a rule book's author are `{ uid, name }`. The test checks every answer for an `email` key, an address and the numeric id keys.
11. **Tools fail closed.** `tool_grants_json` exists, but any non-empty grant is 422 `tool_not_approved` until part 5 checks each grant against the sponsor's D852 approvals.
12. **The audit.** `team_agent_events` records `added`, `paused`, `resumed`, `cap_changed`, `rulebook_changed` (against the scope's live agent in that role) and `removed`. `run`, `refused` and `proposal_decided` are for parts 2 and 3. A rename writes no event, because the kinds are fixed by the CHECK.

### Routes

`routes/team_agents.ts`, mounted at `/api/teams`. Every route is 404 `team_agents_off` unless `TEAM_AGENTS_ENABLED` is `"true"`. The table is in `ROUTE_MAP.md` ("Team agents part 1"). The client is `frontend/src/lib/api/teamAgents.js` (`teamAgentsApi`: `roles`, `listAgents`, `addAgent`, `agent`, `updateAgent`, `removeAgent`, `rulebook`, `saveRulebook`), one line in `api.js`'s block (D527).

- the sponsor's email returned;
- the unique index on the raw scope columns;

- a tool grant accepted;
- the withdrawal dropped;
- `include=projects` ungated;
- the `may_propose` reach check dropped.
