## D6002 — Assign interview segments by hand (#6002)

Venture writers can set, change, or clear an interview’s segment from the
interview record. The ICP module also offers quick assignment for the newest
held interviews counted as unassigned by the chart. It reloads the module after
a successful assignment so the chart, list and count reflect the stored link.

Both entry points use the existing venture-scoped writer and retain the
interview and segment activity. Choices include active segments only. The ICP
assignment data and the record action are available only to venture writers.
