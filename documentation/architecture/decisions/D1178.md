### 2. The pages

**Forecast (UF8)** reads the financial model's own store, `GET /api/financials/:id`, which D1174 kept and D1176 logs. It shows:
- **Ten drivers,** each with its source from `_sources`: Typed, Connector · Stripe, or "Source not recorded". An empty driver is Not recorded and stops the forecast; the page says which to add.
- **Four tiles:** Forecast runway, Average burn, Breakeven month and LTV to CAC, each Modelled. A breakeven outside the forecast reads "Not within 24 months", never a zero.
- **Two charts,** each with a table view: the cash trajectory, marked where cash runs out, and revenue and net.
- **The sensitivity table,** with the base column outlined. A missing cell is Not recorded.
- **Edit drivers and Save forecast:** both open one dialog. It names the drivers that change and saves all ten as numbers through `PUT /api/financials/:id`. The store marks a changed driver Typed; the page never sends `_sources`. The dialog checks what the store's strict validation does: growth may be −100% to 200%, other percentages are 0 to 100, and the length is 3 to 60 months.
- **Export** is the store's existing xlsx.
- **No Capital score.** The store still returns `capital_recompute`, and no page draws it.

**Scenarios (UF9)** draws D1176's `GET /scenarios`. Each card shows:
- the raise, or "Raise not recorded";
- the split bar;
- the runway after the raise (Modelled);
- the milestones funded ("1 of 2 · 1 not costed");
- "No cap-table scenario linked" (§5).

Each write opens a dialog that says what it changes:
- **New scenario:** from the base (`from_id`) or blank.
- **Edit:** name, raise, target runway and the five shares. The shares are all empty, or they total 100. An empty raise is sent as null, never 0.
- **Make this the base:** warns "… runs N months short of your target". It is refused without a complete split.
- **Delete:** the base has neither Delete nor Make this the base.

Pick up to four and **Compare selected** opens Compare with the venture kept.

**Scenarios compared (UF10, UF11)** draws `GET /compare?ids=`:
- **Today**, edged dotted, is the record: runway today from its measured source, and no raise. Each scenario column is Modelled.
- **The rows:** raise, the five sections in money, planned burn, runway after the raise, cash out, milestones funded, founders after the round, and committed so far.
- **The charts,** each with a table: the cash paths, and milestones funded by section.
- No pick says so and makes no read. A failed read says a scenario may have been deleted.
