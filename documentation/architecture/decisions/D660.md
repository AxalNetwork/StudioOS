## D660 — Record a session: the sheet, the Interview Session page and the Recording View

**Issue:** #1473, part of #1469 (D656). **Slot:** S03. **Migration:** none.
**Canvases:**
- `design/incoming/Cycle Action.dc.html` (`action=record`);
- `design/incoming/Interview Session.dc.html` (`start`, `record`, `liveoff`, `text`, `review`);
- `design/canvases/integrated/Recording View.dc.html`, its board (RV1–RV12) and `ring-viz.js`.

This is the page half of D657. It adds no route and no table: every call is a D657 route under `/api/founder/validate`, through `frontend/src/lib/api/interviewSessions.js`.

### Where it lives

- **The sheet** (superseded by D685: the Record a session lightbox, `pages/founder/RecordLightbox.jsx`, replaced it; `RecordSessionSheet.jsx` now holds only the helpers the session page shares) opened from each row of the interview log at `/validate/interviews`, because a session hangs off one interview. It holds How (the five D657 channels), With, Talking points (the interview's hypotheses, or "Not recorded"), Consent, and the live and final model lists.
- **The page** is `/validate/session/:id` (`pages/founder/InterviewSessionPage.jsx`), guarded like the other `/validate/*` stages. A draft shows the start step; a recording or paused session shows the Recording View; a transcribed one shows the transcript. A rail holds the models, the cost receipt and the meter.
- **The Recording View** (`pages/founder/RecordingView.jsx`) covers the screen on a phone and sits in the page on a wider one. Light is the default; dark follows the app's theme.

### The rules, and where each is held

- **Consent.** On an audio channel the sheet's and the page's Start stay disabled until consent is `given` with a method, and the foot says "Start unlocks once consent is recorded." The Worker is still the gate: its 409 `consent_not_recorded` is printed in its own words (`e.message`), never rewritten.
- **The models are the Worker's.** Both lists render from `GET /sessions/models`, with its prices. A model it marks unchoosable (Whisper Tiny EN, which has no price) is listed and disabled. A list that cannot be read says "Unreadable".
- **Capture** (`lib/interviewCapture.js`). `getUserMedia` feeds an `AnalyserNode` for the ring, 16 kHz linear16 PCM to the live socket, and a `MediaRecorder` restarted every 15 seconds so each chunk decodes on its own. Each chunk is put in an IndexedDB queue first and leaves it only when the Worker has it; a failed upload stops the pass and the rest wait, in order, for the next one (on `online`, or on stop). D657 answers a repeat with `duplicate: true`, so a retry is safe.
- **Hide live transcript closes the socket.** A hidden caption that is still streaming would still be billed. Showing it again opens a new socket. A session created with live off opens none.
- **The ring** (`components/discovery/RingViz.jsx` on `lib/ringViz.js`) is the canvas's three bands (20–250, 250–2000 and 2000–8000 Hz) from an analyser at fftSize 2048. Under reduced motion it draws one still frame and schedules no other; paused does not advance time. Stopping draws the progress arc.
- **Eleven states.** `recordingState` picks one of speech, silence, paused, marked, offline, denied, nosound, stopping, reduced, drawer and confirm, in the canvas's order of precedence, from what the capture reports.
- **Stop.** Confirm → `stop` → flush the queue → `transcribe` with the session's final model → re-read until the session leaves `transcribing`. Summarising and Placing findings read "Not recorded" until #1471 (D658) structures the transcript.
- **Speakers.** Each final passage offers Me, the interviewee's name (or "Interviewee") and "Speaker ?" as one-tap chips, written with `PATCH /sessions/:id/segments/:segId`.
- **Money.** The receipt and the meter read `GET /sessions/:id/receipt`. A budget that cannot be read shows the Worker's "Unreadable" reason, never $0. The on-screen clock is the browser's; the duration stored and billed is D657's `active_ms`.

`frontend/test/interview_session_pages_d660.test.mjs` has 9 tests: Start disabled without consent (and for declined, and given without a method); the 409 text printed verbatim; the model lists rendered from the response with prices and the unpriced model disabled; hide closing the socket and later audio going nowhere; reduced motion and pause scheduling no frame; all eleven states reachable with the canvas's words, light and dark; one-tap speakers, the receipt and an unreadable meter; the offline queue keeping what the Worker has not taken, in order; and 16 kHz PCM.

### Part 2: the review step, built and not yet mounted

The review step needs four routes that are in #1494 (D658 part 2) and not yet on main:
- the placements read, `GET …/sessions/:id/placements`;
- `POST …/sessions/:id/structure`;
- `POST …/sessions/:id/ask`;
- the structuring lines of the receipt.

The drift test refuses a client method whose route is not on main (D527). So, at S1's direction, this part builds the components on #1494's response shapes and mounts nothing, as #1486 did for #1474.

- **`pages/founder/InterviewReview.jsx`.** Its components take data as props and import nothing from `lib/api`:
  - placements grouped by Validate module, in the canvas's order, from the latest run, each quoting its cited passage, with accept, edit then accept, and discard;
  - a kind with no store (a market fact) says "Not recorded" and cannot be accepted;
  - the compare view: two runs side by side by module, each named by its model;
  - "Ask the transcript": an answer is shown only with its cited passages, and the Worker's `no_citation` refusal is shown instead of an answer;
  - the structuring receipt: only the Structured, Re-structured and Asked lines, each with its model, any fallback, cost and time;
  - the states: structuring running, no placements, a module write refused (the writer's own words, and the placement stays proposed), an ask with no citation, and the budget refused. D640's budget refusal reaches these routes as 503 with a `budget_*` refusal, and is shown in the budget's own words.
  - Light by default, and dark on request.
- **`frontend/test/_interview_review_fixtures.mjs`** is the one fixture file, in #1494's shapes. If a shape changes in review, that is the one file to update.
- **`frontend/test/interview_review_d660.test.mjs`** has 9 tests. 17 mutants were tried. One survived at first: the fixture was already in module order, so a test now feeds the placements out of order. All 17 are caught.

### Part 3: the review step, mounted

#1494 merged, so its routes are on main.
- **Five client methods** join `lib/api/interviewSessions.js`: `placements`, `structure`, `ask`, `accept` and `discard`.
- **`InterviewSessionPage`** mounts `ReviewStep` under the transcript once a session is `transcribed`, `structured` or `reviewed`. `ReviewStep` reads the placements and wires each action through `makeReviewActions`. Each failure is kept as the Worker's error, so the page tells a budget refusal from a writer's refusal from a citation refusal by the Worker's own fields.
  - A refused run stores nothing and re-reads nothing.
  - A refused write stays on its placement only.
  - A refused ask replaces any earlier answer, so a refusal never sits beside an old answer.
  - An unreadable placements read says "Unreadable", never an empty list.
  - After any model call the receipt is read again, so the meter and the structuring lines are current.
- **Compare.** "Structure again with …" runs the other `structure_interview` alternate. No route lists those models yet, so `STRUCTURE_MODELS` mirrors `aiRouter.ts`'s `alternates`, and a test fails if the two lists part.
- **The fixtures stay.** They are what the component tests render. The page itself reads the Worker.

**Verified.** `frontend/test/interview_review_mount_d660.test.mjs` has 6 tests. 13 mutants were tried and 12 were caught. The one that survived (a refused ask not clearing the earlier answer) is equivalent: the ask clears it when it starts.
