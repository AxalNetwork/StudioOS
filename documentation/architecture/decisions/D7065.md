### What the canvas decides

- The ask is step 3 of the existing request sheet. It is offered only when no teammate can introduce an eligible founder or venture; the team path is free and offers no ask.
- The confirm step names the venture, the receiving founder where visible, the message, "1 quarterly ask", the quarter and the allowance now and after. It has one primary.
- Every state names the slot in words: used, returned, being checked, or no slot used. A spinner never implies a debit or a refund.
- Sending, a second click, a second tab, a failed save and a lost response all resolve to the same receipt on retry. An exhausted or unreadable allowance stops the send.
- One receipt serves both readers. The founder never sees the investor's allowance or other asks.
- The founder accepts or declines from the notification. Accepting opens the existing Trust Center NDA step with a link back; it signs nothing.
- Withdrawal (Scope, Review, Result) returns one slot once. A founder answer that lands first wins, and a previous-quarter withdrawal is its own result.
- Your asks today shows Pending only, "No message was sent", and no Withdraw, as stored on main.
