2. **The screens read `agentEngineApi` directly.** Every engine route is mounted, so there is no seam to wait behind. `useEngineRead` (`EngineParts.jsx`) answers loading, ready, off (`agent_engine_off`), refused or error; off is drawn as "AI agents are not switched on for this team. Goals and plans appear once they are." and an error as "… could not be read. That is not the same as having none."
3. **Words, not codes.** A refusal is the Worker's own sentence (`e.message`, D258) first, then the canvas's sentence for its code (`engineModel.REFUSALS`). A cost that was not recorded reads "Not recorded", never $0. Rates arrive as fractions and are drawn as whole percentages.
4. **The quote comes first.** Draft a plan (TM10c) and Start (TM19f) show the hold before the press. A quote that does not answer leaves the press on, with "Cost can’t be estimated right now"; a 4xx quote is a refusal and turns it off with its sentence.
5. **A plan is accepted as one write** with what was unticked (`remove`) and retitled (`titles`), as D1530 §5 takes it. Accept is off with no item kept.
6. **TM15 draws a role as its persona** (`PERSONA_ROLE`). A role whose persona is already on the team cannot be ticked. Add sends one `addAgent` per row, through `agentsAdapter`, and reports each result in its own words.
7. **Pausing a team confirms first** (TM19a) and sends `{ paused: true }`; resuming sends `{ paused: false }`. The stepper stays within 1 to 5. Who starts a task is "Set by Axal VC". A reader sees the settings and no controls.
8. **A move notice is dismissed in this browser only** (`localStorage`); the move itself stays in TM1g2 / TM1h's confirms.
9. **The daily limit's sentence names the Worker's number** (`DAILY_LIMIT = 20` in `agents/runtime.ts`); the test reads it.
