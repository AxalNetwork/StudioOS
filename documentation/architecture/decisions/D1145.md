### 1. Worker: one store down costs only its inputs (RD16 B)

**Before.** If any Validate store failed, the whole of `recomputePmf` threw, and the part read Unreadable. The issue asks that "a failed input is drawn without and confidence drops", and RD16 B draws exactly that, so the engine and collector needed a per-input state.

- **The collector** (`scoring/pmfCollector.ts`) reads each store through `tryRead`. A failed read marks only the inputs it feeds `unreadable`, worded "Could not be read". Only the read's name is logged.
  - The interviews feed six inputs: ICP interviews, ICP coverage, both pain inputs, claims and Would pay.
  - The pain map feeds pain frequency and claims.
  - The demand signals feed paying demand, Would pay and the waitlist.
  - The waitlist bar and sign-ups feed the waitlist; the experiments feed experiment outcomes.
  - The releases feed product pull. Whether a product is live is then unknown, so product pull counts as applying and unread.
  - If the pivot or Verdict read fails, the whole part is still Unreadable, as before: every input depends on the pivot.
- **The engine** (`scoring/pmf.ts`) has a fifth state, `unreadable`. Like Not recorded, it is out of the value; its grade is 0, so it lowers confidence, and it lowers coverage. It says "Could not be read" and is given no next step. An input that does not apply stays Not expected yet.
- **The store** (`scoring/pmfStore.ts`) returns a partial score but never stores it. `partial: true`, `changed: false`, `inputs_hash: null`, and `id` is the last stored point's.
  - History, checklist item 8 and the audit keep the last complete read. A store that answers again writes the next point as usual.
  - **Below the floor, a partial read is Unreadable**, never "Not enough recorded": what is recorded is not known. D1144's test that drops the interviews still reads Unreadable.

### 2. Frontend: the part (`pages/cycles/PmfPart.jsx`, `pmfModel.js`)

D1141's "Not scored yet" part is replaced. `CycleRoute` passes the module read's `parts.pmf`, `card` and `records`, with the venture and `reload`. All words and numbers come from `pmfModel.js`.

- **The Product-market fit tile (RD1, RD14, RD16).** It has three faces:
  - **Scored:** the number, its band, the confidence dots and word, and "N of M inputs that apply".
  - **Below the floor:** "Not enough recorded", with no number and no band.
  - **Unreadable**, with Try again (`reload`). This is the face for a failed read and also for a module read that carries no part, so neither ever reads as "no score".
  
  A partial score is eyebrowed "partial" and names its unread inputs. The Verdict line is shown, "Shown, not scored". The foot gives the model, the day, and why the point was computed, or that a partial score is not stored. A spark draws the last six stored points.
- **The raise kit tile.** It reads the module's own card bars, so it covers every item whatever the table below filters. It is their mean × 10, as `overallReadiness` computes it. An unscored item, such as Team, is left out, never counted as 0.
- **What Product-market fit rests on (RD1, RD9, RD10, RD18 A).** The ten inputs are drawn in the canvas's four groups. Each row has:
  - its words, and a bar shaded by its grade;
  - its grade, Stale, its source and record count, and As of;
  - its weight and points;
  - its value. A Not recorded, Not expected yet or unread input reads "—" and says why: "lowers confidence, not the score", "Out of the coverage count", or "Left out until it reads". A recorded 0 reads 0.
  - After a pivot, the card says only later records count, and each input counts the records it left out.
- **An input's lightbox (RD2, RD3, RD18 B).** It shows the value of 100, the weight, the contribution in points, As of, the method (model v1's rule), what it read, and a link to its module. It is a bottom sheet on a phone (`Lightbox phone="sheet"`).
- **Gaps (RD1, RD18 C).** The engine's steps come first, in the engine's order, each with its gains ("Up to +N", the confidence change, the coverage change) and its module. Then come each raise kit item's Missing from the checklist; Product-market fit's own row is left out, since its steps are already listed. The All, Product-market fit and Raise kit chips filter the list.
- **Score history (RD8).** It reads `cyclesWrite.raise.pmfHistory(project, { limit: 200 })`, with older points on request.
  - The chart and the table show the same points. A point below the floor has no number and is drawn as a gap. The first point after the pivot is marked, and a change of model is marked.
  - A failed read says "could not be read. That is not the same as no history", with Try again, never an empty chart.
- **Re-score (RD6).** The module's Re-score form (`forms.js`) gains "Product-market fit · from Validate", ticked by default. When it is ticked, the form sends `pmf: true` with the item, as D1144's route requires. Team stays scored on its record (RD7).
- **States.**
  - **Loading (RD15):** the module page's own loading; the history reads "Reading…".
  - **Empty (RD14):** with nothing recorded, the part says "Your score fills as you work in Validate" and links to Customer research and Record a session.
  - **No access (RD17):** the module route's refusals, unchanged.
- **Phone and dark (RD18, RD19).** The tiles and cards stack below `lg`, and phone tap targets are 44px. Every surface has its dark colours.
- **Model v1 (RD4)** stays folded as D1141 drew it. Part 5 (#3032) reads it from the stored model version.
