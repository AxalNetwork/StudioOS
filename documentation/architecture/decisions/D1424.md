### 4. How the header is drawn

- **`WorkspaceShell`** (the bucket routes of all three licences, and Research and Network) gains `primary` and `ask` props and is a `ZonePrimaryScope`. Beside the title it draws, in order: a passed `primary`, the slot, Ask, `actions`, the scope chip.
- **The body owns the handler.** A zone's own `ZoneActions` row (through `ZoneToolbar`) finds its primary and portals it into the header's slot (`ZonePrimary`), so the handler, its busy and its disabled state stay with the page that owns them. Outside a scope, or before the slot mounts, the primary leads the row instead.
- **`ZoneHeader`** draws the same header for the seven investor pages with their own chrome (Positions, Reports, Value-add; LPs, Calls, Accounting, Reporting), in each bucket's CSS family (`i4-heading`, `i6-header` with its breadcrumb), each page wrapped in `ZonePrimaryScope`. The Portfolio and Fund roots carry its tools cluster (`ZoneHeaderTools`). `WorkspaceShell`'s own markup is kept rather than rebuilt on `ZoneHeader`: a dozen tests pin it, and the two now share the slot, the primary and Ask, which is what a reader sees.
- **States:** rest, hover (the deep weight), focus ring, busy ("Label…", `aria-busy`), disabled with its reason on hover. At 390px the primary is 44px tall, and the pills are already lenses (D575).
- **Board → component:** Page/PH4/PH20a/PH22a `InvestorDealsRoutes` + `ClosingZone`; PH0 `ZoneActions` + `ProfileHeaderAsk`; PH1 `DealFlow`; PH2/PH3 `ScreeningZone`/`CommitZone`; PH5 the three Portfolio pages; PH6 the four Fund pages; PH7 `AskZone` + the Network workspace; PH8 `InvestorThesisBand`; PH9–PH11 `AdvisorBucketRoutes` and its zones, with `VisibilityZone`; PH13–PH15 `PartnerBucketRoutes` and its zones; PH16 this file's §5 and §6; PH18 each zone's own states; PH19 the Ask gate.
