### 1. One door to the Worker

- `MyEventsPage.jsx` makes every read: the list, my invitations, the feed status and the suggestions. It also builds `api`, the writes and the per-row reads the tabs need: the public calendar, eligibility, register, answer an invitation, the add-to links, and make or remove the feed.
- `MyEventsTabs.jsx` imports no client. So the page's tests stub one object, and the tabs cannot read around them.
- **Every change opens a dialog** that says what it changes and who is told: accepting or declining an invitation, registering, joining a waitlist, and making or removing the subscription. Nothing uses a browser pop-up.

### 2. Upcoming (MEV1)

- **Needs you,** in the canvas's order, at most four rows, with the total in mono:
  - **Pay to confirm:** a confirmed seat whose payment is pending, with the amount from the place's `amount_cents` and `currency`. Pay now opens the event page, where the payment form is.
  - **Sent back,** with the desk's note (`review_note`) when there is one.
  - **Each invitation** from `GET /api/events/invitations`, with Accept and Decline. Each answers through `POST /api/events/:id/invitation`, by the event's id, after the dialog. The dialog says the seat is free or what it costs, whether the host approves, that the host sees the answer, and that a full event leaves the invitation open.
  - **Off the waitlist** (D1131's promotion). A promoted place that still owes payment is one row, never a second "Pay to confirm".
  - **An invitations read that fails** says so in its own card, with Retry. It is never "no invitations".
- **The month calendar:**
  - a dot for each place you hold (violet) and each event you host (sky); an Explorer sees no hosting dots;
  - today ringed, and a month back and forward;
  - each day a button whose name reads "12 November: 2 events: going to 1, hosting 1".
  - Clicking a day narrows Going to that day. "Show every day" clears it, and a day with nothing else says "Nothing else on that day."
  - Days are the member's own, in their zone.
- **List / Month** sits on Going. Month is MEV11 (§3).
- **Each Going row** has the ⋯ menu:
  - Show ticket;
  - Add to calendar, D1033's Google, Outlook and .ics links in a dialog;
  - Open event page;
  - Cancel my place (or Leave the waitlist), which opens the ticket drawer, where D1035's cancel dialog is.
- **Hosting soon** carries the counts (§4), "[2] of [N]", and Open Hosting.
- **Subscribe:** "Add your events to your calendar", from D1033's feed.
  - The card says whether you are subscribed, or that it could not be read. It never says "not subscribed" in place of a failed read.
  - The dialog says what the address does.
  - Make my address (`POST /api/calendar/my-events/feed`) shows the URL and the `webcal:` link once, and offers no second Make in the same dialog.
  - When subscribed, Make a new address says it stops the old one. Remove the address asks first, inside the dialog.

### 3. Month view (MEV11)

- **The month as a grid,** Monday first, six weeks.
- **Each day** shows up to three chips, "18:00 [title]", by kind:
  - Going, violet;
  - Hosting, sky;
  - a waitlist place, dashed violet, "· Waitlist";
  - an invitation, dashed sky, "· Invitation".

  A legend names the four kinds.
- **"+N more"** opens the day's full list in a dialog.
- **A chip** opens its event: Show ticket, Open the event, or the event page for an invitation. For a place or a hosted event it also offers Add to calendar and "Subscribe to all my events".

### 4. Hosting (MEV4)

- **The counts per event** come from part 2's `counts`:
  - "[42] of [60] taken" with its bar, or "No cap · [14] taken";
  - then "[6] waitlist · [3] to approve · [12] invitations open", each only when there are some.

  The read's 0 is a count, so "0 of 60 taken" is drawn. An event with no counts says "Seats, waitlist and approvals: Not read here yet", never 0.
- **The filters:** search, Status, Visibility, and When (Upcoming by default, Past, Any time).
  - Upcoming keeps a draft, a sent-back or a waiting event whose date has passed, since it still needs its host.
  - No match is said, with Clear the filters.
- **Two sorts:** Starts soonest and Starts latest.
- **The ⋯ menu:**
  - Edit details, Who may register, Registrations, Agenda;
  - Copy link, for a published event, through the clipboard. A refused copy says the address.
  - Download roster (CSV);
  - Cancel event, which opens the hosted drawer, where D1035's cancel dialog is. It is not offered once the event has ended.
- **"1–25 of [N]":** the sorted list is cut into pages of 25, then grouped. So the pager counts events, not groups.

### 5. Discover (MEV8)

- **Month chips:** the next three months, each with its exact count from its own read (`limit: 1`, its `total`). This month reads from now. A failed count is a dash.
- **The chosen month** is one read of up to 100 events with the filters, so the type chips count what is there.
  - When the month holds more than 100, a line says how many and that the first 100 are listed and counted.
  - Search narrows it.
- **Type chips** come with their counts, for the types the month holds.
- **Filters:** Format (Online, In person, Hybrid) and Price (Free, Paid) go to the read. "Free for me" keeps the rows whose eligibility read says the seat is free for the caller.
- **Each row's price line and action** come from what the caller holds, their invitations, and `GET /api/events/:id/eligibility`:
  - a held place: Show ticket;
  - an invitation: "Invitation only", with Accept (the invitation dialog);
  - an event closed to the caller: "Not open to your profile". An invitation-only event with no invitation reads "Invitation only", with no action.
  - a free seat for them: "Free for you · [source] · [N] left", with Register through the dialog;
  - a full event with a waitlist: "Full · waitlist open", with Join the waitlist;
  - a paid seat: Register opens the event page, where it is paid for. It is never taken in place.
- **Eligibility is read once per event,** for the rows on the page, or every row of the month with "Free for me". While it is out, or when it fails, the row opens the event page and says why. It never offers Register blind.
- **"1–25 of [N]"** pages over the month's rows.

### 6. Past (MEV9, MEV10)

- **The list reads 100 places at a time** (D1032's ceiling): `list({ pastLimit: 100 })`. When Past holds more, "[n] of your [N] past places are read here" offers "Read older places" (`pastOffset`).
- **Each place** comes with its outcome in the canvas's words: Checked in, "Registered, not checked in", You cancelled, Declined by the host, Event cancelled.
- **A hosted event** joins Past once its date has passed, as D1030 decided, with "Hosted · [31] of [42] came" from its counts, or "Hosted" without them. A cancelled event still ahead stays under Hosting › Cancelled.
- **The filters:** Went, Hosted, Missed and Cancelled (any chosen, or all), the years Past holds (this year by default), and search.
- **"Your events this year":** Went (violet) and Hosted (sky) by month, through D893's `GroupedColumns`, with its legend and a Chart / Table toggle (`ChartTable`).
  - The current month is hatched, "so far".
  - A month still to come is left blank: `GroupedColumns` gains a `blank` row.
  - When Past was not read in full, a month older than the oldest place read is `null`, not recorded, never a zero bar. The summary says so.
  - The summary line ("You went to [5] events and hosted [3] so far this year.") is the chart's `alt`.
- **Replay** reads "Replay · Not recorded", the canvas's words.
