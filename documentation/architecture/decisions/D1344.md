### 2. The Worker, first

Three changes the page needs:

1. **`GET /api/mail/sent?page=&person=`** (`routes/mail_accounts.ts`). It returns the member's own sent log, newest first, 20 a page. Each row gives the recipient (the member's own, as D1340 shows them), the subject, `sent` or `failed` with our sentence, the draft it came from, that draft's person, and the refs it quoted (`refs_json`). `person` keeps the rows sent from that person's drafts. Another member's rows are never read. Both statements are fixed literals.
2. **`person.address` in the pack** (`services/mail/contextPack.ts`). It is the address the person's own module row records: a capital relationship's `email`, a co-founder holder's `email`, a growth partner's `contact_email`. It is trimmed, lower-cased, and null when it does not look like an address. An interview records none, so it is null and the composer asks for one ("No email address on record"). A Network match's is always null: only `GET /api/mail/shares` serves another member's address, and only once both have shared (D1342). The page reads it there (`recipientFor`).
3. **The OAuth callback lands on `/email?mail=…`**, not `/connectors?mail=…`. D1340 left that landing for this page to read.

### 3. What is not built, and why

- **EM6, an update to several people.** One draft per person, sent one by one. It needs a bulk estimate and a queue no Worker route offers yet. It is the next part.
- **EM9, the phone.** The page and the Sheet follow the shell's phone layout, so the composer is a full-screen page below 640px. The phone boards' own bars, and the Eadwyn phone composer's "Email ‹name› …", are not drawn yet.
- **Schedule.** Nothing on the Worker schedules a send, so the button is not drawn.
- **Settings › Signature and "What Eadwyn may read by default".** Nothing stores them. The reads are chosen per email in "What Eadwyn knows".
- **The other profiles' People panes.** A founder's pane lists their drafts' people and their Network matches. The investor, advisor, operating partner, admin and Explorer groups are `GET /api/mail/people` (D1348, #3583). They are drawn when that lands.
- **Advisors and operating partners** open the composer from `/email?person=advisor:‹id›` or `partner:‹id›`. Their own pages do not carry the Email action yet.
