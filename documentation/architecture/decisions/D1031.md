## D1031 — My events part 1, the fixes: Publish for unlisted and private, notices that open the member's own pages, Advisors as an audience, the suggestion's words (#2872)

- **Status:** S20, 2026-10-25. Part 1 of My events (#2387), from S21's integration in #2852 (D1030) and its map, `documentation/audits/MY_EVENTS_MAP_2026-10-25.md` §1, which re-checks #2387's eleven items. Six were fixed by #2623 and #2626; this decision fixes the other four.
- **Migration:** none.
- **Builds on:** D567 (the event organiser and its audience rules), D917 (the review routes), D594 (the suggestion's archetype framing).

### What changed

1. **Unlisted and private events can be published.** `POST /api/events/:id/submit-review` already published a non-public event, but the editor offered its button only for a public one.
   - `EventEditorPage.jsx` now offers one action for a draft or sent-back event: "Publish" when unlisted or private, "Send for review" when public. A submitted, live or cancelled event offers none, matching the route, which refuses anything but `draft` and `rejected`.
   - The action opens a dialog (`ui/Lightbox`) that says what happens and who is told. The save is the dialog's button. A refusal stays in the dialog. The new status comes from the Worker's reply, not from a guess.
   - The words live in `publishAction(visibility, status)` in `pages/events/eventOrganiserModel.js`:
     - public: the event goes to the admin review queue, is listed only once approved, and nobody else is told;
     - unlisted: it is live now with no review, anyone with its link can open it, it is never listed, and nobody is told;
     - private: it is live now with no review, only invited people can open it, it is never listed, and nobody is told.
2. **Event notices open the recipient's own page.** The public reader serves only approved public and unlisted events, so a notice linking `/events/<slug>` landed a member on "Event not found." for a private, cancelled, sent-back or unapproved event.
   - `cloudflare-worker/src/services/eventLinks.ts` holds the two targets: `memberEventLink(id)` is `/events/<id>`, the signed-in event page; `hostEventLink(id)` is `/events/<id>/manage`.
   - Host notices (live, sent back, back to draft) go to the manage page: `routes/admin_events.ts`.
   - Member notices go to the event page: cancelled and off-the-waitlist (`admin_events.ts`); confirmed, registration update and off-the-waitlist (`events.ts`, four places); the reminder (`services/eventReminders.ts`).
   - Two in-app notices #2872 did not list had the same link and get the same fix: the paid-ticket confirmation (`services/eventTickets.ts`) and the invitation (`services/eventMessaging.ts`).
   - The links in emails keep the public slug link, because an email reader may not be signed in.
   - When part 5's drawers land, the targets become `/my/events/ticket/<id>` and `/my/events/hosting/<id>` in `eventLinks.ts` and nowhere else.
3. **Advisors can be an audience.** `advisor` joins `EVENT_ROLES` (`services/eventOrganiser.ts`) and the manage page's role list (`EventManagePage.jsx`). The two lists are equal and a test holds them so. Advisors could already host. #2368 raised the same point.
4. **The suggestion's words.** `suggestion_reason` reads "Suits [archetype]", as the canvas does, not "Recommended for [archetype]". A free plan still gets "Matches your track" (D594).

### A choice to review

Pointing a cancelled-event notice at `/events/<id>` was not enough on its own. `canViewEvent` let a member read a published event or one they were invited to, and a cancelled event is neither. A registrant of a public event who registered without an invitation would have opened their "Event cancelled" notice onto "Unable to load event details."

`canViewEvent` now also lets a member read an event they hold a registration for, whatever its status. They get no private fields: `includePrivate` is still for managers only. The join link still needs a confirmed seat. A stranger is refused as before.
