## D594 — The archetype is unlocked by each licence's first paid plan, enforced on the server

**A free account keeps profiling, its answers, its skills and its values, and does
not see its archetype. The archetype unlocks with the licence's existing paid plans;
no new price is added.** Slot S07, issue #1233, 2026-10-06. The tiers are Session
1's definition, as the owner asked on #976 ("B, you define what are the best
pricing tiers"). The card's locked state is a separate frontend task (slot S03),
built on the reply below.

### The tiers

| Licence | Unlocks the archetype | Today's price label |
|---|---|---|
| Founder | `subscription_tier` Growth or Studio (`userMeetsTier`, so past-due and unpaid read Free) | Growth $79 / month, Studio $249 / month |
| Investor | effective `investor_tier` Professional or Institutional | Professional $149 / month, Institutional $599 / month |
| Partner, advisor, admin | always: their licence is the paid tier | — |
| Spin-Out Lab participant | always, while `spinout_lab_active` is 1 | — |

**How the investor's plan is read:**
- **An active trial counts.** A `trialing` row whose `investor_trial_ends_at`
  has passed reads Free.
  - `effectiveInvestorTier`'s docstring promises this, but its code never
    checked the date. The archetype check adds that one condition in its own
    code and leaves the shared helper's other callers as they are.
- **A seat reads its primary's plan,** loaded from the primary's row, not
  from the copy made on its own row when it accepted the invitation.

### One check

`services/archetypeEntitlement.ts::archetypeAccess(env, user)` returns one of
two answers:
- `{locked: false}`;
- `{locked: true, required_plan: {licence, tier, price_label}}`. The plan is
  Growth $79 / month for a founder and Professional $149 / month for an
  investor, the plan the canvas's locked line names.

The price labels come from the two tier modules' own upsell payloads, so they
cannot drift.

### What each route returns

| Route | Unlocked | Locked |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/best-fit/me` (the archetype card's read) | as before, plus `archetype_access: {locked: false}` | no `archetype` and no `archetypes` key; `fit`, `axal_values`, `fit_v2` and `archetype_sex` unchanged; `archetype_access` names the plan |
| `GET /api/assessment/results/me` | as before, plus `archetype_access` | each result loses `archetype_slug` and `archetype_label`; its value and skill vectors stay |
| `GET /api/profile/history` | as before, plus `archetype_access` | each snapshot loses `displayed_slug`, `computed_slug`, `secondary_slug`, `confidence`, `margin`, `traits` and `hysteresis`; its `skills`, `values` and `axal_values` stay |
| `GET /api/events/suggested` | the caller's archetype frames the suggestions | `archetype` is null; the track still personalises |

**Locking hides; it never stops or deletes.** The engine keeps scoring on
Free, so an upgrade shows the archetype at once. The routes that return a
person's answers are unchanged.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/archetype_entitlement_d594.test.ts` (3 tests) runs on a
database built from the migrations. It pins:
- every row of the table both ways: a free, a paid and a past-due founder; a
  free, a paid, a trialing and a lapsed-trial investor; a seat of a paid primary
  and a seat of a free primary holding a stale paid copy; partner, advisor and
  admin; an active and an inactive Lab account;
- the four routes, locked and unlocked;
- that no archetype text reaches a free account's best-fit reply.

Each assertion was mutation-checked, and each mutation caught (16).
