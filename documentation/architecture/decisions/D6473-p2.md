### Call-site permissions (P0)

`landing-bot-model.yml` starts with `permissions: {}`. Its three round jobs call `landing-bot-model-round.yml`,
whose `edit` job asks for `contents: read` and `id-token: write`. A called workflow cannot raise what its caller
grants, so the round jobs now grant exactly those two. The resolver's call into the runner already did. A test
reads all three call sites.

Still to prove on a hosted run:
- that the `repair` environment exists and is limited to `main`;
- that the edit job receives its OIDC token or keys;
- that Claude and Codex each answer once.

### Accounts (P1)

The runner now tries the review loop's accounts in its order, then Gemini:

| Order | Name | Credential |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `claude` | Federation, else `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` |
| 2 | `claude-2` | `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY_2` |
| 3 | `claude-3` | `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY_3` |
| 4 | `codex` | `OPENAI_API_KEY` |
| 5 | `gemini` | `GEMINI_API_KEY` |

- An account that is unavailable moves the round to the next one. This covers auth, billing (an Anthropic "credit
  balance" refusal or spend limit included), rate limit, 5xx, federation, and no credential.
- A wrong fix ends the round.
- The manifest's `provider` names the account that made the patch.

Federation stays the primary's alone. Its rule must accept the subject
`repo:AxalNetwork/StudioOS:environment:repair`, which is an owner step.

This is the landing bot's runner, on GitHub Actions. The Worker's agent engine has its own account loop on the
`agents` gateway (D7073-p2, #7096).
