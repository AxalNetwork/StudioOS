## D545 — The community task list's API, for the Eadwyn chat's Tasks panel

**`GET /api/community-tasks` gives a signed-in user the approved community
tasks for their persona and for everyone, and their own suggestions with
their review state; a read that fails is a refusal, never an empty list.**
Issue #1108, slot S08, part of #1101. No migration: the store is D544's
`community_tasks` (migration 383). This entry covers the API; the Tasks panel
in `PersonalAdvisor.jsx` waits on its design (`needs-design`).

**What changed.**
- **`routes/community_tasks.ts`**, mounted at `/api/community-tasks`. It
  returns:
  - `tasks`: approved tasks whose audience is `all` or the caller's persona,
    newest first.
    - The persona follows D544's audiences: `founder`, `investor`, `advisor`
      and `partner` as themselves, and `exploring` as `explorer`.
    - Any other account (an `lp`, for one) sees only the tasks for `all`.
    - An admin, who reviews them, sees every approved task.
  - `my_suggestions`: the caller's own suggested tasks, with their state
    (`pending`, `approved` or `declined`). The decline reason is sent on a
    decline only.
  - `audience`: what the list was filtered by.

  "No tasks yet" is an empty list. A failed read is a 500
  `community_tasks_unreadable` with no list on it, so the panel can say
  "Unreadable".
- **`frontend/src/lib/api/communityTasks.js`** (D527) exports
  `communityTasksApi.list()`, re-exported by one line in `api.js`'s module
  block.

**Not built here.** The chat's Tasks panel, with its "No tasks yet" and
"Unreadable" states and the "Suggest a task" button that opens the ticket form
with Type = Task, waits on the canvas whose prompt is on #1107.
