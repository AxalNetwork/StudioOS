## D670 — /market-intel and /signals are retired, with the market-intel sector digest

Issue: #1533. The owner's call (2026-10-10): remove both pages entirely, and the email that went out from them on a schedule.

### What was decided

The two pages go, and so does everything only they used. What other live pages read stays, because removing it would break Research.

This overrides D422's rule for `/signals`. That rule kept an editor mounted while no canvas zone carried its writes. The Signals page's last write of its own was the admin's manual refresh, and the owner chose to retire it outright.

### What changed

**The pages.**
- `MarketIntelPage.jsx` and `SignalsPage.jsx` are deleted, with `components/signals/` and `lib/signalsMeta.js`, which only they drew.
- Both URLs redirect to `/research`:
  - `/signals` keeps its query string, as D422's redirects do;
  - `/market-intel` drops it.
  - Old bookmarks, and links in digests already sent, land on a real page rather than a 404.
- Gone with them:
  - the Research tab set (Market, Signals) in `FounderWorkspaceTabs`;
  - both paths in the sidebar's `match` lists and in `INVESTOR_FULL_BLEED` and `FOUNDER_FULL_BLEED`;
  - both admin palette entries and their `PAGE_HOMES` rows (26 pages remain);
  - the advisor's "Open Market Intelligence" link;
  - the Research zones' "Signals" links;
  - the assistant's "Investor Signals" shortcut;
  - the help article "Market Intelligence" and its explainer.

**The email.** The sector digest (Task #30) went weekly on Mondays and monthly on the 1st, at 09:00 UTC, to anyone who had pinned a sector.
- `services/market_intel/digest.ts` and its cron block are deleted.
- The digest's unsubscribe link (`/api/market-intel-public/unsubscribe`, GET or POST) now answers with one page saying no more digests will be sent. It changes nothing, so it checks no token.
- The pause and remove page behind it (Task #32) is gone.
- The investor Research overview no longer reads the watchlist that fed the digest.

**The Worker endpoints only those pages read.**
- `/api/market-intel` loses:
  - `macro`, `competitive-intelligence`, and `export` with its tier gate in `index.ts`;
  - `investor-lens`, `geography` and `geography-lens`;
  - the four `watchlist` routes;
  - the seven AT-2 tabs: `sentiment`, `talc`, `demand-supply`, `sector-heat`, `sentiment-geo`, `capital-velocity` and `partner-pulse`;
  - `fit/investor/me`;
  - `platform-personas` and its export;
  - the `investor-signals` alias.
- `/api/investor-signals` is unmounted, with its two aggregation crons (every six hours, and the 04:00 refresh). Its `/coach-match` and legacy `/profile` alias go too; nothing called them.
- `/api/investor-profile` stays: Settings and investor onboarding write it.
- `/api/signals` keeps `GET /` and `/sources`. It loses `/filters`, `/kpis`, `/meta`, `/:id` and `POST /refresh`, and the engine loses `getSignalDetail`, `getKpis` and `getFacets`.

**Smaller fixes.**
- `lib/api.js` drops the methods for every removed endpoint.
- MI Pro's Stripe return URLs point at `/research`.
- The Investor Pro welcome email no longer promises a Market-Intel export.

### What stays, and why

**Research reads the same data.** These read the market-intel rows and indexes, and the `signals` table:
- the overview, markets, sectors and directory (`routes/research_*.ts`);
- the founder Research desk;
- the investor Research overview;
- the Spinout Lab's market page.

**So these keep running:**
- the market-intel ingestion crons, the reducer and the nightly index recompute;
- the signals nightly refresh;
- the market-intel endpoints those pages call: `market-pulse`, `private-rounds`, `studio-benchmarks`, `sector-compass`, `founder-lens`, `citations`, `sources`, `fit/founder/:id`, `contribution-optout` and `admin/reduce`.

**Not done here:**
- **No D1 table is dropped.** A drop cannot be undone, and the watchlist and investor-signal snapshot tables simply stop being read or written.
- **MI Pro billing is untouched.** No page sells it, but `billing.ts`'s MI Pro routes and their webhook stay until the owner checks Stripe for active subscriptions.
- **The dev FastAPI** (`backend/`) keeps its signals and market-intel routers. It is never deployed (CLAUDE.md).

### How it was verified

- `frontend/test/retire_market_intel_signals_d670.test.mjs` pins:
  - the two redirects;
  - the deleted files;
  - that nothing links the paths;
  - the missing API methods, endpoints and crons;
  - the stopped-digest page.
- The tests that guarded the removed code are retargeted or cut to what still exists.
- `npm run test:drift` exits 0 after `npm run build`.
