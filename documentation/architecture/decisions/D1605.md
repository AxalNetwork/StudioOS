### 1. The sync (`services/posthog/validate.ts`)

`POST /api/cycles/validate/posthog/sync` is pressed by the member whose PostHog serves the venture, outside a support session. It runs Validate's registered sync, through one session pinned to the venture's project.

| What it reads | Lands as | Module |
|---|---|---|
| Every metric the founder tracked in the module (`insight-query`) | a `metric` fact for today | Experiments and evidence; Demand and urgency signals |
| The web overview for the last 7 days | a `web_overview` fact (visitors, pageviews, sessions) | Demand and urgency signals, shown beside the waitlist line and never added to `waitlist_signups` |
| Each experiment's results (`experiment-results-get`) | an `experiment_result` fact (variants, exposures, uplift, significance) and one `experiment_evidence` proposal | Experiments and evidence |
| Each survey's stats (`survey-stats`) | a `survey_stats` fact (responses, shown, dismissed) | Experiments and evidence |
| Answers to a question marked "would pay" or "need to have" (`surveys-responses-list`) | one `survey_signal` proposal per answer | Demand and urgency signals |

- **Limits.** At most five experiments and five surveys are read per sync.
- **Partly synced.** A rate limit or a spent budget ends the sync "partly synced", and nothing after it is read.
- **Never a zero.** A figure PostHog did not give lands with no value.
- **No identifiers.** No visit or person identifier is stored: `reads.ts` scrubs them before this file sees anything.

### 2. Marks: what a survey question means (migration 756)

- **The founder decides.** A question proposes nothing until the founder marks it "would pay", "need to have" or "feedback". Its meaning is never guessed from its words.
- **The routes:**
  - `GET /api/cycles/validate/posthog/surveys?project=` lists up to 10 surveys with their questions and marks;
  - `PUT /api/cycles/validate/posthog/surveys/:id/marks` sets or clears one question's mark. The question must be one the survey has.
- **Feedback.** "feedback" is part 6's mark for Product improvement.

### 3. Accept

- **"Link this experiment to a hypothesis"** (PH4):
  - the founder sends the hypothesis and the direction (Supports or Contradicts). Both are required and neither is ever inferred;
  - Accept adds the experiment to the claim with `addExperiment` (method "PostHog experiment", target "Flag <key>");
  - it then links the experiment as `experiment` evidence, strength `measured`, with `addEvidence`.
- **A marked answer** becomes a demand signal through `writeDemandSignal`:
  - its kind is the mark, `who` is empty and `source_kind` is `'posthog'`;
  - its status is `to_confirm`, its date is the answer's day, and the answer is its note.
  - `DEMAND_SOURCES` gains `posthog`.
- **Metric evidence.** `addEvidence`'s `metric` branch now accepts `posthog-<uid>`, once a fact with a value exists for it.

### 4. The SPA (`pages/cycles/ValidatePosthog.jsx`)

Both parts are built on D1604's `FromPosthog`, which gains `renderProposal(p, ctx)` with `ctx.accept(extra)` and an `actions` slot.

- **Experiments and evidence:**
  - experiment results and survey counts, each "From PostHog, as of", with Delete;
  - the link proposal with the hypothesis select, Supports / Contradicts and "Strength · Measured". Accept is off until both choices are made.
- **Demand and urgency signals:**
  - "Last 7 days: 820 visitors · 1,104 sessions", with the line "Shown beside the waitlist. It never changes the waitlist count.";
  - each marked answer with "Add as a signal to confirm";
  - Mark survey questions, in an in-page dialog.
- **Who acts.** Only the serving member acts; a teammate reads.
