### What was decided

1. **The target is the budget at the founder's pace.** The pitch length (2, 3 or 5 minutes) is split across every card of the deck in whole seconds, the first cards taking the leftover seconds: Rehearse's split, so Script, Rehearse and Present agree on every card's budget. A card's word target is its budget × the words a minute of the founder's latest reported rehearsal of the venture's deck (any version), or 150 when there is none or its reading is outside 80 to 220. Regenerate this card keeps the budget the card has in the whole deck.
2. **One quote, one hold, settled per card.** The quote is the sum of each card's own run. The press takes one hold of that sum (refused with 402 before any card runs when the balance cannot cover it) and runs each card as its own run under it, on the rail option `deck:notes`, the same pick as the editor's speaker notes. The hold settles once at what the placed cards cost. An `Idempotency-Key` makes a second press of the same key run nothing (409 `already_pressed`).
3. **No figure that is not on the card.** A card's run sees its title and unbound text, and, read only, the figures it shows: each metric tile's label and value and each bound block's text. A draft that names a digit run not among those, adds a link the card does not have, or is empty once a sentence promising a return is dropped (D1546) fails that card. The failed card is listed with its reason, and its run is settled at zero. A model that did not answer fails the card the same way; a refusal that stops every card (a budget, the kill switch, AI off) ends the run with what was written.
4. **Nothing is saved until Save script.** The drafts come back, are kept as one `ai_rail_proposals` row (D780), and are placed in the tab as "Not saved". Save script is `PUT /api/decks/:id/notes-script`. It copies the version's stored cards as they are, replaces only the named cards' notes (an empty script clears them), and writes **one** version, as the editor's save does. A card not on the deck, or notes over 4,000 characters, are refused and nothing is written. A version that is no longer current answers the version conflict (409).
5. **Out of date is a hint kept in the browser.** When a generated script is saved, the tab keeps a fingerprint of the card's words and figures in `localStorage`. A card whose words no longer match is marked Out of date, with Regenerate this card. Another browser does not mark it; the card model carries no generation record, because the editor's save would drop it.
6. **Download is .txt.** The frontend has no .docx helper, so Download writes a .txt file and says so. Open teleprompter opens Present (its presenter view shows the notes) until the teleprompter lands (#4269).
