### 1. What the runbook covers

One closing, in the operator's order of work, from the pages built for it:

1. open the closing (Contracts › Closings, `POST /api/closings`);
2. prepare and send the paper (D1216's staged envelope, CP3);
3. countersign for Axal (the Super Admin, with step-up, CP4);
4. choose each line's route (CP5–CP8);
5. share the payment instructions (CP9);
6. the investor sends the money, and a Mercury match records Sent;
7. record the receipt (receiving-side evidence only);
8. the founder decides the cap-table change (CP11);
9. Mark Closed, which starts the reporting duties (D1058, CP12).

It ends with who does what, the activity, the common refusal codes and what stays unverified.

### 2. What it says the platform does not do yet

The runbook states these gaps rather than papering over them. Each is a finding from reading the code on `main`:

- **Payment instructions (step 5).** D1217's receiving-account routes are built but not mounted. #3214 is open for the mount. The runbook tells the operator never to send account details through the platform, and to have investors confirm instructions by phone on a number they already hold.

  - the receipt drawer lists evidence but records none;

  So a line cannot reach Received from the UI today. The runbook says to raise it with S1 on #2424, never to write to D1 by hand. **This is a gap #2424 should file as a task.** This part writes no code.
- **The fund's commitment and release** have no route (D1215 §3).
