### 2. What the runbook found that no page does

Writing the steps against the pages found three Worker acts with no control on
any page. No page calls these `closingsApi` methods:
- **add a line** (`POST /api/closings/:uid/lines`);
- **record evidence on a line** (`POST /api/closings/:uid/lines/:lineUid/evidence`);
- **withdraw evidence** (`POST /api/closings/:uid/evidence/:evidenceUid/withdraw`).

**Why it matters:** recording evidence is the only way a line becomes Received.
A closing therefore cannot reach Mark Closed from the pages alone. The investor's
Mercury accept records only the sending side.

**What the runbook does with it:** §10 lists these acts, with the fund's
commitment and release, which have no route, and the unmounted receiving
instructions (#3214). It says how each is done until a control exists.

**This part adds no control.** #3217 is documentation only. A control for each
is a follow-up for #2424's owner to assign.
