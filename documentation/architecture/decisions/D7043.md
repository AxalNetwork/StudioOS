### What the canvas decides

- With 7 selected, Compare is disabled with a visible reason while Add to research still works. New research project is disabled when the selection cannot be compared. Review selection lists each pick with its page and Remove.
- The Compare sheet takes an existing or new project, the companies in column order, and a generated title that fits 160 characters, cut at a whole name.

- On a phone a "By attribute" view reads one attribute across all companies with no panning. Tablet 768 shows three companies at a time with the attribute column kept. Dark is drawn.
