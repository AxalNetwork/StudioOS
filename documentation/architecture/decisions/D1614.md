### 1. The owner's rules

- **Only projects have Work:** the board, backlog, burn-up, activity feed and workload.
  - A company hosts projects and carries the legal and compliance duties.
  - It has no board, and nothing rolls up into it.
- **Agents propose; a named person accepts.** "In review" waits for that person.
- **A compliance obligation stays with the company.** Work done for it becomes an item on a project's board, linked back to the obligation.

### 6. W1, the Worker board (#3969)

- **Built by:** S09 (Claude Code), 2026-11-01, on Codex s28's preparation branch, which this work merged and did not rewrite. Option A, as approved on #3969 (comment 6562550224).
- **Migration 762** (`762_scope_task_work_board.sql`) adds W1's columns to E1's `scope_tasks`. There is no second task store.
  - **Task fields:** `task_number` (unique per project), a Markdown `body`, `type`, `parent_task_id`, `estimate_points`, `position` and bounded `labels_json`.
  - **Board fields:** `board_column`. A CHECK keeps it consistent with E1's `status`: triage, backlog and ready are open or blocked; in_progress is in_progress; in_review is waiting_decision; done is done or cancelled.
  - **Closing:** `close_reason` (completed, not_planned or duplicate) and `duplicate_of`.
  - **Events:** `scope_task_events` gains `moved`, `unblocked` and `dependency_incomplete`. It is rebuilt in a way that keeps every id and the AUTOINCREMENT high-water mark.
  - **Existing rows:** project rows get task numbers in id order. Company rows keep every board field null.
- **Routes:** `routes/work_board.ts`, mounted by `agent_engine.ts` ahead of E1's handlers (the shared-file edit approved on the issue).
  - Project scope only. A company board, and a move of a company task, answer 404. Company tasks keep E1's routes unchanged.
  - A person assignee moves only their own items.
  - A move into ready or in_progress while a blocker is not closed as completed answers 409 `task_blocked`, naming the blockers. This holds for people and agents alike.
  - When the last blocker completes, an `unblocked` event is written and the assignees are notified. A blocker closed as not planned or duplicate does not unblock, and its event says so.
- **Readiness:** E1's `isReady` now offers an agent only Ready-column work on a project board. A task with no column (a company task, or one from before 762) keeps E1's rule.
- **What waits on other issues:**
  - **Milestones:** read "Not recorded" until #3993 (W7), which owns `task_labels`, `task_label_links` and `task_milestones`, and moves `labels_json` into the link table.
  - **Last run and cost:** read through `WorkRunReader`, which reports "no recorded runs" until E3 (#3847) supplies its store.
  - **Client methods:** #3973 (W5) wires `workAdapter.js`'s `board`, `item`, `move`, `assign` and `create` to these routes.
