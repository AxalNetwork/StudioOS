GR-04 and D4862 keep Talent at `/grow/talent` and preserve the earlier entry point: bare `/grow/team-expansion` forwards with its query and hash. The role record, `/new` and `/jobs/:id/manage` remain directly addressable.

| GR-08 evidence | Store and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Headcount | `company_people`, the existing team-card count of active employees, as of the read date |
| Plan | `company_headcount_plan`, the target for the earliest recorded current or future period; period labels retain the Worker’s existing normalization |
| Open roles | `job_postings`, Grow’s existing open/published rule |
| Offers out | Recorded offer stages across the same role statuses shown in the role inventory, including drafts; an unwritten Grow stage falls back to the job board status, matching the applicant table |

GR-12’s shared Not recorded and Unavailable vocabulary applies to source states, and the shared rail frame serves desktop and phone. Follow-ups are exact offer-send timestamps and a saved rank-run timestamp. The board’s examples of elapsed offer time and the date of a ranking require those stores before the page can show them.

The Roles grid item permits shrinking to its container; its existing table wrapper scrolls within the card. A populated long-title browser case at 390px reproduces page overflow without that minimum-width constraint and stays within the viewport with it.

The bare Team expansion forward is also registered as a kept fixed redirect-beacon key. The existing picker-retirement contract checks Talent’s actual shared ventureRef(location.search) entry point, while retaining its startup-read assertion and all other sites’ checks. No second venture parser or scope store is introduced.

Independent review confirmed that stage aggregates trusted legacy defaults and used a narrower posting set than the role inventory. Card and role-row counts now use the same recorded-stage/job-status rule; Open roles still counts only open/published postings. Conflicting plan labels that normalize to one month produce Not recorded with a reason in both the plan line and Talent figure; neither row order wins. Both defects have failing-first SQLite regressions. The bare Team expansion redirect is intentional under GR-08/D4862; record/new/manage paths remain.
