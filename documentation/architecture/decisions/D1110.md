## D1110 — Admin Insights (D893) gaps: a group from any profile mark, "See all countries", and the group read keeps the country (#2990)

- **Status:** built by S24, 2026-10-25 (#2990, part of #2366). **UNVERIFIED** until the owner checks it live.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `0d4ea58af9`, with #2660, #2661 and #2662 merged. Each gap in S1's build-coverage check was re-read against that commit, and all four were still open.
- **Migration:** none.

### 1. The group read keeps the page's country (the bug)

- The group lightbox showed the page's country as a chip, but `groupBlock` (`services/adminInsights.ts`) read the group with `country: null`. Its counts, its weekly lines, its activation rate and its table were therefore every country's.
- The group's filter is now `{ role: group, country }`, and the activation funnel takes the country too.
- The export of "This group only" goes through the same read, so it now matches what the lightbox shows.

### 2. INS3: profile marks open the group

- The canvas says any bar, slice or point opens the group lightbox. A group is a profile, so the owner chose (on this task, 2026-10-25) that **every mark that is a profile** opens it, and the other marks do not.
- **What opens a group:**
  - Who's bars (as before);
  - Growth's stacked parts and its legend chips;
  - AI spend's bars.
  - Each one sets `?group=<role>` and keeps the page's filters.
- **What opens nothing:**
  - "No role", "No account", and a role the canvas does not name (`groupKeyOf`). Before, "No role" was a button that opened nothing.
  - Marks that are no profile: weeks, plan tiers, countries, identity states, completeness bands and ticket states. A group for those would need new group kinds in the Worker.
- **`ui/Charts.jsx` gains two optional props.** Both are additive, and a chart without `onSelect` draws as before:
  - in a stacked column, a part with a `group` is a button;
  - a `Legend` item with a `group` is a button.

### 3. INS1: "See all countries"

- "Where they are" still draws twelve countries and folds the rest into "Other countries".
- "See all countries" opens a lightbox with every country the read counted, then Not stated, each with its share and a search box.
- It is the page's own read (`allCountries`), not another one.
- A block that could not be read offers no link. The list never stands in for an unread block as an empty one.

### 4. ROUTE_MAP

- The Admin Insights row is rewritten to what #2660, #2661, #2662 and this decision built:
  - one read;
  - live filters;
  - every block read for every admin, with the two figures that stay Not recorded;
  - the group lightbox, the export and the scope.
- The row's status moves from UPGRADE to CURRENT.
