## D749 — "Not ours" for an unmatched Mercury credit: a mark Admins set and withdraw, which the matcher respects and the unmatched counts leave out

Issue #1838 (S14), the follow-up #1782 (D717) decision 12 promised. Migration 483. 2026-10-18.

### Why

D710's matcher has no way to say "this credit is not ours". A misrouted wire, another entity's deposit or a duplicate stays unmatched for good. It then counts against every monthly cash summary and every quarterly report (D712 decision 3), and the Super Admin must type an acknowledgement to issue a report around it. The canvas "Fund Administration · Admin" gives an unmatched credit a third action, **Not ours** (board F12).

### What was decided

1. **A mark, not a match.** A mark is its own row in `mercury_not_ours`, never a `mercury_matches` row. 474's `target_kind` CHECK stays, and a match points at a record where a mark says there is none.
2. **What can be marked** is exactly what can be matched: `creditByUid`'s four conditions (a posted credit, not an internal transfer, on a `registered = 1` account). Anything else is 409 `not_matchable`. A debit is never marked.
3. **One live match or one live mark per line, never both.**
   - Marking a line with a live match (`state <> 'rejected'`) is 409 `has_live_match`.
   - A line with a live mark (`active` or `needs_review`) is never matched:
     - the matcher's candidate query skips it;
     - `insertMatch` re-checks it inside its own `INSERT … SELECT … WHERE NOT EXISTS`;
     - `proposeManual` answers 409 `marked_not_ours`.

   The mark's own insert is one `INSERT … SELECT` carrying both NOT EXISTS checks. The partial unique index `idx_mercury_not_ours_one_live` allows one live mark per line.
4. **The reasons** are a closed list of keys in `services/mercury/notOurs.ts`, not a SQL CHECK, so the canvas can rename the labels:
   - `not_fund_transaction`: "Not a fund transaction";
   - `other_entity`: "Belongs to another entity";
   - `duplicate`: "Duplicate";
   - `other`: "Other", which needs a note of 10 to 500 characters.

   The note is optional for the first three, up to 500 characters.
5. **A mark is withdrawn with a reason of 10 to 500 characters.** The row stays (`state = 'withdrawn'`) and the line is unmatched again. A trigger refuses DELETE.
6. **A changed line puts its mark in review.** `upsertTransaction` turns an `active` mark `needs_review` on the same condition it uses for matches: a changed amount, or a cancelled or failed status. A `needs_review` mark counts the line as unmatched again. It still blocks a match, and it offers Withdraw only.
7. **The counts.** A line with an `active` mark is not unmatched in any of these:
   - `GET /api/admin/mercury/transactions?match=none`;
   - `cashSummaryFigures`' `unmatched` and `unmatched_count`;
   - `fundUnmatchedCount`, so the issue acknowledgement no longer counts it.

   `match=not_ours` lists the lines with a live mark, and each item carries `not_ours: { uid, reason, note, state, marked_at } | null`. Each monthly summary gains `reconciliation.not_ours_count` and `reconciliation.not_ours_by_reason`.

   The schema labels (`entity_cash_monthly/1`, `lp_quarterly_report/1`) stay, because the fields are additive. The meaning of "unmatched" changes: it no longer includes a credit marked Not ours. A draft built before this change keeps its frozen figures, and a rebuild picks up the marks.
8. **Who.** An Admin on HQ, through `requireMercuryReader`, the gate matching uses, which refuses a support session and a branch. The two routes join #1779's act row `mercury.reconcile`, relabelled "Propose, confirm or reject a bank match, or mark a credit Not ours". Its tier stays `admin`.
9. **What is recorded.**
   - `logAdminAction` runs after each act: `mercury_not_ours_marked` with `{ transaction_uid, mark_uid, reason }`, and `mercury_not_ours_withdrawn` with `{ mark_uid }`.
   - The audit carries no note and no bank text.
   - The note is returned only on the Admin routes and drawn as text. It is never put in a draft, an export or a log.

### What was built

- `cloudflare-worker/sql/migrations/483_mercury_not_ours.sql`: the table, the one-live index and the DELETE trigger.
- `services/mercury/notOurs.ts`: `NOT_OURS_REASONS`, `markNotOurs` and `withdrawNotOurs`.
- `services/mercury/match.ts`: `creditByUid` is exported; the candidate query, `insertMatch` and `proposeManual` respect a live mark.
- `services/mercury/store.ts`: the mark's `needs_review`.
- `routes/admin_mercury.ts`:
  - `POST /transactions/:uid/not-ours` with `{ reason, note }`, answering 201 `{ ok, uid }`;
  - `POST /not-ours/:uid/withdraw` with `{ reason }`;
  - `/transactions`' `none` and `not_ours` filters and the `not_ours` item field.
- `services/fundCashSummary.ts`: `postedBetween` selects the active mark's reason. The two unmatched filters leave such a line out, and the summary gains its two fields.
- `services/fundAdminAccess.ts`: the `mercury.reconcile` row.
- Frontend, without the canvas's layout:
  - `adminMercuryApi.markNotOurs` and `withdrawNotOurs`;
  - in `fundAdminModel.js`, `NOT_OURS_REASONS` and `notOursText`;
  - on the Reconciliation tab, an **Unmatched credits** group (credits only, each with Not ours behind `FundActControl act="mercury.reconcile"`) and a **Not ours** group (Withdraw with its reason);
  - the Transactions pill reads the mark.

### Guards

- `cloudflare-worker/test/mercury_not_ours_d749.test.ts`:
  - marking, with the audit row carrying no note;
  - the refusals:
    - a debit, an internal transfer, a pending line and an unregistered account;
    - an unknown uid;
    - a bad reason or note;
    - a second mark;
    - a proposed or a confirmed match;
  - a line whose only match was rejected;
  - the matcher and `proposeManual` on a marked line, and again after Withdraw;
  - the race in both orders, the mark landing between the matcher's read and its INSERT included;
  - Withdraw and the DELETE trigger;
  - a sync that changes a marked line;
  - who may act;
  - `GET /transactions`' two filters and the item field.
- `fund_cash_summary_d712.test.ts`:
  - an active mark leaves `unmatched` and is counted by reason;
  - a `needs_review` mark stays unmatched;
  - the quarterly count drops by one per active mark;
  - a withdrawn mark counts again.
- `frontend/test/fund_admin_console_d714.test.mjs`:
  - the keys equal the Worker's list;
  - the pill text;
  - the form posts `{ reason, note }` and needs a note for Other;
  - Withdraw needs ten characters;
  - a refused `mercury.reconcile` hides both buttons;
  - the Transactions pill.
- Mutations: 22 checked, 21 caught. The one survivor drops the matcher's candidate-query NOT EXISTS. It is equivalent, because `insertMatch` refuses the same line in its own statement.

### Not in scope

- **Debits.** D712 still counts every unmatched posted debit.
- **The canvas layout** of F11 and F12: #1779's canvas PR.
- **#1782's registry count**, which reads `match=none` and needs no change.
- **Personal Mercury** (#1778, D713).

### Rollback

Revert the PR. Migration 483 only adds a table, an index and a trigger, and its rows are inert once the code that reads them is gone.
