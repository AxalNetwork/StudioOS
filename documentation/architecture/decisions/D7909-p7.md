Independent review found that a matching acknowledgement body did not by itself
prove timely arrival across restart. The listener now records acknowledgement
arrival time and classifies a late positive delivery before its first durable
acknowledgement write. The outstanding timeout correction survives a subsequent
failure or restart. A genuinely timely acknowledged completion still survives
later cleanup. Older positive journals without timing evidence cannot retire or
claim timely completion merely because the body matches. Negative test truth and
independent removal evidence keep their existing meanings.

The follow-up also distinguishes ACK arrival from returned journal durability.
The first ACK write is provisional; only after it returns is its completion time
recorded. A crash during or immediately after that write leaves no finalized
timing proof, so restart conservatively retains or times out the positive result.
This can sacrifice a timely but unfinalized completion; it cannot manufacture
timely durability from a pre-write timestamp. The final timing marker has its own
synchronous write, whose physical completion cannot be atomically proved by a
timestamp stored within that same write. That remaining completion-marker/host
failure ambiguity is conditional on filesystem behavior and independent whole-job
provider truth; it is not a claimed hard whole-path bound.

They interrupt immediately after acknowledgement persistence, restart cleanup
with a different process origin, and verify both authority result and journal
retirement. A legacy-journal regression checks conservative recovery. No measured
bound for synchronous fsync or actual-node qualification is claimed.
