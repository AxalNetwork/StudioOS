## D793 — AI tokens are never "credits", and Perks credits stay separate

- **Status:** asked by the owner on 2026-10-20 and filed by S1 on #2218, as part of D785 (#2210), D-c. Built by S25.
- **Amends:** D755's wording on HQ's Token packs and AI allowances pages. No route, store or figure changes.

### What the owner asked

> remember 'Perks' credits aren't the same as Token credits

### The rule

- **AI tokens** are a dollar balance spent on AI runs, held in `ai_credit_ledger` (D755). On screen they are "AI tokens", "token balance" and "Buy tokens". The word "credits" never names them.
- **Perks credits** (D608, D638) are claimed for perks, held in `perk_credit_ledger`. On screen they stay "credits", on Perks and the credit pages only.
- **They never share a balance, a ledger, a conversion or a screen line.**

Code identifiers stay as they are: `ai_credit_ledger`, `aiCredits.ts`, the purchase state `credited` and `credited_at`. Only what a person reads changes.

### What was there

The member's Usage page, the rail's Usage box, the cart, checkout and the pack receipt and invoice lines already said "tokens". HQ and the Worker still had twelve strings that called adding tokens "crediting":

- **HQ › AI allowances › Token packs** (`pages/hq/TokenPacks.jsx`, `tokenPacksModel.js`):
  - the purchase state "Credited";
  - "A pack bought before stays credited.";
  - "Paid over an hour ago, not credited";
  - "No paid purchase is waiting to be credited.";
  - "HQ never credits a purchase. Stripe's payment_intent.succeeded credits it; …".
- **HQ › AI allowances** (`pages/hq/PlatformAllowancesPage.jsx`): "A member is credited on what they pay;" and "Nothing here credits a purchase."
- **The Worker's pack errors** (`services/aiTopups.ts`, `services/aiCreditReversals.ts`): "AI top-up credit unreadable", "AI credit reversal webhook is not configured", "AI top-up not credited yet; reversal retried" and "AI credit reversal unreadable" (twice). These are thrown in Stripe's webhook and land in its log and the Worker's, not on a member's screen. They are reworded so every sentence about packs says tokens.

No member-facing pack sentence, email or receipt said "credit". The pack's invoice line was already "$25 of tokens — added to your AI top-up balance" (`services/orders.ts`, `tokenPackLineWords`).

### What was decided

1. **The twelve strings now say tokens.** The purchase state reads "Added", and the four Worker errors name AI tokens.
2. **HQ's one separation line stays.** HQ › Credit rules says "AI tokens are not perk credits. They are on AI allowances › Token packs." (D755, `CREDIT_RULES_LINE`). It is the only place the two words meet, and it says they are not the same, so the guard lets that exact sentence through and no other.
3. **A guard holds the rule:** `frontend/test/ai_tokens_not_credits_d793.test.mjs`.
   - It reads every string literal, template and JSX text from each file's syntax tree (Vite's `parseAst`), so comments and identifiers never count. A lowercase string with no space is a key or a code, never a sentence, and is skipped.
   - **AI-token surfaces never say "credit", "credits", "credited" or "crediting".** The files:
     - Usage (`AccountUsage.jsx`, `usageModel.js`, `lib/aiTokens.js`);
     - the rail (`AiBudgetUsage.jsx`, `WorkerRail.jsx`);
     - the cart and checkout (`TokenPacksSection.jsx`, `ProductsPage.jsx`, `CheckoutPage.jsx`, `CheckoutConfirmationPage.jsx`);
     - HQ (`TokenPacks.jsx`, `tokenPacksModel.js`, `PlatformAllowancesPage.jsx`, `HqAiCards.jsx`, `hqAiModel.js`);
     - the Worker's `aiCredits.ts`, `aiTopups.ts` and `aiCreditReversals.ts`.
     - "Accredited" and `credited_at` do not match.
   - **Perks surfaces never say "AI tokens", "token balance" or "Buy tokens":** `PerksPage.jsx`, `lib/memberCredits.js`, HQ's `MemberCreditsPage.jsx` and `CreditRulesRegistryPage.jsx`, and `lib/creditRulesRegistry.js`.
   - **No Worker file reads both `ai_credit_ledger` and `perk_credit_ledger`,** so no route can return a figure mixing the two balances.
   - A new page for either balance joins its list, including D789's and D792's when they land.

### Not changed

- Introduction credits (`intro_credit_ledger`) and Stripe's proration credits on Billing are neither AI tokens nor Perks credits. They keep their words.
- Every route, column, store and figure.

### Verified

- **On `main` the guard fails, naming the twelve strings above.** With this change it passes, as do `hq_token_packs_d755`, `hq_ai_allowances_d776`, `usage_top_ups_d755` and `ai_tokens_d755`.
- **Mutation checks, each caught:**
  - "Credited" put back in `tokenPacksModel.js`;
  - "AI tokens" added to Perks' allowance line;
  - "AI credit reversal unreadable" put back in the Worker;
  - the separation line's exception removed.
