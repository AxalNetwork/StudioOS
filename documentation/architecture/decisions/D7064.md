### What the canvas decides

- The decision Sheet puts "Accept · 1 credit" beside the spendable balance and the balance after. Decline is free.
- Refresh credits only reads the balance. Find new matches is a separate action the member starts.
- The Sheet shows each side's answer apart: unanswered, your agreement only, the other's only, both agreed, the other side never asked (no mirror row), closed after decline, and expired while open.
- Both agreed means both answers were received. Made is a separate recorded completion, and the outcome is separate again, or Not recorded.
- On a closed pair Accept is hidden. A stale attempt says "0 credits used" once the credit history confirms it; until then the balance reads Provisional.
- Balance states: value, refreshing, zero, Not recorded, Unavailable with Retry. A lost response gets Check status, which reconciles the same action.
- The viewer sees only their own matching preference (Off by default), with a link to its setting. A failed matching read never implies that nobody fits.
- Founder, investor, advisor and partner read the same state words. Private referral terms stay with the recording member.

### Backend required

- Mutation-free reads: a list read, a balance read or a retry creates no matches, mirrors or notifications (#7064).
- Mutual opt-in: a reciprocal card needs both people's current opt-in; today only the other person's is checked (#7064).
- Closed-pair eligibility at charge time: Accept refuses with nothing spent after expiry, after the other side declines or expires, or when either opt-in is off (#7064).
- Safe retries: `spent: 0|1` on a 409; a separate search action; a generic eligibility read (#7064).
- Synthetic pools of 101 and 500 candidates that assert the same eligible set; today the candidate read stops at 500 rows with no order (#7064).
