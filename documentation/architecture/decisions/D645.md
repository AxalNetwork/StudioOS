## D645 — The founder workspace against the Axal Venture Creation System: build the four cycles, drop the Venture Core pages

Epic #1440 (Session 1). On 2026-10-07 the owner asked Session 1 to compare the Axal Venture Creation System blueprint with the founder workspace. This file records what the comparison found, what the owner decided, and where each part was built.

### What the comparison found (2026-10-07)

- **The stage order matched.** The founder sidebar ran Validate, Build, Raise, Grow, as the blueprint does.
- **The centre of the blueprint was missing.** No loop carried a hypothesis through a sprint to evidence, a decision and a next commitment. The pieces existed separately:
  - a claim's text;
  - interview evidence;
  - a Proceed / Pivot / Stop verdict for the venture, which nothing else read.
- **Nothing fed back from a later stage:**
  - the Validate desk's users and revenue signals were stubbed (`routes/progress.ts`, reason `no_metrics`);
  - investor feedback was free text;
  - product feedback had no store.
- **Of the blueprint's 26 modules,** about half were present, several were partial or Spin-Out Lab only, and five were missing outright:
  - Experiments and evidence;
  - Solution design;
  - Product improvement;
  - Market expansion;
  - the Reach, Depth and Momentum grouping with its two bridges.

  The item-by-item table is in #1440's body.
- **Session 1's call then:** open four Spin-Out Lab tools to every founder: the ideal customer profile, market sizing, use of funds and readiness. The Lab keeps its programme: cohort, weekly plan, office hours and certificate.

### What the owner decided

- **2026-10-08.** The owner gave the full module list. Session 1 wrote six Claude Design prompts: navigation and Studio, the Venture Core, and one for each cycle.
- **2026-10-10, the cycles.** The four cycle prompts were answered by the cycle canvases (`Cycle Overview.dc.html` and its boards, #1469). D661 (#1474) built the four cycle overviews and the 26 module pages from the shared cycle reads (#1472, #1475, #1476, #1477).
  - Ideal customer profile, Market intelligence, Use of funds and Fundraising readiness are modules of every founder's cycles, so Session 1's call on the four Lab tools is met.
- **2026-10-10, the rest.** What the later prompts added to the canvases read as noise, so the owner withdrew those prompts and nothing is built from them:
  - "More in this module";
  - "Sent to other cycles" and the bridges;
  - the sidebar states;
  - the history and Venture Core pages;
  - the Studio redraw.

  The Venture Core pages and a Studio redraw are dropped unless the owner asks again. `/studio` stays as built (D564).
- **What was left** was three smaller prompts and one task:
  1. **Clean the cycle canvas,** keeping one addition: "Interviews · [n]" beside Record a session.
  2. **A new-venture board for Build, Raise and Grow.** Only Validate had one.
  3. **The founder's profile:** one profile for the venture, what investors and the public see, and visibility.
  4. **#1539:** a door from the right module for the five tools that had none. Its Part 2 replaced the old tab bars `FounderWorkspaceTabs.jsx` drew on the editor pages with one back link to the module.
