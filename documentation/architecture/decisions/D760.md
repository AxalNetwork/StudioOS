## D760 — Every Workers AI model sits in one catalogue, priced exactly in each unit Cloudflare bills, with the 25% margin written once behind a billing switch left on cost

Status: Built. Date: 2026-10-19. Slot: S22. Issue: #2017. Part of D720 (#1874), task `A1` (the plan's D723). #1874's plan, §2 and §3, is the decision; this file records it as built.

Worker only. No screen draws a billed price yet: `billing` is 'cost' everywhere, and nothing is charged at `AI_MARKUP`.

- **How.** `developers.cloudflare.com` does not resolve from the build sandbox. Every table and model list below was read whole across overlapping chunks, then cross-checked:
  - the five sections of the pricing page connect row to row;
  - the models page's 81 entries were counted against its own "We found 81 models".
- **When.** Read on 2026-10-19:
  - the pricing page says "Last updated Oct 15, 2026";
  - the models page says "Last updated Aug 12, 2026".
- **What is committed.** `cloudflare-worker/test/fixtures/workers_ai_catalogue.json`: both dates, the pricing page's paid-only note, the unit names, and one row per model. Each row lists the URLs it was read from: the models page, the model's own page, the pricing page, and the dated changelog posts it quotes.

### As built

1. **`services/ai/catalogue.ts`.** One literal row per model, with these fields:
   - `id` and `name` (the models page's);
   - `kind`, Cloudflare's task name as printed ("Text Generation", "Text-to-Image", and so on);
   - `capabilities` (the page's badges) and `context_window`;
   - `status`: `ga`, `partner`, `beta`, `deprecated` or `not_found`;
   - `paid_only` and `partner`;
   - `prices`: decimal strings exactly as printed;
   - `docs_url`.

   No `group` or `is_new` field: those wait for the canvas export (D764 or D769).

   It also exports:
   - `isOffered`: status `ga` or `partner`, priced, and past the partner gate;
   - `refusalFor`, `partnerGated` and `defaultDemoted(env)`. `defaultDemoted` is true unless `AI_PAID_MODELS` is 'on'; D764 applies it.

   **The gate:** `AI_PARTNER_MODELS === 'on'` offers the eight new partner models. Nova-3 and Flux (D657, D658) are never gated.
2. **Units.** A price key is the unit the Worker measures, priced per `UNIT_SCALE[unit]`:
   - `tokens_in`, `tokens_cached`, `tokens_out`, each per 1M;
   - `audio_minutes` and `audio_minutes_websocket`;
   - `characters` (per 1k) and `images` (per 1M, ResNet-50);
   - `tiles` and `steps`;
   - `input_tile_steps` and `output_tile_steps` (FLUX.2 Dev);
   - `input_tiles` and `output_tiles` (FLUX.2 Klein 4B);
   - `first_megapixels`, `subsequent_megapixels` and `input_image_megapixels` (FLUX.2 Klein 9B).

   This task measures only the units the router already runs: tokens, and audio minutes from `audioMinutesFromBytes`. D761's later PRs measure the rest from the request, never from a browser.
3. **`services/ai/pricing.ts`.**
   - **`AI_MARKUP = 1.25`.** This is the only place the number is written, and its comment names the source: "owner's canvas AIRail · every page, rail_logic.js:74 usd(); D720 (#1874) §3".
   - **`billingMode(env)`** returns 'cost', always. D762 makes it read `AI_BILLING`.
   - **`multiplierFor(licence)`** is 1 for the `admin` licence (`aiLicenceKey`) and `AI_MARKUP` for everyone else.
   - **`costMicro(model, units)` and `billedMicro(model, units, multiplier)`** sum the printed strings as scaled integers and round up to a whole micro-USD once per run. FLUX.1 Schnell's tile is 52.8 micro-USD: five tiles cost 264, where rounding each tile would make 265. Billed rounds the multiplied total, never cost rounded and then multiplied.
   - **Cached input** is billed at the cached rate only when the provider reports cached tokens and the model prints a cached rate. Otherwise every input token is billed at the input rate.
   - **A refused unit.** A unit the model has no price for throws `model_unpriced`, rather than counting as free.
   - **`billedUnitPrice(raw, multiplier)`** is the exact product as a number: 0.293 × 1.25 is served as 0.36625.
4. **One source of prices in `aiRouter.ts`.**
   - `PRICE_USD_PER_1M_TOKENS`, `PRICE_USD_PER_AUDIO_MINUTE` and `PRICE_USD_PER_AUDIO_MINUTE_WEBSOCKET` keep their names, shapes and keys. Every figure is now read from the catalogue (`tokenPriceView`, `minutePriceView`), so a row the catalogue cannot price fails at load.
   - BGE Base EN v1.5 moves from 0.05 to its published 0.067. No other router price changed.
   - `estimateCostUsd` takes the run's cached tokens (`attempt.units.tokens_cached`). It has no zero path: a model with no row throws.
   - **`run()`** checks the primary and every fallback with `modelRefusal` before the cache, the budget or any call:
     - a model the catalogue cannot price is refused as `model_unpriced` (new in `RefusalReason`);
     - a model it does not offer is `model_not_offered`: beta, retired, or a gated partner;
     - a model it offers but no router price view holds is also `model_unpriced`, because its receipt would have nothing to be computed from.

     Every model `ROUTE` can reach passes with no switch set, and a test holds that.
5. **`aiRouter.test.mjs`** imports the router through `_ts-loader`. Its `new Function` loader and import stripper are gone, so later tasks import `services/ai/*` with no edit there. Its cases are unchanged.
6. **The reads (`routes/ai.ts`).**
   - **`GET /api/ai/catalogue`** (new, signed in) returns `billing`, `guard: { every_run: false }` (D763 sets it true) and the offered models, each with:
     - `kind`, `status`, `paid_only`, `partner` and `context_window`;
     - `cost` and `billed` unit prices, as numbers, for the caller's licence.

     Ordered by kind, with the router's safety model (`ROUTE.safety.model`, Llama Guard) last. Cloudflare has no "Safety" task, so the guard model is placed by its job.
   - **`/pricing`** keeps every field the rail reads, and adds `billing` and `rates`. `rates` holds `cost` and `billed` for every model in the three router views, in every unit, WebSocket rates included.
   - **`/rates`** keeps every row's fields and adds `cost` and `billed` to each, plus `billing`:
     - Nova-3's row gains `per_audio_minute_websocket_usd`;
     - Flux, priced only over a WebSocket, gets its own row and a name in `aiCatalog.ts` ("Flux").
   - The `admin` licence gets `billed` equal to `cost` on all three.
   - No client method is added: nothing in the frontend calls `/catalogue` yet.

### Departures, for review

- **The catalogue is not installed into D761's `factsFor` here.** `setModelFactsSource` would start the context-window skip and the reasoning shapes for every model the catalogue knows. That is router behaviour, D761's, not pricing. D761's PR 2, which stacks on this, installs it with its own tests. The catalogue already carries `kind`, `context_window` and `paid_only` for it.
- **`billedUnitPrice` multiplies exactly.** The issue says `parseFloat(raw) × multiplier`, but that gives 0.36624999999999996 for 0.293 × 1.25, not the 0.36625 the issue's test requires.
- **The canvas's display formula and the Worker are one digit apart.** `Number(x.toPrecision(4))` gives:
  - 0.3662 on the canvas's float product;
  - 0.3663 on the exact 0.36625.

  The check that the canvas's price strings come out of the billed numbers (D764 or D769) has to pick one rounding. The test pins both figures.
- **Four existing tests change how they read, not what they assert:**
  - **`ai_router_prices.test.mjs` and `interview_transcription.test.mjs`.** They read the price tables out of the source text, and there is no literal left to parse. They now read the module's tables; `interview_transcription` also calls the real `estimateCostUsd` instead of a copy.
  - **`aiRouter.test.mjs`.** It moves to `_ts-loader` (step 5).
  - **`ai_usage_reads_d641.test.ts`.** It counts the Flux row and checks the WebSocket rates. Its other assertions are unchanged.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/ai_catalogue_d760.test.mjs` has 15 cases, and 36 of 36 mutations were killed:
- **The fixture.** The catalogue equals the fixture: every id, status, field and raw price string, each row citing its sources, and the counts.
- **Offered and the gate.** The offered rule; the partner gate (only 'on' opens it, and Nova-3 and Flux are always offered); `defaultDemoted`.
- **The margin.** `AI_MARKUP` is defined in exactly one file under `cloudflare-worker/src` and `frontend/src`, nothing multiplies a price by a literal 1.25, and nothing charges it.
- **Exact decimals.** Rounding once per run, billed rounding over the multiplied total, cached input, and refused units and models.
- **One source of prices.** The router's tables are views of the catalogue, and every model `ROUTE` can reach is priced and offered.
- **Refusals.** `model_unpriced` and `model_not_offered` (a beta model) through `run()`, with no model called.
- **The reads.** `/catalogue`, `/pricing` and `/rates` serve `cost` and `billed` with WebSocket rates; the admin licence gets cost; all three need a session.
