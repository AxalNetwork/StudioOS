## D4630 — A cycle form's late Save answer cannot close or navigate another view (#4630)

- **Status:** Built for #4630, 2026-11-07. Frontend only.
- **Builds on:** D695 (the one `FormLightbox` for every cycle Add and Edit) and the `Lightbox` close paths.
- **No migration. No Worker change.**

### The problem

`FormLightbox` let Cancel run while `onSubmit` was pending. Cancel called `onClose`. Then the old save's answer called `onSaved` and `onClose` again.

In the cycle Add flow, `onClose` is `closeAdd`, and `closeAdd` calls `navigate(-1)`. So a slow save could go Back twice, or close a view the member had opened after cancelling. ChannelsTab's `onSaved` closes its editor, so it had the same fault.

### What was decided

1. **A pending Save holds the form open (option: prevent dismissal).**
   - All exits from the form go through one `dismiss` function: Cancel, the close icon, Escape, the backdrop, and the phone page's Back. `dismiss` does nothing while a write is in flight.
   - Cancel is disabled while the write is pending.
   - The footer replaces its note with "Saving. The form stays open until the save answers." (role `status`).
   - The form never says it cancelled anything. A write that has been sent cannot be taken back, so no control offers to.
2. **The answer belongs to the form instance that sent it.**
   - A route change still unmounts the form. For example: browser Back, a link, or a venture switch.
   - The write still lands on the server. But once the form has unmounted, its answer calls neither `onSaved` nor `onClose`. A success or a refusal arriving after unmount does nothing.
   - The next read of the page shows the saved row. No toast reports it, because the member has already left on their own.
3. **The form is keyed by venture.**
   - CycleRoute keys every Add and Edit form by `projectId` as well as by form or record.
   - So a venture change remounts the form instead of reusing it, and rule 2 then applies to the old instance.
4. **A refusal keeps the member's edits.**
   - When the form is still open, a refused write shows its sentence. The typed values stay, and Cancel works again. This is unchanged.

### Why not "close and run in the background"

That option would let the member leave while the write was in flight, and then report the result somewhere else. Two problems:

- No surface exists to report it on.
- A closed form invites the false reading that the write was cancelled.

Holding the form open for the few hundred milliseconds of a typical write is the smaller and more honest change.

### Tests

`frontend/test/form_stale_save_4630.test.mjs` mounts the real dialog with a deferred write. `onClose` pops a history stack, so "one navigation" means one pop.

1. **While the write is pending:**
   - Cancel, the close icon, Escape and the backdrop do nothing.
   - The footer says why.
   - The answer then closes the form exactly once.
2. **A refused write:**
   - the typed value survives;
   - the form can then be closed.
3. **A route change:**
   - The form unmounts and a newer form opens.
   - The old write's success calls nothing, and the newer form stays open.
   - A refusal arriving after unmount is just as inert.

Each test also runs on mutants of `FormLightbox.jsx` that must fail.
