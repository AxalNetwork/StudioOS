### What the canvas decides

- The fund page has a GP view and an LP view. The GP view leads with the next action; the LP view shows Your position and issued reports only (Investor LP Canvas I9, I10).
- A performance band sits on the fund page. For a GP of record who is not an LP, it comes from the latest quarter row until today's read exists.
- Real zeros stay zeros: an empty fund shows LPs 0 and Committed $0.00, and Invested Not recorded (Investor LP Canvas I11).
- `/funds/performance` stays the cross-fund summary, with the quarterly history, the NAV-and-called chart, calls, distributions and positions (Investor LP Canvas I12).
- IRR with no unique solution reads Unavailable with its own glyph, and is never estimated.
- The author view puts figures beside the letter: today's from `/page` and the period's from the matching `/history` row (Investor LP Canvas I14, I15).
- The LP reading view is typeset in the PDF's section order, with print, and shows figures as issued (Investor LP Canvas I16, I17).
- States follow Platform States (D5129); Eadwyn is the one rail (#5130).
