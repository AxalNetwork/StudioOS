### What the audit found

- Every lesson row said "Length not recorded": `CourseOutline.jsx` decides `lengthsSent` by whether the `duration_seconds` key exists, and the route always sends the key, with null. On the phone the row's length pill squeezed the titles into short columns.
- The lesson count appeared twice, in the header and again in the outline.
- The right column gave its space to No resources and an unavailable certificate: `LearningWired.jsx` always draws a Certificate card saying certificates are not issued, and `CourseOutline.jsx` draws "No resources" when the list is empty.
- Start goes to the first lesson that is not ticked, optional lessons included, and the page does not use `learningResume.ts`'s rule (the last started lesson, otherwise the first required lesson not completed). The practical output sat near the end of About this course.

### What the canvas decides

- A band at the top puts the practical output (the 2,000-character decision memo and reusable claim register), the audience, "About 1 h 25 min", 7 required and 3 optional lessons, and Start lesson 1 together (LCO01, LCO10).
- Start opens the first required lesson; Resume follows the D5356 rule (3 of 7 required, lesson 6); Completed reads 7 of 7 (LCO01B, LCO01C, LCO13, LCO14). On the phone the sticky Start or Resume is the same control as on desktop.
- Every syllabus row says Required or Optional and its kind (reading, exercise, exam) in words with a restrained icon; module headers, a legend and an All · Required only filter (LCO03, LCO11, LCO12). The lesson count is drawn once.
- Lesson lengths: a length is shown only where one is recorded; none recorded means no badge on any row and no invented estimate (LCO04).
- Resources appear only when some are attached; nothing about certificates on the overview; five outcomes shown three at a time with Show all 5, and prerequisites, with the cases where nothing is recorded (LCO05, LCO06, and on the phone LCO16).
- Author preview of the draft: a banner, Draft on the band and in the details, the draft exam marked on its row, and Edit in Studio (LCO02, LCO15).
- States: loading, Unavailable with Retry, no such course, no lessons, no required lesson, first required lesson not published (LCO07, and on the phone LCO17); focus order, accessible names, headings and wrapping at desktop, 520px and 390px (LCO08); the route on LCO00 and the handoff on LCO09.
