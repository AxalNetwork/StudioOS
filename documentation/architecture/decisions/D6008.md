- **Status:** built 2026-11-15 to the Validate v2 Discovery and ICP canvas (D5995, boards V27, V28 and V29 H), drawn as owner pick A for both of its guide questions. Those two picks are still pending the owner (below).
- **Builds on:** `services/cycleValidate.ts` (`loadHypotheses`, `claimsOf`, `themesOf`), `services/aiRouter.ts` (`quoteRun`, `run`, `assistOff`), the signed-receipt pattern of D1241's Compare note, and the Talking points drawer (D685 §5.2).

### What is built

- **Store** (migration `202611152338_6008_interview_guides.sql`, additive). `interview_guides` keeps one guide per venture, saved whole, with a `version` that a save must name. `interview_guide_copies` keeps the copy taken onto one interview and which of its questions were asked.
- **Worker** (`routes/interview_guide.ts`, mounted at `/api/cycles/validate/interview-guide`; `services/interviewGuide.ts`):
  - The read lists the venture's open hypotheses, the themes no interview has judged, the active segments, the guide, and whether AI assistance is on.
    - An open hypothesis is one that is not retired and not yet supported or refuted, the rule the recording's Talking points use.
    - An unjudged theme is a pain theme whose severity no interview recorded. The read lists at most six.
  - With no open hypothesis, the read returns three default discovery questions. The page labels them as defaults, and they are saved only if the founder keeps them.
  - A save sends the whole list with the version it read. A stale version is refused with 409 `guide_changed`, and nothing is overwritten.
  - The server decides who wrote each question:
    - A new or edited question is the founder's.
    - A question is Eadwyn's only if it came from an accepted draft and its text is unchanged.
  - Eadwyn's draft:
    - `POST /draft/quote` returns the hold and runs nothing.
    - `POST /draft` must carry that quote, and the run is capped at it.
    - The answer is checked before it is shown. Each question must sit under a listed hypothesis or theme, end with a question mark, and not be a leading "would you pay" question.
    - Nothing is stored. The draft carries an HMAC receipt valid for 15 minutes.
    - `POST /draft/accept` adds only the questions picked from that receipt.
  - The copy: `POST /copy/:interviewId` takes the copy the first time and answers that same copy after. `PUT /copy/:interviewId/asked` changes the copy, never the venture's guide.
  - Access:
    - Reading is the founder board's audience (`canReadBoard`).
    - Every write, the draft included, needs `canWrite` and refuses a support session.
    - AI off for Customer research refuses only the draft, with the switch's words. The guide stays writable by hand.
- **Rail:** option `draft:validate/interview-guide` on Founder · Validate · Customer research. The draft runs `workspace_explain` and is recorded against `AI_SURFACE.validateInterviews`. `RAIL_OPTION_JOBS` maps the option to VA5, Validate's interview writing on the 70B, so no model moves.
- **Frontend:**
  - `pages/cycles/InterviewGuide.jsx` draws the guide:
    - questions grouped by hypothesis code and claim, then by theme;
    - edit, remove, move up and down, and add;
    - Eadwyn's draft row: segment, hold line, and Discard, Edit, Accept on each proposal;
    - AI off; Unavailable; a failed draft; a reader with no tools.
  - It appears in two places:
    - as a section of Record a session, opened on demand;
    - as Customer research's Prepare questions sheet (`PrepareQuestions`).
  - `pages/founder/RecordingGuide.jsx` is the guide while recording, opened from a Guide button beside Points. It shows one question per card, with Mark asked, Skip and a pager. Targets are at least 44px, and Mark asked is 52px.
  - The rules live in `interviewGuideModel.js`.

### Not built here

- Copying the guide onto an interview at Start. The copy is taken when the guide is first opened during the recording, so a session where the founder never opens it gets no copy.
- Reordering by drag. Reordering is done with Move up and Move down, which work on a phone and from the keyboard.
