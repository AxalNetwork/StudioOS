### 1. The decision

A member sees the programmes they can join on their Studio page and applies from one page. That page also lists what they applied to, with its status, and lets them withdraw. Every count and date on it is read from D812. A figure that is not recorded says so; no bracketed placeholder ships.

### 2. As built

**D2, the card** (`pages/productTesting/ProductTestingCard.jsx`):
- It reads `GET /api/testing/summary` and shows "Test new products before they launch", "*n* programmes open" (singular for one), and "See programmes", which opens `/product-testing`.
- It draws a "You're in: *product*" badge for each programme in `picked`.
- Its states are loading and "Unreadable" with Try again.
- **Where it sits:** the founder's in the right column of `FounderStudioBody`, above the profile zone. The investor's and the advisor's go under their last grid. The operating partner's goes above P2, which is a `RailFrame` of its own.
- **A role preview draws nothing.** The summary is the signed-in account's, so an administrator previewing a Studio page would see their own counts as the member's.
- **The founder's phone Studio** (`StudioPhone`, D701) draws no tiles at all, so it has no card. A founder on a phone reaches the page from the account sheet.

**D3, `/product-testing`** (`pages/productTesting/ProductTestingPage.jsx`):
- **Tabs, in the URL:** `?tab=` is `open` (the default) or `applications`. A tab change replaces the URL.
- **Open programmes:** one card per programme in `GET /api/testing/programmes`. Each shows:
  - the product, with its first letter as the mark (no route serves a logo key yet);
  - who runs it, with a "Partner" pill on a partner's;
  - what testers do, the time needed, the testing dates and the close date;
  - places left ("8 of 20 places left") and who can apply;
  - Apply.
- **Apply is held, with its reason, when it cannot succeed** (`applyBlock`, in `apply`'s order):
  1. already applied ("You have applied: Applied.");
  2. closed, or past its close while the page was open;
  3. not open to the caller's role;
  4. every place taken.

  The Worker lists only programmes the caller can join, so a held Apply is one that changed while the page was open. The page never decides what is allowed; `apply` checks every condition again.
- **The application sheet** (a `Lightbox`, full screen on a phone) asks:
  - each of the programme's questions (required, at most 2000 characters, as the Worker takes them);
  - availability (optional, 1000);
  - the required consent switch "Email me about this programme", under which the Worker's own consent sentence and version (`consent`) are shown.

  Submit waits for every answer and the consent, and sends `consent_email: true`. A refusal shows the Worker's sentence and keeps the sheet open. Once applied, the sheet closes, a note says the result comes in the bell, and both lists are read again.
- **My applications:** each application with its testing dates, the date applied and its status pill (Applied, Picked, Not this time, Withdrawn). Withdraw is on an applied or picked one only (`withdrawable`), as the Worker allows. A refusal shows on the row.
- **Every list:** loading, "Unreadable" with Try again, and empty.
- **Desktop and phone:** two columns of cards from `xl`, one column on a phone (`usePhoneWidth`), with the same tabs and sheet.
- **The rail:** the page is a `RailFrame` with `WorkerRail workspace="Product testing"`. Its key is `<Profile> · Product testing`, an off-canvas entry in `SHARED` (`lib/railPages.js`), so every member profile's D781 render check renders it. No `RENDER_NOT_YET` line is added.

**Where it is reached from:**
- **The route** is in `App.jsx`, guarded to every signed-in role: admin, founder, partner, investor, advisor and exploring. D812's programmes name the roles that may apply; for anyone else the page holds Apply and says why.
- **The account menu:** a "Product testing" row after Events, on the desktop dropdown and on the phone account sheet. **No sidebar row:** no canvas draws one. The command palette indexes sidebar rows and the H35 placement map (D284, D563), so it gains nothing either.
- **The bell:** D812's `tell` now passes `link: MY_APPLICATIONS_LINK` (`/product-testing?tab=applications`) on the member's three notices: `testing_applied`, `testing_picked` and `testing_not_picked`. The bell, the inbox and any later digest open My applications. The two notices to a programme's creator name no page until D815 draws the partner's.
