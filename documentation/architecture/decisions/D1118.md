## D1118 — Partner invitations: "Adjust terms", per-invitation terms for Deal-sourcing revenue share and Custom (PI4) (#2998)

- **Status:** built by S09 for S11's task, 2026-10-26 (#2998, under #2370 and D923).
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `f593052d66`.
- **The owner's answer (#2998, 2026-10-26): A.** The Super Admin sets an invitation's own terms in New invitation, and setting them is the approval. An Admin's invitation goes with the standard terms. O-TERMS is A, no bonuses: the signed terms and caps state everything HQ pays.
- **Migration 634** (`634_partner_invitation_terms.sql`) adds three columns to `partner_invitations`:
  - `terms_json`: NULL is the standard terms; otherwise a JSON object, checked by the database;
  - `terms_set_by`: the Super Admin who set them;
  - `terms_set_at`: when.

  Nothing is backfilled: every existing invitation went with the standard terms.

### 1. What the terms are

The four values PI4 draws, for Deal-sourcing revenue share and Custom only:

| Value | Stored as | Bounds |
|---|---|---|
| Rev-share percentage | `revshare_bps`, basis points, never a float | 0.01% to 50%, at most two decimals |
| Attribution window | `attribution_window_days` | 30 to 1,095 days |
| Term | `term_months` | 1 to 60 months |
| Terms note | `note`, or null | up to 500 characters |

`cloudflare-worker/src/services/partnerInvitationTerms.ts` parses the input and reads the stored value. A stored value the writer would not have written reads as `unreadable`, never as the standard terms.

### 2. Who sets them, and the audit

`POST /api/admin/partners/invitations` takes an optional `terms`. Every check runs before anything is written or sent:
- **Not the Super Admin** (`isSuperAdmin`, which is never true on a branch): 403 `super_admin_required`.
- **No type the terms apply to:** 400 `terms_not_applicable`.
- **A figure out of bounds:** 400, with the field's code (`terms_revshare_invalid`, `terms_window_invalid`, `terms_term_invalid`, `terms_note_invalid`, or `terms_invalid` for a malformed object). Each code has one fixed sentence that names the bounds and never echoes the value sent.

The audit:
- `partner_invitation_created` now says `terms: 'own'` or `'standard'`.
- With own terms, a second row, `partner_invitation_terms_set`, records the approval. It names the invitation and the offers the terms reach, never a figure or the note.

### 3. Where the terms go

`buildProposals` (`services/partnerDeals.ts`) takes the invitation's terms:
- **Deal-sourcing revenue share:**
  - the percentage replaces the standard 5% on founder-sourced deals;
  - the window replaces 365 days;
  - the term replaces 12 months;
  - the note is added to the terms and the contract excerpt;
  - **the LP capital line stays the standard 10%.** PI4 draws one percentage, so it is read as the founder-sourced share. The owner can change that reading.
- **Custom:** a revenue-share line with the window, the term, and the note as its description and outline.
- **Every other type:** unchanged.

The terms reach the partner in three places:
- **The invitee's offers** (`POST /partners/onboard/:token/propose`).
- **The chosen deal** (`…/select`). Its `proposal_json`, which the agreement is built from, carries them.
- **The admin record's "offers the invitee saw"** (`GET /invitations/:id`).

Terms that cannot be read stop the offers with 503 `terms_unreadable`, and the record's offers block reads Unreadable. They never fall back to the standard terms.

### 4. The page

- **New invitation (PI4), for a Super Admin:**
  - When Deal-sourcing revenue share or Custom is picked, an "Adjust terms ▾" section appears, closed until opened, with the canvas's four fields and its note.
  - Once open, every figure is required: a blank is never read as 0, and Send waits with the field's sentence. Closed again, nothing is sent and the standard terms go.
  - The Worker checks every bound again.
- **For an Admin:** one sentence, "This invitation goes with the standard terms. Only the Super Admin sets an invitation's own terms." No field and no disabled control.
- **The invitation record:** "What we offered" says "Own terms: 12.5% revenue share · 180-day attribution window · 18 months", who set them and when, and the note. Otherwise it says "Standard terms.", or that the terms could not be read.
- **Refusals:** the Worker's sentence, in the dialog.

### 5. Not built

- **Editing terms after sending.** The owner's A sets them in New invitation only. A change means revoking and sending a new invitation.
- **A separate rev-share for LP capital.** PI4 draws one percentage (§3).
