### What the canvas decides

- **Remind (RAC16, RAC17):** filters for Not opened, Opened but not answered and Not delivered, counted out of the call's recipients. Eadwyn may pre-tick rows. The reminder is an in-app notice, not an email, and has a limit. The result names who was not reminded and why.
- **Export (RAC18, RAC19):** CSV or PDF, with Included and Withheld side by side. Amount headers read "Indicated (USD), not committed". Each export adds a dated row to the call. An empty call exports terms only; an unreadable part is named.
- **A new call from a closed one (RAC20, RAC21):** step 1 shows a copied banner. Terms and the data room carry over; the close date, counsel approval and deck version do not. Step 3 is the owner's and read only for a co-founder. Starting is blocked while another call is open, or when there is no closed call.
- **From response to commitment (RAC22, RAC23):** a confirm sheet over the existing pipeline write records Signed or Wired with a typed amount. Only that typed amount moves Commitments. Conflicts are drawn: already Wired, another currency, a blank amount, no pipeline line.
- **Indicated never becomes committed.** Matching investors are never named before they respond.
- **Eadwyn proposes; it never sends or records.**
- **States, phone and dark (RAC24 to RAC28):** loading, Not recorded, measured zero, unreadable reads, refused co-founder, Admin, Explorer, HQ counsel, `call_changed`, calls switched off, phone at 390 and dark.

### Backend required

All under #6053:

- A remind action, with `last_reminded_at`, a reminder count per recipient and the limit.
- CSV and PDF export routes and an export log.
- A source-call column on `round_calls`, for "Started from".
- A call uid on the typed Signed or Wired line, linking it back to its response.

On `main` today `manageCall` accepts only `close` and `extend`, and there is no remind or export route. The build depends on #1008 item 19 and cannot be checked live while `ROUND_CALLS_ENABLED` is `"false"`.

### Pending the owner

Five questions, each drawn as option A; the owner has not answered. Q1: only the owner can remind and export. Q2: at most 2 reminders per recipient per call, at least 3 days apart. Q3: investor emails are left out of the export. Q4: recording a commitment moves the pipeline line from Soft to Signed or Wired. Q5: counsel is not notified of an export.

The filter chips and selection, the Remind and Export sheet layouts, the copied banner and carried-field marks, the block that sets indicated against recorded amounts, the confirm sheet over the existing write, the phone cards, sticky bar and full-screen sheets, and the dark tokens.
