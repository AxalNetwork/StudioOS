### What the canvas decides

- The word is **Question**, because "Ask for this report" already chases a late period.
- The reader gets a "Question about this figure" link on each figure row and an inline composer (600 characters, a privacy line, Cancel and Send question). A left-out figure is asked about through "Ask about the whole report".
- A question is private to the asking investor and the venture's founders. The investor sees "Your questions" with a state pill: Sent · not yet read, Read, Answered, Withdrawn. An unanswered question can be withdrawn.
- The founder's sent report shows a Questions section above the frozen report, with Unread, Open, Answered and Withdrawn cards, a 1,200-character reply, and figure chips that jump to their question. Eadwyn's draft reply cites the report's own source and as-of date.
- Needs you lists question rows after late periods and "? Asked" requests; the count chip counts every row. With no questions there is no row and no 0.
- After a correction, a question stays on its revision and the new revision links to it ("n questions on revision 1").
- Admins, advisors and partners can read the threads but not reply. A support session and a non-recipient are refused.
- Phone 390 (composer and reply as full-screen sheets with the action pinned) and dark tokens are drawn.
