### 5. Owner decisions

#2430's nine questions take their proposed (A) answers until the owner says otherwise:
1. Founders may share derived bank figures after the NDA. Never a balance, account, line or counterparty. This amends D840 §2.
2. Before the NDA, coverage only.
3. An investor in the raise is a data-room grantee, a round-call recipient or an invited investor.
4. Investors see each shared claim's state.
5. An accepted item shares After NDA by default. Bank-derived items and quotes start Private.
6. SCORING reads only items shared beyond Private.
7. There is no separate customer-evidence item. Product-market fit is READINESS's.
8. Stale after 90 days for metrics, 12 months for interviews and documents.
9. Founders accept and share. Admins read and leave a note.
