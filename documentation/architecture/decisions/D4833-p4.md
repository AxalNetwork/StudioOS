### What moved

D4829 built the Practice root and kept the zones as they were, and said Delivery's redesign was owned elsewhere. This part puts Delivery in that shell the way PX-06 draws it.

- **The header is PX-06's.** The shell heads `/practice/delivery` "Delivery" (the section row's label, which is the canvas's h1) with the canvas's own line, "Work products sent to clients, with every version kept." Before, every Practice zone printed the cockpit's sentence ("What needs attention, what is committed, and what has moved."). `AdvisorBucketRoutes` gains `PRACTICE_ZONE_HEAD`, which lists only Delivery: each other zone is owned by its own issue and keeps the bucket sentence until it moves.
- **One header primary: New work product.** It is `practice/delivery`'s first row in `advisorZoneActions.js`, a page-supplied handler (`newWorkProduct`) marked `primary`, so `ZonePrimary` portals it into the shell's header slot (D1424). It opens the v1 form and writes nothing. The body's own "Work products" heading and its toggle button are gone: the shell's header already says both.
- **The primary is there in every state of the list.** The toolbar now sits above `ZoneBody`, as on Sessions. While the list loads, or when it could not be read, the primary is drawn disabled and says why. The form's engagement picker is read after the list, so the form waits for the list. With an empty list (the first work product) it is enabled, beside the empty state's "Record a work product".
- **The form and its answer sit above the list.** The New work product form is drawn once, under the toolbar, in every state; it gains Cancel. The success or refusal note moved there too, so a refused v1 from the empty state is said, which it was not before.
- **The chips are drawn only over a list that was read and holds a card.** An empty or unread list shows no "Draft 0" (D56/D68).
- **PX-06 A's order.** Chips, the Ready line, the cards, then Post-session reviews. The reviews keep their own section outside `ZoneBody`, with their own states, so they stand with no work product and when the list cannot be read (D4712).
- **A card's client opens the Client Sheet** (D4831) on its Delivery tab, as PX-06 A draws the client as a link. A client with no Axal account has no sheet, and its name stays plain text.
- **Unreadable is Unavailable.** `ZoneBody` is given `what`, so a failed list read draws #5129's shared state (D4828): the Unavailable chip, the Worker's sentence, "Other sections are unaffected" and Try again, never an empty list.
- **Phone (390).** The section row scrolls sideways (D4834). Below 640px a card's Open and its client are 44px targets, the toolbar's chips and actions already are (#6860), and the detail sheet and both dialogs become full-screen pages (the shared `Sheet` and `Lightbox`).
- **Dark mode** uses the existing tokens; `check-dark-mode` passes.

### Departures from the canvas

- **The detail sheet has no Latest version / History tabs.** PX-06 B draws two tabs; the sheet keeps both sections on one scroll, so the history is never one tap away from the version it explains.
- **The header carries no secondary.** PX-06 draws none either. The PR3 ops, Bulk: nudge unopened and Export as client pack, stay in the toolbar beside the chips: they are working controls the v2 header does not draw.
- **The receipt tiles stay,** after the reviews and only over a list that was read. "Follow-ups from sessions" and "What Delivery cannot say" sit behind one disclosure ("What Delivery records, and what it cannot say"), as D4874 asks for long explanations.
- **Group by client stays** (D571's `?view=by_client`).
- **The shell's archetype badge** ("Collection") is the shell's, on every Practice zone; the canvas draws none.
- **Other Practice zones keep the bucket sentence.** Only Delivery is moved; Sessions is owned by its own redesign.
