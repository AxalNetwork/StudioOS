## D1567 — Notion and Google as deck sources, never graded Measured

- **Status:** built by S06 from S1's specification on #3814 (deck builder v2, Phase D, D6; plan on #3716).
- **Owner decision J:** B (2026-11-01). Notion and Google sources ship in Phase D. Any new external account or OAuth app registration stays an owner action.
- **Builds on:**
  - D896's `DECK_SOURCES` and `sourceAndGrade`;
  - the Notion connector (D1085–D1090), whose writers stamp what they land;
  - the Google Workspace connector (D1091–D1095), whose writers do the same.
- **Migration:** none.

### The question

Notion and Google Workspace already land a founder's pages, Docs, Forms answers and Sheets into the venture's own stores. The deck's source list (`GET /api/decks/sources`) did not show them as their own rows, so the one-click run could not bind them. One existing grade was also wrong. An interview whose only session was imported from a Notion page or a Google Doc counted as a recorded session, which graded the interview count **Measured**. A page someone typed is not a recording.

### What was decided

**Two new origins** in `sourceAndGrade`, the one place a store's origin becomes a label and a grade:
- `linked_document`: a Notion page, a Google Doc or a Google Forms answer, read through its connector. It maps to Document, **Sourced**.
- `linked_typed`: a Notion database row or a Google Sheets cell the founder typed. It maps to Typed, **Stated**.
- Neither can be Measured: a person wrote it, and the connector only carried it.

**Two adapters** in `services/decks/sources.ts`, `notionSource` and `googleSource`. Neither calls Notion or Google. Each reads, by the origin its writer stamps, what the founder's own connection already landed for this venture:

| Slot | Reads | Grade | Filled from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `validate.notion_interviews` | non-draft `interview_sessions` with `submitted_via = 'notion'` | Sourced | Validate › Customer research |
| `validate.notion_research` | `market_sources` with `kind = 'notion'` | Sourced | Validate › Market intelligence |
| `build.notion_design_notes` | `solution_design_notes` with `source_path` `notion/…` | Sourced | Build › Solution design |
| `grow.notion_content` | `content_calendar_items` with `source = 'notion'` | Stated | Grow › Distribution |
| `validate.google_doc_interviews` | non-draft `interview_sessions` with `submitted_via = 'google_docs'` | Sourced | Validate › Customer research |
| `validate.google_forms` | `demand_signals` with `source_kind = 'google_forms'`, confirmed or counted | Sourced | Validate › Demand signals |
| `build.google_design_notes` | `solution_design_notes` with `source_path` `google_docs/…` | Sourced | Build › Solution design |
| `grow.google_sheets_days` | `project_metrics` with `source = 'google_sheets'` | Stated | Grow › Revenue and traction |

- **Each item** carries its count, its as-of date (the newest landed row), a record reference and a snapshot hash, so Refresh can say when it moves.
- **Nothing landed** is `not_recorded`, never 0. Its `href` is the module whose "From Notion" or "From Google" panel fills it. A slot may now name its own area, module and link, because one connector feeds several modules.
- **Not counted:** another venture's rows, a draft session, and a Forms answer still "to confirm".
- **The interview grade:** in `interviews`, a session whose `submitted_via` is `notion` or `google_docs` no longer counts as recorded. An interview count resting on one is Sourced, as a hand-logged one is.
- **The Market card** (`newDeck.ts` `CARD_CATALOGUE`) also binds `validate.notion_research`. Interviews, Forms answers and Sheet metrics already reach their cards through `validate.interviews`, `validate.demand` and the revenue slots, so they are not bound twice.

**No client, route, or account.**
- The rows reach the screens through the existing `GET /api/decks/sources` (`SourcesBoards.jsx`), so nothing on the frontend changes.
- No new external account or OAuth app is needed. The adapters read what the existing Notion and Google Workspace connections already wrote, so the owner has nothing to register for this decision.
