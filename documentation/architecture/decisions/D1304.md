### 1. What the Overview draws

**The four figures**
- Accounts, with "+N in 30 days" and the deactivated ones "not counted".
- Active in the last 30 days, its share of the accounts, and those never seen.
- Email not verified, with those over seven days.
- Waiting on you: Explorers ready for a profile and open LP applications, with Open Approvals. It says that Product tester applications are not counted yet.

**The profile mix** is one bar of Founders, Investors, Advisors and Operating Partners, then Other (Explorers, Admins and anyone else), with View as table.

**A widget per profile**, in #2377's names and colours (G1):
- the count, "+N in 30 days", and eight weeks of sign-ups, the last week dashed to a hollow point because it runs to date;
- facts from the read:
  - for each profile, identity check approved and a profile at least 75% complete (`readiness.by_profile`);
  - for Investors, LPs among them;
  - for Explorers, the split (agreement not sent, sent and not signed, signed and ready, with Open the Exploring board);
- "View N in All accounts".

**Sub-account widgets**
- **LPs:** the count, the profiles they hold, and LP applications waiting, with Open.
- **Product testers:** "Not recorded" with D1301's reason.

The Admins line closes the widgets.

**Account readiness**
- The five steps, as a count and a share.
- A segment for All and for each profile, with View as table.
- If the Terms acceptances could not be read, that step says so, and the other four still draw.

**Product testers by profile** says why it is not recorded, and gives the LPs by profile.

**Newest accounts and Needs attention**
- Five rows each. Each row opens its record at `/admin/accounts/all/<id>`.
- A missing name, country or date says "not recorded"; a missing wait start goes last.
- A partial Needs attention names the kinds it could not read.
- Two kinds are listed as not counted yet: tester applications and limited access.

**How is this counted?** gives D1301's definitions.
