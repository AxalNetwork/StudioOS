## D6207 — The Overview's dated basis, read time, and NAV change since the last quarter-end (#6207)

Under #5311 (PF-01). The owner filed #6207 asking the Portfolio Overview to say what date its figures speak for, when they were read, and how NAV has moved since the last quarter-end.

**This reverses a recorded departure.** D4817 part 1 ("The as-of date") and part 3 ("No 'Up $X since 30 Jun' under the hero NAV") left the change line off the Overview on purpose: the hero is the latest book, not a quarter-end, so the change was stated only on Performance's series card, between two quarter-end reads. #6207 asks for the line, so it is now drawn. The line does not compare two quarter-ends. It compares the **latest book (the hero NAV)** with the book **read as of the last completed quarter-end**, and its words say so: "NAV up $1,400,000 since 30 Sep 2026 (quarter-end)". Performance's series card is unchanged and still states the change between its two newest quarter-ends.

**Worker.** `GET /api/positions/analytics` (`cloudflare-worker/src/routes/positions.ts`) adds two fields to every answer, from one clock read:
- `as_of_default`: the Worker's UTC date (`YYYY-MM-DD`), the date a read without `?as_of=` is computed for (IRR's terminal date);
- `read_at`: the ISO instant of the read.

`as_of` keeps its meaning: null unless the caller cut the read at an explicit date, the date asked otherwise. No figure changes. No new route, no schema change.

**Frontend.**
- `performanceOf` (`frontend/src/pages/portfolio/portfolioModel.js`) writes the as-of in this order. A read cut at a date says "As of 30 Sep 2026". A default read says "As of 14 Nov 2026 · latest recorded marks" from `as_of_default`. An answer with neither keeps D4817's "As of the latest recorded marks". The basis strip adds "Read at 14 Nov 2026, 10:32 UTC" from `read_at` (`dayTime`, read in UTC). D4817 part 1 left out the canvas's "read 14:30 UTC" because no read returned its time; this one does. The dates come from the Worker, never the browser clock. The frame's context line and the rail reuse `perf.asOf`, so they now carry the date too, on the Overview and on Performance.
- The quarter-end is `quarterWindowOf`'s `last` (`lastQuarterEndOf`): the reporting read's (`kpi-compliance`) `period_until` for its last completed quarter, which is the Worker's date. `quarterWindowOf` also needs the positions read to carry a first-cheque year. When the window is not `ok` (either read loading or failed, no completed quarter, no position) no quarter-end is known, and no extra read is made.
- Once that date is known, the Overview makes one extra read, `/api/positions/analytics?as_of=<quarter-end>`, through `usePortfolioReads` with `quarterShape(date)`, the same read path Performance uses (`NavChangeLine` in `InvestorPortfolioCanvas.jsx`, keyed by the date, so a reload reads it again). `navChangeOf` turns it into a point with `quarterPointOf` and draws the line only when **both** reads answered with a NAV:
  - the hero read failed, has no NAV, or holds no book: no line;
  - the quarter-end read failed, answered for another date, or is still reading: no line;
  - the quarter-end holds no book (`position_count` 0) or no NAV, which is the gap: no line.
  
  In every one of these cases nothing replaces the line: no number, no zero, no "unchanged". A NAV equal at both ends reads "NAV unchanged since …".
- `PerformanceSummary` (`PortfolioParts.jsx`) takes an optional `change` node and draws it under the hero NAV. It is never drawn when the hero is Unreadable. Performance passes none.
- The hero's own Retry still re-reads only the hero. The quarter-end read has no separate Retry. It is re-read on the page's Refresh, which re-reads the reporting window it depends on.

**What the change means.** The line is a change in carrying value, gross of fees and carry, and it is not a return. A cheque written since the quarter-end raises the latest book at cost, so it counts in the change. The line's tooltip says so ("capital invested since counts in the change"). The multiples and IRR remain the measures of performance.

**Not built.** No Retry of its own for the quarter-end read. No change line when the reporting cadence is monthly: `quarterWindowOf` asks for a quarterly period, and the Overview reads the quarterly default.
