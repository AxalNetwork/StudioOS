### What the canvas decides

- Every amount is formatted from stored cents with two decimals. $99.50 – $99.99 stays exactly that in the form, the saved detail and a reopened form; main shows 100 and 100 today, and saving unchanged would store $100.00 – $100.00.
- The comparable range, the catalog price, the comparables count and the run date or age are four labels that never merge.
- List rows add the range, count, scope and age (as a word and a shape). A service line with none reads No reading yet.
- The form reads metric or service, low and high USD, comparables, run date and scope. The Service line picker appears only when the member has service lines.
- Recording a fresh reading starts with both amounts blank and creates a new record. A stale reading (138 days, Blocked) offers it.
- Inline validation under each field: reversed range, missing amount, too many decimals, a count of 0. Inputs stay as typed, and a failed save keeps them. A true $0.00 – $0.00 is a value.
- Age bands and a thin base (fewer than 6 comparables) are explained in plain words. A count names no engagements and shows no demand.
- A CSV export of readings, and the Eadwyn summary to the cent. Investors see no readings (RS-17).
