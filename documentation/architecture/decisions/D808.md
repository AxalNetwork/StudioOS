## D808 — The Licences canvas's additions to the Administrator's account: the contract in force and pending, its PDF, Needs you, late usage reports with Send now, and the IP licensor as payee

- **Status:** decided by S1 for the owner, 2026-10-20 (#2250; part of D795, #2228). It extends D798, which merged as #2239 before the Licences canvas reached it. It shows payment instructions and adds a write, so S1 reviews it for security before the owner merges.
- **Basis:** `main` at `62e31e22db`, with D797's branch (#2242) underneath. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Builds on:**
  - D798: the Administrator's contract, statements and payment, scoped to the caller's own licence.
  - D797: the licence's terms schedule (`licence_terms`, migration 512), including `licensor_ip_entity` and `usage_report_day`.
  - D266: a branch reports its usage to HQ every morning (`pushUsageReport`).
  - D127: a branch counts its seats used by role.

Every route stays under `/api/licence/mine/*` in `routes/licence.ts`, and resolves the caller's own licence first, as D798 §1 says. The work is in `services/licenceAccount.ts`; Send now is in `services/usageReport.ts`.

### 1. `in_force` and `pending` on `GET /mine/contract`

The Summary card draws both: "Sent 12 Oct · waiting for your signature / In force: v6.0, signed 2 Mar 2025".

- **`in_force` is the latest signed contract, superseded or not.** Drawing a new contract supersedes every earlier row, the signed one included (the contract create in `routes/admin_licences.ts`). "Not superseded" therefore cannot be what ends a signed contract's force: while v6.1 waits on a signature, v6.0 is superseded and still in force. Only a later signature ends it.
- **`pending` is the current contract while it is `sent`,** with `waiting_on_you` when the signing link is the caller's own (D798 §2's `sign_url`).
- **`earlier` and `contract` stay** as D798 gave them.

### 2. `GET /mine/contract/:uid/pdf`

- **The stored body, never the template.** `body_md` goes to `paperModel` with `merge: 'raw'` and `preserveBody`, and the PDF is drawn by `renderLegalPdf`, the renderer the generated documents use (Browser Run, falling back to pdf-lib).
- **A sent or signed contract prints as issued;** a draft prints as a draft.
- **Scoped like the dispute:** the row is read with `uid = ? AND licence_uid = ?`, so another licence's contract answers 404 as an unknown uid does.
- **On a branch** it answers 501 `contract_hq_only`: the contracts are HQ's.

### 3. `GET /mine/needs`

One cheap read for the Summary's "Needs you" and the Licences row's count pill (D800, #2233). It returns `items` in this order, each with its `link` under `/admin/my-licence`:

1. the current contract while it is sent for the caller's own signature;
2. each HQ notice addressed to the caller on this licence and still `issued` or `overdue`;
3. each `issued` statement paid less than it owes;
4. on a branch only, each seat type with 90% or more of its licensed seats used. Seats used are counted as D127 counts them: active accounts by role.

`count` is the pill's number. Each source is read on its own; one that cannot be read is named in `unavailable` with its reason, and `complete` is false. It is never read as nothing to do.

- **On a branch** the contract, the statements and HQ's notices (`admin_notices`) are HQ's tables, so they are listed in `unavailable`, and only the seats are checked.
- **It never pulls the branch's licence copy** (D244). The pill reads it on every page.

### 4. Usage reports by month, and Send now

**The reports are quarterly; the canvas's rows are months.** A branch reports its previous and current quarter every day (D266), upserted per licence, period and stream, so `reported_at` is the latest push only.

`usage_months` lists the last three completed months. Each one is:

- **`sent`, with `sent_at`,** once a report for its period (its quarter, or the month itself if one is ever stored by month) is stamped after the month ended. `sent_at` is the latest stamp. The first report after the month is not stored, because the next push overwrites it.
- **`late`** when no such report exists after `due_on`.
- **`due`** before that.

`due_on` is day `usage_report_day` (D797) of the next month. When the terms record no day, it is day 5, and `usage_report_day_recorded` is false.

**Send now:**

- **`send_now` is false on HQ,** with the reason: HQ keeps the ledger the report feeds.
- **On a branch, `send_now` is true.** `POST /mine/usage-reports/send` sends the report the 05:10 UTC run sends (`pushUsageReport`, through the existing `reportUsage` RPC).
- **The throttle:** at most one send per five minutes per branch, through `RATE_LIMITS`, as the licence pull is limited (D244).
  - The window is written before HQ is called, so a slow push cannot be pressed twice.
  - A missing or unreadable throttle store refuses, because an unlimited button means two calls to HQ per press.
- **On a branch the statements stay HQ's.** `/mine/statements` still answers `available: false`, now with `send_now: true`.

### 5. The payee is the platform IP licensor

D797 splits the licensor into `licensor_ip_entity` (platform IP) and `licensor_brand_entity` (trademark and brand). The territory licence fee is the price of the platform IP licence, so its payee check reads `licensor_ip_entity`.

- When it names anyone other than Holdings, `payment` is the refusal `licensor_not_payee`, as D798 §4 described.
- The brand licensor is never the payee.

**The read** is `accountTerms`. It takes the two terms off the stored `licence_terms` row rather than D797's field list, so it holds while that list changes. On a database without migration 512 the read fails. That is not "not recorded": the route then gives no refusal and the default report day.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/licence_account_d808.test.ts` checks:

- `in_force` and `pending` together, in force only, and pending only;
- the PDF is the stored body, and another licence's contract is refused;
- `/mine/needs` lists exactly the open items in order, nothing for a clean licence, and seats on a branch only;
- a usage month is late after the day and sent with a stamp after the month;
- `send_now` is false on HQ, and Send now is limited on a branch;
- the payee refusal reads `licensor_ip_entity`, not the brand licensor.

Each assertion is mutation-checked.
