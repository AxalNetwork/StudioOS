## D5513 — Public claims: partner verification and the 28-day programme, proposed (#5513)

- **Status:** proposed, 2026-11-16, slot S21. Waiting on the owner's answer on #5513. Not approved wording until then.
- **Record:** `documentation/product/CLAIMS_5513.md` traces every occurrence, the evidence on `main`, and the open questions.

### What was wrong

- **"Verified" partners.** The homepage calls the partner network "verified" and the public directory offers a "KYB-verified partners only" filter. No partner verification is recorded for the network:
  - the directory lists a partner on an admin's `directory_listed` approval;
  - no table holds a KYB status;
  - the directory read returns `kyb_verified: false` and ignores the filter.

  Only formation partners (#6692) have a recorded check: licence, data agreement, who checked it, when, and a review date.
- **"Idea to an incorporated company in 28 days".** 28 days is the programme's length (`COHORT_WEEKS = 4`). The incorporation milestone is recorded when the incorporation order is paid (D585), not when a company is registered. Registration depends on eligibility, the founder's own steps and the jurisdiction.

### Proposed

- **Partners:** "verified" comes off the hero. The pillar says listings are approved before they are listed. The directory's KYB filter is removed until a KYB status is recorded.
- **28 days:** the public copy says it is a 28-day programme from idea to an incorporation order, and that registration timing depends on eligibility and the jurisdiction. Statements of the programme's length are unchanged.
- **Not changed here** (recorded as owner questions in the record):
  - the fixed outcome figures in `spinoutFundModel.js` (37 graduates, 86% on time, $8.6M);
  - the named Demo Day testimonials;
  - the admin catalogue's seeded purpose (migration 560).
