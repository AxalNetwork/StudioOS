### 1. The "N waiting" chip on the profile header's Ask

**The per-page read exists, and the chip already uses it.** The issue left two ways to close this: wire the count to D1442's per-page read, or remove the dead code if no such read exists. The read exists, and it is mounted:
- the Worker route is `GET /api/ai/rail/proposals?page=<rail page>&status=proposed` (`routes/ai_rail_proposals.ts`, D1442 §4);
- the client is `railProposalsApi.list({ page, status })`;
- the seam is `askPanelAdapter.waiting(page)`. It counts the member's open proposals on the page, and a failed read is off, never 0.

`ProfileHeaderAsk` (the Investor, Advisor and Operating Partner zone headers) has drawn `HeaderAsk` since D1443 part 5. `HeaderAsk` is `PageAsk`, which reads `askPanelAdapter.waiting(ask.page)` and passes the count to the shared button. So the chip on the profile header already shows the real count from D1442. Nothing called `profileAskAdapter.waitingCount`. It and `ASK_WAITING` (`reason: 'proposals_by_page_unbuilt'`, `waitsOn: '#3706 (D1442)'`) were a placeholder that D1442's swap made dead.

**What changed.**
- `ASK_WAITING` and `waitingCount` are removed from `workspaces/profileAskAdapter.js`. Its comment now says where the count comes from. `ASK_PANEL` and `askAvailability` stay, because `headerAskGate` reads them.
- `profile_zone_headers_d1424` no longer tests the placeholder. It keeps the rule that an unread or zero count draws no chip.
- No second count was added. The header draws one count, from one read.

**What the chip shows.** With three open proposals on the page, the button's name is "Ask Eadwyn, 3 waiting". With none, or when the read fails, it reads "Ask Eadwyn" and draws no chip. It never shows "0 waiting".

### 2. The article writing panel moves into the Ask panel

**Before.** `pages/articles/ArticleWritingPanel.jsx` was an `<aside>` beside the editor (360px from 1024px, and its own bottom sheet on a phone). The article page also drew two hand-drawn Ask buttons, one in its header and one in the format bar. Above those, the rail's fallback `HeaderAsk` drew a third Ask, because the article header had not claimed the place.

**After.** The writing actions are a **page intent in the docked Ask panel**. It is wired the way `pages/email/EmailDraftIntent.jsx` wires an email draft:
- `pages/articles/ArticleWritingIntent.jsx` (the old file, moved with `git mv`) renders its body through `ui/AskIntent.jsx`. That component opens the Ask panel holding the intent and portals the body into the panel's slot, above the conversation.
- The intent has the eyebrow "Eadwyn · Articles · Writing", the title "Writing", AskIntent's Close, and the old footer line as its footer: "Nothing goes into your article until you press Insert or Replace. The text and image models are chosen in the AIRail."
- The page mounts the intent while `asking` is true, as `RecordCompose` does. Closing the panel (×, Escape, a route change, a detail sheet) ends the intent and calls its `onClose`, which sets `asking` back to false. A field's Ask with an option (Publish…'s blank search title or description) remounts the intent on that option, as `autoRun` did before.
- If no panel has drawn its slot yet, the region draws in place at the top of the editor column. It never draws beside the article.
- On a phone, the Ask panel itself is the bottom sheet at 92% (D1443 §3). The intent draws no sheet of its own.

**One Ask on the article.** `ArticleAsk` (exported from the same file) is the editor's only Ask button:
- It is the shared `AskButtonView`, test id `article-ask`.
- It claims the header place with `useHeaderAskClaim`, as the Pitch deck's `DeckAsk` does. The rail's fallback Ask therefore stands down, and so does D405's phone button (D1443 §10).
- It draws only while the Ask panel serves the viewer (`useAskPanel().available`). A support session, an Explorer, or a page another licence owns gets no Ask (AK13). The same gate decides whether Publish… offers its field Ask (`onAsk={canSendForReview && askServed ? openAsk : null}`).
- The format bar's second Ask is gone.

**Its "N waiting".** The count is the open proposals the intent has read on this article. The page now starts it at `null` instead of `0`, and resets it when the article changes. The intent reports `null` when its read failed and nothing else is open, so an unread count draws nothing.

**Unchanged.**
- **The charged-action quotes.** Each action's hold is quoted once on open, with the same stand-in inputs as before. It reads `holdsLine(hold)` ("Holds up to $X · you pay what it uses"), or the Worker's sentence when AI is off there.
- **A press.** It quotes what it will send, then runs. Its card says `chargedLine(charged, hold)`, never "$0" for an unknown charge.
- **Nothing is written** until the member places a proposal. Insert, Replace, "Use both" and "Use" decide the proposal first (`railProposalsApi.decide(uid, 'accept')`), then hand the text to the page. The page's own save writes it. Discard decides and places nothing.
- **The routes.** The intent calls `articleAuthoringApi.quote`, `articleAuthoringApi.assist`, `railProposalsApi.list` and `railProposalsApi.decide`, exactly as the panel did.
- **No model control.** The intent has no model picker; the models are the AIRail's. It uses in-page controls only, with no `window.alert` or `window.confirm`.

`ArticleWritingPanel.jsx` is deleted, because nothing else used it. The page's two-column wrapper, which existed only for the aside, is now a single column.

### 3. Departures

- **The chip is not re-wired.** Part 1 needed no new wiring, because the header Ask already reads D1442's per-page count through `HeaderAsk`. The placeholder was removed, which is the alternative the issue named, and this section records why.
- **The format bar's Ask is removed.** The issue asks for one Ask entry point on the article page. The bar's Ask opened the same writing actions as the header's, so the header's `ArticleAsk` is now the only one.
