### The owner's answers (option A on each of D6053's questions)

1. Only the venture's owner reminds and exports. A co-founder sees both off with the reason, and can record a commitment and start a new call as a draft (D1191, D1222).
2. At most 2 reminders per recipient per call, at least 3 days apart. The Worker enforces both twice: in the read that decides who may be ticked, and in the claiming `UPDATE` itself.
3. Investor emails are never in an export.
4. Recording a commitment moves the pipeline line from Soft to Signed or Wired.
5. Counsel is not notified of an export.

### Routes

| Route | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/round-calls/:uid/follow-through` | owner, co-founder | Recipients by group (not opened, opened not answered, not delivered, responded), each with its reminders and why it cannot be ticked; each response's pipeline line; the export log; Started from; what this viewer may do. A matching investor who has not responded has no name and no id. |
| `POST /api/round-calls/:uid/remind` | owner | The in-app notice the send uses, to the ticked recipients. Refused on a draft, a call with counsel, a closed call or one past its close date. The result names who was not reminded and why: responded, limit, too soon, account not active, not delivered. A notice that is not delivered does not count toward the limit. |
| `GET /api/round-calls/:uid/export.csv`, `export.pdf` | owner | `?scope=responses` (default) or `terms`. Named recipients only; amounts headed "Indicated (USD), not committed"; a failed questions read marks the file "Questions could not be read". The log row is written before the file is answered: no log, no file. The PDF is drawn with `pdf-lib`, as `useOfFunds/exportPlan.ts` draws its file. |
| `POST /api/round-calls/:uid/successor` | owner, co-founder | A draft from a closed call. Terms and the data room (when still recorded) carry; the close date, counsel approval, deck and audience do not. Refused while another call is a draft, with counsel or open, and when the source is not closed. |
| `POST /api/round-calls/:uid/commitments` | owner, co-founder | Signed or Wired with a typed amount on the response's pipeline line, which keeps the call's uid. Refused: a blank amount, not a response, no pipeline line, another currency, already Wired, Signed again. The indicated amount on the call is never touched. |
