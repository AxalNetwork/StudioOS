- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** none. Every call is a route parts 4 and 5 already serve.

### 1. What changed

D1038's boards read and decided nothing. Now each board reads its store, opens one item over the board, and decides in a dialog. The Worker records each decision and tells the person.

- **Files:**
  - `frontend/src/pages/admin/approvals/LaneBoards.jsx` picks the board by address.
  - Each board has its own file: `TesterBoard.jsx`, `PitchBoard.jsx` and `ExpertBoard.jsx`.
  - `boardParts.jsx` holds what they share.
  - `approvalsModel.js` gains the tabs, rows, chart and dialog words, all as pure functions.
- **The client:** `frontend/src/lib/api/approvalBoards.js` (`approvalBoardsApi`), re-exported by one line in `api.js` (D527).
- **One item's address:** `/admin/approvals/<board>/<id>` opens the board with that item's drawer.
  - The board under the drawer is the tab the row was opened from.
  - Closing the drawer returns to that tab.
  - Tab paths win over ids, so no id can shadow a tab.
  - The routes are `/admin/approvals/product-testers/:item`, `/co-marketing/:item` and `/wellbeing-experts/:item`.

**Every decision opens `DecisionDialog`.** It says three things: what changes, who is told and what is recorded.
- A reason the person sees is required wherever the Worker requires one, with the Worker's own minimum.
- A refusal known before sending is drawn first, and Send stays off. Examples: an unverified email, an expired licence.
- A refusal after sending is the Worker's own sentence, and the dialog stays open.
- A 5xx or a network failure says nothing changed. It is never drawn as done.
- After a decision, the board and the drawer read again, and the board says what happened ("Gus Demo is a product tester now. They are told.").
- Nothing uses `alert` or `confirm`.

### 2. Product testers (APR4, APR5)

- **Tabs:** five tabs at sub-paths: Waiting (`applied`), Active, Paused, Declined and Removed. Each counts from the store's `totals`.
- **Above the table:**
  - the facts line (who can apply, who decides, and that admins cannot apply);
  - the info line.
- **Tiles:**
  - Waiting, with the oldest application's age;
  - Active testers;
  - In a testing program now: the Worker's `in_programme=yes` count, not a sum on the page.
- **The chart:**
  - Applications by profile over the last 12 weeks, with its sentence as the `role="img"` label and "View as table".
  - It is drawn only when every tester was read.
  - The board reads every tester, a hundred a page up to a thousand. When it stops short, the list says so and the chart is not drawn.
- **Toolbar:**
  - search on name or email, from two characters;
  - profile, email verified and email consent filters;
  - three sorts.
- **Table columns:**
  - Waiting: the age and band pill, applicant, profile, applied, availability, email consent, identity, email verified.
  - Active and Paused: since when, by whom, and programs picked or applied.
  - Declined and Removed: when, by whom, and the reason the member was told.
- **The drawer:**
  - the facts, their answers, availability, and consent with its version;
  - their testing programs and their history;
  - the note, which the member never sees, marked as such.
  - **Assign** opens the inbox item (`/admin/approvals/items/product_testers-<id>`), where D1045's Assign lives.
- **Dialogs:**
  - **A, Approve.** Dialog A states the Worker's `email_unverified` refusal before sending (APR5r).
  - **B, Decline.** It needs a reason of at least 10 characters, which the member sees. They can apply again after 90 days.
  - **C, Pause or remove.** It needs a reason. A removal says how many program places it ended.
  - **Resume,** for a paused tester. D1042 built the route.
- **The intake-paused banner.** It shows #2318's answer A: "Member intake is paused. Nobody can apply now." No member apply route exists (D1042 §4). So `INTAKE_PAUSED` is a constant, with a note that it becomes a read when that route lands. The empty Waiting tab says intake is paused.

### 3. Co-marketing pitches (APR6, APR7)

- **Reads:** each of the five states is read alone, 25 a page, on part 5's paging. A failed state is a dash on its tab, never 0, and the other states still draw.
- **Tiles:**
  - Waiting;
  - Approved, not yet published, with the oldest approval when every approved pitch is on the page;
  - Visits and Sign-ups over 30 days, from the published queue's `attribution_30d`. That covers the whole queue, not the page.
- **Tables:**
  - Proposed: Waiting with the band, pitch, partner, proposed date and audience.
  - Approved: the code, 30-day visits, the approval date, and Publish….
  - Published: the four 30-day counts.
  - Declined: the reason the partner was told.
- **The drawer:**
  - the summary, angle, what they bring, channels, date, audience and co-branding notes;
  - the asset link and the attribution code;
  - who decided, from D1132's `decided`.
- **Dialogs:**
  - **Approve:** an optional note, which the partner sees.
  - **Decline:** a reason of at least 10 characters.
  - **Publish:** a complete https link, checked as the Worker checks it.
- **Retired:** the Review queue tab of `CoMarketingPage.jsx`, with its `AdminQueueRow`, its `alert` and its inline buttons. An admin there reads "Pitches are reviewed on Approvals › Co-marketing pitches." `comarketing_attribution_live` now reads the angle and what they bring in the board's drawer.

### 4. Wellbeing experts (APR8)

- **Reads:** part 5's review read, two hundred a page up to a thousand.
- **Tabs:**
  - To verify: pending, at the directory's 70% bar;
  - Profile incomplete;
  - Verified;
  - Declined: part 5's state, new here;
  - Hidden.

  A short read draws no counts.
- **Tiles:** To verify (with the oldest), Verified and Declined.
- **The credentials column:** "Licence and 2 files", or "None given".
- **The drawer:**
  - the bio;
  - the directory profile (categories, languages, time zones, pricing, booking link, website), read alone. An expert with no active profile says so.
  - the licence line and the private files, each opening D1043's download route;
  - the reason they were told, and the decision history.
- **Dialogs:**
  - **Verify:** "I checked their credentials" is required. It sends `credentials_checked: true` and the review version it showed, so the Worker refuses a profile that changed meanwhile. An expired licence is refused before sending.
  - **Decline:** a reason of at least 10 characters.
  - **Hide.**
  - **Restore and Remove verification.** The roster's toggles did these, so retiring the toggles loses nothing.
- **Retired:** the `/admin/wellbeing` roster's Verify and Hide toggles. Each row now links "Decide on Approvals" to the expert's address. The roster still reads.

### 5. Departures, for review

- **Product testers' "Assigned" column is not drawn.** The tester read carries no assignment. The drawer's Assign… opens the inbox item instead.

### 7. Mutation checks

Each mutation was applied alone, the extended test was run, and the file was restored. **23 of 23 were caught.** They covered:

- **The dialog:** Send ignoring readiness and the refusal; the who-is-told line dropped; the recorded line emptied.
- **Refusals before sending:** the unverified email; the expired licence.
- **Reasons and links:** the tester decline minimum at 1; the pitch decline needing no reason; http accepted as a published link.
- **Wellbeing:** Verify without the checkbox; the review version not sent; the declined state dropped; a hidden expert offered Hide.
- **Addresses:** item addresses not parsed; the tab under the drawer lost.
- **Refusal text:** a 5xx shown in the Worker's words, as if it could be acted on.
- **Tiles and reads:** a null figure drawn as a number; the tester read stopping at one page.
- **Product testers:** Active offering no actions; the places-ended count dropped; the note not sent; the chart's 12-week window widened; one-character search filtering; tabs not filtering by status.

A twenty-fourth probe, on the per-state page size, did not match the code as written and was not run.
