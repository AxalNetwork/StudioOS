| Address | What | Who |

- **Why a Super Admin is forwarded.** `/admin/accounts` was Team's address. Notices already sent carry it, and so do bookmarks. So a Super Admin who opens it lands on Team.
- **The notice.** Team then says, once: "Team has moved to its own address under HQ. /admin/accounts is the Admin’s Accounts page now." Dismiss takes `moved` off the address.
- **The other forward.** `/admin/held/accounts` was never a door anyone kept, so it forwards with nothing to explain.
- **Not kept for good.** #2375's route map says neither forward is kept for good.
- **Where it lives.** `pages/admin/accountsAddress.js` holds the addresses, the forwards and the notice, and `pages/admin/AccountsAddress.jsx` holds the two routes' components.
