## D8534 — Fund operations: one investor journey from admission to distribution, reconciled to the bank feed (#8534)

`cloudflare-worker/test/fund_reconciliation_journey_d8534.test.ts` runs one synthetic fund (a fixture Fund I, two investors) through the real routers and services on node:sqlite. The bank feed is `mercuryStub` from `_mercury_fixture.ts`, handed to `syncConnection` as its fetcher. No Mercury call is made, every id, amount and account number is invented, and the Mercury client is unchanged (`mercury_read_only_d710.test.mjs` still passes).

### The journey

1. **Admission.** An Admin approves both applications (`PATCH /api/admin/lp-applications/:id`). The GP then invites both investors (`POST /api/funds/:id/lps`). Investor B's address is already verified, so B's row is linked at invite. Investor A's address is not verified yet, so A's row waits unbound: A's studio view shows an unlinked commitment and no holding. A proves the address and signs in (`claimLpRowsAtSignIn`), and the row is bound to A's account.
2. **Call.** The GP issues a $10,000.00 call (`POST /api/funds/:id/capital-call`). It splits by commitment into $6,000.00 and $4,000.00.
3. **Bank feed.** The stub shows A's wire. The sync stores it, and the matcher proposes it against A's line. No money moves.
4. **Reconciliation.** An Admin confirms the match. This writes one receipt on A's line, and A's paid-in and the fund's deployed capital move once. The fund administrator then posts the receipt to the operational ledger and reconciles the cash line to the bank line (`POST /api/fund-ledger/:id/lines/:lineId/reconcile`).
5. **Distribution.** The `returns_distribution` job computes a pending split, and the GP marks A's line paid.

After each step, `agree()` checks each investor's own `GET /api/funds/lp-portal` against the ledger. It compares owed cents, received cents, status, paid-in and paid distributions, and checks that neither investor sees the other's rows. Then the three invariants run.

### The invariants

- **INV1. One bank item never settles two obligations.** A `mercury:` reference sits on at most one receipt. A bank line has at most one live match. Each confirmed match's receipt is on the match's own line, for the match's own cents, under its own reference. Each bank receipt is held by exactly one match. A statement line reconciles at most one ledger line in a fund.
- **INV2. Every balance change has an approval behind it.**
  - Each LP's paid-in, and the fund's deployed capital, equal their receipts exactly.
  - A line is paid if and only if its receipts cover it.
  - Each bank receipt has a confirmed match, and its confirmer is its recorder. Any other receipt is recorded by the GP or an Admin.
  - Each call is issued by the GP of record or an Admin.
  - Each paid distribution has its `distribution_marked_paid` act.
  - Each posted entry and each reconciled line carries its audit row naming the person.
  - Each LP row answers to an approved, reviewed application.
- **INV3. No fund flow reaches a Stripe payment path.** At run time, every global `fetch` is recorded and refused, and the run must end with none. An investor's own Pay press is refused with `gp_records_payment`. No Stripe identity is written. In the source, the transitive imports of the fund-flow modules reach no Stripe payment module (the provider client, `routes/billing.ts`, `services/stripe*.ts`), and the modules themselves name no Stripe host.

### The cases and their end states

| Case | What happens | End state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duplicate | The same bank line arrives twice in one page and again on the next sync. A second, distinct wire for the same amount arrives on the same day. | The first line is stored once and confirmed once (a second confirm answers 409 `match_confirmed`). The second wire is never proposed. Pairing it by hand is refused: `already_paid` on A's line, `exceeds_outstanding` on B's. It stays unmatched (count 1). In the ledger, the bank line reconciles one cash line, and the same bank line offered to a second cash line is refused (409). |
| Partial | A sends $4,000.00 of $6,000.00. The amount is exactly B's share, so the matcher proposes it to B's line, and the Admin rejects that with a reason. | The Admin pairs the wire to A's line by hand and confirms it. | Unmatched | $7,000.00 arrives. It fits neither line. | It is never proposed, and a hand pairing is refused (`exceeds_outstanding`). Nothing moves, and it counts as unmatched until an Admin marks it Not ours. After that, pairing it is refused (`marked_not_ours`). |
| Reversed (failed) | A confirmed, ledger-reconciled bank line comes back `failed`. | The match turns `needs_review`, and confirming it is refused. ### Defect fixed

**A pending distribution counted as distributed.** `GET /api/funds/lp-portal` and the GP's per-LP report (`GET /api/funds/:id/lp-report/:lpId`) summed every `fund_distributions` row into `distributions_dollars`, DPI and TVPI, pending rows included. As soon as the `returns_distribution` job wrote its split, before anyone marked anything paid, the investor's DPI already counted money that had not reached them. That is an investor view that disagrees with the approvals. Both sums now count only rows with `status = 'paid'`, which is the rule `services/fundPerformance.ts` and the fund report already use. The rows are still listed with their status. The journey's "distribution pending" step guards the fix, and reverting it fails the journey.

### Gaps found (no concept in the platform; tested as they are, not built)

1. **Approval does not gate admission.** `POST /api/funds/:id/lps` admits any address with or without an approved `lp_applications` row. `services/lpApplicationReview.ts` says on purpose that approval grants nothing, and nothing links an LP row to the application that cleared it. INV2's admission check holds here only because this fixture approves before it invites.
2. **No reversal of a receipt.** `capital_call_receipts` is append-only, and there is no reversing entry. When a bank line fails or is restated after it was receipted, the match is flagged `needs_review`, and that is all. The line stays paid, the LP's paid-in and the fund's deployed capital keep the money, and the investor reads paid. Rejecting the match unlinks nothing. The operational ledger has no unreconcile act either. A failed line also drops out of `fundUnmatchedCount`, because it is no longer a posted line, so after a reversal nothing on the cash side shows the gap.
3. **`logActivity` never lands.** `routes/partnernet.ts`'s `logActivity` inserts into `activity_logs` without its NOT NULL `action` column. On the baseline schema the insert fails and is swallowed, so `capital_call_issued` (and every other `logActivity` caller's row) is never written. The call header's `issued_by` is the record INV2 uses instead.
4. **A distribution's outgoing wire is never reconciled.** D710 matches credits only. The bank debit that pays a distribution matches nothing and is counted unmatched, and `fund_distributions` has no link to it.
5. **Mark-paid's `returns` credit is dead.** In `POST /api/funds/distributions/:id/mark-paid`, the second statement of the batch credits `limited_partners.returns` only `WHERE EXISTS (... status = 'pending')`. The first statement has just set that row to `paid`, so the credit never fires (the journey reads `returns = 0` after mark-paid). Making it fire would double-count, because the portal's TVPI and DPI add `returns` to the distributions sum. That is an owner question (below), not a fix.

### Open questions for the owner

- **Reversal (gap 2).** When a reconciled receipt's bank line fails, what should the end state be?
  - (a) A reversing receipt (negative cents, `source = 'reversal'`) that reopens the line and debits paid-in and deployed capital, made by a person from the `needs_review` match.
  - (b) As now: flag only, with the receipt standing, plus a visible "bank reversed" state on the line for the investor and the GP.
  - (c) Block the reject of a `needs_review` match whose receipt stands until (a) is done.
- **Revoked authority after an approval.** Today a pairing or ledger entry made by someone whose authority was later removed stays valid, and any current Admin can confirm or reconcile it. Should such a pairing or entry instead be voided, or held for a second person?
- **`limited_partners.returns` (gap 5).** Should the dead credit be removed, or should it fire, with `returns` then left out of TVPI and DPI?

### Observation, not changed

The ledger's reconcile guard (`NOT EXISTS` plus `idx_fund_statement_line_once`) is per fund. Nothing stops one administrator of two funds from reconciling the same statement reference and line in each. The statement reference is free text that names the account, so this is recorded as an observation and not treated as a defect.
