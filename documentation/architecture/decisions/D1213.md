### 2. What is read, and from where

| Block (boards) | Read |
|---|---|
| Lines (CP1, CP2, CP14) | `GET /api/deals/:id/commitments`, scoped by the Worker: every line for an Admin and the deal's founder, an investor's own, a confirmed total for anyone else |
| The round's total for an investor (CP2) | `GET /api/deals/:id`, `capital_committed` (confirmed only) |
| Checklist (CP1, CP19) | `GET /api/deals/:id/closing-checklist` |

| The desk (CP13) | `GET /api/deals`: each deal with a closing deadline |
| The paper, route, instructions, evidence, cap table, reporting, activity (CP3–CP12, CP15) | none |

**Status words.** A line is Committed when pending, Received when confirmed, and Withdrawn when withdrawn. Anything else is "Not recorded".
- Confirmed is the receiving side's evidence today: the founder's Mercury match, D752.
- An investor's own Mercury proposal is not that evidence. It never makes a line Received (owner question 4, recommended answer).
- Committed sums the live lines. Received sums the Received lines only.
- A line with no amount is not counted, and is named as not counted. It is never a zero.

### 3. What is drawn today

- **The rooms**, each with:
  - the header and the seven-step stepper;
  - the sentence that the platform never moves money;
  - the lines, the checklist and the transfers, as §2 scopes them.
- **The desk:** every deal with a deadline, by deadline, with a deadline under 14 days marked. Each row opens its closing.
- **Not read here yet**, with its reason and never a zero (`closing-wait-*`): the closings record itself, the paper, the route, the payment instructions, the evidence, the cap-table proposal, the reporting duties and the activity.
- **One failed read costs one block.** A failed commitments read leaves the checklist and transfers drawn. A malformed admin address reads nothing.
- **Who sees what** (owner question 3, recommended answer):
  - The founder and an Admin see every line.
  - An investor sees their own line and the round's confirmed total. They never see the checklist, the transfers or another investor's line, and the transfers read is not made for them.
  - An Operating Partner sees the checklist, the transfers and the total (owner question 6: they keep checklist ticks).
- **Departures, each a part's:**
  - no route, paper, evidence or reporting UI (parts 5 and 6);
  - no dark board, phone layout or activity drawer (parts 5 and 6);
  - no Contracts tab (part 6);
  - no countersign (part 3).

### 5. Owner decisions

All eight of #2424's questions take its recommended answers until the owner says otherwise:
1. Contracts › Closings, in both shells.
2. An Axal-managed SPV is a `vc_funds` row of kind SPV.
3. An investor sees their own line and the round's total.
4. Only the receiving side's evidence counts as Received.
5. Countersign, commit and release are Super Admin acts.
6. Operating Partners keep checklist ticks and lose money records.
7. A change to the company's receiving account notifies every unpaid investor, with no hold.
8. A third-party fund's vehicle needs a business check before Received.
