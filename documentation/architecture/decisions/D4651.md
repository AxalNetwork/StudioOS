## D4651 — A plan chosen on Pricing survives registration (#4651)

#4651 (Pricing audit 1/5, slot S15, taken at the owner's request). The audit observed presentation only; this records what the code does, traced on current `main`, and what changed.

### The contract, traced

| Step | What it did | Owning files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A signed-out visitor presses a paid card on `/pricing` | went to `/register?lane=<lane>`. The plan and the billing interval were not in the address; an Advisor card had no lane at all (`/register`) | `lib/pricingModel.js` (`planCards`, `REGISTER`), `lib/sitePricing.js` (`PLAN_CARDS`) |
| `/register` | read `?lane=` (`founder`, `investor`, `lp`, `partner`; `advisor` was ignored although the site doors already link `/register?lane=advisor`), so an Advisor visitor read the founder lane or none; it said nothing of a plan | `pages/RegisterPage.jsx` |
| After sign-up | `?next=` (same-origin, 200 characters) is kept in `localStorage` for 24 hours and is also passed to Google's state; `RequireAuth` consumes it once (`lib/pendingNext.js`, Task #1). The email link and Google both honour it. No plan data existed to carry | `lib/pendingNext.js`, `App.jsx` (`RequireAuth`), `routes/auth.ts` |
| Back on `/pricing` | `?checkout=<plan key>&interval=monthly\|yearly` opens the checkout sheet on that plan, once (D1053) | `pages/PricingPage.jsx` |
| Checkout | `POST /api/billing/checkout/*` buys a plan of the caller's own profile family only (`plannedEntry`/`familyForRole`): a new account is `exploring` until its profile is chosen and the answer is 403 `choose_profile`; the sheet draws that as "Choose your profile first" (`sheetMode` `explorer`) | `routes/billing_checkout.ts`, `services/planCheckout.ts`, `components/checkout/*` |

Catalogue identifiers are the Worker's `PLAN_CATALOGUE` keys (`services/planCatalogue.ts`): `founder:growth`, `founder:studio`, `investor:plus`, `investor:professional`, `advisor:pro`, `partner:pro` (`investor:institutional` is sold by our sales team and has no price card). A paid card's storage plan key is its catalogue key, and a test holds the two lists equal.

So the audit's "state loss" was real for the plan and the interval (never carried), and not a loss of hidden session state: there was none to lose. The destination mechanism (`next`) already worked and is reused.

### What changed

- A paid card's register address now carries `plan=<plan key>`, `interval=monthly|yearly` and a `next` that is exactly the Pricing return D1053 understands (`/pricing?for=<tab>&checkout=<key>&interval=<iv>&seen=<cents>`). `seen` is the amount the card showed. A `next` that would not pass the same-origin rule is left out whole, never cut (`lib/planIntent.js`).
- `/register` reads the plan from the public catalogue and shows it: the plan, its monthly figure, how it is charged and the full commitment ("$80 / month · $960 billed yearly", "The full $960 is charged at checkout, for the whole year"), with "Change plan". A plan that is not offered, a yearly price it does not hold, and an unreadable catalogue are each said in words, and nothing is substituted: no monthly price is drawn for a yearly request (`lib/planSelection.js`, `components/auth/RegistrationPlan.jsx`).
- Audience copy: the `advisor` lane has its own words (an advisor subscription is not a pitch) and sends no role, because the Worker's register route takes only founder, partner and investor. Every audience sees the steps as separate things: create the account (free, no payment on that page), choose a profile (plans belong to a profile; Checkout asks for it first; a new account is reviewed before its workspace opens), check out (payment starts only when the member confirms on Stripe's page; the charging basis for the chosen plan), and, for a founder, the Spin-Out Lab application as an optional, separate step (`lib/registerCopy.js`).
- A signed-in visitor who follows a plan link to `/register` was signed out in silence by `AuthScreen`, losing the plan. They now get a notice: Continue to Pricing (checkout opens there), or Sign out and create a different account (`App.jsx`).
- Back on Pricing, a price different from `seen`, a yearly price the plan no longer holds, and a plan no longer sold are said before anything opens (the sheet shows today's price and Stripe's totals; a plan that is gone opens nothing).
- Abandoned choices do not linger: opening `/register` with no `next` of its own drops a kept Pricing return (and only that), so a later plain sign-up is not sent to an old plan's checkout; choosing another plan overwrites it (`clearPendingPricingNext`).

### What this does not do

- It adds no billing pipeline and no Worker change.
- It does not say what a plan includes or when entitlements start: that is #4653. The copy says only that payment starts when checkout is confirmed and that a plan needs a chosen profile.
- The shared account and application wording (#4578) and the authentication-return verification (#4579, #4580) are reused, not rewritten.
- Whether an Advisor or Operating Partner can complete checkout straight after choosing their profile is the checkout's own rule (`plannedEntry`), unchanged.

### Tests

`frontend/test/pricing_selection_4651.test.mjs`: the key parity with the Worker catalogue; the address round trip for every paid card and both intervals; the card's plan and interval; `selectionFor` for ready, yearly-missing, unknown, unreadable; the plan block, steps and copy mounted for founder and advisor; the unavailable and unreadable states; the signed-in notice; and Pricing's changed-price, no-yearly and gone notices. Each assertion was seen failing under a mutation. Desktop and phone share one layout (a single column inside the auth card); the page has no phone-specific branch.
