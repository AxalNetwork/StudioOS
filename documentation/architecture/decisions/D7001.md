## D7001 — Help guides say what the product does: the sign-up sequence, the role names, the recovery button and the public-tracker warning (#7001)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-22 (#7001, tracker #6976, plan key X19). Copy and one small piece of Help Center v2 (D6861). No route, store or migration.

### What changed

- **Onboarding** (`getting-started/onboarding`, `pages/docs/sections/getting-started.js`). The guide described "4–8 questions" and then a dashboard. Its overview is now `MEMBERSHIP_STEPS` (`lib/registerCopy.js`, D4578) word for word. Its steps are the five in that sentence, in order: account created, email confirmed, membership reviewed and agreement sent, agreement signed, workspace enabled. The profiling chat stays as a tip, saying only what `OnboardingChatPage` asks. No review time is stated.
- **Roles** (`getting-started/roles`). The guide said "one of five roles" with Admin and without Explorer. It now uses the Trust Center's names (`profileName` in `pages/trust/memberTrustModel.js`), which match the registration lanes: Founder, Investor (the LP lane folds into it), Operating Partner and Advisor. Explorer covers every new account until review and signature. Admin is marked "(staff only)" and is not offered at registration. The unverified claims (impersonation and multi-role defaults) are dropped.
- **Account recovery** (`account/auth-recovery`). The guide's button reads "Recover my account" (`RECOVERY_ACTION` in `pages/docs/v2/helpV2.js`; it was "Start recovery", and before D6861 "Open the surface"). The admin-review fallback is now in the overview, the first paragraph, instead of the third step.
- **Contact support** (`troubleshooting/contact-support`). Directly under its ticket button (Go to Your tickets), before the guide body or any field, a note repeats the guide's public-tracker warning (`TRACKER_WARNING`) and links "Read private disclosure options" to `/trust/disclosure`. The note shows only when the button does, so a signed-out reader, who has no ticket button, sees the guide's own warning in its overview.
- **Search suggestions.** The search had no list of suggestions, only the example "invite an investor" in the signed-in placeholder, which is not a public guide. The old chips (capital call, cap table, data room) were gone with D6861. `SEARCH_SUGGESTIONS` names a query and the public guide it finds: account recovery, account roles, onboarding walkthrough and contact support. `searchSuggestions()` drops any whose guide leaves `PUBLIC_HELP_ANCHORS`. The suggestions show as "Try" chips under the search for every reader, and the placeholder's example is now "account recovery".
