## D4316 — Upscale in image editing, through Pruna P-Image-Upscale behind its own switch (#4316)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-04 (#4316, split from #3778). The owner chose **A, Pruna** on 2026-11-04.
- **Number:** D4316 is the issue's number (D4106).
- **Builds on:** D1534 phase 2 (background removal through AI Gateway), D1544 (a tool's result is a child image the founder keeps or reverts).
- **Migration:** none. Migration 766 already allows `deck_images.image_tool = 'upscale'`.

### Why

Upscale stayed greyed "Coming later" because Bria, the only provider decision M = B allowed, has no upscale model on Cloudflare. The owner widened it for this one tool: Pruna's `p-image-upscale`, about $0.005 an image, quoted and charged to the founder. Pruna is a new sub-processor.

### What changed

- **The model.** `pruna/p-image-upscale` is a second row of `GATEWAY_IMAGE_MODELS` (`services/ai/catalogue.ts`), provider `pruna`, at the printed `0.005`, with its page and the date it was read.
- **One switch per provider.** Each row names its own switch:
  - background removal runs while `AI_PROVIDER_BRIA` is exactly `'on'`;
  - Upscale runs while `AI_PROVIDER_PRUNA` is exactly `'on'` (`imageToolOn`).
  Turning one on never turns on the other. Both are off by default. Upscale's flag is switched on only after Pruna's 10-day sub-processor notice (#1008).
- **What it asks for** (`imageTools.ts`, `toolInput`):
  - twice the pixels on each side (4× the area), as `upscale_mode: 'target'` at `target` megapixels, capped at 8 MP;
  - the original's format, so a PNG keeps its transparency and a JPEG stays a JPEG;
  - no detail or realism enhancement, which can change what the picture shows.
- **Refused before any hold.** An image already at 8 MP is said so in the quote (`ai: false`, `image_large_enough`), and a press is 422. Nothing is held or called.
- **Quoted and charged as removal is.** The quote is the printed price at the member's billed multiplier, held through `meteredCall` and keyed by the press's `Idempotency-Key`.
  - A ledger refusal is 402 and calls nothing.
  - A failed call, a result that isn't `https`, or a result that isn't the expected format within 40 MB releases the hold. Nothing is charged, and one usage row records `all_models_failed`.
  - A success settles at the quote, with one `ai_usage_logs` row: units `{ images: 1, provider: 'pruna' }`.
- **The result is a child image** (D1544): a proposed `deck_images` row with `parent_uid` and `image_tool = 'upscale'`. It records its own, larger pixel size and keeps the parent's alt text. The parent is never changed, and Revert discards the child.
- **The same in the editor, the PDF and the PowerPoint.** The toolbar keeps the child and places it in the block's own place (`placedResult`), so the block keeps its crop, filter and fit. Each renderer draws that look from the block:
  - the browser, and the PDF and PowerPoint download taken from the print page, draw it with `imageLookStyle`;
  - the editable PowerPoint draws it as `imageLookParts`, from the picture's own pixel size, so the larger child gives the same `srcRect`.
- **The toolbar** was already generic: Upscale becomes a live button the moment the Worker reports it on. Its quote prints "Pruna P-Image-Upscale · 1 image · …".

### Not verified

Cloudflare's model page (input schema and price) was not reachable from the session that built this. The input fields follow Pruna's published schema. Before switching the flag on, the owner checks the price in the dashboard against the catalogue row, and one upscale on staging confirms the answer shape (a URL, as Bria's is).
