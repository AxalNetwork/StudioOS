### What the canvas decides

- The project workspace gains a sixth tab, Study, with seven sections in a fixed order: Scope and questions; Customers and demand; Size and scenarios; Competition and pricing; Entry strategy and risks; Evidence and findings; Report and monitoring. A project with no study reads None with Start a study; an archived project is read-only; question, objective, scope and exclusions stay edited on Overview and show read-only with "Edit on Overview" (RST-01).
- New market research carries the market into the New research form as a "Started from" block, whose country filter means where listed companies are based; Create saves project and study together or nothing. Founder and admin have the action, advisor and partner see it disabled with its reason, the investor is not offered it (RST-02).
- Scope holds product, boundary, business model, the decision to be made, horizon and segment cards with one beachhead; customer countries start empty, and a study started with a country filter offers "Use [Germany] as customer geography?" (RST-03).
- Questions carry a topic, a status (Open, Answered, Set aside) and "[3] for · [1] against", with no owner column; one question shows Supporting and Contradicting side by side, Context below, "Marked by you" and never "verified", and "Finding removed" keeping its direction (RST-04, RST-05). Overview and the rail read "Key questions answered · 2 of 5" and Open questions, never a guessed 0 (RST-06).
- Completeness is seven topics in words with the counts behind them, no score or percentage, drawn apart from "N of M sources answerable in Ask". A decision (Enter, Do not enter, Test further) is append-only, with its rationale, reversing assumptions and evidence (RST-07).
- Evidence and findings finds an observation for the study's countries, codes and period, attaches it with topic, segment, note and quality rationale, and groups references in the five kinds of figure, Activity proxy drawn empty (RST-08). Customers and demand reads Validate per segment with stage words, counts only and consented quotes, never summing across kinds or currencies (RST-09); Entry strategy reads Grow's channels as "Assumed then" and "Measured now" (RST-10).
- Eadwyn writes only interpretations in Findings, accepted, edited or discarded; it never writes the scope, an answer, the decision, a size, a price, a quote, a verdict or report text (RST-11). Empty states, the states grid, phone 390, dark, the clickable path, keyboard and handoff are RST-12 to RST-18.
