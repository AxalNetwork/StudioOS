## D6887 — Positions read cut at the as-of date (#6887)

Under #6217 (the Shareable Statement, canvas D6217, "Backend required"), filed from #6867. Builds on D4644, which made `GET /api/positions` honour `?as_of=` for marks.

**The defect.** `GET /api/positions?as_of=DATE` cut only the marks at the date. The amount invested (`SUM(invested_amount)`), the rounds count, `latest_round`, `latest_ownership_pct` and the list of companies were computed over every cheque ever recorded. A company first funded after the date was still listed, and the rows' invested total could exceed the analytics `paid_in` for the same date. The statement (#6867) shows a mismatch note whenever the two differ.

**Worker.** `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/positions.ts`, `GET /`:
- With `as_of`, the grouped rows query and the latest-round lookup both keep only cheques dated on or before the date (one predicate in each, the date bound twice). A company with no such cheque leaves the list. `total_invested`, `rounds`, `latest_date`, `latest_round`, `latest_ownership_pct` and `vintage` all speak for the date.
- A cheque is dated the way `/positions/analytics` dates it (`isoDate(position_date) || isoDate(created_at)`): its `position_date` when that starts `YYYY-MM-DD`, otherwise when it was recorded. The issue named `position_date <= as_of`. Analytics' rule is used instead, because `position_date` is stored as free text without a format check, and the acceptance is that the two reads agree. `created_at` is `NOT NULL`, so every cheque has a date.
- Each row adds `first_date`: the first cheque's date (`YYYY-MM-DD`), on or before `as_of` when one is given. It is null when that cheque's `position_date` is free text. This lets the statement say "Funded after" from the date itself.
- Without `as_of` the read is unchanged, apart from the new `first_date` field.

No new route, no schema change, no frontend change: the statement's existing check drops its mismatch note once the totals agree.

**Where the totals can still differ.** Analytics' `paid_in` leaves out a cheque whose `invested_amount` is zero or negative, while the list sums every row. A contribution recorded without a position row is also counted by neither read. The acceptance is stated for books where every contribution has a positive position row.

**Verified.** `cloudflare-worker/test/positions_as_of_cut_6887.test.ts` checks, against the analytics read in the same in-memory database:
- with `as_of`, the rows' invested total equals `paid_in`;
- a company funded only after the date is absent with `as_of` and present without it;
- rounds, latest round and ownership stop at the date;
- a free-text `position_date` is dated by `created_at`, in the rows and in the latest-round lookup;
- a cheque on the date itself counts;
- a date before every cheque lists nothing;
- the default read is the whole book.

The mutations are listed in the file's header. `positions_analytics_dated_basis_6207.test.ts` passes unchanged.
