## D4838 part 10 — PP03c: Draft proposal from a lead (#4838)

Built 2026-11-16 by S25 for #5936, to the owner's answer A on that issue. Part of D4838 (Pipeline v2); board PP03c. Numbered 10, not 9, because #5943 was built in parallel and may take part 9.

- **A lead can carry one saved draft.** `partner_lead_proposal_drafts` (migration `202611161354_5936_partner_lead_proposal_drafts.sql`) holds the firm's shape (fixed project, retainer, time and materials, or not chosen), price in cents with its currency, scope text and origin, one row per partner and need. **A draft is not a proposal.** A proposal is a `quotes` row and exists only once the firm bids. The table has no sent, status or recipient column, and nothing that reads it says a proposal was sent.
- **Routes**, on `routes/partner_pipeline.ts`, behind the same `actingPartner` gate as the zones: `GET`, `PUT` (Save draft and Edit are one write) and `DELETE` (Discard) on `/partner/pipeline/leads/:needId/draft`. A draft belongs to a lead and to nothing else: a need this firm bid on, passed on, or that is no longer open answers 409 with the reason, which is the three-set rule of migration 233. A missing need is 404. Another firm's draft is never read or written: every statement is keyed on the caller's own partner id. An empty draft (no shape, price or scope) is refused; Discard is the way to clear one.
- **Origin.** `manual` for a draft typed from nothing, `eadwyn` when the partner accepts an Eadwyn draft into it. An edit sends no origin and keeps the one it had, so an Eadwyn draft a person reworded is still marked "Eadwyn proposal · not sent".
- **The page.** Leads' card gains Draft proposal as its primary action, beside Pass. It opens the shared opportunity sheet (D4838 part 8) on the lead, with `LeadProposalDraft` under Overview through the sheet's `editors`: the opportunity's context above, the draft below with Edit and Discard (Discard asks in place and names the lead), or Write a draft when there is none. Every label ends "not sent". There is no Send.
- **Eadwyn, cost first.** The sheet's Eadwyn band is the Leads page's existing `pipeline/leads` surface scoped to the one lead (`scope_key` = its need id), with the reader's own cost shown before the run (`useAiSpend`, D424). The gather drafts only for that lead when it is one of this firm's open leads, and answers `nothing_to_draft` otherwise, never falling back to the whole list. Accepting the Eadwyn draft saves its text as the lead's scope with `origin: eadwyn`. Nothing runs on open. The Leads page's own band now reads only its unscoped drafts (`scoped` with an empty key), so a lead's draft does not appear there.

### Where PP03c departs from the canvas or the issue

- **Eadwyn writes the scope, not the shape or the price.** The surface returns prose. Accepting it fills the scope and keeps any shape and price already saved; the partner chooses those.
- **The draft lives under Overview,** not a tab of its own. The sheet's tabs are records that exist (D4838 part 8); a draft is the partner's working note on the lead, and `editors` is the sheet's way to carry a zone's editor.
- **The discard question names the lead only.** The leads read carries the client (the founder, else the project) as one name; it does not carry the company separately.
