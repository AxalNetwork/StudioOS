## D6393 — Count refunds whose amount was not recorded (#6393)

The advisor ledger retains every scoped advisory_refunds_due row, including
null amounts. Both refunds_owed and refunded report all sessions, sum only
recorded cents and add unpriced_sessions. Zero cents is recorded and does
not increase the unpriced count. A sum of zero with unpriced_sessions above
zero is a partial known sum, not a claim that the missing refunds are free.
An empty successful read reports zeros; an unreadable store remains null.
Advisor and booking-created-at window gates are unchanged.

The owner explicitly assigned this narrow read repair to s38. #5305 retains
HQ resolution and its UI; this additive read contract gives consumers the
missing-amount count. Mounted SQLite tests cover open/resolved mixtures,
all-unpriced, zero, empty, unreadable and advisor/window isolation.
No provider call, refund write, schema, permission or production operation
is added. It moves no money.
