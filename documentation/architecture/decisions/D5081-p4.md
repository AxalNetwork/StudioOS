- **Status:** Part 4 built, 2026-11-16 (frontend only; no Worker change, no migration, no new route). Part 1 (Use of funds) is `D5081.md`, part 2 (Capital relationships) `D5081-p2.md`, part 3 (Cap table) `D5081-p3.md`. The Raise navigation (RA-12) is part 5 of #5081.
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Raise v2 · Boards.dc.html` RA-19 A (Home) and B (support read-only), and RA-14's table ("Data room · Yes · Removed · Complete data room"); `design/incoming/Raise v2 Page.dc.html` (`dataroom()`, `support()`).
- **Builds on:** D1101 (the tool), D1106 (Home's blocks), D1105 and D1108 (the checklist read and tab), D1109 (the old addresses forward), D1464 (the support gate), D5072 (Home reads once; the last ninety days name Activity), D5074 (the room read's capability map), D5079 (a refusal or a summary is not an empty room), D5076 (the zero state's first action is the page's one primary), D5081 parts 1 to 3 (`owned`, a module's own primary).

### What was wrong

`/raise/data-room` drew the tool's Home (the counts, Needs you, who has access, what was added, the door to Activity) and then CycleModulePage's generic layer under it: "Data room · at a glance" (the Worker's Last 90 days card: each diligence area's coverage and who opened the room) and a Files records table with its own Upload. The header's primary action was Raise's global Build your deck. On first use Home drew its chips ("0 files · 0 folders"), an empty Needs you, "No investor has access in force now" and "No files yet" over the generic empty card. Checklist's readiness card said "Not read here yet."

### The decision

1. **The tool owns its page.** CycleRoute passes `owned` on Home (`dataRoomOwnsHome`: the module page, no record, no lightbox; Upload at `/new` is drawn over it), so the page draws its header, the tool's tab bar and Home's blocks, and the module's own parts, and nothing generic, on a desktop or a phone. The URLs are unchanged: the old modes and a file's number still forward (D1109). The Files table is the Files tab's, so it is not kept. The card's diligence coverage is the one thing it said that Home did not, so Home now carries it as its Diligence checklist block, from D1105's checklist read (below). The card's opens by investor over ninety days are the Activity tab's By investor (D1108), which Home's last-ninety-days block already links to (D5072).
2. **The primary action is the module's own** (`homeAction`, RA-19's Complete data room), opening Checklist, where each missing item links to Match a file and Upload. None while the room is unread, failed, refused or an admin's summary; for a viewer the room's capability map does not let upload (D5074: a support reader, an admin); on first use, where the empty card's first action is the page's one primary (D5076); and on Checklist itself, where it leads. The tool's other tabs draw the same action from their own room read in place of Build your deck, so CycleRoute no longer hands them its generic action.
3. **Home keeps the one room read and reports its action up.** The room read needs the venture's uid from the venture context (`useVenture`, D5074), which CycleRoute provides around its page rather than holds; hoisting the read into CycleRoute would make a second projects read outside the provider. So Home keeps the read and reports the header's primary to CycleRoute through `onAction`, as it already reports a refusal through `onRefused` (D5079). No import specifier is added to CycleRoute (HUBS.md). On a phone the header is the bar, with no action, so Home draws Complete data room full width above its tabs.
4. **Home shows what diligence still needs** (RA-19 A's readiness block): "n of 12 checklist items have a file", the items no file answers named, and how many answered items no investor can open yet, from `GET /api/data-room/:p/checklist` on its own read; a failure there is Unreadable with Try again and blanks only that block. The figure is the read's own count of items (twelve items in six areas), so it says items, not areas.
5. **First use collapses to its set-up actions** (`isFirstUse`, `setupActions`): the room read answered with no file and no folder. Home is one card, "Nothing is in the room yet.", with **Upload files** (`/new`) and **See what diligence asks for** (Checklist). A viewer the map does not let upload gets one sentence, "The venture's owner uploads its files.", and nothing to press. A read still loading, failed or refused, or a summary, is never first use.
6. **No implementation-stage copy.** Checklist's readiness card says Fundraising readiness scores the data room, with its link, in place of "Not read here yet. … its figure is not carried to this page."

### What the boards draw that no read backs (reconciled against main)

Not built here, and nothing is made up in their place:

- **"8 of 12 diligence areas covered"** (RA-19 A). The checklist read counts twelve items in six areas; Home says "items have a file", which is what the read carries.
- **The Investor access figures** (Have access, NDA signed, Expires soon). Home's Who has access lists each grant in force with its NDA and end, and Needs you names an access ending soon; the three counts are not drawn as tiles, a follow-up of the Data room restyle, not this part.
- **The Investor activity feed on Home** (RA-19 A). The events are D1105's activity read, which the Activity tab draws; reading it again on Home would be a third read on opening the page. The board's own note asks for one Home endpoint instead of many requests (#5072, Worker): with it, Home can carry the feed.
- **The header's Add files beside Complete data room.** The module header carries one action; Upload is first use's set-up action, the Files tab's, and each missing checklist item's.
- **RA-19 B (support read-only)** is already D1464's reason gate (DataRoomSupport). Unchanged.
