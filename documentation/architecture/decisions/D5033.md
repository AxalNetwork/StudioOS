## D5033 — HQ Support: Send again and the SLA save say exactly where they are (#5033)

- **Status:** Built, 2026-11-08 (frontend). HQ Support audit 4 of 6.
- **Builds on:** D205 (the decision panel and `decisionError`), D243 (Send again resends the stored decision), D482 (the tier save), D5032 (the save's distribution).

### Send again

A rejected `api.escalationResend(uid)` went to `reportError` only, and the button could be pressed again while a resend was in flight.

- `resendStep` drives each record's resend state: `sending`, then the push's own answer (`delivered`, or the branch's new reason), or the request's failure.
- While a resend is sending, the button is disabled and reads "Sending…" (`aria-busy`). The page also ignores a second click.
- A failed request is said inline (`role="alert"`, with "Failed:" or "Unknown:" in words). The button stays as the retry.
- No status, or a 5xx, is **unknown**: HQ's recorded decision is unchanged, and whether the branch received it is not known. A 4xx is a refusal, with the route's sentence. A 404 means the escalation left the board.
- A delivered resend updates the delivery line in place, with the time.
- Recorded at HQ and received by the branch stay two facts.

### The SLA save

- `validateTierDraft` checks the server's own rules before anything is sent: whole hours from 1 to 720, a description of 1 to 200 characters, and P1 ≤ P2 ≤ P3. Problems are listed as an alert.
- While saving, the button reads "Saving and sending to branches…" and the inputs are disabled.
- `slaSavedSummary` gives the confirmation (`role="status"`): "Saved as policy vN. Applied on X of Y branches", plus each branch not yet applied, with its reason. Only every branch's acknowledgement reads as all.
- `slaSaveError` maps failures:
  - a 4xx, or the route's pre-write 503 `sla_tiers_unreadable`, is refused, with the reason;
  - no status, or another 5xx, is unknown. It asks for a reload before saving again and says not to treat the hours as applied.
- The decision panel's ambiguity rule (`decisionError`) is unchanged.
