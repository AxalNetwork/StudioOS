## D6187 — Asking the founder for missing figures is a diligence request (#6187)

**Decided by the owner, 2026-11-15: option A.** No new route. On a deal's
Traction tab, an investor who sees months the founder did not report gets
"Ask the founder for an update". It opens a diligence request on the deal's
case through the existing `POST /api/dd/cases/:uid/requests` (`dd.createRequest`).

**The draft.** `askDraft` (`pages/deal/dealInsightsModel.js`) is drawn only
when some figure has a gap in the last twelve months.
- It pre-fills the title with each figure that has a gap, for example "An
  update on MRR and Paying accounts".
- It pre-fills the details with each figure's unreported months.
- A fully reported figure is not asked for. An unreadable history asks
  nothing.
- The investor can edit both fields before sending.

**Who may send.**
- **No case:** the tab says so and links to Diligence to open one.
- **Case owner or lead:** sees the button.
- **Anyone else:** is told only the case's owner can ask.
- The Worker enforces the same rule: requests go out only from the case
  owner or an admin.

**What the founder sees.** The ordinary diligence request: the in-app
notification `dd_request_created` and the request in their requests list,
answered from there.

**Repeat asks.** Repeats are bounded by the request ledger, not a new rate
limit. Every request sits on the deal's Diligence tab with its state. The
investor reminds or withdraws an open request there, instead of sending
another.

**Not built.** A separate "update request" store and its rate limit (option B).

**Verified.** `frontend/test/deal_traction_ask_6187.test.mjs`:
- the draft's title and months; null when nothing is missing or the history
  is unreadable;
- the send goes through the deal's case, only for its owner or lead;
- the no-case pointer.

`deal_insights_tabs_d1154.test.mjs` pins the Traction tab taking the viewer.
