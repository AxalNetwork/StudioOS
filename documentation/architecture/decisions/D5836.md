### Routes (`/api/financial-statements`, read only)

| Route | Answer |
|---|---|
| `GET /:projectId?report=` | `{ items, read_at }`: the latest revision of each statement the venture holds (D5833 `readLatest`), each with `revisions`, `lines`, currency, basis, precision, the requested and returned periods and `period_mismatch` |
| `GET /:projectId/revisions/:uid` | `{ item }`: one exact revision, superseded or not (`readRevision`), with `superseded_by` |
| `GET /:projectId/pins` | `{ items }`: each pin with the revision it points at and whether a later revision superseded it (`readPins`) |

### Who

- **Signed in, then the venture's write access** (`canAccessProject` with `write: true`): its founder, a team member or an admin.
- **Advisors and investors are refused.** An advisor or investor on the venture gets the same `404 project_not_found` as a stranger, so no raw ledger figure reaches them here. This is #5836's rule: "No raw financial values may leak to an investor or advisor".
- **The store checks again:** it re-checks project access on every read (D5833).
- Each answer is `Cache-Control: private, no-store`.

### What the answer keeps

- **Amounts:** the store's exact text and minor units, as D5833 writes them. A line keeps its state (`value`, `zero`, `absent`, `unreadable`), and a negative stays negative.
- **No arithmetic:** nothing is summed, rounded or derived.
- **Corrections:** a correction is a new revision, and a pin keeps pointing at the revision it named.

### Refusals

- `project_not_found` (404): no access, no project, or an unreadable id.
- `snapshot_not_found` (404): the revision is not on this venture, or its uid is not a uid.
- `invalid_report` (400).
- `statements_unreadable` (503).
