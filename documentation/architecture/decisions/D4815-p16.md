### The statuses do not rest on colour

**Three status families, three shapes** (`DealCard.jsx`):
- **Company:** a square monospace tag marked `CO`.
- **My process:** a round violet pill with a dot.
- **Transaction:** a flat tag with a teal rule on its left.

**Each absence its own mark and word** (`ScopeScore.jsx`):
- Locked: a padlock, in bold.
- Unreadable: a ringed `!`.
- Not scored: a dashed line, in italic.
- Not enough recorded: a solid line.
- Not expected yet: a dotted circle.
- Not shared: a dashed square, with a dashed underline.

Every one keeps its shape in dark mode, and every mark carries a `dark:` colour. The table run asserts that each of these families is drawn.

The issue's "five absence states" are the canvas's five (Not recorded, Not reported, Not expected yet, Unreadable, Locked). The built score draws six, because D5900 split Not recorded into Not scored and Not enough recorded and added Not shared. All six are checked.
