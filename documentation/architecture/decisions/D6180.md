### What the card reads

Every figure comes from reads the tab already makes. No new read is added:
- `GET /api/dd/cases?subject_type=project&subject_id=` for the row;
- `GET /api/dd/cases/:uid` for the case.

Opening a case also reads `GET /api/dd/templates`, through the existing `ddDeskApi.templates`.

| Card part | From | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Open flags | the case read's `flags` (findings, and checklist items marked flag or fail) | Every state but Closed is open, as on the case's Flags tab (`openFlags`). Severest first. Each flag shows its severity as a word ("Critical severity"), its state, and its section, or "Case-wide". With none, the card reads "No open flags". If the read carried no list, it reads "Open flags not recorded", never none. Before the case read answers, it reads the list row's `open_flags` count. |
| Progress by section | `sections` and `checklist` | Items worked out of the section's own checklist (`sectionWork`), with a bar, plus the section's verdict in words. |
| Memo and Reports doors | `allowed.memo_read`, `allowed.report` | Both open the case's Sign-off tab, where the Memo and the reports are drawn. There is no Report tab or Memo tab, so the doors cannot open one. A section reviewer (`case.scoped`) gets no doors. |
| Investment committee | `case.decision`, `case.ic_record`, `allowed.decide`, and the row's `stage` | See below. |
| Case address | — | `/due-diligence/:uid`, with `/sign-off` for the doors. `/admin/due-diligence/:uid` is no longer linked from the deal page. |
| Template depth | `GET /dd/templates` → `templates[].depth`, `current.item_count` | The picker is drawn only when more than one known depth is offered. It defaults to Standard. A failed read still opens at Standard, and says so. |

### Departure: Send to IC follows the Worker, not "the ready stage"

The issue asks for Send to IC "when the case stage is 'Ready for a decision'", offered "only to owner/lead". It records who the Worker lets send as not checked. Here is what the Worker does:
- `POST /api/dd/cases/:uid/ic` takes the **case lead or an Admin** (`deciderRefusal`), not the opener once the lead has changed.
- It refuses with 409 unless **Proceed or Proceed with conditions** is recorded.
- It refuses with 409 `already_sent` a second time.

"Ready for a decision" (`READY_EXPR`: every section has a verdict, and the case is still open) is the stage *before* any decision. A button offered there would always be refused.

So `icStep` uses the case page's own rule (`icState`, D1251):
- **Ready for a decision:** "Every section has a verdict. Record Proceed or Proceed with conditions on the case before it goes to IC." The card shows a **Record the decision** door to `/due-diligence/:uid/sign-off`, where the decision dialog is.
- **Decided with Proceed (or with conditions), for a reader with `allowed.decide`:** **Send to IC** opens a dialog. Nothing is sent until the reader confirms. A refusal (archived, already sent, not the lead) stays in the dialog. A sent case shows "The case is with IC." and an **Open the Commit zone** link (`/deals/commit`).
- **Decided otherwise, or for a reader without `decide`:** the reason in one sentence. There is no button.
- **Already sent:** "With IC · status", and the Commit zone link.
- **Not started, in review, or a section reviewer's scoped read:** nothing.

The departure was posted on #6180 before the build.

### Tests

  - the memo door shown without `memo_read`;

- **Templates:**
  - an unknown depth offered;
  - a missing item count read as 0.
- **The drawn page:**
  - the flags list not drawn;
  - the section card dropped;
  - the decide door missing;
  - Send to IC sent without its dialog;
  - the refusal swallowed;
  - Send to IC drawn without `decide`;
  - the picker drawn for one template;
