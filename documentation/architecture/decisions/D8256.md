Both read stores that already exist: `raise_prospects.next_step` and `next_step_due` (migration 442), `raise_prospect_feedback` (463) and `narrative_objection_answers` (624).

### The Worker (`cloudflare-worker/src/services/raiseNextActions.ts`)

**`follow_ups_due`** lists the prospects that:
- are still in the raise (not `passed`);
- have a next step;
- have that step due on or before today + 6 days ("this week", as RA-02 says).

They come earliest first, at most 50, with `capped` past that. Each gives:
- `prospect_id`, its source record;
- `investor` (name and firm);
- `step`;
- `due_on`;
- `overdue`: before today.

The part's `href` is Capital relationships' Outreach tab, the list of follow-ups due (D1193, CRL8). The rule matches Capital relationships' own (`capRelModel.js` `overviewFigures`): a live prospect with a step and a day.

**`open_objections`** groups the venture's investor feedback as the Investor narrative does (D913):
- same words once case and spacing are set aside, keyed by `objectionKey`;
- the newest row's words are shown.

It keeps only the objections no saved section answers (migration 624, the owner's option A on D913). The board's rule is "has no answer in the narrative".

The order is most investors first, then most rows, then the words. Each gives:
- `key`, `text`, `count` and `prospects`;
- `investors`: who raised it;
- `href`: Story with that objection open (`?objection=<key>`, as "Answer it" opens it).

When the answers cannot be read, the whole part is unreadable: no objection reads as open on a failed read.

**Failure and staff reads:**
- Each part fails on its own, to `{ state: 'unreadable' }`. Neither ever reads as "nothing due" on a failed read.
- A staff read (`read_only`, D1191) gets both parts with `investor` and `investors` null; the counts stay. The investor-activity rule already worked this way (D6045).

### The frontend (`raiseOverviewModel.js` `nextActionsView`, `followUpAction`, `objectionAction`; `RaiseOverviewLead.jsx` `RaiseNextActions`)

Next actions are at most three, in the board's order:

| Action | When | Reason | Source line | Opens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Follow up with <investor or N investors> | any follow-up is due | "<who> has/have a next step due this week; N is/are overdue." Past three names: "and N others". | "From Capital relationships · earliest due <day>" | Outreach |
| Answer an open objection | any objection is open | "“<words>” was raised by N investors and has no answer in the narrative." | "From investor feedback · Raised by <who> · N more objections open" | Story on that objection |
| The readiness steps (D1466) | as before | as before | as before | as before |

The readiness steps keep their rules: biggest known gain first, Team never, and the neutral impact label.

Each action names its reason and its source record. A follow-up or an objection has no readiness impact, so it shows its source line instead of "Readiness impact".

**The heading:**
- "N actions to improve readiness" when every action is a readiness step, as before.
- "N actions to take next" otherwise.

**A part that could not be read:**
- It is named under the list: "<Follow-ups due / Open objections / Readiness steps> could not be read, so it is not listed. This is not a claim that nothing is due." The Unavailable chip and Try again go with it.
- The section is Unavailable only when every part present could not be read.
- With nothing left to list from what was read: "Nothing else to list from what could be read."

A read-only viewer gets no links, as before.
