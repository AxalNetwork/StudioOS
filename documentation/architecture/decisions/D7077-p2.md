## D7077 part 2 — The sweep's per-team discovery bound follows the elastic ceiling (#7077)

- **Status:** Built, part 2, 2026-11-22 (Worker only; no migration, no route, no frontend change). Part of #7077.
- **Scope:** S1's note of 2026-11-22: the per-scope sweep bound follows a team's elastic ceiling (D7074), keeping rotation fairness (D4592) and the #5586 boundary. Queue concurrency and batch sizing, and admitted-to-started p95 at 10, 50 and 100 runs, still wait on #5573's benchmark.
- **Builds on:** D7074 (`capacity_mode`, `elastic_max_runs`, HQ's `elastic_runs_ceiling`, `parallelLimit`), D4592 (the keyset cursor and the per-scope candidate cap), D7077 (the bounds named in `sweep.ts`, the model budget's boundary with #5586).
- **Switched on:** nothing. HQ's elastic ceiling is null on every database until a Super Admin sets it, and while it is null every team reads as fixed, so the sweep makes the same offers it made before this change.

### The bound

`readyAfter` in `cloudflare-worker/src/agents/sweep.ts` cut each team (scope) at `DISCOVERY_PER_SCOPE_MAX` (4) candidates a sweep. Each team's cut is now `scope_bound`:

| The team | Its bound |
|---|---|
| `fixed` (every team today), or no settings row | 4, as before |
| `elastic`, HQ's elastic ceiling not set or withdrawn | 4, as before: it reads as fixed |
| `elastic`, HQ's ceiling set | `parallelLimit` less its live runs, at least 0, at most `DISCOVERY_ELASTIC_SCOPE_MAX` (30) |

- **`parallelLimit` as admission reads it.** The team's own `elastic_max_runs`, else HQ's ceiling, never above HQ's ceiling and never below 1: the same figure `admitInScope` refuses at (`scope_busy`). The SQL repeats `capacityOf`'s range rule for `elastic_max_runs` (a whole number 1 to 500, else unset), and a test checks the SQL's figures against `parallelLimit`'s.
- **Less the team's live runs.** Admission refuses once the team's `admitted` and `running` runs reach the limit, so discovery offers only what could start. A team at its limit is offered nothing and spends no inspection. Lanes are unchanged: an agent is still offered one task per free lane (D7074).
- **A ceiling is read at the sweep, not copied into a team's row** (D7074). The sweep already read HQ's ceilings once (`capacityCeilings`, for lanes); the elastic ceiling from that same read is bound into the statement. Withdrawing it takes effect at the next sweep.

### Why a fixed team keeps 4

Admission's fixed limit is `max_parallel_runs` (1 to 5, 2 by default), not 4. Making the fixed bound follow it (or it less live runs) would change what every team on main is offered today: a default team would drop from 4 candidates to 2, a team at 5 would rise to 5. The issue's ready scope is the elastic bound with nothing switched on, and a fixed team's 4 has been the D4592 rule since #4592; the extra candidates a fixed team is offered beyond its limit are refused at admission and cost at most 4 inspections. So a fixed team keeps exactly today's bound. Aligning it with `max_parallel_runs` is option B below.

### Why the cap is 30

`DISCOVERY_ELASTIC_SCOPE_MAX = SWEEP_MAX / 2`. An elastic team's limit may be up to 500, and the scan is ordered by agent id, so without a cap one team with low ids would take every one of the sweep's 60 admissions and 120 inspections. At 30, one elastic team takes at most half a sweep's admissions (the same half D7077 §2's model budget gives a lone team of a model's minute) and a quarter of its inspections, so other teams are still reached in the same scan.

Two elastic teams can still fill a sweep's 60 admissions between them. The cursor then resumes after the last agent examined, so any other team waits at most one more sweep, the D4592 guarantee; a test holds it with the real admission. `SWEEP_MAX` (60), `DISCOVERY_SCAN_MAX` (120) and `RESEND_MAX` (20) are unchanged; raising them waits on #5573.

### One statement

The bound is computed inside the one discovery statement: a `bounds` row of the four bound values, and a `scope_live` aggregate of live runs per team, joined to each agent's row. `scope_live` is empty while no elastic ceiling is offered, so the common path does no extra work, and there is no statement per team. A test counts the statements: eight elastic teams make as many as one fixed team.

### Boundary with #5586

#5586 owns fair admission per venture, workload modality and provider. This bound is per team (the `scope_kind`, company, project of `fairly_scoped`), never per venture: two teams of one venture each get their own bound, and one team's live runs never come off another's. Discovery reads no venture state and no model-budget state (`agent_model_admissions`); the model budget (D7077 §2) still throttles only dispatch. A test sets a tiny `AGENT_MODEL_RPM` and sees the same offers.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/agent_sweep_elastic_7077.test.ts`, 8 cases over the migrated schema with the real sweep (a recording runtime for the offers, the real admission for the rotation):

- no ceiling (no policy row, a null ceiling; no settings rows, elastic and fixed rows): every team 4, the same candidates in the same order;
- an elastic team under the ceiling at its own number, at HQ's when unset, never above HQ's; a fixed team beside it at 4;
- live runs off an elastic team's bound, a full team offered nothing;
- a withdrawn ceiling and a deleted policy row read as fixed at the next sweep, with the team's row unchanged;
- the 30 cap, and the 120 scan with six elastic teams;
- the rotation with the real admission: two elastic teams fill a sweep, the cold team is reached on the next, the rotation wraps;
- one discovery statement, and no more statements for many elastic teams;
- #5586: the bound per team, not per venture; no model-budget or venture state read.

Mutation check: 16 mutations, 14 caught, 2 equivalent (`scope-live-guard`: the guard only empties `scope_live` while no ceiling is offered, and that path never reads it; `setting-range`: the column's CHECK and `capacityWrite` keep any other value out of the row, so the SQL's range rule is defensive).

### Owner decisions

- **A (built).** A fixed team keeps 4. **B.** A fixed team's bound follows `max_parallel_runs` less its live runs, as an elastic team's does: fewer wasted inspections, but a behaviour change for every team today. **C.** A fixed team's bound is `max(4, …)` of B. A stays until the owner chooses.
- The cap of 30 is chosen from the sweep's own budget, not measured. #5573's benchmark may move it with `SWEEP_MAX`.
