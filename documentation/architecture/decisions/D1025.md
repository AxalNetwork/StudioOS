### 1. History (CT10) at `/raise/cap-table/history`

History is the tool's fifth tab, between Scenarios and Access, in the canvas's order. Two blocks, each its own read, each paged with its own exact total:

- **The timeline.** The record's issuances, newest first by the date issued: recorded, void (struck through, with its reason) and dismissed rows. A proposal waits in Needs you, not here. Each row shows:
  - the date;
  - its line in words, for example "Northwind Angels · SAFE recorded · $150,000 · post-money cap $10,000,000", or "Correction: ‘reason’ on [holder]’s record · −100,000 Common";
  - who recorded, voided or dismissed it. An admin is "an admin" and is never named;
  - its document by kind (data-room file, signed legal document, SAFE sent through StudioOS) or the amber "! No document";
  - its source.

  **The toolbar:** search (holder or note, sent on Search), Type, Source, Has document, and a date range. A range that runs backwards is refused on the page and never sent. **Pages of 25.**
- **Changes to scenarios and access:** each scenario edit, deletion or new variant, each grant or removal, and each share link made or revoked, with who did it and when. Pages of 10.

A failed read says so in its block, never "Nothing is recorded yet", and the other block still draws. A recorded row offers the owner **Void** and an admin **Correct**.

**The Worker: one read, two sections.** D1017's history read merged issuances with the cap table's own `captable.%` audit rows, and paged them together. Scenario and access changes are logged as `captable_scenario_*`, `captable_variant_created`, `captable_access_*` and `captable_link_*`, so they were not in it at all. `GET /api/captable/record/:projectId/history` now also takes:

- `section=record`: the timeline. Its filters are `q`, `kind`, `source`, `has_document` (a document or a linked SAFE), `from` and `to` on the date issued.
  - Each filter is a bound optional value in one fixed query (`TIMELINE_COUNT`, `TIMELINE_PAGE`). A `%` or `_` in the search is a character, not a wildcard.
  - A bad value is 400, never ignored.
  - Each row carries `recorded_by_who`, `voided_by_who` and `dismissed_by_who` as `{ name, admin }`, with an admin's name left out.
- `section=changes`: the activity rows above, newest first.

With no section, the read is D1017's, unchanged; the export (D1026) still pages it. The gates are the record's.

### 2. The proposal drawers (CT11) at `/raise/cap-table/proposal/<id>`

This is the tool's one route of its own, `guard(labRoles(['admin', 'founder']))`, drawn by CycleRoute (`capTableProposal`) over Overview. Needs you's Review opens it in place of D1022's dialog. Each Accept says in one line what it records and that "Co-founders see it at once; investors as their access allows."

- **`mercury-<n>`: a Mercury SAFE request (CT11a).**
  - It shows the facts as Mercury sends them (investor, amount, post-money cap, request status, date, from).
  - The holder and the post-money basis are preset.
  - The discount reads "Not recorded", with "Mercury sends none. Enter it if the SAFE has one."
  - Accept is D840's (`POST /api/cycles/raise/mercury/proposals/:id/accept`), which now takes `discount_bps`. The record's writer checks it as it checks every field: a bad one is 400 `bad_discount_bps`, and the request is handed back to be accepted again.
  - Only the owner sees Mercury's requests; anyone else is told so.
- **A close's proposal (CT11b).**
  - The record read now gives each close proposal its `close_uid`, through its prospect (`raise_prospect:<uid>`). It is looked up only when such a row is in hand, so a venture with no close reads no raise table.
  - The drawer reads the close (D1021) and lists its investors with their class, shares and price, each "Signed" or "Not signed yet".
  - "Record [n] issuances" accepts the signed rows; an unsigned row waits. "This is the same proposal as the closing room’s: accepted once, here or there."
- **Any other proposal** (a co-founder's, an import's, the owner's): its line, then Accept or Dismiss with an optional reason. A refused Accept names what the proposal still lacks.

A co-founder opens any of them and reads that only the owner decides.

### 3. Eadwyn's draft (CT11c)

In Record an issuance, a data-room file chosen as the document offers "Let Eadwyn read its terms". This is D1021's `POST …/document-read`, metered on the Cap table page's rail row. The draft drawer shows:

- each term Eadwyn found (holder, instrument type, basis, MFN, date signed) with its page, or "Not found in the document";
- "Amounts are yours to enter": each money field's input is empty, its placeholder is "Enter from page [n]" or "Not found in the document", and nothing fills it.

**Accept** opens once the amount and the cap are entered (or the share count, for shares). It records, or for a co-founder proposes, with `source: 'document'` and the file. **Discard** decides the rail proposal (`POST /api/ai/rail/proposals/:uid/decide`, `discard`) and records nothing.

**Not here:** the option itself (`draft:raise/document-terms`) is not added to D771's founder inventory or drawn with `useRailOption`. The run takes the page's row, as D1021 §4 allows until then.

### 4. The dialogs (CT12)

- **Record an issuance (CT12a)** replaces Add holder at `/raise/cap-table/new`.
  - It opens over Overview, and CycleRoute no longer opens the module's Add form there.
  - **The seven types:** founding, priced, SAFE, convertible note, option grant, exercise, transfer. Each asks only for its fields:
    - a holder from the record or a new name;
    - the class, or the pool for a grant or an exercise;
    - shares, price or strike;
    - amount, cap, basis, discount, interest and maturity for money;
    - the date, and a document from the data room.
  - **Units:** values go in the record's units (the currency's smallest unit, price in micros, basis points). A blank field is left out, so it reads "Not recorded", never 0.
  - What is missing is named before anything is sent, and the Worker's refusal shows in place.
  - **The footer:** "This is recorded, not modelled. Co-founders see it at once."
  - **By role:** a co-founder's dialog is "Propose an issuance", which waits for the owner. An admin's carries the reason the founder will see.
  - **Vesting:** the canvas's Vesting section is not drawn. The record's row holds no vesting, and a grant's schedule is the equity plan's (D1020), which Option pool writes.
  - The tool bar's "Record an issuance" (a co-founder's "Propose an issuance") and CT19a's "Record founding shares" open it. Below Raise + Grow, the tool bar's CT24 note stands instead.
- **Void (CT12b):**
  - It shows the date, holder and figures, and "It stays in History, struck through, with your reason."
  - The reason is required. Nothing is sent without it.
  - It is offered on each recorded row of History and of the holder drawer, which now carries Void per row instead of one footer button.
- **Correct a record (CT12c), an admin's.** It shows the row, the share count it should have, and the reason the founder will see (required). It records a correction for the difference (D1017), linked to the row, and never edits it. It is offered on recorded share rows (not a SAFE, a note or a correction) in History and the holder drawer.
