## D6086 — Cap table: record a vesting schedule on a grant, without Carta (#6086)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-15. RA-27's Vesting block of the Raise v2 Cap Table and Use of Funds canvas (D6088), with the parts of RA-28 and RA-29 that carry it. Owner question 1 of D6088 (where vesting lives) is built as drawn, option A: one schedule per grant.
- **Why:** a founder who does not use Carta could not record an employee's or advisor's vesting. Record an issuance had no vesting fields, Add holder offered vesting to founders only, and every other `cap_table_vesting` row came from a Carta sync, so Option pool › Grants and the holder's Vesting block stayed empty.

### What the record keeps

- **The body.** `POST /api/captable/record/:projectId/issuances`, `/proposals` and `/proposals/:uid/accept` take `vesting: { start_date, total_months, cliff_months }` on a `grant`. It is checked by `cleanVesting` (`services/capTableWrites.ts`, the validator Add holder's founders use, now exported), plus a real calendar day and at most 600 months (`VESTING_MONTHS_MAX`). A schedule on any other kind, or one that fails the check, is `400 bad_vesting`. An Accept with no `vesting` keeps the proposal's; `vesting: null` clears it.
- **Two places (migration `202611152340_6086_grant_vesting.sql`).** `cap_table_issuance_vesting` is the schedule an issuance carries, one row per grant, written in the issuance's own batch. `cap_table_vesting.issuance_id` ties the plan's row, the table the equity plan, the holder drawer and the Raise readers already read, to the grant. The plan row is written only when the grant is recorded (directly or on Accept), on the holder's account as Add holder writes a founder's, with the grant's options as `total_shares` and `source = 'manual'`. So a co-founder's proposal reaches no plan reader before the owner accepts it, and a dismissed one never does.
- **Void** deletes the plan row with the grant, in the void's batch. The struck-through row keeps its schedule in History, as it keeps its other terms.
- **Carta.** While Carta is the venture's record (a Carta plan row or pool for the venture, or an issuance recorded from Carta), a schedule is `409 carta_is_record`: Carta's plan is read only here (D1020 §4). A grant without a schedule still records.
- **Reads.** `GET /record/:projectId` gives each grant `vesting` (the schedule, or null). `GET /equity-plan?project_id=` gives each grant `issuance_uid`, `unvested_shares`, `vested_basis` and `vested_as_of`.

### Vested is worked out from the dates, only where nobody reported it

The decision the issue asked for. A row with a stored vested count keeps it (`vested_basis: 'recorded'`), so Carta's stay as imported and are never recomputed, as D1020 kept them. A row StudioOS holds with no stored count (a grant's schedule, and a founder's from Add holder, which never stored one) is worked out on the read from its dates (`'schedule'`, `vested_as_of` the day used): nothing before the cliff, at the cliff the whole months since the start, then one month at a time, rounded down, up to the total. A month completes on the start's day of the month, or on the last day of a shorter month. Nothing is stored, so the figure cannot go stale. A Carta row with no count stays null. `vestedOnSchedule` and `monthsElapsed` (`services/capTableRegister.ts`) take the day as a parameter: the route passes today's date, the tests fixed ones. The screens say "Worked out from the schedule on <day>" under the figure.

### Screens

- **Record an issuance, Option grant** (`IssuanceDialogs.jsx`): a Vesting fieldset after Granted on, with the three presets as a radio group (4 years, 1-year cliff; 4 years, no cliff; Custom), Start, Months and Cliff months, and the Modelled preview with "Worked out from the schedule typed above. It is not the record." A cliff longer than the total says "The cliff can’t be longer than the vesting: 60 months is more than 48." and hides the preview; nothing is sent. A co-founder's Propose an issuance sends the same schedule.
- **The proposal drawer** (`ProposalDrawer.jsx`) shows the schedule, or No schedule; Accept sends nothing extra, because the schedule travels with the proposal on the Worker.
- **Option pool › Grants** (`OptionPoolTab.jsx`, `poolModel.js`): an Unvested column, the basis line under Vested, and "On the grant" under a schedule written with a grant. A plan row written with a grant reads that grant's strike and date, not the holder's latest. With ten columns the table keeps a 1,040px minimum and scrolls inside its card, at a desk and on a phone, instead of wrapping each cell.
- **The holder drawer** (`HolderDrawer.jsx`, `overviewModel.js`): Vesting shows vested and unvested and the basis line.
- Desktop, the 390 phone sheet and dark follow the existing dialog and table; checked in Chromium at 1440 and 390, light and dark, with no page wider than the screen.

### Departures from the canvas

- The grant keeps today's Record an issuance dialog, with Vesting added inside it. RA-27's side sheet, the pool room after the grant, the Note field and the source line are D6088's frontend work, not this issue's.
- Start has no default. A blank Start records the grant without a schedule and says so under the presets; the canvas draws Start filled. Nothing is guessed from Granted on.
- RA-28 B's review page with Edit then accept is not built: the existing proposal drawer shows the schedule and accepts it.
- RA-27 D's highlighted new row and toast, and RA-29's other states (no pool, a pool with no size, over the pool, the refusals and the conflict), are not built here.
- The dialog does not know beforehand that Carta is the record. The Worker's `carta_is_record` sentence shows under the buttons.

### Pending the owner

Of D6088's four questions only Q1 touches this issue; it is built as option A. Q2 (warn or refuse a grant over its pool) waits: no refusal at save is added. Eadwyn drafting the pool and vesting from a grant letter (RA-27 B's ◇ #6086 tag) is not built and needs its own slice.

### Limits

- A grant's schedule is fixed when it is recorded. An admin correction of the grant's share count does not change the plan row's total; void and record again.
- Accept writes the schedule only onto a proposal that was already a grant.
