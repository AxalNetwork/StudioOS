### 2. What is read, and from where

Each read answers alone. A failed read costs only the blocks it feeds.

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/admin/studio/glance`, lanes `events` and `jobs` | Waiting on you; the Events and Job board figures on the consoles strip |
| `GET /api/admin/events?status=published` and `?status=pending_review`, every page | The next 30 days; Next up; the events half of Needs a decision |
| `GET /api/admin/events/analytics`, its rows only, under D915's Guard G | Seats taken on upcoming events |
| `GET /api/admin/jobs`, every page | Postings by status; most applications; the jobs half of Needs a decision |
| `GET /api/admin/circles` | Published circles; circles by type; published, draft and featured; members as entered; drafts on the strip |
| `GET /api/admin/network-profiles` | Active on the roster; the roster by kind |

- **Every list is read to its end.** A list longer than 10,000 rows is refused, not drawn as a partial total.
- **The analytics `summary` is never read.** It counts all time, and the page's figures are "now" or "next 30 days". Its rows stop at the 200 latest starts. So, under Guard G:
  - an event missing from them reads "Not in this read";
  - rows that failed, or no rows while events exist, read "could not be read";
  - neither is ever drawn as 0.
- **The roster read is reduced to the kind and whether a row is active, at the read.** No name reaches the page's state.
- **Two reads the prompt named are refused:**
  - `GET /api/wellbeing/aggregate` decrypts every pulse in the window to average six scores. It also logs an access each time. The canvas wants a check-in band and no averages, so this page never calls it. Part 1 counts the band without reading answers.
  - `GET /api/wellbeing/experts` ranks and stops at 50 experts, and its count includes the sample experts. So "verified of N" would be wrong past 50, and wrong with samples. It waits for part 1.
- **A lane's `oldest_at` is when the item was created, not when it was submitted.** So the page says how long nothing has waited. "Waiting [n] days" and Overdue wait for the submitted times: #2700 for events, part 1 for jobs. Overdue then uses D915's rule: more than 72 hours.
- **The words are the canvas's,** with one correction (§5).
  - "Not read here yet" is a figure a store holds but no read sums. It always names the part that brings it.
  - "Not recorded" is a figure no store holds.
