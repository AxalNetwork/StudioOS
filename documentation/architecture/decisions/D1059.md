### 1. Migration 580

A report is still one `portfolio_updates` row (#2427: never a second updates store). The columns it adds:

| Column | What |
|---|---|
| `kind` | `update` (Share an update, D654) or `report`. Every existing row is an update. |
| `figures_json` | The report's figures, each with its value, source, `as_of` and the founders' choice. Re-read from the stores while a draft; frozen when sent. |
| `figures_sha256` | SHA-256 of `figures_json`. Send refuses when the draft the founder read is not the one stored. |
| `revision`, `corrects_update_id` | 1 for a first send; a correction is the next revision, pointing at the one it corrects. |
| `correction_reason` | What the correction changes, in the founders' words (RPT6). Not in the issue's list; a correction needs it, so it is added here. |

`report_documents` holds a statement: a PDF in the private `FILES` bucket under `reports/<project uid>/<report uid>/<document uid>.pdf`, with its size and SHA-256. The key never leaves the Worker.

**What the database holds itself:**
- a trigger refuses any change to a sent report's figures, digest, revision or chain;
- a revision is corrected at most once (a unique index on `corrects_update_id`);
- a venture has at most one first-revision draft per period window (a partial unique index), so preparing twice opens the same draft.

### 2. REPORT_FIGURES (`services/reportFigures.ts`)

One registry of the figures a report carries: each key, its label, its Grow module, its unit, the sources it may come from, and the other keys it is known by.

- **It unifies the two key sets.** Share an update's five (`METRIC_KEYS`: `mrr`, `growth`, `customers`, `burn`, `runway`) and the KPI rule keys of `portfolio_kpi_definitions` (migration 168: `cash_balance`, `net_burn`, `runway_months`, `revenue`, `arr`, `headcount`, `gross_margin`, `net_retention`) map onto one key per figure: `burn` is `net_burn`, `runway_months` is `runway`.
- **It adds the other modules' counts:** open roles, signed partners, markets entering, items shipped and the durability checklist done (RPT3's sections).
- **Two figures have no store yet.** `revenue` (`revenue_movements` holds MRR movements, not a period's revenue) and `gross_margin` are always "Not recorded", with that reason. They are never computed from something else.
- **Required figures.** No signed clause names a figure yet, so D1058 stores `required_figures` empty. Here a duty with figures keeps them. Otherwise a monthly financial duty requires migration 168's required monthly KPI rules, and a quarterly KPI duty requires those and the quarterly ones; other kinds require none. The duty row is never rewritten. A test holds `KPI_RULES_REQUIRED` to the migration's seed rows.

### 3. The assembler (`assembleReport(projectId, period)`)

It reads each module's stores as they are now. Each figure comes with its value, its source and its `as_of`.

| Module | Figures and store |
|---|---|
| Revenue and traction | MRR, customers, ARR, net revenue retention, net burn and cash: the latest `project_metrics` snapshot in the period recording each. Growth is computed from this period's MRR against the last one before it. Cash falls back to Mercury's `cash` fact. |
| Durable company-building | Runway from cash over burn, else Mercury's `runway` fact; the checklist items done by the period's end. |
| Team expansion | Headcount from a snapshot, else the active employees on the roster; open roles. |
| Partner network, Market expansion | Signed partners; markets entering. |
| Product improvement | Items shipped in the period (`shippedItems`). |

- **Source words** are PIPE-RAISE's (#2430): a Stripe or Mercury figure is Connector, a typed snapshot is Typed, a store count is Recorded. A computed figure carries the word of what it read and `computed: true`.
- **Nothing missing becomes 0.** An unrecorded figure is `value: null` with its reason. A store that cannot be read makes its module "Unreadable", while the other modules still answer.
- **Team only.** A figure from the founder's Mercury is `team_only`, and so is a runway computed from one. On one date a Stripe or typed row is preferred to a Mercury one.
  - In a draft, the value is shown only to the venture's team (`isTeamViewer`); anyone else reads "Team only".
  - It goes into a report only when the venture's founder ticks it in their own session (`isVentureFounder`, never a support session). Anyone else's tick is refused, not ignored, and each share is a `mercury_figure_shared` event holding the key, never the value.
  - Each Mercury-derived figure is ticked on its own: sharing the burn does not share the runway computed from it.

### 4. The routes (`routes/reporting_reports.ts`, on `/api/reporting`)

**The founders** (the owner and accepted co-founders, `founderTeam`) prepare, choose and send. An admin is refused (#2427 owner question 6), an advisor too, and a support session writes nothing.

| Route | What |
|---|---|
| `POST /periods/:uid/draft` | The period's report, assembled with the defaults: a recorded figure in, a team-only one out. It covers every open period of the venture with the same window, so one Q3 report serves every investor owed Q3. Preparing twice opens the same draft. Writes `draft_built`. |
| `PUT /reports/:uid` | Title, the founders' words, highlights and lowlights (every line cites its source, D654's rule), figures in or out with a reason, a Mercury figure shared, at most eight typed figures ("Typed · entered by [company]"), the team or not, and a correction's reason. Each save re-reads the stores and stores a new digest. |
| `GET /reports/:uid/preview?as=investor\|team` | Exactly what that group reads, the periods it meets and what each would be sent without, the digest Send needs, and `stale` when the stores changed since the save. Writes `preview_opened`. |
| `POST /reports/:uid/send` | See below. |
| `POST /reports/:uid/correct` | `{ reason }`, 3 to 280 characters. A new draft revision of a sent report, with its choices and statements carried over and its figures re-read. Once per revision. |
| `GET /reports/:uid` | See "Reading" below. |
| `POST /reports/:uid/documents` | A PDF on a draft: checked by its `%PDF-` header, at most 6 MB, at most ten per report. |
| `GET /documents/:uid` | The statement, `private, no-store`, as an attachment with `nosniff` and a sandbox CSP. To the founders; once sent, to its recipients, an admin and the venture's readers. A draft's statement is the founders' only. |

**Send** (`{ figures_sha256 }`):
1. The digest must be the stored one (D712's `contentChanged`, 409).
2. Every sourced figure is read again from its store, with the saved choices. A drift is 409 `figures_changed`: a value the browser held is never what is sent.
3. Each required figure left out needs a reason (400 `reason_required`, naming the figures). The investor reads the reason.
4. **#2411's privacy check** (`lintForSend`) runs over every text the report sends: title, words, lines, reasons, typed labels and the correction's reason. A finding names its kind and where, never the words it caught (422 `pii_found`).
5. **Who receives it:**
   - each holder owed a covered period;
   - the founder team when chosen;
   - for a correction, everyone the last revision reached.

   Never the sender, never the network, never an advisor.
6. **One batch:**
   - the send is the first statement and carries every precondition;
   - the recipients follow, then each covered period marked met (`met_update_id`, `met_at`; never one waived in the meantime);
   - then one `sent` event per period (or `corrected`), with the digest's prefix and the recipient count;
   - then one `figure_left_out` event per left-out figure, with its reason.
7. Each recipient is told through their notification settings. The answer lists each period met and what it was sent without ("sent without [figure]: [reason]"), and any period waived during the send, which is not met.

**Reading** (`GET /reports/:uid`):
- **The founders** see the team view: every figure (Mercury values only to the team), the periods with their holders, and the recipients with delivered and opened.
- **A recipient** reads the frozen report.
  - An investor sees only what their own duty requires, and never another recipient.
  - Their first read sets `update_recipients.opened_at` and writes one `opened` event.
- **An admin, an advisor or a partner of the venture** reads a sent report read-only.
- **A corrected revision** says "corrected by revision n on [date]" with the reason. It keeps its own frozen figures, and its periods stay met by the first revision.

**Share an update's routes refuse a report row.** `PUT /:uid`, `POST /:uid/submit`, `PUT /:uid/share` and `POST /:uid/send` return 409 `report_row`, so no report is changed or sent outside the freeze and its checks. A report keeps `audience_json`'s `investors`, `team` and `network` false, so D889's grants admit only its recorded recipients.

**The client:** `reportingApi`'s `draftReport`, `report`, `saveReport`, `previewReport`, `sendReport`, `correctReport`, `attachReportDocument` and `reportDocument` (a blob with the session's token, as `fundNotices.js` reads a PDF).
