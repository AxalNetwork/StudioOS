## D6296 — The diligence report carries the checklist, the requests, the decision and the memo (#6296)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-14 for #6296. Worker, plus one sentence on the page. No migration.
- **Number:** D6296 is this issue's number (D4106).
- **Code:**
  - `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/dd.ts`: `POST /api/dd/cases/:uid/report` decides who is on the deal team, reads the new parts and hands them to the renderer.
  - `cloudflare-worker/src/services/dueDiligence.ts`: `loadReportParts` reads them; `renderReportHtml`, `renderReportPdf` and `renderReportArtifact` take an optional `ReportExtras` and draw them.
  - `frontend/src/pages/duediligence/DdCaseDialogs.jsx`: `ReportDialog` says what the report carries.
- **Test:** `cloudflare-worker/test/dd_report_contents_6296.test.ts`. It runs the real router over SQLite built from the baseline plus migrations 339, 620, 621, 622 and 623. Each of its 16 source mutations is caught.

### Why

D1246 §7 recorded that the generated report "does not yet carry the memo, checklist, requests or sign-offs". The Sign-off tab offers Generate report and Send to IC, so the paper the IC reads left out most of the case.

### What changed

The report now carries these parts. Each is read from the stored case at generation, and no model writes any of it.

| Part | Read from | Drawn |
|---|---|---|
| The decision | `dd_cases.decision`, `decided_by` (a name from `users`), `decided_at` | "Proceed with conditions · recorded by … · day" |
| Its reason | `dd_cases.decision_reason_enc`, decrypted | under the decision |
| The memo | `dd_case_memos`: summary, verified, on trust, conditions, decrypted | the four parts, with the Sign-off tab's labels |
| Each section's checklist | `dd_checklist_items`: title, status, severity | in its section: status (with severity on a flag or fail) and evidence count |
| Evidence count | `dd_item_evidence`, counted per item | "No evidence", "1 piece of evidence", "n pieces of evidence" |
| Verdicts and sign-off (D466) | `dd_sections.verdict`, `signed_off_by`, `completed_at` | kept in the HTML; **now also drawn in the PDF**, which had left it out |
| Section flags (D1247) | `dd_findings` with a section: state, resolution | kept |
| Case-wide flags | `dd_findings` with no section | a block of their own. The report used to drop them. |
| Requests | `dd_requests`: title, section, state, and the dates asked, due, answered, reviewed, withdrawn | one line each. A withdrawn request reads Withdrawn. |

- **An empty part reads "Not recorded".** This covers no decision, no reason, a memo part never written (or no memo), a section with no checklist, no requests and no case-wide flags. Nothing stands in for a missing value.
- **An evidence read that fails** reads "Evidence could not be read" on each item. It never reads as "No evidence".
- **The HTML and the PDF draw the same parts in the same order:** summary, decision, memo, sections, case-wide flags, requests.

### Who gets which parts

The route's gate is unchanged: a TOTP session, a recent step-up, and a case writer who is an Admin or holds the partner licence (D1245 `report`).

- **The deal team gets every part.** That is an Admin, the case lead or the opener: the same people `memo_read` names (`caseAllowed`, `GET /memo`).
- **A section reviewer who holds the partner licence reaches the route today.** That was checked, and the test pins it. Their copy follows the case read's rules for them:
  - **Not in their copy:** the memo (they do not hold `memo_read`), the decision's reason, the requests and the case-wide flags. Each of these reads "Not in this copy: only an Admin or the case lead or opener reads this part." They are not read from the database for this caller.
  - **In their copy:** the decision itself.
  - **The checklist:** only for the sections they review. Every other section's checklist reads "Not in this copy".
- **A section reviewer without the partner licence** (an investor or advisor) is refused with 403, as before, and nothing is rendered.

### The founder-facing share

`POST /cases/:uid/report/share` is unchanged. It sends the subject a notice and nothing of the report. The test pins what the subject receives:
- the one `notifications_inbox` row: type, title, body, no link, no payload, and category;
- the response `{ ok: true }`;
- no memo text, reason, decision, checklist item or flag;
- no report generated by sharing.

### Left as it is

- **A section reviewer's copy still has every section's verdict, flags and reviewer notes, and the case notes**, as the report gave them before this change. The case read scopes these to their sections. Narrowing that part of the report is a separate change. This change only makes sure the new parts do not widen what that reader sees.
- **The report list and re-download** (`GET /cases/:uid/reports`, `POST …/reports/:id/download` behind step-up) are unchanged. The test checks the band at generation on the list.
- **Request details and the subject's answer text** stay out of the report. It draws the title, state and dates.
