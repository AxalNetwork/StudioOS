## D4648 — Learning discovery and reporting say what they count (#4648)

#4648 (Learning audit 3/5, slot S12, taken at the owner's request).

### Decisions

- **Exercises and Exams search by title.** `GET /api/learning/exercises` and `/exams` take `q` (title only, `LIKE … ESCAPE`, wildcards literal, 100 characters at most) and the page sends it. The offset is keyed to tab and query, so a new query starts at the first page and Back restores the query with its own first page and total. "No exams title matches “q”" is a different screen from "nothing published", "could not be read" and "reading".
- **Your progress names its figures.** Each tile carries its source, definition and window. "Watch time this month" is retired: the store records a resume position, not time watched, so the figure was not what its label said. The interim measure is **videos played this month** (a video with a recorded position above zero, updated in the month). Not recorded stays "Not recorded", never 0.

- Course-in-progress and completion totals agreeing with the destination lists: that depends on #4643 and #4646 and is not claimed here.
