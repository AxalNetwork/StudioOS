## D4648 — Learning discovery and reporting say what they count (#4648)

#4648 (Learning audit 3/5, slot S12, taken at the owner's request).

### Decisions

- **Exercises and Exams search by title.** `GET /api/learning/exercises` and `/exams` take `q` (title only, `LIKE … ESCAPE`, wildcards literal, 100 characters at most) and the page sends it. The offset is keyed to tab and query, so a new query starts at the first page and Back restores the query with its own first page and total. "No exams title matches “q”" is a different screen from "nothing published", "could not be read" and "reading".
- **Your progress names its figures.** Each tile carries its source, definition and window. "Watch time this month" is retired: the store records a resume position, not time watched, so the figure was not what its label said. The interim measure is **videos played this month** (a video with a recorded position above zero, updated in the month). Not recorded stays "Not recorded", never 0.

### Part 2: the totals agree with their lists (after #4643 and #4646)

- **Cause of a disagreement.** `GET /api/learning/me` read the learner's whole record with one `LIMIT 500` over every kind. A learner with more than 500 item or lesson rows more recent than their course rows got no course in Continue learning, while "Courses in progress" (`/me/stats`, a count of the course rows with status started) still counted them.
- **Fix.** `/me` reads course rows and item/lesson rows separately, each with its own bound, and merges them newest first. The tile and the list now read the same course rows. A completed course, another learner's rows and a course whose course row is gone are counted alike by both; the last one stays in the list with its title and link "not recorded" instead of disappearing.
- **Lessons completed this month** equals the completed lesson rows of the record whose completion fell in the same UTC month; a test holds the two equal.
- **Completion** itself is #4643's derivation (`refreshCourses`); this change reads it, it does not redefine it.

### Watch duration: kept on the interim measure

The home keeps **Videos played this month** (the store records a resume position, which moves back on a rewind and says nothing of pauses or seeks). Collecting bounded playback intervals (pause, seek, replay, deduplication of duplicate or late events, month attribution) needs a player event and a table, so it is not built here. The two criteria that apply only if a watch-duration label comes back are met by there being no such label, and are ticked with that reason.
