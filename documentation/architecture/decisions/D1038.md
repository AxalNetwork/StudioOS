### 2. What is read, and from where

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/admin/studio/glance` (`requireAdmin`) | Every lane's count and oldest age, the tiles, the chart, the Inbox lanes, the Lanes tab |
| `GET /api/comarketing/admin/queue?status=` (five reads, one per state) | The Co-marketing board's tabs and rows |
| `GET /api/wellbeing/experts?limit=50` | The Wellbeing board |

- **Each read answers alone.**
  - **A failed lane** is "Could not be read" with Try again.
  - **The Waiting tile** shows the sum with a "+" and names the failed lane.
  - **A failed group** has no total.
  - **A failed glance** shows "We could not load Approvals." and is never an empty inbox.
  - **A failed pitch state** is a dash on its tab.
- **Counted lanes and uncounted ones.** The glance counts eleven of the twenty lanes. The nine this redesign adds say "Not read here yet" with what they wait for, never 0. Explorers says it counts every Explorer until part 1.
- **Per lane, never per item.** The Inbox lists the lanes that wait, oldest first, with their age, band and door. Items, who sent them and who holds them come with part 2's inbox read. The chart colours each lane's bar by its oldest item's band, and says the split by age waits for item ages.
- **Ages.** The canvas's bands apply: no pill under 24 hours, amber from 24, red from 72. An unknown age sorts last and reads "Age not recorded".
- **Experts.** The read stops at 50 ranked experts and carries `total_active`. When it holds fewer than the total, the tab counts are not drawn and the page says why. "To verify" is the directory's own bar: active, unverified, not hidden, at least 70% complete, and not a sample.
