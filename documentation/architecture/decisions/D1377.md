## D1377 — On a phone, Start Call opens the meeting in the Zoom or Google Meet app

- **Status:** asked for by the owner on 2026-10-29 ("On the phone, it should open the zoom app or google meet"). Built by S21.
- **Builds on:** D1362: one Start opens the meeting in a tab; D1372: the bot fallback.
- **Migration:** none. Frontend only.

### What changed

`recordSetup.js` gains two functions:

- **`phoneOs(navigator)`:** reads `'ios'`, `'android'` or `null` from the browser's own user agent. An iPad that reports a Mac is known by its touch points.
- **`meetingAppHref(provider, link, os)`:** turns the already-checked https link (`meetingHref`, the provider's own host only) into the app's link:
  - **Zoom, on iPhone and Android:** `zoomus://zoom.us/join?confno=<id>&pwd=<passcode>`, from the link's meeting id and passcode;
  - **Meet, on Android:** an intent for the Meet app (`com.google.android.apps.tachyon`), with the https link as the browser's fallback when the app is not installed;
  - **null otherwise:** a computer, Teams, Meet on an iPhone (its https link is one the Meet app takes on its own), or a Zoom link with no meeting id, such as a personal room name.

In Record a Session's Start (`RecordLightbox`), a phone that gets an app link opens no browser tab. When the call is saved, the page hands the app link to the phone (`location.assign`), and the phone opens Zoom or Meet. Everything else still opens in a tab, as before.

### Verified

- **Tests:** `record_call_create_meeting_d1346` covers:
  - the phone reading;
  - each app link, and the cases with none;
  - only the provider's own host;
  - on a mounted dialog: Zoom on an iPhone and Meet on Android hand off with no tab, while Meet on an iPhone and Zoom on a computer keep the tab.
- **Mutation check:** without the hand-off, the dialog test fails.
- **Neighbouring tests:** the Record a Session suites pass, 73 of 73.
- **Not verified:** on a real phone. Whether the app opens depends on it being installed. Without it, Android's Meet intent falls back to the browser, and Zoom's link does nothing until the app is installed.
