## D9192 — One body for an unauthenticated refusal; calendar connect answers 503 when OAuth is not configured (#9192)

- **Status:** built by S12 on #9192, 2026-11-27.
- **Chosen by:** Session 1's audit of 2026-11-27 (PROD-7, RL-13), as filed on #9192.

### The problem

An anonymous request to a signed-in API got one of five bodies: `{detail:'Unauthorized'}` from `app.onError`, `{error:'Unauthorized'}` from `routes/events.ts` and `routes/jobs.ts`, `{error:'unauthorized'}` from `routes/event_recommendations.ts`, and `fund_notices_refused` with "This account cannot use fund notices." from `routes/fund_notices.ts`, a sentence about an account the caller had not signed in to. `events.ts` and `jobs.ts` also caught every throw from `requireAuth` as 401, so a failed session read sent a signed-in member to sign in.

Google and Outlook calendar connect answered a missing OAuth setting with 500 and a `message` and `missing` array that named the server's settings.

### What was decided

- `util/authErrors.ts` holds the body: `unauthenticatedBody()` is `{ error: 'unauthenticated', message: 'Sign in to continue.', detail: 'Sign in to continue.' }`. `authRefusal(c, err)` is for a route that catches its own `requireAuth` throw. No session gets the shared 401. A mapped refusal (403, 423) keeps its status and the `{detail}` body `app.onError` gives it. Anything else is thrown again, so `app.onError` answers it as a server error.
- `events.ts`, `jobs.ts` and `event_recommendations.ts` answer through `authRefusal`. `fund_notices.ts`'s `onError` answers `requireAuth`'s and `requireAdmin`'s no-session throw with the shared body before its role refusal.
- Calendar connect without its OAuth settings answers **503**, keeps the nested `{error:{code:'oauth_config_missing', message}}` shape and the code the SPA keys on, and gives a plain sentence. The setting names go to the Worker log (`[CAL:oauth_config_missing]`) and not to the response. They are fixed setting names, not member data, and `reportPrivateError` keeps only a code, which would leave the operator without the names.

### Not in this decision

`app.onError`'s own body for `Unauthorized` (`{detail:'Unauthorized'}`) is unchanged here. #9186 owns that handler and can switch it to `unauthenticatedBody()`.

Tested by `cloudflare-worker/test/unauthenticated_body_9192.test.ts` and `cloudflare-worker/test/calendar.google-oauth.test.ts`.
