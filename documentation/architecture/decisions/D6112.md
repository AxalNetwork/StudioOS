## D6112 — Partner network: enter a month's partner revenue (#6112)

Under #4861. `PUT /api/cycles/grow/partners/:uid/revenue/:month` and its
writer `setPartnerRevenue` (`services/growWriters.ts`) existed, but nothing in
the app called them, so a partner's Revenue sourced, the module table's
Revenue sourced column and the module chart stayed "Not recorded" for any
venture without them.

### What the record gains

- **Enter revenue** (`enter-revenue`), a form action on the Partner network
  record (`services/records/grow.ts`): a month (`YYYY-MM`, opening on this UTC
  month) and an amount in US dollars as typed (`1,200`, `$40.50`). The Worker
  reads the amount through `parseMinor(…, 'USD')` from
  `frontend/src/lib/money.js` (D5373: no bare `* 100`) and runs the route's own
  writer, so the record and the route write a month one way and log it one way
  (`grow_partner_revenue`). A bad amount is a 400 on `amount`; a bad month a
  400 on `month`; nothing is written. Typing a typed month again replaces it.
- **Revenue by month** (`revenue`), a main section between Deals sourced and
  Terms: the partner's last 24 months that have a row, newest first, each with
  its source and amount. A month no one entered has no row (never a $0 row);
  with none at all the section reads "No month entered." and offers Enter
  revenue. It is shown to every reader, as the Revenue sourced fact already
  was: an amount names no third party.
- A reader without write access gets no action: the record route only
  declares actions to a writer (`routes/cycle_records.ts`), and the action's
  POST is a 403 for them.

### Source, and Mercury

`partner_revenue.source` is `CHECK (source IN ('manual'))` (migration 443,
carried through 462). Mercury never writes this table: its Partner network
feed is *payments to* a partner, landed as proposals in
`mercury_partner_proposals` (`services/mercury/growFeeds.ts`), which are a
cost and not revenue the partner sourced. So today every month is typed, and
reads **typed** — the word the module table already uses
(`adapters/grow.js`, `"$1,200 · typed"`).

For the day another source fills a month, the behaviour is decided now, with
no schema change:

- the section reads `mercury` as **From Mercury** and any other value as
  stored (`partnerRevenueSource`);
- **typing never takes over such a month.** `setPartnerRevenue` reads the
  month's row first and refuses with a 409 ("The 2026-09 figure came from
  Mercury, so it is kept and cannot be typed over"), and its upsert's
  `DO UPDATE … WHERE partner_revenue.source = 'manual'` holds the same line
  against a race. The route and the record action both run that writer.

Widening the CHECK is that future connector's migration, not this one.

### Not done

- The optional "Count it in that month's revenue" offer after Add deal. A deal
  carries its own currency and partner revenue has none, and whether a deal
  adds to or replaces a typed month is a product call; neither is small.
- `cyclesWrite.grow.partnerRevenue` (`frontend/src/lib/api/cycleWrites.js`)
  is **kept**. The record page sends the action through `recordAction`, so the
  method has no caller, but its route is still mounted and runs the same
  writer, so `npm run test:drift` stays green and a later page can call it.
