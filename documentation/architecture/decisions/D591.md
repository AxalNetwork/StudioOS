## D591 — The confirmed ICP recommends a customer template and prefills a draft page

**Issue:** #1218, part of #1009, from D353. **Decided by:** S1, 1A and 2A.
**No migration.**

### What was decided

- **1A. Keep the 18 templates** (`lib/brand/templates.js`) and add a
  recommendation by page type and sector. The Customer Templates canvas, a
  page-type × sector catalogue, stays out of scope.
- **2A. Prefill field by field** from the confirmed ICP
  (`projects.icp_definition_meta`, D353) and the startup record.

### The mapping, in one place

`frontend/src/lib/brand/icpPrefill.js` holds `ICP_PREFILL_MAP`. Every entry
pairs a source field with the field or template content key it fills.

| Fills | From |
| --- | --- |
| page `name` | the startup's name, then the template's label |
| `headline`, `audience_customer_headline` | ICP value proposition |
| `subheadline`, `audience_customer_body` | ICP desired outcome |
| `cta_text`, `audience_customer_cta` | ICP urgency: Active gives "Book a demo"; Latent gives "See how it works"; otherwise "Join the waitlist" |
| Customer Acquisition `problem_lead` · `product_lead` | primary pain · differentiator |
| Customer Audience `segments[0]` (label, headline, subhead) | persona, value proposition, outcome |
| Customer Audience `problem_lead` · `how_lead` | primary pain · differentiator |

- **Proof Builder** maps no template content. Its sections are steps,
  before/after, metrics and quotes, and no ICP answer is one of those
  without inventing copy.
- **The CTA labels** are the only words the mapping writes itself.
- **Every mapped string is capped** at 2,000 characters, the per-string cap
  in `sanitizeLandingContent`. The Worker then runs `content_json` through
  that sanitizer as it does for every page write.

### The recommendation

`recommendCustomerTemplate(project)` returns null unless the ICP is
confirmed, so nothing is claimed without one.

- **Two kinds of buyer** (type "Both") → Customer Audience, with one page
  and a segment switcher.
- **Buyers looking now** (urgency "Active") → Customer Acquisition, with a
  waitlist or a demo.
- **Otherwise** → Proof Builder, which validates demand with a waitlist
  first.

The pick's sentence names the page type and the startup's sector. None of
the 18 templates has a sector variant, so the sector cannot change the pick
on its own. A sector-keyed choice needs templates that do not exist.

### Where it shows

- **The ICP card** (`components/discovery/IcpDefinitionCard.jsx`):
  - for a confirmed ICP, and only to someone who may edit it, the card draws
    the recommendation and "Generate landing page →", which replaces D353's
    "not built" line;
  - the button creates the page through
    `POST /brand/landing/by-project/:pid/pages`, which is owner-scoped and
    creates it unpublished;
  - the card then says nothing is published. Publishing stays the founder's
    own action in Brand & Pages.
- **The Brand library** (`pages/SpinoutLabBrandPage.jsx`) names the
  recommendation above the grid and marks that template in the grid and the
  list. It also prints the template count from `TEMPLATES.length`.
- **The arsenal** (`lib/spinoutLabArsenal.js`) says "Eighteen templates",
  read from the same list. It used to say "Sixteen".
