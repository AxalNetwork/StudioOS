## D6241 — The GP of record reads the ledger of the fund they run, read only (#6241)

**Decided by the owner, 2026-11-15: option A.** The GP of record reads a
fund's operational ledger in full (Summary, Journal, Fees and the chart of
accounts), and writes nothing.

**Who reads what** (`routes/fund_ledger.ts`):

| Caller | Lists and reads | `full_read` | `can_manage` (writes) |
|---|---|---|---|
| An admin | every fund | yes | yes |
| The assigned fund administrator | their fund | yes | yes |
| **The GP of record** | the funds they run | **yes** | **no** |
| An LP (incl. a claimed legacy row) | their fund's summary | no | no |
| Anyone else | nothing (404) | — | — |

**"GP of record."** This is the operating gate's own scope:
`managedFundIds` in `services/fundGpAccess.ts`, the same rule `requireFundGp`
uses.
- `vc_funds.gp_user_id` is the caller;
- the caller holds the institutional investor tier;
- the fund matches the caller's verified active company.

A GP below the tier, or under another company, is not GP of record for this
read. It is evaluated on every request.

**Writes are unchanged.** Every write (accounts, periods, fee terms, entries,
accruals, expenses, reconciliation, period state, the administrator) still
requires the administrator or an admin and asserts its D714 act. A GP's write
is a 404 and writes nothing.

**The read.** `readFundLedger` takes `canManage` apart from `full`, and the
answer carries `full_read` beside `can_manage`. `FundLedgerPanel.jsx` draws
the Journal and Fees views from `full_read`, and its period line says "GP of
record read · only the fund administrator writes".

**Verified.**
- `cloudflare-worker/test/fund_ledger_d628.test.ts` covers:
  - the GP's full read, both `can_manage` false and `full_read` true, the
    list, and the chart of accounts;
  - two refused writes with nothing written;
  - the administrator still manages, an LP gets the summary, an unrelated
    caller is refused;
  - a GP below the tier is refused.
- Dropping the GP from the full readers, or opening writes to them, each fail
  that test.
- `frontend/test/fund_ledger_d628.test.mjs` covers the panel's GP read.
