### 1. One rule for every block

Part 1 answers each block in one of four shapes. `catalogueModel.blockState` reads them, and every tile, series, row, month and card figure follows it:

- `available: true` is read, and its figure is drawn;
- `pending: true` waits on another part's read: "Not read here yet" with part 1's reason, never 0 and never "Could not be read";
- `unreadable: true` is a failed read: "Could not be read", "That is not the same as none." and Try again;
- `recorded: false` is a figure no store holds: "Not recorded" with its reason.

A catalogue read that fails, or has not answered, fails or loads every block it feeds. Lab applications are their own read and fail only their line.

### 2. The period

- 4, 12 and 52 weeks, 12 by default, as `?weeks=` in the address (left out at 12) and as `weeks=` on the read.
- The tiles and the three charts follow it. The cards do not (S-PER): while a new period reads, they keep the last answer for the same studio, so the doors and figures do not flicker.

### 3. The tiles

- **Programs running:** "[n] of [m]", where n is the registry's `live` rows and m all its rows. "[a] not offered yet · [b] proposed": b is the `proposed` rows and a is the rest that are not live (m − n − b).
- **People taking part:** the count, its delta on the period before (none when the period before had none), the programs it counts, and the Lab left out with part 1's reason.
- **Waiting for you:** the sum, the oldest, and an amber Overdue from 72 hours. "Could not be read" when any queue it sums failed, never a smaller total. Strategic referral access is named as left out, with its reason.
- **Credits paid out:** "[n] paid out" over "[m] spent on perks". For an Admin it is "Not read here yet" with "HQ reads these figures.", which is not a failure.

### 4. The charts

Each is `ui/Charts.jsx`'s, with a Chart/Table switch, its own skeleton while it reads (PG17) and Unreadable with Try again when it fails (PG18a).

- **Joins per week:** Lab applications violet, Referrals sky, Learning enrolments amber, Perk claims emerald; the y-axis "per week"; the current week hollow. A failed series draws no line and is named "Could not be read"; a pending one "Not read here yet". A week a series does not answer is a gap, never 0. For an Admin, Learning reads "in the programs assigned to you". "A re-enrolment counts again." The foot "Referrals rose [n]% on the period before." is drawn only when both periods were read.
- **Waiting by program:** a row per queue (cohort, Refer & Earn reviews with those waiting on the member, strategic, perk listings "since last edit", office-hours hosts), in three bands (under 24h, 24–72h, 72h or more). A queue that was not read is a row in words, never a bar. Each row's door is literal. The foot "Of the queues read, nothing has waited 72 hours or more." only when that is true.
- **Credits in and out by month:** granted above the axis by source, spent on perk claims below it. `Columns` now draws a row's `below` under a zero axis on the same scale. `other_rule` and `no_rule` are drawn together as a muted "Other rules". Referrals are "Not recorded" with "Referral rewards are not paid in perk credits yet.", and are not drawn as a zero bar. Deducted and refunded are named apart, never netted. An Admin sees "Not read here yet" with "HQ reads these figures." The foot "1 credit is worth $1 of perk value."

### 5. The cards

- From part 1's per-program block, in the registry's order, with its status words. "Catalogue" with "[n] programs" counts the registry's rows.
- The held-mode badges (S-HELD) and the reasons a missing purpose reads come from the frontend constant, which also stands while the registry reads or fails, so the doors are always drawn.
- A Super Admin gets no Needs-you pills. The doors stay literal.
- **Lab applications** on the Spin-Out Lab card: `counts.pending`. `counts` holds only the statuses that have rows, so no `pending` key is a real 0; a reply with no `counts` is a failed read.

### 6. The studio filter

- For a Super Admin only: D893's Scope select on R12. HQ-held alone shows `SCOPE_NOTE`. A pick sets `?scope=`; HQ removes it.
- While R12 reads and a studio is asked for, neither the catalogue nor Lab applications is read, so HQ's figures never sit under a studio's name.
- Under a studio, Lab applications (the card line and the Joins series) read "Not read here yet": that read has no per-studio form.
- A plain Admin's `?scope=` is ignored, and no scope is sent.
- A code R12 does not list reads HQ's own figures under HQ's chip.

### 7. Phone (PG20a)

The header has no buttons on a phone, so there is no studio filter, no Updated and no Refresh. The tiles stack in one column, and the doors are 44px tall.

**Waiting by program and Credits in and out by month are not drawn on a phone.** PG20a shows neither. The Waiting for you tile carries the total, and each queue's own page is a tap away.

Only violet changes, to `#a78bfa`. Sky, amber and emerald keep their colours.

### 9. What changed in D953's rules

- Guard H is lifted for `GET /api/admin/spinout-applications`, whose failure has been a 503 since D960. `GET /api/admin/spinout-participants` still answers `[]` on any error and is still not read.
