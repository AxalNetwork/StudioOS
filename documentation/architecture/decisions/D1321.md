### 2. Publish to… (PUB1, PUB3, PUB10A, PUB12)

The sheet is D1317's `Sheet` at 560px, docked beside the rail's spine. The eyebrow is "PUBLISH FROM · ‹MODULE›" and the title "Publish to…". Below 640px it is the shared Sheet's full-screen page (PUB10A).

- **Destinations** are `GET /destinations`. Every place the founder's connections list for the company is shown:
  - **Nothing is pre-ticked.** The count reads "0 of 3 ticked".
  - **A place switched off** is muted. It carries "Off for this company" and "Turn on", which links to its connector page. A place the read does not say is on counts as off.
  - **Manage in Connectors** links to `/account/connectors`.
  - **A member `can_publish` refuses** reads "Only the founder publishes for this company." There are no boxes, and Review and send stays off.
  - **No places at all:** the sheet says to connect one first.
- **The draft** is the founder's own text.
  - **One `publish_drafts` row** is created on the first check and saved on each one after.
  - **Closing the sheet archives that draft,** unless Save draft was pressed or something was sent.
- **The preview** has one tab per ticked place, in the order ticked. Each tab shows:
  - the account and the audience;
  - the text;
  - the Worker's problems;
  - the media line;
  - the mono counter ("212 / 280"), with its text equivalent as its accessible name ("212 of 280 characters").
- **The check** runs after a pause in typing. It saves the text, then previews every ticked place.
  - **Findings come from the previews' lint.** Each is listed once, with what it is, the masked match and how to fix it. They are highlighted in the text at the Worker's offsets.
  - **Two findings can be cleared**, each by its own setting saved to the draft:
    - a named member, by "They agreed to be named" (`named_consent`);
    - a team-only figure, by "Include team-only figures in this post" (`team_only_included`, #2400 decision F).

    A cleared finding stays listed as "✓ Cleared", and unticking it puts it back.
  - **There is no override** (decision E): "A finding is fixed in the text. Nothing sends past one."
- **Review and send** is off until there is text, a ticked place, and a sendable preview for every ticked place. The words beside it say which is missing:
  - "Write your post to continue.";
  - "Tick a place to continue.";
  - "Checking…";
  - "Remove the highlighted text to continue.";
  - "Fix the problems the preview lists to continue."

  **Save draft** saves without sending.

### 3. The confirm and the result (PUB4, PUB5, PUB6, PUB10B)

- **The confirm, "Send to 3 places?",** is the page's own dialog. It is centred on the desk and a bottom sheet on a phone.
  - **It names every account and its audience,** with the place's delete rule: "Public · Can be deleted later", "Draft only: not sent to subscribers", "Public · Cannot be deleted from Axal VC".
  - **It ends with** "This posts in your name. Nothing else is sent."
  - **Focus starts on Cancel.**
- **Send** is the only call that publishes. It makes one `POST /drafts/:uid/confirm` per place.
  - Each place gets its own idempotency key, made when the dialog opened.
  - The press is held by a ref, so two presses in one tick make one confirm per place. The mutation check found this: the busy state alone let both through.
  - A confirm the Worker refuses (the daily cap, a changed text) shows its sentence on that place's row.
- **The result:**
  - The title is "Sent to 2 of 3", with "Confirmed by you · ‹date›".
  - The line reads "2 places received it. Facebook did not. Nothing was sent anywhere else."
  - Each place's row has its pill and its link, "View on X ↗" or, for a draft-only audience, "Open in Beehiiv ↗".
- **A failed place** shows its reason ("Facebook is busy. Try again after 14:20." after a 429) and Retry. Retry opens the confirm again for that place only, then calls `POST /sends/:uid/retry`.
- **An outcome not known** (PUB6) reads "Not known whether this arrived. Check X before sending again." It has Check and Mark as not sent, and never Retry.
  - **Mark as not sent's dialog** warns "If it did arrive, sending it again posts it twice."
- **The footer** is "Open sent history ›" and Close.

### 4. Published outside (PUB7, PUB9, PUB11)

**Decision G is (a).** The list is the second list on Grow's Updates history. On main that history is the Reporting desk's History, so `ReportingDesk.jsx` draws `PublishedOutside` under it.

- **The read** is `GET /sends`, which the venture's team may make. Anyone else gets a 404, and the list is not drawn for them. A failed read says "Unreadable", with Try again.
- **Filters:** module, destination and status are sent to the Worker. The search reads the text here.
- **A row** shows the date and time, the module, the destination, the pill, and its link or a dash. Retry shows on a failed row, for the founder only. At 390px each row is a card with the first line of the text (PUB11), and every target is 44px.
- **A row's detail is a Sheet with an address,** `?sent=<uid>` on the desk's own. A link to a post not in the list is read with `GET /sends/:uid`. ×, Escape and the scrim go back to the list's address, and focus returns to the row that opened it.
  - **The detail shows** the exact text, then Account, Audience, Confirmed by, Sent at, and the Post ID with Copy.
  - **What the founder can do** follows from the post's state:
    - an outcome not known has Check and Mark as not sent;
    - a post X lets Axal VC delete has Retract…, behind its dialog: "Delete this post from @… on X?", then "People who saw it may have copied it." (PUB7);
    - a post on a place that cannot be deleted from here (`retract` not `delete`) has the line "Axal VC cannot delete posts on TikTok. Delete it there, then mark it here.", with "Delete it on TikTok ↗" and Mark as removed.
- **Only the founder acts.** `can_publish` from `GET /destinations` decides whether any action shows. A co-founder reads the list and the detail with none.

### 6. Where it departs from the canvas, and why

- **"Ask Eadwyn for a draft" and the lavender proposal card (PUB2)** are not drawn. The `draft:publish` run is part 4's second half (#3483), so a button now would be a dead control (D17). PUB2's footer line is replaced by "Write your post to continue."
- **The highlights** are drawn in a copy of the text under the box, not inside the box. A `<textarea>` cannot mark its own text.
- **Image upload, "Add image" and "Edit for this destination"** are not drawn. Part 1 has no upload route, and no board shows the per-place editor. The media line shows what each place accepts.
- **The row's ⋯ menu (PUB9)** is not drawn. A row opens through its account name, and the detail carries the actions, so a menu would only repeat them.
- **"Confirmed by"** reads "You" or "The founder". Only the founder confirms, and the send row stores an id, not a name.

- **`PublishUpdate`** is Publish to… on a sent update, beside each sent row of the Reporting desk's History (`UpdatesHistory`'s new `rowAction`).

  - **A refusal** says why, beside the button: an update not sent, a report, or only the founder.

**The pre-fill** (`PublishSheet`'s `start`):
- **The draft is made when the sheet opens,** with no text, so D1333's builder fills it. Every later check saves onto that draft and never makes a second one.

- **A pre-fill that fails** leaves the box empty for the founder's own words.
- **A pre-filled draft closed untouched is archived,** as any other unkept draft is.
