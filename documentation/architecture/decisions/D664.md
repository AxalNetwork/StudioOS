### The shared read: `services/cycleCards.ts` (D659) and D661's shape

This task first built its own `cycleCards.ts`; #1487 (D659, #1472) built the
same file, and S1 ruled on #1488 that #1487's is the base. Grow now uses it as
merged:
- `safeCard` and `safeFigure`: a thrown read makes only that card or figure
  `unreadable`, naming the source; the error text is logged, never returned;
- the five states, `MIN_SERIES_POINTS = 3`, `countState`, `pageOf`, `PAGE_SIZE`.

The shape is D661's (`frontend/src/pages/cycles/cycleModel.js`), and the tests
check the real output through it:
- **`series`** is a list of chart specs `[{ kind, … }]`: `stack`, `hbar`,
  `funnel`, `vstack`, `heat`, `line` (with `plan`), `rows`, `vbars`,
  `timeline`, `progress`. A value no store holds is `null` in a spec.
- **`records.cols`** is `[{ key, label }]`; **`records.rows`** is
  `[{ id, cells }]`, a missing cell being `null` ("Not recorded").
- **`filter`** takes several `key:value` pairs, comma separated, all of which
  must match.

### Revenue movements (`services/revenueMovements.ts`)

- **Recurring rows only.** A customer's MRR in a month is the sum of their
  recurring rows that month.
- **Consecutive months only.** A movement is computed only between two months
  that both have rows. A month with no rows is not recorded, so a gap never
  reads as every customer churning.
- **Splitting new from expansion.** Expansion is split from new only when the
  rows can tell customers apart. A Stripe charge with no customer
  (`STRIPE_NO_CUSTOMER`) cannot be told apart, so that month is stored as
  `unsplit`, and no churn is claimed for it.
- **The three movements:**
  - **New** is a customer with no MRR the month before.
  - **Expansion** is a rise.
  - **Churned** is the amount lost to lost customers plus any contraction. Its
    customer count is lost customers only; a customer who spent less is not a
    churned customer.
- **Churn needs two billing periods in a row.** With fewer, the read says
  "Needs 2 billings", never 0. Two periods with a gap month between them say
  "no two billing months in a row".
- **MRR is cash by the month it was received.** Every recurring row counts in
  the month it landed, so an annual invoice reads as churn the next month and a
  late payment as churn then new. The ledger cannot tell these apart, and the
  Revenue card's caption says so.
- **`source`** is the weakest proof level of the rows behind the figure.
- **Computed at read time.** The Revenue card and its records compute the
  movements from the ledger they already read, so a venture whose ledger
  predates migration 443 shows them at once (S1's review found
  `revenue_movements` empty with four billing periods on the ledger).
- **The stored copy:** `revenue_movements` is rewritten by
  `recomputeRevenueMovements` after every ledger write in `routes/revenue.ts`:
  - an entry;
  - a CSV import;
  - the Stripe import;
  - an edit or a delete.

  A failure there is logged and never fails the write.
- **Derived reads:**
  - **Net revenue retention** is revenue kept from last month's customers ÷
    last month's total, for months that can be split.
  - **Net revenue retention** is month on month, previous calendar month to
    this one, not a rolling 30 days.
  - **Cohort retention** is by signup month. A month with no rows, or whose
    rows name no customer, is `null`, not 0%.

### `/api/cycles/grow` (`routes/cycle_grow.ts`)

- **`GET /overview?project=`** returns the question, the four figures and the
  seven cards in tab order.
  - The figures are MRR, net revenue retention, customers and headcount.
  - A figure nothing records is `null`, with `not_recorded` and a reason.
  - A typed figure carries `source: 'manual'`. A computed one names its source
    and date.
- **`GET /modules/:slug?project=&q=&filter=&sort=&page=&page_size=`** returns
  the card and its records, with the canvas's columns.
  - `filter` is one or more `field:value` pairs.
  - A missing value sorts last, never as 0.
  - Failing records read `records: { state: 'unreadable', reason, total: null }`.
  - Records are paged in memory: each module's rows are one venture's.
- **Each card's `next`** is computed from the records:
  - "Tell the [n] customers who asked that [item] shipped": shipped customer
    feedback with an email;
  - "Pause [channel]: CAC [$n]";
  - "Name the customer on the Stripe charges…";
  - "Review the [n] new applicants";
  - "Agree terms with [partner]";
  - "Source the [part] score for [market]";
  - "Give [obligation] an owner", or the first checklist item still open.
- **Writes** need the owner or a co-founder; an advisor reads only, through
  `canAccessProject`. Each row records its actor, and each write is logged to
  `activity_logs`. The writes are:
  - feedback;
  - channels and their monthly spend;
  - a role's team and target start;
  - an application's stage;
  - partners and their monthly revenue;
  - markets;
  - obligations;
  - checklist items.

  A market score needs its source, which both the route and a CHECK enforce.
  An obligation's owner is a typed name or the user id of someone on the
  venture (its founder or an accepted member). Every request value is bound.
- **One label for a typed value:** `manual`, here and in Share an update
  (`updateShare.ts` said "Entered by hand").

### Share an update (#1466): market replies

The owner chose A1 on #1466: a market reply stays on its ask until a store
exists. Now that `markets` exists:
- **A market ask links to one of the company's markets.**
  - `PUT /portfolio-updates/:uid/share` refuses a market that isn't the
    company's.
  - The sheet offers the company's markets.
- **A reply to a linked ask lands as `market-expansion`**, keyed to the
  market's uid.
- **The market's records read the replies through a join.** They are never
  copied.
- **Earlier replies** were made to asks that named no market. They stay on
  their ask. Migration 443 replaces their stored reason, "there is no Market
  expansion store yet", with "the ask is linked to no market".
- **No link to a page yet:** the sent update names Market expansion for a
  landed market reply but does not link it until #1474 mounts
  `/grow/market-expansion`. The sheet says "Linked to no market, so a reply
  stays on the ask" when no market is chosen.
- **Who reads them:** anyone who can read the venture, advisors included, as
  for hire and customer replies.

No backfill is possible (S1's call on #1488): nothing in an old ask says which
market it meant.

The metrics an update fills in still come from `project_metrics` with the same
`source` and `as_of` that `services/updateShare.ts` reads.

### Tests

**`cloudflare-worker/test/cycle_grow_d664.test.ts`** (22 tests). The fixture
builds `compliance_events` from `schema_baseline.sql`, and every card and table
is read through D661's `cardView`, `cellView` and `figureValue`. These four are
the ones the issue asks for:
- **Churn under two billing periods** is not recorded ("Needs 2 billings"),
  never 0.
- **Expansion splits from new only when customers can be told apart.** A
  Stripe charge with no customer makes the month `unsplit`.
- **One failing source** (a dropped table) makes only its card `unreadable`.
- **A typed figure is labelled `manual`.**

The others cover:
- gaps (a gap month is not a second billing period), cohorts (null for a
  month of anonymous charges) and churned-customer counts;
- a hiring plan labelled by month, quarter or half;
- readiness, and records with several filters and sorting;
- the overview of an empty venture (every figure `not_recorded`);
- four billing periods drawing the Revenue card with nothing stored;
- CAC counting customers in the spend's calendar month;
- access: a stranger is refused, an advisor reads but cannot write, and each
  write is logged;
- each module's next step;
- the stage moving, and a quarter's plan drawn;
- obligations written in production's columns, the owner check, and the Lab
  checklist scoped to a founder's one venture;
- market replies, and migration 443 replacing the stale reason;
- a ledger write through the revenue route rewriting the stored movements.
