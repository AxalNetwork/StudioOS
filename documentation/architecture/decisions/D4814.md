## D4814 — Admin › Approvals v2: the queue is the page, and ownership is a first-class state (#4814)

- **Status:** canvas landed 2026-11-07 (`design/incoming/Approvals v2 Page.dc.html`, `design/incoming/Approvals v2 · Boards.dc.html`, boards AP-01 to AP-19). Not built. The build is a separate task.
- **Builds on:** D1038 (the inbox), D1040 (assignment), D1044 (Decided), D1045/D1046 (item and board routes), D1132 (search, filters, age bands).
- **Migration:** none expected. See "Data" below.

### The question

The live inbox has the data an operator needs, but it leads with analytics, spreads eight headline tiles, and treats an unowned item as ordinary. The owner's flow-health review (2026-11-07) graded the queue "functionally strong, structurally over-specified" and ownership "poor live state": the queue is effectively unassigned and nothing says so.

### What the canvas decides

**Order: action before analytics.** Shared admin header (title, copy, scope chip) and the Inbox · Lanes · Decided tabs, then one compact urgency strip, then work views, then the queue. Workload charts sit below the queue. There is no second dashboard header.

**The summary is Waiting · Overdue · Unassigned · Oldest.** Assigned to me moves out of the headline into the work views (Needs attention · Assigned to me · Unassigned · All). The counts agree everywhere: the Inbox tab, Waiting and All show the same number.

**Five functional columns:** Request · Waiting · Owner · Review · ⋯, plus a selection checkbox. Rows group into bands: overdue (over 72 h), then due soon (24–72 h), then the rest, oldest first within each.

**Ownership is visible and fast.**
- An unowned row says Unassigned in amber and has an inline Assign button.
- When unowned work is the main reason the queue is ageing, a callout says so ("71 of 133 have no owner, including 4 overdue") with Show unassigned and Assign overdue….
- The drawer puts the owner first, with Assign to me and Change….

**Routing says where review continues.** The Review button carries "in KYC & KYB" or "in Approvals" under it. The drawer's action reads "Continue review in KYC & KYB →" with one line of why. Never "Decided on", never "Here".

**One Filters control and one Sort.** Filters · N when active; chips render only when filters exist; filters live in the URL.

**Assign dialog.** It says "Assigning changes ownership only. It does not decide these items." Reviewers are a radio list ordered you, lowest workload, then alphabetical, each with their current load.

**⋯ is a disclosure popover, not an ARIA menu.** It holds Assign…, Copy link and Open in <destination>. Review is never only inside ⋯.

**Bulk selection** floats a bar over the bottom of the queue (N selected · Assign… · Clear), so nothing reflows.

**Native boards** (Product testers, Co-marketing, Wellbeing experts) share one grammar: breadcrumb, title and explanation, status tabs, the same four-cell summary, toolbar, queue, drawer, decision dialog. Every decision dialog states what changes, who is told and what is recorded. Co-marketing shows approval and publication as separate lifecycle steps. Wellbeing verification needs an explicit credentials acknowledgement.

**States:**
- A true empty inbox ("Nothing needs approval") shows only when every lane was read.
- When one lane fails: the banner, the lane state, and figures marked `+` (127+), never 0.
- A changed-while-reviewing failure and a decision-recorded-but-notification-failed message, each worded as the backend can guarantee.

**Waiting on HQ** appears only in a studio shell; it is outbound tracking, not approval work. **Eadwyn** is a 44px rail by default (AP-19 shows it opened on purpose).

### Data

- Inbox, filters, age bands, `assigned`, `unreadable` and history come from `GET /api/admin/approvals` (D1040, D1132). Decided comes from `/decided` (D1044).
- The Unassigned figure needs a count of items with no assignee across all lanes, not one page. Confirm `hqInbox` returns it, or add it to the tiles. It is a read, not a migration.
- The due-soon band (24–72 h) is drawn as amber. Confirm the band boundaries match D1132's `age` split.
- A lane with no arrival time keeps its "Not recorded" reason; it is never placed in a band.

### What the canvas does not decide

The canvas's numbers are sample content. Keyboard behaviour (radiogroups, disclosure popover, dialog focus trap and restoration) and screen-reader output need testing in the built page; the boards are static.

### Built (part 2)

2026-11-08. The Inbox's ownership and action layer (AP-01's callout, AP-03, AP-04, AP-05, AP-14, AP-15, AP-17). Frontend only: every figure it needs was already on `GET /api/admin/approvals` (D1040, D1132, part 1's tiles). No route, no migration.

- **Assign dialog (AP-05).** It opens with "**Assigning changes ownership only.** It does not decide these items.", lists who it is about ("Person A, Project C and 3 more"), and offers the reviewers as a radio list: you, then lowest workload, then alphabetical, each with the items they own now ("You · 14 assigned", "4 assigned"), from the Worker's `tiles.per_admin`. The order line under the list says so. When the Worker withholds `per_admin` (a lane unread or cut at the cap) no load is shown rather than a short one, the order is you then alphabetical, and the line gives the Worker's reason. The button names the person ("Assign to Admin C", "Assign to me"). The success toast names who and to whom.
- **Ownership callout (AP-01)**, inside the queue card above the rows: "71 of 133 waiting items have no owner, including 4 overdue." with Show unassigned (not drawn inside the Unassigned view) and Assign overdue…. Rules, since the canvas only shows one case: it needs the Worker's exact figures (`tiles.complete`, nothing unread), and shows when at least half of what waits has no owner or any overdue item has none. The canvas's second sentence, "Unowned items are the main reason this queue is ageing.", is a causal claim, so it is said only when most overdue items have no owner.
- **Assign overdue…** reads the overdue items nobody holds from every lane with the inbox's own filters (`assigned=none&age=past`), page by page, oldest first, up to 40 — the Worker's `ASSIGNMENT_CAP`, which a test reads so the two cannot drift. When more wait, the dialog says "The oldest 40 of 52 are listed … The rest stay unassigned until you assign them." If none are left it says so and re-reads the inbox.
- **⋯ is a disclosure popover (AP-17)**, not an ARIA menu: `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` on the trigger, no `aria-haspopup`, no `menu` or `menuitem` roles. It holds Assign…, Copy link and "Open in [destination] ↗" (the lane's literal door, so the reachability walk still reads it). Esc closes it and returns focus to ⋯; a click outside or tabbing out closes it. Review stays in its own column. The phone's ⋯ sheet is the same list, without menu roles.
- **Floating bulk bar (AP-03).** "N selected · Assign… · Clear" sits in a sticky holder of no height at the bottom of the queue, so selecting a row moves nothing. Selected rows are lavender with `aria-selected`; the header box is mixed (`indeterminate`, `aria-checked="mixed"`) for a partial selection.
- **Drawer, owner first (AP-04, AP-15).** Owner (with "Owner since 3 Nov 2026, 09:30 · assigned by Admin A" from the latest assignment in History, or "Nobody owns this item yet"), Assign to me and Change…, then Facts (Submitted, Profile / request, Item ID, Lane), then History. The header carries "Item · KYC", the wait with its pill, and "2 of 4" with ‹ › to the rows before and after it on the page read. The footer is "Continue review in KYC & KYB →" with "This decision is completed in KYC & KYB, where its evidence and notification rules live."; the native boards say "This decision is made on the Product testers board in Approvals, …".
- **Phone cards (AP-14).** Name, request · lane, the age with its band in words, the owner (Unassigned in amber, "Owner not read"), where review continues, then Open, Assign only when nobody owns the item, and ⋯.
- **Existing tests changed** (`frontend/test/admin_approvals_d1038.test.mjs`), each because v2 changes the copy: Assign's choices read "You · 14 assigned"; the dialog's sub-line is the ownership note; the toast names the items; the drawer's header, owner box and "Profile / request" label; "Continue review in …" replaces "Open in …"; the phone card's order and the sheet's Open in.

**Where this departs from the canvas, and why:**
- **"6 of 133" counts within the page read.** Prev and next step through the 25 rows on the current page; the drawer has no position when it is opened from a link to an item not on that page, rather than inventing one. Stepping across pages would need a second inbox read per step.
- **Assign to me is offered only to an account the Worker lists as a reviewer** (an active `role = 'admin'` user). A Super Admin who is not one cannot be assigned (`assignReviewers` refuses `not_a_reviewer`), so the button is not drawn for them; Change… still is.
- **The reviewer search box in AP-05 is not built.** The list is every active admin; it can be added when the territory has enough admins to need it.
- **Assign to me assigns at once**, without the dialog: it changes ownership only, as the dialog says, and the toast confirms it.

### Built (part 3)

2026-11-09. The three native boards on one grammar (AP-08 to AP-13): Product testers, Co-marketing pitches and Wellbeing experts. Frontend only; no route, no migration.

- **Breadcrumb** Approvals › Lanes › [board] on every board (`boardCrumb`). `AdminPageHeader`'s `crumb` takes an optional `via` level for it, so other headers are unchanged.
- **Status tabs** take the canvas's words where the store agrees: Product testers' first tab is **Pending**, Wellbeing's is **Needs verification**. The addresses are unchanged.
- **The same four-cell summary** (`boardStrip`, `BoardStrip`), replacing each board's own tiles: the waiting figure (Pending · Proposed · Needs verification), Overdue, Unassigned and Oldest. Co-marketing's fourth is **Approved, not published**, with the oldest approval. Overdue, Unassigned and Oldest are counted over the waiting rows the board read, so they are drawn only when those rows are every waiting item. A short read says "Could not be read" with why, and a measured 0 says "measured · nothing over 72 h". The waiting figure is the store's own count.
- **Owners** come from the inbox's assignment record (D1040), read through `GET /api/admin/approvals?lane=<lane>` page by page, up to 8 pages (`readLaneOwners`). The boards key pitches and experts by `uid`, the inbox by numeric `id`. Both board reads already carry `id`, so `pitchRows` and `expertBoard` rows now include it and owners join on it. A row the owner read did not reach, or an unreadable assignment record, is "Not read", and then the summary draws no Unassigned count.
- **Queue columns** on each board's waiting tab follow the canvas:
  - Product testers: Applicant (name, email, and under it what would stop a decision: "! Email not verified", "! No email consent", plus the availability) · Applying as · Waiting (with "! Overdue" / "◷ Due soon" in words) · Owner (Unassigned in amber with Assign) · **Review →**.
  - Co-marketing: Pitch (title, audience) · Partner ("Proposed by …") · Format (type, date) · Waiting · Owner · **Review →**.
  - Wellbeing: Expert · Credentials · Waiting · Owner · **Verify →**.
  - The other tabs keep their tables.
- **Assign** on a board opens part 2's Assign dialog with the lane's reviewers and loads. It posts the lane and the numeric id.
- **The drawer, owner first** (`OwnerBox`): Unassigned with "Nobody owns this item yet", or the owner (· you), with Assign to me (only for an account the Worker lists as a reviewer, and not for the item's own owner) and Change…. Only a waiting item is on the inbox, so only it shows the box. Every board's drawer ends with "Decided here, in Approvals. You'll see what changes before anything is recorded." Product testers' "Assign…" link out to the inbox item is gone.
- **The decision dialog** (`DecisionDialog`) states **What changes · Who is told · What is recorded** as three labelled lines before any field, for every board's every decision. The copy is unchanged (`decisionCopy`).
- **Co-marketing's lifecycle** (`Lifecycle`): "1 · Proposed — review here · 2 · Approved — attribution code issued · 3 · Published — a separate step, from Approved" under the tabs, with the tab's step current. The drawer repeats it, before the owner, with "Approving does not publish. Publishing happens from the Approved tab once the content is ready." A declined or withdrawn pitch has left the line.
- **Wellbeing, credentials first**: the drawer opens on "Credentials and evidence" with the register note ("Register lookups aren't automated…"), then the owner, then the profile. The action is "Verify expert…". The acknowledgement reads "I have checked their licence against the issuing register and the documents in this drawer. Required to verify."
- **What moved below the queue:** Product testers' pool (Active testers, In a testing program now, the chart by profile, who can apply) and Co-marketing's results from published pitches over 30 days. Wellbeing's Verified and Declined tiles are gone, since the tabs already count them.
- **Existing tests changed** (`frontend/test/admin_approvals_d1038.test.mjs`), each because v2 changes the board: Pending and Needs verification for the tab words; the four-cell summary in place of the waiting tiles; the testers' and pitches' new row contents; the tester drawer's owner box in place of the Assign link; the expert drawer's credentials-first order.

**Where this departs from the canvas, and why:**
- **No row selection or ⋯ on the boards.** The inbox has both; a board's bulk action would be a bulk decision, which no Worker route offers. The canvas draws the checkbox but no bulk decision.
- **No Filters or Sort control redrawn.** Product testers keeps its own filters (profile, email verified, consent) and sort; the other two boards had none and still have none. Adding them is its own slice.
- **Co-marketing's later tabs keep "Approved, to publish" and "Declined"**, where the canvas says Approved and Rejected. "Approved, to publish" says what is left to do, and every board says Declined. Wellbeing keeps its Profile incomplete tab, which the store has and the canvas does not draw.
- **Prev/next and "2 of 6" are not in the board drawers.** The inbox drawer has them (part 2); a board's own order varies by tab and filter.
- **The owner read stops at 8 inbox pages (200 items).** Past that, the rows it did not reach are "Not read".
