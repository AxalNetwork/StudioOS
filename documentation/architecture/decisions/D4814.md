## D4814 — Admin › Approvals v2: the queue is the page, and ownership is a first-class state (#4814)

- **Status:** canvas landed 2026-11-07 (`design/incoming/Approvals v2 Page.dc.html`, `design/incoming/Approvals v2 · Boards.dc.html`, boards AP-01 to AP-19). Not built. The build is a separate task.

- **Migration:** none expected. See "Data" below.

### The question

The live inbox has the data an operator needs, but it leads with analytics, spreads eight headline tiles, and treats an unowned item as ordinary. The owner's flow-health review (2026-11-07) graded the queue "functionally strong, structurally over-specified" and ownership "poor live state": the queue is effectively unassigned and nothing says so.

### What the canvas decides

**Order: action before analytics.** Shared admin header (title, copy, scope chip) and the Inbox · Lanes · Decided tabs, then one compact urgency strip, then work views, then the queue. Workload charts sit below the queue. There is no second dashboard header.

**The summary is Waiting · Overdue · Unassigned · Oldest.** Assigned to me moves out of the headline into the work views (Needs attention · Assigned to me · Unassigned · All). The counts agree everywhere: the Inbox tab, Waiting and All show the same number.

**Five functional columns:** Request · Waiting · Owner · Review · ⋯, plus a selection checkbox. Rows group into bands: overdue (over 72 h), then due soon (24–72 h), then the rest, oldest first within each.

**Ownership is visible and fast.**
- An unowned row says Unassigned in amber and has an inline Assign button.
- When unowned work is the main reason the queue is ageing, a callout says so ("71 of 133 have no owner, including 4 overdue") with Show unassigned and Assign overdue….
- The drawer puts the owner first, with Assign to me and Change….

**Routing says where review continues.** The Review button carries "in KYC & KYB" or "in Approvals" under it. The drawer's action reads "Continue review in KYC & KYB →" with one line of why. Never "Decided on", never "Here".

**One Filters control and one Sort.** Filters · N when active; chips render only when filters exist; filters live in the URL.

**Assign dialog.** It says "Assigning changes ownership only. It does not decide these items." Reviewers are a radio list ordered you, lowest workload, then alphabetical, each with their current load.

**⋯ is a disclosure popover, not an ARIA menu.** It holds Assign…, Copy link and Open in <destination>. Review is never only inside ⋯.

**Bulk selection** floats a bar over the bottom of the queue (N selected · Assign… · Clear), so nothing reflows.

**Native boards** (Product testers, Co-marketing, Wellbeing experts) share one grammar: breadcrumb, title and explanation, status tabs, the same four-cell summary, toolbar, queue, drawer, decision dialog. Every decision dialog states what changes, who is told and what is recorded. Co-marketing shows approval and publication as separate lifecycle steps. Wellbeing verification needs an explicit credentials acknowledgement.

**States:**
- A true empty inbox ("Nothing needs approval") shows only when every lane was read.
- When one lane fails: the banner, the lane state, and figures marked `+` (127+), never 0.
- A changed-while-reviewing failure and a decision-recorded-but-notification-failed message, each worded as the backend can guarantee.

**Waiting on HQ** appears only in a studio shell; it is outbound tracking, not approval work. **Eadwyn** is a 44px rail by default (AP-19 shows it opened on purpose).

### Data

- Inbox, filters, age bands, `assigned`, `unreadable` and history come from `GET /api/admin/approvals` (D1040, D1132). Decided comes from `/decided` (D1044).
- The Unassigned figure needs a count of items with no assignee across all lanes, not one page. Confirm `hqInbox` returns it, or add it to the tiles. It is a read, not a migration.
- The due-soon band (24–72 h) is drawn as amber. Confirm the band boundaries match D1132's `age` split.
- A lane with no arrival time keeps its "Not recorded" reason; it is never placed in a band.

### What the canvas does not decide

The canvas's numbers are sample content. Keyboard behaviour (radiogroups, disclosure popover, dialog focus trap and restoration) and screen-reader output need testing in the built page; the boards are static.
