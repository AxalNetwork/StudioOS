## D553 — Investor fund LPs: an LP's type, a reminder draft per call line, and a comms log (migration 387)

**The fund's LP and capital-call zones (I6 of
`design/incoming/Investor LP Canvas.dc.html`) drew three things nothing
stored. They are now stored, and the pages read them.** Slot S08, issue #1146,
2026-10-05.

**What was true on main.**
- `limited_partners` had no type, so `InvestorFundLPs.jsx` showed
  `lp.type || lp.entity_type`, two columns that do not exist, and every row
  read "Unrecorded".
- There was no `funds/calls` entry in `DRAFT_SURFACES`. The calls page's rail
  said so: "Eadwyn drafts no reminder for this page yet".
- Nothing recorded what was sent to which LP, or when.

**Migration 387.**
- `limited_partners.lp_type` is a closed set, enforced by a CHECK:
  `individual`, `family_office`, `institution`, `fund_of_funds` and
  `corporate`. It is nullable and has no default, so an LP nobody has typed
  reads "Not recorded" and is never given a guessed type.
- `fund_lp_communications` holds:
  - the fund, the LP and the kind (`call_reminder`, `call_notice`, `update` or
    `other`);
  - the subject and `sent_at`;
  - `logged_by`, the actor;
  - optionally `capital_call_id`, the one LP's call line it was about.

  **It has no body column.** The message lives wherever it was sent from. A
  copy here would be an LP's correspondence held where nobody reviews its
  retention.

**Routes** (`routes/funds.ts`, logic in `services/fundLpComms.ts`). All are
behind `requireFundGp`: the fund's GP of record on the institutional tier, or
an admin. Every id is bound.
- `PUT /api/funds/:id/lps/:lpId/type` takes `{ lp_type }`, one of the five or
  null to clear it.
  - The UPDATE is scoped `WHERE id = ? AND fund_id = ?`, so another fund's LP
    is a 404.
  - The actor goes into `activity_logs` (`action = 'lp_type_set'`).
- `GET /api/funds/:id/comms?lp=` lists the log, newest first, 100 a page with
  `truncated`. A failed read is a 503.
- `POST /api/funds/:id/comms` takes `{ lp_id, kind, subject, sent_at?,
  capital_call_id? }`.
  - Only those five fields are read: a `body`, a `message` or a `logged_by` in
    the request has nowhere to go.
  - `logged_by` is the session's user.
  - The INSERT's own SELECT requires the LP to be on this fund and the call
    line to be that LP's. Otherwise the answer is a 404 and nothing is
    written.
  - `sent_at` is a date or a date and time, and never in the future (five
    minutes of clock skew is allowed). If it is absent, it is now.
  - The subject is required and at most 200 characters.

**The draft surface** `funds/calls` (`routes/research.ts`) drafts a reminder
to one LP about one call line. The scope is the line id, which must be
written as one (`900`, not `900.0` or ` 900`).
- `callReminderMaterial` is the licence and ownership gate. `/research/drafts`
  authorises on sign-in alone and leaves the scoping to each surface. The
  caller must be an investor who is the fund's `gp_user_id`, or an admin.
  Another fund's line, a plain LP, a founder named as GP, or a paid line gives
  nothing to draft (409 `nothing_to_draft`).
- The material is the LP, the fund, the call number and purpose, the share,
  what has been received and what is still owed (in cents, as the ledger
  computes it), the due date, and how many reminders are already logged for
  the line.
- The instruction forbids threats, citing the LPA or a penalty, naming other
  LPs, or saying the message was sent.
- **There is no `accept` hook.** Accepting stamps the draft and writes nothing
  else, and nothing is ever sent.

**The pages.**
- **`/funds/lps`:**
  - the Type column shows the type, or Not recorded, with a select to set
    it;
  - the "By type" filter has one chip per type held, plus "Type not recorded"
    when any LP is untyped;
  - a Comms log card reads the log in four states. A failed read says it is
    not a claim that nothing was sent.
- **`/funds/calls`:** each unpaid line gains "Draft reminder" and "Log as
  sent".
  - "Draft reminder" opens the `ZoneDraft` band on that line alone.
  - "Log as sent" asks for a subject (prefilled "Reminder: capital call N")
    and the date sent, and posts a `call_reminder` linked to the line. The
    form has nowhere to paste a message.
  - The footer and the rail now say a reminder can be drafted and that
    nothing sends it.

**Not here.** Report packs and their delivery log wait on the owner (#1138).

**Found on the way.** `logActivity` (`routes/partnernet.ts`) inserts into
`activity_logs` without `action`. That column is NOT NULL on the baseline and
on a database built from the migrations, and the helper swallows the error. So
every `logActivity` call (`lpa_signed`, for one) records nothing. The type
route writes its actor row directly instead. The helper is left for its owner
to fix, since every caller of it is outside this issue.
