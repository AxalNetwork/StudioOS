## D4655 — Pricing comparison and control accessibility (#4655)

The owner split #4655: routine semantics/keyboard/reflow corrections go ahead;
material mobile-layout and outcome-showcase changes wait for #5128, and the
research showcase waits for #3701. This change follows the merged #5482 work
and preserves the existing pricing canvas and comparison structure.

- Audience and per-plan tabs now have one keyboard stop, arrow-key wrap,
  Home/End navigation, selected state, and explicit controlled panels.
- The mobile plan comparison has Feature and selected-plan column headers,
  alongside its existing row headers and table name. Desktop retains its
  explicit table/row/header/cell semantics.
- Included/not-included symbols use named image semantics, so their labels
  are valid and exposed to assistive technology.
- FAQ buttons reference persistent answer elements and expose expanded state.
  Existing billing buttons retain their names and pressed state.
- #4577's shared registration labels are reused, not copied: browser checks
  navigate from a priced plan to signup and click each associated label to
  confirm focus reaches its input.

Browser checks use synthetic catalogue prices and a local Worker/SQLite
fixture, with off-host requests blocked. They cover 1440px and 390px, plus
320 CSS-pixel reflow (the width equivalent of 1280px at 400% zoom), exposed
table/header context, tab selection/focus, billing and FAQ state, and an axe
WCAG 2 A/AA scan of Pricing. Important states are captured as screenshots.
They do not substitute for NVDA/VoiceOver speech testing or actual browser-UI
zoom, and no pixel baseline is regenerated on the host browser.
