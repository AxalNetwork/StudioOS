## D1216 — Closing part 3: an envelope signed in order by several parties, with Axal's countersign last

- **Status:** built by S15 for #3213 (part 3 of #2424), 2026-10-26.
- **Migration:** 624 (`624_staged_signing.sql`), assigned on the issue by S1. The booklet's tables did not fit: see §1.
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Closing Room Page.dc.html` and `Closing room · Boards.dc.html`, board CP4 (D1213).

Paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/` unless they say otherwise. Every route is under `/api/legal/esign`.

### 1. Why a sibling of the booklet, not the booklet generalised

The subscription booklet's stages (migration 485, `fund_booklet_envelopes`) belong to a booklet: `booklet_id` is NOT NULL, there are two stages (`applicant`, `gp`), and the triggers are keyed on the booklet's status and digest. A SAFE, SPA or SPV Joinder between the company, the investor and Axal has no booklet and three stages. `esign_recipients` has no order and no role.

So `services/bookletSigning.ts` stays exactly as it was, and its tests pass unchanged. A closing line's paper uses the sibling `services/stagedSigning.ts`, on two new tables.

### 2. The store (migration 624)

- **`esign_staged_envelopes`:** one document signed in order.
  - `body` is the exact text every stage signs, and `digest` is its SHA-256.
  - `deal_id` is the closing and `commitment_id` its line. A closing is its deal, and a line its commitment, until #3212's closings store.
  - `countersign` = 1 adds Axal's stage.
  - A trigger lets only `voided_at` be written, once. The text never changes.
- **`esign_staged_signers`:** the stages, in `position` order. `role` is `company`, `investor` or `axal`. `envelope_uuid` and `sent_at` are set when the stage is sent, and `signed_at` when its envelope completes.

**The order is the database's, not only the route's.** Its triggers refuse:
- an `axal` stage unless `countersign` = 1;
- any stage added after the `axal` one, so Axal signs last;
- sending a stage while a stage ahead of it is unsigned;
- writing a stage's sending, signing or signer twice, or moving it.

Nothing secret is stored: no token, no email and no signature image. The token lives on `esign_recipients`, as for every envelope. Nothing is added to `users` or to any existing table.

### 3. Each stage is an ordinary envelope

A stage is sent as a single-recipient envelope through `createAndSendEnvelope({ renderedBody })`, so the existing sign route, its audit chain and its certificate all apply. After the send, the stage's `esign_envelopes.body_sha256` is checked against the recorded digest. On a mismatch the envelope is voided and nothing is recorded (`text_changed`).

When an envelope completes, the sign route calls `onStagedEnvelopeCompleted`, in its own `try`, before the Y-1 Trust Center hook. That call:
- records the stage's signature;
- sends the next stage, unless it is Axal's, which waits for its release.

A send that cannot happen logs only `[stagedSigning] next stage not sent staged=<id> code=<code>`. It never fails the signature.

### 4. The routes

| Route | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /staged/preview` | signed in | The text a stage would sign, with the fields still unfilled. A counsel draft previews with its sentence (§5). |
| `POST /staged` | the closing's founder or an admin | Records the stages and sends stage 1. 201. |
| `GET /staged/:uid` | the closing's founder, the line's investor, admins | The status per signer. |
| `GET /staged/line/:commitmentId` | the same | Every staged envelope on one closing line. |
| `POST /staged/:uid/countersign` | a Super Admin, with a fresh step-up | Releases Axal's stage to the caller and returns their signing token. |

**`POST /staged`** takes `{ deal_id, commitment_id?, document_type, title?, fields?, signers: [{ role, user_id }], countersign? }`.
- Anyone else gets 404, the same as a closing that does not exist.
- The line must be in the closing (`line_not_in_closing`).
- Each signer is `company` or `investor` and has an account (`signer_role`, `signer_unknown`). Axal is never named here; it is added through `countersign`.
- The investor is the line's investor (`investor_not_on_line`).
- There are at most six stages.
- Every field must be filled (`fields_unfilled`, with the list).
- The text is filled once, for everyone.

**The countersign** is `closing.countersign`: `requireSuperAdmin`, then `requireStepUp`. A TOTP login inside the step-up window counts, as everywhere else. Its refusals:
- a stale session is refused with `step_up_required` and nothing is sent;
- an envelope with no Axal stage is refused with `no_countersign`;
- a release before every stage ahead has signed is refused with `not_your_turn`, by the service and again by the trigger.

The stage then names the Super Admin who released it.

**Each stage reads as one of:**
- `waiting`;
- `awaiting_release`: Axal's stage, once everything ahead has signed;
- `sent`, `signed` or `declined`;
- `void`.

The read also carries `closing: { deal_id, commitment_id }`, the link from a signed envelope to its closing line, and `complete`.

Refusals use the shared `refuse` helper with the service's own sentence, never an exception's text.

### 5. A counsel draft

`templateBodyFor` returns null for a document type with no counsel text on file. A SAFE is one until D578's text is on file.
- With no draft text supplied, preview and start are both refused with 409 `counsel_draft_only`.
- With a draft supplied, preview returns it with `sendable: false` and the line "This is a counsel draft. It can be previewed, never sent, until counsel’s text is on file (D578)."
- `POST /staged` refuses a counsel draft with the same sentence. It never sends one.

### 6. Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/staged_signing_d1216.test.ts` covers:
- **The order:** stage 1 goes to the company. The investor is sent only when the company signs. Axal's stage reads `awaiting_release` and is never sent on its own. Every stage's envelope hashes to the recorded digest.
- **Out of order:** a stage cannot be sent or released before the one ahead of it signs.
- **The countersign:** refused without a fresh step-up, with nothing sent. With one, Axal signs last and the envelope is complete. The signing token never reaches a log.
- **Who reads:** the founder, the line's investor and admins. Anyone else reads 404.
- **Who starts:** only the founder or an admin. Each signer is an account, and the investor is the line's.
- **A counsel draft:** previewed with its sentence, never sent.
- **The store's triggers:** an Axal stage needs `countersign` and is last; the text never changes.

The booklet's suite (`booklet_esign_d759`) and the other e-sign suites still pass.

**Mutation checks, each caught:**
- the countersign without step-up;
- the service's order check removed;
- the order trigger removed;
- the next stage not sent;
- Axal's stage sent on its own;
- anyone reads;
- a counsel draft sendable;
- a signature not recorded.
