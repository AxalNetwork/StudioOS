### 1. Where it lives

- **One sheet, in place.**
  - `components/checkout/CheckoutSheet.jsx` opens over the page the member is on, with no route change.
  - It is a modal dialog: Esc and Close shut it, focus moves in, Tab stays inside, and focus returns to the opener.
  - On desktop it is 880 wide with two columns, "Your plan" and "Payment". Below 640px it fills the screen.
- **Who opens it today.** On `/pricing`, a signed-in member's Upgrade or "Get …" on a paid card. `planCards` gives such a card `button.checkout` and its `plan`, instead of a link to `/pricing/products/<id>`.
  - A visitor still registers first.
  - "Manage plan" still goes to Billing.
  - The paywall, Billing and Usage join in part 6. `/pricing/products` stays for one-time add-ons.
- **No Eadwyn.** The sheet has no Eadwyn mark and no rail.

### 2. What is read, and from where

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/public/plans-and-packs` | The summary: the plan's family, name, the price of each interval with its `price_id` (from `prices[]`, because the `monthly` and `yearly` views carry no id), the Worker's `yearly_saving_percent`, and the AI allowance |
| `POST /api/payments/intent` (through `AxalCheckout`) | The payment, behind Continue |
| `POST /api/payments/promo/validate` | Promo codes, inside the payment form |

- **Continue first, then the payment form.** The intent makes a subscription in Stripe as soon as it is called, so the sheet never calls it on open. `AxalCheckout` is lazy-loaded on Continue, which also keeps Stripe out of Pricing's bundle until then.
- **Since D892 the intent is safe to sit on.** It stamps the plan on the subscription, so the webhook grants it, and it refuses a second subscription in the same family with 409 `subscription_exists`.
- **Nothing is computed in the browser.**
  - The yearly monthly figure is the yearly price divided by 12, as Pricing's own cards show it.
  - Tax, a discount and the amount due today show in the payment form, not in the summary.
  - Storage is "not read here yet" until #2449 lands.
  - A price in another currency is not sold here.

### 3. What the sheet draws today

- **Your plan:**
  - the family pill in the profile colour, the name and the card's tagline;
  - Monthly and Yearly with "Save n%" when the plan sells both;
  - the price, with "Renews each billing period. Cancel any time from Billing.";
  - Included: the AI allowance (or "AI runs from AI tokens you top up"), storage as not read, then the card's lines;
  - the subtotal, and where the rest of the total shows.
- **Payment, by who is paying** (`sheetMode`):
  - **pay:** Continue to payment, then today's Payment Element with promo codes and inline 3-D Secure, with "still being confirmed" under it.
  - **change**, for a member who already pays in the family: "You already pay for a plan in this group", with Billing, where the swap prorates, until part 3.
  - **staff:** "Staff account · no plan needed".
  - **explorer:** "Choose your profile first", with `/onboarding`.
  - **other**, for a plan from another profile: "This plan is for another profile."
  - **unsellable**, for a plan with no price on sale: "This plan's price couldn't be read just now. That doesn't mean it's free."
  - The secure and seller lines show in every mode.
- **After payment:** "Payment received", with the activating line, and Billing for the receipt. What changed on the plan waits for part 3's status read.
- **Not drawn now, and why:**
  - the quote, a change in place with step-up, saved cards, and the activating poll (parts 2 and 3);
  - the designed refusals and the decline map, the promo states, Accepted with what you get now, loading and errors (part 5);
  - the renewal banner, and the paywall, Billing and Usage entries (parts 4 and 6);
  - phone and dark polish (part 7).
