`/raise/evidence` reads `GET /api/cycles/raise/evidence?limit=100` once. Each block of that read is drawn on its own.

Each has its state, where it is stated (narrative or deck version, slide), its record's Source label, provider and date, the best item's strength and sharing, and Changed since pinned or Stale. **Every change opens an in-page dialog first** (`ChangeDialog`, a `Lightbox`, a bottom sheet on a phone). The dialog says what the change does: Accept, Dismiss, a batch, Share, a sharing level, Confirm, Unpin, Pin again, Link or Unlink. A refusal shows the Worker's own sentence and changes nothing. There is no browser pop-up.

**Look again** is the Worker's refresh (`POST …/evidence/refresh`). Any change reads the page again, so each claim is checked again (D1264 §4).

### 2. Facts by code

- **Nothing on the page sets a state, a Source label, a strength or a date.** Each is the Worker's, read from the record.
- **Typed-only is never Backed.** The tile counts only `backed`. A `founders_figure` claim reads "Founder's figure", and its drawer says why: "Only a figure you typed agrees …".
- **The record's own date, never the sync's.** An item with no date shows the Worker's `date_reason`.
- **No figure is invented.** A count over a page with more says "100+". An unreadable block says Unreadable with its own Try again; its tile reads Unreadable, never 0. A claim with no record says "No record of this kind on StudioOS".
- **The labels are D1261's.** The five Sources, the states and the sharing levels come from `evidenceModel.js`. `evidenceView.js` adds the kind and figure words (the Worker's `FIGURE_LABEL`) and no grade.

### 3. What the Worker adds (worker first)

- **`opens` on the founder's read** (`readRaiseEvidence`):
  - the last 30 days of `data_room_access_log` rows with `action = 'open_evidence'`;
  - a count, the number of distinct investors, and the newest 20, each with the investor's display name (else their name) and the time;
  - never an email;
  - its own `unreadable` block. A studio admin reads it too, as on VR12.
- **`GET /api/cycles/raise/evidence/preview?project=&nda=0|1`**, founder only:
  - It is `previewEvidence`: the same projection `investorEvidence` returns, factored out as `sharedView`.
  - It is read as an invited investor with an unscoped grant who has not committed (`PREVIEW_AS`, shown on the page). A scoped grant or a committed-only file can open less or more.
  - It writes no `open_evidence`.
  - `audience` counts the investors with an active grant now, and those of them with an active NDA. Counts only; no investor is named.
  - An admin, a support session and a bad `nda` are refused.
- **The client** gains `raiseEvidenceApi.preview`.
- **All statements are bound.** No SQL is interpolated.
