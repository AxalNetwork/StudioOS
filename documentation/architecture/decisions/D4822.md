- **Status:** canvas landed 2026-11-09 (`design/incoming/Delivery v2 Page.dc.html`, `design/incoming/Delivery v2 · Boards.dc.html`, boards DL01 to DL09, canvas sections PD1 to PD9), drawn 2026-11-07 from the #4822 prompt and its live browser audit addendum. Not built.
- **Builds on:** `partner_delivery.ts`; the Partner shell in `frontend/src/workspaces/shellConfig.js`.

### What the canvas decides

- `/delivery` lands on **Board** (Attention and All views); zones Deliverables, Capacity (with overload detail), Status reports (list and composer), Health (risk board and engagement detail); one cross-zone engagement sheet reusing the zone reads.
- **Zero state:** the five zero cards become one upstream-aware state with Open Pipeline as the primary action. No 0-of-0 commentary.
- **The primary action follows state:** Open Pipeline / Review attention / Draft status report.
- **Copy:** “Read time unavailable” and “Capacity limits not set” replace implementation language; sent reports are records that a person sent them (`/send` delivers nothing).
- **Attention reasons** derive from overdue milestones, open blockers, health and due reports. Eadwyn is collapsed and secondary.

### Backend required

- Client open tracking (nothing sets `opened_at`).
- One basis for capacity: limits are weekly hours, allocations are monthly (#4690).
- A Blocked field on status reports (today `shipped` and `next_up` only).
- Client review history (only the latest satisfaction and its source are stored).
- An expected-version check on writes, for the conflict state.
