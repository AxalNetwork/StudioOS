## D4822 — Partner › Delivery v2: an engagement-first workspace that is honest about what it cannot see (#4822)

- **Status:** canvas landed 2026-11-09 (`design/incoming/Delivery v2 Page.dc.html`, `design/incoming/Delivery v2 · Boards.dc.html`, boards DL01 to DL09, canvas sections PD1 to PD9), drawn 2026-11-07 from the #4822 prompt and its live browser audit addendum. Not built.
- **Builds on:** `partner_delivery.ts`; the Partner shell in `frontend/src/workspaces/shellConfig.js`.

### What the canvas decides

- `/delivery` lands on **Board** (Attention and All views); zones Deliverables, Capacity (with overload detail), Status reports (list and composer), Health (risk board and engagement detail); one cross-zone engagement sheet reusing the zone reads.
- **Zero state:** the five zero cards become one upstream-aware state with Open Pipeline as the primary action. No 0-of-0 commentary.
- **The primary action follows state:** Open Pipeline / Review attention / Draft status report.
- **Copy:** “Read time unavailable” and “Capacity limits not set” replace implementation language; sent reports are records that a person sent them (`/send` delivers nothing).
- **Attention reasons** derive from overdue milestones, open blockers, health and due reports. Eadwyn is collapsed and secondary.

### Backend required

- Client open tracking (nothing sets `opened_at`).
- One basis for capacity: limits are weekly hours, allocations are monthly (#4690).
- A Blocked field on status reports (today `shipped` and `next_up` only).
- Client review history (only the latest satisfaction and its source are stored).
- An expected-version check on writes, for the conflict state.

### Built, part 1 (2026-11-09, S22): Board lands, the Sheet opens

Session 1 set the order: Board first, then the engagement Sheet, then the other zones. This part builds the first two. The issue stays open for Deliverables, Capacity, Status reports, Health and the folded-in UI from #4698 and #4710.

- **`/delivery` is Board.** `PartnerBucketRoutes` renders Board at the bucket root and lights the Board tab. It does not redirect, so `/delivery?engagement=…` keeps its query, and `App.jsx` keeps one route per bucket root. Every other bucket root still renders its overview.
- **Action-first rows (DL01).** The columns, in order, are Client, Next action · why, Health, Progress, Owner, Mode · value. The views are Attention, All, Projects and Embedded, each with its count. Attention is the default view when anything needs attention.
- **Attention reasons are facts** (`boardAttention.js`): an overdue open milestone, an open blocker (and whose side it is on), recorded scope drift, no owner, or a health the Worker's `healthFor` rated at risk or blocked.
  - An unrated engagement is not attention-worthy for that alone.
  - A report not yet sent for the period is a next action ("Draft the November report"), never an alarm, because no reporting cadence is stored to call it due.
  - Closed engagements (cancelled, or every seat revoked) ask for nothing.
- **The primary action follows state:**
  - no engagement: Open Pipeline;
  - anything needs attention: Review attention;
  - a period report not yet sent: Draft status report;
  - otherwise none.
- **One empty state (DL07):** "No active client work yet", the Pipeline → Proposal → Engagement → Delivery line, Open Pipeline, and what each zone will hold. No zero is printed. The v1 tile strip and its 0-of-0 copy are gone.
- **Health is word and mark (`HealthPill`).** Not rated is dashed and never green.
- **Phone (DL01m):** one card per engagement, with the next action before the metrics and a 44px "Open engagement" button. Desktop keeps a semantic table with a caption and row headers.
- **The Sheet (DL06, PD6).** It opens from a row, keeps the selection in the URL, and is built from the same row (no second read or data model). It has an accessible dialog (labelled, Esc closes, focus returns). It shows the next action and one line each for Progress, Deliverables (read status unavailable), Status report, Health (with the scope assessment) and Capacity, each linking to its zone with `?engagement=`. Part 2 makes every zone read that parameter (below).
- **Worker (`GET /partner/delivery/board`).** Each row now also carries:
  - `owner_user_id` and `owner_name`;
  - `scope_state` (migration 232's rows, as Health reads them);
  - `milestones_overdue`;
  - `open_blockers` and `open_blockers_client_side`;
  - `deliverables_sent` and `deliverables_signed_off`;
  - the period's `report_state` (`sent`, `draft` or null), with `report_period`.

  All are counted from rows the read already scopes to the firm.
- **Unchanged:**
  - mode is derived from seats;
  - health is the one `healthFor`;
  - progress is two measures, never averaged;
  - a revoked seat stays visible;
  - the lifecycle and invoice ledger stay below the table;
  - the Eadwyn draft band stays, below the work.

## Built, part 2

- **Every zone keeps the Sheet's engagement (`engagementFocus.jsx`).**
  - `?engagement=<uid>` (the id works too) narrows Deliverables, Status reports and Health to that engagement's rows, and Capacity to its seats.
  - A bar above the rows says whose rows they are and how many of the zone's total, and links Back to engagement and Show all.
  - The zone's create form starts on that engagement: Log deliverable, the report composer, and the grant and hours forms.
  - A value no row carries narrows to nothing and says "an engagement this zone has no records for". It never falls back to the whole firm or another engagement.
  - The firm-wide tile strips (Status reports, Health) are hidden while a zone is focused, so a firm figure never sits above one engagement's list. Capacity's roster stays firm-wide, and its bar says so.
- **Canvas copy and states:**
  - **Deliverables:**
    - Shipped work.
    - "No deliverables yet" with Log first deliverable.
    - "Read status unavailable · client opens are not currently tracked", never "ignored". The median time to open stays empty.
    - No schema word (`opened_at`) in product copy.
    - While opens are not tracked (`read_tracking.available` false), the Never opened chip and Chase unopened are hidden and the log opens on All: neither could match anything true. Both return by themselves once opens are tracked.
  - **Capacity:**
    - People and allocation.
    - A basis line: month · monthly totals · limits are hours a week.
    - "Capacity limits not set — Set available hours to identify over-allocation" with Set capacity, which jumps to the limits form. The Worker's `cap_note` says the same.
  - **Status reports:**
    - Recurring client updates.
    - "No status reports yet" with Draft status report.
    - The composer opens with an anchor that names the client, the period in words, and New report / Draft / Sent · read only.
    - Shipped, Next and Blocked are headed sections, each saying where it reads from.
  - **Health:**
    - Renewal and delivery risk.
    - "No engagement health yet".
    - Utilisation is marked as read from Pipeline · Retainers.
- **Worker:**
  - `satisfaction_note` is null when no live engagement exists (no "0 of 0" sentence).
  - `median_days_to_open_note` and `cap_note` use the canvas wording.
- **Still not built** (the canvas's own "Backend required" list):
  - client-open tracking;
  - one capacity basis (#4690);
  - a stored Blocked field and review history on reports;
  - expected-version conflict.

  #4710's report UI stays with its own slot.
