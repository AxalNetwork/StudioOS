## D663 — The Raise stores and /api/cycles/raise, and the pitch deck stuck at DRAFT outside the Lab

Issue #1476, 2026-10-09, part of #1469 (D656). Migration 442. S07, by the owner's instruction (moved from S04).

This file grows with each part of #1476. Part 1 is the DRAFT-deck fix, which S1 asked to ship first because it is a live bug.

### Part 1: a founder outside the Lab never got a final deck

**The bug.** A founder who never joined the Spin-Out Lab has no Lab start date.
- `daysRemaining(null)` returns 28 (`axalSpinoutDemoDay.ts`), so the programme day is 0.
- `draft = programDay < 28 || gaps.length > 0` (`spinoutDeckData.ts`) then marked every such deck DRAFT.
- The same rule was repeated in the override layer (`spinoutDeckOverrides.ts`), so retiring every gap by hand did not help either.
- The cover also printed "DEMO DAY · Day 0 / 28" and "LAB STATUS: Day 0 / 28" for someone who was never in the programme.

**The fix: the programme day counts only for a Lab member.**
- **Who counts as a member.** A member is someone active in the Lab now, or with a Lab start date on record (`isLabMember`). That covers both current and former members. The fill writes it as `meta.lab_member`.
- **`programmeHolds`.** The deck bundle carries `programmeHolds`: true only for a member before Day 28.
- **When a deck is a draft:**
  - `draft` is `programmeHolds || gaps.length > 0`, in both the mapper and the override layer.
  - A non-member's deck is a draft only while it has gaps.
  - A member's deck before Day 28 stays a draft whatever is overridden, as before.
- **The cover for a non-member** reads "DEMO DAY", and its LAB STATUS row reads "Not in the Lab".
- **Older inputs keep the old rule.** A source or bundle that carries no membership flag (an older caller or a fixture) is treated as a member.

**What still holds decks in draft, for everyone.** The traction slide always raises a gap:
- "monthly revenue history is not tracked yet" when revenue is logged;
- "log revenue proof" when it is not.

Neither gap names a field, so no override retires it. Until a monthly revenue history exists, every deck (Lab or not) stays a draft through that gap. This is a data gap rather than the clock bug fixed here. The Grow stores' `revenue_movements` (#1477) are the likely source of that history.

### Part 2: the Raise stores and `/api/cycles/raise`

**Migration 442** adds what the seven Raise modules had no home for. Each part is additive:
- `readiness_items` and `readiness_scores`: each item scored out of 10, with its evidence (anecdote, pattern or measured), what is missing, whether a person or the computed check gave it, who, and when.
- **Narrative versions on `spinout_deck_overrides`**, as the issue asked, not a parallel store. The table gains `version`, `investor_type` and `deck_version`, and its unique key becomes `(project_id, field_key, version)`.
  - Version 0 is the live deck wording, exactly as before. The deck reads only version 0, and the override editor writes only version 0.
  - Saving a version copies version 0 to version N, with the investor type and the current deck version. A marker row (`_version`) keeps a version that had no overrides.
  - The issue's work list names a `narrative_versions` table. That would have been the parallel store the same issue rules out, so it was not created.
  - A database migrated before 442 is healed on first use: the columns are added and the old two-column key is dropped.
- `narrative_checks`: each mismatch the consistency check found for a saved version.
- `deck_view_slides`: seconds per slide for each share-link view.
- `data_room_files.checklist_item`.
- `raise_prospects.next_step`, `next_step_due` and `warm_path`.

**The read.** `routes/cycle_raise.ts` serves `GET /overview` (four figures, seven cards) and `GET /modules/:slug` (one card and its records), in D656's and D661's shapes, through `services/cycleCards.ts`. Each card fails on its own.

**Who.** Only the venture's own and admins (`canWrite`), for reads too. Validate's board is open to partners; the Raise cards are not, because they carry the cap table, the investor pipeline and who opened the data room. The pipeline, cap table and data-room tools already keep those to the venture and admins.

**The four figures:**
- **Readiness score:** the mean of each scored item's newest score, out of 100.
- **Committed against target:** a pair, committed amount and the active round's target. With no target it is the committed amount alone, with a reason.
- **Investors in conversation:** contacted, meeting or diligence. No stage history is stored, so no 30-day change.
- **Months of runway:** cash over net burn from the newest metrics snapshot that has both. Not recorded without one, or when nothing burns.

**The seven cards:**
- **Fundraising readiness.** Each item's score shaded by evidence, and the overall score over time.
  - With no items the card is `empty`, and its action is "Score fundraising readiness".
  - The computed rescore creates the seven items and scores six of them. These are the Lab capital page's data-room readiness rules, now stored, so a Lab venture and a founder see the same scores:
    - deck: a pitch deck exists;
    - use of funds: an allocation and a raise target (half for one of them);
    - cap table: a scenario exists;
    - traction: revenue, MRR or paying customers recorded;
    - data room: the share of the checklist that files answer;
    - narrative: 10, less 2 per mismatch in the newest version.
  - Team has no store to read, so a person scores it.
- **Investor narrative.** Versions by investor type, and what the check found in the newest version.
  - **The consistency check** compares the figures the deck states (`projects.mrr`, `paying_customers`, `funding_needed`, which the deck's traction and ask slides read) and the figures the version's wording states (read by fixed patterns, never by a model) with the stored metrics:
    - the newest `project_metrics.mrr` and `paying_accounts`;
    - the active round's `target_amount`.
  - Figures within 2% agree. A figure with no stored metric is not a mismatch.
  - Every mismatch stores and shows both values and both sources.
  - Eadwyn phrasing the fix is deferred.
- **Pitch deck.** Views per week and seconds per slide.
  - The share viewer now counts a second for the slide being read, once a second while the tab is visible, and sends its running totals with the heartbeat.
  - The Worker stores each total bounded in the same statement by what the view's other slides hold, so a view's slides never add up to more than its `read_seconds`.
  - A share link records no investor, so the records name each link by its creation day.
- **Data room.** Each diligence area's coverage, and who opened the room in the last 90 days.
  - The checklist is fixed in `raiseCycle.ts`: twelve items across Company, Financials, Legal, Customers, Team and Product.
  - Coverage is drawn by checklist area rather than by folder, because folders are free-form and an area is what diligence asks for.
- **Cap table.** Ownership by Founders, Option pool and Investors, before and after the first planned round of the canonical scenario.
- **Use of funds.** The planned split, and cash over 18 months with and without the raise.
  - A category's monthly spend is Not recorded: no store splits burn by category.
  - A category's milestone column is the milestone costs the Use of Funds page maps to its bucket.
- **Capital relationships.** Prospects by the furthest stage they reached (passed excluded), and the follow-ups overdue and due within seven days.
  - **A prospect's last contact** is its stored contact's `last_activity_at`, joined on the same project, and nothing else. A prospect with no contact, or a contact with no activity, shows Not recorded.

**The writes** go through `services/raiseWriters.ts`, and each records its actor in `activity_logs`:
- `PUT /readiness/:key`;
- `POST /readiness/rescore`;
- `POST /narrative-versions`;
- `PUT /data-room/files/:id/checklist`;
- `PUT /prospects/:id/next-step`.
