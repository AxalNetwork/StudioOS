## D4817 part 10 — The Admin Investor preview reaches the company workspace (#4817)

Part of #5311: PF-08's Admin Investor preview (D4817 part 8), extended to the company workspace PF-06 built (D4817 part 6) at `/portfolio/companies/:uid` and its tabs (`/financials`, `/reports`, `/support`, `/activity`, and the old `/terms`, which lands on Financials' Ownership & rights). Frontend only. No route, Worker or schema change.

**What was there.** Part 8 left the company workspace out ("Not covered": PF-07 was being built in parallel). An administrator previewing the Investor shell who opened a company from the preview boards got the real workspace, read as the administrator: `/api/positions`, `/api/reporting/companies/:uid`, health, the support ledger, the company's rounds and marks, its updates, its cap table and its activity. So the page showed the administrator's book (most often "not held") under an investor's heading; where it did draw a company, Ask for this report, Waive this period, Record support… and the cap table's Ask for access were live and went out as the administrator; and the chrome was the shell's strip, which says writes still go out, not the page banner the six boards carry.

**What is built.**
- `previewModel.js`: `previewCompanyTabOf(pathname)` names the tab of a company address (the same six routes App.jsx mounts), or null. `pageOwnsPreviewBanner` now answers true for those addresses as well as the six boards. `COMPANY_WITHHELD` ("Company withheld in preview"), `PREVIEW_COMPANY_FACTS` (the six key facts `keyFactsOf` draws) and `PREVIEW_COMPANY_TABS` (each tab's sections, headed as the real tab heads them, and the writes it offers) are the preview's words.
- `CompanyPreview.jsx`: `PortfolioCompanyPage` draws it in place of the workspace when `isInvestorPreview` holds (the same rule as the boards: an administrator, not in a support session, browsing as `investor`). It draws the frame with the one `PreviewBanner` in its `banner` slot, the crumb, the five tabs as links with `aria-current`, the six key facts and each tab's sections, each with a `PrivateChip`, and the tab's writes **disabled**, `aria-describedby` the reason beside them (PF-08's `OffWrites`). It reads nothing and draws no rail. The title is `Company · <tab> · Portfolio · Investor preview · Axal VC`.
- `PortfolioPreview.jsx` exports `WithheldSection` and `OffWrites` so the company preview draws the same withheld section and off writes; `OffWrites` gains an optional `title` (default unchanged).
- `App.jsx`, additive and with no new import (D5615): `PREVIEW_BANNER_COMPANY`, its copy of the company addresses, and `pageOwnsPreviewBanner` consults it too, so the shell's strip stands down on the company tabs. The new test holds it to `previewCompanyTabOf`.

**Who the company is.** The company's name, stage, sector and holding route come only from the investor's own reads (the positions list and the reports owed); there is no read the preview could make that names a company without reading an investor's record. So the heading says "Company withheld in preview", the line under it carries the chip for the company and the holding, and Health in the header carries the chip. The uid in the address is not drawn as a name.

**Writes, per tab.** Reports: Ask for this report, Waive this period. Support: Record support… (on the real tab a link to Value-add's write flow, shown here as the write it leads to). Financials: Ask for access (the read-only cap table's ask to the founder). Overview and Activity offer no write. The Refresh button and the Activity source filter are not drawn: there is nothing read to refresh or filter.

**Unchanged.** A real investor, and an administrator in a support session (View As, whose session is the member's own), get the company workspace exactly as before, with its reads and its live writes. Everywhere off Portfolio, and on `/portfolio/waterfall`, the shell's strip is the one banner.

**Departures.**
- **Section headings that name the company say "this company"** ("Support for this company"), since the name is withheld.
- **The heading cites #4817**, as `check-decision-ids` requires of a part file (a part cites its parent's issue); the work is part of #5311.
- **Next actions, the timeline and the cap table are each one withheld section**, not a skeleton of rows: a row count would itself be a figure read from the investor's record.

**Tests.** `frontend/test/portfolio_company_preview_d4817.test.mjs` mounts the whole App against a stubbed `fetch` that would answer every company read with a held company, as an administrator previewing Investor, on each tab: one banner and no strip; the five tabs with the current one marked; the company never named, every fact and section chipped, no `$0`, empty list, Not recorded or Unreadable, and no private request made (none to positions, reporting, health, support, updates or the cap table, and none naming the company); only the way back enabled, each write disabled with its reason; the way back lands on `/hq`; a real investor and a support session get the named workspace, its reads and a live Ask; and the model and App.jsx agree. Ten mutations are listed in its header, each failing and then passing again when reverted.
