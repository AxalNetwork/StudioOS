### What the canvas decides

- The settings open as a 560px sheet over `/portfolio/updates` from the Reports toolbar. There is no new route.
- The toolbar button "Edit rules" is renamed "Reporting settings".
- The GP of a fund edits that fund's required figures, grouped Monthly then Quarterly, each with its last completed period. Each row shows unit, cadence, Required or Optional, Applies to, the definition and a drag handle.
- Below the list sit the due day, who the rules cover and the period they apply from. Save rules is the one primary action.
- A figure is edited in the 640 dialog. Its key is read-only once saved, and a duplicate key is refused, naming the figure that holds it.
- A licensed investor who runs no fund reads the settings, read-only. A fund with no rules of its own inherits the HQ set and can make it its own.
- Each cadence is read on its own, with its own loading, Unreadable and Partial states; Save waits for a failed read. A save conflict shows a compare.
- After saving, the sheet returns to Reports with the Rules chip selected and a receipt. Eadwyn only proposes a change from the period's submissions.

### Backend required

All tagged #6213 → #6214:

- A write route that refuses a duplicate key itself, because a NULL `fund_id` escapes the UNIQUE.
- A per-fund read that falls back to the HQ set.
- `updated_at` in the read, and an `updated_by` column.
- A due-day column, decided together with #6204.
- An effective-from period, or set history.
- Fund and stage per company in `companies[]`.
- An `updated_at` precondition for the save conflict.
- A failed read reported as a failure, not an empty `kpi_set`.
- `KPI_RULES_REQUIRED` read from the table instead of the two hard-coded copies.
