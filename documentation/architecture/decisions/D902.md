## D902 — Pitch deck studio part 8: the share dialog, the NDA gate, and Share & analytics per version (#2681)

- **Status:** built by S14, 2026-10-24 (#2681, part of #2434). The PR stays a draft until the owner answers question 3 (what a link includes); it builds answer A, as the canvas draws it.
- **Canvas:** "Pitch Deck Studio", PD18 (the share dialog), PD19 (Share & analytics), PD20 (the investor's viewer: A the NDA gate, B the figures with chips) and PD22 (a closed link). See D894 for where it is committed.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `b50bed8`.
- **Migration:** `540_deck_share_nda_include.sql` adds `nda_required` (0 or 1, default 0) and `include_json` (NULL is the default set) to `pitch_deck_share_tokens`. A link made before 540 reads as no NDA, the default set.

### 1. One link writer

- **Every link is written by `createDeckLink`** (`services/raiseWriters.ts`), behind `POST /api/cycles/raise/deck-links`. The editor's Share and the studio's "New link" open one dialog (`raise/pitch/ShareDialog.jsx`), and it sends one body (`shareModel.js` `shareBody`).
- **The writer takes three new fields:** `view_limit` (a whole number 1–100, or none), `nda_required`, and `include`. An `include` with an unknown key or a value that is not true or false is refused with 400, and no link is made.
- **`POST /api/decks/:id/share` stays the Spin-Out Lab's,** with its old body.
- **Two links made in the same second differ.** The signed token now carries a random nonce. Before it, the two tokens were identical and the second insert broke the unique hash.
- **The link is shown once,** with Copy. Only its hash is stored. The dialog turns only a `/share/deck/<token>` path that the writer returned into a link.
- **The dialog's defaults are the canvas's:** 14 days, Download off, Forwarding off, the NDA on, and the figures and the sources appendix in. Speaker notes and the Scorecard and Product-market fit bands are off.
- **The dialog says what Create link writes** before it writes it.

### 2. What a link includes (owner question 3: answer A)

`services/decks/shareInclude.ts` filters every share read. The switches are figures, sources, notes and bands.

These never leave on a link, whatever the switches say:
- an internal score (`vr_*` fields);
- a bank balance, an account or a counterparty;
- a quote whose consent is not recorded (`consent: true`). The editor's save now keeps `consent`;
- a figure's record. A D896 binding goes out as its grade and date only, without its `source_key`, `record_ref` or `snapshot_hash`. A field bound to a score, a bank slot or a band is judged by that slot, as by its own key.

The admin's deck notes (`deck_notes`, D905) are never on a link. "Speaker notes" means a slide's own `notes`, which no template writes yet.

### 3. The viewer (PD20, PD22)

- **The NDA gate.** A link that asks for the NDA sends no card until the viewer has accepted it on this link: a `nda_signed` conversion with this link's `share_token_id` and this user. The gate's answer is the venture's name, the version, and whether the viewer is signed in. Reading the gate uses no view.
  - The NDA's text comes from `GET /api/decks/share/:token/nda`. Its "[Your name]" and "[Your email]" placeholders stay until the viewer signs in.
  - **Accepting needs an account.** It is the existing `POST /share/:token/nda`. Without an account, the gate offers Sign in and comes back to this link. Whether an anonymous investor may accept is the owner's call and is not built.
- **A closed link** (expired, used up, or withdrawn) answers 410 `link_closed`. It shows "This link is no longer open." and the venture to ask, and nothing from the deck.
  - An expired token's deck is found only after its signature verifies.
- **The frozen note.** The viewer's header says "Frozen at vN as of DD Mon. The deal page shows this same viewer." (DEAL #2421).
- **Chips.** Each figure on a shared version shows a chip. A figure bound to a record (D896) shows that record's grade: Measured, Reviewed, Sourced, Stated or Not recorded. Otherwise it shows the field's recorded source: From records, Computed, Drafted, or Not recorded.
  - The chips sit in a strip under the deck, not on the card. The card renderers are the export's, and they draw no chips.
- **No deal pack on a studio link.** A link made for an investor (a prospect, or a typed name) does not offer the deal pack. Its deal-pack reads answer 404 `deal_pack_not_offered`, and the viewer hides the fundraising ask.

### 4. Share & analytics per version (PD19)

`GET /api/decks/:id/engagement` gains `analytics` (`services/decks/shareAnalytics.ts`), computed over every view of this one version, not a page of them:
- **viewers:** one per browser.
- **median time per viewer.**
- **seconds per card:** this version's, with the version before it, drawn dotted.
- **reached the ask:** viewers whose furthest card read is at or past the ask card. A version with no ask card says so, and is never drawn as 0.
- **per link:** the investor, the settings, and NDA signatures.

If the block cannot be read it answers `available: false, unreadable: true`, and the studio says "could not be read". It does not draw 0.

The funnel's denominator is now unique viewers. Without the block it falls back to the capped list and says so.

### 5. Known limits

- **The view limit has no "no limit" in the store** until #2674's migration lands. "No limit" is still the writer's sentinel.
- **With sources off,** an appendix card placed before the ask would shift the card numbers. Every template puts the appendix last.
- **The Raise module's own New link form** (`pages/cycles/forms.js`) is unchanged. The Worker's defaults apply to the links it makes.
