### What a finding is

A sentence kept with a Research Project, labelled by whose sentence it is:

- **Source fact:** your sentence, standing on at least one passage that one of the project's Ask answers recorded from a document in the project's evidence.
- **Eadwyn interpretation:** Eadwyn's words from one of the project's Ask answers, with the sources that answer cites, kept once you accept, edit or discard them.
- **Your conclusion:** your own words, with or without a source.

### Built

1. **The store.**
   - `research_project_findings` holds the project, owner, kind, text, `proposed_text`, the Ask answer it came from, `state`, `decided_by`, `decided_at` and timestamps.
   - CHECKs keep `state` (accepted, edited or discarded) and `proposed_text` (Eadwyn's words as proposed) on interpretations only. An edit never overwrites what Eadwyn said.
   - A partial unique index allows one interpretation row per answer per project. A new decision updates that row.
   - `research_project_finding_citations` freezes each citation: the document's uid and title, the passage and page as the Ask answer recorded them, and the answer. Deleting the document sets `document_id` NULL, and the page says the source was removed. Deleting the answer keeps the decision.
2. **Worker routes** (`routes/research_project_findings.ts`, mounted at `/api/research/projects` beside part 1's router through the #4105 registry). Every route reads the project through part 1's `checkProjectOwner`, so another member's project is a 404.
   - `GET /:uid/findings` returns the findings with each citation's source state now (available, removed, or not recorded for an old citation without a document uid; in the evidence or not; index state and In Ask), the open proposals, the passages a source fact may cite, the project's evidence for a conclusion's sources, and counts by kind with discarded counted apart.
   - `POST /:uid/findings` saves a source fact or a conclusion. A citation is `{ answer_uid, n }` or `{ document_uid }`, and its document must be in the project's evidence (400 `citation_not_in_project`). A source fact needs a passage (400 `passage_required`). An interpretation cannot be typed in (400 `invalid_kind`). At most 8 citations, at most 4,000 characters.
   - `PATCH /:uid/findings/:findingUid` edits your conclusion's text and, optionally, its sources. Any other kind is 409 `finding_not_editable`.
   - `DELETE /:uid/findings/:findingUid` deletes a source fact or your conclusion. An interpretation is 409 `finding_not_deletable`: Discard is how it is set aside, and the decision stays on record.
   - `POST /:uid/interpretations/:answerUid { decision: accept | edit | discard, text? }` decides on one of the project's answered Ask answers. An answer the project does not hold is 404. An answer with no words (no source found, model unavailable) is 409 `nothing_to_interpret`. An edit identical to Eadwyn's words is 400 `text_unchanged`.
   - An archived project's findings are read-only (409 `project_archived`), as in part 1.
   - Every write leaves a receipt: `research_project_finding_added`, `_updated`, `_deleted` and `research_project_interpretation_decided`. Each names the project, the finding, its kind or decision and the citation count. None carries the finding's text.
   - All SQL is bound. The one list of document uids is passed as a bound JSON array through `json_each`.
3. **Client:** `frontend/src/lib/api/researchProjectFindings.js` (`researchProjectFindingsApi`), in the generated index.
4. **The Findings tab** (`/research/projects/:uid/findings`, `ProjectFindings.jsx`). The tab is now a link, with a badge counting kept findings. The badge is absent, never 0, while the read is not ok.
   - The chips are All, Source facts, Eadwyn, Yours and Discarded, each with its count.
   - Each card shows the kind word first, then the interpretation's state as a word with a glyph (Needs review, Accepted, Edited by you, Discarded), the text and the numbered citation chips. An Eadwyn card has a dashed border. An edited interpretation keeps "Eadwyn's words" under a disclosure.
   - Accept, Edit and Discard on an interpretation. Edit and Delete on your conclusion. Delete on a source fact. Delete asks once in the card first.
   - **Save a source fact** picks one of the recorded passages and asks for the fact in your words. **Write a conclusion** may tick documents from the evidence.
   - **The citation panel** is a side panel on desktop and the shared `Sheet` as a bottom sheet on a phone. It shows the source's availability, year, page, Freshness (Not recorded), index state and In Ask, the passage as recorded, and Open in Library. A removed source keeps its passage and opens nothing.
   - Loading, "No findings yet" and Unavailable with Retry are separate states. An unreadable read is never drawn as empty.
   - A standing line says a numbered source is a reference, not a check that the passage supports the sentence (D4613).
5. **Overview:** Most recent findings reads the same store. It shows the newest two kept findings and "N Eadwyn interpretations need your review", or "No findings kept yet", or Unavailable with Retry, and links to All findings.

### Departures from the canvas

- **No interpretation run.** The canvas's interpretations come from a structured run (RS-08 step 10, "proposals · not saved"), and no server path runs one. The proposals here are the project's own answered Ask answers, Eadwyn's words as Ask recorded them. Nothing is stored until you decide, and no model is called. The structured run remains proposed (D3701).
- **Fresh / Stale is not drawn.** No store records a source's freshness, so the panel's Freshness reads Not recorded and no card carries Fresh or Stale.
- **Flagged and Conflicting are not built.** Nothing records a flag or detects two sources disagreeing, so there is no Flagged chip and no "Sources disagree" card or "Compare the two passages" (Comparisons is a later part).
- **Publisher and Document date** are not recorded per document. The panel shows the source year and the page instead.
- **Source facts are typed by you.** The canvas does not say who writes a source fact's sentence. Here it is your sentence, and it must stand on a recorded passage from the project's evidence, so it is never a sentence without its passage.
- **Re-deciding is allowed.** An accepted or edited interpretation can later be discarded, or a discarded one accepted, from the Discarded chip. Each decision updates the one row and leaves its own receipt.
- **Delete is confirmed in the card,** not Scope → Review → Result: it removes one sentence you wrote, and its sources stay in the Library and the project.
