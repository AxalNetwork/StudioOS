## D589 — A scoring benchmark shows the cohort median and the founder's percentile

**The Lab scoring benchmark adds the founder's cohort median and their percentile, at the same Lab week, once five ventures have an official run.** The owner's answers on #1216 (from #1009), moved from S07 to S06:

- **1A.** No figure below five scored ventures. Five is the threshold D358 set for profiling trends.
- **2A.** The median, and the founder's percentile. Not the median alone.
- **3A.** The founder's Lab cohort (`user_spinout_flags.spinout_lab_cohort`, set when the cohort activates them) at the same `users.spinout_lab_week`.

No migration. `GET /api/scoring/cohort-benchmark/:projectId` is gated like `GET /api/scoring/scores/:projectId`. Each venture contributes its latest official run whose integrity check passes. Practice runs are left out. The body names the cohort and the week, the median, and the percentile. It does not name another venture, and it does not return another venture's score. Below five, or with no cohort, or with no official run on this project, the body has a reason and no median and no percentile.

The median of an odd count is the middle score. Of an even count it is the mean of the two middle scores. The percentile is the rank of the founder's score in that set: one hundred times (the count strictly below, plus half the ties) divided by the count, rounded to the nearest whole number.
