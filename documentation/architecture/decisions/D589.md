## D589 — A scoring benchmark shows the cohort median and the founder's percentile

**The Lab scoring benchmark adds the founder's cohort median and their percentile, at the same Lab week, once five founders have an official run.** The owner's answers on #1216 (from #1009), moved from S07 to S06:

- **1A.** No figure below five scored ventures. Five is the threshold D358 set for profiling trends. The five are founders, not projects.
- **2A.** The median, and the founder's percentile.
- **3A.** The founder's Lab cohort (`user_spinout_flags.spinout_lab_cohort`, set when the cohort activates them) at the same `users.spinout_lab_week`.

No migration. `GET /api/scoring/cohort-benchmark/:projectId` is gated like `GET /api/scoring/scores/:projectId`. Each founder contributes one run: the newest official run whose integrity check passes and whose review status is `auto_approved` or `approved`. Practice runs, flagged runs, rejected runs, and deleted projects are left out. The founder of the project in the URL contributes only that project, so their other projects cannot fill the five. Other founders contribute the newest such run across their projects.

The body names the cohort, the week, the median, the percentile, and the placed run's own score and date. It names no other venture. An odd count's exact median can equal one venture's score; the body never says whose. The median the body returns is that exact median rounded to the nearest 5 points, and the percentile is the exact rank rounded to the nearest 10. The week changes one venture at a time, and two exact reads either side of a move would otherwise recover the mover's score. Below five, or with no cohort, or with no official run on this project, the body has a reason and no median and no percentile.

The exact median of an odd count is the middle score. Of an even count it is the mean of the two middle scores. The exact percentile is the rank of the founder's score in that set: one hundred times (the count strictly below, plus half the ties) divided by the count, rounded to the nearest whole number. The sentence on the card is about that official run, which can differ from the composite when the newest run is a practice run.

A partner or admin read is written to `activity_logs` the same way a scores read is.
