## D702 — Founder profile pages: You, Your venture, What others see and Visibility

Issue #1664. A founder gets four pages from the Studio · Mobile canvas (S6m You `/profile`, S7m Your venture `/profile/venture`, S9m What others see `/profile/preview`, S10m Visibility `/profile/visibility`) and the archetype card's phone layout (S8m). The work ships in two PRs. PR 1 (`Part of`) is the Worker, migration 467, `/account`'s three visibility labels and their tests. The labels ship with the behaviour they describe, because once Hidden is served to the member alone, "Private — admins only" is false (D-s). PR 2 (`Closes`) is the pages, built on #1661's `PhoneBar`, `PhoneRows` and `usePhoneWidth` and #1663's Eadwyn menu, once both had merged. Decisions 6, 9 and 10 below are PR 2's, and are recorded here so both PRs share one entry.

**Status: built, PR 2 of 2 (2026-10-17).** PR 1 (#1678) shipped the Worker and the labels; PR 2 ships the pages, the routes and menus, the archetype card on a phone, and moves both Studio canvases to `design/canvases/integrated/`.

### Migration 467

`cloudflare-worker/sql/migrations/467_founder_profile_pages.sql` adds two nullable columns, with no backfill. Neither existed in `schema_baseline.sql` or in any migration before it (D-t).

- `projects.investor_findable INTEGER CHECK (investor_findable IN (0, 1))`: "Let investors find my venture". NULL means not chosen, and NULL and 1 both read as findable.
- `user_settings.visibility_chosen_at TEXT`: stamped whenever `PUT /api/settings/privacy` carries `visibility`. NULL means the member never chose.

`ensureUserSettings` declares `visibility_chosen_at` in its runtime `CREATE TABLE`, with an idempotent ALTER beside the others, so `check-schema-pair-drift.mjs` finds one shape (the migration 271 `archetype_sex` precedent). The local check is the repo's runner, in two runs over one fresh directory: `--bootstrap` and then the plain run. Both exit 0, and the second logs `applying 467_founder_profile_pages.sql`.

### The Worker routes

`routes/founder_profile.ts` is mounted at `/api/profile` after `profile_history.ts`. None of its paths collides with `/evidence-blend`, `/history`, `/reask`, `/archetype-published` or `/published-members`.

| Route | Gate | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/profile/me` | signed in; the caller only, and `?user_id=` is ignored | person, bio, background, links and preferences (decision 7) |
| `GET /api/profile/venture?project=N` | `canWrite` | card, problem, facts and module rows (decision 8) |
| `PATCH /api/profile/venture?project=N` | `canWrite` | writes only the seven facts; any other key answers 400 naming it |
| `GET /api/profile/preview?project=N&audience=…` | `canWrite` | decisions 2 and 3; any audience other than investors, founders or partners answers 400 |

Every error body carries `error`. The venture and preview refusals go through `refuse` (`util/refusal.ts`).

### Decision 2 — the preview is the real read

For each audience the preview runs that audience's own reads, through the same functions the routes call. The viewer has that role and matches no row: id 0, no NDA, no introduction and no membership. It applies no masking or visibility rule of its own.

| Audience | Reads it runs |
| --- | --- |
| Investors | the public person read, the public startup read, the published-members read, and the investor arm of `GET /api/projects/:id` (masked; 404 when decision 4 hides the venture) |
| Founders | the same three public reads, and `GET /api/projects/:id` as a founder who is not a member (403) |
| Partners | the same three public reads, and `GET /api/projects/:id` as a partner (the whole row) |

The extractions, each called by its route, which answers as before apart from what decisions 4 and 5 change:

- `publicPersonCard(env, handle, viewer)` and `publicStartupCard(env, handle, viewer)` in `routes/public.ts`. `effectiveFlags` stays exported.
- `projectReadFor(env, user, id)` in `routes/projects.ts`, returning `{ status, body }`.
- `maskForInvestorRow(row, founderUserId)` in `services/trust.ts`, the pure mask step of `maskFounderForInvestor`.
- `publishedMembersFor(env, viewer, slugs)` in `routes/profile_history.ts`. The caller's own lock (`archetypeAccess`) stays in the route, because it is the viewer's plan, not the founder's choice.

The person is the venture's founder user (the lowest `users.id` whose `founder_id` is the venture's, as the public startup read resolves it), not the caller. An admin who previews someone's venture sees that founder.

Search hits and the investor portfolio serve no field these reads do not serve, so the preview does not run them (see the census).

The `SELECT *` rows of `GET /api/projects` and `/:id` now carry `investor_findable`. That is decision 4's change.

### Decision 3 — the preview says what happens

| Row | Field | Served by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MRR | `projects.mrr` | the app read only |
| Revenue | `revenue` | the public startup read (`traction.revenue`) and the app read |
| Users | `users_count` | the public startup read (`traction.users`) and the app read |
| Raise | `funding_needed` | the public startup read (`traction.funding_needed`) and the app read |

`figureRows(startup, app)` composes the rows from what the reads returned:

- A row shows its value when any read of that audience serves it unmasked.
- It shows `masked` (drawn "••••") when the only read that serves it masks it.
- It is left out when no read serves it. A startup read that answers 404 serves nothing.

**No Interviews row.** No read of any audience serves an interview count, so the canvas's Interviews row is not drawn.

**The archetype chip.** The chip is the founder's latest `displayed_slug`. It is drawn only when `publishedMembersFor`, run as viewer 0 for that slug, lists the founder. A member whose own archetype card is locked does not get that list, because the route's lock is that viewer's.

`previewNotes(audience, startup, app, status)` writes one sentence per fact that happened:

- "<Revenue, users and the raise> are on your public venture page, for anyone with its address." It names only the fields served.
- Investors, when the app read masks: "In the app, figures stay masked until an NDA is signed through an introduction." This is the canvas's line, and it is true: an investor with an active pairwise NDA reads the row unmasked. The NDA starts from `POST /api/trust/intro/request`.
- Investors, when decision 4 hides the venture: "Investors without an introduction cannot find your venture in the app."
- Founders: "Other founders cannot open your venture in the app."
- Partners: "Partners can open your venture in the app and read every figure."
- When the startup read answers 404: "Your venture page is not public while the venture is in <status>."

**Public addresses.** These are `https://axal.vc/u/<users.uid>` and `https://axal.vc/startups/<projects.uid>`. Each carries `served_to` (everyone, members, only_you or no_one) and the reason.

### Decision 4 — "Let investors find my venture"

The flag is NULL until the founder sets it, and NULL and 1 behave as before. No venture's reach changes until its founder turns the toggle off, and the page draws the toggle on for NULL.

With 0, an investor reaches the venture only through an introduction. An introduction is either of two things, both checked in one place, `services/investorFindable.ts` (`hiddenFromInvestor`, `findableBy`, `keepFindable`):

- the venture is in `investorProjectIds(env, investor, null)`: a deal-room membership, an introduction row, or a converted watchlist item;
- an active pairwise NDA exists between the investor and the venture's founder user (`hasActivePairwiseNda`).

Admins, partners and founders get an empty hidden set. A database without the column hides nothing.

**The census of investor reads.** I grepped `role === 'investor'`, `isInvestor`, `isPrivileged`, `maskFounderForInvestor` and `FROM projects` for every Worker read where the investor role lists or opens a venture.

The six reads the issue names are filtered, for an investor caller only:

| Read | With 0 and no introduction |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/projects` | omits the venture |
| `GET /api/projects/:id` | 404 `{ error: 'Project not found' }`, as a missing id answers, never 403 |
| `GET /api/search` | drops the venture's project hit |
| `GET /api/private-data/portfolio/metrics` | omits it from `portfolio` and from the count |
| `POST /api/watchlist` (by `project_id` or `project_uid`) | 404 `Project not found`; no row written |
| `POST /api/follows` | 404 `Not found`; no row written |

The census found six more reads that open or name a venture for an investor. They are filtered the same way:

| Read | With 0 and no introduction |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/dashboard` `proprietary_deal_flow` | omits the venture before the mask |
| `POST /api/ic` with `project_id` | 404 `Project not found`; no decision written |
| `POST /api/journal` (`resolveTargets`, by id or uid) | 404 `Project not found`; no entry written |
| `routes/decks.ts` `projectOwned` | NotFound |
| `routes/brand.ts` `projectOwned` | NotFound |
| `routes/compliance.ts` `loadProject` | null, so 404 |

The dashboard is cached per user, for 10 seconds per isolate and 60 seconds in KV. So for up to a minute after the founder flips the toggle, an investor can still be served a cached deal-flow list that names the venture. `?fresh=1` skips the cache. No other filtered read is cached.

The rest of the census is recorded and not filtered:

| Read | Why it is not filtered |
| --- | --- |
| `deals.ts`, deal rooms, `legalcap.ts` deal documents | A deal exists only because the founder or the studio opened one, which is itself an introduction. The open-deal list is a marketplace by design. |
| `dd.ts` | Needs a deal-room membership, which is an introduction. |
| `portfolio.ts`, `portfolio_updates.ts`, positions, `portfolio_support.ts` | Already scoped by `investorProjectIds`. |
| `insights.ts`, `monitoring.ts` | Aggregates only; no venture is named. |
| `liquidity.ts` | Secondary listings, tied to deals. |
| The two research gathers | Read ventures through deals. |
| `founder_risk.ts`, `progress.ts`, revenue, financials, `legal_83b.ts`, company EIN, section 83(b) | Investors are already refused. |
| `writeRouter`, onboarding checklist, `admin.ts` | Open no venture for an investor. |
| `POST /api/trust/intro/request` | It takes a founder's user id, not a venture. It answers only a status, the envelope id and the investor's own signing URL, so it serves no venture field. The NDA it starts turns active only when all three parties have signed, which is the founder accepting the introduction. |
| The public reads (`/api/public/*`) | Out of scope: the public venture page is unchanged. |
| Watchlist items and follows made before the choice | The investor had already found the venture, so their own lists keep it. |

**Found while taking the census, not changed here.** Any investor can read any venture's decks (`decks.ts` `projectOwned` returns the row for `investor`), brand assets (`brand.ts`, the same), and compliance records (`compliance.ts` `isPrivilegedReader` includes `investor`). These are unmasked and need no NDA or introduction. D702 hides a venture whose founder turned the toggle off. The wider access predates D702 and needs its own issue.

### Decision 5 — visibility is enforced where it is drawn

A member with no `user_settings` row reads as `network` with NULL.

| Stored `visibility` | `visibility_chosen_at` | Effective | `GET /api/public/u/:handle` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| private | any | Hidden | served only to the member themself; 404 to everyone else |
| network | set | Members only | 404 to a caller who is not signed in |
| network | NULL | Public | served to anyone, as before |
| public | any | Public | served to anyone |

- `effectiveVisibility`, `visibleToViewer` and `effectiveVisibilityFor` live in `services/userSettings.ts`.
- The startup read drops a founder card that the effective visibility hides from that caller.
- `GET /api/settings/privacy` returns the effective value and `visibility_chosen`, which is false until the first choice.
- `/account` sends only the field that changed, so a toggle never stamps a visibility choice.

**Members who picked "Network only" before D702.** These members cannot be told apart from the default, because both are stored `network` with no stamp. They read Public until they choose again. Only members who chose Hidden ('private' was never the default) or who now choose Members only see a change.

**Scope.** Visibility governs the profile page and the startup page's founder cards only, not the directory, matching or author pages.

**`/account`'s three labels** (`SettingsPage.jsx`, PR 1) now take the canvas's words:

| Before | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Public — anyone with the link | Public — anyone with the address |
| Network only — Axal VC members | Members only — signed-in Axal VC members |
| Private — admins only | Hidden — only you |

"Private — admins only" was never true, because no admin arm reads a private profile. The `/docs` Privacy how-to (`frontend/src/pages/docs/sections/account.js`) says the same three words and adds "Until you choose, your profile is public." This one line is outside the issue's file list. It is changed so the help text does not contradict the page.

### Decision 6 — the four toggles (PR 2 draws them)

- **Show me in the directory** is `show_in_directory`. The member directory, the co-founder browse and the leaderboard read it.
- **Appear in co-founder matching** is `matching_opt_in`, through the existing completeness gate. The gate still answers 400 below 60%. It also governs the best-fit pools and members' introduction proposals, so the row's sub reads "Also member introductions and best-fit matches".
- **Let investors find my venture** ("Through introductions only") is decision 4. It is written through `PUT /api/settings/privacy?project=N` with `investor_findable: boolean`, and `GET` with `?project=N` adds it (true for NULL).
  - `investor_findable` without `project` answers 400 `{ error, field: 'investor_findable' }`.
  - A project the caller cannot `canWrite` answers 403 on `GET` and `PUT`, and nothing is written.
  - A missing or deleted project answers 404.
- **Show my archetype to members** is `archetype-published`. The canvas's "on my profile" is reworded, because no profile page shows the archetype.
- Each toggle writes on change, rolls back on a failure and says so, and needs no Save.

### Decision 7 — You reads its own stores

`GET /api/profile/me` copies nothing:

- **Person.** The name, with "Founder · <`cofounder_profiles.location_city`>", or "Founder" without one. It never reads `users.city` or any other KYC field.
- **Bio.** `users.bio`.
- **Background.**
  - Experience rows read "<title> at <company>", with "<start> – <end>".
  - Education rows read "<degree>, <school>", with the end year.
- **Links.** LinkedIn, Website (`user_profile_ext.website`, else `socials.website`) and GitHub.
- **Preferences.** From the co-founder profile: looking for, commitment (Full-time, Part-time or Exploring), and location ("<city> · remote" when `remote_ok`). Without a co-founder profile they read Not recorded, "No co-founder profile yet".

### Decision 8 — Your venture

**The card** carries the name, `description`, `sector`, and the lifecycle stage. The stage is `buildLifecycleResponse`'s `stage`, with that stage's label from the same response. That function's export is the only change to `progress.ts`.

**The problem** is `problem_statement`.

**The facts** are `founded_year`, `hq`, `employee_count` and `website`.

**`PATCH /api/profile/venture`** writes only these seven fields:

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `description` | ≤280 |
| `sector` | ≤80 |
| `problem_statement` | ≤1000 |
| `founded_year` | an integer from 1900 to this year |
| `hq` | ≤120 |
| `employee_count` | ≤40 |
| `website` | an http(s) URL, ≤300 |

The name is not editable here. When `description`, `sector` or `problem_statement` changes, it enqueues `embed_entity`, as `PUT /api/projects/:id` does.

Module rows call each module's own export and never re-count:

| Row | Value | Empty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interviews | `indexCountsOf(...).interviews_total` | "No interview logged yet" |
| Hypotheses supported | `hypotheses_supported` from Validate's figures, "<value> <unit>" | its reason |
| Waitlist sign-ups | `waitlist_signups`, "<n> of <bar>" when a `metric_targets` bar exists | "No waitlist sign-up yet" |
| MRR | `mrr` from `growFigures` | Not recorded, sub "Grow · no MRR recorded yet" |

**The two MRR stores.** Your venture's MRR is Grow's snapshot (`growFigures`). The preview's MRR is `projects.mrr`, which the investor and partner reads serve. They are different stores and can differ.

PR 2's Edit form is "Edit your venture" with the CTA Save, built on `ui/Lightbox.jsx` with `pageBelow="lg"` (D695, on `main`): below 1024px it is a page, and `VenturePage` draws it as the frame's only content, so its back bar ("‹ Your venture") is the only bar and closes the form. `FormLightbox` is not used: it is bound to the cycle modules' form registry. ### Decision 11 — gates, by role

| Caller | `/me` | venture GET / PATCH | preview | privacy `?project=` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Signed out | 401 | 401 | 401 | 401 |
| The venture's founder | own data | 200 | 200 | 200 |
| Admin | own data | 200 | 200 (sees the venture's founder) | 200 |
| Partner, investor, advisor member, co-founder member, another venture's founder | own data | 403 `{ error }`; nothing written | 403 | 403; nothing written |
| Missing or deleted project | — | 404 | 404 | 404 |
| The founder's venture outside their active company (`X-Company-Id`) | — | 404 | 404 | 404; nothing written |

The project id is bound in every query, and no read or write takes a second id. A founder's venture outside the company they have selected is narrowed with `projectInActiveCompany`, as `progress.ts` `loadProject` narrows it. With no company selected, the venture is in scope. Admins are exempt, because `company_id` is the founder's company. Without this, `frontend/test/company_switcher.test.mjs` would count `founder_profile.ts` as a founder-ownership read that ignores the active company.
