## D5331 — Repurpose a Distribution Calendar plan (#5331)

Repurpose uses the Distribution Ask quote and hold. It takes one dated
planned piece and up to eight distinct other channels in the same venture.
The source material is the recorded title and the member's linked draft
copy when present. Each target yields one post proposal.

Accept or Accept edit saves a manual planned piece on the source's planned
day, with no time or campaign inherited. Its text is saved as a linked
Distribution draft. The Calendar shows that copy to its author. The plan,
linked draft, audit and proposal decision land in one D1 batch. Discard
only decides the proposal.

Repurpose post proposals qualify for Accept all safe. Acceptance never
calls a publisher, sends, schedules, or incurs another AI charge.
