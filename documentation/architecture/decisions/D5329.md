## D5329 — Company editors manage the teams of projects under their company

Project team and agent management consult the project's current company. An
editing seat under D4960 gives its holder roster and agent management rights
without granting a project seat. A move removes that inherited authority
when the project's company changes.

The existing project-owner agent permission remains. Roster and lifecycle
writes keep D5366's company-seat precondition. Support sessions cannot use
the inherited permission. Standalone projects keep the existing manager
rule, and transfer still requires the original ownership permission.

The roster and the agent list may be read by the managers who use them.
General project reads, agent runs and task and proposal decisions retain
their existing scope checks. Settings scope returns the viewer's authority
and the names of company editors.
