### 1. What each item carries

`GET /api/calendar/events` describes each item for the person reading it (`services/calendar/items.ts`, `describeItems`). Only this read does: the .ics feed, the Google and Outlook sync and the one-event push keep the plain event.

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `family` | D1205's five families: `events`, `meetings`, `sessions`, `other`. `deadlines` has no kind yet. |
| `my_role` | `{ code, words }`, in the canvas's words: Organiser, Invited, You give this session, Booked by you, Ticket holder, Host, From Calendly. |
| `can` | `rsvp`, `reschedule`, `cancel`, `copy`, each true or false. |
| `all_day` | True for a date with no time. Every reader writes a time today, so it is false for every item. |
| `href` | The item's own page, or null. |
| `cancel_reason` | Why a cancelled item was cancelled, or null. |
| `deal_name` | An IC meeting's deal, by its venture's name. A deal has no name of its own. |
| `project_name` | A check-in's or an investor meeting's venture. |
| `time_zone` | The zone a row records: an advisor's `timezone` on their bookings. Null everywhere else, because nothing else records one. The page then shows the reader's own zone. |

- **`my_role`.**
  - An IC meeting's organiser is `organizer_user_id`; everyone else on it is Invited.
  - A check-in stores no booker. Its organiser is the one this reader has always named in `organizer_email`: the counterpart, else the founder.
  - On an advisor or partner booking, the side that gives the session reads "You give this session" and the requester reads "Booked by you".
  - An expert session is "Booked by you", a ticket "Ticket holder", a hosted event "Host", an investor meeting "Organiser", a Calendly booking "From Calendly".
- **`can`.**
  - Nothing is offered on a cancelled item. On an item that has ended, only Copy is offered.
  - **RSVP:** an IC meeting you are invited to.
  - **Reschedule and Cancel:** on an IC meeting, its organiser or an admin. On a check-in, either of its two people (its Cancel route's rule, since it stores no booker). No other kind: "The booking page handles it", and StudioOS cannot change a Calendly booking.
  - **Copy:** the six kinds `POST /calendar/push` copies. The route now reads the same list (`COPYABLE_KINDS`).
- **`href`.**

  | Kind | `href` |
  | --- | --- |
  | IC meeting | `/deals/:id` |
  | Check-in | `/projects/:id` |
  | Advisor booking, requester | `/advisors/:uid` |
  | Advisor booking, advisor | `/practice/sessions` |
  | Partner booking, partner | `/partner/office-hours` |
  | Expert session | `/wellbeing` |
  | Ticket | `/my/events/ticket/:id` |
  | Hosted event | `/my/events/hosting/:id` |
  | Investor meeting | `/raise/capital-relationships` |
  | Calendly booking | null: it lives in Calendly |
  | Partner booking, requester | null: no page lists a founder's partner bookings yet |

- **One failed block never blanks the page.** If the deal or venture names cannot be read, they are null on every item, and the items still come back.

### 2. Cancelled and pending rows

- **Cancelled rows come back on the page read.** The four readers that dropped them now return them when asked (`ReadOptions.withCancelled`): IC meetings, check-ins, and advisor and partner bookings. Only `GET /calendar/events` asks.
  - The strict read (`fetchUserEvents`), and so the .ics feed, the sync and the push, never sees one. A cancelled meeting is never copied back into anyone's calendar.
  - Calendly rows already came back with every status, and still do.
- **Each cancelled row carries its status and reason.**
  - An IC meeting gives its `cancel_reason`, the sentence its Cancel took.
  - A booking gives its stored code in words: `slot_cancelled` reads "The host cancelled this time." and `capacity_race` reads "The time filled up before this booking was confirmed." Free text passes as written.
  - `founder_checkins` has no reason column, so a cancelled check-in's reason is null. Adding one is a migration, and this part adds none.
- **Pending stays pending.** A booking written `pending` reads `pending`. The readers never rewrote it; the test holds it.

### 3. Reschedule

`PATCH /api/calendar/ic-meetings/:id` and `PATCH /api/calendar/founder-checkins/:id` take `start_at` and an optional `duration_min`.

- **Who may move it.** An IC meeting: its organiser or an admin, as Cancel. A check-in: either of its two people or an admin, as its Cancel.
- **The rules.**
  - The start must be ahead, as at create.
  - A length outside the kind's range is held to it, as create holds it: 10 to 600 minutes for an IC meeting, 10 to 240 for a check-in.
  - No length keeps the current one.
- **Refused.**
  - Missing or past start: 400.
  - Someone else: 403.
  - Unknown id: 404.
  - A cancelled one: 409 `cancelled`, "A cancelled meeting cannot be moved."
- **What it leaves.** One audit row each: `calendar_ic_rescheduled` and `calendar_checkin_rescheduled`.
- **The copies move.** Each route then calls `onAxalSessionUpdated` (`services/calendar/sync.ts`), deferred and best-effort, with the same event the create sends. This is its first caller. Each attendee's existing Google or Outlook copy is updated through its `calendar_sync_records` row, never duplicated.
  - Create and Reschedule now build that event in one place each (`icSyncEvent`, `checkinSyncEvent`). The two creates call them too, so the event is the same one as before.

### 4. The people and deal pickers

- **`GET /api/calendar/people?for=ic&deal_id=&q=`.** The people on that deal: its lead partner, invited and committed investors, the venture's founders, team and advisors with a sessions grant.
  - Each is kept only when `isDealParty` passes, the rule the IC create applies to each attendee.
  - The deal comes first: no `deal_id` is a 400 `deal_required`.
  - The caller needs the create's own gates: investor or admin; the deal readable; and, for an investor, still on the deal.
- **`GET /api/calendar/people?for=checkin&q=`.**
  - A founder is offered anyone on their ventures, each kept by `isProjectParty`, the create's `related`.
  - Anyone else is offered the founders of the ventures they are on, by that same rule. Only a founder is offered, because the create books only a founder.
- **What each person carries.**
  - `uid`, the name (`display_name`, else `name`), `profile` (their role in words: Admin, Founder, Operating Partner, Investor, Advisor, Member), `headline`, `how` (sentences such as "Lead partner on Acme" and "Advisor to Acme") and `ventures` (`{ id, name }`, so New check-in can fill its Venture).
  - **Never an email and never a user number.** The test sweeps each response for both.
  - At most 20 people, by name.
- **The creates take the uids.** `POST /ic-meetings` takes `attendee_uids` and `POST /founder-checkins` takes `founder_uid` and `counterpart_uid`. Each is resolved to an active account, then the same checks run as before.
  - An unknown attendee uid is 400, an unknown founder uid 404 and an unknown counterpart uid 400.
  - The numeric fields are still taken, so nothing that calls them today breaks.
- **`GET /api/calendar/deals?q=`.** The deals the caller can open (`userMayReadDeal`), by venture name, with `id`, `name` and `status`, at most 20.
  - An investor sees only the deals they are still on, because the IC create refuses the others.
- **Never another studio's people.** A person is offered only through a deal or venture the caller shares with them, by the create's own rule. Someone on no shared venture is never in the list.

### Tests

**`cloudflare-worker/test/calendar_items_d1207.test.ts`** has 7 tests. It runs on the production baseline plus migrations 235 and 277, with Google stubbed.

- **The item fields**, for an organiser and an invitee of one IC meeting, a check-in's founder, a booking's requester and its advisor.
  - A cancelled IC meeting comes back with its reason and no actions.
  - A pending booking stays pending, and a cancelled one gives its reason in words.
  - The strict read and the .ics feed carry no cancelled row.
- **The item rules, case by case:** an admin attendee, an ended meeting, Calendly, and the event pages.
- **Reschedule an IC meeting.**
  - Refused for an invitee, a past start, no start, an unknown id and a cancelled meeting, with nothing copied.
  - Moved by the organiser: the row, the audit row, and a PATCH to the attendee's stored copy with the new start and end, and no new copy.
  - An admin keeps the length.
- **Reschedule a check-in:** refused for an outsider and a cancelled one; moved by the counterpart, length held to 240, the copy moved.
- **The IC people picker:** exactly the deal's people. Not the caller, the removed advisor, a partner with a stray commitment row, or the other deal's investor. Words and filter as described; every gate refused; no email or user number.
- **The check-in picker** for a founder, an advisor (and with no sessions grant) and an outsider, and the creates taking uids, with the create's rules still applying.
- **The deal picker** for an investor, the other deal's investor, an admin, a founder, an investor who passed and an outsider.
