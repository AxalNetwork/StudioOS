- **Status:** built by S08 for #2902, part 3 of #2451 (D1048's table), 2026-10-26.
- **Basis:** `main` at `c85d3d2172`. No migration.
- **Owner decisions** (#2451, answered through S21):
  - **Question 7, A.** An upgrade applies now, and the prorated difference is charged today. A downgrade applies at the end of the paid period, and nothing is charged.
  - **Question 8, C.** A new card needs no step-up. A saved card, and any change to a plan already paid for, needs a recent authenticator code and a step-up.
- **Code:**

### 1. Who buys what

| Role | Buys |
|---|---|
| founder, investor | their own family's plans |
| advisor, partner | their own Pro plan |
| admin | none: `staff_no_plan` |
| anyone else | none yet: `choose_profile` |

A plan of another family is `other_profile`, an unknown key `unknown_plan`, a plan the catalogue does not sell `not_sellable`, and a token pack `token_pack_checkout_only` (D755 §9).

**The price is Stripe's.** It is read from the catalogue mirror by the plan's own metadata (`planCatalogue.ts` `matches`): an active recurring price, every one month or one year. The client sends a plan key and an interval, never an amount or a price id.

### 2. `POST /api/billing/checkout/quote`

Read only. It returns the plan, the interval, the Stripe price and currency, the yearly saving, the AI allowance, the storage and what is included.

- **A new subscriber** (`mode: 'new'`): the promo discount through the existing promo rules, VAT from Stripe's upcoming invoice when Stripe Tax is on and a customer exists, `total_due_today`, and `step_up_required: false`.
- **A change** (`mode: 'change'`):
  - `direction`: `upgrade` or `downgrade`, by what the two prices cost a month;
  - `proration_amount`: the proration lines of Stripe's upcoming invoice for an upgrade, and 0 for a downgrade;
  - `change_at`: `now`, or the end of the paid period;
  - `discount: null` with its reason, because a change takes no code;
  - `step_up_required: true`.
- The plan already held is `already_on_plan`.
- Any field that cannot be read is null with a reason, never 0.

### 3. `POST /api/billing/checkout/start`

- **A nonce** of 8 to 64 characters is required (`nonce_required`). It goes into the idempotency key, so a retry of one form never makes two subscriptions.
- **A new subscription** is created `incomplete`, with the plan's metadata (`planMetaParams`) and `metadata[plan_key]`, and returns the `client_secret` for the in-page form. An investor plan is on the investor customer.
- **A saved card** (`pm_…`) needs the step-up, and must belong to the caller's customer. Otherwise it is 404, never a hint that the card exists.
- **A change** sits behind `requireFactor('totp')` and `requireStepUp`:
  - **An upgrade** updates the subscription item now, with `always_invoice` and `pending_if_incomplete`. It returns `paid`, or a `client_secret` when the card needs confirming.
  - **A downgrade** goes through a subscription schedule: the current phase runs to the period end, then the new price with `proration_behavior: none` and the new plan's metadata. It returns `scheduled` and `change_at`.
- **When D1 records a plan that Stripe does not confirm**, the start refuses 409 `subscription_exists`, never a second subscription.
- **The record.** `activity_logs` gets `checkout_started`, `plan_changed` and `promo_applied`, against the Stripe subscription. Their details hold the plan key, the interval, the price, the mode, the direction, the plan before and when the change applies. They hold no email and no card.

### 4. `GET /api/billing/checkout/status?ref=sub_…`

- **Only the caller's own.** The ref must be a subscription that the caller's own `checkout_started` or `plan_changed` row names. Another member's ref, or an unrelated one, is 404. A malformed ref is 400. An unreadable store is 503, which is never the same as a failed payment.
- **The states:**
  - `activating`;
  - `active`: the plan columns hold the plan, and for a change `member_subscription_amounts.price_id` is the new price, so an interval change is not read as done early;
  - `failed`;
  - `scheduled`: a downgrade waiting for the period end. This is an addition to D1048's three states.
- **When active,** `result` gives the plan, its family, the interval, when it took effect, what is included, the AI allowance and the storage. Anything not recorded says "Not recorded: …".
- **`changes`** gives the plan before and after. When the plan before was not recorded, it says so (`before: null` with a reason).
