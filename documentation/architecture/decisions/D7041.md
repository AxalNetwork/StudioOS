### What the canvas decides

- "Run competitor analysis →" on a profile opens setup with that company as the subject. A subject card (name, website, field source, Not verified) stays on setup and on results.
- Market question, startup and known competitors are separate groups. The website is never written into Known competitors, and a loaded startup list never switches mode or replaces the subject.
- The subject card has its own states: Not verified, no website (Not recorded), Loading, and Unavailable with Retry. A run from Saved analyses starts with an editable Market field.
- Supported results cite a source for each finding. Interpretation is kept apart, and Suggested research questions have their own block. The subject is listed as excluded, and each Direct or Adjacent gives its reason and sources.
- Partial results mark a finding with no source as "No source in this run". Failed source reads read Unavailable with Retry.
- A run with 0 sources and 0 candidates is Insufficient evidence: no gaps, classifications or wedge. Add a source is the primary action, and Revise the search is offered.
- No candidates (the search ran) is shown apart from Sources Unavailable (the reads failed). Run failed, saved list Unavailable, Refused (403) and Not found / market required are drawn.
- The export preview carries the subject and the evidence status. Phone 390, tablet 768 and dark are drawn.
