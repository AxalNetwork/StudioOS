## D1415 — A company as a legal entity, and slugs per scope

- **Status:** part 1 of #3697 (Portfolio, server side), built by S17 from S1's specification on the issue. Parts 2 to 4 follow under the same decision.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1136, which made `company_profiles` the member-editable company record;
  - D1137, which archives companies and moves projects;
  - D1140, which reserves `settings` as a path segment;
  - D579, the per-project EIN;
  - D376, the staff-only fund fabric (`entities`).
- **Migration:** `685_company_legal_record_and_slugs.sql`.

### The question

A company in Axal held a name, a stage and a profile, but no legal record. The legal data that does exist lives where a company member cannot use it:
- `entities` is the fund fabric. Its `entity_type` CHECK has no room for an LLC or a C-Corp, and only staff write it.
- `company_eins` is keyed to a project.

A founder therefore had nowhere to record their company's legal name, form, jurisdiction, registration number, incorporation date, officers, addresses or tax ID.

### What was decided

**The legal record lives on `company_profiles`.**
- New columns: `legal_name`, `legal_form` (`c_corp`, `s_corp`, `llc`, `lp`, `ltd`, `plc`, `gmbh`, `sas`, `bv`, `other`), `jurisdiction`, `registration_number` and `incorporation_date`.
- `jurisdiction` is an ISO 3166 code, or a country and subdivision code (`GB`, `US-DE`), stored upper-case.
- A field not recorded is NULL and reads as not recorded.

**The tax ID is a company record of its own, `company_tax_ids`** (owner question A, answer a).
- It is sealed with `services/columnCipher.ts` under the AAD `company_tax_ids:tax_id_enc:<company_id>`, so a ciphertext copied onto another company's row does not open.
- Kinds are `ein` (`NN-NNNNNNN`), `vat` (country prefix and 2 to 13 characters) and `other`.
- Only the last four characters are readable without decryption.
- The number goes only to an editor who is not a platform admin and asks for it (`?full=1`), the rule D579 set for the project EIN.
- A refusal is a code and never echoes what was sent.
- An admin write is audited with the company and the kind, never the number.
- The project EIN stays on the project. Nothing is copied or moved.

**Officers and directors are `company_officers`.**
- Each row holds a name, `director` or `officer`, a title and the dates.
- `user_id` is optional and must name a member of the company.
- A person who leaves office is **ceased**, never deleted. A ceased record is kept as it was: no further edits, and a second cease answers 409.

**Locations are `company_locations`.**
- The **registered address is the open `registered` location**, not a set of columns, so it has one source.
- A partial unique index allows one open registered address per company. The route checks first, and the index decides a race. Either way the answer is 409 `registered_exists`.
- A location is **closed**, never deleted. Closing the registered address frees the slot.

**Slugs are unique per scope.**
- A company slug is unique among companies.
- A project slug is unique within its company, or among standalone projects (two partial unique indexes on `projects`).
- Shape: 1 to 50 lower-case letters, digits and hyphens, starting and ending with a letter or digit.
- Reserved: `settings`, `new`, `c`, `p`, `projects`, `funds`, `engagements`, `invitations`.
- A rename writes the old slug to `slug_history` **in the same batch**, so a rename never lands without its history.
- A retired slug stays reserved for its target for ever and resolves to it (owner question C, answer a). Its own company may take it back.
- Existing rows keep `slug = NULL` and are addressed by uid or id. Nothing is backfilled.

**Who.** These are `company.ts`'s rules, restated in `routes/company_legal.ts`:
- **Member:** holds a link to the company, or is a platform admin.
- **Editor:** a member whose role is Owner, Admin or Founder, a primary admin, or a platform admin.
- A caller who is not a member reads 404, the same answer as an unknown company.
- A member who is not an editor gets 403 on every write.
- An archived company is read and takes no write (409 `archived`).

### Part 1 routes (`routes/company_legal.ts`, mounted at `/api` before `company.ts`)

| Route | Who | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /company/:uid/legal` | member | the five fields, `slug`, `path`, `registered_address`, `tax_id`, `officers`, `locations` (each a block: `{ available, value }` or `{ available: false, reason }`), `archived`, `can_edit` |
| `PUT /company/:uid/legal` | editor | partial; `null` or blank clears. 400 `legal_form_invalid`, `jurisdiction_invalid`, `incorporation_date_invalid` (a real date, YYYY-MM-DD), `text_too_long` (over 200), `field_invalid` |
| `GET /company/:uid/tax-id` | member | `{ recorded, kind, last4, masked, recorded_at, can_edit }`, plus `tax_id` for an editor's `?full=1` |
| `PUT /company/:uid/tax-id` | editor or admin | `{ kind, tax_id }`. 400 `tax_id_kind_invalid`, `tax_id_invalid` |
| `GET` / `POST /company/:uid/officers`, `PATCH …/:officerUid`, `POST …/:officerUid/cease` | read: member; write: editor | 400 `name_required`, `title_required`, `office_invalid`, `appointed_on_invalid`, `ceased_on_invalid`, `ceased_on_before_appointed`, `user_not_member`; 409 `already_ceased` |
| `GET` / `POST /company/:uid/locations`, `PATCH …/:locUid`, `POST …/:locUid/close` | read: member; write: editor | 400 `kind_invalid`, `country_invalid`, `closed_on_invalid`; 409 `registered_exists`, `already_closed` |
| `PUT /company/:uid/slug` | editor | `{ slug, path, previous_slug }`. 400 `slug_invalid`, `slug_reserved`; 409 `slug_taken` |

`jurisdiction_invalid` was added to the specification's codes, because a jurisdiction is stored as a code and a free-text value such as "Delaware" would otherwise be stored as given.

The client is `frontend/src/lib/api/companyLegal.js` (`companyLegalApi`), re-exported from `api.js` (D527). Screens wait for the canvas (#3696).

### Part 3 — incorporation completion links or creates the company (#3855)

- **Status:** built by S17, 2026-10-31. The issue was filed for S23; the owner moved it to S17. The owner's model is #3697's comment 6542845870: filing links or creates the company.
- **Basis:** #3861's branch (D1593, migration 749), over `origin/main` at `ce9e885b33`. There is no new migration: 685 and 749 hold every column.

**One batch.** `markFiled` (`services/incorporationFiling.ts`) runs D601's `SET_FILED` first, then the statements `filingCompanyStatements` (`services/incorporationCompany.ts`) returns, in one `DB.batch`.
- Every company statement is guarded by "the order is `filed` and `incorporations.company_id IS NULL`", and the batch ends by setting `company_id`.
- A second call, or two admins at once, therefore writes nothing past the first, and a repeat still answers 409. A failed batch leaves the order `packet_ready` with no company.
- The new company is named by a uid chosen before the batch, so every statement after its INSERT reaches it by `uid` without a row id.
- Nothing is deleted, and no value already set is overwritten.

**The venture has no company:**
- A company is created with `company_name` and `legal_name` from the order, `jurisdiction` and `legal_form` from the map below, `incorporation_date = filed_on`, and `registered_agent_name` from the order (D1593).
- Its slug is made from the name with `checkSlug` and `slugHolder`: the name itself, then `-2`, `-3` and so on, up to 20 tries, else NULL. #3858's `nextFreeSlug` had not merged.
- The venture's owner account (`ownerUserId`, as the move route resolves it) becomes its primary admin. The link row and its `join` event are written as `POST /company/create` writes them; the actor is the admin who recorded the filing.
- The venture moves in through `projectMoveStatements` with its `project_move` event.
- The order's `registered_agent_address` becomes the open `registered` location: `line1` is that text and `country` is the jurisdiction's country.
- `incorporations.company_id` is set.

**The venture is already in a company:**
- Only its NULL fields are filled, with `COALESCE` in one UPDATE: `legal_name`, `legal_form`, `jurisdiction`, `incorporation_date` and `registered_agent_name`.
- The registered location is added only when none is open.
- The order is linked.
- An archived company is linked only: `reason: 'archived'`, nothing filled.

**The venture has no owner account:** the filing stands, and no company is written (`company: { created: false, reason: 'no_owner_account' }`).

**The map:**

| Jurisdiction id | `jurisdiction` | `legal_form` |
|---|---|---|
| `us_de_ccorp` | `US-DE` | `c_corp` |
| `us_wy_ccorp` | `US-WY` | `c_corp` |
| `us_de_llc` | `US-DE` | `llc` |
| `uk_ltd` | `GB` | `ltd` |
| `sg_pte` | `SG` | `ltd` |
| `ee_oy` | `EE` | `ltd` |

An id the map does not hold leaves both NULL.

**One writer of `projects.company_id`.** `services/projectMove.ts` holds `projectMoveStatements(env, move)`, which returns the UPDATE and its `project_move` event, bound and not run. It has two forms:
- a company that exists now (or null), used by `POST /api/projects/:id/move`;
- a company created earlier in the same filing batch, named by uid and carrying the filing's guard.

`ownerUserId` moved there too. #3850 (agent engine E6) adds its transfer statements to this helper.

**The answers:**
- `POST /api/admin/incorporations/:id/filed` returns `company`: either `{ uid, created, filled: [field names] }` (with `reason: 'archived'` when linked only) or `{ created: false, reason }`.
- `logAdminAction` records `company_uid` only: no name and no address.
- `GET /api/legal/incorporate/status` and each row of `GET /api/legal/incorporate/orders` carry `company_uid`. It comes from `incorporations.company_id`, never from the venture's company now, so a venture moved later keeps showing the order's company. It is null until the order is linked.
