## D4860 part 4 — Detected incidents on the overview, and one severity rule (#4860)

Part of #4860: item 1 of S1's notes of 2026-11-09 (23:31Z, 23:50Z). Detection itself is D4854's (`cloudflare-worker/src/services/platformHealth.ts`). This part reads it, and changes one Worker constant (Q4, below).

**What the overview reads.** `health` on `GET /api/admin/platform/summary`, through `detectedIncidents` in `platformOverviewModel.js`:
- Each detected incident becomes a **Needs attention** item at the Worker's severity. The item carries the Worker's console link: its `remediation.surface`, or Monitoring for a kind with none (jobs, dead letters). It also carries its runbook in `PLATFORM_OBSERVABILITY.md` §7 and a "Detected by the platform" line.
- A new **Incidents** block, after the core systems, counts the detected incidents by severity and lists them. Published status-page incidents sit beside them, apart, as entered by hand. It also lists the signals the Worker could not judge (`health.unknown`), and keeps what it does not detect (`health.not_detected`) behind "What is not detected".
- The Infrastructure block's **Detected incidents** row is the count, linked to that block.
- **A summary with no `health`** (a Worker older than D4854) reads **Not recorded** in both places, never 0.

**One severity rule: the Worker's.** The model had its own severities, and they disagreed with the Worker's. Both now follow the Worker:
- The model's thresholds are the Worker's constants, and `platform_detected_d4860.test.mjs` holds them equal:
  - `WEBHOOK_REFUSED_RUN` 3 and `WEBHOOK_CRITICAL_RUN` 10, where Critical had been 3 refusals;
  - `DLQ_HIGH` 100, where a backlog of 100 had been Critical and any backlog High.
- **A backlog** is now Medium, and High from 100. It is Degraded, never Critical.
- **A refused run** is High and Degraded from 3 in a row, and Critical from 10. A single refusal short of a run is Medium.
- **A failed job** is Medium and a late one High. Unreadable telemetry is High. A branch failing its live read is Medium and Degraded. Before, these were High, Medium, Medium and Critical.
- **Where the Worker detected an incident**, that incident replaces the page's own items for the signal. The signal reads Critical only when an incident is critical; otherwise it reads at most Degraded. A High or Medium incident lowers a signal the page read as Healthy. So one signal never reads Critical in the strip and High in the list.
- **Where the Worker detected nothing**, the page's own observation stays, on the same scale. The page reads things the Worker does not, such as the traffic read and a branch's live answer.
- **A detected kind with no overview signal** (the meeting bot's starts) gets a signal of its own.
- **Low items never lower health**, as the Worker's `healthStateOf` says. That covers webhook silence, a branch nobody checked, and configuration.

**Q4 answered B (owner, 2026-11-12): one silence threshold, 24 h.** The overview already called a silent webhook Stale after 24 h (owner answer C). The Worker's `WEBHOOK_SILENCE_HOURS` in `platformHealth.ts` goes from 72 to 24, so its Low silence incident appears at the same moment. Runbook §7.2 names the value. `platform_health_4854.test.ts` holds the boundary: 30 h silent is noted, 20 h is not. `platform_detected_d4860.test.mjs` holds the overview's constant equal to the Worker's. This is the one Worker change in this part.
