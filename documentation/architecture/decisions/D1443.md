### 2. Who Ask serves

`askServes` (`ui/askPanelModel.js`) returns true only when all of these hold:
- the page has a rail page key;
- no support session is active (`lib/supportSession.js`);
- the viewer is not an Explorer;
- the viewer's licence is the page's own (the rail's `role`). Staff ask on the staff consoles only.

The last rule is S1's note on #3706 (2026-11-01): an HQ admin previewing a founder's or an investor's page is reading someone else's record, so gets no Ask. The Worker refuses a support session and an Explorer too, in D1442.

### 3. Where it sits

`WorkerRail` mounts the panel; no host changes.
- **1024px and up:** inside the rail's own `<aside>`, as `.fwr-ask-dock` at the rail's left edge (`right: 100%`). The dock spans the column from under the header to the footer. The panel is sticky inside it, at most the window's height under the header and never taller than the column. So it passes neither the header nor the footer, and the page stays usable: it is `role="complementary"`, not modal. It overlays the content column only, never the header, the sidebar, the rail or the footer.
- **640–1023px:** portalled over the content, under the header.
- **Below 640px:** a bottom sheet at 92% with a scrim, `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, 44px controls.
- Escape and × close it, as do a route change and a D1317 detail sheet opening. Opening Ask closes D698's phone panel and the reverse, so one Eadwyn surface shows at a time.
- **Not yet:** the content narrowing beside the panel from 1440px (AK12). About 28 hosts draw their own rail column, and each must read the wider track. That is #3992.

### 4. What it draws (`ui/AskPanel.jsx`)

| Part | Built from |
|---|---|
| Head | the mark, the page's name, "Eadwyn · <section>", × |
| Suggested for this page | the page's options from `GET /api/ai/rail?page=` (already read by the rail). A **run chip** runs in the panel on `POST /api/ai/rail/run` and comes back as proposal cards: today `sessions:block` (D1596). `studio:layout` (D1420) lives in Studio's own chat (AK9). Every other option is pressed on the page itself, so its chip puts its words in the composer as a typed question: it never claims to run the page's control. |
| The conversation | D652's conversation for the page (`/api/eadwyn/conversations?workspace=<page key>`), and a new one on the first question |
| Proposal cards | one `ai_rail_proposals` row each: eyebrow, text, source, and Accept, Edit (to the page it lives on) and Discard; "Accept all (n)" from two open cards, capped at 50. The run's line is "Charged $x of $y held", "Not charged", or "Charge not recorded", never "$0.00" for an unknown charge |
| States | nothing to propose, running, an error with Try again, out of AI tokens (Add tokens), AI paused, AI off on this page, each in the Worker's words (402 `budget_*`/`spend_limit`, 403 `ai_paused`/`assist_off`) |
| Composer | "Ask about <page>…", the cost line, Send, and "Nothing is written until you accept." |
