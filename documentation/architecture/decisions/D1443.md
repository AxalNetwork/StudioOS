### 2. Who Ask serves

`askServes` (`ui/askPanelModel.js`) returns true only when all of these hold:
- the page has a rail page key;
- no support session is active (`lib/supportSession.js`);
- the viewer is not an Explorer;
- the viewer's licence is the page's own (the rail's `role`). Staff ask on the staff consoles only.

The last rule is S1's note on #3706 (2026-11-01): an HQ admin previewing a founder's or an investor's page is reading someone else's record, so gets no Ask. The Worker refuses a support session and an Explorer too, in D1442.

### 3. Where it sits

`WorkerRail` mounts the panel; no host changes.
- **1024px and up:** inside the rail's own `<aside>`, as `.fwr-ask-dock` at the rail's left edge (`right: 100%`). The dock spans the column from under the header to the footer. The panel is sticky inside it, at most the window's height under the header and never taller than the column. So it passes neither the header nor the footer, and the page stays usable: it is `role="complementary"`, not modal. It overlays the content column only, never the header, the sidebar, the rail or the footer.
- **640–1023px:** portalled over the content, under the header.
- **Below 640px:** a bottom sheet at 92% with a scrim, `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, 44px controls.
- Escape and × close it, as do a route change and a D1317 detail sheet opening. Opening Ask closes D698's phone panel and the reverse, so one Eadwyn surface shows at a time.
- **Not yet:** the content narrowing beside the panel from 1440px (AK12). About 28 hosts draw their own rail column, and each must read the wider track. That is #3992.

### 8. Part 4: the Fill sheets move into the panel (#3992, S14, 2026-11-01)

There is no `Sheet` left on either Fill surface, so the owner's decision 3 holds: there is no separate proposals container.

- **An intent.** The store's `ask.intent` is what the panel shows above the conversation while a page's own Eadwyn task is open in it.
  - `openAskIntent({ label, onClose })` opens the panel holding it.
  - `endAskIntent(id)` ends it, and the panel stays open.
  - The panel closing (×, Escape, a route change, the scrim, a detail sheet) ends the intent and calls its `onClose`.
  - There is one intent at a time; a second replaces the first, which is told it closed.
- **`ui/AskIntent.jsx`** draws the task: eyebrow, title, a Close, the body and the footer line.
  - It portals into the slot `AskPanel` hands over (`setAskSlot`).
  - Its host keeps the state and the callbacks, so Accept, Edit and Discard, and Accept all capped at 50, behave as they did.
  - With no panel serving the viewer, the same labelled region is drawn in place.
  - Focus keeps D1317's contract: into the region on open, back to the opener on close.
- **`ui/FillSheet.jsx`**, Sources and Preview, is the panel's form intent.
  - Accept places values in the open form (`onPlace`), and nothing is written until the form's Save.
  - `useScopedFill.jsx`'s six records open the same intent, because they render `FillSheet`.
- **`pages/cycles/FillRowsPanel.jsx`**: the module's row proposals are the panel's row cards.
  - `FillModule`'s trigger is the shared Ask (`AskButtonView`) with its "N waiting". A failed read draws no count.
  - Discard all still asks first, in its own in-page dialog.
- **The open form keeps the rail live** (S1's B = a).
  - `Lightbox` takes `keep`, selectors left un-inert behind the dialog. `FormLightbox` passes `FORM_KEEP = ['[data-sheet-keep]']`.
  - That is the rail's own D1317 mark, now also on the Ask layer, so Ask opens beside the open form.
