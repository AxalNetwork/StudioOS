### 4. Who sees what

- Unchanged: an Admin and a Super Admin read the same queue and decide the same way (`requireAdmin`; TOTP step-up and branch-not-suspended on decisions).
- Under owner question 4's recommended answer, part 2 sends a PEP disclosure or an unreviewed possible sanctions match to HQ. There a Super Admin decides, under `requireSuperAdmin`.
- Licensed studios' KYB, the renewal periods and the audit export are the Super Admin's (KYC17).

### 5. Owner decisions

#2384's five questions take their recommended answers until the owner says otherwise:
1. **Who needs KYC.** Unchanged: investors, plus partners on a capital partnership. Widening it is the owner's call, and no part does it.
2. **Which businesses need KYB:** founders' companies, investing entities, partner firms, imported partners and licensed studios. HQ's own entities and funds stay out.
3. **How long a check lasts.** 24 months for KYC and KYB, 12 for accreditation. HQ may change them, and new periods apply only to new approvals (part 4).
4. **PEP and possible sanctions matches** go to a Super Admin.
5. **Accreditation** stays out of this page for now.
