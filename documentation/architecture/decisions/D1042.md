## D1042 — Approvals part 4: the product tester store and HQ's decisions

- **Status:** built by S17, 2026-10-25 (#2890, part 4 of Approvals, #2390; #2377's task 3).
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `39f4afc2b7`.
- **Canvas:** the Approvals canvas's tester decision sheet (APR5) gives the words each decision shows; the page itself is a later part.
- **Migration:** 576 (`576_product_testers.sql`). **Worker:** `routes/admin_product_testers.ts` at `/api/admin/product-testers`, with its rules in `services/productTesters.ts`.

### 1. The decision

A member who applies to test products is one `product_testers` row, whatever their profile. Testing programs (migration 516) recruit from the active ones. Only HQ changes a row's status today. The member's apply route waits on #2318's intake decision and is not built here.

### 2. The store (migration 576)

- **`product_testers`:** one row per member (`user_id` unique).
  - **Status:** `applied`, `active`, `paused`, `declined` or `removed`. A row that is not `applied` must carry `decided_at`.
  - **Recorded:** the decision's time and who made it (`decided_at`, `decided_by`).
  - **What the member gave:** `answers`, `availability`, and the email consent with the version of the sentence they agreed to (`consent_version` is required when `consent_email_at` is set, as 516's applications do).
  - **`note` and `reason`:** `note` is HQ's and the member never sees it. `reason` is the sentence the member is told with a decline, a pause or a removal. Approve and resume clear it.
- **`product_tester_events`:** one row per change, with who, what, the statuses before and after, the note and the reason. Two triggers make it append-only.

### 3. The routes, all `requireAdmin`

Admin and Super Admin can both use them. The HQ freeze refuses the writes, as `requireAdmin` does everywhere.

| Route | Does |
|---|---|
| `GET /?status=&page=&page_size=` | The testers, one status or all, oldest application first, 25 a page (100 at most). Each row has the member's name, email, role, `email_verified` and `kyc_status`. The response counts every status and gives `matching` for the filter. |
| `GET /:id` | One tester, the testing programs they applied to (product, program status, their application's status and day), and their history. |
| `POST /:id/approve` `{ note? }` | applied → active |
| `POST /:id/decline` `{ reason, note? }` | applied → declined. The reason must be 10 to 500 characters. They may apply again 90 days after the decision (`reapply_after`). |
| `POST /:id/pause` `{ reason, note? }` | active → paused. A reason is required. |
| `POST /:id/resume` `{ note? }` | paused → active |
| `POST /:id/remove` `{ reason, note? }` | active or paused → removed. A reason is required. |

**Resume** is not in the issue. A paused tester needed a way back, and the canvas's pause words ("no new programs; current ones carry on") imply one.

**Each decision tells the member** by email and in their inbox. The notification has no category, so it is critical and skips the digest and quiet hours. It links to `/studio`. Each message follows the canvas's line for that decision:
- **Approve:** testing programs can pick them.
- **Decline:** the reason, and the day they can apply again.
- **Pause:** no new program picks them, and their current programs carry on.
- **Remove:** they leave the pool, and their open program places end.

**Refusals, each with a `code`:**
- **`email_unverified` (409):** "[Name]’s email is not verified, so they cannot be told. Ask them to verify it first."
- **`admins_cannot_test` (409):** an admin or Super Admin account. Neither role can be a tester.
- **`wrong_status` (409):** a decision from a status it does not apply to.
- **`reason_required` (400):** the reason is missing or too short.
- **`tester_changed` (409):** another decision landed between the read and the write. The UPDATE names the status it read. The event row is written in the same batch only when that UPDATE stamped this decision, so a raced decision writes nothing and tells no one.
- **`product_testers_unreadable` (503):** a D1 error. It is never shown as an empty list.

### 5. Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/product_testers_d1042.test.ts` has 12 tests. They run on the baseline plus every migration above the cutoff, so migration 576 runs as it ships.

**What they check:**
- the list's order, member fields, totals, filter, pages and page cap;
- one tester's programs and history;
- a member is refused;
- each transition: its event, the inbox message (the note never in it), and a second decision refused;
- the decline reason's minimum and the reapply day;
- resume clearing the pause reason;
- the unverified email and the admin refusals, each changing nothing;
- a raced decision writing no event and telling no one;
- the append-only triggers and the table's checks.

**Mutation checks.** Each of these twelve mutations was caught:
- the race guard dropped from the UPDATE;
- the event written whatever the UPDATE did;
- the `changes` check dropped;
- the unverified email check dropped;
- the admin check dropped;
- the decline minimum set to 1;
- 30 days in place of 90;
- resume keeping the pause reason;
- newest first;
- remove refused from paused;
- the totals counted on one status;
- the note leaked into the member's message.
