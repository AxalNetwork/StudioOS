## D5938 — Negotiations drawn to Pipeline v2: a board of the recorded stages, a terms sheet and a phone stage selector (#5938)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-14. Part of #4838 (D4838, the `Pipeline v2` canvas). Builds boards PP05 (A · Board), PP05b (B · Terms sheet) and PP05m (Phone · stage selector) of `design/incoming/Pipeline v2 · Boards.dc.html`, at `/pipeline/negotiations`.
- **Its own file, not `D4838-p<k>.md`.** #5938 is its own issue filed under the #4838 umbrella, as #5996 is under #4837, so it records its decision under its own number. `D4838.md` is not edited.
- **No Worker change, no migration.** The zone reads and writes what it did: `GET /partner/pipeline/negotiations`, `PUT /partner/pipeline/negotiations/:quoteId`, `POST /partner/pipeline/negotiations/:quoteId/terms`, `PATCH` and `DELETE /partner/pipeline/negotiation-terms/:id`, and the zone-draft routes under `/research/drafts` for the counter.

### What is built

- **The board (PP05).** Four lanes, the recorded stages `scoping`, `terms`, `legal`, `ready_to_sign`; each header is the stage name and its count. `closed` is a stage, not a lane. No WIP limit is drawn: none is stored (D4838, Backend required), and the zone action stays `unbuilt`. The chips narrow the lanes as before (`visible`).
- **Each card answers five questions.** Who (`founder_name`, else the need's title, else "Client not recorded"); how much (`quotes.price`, else "Amount not recorded"); what is still open (the open question, else "No blocker named", plus the count of open terms); who has the ball ("● Us" / "○ Client", a shape and a word); how long it has waited (`days_stalled`, days since the last recorded move, computed by the Worker from `last_moved_at`). A negotiation with no recorded move reads Not recorded, "no move logged", never 0. Seven days or more adds "· stalled" in words.
- **Moving a card is two buttons.** "← <previous stage>" and "<next stage> →", named "Move <who> back to …" / "on to …". Each writes `PUT /negotiations/:quoteId` with `{ stage }` only, so the ball and open question are kept and the Worker's clock advances (a stage change is a move). Focus follows the card to its new lane. Nothing is dragged.
- **The terms sheet (PP05b).** "Open terms" opens one negotiation in the shared detail sheet (`ui/Sheet.jsx`, D1317) at 640px: eyebrow "Negotiation · Terms", the who and amount, the ball, the wait and the open question. Its table is the canvas's: Term, We proposed, They proposed, Current position. A clause whose two recorded positions differ (ignoring case and spacing) is marked "≠" with screen-reader text "Sides differ:" and bold, not only colour; a clause with one side unrecorded is not marked, and its cell says Not recorded. Current position is the landing and its state ("$6,000 · agreed"), or the state alone ("Open"). The sheet keeps the zone's edits: a term's fields, Add a term, Remove, and stage, ball, open question, Save and Log a move. On a phone the sheet is full screen and the table becomes one block per term.
- **Phone (PP05m).** Below 1024px the lanes give way to a radio group of the four stages with their counts ("Scoping 1 … Ready 0", 44px) and the chosen stage's cards stacked below. Arrow keys, Home and End move the choice. The first stage with a card is chosen first.
- **Off the board.** Quotes nobody is tracking and negotiations that are closed or whose quote is decided are listed under "Not on the board", each with Start tracking or Open terms, so no record the zone used to show is lost.

### The Eadwyn counter: what exists today

The issue left this unchecked. **A counter draft exists in the Worker and the zone today.** `routes/research.ts` allow-lists the surface `pipeline/negotiations`: `POST /research/drafts` gathers up to 25 open negotiations of the caller's firm, oldest recorded move first, with every clause's three positions, and asks for one counter built only from those clauses. The draft is stored in `research_zone_drafts`, one row per run, for the caller only. Before this build the zone drew it with `ZoneDraft` as "Proposal · counter drafted" with Save counter draft.

What it is not: it is **not scoped to one negotiation** (the gather ignores `scope_key`), it is **free text, not terms**, and **Accept stamps `accepted_at` on the draft and writes nothing else** (D779 part 3 holds that Accept writes only the draft).

So the board draws the counter as PP05b names it, "Eadwyn counter proposal" with a "Not sent" status, Accept counter draft, Edit terms and Discard, **only while a draft exists**. With no draft the band offers "Draft the counter" and draws no "Not sent" and no accept. Nothing says Send counter. `ZoneDraft` gains two optional props for this, `editLabel` (default "Edit first") and `status` (drawn only beside a shown draft); every other mount is unchanged.
