### What the verification found

Every acceptance criterion had Worker code and a mutation-guarded test on main
(`licence_fee_receivables_5036`). The executive summary drew the ledger per currency (D5082). Two gaps remained on
screen:

1. **The Revenue streams table contradicted the summary.** Its licence row still drew Invoiced and Collected as Not
   recorded, with coverage "Run-rate only", whatever `licence_invoices` said. The component's header still said they
   stay Not recorded "until a ledger backs them". D5082 left `RevenuePage.jsx` and its parts alone while #5132 and
   #5166 were open, and both have since merged.
2. **No screen named an overdue invoice.** The Worker lists each overdue invoice and the cockpit counts them. But the
   licence's Annual fee invoices tab drew an overdue invoice as plain "Issued".

### Decision

#### The streams table

The licence row reads `licence_invoices.by_currency`, the invoices issued in the selected period, for the row's own
currency only.

- **Without the field:** Not recorded, and "Run-rate only", as before.
- **Unreadable ledger:** Unavailable, never 0.
- **Readable ledger, no invoice in this currency:** a measured zero in that currency.
- **Coverage once the ledger is read:** "Run-rate and invoices", and its Why opens the ledger's explanation.
- **A currency with invoices in the period but no active licence priced in it:** its own row, with No active
  licence fee as the run-rate. It is never folded into another currency.
- **No total row.** Nothing is summed across currencies.

The run-rate column keeps its "Contracted run-rate" heading.

#### The overdue mark

An invoice is drawn as Overdue when all three hold:

- it is not void;
- a balance is left;
- its due date is before today, as a UTC day.

This is the Worker's own `isOverdue` rule. The tab adds "N days past due" under the mark. The component takes
`today` as a prop, defaulting to the UTC day now, so tests pass a fixed day.

### Not done here

- The cockpit still does not draw the Worker's per-invoice overdue list (`licence_invoices.overdue`). The licence tab
  names each overdue invoice, and the cockpit counts them. Placing that list in the cockpit is a layout choice for
  the Revenue v2 canvas.
- `RevenuePage.jsx` still passes the rail an `unavailable` row that says "Invoiced, collected, outstanding, overdue:
  No ledger reconciles…". The rail no longer draws `unavailable` (`RETIRED_RAIL_PROPS`), so nobody sees it. Removing
  it is cleanup.
