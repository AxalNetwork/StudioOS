### 1. HQ presses it, from the licence's start day

The owner's answer says "at licence start". It does not say whether the invoice is raised on its own at activation or
pressed by HQ. Annual invoices are pressed by HQ (D802), so this one is too:
`POST /api/admin/licences/:uid/fee-invoices` with `{ "kind": "initial" }`. It takes the same write bar as the annual
invoice. It is available from the licence's `starts_on`. Before that day it is refused with `licence_not_started`,
and the refusal names the date. Nothing issues it on its own: no activation hook, no cron, no backfill.

**Licences that started before this shipped get nothing automatically.** HQ issues their initial invoice by hand if
one is owed. A licence whose initial fee was settled outside StudioOS is left alone.

The route stays the existing POST so that #4707's pinned route table does not change. A POST with no body, or with
`"kind": "annual"`, is the annual invoice, unchanged.

### 2. What it bills

The figures come from the **latest** `licence_pricing_decisions` row for the licence (D5017): its accepted
`initial_fee_cents` and accepted `regulatory_surcharge_cents`, in the decision's `currency`. The amount is the fee
plus the surcharge, printed as two lines and a total. The amount, both components, the decision uid and the printed
figures with their digest are frozen at issue, as on an annual invoice.

- **No decision recorded:** `no_pricing_decision`. Nothing is issued.
- **No initial fee recorded on the decision, or a fee of zero:** `no_initial_fee`, and the message says the decision
  records none. A surcharge on its own is not invoiced.
- **No surcharge recorded:** no surcharge line. The column stays NULL and is never stored or billed as 0. A surcharge
  recorded as zero prints no line either, but it is stored as 0, which is different from not recorded.
- **Unknown currency, licensor not the payee, no payment instructions:** refused, as for an annual invoice. No
  initial invoice is ever issued without payment instructions.
- **Decisions that cannot be read:** `503 pricing_unreadable`. This is never treated as "no initial fee".

An apply is unchanged: it still writes only the annual fee and the royalty into the core terms.

### 3. One per licence, held like a licence year

The migration adds `kind` (`annual` | `initial`, default `annual`), `initial_fee_cents`,
`regulatory_surcharge_cents` and `pricing_decision_uid` to `licence_fee_invoices`. Using a kind rather than a sibling
table means paying, voiding, the D4694 revision guard, the PDF and the licensee's read all run the annual invoice's
own code.

- A partial unique index allows one `initial` row per licence. A void one still holds the licence, as a void annual
  invoice still holds its year.
- The initial row's period is the first licence year (`starts_on` to the day before the first anniversary), and its
  label is "Initial fee". The first annual invoice starts on the same day. So D802's one-per-year index is rebuilt to
  count `annual` rows only, and the annual duplicate check ignores the initial row. The annual rule is otherwise the
  same: one per licence year, void included.
- A trigger refuses an insert whose shape is wrong: an initial row needs a fee, a decision, and an amount equal to the
  fee plus the surcharge; an annual row carries no component. A second trigger freezes an issued initial invoice's
  amount, currency, components, decision and figures, and stops any row from changing kind. Payments and a void are
  recorded as usual.
- The payment reference is `AXAL-LIC-<licence ref>-INITIAL`.

### 4. The ledger counts it

In D5036's receivables, the initial invoice counts in invoiced, collected, outstanding, overdue and void like any
invoice. Overdue rows now carry `kind`. It holds no licence year, so it never closes the first year's "expected, not
invoiced" gap.
