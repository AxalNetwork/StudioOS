## D1065 — Prepare a Build plan from accepted Validate evidence

Issue #2935, part 2 of #2426. Migration 581 adds `build_plans`,
`build_plan_items` and `build_evidence_links`. One draft/open/paused plan can
exist per venture and scope; item keys are unique within a plan, markers
within a venture, and landed-row/evidence links cannot duplicate.

The shared AUTOFILL source columns were absent from migration 506 and main.
581 adds nullable `source_kind`, `source_ref` and `source_label` to
`ai_rail_proposals`, preserving every existing row; AUTOFILL reuses them.
No applied migration or runtime schema bootstrap is changed.

`services/buildPlanner.ts` reads committed Validate stores. It projects
curated pain themes and distinct interview counts, live hypotheses and their
resolved supporting/contradicting evidence, planned experiments, confirmed
would-pay/LOI/pre-sale signals, objection categories and counts, segments,
confirmed ICP positioning, accepted alternatives and manually entered or
edited complete competitor records, and open decision commitments. An orphan
evidence link does not count. It never reads pending Validate proposals,
transcripts, quote fields, interviewee/contact columns or decision reasoning.
Only structured labels and counts leave the reader. Contact/link/quoted-text,
ISO-date and currency patterns are removed from carried labels. Operational
record identifiers remain for provenance; they are not invented evidence.

`planRules` has one branch per mapping row in #2426. Three distinct interviews
(`PLAN_MIN_INTERVIEWS`) and a Severe/Real judgement plus supporting evidence
unlock a feature. A given Proceed unlocks a supported claim's must-have only
when that claim is the decision's scope or has supporting evidence in its frozen snapshot;
untested claims, planned experiments and a Pivot's new claim unlock tests.
Confirmed signals attach an audience count to a test. Objections and contrary
evidence produce risks with a named count threshold. The brief contains only
accepted labels and confirmed positioning. Accepted features map to Now,
Next or Later and would-pay signals unlock the paying-accounts KPI. Open
commitments carry their text only: D1065 explicitly forbids proposing dates,
so this part carries neither the owner's name nor a due date. A given Proceed
without an entity proposes only missing Incorporate/IP steps. Connector
entries are doors justified by proposed work, never system writes.
Founding team, cap-table changes, amounts and verdicts are never proposed.

Preparation writes the plan snapshot, SHA-256 evidence hash, proposal rows and
items in one D1 batch. Each proposal has `kind='proposal'`,
`source_kind='module'`, the source reference and label, `record_ref=project:N`,
`rail_option=draft:build/plan`, no model and no usage-log row. It costs no AI
run. Updates refresh undecided items and withdraw unsupported ones. Accepted,
edited and discarded items are never re-proposed or edited; a newer decision
supersedes the current plan, withdrawing only undecided proposals, and accepted
items remain visible. Preparation is idempotent under concurrent requests.
A failed batch writes no partial plan/item/proposal set. No Build record or
provenance/evidence link is written until D1066's acceptance writer runs.

`routes/build_plan.ts` is mounted before the standing Build router:
`GET` and `POST /api/cycles/build/plan?project=N&scope=venture|h-N`.
POST is the venture's founder's or admitted Lab owner's action; admins read.
Unrelated/trashed ventures and foreign hypothesis scopes are `not_this_project`.
A new empty plan refuses `no_evidence`; a paused plan or current given Stop
refuses `plan_paused`. Store failures answer 503 Unreadable, never empty data.
An existing plan may update to no pending items while retaining accepted ones.

A partner must have the project's recorded company membership. Their read
contains only accepted/edited items with a landed row, never pending proposals,
the private plan, its brief, decision references or source metadata. An admin
cannot prepare on behalf of a founder. `frontend/src/lib/api/buildPlan.js`
provides the two methods, re-exported once alphabetically from `api.js`.

D1066 owns Accept/Edit/Dismiss, the Build writers, provenance and evidence
trail. D1067 owns the Verdict trigger and pause/resume actions. This part
provides their preparation helper; it does not install a trigger, run AI,
send to a tool, or deploy. Validation and mutation results are recorded in the
single draft PR. Roll back the Worker/client by reverting; the additive
schema and nullable source columns may remain.
