## D5129 part 2 — The shared state component as built, and where it departs from the canvas (#5129)

Part 1 (`D5129.md`) records the vocabulary and the canvas. This part records the component built from board ST-13 and the choices made while building it.

### As built

- `Unreadable` draws the Unavailable chip, then "{what} could not be read. {claim}", then Retry. `DataState` is `role="alert"` only after a Retry (ST-09); the `Unreadable` alias and the folded copies keep the alert on first load (`announce="always"`) as they shipped, because earlier decisions (#4698 and others) rely on the announcement. A redesigned surface passes `announce="after-action"`.
- The folded copies keep their shipped "Try again" (`retryLabel`); the canvas's "Retry" is the default for new surfaces.
- `Unrecorded` draws the Not recorded chip. Its `reason` stays on the chip's title by default, because about 45 of its `reason=` call sites already print the same reason beside it; `reasonVisible` (and `DataState`, by default) draws it as text. Each surface turns it on when its redesign is built.
- `stateFromRead` maps a gap or an empty list in a `partial` read to Unavailable, not Not recorded or None: the part that could not be read may be where it lives.
- Folded in: `lib/fundAnalytics.js`, `advisor/expertise/kit.jsx`, `components/deals/DealRoomKit.jsx`, `investor/InvestorFundLanding.jsx`, `admin/community/page/parts.jsx`, `research/CompanyProfile.jsx`, `hq/aiAgents/HqAiAgentsPage.jsx`. Their test ids and exported names stay.
