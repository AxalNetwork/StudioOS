## D6114 — A partner's deal and an obligation's agenda item can be edited and removed (#6114)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-18 for #6114 (slot S18). No migration:
  `growth_partner_deals` and `obligation_agenda_items` have had `uid` and
  `updated_at` since migration 462.
- **Code:** `cloudflare-worker/src/services/growWriters.ts`
  (`updatePartnerDeal`, `removePartnerDeal`, `updateAgendaItem`,
  `removeAgendaItem`), `cloudflare-worker/src/services/records/grow.ts`
  (Partner network's Deals sourced, Durable company-building's Agenda),
  `records/types.ts` and `records/owned.ts` (two stores).
- **Tests:** `cloudflare-worker/test/grow_deal_agenda_edit_6114.test.ts`.

### What was wrong

Both tables were insert-only (`addPartnerDeal`, `addAgendaItem`). A typo in a
customer's name or an agenda line stayed for good, and a partner with a deal
added by mistake could not be removed at all, because D6115 keeps a partner
that has deals.

### What it does now

Each row is worked the way D6113 works a market's entry step: the row names
itself by uid and carries two further actions, **Edit** (a form opened on
that row's values) and **Remove** (a danger write behind a confirmation). Both
are `row_only`, so the record's own actions row never draws them, and the
generic record renderer draws them with no new frontend code.

- **Scope.** A row is checked twice: in its own store (`ownedRow`, through its
  parent to the venture) and by the writer, which reads it joined to the
  parent the record names. A deal of another partner, an item of another
  obligation, either one under another venture's record, or no row at all is a
  404 that writes and logs nothing.
- **Edit** changes only the keys the form sends (`DEAL_EDIT`, `AGENDA_EDIT`).
  A blank customer or item is refused (400, with the field). A deal's amount
  keeps its stored currency unless the edit names another; clearing the amount
  clears the currency, as `addPartnerDeal` stores none without an amount.
- **Activity** is one line on the parent record: "Deal updated", "Deal
  removed", "Agenda item updated", "Agenda item removed". It never names the
  customer (a third party) or the item's text.
- **Who.** Writers only. A deal's row carries its actions only for a writer,
  because the Edit's values hold the customer's name, which a reader is never
  shown ("A deal"). The route also strips row actions for readers (D6113), so
  this is a second line, not the only one.

### Not changed

- Removing a deal is a hard delete; once a partner has no deal and no revenue,
  D6115's Remove may take the partner too.
- An agenda item on an obligation that has been held is still editable: the
  agenda is the plan for its next meeting, and the held meeting's notes and
  minutes live on its occurrence.
- The amount field takes cents, as Add deal and the partner's Edit already do.
