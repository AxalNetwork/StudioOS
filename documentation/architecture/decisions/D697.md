## D697 — Customer research, Problem discovery and Experiments and evidence carry everything the Interviews, Pain map and Hypotheses pages did, and an interview opens as Customer research's record

Issue #1659 (S07; moved from S02 to S12, then to S07, on 2026-10-17). Part of #1654 (D689).

Validate's three bottom sections take over what `FounderValidateWorkspace` (FVW) drew on `/validate/interviews`, `/validate/pain-map` and `/validate/hypotheses`. An interview opens as Customer research's record at `/validate/customer-research/:id`. The issue holds S1's 21 decisions. This file records them as each part ships:

- **PR 1:** the sections and the parts mount, with the two history views moved from the verdict zone.
- **PR 2:** the record, the audio player and the census.

PR 1 merges after #1745 (#1658's PR 2), #1691 (on `main` as #1749), #1692's closing PR and #1703. PR 2 merges after #1692's closing PR.

### The start-now part (`Part of #1659`)

S1 opened one part on 2026-10-17 that draws no read and needs none of those dependencies. It changes no page anyone sees today.

- **`PainSeverity` moved, not forked (decision 16).**
  - "How badly did they need it?" goes from `FounderValidateWorkspace.jsx` to `pages/founder/validatePieces.jsx`, with its docblock, byte for byte, now exported.
  - `validatePieces.jsx` imports `api` for it and names it in its own docblock. It is the one piece there that writes for itself (`api.setInterviewPainSeverity`).
  - FVW imports it from there and draws it where it did, until #1666 deletes FVW. The record's tools (step 8) will draw the same component.
- **`TranscriptReview`'s optional `time` prop (step 7).**
  - With `time`, each passage starts with its start as `[hh:mm:ss]`, from the `start_ms` the session transcript already carries.
  - A passage with no readable start draws no time, never `[00:00:00]`.
  - `/validate/session/:id` does not pass it, so that page is unchanged, and neither, until PR 2, is the review page.
- **Three pure rules in `interviewReviewModel.js` (step 10), for a Node test:**
  - `clock(ms)`: the `[hh:mm:ss]` above. Seconds are floored. Null for an absent, negative or unreadable start.
  - `findPassages(segments, text)`: the passages Search keeps. These are the passages `TranscriptReview` draws (the final segments when there are any), then those whose words hold the text, case aside. Blank text keeps them all, and it reads nothing.
  - `afterDelete(projectId)`: where the record lands after Delete, `/validate/customer-research?project_id=N`. An id that is not a positive whole number is left off the URL.
  - `recordParts(read)` is left out: it reads #1692's record, and comes with PR 2.

No client method, Worker route or migration. `CycleRoute.jsx`, `App.jsx`, `adapters/validate.js` and `InterviewReviewPage.jsx` are untouched, as S1's ruling sets.

### PR 1: the three bottom sections (`Part of #1659`)

`frontend/src/pages/cycles/ValidateSections.jsx` draws three sections under the module tables: `InterviewsSection` (Customer research), `PainThemesSection` (Problem discovery) and `HypothesesSection` (Experiments and evidence). `CycleRoute.jsx` mounts them as Validate's `parts`, as Grow's and Build's further parts are mounted. Validate had no `parts` map before; #1704 adds `parts.verdict` to this one.

**What S1 decided, as built.** The decision numbers are the issue's.

1. **The pages leave last (1).** `/validate/interviews`, `/pain-map` and `/hypotheses` stay mounted, and the module pages still link to them as their tools, until #1666 removes them. #1666 redirects `?new=notes|upload` to `/validate/customer-research?log=notes|upload`, the opener built here.
2. **Record a session stays Customer research's Add (3).** Log an interview and Export interviews are secondary actions under the table. Record a session's text mode needs a conversation, so structured notes would otherwise have no way in.
3. **State replaces Stage (4).** The fallback filters drop Stage, and Consent's Agreed option reads "Agreed · deck-eligible": FVW's Deck-eligible view is `quote_consent === true`, the same rows as Agreed. Strong fit and Not ICP are the ICP fit filter's Strong and None. The Worker keeps `filter=stage:pipeline` and `sort=booked` for Record a session's recruiting picker.
4. **Order of the named parts (6).** In `CycleRoute.jsx` this PR adds Validate's `parts` map, `query` and `openDialog` in the parts context, and the open-dialog state with its two render lines. In `adapters/validate.js` it adds `TITLES`, the three modules' fallback filters and sorts, and Experiments' Retired and Moved marks. #1691's `keys` line and `state` cell, and #1658's row-id lines, are untouched.
5. **Table tools under the table (7).** What FVW drew per interview row goes to the record (PR 2).
6. **The sections read nothing (8).** Every count is the module read's `parts`. Every write is a callback from Validate's `parts` entry. The one other read is Link to a pain's lists, which `CycleRoute.jsx` makes when that dialog opens. The chosen filter reaches a section through the parts context's `query`.
7. **Writers only (9).** Log an interview, both exports, the Problem slide link, the unthemed-quotes panel (Assign, New theme), New hypothesis and Link to a pain are drawn only when `parts.can_write` is `true`. A missing `can_write` is not a write gate. The strips, the Ungrouped phrases tile and More tools are everyone's.
8. **One vocabulary (10).** "Blocking the verdict" is Status's "Fit not recorded", and "Retired" is a Status option. Validated, Invalidated and Unproven are not drawn.
9. **Source stays (11).** The Hypotheses table keeps Eadwyn's mark as its last column, a departure from CO:616.
10. **Pain-map curation is split (12).** Grouping the unthemed quotes is this section's panel. Merge, Split, Rename, Delete, Attach a quote and Link are the theme record's (#1692, #1658). FVW's Need-to-have chip is the Need-to-have filter, beside ICP only.
11. **Recently moved keeps 14 days (13),** `RECENTLY_MOVED_DAYS`, read by #1691 as `moved: 'recent'`.
12. **More tools (15).** The Marketplace and Advisory card is titled "More tools", each link saying what it is: "Marketplace: partners' services, and the needs you post" and "Advisory: the advisor directory". Neither finds interviewees, and FVW's sentence about zones goes with the zones.
13. **Moved, never forked (16, 20).** `LogInterviewModal`, `NewHypothesisDialog`, `LinkPainDialog` and `lib/verdictHistory.js`'s `RECENTLY_MOVED_DAYS`, `daysBefore`, `startOfMonth` and `historyReaches` are imported where they live, unchanged. `ValidateSections.jsx` imports `ValidateDialogs.jsx`, so #1666 keeps that file.
14. **One width rule (18).** Below 1024px every strip and list is one column.
15. **The two history views (19).** As of last week and Changed this month are the Hypotheses table's History filter, read by #1691's `history:last_week` and `history:this_month`. The note for a window that opens before `history_since` is drawn as described below.
16. **Link to a pain and New hypothesis (20).**
    - Link to a pain hands `LinkPainDialog` the board's unretired claims and its themes, as FVW did. It saves through #1692's `link` on the theme, `recordAction('validate', N, 'problem-discovery', <theme>, 'link', { hypothesis_id, direction })`, so both sides log it.
    - New hypothesis saves through `api.createHypothesis(N, { claim })`. It is drawn only while `formFor('validate', 'experiments')` is null; once #1656's PR 2 registers the table's Add, the section draws none.
17. **No dialog inside the size container (21).**
    - The parts context carries `openDialog(element, { page })`, and `openDialog(null)` closes it. `CycleRoute.jsx` holds the one open dialog and clears it when the cycle, the module or the record changes.
    - It draws the dialog after the size container, beside the action `Mount`, or as the body below 1024px when it is a page.
    - The three section dialogs are not pages, so they are drawn after the container at every width.
