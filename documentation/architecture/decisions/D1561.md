## D1561 — The ⋯ menu's whole-deck actions: Reorder for stage, Check wording and Replace all images (#3808)

- **Status:** Built for #3808 (deck v2 C13, part of #3716), 2026-11-02, to S1's final-canvas revision of the issue: Worker routes, client methods and the adapter swap. It also adds the three pieces of UI the canvas draws that did not exist yet: pd23's stage sheet, pd25's Wording section and pd13's Replace all images sheet.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1522 (the stage plan and its order);
  - D1523 (the deck's figures: Linked, Stated, Uncited);
  - D1555 (C7's consistency rules);
  - D1557 (the critic's run shape, which the wording pass follows);
  - D1604 (the v2 seam);
  - D1505 (#3756's image pipeline, which Replace all images draws through).
- **Migration:** none.
- **Owner decision:** none.

### Why

The final canvas folds the v2 deck toolbar into the ⋯ menu. S1 built the menu (`DeckMoreMenu`) and the Check claims sheet, but Reorder for stage and Replace all images were greyed "Coming later". The Wording pass of pd25 had no route either.

The one-click run also needs two things it can call with nobody pressing: Reorder for stage, so the order matches the inferred stage, and Replace all images as one quote.

### What was decided

1. **Reorder for stage is one undo step.** `POST /api/decks/:id/reorder-for-stage` reads D1522's stage plan for the stage asked for, or the deck's own stage. `reorderForStage` (pure) puts the cards in the plan's order, and the result is saved as **one** new version. Undo is the version history.
   - Every card is kept, and none is added.
   - A card the plan does not know (a founder's own) moves with the card before it.
   - Two cards of one kind keep their order.
   - A deck already in order writes nothing (`unchanged`).
   - The opened version is the base. If a newer version exists, the route answers the version conflict (409) and never saves a reordered copy of old cards. Cards that cannot be read are refused (503) and never saved as an empty deck.

   It is free: no model is run.
2. **Check wording is charged only when pressed.** The Wording section reads its quote first and shows the hold on the button, and the run is one press under the new rail option `deck:wording`. It names two kinds of wording: guarantee language and unsupported claims.

   A finding is shown only when:
   - its quote is on its card word for word (case and spacing aside);
   - its reason holds no number that is not in the quote.

   Any other finding is counted (`dropped`) and never shown. The cards reach the model as framed data (`untrustedBlock`). A failed run shows Try again, and AI off answers `off` at no cost.
3. **Check claims' free checks are unchanged.** The Stated and Uncited figures come from D1523's `GET /figures`, and the consistency findings come from D1555 on the check reply. One test reads both on the issue's fixture and finds every Stated and Uncited figure, plus both C7 failures: use of funds against the raise, and runway against cash ÷ burn.
4. **The stage sheet (pd23 in the editor).** ⋯ › Stage and ⋯ › Reorder for stage open one sheet. It shows:
   - the stage chips;
   - the plan's order with its why lines, marking a card "Not in this deck";
   - the missing-proof warnings;
   - the button "Order for [stage] · one undo step".

   The button is held while the editor has unsaved edits, and a reader never sees it. Reorder for stage is a `write` item in the menu, so a reader sees it greyed.
5. **The adapter.** `deckActions` is now Reorder for stage, and `replaceImages` is Replace all images' quote, each through its client. One row stays off: `outlineReorder`, the New deck outline's Reorder for stage. The outline exists before any deck, and the browser has no stage order to apply to it yet.
6. **Replace all images: one quote, one hold.** It runs on #3756's pipeline (D1505).
   - **The areas.** `replaceAreasOf` (pure) lists every `image` block on every card, at most 24. Each one gets the area of the card it fills, the aspect nearest its box, and a description: its own alt text, else the card's heading. No digit or currency sign is kept, so no figure reaches a prompt.
   - **The quote.** `POST /api/decks/:id/images/replace-all/quote` prices each area exactly as its own press would, through the same checks, model pick and art direction (`pricedAsk`, shared with `POST /images`). It answers one figure for the deck, plus a hold of twice that, which covers one redraw per area. An area whose words are refused (a person, a figure, a chart) is left out and named. Any other refusal stops the whole action.
   - **The press.** `POST /api/decks/:id/images/replace-all` reserves that **one** hold. It draws each area with `drawDeckImages` under it as the parent hold, with one redraw, and settles the hold once, at what the passed images cost.
     - A refused image is charged zero.
     - The images are proposals, kept card by card as any other.
     - An `Idempotency-Key` makes a second press of the same key draw nothing (409 `already_pressed`).
   - **The one-click run** can take the same path: `replaceAreasOf` plus `drawDeckImages` with its own parent hold.
   - **The sheet.** ⋯ › Replace all images opens it. It shows the area count and "holds up to $…", the areas left out and why, and one press. It never opens a browser pop-up.

### Not in this decision
