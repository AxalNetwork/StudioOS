- **Routes:** none minted. `/research/markets` (`MarketDirectoryZone.jsx`, the "Your market readings" card) and `/research/markets/:uid` (`MarketReading.jsx`).

### What was built

- **One money rule.** `pages/research/marketReadingRead.js` converts through `lib/money.js` at USD (`parseMinor`, `formatMinor`, `toMajorString`): `usd`, `catalogLine` ("Catalog price $120.00"), `readingFields` (99.50 and 99.99 reopen as typed), the list rows and the CSV. A reading's cents carry no currency column and are US dollars by contract. More than two decimals is refused, never rounded. Its five manual `/ 100` conversions are gone from the D5373 allow-list.
- **The Eadwyn summary (◇) is not built here.** The canvas was checked against `87bf3ac4ac`, before D6285 (`36c583be`) removed the `research/market` draft surface from `routes/research.ts` with the unmounted `MarketZone` body. On main there is no summary that rounds, so there is nothing to change to two decimals. Bringing the summary back needs a mounted band (`research_ask_session` holds the Worker's allow-list and the mounted bands as one set), and a mounted band needs a rail option in `lib/railOptions/{founder,advisor,partner,investor}.js` and the Worker's job and page maps. Those files are outside this issue, and the investor registry would need a row for a band an investor never sees. The question is on #7174. Until it is answered, `routes/research.ts` is unchanged.
- **The list (RMP-01, RMP-04B, RMP-13A).** A six-column table from `sm` (what it prices with service line and scope, the range to the cent, comparables, run date, age as a word and a shape, Open ›) and cards at 390. A service line never read keeps its row: No reading yet, Not recorded, Nothing to age, and its own Record a reading. Export CSV beside the one primary, columns `metric,service_line,scope,low_usd,high_usd,comparables,ran_at,age_days,age`, empty cells for Not recorded. The card is full width.
- **The form (RMP-02 to RMP-03C, RMP-08).** Scope on the directory form (sent trimmed, blank as null); a correction under each field (`readingErrors`), tied by `aria-describedby`, with the reversed range as the row alert that keeps the button disabled; save in progress read-only with a Saving… chip; a refusal or no answer keeps every input with "This reading was not saved." The button is sticky above the keyboard on a phone; amounts use `inputMode="decimal"`.
- **The detail (RMP-04 to RMP-06C).** Arriving from a save on the list opens with "Recorded. This is the reading as stored." and the stored figures; the page's own save with "Recorded as a new reading. The one you opened is unchanged."; focus moves to the h1. The catalog price sits in its own box beside the range (Not applicable without a service line, Not recorded for an unpriced one). A "What this reading says" card. "Record a fresh reading" retitles the form and focuses Low. A missing or foreign reading reads "This reading is not on your list."
- **Roles (RMP-11).** An investor's Markets view draws no readings card and reads none; its reading page shows the not-on-list card without reading (D6273 Q2). The founder's own-record line (#6279) is unchanged.
- **Violet.** `accentVars` returns `ACCENT.founder` for every licence, replacing D593's licence accent.
