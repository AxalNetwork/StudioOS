### 2. The owner's answers, as built

- **Q5.** One price, the catalog quote's (`GET /api/legal/incorporation/quote`), with the registered-agent offer beside it as optional, and "The state fee is confirmed at filing." No itemised breakdown is drawn.
- **Q6.** No gate: INC5e (locked by plan) is dropped. `POST /incorporation/order` checks ownership only.
- **Q7.** No "Open a bank account" anywhere: neither the timeline nor Done draws a bank row or link.
- **Q9.** A company appears at payment, marked "Filing in progress". The UI follows `company_uid` when the order carries it: `incorporateAdapter.orderCompany(order)` answers it, and the timeline then says "[Company] now appears in Portfolio, linked to [project]. Filing in progress." with Open company, and Done draws Open company. `GET /api/legal/incorporate/status` does not carry `company_uid` on this base (D1415 part 3, **#3855**), so today neither is drawn: never a promise.

### 3. Rules kept

- **Your own projects only.** Never `GET /api/projects`. Start fresh creates the project first (`POST /api/projects`, `company_id: null`), then orders it.
- **The timeline is the order's own row** (D601): Submitted (`paid_at`), Packet ready (`packet_document_id`), Filed (`filed_on`) or "Waiting for Axal VC to record the filing". EIN is its own row (D579: "•••• 1234", or Not recorded with Add EIN), never a status. A paid order whose packet is not built still says so (`PACKET_NOT_BUILT`, D367).
- **The device draft** (INC5a): the answers are kept in `localStorage` (`incorporate:draft`) until the order is paid, and "Your answers are saved on this device. Pick up where you left off." offers Resume. Every read and write is in try/catch; a store that throws keeps nothing and the flow goes on.
- **The Stripe terminal loads with its step** (`lazy`), since its module reads the publishable key when it loads.

### 5. Deviations from the plan and the canvas

- **Goals and the project picker share step 1.** The canvas draws the picker with Compare on INC1; here step 1 is the picker and the four questions, step 2 Compare, so the recommendation is made before the options are shown.
- **The registered agent is the order's** (`registered_agent_name`, `registered_agent_address`), as before; the company's own registered agent (D1593) is filled at filing by #3855.
- **The success page** (`/incorporate/success`, the legacy Checkout return) keeps D601's packet line and does not mount the timeline: its status read carries no project, so the EIN row could not be read.
- **The compliance add-ons on Done are not kept.** The canvas has none, and the registered-agent opt-in after payment is.
