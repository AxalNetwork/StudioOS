## D6203 — Value-add draws In progress, Overdue, due dates and meetings resulting from the ledger (#6203)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-16. Frontend, plus a small Worker read change. No migration.
- **Builds on:** D6202 (#6202), the ledger's lifecycle: In progress and Cancelled, a due date, an introduction's meeting, and their totals. D4817 part 5 (#5311, #5845) is the Value-add page and the Overview card. D4642 adds the revision guard and history.

### What changes

- **Figures.** On `/portfolio/value-add` the figure row draws In progress and Overdue from the Worker's figures (`totals.in_progress`, `totals.overdue`). Open commitments is both open states (`totals.outstanding`). Introductions made is the Worker's counted figure (`by_kind.intro.delivered`), with its meetings resulting beside it.
  - A measured 0 stays 0.
  - A figure whose read failed, or that the read did not carry, is Unreadable, never 0.
  - Introductions made no longer needs a read that holds every entry, because the Worker counts it over the whole scope.
- **Each row.**
  - **Due date:** the row shows its due date, or "No due date".
  - **Overdue:** an Overdue chip appears only where the Worker said so. The row's `overdue` is computed in the read's SQL with `date('now')`, by the same rule `totals.overdue` counts with (an open entry, a due date, before the Worker's date, UTC). The page never compares a due date with the browser's clock. A past due date the Worker did not call overdue draws no chip.
  - **Closed dates:** a cancelled entry shows its cancellation date.
- **An introduction's meeting.** It reads Yes with its date, Yes with "date not recorded", No, or Not recorded. A meeting nobody recorded reads Not recorded, never No. The Introductions table and each introduction row both show it.
- **Moves, confirmed in place.**
  - A promised entry offers Start, Mark delivered, Withdraw and Cancel entry. An in-progress entry offers the same without Start: the Worker refuses moving back (`cannot_reopen`), so the page does not offer it.
  - Every move names the entry and its company and says what cannot be undone, as #5845's Mark delivered and Withdraw already did. Nothing is sent until the member confirms.
  - Cancel's dismiss button reads "Keep entry", not a second "Cancel".
  - A refused move (`state_is_terminal`, `cannot_reopen`) is reported against that entry.
- **Record meeting.** An introduction that is not withdrawn or cancelled offers Record meeting, in place.
  - The member chooses Yes (with an optional date) or No.
  - A date that is not a real day is refused in the form, before anything is sent.
  - It is the same PATCH as a move, with its revision, and it moves no state (D6202).
- **Log support.**
  - State may be logged as In progress.
  - An open entry takes an optional due date, in a labelled date field. A mistyped date is refused with the error tied to the field (`aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`).
  - A delivered entry is never overdue, so it is not asked for a due date. A date typed before choosing Delivered is not sent.
- **Outstanding** (the filter chip) is both open states. The Worker reads `state=open` as promised or in progress, and its `matching_total` follows. Before this, Outstanding read `state=promised`, and an entry that had started dropped out of it.
- **Overview.** The Value-add card adds Introductions made and Meetings resulting from the same figures. Open commitments is both open states. A figure the read lacked reads Unreadable, never 0. The card's foot says a meeting nobody recorded is not counted as no meeting.

### The Worker change

`routes/portfolio_support.ts`, the list read only:

- Each entry carries `overdue` (a boolean), computed in SQL against `date('now')` with `totals.overdue`'s rule.
- `state=open` reads both open states. A single state still reads only that state, and an unknown state is still no filter.

No write path changes. Pinned by `cloudflare-worker/test/support_row_overdue_d6203.test.ts`, which runs the real router on the migrations' tables. It was mutation-checked against:
- dropping the state test from the row's rule;
- removing the open clause;
- returning the raw 0/1;
- miscounting `matching_total`.
