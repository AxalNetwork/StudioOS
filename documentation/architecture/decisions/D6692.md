## D6692 — Formation partner registry: partners, licences, data terms, coverage, prices, members (#6692)

Part of the global incorporation plan (#6680, key P1). Before this, no record
said which partner firm may do which formation work, where, under which
licence and which data terms. A form may be switched on for founders only
when such a record exists, and #5513 needed a definition of "verified".

**Store.** Migration `202611151734_6692_formation_partners.sql` adds five
tables:

- `formation_partners`: one row per firm in `partners` (unique), with a
  status (`draft`, `verified`, `suspended`, `ended`), an optional
  `partner_deals` link, what verifying it checked, the next review date and
  the data terms (data role, the agreement document and the day it was
  signed, transfer mechanism, retention, deletion contact, restricted
  places).
- `formation_partner_credentials`: a licence in one country (ISO alpha-2),
  with type, number, regulator, `valid_until` (a date, valid through that
  day), the evidence document and who checked it on which day.
- `formation_partner_coverage`: a country, a form key and one of ten
  services, with target hours, priority and on or off. Created off.
- `formation_partner_prices`: a price for one coverage row and period:
  `founder_price_minor` and `cost_minor`, integer minor units of one
  currency, with a cadence (`one_time`, `monthly`, `annual`).
- `formation_partner_members`: a user who acts for the firm, with role
  (`lead`, `staff`), who granted it and when it was revoked. One live
  membership per user and firm.

Documents are `documents.id`. Form keys are free keys in the shape the form
catalogue will use (lower case, digits, underscores); nothing checks them
against a catalogue yet.

**Routes.** `routes/admin_formation_partners.ts` mounts itself at
`/api/admin/formation/partners`: list, read, register an existing firm, edit
the record and its data terms, add and edit licences, coverage and prices,
verify, suspend, end, add and revoke members. The client is
`frontend/src/lib/api/adminFormationPartners.js`. No screen uses it yet
(#6702).

**Who.** Every route is `requireHqAdmin`, so a branch is refused. Verify,
suspend, end and any write that carries `cost_minor` are the Super Admin's,
behind `requireSuperAdminWriteBar`. A plain Admin is refused
`super_admin_required` before being asked for a code. `cost_minor` is
returned only to the Super Admin; for anyone else the key is absent, never 0,
and `cost_visible` says so. `founder_price_minor` is returned to every HQ
admin. Every write records its action through `logAdminAction`
(`FORMATION_PARTNER_ACTIONS`).

**What "verified" means.** The Super Admin verified the firm after a signed
data agreement was on record (`dpa_signed_on`; otherwise `dpa_missing`), and
recorded what was checked and when it is next reviewed. A verified partner
cannot clear its signing day (`dpa_missing`); it is suspended first.

**Coverage on.** A coverage row is switched on only when the partner is
verified (`partner_not_verified`) and holds a licence for that country
(`credential_missing`) that has not expired (`credential_expired`). Switching
off needs neither. Requiring a verified partner is this decision's reading of
"a form may be switched on only when such a record exists": a draft or
suspended firm is not that record.

**Suspend and end.** A suspension (from verified) or an end (from any other
state) writes the status change and switches every coverage row off in the
same `DB.batch`. The coverage statement acts only if the status is now the
one written. Re-verifying a suspended partner does not switch coverage back
on; HQ does that row by row, under the same licence check. An ended partner
no longer changes, except that a member can still be revoked.

**Out of scope.** Assigning cases (#6710), partner-facing routes, paying
partners and any screen (#6702).

Tests: `cloudflare-worker/test/formation_partners_6692.test.ts`.
