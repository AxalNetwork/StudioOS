## D4717 — Community: one population per count, the roster from the server, one clock (#4717)

**Admin Community audit, slot S26 · 2026-11-06.** Packages 1 and the clock half of 3. No migration. The rest of #4717 follows in later PRs (see "Still to do").

### What was wrong

- **The check-in band counted everyone.** The band is labelled as founders, and its privacy floor is seven. It counted every distinct `user_id` in `wellbeing_daily_pulses`, so five founders and six investors showed a band that should have been withheld.
- **The roster was counted from a capped list.** The roster tile, the Circles card and the consoles strip all counted `GET /api/admin/network-profiles`, which answers 200 rows by default and says `truncated`.
- **Archived profiles were in the network totals.** The server's network aggregate (`blocks.network`) counted archived profiles (migration 551, `archived_at`) as inactive roster members.
- **The clock was frozen at mount.** The page's clock was `useState(() => Date.now())`, so Refresh fetched new rows but measured days open, waits and Overdue from the moment the page was first opened.

### What changed

- **Check-ins (`services/communityOverview.ts`).** The band counts distinct `user_id`s whose account's role is `founder`, joined to `users`. A pulse whose account is gone counts for nothing. Still no answer column is read.
- **Network block.** It is grouped by kind and state:
  - **active:** `is_active = 1`, not archived;
  - **hidden:** not active, not archived;
  - **archived:** `archived_at` set.

  Archived rows are reported only as `archived`, in no total. The new `by_kind_state` gives each kind's active and hidden counts. `by_kind`, `active` and `inactive` keep their meaning, without archived rows.
- **The page's roster (`communityModel.rosterOf`).** The roster comes from that block, over every row, and the page no longer calls `network-profiles`. A count that is missing, negative or not a whole number makes the roster unreadable, never 0.
- **The page's clock (`communityModel.clockOf`).** The clock is the Community read's `as_of`. Before that read answers, it is the time the page's reads last settled. Refresh brings a new `as_of`, so every time-relative figure moves with it.

### Tests

- **Worker (`admin_community_d931.test.ts`).**
  - Five founders and six other roles withhold the band; eight founders and twenty others show the founders' band. Mutation caught: counting every `user_id`.
  - An archived advisor is in no total. Mutation caught: dropping the archived branch.
- **Frontend (`admin_community_d930.test.mjs`).**
  - A 600-row roster is counted in full.
  - Archived rows are in neither column.
  - String, missing and negative counts are unreadable. Mutation caught: accepting a numeric string.
  - The clock follows `as_of`, and the source no longer freezes `now`.
  - The mounted page makes no `network-profiles` read.
- **Frontend (`admin_community_d933.test.mjs`).** The waiting event is dated from the fixture's own `as_of`, as the page now measures.
