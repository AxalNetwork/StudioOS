## D4717 — Community: one population per count, the roster from the server, one clock (#4717)

**Admin Community audit, slot S26 · 2026-11-06.** Packages 1 and the clock half of 3. No migration. The rest of #4717 follows in later PRs (see "Still to do").

### What was wrong

- **The check-in band counted everyone.** The band is labelled as founders, and its privacy floor is seven. It counted every distinct `user_id` in `wellbeing_daily_pulses`, so five founders and six investors showed a band that should have been withheld.
- **The roster was counted from a capped list.** The roster tile, the Circles card and the consoles strip all counted `GET /api/admin/network-profiles`, which answers 200 rows by default and says `truncated`.
- **Archived profiles were in the network totals.** The server's network aggregate (`blocks.network`) counted archived profiles (migration 551, `archived_at`) as inactive roster members.
- **The clock was frozen at mount.** The page's clock was `useState(() => Date.now())`, so Refresh fetched new rows but measured days open, waits and Overdue from the moment the page was first opened.

### What changed

- **Check-ins (`services/communityOverview.ts`).** The band counts distinct `user_id`s whose account's role is `founder`, joined to `users`. A pulse whose account is gone counts for nothing. Still no answer column is read.
- **Network block.** It is grouped by kind and state:
  - **active:** `is_active = 1`, not archived;
  - **hidden:** not active, not archived;
  - **archived:** `archived_at` set.

  Archived rows are reported only as `archived`, in no total. The new `by_kind_state` gives each kind's active and hidden counts. `by_kind`, `active` and `inactive` keep their meaning, without archived rows.
- **The page's roster (`communityModel.rosterOf`).** The roster comes from that block, over every row, and the page no longer calls `network-profiles`. A count that is missing, negative or not a whole number makes the roster unreadable, never 0.
- **The page's clock (`communityModel.clockOf`).** The clock is the Community read's `as_of`. Before that read answers, it is the time the page's reads last settled. Refresh brings a new `as_of`, so every time-relative figure moves with it.

### Tests

- **Worker (`admin_community_d931.test.ts`).**
  - Five founders and six other roles withhold the band; eight founders and twenty others show the founders' band. Mutation caught: counting every `user_id`.
  - An archived advisor is in no total. Mutation caught: dropping the archived branch.
- **Frontend (`admin_community_d930.test.mjs`).**
  - A 600-row roster is counted in full.
  - Archived rows are in neither column.
  - String, missing and negative counts are unreadable. Mutation caught: accepting a numeric string.
  - The clock follows `as_of`, and the source no longer freezes `now`.
  - The mounted page makes no `network-profiles` read.
- **Frontend (`admin_community_d933.test.mjs`).** The waiting event is dated from the fixture's own `as_of`, as the page now measures.

### Package 2 — one snapshot (2026-11-07)

**The problem.** The dashboard made seven reads. Five were raw lists: the Studio glance, every page of the events list, the events analytics, every page of the job board, and the circles list. The page then recomputed, in the browser, counts the Community read already held. So two definitions could disagree. The clearest case was Next up, which listed published events only, while the Upcoming tile counted published and waiting events. The figures also came from five different moments.

**Now the page makes one read,** `GET /api/admin/community/overview`, plus Recent activity's own. Every figure, and every row behind one, comes from that read under one `as_of`.

**The rows behind three counts, read from the same rows as the counts:**

- **`events.next_up`** (D918's `readOverview`): the soonest three of the events `upcoming` counts, published or waiting. A waiting event is listed, and marked "Waiting for review". `total` equals `upcoming.events`, so the list can never disagree with the tile. This settles package 1's Upcoming-events item.
- **`queue.event_rows`** (D918's `review`): the oldest six of the events `queue` counts. Each row has its wait, its Overdue flag (D915), and whether the wait counts from creation (D917).
- **`queue.jobs.review`:** the oldest six waiting postings. A posting submitted before migration 550 is listed last, undated, and never Overdue.
- **`jobs.most_applied`:** the five postings with the most applications of all time, each with its days open (D1121).

**Counts the page reads from the overview:**

- **`events.by_status`** (every event in every status) carries the page's empty rule. That rule now reads only the overview's own counts.
- **Waiting on you and the consoles strip** read the queues' counts instead of the glance's lanes. Needs a decision's total and Overdue figures are the queues' counts, not the number of rows listed.

**What travels in the response.** A record's `event_id` or `posting_id`, its title, its company, its times, capacity and seats taken. Never a host, an applicant or a member. The worker test allows an event title only inside these rows.

**From package 3:**

- The Events card is drawn whenever the read answered. Next up, the funnel and events by type each show their own failed state, so one block that can't be read no longer hides the others.
- A period figure without whole numbers is unreadable, never 0 and never a crash.
- A circles, jobs or queue count that isn't a whole number makes its block unreadable.

**If the read itself fails,** the page says nothing is a zero and draws no figure. The consoles strip still works.

**Tests:**

- **Worker** (`admin_community_d931`): 20 pass.
  - The next-up total equals Upcoming; the review total equals the queue.
  - Mutation caught: listing published events only.
- **Frontend** (`admin_community_*`, `community_lightboxes_d934`, `community_gaps_d1121`, `hq_studio_figures_d555` and the other files that read these pages): 248 pass.
  - The mounted page makes exactly two reads: the overview and activity.
  - Mutations caught: Needs a decision counting its listed rows instead of the queues, and a status count that isn't a whole number accepted.

### Package 3 — the read's shape is checked, never patched (2026-11-07)

Package 2 made the roster, circles, jobs, the queues and the period tiles strict. The other builders still read `Number(x) || 0`. A count that was missing, a string such as `"3"`, negative or fractional was drawn as a zero that looked real. The show-up rate, the funnel, the waitlists, events by type, the weekly series, Who takes part, the experts, bookings and the check-in band all did this.

**Now every block passes a check before anything is drawn** (`communityModel.js`):

- **Counts are whole numbers.** `countsAt` reads the counts a part needs, and `countMap` reads a map that must carry every key the card draws. The worker zero-fills events by type (all twelve types), the job statuses and the profiles, so a key that is left out is a broken answer, not a zero.
- **Each part fails alone.**
  - In the Events card, the funnel, the waitlists and events by type each show their own Unreadable.
  - A weekly series with a broken week, a week that is not on the read's axis, or totals that are not whole numbers is named as missing. It is not drawn with a hole.
  - A Who takes part part without every profile is named, and the other part is drawn.
- **The queues are checked once** (`eventQueueOf`, `jobQueueOf`), and Waiting on you, the oldest wait and Needs a decision all read them:
  - waits are whole hours, or null when none is recorded;
  - Overdue and undated cannot exceed the queue.
  - Needs a decision no longer counts a queue it could not read as 0. It lists and counts the other queue, and says which one is missing.
- **Ranges hold.**
  - Show-up's rate is a share from 0 to 1, and attended cannot exceed the seats taken. The tile's percentage is now worked out from the checked counts, so it always agrees with "x of y seats".
  - A Next up row cannot outnumber its total. Its seats and cap are whole numbers, or the row reads "Seats not recorded", never `NaN`.
- **Check-ins.**
  - "Too few check-ins" is shown only when the read says `withheld: true`.
  - A band is shown only when both of its ends are whole numbers and its top reaches the cohort of seven. Seven founders are the band 5 to 9.
  - Anything else is unreadable. It used to read `withheld || !band`, so a broken band was shown as a statement the read never made.
- **Rows.** A Next up, Needs a decision or most-applied row without its title is not listed as real.

**Tests:**

- `community_shapes_4717` breaks each of thirty fields four ways: missing, `"3"`, `-1` and `1.5`. A field that may lawfully be null (a wait, `confirmed` from an older read) is checked for that too.
- Each break must leave only its own part unreadable.
- A mounted page draws a broken count as Unreadable, never as 0, and still draws the other parts.
- Mutations caught:
  - the waitlists read `|| 0`;
  - a broken week drawn as a gap;
  - `withheld || !band`;
  - no range check on the rate;
  - a broken queue counted as 0 in Needs;
  - a job status left out read as 0.
- The fixtures in `admin_community_d933` and `community_gaps_d1121` now carry every type and status, as the worker sends them.
