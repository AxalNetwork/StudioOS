### 2. One gate (RR00 to RR02, RR13)

`ReasonGate` (`frontend/src/ui/ReasonGate.jsx`) draws, in order:
- the venture (`venture.name`, its logo, and "Founder: …" only when the Worker names exactly one founder; G2, D1465);
- the record's name and its access pill ("Read only · HQ access" or "· Studio admin access");
- the question, "Why are you opening this narrative?";
- three lines: who sees the reason, the record's duration rule, "Every open is recorded.";
- the four quick picks;
- the field with its counter;
- Open read only, and Back.

The words and rules are in `ui/reasonGate.js`, which is pure.

**Each record keeps its Worker rule (Q2 A).** `rule` draws "Access lasts 8 hours." for a grant and "This opens once; each open asks again." for a per-open read. `min` and `max` come from the record, never one shared number:

| Record | Rule | Characters |
|---|---|---|
| Narrative | grant, 8 h | 3–200 |
| Cap table | per open | 10–500 |
| Data room | per open | 1–500 |
| Capital relationships | per open | 1–500 |

**While typing (RR02):**
- Too short is a muted hint, because nothing is wrong yet. The cap table keeps its own sentence.
- Over the limit is red. In both cases Open is disabled.
- Sending reads "Opening…" with the field read only.
- A refused save keeps the field, with "The reason could not be saved. Try again."
- A quick pick fills the field and stays editable.
- The reason is sent with its spaces squashed, as the Worker counts it.

**Refusals (RR17, RR18).** `refusalView` sorts a refusal:
- 409 `support_owner_unresolved` is a muted line;
- a 403 other than `read_reason_required` is a plain line.

Neither draws a form or a Try again, because neither can succeed. A 400 or anything else keeps the form.

**Nothing of the record before the reason.** `preview` is `RecordSkeleton`: grey bars, `aria-hidden` and `inert`, under "Opens read only after you give a reason."

**`inline` (RR13)** is the compact form inside #3712's New version dialog: no venture header and no preview, with Continue and Cancel.

### 3. The access banner (RR04 to RR06)

`AccessBanner` is amber, above the record: "Read only · HQ access for <reason> · ends in 7 h 52 m".

The countdown:
- counts to the grant's `expires_at` in whole minutes, rounded up, so the last minute reads "1 m" and is marked;
- moves each minute without asking;
- at zero, calls `onExpire`. The narrative then re-reads `GET /narrative/reads`, the gate returns, and RR06's line shows above it: "Your 8-hour read ended at HH:MM.";
- is not drawn when the end is not read, never guessed.

A per-open read has no timer.

**End access now (RR05)** shows only when the record passes `onEnd`. It opens "End your read now?", and a failure is the white inset "Your read could not be ended. Try again." The narrative passes no `onEnd` until D1465 adds the route (`DELETE /narrative/reads/mine`) and its client.

Under the banner, D914's two lines stay: "Opened by HQ for “…”. The founder sees this reason." and "Read only. Notes are visible to the founder."

### 4. The Investor narrative

`NarrativeAdminGate` draws the gate in grant mode, then the banner with the countdown. Its Back goes to Raise.

The venture comes from `reads.venture` when the Worker sends it (D1465 G2). Until then the header reads "This venture", with no founder line.

### 5. The cap table (RR10)

Open for support… replaces the table with the gate in per-open mode (10–500, the cap table's own too-short sentence), with Cancel. Nothing of the table is drawn behind it.

After the reason:
- the amber banner, with no timer and no End access;
- D1026's lavender line, unchanged;
- the full table, read only.

A reload asks again (the Worker's rule).

### 6. The founder: who read the narrative (RR07)

`NarrativeReads` heads its list with "An admin gives a reason before reading. You see each read here." Each row ends with its span:
- "8 h", from the row's own `created_at` and `expires_at`;
- "Ended early" once D1465 records `ended_at`;
- nothing when the times are not read.

The list is on the module's **Home** as well as Story, so a founder with no saved version still sees who read it. An admin never sees it.

### 7. The rail on a founder's record (RR03, Q1 A)

`WorkerRail` takes `readOnlyFor="founder-record"`. The rail then:
- keeps the crumbs (who and where);
- draws one lavender note, "Eadwyn is off while you read a founder’s record.";
- draws no AI switch, no suggestions, no models, no Usage, no Ask and no floating button. A collapsed spine shows the greyed mark and the sentence as its title.

The admin's own spend is not shown on a founder's record, and a founder's spend never was.

`CycleRoute` passes it whenever an admin is on the Investor narrative. D1464 passes it on the per-open pages while a read is showing. The cap table read (`/cap-tables/:projectId`) draws no rail.
