### 1. Activity (DR10, DR11, DR22c)

**The read.** It reads `GET /api/data-room/:projectUid/activity` for the chosen period, investor, event and page.

**The range:**
- 7, 30 or 90 days, ending today.
- An investor filter offers every reader with a grant, from the unfiltered read.
- Export (CSV) asks `activity.csv` for the same period, investor and event, with no page.
  - The export changes nothing, so it opens no dialog.
  - More than 10,000 rows (413) says so in one sentence and asks for a shorter period, one investor or one event.

**Events by day:**
- Three lines from `ui/Charts.jsx`, in #2375's palette: room opens (violet), links issued (sky), blocked by the NDA (amber).
- The last day is drawn as "so far".
- "View as table" shows each day's three figures.
- The sentence under it gives each total and the same total for the days before, for example "Opened the room 7 (4 in the 30 days before)".
- The read carries only totals for the period before, not a daily series, so the canvas's dotted line for the period before is not drawn.

**By investor:**
- One row per reader, with room opens, links issued, blocked and last event.
- The busiest reader is first, as the read orders them.
- A reader with no grant here is "Someone without access here", never named, because D1105 does not name them.
- Zeros here are real: they are counts of the venture's own log.

**Event log:**
- Fifty rows a page, newest first, with Newer and Older, "1–50 of 65", and the event filter.
- A change of filter or period goes back to the first page.
- **The words.** Opened the room. "Link issued for [file]", never "downloaded". "Blocked: NDA needed for [file]", never an open. The foot says "A link issued means a one-time download link was made. Whether it was followed is not recorded."

**Not drawn:**
- The canvas's "Folder" column and its per-investor sparkline. The log rows carry no folder, and the read has no per-investor daily series.
- The log's search box and Sort control. The read takes neither.

**The phone (DR22c):** the range, then the last seven days of the period as a table (newest first, "so far" on today), then the investors. Export is not offered on a phone, as the canvas draws it.

**A failed read** is Unreadable with Try again, "This is not a claim that nobody opened the room". The range stays drawn. It never shows zeros.

### 2. Checklist (DR12)

**The read.** It reads `GET /api/data-room/:projectUid/checklist`.

**The summary line:** "[answered] of [12] items answered · [openable] an investor can open · [n] investors with access".

**Each area** shows its items with "[answered] of [total]".

**Each item:**
- **Its state, the Worker's** (the investor read's own rule, D1105 §4):
  - "Answered · investors can open it";
  - "Answered · no investor can open it", with the Worker's reason;
  - "Missing", with "No file answers this yet."
- **Its files,** each with:
  - its gate (NDA or Open);
  - its tier (Everyone invited, Committed only, Private to you);
  - its source when the platform filed it ("From Mercury", "From the cap table");
  - how many investors with access can open it. A missing count is "Not recorded", never 0.
- **What to do next:**
  - a missing item links to Files (Match to checklist, D1106) and to Upload;
  - an item no investor can open links to Files, where its gate is changed.

**This tab writes nothing.** The match and the gate keep their own dialogs where they are.

**"In Fundraising readiness"** says "Not read here yet". Fundraising readiness scores the data room, but its figure (the canvas's "Data room [7] of 10") is not carried to this page. The block links to `/raise/fundraising-readiness`.

**A failed read** is Unreadable with Try again. The proposals and readiness blocks stay drawn.

### 3. Preview (DR13, DR13e)

**View as:** one chip per grant, the ones in force first. An ended grant reads "[investor] · access ended [day]", and a revoked one reads "access revoked".

**The read.** It reads `GET /api/data-room/:projectUid/preview?grant=` for the chosen grant. The Worker runs the investor read's own filter for that grant (`investorView`).

**The line:** "Preview. This is what [investor] sees today. Nothing here is logged or sent."

**The room as on their deal page:**
- **The banner** reads "You have access." When files wait for the NDA, it reads "You have access. [n] files need your NDA."
- **Each folder the grant sees,** with its file count and the files' names.
- **"[n] files need your NDA"** with "Their names show once you sign."
- **No Open button:** a founder's preview opens nothing. The foot says what the investor's Open does.

**Only you see this:**
- **Counts, never names:**
  - files the tier, end date or folders hide;
  - files behind the NDA;
  - folders the investor does not see.
- **"Committed only files show because [investor] is committed",** when the grant is committed.

**Nothing the grant cannot open is ever named.** Every folder and file named comes from the preview read. The founder's room read supplies only the grants and how many folders the room has.

**An ended or revoked grant (DR13e)** reads "The founder has not opened their data room to you." and "This is all [investor] sees. No folder or file is named."

**A room shared with no one** says so, links to Access, and makes no preview read.

**Not drawn:** the canvas's "An invited investor with no NDA" chip. The preview read takes a real grant, not a hypothetical investor.
