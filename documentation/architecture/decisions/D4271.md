### 1. What the tile draws (`ReviewTile.jsx`, above `PitchReview.jsx`)

| Section | Read | What |
|---|---|---|
| Comments across the deck (ps6) | `GET /api/decks/:id/comments` (D1570) | Every thread, newest first, with its card ("Card 4 · Traction"), its person by name and initials, its reply count, its assignee, Assign and Resolve. Filters **Open · Assigned to you · Mentions you · Resolved** run client-side over the one read, each with its count. A mention in a reply counts. |
| Approvals | `GET /api/decks/:id/approval` (D1570) | Each approver by name, Approved (with its date) or Waiting, and the read's own sentence. None named says so. D1570 already stores approvals, so no "Request approval" stand-in and no gap to file. |
| AI review (ps6b) | `GET /api/decks/:id/reviews` (D900) | Unchanged, except "**v7** has not been reviewed yet" names the version, and "Last run on v6 · 72 / 100" names the newest review that returned a score. A run with no score is passed over, never shown as one. |
| Figures and their sources (ps6b) | `GET /api/decks/:id/figures` (D1523) and `GET /api/decks/:id/check` (D900) | Each figure, its card, its source ("Linked · Revenue · as of …", "Stated · typed on the card", "No source", or Not recorded) and its check: **Checked** only when the deck check backs it; its other states by name; **Not checked** when the check holds no claim for it. Uncited first. |

### 2. Where a row goes

- **A thread** opens its card in the editor with the card's comments open: `PitchEditor` now reads `?card=<n>&comments=1` once, when it opens (`editorCardOf`, counted from 1; a card the deck does not have opens the first). This is the Comment mode P1's deck bar (psR1–psR3) will dress; the content is the existing `CommentsSheet`.
- **A figure** opens its claim on Evidence (`/raise/evidence/claim/:uid`, the drawer CheckPanel already links) when the check holds one, else its card in the editor. A figure is matched to a claim on the same card by its label, or as the card's only claim.

### 3. Who

Comments and approvals are the founders' and the advisors they invite (D1570): an admin (read only) is not read for them and is told their thread is Notes. Figures and the AI review read as before.
