## D6118 — Partnerships is one page: partners and co-marketing (GR-09) (#6118)

- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-17, slot S21, on the owner's answer on #6118. It supersedes D4862's "keep both" for this pair only. Talent and Team expansion stay a pair (#6109).
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Grow v2 Page.dc.html`, board GR09: "Partners by stage and the revenue they bring", an Add partner action, a Mercury-sourced proposal on top, and a partner table with Partner, Type, Stage, Revenue and Terms.

### Before

Two pages shared the Partnerships tab, each with half of the subject:
- `/grow/partnerships` (`FounderGrowPartnerships`) read the co-marketing pitches and attributions.
- `/grow/partner-network`, the Partner network module page, read `growth_partners`: partner records with stage, revenue and terms, Rank by fit, and the Mercury proposals.

The tab opened the first and never showed the second. #6109 linked them to each other as a stopgap.

### Decision

- **`/grow/partnerships` is the one Partnerships page.** It renders the partner-network module (`<CycleRoute cycle="grow" slug="partner-network" />`), headed "Partnerships". The module's description is GR-09's, "Partners by stage and the revenue they bring".
  - The Mercury proposal (`parts.from_mercury`) moves above the partner table (`aboveRecords`), as GR-09 draws it, with its Accept or Dismiss step unchanged.
  - Rank by fit (`POST /api/cycles/grow/partners/rank`) is unchanged; it keys on the slug, not the address.
  - Co-marketing is a part under the table (`pages/cycles/PartnershipsCoMarketing.jsx`). It holds the pitches and attribution events from the founder-side read `GET /api/comarketing/founder/by-project/:pid`, and the All / In motion / Signed / Dormant filters over the persisted pitch statuses (D613, unchanged). It infers no signed value, delivery or referral. A figure with no record reads "Not recorded", and a failed read reads "Unreadable" with a retry (#5129).
  - The phone page draws the same parts and the same section above the table.
- **`/grow/partner-network` forwards to `/grow/partnerships`**, keeping the query and hash (`ParamRedirect`). It is listed in `redirectRoutes.ts` with `keep`, because sent digests and mail drafts carry it.
- **Unchanged addresses.** `/grow/partner-network/:id` (a partner record), `/grow/partner-network/new` (Add partner) and `/grow/partner-network/co-marketing` (the co-marketing workspace, and its `/comarketing` alias) keep their addresses. Links already sent keep working.
- **Links name the page, not the forward:**
  - the co-marketing workspace's back link (`MODULE_PAGES` in `founderToolNavigation.js`, "← Grow › Partnerships");
  - the sent update's Partnerships link;
  - the Worker's Needs-your-attention items (`growAttention.ts`) and `moduleHref('partner_network')`.
- **Retired:** `FounderGrowPartnerships.jsx` and its stylesheet are deleted. #6109's pair links for this pair are removed (`grow.tools['partner-network']`), since there is no other half to link to.
