## D1525 — Deck v2 B7: the judge, a blind Clef ranking of an area's variants under the family rule (#3783)

- **Status:** built by S14, 2026-11-03 (#3783, part of #3716). The issue gives the D number. No migration.
- **Spec:** the issue's "Final canvas revision (2026-11-01)": the original worker scope, plus a client method, an adapter key, pd15's ranking line and a judge AUTO-5 can call.
- **Depends on:** #3764 (A3, `deck_area_proposals`, merged in #4241) and #3770 (A7).

### 1. The decision wire

Clef's typed questions already run through `run()` (#3938): `specialist.operation: 'decision'`, checked by `moduleShapes.ts` and called through `services/ai/decide.ts`. This decision adds two things:

- **The quote.** Clef is priced on input tokens only (`catalogue.ts`: `prices: { tokens_in }`). A decision's answer is bounded JSON (`SPECIALIST_OUTPUT_MAX`, 8,192 characters). So `holdQuoteMicro` holds the guard's output slice for that answer, not for an output allowance, and the hold moves with the input alone. Before this change, a larger `maxTokens` raised a decision's hold through the guard's share.
- **What it charged.** `decide()` returns the run's usage row (`usageLogId`), so a caller can read the charge. When that is not recorded, the route says null, never 0.

### 2. The judge (`services/decks/judge.ts`)

- **Candidates.** The judge's candidates are the `decide` kind's models in #3721's table (`modelUseCases.ts`, from D760's catalogue), lead first, each one HQ offers. No model id is written in the judge's code.
- **The family rule.** It is dk-auto AUTO-5's rule, with `critic.ts`'s `familyOf`. The judge is the first candidate whose family is none of the writers'. Writers are read from the run's own steps (`model_used`, else `model`).
  - A writer whose family cannot be read refuses the judge (`generator_unknown`), so the rule is never skipped.
  - A run with any writer of the judge's family is refused (`same_family`) before anything runs.
  - An answer that came back from a writer's family, through a fallback, is not shown and was charged (`answered_same_family`), as the critic does.
- **Blind labels (`gridJudge`).** One `choice` question with options "Variant A", "B" and "C". The order is drawn from the proposal's uid, so it is stable for one proposal and varies across proposals. Neither a variant's number nor its model reaches the judge. Each variant's words, and the card's own words for the area, are marked as data, never an instruction.
- **What can be ranked.** Only words can be compared: a text variant, or a chart's title and series. An image or a records element is not ranked, and fewer than two such variants is `nothing_to_rank`.
- **`judgeJson`.** Clef's answer is mapped back through the labels.
  - The chosen variant ranks first. The others rank by Clef's probabilities.
  - A probability Clef did not send is null, and that variant is "Not ranked". There is never a guessed rank.
  - The score is the chosen variant's probability.
- **Agreement with the panel.** The panel is the free checks every text variant already carries: fits its area, reads in seconds, and the deck's contrast. Its pick is the variant passing the most checks; a tie, or no checks at all, is no pick. The reply gives `{ best, agrees, line }`, with `agrees` null when the panel has no pick.

### 3. The route (`routes/deck_judge.ts`)

The route self-mounts at `/api/decks`.

- **Endpoints.**
  - `POST /api/decks/areas/:uid/judge/quote` runs nothing.
  - `POST /api/decks/areas/:uid/judge` ranks.
- **Who.** The deck's writers. Anyone else, or a proposal that does not exist, gets 404, so a uid tells a stranger nothing. A proposal that is no longer open gets 409 `not_open`.
- **What it never does.** Ranking accepts nothing, and the client never sends a model.
- **Rate limit.** The ranking is under its own `deck_judge` bucket (30 a minute), not `deck_generate`, so ranking does not use up generation runs.
- **Contract.** `contracts/deckJudge.schema.json`, row `judge` (D1511).

### 4. AUTO-5: the judge as a function

`judgeArea(deps, area)` is what the one-click critique loop calls.

- **Inside the run's one quote.** Given `perJudgeMicrousd` and `budgetMicrousd`, it runs only while its hold fits in what is left of the quote. A charge counts at most as the judge's hold, and an unknown charge counts as the whole hold.
- **What the loop gets back.** A score to hold against `JUDGE_THRESHOLD` (0.5), with `clears`. The run then either clears the bar or reports what it could not fix.
- **The route's case.** The route passes neither figure: its run is its own hold.

### 5. The screen

- **Client.** `frontend/src/lib/api/deckJudge.js` (`deckJudgeApi.quote`, `.rank`), indexed by `build-api-index.mjs`.
- **Adapter key.** `SURFACES.judge = { issue: 3783, what: 'Rank the variants' }`, swapped to `through(...)` in the same PR because its route merges with it.
- **pd15's line.** In `AreaResults.jsx`, every variant of an area with two or three variants reads a greyed "Ranked — of m". The one Rank control works in two steps:
  1. "Rank the variants" reads the quote: the judge's model, of another family than the writer's, and what it holds.
  2. Rank runs it. Each variant then reads "Ranked n of m", and the line under the area gives the panel's agreement, what was charged ("Not recorded" when not recorded) and "Accept stays yours".
  A refusal shows its sentence. A Worker without the route reads "Ranking · Coming later".
- **No model control in the results view.** The judge is the AIRail's Vision and checks group's (`deckRailGroups.js`, which now names "Rank the variants").

### 6. What is left

A `DK` row for the judge is a proposal to that plan, not an edit to the generated rows. Until it lands, the Vision and checks group names the job but offers no separate pick for it.
- **Ensembles** stay out of scope (C3, #3798).
