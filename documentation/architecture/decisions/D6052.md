## D6052 — Invite a pipeline investor to Axal through the Contacts invite (#6052)

**Owner decision (2026-11-16):** reuse the Contacts invite. There is one invite path and one audit
record. No call-specific invite exists.

### The Worker

- `POST /api/contacts/invite` (`routes/contacts.ts`) takes a `prospect_id` as well as the founder's own
  invite. With a `prospect_id`, it invites that `raise_prospects` row by email:
  - **Who:** the founder who owns the venture (`canInviteProspect`, `services/prospectInvites.ts`).
    Staff and support sessions are refused as `admin_read_only` (D1191). A co-founder member, a Spin-Out
    Lab account, another founder and another venture's prospect are refused before anything is written.
  - **Address:** the prospect's own address. A typed address fills a prospect that has none and never
    replaces one it has (`prospect_email_differs`). An address with a verified investor account behind it
    is refused (`already_on_axal`): that investor can already be chosen.
  - **Record:** each attempt writes one `raise_prospect_invites` row: venture, prospect, `invited_by`,
    `invited_at`, the lower-cased address and `email_sent`. It also writes one `activity_logs` row,
    `raise_prospect_invited`, with no address. Both rows go in the same batch. The prospect's contact is
    reused, or created and linked as `contact_id`. Its history says the invitation went out only when the
    email left.
  - **Rate limit:** the new `contact_invite_send` bucket (`middleware/rateLimit.ts`) allows 20 invitations
    an hour per founder and fails closed. It covers the whole route. A prospect also waits
    `PROSPECT_INVITE_GAP_HOURS` (24) after a sent invitation (`invite_recent`, 429). A failed send blocks
    nothing.
- `GET /api/contacts/raise-prospects/invites?project_id=` returns each prospect's `has_email`,
  `invited_at` (latest sent) and `joined_at`, plus `can_invite` and its `reason`. It returns no address.
  The venture's owner and accepted members can read it, through the raise pipeline's D1191 scope.
- **Linking on acceptance:** this read stamps `accepted_user_id` and `accepted_at` on each sent invitation
  whose address now has an account (`linkAcceptedInvites`). Every sign-up path (password, Google, deck
  share, branch invitations) creates users, and none had to change. An invitation that never left links
  nothing.
- **The audience is unchanged.** A call still chooses only an active investor whose KYC is approved,
  matched by the prospect's address (`pipelineInvestors`, `services/roundCalls.ts`). The NDA and data-room
  gates are unchanged. Inviting someone, or their joining, adds nobody to the audience.
- Migration `202611161518_6052_raise_prospect_invites.sql` creates the table. `invited_at` joins
  `check-timestamp-comparisons`' column list because it is compared against a bound clock.

### The page (CLS5)

The audience step of the new-call sheet (`RoundCallSheet.jsx`) reads the invitations alongside the
matches. Each pipeline investor who is not on Axal shows one of these:

- **Invite to Axal:** opens a small form. If the pipeline holds no address, the form asks for one. Send
  invitation sends it.
- **Invited · date:** after a send that left, or as read from the Worker.
- **Joined · not verified yet:** the investor has an account but is not a verified investor yet.

The checkbox stays disabled until the investor is a verified investor. They then appear under the chosen
pipeline on the next read. If the viewer cannot invite, the button is disabled with the Worker's reason. A
failed read says the invitations are unreadable and offers Try again. CLS5 and RA-22 draw no invite flow,
so the form reuses the sheet's buttons and inputs.
