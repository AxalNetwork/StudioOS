### One source for figures, explicit scope for explanations

`planCatalogue.ts` owns plan identity and sellability. Stripe's D1 mirror owns
prices; `planAllowance.ts` owns AI funding and `storagePlans.ts` owns storage.
No price, storage quantity or AI allowance is added here. The public
`plans-and-packs` read now adds `catalogue.entitlements` to each plan and the
complete catalogue. Investor defaults are projected from `INVESTOR_QUOTAS`,
the same object the request and colleague-seat gates use. They describe
account defaults, not a member's overrides or an Institutional agreement.

`pricingEntitlements.js` shares the reviewed explanations between Pricing's
FAQ, Checkout's existing summary and Help's Account/Billing guide. Paid cards
and the pricing-origin registration summary identify the price as per licence.
Research search/export allowances remain unpublished. Human services and
external fees require a separate quote, with no included support SLA or
onboarding bundle; a failed quota projection is unreadable,
never zero or unlimited. No entitlement, payment or access gate changes.

### Owner decisions, 2026-11-15

Recorded on [#4653](https://github.com/AxalNetwork/StudioOS/issues/4653#issuecomment-6896110094):

1. Keep the four Institutional colleague seats; no extra-seat purchase until
   separately approved. The displayed count remains derived from enforcement.
2. Plans include software/booking access. Human services and external fees
   require a separate agreed quote with jurisdictions and charges stated.
3. Publish the enforced room/request capacities. Source coverage/freshness,
   search/export and reuse promises await an approved data contract.
4. List verified support channels, with no response-time SLA or included
   onboarding bundle. Help/Contact and member tickets exist in current routes;
   the public-ticket privacy limitation is disclosed with those channels.
5. Keep the alpha label and describe verified live capabilities. Planned or
   unverified capabilities are not guaranteed by a plan.

These settle the five policy questions; they do not supply a research data
contract, a service-specific quote or live billing acceptance. Public read
metadata and shared Pricing/Checkout/Help copy reflect these policies.
