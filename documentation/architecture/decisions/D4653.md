### One source for figures, explicit scope for explanations

`planCatalogue.ts` owns plan identity and sellability. Stripe's D1 mirror owns
prices; `planAllowance.ts` owns AI funding and `storagePlans.ts` owns storage.
No price, storage quantity or AI allowance is added here. The public
`plans-and-packs` read now adds `catalogue.entitlements` to each plan and the
complete catalogue. Investor defaults are projected from `INVESTOR_QUOTAS`,
the same object the request and colleague-seat gates use. They describe
account defaults, not a member's overrides or an Institutional agreement.

`pricingEntitlements.js` shares the reviewed explanations between Pricing's
FAQ, Checkout's existing summary and Help's Account/Billing guide. Paid cards
and the pricing-origin registration summary identify the price as per licence.
Unknown research search/export allowances and human-service/support terms
remain explicitly unspecified; a failed quota projection is unreadable,
never zero or unlimited. No entitlement, payment or access gate changes.
