## D6958 — Sign In v2 built: three choices first, the Email methods inside, a desktop card and a phone sheet (#6958)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-16 from the Sign In v2 canvas (D6956, `design/incoming/Sign In v2 Page.dc.html`, boards SGN-00 to SGN-14), on top of Sign in with Apple (D6957). Desktop, iOS and Android verification is #6959.
- **Files:**
  - `frontend/src/pages/LoginPage.jsx`: the steps;
  - `frontend/src/lib/loginSteps.js`: pure step logic and the Apple error copy;
  - `frontend/src/components/auth/SignInSurface.jsx` and `signInSurface.css`: the card and the sheet;
  - `AuthShell`'s opt-in `signInScene`.

### What the page does now

- **First view.**
  - The heading is "Sign in to Axal VC". For a kept destination it is still "Sign in to continue to Grow" with the area's sentence (#4579).
  - Three buttons follow, in this order: Continue with Email, Continue with Apple, Continue with Google.
  - Below them sits one quiet "New to Axal VC? Create an account" link, which keeps `next=` (#4578, #4579).
  - The page has no field and no Turnstile, and no request is sent before a press. The only exceptions are the two env-only config reads at idle (Google's window, D722; Apple, D6957).
- **Apple** shows only when `/api/auth/apple/config` answers `configured`, so a deployment without Apple never shows a button that cannot work. That is the D6956 ⚑ proposal.
  - Its press sends the whole tab to `/api/auth/apple/start`.
  - The answer returns as `?apple_error=` with our own sentence (`APPLE_ERROR_COPY`), and `next=` is kept.
- **Google** keeps its window and redirect fallback (D722) unchanged. It is always rendered, and it no longer reads an address typed in the Email branch, because the provider launches from the first view, independent of any draft.
- **The Email branch.** Opening it sends nothing and starts no device prompt. It offers:
  - **Email me a sign-in link.** Fields: address, Turnstile, Send. Then "Check your email", with a resend cooldown of 60 s, Change address (Back) and Use a different method.
  - **Use a passkey.** Shown only where the browser has WebAuthn. It starts the existing WebAuthn sign-in on that press, and a discoverable passkey needs no address. A cancellation returns to the methods and says so. Any other failure stays on the step with "Try passkey again".
  - **Use an authenticator code.** Fields: address, a labelled "6-digit code" with one-time-code autofill, paste and a Paste button, and Turnstile. A recovery code is accepted in the same field, as `/api/auth/login` already allows. Recovery stays one link away (`/auth/recover`).
  - A `?magic_error=` return opens the link step with the retry sentence. `inactive` opens the first view.
- **Turnstile** is mounted only with the link and code forms, in `interaction-only` mode, and is removed with them. A token never outlives its widget. If the script never arrives, the link step says a link can still be sent. The code step offers the link instead, because `/api/auth/login` fails closed without Turnstile.
- **History.** Each step is `?step=…` with `next=` kept, so the in-card Back, the browser's Back and Android's back gesture all go one step up. A reload of `sent` or `passkey` reopens the step that starts that action, never the action itself.
- **The typed address** is kept for the whole visit. **One-time codes** are cleared when the code step is left.
- **Focus.**
  - A step change moves focus to the new heading (`tabindex=-1`).
  - Back returns focus to the control that opened the step.
  - Escape inside a step is Back.
  - Errors are `role=alert`; cancellations and fallbacks are `role=status`.
  - One polite live region carries "Sending link…", "Waiting for your passkey…", "Waiting for Google…" and similar.
- **Phone (below 640px).**
  - The sheet is open on arrival, rises over 280 ms (a 120 ms fade under reduced motion), has a rounded top and sits over a flat scrim. The skyline stays visible above it.
  - It is a modal dialog with focus contained in it.
  - Close ("Close sign-in") reveals a "Sign in" button above the home indicator that reopens the sheet, and focus moves both ways.
  - The sheet is limited to the visual viewport and scrolls inside itself, and a focused field is scrolled into view, so the field and its button stay above the keyboard.
  - Inputs are 16px, so iOS does not zoom.
  - Desktop keeps one centred card with no entrance animation.
- **AuthShell** gains `signInScene`, which provides the artwork full-bleed under a flat scrim, the wordmark, and the page's own layout. Registration and onboarding do not pass it and are unchanged.

### Left as it was

- Server-side behaviour of every method: magic-link limits and the 15-minute single-use link, `/api/auth/login`'s Turnstile and TOTP or recovery checks, passkey options with or without an address, Google's rules (D722), Apple's (D6957).
- The open question on D6956 — whether `magic/start` should also check Turnstile on the server — is outside this change.
