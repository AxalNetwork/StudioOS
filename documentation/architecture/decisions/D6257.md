### Rules

- **Three sources, three counts.** Team requests, curated matches and asks are counted in their own sections and never added together. Team requests use no credit; accepting a match uses 1; an ask uses one of the quarter's asks.
- **Nothing unreadable is drawn as 0.** A failed who-knows read is Unavailable with Try again ("This is not a claim that nobody does"), never "nobody knows them". A refused one is Refused. A failed reminders read makes the Open reminders column "? Could not be read" and the detail's card Unavailable with Try again, never None. A failed company read keeps the D6258 wording and adds "Your ties still show in Relationships, without their company."
- **No touch is Not recorded, never cold.** Latest touch with no logged touch reads "— Not recorded".
- **An ask is offered only when it can name someone.** It names a founder or a startup on Axal that one of the investor's own ties links (`introTarget`). The sheet sends nothing to the target at any step.
