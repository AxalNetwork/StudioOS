## D4636 part 3 — Partner reads narrowed to seats, and a GP of record's fund positions (#4636)

- **Status:** Built, part 3, 2026-11-13 (Worker only; no migration, no frontend change).
- **Scope:** the owner's answers of 2026-11-13 to the three judgement calls in D4636-p2 §3.
- **Reuses:** `getMemberProjectIds` (`services/projectAccess.ts`), the rule D4636's update table and `portfolio_updates.ts` use for a partner: the ventures where the caller has a `project_members` row with `status = 'accepted'`. `fundManageScope` (`services/fundGpAccess.ts`), through `managedFundIds`, which moves beside it from `lp_reports.ts` so the LP report reads and the positions reads list a caller's funds with one function. No new access predicate is written.

### 1. The owner's answers (2026-11-13)

1. **Partner reads narrow to their seats.** `GET /api/portfolio/health`, `GET /api/portfolio/health/:uid` and the coverage dashboard (`GET /api/portfolio/coverage`) show a partner only the ventures where they hold an accepted seat, matching D4636's partner row. This replaces T14's and Task #18's every-venture rule.
2. **A fund's GP of record sees their fund's positions** in `/api/positions*`. The fund-scoped arm uses the `requireFundGp` scope, alongside D1057's held scope.
3. **Positions stay company-scoped for holders**, unchanged.

### 2. The rule per read

| Read | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/portfolio/health` | Admin and partner: every venture. Investor: related ventures. Founder: their own. | Admin: every venture. Partner: the ventures where they hold an accepted seat, plus whatever another licence they hold shows them (investor's related ventures or founder's own). Investor and founder unchanged. A partner with no seat gets the empty response (200), as their update list is empty. |
| `GET /api/portfolio/health/:uid` | As the list; outside scope 403 | As the list; a venture without an accepted seat is 403 to a partner |
| `POST /api/portfolio/health/recompute/:uid` | Reads the same scope | Follows the list's scope, so a partner recomputes only a seated venture |
| `GET /api/portfolio/coverage` | Admin and partner: every venture; `fund_id` through `requireFundGp` | Admin: every venture. Partner: seated ventures only, with or without `fund_id` (the fund filter still goes through `requireFundGp` first). No seat: `companies: []`, 200. |
| `GET /api/positions` | Admin: every position. Investor: every round in companies held through a confirmed commitment (D1057). | Unchanged for admin and holders. Adds the GP arm: the rounds attributed (`portfolio_positions.fund_id`) to the funds `fundManageScope` returns for this request: institutional tier, GP of record, under the active company. A company the caller reaches only as GP is summed over those funds' rounds, carried at those funds' marks or a firm-level one (below). The response adds `fund_scope` (`rule: 'gp_of_record'`, `fund_ids`), `null` when the caller runs no fund. |
| `GET /api/positions/marks` | Marks of held companies | Plus, for a company reached only as GP, the marks of the funds the caller runs and the firm-level marks (`fund_id IS NULL`). Another fund's marks are not listed. |
| `GET /api/positions/analytics` | Over held companies | Plus the GP arm's rounds, the distributions of the funds the caller runs, and the GP arm's carrying values. |
| `GET /api/positions/kpi-compliance` | Held companies | Plus the companies where a fund the caller runs holds a round. Whether a company reported is a company fact, read as before. |
| `GET /api/positions/:projectUid` | Held company, else 403; investor projection, admin whole row | A held company is read as before (every round, mark and distribution). A company reached only as GP: that fund's rounds, the firm-level and those funds' marks, and those funds' distributions, in the **investor projection** (no internal row id, recording account or closing line), with the company's cap-table snapshot as a holder reads it. Anything else 403. |

**A company the caller both holds and reaches as GP reads as held**, so holders keep exactly D1057's company-scoped view (answer 3).

**Carrying value for a company read as GP.** `latestProjectMarks`' fund scope, per fund the caller runs, merged by the same precedence: the newer effective date wins; on one date a fund's own mark beats a firm-level one; two of the caller's funds with marks on one date are `fund_marks_conflict`, as in the firm scope. Held companies keep the firm scope these reads always used.

**The projection.** The narrower of the two options: a GP reads the company-facing fields an investor reads, not the whole row. The whole row stays with the admin who records positions.

**The licence gate is unchanged.** These reads still require `canViewLpData` (admin or an investor licence); the GP arm widens what an admitted investor reads, not who is admitted. A GP of record without an investor licence is refused as before. *(Superseded for the five position reads by part 4, `D4636-p4.md`: a GP of record without an investor licence reads their own funds' positions.)*

**Every arm is evaluated per request.** A lapsed tier, a reassigned GP of record, a switched active company or a seat no longer accepted applies to the next read.

### 3. Judgement calls

- **Empty, not 403, for a partner with no seat.** It follows the update reads, where a partner with no seat reads an empty list.
- **A partner who holds another licence** reads the union of their seats and that licence's ventures. Before this part such a caller read every venture; the union keeps what the other licence already gave.
- **Coverage is seat-scoped even with `fund_id`.** The fund filter answers which of the fund's reserve-plan companies the caller may see, so a partner who also runs the fund still reads only seated companies.
- **Firm-level marks are shown to the GP arm** with their fund's marks, as the fund page's own valuation read (D4644 fund scope) does. Firm-level rounds (`fund_id IS NULL`) are no fund's and stay out of the GP arm.
