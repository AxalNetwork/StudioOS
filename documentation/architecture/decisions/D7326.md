## D7326 — Verified worldwide fund-manager discovery records (#7326)

**Date:** 2026-11-18. **PR:** #7317. **Slot:** S26, ChatGPT.

The owner requested more worldwide entries in Research > Funds and then asked
to complete the existing PR. Continue its existing branch. The original,
unmerged numbered migration mixed manager records with managers already held
as specific vehicles, reused retired UIDs, and included obsolete names, URLs,
and investment regions in the HQ field. It is replaced before application.

### Decision and evidence

Add 47 independently verified manager/platform discovery records through
`202611181335_7326_research_global_funds.sql`. The source snapshot is
`attached_assets/research-global-funds-2026-11-18.json`: every candidate has its
official website, supporting first-party URLs, verification date, identity
aliases, and a short explanation of what was established. Forty-three original
candidates are explicitly excluded because served manager/vehicle records
already cover them. Sawari Ventures and Icehouse Ventures add independently
verified Egyptian and New Zealand bases after the duplicate check.

These are manager-level records. They do not assert a specific fund vehicle,
an open fundraise, new-investment availability, a ticket range, fund size,
vintage, investment geography, stage, sector or LP commitment. The migration
leaves those unrecorded fields NULL. Zero EIF/EIFO storage flags with no LP
evidence continue to present as unknown through the existing service.

Headquarters/base countries are recorded only when the manager's own material
supports them. An office list, investment market, legal domicile or
administrator address alone does not establish headquarters. Twenty-two
records have supported HQ geography covering 13 country/territory codes;
25 have unknown HQ. Evidence notes retain qualifications on older statements.
This does not describe the overall directory's country coverage or claim that
the new list is exhaustive. The names and links span North America, Latin
America, Europe, Asia, Israel, MENA, Africa and Oceania.

The shared source remains `cite only`, with no assertion of permission to
republish website content. Its published URL opens the committed evidence
manifest, so the aggregate source footer is neither a self-link to Axal's
directory nor an arbitrary manager's URL. Every row also retains the official
manager website and source URL. No third-party directory or website prose is
copied into this compilation.

### Preservation and duplicate handling

`scripts/import-global-research-funds.py` produces the SQL deterministically
from the committed manifest. `--check` checks byte-for-byte reproducibility.
Use a new dated migration for subsequent imports or corrections once this one
has been applied.

The migration uses `axal-`-prefixed identities for the independent compilation.
Some legacy rows with matching names belong to a withheld source. They retain
their original identity, data and source restriction. The new entries use only
independently verified facts and do not relabel or expose those withheld rows.

Before inserting, the migration suppresses a candidate if a served record
matches a known normalized name alias, a manager/vehicle UID alias, or an exact
official host. A name or concatenated UID may also match an alias followed by
its explicitly recorded `fund_number`, with an optional literal `Fund` before
that number. This covers older vehicles such as Golden Ventures V without
guessing Roman/numeric suffixes in distinct manager names such as Norrsken22.
An audit of historical served vehicles also excludes verified legacy identities
and named strategies, including SOSV Investments and TLCOM's TIDE Africa Fund I;
the exclusion records retain their official supporting evidence.
URL schemes, `www`, case, paths, query strings, fragments and
ports are normalized; similar-looking hosts are not equal. It also excludes
any candidate UID recorded as retired. `INSERT OR IGNORE` is a final primary-key
guard and preserves any later enrichment on replay. Source reviews also use
`INSERT OR IGNORE`, preserving a later `not allowed` verdict.

Malaysia is added to the existing country reference table. Gobi's explicitly
documented Hong Kong / Malaysia base receives a complete two-target HQ override
only when no existing manual mapping or explicit unmapped pin exists. No
country rule, manual decision, API contract or UI flow is replaced.
