### 1. The answers

- **OD3 (A): a referral partner is a member HQ adds.**
  - HQ adds the member to `referral_partners`, with a segment.
  - It is not a new account role, and not every code holder. A partner deal's `PART-` code stays on Partner invitations (D923), so `routes/auth.ts`'s `PART-` branch is untouched.
  - The member's own referral code (`users.referral_code`) is the code that earns.
  - **Segments:** Event organisers, Venture scouts and Deal makers (`event_organisers`, `venture_scouts`, `deal_makers`).
  - An admin or Super Admin account is refused (409 `admins_cannot_refer`).
  - **The facts strip** (`FACTS`, returned by the list and the summary):
    - Who: "Members HQ adds as referral partners, in one of three segments…".
    - Join: "By invitation. HQ adds the partner; their own referral code earns."
    - Terms: "Standard commission rules, or custom terms the Super Admin approves and the partner signs."
- **O-TERMS (A, no bonuses).**
  - The Super Admin approves custom terms, behind the step-up bar.
  - Elevation comes first, as in the settlements route, so a plain Admin gets 403 `super_admin_required` and is never asked for a code.
  - A commission paid under terms pays no L2 or L3 bonus. The signed terms and their caps state everything HQ pays for an introduction.
- **O-MONEY (A).**
  - Accrued and paid figures are the Super Admin's: on the list, the summary and the series.
  - A plain Admin's read carries `{ available: false, reason: 'hq_reads', message: 'HQ reads these figures.' }` in their place, never a 0.
  - The rules read stays `requireSuperAdmin`, as today.
  - A terms row's own amounts are not earnings. HQ admins draft the terms, so they read them.
- **OD4 is open.**
  - Only `referral_kyc_approved` has an event (`routes/kyc.ts`).
  - The rules read now says so with `fires`.
  - Terms may name only a rule that fires. Any other trigger is 400 `trigger_unknown`.

### 2. Migration 563

- **`referral_partners`:** one row per member (`user_id` unique), with the segment, a note, who added them and when.
- **`referral_partner_terms`:** the issue's fields, and who and when for each step.
  - `status` allows PG8's eight steps.

- **`commissions`:**

  - `reversed_amount_cents` and `reversed_at` are declared here at last. Only dev and preview added them (`services/referralCommissions.ts`).
  - Their two entries leave `scripts/runtime-schema-declared-baseline.json`, whose reason asked for a migration number (D359).
  - **Production was read before writing the `ALTER`s** (read-only, 2026-10-26): `commissions` has neither column, and none of the three tables exists. So 563 applies cleanly.

**Refusals,** each a `refuse` code:
- **Triggers:**
  - `trigger_amount_ambiguous` (flat or share, never both and never neither);
  - `trigger_unknown`;
  - `trigger_share_unpaid` (a share of an identity check, which carries no amount);
  - `trigger_repeated`, `triggers_required` and `trigger_amount_invalid`.
- **Fields:** `invalid_dates`, `invalid_cap`, `invalid_currency` and `invalid_sectors`.
- **Order:** `terms_overlap`, `terms_not_draft`, `terms_not_submitted`, `terms_not_approved`, `terms_contract_missing`, `terms_not_active` and `terms_changed`.
- **Approval:** `super_admin_required`.

**Each step names the status it read** in its UPDATE, so a raced step changes nothing and answers `terms_changed`.

**One set of terms at a time.** Submit and approve refuse dates that overlap another of the partner's live rows (submitted, approved, sent, signed or active, not expired) with 409 `terms_overlap`. A later, non-overlapping set may wait its turn.

**The send.** The send is today's envelope send. `envelopeHandler` now takes the input from the caller.
- The input is built from the terms: their `contract_slug`, the `template_version` given, and the partner on record as the signer (their account's email and name). A caller cannot name another signer.
- The published template, the preview hash (409 `preview_changed` when the send does not match the preview), the provider and the audit row are checked exactly as on a direct send.
- On success the row stores `envelope_id` and reads `sent`.
- The template's wording is not changed here (the issue's "Not in this part"). Merge fields pass as `fields`.

**Every write is logged** through `logAdminAction`:
- `referral_partner.added` and `referral_partner.updated`;
- `referral_terms.created`, `.updated`, `.submitted`, `.approved`, `.sent` and `.ended`.

They are listed for Referral partners in `PROGRAM_ACTIVITY_ACTIONS`. A terms row is named by its `uid`, a partner change by its segment, and never by the person.

### 4. Signed terms take effect

- **The completion hook.** E-sign completion (`routes/esign.ts`), after the partner-deal activator and in its own `try`, calls `onReferralTermsEnvelopeCompleted`.
  - The terms row that names the envelope gets `signed_at` and reads `signed`.
  - A second completion changes nothing.
- **State is read, not swept.** A signed row reads `active` from `starts_on`, and `expired` after `ends_on`.
  - Every reader computes this: `effectiveState`, or the same `CASE` in SQL with today bound.
  - The engine uses the same predicate, so a read and a payment never disagree, and nothing runs at midnight.

### 5. The engine pays terms first

`fireCommissionEvent` finds the referrer, reads the rule, then reads the referrer's active terms carrying that trigger (`termsPayment`).

**With active terms:**
1. The amount is the trigger's flat amount, or its share of the event's amount.
2. It is capped at `cap_per_intro_cents`.
3. Then it is capped at what `overall_cap_cents` has left after every commission those terms paid, less what refunds reversed.

The commission is written with `terms_id`, the terms' currency and "Under signed terms: …". It stays idempotent on `(user_id, source_type, source_id)`. No L2 or L3 bonus is fired.

**Caps and switches:**
- A spent cap pays nothing, and the standard rule does not step in.
- Terms are a signed contract, so they pay even when HQ has switched the standard rule off.

**Without active terms,** the standard rule pays as before, active rules only, with its bonuses.

**Introductions.** No outcome column was added. An introduction is a `referrals` row whose referrer is the partner (a sign-up with their code), and "converted" means it earned a commission. `referrals.status` holds only `pending` and `converted`. The canvas's Submitted, In conversation, Converted and Closed are Refer & Earn's statuses, another store, so the series reports these two outcomes and says so (`outcomes`).

**Money.**
- Paid is completed payouts (`payouts.status = 'completed'`), never `commissions.status = 'paid'`.
- Accrued is every commission earned, less `reversed_amount_cents`, by currency. It is never summed across currencies.
