## D5980 — Trusted section incident routing (#5980)

Authenticated operations, verified profile or branch operations, and trusted
subsystems use the same bounded event contract. Producers verify identity and
scope before calling the router. The router projects recognized codes and
bounded counters, and discards additional fields.

The fixed audience and section combinations follow D5677's vocabulary and
the D5682 destination inventory. A missing or unmapped combination goes to
internal platform triage, even for count-only events. #5682 can map verified
route families to these section codes when the route inventory lands.

Expected 4xx outcomes and unconfirmed failures go to digest counters.
Confirmed impact makes an episode decision and recovery updates it. Healthy
events with a known scope can be dropped. Correlated and sampled browser
counts can add evidence for a trusted failure. Scope, outcome and episode
eligibility are determined entirely by the trusted observation.

The rules are pure. Dispatch, episode identity, deduplication, and Slack
delivery belong to the producer and outbox slices of #5679.
