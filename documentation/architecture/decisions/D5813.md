## D5813 — Meeting bot: Teams tenant refusals, lobby and capture states in the member's run view (#5813)

- **Status:** part 3 of 5 of #3709 (D1451 §6, "Part 3"), built by S15 on #5813, 2026-11-28, after #5812 (PR #8973) supplied the allowlisted Teams stages. This file is the part 3 record D1451 §6 points to, as D5812 is part 2's; D1451 itself is not edited.
- **Migration:** none. **Switch:** `MEETING_BOT_TEAMS` stays `"off"` everywhere. No live Teams acceptance is claimed here (part 5).
- **Builds on:** D1451 (the Teams join path), D5812 (`TEAMS_STAGES`, `teams_<screen>` in `failure_detail`), D1368 (`failure_detail`, never shown to the member), D1456 (the creator-only bot read's code-only detail), D1455 (one stepper, one failure panel), D5810 and D5811 (the Teams capability contract), D886 B2 (the notice before capture).

### 1. Why

A Teams tenant can refuse an anonymous guest in three ways the bot now recognises (D5812): anonymous join off, detected bots blocked, and a captcha. All three reach the Worker as `failure_code = runtime_failed`, so before this part the member read "Eadwyn AI could not record this call", with the step's generic causes ("the meeting link changed or expired"), and no way to tell that only the organizer, or a fallback, could help.

### 2. What was decided

1. **One member-safe field, `blocked`, on the bot view** (`botView` in `services/meetingBots.ts`, so the Durable Object's reads and `GET /sessions/:id/bot` carry it). It is `guests_off`, `bot_blocked` or `captcha` only when the run is Teams, `failed`, `runtime_failed`, and its stored `failure_detail` is exactly `platform_blocked@teams_anonymous_blocked`, `…@teams_bot_blocked` or `…@teams_captcha` (`TEAMS_BLOCKED`). Anything else is null: an unknown Teams stage, a stage with a `:state` suffix, the lobby timeout (`not_admitted@teams_lobby`, stored as `admission_refused`), a Meet or Zoom run, an upper-cased or padded value. This is the stated exception to D1368's "never shown to the member": a fixed word derived from the code, never the code itself.
2. **Raw diagnostics keep their existing gate.** The creator-only read still carries D1456's pattern-filtered `failure_detail` for HQ and agents; no screen draws it, and another member's read is `404` with nothing in it. Page text, links and passcodes were never stored (D1368's shape check) and are not added.
3. **The frontend trusts only its own list.** `meetingBotSession.js`'s `botView` maps a known `blocked` on a failed Teams run, not the service's own failure, to its sentence, its one cause, the organizer's action and the fallback; any other value keeps the generic failure. The words:
   - `guests_off`: the meeting does not let anonymous guests join; ask the organizer or their Teams admin to let anonymous users join meetings.
   - `bot_blocked`: the organization blocks meeting bots; ask the organizer's Teams admin to allow Eadwyn AI.
   - `captcha`: Teams asked for a verification challenge, which Eadwyn AI never answers; no organizer action, only the fallback.
   Each says "Nothing was recorded." (capture starts only after admission and the notice, D5812 §2.3).
4. **Honest states for every step.** One line per state from the same view: joining (Teams: a browser guest named Eadwyn AI, microphone and camera off); waiting (Teams: in the lobby, the organizer admits it; every platform: nothing is recorded until it is admitted and has posted its notice); recording (the notice was posted, it is recording); processing; done (saved and processed). A Teams lobby timeout or denial reads "not admitted from the Teams lobby", with the organizer's lobby action. `notice_failed` and `processing_failed` get their own sentences instead of the generic one. Zoom's "Join the call first" keeps its own line.
5. **On a refusal, the fallback and the way back.** The bot card (when it shows failures) and the session page's one failure panel show the organizer's action, the cause in place of the step's generic causes, and the existing routes: "Upload the recording" (`/validate/record/calls/upload`) and "Paste the transcript" (Record a session for the same interview). Every failed join's panel also links "Back to the interview" (`/validate/customer-research/<interview>`), so a refused run never strands the member on the session.
6. **One capability sentence.** `TEAMS_BOT_GUEST` ("joins as a guest with the meeting link… does not need your Microsoft connection") and `TEAMS_BOT_OFF` ("Upload the recording or paste the transcript after the call.") live in `meetingBotSession.js`; the Teams connector page's Eadwyn AI row (D5810, `teamsConnector.js`) and the refusal panel both use them, so connecting Microsoft is never offered as a fix for a tenant refusal.
7. **Settlement is unchanged.** A refused run settles its hold through the supervisor's existing `finish` path; nothing new is charged or refunded. A replayed callback is a duplicate and a later one is `409 bot_finished`, so a run has one terminal state and keeps its first reason; a run the member stopped stays `stopped`. Sending again to the same draft session (nothing was recorded) makes a new run, and the read shows only the newest.

### 3. Canvas departures

No canvas in `design/incoming/` draws a Teams refusal, the lobby line or the fallback links. The build extends `Interview Record Page.dc.html`'s failure panel (`FAILP`) and the D1455 stepper:
- the canvas shows "Detail codes" under the failure details; this build never shows `failure_detail` to the member (the issue's privacy rule), only the stored `failure_code` as before;
- a recognised refusal shows "Cause" with one item instead of "Likely causes";
- "What the organizer can do", the two fallback links and "Back to the interview" are not drawn in the canvas;
- the canvas's waiting line is "Admit it from the call"; Teams reads "in the Teams lobby".
