### 2. The board and the list (ART1, ART2)

- **The board** reads the open stages once: `stage=open`, oldest first, up to 100.
  - **Columns:** Submitted, In review, Changes requested, Ready to publish, Scheduled.
  - **Each column's count** is the whole stage's, from D1291's `counts`, with its oldest wait. The page's rows never set the count.
  - **A card:** its title, author, sector, words, wait, open comments and rounds.
  - **Show N more** opens a long column. A stage with more than the page holds links to its list.
  - **Open the next submission** opens the oldest Submitted.
- **The count strip** above both views gives all seven stages, Published and Rejected included. Each stage opens its list.
- **The list** (`?view=list`) sends what its address says.
  - **Search:** title or author.
  - **Stage:** Open, one stage, or Every stage.
  - **Filters:**
    - author and sector, from the rows read;
    - Reviewer: Mine;
    - the submitted range;
    - has open comments;
    - Clear all.
  - **Sort:** D1291's five.
  - **Paging:** totals ("21–40 of 46"), rows per page (20, 50, 100), and page.
  - **Every change is a new address and goes back to page 1.** A value the read would refuse is left out of the address, never guessed.
- **The columns:** Article, Author, Stage, Reviewer, Waiting or due, Words, Comments open, Rounds, Sector.
- **An article opens at `/admin/articles/<id>`,** with the list's address kept behind it. Closing the drawer returns to that list.

**Waits, never guessed:**
- Submitted: "Waiting 4 days", or "submitted time not recorded".
- In review: the reviewer.
- Changes requested: "With the author". No column records when changes were requested, so there is no date.
- Ready: the approval day.
- Scheduled: the time it goes out, in UTC.
- Published: the day.

### 3. The drawer (ART3–ART5)

The drawer is the shared `Lightbox`: in-page, focus held, Escape closes it, and it is a bottom sheet on a phone.

- **Article:** as readers see it, or its Markdown. The rendered view is D1293's `rendered_html`, from `renderMarkdown`, which escapes every character of the author's text before it adds tags, as the reader page's `body_html` is.
- **Comments:** each thread, with its paragraph ("On ¶ 2: …") or "On the whole article", and its replies (the author's marked).
  - Each comment shows who wrote it, when, and whether it was edited.
  - A resolved thread says who resolved it, or that it was not recorded (threads older than migration 636).
  - **Actions:** Reply (`parent_id`), Resolve or Reopen, and Delete…, which asks first in the page (ART6 I).
  - **A new comment** is on the whole article or one paragraph. The anchor is `¶ n: “first words…”`, at most 120 characters, as the author's editor shows anchors.
- **Revisions:** each with its reason, its stage at save, who saved it ("Saved by an account that no longer exists" when gone), and when. Pick two and Compare: D1293's revision text, line by line, removed struck through and added tinted. A failed compare says so with Try again.
- **Details:**
  - author and role, stage, submitted, reviewer, approved by (Not recorded before migration 635), goes out, published;
  - featured, sector, length, address, views (all time);
  - the follower reach of each publish.

  "Views by day are not recorded yet" stays until D1297.

### 4. The dialogs (ART6)

Every change opens `QueueDialog`, 560 wide, saying what it changes. A refusal shows in the dialog with nothing changed.

| Action | Offered on | The dialog |
|---|---|---|
| Start review | Submitted | Makes you the reviewer |
| Approve | Submitted, In review | Ready to publish; the author is told |
| Approve anyway | the Super Admin's own article | A reason, sent as `override_reason` (D1292) |
| Request changes, Reject | Submitted, In review | A reason the author reads |
| Schedule | Ready, Scheduled | A date and a time in the admin's zone, sent as UTC (D484's `articlePublishAt.js`), in the future and at most a year ahead; the current time shown in UTC |
| Clear time | Scheduled | Back to Ready to publish |
| Publish now, Publish anyway | Ready, Scheduled | Live at once; "anyway" takes a reason |
| Unpublish | Published | A reason the author reads (D1293) |
| Feature, Stop featuring | Published, Super Admin only | Up to three |
| Delete comment | Any comment | The author loses it too; Resolve keeps it |

- **The reason rule:** a reason is at least 8 characters, sent trimmed.
- **A click that went stale (ART15d):** a 409 that is not `featured_full` closes the dialog, reads the article again, and says "This article moved to Ready to publish before your click. Nothing else changed."
- **Other refusals** are our sentence by code, never the error's own text.

### 5. The Admin's view and no permission (ART16, ART17)

- **The Admin's view** differs only in what is offered:
  - no Feature;
  - no crumb to HQ › Content.
  - **Their own article:** no Approve, Publish or Schedule, and "You wrote this. A second admin approves an author's own article." (D1292 §2). The canvas's "Ask another admin" would tell the other admins, and no route sends that notice, so it is not drawn.
- **ART17a:** an Admin on HQ › Content's overview (`/admin/content`, `hqOnly`) reads "HQ runs the Content overview. You can still review articles in the Content queue.", with Back to the Content queue (`hqNoticeModel.js`).
- **ART17b:** a member who is not an admin, on any `/admin/articles` address (a notice's link, say), reads "This page is for studio admins. If you wrote this article, open it in your writing workspace.", with Open my articles (`/articles/draft`).
  - It is drawn by `ROUTE_BOUNDARIES` in App.jsx, as Fund administration's refusal is.
  - An admin previewing another role keeps today's redirect.

### 6. The states (ART13–ART15)

| State | What the page says |
|---|---|
| No articles at all | "No articles yet. When a member sends an article for review, it appears here." |
| No match | "No articles match these filters.", with Clear filters and Search every stage |
| Loading | The board's shape and "Reading…" |
| The queue unreadable | "We could not load the queue. The rest of the admin still works." and Try again |
| The counts unreadable | "Could not be read" on each stage, never 0; the rows still stand |
| A drawer block unreadable | "Could not be read. This is not a claim that there are none." |
