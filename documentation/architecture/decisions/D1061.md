The issue names part 2's company read and part 3's frozen figures. Three things were missing from them.

| Added | Why |
|---|---|
| Each period's `report_uid`: the report that met it, null until sent. In the investor's and the founder's reads (`periodsOf`). | A period said it was sent, but not by which report, so Read had nothing to open. |
| Each of the investor's duties carries `holding: { route }`, read from its closing line's route. | RPT13's variants turn on the route. The investor's rows still carry no closing line, holder or name, as D1058 §5 requires. |

### 2. RPT9, Portfolio › Reports

The block is at the top of the investor's Portfolio › Reports (`/portfolio/updates`), above the existing inbox. It reads `GET /api/reporting/portfolio` and the investor's own positions.

- **Four tiles:**
  - Due this month: open periods only;
  - Received this quarter, of those due this quarter;
  - Late, with the expected date;
  - Missing, which says "Nothing came due unsent" when there are none.
- **One row per company the investor signed into:**
  - the holding: its route, and the position's amount when there is one, never "$0 invested";
  - the next due, and the last received;
  - the state of the period that needs the most attention (missing, then late, then due, then upcoming);
  - the last eight periods as the canvas's strip: ✓ ✓● – ◷ ⌀ ○.
- **Search, filters and sort:** filters by State, Kind and Holding; sorts by Due soonest (no next due last), Company and Last received.
- **No score, no rank, no red.** A sent period is green, late and missing are amber, and everything else is neutral.
- **A failed read** says it is not a claim that nothing is due or late.
- **An investor owed nothing** reads why, and the LP note (§5).

### 3. RPT10, the company page

The page reads the investor's position and part 2's company read on their own.

- **Who sees it.** The page draws for a company the investor holds a position in **or** is owed reports by. A holder through an SPV or a fund may have a duty but no position. A company with neither shows nothing of it, as before.
- **The header** adds the holding's route to D1056's holding line.

**Figures:** five small multiples (MRR, Customers, Net burn, Runway, Headcount) from §1's figures read.
- **Line styles:**
  - solid between reported values;
  - **dashed** across a report with no figure, and after the last one, with "not reported";
  - **dotted** for the same period a year earlier when a report carried it;
  - **a ring** on a corrected report's value.
- **View as table** lists each period's value, or "Not recorded" with the founders' reason.
- **Each chart's sentence** is its accessible label: what changed, which periods are not recorded, and which were corrected.
- **No report received yet** says there is nothing to chart. Nothing is estimated, and a missing value is never 0.

**Eadwyn's "What changed since the last report"** comes from frozen figures only:
- each figure the last two reports both carried, with its source and the two periods;
- each period that is late or missing.

It never fills in a figure and never gives advice.

**Reports:**
- the timeline of every period, newest first, with its pill and its due, expected or sent line (with the founders' note);
- Read when sent;
- Ask for this report while late or missing and not yet asked;
- Waive this period until sent or waived.

D1056's list of updates received stays under it, as "Updates you received".

**Terms** (RPT8, read only):
- each duty's rule ("45 days after quarter end"), the figures it names, the document and clause;
- whether it came from a signed envelope or the closing's template;
- "Default, not in the paper" where the paper left a term blank;
- the holding's route.

The investor's Cap table panel (D1027) stays beside it.

### 4. RPT11, the reader, Ask and Waive

**The reader** is a dialog 640 wide, a bottom sheet on a phone. It reads part 3's `GET /reports/:uid`, which records the open.
- the meta line: sent, revision, and "corrected by revision n on [date]" with the reason;
- the periods it meets, and the founders' words;
- **every figure by Grow module** with its source ("Connector · Mercury · shared by the founder", "Typed · entered by the company") and its date;
- **what was left out, and why**, in the founders' words;
- the statements. Each is fetched only when tapped, then handed to the browser to save.

**Ask for this report** and **Waive this period** each open an in-page dialog that says what it changes:
- Ask: "The company is told that you asked. Nobody else sees your request. You can ask once per period."
- Waive: "The record shows it waived by you, and the company sees that on its desk."

Nothing is posted before the press. A refusal, such as "already asked", is drawn as the Worker's own sentence. There is no `alert` or `confirm`.

### 5. RPT13, holdings, and what is not built

- **A syndicate (SPV) holding:** "Reports reach you through the SPV, and only the SPV’s lead can waive a period." The holder of record on a syndicate line is its investor, who is the lead (#2427 owner question 1), so only the lead holds the duty and its Waive.
- **An Axal VC fund holding:** the row says "Feeds the fund’s quarterly LP report", and the page carries the note.
- **A third-party fund holding:** a note that the fund reports to its own investors.
- **An LP:** with no company owing them, Portfolio › Reports says company reports go to the fund and links Axal VC Fund's reporting.

**Not built, and why:**

| Not built | Why |
|---|---|
| An SPV member's own view of a company's reports | A member is not a holder of record. They have no duty and no period, so nothing reaches them to draw. The note says how reports reach them. |
| Saving Eadwyn's lines as the investor's own note | No store keeps an investor's notes. The card says so, and nothing is saved or sent. |
| A report's asks with Reply | Part 3's reports carry no asks (D1059 §4). The reader says questions go to the company. |
| Detecting an LP by their fund records | The LP note shows when no company owes the investor reports. It is worded as "if you are an LP". |
