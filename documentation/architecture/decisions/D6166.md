## D6166 — The founder grants and withdraws a frozen deck version for an investor on a deal (#6166)

- **Status:** built by S23. **Owner answer, 2026-11-16 (on the issue):** a plain control now, in the deck studio, matching the plain read-only viewer chosen for the investor side (#6165). A designed version may replace it later; #3716 governs placement only.

- **Why not a D1587 part file:** D1587 was numbered before D4106, so it is not its issue's number, and a part file is only for a decision numbered by its issue (D5615). This issue's own number holds it.

### What was decided

**"Shared on a deal" sits on Share & analytics (`/raise/pitch/share`), under By investor** (`frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/DeckGrants.jsx`, words in `deckGrantsModel.js`).
- The founder picks one of the venture's deals, read from `GET /api/deals` (the founder's own deals) and narrowed to the open venture. Each deal is "Deal #9 · Active · opened 1 Oct": a deal carries no name of its own.
- **The list** is `deckGrantsApi.grants`: each grant's investor name, firm, version, ticks ("Figures · Sources", or "None of the four"), granted date, and withdrawn date or Live. A row is built field by field, so an investor's note, score or decision cannot reach the screen even if a read carried one. A missing name or firm is "Not recorded". A replaced grant stays listed as withdrawn, as the Worker keeps it.
- **"Share a version…"** opens an in-page dialog: the deal, the investor from the read's `investors` (one per person, since the Worker grants to a person's first firm on the deal), the saved version (newest first), and the four ticks at the Worker's defaults (figures and sources on, notes and bands off). The POST sends exactly the four keys.
- **Replace is said before it is sent.** When the chosen investor has a live grant, the dialog says "[Name] already has v2 on this deal, shared [date]. Sharing replaces it: v2 stops opening for them and v3 opens instead, with the ticks below." and the button reads "Replace and share".
- **Withdraw asks first** and says what the investor loses: they can no longer open the version on their deal page, their deck panel will say the venture has not shared its deck, and the row stays with its withdrawn date.
- **Refusals in the Worker's words.** A refused grant or withdraw keeps its dialog open with the Worker's sentence; a refused or failed grants read shows the sentence with Try again, never "No investor has been given a version". A failed deals read says "Your deals could not be read." with Try again, never "no deal".
- A reader of the studio (an ordinary admin, PD21) sees the list with no Share or Withdraw. No Worker change: the routes and the client were already on main.
