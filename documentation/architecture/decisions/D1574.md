## D1574 — The structured PowerPoint writer: native text, shapes and charts on The Grid, for the download and Google Slides (#3821)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-03 by S08 for #3821, part of #3716 (deck builder v2, Phase E, task E1). It serves pd8b's PowerPoint card, "Keeps your layout".
- **Migration:** none. **Charge:** none. No model runs.
- **Builds on:**
  - D904 (the editable PowerPoint Google Slides uploads);
  - D1112 (the print card);
  - D1533 (decor and the Arrange set);
  - D1534 (image crop and filter, `imageLook.ts`);
  - D1546 (the compliance footnotes);
  - D1603 (the .odp writer and its `boxOf` placement).

### 1. Why

The PowerPoint download was one PNG per slide (`renderDeckPPTXWithImages`), photographed by the headless browser. It looked like the deck but could not be edited. Its words could not be searched or read aloud, and a failed browser shot meant no file at all. pd8b promises "Keeps your layout", so the file has to be editable and laid out as the editor shows it.

### 2. One writer, two exports

`services/decks/pptxEditable.ts` (`writeEditableDeck`) is now both:
- the PowerPoint download, `POST /api/decks/:id/export {format: 'pptx'}`;
- the file the Google Slides export uploads, `POST /api/decks/:id/export/google-slides` (owner question I = a: keep both).

Both read each bound chart series from its store through `boundSeries` (`chartSeries.readSeries`). The PDF is unchanged.

### 3. What the writer does

- **Placement is The Grid's.** Every area sits at `designSystem.boxOf`: margins 112 / 96 / 88 and a 24 px gutter on the 12 × 6 grid. That is the box the editor draws (`gridModel.boxOf`) and the box the .odp writer uses, so PPTX and ODP lay out the same. The print card's 3% padding (`printRect`) is kept only to clip a chart's picture from the print page.
- **Text is text.**
  - Runs are `a:r`.
  - A paragraph's size is set so it fits inside its area (`fitParagraphs`), down to 40% of its size (`FIT_FLOOR`). The estimate is a generous glyph advance with 1.2 leading, against PowerPoint's own insets.
  - The sizes are written into the file, because PowerPoint applies `normAutofit` only once the text is edited.
- **Charts are native** (`services/decks/pptxChart.ts`).
  - Each chart is a `c:chartSpace` part with its numbers in its cache, plus an embedded workbook (`ppt/embeddings/chartN.xlsx`), so "Edit Data" opens a sheet.
  - How the types map:
    - line and projection become a line chart;
    - columns, waterfall and nested bars become clustered columns;
    - stacked becomes stacked columns, and 100% bar becomes percent-stacked columns;
    - ranked bars and funnel become horizontal bars, biggest first.
  - The plotted points are only what the deck holds: typed points, or a bound series' store points cut at the binding's `as_of`.
  - A category a series has no point for is a gap (`dispBlanksAs="gap"`), never a 0.
  - A chart with no point, or of a type with no native counterpart (cohort heatmap, timeline, metric tiles, comparison table, market circles), is the print page's picture of it when the browser is bound, and otherwise its type and series as words.
- **Images** keep D1534's `srcRect` crop, blip filter and wash.
- **Decor.** A shape with a native geometry stays a native shape (D1533). An icon, texture or other drawn item from #3779's library is now its SVG (`decorSvg`), with the tone written as a colour, and a PNG fallback through Office's `asvg:svgBlip` extension. The fallback is a transparent 1 × 1 PNG: the Worker cannot rasterise SVG, so a reader without SVG support shows nothing there rather than a wrong picture.
- **Notes** are each card's, when asked for.
- **Reading order.** Blocks are written top to bottom, then start to end, which is the order a screen reader takes. A card whose areas overlap keeps its own order, because shape order is also stacking order.

### 4. pd8b: "A projection keeps its label in every format"

A chart with a point after today's month is a projection (the editor's `projectionOf`). Every writer heads it through `pptxChart.chartHeading`:
- **PowerPoint:**
  - the native chart is titled "Projection", and each line point past today is dashed;
  - the frame's description reads "Projection · Line chart";
  - the words fallback reads the same.
- **.odp:** the caption under the chart reads "Projection · Line chart".
- **HTML:** the chart's heading reads "Projection · Line chart".

D1546's footnote stays where it was.

### 5. The one-click bar (dk-auto AUTO-4)

- **Cut cards stay out:** `exportDeckOf` → `visibleCards`.
- **Text stays inside its area:** §3.
- **The layout is the editor's:** §3.
- **No 0 for missing data:** a gap in a chart, and "Not recorded" for a metric.
- **"Not recorded".** The writer does not drop a metric with no figure. A one-click deck carries no empty area, because AUTO-4 drops it before the export (D1553's `reflowWithout`). A founder's own deck with an empty metric still reads "Not recorded", as the editor shows it and as D3662's acceptance test requires. The test for this bar exports a complete deck and finds neither "Not recorded" nor a 0.

The D904, D1112, D1533, D3655, D3662 and D895 tests now describe the editable file.

**SSIM ≥ 0.95, from the original spec, is not measured in CI.** It needs a PowerPoint renderer, and CI has none. What CI asserts instead:
- **The geometry is identical:** every area's box equals the editor's.
- **Text is 100% editable:** every word of a complete card is a text run or a chart value, and nothing on it is a picture.
