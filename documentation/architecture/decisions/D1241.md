### 1. New for you (DLS6)

**The read** is `GET /api/deals/flow/inbox`, replacing the pending-invitations read: every way in not yet in the firm's flow, newest first. The ways in are invitation, invitation requested, commitment, introduction, open syndicate and watchlist.

**Each row shows:**
- its way in as a chip, and its date;
- the venture, its sector and the round's word;
- the line that brought it, in the item's own facts. An invitation quotes the studio's message; a room says "You joined this room on [date] and asked to be invited. The studio decides.";
- its scope strip from SCORING's batch.

**Its actions:**
- Add to my deal flow (D1240's dialog, now sending the row's `via_key`);
- Pass;
- Open, only where `can_open` holds;
- Not for me.

An invitation requested waits on the studio, so it only opens.

**What could not be read:**
- A way in that could not be read is named ("Unreadable: your watchlist could not be read. The rest are listed.").
- Thesis matches say why they are not recorded.
- Nothing is a zero, and an empty inbox says where rows come from.

**Not for me** opens a dialog that says it hides the row for the whole firm and is not a pass. It sends the optional reason. There is no undo, because D1238 has no un-dismiss route.

### 2. The stage dialogs (DLS10)

`movesModel.moveKind` decides each move:

| Move | How it is made |
|---|---|
| Forward, to Screening, Diligence or Closing, or off Passed | Immediate, with D1240's Undo toast |
| To an earlier stage | **Move back** dialog: "Move [venture] back to [stage]?", "It keeps its history." The canvas's optional reason is not drawn: D1238's `PATCH` takes none, and its one note would be overwritten. |
| To Commit | **Move to Commit** dialog: "This does not open a vote. Open one on the deal page when you are ready." |
| To Passed | Only through **Pass** |

**Pass on [venture]?**
- **One of the five reasons is required,** in the Worker's order (`dealPassTaxonomy.ts`), with the canvas's sub-lines. Without one, nothing is sent: "Choose why you are passing."
- **When it comes back:**
  - at its next round;
  - or on a date after today.

  The canvas's "a milestone you set" is a date: D1238 stores only that.
- **An optional note.**
- **The box:** "This is your pass. The round stays open for other investors. The studio sees that you passed, not your note." A row that came by invitation adds "The studio that invited you is told you passed."

**Closing without a commitment** is the Worker's `closing_needs_commitment`, in its own words. It shows in the toast after a move, or inside the dialog that made the move, and the card stays where it was.

### 3. The peek (DLS7, DLS7b)

**Opening it:** Details, on every board card, table row and phone card, opens a 560-wide `Sheet`.

**It shows:**
- the stage, with Move to (through the same dialogs);
- the round;
- Open deal page, only where `can_open` holds;
- the scores by scope as bands and confidence words, never a number;
- the facts the flow holds: ask, fit, owner, entered via and days in stage. Valuation cap, minimum check, closing deadline and amount committed so far are on the deal page;
- the firm's note, saved only on Save note through the flow's `PATCH` note;
- Pass, and Add to compare.

**The breakdown (DLS7b).** A score opens SCORING's own breakdown panel (`VentureScorecard`, D1257), which reads `GET /api/venture-scores/deal/:dealId` by its visibility rule. It opens only on a deal the investor may read. Elsewhere the rows are disabled, and a line says why.

### 4. Compare (DLS8, DLS9)

**Reads:** the deal list and the flow (`flowRows('all')`), SCORING's batch and the fit, for the picked ids.

**What it draws:**
- **Venture cards:** each venture keeps one colour (violet, sky, amber, teal), with a dark pair. A card shows the colour, name, sector, stage, round, ask and strip.
- **Grouped bars,** one per venture per scope:
  - a bar's length is the band's rank (Weak 1/3, Fair 2/3, Strong 3/3), never a value;
  - low confidence is hatched;
  - a scope without a score is a dashed line with its word.
- **The summary** speaks in counts of scopes only: "[A] has the strongest band alone on n of 11 scopes", and who has not shared or has scopes without a score.
- **The table:** the same bands, the strongest alone in a row in weight 800 (a tie marks no one), then ask, your stage, days in stage and fit.
- **Export CSV** writes the table in words, built in the page.

**Light and dark** are the same page, with every class carrying its dark pair (DLS9).

**Not drawn:**
- The previous-score tick: the trajectory is founder and staff only.
- Open flags: no investor-scoped read.

### 5. Eadwyn's comparison note

**Draft.** `POST /api/deals/flow/compare-note { deal_ids }` takes two to four ventures.
- Every deal must be one the investor may read (`userReadableDealIds`). One that is not refuses the whole draft (404 `not_shared`).
- **The material** is each venture's name and, per scope, its state, band and confidence word (`stripView`). Never a value: a test plants values and finds none in the prompt.
  - A founder's switch off makes the venture "locked", with no scope.
  - With fewer than two ventures scored, nothing runs (409 `scores_not_recorded`).
- **The run** is on surface `/deals/compare`. It is told never to recommend or advise, and never to write a number but a count of scopes.
- **The check, before anything is shown.** The draft is refused, never shown, if it contains:
  - any number above 11 or a decimal;
  - a percentage;
  - a word of advice (recommend, should, invest in, pass on…).

  Such a draft is 503 `note_ungrounded`. A refused run answers in `refuseRun`'s words; a provider's text never does.

**Save to notes.** The draft comes with an HMAC receipt (15 minutes). `POST /compare-note/keep { receipt }` writes one `research_zone_drafts` row:
- surface `deals/compare-note`;
- scoped to the sorted deal ids;
- `accepted_at` set;
- and one activity row with a count only.

A second save is the same row. Another investor's save, or a stale one, is refused.

**Discard** writes nothing, and **Eadwyn never changes a score.**

**Read back.** `GET /compare-note?ids=` reads the notes this investor saved for that set, in any order. A row failing its signature is counted, never shown.
