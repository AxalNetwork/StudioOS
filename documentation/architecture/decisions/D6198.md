## D6198 — An investor records "I have sent it" without Mercury (#6198)

**Decided by the owner, 2026-11-15: option A.** In the closing room, an
investor records on their own line that they sent their money. The line reads
**Sent, not yet confirmed**, and never Received. Only the receiving side's
evidence (`closing_evidence.side = 'receiving'`, recorded by an admin) turns a
line Received.

**Store.** Migration `202611151545_6198_closing_sender_declarations.sql`
adds `closing_sender_declarations`:
- columns: `line_id`, `declared_by`, `sent_on`, `amount_cents` (integer
  cents), an optional `reference` of up to 40 characters, `withdrawn_at` and
  `created_at`;
- one active declaration per line, enforced by a partial unique index; a
  withdrawn declaration stays as history.

It is its own table because `closing_evidence.kind` is a CHECK list (612),
and a declaration is the investor's word, not a stored record.

**Line status** (`recompute` in `services/closings.ts`):
- withdrawn stays withdrawn;
- active receiving evidence makes it received;
- active sending evidence, or an active declaration, makes it sent;
- otherwise it is open.

Withdrawing a declaration never undoes received evidence.

**Routes** (`routes/closings.ts`). Both require the line's own investor; a
support session is refused.
- **`POST /api/closings/:uid/lines/:lineUid/declaration`**
  `{sent_on, amount_cents, reference?}`, answered `201 {uid, notified}`.
  - Who: only the line's own investor. Another investor, the founder and an
    admin read the line as missing (404).
  - When: the closing must be open, and the line not withdrawn or received.
  - Refused input:
    - a future or invalid day;
    - a non-integer or non-positive amount;
    - a second active declaration;
    - a reference that looks like an account or card number (a run of 8+
      digits, or an IBAN shape), with "Leave out account and card numbers".
  - No bank memo or account number is stored.
- **`POST /api/closings/:uid/lines/:lineUid/declaration/withdraw`** withdraws
  the caller's own active declaration.
- **Events.** `closing_events.event` is also a CHECK list (612), so both
  writes log `evidence_added` and `evidence_withdrawn`, with `source_ref`
  `closing_sender_declaration:<uid>` telling them apart from stored evidence.
  The event, the row and the status recompute are one batch.
- **The studio is told.** After commit, the closing's owner (else its
  opener) gets one in-app notice linking to the admin closing page. A failed
  notice never undoes the declaration.
- **The read.** `readClosing` returns each line's `sender_declarations`.

**Room** (`pages/closing/RecordRoom.jsx`, `roomModel.js`). On the "I have
sent it" step of an open closing:
- "Record that I sent it" opens three fields: the day, the amount in the
  closing's currency (parsed by `lib/money.js`) and a short optional label.
- Once recorded, the room shows what was recorded, "Sent, not yet confirmed",
  and "Withdraw what I recorded".
- `ROOM_WAITS.sentWord` no longer says no route records it.

**Verified.**

  - the second-declaration refusal;

  - account-like references, bad days and bad amounts refused, with nothing

- Removing the own-line check fails it. Five closing tests load the
