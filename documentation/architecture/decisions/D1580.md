### The question

pd38 draws a rehearsal: choose a pitch length (2, 3 or 5 minutes), speak through the deck while each card's time runs against its share ("0:42 of 0:30", amber when over), with the microphone's level and the card's notes as a checklist. The line under it reads "Transcript · Holds up to $0.02 · you pay what it uses" and "Your audio is turned into text. The audio is deleted afterwards unless you keep it."

pd39 draws the report: a table per card (Card · Time · WPM · Filler words · Pauses · Notes covered, amber over budget), a figure check ("You said ‘$2M ARR’. Your record says $1.4M · Linked · Measured"), a "Likely questions" slot for #3829, and "Keep the audio", off by default.

### What was decided

**One run, counted in code.**
- The recording is transcribed by one `run()` of the `transcribe` task on the rail option `transcribe:rehearsal` (page "Founder · Raise · Pitch deck"). The model is the member's pick for that option in the AIRail; the run is quoted (`quoteRun`) and the balance checked before anything is held.
- The quote holds for the longest recording read: twice the pitch, at most ten minutes. The run is charged on the longer of the stated length and the bytes' length at 32 kbps (the recorder's bitrate), so a short claim never underpays.
- Everything in the report is counted from the transcript's segments and the client's card marks (`services/decks/rehearsal.ts`), never estimated by a model:
  - **time** per card, from the marks; a card shown twice adds both stretches;
  - **budget**: the pitch shared evenly across the cards, whole seconds each (`cardBudgetsMs`, the helper #3828's timing shares);
  - **words a minute** from the segments whose middle falls on the card; a card never shown has none, not 0;
  - **filler words**, exact counts of a fixed list (um, uh, erm, er, ah, hmm, "you know", "I mean", basically, literally). "Like" and "so" are not counted: they are words with meanings;
  - **pauses**, gaps of two seconds or more between two segments on the card;
  - **notes covered**: each line of the card's notes is covered when 60% of its content words were said while the card was up.

**The figure check reads only linked figures.**
- A figure said on a card is read with D1555's `lineFacts` and compared with the deck's metric blocks bound to a slot (`deckConsistency.boundFigures`), that card's own first. One that disagrees (outside `agrees`'s 2%) is reported with the record's value and grade.
- Typed figures, "Not recorded" bindings and forward-looking lines ("by 2027 we project …") are never compared.

**The audio is held, then deleted.**
- The audio is stored in R2 while the run happens and held for an hour after the report (`services/decks/rehearsalAudio.ts`), so the founder can turn "Keep the audio" on. The cron's five-minute step deletes every unkept audio past its hour; turning the switch off deletes it at once. The transcript and the report stay.
- A failed run deletes the audio and stores nothing.

**Whose it is.**
- A rehearsal is its maker's alone: every read is by deck, user and uid. Rehearsing needs write access to the venture; a support session is refused.

**The swap.**
- `deckV2Adapter.rehearse` is swapped to `through(() => deckRehearsalsApi.list(deckId))`, with the contract `deckRehearsals.schema.json`. Present's Rehearse panel runs, reads and keeps through the same client.
