### The question

pd38 draws a rehearsal: choose a pitch length (2, 3 or 5 minutes), speak through the deck while each card's time runs against its share ("0:42 of 0:30", amber when over), with the microphone's level and the card's notes as a checklist. The line under it reads "Transcript · Holds up to $0.02 · you pay what it uses" and "Your audio is turned into text. The audio is deleted afterwards unless you keep it."

pd39 draws the report: a table per card (Card · Time · WPM · Filler words · Pauses · Notes covered, amber over budget), a figure check ("You said ‘$2M ARR’. Your record says $1.4M · Linked · Measured"), a "Likely questions" slot for #3829, and "Keep the audio", off by default.

### What was decided

**One run, counted in code.**
- The recording is transcribed by one `run()` of the `transcribe` task on the rail option `transcribe:rehearsal` (page "Founder · Raise · Pitch deck"). The model is the member's pick for that option in the AIRail; the run is quoted (`quoteRun`) and the balance checked before anything is held.
- The quote holds for the longest recording read: twice the pitch, at most ten minutes. The run is charged on the longer of the stated length and the bytes' length at 32 kbps (the recorder's bitrate), so a short claim never underpays.
- Everything in the report is counted from the transcript's segments and the client's card marks (`services/decks/rehearsal.ts`), never estimated by a model:
  - **time** per card, from the marks; a card shown twice adds both stretches;
  - **budget**: the pitch shared evenly across the cards, whole seconds each (`cardBudgetsMs`, the helper #3828's timing shares);
  - **words a minute** from the segments whose middle falls on the card; a card never shown has none, not 0;
  - **filler words**, exact counts of a fixed list (um, uh, erm, er, ah, hmm, "you know", "I mean", basically, literally). "Like" and "so" are not counted: they are words with meanings;
  - **pauses**, gaps of two seconds or more between two segments on the card;
  - **notes covered**: each line of the card's notes is covered when 60% of its content words were said while the card was up.

**The figure check reads only linked figures.**
- A figure said on a card is read with D1555's `lineFacts` and compared with the deck's metric blocks bound to a slot (`deckConsistency.boundFigures`), that card's own first. One that disagrees (outside `agrees`'s 2%) is reported with the record's value and grade.
- Typed figures, "Not recorded" bindings and forward-looking lines ("by 2027 we project …") are never compared.

**The audio is held, then deleted.**
- The audio is stored in R2 while the run happens and held for an hour after the report (`services/rehearsalAudio.ts`), so the founder can turn "Keep the audio" on. The cron's five-minute step deletes every unkept audio past its hour; turning the switch off deletes it at once. The transcript and the report stay.
- A failed run deletes the audio and stores nothing.

**Whose it is.**
- A rehearsal is its maker's alone: every read is by deck, user and uid. Rehearsing needs write access to the venture; a support session is refused.

**The swap.**
- `deckV2Adapter.rehearse` is swapped to `through(() => deckRehearsalsApi.list(deckId))`, with the contract `deckRehearsals.schema.json`. Present's Rehearse panel runs, reads and keeps through the same client.

### Part 2a: coaching, and kept audio on the storage plan

**`rehearse_coach`.** The report has a **Coaching** block between Figures and Likely questions.
- Before coaching, it shows a note, the hold line "Coaching · Holds up to $x · you pay what it uses", and **Coach me**. pd39 draws no such block; it is the smallest one that gives the coaching a call site, and it awaits design.
- The press runs the router's `rail_reason` task once, on the rail option `rehearse:coach`, page "Founder · Raise · Pitch deck". The model is the member's AIRail pick for that option. The run is quoted (`quoteRun`) and the balance checked before anything is held.
- The model reads the transcript as untrusted text, and the report's per-card counts as data.
- It returns at most six points, each tied to a card. A point is shown only when all of these hold (`services/decks/rehearseCoach.ts`, `coachPoints`):
  - its card is in the deck;
  - it is at most 280 characters;
  - every number in it appears in the report or the transcript.

  A point that fails is counted (`dropped`), never shown, so the coach never invents a figure.
- The coaching is written once. A second press is 409 `already_coached`.
- **The audio's hour now counts from the coaching (U = A).** Writing the coaching resets `audio_delete_after` to an hour from then. Kept audio and deleted audio are left as they are.

**Kept audio counts against the storage plan.**
- The recording's size is stored (`audio_bytes`).
- A new storage area, `deck_rehearsals` (kind recordings, by venture), counts a rehearsal's audio while it is kept and still stored. Held audio, in its hour, is not counted, since the sweep deletes it.
- A part 1 rehearsal has no size row. Turning Keep the audio on writes one, with the stored object's size from R2. Part 1 audio that was already kept before part 2a shipped is not counted until its switch is turned again.
- Turning **Keep the audio** on asks `assertStorageRoom` first. A plan without room refuses with 413 `storage_full` and keeps nothing. Once kept, `noteStorageLanded` raises the 80/95/100 notices.

**A table of its own, not ALTERs.** The size and the coaching live in `deck_rehearsal_extras`, one row per rehearsal, keyed by the rehearsal's id. They are not new columns on 743's table.
- D4106 names this migration by its UTC minute (`2026…`). About thirty test fixtures and two guards (`check-baseline-drift`, `check-migration-column-shapes`) build the schema by sorting migration names as plain text, which puts a stamped file before `743_`.
- An `ALTER TABLE deck_rehearsals` there runs before the table exists. A `CREATE TABLE` that names it in a foreign key does not depend on that order, so this migration works under either sort.
- The fixtures were left as they are. The first stamped migration that must alter an older table will need them, and those guards, to sort with `compareMigrations`. That is noted on #3827 for the owner.

### Part 2b: live words a minute, and the notes ticked as they are said

**One relay, two owners.** The interview's live relay is split (`services/liveTranscription.ts`):
- `relayLive(env, ctx, owner, first)` is the relay itself: the socket pair, a block of `LIVE_BLOCK_MINUTES` held before it opens and the next held as the stream crosses into it, a refused block ending the stream with close code 4402 and the budget's own message, and the holds settled when it ends.
- The **owner** says whose stream it is: its model, its language, its surface, what each finished passage does (`onSegment`) and how the stream settles (`settle`). This is the "live session's `owner_kind`" part 1 named, done as a callback rather than a column, because a rehearsal's live text is never stored.
- `openLiveRelay` (Record a Session) is now a wrapper that passes the interview's owner: passages stored and labelled, `settleStream` as before. Its tests are unchanged.

**The rehearsal's owner** (`services/decks/rehearsalLive.ts`, routes in `deck_rehearsals.ts`):
- `POST /api/decks/:id/rehearsals/live/quote` → `{ model, hold_microusd, block_minutes, balance }`. `GET /api/decks/:id/rehearsals/live` is the WebSocket: 426 unless an upgrade, then the deck's write check, then the model check, then the first block's hold (402 with the budget's code).
- The model is the member's AIRail pick for the new rail option **`transcribe:rehearsal-live`** (kind `live`: Deepgram Flux by default, or Nova-3), checked by the live job's rules (`liveChoice`, VA2): a model the job does not offer is 409 `model_not_offered`, one the plan locks is 402 `plan_locked`, both before any hold.
- The option kind `live` is new. It reads job VA2 (`RAIL_KIND_JOBS`), lists what `listen_live` can stream (`fitsRailKind`; Nova-3's own operation is batch transcription, so the operation alone would drop it), and streaming now fits Raise in the coverage table (`coverage.ts` `FITS`). `AI_MODEL_COVERAGE.md` is re-rendered: Flux is offered on Raise.
- **The price is the pitch deck's, not the interview's.** The interview's stream pays Deepgram's opt-out rate, because interview audio opts out (#3936 part 3). A rehearsal streams on `/raise/pitch`, which does not opt out, as part 1's batch transcript does not. So `livePerMinute`, `blockQuoteMicro`, `blockHoldMicro` and `holdBlock` take the surface, defaulting to the interview's, and the rehearsal passes its own. Holding at the interview's rate would have held, and charged, twice the minutes.
- The finished passages are kept in memory only (`liveText`, at most 40,000 characters) for the output guard at settle. Settle charges the minutes and the guard against the holds and writes one `ai_usage_logs` row on `/raise/pitch`, naming the rail option and `deck:<id>`; the guard's rows name it.
- **Nothing of the live text is written.** The rehearsal's record is still part 1's transcript of the recording; the report is counted from it.

**The panel (pd38).** Setup has **Count words live** (on when the quote allows it) with its hold line, "Live words · Holds up to $x for each 4 minutes · you pay what it uses", and a note that the live text is not kept.
- While rehearsing, the same microphone source feeds a ScriptProcessor that sends 16 kHz PCM (`interviewCapture.downsampleTo16k`) to the socket (`openLiveSocket`, `readLiveEvent`).
- Under the meter: live words a minute over the whole pitch so far, interim text included, and nothing under five seconds ("Words a minute show after a few seconds of speaking", never 0).
- The card's notes are ticked ✓ as they are said, by the report's own rule (60% of an item's content stems), over what was said while that card was up. The word, stem and stop-word rules are mirrored from `services/decks/rehearsal.ts` in `rehearseModel.js`, and a test reads both sources so they cannot drift silently.
- If the socket fails, the line says "Live words stopped"; if the balance runs out, it says so. The rehearsal carries on either way, and the report is unchanged.
- pd38 draws the live line and the ticks; the Setup switch and its hold line are the smallest addition that gives the charge a consent and a call site, and they await design.
