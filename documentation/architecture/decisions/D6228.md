### What the canvas decides

- Ownership & rights on a company's Financials tab leads with the holding line and your rounds, with shares and price Not recorded where they are.
- Seven rights follow, each with its document and clause, what is recorded and what you can do. A right not recorded at closing reads Not recorded. Then the duties table and the read-only cap table. There is no primary action.
- A SAFE with no right and no duty reads None, with Ask HQ to record a right. A cap table not shared reads Refused, not an empty table, with Ask the founder for access.
- An investor requests a mark from the Stale chip on Positions: Prepare as a sheet, then Review, Confirm and Receipt. Eadwyn drafts the reason; the investor accepts, edits or discards it.
- The investor's requests list newest first as Requested, Answered (linked to the mark) or Declined (with HQ's reason). Requested also shows on Positions and in the mark history.
- The admin queue sits above the register. Record a mark is prefilled from the request and its receipt answers it. Decline goes through Scope → Review → Result with a reason the investor sees. Eadwyn proposes a basis and source, never a value.
- Unavailable is the failed-read word (D5129), and each read fails on its own section. One open request per company per investor.
