## D6139 part 1 — Accreditation letters: the upload, the member's states and the review route (#6139)

Part of #6139 (`D6139.md`). The Worker routes, the migration and the member side. The HQ review surface is part 2.

### Owner answer A (2026-11-27)

The only evidence an investor may upload is a verification letter from a CPA, an attorney, a broker-dealer or a registered adviser. It is kept for the 12-month accreditation period plus the investor-record retention period that #5510 sets.

### Retention: one named setting, not yet set

#5510 has not set its figure. So it is one named setting, `INVESTOR_RECORD_RETENTION_MONTHS` in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/accreditationEvidence.ts`, and it is **null**. No number was invented.

- While the setting is null, `letterKeptUntil` answers null.
- HQ's list shows `kept_until: null`, and the member's page says the period "is not set yet".
- No sweep deletes a letter. Part 1 stores and reviews letters and never erases one, except the rollback of a file whose row could not be written (`unreferenced`, D6651).
- The R2 inventory class `files-accreditation-evidence` records the period the same way.
- When #5510 decides, setting this one value is the change. A sweep through `eraseRecordObjects` (reason `retention_expired`) is its own PR.

### What is stored

The store is `accreditation_evidence`, migration `202611272256_6139_accreditation_evidence.sql`.

- **One row per letter.** Each row holds:
  - `basis`, which of the four professionals wrote it;
  - the R2 key, the name, the type, the size and the SHA-256;
  - `status`: `submitted`, `in_review`, `accepted` or `sent_back`;
  - the reviewer, the date of the decision, the reason it was sent back, and `expires_at` once it is accepted.
- **"Expired" is not stored.** An accepted letter whose `datetime(expires_at)` has passed reads `expired`.
- **One letter open at a time.** A partial unique index allows one letter per member in `submitted` or `in_review`.
- **Triggers:**
  - the file and the basis are written once;
  - a decided letter is never reopened. A new letter is a new row.
- **The file.** It is a `record` under `accreditation-evidence/<user_id>/<uuid>.<ext>` in `studioos-files`, written once through `putRecordOrThrow`.
  - The prefix is in `R2_INVENTORY.json` and in `RECORD_FILE_CLASSES`.
  - A file is accepted only when its bytes are a PDF, PNG or JPEG, never by its name or its declared type.
  - It is at most 10 MB.

### The routes (`routes/trust_accreditation.ts`, self-mounted)

**The member, at `/api/trust/accreditation`:**

- **`POST /evidence`** (multipart `basis` + `file`) is open only to a member asked for `accreditation_v1` (required, not waived). An admin previewing as an investor is refused.
  - A pending or expired requirement moves to `in_review`.
  - A satisfied requirement stays satisfied while a renewal letter is read.
  - The refusal codes are `bad_basis`, `letter_required`, `letter_too_large`, `letter_type`, `accreditation_not_asked`, `letter_open` and `storage_unavailable`.
- **`GET /evidence/mine`** returns the requirement, the caller's letters and the retention settings.
  - Each letter has its basis, uploaded date, state, and the decision's date, period and reason.
  - No list response carries the file, its key or its digest.
  - A failed read is `503 accreditation_unreadable`, and the page says Unreadable.

**The fund team, at `/api/kyc`,** mounted ahead of `kyc.ts`, whose `/admin/:userId` would read "accreditation" as an account id:

- **`GET /admin/accreditation?status=open|all`** (`requireAdmin`) lists the letters, oldest open letter first, with no file in the list.
- **`GET /admin/accreditation/:id/file`** (`requireAdmin`) streams the letter with `private, no-store`.
- **`POST /admin/accreditation/:id/decision`** needs `requireAdmin`, a recent step-up, and a branch that is not suspended. The decisions are:
  - **`in_review`:** the reviewer has opened the letter.
  - **`accept`:** the letter, and `accreditation_v1`, are satisfied until now plus the accreditation period HQ sets. That period is `kyc_renewal_periods` through `renewalPeriods`: 12 months unless HQ changed it. No new expiry rule was added.
  - **`send_back`:** needs a reason of 10 to 500 characters, which the member reads. A requirement this letter put in review is pending again; a renewal keeps its running period.
- **Who may decide.** A reviewer never decides their own letter (`own_letter`). Another admin's letter is visible only to the Super Admin.

**The accept decision is the one writer of `accreditation_v1 = 'satisfied'`.** It stamps `evidence_meta.source = 'accreditation_evidence'`, which `/trust/me` shows as "Accepted from your verification letter".

- `services/trust.ts` keeps accreditation out of the signature and clickwrap satisfiers.
- `obligation_satisfiable.test.ts` now reads this file. Its recorded gap for `accreditation_v1` is closed, so the set of working satisfiers is ten.
- The KYC periods read reports the accreditation period as applied.

### Audit

Each admin read and decision writes `activity_logs` under an action listed in `KYC_AUDIT_ACTIONS`, so the KYC audit read and its export show them:

- `accreditation_queue_access` and `accreditation_document_access` (looks);
- `accreditation_review_started`, `accreditation_accepted` and `accreditation_sent_back` (decisions).

The sentence names the letter and the member's id, never the reason typed. The member's upload logs `accreditation_evidence_submitted`.

The member's own read of their own status is not logged. It carries no document, as `GET /kyc/status` is not logged.

### The member side

- **`/trust/me` › Verifications › Accredited investor verification** draws `trust/AccreditationEvidence.jsx`. The state is `accreditationState` in `trust/memberTrustModel.js`:
  - **upload:** no letter on file;
  - **in review:** with the uploaded date;
  - **accepted until a date:** with "upload a new letter before that date to renew it";
  - **sent back:** with the reason;
  - **expired.**
- **The form.** It asks who wrote the letter and takes the file. It is hidden while a letter is with the fund team, and the Worker's sentence is shown on a refusal.
- **The requirement sheet's action** is "Open Verifications" (`/trust/me?tab=verifications`), not the Help Center.
- **New statuses.** `STATUS_TONE` gains `submitted`, `accepted` and `sent_back`. `trust_center_contract.test.mjs` reads the new router and the migration's `CHECK`, so a status added there needs a tone.
- **The LP workspace's onboarding row** (`FundOnboardingSection`, reached from `SpinoutLabLpWorkspacePage`) reads the same state through the same endpoint:
  - Verified until a date, Letter in review, Letter sent back, or Expired;
  - with no letter, the application's self-certification as before.
  - Once a letter is on record, it no longer says "not verified here".
