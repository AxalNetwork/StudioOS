### 1. The content calendar: a plan, never what was sent

`content_calendar_items` holds one planned piece per row:
- `title`, required;
- `planned_on`, a full day or none;
- `status`: `idea`, `planned`, `scheduled`, `published` or `dropped`;
- `url`;
- `channel_id`: one of the venture's `growth_channels`, or none. Deleting the channel leaves the piece, with no channel (`ON DELETE SET NULL`);
- `source`: `manual` or `notion`;
- who wrote it, and timestamps.

A `published` piece is still the founder's plan. Nothing here says what was sent; that is #2400's `publish_drafts`.

**Typed by hand, so the store is never Notion-only.** All three routes are gated by `canAccessProject` for writing, and each write logs one `grow.distribution` row of ids only (`grow_content_added`, `_updated`, `_deleted`).
- **`POST /api/cycles/grow/channels/content`** adds a piece:
  - the status defaults to `idea`;
  - a channel is named by its uid, and another venture's channel is 404;
  - a date must be a real `YYYY-MM-DD`;
  - a link must be `http` or `https`.
- **`PATCH /api/cycles/grow/channels/content/:uid`** changes only the fields it is sent.
  - A piece from Notion is refused with 409: "This piece comes from Notion. Change it there, and the next sync brings it here." An edit here would be undone by the next sync.
- **`DELETE /api/cycles/grow/channels/content/:uid`** removes any piece. A piece from Notion stays deleted: its ledger row keeps no local id.

**The read.** Distribution's module read carries `parts.content_calendar`:
- `{ state: 'ready', items }`, soonest first, with an unplanned piece last;
- or `{ state: 'unreadable', reason }`. The channels table still loads.

**The card** (`ContentCalendar.jsx`), above "From update asks":
- each piece shows its date ("No date yet" when there is none, never a guessed one), its channel ("No channel"), its status, and a "From Notion" pill;
- Plan a piece and Edit (a typed piece only) open an in-page dialog that says "Nothing is published or sent";
- Delete opens one that says a piece from Notion stays deleted;
- an unreadable calendar says so, with Try again.

### 2. Distribution from Notion

A mapped content-calendar database lands as facts through the ledger (D1086). Each row is one piece with `source = 'notion'`:
- the title;
- the date as `planned_on`. A month alone is no date;
- the status through the value map, else `idea`;
- a web link (anything else is left empty, never refused mid-sync);
- the **channel matched to a `growth_channels` row of the same venture by name, case-insensitive and trimmed, else none**. A channel is never made.

**A re-sync** writes only the fields the founder mapped, and only to the piece its own ledger links. A typed piece is never edited.

### 3. Product improvement from Notion

A mapped feedback database lands as facts. Each row is one `product_feedback` row through `addFeedback`, the store's own writer:
- the text as `body`;
- `theme`;
- `status` (`open`, `planned` or `shipped`, else `open`);
- `source`.

**`source`, never guessed:**
- It comes from a mapped Source column, else the founder's default.
- A map with neither is refused: 400 `source_required`, "Map a Source column, or choose where this feedback comes from."
- A row whose Source cell is empty, with no default to fall back on, is skipped as `empty_required` on `source`, and the source's outcome says so.

**A name or an email only when mapped.** `from_name` and `from_email` are new optional fields of the module, written only when the founder maps a column for each. An email that is not one is left empty.
- From Notion's view of a row shows the feedback, its theme, status and source, never who gave it.
- A re-sync writes the mapped fields of its own row. Moving to `shipped` sets `shipped_on`, as `updateFeedback` does.
