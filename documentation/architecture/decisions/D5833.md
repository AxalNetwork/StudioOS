### 1. Identity, revision and fingerprint

A statement is one provider, entity (QuickBooks realm, Xero tenant), report
(balance sheet or profit and loss), requested window (an as-of date or a
start..end period), basis (`cash`, `accrual` or `unknown`) and currency. Each
write of it is compared with its latest revision by a SHA-256 fingerprint of
what the source said: basis, currency, precision, returned window and every
line's key, label, section, kind, state and exact amount. Read time is not
content. The same fingerprint writes nothing (`unchanged`); a different one
appends the next revision, which records the one it supersedes. A source that
goes A → B → A makes three revisions, not two.

The requested window is the identity; the returned window is stored beside it
and `period_mismatch` says when the provider answered for something else.

### 2. Exact amounts and four states

An amount is stored as a canonical signed decimal at the statement's precision
(`-1250.40`) and as minor units in a decimal integer string (`-125040`), so it
never passes through a float and stays exact past 2^53. A line is `value`,
`zero`, `absent` (an empty cell) or `unreadable` (anything that is not an
amount at this precision). A digit past the precision is unreadable, never
rounded. A JSON number is accepted only as an integer, because a fractional
one has already been through a float.

### 3. Atomic, retry-safe writes; traceable pins

The revision row and its lines are one batch. Concurrent writers race on the
revision's UNIQUE key; the loser reads again and either finds the same content
(`unchanged`) or appends after it. Revisions and lines cannot be updated
(triggers). A founder pins one exact revision; a correction never moves the pin,
and reading the pinned revision says which revision superseded it.

### 4. Guards

A write needs project write access (owner or co-founder; an advisor reads only)
and the writer's own active connection to that provider. A read needs project
access; a stranger gets `project_not_found`, by project or by revision uid.

### 5. What a line may support

| Figure | Report | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Cash balance | balance sheet | the provider's cash total (QuickBooks `BankAccounts`, Xero `Bank`) |
| Revenue | profit and loss | the provider's income total (QuickBooks `Income`, Xero `Income`/`Trading Income`/`Revenue`) |
| Profit | profit and loss | net income (QuickBooks `NetIncome`, Xero `Net Profit`) |
| Change in cash | two balance sheets | the cash totals of the same entity, basis, currency and precision, earlier then later |

Axal never sums accounts to make a total. A net loss is profit, not net cash
burn, and nothing in this contract produces runway. A ledger figure is set
beside Stripe receipts or Mercury movements with both labels; a difference is
given only for the same currency and period, and nothing is totalled.

### 6. Not in this change

No route, provider client, OAuth or sync job: those follow the pilot choice in
#5832. The statements write no `project_metrics`, Mercury or revenue row, and
the Use of Funds runway read returns the same value before and after a write.
