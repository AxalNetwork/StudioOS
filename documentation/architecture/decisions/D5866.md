### What it decides

- **Who acts is the read's call.** Sync, Delete and Accept/Dismiss are drawn only when `parts.from_tiktok` says `can_sync`, `can_delete` and `proposal.can_decide`; the page never works a right out of a role. The Worker gives Sync to the member whose TikTok serves the venture (the first sync links it), Delete to a writer, and Accept/Dismiss to the venture's founder. A reader sees the figures and "Reads only".
- **Reach reads no clock.** "This month" is the month of the latest sync (`as_of`): reach is the sum of the views of the videos posted that month and how many there are, so it has no Delete of its own; deleting a video takes it out. A video with no view count is left out and the line says how many. No video that month draws "None this month", never 0 views.
- **The top three by views of that month's videos**, with "Show all N videos" expanding in place. Desktop draws a table with a caption and headers; under 640px each video is a card with the four counts in a 2 × 2 grid and 44px targets.
- **Audience on TikTok** is its own block on Revenue and traction (followers, total likes, videos, the latest snapshot), marked "Audience, not revenue. These figures never count toward MRR or a KPI."
- **States.** Not connected (`not_linked`) links to `/account/connectors/tiktok` (any other `connect_href` is dropped) and keeps Sync for the member who may link. A scope not granted reads Not recorded with "Reconnect to share them". A failed read reads Unreadable with Try again, and the rest of the module still shows. Syncing, Partly synced (the read's `link.partly_synced`, or the sync's own counts) and Sync failed each say so; a failed write keeps what was there.

### Departures from the canvas

- **One Delete per audience snapshot**, not one per figure: the Worker deletes a whole `tiktok_account_facts` row (`kind=account`), so three Deletes would each remove all three.
- **The source line names the member whose TikTok serves the venture** (`link.served_by.name`), not a TikTok display name: none is stored. No "last synced" time: the read gives the snapshot date only.
- **Not drawn:** "Not available yet" (GR-24c; the read does not say the app is unapproved), the Overview reach line (GR-23, #5867), Add channel stepping down to secondary while the proposal waits (the Distribution header is not this part's), and "Open channel" after Accept (the accepted state names the channel instead).
- **"Unreadable"** keeps the house word in the Platform States shape (`StateChip kind="unavailable" label="Unreadable"`), as the issue asks.
