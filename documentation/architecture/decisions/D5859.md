### 1. Which venture a Page feeds (owner, option A)

Part 1 saves the member's chosen Pages per company (`config_json.pages_by_company`), and a company can hold several ventures (migration 189). So a venture names its one Page in `facebook_page_links`.

**`PUT /api/cycles/grow/facebook/page { project, page_id | null }`** links, changes or unlinks it. It checks that:
- the caller's own connection is active and switched on for the venture's company;
- the Page is one that member chose for that company (`page_not_chosen`);
- the token still lists the Page (`facebook_page_not_granted`).

**Refusals:**
- A venture another member's connection already links is `venture_served` (Beehiiv's precedent).
- The same Page on a second venture of the same connection is `page_elsewhere`.

**Constraints:**
- `project_id` is unique.
- `UNIQUE (integration_id, page_id)` keeps one Page to one venture per connection.

**Changing the Page** restarts its history. The old Page's figures stay until they are deleted, and the part reads only the linked Page's (`subject = page_id`).

### 2. What lands

| Kind | Module | From | Ledger kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| `followers` | Revenue and traction | the Page's follower count, one snapshot per sync day | `facebook_page_fact` |
| `views`, `viewers`, `engagements` | Distribution | the Page's daily insights, up to 12 months back | `facebook_page_fact` |

`facebook_page_facts` follows #2399's Backend section: `integration_id`, `project_id`, `module`, `kind`, `period` (the day), `subject` (the Page id), `value` and `as_of`, with `UNIQUE (project_id, kind, period, subject)`.

**Not stored:** a Page token, a Page name in a log, or a post.

**Not written:** no figure goes into `project_metrics`, `custom_kpi_values` or the funnel's Visit row. A view of a Page is not a visit.

**`parts.from_facebook`** is on the `distribution` and `revenue-and-traction` reads.
- **Revenue and traction:** followers, and the change since the previous snapshot.
- **Distribution:** the 28 days ending at the newest recorded day, each figure or "Not recorded" with its reason.
  - Views and engagements carry a total over the days recorded (`days_recorded` of 28).
  - Unique viewers carry no total, because they are counted per day and do not add up.
- **States:**
  - `not_linked`, with `connect_href`;
  - `not_recorded` (linked, not synced);
  - `ok`;
  - `unreadable`, in which case the rest of the module still loads.
- **Permissions:**
  - `can_sync` is true for the member whose connection holds the link.
  - `can_link` is true for that member, or for anyone before a link.
  - `can_delete` is true for any writer.
  - None of them in a support session.

### 3. The sync and the delete

**`POST /api/cycles/grow/facebook/sync { project }`** works in this order:
1. **Re-read the Pages.** It re-reads `/me/permissions` and `/me/accounts` and refuses a Page the token no longer lists (`facebook_page_not_granted`). The Page access token comes from that answer, is used for this sync only, and is never stored.
2. **Today's follower count.**
3. **The daily insights, in windows of 90 days.** Meta caps a daily range at 93 days (UNVERIFIED).
   - The newest window first, so recent days refresh.
   - Then back from `insights_cursor`, at most `WINDOW_BUDGET` (3) requests per sync, and never beyond 12 months.
   - Until the 12 months are read, the sync is "Partly synced" and keeps its cursor. A fresh link takes two syncs (270 days, then the rest). Once read, `backfilled = 1` and later syncs ask only for the newest window.
- **A failed read** keeps what was read. **Nothing read** answers the refusal (`facebook_unreadable`, `rate_limited`, `sign_in_again`).

**Refusals:**
- a support session (403);
- no active Facebook (409 `facebook_not_connected`);
- `company_not_enabled` (403);
- no link (409 `facebook_page_not_linked`);
- another member's link (409 `venture_served`).

**On demand only.** Nothing reads Facebook on a schedule (#2399, Jobs).

**Landing.** It reads the ledger and the store once each, then writes in batches of 100.
- A ledger row with no `local_id`, or whose fact is gone, is kept deleted and counted (`kept_deleted`).
- **Logging** goes through `logGrowWrite`, with counts only: "Grow synced from Facebook: 1 follower snapshot, 810 daily figures, 0 kept deleted, partly synced".

**`DELETE /api/cycles/grow/facebook/facts/:id?project=`** is open to any writer. It unlinks the ledger row, so the figure stays deleted.

### 4. Disconnect and the Meta deletion callback

- **Disconnect** (`providers/facebook.ts`): after Facebook revokes the permissions, `purgeFacebookConnection` deletes the connection's facts, their ledger rows and its Page links. A failed revoke throws first, and nothing is deleted.
- **The deletion callback** (`routes/meta_data_deletion.ts`): D5292 said to extend it when Facebook data is stored outside the integration tables. Its batch now starts with `purgeByAccountStatements` (the same three deletes, by Meta user id, with the subquery written out in each), before the rows they name are deleted.

### 5. UNVERIFIED facts

#2399 marks the follower field and the insights metric names UNVERIFIED. This session could not reach `developers.facebook.com` on 2026-11-15 (the request got no answer). No page was re-read, so each name below is #2399's reading.

Each is a constant in `services/facebook/insights.ts`, and nowhere else:
- `GET /{page-id}?fields=followers_count`.
- `GET /{page-id}/insights?metric=page_media_view,page_total_media_view_unique,page_post_engagements&period=day&since=&until=`, answered as `data: [{ name, period, values: [{ value, end_time }] }]`.
- **A daily value's `end_time`** is taken as the next midnight, so the day it counts is the day before.
- **The 93-day cap** on a daily range.
- **The Page access token** comes from `/me/accounts?fields=id,name,access_token`.

**The check:** the owner's first live sync, after Meta App Review (#1008). The connector page's `verified_on` stays null.
