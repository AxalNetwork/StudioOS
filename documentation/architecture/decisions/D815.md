### 1. The decision

Prompt 2 asks for two boards:
- **D4,** a partner's testing programmes in the operating partner workspace: the list by status, "New programme" with "Submit for HQ approval", and one programme's applicants with Pick and Decline.
- **D5,** HQ › Product testing for the Super Admin: programmes waiting for approval with Approve and "Send back", every programme by status, and "New programme" for an Axal VC product.

### 2. As built

**Where they sit.**
- **D4 is a zone: Offers › Product testing, at `/offers/testing`.**
  - A sidebar row cannot be added: `partner_shell.test.mjs` pins the partner's seven rows.
  - A bucket zone takes the shell, the zone pills and the rail from `PartnerBucketRoutes`, as every other partner zone does.
  - Offers ("Package what we sell") is the bucket a product the firm puts in front of members belongs in.
- **D5 is a console: `/admin?tab=product-testing`,** on HQ's Platform row after Bank accounts. It is registered where Bank accounts is: `ADMIN_SECTIONS`, `hqStrips.js`, the App strip, `adminPlacement.js`, `_adminConsoles.mjs` and the consoles README. The Super Admin gate is inside the console, as Bank accounts' is (`isSuperAdminUser`). D812's routes refuse anyone else anyway.

**Shared, in `pages/testing/`:**
- **`programmeModel.js`**, the pure rules:
  - the board's status words (Draft, Waiting for HQ, Approved, Open, Testing, Closed, Sent back) and their tones;
  - "[n] of [n] places filled";
  - which actions a status allows, as D812 allows them: a partner submits a draft; HQ approves or sends back a submitted programme, opens HQ's own draft or an approved partner programme, and closes an open one;
  - the form's checks and body;
  - refusals in words.
- **`ProgrammeForm.jsx`:**
  - product, summary, what testers do, time needed, the four dates, places, who can apply, and up to five screening questions;
  - it checks what the Worker checks before the round trip, then shows the Worker's own sentence for anything refused.
- **`ProgrammeApplicants.jsx`:**
  - the counter and the note "Testers hear from you through Axal VC Mail.";
  - each applicant's answers under their question, with availability;
  - Pick and Decline once the programme has opened;
  - a pick past the places shows the Worker's refusal ("Every place is taken. Decline someone first.").
  - No email address is drawn; D812 sends none to an owner.

**D4, the partner's zone** (`pages/partner/offers/TestingZone.jsx`):
- programmes grouped by status, each with its places, applicants and dates;
- HQ's note on a programme sent back;
- Edit (a draft, or one sent back, which returns to draft), Submit for HQ approval, and Applicants;
- **the header row** is "New programme" and Export, with the chips All, Drafts, Waiting for HQ, Live and Closed. The row is built with the shared `makeZoneActions` and `makeZoneFilters`, but in the zone's own file. `PARTNER_ZONE_ACTIONS` and `PARTNER_ZONE_FILTERS` are pinned to the Partner Operator Canvas's artboards, and that canvas has no board for this zone;
- **the states:** ZoneBody's loading, "This did not load" with Try again, and the empty state; and the no-firm line for an account attached to no firm, with the header row kept;
- a `StatedLimit`: no email address, no delivery or open figures, and no logo upload yet.

**D5, HQ's console** (`pages/admin/consoles/ProductTestingConsole.jsx`):
- **waiting for approval:** each programme in full (summary, what testers do, time needed, who can apply, and the questions) with Approve and "Send back". Send back opens a dialog whose reason is required, and the partner reads it in the bell and on the programme;
- **every programme, by status,** with Open and Close where D812 allows them;
- **HQ's own:** "New programme" for an Axal VC product, Edit while a draft, and the same Applicants and Pick screen as a partner's;
- **the states:** `CONSOLE_LOADING`, `ConsoleUnreadable` with Try again, and the empty lines.

**The partner bucket's root board** gains a Product testing section, one per zone, as `bucket_board.test.mjs` requires: product, status and places.

**The AI rail.** `/offers/testing` is `off(…, key('Operating Partner', 'Offers', 'Product testing'))` in `railPages.js`. The rail canvas predates product testing, and the route draws its rail through `PartnerBucketRoutes`, so the D781 render ratchet passes it with no `RENDER_NOT_YET` line.
