### 2. What is read, and from where

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/personas/admin/list` (`requireAdmin`): accounts with their primary persona (`persona_id`, `confidence` 0–1, `source`, `updated_at`) and `role`, newest accounts first, `LIMIT 500` | Every count, the charts, Unclassified, and today's re-tag table |
| The taxonomy, `fe/lib/personas.js` (the Worker's twelve, mirrored and held equal by a test) | The segment cards: name, who they are, the number of sign-up questions |

- **Admins are left out of every count,** as the canvas says. The list carries them; the page filters them.
- **Guard L.** The list stops at 500 accounts. When it comes back at 500, every block says "Counted over the newest 500 accounts: the list stops there, so these are not every member."
- **The profile is the member's account role,** not the persona's own `role_alignment`, which is wrong today (ten personas align with `partner`; none with Investor or Advisor). Part 1 fixes the mapping.
- **A confidence nothing stored is not 0%.** It is counted apart from the bands. A source that is not one of the three is "Not recorded", counted apart.
- **A failed read** says "Persona segments could not be read. This is not a claim that no one has a segment." with Try again.

### 3. What each tab draws today

- **Overview (SEG1):**
  - In a segment (of members, with the percentage) and No segment;
  - Segment and profile differ, and Moved by an admin in 30 days: "Not read here yet" (parts 1, 5);
  - Segments by profile: one group per profile (Founders, Investors, Advisors, Operating Partners, Explorers; Other only when it holds someone), a bar per segment in the profile's colour, a hollow "No segment" bar, and "No segment for Advisors yet" when advisors hold none;
  - How segments were set (Suggested at sign-up, Chosen by the member, Set by an admin) and How sure the sign-up suggestion was (four bands, over members suggested at sign-up);
  - New segment tags per week: "Not read here yet" (part 2);
  - Your segments: the twelve, each with who they are, members and the number of sign-up questions;
  - Needs and offers: "Not read here yet" (part 3);
  - Move a member: today's re-tag table (`PersonasPanel`), until part 8 draws the Members tab and the Move dialog.
- **Unclassified (SEG5):**
  - members with no segment, newest accounts first;
  - profile chips with counts (All, Founders, Investors, Advisors, Operating Partners, Explorers), filtering the list;
  - search from two characters, and pages of 25;
  - the table: Member and Profile. Joined, last active, the sign-up chat's state, the reasons, Place in a segment and Ask to finish are part 5.
- **Not drawn now, and why:**
  - the profile filter and sub-account chips (parts 1, 2);
  - a segment's page, its answers and archetypes (parts 3, 4, 8);
  - the Move dialog (part 8);
  - Edit segment, Suggest, Suggestions and the HQ scope select (parts 3, 6, 9);
  - Activity (parts 5, 9);
  - the states beyond each block's own;
  - phone (part 10) and dark, with each part.

### 4. Who sees what

An Admin and a Super Admin read the same counts (`requireAdmin`). Moving an admin's own account stays the Super Admin's (403 `super_admin_required` on the retag route). Writing segments, deciding suggestions and the scope across studios will be the Super Admin's (parts 3, 6); an Admin will see "HQ writes the segment profiles." in their place.
