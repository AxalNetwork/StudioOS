## D914 — Investor narrative part 8: Admin read only, notes, the logged read reason, and the no-permission panels (#2698)

- **Status:** decided and built by S13, 2026-10-24 (#2698, part of #2454).
- **Canvas:** "Investor narrative", boards NAR13 (Admin), NAR13hq (Super Admin), NAR14a (other profiles) and NAR14b (another venture).
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `5ed26088`, with D906 (#2706) and D908 (#2710) merged.
- **Migration:** 545, `narrative_notes` and `narrative_read_reasons`.
- **Owner's answer:** #2459 decision 3, for the narrative, answered by the owner in the session on 2026-10-24 and recorded on #2698:
  1. An admin sees the full Story, read only.
  2. The read reason is required before the narrative opens.

### 1. Who reads, who writes

| Who | Reads the narrative | Writes it | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| The venture's founders (`canWrite`: the owner and accepted cofounders) | yes | yes | read and answer |
| An Admin on this deployment, or the Super Admin on HQ | only after giving a reason, for 8 hours | **never**: 403 `admin_read_only` | read and post, once a reason is given |
| An Investor, an Advisor, an Operating Partner or an Explorer | no: 403, and the page draws NAR14a | no | no |
| A founder of another venture | no: 403, and the page draws NAR14b | no | no |

- **The refusal lives in the narrative's routes,** in `services/narrative/adminRead.ts` (`narrativeAdminGate`), and not in `_founder_validate_helpers.ts`'s `canWrite`, which the rest of Raise shares. This is the same shape as D895's deck rule (`canWritePitchDeck`). The rest of Raise keeps today's admin access; a test shows the Data room module still reads.
- **What the gate covers:**
  - **Writes** (`admin_read_only`):
    - `POST` and `PATCH /api/cycles/raise/narrative-versions(/:v)`;
    - `PUT /narrative/positioning`;
    - `PUT /narrative/:v/sections/:s`;
    - every `PATCH`, `DELETE` and action on `/api/cycles/raise/modules/investor-narrative/records/:v`.
  - **Reads** (`read_reason_required` without a reason):
    - `GET /modules/investor-narrative`;
    - `GET /narrative/:v`, `/narrative/compare` and `/narrative/positioning`;
    - `GET /narrative/notes`;
    - the module's `form-options`, `records/:v` and `records/:v/activity`.
- **A read log that cannot be checked keeps the narrative closed,** answering 503 `read_log_unreadable`. It never opens it.
- **An admin's version record carries no action** (`actions: []`). The page therefore offers nothing the Worker would refuse.
- **The Raise overview** (`GET /overview`) is not the narrative and is not gated: its narrative card is a summary.
- **Eadwyn's "Phrase the fix" band** changes no record and resolves its project as the founder's (`founderProject`). The page does not draw it for an admin.

### 2. The read reason (`narrative_read_reasons`)

- **`POST /api/cycles/raise/narrative/reads {reason}`** is for an admin only; a founder gets 403. The reason is 3 to 200 characters, with whitespace collapsed. The row records:
  - the reader;
  - `reader_kind`: `admin`, or `super_admin` for the Super Admin on HQ;
  - the reason;
  - `expires_at`, 8 hours later.
- **The founder reads every reason.** `GET /narrative/reads` returns them newest first, each with who gave it and when. An admin calling it also gets `mine`, their own unexpired reason.
- **One activity row per reason:** `raise.investor-narrative` `narrative_read_opened`, "An admin opened the narrative to read it" or "HQ opened the narrative to read it". The reason's words are never in the activity log (D691 decision 12); they live in their own table, which the founder reads.

### 3. The notes thread (`narrative_notes`)

- **#2684's deck notes (migration 538) are not general enough to reuse.** They are keyed to a `pitch_decks` row and a card index. The narrative gets its own thread, keyed to the venture and, optionally, a saved version.
- **The routes:**
  - `GET /narrative/notes` returns the thread, oldest first.
  - `POST /narrative/notes {body, version?}` posts a note. An admin needs a reason, as for any read; the founder answers.
- **The version** must be one of this venture's saved versions; another venture's is 404, and version 0 (the live deck) is 400. The body is 1 to 2,000 characters.
- **One activity row per note:** `narrative_note_posted`, "Note posted on v4" or "Note posted on the narrative". It never carries the note's words.
- **A note is the founder's to read, never an investor's.** Only `routes/cycle_raise.ts` selects the table.

### 4. The pages

- **NAR13 and NAR13hq: an admin on any Investor narrative page.**
  - `CycleRoute` wraps the page in `NarrativeAdminGate`. Nothing of the narrative is drawn before the reason form ("Why are you opening this narrative?").
  - After the reason, the banner "Read only. Notes are visible to the founder." heads the page. Under it: "Opened by an admin for "…". The founder sees this reason." (or "Opened by HQ").
  - There is no New version, no Add, no row edit, no fill band and no draft band. The Story's link reads "Open v4", not "to edit it".
  - Story carries the Notes thread.
- **The founder's Story** carries the Notes thread (answer) and "Who read this narrative".
- **Every note post opens a dialog first:** "This posts your note on v4. The founder and the venture's admins see it. It changes nothing in the narrative."
- **NAR14a.** An Investor, an Advisor, an Operating Partner or an Explorer who opens `/raise/investor-narrative` (or any address under it) reads the boundary inside their own shell: "The Investor narrative is for the venture's founders. Ask them to share their deck." It replaces the redirect.
  - **How:** `RoleGuard` (App.jsx) draws it where it would have redirected, from its `ROUTE_BOUNDARIES` table.
  - **What stays the same:** the route guards (`labRoles(['admin', 'founder'])`), so no route is added (D906) and no rail page key changes. An active Spin-Out Lab member is still admitted as before.
  - **Nothing of any narrative is read,** and the Worker's 403 stays.
- **NAR14b.** A founder whose versions read is refused (403 or 404) reads "This narrative belongs to another venture. You can open the narrative of a venture you found." with "Open your own narrative".
- **The Worker decides who is an admin,** by role. An admin previewing another role through "View as" is still an admin to the Worker, and so to the page.
