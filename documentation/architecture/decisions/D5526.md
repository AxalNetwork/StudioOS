## D5526 — Retry transient Python advisory lookups (#5526)

The Python CI audit still scans the pinned requirements in strict mode
with the existing advisory exception. A PyPI transport failure or a
timed-out scan gets up to three bounded attempts, with five- and ten-second
delays. At exhaustion the job reports an incomplete audit and fails.
A completed scan that reports a vulnerability preserves its output and
failing exit code. Each attempt records an outcome in the job summary.

The workflow runs the retry fixtures before installing pip-audit.
They simulate a timeout, persistent upstream failure, and a finding
after recovery without contacting the advisory service.
