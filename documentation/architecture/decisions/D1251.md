### 1. Access stays as `dd.ts` decides it

The page draws a control only when the case read's `allowed` says the viewer may use it. D1245 named seven flags; this part adds the rest, each the same test its route makes:

| Flag | True for | Route |
|---|---|---|
| `decide`, `update` | an Admin or the case lead, on a case that is not archived | `POST …/decision`, `PATCH /cases/:uid`, `PUT …/memo`, `POST …/ic` |
| `memo_read` | an Admin, the lead or the opener | `GET …/memo` |
| `widen` | an Admin or the lead, on an open or in-review case | `POST …/widen` |
| `team` | an Admin, the lead or the opener, on a case that is not archived | `DELETE …/reviewers/:userId` |
| `work` (per section) | an Admin, the lead or the opener; otherwise the section's reviewer, never an observer | `PATCH …/items/:id`, `POST`/`PATCH …/findings` |
| `work_case_wide` | an Admin, the lead or the opener | a flag with no section |

A control that is not the viewer's is one sentence saying who does it (`ddCaseModel.WHO`), never a disabled button. Nothing here widens a read.

### 2. The tabs

- **Who sees which:** the deal team gets all nine. A read scoped to a section reviewer (`case.scoped`) gets RT's four: Overview, Checklist, Flags and Activity. A reviewer at a deal-team address is told the tab is the deal team's, with a link to Overview.
- **The header:** the name; the type, template, stage and id; the lead, the day it was opened, and the target IC date in days ("in 5 days", "today", "2 days past"). Record decision is in the header when the viewer may decide.
- **Overview (DDL3):**
  - the decision strip;
  - four tiles: the checklist worked, the open flags by severity, the requests waiting, the sections signed off;
  - a card per section, with its sign-off line (D466), its verdict, Assign and Record verdict;
  - the risk band ("Not scored yet" without one), the deal context and the subject (the deal team only).
- **Checklist (DDL4):**
  - chips by status with their counts, and a search;
  - one group per section, each item with its owner, its due date and how much evidence it has;
  - the status buttons, for who works the section;
  - choose items for the bulk changes: Set owner, Set due date, Mark N/A.
- **Flags (DDL5):**
  - a headline: the open flags by severity, and how many blocking sections have one;
  - filters by severity, state, section and source;
  - open flags severest first, closed ones apart;
  - a flag opens its dialog (DDL14 C) with its history.
- **Requests (DDL6):**
  - four tiles;
  - the table, each row with its due date, its lateness, its files and its reminders;
  - each row's actions: Mark reviewed, Send a reminder, Change due date, Withdraw;
  - Compose (DDL6b).
- **Documents (DDL7):**
  - the subject's files, evidence on items and reviewer NDAs, each opened through a two-minute link of the viewer's own;
  - a reviewer without an NDA, named;
  - a block the read could not carry says Unreadable with Try again.
- **Cross-checks (DDL8):** the external sources with Run external scan, and the platform checks (D1249).
- **Team (DDL9):**
  - the lead, with Change lead;
  - each person per section: their role, invitation, acceptance, NDA, conflicts and verdict;
  - Resend invitation and Remove;
  - each section no one reviews, named.
- **Sign-off (DDL10):**
  - the sections' sign-offs;
  - the memo's four parts;
  - the decision strip and the conditions to close;
  - IC: sent, ready, or why not;
  - the reports, with Generate and Download again, and Notify founder;
  - Widen scope and Archive.
- **Activity (DDL11):**
  - the case's audit rows, filtered by action and by person;
  - each row names the person, never an email;
  - "Showing the latest 200" when the read is at its cap.

### 3. The Proceed rule before the dialog

D1246's rule: Proceed is refused while a blocking section has an open High or Critical flag.
- **The decision strip** says so.
- **The decision dialog (DDL14 D)** does not offer Proceed.
- **`decisionBody`** refuses it.
- **The Worker** still refuses it (409 `blocking_flag_open`). The page shows that refusal in the Worker's words.

### 4. Every change is a dialog

Every change opens an in-page dialog that says what it changes and who is told. This includes:
- a checklist item's status;
- saving the memo;
- Send to IC, Run scan, Mark reviewed, Send a reminder, Resend invitation and Remove.

The page has no `window.confirm` and no toast:
- **A refusal** stays in its dialog, in the Worker's words, keyed on its code where the case's routes send one (`caseRefusal`).
- **When a dialog closes,** the page says what changed in one line and reads the case again.

Generate report and Download again ask for the authenticator code through the app's step-up prompt when the session has not had one recently.
