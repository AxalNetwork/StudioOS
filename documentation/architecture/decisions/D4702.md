## D4702 — Practice reporting uses one population and one definition per figure (#4702)

- **Status:** built by slot S21 on 2026-11-07. Closes #4702 (Practice audit 4/6).
- **Migration:** `202611071558_advisor_booking_decision.sql`: three columns on `advisor_bookings` (`advisor_decision`, `advisor_decided_at`, `cancelled_by`) and a backfill that states only what a row proves.
- **Related:** D4713 (#4880) and D4712 (#4885) change the same ledger route, `EarningsZone`, `DeliveryZone` and `SessionsZone`; each is a separate PR from `main` and this one is merged with them as they land.

### What was wrong

1. The `/practice` overview's Engagements section counted confirmed **bookings** and its Delivery section completed **bookings**; the pages behind them read `advisor_engagements` and `advisor_deliverables`. Its Opportunities section called every `pending` booking "awaiting you", while the Opportunities page calls one whose slot has passed "expired".
2. "Past sessions" asked the server for the **next** 31 days and then kept the ones already over: nothing.
3. Delivery's review coverage read each held session's reviews one request at a time, stopped at the first 20, stored a failed read as "no reviews", and held the work-product list on a skeleton while it did.
4. The ledger summed the **2,000 newest** bookings. The 2,001st dropped out of every total while the page still said "all time". The annual summary read every row in memory by a different path.
5. The Retainer column was the contract's per-cycle value for every non-draft engagement, whatever the window, beside revenue columns for the window.
6. A booking cancelled after the advisor accepted it was counted as a decline, so a client leaving lowered the advisor's acceptance rate.

### What was decided

**Each overview section summarises the entity its page shows.**
- Opportunities: the bookings, classified by `classifyBooking`, the function the page uses (one instant per render). "Expired unanswered" is counted and listed apart from "awaiting you".
- Active engagements: `advisor_engagements`, using the page's own `totals.active` and `renewal_due`; a drafting row is not an engagement.
- Delivery desk: `advisor_deliverables`, with the page's own `totals.work_products | sent | opened` and each work product's state.
- Sessions and Earnings keep their stores.

**Past sessions reads the window that just ended.** `windowFrom('past', days)` starts it `days` ago; every other view reads forward. A window that fills the 500-row cap says so (`truncated`).

**Review coverage is one read over every held session, and unknown stays unknown.**
- `GET /me/review-coverage`: `held`, `reviewed`, `awaiting`, `ratings { count, sum }` as database aggregates over all completed sessions, and `items` (per session: review count, whether the advisor filed, average rating) for the 1,000 newest, with `items_complete`.
- The page renders the work-product list as soon as it arrives; the held sessions and the coverage are read together afterwards. A failed coverage read shows "Reviews not read" and "review coverage could not be read"; it is never "No review" or zero, and no review form is offered over reviews that were not read.
- `GET /me/bookings` now returns `total` (and `limit`), so a page can say "newest 10 of 25" rather than presenting the window as the whole.

**The ledger covers every booking, and says how complete it is.**
- `ledgerAggregate` is one SQL aggregate over every booking in the half-open window; `GET /me/ledger` and `GET /me/tax-summary` both use it, so the table, the annual summary and the CSV cannot disagree. A line's cut is the cut it was **stamped** with; only a line priced before the cut was stored is derived, at its own rate (else the current one), rounded down.
- The client list is the top 500 by gross. Everyone past it is one summed row (`clients_other`), so the listed rows plus that row equal the totals. `completeness` reports the sessions covered, clients listed of total, and whether the contracts were read in full.
- The page prints what the figures cover, shows the "N more clients, summed" row, and the CSV carries that row, so its rows add to its Total. The AI narrative's coverage lines state the same population.

**Retainer is a contract value, not period revenue.** The column is the contract's value per cycle for retainer engagements **in effect at some point in the window** (signed, renewal due, or ended with an end date inside or after the window start; an unsent proposal and a drafting row are not). The page says so beside the table. Revenue earned in the window is the priced sessions in the other columns.

**The advisor's answer is its own fact.** `advisor_decision` (`accepted` | `declined`) is written when the advisor answers: confirming is an accept; the advisor cancelling a request still waiting is a decline. A cancel by anyone of an already accepted session leaves the accept as it was and records `cancelled_by`. A client withdrawing a request before any answer records no answer (`cancelled_by: client`), and the worker's own cancels (a withdrawn slot, a lost seat race) are the system's.
- `classifyBooking`: a cancelled row with an `accepted` answer is **accepted**; `declined` is **declined**; a system or client withdrawal is **withdrawn**; a cancelled row with no answer on record (from before this column) is **unrecorded**.
- `acceptanceRate` is accepted of accepted + declined. Awaiting, expired, withdrawn and unrecorded are not answers.
- The backfill marks confirmed, completed and no-show rows accepted (they cannot have got there otherwise). It does **not** guess a cancelled row's answer: it may have been a decline or a client leaving, so it stays unrecorded and out of the rate.

### Not decided here

- "Average rating" still averages every review on a held session, both sides' (as before). Whether it should be the clients' ratings only is a product call.
- `GET /me/bookings` is still the 200 newest; `total` makes that visible.
- Re-deriving old cancelled rows' answers from reasons is deliberately not done.

### Tests

- Worker: `advisor_money_model` (more than 2,000 bookings reconciling across the ledger, its client rows and the annual summary; the cut of an old line; the retainer window), `advisor_stores` (the answer recorded on confirm, kept through a client cancel, a decline versus a withdrawal versus the system's cancel; the backfill; review coverage over 25 sessions in one read).
- Frontend: `practice_reporting_4702` (the overview with different counts per store; an old pending request expired on both; Past sessions asks for and shows the historical window; coverage tiles and a failed read; the list not waiting on the secondary reads; the completeness line, the summed row and a CSV whose rows add to its Total) and `advisor_practice_pr1` (the decision log and rate).
- Mutations checked and caught: ledger client cap, totals from the listed rows only, the answer overwritten on cancel, every pending cancel a decline, no retainer window, unpriced clients counted in the summary, the accept ignored, an unknown cancel a decline, no historical window, engagements read from bookings, a failed coverage read treated as empty, and the CSV's summed row dropped.
