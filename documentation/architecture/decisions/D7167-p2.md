### The project picker (FND-P05, RFP08 C)

- `AddToResearch.jsx` reads `GET /api/research/projects?status=all` instead of `active`. Active projects are offered; archived ones follow under **Archived · read-only**, each with its archive date and "restore it in My Research to change it", as disabled radios with a Read-only chip. Before this the picker hid them.
- For each active project, up to `MEMBERSHIP_READS` (20, the most recently updated), the picker reads `GET /api/research/projects/:uid/comparisons`, whose `subjects` say what the project holds. A row then reads "Funds · 2 · updated 12 Nov 2026". A project that already holds every item says **Already here** and is not offered for Add, so membership is known before the press (the canvas's note: today it "learns about membership only after the add"). For two or more items, a project holding some says "1 of 2 already here" and stays offered. While a read is in flight the row says "Checking what it holds…"; past the twentieth it says "What it holds was not checked"; a failed read says "Unavailable: what it holds could not be read" and the project stays offered, since the add answers `already` itself. None of these is a count of zero.
- An add that finds the items already there now ends "Nothing was added." (RFP05B).
- The rows come from one pure function, `pickerView` in `researchComparisons.js`. An Ask answer (`kind: 'answer'`) has no subjects, so its picker shows no membership.

### Compare in project into an existing comparison (FND-P06 B)

- Compare in project for one fund (or one company), once a project is chosen, offers **Add it to a comparison**: that project's comparisons of the same kind, each with "n of 6 funds", and **New comparison**, chosen by default. A full comparison reads "6 of 6 funds · remove one there first" with a Full chip, and one that already holds the fund reads Already in it. Neither is offered (`joinChoices`).
- Joining one calls `POST /api/research/projects/:uid/comparisons/:comparisonUid/members` and says "Added Dawn Capital Opp to “…”. 3 of 6.", with Open the comparison → (`joinOutcome`). A 409 `comparison_full` (another tab filled it) says "A comparison holds at most 6 columns. Remove one first, or start another comparison. Nothing was added." Any other refusal before a write (4xx, a 410 withdrawn source, a 503 `directory_unreadable`) also says nothing was added. Any other failure says it may have been added and links the project, never "nothing was added".
- A selection of two or more always makes a new comparison, as before. The members route takes one fund at a time, and joining with several could stop part-way.

### Back to results (FND-P10)

Part 1 kept the search, filters, sort and page (D4612, D7166), and D7166 already focused the row once the list answered. Two things were missing. The row of the fund just viewed now keeps its selected style until another row is opened (`returnedUid`, marked `data-returned`). A fund that is no longer on that page of results (withdrawn, or the list changed) hands focus to the results heading (`returnTarget` in `fundProfileRead.js`) instead of leaving it nowhere.

### Removing a column (FND-P10)

When a column is removed, focus moves to the column that took its place: the next one, or the new last one. With no column left, it moves to Add fund (`columnAfterRemoval`). The table, By attribute and By fund all work this way.

### The phone comparison (FND-P08 D and E)

On a phone, a comparison of funds switches **By fund** and **By attribute** (the switch is named "Comparison view"). By fund pages one fund at a time with Previous and Next and "Fund 3 of 6", stopping at either end (`fundPage`), and the card offers Remove from comparison. By attribute is the existing list (D7177) and stays the default. A fund comparison's line reads "6 of 6 funds · as reported by each source · no conversion, total or score". Comparisons of companies keep By attribute and By company.

### Departures from the canvas

- The project is a list of radios in Compare in project, not the drawn select. The radios are the same control Add uses, and they carry the membership and archived rows.
- The drawn update time ("updated 14:02") is a date ("updated 12 Nov 2026").
- Membership is read for the twenty most recently updated active projects. The rest say it was not checked.
- Joining an existing comparison is offered for one fund at a time, which is what the canvas draws.
- By attribute keeps D7177's stepper beside the select. The By fund card labels its rows with the comparison's full attribute names.
