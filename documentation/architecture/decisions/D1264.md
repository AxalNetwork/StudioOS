### 1. The states

From the founder's **pinned** items (`raise_evidence_items.state = 'pinned'`), never a model:

| State | When |
|---|---|
| `backed` | the best-sourced record is not Typed and agrees (`agrees`, unchanged: within 2%, or within 1 for a small count) |
| `founders_figure` | the best-sourced record is Typed and agrees. Typed-only is never Backed |
| `mismatch` | the best-sourced record disagrees. The claim keeps the stated figure, and the read returns the record's figure, source label and date: both figures and both sources |
| `not_backed` | no pinned record measures it |

- **Best source first:** Connector, Signed, Document, Recorded, Typed, then the newest observation date. A Typed figure that agrees never outranks a Connector that does not.
- **Every record that bears on a claim** is linked as `how = 'computed'`, so the claim drawer can list them. The founder's and Eadwyn's links (`founder`, `eadwyn_accepted`) are never touched.
- **Stale** is not stored. It is a display state from each item's date (D1263).

### 2. The claims, by surface

| Surface | Version | A claim per | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `narrative` | `v<n>`, the newest saved version | field and figure (`traction#mrr`), including Why now | the pinned items of the figure's kind (§3) |
| `deck` | `v<n>`, the current deck | slide and figure (`slide:2#runway`), from every string on the slide | the same |
| `readiness` | `current` | Traction proof, Team, Product-market fit, where the venture's readiness list holds them | presence: any pinned item whose rule `backs` that kind (`traction_proof`, `team`, `customer_evidence`), or one the founder linked |
| `use_of_funds` | `current` | each planned section's share | the share of the bank spend mapped to that section (D840's accepted mappings), over the shortest window pinned (3, 6 or 12 months). A section with no spend in a window that has spend is a measured 0% |
| `cap_table` | `current` | each founder holding's shares | the vesting pinned for that holder. The holding itself never backs its own row |
| `capital_model` | `current` | growth, churn, CAC, gross margin | bank revenue growth; the KPI churn and CAC. The model's own assumptions never back themselves |

### 3. The figures (#2430's list)

- **The fifteen figures:** MRR, ARR, paying customers, monthly revenue, revenue growth, net burn, runway, churn, CAC, interviews, letters of intent, waitlist sign-ups, pilots, headcount and the raise.
- **Each is read by fixed patterns that need its words beside the number,** so a bare number ("Founded 18 months ago", "15 customers") states nothing.
- **Values:**
  - **A value one record holds:**
    - MRR, ARR, paying customers, net burn and headcount: the KPI snapshot of that metric (`<row>:<metric>`).
    - Monthly revenue: bank revenue in cents.
    - Revenue growth: bank revenue as a percentage.
    - Runway: bank runway in months.
    - Churn and CAC: the KPI, or the model's assumption, which is Typed.
  - **A count of pinned records:**
    - interviews: the distinct interviews behind pinned ICP-fit and quote items;
    - letters of intent: demand signals and LOI files whose signal is an `loi`;
    - waitlist sign-ups: signals of kind `waitlist`.
    - A count that agrees only once Typed records are added is the founder's figure.
- **Not backed by design until a rule records them:** the raise, pilots and gross margin. D1263's rule table has no kind for any of them. A pilot is not a `pre_sale`.
- **`narrative_checks` keeps its four figures** (`NARRATIVE_CHECK_FIGURES`: MRR, paying customers, the raise, monthly revenue) and its rows for readiness, as #3287 says. The record's consistency card lists the same four.

### 4. When it runs, and what it writes

- **On the founder's Evidence read,** after the candidate builder. Linking, sharing and unpinning reset a claim to `not_backed` with no `checked_at` (D1263), and the next read checks it again.
- **An admin's read runs nothing.** It reads the last states, with no record figure (`record` and `stated_value` are founder-only).
- **Writes are made only when something changed:** the kind, the stated value, the state, or the computed links. A read where nothing changed writes nothing.
- **At most 500 claims,** in batches of 50, as D1263's writes are.
- **A claim of the current version that is no longer stated is removed,** unless a person linked evidence to it. Older versions keep their last state ("Mismatch since v3").
- **Each surface is read on its own.** One that cannot be read (a deck whose slides are not JSON, a malformed model) leaves its claims as they were. `claim_check` says `partial`, with the surface `unreadable`, and the rest are still checked.
- **No figure, name or value is logged.** All SQL is bound, and no SQL is interpolated.
