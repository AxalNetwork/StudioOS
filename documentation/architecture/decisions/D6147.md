### What is a match

An open round (`deals.status = 'active'`, venture not deleted) that is not in
this flow, whose `thesis:<deal_id>` item is not dismissed for this flow, and
that no other way in already brings (listed or dismissed), whose fit to the
caller's own thesis is **High**. The fit is D1239's scorer, `computeThesisFit`,
called once per round: nothing is re-scored here. High is the owner's call on
#6147, named `THESIS_HIGH_FIT = 70`: the fit score (the percentage of comparable
criteria that match) at or above 70, the boundary D1239's High band uses. A fit
that is `not_set` or `not_enough_recorded` is never a match.

The thesis is the signed-in investor's own, as `GET /api/deals/fit` reads it,
also for a firm seat; the dismissals and the flow are the firm's (`scope_key`),
as for every other way in. Each item carries `reasons`: the keys of the
matching criteria in the fit's order (`sector`, `stage`, `region`, `ticket`).
The row's line is "Matches your thesis: <the first three, in words>."

### What an uninvited round's match reads

The audience is D4631's `dealAudiences`, as for every other way in; `can_open`
is true only where it is not `none`. A round the caller may not open is
compared only on what the marketplace (`GET /api/deals`) already shows them:
the venture's sector and the round's minimum check and currency. The venture's
stage and its recorded markets go into the fit only for a round the caller may
open, which is exactly what `/fit` computes on. So no match and no reason rests
on a field an uninvited investor cannot read; an uninvited round's reasons can
only be `sector` and `ticket`. The item has the same fields as every other way
in plus `reasons`; no score is sent.

### Bounds and the response

One read is four statements whatever the number of rounds: the thesis, the
newest `THESIS_SCAN = 200` open rounds (flow and dismissal excluded in SQL),
their audiences in one batch, and, when the thesis names regions, the markets
of the readable ones in one batch (`readFitGeographies`). At most
`THESIS_MATCH_CAP = 20` matches are listed, newest round first
(`COALESCE(stage_changed_at, created_at)`). A test counts the statements for 6
and for 40 open rounds.

`sources.thesis` keeps the one `{ recorded, reason }` family `thesisLine` read
before (the issue's `{ available, none_reason }` was not adopted):

| Case | `sources.thesis` |
| --- | --- |
| matches read | `{ recorded: true, available: true, thesis_set: true, threshold: 'high', min_score, cap, scan, listed, more }` |
| no thesis set (no profile, or every preference empty) | `{ recorded: false, available: true, thesis_set: false, reason, threshold, min_score, cap, scan }`, not an error |
| the thesis, the rounds, the audiences or the markets unreadable | `{ recorded: false, available: false, unreadable: true, reason }`; every other way in still lists |

`more` is true when more High-fit matches were found than are listed, or the
scan reached its bound. A single round whose market row is malformed is not
called a match; the rest still are.

### The page

The chip is the existing `ENTERED_WORD.thesis`, "Matches your thesis". The
row offers Add, Pass and Not for me, and Open only with `can_open`. No thesis
set: the source's reason with a **Set your thesis** link (`THESIS_PATH`). More
than the cap: "Only the newest 20 matches to your thesis are listed. Each is a
High fit." Unreadable: named in the existing unreadable line, never as "none".

### Not done

- A round whose fit is High only because an excluded sector or stage is
  outweighed by three matches (75) is listed; D1239's score counts an
  exclusion as one mismatch. Making an exclusion a hard no is a thesis-fit
  change for D1239's owner, not made here.
- Whether an uninvited investor may read a venture's stage or markets for
  matching is #4631's to widen; until then they are left out of the match.
