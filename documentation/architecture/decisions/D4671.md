## D4671 — Licence contract integrity: the document stored is the document signed (#4671)

- **Status:** built by Claude Code for slot S26, 2026-11-07. Not deployed and not checked live.
- **Part of:** the Licences audit, package 1 of 7 (#4671). Takes over the branch slot S31 held; none was pushed.
- **Migration:** `202611071500_licence_contract_integrity.sql`: `licence_contracts.body_sha256`, a backfill that marks the older of any two current rows superseded (nothing deleted), and the partial unique index `idx_licence_contracts_one_current`.
- **Code:** `routes/admin_licences.ts` (instantiate, send, `PATCH /:uid/entity`), `services/licenceContract.ts` (`pctFromBps`, `moneyFromCents`, `unresolvedFields`), `frontend/src/lib/tokenSplit.js`, the contract card and labels in `AdminLicences.jsx`, `MyLicencePage.jsx`, `licencePage.js`, `licenceHistoryModel.js`.

### 1. What was wrong

Sending a draft ignored the draft. The route selected no `body_md`, merged today's ledger into today's template and sent that, with `refuseUnfilled: false` and the `terms.*` values missing from the merge. Editing a licence or a template between review and send changed what was signed, and an unresolved `{{licence.x}}` could go out in an envelope. Instantiation superseded the old contract and inserted the new one as two statements, so a failure between them left a licence with no current contract. `pctFromBps` rounded to one decimal (3501 read 35.0%). `token_split_bps` was "to HQ" on HQ's screens and "the Licensee's share" in the agreement.

### 2. What changed

1. **Send uses the stored text.** `createAndSendEnvelope({ renderedBody: { title, body }, templateVersion })`. The envelope's body and `body_sha256` are the stored body and its hash; its `template_version` is the one the draft carries. A later template edit, version bump, or licence edit changes nothing that is sent.
2. **A fingerprint.** `body_sha256` is written at instantiation and checked at send (409 `contract_body_changed`). A row from before the migration has none, and is hashed from its stored body at send. After sending, the envelope's own `body_sha256` must equal the stored body's hash; an already-pending envelope for the same signer and deal built from older text is refused, not linked (409 `envelope_body_mismatch`).
3. **No send with a hole.** Any `{{field}}` left in the stored body (`unresolvedFields` reads the body, so it catches namespaced fields and ones a hand edit left) is 409 `contract_incomplete`, before a recipient is looked up and before any envelope or email exists. The send button is also disabled while the draft lists unfilled fields.
4. **Atomic reissue.** The supersede and the insert are one batch; the partial unique index means two racing issues cannot leave two current contracts (the loser is 409 `contract_conflict`). Prior rows are never deleted.
5. **Exact numbers.** `pctFromBps` is `pctExact` (3501 reads 35.01%; a half basis point is not a rate, so it stays unfilled). `moneyFromCents` keeps a fraction of a cent (1234.5 cents reads `EUR 12.345`).
6. **`token_split_bps` means the Licensee's share.** The agreement is the instrument people sign, and clause 4.3 already says so, so no template migration is needed. The console, the history labels and the branch's own page now say "to the Licensee", "your share of AI token revenue" (`lib/tokenSplit.js`), and a test fails if a surface names HQ again. Statements use the per-stream `share_*_bps` terms, not this column, so no financial figure moves.
7. **Entity and signatory completion.** `PATCH /api/admin/licences/:uid/entity` with a card on the contract step. Values are validated and cannot be a placeholder.
8. **Counsel review is kept.** `COUNSEL_REVIEW_SLUGS` still marks `subsidiary_licence_agreement` in the template picker, and its DRAFT banner travels in the stored body that is sent.

### 3. Left alone, and why

- A contract already sent is not touched. Its envelope was built from whatever text it carried then; nothing here rewrites it.
- The D758 refusal when a *template* has lost its body now applies at instantiation only. At send, the stored, reviewed text is what is signed, whatever has since happened to the template (the old test is rewritten to say so).

### 4. Tests

`licence_contract_send_d451.test.ts` (envelope body, hash and version equal the stored ones after the licence and template are edited; tampered hash; three kinds of unresolved field; a pending envelope under older text; reissue keeps every row with exactly one current; a failed insert leaves the old contract current; entity validation), `licence_contract_instantiate.test.ts` (3501 bps, fractional cents, `unresolvedFields`) and `frontend/test/token_split_meaning_d4671.test.mjs`. Each new assertion was mutation-checked (body altered, hash check off, unresolved check off, envelope check off, version unpinned, lossy percentage, non-atomic reissue): every mutation fails a test.
