## D6143 — IC voting roster per decision, returned with the condition owner from the commit room (#6143)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-18 for #6143 (under #4815). Worker and client only; the screens are #5910, #6144 and #6154.
- **Migration:** `cloudflare-worker/sql/migrations/202611180056_6143_ic_decision_voters.sql`, a new table only.
- **Code:** `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/ic.ts`; client `frontend/src/lib/api/icRoster.js` (`icRosterApi`, D527).
- **Tests:** `cloudflare-worker/test/ic_roster_6143.test.ts`; the outsider matrix in `ic_company_scope.test.ts` gains the two roster routes.

### 1. Who is eligible: the author picks, from the firm's Investor licence holders

The issue left one question open: who may vote by default. The owner answered it on the #6153 brief (`D6153-p2.md`, 2026-11-16): **Q4 A**, "the author picks the voters when opening, offered from the firm's Investor licence holders". Option B, every Investor licence holder in the firm automatically, was not chosen. So there is **no default roster**. A decision has a roster only when its author, or an Admin, sets one.

A voter is a member of the decision's firm (`user_company_links` against `ic_decisions.company_id`, the membership `icDecisionScope` reads) who holds the Investor licence (`heldLicences`). Anyone else is refused with 400 `roster_voter_not_eligible`, which names the ids. That includes a Partner-only member and an investor at another firm. A decision with no firm (`company_id` null) has no such list, so it is refused with 409 `roster_needs_firm`. Every voter therefore already sees the decision through the firm branch of the scope. The scope itself is unchanged.

### 2. Storage

`ic_decision_voters` holds the decision, the user, `added_by` and `added_at`, and the pair is unique. Nothing is added to `ic_conditions`: the condition owner (`owner_user_id`, `owner_label`) already came with D6156, and this issue only checks that it round-trips through `/:uid` and `/commit-room`.

### 3. Routes

- `POST /api/ic` accepts an optional `voter_user_ids`. It is checked before anything is written, so a refused roster opens no decision.
- `GET /api/ic/:uid/roster` returns the roster and `may_edit`. It returns `candidates` (the firm's Investor licence holders) only to whoever may set the roster; anyone else gets null.
- `PUT /api/ic/:uid/roster` takes `{ user_ids }`, one to fifty members, and replaces the roster. Only the author or an Admin may call it, which is `PUT /:uid`'s rule; anyone else gets 403. A member who has voted or recused stays on the roster, and dropping one is 409 `roster_omits_voter`. Super Admin investor preview is refused as on the other writes.
- `POST /api/ic/:uid/vote`: **where a roster exists**, a caller not on it is refused with 403 `not_on_roster`, for a recusal as well as a vote. With no roster the rule is unchanged: anyone who may see the decision may vote. Every decision opened before this works as it did.

Each route reaches the decision through `loadDecision`, so a decision outside the caller's scope is the same 404 as one that does not exist. No new `SELECT … FROM ic_decisions` was written. The roster rides `scopedDecisions`, which is the one scoped list read.

### 4. The figures (`rosterSummary`)

`/:uid` (as `roster`) and each `/commit-room` row (as `decisions.rows[].roster`) carry:

- `eligible`: the roster's size.
- `voted`: members who voted yes, no or abstain. An abstention is a vote cast.
- `recused`: members who declared a conflict.
- `to_vote` and `not_voted`: the members with no vote, and their names.
- `counted`: eligible less recused, the tally's denominator. A recusal leaves it; an abstention stays in it.
- `off_roster`: votes from members not on the roster, cast before it was set, so no vote is silently dropped.

DE07's "6 eligible · 4 voted · 1 recused · 1 to vote" is `eligible`, `voted`, `recused` and `to_vote`.

**No roster is not zero.** Without one, `available` is false, every figure is null and `reason` says why. A 0 would claim that nobody may vote.

### Left for other issues

- Refusing votes, recusals and roster edits on a Decided record (Q5 A of #6153) is a separate backend item in D6153. A roster can still be edited after the decision.
- Quorum is still not stored.
- The meeting invitation list (`ic_meeting_attendees`) is still not a roster. The commit room's `room.note` now says that who may vote is the decision's own roster.
