### What is built

- **The header, in DE04's order.** Back to Deals (a founder still goes back to Capital relationships); the venture as the one `h1`; the company-stage chip; the website icon; sector · place; and the investor's progress as its own chip ("Diligence · Step 3 of 8"), drawn from the stage read and nothing else. On the right, Pass (only where the page can record one, below), Invite Investors for an Admin, and the one next action.
- **One financial strip:** Round, Instrument, Committed, Minimum, Closes, all from `GET /api/deals/:id` (`target_raise` else `amount`, `instrument`, `capital_committed`, `minimum_check`, `closing_deadline`). A field the deal does not hold reads **Not recorded** with its reason on hover; a field it holds that cannot be read as what it is (an amount that is not a number, a currency that is not a code, a date that is not a date) reads **Unreadable**. Neither is ever 0. Committed is the round's confirmed total across every investor, labelled so. A failed deal read stays the whole page's Unreadable (DL20b).
- **The figures are not repeated above the fold.** Terms at a glance drops Instrument and Minimum; the Overview's round card no longer prints committed of target; the Overview's "Your next step" card is gone, since the next action is the header's.
- **Investment progress:** the eight steps as a sequence, done quiet with a tick, the current one highlighted (`aria-current="step"`), the rest muted; a firm with no IC record shows its IC step "Not recorded" once past it. Under it, one short reason: the sentence for the step the read names (`WHY`). The sequence draws no action. A failed stage read is Unreadable with Try again; no stage says the Worker's reason.
- **One next action:** the Worker's `next_action`, in the header on a desktop, fixed at the foot on a phone (DE15). Never a second one.
- **Your position** (on Overview, an investor only): the viewer's own live commitment, its status and the firm it is held for, with Open investment.
- **The eight tabs:** Overview, Scorecard, Evidence, Diligence, Data room, Terms, Investment, Activity. A tab holding two or more of the viewer's views draws them as a second row of links.

### Another firm's commitment is never the viewer's

The page read `GET /api/deals/:id/commitments` once per deal and took the first live row not marked `in_context: false`. Two gaps: a firm switch did not read again, so the earlier firm's commitment stayed on screen as yours; and a row was never checked to be the viewer's own (a staff-shaped read carries every investor's rows, without `in_context`). Now the page reads again whenever the selected firm changes, stamps the read with the firm it was for, and `viewerCommitment` shows a row only when it is the viewer's (`investor_user_id`), on this deal, live, `in_context: true` and held in the context the Worker read under (`company_id` = `firm_context`). A read for another deal or firm shows Reading until the right one lands.

Two addresses are new: `/deals/:id/data-room` and `/deals/:id/investment`, each opening its tab's first view the viewer holds. An investor's `/deals/:id/activity` now opens their Notes view (which carries the activity feed); before, it fell back to Overview.

### Steps with no field behind them

The eight steps are all backed: `deal_context.ts` derives each from a record the investor may read (Invited `deal_invitations`, Reviewing a deal-room membership or an interested answer, Diligence `dd_cases`, IC `ic_decisions`, Terms the decision's `terms_json` or a sent envelope, Committed `commitments`, Signed the investor's signed `esign_recipients` row, Funded a received commitment). What the canvas draws that no field holds, and is therefore not drawn:

- **A date or count on a step.** The stage read sends no dates, and no counts.
- **The reason's figures** ("3 open diligence requests. Review the founder’s answers."). The stage read carries no request count; the reason is the sentence for the step the read names.
- **Pass at every step.** The page records a pass only as the answer to an invitation (Invited). A pass recorded on the investor's deal flow is not on the stage read, so Pass is drawn at Invited alone.
- **The company's stage and place.** `projects.lifecycle_stage` (and `stage`) and `projects.hq` exist, but `GET /api/deals/:id` serves neither, so the chip says "Company stage · Not recorded" and the line says "Place not recorded". Serving them is a Worker change and, for `hq`, a visibility decision (before the NDA, D1151's deal insights serve a company's name, sector, stage and headline, never `hq`), left to its own issue.
- **Your position's Route** ("Direct"). The commitments read carries no route.

### Departures from the canvas

- **Company stage and place read Not recorded** (above).
- **Commit Capital left the header.** The header carries the one next action from the stage read (for Terms, Review terms). Commit is under Investment, where it was already offered to an investor with no commitment.
- **The staff and founder "Investors" count left the strip**, which is the five DE04 fields; the count is the Investment tab's commitments table. The investor's own commitment left the strip for Your position, which is drawn on Overview, as DE04 draws it.
- **The round status pill** (`deals.status`) is drawn for staff and the founder, not for an investor, who reads their own progress; DE04 draws neither. The lead partner line stays.
- **Join and Leave (D630) stay** under the progress section; the canvas draws no place for them.
- **A second row of views** under Evidence (Shared evidence, Team, Market, Traction) and, for an Admin or Operating Partner, under Investment (Commitment, Invitations, Transfers). The canvas draws the eight tabs only; the row keeps every view one tap away.
- **Phone:** the strip keeps all five fields in two columns (DE15 draws four, without Instrument); the tabs scroll sideways (DE15 shows four); every link and button is at least 44px.
- **Dark:** DE04 has no dark board; the frame follows DE16b with the tokens' dark pairs.
- **The Overview body** (the scorecard summary cards, What's new restyled to material changes, the investment case) is not this part; it stays as D1152 drew it, less the repeated figures.
- **The date reads "1 Dec 2026"**, the canvas's form, where the frame printed `2026-12-01`.
