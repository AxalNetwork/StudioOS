## D4636 part 5 — A founder who is GP of record passes the Studio paywall on the five position reads (#4636)

- **Status:** Built, part 5, 2026-11-14 (Worker only; no migration, no frontend change, no new mount).
- **Scope:** the owner's decision of 2026-11-14: a founder who is GP of record skips the Studio paywall, but only on the five position reads (`GET /api/positions`, `/marks`, `/analytics`, `/kpi-compliance`, `/:projectUid`) and only for the funds they run. This lifts the second judgement call of D4636-p4 §2 ("The studio paywall still applies in production") for those reads.
- **Reuses:** `managedFundIds` (`services/fundGpAccess.ts`) for "runs at least one fund", and `positionScope` (`routes/positions.ts`, part 4) for what the reads return. The handlers are unchanged.

### 1. Where the gate lives, and the exemption

`index.ts` mounts `requireTier('studio')` on every `STUDIO_PREFIXES` entry (`middleware/paywallPrefixes.ts`), `p` and `p/*`, before the routes. `/api/positions` is one of them. This part does not touch `index.ts` (the hub freeze, D5615): the exemption is inside the middleware itself, in `middleware/requireTier.ts`.

`requireTier` used to be: authenticate, pass if `userMeetsTier`, else 402. It now has one more step before the 402, for the `studio` gate only:

| Step | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 1. The request | Is one of `STUDIO_GP_READS`: `GET` on `^/api/positions$` or `^/api/positions/[^/]+$`. The second pattern covers `/marks`, `/analytics`, `/kpi-compliance` and the detail; there is no other `GET` route under the prefix. Any other method, any deeper path and any other prefix is refused here. |
| 2. The caller | Holds the Founder licence (`holds(user, 'founder')`, D4729's test, the same one `userMeetsTier` uses). |
| 3. The funds | `managedFundIds(c, user)` is non-empty: GP of record of at least one fund under the verified active company, read from D1 on this request. |

Only a caller who fails the plan check reaches step 1, and the D1 lookup in step 3 runs only on those five reads, after steps 1 and 2 passed. No other paywalled request pays for it. `index.ts` mounts the gate twice on the prefix (`p` and `p/*`), and both match the list, so the answer is memoised per request (`WeakMap` on the raw `Request`).

**Fail closed.** If the lookup throws, the exemption is refused and the caller gets the same 402 as before.

| Caller | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder below Raise + Grow who runs a fund, on the five reads | 402 | Passes the paywall; `positionScope` scopes them to their funds' companies (part 4) |
| The same founder, on a positions write (`POST /`, `PUT /:uid`, `POST /:uid/marks`, `POST /:uid/distributions`), a deeper `GET`, or any other paywalled path | 402 | 402, unchanged |
| Founder below Raise + Grow with no fund, on the five reads | 402 | 402, unchanged |
| Founder who stops being GP of record | — | 402 on the next read |
| Founder on Raise + Grow (with or without a fund) | Passes | Unchanged |
| Partner, advisor, investor, admin | Bypass | Unchanged |
| A GP of record who neither holds Founder nor bypasses the gate (an `exploring` account) | 402 | 402, unchanged: the decision names founders |

### 2. Judgement calls

- **"Founder" means holding the Founder licence**, as D4729 reads every plan gate, not `role === 'founder'`. A partner who also holds Founder below Raise + Grow is checked on the Founder plan (D4729), so they are a founder for this rule too.
- **The exemption is in `requireTier`, not a new middleware in `index.ts`.** A new factory in the paywall loop would be a new `USE` name in a frozen hub. `requireTier('studio')` is mounted only on `STUDIO_PREFIXES`, and the patterns are anchored to `/api/positions`, so no other mount can match them.
- **Trailing slashes and `HEAD` are not exempt.** The list matches the paths the five handlers are registered on, and `GET` only. Anything else falls back to the paywall, which fails closed.
- **The Studio module locks are unchanged.** `services/studioModules.ts`' `lockOf` is synchronous and cannot ask D1 about funds. The one module that reads `/api/positions` (`portfolio_glance`) is an investor-profile module, which a founder's Studio does not offer.
- **The inline `ensureTier` guard is unchanged.** Only the middleware carries the exemption; no handler calls `ensureTier('studio')` on these reads.

### 3. Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/fund_reads_d4636_p5.test.ts` drives the real Worker (`src/index.ts`), so the paywall loop runs before the routes, over the real schema (`loadSchema`) with synthetic fixtures. Six tests:

1. A Free founder who is GP of record gets 200 on all five reads, scoped to their fund (list, marks, analytics, KPI compliance, detail in the investor projection); another GP's company and an investor's holding are 403, an unknown uid 404.
2. The same founder gets the Raise + Grow 402 on `POST` marks, `POST` distributions, `POST` a position, `PUT` a position, a `GET` two segments deep, and `/api/capital`, `/api/funds`, `/api/portfolio`, `/api/lp-reports` and `/api/watchlist`.
3. A Free founder with no fund gets 402 on the five reads and on an unknown uid.
4. A founder reassigned off their fund gets 402 on the next read.
5. A Studio founder with a fund reads their fund and is refused the write by `requireAdmin` (403); a Studio founder with no fund gets the handlers' 403; a partner GP, an investor holder and an admin read as in part 4.
6. An `exploring` GP of record (no Founder licence, no bypass) gets 402.
