## D6137 — Spin-Out Fund figures come from records, and a visitor sees the headline only (#6137)

**Decided by the owner, 2026-11-16** (the three answers on #6137).

The LP workspace, the investor page and the fund brief PDF used to fall back to
`frontend/src/lib/spinoutFundModel.js`, a port of a design file, for the
target, committed capital, 31 LPs, eleven named positions, MOIC, the Cohort 4
list and the raise. Those figures are not fund facts (Q1). They are no longer
drawn anywhere on these surfaces.

**What `GET /api/spinout-lab/fund-metrics` returns in `fund`.**

| Key | Who gets it | Backed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `available` | everyone | a `vc_funds` row with slug `spinout-fund-i` |
| `target` | everyone | `vc_funds.fund_size_cents` (0 is "no size recorded") |
| `close` | everyone | first close 2026-09-15, owner-confirmed 2026-11-16; `outcome_recorded: false` |
| `raise` | committed LPs | `limited_partners` rows: committed, soft-circled, LP count, median ticket |
| `portfolio` | committed LPs | `portfolio_positions` and `portfolio_marks`: positions, invested, held at latest marks, follow-ons, reserve, gross MOIC, as-of date |

Every figure carries `available`. One without a record is
`{ available: false, value: null, reason }` and the page and the PDF draw
**Not recorded** (**Unreadable** when the read failed).

**Who sees what (Q2).** A visitor's `fund` has exactly `available`, `target`
and `close`. The LP keys are absent from the JSON, not null. A committed LP is
a caller with a `limited_partners` row in this fund whose
`commitment_amount > 0`, the "committed" rung of the page's access ladder. The
fund side (admin, partner licence) also gets them. The investor licence alone no
longer does: that is the invited visitor.

**What is Not recorded, and why.**
- *Target*, until the fund record carries a size.
- *Held at latest marks* and *MOIC*, unless every held company has a mark: a
  total over some companies would read as the whole portfolio.
- *Reserve remaining*: no store records the fund's reserve
  (`fund_reserve_allocations` is the fund simulator's plan, not a reserve held).
- *The Cohort 4 pipeline*: no store holds it, so the page names no company, has
  no allocation sliders and the brief prints one "Not recorded" line.
- *The first close's and demo day's outcome (Q3)*: the dates stay, with "date
  passed; outcome not recorded", until Fund Admin records an outcome.

A follow-on is a position row after a company's first (earliest
`position_date`, then lowest id). The portfolio's `as_of` is the newest date
among the records read.

**The PDF matches the page.** Both draw from one read through
`frontend/src/lib/spinoutFundFigures.js`; the page passes that read to
`exportFundBriefPdf({ metrics })`.

`spinoutFundModel.js` stays: the quarterly report, the terms, the tiers, the
access ladder and the program fallback still read it. Its `FUND` raise
figures, `fundModel()`, `COHORT_4` and `allocationCandidates()` are no longer
read by these three surfaces.

**Verified.**
- `cloudflare-worker/test/spinout_fund_metrics_6137.test.ts`: a visitor's
  `fund` has only the headline keys; a committed LP's has the raise and the
  portfolio from the records; no record answers `available: false`.
- `frontend/test/spinout_fund_figures_6137.test.mjs`: the pages draw Not
  recorded, no LP figure from a visitor's read, and nothing from the model.
- `frontend/test/fund_brief_model.test.mjs`: the brief prints the page's
  strings and no model figure.
