## D1513 — The Grid in the editor: the 17 structures, dividers, and the structure's reading order

- **Status:** Built by the S14 session (#3765, deck v2 A4, part of #3716; 2026-11-01).
- **Builds on:** D1499 (#3749: the 17 structures, `boxOf`/`placeOf`, a card's `structure` and `areas`), D1604 (#3885: area selection, the keyboard grid, `GridStage.jsx`), D898 (`placementProblem`) and D1554 (`deckReflow`: text capacity and the cut at a word).
- **Migration:** none. A structure and its areas live in the version's `slides` JSON (D1499), and a divider move changes only `block.grid`.
- **Owner decision:** none.

### Why

S1 built selection and the keyboard grid (D1604). Three parts of the Phase A canvas were left for this issue: the 17-structure picker (pd10), divider drag (pd12) and reading order from `area.order`. All three needed #3749's areas, which D1499 now supplies.

### What was decided

1. **⋯ › This card › Structure offers the 17 structures** (`DeckSheets.jsx`, `StructureSheet`). Each tile shows its areas on a mini slide, with its name and use. Hero + 3 and Big number also say "Recommended for Seed". The 12 presets that fill a card kind by kind stay below, under "Presets", with their own dialog.
   - **Preview.** Pointing at a tile, or focusing it, says what would move. For example: "Full: 2 blocks move to the notes (text, metric)." The preview changes nothing.
   - **Apply.** Choosing a tile is one edit (`structureModel.applyStructure`), so it is one undo step once #3767 brings undo.
     - The blocks, in reading order (top to bottom, then left to right), take the structure's areas in the structure's order. Each block takes the first free area that is big enough for its kind.
     - A block with no area moves to the card's speaker notes, under "Moved from the slide when its structure changed:". A text block moves its words; a metric moves "label: value"; any other kind moves its kind and title. Nothing is lost.
     - The card keeps `structure` and the structure's `areas`, which D1499's save rules accept. A line above the stage says how many blocks moved.
2. **Minimum sizes** (`structureModel.MIN_CELLS`). A chart needs at least 4 × 3 cells. Every other kind takes any whole cell. A structure passes a chart over areas that are too small. A divider that would shrink a chart below 4 × 3 is refused with pd12's sentence: "A chart needs at least 4 × 3 cells."
3. **Dividers** (`gridSelection.dividersOf`, `moveDivider`; `GridStage.jsx`).
   - **Where.** A divider exists where two blocks meet edge to edge and share at least one row (or column). It is a `role="separator"` with its orientation, its value and a readout.
   - **Keyboard.** The arrow keys along its axis move it one cell. The keydown stops there, so the area focus does not move too.
   - **Pointer.** A pointer drags it, snapped to whole cells through `cellAt` on the same slide geometry. The violet readout "7 | 5 → 6 | 6" shows during the drag, and the drop commits it.
   - **One edit.** A move resizes both blocks together (the editor's `onCard`).
   - **Refusals.** A move is refused with its sentence, and changes nothing, when:
     - a side would reach zero cells ("Each side keeps at least one cell.");
     - a minimum would break;
     - `placementProblem` finds an overlap with any other block.
   - **Read-only.** A reader's card has no dividers.
4. **Overflow.** After a divider makes a text block smaller, the stage checks whether the block still holds its words at the deck's aspect (`deckReflow.textCapacity`, the Worker's capacity rule). If not, it says "N words over · Move overflow to notes". Move overflow keeps the words that fit, cut at a word, and puts the rest in the notes under "Moved from the slide when its area got smaller:". That is one edit, and no word is lost.
5. **Reading order.** On a structured card, `areasOf` gives every area (block or free) the `order` of the structure area its top-left cell sits in, and returns the areas in that order. The DOM order of the area buttons, and so the screen-reader order, follows it. `nearest` uses `order` to break a tie before the distance across. A card without a structure keeps D1604's order.
