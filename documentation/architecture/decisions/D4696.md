## D4696 — Offers: the overview, the metrics and the fit rules say what the records say (#4696)

- **Status:** built by S22 on #4696 (Offers audit 4/6, baseline `d85951ca9`, 6 November 2026).
- **Builds on:** D413 (Perk deals lifecycle), 034 and 227 (service requests and the catalogue), 188 (engagement invoices), 209 and 229 (fit rules and signals).
- **Does not build:** the proof and consent model (#4672), catalogue permissions and archive-instead-of-delete (#4680), first-use creation and failed saves (#4688). Where a figure would need a snapshotted request amount (#4680), it is stated unavailable.
- **Migration:** none.

### The question

Five places on Offers said something the records did not.

1. The overview's Perk deals section called `GET /perks/mine`, the viewer's claims on other firms' perks, and drew them as the firm's submissions, reading `title`, `name` and `status === 'live'` off rows that carry none of them.
2. The catalogue's "Booked to date" was the entry's current price times every non-cancelled request. A pending request read as a sale, and repricing an entry restated the apparent history.
3. Visibility's "Won" summed the price of every attributed engagement, cancelled ones included.
4. The fit-rule response and the zone said nothing scores, filters or declines against the rules, while Pipeline scores leads with them and leaves an excluded lead out of its ranking.
5. The Audience fit signal chip narrowed the list and the export and left the profile table showing the whole library.

### What was decided

**One listing view model.** `frontend/src/lib/perkListings.js` holds `perkState`, `matchesPerkChip`, the review and lifecycle labels and the counts. The overview reads `GET /perks/partner` (`api.perkSubmissions`) and draws Perk, Review and Lifecycle from it; Perk deals draws its names, states and strip from the same functions. Live means approved and not ended, on both.

**Requests are counts, never money.** `GET /services/offerings` serves `requests` on each row (`requested`, `pending`, `booked`, `delivered`, `invoiced`, `paid`, `cancelled`), the total as `measures`, the basis of each in `measures_basis`, and `booked_value_cents: null` with the reason. A request stores no amount and no invoice, so no historical value is stated. `sold` is gone. The catalogue tile reads "Requested" with the booked and pending counts, and the column reads "Requested". This departs from the `po1` artboard's "Booked to date" and "Sold" on purpose.

The statuses each count reads are in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/serviceMeasures.ts`. Today only `pending` is ever written for a request, so booked, delivered, invoiced and paid read zero rows, and the response says so rather than letting a zero read as an answer.

**Won is accepted work that was not cancelled.** `GET /offers/visibility` returns, per surface, `engagement_count` (everything attributed), `won_count`, `won_value`, `cancelled_count`, `delivered_count`, `invoiced_count` (an invoice that is not void) and `paid_count` (the firm recorded payment). `won_value` sums `engagements.price`, which was copied from the accepted quote, so it is a transactional amount that a catalogue reprice never moves. The overview column reads "Won (not cancelled)".

**Fit rules are used for scoring and ranking, and decline nothing.** `GET /offers/fit-rules` answers `enforcement: 'score_and_rank'`, `auto_decline: false`, and what the rules are used for and not used for. Pipeline reads the active rules: a budget floor, a best-fit profile and its best fit or weak intent signal become receipts on a lead's score; a declined sector or an absent capability that a lead mentions marks it excluded (the firm's sentence, no number) and leaves it out of the ranking. An excluded lead stays on Leads and passing is the firm's click. The zone, the overview and the Offers rail say the same.

**One row set per signal.** `frontend/src/lib/fitRuleView.js` decides it. Under a signal the profile table, the editable list and the export are the same profiles. Under All the table is the profile slice and the list and export are the library. The tiles and the "who we are not for" card count the whole library and say so.

### What was not done

- No monetary measure for catalogue requests, until a request records a snapshotted amount (#4680).
- The AI ranking route is described, not exercised: a test would need the match engine stubbed. The scoring path is tested on the real route.
- `audienceFitQuery` lists weak-intent profiles under "Best fit" in the ranking query. That is Pipeline's code and is left for its package.
