### Implemented independently of the new data stores

Formation defaults to Lab ventures and sends `scope=lab` to both existing
Worker desks. All startups sends `scope=all`. Changing the scope remounts both
desks, resetting their pages, filters and dialogs, so an old venture's data is
not drawn under a new scope. The filtering remains on the Worker (D946), never
in a browser list. A failed desk does not blank the other.

SL6's Messages and History use D971's paged activity read with the addressed
cohort bound in every request. They keep their own retries and pages. The
capacity check has its own mounted read, `/api/admin/cohort/cohorts/:cohort/capacity`:
it returns the latest stored check event and its time, never recomputes the
past using current thresholds. Counts are parsed only from the scheduler's
known message formats; an older or unknown format has null counts. No event
is Not recorded; a source failure is 503 Unreadable. Legacy free-form event
payloads and staff identities are not returned. These three reads remain
available when the overview or Settings read fails.

Grace and outcome decisions on SL6 reuse D949's in-page dialogs through the
participants desk, locked to that cohort. The former all-cohorts console and
its browser prompts are not mounted. The Users console's Spin-Out admit door
now uses D945's mounted structured admission with an open cycle ID and reason,
plus an unchecked explicit over-places confirmation. It no longer sends the
free-text cohort label D945 refuses. A failed read cannot admit, and unconfirmed
email delivery is stated rather than called sent.

The Programs crumb names `/admin/programs` directly. SL16e reads D984's dated
program copy on a branch; tools mount only for an in-force Lab grant. Missing
copies are Unreadable, not a missing grant. HQ-held pages have no subsidiary
grant. The Request it door opens the existing licence Programs page. SL19a
adds the Lab-specific refusal with the member's Lab and home doors, retaining
the existing role policy and impersonation handoff.

### Interviewed and reached weeks (migration 602, S29 · Claude, 2026-10-27)

S1 allocated migration **602** on 2026-10-25 and returned the remainder to
`state:ready` after #3052 merged. This part closes #3003. Base: main `b87dc4e4`.

**What the migration stores** (`602_lab_interview_outcome_and_reached_weeks.sql`):
- `spinout_application_interviews.outcome` (`held` or `missed`), with `outcome_at` and `outcome_by`. NULL is "no outcome recorded", never "missed".
- `lab_metric_capture`: when outcome recording began, stamped when the migration is applied.
- `spinout_week_reached`: one row per founder per cohort week reached. The first reach is kept, with its source: `deadline`, `scheduler` or `admin`.
- `spinout_week_reach_coverage`: one row per cohort week whose deadline job began with the ledger in place.

Nothing is backfilled. No attendance or reach is inferred from schedules or outcome rows.

**Recording an interview** (`POST /api/admin/cohort/applications/:id/interview/outcome`, `admin_cohort.ts`):
- The admin records held or missed on the latest scheduled interview, once its time has passed. Admin only, behind the Lab grant check.
- It is written once, by compare-and-set: a second answer is 409 `outcome_recorded`.
- A time still ahead is 409 `interview_not_yet`.
- A recorded interview is never cancelled or moved. Both admin cancel paths, the founder's withdraw and the founder's reschedule request skip it. Scheduling again adds a new interview beside it.
- The audit line says which outcome and nothing else.
- On SL3, a past interview offers **Record what happened**, an in-page dialog with two choices and nothing preselected. A recorded interview shows Held or Missed and when it was recorded, with no Move or Cancel. The founder is not told.

**Recording a reached week** (`cohortTiming.ts`):
- At each week's deadline, `decideUserWeek` writes the reach for every active participant with no earlier week failed or in grace. That happens before it skips a founder already decided.
- `applyWeekDecision` writes a reach for every decision, whether by the scheduler or by an admin override.
- A deadline job claimed `new` writes its week's coverage row before deciding anyone. A job resumed from before the ledger may have decided founders without it, so it writes none.
- A failed ledger write never stops a decision.
- What "reached" means: in the cohort, active, at that week's deadline, with nothing earlier failed or in grace, or decided for that week. A founder who left before the deadline did not reach it.

**What `/overview` returns** (`admin_lab.ts`):
- **`interviewed`:** the applications in the cohort pool with an interview recorded as held, plus `awaiting`, the past interviews from after recording began that have no outcome yet. It is Not recorded:
  - when an interview from before recording began has no outcome; the reason gives the count and the date, and recording each one as held or missed makes the cohort countable;
  - when recording has not begun on that database.

  If a source cannot be read, it is Unreadable.
- **Each week's `reached`:** the founders who reached the week and `passed`, those of them whose decision in force is passed. A week without coverage is Not recorded with its reason; an unreadable ledger is Unreadable. Never 0.

**What the page draws** (`labOverviewModel.js`, `LabOverviewBlocks.jsx`):
- **The funnel's foot:** "Interviewed [n] · [x]% of applied", with "[k] past interviews have no outcome recorded yet." when there are any. There is no share when nobody applied.
- **A covered week's heat cell:** "[passed] of [reached]", with "[x]% of those who reached it" in its title and its table row.
- **A week decided before the ledger** keeps "[n] passed", and its title gives the reason. A covered week that nobody reached also keeps its count, because a share of nobody is not a share.
- **The note:** it is the canvas's when every decided week has a share, and otherwise says which cells have none.
