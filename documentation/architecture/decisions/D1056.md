### 1. Where it lives

- **The founder's desk**, at `/grow/reporting` (Desk) and `/grow/reporting/duties` (Duties).
  - Both routes sit ahead of `/grow/:slug` and render `CycleRoute cycle="grow" reporting="desk|duties"`.
  - `CycleRoute` draws `pages/cycles/ReportingDesk.jsx` there, under Grow's header and tab row. It makes no overview read.
- **Grow's tab row.** The history tab is "Reporting" (`CYCLES.grow.history`), in place of "Updates". It is lit on the desk (`cycleTabs` marks the history tab active when its key is the active one).
  - `/grow/updates` forwards to `/grow/reporting`, keeping the query. It is already on the Worker's redirect list.
  - Durable company-building still draws its Updates section until part 4's desk read replaces it.
  - The primary action stays "Share an update".
- **Portfolio › Reports.** The investor's tab and zone that read "Updates" read "Reports", at the same address, `/portfolio/updates`. Each held company's name links its page.
- **The company page**, at `/portfolio/companies/:uid` (Figures), `/reports` and `/terms`. It is `pages/investor/PortfolioCompanyPage.jsx`, in the investor shell, for `admin` and `investor`. The uid is the project's uid; any other shape is no company.

### 2. What is read, and from where

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/cycles/grow/modules/durable-company-building`, `parts.updates` | The desk's History: each update sent, with its counts, opens and replies |
| `GET /api/positions` | The company page's header: name, stage, the round, the amount invested and the first cheque's date, from the investor's own position |
| `GET /api/portfolio-updates?project_id=` | The company page's Reports: that company's updates the investor may read (D889's audience rule), newest first |

- **Each read answers alone.** A failed read says it is not a claim of none, and the other blocks still draw.
- **Not read anywhere yet, so never drawn:** a duty, a period, a due date, late, missing, waived or expected, a frozen figure, a revision, a document, a holding's route. #2424 and parts 2 and 3 store them.
