### 1. The engine (`services/captable.ts`, `services/safeConversion.ts`)

**Every existing scenario keeps its numbers.** Each field below is an addition: a scenario that sends none of them takes the same code path and gets the same shares, prices and payouts. `captable_simulate_golden` passes unchanged, and D1018's tests pin the legacy ledger again.

| Input | What it does |
|---|---|
| `exit_values` | Up to four exits (CT6). `waterfalls` holds one waterfall per value, in order, each computed on its own, never one rescaled. With no `exit_value`, `waterfall` is the first. A fifth value, or a negative one, is refused. |
| a round's `top_up_payer` | Who pays for the pool top-up: `pre_money` (founders, the default and today's behaviour) or `post_money` (everyone). Post-money prices the round on the shares before any top-up, then tops the pool up to its target share of everything, the new investors included. |
| a SAFE's `converts: false` | The instrument stays outstanding past the round (CT6, "Instruments that convert", all by default). It is listed in `outstanding_instruments`. An exit after the rounds with one outstanding is not calculated, with a warning, rather than invented. |
| a SAFE's `mfn` | Read against later SAFEs (§2), never applied to the conversion. |
| `start` | The scenario begins from recorded holders (§3) instead of typed `founders` and `option_pool_pct`. Sending both is refused. |

What a run now answers:

- **Each round's `conversions`** (CT4), one entry per instrument that converted, as numbers the page draws without re-deriving:
  - the cap price, the discount price (each null when that term is absent) and the round price;
  - which price won (`binding`), the price paid, the shares, and the % after the round;
  - for a note, its accrued interest and the amount that converts.

  The cap and discount prices come from the solver itself (`safeConversion.ts` now returns them), so they are the prices used, not a second calculation.
- **A round's `top_up`:** the shares added and who paid.
- **`mfn`** (§2).
- **`post_money_check`** (§4): the fraction, the percentage and `over_100`, for the inputs and for each round.
- **At an exit before conversion,** each SAFE's row carries `election`: `cash_back` or `as_converted`. That is CT4's "cash back, or common at the cap, whichever pays more", which #2446's pre-round waterfall already computed.

**Unchanged on purpose:** a SAFE with no discount whose cap price is above the round price still converts at its cap price, as before. The conversion detail shows both prices, so the page can say so.

### 2. MFN

For each instrument with `mfn: true`, the read lists every later SAFE:
- **Later** means a later `issue_date` when both are recorded (`ordered_by: 'date'`), else later in the list (`ordered_by: 'listed'`).
- **The verdict** is `better_cap` when the later SAFE's cap is lower (or this one has none), else `no_change`.

Notes are not compared. The read reports; it changes no conversion, because whether a holder takes the better terms is theirs to elect, and no election is recorded.

### 3. A scenario starts from Today, the record (`services/capTableToday.ts`)

**`startFromRecord`** turns D1017's record read into the engine's inputs:

- **Holders** take their recorded shares by class kind:
  - common, as the engine's founder type;
  - preferred;
  - options granted from a pool, as `options`, which count in the pool's group and participate at an exit, as if exercised.
- **The pool** is the record's own unallocated figure. With no pool class, no pool line is drawn, which is not a pool of 0.
- **A preferred holder's preference** is what they paid in: the sum of their recorded priced issuances into a preferred class, in the venture's currency. Any other row that put preferred shares in their hands leaves it `null`. An exit after a round that needs it is then not calculated, with a warning. Before a round, recorded preferences are not modelled at all, so no pre-round exit is given.
- **Instruments** are the record's recorded SAFEs and notes that no recorded conversion has converted. Amounts, caps and discounts come from cents and basis points; basis, interest, date and MFN as recorded.
- **What the record does not hold is not guessed.** An instrument without the figure it needs is left out and listed in `not_modelled`, each with its reason and sentence:
  - `amount_not_recorded`;
  - `basis_not_recorded`;
  - `no_cap_or_discount`;
  - `issue_date_not_recorded` (a note with interest);
  - `other_currency`.
- **A record block that cannot be computed** (`nothing_recorded`, `negative_balance`, `pool_size_not_recorded`, `pool_overgranted`) makes the start unavailable with the record's reason. It never yields a partial table.

**Routes** (`routes/captable.ts`, before `/scenarios/:uid`, behind the venture's read gate: `loadProject` then `canReadProject`):

| Route | What |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/captable/scenarios/by-project/:projectId/today` | `today`: the start, its inputs, the engine's result and `not_modelled`, or `available: false` with the record's reason. `model`: the canonical saved scenario, named "Cap table · model" (CT19b), its stored name kept as `stored_name`. `offer`: §5. `assumptions`. |
| `POST /api/captable/scenarios/by-project/:projectId/simulate` | `{ rounds, exit_values, converting? }`. A scenario from Today: the record's holders and instruments with the caller's rounds (each with its `top_up_payer`), up to four exits and the instruments that convert, by name. It saves nothing. Nothing recorded is 409 with the record's reason. An instrument named to convert that is not on the record is 400 `unknown_instrument`. |

**Not here:** saving a scenario from Today, the editor, Duplicate, Rename and Delete. Those are part 9 (#2861).

### 4. The over-100% check (CT3)

Post-money caps that together promise 100% or more of the company (Σ amount ÷ cap over post-money instruments with a cap) answer:
- `post_money_check: { fraction, pct, over_100: true }` and `over_100: true` on the result;
- the same check on each round, from the solver's cap-bound set;
- **no waterfall after a round:** every exit is null, with a warning naming the percentage.

Before a round, #2446's pre-round rule decides, as it did. A SAFE that takes cash back converts nothing, so at a low exit every holder can be paid even when the caps promise more than everything; when the converting caps reach 100% that rule refuses too.

The round's ledger still carries the solver's "these terms cannot all be honoured" event. The figure and the flag go to the page; nothing is quietly rescaled.

### 5. The model's rows, offered as proposals (CT19b)

`offerFromModel` turns the canonical model's founders and SAFEs into part 2's proposal bodies, for "Record them from the model":
- **Founders:** `founding`, with the holder and shares.
- **SAFEs and notes:** amount, cap, discount, interest and date.
- **Basis:** the model's stated basis, or `pre_money`, which is the engine's default for a SAFE that names none.

A holder the record already holds, or has a live proposal for, with the same kind, is left out. The model's pool percentage is not offered: on the record a pool is a class with a size, and `pool_note` says so.

**Nothing is written by the offer.** Each body goes through `POST /record/:projectId/proposals`, so a co-founder's proposal waits for the owner and the audit row is part 2's. The model's own row in `cap_table_scenarios` is kept as it is.

### 6. One record reader (`readCapTable`)

`readCapTable(env, projectId)`:
- answers `source: 'record'` with Today whenever the record holds shares;
- else `source: 'model'` (the canonical model, `modelled: true`, with the reason the record was not used);
- else `source: 'none'`.

**A failed read throws.** The one exception is a database without migration 573, whose missing tables read as nothing recorded.

| Reader | On record | Otherwise |
|---|---|---|
| The deck (`services/decks/axalSpinoutDemoDay.ts` `loadSimSegments`) | Slide 08's segments are today's ownership from the record; `cap_table.sim_source: 'record'` | the model's, as before; `sim_source: 'model'`, and the donut's caption reads "fully diluted · modelled" (`spinoutDeckData.ts`) |
| Build (`routes/cycle_build.ts` `optionPool`) | the pool's share of the fully diluted count (options granted plus unallocated); `option_pool_source: 'record'` | the model's `option_pool_pct`; `'model'` |
| The Raise card (`services/raiseCycle.ts` `loadCap`) | "Today" from the record; the model's planned round run from Today is labelled "After <round> · Modelled". Rows carry the record's `h-<holder>` id. | "Before" and "After <round>" from the model, as before |
| Mercury's SAFE Accept (`services/mercury/raiseFeeds.ts` `acceptSafe`) | records the SAFE and writes no model entry | records it and keeps the model's Investors · SAFE entry beside it, refused (and the record row taken back) when the model has no founder, as D1017 left it |

Granted options are a new ledger type, `options`. The Raise card counts them in the Option pool group, as "Options".
