### 1. FILL_FIELDS: which fields may be filled, how and from where

There are two copies, held equal by a test: `cloudflare-worker/src/services/fills/fields.ts` and `frontend/src/lib/fillFields.js`.

**One entry per field:** `{ page, form, field, label, table, column, class, sources }`.
- `class` is one of `types.ts`'s three (`restatement`, `sourced`, `composition`) or `measured`: a figure a connector or module measured, which Eadwyn never fills.
- `sources` lists `eadwyn`, `connector:<key>` and `module:<cycle>/<slug>`.
- The form's rail option is `form:<form>`.
- **A field with no entry has no trigger.**

**It starts with the forms the canvas draws:**
- AF1 and AF3, Build › Solution design's **Add feature**:
  - Feature (`mvp_features.title`): composition, from GitHub or Eadwyn;
  - Answers pain theme and Tests hypothesis: restatements, picked from Validate's pain themes and unretired hypotheses, or from Eadwyn;
  - Effort: composition, Eadwyn's.
  - **Impact is left out:** it is the founder's call, and the canvas draws it with no trigger.
- One **measured** field: Grow › Revenue and traction's MRR (`project_metrics.mrr`), from Stripe or Mercury, never Eadwyn.
- Part 5 (#3489) adds the founder's other forms, in parts of at most 30 fields.

**The registry's own check** (`fillFieldProblems`, empty on `main`) refuses:
- a measured field that lists Eadwyn;
- a field the never-fill registry names;
- an unknown class or source;
- a field listed twice, or with no source;
- a form whose fields write two tables.

`offeredToEadwyn` is the second lock: Eadwyn is listed, the field is not measured, and the field is not never-fill.

### 2. Migration 643: where a value came from, and what it replaced

`fill_provenance` gains seven nullable columns:

### 4. marksForRows

`marksForRows(env, table, ids, current)` returns, per row and column, the mark of a value that **still holds what a fill wrote**.

- The newest provenance row per column counts only while the row's value equals what it wrote. That is `filledColumns`' rule, so a value typed over is the member's.
- An undone row (`undone_at`) is never a mark.
- A mark carries its source (`source_kind`, `source_ref`, `source_label`, `source_as_of`), `edited`, the run, the rail proposal and the provenance id that part 3's undo names. **It carries no model.**
- A failed read throws, and the caller answers Unreadable.

`claimFills` (Experiments' claims) and the market read are its two special cases. Part 4 moves their pages onto it.

### 5. GET /api/fills/sheet

`routes/fills.ts`, mounted at `/api/fills`; the client is `frontend/src/lib/api/fills.js` (`fillsApi.sheet`), one line in `api.js`.

**The request:** `GET /api/fills/sheet?page=&form=&project=&record=`.

#2431 names `page`, `form` and `record`. The read also takes `project`, the venture, because an Add form has no record yet and its sources are the venture's.

**Per field:**
- the value now (for a record);
- its mark;
- what each source has waiting;
- the choices a module source offers;
- the never-fill flag;
- `offered_to_eadwyn`;
- `trigger`: shown to someone who may fill, on a blank field that some source can fill and that is not never-fill.

**Then:**
- each source's state (`ok`, `not_connected`, `unreadable`) and as-of;
- **Eadwyn's quote:** the member's own average charge for this form's option, over their settled runs. With no run it reads `runs: 0, average_microusd: null`, never a zero.

**FILL_SOURCES** (`services/fills/sources.ts`), each authorised as its own store is:
- **eadwyn:** the member's own `ai_rail_proposals` that are still `proposed` for `form:<form>` and this record. Nobody else's, none decided, none for a field the form lacks or that is never-fill, and the newest per field.
- **connector:** the member's own connection for that provider: connected or not, and its last sync. Its proposals arrive with PIPE-BUILD, so it offers no value yet.
- **module:** this venture's pain themes, or its unretired hypotheses, offered as choices.

**Who sees what:**
- Anyone who can read the venture gets the marks.
- `can_fill` (the triggers, waiting values and choices) needs write access in the member's own session. A reader, such as an advisor on the venture, and a support session get marks and no trigger.
- Another founder is refused 403. An unknown form is 404 `unknown_form`, and another venture's record is `record_not_found`.

**One read per block.** The marks, each source and the quote fail on their own (`marks_state: 'unreadable'`, a source's `state`, the quote's `state`). A failed mark is never drawn as "typed by hand".

**Rows** are read by one literal statement per registered table (`ROW_READS`), so no table name is spliced into SQL. A test holds `ROW_READS` to `FILL_FIELDS`' tables.
