### 1. People (KYC1, KYC2, KYC11–KYC16)

People reads part 1's queue (`GET /api/kyc/admin/people`) and its figures (`/admin/figures`). Each read is logged by the Worker. The older `/admin/queue` is no longer read by any page; the route stays.

- **The figures.** Four tiles and two charts.
  - **Waiting** and **Needs information** come from the queue's counts. Those follow the filters, so with a filter applied the tile says "Under these filters".
  - **Decided, last 4 weeks** adds the recorded approvals, rejections and requests for information.
  - **The median time to a decision** covers the Worker's 90 days.
  - **How long they have waited** draws the four wait bands.
  - **Decisions per week** covers eight weeks, this one last. A week with no recorded decision is drawn dashed as Not recorded, never as a zero bar: the history began with migration 614 and holds no earlier decision.
  - Each chart has View as table.
- **The status chips:** Waiting, Needs information, Approved, Rejected, Expired, Not started and All, each with its count.
  - All is every case someone has opened. It leaves Not started out, as the Worker's `all` does.
  - No counts read shows no number, never a zero.
- **Search** covers the name or email.
- **The filters** are the ones the Worker reads: ID type, country, one flag (PEP, Automatic check failed, No document) and assigned (anyone, me, no one).
  - They apply together on Show results.
  - Each applied filter is a chip that clears it. Clear all clears them all.
- **The sorts:** Waiting longest first (the default), Submitted newest, Decided newest, and Name A to Z.
- **Paging** at 10, 25 or 50 rows.
- **Each row** shows:
  - the person;
  - the profile;
  - the status pill;
  - the wait, which is "Under a day" and never "0 days";
  - the submitted date, or "On the member" for Needs information;
  - the ID type and country;
  - the flags;
  - the assignee.
- **The row menu** has Open case and Assign to….
- **The bulk bar** appears once rows are picked. It only assigns, one write per person, and says there is no bulk approve.
- **The queue decides nothing.** D648's Review modal and its `alert` calls are gone. Every decision is on the case.
- **States:**
  - Empty: "Nothing is waiting." When filters match nobody, the chips stay so they can be cleared (KYC11).
  - Loading: tiles say Reading…, and the chip counts are grey bars (KYC12).
  - A failed figures read fails its charts alone, and the table still answers. A failed queue read is Unreadable and never "Nothing is waiting" (KYC13).
  - No permission (KYC14) is the route's `guard(['admin'])`, unchanged.
- **On a phone** the table becomes cards (KYC15a), and the filters open full screen (KYC15b).
- **Dark mode:** every status pill keeps its own dark pair, and every light `hover:` has its `dark:hover:` (KYC16).

### 2. One person's case (KYC3, KYC3b, KYC13b, KYC15c)

The case makes two reads: the record (`GET /admin/:userId`, with part 2's `screening` and `hq`) and the case beside it (`/admin/:userId/case`). Only the record can blank the page. A failed case read leaves its documents, history, notes and assignment Unreadable with Try again, and never "nothing on file".

- **The header:** the name, the profile, the status, and the assignee with Reassign.
- **The decision bar** follows below the header (on a phone it sticks to the foot). An earlier rejection, if any, sits under the bar.
- **What they submitted:** legal name, nationality, country of residence, ID type, submitted time, provider and email. The date of birth, ID number, address and phone stay masked until Show, and each showing is logged (D634).
- **Documents:** part 1's several, each with its kind, plus the single document the older form stored. A removed document says when it went and never opens.
- **The seven automatic checks** from the provider's result (`runMockKyc`), with its reference.
  - A provider stub with no checks is Not recorded, never "passed".
- **Sanctions and exposure** (part 2) take one of these states:
  - **Clear:** "No match", only for the Worker's `clear`;
  - **A match:** the count, the severity and the review, with Review match… while it is unreviewed;
  - **Unreadable**, **never screened**, or **no block on the record:** each says "This is not a claim that there is no match."
  - The PEP answer and the sanctions consent follow the screening state.
- **The history** shows the newest first, with who and when. An assignment's note is an id and is not shown.
- **Notes for admins** are saved inline, and the member never reads them.
- **With HQ:** a case HQ holds says since when, and why.

### 3. The decisions (KYC5)

Every change opens a dialog in the page that says what it changes. No page uses `alert`, `confirm` or `window.open`, and the test fails on any of them in the KYC files.

| Dialog | Write | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A · Approve | `PATCH /approve { note? }` | The note is optional. While the screening is not clear (unreadable, never run, a match, or missing) a note of 5 or more characters is required (KYC13b). The dialog says the member is told, and that a referrer is credited. |
| B · Reject | `PATCH /reject { reason }` | Quick reasons, and a reason of 5 or more characters, which the member reads. The dialog says the uploaded documents are deleted. |
| C · Ask for information | `PATCH /needs-info { message }` | What to send, and the member's notice shown word for word as `kycCase.ts` `noticeFor` sends it; the test reads the Worker's source to hold them together. |
| D · Re-check | `PATCH /recheck { message }` | "Their approval ends now", because the Worker moves the person to Needs information (D1229 §4). The canvas's "approval stays until…" choice has no route, so it is not drawn. |
| F · Send to HQ | `POST /:userId/hq { note }` | A note is required. The dialog says that PEP answers and possible matches go to HQ on their own when the member submits. |
| G · Review a match | `POST /trust/sanctions/:id/review { notes }` | "Not the same person" or "Possibly the same person", with a note. A review does not decide the case. |
| Screen again | `POST /trust/sanctions/screen/:user_id` | Screens the legal name and nationality. The date of birth is not sent from the page, because it stays masked. |
| Assign | `PATCH /assign { assignee_id }` | Me, another admin found through `/admin/users` search, or no one. The Worker re-checks that the holder is an admin. |

- **What each status allows** (`peopleModel.actionsFor`), as the Worker allows it:
  - **Waiting:** Ask for information, Reject, Approve and Send to HQ.
  - **Rejected:** Approve and Send to HQ.
  - **Needs information:** Send to HQ only, while the case waits on the member.
  - **Approved and Expired:** the re-check.
- **When HQ holds the case, or its HQ state cannot be read,** the studio has no verdict: the Worker would refuse it (`sent_to_hq`, `hq_unreadable`).
- **The authenticator code** is the app's step-up (BLOCK-AUTH-03).
  - Approve, Reject, Ask for information and the re-check keep `requireBranchNotSuspended` and `requireStepUp` on the Worker, and the test reads `kyc.ts` to hold that.
  - When the check has lapsed, `request()` asks for the code through `StepUpModal` and sends the decision again once.
  - The canvas draws the code inside each dialog. Here the dialog says the code is asked for. A second code field would duplicate the global step-up, which is what the Worker reads.
- **A refusal is said in the dialog,** and the dialog stays open. A paused studio's 423 reads "A paused studio cannot decide checks. Nothing was changed."

### 4. The viewer (KYC4, KYC4b, KYC15d)

- **Inside the page, never a new tab.** The bytes come through `kycPeopleApi.documentBlob`, a raw `fetch` with `request()`'s headers (`rawRequestHeaders`), as `contracts.js` does. The token is no longer read from local storage.
- **Watermarked** with "Viewed by [name] · [time]", with Rotate and 100% / Fit. No download is offered: no link, no save control, the context menu is suppressed on the page, and a PDF opens with its toolbar hidden.
- **Every view is logged** by the Worker (`kyc_document_access`).
- **The refusals (KYC4b):**
  - **Cloudflare Access** answers with a redirect (the fetch uses `redirect: 'manual'`), a 403 `cf_access_required`, or an HTML page. Each reads "Your HQ staff sign-in is needed", with Try again.
  - **A document a rejection removed** reads "This document was deleted when the check was rejected on [date]. The decision stays on record." and is never fetched.

### 5. What is not drawn, and why

- **Limited access** (dialog E, the Limited access chip and flag, Grant…). The grant route exists (`PATCH /admin/users/:id/access-level`), but no KYC read carries a person's `access_level`, so the case could not say whether it is on.
- **Filters with no Worker parameter:**
  - profile, the wait band, the submitted range and who decided;
  - several flags at once;
  - the Investors only and Assigned to me chips.

  The filter dialog says so.
- **On the row:** the automatic-check column (the row carries only the `auto_failed` flag), a sanctions-match flag, and "Re-check" as a flag.
- **"1 of 17 in the queue · Previous · Next"** on the case.
- **Open account in Accounts.** Accounts has no address for one account.
- **On the case:** What this unlocks, Obligations, Renewal (part 4's read) and Eadwyn's case summary (no route reads a case for Eadwyn).
- **HQ's decision** on a case it holds (KYC17b), which is part 7's.
- **"−3 on last week" and "Oldest: 6 days"** on the Waiting tile.

### 6. Found, not changed

`index.ts` mounts `requireCfAccess()` on `/api/kyc/admin/:userId/document{,/*}` only. Part 1's `/documents/:docId` is not under it, so the new documents are behind `requireAdmin` and not behind Access in the Worker.

- **Why it is not changed here:** a Worker mount is outside this part's files. GOTCHAS (h) records that arming Access on a path the edge Access app does not also cover returns 403 to every admin.
- **What it needs:** the owner's Access app checked first, then the two mounts mirrored for `/documents/*`.
