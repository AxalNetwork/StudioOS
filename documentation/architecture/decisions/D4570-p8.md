## D4570 part 8 — Venture scorecards: the six ledger routes, tested and verified (#4570)

#4570 slice 8, after slices 1 to 7 in `D4570.md` and `D4570-p5.md` to `D4570-p7.md`. It covers the six `unreviewed` ledger routes under `/api/venture-scores`. No product code changes: every gate was already right, and this slice proves it.

Unpublished scoring shown to an investor is one of the historical classes #4570 asks to revalidate. That is the main reason this family was taken next.

### Routes and who may use them

| Route | Allowed | Refused |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /:projectId` | the founder entity, an accepted owner or co-founder; Operating Partner and Admin; an advisor the founder shared it with (read-only, no analyst note) | another venture's founder, an advisor with no share or a share switched off, a removed co-founder, a roster advisor, an investor by venture id |
| `GET /:projectId/sharing` | the venture's founders | everyone else, staff included |
| `GET /:projectId/trajectory` | the venture's founders; Operating Partner and Admin (logged) | any advisor, a removed co-founder, an investor, another founder |
| `GET /deal/:dealId` | an investor invited to or committed on the deal; otherwise the venture read's rule | an investor off the deal |
| `POST /:projectId/corrections` | the venture's founders | everyone else, staff included |
| `POST /:projectId/recompute` | the venture's founders; Operating Partner and Admin | any advisor, a removed co-founder, an investor, another founder |

### What the suite proves

`cloudflare-worker/test/authz_venture_scores_d4570.test.ts` runs the mounted router on the full schema.

- **Refusals:** each one is a 403. No credentials, or a disabled founder, is a 401. A refusal names nothing of the venture: not its name, a score, an input or the analyst note. It changes no `venture_scores`, correction, sharing, review or `activity_logs` row. The corrections refusals are sent a valid correction, so the gate is what stops them, not the body.
- **The investor's read:** it opens only through a deal they are on, and only while the founder's investor switch is on. Its inputs are masked until a pairwise NDA with the founder exists. It never carries an analyst note, even after the NDA.
- **Unknown or malformed ids:** an unknown venture or deal, and a malformed id (`10' OR 1=1--`, `-1`, `1e3`), get 404. The id is parsed to a safe integer before any statement runs.
- **Rightful parties:** the last test runs each one through, so every refusal above is the gate. Staff and shared-advisor reads are logged; the founder's own read is not.

Mutation check: 14 of 14 gate mutations are caught. They are:
- each route's refusal;
- the removed-co-founder and role checks in `isFounderOf`;
- the share's `enabled` flag;
- the founder-entity match;
- the advisor's no-notes read;
- the investor's deal and switch checks.

### By design, not a finding

Operating Partners (`partner`) and Admins read every venture's scorecard in full, with analyst notes, and re-score any venture. That is D1256's rule, "This matches the deal page's rule, which lets staff read every deal", and the suite tests it as such.

An authenticated caller can tell an unknown venture (404) from a venture they may not read (403). Venture ids are sequential integers, so that reveals only that an id exists. It names no venture and is not exploitable as the code stands. If the owner wants to close it, the fix is to answer 404 for a refused read as well, as the money routes do (D4570-p6).
