## D5885 — Admin › Approvals › Best-Fit: the consultations board, built, and decided where it is counted (#5885)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-16 to the D5884 canvas (boards AP-20 to AP-31). Part of #4716 (package 2: "a counted Best-Fit item is always reachable and decidable").
- **Builds on:** D5884 (the canvas), #5301 (`routes/consultations.ts`: the list, `POST /:id/status` with `expected_version` and `CONSULTATION_TRANSITIONS`, the history table), D4814 (the native-board grammar: `boardGrammar.js`, `boardParts.jsx`), D5879 (`approvalLaneRegistry.ts`).

### What is built

- **The board,** `frontend/src/pages/admin/approvals/BestFitBoard.jsx` on `bestFitModel.js`, at `/admin/approvals/best-fit` with `/confirmed`, `/completed` and `/cancelled` as path tabs and one consultation at `/admin/approvals/best-fit/:item` as a drawer over the tab it came from (option A of D5884). Its routes are a module, `frontend/src/routes/adminApprovalsBestFit.jsx`, under `guard(['admin'])`; `App.jsx` is not edited.
- **The doors.** `LaneDoor` links the board, as a literal link. The item drawer's "Continue review in Best-Fit →" and a row's "Open in Best-Fit ↗" land on the item itself, by a literal template. Decided's Open ↗ opens the item by its id. The Review line reads "in Best-Fit · Approvals". The three "no review page yet" sentences are gone.
- **Decisions.** Only the Worker's transitions are offered: Requested → Confirm… or Cancel…, Confirmed → Complete… or Cancel…, Completed and Cancelled "Nothing left to decide.". The board's copy of the transitions is held to `CONSULTATION_TRANSITIONS` by a test that reads the route. Each decision sends the version the drawer read. Confirm sends `slot_at` only when the field has text, and starts from the recorded slot.
- **Every dialog** says what changes, who is told (since D5886: "The member, in app" on Confirm and Cancel, "Nobody" on Complete; the result line reports the Worker's `notified`) and what is recorded. Cancel's dismiss is "Keep consultation". Complete is history, not a second approval, and its result line offers no View in Decided.
- **A stale write.** On a 409 `version_conflict` the dialog says "This consultation changed after you read it." and "Nothing was recorded by you.". On `illegal_transition` it gives the Worker's sentence. In both cases it shows the Worker's `current` row (status, slot, the decisions it now allows), and the drawer redraws from that row with only its decisions. When there is no answer it says "We could not tell whether this was saved." and offers Read again. In every case the typed slot time stays in the field, and none of them says someone else decided.
- **Unreadable.** A failed list read shows a banner, "Could not be read" in every summary cell and a dash in every tab count, with no rows. It is never an empty queue and never a 0. An empty read that succeeded says "No Best-Fit consultations are waiting." and shows 0 as measured.
- **History.** The drawer lists the request, then every recorded change: from → to, the actor's name and the UTC time. A consultation decided before history began (10 November 2026) reads "Not recorded", and an unread history says so and invents nothing.
- **Owners.** Requested items show the Approvals owner (the inbox filtered to `best_fit`, D4814), with Assign. The report is "✓ Recorded" or Not recorded only (option A).

### Worker

- `GET /api/admin/consultations/:id`, behind the list's gate (`requireAdmin`), answers `{ booking, history }`:
  - `history` is `{ available: true, since, items }`, oldest first, each item with its actor's name.
  - A failed history read gives `{ available: false, since, reason }` and keeps the booking.
  - A missing booking is 404 `not_found`; a failed booking read is 503 `consultation_unreadable`, never a 404.
- The list and the per-item read now carry:
  - `admin_name` ("first triaged by");
  - the latest recorded transition (`decided_at`, `decided_by_id`, `decided_by_name`), null when none was recorded.
- `approvalLaneRegistry.ts`: `best_fit` is decided on "Approvals › Best-Fit consultations", `decisionPath` `/admin/approvals/best-fit`.

### Not built here

- **Eadwyn for one booking** (AP-21's proposed summary and slot wording). No Eadwyn reader covers a booking yet, and adding one is an AI surface with its own spend path. It needs its own task; the board draws no Eadwyn panel rather than a dead one.
- **The owner of a Confirmed, Completed or Cancelled item.** The inbox reads Requested rows only. The drawer shows "First triaged by" instead.
- **Paging the list.** The list read stays unpaged, as D5884's open question says; it is fine at today's volume.
