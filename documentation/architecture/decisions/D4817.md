### What the canvas decides

- **Overview** leads with NAV, mark coverage and multiples, each with its definition, basis and as-of date; then a Needs attention queue backed by evidence, Reporting and Value-add summaries, and a dense company table. IRR reads Unavailable when no rate solves or a read fails.
- **Positions** (register and mark history), **Performance** (chart and table of the same quarter-end reads, no interpolation, a methodology panel), **Reports** (obligations inbox and detail), **Value-add**, and a **company workspace** (Overview, Financials, Reports, Support, Activity).
- **Money actions** go Prepare → Review → Confirm → Receipt around the existing admin-only mark and distribution writes, naming the company in every step, with a conflict state and a failed distribution save.
- **Unmarked positions carry at cost** for NAV today; the boards say so and leave the policy to #4644.
- Admin Investor preview: one banner and compact "Private in preview" chips. 17 states; tablet 834 and phone boards. No dark board (the brief asks for desktop, tablet and phone).
