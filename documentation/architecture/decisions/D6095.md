## D6095 — Filter the reporting history (#6095)

The Reporting desk filters its loaded history by the row's stored kind,
year sent or submitted in UTC, and title. The module read projects the
existing portfolio update kind so reports and updates from the same period
remain distinguishable. History shows the count against the loaded rows
and identifies the most recent entries when the list reaches its 100-row cap.
A filtered empty result has its own message. The shared Updates section
keeps its existing presentation.
