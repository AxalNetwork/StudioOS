## D1599 — New deck's brief is inferred from the venture's records, and each connector says how fresh it is (#3877)

- **Status:** built 2026-10-31 by S16 for #3877, part of #3716 (deck builder v2, final canvases pd1 and pd2).
- **Migration:** none. **Charge:** none. No model runs and nothing is written.
- **Builds on:** D897 (New deck and `GET /api/decks/new/context`), D896 (DECK_SOURCES), D840 (Mercury is the founder's own).

### 1. Why

`GET /api/decks/new/context` answered a hard-coded fast path: `{ audience: 'seed_fund', length: 'standard' }`. Nothing read the venture's stage or its round. Nothing told the founder that a connector had last synced ten days ago, before a run spent their tokens.

### 2. The inferred brief (`services/decks/inferBrief.ts`)

`inferBrief({ projectStage, round })` reads two inputs:
- `projects.stage`, which takes lifecycle words (`idea` … `grow`) or older funding words (`seed`, `series_a`, …);
- the active round, as `activeRound()` already reads it: the target and the latest `round_calls.instrument`.

The rules are one table, `BRIEF_RULES`, and the first rule that matches wins:

| Rule | When | Audience | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| `series_a` | a priced (`equity`) round of $3M or more, or stage `series_a` | Series A funds | standard |
| `seed` | a priced round under $3M, or stage `seed` | seed funds | standard |
| `angel` | no active round, and an early stage (`pre_seed`, `idea`, `validate`) | angels | short |
| `pre_seed` | a SAFE or note under $1M, or an early stage with a round | pre-seed funds | short |
| fallback | anything else | seed funds | standard, "Not enough recorded to infer; the standard seed deck." |

- **The order matters.** The `angel` rule comes before the `pre_seed` rule, so an early venture with no round is pitched to angels.
- **A target that is missing or 0 is not an amount.** `round.amount` is `null`, never `0`.
- **Every answer carries one plain sentence** (`why`) saying which records led to it.

### 3. Freshness (`services/decks/freshness.ts`)

`freshnessFor(env, projectId, { mercury, items, today })` answers `[{ provider, as_of, stale, sync }]`.

- **Which connectors:** the venture's active integrations switched on for its company (the join `projectEnabled` makes), for the providers DECK_SOURCES reads: Stripe, Carta and GitHub.
  - **Mercury** appears only for the venture's own founder, from their own connection (`mercuryFor`, D840). For a cofounder or an admin it is left out, not marked stale.
  - **A connector that is not connected** is absent.
- **`as_of`** is the newer of two dates:
  - what the provider's DECK_SOURCES adapters read from the connector itself (`PROVIDER_ADAPTERS`: Stripe to `revenue`, Mercury to `mercury`, Carta to `cap_table` and `team`, GitHub to `github`; only connector-graded, recorded slots count);
  - the row's `last_synced_at`.
- **`stale`** is true when `as_of` is more than the provider's cadence old (`FRESHNESS_CADENCE_DAYS`: Stripe, Mercury and GitHub 7 days, Carta 30, anything else 30). Exactly 7 days is not stale. Nothing ever read or synced is stale.
- **`sync`** names the existing `POST /api/connectors/:uid/sync`. This read never starts a sync; the screen asks the founder first.
- **A deviation from the issue:** `freshnessFor` takes the reader's Mercury access and the DECK_SOURCES items from the route. It does not take the user, because the founder check needs the request (`mercuryFor`).

### 4. The route

`GET /api/decks/new/context` adds two keys, `inferred` and `freshness`. Every existing key is unchanged.
- **`fast_path`** is now the inferred audience and length. `FAST_PATH_AUDIENCE` (`seed_fund`) is used only when nothing could be inferred.
- **A read that fails leaves its key out.** That means no guessed `inferred` and no empty `freshness` that would read as "all fresh". The failure is logged with the venture id only.

### 5. For the screens (S1)

- **No new client method:** `newDeckApi.context(projectId)` returns the two keys.
- **A missing `inferred`** shows the founder's own picks with no reason line.
- **"Sync"** calls the existing `connectors.sync(uid)` after the founder confirms.
