### What is built

**Store.** Migration `202611161152_6868_portfolio_rights.sql` adds two tables.

- `portfolio_rights`: one right one holder has in one company, as HQ recorded it from the signed paper.
  - The type is one of pro rata, information rights, board seat, observer seat, liquidation preference or anti-dilution.
  - It also stores the document's title, the clause, the terms in words, and the signing date when known.
  - There is one row per company, holder and type. A type with no row reads "Not recorded at closing", never "none".
- `portfolio_right_requests`: an investor's request that HQ record a right of one type.
  - It says where the right is written.
  - It is `open`, `recorded` (linking the right) or `declined` (with the reason the investor reads).
  - A partial unique index holds one open request per company, investor and type. CHECKs keep an open request free of an answer, make a recorded one link its right, and require a declined one to give a reason.

**Worker.** `routes/portfolio_rights.ts`, mounted at `/api/portfolio-rights`.

| Method | Who | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /:projectUid` | an investor who holds the company; an admin | The investor reads the six rights in order, each recorded or not, and their own requests. Not held and not found answer alike (403 `not_held`). The admin reads every holder's rights, every request (with who asked and the seat holder it records for) and the company's holders. |
| `POST /requests` | an investor, not an admin | Held companies only. A second open request for the same type is 409 `request_open`, with the open one. Every active admin is told in-app. |
| `PUT /:projectUid/holders/:holderId/:type` | admin | Records or corrects one holder's right. The holder must hold the company through a confirmed commitment. With `request_uid`, the same write answers that open request (same company, type and seat holder) as recorded, and the investor is told. Audited (`portfolio.right_recorded` or `portfolio.right_corrected`). |
| `PATCH /requests/:uid` | admin | Declines an open request with a reason. Audited, and the investor is told. An answered request is not reopened (409). |

`seatOwnerId` in `_investorProjectScope.ts` is now exported, so a seat member reads the seat holder's rights.

**Investor page.** On `/portfolio/companies/:uid/financials#ownership-rights` (`OwnershipRights.jsx`, model `rightsModel.js`), above the duties and the cap table:

- **The holding line.** Holding route, rounds, invested and ownership as recorded with its date. Each reads Not recorded with what is missing.
- **Rights under your documents.** First the reporting duties row: a count from the duties read, or that read's own state. A failed duties read is not "none".
  - Then the six rights, each with its document, clause and signing date, what is recorded, and what you can do: Read only, or for an unrecorded right **Ask HQ to record…**, or Requested with its date.
  - When no duty and no right is recorded (PRM19 a), the section says so plainly and offers **Ask HQ to record a right…**.
- **The ask.** The ask form names the right and where it is written. It is checked before sending. A refusal is worded by its `code`, so a Worker detail never reaches the page.
- **Your requests.** These list newest first as Requested, Recorded or Declined, with HQ's reason.
- **Failed reads.** A failed rights read says so with Retry and offers no ask. The holding line, duties and cap table stand on their own.
- **Support sessions.** A support session reads the rows and is offered no ask (`can_act`, as for Ask and Waive).
- **Unchanged.** The cap table not shared still reads its CT15n Refused state with Ask for access (`InvestorCapTablePanel`).

**HQ's side.** `RightsAdmin.jsx` on the admin position sheet (Cap Table tab), under Investors' requests:

- **The queue.** Open requests come first, oldest first. Record… is prefilled with the seat holder and the right, and its save answers the request. Decline… takes a reason.
- **The holders.** Each holder's six rights follow, with Record… or Correct….
- **Answered requests** follow last.
- **States.** A failed write keeps its form and says why. A request answered meanwhile is read again.

### Departures from the canvas, recorded

- **Your rounds.** PRM18's "Your rounds" table is not repeated in Ownership & rights. The same rounds, with shares and price Not recorded where they are, are the Financials tab's Investment history directly above. The holding line names the rounds and the latest.
- **Who records rights.** Rights are recorded by HQ from the signed paper (the admin panel), not captured automatically at closing. The template registry `INFORMATION_RIGHTS` covers reporting duties only, so a right inferred from a template would be a guess.
- **The ask.** It is an inline form in the section, like D6205's Ask HQ, not a separate sheet. PRM19 draws only the button.
- **The failed-read word.** It stays Unreadable, as on the rest of the company workspace. D5129 Q2 (Unavailable) is still open, and changing one section alone would make the page use two words.
