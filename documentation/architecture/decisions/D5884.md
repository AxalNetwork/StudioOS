### What the canvas decides

- Best-Fit gets its own board at `/admin/approvals/best-fit`, with tabs `/confirmed`, `/completed` and `/cancelled` as path segments, and the item at `/admin/approvals/best-fit/:item`. It uses the AP-08 to AP-13 board grammar.
- A requested item offers Confirm… and Cancel… only. A confirmed item offers Complete… and Cancel…. Completed and Cancelled are read-only ("Nothing left to decide.").
- Complete is history, not a second approval. Cancel is a danger action that cannot be undone. No dialog has a note field.
- Every dialog says what changes, who is told and what is recorded. Until #5886 is decided, "who is told" reads "Nobody yet. The member is not notified by this decision."
- The page sends `slot_at` only when the field has text, because the Worker blanks a recorded slot when sent an empty string.
- A stale write (`version_conflict`, `illegal_transition`) or no answer keeps the typed slot time and redraws from the 409 `current` row. It never says someone else decided.
- Eadwyn, opened on purpose, proposes a summary and a slot wording. Accepting only fills the Confirm field.
- The fit report shows Recorded or Not recorded only. Decided rows before 10 November 2026 read Not recorded.
