## D6252 — Eadwyn's fund brief and letter opening on the fund pages (#6252)

**Status:** Built. The owner answered §5's question with A on 2026-11-15: the brief's Accept keeps a `fund_notes` row.

The F1/F2 canvas (`design/canvases/integrated/ops-data.js`, `fd-page` and `fd-report`) gives each fund page's rail one
Eadwyn task, and D631 shipped the pages without them:

- **Fund brief** (`/funds/:fundId`): "Draft a one-paragraph description of this fund from its recorded facts. Accept
  writes a note. It changes no fund record."
- **Letter draft** (`/funds/:fundId/reporting/:periodId`): "Draft the letter's opening paragraph from this period's
  recorded capital movements. Accept writes into the draft. Issuing stays a human click."

### 1. The Worker: one router, two POSTs, nothing stored

`cloudflare-worker/src/routes/fund_drafts.ts` mounts at `/api/funds`, ahead of `funds.ts`:

- `POST /api/funds/:id/drafts/brief`
- `POST /api/funds/:id/report-periods/:periodId/drafts/letter`

What they read and say lives in `services/fundDrafts.ts`. Each POST:

- runs behind `requireFundGp`, the gate every fund write uses;
- reads the records and refuses before any model call when there is nothing to draft from;
- calls `run()` with `workspace_explain`, as every zone draft does, so the run is metered against the member's budget,
  logged with its page, option and record, and refused in the switch's, budget's or guard's own words
  (`runRefusalView`);
- answers `{ draft: { body, sources, model, cost_usd, stored: false } }`.

**Nothing is written.** The draft is not stored; the page holds it until Accept or Discard.

### 2. Who sees it

- An LP or reader who cannot manage the fund sees no band. The Worker answers them as the fund's other operations do:
  404 when they are not its GP, and 402 first when they are below the institutional tier.
- An admin operates every fund, as `requireFundGp` allows.
- The letter's band shows only on a period the viewer can edit: managed by them and not issued. An issued period is
  refused with 409.

### 3. The material and its sources

- **Brief.** The fund's recorded facts from `readFundPage`: status, vintage, target size, LPs counted live with their
  commitment, invested amount, GP of record, recorded providers and the LPA's status.
  - A fact that is not recorded is not material. With no LP, the page's $0.00 commitment is an empty sum, not a fact.
  - A fund with nothing beyond its name is refused: "This fund has no recorded facts beyond its name, so there is
    nothing to draft from."
- **Letter.** The period's capital calls (recorded between its start and end dates, inclusive) and its paid
  distributions, summed per date and type because they are one row per LP.
  - A period with no dates is refused, and so is one with no movement: "This period has no recorded capital calls or
    paid distributions, so there is nothing to draft from."

Each fact or movement is numbered. The instruction asks the model to cite [n], and the band lists what each number is,
with its date.

**The library skill `lp-quarterly-letter-draft` is reconciled, not duplicated.** That skill drafts a whole letter in an
agent run. This drafts only the opening paragraph a GP accepts into the editor, so it keeps the skill's rules rather
than its prompt: amounts as recorded with their dates, "Not recorded" for what is missing, and never a projected
return. The Worker test holds both texts to "Never project returns".

### 4. The page

- **The bands sit on the page.** The shared WorkerRail draws no task of its own: its `action` prop is retired until
  #5130's shell-wide rail. So the two drafts are page bands (`frontend/src/pages/FundDraftBand.jsx`) in the canvas's
  words, beside the rail rather than in it, as ZoneDraft's are. When #5130 lands, its canvas decides whether they move
  into the rail; the Worker does not change.
- **Nothing runs on mount.** The member presses Draft. A refusal is the Worker's sentence beside the button.
- **Letter Accept.** It puts the paragraph first in the letter, in the unsaved editor. Save draft and Issue stay the
  page's own buttons, and the leave guard covers the unsaved change.
  - A letter already at its 50 paragraphs refuses the opening rather than dropping one.
  - A period that is not yet saved, or whose dates changed and are not saved, is told to save first: the opening is
    drafted from the saved dates.
- **Not in the investor rail-option registry.** `lib/railOptions/investor.js` lists ZoneDraft bands on investor board
  files, and its tests hold every row to that. These bands are neither, so they are not rows. Each press still sends
  its option key (`draft:fund-brief`, `draft:letter-opening`) for the usage log.

### 5. Where the fund brief is kept on Accept: a fund note (owner's answer A)

No existing store fitted: `vc_funds` is the fund record, which the canvas says Accept must not change, and the
zone-draft store (`research_zone_drafts`) is private to the member who ran the draft. The owner chose A (2026-11-15,
on #6252) over B (the member's own draft store) and C (no store).

- **The table.** Migration `202611152331_6252_fund_notes.sql` adds `fund_notes` (`fund_id`, `author_user_id`, `kind`
  `'brief'`, `body`, `sources_json`, `created_at`), indexed on fund, kind and date. A row is a kept brief; nothing
  edits or deletes one, so a newer Accept is a newer row.
- **Accept.** `POST /api/funds/:id/notes/brief` takes the drafted paragraph and the sources it cites, behind the same
  `requireFundGp` gate as the draft. It refuses, in its own words and before writing:
  - an empty paragraph;
  - one over 4,000 characters;
  - any source that is not one of this fund's recorded facts as read now. A source must match a current fact by kind,
    reference and number, and the stored source is the Worker's own record, not the page's copy.
  It writes one row and changes no fund record. It never calls the model.
- **The read.** `GET /api/funds/:id/notes/brief` answers the newest brief with its author and date, or `null`, to
  everyone who can manage the fund (the same gate); an LP or reader is refused as for the draft.
- **The page.** The brief band's Accept writes the note, and the fund page then shows "Fund brief · kept": the
  paragraph, who kept it and when, and what it was drawn from. A refused Accept keeps the draft on screen with the
  Worker's sentence. An unreadable note says so; none kept shows nothing. The band's Accept may now be async
  (`FundDraftBand`), which the letter's synchronous Accept still satisfies.
