### 1. The desk (CP13, CP21)

`/admin/contracts/closings` reads `GET /api/admin/closings`. It no longer reads the deals list, as D1213 did.

- **Rows:** one per open closing, deadline first. Each row shows:
  - the company and round;
  - lines, and how many wait;
  - committed and received, in integer cents with the closing's currency;
  - **Waiting on:** the Worker's `next_actor`, drawn as Studio team, Investors or Founder, with its reason;
  - the deadline, marked under 14 days and past it;
  - the owner.

  A figure the Worker does not return reads "Not recorded", never `$0.00`.
- **Search, filters and sort:**
  - search by company or round;
  - filter by Waiting on, or by Owner;
  - sort by deadline, company or committed. A closing with no deadline, or no committed figure, sorts last.
- **Tiles:**
  - Open, and Waiting on the studio team, investors or the founder, all from the desk;
  - Closed this quarter, from `GET /api/closings`, which lists every closing for an admin.

  Each tile fails with its own read and reads "Unreadable", never 0.
- **Open a closing:** an in-page dialog.
  - It lists only deals that hold no open closing, from `GET /api/deals` and `GET /api/closings`.
  - It takes an optional round label and deadline, posts `POST /api/closings`, and lands on the new closing.
  - With no deal chosen, it refuses and writes nothing.
- **CP21 (dark):** every light surface, text and border in these files has a dark counterpart. A test reads each quoted class string on its own, including a `const` and each branch of a ternary.
- **The Route and Step filters, and the Step column, are not built.** The desk carries no route or step per closing; §5 says so on the page.

### 2. One closing (CP14) and its activity (CP15)

`/admin/contracts/closings/:dealId` keeps the deal-id address, as D1215 §5 decided.

**Finding the closing.** The page reads `GET /api/closings` and takes the deal's open closing, else its newest. It then reads `GET /api/closings/:uid`.
- With no closing, it draws D1213's deal room, with Open a closing.
- A failed list read says so, and claims nothing.

**What it draws:**
- **Tiles:** Lines, Committed (`total_cents`), Received (received lines only) and the deadline.
- **Lines:** each line's investor, amount, route and vehicle, paper and status.
  - **Status:** Waiting for money, Sent (not yet confirmed), Received or Withdrawn. D1215 recomputes it from evidence; only receiving-side evidence makes a line Received.
  - **Paper:** each line's staged envelopes, from `GET /api/legal/esign/staged/line/:commitmentId`, read one line at a time. A failed read blanks only that cell.
  - **A Raise prospect line** has no commitment, so no paper is read for it.
- **Line acts**, on an open closing:
  - Choose the route;
  - Prepare paper, for a commitment line;
  - Third-party fund;
  - Open the route, for a syndicate or Axal VC fund line;
  - Withdraw.
- **Mark Closed:**
  - It is offered only when the founder has accepted or dismissed the cap-table proposal. Otherwise the page says why, in D1215's own words, before anyone asks.
  - Its dialog says nothing changes afterwards, and that reporting duties start.

**Axal acts.** An Admin never sees a Super Admin act enabled. `actControl` decides each one:
- **An Admin** gets the act's sentence: the act table's, or D1215's when that table is absent or failed. This holds even when the table says "allowed".
- **A Super Admin** gets a live control, which reads "Requires your authenticator code", unless the table refuses them.

The four acts:
- **Countersign for Axal (`closing.countersign`):** a button for each envelope whose Axal stage reads `awaiting_release`.
  - The button opens a dialog. Only its confirm calls `POST /staged/:uid/countersign`.
  - The step-up is the request helper's: a 403 `step_up_required` opens the global prompt, then the call retries.
  - The signing link is drawn for that Super Admin alone, and is never logged.
- **Commit fund money (`fund_investment.commit`)** and **Record the release (`fund_investment.release`):** neither has a route (D1215 §3).
  - A Super Admin reads "No route records this yet", with no button.
  - An Admin reads the act's sentence.
- **Issue capital call:** a link to Fund admin's Capital calls. An Admin also reads "A Super Admin issues capital calls."

**Activity (CP15).** The activity is `closing_events`, newest first, with a step for each event:
- opened and line added: 1 Paper;
- route set: 3 Route;
- evidence added or withdrawn: 5 Receipt;
- cap-table decided: 6 Cap table;
- closed: 7 Reporting.

A line withdrawn has no step and shows under All. An event this page does not know reads as such, and is never guessed.

The drawer filters by step and exports the rows drawn as CSV. A cell that starts with `= + - @` is quoted as text. The log holds no name, so an actor is "Account #n" (§5).

### 3. Prepare the paper (CP3)

The drawer works on D1216's staged envelopes:
1. Choose one of seven closing papers.
2. Preview: `POST /staged/preview`.
3. Fill the fields the text leaves open, then preview again.
4. Send for signature, then confirm in the same dialog: `POST /staged`.

**A counsel draft.** A document with no counsel text on file is refused by the Worker (`counsel_draft_only`). Its sentence is drawn, and Send stays disabled.

**Fields.** Three facts are sourced:
- the company, from the deal;
- the investor, from the line;
- the purchase amount, from the line's cents.

Each says where it came from. Every other field is typed, and editing a sourced field marks it Typed. A money field (amount, price, cap, valuation, discount, fee, carry) is never proposed: "Eadwyn never fills money." Eadwyn's proposals for the other fields have no read and say "Not read here yet" (§5). When the amount is the line's own figure, the drawer says the paper's amount matches the line.

**Signers.** The company signs first, through the deal list's `founder_user_id`. The line's investor signs second. Axal countersigns last, by default.
- A missing founder account, or a line with no investor account, is named, and the paper cannot be sent.
- Reordering is not offered: D1216's order is the database's.

### 4. The routes (CP5–CP8)

- **CP5, Choose the route:** a card per route, with the canvas's description and what it needs.
  - **Signed paper** is read from the line's envelopes.
  - **The other needs say "Not read here yet":** the receiving account, the identity and business checks, the joinders and the fund's cash.
  - **Every route but Direct names its vehicle.** A syndicate is chosen from the venture's syndicates (`GET /api/networkfx/syndicates?project_id=`) and stored as `syndicate:<id>`.
  - **A route with no vehicle is refused before anything is sent.** The dialog then calls `PUT /api/closings/:uid/lines/:lineUid/route`.
- **CP6, the SPV:**
  - It reads the syndicate the vehicle names (`GET /api/networkfx/syndicates/:id`; D1214 lets only its lead, a member or an admin read it).
  - It draws the members and what each committed, in cents, and the SPV's one payment to the company as the line's status.
  - What each member paid the SPV, the joinders and the members' identity checks say "Not read here yet".
  - `SyndicatePanel` is exported, so the room (part 5, #3215) can draw it for the SPV's lead. That mount is part 5's: `ClosingRoom.jsx` is not this task's file.
- **CP7, the Axal VC fund card:** the same card for both roles.
  - The fund's commitment and release follow §2's act rules.
  - Cash and the capital call link to Fund admin (#2388) and redraw nothing.
  - Whether a position names the line says "Not read here yet".
- **CP8, the third-party fund drawer:**
  - It records the fund by its legal name, as the line's vehicle, with route `third_party_fund`.
  - It shows the amount from the line, the signed paper, and the line's evidence that has not been withdrawn.
  - The manager, jurisdiction and business check have no store and say so; the drawer links to KYC & KYB.
  - It says StudioOS does not run that fund's calls or hold its money.
