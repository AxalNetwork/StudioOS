## D1217 — Company receiving instructions and sender-only Closing reports

**Task:** #3214, part of #2424. **Slot:** S17. **Migration:** 613.

### Built in this draft

Migration 613 creates company-owned, versioned receiving instructions and a
separate reveal-attempt ledger. It also adds sender-only `sent_*` metadata to existing
commitments, the fallback expressly permitted by #3214 while #3212 builds the
Closing line store. It does not change pending/confirmed/withdrawn status.
New reported amounts use integer cents and an explicit currency.

`companyReceivingAccounts.ts` prepares masked reads, founder writes, logged
investor reveals and account-change notices. Instructions use the existing
column cipher with an immutable version identity. Ordinary responses contain
only the account's last four digits, or last two for numbers of six or fewer
digits. Even the shortest accepted number stays partially hidden. The primary company administrator must
also own the project's founder record; an admin or support session has no
bypass. A conditional version change and its actor record commit together.
Stale saves return a conflict; unchanged normalized instructions are a no-op.
The owner can replace instructions that are unreadable after a key change.

The private founder account editor does not use D107’s studio administration
suspension gate. Company ownership and signed-investor access still apply;
reads and reveals remain available. The existing branch-freeze parity test
continues to describe the unchanged studio administration gates.

An account replacement sends the existing notification service's in-app and
email notice to each distinct investor with a pending commitment across that
company's projects. Sent but unconfirmed lines remain pending. Confirmed,
withdrawn and other companies' lines are excluded. The notice carries CP9's
warning to call the company on a number already held. It contains no account
number and introduces no payment pause or hold. Delivery follows existing
notification preferences and best-effort transport; this draft does not
promise email delivery or add a delivery queue.

The prepared investor reveal writes its number-free pending audit entry before
decryption. The ledger records `unreadable` or `currency_mismatch` when validation
fails, and `prepared` with its own timestamp only after successful validation.
An outcome-write failure refuses the full number and leaves the attempt pending.
Immutable account-version and company UIDs stay in the ledger. Deleting an
account version or company removes its bank data and clears the optional
account FK, while preserving these identities, the actor and outcome. This
does not add a hard-delete restriction to the company lifecycle.
`prepared` means the server approved a full-number response; it does not prove
network delivery or that the investor viewed it. Audit failure refuses the reveal.
The response specifies a 30-second display period and carries no-store headers. A client must enforce
that display period when a later task connects CP9; an API response cannot
retract a number already disclosed. A mismatched account/line currency refuses
unmasked instructions.

Accepting the investor's own Mercury Closing proposal conditionally accepts
the proposal, records the sender metadata and writes its actor log in one D1
batch. Stale, foreign, withdrawn, received and already-sent lines cannot be
accepted. The line remains pending: `sent_at` means **Sent, not yet confirmed**,
never Received. Confirmed totals and receiving-side evidence are untouched.
Only currently unsent pending USD commitments enter the existing USD matcher.
No bank request or money movement is introduced. Other Mercury proposal kinds
keep their existing behavior.

### Completed by #3214's follow-up (2026-10-27)

S01 answered on 2026-10-26 (A, and the routing), and part 2 (#3212, D1215) is
closed. The first PR (#3235) merged the work above. This follow-up finishes it.

- **Mounted.** `company_receiving_accounts.ts` is at
  `/api/closings/company-receiving-accounts`, in `index.ts`, registered before
  the Closing router so `/api/closings/:uid` never takes it:
  - `GET /:projectId`: the masked read;
  - `PUT /:projectId`: the founder's save;
  - `POST /:projectId/reveal`: the investor's reveal.

  The client is `frontend/src/lib/api/companyReceivingAccounts.js`, one line in
  `api.js` (D527). No page calls it yet; CP1 and CP9 are a later part.
- **The signed line is D1215's line, and the SAFE bridge is gone.** A reveal needs
  all of the following:
  - the investor's own non-withdrawn commitment;
  - its line on the deal's **open** closing, not yet received or withdrawn (`open` or `sent`);
  - the line's own envelope (`closing_lines.envelope_id`) `completed`;
  - this investor's `signed` recipient row on that envelope, with a signing time.

  A SAFE for the same amount, an unrelated signed document, or a commitment
  with no line no longer reveals anything. The audit INSERT is still the
  authorization point. The currency check reads the line's own currency, not
  the deal's.
- **Sent on part 2's line.** Accepting the investor's own Mercury Closing
  proposal still records `sent_*` on the commitment. When the commitment has an
  open line on an open closing, the same batch also does three things:
  - it adds D1215's `investor_mercury` evidence, on the **sending** side, recorded and accepted by the investor;
  - it recomputes the line's status, which reads `sent` ("Sent, not yet confirmed") and never `received`;
  - it appends one `evidence_added` event, which carries no amount.

  Each write is conditional on the commitment's sent record from this
  proposal. A failed Accept writes no line evidence. A closed, withdrawn or
  absent line keeps only the commitment record. The evidence names the Mercury
  transaction as D1215 asks (`mercury:<id>`), or the proposal when the id is
  outside that shape.
- **The account-change notice** also reaches an investor whose unpaid line is
  open or sent on an open closing of the company's ventures. Such a line may
  come from a Raise prospect with no commitment. A received line is not told
  to pay.

### Verification and owner's live check

Synthetic tests apply the baseline plus all later migrations and use real
SQLite transactions, authenticated Hono routes and the column cipher. Mercury,
e-sign and bank evidence are fixture rows; Gmail is stubbed. Tests exercise
role/line boundaries, encryption and wrong-key reads, durable reveal auditing,
version conflicts, concurrent saves, notices, sender-only totals, stale Accepts
and audit rollback. The standing receipt-writer guard allows the investor
feed only the seven sender metadata columns; status, amount and receiving-side
fields remain forbidden. Mutation results and command exit codes are recorded in
the PR; built `docs/` output is never committed.

The follow-up's tests (`company_receiving_accounts_d1217.test.ts`, 24 tests) add:
- the signed-line reveal, with each broken condition refused: an envelope not
  completed, an unsigned or another person's signature, a line with no
  envelope, a withdrawn or received line, a closed closing, another
  commitment's line, no line, and the SAFE alone;
- a sent line still revealing;
- the line's currency;
- Sent as sending-side evidence with its event, and none on a closed,
  withdrawn or absent line, or after a failed Accept;
- the prospect line's notice;
- the mount order and the client's paths.

`mercury_investor_feeds_d840` gains the closing tables in its fixture. `closing_room_d1218` now pins the mount (it pinned the route as unmounted until this part), and `connectors_api_d651`'s re-export neighbours include the new client line.

**Mutation check:** 16 mutations; 13 caught. Three survived, each because the
same guard sits in both the line lookup and the conditional insert (the
insert's copy covers a race). Mutating both copies of each guard together is
caught.
