## D4609 — One introduction destination, built once and resolved once, and the partner overview counts the book its zones read (#4609)

#4609 (Network audit 3 of 5, slot S11, taken at the owner's request).

### The destination

There are two introduction systems and they are unrelated: **propositions**
(`intro_propositions`, the platform's double opt-in matches) and **teammate
requests** (`relationship_interactions`, D4329, where one member asks a
teammate to make an introduction). Nine notification sites wrote their own URL
in two incompatible shapes — five wrote `/network?tab=introductions&intro=<uid>`
and four wrote `/network/introductions?intro=<uid>` — and each shape was
honoured by a different component.

`?tab=` is read by `NetworkPage` (operator, founder) and by
`InvestorNetworkWorkspace`, and by nothing else. `NetworkWorkspace` resolves
its zone from the pathname and has never read the query, so on `/network` it
rendered the overview board: **a partner or advisor following a proposition
notification landed on an overview with the record silently discarded.** Two of
the four member licences had a notification that reached nothing.

The canonical destination is now built in one place,
`cloudflare-worker/src/services/introDestination.ts`, and is the path form
carrying its system:

    /network/introductions?intro=<uid>&kind=proposition
    /network/introductions?intro=<uid>&kind=request

`kind` is not decoration. Both surfaces render on that one zone and each
matched the uid against its own rows to decide whether to highlight it, so a
uid from the other system was indistinguishable from a deleted record: the
teammate desk announced *"that request is no longer here"* for every
proposition link, about a record sitting intact on the body below it.
`introTargetIsMine` is how a surface asks whether a target is addressed to it.

**No licence appears in a destination, deliberately.** Under #3704 one account
can hold Founder, Investor, Advisor and Operating Partner at once and there is
no switcher; Network appears once in the union sidebar, so there is one
destination and the shell chooses the body from the licences held. The record's
access boundary is unchanged and enforced where it always was — every read of
either table names the caller in its `WHERE` clause, so a record whose licence
the account does not hold is 404 at the API, not hidden by a URL.

**Links already sent still work.** `frontend/src/lib/introDestination.js` reads
both shapes; a link with no `kind` resolves to `kind: null`, which both
surfaces may claim — exactly the uid-matching behaviour that preceded the
contract. `NetworkWorkspace` additionally forwards a legacy `/network?tab=<zone>`
address to the zone it names, carrying `intro` and `kind`, which is what makes
an already-sent proposition link open its record on partner and advisor.
Nothing emits the legacy shape any more.

### The partner overview's source

`api.js` says it at the method: `partnerBook()` is the **firm relationship
book** (migration 224 — a person, their organization, who at the firm owns
them, when they were last interacted with) and `partnerRelationships()` is the
**account-to-account edge** between two platform members. Two tables, two
envelope shapes, two populations.

`boards/network.js` pointed both its Relationship-book card and its
Organizations roll-up at the edge, while the pages those cards link to —
`RelationshipsPage` and `partner/OrganizationsZone` — read the book. A partner
whose firm book held forty contacts across nine organizations could open
`/network`, be told "No relationship names an organisation yet", click through
and find nine. The overview was not stale; it was counting a different table.

The source is per licence now: **partner reads `partnerBook()`** because its two
destination zones do, and **advisor keeps `partnerRelationships()`** because
`advisor/network/RelationshipsZone` reads that one — changing the advisor arm
would have moved the same bug one licence over. Sections, order, spans, columns
and the gap card are untouched; each cell reads a different field on the partner
arm, because a book row has `organization` where an edge has
`other.organization`. The organisation count is the zone's own `rollUp`,
imported from `lib/partnerBookRollUp.js` rather than re-derived, so the two
cannot drift. #3701 / #4799 own how this overview looks; this is which store it
counts, which is the contract handed to them.

`rollUp` moved out of `OrganizationsZone.jsx` into `lib/` because importing it
from the page pulled a page — and `WorkerRail` — into the board graph, which
`rail_pages_d768.test.mjs` caught by reporting a rail on `/events/:slug`. The
census reads imports rather than renders, and it was right to.
