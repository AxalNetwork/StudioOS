### What failed, and the fix

1. **Reports, the chase outcome was not announced.** "Northwind Labs … has been asked", or a failed chase's sentence, was drawn in a `role="status"` paragraph that was only added to the page together with its text. Screen readers do not reliably announce a live region that appears with its message already in it. The status region is now in the page from the start and empty (`region-chase-note`); the message is drawn inside it. `.pf-live:empty` cancels the body's column gap, so the empty region takes no space.
2. **Value-add, a move's conflict or refusal was not announced.** This was the same defect, on the notice that says "someone else changed this entry …" or that the entry is closed. The notice now sits in a status region that is already in the page (`region-va-support-notice`).
3. **Value-add, focus fell to the page when an in-place confirmation closed.** Cancel, a conflict and a refusal all removed the control that had focus, so keyboard and screen-reader users landed at the top of the document. Focus now goes back to the move that opened the confirmation. If the ledger is read again without that move, focus goes to the entry's row. Focus is moved only when it has fallen to the page, so a member who has already moved on keeps their place.
4. **Company Reports, Ask and Waive: focus was lost at the Receipt.** The Review and the Receipt are one shared `Sheet` that swaps its view, so the confirm that had focus went away and focus fell outside the sheet. Both views now pass `focusKey={step}`, the Sheet's existing way to move focus into a swapped view (`ui/Sheet.jsx`).
5. **Company Reports: focus was lost when the Receipt closed.** Closing reads the company again, and that read replaces the Ask control the Sheet hands focus back to. Focus now lands on the obligation card that carries the new record. It goes to the read's Retry if that read fails. The card has `tabIndex={-1}`.
6. **Reports at 390, text spacing (WCAG 1.4.12).** Each obligation card's notes (the due date's reason, the last update's title and KPIs) were clamped to two lines. With 1.4.12 spacing they ran past the clamp and the rest was cut off. A phone card has the full width, so the clamp is lifted below 600px.

### Observed, not changed

- **Two-line clamps at 1280 on Reports.** At desktop width, the Due column's "no due date" reason and the Received column's update title and KPIs are clamped to two lines even without extra spacing. The full text is on the row's `title`, in the Due date tile and in Report detail. The spacing check measures only what the spacing newly cuts off, so these clamps do not fail it. Whether the desktop clamp should go is left to the owner. It is a design choice in PF-04 (#5807), not something this part shows to be broken.

### Not exercised, and why

- **Contrast measured by hand in light and dark.** axe's `color-contrast` rule runs on every board above, in both themes. No separate measurement was made.
- **Late responses across a company or account switch.** That covers a money write whose company changes during a delayed response, and a preview context that receives another account's late answer. The harness answers each read from fixtures, so it cannot switch the active company or account between a request and its answer. The node suite `portfolio_write_flow_d4817` pins the re-read before a write. The preview check above shows no private read is made at all.
- **Support Deliver and Withdraw saved successfully.** The fixture ledger is static, so a successful move reads back unchanged. The browser checks the confirmation, the conflict, the cancel and the history. `portfolio_value_add_d4817` and `value_add_ledger_d6203` cover the saved transitions.
- **Reporting delivery-unknown.** The Ask Receipt says that email delivery is not confirmed. No Worker state for an unknown delivery was mocked.
- **Screen-reader output.** Announcements are checked by structure: one live region, already in the page or an `alert`. No assistive technology was run.

### Mutation check

Each new assertion was checked both ways by hand: break the product code, rebuild the SPA, run that check alone, then restore from a backup copy and confirm the SHA-256 matches. Mutation check (`portfolio.a11y.spec.mjs`): 17 mutations, 15 caught, 2 equivalent (`reduced-motion-skeleton`: the app-wide reduced-motion guard in `index.css` stops the skeleton too; `reduced-motion-global`: the skeleton's own rule and `sheet.css`'s rule still stop it. Removing both at once is caught.) What was broken, one at a time: the chase and Value-add status regions; focus return after a confirmation closes, and its wait for the re-read; both `focusKey`s on the Ask sheet; the obligation-card refocus; the phone unclamp; the company tab's `aria-current`; the company tab's document title; the preview branch in `PortfolioWorkspace`; Log support's `aria-describedby`, and its kept entries on a failed save; the money conflict's `role="alert"`; and the Sheet's focus return.
