## D8772 part 2 — Pitch › preflight: the element on each row, grouping, the headline rules, and the repair re-checked on the same draft (#8772)

- **Status:** built, frontend only, Part of #8772. Part 1 (`D8772.md`) is the canvas and its questions.
- **Owner questions:** built to option A of Q1 to Q3, as the canvas draws them. The owner accepted option A for every question on 2026-11-27 (relayed on #8772).

### Built

- **The element on each row.** `preflightUiModel.js` `rowOf` names the element by what the slide shows (`elementOf`: a metric's label and value, an image's alt text, a chart's title or first series, a link's label), so the two tiles read "Slide 3 · Metric · MRR $34.2k" and "Slide 3 · Metric · Paying customers 38". Each row carries a severity chip, "Blocks export" or "Warning" (Q1 A), and its Show and Dismiss buttons have their own accessible names ("Show metric MRR $34.2k on slide 3"). Rows on one slide that still read alike are told apart by `distinguish`: a text block's first words, else its place ("block 3 of 4").
- **Stale tiles.** `preflightModel.js` `staleTargets` lands each stale or gone refresh row on the block its `field: block:j` names, trusted only while that block still carries the row's `source_key` (the refresh read is of the saved version, the check may be of the draft), else on the slide's block that carries it, else on the slide as before. Two stale tiles are two findings.
- **Grouping (Q2 A).** `clustersOf` puts rows with the same slide, check and headline under one heading ("Slide 3 · The source's date is not recorded · 2 elements") with a row per element, in the panel and in the gate. Finding ids are unchanged, so dismissals keep working; a stale finding that used to be slide-level now has its tile's id, so a dismissal of the old slide-level row does not carry over.
- **Headline rules (PTF13).** `headlineOf` gives the text, the tone, what export allows and what is announced: blockers ("1 to fix before you export or share"), warnings only ("No blockers · 3 warnings to confirm"), and "Nothing to fix" only when nothing is found; what could not be checked is added to each and never called a pass. `GateBody` draws it as a `role="status"` with that announcement, so Export's and Share's own checks no longer print "Nothing to fix" above warnings; the panel's counts region announces the same words.
- **Repair and return (PTF02 to PTF04).** Show on a source finding for a bound metric offers its repair in the finding strip (`repairOf`): with the store on the same snapshot, "Add the source date · 20 Nov 2026" binds the tile again, keeping its value and adding the date; with the store moved, "Refresh to …"; otherwise the line says why there is nothing to press. The change is an edit to the draft. Back to the check re-runs the check on the same draft when it is out of date, shows "Fixed since the last check: Slide 3 · Metric · MRR $34.2k", and focuses the same row, else the next one (`focusAfter`).
- **Draft or version.** With unsaved edits the panel's sub line reads "Your draft, edited from v7", not "v7", and `draftLine` says the export sends the saved version and checks it again; after a repair, "Fixed in your draft. The saved v7 still has it: save a new version, and Export and Share check that version again."
- **The recipient's source line (Q3 A, owner 2026-11-27).** A figure whose source has no recorded date now says so where the recipient reads its source, the counterpart of the warning "The source's date is not recorded". `presentExportModel.js` `sourceOf` writes the canvas's words, "Source: Grow · Measured · date not recorded" (PTF08, "v7 link · proposed"), as the HTML export's appendix already did (`offlineHtml.ts` `sourceLines`); an `as_of` that is not a day counts as no date. That line is Present's "Show sources" and its phone viewer, under a grid card's metric tile (`BlockGrid`) and under a fielded card's figures (`cardOf`). The investor's link viewer draws a chip under each figure (`shareModel.js` `figureChips`, `FigureSources`), which now reads "Card 02 · Measured · date not recorded" by the same rule (`undatedSource`, the cell's own binding first, as `cardOf` reads it). A dated source keeps its day ("Source: Grow · Measured · 20 Nov") and its chip is unchanged; a figure with no source ("Source: Not recorded", "Not recorded on StudioOS", "From records") says nothing of a date; the link's card view and the audience view draw no source line at all. Pinned by `pitch_viewer_undated_source_8772.test.mjs`; `pitch_deck_gaps_d1111.test.mjs` pinned the old line with the date left out (option B) and now pins A. No screen with a visual baseline draws a source line, so no baseline changes.
