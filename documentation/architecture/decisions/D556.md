## D556 — One frame for the Worker AI rail, on every page that mounts it

**The Worker AI rail now sits in one frame wherever the page does not draw the
founder desks' frame itself: `ui/RailFrame.jsx`, styled once by the
`.fwr-frame*` rules in `ui/workerRail.css`.** At 1024px and up it is the
founder panel: docked to the right edge, as tall as the page, a hairline seam,
a white ground, 18px of inset, and a track that narrows to the spine through
`--fwr-track`. Below 1024px the column is hidden and the rail is the floating
button and sheet from D405. Admin Studio gains a rail, workspace "Studio",
whose coverage is the studio glance. Issue #1156. No migration.

**Why.** The owner: "The AIRail doesn't appear properly, on the right. Check
the other pages too. For reference check the Founder pages: you'll see the
AIRail is well configured." On the admin Approvals and Accounts pages the rail
was a plain column of text: no panel, 24px short of the edge, and its model
cards running the page down to their length.

**What was true on main (`df75097731`).**
- The founder desks draw the frame in their own stylesheets, one class each
  (`.fb-worker-rail`, `.a5-rail`, `.fr-capital-rail`, …), inside a
  `*-shell` grid whose track reads `var(--fwr-track, <width>)`. The investor
  desks (`.i4-rail`, `.i6-rail`, `.ir-rail`, …) and `/perks` (`.perks-rail`)
  do the same, from the same canvases.
- `WorkspaceShell` drew a panel too (`fwr-shell-slot`), but only from 1280px
  (`hidden xl:block`). Between 1024 and 1279px a workspace page drew neither the
  panel nor the D405 button, which is hidden from 1024px up.
- Thirteen HQ pages, `HeldZone`, `BranchZone`, Send for signature, the data
  room and the partner home put `<WorkerRail>` in a bare grid column
  (`lg:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_280px]`, or a `lg:sticky` wrapper) inside the
  app's padded page container. There was no border, no ground and no inset,
  and the column sat 24px short of the edge. The HQ pages' literal `280px` also
  meant the rail's collapse left a 280px hole there, which
  `branch_rail_mount.test.mjs` recorded as a known gap.
- `AdminStudioHome` mounted no rail.

**The frame.** `RailFrame({ rail, children })` renders the body in
`.fwr-frame-main` and the rail in `.fwr-frame-rail > .fwr-frame-dock`.
- **Size.** The row is a flex row with `align-items: stretch` and
  `min-height: 100%`, the arrangement `WorkspaceShell` already used so the seam
  reaches the footer. The column is `var(--fwr-track, 280px)` wide, and collapsed
  it gives up its inset like the shell slot did.
- **The dock** is sticky and scrolls on its own when the rail is taller than the
  window (`max-height: calc(100vh - 56px - 36px)`), so the model cards stay
  inside the panel.
- **Docked to the edge.** App.jsx pads its page container 24px (`md:p-6`)
  except on flush surfaces. A frame that is a direct child of that container
  takes the padding back with an equal negative margin: on the right and the
  bottom always, and on the top when it is the first child. It then hands the
  same padding to the body, so the body's content does not move and the panel
  meets the edge. The left is the body's side and is untouched.
- **Nested frames don't bleed.** A frame nested deeper does not bleed, because
  cancelling padding it is not adjacent to would pull it over its siblings. So
  each converted host makes the frame its outermost element: HqHomePage's
  view-as overlay now returns `HqBranchOverlay` directly, and the admin branch
  of `Dashboard.jsx` returns a fragment instead of a `space-y-6` wrapper.
- **Phones.** Below 1024px `.fwr-frame-rail` is `display: none`. The rail stays
  mounted, so its portalled button and sheet behave exactly as D405 shipped
  them. The frame decides nothing in JavaScript.
- **Dark mode.** `.dark .fwr-frame-rail` uses the shell slot's dark pair
  (`#1f2937` seam, `#111827` ground).

**Every host, by how it is framed.** 72 files mount `<WorkerRail>`; the guard
below sorts each into one of four groups, and a file in none fails the build.

| How | Files | Which |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RailFrame` | 18 | `pages/admin/HeldZone.jsx`, `pages/branch/BranchZone.jsx`, the thirteen `pages/hq/*` pages that mount a rail (Accounts, Content, Contracts, Analytics, Branch overlay, Funds, Home, Support, Platform, Switches, Topology, Revenue, Security), `pages/legal/SendForSignaturePage.jsx`, `pages/raise/DataRoomPage.jsx`, `pages/partner/PartnerHomeP2.jsx` |
| `WorkspaceShell` (which uses `RailFrame`) | 13 | the six workspace route files and the seven `pages/research/*` detail pages |
| A rail component mounted in `RailFrame` by name | 1 | `pages/admin/StudioRail.jsx`, in `AdminStudioHome.jsx` |
| Their own stylesheet's frame (left as they are) | 40 | 27 under `pages/founder/`, 12 under `pages/investor/`, and `pages/PerksPage.jsx` |

**The 40 left as they are, and why.** The founder desks are the reference and
the issue says they must not change. The investor desks and `/perks` were drawn
from the same canvases and already draw the same panel in their own stylesheet,
each inside a grid whose track reads `--fwr-track`. Moving them onto
`RailFrame` would re-lay-out forty working pages for no visible gain, and their
widths differ on purpose (258–288px), which a single frame would flatten. The
guard only lets a file skip the shared frame when it is under one of those
three paths and its rail class has a stylesheet rule that sets both a border
and a background.

**Admin Studio's rail.** `pages/admin/StudioRail.jsx` mounts `WorkerRail`
with workspace "Studio", role `admin`.
- **Coverage.** One coverage line per block of the studio glance, in the page's
  own words: "read", "not recorded here" or "could not be read". The blocks are
  Seats, Approvals, Programme clock, Template library, Agreements ending and
  Share rate on a branch, and Accounts, Approvals, Programme clock, Template
  library and Insights on HQ.
- **Failure.** A glance that failed whole draws its one reason, "The studio
  glance could not be read.", and no line per block.
- **No read of its own.** It calls `loadStudioGlance()`, which shares one
  in-flight request with the strip and the cards, so the page still makes no
  read of its own (D512).
- **The test it re-aims.** `admin_studio_overview.test.mjs` used to forbid any
  `WorkerRail` on this page as "a second assistant beside Eadwyn". It now pins
  the one mount through `StudioRail` in the frame. A second Eadwyn and a rail
  inside the cards stay forbidden.

**Behaviour that changes.**
- Workspace pages show the panel from 1024px instead of 1280px.
- Every converted host's rail scrolls inside its panel when it is taller than
  the window.
- The HQ pages', `HeldZone`'s and `BranchZone`'s rails collapse to the spine
  like the founder desks'.
- The partner home's rail column is 280px instead of 300px.
- `SendForSignaturePage` keeps its centred `max-w-6xl` body inside the frame.
- `DataRoomPage`'s frame sits under the page's own header, so it is nested and
  does not bleed to the edge. It is framed, 24px short of the edge, until that
  page's header moves inside the frame.

### Follow-up, 2026-10-06: the window's edge, the footer and the HQ strips (#1196)

**The owner, after D556 merged,** with screenshots of the admin HQ-held
Insights, Community, Programs and Approvals pages beside the founder's
Validate desk: "The Admin's AIrails across different pages are still not
fixed … check the AIrails in Founder profile, and you'll see it fits perfectly
vertically and it's on the right border, and has its original border to
separate body from AIRail. Check all admin pages."

**What D556 missed.** Measured with Playwright on the built bundle across 56
admin, HQ, branch and Studio routes at 2000×1240, against the founder desk. The
API was stubbed, so the shots show layout only.
- **The edge was the column's.** On a licence that is not full-bleed, the page
  container is also `max-w-7xl mx-auto` (App.jsx). D556 docked the frame to
  that container's edge, which is the window's edge only while the window is
  narrower than 1280px plus the sidebar. D556's own screenshots were at 1440px,
  where it is. At 2000px, all 29 framed pages stopped 232px short of the
  window's edge.
- **The height was the content's.** Nothing gave the container a height, so the
  frame's `min-height: 100%` had nothing to resolve against, and the panel
  ended with the content. On 20 of the 29 pages it stopped 7 to 353px above the
  footer.
- **The HQ strips sat above it.** On `/admin/content` and the three
  `/admin/platform` pages, the shell draws H36's Content or Platform strip above
  the page container and across the rail's column. So the panel started 47px
  under the header.

**The fix.**
- **To the edge and the footer (`workerRail.css`).** A page container that
  holds a docked frame drops its cap: `max-width: none`, and no auto margins. It
  also becomes a column that grows (`flex: 1 0 auto`) to the end of the scroll
  column, where the footer already sits on its `mt-auto`. The frame grows with
  it, on the rule every docked frame has. The container knows it holds a frame
  through `:has()`, which `index.css` already relies on. A page with no rail
  keeps the centred column.
- **The strip in the body (`RailFrame.jsx`, App.jsx).**
  - **The hand-off:** the shell offers its strip through `RailStripSlot`, and a
    frame with a rail takes it in a layout effect.
  - **One copy:** the shell then leaves its own copy out. The frame draws the
    strip at the top of the body, where a founder desk has its tabs, so the
    panel runs from the header. Only one copy is drawn at a time, and nothing
    is painted twice.
  - **The same place:** 12px under the header, 16px from the edge on a phone
    and 24px from 768px, with the page's own gap below it. It keeps H36's
    literal links in App.jsx.

**Result.**
- **The 29 framed admin, HQ, branch and Studio pages:** at 2000, 1440, 1280
  and 1100px, every one is flush to the window's right edge and runs from under
  the header to the footer, with the 1px seam and the white ground.
  - **The top:** the header ends at 104px under the Super Admin bar, at 56px
    without it.
  - **Content and Platform:** the strip sits in the body and the panel starts
    at the header.
- **Other hosts in a centred container dock the same way:** `/account`,
  `/wellbeing`, `/learning`, `/learning/me`, `/partner/earnings` and
  `/partner/referrals`.
- **Phones and tablets:** at 390 and 768px no page scrolls sideways, and the
  strip is where it was.
- **The founder desks are full-bleed and untouched.**
- **Tests:** `rail_frame_d556` adds two (11 in all). 15 of 15 mutants are
  caught, among them the cap kept, the container or the frame not growing, the
  strip drawn twice or taken without a rail, and the frame never letting go.
