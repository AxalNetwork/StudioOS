## D716 — Notices and the Subscription Booklet are document kinds: each notice prints its payee's own payment instructions or refuses, and the booklet assembles nine parts, each with its own signature page

Status: Decided. Date: 2026-10-17. Slot: S10. Issue: #1781. Decided by S1 under the owner's standing rule. The start-now PR (steps 1 and 2) starts this record; PR 1 and PR 2 complete it.

The Fund's capital call, distribution and default notices sat in the `resolution` kind "until a `notice` kind exists", nothing built a notice from the records, and the booklet's parts were separate catalog stubs nothing put in order. This adds the two kinds that boards L6 and L7 of the approved canvas "Legal Documents · Templates" draw.

### Decisions settled by the start-now PR

1. **Two kinds, in `legalDocFormat.ts` and its mirror.** `DocumentKind` gains `notice` and `booklet`, and `classifyDocument` checks them first:
   - `NOTICE_SLUGS`: `capital_call_notice`, `distribution_notice`, `default_notice`, `management_fee_statement`, `management_fee_invoice`, `platform_licence_fee_invoice` and `payment_instructions_letter`;
   - `BOOKLET_SLUGS`: `subscription_booklet`.

   The three Fund notices leave `RESOLUTION_SLUGS` and `GP_PARTY_SLUGS_D706`. They are routed by the entity the money goes to, checked right after the D706 sets:

   | Set | Slugs | Party |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | `FUND_PARTY_SLUGS_D716` | the three Fund notices, `management_fee_statement`, `subscription_booklet` | the Fund, through its GP |
   | `GP_PARTY_SLUGS_D716` | `management_fee_invoice` | the GP |
   | `HOLDINGS_PARTY_SLUGS_D716` | `platform_licence_fee_invoice` | Holdings |

   The payment instructions letter takes its sender from its purpose, so it is in no set. The `subscription booklet & lpa` doc type stays an `agreement`: it is an inline e-sign body, not this packet. `securities_legend` joins `POLICY_SLUGS`.
2. **The two blocks.**
   - `buildAddresseeBlock({ to, lpNumber, from })`. To one LP: `To: <name>` and `Limited partner no. L<id>`; the platform holds no LP notice address, so none prints. Between Axal entities: `From: <name>, <AXAL_LEGAL_ADDRESS>` and `To: <name>`.
   - `buildExecutionBlock({ kind: 'notice', axalEntityKey })` is the sender alone: its heading, the `through` line for the Fund, Name, Title and `Date:`, with an empty By line, an empty recital and `isSigner: false`. A notice is issued, not signed. The two invoices print no block; their builder (PR 1) skips it.
   - `buildExecutionBlock({ kind: 'booklet' })` has no parties: the booklet's blocks are per part.
   - Until PR 1 teaches `pdf.ts` to skip an empty By line, an empty recital and a block with no parties, the pdf-lib renderer draws them as blank lines. No notice issues before PR 1.
3. **The booklet is nine parts, in order** (`SUBSCRIPTION_BOOKLET_PARTS`, plain data in both files):

   | Part | Title | Source | Signature page |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | 1 | Cover | `securities_legend` | none |
   | 2 | Instructions and Checklist | generated | none |
   | 3 | Subscription Agreement | `subscription` | the Fund and the applicant |
   | 4 | Investor Questionnaire | `lp_investor_questionnaire` | the applicant |
   | 5 | AML/KYC and Beneficial Ownership Certification | `lp_aml_kyc_certification` | the applicant |
   | 6 | Tax Status Certification | `lp_tax_status_certification` | the applicant |
   | 7 | Electronic Delivery Consent | `lp_electronic_delivery_consent` | the applicant |
   | 8 | Signature Pages | the applicant's counterpart page to `lpa_amended_restated` | the applicant (`unilateral`) |
   | 9 | Acceptance by the General Partner | `gp_closing_certificate` | the Fund, through its GP, last |

   The catalog routing of `gp_closing_certificate` and `lpa_amended_restated` does not change. The booklet prints no LPA body.
4. **Migration 478 (`478_fund_notices_booklets.sql`).**
   - `legal_notices`: one row per issued notice, frozen. It keeps the account's uid and its last four, never a number. A partial UNIQUE gives one first issue per (`notice_type`, `source_ref`), and a second on `supersedes_id` keeps each source's chain linear; a superseding row needs its reason.
   - `legal_notice_files`: each kept PDF, UNIQUE on (`notice_id`, `stamp`).
   - Both are append-only (triggers refuse UPDATE and DELETE).
   - `fund_subscription_booklets`: one live booklet per application (`fund_slug` is `spinout-fund-i`). Its status moves only from open to submitted or withdrawn, or from submitted to withdrawn. Submit sets the frozen copy and its digest once; a withdrawal names who and why; no booklet is deleted.
   - `fund_booklet_answers`: the ticks, one row per field, changeable only while the booklet is open.
   - The `securities_legend` stub ("Securities Legend — Fund I Offering Documents", layer `fund`), inserted only when the slug is free, as 468 does. Counsel writes the legend.

### PR 2: the booklet store (steps 8 and 9, without the paper)

PR 2 builds the store. The two routes that draw the paper (`GET /mine/paper` and `GET /:uid/pdf`) land with the notices PR, because they draw #1776's paper.

5. **The parts resolved against the store** (`services/subscriptionBooklet.ts`, `resolveParts`):
   - The booklet reads the bodies of Part 1 (the cover's legend), Parts 3 to 7 and Part 9. Part 2 is generated, and Part 8 prints no LPA body.
   - A part whose row is missing, a stub or empty is listed in `missing_parts`.
   - Only Parts 3 to 7 carry the applicant's fields.
6. **Fields and the digest.**
   - A field is a task item (`- [ ]`) in a part's body. Its id is `<slug>:<n>:<first 12 hex of the SHA-256 of its text>`.
   - The digest is the SHA-256 of canonical JSON (sorted keys) over each part's slug, version and body hash, and the current fields' ticks, sorted by field id.
   - `GET /mine` answers that digest. Once submitted, the booklet answers the frozen digest, whatever the store does later.
7. **Submit** (`POST /mine/submit { digest }`) refuses in this order:
   - 409 `booklet_not_open`;
   - 400 `digest_invalid`;
   - 409 `booklet_part_missing`, naming the parts;
   - 409 `content_changed`.

   It then writes `frozen_json`, `digest` and `submitted_at` in one UPDATE guarded on `status = 'open'`. Migration 478's triggers then seal the ticks and the frozen columns.
8. **Who.**
   - **The applicant** is the user of an `approved` `lp_applications` row on `spinout-fund-i`; anyone else, and anyone with no live booklet, gets 404 `booklet_not_found`. A support session cannot answer or submit.
   - **The admin routes** answer an Admin on HQ only (a branch gets 403 `hq_only`), and each asserts its D714 act after that refusal. That act refuses a support session:

     | Act | Routes |
     | --- | --- |
     | `fund_booklet.read` | the list, which carries no ticks; one booklet with its ticks, audited as `fund_admin.fund_booklet.read` |
     | `fund_booklet.withdraw` | a withdrawal, which needs a 10–200 character reason and is audited |

     Both acts are Admin tier, owner `#1781`.
   - `/api/fund-booklets` is in `COOL_OFF_PREFIXES`.
   - `routes/fund_booklets.ts` is in `FUND_MODULES`. `services/subscriptionBooklet.ts` is not: it does not match D707's pattern.
