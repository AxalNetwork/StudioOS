## D716 — Notices and the Subscription Booklet are document kinds: each notice prints its payee's own payment instructions or refuses, and the booklet assembles nine parts, each with its own signature page

Status: Decided. Date: 2026-10-17. Slot: S10. Issue: #1781. Decided by S1 under the owner's standing rule. The start-now PR (steps 1 and 2) starts this record; PR 1 and PR 2 complete it.

The Fund's capital call, distribution and default notices sat in the `resolution` kind "until a `notice` kind exists", nothing built a notice from the records, and the booklet's parts were separate catalog stubs nothing put in order. This adds the two kinds that boards L6 and L7 of the approved canvas "Legal Documents · Templates" draw.

### Decisions settled by the start-now PR

1. **Two kinds, in `legalDocFormat.ts` and its mirror.** `DocumentKind` gains `notice` and `booklet`, and `classifyDocument` checks them first:
   - `NOTICE_SLUGS`: `capital_call_notice`, `distribution_notice`, `default_notice`, `management_fee_statement`, `management_fee_invoice`, `platform_licence_fee_invoice` and `payment_instructions_letter`;
   - `BOOKLET_SLUGS`: `subscription_booklet`.

   The three Fund notices leave `RESOLUTION_SLUGS` and `GP_PARTY_SLUGS_D706`. They are routed by the entity the money goes to, checked right after the D706 sets:

   | Set | Slugs | Party |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | `FUND_PARTY_SLUGS_D716` | the three Fund notices, `management_fee_statement`, `subscription_booklet` | the Fund, through its GP |
   | `GP_PARTY_SLUGS_D716` | `management_fee_invoice` | the GP |
   | `HOLDINGS_PARTY_SLUGS_D716` | `platform_licence_fee_invoice` | Holdings |

   The payment instructions letter takes its sender from its purpose, so it is in no set. The `subscription booklet & lpa` doc type stays an `agreement`: it is an inline e-sign body, not this packet. `securities_legend` joins `POLICY_SLUGS`.
2. **The two blocks.**
   - `buildAddresseeBlock({ to, lpNumber, from })`. To one LP: `To: <name>` and `Limited partner no. L<id>`; the platform holds no LP notice address, so none prints. Between Axal entities: `From: <name>, <AXAL_LEGAL_ADDRESS>` and `To: <name>`.
   - `buildExecutionBlock({ kind: 'notice', axalEntityKey })` is the sender alone: its heading, the `through` line for the Fund, Name, Title and `Date:`, with an empty By line, an empty recital and `isSigner: false`. A notice is issued, not signed. The two invoices print no block; their builder (PR 1) skips it.
   - `buildExecutionBlock({ kind: 'booklet' })` has no parties: the booklet's blocks are per part.
   - Until PR 1 teaches `pdf.ts` to skip an empty By line, an empty recital and a block with no parties, the pdf-lib renderer draws them as blank lines. No notice issues before PR 1.
3. **The booklet is nine parts, in order** (`SUBSCRIPTION_BOOKLET_PARTS`, plain data in both files):

   | Part | Title | Source | Signature page |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | 1 | Cover | `securities_legend` | none |
   | 2 | Instructions and Checklist | generated | none |
   | 3 | Subscription Agreement | `subscription` | the Fund and the applicant |
   | 4 | Investor Questionnaire | `lp_investor_questionnaire` | the applicant |
   | 5 | AML/KYC and Beneficial Ownership Certification | `lp_aml_kyc_certification` | the applicant |
   | 6 | Tax Status Certification | `lp_tax_status_certification` | the applicant |
   | 7 | Electronic Delivery Consent | `lp_electronic_delivery_consent` | the applicant |
   | 8 | Signature Pages | the applicant's counterpart page to `lpa_amended_restated` | the applicant (`unilateral`) |
   | 9 | Acceptance by the General Partner | `gp_closing_certificate` | the Fund, through its GP, last |

   The catalog routing of `gp_closing_certificate` and `lpa_amended_restated` does not change. The booklet prints no LPA body.
4. **Migration 478 (`478_fund_notices_booklets.sql`).**
   - `legal_notices`: one row per issued notice, frozen. It keeps the account's uid and its last four, never a number. A partial UNIQUE gives one first issue per (`notice_type`, `source_ref`), and a second on `supersedes_id` keeps each source's chain linear; a superseding row needs its reason.
   - `legal_notice_files`: each kept PDF, UNIQUE on (`notice_id`, `stamp`).
   - Both are append-only (triggers refuse UPDATE and DELETE).
   - `fund_subscription_booklets`: one live booklet per application (`fund_slug` is `spinout-fund-i`). Its status moves only from open to submitted or withdrawn, or from submitted to withdrawn. Submit sets the frozen copy and its digest once; a withdrawal names who and why; no booklet is deleted.
   - `fund_booklet_answers`: the ticks, one row per field, changeable only while the booklet is open.
   - The `securities_legend` stub ("Securities Legend — Fund I Offering Documents", layer `fund`), inserted only when the slug is free, as 468 does. Counsel writes the legend.

### PR 2: the booklet store (steps 8 and 9, without the paper)

PR 2 builds the store. The two routes that draw the paper (`GET /mine/paper` and `GET /:uid/pdf`) land with the notices PR, because they draw #1776's paper.

5. **The parts resolved against the store** (`services/subscriptionBooklet.ts`, `resolveParts`):
   - The booklet reads the bodies of Part 1 (the cover's legend), Parts 3 to 7 and Part 9. Part 2 is generated, and Part 8 prints no LPA body.
   - A part whose row is missing, a stub or empty is listed in `missing_parts`.
   - Only Parts 3 to 7 carry the applicant's fields.
6. **Fields and the digest.**
   - A field is a task item (`- [ ]`) in a part's body. Its id is `<slug>:<n>:<first 12 hex of the SHA-256 of its text>`.
   - The digest is the SHA-256 of canonical JSON (sorted keys) over each part's slug, version and body hash, and the current fields' ticks, sorted by field id.
   - `GET /mine` answers that digest. Once submitted, the booklet answers the frozen digest, whatever the store does later.
7. **Submit** (`POST /mine/submit { digest }`) refuses in this order:
   - 409 `booklet_not_open`;
   - 400 `digest_invalid`;
   - 409 `booklet_part_missing`, naming the parts;
   - 409 `content_changed`.

   It then writes `frozen_json`, `digest` and `submitted_at` in one UPDATE guarded on `status = 'open'`. Migration 478's triggers then seal the ticks and the frozen columns.
8. **Who.**
   - **The applicant** is the user of an `approved` `lp_applications` row on `spinout-fund-i`; anyone else, and anyone with no live booklet, gets 404 `booklet_not_found`. A support session cannot answer or submit.
   - **The admin routes** answer an Admin on HQ only (a branch gets 403 `hq_only`), and each asserts its D714 act after that refusal. That act refuses a support session:

     | Act | Routes |
     | --- | --- |
     | `fund_booklet.read` | the list, which carries no ticks; one booklet with its ticks, audited as `fund_admin.fund_booklet.read` |
     | `fund_booklet.withdraw` | a withdrawal, which needs a 10–200 character reason and is audited |
     | `fund_booklet.accept` | D759 (#1858): a Super Admin sends the General Partner's stage again (`POST /:uid/acceptance`); Super Admin tier, owner `#1858` |

     Both acts are Admin tier, owner `#1781`.
   - `/api/fund-booklets` is in `COOL_OFF_PREFIXES`.
   - `routes/fund_booklets.ts` is in `FUND_MODULES`. `services/subscriptionBooklet.ts` is not: it does not match D707's pattern.

**Files (PR 2):** `services/subscriptionBooklet.ts`, `routes/fund_booklets.ts`, `index.ts` (the import, the mount, the cool-off prefix), `services/fundAdminAccess.ts` (two acts), `services/moneyRails.ts` (`FUND_MODULES`), `routes/README.md`, `ROUTE_MAP.md`, `test/fund_booklet_d716.test.ts`, and `test/fund_admin_access_d714.test.ts` (the tier table and `ROUTERS`).

### PR 1: the notices (steps 3 to 7 and 10, the seven types) and the booklet's paper

PR 1 stacked on #1776's PR 1 (#1811, merged), which owns `pdf.ts` and the paper. #1774's reads (#1799) merged before PR 1 opened, so the two fee types ride here (the issue's rule) and `NOTICE_TYPES` lists all seven.

9. **The renderers skip what a notice does not have.**
   - `pdf.ts`'s `drawParty` prints no empty By line, and counts none in its `need()`. `drawExecution` prints no empty recital, and draws nothing at all for a block with no parties.
   - The paper does the same. A notice's sender block sits inline under the notice, not on its own page, and prints no "[Signature page follows]".
10. **The paper's notice sections** (`legalPaper.ts`, `legalPaperCss.ts`), each optional:
    - the addressee lines;
    - key figures in rows;
    - figures tables, right-aligned columns in mono, with an optional totals row;
    - the warning box;
    - the payment block, or the resolver's sentence in its place;
    - the stamp (`Issued` or `Superseded`);
    - the audit line: the notice uid and the first 12 hex of `figures_sha256`, or "Notice id and figures hash assigned when issued" on a preview.

    The CSS adds rules on #1776's tokens and no colour literal.
11. **`services/fundNotices.ts`.**
    - `NOTICE_TYPES`, `TAX_CHARACTERS` and `NOTICE_SENTENCES` (the fixed sentences below).
    - `paymentBlock`, which turns #1773's masked answer into the block: beneficiary and address, bank and address, routing, alternate routing, SWIFT, intermediary SWIFT, "ending NNNN", and, to an LP, the full-number sentence.
    - `buildNoticeFigures(env, { type, source, inputs }, today)` answers `{ figures, refusal }`. `figuresDigest` is the SHA-256 of their canonical JSON. A preview prints even with a refusal; an issue refuses.
    - **Sources:**

      | Source | Means |
      | --- | --- |
      | `{ line_id }` | a call line |
      | `{ proposed: { fund_id, lp_id, amount_cents, due_date?, purpose? } }` | a proposed call, split as `POST /:id/capital-calls/preview` splits it; preview only |
      | `{ distribution_id }` | a distribution |
      | the inputs `{ licensee, period_start, period_end, amount_cents, due_date }` | a licence invoice |
      | the inputs `{ purpose, entity?, to? }` | a letter |

    - **The merge context** each Fund notice fills (an unfilled field is a bracket label on a preview and `merge_field_unfilled` on an issue):

      | Notice | Fields |
      | --- | --- |
      | capital call | `lp.name`, `lp.number`, `fund.name`, `call.number`, `call.amount`, `call.due_date`, `notice.date`, `notice.reference` |
      | distribution | `lp.name`, `lp.number`, `fund.name`, `distribution.amount`, `distribution.date`, `distribution.type`, `distribution.tax_character`, `notice.date` |
      | default | `lp.name`, `lp.number`, `fund.name`, `call.number`, `default.amount`, `default.due_date`, `default.cure_date`, `default.lpa_section`, `notice.date`, `notice.reference` |

    - **The fixed sentences:**
      - "The full account number is shown in your LP workspace, after you confirm these details by phone." (to an LP, in the block);
      - "Stub — counsel to draft." (a stub's prose on a preview);
      - the letter's "These are the only instructions on which <entity> accepts payment." and "This letter replaces any earlier payment instructions from <entity>.";
      - the distribution's date label, "date recorded when marked paid".
    - D758: `noticeSpecimen(env, type)` builds each type's page with bracket figures for the Templates catalog, from no record and no account; a test holds its labels to each builder's.
12. **`services/fundNoticeStore.ts`.**
    - `issueNotice`:
      - It builds the figures again and refuses a refusal (409).
      - It refuses a digest the issuer did not see (`content_changed`).
      - It answers `already_issued` for the head's figures, writing nothing.
      - Different figures need a 1–200 character reason (`reason_required`) and write a row that supersedes the head.
      - A UNIQUE conflict reads the head back.
      - A proposed call previews only (`proposed_call_not_issued`).
    - `issueCallNotices` builds every line first and refuses on the first refusal, naming the line. It then writes the new notices in one `DB.batch`: all or none.
    - A line whose head has other figures is refused there, and issues on its own with a reason.
    - `logAdminAction` (through `auditFundAct`) runs after the write, with uids, the type and the reference.
    - `noticeFile` keeps one PDF per (notice, stamp): drawn through `renderLegalPdf(…, { fallback: false })`, put at `fund-notices/<uid>/<file uid>.pdf`, then recorded `ON CONFLICT (notice_id, stamp) DO NOTHING`. A request that loses that race deletes its object and serves the winner's. A failed render answers 503 `notice_render_unavailable` and keeps nothing.
13. **`routes/fund_notices.ts`** at `/api/fund-notices`.
    - **Admin on HQ** (support session refused):
      - `GET /types`;
      - `POST /preview` and `POST /calls/:callUid/preview` (`fund_notice.preview`);
      - `GET /`, `GET /:uid` and `GET /:uid/pdf`.
    - **The Super Admin:** `POST /issue` and `POST /calls/:callUid/issue`, each `fund_notice.issue` after the `hq_only` refusal, so a plain Admin gets `super_admin_required` before any TOTP or step-up demand.

15. **The booklet's paper**, added once PR 2 (#1817) was on `main`:
    - `subscription_booklet` joins #1776's `COVER_SLUGS`. The paper is one composite: `PaperInput.parts` lists the nine parts, the input's own body is Part 1's legend (it prints on the cover), and the contents lists Parts 2 to 9.
    - Each part prints in its own `section.booklet-part`, on its own CSS named page (`@page part-<n>`), so it starts a new page and its footer reads "Subscription Booklet · Part <n>".
    - Each part prints its own block from `buildExecutionBlock`, with the applicant as counterparty: Part 3 the Fund and the applicant, Parts 4 to 8 the applicant alone, Part 9 the Fund through its GP, last. No part prints "[Signature page follows]".
    - In the PDF's body pass only the part headings link (`#part-<n>`), so #1776's two-pass `renderLegalPdf` numbers the contents from each part's first page, with roman front matter.
    - `bookletPaperInput` (`services/subscriptionBooklet.ts`) puts the applicant's ticks into each body by field id (`tickedBody`), so a ticked field prints an X square and an unticked one an empty square. Part 2 is generated: "Complete Part <n>, <title>." for Parts 3 to 8, then "Send no money until you receive a Capital Call Notice." A part with no text from counsel prints "Stub — counsel to draft.", and the paper is then a Draft.
    - The cover prints the Fund, "Subscription Booklet", "Confidential", the booklet uid as the copy number, the applicant's legal name (else their account name) and the date. The audit line is the booklet uid and the first 12 hex of its digest, or "digest assigned when submitted".
    - The paper prints the store's current bodies with the booklet's stored ticks, which the triggers seal at submit. The e-signature send S1 files next signs the frozen copy.
    - **Signed (D759, #1858).** A submitted booklet's paper now prints its frozen text (each part at its frozen version, hash-checked); the applicant signs Parts 3 to 8 and the General Partner Part 9, each in its own envelope over `frozen_json` under the booklet's digest. See `D759.md`.
    - **Routes:** the applicant's `GET /mine/paper` answers `{ html, digest, page_size }` (screen HTML, faces inline, `no-store`). The Admin's `GET /:uid/pdf` (`fund_booklet.read`) draws on request through `renderLegalPdf(…, { fallback: false })`, keeps nothing, answers `private, no-store`, audits the read as reading the ticks is, and answers 503 `booklet_render_unavailable` when Browser Run fails or is unbound. Both take `?page=letter|a4` and `?cover=dark|light`.
    - D758: `bookletPaperParts` is the one part assembly, and `bookletSpecimenInput` draws it with no booklet (no ticks, no applicant, no copy number) for the Templates catalog.

16. **The two fee types** read #1774's period and statement (`readPeriod`, `statementFor`), never its tables.
    - The source is `{ fund_id, quarter }` (the invoice) or `{ fund_id, quarter, lp_id }` (the statement). A malformed one is 400 `source_invalid`; another fund is 409 `not_an_axal_fund`; a missing period or line is 404 `source_not_found`; a period still in draft is 409 `fee_period_not_issued`.
    - **The statement** (Fund to the LP) prints the window and its days, the rate, the basis with its days, then gross, catch-up, offset and net, and the line's `reference_code`. It prints no payment block and no account, masked or not, and closes with "Nothing is due from you against this statement." (#1774's `pay_to.sentence`). It previews and draws its paper only; an issue answers 409 `statement_not_issued_here`, because it reaches an LP through #1777's queue.
    - **The invoice** (the GP to the Fund) prints the window, the amount due, one row per fee line (the LP number, days, gross, catch-up, offset, net) and the period's totals, then the GP's masked `management_fee` block with the period's `remittance_reference`. It prints no block of its own, and only the Super Admin issues it (`fund_notice.issue`).

### Screens: the LP's notices (#1860)
