## D7662 — The Worker rail's absent line at 4.5:1 in light mode (#7662)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-19. Filed from D7517's follow-ups (#7517, #7544) on the owner's answer of 2026-11-19.
- **Builds on:** D7517 (the axe scan that found it), D698 (the rail palette), D1511 (`--d698-faint` darkened to `#716c7e` for the same axe rule).
- **Scope:** the colour only. The rail belongs to #5130 (the shell-wide Eadwyn rail redesign, open). Nothing here moves, resizes or rewords the rail.
- **No migration, no Worker route, no client method, no new dependency.**

### The question

`.fwr-absent` is the rail's "absent" line: `Not recorded. …` and `Loading…` in `WorkerRail.jsx` and `RailGroups.jsx`, `Reading this page…`, the empty state and the status line in `RailSteps.jsx`, and the note in `EadwynComposer.jsx`. In `ui/workerRail.css` it was `color: var(--fwr-faint)`. In light mode that is `#8b8494`, **3.60:1** on white, under WCAG 2.2 AA's 4.5:1 (axe `color-contrast`, serious, from 1024 px where the rail shows). Issue #7662 names the colour "`text-worker-rail-model-none`". That is the `data-testid` of the rail's Not recorded line, not a token. The colour is the CSS custom property `--fwr-faint`.

### The decision

`.fwr-absent` reads its own property, `--fwr-absent`:

- **light** (`.fwr`): `#716c7e`, the rail palette's faint grey that D1511 already measured for this rule (`--d698-faint`);
- **dark** (`.dark .fwr`): `var(--fwr-faint)`, which is `#8b849c`, the value it had.

`--fwr-faint` itself is unchanged. It also colours the rail's small-caps title, block labels, toggle, `em` and foot note. Those are not this issue's, axe did not report them in D7517's scan, and changing the shared value is a rail-wide restyle that belongs to #5130.

Contrast (WCAG relative luminance):

| Ground | Before `#8b8494` | After `#716c7e` |
|---|---|---|
| `#ffffff` (rail paper, `--fwr-paper`, `--d698-paper`, composer panel) | 3.60:1 | **5.06:1** |
| `#f9fafb` (app page ground) | 3.45:1 | **4.84:1** |
| `#f7f7f9` (investor hosts' ground) | 3.37:1 | **4.73:1** |
| `#f4f3f7` (`--d698-ground`) | 3.26:1 | **4.58:1** |
| `#f5efff` (founder `--fwr-accent-soft`) | 3.21:1 | **4.50:1** |
| Dark: `#8b849c` on `#1a1822` / `#111827` / `#0b1220` | 4.91 / 4.97 / 5.24:1 | unchanged |

### Tests

- `frontend/test/rail_absent_contrast_7662.test.mjs` (3 tests) reads `workerRail.css`. It checks that `.fwr-absent` uses `var(--fwr-absent)`, computes the light value's contrast on the rail paper, the rail ground, the accent soft and the page ground (each at least 4.5:1), and checks that the dark value is `var(--fwr-faint)` (`#8b849c`, at least 4.5:1 on the dark paper). Mutation-checked: pointing `.fwr-absent` back at `--fwr-faint`, the old light value, `#7d7889` (4.27:1), a changed dark value, and a missing dark pair each fail it (5 of 5 caught).
- In Chromium (the D7517 fixture pattern at 1440 px, the rail open with its Not recorded line), axe under the WCAG 2.0 to 2.2 A and AA tags finds no violation on `.fwr-absent` in light or dark, nor anywhere in `aside.fwr`. Before the change, light reported `color-contrast: .fwr-absent`. Computed colour: `rgb(113, 108, 126)` light, `rgb(139, 132, 156)` dark (unchanged). That check was a local scratch spec and is not committed. The rail is still outside `expertise_axe_7517.spec.js`'s scope.
