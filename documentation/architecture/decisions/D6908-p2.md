## D6908 part 2 — the Pro rata row's live round and offer, and the founder's offer inputs (#6908)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-21. Frontend only. Part 1, the Worker read and the founder's offer fields, merged in #6921. #6868 (#7542) created Ownership & rights.
- **Builds on:** D6908 (the read, `GET /api/portfolio-pro-rata/:projectUid`), D6868 (Ownership & rights, PRM18).

### What

**The investor's row.** On Ownership & rights, the Pro rata row's "What you can do" reads the company's live round and the caller's own offer, through `portfolioProRataApi.get`. It is a separate read from the rights, so a failure in one leaves the other standing. `proRataOfferModel.js` turns that read into one line:

| The read | The row |
|---|---|
| a live round and an offer | "Series A is live · offer to you: up to $310,000 · respond by 30 Nov 2026" · **Open the round →** |
| a live round, no offer recorded | "Series A is live · Offer not recorded" · **Open the round →** |
| a live round with no deal page on Axal | the line, then the Worker's reason ("This round has no deal page on Axal."), with no link |
| no live round | "No live round" and no action |
| the read failed | "The live round could not be read. That is not a claim there is none." · **Retry** |

The offer shown is always the one the founder recorded, in the offer's currency. Nothing is computed from the ownership % or the round size. A link is followed only when it is an address on this app (starts with `/`).

**The founder's inputs.** The round manager's Pro-rata panel (`RoundClosesPanel.jsx`, `ProRataPanel`) gains two columns, **Offer to them** and **Respond by**. Both save on blur through `PUT /api/contacts/raise-pro-rata/:id` (`offer_amount`, `respond_by`). An unchanged value sends nothing, and a blank clears the field. A value the Worker would refuse is caught by the model before anything is sent.

### Rollback

Revert the commit. The row then shows only its rights state, the panel loses the two columns, and the Worker read stays as part 1 left it.
