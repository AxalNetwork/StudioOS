## D788 — A member's monthly AI spend limit in dollars: stored, read and set by the member, enforced at the hold, with 50/80/100% notices

- **Status:** decided by S1 for the owner, 2026-10-20 (#2213; part of D785, #2210, item D-f). This is a money path, so S1 reviews it for security before the owner merges.
- **Basis:** `main` at `84f6e33b`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Builds on:** D629 and D640 (every run is held before it starts and settled after), D755 (tokens), D762 (what a run is charged), D783 (the plan's allowance).

### What was there

Every run's estimate was held before it started (`services/aiBudget.ts`, `reserveAiBudget`) and settled afterwards. The hold drew first from the plan's monthly allowance (D783), then from the member's tokens (D755). The organisation cap and the kill switch protected Cloudflare's bill. A member could not limit their own spend.

### 1. The store (migration 508)

- **`member_ai_spend_limits`:** one row per member, with `monthly_limit_microusd` (0 or more) and the three notice switches `notify_50`, `notify_80` and `notify_100` (on by default). No row means no limit.
- **`member_ai_spend_notices`:** one row per member, UTC month and threshold sent. Its primary key is what sends each notice once.

Both tables are new: neither name was in `schema_baseline.sql` or any migration, and no open branch used 508.

### 2. What the limit counts

What the member's runs are charged in the UTC month, the plan's allowance included. The limit is on what the member spends, and the allowance is part of it.

A month's charge is its holds in `ai_budget_reservations`, by `month_key`:
- a hold that is still pending or in review counts at what it holds;
- a settled or refunded hold counts at what it was charged (`actual_included_microusd + actual_topup_microusd`), so a refund counts nothing.

This is `monthCharged` in `services/aiSpendLimit.ts`. The hold's limit clause uses the same expression, written out in the statement rather than interpolated (`check-sql-prepare`).

### 3. Enforcement at the hold

The rule: if this month's charge plus the hold's estimate passes the limit, the hold is refused.

- **The check is in the hold's own conditional insert**, as its last clause. It is the same statement that already refused a hold that the allowance and tokens could not cover. D1 runs one write at a time, so when two holds race, the second insert counts the first, and only one passes a limit that fits one. A test holds it.
- **The refusal is `spend_limit`.** `reserveAiBudget` returns it with `limit_microusd` and `resets_on` (the first day of the next UTC month). Nothing is held, nothing runs, and nothing is charged. When the limit refuses a run, the member is told about the limit, whatever the allowance or tokens could also cover.
- **The words.** A member route answers 402 with: "This run would pass your monthly AI spend limit of $X. Raise it on Usage › Limits, or wait until 1 November 2026."
  - `run()` writes that sentence into its `error`, and `refuseRun` passes it through.
  - A refusal that reaches a caller with only its code says the same thing without the amount.
  - `spend_limit` joins the budget refusals in `railContext.ts`, `assistantInterviews.ts`, `validateEngine.ts` and `interviewProcessing.ts`. The direct holds in `liveTranscription.ts` and `founder_validate_sessions.ts` pass the limit and reset date.
- **No limit set** changes nothing: the clause is true when the member has no row.

### 4. The routes

- **`GET /api/ai-budget/limit`** returns:
  - `limit_microusd`, which is null when there is no limit;
  - `month`, `charged_microusd`, `share_used` (a percentage, null without a limit) and `resets_on`;
  - `notify` and `updated_at`;
  - the plan's `allowance` from `resolveAiCaps`, for context.
- **`PUT /api/ai-budget/limit { monthly_limit_usd, notify }`:**
  - `null` removes the limit.
  - It refuses a non-number, a negative amount, more than two decimals or more than $100,000, as 400 `invalid_spend_limit` with the `field` named.
  - `notify` takes only the keys 50, 80 and 100, each true or false.
  - It needs a recent sign-in, as billing does: `requireFactor(c, 'totp')`, then `requireStepUp(c)`. The route answers the step-up refusal itself, with `stepUpRefusalBody`, so the app gets the `step_up_required` code it prompts on.
- The frontend methods are `aiBudgetApi.spendLimit()` and `aiBudgetApi.setSpendLimit(usd, notify)` (`frontend/src/lib/api/aiBudget.js`), for D792's screen. This task adds no UI.

### 5. Notices

When a settled run takes the month's settled charge past 50%, 80% or 100% of the limit for the first time, and that notice is switched on, the member gets one in-app notification and one email. These go through `notify`, with category `billing`, so quiet hours and the digest do not hold them back.

- **What they say:** the threshold, the limit, the amount charged and the reset date. Never the run, its prompt or its output.
- **When a run passes two thresholds at once:** the higher one is sent, and the lower one is recorded unsent, so it never fires later.
- **A switched-off threshold** is neither sent nor recorded.
- **Every UTC month** starts the notices again.
- **They are hooked into settlement:** `settleAiReservation`, and a Super Admin's charge of a held review. They are best effort, so a notice that fails never fails a settlement.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/ai_spend_limit_d788.test.ts`, 12 tests, on the real budget migrations over SQLite. They cover:
- no limit;
- a limit just below a run's estimate, with nothing held;
- what counts, including the allowance, open holds and refunds;
- two racing holds, of which exactly one passes;
- the UTC month boundary;
- the `GET`;
- `PUT` validation and the recent-sign-in requirement;
- each notice once per month, only when switched on, and the higher of two;
- the email, captured over a stubbed mailer;
- removing the limit restoring today's behaviour;
- the 402's words.

16 mutations were made, each breaking one of these rules, and every one was caught. `_ai_budget_fixture.mjs` now loads migration 508, because every hold reads the limit table.
