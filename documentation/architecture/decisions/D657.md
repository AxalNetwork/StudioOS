## D657 — Voice in the Worker: interview sessions, live and final speech-to-text, consent, audio in R2

**Issue:** #1470, part of #1469 (D656). **Slot:** S03. **Migration:** 438.
**Canvases:**
- `design/incoming/Interview Session.dc.html` (`start`, `record`, `liveoff`, `text`, `review`);
- `design/incoming/Recording View.dc.html`;
- `design/incoming/Cycle Action.dc.html` (`action=record`).

This is the Worker half of "Record a session". #1471 structures the transcript, and #1473 draws the pages.

### What was true

`discovery_interviews` held one recording (migration 215):
- `POST /interviews/:id/recording` put the audio in R2;
- `POST /interviews/:id/transcribe` ran the router's `transcribe` task on the whole file in memory, under a 20 MB ceiling.

There was no session, no live transcript, no speakers, no language and no channel. There was no consent gate on recording: `quote_consent` (migration 211) is consent to *quote*. And there was no model choice.

### What a session is

**Migration 438 adds five tables.** A session hangs off one `discovery_interviews` row, so it adds no second recordings store. A Spin-Out Lab venture is an ordinary `projects` row, so a Lab venture's interviews get sessions with no Lab-specific code. `spinout_application_interviews` (Lab admission interviews) is untouched.

- **`interview_sessions`:**
  - `channel`: `in_person`, `video`, `phone`, `text` or `manual`;
  - `status`: `draft` → `recording` ⇄ `paused` → `stopped` → `transcribing` → `transcribed` (then `structured` and `reviewed`, which #1471 sets);
  - consent to record (`given`, `declined` or `not_recorded`), with how and when;
  - the detected language and the live and final models;
  - the duration, as `active_ms`.
- **`interview_session_chunks`:** the audio, one R2 object per sequence number.
- **`interview_session_marks`:** the moments the founder marked.
- **`interview_transcript_segments`:** start and end offsets, the speaker label, the text, `source` (`live` or `final`), and the model.
- **`interview_session_receipts`:** one row per speech-to-text job: the model, the minutes, the cost in micro-USD, any fallback, and the outcome.

### The rules, and where each is held

- **Consent is the Worker's.** On an audio channel, `start` and `resume` return 409 `consent_not_recorded` unless consent is `given`. A chunk upload is refused too. Text chat and manual entry record no audio and need none. `consentBlocks` in `services/interviewSessions.ts` is the one test.
- **Audio is chunked and idempotent.** `PUT /sessions/:id/chunks/:seq`:
  - The key is derived on the server: `validate-audio/<user>/sessions/<session uid>/<seq>.<ext>`.
  - The primary key `(session_id, seq)` makes a repeat a no-op, answered `duplicate: true`, with no second R2 write. That is what keeps the canvas's `offline` state honest: chunks queue on the device and upload on reconnect.
  - A chunk is at most 5 MB. Each must be independently decodable (the recorder restarts per chunk), because each is transcribed on its own.
- **No recording is held whole in memory any more.** The 20 MB in-memory path stays only for single-file recordings made before sessions.
- **The duration is the server's.** `transition` adds each interval from `recording_since` to pause or stop on the Worker's clock. A duration sent by the client is never read.
- **The final transcript replaces the draft.** `POST /sessions/:id/transcribe` runs the chosen model on each chunk through the router. `replaceWithFinal` then does all of this in one batch:
  - deletes the live draft;
  - writes the `final` segments, with each chunk's offset;
  - stores the language and model;
  - copies the plain text to `discovery_interviews.transcript` (and `transcribed_at`, `transcribed_by_model`), so every existing reader of that column keeps working.

  A failed or refused chunk restores the session's status and stores nothing.
- **Speakers.** Nova-3 numbers speakers in the order it hears them; the founder opens the conversation. So 0 is "Me" and 1 is the interviewee, by name when the interview has one. Any other passage, and every Whisper passage, is "Speaker ?" until `PATCH /sessions/:id/segments/:segId` assigns it.
- **Text and manual sessions.** The posted `messages` become `final` segments at once, each with its author and time. No model runs.
- **Deletion.** `DELETE /sessions/:id` does three things:
  - deletes every chunk's R2 key (never one outside `validate-audio/`);
  - deletes the session's rows;
  - writes an `interview_session_deleted` audit row with the counts and the session's total cost.

### Models and prices

**Final.** The router's `transcribe` task now has alternates:
- `@cf/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo` is the default ($0.0005 a minute);
- `@cf/deepgram/nova-3` batch returns speaker labels ($0.0052);
- `@cf/openai/whisper` is the plainest ($0.0005).

The fallback goes one way only, to Turbo, and a session's receipt records it. `@cf/openai/whisper-tiny-en` is listed but cannot be chosen: Cloudflare publishes no price for it, and an unpriced model bills as zero.

**Live.**
- `@cf/deepgram/nova-3` over WebSocket costs $0.0092 a minute.
- `@cf/deepgram/flux` costs $0.0077 a minute and is WebSocket only.
- They live in the router's new `PRICE_USD_PER_AUDIO_MINUTE_WEBSOCKET`, because Nova-3 has a different price per transport.

**One table.** `GET /sessions/models` reads every price from the router's tables, the same numbers a run is reserved and settled against. A retired model stays listed for past sessions but cannot be chosen. The prices were read from Cloudflare's pricing page on 2026-10-09.

**Model shapes.** `services/sttFormats.ts` holds each model's request and response, and its live events:
- Nova-3 `Results` with `is_final`;
- Flux `EndOfTurn` with `audio_window_start` and `audio_window_end`.

### The live transcript

`GET /sessions/:id/live` takes a WebSocket upgrade. The Worker opens `env.AI.run(model, { encoding: 'linear16', sample_rate: '16000' }, { websocket: true })`, relays the audio in and the events out, and stores each finished passage as `live`. Interim results are relayed but not stored.

"Live transcript off" (`live_model: null`) is 409 `live_off`: no socket and no budget hold.

### Costs (D640)

- **Every job reserves and settles** through `services/aiBudget.ts`.
- **The final transcript is held for the whole job first.** The quote is the job's dearest cost (the chosen model, or the default it may fall back to). The hold is then released, and each chunk is billed through the router as it runs. So a budget that covers only part of a job runs none of it, and a spent budget runs nothing and stores nothing (402, with the budget's own message).
- **The live stream holds four-minute blocks**, under the reservation's five-minute expiry: one before the socket opens, and the next as the stream crosses into it. A refused block closes the stream with the budget's message. When the stream ends, each block is settled against the whole minutes streamed, on the Worker's clock.
- **The budget's words** come from one function, `budgetRefusalMessage` in `aiRouter.ts`, which the router, the live relay and the session job share.
- **`GET /sessions/:id/receipt`** returns each job's receipt and the budget left today and this month, computed as `/api/ai-budget/me` computes it. A budget that cannot be read is `budget: null` with an "Unreadable" reason, never zero.

### Verified

`cloudflare-worker/test/interview_sessions_d657.test.ts` has 19 tests. They run migration 438 and the D629/D640 budget migrations on SQLite, with in-memory R2 and Workers AI that count every call. They cover:
- consent refusing `start` and chunks, with `declined` named;
- a chunk sent twice stored once;
- the final transcript replacing the live draft, with `discovery_interviews.transcript` equal to it;
- Nova-3's speaker labels and reassignment;
- a spent budget, and a budget that covers half the job, each running nothing and storing nothing;
- unpriced and unoffered models refused;
- deletion removing every audio key, every row, and leaving the audit row;
- a Lab venture's session;
- the server-measured duration;
- the receipt;
- the model list's prices;
- live off, and a live stream refused for budget;
- live event parsing, block and split math, and Whisper Turbo segments.

**Mutation checks.** 15 mutants on those rules were tried and all were caught, after two survivors were addressed:
- The whole-job hold was added to a test (the budget that covers half the job).
- The price check in `isChoosable` was redundant with the router's alternates, so it was removed, and a test now pins that every listed model is priced.
