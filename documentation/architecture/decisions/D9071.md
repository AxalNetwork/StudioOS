### What it decides

- **The Shared directory is the first scope of `/network/organizations`** (NPO-01). The organization zone opens on it for every licence; `?scope=mine` is the licence's own organization body as before, unchanged, and Team paths is drawn disabled. The directory reads `GET /api/network/directory/entries?kind=organization` with the same address, total and later-page rules as People (D9025).
- **Distinct people per organization come from the Worker** (`GET /api/network/directory/organizations/people-counts?uids=`, new in `routes/network_directory.ts`, `services/networkDirectoryOrgPeople.ts`). For up to 50 organizations it counts distinct published, not-withdrawn people through not-withdrawn affiliations, each person once: current if any affiliation there is current, else former if any is former, else status not recorded. It answers counts only (no name, no person id, nothing from an overlay or a private source) and leaves out any uid that is not a visible organization, so it says nothing about whether one exists. Signed in, the same gate as `GET /entries`.
- **A count is never an unknown 0.** "Counting…" while the counts load, "Unavailable" with Count again when they fail, and "No people recorded" for an organization the Worker counts at zero.
- **An organization opens on its people** (`/network/organizations/:uid`): every published affiliation from `GET /entries/:uid`, one row per distinct person with the status the Worker counts by, filtered All, Current, Former or Status not recorded, each with role, dates (Not recorded when the source gave none), verification and observed date. A person opens the People sheet at `/network/organizations/:uid/people/:personUid`, with a context bar naming the organization, role and status.
- **A firm is never shown as a fund vehicle.** The member projection carries no organization type, so every organization reads "Type not recorded" with the reason, and no page draws a fund mark, a vintage or a managed fund. Source categories are not drawn as a type.
- **An organization with no people recorded** says so, says why an introduction needs a named person, and offers nothing that contacts anyone (NPO-10).
