## D754 — WhatsApp Business: switched on, and a way into Record a Session

Tracking issue: #1871. Slots S07–S25 (S1’s reconciled allocation). Amends D684 (interview channels) and builds on D685 (Record a Session), D657 (sessions, consent, R2 chunks), D665–D668 (the AI-assistant path's consent rule) and D753 (a page for every connector). Every `path:line` below is on `origin/main` at `0ce63b344` (2026-10-18); #1892 re-checked its edited paths at `ab12a1948` before building. S1’s reconciliation below governs the current task allocation.

### Why

On 2026-10-18 at about 13:40Z the owner shared the Connectors card:

> WhatsApp Business — Not available yet — Axal has not switched this on yet. There is nothing for you to set up until it does. Calls: not yet. WhatsApp calls do not reach Axal; record a call with the in-app recorder instead.

The owner asked: "Enable and build backend connector WhatsApp Business, mainly used for Record a Session in Validate."

- **The connector is built (D684), and it is off only because four Worker secrets are unset.** `availability()` requires `WHATSAPP_APP_ID`, `WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET`, `WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN` and `WHATSAPP_EMBEDDED_SIGNUP_CONFIG_ID` (`cloudflare-worker/src/services/channels/connections.ts:84-104`). The card's words are `UNAVAILABLE_WHY` (`frontend/src/pages/account/channelsModel.js:207`, `:233`) and the calls line (`frontend/src/pages/account/ChannelsGroup.jsx:301`). No code gate exists.
- **Four things the built connector does would break Record a Session's rules if it were switched on as it is:**
  - voice notes are transcribed with no recording consent, because a chat session is `text` and `consentBlocks` gates only audio channels (`services/interviewSessions.ts:30`, `:74-76`);
  - the audio is discarded rather than stored like the recorder's ("Only the text is kept; the audio is never stored", `routes/channels.ts:51`);
  - the job calls `aiRun` directly, outside the recorder's pipeline, so it writes no per-session receipt and has no stages (`routes/channels.ts:785-803`);
  - nothing tells the interviewee the chat is recorded, and no consent is stored (`D684.md:336`).
- **A founder with no WhatsApp Business number has no way in.** Embedded Signup needs a Meta business portfolio and the WhatsApp Business app (`D684.md:79`).

### Decisions

#### 1. What is switched on

One Meta app, one webhook (`https://axal.vc/api/channels/whatsapp/webhook`), and two ways in.

- **A. Your business number** (built in D684; the main way).
  - A member connects their own WhatsApp Business number through Embedded Signup in coexistence mode, so the WhatsApp Business app keeps working on their phone (`frontend/src/pages/account/whatsappSignup.js:36-43`).
  - Their customers' chats wait in the member's inbox. The member adds a chat to an interview.
  - Axal holds the member's business token, encrypted (`services/channels/whatsapp.ts:513-527`), and never owns a number on this path.
- **B. Send to Axal** (new).
  - Axal runs one WhatsApp Business number of its own.
  - A member links their own WhatsApp, personal or business, to their Axal account.
  - The member then forwards voice notes and audio files of interviews they held (in person, or a call recorded on their phone where the law allows) to Axal's number. Each one becomes a draft interview with a transcript, after the member confirms consent.
  - **This partly reverses D684 §7** (`D684.md:194`). That alternative was rejected as the *only* model ("personal capture only"). B carries the member's own recordings only. Customer conversations still arrive through A. **B needs the owner's yes, because Axal pays Meta for this number's messages** (owner action 0). Until the three Axal-number secrets are set, B is absent: its page section is hidden and its webhook branch ignores everything.
- **The rollout switch.** Independent wrangler vars, `WHATSAPP_CHANNEL` and `TELEGRAM_CHANNEL` (D756 §1.1), takes `off`, `canary` or `on`. An absent, empty or unrecognised var means `canary`; each row follows only its own var.
  - `off`: members see "Not available yet". A connection that already exists reads "Axal has switched this off for now…" (`channelsModel.js:222`). Webhooks still verify and acknowledge, but store nothing.
  - `canary`: only Super Admins can connect or link. Members see "Not available yet".
  - `on`: everyone.
  - It follows `GOOGLE_SIGNIN_POPUP`'s rollout (`documentation/architecture/PRODUCTION.md:189`). Setting the secrets therefore never shows WhatsApp to members before the consent work below has merged and the owner's live check has passed.

#### 3. The ways into Record a Session

- **A chat with a customer, including their voice notes (way A).**
  1. In Record a Session › Text chat › WhatsApp (`frontend/src/pages/founder/recordSetup.js:65`; `RecordLightbox.jsx:136-138`), or in the inbox, the member picks a waiting chat and names the interviewee.
  2. Consent is asked (§4). Until it is given, nothing from the chat enters the interview.
  3. Once it is given, the waiting text moves into the session (`threads.ts:499-525`). Each voice note is then downloaded, stored as a numbered audio chunk of that session, transcribed through the recorder's per-chunk stage, and placed in the transcript at the time it was sent (§6).
  4. Findings come from the existing Structure step (`chat_assigned`, then the quiet-chat sweep, `sessionAutoStructure.ts:131-143`).
- **A recording the member forwards (way B).**
  1. The member forwards one or more voice notes or audio files to Axal's number.
  2. Axal replies in that chat with the consent question (§4).
  3. On "Yes", the Worker downloads the audio and opens a new draft interview with one audio session (`in_person`, `submitted_via = 'whatsapp'`). It stores each file as a chunk and hands off to the recorder's own `finishSession` (`services/interviewProcessing.ts:195-206`). From there the pipeline runs unchanged: transcribe, summarise, then place as proposals (`:312-373`).
  4. The draft opens in Customer research as "Imported from WhatsApp" (`services/records/validate.ts:399`).
  5. Files forwarded before the member answers join the same draft: at most 20 files within 30 minutes. 20 is the pipeline's chunks per message (`interviewProcessing.ts:43`).
  6. The member names the interviewee on review.
- **Calls: deferred. WhatsApp calls do not reach Axal in D754.** The exact reasons:
  1. **The number type.** Every number Axal onboards uses coexistence mode (`whatsappSignup.js:36-43`). Third-party providers (360dialog, Wati) report that calls on a coexistence number stay in the WhatsApp Business app and never reach the Calling API. No Meta statement could be read: agent sessions cannot reach `developers.facebook.com`. If the reports are right, no member number can hand a call to Axal.
  2. **Eligibility.** Calling needs a messaging limit of at least 2,000 (`whatsapp.ts:384`), and an early founder's number rarely has one.
  3. **The media leg.** The Calling API delivers WebRTC: an SDP offer in the `calls` webhook, to be accepted within about 30–60 seconds. A Worker cannot carry WebRTC media, so the member's browser would have to answer. The customer's call would ring in an open Axal tab, not on the founder's phone. The ring would also need a push to that tab: a new Durable Object binding in `wrangler.toml`, or polling.
  4. **Recording.** Meta's "Call recording" (opted in per call, an announcement to both sides, a media id after the call) is known only from search summaries of a page dated 2026-07-09 that could not be opened. Without it, the browser records locally, which is D684's phase 2 design (`D684.md:186-190`).
  5. **Way B cannot help.** The Calling API is one-to-one between a business number and one WhatsApp user. A call to Axal's number reaches Axal, not the interviewee.
  6. **The owner's go** is still open (#1008 item 16).

  **The way today:** the in-app recorder's "WhatsApp call" chip (phone on speaker) and "WhatsApp video" chip (tab audio), under the recorder's consent gate (`recordSetup.js:55`, `:60`; `D685.md:116-122`). With way B, a recording made on the phone can also be forwarded. The card keeps its line, which gains the number's own reason from the split eligibility (§11).

  **The calls work re-opens when all of these hold:**
  - the owner says go on #1008;
  - S1 or the owner reads Meta's Coexistence and Call recording pages on a machine that can reach Meta, and finds that coexistence numbers do reach the Calling API, or the owner accepts API-only numbers (which then need `POST /{phone-number-id}/register` with a PIN that is never stored);
  - one member number shows a messaging limit of 2,000 or more with calling enabled.

  S1 then files two tasks:
  - a Worker task: `'call'` in `channel_events.kind` (a rebuild like 450's), the `calls` normaliser, Graph `pre_accept` / `accept` / `reject` / `terminate`, consent before accept, and the recording media id taken through way A's audio path into a `phone` session;
  - a browser task: the incoming-call banner, the consent confirm, the `RTCPeerConnection` answer, and the processing hand-off.

  Users only call first; business-initiated calls, and with them call permissions, come later.

#### 4. Consent

**The rule.** An interview is recorded, and a voice note or forwarded recording is downloaded or transcribed, only with explicit consent that was captured and stored first. Nothing is downloaded, stored as audio, transcribed, structured or quoted before it.

- **Way A, a customer chat.**
  - **The notice is always sent in the chat first** (§5). Consent is given in one of two ways:
    - **The interviewee's own answer.** They tap "Yes, I agree", or type `YES`, in reply to Axal's notice. It counts only when it comes **from the contact**, on that thread's chat, and answers that thread's open request (the button id carries the request uid). A message from the member's own side or an echo never counts.
    - **The member's word**, with the recorder's methods (`verbal | written | pre_agreed`, `interviewSessions.ts:32`). It is accepted only after the notice was delivered to that chat (WhatsApp reported the message delivered or read). This is for someone who agreed by email or out loud, so the notice still reached them.
  - **Without consent:**
    - the chat waits linked to its interview, which reads "Waiting for consent";
    - its text stays in the encrypted pending store and is purged after 30 days (`channels/types.ts:42`);
    - its voice notes are never downloaded, and WhatsApp's media ids lapse after 7 days;
    - nothing reaches the interview.

    After 30 days, an empty draft session, and the interview opened for it, are deleted.
  - **"No", or `STOP` before consent:** consent is declined. The waiting copies are deleted, the chat is set aside (`setThreadIgnored`, `threads.ts:650`), and the member reads "<Name> declined. Nothing from this chat was kept."
  - **"No" or `STOP` after consent:** consent is withdrawn.
    - Nothing new from the chat is stored or transcribed.
    - Quotes are switched off.
    - Structure refuses the session.
    - The member is offered "Discard this chat's recording" (`deleteSession`), as the recorder does (`D685.md:612`).
- **Way B, a forwarded recording.** The member is asked, in their chat with Axal: "Before Axal transcribes this: did everyone in the recording agree to be recorded and transcribed before it started?"
  - The buttons are "Yes, out loud", "Yes, in writing" and "No". "Agreed beforehand" is offered in Axal.
  - The answer is the member's attestation, stored with its method and time. That is the recorder's standard (`D657.md:36`) and the AI-assistant path's (`services/assistantInterviews.ts:186-196`).
  - "No", or 7 days with no answer: nothing was downloaded, and the waiting reference is deleted.
- **Where consent is stored.**
  - The provider-neutral `channel_consents` table built by #1893 (§15) is the record. Each row holds the provider in its own column, `method` (`chat_reply | member_attested`), the thread, `interview_id` (NULL until assignment), the session when known, status (`requested`, `given`, `declined`, `withdrawn` or `expired`), notice version, `notice_ref_hash`, `answer_ref_hash`, and the asked, answered and withdrawn times. A member attestation retains its recorder method separately as #1893 specifies.
  - On `given`, the session takes `consent_status = 'given'`, `consent_method` and `consent_at` (`interview_sessions` columns, `438_interview_sessions.sql:33-36`). The pipeline's own gate then passes (`interviewProcessing.ts:321-325`).
  - Its rows go when their thread, capture or session is deleted.
- **The gate itself.**
  - `consentBlocks` treats a session that holds any audio chunk as an audio session, whatever its channel.
  - The voice job, `queueVoiceTranscriptions` (`threads.ts:297`), the chunk route, `finish`, `process`, the audio route and Structure all ask it.
  - Quote consent stays separate. It is still set only by a literal `true` (`routes/channels.ts:363-365`).
- **Telegram.** The store, the gate and the member's word are provider-neutral. A per-provider `consentRequired(provider)` is `true` for WhatsApp from the first PR.
  - Telegram keeps D684's behaviour until D756 adds its own in-chat request and turns its switch on. No Telegram chat is held behind a consent step that it has no screen to answer.
  - Until then, Telegram audio is never stored.

#### 5. Notice, opt-in, the 24-hour window and templates

- **Axal never messages anyone who has not written first.** Every message Axal sends in D754 is a free-form reply inside the 24-hour window that the other person opened. The window counts from their last inbound message (`routes/channels.ts:381-385`; `channels/types.ts:61`). D754 sends no template, so no template approval is needed.
- **The notice (way A), version `wa-consent-v1`.** It is an interactive message with two reply buttons, sent from the member's own business number when the member presses "Ask for consent". It is never sent on its own initiative (`D684.md:199`). The text:

  > "<Member first name> at <Venture> would like to record and transcribe this chat, including voice notes, for customer research, using Axal VC. It is kept until <Member first name> deletes it. Tap Yes to agree or No to decline. You can send STOP at any time."

  The buttons are "Yes, I agree" and "No, thanks". The words are English only in v1, and the member cannot edit them; a new text is a new version.
  - It is sent at most twice per thread per version, at least 24 hours apart, and once per `client_key` (`outbound.ts`).
  - With the window closed, the page says: "<Name> last wrote more than 24 hours ago, and WhatsApp only lets Axal write within 24 hours of their last message. Ask <Name> to send you a message from your WhatsApp Business app, then ask again." Nothing is sent.
- **Way B's messages** go to the member, in the chat the member opened: the link confirmation, the consent question, and "Saved to Axal" with a link that needs sign-in.
  - They never carry an interview's content, a name or a venture, so a reassigned number learns nothing.
  - Linking is the member's opt-in. `STOP` ends it.
- **Later, not in D754.** A consent request outside the window would need a utility template in each member's WhatsApp Business Account (`message_template_status_update`). It would also need stored proof of the interviewee's opt-in.

#### 6. Audio storage and the pipeline

- **Stored like the recorder's audio.**
  - Each consented voice note or forwarded file is put through `putChunk` (`interviewSessions.ts:139`) under `validate-audio/<owner>/sessions/<session uid>/<seq>.<ext>` (`chunkKey`, `:129-131`), with an `interview_session_chunks` row.
  - The existing signed download (`GET /sessions/:id/audio`, `routes/founder_validate_sessions.ts:792-820`), Open recording, and `deleteSession` (`interviewSessions.ts:239`) therefore work unchanged.
- **Transcribed by the same pipeline.**
  - Way B ends in `finishSession`, so `interview_process` runs exactly as it does for the recorder.
  - Way A's voice notes are chunks inside the chat's own session. Each one is transcribed once by the recorder's `transcribeChunk` (`interviewProcessing.ts:102-132`), with its staged transcript, an `interview_session_receipts` line, the session's language and its final model (`@cf/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo` by default, `services/sttModels.ts:46`).
  - The voice-note step allocates each note’s `seq` atomically and appends with `appendSegments`, never `replaceWithFinal`. The result is appended as one `final` segment at the note's time, with the side's speaker label (`threads.ts:113-117`), preserving the chat's text.
  - The direct `aiRun` in `routes/channels.ts:785-803` is removed for WhatsApp.
- **Size and formats.**
  - The recorder's 5 MB chunk limit is kept (`interviewSessions.ts:41`). It is a memory bound for Workers AI, and 5 MB holds about 30–40 minutes of WhatsApp voice (Opus).
  - Larger files are refused with "This recording is larger than Axal can take from WhatsApp (5 MB). Record it with the in-app recorder instead." The `too_large` sentence that says 16 MB (`services/channels/voiceFailures.ts`) changes to match.
  - Accepted types are `CHUNK_MIME` (`interviewSessions.ts:45-52`), with MIME parameters stripped, so `audio/ogg; codecs=opus` is accepted. `audio/aac` and `audio/amr` are refused in plain words until a Whisper fixture proves they decode.
  - Way B also accepts a `document` message whose MIME type is one of those.
  - Images, video and other documents are still ignored (`whatsapp.ts:127`).
- **Downloads.** They happen only in a job, never on the webhook path. They use the right token (the member's for A, Axal's system token for B) and only Meta hosts (`whatsapp.ts:59`, `:270-297`), through one bounded, streamed helper in `providerHttp.ts`, which Telegram reuses.
- **Media ids expire.** WhatsApp media ids can be fetched for 7 days. A waiting voice note shows "Add this chat to an interview by <date> to keep its voice notes" (WhatsApp only). After that date a fetch is recorded as `expired`: "WhatsApp keeps a voice note for 7 days; this one can no longer be fetched."

#### 8. Webhook security and idempotency

- **Kept as built.**
  - `X-Hub-Signature-256` is checked as an HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body with the app secret, compared in constant time, before anything is parsed (`whatsapp.ts:85-97`).
  - The body is capped at 256 KB (`events.ts:116-137`).
  - The GET challenge compares the verify token in constant time (`whatsapp.ts:69-77`).
  - Each webhook answers 503 without its secret (`routes/channels.ts:684-707`).
  - Tenancy for way A comes only from `metadata.phone_number_id` (`connections.ts:72-82`).
- **New.**
  - Way B's events are recognised by Axal's own `phone_number_id`, and their tenancy comes only from an active link that matches the sender's HMAC. A number written in a message's text is never read.
  - Consent replies, `LINK` and `STOP` ride `channel_events.kind = 'command'` (`450_channel_events_delete_kind.sql:31`), so no table rebuild is needed.
- **Each event is claimed once** (`UNIQUE(provider, external_event_id)`, `events.ts:33-40`).
- **Each write is idempotent:**
  - a consent reply is unique on its reply hash;
  - way B's session uses `client_key = 'whatsapp:' + sha256(first message id)`, under migration 452's unique index (`452_record_session_setup.sql:21-23`);
  - each chunk is unique on `(session_id, seq)`;
  - each outbound send is once per `client_key`.
- **Redeliveries.** A redelivery answers 200 and does nothing. A failure before anything was stored answers 500 so Meta retries (`routes/channels.ts:660-663`). Meta retries for up to 7 days.

#### 9. Rate limits

- **Webhook events:** 120 a minute per connection or link (`channels/types.ts:49`, `events.ts:80-93`).
- **Transcriptions:** 50 voice notes or files a day per connection or link, reserved before any download and failing closed (`events.ts:100-108`), plus the member's AI budget (D640).
- **The notice:** at most twice per thread per version (§5), and one open request per thread.
- **Linking:** one live code per member; codes per member per hour and wrong codes per sender per hour are counted (`channel_link_attempts`).
- **Way B:** at most 20 files per draft.
- **Outbound:** Meta's per-number throughput is 20 messages a second on coexistence (`D684.md:79`), far above this use.

#### 10. Costs

These are reported by third parties (Twilio, Respond.io, YCloud, Wati) and must be checked on Meta's pricing page [unverified]:

- **Inbound messages are free.**
- **Free-form replies inside the window** are free up to 1,000 a month per business number since 2026-10-01, and are charged at the market's utility rate after that.
  - Way A: the notice is sent from, and billed to, **the member's own WhatsApp Business Account**. Axal pays Meta nothing on this path. How Meta bills a Tech Provider's clients is for the owner to confirm.
  - Way B: about three messages per recording, from **Axal's** account, which needs a payment method once past 1,000 a month.
- **Transcription:** Workers AI Whisper turbo at $0.0005 a minute (`services/aiRouter.ts:270-276`). It is charged to the member's AI budget, with a receipt line, as the recorder is.
- **Storage:** R2, as the recorder's.
- **Calls:** none built. User-initiated calls are free; business-initiated calls are billed per minute.

#### 13. Provider-neutral parts (for D756 and D757)

These are keyed by `MessagingProvider` and tested with both providers:

- `channel_consents`;
- the consent-request route, with a dispatch per provider (WhatsApp's interactive message here; Telegram's inline keyboard in D756);
- the member's-word route;
- `consentRequired(provider)`;
- the audio acceptance table (MIME and size);
- the bounded download helper;
- the per-chunk voice-note step;
