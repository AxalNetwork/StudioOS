## D7905 part 5 — Fixes from S32's review of c36bf920 (#7905)

S32's independent review of `c36bf920` reproduced timing and cleanup gaps in
the executor. This part fixes the five that are executor code. It raises no
timeout, cap or lease: candidate work only gets shorter.

- **Reserves (F1).** Removal is three engine calls of 30 s each: inspect, rm
  and the absence check. `REMOVAL_RESERVE_MS` is therefore 90 s, and
  `FINISH_RESERVE_MS` is 150 s (60 s for pause, copy and seal, plus removal).
  Export receives the bound less the removal reserve.
- **Watchdog rounding (F2).** `date +%s` truncates the current second, so the
  in-container sleep now subtracts one. It can no longer stop after the
  receipt's deadline.
- **Run-step wrapper (F3).** Work, export and removal are bounded by the
  earlier of the receipt deadline and the Linux Run step's 26-minute wrapper.
  That is measured from process start (`performance.timeOrigin`) less a 5 s
  margin. S32's full-length counterexample, success at 1,589,999 ms against a
  1,560,000 ms wrapper, now ends inside the wrapper.
- **Export transitions (E1).** The exporter re-checks cancellation and the
  absolute budget after pause, before the copy starts (including an abort
  that arrived earlier), before sealing and before publication. A pause that
  outlives the budget, or aborts the run, publishes nothing.
- **Unconfirmed create (C3).** Once `docker create` has been attempted, a
  missing or unconfirmed ID reports cleanup as `unknown`
  (`create_unconfirmed`), not `none`, and `cleanupComplete` is false. Nothing
  is deleted without the exact ID.

Not fixed here: F6. Engine calls are synchronous, so a signal can wait
through staging, create, start and the isolation probes. Fixing it needs
asynchronous engine calls. F4/F5 (hosted cap, fallback) are s39's
coordinator. F7/F9 (install-inclusive and resource fit) stay unmeasured.
Pinned-engine and native behaviour remain unqualified.
