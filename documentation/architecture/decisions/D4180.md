### 1. The reads

| Read | Board | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/learning/exercises` | lr2a | title, the published course or programme that holds it, your latest submission's status (null when not started), updated |
| `GET /api/learning/exams` | lr2b | question count, time limit, pass mark, attempts left (null is unlimited), your best submitted score (null is "Score not recorded") |

### 2. Visibility

- **The lists are member lists.** They show published items, and a `program` item only to a member enrolled in a programme that holds it. This holds for every caller, the Super Admin included: drafts stay in the Studio. Every row is also passed through `canSeeLearning`.
- **"Me" reads bind the session's user id and nothing else.** Rows are kept only while their exercise or exam is still visible to the caller. No email, no reviewer and no other member's work is sent.
- **The course or programme shown beside an item** must itself be published, and a course must be open.

### 3. Not recorded is null, never 0

- **A best score** with no submitted attempt is null.
- **Watch time** is the sum of this month's recorded `learning_progress.position_seconds`, which D3726's player writes. It is null when no position was recorded this month.
- **A figure whose store cannot be read** is null.
- **A count over a readable store**, such as courses in progress, is a real number, 0 included.
- **"This month"** is the UTC calendar month. Progress timestamps are ISO strings, so the comparison is on their first seven characters.

### 4. Transcript search

The catalog's `q` now also matches `transcript` (bound `LIKE`), for videos and readings only.

- **The transcript is not sent in the list.** It is selected only while `q` is set, and only to work out `match`.
- **`match`** says where the term sits when the title and summary do not hold it. It carries up to about 160 characters around the term, and `at_seconds`: the time of the nearest timestamp before the term. A timestamp is "04:12", "[04:12]" or a WebVTT cue's start. When the transcript carries none, `at_seconds` is null.
- **`max_minutes`** (1–1440) keeps videos and readings with a recorded `duration_seconds` within it, and courses with `estimated_minutes` within it. Anything with no recorded length is left out rather than claimed to fit. Articles, publications and events have no length, so they are `filtered_out` with the reason.

### 5. Webinar attendance

The detail read adds `attended_count` and `registered_count` for the host only: the Super Admin, or the webinar's `created_by`.

- `attended_count` is null while no registration has `attended` recorded.
- A member's read carries neither field.

### 6. The page

- **Exercises and Exams tabs:** both are live. They read their own lists a page at a time (`AssessmentList`). A failed read is "could not be read", never an empty list. `UNLISTED` and `TAB_NOT_LIVE` are removed.
- **My learning:** Exercises shows one row per exercise, with the latest status. Exam results shows one row per exam, with the best submitted score, else "Score not recorded".
- **Catalog:** a transcript hit reads "Found in a transcript "…" at 04:12".
- **Course outline:** lesson lengths appear on their own, because the outline already draws `duration_seconds` once the read sends it.
- **Not drawn yet:** the "Your progress" strip (`statsTiles`) and the length filter control. They have no screen on `main` yet; the D3726 screen task draws them on these reads.
