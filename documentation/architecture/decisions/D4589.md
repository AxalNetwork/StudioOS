## D4589 — One next-action policy for Validate, on desktop and phone (#4589)

Status: proposed, 2026-11-06. Part of #4584 (phase 3).

Validate's overview read (`GET /api/cycles/validate/overview`) already returned
the phone root's `focus`. That focus is now produced by one pure policy,
`cloudflare-worker/src/services/validateNextAction.ts` (`nextAction`), over
facts `services/validateFocus.ts` reads. The desktop overview draws the same
`focus` in its lead row (`frontend/src/pages/cycles/ValidateNextAction.jsx`)
through the same browser adapter as the phone (`validatePhone.js`
`phoneFocus`), so the same venture state gives the same action on both. No
new route, store or migration.

### Order (the first rule that holds)

0. A fact a rule needs could not be read: `unavailable`, with no primary
   action, the reason naming what failed, and "This is not a claim that
   nothing is waiting." A failed read never falls through to a lower rule.
1. No interview: record the first conversation (unchanged).
   A caller who cannot write gets the first card's own next (unchanged, D692).
2. An interview session waits for review: review it, at
   `/validate/customer-research/<interview>?project_id=<venture>&session=<id>`.
   The session is chosen as today (newest session); #4587 owns the queue
   ordering and sends the same href.
3. The venture's current decision is Stop: review that decision. The panel
   offers no recording or building; "Record a new decision" is the
   deliberate reopen. Rule 2 precedes it: evidence already gathered is the
   policy's one reason to revisit a Stop.
4. A claim's supporting interviews have no ICP fit recorded (its status is
   Not recorded): record the fit, on the claim's record.
5. An experiment is marked done with an empty result: record the observed
   result. Planned and running experiments are not unfinished results.
6. A claim has supporting and contradicting interviews and is neither
   supported nor refuted: plan the next test.
7. No current venture decision and a claim is supported or refuted: consider
   the decision, linking the founder's own decision form. The policy never
   records Proceed, Pivot or Stop, and its words say the decision is the
   founder's.
8. The current decision is Proceed or Pivot and the caller owns the venture:
   the existing Plan the build handoff (D1069), at the href the Verdict
   record declares (`planBuildHref`, now shared by both). An admin who is not
   the founder is not offered it, as on the Verdict record.
9. Otherwise record the next customer conversation (unchanged).

Every primary action has a short `why` and an href naming the venture.
`focus.secondary` keeps Record a session in reach (not under a Stop), and the
desktop header's Record a session is unchanged.

### Dependencies

The facts are today's: claim status from `claimsOf` (interview evidence
through pain links), experiment status/result as stored. #4585 (evidence
contract) and #4586 (methodology) may change what "supported", "missing fit"
and "observed result" mean; when they land, only the fact readers change, not
the policy or its two presentations. #4601 owns the shared card retry; this
does not touch it.

### Design

No canvas draws a desktop next-action panel. The desktop panel reuses the
approved Plan the build panel (VB2a) for one line and one button; the phone
draws the existing One Next Thing root (COM). A canvas for a dedicated
desktop panel, if wanted, follows the design-brief process.
