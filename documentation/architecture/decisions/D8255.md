## D8255 — Grow Markets: the WGI and FATF country indices on a market's record (#8255)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-22. Follows D6120, whose evidence line read "WGI and FATF indices … Not readable from Grow yet", and D1259, which owns the indices and their licences. No migration, no new route.

### What this decides

1. **The record read carries the indices** (`parts.market.indices`, `services/records/grow.ts`), from `countryIndexLines` in the existing index service (`ventureScoring/countryIndex.ts`). It applies the rule the scorer applies (D1259 §3): a source with no recorded licence is not read, and a value counts only while its edition's licence is the one recorded now. The newest such edition is read.
2. **Each source is a line** with its publisher, edition, published day (the as-of date), citation link and values. WGI shows its four percentile ranks (0 to 100); a measured 0 is a value.
3. **FATF reads Not recorded today**, with its reason: the FATF's licence is not recorded (D1259 §3, the owner decides), so nothing is read from it even where an edition is stored. Recording the licence there is what turns it on; no code changes.
4. **No figure is Not recorded, never 0:** a WGI edition without the country, no edition imported, or a market with no country. A list edition (FATF, EU) that does not name the country says it is not named, with the edition and day, because for a published list absence is the publisher's statement.
5. **A failed read is Unavailable** for that source and marks the read `unreadable`; the other sources still show.
6. **Frontend:** `indexLines` (`marketsModel.js`) draws the lines in Regulation's evidence; `MarketRecord.jsx` shows Unavailable beside Not recorded and links the source.

### Rollback

Revert the commit. Nothing is written; the stored editions are untouched.
