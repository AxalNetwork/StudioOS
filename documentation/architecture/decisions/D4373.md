## D4373 — HQ refunds a deck generation run by its batch uid (#4373)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-04 (#4373, split from #4317).
- **Number:** D4373 is the issue's number (D4106).
- **Migration:** none.

### Why

A deck run holds once and settles that hold once, at the sum of its steps'
charges (D1502). When a one-click build failed on Axal's side, support had no
way to give the run back: it could only add one adjustment by hand, of an
amount it had to work out from SQL.

### What changed

- **The route.** `POST /api/ai-budget/deck-batches/:uid/refund`, HQ Admin or
  Super Admin (`requireHqAdmin`), with `{ "reason": "<10 to 400 characters>" }`.
  It sits behind the recovery cool-off, as an adjustment does.
- **What it refunds.** `services/decks/batchRefund.ts` `refundBatch` refunds
  every step with a charge, on a run whose hold is `settled`, to the founder's
  AI tokens (the top-up balance). In all it never refunds more than the hold
  settled (`actual_included_microusd + actual_topup_microusd`).
- **One row each.** Each charge is one `adjustment` row in `ai_credit_ledger`
  (`source_ref = 'adj:deck-refund:<batch uid>:<seq>'`), its balance change and
  one `admin_audit_log` row (`deck_batch_refund`), in one D1 batch.
- **Idempotent.** `UNIQUE(kind, source_ref)` keys each charge, so a second
  press, or a concurrent twin, writes nothing again and reports the charge
  `already_refunded`.
- **Nothing settled.** A hold `pending`, in `review`, already `refunded`, or no
  hold refunds 0 and says why (`nothing_settled: true`, with a message).
- **Not goodwill.** These rows give back a charge, so `recordAdjustment`'s
  $100 bound does not apply (the charge is the bound), and its $250-in-30-days
  window leaves them out.
- **The member** gets one notice per press, with the amount and never the
  reason.
- **The runbook.** DECK_GRID.md §2's `settled` step uses the route.
