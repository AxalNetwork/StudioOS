Paths are relative to `frontend/src/pages/events/` unless they say otherwise. "A phone" is below 1024px (`PHONE_QUERY`, read through `ui/phone/usePhoneWidth.js`).

### 1. The frame: D700's A7m is retired for this page

- **Removed** from `MyEventsPage.jsx`: `EventsPhoneView`, `AttendingList`, `eventsPhoneGroups`, `StatusBadge` and their styles and date helper. A phone no longer draws A7m's three sections.
- **The bar** is D700's contract for an account page: `PhoneBar` with Back, `ACCOUNT_PHONE_PAGES.events`'s title and sub, and Eadwyn under the same title.
- **Then, in a column:** the explainer, the Explorer line where it applies, the same four tabs as a desktop (`/my/events`, `/hosting`, `/discover`, `/past`, as literal links, scrolling sideways), Hosting's New event across its row, and the tab's body.
- **One tab bar, one body:** the page builds the tabs and the body once (`nav`, `body`) and places them in the phone frame or the desktop one. Hosting, Discover and Past are the desktop tabs inside the phone root.
- **The root** carries `PHONE_PAGE` and `my-events-phone` (`PHONE_ROOT`, `myEventsPhoneModel.js`).

### 2. MEV18a: Upcoming

`PhoneUpcoming` (`MyEventsPhone.jsx`):

- **Needs you,** as on a desktop.
- **The week strip** in place of the month (`WeekStrip`, from `weekStrip` in `myEventsPhoneModel.js`):
  - seven days, Monday first, of the week that holds today in the viewer's zone;
  - each day with a dot per event it is going to and one per event it hosts (an Explorer's hosting is left out), and its words for a screen reader ("Thu 12 Nov: 2 events: going to 1, hosting 1");
  - ‹ and › move a week, Today comes back; the title names the week ("9–15 Nov", "30 Nov – 6 Dec");
  - a day's tap shows only that day's events under Going, with "Show every day" to clear it.
- **The next event, full width:** its pills (the place, a recorded seat source, You're presenting), then Show ticket, Add to calendar and, for an online event, the timed Join (D1131).
- **Going as cards:** grouped as on a desktop, each with Show ticket and the row's menu.
- **The empty state** is the desktop's, pointing at Discover.

### 3. MEV18b: the ticket full screen

- **`/my/events/ticket/:id` on a phone** is the ticket alone: `TicketDrawer` with `page`, which is `Lightbox`'s page mode (`pageBelow="lg"`), under a `PhoneBar` whose Back reads "Events".
- **The code** is drawn at 240 (210 in the drawer), and under it the code line carries "Turn your screen brightness up at the door." (`BRIGHTNESS`), on a phone only.
- The drawer's Close is not drawn on the page (Back is the way out); Cancel my place stays.

### 4. MEV18c: check in from Hosting

- **On the event's day,** Hosting's "Check in" on a phone is a button that opens `CheckInSheet` (`MyEventsPhone.jsx`), a bottom sheet. On a desktop it is the link to the manage page's Check-in, as before.
- **The camera** is the manage page's: `CheckinScanner.jsx` now exports `useScanner` (the camera, the decoder, the typed code) and `ScannerView`; its default export is the same modal as before.
- **The sheet's line:** "[event] · [31] of [42] checked in" (`checkInLine`), from the row's `checked_in` and `seats_taken`, plus each new check-in in this sheet. A count the list did not carry reads "Checked in: not read here yet", never 0.
- **Each scan** says who was let in, or that they already were (`checkInResult`, D1036's words); an already checked-in ticket does not move the count. A refused code says why.
- **Enter a code** switches to the typed code; **Done** closes the sheet and the lists re-read.

### 5. Sheets and 44 × 44px targets

- **Bottom sheets:** on a phone every dialog and drawer of the page but the ticket is a sheet (`Lightbox`'s `phone="sheet"`, D1014): the hosted-event drawer, the change and cancel dialogs, a month day, the chip panel, Subscribe, Add to calendar (both) and the check-in sheet.
- **Touch targets:** `myEventsPhone.css` is plain CSS outside Tailwind's layers, imported by `MyEventsPhone.jsx`. Under `my-events-phone`, a button and a select are at least 44 × 44px; a field, a textarea and a link at least 44px tall; the tabs are boxes; the label around a checkbox or radio is 44px tall. `Lightbox` is not portalled, so the sheets sit inside the root and follow the rule, the × included.
