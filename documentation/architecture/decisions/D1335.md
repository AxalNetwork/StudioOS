### 1. What the owner chose

The owner answered #2318 on 2026-10-27: a member flow, with apply and withdraw, reached from Studio and Inbox. This is TST10's option C, "Pool, then programmes":
1. A member joins the Product tester pool once.
2. An Admin or Super Admin decides in Approvals' Product testers lane (#2390) or on the account (#2377).
3. An active tester applies to each open programme and may withdraw.

The member's `/product-testing` page and its Account entry stay removed (#2314, #2316). There is no new top-level page or menu entry. Every detail and form opens in the shared right Sheet (D1317).

### 3. Rules the build keeps

- **No email is shown to anyone, and no other tester's name.** A firm's answers go to that firm only, as the canvas says.
- **An Admin account sees no card.** Admins cannot be testers (migration 576).
- **Any member with a profile may join, Explorers included** (the #2377 story). A declined member may join again from `reapplyAfter`'s date, never before.
- **Consent is recorded with its version.** The pool join stores `consent_email_at` and `consent_version`, as an application already does. A new consent version asks again.
- **Leaving the pool** ("I can leave the tester pool at any time") sets the member's own row to `removed`, with reason "Left by the member", and is logged in `product_tester_events`. Picked places carry on (D1132's removal rule counts them for HQ).
