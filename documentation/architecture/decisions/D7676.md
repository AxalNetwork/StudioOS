### What the canvas decides

- A person is three separate attributes: a person ID that exists without an account, an optional account link, and the viewer's private relationship (NPD-00). Network people stop being only rows in each member's own `network_connections` book.
- People search has three scopes, each with its own true total: Discoverable, My relationships and Team relationships. Filters live in the address, pages hold 50 with a total true across pages, and people whose geography is Not recorded are listed in their own group under the results (NPD-01).
- The Person Sheet keeps one person across several sources (NFX Signal, Folk, Airtable), shows concurrent and former affiliations, and says a sourced profile is not a verified identity, consent or endorsement. The viewer's private record and team warm path sit in a dashed area of their own, and observed dates stay apart from interactions (NPD-02). Opened from People, Relationships, an organization or an introduction request, the sheet is the same; only the private area changes with the viewer (NPD-03).
- A member-confirmed fact is never replaced by a newer import. Members Suggest a correction and see one of four results (NPD-04).
- Four actions each say what they contact: save, request introduction (the one primary, by person ID), correction and invite (NPD-05). A missing person is added as a provisional target, private to the requester until a steward reviews it (NPD-06).
- Duplicate review shows evidence on both sides; a name is never evidence, and two namesakes stay separate. A merge runs Scope → Review → Result and leaves a redirect (NPD-07). Claiming and linking an account is its own flow (NPD-08).
- Data states, the scope and access matrix and deep links are drawn (NPD-09 to NPD-11), with tablet 768, phone 390, dark and accessibility (NPD-12 to NPD-15).
