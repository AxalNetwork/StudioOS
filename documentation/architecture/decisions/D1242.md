### 2. What is drawn

- **Header.** "Studio › Deal flow", the title "Deal flow", and the canvas's line "Every round on the platform, how it scores, and who is looking at it." An admin also gets "Draft a deal", which opens the moved draft.
- **Toolbar.** Board, Table and Not raising (each one an address), and a search over venture, sector, lead partner and source.
- **The intake notice.** "[n] rounds were set to Not raising by intake scoring, not by a person." with "Review them". It is shown only when intake set at least one.
- **An Operating Partner's line.** "You can move the rounds you lead. The studio moves the rest."
- **Board.** Its columns are the round's status: In intake, Screened, Raising and Funded. Each column heads with its count and its asks per currency, and those with no ask are counted apart, never summed as 0. Each card shows:
  - the venture and sector;
  - the source (or "Source not recorded");
  - "You lead this";
  - the ask;
  - the investors;
  - the days in stage with the SLA band;
  - the lead partner;
  - its ⋯.
- **Table.** Venture, Round, Ask, Scorecard, Lead partner ("You" on a round the caller leads), Investors and Source, then ⋯.
  - The Scorecard says Not read here yet until SCORING (#2428).
  - Under Not raising it adds "Set by": "Passed by [name]", "Set to Not raising by intake scoring, not by a person", or "Who set it is not recorded".
- **Investors.** "[n] invited · [n] interested · [n] committed" means:
  - invited: every invitation;
  - interested: invitations answered interested;
  - committed: investors with a confirmed commitment.
  With none, it reads "Not invited yet". A count the read did not return reads Not recorded.
- **Each row's ⋯** (a disclosure, not an ARIA menu; Escape closes it):
  - Advance and Pass… appear only where the read's `can_move` says the Worker allows it.
  - Invite investors is for an admin on a Screened or Raising round (the Worker's own rule). It opens the deal page's Invitations tab, `/deals/:id/invitations`.
  - Edit terms appears where the round may be moved.
- **The dialogs say what changes before anything is saved:**
  - **Advance:** "Move [venture] to [status]?", and that the round moves for every investor and the founder is told.
  - **Pass:** the moved dialog, a reason required, with the canvas's line "This closes the round for every investor and tells the founder."
  - **Edit terms:** the moved editor in a dialog.
- **After a write.** A refusal stays in the dialog, in the Worker's own sentence. A success closes the dialog, says what was done, and reads the lens, the funnel and the passes again.
- **Failed reads.** Each block that cannot be read says so with Retry, never a zero. The header and toolbar always draw.

### 3. The Worker

- **The read.** `GET /api/deals/studio` (`services/dealStudioLens.ts`) is for an admin or an Operating Partner (`requireRole('partner')`). It returns every round, newest first, each with:
  - its lead;
  - its investor counts;
  - its source;
  - its terms (for Edit terms);
  - `not_raising_by` and the passer's name;
  - `you_lead`;
  - `can_move`.
  It also returns `intake_not_raising`. It returns no pass note. The client is `lib/api/dealFlow.js`, with one line in `api.js` (D527).
- **Who moves a round.** `canMoveRound` lets an admin move any round, and an Operating Partner only the rounds they lead (`deals.lead_partner_id`).
  - `POST /:id/advance`, `POST /:id/pass` and every write through `PUT /:id` refuse anyone else with 403 `not_your_round`, before the stage event and before anything is written.
  - Only an admin changes `lead_partner_id` (403 `lead_admin_only`). Otherwise a partner could take a round and then move it.
- **The pass analytics.** `/pass-analytics` no longer counts intake's Not raising rows as passes "before a reason was required" (#2423 item 6). It reports them apart as `intake_not_raising`, and Why we passed says so.

### 4. Intake told apart from a person

The issue asks for a `kind` on the stage event. What is true on the basis changes how that lands:
- **Intake no longer writes `rejected`.** Since #2643 (2026-10-23, `55e06dd290`), a score under 70 leaves the round In intake with a low-score flag for a human decision. Only rounds scored before then carry intake's `rejected`.
- **A new `kind` value needs a migration.** The events' `kind` has a CHECK for `advance`, `pass` and `set`, so a new value means rebuilding the table, and the issue says "Migration: none".

So, with no migration:
- **The writer.** Intake records its arrival as a stage event of kind `set` with no actor. A person's moves always carry their actor, so the missing actor is what tells intake apart.
- **The old rows.** `notRaisingBy` reads the evidence already recorded:
  - **a person:** `passed_by_user_id`, or a stage event to `rejected` with an actor;
  - **intake:** the project's own `auto_scored` line reading "Status: rejected", which intake wrote until #2643. It is matched with a bound `LIKE`.
  - **not recorded:** anything else (a CRM sync, a queue job, an old direct write). It is never guessed to be a pass.

If the owner wants an `intake` kind in the column itself, that is a migration rebuilding `deal_stage_events` with the wider CHECK, plus a backfill from the same evidence. That would be a follow-up.
