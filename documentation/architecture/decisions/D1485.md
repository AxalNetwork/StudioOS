## D1485 — Calendar parts 3 and 4: Google and Outlook events read-only on the calendar, de-duplicated, and "Copy new Axal sessions to…"

- **Status:** built by S12 for #3979 (parts 3 and 4 of #3723), from S1's specification on #3723 (sections "Connected calendars, read-only", "Routes › copy_target", "Tests" and owner steps A, B and E).
- **Builds on:**
  - D1484 (the source registry, `source.account`, and part 2's `calendar_settings.copy_target`, migration 708);
  - D1209 (`calendar_sync_records` and the sync error codes);
  - D1211 (the item drawer) and D1212 (the connected calendars, CAL9).
- **Migration:** none. Owner question A, S1's pick (a): read live per window, and store nothing external.

### The question

A member who connected Google or Outlook saw Axal's sessions copied out to that calendar, but never their own calendar on Axal's. The calendar could not be "the one timeline" without them. Reading them in raises three risks: storing a member's private calendar, showing a session twice (once as Axal's item and again as the copy Axal made of it), and copying an event that came in back out again, which would loop. Separately, a member with both providers connected had no way to say where new Axal sessions should be copied.

### What was decided

**Two kinds, page only** (`services/calendar/externalReaders.ts`)
- `external_google` and `external_microsoft`, registered in `SOURCES` with family `external`, `page_only: true`, `pushable: false`.
- Being page only keeps them out of `.ics`, both feeds and the provider copy (`fetchUserEvents` drops page-only kinds). `/push` refuses them (400), because they are not in `PUSHABLE_KINDS`.
- They are in `PERSONAL_ONLY_KINDS`, so no staff scope lists them.
- Each item is `read_only: true`, `source_id: 0`, `source_uid` the provider's id, and carries `external: { provider, ical_uid, account: { provider, address } }`. `source.account` is that account, on the owner's own read (`GET /api/calendar/events` is only ever the member's own).

**Read live, capped**
- Google pages `events.list` on the primary calendar (`singleEvents=true`, `timeMin`/`timeMax`, `maxResults=250`), with `fields` limited to `id,iCalUID,summary,start,end,status,htmlLink,hangoutLink`, following `services/google/raise.ts`.
- Microsoft reads Graph `/me/calendarView` with `$select`, `$top=250` and `Prefer: outlook.timezone="UTC"`, and follows `@odata.nextLink` only when it is on `graph.microsoft.com`.
- Each stops at 4 pages × 250. A read that stopped there adds `truncated: true` to that source's status.
- Cancelled events are not shown. An event with no title reads "Busy".
- Google reads only when the stored grant names a calendar scope. Owner step E confirms that production's consent screen covers `calendar.readonly`.

**Failures stay with their source**
- `CalendarSourceStatus.error` gains `'reconnect'` (an expired or revoked token: a refused refresh, a 401 or 403, or a Google grant with no calendar scope) and `'rate_limited'` (a 429). Anything else is `'unreadable'`.
- Only that source fails. The Axal sources and the other provider still answer.
- A member with no connection gets no external source at all. Absence is not an error.

**Dedupe** (`dedupeExternal`, applied by `GET /api/calendar/events` before `describeItems`)
- An external event whose id is a `calendar_sync_records.external_event_id` for this member and provider is Axal's own copy. It is dropped, and the Axal item it copies gains `also_on: [provider]`.
- An external event whose `iCalUID` equals an Axal item's `source_uid` is merged the same way.
- One that starts within a minute of an Axal item and shares a title word is flagged `possible_duplicate: true` and kept. Nothing is merged on a guess.

**`copy_target`** (`services/calendar/sync.ts`)
- `onAxalSessionCreated` (and `onAxalSessionUpdated`, the same function) copies a session to a provider only when the attendee's `calendar_settings.copy_target` is `'all'` or names that provider.
- The check runs before the token is loaded, so a provider left out gets no call at all: `'google'` makes zero Microsoft calls, and `'none'` makes none.
- A copy the provider already holds (a `calendar_sync_records` row) keeps being updated, so changing the choice never leaves a stale copy behind.
- No row, or a setting that cannot be read, is `'all'`: today's behaviour.

**The page**
- `CalendarConnected.jsx` gains "Copy new Axal sessions to…" (`PUT /api/calendar/settings`). A calendar that is not connected cannot be chosen, and a refusal says why.
- CAL9 gains a seventh state, `reconnect` ("Reconnect to show its events"), taken from the events read's `sources`, with Reconnect as its first action.
- An external item opens a read-only sheet at `/calendar/external/:kind/:id`, drawn from the page's own read, which offers "Open in Google" or "Open in Outlook" and nothing else.
- The two kinds sit in "From other calendars", beside Calendly.

**Never logged:** titles, attendees, addresses or provider bodies. A failed source logs its kind and its code.

### Tests

- `cloudflare-worker/test/calendar_external_d1485.test.ts`, with a stubbed fetch, covers:
  - the page cap and `truncated`;
  - the Graph nextLink host;
  - each provider status as its code (401 and 403 `reconnect`, 429 `rate_limited`, else `unreadable`);
  - `reconnect` for one source while the others answer;
  - `rate_limited`;
  - no source without a connection;
  - both dedupe rules giving `also_on`;
  - the near-match flag;
  - `/push` and `.ics` refusing both kinds, which never reach the copy out or a staff scope;
  - a `console.warn` spy that sees no title, address or provider body;
  - `copy_target` for `'google'`, `'none'`, `'all'`, no row, and a held copy.
- Twenty-two mutation checks were each seen red (sixteen in the Worker, six in the page models and wiring, `frontend/test/calendar_external_d1485.test.mjs`), including the issue's three: removing the `external_event_id` dedupe, adding an external kind to `FEED_KINDS`, and defaulting `copy_target` to `'none'`.
