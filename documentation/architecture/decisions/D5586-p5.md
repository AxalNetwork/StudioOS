## D5586 part 5 — Swarm admission retention runs daily at 03:17 UTC (#5586)

Part 4 (D5586-p4) built `runSwarmRetention` and left its schedule to the owner (§6). On 2026-11-28 the owner answered
all three questions with the recommended options:

- **A1.** A company's tenant keeps the siblings' rule: named, then refused if another tenant holds it. No company →
  tenant record is added.
- **B1.** D1's `swarm_admission_events` stays append-only and is never pruned.
- **C1.** Retention runs from a daily cron at `17 3 * * *` UTC, declared in all three `wrangler.toml` tables.

The owner gave no values for the coordinator's caps per provider and did not approve hosted-stage cost or quota, so
both stay open.

### 1. What runs, and when

- **`services/agents/swarmRetentionSchedule.ts`** holds the minute (`SWARM_RETENTION_CRON`, `isSwarmRetentionMinute`)
  and `scheduledSwarmRetention(env)`.
  - It makes one `runSwarmRetention` call, a `POST /retire` on the `WorkspaceAdmission` coordinator.
  - It turns the answer into one log line. Anything retired, or a pass that stopped at its batch bound (`more`), is
    `info`. A refusal is `warn` and names its code. Nothing retired is quiet.
  - A deployment with no `WORKSPACE_ADMISSION` binding (`admission_unbound`, a branch Worker) is quiet. The function
    never throws.
- **The cron handler (`index.ts`)** calls it in the every-minute firing whose wall clock reads 03:17 UTC. Every other
  daily job there is gated the same way.
  - It is **not** gated on `hqCadences`: each deployment has its own coordinator, so each prunes its own.
  - The module is loaded by a dynamic `import()`, so the frozen hub's static import list (D5615) is unchanged.
  - A throw goes to `taskFailed('swarm retention', …)`, like its neighbours.
- **`17 3 * * *`** is added to `[triggers]`, `[env.production.triggers]` and `[env.preview.triggers]`, and to
  `CRON_TRIGGERS` as `swarm_retention`.
  - With the line, HQ · Platform grades the run against its own schedule (D201), and Cloudflare's dashboard shows it.
  - The firing is deduplicated with the every-minute firing by the existing per-minute lease, so the job runs once.
  - Preview is included because it has its own `WorkspaceAdmission` namespace, and the owner named all three tables.
  - A branch's crons (`BRANCH_CRONS`) are unchanged. A branch Worker has no binding, and its every-minute tick would
    be quiet anyway.

A pass that does not finish (`more`) carries on the next night. The batch bounds are D5586-p4 §3's.

### 2. The minute

Minute :17 is shared. The hourly `hqCadences` jobs at :17 and the 03:00–03:59 profile-evolution tick also run then, and
D358 skips only :15 and :45. One retention call is one bounded request to one object, so it adds little to that
invocation. The owner chose 03:17 knowing the other 03:xx jobs, and no other block uses 03:17 for a daily job.

### 4. Not in this part

- **Hosted test stages.** They wait on the owner's approval of cost and quota.
- **The scheduled `admit`/`reconcile`/`promote`, and callers.** They would share this minute's pattern but act on
  admissions. That waits on the caps.

### Rollback

Revert the commit: the line, the block and the module go together. Retention itself is D5586-p4's. A run that has
happened retired only settled or withdrawn tickets past their age, and carried their totals forward. Reverting stops
later runs and undoes nothing.
