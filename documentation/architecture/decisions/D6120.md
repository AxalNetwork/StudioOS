## D6120 — Grow v2 Markets: /grow/market-expansion built to its boards (#6120)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-22 to the canvas `design/incoming/Grow v2 Markets Page.dc.html` (boards GR-21 to GR-29, D6119), with the owner's answers of 2026-11-16 (option A on all three, D6119-p2). No new route: Markets stays `/grow/market-expansion`, and its record `/grow/market-expansion/:uid`.
- **Route-map row:** `route-map/canvases/part-3/0163-grow-markets-gr21.md`.

### What this decides

1. **Markets owns its page and its record.** `defs/grow.jsx` gives `market-expansion` a `homes` entry (`MarketsHome.jsx`) and a `recordPage` entry (`MarketRecord.jsx`). `CycleRoute.jsx` (a frozen hub) is not edited: both hooks were already there. `CycleModulePage.jsx` and `PhoneModulePage.jsx` now also hand a home its table's `query` and `onQuery`, so a page that owns its list filters and sorts through the read; the other homes ignore the two props.
2. **The list read carries what the next step and Compare need** (`routes/cycle_grow.ts`, no new store): each market's attached country and region, its entry steps (all, open, overdue: due before today and not done) and localisation checks (done, and the keys still open in order); `parts.state_counts` over every market, whatever the filter; `parts.can_write`; and `replies_state` / `plan_state`. A read that fails leaves its fields `null` and its state `'unreadable'`: never a zero, never an empty list. `marketReplies` keeps its old contract; `marketRepliesRead` reports the failure (the brief's "a replies read that reports failure").
3. **The record read carries the market as stored** (`services/records/grow.ts`, `parts.market`): the four scores with their sources, readiness and the missing parts, the decision and its day, the country, the size, the entry steps with due and done days, the localisation checks with their days and the replies to a market ask. Each store is read on its own and says `*_state: 'unreadable'` when it fails. The record's sections and actions are unchanged, so the generic record contract and #6113's step actions still apply.
4. **Next step, in order:** the first unscored part ("Score regulation, and say where it came from", opening Edit market); then, for a fully scored market that is not Not now or Exit, "Add the first entry step" when it has none, then "Tick <first open check>". A reader sees no next step.
5. **Compare is built from the list read alone**, since the list now carries the plan counts; an unscored part and an unscored readiness are named as not comparable, never compared.
6. **Eadwyn writes nothing here.** No proposal is drawn, because nothing stores or produces one on a market yet; the page says what it reads and the founder's own Edit market writes the score and source in one save.
