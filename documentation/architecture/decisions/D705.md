## D705 — Every document names Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP, and the Fund signs through its general partner

Status: Decided. Date: 2026-10-17. Slot: S12 (moved from S20 at the owner's instruction). Issue: #1716 (part 1 of 2; #1717 changes the template bodies). Decided by S1 under the owner's standing rule, on the owner's instruction of 2026-10-17. This record is the build's.

The owner's instruction:
- The Fund's legal name is **Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP**.
- The owner signs for Axal VC Management LLC as **Managing Partner**, and for Axal VC GP LLC as **General Partner**.
- Both LLCs are at 16192 Coastal Hwy, Lewes, DE 19958, United States.

### What was there

- The repo used three names for one fund, and none was the legal name:
  - documents printed "Axal VC Fund I, LP" (`legalDocFormat.ts` and its JS mirror, `brand/gvpn.ts`, the e-sign intro, `README.md`, `LEGAL_ENTITIES.md` and three pages);
  - the `vc_funds` row is "Spin-Out Fund I";
  - the UI said "Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I".
- Every Axal signature block printed `Title: Managing Partner` (`AXAL_SIGNATORY_TITLE`), the GP's included.
- The Fund never signed. `axalEntityKeyForDoc` routed every fund and portfolio-investment document to the GP, so a subscription or a SAFE named Axal VC GP LLC as the party. That contradicted `LEGAL_ENTITIES.md` rule 4 ("the Fund (or an SPV) as investor"), and left `AXAL_ENTITIES.fund` unused.

### Decisions

1. **The name.** `AXAL_ENTITIES.fund.name` is `Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP`, exactly as the owner wrote it, in `legalDocFormat.ts`, `legalDocFormat.js` and `gvpn.ts`. If the Certificate of Limited Partnership on file reads differently, the certificate wins, and the owner says so on the PR before merging.
2. **Titles by party.** Each Axal entity carries its own `signatoryTitle`:

   | Party | Title |
   |---|---|
   | Management | Managing Partner |
   | GP | General Partner |
   | Fund | General Partner, signing through the GP |
   | Holdings | Managing Partner, until the owner gives another |

   `AXAL_SIGNATORY_TITLE` stays exported as `Managing Partner`, because the graduation certificate signs as Management (`lib/graduationCertificate.js`).
3. **The Fund signs through its general partner.** A `fund` block reads:

   ```text
   Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP
   By: Axal VC GP LLC, its general partner
   By: ___________________________
   Name: Joseph Gabriel Guillaume Lauzier
   Title: General Partner
   Date: ___________________________
   ```

   - `SignatureParty` gains an optional `through` line, set from `AXAL_ENTITIES.fund.through`.
   - `buildExecutionBlock` takes `axalEntityKey`, which sets the title and the `through` line. Without a key it is Management, as before.
   - `pdf.ts` passes the key it already resolves, `drawSignatureParty` draws the line under the heading, and `executionBlockHeight` counts it.
   - `PaperPreview.jsx` draws it the same way.
   - The heading prints the name as every other block does, not upper-cased as the issue's illustration shows: the blocks share one style.
4. **Which documents the Fund signs.** `axalEntityKeyForDoc` returns `fund` for:
   - the LP's subscription: `subscription`, `investor_subscription_pro`, `investor_subscription_inst`, `subscription booklet & lpa`;
   - its side letter: `co-investment side letter`;
   - the instruments the Fund invests through: `safe`, `term_sheet`, `spa`, `voting_rights`.

   The rest of the old fund set stays `gp`, and so does the regex fallback: `lpa`, `ppm`, `mgmt_company`, `carried_interest`, `investor_nda_axal`, `accreditation_v1`, `spv joinder agreement`, `strategic side letter / focused spv` and `secondary purchase agreement`. D706's named sets (#1718, migration 468) are read first and do not change. A `fund` preamble names "Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP, a Delaware limited partnership, acting by its general partner Axal VC GP LLC".
5. **The e-sign intro** (`esign.ts` `buildTemplateBody`):
   - the Subscription Booklet & LPA and the Co-Investment Side Letter read `Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP, a Delaware limited partnership acting by its general partner Axal VC GP LLC ("Axal")`;
   - the SPV Joinder Agreement and the Strategic Side Letter / Focused SPV read `Axal VC GP LLC, as general partner of Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP ("Axal")`.
6. **Prose and pages** take the new name:
   - `README.md`;
   - `LEGAL_ENTITIES.md`, whose entity table gains a signatory column with decision 2's titles, and whose rules 3 and 4 now match decision 4;
   - `TeamPage.jsx`, `InvestorDealflowHomePage.jsx` and `RiskDisclosuresPage.jsx`.

   U5 in `UNRESOLVED_ITEMS.md` gets a dated line: the owner gave the legal name, which settles the naming half of U5 and not its terms.
7. **Not here.**
   - Template bodies and the Terms and Privacy pages are #1717's, because changing their text needs new template versions.
   - The `vc_funds` row stays as it is: it is an admin-editable display name.
   - The documents the Fund still needs are #1718's.
   - How each document looks waits for the owner's "Legal Documents · Templates" canvas.
   - **Counsel confirms decision 4 before any of these documents is sent to an LP.** Nothing here sends a document.

### Part 2 (#1717) — the template bodies take the legal name, in a new version

A template body changes only in a new version, so a document already signed can still be reproduced exactly as it was signed (`templates/legal/README.md`, "Rules"). Part 2 versions every body that printed "Axal VC Fund I, LP".

**Decisions.**

1. **The store gets new versions, never an in-place edit.** Migration 469 handles every active `legal_templates` row whose `body_md` still holds the old name, as the template editor does:
   - it copies the row as it stands into `legal_template_versions`;
   - it replaces each "Axal VC Fund I, LP" with "Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP";
   - it adds 1 to `version` and sets `updated_at`.

   Both statements re-check the old text, so a second run changes nothing and an admin's later edit is never overwritten. Inactive rows keep what they had. `created_by` and `updated_by` stay as they were, since no person made the change.
2. **The files get new versions.** Each file that printed the old name has a `_v2` file, and the Terms have `tos_v3.md`. The registry (`services/legalTemplates.ts`) reads the new file under the same key, so every caller and every recorded obligation key stays as it was. The `_v1` files stay, because a signed document names its version.
   - `tos_v3.md` descends from `tos_v1.md`, the file the registry imported (S1's ruling).
   - `tos_v2.md`, which nothing imports, stays as it is and still prints the old name.
3. **Terms and Privacy are versioned like the rest, and no member accepts again.** Re-acceptance is keyed by obligation, not by a template's version or body:
   - `GET /auth/me` reports `terms_acceptance_pending` from `legal_obligations` rows `tos_v1` and `privacy_v1` that are `required` and still `pending` or `in_review` (`routes/auth.ts`);
   - an acceptance is recorded against the same obligation key (`services/trust.ts`, migration 245).

   Nothing reads a template's version or body to decide who must accept, so `tos_v1` and `privacy_v1` move to version 2 in the store, the files to `tos_v3.md` and `privacy_v2.md`, and `TermsPage.jsx` and `PrivacyPage.jsx` take the name in the same change.
4. **Only the name changes.** Every other word of each body is as it was, including any draft-for-counsel banner. A test holds each new file equal to its old one with only the name replaced.
