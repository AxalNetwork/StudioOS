### The overdue card (⚑1 A)

The card makes the Inbox's own read, `GET /api/admin/approvals?age=past` (`adminApprovalsApi.inbox(1, { age: 'past' })`), so Studio's count and the landing's rows come from one read and one criterion, the Worker's `sla = past` (72 h). This relaxes D512 for that one read; the page component itself still reads nothing. `studioOverdue.js` turns the read into a view:

| State | Card says |
|---|---|
| reading | "Reading the approval queue…" |
| failed | Unavailable, "Approvals could not be read. This is not a claim that nothing is overdue. The rest of Studio read normally.", Retry, Open Approvals → |
| overdue, one lane | "1 overdue · over 72 hours", "in Approvals · {scope}", the oldest item (who, what, lane, age, "! Overdue", owner or "Unassigned · No one holds it yet"), **Review overdue in Approvals →**, "Opens Approvals with Age: Over 72 hours in {scope}.", "2 more waiting under 72 hours, not overdue." |
| overdue, several lanes | "3 overdue · over 72 hours in 2 lanes", one line per lane with "Lane: {label} →" to `?age=past&lane={key}`, **Review 3 overdue in Approvals →** |
| a lane did not answer | Partial chip "Partial · 19 of 20 lanes", "{lane} could not be read, so there may be more overdue.", Try again; the figure is a floor ("1+") |
| 0 overdue, some on time | "0 overdue · 2 waiting under 72 hours", "Nothing is overdue." |
| nothing waiting | "0 nothing needs a decision", "✓ Every lane answered", "Nothing is waiting in Approvals for {scope}: all {n} lanes answered with 0 open." — only when no other exception sits beside it |
| suspended | still drawn, with "Suspended · writes are blocked. Reading is never gated. Deciding is blocked until HQ lifts the freeze." |

The scope is `branchLabel(user)` on a branch and `HQ-held accounts · axal.vc` on HQ. A withheld total is a floor, never short; a missing on-time figure claims neither "2 waiting" nor all clear.

### The landing and the way back (⚑2 A; SAD03, SAD04, SAD13)

- Every Studio link into Approvals goes to `/admin/approvals` on both tiers, with router state `{ from: '/studio' }`. Only then does `ApprovalsPage` pass `crumb` ("Studio › Approvals", "‹ Studio" on a phone); a direct visit or a shared link has none.
- The Inbox's `setParams` keeps the router state when the reader removes a chip or refines the view, so the crumb stays for the visit.
- On a branch, the scope chip reads "{branchLabel} · this territory" (`TerritoryChip`) instead of the fixed HQ-held label; HQ keeps the fixed label.
- A filtered read says what it hides: `hiddenByFilter` gives "2 more waiting are hidden by this filter." in the queue's footer and under "Nothing matches these filters.". It is drawn only when the Worker sent both totals and a chip is in force.
- A failed filtered read says "Your filter Age: Over 72 hours is kept." (SAD13 L2).
- **Back.** The action notes the scroll position and its own id in `sessionStorage` (`axal:studio-return`, 30-minute life, every access guarded). When the card next draws on Studio — after browser Back, the crumb or "‹ Studio" — it restores the scroll and focuses "Review overdue in Approvals", or the "Needs a decision" heading when that action is gone because the item was decided meanwhile (SAD20).

### Departures from the canvas

- The canvas's territory header under the h1 (territory, areas, status, "Read 08:10") is not built. The territory and status are already in At a glance's licence line, and the Worker's reply carries no read time to print.
- The on-time line says "2 more waiting under 72 hours, not overdue." without the per-lane ages ("KYC 1 d 6 h · Events 5 h"): the `age=past` read does not carry the items under 72 hours, and a second read for them is not in ⚑1 A.
- "N more under 72 hours are hidden by this filter" reads "N more waiting are hidden by this filter": an item whose age was not recorded is hidden by `age=past` too, so "under 72 hours" would be a claim the read cannot make.
- The all-clear card does not add "Seats are within the licence and no programme gate closes within 24 hours". Those are the glance's figures, which show as their own exception cards when they apply; the card speaks for Approvals only and withholds "nothing needs a decision" whenever such a card is beside it.
- The failed card's retry is the platform's Unreadable button, labelled "Retry", not "Try again".
- At a glance keeps the computed worst-first order (`orderNeedsDecision`, D246) rather than the canvas's fixed Approvals, Seats, Programme, Contracts.
- The empty filtered landing has no separate "‹ Back to Studio" link; the crumb is the way back.
