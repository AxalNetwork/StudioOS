## D627 — Fund performance from attributed positions and recorded marks

Task #1370, slot S18. Migration 417 adds `fund_position_valuations` without
backfilling investment cost as fair value. `portfolio_positions.fund_id` already
records attribution, so no replacement attribution column is added.

### Inputs and formulas

A mark records total position value in integer USD cents, a non-future calendar
date, a source and `recorded_by`. The route appends marks. For each currently
attributed position, the latest mark on or before the UTC read date wins; an
identical valuation date is resolved by the newest record ID. An explicit zero
mark is valid. An absent position or an unmarked position leaves NAV unavailable.

NAV sums investment marks only. There is no recorded fund cash balance or
liability store here. It excludes cash, liabilities, fees and carry and is never
labelled net asset value inclusive of those items. A stale mark remains the latest
recorded mark; it is not projected forward or replaced by cost.

Paid-in means **capital called**, as specified by the owner, not collected
receipts. Numbered call headers are counted once using their integer-cent
amounts and issue dates. Legacy unnumbered LP lines use recorded cents, or round
recorded dollar amounts to cents, and their issue dates. Numbered lines and
receipts do not add a second copy of a call. Paid `fund_distributions` rows are
the LP distributions; pending/failed rows and company exit proceeds are excluded.

- RVPI = investment NAV / capital called.
- DPI = paid distributions / capital called.
- TVPI = (paid distributions + investment NAV) / capital called.
- IRR is XIRR over dated negative calls, dated positive paid distributions and
  terminal investment NAV on the UTC read date, with actual days divided by 365.

The solver scans log(1 + rate) from -20 to 20 and bisects a unique sign-changing
root. Ambiguous, same-day, missing-date, future-flow or unsolvable series have no
IRR. This bounded solver withholds a rate outside its supported interval. It is
not a net-of-fees/carry IRR and the page says “called-capital basis”.

No records means unknown, including a fund with no paid distribution rows.
Recorded zeros remain zeros; ratios need positive called capital. Missing inputs
produce null and a reason for each affected metric. Unsafe cent sums are withheld.
A failed store read refuses the whole response as `Unreadable`; a partial total
never masquerades as the fund total.

### Access and writes

`/api/fund-performance` lists performance, and `/:id` reads one fund. Admins may
read all funds. Investors may read only funds in which they have an LP record,
using the canonical `lpMembershipScope` and legacy-email claim flow; a claimed
row cannot be inherited through its denormalized email. A manager without an LP
record does not get a read exception. Other funds, absent funds and unsupported
roles receive the same 404. The reviewed SQL baseline adds only the canonical
scope fragment, never request text; all values remain bound.

`PUT /positions/:id/fund` changes attribution; `POST /positions/:id/valuations`
appends a mark. Admins or the fund's recorded GP may write through the existing
`requireFundGp` gate, including its institutional-tier and verified active-company
checks. Moving a position requires authority over both old and new fund. An
unassigned position may be taken by its creator acting as the destination's
manager, or by an admin. Detaching a position requires its current fund's gate.

The data write and `activity_logs` actor row commit in one D1 batch. Audit failure
rolls back the data write. Each mark also records its actor directly. Conditional
writes refuse stale attribution with 409 and do not create an audit for a failed
write. There is no runtime schema bootstrap and no hand-created trigger.

### Screen and canvas

`/funds/performance` retains its existing workspace tab. I6 in
`design/incoming/Investor LP Canvas.dc.html` supplies the fund summary, multiples
and investment NAV. The page repeats the summary for each accessible fund, with
loading, Not recorded, Unreadable/retry and recorded figures. The source reasons
are shown alongside missing figures and on their titles. Recorded cents remain
visible to the penny. Status filters remain.

The canvas includes cash within NAV and a GP back office; the task's narrower LP
read policy and the absence of fund cash override those claims. No unsupported
cash-inclusive NAV, family blend, chart, net IRR or invented fee adjustment is
shown. Valuation and attribution writes are API operations; this task adds no
unapproved editor layout. Legacy analytics used by accounting retain their
existing behavior until D628. Owner handles merge, migrations and deployment;
this task does not access production user data or verify a production deploy.
