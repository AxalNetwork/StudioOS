## D5031 — HQ Support: HQ's desk does not wait for a branch (#5031)

- **Status:** Built, 2026-11-08 (Worker and frontend). HQ Support audit 2 of 6.
- **Builds on:** D204, D485 (the `openTickets` fan-out), D717 (the branch deadline), D150 (`withRegistry`).

### What was slow

`GET /api/admin/hq-support` read HQ's stores, then awaited `fanOut(..., 'openTickets')`, then read the escalation board. A branch at its 2-second deadline added 2 seconds to the HQ queues.

### What changed

- **`GET /api/admin/hq-support/local`** answers HQ's own desk and asks no branch: the escalation board, HQ's ticket queues, the mirror strip, the SLA policy and the taxonomy. When a branch is deployed, its total is `null` with a reason, because those branches' administrators' tickets are part of the total.
- **`GET /api/admin/hq-support`** keeps its payload. Its reads, including the escalation board, now run in one `Promise.all` together with the fan-out.
- **The page** calls both at once (`mergeSupport`):
  - HQ's queues are drawn when the local read lands.
  - The branch section shows "Asking each branch host…" until the full read lands.
  - A failed full read costs only the branch section and the matrix. Their retry asks the branches again without clearing HQ's queues.
  - The page reads as failed only when both reads fail.
  - When a quiet refresh makes the local read newer than the full one, the local read's queues and total win. The older full total is never shown beside newer queues.
- **Per-source states are unchanged.** Unreadable stays unreadable, not deployed stays not deployed, and a cut count stays a cut count. An unreadable deployment registry now withholds the full total too, rather than summing only the branches that answered.

### Verified

`hq_support_audit_d5030.test.ts`:

- `/local` with a hanging branch binding: no branch call is made, and the total is withheld while deployed but measured when nothing is deployed;
- the full read with one hung and one healthy branch: it answers within the deadline, keeps HQ's queues, names the unreadable branch, and withholds the total;
- the unreadable registry.

`hq_support_audit_d5030.test.mjs` drives `mergeSupport` in every arrival order and the branch section's states.
