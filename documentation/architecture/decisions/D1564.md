## D1564 — The web card view, and a share link published as a page

- **Status:** built by S25 from S1's revised specification on #3811 (deck builder v2, Phase D, D3), after the owner answered decisions A, E and T on 2026-11-01 (recorded on #3716).
- **Builds on:**
  - D902's share link, its NDA gate and what a link includes (`include_json`, migration 567);
  - D1565's one link per investor, and ShareDialog (Share ▾ › Share link…);
  - D1112's `BlockView`, D1546's compliance chrome;
  - D1439 Part D (#3916): a token share link previews as the static private card.
- **Migration:** none.

### The question

The investor's reader at `/deck/share/:token` (`PitchDeckPrintPage`) draws the 16:9 slides only. #3811 asked for a read-only responsive card view and a publishable page, refused for 506(b) rounds, with `/embed/:token` for allowed origins and `og:image` per link. No final board draws it. The owner's answers:

- **A = B.** 16:9 stays canonical, plus a read-only responsive card view.
- **E = B.** A link can be published, with the email gate on by default, and a 506(b) round is refused.
- **T = counsel's wording.** Until it lands the compliance footnote stays on and public views stay off.

The instruction: build the card view and the gated publish path now, with public publishing switched off behind T, and no migration.

### What was decided

**The card view** (`frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/DeckCardView.jsx`, `cardViewModel.js`)
- The version's cards, one under another, each block in reading order (row, then column), drawn through `BlockView` live with sources off.
- No "Not recorded". A block cut for missing data stays out: a metric with no figure, an image with no picture, a video, audio or link with no https address, an empty table, a chart with no point, and a market-circles or projection chart whose numbers make no shape. A card left with nothing stays out. A fielded or template card is read through Present's `cardOf`, with its unfilled parts left out.
- No source or grade badge. Blocks go out with no binding, a chart series is named by its label (never its raw source key), and a bound table cell keeps only its own words, because `cellText` would draw its source key.
- The compliance chrome stays (T): each card's footnotes under it, keyed to its place in the version, and Confidential. The recipient watermark too.
- Built for 390 px: one column, a 16 px gutter, a 720 px cap that only binds on wider screens, pictures and charts in a scaling 16:9 box, tables scrolling inside their card.
- The reader offers it with a Cards / Slides switch (a 44 px target) in its header. It opens on cards on a screen up to 640 px wide, or with `?view=cards`. `/embed/:token` (`DeckEmbedPage.jsx`) draws only the card view.

**The publish state** (`cloudflare-worker/src/services/decks/publish.ts`, pure)
- It rides the link's own `include_json` under a `publish` key: `{ on, gate, exemption, embed_origins, at }`. `parseShareInclude` reads the four switches by name, so it never sees it, and nothing else writes `include_json` after the link is made. That is why there is no migration.
- `publishOf` reads it. A state whose exemption is not 506(c) never reads as published, whatever wrote it.

**The 506(b) refusal.** No table records a round's Reg D exemption: `raise_rounds` has no such column, and grep 506 finds only the accreditation templates. With "Migration: none", the founder states the exemption when publishing.
- 506(b): 409 `exemption_506b`, "A 506(b) round cannot be published: Rule 506(b) allows no general solicitation. Share it one investor at a time." Nothing is written.
- Unstated: 422 `exemption_missing`.
- 506(c) publishes.

**Public views are off until T** (`PUBLIC_VIEWS_ON = false`, one line to change):
- an un-gated publish is refused (409 `public_views_off`);
- the share read (`decks.ts` `GET /share/:token`) answers a published link with `{ gate: 'email', project_name, title, frozen }` and no card, and uses no view, until this browser has given an email on this link. A stored gate of `none` is still gated while T is open (`gateFor`);
- no link previews as itself: `serveDeckShareCard` answers null and the static private card of #3916 stays.

**The email gate** (`POST /api/decks/share/:token/email`)
- It takes `{ email }` for a live, published link, and records it as a `deck_share_conversions` row of type `email_gate`, with `ref_id` holding the hash of the browser's gate cookie and the address.
- It sets `axal_deck_gate` (HttpOnly, Secure, `SameSite=None; Partitioned`, so it also works inside a listed site's frame). The cookie is the gate's alone, never the forwarding cookie.
- The address is never logged.

**`/embed/:token`** (`serveDeckEmbed`, called by `index.ts` before Hono; `/embed/*` added to both `run_worker_first` lists)
- It answers the SPA shell only to a frame (`Sec-Fetch-Dest: iframe`) whose page's origin (its `Referer`) is one the founder listed on a published, live link: https origins only, no wildcard, at most five.
- The answer carries `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors <those origins>` and no `X-Frame-Options`, `noindex` and `no-store`.
- Anything else gets a plain 403 or 404 with `X-Frame-Options: DENY`. `frontend/public/_headers` keeps `X-Frame-Options: DENY` for every other path.

**`og:image` per link** (`serveDeckShareCard`): a published link's card names the venture and the deck, with its first card's own picture (`linkOgImage`, https only), else the site's card. It is built and tested, and switched off with public views.

**The publish control** (`frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/PublishPanel.jsx`) lives only in ShareDialog, under the link just made, never in the header.
- It says public pages are off until counsel's wording.
- The exemption has no default; 506(b) shows why it cannot publish and Publish stays off.
- The email gate is on and fixed while T is open.
- The sites that may embed it are optional; once published, it shows the iframe snippet and Unpublish.
- It writes only on Publish, through `deckPublishApi` (`frontend/src/lib/api/deckPublish.js`). The reader's `EmailGate` lives in `ShareViewerParts.jsx`.

**The routes** (`routes/deck_publish.ts`, mounted at `/api/decks` before `decks`; `projectOwned`, writes with `write: true`):

| Route | Answer |
|---|---|
| `GET /:id/investor-links/:shareId/publish` | `{ link_id, publish, gate, public_views, note }` |
| `PUT /:id/investor-links/:shareId/publish` | `{ on: true, exemption: '506c', gate?, embed_origins? }` or `{ on: false }`; the same answer. 409 `exemption_506b`, `public_views_off`, `link_closed`; 422 `exemption_missing`, `gate_invalid`, `embed_origins_invalid` |
| `POST /share/:token/email` | `{ ok }` and the gate cookie; 404 `not_published`, 422 `email_invalid` |

### Departures

- **The exemption is stated per link, not read from the round.** No record of it exists, and a column on `raise_rounds` is a migration, which the owner ruled out for this task. A founder can therefore state 506(c) for a round that is really 506(b); the refusal holds only against what is stated. The right home is a `raise_rounds.exemption` column, read by `publishOf`'s caller before the stated one. That is a follow-up task with a migration.
- **Publishing changes nothing else about the link.** Its expiry, view cap, forwarding, NDA and include switches all still apply, so a link meant as a page is made with No cap and Forwarding on. A forwarding-off link cannot be embedded, because no browser is bound to a frame.
- **The addresses the gate records are stored, not yet shown.** No surface lists them; the venture's list of readers is a follow-up.
- **No adapter method.** `deckV2Adapter.js` lists no #3811 surface, so nothing was swapped there and no contract was added. The panel and the gate call `deckPublishApi` directly, as `ExportDialog.jsx` calls its own client.
- **The Projection label on a projected chart stays in the card view.** It is the forward-looking label T keeps on, not a source badge.

### Unverified

- **The 390 px check is static.** It reads the rendered markup's classes and inline styles and answers the reader's media query at 390 and 1280. No browser laid the page out. A real 390 px screenshot of the card view, the email gate and the reader's header is still owed.
- **The embed's frame-ancestors answer has not been seen by a browser.** The answer depends on the `Referer` a framed request carries. Under `strict-origin-when-cross-origin` that is the embedding page's origin, but a site whose own policy is `no-referrer` sends none and is refused. The headers are tested; a live embed on a listed origin is not.
- **The `Partitioned` gate cookie inside a third-party frame** is tested for its attributes only.
