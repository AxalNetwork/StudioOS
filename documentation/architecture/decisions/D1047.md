### 1. APR15: the phone at 390

**The top bar.** The canvas's phone top bar (menu, "Approvals", the Eadwyn button) is drawn by what every admin page shares, and none of it changed here:
- the app's phone bar draws the menu;
- the page header (`AdminPageHeader`, D936) draws "Approvals";
- the rail (`WorkerRail`, through `AdminBoardFrame`) draws its Eadwyn button on a phone.

**Tiles and charts.**
- The tiles are two by two below `lg`, with a tighter gap below `sm`.
- The charts are full width below `lg`, each with View as table. The toggle is 44px tall on a phone.

**Filters.**
- Below `sm` the filter strip is hidden. In its place, two buttons:
  - "Filters · [n]", where n counts search, group, lane, age and assigned, not the order (`filterCount`);
  - "Sort: [order] ▾".
- Either button opens one bottom sheet, "Filters and sort" (`Lightbox phone="sheet"`), with:
  - Search;
  - Group with its counts;
  - Lane (the twenty, and HQ's for a Super Admin);
  - Age;
  - Assigned;
  - Sort.
- Each choice lands in the address as it is made, as on desktop, and the count follows.
- Clear empties the address. "Show [total]" closes the sheet.

**Rows as cards.**
- Below `md` the table is hidden, and each item is a card with:
  - the lane eyebrow;
  - the name, linking to the item's address;
  - the one line;
  - the age pill: the age with its band, e.g. "4 d 7 h · Over 72 hours", from `cardAge`. An unknown age reads "Age not recorded" with no band;
  - "Decided on [page] ›".
- The card's ⋯ is 44 by 44 and opens a bottom sheet with Open, Assign… and Copy link, each 44px tall.
- Selecting several items for a bulk Assign stays on the table, from `md` up.

**Drawers.**
- Each drawer (the item, a tester, a pitch, an expert) is already full screen on a phone (the shared `Lightbox`).
- Each now starts with "‹ [where it came from]" ("‹ Approvals", "‹ Product testers", "‹ Co-marketing", "‹ Wellbeing experts"). It is 44px tall, hidden from `sm` up, and closes to the board it came from.
- Drawer and dialog buttons share `FOOT`, full width on a phone: each decision dialog, Assign, and each drawer's actions.

**Tap targets.** Every button, chip and select on the page and the boards is at least 44px tall below `sm` and keeps its desktop height from `sm` up. The card's ⋯ is 44 by 44.
