## D1050 — Checkout part 2: one plan catalogue, and every typed price removed with a parity test (#2901)

- **Status:** built by S10 in slot S06, 2026-10-25 (#2901, part 2 of #2451, under D1048).
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `c64a064b9c`.
- **Map:** `documentation/audits/CHECKOUT_MAP_2026-10-25.md` §1, item 3. Prices were typed in four places, and they disagreed with the owner and with each other.
- **Migration:** none.

### 1. The decision

There is one plan catalogue, `cloudflare-worker/src/services/planCatalogue.ts`. Every surface that names a plan is held to it: Pricing's cards, the paywall, the checkout sheet, Billing, the door pages and the upsell a 402 carries.

**No price is in the catalogue.** A price is Stripe's, mirrored in D1 (`stripe_products`) and read through the Worker on `GET /api/public/plans-and-packs`. No file that draws a plan types a dollar amount, and the parity test fails on one.

### 2. The catalogue

One entry per plan key: `founder:free|growth|studio`, `investor:free|plus|professional|institutional`, `advisor:free|pro`, `partner:free|pro`, `other`, `lp_access` and `admin`.

| Field | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `family`, `name`, `tagline` | The Pricing canvas's words (`frontend/src/lib/sitePricing.js` `PLAN_CARDS`, and `LP_ACCESS`). |
| `colour` | The profile colour on the plan pill: Founder violet, Investor sky, Advisor amber, Operating Partner emerald, the rest muted. These are D1048's sheet colours. |
| `licence_key`, `matches` | The Stripe product metadata the plan is sold under, as `planOf` (`services/aiCredits.ts`) reads it. Null for a plan with no product. |
| `sellable`, `sellable_reason` | Free plans, LP access, staff and "no plan yet" are not sold. **Investor Plus is not sold (D786)**, and no product maps to it. Institutional reads "Priced by our sales team" because Pricing shows it as Contact Sales (§5). |
| `storage`, `best_suited_for` | #2449's. Neither is decided yet, so each is null with its reason, never a figure. |
| `included` | The plan's lines: the Pricing card's own. |

### 3. What changed

- **The plans read** (`plansAndPacks`) now carries `catalogue` on each plan row, and the whole catalogue on the body, plans with no product among them. The plan's name goes out as `label`, because the public read carries no `name` key (D755's rule).
- **The checkout sheet** (`checkoutModel.includedLines`) draws "Included" from `plan.catalogue.included`, and storage from `plan.catalogue.storage`. Its reason shows until #2449 sets a figure. A read from before the catalogue falls back to the card's lines.
- **The paywall** (`PaywallModal.jsx`) no longer types a price.
  - `TIER_PLANS` has the catalogue's key, name, line and lines. Investor Pro is the paywall's "Professional" renamed, as Pricing names it.
  - The card's price is read (`lib/planPrices.js`). It says "Reading…" while the read runs, and "Price not read" with "That doesn't mean it's free." when there is none, never a zero.
  - Raise + Grow leads with "Everything in Validate + Build, plus:", as Pricing's card does (D683).
- **The 402 upsells** (`requireTier.ts`, `requireInvestorTier.ts`) no longer carry a typed `price_label`.
  - They name the plan by its catalogue key (`plan_key`) and its catalogue name.
  - No page drew `price_label`; the archetype lock names the plan from `name` and `tier` (D594).
  - **Why the price was not read from the mirror here:** about fifteen callers build these bodies synchronously. A price in the body would mean a D1 read on every refusal. The client already reads the price from the plans read.
- **Billing** (`SettingsPage.jsx`):
  - the founder ladder, the investor ladder with its monthly and yearly cycle, and the advisor and partner plans all read their prices through `priceText`;
  - Starter is "Free", and Enterprise stays "Custom";
  - the investor plan names are the catalogue's.
- **The door pages** (`/for-founders`, `/for-investors`, `pages/site/DoorPage.jsx`) took their prices from the paywall's typed figures, through `data/pricing.js`.
  - They now price each tier from the plans read with `pricedTier` (`lib/plansPricing.js`), and name each plan as the catalogue does.
  - `data/pricing.js` types no price: a paid tier's `priceMonthly` and `priceAnnual` are null until priced, and its typed yearly figures are gone.
  - The page built ahead of time shows "Reading…" until the read answers in the browser.

### 4. The parity test

`frontend/test/plan_catalogue_d1050.test.mjs` (12 tests) holds every surface to the catalogue:
- **The catalogue itself:**
  - the fourteen keys, with no price and no typed amount;
  - each family's colour equal to the sheet's pill colours;
  - a reason exactly when a plan is not sold, and Investor Plus not sold;
  - storage and "Best suited for" null with the reason;
  - each sold plan's metadata mapping to its licence key through `planOf`.
- **The surfaces:**
  - Pricing's eleven catalogue cards, and LP Access: name, line and every included line;
  - the paywall's four plans: name, line and features, and no price field;
  - Billing reading its prices and naming plans from the table;
  - the sheet's Included lines and storage from the catalogue.
- **No typed amount:** none of the files that draw a plan types a non-zero dollar amount. Funding-stage sizes such as "$1M" are not prices.
- **The read:**
  - each row's `catalogue`, the whole catalogue, and no `name` key;
  - a price by state: reading, priced, or not read, never a zero, and never another currency quoted in dollars;
  - the door pages' prices by state;
  - the paywall card drawn in each state.

### 5. Found, and left

These were found while building and left alone, because the issue names other files:
- **`frontend/src/data/productPages.js`** types `$29` and `$99` on the advisor and partner door plans. DoorPage reads only that file's `meta`, so they are not drawn.
- **`frontend/src/pages/InvestorPricingPage.jsx`** types the investor prices. It is not routed.
- **`cloudflare-worker/src/middleware/miAccess.ts`** types the Market Intelligence price labels (`$49 / month`, `$490 / year`). Market Intelligence is not in #2901's list.
- **Institutional:** Pricing shows it as Contact Sales, while Billing's investor ladder still starts a checkout for it. Which is right is the owner's call. The catalogue follows Pricing.
