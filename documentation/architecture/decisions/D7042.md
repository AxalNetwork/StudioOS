### What the canvas decides

- The Saved analyses list shows only what the list read returns: title, mode, run state, the edited flag and dates. Loading, a true empty list, Unreadable with Try again and an analysis not on your list are drawn.
- The dossier labels four kinds of content: Generated by the run, Yours (title, conclusion, notes), Attached by you and Fetched. A conclusion field is new.
- Save states are Saved, Unsaved changes, Saving and Save failed. After a failed save the draft stays editable, with Retry save and Copy my changes.
- Refresh sources and Rerun discovery are explained side by side before either is pressed. Refresh re-checks the stored sources only, keeps the candidates and the unsaved title, and dates any source it could not fetch again.
- Rerun opens a review against the saved version (additions, removals, rewrites). Your work is listed apart and is not part of the review. Cancel returns to the exact draft; Accept saves a new version. A failed rerun replaces nothing.
- Export is of the saved version. With a dirty draft, the reader chooses, and Save, then export is the default. A failed export (a 503, or a body that is not Markdown) keeps the dossier open with Retry and gives no file.
- Tablet 768, six phone boards at 390 with an Actions sheet, and dark are drawn, with focus return and live regions.
