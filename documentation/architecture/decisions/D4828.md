## D4828 — Expertise UX: shared states, one feedback pattern, phone sections and accessibility checks (#4828)

- **Status:** part built 2026-11-17 (Part of #4828). Boards EX-04a, EX-04b, EX-14 and EX-16 of the Expertise v2 canvas (D4823, `design/incoming/Expertise v2 Page.dc.html`). Frontend only.
- **Builds on:** D5129 and D5129 part 2 (the shared state components), D4697 (each section reads and retries on its own), D4711 (announced saves, managed focus), D4674 (limits refused, never cut), #5363 (the licence refusal).
- **No migration, no Worker route, no client method.** The rail is #5130's and is not touched here.

### What was built

1. **States on #5129's components (EX-14).**
   - `ZoneBody` takes `what` ("Your services"). A zone that passes it draws `SectionState` (`pages/advisor/expertise/sectionStates.jsx`): loading is the shared skeleton plus the word ("Loading your services…"); a failed read is Unavailable with the zone's fixed sentence, "Other sections are unaffected" and its own Try again, announced as an alert; the Advisor licence refusal is Refused, with "Ask HQ to add it" and no Try again. All five zones pass it. Every other zone on the kit is unchanged.
   - Visibility's per-block failures lead with the Unavailable chip, keeping "could not be read · Try again".
   - The Overview board sets `states: 'platform'` and `refusals` (`workspaces/boards/advisorExpertise.js`). `BucketBoard` then draws each section's loading, Unavailable and Refused states with `BoardSectionState` (`workspaces/BoardSectionStates.jsx`). `useBucketSources` carries the refusal's code beside its fixed sentence, so a refusal is told from a failure. A slow source keeps only its own section loading (D4697, now pinned on the Expertise board).
2. **One feedback pattern (EX-14).** `InlineSaveStatus` is the one save status; `SaveNote` is the same component under its shipped name, so every importer gets it. A tick or a cross leads the sentence, so saved and not saved differ by shape as well as colour. Profile says "Profile updated." on a save and "Not saved, your text is kept. …" on a failure (the form keeps what was typed). Strings live in `feedback.js`.
3. **Field limits (EX-16).** `Field` takes `limit`: a live counter ("12 of 200 characters") tied to the control by `aria-describedby`; over the limit the control is `aria-invalid` and the counter says by how much, in words and with a cross, announced politely. The `maxLength` attributes are gone from Profile and Services, so a paste is never cut; a value over its limit disables Save, shows "Not saved, your text is kept. Headline is over the limit." and sends nothing. Counting matches the Worker (trimmed length).
4. **Phone and tablet (EX-04a, EX-04b).** The Expertise bucket declares its section row in `shellConfig.js` (`row: { overview, scroll, more: ['thinking', 'visibility'] }`), and `ZoneNav` reads a bucket's own row when the shell passes none. Below 1024px the row is Overview · Profile · Services · Proof · More ▾ (the canvas's `mtabs`), each 44px; More opens Thinking and Visibility as 44px rows in the page's flow (`ZoneNavMore.jsx`) and names the folded section that is current. On the Overview, rows are blocks below 768px instead of a sideways table. Proof cards sit two a row at 768px. Profile and Services forms are one column on a phone with full-width primary buttons.
5. **Accessibility details (EX-16).** The kit's buttons have a visible focus outline and a 44px target below 1024px; the completeness meter has `motion-reduce:transition-none`; Proof's destructive button and Visibility's window buttons are 44px on a phone.

### Already in place

- Each section loads, fails and retries on its own (D4697), and a failed read is never an empty list (D4689, D4711).
- Saves were live regions mounted empty, failures alerts (D4711); focus moves into a form and back to its opener on Escape (D4711).
- The destructive confirmation names the third-party data that goes with a claim: "It also deletes the consent record behind it (name): names, emails and statements. … It cannot be undone." (D4681). Its words differ slightly from EX-14's sample ("Its 1 confirmation request goes too, including R. Haddad's name and email") and were kept.
- Not recorded is the shared chip (D5129 folded the kit's copy).

### Left, and why

- **A full-width "Next step" on the phone Overview (EX-04b).** It needs the NextActionCard of the Overview (EX-01, #4823), which is not built; nothing here invents a step.
- **The Overview's ExpertiseStatus cells and the four status dimensions as their own strip** (Completion, Publication, Verification, Availability): they belong to the EX-01 overview and wait on #4823. The zones keep the words they already draw for those dimensions (Active/Inactive, Self-stated/Confirmed, the eight editorial states).
- **Permission denied and Unavailable for an admin preview with no advisor selected (EX-03):** the preview route is #4823's.
- **Playwright visual or axe checks.** The repo has no `tests/visual/` and its `tests/e2e/` specs do not run axe, so the checks are render tests (`frontend/test/expertise_ux_4828.test.mjs`). An axe pass at 390, 768 and 1440 is a follow-up.
- **Code entry as six inputs with paste (EX-16)** is on the attestation page (EX-10, #4825), not on these zones.
