## D532 — The owner's answer settles a pending question, and a screen with no canvas waits for one

**Two rules joined the task protocol in `AGENTS.md`: every queue check reads
the owner's answers on the slot's own waiting issues, and a screen that no
canvas covers is designed in Claude Design before anyone builds it.** Issue
#1075, slot S01, at the owner's request on 2026-10-04. No migration, no route,
no `frontend/src` change.

**Owner answers.** The owner answered #973 (D500) with a plain "A" at 09:53Z.
Nothing acted on it until 11:15Z, after the owner pointed at it. No rule
told a slot to read its own `needs-decision` issues, or said what an answer
looks like. Session 1, the slots and some other agents all write as the owner
account, so the author alone does not mark an answer. `AGENTS.md` now says:
- An answer is a comment that does not start with `S1:` and carries no STATUS
  block.
- Every queue check reads the new comments on the slot's own issues labelled
  `needs-decision` or `state:blocked`.
- An answer settles the question: the slot removes `needs-decision`, sets
  `state:in-progress`, posts a STATUS saying what the owner chose, and
  finishes the task.

Session 1 relays each answer it finds as an `S1:` comment, for an agent that
does not check. Step 4 adds two practices that #992 needed:
- A question comes as lettered options with the slot's pick, so one letter
  answers it.
- A part that waits doesn't hold the rest: that PR says `Part of #<issue>`,
  and the issue stays open.

**Missing UI.** Nothing sent a screen that no canvas covers back to design,
so an agent building one improvised it. The owner's report on the Research
pages on 2026-10-04 is why this changed: the company, market and fund screens
shipped without parts such pages need, among them search, geography and
charts. The flow is now:
- The task takes the new label `needs-design`. The slot describes the screen:
  its route, its audience, what each part shows and from which store, and its
  empty, `Not recorded` and `Unreadable` states.
- The slot builds what doesn't depend on the layout.
- Session 1 makes the Claude Design canvas from what the platform records, and
  links it on the issue.
- Once the owner approves the canvas, Session 1 commits its export under
  `design/incoming/`, the intake folder `design/incoming/README.md` already
  describes, and sets `state:ready`.
- The screen is built to the canvas, at desktop and phone widths, and the PR
  says where it departs.

The label is declared in `.github/labels.yml`, so `labels-sync.yml` creates
it on the merge.

**Verified.** `agents_owner_answers_d532.test.mjs` pins the label's entry, its
row in the label table, both step-4 practices, the two sections' operative
sentences, and their place inside the task protocol. Each assertion was
mutation-checked both ways.
