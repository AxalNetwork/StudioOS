### One reading of the status record

`/status` and `/trust` already read the same `GET /api/public/status`, but each summarised it
on its own: Trust turned any down probe into "A major outage is reported" with nothing else,
while Status named the affected service. `statusSummary()` is now the one reading both draw:

- the headline in `/status`'s words (`All monitored services operational`, `Degraded`, `Outage`,
  `Unknown`);
- each affected function with its impact (an inventory-only row is never affected);
- the newest unresolved incident, linked at `/status#incident-<id>`;
- that incident's newest update, as `16 Nov 2026, 09:40 UTC`.

A probe outage with no incident on record reads "No incident has been recorded for this yet." and
"Last update: Not recorded". The time the page was read is never passed off as an update. No line
is a percentage (D1072). The strip's "Days with no incident reported" is unchanged: it is counted
from recorded incidents and the days since records began (D1076).

### A tick needs a check date

D1072 asks every line to show when it was checked. A recorded line now carries a tick only when it
has a recorded check date (`lineMark`). Without one it reads Not recorded with "No check date is
recorded for this yet.", and the page's own fallback lines (which carry no date) therefore show no
tick. Entering owners and check dates is the owner's, through the trust editor (D1079).
