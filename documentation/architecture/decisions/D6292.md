## D6292 — Reachable and shareable Diligence cases (#6292)

The owner chose to always show Diligence to eligible investors, including
before their first case. Both sidebar insertion and the involvement hook
implement that rule without needing a successful case-list read. Partners
and advisors retain their involvement rule; other roles are unchanged. Hook
answers stay scoped to their account and role.

The empty desk names a deal's Diligence tab as a place to open a case and links
to Deals, retaining its own Open a case dialog. An unscoped case Overview
links recorded deal and project subject ids to the existing member routes,
and a recorded deal's Documents tab as the data-room door. Ids must be
positive safe integers. Missing ids remain Not recorded; a scoped review
never exposes these context links. No /admin address, new grant or API read.
Current /deals/:id and its Documents address remain valid under #5903's
requirement to retain old tab addresses.

Cases state comes from the address: filters, stage, search, sort, page and
page size. Parsing accepts the existing controls' allowed values; writes keep
unrelated query parameters and the hash. Controls reset paging together with
their filter change. Debounced search replaces its current history entry;
other controls preserve browser-back navigation. Out-of-range pages recover
to the actual last page. Responses from an older address cannot overwrite the
current list, including after back or refresh.

Regressions exercise a shared-address reload, controls, search, history,
stale responses, page recovery, empty-state door, recorded/missing/scoped
context links and the sidebar's role/cache behavior. The old D1252 investor
expectations change only to reflect the owner's new choice; partner/advisor
positive and negative behavior stays covered. Mutation and complete validation
results accompany the batch review. Roll back by revert; no schema, Worker,
permission or deployment configuration changes.
