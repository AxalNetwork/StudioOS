### What was decided

1. **One row per vehicle, keyed by manager plus fund number.** Migration `202611180052_7166_research_fund_directory_vehicles.sql`, written by `scripts/import-research-funds.py eurotech-migration`, replaces the EuroTech list's rows and reports. A vehicle's uid is the manager's key, a hyphen and the fund number's key (`dawncapital-v`, `dawncapital-opp`); a row with no fund number keeps the manager's key. The same inputs always give the same uid, so a re-import never moves a vehicle. Dawn Capital Opp (€75m) and Fund V (€582m) are two rows, and each vehicle's reports are its own. 1,076 manager rows became 1,364 vehicle rows.
2. **Old uids move with what pointed at them.** Each migration-378 uid that no longer names a row is recorded in `research_fund_directory_uid_moves` (old uid, new uid, date). It maps to the vehicle 378's row showed: the fund number on the manager's first sheet row, which 378 never updated. The same migration moves `research_funds.catalogue_uid`, `raise_prospects.research_fund_uid` and fund `research_project_subjects.directory_uid` through that table. `GET /api/research/fund-directory/:uid` with a retired uid answers the vehicle it moved to, with `moved_from`.
3. **Sizes fixed.** On the sheet's newer rows the exact-euro column holds the shorthand ("€ 121m"); 378 read it as full euros, a millionth of the size. The importer reads a value ending in "m" as millions. D7031 counted 232 rows under €1m: the 229 from 2025–2026 were this misreading and now hold the listed size (Angular Ventures III is €121m). The other three are 2020 rows the sheet itself records as €0m, kept as the sheet wrote them.
4. **Each list in its own currency.** `LIST_CURRENCY` marks EuroTech as EUR and the Shai Goldman list as USD (migration 429 stores US cents). Rows and reports carry `size_currency`, and coverage adds `size_usd`. `size_currency` (EUR by default, or USD) picks the one currency size_min/max, size_band, the size sort and the size facet use; every other match is left out of them and counted in `size_not_comparable`. Amounts are never converted.
5. **The route's `source` names the lists served**, from the answer's own `sources`, so a list withheld under D597 is never named as a source.
6. **Return after sign-in.** `/research/funds` is in `RETURN_AFTER_LOGIN_PREFIXES`, and a Funds entry in the destination areas makes the sign-in page read "Sign in to continue to Funds". A link longer than the 200-character cap returns to the Directory without its filters, and the page says the filters could not be kept (option A as drawn).
7. **The Directory, results first.** Search fund vehicles or managers, the filters, sort, the active chips with Clear all, then "N fund vehicles match" beside "of N in the directory, unfiltered", announced politely. Coverage, the lists and "Where the money is" sit in a closed disclosure below the results. A row starts with the vehicle (from `fund_number`) and its manager, then the size with its currency code. The size filter is From and To in millions of one currency, on the existing `size_min` and `size_max`, and says how many funds it leaves out. On a phone the rows are cards and nothing scrolls sideways. "Add to my funds" is renamed Save to shortlist. A link opened with filters says so. Open full profile carries the query, and Back to results returns to it and focuses the row. An empty My funds offers Browse fund directory and Add a fund by hand, with no row of zero counters. Investment geography reads Not recorded: no list records it.
