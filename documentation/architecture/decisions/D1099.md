### 1. The facts

`projectFacts(project, installation)`:
- **The account:** the installation's `teamId` decides it. With none, it is a personal Vercel account; with one, a Vercel team.
- **The plan:**
  - the project's `plan` from `GET /v9/projects/{id}`, when it is one of hobby, pro or enterprise;
  - else Hobby for a personal account, which is Vercel's rule (Hobby is the personal plan);
  - else `null`, drawn as "Not recorded: Vercel did not give this team's plan." It is never guessed, and nothing is proposed.

`plan_source` keeps which rule gave the plan (`project`, `personal_account`, `none`).

### 2. The proposal

`syncVercelCompany(env, integrationId, projectId, client, vercelProjectId, installation)` makes one read, `client.project(id)`, then one batch:
- it upserts the facts;
- **on Hobby**, it proposes "Move the production project to the company's Vercel team on a paid plan", with Vercel's reason ("Vercel's Hobby plan is for personal, non-commercial use."). It does this once per connection, venture, project and kind (`UNIQUE(integration_id, project_id, vercel_project_id, kind)`, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`), so a decided proposal never comes back;
- **off Hobby**, it withdraws a pending proposal.

A Pro or Enterprise team proposes nothing.

**Accept** (`POST /api/cycles/build/company-creation/vercel-proposals/:id/accept`, `canWrite`):
1. It claims the proposal: pending → accepted, once.
2. One D1 batch then writes:
   - the `formation_steps` row (`todo`, kind `other`, at the end);
   - the step's id on the proposal;
   - the ledger row (`connector_fed_items`, `'company_creation'`, `'hosting_step'`, `hosting_step:<project>:<vercel project>`);
   - the activity line `build_hosting_step_accepted`.
3. A failed batch hands the claim back to pending.

A decided proposal answers 409, and an unknown one 404. Nothing is sent to Vercel.

**Dismiss** (`…/dismiss`) is pending → dismissed, once, with its activity line. A re-sync never brings it back.

**Disconnect.** `deleteVercelCompanyOf` deletes the connection's facts and its pending proposals. Decided proposals stay as history. The facts also cascade with the `integrations` row.

### 3. The page

- `parts.vercel` on Company creation's module read is `companyFromVercel`. A venture member reads each project's account, plan and name, and the pending proposals. Anyone else reads only how many projects arrived.
- Its own failure reads "What Vercel brought could not be read.", and the module and GitHub's part stay.
- `CompanyFromVercel.jsx` (`#from-vercel`) draws:
  - **VC4b:** the facts, and the "Proposal · from your Vercel facts" card with **Accept…** and **Dismiss**;
  - **VC4d:** the in-page dialog:
    - "Accept this proposal?";
    - "“Move the production project…” becomes a step in Company creation, for <venture>.";
    - "Adds a formation step. Nothing changes on Vercel.";
    - **Cancel** and **Add the step**.
- A refused Accept says why inside the dialog and keeps it open.
