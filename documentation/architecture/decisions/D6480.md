## D6480 — The landing rules for sessions, rewritten for the landing bot (#6480)

- **Status:** built. Documentation, skills and text-pinning tests only. No workflow, script, variable, label,
  ruleset or queue entry changes. Part of #5600 (plan key D3). T2: the owner lands it.
- **Why:** AGENTS.md forbade what the owner now asks the landing bot to do. D5398 said "Do not press Update branch
  on every PR at once" and "no branch updates" without naming the bot's serial refresh; step 5 said only that the
  owner lands. The bot's workflows (#6474 to #6478, #6481) are on `main`, so a session reading AGENTS.md had no way
  to know what the bot may add to its branch or what its labels ask of it.

### The owner's answers this records

Recorded on #6480 on 2026-11-16, and written into AGENTS.md and `.github/workflows/README.md` as filed:

1. **Re-queue.** The bot may restore an entry the owner queued and no model touched (#6476). Since 2026-11-17 an
   entry the landing App itself made (the feeder's first admission) is consent too, and only the App's exact login
   counts (`consent()` in `scripts/landing-bot/ejection-rules.mjs`). Only a no-model merge of `main` (the keeper's
   `Landing-Bot-Kind: keeper` merge, or GitHub's update-branch merge) keeps consent; any other later commit voids it.
2. **Who lands model-changed PRs.** The owner, as T2 (#6475). The feeder holds any PR with a model or unclassified
   bot commit, and any PR labelled `bot:resolved` or `main-repair`.
3. **Serial refresh.** Yes, one PR at a time at a set rate (#6478). It is the stated exception to D5398's
   no-bulk-refresh rule, with its breakers.

### The limits the text states, as they are on main at `d26a93a25`

- **Switch.** `LANDING_BOT_ENABLED`: `true` acts on every trigger, `dispatch` acts only in a manual run on one named
  PR (main repair: one manual run), anything else, unset included, is off (`mode()` in `rules.mjs`). The feeder also
  needs `LANDING_AUTO` `true`. The conflict indicator has its own switch, `LANDING_CONFLICT_LABELS`, and only labels.
- **Commits.** The bot adds only merges of `main` to a PR branch, pushed normally: the keeper's and the refresh's
  no-model merges, and the resolver's model-resolved merge of the conflicted files. Never a rebase, a force push, a
  push to `main` or a fork, an edit to an existing test or a T2 path (`pathGuard()`), an approval or a merge. Main
  repair pushes only its own `repair/main-*` branch.
- **Per PR.** Two attempts per kind for the PR's life, four bot commits and two model runs in any 24 hours, three
  keeper attempts for good (D6476-p6).
- **Breakers.** Main's newest completed CI push run failed, or `[alert] main is red`, `[alert] the Actions queue is
  backed up` or `[alert] merge queue stalled` is open; the refresh also stops above `LANDING_BOT_MAX_QUEUED_RUNS`
  queued runs. Main repair runs only while main is red and stops on the Actions-queue alert.
- **Main repair** may claim the repair issue with `scripts/claim.mjs`, and steps aside for a session's claim,
  `state:in-progress`, a linked open PR or any open `main-repair` PR. D5398's "exactly one repair PR" holds.

### What sessions keep

Sessions still never merge `main` in bulk, never update another slot's branch, never re-queue an ejected PR, never
approve, merge outside step 5, force-push or use `--admin`, and never change the bot's variables. The cross-vendor
review (AGENTS.md § Review) is advisory: it is not a GitHub approval, and the feeder checks no approval itself.
Any approval the `main` ruleset requires is GitHub's to enforce at queue entry.

A session may add `bot:off` to its own PR, as the bot's own hand-back comment invites (`giveUp()` in `rules.mjs`),
and remove it again; never on another slot's PR.
