### The header ops (RMD-01, RMD-08)

The `research/markets` row in each licence's zone action table is now the directory canvas's, not the older `Pages · …` artboard's, which `profile_zone_actions.test.mjs` records per profile as `withheld` (the artboard's ops) and `offCanvas` (the D7036 ops).

- **Export view** is a page-supplied handler (`exportFilteredView`), not the builder's `kind: 'export'`, because the builder can only write rows already loaded. It writes every market the view holds: the directory panel hands the zone its read with the exact request it made (`onRead`), and `marketDirectoryExport.js` pages `GET /api/research/markets` with that request from offset 0, 100 a read, until the Worker's `total`, in the view's order. Search, country, freshness, source type, sort and the thesis switch's sectors all travel. A value no source holds is written "Not recorded" (as D7037 Q2 A settled for the listed exports), and an investor with a thesis gets a "Your thesis" column. A view past 5,000 markets is refused with a sentence, never cut short. The op is disabled while the read is loading, failed or empty, and on the Listed companies tab (its own exports are #7172's).
- **New market research** opens My Research's existing New research form (`/research/projects?new=1&question=`, D5157) with the market named: the open row's market, else the search. It creates nothing. `/research/projects` admits founders and admins only (`routes/researchProjects.jsx`), so advisor and partner draw it disabled with the reason on hover; opening My Research to them is a shell change for the owner.
- **Edit thesis** opens Settings › My thesis (`/account/security-privacy#my-thesis`), where the investor profile's thesis is edited today. No new page.
- They replace the unbuilt ops (New deep-dive, Cite in deck, Cite in a memo, Attach to proposal, the advisor's unbuilt Re-run stale, Add source) and the old Export, which wrote the 25 rows on screen.

### Phone (RMD-11)

- Below `md` the toolbar is the search, then **Filters · N** and **Sort** side by side. Filters opens one bottom sheet (`MarketDirectoryPhone.jsx`) with Country (the five largest, then "All N countries"; a chosen one stays in view), Source freshness and Source type, each with the Worker's counts. Choices are a draft; **Reset** clears it; **Show N markets** applies it, N being the Worker's `total` for the draft (the page's own request, `limit=1`, read 250 ms after the last change). While that count is being read or cannot be read the button says "Show markets", with no number. Escape, ✕ and the scrim close it and return focus to Filters.
- An investor's thesis is one line that opens: "Your thesis · <profile sectors>" and "N of M markets match · tap to read the thesis"; open, the thesis, how a match is made, "Show only markets that match" and Edit thesis. No thesis and Unavailable keep part 1's words. From `md` the full section is drawn as before.

### Partial (RMD-09B) and the thesis switch

The canvas's Partial was "the sectors read failed"; since part 1 the directory reads no sectors list. The one split read the directory has is the investor's: the markets and the investor profile. So:

- Markets read, profile not: a Partial banner (`PartialBanner`, "1 of 2 sources unavailable · your investor profile") above the results says the Your thesis column and its matches wait for that read, with Retry.
- **A fix part 1 left:** `thesis=1` sent no `sector` while the profile was loading or unreadable, so the whole directory was listed under a "Matches your thesis sectors" chip. Now the list waits while the profile loads; if the profile cannot be read, the chip reads "· not applied", the result line drops the switch, and the banner says the markets shown are those the other filters leave. A profile with no sector the crosswalk maps says "No market can match your thesis sectors" with "Show all markets", never the whole directory.

- Export view on the Listed companies tab is disabled; #7172 (PR #7203) builds that tab's own scoped exports and also a directory export ("Export filtered markets · N rows") over the same `onRead` request. The two should merge into one: the owner's call.
