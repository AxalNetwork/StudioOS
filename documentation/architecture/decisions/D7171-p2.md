### Phone (RMD-11)

- Below `md` the toolbar is the search, then **Filters · N** and **Sort** side by side. Filters opens one bottom sheet (`MarketDirectoryPhone.jsx`) with Country (the five largest, then "All N countries"; a chosen one stays in view), Source freshness and Source type, each with the Worker's counts. Choices are a draft; **Reset** clears it; **Show N markets** applies it, N being the Worker's `total` for the draft (the page's own request, `limit=1`, read 250 ms after the last change). While that count is being read or cannot be read the button says "Show markets", with no number. Escape, ✕ and the scrim close it and return focus to Filters.
- An investor's thesis is one line that opens: "Your thesis · <profile sectors>" and "N of M markets match · tap to read the thesis"; open, the thesis, how a match is made, "Show only markets that match" and Edit thesis. No thesis and Unavailable keep part 1's words. From `md` the full section is drawn as before.

### Partial (RMD-09B) and the thesis switch

The canvas's Partial was "the sectors read failed"; since part 1 the directory reads no sectors list. The one split read the directory has is the investor's: the markets and the investor profile. So:

- Markets read, profile not: a Partial banner (`PartialBanner`, "1 of 2 sources unavailable · your investor profile") above the results says the Your thesis column and its matches wait for that read, with Retry.
- **A fix part 1 left:** `thesis=1` sent no `sector` while the profile was loading or unreadable, so the whole directory was listed under a "Matches your thesis sectors" chip. Now the list waits while the profile loads; if the profile cannot be read, the chip reads "· not applied", the result line drops the switch, and the banner says the markets shown are those the other filters leave. A profile with no sector the crosswalk maps says "No market can match your thesis sectors" with "Show all markets", never the whole directory.
