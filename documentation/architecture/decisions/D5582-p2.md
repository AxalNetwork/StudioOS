### Where a candidate lives, and how it becomes the head


The owner chose narrowed option B on 2026-11-14, after a correction on #5582. A merged candidate lives in its
domain's own draft store, written the way that domain already writes drafts. The WorkGraph revision points at that
row.

| Kind | The candidate | One at a time | Publication (a person's act) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pitch_deck` | one `pitch_deck_proposals` row (D767), `method_id` NULL as `proposeVariant` writes it | one waiting is never replaced (`proposal_waiting`), D1588's rule | the founder's **Keep**, which writes the next current `pitch_decks` version through the existing insert |
| `legal_template` | one `legal_template_research_drafts` row, `pending_counsel_review` (new writer, `legalTemplateDrafts.ts`) | one per template and jurisdiction; a second is refused (`draft_waiting`), never overwritten | counsel's review and the existing publish path |
| `offer` | **not stored**: `quote_versions` has no draft state, so the plan and its digest are returned | n/a | the partner's save of the next version through the existing quote route |

Two notes on these stores:

- A deck's fill and new-deck flows (`routes/decks.ts`, `routes/deck_new.ts`) clear a project's waiting proposals
  when they write their own. A WorkGraph candidate can be replaced that way, by the founder's own act, as every
  proposal already can. Its revision then names a row that no longer exists, and no head ever moves to it.
- The draft writer's explicit "never overwrite" check duplicates the table's `UNIQUE(template_id, jurisdiction)`.
  On a race the insert fails, and the refusal is the same (`draft_waiting`).

**The flow** (`workGraphDrafting.ts`):

1. `registerDraftedAsset` registers a deck, template or offer, always under `human_publication`. Signed evidence,
   composites and media are refused (`not_drafted_kind`).
2. `adoptCurrent`: a **person** names the domain's current row as the head, and that act is the approval. An agent
   gets `person_required`.
3. `proposeCandidate` runs part 1's `planMerge` over the head's units and two drafts, then:
   - refuses, writing nothing, on a stale head (`stale_revision`), a misrouted kind (`kind_mismatch`) or a conflict
     (`merge_conflict`, which returns the plan);
   - on a deck drafted by an agent, refuses a changed text unit that types a figure (`figure_in_text`, D1588 §4.3);
   - otherwise writes the candidate through its domain's draft store and records one revision. Its parent is the
     head, its `SourceRef` names the draft row, its digest is `unitsDigest`, and its recipe names the two
     contributors' runs, ids only.

   **The head does not move.** A retry with the same key, or a draft already waiting with the same digest, is the
   same proposal and writes nothing new.
4. `confirmPublished`: after the person's publish, their domain row is read back. Its units must have exactly the
   approved digest, or the call refuses with `stale_approval` and the head stays. Then it is recorded as a revision
   on the head and the head moves, with the person's approval of that digest (#5581's `advanceHead`). **The head is
   always a published domain row, never a draft.**

The domain's own form is rebuilt from units exactly:

- `slidesFromUnits` rebuilds cards in order, with each card's blocks in the order now recorded in its `__layout`
  unit. Loose blocks keep their place, and a block a side added follows the card's other blocks.
- `markdownFromUnits` joins clause texts.

Because the block order is recorded in `__layout`, adding or removing a block counts as a layout edit. Two writers
adding blocks to one card therefore conflict and go to a person.
