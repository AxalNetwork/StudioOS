### What the canvas decides

- The notification link opens the record Sheet directly, even when the log filter hides the row, and survives sign-in. A uid that is not yours reads "no longer on your desk" and discloses nothing.
- Every state has a Next line naming who acts. A team introduction uses 0 credits.
- The recipient decides in the Sheet: Accept or Not now, each with an optional note that says who will read it. Accepting sends nothing and leaves Made unset.
- Write introduction sends from the teammate's connected mailbox. The composer sits in the Sheet with sender, recipient, subject and editable body, then Review and send. Eadwyn can draft after quoting its cost; writing by hand is the alternative. A saved draft does not record Made.
- Delivery recovery is for the introducer only. A confirmed send with a pending record gets Update the record, which sends no email, and there is no Resend. Unknown delivery asks It arrived / It did not arrive before any new send. Not sent keeps the draft.
- The requester sees "Waiting on [teammate] to make the introduction" and no mailbox details. They can withdraw while pending; a 409 shows when the teammate answered first.
- After Made either person may record an outcome, separate from delivery. Each person's own follow-up reads due, snoozed, done or none, and Could not be read when unreadable.
- After any 409 the Sheet reads the record again and keeps the typed text.

### Backend required
