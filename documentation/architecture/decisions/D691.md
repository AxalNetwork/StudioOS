All five read states can round-trip through PATCH. Due is required; missing
Add or Edit due returns 400, “An obligation has a due date.” A collision on
`UNIQUE(project_id, event_type, due_date)` returns 409,
“Another <kind> obligation is already due on <date>. Pick another date.”
A changed due date resets `reminders_sent_json` to `[]`; other edits preserve it.

Entity and jurisdiction follow `routes/compliance.ts`: use the project's
`entity_id`, look up the entity, and store its jurisdiction label; without an
entity or jurisdiction, store `Unspecified`. Do not store a jurisdiction id.
New events explicitly use `source = 'manual'`, and both `created_by_user_id`
and `created_by` identify the caller. Existing `canAccessProject` gates remain.

#### Part 3 (#1698): the records, their actions, form-options, the theme band and the daily step

**Records.** `services/records/grow.ts` registers Grow on D690's contract.
`GET /api/cycles/grow/modules/:slug/records/:id?project=` answers all seven
modules in CO:382-388's shape: a title, a sub, pills, four facts, and the main
and side sections. The ids are Product improvement's feedback uid, a channel
uid, a KPI key (`mrr`, `paying_accounts`, `new_mrr`, `expansion_mrr`, `churn`,

**Gates (D-u, decision 9).** Every gate is `canAccessProject`, unchanged. A
D690 `Gate` may now be async (`services/records/types.ts`,
`routes/cycle_records.ts` awaits it), because telling a co-founder member from
an advisor member reads the roster. An advisor member reads every record
without a customer's, contact's, deal customer's or acquired customer's name,
and gets `actions: []`. Applicant names in Shortlist, the Posted on link to
`/jobs/<id>/manage`, Close role and Edit's `publish` on a draft are the
posting's host's or an admin's (`jobs.ts`'s `canManage`). Close role is not
declared for anyone else, so the record route answers such a key as it answers
any undeclared one, with 404 ("No such action on this record."), and the
posting stays open; a non-host's `publish` reaches `updateRole`, which refuses
it with 403 and writes nothing.

**The theme band** is the venture's owner's and an admin's. The propose and
accept routes gate on Validate's `canWrite`, which is narrower than Grow's
write gate, so a co-founder member or a partner sees no band: the band's list
read is refused and it draws nothing.

**Mark held (decision 2)** writes an occurrence (date, notes, an optional
minutes file) and moves the same obligation's `due_date` forward by calendar
months (monthly 1, quarterly 3, annual 12), clamped to the target month's last
day, and resets `reminders_sent_json` to `[]`. A `one_time` obligation becomes
`completed` instead. The occurrence and the move are one batch, so a clash with
`UNIQUE (project_id, event_type, due_date)` answers 409 ("Another <kind>
obligation is already due on <date>. Pick another date.") and writes neither.
Minutes are a PDF or a DOCX, 20 MB at most, stored in `env.FILES` under
`grow-minutes/<project id>/<uid>.<ext>`, a key with no file name. Open minutes
answers one signed download (`mintDownloadToken`, two minutes) for an
occurrence of this obligation whose key is under this venture's prefix; any
other occurrence or key answers 404. `compliance.ts`'s own completion keeps
inserting a next row for its own route.

**Reminders and alerts (decision 3)** are sent in the app only, through
`notify()` with `channels: ['in_app']`. The daily step
(`services/growDaily.ts`, 05:35 UTC) sends, for each N in an obligation's
`remind_days_json`, one notification once the day `due_date` − N has come and
while the obligation is not completed, to `owner_user_id`, else to the
venture's founder; N is then appended to `reminders_sent_json`, claimed before
the send, so it goes once and a missed day is caught by the next run (category
`compliance`). An alert is crossed when its KPI's value, or its change over 30
days in percent, is past the threshold; a crossing notifies the alert's
creator once and sets `last_crossed_at`, and crossing back clears it, which
re-arms the alert (category `proactive_nudges`). A hand entry checks that KPI's
alerts at once (`growKpis.ts`'s `checkKpiAlerts`, best effort), a Burn entry
also Runway's; the daily step checks every venture's alerts, which catches
Stripe and CSV imports within a day. Build's filing reminders are not read.

### Deck share signup guards

Issue #1705, found while checking #1699. `POST /api/decks/share/:token/signup` (`routes/deck_share_actions.ts`) creates a user account for anyone holding a live share token. It inserts a `users` row, an `activity_logs` row and an `onboarding_progress` row, then mints a session. It had neither guard that `POST /api/auth/register` has. Its comment said Turnstile was bypassed because "the share-token-gated flow already proves intent". It does not: a token proves access to one deck, and a link with no expiry and no view cap reaches whoever it is forwarded to. Its only ceiling was the generic 200/min/IP bucket, so one IP could mint about 200 accounts a minute from a leaked link.

**Turnstile, as register does it.** The body must carry `turnstileToken`, verified with `verifyTurnstile(env, token, clientIp)`. A missing or failed token answers 403 "Bot verification failed. Please try again." It also writes a `turnstile_failed` activity row, as register does. That row holds only a hashed email (`hashEmail`), a /24 (IPv4) or /48 (IPv6) bucket from `ipPrefix`, and the deck id, never the raw email or the full IP. The check runs after the token and the input are validated and before any other read or write. That includes the existing-email lookup, so a script cannot use its 409 to learn which addresses have accounts. With no `TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY`, `verifyTurnstile` fails closed in production and open in development, as it does for register.

**A rate-limit bucket.** `deck_share_signup` in `middleware/rateLimit.ts`, beside `register`, allows 6 requests per 60 seconds per IP, `failClosed: true`, matching POST on `DECK_SHARE_SIGNUP` (`^/api/decks/share/[^/]+/signup$`). The pattern is anchored, so the share family's other routes (`nda`, `feedback`, `context`, `deal-pack`) stay on the generic bucket. If KV is down, the account-creating write answers 503 instead of running uncapped.

**The modal.** `ShareViewerSignupModal.jsx` loads Turnstile with `loadTurnstile` and the same site key as `RegisterPage`. It renders the widget on the signup step, sends `turnstileToken`, keeps Continue disabled until a token arrives, and resets the widget after a refused submit, because a token is single-use. When the widget cannot load (a bounded poll of about 10 seconds), it shows register's fail-visible line: "Human verification couldn't load — usually an ad blocker or strict network."

**Departure: the second sentence of that line.** Register's notice goes on "You can still continue with an emailed sign-in link below." That is true there and not here, because the modal has no other way to create an account. So the modal says instead "Allow challenges.cloudflare.com or try another network, then reload to create your account" (D-s). `api.deckShareSignup` is unchanged: it already sends its payload as given.

**Unchanged.** The existing-email refusal (409 `requires_login`), the role whitelist, the conversion log and the session mint.
