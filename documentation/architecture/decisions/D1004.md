### 1. What Overview reads now

Overview stops counting from the persona list. It reads three things, each on its own:

| Read | Draws |
|---|---|
| D999's segment counts | The four tiles, the sub-account chips, segments by profile, how set, the confidence bands, the weekly line, each card's members and 30-day delta, and the Advisors' empty card |
| D1000's profiles | The cards: name, who they are, profile group, status |
| D1000's needs read | Each card's top need, needs met and gaps, and the needs and offers table |

- **No Guard L on Overview.** D999 counts every account but admins, not the newest 500, so the "newest 500" line leaves Overview.
- **What still reads the list:**
  - Unclassified (part 9 moves it);

- **The functions that counted Overview from the list** (`tiles`, `segmentsByProfile`, `howSet`, `confidenceBands`, `segmentCards`) are removed, with D997's tests of them. Their replacements are tested on D999's blocks.

### 2. What it draws

All of it is in `frontend/src/pages/admin/segments/`. The derivations are pure functions in `segModel.js`.

1. **The tiles** (`overviewTiles`):
   - In a segment, with "of N members · P%" and "+N in 30 days";
   - No segment, with "Open Unclassified ›";
   - Segment and profile differ;
   - Moved by an admin, 30 days.

   Under a profile filter, In a segment and No segment come from that profile's group. The other two are counted across every profile, so they read "Counted across every profile: the read does not split it by profile." rather than a number that is not the profile's.
2. **The profile filter:** All, Founders, Investors, Advisors, Operating Partners, Explorers. It narrows the tiles, segments by profile, the cards and the table. How set and the bands are not split by profile, and say so under a filter.
3. **The sub-account chips:**
   - "LPs N", D999's approved LP applications. When it is on, each card shows its LPs, and the line says how many LPs have no segment.
   - "Product testers · Not recorded", disabled, with D999's reason. Nothing marks one yet.
4. **The charts:**
   - Segments by profile, how set and the bands keep D997's bars, with the hollow No segment bar.
   - **The weekly line** is D893's `WeekLine` through `Chart`, with "View as table" (`ChartTable`) and an `alt` sentence. A week before any segment was dated is a break, never 0, and the current week is the hollow, dashed point "to date".
   - The summary sums the last four weeks: "10 members were given a segment in the last 4 weeks, 2 of them this week to date."
5. **The cards**, grouped by HQ's profile: Founders, Investors, Advisors, Operating Partners, Explorers, Any profile. Each card shows:
   - members and the 30-day delta;
   - the top need (HQ's first), needs met "n of m", the gaps pill and the status pill (Active, Draft, Retired);
   - when HQ wrote no needs: "Top need: Not recorded", "Needs met: Not recorded" and the reason.

   **An empty Advisors group** is SEG1's dashed card, "No segment for Advisors yet.", with how many advisors have none. An Admin also reads "HQ is defining them." A Super Admin does not, since they are HQ.

   A segment HQ holds with no D999 row has no members yet, and reads 0.
6. **The needs and offers table:** one row per segment, gaps first, then by name, with search and the profile filter.
   - **Cells** (Programs, Perks, Events, Content, Sidebar links): the number offered; "Gap" (or "N · Gap") where a need asks for that kind and nothing of it answers; a dash where none is offered and no need asks for it; "Could not be read" for a store that failed.
   - **Needs met:** "n of m", "Unknown of m" when a store could not decide it, or "Not recorded".
   - **The gaps a row sorts by** are its Gap cells plus its unmet needs.
7. **SEG7, for a Super Admin:**
   - a Scope select holding HQ-held accounts, with the canvas's line ("Today only HQ-held accounts exist. Subsidiaries appear once they are deployed.");
   - a Suggestions inset, "Waiting for HQ".

   Both say they arrive with D997 part 6 (#2818), which is in review.
8. **States:**
   - SEG10, no member has a segment yet: the charts give way to the canvas's centre card with Open Unclassified. The cards and the table stay.
   - SEG11, loading: each tile reads "Reading…" and each block draws a skeleton.
   - SEG12, a failed block: "Could not be read. This is not a claim that there are none." with its own Try again, which reads that store again. The rest of the page answers. A failed tile reads a dash and the sentence, never 0. A card whose needs failed says "Needs could not be read." and keeps its members.
   - SEG12f, all three reads failed: one centred "Persona segments could not be read." with Try again.
   - Dark: every new class has its dark pair, and `check-dark-mode` passes.
9. **The Admin rail key (#1874).** A plain Admin's page sits in `AdminBoardFrame`, workspace "Persona segments", with its own note. `lib/railPages.js` gives the route `Admin · Accounts · Persona segments` as an off-canvas key, because the AIRail canvas draws no Admin board for it. HQ keeps H22 ("Super Admin · Content · Personas").
