### What the canvas decides

- **The job:** create a private draft → save and resume → assemble evidence and terms → submit for review → resolve changes → approve an exact version → preview what the permitted audience sees → explicitly publish. Approval and publication are different acts; publication is not an investment decision, signature, commitment, payment or funded status.
- **Three indicators, never one pill:** the working draft (Draft, In review, Changes requested, Approved), the release (Not released, Published · vN, Withdrawn, Archived) and the round stage (the existing Sourced to Closing words, secondary). Published v2 and private draft v3 coexist; the version being edited, reviewed or previewed is labelled on every screen.
- **Typed creation:** the transaction family comes first (direct company financing; syndicate or SPV raise with its separate underlying investment; fund raise and LP admissions; fund deployment or co-investment). Debt and secondary transfers are visible as Not available and never selectable. Then the **issuer company** and **one of its projects** are chosen as two fields and verified as a pair (or a project resolves its verified company). Fund and SPV identifiers add context and never replace company or project. The issuer is never taken from the active company. A project with no company keeps research only: "Link the project's company before creating a deal".
- **Parties are separate:** issuer with representative and signatory authority, investing entity or individual, GP or manager, broker. Individuals need no company. Four checks are shown apart for each actor: software entitlement, mandate or scope, regulatory permission where applicable (evidence with verification date, next review and suspension, not a green badge), and the specific approval needed (issuer approval, GP acceptance, broker supervisory approval, signatory authority, payment authorization).
- **Money stays apart:** soft interest, allocated, signed commitment, called, received and deployed are separate figures; an SPV's two legs are never added together. Fund Admin stays the system of record, reached by direct, state-preserving handoffs for admissions, calls and receipts, positions and reporting.
- **States:** company missing, invalid company/project pair, legal details not recorded, unauthorized signatory, project moved since approval (the approval stops covering new actions; released terms and signed paper keep their legal party), a legacy deal with no company (never backfilled), conflict with your text kept, offline, session expired, and the Platform States vocabulary throughout. Confirmations and the contract handoff name company, project and revision.
- **No self-approval fallback:** with no recorded publishing policy, Approve and Publish are unavailable and drafting continues. AI proposals from the shared rail are accepted into the private draft only by a person.
