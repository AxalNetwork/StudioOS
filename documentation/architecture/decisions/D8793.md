Four findings:

1. **Only four areas kept their page after a 401** (D7178: Grow, Companies, Funds, Learning). Every other protected page, HQ's and the Investor's included, was sent to a bare `/login` that looks exactly like a first visit. That is the audit's reading.

2. **One tab's interruption signs every tab out.**
   - A 401 on a protected page clears the stored `user`.
   - Every other open tab mirrors that (`useAuthSync`'s `storage` listener) and meets its route guard signed out, without asking the server.
   - The audit's "already-open HQ tab landed on /login" matches this.

3. **The inactivity sign-out runs per tab.**
   - `useInactivityTimeout` listens on its own `window` only, so an idle tab signs the whole browser out while the reader is active in another. It calls `/api/auth/logout`, so the server session ends too, and the active tab's next read meets a 401.
   - This alone reproduces the audit's pattern: read-only browsing in an Investor tab, then every tab on `/login`.
   - It is the likeliest cause, but not a proven one: the live walkthrough's timings were not recorded, and its build and server logs were not read.

4. **The warning lasts 20 minutes, not 60 seconds.**
   - The hook's effect depends on `warningOpen`. When the warning opens, the effect re-runs and its cleanup clears the 60-second logout timer, then schedules a fresh 19-minute warning timer. Only that second cycle signs out, at 39 minutes.
   - Its countdown interval is cleared the same way, so "60 seconds" never counts down.
   - This is recorded, not changed (§3).

**After signing in again** the page is honoured by every method: email link (`storePendingNext`), Google, Apple, passkey and authenticator all read `safeNextPath()` in `LoginPage.jsx`. Signing in was not driven end-to-end in the browser.

### 2. What changed (no layout)

- **`frontend/src/lib/pendingNext.js`:**
  - `sessionEndedRedirectFor(location, reason)` keeps every protected page through the same sanitiser. A page it cannot keep (too long, not same-origin, a sign-in page) goes to `/login?ended=1`, so the page can still say why. `reason` is `ended=1` (a 401) or `ended=idle` (inactivity).
  - `sessionEndedNotice(next, search)` gives the sign-in page's sentence:
    - D7178's line in a named area, otherwise a generic one;
    - never the page, the project or the account;
    - null for an ordinary visit or after a chosen sign-out.
  - A two-minute marker, `gvpn:session-ended` (`markSessionEnded`, `sessionEndedRecently`, `clearSessionEnded`), lets other tabs tell an interruption from a sign-out. `signedOutRedirectFor` is the route guard's answer: `loginRedirectFor` unless the marker is fresh.
- **`frontend/src/lib/api.js`:** the 401 handler leaves the marker before it clears the stored user.
- **`frontend/src/hooks/useInactivityTimeout.js`:** the timer's own sign-out passes `{ reason: 'inactivity' }`. "Log out now" in the warning stays a chosen sign-out.
- **`frontend/src/App.jsx`:**
  - `logout(opts)`: an inactivity sign-out marks `idle` and lands on `/login?next=<page>&ended=idle`;
  - a chosen one clears the marker and lands on `/login`, as before;
  - the route guard uses `signedOutRedirectFor`.
- **`frontend/src/pages/LoginPage.jsx`:** draws `sessionEndedNotice` in D7178's existing `login-session-ended` status line. The wording is interim: the canvas supplies the copy.
- **Tests:**
  - `frontend/test/session_interrupted_8793.test.mjs` (6);
  - `research_companies_access_7178.test.mjs` and `learning_recovery_4646.test.mjs` updated where they pinned the bare `/login` for other pages and the guard's call.

### 4. questions:

- **Route:** `/login`, reached three ways:
  - directly;
  - after an interruption: `/login?next=<page>&ended=1` after a 401, `&ended=idle` after inactivity, `/login?ended=…` when the page cannot be kept;
  - after a chosen sign-out: bare `/login`.

  After sign-in, the kept page.
- **Who sees it:** anyone signed out. After an interruption, a member of any role: HQ, Admin, Investor, Founder, Advisor, Operating Partner, Exploring.
- **The parts and their stores:**
  - **Heading:** "Sign in to Axal VC", or D7178's "Sign in to continue to <area>" for a named area (`destinationArea`).
  - **Notice:** `sessionEndedNotice` from the URL's `ended` and `next` alone. Nothing is read from the server and no private detail is shown.
  - **Methods:** Email, Google and Apple (when configured), each carrying `next` (`safeNextPath`).
  - **Other tabs:** they read the `gvpn:session-ended` marker (localStorage, two minutes).
  - **Inactivity warning:** `InactivityWarningModal`, opened by `useInactivityTimeout`.
- **States to draw:**
  - an ordinary sign-in;
  - session ended (401) with a kept page;
  - session ended with nothing kept;
  - signed out for inactivity, with and without a kept page;
  - the second tab of either;
  - a chosen sign-out;
  - the return to the kept page;
  - **a destination no longer available** (signed in as an account without access to it, or the page gone);
  - **Not recorded:** a reason the client cannot tell (a stale marker, an unknown `ended` value), which draws the ordinary page;
  - **Unreadable:** the identity read (`/api/auth/me`) failing after sign-in, which today falls back to the cached user;
  - each at desktop, 390 and 320 px, with keyboard focus.
- **Questions for the owner:**
  1. Should inactivity be measured across all of a browser's tabs (activity in any tab keeps every tab signed in), or stay per tab?
  2. Fix the warning timer so the sign-out happens at the stated 20 minutes, with a live 60-second countdown? Today it is 39.
  3. After signing back in as a different account that cannot open the kept page, should the page say so ("That page belongs to another account or is no longer available") rather than silently landing on the new account's home?
  4. Should the other tabs stay on their page with a "Signed out — sign in again" bar instead of going to `/login` themselves?
