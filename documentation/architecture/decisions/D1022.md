## D1022 — Cap table part 7: Overview complete and the holder drawer (#2859)

- **Status:** built in slot S04 (claimed by the S25 session at the owner's request), 2026-10-25. Part 7 of Cap table (#2432, D1015 §6).
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Raise Cap Table Page.dc.html`: CT1 with CT1c, CT2 with CT2p, CT16, CT19a–b, CT20, CT21a–c, CT22a–b and CT24.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` after #2852 (D1015), #2907 (D1017, part 2) and #2881 (D1019, part 4).
- **Migration:** none.
- **Why:** D1015 left Overview as the module page with a Modelled line, because nothing recorded what was issued. Part 2 is that record. This part draws it: who owns, the side column and the proposals waiting, all from the record, and the holder drawer.

### 1. Where it sits

Overview is still CycleRoute's module page. `CapTableHomeNav` keeps the tabs and draws `CapTableOverview` under them; the module page's Holders list and Planned round card follow, still the model and tagged Modelled. The line under the tabs now says so: "Figures tagged On record come from the record of what was issued. The Holders list and the Planned round card below still come from your saved model."

**The holder drawer is `/raise/cap-table/h-<n>`.** `isCapTableTab` now reads `h-<n>` as the tool's own page, so CycleRoute makes no cycle record read for it and draws `CapTableTabPage`, which draws Overview's record blocks with the drawer over them. `e-<n>` and `pool` stay record pages. No route is added; `isCapTableTab` and CycleRoute's branch stay the only wiring.

**One Worker change.** The record read's `by_holder` rows now carry `kind`, the register row's kind (founder, investor, employee, advisor), read from `cap_table_holders` for this venture only. A holder named only on an issuance reads `kind: null`. Without it, Who owns could only group by the class a holder holds, and a common holder would have been called a founder.

### 3. The blocks

- **The tool bar:**
  - The chips: As of (the latest recorded change, never today's date), Carta (not connected, synced, or last sync failed), the 409A (in the Worker's state) and "[n] proposals waiting".
  - The owner's "Record an issuance" and a co-founder's "Propose an issuance" are part 10's dialog (#2862). The tool bar takes `onRecord` and draws the button only when one is given, so nothing is drawn yet.
  - Below Raise + Grow (`hasTier(user, 'studio')`), CT24's note and See plans replace them. Reading stays open.
- **Needs you:**
  - The proposals, newest first, at most four, each in words from its fields. A missing figure is "not recorded", never invented.
  - Review opens a dialog that says what recording changes and who sees it. The owner records or dismisses it there (`accept`, `dismiss`).
  - A refused Accept shows the Worker's sentence and what the proposal still lacks.
  - A co-founder opens it and reads that only the owner decides.
  - Part 10's proposal drawers replace this dialog when they land.
- **Who owns** has two switches, Outstanding / Fully diluted and By holder / By class:
  - **By holder** groups Founders, Investors, Employees and advisors, Other holders, and the pool's unallocated shares, each with a subtotal. A row shows the holder's classes, shares, % fully diluted, % outstanding, its source and last change, and links to its drawer when the holder has a register row.
  - **Outstanding** leaves out options and the pool.
  - A holder's group is the register's kind. Failing that, preferred makes an investor and options alone a person. Common alone is "Other holders", never a founder by guess.
  - **By class (CT1c)** shows authorized, issued, outstanding, price per share (to six places: common is often $0.0001), liquidation preference, conversion ratio and holders, with the footer sum.
- **The side column:**
  - On record with As of.
  - The tiles: founders, investors, pool granted, pool unallocated, SAFEs and notes. No pool class is "No pool is recorded", not a pool of zero.
  - Three 100% bars: fully diluted and outstanding (On record), and "After [round] · Cap table · model" (Modelled). With no round planned, the third is a dashed note, "No round is planned. Plan one in Scenarios."
  - The outstanding instruments and the 409A line.

A block the record cannot compute (`nothing_recorded`, `negative_balance`, `pool_size_not_recorded`, `pool_overgranted`) shows the Worker's sentence and no number. A missing fully diluted total leaves its percentages "Not recorded", while outstanding still counts.

### 4. The holder drawer (CT2, CT2p)

- **The header:** the holder's group, % fully diluted and the On record tag.
- **Positions:** what they hold, by class.
- **Issuances:** every recorded or void row naming them, void rows struck through with their reason. Each shows its price, the admin's correction reason, its source, and its document or "! No document".
- **For a founder or a person:**
  - vesting from the venture's equity plan (start, cliff, total, vested of total, a bar);
  - 83(b) filed, not filed yet, or Not recorded.
- **What they can see:** the grant naming them (by holder name, as D1019's `holder_ref` matches), in D1019's own words. With no grant, "Not shared".
- **Each read answers alone.** A failed vesting, 83(b) or grants read says so and is never "none".
- **Void** is part 10's dialog: the drawer takes `onVoid` and draws nothing until it is given one.

### 5. The states

| Board | What draws |
|---|---|

| CT19a | nothing recorded and no model: "Nothing is recorded yet." No tile, so no number. |
| CT19b | nothing recorded with a saved model: "Your earlier model is kept as a scenario, Cap table · model." The owner's "Record them from the model" opens a dialog, then proposes each model founder's shares and each SAFE or note through part 2's proposals route (D1017), in the record's units (cents and basis points). Nothing counts until each is recorded. Part 3 (#2855) may offer these from the Worker instead; the button then calls that. |
| CT20 | while the record reads: the table and the tiles as skeletons, the chips "Reading…". |
| CT21a | a failed record read: "The cap table could not be read. That is not the same as an empty cap table. Nothing can be saved until it reads." with Try again. The tool bar offers nothing to save. |
| CT21c | a failed 409A read says so in its card and its chip; the rest draws. |
| CT22a | an Explorer: "Cap tables open once your profile is set." Nothing is read. |
| CT22b | a refused record read: "This cap table belongs to another company." The company is not named, since the refusal does not name it. |
| CT24 | below Raise + Grow: "Recording and scenarios are on the Raise + Grow plan." with See plans. |

CT21b (rows from a last good Carta sync) is not drawn. The record holds no Carta snapshot to fall back to, so a failed sync shows only in Carta's chip.
