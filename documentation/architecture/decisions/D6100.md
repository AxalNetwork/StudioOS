## D6100 — Grow's Feedback and KPI pages open on one first action (#6100)

- **Status:** built under slot S04, 2026-11-15 (#6100, filed under #4861).
- **Number:** D6100 is this issue's number (D4106). No route, no migration, no Worker change.
- **Builds on:** D5076 (the zero / few / many stages and their copy), D5077 (the closed Sources region at zero).
- **Files:** `frontend/src/pages/cycles/progressiveList.js` (Grow's copy, the unreadable guard),
  `CycleModulePage.jsx` and `PhoneModulePage.jsx` (`Sources` takes the module's `fold` label),
  `frontend/test/grow_progressive_zero_states_d6100.test.mjs` (new).

### 1. The problem

Grow's Product improvement and Revenue and traction opened on an empty chart card, a table header, search,
filters and "No records yet", with every connector part (Notion, PostHog; Mercury, Google, PostHog) drawn at
full weight before the founder had entered anything.

### 2. What changed

`CycleModulePage` and `PhoneModulePage` look the copy up by cycle key (`progressiveFor(meta.key, slug)`), so a
`grow` entry in `PROGRESSIVE` is all the stages need. Two modules are listed:

| Module | Heading | Single action |
|---|---|---|
| Product improvement | Hear what your customers ask for | Log feedback |
| Revenue and traction | Record the numbers that show traction | Enter a KPI |

- **The action** is the module's existing form button (`FORMS.grow`), with the same label, href and dialog.
- **The fold.** At zero, the connector parts move into the closed region D5076 named "Bring it in from your
  tools". Each Grow entry carries that label as `fold`; `Sources` draws it in place of "Sources" when it is
  set. Build's entries set no `fold`, so Build keeps D5077's "Sources" line.
- **Few and many** are D5076's: a plain count ("3 KPIs", "1 piece of feedback") below 10, the full table from 10
  or while searching.
- **Honesty.** A records read the adapter marks `state: 'unreadable'` is `many` in `listStage`, even if it also
  carries a total of 0, so it keeps the table's Unreadable and Retry. A whole-module unreadable read draws
  Unreadable before any stage is worked out. Neither is ever drawn as a first-use state.
- **Phone.** The same copy: "One next thing" carries the heading, the outcome and the one CTA, and the parts
  fold under the same label.

### 3. Departures

- The issue asked for copy only. The fold label needed a three-line change to `Sources` and its two call
  sites, because D5077 renamed the zero-state region to "Sources" after D5076 named it. The label is copy in
  `progressiveList.js`; the render code only reads it.
- The canvas moves KPIs into Focus (GR-05) and Feedback into Customers (GR-06). Nothing here moves them.
  If those slices land first, the copy stays valid wherever the two module pages still draw.
