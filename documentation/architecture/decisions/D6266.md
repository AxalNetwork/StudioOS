### 1. The key exists

`GET /api/research/diligence` and `/diligence/:grantUid` said "a data-room grant and a deal are
separate records with no key between them". They share their company: `deals.project_id` and
`data_room_grants.project_id`. Each room now returns `deals: [{ id, stage, name }]`.

### 2. The audience is the deal page's own

A deal is returned only when `investorHasDealRelationship` (`services/dealAccess.ts`, D4631) holds
for the caller: invited to it, or committed on it. That is the same predicate the deal page and the
diligence case use, called per deal rather than copied. A deal the caller cannot open is never named
or counted. `name` is the company's name, since a deal has no name of its own.

### 3. Words

- **Stage:** the deal's stored `status`, capitalised, the word the deal page's header shows
  ("Status not recorded" when absent).
- **No deal:** `deal_stage_note` reads "No deal you are invited to is recorded for this company."
  It is sent only then, and drawn as a plain sentence, never as Not recorded.
- **Failed read:** the room still opens, and the note reads "Unreadable: the deals on this company
  could not be read. That is not a claim that there are none."

### 4. Attach to deal

The IR2 action stays disabled. Its reason is now that a room already shows the deals on its company
the investor is invited to, so there is nothing to attach by hand. The proposal that also named this
row gives it up to this issue.
