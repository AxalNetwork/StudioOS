## D6255 — In-app notices for issued fund reports (#6255)

The owner chose one in-app notice per LP for each issued reporting period.
Both the GP issue path and the reporting queue publish after their period
transaction commits. Drafts and the queue's unrelated document types send
nothing. A correcting period has its own record and notice.

The existing notification inbox carries the notice and links to the
membership-gated report. Recipient selection uses the fund's committed and
active LP records. A linked active account receives one notice even when
multiple LP rows point to it. A row without an active linked account is
counted as undeliverable. The notice contains only an invitation to open
the issued report. No email is sent.

The inbox batch also commits a delivery receipt keyed by period ID, so
retries and dismissed notices do not create duplicates. A failed batch
leaves the period issued and records a failed delivery receipt separately.
The manager archive and period page distinguish complete, partial, failed,
unreadable and unsent delivery. Receipt details are manager-only.
