### 2. Not recorded is never 0

- **A sum is known only when every row's charge is.** A month with one NULL `charged_microusd` reads `null`, not a smaller number. The same holds for an area, a spend line, and the projection.
- **A rate with nothing to rate is `null`.** That covers proposals accepted % with no decision, and runs failed % with no ended run.
- **A count read from its table is a count, 0 included.** A count whose table cannot be read is `null`.
- **The platform budget is H4's** (`ai_agent_policy.platform_budget_microusd`, row 1). This migration creates the one-row table with no row; H4 adds the rest of its policy to it. Until H4 sets a budget, the budget, and the share spent against it, read `null`.
- **Some Needs attention items have no source yet.** H2 records failing golden cases; H3 records imports waiting for review and flagged company skills. Until those land, each item answers `{ available: false, reason: 'Not recorded' }`, never an empty list.
  - "Agents paused for their cap" is read from `team_agents` paused for `cap`, `spend_limit` or `daily_limit`.
  - "Model errors" are bursts of at least 5 failed or refused agent calls on one model within one hour, over the last day.

### 3. People by name, never by email

- **Agents** are named by their persona name (`team_agents.first_name`, `last_name`), never by a job title.
- **Teams** are shown as company › project by name. A project team's company is its project's company.
- **Admins** are named by `users.name`.
- **No route selects an email.** Free text from a member can still hold one: a step's summary, or a proposal's payload. `redactEmails` replaces any address at any depth in a run's answer.
- **The audit's CSV names the admin.** A cell that opens with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is written as text.

### 4. The run log and one run

- **Status.** The engine's statuses map to five:
  - `done` → succeeded;
  - `refused` → refused;
  - `failed`, `step_lost` and `halted` → failed;
  - `cancelled` and `scope_moved` → cancelled;
  - the live ones → running.
- **Proposal outcome.** Accepted and edited → accepted (an edit is an acceptance). Discarded → rejected. Proposed → pending.
- **Model, tokens and cost** are the run's usage row. Without one, all three are `null`.
- **Skills** are the agent's linked skills, with their versions.
- **Area** is the radar axis most of those skills' taxonomy tags fall in. An agent whose skills tag none has no area.
- **One run** adds:
  - its steps;
  - its inputs, as record references only: `task:<uid>`, `goal:<uid>`, `project:<id>` or `company:<id>`, `module:<key>`, `skill:<uid>@<version>`;
  - its output: the proposal's reference, kind, outcome and payload, redacted;
  - its cost against the hold: the charge, and the reservation's held amount when the usage row names one;
  - its errors: the refusal code, and every failed, refused or halted step.

### 5. Spend

- **Month to date**, grouped by agent, skill, company or model.
- **By skill**, a run's charge is shared evenly among the skills its agent carried. An agent with none counts under "No skill".
- **The projection** is month to date plus the last 7 days' daily pace times the days left. It is `null` when either part is unknown.
- **Teams at their cap** are the agents paused for a cap, plus those whose known month spend has reached `monthly_cap_microusd`.

### 6. The admin audit

- **`ai_admin_audit`** columns: `id`, `at`, `admin_user_id`, `action`, `area` (roster, skills, company_skills, policy), `subject` (a record reference), `before_json`, `after_json` and `reason`.
  - Every row needs a reason, of 1 to 500 characters.
  - Two triggers refuse any UPDATE or DELETE.
- **Writing it.** H1–H4 write through `aiAdminAuditStatement`, which goes in the same `DB.batch` as the change, so the two land together. `recordAiAdminAction` is the same statement on its own. An action with no admin, action name, area, subject or reason throws, so it cannot happen unaudited.
- **Read-only routes.** There is no route that writes, updates or deletes the audit. A test sends POST, PUT, PATCH and DELETE to the audit paths and gets 404 for each.

### 7. Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/admin_ai_agents_d4163.test.ts` has 8 tests, all passing. It covers:
- Super Admin only, on all six routes;
- the audit has no write route, and the table refuses UPDATE and DELETE;
- the audit names admins, needs reasons and filters, and its CSV has no email and neutralises formulas;
- null, not 0, for an unrecorded charge, an empty rate and the unset budget;
- spend against H4's budget, on every `by`;
- the run log's names, status, outcome and filters;
- one run's references and redaction.

**Mutations, each caught:**
- an unrecorded charge summed as 0;
- an empty rate read as 0;
- no email redaction;
- one route gated by `requireAdmin`;
- an audit delete route;
- a missing budget read as 0.
