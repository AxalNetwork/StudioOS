### §a — Bot calls and recorded sessions (#3980, 2026-11-01)

Built by S17. The kind is `bot_call`: family Meetings, module "Validate › Record a session", label "Recorded call". It is page only and not pushable.

- **The store, confirmed:** `meeting_bots` (migration 531).
  - `scheduled_at`, `started_at` and `ended_at` are INTEGER epoch **milliseconds** (`dispatchBot` stores `Date.parse`; the run's Durable Object stores `Date.now()`).
  - `status` is one of queued, joining, waiting_room, recording, processing, done, failed or stopped.
  - The table has no integer id, so the item's `source_id` is the row's `rowid`, and `source_uid` is the run's `uid`.
- **Owner rule:** `b.user_id = ?`, bound. The venture must be live (`deleted_at IS NULL`) and the member active.
- **Times:** epoch milliseconds become ISO.
  - A run starts when the bot joined (`started_at`), else when it was scheduled.
  - It ends when the bot left (`ended_at`), else at its limit (30 or 120 minutes).
  - The window is half open on the start.
- **Words** (`BOT_STATE_WORDS`):
  - `done` alone reads **Recorded**; the other states read Scheduled, Joining, In the waiting room, Recording, Processing the recording, Did not record and Stopped.
  - **"Transcript ready" (join confirmed).** It is said only when the run is `done` and its interview session holds its final transcript. That means rows in `interview_transcript_segments` with `source = 'final'` for the run's `session_id`, which `meetingBotAssembly.ts` writes in the same batch that marks the session transcribed. A live passage alone is not a transcript, and a run not yet done never announces one.
  - The item carries `bot: { state, words, transcript_ready }`, and the agenda row prints `words`.
- **Privacy (D886):**
  - the reader never reads `meeting_url_enc`, `participants_enc` or the event payloads;
  - the item's `location_uri` is null;
  - the title names the interviewee, as the interview call does;
  - a failing read logs only `{ kind, outcome }`.
- **Where it goes:** `href` is the venture's Validate › Customer research. The page gives it a hue in both themes and its own dot shape.
- **Verified:** `cloudflare-worker/test/calendar_bot_calls_d1486.test.ts` (5 tests):
  - owner only, with a second user who sees none, and in no staff scope;
  - epoch to ISO, joined-else-scheduled to left-else-limit;
  - `done` gives "Recorded", and no transcript means no "Transcript ready";
  - not in `.ics` or a feed;
  - no link or participants read, and a failing read logs no title.
  - Mutations, each caught: the owner predicate dropped; the transcript EXISTS made always true; the `done` guard dropped.
- **Updated in step:** the kind-count pins in the files below. With §b's two kinds, the count is eighteen.

  - `calendar_compliance_d1384`;
  - `calendar_page_c1`;
  - `calendar_views_d1205`;

### §b — Publish's sends and Grow's planned content (#3981, S20, 2026-11-02)

Two readers in part 1's registry, both page only, never pushed, and never in a staff scope (`PERSONAL_ONLY_KINDS`). They live in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/calendar/growReaders.ts`.

**`publish_send`.** Family `grow`, module "Publish", label "Publish send".
- **Store:** `publish_sends` (`642_publish_core.sql`).
- **Where it sits:**
  - A send with `status = 'sent'` sits on its `sent_at`, titled "Sent · <destination>".
  - A ready, sending, failed or unknown send sits on its `scheduled_for`, titled "Scheduled · <destination>".
  - A retracted or removed send is not on the calendar. Neither is a send with no time.
- **Who sees it:** Publish's own rule, as `routes/publish.ts` applies it through `team` → `isTeamViewer` (the venture's founder, or a member of the venture's company), plus `confirmed_by` = the reader. A send is the member's own. A member who has left the team stops seeing even the sends they confirmed, as Publish would stop showing them.
- **Its page:** the send's Grow module (`/grow/<module>`, or `/grow` for `overview`).

**`content_planned`.** Family `grow`, module "Grow › Distribution", label "Planned content".
- **Store:** `content_calendar_items.planned_on` (`587_content_calendar_items.sql`, D1090).
- **Where it sits:** an all-day item on its date, with the end exclusive, as D1208's deadlines (`isAllDay`). A `dropped` piece and one with no date are not shown.
- **Who sees it:** the same team rule, with no `confirmed_by`: a planned piece is the venture team's.
- **Its page:** `/grow/distribution`.

**Why the team rule, and not "the venture's founder" alone.** The issue asks for "the Publish owner rule", and Publish lets every team member read a venture's sends. A calendar that showed less would hide what the member can already open; one that showed more would show what Publish refuses. Admin access never widens either read.

**On the page.**
- A seventh family, "Grow", holds the two kinds (`calendarViews.js` `FAMILIES`). Both kinds are in `CalendarPage.jsx` `KINDS` with their module words.
- Each kind has its own mark in light and dark (`calendarPage.css`).
- The Explorer's families are unchanged.

**Tests.** `cloudflare-worker/test/calendar_grow_sources_d1486b.test.ts` covers:
- the team only, with a second founder, a company member, an admin, and a confirmer who has left the team;
- "Sent" only when sent;
- all day for `planned_on`;
- no retracted or dropped rows;
- not in an `.ics` file or a feed.

**Mutation checked, 9 of 9 caught:**
- the team predicate dropped on either reader (the issue's mutation);
- `confirmed_by` dropped;
- the title always "Sent";
- `planned_on` read as an instant;
- dropped pieces kept;
- retracted sends kept;
- `page_only` turned off;
- a kind left out of `PERSONAL_ONLY_KINDS`.

The calendar suites that count kinds and families now count 17 kinds and 7 families.
