### §b — Publish's sends and Grow's planned content (#3981, S20, 2026-11-02)

Two readers in part 1's registry, both page only, never pushed, and never in a staff scope (`PERSONAL_ONLY_KINDS`). They live in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/calendar/growReaders.ts`.

**`publish_send`.** Family `grow`, module "Publish", label "Publish send".
- **Store:** `publish_sends` (`642_publish_core.sql`).
- **Where it sits:**
  - A send with `status = 'sent'` sits on its `sent_at`, titled "Sent · <destination>".
  - A ready, sending, failed or unknown send sits on its `scheduled_for`, titled "Scheduled · <destination>".
  - A retracted or removed send is not on the calendar. Neither is a send with no time.
- **Who sees it:** Publish's own rule, as `routes/publish.ts` applies it through `team` → `isTeamViewer` (the venture's founder, or a member of the venture's company), plus `confirmed_by` = the reader. A send is the member's own. A member who has left the team stops seeing even the sends they confirmed, as Publish would stop showing them.
- **Its page:** the send's Grow module (`/grow/<module>`, or `/grow` for `overview`).

**`content_planned`.** Family `grow`, module "Grow › Distribution", label "Planned content".
- **Store:** `content_calendar_items.planned_on` (`587_content_calendar_items.sql`, D1090).
- **Where it sits:** an all-day item on its date, with the end exclusive, as D1208's deadlines (`isAllDay`). A `dropped` piece and one with no date are not shown.
- **Who sees it:** the same team rule, with no `confirmed_by`: a planned piece is the venture team's.
- **Its page:** `/grow/distribution`.

**Why the team rule, and not "the venture's founder" alone.** The issue asks for "the Publish owner rule", and Publish lets every team member read a venture's sends. A calendar that showed less would hide what the member can already open; one that showed more would show what Publish refuses. Admin access never widens either read.

**On the page.**
- A seventh family, "Grow", holds the two kinds (`calendarViews.js` `FAMILIES`). Both kinds are in `CalendarPage.jsx` `KINDS` with their module words.
- Each kind has its own mark in light and dark (`calendarPage.css`).
- The Explorer's families are unchanged.

**Tests.** `cloudflare-worker/test/calendar_grow_sources_d1486b.test.ts` covers:
- the team only, with a second founder, a company member, an admin, and a confirmer who has left the team;
- "Sent" only when sent;
- all day for `planned_on`;
- no retracted or dropped rows;
- not in an `.ics` file or a feed.

**Mutation checked, 9 of 9 caught:**
- the team predicate dropped on either reader (the issue's mutation);
- `confirmed_by` dropped;
- the title always "Sent";
- `planned_on` read as an instant;
- dropped pieces kept;
- retracted sends kept;
- `page_only` turned off;
- a kind left out of `PERSONAL_ONLY_KINDS`.

The calendar suites that count kinds and families now count 17 kinds and 7 families.

### §c — Raise: a round's close and an NDA's expiry (#3982, S31, 2026-11-01)

Two readers in part 1's registry, family `raise`. Both are page only (never in an `.ics` file, a feed or a provider copy), all day, and never in a staff scope (`PERSONAL_ONLY_KINDS`). The reader is `services/calendar/raiseDates.ts`.

| Kind | Store | Who sees it | Item |
|---|---|---|---|
| `round_close` | `raise_rounds.close_date` of an `active` round | the venture's founder (`users.founder_id`) and its `accepted` owners and co-founders (`project_members`) | "Round closes · <round name>", linking to Capital relationships |
| `nda_expiry` | `pairwise_ndas.valid_until` of an `active` NDA with no `voided_at` | its two parties only | "NDA with <the other party's name> expires" |

- **The round's reader** follows the rule Capital relationships reads the round by (`ownedProjectScope` with `capitalMemberProjectIds`). A pending member is not a member. The investor side is D1208's `deal_closing`.
- **The NDA's item** names the other party by name, never by email, and says nothing else about the NDA: no notes, no envelope and no signers.
- **Expired, revoked and voided NDAs are not shown.**
- **On the page:** both are in the Deadlines family. The registry gives the modules "Raise › Round" and "Raise › NDAs" and the labels "Round close" and "NDA expiry". Each has its own light and dark colour and dot shape.
- **Investor-update cadence is not built:** it has no store (owner question F, S1's pick a).

**Verified.** `cloudflare-worker/test/calendar_raise_dates_3982.test.ts`, 4 tests:
- the founder and an accepted co-founder see the round; a pending member, another founder and an investor do not;
- each NDA party sees the other's name, and a third user sees none;
- an expired, a revoked and a voided NDA are not shown, and no email reaches the item;
- both are all day on their day, name their source, and are in no `.ics` file, feed or staff scope.

**Mutations: 9 of 9 caught**, each applied alone:
1. the NDA party predicate dropped;
2. the founder predicate dropped;
3. a pending member counted;
4. a closed round shown;
5. an expired or revoked NDA shown;
6. a voided NDA shown;
7. the NDA naming the reader instead of the other party;
8. `round_close` in the feed;
9. `nda_expiry` in a staff scope.

**Pins updated for the two kinds:** `calendar_compliance_d1384` (the page-only set), `calendar_registry_d1484` (the union is 19 with §b's two), `calendar_page_c1`, `calendar_grids_d1210` and `calendar_views_d1205` (the kind table and the Deadlines family).
