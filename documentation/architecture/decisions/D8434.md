## D8434 — A change to a ci.yml job updates its own pinned digest, and S39 reviews that line (#8434)

`frontend/test/ci_capacity_pilot_7909.test.mjs` pins a digest of every ordinary job in
`.github/workflows/ci.yml` (`BASE_JOB_DIGESTS`, every job but `plan`). It shows that the capacity pilot did not
change ordinary CI. It also fails on any change to a job, and the file belongs to S39 (#7909).

The owner chose option A on 2026-11-24.

### The rule

A PR that changes a job in `ci.yml`:

1. updates that job's digest line in `BASE_JOB_DIGESTS`, in the same PR;
2. touches no other line of that file;
3. names the job and gives the old and new digest in its body;
4. asks S39 to review that line.

If the change touches `suite` or `capacity-submit` in a way the pilot depends on, S39 says so on the PR and it
waits for S39.

### Options not taken

- **B. S39 makes each update on request.** Every CI change would wait on the slot carrying the capacity work.
- **C. Narrow the pin to the jobs the pilot composes with.** Fewer collisions, and a weaker statement.

### Order

One `ci.yml` job change goes through the merge queue at a time. The plan in #8439 orders them: #8435, then
#8436, then #5606.
