## D8257 — Raise overview: per-area readiness status and item keys on the readiness card (#8257)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-22. A follow-up of #6046 (D6046, "To file", second item).
- **Read:** `GET /api/cycles/raise/overview`: the Fundraising readiness card (`readinessCard`, D663) as it already is. No new route, client method or migration.

### What changes

**Worker (`cloudflare-worker/src/services/raiseCycle.ts`).** Each bar of the card's `hbar` series keeps `label`, `value` and `tone`, and adds:
- `key`: the readiness item's key (`narrative`, `deck`, `data_room`, `cap_table`, `use_of_funds`, `traction`, `team`, `pmf`), or null for a key the list does not know;
- `status`: from `readinessAreaStatus`;
- `assessed_at`: the day of the newest score, or null when nothing scored it.

`readinessAreaStatus` follows RA-23's rule, the one Fundraising readiness draws (`readinessModel.js` `statusOf`). The Overview and that page therefore count the same areas. The order of the checks:

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `not_recorded` | no score, or a score row with neither a score nor a gap |
| `unavailable` | the score's gap says Unavailable (its check could not read its store) |
| `stale` | D5071 freshness says its inputs changed since it was scored |
| `ready` | scored, nothing missing |
| `attention` | scored, with something missing |
| `missing` | a gap with no score, or a measured 0 |

Unavailable is checked before Stale, and Stale before Ready and Needs attention.

A freshness that could not be worked out (`unavailable` in D5071) is not Stale: the area keeps its score's status.

Freshness is `readinessFreshness`, the same call the readiness records already make. The card now runs the readiness checks once more to compare hashes. If they throw, freshness is Unavailable for every item and the card still draws.

**Frontend (`frontend/src/pages/cycles/raiseOverviewModel.js`, `RaiseOverviewLead.jsx`, `RaiseOverview.jsx`):**
- **PMF by key.** `readinessView` reads each area's key, status and day. It leaves Product-market fit out by its key (`pmf`), never by its label (RA-16, D6049). The label regex is gone: no rule in the Overview matches an area by its label.
- **Coverage.** An area counts as assessed unless its status is Not recorded, as on Fundraising readiness.
- **RA-01.** The summary says "N need attention" (`attentionWords`): Missing, Needs attention, Stale and Unavailable, as `coverageLine` on Fundraising readiness counts them. With none, it reads "None needs attention".
- **RA-09 Stale.** When an area is stale, the summary draws the Stale chip from the oldest stale area's scoring day (`staleReadiness`). It names the areas: "<areas> was/were assessed before its/their evidence changed. Re-assess to update." Re-assess opens Fundraising readiness. A read-only (staff) viewer sees the note but is not offered Re-assess.
- **RA-07.** A stale area carries the Stale chip in both the bars and the table.
- **Older reads.** A bar from a read with no status, or an unknown one, falls back to its score: Ready when scored, Not recorded when not. A value that is not a day is never drawn as a day.
