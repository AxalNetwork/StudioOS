### 2. Propose a program

**The steps:** Basics → How it runs → Terms → Tools → Review, as buttons with `aria-current="step"`, with Back, Save draft and Next.

**Saved means stored.**
- "Draft saved [time]" is drawn only from part 2's stored `updated_at`. Before the first save it reads "Not saved yet".
- Nothing is held in the browser as if it were saved.
- The first save starts a draft (`POST`) and moves the address to `?proposal=<uid>`; later saves `PATCH` the same draft.

**Opening a stored proposal.** `?proposal=<uid>` opens one through part 2's single-proposal read.
- A Draft or Sent back proposal opens in the form. A Sent back one shows HQ's note above the steps.
- Any other status opens read only: its status, when it was sent, HQ's note and the Review.
- An unknown one, or another studio's, says so in one sentence.

**Basics:** the name, the one-line purpose, who it is for, and the group.
- The roles are Founders, Investors, Advisors, Operating partners and Explorers. "Product testers" waits for #2377. A stored "Product testers" is dropped from the form, not shown as chosen.
- The group chips are the catalogue's six names, so "Network and referrals", not the canvas's "Network".

**How it runs:** the join method, the cycle (Rolling, or Cohorts with their weeks), the seats and the eligibility.
- The cycle says it is saved as text and sets no calendar, because the only cohort calendar is the Lab's four weeks a month.

**Terms:**
- **Rewards** say they are what the studio asks for, and that nothing pays from them until HQ sets a rule. None stands alone, as part 2 refuses it with another.
- **Contract templates** come from `contractsApi.templates()`. A failed read, such as its 503 `templates_unreadable`, marks that field alone with Try again.
- **What HQ receives.**
- **The foot's sentence** has no link until #2376.

**Tools:** the seven switches, each `role="switch"` and labelled "Needs [tool]", with a line saying nothing is switched on for the program.

**Review:**
- **The playbook as HQ will read it**, built from the form with part 2's own rules. Who and Tools are lower-cased after the first, and How people join reads like "By application · cohorts of 12 weeks · 12 seats". An empty section reads "Not set".
- **Sending:**
  - Send to HQ waits until part 2's five needs are filled, and says which are missing.
  - It opens a confirmation dialog. Confirm saves, then sends.
  - A refusal is one sentence under the button. While OD-PROP is open, part 2 answers 403 `proposals_closed`, and its own sentence is drawn: "HQ has not opened program proposals yet, so this proposal cannot be sent. It stays saved as a draft."

### 3. The two lists

**Your proposals** (PG1, an Admin's):
- **Columns:** Program, Sent ("Not sent" for a draft), Status, HQ's note ("No note yet") and Open.
- **Tools:** search from two characters, Status and Sort (Newest, Oldest), a count ("1–3 of 3") and pages.
- **The statuses** are Draft, Sent to HQ, Approved, Sent back and Declined, in their own pills, apart from a program's "Proposed".
- **Open** goes to `?proposal=<uid>`, which opens a Draft or Sent back proposal in the form and any other read only.
- **An empty list** says "Your studio has not proposed a program yet." with "Propose a program". A filtered empty list says "No proposal matches these filters."

**Proposals from studios** (PG2, a Super Admin's), on the catalogue and on `/admin/programs/proposals`:
- **Columns:** Program, Studio, Sent, Status, and Decide (on a proposal sent to HQ) or Open.
- **Tools:** Studio, Status and Sort ("Oldest first" by default), a count and pages.
- **The Studio filter** lists the licences from HQ's program grants read, and HQ-held. If that read fails, the filter says "Could not be read" and the list still answers.
- **The studio** in a row is what part 2 records: the licence's brand name, or HQ-held.

**Each list draws its own state:** Reading…, "Could not be read" with Try again (never an empty list), empty, or rows.

### 4. HQ's side

**The tab strip** "Catalogue · Proposals [n]" appears for a Super Admin on the catalogue and on the proposals page. It sits in `ProposalsPage.jsx` as `ProgramsTabs`.
- The count is the queue's `counts.sent`, amber, from its own read (`status=sent`). Zero shows no badge, and a failed read says so in place of a number.
- The line is now "Every program on StudioOS, what needs HQ now, and what studios propose." (`LINE_SUPER`).

**New program** opens a dialog on `POST /api/admin/programs`.
- It takes the Basics and How it runs fields; Terms and Tools are a proposal's.
- Part 2's refusals sit under the buttons and the dialog stays open, among them `program_key_taken`: "A program with this name already exists. Change the name and try again."
- On success the catalogue says the program is added as Proposed with an empty playbook draft, and that the page has no card for it yet. The catalogue still draws the nine programs of its frontend constant (S-RUN). A card read from the registry is a later part.

**Decide** (PG15a) is a `Lightbox`: "Decide on [Program]" and "From [Studio] · sent [day]."
- **The four choices** are radio buttons, each with what part 2 records for it today. An approval adds the program as Proposed, for that studio only or for every studio, with its playbook as a draft.
- **The note** is required for Send back and Decline: Confirm without one refuses in place and sends nothing.
- **The foot's grant sentences** wait for part 3 and #2376. Until then the dialog says an approval sets no grant yet.
- **On Confirm**, a refusal sits under the buttons and the dialog stays open. A decision that lands closes it and reloads the list and the tab count.
