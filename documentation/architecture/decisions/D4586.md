## D4586 — Validate methodology v2: coverage, direction, sufficiency and the founder's decision, kept apart (#4586)

- **Status:** proposed, for the owner's review. #4586 requires the methodology to be reviewed before any calculation changes, so this PR changes none. The rules below exist as pure functions in `cloudflare-worker/src/services/validateMethodology.ts`. The table in § 3 is that code's output, pinned row by row by `cloudflare-worker/test/validate_methodology_4586.test.ts`. Nothing in the product imports the module yet; a test holds that. Wiring it in is the follow-up, once this is approved.
- **Context:** the 6 November 2026 source audit of commit `d85951ca9`, re-checked on `main` at `006b50d4e` (2026-11-07). All four findings still hold:
  1. `verdictFor` (`routes/_founder_validate_helpers.ts`) returns `validated` once five ICP interviews support a claim, before it looks at contradictions. 5 supporting against 50 contradicting reads as Supported.
  2. `recordDecision` (`services/verdict.ts`) counts checked **links** against `DECISION_EVIDENCE_BAR`. Five links to one interview count as five.
  3. A hypothesis-level decision checks that each piece of evidence belongs to the venture, not that it belongs to that hypothesis.
  4. A linked experiment counts whatever its state. `setExperimentStatus` (`services/validateWriters.ts`) accepts `done` with no result, and the evidence label then reads only "done".

  The parts of v1 that were deliberate stay, unless § 4 lists them as changed. Those are the five-interview bar, ICP-only support against any-fit contradiction, an interview counted once per claim, and the founder choosing the decision.
- **Coordination:** #4585 (PR #4942) labels the two evidence populations (interview support and linked evidence) and leaves the scoring policy alone. This methodology keeps claim status interview-only, as #4585's contract does, and uses #4585's source identities (`kind` + `ref_id`). #4627 (proposal identity) and #4629 (historical coverage) are unaffected.

### 1. Four things, never merged into one number

| Concept | What it is | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Coverage** | How much evidence there is, in named units: links, sources, interviews, interviewees, organizations | Evidence lists, the decision record |
| **Direction** | Supporting and contradicting, always shown together | Every claim figure |
| **Sufficiency** | Whether the claim's computed status, or a decision's evidence bar, is met | The claim's status; the decision's given or withheld state |
| **Decision** | The founder's Proceed, Pivot or Stop, with their reasoning | The decision record only |

The computed status is never the founder's decision. No status chooses Proceed. A decision below its bar is still recorded, as `withheld`, with its reasoning and with the shortfall visible.

A source count is not statistical proof. "Supported" means that this many ICP interviews support the claim against a fixed bar, with contradictions read first. It does not mean the claim is true.

### 2. Units

| Unit | Definition | Counted as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Link | One relationship: a pain link × an interview naming that theme, or one `hypothesis_evidence_links` row | Every link, so five links to one interview are **five relationships** |
| Source | The underlying record: `interview:<id>`, `signal:<ref>`, `metric:<key>`, `experiment:<id>` (`sourceKey`) | Once, whatever links reach it: five links to one interview are **one interview** |
| Interviewee | Distinct `interviewee_name` (trimmed, case- and space-folded) across counted interviews | Shown only when every counted interview records it; otherwise **not recorded**, never a smaller number. The name is read to count and never returned |
| Organization | Distinct `interviewee_company`, same rule | Same |

### 3. Claim status (interviews only, as in v1 and #4585)

Notation:
- `S`: distinct supporting ICP interviews (strong or partial).
- `C`: distinct contradicting interviews of any fit.
- `U`: distinct support-side interviews whose fit was never recorded.
- The bar is 5.

Rules, applied in this order:
1. **Contradicted** when `C ≥ 2` and `C > S`. Contradiction is read **before** the bar.
2. **Contested** when `S ≥ 5` and the contradiction is material, meaning `C ≥ 2` and `2·C ≥ S`: at least one contradicting interview for every two supporting.
3. **Supported** when `S ≥ 5` otherwise.
4. **Insufficient** otherwise.
5. **Unknown** when `U > 0` and counting those interviews as ICP would change the answer. Otherwise the answer stands, and the page still says how many fits are missing.

An interview that touches both directions is counted in both `S` (if ICP) and `C`, as in v1. It is reported, never dropped.

### 4. The decision's evidence bar

The founder still checks evidence and still chooses. v2 changes what counts toward the bar of 5:

- **Sources, not links.** Each underlying source counts once, whichever links reached it. Repeat links are kept in the snapshot and marked `duplicate_source`.
- **Relevance.** A hypothesis-level decision counts only that hypothesis's evidence. Evidence from another claim is kept, marked `other_hypothesis`, and does not count. A venture-level decision counts every claim's evidence.
- **Observed results.** An experiment counts only once a result is recorded. A planned or running experiment, or one marked done with no result, is kept and marked `no_observed_result`. A done experiment with no result reads "Done · no result recorded", never just "done".
- **Contradiction counts.** Contradicting sources count toward the bar like supporting ones, because they can be the reason for a Pivot or a Stop. A source linked in both directions counts once and appears on both sides.
- **What the record shows.** The record shows links, sources, the counted sources by direction, interviews, interviewees and organizations (or "not recorded"), each excluded link with its reason, and the bar.

### 5. Deliberate changes from v1

1. Contradiction is read before the bar, so 5/6 and 5/50 are no longer Supported.
2. **Contested** is a new status, for material contradiction once the bar is met (5/3 through 5/5).
3. Unknown fit makes the status Unknown only when those interviews could change it. Rows 0/3/1 and 2/0/1 now give an answer where v1 gave none. Row 5/3/2 becomes Unknown where v1 said Supported.
4. The decision bar counts distinct sources, not links.
5. A hypothesis-level decision counts only its hypothesis's evidence.
6. An experiment counts only with an observed result.

### 6. History keeps its meaning

- Past `validation_decisions` and their `validation_decision_evidence` snapshots are never recomputed. When v2 is wired in, a migration adds:
  - `methodology_version` to `validation_decisions` and `hypothesis_verdict_history`, NULL meaning v1;
  - `counted` and `exclusion` to `validation_decision_evidence`.
  New rows record version 2; old rows keep reading as v1 decisions.
- The first v2 read of a claim whose status changes only because the method changed is written to history with `methodology_version = 2`. The history view then labels that row "method changed", not as new evidence.

### 7. Questions for the owner

2. **Cross-hypothesis evidence.** Should it be kept as uncounted context (proposed), or refused outright on a hypothesis-level decision?
3. **The experiment writer.** Should `done` without a result be refused, or allowed and shown as incomplete (proposed)?
4. **Interviewee and organization breadth.** Display only (proposed), or also part of the decision bar?
