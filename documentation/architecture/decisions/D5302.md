- **Builds on:** D1043 (expert decision trail, co-marketing notices), D1044 (Decided, one read per lane from its own store), D1231 (KYB cases and their events), D1246 (due-diligence decisions, migration 620), D1132 (co-marketing "already decided").

### What was wrong

D1044 and the stores it reads landed on the same day (2026-10-26), and Decided was written before three of them could decide anything. On main the inbox named a page that decides every lane, and the stores recorded who decided and when, but Decided still said:

| Lane | Decided said | The store keeps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wellbeing experts | "sets a flag with no time or decider" | `expert_review_decisions` (577): one append-only row per verify, decline, unverify, hide and restore, with actor, time and reason, written in the same batch as the expert's current fields |
| Due diligence | "no route completes or archives one" | `dd_cases.decision`, `decided_by`, `decided_at`, `archived_by`, `archived_at` (620), written by `POST /api/dd/cases/:uid/decision` and the archive |
| KYB | "No admin route decides a business verification yet" | `kyb_case_events` (616): one row per staff act that committed, with its action and actor |

Co-marketing recorded its decisions, but publishing wrote the publisher into `reviewed_by_user_id` and the publish time into `reviewed_at`, so a published pitch had lost who approved it and when; Decided left published pitches out for that reason. A pitch the partner withdrew after approval also dropped out (Decided read `status IN ('approved', 'rejected')`), although its review columns were intact.

On the page, Lanes said HQ's Program proposals "is not built yet (#2371)" while the inbox and Decided both have it decided on Programs › Proposals (`/admin/programs/proposals`, D964).

### Decision

1. **Wellbeing experts reads its decision trail.** Each `verified`, `declined` and `unverified` row is a decision: Verified (approved), Declined (declined, its reason shown to the expert) and Verification removed (other). A hide or restore is the directory's visibility, not a decision on the verification, and is not listed; nor is a sample expert (`seed_`), as on the board. The trail, not `experts.decided_*`, is read: a credential change clears the current fields and keeps the trail, so a later state change no longer erases a decision. The lane's `since` is 2026-10-26: an expert verified before migration 577 has no actor or time, and the lane says so.
2. **Publishing is not a review.** The publish route writes `published_at` and the new `published_by_user_id`, and leaves `reviewed_by_user_id` and `reviewed_at` as the approve route wrote them. Decided lists a pitch under its approval when it is approved, rejected, withdrawn after approval (only an approved pitch can be withdrawn with a review on it), or published since the fix (`published_by_user_id` set). A pitch published before the fix holds its publisher in the review columns; it is left out, and the lane's caveat says so, rather than showing its publish as its approval. The board's one-pitch read still answers a published pitch with its publisher and publish time.
3. **Due diligence reads migration 620's columns**: a decision (Proceed and Proceed with conditions approve, Do not proceed declines, Withdrawn is other) and an archive (other) are each a row. Deciding a case again keeps only the latest decision, and the reason is ciphertext, so the log shows no note; the caveat says both.
4. **KYB reads `kyb_case_events`**: approved, rejected, documents requested (other) and import dismissed (other). An owners edit, a document upload or a link is not a decision. Its item id is the inbox's (`kyb_businesses.id`) and its scope is the inbox lane's: this studio's cases (`studio_code`, bound from `BRANCH_CODE` like the inbox source's `bindings`), no licensed studio, no case linked to an admin. A Decided lane can now carry `bindings`, bound ahead of the filter's values in the log and the weekly series.
5. **Program proposals opens its page.** The Lanes model's door is Programs › Proposals and `HqDoor` links `/admin/programs/proposals`.

Jobs, Best-Fit and Held AI runs still keep no decision time or decider and read "Not recorded" with their reason, never a zero. Best-Fit's history is package 2 of #4716; the lane registry is package 6.
