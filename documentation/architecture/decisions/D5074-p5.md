## D5074 part 5 — Use of funds' edit controls from the Worker's capability map (#5074)

Parts 1 and 2 are `D5074.md`; part 3 (`D5074-p3.md`) is the Investor narrative and part 4 (`D5074-p4.md`) Capital relationships. This part moves Use of funds onto the same pattern.

`cloudflare-worker/src/services/useOfFunds/capabilities.ts` computes Use of funds' capabilities once, from who the caller is on the venture (`uofStanding`: admin, the venture's founder account, and whether the token is a support session):

| Capability | Founder (own session) | Admin | Support session | What it governs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `edit` | yes | yes | no | every plan write (any method but GET): the split, scenarios and make-base, milestones, hires, the round size (`support_read_only` otherwise) |
| `mercury` | yes | no | no | the founder's own Mercury figures: runway today, actual spend, cash over time |
| `admin_logged` | no | yes | as the account it acts as | the caller's changes are logged under an admin's name (the UF16 banner) |

- **Who opens the plan is unchanged.** The shared Raise gate (`canWrite`: the venture's founder or an admin) still admits the caller. Anyone else, such as another founder, an investor or an advisor, is refused 403 `forbidden` with no map.
- **The read carries it; the writes ask the same function.** `routes/cycle_use_of_funds.ts` builds the map once, in its middleware. It refuses every write outside `edit`, sets the scope's read-only flag and the Mercury read from `edit` and `mercury`, and returns the map on `GET /plan` as `capabilities`. The plan's older `viewer` field still rides beside it, derived from the same standing, for any other reader; the page no longer reads it.
- **The page renders from it.** `uofViewer(capabilities)` (`useOfFundsModel.js`) reads only the map and keeps the page's words (`kind`, `can_write`, `mercury`), and `planViewer(plan)` is the one way a page asks. Every edit control (Edit split, Add milestone, a hire's Edit, Choose the round size, a Needs-you action), the admin banner, the split dialog's admin line, and the Mercury states (Founder only, actual spend, cash over time) read it. When the map and the old field disagree, the map wins. No map allows nothing.
- **The venture comes from the route helpers.** `tabSearch` is `ventureSearch(ventureRef(search))`. `useOfFundsPaths.js` and `UseOfFundsMovedRedirect` stay the input-compatibility redirects.
- **Tests.**
  - `cloudflare-worker/test/uof_capabilities_5074.test.ts` checks the pure map for every standing and pins the frontend mirror (`frontend/test/_uof_capabilities.mjs`) to it. Over HTTP:
    - The founder's and an admin's plan carry their map, and a write lands.
    - A support session acting as the founder or as an admin gets `edit` and `mercury` false. Its scenario and split writes are refused `support_read_only`, and nothing is written.
    - Another founder and an investor read no map.
    - A source scan pins the middleware to the map.
  - `frontend/test/uof_capabilities_5074.test.mjs` checks the pure view, the link form, and that no Use of funds file reads `plan.viewer` or the account, or parses `project_id || project`.
  - In `use_of_funds_tool_d1177`, the plan fixture serves the map the Worker sends each viewer. A mounted case checks Edit split and the admin banner for the founder's, an admin's and a support session's map, and with no map, while the old field says otherwise.

**With D6084's Export.** The plan read's `viewer.capabilities` (`export`, `add_to_data_room`, #6084) depend on the plan's data, not only on who the caller is, so `GET /plan` adds them to the caller's map: `capabilities` is the standing's three keys plus those two. The page reads that one map, the Export menu included; the old `viewer` field keeps its copy for any other reader.
