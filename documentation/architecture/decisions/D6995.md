## D6995 — Each indexable page ships its heading, offer and links before JavaScript runs (#6995)

**Decision date:** 2026-11-22. **Scope:** the build's prerender step and the Open Graph registry. No Worker,
route, `wrangler.toml` or `[assets]` change. Part of #6976 (plan key X13). Builds on #6988 (X6, PR #7028).

### What changed

Before this, every sitemap URL answered 200 with its own title and canonical but the same body: one "Axal VC"
heading, a fallback sentence and links to Privacy and Terms. The page's content appeared only after JavaScript.

- **The registry.** Each entry in `frontend/src/lib/ogRegistry.js` may carry `heading` (the page's own h1),
  `summary` (one or two sentences of its offer, in the page's words) and `primaryLink` (`{ label, to }`, its main
  action). `staticBodyFor(path, overrides)` reads them. A route with none uses its `title` and `description`; a
  program page's overrides win, as they do in `ogTagsFor`. The default action is Join Axal VC, `/register`.
- **Where a page keeps its words in a constant, the registry imports it.** The home hero's headline, offer and
  action now live in `frontend/src/lib/siteHome.js`, which `LandingPage.jsx` draws and the registry imports. The
  four door pages come from `lib/siteDoors.js` (`h1`, `who`, `primary`). Both load in plain Node, which the
  prerender needs.
- **Copies are held to their source.** `sitePricing.js` and `siteNetwork2.js` import without a `.js` specifier,
  and `ProgramsIndexPage.jsx` is a React page, so plain Node cannot load them. The registry holds a copy of
  their words, and `frontend/test/initial_html_6995.test.mjs` compares the copy with the constant. Pages whose
  hero text is written inline in JSX (Demo, Firm, Team, Methodology, Platform, Administrators, Intelligence,
  Contact, Newsletter, Articles, Directory, Resources, Changelog, Status) are checked by finding the registry's
  heading and offer in the page's source.
- **The prerender.** `scripts/prerender-og.mjs` writes, between `<!-- BEGIN static-body -->` and
  `<!-- END static-body -->` inside `#root`:
  - the header's role and Pricing links (`DOOR_LINKS` from `lib/siteLinks.js`, plus `/pricing`);
  - the heading as the page's one `h1`;
  - the offer and the primary link;
  - the Privacy and Terms links.

  The markers stay in the output, so a second run replaces the block again and converges.
- **Replaced, not appended.** `createRoot(document.getElementById('root')).render(...)` replaces everything
  inside `#root` when the app boots. The block is never added to the page React draws, so no second copy appears
  below the page.

### What a route without its own file shows

The static-assets fallback serves `docs/index.html` for every path that has no file of its own, and that file
is the prerendered `/`. An article (`/articles/<slug>`), an author page or an app route therefore shows the home
page's block until JavaScript runs, as it already showed the home page's head tags. There is no build-time list
of published articles, so no article gets its own title and first paragraph here. Server-side rendering is out
of scope (#6995).

### Not here

Structured data (#7004, X22). New copy: the block follows the page's words and picks up #7006 (B2) when it lands.

### Rollback

Revert the PR. The shell's fallback block is unchanged apart from its two markers, so a reverted prerender serves
the previous body.
