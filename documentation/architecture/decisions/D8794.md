## D8794 — Trust: the 90-day incident history needs 448 px and widens the phone page once it loads (#8794)

- **Status:** browser retest and record by S21 for #8794, part of the browser-audit handoff #8795. Nothing is built: the containment is a layout decision, so `needs-design` stays.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `d26a93a25`, SPA built locally from `frontend/src` and served from `docs/`. `GET /api/public/status` was answered in the browser by a fictional service history, and every other read was refused, so those parts drew Unreadable. No production.
- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** unchanged.

### 1. Retest (2026-11-27)

- **Browser:** Chromium 1243 (Playwright 1.64.1) at 1440, 768, 390 and 320 px, light and dark, on `/trust`.
- **Three reads of the status history:**
  - **loading:** the response held back until the first measurement;
  - **partial:** 90 days, the first 60 before records began (`not_recorded`) and 30 recorded;
  - **complete:** 90 recorded days, a few with an incident.

| Settled document width | 1440 | 768 | 390 | 320 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| While the history loads | 1440 | 768 | 390 | 320 |
| Partial history (60 unrecorded + 30 recorded) | 1440 | 768 | **483** | **483** |
| Complete 90-day history | 1440 | 768 | **483** | **483** |

| The strip at each width | 1440 | 768 | 390 | 320 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Status card (left – right) | 200 – 1240 | 30 – 738 | 16 – 374 | 16 – 304 |
| Strip client width / content width | 1002 / 1002 | 670 / 670 | 320 / **448** | 250 / **448** |
| Right edge of the last day mark | 667 | 497 | **483** | **483** |

Dark measured the same as light.

- **Reproduced:** the defect #8794 reports, exactly: the document grows from 390 to 483 px once the history arrives, and from 320 to 483 at 320 px.
  - **Partial history overflows as much as complete history.** An unrecorded day is still drawn as a mark (a dashed outline), which is right: it keeps the meaning of the days before records began.
- **Cause:** `StatusStrip` in `frontend/src/pages/site/TrustCenter.jsx` draws each day as `inline-block h-6 w-[3px] flex-none` inside `flex gap-[2px]`.
  - 90 × 3 px + 89 × 2 px = **448 px** at every width, because `flex-none` stops the marks shrinking, and nothing contains the row.
  - The last mark ends at 16 (card left) + 19 (padding) + 448 = 483 px.
- **What still fits at 390 and 320:**
  - the count line ("Days with no incident reported: 29 of 30 days recorded"), which wraps;
  - the four-entry legend (No incident reported, Incident reported, Outage reported, Not recorded), which wraps;
  - the "Status page ›" link.
- **Text without colour or pointer:**
  - the strip is `role="img"`, and its `aria-label` is the summary: "Incident history: no incident reported on 29 of 30 days recorded; the 60 days before records began are not recorded.";
  - each day's own status is only in a `title` tooltip, reachable by pointer alone.
- **Trust tabs, checked separately:**
  - they are links in their own horizontally scrolling strip;
  - Tab reached Disclosure as the 9th stop at 390 and 320 (15th at 1440), and focus scrolled the strip locally (`scrollLeft` 244 at 390, 314 at 320);
  - Enter opened `/trust/disclosure`, which settled at 390 and 320, since it has no status strip.

### 2. What changed

Nothing in the page. `frontend/test/trust_incident_history_8794.test.mjs` (4) pins what the canvas must keep, and the geometry that overflows today:
- loading and unreadable draw no marks;
- partial history keeps its 60 unrecorded marks and says so in words;
- complete history's summary;
- the 3 px / 2 px / `flex-none` strip, 448 px for 90 days, wider than the 320 px and 250 px content boxes it sits in.

The build to the canvas changes that last check on purpose.

### 3. What waits on the canvas

How 90 days fit a 250 – 320 px card:
- narrower marks;
- marks that shrink to the card;
- fewer days on a phone (30, with a link to the full history);
- or a labelled, deliberately scrollable region that never widens the document.

The canvas also decides whether a day's status is reachable without a pointer, and the card gutters at 320, 390 and desktop.

### 4. questions:

- **Route:** `/trust`, the Trust overview. Signed out it is drawn on its own (`<TrustCenter />`); signed in, the same page is drawn inside the member's shell (`<TrustCenter inShell />`). The retest measured the signed-out page.
- **Who sees it:** every visitor.
- **The parts and their stores:**
  - **Status card:** `GET /api/public/status` (D1076), one cell per day across the monitored services, the worst of them that day (`statusStrip` in `frontend/src/pages/site/trustCenterModel.js`).
    - Its title is "Status", or "Status · records began on <day>" while unrecorded days are shown.
    - Then the count line, the legend and the "Status page ›" link to `/status`.
  - The overview's other cards come from `trustPublicApi.read()` and `trustPublicApi.matrix()`.
  - The Trust tabs: Overview, Security, Data and privacy, Subprocessors, Disclosure, Documents.
- **States to draw:**
  - **loading:** "Reading…" bars, and the page at its final width;
  - **Unreadable:** "The status history: …" with Try again;
  - **none:** "No monitored service keeps a history yet.";
  - **Not recorded:** partial history, the first 60 of 90 days before records began, drawn as dashed marks and named in the summary;
  - **complete:** 90 recorded days with some incidents and an outage;
  - each at 320, 390 and desktop.
- **Questions for the owner:**
  1. On a phone, should the strip show all 90 days (thinner marks, or a scrolling region), or the last 30 with a link to the full history on `/status`?
  2. Should a single day's status be reachable by keyboard and touch (today it is a pointer-only tooltip), or is the written summary enough?
  3. Does an unrecorded day keep its dashed mark at a small size, or become one labelled span ("60 days before records began")?
  4. Should desktop keep its 90 individual marks as they are now (448 px inside a 1002 px card)?
