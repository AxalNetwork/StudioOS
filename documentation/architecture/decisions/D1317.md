## D1317 — Every detail panel is one right-side Sheet, docked beside the Eadwyn rail

S21, 2026-10-27, from the owner's instruction of the same day. No migration. Basis:
`main` at `25b5fa6acb`, with #3477 (D1315) merged in for Account › Builder.

### 1. What the owner asked

Every detail panel in Axal VC opens as a right-side sheet docked beside the Eadwyn
rail. The model is Grow › Publish to… in the "Publish to… and sent history" canvas.

### 2. The sheet (`frontend/src/ui/Sheet.jsx`)

- **Position.**
  - It opens over the current page, full height.
  - Its right edge meets the rail's left edge.
  - While a sheet is open, the rail draws its 44px spine, undimmed and clickable.
  - On a page with no rail (Account › Builder), the sheet meets the window's edge.
- **The dock is CSS.**
  - A mounted `WorkerRail` sets `data-eadwyn-rail="docked"` on the root.
  - `sheet.css` sets `--axs-dock: 44px` at 1024px and up, the spine's own width
    (`--fwr-track: 44px`).
  - The sheet's layer and its scrim stop at the dock, so the scrim never covers the rail.
- **Width.**
  - Each panel keeps its own width: 480, 520, 560, 640, 720 or 760px.
  - The cap is `max-width: calc(100vw - 108px)`.
  - A panel whose width is off that list takes the nearest width on it, with a tie
    going wider (`sheetWidth`): 600 and 620 become 640, 680 becomes 720, and 860, 940
    and 960 become 760.
- **Moving a `Lightbox` drawer is a change of tag.**
  - `Sheet` takes the same props.
  - `size="tall"`: the body does not scroll; the panel's child owns the scroll.
  - `pageBelow="lg"`: below 1024px the panel stays the page it was, drawn by
    `Lightbox`'s page mode.
  - `phone`: the panel's own phone form. A page's `LightboxPhoneProvider` applies too.
- **Look.**
  - Card ground.
  - A 1px hairline on the left only: `#ececf1`, or `#374151` in dark mode.
  - A 16px radius on the left corners only.
  - Shadow `-24px 0 56px rgba(9,9,11,.2)`.
- **Layout.** The header (eyebrow, title, ×) and the footer stay fixed. Only the body
  scrolls.
- **Motion.**
  - It slides in from the rail's edge in 200ms.
  - Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it shows with no motion.
  - The slide leaves no transform behind, so a confirmation opened inside the sheet
    is not clipped.
- **Accessibility.** It keeps `Lightbox`'s contract (D685):
  - `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal`;
  - named by its title, through `aria-labelledby`;
  - focus moves in on open, stays trapped, and returns to the opener on close;
  - Escape closes it, on the same stack as `Lightbox`, so a confirmation over a
    sheet closes first;
  - the scrim and the × close it;
  - the page behind does not scroll and is inert, **except the rail**
    (`[data-sheet-keep]`; `inertOutside` gains `keep`).
- **The rail.**
  - `sheetStore.js` tells `WorkerRail` a sheet is open.
  - The rail draws its spine without touching the member's stored choice.
  - The spine's button closes the open sheets before it opens the rail.
- **Phones.** Below 640px a sheet is a full-screen page, as the panels were. With
  `phone="sheet"` it is a bottom sheet instead.
- **Addresses.**
  - A panel with an address is mounted by its list page while the address names a
    record. Examples: `/admin/accounts/all/[id]`, `/account/builder/assistants/[id]`,
    `/admin/platform/payments/products/[id]`.
  - Its `onClose` navigates back to the list's address.
  - The sheet makes no route or server call of its own.

### 3. What does not change

- Confirmation and step-up dialogs stay centred (`Lightbox`).
- Pickers and editors that are lightboxes by design stay centred:
  - the pitch deck block picker;
  - the chart library;
  - the image generator.
- Phone layouts under 640px.
- The left sidebar, and the rail's own open and collapsed states.

### 4. Where

Every detail panel is moved onto `Sheet`. The list of components moved is in the PR.

### 5. Tests

`frontend/test/sheet_d1317.test.mjs` covers:

- **The frame:** its name, the width cap, the look and the dark pair.
- **Scrolling:** only the body scrolls; the header and the footer are fixed.
- **The dock and motion:** the dock, the reduced-motion rule and the phone forms.
- **The rail:** its wiring, and that it stays live while everything else is inert.
- **The store:** the spine closes the top sheet first.
- **Opening and closing:**
  - focus moves in;
  - the scroll lock;
  - Escape, including one already handled;
  - the scrim and the ×;
  - focus returns to the opener.
- **A deep link:** `/account/builder/keys/k7f3` opens the sheet over API keys, and
  closing it returns to `/account/builder/keys`.

Each moved panel's own test checks that it is a `Sheet`, at its width.
