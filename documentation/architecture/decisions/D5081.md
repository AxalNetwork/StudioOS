## D5081 — Raise tools own their pages, part 1: Use of funds (#5081)

- **Status:** Part 1 built, 2026-11-15 (frontend only; no Worker change, no migration, no new route). Capital relationships (RA-22), Cap table (RA-20), Data room (RA-19) and the Raise navigation (RA-12) are later parts of #5081.
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Raise v2 · Boards.dc.html` RA-14 (framework-layer cleanup, before and after) and RA-21 A and B (Use of funds); `design/incoming/Raise v2 Page.dc.html` (`cleanup()`, `uof()`). Released by D4863.
- **Builds on:** D1173, D1177, D1178 (the tool), D6081 (Committed from the round, Remaining only beside a read Committed; reused unchanged), D5076 (the zero state's first action is the page's one primary), D4484 (a module may replace its cycle's primary).

### What was wrong

`/raise/use-of-funds` drew the tool's Home (Raise, Runway today, Runway after the raise, Committed, where the money goes) and then CycleModulePage's generic layer under it: "Use of funds · at a glance" (the Worker's Next 18 months card and its Edit split step), the Open the forecast door and the Categories records table with its own Edit split. The same figures appeared twice and there were two ways to edit them. The header's primary action was Raise's global Build your deck. On first use Home drew four tiles reading Not recorded and an empty bar beside its one prompt. The cash card said "Without the raise is not modelled yet."

### The decision

1. **The tool owns its page.** `CycleModulePage` and `PhoneModulePage` take `owned` (`{ sub }`). With it, the page draws its header, the tool's `subnav` (the tab bar and Home) and the module's own parts (From Mercury, Evidence, Eadwyn), and nothing generic: no "at a glance", no tool doors, no records, and on a phone no One next thing. CycleRoute passes it on Use of funds' Home only; every other module is unchanged. The URL is unchanged. The Categories rows duplicate Home's section table and Allocation, and the Next 18 months card duplicates Cash over time, so neither is kept. The one thing the card said that Home did not (cash without the raise) is the runway today figure, and the cash card now says so in place of "not modelled yet".
2. **The primary action is the module's own.** On Home it is Set raise & allocation, which opens Edit split in place (RA-14's table, RA-21's header). CycleRoute holds Home's plan read and open dialog (`useUseOfFundsHome`, in the small `useOfFundsRead.js` so the tool's step stays lazy, D4574), so the header and Home draw from one read and open one dialog. `homeAction` gives no action while the plan is unread or unreadable, for a viewer who cannot write, and on first use, where the empty state's first action is the page's one primary (D5076). On a phone the header is the bar, so Home draws the action full width. On the tabs, `raise.actions['use-of-funds']` replaces Build your deck with Set raise & allocation opening Allocation; on Allocation the tab's own Edit split is the action and the header has none.
3. **Home answers "What does this round buy us?"** (the header's line). Where the money goes is renamed What the raise buys. Home adds Runway (today Recorded from its measured source, after the raise Modelled, and the target), Dependencies and Milestones funded.
4. **Each dependency is marked from the plan read** (`dependencyRows`), in RA-23's words:

   | Row | Read | Current | Stale | Unavailable | Not linked |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | Round | `round` | a chosen size, no disagreement | recorded round sizes differ | no `round` block | no size recorded |
   | Capital relationships | `committed` (D6081) | read | — | unavailable or missing | no active round (links Commitments) |
   | Cap table | `ties.cap_table` | same raise as the plan | a different raise (names both) | unreadable | no scenario, or no round amount |
   | Pitch deck | `ties.deck` | Ask slide marked in sync | not marked in sync | unreadable | no deck |
   | Mercury | `runway_today.sources.mercury` | a balance and burn read (its date) | — | unreadable | none recorded (links Connect Mercury) |

   A refused commitments read is Refused and Mercury for a non-founder is Founder only, never a figure. Committed Unavailable keeps D6081's notice and hides Remaining (RA-21 B).
5. **First use collapses to its set-up actions** (`setupActions`, at most three): Set raise & allocation for a writer, Connect Mercury when no runway today is measured and Mercury is the viewer's to connect, Open Commitments when no active round holds commitments. No tile, bar, card or table is drawn until a split is saved.

### What the boards draw that no read backs (reconciled against main)

Not built here, and nothing is made up in their place:

- **Readiness as a dependency** (S1's list). The plan read carries no readiness block. Worker follow-up: add the readiness score's state and age to `planRead`.
- **Hiring plan, Not linked** (RA-21 A). Hires are inside the plan; no external hiring plan exists to link.
- **Mercury Stale** and **Cap table "Stale · Changed 6 Nov"**. The read gives Mercury's `as_of` but no freshness rule, and no link between a Use of funds scenario and a cap-table scenario (`link_state: 'not_recorded'`, migration 606). Stale here means only that the two raises differ. Worker follow-ups: a freshness threshold for connector facts, and a stored scenario link with its change time.
- **"Target runway · Modelled" as a tile** (RA-21 A). The target is what the founder typed (`target_runway_months`, source Typed). It is shown in the Runway card with that source rather than relabelled Modelled.
- **Retry on the Committed notice** (RA-21 B). D6081's notice already re-reads the plan with Try again.
