## D1052 — Billing alerts (migration 578), the alerts read, grace while Stripe retries, and refunds and disputes on plans (#2903)

- **Status:** decided and built by S09, 2026-10-25 (#2903, part 4 of Checkout, #2451, from S21's integration in #2852, D1048). S1 reviews it for security.
- **Basis:** D892 (#2453), which tells the member of each failed renewal attempt and left access while Stripe retries to #2451's owner question 4.
- **Migration:** `578_billing_alerts.sql`.
- **Why:** D892 tells the member that a renewal failed, but:
  - access still ended at `past_due`;
  - nothing recorded that an alert was open, so no banner could show;
  - a bank's confirmation request (`invoice.payment_action_required`) was not handled;
  - a refunded or disputed plan payment was not recorded, and the member was not told.

### 1. Grace while Stripe retries (owner question 4, as #2453 proposed)

`past_due` is a renewal Stripe is still retrying. Until the owner answers question 4, a plan in `past_due` keeps its access. `unpaid` is Stripe giving up, and `cancelled` is the plan's end: both still drop it.

**What changed:**

| Check | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `userMeetsTier` (`middleware/requireTier.ts`; also `aiLicenceKey`, the MCP registry's plan gate) | false at `past_due` or `unpaid` | false at `unpaid` |
| `effectiveInvestorTier` (`middleware/requireInvestorTier.ts`) | free at `past_due`, `unpaid` or `cancelled` | free at `unpaid` or `cancelled` |
| The investor intro allowance (`services/introductions.ts`) and seats (`routes/investor_seats.ts`) | lapsed at `past_due` | lapsed at `unpaid` or `cancelled` |
| MI Pro (`middleware/miAccess.ts`) | `active` or `trialing` | and `past_due` |
| The investor thesis paywall (`services/advisor/writeRouter.ts`) and the advisor tiers (`routes/advisor.ts`) | `active` | `active` or `past_due` |

**What did not change:**
- **Account plans gate nothing on the server** but the AI allowance. That allowance counts paid periods only (`services/planAllowance.ts`, #2124's question 2): a `past_due` period has not been paid, so it gives no allowance. The plan's other access is the client's, from `/api/billing/plan/status`, which already reports the status as Stripe sends it.
- **Reporting** still counts a `past_due` MI subscription with the lapsed ones (`services/analyticsReports.ts`), because those reports read churn risk, not access.

**The pins:** D629, D594, D632, D846 and the investor intro-allowance test held today's rule. They now hold the new one, and each has an `unpaid` case that still drops the plan. D892's own pin now says owner question 4 is answered as proposed.

### 2. The table (migration 578)

**`billing_alerts`:** one row per thing the member should know about.

| Column | Holds |
|---|---|
| `user_id` | the member |
| `kind` | the kind of alert, below |
| `state` | `open` or `closed` |
| `object_id` | the Stripe object the alert is about |
| `subscription_id`, `invoice_id` | the subscription and invoice |
| `plan_family` | from migration 505 |
| `attempt_count`, `next_attempt_at` | the renewal's attempt and next try |
| `amount_cents`, `currency` | the amount |
| `closed_reason`, `closed_at` | why and when it closed |

**The four kinds:**

| Kind | Opened by | `object_id` | Closed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `payment_failed` | `invoice.payment_failed` on a renewal | the invoice | `invoice.paid` ("paid"); the subscription active or trialing again ("recovered"); canceled, `incomplete_expired` or deleted ("ended") |
| `needs_confirmation` | `invoice.payment_action_required` on a renewal | the invoice | the same |
| `refund` | `charge.refunded` on a plan payment (written closed, "refunded") | the charge | (nothing left to do) |
| `dispute` | `charge.dispute.created` on a plan payment | the dispute | `charge.dispute.closed`, with Stripe's outcome (won, lost, …) |

**A renewal** is an invoice whose `billing_reason` is `subscription_cycle`, `subscription_update` or `subscription_threshold`. A first payment is the card form's to show. An `unpaid` subscription keeps its `payment_failed` alert open and clears its next try: Stripe has stopped retrying.

**The rules on the table:**
- **Never on `users`**, which is at D1's column limit.
- **No card data.** The columns hold ids, counts, dates and an amount. The test checks that no column names a card or a secret.
- **Idempotent on the event object's id.** `(kind, object_id)` is unique, so a redelivered event updates the row it wrote, or does nothing:
  - an attempt moves forward and never back;
  - a refund's total only grows;
  - a closed row is never reopened by a late event.
- **Every statement is bound.**

### 3. The webhook

Each case runs only after signature verification, in `handleStripeEvent` (`routes/billing.ts`). The writes are `services/billingAlerts.ts`.

- **`invoice.payment_failed`** opens or moves the alert. D892's notice still goes once per attempt.
- **`invoice.payment_action_required`** opens a `needs_confirmation` alert with the invoice id. Nothing is fetched from Stripe when it is written.
- **`invoice.paid`** closes the invoice's alerts.
- **`customer.subscription.updated` and `.deleted`** sync the subscription's alerts. This runs before the switch, because the plan cases inside it return early.
- **`charge.refunded`, `charge.dispute.created` and `.closed`** go to D755's token-pack reversal first. A charge that is no pack's is traced to its plan:
  - charge → invoice → subscription → member;
  - Stripe is read for the invoice, and for a dispute's charge, when the event does not carry them;
  - a charge with no invoice, or a subscription no member owns, is left alone.

  The member is told in the app and by email (owner question 9):
  - a refund, each time its total grows ("A plan payment was refunded", or "Part of a plan payment was refunded");
  - a dispute, once.

  A Stripe read that fails throws, so the webhook answers non-2xx and Stripe retries. With no Stripe key nothing is traced and nothing is guessed.
- **Before migration 578** every alert write is skipped, and the rest of the webhook runs as it did.

### 4. The reads

- **`GET /api/billing/alerts`** returns the caller's open alerts, newest first, at most 20. Each alert carries:
  - its id, kind and plan family;
  - the attempt and the next try;
  - the amount and currency;
  - `can_confirm`;
  - when it was opened and last updated.

  No Stripe id and no card data is in the answer, and it is not cached (`private, no-store`). Before 578 the list is empty.
- **`POST /api/billing/alerts/:id/confirm`** is Confirm pressed. It reads the caller's own open `needs_confirmation` alert, then fetches its invoice's payment intent from Stripe, now, and answers `{ client_secret, payment_intent }` for Stripe.js to finish the bank's check. The secret is not stored.
  - Another member's alert, or an alert of another kind, is 404 and Stripe is not asked.
  - A payment that no longer needs the member is 409 `confirmation_not_needed`.
- **The client** is `frontend/src/lib/api/billingAlerts.js` (`billingAlertsApi.list`, `.confirm`), re-exported by `api.js`. The banner belongs to a later part of #2451.

### 5. Verified

- `cloudflare-worker/test/billing_alerts_d1052.test.ts` has 9 tests through the real webhook route, with Stripe stubbed:
  - migration 578's shape;
  - a failed renewal's alert per attempt, with D892's notice unchanged;
  - no alert for a first payment, a stranger's subscription or a malformed id;
  - paid, recovered, `unpaid` and ended;
  - an account plan's alert closed through its early-returning case;
  - the read: the caller's own alerts, open only, no card data, not cached, empty before 578;
  - Confirm: fetched only when pressed, the caller's own alert only, not stored, 409 when not needed;
  - a refund told once per total, a non-plan charge left alone, and a Stripe failure retried;
  - a dispute told once and closed with its first outcome;
  - grace in every gate above.
- The updated pins pass: D629, D594, D632, D846, the investor intro allowance and D892.
- **Mutation checks:** 30 mutants across the alerts service, the webhook wiring and the three middleware gates.
  - 29 are killed. Two of those first survived and are killed by the assertions added for them: a deletion whose status is not `canceled`, and a dispute with no Stripe key answering 200.
  - One is equivalent and survives: skipping the token-pack reversal's early return. A token pack's charge carries no invoice, so the plan trace that follows finds nothing.
