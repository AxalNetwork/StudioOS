## D1511 — Deck v2 test infrastructure: the fake AI binding, contract schemas, property tests, visual regression and axe

- **Status:** built by the S26 session (#3762, deck v2 A18, 2026-11-01). Test infrastructure and CI only, plus four fixes that the new suites found (see "What the suites found"). Draft PR.
- **Spec:** the issue's "Final canvas revision (2026-11-01)", which is S1's [comment 6550937313](https://github.com/AxalNetwork/StudioOS/issues/3762#issuecomment-6550937313). S21's [comment 6558489932](https://github.com/AxalNetwork/StudioOS/issues/3762#issuecomment-6558489932) adds two points: A2 (#3749) is on main with 4:3, so the matrix has two aspects; and the theme axis reads `BASE_THEMES` instead of a fixed count.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1499 (#3749): The Grid, and `GET /api/decks/:id/grid`;
  - D1554 (#3801): the 4:3 reflow;
  - D1604 (#3885): the v2 editor screens and `deckV2Adapter.js`;
  - D897 (New deck, `POST /api/decks/generate`);
  - D761 and D763: the router's fallbacks, timeouts and guard.
- **Migration:** none. No route changes, so `ROUTE_MAP.md` is unchanged.

### Why

Every Grid task needs four things to test against:
- an AI binding that can fail on purpose;
- a contract for each route the v2 adapter swaps onto;
- properties of the Grid models that hold for any input, not just a few examples;
- a picture and an accessibility scan of the editor itself.

Before this, `test:drift` checked only that a route existed. Nothing drew the editor, and nothing ran axe on it.

### What was decided

1. **One fake AI binding, `cloudflare-worker/test/_fake_ai.mjs`.**
   - It answers from recorded outputs in `cloudflare-worker/test/fixtures/fake_ai_recordings.json`. Each recording is matched by model and by a prompt pattern.
   - A call that nothing recorded **throws**, so a test can never pass on an answer nobody wrote down.
   - `inject(kind, { model, times })` arms a failure, in the shape the router reads:
     - `rate_limit_429` throws with `status: 429`;
     - `capacity_3040` throws "3040: Capacity temporarily exceeded";
     - `timeout` never settles, so the router's own limit cuts it (8 s for chat; the test drives `t.mock.timers`);
     - `guard_block` makes Llama Guard answer `unsafe` with `S1`;
     - `bad_json` answers text that is not JSON;
     - `over_capacity_text` returns the recorded answer with every string repeated 40 times;
     - `non_image` makes an image model answer with text.
   - The guard's own calls are logged apart (`guardCalls`), so `calls` is the test's runs.
2. **The one-click run happens on the fake binding.**
   - `fake_ai_d1511.test.ts` runs the build as `OneClickBuild.jsx` does: the brief from `GET /api/decks/new/context` (the inferred `pre_seed_fund`, `short`), then `POST /api/decks/new/quote`, then `POST /api/decks/generate`, then Accept (`keep-slides`, `all`, `save`).
   - It runs over the real migrated schema, seeded by `fixtures/oneClickVenture.ts`: a synthetic venture whose records fill every card, Mercury facts included.
   - Every check reads the route's own reply:
     - **0 "Not recorded" placeholders:** no card in `proposal.slides` holds the text, or a binding graded so. Every `progress[].missing` is empty.
     - **0 fit or overflow problems:** the reply carries no fit list. The test runs the Worker's own `fitProblems` (`services/decks/designSystem.ts`, the check `buildCards` itself applies) on the non-appendix cards of `proposal.slides`. This is the one measure that is computed, not read: the route refuses a deck with fit problems (500 `cards_unsound`) and never lists them.
     - **A quote at least the settled charge:** `hold_microusd` from the quote is compared with `cost.charged_microusd` from the build. The test also checks that the charge equals the usage row's, and that the quote equals the hold the run reserved.
   - The same bar is checked for every audience × every length (21 builds) on the same records.
   - The AUTO-1 autopilot has not landed, so it is not driven here. Its tests can call the same `world()` and `assertBar`.
3. **Each injected failure runs through the real router and route:**
   - **One 429 or 3040:** the router moves down the chain, and the sibling model builds the deck.
   - **Every model failing:** the build gives the figures-only deck at no charge, with the reason.
   - **A timeout:** cut at 8 s of mocked time, not before; then the sibling builds the deck.
   - **A guard block:** 422 `guard_blocked`. No model runs, nothing is written and nothing is charged.
   - **Bad JSON:** the figures-only deck. It says the words could not be read, and nothing from the broken answer reaches a card.
   - **Over-capacity text:** no card overflows, and every heading is at most `HEADLINE_MAX`.
   - **A non-image answer to a drawing:** `all_models_failed` with "unexpected image response". The recorded PNG draws at its real size.
4. **Contract schemas live in `cloudflare-worker/src/contracts/`, and `test:drift` validates real replies against them.**
   - When this was started, `SWAPPED` held one method, `grid`. While it was built, main swapped five more over three merges: `stagePlan` (D1522) and `remix` (D1553); then `investorLinks` and `withdrawInvestorLink` (D1565); then `elementModels` (D1503). Each time, the test below failed on the merge until they had schemas too. That is the check doing its job. So there are six schemas:
     - `deckGrid.schema.json`: `GET /api/decks/:id/grid`, with or without `?aspect=`;
     - `deckStagePlan.schema.json`: `GET /api/decks/:id/stage-plan`, with and without `stage` and `investor_type`;
     - `deckRemix.schema.json`: `POST /api/decks/:id/remix`, with and without `remove`;
     - `deckElementModels.schema.json`: `GET /api/decks/:id/element-models`, with a seeded deck pick so one element answers a pick and a `deck_element` source;
     - `deckInvestorLinks.schema.json`: `GET /api/decks/:id/investor-links`, over three seeded rows per deck: an accepted NDA with views, a variant with no cap or expiry, and a withdrawn link;
     - `deckWithdrawInvestorLink.schema.json`: `POST …/investor-links/:shareId/withdraw`, the first press and a repeated one. Its link definition is the list's, and a test holds the two equal.
   - Every object the routes build is closed, and each schema is tagged with `x-adapter`, `x-issue` and `x-route`. The remix's `card`s are the slide model a save keeps (`sanitizeModel`, tested on its own). Their schema holds only what the remix screen reads: blocks on whole cells.
   - `contracts_d1511.test.ts` (in `test:worker`, so in `test:drift`) does three things:
     - it reads `SWAPPED` and the `SURFACES` issues from `deckV2Adapter.js`, and fails on a swap with no schema, or a schema with no swap;
     - it runs the **real Worker** (`src/index.ts`) over the migrated schema, for each method's request forms on three decks: pre-v2, every structure, and empty at 4:3;
     - it validates all 33 replies, and shows that each schema refuses a reply that drifts.
   - The validator is in-repo, `cloudflare-worker/test/_json_schema.mjs`, with no new dependency. It throws on any keyword it does not support, so a schema cannot rely on a check that is silently skipped.
5. **Property tests on a seeded PRNG** (mulberry32, seed `0x1511`, no dependency), in `frontend/test/grid_properties_d1511.test.mjs`. A failure names its case and seed. They check the Worker's `structures.ts` and `reflow.ts` and the editor's `gridModel.js`, `deckReflow.js` and `gridSelection.js`:
   - **No overlap and whole cells:**
     - `areasProblem` and `sanitizeAreas` agree with a brute-force cell oracle;
     - the editor's `areasOf` never overlaps on any of the 17 structures;
     - without a structure, the editor's blocks and free ranges tile every cell exactly once.
   - **`deriveAreas`:** the result is sound, with one area per first non-overlapping block, in reading order, and it round-trips.
   - **Reflow maps:** at 4:3 each area keeps its top and height and loses only width. `width_kept` is the rounded ratio, and the frontend's boxes are the Worker's.
   - **Reflow:** the result fits at 4:3, and no word is lost (kept plus moved text is the original). A second reflow moves nothing, going wider moves nothing, and `reflowCards` equals `reflowSlides`.
   - **Op and inverse:**
     - `boxOf` and `cellAtPoint` / `nearestCell` are inverses;
     - a nudge followed by the opposite nudge restores the range;
     - shift-picking the same area twice changes nothing;
     - `spanOf` is symmetric, and the marquee selects only what it touches;
     - `withAspect` and `aspectOf` round-trip.
6. **Visual regression and axe run on the integrated editor at HEAD, in `frontend/tests/visual/`.**
   - **The server (`server.ts`)** serves the built `docs/` and answers every `/api/*` with the real Worker, over SQLite and the fake binding. Nothing is stubbed by path. It pins its clock, since the Worker dates "as of" and "out of date". It seeds:
     - one venture per theme × aspect, whose deck has one card per structure;
     - the one-click venture, which it builds and accepts at start.
   - **The axes are read, never counted:**
     - themes are `BASE_THEMES`' keys. There were three when this started; #3769 landed six while it was built, and the matrix grew to six on the merge with no spec change. The new cells only needed baselines;
     - aspects come from `ASPECTS`, and structures from `STRUCTURES` (17).
   - **The spec reads the theme keys from `designSystem.ts`'s source,** because the module imports JSON, which Playwright's loader rejects. Its first test holds that list to the server's, which imports the object itself.
   - **Baselines** (221, committed under `__screenshots__/`, 3.2 MB):
     - the matrix, 17 × 6 × 2 = 204 card faces;
     - the accepted one-click deck, 6 cards, in its picked theme. That is Neutral light, because there is no base version and no brand kit (`oneClickModel.themePick`), so Accept writes no theme;
     - the editor in light (pd0/pd3) and in dark (pd3-d);
     - the grid overlay;
     - two areas selected, with SELBAR and the instruction box (pd11);
     - the area lightbox (pd4), ⋯ (DeckMoreMenu) and the deck's Ask;
     - Studio home;
     - the tablet and phone widths (pd9l, pd9k).
   - **The comparison is exact:** `maxDiffPixels: 0`, `threshold: 0.05`. A looser 1 % let three real changes through (see the mutation table).
   - **axe** (`@axe-core/playwright`, a new frontend devDependency added by npm) runs WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rules over each editor state, and asserts zero serious or critical violations.
     - **States scanned:** at rest, the grid overlay, a keyboard selection, the lightbox, ⋯, the Ask, Studio home, dark, tablet and phone.
     - **Scope:** the editor's frame (`pitch-editor`: header, toolbar, Ask, strip, stage, instruction box and the AIRail) and every dialog or menu it opens.
     - **Keyboard path:** one tab stop on the stage; Space selects, Shift + arrow extends and Escape clears.
     - **Lightbox focus:** focus stays inside the dialog for 25 Tabs, and returns to its opener on Escape.
7. **Determinism.**
   - Fonts are the suite's own files: Inter, Roboto Mono and Space Grotesk (variable, latin, OFL), plus the repo's Public Sans and Bricolage. They are served in place of Google Fonts.
   - The three new font files are plain blobs, not LFS pointers, because the CI checkout fetches no LFS. `.gitattributes` and `scripts/lfs-size-gate.mjs` (`LFS_EXEMPT_PATHS`) say so, as they do for the legal paper's faces. The 500 KB size gate still applies to them.
   - Every other request that leaves 127.0.0.1 is aborted.
   - The run uses one worker and no retries, with reduced motion, UTC and a fixed viewport. Service workers are blocked, and both the page clock and the server clock are pinned.
8. **CI: the new `visual` job in `ci.yml`** runs the suites in the pinned image `mcr.microsoft.com/playwright:v1.56.1-noble`.
   - That image ships Chromium build 1194 (141.0.7390.37), which is the browser that drew the baselines, on Ubuntu 24.04 as here.
   - The runner is `@playwright/test` 1.64.1 from the lockfile, pointed at that browser by `PW_CHROMIUM_PATH`.
   - A step asserts the browser version first, so a changed image fails loudly instead of drawing different pixels.
   - On failure, the report and the diffs are uploaded.
   - The other three suites run in `test:drift`, which the `gate` job already runs.

### What the suites found, and the fixes

1. **The area lightbox printed "[object Object] per variant."**
   - `GET /api/ai/rail` answers each model's price as unit prices (`{ cost, billed }`, `routes/ai.ts` `unitPrices`). `areaLightboxModel.modelChip` expected a string.
   - D1604's own test used a string, a synthetic shape that differs from the route's reply. That is the drift these contracts exist for.
   - **Fix:** the chip prints the object as the AIRail does (`railModelPrice`), and keeps a string as before. `pitch_v2_lightbox_d1604.test.mjs` gains the real shape.
2. **axe `label-content-name-mismatch` (serious) on every Grid area.**
   - `GridStage` named an area button by its title alone (`aria-label`), so the block's visible words were not in its name (WCAG 2.5.3).
   - **Fix:** the title is now a hidden span beside each button (`pitch-editor-area-name-<id>`), and the button's own text is unchanged.
     - A text or figure block is named by its own words, and described by the title (`aria-describedby`).
     - Any other area is named by the title and then its own content (`aria-labelledby="<title> <self>"`), so an empty area or a chart still has a name.
     - Naming a text block the second way was tried first. axe still flagged a bulleted list, because the computed name joins list items without a space.
   - `pitch_v2_selection_d1604.test.mjs` now checks `aria-describedby` and that span instead of `aria-label`.
3. **axe `color-contrast` (serious) in the AIRail.**
   - `--d698-faint` (`#7d7889`) is 4.27:1 on white.
   - **Fix:** it is now `#716c7e`, which is 5.06:1 on white and at least 4.6:1 on the rail's grounds. The dark value is unchanged; it passes.
4. **The 4:3 reflow could leave a block that overflows (property test, after merging #3769).**
   - D1554's reflow keeps the words that fit a character count. D1517 (#3769) then made `fitProblems` check a balanced word wrap, stepping the type down first. A wrapped line can hold fewer characters than the count, so the reflow sometimes kept text that `fitProblems` calls one character over at 4:3. This broke D1554's own promise of zero overflow after a switch.
   - The reflow property failed on main's code at the first seeded case.
   - **Fix:** after the count, the reflow drops words until the wrapped fit holds. The Worker uses `fitText`. The editor's mirror (`deckReflow.js`) gets `fitsWrapped`, a port of D1517's wrap, and a new property holds it equal to `fitText`.
   - Text that already passed both checks reflows exactly as before. `deck_reflow_d1554` and `pitch_size_reflow_d1554` pass unchanged.
5. **Outside the scan's scope, not fixed here:** the app shell's company switcher (`switcher-trigger`) also fails `label-content-name-mismatch`. Its `aria-label` "Switch company or project" leaves out its visible "No company". The shell is not the editor and belongs to no deck issue, so it is reported here and not scanned.

- **M25 to M27** first passed under a 1 % / `threshold 0.2` comparison, which is why the comparison is now exact.

### Not done here, and why

- **The baselines were drawn in this session's container, not in CI.** The container's Chromium is build 1194 on Ubuntu 24.04, the browser the pinned image ships. This session has no Docker and cannot run GitHub Actions, so the first CI run of the `visual` job is the check that the two environments draw the same pixels. If they differ, the job's artifact holds every diff. The baselines are then redrawn in the image with `--update-snapshots` and reviewed.
- **The AUTO-1 autopilot is not driven:** it is not on main.
- **The staging load run against real Workers AI is out of scope,** as the issue says.
