## D4447 — Grow campaign plans, Part A (#4447)

Part of #3722. This records the specification's previously unwritten D1481,
with #4447's corrections. A campaign is a plan: these routes send nothing,
post nothing and move no money. Part B, Distribution tabs and New campaign
wait for DS1–DS11; owner questions A–G remain outside this change.

The additive UTC-minute migration creates `grow_campaigns`,
`grow_campaign_channels` and `grow_campaign_items`. Unset budgets are NULL,
with a Not recorded reason in reads; zero is only an explicit recorded amount.
UTM tags are trimmed and lowercase, unique within a venture including archived
campaigns. Dates are real calendar days, and the end cannot precede the start.
Campaigns start draft, then active, paused/active and ended; ended cannot
transition again. Archive retains links and refuses further writes.

`cycle_grow.ts` uses its existing project read and write gates. Advisors can
read; writers can change plans. Every linked segment, channel, content item
and publish draft must belong to this venture; missing and foreign identifiers
both return 404. Refusals retain `{ detail, field }`. Writes record the actor
and Distribution activity, without campaign or contact names in the log.
Creation and replacements use atomic batches. Snapshot checks refuse stale
campaign edits instead of overwriting an intervening change.

Spend sums recorded monthly channel spend between the start/end months,
inclusively. Missing months contribute no invented zero; no recorded month
returns null with a reason. Without both dates the window is Not recorded.
Leads count venture contacts whose parsed UTM campaign matches the normalized
tag, without a date filter; malformed attribution is ignored as by utmSource.
A list reads and groups contact attribution once per venture, shared by all
its campaigns, so adding campaigns does not multiply contact scans/parses.
Spend and contact read failures independently return null and Unreadable reasons.
No tag means leads are Not recorded, not zero. Item counts join their original
stores and venture, so deleted items do not count. The content-calendar
source boundary permits this reader and explicitly refuses calendar writes
from campaign plans.

The Grow cycle client exposes eight campaign methods in `cycleWrites.js`.
There is no new Grow module, page or UI and no new provider integration.
