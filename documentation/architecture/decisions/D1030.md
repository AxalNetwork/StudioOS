## D1030 — My events: Upcoming, Hosting, Discover and Past at their own addresses, open to Explorers, and the blocks three existing reads carry (#2387)

- **Status:** decided and built by S21, 2026-10-25 (#2387, under #2711).
- **Canvas:** the owner's "My events", MEV1–MEV18 with lettered boards (artifacts `FmbzyxCRunXN3NDxo2dC8P`, the board grid, and `NydozL4ZQeMNZ7PaC3XPg3`, the page). It is committed as `design/incoming/My Events · Boards.dc.html` and `My Events Page.dc.html`.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `154cd86cf5`. The map is `documentation/audits/MY_EVENTS_MAP_2026-10-25.md`.
- **Migration:** none here.
- **Why:** the owner asked for the members' Events page to be redesigned for every user (#2387). Today it is two tabs, Hosting and Attending, closed to Explorers, with no "what's next", no past and no discovery inside the shell. This decision is the integration: the four tabs at their own addresses, who sees which, and the blocks the existing reads carry. The rest is seven slot tasks (§6).

### 1. Where it lives

| Address | What it is |
|---|---|
| `/my/events` | Upcoming (MEV1, MEV2) |
| `/my/events/hosting` | Hosting (MEV4, MEV13) |
| `/my/events/discover` | Discover (MEV8) |
| `/my/events/past` | Past (MEV9) |

- **Every address takes one guard:** `guard(['admin', 'founder', 'partner', 'investor', 'advisor', 'exploring'], <MyEventsPage />)`. The page reads its tab from the address.
- **Explorers are in** (owner question 1, read as answered A: MEV2 draws an Explorer's Upcoming). They get Upcoming, Discover and Past, no Hosting tab and no New event, and the line "Hosting opens once your profile is set." with "Set up your profile". An Explorer at `/hosting` sees Upcoming.
- **Events stays in the account menu** (owner question 2, read as answered A: MEV1 draws the menu open and no row lit). `calendar_events_navigation` still holds.
- **Its own page** (owner question 3, read as A: the canvas is My events at `/my/events`).
- **The header:** "Events", the canvas's line, "Public calendar" and "My calendar", and New event (not for Explorers). The tab bar is a nav labelled "Events" of literal links; a count is a number from the read, or a dash while it is out or failed.
- **The drawers** (`/my/events/ticket/[id]`, `/my/events/hosting/[id]`) wait for part 5. A tab is not added before its feature (D953's rule).
- **The phone** keeps D700's A7m sections until part 7.

### 2. What is read, and from where

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/events` | every count, Upcoming, Hosting and Past |
| `GET /api/events/suggested` | Suggested on Upcoming and Discover |
| `GET /api/public/events?limit=25&offset&q&from=now` | Discover's list |

- **Each read answers alone.** A failed list says "Your events could not be read. That is not the same as having none." with Retry, and the counts are dashes. A failed public read says so on Discover only, never "nothing on".
- **A place in the canvas's words:** `confirmed` is Going; `registered` is "Waiting for the host" (a seat held while the host approves); `waitlisted` is "Waitlist · #n"; `attended` is "Checked in". An event's own status never shows as a place.
- **The check-in code** shows on the next event only, as the read sends it (a confirmed, paid seat). Otherwise: "The check-in code appears once your seat is confirmed and paid."
- **Sent back** is how the page draws `rejected`, the desk's stored word.
- **Past** holds your past places and the events you hosted that ran or were cancelled. A draft whose date passed never ran, so it stays in Hosting's Needs you.
- **Not read, and said so:** seats, waitlist, approvals and invitations per hosted event; cancelled and declined places; replays ("Replay: Not recorded", "Events have no recording link yet.").

### 3. What each tab draws today

- **Upcoming (MEV1, MEV2):** Needs you (a hosted event sent back, with "Edit and resend"); your next event with its date tile, time with your own time, place, place pill, "Free seat", the code, ".ics", Join for an online seat holder and the event page; Going grouped This week, Later this month and Later; Hosting soon (two); Suggested (three) with "See all in Discover".
- **Hosting (MEV4, MEV13):** search from two characters; groups Needs you, Drafts, Waiting for review, Published, Cancelled, an empty group hidden; each row's one action (Edit and resend, Edit details, Manage, or "Waiting for an admin"); an admin's line to the review desk at `/admin/events/review`.
- **Discover (MEV8):** Suggested (four), search, every upcoming public event with its price and "Going" when you hold a seat, in pages of 25.
- **Past (MEV9):** each past place with its result, each hosted event that ran ("Hosted") or was cancelled, and Replay "Not recorded".
- **Not drawn now, and why:** the ticket and hosted-event drawers and the dialogs (part 5); the month calendar, Subscribe, Add to Google or Outlook (part 3); Pay to confirm, invitations, off the waitlist, counts per event, filters, the chart (parts 2, 4); the host pages' shared header (part 6); phone (part 7).

### 4. Who sees what

Every signed-in profile reads its own events; the Worker already lets any signed-in account read its list and register (`requireAuth`). Host actions stay `canManage` (the host or an admin) on the pages the rows link to.
