## D6216 part 2 — The founder's promise summary and a capital ask's handoff to its round call (#6216)

Part 1 is `D6216.md`: the recipient-scoped asks read in Report detail and the Value-add promise linked to an ask. This part builds what part 1 left on the owner's 2026-11-16 answers in D6215 (No, No, Yes).

- **The founder's summary.** The author's `GET /api/portfolio-updates/:uid/page` gives each ask a `promises` list read from `portfolio_support_entries` by `ask_uid`, limited to the update's own company. Each item carries only the investor's name, the support kind and the promised, delivered, withdrawn and cancelled dates. Hours, outcome and the entry's own words are not sent. A promise with no promised date reads the day it was recorded. Cancelled (D6202) is included so a cancelled promise does not read as still open. If the read fails, the page still answers, with `promises_readable: false` and no `promises` key, and `/updates/:uid` says "Unavailable" rather than showing nothing. A recipient's view of the page carries no promises.
- **Names between recipients.** The investor read keeps `answered` as a yes or no, and Report detail says "Answered". It never names who answered.
- **No open recorded.** Report detail still reads `/:uid/asks`, never `/:uid/page`, so reading the asks records no open.
- **A capital ask on a round call.** When `updateAskLinks` resolves a live call the investor can open, Report detail's Promise support goes to `/round-calls/:uid` and says that replies go to the call. `POST /api/portfolio-support` refuses the same ask with 409 `ask_on_round_call` and the `call_uid`, and writes nothing. Value-add then shows the call link. A capital ask with no open call, or with round calls off, stays a Value-add promise.
- **Already promised by you.** The ask shows "View the entry", which goes to Value-add, in place of a second Promise support.
- **No migration.** The `ask_uid` column and index from part 1 carry this part.
