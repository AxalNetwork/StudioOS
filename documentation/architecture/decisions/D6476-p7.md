## D6476 part 7 — A fresh landing decision before every re-queue (#6476)

- **Status:** built by S21 on 2026-11-27, from the 2026-11-27 coordinator audit on #6476 and the owner's answer that
  day: build the missing re-queue policy check; the hosted rehearsal stays the owner's to run.
- **Builds on:** D6476-p4 (#8054: wait versus hand-back), D6326 (`landPr.decide()`, the land-pr gates), D6481 (the
  feeder's reader, `readLandingDecision()`).

**The gap.** S34 reproduced it on 2026-11-21 (#6476, #6481): a queue-cleared ejection with green current-head checks
was re-queued while `LANDING_AUTO` was false. The handler checked consent, the current head's checks, `eligible()`,
the breakers and its budgets. It never read the landing policy that the feeder's first entry needs. S34's adoption
packet was never published. This part writes the check from that reproduction, reusing the existing readers.

**The rule.** `decide()` in `ejection-rules.mjs` returning `requeue` is no longer permission. The sweep then:
1. reads `readLandingDecision(pr)` from `feeder-github.mjs`, the reader the feeder uses. That reader runs
   `landPr.decide()` from `scripts/lib/landPr.mjs` on facts read now: the repository settings, main's rules, #5500,
   the PR's files and check runs, the `landing policy` job log for this head (`parseLandingLog()`), the tier from the
   trusted classifier, and the open `[alert]` issues from a complete search;
2. passes the result to `requeuePolicy()`;
3. passes that verdict to `gateRequeue()`.

Only a `requeue` that survives `gateRequeue()` reaches `enqueuePullRequest`, which still carries `expectedHeadOid`.

**What `requeuePolicy()` requires.** All of these:
- the repository variable `LANDING_AUTO` is `true` now. The workflow passes it in. Classifying and labelling do not
  need it;
- the decision is for this PR and for the head the enqueue names. A decision for another head waits for the next
  sweep;
- `mayQueue` is true and every gate holds:
  - #5605 rows 3 and 6, with `LANDING_AUTO` true on the `landing policy` report for this exact head SHA;
  - #5500 closed as completed;
  - main still has its `merge_queue` rule;
  - required checks are `success` on the head;
  - the report says T0 or T1 for the head;
  - `landing-policy.yml` is untouched;
  - `[alert] merge queue stalled` is not open;
- the PR is not a draft.

A read that throws, or a result without its gates, is a refusal. It is never a pass.

**Wait or hand back.** Two gates and two facts are the owner's, so they label `ejected` at once:
- `PR open on main` and `landing-policy.yml untouched` (`OWNER_POLICY_GATES`);
- a T2 report, because the owner lands T2;
- a draft.

Every other refusal (`RETRY_POLICY_GATES`, `LANDING_AUTO`, an unread or stale decision) can clear by itself or with
a setting. It waits, unlabelled, with its reasons in the sticky comment, until `RECOVERY_WINDOW_HOURS` after the
ejection, and is then handed back as in D6476-p4. A test pins that every gate name `landPr.decide()` emits is on
exactly one side.

**Holds.** The hold alerts in `BREAKER_ALERTS` (main red, Actions queue backed up, merge queue stalled) already open
the breakers that `decide()` reads. `[alert] merge queue stalled` is now also read from the landing decision's
complete search, so the handler's 100-issue alert read can no longer miss it.

**Cost.** The policy read (a `git fetch` of base and head, and the classifier) runs only for a PR that every other
condition would already re-queue. It does not run for one that is waiting or being handed back.

**Consequence today.** #5500 is open, so `agents may queue` fails, and no ejection is re-queued until it closes. Each
ejection waits 24 hours with that reason and is then handed back. This is the feeder's own condition: a re-queue is a
landing just as a first entry is.

**Unchanged:**
- `landing-policy.yml`, `landPr.mjs`, `feeder-github.mjs` and `ci.yml` are not edited;
- the classes, consent, the keeper flow, mergeability, budgets, breakers and stranded labels.

**Rollback.** Revert the PR. The handler then re-queues without the policy read, as before. No data or settings
change. The workflow's `LANDING_AUTO` line is only read.

- the 24-hour hand-back;
- no policy read while checks are pending;
- the real `readLandingDecision()` over an in-memory REST API, with `LANDING_AUTO` false and then true in the job log;
- the gate split and `gateRequeue()`.

`_landingDecision_6476.mjs` builds each decision with the real `landPr.decide()`.
