- **Status:** decided by S1 for the owner, 2026-10-20 (#2216; part of D785, #2210, D-h). Part 1 (`Part of #2216`) is the Worker. Part 2 (`Closes #2216`) is the page, after the "Usage & Billing console" canvas. **Pause AI touches the hold, so S1 reviews it for security.**
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `84f6e33b`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Before this:**
  - HQ › AI allowances showed plan caps, held runs, refunds, billing and models, and one member's month (D776 PR 2).
  - The router held the organisation cap, the kill switch and the KV spend buckets.
  - Nothing showed who uses AI platform-wide, and nothing could stop one member.

### 1. The read: `GET /api/admin/ai-usage?range=7d|30d|mtd`

- **Who.** A Super Admin with a recent step-up (`requireStepUp`, then `requireSuperAdmin`), as the issue asks. HQ's other money reads (`admin_revenue.ts`) take the elevation alone. This read names members and what they were charged, so it takes the step-up too.
- **What is summed** (`services/aiUsageAdmin.ts`, as D776's `aiConsumption.ts` sums a member's month):
  - `runs` are runs that ran: no refusal, and not a Llama Guard row (`guard_for` set);
  - `refused` counts the refused rows;
  - `cost_microusd` is every row's `est_cost_usd`, guard rows included;
  - `charged_microusd` is every row's `charged_microusd` (D762). A NULL charge (a row from before migration 493, or a run held for review) is never a zero. It is counted in `not_recorded`;
  - `margin_microusd` is the charge less the cost of the rows that recorded a charge, so an unrecorded row moves neither side.
- **The splits.** Totals by day, by model, by role and by plan. The role and the plan (the AI licence, `aiLicenceKey`) are read from each member's `users` row at read time. A row with no member (a system run) reads `system`.
- **Top members.**
  - The top 20 by charged.
  - The top 20 by velocity: today's charge over the member's 7-day daily average. The average counts as at least $1 (`VELOCITY_FLOOR_MICROUSD`). Without that floor, thirty members spending ten cents on their first day outranked one who went from $2 a day to $20. Only members charged today are listed.
- **Paused and capped.** The read also lists who is paused and who is capped, with who did it and when.
- **Who a member is.** `{ id, name, email }`: the user ID, the display name (else the name HQ already shows) and the account email.
  - The issue first said "never an email in a list". The owner reversed that for this page on 2026-10-20 (S1 on #2227): HQ › AI usage names each member by email and ID beside their Cloudflare cost and what they were charged, so a Super Admin can find and reach the member behind a figure.
  - Only this page. Every read keeps the step-up and `requireSuperAdmin`; no member-facing answer gains an email; the pause and cap audit lines keep the user id, not the email. Each query reads `u.email` by name, never `u.*`.
  - No prompt and no output anywhere: `ai_usage_logs` stores neither.
- **Per member.** Runs, `cost_microusd`, `charged_microusd`, `margin_microusd`, and `topups_microusd`: the AI tokens the member bought in the range (`ai_topup_purchases` credited in it, bonus left out).

### 1b. Finding a member, and one member

- **`GET /api/admin/ai-usage/members?q=&range=`.** `q` of digits alone is an exact user ID, so "2" never lists member 20. Anything else is a case-insensitive email prefix (`lower(u.email) LIKE ?`, with `%`, `_` and `\` escaped, so they match themselves). At most 50 rows (`SEARCH_MAX`), ordered by email, each with the top members' sums; a member with no runs reads zeros. An empty `q`, or one over 254 characters, is 400.
- **`GET /api/admin/ai-usage/members/:id?range=`.** The member's totals (the same as their top-members row for the range), by model and by day with cost beside charge; their top-up balance, allowance (`resolveAiCaps`, the member cap included) and spend limit (D788); their pause and cap, with who set them; and the history of both from `activity_logs`, newest first. The balance, the allowance and the spend limit each read `{ state: 'unreadable' }` when they fail, never a zero. An unknown ID is 404.

### 2. The flags

The thresholds are one block, `AI_USAGE_FLAGS`, returned with the read as `thresholds`. Each flag says why and since when, from the read's clock rather than the range: a flag is about now.

| Flag | Raised when | Since |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `spike` | Today's charge is more than 5× the 7-day daily average and at least $5, compared in integers (`today × 7 > 5 × prior`) | today, 00:00 UTC |
| `refusals` | 20 or more refused runs in the last 24 hours | the first of them |
| `held` | 3 or more runs held for review | the oldest hold |
| `refund_after_spend` | A refund or dispute of a token pack, in the last 30 days, after more than 80% of the refunded share was spent | the first such reversal |
| `new_heavy` | An account under 7 days old that topped up and was charged more than $20 within 24 hours of its first top-up | the first top-up |

**Two readings to check:**
- **`refund_after_spend`.** D755 §8 records what a reversal could not take back as `shortfall_microusd`: the share of the refunded tokens already used. The flag is raised when that shortfall is more than 80% of the share the reversal targeted (`shortfall + taken`).
- **`new_heavy`.** "Within 24 hours" is read as within 24 hours of the first top-up: buying a pack and burning it.

### 3. Pause AI

- **Storage.** Migration 510, `member_ai_pauses`, as the issue gives it, with the reason checked to at least 10 characters.
  - The issue gives the number 510; 508 and 509 are D785's other tasks. The sql README asks for one higher than the maximum; the issue's number wins.
  - No branch on origin uses 508–510.
- **Routes.** `POST` and `DELETE /api/admin/ai-usage/members/:id/pause`, each with `{ reason }` of 10–500 characters.
  - The change and its `activity_logs` line (`ai_member_paused`, `ai_member_unpaused`) are one batch. The line is written only if the row changed (`WHERE changes() = 1`, and the unpause's line is selected from the row it deletes).
  - Pausing twice is 409 `already_paused`; unpausing nothing is 404 `not_paused`. Neither writes a line.
- **Refused at the hold.** `reserveAiBudget` refuses a paused member as `ai_paused` before anything is held, so every caller of the hold refuses: `run()`, live transcription, and a recording session's transcription quote.
  - The hold is the one place it is checked. A member's run reaches its cache only after the hold (D763: a guarded run looks in the cache after its input passed the guard, and the guard is charged under the hold), so no member is answered around it. The refused run's row records `ai_paused` with no charge.
  - Every member path answers 403 with "AI is paused on this account. Contact support.": `refuseRun` (`util/railContext.ts`), a recording session's quote, the assistant's structuring tool, the Validate digest note and the interview pipeline's messages.
  - A pause that cannot be read refuses the run as `misconfigured`, as `assistOff` does. A database before 510 has no pauses.
- **Reloads too.** D794's `reloadBlocked` (`services/aiAutoReload.ts`) answers `paused` for a paused member, before the crossing is claimed or Stripe is called, so a paused member's card is never charged. Auto-reload stays on, and the same crossing reloads once the pause is lifted (`ai_auto_reload_d794.test.ts`).
- **Notified once.** The member is told in the app, once, when the pause is new (`notify`, `ai_paused`). A second press tells nothing.

### 4. A member's own cap

- **Storage.** `PUT /api/admin/ai-usage/members/:id/cap { monthly_usd, reason }` writes `member_ai_caps`, in the same migration 510: $0 to $100,000, or null to clear it.
- **Lower wins.** `resolveAiCaps` reads it after the licence's cap, and the lower one wins:
  - a member's plan allowance, with the day reading the month, as D783 has it;
  - or the admin licence's typed monthly cap.
- **Narrows only.** A cap above the licence's changes nothing, so it only ever narrows an allowance. When a cap is set, the caps carry `member_cap_microusd`, which `/api/ai-budget/me` then shows.
- **Audit.** `ai_member_cap_set` or `ai_member_cap_cleared`, with the cap before and after, in the same batch as the write.
- **Reason required.** The issue asks for an audit, not a reason. HQ's other money writes take one, so this does too.

### 5. The page (part 2)

`/admin/platform/ai-usage`, the Super Admin's, is built from board B5 of the "Usage & Billing console" canvas (`design/incoming/Usage Page.dc.html`, `screen: 'hq'`), at desktop. It is on the HQ Platform strip after AI allowances, and AI allowances links to it. It docks AI allowances' rail: an alias in `railPages.js`, so the Super Admin's canvas gains no board.

**What the page draws** (`pages/hq/AiUsagePage.jsx`, with the arithmetic in `pages/hq/aiUsageModel.js`):
- **The range switch:** 7 days, 30 days (the default) and month to date. Each switch re-reads.
- **Five totals:**
  - Runs, with the refused runs named and not counted;
  - Cloudflare cost;
  - Charged to customers, with the runs whose charge was not recorded named;
  - Margin, with its share of Cloudflare's cost;
  - AI tokens bought.
- **The daily chart by model.** Every day of the range, stacked by model in `lib/modelColours.js`'s colours (shared with Usage and Cost), on Cloudflare cost or Charged. Each column names its day and amounts.
- **The splits** by model, role and plan.
- **"Members":**
  - "All members" shows the top 20 by charge, or a search's answer: an exact user ID or an email prefix, from the Worker.
  - The columns are User ID, Email, Member (name, role and plan), Runs, CF cost, Charged, Margin, Tokens bought and Flags. The money and count columns sort.
  - Each row has Set cap and Pause AI, or Resume AI when it is paused.
  - "Fastest rising" shows today against the 7-day average.
- **The member panel**, opened by a row or a flag:
  - ID, email, role, plan and join date;
  - Cloudflare cost against the charge, by model and by day;
  - the token balance, purchases in the range, the plan allowance (with the member cap) and the spend limit. A read that failed says "Unreadable", never $0;
  - the pause and cap state, with its history: when, what, who and why;
  - Open account, Set cap and Pause AI or Resume AI.
- **"Flags":** the five kinds, each with its member, why and since.
- **The dialogs** for Pause AI, Resume AI and Set cap. Each needs a reason of 10 to 500 characters. The cap is 0 to 100,000 dollars, and empty clears it. The page re-reads after each write.

**The read gained what the board draws, Worker first.** None of it is a prompt or an output:
- `by_day_model`: each day's cost and charge by model, for the chart;
- `topups_microusd`: every member's packs credited in the range, bonus left out ("AI tokens bought");
- each member row's `role` and `plan` (`aiLicenceKey`, read now, as the splits read them);
- the detail's `role`, `plan` and `joined`;
- each history line's `actor_name`, while the line itself keeps the id.
