### 1. The card reads two stores, apart

Billing draws "Initial invoice · one-off" above the unchanged annual list. The list no longer shows the initial
invoice: `GET /:uid/fee-invoices` returns it with `kind: 'initial'`, and it holds no licence year. Before an invoice
exists, its lines come from `GET /:uid/pricing`'s `latest_decision.accepted` (the initial fee and the regulatory
surcharge, in the decision's currency), and the surcharge's class from the pricing read's countries. The two reads
load and fail apart:

- **Invoices unreadable:** Unavailable. Issue stays off, because one may already be issued and a second can never be.
- **Decision unreadable:** Unavailable, "unknown, not zero". Issue stays off.
- **No decision:** one action, Open Pricing & Market.

### 2. Issue is offered only where the Worker would allow it, and the Worker still decides

The card offers Issue initial invoice… only for an active or suspended licence, from its start date, with an accepted
initial fee and no initial invoice yet. These are the same conditions `issueInitialFeeInvoice` checks. Otherwise it
says why, in the canvas's sentences. The press goes through the D4819 consequence sheet (L42): lines, total, what can
never happen twice, payee and due date from the Terms schedule, and Void as the undo. A refusal is shown in the outcome
card as the Worker words it.

A surcharge that is not recorded prints no line and one sentence, never €0. A surcharge recorded as zero prints no
line and no sentence. A fee that is not recorded reads Not recorded and the total is not calculated.

### 3. The Apply review names each figure's destination

The Apply form on Terms › Pricing & Market lists the five figures before → after, each with where it goes:

- the annual fee and the royalty: the core terms;
- the minimum royalty: the schedule's § 4.4 minimum guarantee;
- the initial fee and the surcharge: the one-off initial invoice.

The form reads the fee invoices apart. If an initial invoice is already issued, those two rows say "Recorded on the
decision only". If the invoices cannot be read, they say whether one is issued is unknown. A figure the recommendation
does not record reads "Not recorded · no change". The acknowledgement names the minimum guarantee too (it was already
asked for when only that changes, D5953). After an apply that records an initial fee with no initial invoice, the
receipt links to Billing. The panel's intro sentence now says where each figure goes, replacing "applying it changes
the annual fee and royalty above".
