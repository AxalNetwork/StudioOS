## D4681 — Expertise: services edited in place, deliberate proof deletion, real article states (#4681)

### Decision

Expertise's Services, Proof and Thinking zones say what each record is and
who can see it, and each change is made in place.

1. **Services are edited in place.** A saved service opens an edit form on its
   own row: title, kind, price, currency, duration, scope and state. Save sends
   one owner-checked `PATCH /api/advisors/me/services/:id` carrying only what
   changed. The Worker now refuses a blank title (it used to keep the old one
   and report a save) and a currency that is not three letters (it used to
   cut any text to eight characters and store it).
2. **A service is not public and not bookable.** `GET /api/advisors/:uid`, the
   public profile, returns session types and public booking links and no
   services. The zone says so and links to Practice › Sessions, where what a
   founder books is set. "Hide / Show" and the "Hidden" pill became
   "Mark inactive / Mark active" and "Inactive": a state of the advisor's own
   record, not a public listing.
3. **Proof is private, and publishing waits on P1.** `is_public` is stored,
   but no page shows a proof item to anyone else. The zone says so. Profile's
   *Preview as founder* and Proof's *Public preview* said "no public profile
   page exists"; `/advisors/:uid` does (D571), so both open it, disabled until
   a saved profile gives it an address. Publishing proof is not built here: it
   waits on the attestation rules (P1).
4. **Deleting proof is deliberate and whole.**
   - The trash button asks first. It names the consent records that go with
     the claim and any link still out, and says it cannot be undone.
   - The Worker deletes the consents and the item in one `DB.batch`, which D1
     runs as one transaction, so a failure leaves both.
   - The item's DELETE re-checks the owner.
   - The response reports `consents_removed`.
   - **Retention:** there is no recovery. Axal keeps no copy, because a
     deleted claim's consent rows hold a third party's name and email with
     nothing left to attest to.
5. **Thinking draws each article state.** The shelf drew "Draft" for anything
   unpublished. Each piece now carries its real state: draft, submitted, in
   review, changes requested, not accepted, approved, scheduled (approved with
   `publish_at`, D484) or published.
   - Each state says who can read the piece.
   - Each has one next step: the editor (`/articles/edit/:id`), or the
     published piece (`/articles/:slug`).
   - *History* lists its saved revisions through D1406's author read.
   - `GET /me/thinking` adds `publish_at`.
   - The "In draft" tile became "Not public yet", because a submitted piece is
     not visible only to its writer.

### Not claimed

- No public reach, booking count or publication is claimed.
- `units_sold` stays null.
- Talk reach stays "Not recorded".
- Nothing new is published.
