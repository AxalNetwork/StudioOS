## D7178 — Research › Companies access: back to the exact page after sign-in, by held licences (#7178)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-18 from the canvas D7044 records (`design/incoming/Research Companies Sign-in and Licence Access Page.dc.html` and its boards, RS-16 to RS-16 C), with the owner's answers of 2026-11-18 (every question answered A, as drawn). Part of #3701.
- **Builds on:** D4473 (RoleGuard admits on any held licence), D4579 (Grow keeps its destination), D4612 (Research links open only where their reader is admitted), D4646 (Learning keeps its destination), D914 (route boundaries), D5129 (Platform States).

### The rule

Research › Companies (`/research/companies`, its profiles, saved analyses and candidates, and the Worker's `GET /api/research/company-directory` and `/company-directory/:uid`) is open to a **held** Founder or Advisor licence, an active Spin-Out Lab, or an admin. Held means the licences `/api/auth/me` reports (D4472), not the primary role.

- **Worker.** `services/researchCompaniesAccess.ts` `admitsCompanies` reads the principal's hydrated `licences`, `spinout_lab_active` and role; the two directory routes answer anyone else `403 { error: 'companies_licence_required' }` with our sentence, before the record is read, so a refusal never says whether a company exists. A support session is the member's own principal, so the member's licences decide.
- **One frontend rule.** `lib/researchAccess.js` `researchRoleFor` decides the shell a Research page is drawn in: the role browsed as wherever its shell is admitted to the page's area (every page that worked before is unchanged); otherwise the first held licence, in licence order, whose shell is (Investor + Founder reads Companies as the founder, Partner + Advisor as the advisor, Founder + Investor reads Diligence as the investor); an active Lab without such a licence reads Companies and Funds in the founder shell; an admin reads as the founder, as before. App.jsx's `researchRole` is that function (re-exported by `lib/activeRole.js`), so the zone list (`RESEARCH_ZONES[researchRole]`), `canAddToResearch(researchRole)` and `canOpenResearch` read one answer. `canOpenResearch` also admits on a held licence, so a Markets page links the company for an Investor who holds Founder.
- **Owner's answers (all A).** Actions are the union of held licences (Investor + Founder gets Add to research and Compare on Companies). The refusal offers Open Markets, then See licences, then Home. An admin's "View as Investor" shows the boundary: the four Companies routes use `labRoles([...], { preview: false })`, which leaves the previewed role out. A link opened on another device keeps no destination there and goes home (unchanged).

### What the build does

- **The kept destination.** `lib/pendingNext.js` adds `/research/companies` to `RETURN_AFTER_LOGIN_PREFIXES`, `DESTINATION_AREAS` (name "Companies", what, "You need a Founder or Advisor licence.") and `SETUP_BEFORE_DESTINATION_PREFIXES`. The sign-in and sign-up pages already read the area, so they say "Sign in to continue to Companies" and never the query or the company.
- **A stale session.** A 401 on a protected page goes to `sessionEndedRedirectFor(location)`: `/login?next=<page>&ended=1` for an area that keeps its destination (the route guard's sanitiser), a bare `/login` otherwise. The sign-in page then says "Your session ended. Sign in again to go back to Companies."
- **After setup.** A destination whose area is marked `ready` (Companies) waits on "Your account is ready · Continue to Companies" with "go to your home"; Grow returns as before.
- **Restored.** The page sign-in, sign-up or setup returned to shows "Restored · Signed in. This is the page you asked for." (the directory with a query: "Your search and filters are back."), once per page load, as a status that does not take focus.
- **The boundary.** `ROUTE_BOUNDARIES` gains `/research/companies` for investor, partner and exploring (`COMPANIES_BOUNDARY_ROLES`, a test compares App.jsx's copy). It names the held licences, offers Open Markets (not to an account with no licence), See licences (not in a support session) and Home, and moves focus to its h1. App.jsx is a frozen hub (D5615), so the boundary and the ready screen live in `pages/research/CompaniesAccess.jsx` and load through `pages/founder/GrowBoundary.jsx`, which re-exports them.
- **The public card.** The Companies card on `/intelligence` gains "Open Companies →" to `/research/companies` (`siteIntelligence.js` `to`).
- **Profile states.** Company not found adds "It may have been removed or merged." and Back to Companies; a 403 reads Refused, never Unavailable.
