### Venture and cash timing

Financials reads `project_id` or legacy `project` from the URL, validates it
against the accessible ventures and follows browser Back/Forward. Invalid
selection, or multiple ventures with no selection, never falls back to the
first venture. The old Capital tab helper already preserves both aliases;
CapTable now consumes `project_id` too. Stale model renders/exports are gated
on the selected venture.

The existing card's with-raise line injects the recorded target only in the
latest active round's recorded close month. Missing, invalid or past-month
close dates leave that line Not recorded with a reason. A failed close read
says Unreadable and keeps the without-raise baseline. Both lines are Modelled;
recorded current-month close dates can legitimately be month 0. A close beyond the 18-month horizon is labelled as such and draws no with-raise line. No close date
is inferred. Parts 2–7 own the new plan engine, tabs and old-tool retirement.
