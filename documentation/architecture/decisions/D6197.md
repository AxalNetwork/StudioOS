## D6197 — Closing room: a signer's own link again, and the executed copy (#6197)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-18 (#6197, under #4815).
- **Builds on:** D410 (no signing token outside the recipient's inbox), D411 (the sender's remind and void), D1216 (staged signing), D1218 (the room's Review and sign), D6184 (the Deal paper card), D6199 (the investor's own signed copy).
- **Migration:** none.
- **Worker:** `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/esign.ts` (`POST /api/legal/esign/:id/resend`), `services/stagedSigning.ts` (`you` on the staged read), `middleware/rateLimit.ts` (`ESIGN_SIGNER_RESEND` in the `esign_send` bucket).
- **Frontend:** `frontend/src/pages/closing/RoomDialogs.jsx` (`SignDrawer`, `saveBlob` moved here from `RecordRoom.jsx`), `roomModel.js` (`signActions`, `SIGN_WORDS`), `frontend/src/pages/deal/DealPaper.jsx` and `dealWorkModel.js` (`PAPER_WORDS`), `frontend/src/lib/api/closings.js` (`resendSigningLink`).

### 1. What the two routes allowed before

The issue asked for this check first.

- **`POST /:id/remind` is the sender's.** It loads the envelope by `created_by = caller` and mails every unsigned recipient, once a day per envelope. A signer calling it gets a 404, so asking for your own link needed a new route.
- **`GET /:id/document` is read-scoped.** It uses `esignEnvelopeScope`: the sender, the subject (`user_id`) or a recipient by account. It serves only a `completed` envelope.
  - **A staged envelope is not one envelope.** Each stage is its own single-recipient envelope over the same text, sent by the paper's creator (D1216).
  - **So the scope covers each stage's own signer and the creator, not every party to every stage.** The line's investor reads their own stage's envelope. They do not read the company's stage's envelope, unless the investor started the paper.
- **The room had no numeric id.** It reads staged envelopes by `uid`, and both routes take a numeric id.

### 2. `POST /api/legal/esign/:id/resend` — my link again

- **Who:** the caller's own recipient row only, matched by account (`esign_recipients.user_id`), the key `esignEnvelopeScope` reads a recipient by.
  - Everyone else gets the same 404 a missing id gets (D410): the sender, another recipient, an admin who is not the recipient and a stranger.
- **What:** emails the caller's signing link to the address on their row, through the same agreement email the send uses. A lapsed link is replaced, as `/remind` does.
  - The answer is `{ resent, link_renewed, expires_at }`. It never carries the token or a URL (D410).
- **How often:** once an hour per signer, read from `signing_link_resent` rows on the envelope's audit trail for that signer.
  - It is also metered by the fail-closed `esign_send` bucket (`ESIGN_SIGNER_RESEND`), since it sends Axal-branded mail.
  - A failed send writes `signing_link_resend_failed` and does not count against the hour.
  - The signer's hour and the sender's day (`reminder_sent`) are separate clocks.
- **Refusals,** each with a sentence:

| Code | Status | When |
|---|---|---|
| `envelope_not_found` | 404 | not the caller's own row |
| `provider_managed` | 409 | a DocuSign envelope |
| `already_signed` | 409 | the caller's row is signed |
| `envelope_not_pending` | 409 | executed, voided, declined or otherwise not waiting |
| `resend_too_soon` | 429 | within the hour, with `next_resend_at` |
| `resend_not_sent` | 502 | the email could not be sent |

### 3. The room reaches the numeric id: `you` on the staged read

`readStaged` (and so `/staged/:uid` and `/staged/line/:commitmentId`) returns `you`: the caller's own stage, matched by `esign_staged_signers.user_id`, as `{ position, role, state, envelope_id }`.

- `envelope_id` is the numeric id of the envelope sent to the caller, or null before their stage is sent.
- A reader who signs no stage gets `you: null`: an admin, or the founder of a paper with no company stage.
- The read names an id; the two routes still decide. `/:id/resend` checks the caller's own row and `/:id/document` checks its scope, so a forged id reaches nothing more.
- No token is ever in the read.

### 4. The room's Review and sign (CP4) and the Deal paper card

- **Send me the link again:** shown while the viewer's own stage is `sent`.
  - After the Worker answers, the room says the link was sent again. A refusal is drawn as the Worker's sentence.
  - No link is shown or built in the browser.
- **Download the executed copy:** shown once every stage has signed (`complete`). It reads the viewer's own stage envelope through `GET /:id/document`, as D6199's "Download your signed copy" does.
  - Each stage's copy carries that stage's signature over the same text (the staged `digest`, D1216). No single PDF carries every stage's signature.
  - The drawer says this in one line, under the button.
- **A viewer who is not a party** (no `you`) sees the signing order and neither action, and the room makes no e-sign call for them.
- **The Deal paper card (D6184):**
  - A row that waits on the viewer (`paperSigning` → `yours`) offers "Send me the link again" on that envelope's id, with the Worker's refusal drawn.
  - A fully signed row's button now reads "Download the executed copy". It still reads the same route.
- **Words:** "executed", not "signed", for the finished paper (PR #5796).

### 5. "Open the paper" is not built here

No read serves a stage's document to a signer before they sign except `GET /sign/:token/paper`, and that is keyed by the signing token. The token is the signer's inbox's alone (D410), so the room cannot call it.

The signer reads the paper on the e-sign page their link opens, and the room now says so under "Send me the link again". A read of the paper by envelope id, scoped to its own recipient, would be a new Worker route. It is left for a follow-up if the owner wants the paper readable in the room.
