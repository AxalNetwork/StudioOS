### 1. The tabs

- **Activity** is on Mentor cohort access, Refer & Earn, Perks and Learning. Each tab is a static sub-path (`/activity`) with a literal `NavLink`, routed with `guard(['admin'])`.
  - The four routes resolve to their page's rail key through the existing subtree aliases, so no alias row is added.
  - Each tab draws part 1's activity read and nothing else: newest first, 50 a page, with Show older on its cursor (`ActivityTab.jsx`). A row is a sentence that names no person.
  - Mentor's tab says "Founders hiding from a mentor, and mentors' role changes, are not listed here yet." Its assign and end rows depend on the read listing them (#2922).
  - Whatever the Worker names as `not_audited` is drawn as its own line, never as an empty list.
- **Terms is on no page.** Part 1's store holds no program's terms.
- Settings stays Refer & Earn's read-only card until O-EXP. Mentor and Learning still have no Queue.

### 2. How it runs reads part 1's playbook store

`PlaybookEditor.jsx` replaces the frontend constant on all four pages.

- **An Admin** reads the published version: each section with its lock and "Set by HQ", and the foot "HQ writes the playbook."
- **A Super Admin** reads the draft, with the version and the edited date (PG5s), Edit on each section and Publish changes. Each is a dialog.
  - Edit sends only the fields that changed. A computed section takes a title only.
  - Publish is disabled while the draft is what is published. Its 409s sit in the dialog.
- **Mentor's computed and live sections:**
  - `cycle` is the cycle strip;
  - `measure` reads founders hidden (part 7) and sessions booked with a mentor (part 1), in place of "Not read here yet";
  - `terms` names a template only if the store links one.
- **Part 10 (#2796)** reuses the editor for AI-native Venture.
- **Not built:** editing a section's template slugs needs the templates list.
- **The seeded text.** Refer & Earn's seeded `strategic` section still says "No one can be granted access yet."
  - This part adds no SQL, so the store's text stands until a Super Admin edits it in the editor, or a later migration rewrites the unchanged seed (D953 §6's rule).
  - The Overview no longer says it.

### 3. Mentor cohort access

- **The tiles.**
  - Active mentors and Cohorts covered leave out deactivated accounts (part 7's `advisor_is_active`; the same change as #2922's). A cohort whose only holder is deactivated reads uncovered.
  - Founders hidden from a mentor is part 7's `founders_hidden_total`. Null reads "Could not be read", never 0.
  - **Ended.** D962's window is the last 30 days, said on the tile. It is read from 565's history, newest 200; past a full page whose oldest row is still in the window, it reads "at least".
- **Mentors per cohort** has one bar per cohort S-COV counts, mentors only: the amber "! None" bar and "[Cohort] has no mentor yet.", with Chart and Table. The founder count waits for O-FND and is not drawn.
- **Needs you.** A no-mentor row opens Assign with its cohort chosen. A role-changed row opens End access.
- **Recent activity** is the five newest rows, with See all to Activity and the same line on hides and role changes.
- **People** is `AdvisorCohortAssignmentsBody`.
  - The toolbar has search by mentor or cohort; Cohort, Status and Role changed; and Sort by Assigned, Cohort or Name. It shows 25 a page with "1–25 of [n]", and "within the newest 500" at the cap.
  - **The columns:**
    - Mentor: name, current role, "Not an Advisor now" and "Deactivated", and no email;
    - Cohort: "· week [n]", "· not started" or "· ended", or "Cycle #[id]" outside the twelve newest;
    - Assigned: "[date] by an admin";
    - Ended;
    - Founders visible: "—" on ended, role-changed and deactivated rows, otherwise "Not read here yet" until O-FND;
    - Note.
  - Ended rows stay, muted.
  - Earlier episodes come from 565's history (N16), and the foot reads "Ended access stays on record."
- **End access** is a dialog. It says the three things, is offered on active rows only, and draws part 7's 409 `already_ended` in place.
- **Assign a mentor** is a `Lightbox` (`MentorDialogs.jsx`).
  - The advisor search runs over the assignable list and keeps "No advisor profile yet".
  - The cohorts are scheduled or running, with their dates.
  - An active pair is marked and nothing is sent. The Worker's 409 `already_assigned` sits in place.
  - The note holds 1,000 characters.
  - The dialog says everything the mentor reads, and "Founders are told once their first week is recorded."
  - It shows no "founders told" figure.
  - Its refusals are keyed on `code`, and a codeless 400 or 404 reads the Worker's own sentence.
- **No mentors yet** (PG16b) shows when no assignment exists, naming the next scheduled cohort that is not postponed. A failed read is never this.
- **On a phone** (PG20b–PG20d):
  - Overview is the tiles and Needs you.
  - People is cards with a count line and a 44 px menu, and there are no header buttons.
  - Assign is a full page below 1024 px, with "‹ People" and the dialog's Back and Assign.

### 4. Refer & Earn

- **The tiles.** Referrals and Converted are exact totals from part 4's paged reads, each "All time.", because those reads take no window. Converted counts `converted`, `reward_eligible` and `reward_issued`.
- **Waiting for review** sums each open status's exact total, so "200+" goes. The bands come from the oldest 200 open and say what they cover when the total is larger. The oldest and Overdue still come from the glance's `referrals` lane.
- **The funnel.** No read counts the referrals that reached each stage, so its bars read "Not read here yet". Only the tags draw (More info needed, Rejected, Closed), as current totals.
- **Referrals per week by category** is part 4's weekly read, twelve weeks, with "Platform user" for `customer`.
- **Strategic referral access** lists part 4's requests: name, role, the date asked and the note. Grant and Decline are dialogs on the decision route, and 409 `already_decided` sits in place.
- **The Queue** is part 4's paged list with its total.
  - It opens on the open statuses, waiting longest first, and shows "1–25 of [n]".
  - The filters are Status and Category. There is no search: the read serves none.
  - The Referrer role filter waits until a role is served.
- **One referral, opened** (`ReferralDialog`):
  - The timeline is from the single-referral read.
  - Status offers only D957's moves and is sent only when it moved.
  - The fields are Next step (240), Reward (120, a note), Fit notes (2,000) and the Note to the referrer (2,000).
  - The dialog says "The referrer sees this on their page and is not notified."
  - A save replaces the timeline with the `history` the PATCH returns, and shows its result in place. There is no toast.

### 6. Learning

- **OD9, as D962 sets it.**
  - A content row is Done when a course or item is `completed`, an exercise's latest submission is `accepted`, or an exam is `passed`.
  - A learner has finished a program when every row in it is Done. A program with no contents has no one finished.
- **The tiles.**
  - Published programs and Active learners are unchanged.
  - Finished is "[%]" with "[a] of [b]", over each published program's learners. It is unreadable if any program's learners could not be read.
  - Enrolled this period is part 1's weekly total, with "The last 12 weeks, this week so far. A re-enrolment counts again."
- **Completion by program** has one bar per published program, "[a] of [b] finished", and **Enrolments per week** is a line, both from part 1's weekly read.
- **The programs table** gains Assigned admins, part 1's `admins` count.
- **A learner, opened** (PG12d) is a dialog on the learner read.
  - It shows the name, the program and region, and the last activity.
  - Each row shows its kind (Video, Reading, Course, Exercise, Exam), title, Done or Not yet, and the date reached. An exam row also shows its best score and attempts.
  - It has no webinar row and never an email.
  - A 403 `not_assigned` or a 404 `not_enrolled` is one sentence.
  - The foot reads "No email is shown here. Progress is read only."
