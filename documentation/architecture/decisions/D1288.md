### 1. One address: `ADD_TOKENS_PATH`

`frontend/src/lib/aiTokens.js` holds the address and the helpers.
- **`ADD_TOKENS_PATH`** is `/pricing?for=models#add-tokens`: Pricing's Models tab, with the hash that opens the sheet. It was `/pricing/products#token-packs`, the old storefront.
- **`TOKEN_PACKS_PATH`** is the same address, kept as a name for the old storefront's callers.
- **`addTokensHref(uid)`** puts one pack in the address, as `&pack=<uid>`. With no uid it is `ADD_TOKENS_PATH`.
- **`isAddTokensHref` and `packOfHref`** recognise that address and read its pack.
- **`MODELS_PATH`** is `/pricing?for=models`.

D755's one-path rule stands, and is wider. No file but `lib/aiTokens.js` may name:
- the old path, `/checkout?ai_topup=1` or `#add-tokens`;
- or a pack's old checkout address (`ai_tokens_d755`).

### 2. One sheet: `openAddTokens` and `AddTokensHost`

- **`openAddTokens({ from, pack_uid })`** asks for D1287's sheet in pack mode over the current page, with no route change. `from` names the page for the landing's "Back to [page]".
- **`AddTokensHost`** (`components/checkout/AddTokensHost.jsx`) is mounted once for a signed-in member, beside `CheckoutHost` in `GlobalCheckoutMount` (`App.jsx`). It renders nothing until asked. Closing the sheet, or its landing's Back, leaves the member where they were.
- **It is asked two ways:**
  - **`openAddTokens`**, from Billing's Buy tokens and each pack's Buy (`pages/account/BillingCards.jsx`, `from: 'Billing'`, on that pack). They went to `#buy-tokens` and to `/checkout?token_pack=` before.
  - **A click on any link to `ADD_TOKENS_PATH`.** The host listens in the capture phase, so a router link sees the click handled and does not navigate. It takes only a plain left click on a link in the same tab, off `/pricing`, and reads the link's pack.
- **Callers that already linked to `ADD_TOKENS_PATH`** keep their one link and open the sheet in place:
  - the rail (`ui/WorkerRail.jsx`) and the Assist card (`ui/AiBudgetUsage.jsx`);
  - Usage (`pages/account/AccountUsage.jsx`), AI tokens (`AiTokensPage.jsx`) and the phone Pricing screen (`PricingPhone.jsx`);
  - Build (`pages/cycles/BuildPlan.jsx`) and the narrative (`StoryTab.jsx`);
  - the pitch deck (`newDeckModel.js`, drawn by `AssistParts.jsx`);
  - a session digest (`sessionDigestModel.js`, drawn by `SessionSummary.jsx`) and `ChannelSessionParts.jsx`.

  The old storefront's Buy links (`components/products/TokenPacksSection.jsx`) now point at `addTokensHref(uid)`.

  **This is why the host takes the click rather than each caller calling `openAddTokens`.** Several callers are models that return an `href`, and the rail is `ui/WorkerRail.jsx`, which open PR #3493 owns. A link also stays a link: a visitor, a new tab (middle click, ⌘-click, `target="_blank"`) or a link from outside the app reaches Pricing, which opens the same sheet.
- **"Back to [page]"** comes from the page the member was on (`fromOfPath`): Usage, AI tokens, Billing, your account, Narrative, Pitch deck, Build or your cycle. Anywhere else it is "where you were".
- **A visitor** gets no host. Every Add tokens link takes them to Pricing's packs, whose Buy signs in and comes back to `ADD_TOKENS_PATH`.

### 3. Pricing opens the sheet on `#add-tokens`

On `/pricing?for=models#add-tokens`, `PricingBody` opens the sheet for a member, on `?pack=` when one is named. It then drops the hash and the pack from the address with `replace`, so Back does not reopen it. A visitor's address is left alone: the hash is the tokens band's anchor (`id="add-tokens"`).

### 4. The old addresses redirect, keeping the pack

`TokensRedirect` sits in `AddTokensHost.jsx`, and `App.jsx` puts it round the two routes:

| Old address | Now |
|---|---|
| `/pricing/products#token-packs` | `/pricing?for=models#add-tokens` |
| `/checkout?token_pack=<uid>` | `/pricing?for=models&pack=<uid>#add-tokens` |
| `/checkout?ai_topup=1` | `/pricing?for=models#add-tokens` (it already went to the old path) |

The storefront itself (`/pricing/products`, any other hash) and the cart checkout (`/checkout` with no token query) are unchanged. `/checkout` stays behind `guard`.

**Not removed here.** `CheckoutPage.jsx`'s token-pack branch (`TokenPackCheckout`) is no longer reachable from the router, because the redirect runs first. It is left in place: its own tests (`checkout_token_pack_d755`) still mount it, and removing it is the storefront's retirement, not this issue's.

### 5. The old storefront's back links

These now read "Back to Models" and go to `MODELS_PATH`, not "Back to Plans & Pricing" to `/pricing/products`:
- `CheckoutPage.jsx`: the unreadable pack, the empty order and the page's header;
- `CheckoutConfirmationPage.jsx`: the error's button. A missing or unknown order also lands on Models.
