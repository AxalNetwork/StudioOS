### 3. The investor's proof of capital (`capital_attestation`): #1888, migration 491

- **Targets.** Exactly three, each the investor's own:
  - `deal_commitment`: the caller's `pending` `commitments` row on a deal they were invited to;
  - `lp_application`: the caller's Fund I application, status `pending`, `in_review`, `needs_follow_up` or `approved`;
  - `lp_commitment`: the caller's `limited_partners` row, read through `lpSelfScope` (`services/tenancyScope.ts`).
- **The amount.**
  - Whole dollars, at least $1,000, and never above the target's figure. That figure is the commitment amount, `target_commitment`, or the uncalled amount (commitment minus called, counted as `routes/funds.ts` counts calls).
  - It defaults to that figure.
  - It is a **floor**, "at least $X". It is never a range and never a balance, because a range would disclose an upper bound.
- **Accounts.** The investor picks which checking and savings accounts count. The picker shows each account's name, kind and last four. No route returns a balance. Treasury and other kinds are not read (`services/mercury/parse.ts`).
- **Consent.** The `POST` carries `consent: true` and the consent version, or the Worker answers 400 `consent_required`. The sheet shows the exact sentence admins will read.
- **The check.** The Worker re-reads the picked accounts inside the investor's own request.
  - Below the amount: 409, "Nothing was shared."
  - The sum is never stored, returned or logged.
- **The record** stores the target; the floor; the date read (`as_of`) and an expiry 30 days later; the organization's legal name as read; the number of accounts counted; the consent version; the state (`active`, `superseded` or `revoked`); and the admin view count and the last view. It stores no balance, last four, account id, transaction or counterparty.
- **Revocable.** The investor sees each statement's views and revokes it at any time. Disconnecting does not revoke a dated statement.
- **Not accreditation.** It is proof of funds for this commitment, not evidence of accredited status.

### 4. What an admin reads: #1890, screens #1891

- Admin and Super Admin, on HQ, read a statement on three surfaces:
  - the LP application;
  - the deal commitments, in an admin view only, never on `GET /api/deals/:id/commitments`, which other invited investors read;
  - the D714 console's Calls tab.
- Every read writes its `admin_audit_log` row before it answers, or answers 503 `audit_unavailable` (`services/mercury/access.ts`).
- The admin route never reads `integrations` or `mercury_personal_matches`.
- A revoked statement shows no amount and no holder.
- Never on `/funds/lps`, which outside GPs share.
