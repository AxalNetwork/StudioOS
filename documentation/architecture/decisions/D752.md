## D752 — Mercury for every member: investors prove capital to Axal's admins; founders track their spending, revenue and investment deals

Issue #1886 (S08) writes this file and builds sections 1 and 2; the plan is #1869. Each later section is written as its sibling issue decides it, and the sibling PR that builds it replaces the section with its final text and its test list.

### Why

- **Date:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** Proposed (S1). Sections 1 and 2 hold once this file's PR merges; each later section holds once its own PR merges.
- **The owner's ask**, 2026-10-18 at about 13:20Z: "Connector here, should be for other users to connect Mercury … used by Investors to commit capital to prove they have capital to deploy for admins, and Founders to track spendings, revenue, investment deals … find icon or logo as well."
- **The owner's card is the entity card.** Only an Admin on HQ gets audience `entity` (`audienceFor`, `cloudflare-worker/src/integrations/registry.ts`), so the Super Admin will only ever see that card.
- **Founders and partners could already connect their own Mercury** (D713, #1843). **Investors got no card, on purpose** (D713 decision 1). D713 decision 11 also left out cash, burn and a founder's outgoing payments.
- **No proof of funds exists anywhere in the repo.** What investors commit is self-stated:
  - a deal commitment: `POST /api/deals/:id/commitments` (`routes/deals.ts`), from the "Commit Capital" modal (`frontend/src/pages/DealRoomPage.jsx`), stored `pending` forever;
  - the Fund I LP application's `target_commitment` (`sql/migrations/165_lp_applications.sql`);
  - the GP-typed `limited_partners.commitment_amount`.
- **What D752 departs from:** D713 decisions 1, 4 (the uses table), 5 and 11, and D715 decision 10. Each carries an "*Amended by D752*" line.
- **What still holds** from D713 and D715:
  - read-only, connect only;
  - a re-check on every read;
  - an Axal entity's organization is refused (`providers/mercury.ts`, `entityAccountRefusal`);
  - read on demand only, never by a cron, an admin route, an Eadwyn reader or an MCP tool;
  - logs hold counts only;
  - no support session.

### 3. The investor's proof of capital (`capital_attestation`): #1888, migration 491

As built in #1888 (S22). Worker only: `routes/mercury_attestations.ts` at `/api/mercury/attestations`, `services/capitalAttestations.ts` (the only new importer of `mercuryPersonal`), `services/capitalAttestationTerms.ts` (pure: the words, imported by #1890) and `sql/migrations/491_mercury_capital_attestations.sql`.

1. **One use, investors only, on HQ only.** `GET /targets`, `GET /accounts` and `POST /` run `personalPolicy(c, user, 'capital_attestation')`: 403 `support_session_refused`, `purpose_not_allowed` or `hq_only`. `GET /` and `POST /:uid/revoke` refuse a support session and a branch but not a role, so a member whose role changed can still see and revoke what they shared.
2. **Three targets, each the caller's own.**
   - `deal_commitment`: their `pending` `commitments` row on a deal that is not `rejected` and not passed.
   - `lp_application`: their Fund I application in `pending`, `in_review`, `needs_follow_up` or `approved`, with `fund_id` set to Fund I's id when it is found.
   - `lp_commitment`: their `limited_partners` row through `lpSelfScope`, after `claimLpRowsByEmail` as `/lp-portal` does, on Fund I only (`fundPayeeOf`).
   - Another member's target and a missing one answer the same 404 `target_not_found`. A purpose whose store cannot be read is listed in `unreadable`, never as having no targets.
3. **The amount is a floor**, in whole dollars, from $1,000 to the target's figure rounded down: the commitment, `target_commitment`, or the uncalled amount (commitment minus each call's owed cents, counted as `routes/funds.ts` counts them). Omitted, it is the cap. 400 `invalid_amount` or `amount_above_target`. A target with no figure is `amount_missing`, one under $1,000 `below_minimum`, an LP commitment whose calls cannot be read `calls_unreadable`; sharing on one is 409 `target_not_attestable`.
4. **The investor picks the accounts.** `GET /accounts` lists checking and savings accounts by id, name, kind and last four, with the holder's legal name. No balance. The account id goes only to its owner and is never stored or logged.
5. **Consent is explicit and versioned:** `CONSENT_VERSION = 'd752-1'` and `CONSENT_TEMPLATE`, served by `GET /targets`. 400 `consent_required` without `consent: true`; 409 `consent_outdated` on another version. A test holds the words and the version together.
6. **The check runs inside the investor's own request.** Every refusal that needs no bank answers first. Then `personalConnection` makes exactly two GETs and re-runs the read-only and entity checks. Then:
   - 409 `account_not_found` for a picked id that is not now one of their checking or savings accounts;
   - 502 `mercury_unreadable` when no picked account gave a balance (an account without one counts nothing, so the Worker fails closed on the UNVERIFIED field name);
   - 502 `holder_unreadable` with no legal name;
   - 409 `balance_below_amount`, with no figure.

   The sum is one local variable: never stored, returned or logged.
7. **The record is the statement and nothing else** (migration 491): purpose, target, deal or fund, floor, `as_of`, `expires_on` (`as_of` + 30 days), holder, accounts counted, consent version and time, state, `revoked_at`, admin view count and last view. No balance, account id, last four, transaction, counterparty or `integration_id`, so disconnecting does not revoke. DELETE is refused.
8. **One active statement per target.** A new one supersedes it in the same D1 batch, under a partial unique index.
9. **Fixed and final.** A trigger refuses any change to what a statement says. The only moves are active → superseded, active → revoked and superseded → revoked. The view count and the last view may change in any state.
10. **Expiry is computed** (`expired`), never stored. The state stays as it was.
11. **Logs hold counts only:** `capital_attestation_check` (`use=capital_attestation accounts=<picked> unreadable=<n>`, the same for a shared and a refused check) and `capital_attestation_accounts`.
12. **Unchanged:** `READ_PATHS`, no money method, and no cron, admin route, Eadwyn reader or MCP tool reads this. It is proof of funds for a commitment, not evidence of accredited status.

### 5. The founder's spending, burn, cash and runway (`venture_spend`): #1889, screens #1988

As built in #1889 (S22). Worker first: `services/mercurySpend.ts` (pure), two routes in `routes/revenue.ts`, the lock in `routes/progress.ts`, and the founder's words in `integrations/registry.ts` and `pages/account/mercuryModel.js`. No migration. This departs from D713 decisions 4, 5 and 11; every other D713 rule holds.

- **Who.** The project's own founder, on HQ or a branch, from their own connection (`mercuryOwnerOnly`, `routes/revenue.ts`). Never an admin, a partner, an investor, a Lab member who is not the founder, a support session, a cron, an Eadwyn reader or an MCP tool.
- **The read.** `GET /api/revenue/projects/:projectId/mercury-spend?months=1..12`, default 6. It is built per request, and nothing is stored.
  - A month is the UTC calendar month of a line's posted date, else its created date. Months are listed oldest first. The current month is marked partial.
  - Each month has money in from operations, money in from financing, money out and net burn (money out minus operating money in). It also counts the posted, pending, failed and internal lines. A month with no posted line has no figures, never zeros.
  - **Financing** is a credit whose counterparty names an Axal entity, or a line the founder confirmed as a raise receipt (section 6). It is never income and never lowers burn.
  - Internal transfers are neither in nor out. A card purchase is a line like any other, and no card endpoint is read.
  - Each month lists its top five counterparties of money out, and a category only when Mercury reports one (an UNVERIFIED field). These go only to the founder, and are never stored or logged.
  - **Cash now** is the summed available balance of the checking and savings accounts, from the same `/accounts` read. An account without one is counted as unreadable, never as 0.
  - **Runway** is cash divided by the average net burn of the last three full months, through `saasMetrics.runwayMonths`. Otherwise the read gives a reason: not burning, needs three months, cash not read, or partial.
  - At 1,000 lines the result is partial. It gives no runway and cannot be saved.
- **Saving.** `POST …/mercury-spend/snapshot { include_cash, confirm_visible: true }`.
  - Without the confirmation it answers 400 `visibility_confirm_required`, and nothing reaches Mercury.
  - It re-reads the last full month and upserts one `project_metrics` row: `source = 'mercury'`, today's date, that month's `net_burn` and, only with `include_cash`, `cash_balance`. Saving again the same day replaces both.
  - Its `notes` name the month and the read date.
  - The founder is told first: "Saved figures are read wherever your metrics are read: by Axal's admins and partners, on your Grow and Raise pages, in the investor updates you send, and in the drafts you ask the assistant for."
- **The source lock.** A typed snapshot cannot claim `stripe` or `mercury` (400 `source_reserved`). A Mercury row's date and figures cannot be edited (409 `mercury_figure_locked`). Its notes can, and it can be deleted.
- **Stored.** Only what the founder saves: one net burn and, by choice, one cash figure per day. No line, counterparty, category or account is stored.
- **Logs.** One `integration_logs` row per read, with counts only.

### 6. Raise receipts (`raise_receipts`), the founder's investment deals: #1887, migration 490 (built)

#1887 calls this "§5"; in this file's numbering it is section 6. It moves no money and adds no Mercury path: every bank read is the founder's own, through D713's GET-only client.

1. **Two target kinds and one use.** `raise_prospect` and `deal_commitment`, words `raise` and `commitment`, both under `raise_receipts`, which only a founder holds, on HQ and on a branch. `commitment` always means a deal commitment, never an LP commitment. `/status` lists `raise_receipts` for every role, available to a founder only.
2. **A raise prospect is a target** when its `stage` is `committed` and its project's `founder_id` is the caller's `founder_id` (bound, never `user.id`, never `canAccessFounderResource`). A caller with no `founder_id` has none. Its label is "<name or firm> · <project name>".
3. **A deal commitment is a target** when it is `pending` on a deal whose project's `founder_id` is the caller's, through `commitments → deals.project_id → projects.founder_id`. Its label is "<investor's name> · <project name>", the name the founder already sees in the deal room, and never the investor's email.
4. **Whole cents, or not matchable.** Both amounts are REAL dollars, converted with `toCents` (`services/mercury/parse.ts`). A missing or non-positive amount is `amount_missing`, and a fraction of a cent is `amount_not_whole_cents`. Such a target is listed with `matchable: false`, gets no candidates, and confirming it is 409 `not_matchable`. Amounts are USD: neither table has a currency.
5. **The window** runs from 30 days before the target's `created_at` (`RAISE_BEFORE_DAYS`) to today, and never starts more than 366 days before today.
6. **What fits.** A posted credit, not internal and not voided, of exactly the expected cents, posted inside the window. The counterparty is shown and never checked, because investors wire from SPVs, trusts and banks. Several fitting lines are all listed, and the founder picks one.
7. **Confirming** writes the match, and in the same batch:
   - for a prospect, `commit_status = 'wired'` while it is still `committed`;
   - for a commitment, `pending` → `confirmed`.

   Each effect runs only if this confirmation's row exists (`EXISTS (… uid …)`). `deals.capital_committed` does not move. Nothing else is written, and nobody is notified.
8. **Withdrawing** takes a reason of at least 10 characters. A prospect keeps its `commit_status`, the founder's own word. A commitment returns to `pending`.
9. **A changed prospect withdraws its receipt.** Migration 490's trigger `trg_raise_receipt_target_changed` withdraws a confirmed receipt when the prospect's `amount` or `stage` changes, with the fixed reason "The prospect's amount or stage changed after the match (D752)." It covers both writers (`routes/contacts.ts` and the D691 writer in `services/raiseWriters.ts`). The same values saved again, or `commit_status` alone, do not fire it. A commitment's amount has no writer: no file but `services/mercuryPersonal.ts` updates `commitments`, and a test pins that.
10. **One line, one meaning.**
    - A confirmed receipt's line is refused as revenue, `raise_receipt`.
    - A line already on the revenue ledger is never proposed, and confirming one is 409 `imported_as_revenue`.
    - Both checks also sit inside each INSERT (`INSERT … SELECT … WHERE NOT EXISTS`), so an import and a confirmation racing on one line cannot both win.
    - 477's index (one confirmed match per line) stops one line settling two targets of any kind.
11. **A hint, not a refusal.** In `GET /api/revenue/projects/:id/mercury-credits`, each credit that is not a receipt carries `fits_raise_target`: whether it fits one of the founder's open, matchable raise targets. The import is not blocked, because a customer can pay an amount equal to a commitment.
12. **Who sees what.**
    - **The founder** sees their targets, the candidate lines (date, amount, last four, counterparty) and their receipts.
    - **The Raise pipeline's stage cell** reads "Committed · received <posted date>" for anyone who may read the Raise cycle (the venture and HQ admins). It replaces "· soft", and shows no last four, counterparty or bank figure. `services/raiseCycle.ts` reads the stored match's posted date only, and never imports `mercuryPersonal`.
    - **An investor** sees only the commitment's status ("Confirmed") through the unchanged `GET /api/deals/:id/commitments`, with exactly the keys it returned before. They hold no `raise_receipts` use, so `?target=commitment` is 403 `purpose_not_allowed` for them.
    - **Logs** hold counts only: one `integration_logs` row per read, `use=raise_receipts`.
13. **Storage.** A receipt is an ordinary `mercury_personal_matches` row. Migration 490 rebuilds the table, because SQLite cannot widen 477's `target_kind` CHECK. Every row is copied by name, with ids, uids and states kept, and both partial unique indexes are recreated under their names. One table keeps "one confirmed match per bank line" across every kind.
14. **One frontend sentence.** `frontend/test/mercury_model_d715.test.mjs` holds `USE_WORDS` equal to the Worker's `PersonalUse` type, so the key and its words land in one PR. `USE_WORDS.raise_receipts` is that sentence, and a founder's Mercury sheet now lists the use. task `raise-match-screens` adds the match panel and the `raise_receipt` reason's sentence.
15. **Spending.** Receipts are financing, not operating income. `raiseReceiptIds(env, userId, ids?)` is exported for #1889's financing-in. #1889 was not on `main` when this merged, so #1889 wires it.
