## D752 — Mercury for every member: investors prove capital to Axal's admins; founders track their spending, revenue and investment deals

Issue #1886 (S08) writes this file and builds sections 1 and 2; the plan is #1869. Each later section is written as its sibling issue decides it, and the sibling PR that builds it replaces the section with its final text and its test list.

### Why

- **Date:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** Proposed (S1). Sections 1 and 2 hold once this file's PR merges; each later section holds once its own PR merges.
- **The owner's ask**, 2026-10-18 at about 13:20Z: "Connector here, should be for other users to connect Mercury … used by Investors to commit capital to prove they have capital to deploy for admins, and Founders to track spendings, revenue, investment deals … find icon or logo as well."
- **The owner's card is the entity card.** Only an Admin on HQ gets audience `entity` (`audienceFor`, `cloudflare-worker/src/integrations/registry.ts`), so the Super Admin will only ever see that card.
- **Founders and partners could already connect their own Mercury** (D713, #1843). **Investors got no card, on purpose** (D713 decision 1). D713 decision 11 also left out cash, burn and a founder's outgoing payments.
- **No proof of funds exists anywhere in the repo.** What investors commit is self-stated:
  - a deal commitment: `POST /api/deals/:id/commitments` (`routes/deals.ts`), from the "Commit Capital" modal (`frontend/src/pages/DealRoomPage.jsx`), stored `pending` forever;
  - the Fund I LP application's `target_commitment` (`sql/migrations/165_lp_applications.sql`);
  - the GP-typed `limited_partners.commitment_amount`.
- **What D752 departs from:** D713 decisions 1, 4 (the uses table), 5 and 11, and D715 decision 10. Each carries an "*Amended by D752*" line.
- **What still holds** from D713 and D715:
  - read-only, connect only;
  - a re-check on every read;
  - an Axal entity's organization is refused (`providers/mercury.ts`, `entityAccountRefusal`);
  - read on demand only, never by a cron, an admin route, an Eadwyn reader or an MCP tool;
  - logs hold counts only;
  - no support session.

### 3. The investor's proof of capital (`capital_attestation`): #1888, migration 491

As built in #1888 (S22). Worker only: `routes/mercury_attestations.ts` at `/api/mercury/attestations`, `services/capitalAttestations.ts` (the only new importer of `mercuryPersonal`), `services/capitalAttestationTerms.ts` (pure: the words, imported by #1890) and `sql/migrations/491_mercury_capital_attestations.sql`.

1. **One use, investors only, on HQ only.** `GET /targets`, `GET /accounts` and `POST /` run `personalPolicy(c, user, 'capital_attestation')`: 403 `support_session_refused`, `purpose_not_allowed` or `hq_only`. `GET /` and `POST /:uid/revoke` refuse a support session and a branch but not a role, so a member whose role changed can still see and revoke what they shared.
2. **Three targets, each the caller's own.**
   - `deal_commitment`: their `pending` `commitments` row on a deal that is not `rejected` and not passed.
   - `lp_application`: their Fund I application in `pending`, `in_review`, `needs_follow_up` or `approved`, with `fund_id` set to Fund I's id when it is found.
   - `lp_commitment`: their `limited_partners` row through `lpSelfScope`, after `claimLpRowsByEmail` as `/lp-portal` does, on Fund I only (`fundPayeeOf`).
   - Another member's target and a missing one answer the same 404 `target_not_found`. A purpose whose store cannot be read is listed in `unreadable`, never as having no targets.
3. **The amount is a floor**, in whole dollars, from $1,000 to the target's figure rounded down: the commitment, `target_commitment`, or the uncalled amount (commitment minus each call's owed cents, counted as `routes/funds.ts` counts them). Omitted, it is the cap. 400 `invalid_amount` or `amount_above_target`. A target with no figure is `amount_missing`, one under $1,000 `below_minimum`, an LP commitment whose calls cannot be read `calls_unreadable`; sharing on one is 409 `target_not_attestable`.
4. **The investor picks the accounts.** `GET /accounts` lists checking and savings accounts by id, name, kind and last four, with the holder's legal name. No balance. The account id goes only to its owner and is never stored or logged.
5. **Consent is explicit and versioned:** `CONSENT_VERSION = 'd752-1'` and `CONSENT_TEMPLATE`, served by `GET /targets`. 400 `consent_required` without `consent: true`; 409 `consent_outdated` on another version. A test holds the words and the version together.
6. **The check runs inside the investor's own request.** Every refusal that needs no bank answers first. Then `personalConnection` makes exactly two GETs and re-runs the read-only and entity checks. Then:
   - 409 `account_not_found` for a picked id that is not now one of their checking or savings accounts;
   - 502 `mercury_unreadable` when no picked account gave a balance (an account without one counts nothing, so the Worker fails closed on the UNVERIFIED field name);
   - 502 `holder_unreadable` with no legal name;
   - 409 `balance_below_amount`, with no figure.

   The sum is one local variable: never stored, returned or logged.
7. **The record is the statement and nothing else** (migration 491): purpose, target, deal or fund, floor, `as_of`, `expires_on` (`as_of` + 30 days), holder, accounts counted, consent version and time, state, `revoked_at`, admin view count and last view. No balance, account id, last four, transaction, counterparty or `integration_id`, so disconnecting does not revoke. DELETE is refused.
8. **One active statement per target.** A new one supersedes it in the same D1 batch, under a partial unique index.
9. **Fixed and final.** A trigger refuses any change to what a statement says. The only moves are active → superseded, active → revoked and superseded → revoked. The view count and the last view may change in any state.
10. **Expiry is computed** (`expired`), never stored. The state stays as it was.
11. **Logs hold counts only:** `capital_attestation_check` (`use=capital_attestation accounts=<picked> unreadable=<n>`, the same for a shared and a refused check) and `capital_attestation_accounts`.
12. **Unchanged:** `READ_PATHS`, no money method, and no cron, admin route, Eadwyn reader or MCP tool reads this. It is proof of funds for a commitment, not evidence of accredited status.

**Tests:** `cloudflare-worker/test/capital_attestations_d752.test.ts` (24 cases, Mercury stubbed throughout), and `mercury_personal_guard_d713.test.mjs` with the three new files in `PERSONAL` and `services/capitalAttestations.ts` in `callers`. Mutation checks run, each failing the file: consent check removed; cap removed; `MIN_FLOOR_CENTS` at 100; the deal query without `c.investor_user_id`; the `fundPayeeOf` filter dropped; the supersede UPDATE dropped; the sum returned as `sum_cents`; the sum logged; an unreadable balance skipping the check; the revoked clause dropped from the trigger; `personalPolicy` in place of `ownerGate` on revoke; and 17 more listed in the PR.

**Rollback:** revert #1888 together with #1891 and #1890, which build on it. Never edit 491: the table and its rows stay, and nothing reads them. Dropping the table takes a new decision and a new migration.

### 4. What an admin reads: #1890, screens #1891

As built in #1890 (S22). `GET /api/admin/capital-attestations` (`routes/admin_capital_attestations.ts`), the admin helpers in `services/capitalAttestationTerms.ts`, `proof_of_funds` on the LP application queue (`routes/admin_lp_applications.ts`), and three screens built from existing parts (`components/ProofOfFunds.jsx`, `lib/proofOfFunds.js`). No migration.

1. **Who reads.** An Admin on HQ, the Super Admin included, through `requireMercuryReader`: 403 `support_session_refused`, 403 `hq_only` on a branch, 403 for anyone else. Never a cron, an Eadwyn reader or an MCP tool.
2. **What it reads.** `capital_attestations` and `users.name` only. It imports `services/mercury/access`, `capitalAttestationTerms`, `hono`, `types` and `util/` only, so it never reaches a connection or calls Mercury. Labels come from the purpose and ids: "Commitment on deal #<id>", "Fund I application", "Fund I commitment".
3. **Three read forms, one per request:** `?purpose&target_id`, `?deal_id` or `?fund_id` (applications and LP commitments only). Anything else is 400 (`invalid_query`, `invalid_purpose`, `invalid_target_id`, `invalid_deal_id`, `invalid_fund_id`), reading nothing and writing no audit row.
4. **One statement per target:** the active one, else the newest revoked one. Superseded statements are never shown, counted or returned.
5. **What a statement shows.** Active and expired: the floor, `as_of`, `expires_on`, holder, accounts counted, `shared_on` and #1888's `statementSentence`; expired keeps its amount. Revoked: `revoked_on` and "Revoked by the investor on <date>.", with no amount, holder, date read, account count or other statements. Never the consent version, view count or timestamps.
6. **Other active statements** of the same investor sit beside each active item (`other_active`, at most 10, and `other_active_count`): one balance can back more than one statement.
7. **Audited before it answers.** Gate, validate, read, then `auditRead(…, 'capital_attestations_read', { purpose, target_id, deal_id, fund_id, count, viewed })` (ids and counts only), then the view count, then the answer. No audit row: 503 `audit_unavailable`, nothing shown, no view counted. A failed read: 503 `attestations_unreadable`, "This is not a claim that none was shared.", and no audit row.
8. **Each read counts as a view.** One UPDATE adds 1 to `admin_view_count` and sets `last_admin_view_at` on every active statement whose amount the answer carries. Revoked and superseded statements are never counted. A failed count is 503 `audit_unavailable`.
9. **The LP application queue carries a state only:** `active`, `expired`, `revoked`, `none` or `unreadable` (never `none` when the read failed), from the applicant's own statements on that application. No figure, no audit row, no view. Null on a branch or in a support session.
10. **When each surface reads.** The LP application: once per selection (`ProofOfFundsRow` keyed by the application); a decision's reload does not read again, and `none` reads nothing. A deal's Commitments tab: once when it opens, for an admin on HQ only. The Calls tab: only on "Show proofs of funds"; Hide folds it.
11. **Today's figure beside the floor:** "Covers the full amount" or "Covers $X of $Y" against `target_commitment` or the commitment's amount. The Calls panel points to the call ledger for the uncalled figure.
12. **Never anywhere else:** not on `GET /api/deals/:id/commitments`, `/funds/lps`, any founder, partner or investor surface, an export, an Eadwyn reader, an MCP tool or an email.
13. **Not accreditation.** Every proof row says "Proof of funds for this commitment, not evidence of accredited status."; on the LP application it sits under the Rule 501 row.
14. **The icon:** `<BrandIcon provider="mercury" name="Mercury" size={24} />` beside the word "Mercury" on an active or expired statement, drawn by key. A revoked statement, or none, draws no mark.
15. **Unchanged:** no migration, no Mercury path; `GET /api/deals/:id/commitments` and `presentQueueRow` keep their fields.

*Tests:* `cloudflare-worker/test/admin_capital_attestations_d752.test.ts` (12 cases) and `frontend/test/admin_proof_of_funds_d752.test.mjs` (7), with `fund_admin_console_d714.test.mjs` and `brand_icons_d687.test.mjs` extended. The issue's mutation checks were run and each failed a test. Two notes: the personal guard's `callers` test sees only direct imports of `mercuryPersonal`, so a transitive import through `capitalAttestations` is caught by this route's own static test; and dropping the fund read's purpose filter is an equivalent mutant, because migration 491's CHECK already keeps `fund_id` off a deal commitment.

*Rollback:* revert the PR. Nothing is stored by it but audit rows and view counts. The audit rows stay; 491's `admin_view_count` and `last_admin_view_at` keep their values, and nothing then writes them. No migration is involved.
