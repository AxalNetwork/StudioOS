### 1. The Access workspace

`frontend/src/pages/hq/security/AccessWorkspace.jsx` replaces the `access` entry's session zone in `SecurityConsole`'s `workspaces` map. The four tiles and H7's Data access zone stay around it.

- **The sessions table.** Columns: Account / role, Browser and OS, Started, Last active, Status, Expires, and the row's acts. It is a real `table` with a `caption` and `th scope`. Checkbox labels name the account and the device.
  - **Browser and OS** come from the stored user agent, parsed in the client (`parseUserAgent.js`), for example "Firefox 132 · Windows 10 or 11". Unrecognised is "Browser not recognised", never a guess. A row with no user agent says "No user agent stored".
  - **The raw user agent, the IP, the session id, the sign-in factor** and the times in the viewer's own zone sit under View details, which is a disclosure (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`).
  - **Times** are UTC to the second ("Sat 7 Nov 2026, 09:47:12 UTC"), in `<time dateTime>`. Last active adds a relative time. A D1 time with no zone marker is read as UTC, never as local time.
  - **Expires** is created + `token_hours` (24). It is derived, and the details say so, because nothing stores it.
  - **Status** is one of four words, used the same way in the table, the phone cards, the dialogs and the counts: Current, Active, Expired, Revoked. Each is drawn with part 1's `StatusBadge`: Current over Pending and Active over Healthy, as the canvas draws them, Revoked over Verified, and Expired over Not applicable.
  - **Your own row** reads Current, has no checkbox, and offers Sign out account only. The other active rows offer Revoke this session… and Sign out account…. An expired row offers no revoke.
- **Search.** "Filter by account" filters the rows that were read, by name, address, role, browser, OS or IP.
- **Bulk revoke.** Select rows, then Revoke selected (n)…. Select-all takes the active rows shown, never your own current session, and stops at `SESSION_BULK_LIMIT` (100). At 100 it says so, and the other checkboxes are disabled.
- **The support-sessions table.** Support admin, Acting as, Started, Context, Status and two acts: End support session… and Sign out {person}….
- **"What each action reaches"** is a card that states the four acts' reach in one line each, and the write bar that all of them share.
- **The emergency card** sits beside it: "Signs out every HQ-held account, you included. Branch databases are not touched."
- **Phone (390).** The same table rows become stacked cards: the column headings are hidden and each cell carries its own label. Controls are 44 px and the dialogs' buttons are 48 px. The dialogs become bottom sheets with the same three steps (SE11c, SEC "Touch").

**The states.**
- **Loading** claims nothing: "Loading sessions — nothing is claimed yet."
- **Unreadable** (the page's `UNAVAILABLE`) draws no table, no count, no session act and no 0, only "The session list could not be read. This is not a claim that nobody is signed in." and Retry. A count the reply did not carry is Unavailable with Retry, through `ReadValue`.
- **Empty** says what was read. With no row at all: "The table was read; it held no active row." With only yours: "Only your own session is active." No live support session is a Measured zero ("0 live").
- **Read-only until step-up.** The list carries your own row and its `factor`. When it is not `totp`, every act would be refused by `requireFactor('totp')`, so each one is disabled and the page says why. A write-bar refusal from the server also turns the page read-only. When your own row is not among the rows read, the page says it cannot tell and leaves the decision to the server.
- **What changed since the read.** The list is not re-read behind an act. A confirmed revoke marks its row Revoked, in the dialog's word, and a confirmed sign-out marks every row of that account. A "n changes confirmed since this list was read · Re-read" line appears. Re-reading on its own would have been wrong twice over: the row would vanish instead of reading Revoked, and after the two acts that sign the operator out, the next request answers 401 and the page leaves for /login before the result can be read.

### 2. One Scope → Review → Result pattern

`ActionDialog.jsx` is the one shell. `AccessActions.jsx` holds the five acts in it: revoke one, revoke selected, sign out account, end support session, and emergency re-authentication.

- **Scope** states the exact reach (`ScopeBox`) and whether you are affected (`YouAffected`). It collects that act's own reason. Review cannot be reached with fewer than `MIN_REASON` (8) characters, and the error is linked by `aria-describedby` and announced. Scope also notes the write bar: "Needs a session signed in with your authenticator (TOTP) and a recent step-up. If the step-up has lapsed you will be asked for a code, and the request continues after it." That is `request()`'s own step-up retry.
- **Review** lists scope, you, the reason and authority. Bulk revoke adds every row and the limit. Emergency adds who is not affected, the count as Not instrumented, and the typed confirmation.
- **Result** is reached only from the request's answer. While the request is in flight the dialog says "Waiting for the server. Nothing is shown as done until it answers." Esc is ignored then, and no outcome is drawn.

| Act | Exact scope, as the code does it | You |
|---|---|---|
| Revoke this session | one `user_sessions` row; the account's other sessions stay signed in | "You are not affected", or, for another of your own sessions, that the one you are using stays signed in. Your current session is not offered; a 409 `own_current_session` is drawn as Refused. |
| Sign out account | every session of that account, on every device (the floor bump); "n of their sessions are in the list read on this page; any it does not show are reached too" | for your own account: "You will be signed out too", and the result ends at Sign in again |
| End support session | closes one record; it can no longer be extended; its token stays valid until it expires, "no later than {now + 30 min}"; the person is not signed out | your own admin session continues |
| Emergency re-authentication | "every HQ-held account; branch databases are not touched; you will be signed out too", with a typed confirmation (`SIGN OUT HQ`) | "You will be signed out too", and the result ends at Sign in again |

**Eight results**, each with its own words and state (`RESULTS`, `resultOfError`):

| Result | When | Drawn as |
|---|---|---|
| Done | a 2xx reply | Verified · "Confirmed by the server; reply received …" |
| Partly done | a bulk reply with some rows revoked | Action required · "Revoked x of n sessions" |
| Refused | any other 4xx: own current session, write bar, reason | Action required · the reason, nothing marked |
| Failed | a 5xx | Failed · "re-read before retrying", with Re-read and Try again |
| Outcome unknown | no HTTP status: a timeout or a dropped connection | Unknown · "The server didn't confirm the change. Nothing is shown as done.", with Re-read and Try again (SE05g) |
| Already revoked | 409 `already_revoked`, or `already_ended` for a support session | Not applicable · the row is marked Revoked, because it is |
| Not found | 404 `session_not_found` or `user_not_found` | Not applicable · nothing marked |
| Expired | 401 (your session) or `past_ceiling` (the support record) | Stale · nothing marked |

**Bulk outcomes per row**, from the route's `results`: Revoked, Already revoked and Not found. A row the reply does not mention reads "No answer for this row" (Unknown), never assumed revoked.

**Keyboard and focus (SEC).**
- Focus moves to the step's heading when the dialog opens and at each step, so the result is read first.
- Tab and Shift+Tab wrap inside the dialog.
- Esc closes the dialog, except while a request is in flight.
- On close, focus returns to the control that opened the dialog. When that control is gone (a revoked row has no Revoke button), focus goes to the table's caption.
- The fields are labelled. The close button is "Cancel and close".

### 3. How it follows D4803's A1, B1 and C1

- **A1 (HQ-only wording).** "every account on every tenant" and "Force re-auth · all tenants" are gone from the page. The emergency act says "every HQ-held account; branch databases are not touched; you will be signed out too" in the reach card, the Review step and part 1's Overview, and the Scope step lists each of the three facts. Part 1 had left the Overview Emergency card unbuilt (D4800 §4). It is now a small card at the foot of Overview with that wording and a link to `?tab=access#emergency`. The act runs only from Access.
- **B1 (one reason per bulk act).** One reason for the selection, shown in the Review step beside the rows, with "Each revoke is recorded separately, with the one reason below".
- **C1 (support-session cut-off).** The result says "Support session record closed — its token is still valid" and "no later than {reply time + 30 min}". It offers "Sign out {person} now…", which opens that account's Sign out dialog. The table row then reads "Record closed" with the same time.

### 4. Departures

- **The reason floor is 8, not 10.** SEB and SE05d say "at least 10 characters (server's MIN_REASON)". The server's `MIN_REASON` is 8 (`admin_security.ts`), and the issue's comment says 8. The page follows the server, so it never offers a reason the server would refuse, or refuses one it would take.
- **No count before the emergency run.** SE05e draws "HQ-held accounts · 14 active" and "Sign out 14 HQ accounts". No read returns that number: the count is `affected`, known only after the run. Review draws "How many: Not instrumented", the button says "Sign out every HQ-held account", and the result shows `affected` (Unavailable if the reply has none, never 0).
- **Expires is 24 hours.** SE04b draws issued + 7 days. The token lifetime is `token_hours` (`JWT_EXPIRY_HOURS`, 24) and the list's own predicate uses it.
- **Windows 10 or 11, and macOS with no version.** The canvas draws "Windows 11" and "macOS 15". The user-agent string does not distinguish Windows 10 from 11, and every Mac sends 10.15.7.
- **Every act has all three steps.** SE05a, SE05f and SE11c draw revoke-one and end-support with the confirm button on the Scope step. #4801 asks for one Scope → Review → Result pattern, so Review is its own step for all five.
- **Administrator sign-in strength is not a table.** SE04a lists each admin with their second factor. `/overview` returns two counts only (D4800 §5), so the "Admins with MFA" tile and the Overview finding stay. Per admin, the factor is not read.
- **"Revoked in last 7 days" tile is not drawn.** No read returns it. The Failed sign-ins tile stays where it was.
- **The emergency card survives an unreadable list.** "No list, no actions" applies to the session acts. Emergency re-authentication does not depend on the list, and in an incident it must stay reachable.
- **Names, with the address as fallback.** People are named by `name`. When an account has none, the address stands in (the D481 rule), and then "Account #id". The canvas draws an address for the support target ("founder@lumen.example"); where a name exists the page shows the name.
- **The four Access tiles draw Unavailable** (`StatusBadge`) for a figure that could not be read, instead of "Not recorded". The other zones' tiles keep their words until their slice. "Impersonations live" is renamed "Live support sessions", to match the table.

### 5. What is Not instrumented

Each is drawn with the Not instrumented badge and its reason, where it would appear:
- **Location**: only the IP is stored (View details).
- **Device name**: only the raw user agent is stored (View details).
- **Who revoked a session**: the session row does not record it, only the admin audit log does, in Events (View details).
- **Which devices a sign-out reached**: the floor bump does not list sessions (the Sign out result).
- **How many accounts emergency re-authentication reaches, before it runs**: the server counts them as it signs them out (Review, and the emergency card).
