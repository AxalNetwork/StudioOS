### 2. What the ring does now

| Rule | Where |

### 3. Two readings of the request, named

- **A wave is emitted on the raw band level, not the smoothed one.** With the 400ms
  release, the smoothed level stays above 0.12 for about half a second after speech
  stops, so waves would keep leaving for up to 2s. On the raw level the last wave
  leaves within a frame of silence and fades by 1.4s, as the request's "Waves stop
  within 1.4s of silence" asks.
- **"Back to circles within about 0.5s"** is read as the ring's outline: within 0.6s of
  silence every ring is within 1px of a circle. Its radius settles once the gate closes
  (under a second from speech level), because breathing fades with the gate.

The rings' state now outlives a restart of the drawing loop (a new mode or a mark), so
a pause eases the rings back instead of snapping them.
