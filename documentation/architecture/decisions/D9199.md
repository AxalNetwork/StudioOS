## D9199 — A section says what happened: failed revenue reads are Unavailable, a Due diligence plan gate is locked (#9199)

- **Status:** built by S23 on #9199, 2026-11-28.
- **Chosen by:** the coordinator audit of 2026-11-27 (UXAPP-3, UXAPP-6), as filed on the issue.

### The problem

- **HQ Revenue.** With no currency recorded, `ExecutiveSummary.jsx` drew Contracted run-rate, Refunds and Open disputes as Loading whenever any of the three reads was not ready. A failed read is not ready either, so a disputes read that answered 503 `stripe_not_configured` kept all three tiles on Loading forever, under the comment that promised "Unavailable, never 0".
- **Due diligence.** `DdCasesTab.jsx` drew any failed list read as "We could not load the cases." with Try again, including the 402 the `/api/dd` investor Professional prefix (D1421) answers. Retrying cannot change a plan. The sub-copy also said "The rest of Approvals still works" on the Due diligence page.

### What was decided

- `revenueModel.js` `placeholderTiles({ summary, refunds, disputes })` decides the no-currency tiles. It returns `null` once all three reads answered, and the page then says there is nothing in any currency. Otherwise each tile takes its own read's state: `pendingState` gives Loading while the read is out and Unavailable once it failed. A read that answered with nothing is a measured Zero, or Unavailable when the endpoint said it could not read its source (`available: false`). The Retry buttons are unchanged.
- `ddListModel.js` `listFailure(e)` sorts a failed list read into three kinds. A 402, or an `investor_tier_required` / `tier_required` body, is `locked`. A 404 is `unavailable` (the dev FastAPI, as before). Anything else is `failed`. When the list is locked, the Cases tab draws one locked state in place of the tiles, charts and list. It names the plan the Worker gave (`lockedTitle`), shows See plans to `/pricing?for=investor` (the link every investor plan gate uses, e.g. `DealFreePlan.jsx`), and has no Try again. Open a case is hidden. Every other failure keeps the failure state with Try again.
- `ddDeskApi.cases` and `ddDeskApi.overview` pass `inlinePaywall: true` (D651), because the tab draws the plan refusal itself and the paywall modal does not open over it. A 402 on the overview is not reported as an error.
- `LIST_FAILED_SUB` now says "The rest of Due diligence still works."
