### DX6 built 2026-11-14: Screening as a decision table (#5909)

`/deals/screening` is drawn to board DE06 (canvas section DX6). Frontend only: no Worker route, migration or API module changed.

- **The summary is the canvas's four figures, each from a read.** *Deals to screen* is the desk (`GET /api/deals/screening`) less the deals you have already decided in your deal flow (`GET /api/deals/flow`: moved to Diligence, Commit or Closing, or passed). *Scorecards ready* is the desk's deals whose scorecard (`POST /api/venture-scores/batch`) is open to you with a scope scored. *Low-confidence scorecards* is the ready ones whose every scored scope reads Low; a scored scope with no confidence word is not counted as Low. A figure whose read failed says Unreadable and one still in flight says Reading…; neither prints 0.
- **The table is Venture, Scorecard, Fit, Material risks, Confidence, Last change and Your decision.** Venture links to the deal page; Scorecard is the eleven-scope strip with its panel link; Fit is the investor's thesis fit (`GET /api/deals/fit`, D1239), shown as score · confidence as on Deal flow; Confidence counts the scored scopes by the word each carries (for example “High 1 · Medium 1”).
- **Your decision is the investor's own deal flow.** An undecided deal offers *Move to diligence* (adds the deal to the flow at Diligence, or moves its Sourced or Screening row there) and *Pass…*, which opens the deal flow's existing reason dialog (`PassDialog`, D1241): nothing is sent until one of the five reasons is chosen, so Passed is reached only with a reason. A decided deal shows its decision (“In Diligence”, “Passed · Valuation”). An unreadable flow shows Unreadable with Try again, never Pass. A reader without an investor licence (the flow refuses them) is told an investor records this in their own deal flow.
- **Pass reasons is a secondary view.** It sits behind a disclosure under the table, closed at first; the Pass reasons chip opens the same view. Its note says what it counts: the studio's passes, each of which closes a round (`GET /api/deals/pass-analytics`). A pass recorded in the table is the investor's own and does not change those counts.

Not recorded, because no read carries a field behind them:

- **Material risks** — the batch sends each scope's band and confidence word, not the venture's named risks or its risk layers.
- **Last change** — neither the desk nor the scorecard batch says when a deal or its screen last changed.
- **Stale screens** — follows from Last change: with no time of last change there is nothing to call stale.

Each is drawn as Not recorded and stated in the page's “What this desk does not do” block. Serving them needs a Worker change (an `updated_at` and the risk layers on the screening read), which #5909 did not include.

Departures:

- **The chip and ops rows stay.** DE06 draws neither, but the ops row carries Export (and two stated-unbuilt controls) and the chips are declared in `investorZoneFilters.js`; the chips now open or close the Pass reasons view instead of replacing the table.
- **Confidence is a count per word, not one word per venture.** The canvas draws “Medium”; a scorecard has eleven scopes and no total, so a single word would be an aggregate nobody computed.
- **Phone width:** the summary is two figures a row; the table scrolls sideways, as the sibling zones' instruments do; the decision buttons and the disclosure are 44px targets on a phone.
