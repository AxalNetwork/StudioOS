### What the walkthrough found

- In the Operating Partner preview, Pipeline opened on "Set up your pipeline", Offers on "Start with what you sell" and Delivery on "No active client work yet": three starting states, and no page said which comes first. On empty Delivery the Eadwyn rail was the main-area button.
- `DeliveryEmpty` always links to `/pipeline/proposals`, and the `delivery-primary` bar draws a second "Open Pipeline". Neither reads the catalogue or the proposals, so a firm with no offer and a firm with one see the same thing.
- No partner screen sends a proposal. `LeadProposalDraft.jsx` saves a draft "not sent", and the only caller of `api.submitQuote` is `QuoteFormModal` on `/build/marketplace`, which is guarded to founder and admin, although the Worker's route accepts partners.
- A founder's accept inserts the engagement in the same batch, with status Accepted; Start is a partner click. A catalogue request (`service_engagements`) is never an engagement.
- A won proposal links to `/delivery/board`, not to its engagement: the rows from `readProposals` carry `engagement_id` only. `GET /board` filters on `e.partner_id` and ignores the active company, while the catalogue and proposals are company-scoped.

### What the canvas decides

- Empty Delivery reads the firm's offers and proposals and shows one primary for its state: with no offer, **Add your first service**, with the path to a first delivery in four steps (OPO01); with an offer and no proposal, **Review matching leads** (OPO03); with a proposal waiting on the founder, **Open the proposal** (OPO05). The duplicate bar goes.
- **What creates a delivery** explains the condition: three manual hand-offs and one automatic step, each with who may do it, the data it needs and when it is refused, and what does not create a delivery. Nothing converts a proposal on its own (OPO06, OPO05).
- A partner sends a proposal from the saved draft in the lead's opportunity sheet, after **Review before sending**, then **Send proposal** (OPO04, ⚑ Q1).
- The first engagement appears on Board with **Assign an owner** and **Start work** (OPO07). A won proposal opens that engagement's sheet directly (OPO08).
- Offers › Catalog, Leads and Proposals opened from Delivery carry **Back to Delivery**; the firm context survives the round trip, and on return focus moves to the primary with one polite status (OPO02, OPO09).
