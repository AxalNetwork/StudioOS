## D710 — Mercury as a read-only connector for fund administration: entity connections a Super Admin adds, synced hourly and reconciled against capital-call lines and fee periods, with no code that can move money

Status: Decided, partly built. Date: 2026-10-17. Slot: S14 (moved from S02). Issue: #1775.

The owner chose Mercury as the bank provider that D686 left open
(`D686.md:85`, `:280`, `:544`). Admin accounts connect to Mercury for automated
reporting and fund administration. Other profiles will use Mercury only for
their own income, revenue and partner deals. That is #1778, a later task with
its own tables.

## What was decided

1. **Mercury is a registry provider.** Its key is `mercury`, category
   `Accounting and banking`, `auth_type: 'api_key'`, `status: 'beta'`,
   `tier: 'growth'` and icon `Landmark`. Its `pulls`, `sends` and `feeds` are
   empty, and it has one permission sentence. No per-user `ProviderImpl` is
   registered, so `has_implementation` is false. The code lives in
   `cloudflare-worker/src/services/mercury/`.
2. **Read-only by construction.**
   - `services/mercury/client.ts` is the only file that names Mercury's API host.
   - It sends GET only, to a frozen allowlist of read paths, with
     `redirect: 'manual'`. A 3xx is an error and is never followed.
   - An error carries a status and a code, never a body.
   - Nothing sends, transfers or requests money, approves a request, or edits a
     transaction, note or category.
   - There is no webhook; the scheduled pull is the only source.
3. **Entity connections are their own store,** `mercury_entity_connections`
   (migration 474), not `integrations` rows.
   - There is one live connection per Axal entity, keyed by `AxalEntityKey`.
   - The token is `v1.` ciphertext under KEK_PII, with its AAD bound to this
     table and row.
   - **The departure from D649 and D650.** Those decisions make every connection
     one member's (`D649.md:34`, `D650.md:35`). Four Axal organizations, shared
     by several admins, must keep syncing when a person leaves, and the LLCs are
     not `company_profiles`. So for fund administration only, a connection
     belongs to the entity, not to the member who added it.
   - **The connect check** compares the token's checking and savings accounts
     with #1773's `entity_bank_accounts`, by entity and the clear last four only.
     It never decrypts a number. The organization's legal name is a flag, not
     the gate.
4. **Who may do what** (`services/mercury/access.ts`).
   - **Connect, replace and disconnect** are the Super Admin's, behind
     `requireSuperAdminWriteBar`. A plain Admin gets 403 `super_admin_required`
     before any TOTP or step-up demand, and nothing is written.
   - **Reads, sync now and reconciliation** are an Admin's on HQ.
   - Every route refuses a support session and a branch.
5. **The catalogue knows who may see Mercury.**
   - `ProviderDescriptor` gains `entity_roles` (`['admin']`) and
     `personal_roles` (`[]`).
   - `GET /available` lists Mercury to an admin on HQ only, with
     `connection_source: 'mercury_entity_connections'`.
   - Everyone else, and every role on a branch, is answered as if the key did
     not exist. An HQ admin's `POST /connect` gets 409 `entity_connection_only`.
6. **Store only what a use needs.**
   - **Accounts:** the Mercury id, kind, last four, nickname, status and the
     balances in cents.
   - **Transactions:** the Mercury id, signed cents, dates, counterparty, masked
     memo, status and kind.
   - **Organization:** the id and legal name. Every other field is dropped in
     `parse.ts`, the account, routing and EIN fields included.
   - `SECRET_KEY_RE` masks `account_number`, `routing_number`, `tax_id` and a
     whole-key `ein`.
7. **Every read is audit-logged first, and does not happen without its row.**
   Writes call `logAdminAction` after the act.
8. **Sync hourly, from HQ only.** It runs at minute 17, gated on `hqCadences`.
   - One run at a time per connection, and sync now at most once in five minutes.
   - The first sync reads from 1 January of the previous year. Later syncs start
     from a watermark: the latest stored date minus ten days, or the oldest
     pending line's date if that is earlier.
   - Lines are read oldest first, at most 2,000 per run. A run that stops at the
     cap records `partial`.
   - A 401 or 403 turns the connection `error`. A 429 or a timeout keeps it
     active.
9. **Reconciliation proposes; a person confirms.**
   - Only a posted credit on a registered account, and not an internal transfer,
     is proposed. A match is found by amount and date window, never by memo.
   - Confirming a capital-call match writes exactly one receipt with
     `reference: 'mercury:<transaction id>'`. A unique index on that prefix makes
     a second receipt for one bank line impossible.
   - Confirming a fee-period match records the link only.
   - A rejected pair is never proposed again.
   - A line Mercury later changes or cancels turns its match `needs_review`.
     Nothing a confirmed match wrote is undone (D371).
10. **No Fund cash before the Fund has an account.** A credit on the GP's or
    Management's account is never matched to a capital-call line.

## Step 7: wiring #1773 and #1774

#1773 (the registry and `fundPayeeOf`) merged while #1798 was open; #1774 (fee
periods, #1799) merged after it, and the closing PR wires it.

- **The connect check** reads `entity_bank_accounts` (migration 472): active
  rows, by entity and the clear last four. An entity with no registered account
  answers 409 `mercury_entity_mismatch`.
- **Fund I.** `match.ts`'s default `fundOneId` reads the `spinout-fund-i` fund
  through `Funds.bySlug` and keeps it only when `fundPayeeOf` answers `'fund'`.
- **The fee periods.** The default `feePeriods(env, fundId)` reads Fund I's
  issued or paid `fund_fee_periods` (migration 473), with `net_cents` as the
  net total. A draft, or another fund's period, is never matched; with no Fund
  I there are none.
- **Collected.** `readPeriod` in `services/fundFees.ts`, which
  `GET /api/fund-fees/:fundId/periods/:quarter` answers, carries
  `collected: { collected_on, last_four } | null` from `collectedFor`. LP
  statements do not carry it.
- **The write bar.** #1779's `assertAdminAct` names no Mercury act, so
  `requireMercuryCredentialWriter` keeps its inline `super_admin_required`
  check.

## The Mercury API (step 0)

**UNVERIFIED, 2026-10-17.** docs.mercury.com, mercury.com and Mercury's brand
page could not be reached from the building session: the proxy refused them.
These facts are built from memory and are marked `UNVERIFIED` in the code. The
owner's first connect is the live check.

- **Base URL and auth:** `https://api.mercury.com/api/v1`, with `Authorization: Bearer <token>`.
- **Token permissions:** Mercury issues read-only and read-write tokens.
  Whether the API reports a token's permission is not known, so the connect
  form requires `confirm_read_only: true`, and the GET-only client is the
  guarantee.
- **Read paths:** `GET /accounts` (`accounts[]`), `GET /organization` (its
  legal name in `legalBusinessName`) and `GET /account/:id/transactions`
  (`transactions[]` and `total`).
- **Account fields:** `id`, `accountNumber`, `kind` (`checking` or `savings`;
  other kinds are skipped), `nickname` or `name`, `status`, `currentBalance` and
  `availableBalance`.
- **Transaction fields:** `id`, `amount` (signed dollars), `counterpartyName`,
  `createdAt`, `postedAt`, `status` and `kind`. The memo is `externalMemo`.
- **Values:** a settled line's status is `sent`, and a voided one is `cancelled`
  or `failed`. A move between two accounts of one organization has kind
  `internalTransfer`. How a transfer between two Axal organizations is marked is
  not known, so such a credit is proposed only if its amount and window fit.
- **Pagination:** `limit`, `offset`, `start` (YYYY-MM-DD, assumed to filter on
  the creation date) and `order=asc`. Rate limits are not known; a 429 is
  recorded and retried next hour.
- **Amounts:** dollars as JSON numbers. They are converted to cents only when
  they are a whole number of cents.

**Denied paths.** The guard test (`mercury_read_only_d710.test.mjs`) holds the
client away from Mercury's money-movement, recipient, webhook and token paths:
`/send-money`, `/request-send-money`, `/transfer`, `/internal-transfer`,
`/recipients`, `/recipient`, `/webhooks`, `/webhook`, `/api-tokens`,
`/tokens`, `/categories`, `/note` and `/approve`. It also holds every source
file away from `send-money`, `sendMoney`, `transfer-money`, `requestSendMoney`
and `requestTransferMoney`.

## The brand icon

Simple Icons 16.35.0 has no Mercury mark: its npm tarball lists no
`icons/mercury.svg` (read 2026-10-17). Mercury's brand terms could not be
read. So under D687, `mercury` is a `BRAND_MONOGRAM` entry, never the
canvas's placeholder colour.

## Owner steps (after the screens ship)

- In each Mercury organization, create a read-only API token, and enter it only
  in the connect form.
- Connect Holdings, Management and the GP as the Super Admin, once #1773 has
  registered their accounts.
- After a disconnect, delete the token in Mercury too. The Worker calls no token
  endpoint.
- Confirm that `KEK_PII` is set on the production Worker (`PRODUCTION.md` §4).
  Changing it leaves every stored token unreadable until it is entered again.
- Connect the Fund once its account is open and #1773 registers it.

## How it was verified

The new tests are `mercury_entity_connections_d710.test.ts`,
`mercury_sync_d710.test.ts`, `mercury_matches_d710.test.ts`,
`mercury_read_only_d710.test.mjs` and `connectors_audience_d710.test.ts`, with
the frontend pins in `brand_icons_d687.test.mjs` and `connect_tool_d686.test.mjs`.
They use real `node:sqlite` with migrations 472 and 474, registry rows that
satisfy 472's checks, and a stub for every Mercury call. Each was mutation-checked. Then
`npm run build` and `npm run test:drift` were run.
