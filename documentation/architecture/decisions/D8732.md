### What the walkthrough found

- `todayTile` draws booked rows and open slots as plain `div`s. The card's only link is Open calendar → `/calendar`, so an advisor preparing for a session has to find it again in the calendar.
- `advisorToday.js` drops cancelled and no-show items and keeps only items starting at or after now, so a session leaves Today the moment it starts.
- The Practice focus link never lands: `recordLink` sends `?record=session&id=` to `lifecycle-<id>`, but `SessionActions.jsx` renders `session-card-<id>`, and after 15 s the page says the record could not be located.
- The preview withheld Today's contents; the populated card was checked in source and still awaits a live booking under #8688.

### What the canvas decides

- The shipped Advisor Studio stays as it is (Customise as the one primary, Eadwyn's interview, the locked profile band, the five modules, no side rail). Only the Today card changes.
- Today opens with a day line (date, "From 09:10", the advisor's time zone). Rows lead with time and person, so two sessions on one day with the same topic stay distinct.
- Each booked row is one link to the existing Calendar item drawer (`/calendar/item/advisor_booking/<id>`): When, Where with Join from 15 minutes before, People, Open booking, the founder's booking notes as preparation context, Copies and "No cancel here". Close returns focus to the same row.
- Row statuses are words: Confirmed, Awaiting your answer, ◌ Not booked, ✕ Cancelled, In progress. Actions are Open session, Manage availability (open slots, → `/practice/sessions`) and View.
- When the detail cannot open, a 503 shows Unreadable with Try again and a dated fallback from Today's read, a 404 shows the not-party refusal with the same fallback, and a stale tab shows that the booking was cancelled after Studio read it. Each offers "See Thu 26 Nov in Calendar" (`?date=`).
- Today states: loading, partial with Retry and the remaining bookings shown, a cancelled booking, an empty day leading to Calendar, all sources unavailable, and Stale with Refresh after 30 minutes.
- Phone 390 draws the same three steps as a sheet with Back, plus partial, cancelled, unreadable, empty and stale. Dark, focus order, headings, keyboard, dates and live regions are drawn.
