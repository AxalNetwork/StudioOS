### 1. When the phone layout applies

Below 1024px, the shared phone width (`usePhoneWidth`, `PHONE_QUERY`). At 1024px and up nothing changes: the tables, the toolbar filters, and the record as PRF3's lightbox.

### 2. The queue and All profiles (PRF11a)

**Cards in place of the tables.** Each card shows:
- on the queue, the age pill (amber from 72 hours); on All profiles, the status, and "● Changed after verified" when it applies;
- the name and email;
- the onboarding category, a Founder's track, and the sources;
- a › that opens the profile at its own address.

A card is one button, at least 56px tall.

**Filters in a bottom sheet** (`PhoneSheet`, titled "Filters and sort"):
- "Filters · n" opens it and counts what is set. On the queue there is also "Sort: [current] ▾", which opens the same sheet.
- Each filter applies as it is chosen, one read per change, as on a desktop.
- "Clear filters" clears that tab's filters and keeps the sort. "Done" closes the sheet.
- **The queue's sheet:** Source, Onboarding category, Founder track and Sort.
- **All profiles' sheet:** every filter of its toolbar, then Sort.

**Not drawn on a phone:**
- the row checkboxes, the Columns picker and each row's "⋯";
- Rows per page (25 a page).

What the "⋯" did is still reachable: Export this view is in the header's More, and Reopen is on the profile's own page.

### 3. One profile (PRF11b)

**A full page in the list's place.** The record is the same `Lightbox`, drawn as a page (`pageBelow="lg"`), and the list stays mounted under it, hidden. So its search, filters, page and the card that opened it are all there on the way back.

**The head:**
- "‹ To review" or "‹ All profiles", the list it came from;
- the name, the status and the sources;
- "email · ref · Opening this profile is recorded."

**The blocks, in one column:**
1. Who they are (with the tax ID), the track details, Eadwyn's summary, the conversation, an invitation's and Eadwyn's answers;
2. the account and the identity check, read only (and Unbind for a Super Admin, or its one sentence for an Admin);
3. the "Agreement and notes" row;
4. PRF6's result, when there is one;
5. History.

**Not drawn on a phone:** Previous and Next.

**The bar.** Reject… on the left and Verify… on the right, wider, in a bar kept at the bottom of the screen (`sticky bottom-0`, above the safe area). A decided profile's bar shows its verdict and Reopen…. A Draft has no bar. The dialogs are PRF5's, unchanged.

### 4. Agreement and notes (PRF11c)

**The row** says what is chosen, whether it is ready, and whether a note is written (`agreementRowLine`):
- "Subscription Booklet & LPA · Ready · 1 note", or "No agreement · No note";
- an invitation's profile reads "Sent by the invitation · …";
- an agreement no longer in the list says so;
- while the agreements are unread, no readiness is claimed; when they could not be read, the row says that.

**Its page** has the same three fields as the Decision card: first agreement, send with, and notes. Its head reads "‹ [name]", "Agreement and notes", and "Choose a first agreement if one fits, and leave a note for other admins."

**Choices to check:**
- **Back and Done.** The page edits a copy. Back leaves the record's choice as it was; Done keeps the change.
- **Nothing is saved here.** As on a desktop, the agreement and notes are written by Verify or Reject.

### 5. 44 by 44 px

**The shared class.** `ppStyles.js` exports `TAP` (`max-lg:min-h-[44px] max-lg:min-w-[44px]`). It is on every button and input class the page, the record and the dialogs share.

**Also sized up below 1024px:**
- the selects and the search (`max-lg:h-11`);
- the Refresh button;
- Details, View as table, the status chips, the radio labels;
- the "connect DocuSign" and "See them in All profiles" links.

**The header** (D936's `AdminPageHeader`) is already 44px on a phone.
