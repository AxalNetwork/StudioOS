### 1. The founder's page (SC8) at `/raise/scorecard`

The page reads part 3's full read (`GET /api/venture-scores/:projectId`), which recomputes on read.
- **Which venture:** `?project_id=` (or `?project=`); with neither, the first venture the caller can open.
- **No venture yet:** the page says so.

**Two tabs**, each at its own address: Scorecard (`/raise/scorecard`) and Trajectory (`/raise/scorecard/trajectory`, §4).

**Scorecard** has two views, "My view" and "What investors see", with Sharing beside them.

- **My view: each of the eleven scopes.**
  - **A scored scope:** its value, band, confidence and coverage ("3 of 4 inputs").
  - **A scope with no value:** why — Not enough recorded, Not expected yet, or Could not be read. It is never 0 and never 50.
  - **A review:** shown beside the computed value, with whether it was made against an earlier score.
- **Opening a scope** lists its inputs:
  - each input's value as the Worker words it, or why it is missing;
  - its Source and grade chips, with an older input marked;
  - Ask to correct on each (SC9, §2);
  - an input with an open correction says "Correction asked · waiting for an Admin", because one may be open per input.
- **What investors see:** the Worker's own `investor_preview`, before and after an NDA.
  - Before an NDA: the band and confidence word only, with Risk read as a risk word.
  - After: values and inputs, with personal and team-only inputs "Counted, not shown".
- **Sharing:** the investors' switch, and the venture's own advisors (§5).
  - Each change opens a dialog that says who gains or loses access. Nothing is saved until it is confirmed.
- **Recompute:** re-scores (`POST /recompute`) and says how many scopes changed, or that nothing moved.
- **States:** a failed read is Unreadable with Try again, never an empty list. A refusal shows the Worker's sentence.
- **An Admin** opening the page reads the staff view: the scopes with Add review (SC10, §3), and no sharing or preview. The Worker logs the read.

### 2. Ask to correct (SC9)

A dialog names the input with its value, source and grade, and takes "What is wrong" in 10 to 2000 characters, the Worker's own rule.
- **What it sends:** `{ scope, input_key, message }` and nothing else. An Admin answers it; nothing changes until they do.
- **Not offered:** attaching a file, because the Worker's route takes none. The dialog says so, and says that adding the file to the record the input reads counts on the next score.

### 3. Add review (SC10)

The staff view's dialog offers "Note only" or "Adjust".
- **Adjust:** −15 to +15, other than 0, with a reason of at least 10 characters (the Worker's `REASON_MIN`; the canvas drew 20).
  - "Shown as: computed 51 · after review 57" previews the Worker's own clamp to 0–100.
- **An unscored scope** takes a note only.
- **What it sends:** `{ adjustment, reason, note }`, never a value. The Worker refuses over the cap and never clamps.

### 4. The trajectory (SC12) at `/raise/scorecard/trajectory`

**New Worker read:** `GET /api/venture-scores/:projectId/trajectory`.
- **Who:** the venture's founders, and studio staff (whose read is logged). An advisor, an investor and an Explorer are refused.
- **What it returns:** every stored row of each scope, oldest first, with its model version, state, value, band and confidence word. It never returns inputs or a signature.
- **The models:** each model version is listed with the first time it was used.

**The page:**
- **The chart:** a line per scored scope, in scope order, on 0–100. Rows are written only on a change (D1255), so a point is a change.
- **A gap:** a stretch with no value is a gap, never a line at 0.
- **A model change:** a dotted marker at the first row computed under each later version.
- **View as table:** lists every point, with a gap said in words.
- **An empty trajectory** says the line starts with the first score.

### 5. Sharing with advisors, and the advisor's view (SC21)

- **Whom to share with:** the founder chooses from the venture's own advisors.
  - These are accepted roster members with the advisor role and an active advisor account.
  - They come from the new `candidates` in the sharing read, shown to the founders only.
  - A failed read says so; it never reads as none.
- **The notice:** an advisor newly shared with is told in their inbox, with a link to `/practice/scorecards/:projectId`.
  - Sharing again, stopping, or the investors' switch notifies no one.
  - A notice that fails never undoes the share.
- **`/practice/scorecards/:projectId`:** the advisor's read-only view.
  - It shows bands, values, confidence and any review in force. Opening a scope says inputs stay hidden.
  - There is no Ask to correct and no Add review.
  - An advisor not shared with gets the Worker's 403, and the page draws nothing of the scorecard.

### 6. Your weighting (SC7)

Deal flow's toolbar shows "Your weighting: Equal" or "Your own", with Edit weighting.
- **The dialog:** a slider of 0–5 per scope, Reset to equal, and Save.
- **What it saves:** all eleven weights, or `null` when every weight is equal. All at 0 is refused.
- **Roles:** a role the Worker gives no weighting (403) is not offered the control.
- **It never touches a score.** It changes the caller's sort only.

### 7. Not built, and why

- **The sort by weighting and the canvas's live preview of the order.** The deal list's strip is fed by part 4 (#3280, D1257) from `POST /batch`, which returns bands and confidence words, never a number. The order waits for that list. The dialog saves the weighting the sort will use.

Listing it needs an overview card and a records adapter there, and every test that pins seven Raise modules updated. The page is reached at its address and from part 3's review notices.
- **Add review on the deal page's Scorecard tab.** That panel is part 4's (`Scorecard.jsx`). `ScopeList` and `AddReview` are exported for it.
- **What would raise confidence** (Eadwyn's proposals, drawn on SC8): no read proposes them yet.

### 8. Rules kept

- Scores are derived, never typed: no dialog sends a value, band, confidence or grade.
- Every new statement is bound. Neither reads a never-input or a signature (the Worker test scans them).
- Every change is an in-page dialog that says what it changes; never a browser pop-up.
- The pages import the Lightbox from its own file, not the `ui` index, so none reads as drawing the rail (`rail_pages_d768`).
