## D5036 — Licence fees: the run-rate is labelled as one, and the invoice ledger answers invoiced, collected and outstanding (#5036)

#5036 (slot S17). `GET /api/admin/revenue/summary` showed a quarter of each active licence's `annual_fee_cents` in a column headed "This quarter". That is what the licences are contracted to bring in at today's terms, not invoices issued, not cash collected, not prorated for a mid-quarter start, while `licence_fee_invoices` (migration 513, D802) already recorded what HQ invoiced and what it recorded receiving.

### Decision

1. **Four concepts, kept apart.** The summary's `licence_fees` keeps its figure and gains `measure: 'contracted_run_rate'` and `label: 'Contracted quarterly run-rate'`; its basis says it is not what was invoiced or collected. The page draws that label beside every licence-fee row, and the column is no longer "This quarter".
2. **`services/licenceFeeReceivables.ts`** reads the invoice ledger and returns `licence_invoices` on the summary, its own failure domain (an unreadable ledger is `available: false`, drawn Unreadable, and leaves the run-rate standing):
   - per currency, for the invoices **issued in the period**: invoices, invoiced, collected (HQ-entered `paid_cents`, D.8, attributed to the invoice's issue period, because the ledger records no payment dates of its own), outstanding, overdue (count and amount), and void (count and amount);
   - the **receivable as at today**, per currency, across every open invoice whatever period issued it, with the overdue part; and the overdue invoices listed one by one, oldest due first, up to 50 (the totals count all);
   - **expected, not invoiced**: each billable licence (active or suspended, D802's `BILLABLE_STATUSES`) whose current licence year has started with no invoice for it, with the fee it would carry, and the years whose only invoice is void (D802 refuses a second one); and the billable licences that cannot be invoiced at all (no fee, no start date), each with why.
3. **Void counts nowhere.** A void invoice is in no invoiced, collected, outstanding, overdue or receivable figure; it is counted apart.
4. **No cross-currency total.** Every figure is integer minor units of the invoice's own currency in one bucket per currency. No FX policy exists, so nothing is added across buckets.
5. **A period is selectable.** `?period=YYYY-Qn` sets the summary's quarter (every zone that reads one), and the page offers the current quarter and the seven before it. A malformed period, or one that has not begun, is refused with 400: a future quarter would be a page of zeros.

### Not changed, on purpose

- `quarter_cents` keeps its name and value: it is the run-rate, now labelled. A prorated run-rate is not computed.
- Collected is not split by payment date: `paid_cents` is a running total, and inventing a date for it would be a figure the platform does not record. The canonical financial-event ledger (#5038) is where payment events get their own dates.

### Verified

`licence_fee_receivables_5036` (8, on the migrations' own DDL): run-rate labelled; invoiced, collected, outstanding per currency with no top-level total; overdue listed and in the receivable of any period; void in no figure; the gap list with not-invoiced, void-only, no-fee and no-start-date licences; an unreadable ledger leaves the run-rate; the period parameter and its refusals. `revenue_licence_invoices_5036` (7) draws every state. Mutation checked: collected drawn in the outstanding column; the overdue list dropped; an unreadable ledger drawn as empty; a future quarter offered; a void invoice counted as invoiced; a void-only year treated as invoiced; a paid invoice called overdue.
