### 1. Businesses (KYC6, KYC17a)

Businesses reads part 3's queue (`GET /api/kyc/businesses`), oldest first, 25 to a page.

- **The tiles** come from the read's counts by status:
  - **Waiting** is In review plus Not started, with the oldest of them ("Oldest: 11 days").
  - **Needs documents**, **Approved** and **Rejected**, with the expired count under Rejected.
  - A read without counts draws no tile figure. It never draws a zero.
- **The chips** are the Worker's own filters: kind (Companies, Investing entities, Partner firms, Imported, and Licensed studios for the Super Admin alone, as the Worker returns them to no one else) and status. Search sends `q`, which the Worker matches against the legal name or the full registration number.
- **A row** draws the legal name, entity type and masked registration number; kind and jurisdiction, with a high-risk country said; the status; the wait while the case waits; owners (Disclosed, Not disclosed, or No account yet for an unlinked import); and when it was last reviewed.
- **A failed read** is Unreadable with Try again. It is never an empty table and never zero tiles. An empty read says nothing is waiting, or that nothing matches the filters.
- Each legal name opens `/admin/kyc/businesses/:uid`. The queue decides nothing.

### 2. The case (KYC7, KYC7b)

The case reads `GET /api/kyc/businesses/:id`. The Worker logs the read (`kyb_record_access`). A 404 says no case has that id in this studio; any other failure is Unreadable with Try again.

- **The header:** legal name, kind, entity type, jurisdiction, status, the wait, a high-risk country, and the account it belongs to (a link to that person's case), or that an import is not linked yet.
- **Every block is one of three things:** read; Not recorded, with the Worker's reason written out; or could not be read. A block that could not be read never says "nothing on file".
  - **The entity:** the registration number and the registered address masked, each with Show through `GET /:id/sensitive/:field`, which logs `kyc_field_shown` before it answers; the tax ID's last four; the signing authority; submitted and expiry dates.
  - **Owners:** share, nationality, date of birth (masked, with Show by index), the identity check (a link to the person's case when an account is linked), and sanctions as the Worker sends them. Under the table: how many owners of 25% or more have no approved identity check, and what the recorded shares add up to. A share that is not recorded counts as 25% or more, as the Worker counts it.
  - **Directors**, **Documents** (each opening in `KycViewer`, inside the page, watermarked, through `kybApi.documentBlob`; the documents already asked for are listed under it), **History** (newest first).
  - **Registry:** whether the registry record matched and the good standing recorded at approval.
  - **Due diligence:** read only, each case linking to `/admin/due-diligence/:uid`. It is decided there, not here.
  - **Sanctions:** when the business was last checked, or Not recorded.

### 3. The decisions (KYC8 A, B, C, E; Link from KYC7b)

`businessModel.actionsOf` gives what a case offers, and the Worker refuses anything else:

- an unlinked import offers Link to an account and Dismiss;
- a case not yet approved offers Ask for documents, Reject and Approve;
- an approved case offers Reject;
- a dismissed case, or a case the reader cannot review (a licensed studio for anyone but the Super Admin), offers nothing.

Each decision is a dialog in the page, never a browser pop-up, and says what it changes.

- **Every write sends the `review_token` of the case read** as `If-Match`. A case that changed while it was open is refused with 409 `kyb_changed`. The dialog then says so and offers Read it again. It never retries on its own.
- **Approve** needs the three checks the Worker needs (registry matches, owners of 25% or more identified, every owner's identity check approved or explained). Good standing is optional. When an owner of 25% or more has no approved identity check, the dialog names them and asks for a reason; `unchecked_owner_reason` is sent only then. Approve is disabled, with the reason said in the bar, while the owners are not disclosed, not recorded or could not be read.
- **Reject** and **Dismiss** need a reason of at least 5 characters, as the Worker does.
- **Ask for documents** sends the Worker's kinds and the request. The request is written from the kinds until it is edited.
- **Link to an account** sends an account number. The Worker checks that the account is in this studio and is not the reader's own.
- Step-up stays on the Worker (`writeUser` calls `requireStepUp`). `request()` asks for the authenticator code and sends the decision again once.

### 4. Not built here, and why

- **Send to HQ (KYC8 D):** no Worker route sends a business to HQ. It is not drawn.
- **Assignment:** part 3 records no assignee for a business. The queue says so; no figure is drawn.
- **Recording owners and directors** (`PUT /:id/owners`), **adding a document** (`POST /:id/documents`) and **adding a licensed studio** (`POST /licensed-studios`): the client methods exist, but no dialog is drawn yet.
