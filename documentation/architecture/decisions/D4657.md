## D4657 — Spin-Out Lab: admission follows the recorded enrollment, progress follows the saved work (#4657)

- **Status:** built by S15 on `agent/claude-s15/4657-lab-admission`. Not merged.
- **Audit:** Spin-Out Lab audit 1 of 5 (findings 1–2), revision `d85951c`. #4658 and #4659 build on this.
- **Migration:** `202611071700_spinout_lab_enrollments.sql` (additive: one table, five nullable columns on `spinout_lab_milestones`, a one-row cursor table, and the backfill of today's founders).

### Decision

1. **An enrollment is a recorded fact.** `spinout_lab_enrollments` has one row per founder per cycle:
   - **`cohort`.** Written by the cycle activation when it admits an approved applicant. It carries the assigned cycle, the applicant, the application and the cycle's start instant.
   - **`legacy`.** Every founder in, or once in, the Lab before this change who has no activated application. Their cycle is the one their start fell in (`cycle_source = 'inferred_start'`), so their calendar is unchanged.

   The calendar gate and a cycle's participants read the enrollment's cycle, never the start instant's month. An activated applicant whose row is missing (written by an older Worker) is healed from the applicant on read.
2. **One eligibility policy** (`services/labEnrollment.ts`). `/start`, every milestone write, the reconciler and the activation all ask it, and it answers one of five states:
   - **eligible:** an admitted member whose cycle has started;
   - **legacy:** an explicit legacy enrollment;
   - **not_started:** the cycle opens later; the answer gives `opens_at`;
   - **ineligible:** `not_admitted`, `restricted` (a moderation sanction in force, D448's predicate) or `graduated`;
   - **unavailable:** a read failed; the caller answers a retryable 503.

   An unreadable state never permits anything. `/start` no longer switches the Lab on for an account that was never admitted, or for one an admin suspended: before, it checked only "already graduated". A cohort member starts on the cycle's own instant. Onboarding no longer calls `/start`; choosing the Lab there means applying.
3. **A milestone is proved by saved work** (`services/labEvidence.ts`). Each catalog key names the row that proves it, and the tests pin the catalog and the rules to the same keys.
   - **Ownership.** Project work counts only from a project the founder owns; once #4659 names the enrollment's project, only that one. Person-level steps (bookings, profiling, co-founder requests, the paid order) are the user's own.
   - **Steps that save nothing.** The two clipboard-only steps (`market_research_shared`, `revenue_summary_generated`) are recorded as `attested`, and only once the work they summarise is saved.
   - **What is stored.** Each milestone stores its `evidence_kind`, its `evidence_ref` (the row or count), `evidence_project_id`, the enrollment and `recorded_by`. Rows from before the migration are `legacy`.
4. **One recorder** (`services/labProgress.ts`). The Lab page, Eadwyn's write router, the paid incorporation order and the saved EIN all record through `recordLabMilestone`, in this order:
   - policy;
   - Lab on;
   - calendar gate (a gate it cannot read is 503, no longer "legacy, carry on");
   - evidence;
   - an insert-once of the evidence row;
   - the existing pure week advance;
   - the D541 credit.

   The credit is paid only when that call's insert recorded the row. This replaces the route's read-before-insert, and the advisor router's own insert, advance and credit. The graduation certificate and bonus are unchanged, issued on the insert of `incorporation_completed`.
5. **Recovery without the browser.** `reconcileLabProgress` does two things for one founder:
   - records every step whose saved work exists but was never recorded;
   - pays every evidence-backed milestone with no grant, through the same idempotent `lab:<user>:<key>` source ref.

   The cron walks the enrolled founders three per tick, behind a stored cursor. `/state` schedules the caller's own pass after the response, at most every 30 minutes. Legacy rows are left to D541's backfill. There is still one ledger (`perk_credit_ledger`), and a revoked grant stays revoked.

### Why

The audit found two things:
- activation followed a button, not the recorded admission;
- progress and rewards followed what the browser reported, not what the participant had saved.

Each repair is the smallest one that gives every reader the same answer:
- the enrollment row replaces three loose columns and a date guess;
- the evidence rule replaces the client's word;
- the one recorder replaces four copies of the gate, insert and credit, each with its own way of failing open.

### Not done here

- **The enrollment's project** is left NULL: #4659 owns venture identity.
- **Atomic multi-table activation, monotonic week updates and the approval/withdrawal winner** are #4658's.
- **Graduation's shared rule** is #4659's.
- **Existing milestones and grants** are not re-checked: rows from before this change keep `evidence_kind = 'legacy'`, and no grant is revoked.
