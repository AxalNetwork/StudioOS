### 1. The proposal run

`POST /api/cycles/raise/narrative/positioning/draft?project=&version=` (`routes/cycle_raise.ts`):

- **Rail:** page "Founder · Raise · Investor narrative", option `draft:narrative/positioning`, record `project:<id>`.
  - It is one `rail_write` run through the router. The router holds the Manual switch, guards the output, meters and charges the pressing member (D778).
  - The surface is the new `AI_SURFACE.raiseNarrative`, `/raise/investor-narrative` (D552's table).
- **No figure enters** (`services/narrative/positioningDraft.ts`, `positioningMaterial`). The run reads qualitative records only:
  - pain themes, each with its reach in words ("raised in most interviews"), never the count;
  - segments and tracked competitors, by name;
  - the venture's current Verdict decision word, never its reasoning;
  - the solution as written;
  - the team's roles, never a name.

  Any item that holds a digit is held back, so a figure typed into a segment's name or the solution cannot reach the model. The answer counts them (`withheld`) and never lists them.

  This closes bug 6's remainder. `POST /api/decks/positioning` sends the financial model's runway, burn and LTV:CAC, and the team's names.
- **No figure leaves** (`screenAnswer`). A field of the answer that holds a digit is dropped before anything is stored or shown, as `unfoundFigures` does with an empty set. The answer says how many were dropped (`removed`).
- **What is stored.** Each surviving field is one `ai_rail_proposals` row (kind `proposal`, payload `{ kind, position, text, version }`). A new run withdraws what the last one left open. A run writes no positioning and no narrative log row.
- **Refusals:**
  - nothing to read but the team's roles: 422 `nothing_to_read`;
  - version 0, the live deck: 409;
  - a failed model: 503, with nothing stored;
  - another venture, a partner or an investor: 403, through `scope()`.

### 2. The reads and decisions

- `GET /narrative/positioning/draft` returns:
  - `read_from`;
  - `segments` and `competitors`, each `{ state: 'ok', items }` or `{ state: 'unreadable' }`;
  - `withheld`;
  - the caller's open `proposals`.
- `POST /narrative/positioning/proposals/:uid` takes `{ decision: 'accept' | 'discard' }`.
  - **One at a time:** an already-decided proposal answers 409. Another member's, another venture's or another option's answers 404.
  - **Accept** writes the field through `savePositioning`, with source `{ kind: 'eadwyn', ref: 'ai_rail_proposals:<uid>' }`. So the store's own refusal of a digit in Eadwyn's wording holds again, and its `narrative_positioning_saved` row is the one log row.
  - **Discard** keeps nothing and logs one `narrative_positioning_discarded` row.
  - No row holds the words, a figure or an investor's name.
- **No Accept all.** NAR5 and NAR19d draw "Accept all" and "Discard all". The one-at-a-time rule (`routes/ai_rail_proposals.ts`, D777 part 3) forbids a bulk decision. They are not drawn, and the question is put to S1 on #2695.

The client is `narrativeApi` in `frontend/src/lib/api/narrative.js`, one alphabetical line in `api.js` (D527). It also carries D908's `positioning` and `savePositioning`, which had no client.
