### What the canvas decides

- Import runs in nine steps: source, snapshot, classify, map, validate, review, plan, publish, result. A source record is not a person (NSI-00).
- Sources and runs are listed with their state (Airtable, Folk, NFX partial, uploaded export), including a superseded run whose mapping read a blank as No (NSI-01). A source that is refused, needs sign-in, fails to fetch or paginates incompletely keeps its mapping and is never worked around (NSI-02).
- A snapshot records URL, retrieved time, id, hash, row count, fields, row key and mapping version; a page title that disagrees with the rows read is shown, and a sample is labelled as a sample (NSI-03).
- Rows are classified person, organization, mixed (split only when the cell names both) or unknown; a firm-only row gives an organization with "No people recorded" (NSI-04). Mapping keeps missing, unrecognized and invalid apart (NSI-05), and validation has an error summary (NSI-06).
- Identity review shows evidence on both sides with one reason, covering namesakes, firm vs fund vehicle, a shared identifier, a member-owned conflict, a stale second affiliation and an organization row carrying a person's URL. Hidden records give no hint (NSI-07).
- The NFX coverage ledger counts discovered, fetched, partial, index only, pending and blocked, and is labelled Partial, not a full-library import; one investor on four lists is deduplicated by profile id (NSI-08). Person → affiliation → firm → Research › Funds as fund manager; a fund vehicle is never offered without evidence naming it (NSI-09).
- The publish plan reconciles counts by entity and disposition and says what becomes discoverable and what stays restricted or private, with a stale preview (NSI-10). Partial failure resumes, the receipt shows row outcomes and a replay is a no-op (NSI-11). A row removed upstream goes Scope → Review → Result; stale, suppressed and withdrawn are three states and none deletes (NSI-12).
