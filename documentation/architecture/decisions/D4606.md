## D4606 — Accepting an introduction debits the bucket it chose, atomically, and says why it failed (#4606)

- **Status:** Built for #4606, 2026-11-07. It covers the Worker (`services/introductions.ts`, `routes/introductions.ts`) and two pages.
- **Builds on:**
  - D755 (packs retired; allowance, referral and purchased buckets stay);
  - the `intro_credit_ledger` and `intro_propositions` tables.
- **No migration.** The ledger stays the single source of every balance.

### What was decided

1. **The debit guards its own bucket.**
   - `POST /propositions/:uid/accept` chose a bucket from a read, then guarded the debit with the *total* balance. Two requests for different propositions could both spend the last allowance credit while purchased credits remained: the allowance bucket went to -1 and the shown balance read above what could be spent.
   - The debit now requires the chosen bucket's own sum to be positive (the allowance bucket within the month it was chosen for) and, for the allowance, that the database month still is that month.
2. **A lost debit is retried safely.** `acceptIntroProposition` re-reads the proposition and the credit state and picks again, up to three attempts. The proposition flips to accepted in the same batch, and only when its own spend row exists.
3. **Repeats cannot debit twice.** A spend row per proposition (`intro:<uid>`) already exists in the ledger, and the debit refuses a second one.
4. **Failures say which.**

| Case | Status | `error` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| no credit in any bucket | 402 | `no_credits` |
| proposition already accepted, declined or expired | 409 | `already_responded` (with its status) |
| the debit lost every retry; nothing was spent | 409 | `credit_conflict` (`retryable`) |
| not the caller's proposition | 404 | `not_found` |

   Both pages show a conflict as "Your credits changed at the same moment. Nothing was spent. Try again."
5. **The displayed balance is what can be spent.** The balance sums each bucket clamped at zero, and the debit only takes from a bucket above zero, so the two now agree.
6. **Month rollover.** A spend carries the month it happened in, so last month's allowance and its spends still net to what they did. A request that straddles midnight on the 1st finds its bucket's month changed, loses the debit, and retries in the new month.
