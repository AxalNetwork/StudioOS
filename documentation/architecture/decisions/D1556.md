## D1556 — Retrieval of consented interview quotes for a deck

- **Status:** built by S24 from S1's specification on #3803 (deck builder v2, Phase C, C8; plan on #3716).
- **Owner decisions:**
  - **H = A** (2026-11-01): quotes come from consented sessions only, attributed by role and never by name. A session without recorded consent is excluded, not just hidden.
  - **L = A** (2026-11-01): Cloudflare spend is approved item by item, with its monthly cost stated first. The `axal-deck-quotes` index is one such item, so it is not created or bound until the owner approves the estimate below.
- **Builds on:**
  - D657's interview sessions and transcript;
  - D685 §5.1.4's consent to quote (`quote_consent`);
  - D873's rule that only the interviewee's own lines are theirs;
  - D760's model catalogue and the AI router's module tasks.
- **Feeds:** B5 (#3781), which owns the manual pick and migration 732, and the one-click run's Quote area (dk-auto §3).
- **Migration:** none.

### The question

A deck's Quote area needs a customer's own words. The one-click run must never invent one, must never use one without consent, and must never take one from another venture. It needs ranked candidates it can bind, each with where it came from, or a clear "none".

### What was decided

**What is quotable** (`services/decks/quoteRetrieval.ts`, one WHERE clause that indexing and searching share). A transcript line qualifies only when all of these hold:
1. **Recorded consent:** its session has `consent_status = 'given'` with a `consent_at`.
2. **Consent to quote:** its interview has `quote_consent = 1` ("Agreed · deck-eligible").
3. **The final transcript:** it is `source = 'final'`, not the live draft.
4. **The interviewee's line:** the speaker is the interviewee's name or "Interviewee". "Me" is the founder and "Speaker ?" is nobody known, so neither is ever attributed to the customer.
5. **At least 20 characters.**
6. **This venture's line.**

**The index.** `axal-deck-quotes` is a second Vectorize index: bge-m3, 1024 dimensions, cosine. It holds one vector per quotable line, `quote:<segment id>`, with `project_id` and `segment_id` in its metadata.
- `indexProjectQuotes` first deletes the vector of every line of the venture that is no longer quotable: a withdrawn consent, a renamed speaker, a declined session. It then embeds and upserts the quotable lines, at most 500.
- An unconsented line is never embedded.

**A search** (`retrieveQuotes`):
1. Embed the question.
2. Ask the index for the 50 nearest lines, filtered to the venture.
3. **Re-check every hit in D1** against the same clause. A vector left from before a consent was withdrawn, or another venture's vector from an index that ignored its filter, is dropped.
4. Rank the survivors with `bge-reranker-base` and return the best, at most 10.

**Each quote** carries:
- its text, clipped to 300 characters;
- `attribution`: the interviewee's role, or "Interviewee" when none was recorded, with `role_recorded`;
- its provenance: segment id, session uid, interview id, the day it was held, its start and end in the recording, and `consent: 'given'`.

The name, the company and the speaker label never leave the service.

**Nothing invented.** Every outcome without quotes is a `{ available: false, reason }` block:

| Situation | Reason |
|---|---|
| No index bound | "Quote retrieval is not switched on yet." |
| No consented match | "No interview quote with recorded consent matches." |
| A model failure | "Your interview quotes could not be searched right now." |

The run then cuts the area or leaves it out. It never writes a quote.

**Models.** The embedding is `@cf/baai/bge-m3` and the rerank `@cf/baai/bge-reranker-base`, both catalogue ids (D760). Both run through the AI router's `module_embed` and `module_rerank` tasks as the member's own run, logged at cost on `/raise/pitch`. There is no second model list.

**The routes** (`routes/deck_quotes.ts`, mounted at `/api/decks` before `decks`):

| Method, path | Gate | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/decks/quotes/search` `{ project_id, query, limit? }` | `projectOwned` | `{ project_id, quotes }`, where `quotes` is a block |
| `POST /api/decks/quotes/index` `{ project_id }` | `projectOwned`, `write: true` | `{ project_id, index: { indexed, removed, skipped } }`, as a block |

- Both are POSTs because both run paid models, so neither runs from a page load.
- No client method is added (S1's revision): B5 binds the builder's `ventureElements` and calls this when it exposes retrieval.

**Not switched on until the owner approves.**
- `Env.DECK_QUOTES` is optional and is not in `wrangler.toml`.
- A binding to an index that does not exist would fail the deploy, and creating the index is the spend L asks to approve first.
- Until both happen, every read answers "Quote retrieval is not switched on yet."

### Estimated monthly cost (for the owner, decision L)

These figures come from Cloudflare's published Vectorize pricing (2026-10-30). The formula is `((queried vectors + stored vectors) × dimensions × $0.01 / 1M) + (stored vectors × dimensions × $0.05 / 100M)`. The Workers Paid plan includes 50M queried and 10M stored dimensions a month, shared with `axal-search`, and 50,000 indexes, so the index itself costs nothing.

| Assumption | Stored vectors | Searches a month | Vectorize, before the included allowance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Now: 50 ventures × 500 lines | 25,000 | 5,000 | about **$0.32** |
| Growth: 500 ventures × 500 lines | 250,000 | 50,000 | about **$3.20** |

**Workers AI, at the catalogue's prices:**
- **Embedding** (bge-m3, $0.012 per M tokens): about $0.02 to index 25,000 lines, and about $0.18 for 250,000.
- **Rerank** (bge-reranker-base, $0.003 per M tokens): about $0.06 for 5,000 searches, and about $0.60 for 50,000.

**Total:** under **$1 a month** at the first assumption, and about **$4 a month** at the second.

### Owner steps, after approval

1. `npx wrangler vectorize create axal-deck-quotes --dimensions=1024 --metric=cosine`
2. `npx wrangler vectorize create-metadata-index axal-deck-quotes --property-name=project_id --type=number`. The search filters on it, and D1 re-checks every hit either way.
3. Add the `DECK_QUOTES` → `axal-deck-quotes` binding under both `[[vectorize]]` and `[[env.production.vectorize]]` in `wrangler.toml`. `check-wrangler-binding-parity` requires both. Then deploy as usual.

### Verified

- `cloudflare-worker/test/deck_quotes_d1556.test.ts`: 6 tests on SQLite (the baseline and every migration), with in-memory stand-ins for the index, the embedder and the reranker.
- 15 mutations, each turned a test red:
  - recorded consent not required;
  - `consent_at` not required;
  - consent to quote not required;
  - live drafts quotable;
  - any speaker quotable;
  - withdrawn vectors not deleted;
  - hits not re-checked in D1;
  - no venture filter on the query;
  - the index's order instead of the reranker's;
  - a name as the attribution;
  - the limit ignored;
  - a missing index answering `[]`;
  - a rerank failure answering `[]`;
  - search without the gate;
  - indexing without the write gate.
