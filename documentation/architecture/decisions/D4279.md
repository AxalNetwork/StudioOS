## D4279 — Licence account disclosure tests ignore unrelated dates (#4279)

The two refused-payment checks in `licence_account_d798.test.ts` inspect
account fields, masked `ending NNNN` text and the full synthetic account
number across the reply. They no longer treat a bare last-four substring in
a statement date as evidence of an account leak.

The fixture keeps a runtime-generated six-digit prefix and deliberately ends
in `2026`, the year of its statements. Both refusal cases therefore exercise
the reported collision on every run. Account numbers remain synthetic and
are never recorded in source. The successful payment read still checks the
expected masked last four; both refusal cases still reject account data.

This changes tests only. No production behavior, permissions, payment
instructions or schema changes.
