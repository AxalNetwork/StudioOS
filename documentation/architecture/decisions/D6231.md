## D6231 — Fund Accounting reads one fund, with one view row and copy that names its one write (#6231)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-14 by S10 for #6231, under #5994. Frontend only.
- **Number:** D6231 is this issue's number (D4106).
- **Code:** `frontend/src/pages/investor/InvestorFundAccounting.jsx`, `FundLedgerPanel.jsx`
  (scoped mode), `investorFundAccounting.css`, `frontend/src/workspaces/investorZoneFilters.js`
  (`funds/ledger`), `frontend/src/workspaces/zoneFilterBuilder.js` (`locked`).
- **Tests:** `frontend/test/fund_accounting_scope_6231.test.mjs`.

### 1. One fund, chosen like the other Fund zones

The page uses D371's `useManagedFund` and `FundPicker`, so `?fund=` chooses and an LP-only fund is
never picked. Every block follows that one fund:

- the capital lines and per-LP capital (the analytics row with the fund's id, never `items[0]`);
- the operational ledger panel;
- Mercury's ledger proposals.

The fund list failing ("Unreadable … not the same as having none", with Try again) and operating no
fund are two different sentences.

### 2. One view row

The header row is the panel's only view control: Summary · Journal · Fees, and Audit trail (no read
exists; it says so). The panel's own fund and view chips are not drawn on this page (`scoped`), and a
chosen fund with no ledger this reader may read says so in one sentence, never showing another fund.
Fund Administration's Books tab keeps the panel's unscoped behaviour unchanged.

Journal and Fees are live for the fund's assigned administrator or an admin (the ledger row's
`can_manage`). For anyone else they are drawn disabled, with the reason as their title, and the
reason is said once under the row. The new `locked` option of `makeZoneFilters` draws a live view as
disabled and never selectable; no existing caller passes it.

### 3. Copy

The page writes one thing: accepting a Mercury proposal posts that entry to the fund's operational
ledger (`FromMercury section="ledger"` sends the period and accounts on Accept). The boundary card,
footer and rail say that; nothing on the page claims "no write" or "read-only" any more.

### 4. Refresh and the tiles

A refresh control re-reads the fund list, the analytics and the ledger, and remounts the Mercury part.
The three tiles draw in three columns (`.if3-stats--three`), and in one column on a phone.
