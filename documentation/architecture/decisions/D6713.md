## D6713 — Source-backed obligation packs and registration seeds (#6713)

The formation catalogue is the production pack reader for founder compliance
and fund-entity calendars. `formationPacks.ts` reads a case's pinned version;
legacy calendar doors use the published current version. Legacy exported
constants and the old pure US schedule helper remain compatibility/parity
fixtures. They no longer select production pack content.

The existing `phase` CHECK permits only before_filing, filing and after_filing.
Preserve it and add nullable `obligation_phase` for post_registration/ongoing,
plus source, checked day, due-rule, event-kind and recurrence fields. Published
catalogue rows remain immutable: these columns do not authorize edits or
publication of an existing version. No applied migration is edited or CHECK
rebuilt. `pack_item_key` is additive on formation_steps and compliance_events.
Company-level compliance_events already exists in the shipped schema.

A v2 transition to registered appends its pack statements to the same guarded
D1 batch, after company-link statements. Each seed also requires the unique
transition token in the winning event, so a losing concurrent transition cannot
replace stage evidence or dates after the winner commits. Project cases get todo formation_steps;
source-backed dated items also get company-level compliance_events. Those
events carry company_id and a null project_id: project reminder coverage comes
from formation_steps, and distinct same-day pack items must not collide with
the legacy UNIQUE(project_id,event_type,due_date) calendar constraint. Keys are
stable per case/form/item and unique indexes make replay idempotent. The registered row records the pack. When activation/handoff are actually
entered, their rows inherit the item list and named handoff recipient; no future
workflow row is pre-opened. Technical owners follow D6686 part 2: HQ for
activation and the founder for handoff. The canvas calls the final business
phases Operational activation (10) and Ongoing ownership (11); these are not
eleven new workflow enum values. The owner confirmed eight recorded technical
stages in D6686 part 2, which this change preserves. Legacy
transitions retain their existing behavior. A form with no pack returns [].

No due date is produced without a catalogue source and checked day. Relative
rules also need the registry date, never the packet date or a guessed formation
date. Unknown kinds map to other while retaining their label; a non-US legacy
franchise-tax kind also maps to other. Company-only items return reminders:false
until #6039 reads compliance_events; no second reminder job is introduced and
cycle_build.ts is unchanged.

### Content readiness limitation

The published v1 catalogue stores the copied legacy compliance rules as
provisional facts without source IDs. Those labels can be read but their due
dates remain null. Project steps can remain undated; compliance_events requires
a due date and receives no undated placeholder. The shipped v1 catalogue does
not yet provide the complete post-registration EIN/83(b)/Mercury schedule asked
for by the issue. No legal deadline, pack content, source approval or publication
is invented here. Completing that production content requires a reviewed new
catalogue version with actual source-backed rules. This implementation remains
a draft until that compatibility/content gap is resolved with the catalogue
owner, as raised on #6713. It does not claim the issue fully complete.

Tests use the migrated schema and synthetic, explicitly sourced rule fixtures
to exercise real registration batches, replay, US schedule parity, company-only
coverage and unknown/UK kinds. Existing entity-calendar route tests use the
same catalogue boundary. Local validation is recorded in the PR.
