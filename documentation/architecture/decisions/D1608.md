### 1. The sync (`services/posthog/grow.ts`)

`POST /api/cycles/grow/posthog/sync` is pressed by the member whose PostHog serves the venture.

| What it reads | Lands as |
|---|---|
| Every metric tracked in Revenue and traction or Product improvement | a `metric` fact. Each is also a KPI row `posthog-<uid>` (D1604). |
| A tracked metric whose role is retention | its cohorts by first-seen week (`retention:<uid>`): each cohort's size and the share seen again each week after. A week with no count is null, never 0. A cohort of no one is dropped. |
| Each survey's stats (at most 5) | a `survey_stats` fact for the month: responses, shown, and the NPS or average when PostHog states one. Otherwise there is no score. |
| Answers to a question marked "feedback" (D1605's marks) | one `survey_feedback` proposal per answer. A "would pay" or "need to have" question is never feedback. |

Product retention is never written to `monthly_churn_pct`, never merged with revenue cohort retention, and no row lands in `project_metrics` or `custom_kpi_values`.

### 2. Accept: "Add as customer feedback"

- **The writer.** Accept writes `product_feedback` through Grow's own `addFeedback`, with `source 'customer'` and the answer as its body.
- **No identity.** No name, email or company is sent, even if the request carries one.
