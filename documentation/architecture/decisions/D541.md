## D541 — Spin-Out Lab milestones pay credits to accepted members

**An accepted cohort member is paid each milestone's credits once, when the
milestone is first recorded, and the graduation bonus once, when their
certificate is issued; the Lab checklist shows what each row pays and what
has been earned.** Issue #1104, slot S08, part of #1101. No migration: the
amounts are D540's `lab:*` rows (migration 381), paid through D540's
`grantCredits`.

**What was true on main.**
- `POST /spinout-lab/milestone` recorded a milestone with `INSERT OR IGNORE`
  and said nothing about whether this call was the one that recorded it.
- Nothing paid for a milestone.
- `POST /spinout-lab/start` does not check admission, so being in the Lab is
  not the same as being accepted.

**What changed.**
- **`services/labMilestoneCredits.ts`.**
  - **Who earns.** "Accepted" is `user_spinout_flags.spinout_lab_admitted =
    1`, which both acceptance paths set: the admin decision in
    `routes/admin.ts` and the cycle activation in
    `services/cohortApplications.ts`. A flag that cannot be read pays nothing.
    Staff are refused by `grantCredits`.
  - **`creditLabMilestone`** pays `lab:<key>` with source
    `lab:<user_id>:<key>`.
    - A milestone with no rule (week 1's required four) is skipped.
    - Of `advisor_meeting_booked` and `cofounder_request_sent`, only the first
      paid is paid (#1102 question 3: "the first of"). Each half passes the
      other's source ref to `grantCredits` as `unlessPaid`, which the ledger
      insert checks in the same statement, so two halves recorded at once
      land one grant. (A read-before-write check shipped first and let both
      through; the follow-up below replaced it.)
  - **`creditLabGraduation`** pays `lab:graduation`, source
    `lab:<user_id>:graduation`, only when that user's
    `incorporation_completed` was itself paid.
    - That carries #1102 question 4's pick A (only milestones completed from
      launch day earn) to the bonus without a launch date in the code.
    - A pre-launch graduate's certificate, issued later, pays nothing at the
      time. The cohort backfill below pays it, once the owner answered Q4
      with A and B.
  - **`milestoneCreditsFor`** gives each `lab:*` rule's amount and whether it
    was earned. A revoked grant is not earned. The unpaid half of the pair is
    `superseded`. It also says whether the user can earn, and if not, why. It
    throws on a failed read.
  - **None of these throws.** A grant never fails a milestone or a
    certificate (D111).
- **`routes/spinout_lab.ts`.**
  - **`POST /milestone`** reads whether the milestone existed before
    recording it (a failed read counts as "existed") and pays only when this
    call recorded it. Two racing calls are paid once by the grant's source
    ref.
  - When the graduation milestone's certificate comes back `issued`, it pays
    the bonus.
  - The response gains `milestone_credit` and `graduation_credit`.
  - **`GET /state`** gains `milestone_credits`, and
    `milestone_credits_unreadable` when that read fails. The Lab still loads.
- **`routes/spinout_certificates.ts`.** An admin issue (outcome `issued`) and
  a hand-issued certificate pay the bonus on the same terms; the response
  gains `graduation_credit`.
- **`SpinoutLabWorkspace.jsx`.**
  - Each deliverable row shows `MilestoneCreditBadge`:
    - "+N credits (US$N)";
    - "· earned" once every paying key in the row was paid;
    - "· partly earned" for a row such as five interviews, of which two pay;
    - "Credits unreadable" when the read failed.
  - A row that pays nothing gets no badge, never "+0".
  - Someone who cannot earn sees no amounts and, once above the checklist,
    why (`MilestoneCreditNote`).
  - The arithmetic is in `lib/labCredits.js`.

**Question on #1104, and the part that waits on it.** Two paths outside the

- `services/advisor/writeRouter.ts`, Eadwyn's answer flow. It records

- The other Lab pages that name milestones (Roadmap, Office Hours, Startup)

**Follow-up (Part of #1104, after #1112 merged).** Codex found the first-of
check racy. `hasGrant` read the other half's ref before either grant was
inserted, and the two halves had different refs, so the ledger's unique
`(user_id, kind, source_ref)` index did not arbitrate them. Both 50-credit
grants could land.
- **`grantCredits` (D540) takes `unlessPaid`.** It is a source ref of the
  same user whose grant forbids this one. The condition sits inside the
  ledger insert, next to the bounty cap, so it is decided in the same
  statement. A hit is refused with the new code `superseded`, and
  `creditLabMilestone` reports it as `pair_already_paid`.
- **Each half keeps its own `lab:<user_id>:<key>` ref.** A first version of
  this fix moved both halves to one shared ref. Codex then pointed out that a
  Worker rollback restores code but not D1, so the predecessor would not see
  a shared-ref row and would pay the second half. With per-milestone refs,
  every version writes and reads the same shape.
- **Tests.** Two tests were added:
  - both halves granted together land exactly one grant, under the paying
    half's own ref (it fails on the code before this change);
  - the repeat, the other half later, and a grant written by #1112's code.

  5 of 5 mutations were caught across `grantCredits` and
  `creditLabMilestone`. `credit_grants_d540` passes unchanged.

**The cohort backfill (Part of #1104, after #1102 Q4 was answered).** The
owner answered #1102 question 4 with **A and B**. Milestones completed from
launch day earn as they are recorded (A, built above). The current cohort is
also paid once for the milestones it completed before (B).
- **`backfillLabCredits`** in `services/labMilestoneCredits.ts`.
  - **The walk.** It walks each accepted member (`spinout_lab_admitted = 1`)
    in user-id order. It passes every recorded milestone, oldest first, to
    the same `creditLabMilestone`. A member holding an `issued` certificate
    then gets `creditLabGraduation`.
  - **What the shared path gives it.** The pair pays its earlier half, and a
    milestone with no rule pays nothing. Graduation still needs the
    incorporation grant, which the walk has just paid.
  - **Idempotent on the ledger's key.** Every grant keeps its
    `lab:<user_id>:<key>` source ref, so a second run pays nothing, and so
    does a milestone already paid live. A grant the Super Admin revoked stays
    revoked, because its row still holds the ref.
  - **It also pays milestones Eadwyn recorded.** These are milestones
    `writeRouter.ts` recorded before the backfill ran: they are in
    `spinout_lab_milestones` like any other. The open question above, about
    paying them live, is unchanged.
  - **Paged.** Each call walks at most 8 members, 5 by default. A member's
    walk is about a hundred D1 queries, and an invocation allows 1,000. The
    answer carries `next_after` until every member has been walked.
  - **Failures.** A member list that cannot be read is an error, never
    "nobody to pay". A grant that fails is counted in `failed`, and the walk
    goes on.
- **`POST /api/admin/credit-rules/lab-backfill`** (`?after=`, `?limit=`).
  - It is the Super Admin's.
  - It takes a typed reason of at least ten characters, like every
    credit-rules write.
  - It returns the counts: members, milestones, granted, credits,
    already_paid, graduations, skipped by reason, failed and next_after.
  - It is recorded through `logAdminAction` as `credit_lab_backfill`.
  - There is no screen yet. The HQ credit-rules page has none either.
- **Tests.** Five tests were added:
  - the cohort paid once, with the second run writing no ledger row;
  - a revoked grant kept revoked, a revoked certificate not counted as a
    graduation, and the paged walk;
  - an unreadable member list;
  - failed grants counted;
  - the route's gate, reason and audit row.

  17 of 17 mutations were caught.

### Eadwyn's answers and the certificate backfill (#1104, question 1 A)

Two paths recorded Lab milestones or issued certificates and paid nothing. A
founder who finished a milestone through Eadwyn rather than the Lab page
earned nothing. Question 1 on #1104 was left open. The owner asked Session 1
to settle blocked tasks on the slot's own picks (#1205, 2026-10-06), so this
builds S08's pick, **A**:
- **Eadwyn's answers** (`services/advisor/writeRouter.ts`,
  `recordSpinoutMilestoneAndAdvance`). These are the interview, project,
  brand, OKR and deck milestones.
  - The helper calls `creditLabMilestone` when its insert recorded the
    milestone, the same rule `POST /milestone` follows. A milestone already
    recorded, by either path, pays nothing more.
  - A credit that fails is logged and never stops the milestone or the week
    advance.
  - The incorporation answer already pays through `syncIncorporationMilestone`
    (D585).
- **The certificate backfill** (`services/certificateIssuance.ts`,
  `backfillCertificates`). A certificate it issues calls `creditLabGraduation`,
  on the terms a live issue uses: an accepted member whose graduation
  milestone was paid.

There are 4 new tests in `cloudflare-worker/test/lab_milestone_credits_d541.test.ts`,
and 6 of 6 mutations were caught. One test pins that `writeRouter.ts` has a
single milestone insert, so a new answer path cannot bypass the credit.
