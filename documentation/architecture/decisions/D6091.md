## D6091 — Reporting periods in Grow attention (#6091)

For writers, Grow reads the earliest active, outstanding reporting period.
The window starts at the largest saved advance reminder offset. With no saved
settings it uses the reporting defaults, and a zero-length window includes
the due day. Sent and waived periods are excluded. Due and late periods rank
ahead of setup, inside the existing four-item cap, and link to the venture's
Reporting desk. An unreadable reporting source makes attention partial.
