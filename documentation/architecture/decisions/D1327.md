## D1327 — Fill with Eadwyn part 4: one mark and one sheet

S22, 2026-10-27, for #3488 (part 4 of #2431). No migration and no Worker change. It reads
D1325's `GET /api/fills/sheet` and D1326's run, record and undo. Basis: `main` at the
claim.

### 1. `FillMark` (`frontend/src/ui/FillMark.jsx`), AF2 and AF8

- **`FillTrigger`.** The 20px round trigger at the end of a blank field's label row, with
  a 32px hit area (44px on a phone).
  - Its name is "Fill [field] with Eadwyn". While AI is off and a source can still
    fill, it draws the neutral glyph and reads "Fill [field] from your sources".
  - It is drawn only when the sheet read's `field.trigger` is true. The Worker decides,
    so a typed, never-fill or decision field, and any reader, has no trigger.
  - `FillFormTrigger` is the form's ghost "Fill". `FillModuleTrigger` is a module's
    "Fill with Eadwyn", with "N waiting".
- **`FillMark`.** The provenance pill after a filled value's label. It reads one of:
  - "Eadwyn", "Eadwyn · edited" or "Eadwyn · sourced";
  - "Connector · Mercury · 12 Oct";
  - "Recorded · From Validate";
  - "Not saved", before the form's Save;
  - a reader's muted "Typed";
  - "Unreadable" when the marks could not be read. A failed read is never "Typed".
- **The card.** Pressing the mark opens its card:
  - the source;
  - the quote;
  - what Eadwyn proposed, when the member changed it;
  - the run line (`fillRunLine`, which names the control, never the option key).

  The card has Undo when the viewer may write and the fill has an id. Undo asks first
  ("Restore the value that was there before this fill?") and names what the field goes
  back to, then calls `POST /api/fills/:id/undo`.
- **`ConnectorMark`.** The read-only mark on a "From GitHub" or "From Mercury" head.
- **No model name and no option key** is drawn in a mark, its title or its card.

### 2. `FillSheet` (`frontend/src/ui/FillSheet.jsx`), AF4 to AF14 and AF24

It is D1317's `Sheet` at 520px. Below 640px it is a full-height page, as every sheet is.
Focus returns to the trigger on close.

- **Sources (AF4).**
  - Connectors and modules come first, each with a checkbox ("Use GitHub"), what it
    holds, its last sync, and "No AI tokens".
  - A connector that is not connected links to Connect.
  - Eadwyn comes last, "For what's left", with the member's own average ("Your average:
    $0.0011 over 9 runs", or "No average yet").
  - "No source has anything for this form yet." when none does.
- **Preview (AF5).** A real table: Field, Now, Will fill, Source, Cost and Accept. Each
  checkbox is named "Accept [field]".
  - The top line is "5 fields · 3 from your sources · 2 from Eadwyn · charged $0.0012".
  - A module offers its records to choose from, never a value of its own.
  - "Not filled here" names each never-fill field.
- **Conflicts (AF6).**
  - A typed value is "Yours · kept", and Accept all never takes it.
  - "Use GitHub's instead" takes that one field, by the member's own press.
  - Two sources on a field open "2 sources", in AF6's order: a connector's fact before
    a module before Eadwyn.
- **The run.** It runs only when Eadwyn is ticked and a field is left that no source
  fills. It calls `POST /api/ai/rail/run` with `option: 'form:<form>'` and reads the
  stream's `done`. `meta`, which names the model, is dropped as it is read
  (`parseStream`).
- **The states.**
  - **Running (AF9):** "Eadwyn is checking this draft." Accept all waits ("Ready when
    every value is checked."), and the footer says a run that started is still charged.
  - **Partial (AF10):** "Filled 3 of 5.", and each blank row says why.
  - **Refused (AF11):** AI off (with "Turn it on" where the page has the switch), the
    guard, a changed form ("Preview again"). The guard says what was charged.
  - **Out of tokens (AF12):** "Add tokens", and "Fill from your sources only".
  - **Empty (AF13):** "Nothing to fill. Every field here already has a value."
  - **Error (AF14):** "We could not read your sources. Try again.", with Details and a
    reference.

  The sources' values stay offered whatever the run says.
- **Nothing is written by the sheet.** Accept hands the chosen values to the form
  (`onPlace`), with each Eadwyn value's proposal uid. The form marks them "Not saved".
  The form's own Save writes, then sends `POST /api/fills/record` (D1326 §2). Mounting
  the sheet on the founder's forms is part 5 (#3489), and so is Accept all for row
  proposals (`POST /api/fills/rows/accept`).
- **Dark mode (AF24):** every surface, note and pill has its dark pair.

### 3. What it replaces

- The Market page's `FilledMark` is the shared `FillMark`, through `marketFillMark`.
- Validate's Experiments `PROVENANCE` pills are `{ fill, text }` cells, which `Cell` draws
  as the mark. `text` stays for the phone list and exports, which read cells as words.
- The "From GitHub" and "From Mercury" heads gain `ConnectorMark`.
