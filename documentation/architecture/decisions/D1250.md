### 1. The Worker: a case opens with its context

DDL2 opens a case with its round, cheque, target IC date, lead and linked deal. On `main`, only `PATCH` took these, so opening would have been two writes, and a refused context would have left a case open without it.

- **One write.** `POST /api/dd/cases` takes `round_label`, `cheque_cents` with `cheque_currency`, `target_ic_date`, `lead_user_id` and `deal_id`.
- **The same checks as `PATCH`.** They live in `caseContextFrom`, which both routes call:
  - a lead is an Admin, a partner or an investor;
  - the date is a real day;
  - the cheque is whole cents with a three-letter code;
  - the round is at most 80 characters.
- **Checked before anything is written.** A refused field opens no case.
- **The linked deal** must be the startup's own. An investor may link only a deal they are invited to or committed on. That is the relationship `POST /cases` already checks for the startup (D1149).
  - `PATCH` keeps D1246's rule (any deal), as it was.
- **When the lead is someone else,** they are told in the bell, as `PATCH` tells a new lead.
- **The audit row `case_opened`** names which context fields were given, never the figures.
- **`GET /api/dd/subjects`:** a startup now comes with its `stage` and its `deals` (id, status, instrument, opened). An Admin or a partner sees every deal of the startup; an investor sees only their own.
  - The ids go in as one bound JSON array (`json_each(?)`), so the SQL text never changes with the request.

### 2. Cases (DDL1, DDL19)

- **The tiles,** from `GET /overview`:
  - **Open cases:** "N in review · N not started".
  - **Ready for sign-off.**
  - **Open High or Critical flags:** "Across N cases".
  - **Requests waiting on subjects:** "N past due". When no waiting request has a due date, the tile says so (D1248's `past_due_reason`), never "0 past due".
- **The charts.** Each has its sentence, "View as table", and the legend.
  - **Open cases by age:** each age bucket stacked before target, past target and no target date. A missing date is a dashed hairline part.
  - **Decisions, last 12 months:** stacked by decision, with archived without a decision. A month before the first recorded decision is a dashed hairline column reading "Not recorded", never an empty column. This month is marked "to date".
  - **Where they are drawn:** in `DdCharts.jsx`, so the shared `ui/Charts.jsx` is untouched. They use its `Legend`, `ChartTable` and `useBoxWidth`.
- **The stage strip** filters by one stage at a time, as `GET /cases?stage=` reads it.
  - **Decided counts every decided case,** because its filter lists them all. The last 90 days are its sub-line.
  - **Why not the canvas's default view** (open cases and those decided in 90 days, together): the Worker filters one stage at a time, so with no stage chosen the list is every case.
- **The toolbar:**
  - search by subject or case id, read on the server from page 1;
  - Filters: type, risk band (Not scored yet included), template, decision (Not recorded included), Led by me, Past target IC date. Each filter is a chip with ×, and Clear all;
  - sort; "Read at HH:MM · Refresh"; "1–10 of 26"; previous and next; rows per page 10 · 25 · 50.
- **The columns** are the canvas's:
  - Subject and short id; type and template; stage, with the decision when decided; lead; checklist bar and "38 of 52"; open flags with the worst severity; requests waiting; risk band; target IC date; opened.
  - **The target IC date cell** shows the day and how far it is: "In 9 days", or "4 days past" in red. A decided case shows "Decided 29 Oct". No date reads "Not recorded".
  - **A case with no band** reads "Not scored yet", never Green.
- **Each row's ⋯ menu:**
  - **Open.**
  - **For the case lead or an Admin:** Change lead…, Set target IC date… and Archive…, each an in-page dialog on D1246's `PATCH`.
  - **Anyone else** reads "Only the case lead or an Admin can change this case.", never a disabled item. An archived case says it takes no further change.
  - **Change lead** searches people by name (`GET /people`). Only an Admin or a partner may search, so an investor lead is told to ask an Admin.
  - **Archive** needs its reason, and says the case leaves the open cases and is counted as archived without a decision.

### 3. Open a case (DDL2, DDL2b)

- **The subject** is picked by name from `GET /subjects`, never a typed number. The label is prefilled from it and can be edited.
- **The context:**
  - round;
  - cheque, an amount turned into cents, with a currency;
  - target IC date;
  - case lead: you, or a person found by name;
  - linked deal: the startup's deals, by instrument and the day each opened.
  - Only filled fields are sent.
- **The template cards** come from `GET /templates`: each depth's sections, items and version. Standard is the default. The line under them reads "You can widen scope later, never narrow it."
- **Private:** legal name, email and notes, as before.
- **The footer's line** says what is seeded ("This seeds the Standard template, version v3, for a startup.") and who is told:
  - nobody, until a reviewer is assigned or a request is sent;
  - or the chosen lead.
- **On success** the new case opens at its address.
- **The investor (DDL2b):**
  - the type is fixed to Startup, with "Investors open cases on startups only.";
  - the search lists only startups they share a deal with;
  - they lead the cases they open, and the people search is never read.

### 4. Templates (DDL13)

- **Who edits is the Worker's `can_edit`:** a Super Admin on HQ. They change a section's weight (above 0, at most 5) or whether it blocks.
- **The share column** is each weight's percent of the total.
- **Save as new version:**
  - opens a dialog listing each change;
  - says the version applies to new cases while open cases keep theirs;
  - posts every section with the items it already seeds. A template chooses the catalog's items and never rewrites them.
- **Everyone else** reads the same table with "HQ edits templates. You choose one when you open a case.", and no input, disabled or not.
- **A failed read** says so ("This is not an empty store: the built-in catalog still opens cases.") with Try again.

### 5. The states (DDL16, DDL17)

- **Loading:**
  - the header, the tabs, the strip and the toolbar draw at once;
  - the tiles and charts say "Reading…";
  - the table draws skeleton rows.
- **No case yet:** "No diligence cases yet.", with Open a case. The tiles are measured zeros: None yet, None raised, None waiting.
- **Filters that match nobody:** "No cases match these filters.", with what was asked ("No case matches led by me, past target ic date.") and Clear filters.
- **One failed block never blanks the page:**
  - a failed overview turns each tile and chart to Unreadable with Try again, and the rows stay;
  - a failed list says "We could not load the cases. The rest of Approvals still works. This is not an empty list." with Try again, and the tiles stay.
- **Paused after an account recovery:** `/api/dd` is in the cool-off list, so a write answers 423. Every dialog then says "Diligence changes are paused for 24 hours after an account recovery. You can still read cases." Reading goes on.
- **An advisor** sees "Advisors review the sections they are invited to." where Open a case would be.
- **A founder** on `/due-diligence`, `/my-reviews` or `/templates` is sent to `/due-diligence/requests`.
  - The redirect sits on the route in `App.jsx`, ahead of the guard, so the guards keep their four roles and the rail map (D768) gains no founder key.
  - The admin base and the case addresses never admit a founder. The Worker refuses a founder on every DD read either way.
- **A member's links** stay on `/due-diligence/…`. None is an `/admin/` address.

- **The tab counts beside Cases and My reviews:** My reviews is part 8's read. The range line already counts the cases.

- **The phone (DDL18a):** the table scrolls sideways on a narrow screen. The phone's cards are a later part.
