## D1380 — One Google consent: connecting Google also asks for the Meet permissions

- **Status:** asked for by the owner on 2026-10-29 ("why isn't Allow transcripts embedded when users connect?"). Built by S21.
- **Builds on:**
  - D757: Meet transcript import, an explicit opt-in;
  - D1376: Axal creates the Meet call, which needs `meetings.space.created`.
- **Migration:** none.

### Why

D757 kept the Meet scope off the ordinary Google connect. Google refused that scope until the owner had added it in Google Cloud, so asking for it on every connect would have broken connecting Google for everyone. A member who wanted Meet pressed a second button, **Allow transcript import**, and went through Google's consent a second time.

On 2026-10-29 the owner added both Meet scopes to the consent screen and asked for Google's verification. The second round trip then became the only thing between a member and a Meet call Eadwyn AI can join. The owner's own connection still lacked the scopes after connecting, and six Start Call runs failed as `platform_blocked`.

### What changed

`startGoogleOAuth` (`routes/calendar.ts`) asks for both Meet scopes on every ordinary Google connect and reconnect, once the owner has switched Meet on (`MEET_TRANSCRIPTS_ENABLED`). The decision lives in `meetScopesOnConnect`.

- **The scopes ride as preserved (`mp_`), not as a new Meet grant (`gm_`).** The callback therefore never refuses a member who unticks the Meet boxes on Google's page; they connect Calendar as before.
- **The Allow transcript import button stays** for that member. It keeps its own `gm_` grant, which does require the Meet scopes.
- **With Meet switched off,** the connect asks for Calendar only, as before.

Until Google's verification completes, a connect shows Google's "unverified app" screen, because the Meet scopes are sensitive.

### Verified

- **New test** in `calendar.google-oauth`: `meetScopesOnConnect` is on only when Meet is switched on and the explicit button is not in use. An ordinary connect's URL then carries both Meet scopes and the Calendar scopes, under an `mp_` state.
- **Mutation check:** forcing the function off fails the new test.
- **Neighbouring tests:** all 654 Google, Calendar, Meet and Workspace Worker tests pass. `tsc` reports no errors.
