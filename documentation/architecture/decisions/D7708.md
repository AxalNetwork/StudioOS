## D7708 — A sector index counts each observation once, over a stated window, and says which providers fed it (#7708)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-22. Filed from the owner's Research › Markets audit of 2026-11-19 (2B, 2D), under #3701.
- **Builds on:** D538 (live rows only), D7707 (period measured, retrieval date, `scope`, the observation identity), D547, D536, D533.
- **Owner answers, built as A:** Q1 keep the rolling 30 days and print its dates; Q2 one weighted mean over distinct observations (a provider that reports daily still weighs more than one that reports weekly, and the mix shows it); Q3 and #7737 Q6 keep capital and talent, labelled as global context, the same for every sector. No disabled provider is activated; demand, supply and sentiment stay Not recorded where no live source feeds them.

### What was wrong (checked on main)

- The composite `source_count` was a row count (`recomputeIndexes`); a dimension's was distinct providers (`scoring.ts`). Pages printed both as "sources".
- Every stored row added weight, so a figure fetched again counted again: a World Bank year read every six hours weighed four times a day.
- The sector page's source mix was row share by calendar month (`routes/research_sectors.ts`), beside a score over a rolling 30 days. A point is keyed by month but holds the 30 days before its last recompute, so "vs previous month" compared two overlapping windows and named neither.
- Capital and talent are fed only by world or regional figures copied to all twelve sectors (World Bank, OECD, Eurostat, D7707 `scope = 'context'`), and nothing said so.

### The decision

**Scoring (`services/market_intel/scoring.ts`).**

- *Once.* `distinctObservations` keeps one row per D7707 observation identity (`observationKey`: source, sector, geo, metric, and the period measured or the UTC day of `ts`), the latest stored by retrieval date, then id. A row of an unregistered source is dropped there and never counts.
- *In the window.* A row is in the window when its provider last returned it inside the 30 days before the compute (`retrievedAt`: `retrieved_at`, or `ts` for a row written before D7707). Recency decay runs on that date. The period measured never decides membership: a 2023 figure confirmed this week is in. The same rule is used where a read asks whether live data exists in the window: `readCompassFreshness` (`routes/market_intel.ts`) and the probe in `routes/research_overview.ts` read `retrievedAtSql()`.
- *Count.* `source_count` is the distinct registered providers that contributed, for a dimension and, through `compositeMix`, for the composite.
- *Mix.* Written at compute time beside the point: per provider, its share of the weight the score used (registry weight × decay, summed over its distinct observations), its count of distinct observations, its `scope` (`sector` or `context`), the coverage a copied figure has, and the earliest and latest period measured. The shares of one point sum to 1. The composite is an equal mean of the dimensions with a value, so a provider's composite share is the mean of its dimension shares. `context_only` is true when every entry is a copied figure.

**Storage.** Migration `202611220337_7708_market_intel_index_window.sql` adds nullable `window_start`, `window_end` and `source_mix_json` to `market_intel_indexes` (expand only, no backfill). `services/market_intel/schema.ts` declares the same columns, in its `CREATE TABLE` and through `runSchemaBootstrap`. `recomputeIndexes` writes them; every read returns them through `storedWindow()`. A point written before D7708 has none: it reads `Window not recorded`, never a derived date, and its mix is empty. Its composite `source_count` was a row count; no surface prints a composite count.

**Reads.**

- `GET /api/research/sectors` and `/sectors/:slug`: each point returns `window_start`, `window_end`, `window_reason`, `context_only`, its stored `source_mix` (with display names) and `latest_point` (was `month_to_date`); a change carries `change_against`, the previous point's period and window. The second read that recomputed a row-count mix by calendar month is gone, so the route reads `market_intel_rows` no more. `source_mix_basis` now says what the mix is.
- `GET /api/research/overview`: `heat` and `demand_vs_supply` return the current window and the previous point's (`previous_window`); each heat cell carries `context_only`.
- `GET /api/market-intel/sector-compass`: the body returns its window. `INDEX_HISTORY_NOTE` and `DELTA_NOTE` say each value is the score over its own 30-day window. `COMPASS_KV_KEY` is `compass:global:live:v7708`, so a snapshot holding the old counts is never served.

**What moves when this ships.** Scores move once: a figure stored several times now weighs once. Fixture (`research_counts_freshness_7708.test.ts`): OpenAlex's one window fetched three times, Crossref once, World Bank's 2024 figure twice, Hacker News once.
