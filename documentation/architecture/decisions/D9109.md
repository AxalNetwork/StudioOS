### What changed

The Investment case used to be put together in the browser from three reads: the thesis-fit read (`GET /api/deals/fit`), two notes reads tagged Question and Risk, and the scorecard strip for Missing. It now makes **one** read, `dealContextApi.investmentCase(id)`, through the Overview's `useRead` with its own key (the deal and the chosen firm). The Scorecard card keeps the strip read for itself. `thesisFitOf`, `caseNotes`, `CASE_TAGS` and `missingEvidence` had no other caller and are gone.

`caseView(read)` turns the answer into what each half draws.

| Block | Recorded | Not recorded |
|---|---|---|
| Thesis fit | "Fit to my thesis {score} ({confidence_label})", with the criteria that match or are outside the thesis as reasons | the block's `reason`, word for word |
| Main evidence | up to five groups, "{area} · {kind} · {count}" and the newest day when there is one; when `partial` is true, a line saying the list may be incomplete. "Open the shared evidence" (the Evidence tab) stays the way in | the reason |
| Open questions · risks | the firm's notes tagged Question or Risk, newest first, the first line cut at 140 characters; three on the card, then "{n} more in Firm notes" | the reason, with "Add one in Firm notes" |
| Missing | "Missing: {scope} ({why})", where why is "not enough recorded", the coverage as a percentage, or "coverage not recorded" for a scored scope whose coverage is `null`. With no thin scope: "No scope is below half its evidence." | the reason: before the NDA, or while the founder's sharing switch is off |

**States.**
- Loading: each half says "Reading…" on its own line, `aria-live="polite"`.
- An `unreadable` block shows its reason and its own Try again; the others keep drawing. Try again reads the whole case again, and the blocks that drew keep what they show until the answer arrives (`useRead`'s `keep`); only the failed block says "Reading…".
- A block missing from the answer, or recorded in a shape the card cannot draw (a fit with no score), is unreadable, never empty.
- The whole read failing (a network error or a 5xx): both halves say they could not be read, "That is not a claim that there are none", each with Try again.
- 409 `firm_required`: "Choose the firm this deal is for." The deal page has no firm chooser of its own, so it points at the company switcher; the read's key carries the chosen firm, so choosing one reads the case again.
- 403 or 404: the card is not drawn. The page around it handles access.

**Reads.** One `GET /api/deals/:id/case` per visit and per Try again. No `/api/deals/fit` and no tagged `/notes` read for the card, and never `GET /api/deals/:id/evidence`, which logs an evidence open (D1265, D1266); the case read logs none.

**Eadwyn.** The case serves no draft, so the card offers none. An Eadwyn proposal is saved by the investor as a Question or Risk note (D6173), and then appears here.

### Departures from the canvas

| Board | Departs from the canvas | Why |
|---|---|---|
| DE04 | Main evidence lists counts by area and kind ("Capital model · Revenue receipt · 3"), not "3 paid pilots, financial model" | The case read sends counts by area, kind and source, never an item or a sentence (D6173). |
| DE04 | Thesis fit's reasons are one line per criterion ("Sector matches your thesis"), not "robotics, EU, seed" | The fit read sends each criterion's state, not the recorded value it compared. |
| DE04 | Open questions · risks lists the firm's tagged notes, with their tag, not "open" questions | A note has no open or answered state yet, and no read ranks risks. |
| DE04 | The card shows three notes and "{n} more in Firm notes"; at twenty notes it says "{n} or more" | The canvas draws two; the read returns at most twenty, so a full page may hold back more. |
| DE04 | Missing names why each scope is listed (not enough recorded, a coverage, or coverage not recorded) | The read sends the state and coverage; the canvas's "customer references" is not a scope. |
| DE04 | Summary, fit and evidence are separate lines, not one sentence | Each comes from its own block and has its own state. |
| DE04 | Every Not recorded, Unreadable, loading and firm-required state | The canvas draws only the recorded case. |
| DE16b | None beyond the light board's: every line carries a `dark:` colour on the card's dark ground | Compared with DE16b by its classes; not seen in a browser in this build. |
| DE15 | Halves stack below `sm`; links on their own line and Try again are 44px targets | As D4815 part 15 builds the phone; the canvas has no phone board of this card alone. |
