### 3. The links

- **A C1 bar** opens `/admin/partner-invitations/invitations?stage=<key>` (`invitationsAtStage`). So does each closed count. Invitations reads `?stage=` into its stage chips (`stagesFromSearch`), keeping known keys only. The chip filter uses the bar's stage rule (`chipOf`, Guard I), so the list counts what the bar counted. The tests hold this bar by bar.
- **A C3 segment** opens `/admin/partner-invitations/deals?type=<type>&state=<s1,s2,…>` (`dealsOfTypeState`). Deals reads both into its type select and state chips (`dealFiltersFromSearch`); an address without a known state keeps the Active default. Ended opens all three ended states. "Another deal type", which the filter cannot name, opens nothing.
- **Bars and segments are buttons:** they open on click, Enter or Space, and their label names what they open.

### 4. The chart parts

`ui/Charts.jsx` gains three parts, all in the one palette: violet, sky, amber, emerald and muted, with only violet changing in dark mode. There is no second palette and no new `axal-*` token.

- **`GroupedColumns`:** columns grouped by series. The current week is hatched in each series' own colour (C2).
- **`StackBars`:** absolute stacked horizontal bars to one scale. Their segments are optional buttons (C3). `Bar100` is percentages only.
- **`WeekLines`:** a weekly line with several series. A series may be dashed, a null breaks its line, and the last point is hollow (C4).

`HBars` takes a per-row `aria` and a `testIdPrefix`. `Legend` takes `mark: 'line' | 'dashed'` for line swatches, and the hatch swatch gains its dark pair. `ChartTable` draws one column per series or segment for the new types.

**No colour carries meaning alone.** Every series and segment is named in a legend or a label, C4's series also differ by dash, and every chart has its table.

### 7. On part 4's overview read (S26, after #2825)

Every Overview figure now comes from **`GET /api/admin/partners/overview`** (D927 §2), one read in `usePartnerDesk` (`overview`, checked whole by `overviewReply`: a reply without its blocks is a failed read). It counts every row in D1, so **Guards L and F no longer touch the Overview**: no "within the newest 200" line, no fan-out, no cap. Only first use still reads R1, because it is "no invitation at all", not a figure.

- **Each block draws alone** (`overviewBlock`): Reading… while the read answers; could not be read, with the Worker's own reason, when the read or its block failed; otherwise its figures, a zero only there. A stale read keeps its figures under "Could not refresh the overview".
- **Tiles.** Row 1 is `open` (with the oldest first send), `lapsed`, `signed_90d` and `days_to_sign` ("None signed yet" when no median, never 0). Row 2 is `active_deals` (ending within 30 days, past the end not yet closed), `awaiting_signature` (whose invitation lapsed), `redemptions_90d` and **Sends that failed**: `failed_sends_30d`, the invitations whose latest send in 30 days was Not sent, with its reasons in words (`SEND_REASONS`: suppressed, email not set up, refused, failed).
- **Needs you** draws every kind the read returns, each with one action:

  | Kind | Row | Action |
  |---|---|---|
  | `lapsed` | "The invitation to [name] lapsed on [day] at [stage]." | Resend, or Open invitation when it is outside the newest 200 |
  | `failed_send` | "The latest send to [name] on [day] was Not sent: [reason]." | Resend, or Open invitation |
  | `agreement_blocked` | as D923 | Open in Contracts |
  | `deal_ending` | "The [deal] ends on [day].", soonest first | Open deal |
  | `due_diligence_open` | "The partner on the [deal] has a due-diligence case open / in review." | Open in Due diligence (`/admin/due-diligence/:uid`) |
  | `unattached_account` | one row, the count | Open firm links |
  | `revshare_closing` | one row per deal: "[n] rev-share windows on the [deal] close within 30 days." | Open Referrals for that deal |

  The read gives ids; names come from R1 and the six deal lists, and a row outside them is named by its number, never dropped. A kind the Worker could not read is named (`unreadable_kinds`), and a failed read is "The overview could not be read", never "Nothing here needs you". The Overview tab's count is these rows.
- **The banner** follows the Worker's agreement-ready answer, carried with Needs you (`overviewAgreement`), so the banner and the Agreement rows read one rule.
- **C1** gives each stage's count and **how long its oldest has been at that stage**, from when it entered it (D927). An invitation with no record of when it got there is counted and said in a line, never timed as 0. Closed is still Signed · Lapsed · Revoked (Lapsed under Closed stays as #2767 built it until the owner rules), and a stored `expired` is said.

- **C3** counts the Worker's `term_over` (active past its end, before the daily sweep) as **Ended**, as the foot always said, and says that Deals lists those as Active. A state the read does not name is said, not drawn.
- **Links** are unchanged: a C1 bar or closed count opens Invitations at its stage, a C3 segment Deals of its type and states.
