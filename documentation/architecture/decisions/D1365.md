## D1365 — The checkout sheets' Close stays in reach on a phone

- **Status:** decided by the owner on 2026-10-28, from a phone screenshot of Add tokens: "it doesn't seem there any option to exit the AI tokens screen". Built by S21.
- **Migration:** none.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1048: the checkout sheet;
  - D1287: the Add tokens sheet, its pack mode;
  - D1288: Add tokens over any page.

### The fault

On a phone, both sheets fill the screen. The page asks for `viewport-fit=cover` (`frontend/index.html`), so a full-height sheet starts under the status bar.

- **Add tokens** has its only Close (×) in the top bar. That bar sat under the status bar, and it scrolled away with the sheet.
- **The checkout** has "‹ Back" in the same place, with the same fault.

### The rule

`SHEET_BAR` (`components/checkout/CheckoutSheet.jsx`) is the sheets' top bar:
- **sticky** at the top of the sheet's scroll box;
- **opaque**, in both themes;
- **padded by the top safe area** (`env(safe-area-inset-top)`), on a phone only.

`SHEET_SCROLL_PAD` pads the scroll box by the bottom safe area, so the last control clears the home indicator. Both sheets use the two, so Close or Back is in reach however far the member has scrolled. On a desktop nothing changes.

### Tests

- **`frontend/test/checkout_pack_mode_d1287.test.mjs`:** with nothing on sale, the bar is sticky, clears the status bar and is opaque. The sheet clears the home indicator, and Close closes it. Mutation checked: without `sticky`, the test fails.
- **`frontend/test/checkout_sheet_d1048.test.mjs`:** the checkout's bar, the same way.
