## D4815 part 7 — Deals v2 DX7: Commit as the Investment committee page (#4815)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-15 for #5910, part of #4815. The first part is
  `D4815.md`; this record sits in its own file because a later part never
  appends to an existing decision file (D5615, D6466).
- **No migration.** No new Worker route and no new client method.

`/deals/commit` is drawn to boards DE07 and DE07b. The strip reads Current
decision, Votes cast, Open conditions, Rationales and Meeting. The current
decision is the newest one not yet decided; with none, the page says “No
investment decision is open.” with How voting works. The decision card shows
eligible voters, voted and recused, then Partner, Vote, Rationale and
Recorded. No voting roster is stored, so eligible voters read Not recorded
and Votes cast has no denominator; meeting invitees are not counted as
voters. A recusal is a dashed ⊘ mark on a muted row that says it is not
counted in the tally; an abstention is a solid mark that says it is a vote
cast. Conditions add a Decision column to the board's four; an open one is
marked as blocking Closing. A condition's owner is its `owner_label` or its
owner's name (D6156), read from `GET /api/ic/conditions` because the commit
room carries the id and not the name; with neither it reads Not recorded.
Minutes are shown, or their absence is stated, and only for the meeting of
the decision on the card. The method sits under How this works. The page
title stays the zone's “Commit”, the vote values are the store's Yes, No,
Abstain and Recused (not the board's Invest and Defer), and no “Not voted
yet” rows are drawn without a roster. The dialogs are unchanged until DX14.

**Review on #6876, 2026-11-16.** Three fixes:
- **Rationale counts:** the All decisions table and the zone export count rationales with `ballotOf`, as the card does, so a recusal and its declaration stay out of "N of M" everywhere.
- **Open conditions:** in the Current decision view, the open-conditions tile and the card's chip count only that decision's conditions. The Conditions view's list over every decision says "open across your decisions · each blocks its own deal's Closing".
- **Minutes:** the Minutes card names the decision whose meeting it is. The minutes export carries a Decision column, and is disabled in the Current view when the meeting belongs to another decision.
