### 1. The two kinds

| Kind | One per | `source_id` | Listed while |
|---|---|---|---|
| `event_ticket` | registration the member holds | the registration's id | it is registered, confirmed, attended or waitlisted, its event is not cancelled, and the member does not host the event |
| `event_hosted` | event the member hosts | the event's id | it is not cancelled |

- **A ticket's id is the registration's,** so each member's copy has its own `calendar_sync_records` row. Cancelling one place never touches anyone else's copy.
- **An event the member hosts is listed once,** as hosted, even if they also registered for it.
- **The place is marked in the title,** because a calendar client shows no pill. The words are the canvas's ("Your place at an event"):

  | Registration | Place | Title | Calendar status |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | confirmed, payment none, free or paid | Going | the event's title | CONFIRMED |
  | attended | Checked in | the event's title | CONFIRMED |
  | confirmed, payment pending | Pay to confirm | "Pay to confirm: [title]" | TENTATIVE |
  | registered (the host approves) | Waiting for the host | "Waiting for the host: [title]" | TENTATIVE |
  | waitlisted | Waitlist · #[n] (or Waitlist, with no position) | "Waitlist · #3: [title]" | TENTATIVE |

  A cancelled or declined place is not on any calendar.
- **A hosted event that is not published is marked** "Draft:", "Waiting for review:" or "Sent back:" (`rejected`, D917), and is TENTATIVE. A published one carries its plain title.
- **Each copy carries:**
  - the event's times, its end being its start when no end is recorded, as `buildEventIcs` writes it (never an invented hour);
  - the venue words (`location_text`) as `place`;
  - the event page (`/events/[id]`) as `url`;
  - the place sentence in its notes, with "The join link shows once you hold a seat." when one is withheld, then the event's summary.
- **Nobody is invited.** The kinds carry no attendees and no organiser, so a Google or Outlook copy is private and sends no invitation. The roster stays the host's.

### 2. The join link (#2623), in the feed too

A copy carries the event's `location_url` only when `canReadJoinLink` would give it:

- an admin;
- the host;
- an in-person event, whose address is not a join link;
- or a seat that is confirmed or checked in, with no payment pending (`hasConfirmedEventSeat`).

The rule is `joinLinkAllowed` in `myEvents.ts`, applied to the row the reader already holds. The same rule decides the add-to links (§3). A waitlisted, approval-pending or unpaid ticket gets the place and the event page, never the link: not on `/calendar`, not in the `.ics`, not in Google or Outlook.

### 3. Where they reach

- **`/calendar`** (`GET /api/calendar/events`) lists both kinds as two chips, "Event ticket" and "Hosting", with their source in the legend (`event_registrations`, `events`). The marks follow `expert_booking`'s rule: two shapes no other kind uses (a dome and a bowl), and two hues apart from the drawn six and the violet accent. Hosting is sky, as MEV1 draws it, and a ticket is lime. A row shows the venue words beside the format, and "Event page".
- **The `.ics` feed** (`GET /api/calendar/events.ics`) carries both kinds. Each VEVENT has `URL`, `STATUS` (CONFIRMED or TENTATIVE) and a `LOCATION` that joins the venue words and the link. It now takes `kinds` as `/events` does, so `?kinds=event_ticket,event_hosted` is "my events" alone. An unknown kind is 400, never an empty file, and so is an unreadable `from` or `to`.
- **Google and Outlook:**
  - "Sync now" and the push of one event carry both kinds. A tentative copy is `status: tentative` in Google and `showAs: tentative` in Outlook.
  - **A cancelled place leaves on the next sync.** The push only adds and updates, and no event route calls `onAxalSessionCancelled`. So each sync reads the copies it wrote for these two kinds whose source has left: a place cancelled or declined, an event cancelled, a host changed. It deletes them from the provider and drops their mapping. These copies are found from the source rows, not from the sync's date window.
  - A delete the provider refuses keeps its mapping, with `last_error = 'delete_refused'`, so the next sync tries again.
  - The summary's `removed` counts what left.
- **"Add to Google Calendar" and "Outlook"** come from `GET /api/calendar/event-links/:event_id`. It answers whoever may see the event (`canViewEvent`, as for its `.ics`) with:
  - `google` (`calendar.google.com/calendar/render?action=TEMPLATE`);
  - `outlook` (`outlook.live.com`) and `office365` (`outlook.office.com`);
  - `ics` (`/api/events/[id]/ics`);
  - `join_link_included`.

  Each URL holds the title, the times, the place and the event page, plus the join link only under §2. It is 404 for an unknown event and 403 for one the caller cannot see. An unreadable start or end refuses to build a link rather than write a wrong one.

### 4. "Subscribe to my events": waiting on a number

The issue says the feed should have "a per-member token like the existing calendar feed". No such token exists on `main`. `GET /api/calendar/events.ics` is `requireAuth`, so it works as a download, but not as a subscription that Google or Outlook fetch with no session.

A subscription URL that carries join links has to be revocable, so the token needs a table. The design, asked about on #2874:

- **Table:** `calendar_feed_tokens`, with `user_id`, scope `my_events`, the SHA-256 of the token (never the token), `created_at` and `last_used_at`.
- **Routes:**
  - `POST /api/calendar/my-events/feed` mints the token or replaces it, and returns the URL once;
  - `DELETE` on the same path revokes it;
  - `GET /api/calendar/feed/:token/my-events.ics` needs no session and serves `?kinds=event_ticket,event_hosted` for the member that hash names.

AGENTS.md gives migration numbers only through the issue, so this part waits for one. Until then, "Subscribe" can offer the signed-in file only.

### 5. Not in this part

- **Immediate removal** from Google and Outlook when a place or event is cancelled. That would mean calling `onAxalSessionCancelled` from `DELETE /api/events/:id/registration` and from the cancel routes in `routes/events.ts` and `admin_events.ts`, which other issues own. Until then the next sync removes the copy (§3).
- **The page's "Add to calendar" menu and "Subscribe" button** are frontend parts 4 and 5 of #2387. `frontend/src/lib/api` gets no method here, since nothing draws one yet.
- **A copy's title after a place changes** (off the waitlist, payment settled) updates on the next sync, like every other kind.
