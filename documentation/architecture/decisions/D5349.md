## D5349 — Exercise pitch draft recovery in Chromium (#5349)

The editor visual regression and axe job discovers the draft recovery spec
through its existing Playwright config. It opens a synthetic deck at 1440 and
390 px and uses the real local Worker to write and read draft revisions.
The tests interrupt draft writes to exercise IndexedDB recovery and simulate
a competing edit through the same card endpoint.

The editor exposes its undoable remove-card operation under **This card** on
both layouts. A confirmation identifies the draft as the affected copy.
Removing the last card leaves the deck empty, and the active card index
stays within the range of cards left after removal, undo or restore.
