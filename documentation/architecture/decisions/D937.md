### 2. What is read, and from where

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/admin/studio/glance` (R1): the `cohort` lane, pending `cohort_applicants`, with `count` (null when the lane failed) and `oldest_age_hours` | Applications waiting; the Applications tab's count; Needs you |
| `GET /api/admin/cohort/applications` (R2): the 12 newest cycles, each with `applicant_counts` by status (uncapped), `app_status`, the application window, and `settings` (null when unreadable) | Next cohort; the cohort table's window and pool |
| `GET /api/admin/cohort/timeline` (R3): the same 12 cycles with their week windows and `participant_count` | In the Lab now; the cohort table |
| `GET /api/admin/cohort/review` (R4): `at_risk` this week, and the newest 200 failed or grace rows | Needs you |
| `GET /api/admin/lab-hosts?status=pending` (R5): all-time `counts` by status | the Hosts tab's count; Needs you |
| `GET /api/spinout-lab/certificates` (R6): `eligible_total`, the newest 500 certificates, `awaiting` (null when unread) | Graduated; Needs you |

- **Every one of these reads is truthful:** a D1 error is a 5xx, or a null the page reads as unread.
- **Guard H (D953 §2).** `GET /api/admin/spinout-applications` and `GET /api/admin/spinout-participants` answer `[]` with 200 on an error. No figure here comes from them. The Participants tab's count is a dash, and its empty list reads "No participant is listed. This read cannot yet tell an empty Lab from a failed read, so this is not a claim that no one is in the Lab." D953's part 7 makes them truthful.
- **Guard L (D923, D953).** A list that returns its cap is incomplete. The cohort table says "The 12 newest cohorts the calendar holds." when R3 returns 12. A grace count from a full R4 reads "[n]+". A certificate count from a full R6 says it is among the newest 500.
- **Each read answers alone.** A failed read marks only its blocks, with "Unreadable", the reason and Try again. When all six fail, the page says "The Lab's figures could not be read. Nothing here is a zero." and every tab still opens its console.
- **The words.**
  - A cohort is "Open for applications", "In review", "Running", "Finished" or "Moved to [next month]", read from the Worker's `status` and `app_status`.
  - "Approved" counts `approved` and `activated`, the Worker's own minimum rule.
  - Times are ET. A week's deadline reads "[29 Oct] 00:00 ET", inclusive, never "[28 Oct] 23:59" (D953).
  - Ages use the shared bands: Overdue from 72 hours, not the canvas's two days (D953 S-AGE).
- **The writes are today's,** through today's consoles. No client method is added, so the drift test needs no change.

### 3. What each tab draws today

- **Overview (SL1).**
  - Four tiles:
    - **Applications waiting:** R1's count, "oldest [age]", and "[Nov 2026] closes [25 Oct] 23:59 ET" from R2's next open window. An amber Overdue past 72 hours, and a Review link.
    - **Next cohort:** the first cycle not yet started, with "[9] approved of [25] places · minimum [1] met". When the settings cannot be read, it says "Minimum and places could not be read, so no cohort is judged against them." (SL18 B).
    - **In the Lab now:** the running cycle's `participant_count`, "in the [Oct 2026] cohort · Week 4, day 24 of 28". With no running cycle it reads "None running" and the next start date.
    - **Graduated:** R6's `eligible_total`, with "[n] certificates issued".
  - **Cohort calendar,** drawn as a table until part 5 draws the timeline: cohort (with "now"), status, applications window, start, Week 4 deadline, the pool, and how many started. Under it, "Dates follow the Lab's rule. They cannot be moved here."
  - **Needs you:** one row per readable queue that holds something, each with the tab that acts on it: applications waiting, founders at risk this week, founders in grace, hosts waiting, and graduates with no certificate. A queue that could not be read says so in its own row. Paid formation orders with no packet stay on Formation, whose list is every startup's, not only the Lab's (defect 12).
  - **"Not read here yet",** each with what it waits for: Applications by cohort, Weeks passed by cohort, From application to company, and Applications per week (part 2).
- **Applications, Cohorts, Participants, Hosts, Formation and Graduation** are today's consoles, unchanged. Its Worker route stays until part 1 routes it through the cycle decision; production holds no `spinout_applications` row today (read 2026-10-24), so no row loses its door.
