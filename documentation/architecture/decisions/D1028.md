## D1028 — Cap table part 13: the phone layout (CT23) and dark (CT8 and every board)

- **Status:** built for S25 by S13's session at the owner's request, 2026-10-26 (#2865, part 13 of #2432).
- **Split:** D1015 §6, from S21's integration in #2852.
- **Builds on:** parts 7 to 11 (D1022 to D1026), all on `main`.
- **Migration:** none.
- **Worker:** no change. Every figure on a phone comes from the reads the desktop page already makes.
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Raise Cap Table Page.dc.html`, boards CT23a–c (390 wide) and CT8.

Paths are relative to `frontend/src/pages/raise/captable/` unless they say otherwise. "A phone" is below 1024px, CycleRoute's own rule (`PHONE_QUERY`, read through `ui/phone/usePhoneWidth.js`).

### 1. CT23a: Overview

- **The header:** CycleRoute's phone module page as it was: its bar back to Raise, and the rail as the Module select. Nothing of it changed here.
- **The tabs:** the tool's tab bar, which already scrolls sideways (`overflow-x-auto`). Each tab is now a 44px target.
- **The tool bar:** the chips, then its one action (Record an issuance, or a co-founder's Propose an issuance), which fills its row.
- **Then, in a column** (`PhoneOverview` in `CapTableOverview.jsx`):
  - Needs you, as on a desktop;
  - **Show:** the Outstanding / Fully diluted switch and By holder / By class;
  - **the group bar:** Ownership by group, once;
  - **Who owns, as cards:** one card per holder, from `holderCards` (`overviewModel.js`). Each card has:
    - its group as the pill;
    - its classes and count ("Common · 4,400,000 shares", or "options" for options alone);
    - its share by the switch.

    The first six show (`PHONE_HOLDERS`), with "1–6 of 7" and Show more. Outstanding leaves options and the pool out, as the table does. A holder with an id opens its page; one without has none to open. By class keeps its table, which scrolls sideways;
  - the rest of the side column: the tiles, the outstanding instruments and the 409A. Its bars are not drawn twice.

The Planned round card and the Holders records under the tabs are still CycleRoute's (D1015), as they were.

### 2. CT23b: a holder, full screen

- **The page:** on a phone `/raise/cap-table/h-<n>` is the holder alone. There are no tabs, no tool bar and nothing of Overview behind it.
- **How it is drawn:**
  - `CapTableTabPage` draws only `CapTableOverview` with the holder;
  - `CapTableOverview` returns only `HolderDrawer` with `page`, which is `Lightbox`'s page mode (`pageBelow="lg"`).
- **Its bar** is the cycle's own (`LightboxBarProvider` in CycleRoute): Back reads "Cap table", and the title is the holder.
- **Its body:** the pill, "% fully diluted", On record and the sections, as in the drawer.
- **Void and Correct** open their dialogs from the page, as bottom sheets.

### 3. CT23c: scenarios compared

On a phone `/raise/cap-table/scenarios/compare` draws `PhoneCompare` (`ScenariosPages.jsx`) in place of the wide table and the two charts.

- **The cards:** one scenario per card (`swipeCards`, `scenariosModel.js`), in a row that snaps as it is swiped.
  - Each card says "1 of 3 · swipe ›" and has the compare table's rows.
  - **A share reads "Today → after"** ("88.89% → 61.54%") when Today has one. A figure Today does not have reads alone: the price per share, the post-money, an instrument's conversion. A run still reading says so and draws no arrow.
  - **‹ and › step** between the cards. Each is a 44px button, disabled at its end.
- **The exit waterfall:** a table of the card in view (`exitTable`), headed "Exit waterfall · [scenario]". It has a row for each exit and columns for Founders, Investors, and Employees and advisors.
  - An exit the engine did not calculate reads "Not recorded" in every cell, never $0.
  - The assumptions line follows.
- **Eadwyn's read of the round,** and its Attach dialog, follow as on a desktop.

The founder-ownership small multiples are not drawn on a phone, because CT23c draws only the cards and the waterfall table. A desktop still draws both charts and the table.

### 4. Drawers and dialogs are bottom sheets; 44 × 44px targets

- **Bottom sheets:** on a phone every dialog and every drawer but the holder's is a bottom sheet (`Lightbox`'s `phone="sheet"`, D1014). That is:
  - `CapTableDialog`, which every change opens, on every tab;
  - the instrument drawer;
  - the proposal drawers;
  - Eadwyn's draft;
  - Record them from the model;
  - and the closed Record an issuance.

  The two that were pages below 1024px (`CapTableDialog`, the instrument drawer) are sheets now.
- **Touch targets:** `capTable.css` is plain CSS outside Tailwind's layers, imported by `CapTableTool.jsx`. Under the class `captable-phone` (`PHONE_ROOT`), every one of these is at least 44px tall:
  - a button (also at least 44px wide);
  - a select (also at least 44px wide);
  - a field;
  - a link laid out as a box;
  - and the label around a checkbox or radio.

  A link inside a sentence is inline, and keeps its line.
- **Where the class is:** on a phone it is on every root of the tool:
  - Overview's (`CapTableHomeNav`);
  - each tab page's (`CapTableTabPage`), the holder's page included;
  - the shared read's (`SharedCapTable.jsx`'s `Frame`).

  `Lightbox` is not portalled, so the sheets sit inside the root and their buttons follow the rule, the × included.
