### 1. Which room is drawn

`ClosingRoom` keeps both addresses (`/raise/capital-relationships/closing`, `/deals/:dealId/closing`).

- **Who may enter (CP19).** The founder, an investor, an Admin and an Operating Partner. Anyone else (an Explorer, an Advisor) meets "Closing rooms open to the founders and investors of a deal.", with Go to your home, and **no read is made**.
- **The deal's closing.** For the founder, an investor and an Admin, the room reads `GET /api/closings` and takes the deal's open closing, else its newest (`closingForDeal`, D1219). It then reads `GET /api/closings/:uid` (`RecordRoom`).
- **No closing yet.** Until the studio team opens one, the room is D1213's deal room, with one sentence saying so.
- **A failed list read** costs only that sentence. It reads "This deal’s closing record could not be read", and the deal room still draws (CP18).
- **An Operating Partner** reads no closing record (D1215 §2 gives them none). They keep D1213's checklist, transfers and total, marked "Read only · Money steps are the studio team’s." (CP19).
- **An investor whose closing read answers 404** meets "Only this line’s investor, the founder and the studio team see it." (CP19).

### 2. The founder's room (CP1), and an Admin's under the deal

- **The header:** "Raise › Capital relationships › Closing", "Closing · [company] [round]", and the instrument.
- **The seven steps**, each Done, Now, still to come, or Not read. They are read over the lines not withdrawn:

  | Step | Done when |
  |---|---|
  | Paper | every line has paper |
  | Sign | every line's paper is signed |
  | Route | every line has a route |
  | Pay | every line is sent or received |
  | Receipt | every line is received |
  | Cap table | the founder accepted or dismissed the proposal |
  | Reporting | the closing is Closed |

  A paper read that failed makes Paper and Sign "Not read here", never done. One still running reads "Reading…".
- **Tiles:**
  - Committed: `total_cents`, every line not withdrawn;
  - Signed: lines whose paper is complete, or Not recorded when a paper read failed;
  - Received: received lines only;
  - the deadline and the days left.
- **Lines:** investor or vehicle, route, amount, paper, money and next, as the canvas's table.
  - **Paper** comes from D1216's staged envelopes, read one commitment line at a time (`GET /api/legal/esign/staged/line/:commitmentId`). A Raise prospect line has no commitment, so no paper is read for it.
  - **Money** is D1215's status: Waiting for money, Sent (not yet confirmed), Received or Withdrawn.
  - **Next** says who acts, in order:
    1. the studio team sets the route, then prepares or sends the paper;
    2. a signer signs: "Your turn: sign" for the viewer's own stage;
    3. the Super Admin countersigns;
    4. the investor sends the payment;
    5. the studio team confirms receipt;
    6. the founder decides the cap-table change.

    A failed paper read says so. A missing amount is Not recorded, never `$0.00`.
- **Needs you:** each line where it is the founder's turn, and the cap-table decision while it is proposed and the closing is open.
- **Your company's receiving account:** Not read here yet (§4).
- **An Admin** reads the same lines and tiles. Instead of the account and Needs you, they get one card: "Open in Contracts › Closings". Every act is there (D1219).

### 3. The investor's room (CP2, CP20)

- **What is drawn:** the investor's own line or lines, from D1215's `reach: 'own'` read:
  - the route and the amount;
  - three boxes: Paper, Money, Route;
  - the round's total, "Round: [total] committed";
  - "Other investors in the round are not shown to you."
- **What is never drawn:** another investor's name, line or amount. No read of the commitments, the checklist, the transfers or the reporting desk is made for an investor.
- **What you can do:**
  - **Review and sign.**
  - **See payment instructions** (CP9, §4).
  - **I have sent it.** It says the investor's word is recorded when they accept a match from their Mercury, since no route records it without one.
- **From Mercury:** the investor's own closing proposals (`GET /api/mercury/investor/proposals?kind=closing`, D840 part 4). Only proposed ones for their own live commitments are shown.
  - Each opens "Accept this match?", in an in-page dialog (a bottom sheet on a phone).
  - Accept calls `POST /proposals/:uid/accept`: D1217's writer, which records Sent, not yet confirmed, and never Received. Not this one dismisses it.
- **A syndicate line** with a `syndicate:<id>` vehicle draws D1219's `SyndicatePanel` (CP6) for its lead and members. D1214 lets only them read it.
- **The phone (CP20):**
  - one column;
  - the step as one line ("Step 4 of 7 · Pay · 1 line not sent");
  - Review and sign, payment instructions and the match at full width, 48px or 44px tall;
  - every drawer and dialog as a bottom sheet (`phone="sheet"`).
- **The founder's lines on a phone** are one card per line, never the table.

### 4. Departures, and why

- **CP9's instructions, CP1's receiving account, and the account-change notice.** D1217's routes (`/api/closings/company-receiving-accounts`) are **not mounted on `main`**. D1217 left them unmounted pending S01's answer, and #3214 is still open for that remainder. The house rule forbids a client without a mounted route.
  - The drawer opens with CP9's warning first: call the company on a number you already hold.
  - It then says the instructions are not read here yet, naming #3214, and that nothing here tells anyone where to pay.
  - No account number, mask or reveal is drawn. The 30-second reveal and the change notice wait for the mount.
- **CP4's Sign button.** A stage's signing link goes to its signer by email (D1216 §3). No read returns a founder's or investor's token, only the Super Admin's countersign token.
  - The drawer shows the signing order, with Signed and its day, Your turn, Waiting, Declined, and Waiting for the Super Admin.
  - It says when the link was sent, and that the signer signs on the e-sign page.
  - Eadwyn's clause summary and the comments have no read, and say so.
- **CP10 as the founder and investor read it.** The drawer lists the line's evidence, newest first, with its side; only receiving-side evidence makes a line Received. Recording a transfer or uploading a confirmation is an Admin's act on Contracts › Closings (D1219), which the drawer links for an Admin.
- **CP11's before and after.** No read models a closing line's fully diluted change. The dialog lists the received lines, says the model is not read here yet, and offers:
  - Add to cap table: `POST /captable/accept`;
  - Dismiss the proposal: `POST /captable/dismiss`;
  - Not now, which writes nothing.

  Accepting records the decision (D1215). Raise › Cap table writes the holder (#2432). The canvas's "the same proposal waits on Raise › Cap table" is not claimed: that page does not read a closing's proposal yet.
- **CP12's Start reporting duties.** D1058 creates the duties when the closing is marked Closed, so there is no start to press.
  - After Closed, the card reads D1058's desk (`GET /api/reporting/desk?project=`) and keeps the duties whose `closing_line_uid` is one of this closing's lines.
  - Each duty shows its holder, title and source, and "Default, not in the paper" where the paper left a term blank.
  - No route confirms a default term from both sides, so no control does.
  - An investor's card says their duties are not read here yet: D1058's investor read names a company, not a closing line.
- **The activity** (CP15) stays on Contracts › Closings (D1219). The founder's room does not repeat it.

### 5. The states (CP16–CP18)

- **Empty:** "No commitment on this deal yet. Lines appear here when an investor commits in the deal room." In the receipt drawer: "No evidence recorded for this line yet."
- **Loading:** the header and the seven steps draw at once; the tiles and lines pulse.
- **Error and partial:** one failed block never blanks the room. The blocks that fail on their own are:
  - a line's paper: that cell, the Signed tile and the strip's Paper and Sign;
  - the investor's Mercury: "Your Mercury could not be read", with Try again;
  - the reporting desk;
  - the closing list.

  A failed closing read says "This closing could not be read. That is not the same as there being nothing to close.", with Try again.
