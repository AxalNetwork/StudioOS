## D1298 — HQ › Content board: Ready to publish and Scheduled lanes, article card links, and the stale rail line (#3394)

- **Status:** decided and built by S17, 2026-10-27 (#3394, part of #2383).
- **Canvas:** the owner's "Content queue", boards ART1–ART18 (`design/incoming/Content queue · Boards.dc.html` and `Content Queue Page.dc.html`), integrated by D1290 (#3386). The map is `documentation/audits/CONTENT_QUEUE_MAP_2026-10-26.md`.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `b1984e2643`.
- **Migration:** none. `publish_at` was added in migration 346 (D484, #1025).

### 1. What changed

Three items from #2383 item 15 and the board read:

1. **HQ › Content's board splits approved articles by `publish_at`.**
   - `ready_to_publish`: approved articles with no `publish_at` time. They wait for someone to publish them by hand.
   - `scheduled`: approved articles with a `publish_at` time. They go out when that time passes.
   - Implemented in `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/admin_content.ts`: `LANES` now has five pipeline lanes, and both `pipeline.lanes` and `board.lanes` use a bound `SUM(CASE WHEN publish_at IS NOT NULL ...)` read to place each approved article.

2. **Article cards link to `/admin/articles/<id>`.**
   - `frontend/src/pages/hq/ContentPage.jsx` renders an article `BoardCard` as a `Link` to the single-article review.
   - Publications and escalations stay unlinked: publications have `/admin/publications/:id` (not this board), and escalations live in a branch's database.

3. **The stale "Publish time" rail line is gone.**
   - `frontend/src/pages/hq/ContentPage.jsx` no longer lists "Publish time" in the `WorkerRail` `unavailable` list, because `publish_at` stores when an approved article goes out.
   - The page subtitle and comments now name seven lanes.

### 2. Stores and queries

- `articles.status = 'approved'` plus `publish_at IS NULL` → Ready to publish.
- `articles.status = 'approved'` plus `publish_at IS NOT NULL` → Scheduled.
- A database without migration 346 cannot split approved articles; both lanes read as `Unreadable` with the migration reason, and the pipeline total stays honest.
- Every SQL statement is static; the three `publish_at` variants in `newestCards` are literal prepared strings, not interpolated.
