## D9024 — Calendar: a venture's Grow items link to Grow › Distribution only for a reader its routes admit (#9024)

- **Status:** built in the PR that closes #9024. It carries out the owner's answer Q18 = b on #3723 (2026-11-10).
- **Builds on:** #5131 (D1486 §e, Grow's campaigns, ad flights, newsletter sends and sequence steps on the Calendar), D4473 (RoleGuard admits the role browsed as or a held licence) and D4484 (the explicit Distribution tab routes).

### The decision

- An advisor who is an accepted member of a venture keeps seeing its Grow items on their Calendar (campaigns, ad flights, newsletter sends and sequence steps). The gate on which items appear is unchanged, and so is the `.ics` feed: Grow items are page only.
- An item carries a link to `/grow/distribution/...` only when its reader may open that page. Every other reader gets the item with no `url`, and its drawer `href` is null. Neither the list's "Event page" nor the drawer's link is drawn for them.
- **The decision uses the route guard's own rule, not a copy of it.** `frontend/src/lib/growDistributionAccess.js` exports `opensGrowDistribution(role, held)`. It applies `guardAdmits` (`frontend/src/lib/activeRole.js`), the function `App.jsx`'s RoleGuard now calls, to `GROW_DISTRIBUTION_ROLES`. The Worker (`services/calendar/growDates.ts`) imports that module and gives it the reader's `users.role` and `heldLicences` (what `/api/auth/me` reports and RoleGuard reads). So an advisor who also holds a founder licence keeps the links, as RoleGuard lets them in.
- `routes/growDistribution.jsx` keeps its literal `guard(['admin', 'founder'], …)`, because the route scanners under `frontend/test/` read it as written. Tests on both sides read that file and fail if any of its guards differs from `GROW_DISTRIBUTION_ROLES`: `frontend/test/calendar_grow_links_9024.test.mjs`, and `cloudflare-worker/test/calendar_grow_dates_5131.test.ts`, whose test also checks that the roles given a link are exactly that list.
- An admin's "View as" preview is not visible to the Worker. An admin is treated as role `admin`, and the guard list admits that role.
