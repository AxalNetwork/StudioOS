### What part 1 draws (GR-21, GR-24, GR-27a and d)

1. **The due strip.** Overdue, Next 30 days, Next 90 days and Later sit above the obligations, in that order.
   - Each window is a toggle button (`aria-pressed`, 44px). It sets or clears the table's `due_window` filter, back to page 1.
   - The count comes from the read's `parts.due_counts`, taken over every obligation, so it does not change when a filter is applied.
   - Overdue carries ⚠ and its word when it holds one.
   - A read without counts draws no strip, never four zeros.
2. **A done or snoozed obligation is in no due window**, whatever its date (`dueWindow`). The canvas reads "1 snoozed (in no due window)", and the line under the strip counts these as "done or snoozed (in no due window)". Before this change, a done obligation due next week was counted in Next 30 days.
3. **Under the header:** "Next 90 days · {day} – {day + 90}", counted from the Worker's own day (`parts.due_on`), never the browser's.
4. **Calendar → Compliance is named** as the second editor of the same `compliance_events` rows: "Both write the same records."
5. **The rows.**
   - Repeats is its own column.
   - Overdue and Snoozed are words (D5129), and Culture is a kind.
   - An ownerless row reads "No owner named" (warn).
   - A repeat the store does not hold is Not recorded, never "Once".
6. **The checklist (GR-24).** Each item is a ✓ or an empty box, with one line under it:
   - a done item says what ticked it: "Spin-Out Lab · date", "By hand · date" or "From Mercury, as of …";
   - an open item says what will: "Open · Ticks from the Spin-Out Lab" or "Open · Tick by hand";
   - "done" and "open" are also said in words for a screen reader.
   - `checklist()` now sends `ticks` (`lab`, `mercury` or `hand`), so an open item can say this.
7. **Updates history** shows the last five, titled "Updates history · last 5", with "Write the update in Reporting →".
8. **Phone (GR-27a):** the same strip, two by two, above the stacked rows.
