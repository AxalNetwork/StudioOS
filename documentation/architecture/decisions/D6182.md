## D6182 — The deal page's reference calls ask before Delete and Record consent (#6182)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-15 by S04. D6735 restores the firm-visibility line after narrowing the Worker's read rule.
- **Parent:** D630 (the deal-room screens), D1153 (the deal page's working tabs). Under #4815.
- **No migration. No Worker change.**

### What changed

The reference calls card on `/deals/:dealId/diligence` (`components/deals/DealReferencesTab.jsx`) follows the deal page's rules (D1153):

- **Delete and Record consent ask first.** Each button opens the shared `Lightbox` (`ReferenceConfirm`), which says what changes. The write (`api.deleteReference`, `api.captureReferenceConsent`) is made only from the dialog's confirm button. A refusal stays inside the dialog, which stays open so the press can be retried; Back writes nothing. No one-click destructive action remains on the card.
  - Delete: the check, its consent and its notes leave the deal for everyone who reads it, and only its author or an admin can delete it (the Worker's rule, `routes/references.ts`).
  - Record consent: it records that the person agreed, now, with the caller as the one who recorded it. It records or transcribes no call.
- **The deal page's styling.** The card is `CARD` with `H3` and `SUB` from `pages/deal/DealFrame.jsx` and the axal tokens; the buttons are the shared action classes. A gray class appears only as a `dark:` pair. Every button and field is 44 px to touch below `md`. The D630 kit's four states (loading, empty, Not recorded, Unreadable) are kept: they are shared with the other deal-room screens.
- **The canvas's pills** (`design/incoming/Investor Deal Page.dc.html`): `Consent given` when consent is recorded, `Consent asked` when it is not, and `Notes kept` when the check has notes. The words live in `dealRoomCopy.js`.

### Recording features left out

- **Recording, transcript and summary** stay "Not available yet" with no control: those routes answer 501.

### Tests

`frontend/test/deal_rooms_d630.test.mjs` (sp-references): the pills; the firm-visibility line restored by D6735; the card's classes, 44 px targets and no hex colour; for each of Delete and Record consent, the dialog opens without a write, Back writes nothing, a refusal stays in the dialog, and a confirm writes once and re-reads.
