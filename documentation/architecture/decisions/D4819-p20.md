## D4819 part 20 — Phone triage of a licence (L20) (#4819, #5932)

Built for #5932, after parts 1 to 3 in `D4819.md` and part 12 in `D4819-p12.md`. As with part 12, the part number is the board number (L20), so it cannot collide with a sibling slice's part 4. It is frontend only, with no Worker change and no migration.

### The decision

On a phone the Licences console supports triage. It does not reproduce the desktop workstation. "Phone" is below `md` (767 px and narrower), the rule the ledger's stacked list already used (part 1). The page reads it with `usePhoneWidth(BELOW_MD_QUERY)` (`ui/phone/usePhoneWidth.js`, D717's `md` rule). The layout differences that are pure CSS use Tailwind's `md:` and `max-md:` variants.

**The terms editor is not offered on the phone.** This settles the question part 2 left to "the phone slice". The Terms section on a phone is the read-only Terms view alone. Amend terms is replaced by one sentence: "Terms are amended on a wider screen. A phone shows them read-only." The Territory picker and the Terms schedule form are editors too (`termsEditor` in `licenceNavModel.js`). So a phone does not show them as sub-tabs (`phoneSectionTabs`), and a URL that names one (`?tab=territory`, `?tab=schedule`, or "Open terms" from a request) draws the Terms read instead (`phoneStep`). The Terms read already shows the countries and every schedule term with its contract section. Nothing readable is lost; only the writes are. An amendment session that is already open when a window narrows is kept, so nothing typed is lost.

### The order on a phone (L20)

1. **Search.** It is a search box above the licence (`licence-phone-search`, drawn below `md`). Submitting it returns to the ledger with the query typed into the ledger's own search. The next licence is then one query away, not a Back and a scroll.
2. **Identity.** The name, then "reference · territory" on one line (`phoneIdentity`).
3. **Primary status.** The header's Status fact. The header's other six facts are not drawn on a phone. The agreement follows as a triage row; renewal, branch and type are in the Overview's cards and the Needs HQ items.
4. **Items that need HQ.** The same strip, with each item on its own line.
5. **Requests and decisions.** With requests waiting this is the one primary action, "Review N requests", which opens Operations › Requests and compliance (part 12's card, where Approve and Decline are). With none it is a row that says None, Reading or Unavailable, never "Review 0".
6. **Agreement.** A row with the agreement fact's word and detail. It opens Agreement.
7. **History.** A row that opens History. It says "Who changed what, and when", not a latest event, because the History read belongs to that section and the row does not guess one.

Rows 5 to 7 are `PhoneTriage` (`LicenceOverview.jsx`, model `phoneTriage`), drawn on the Overview only and above the tabs. Below the tabs, the Overview on a phone leaves out the Licence, Agreement and Requests cards, which sit above. The Commercial, Access, Branch and Billing cards and Sources stay. The requests side panel is not drawn on a phone: the Requests row opens its card. A failed read keeps its own Try again beside its row, never a button inside a button.

### The tab strip

On a phone the six sections are a 3 × 2 grid of tabs, each at least 44 px tall. The second row (a section's views) wraps instead of scrolling. So neither row can make the page scroll sideways. From `md` up the strip is as part 3 left it: one row that scrolls inside itself. The tablist, roving `tabIndex` and the arrow, Home and End keys are unchanged at every width. The panel's grid is now `grid-cols-1` below `lg`, one `minmax(0, 1fr)` column, so a wide child cannot widen the page.

### Touch targets

Below `md`, every button, select and text field in the Licences page is at least 44 px tall. The page wrapper sets this (`PHONE_TARGETS` in `AdminLicences.jsx`), so a control added later is covered too. Other targets:

- The triage rows are 56 px, and Review is 48 px.
- The breadcrumb's Licences link is 44 px.
- Refresh is 44 × 44.
- A ledger row on a phone is one target of at least 56 px: the licence link covers the whole row.

### Seen at 390 px

**Seen, on synthetic data.** The production build (`npm run build`) was served from `docs/` with an SPA fallback, and every `/api/*` read was stubbed with the L04 example: active, executed v4, branch on 142 of 143, an overdue invoice and two requests waiting. Chromium (Playwright) rendered it at 390 × 844 with deviceScaleFactor 1, signed in as a Super Admin. On the licence's Overview, Terms (opened as `?tab=schedule`), Operations › Requests and compliance, and the ledger:

- `scrollWidth` equalled `clientWidth` (390) on all four, so nothing scrolled sideways.
- No visible button, select, text field or tab in the page was under 44 px.
- The triage order measured top to bottom as listed above, with the tabs after History.
- Terms showed the read with the phone sentence. It had no Amend terms and no schedule form.
- With focus on the Overview tab, ArrowRight moved focus to Terms and wrote `?tab=terms`.

Production holds no licence, so this was not seen on real data. Dark mode was not looked at in the browser; the new markup uses the page's Tailwind `dark:` pairs (`check-dark-mode` passes).

### What this leaves out

- **Country pricing (D5017)** still draws under the Terms read on a phone. It is a recommendation with its own Apply, Override and basis writes, not the terms editor. Hiding it changes a test that pins its mount, so it is left to whoever decides how the phone treats pricing.
- **The canvas's phone top bar** (Back, title, More) is not drawn. The page keeps the HQ shell's header and the breadcrumb. The lifecycle actions stay where the header puts them; #5927 (part 4) moves them into a More menu.
- **Other editors** (Seats, Administrators, the domain detach, notices) are still offered on a phone, at 44 px. L20 names only the terms editor.
- **The ledger at 390 px** keeps part 1's layout, with 44 px controls and whole-row links. The Needs HQ chips and filters are not redesigned for the phone.

### Tests

`frontend/test/licence_phone_triage_d5932.test.mjs` (8 tests) mounts the detail view and the page on fake Worker reads, with `matchMedia` answering the phone query and, as a twin, not answering it. It covers:

- the model: the terms editors dropped and remapped; triage order, the primary, None, Unavailable and Reading;
- the phone order and what the header and Overview leave out;
- Try again outside its row;
- no terms editor for `terms`, `territory` and `schedule`, while the desk still amends;
- the 3 × 2 grid with 44 px tabs, roving focus and keys, and the wrapping second row;
- the triage rows' heights;
- search first, returning to the ledger with the query; the page's 44 px rule; the whole-row ledger link.

Fifteen mutations of the new lines were made and all fifteen were caught.

Existing tests changed without weakening. `licence_navigation_d4707` now pins the phone-aware `step` and the `md:`-scoped scrolling row, and adds the phone grid. `licence_brand_kit_d198` and `licence_kind_ui_d196` accept `phoneSectionTabs`, which still filters `sectionTabs(section, kind)`.
