## D8525 — Project move and rename commit only if the scope and slug they read are still current (#8525)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-25. Fixes a defect S21 found on `main` `efabf0ce9b` (tracker #8509, row E1). None of the interleavings below has been observed in production.
- **Migration:** `202611250054_8525_project_move_followups.sql` — `project_move_followups` (project, from and to company, actor, `pending | done`, attempts, last error), with an index on `(status, id)`. Additive.

### What was wrong

The move route (`POST /api/projects/:id/move`) and the rename route (`PUT /api/projects/:id/slug`) read the venture's company and slug, checked against them, then wrote with no condition that they were still so. Two moves from A both committed, each with a `project_move` event "from A" and an agent transfer from A. A move racing a rename could leave two projects in one company sharing an address, or retire a slug in the wrong scope. After a move, `afterMove` caught any failure, and the move had committed, so a retry answered 409 `unchanged` and the step never ran again.

### What this decides

1. **Compare and set.** Each batch opens with a guard statement that aborts the whole batch unless the venture is still where the route read it. The guard is the repository's existing idiom: `SELECT CASE WHEN … THEN 1 ELSE json('project_conflict') END`, as in `team_skills.ts`, which raises `malformed JSON` and rolls the batch back.
   - The move's guard (`projectMove.ts` `moveGuard`) requires all of these:
     - the venture still sits in `from` with the slug read;
     - the company it leaves is not archived;
     - the company it goes to exists and is not archived;
     - the owner the route checked still belongs to that company.
   - The rename's guard (`renameGuard`) requires the venture still sits in the scope read, with the slug it replaces.
   - The `UPDATE` in each batch carries the same `company_id IS ? AND slug IS ?` condition, so it stays safe even without the guard.
2. **A losing request writes nothing and answers 409 `conflict`** with the venture's current `company_id` and `slug`, so the client rereads. The event, the retired slug, the agent transfer and the owed step are all in the aborted batch.
3. **The step after a move is owed, not lost.**
   - The move's batch ends with a `project_move_followups` row, `pending`.
   - After the batch commits, the route runs the step through `runFollowup`. A success marks the row `done`; a failure counts the attempt and leaves the row pending.
   - The Worker tick (`retryMoveFollowups` in `scheduled()`) retries pending rows once they are at least a minute old, oldest first, ten at a time.
   - Running the step twice ends no run twice: a run is ended only while it is `admitted` or `running`. A board or a team may be told twice, which is harmless.
4. **This is a minimal record of its own.** #8516 (S30) is building a per-effect record for what follows a signature, and it had not landed. When it does, the two can merge.
5. **The incorporation path is unchanged.** `toNewCompanyUid` already guards every statement on the order's state, and the filing still calls `afterMove` as before. `afterMove`'s never-throw contract stays for it.
6. **The owner check is conditional on the caller naming the owner.** The move route always names the owner it checked. A direct caller of `projectMoveStatements` that names none, such as the agent-engine tests, skips that one clause; every other clause still applies.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/project_move_race_8525.test.ts`. Each race holds both requests at their write with a barrier in the test D1 until both have passed their checks, then runs the batches one after the other in a chosen order:

- move against move;
- move against rename, each order;
- rename against rename;
- the destination archived between read and write;
- the owner leaving the destination between read and write;
- a failed after-step retried by the tick, ending the run exactly once;
- the tick leaving a fresh row to the move's own attempt.

`agent_transfer_d1528`'s source-order check now pins `runFollowup` after the move's batch. The shared `_agent_engine_tables.ts` adds the new migration, because every fixture that moves a project needs it.
