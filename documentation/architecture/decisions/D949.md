### 3. SL8: one founder

A uid that is not a participant says so. A section whose read failed says so in its place.

### 4. The three dialogs (SL8a–c)

Each says what it changes, keeps nothing preselected, and shows the Worker's refusal or its result in place until Done.

| Dialog | Write | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| A · Give grace for Week n | `POST /api/admin/cohort/grace` `{ user_id, cycle_id, week, hours, reason }` | 24, 48, 72 or custom from 1 to 168 hours, and a reason. The new cutoff is shown in ET before saving. |
| B · Change Week n | `POST /api/admin/cohort/override` `{ …, decision: pass \| fail, reason }` | Passed ("Week n+1 opens for them now") or Failed ("Their workspace pauses at Week n. They are told."), and a reason. |
| C · Open the workspace | `POST /api/auth/step-up` `{ totp_code }`, then `POST /api/admin/impersonate/:userId?context=` | A reason of ten characters or more and a six-digit authenticator code. The code is confirmed first, because the route needs a recent step-up; a refused code opens nothing. |

The week a dialog changes is the one the founder is held at: their failed week when paused, else their current week. A founder with no cohort cycle or no week (admitted, not started, or a start no cycle holds) gets a sentence saying why nothing can be changed, and no write.

### 5. One Worker change: `user_id` on D946's rows

The grace, change-a-week and open-workspace writes are keyed by the account's numeric id, which D946's rows did not carry (only `uid`). `services/labParticipants.ts` now adds `user_id` to each row; D946's test pins it. It is not personal data, and no email leaves the read.
