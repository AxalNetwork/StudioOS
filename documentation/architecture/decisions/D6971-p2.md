### Departures from the canvas

- The three steps are three numbered cards, on the page and on slide 3, not the journey line: the kit's journey is laid out for four steps, and three would leave a blank column. Each step's credit is a line of words ("Credit: none yet.", "Credit: +1 introduction credit for you."); the dashed square, the circle marked I and the square marked P are not drawn. On slide 4 the credits are labelled "I ·" and "P ·" in words.
- The cover has no diagram: the kit's cover takes artwork or nothing.
- The availability panel's facts are one paragraph, not a four-row list, and "New to Axal VC? Create a free account" is a line pointing to Next step (the panel's note cannot hold a link). Only the signed-out state is built: the kit's fixed panel reads no session, so a signed-in visitor also sees "Sign in to get your link →" (PGE02B's "Open my referral link →" is not built), and the paused state is held.
- The credit table is two cards (introduction credit, perk credits) with the strategic line beneath, not one three-row table.
- "Referral partners →" links `/programs/referral-partners`, which #6970 maps; until that lands it shows the missing-programme page.
- The link preview uses the generic site image; the site is light only, as on the pilot.
