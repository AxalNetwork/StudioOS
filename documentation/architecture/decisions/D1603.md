## D1603 — The deck exports as a slides-suite file (.odp), recorded in the export ledger (#3882)

- **Status:** built 2026-10-31 by S07 for #3882, part of #3716 (deck builder v2). It serves pd8b's "Slides-suite file (.odp)" card.
- **Migration:** 754 (`deck_exports` rebuilt so `format` takes `'odp'`).
- **Charge:** none. No model runs.
- **Builds on:**
  - D895 (deck access);
  - D901 (the HTML export and the ledger);
  - D904 (the editable PowerPoint and its chart pictures);
  - D1112 (the 12 × 6 grid).

### 1. Why

The final canvas offers four formats: PDF, PowerPoint, HTML and an OpenDocument Presentation. An ODP opens natively in LibreOffice Impress and the other open suites. A founder whose investor uses neither PowerPoint nor Google still sends a file they can edit.

### 2. The writer (`services/decks/odp.ts`)

`writeOdp({ title, version, slides, renderable, images, brand, notes, chartShot })` returns an ODF 1.3 package.

- **The package** is the stored zip `buildZip` writes:
  - `mimetype` is the first entry, stored and with no extra field, holding `application/vnd.oasis.opendocument.presentation`;
  - then `META-INF/manifest.xml` (every part and picture, each with its media type), `content.xml`, `styles.xml`, `meta.xml` and `Pictures/*`.
- **One master page at 16:9:** 28cm × 15.75cm.
- **Text stays text.**
  - Each block is a `draw:frame` > `draw:text-box` with real paragraphs, placed by `boxOf` on the 12 × 6 grid.
  - A card made of fields (D897), and the Sources appendix, are a title frame and a body frame.
  - A metric with no figure reads "Not recorded", never 0.
- **Images** are the export's fetched, byte-capped and sniffed set (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP; never SVG input). An image that could not be fetched says so in its frame (`IMAGE_MISSING`).
- **Charts** are pictured from the print page when the headless browser is bound (`chartShot`, shared with the Google Slides export). Otherwise a chart is its type and series as text. Either way, a text frame under it carries its label and source line ("Source: grow.paying_accounts, as of 2026-10-10", or "Source: Not recorded"). A series with no points reads "Not recorded", never a 0 bar.
- **Speaker notes** are `presentation:notes` on each page, only when asked for.
- **Brand:** the venture owner's footer and watermark (`ventureBrand`), never the exporter's.
- **Escaping and logs:** every text node goes through `xmlEsc`, and nothing logs a URL, a title or a figure.

### 3. Two departures from #3882, and why

- **Charts are not SVG with a PNG fallback.** No SVG chart renderer exists on `main`, and redrawing a chart here could plot a figure the deck does not hold. The ODP uses the print page's own picture of the chart, as the Google Slides export already does, and text when that cannot be taken. A single PNG `draw:image` is valid ODF. An SVG source can be added beside it once a chart renderer exists.
- **No "Projected" label yet.** Nothing on `main` marks a figure as projected, and no export writer draws such a label. The label and its footnote are #3790's work (B14: the compliance labels, which wait on owner decision T). When that lands its helper, `odp.ts` calls it beside the HTML and PowerPoint writers. Until then there is nothing for the label to apply to.

### 4. The route

`POST /api/decks/:id/export/odp` in `routes/deck_exports.ts`, beside `/export/html`. The body is `{ notes?: boolean, sources?: boolean }`.

- **Access is HTML's** (`projectOwned(..., { write: true })`):
  - founders export;
  - plain admins, advisors and anyone outside the venture get 403.
- **The 60-card cap applies.** The Sources appendix is one more page.
- **On 200:** `Content-Type: application/vnd.oasis.opendocument.presentation`, `Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="<slug>-v<version>.odp"`, and `X-Content-SHA256`.
- **The ledger comes first.** One `deck_exports` row (`format: 'odp'`, the SHA-256) and one `raise.pitch-deck` audit row are written in one batch through `exportStatements`. When they cannot be written, the answer is 503 `export_not_recorded` and no file.
- **Where `'odp'` is registered:**
  - `EXPORT_FORMATS` includes it;
  - `FORMAT_OF_TYPE` maps the ODP type, so a future consolidated route records it;
  - `GET /:id/exports` lists ODP rows with no other change.
- **A shared helper:** the chart-picture closure moved into `chartShotFor`, now used by both the Google Slides and the ODP export. Its behaviour is unchanged.
