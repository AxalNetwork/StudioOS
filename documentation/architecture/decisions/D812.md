### 1. The decision

The owner's answer:
- HQ and partners run programmes.
- HQ approves a partner's programme before it opens.
- Members apply from their dashboards.
- The programme's owner picks the testers.

### 2. As built

**Migration 516** adds three tables.
- **`testing_programmes`:**
  - `owner_kind` is `hq` or `partner`.
  - A partner's programme carries its firm in `owner_partner_id` (`partners.id`), so a colleague at the same firm works on it too. A CHECK holds the pairing.
  - `created_by` is the person told when HQ approves the programme or sends it back.
  - The columns are the issue's:
    - name, logo key, summary, tester tasks and time needed;
    - the four dates and `places`;
    - `eligible_roles` and `questions`, both JSON;
    - `status` and `review_note`.
- **`testing_applications`:**
  - UNIQUE on (programme, user).
  - `consent_email_at` and `consent_version` are both set or both null.
  - A CHECK requires the version whenever the consent time is set.
- **`testing_events`:** append-only, enforced by triggers that refuse UPDATE and DELETE (as `ai_credit_ledger`'s).

**The lifecycle.**
- **A partner's programme:**
  - It goes draft, then `submitted` (the partner), then `approved` or `rejected` (HQ, with a note to send it back), then `open` (HQ), then `closed` (HQ).
  - The routes refuse any other order. `open` on a partner's programme takes `approved` only.
- **HQ's own programme:**
  - It skips the review and opens from draft.
  - Approve and reject refuse it.
- **Sent back:** the partner's next edit returns it to draft, to submit again.
  - The issue says PATCH is "draft only". I read a programme that was sent back as a draft returned with a note. Otherwise `rejected` would be a dead end.
- **`testing`** is in the CHECK for D814 and D815. `close` accepts it; nothing sets it yet.
- **Picking:**
  - The owner picks or declines while the programme is open, testing or closed, so picks can follow the close of applications.

**Applying.**
- A member applies only when all of these hold:
  - the programme is `open`;
  - now is between `opens_at` and `closes_at`;
  - the member's `role` is in `eligible_roles`;
  - a place is left (picked count below `places`).
- The member answers every question.
- `consent_email: true` records the time and the version of `CONSENT_TEXT` ("Axal may email me about this testing programme, including whether I was picked.").
  - The member list returns that sentence and version, so the page shows the words that are recorded.
- A second application is 409 `already_applied`. A withdrawn application does not reopen.
- **Withdrawing** takes an applied or picked application, and frees a pick's place.

**Places are held in SQL.**
- A pick is one `UPDATE … WHERE (picked count) < places`, so two owners picking at once cannot pass the limit.
- A failed pick on an application that could be picked answers 409 `programme_full`.

**Every statement is fixed SQL.** Create and edit bind all twelve columns (an edit merges its fields onto the stored row and checks the date order on the result), so `check-sqlite-columns` reads each statement and its skip count stays where `main` has it. The only interpolations left in a `prepare` are three module constants (`SELECT_PROGRAMME`, `SELECT_APPLICATION`, and the owner clause, which is one of two fixed strings).

**Events.**
- Each change is one D1 batch: the conditional write, then `INSERT INTO testing_events … WHERE changes() = 1`.
- A refused or lost write leaves no event.
- The actions: `created`, `edited`, `submitted`, `approved`, `rejected`, `opened`, `closed`, `applied`, `withdrawn`, `picked` and `declined`.

**The bell** (`notify`, in-app only, category `product_testing`):
- **to the member:** `testing_applied`, `testing_picked` and `testing_not_picked`;
- **to the partner who created the programme:** `testing_programme_approved` and `testing_programme_sent_back`, with HQ's note.
- Nothing here emails anyone. Mail sends "You're in" through its own approval.

**Who sees what.**
- **Partners:**
  - Every owner route scopes to the caller's firm.
  - Another firm's programme or application answers 404, the same body as a missing one.
- **HQ:**
  - HQ is `requireSuperAdmin`.
  - HQ's owner routes (create, edit, applicants, pick, decline) reach HQ's own programmes only.
  - Review, open and close reach any programme.
- **Owners' applicant reads:**
  - They carry name, role, answers, availability, consent and status.
  - **They carry no email address.** The only list with addresses is Mail's.

**The testers for Mail.**
- `testersAudience(env, uid)` returns the picked applications whose member consented, on active accounts.
- Each comes with `user_id`, `email`, `name`, `first_name` and `consent_version`.
- A member who withdraws leaves the list.
- Part 2 mounts it on D811's link as `testers:<uid>`.

**The Studio card** (D814) reads `GET /api/testing/summary`:
- `open_count`, the programmes the caller could apply to now;
- `picked`.

### 5. Part 2a: the two reads for Mail's Product testing page (#2258)

These are for AxalNetwork/mail's Product testing page. S1 split part 2 on 2026-10-20 so that this half does not wait on D811 (#2257). The two reads sit in `services/testing.ts`, beside `testersAudience`. **Nothing mounts them yet:** no route in this Worker reaches them, and a test fails if `index.ts` or `routes/testing.ts` names them.

- **`mailProgrammes(env)`** lists every programme, drafts included, because Mail's page reads them all. Each one carries:
  - its owner: Axal VC, or the partner firm (`company`, or else `name`);
  - its status and places, places left and picked count;
  - its applicant count, which leaves out withdrawn applications;
  - its four dates.

  It reuses HQ's `allProgrammes` read, so no new statement is added. It carries no address.
- **`mailApplicants(env, uid)`** gives the programme (owner, status, places) and its applicants, leaving out withdrawn ones as the owner's list does. Each applicant carries:
  - name and role;
  - `first_answer`, the first line of the first answer, at most 140 characters;
  - `applied_on` (the date) and status;
  - `consented` and `email`.
- **`email` is set only for a picked tester who consented, on an active account.** This is the rule `testersAudience` reads, and it is written into the query's `CASE`, so no other address leaves the database. For everyone else, `email` is null:
  - picked without consent;
  - applied but not picked;
  - declined;
  - an inactive account.

  A test asserts the addresses are exactly `testersAudience`'s. An unknown uid returns `null`, never another programme's list.
- **`testersAudience` stays the only list Mail sends to.** These reads show Mail who applied; they send nothing.
