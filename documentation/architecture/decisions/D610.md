## D610 — Co-founder matching: the other person's email stays hidden until both have signed the NDA

**A co-founder connection shows the other person's name at every status, but
shows their email only once both NDA signatures exist.** Slot S10, issue
#1310, 2026-10-07. Found by #1165's audit (area 8). No migration.

### What was wrong

`serializeConnectionFor` in `routes/cofounder.ts` returned `counterparty.name`
and `counterparty.email` at every status, including `pending_nda`. Every
connection read and write reply uses it: `GET /api/cofounder/connections`,
`GET /api/cofounder/connections/:uid`, and the sign and close responses. So a
founder who had expressed mutual interest received the other founder's email
before either had signed. The file's header claimed identity was exposed only
after both signed, which was not true.

### The rule (S1's decision on #1310)

- **The name stays visible before signing.** The NDA each side signs names
  the counterparty (`renderNda` fills `{counterparty_name}`). Hiding the name
  would hide one of the document's own parties, and the name is part of what
  each side agrees to.
- **The email shows only once both signatures exist,** `nda_signed_at_a` and
  `nda_signed_at_b`, whatever the status. The rule is keyed on the
  signatures, not on `status`:
  - a connection closed **after** both signed still shows the email, because
    both parties consented;
  - a connection closed **before** both signed keeps it hidden.

  Until both have signed, `counterparty.email` is `null` with
  `email_withheld: 'until_both_sign'`. That holds for both sides, including
  the one who has already signed.
- **An admin's read is unchanged:** the email at every status. The rule lives
  in `emailVisible(connection, viewer)`, which the serialiser calls, so every
  read and write reply follows it.

### The page

On the Connections tab, `ConnectionCard` in `CofounderPage.jsx` (now
exported) says why no email is shown when the reply carries `email_withheld`:
- pending: "Email: Shown once you have both signed the NDA";
- closed before both signed: "Email not shared: you did not both sign the
  NDA".

The other place that renders `counterparty.email` is the match picker in
`ProjectDetail.jsx`. It already falls back from the name, and the name is
always sent, so it needs no change.

### Guard

- `cloudflare-worker/test/cofounder_email_nda_d610.test.ts` runs the real
  router on the baseline tables:
  - no email for either side while neither or only one has signed, with the
    name kept;
  - the email once both have signed, including a connection closed after both
    signed;
  - a connection closed before both signed keeps it hidden;
  - an admin sees it at every status;
  - the list and the single read agree for every connection.
- `frontend/test/cofounder_email_nda_d610.test.mjs` renders `ConnectionCard`
  with `renderToStaticMarkup`:
  - a pending card shows the line and no email;
  - an active card shows the email and no line;
  - a card closed before both signed shows the "not shared" line.

Seven mutants were each caught:
- one signature enough;
- the email always shown;
- the admin exception dropped;
- the rule keyed on `status` instead of the signatures;
- `email_withheld` missing;
- the line never rendered;
- the closed copy collapsed into the pending one.

#1338, an outside PR for the same issue, was closed unmerged. This build takes
S1's two review points from it: the line is tested rendered, and the "closed
after both signed" case tells the signature rule apart from a status rule.
