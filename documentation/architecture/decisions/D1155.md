## D1155 — Deal page part 7: the founder's, the admin's and the Operating Partner's views, and who sees what (DL15–DL17)

- **Status:** built by S25 for #3060 (part 7 of #2421), 2026-10-26.
- **Canvas:** DL15, DL16, DL17a and DL17b of "Investor Deal Page" (`design/incoming/Investor Deal Page.dc.html`), landed by D1148.
- **Depends on:** part 4 (D1152) and part 5 (D1153), both merged.
- **Owner questions:** 1, 3, 7 and 9 take the recommended A (D1148 §5). Question 7 A is the one this part acts on: an Operating Partner keeps the studio-wide read, without notes or Mercury figures.
- **Migration:** none.

### 1. What changes on the Worker (`routes/deals.ts`)

**The commitments read, `GET /api/deals/:id/commitments`, follows DL15's rows 5 and 6.** It is still gated by `loadDealForRead`, which keeps a founder to their own deal.

| Reader | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| An investor | Their own rows, with their note | Unchanged |
| The deal's founder, an Admin, a Super Admin | Every row, with every investor's note | Every row by name, amount and status; **never a note** |
| An Operating Partner | The confirmed total only | Every row by name, amount and status; never a note (question 7 A) |
| Anyone else the gate admits | The confirmed total | Unchanged |

**The activity, `GET /api/deals/:id/activity`,** names investors for an Operating Partner too. Before, only an Admin and the founder were named (D1152); DL15's Activity row gives an Operating Partner the full list.

**A new read, `GET /api/deals/:id/transfer-gate`** (an Admin only), answers `{ held, open_conditions }`.
- **Why it exists:** a wire dialog can say, before the press, that an open IC condition holds wires.
- **The rule:** it is `openConditionsOnDeal`, the same gate `POST /:id/transfers` enforces.
- **A condition the Admin cannot see** (D467) holds the wire with `open_conditions: null`, so it gives no count.
- **A failed read** is a 503, `transfer_gate_unreadable`, never "nothing holds it".
- **The log** names the error, never its text.

**Unchanged:**
- **`POST /:id/transfers`** stays an Admin's act (D1215).
- **#3272**, open when this was built, touches other lines of `deals.ts`: none of these three routes.

### 2. The views (DL16, DL17a, DL17b)

**The page.** `DealRoomPage.jsx` draws `deal/DealCommitmentsStaff.jsx` on the Commitment tab for everyone but an investor. `deal/dealAudienceModel.js` `viewerOf` names who is reading:
- the deal's own founder;
- an Admin, or a Super Admin (`isSuperAdminUser`);
- an Operating Partner.

**The table** applies to all three:
- **The heading:** "Commitments · [n] in · [total]". A withdrawn commitment is not in. A line with no amount is not counted, and is named as not counted, never a zero.
- **The tools:** search by investor, a status filter, three sorts (amount, recorded, name) and pages of 25.
- **The status words** are the closing room's (D1213 §2): Committed, Received and Withdrawn.
- **No note is ever drawn,** whatever a reply carries.

**Each viewer:**

| Viewer | Board | What they get | Line under the table |
|---|---|---|---|
| The founder | DL16 | Match to my Mercury on a Committed or Received line (D752's `MercuryMatch`, unchanged). The tab keeps the closing room's door. | "Investors' notes are private to them. A withdrawn commitment does not count toward the round." |
| An Admin | DL17a | Record a wire on a Committed line, and D752's shared proof of funds on HQ, from its own audited read as before | "Admins see every commitment with names, never a note." |
| An Operating Partner | DL17b | No action; a "Read only" pill beside the header | "Investors' notes and Mercury figures are not shown to you. An Admin records wires; you tick the closing checklist." |

**Record a wire** is an in-page dialog. It:
1. reads the transfer gate first;
2. draws "[n] IC conditions are open on this deal. Wires are refused until they are met." with the press disabled, when a condition holds wires;
3. takes the amount (pre-filled from the commitment), a reference, the phone check ("Called back on the number on file") and an optional note;
4. posts `POST /:id/transfers` in whole cents.

A refusal stays in the dialog, in the transfers tab's own words (`transferRefusal`).

**An Operating Partner records no wire.**
- **The conflict:** DL17b draws "Record a wire" for them. D1215 (#2424 owner question 6) made recording an Admin's act, and the Worker refuses an Operating Partner with 403 `admin_records_transfers`.
- **What this part does:** it follows the Worker. Their Transfers tab no longer offers "Record a transfer" (`canRecord={isAdmin}`), which before opened a form the Worker refused.

**The closing room keeps its own rule** (#2424 owner question 3): an Operating Partner sees the total there, never the lines. The commitments read now gives them the rows, so `ClosingRoom.jsx` draws no lines for a partner.

**D752's proof door is back on the investor's own commitment.**
- **What it is:** an investor on HQ shares a proof of funds from their own line.
- **Where it was:** the door sat in the old commitments tab. Since D1153, investors no longer reached that tab, so the door had been unreachable.
- **Where it is now:** it sits in `DealCommitment` (`DealWorkTabs.jsx`).

**Who reads an investor's note.** The investor's Commitment tab now says: "Only you read this note. The founder and the studio see your amount and status, never the note." D1153's line, which said the founder and the studio read it, was true until this part.

### 3. Who sees what (DL15)

**The table.** `dealAudienceModel.js` `WHO_SEES` is DL15's table in the canvas's words: twelve kinds of information against seven viewers, each cell a word.
- **Each kind names the read that holds it.**
- **Each departure is listed:** every cell where today's Worker does not give what the canvas draws is named, with why. None is hidden.

| Kind · viewer | The canvas | Today | Why it is not changed here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your commitment · your firm's colleagues | Full | Not shared | The read is per person; sharing with the firm needs a firm scope on the read. |
| Data room files · Admin, Super Admin | Counts only | No count on the deal page | Staff's Documents tab lists titles only. |
| IC record and votes · Admin, Super Admin | Never | Every record | An Admin is unscoped in `icDecisionScope` (`ic.ts`). |
| Reference calls · Admin, Super Admin | Never | Admins keep Diligence and its calls | D1148 gives Admins the Diligence tab. |
| Mercury capacity · Admin, Super Admin | Never | A shared proof statement on HQ | The proof statement is the investor's consent (D752); the figures stay theirs. |
| Syndicate members · founder and staff | Count and total (founder); Full (staff) | The syndicates they lead or belong to | `GET /networkfx/syndicates`'s own scope (D1153 §5). |

**#2421's map, §2 item 12** ("Investors are refused Transfers"), is decided by DL15 and D1213: an investor never reads the recorded transfers. `documentation/audits/DEAL_PAGE_MAP_2026-10-25.md` gains a dated status note saying where each part landed. With part 6 (D1154, #3059) merged, every part has.
