### What the canvas decides

- New licence is six steps in L21's frame: Legal entity, Territory, Commercial terms, Administrators, Agreement, Issue. Each step has Back and "Continue to …". Territory stays as L21 draws it.
- Step 1 creates the Draft. Reference and kind are fixed after it. Later steps save to the Draft. Only Issue makes it Active.
- A white-label licence sets its brand kit colours on step 1, with the authenticator code.
- Commercial terms is two writes, seats then money terms. If the second fails, the page says "Seats were saved. The money terms were not."
- Administrators is optional (Skip for now). Appointing needs a reason of 10+ characters and the authenticator code.
- Agreement shows template states and the generated draft with its unfilled fields and where each is set. It does not send for signature.
- Issue review groups the values with Edit links, shows Not recorded for blanks, lists "What still blocks issuing" from the Worker's activation blockers, and asks no reason.
- Eadwyn proposes a renewal date, lists unfilled agreement fields and summarises the review. It fills and saves nothing.
