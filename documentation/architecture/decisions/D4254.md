### 1. The canvas wins over the prompt

The prompt in #4254 asked for six tabs and "HQ notes". The canvas the owner settled on differs in five ways, and the canvas is the decision:

1. **A tile row:** Decks · Script & rehearsal · Review · Share & analytics · Notes. The **Overview is not a tile**: it is `/raise/pitch` with no tile lit, and each of its status cards opens a tile. The row replaces the Studio's own tab bar.
2. **Notes** covers the team and Axal HQ. It is the existing `deck_notes` thread (D905), where founders post and HQ reads and answers. No new store.
3. **The deck bar.** While a deck is open (Decks › Open, and Review in Comment mode):
   - the page header, the Raise module pills and the tile row collapse into one bar: ‹ Pitch, the deck and its version, a section menu of the five tiles, Edit or Comment, Present, Share, Save version and Ask;
   - with the Eadwyn chat docked, the bar goes compact (‹, ⋯ for Present and Share);
   - the AIRail stays.
4. **The header's primary follows the tile:**
   - New deck on the Overview and Decks;
   - Rehearse on Script & rehearsal;
   - New link on Share & analytics;
   - only Ask on Review and Notes;
   - "Build your deck" while the venture has no deck.
5. **The deck screens are reused, not redrawn.** Decks is PD-1; Review is PD-33, PD-34 and PD-25; Share is PD-35 to PD-37; the editor is PD-0 to PD-16. Build those from the Pitch deck studio v2 frames.

### 5. Rules every part keeps

- **No invented figure:** an unreadable value is "Not recorded" (`null`), never 0.
- People appear by name, never by email.
- Ask proposes and the founder saves; placed text reads "Not saved".
- Costs are quoted before a run.
- The NDA is set per link, and the page says so.
- A connector that is not connected is one quiet line, never red text.
- Likely questions and their answers are never shown to an investor: no share, export or public read selects them.
