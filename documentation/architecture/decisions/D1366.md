### 1. Why nothing was on sale

Production had no row in `ai_token_packs`, and `STRIPE_PRICE_AI_TOPUP` was unset. So `packsOnSale` listed nothing. The code was right: a member is never offered a price that cannot be charged.

### 2. The packs

Migration 654 seeds four packs, on sale, on the Stripe account production uses. That is the sandbox, the same mode as `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`.

| $10 | adopted | the "AI top-up" product (D629), `price_1UNzpEF1hAXeCvFjjXynLkhe` |
| $25 | created | `price_1UVb4qF1hAXeCvFj0nAOI1vZ` |

- **How they were made.** The three created packs were made the way HQ › New pack makes one: one product per pack, `metadata.kind = token_pack`, and a one-time USD price.
- **Managing them.** They are ordinary rows, so HQ › Token packs edits, offers or archives them.
- **The catalog mirror.** `mirrorPackPrices` runs on each member read of `GET /api/ai-budget/top-up`. A pack price the mirror lacks is fetched into it, once per 10 minutes per price per isolate (`mirrorPriceOnce`, D750 decision 2). So the seeded packs go on sale without a Sync from Stripe. A fetch that fails leaves the pack `missing_from_catalog`, as before.

### 3. The custom amount

A member may top up any whole amount from **$5** to **$500**.

**The price.** One Stripe price serves every custom amount: `STRIPE_PRICE_AI_TOPUP_UNIT` names a one-time USD price of exactly $1. In production this is the "AI tokens, any amount ($1 each)" product (`metadata.kind = token_pack`), `price_1UVb4zF1hAXeCvFjIoEfL73w`. $N of tokens is a cart of one line: N of that price.

**The rules.** `AI_TOPUP_UNIT` (`services/aiTopups.ts`) holds them: $1 a unit, from $5 to $500.
- `tokenPackQuote` sends the unit price to `customAmountQuote`. That function refuses:
  - any N outside the range, or a fractional N: `token_amount_invalid`, "Choose a whole amount from $5 to $500.";
  - a price that is not a $1 one-time USD price in the mirror: `token_pack_not_on_sale`.
- A custom amount is credited at face value, with no bonus.
- It meets the same limits as a pack, through one helper (`accountLimits`): five orders a day, the $1,000 balance ceiling and a hold.

**Where it shows.** `GET /api/ai-budget/top-up` adds `custom`: `{ price_id, unit_amount: 100, currency, min_cents: 500, max_cents: 50000 }`, or null.
- The Add tokens sheet draws **Custom amount** beside the packs, with a dollar field and the range under it.
- Typing an amount chooses that row.
- Below $5, above $500, or not whole dollars, the row says so, and Pay stays off.

**How it is paid.** The order intent sends `{ price_id, quantity: N }`. The verified webhook credits it, exactly as it credits a pack.

**Recognised as a token pack.** The unit price and its product count as a token pack (`isTokenPackPrice`, `isTokenPackProduct`). So the unit price is never sold as an ordinary product, and never takes a promo code.
