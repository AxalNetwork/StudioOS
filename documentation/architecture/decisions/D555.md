## D555 — Admin Studio on HQ shows HQ's own figures: accounts, approvals and insights

**The overview carries the information.** Slot S08, issue #1155, 2026-10-05,
at the owner's request: "It doesn't seem admins get any overview data in
/studio. What is the point of having an overview if users need to click every
time on a page to have the information instead of having information in the
overview."

**What this reverses.** D443 and D447 had the HQ glance answer
`recorded: false` for `seats`, `approvals` and `insights`, on the ground that
these are a subsidiary's figures, and that counting HQ's own tables "would be
a different measurement wearing that name". The HQ-held pages
(`HeldApprovals`, `HeldAccounts`, `HeldCommunity`, `HeldPrograms`) said "none
counted here", and `HeldInsights` said no console fits.

D555 keeps D443's honesty about *whose* figures these are, and reverses its
choice to show nothing:
- **HQ's own figures are shown, labelled "HQ-held accounts · axal.vc".**
- **Per-subsidiary figures stay out.** Seat use, the share rate and the
  licence summary still read `recorded: false` (U1). So does the territory
  benchmark (D148): HQ pushes the median and keeps no copy.
- **The branch path is unchanged.**

**The Worker** (`services/studioGlance.ts`, the HQ path of
`GET /api/admin/studio/glance`; same gate, `requireAdmin`). Aggregates only:
no name, email or id is in the payload.
- **`accounts`:**
  - active accounts by role, and the total;
  - new in the last 7 and 30 days, measured from the glance's own `now`;
  - deactivated accounts, counted apart;
  - people still exploring, which is the Exploring lane's own count, so the
    card and the lane cannot disagree;
  - projects in Trash.

  A failed read is `available: false` with its reason.
- **`approvals`:** every lane `HeldApprovals.jsx` draws.
  - The eleven with a pending queue are counted by `laneCounts`
    (`approvalSources.ts`, D130), the same predicate each console lists its
    pending items with. They are shaped by `queuePressure`, now exported from
    `branchHome.ts` so the branch home and HQ share one function: pending,
    oldest age and SLA band, worst first.
  - A lane whose read fails is `count: null` with its reason, and the other
    lanes still answer.
  - `pending_total` is null when any lane is unreadable.
  - The four with no pending notion (`NOT_LANED`: Directory, Circles, Partner
    invitations, Advisor cohort access) carry their reasons.
  - Content to HQ is not applicable.
- **`insights`:**
  - sign-ups per week over the last 8 weeks, Monday-based, with the current
    week marked "to date";
  - AI spend this month, from `ai_usage_logs`, by the month predicate the
    caller's own meter uses;
  - open support tickets, through `ticketBacklog`, HQ Support's own read.

  Each figure answers alone. **Active accounts are not recorded:**
  `users.last_active` is written only by `logActivity`, whose earlier INSERT
  fails (D553), so no store records a sign-in per account. `api.hqAnalytics`
  counts active accounts from Analytics Engine behind Super Admin, and the
  glance does not borrow it.

**The frontend.**
- **`AdminStudioOverview`:**
  - Accounts shows the total, counts by role, new this week and in 30 days,
    exploring, and Trash.
  - Approvals shows the pending total, or why there is none, then the lanes
    with something pending, worst first, each with its oldest age and a link
    to its console.
  - Insights shows the headline figures, plus active accounts and the
    benchmark as Not recorded with their reasons.
- **`StudioNeedsDecision`:** on HQ the Seats tile is Accounts, and Approvals
  shows the total and the worst lane.
- **`HeldApprovals`:** the "State here" column shows pending and oldest age,
  "0 pending", Unreadable, or "No queue" with its reason.
- **`HeldAccounts`, `HeldCommunity` and `HeldPrograms`:** each card shows its
  number, or Not recorded with the reason where the glance carries none.
- **`HeldInsights`:** the figures replace S20's sentence.
- All of them read the glance through `heldFigures.jsx` (`useHeldGlance`,
  `Figure`, `laneOf`).
- **States:** reading, ready, an unreadable lane or figure, and a measured 0
  kept apart from Not recorded.
- `HeldZone.jsx` is untouched (#1156).
