## D1007 — Persona segments part 10: the phone layout (SEG14a, SEG14b)

- **Status:** filed as #2822, part 10 of Persona segments (#2378, D997 §6). Built by S15, 2026-10-27. Not deployed or live verified.
- **Basis:** `main` at `4e10fe7b`.
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Persona Segments Page.dc.html`, boards SEG14a (Overview) and SEG14b (Move).
- **Builds on:** D997's page and header (D936's `AdminPageHeader`), D1004's Overview, D1005's shared Move dialog, D1006's Unclassified.
- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** no change.

### 1. What a phone is

Below `sm` (639px), the width at which the admin page header turns its crumb into "‹ Section". `segModel.js` names it `PHONE_QUERY`. The Lightbox's full-screen sheet applies at the same width (`max-sm:`).

### 2. Overview (SEG14a)

- **The header** reads "‹ Accounts" (or "‹ Content" in HQ's shell). Its actions stand one under the other at full width, as D936 draws every admin header.
- **The tiles** are two by two.
- **Every chart** is full width, with "View as table". The weekly chart already had it. Segments by profile, How segments were set, and How sure the sign-up suggestion was now have it too, at every width, as the canvas draws them. The table shows the same labels and counts as the bars, and nothing more.
- **The cards** are one a row.
- **Needs and offers** is one line a segment, in the table's order (gaps first):
  - "Founder — New Venture · Needs met 1 of 2 · 2 gaps", with a gap pill;
  - "Needs: Not recorded" where HQ has not written needs;
  - "N kinds could not be read" where an offer kind's store failed. It is never counted as no gap.

  Each line opens its segment's page, where the kinds are. Above a phone the table is unchanged.
- **A failed block** still says "Could not be read" with its own Try again. The lines never stand in for an unread store.

### 3. The Move dialog (SEG14b)

- **On a phone,** it is a full-screen sheet (`Lightbox phone="full"`), no longer D1014's bottom sheet.
  - Its own bar says where Back goes: "‹ Members" from a segment's Members tab, "‹ Unclassified" from Place in a segment…. That bar replaces the shell's 36px close.
  - The body scrolls under the bar.
  - Cancel and Move stay at the foot, side by side (1 to 1.4) and 44px tall.
- **Above a phone,** the titled header, the close and the right-aligned buttons are unchanged.
- **The rules hold at both widths:** nothing is written before Move, and a refusal stays in the dialog.

### 4. Tap targets

On a phone, every button, link, select and field on Overview and in the Move dialog is at least 44 by 44 px:
- the chips, the scope select, the search, Refresh and the shared button;
- `TAP` for a text button or link: View as table, Open Unclassified, Try again, a card's name and a needs line;
- the dialog's select, Back, Cancel, Move and Try again.

Above a phone each keeps its own size.

### 5. Departures

- **The bar charts' "View as table"** shows at every width, not only on a phone, because the canvas's desktop boards draw it too.
- **A needs line is a link** to its segment. The canvas's line is plain text, but on a phone the line is the only way to reach that segment's kinds.
