## D5039 — HQ › Revenue v2: an executive cockpit where every figure says its period and its coverage (#5039)

- **Status:** canvas landed 2026-11-08 (`design/incoming/Revenue v2 Page.dc.html`, `design/incoming/Revenue v2 · Boards.dc.html`, boards RV01 to RV15), drawn from the browser-grounded Revenue V2 prompt on #5039. Part 1 (the read-only cockpit) built 2026-11-08; part 2 (governed receivable actions and the scoped licence picker) built 2026-11-09; see below.
- **Builds on:** `admin_revenue.ts` (`GET /summary`), `admin/statements.ts` (draft → issued → paid / disputed; drawing held by the owner’s policy, D514), `admin_billing.ts` (refund is a Super Admin write behind TOTP step-up), `admin_promos.ts`, `frontend/src/pages/hq/RevenuePage.jsx`.

### What the canvas decides

- **Controls:** Period, Scope and Stream, the read time, and a coverage button (“4 of 9 sources complete”) that opens the Data coverage drawer, where every “why” on the page is explained once.
- **Temporal labels on every figure:** the selected period, Live, Last 30 days, As of now. **Currencies are never summed;** EUR and GBP rows stay apart.
- **Licence fees today are a run-rate** (a quarter of each active annual fee). The design labels it “Contracted run-rate” and never calls it collected.
- **Eight value states:** Recorded, Zero, Incomplete, Not recorded, Not reported, Stale, Unavailable, Loading, each with a word and a shape. A partial read shows `€128,400+` with the gap named; nothing falls back to €0.
- **Order:** Needs attention, streams, trend (missing months drawn as gaps; the table says No data), AI economics (margin first), receivables (overdue first, statement drawer, Record a payment with before → after), subsidiaries (reporting health first), billing exceptions, promotions (ceiling, push, enforcement, issued, remaining).
- **Refund** is five steps: Lookup, Review, Confirm, Step-up, Result.
- Tablet at 834 and phone at 390; Eadwyn open on one board.

### Backend required

- A `?period=` parameter on `/summary` (it computes the current quarter today).
- Invoice-backed licence revenue and a reconciled revenue ledger for Invoiced, Collected, Outstanding, Overdue and the monthly trend (#5027, #5028, #5029).
- A read-only payment lookup for the refund Lookup and Review steps (#5037).
- A read-back of branch promotion enforcement (today only “push accepted” is known).
- A scoped, lazily loaded licence picker instead of fetching the whole ledger.

Drawing new statements stays held by D514; the boards show it as policy, not as work.

### Built, part 1 (2026-11-08): the read-only cockpit

Part of #5039, slot S25. `/admin/revenue` is now the canvas's cockpit, read-only. Every governed write is the next slice.

**Worker.** `GET /api/admin/revenue/summary` takes `?period=YYYY-Qn` (`SUMMARY_PERIOD_RE`: a year from 2000 to 2999 and a quarter 1 to 4). Any other spelling, including an empty `?period=`, gets 400 `bad_period` and no figures. Without the parameter the reply is today's quarter, as before. The token cost and margin and the branch usage-report coverage follow the period. The licence run-rate (licences active now) and the promotion counts (what checkout would honour now) do not. The reply says which is which in `temporal` (`period`, `as_of_now`, `not_recorded` per block), and adds `quarter.key`, `current_period`, `period_requested` and `read_at`. Still Super Admin only. No migration. `api.hqRevenue(period)` calls the same mounted route.

**Frontend.** `RevenuePage.jsx` is the host. The parts are in `frontend/src/pages/hq/revenue/` (one file per part, as `pages/admin/licences/` is split), and their rules are pure in `revenueModel.js`. Built:
- **Header:** Period (the current quarter and the seven before it), Scope (All subsidiaries or one licence), the read time, and "N of M sources complete", which opens the Data coverage drawer.
- **Data coverage drawer:** ten sources, each with its state and one reason. Every "Why?" on the page opens it at its source, and no reason is repeated on the page.
- **Financial position:** Invoiced, Collected, Outstanding and Overdue are drawn once, as Not recorded, with their temporal labels. Then each native currency gets its own row: contracted run-rate (As of now), refunds (Last 30 days) and open disputes (Live, with the amount at risk). Then subscriptions (Not recorded) and the AI margin (the period, USD).
- **Needs attention** shows only what a read shows: dispute evidence past its deadline or due within three days, a statement with a dispute recorded, a current-quarter ceiling the branch has not received, an active licence with no fee, and a current-quarter report that is missing or stale. With nothing to show, the section is not drawn.
- **Streams:** Coverage replaces Read. The licence row is "Contracted run-rate", and its invoiced and collected cells are Not recorded. The period's revenue-share statements are shown as owed (claim) and paid (recorded by HQ).
- **Trend:** an honest no-series state. Nothing is charted.
- **AI economics:** the margin comes first, then its breakdown.
- **Receivables:** built from the drawn statements, with the statement drawer. Drawing is shown as a D514 policy state with no action.
- **Subsidiaries:** reporting health first.
- **Billing exceptions:** open disputes and refunds HQ issued, each with its own loading, unreadable and empty state.
- **Promotions:** badge columns, plus "No ceiling configured · Discounts currently allowed". Remaining is shown only beside a reported Issued figure.
- **States and labels:** eight value states (word and shape, using Security v2's glyphs) and a temporal label on every figure.
- **Layouts:** responsive grids for tablet and phone, and receivables as cards below 1024px.

**Departures, each on purpose:**
- **The four ledger figures are drawn once, not per currency.** No ledger backs any of them, so repeating Not recorded under every currency would say the same nothing several times.
- **No Overdue status, no Overdue chip, and Due is Not recorded.** No statement records a due date. Receivables are statements only: licence fee invoices are not joined in until the invoice-backed aggregation exists.
- **No Stream control.** The STATUS scope named Period and Scope only.
- **Scope narrows only what names a licence:** receivables, subsidiaries, promotions and Needs attention. The financial position, streams, AI economics and billing exceptions stay platform-wide, and the page says so (U1).
- **No total row in streams.** The only per-currency figures are a run-rate and a claim, which are different kinds and cannot be added.
- **The trend has no chart and no Measure, Interval or View toggles.** No series exists to draw.
- **The statement drawer's history is the row's own timestamps** (drawn, last changed, paid). Who did each is not recorded, and the drawer says so.
- **Today's writes are kept unchanged.** `StatementActions` (D453, D458, D514) is the drawer's footer. The D228 ceiling editor is under "Set a ceiling…". The refund form (D224, the same step-up and reason rule) is under "Refund a payment… · step-up". Their type was raised to 12px; nothing else changed. The five-step refund waits on #5037.
- **Billing exceptions has no incomplete-subscription row.** Its only source is a reconciliation POST. A dispute row shows its payment id, because the dispute carries no customer.
- **No Retry sync on promotions** (it is a write). "Enforced" is read from an accepted push.
- **A report is stale after 7 days, on the current quarter only.** That threshold is this page's choice: branches report every morning (D266).
- **The Eadwyn rail is unchanged** apart from reading its coverage lines from the coverage sources. The finance prompts are later work.
- **The licence ledger is still read whole,** and only for the ceiling editor's picker, as before. The scoped picker is later work.

### Built, part 2 (2026-11-09): governed receivable actions and the scoped licence picker

Part of #5039, slot S25. The statement drawer's Record a payment, Mark issued and Void are governed actions now, and the ceiling editor picks its licence from a scoped, lazily read picker instead of the whole ledger. The refund (#5037) and invoice-backed licence fees (#5036) are S17's, in #5132, and are not touched here: `admin_billing.ts`, `admin_revenue.ts` and the refund area are unchanged.

**Worker.** Three routes in `routes/admin_statements.ts`, over the pure rules in `services/statementActions.ts`:
- `POST /api/admin/statements/:uid/issue` — draft to issued. Super Admin. A floor (a stream unreported or estimated) is refused, as D514 refused it.
- `POST /api/admin/statements/:uid/payments` — adds `amount_cents` (and an optional bank `reference`) to what was paid. Partial payments are supported: the statement stays issued while anything is outstanding and becomes paid when paid reaches owed. Paid and disputed together never exceed owed, and a floor is never paid in full. Super Admin behind the write bar (`requireSuperAdminWriteBar`: a TOTP session and a recent step-up), the gate the refund uses (D224).
- `POST /api/admin/statements/:uid/void` — draft or issued to void, only with nothing paid against it. The same write bar.

Every one of them requires a reason of 10 characters or more, `expected_revision` and an `idempotency_key`. The order is: gate, 404, request shape, replay, rules, revision, then one batch. A key already used by the same admin returns the stored reply with `replayed: true`, before any status or revision check, so a retry after a lost answer never pays twice; the same key with a different request is 409 `idempotency_key_reused`. A revision other than the stored one is 409 `changed_since_open` with the row as it stands (the licence-terms CAS shape). An action from a status it cannot start from is 409 `invalid_status`. The statement update is guarded by the revision and stamps `last_action_uid`; the action row is inserted only where that stamp is, in the same D1 batch, so both land or neither does. Then `logAdminAction` writes the audit row (`subsidiary_statement_issued`, `_payment_recorded`, `_void`) with the reason, the action uid, both statuses and revisions, and the before and after paid figures. The reply carries `outcome: 'applied'`, `settled`, `before`, `after` and a `receipt`.

Migration `202611090909_statement_actions.sql`: `subsidiary_statements.revision` (bumped by every write: a draw, the dispute PATCH and each action) and `last_action_uid`, and the table `subsidiary_statement_actions` (one row per applied action, unique on `(actor_user_id, idempotency_key)`, holding the reply it replays). A refused action writes no row.

`PATCH /api/admin/statements/:uid` keeps only the dispute (D514). A body carrying `paid_cents` or `status` is 409 `governed_action_required`, naming the three routes, and nothing in it is written; left open, the same changes would have been one PATCH away from the reason, the revision, the key and the step-up. The payment and void routes are on `COOL_OFF_ROUTES` in `index.ts` (D248's exact-route way), so the ledger and the dispute stay open to a freshly recovered admin while money does not.

`GET /api/admin/licence-picker?q=&cursor=&limit=` (Super Admin) answers `{ items: [{ uid, name, territory }], next_cursor, limit }`: name is the brand name, else the reference; territory is the licence's country codes. Ordered by name, a keyset cursor, 20 a page and at most 50, searched by name, reference, legal entity or an exact country code, with `%`, `_` and `\` taken literally. Nothing else from the ledger is in it.

**Frontend.** `frontend/src/lib/api/adminStatementActions.js` is the client (D527; the generated index re-exports it, so `api.js` is unchanged). In `pages/hq/revenue/`:
- `statementActionModel.js`: what each statement offers and why not (`actionAvailability`), the payment's before → after in the statement's own currency through D5029's money layer (`paymentPreview`), Mark issued's and Void's rows (`statusPreview`), the body (reason, `expected_revision`, a key from `newIdempotencyKey`), the outcome (`classifyOutcome`) and the sender (`createActionRunner`).
- `StatementActionFlow.jsx`: the flow inside the drawer. Record a payment is Prepare → Review (before → after) → Confirm → Authenticate → Receipt; Mark issued is Prepare → Review → Confirm → Receipt, since it moves no money and takes no step-up; Void is Scope → Review → Authenticate → Result. Authenticate is the request in flight: the Worker's `step_up_required` opens the app's step-up dialog through `request`, which retries once.
- `StatementActions.jsx` is the drawer footer: Record payment…, Mark issued… and Void… where the statement offers them, the reason beside one it does not, and Record dispute… (the PATCH, unchanged in substance).
- `Receivables.jsx`: the drawer swaps its body for the flow. Escape closes the flow first, and neither Escape nor × does anything while a request is out.
- `Promotions.jsx`: `LicencePicker` (search, a page, Show more) and the ceiling editor using it. The editor mounts only when "Set a ceiling…" is opened, so the picker is read then and not with the page. `RevenuePage.jsx` no longer reads the ledger (`licenceLedgerApi.all()` is gone), and passes the action handler and a refresh that keeps the drawer open.

**The outcomes.** Six, never folded: settled, partial (paid, something still outstanding), applied (issue or void), refused (nothing written), stale (changed since opened; nothing written; prepare again) and unknown. Unknown is a missing answer, a timeout, a 5xx, or a 200 that does not say `applied`: it is drawn as "Outcome unknown" with Check again, which resends the same key, and it shows no figures, because an unknown is not a zero. A second send while one is out sends nothing (`createActionRunner`). There is no `window.confirm` anywhere in the flow.

**Departures, each on purpose:**
- **Mark issued takes no step-up.** It moves no money; it is Super Admin, a reason, the revision and a key. Record a payment and Void take the step-up.
- **Void needs nothing paid.** A void with a payment against it would leave a receipt with no claim; the canvas does not say, and the drawer says to record a dispute instead.
- **The payment is added, not set.** The old "Set total paid" replaced the running total; a governed payment is one receipt, so a second wire is a second payment.
- **Review and Confirm are two steps** where RV05b draws one dialog, because the task names them apart. The Confirm button says the amount ("Record €3,800.00 payment"), as the canvas's does.
- **Record dispute stays the PATCH,** with no revision or key. It is not one of this slice's three actions, and it moves no money. It now bumps the revision, so a governed action prepared before a dispute is refused as stale.
- **No due date and no Overdue,** as in part 1: the statement records none.
- **The drawer's history is still the row's timestamps.** Who did each action and why is in the action record and the audit log; a read of the action history for the drawer is later work.
- **The picker carries no currency** (the task's "id, name and territory only"), so the ceiling editor no longer prefills it: the currency is typed, and the Worker refuses one that is not a listed ISO 4217 code. The select became a radio list with a search box and Show more.
- **The drawer stays open after a write.** The re-read after a statement write (governed or dispute) no longer passes through Loading, which used to unmount the drawer.
