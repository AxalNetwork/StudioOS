### 3. What is drawn today

- **The list:** the canvas's title "Due diligence" and its line; no shield icon. Then Cases · My reviews · Templates, each a link on the caller's base.
- **The case:** today's header, then the nine tabs.
  - **Overview** is today's whole case page: band, counts, checklist progress, heat map, reviewers, sections, sources and requests.
  - **Every other tab** says where its blocks are on Overview today, links back, and shows what waits (`ddModel.ON_OVERVIEW`, `WAITS`). Sign-off also says the decision waits.
  - The dialogs and the audit drawer open from every tab.
- **The band.** A case with no band reads "Not scored yet", never a colour and never "Not recorded". `computeScore` already returns a null band until something is reviewed.
- **Departures, each a part's:** the tiles, charts, stage strip and paging; the open dialog's subject picker; each tab's own body; the dialogs A–H; the reviewer view; the phone layout.
