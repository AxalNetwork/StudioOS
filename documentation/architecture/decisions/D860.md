### 1. What the WhatsApp Business Calling API allows

**How this was read.** Agent sessions cannot open `developers.facebook.com`: the environment's network policy refuses the host. The same was true for D754. Every Meta fact below is therefore taken from search summaries of Meta's pages and of Meta partners' copies of them (360dialog, Chatarchitect, Plivo, Pipecat, Positus, Twilio and others). Each is marked **(unverified)**. The facts about Cloudflare were read in Cloudflare's own documentation and are marked **(Cloudflare docs)**. Before the live build, S1 or the owner must confirm each unverified line on Meta's pages (§6, question 2).

| Topic | What the sources say | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **User-initiated calls** | The WhatsApp user taps call. The business gets a `calls` webhook with `event: "connect"` and the caller's **SDP offer** (`session: { sdp_type: "offer", sdp }`). The business answers with its own SDP through `POST /{phone-number-id}/calls`, using `action: "pre_accept"` and then `"accept"`, or `"reject"`, or later `"terminate"`. Media should start only after the accept's 200. Unanswered after about 30–60 seconds, the call ends as not answered. | unverified; consistent across Meta's page summary, 360dialog and Pipecat |
| **Business-initiated calls** | The business POSTs an SDP offer with `action: "connect"`, and the user's answer arrives in a `connect` webhook. This needs the user's **call permission**: a permission-request message, at most 1 per 24 hours and 2 per 7 days, granted for 7 days; it is revoked after 4 missed calls in a row. Error 138006 means no permission. The per-day call cap is reported as 5 per user by 360dialog and Plivo, and as 100 connected calls per number by one copy of Meta's page. | unverified; the cap conflicts |
| **Webhook events** | Field `calls` under the WABA object. In `value.calls[]`: `connect` (with the SDP) and `terminate` (with `status` COMPLETED or FAILED, `duration` in seconds, and start and end times). In `value.statuses[]` with `type: "call"`: RINGING, ACCEPTED, REJECTED. | unverified; consistent across Positus, 360dialog and Pipecat |
| **Media** | **Media is reachable.** The business is a WebRTC peer and terminates the call's audio itself: Opus, ICE (Meta's side is ice-lite in Meta's Messenger appendix), DTLS-SRTP and BUNDLE. Pipecat ships a WhatsApp transport that answers calls with a bot, which shows that the audio reaches the business's endpoint. | unverified for WhatsApp; Messenger's appendix shows the same SDP shape |
| **Recording** | The sources conflict. The newest (MonoChat, 2026-10-16; a GoHighLevel request from July 2026) describe a native, per-call opt-in for recording and transcription. A spoken announcement with the business's stated purpose (up to 250 characters) is played to both parties, and a call with recording on but no purpose is rejected. Audio or a transcript is delivered by webhook after the call and kept 7 days. Older sources (Infobip, Respond.io, Chakra) say there is no native recording. D754 §3 met the same feature, in summaries of a Meta page dated 2026-07-09. | unverified; likely a 2026 addition |
| **Coexistence** | **Calls on a coexistence number never reach the API.** Meta's coexistence table, as quoted by several vendors, lists voice and video calls as "Not supported" for the Cloud API and "No change" for the app. Calling requires a number in use with the Cloud API, *not* the WhatsApp Business app. | unverified; consistent across every source, and D754 §3 found the same |
| **Eligibility** | A messaging limit of at least 2,000 a day (already encoded: `whatsapp.ts` `CALLING_MIN_LIMIT`). Business-initiated calls are not offered for numbers in Nigeria, Egypt, Vietnam, the US or Canada (`RESTRICTED_CALLING_CODES`). | as encoded on `main` in D754 §11 |
| **Pricing** | Billed per minute. User-initiated calls are reported free (D754 §7). Business-initiated calls cost a per-country connectivity fee: for example $0.0114 a minute outbound for Australia or Japan in Twilio's table, plus a BSP's own fee. | unverified |
| **Cloudflare Realtime SFU** | Accepts a remote endpoint's SDP offer through `POST /apps/{appId}/sessions/{id}/tracks/new` (with `location: "local"` and the offer's audio `mid`) and returns an answer. It supports Opus and G.711. The **WebSocket adapter** (GA 2026-09-30) streams a publication to a WebSocket endpoint such as a Durable Object, as Opus decoded to 48 kHz stereo PCM. It reconnects for up to 15 seconds and keeps a short, bounded backlog. Cloudflare's own AI-audio example transcribes this way with Workers AI. | Cloudflare docs |

### 2. The verdict

**Not possible today, on the numbers Axal onboards.** Every member number connects in coexistence mode (D754 §3, `whatsappSignup.js`), and coexistence calls stay in the WhatsApp Business app. No call from a member's customer can reach Axal while that holds. Nothing in this study changes D754 §3's reasons 1 and 2.

**Possible on an API-only number, with all-party consent.** D754 §3 reason 3 ("a Worker cannot carry WebRTC media") no longer forces the browser to answer: Cloudflare's SFU can now terminate the call and hand its audio to a Durable Object. One path is technically sound and keeps D754's rules. It needs the owner's answers in §6 and a live check.

### 3. The path, if the owner opens it

Each step names the code it reuses.

1. **The number.** An API-only number with calling switched on and a limit of 2,000 or more. Either the member moves their number off the Business app (registered with a PIN that is never stored), or the member uses a second number (§6, question 1).
2. **Consent before the ring is answered.** The call is from a contact whose chat already exists (`threads.ts`).
   - `decideCall` declines unless that chat's consent is `given` under a notice version that names recorded calls (`CALL_NOTICE_VERSIONS`, empty today, so every ring is declined).
   - A new notice version (`consentNotice.ts`) adds the call. The owner approves its words; the notice page `/privacy/interviews?n=<version>` gains the paragraph.
   - **The default rule is all-party** (`RECORDING_RULE`). Every party is told whatever the jurisdiction's one-party or all-party rule says, so a single rule holds in every country.
3. **A spoken notice at the start,** on either media path:
   - Meta's native recording announcement, if Meta's page confirms it (§1), with the purpose sentence;
   - otherwise a short pre-recorded notice, played into the call through an SFU WebSocket ingest adapter before any audio is kept.
   Audio from before the notice ends is discarded, never stored.
4. **The media, two options; recommended first:**
   - **a. Meta's native recording (if confirmed).** No WebRTC on Axal's side for capture. After the call, the recording's media id arrives by webhook. It is fetched through the existing `fetchWhatsAppMedia` and `boundedDownload` path, admitted by `channelAudio.ts`, and stored as recorder chunks of a `phone` session (`ingestChannelAudio`). It is then transcribed by `transcribeVoiceNote` (`whisper-large-v3-turbo`, or `@cf/deepgram/nova-3`) through `aiRouter` and the AI token ledger. The founder still has to talk to the caller, and on an API-only number the Business app cannot answer. So the founder's Axal tab answers: option b's answer step, without the SFU egress.
   - **b. Founder's tab plus Realtime SFU.** The webhook's offer goes to an SFU session (`tracks/new`, the offer's audio `mid`), and the SFU's answer goes back in `pre_accept` and `accept`. The founder's browser subscribes to the caller's track and publishes their microphone. A WebSocket egress adapter for each side streams 48 kHz PCM to a Durable Object, which writes recorder-format chunks and relays live text as `liveTranscription.ts` does. The session is then structured by `sessionAutoStructure.ts`, and its suggestions follow #2431 / D777: Eadwyn proposes, the founder accepts.
     **The open technical risk:** to carry the founder's voice to the caller, the SFU must send audio on WhatsApp's own PeerConnection. That needs a renegotiation (an SFU offer to WhatsApp) mid-call, and no source says whether the Calling API accepts one. **A live spike must prove this before anything else is built.** If it fails, the founder's tab answers WhatsApp directly (D754 §3's design), and the recorder captures both sides in the browser as its "WhatsApp video" chip already does for tab audio.
5. **The ring.** A pending call must reach an open tab within about 30–60 seconds. That needs a push (a Durable Object WebSocket, as option b needs anyway) or the call is declined. Rings to a member with no Axal tab open are declined with a chat message offering a time.
6. **Translation** (`@cf/meta/m2m100-1.2b`, in the catalogue and unused) is out of scope here. It is a separate step on the stored transcript, metered like any model call.

**If the owner keeps calls closed,** the way stays as D754 §3 says:
- the in-app recorder's "WhatsApp call" and "WhatsApp video" chips, under the recorder's consent gate;
- way B: a call recorded on the phone, where the law allows, and forwarded to Axal's number (`whatsappCapture.ts`). It becomes a draft interview after the member attests consent.

### 4. Groundwork built here (off)

- **`services/channels/whatsappCalls.ts`:**
  - `whatsappCallsEnabled(env)`: on only when `WHATSAPP_CALLS` is exactly `"true"`.
  - `normaliseCallEvents(body)`: reads a `calls` delivery's connect, terminate and call-status events. The SDP is never copied: an event says only `hasSdp`. The other party's id is kept only to find the chat's consent, and is never logged. Unknown shapes are skipped.
  - `decideCall(env, event, consent)`: with the flag off, every event is ignored. With it on, a ring is declined in this order:
    - no offer;
    - no consent given;
    - consent under a notice that does not name calls;
    - and, until the media path exists, every remaining case (`not_built`).
    No path answers a call.
  - `RECORDING_RULE = 'all_party'` and `CALL_NOTICE_VERSIONS` (empty).
- **`WHATSAPP_CALLS = "false"`** in `wrangler.toml`'s top-level, production and preview vars, and in `Env`.
- **What is deliberately not built:**
  - **Webhook wiring.** The webhook does not subscribe `calls` (GOTCHAS), and recording a call event needs `'call'` in `channel_events.kind`, a migration this task does not have.
  - **Any Graph call.** No `pre_accept`, `accept`, `reject` or `terminate`.
  - **Any SFU binding.**
  The webhook's signature check is unchanged: a `calls` change arrives in the same signed POST, and `normaliseWhatsAppPayload` ignores it, so with the flag off a call moves nothing.
- **The connector card** keeps its line: "Calls: not yet. WhatsApp calls do not reach Axal" (`connectorPages.ts:637`). It stays true.

### 5. Verified

`cloudflare-worker/test/whatsapp_calls_d860.test.ts` (7 tests, stubs only, no Meta call, invented ids):
- **Signature.** A `calls` delivery signed with the app secret verifies. A tampered body, a missing header and an unset secret do not.
- **Isolation.** The message pipeline reads nothing from it.
- **Normalising.** Connect, terminate and call-status events are read, and no SDP is copied. A business-initiated call's other party is `to`. Unknown objects, fields, events and shapes are skipped, and a session with no SDP is no offer.
- **The flag.** It is off unless exactly `"true"`, all three wrangler blocks say `"false"`, and with it off every ring is ignored.
- **Consent first.** Not a ring, no offer, each non-given consent state, a notice that does not name calls, and the `not_built` end.
- **The defaults.**
- **Mutations.** 17 mutations of the module, each caught.
