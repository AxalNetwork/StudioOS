### 1. One header over every host page (MEV12a)

`/events/:id/edit` and each `/events/:id/manage*` tab draw `HostHeader`, top to bottom:

- **The crumb** "‹ My events · Hosting", to `/my/events/hosting`.
- **The title**, then the status pill (Draft, Waiting for review, Published, Sent back, Cancelled, in My events' words) and the visibility pill (Public, Unlisted, Private), each with the canvas's mark.
- **One primary action that fits the status:**

  | Status | On Details | On the manage tabs |
  |---|---|---|
  | Draft, sent back | the editor's own action, D1031's "Send for review" or "Publish" | "Edit details", or "Edit and resend" when sent back, to Details |
  | Waiting for review | "Waiting for an admin", no button | the same |
  | Published, on its day | "Check in", to Check-in | "Check in", except on Check-in itself |
  | Published, another day | "Open event page" | "Open event page" |
  | Cancelled | nothing | nothing |

  On Details the editor passes its `publishAction` label to the header, so the two never disagree: the header offers that action only when the editor does.
- **The time line,** in the event's own zone: "Today · 17:00–20:00 Berlin time · In person · Venue, Berlin", or the date ("Wed 21 Oct · …") on another day.
- **The publish gate,** the canvas's four lines: published public, waiting for review (amber), published private, published unlisted. A draft, sent-back or cancelled event has none.
- **The tabs,** each a literal link so the reachability walk finds every door: Details, Who may register, Registrations, Agenda, and Check-in on the event's day only.

**Registrations' count** is the people waiting for the host's decision: the manage page's Pending tab (`registrationTab`). The manage page counts it from its roster. The editor reads the roster for the header alone, and a failed read shows no number, never a 0.

**The event's day** is the event's own zone's: every calendar day from its start through its end. 00:30 in Berlin is the next day there, whatever UTC says. An event with no start is never on its day.

**`WorkspaceShell` is gone from the manage page.** Its own title and crumb would have stood above the shared header. The editor never had it. The shell's rail note stays, as a line under the header ("Save audience rules before checking the member preview…").

### 2. Check-in at `/events/:id/manage/check-in`

- **The route** sits under the manage pages' guard (admin, founder, partner, investor, advisor). The Worker's `canManage` still decides who may scan.
- **On the event's day** the page opens today's `CheckinScanner` ("Open the scanner"). Each scan says who was let in, or "Already checked in", and the roster reloads, so Registrations' count follows.
- **On another day** it says "Check-in opens on the event's day" with the time line, and offers no scanner. That page is reached only by its address, since no tab links it that day.
- **Registrations' "Check in" button** stays as it was: it opens the same scanner over that tab.

### 3. A new event (MEV12b)

- **`/events/new` draws `NewEventHeader`:** "‹ My events", "New event", Save draft, the visibility's own action ("Send for review" when public, "Publish" otherwise), and the line "Public events go to an admin for review. Unlisted and private events publish at once." It has no tabs.
- **The action** opens D1031's dialog first. Its confirm creates the draft, submits it, and lands on its Details tab.
- **After any save** a new event lands on its Details tab (`/events/:id/edit`), not on the manage page.

### 4. MEV13: an admin's line on Hosting

- **The line** reads "You also review members' public events. [n] waiting." with the review desk's link.
- **The count** comes from `GET /api/admin/events?status=pending_review` (`requireAdmin`): the reply's `total`, read with `limit: 1`. A total of 0 reads "None waiting."
- **A failed read, or a reply with no total,** reads "The waiting count could not be read.", never 0.
- **A host who is not an admin** is never asked.
