## D5890 — Investor cohort access in the generic Programs shell (#5890)

- **Status** Built as a frontend-only part of #4873, following D5313 and PG03a.
- **Addresses** Overview and `/activity` keep their routes and test IDs. Playbook, Availability & grants, Participation, and Settings use literal admin routes onto the same page. The History label points to `/activity`.
- **Registry** The shell reads modules, grants, and member access from the program detail. Registry failure marks those sections and the Playbook. The cohort summary, NDA, investors table, and History use their own reads.
- **Participation** The existing summary supplies verified investors with access and cohorts with an open grant. Viewing cohort materials is not recorded in the summary.
- **Departure from PG03a** The two capability modules, Cohorts and NDA signature, both map to Overview. They have no tabs. The existing cohort and NDA screens and grant actions remain on the built-in page.
