### 1. The founder at 390 (RPT20)

"At 390" means below 1024px, where `CycleRoute` passes `phone` and the phone tab bar is drawn.

- **The desk (RPT20a)** shows these blocks, in this order:
  - **Due next**, as before.
  - **Needs you.** It is kept between the two, because it carries the expected-date door.
  - **Periods,** a vertical list of every period by due date. Each row shows "[period] · [duty]", "[holder] · due [day]" and the period's state pill, in the calendar's colours.
  - **The twelve-column calendar is not drawn on a phone.** Twelve columns do not fit at 390, and "View as table" would repeat the list.
  - Pressing a row selects the period and opens the same panel the calendar opens, with Prepare it and Give an expected date.
- **The pinned bar (RPT20a).**
  - "Prepare [period] report" moves from the header to a bar pinned to the foot of the page (`PinnedBar`, the canvas's `pbar`). Due next's own Prepare is not drawn on a phone, so the press appears once.
  - The bar sits above the phone tab bar while one is drawn: `bottom: var(--mobile-tabbar-h, 0px)` (D425). It is never under the tab bar.
  - There is no bar for a reader (an advisor, a partner or an admin), or while no period is open.
- **The builder (RPT20b)** shows one step to the screen.
  - **The heading** says where the founder is: "Step 2 of 4 · Narrative and documents".
  - **The step before** is one press back: "‹ Figures".
  - **The row of step pills** is the desktop's only.
  - **Save draft and Next** are pinned to the foot.
    - Save draft saves with no change, which re-reads every store (D1059 §4). It then says "Draft saved HH:MM".
    - The last step has no Next. Preview's own Send is its press.
- **Send (RPT20c)** is a bottom sheet: the `Lightbox` `phone="sheet"` (D1014). The other dialogs here stay full screen on a phone.

### 2. The investor at 390 (RPT21)

"At 390" here means below 640px, the width at which `Lightbox` draws a full-screen sheet (its `max-sm:`). So the sheet's own crumb is drawn exactly where the sheet is (`SHEET_QUERY`).

- **Figures (RPT21a):** one chart per screen width (`grid-cols-1`, two from `md`), as part 5 already drew. Each chart's View as table is a 44px target.
- **Reports (RPT21b):** a list. Each period is one row: "[period] · [duty]", the due or sent line, its pill, and Asked.
  - The row is the press.
  - A sent period opens the reader. A period still owed opens its own sheet.
  - The desktop keeps Read, Ask and Waive on each row.
- **A late period (RPT21c)** opens full screen:
  - "‹ Reports" back, the title "[period] update", the pill "Late · N days", and "[duty] from [company]";
  - when it was due, and the expected date and note the founders gave;
  - "Ask for this report" pinned at its foot, which opens part 5's Ask dialog as a bottom sheet.
  - A period not yet late offers Waive alone: a report is asked for only once it is late (D1061).
- **The reader** is full screen on a phone too, with the same head ("‹ Reports", the title, the pill, the duty and the company) in place of the dialog's eyebrow and ×. Part 5 drew it as a bottom sheet.

### 3. Accessibility

- **44px targets at phone width:**
  - the desk's and the company page's tabs;
  - the period rows;
  - the pinned bars' buttons, which share the bar's width;
  - the desk's Prepare it, Give an expected date and View as table;
  - every reporting dialog's two buttons, which share the row below 640px;
  - the reminder dialog's checkbox rows;
  - the crumbs.
  - The desktop keeps its compact sizes (`lg:min-h-0`, `sm:min-h-0`).
- **The keyboard path:**
  - the tabs are links and every row is a button, each in tab order;
  - the rows show a visible ring (`focus-visible:ring-2`);
  - the desktop calendar keeps its one tab stop moved by the arrow keys, Home and End (D1060);
  - every sheet is a `Lightbox`, so focus moves in, Tab is held inside, Escape closes, and focus goes back to the row that opened it;
  - the steps heading and the saved line are `aria-live`.
- **The switches** (the include and share boxes, and the reminder choices) are native checkboxes in their labels, as parts 4 and 5 drew them.

### 4. Dark

- **Every light `hover:` has a `dark:hover:` pair in the same class string.**
  - Part 5's two investor buttons had none. They now have `dark:hover:border-violet-400/60` and `dark:hover:bg-violet-500`.
  - The test reads all six files, as `check-dark-mode` does.
- **The status colours keep their own dark pair:**
  - the desk's `STATE_TONE` and the investor's `PILL_TONE`;
  - lateness is amber in both themes, never red.
- **The pinned bar** is `bg-white` / `dark:bg-gray-950`, with a hairline / `gray-800` top edge.

### 5. Not built

- **The phone desk has no More menu.** On a phone the desk's header is `CycleBar`, as part 4 built it. So Share an update and Reminder settings are reached from the desktop. The canvas's RPT20 draws no More either.
