## D5345 — Pin browser CI and require download token storage (#5345)

Pin both Playwright workflows to the registry digests of their current image
tags. WF-2 now enforces digest pins across all workflow containers.

Production fails closed at signed-download issuance and consumption when the
token store is absent. Issuance also requires successful registration. The
KV entry lasts at least 60 seconds to satisfy Workers KV, while the signed
expiry continues to enforce the requested link lifetime.

Access-policy and preview-verification work remain tracked on #5345.
