## D1265 — Raise evidence part 4: what an investor may see, and SCORING's one read

S20, #3288, part 4 of #2430. No migration. Built on D1263's stores (migration 628)
on `main` at `1035eca9ef`. #2430's owner questions 1–4 and 6 take their (A) answers (D1261 §5).

### 1. Who reads (`evidenceAudience`)

An investor in the raise (owner question 3) is an active, non-admin account, not the
venture's own founder, holding any of:

- an active, unexpired data-room grant on the venture;
- a recipient row on one of the venture's round calls that has been sent (`sent` or `closed`);
- a deal invitation or a commitment on one of the venture's deals (the deal page's rule).

**Departure:** the issue also names "an advisor grant with an evidence scope".
`advisor_client_grants` has no evidence scope and this part has no migration, so an
advisor grant admits no one here. A later part adds the scope column and the branch.

No grant opens no folder: the audience then carries an empty folder scope, so
`tierAdmits` refuses every file.

### 2. The NDA, then each item's sharing

The NDA is the room's: an active `pairwise_ndas` row between an account of the
venture's founder and the investor (`hasActivePairwiseNda`, checked per founder account).

- **Before the NDA (owner question 2):** coverage only. Each group is an area, a kind,
  a Source label, a count and the newest observation date. There are no values, items or claims.
- **After the NDA:**
  - each pinned item the founder shared appears;
  - After NDA items carry their value;
  - Summary items carry none ("Shared as a summary only.");
  - each claim linked to a shown item gives its state (owner question 4), never its stated figure.

### 3. What is left out, computed at read

Private and unpinned items are never read. Each shared item must also pass every one of these checks:

1. **Fingerprint.** The source still matches the fingerprint the founder accepted. The
   source is re-read now with the accepting founder as the bank owner, never the reader. A
   changed or vanished source leaves the item out. A source block that cannot be read
   leaves its items out and sets `partial`.
2. **Quote consent.** A quote's interview still has `quote_consent = 1`. Withdrawn
   consent already removes the source candidate, so this check is a second lock.
3. **Files.** A file-backed item (`bank-statement`, `revenue-proof`) passes only if the room would
   give this reader the file:
   - its tier and expiry;
   - the grant's folders;
   - then its own NDA gate.

**Values withheld even after the NDA** (each item says why):

- A bank line: a bank-derived item that is not a derived figure. Derived figures are
  runway, bank revenue, mapped spend and a KPI. This follows owner question 1: never a
  balance, an account, a line or a counterparty.
- One investor's money: round-call responses, typed commitments, confirmed receipts and
  accepted SAFEs.

Items carry no source reference, fingerprint, private reason, actor or name.

### 4. Routes

All three routes read through `investorEvidence`. Each open writes `open_evidence` to
`data_room_access_log`.

- `GET /api/data-room/shared/:projectUid/evidence` (`routes/data_room.ts`).
  - An admin is sent to a support read.
  - An outsider and a missing venture both get 404.
- `GET /api/deals/:id/evidence` (`routes/deals.ts`).
  - The deal's own read gate applies.
  - An investor reads.
  - Anyone else gets 403 `investor_only`; the founder reads Raise › Evidence.
- `evidence` on an investor's `GET /api/round-calls/:uid` (`services/roundCalls.ts`
  `investorPage`).
  - It is null when the read does not admit the recipient.
  - It is Unreadable when the read fails, and it never blanks the call.

The data-room activity log ignores the new action in its figures and prints it raw in
its CSV until a later part names it.

Clients: `raiseEvidenceApi.shared(projectUid)` and `raiseEvidenceApi.forDeal(dealId)`.
No page calls them yet. The deal page's Evidence block keeps D1261's wait until the UI
part.

### 5. SCORING (`evidencePack(env, projectId, { audience: 'scoring' })`)

The pack reads only items shared beyond Private (owner question 6) and current at read:

- the same fingerprint check and the same withheld values;
- each item's `backs`;
- the states of the claims linked to its items;
- `pack_hash`, a SHA-256 of exactly what was read.

Any other audience is refused. A SCORING read is not an investor open, so it logs nothing.

### 6. Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/raise_evidence_investor_d1265.test.ts` runs eight tests on real SQLite
with the production schema and migrations. They cover:

- the audience;
- before and after the NDA;
- never a private item, a balance, a bank line, another investor's money or name, an
  unconsented quote or a refused file;
- a changed source;
- a recipient without a grant;
- claim states;
- the three routes and their logs;
- the pack and its hash.

Each guard was mutation-checked, and its test fails when the guard is removed:

- private items;
- the fingerprint;
- the file gate;
- one investor's money;
- the NDA;
- a sent call;
- the open log;
- the pack hash.

The quote check is not mutation-checked separately, because the fingerprint already
covers it (§3).
