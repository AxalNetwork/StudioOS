## D4570 part 10 — Research: the 29 `/api/research` ledger routes, tested and verified (#4570)

#4570 slice 10, after `D4570-p9.md`. It covers every `unreviewed` ledger route under `/api/research`, the largest
family left that is not public or pre-sign-in by design (`/api/public` and `/api/auth` need a different treatment). No
earlier part holds research back; slice 7 (`D4570-p7.md`) already verified the document download. No product code
changes: every gate was already right, and this slice proves it.

### How the gates work

The routes fall into three groups.

**A member's own records (21 routes).** Library documents, saved Ask answers, benchmarks, collections, zone drafts, fund
dossiers and their quarterly reports (`research.ts`, `research_collections.ts`), and market readings, brief notes and
proposal attachments (`research_client_prep.ts`). Each statement names the record and the caller together, as
`WHERE uid = ? AND owner_user_id = <the caller>`, with the caller's id taken from the session.

- **Nothing else opens them.** No role, share or grant does. A document shared with an advisor opens its download
  (slice 7) and nothing more, because every write resolves it through `ownDoc`, the owner's own read.
- **A record that joins two records checks both against the caller:**
  - adding a document to a collection, and removing one from it, checks the collection and the document;
  - a fund's report is read or deleted only where the fund, the report and the owner all match.
- **Who is refused, and how.** Every other account gets the 404 a missing record gets, the Super Admin included. A
  member's research belongs to that member alone, so no admin reach is designed in.

**The research catalogue (7 routes).** These are company, fund, market, geography, sector and country-statistics pages.
No member owns a row in them.

- Any signed-in account reads them (D533, D535, D7318).
- Companies follows D7178's licence rule (`admitsCompanies`), decided before the record is read. So an account outside
  the rule gets one 403, whether or not the company exists.

**The Google Sheets OAuth callback (1 route).** It carries no session.

- **The gate is the `state` value.** It is HMAC-signed, stored, used once and expires after ten minutes
  (`consumeSheetsState`). It names the account that started Connect.
- **Only a Super Admin's state can store a token.** If the state's account is not a Super Admin, the callback refuses
  before the code is exchanged.

### What the suite proves

`cloudflare-worker/test/authz_research_d4570.test.ts` mounts the research routers in the Worker's order (market data,
markets, sectors, client prep, then `research.ts` with its collections) on the full migrated schema. It answers a thrown
refusal with the Worker's own status table (`AUTH_ERROR_STATUSES`). Every person and record in it is synthetic.

**Who asks.** The owners are a founder (Library, Ask, benchmarks, collections, drafts and fund dossiers), an advisor
with a live grant on the founder's venture (a brief note) and an Operating Partner (a market reading and its
attachment). The other callers are:
- the advisor the founder's document was shared with;
- an advisor holding an active share whose grant was revoked;
- another founder;
- an investor;
- another firm's partner;
- an admin;
- the Super Admin.

**What it checks:**
- **Another account** gets 404 on every owned route. The answer has the same status and body as the answer for a record
  that does not exist, names nothing (no title, metric, fund, note or reading), changes no row and erases no stored
  object.
  - The suite compares 17 tables before and after, plus the admin audit count, the route-written activity count and the
    object store.
- **No credentials, or an owner whose account is disabled,** get 401 on every route, the catalogue included.
- **One's own record cannot reach another member's:**
  - another founder's collection cannot take the owner's document;
  - the share recipient's collection cannot take the document shared with them;
  - another member's report cannot be deleted through one's own fund, nor one's own report through another member's
    fund.
- **The catalogue:**
  - every signed-in account reads each page (200);
  - an investor or partner without a Founder or Advisor licence gets the same 403 `companies_licence_required` for an
    existing and a missing company.
- **The Sheets callback** refuses each of these, whether or not a Super Admin's session carries it back: no state, an
  unsigned nonce, a forged signature, a signed nonce never issued, an expired state, and the state of an admin who is
  not the Super Admin or of a founder. Each refusal redirects to the error page. No code is exchanged and no token is
  stored.
  - The Super Admin's own state stores one token, on the Super Admin, after one exchange.
  - That state is spent: a second use is refused.
- **The rightful owner passes every gate** on a fresh copy of the database. Their reads carry the record and their
  writes change it. Deleting their document erases its stored object.
- **Completeness.** The first test reads the ledger. It fails if a route the suite covers is not marked verified by it,
  or if any `/api/research` route is left `unreviewed`.

**Mutation check.** 29 gate mutations, each run alone, seen red and restored: 28 caught, 1 equivalent. Each owner
binding is widened to match every row (`owner_user_id = ? OR 1`), so the statement still binds the same values. The
mutations:
- the owner binding in `ownDoc`, the Ask answer read, the benchmark delete, the draft accept and the draft discard;
- the owner binding in the fund edit and the fund delete;
- the fund check before the reports list and before the report create;
- the report delete's fund binding and its owner binding;
- the owner binding in `ownCollection`;
- the collection add's document check, alone and together with its insert's;
- the collection remove's document check;
- the owner binding in the market reading read and delete, the brief note settle and delete, and the attachment delete;
- `admitsCompanies` admitting everyone;
- `requireAuth` removed from a market directory row and from a geography;
- `getCurrentUser`'s `is_active` refusal removed;
- the callback's Super Admin test removed;
- in `consumeSheetsState`: the signature comparison removed, the length test and comparison removed, the expiry test
  removed, and the single-use delete removed.

**The equivalent one** is the collection remove's document check. The collection is checked against the caller first,
and the add checks both the collection and the document, so a member's collection never holds another member's
document. With the check widened, nothing matches either way. The add's checks are what keep it so, and both are
caught.

Two first-run misses were closed by strengthening the suite, not counted as equivalent:
- **The draft accept's read.** Widening it alone let the owner-bound write and re-read answer the same 404. The draft
  fixture is now a fund brief whose accept writes into the fund's note, so a widened read reaches that write and
  answers differently.
- **The fund edit's read.** Widening it alone was covered the same way, except that a stale revision would return the
  stored row for comparison. The suite now sends another member's edit with a stale revision, and asserts the
  missing-record 404, with nothing named.

### By design, not findings

- **Admins have no reach into a member's research.** The admin and the Super Admin get the same 404 as any other
  account, as the file header of `research.ts` states ("no cross-user listing anywhere in this file by construction").
- **The Markets, Geographies and Sectors pages list catalogue companies to every signed-in account.** That includes
  accounts the Companies rule refuses. D7178 sends a refused account to Markets ("The refusal offers Open Markets"), and
  these are discovery rows from the same public catalogue, not anyone's record.

### Hardening, not findings

None of these is exploitable as the code stands, and none is changed here.

- **Market readings.** `GET /market-readings` and `GET /market-readings/:uid` do not refuse an investor-only account,
  while the two summary routes do (D6273 Q2). An investor reads only readings they made themselves, so nothing crosses
  an account. Remediation class: one licence rule for every market-reading read.
- **Brief notes.** An advisor can still settle or delete their own brief note after the founder revokes the grant or it
  expires. The list and create routes need a live grant; settle and delete check only the owner. The note is the
  advisor's own record, and once the grant is gone they can no longer list it. Remediation class: the same live-grant
  rule on every brief-note route.
- **The Sheets callback.**
  - It does not check that the state's account is still active.
  - It does not bind the state to the browser that started Connect. Whoever completes Google's consent screen with the
    Super Admin's state links their own Google account to the Super Admin's connection. Reaching that state takes the
    Super Admin's own ten-minute authorisation link.
  - Remediation class: check `is_active` on the state's account, and bind the state to the session that started Connect
    (a cookie or PKCE verifier).
