- **Owner questions 3 and 8 take answer A:**
  - 3: only a primary admin archives or restores a company;
  - 8: a venture moves only to a company its owner belongs to.
- **Depends on part 1 (D1135, #3015) for leave.** Part 1 is open. Today a member who removes themselves through the existing removal route is recorded as a leave. Part 1's own leave route records it through the same `membershipEvent`.

### 1. Migration 597: `597_company_archive_and_events.sql`

- **`company_profiles.archived_at` and `archived_by`.** NULL means not archived, which every existing company is.
- **`company_membership_events`:**
  - columns: company, venture (`project_id`), actor, target, kind, `before_json`, `after_json`, at;
  - the twelve kinds: join, role_change, remove, leave, transfer, archive, restore, rename, project_move, project_transfer, seat_add, seat_remove;
  - a CHECK holds the kinds and requires a company or a venture;
  - `before` and `after` hold only the fields a change touched (a role, a flag, a company id, a name), never a token, an email or an invitation code.

### 2. The routes

All are Worker-only and use bound SQL. **Every change and its event are one `DB.batch`,** so neither commits without the other.

**`routes/company_lifecycle.ts`**, mounted at `/api` before `company.ts`:

| Route | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/company/:uid/archive` | a primary admin | Sets `archived_at`/`archived_by`, with an `archive` event. Archiving twice is 409 `already_archived`. |
| `POST /api/company/:uid/restore` | a primary admin | Clears them, with a `restore` event. Restoring a company that is not archived is 409 `not_archived`. |
| `POST /api/company/:uid/transfer` | a primary admin | `{ user_id, demote? }`. Grants primary admin to a member and, when `demote` is `true`, removes the caller's, with a `transfer` event, in one batch. |

**How the company routes refuse:**
- A non-member reads 404, as `GET /company/:uid` answers one. A member who is not a primary admin reads 403.
- A platform admin is not a primary admin: owner question 3 A names the primary admin only.

**A transfer never leaves a company without a primary admin.**
- Before this, primary admin moved by two member edits, which could stop halfway or demote first.
- Now the grant and the demote are one batch, the grant first.
- The target must already be a member. Transferring to yourself is 400, and a non-boolean `demote` is 400.
- Transferring to someone who is already a primary admin without `demote` is 409 `already_primary`.
- An archived company takes no transfer (409 `archived`).

**`routes/project_lifecycle.ts`**, mounted at `/api/projects` before `projects.ts`. Only the venture's owner or an admin may call these, by `isProjectManager`'s rule (D889):

| Route | What |
|---|---|
| `POST /api/projects/:id/move` | `{ company_id }`: to a company the owner's account belongs to (owner question 8 A), or `null` to take the venture out of its company. Writes a `project_move` event with the before and after company. |
| `POST /api/projects/:id/transfer` | `{ user_id }`: the owner seat to an accepted co-founder of the venture whose account is a founder's, active and on a founders row. `projects.founder_id` moves to theirs; the previous owner keeps a seat as a co-founder. Writes a `project_transfer` event. |

**How the venture routes refuse:**
- **Moving** to a company the owner does not belong to reads 404 `not_owner_company`, so the route confirms no company. An archived company is 409 `archived`, and the venture's current company is 409 `unchanged`.
- **Transferring:**
  - someone without a co-founder seat, an advisor seat included, is 404 `not_cofounder`;
  - a co-founder whose account is not a founder's, even on a founders row, is 409 `no_founder_account`;
  - a co-founder who does not belong to the venture's company is 409 `owner_not_in_company`, and the dialog asks for a move first. Nothing changes the company behind the owner's back.

### 3. An archived company is nobody's active company

- **`resolveActiveCompany`** reads an archived company as none. Its membership check gains `NOT EXISTS` on an archived `company_profiles` row, so a link with no company row reads exactly as before.
- **`GET /api/company/memberships`** leaves archived companies out, so they leave the switcher. `?include=archived` lists them with `archived_at`, which is where a primary admin restores one.
- **`GET /api/company/me`** skips them.
- **Detail payloads** carry `archived_at`.
- **Invitations:** accepting one to an archived company is 410 `archived`.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Members, ventures and records stay where they are, and Restore brings the company back as it was.

### 4. The membership audit

Each existing route now writes its event in the same batch as its change:

- **Joins:**
  - `POST /company/create`: the creator's `join`;
  - `POST /company/invitations/accept`: `join`.
- **Rename:** `PATCH /company/:uid`, only when `company_name` changes.
- **Role change:** `PATCH /company/:uid/members/:userId`, only when `role_in_company` or primary admin changes. Title, authority and carry are not membership.
- **Removal and leave:** `DELETE /company/:uid/members/:userId` writes `remove`, or `leave` when the member removes themselves.
- **Seat changes on a venture:**
  - `POST /api/projects/:id/members` and `POST /api/projects/invitations/accept`: `seat_add`;
  - `DELETE /api/projects/:id/members/:userId`: `seat_remove`, only when there was a seat to remove.

These project routes' writes move from the `sql` tag to bound `DB.prepare` statements, so the seat and its event batch together.

### 5. Departures, for review

- **Archive does not freeze the company.** Edits, invitations and team changes on an archived company still work. Only the transfer, a move into it and accepting an invitation to it are refused. A freeze across every company write was not asked for, and it would touch every handler part 1 is changing.
- **There is no read of the events yet.** The issue asks for the writes. A history read for a primary admin can follow when a page draws it.
- **About 40 worker test fixtures gain `company_profiles`, or its `archived_at` column,** because `resolveActiveCompany` now reads it. Positional inserts into it name `id`.
  - Three fixtures with their own D1 stubs now run `batch()`'s statements, all or none, as D1 does.
  - `company_duplicates.test.ts` counts the join event among create's inserts.
  - The Mercury fixtures (`_mercury_d713.ts`, `_mercury_grow_fixture.ts`, `_mercury_raise_fixture.ts`) gain the same table.
  - No assertion was weakened.
- **`project_lifecycle.ts` joins the company switcher guard's `WIDE_ON_PURPOSE` list** (`frontend/test/company_switcher.test.mjs`). It writes to one named venture, gated to its owner. `founder_id = ?` only finds the owner's account, and a move has to reach a company other than the active one, so narrowing it to the active company would be wrong.

### 6. Tests

**`cloudflare-worker/test/company_lifecycle_d1137.test.ts`** has 6 cases on SQLite with the routers mounted as `index.ts` mounts them:
- **Archive and restore:** a primary admin only; never active once archived; out of the memberships list and `/company/me`; back on restore.
- **Transfer:**
  - refused for a non-member, for yourself, and for a string `demote`;
  - a failed event write rolls back both the grant and the demote, so it is never halfway;
  - with and without demote;
  - refused on an archived company.
- **Move:** only to the owner's companies, never to an archived one, and out of a company with `null`. A co-founder cannot move it; an admin can.
- **Owner transfer:**
  - an advisor seat, an advisor account, and an exploring account on a founders row are all refused;
  - a co-founder outside the company is refused until the venture moves;
  - the previous owner keeps a co-founder seat.
- **Events:** join, rename (and none without one), role change (and none for a title edit), remove and leave, seat add and remove, the writer's refusals, and the migration's CHECK.

**25 of 25 mutations killed.** The first try of `demote` read as `Boolean(...)` was equivalent, since non-booleans are already refused. It was replaced by "demote unless false", which is caught.
