## D6867 — Investor › Portfolio v2: the shareable gross statement PDF for a chosen quarter-end, built (#6867)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-16 to the D6217 canvas (`design/incoming/Portfolio v2 Shareable Statement Page.dc.html` and its boards, PF-13a to PF-13i). The owner answered option A on every D6217 question:
  - gross is the only basis;
  - the investor downloads and sends it, and Axal neither sends nor records it;
  - it opens as a sheet, with no new route;
  - any completed quarter-end can be chosen, with the mismatch note and Funded after rows.
- **Builds on:** `routes/positions.ts` (`/analytics?as_of=`, `GET /?as_of=`), `pages/portfolio/portfolioModel.js` (`quarterWindowOf`, `quarterPointOf`, `cohortsOf`, `DEFINITIONS`), `lib/pdfBoard.js` and the layout of `lib/quarterlyReportPdf.js`.

### What was built

- **The Statement action** sits on Overview (`InvestorPortfolioCanvas.jsx`) and Performance (`InvestorPortfolioPerformance.jsx`). It opens `pages/portfolio/StatementSheet.jsx` over the page.
- **The sheet** offers a chooser of every completed quarter-end, newest first, from the first cheque's year to the reporting read's `period_until` (`statementDatesOf`).
  - Each date is its own read: the analytics read and the positions read, both cut at that date.
  - The preview strip shows NAV, paid-in, TVPI and coverage, with the basis line "Gross of fees and carry · USD · Net returns: Not recorded".
  - A date whose read holds no position reads "No book · before the first cheque" and cannot be downloaded.
  - A failed read names itself, says nothing was downloaded, keeps the date and retries that read only.
  - Download PDF is the one primary action, held while reads are pending.
  - After the download, a confirmation says Axal sent nothing and kept no copy, and offers "Open Performance as of <date>".
  - The sheet shows the scope sentence, and the GP of record's second sentence when the read returns `fund_scope`.
- **The mismatch note** (`mismatchOf`) appears on the sheet, page 2 and page 4 whenever the positions' invested total differs from paid-in at the date.
  - A row reads "Funded after" when its first cheque's year is later than the date, or its only cheque is dated after it (`fundedAfter`).
  - A company with an earlier cheque and a later one is not caught this way; the note covers it.
- **The PDF** (`lib/portfolioStatementPdf.js`, jsPDF on `pdfBoard.js`, US Letter) has five sections:
  - **Page 1, Summary:** eleven rows, each naming what it counts and for which date, then the scope, then the basis with "Net returns: Not recorded".
  - **Page 2, Positions:** at-cost and unusable rows carry the API's own sentence; pills read Below cost, At cost, Mark unusable and Funded after. The header repeats on a continuation page.
  - **Page 3, NAV at quarter-ends:** bars and a table. No book and Not read gaps are drawn as labelled empty slots, never as zeros.
  - **Page 4, Cohorts:** carrying value ÷ invested, never TVPI.
  - **Page 5, Methodology.**
  - Every page head says "Gross of fees and carry", and every footer says "Page x of y". The last footer says "not an audited statement".
  - Unavailable returns print with their reason, a total loss prints as −100%, and a measured zero stays 0.
- **Methodology is one list.** `METHODOLOGY` moved from the Performance page into `statementModel.js`. Performance draws its card from it, and page 5 prints it.
  - The PDF core faces have no Σ or −, so the PDF prints "Sum of" and "-" in their place (`pdfText`). The page shows the original glyphs.
- **Unreadable produces no statement** (`statementOf` returns `ok: false` with the failed reads), with one exception: a single failed quarter-end is drawn on page 3 as a Not read gap, and the page lists it.
- **Admin preview:** an admin's positions read has scope `all_positions`. Their statement says "Every position the studio recorded.", the sheet carries a preview banner, and every page carries "Preview, not for distribution".
- **The cover note** is a tick box, "Include as a cover note", with a text box. Its text is printed above the figures on page 1 as "Note from <investor> · the investor's own words, not Axal's". Nothing is printed unless the box is ticked.
- **`?as_of=` on Performance:** a quarter-end in the address cuts the summary at that date and says so ("The summary is the book as of …", with a link back to the latest book), so "Open Performance" lands on page 1's figures.
- **Phone 390:** a full-width sheet, one-column cards, 44px targets and the Download bar pinned.
- **Dark:** the sheet draws on the Portfolio frame's tokens, so `.dark .pf-shell` applies. The PDF stays paper.

- Net of fees and carry: no read supplies it (D4817-p3). It is printed as "Net returns: Not recorded".
- Distributions per company: cohorts stay carrying value ÷ invested.
- No record of a produced or sent statement, and no sending to a named person (owner pick A).
- A distinct Refused state: the Portfolio reads keep no HTTP status (`readOf`, #5365 F-26), so a 403 reads as Unreadable on the sheet as it does on the page.
- The Admin Investor preview (`PortfolioPreview.jsx`, D4817 part 8) withholds every investor figure, so it offers no Statement action. The watermark applies to an admin reading the studio's book on the real pages.
