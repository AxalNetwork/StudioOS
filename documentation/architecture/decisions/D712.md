## D712 — Automated fund reporting: drafts into an admin review queue that only a Super Admin issues from

Status: Decided. Date: 2026-10-17. Slot: S12. Issue: #1777. The owner asked on 2026-10-17 for admin accounts connected to Mercury to report automatically. This is the reporting half; #1775 stores the Mercury data, #1773 registers the entities' accounts and #1774 produces fee statements. S1 decided every point (the issue's decisions 1 to 12). This file records what was built and the interpretations made while building it.

PR 1 (this file's first version) builds the queue, the gates and the quarterly pack from the fund records. PR 2 adds the monthly cash summary, the Fund's cash, the unmatched counts and the fee statements once #1773, #1774 and #1775 are on `main`.

### What was built in PR 1

- **Migration 476** creates two tables:
  - `fund_report_drafts`: the kind, audience, subject and period; the revision and status; the frozen `figures_json` and its SHA-256; the narrative revision; the correction it makes; and who built, prepared and issued it, with when.
  - `fund_report_draft_events`: one sealed row per act.

  The triggers refuse:
  - every DELETE;
  - an edit of the figures or the digest;
  - a change to a row's identity;
  - any change to an issued or superseded row;
  - every state change outside `draft ↔ prepared`, `draft|prepared → superseded` and `prepared → issued`;
  - an issue by the account that prepared the row.

  A partial unique index keeps one live draft per kind, subject and period.
- **`services/fundReportBuild.ts`** builds the figures of decision 8. All money is integer cents. A missing input is `{ cents: null, reason }`, never 0.
  - The allocated lines are split by percentage interest through `splitCall`, so the LPs' shares sum to the fund figure exactly.
  - Fees, expenses and liabilities count only when closed D628 periods cover every day of the quarter.
  - NAV needs investment value, the Fund's cash and liabilities. In PR 1 the Fund's cash reads "Not recorded: the Fund has no bank account on record" (decision 9), and so does NAV.
- **`services/fundReportDrafts.ts`** holds the state machine of decisions 2 to 4.
  - Each act commits with its event rows in one batch. The batch's first statement carries every precondition, and every later statement requires that row's new state.
  - A refused issue changes nothing.
  - The quarterly pack is built, rebuilt, prepared, returned, issued and corrected as one unit.
- **`routes/fund_reporting.ts`** serves `/api/fund-reporting`.
  - Every route is an Admin's, on HQ.
  - An issue asserts D714's act `fund_report.issue` (Super Admin tier) through `assertAdminAct`. This PR adds that row to `FUND_ACTS`, as #1777 asked once #1779 was on `main`.
    - The gate refuses a support session (`support_session_refused`), applies the recovery cool-off, answers an Admin without the elevation 403 `super_admin_required` before any TOTP or step-up demand, and then runs `requireSuperAdminWriteBar`.
    - A refused issue writes nothing. A successful one also writes D714's `fund_admin.fund_report.issue` audit row.
  - `GET /drafts/:uid` and its export write their event row before any figure leaves; a failed write answers 503. Both are mirrored to `logAdminAction`.
- **The schedule** is one block in the every-minute cron at 05:20 UTC, gated on `hqCadences`.
  - It builds each administered fund's pack for the last closed quarter, once it is due (the 5th day after the quarter ends) and missing in every status.
  - 05:20 was free on `main`.
- **Decision 6:**
  - `POST /api/funds/:id/report-periods` refuses an admin's `issue: true` with 409 `issue_from_reporting_queue`.
  - `lp_reports` create, edit and publish go through `requireFundGp` on the report's fund. An admin's publish gets the same 409, and a published row answers 409 to an edit.
- **Reads:**
  - `GET /lp-portal` adds the caller's own issued `capital_statements`.
  - An issued period issued through the queue carries its frozen fund-level `figures`, without the statement list.
  - `GET /funds/:id/lp-report/:lpId` adds the LP's issued statements and, for an admin, the live drafts by uid and status only.
  - A failed statements read is `capital_statements_recorded: false`, never an empty list.
- **Frontend:**
  - `fundReportingApi` (`lib/api/fundReporting.js`).
  - The quarterly report's frozen variant: nothing from `spinoutFundModel.js`, every missing line Not recorded, and the PDF draws each model-only section as Not recorded.
  - Issued periods print their recorded `issued_at`.
  - The admin's live draft opens through the audited read.
  - The period page's Pack card replaces the admin's Issue button and correcting-period link. The LP reporting page shows an admin "Admins issue LP reports from the reporting queue."

### Interpretations made while building

1. **A statement acts with its report.**
   - Prepare, return and issue on an `lp_capital_statement` answer 409 `pack_member`: the pack is one unit, so its statements move through the report.
   - Correcting a statement corrects its whole pack.
2. **Pack membership excludes LPs without a positive commitment** (decision 5). So "with one commitment missing" cannot reach an allocation: such an LP is outside the pack, and its statement is not built. A fund figure that is itself Not recorded makes every LP's share Not recorded.
3. **Building is "missing in any status"** for the schedule and for `POST /build` alike.
   - A pack is not built when its quarter already has a report draft, when the period was issued outside the queue, or when the fund has no LP in the pack.
   - A pack is rebuilt only through `POST /drafts/:uid/rebuild`.
4. **Paid-in and unfunded both read Not recorded when receipts and `invested_amount` do not tie.** The statement records both figures and picks neither (D372's check).
5. **Opening NAV with no issued statement** is zero only when the LP paid nothing in before the quarter started. Otherwise it is Not recorded. The roll-forward prints only when both NAVs are recorded, which in PR 1 they never are.
6. **The export flattens the frozen figures** to `field, value, note` rows: the path, the cents, and the reason a missing line gives.
   - It defuses a text cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return.
   - It quotes every cell.
   - Amounts are plain numbers.
7. **A correcting pack keeps the quarter's label** (`Q3 2026`) on its drafts. Its period row is the correcting one, `Q3 2026 · correction 1`, whose `_receipt.corrects_period_id` names the issued period. Only one correction of a quarter is open at a time.
8. **The LP reporting page's Publish button only ever showed to admins**, so no account publishes from that page now. A GP of record still publishes through the route.

### Not built, by design

- No route returns a draft's figures except the audited read and its export.
- No file this decision adds imports `integrations/secrets.ts` or a provider. It never calls the Mercury API, decrypts a token or moves money.
- The draft builders are PR 1's quarterly pack only. `POST /build` refuses `entity_cash_monthly` and `mgmt_fee_statement` with 409 `builder_not_available` until PR 2.
