## D6130 part 2 — the fund workspace's first screen follows the reader's standing (#6130)

Part of #6130. Part 1 is `D6130.md` (the section files and the section links).
The canvas is `Axal VC Fund v2 and LP Workspace` (D6127, owner's answers in
`D6127-p2.md`); this part builds boards VF-01 to VF-05, VF-15, VF-15B and
VF-16A, at desktop, 390px and dark.

### What is built

- `/spinout-lab/investor-workspace` (and the embedded Axal VC Fund page) draws
  one first screen per standing, chosen by `firstScreenKind()` in
  `frontend/src/lib/fundFirstScreenModel.js` from the access ladder
  (`lpAccessState`): visitor, applicant (application in review), approved with
  no position, position with the LPA unsigned, approved with nothing
  committed, committed, full rights. While the position is read the page shows
  a skeleton and a "Reading your standing…" chip; a failed read is its own
  screen ("Your position could not be read. This is not a $0 commitment."),
  never Visitor.
- A committed or full-rights LP sees What is due, What to sign and What
  arrived first, then their commitment figures, then their sections. The fund's
  sales page (Fund and terms) comes after their own sections. An applicant
  meets Apply and onboarding before Fund and terms; the section links follow
  the same order (`sectionOrder()`).
- What is due: the open capital-call lines from `GET /api/funds/lp-portal`,
  soonest due first, with the masked account and reference the portal already
  carries; "Wire details for call #N" goes to My commitment's phone-checked
  wire gate. A failed calls read says so and is not "nothing due".
- What to sign: the subscription booklet from `GET /api/fund-booklets/mine`
  (404 is "none on the platform") and the LPA from the LP rows. The block
  says "nothing left" only when every source was read.
- What arrived: the latest three of the notices (`GET /api/fund-notices/mine`),
  capital and fee statements and issued reports, newest first; a source that
  could not be read is named.
- The commitment figures are each from the record or say why not: committed,
  called, received on calls, uncalled, distributions, TVPI, DPI. A multiple with
  nothing paid in is "No multiple yet", never 0.00×.
- The applicant's "under review" card and its proof-of-funds door moved from
  the participation section to the first screen.

### Departures from the canvas

- **No new route, no new read.** Both added reads are existing mounted routes.
  The notices and the booklet are read once more by their own sections
  (My notices, Subscription booklet) until part 3's documents shelf owns them.
- **"Received on calls (to date)"**, not "Paid in": My commitment already
  prints "Paid in" for the record's invested amount, a different figure.
- **No "Request an increase" button**: no store holds a request, and the issue
  forbids a control that sends nothing. "Email the GP of record" remains.
- **Full rights (VF-05) has no rights block.** Allocation preferences (D6138)
  and co-invest offers (D6140) were removed by the owner; cohort access dates
  are not on an investor's read. Full rights is the committed screen without
  the increase line.
- **The call sheet (VF-07)** is not built; the wire action goes to My
  commitment's existing wire gate.
- **The header** keeps the existing workspace header and part 1's standing
  chip words; the canvas's header actions and chip words are not drawn.
- **VF-03's reports-archive summary and the visitor's thesis card (VF-01)**:
  the Reports and Fund and terms sections that follow carry them.
- **Declined or withdrawn** returns to the Visitor screen; the review note is
  not yet kept on Apply and onboarding.
- **Phone**: the section links wrap (part 1) rather than scroll as a chip row.
