## D801 — HQ's licence console: the terms schedule editor, and answering the Administrator's requests

- **Status:** decided by S1 for the owner, 2026-10-20 (#2234; part of D795, #2228, items D-b and D-d).
- **Basis:** D797's branch (#2242), with D799's (#2249) merged in. Frontend paths are relative to `frontend/src/`.
- **Depends on:** D797 (#2242: the terms store and HQ's `PUT`) and D799 (#2249: the request kinds and their fields). The design is canvas board L9 (`design/incoming/Licences Page.dc.html`, `board: 'hq'`).

### What was there

- **HQ's licence page,** `/admin/licences/:uid` (`pages/admin/AdminLicences.jsx`), issued, activated, re-termed, suspended and terminated a licence. It also held the licence's contract, admins, notices, domain and brand.
- **D797** added a terms schedule with an API (`licenceTermsApi`), but nothing drew it.
- **D799** added four request kinds that an Administrator sends HQ. HQ could answer them only from the Support queue, where they read as bare keys.

### 1. "Terms schedule" on the licence page

`pages/admin/LicenceTermsSchedule.jsx` is a new unnumbered tab beside Admins and History. Like those tabs, it is not a step of the issue flow.

- **The fields are D797's own list.** `lib/licenceTermsForm.js` imports `LICENCE_TERM_FIELDS` from `cloudflare-worker/src/services/licenceTerms.ts`, the same module the `PUT` validates with.
  - A term D797 adds appears with no edit here, and the editor can never offer a key the Worker refuses.
  - A test asserts that the list is the Worker's object and that no key is spelled out in the editor.
  - The only thing this page adds is the section titles from the canvas.
- **Sections.** The six titles that hold D797 groups are drawn: Parties, Territory and exclusivity, Term and renewal, Fees and money, Deal terms, and Compliance and reporting.
  - A group D797 adds later renders under its own key.
  - The canvas's "Seats" and "Brand and platform" hold no D797 term. They are the Seats and Brand steps.
- **Each value is edited in its unit:**
  - cents as money in the licence's currency;
  - bps as a percentage;
  - whole numbers with their unit (months, days, business days, day of month);
  - an enum or a yes/no as a choice;
  - text as text.
- **Conversion is exact both ways.** "1234.5" is 123450 cents, and "12.5" % is 1250 bps. More than two decimals is refused, not rounded.
- **Contract §.** Each row has its current value ("Now: …", or "Not recorded") and a "Contract §" field beside it.
- **A cleared field saves `null`,** which reads "Not recorded".
- **"Save terms"** asks for the reason ("Reason for this change · kept in the licence's history"). The save is refused without one, and with nothing changed.
  - `buildSave` sends only the terms and sections that changed, so a save never rewrites a term nobody touched.
  - The call is `PUT /api/admin/licences/:uid/terms` through D797's module. It takes the money-write bar, so a lapsed step-up raises the shared step-up prompt (`StepUpModal`) and the call is retried, as every other money write is.
  - After a save the page reloads, so History shows the `terms_changed` event.

### 2. "Requests from the Administrator"

`pages/admin/LicenceRequestsPanel.jsx` is a side panel beside every tab of the licence page.

- **What it lists:** this licence's open escalations of D799's four kinds, plus `seat_increase`.
  - D799 gave `seat_increase` the same request fields, and the canvas draws it in the same list ("Seats · Founder +20").
  - Each request shows its date, its sender, its response-time band (within, due soon, past) and its note.
- **How it reads them:** `GET /api/admin/escalations?status=open&kind=…` once per kind, through the existing `api.escalations`. No new route.
  - A request raised on HQ's tier names the licence in `subject_ref` (D799).
  - A branch's request is matched by branch code when the licence has one. No branch is deployed today (D286), and the licence read carries no code yet.
  - An unreadable board reads as unknown, not as none.
- **"Answer"** opens the Support queue's own `EscalationDecision`, so the answer is the existing `PATCH /api/admin/escalations/:uid`, and D799's bell notice follows.
- **A `terms_change`** shows the term as HQ's ledger holds it, how the Administrator reads it, and what is asked, with "Open terms".
  - A D797 key opens the schedule and highlights its row.
  - D799's four ledger terms open their own step: fee and term open Terms, seats open Seats, and territory opens Territory.
- **A `delegate`** shows "Grant admin". It opens the Admins tab with the email filled and the role set to delegate. HQ still reads the form and appoints through the existing `POST /api/admin/licences/:uid/admins`, because accepting a request never grants the role (D799).
- **A `non_renewal`** shows the date the notice was given (the day HQ recorded it, D799). It sets that against the renewal date less `non_renewal_notice_days`, and says whether the notice was in time or how many days late. With either input unrecorded, it says so.

### 3. The escalations console

On HQ › Support, `lib/escalationKinds.js` gives the four new kinds their words: "renewal", "change of terms", "delegate" and "notice of non-renewal".

- The older kinds keep the words they always had ("seat increase").
- The escalation queue gains a kind filter: All, then each kind on the board with its count, with the four new kinds always offered.
- The filter rides on the page's existing `decide` handle, so `escalationCardProps(ready, failed, decide)` keeps its call.
- A filtered queue counts what it shows.

### No new API

There is no new `/api/*` method. The page uses D797's `licenceTermsApi`, the existing `api.escalations` and `api.escalationAnswer`, and `api.licenceAdminAdd`.

### Tests

`frontend/test/hq_licence_terms_d801.test.mjs` has 12 tests. They cover:

- the field list being D797's object, every key once under the canvas's titles;
- cents and bps converted exactly both ways, a cleared field as `null`, and over-precise or out-of-range values refused;
- the save sending exactly what changed, and refusing without a reason, with nothing changed, or with a bad value;
- the form's rendering;
- the panel listing only this licence's open requests of its kinds;
- the panel reading the existing route once per kind, open only, with an unreadable board as unknown;
- each request kind's detail;
- Answer opening the existing decision panel;
- Open terms and the non-renewal check;
- the page's wiring and Grant admin's prefill;
- the console's labels, filter and counts.

31 mutations were made, each breaking one of these rules, and every one was caught.
