### 2. The changes

Each change states the rule the read now follows. Security-specific evidence stays in the private process (SECURITY.md), not here.

- **LP reports: the manager arm is the fund operating gate.** A non-admin reads a fund's reports, drafts included, through the funds `fundManageScope` returns for this request: the institutional tier and GP of record under the active company. A change of GP of record, tier or active company applies to the next read, as D4636 §1 requires. Anyone else reads as an LP (published reports of their funds) or not at all.
- **LP reports: the LP projection.** A reader who does not run the fund receives the published report without `created_by`. No screen reads that field.
- **Coverage: narrowing to one fund is a fund read.** `?fund_id=` goes through `requireFundGp`, so it follows the table's `GET /api/funds/:id` column. No screen calls this route with `fund_id`.
- **Position detail: the investor projection.** An investor receives the round's company-facing fields (uid, fund, round, amount, shares, price, ownership, date); the admin who records positions reads the whole row. The page keys rounds on `uid` and reads the round name, amount and ownership, all kept.

### 3. Judgement calls left as they are

- **Partner licence.** Portfolio health (`/health`, `/health/:uid`) and the coverage dashboard answer a partner with every venture, as T14 and Task #18 documented. D4636's update table gives a partner only the updates of ventures where they hold an accepted seat. This part leaves the health and coverage rule unchanged, because changing it would change what the partner role does in the product. The owner should decide it.
- **A GP and their fund's positions.** `/api/positions*` reads D1057's held scope: confirmed commitments by the caller. The GP of record of a fund that holds a position does not see it there unless they hold a commitment themselves. This is narrower than the table's GP column. It is not widened here: it would change what Portfolio's book means.
- **Positions are company-scoped, not fund-scoped.** A holder reads every round recorded in a company they hold, including one attributed to a fund, as D1057 states ("the positions the studio recorded in them"), next to the company's cap-table snapshot. Narrowing that by fund relationship belongs with how a holding is recorded (#2424).
- **Status codes.** `GET /api/lp-reports/:uid` keeps 403 for a published report the caller may not read. Its uid is not sequential, so it is not the counting oracle that the fund routes' 404 avoids.
