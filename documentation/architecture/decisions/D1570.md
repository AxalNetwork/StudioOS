### 1. Comments are their own store

The deck's notes (D905, migration 538) are the admins' thread to the founders. Comments are the venture team's, between people, and are a separate store.

**Migration 741**
- `deck_comments`: one comment, pinned to a card (its place and, from A8, its uid) and optionally one area on it, written on one version. A thread is its first comment and the replies that name it as `parent_id`. The assignee and Resolve are the first comment's.
- `deck_comment_mentions`: who a comment mentions, one row per person, with `notified_at`.
- `deck_approvers`: who must approve the venture's deck before it is shared, and the version each one approved. Removing an approver stamps `removed_at`; the row stays.
- `advisor_client_grants.scope_deck`, off by default, so no grant made before 741 opens the deck.

Project hard-delete (`services/projectTrash.ts`, #3747) erases the three tables with the venture.

### 2. Who (owner decision Q = B)

`routes/deck_comments.ts` decides on every request:
- **Founders**: the venture's owner, or an accepted owner or cofounder member, through `projectOwned` with write access. They read, comment, assign, resolve and name approvers.
- **Advisors and Operating Partners**: only with a live `advisor_client_grants` row that has `scope_deck`, not revoked and not expired, and only while their role is still `advisor` or `partner`. They read and comment; they assign or resolve only a thread they started, and resolve one assigned to them.
- **Everyone else is not found**: an advisor member without the grant, a grant without the scope, an investor, another venture's founder, and an Admin. Admins keep their own thread with the founders, the notes.

The people a comment can mention, a thread can be assigned to, and the founders can name as approvers are exactly those readers (`peopleOf`).

**The grant.** `advisor_grants.ts` takes `scope_deck`. An Operating Partner (`partner`) may hold a grant that opens only the deck's comments (`scope_deck` true and the three other scopes off). Any wider grant to one is still refused with the old sentence. `AdvisorGrantSection.jsx` has a fourth line, "Comment on your pitch deck".

### 3. @mentions notify once

A post names its mentions as user ids picked from the people listbox. The route:
- refuses an id that is not one of the readers, before anything is written (400 `mention_not_on_deck`);
- drops duplicates and the author;
- writes one `deck_comment_mentions` row per person;
- claims each row's `notified_at` with one UPDATE, and sends the notice only when that UPDATE changed the row.

The notice is `notify()` with type `deck_comment_mention` and category `mentions`: "[name] mentioned you on card [n]." It carries no words of the comment.

### 4. Assignee and Resolve

`POST /:id/comments/:uid/assign` takes `{ user_id | null }`; the assignee must be a reader. `POST /:id/comments/:uid/resolve` takes `{ resolved }` (true by default; false opens it again). Both act on a thread's first comment only; a reply is refused 400 `not_a_thread`.

### 5. The approval gate on share creation

`services/decks/deckApproval.ts` holds the rule, and both link writers ask it before they write:
- `POST /api/decks/:id/share` answers 409 `{ error: 'approval_needed', message, pending }`;
- `createDeckLink` (D902's one link writer, behind `POST /api/cycles/raise/deck-links` and `POST /api/decks/:id/investor-links`) throws `WriterError(409, message)`.

The message is pd33's inset: "Approval needed before sharing · Sam has not approved yet."

**An approval is of one version.** A link is frozen to one version (D902 §0.4), so `approved_deck_id` names the `pitch_decks` row the approver signed off, and a later saved version waits again. An approver who is removed no longer holds Share; named again, their earlier approval does not come back.

A database without migration 741 has no approvers, so nothing waits. Any other read failure refuses the link.

**Routes:** `GET /:id/approval` (approvers, `pending`, `message`), `PUT /:id/approvers` `{ user_ids }` (founders only, at most 10, each a reader), `POST /:id/approve` (only a named approver).

### 6. No AI anywhere in a thread

The owner ruled out chat. Nothing in `deck_comments.ts`, `CommentsSheet.jsx` or `commentsModel.js` reads or calls a model, and Eadwyn never replies in a thread. A test reads each file's imports.

### 7. The screens

- `frontend/src/lib/api/deckComments.js` (`deckCommentsApi`), re-exported by one line in `api.js` (D527).
- `deckV2Adapter.js`: `comments` is swapped to `through(() => deckCommentsApi.list(deckId))` and joins `SWAPPED`. Its contract is `cloudflare-worker/src/contracts/deckComments.schema.json`, checked against the real route's reply in `deck_contracts_d1511.test.ts`.
- `CommentsSheet.jsx` is the pd33 sheet: "Comments · card [n]" with "[N] open", the card's threads numbered from 1 with their replies, a composer whose "@Sa" opens the people listbox (a combobox with arrow keys, Enter and Escape), "Assign to" ▾, Resolve and Open again, and at its foot who must approve, with the founders' "Ask to approve" and Remove and the approver's own "Approve v[n]". The Q line closes it. Nothing is posted until Post.
- It opens from the card's ⋯ (`cardMenuItems`) and from ⋯ › This card › Comments. There is no header button.
- A comment started while one area is selected is pinned to that area.

### 8. The pre-share confirm

#3826 (E6, pd34's "[N] cards are not Done. Share anyway?") is not on main; it is open and blocked. So this PR adds the single pre-share confirm to `ShareDialog.jsx` for #3826 to join:
- Create link reads `GET /api/decks/:id/approval` first.
- With nobody waited on, the link is made at once.
- With an approver waited on, the confirm opens with the amber line. Share anyway is disabled: the Worker refuses the link until they sign off, so there is no way past an approver.
- The lines come from `commentsModel.preShareGate`, which is where #3826 adds its line.
- If the read fails, the link is still asked for; the Worker refuses a held one and its sentence is printed.

### 9. Departures

- **Pins on the stage are not drawn.** pd33 draws numbered pins on the card under a scrim. The sheet numbers its threads 1, 2, … and says which are on an area, but the Grid stage draws no pins. That needs the stage's own overlay and is left for a follow-up.
- **Granted advisors and Operating Partners have no page to open the sheet.** The editor route (`/raise/pitch/edit`) admits admins and founders only. The Worker admits a granted advisor or Operating Partner; a page for them to read the deck and its threads is not built here.
- **Assignment sends no notice.** Only mentions notify, as the issue asks.
- **Deck grants (D1587) are not gated.** The gate is on share links, as the issue says ("share creation"). A deck grant to an investor on a deal is not held by an approver.
- The card's ⋯ Comments item reads no adapter. `cardMenuItems` greys any item with an `adapter` key unless the adapter answers `available: true`, and a swapped row's `availability` is null. The item is wired directly instead.
