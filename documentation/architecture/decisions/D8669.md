### What the audit found

- The studio header showed six "New …" links and a second New webinar: `LearningStudio.jsx` puts the content table, the webinar list and curriculum coverage on one page, each with its own creation links.
- "All kinds" sat in one chip row with Published, Draft, Archived and Program-only: `STUDIO_FILTERS` in `StudioContentTable.jsx` mixes type, status and visibility, and only one applies at a time. The Programs column reads Not recorded (`programs: null`).
- Coverage correctly named all four founder courses as unreachable drafts (`learningCoverage.ts`), but only at the bottom of the page, away from the course rows.
- Publish posts at once (`StatusActions`) and the only server check is `no_lessons`; nothing checks what a lesson opens. A Super Admin sees everything through `canSeeLearning`, so there is no way to see a draft as a member would.

### What the canvas decides

- The studio has one h1, one primary (a **Create** menu, course first) and studio tabs. A journeys strip from curriculum coverage says which audiences are Not reachable or Missing (LST02).
- Type, Status and Visibility are three separate filters with counts, kept in the address, over the 46-item inventory at 20 per page. A computed **Who can open it now** column comes from status and visibility (LST02, LST02B, LST02C).
- Founders → Course + Draft returns the four drafts, each row saying why a founder cannot start it while keeping its real status and visibility (LST03).
- A selected course opens a workspace: an editorial checklist for readiness, access and review beside the learner-facing preview built from the summary, practical output, audience, cover and ten-row syllabus the builder already stores (LST04 to LST05). Each checklist row is Ready, Blocks publishing, Check, Not recorded, Unavailable or Proposed, and names its source on main.
- **Preview** changes nothing: under a black Preview bar it shows an eligible member's view (founder outside a program, founder in one, investor), and Start records nothing (LST06, LST06B).
- **Review and publish** is the only control that changes who can see a course. It stays unavailable while a check blocks and says why; confirm, result, the route's own refusal, unpublish and archive are drawn, and a visibility change on a published course goes Scope → Review → Result (LST07 to LST07C).
- New course (`?new=course`) groups the fields main stores, with one primary (Create draft), a live card preview and refusals beside their fields (LST08, LST08B). Empty, filtered zero, partial and full Unavailable, and every save state are drawn (LST09, LST09B).
