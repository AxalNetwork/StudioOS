## D4636 part 4 — A GP of record passes the positions licence gate for their own funds (#4636)

- **Status:** Built, part 4, 2026-11-13 (Worker only; no migration, no frontend change).
- **Scope:** the owner's decision of 2026-11-13: a fund's GP of record reads their own funds' positions and marks even without an investor licence. This lifts the last line of D4636-p3 §2 ("The licence gate is unchanged") for the five position reads.
- **Reuses:** `positionScope` (`routes/positions.ts`, part 3) and `managedFundIds` / `fundManageScope` (`services/fundGpAccess.ts`). No new access predicate is written.

### 1. The rule

Before this part every read in `routes/positions.ts` began with `if (!canViewLpData(user)) return 403`. The five reads part 3 gave a GP arm (`GET /api/positions`, `/marks`, `/analytics`, `/kpi-compliance`, `/:projectUid`) now begin with `positionScope(c, user)`, which is also their gate:

| Caller | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admin | Every position | Unchanged |
| Investor licence (holder, and a holder who is also a GP) | Held companies plus the GP arm (part 3) | Unchanged |
| No investor licence, GP of record of at least one fund (`managedFundIds` non-empty: institutional tier where the tier applies, GP of record, active company) | 403 | Admitted, scoped to the GP arm alone: their funds' rounds, those funds' and the firm-level marks, those funds' distributions, in the investor projection. No held rows. |
| No investor licence, no fund | 403 | 403, unchanged, before the company lookup on the detail read |

**`held` is forced to `[]` without the licence.** `investorHeldProjectIds` answers `null` for a caller who does not hold the investor licence, and `null` means "every row" to these reads. So `positionScope` never calls it for such a caller; it sets `held = []` and then reads `managedFundIds`. With no fund it returns `null`, and each read answers 403.

**The detail read gates before the lookup.** It used to refuse on the licence before looking the company up. It now computes the scope first, so a caller with neither licence nor fund still gets 403 for any uid, existing or not, and cannot tell which uids exist. An admitted GP gets 404 for an unknown uid, as an investor does.

**Writes are unchanged.** Recording a position, a mark or a distribution and editing a position stay `requireAdmin`. No other route changes.

**Every arm is evaluated per request**, as in part 3: a GP of record who is reassigned loses access on the next read, and without a licence that means 403.

### 2. Judgement calls

- **The list's `scope` statement for a GP without a licence** is still `HELD_SCOPE` (`confirmed_commitment`), over an empty held set, with `fund_scope` naming their funds. The response shape stays the same for every caller, and `fund_scope` already says where the rows come from.
- **The studio paywall still applies in production.** `/api/positions` is in `STUDIO_PREFIXES`; partners, advisors, mentors, investors and admins bypass `requireTier('studio')`, but a founder who runs a fund reaches these handlers only on the Studio plan. That is the paywall's rule, not the licence gate's, and this part changes no other route.
  Part 5 (`D4636-p5.md`, owner 2026-11-14) lifts that for the five reads: a founder who runs a fund passes the Studio gate on them, and on nothing else.

### 3. Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/fund_reads_d4636_p4.test.ts` (real schema via `loadSchema`, real routes, synthetic fixtures) covers these cases. A partner and a founder who are GPs of record without an investor licence read their fund's positions, marks, analytics, KPI compliance and detail, in the investor projection. They read nothing of another fund's company, of a company held by an investor through another fund, or of a firm-level round in which the GP has a confirmed commitment of their own. Each of those is 403 on the detail. A partner and a founder with no fund get 403 on all five reads, and also on an unknown uid. A GP who is reassigned gets 403 on the next read. An investor who is also a GP, a holder and an admin read exactly as in part 3.

`frontend/test/investor_portfolio_ip1.test.mjs` asserted `canViewLpData(user)` in the `/:projectUid` and `/marks` handlers. It now asserts that each handler opens with `positionScope` followed by the 403, that the detail's gate comes before the company lookup, and that `positionScope` asks `canViewLpData`, reads the held scope only with the licence, and returns null without a licence or a fund.

Each mutation made at least one test fail. With the code restored, all fourteen tests in p3 and p4 pass:

| The old licence gate kept on `/analytics` | p4 marks/analytics/KPI |
