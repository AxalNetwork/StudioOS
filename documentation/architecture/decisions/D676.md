## D676 — The four door pages, built to the owner's website canvas

Issue: #1550. Part of #1546 (D673). Source: `design/canvases/integrated/Axal VC Website.dc.html`, view DOORS, and the `DOORS` object and `doorFor()` in its logic script.

### What was built

- **One template, `pages/site/DoorPage.jsx`, takes a door key:** `founders`, `investors`, `partners` or `advisors`.
  - It is the canvas's per-door logic: the accent and its soft tint, the flow chips (the last one filled), the chapter helper and the plan helper.
  - Each page sets `--door-accent` and `--door-soft`, and `pages/site/doors.css` colours everything the canvas colours per door from them. The buttons stay the site's violet, as in the canvas.
- **The copy is `lib/siteDoors.js`,** taken from the canvas.
- **Each page follows the canvas's order:**
  1. the hero: the label, the h1, the "who" line, and the primary and secondary CTAs;
  2. the problem, and what you get;
  3. how it works: the chapters, with their flow chips, lists and "Coming soon" tags;
  4. the Eadwyn note;
  5. in the product: each item live or coming soon;
  6. plans, only when a plan resolves in the catalogue;
  7. the aside, when the door has one;
  8. questions;
  9. the closing band.
- **The doors, their routes and their images:**

  | Door | Route | Accent | Image |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Founders | `/for-founders` | `#6d28d9` | `lane-founders.webp` |
  | Investors & LPs | `/for-investors` | `#059669` | `lane-investors.webp` |
  | Operating Partners | `/for-service-partners` | `#2563eb` | `lane-partners.webp` |
  | Advisors | `/for-advisors` | `#d97706` | `lane-advisors.webp` |

  - The export carried a door image only for Founders. The other three use the lane images, the same scenes in a wider crop.
  - The hero image keeps the canvas's alt text. The closing band repeats it as decoration.
  - **The founders hero is the founders lane picture (owner, 2026-10-12).** The canvas gave the founders door its wide landscape, `door-founders.webp`, while the other three doors reuse their home-page lane pictures. The owner asked for the founders door to match them. The canvas's alt text already described the lane picture. `door-founders.webp` remains the backdrop of the home page's closing band and of `CtaBand`'s default.
- **The title and the description come from `data/productPages.js`,** which `lib/ogRegistry.js` mirrors.
  - The partners' title is now "For Operating Partners", the canvas's name and the nav's. `ogRegistry.js` must change with it, or `validate-og-tags.mjs` fails its drift check.
- **`ProductAudiencePage` is kept** until S1 points the four routes at `DoorPage`. Nothing else renders it.

- **Plans read the catalogue `/pricing` reads,** `data/pricing.js` (`FOUNDER_TIERS`, `INVESTOR_TIERS`).
  - A canvas plan names its catalogue tier. The page takes that tier's monthly price and its feature list.
  - The canvas's plan features are not used: the catalogue's list is the one the paywall enforces and `/pricing` shows. The canvas's Pro list also claimed Plaid and "every deal room", which the catalogue's five-room cap contradicts.
  - The plan's name, line, tag and button label stay the canvas's. Every plan button goes to the door's lane link: `RegisterPage` reads no `plan` parameter.
  - A plan whose tier the catalogue does not have does not render. A door left with no plan has no plans section, as the canvas's own `hasPlans` would have it.
  - What that gives:
    - **Founders: no plans section.** The canvas's plans are a four-step ladder, Validate, Build, Raise and Grow. The catalogue's founder tiers are Free, Growth and Studio. The Starter strip sits inside the plans section in the canvas, so it goes too.
    - **Investors: Starter and Pro,** from the catalogue's Free and Professional tiers. The canvas's Enterprise plan is custom-priced, with unlimited introductions and team seats. The catalogue's top tier, Institutional, is a fixed monthly price with 100 introductions a quarter and four seats, so it is not that plan, and Enterprise does not render. Setting its `tier` to `institutional` would show it.
    - **Partners and Advisors: no plans section.** The catalogue has no partner or advisor tier. Their asides already say there is no price grid.
  - The founders' chapter line "Starter · $0, forever" reads its price from the free tier.
- **The primary CTAs keep the lane links:** `/register?lane=founder`, `/register?lane=lp`, `/register?lane=partner` and `/register?lane=advisor`.
  - The canvas's investor button used `lane=investor`. It is `lane=lp`, as the task asks; both fold into the investor role.
  - The canvas's advisor page led with "Apply to advise" (`/contact`). The lane link is the primary now, so the two buttons trade places: "Create an account" leads, and "Apply to advise" is the secondary.
  - The secondary CTAs and the asides' buttons go to routed pages: `/spinout-lab`, `/pricing/investor`, `/directory` and `/contact`.
- **A claim that is not true of the code is dropped, not reworded.**
  - Where only part of a sentence was false, that part is cut and the rest is the owner's text.
  - Each cut is noted in `siteDoors.js` where it was made.
- **Chapters and questions are numbered by position,** so a dropped one leaves no gap.

### What was dropped, and why

- **"Labelled that way in the product":** nothing in the product labels a slide reviewer or an LP portal as coming soon.
  - It is cut from the founders' status note and their slide-reviewer answer, and from the investors' LP chapter.
- **Plaid** (investors): no Plaid integration exists. The worker verifies Stripe revenue only.
  - Dropped: the live item "Stripe and Plaid verification" and the chapter "Verification".
  - Cut: ", and numbers verified via Stripe and Plaid" from the second fact.
  - Not moved to "soon": half of it, Stripe, already runs.
- **KYB, conflicts and verification badges** (partners): `kyb_verified` is never set, KYB has no working satisfier, and no conflict check exists.
  - Dropped: the live items "Verification badges" and "KYB, conflicts, and contracts", the chapter "Trust", and the third fact.
- **Stripe Connect payouts** (partners): the partner Connect endpoints answer `stripe_connect_not_configured`.
  - Dropped: the live item, the chapter "Payouts", the question "How do payouts work?" and the aside's item.
  - Cut: ", and get paid through Stripe Connect" from the aside, and "verified, paid" from the chapters' title. What is left of the title is "Listed, requested."
- **Partner insights as views, leads and conversion:** nothing counts a profile view. `/partner/insights` reads founder demand.
  - Dropped: the chapter "Insights".
  - The live item "Partner insights" stays: that page runs.
- **Advisor matching by sector and availability:** matching scores expertise against the founder's skill gaps, plus values and skill complementarity. It reads no sector and no availability.
  - Dropped: the live item, the fact, the chapter "Matching" and the question "How does matching work?".
- **What Eadwyn does:** no AI task drafts request replies, edits a profile, schedules sessions or writes notes.
  - Cut: "and keeps your notes current" (investors).
  - Cut: "Eadwyn drafts replies to requests and keeps your profile current." (partners).
  - Cut: "It handles scheduling and notes so sessions stay about the work." (advisors).
- **The investors' Enterprise plan** does not render, so its sentences go too: "Funds investing at scale get a plan shaped around them." and "Enterprise is priced per fund."

### Revision, 2026-10-13: the whole founder product, real plans, and tokens

The owner, looking at the founders door's "How it works": "it seems we're talking just about the Spin-Out Lab, what about all the other features". And: "what the product plans include in simplified terms, include tokens as this is different revenue model".

- **The founders door walks the whole workspace.** There are nine chapters, in the founder's order:
  - the two ways in: the Spin-Out Lab, or an existing company starting straight in the workspace;
  - the four cycles, Validate, Build, Raise and Grow, each listing its screens by the names the founder's menu gives them;
  - Network, which keeps the owner's introductions chapter;
  - Research;
  - the founder's own profile: archetype card, venture-readiness score, public profile, co-founder match, Wellbeing and Academy.
- **"In the product" grows from 8 items to 24.** Each item names its route, under the existing guard. "Connecting your own AI assistant" joins the slide reviewer as coming soon: #1555 is built and switched off until its OAuth store exists.
- **The founder plans render.** The canvas's four-step ladder (Validate, + Build, + Raise, + Grow) was never in the catalogue, so the door showed no plans and typed "Starter" and "Pro" instead. Neither plan exists.
  - The door now shows the plans `/pricing` sells: Free, Growth and Studio, renamed and repriced the same day by D683 to Free, Validate + Build and Raise + Grow.
  - Each card adds a one-line `desc` saying in plain words what the plan is for. The price and the feature list stay the catalogue's.
- **The investor plans take the catalogue's names.** They were Free, Professional and Institutional sold as "Starter", "Pro" and an unrendered "Enterprise". Institutional now renders: it is the tier that opens Fund operations.
- **Tokens (D629), under the plans of every door that sells them** (founders and investors). The plan pays for the workspace; tokens pay for the AI:
  - every licence, Free included, has a daily and a monthly allowance, drawn on first;
  - past it, a top-up balance, one dollar paid for one dollar of AI use, that never expires.
  
  No allowance amount is typed: the Super Admin sets each licence's. Production has no override today, so every plan has the same deployment default, and no plan is said to include more. Both doors' FAQs gain "What are tokens?".
- **For the owner:** the "add tokens" promise holds only once `STRIPE_PRICE_AI_TOPUP` names the top-up price (D629). Without it the product shows "Top-up not configured" and no purchase button. As of this revision no top-up has been bought.
