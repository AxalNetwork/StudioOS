### What was decided

1. **The tour's steps are per licence and per viewport (`components/productTourModel.js`).**
   - The founder, investor, advisor and partner Studios are offered six steps:
     - Your Studio (`studio-modules`, with that licence's purpose line);
     - a module (`studio-tile`, the first one drawn);
     - Customise (`studio-customise`);
     - the sidebar (`sidebar-nav`, desktop only);
     - Settings (`settings`, desktop only);
     - the Account menu (`account-menu`).
   - An admin is offered the last three.
   - The onboarding-checklist step is gone with its panel. Its anchor had been missing since the panel was removed, so the tour opened on an unanchored box.
   - The old "settings" step's anchor was never drawn anywhere. Its copy claimed that Settings re-runs the tour and resets the checklist, which was not true of the sidebar's Settings. The step now names what that entry opens: the settings of the company or project in view.
2. **A step whose anchor is not drawn is skipped.**
   - "Not drawn" means no element, no box (`display: none`), or a box off the screen sideways. The last is the closed sidebar drawer on a phone. An anchor below the fold counts, and is scrolled into view.
   - The Account step is the one page-level step. With no anchor it explains the Account menu from the middle of the screen ("under your name at the top right").
   - The founder's phone band draws its account button with the anchor. The desktop Account menu's button is in `App.jsx`, which #4474 is editing, so it has no anchor yet and its step is page-level there.
   - The list is read again on every move, so a home that mounts late still gets its steps. When the current step's anchor goes, the tour moves to the next one in order.
   - Because the homes are lazy (D4622), a Studio licence waits up to three seconds for `studio-modules` before it opens. If it never draws, the tour opens on whatever is drawn.
3. **The copy follows the Account model.**
   - Licences, billing and account settings live in the Account menu.
   - Nothing mentions a licence switcher, an active licence or a checklist, and nothing says Fill.
   - The tour can be run again from Onboarding in the account settings (`/account`, `OnboardingSettingsTab`).
4. **The coachmark keeps the lightbox's modal contract through its helpers, not a copy of them.**
   - `ProductTour.jsx` uses `ui/lightboxFocus.js` (`inertOutside`, `tabbable`, `tabTarget`) and `components/useEscapeClose.js`, the shared Escape stack.
   - `Lightbox` itself is not used, because it centres its card and the coachmark has to sit beside its anchor.
   - On open and on each step, focus moves to the main action (Next or Done). Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside the card.
   - The arrow keys move between steps. Escape closes through the stack, so one Escape closes one layer.
   - The page behind is inert while the tour is open. Focus goes back to what held it, after the page is live again (an inert control cannot take focus).
   - The card is `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal`, labelled by its title and described by its body. It is portalled to `<body>`.
5. **The card stays on the screen.**
   - `placeTip` puts it right of the anchor, else below, else above, else over the anchor's lower edge, and centres it with no anchor.
   - It is at most 320px wide and never wider than the screen less two 16px gutters. It is clamped inside the gutters, and a screen too short for it scrolls the card itself (`maxHeight`).
   - It is placed again on every resize, and on every scroll in the capture phase, so the shell's own scroller (`[data-app-scroll]`) counts.
6. **The match-score module says what its scores are (`investorTiles.jsx`, the score module only).**
   - The source contract is `GET /api/dashboard`'s `ai_scored_opportunities`: `match_scores` rows (`SELECT ms.*`) joined to the project's name, sector and stage. Only the matching engine D378 deleted ever wrote them, and nothing writes or recalculates one now (`routes/matches.ts`).
   - The engine's upsert set `created_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP` on every (re)calculation, so `created_at` is the calculation time. It is read as UTC (`ui/parseStamp.js`) and drawn with "UTC".
   - `model` says how the score was made: `rule-based` reads "Rule-based", and any other recorded value reads "Language model". The model id is never drawn.
   - `score_type` is the kind of match: Deal flow, Co-invest or Referral.
   - The thesis answers a score was taken against were never stored, so Inputs always reads Not recorded.
   - Every row says "Historical" and the module says nothing recalculates its scores. Its count is "N historical", not "N scored".
   - The old card note credited the scores to Eadwyn. They were the retired engine's, so the note is gone.
   - Each missing part reads Not recorded on its own. The dashboard's answer time and the clock are never used.
   - `deal_id` holds the project id. The row's own `id` is no longer used as a project link, so a referral score (no deal) offers no Open link, where before it opened `/projects/<row id>`. A missing name reads "Deal name not recorded", not "Confidential deal".
   - The catalogue title (`services/studioModules.ts`) and its fallback (`studioFallback.js`) are now "Historical match scores".
   - The module stays in the investor's default layout. Its rows are real, recorded scores with a recorded time and method. Taking it out of the default would hide them from the investors whose layout is the default.
   - No new scoring engine was built.

### Unverified

- No real browser was driven: the mounted tests run on the repository's minimal DOM, and the coachmark's look at phone width was not screenshotted.
- Whether production `match_scores` rows have `created_at` and `model` filled was not read. The schema's default fills `created_at`, and the deleted engine always wrote `model`, so rows written by it should have both. The module draws Not recorded for whichever is missing.
- The quick-stats tile's "Avg AI match" (`quick_stats.ai_score_avg`, drawn by `investorStudioParts.jsx`) averages the same historical rows. It is not the score module this part was scoped to, so it was left as it is. It should get the same historical label in a follow-up.
