## D1553 — Remix: four layouts for one card, and the remove-a-block reflow

- **Status:** Built by the S07 session (#3800, deck v2 C5, part of #3716; 2026-11-01), to S1's final-canvas revision of the issue.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1499 (The Grid: the 17 structures, areas, `areasOf`);
  - D898 (the slide model and the save check);
  - D1604 (the v2 seam and the ⋯ menu).
- **Migration:** none.
- **Boards:**
  - PD-5 · pd5, "Remix this slide · 4 layouts, no AI charge";
  - PD-4 · pd13, ⋯ › This card › Remix this slide;
  - PD-9 · pd9e, "AI paused", whose "Still works" list includes Remix.

### Why

A founder wants other ways to lay out a card without paying for a model and without losing what the card says. The one-click run (dk-auto AUTO-4) also needs to drop an empty or unsupported area and reflow the rest, with no UI.

### What was decided

1. **Remix is deterministic and free.** `POST /api/decks/:id/remix` (`routes/deck_remix.ts`) answers from `services/decks/remix.ts`:
   - it runs no model, places no hold and charges nothing, so it answers with AI paused;
   - the same card always gets the same answer;
   - it writes nothing.
2. **It remixes the card as posted**, not the saved version, because the editor's unsaved edits are part of the card. The posted card is cleaned the way a save is (`sanitizeModel`).
3. **Every option keeps the card's evidence.** An option holds exactly the card's blocks, with the same content, bindings and values; only `grid` changes (`sameEvidence`). A structure that cannot hold every block is not offered. Remix never drops a block to make a layout fit; "Remove a block" is how a founder makes room.
4. **How a card fits a structure:**
   - each evidence block (a figure, chart, diagram, image, media or link, or bound words) takes an area of its own;
   - plain words may share one area, stacked by rows with the heading on top, when the structure is short of areas;
   - a heading goes to the structure's title area when it has one;
   - Big number's metric area takes the first figure, and a chart-first split (7/5, Sidebar 8/4) gives its main area to the chart;
   - leftover areas are reflowed away, so no option keeps an empty area.
5. **The four options** are the first that fit, in a fixed order, never the card's own structure: Hero + 3, Split 7/5, Big number, 2×2, Thirds, Halves, Quarters, Split 5/7, Sidebar 4/8, Sidebar 8/4, Z-pattern, Title band + body, Full. Team, Timeline band, Overlay and Appendix table are kept for their own cards. Each option carries the canvas's line where the card supports it:
   - "Fits your 3 recorded figures";
   - "Puts the chart first";
   - "Leads with MRR" (the first figure's label);
   - "Gives each proof its own cell".
6. **Remove a block, and the rest reflows inside the grid** (pd5). `removeBlock(card, id)` takes the block off the card.
   - When the block was alone in its area, the area goes too. The areas in the same row band share the band's columns (Runway removed: MRR and Customers widen to 6 + 6). Otherwise the areas in the same column stack share its rows.
   - When the block shared its area with words, the area stays and the remaining words take its rows.
   - The card becomes `custom`.
   - The function is exported for the one-click run (AUTO-4), so a zero-edit deck never keeps an empty area.
7. **"Use this layout" applies; it does not propose** (plan §0 row 5). It is one edit, held until a version is saved, and the sheet's **Undo** puts the card back as it was, unless something changed it since.
8. **Where it lives in the UI:** only ⋯ › This card › Remix this slide · Free. It is not a toolbar button or a per-area chip.
   - The sheet is `RemixSheet` in `DeckSheets.jsx`, and the editor opens it through `act('remix')`.
   - The client is `frontend/src/lib/api/deckRemix.js`, re-exported by one line in `api.js` (D527).
   - `deckV2Adapter.remix` now answers through it, and `SWAPPED` lists `remix`.
9. **Seam fix:** `moreState` greyed an adapter item whenever `availability()` returned nothing. That is exactly what a swapped row returns, so no swapped row could become live in the ⋯ menu. An item is now greyed only by an unavailable answer.
10. **Overlap with #3808 (C13):** Remix is built here. #3808 should reference this decision and not build it again (S1's revision, item 8).
