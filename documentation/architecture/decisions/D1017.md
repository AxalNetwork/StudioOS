## D1017 — Cap table part 2: the record of issuances, share classes, outstanding and fully diluted, proposals, history and a currency per venture (migration 573)

- **Status:** built by S12 for #2854 (part 2 of #2432), 2026-10-25.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `154cd86cf5`.
- **Split:** D1015 §6, from S21's integration in #2852. That PR carries D1015 and `documentation/audits/CAP_TABLE_MAP_2026-10-24.md`; it is not on `main` yet.
- **Owner questions read as A** (D1015 §5):
  - 1: the record is kept apart from the model;
  - 2: a co-founder proposes and only the owner records;
  - 8: a currency per venture.
- **Scope:** Worker only, plus one client module. Part 10 (#2862) draws the history, the proposals and the dialogs.

Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.

### 1. Why

On `main`, nothing recorded what was issued. The Holders list, the Planned round card, the deck, Build and Mercury all read the canonical row of `cap_table_scenarios`, which is a model (map §3). The canvas keeps **On record** (it exists, with a date, a source and a document) apart from **Modelled** (what a scenario computes). These tables are the record. A scenario models the record but never writes to it.

### 2. The store: migration 573

**`cap_table_share_classes`**, one row per class of a venture:
- `kind`: common, preferred or `option_pool`. A pool is a class, and its `authorized_shares` is its size.
- `authorized_shares` and `price_per_share_micros` are NULL when not recorded, never 0.
- `liquidation_multiple_bps` defaults to 10000 (1×), `participating` to 0 (non-participating) and `conversion_ratio` to 1, unless stated.
- The name is unique per venture.

**`cap_table_issuances`**, one row per issuance, per venture:
- **Kind:** founding, priced, safe, note, grant, exercise, transfer, conversion, cancellation or correction.
- **Holder:** a `cap_table_holders` row of the same venture (`holder_id`), or a name.
- **Class:** `class_id`. An exercise also names `from_class_id`, the pool it draws on. A transfer names its giver in `from_holder_*`.
- **Link:** `related_issuance_id` points to what a conversion converts, a cancellation cancels or a correction corrects.
- **Shares** are whole numbers; only a correction's may be negative.
- **Money:** `amount_cents` is in the smallest unit of `currency`, which is the venture's currency when the row is written. `price_per_share_micros` is the price.
- **Date:** `issued_on`.
- **Terms:** `cap_cents`, `discount_bps`, `basis`, `mfn`, `interest_bps` and `maturity_on`. Each is NULL ("Not recorded") when unknown.
- **A SAFE sent through StudioOS** links `safe_issuance_uid` and copies none of its terms (§5).
- **Document:** `document_ref` is `legal_documents:<id>` (through its deal's venture) or `data_room_files:<id>`, and must be this venture's.
- **Source:** connector_carta, connector_mercury, signed, document or typed.
- **Status:**
  - `proposed` changes nothing;
  - `recorded` counts;
  - `void` keeps the row, struck through, and the table refuses a void row without `void_reason`;
  - `dismissed` is a declined proposal, kept for the history.
- **Origin:** `origin` and `origin_ref` say who proposed it (owner, cofounder, admin, mercury, import, close) and the source's own key. A partial unique index keeps one live row per source item, and one per linked SAFE.
- **Admin's reason:** `correction_reason`, which the founder reads.
- **Who and when:** `recorded_by`/`recorded_at`, `voided_by`/`voided_at` and `dismissed_by`/`dismissed_at`.

**`cap_table_currency`** holds a venture's ISO 4217 code. A venture with no row reads USD, marked `source: 'default'`, because no company or project stores a currency.

### 3. Routes: a section of `routes/captable.ts`, under `/api/captable/record/:projectId`

Every handler calls `services/capTableRecord.ts`. The section sits before the `/:projectId` alias.

| Route | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /record/:projectId` | the venture's read gate | the record (§4) |
| `GET /record/:projectId/history?page=&page_size=` | read gate | §6 |
| `POST /record/:projectId/proposals` | owner, co-founder, admin | a `proposed` row: 201 `{ issuance }` |
| `POST /record/:projectId/proposals/:uid/accept` | owner, admin | records it; the body may fill what it left empty |
| `POST /record/:projectId/proposals/:uid/dismiss` | owner, admin | `{ reason? }` |
| `POST /record/:projectId/issuances` | owner, admin | records directly: 201 `{ issuance }` |
| `POST /record/:projectId/issuances/:uid/void` | owner, admin | `{ reason }`, required |
| `POST /record/:projectId/classes`, `PATCH …/classes/:uid` | owner, admin | a class; its kind never changes |
| `PUT /record/:projectId/currency` | owner, admin | `{ currency: 'EUR' }` |

**The gates:**
- **Reads** take the same gate as the rest of the cap table: `loadProject` then `canReadProject` (#2436, #2651). An advisor reads; an investor, a partner and another venture's founder get 403.
- **Every write** needs:
  - the Raise + Grow plan (`ensureTier(…, 'studio')`, 402);
  - the venture's write gate (`canWriteProject`, so an advisor gets 403);
  - the member-write attribution row (`auditMemberWrite`).
- **The owner** is the project's founder or a member whose recorded role is `owner`. Anyone else who passes the write gate is a co-founder. A co-founder's record, accept, dismiss, void, class or currency call is 403 `owner_records`.
- **An admin** may do what the owner does, and every admin write carries `reason` (400 `reason_required`, or `incomplete` with `missing: ['reason']`). The reason is stored on the row as `correction_reason` and in its audit row, so the founder reads it in the history.

**One audit row per write.** It goes in `activity_logs` (`captable.issuance.recorded`, `captable.proposal.created|accepted|dismissed`, `captable.issuance.voided`, `captable.class.created|updated`, `captable.currency.set`), in the same batch as the write:
- **Accept, dismiss and void** are UPDATEs guarded on the status they expect. Their audit row is an `INSERT … SELECT … WHERE changes() = 1`, so an Accept that loses a race writes nothing and answers 409 `not_proposed`.
- **Audit rows hold no amounts**: only the uid, kind, origin and reason.

**What a recorded row must carry.** A missing piece is 400 `incomplete`, with `missing`:
- **Every row:** a holder and a source.
- **Share kinds:**
  - all of them need `issued_on`, a class and shares;
  - priced needs a price;
  - transfer needs its giver;
  - exercise needs its pool;
  - conversion needs the recorded SAFE or note it converts;
  - correction needs its reason.
- **A SAFE or a note:** its amount, unless the SAFE is linked.
- **Refused outright:**
  - shares or a class on a SAFE or a note (`bad_shares`, `bad_class_uid`);
  - a grant outside a pool, or shares issued into a pool (`bad_class_uid`);
  - a holder, class, SAFE or document that is not this venture's (`unknown_*`).
- **A row that takes shares** (a transfer, cancellation, exercise or negative correction) must find them held: otherwise 409 `insufficient_shares`, with `held`.

### 4. The record read

Each block carries `available` and a `reason`, and a block that cannot be computed carries no number:

- **`by_holder`:** each holder's shares and options, and their shares by class.
- **`by_class`:** each class with `issued` (common and preferred); a pool shows `options_granted`, `options_exercised` and `unallocated`.
- **`outstanding`:** shares issued and held in common and preferred classes, with `common` and `preferred`.
- **`fully_diluted`:** outstanding, plus options granted and not exercised or cancelled, plus each pool's unallocated shares.
  - Unallocated is the pool's size minus the options outstanding and the options exercised.
  - A cancelled grant returns its options to the pool.
  - A venture with no pool class reads `unallocated_pool: null, pool_recorded: false`: no pool is recorded, which is not a pool of 0.
- **`instruments`:** recorded SAFEs and notes with their terms. They are never added into a count; they convert only in a scenario (part 3, #2855).
- **`issuances`:** recorded and void rows. **`proposals`:** proposed rows. **`classes`** and **`currency`**.

**Reasons:**
- `nothing_recorded`: no share issuance is on record.
- `negative_balance`: a void or correction left a holder below zero.
- `pool_size_not_recorded`: a pool has no size.
- `pool_overgranted`: more options are recorded than the pool allows.

A failed query is not caught, so the route answers 5xx, never an empty record. Each read is one fixed query that narrows by bound optional filters (`? IS NULL OR …`); no SQL is interpolated.

### 5. Proposals and their sources

A `proposed` row changes nothing until a person accepts it. Accept may take the fields the proposal left empty, which is how part 6's document read leaves the money to a person (#2431). Eadwyn never fills an amount, cap, discount, price or share count.

| Source | How it arrives |
| --- | --- |
| A co-founder (or the owner, or an admin) | `POST …/proposals`, origin from the caller |
| Mercury's SAFE requests (D840 part 2) | see below |
| An import (part 5, #2857) and a close (part 6, #2858) | `origin` `import` or `close` with the source's key in `origin_ref`; one live row per key |

**Mercury's Accept now lands on the record.** `acceptSafe` in `services/mercury/raiseFeeds.ts` calls `recordFromSource`. That writes a recorded `safe` row with:
- `basis: 'post_money'`;
- `source: 'connector_mercury'`, `origin: 'mercury'`, and the Mercury SAFE's id as `origin_ref`;
- its amount and cap;
- discount and date "Not recorded": Mercury's SAFE requests carry no discount, and their date is the request's, not the signing's.

The ledger row (`connector_fed_items`) now points at `cap_table_issuances`.

The model keeps its Investors · SAFE entry beside the record until part 3 makes the model read the record. When the model refuses the SAFE (no founder on the table), the record row and its audit row are taken back too (`unrecordFromSource`), and the proposal is handed back as before.

**A SAFE sent through StudioOS** (`safe_issuances`) is recorded by linking its `uid`:
- its amount, cap and currency are read from that row, and its basis is post-money (the table's only form);
- the issuance row copies none of them; sending them with the link is 400 `linked_terms`;
- `envelope_status` comes with it;
- its discount is "Not recorded", because that table stores none.

### 6. The history read

The read returns the venture's issuances (every status) and the cap table's own `activity_logs` rows (`captable.%`, and the gate's `captable_member_write_authorized`):
- **Order:** newest first.
- **Paging:** `page`, and `page_size` up to 100 (25 by default). Anything else is 400.
- **Total:** an exact `total`, the two counts summed. Another venture's rows and other modules' rows are not in it.
- **Activity rows** carry their action, actor, the uid they name and their parsed details, the admin's reason among them. They never carry an IP address or a user agent.

### 7. A currency per venture

- **No row:** USD, with `source: 'default'`.
- **`PUT …/currency`:** takes an upper-case three-letter code.
- **Recorded amounts** keep the currency they were recorded in. Changing the venture's currency changes no row already on the record.

### 8. The pin that moved

The test `frontend/test/captable_equity_plan.test.mjs` ("the panel says SAFE conversion is absent, and why") asserted that no migration declares a discount or MFN column. It now pins where they live:

The regex no longer matches a cart's `discount_cents` (152), which is a price cut, not a SAFE term.

The panel itself is unchanged. It reads Carta's per-account pools and vesting, not the record, and the conversion detail is part 3's. Its sentence "those terms are not stored" is true of what it reads. Part 8 (#2860), which replaces the panel, draws the record's terms.

### 11. Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/cap_table_record_d1017.test.ts` has 14 cases. It runs on a D1 built from the baseline and every migration, with 573 applied whole. The cases cover:
- 573 applying, and its CHECKs: a void needs its reason, a row needs a holder, and the currency is upper case;
- an empty record: every block says `nothing_recorded`, and none carries a number;
- a worked example done by hand, with every share kind, a SAFE and a note: outstanding 11,100 (common 10,100, preferred 1,000) and fully diluted 12,000 (200 options, 700 unallocated), by holder and by class, with the SAFE and the note in no count;
- a pool with no size, an overgranted pool, a class's kind not editable, and a duplicate class name;
- the gates:
  - an investor, a partner and another founder get 403;
  - an advisor reads and is refused writes;
  - the free plan gets 402;
  - a co-founder's record calls get `owner_records`, while their proposal counts for nothing until the owner accepts;
  - one audit row per accept, and a second Accept from a stale read writes none;
  - a member whose role is owner records;
- Accept filling what a proposal left empty, and Dismiss;
- void with its required reason: struck through, out of the counts, and `negative_balance` once a void leaves a holder below zero;
- `insufficient_shares`, cancellations returning options to the pool, a correction with its reason, and sixteen field refusals;
- the admin's reason, read by the founder in the history;
- the history: newest first, paged, with an exact total that leaves out other ventures' and other modules' rows;
- a linked SAFE read from `safe_issuances`, nothing copied, recorded once;
- the currency default, setting it, and recorded amounts keeping theirs;
- a connector item recorded once, a failed read answering 5xx, and the routes matched before the alias.

**The suites whose pins moved:**
- `test/mercury_raise_feeds_d840.test.ts`: the SAFE lands on `cap_table_issuances`, post-money, from Mercury, with one audit row, and a refused Accept takes both back.
- `test/_mercury_raise_fixture.ts` applies 573 and `esign_envelopes`.
- `frontend/test/captable_equity_plan.test.mjs`, as §8.
- `test/iso_bind_sql_columns_d162.test.ts`: `recorded_at` was one of D160's two undeclared names. 573 declares it, and the record writes it with `datetime('now')`, so the test now asserts that the derivation finds it clock-written on `cap_table_issuances`.

**Mutation check:** 34 mutations across the service, the route gates and Mercury's Accept, each applied alone. All 34 are caught. The two that first survived (a cancellation's sign, and the audit row's `changes()` guard) led to the cancellation cases and the stale-read Accept.
