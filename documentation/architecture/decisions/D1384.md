## D1384 — Compliance moves into Calendar: `/compliance` retired, the compliance calendar is a layer of `/calendar` (#3692)

- **Status:** built by S15, 2026-10-30 (#3692). Not deployed or live verified.
- **Basis:** `main` at `f9cdca6d`. The map of both pages, with file:line, is on #3692.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1205's Calendar and its source registry (`READERS`, `services/calendar.ts`);
  - D1208's `complianceDueEvents`, written and never wired;
  - D1211's item drawer in D1317's shared Sheet;
  - D1206's staff scope.
- **Migration:** none. **Store:** `compliance_events`, as before, written only through `/api/compliance/events`.

### 1. The source

`compliance_due` is one more entry in `READERS`, so `KNOWN_KINDS`, `gather`'s per-source status, the item read and the page's Kinds filter take it with no list of their own. This is the registration #3723's source registry builds on.

- **Whose deadlines.** A founder sees their own ventures, D1208's rule, narrowed to the active company exactly as `GET /compliance/events` narrows it. `ReadOptions.companyId` carries it, set by `/calendar/events` and `/calendar/item/:kind/:id` only. So every deadline shown is one the compliance writes accept.
- **No role widens a personal read.** The "Mine" calendar holds no venture's deadlines for an admin or a partner (D1208, D1209's feed rule). An admin's staff scope (`scope=studio|all`) reads every scoped venture's deadlines, read-only, through a `compliance_due` case in `scopedRows`.
- **Done deadlines.** They stay on their day on the page read (`withCancelled`, the page's own ask), so Reopen is reachable. Without it they are left out, as D1208 wrote.
- **Each item carries `compliance`:**
  - its state: `done` wins over the date, then `overdue` before today, else `due`;
  - the due day, type, jurisdiction and recurrence;
  - the owner where Grow recorded one (`owner_name`), else null;
  - its source.
- **Never in a feed.** `PAGE_ONLY_KINDS` keeps it out of `fetchUserEvents`, so no `.ics` file, subscription or provider copy holds one: a whole day has no time for `eventsToIcs` or the provider payloads to write. `/events.ics?kinds=compliance_due` is refused (400), and the whole-calendar feed lists `FEED_KINDS`.

### 2. The layer on `/calendar`

- **In the page.** `compliance_due` joins the Deadlines family with its own hue (`#57534e`, lifting to `#d6d3d1`) and dot (a flag, `0 50% 50% 0`), in the idiom the canvas's kinds use. The legend row reads "Compliance".
- **`?layer=compliance`.** It reads as `?kinds=` of that source's kinds (`LAYERS`). An explicit `?kinds=` wins, and choosing kinds drops the layer. #3723's settings can replace the map without touching a link.
- **The agenda row:** "All day", the state ("Overdue by 3 days", "Due in 12 days", "Done") and the venture.
- **The state's words.** The Worker's state decides the word; the reader's own day count only qualifies it, and is dropped where the two disagree (UTC against local).
- **The Sheet** (`CalendarComplianceSheet.jsx`) shows the state, the day, the venture with Open venture, the jurisdiction, Repeats, the owner ("Not recorded" with why) and the notes.
  - **Mark done**, **Reopen** and **Delete** each open an in-page dialog that says what changes first, then call `PATCH` or `DELETE /api/compliance/events/:id`.
  - Mark done names the next deadline a repeat adds: the Worker's +365, +91 or +30 days.
  - A refusal stays in its dialog.
- **New › Compliance deadline** (founder and admin) is `/compliance`'s create as a dialog, checked as `POST` checks it. The plan gate (402) is said in words.

### 4. Departures from the issue

- **Owner.** /compliance never showed one. The layer shows `owner_name` where Grow recorded it, and "Not recorded" otherwise.
- **Reminders.** The old page promised 30, 14, 7 and 1-day pings from a FastAPI service the Worker does not run. Only `growDaily` sends reminders, for rows with `remind_days_json`. The layer promises nothing, and that gap is unchanged.
- **Inbox links.** None pointed at /compliance. Grow's reminders already open `/grow/durable-company-building/:id`.
- **`/raise/legal-engine/compliance`** still embeds `CompliancePage`. It is a different address, so it is left as it is; the owner decides on #3692.
- **Partners** lose the all-ventures view /compliance gave them. The staff scope is admin-only (D1206). Flagged on #3692.
- **The legend's store names** (`ic_meetings`, …) are #3723's to replace. The canvas test pins them.
