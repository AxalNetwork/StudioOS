## D4631 — One deal-relative audience for every deal read (#4631)

Package 1 of 5 of the Deals follow-up. Worker first, plus one frontend state. No migration. Platform-wide authorization work stays with #4570. Sensitive evidence and verification are in the private review channel, not here.

**The policy** (`services/dealAccess.ts`). A caller's audience for a deal is decided from their relationship to THAT deal and the licences they hold (D4472), never from their primary role alone:

| Audience | Who | Sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `staff` | the platform admin, or a holder of the partner licence | every field; every investor named |
| `owner` | the founder of the deal's own venture | every investor named; no studio-internal field |
| `investor` | an invited or committed investor (the existing D1149 relationship) | their own rows; no other investor named |
| `none` | anyone else | refused, as before |

- **Order.** Staff wins over owner, and owner over investor. A founder who also invests reads their own venture as its owner and another venture as an investor. A second licence adds access but never changes what a given relationship may see.
- **Studio-internal fields** (the deal team's notes, a pass's note and who passed) leave only to staff.
- **The venture's NDA** binds everyone but the admin and the venture's own founder. An Operating Partner reads every deal but is an outside member (#2460), so the NDA binds them too.
- **Helpers:** `dealAudience`, `isDealStaff`, `namesInvestors`, `seesStudioInternals`, `ndaExempt` and `hasActiveDealNda`. `canReadDeal` is the same rule, written out so the auth inventory still sees its relationship query.

**Endpoints brought onto it** (`routes/deals.ts`, `routes/deal_insights.ts`):
- `GET /api/deals` and `GET /api/deals/:id`: the internal-field mask is applied by staff licence, not primary role.
- `GET /:id/commitments` and `GET /:id/activity`: who is named follows the audience.
- `GET /screening` and `GET /transfers`: anyone who is not staff is narrowed to their own deals, whatever their primary role.
- `GET /pass-analytics`: the taxonomy (counts, reasons, the matching deals) stays a shared aggregate; the pass's note and who passed leave only to staff.
- `GET /:id/documents`: behind the venture's NDA for the investor audience, as `team`, `market` and `traction` already were. The refusal is `403 nda_required`, and the deal room says why instead of showing "unreadable".
- `GET /:id/team|market|traction`: the NDA exemption follows the audience, not the primary role.
- The closing checklist's three writes need `canMoveRound`, as PUT, advance and pass already did.

### Built (part 2), 2026-11-09

The second PR applies the same policy to the surfaces part 1 left out. The items are the five S1 listed on the issue after #4918 merged. Worker only. No migration, no frontend change.

**1. One NDA rule** (`services/dealAccess.ts`). `hasActiveDealNda` (keyed by the venture's founder id), `hasActiveVentureNda` (keyed by project id) and `hasActivePairNda` (one named pair) are the same literal, bound predicate. An NDA is in force when:
- party A is an **active** account of the venture's founder, and party B is the reader;
- its `status` is `active`, so pending, partially signed, expired and revoked never count;
- it has no `valid_until`, or one later than now as SQLite's `datetime()` reads it. A date that `datetime()` cannot read is not in force.

Every reader of the venture's NDA now goes through that rule:
- the data room's `projectNdaActive` and `ndaActive`: the listing, a download, and the deck-proof file gate;
- the founder's "who can open this" record (`services/records/raise.ts`);
- the investor's evidence pack (`evidenceNda`);
- Research's diligence list and held rooms;
- a round call's room;
- the deal's documents and its team, market and traction sections.

The record, Research and the round call used to key the NDA to the account that granted the room. They now key it to the venture, as the data room's own download gate already did.

Two choices go with this. First, an NDA with any active account of the venture's founder counts; the deal surfaces had read only the first such account. Second, the founder-side counters say an NDA is signed only under the same rule: the grant row's "signed", the checklist's "opens for", the "who can see this" count and `readableRoomFiles`.

**2. Deck grants** (`routes/deck_grants.ts`).
- The founder's three routes are the `owner` relationship (the new `isDealOwner`), or the admin. A second licence never takes them away from the venture's founder, and a partner licence never grants them.
- The investors a founder may grant to are the deal's `investor` audience: an invitation or commitment, held by an active account that holds the investor licence now. Anyone else drops out of the picker, and a grant to them answers `409 investor_not_on_deal`.
- The investor read was already that audience narrowed to one firm (`deal_context.ts`). It ends with the licence or the relationship.

**3. Invitation inbox** (`GET /api/deals/invitations/mine`). Scoped to the verified active firm, as the list's `scope=mine` is: that firm's invitations, plus those that name no firm. With no firm selected, the caller reads all of their own invitations, as before. Each row now carries its `company_id`.

**4. The deal list's branch** (`GET /api/deals`). The branch follows the licences held, through the audience policy:
- deal staff read the whole list;
- a holder of the investor licence reads the marketplace, with `can_read` set from each row's audience (`dealAudiences`, a batched `dealAudience`), so their own venture's deals read as readable;
- anyone else reads their own venture's deals, which now also needs the founder licence.

The internal key that decides a row's audience is stripped before `serializeDeal`.

**5. The matrix.** Route-level cases on synthetic fixtures:
- `deal_who_sees_what_d1155`, 3 cases: the list for a founder who invests, for each single-licence reader, for a residual founder id and after a revoked licence; and the inbox per firm.
- `data_room_grants_2461`, 1 case: every NDA status, a lapsed or unreadable term, a deactivated founder account, another venture's founder and a second founder account. The listing, the download and the grant row are checked together.
- `raise_evidence_investor_d1265`, 1 case: the same NDA rule on the evidence pack.
- `deck_grants_d1587`, 3 cases: the owner relationship with combined licences; the investor audience, with a revoked licence and a deactivated account; a changed relationship.
- Updated: `round_calls_d653` (the room opens on the venture's NDA; its mutation probe was replaced), `expiry_gate_datetime_d124` (the pair statement now lives in `dealAccess.ts`; 19 normalised comparisons), `research_stores_scoping` (the import), `deal_privacy_2437` (the list's serializer line).

The list case reads rows by id, because the list's `founder_user_id` join repeats a row for each account that shares a venture's founder id. That repetition predates #4631; it is noted here and not changed.
