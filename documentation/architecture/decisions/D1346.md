## D1346 — Record a Session › Call: "Create the meeting for me", honest copy per platform, and Send Eadwyn AI disabled up front

S29, 2026-10-27, for #3554. It follows the meeting bot (#2577, D886) and Record a Session (D685, D878). No migration.

**This does not deploy the bot.** The owner saw "Eadwyn AI is not available on this deployment yet" because
`MEETING_BOT_RUN` is not bound, so `botConfigured` is false (`services/meetingBots.ts`). The deploy steps
are on #2587 and #1008, from `PRODUCTION.md` "Owner-only first deployment". What the owner described (a
link, then a bot in the meeting that the host admits, as Otter.ai does) is D886's design. This decision
fixes what the dialog says, and adds the missing "create the meeting" step.

### 1. Worker (`routes/founder_validate_meetings.ts`)

| Route | What it does |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/founder/validate/meetings/bot` | `botAvailability` (`services/meetingBots.ts`): `{ available, platforms: { zoom, google_meet, teams }, limit_minutes, price: { bot_microusd_per_minute, transcription_microusd_per_minute } }` |
| `POST /api/founder/validate/meetings/instant` | `{ provider: 'zoom' \| 'google_meet', duration_min?, topic? }` → 201 `{ provider, join_url }` |

**The availability read:**
- It makes the two checks `dispatchBot` makes, `botConfigured` and `botMemberEnabled`, for each platform.
  So canary is Super Admins only, and Zoom also needs the bot's own Zoom client.
- The price is D886 §5's: `botRate` for bot time, and Nova-3 per audio minute for the transcript, both at
  the member's own multiplier. An unmeasured price is null, never 0. Prices are read only when the bot is
  available.
- It reads no run, link or token.

**The instant meeting:**
- **Zoom:** `services/channels/zoom.ts` `createInstantMeeting`, a type 1 (instant) meeting on the
  member's own Zoom user, with the waiting room on, no join before the host, and no auto-recording.
  `meeting:write:meeting` was already in the Zoom scopes, so nothing new is asked for. The connector's
  permission line now names this meeting too.
- **Google Meet:** `makeMeetLink` (`services/calendar/itemRead.ts`), the same call D1211's "Add a Google
  Meet link" makes, with a Calendar event starting now for `duration_min` (30 by default).
- **The reply** is the join link only. Zoom's `start_url` (the host's signed-in link), the meeting id and
  every token stay in the Worker.
- **Nothing is saved.** The link fills the dialog's Meeting link field, and the session keeps it at Start,
  as a pasted link is kept (`POST /sessions` `meeting_url`; for a bot run, B1's cipher, D886 B7).
- **Refusals:**
  - 400 `provider_invalid` for anything else: Teams is paste only.
  - 400 `duration_invalid`.
  - 503 `provider_unavailable` when the provider is not set up here.
  - 409 `connect_first` ("Connect Zoom in Account › Connectors first, then create the meeting.").
  - A provider failure in `MEETING_ERRORS`' words (502 `zoom_create_failed`).
- **Tests:** `cloudflare-worker/test/record_call_create_meeting_d1346.test.ts`.

### 2. The dialog (`RecordLightbox.jsx`, rules in `recordSetup.js`)

- **Create the meeting for me** sits below Meeting link for Zoom and Google Meet (`createMeetingOffer`).
  - The link fills the field only when it is on the provider's own host (`createdLink`). A refusal is
    printed in the Worker's words.
  - Open the meeting opens the link in a new tab, with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. It shows for any valid
    link, so a pasted link can be opened too.
  - Unconnected, the button is disabled, with "Connect Zoom (or Google) to create the meeting here" linked
    to Connectors. When the connections could not be read, the Worker decides.
  - Microsoft Teams reads: "Create the meeting in Microsoft Teams and paste its link here. Axal cannot
    create a Teams meeting yet."
- **Send Eadwyn AI disabled up front.**
  - The availability is read once, when the dialog opens (`channelsApi.botAvailability`).
  - For a Zoom, Meet or Teams call that Eadwyn AI cannot join, a disabled Send Eadwyn AI is drawn before
    any press. It is described by: "Eadwyn AI can’t join calls yet on Axal. Record this device instead,
    or paste the link to keep it for later."
  - Start then records this device.
  - While reading it says so. An unreadable read keeps Send off and says "Unreadable … This is not a
    claim that it cannot.", with Try again.
- **Copy per platform** (`validateSourceWording.js` `recordSourceLine`):
  - With Zoom, Meet or Teams the line is `BOT_ENGINE`: "Eadwyn AI joins the call as a participant. The
    host admits it, and it posts a recording notice in the meeting chat."
  - The device line stays for In person and for the phone calls (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, This phone,
    on speaker), which this device records.
- **The Meet chip** is "Ready" when Google is not connected, and "Connected" when it is. It never says
  "Connect", because Eadwyn AI joins Meet as a guest. Connecting Google is offered only beside Create the
  meeting for me. Zoom's chip keeps Connect: the bot needs the member's Zoom authorisation
  (`obf_unavailable`).
- **Device options only for this device.**
  - Live captions, the final transcript, the language and the device estimate show only when this device
    records.
  - When Eadwyn AI records, the Transcript section shows its own transcript line and its price
    (`botTranscriptLine`): time and transcript per minute, and the plan's call limit. An unmeasured price
    reads "not priced yet".
- **Tests:** `frontend/test/record_call_create_meeting_d1346.test.mjs`. Updated pins are in
  `record_setup_d685`, `record_lightbox_d685`, `meeting_bot_ui_d886` (which passes an available read),
  `connector_wording_d757` and `interview_channels_d684` (the two new client calls).

### Connection refresh correction (#3663, S33)

An open Record a session form used to retain its first channels read after the
member connected Google Calendar in another tab. It now re-reads on window
focus and when the document becomes visible. Only the latest read can update
the form; a failed latest read clears the status to unreadable, and closing the
form removes listeners and prevents outstanding reads from updating it. Entered
interview details are preserved. Explicit caller-provided channels remain
controlled by that caller.

Meet creation uses the existing Google Calendar connection. Its connection link
now says "Connect Google Calendar to create a Meet meeting here" and goes to
`/account/connectors/google`. No token, scope, account ownership, Worker route,
or bot behaviour changes. Regression coverage is in the existing D1346 suite.
