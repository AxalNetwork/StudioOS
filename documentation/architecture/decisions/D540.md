## D540 — Credit reward rules and one grant service on the perk credit ledger

**Every reward in the ticket bounty epic pays through one service,
`grantCredits`, at an amount read from one table the Super Admin edits.** Issue
#1103, slot S08, part of #1101. Migration 381. The owner's answers to #1102 are
recorded here by Session 1 when they come; until then the amounts are S1's
defaults from #1102 question 3.

**What was true on main (`dc147f0c8`).**
- Credits entered the ledger only through `POST /api/perks/admin/credits`: any
  admin, any amount, typed by hand, with no record of who granted it beyond the
  ledger row and no notice to the user.
- No amount for any reward existed anywhere.
- `GET /api/perks/mine` sent each ledger line's note and kind. The page showed
  "granted by Axal" for every grant, and the header showed nothing at all when
  the catalogue read failed.

**What changed.**
- **Migration 381.**
  - `credit_reward_rules` (`key`, `credits`, `usd_value_cents`, `active`,
    `updated_by`, `updated_at`), seeded with #1102's amounts at US$1 a credit:
    - bugs 150 / 60 / 20;
    - `feature_shipped_high` / `_medium` / `_low` on the bug tiers (#1102
      question 2, pick B);
    - `task_approved` 10;
    - one `lab:<milestone_key>` row per Lab milestone.
  - The required Lab path from week 2 totals 1,000. Week 1's required
    milestones carry no row and pay nothing. `advisor_meeting_booked` and
    `cofounder_request_sent` both carry 50; #1104 pays only the first recorded.
  - `cap:bounty_monthly` (500) is a row in the same table, so every amount is
    one the Super Admin can edit. It is never paid as a reward.
  - `perk_credit_ledger.rule_key`, nullable with no backfill, names the rule a
    grant was paid under.
- **`services/creditGrants.ts`.**
  - `grantCredits({ userId, ruleKey, sourceRef, actor, note })`:
    - reads the amount from the rule at grant time;
    - refuses an unknown, inactive or zero rule;
    - reads the recipient's role and refuses `admin` (the Super Admin
      included) and `partner`;
    - writes a `grant` line keyed on `sourceRef`. The ledger's unique index
      makes a repeat a no-op, reported as `already_granted`;
    - records the actor: `logAdminAction` for an admin, `activity_logs` with
      `system:<source>` for a system grant;
    - puts an inbox notice in the user's inbox saying what they earned, its
      dollar value and why.
  - **The cap.** Bounty rules (`bug_*`, `feature_shipped_*`, `task_approved`)
    may not take a user past `cap:bounty_monthly` in a calendar month (UTC).
    - The check sits inside the `INSERT … SELECT … WHERE`, so two grants racing
      cannot both slip under it.
    - A grant that would cross the cap is refused whole, with what has been
      granted so far.
    - A revoked grant stops counting.
    - Lab milestones are not bounties and are not capped.
  - `revokeGrant` writes an `admin_adjust` line of the grant's negative,
    keyed `revoke:<ledger id>` so it happens once, carrying the Super Admin's
    reason. It logs `credit_grant_revoke` and tells the user why. It is refused
    when the credits are already spent, because the ledger never goes below
    zero (the rule `POST /perks/admin/credits` already holds).
- **`routes/admin_credit_rules.ts`**, at `/api/admin/credit-rules`, all behind
  `requireSuperAdmin`:
  - `GET /` lists the rules;
  - `PUT /:key` edits credits, value or on/off;
  - `GET /grants` lists rule grants with their revoke state;
  - `POST /grants/:id/revoke` takes one back.

  Each write needs a typed reason of at least ten characters and is logged
  with `logAdminAction`; a rule edit is logged with its old and new values.
- **`GET /api/perks/mine`** adds, on each ledger line:
  - `rule_key`;
  - `reason` (the note, else the rule's reason);
  - `source` (a ticket, a task, a Lab milestone, a claim or a revoke, read off
    the source ref);
  - `revoked`.

  It also adds `credit_usd_cents: 100`. No new route.
- **`PerksPage.jsx`.**
  - `LedgerLine` shows each line's reason, source and a "revoked" mark.
  - `CreditBalance` shows the balance with its US$ value. When the read
    failed it shows "Balance unreadable" with Retry, never a figure.
  - The ledger says credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred.

**After review (Codex on #1111).** D540 reached main inside #1113's squash.
These two fixes follow on #1111:
- **A paid source is answered first.** `grantCredits` looks up an existing
  grant for `(user_id, source_ref)` before it reads the rule or the
  recipient. A retry of an event already paid reads `already_granted` even
  after the rule is switched off or the recipient's role changes, so a
  webhook or job never mistakes a processed event for a failure.
- **1 credit is US$1, always.** A rule's `usd_value_cents` is now
  `credits × 100` on every edit. A `PUT` that sends a different value is
  refused (`usd_value_fixed`). The Perks balance and ledger convert at that
  one rate, so a rule priced otherwise would show the same credits at two
  prices.

Two tests cover them, and 3 of 3 mutations were caught.

**The owner's answers on #1102 (2026-10-06).** The four questions the bounty
epic left open were answered on the issue, one letter each, and S1 relayed
where each one lands.
1. **B: tickets stay in this public repository.** The mirror strips the
   submitter's identity. A security-related ticket goes to a private security
   advisory, never to an issue. Lands in #1105 (bug bounties, S09) and #1106
   (the triage protocol).
2. **A: feature requests earn no credits.** Approving one grants nothing. An
   approved suggested task still earns 10. Migration 381 seeded
   `feature_shipped_high`, `feature_shipped_medium` and `feature_shipped_low`
   (150, 60 and 20 credits); #1107 switches those three rules off, so nothing
   can pay them.
3. **A: the amounts as proposed**, which are migration 381's seeds:
   - bugs: 150, 60 and 20 by severity;
   - a suggested task: 10;
   - the Lab: 1,000 across the required path, and 10 per optional milestone;
   - the cap: 500 credits per user per month.

   They land in #1104, #1105 and #1107.
4. **A and B: launch forward, plus a one-time backfill.** Milestones
   completed from launch day earn credits, and the current cohort is credited
   once for the milestones it had already completed. The backfill is #1104's
   (D541, PR #1202): idempotent through `grantCredits`' `(user_id,
   source_ref)` check, so a second run pays nothing.

#1103's three questions above (staff, the cap, revoking spent credits) were
not part of #1102 and stay built on S08's picks.
