## D6686 — Formation case store: tables, one transition service, status projection, backfill of paid orders (#6686)

- **Status:** Built for #6686, 2026-11-15. Worker only; no screen.
- **Part of:** #6680, the global incorporation plan (plan key E6, Phase 1).
- **Builds on:** D601 (the two hand steps on a paid order), D585 (payment records the Lab milestone), D1415 Part 3 (filing links or creates the company), D235 (runtime DDL is a safety net for a declared object).
- **Migrations:** `202611151733_6686_formation_cases.sql` (the tables) and `202611151734_6686_formation_cases_backfill.sql` (one case per paid order).
- **Out of scope:** filings (#6694, E9), founder routes (#6703, E11), any screen.

### What was decided

1. **A case is the record of one formation.** `formation_cases` carries the intent, the track (`legacy_v1` or `v2`), the path, the stage and when it was entered, the party it is waiting on, a hold reason, the form, the catalogue version, the structure gate, the partner, HQ's owner, a revision and the close. `formation_case_orders` links it to its orders (`incorporation_id` is unique and a plain integer, with no declared foreign key, so a later rebuild of `incorporations` is safe; a retried payment adds a row). `formation_case_stages` holds the playbook's record of each stage. `formation_case_events` is the append-only timeline.
2. **One stage list.** draft, options_shown, chosen, structure_review, details, quoted, paid, preparing, ready_to_file, submitted, registry_query, registered, activating, handed_off. A hold is `hold_reason` plus `waiting_on`, not a stage.
3. **The stage projects onto the order's status.** This only applies when the case has an order.

   | Stage | `incorporations.status` |
   | --- | --- |
   | before paid | `pending_payment` |
   | paid | `paid` |
   | preparing | `packet_processing` |
   | ready_to_file, submitted, registry_query | `packet_ready` |
   | registered, activating, handed_off | `filed` |

   A hold and a close never change the status. The five status values and every past date are unchanged.
4. **One writer.** `transition(caseId, to, actor, payload)` in `services/formationCase.ts` is the only code that moves a stage or the status it projects to. It is one D1 batch, in this order:
   1. Insert the event, only if the case is at the stage and revision that were read, and the order is at the status that was read. The event carries `to_revision`, and `(case_id, to_revision)` is unique.
   2. Update the order's status (and the D601 fields the step records), under the same condition.
   3. Update the case, only if it is still at that stage and revision **and** the event from step 1 exists.

   Nothing between the statements changes the case or the order, so all three write or none does. The service reads `meta.changes` on step 3 and answers 409 `case_moved` when the case did not move. The order's new status is chosen from a fixed table of SQL text, one entry per status, and is never interpolated.
5. **Admin writes are logged.** A transition by an HQ actor calls `logAdminAction` as `formation_case_transition` (case uid, from, to, revision, order id). The two admin routes keep their own `incorporation_packet_ready` and `incorporation_filed` entries.
6. **Every path through the old status goes through the service.**
   - The paid recorders (Checkout and PaymentIntent) open the case at `paid` once the order turns paid. A failure there is logged and never fails the webhook; the packet job, or the next case read, opens it instead.
   - `startIncorporationPacket`, which the `incorporation_packet_start` job runs, is `transition(paid → preparing)`. A case not at `paid` is left alone, so a replayed job changes nothing.
   - `POST /api/admin/incorporations/:id/packet-ready` and `/filed` keep their URL, body, response and error codes. They are now `ready_to_file` and `registered` transitions. A v2 case is refused with 409 `case_not_legacy`, before any other check. D1415's company statements ride the filing's batch after the three.
7. **The backfill.** Each order at paid, packet_processing, packet_ready or filed gets one case: `legacy_v1`, uid `fc_legacy_<order id>`, at the stage its status projects from. Its timeline is synthesised (kind `backfill`) from `paid_at`, `packet_ready_at`/`by` and `filed_recorded_at`/`by`.
   - A `pending_payment` order gets no case.
   - An order with any other status gets no case and is listed in `formation_backfill_skips`, the migration's report.
   - No `incorporations` value is written.
   - Every statement is `NOT EXISTS` or `OR IGNORE`, so a second run changes nothing.
8. **Ensure case.** `ensureCaseForOrder` makes the same `fc_legacy_<id>` case for an order paid between the migration and the deploy. It runs on the paid recorders, the packet job and both admin steps, and is the read every later case read goes through. The uid and the order link are unique, so the backfill and the Worker cannot make two cases for one order.
9. **One open v2 case per project and per company.** These are partial unique indexes on `track = 'v2' AND closed_at IS NULL`. Legacy cases are exempt, because a project may hold several paid orders.
10. **Runtime mirror.** `ensureIncorporationsSchema` declares the four tables and their indexes column for column, as D235's safety net. A test holds the mirror's column lines to the migration's.

### Retention and erasure

- Events are append-only: no code updates or deletes a `formation_case_events` row.
- An erasure replaces the person fields of an event (`actor_user_id` and any person in `detail_json`) with a tombstone and keeps the event row, so the timeline keeps its order and its count.
- The retention period is "not recorded" until counsel sets it (owner decision 14 on #6680). Until then, nothing is deleted on a schedule.

### Not yet decided here

- **`formation_case_stages` is declared and not yet written.** The issue calls it "the playbook's eleven stages" but lists fourteen stage values. Which eleven carry a stage record, and which role is responsible for each, belongs with the playbook (E7 onward). This decision only fixes the table's shape: one row per case and stage.
- **Which moves are allowed** (forward only, `registry_query` back to `submitted`, and so on) is left to the engine. Today `transition` refuses an unknown stage, a closed case and a move to the stage the case is already at.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/formation_cases_6686.test.ts` runs over the full migrated schema. It covers:
- the backfill's count, idempotence, unchanged orders and skip report;
- the webhook followed by the packet job;
- two raced transitions, and an order moved under a transition;
- the v2 refusal on both admin steps;
- the admin steps moving the case and logging the admin;
- the projection;
- the v2 uniqueness index;
- the runtime mirror.

13 mutations were run: 12 were caught, and 1 is equivalent. That one adds `pending_payment` to the backfill's status list: the row maps to a NULL stage and `INSERT OR IGNORE` drops it, so no case is made either way. A 14th mutation (dropping the stage and revision guard) fails the race test. D601, D585 and the refund-review tests pass unchanged. `agent_transfer_d1528`'s source-order guard now finds the filing's batch at the `transition` call, which returns only after the batch commits.
