## D790 — Token pack offers: a window for the bonus, and first-purchase packs

- **Status:** decided by S1 for the owner, 2026-10-20 (#2215; part of D785, #2210, D-g). A money path: S1 reviews it for security before the owner merges.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `84f6e33`. Builds on D755 (#1870, #1905; migration 486).
- **Open with S1** (#2215, the questions comment): the three choices below marked **(asked)**. The PR builds the recommended answer to each; each can be switched.

### 1. What D755 already does, and what this adds

Grepping first (D689 D-t) showed that D755 built most of the bonus the issue describes:

- `ai_token_packs.bonus_microusd` exists, capped at 10% of face value by its CHECK (`TOKEN_PACK_LIMITS.maxBonusPercent`).
- The bonus quoted at checkout is stored on `ai_topup_purchases.bonus_microusd`, and `recordAiTopupPurchase` refuses a changed quote. So an edit after payment never changes a paid order.
- The webhook credits it once, as ledger kind `bonus` keyed `pi:<payment intent>`, beside `purchase` (`services/aiTopups.ts`).
- Refunds and disputes reverse credit + bonus in proportion, as one `refund` or `dispute` row (`services/aiCreditReversals.ts`); a shortfall is recorded, never a negative balance (D755 §8).

D790 adds the offer window, first-purchase packs, and the fields members are shown.

### 2. Migration 509

Five additive columns, no table rebuilt (`cloudflare-worker/sql/migrations/509_token_pack_offers.sql`):

| Table | Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ai_token_packs` | `offer_starts_at`, `offer_ends_at` | When the bonus is offered: start inclusive, end exclusive, either end open (NULL). ISO 8601 UTC. |
| `ai_token_packs` | `first_purchase_only` | 1: sold only to a member with no purchase on the ledger. |
| `ai_topup_purchases` | `first_purchase_only` | The pack's flag when the order was quoted, fixed like the bonus. |
| `ai_topup_purchases` | `bonus_withheld_microusd` | A quoted bonus the webhook did not credit (§4). |

`bonus_microusd` is not re-added: it exists (486).

### 3. Buying

- **The window.** `offerOpen` (`services/aiCredits.ts`) decides whether the bonus is offered now. `tokenPackQuote` quotes the bonus only inside the window; outside it the pack still sells, at face value. The quoted bonus is the one stored on the purchase row and credited.
- **First purchase only.** `tokenPackQuote` refuses a first-purchase pack to a member with any `purchase` on `ai_credit_ledger` (refunded or not). `POST /api/orders/intent` answers 409 with a sentence: "This pack is for your first token purchase, and you have bought tokens before. Choose another pack." No PaymentIntent is made.

### 4. The webhook

The credit batch's first statement handles a first-purchase order paid after the member's first purchase was credited (two checkouts racing). Its bonus moves to `bonus_withheld_microusd`, and `bonus_microusd` becomes 0. Everything after it reads the row:

- the `bonus` ledger row is not written;
- the balance rises by the face value only;
- the activity log records `ai_topup_bonus_withheld`;
- a later refund or dispute shares on credit + 0, so it never takes back a bonus that was never given.

The batch is one D1 transaction, so of two racing webhooks the one applied second sees the first's `purchase` row. The statement runs only while this purchase is uncredited, so any `purchase` on the ledger is another one.

**(asked) 3.** The member paid; the choice is between crediting the face value without the bonus (built), refunding through Stripe, or crediting in full.

### 5. HQ

`PATCH /api/admin/catalog/token-packs/:uid` takes `offer_starts_at`, `offer_ends_at` (an ISO time or null) and `first_purchase_only` (a boolean). An end that does not follow its start is refused (`token_pack_offer_window_invalid`), as is an unreadable time (`token_pack_offer_invalid`). Every change is audited in `token_pack_update`, with the before values. HQ › AI allowances' pack row shows the offer, and **Offer…** edits it (`pages/hq/TokenPacks.jsx`, `tokenPacksModel.js`'s `parseOffer`).

**(asked) 1. The bonus cap.** The issue asks for up to 100% of the pack. D755's CHECK on `ai_token_packs` allows 10%, and raising it means rebuilding that table, which the ledger and purchases reference. Built: the cap stays 10%.

**(asked) 2. The ledger kinds.** The issue names `pack_bonus` (`pb:<pi>`) and `rb:<refund>`. The existing `bonus` kind and the proportional reversal already do the same, idempotently; new kinds would mean rebuilding the append-only ledger's CHECK and its triggers. Built: the existing kinds.

### 6. What members see

`packsOnSale` (the member read `GET /api/ai-budget/top-up` and the public pack read) gives each pack `bonus_microusd` and `bonus_usd` as offered now (0 outside the window), `offer_ends_at` while a bonus is live, and `first_purchase_only`. The pack list (`components/products/TokenPacksSection.jsx`) and the checkout summary (`pages/CheckoutPage.jsx`) draw them through `lib/aiTokens.js`'s `packOfferLines`. Billing's "Buy tokens" (D792) and `/pricing`'s Models tab (D786) are not built yet; they read the same pack fields when they are.
