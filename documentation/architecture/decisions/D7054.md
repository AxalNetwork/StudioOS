### What the canvas decides

- One row per company profile. Two profiles with the same name stay two rows, each openable, told apart by the person who linked it and their role ("Linked by …").
- Known people counts distinct accounts. Two people with one name both count; one person with two ties counts once.
- No identifier appears in a label.
- A company that is only text, from a hand-added contact or an import, keeps its source and is never matched to a profile.
- Co-investors is live and reads "has a co-investor contact", from `relationship_type = co_investor`. Portfolio, Passed and LPs stay off with their reasons.
- The directory waits only for ties. Organizations makes no call to propositions, because reading that endpoint expires and generates rows.
- The directory and the company lookup each have their own Unavailable state and Try again; Partial is drawn.
- Search, open a company, open a person and close returns to the same search, view and focused row, and announces it. The ask flow reuses the existing confirm and quota states.
