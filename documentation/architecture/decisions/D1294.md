### 1. Daily views with no personal data (#2383 owner question 5)

- **The store:** `article_daily_views` holds one row per published article per UTC day: `article_id`, `day` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, checked) and `views`. Like `public_pageviews` (migration 054), it has **no column for an IP, user agent, cookie, account or referrer**, and none is read to count.
- **Where it is counted:** only on the public read, `GET /api/articles/:slug`. It is counted beside the all-time increment, in `waitUntil`, ahead of the edge cache, so a cache hit counts.
  - The insert selects the article by slug and `status = 'published'`, so an unpublished or unknown slug writes nothing.
  - The day boundary is midnight UTC.
- **Not counted:**
  - **an admin;**
  - **the article's own author;**
  - **an impersonated session.**

  The caller's token is decoded (`selectJwt`) only to tell these apart. Nothing from it is stored.
- **Previews:** an author's or admin's preview of a draft goes through `/api/articles/draft/:id` or `/api/admin/articles/:id`, which never count.
- **`articles.views` is unchanged:** one all-time total that counts every load, admins' and repeats included. The authors read names it "Views, all time" and says so.
- **Nothing is backfilled.** No day before migration 637 has a count.

### 2. Follower reach

- **The store:** `article_follower_reach` holds one row per publish: `article_id`, `published_at`, `followers` and `notified`. `followers` is the author's followers the fan-out tried, never the author. `notified` is how many notices `notify` wrote. **No follower id is stored.**
- **When it is written:** `notifyAuthorFollowers` writes the row after its loop, through both publish paths: the manual publish and the scheduled one (`publishApprovedArticle`). Writing it is best-effort, like the fan-out, so it never fails a publish.
- **Where it is read:** `GET /api/admin/articles/:id` returns each publish's reach, newest first, and the performance read sums it over its range.
- **Before migration 637,** no publish has a reach recorded. The read says so.

### 3. `GET /api/admin/articles/authors`

The route is admin only. It is registered before `/:id`, which would otherwise take `authors` as an id.

Each author with an article past draft gets:
- the counts by stage, with published, waiting (submitted and in review) and rejected;
- all-time views;
- followers: `follows` of kind `user`, without the author's own follow, or null when that read fails;
- **this week's submissions against `SUBMISSIONS_PER_WEEK`:** `{ used, limit, at_limit }`, over the same rolling seven days and the same space-form stamp the submit route's cap uses;
- the last and first submission;
- approvals: approved articles of those decided. Decided means approved, or rejected and never approved;
- the median hours from the latest submission to the decision, null with none.

There is no email in the answer.

The summary carries the canvas's tiles:
- authors with a published article;
- new authors in the last 90 days, by first submission;
- articles waiting from authors with nothing published;
- authors at the weekly limit.

### 4. `GET /api/admin/articles/performance?from=&to=`

The route is admin only. Both dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`, at most 400 days apart. By default the range is the twelve Monday weeks to today. A bad range is 400 `invalid_range` or `range_too_long`, with a sentence.

**Per Monday week (UTC)** that touches the range, cut to the range:
- **published:** by `published_at`;
- **submitted:** by `article_submission_log`;
- **approved:** by `approved_at`;
- **rejected:** by `rejected_at`. An article rejected and later approved still counts its rejection that week;
- **change requests:** `article_revisions` rows with reason `request_changes`;
- **views:** from the daily rows.

**Never a zero for a missing value:**
- **Before the first daily row,** a week's views are `null`, and `views_reason` says Not recorded and why.
- **The week the count started in** carries `views_partial: true`.
- **After the first daily row,** a week with no read is a real 0.
- **Views by sector and the top articles** are `null` until there is a daily row. They count only daily rows, never the all-time total.

**Also returned:**
- **The time to a decision:** the median hours from an article's latest submission to its approval or rejection.
  - A decision older than the latest submission, such as an approved article sent back by an edit, is not timed.
  - A change request is counted per week but not timed. Once the author resubmits, the submission it answered is not kept.
- **Follower reach:** the publishes in range with a recorded reach, their followers and the notices written.
- **`not_recorded`:** unique readers, read-through, sources and newsletter clicks. Each needs a reader-analytics decision first, as #3390 says. Daily views keep no referrer, so sources cannot come from them.
