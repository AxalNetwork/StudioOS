- **Status:** decided and built by S21, 2026-10-25 (#2425, under #2711).
- **Canvas:** the owner's "Trust center", boards TRU1–TRU20 with lettered sub-boards. The two artifacts are `2zg26b61pd4Xm2WP4K6kbP` (the board grid) and `2KSActnmAyVMcyYt59Dk55` (the page). Both were decoded with `scripts/read-canvas.mjs` and committed as `design/incoming/Trust center · Boards.dc.html` and `Trust center Page.dc.html`.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `751a056890`. The map is `documentation/audits/TRUST_MAP_2026-10-25.md`.
- **Migration:** none here. Parts 2 and 3 take 583 and 584.
- **Why:** the owner asked for a trust center for founders' and investors' data and money (#2425). Today, a signed-out visitor who opens `/trust` is sent to `/login`. This decision is the integration: the addresses, the public frame, the facts the code shows, and the doors. The rest is seven slot tasks (§6).

### 1. Where it lives

- **The public part: `/trust`** (Overview), `/trust/security`, `/trust/data`, `/trust/subprocessors`, `/trust/documents` and `/trust/disclosure`.
  - Each route renders `pages/site/TrustCenter.jsx` in the public site frame (`SitePage`), with no guard.
  - All six paths are in `isPublicPath` (`lib/api.js`), so a 401 on them never sends a visitor to `/login`.
  - The tabs are literal links. `tabOf` maps any other address under `/trust` to the overview.
- **The member's part: `/trust/me`.** It is today's `TrustCenterPage`, unchanged, behind the same six-role guard (admin, founder, partner, investor, advisor, exploring) and the investor workspace frame.
  - A signed-in member sees "Your trust" in the public tab row, linking to `/trust/me`.
- **The doors** now open `/trust/me`:
  - the account menu;
  - the phone account sheet (`ui/phone/AccountSheet.jsx`, `pages/account/accountPhone.js`);
  - the investor sidebar (`sidebarConfig.js`) and the shell rows (`workspaces/shellConfig.js`);
  - Settings' compliance bridge;
  - Help's "What we can see" (`pages/docs/DocsLayout.jsx`);
  - the Spin-Out Lab LP workspace;
  - the founder's team paperwork strip.
- **The site footer** (`LEGAL_FOOTER_LINKS`, `lib/siteLinks.js`) gains "Trust center" and "Status".
- **Owner question 1 is read from the canvas:** answer A. The public trust center is `/trust`, and every signed-in door opens `/trust/me` (TRU1 signed out, TRU18a "Signed out · /trust/me").
