### 3. Built here: the top articles

The page canvas ranks Performance's top articles by views all time, with views per day since publish. Until now they were ranked by the range's daily rows, and drawn as "Not recorded" before the first one.

- **Worker:** `services/articleMetrics.ts`, `GET /api/admin/articles/performance`.
  - `top_articles` lists every published article, ranked by `articles.views`. This is the all-time count Authors already reads (D1294). Ties go to the newer publish. There are ten at most.
  - Each article carries `views_all_time` and `views_per_day`. `views_per_day` comes from `perDaySincePublish`: views over the whole days since publish, with the first day counted as one, to one decimal place. With no publish date it is null.
  - `top_articles_of` counts the published articles, for "1–10 of N".
  - The period does not change them. Views by sector still come from the daily rows.
- **Frontend:** `contentQueue/statsModel.js` and `PerformanceTab.jsx`.
  - The columns, with "Views, all time" marked as the sort.
  - "1–N of M" beside the heading.
  - On a phone, the card line gives both figures.
  - With nothing published, it reads "No article is published yet."
