### 1. What a phone is here

Below 640px. That is Tailwind's `sm`, where these pages' `max-sm:` classes already stacked the table rows. `PHONE_BELOW_SM` is `(max-width: 639px)`, read through `usePhoneWidth`.

It is not the lens's 1024px rule (`PHONE_QUERY`). A tablet keeps the table rows, so the cards and the classes change at one width.

### 2. Accounts and one account (BNK12a, BNK12b, BNK12d)

- **Add an account comes first and runs the full width.** Structure of the Fund follows it, so both stay reachable. The entity's own "Add an account for …" is full width too.
- **Each account is a card** (`AccountCard`). It shows, in the row's own words and cells:
  - the entity;

  - Its bar holds Reveal full number…, the full width beside Close. While the number shows, the bar holds Hide now instead (BNK12d).
  - The desktop footer is unchanged.

### 3. Dialogs and Cash

- **Dialogs.** Every Bank accounts dialog is a bottom sheet on a phone (`phone="sheet"`, D1014), with Cancel and the change side by side.

### 4. One shared fix: Lightbox's phone margin

On a real phone at 390 wide, every Lightbox kept a 20px margin. Its layer class was written `max-sm:p-0` directly followed by the `${…}` interpolation, and Tailwind's scanner never read `max-sm:p-0` as a class, so the CSS never held it.

A space before the interpolation fixes it, for every sheet in the app: D1014's bottom sheets and every full-screen dialog. The test fails if any `max-sm:` class in Lightbox is glued to an interpolation again.
