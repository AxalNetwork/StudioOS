### What the canvas decides

- Report detail gains a section, "Asks in this update". Each ask shows its kind in words, its text as written, who answered it, a capital ask's round call, Promise support and Open the update.
- Promise support opens Value-add's Log support card filled from the ask: the company is fixed, the kind is mapped (editable), What happened is the ask's text, and hours and outcome stay blank. A confirmation and a receipt follow.
- The ledger row records that it answers an ask, and later reads Delivered or Withdrawn.
- The founder's `/updates/:uid` shows "[Investor] promised support · [kind] · [date]", and later Delivered. Replies and Ask again are unchanged.
- Edge states: no asks, a report, asks only on an earlier update, already answered, already promised by you, and a capital ask with no call.
- Unavailable is the failed-read word. Refused covers a reader who is not a recipient, has no Investor licence, or does not hold the company.
- There is no new route. Eadwyn proposes only from the ask's own text.
