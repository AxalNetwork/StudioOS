## D6237 — GP corrections and safe removal of LP records (#6237)

The Worker/client portion is independent of the Investor Fund v2 form. A separate
registered router composes before `funds.ts`, preserving the shared fund routes.
PATCH and DELETE both require the existing GP, institutional tier, active-company
and `fund.write` gates. A foreign LP and a missing LP return the same 404.

PATCH accepts only name, email, commitment_amount (legacy USD major units) and
status. USD commitments are parsed exactly through the shared money layer;
sub-cent values are refused instead of rounding a lower commitment across a call floor.
It refuses invalid values and unknown keys rather than silently dropping
fields. Contact corrections belong to the fund register; linked account identity
is unchanged. The register prefers the LP contact over its account fallback.

A commitment reduction is checked against the sum of every existing LP call
line, with integer cents preferred and legacy dollars rounded to cents. This
includes historical lines, conservatively retaining what has already been called.
The predicate is inside the UPDATE, so a new line after the initial read cannot
bypass it. Equality and a legitimate zero with no calls are allowed.

The current LP table has no soft-delete columns or established soft-delete
pattern. DELETE is permitted only with no call line and no receipt, checked inside
the DELETE. The append-only activity log preserves LP id, fund, actor and time.
Mutation, activity row and deletion recount share one D1 batch and roll back
together if any statement fails. No contact values are copied into audit metadata.
The existing best-effort admin act log remains additional to this domain audit.

No migration, production write or form is included. The eventual PR says Part of
#6237. Validation and any deployment limitations are recorded on the PR; synthetic
SQLite fixtures are evidence of local behavior, not deployment evidence.
