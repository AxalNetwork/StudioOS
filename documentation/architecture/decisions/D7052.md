### What the canvas decides

- The populated company list leads; the "Nothing to roll up yet" card goes. A source line counts contacts read, with a company and without one.
- A type filter (Client, Prospect, Referral source, Mixed, Not recorded) carries counts that add up to the company total.
- Mixed rows give a plain breakdown of who says what. A type with unset contacts reads Partial, "3 of 4 set".
- A company opens to its people, each with the company as typed; the Person Sheet edits one contact.
- Changing a group's type has four steps: Choose stages a value (Clear the type included), Review shows every contact current → proposed, an explicit button commits ("Apply to 3 contacts", "Clear for 3 contacts"), and the result reports the server's count.
- Outcomes are told apart: Saved, Not saved (nothing changed), Outcome unknown (the group reloads before anything is offered), and Group changed since review (nothing saved, a new preview).
- Back on the company after a save, search, filter and focus are restored. Desktop uses a dialog, phone a sheet, with Escape, focus containment and focus return.
- Headcount, revenue and engagement sourced are not shown: no field holds them.
