## D6078 — Export Capital relationships rows (#6078)

Pipeline and Commitments offer **Export this view** in their CRL16a More menus.
The browser serializes the current venture's loaded rows. The filename includes
the row count. This uses the existing reads and adds no endpoint or audit write.
The existing staff support view mounts neither export menu. #5074 owns the
future shared capability contract.

Unrecorded cells are empty and a recorded zero is preserved. Fit and last
contact are empty when absent from the prospect read. Close stays empty when a
commitment line has no recorded close in its read. Text beginning with a
spreadsheet formula prefix is escaped before CSV quoting.
