### What the canvas decides

- The page reads one startup, chosen in the project switcher. A scope strip counts organizations, people with an organization, and contacts with none, and links to the relationship book.
- The source is named: "the organization typed on each project contact, as entered". Spelling variants stay as separate rows.
- Each row shows the organization, its people, the strongest recorded tie and Last record activity. Last interaction is kept for a real interaction, because any edit stamps `last_activity_at`.
- When several people share the strongest rating, the row says so and Inspect lists them.
- The table range (1–10 of 41) is the page; the figures above it count the whole startup. Counts read Counting until every page is read.
- Search opens a company's people in place. A person opens the contact sheet, and Back restores startup, search, open company, scroll and focus.
- Export names its scope: the search result across all pages, or the whole startup, with contacts without an organization as an option.
- A missing organization is fixed in the relationship book with the existing inline editor.
- States: loading, switching startup with a read pending (the late answer is dropped), no startup, an empty startup, contacts without an organization, no match with Clear filters, a failed read with Try again and no zeros, and a failed second page.
