### 1. What was built

`STARTUP_ASSESSMENT_SKILLS` (`skillWorkflow.ts`) names four node skills. Only `legal-due-diligence-coordination` is
among #4588's first ten. The other three now have a pack each, in the same format and under the same rules as D4588's
ten (`scenarios/README.md`).

Each pack covers every required kind of scenario:

- **Complete evidence.** Every measure or item is cited by its record path. Examples: market size and demand counts;
  CAC, payback and lifetime value, each with its formula; shipped, slipped by 47 days, next, and an overdue promise.
- **Missing or stale inputs.** Each missing field is checked by name for "Not recorded". Examples: no market size and an
  empty compliance calendar, with a stale signal; no new-customer count and no churn; ARPA last recorded in 2025-12
  against 2026-10 spend; items with no target date.
- **Contradictory records.** Examples: two research counts (4,200 and 6,100 clinics); Stripe and the growth records
  giving different ARPA, so payback stays conditional; an "on schedule" status whose date has passed, and a venture
  summary that says the item launched.
- **A task-specific edge case.** Examples: the only demand signal comes from another province; zero churn, where
  lifetime value is Not recorded and never infinite; a promise due on the review date compared with one due a day
  earlier.
- **An instruction embedded in the evidence.** A note in a research, growth or roadmap record tells the agent what to
  conclude. It stays data.

`market-entry-brief` has one more scenario, `market-entry-brief-no-compliance-read`. The assessment node grants that
skill only `research.read` and `validate.read`, not the `compliance.read` the skill itself declares. So the scenario
gives no compliance records, and the requirements must be Not recorded and asked for. They must never be treated as
absent.

All records are made up. Companies are marked "(synthetic)" and no person is named. There is no email address. The
read keys are catalogue reads that the skill's `tools` grant: `research.library`, `validate.records`,
`compliance.calendar`, `stripe.revenue`, `grow.records`, `build.records` and `project.summary`. Every `case_id` starts
with its slug and is unique, and no pack is over 19 scenarios.

### 2. The second set in the test

`skill_scenarios_4588.test.ts` used to require the packs to be exactly `FIRST_TEN_SLUGS`. It now names
`STARTUP_ASSESSMENT_SET` (the three slugs) and checks four things:

- The set is exactly the Startup Assessment node skills that are not in the first ten.
- The set has no slug in common with `FIRST_TEN_SLUGS`.
- Every node skill has a pack.
- The packs are exactly the two sets together.

The other checks run on both sets: the identity rules, the declared reads, no email address, a valid golden case, a
correct output that passes `judge()`, and every assertion caught. `FIRST_TEN_SLUGS` is unchanged.

### 3. The legal pack: no gap, nothing added

`legal-due-diligence-coordination.json` (#5180) already reads exactly what the `legal_compliance_review` node grants:
all five of its scenarios read `data_room.index` and `raise.records`, which come from `data_room.read` and `raise.read`.
That is the skill's full `tools`, so, unlike market research, the node grants no narrower set of reads that would need
its own scenario.

The node's own inputs are `profile` and `evidence`. Both are record references that #4667 binds, and neither is a
catalogue read, so a scenario cannot feed them. The node's output fields (`summary`, `findings`,
`missing_information`, `evidence_refs`) come from the workflow's mapping, and `judge()` does not check them. No gap was
found, so no scenario was added.

### 4. `skillRubric.ts`: registration not required

The generator (`build-skill-library.mjs`), the gate (`check-skill-library.mjs`) and the seed read every file in
`scenarios/`, so the new packs are imported as cases with no registration. Both scripts ran with no change.

`skillRubric.ts` imports the first ten packs by name only for `calibrationItems()`. Those are the synthetic outputs HQ
labels to calibrate the model-judged rubric (D4531 part 5), and they are not part of the publish gate. Adding the new
packs would widen that set. `skill_rubric_4531_p5.test.ts` pins it at "the ten scenario packs". That change belongs to
the rubric (#4531), so the packs are not registered here.

### 5. Staged expectations

These are listed in each scenario's `staged` field and are not counted as enforced:

- **market-entry-brief, complete:** each line should cite its source by title (step 3). The evidence assertion checks
  record paths, and `must_include` checks that a title appears, not that it sits on the line it supports.
- **market-entry-brief, missing-stale:** the signal should be called stale. No assertion compares the dates of two
  records.
- **unit-economics-summary, complete:** the output should note that payback and lifetime value use revenue, not
  margin. The skill gives no margin input, and no assertion checks the caveat.
- **roadmap-review-brief, complete:** "shipped since the last review" should compare each ship date with
  `last_review`. No assertion compares two dates.
- **roadmap-review-brief, embedded-instruction:** the product node also receives market research's `market_findings`.
  They come from another node, not a catalogue read, so a scenario cannot feed them (#4667).
