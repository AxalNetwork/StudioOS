## D4652 — Pricing disclosures from the catalogue, not a second AI balance (#4652)

**Status:** Source-backed disclosure implementation proposed for review on 2026-11-11. **Not** approval to change Stripe prices, credit grants, taxes or legal terms. The account and commercial review checks below remain outstanding.

### Sources inspected

- `frontend/src/lib/pricingModel.js` reads the `/api/public/plans-and-packs` catalogue; `priceOf(plan, 'month'|'year')` uses actual price rows with `allowance_microusd` and `unit_amount`. No invented annual amount.
- `frontend/src/pages/PricingPage.jsx` renders `planCards` by the billing selector and uses the same catalogue rows for visitor/member checkout. D4651 preserves a chosen plan/interval across auth; its actual quote is checked again on return.
- `frontend/src/lib/planAllowance.js` and `cloudflare-worker/src/services/aiBudget.ts`: no included funded AI allowance without a paid member plan; monthly allowance is distinct from bought AI tokens, and reads distinguish unknown from zero. `frontend/src/lib/aiTokens.js` keeps bought tokens as a nonexpiring separate balance. Introduction quotas and Perks credits are not tokens.
- `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/billing.ts` `/subscription/cancel` schedules `cancel_at_period_end`; resumption and cancellation outcomes must be read back from the subscription. No application-side immediate refund is implied. `frontend/src/pages/TermsPage.jsx` §11 (current effective text) speaks of renewal and written cancellation before renewal, plus refunds as required by law. #5511 owns reconciliation/approved legal text; this PR does not modify Terms.
- `frontend/src/lib/sitePricing.js` previously said every plan includes AI tokens and that AI is included with every plan, while Free requires purchased tokens. Both are corrected in display copy.

### What changes

The public Price page shows a *catalogue-derived* per-plan comparison of included monthly AI allowance when the monthly vs yearly price rows both have recorded allowances, plus the full yearly charge. If any row or allowance is absent, no amount is fabricated, and the existing price/availability states remain. The Billing selector names the comparison. The public Hero/AI band/FAQ distinguish (1) AI feature access, (2) funded monthly paid-plan allowance, (3) purchased AI token balance, (4) introduction quotas and (5) Perks; clarify possible differing annual allowance, billing, renewal, period-end cancellation and transaction review without hard-coding a price or changing a grant. Known calendar-month reset and token expiry facts remain separate.

### Outstanding owner/counsel and test gates

1. **Consumer and tax/legal disclosure:** #5511 approves the effective Terms/refund/cancellation and required market withdrawal/consumer-rights text. No blanket no-refund or withdrawal waiver is approved here.
2. **Entitlement matrix:** #4653's current source/audit and #4638 Help must reconcile first-month and cancellation/downgrade access, included seats, multi-licence grants, document/shared-link access and product-specific refund behavior; no independent limits invented.
3. **Hosted purchase journeys:** in an approved test Stripe environment, verify Free, monthly and annual sign-up → checkout quote/taxes → receipt → cancellation → access after period end, including timezone and lifecycle states; store redacted evidence. No financial operation or production account was exercised from this PR.
4. **Unknown conditions:** if public catalogue is unreadable, model allowance comparison is suppressed, not shown as $0. A member's current balance must be read from the own account and usage endpoints.

### Why no new product policy?

An annual discount naturally reduces the *monthly-equivalent* price that funds the 25% monthly allowance. Giving identical allowances, changing the starter grant, altering monthly/annual amounts or accepting a different refund policy requires a separate owner decision and billing tests; this PR does **not** authorize any such change.
