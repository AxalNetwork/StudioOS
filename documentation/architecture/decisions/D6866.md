### Where the workspace lives

The workspace lives at `/deals/closing?deal=<id>`, not on a new route. A deal row's Open lands there, and "‹ Deals at closing" clears the parameter. The route's guard (`admin`, `investor`) is unchanged. Opening it to the round's lead Operating Partner (Q1 A) is a route change that stays #5912's.

### What it draws, from reads main already serves

| Tab | Read | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Checklist | `GET /deals/:id/closing-checklist` (owner and due date from D6156) | Done, Pending, Overdue, Blocked and Skipped, each a word and a glyph. Overdue is a Pending item whose due date has passed; an item with no due date is never Overdue. The tab reads "done of total". |
| Paper | `GET /legal/esign`, scoped at its route, filtered to the deal | Executed (completed only), Partly signed, Sent, Draft, Voided and Rejected. Signatures are counted per envelope. An investor sees their own paper. |
| Conditions | `GET /ic/conditions`, every status, decision-scoped (D461) | Read only. Open first, then Met, then Waived. "n open conditions hold every transfer on this deal", with Open in Investment committee as the one action. |
| Transfer | `GET /deals/:id/transfers`; `GET /deals/:id/transfer-gate` (Admin) | The disclaimer appears once. The total says what it counts, all time on this deal. Record transfer is on only when the gate says nothing holds it. A visible hold names its count. A hold on a decision the caller cannot see has no count. An unreadable gate keeps the button off with Try again; it never reads as "nothing holds it". Only an Admin records (D1215). |
| Audit (not for investors) | Checklist events (D6156), transfers, paper, and the closing record's events | Newest first, with filter counts. A closing event's actor reads "Name not shown yet", because the read carries no name. |

The side sheet edits status, owner (a label), due date and note, and sends only what changed. The route's own refusal sentence shows with the edits kept. The three before-close rules are the ones `completeClosing` enforces: every line is Received or Withdrawn, at least one is Received, and the cap table is decided. Each is written beside the unavailable Close action, or the panel says no closing record is open, or that it is closed.

A read that fails says Unavailable (D5129) in its own tab, and the other tabs stay standing.
