### What the audit found

- Videos showed zero items under default filters yet said no videos matched: `VideosLibrary.jsx` draws "No videos match these filters" for any empty result, although `LearningHome.jsx` already reads the unfiltered library and could tell the three cases apart.
- "How Global Partnerships Accelerate Scaling" offered no way back to Learning: `ArticleReaderPage.jsx` renders PublicNav and "‹ All articles" to `/articles` only, and `ArticleList` keeps its page in component state, so it is lost on return.
- "See past events" opened the broader Programs & Events page: `EventList` links to `/events`, whose Upcoming/Past choice lives in component state, although `events_public.ts` already returns past events with `past=1`.
- Publications ended in "No publication yet" with no action (`PublicationList`). Webinars were correct and stay as shipped.

### What the canvas decides

- Videos have three different screens: nothing published ("No video published yet", Browse courses), filters that match nothing ("0 of 6 videos", the active filters as chips, Clear all filters and Remove one filter), and a failed read ("Unavailable", Retry, never drawn as 0) (LMA01 to LMA01E).
- One empty-state component serves every format in four kinds: None, No match, Unavailable and Refused, each with its own recovery action.
- Webinars keep distinct states: Live now with Join now, Scheduled with Register, Registered, Full, Cancelled, a recording, and a past webinar without one, each list with its own none, miss and failed states (LMA02, LMA02B).
- Articles show byline, date and reading time. Opened from Learning, the reader shows "‹ Back to Learning · Articles" at the top and the end, and returning restores the list page with the row focused. A direct visitor keeps the standalone page with "‹ All articles"; not found and could not be read each keep their way back (LMA03 to LMA04C).
- Publications keep the PDF as Not recorded, and their empty state offers Read articles (LMA05, LMA05B).
- Events gets an Upcoming/Past choice inside the tab, "No upcoming event" with Show past events, and a link label that names its destination (LMA06 to LMA06D).
- Formats empty in production are filled with rows labelled "Synthetic fixture"; the observed article is labelled "Observed".
