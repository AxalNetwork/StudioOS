## D1040 — Approvals part 2: HQ's inbox read, and assignment and history on HQ

- **Status:** built by S12 for #2888 (part 2 of #2390), 2026-10-25. The owner moved the task to S12 from S05.
- **Split:** S21's integration in #2852 (D1038).
- **Migration:** none. The tables are 341's (`approval_assignments`, `approval_events`).
- **Scope:** Worker, plus one client module. The Inbox that draws it is a later part.

Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.

### 1. Why

D1038's Inbox lists lanes, not items, because HQ had no item read. The branch had one, `approvalBoard` (`services/approvalSources.ts`), but only at `GET /api/branch/approvals`, which refuses off a branch. Assignment refused off a branch too (`routes/branch_approval_assignments.ts`).

### 2. Routes: `routes/admin_approvals.ts`, at `/api/admin/approvals`

All three are `requireAdmin`, with no branch tier gate.

| Route | What |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /?page=` | the inbox (§3) |
| `GET /history?lane=&item_id=` | an item's assignment events, as the branch's |
| `POST /assignments` | `{ assignee_user_id, items: [{ lane, item_id }] }`, as the branch's; `requireBranchNotSuspended` is a no-op on HQ |

**The branch routes share the steps rather than copy them.** The body check, the visible-lane check, the write and one admin-audit row per item are in `services/approvalAssignments.ts` (`assignFromBody`, `readHistoryQuery`). `routes/branch_approval_assignments.ts` now calls them too, and its behaviour is unchanged (D470's tests pass as they were).

**HQ lanes are a Super Admin's.** A source in `APPROVAL_SOURCES` may carry `scope: 'hq'` (part 3's HQ lanes). `sourcesFor(superAdmin)` leaves those out for an admin without the elevation, who then:
- does not read them in the inbox, its lanes or its total;
- cannot assign on them (400 `bad_item`);
- cannot read their history (400 `bad_item`).

On a branch nobody holds the elevation: auth's `hydrateSuperAdmin` clears it there, so a branch's admin never reads an HQ lane.

The branch board itself still reads `APPROVAL_SOURCES` whole. Whether it shows part 3's HQ lanes is part 3's call.

**`isAssignmentLane` takes part 3's new lane keys.** It now reads `APPROVAL_SOURCES` on each call instead of a set built at load, so a lane added there is assignable.

### 3. The inbox read

`hqInbox` reads `approvalBoard`'s items, shared and not copied, through the caller's lanes. Each item keeps the board's fields (`lane`, `lane_label`, `id`, `who`, `what`, `created_at`, `age_hours`, `sla`) and adds `assignee`: `{ user_id, name }`, or null when nobody is assigned.

**When the assignment record cannot be read:** items carry no `assignee` at all and `assignments.available` is false. A null would claim that nobody is assigned.

**Paging:**
- Oldest first, 25 a page.
- An item whose age cannot be read sorts after every known one (`approvalBoard`'s sort).
- `page` is a whole number of at least 1; anything else is 400 `bad_page`.

**The total is exact.**
- Each lane is read to `INBOX_LANE_CAP` (1000), oldest first.
- When a lane fills the cap, it is named in `capped` and its share of the total comes from its count query (`laneCounts`).
- If that count cannot be read either, the total is null with `total_reason`, never a short number.

**A failed lane is named, never empty.**
- It is listed in `unreadable` and `reason`, and its lane count is null rather than 0.
- The other lanes still answer, with every row they hold.
- The total is the open items of the lanes that answered.

**The payload** is the branch's, minus its branch fields (`branch`, `per_lane_limit`): `lanes`, `assignments`, `reviewers`, `sla_bands`, `not_laned`, `decides: false` and `decide_note`. The inbox reads; every decision stays in the queue's own console.

**The tiles:**
- `over_72h`: items at or past 72 hours;
- `assigned_to_me`: items assigned to the caller;
- `per_admin`: each active admin with their count ("[Admin A] · 14").

Each tile counts every open item. A tile that would be short is null, with `tiles.reason`, rather than a smaller number. That happens:
- when a lane could not be read or was capped (all three tiles);
- when the assignment record cannot be read (the two assignment tiles);
- when the admin list cannot be read (`per_admin`).

**Assignment reads the board to the inbox's cap**, so an item the inbox shows can be assigned, even one past the branch board's first 100 in its lane.

### 6. Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/admin_approvals_d1040.test.ts` has 8 cases, on a D1 built from the baseline and every migration. They cover:
- the inbox: oldest first across two lanes, 25 a page, an exact total, an unknown age last, a founder refused, bad pages refused, and no branch fields;
- unknown ages from either lane after every known one;
- assignment on HQ, with each item's assignee, history, refused reviewers and items, the audit rows, and the three tiles counted by hand;
- a failed lane named, its rows gone and no other lane's, with the tiles null and their reason;
- an unreadable assignment record: items with no `assignee`, and only the age tile counted;
- a lane read to its cap: the total exact from its count, the tiles withheld, and an item past the 100th assignable;
- an HQ lane: read, assigned and its history for a Super Admin, refused to an admin, and absent on a branch;
- the mount, the shared steps and the client.

D470's, D130's and D215's suites pass unchanged on the shared steps.

**Mutation check:** 18 mutations across the sort, the failed lane, the tiles, the cap, paging, the HQ gate and the lane check, each applied alone. All 18 are caught.

The two that first survived:
- the sort's other unknown-age branch, now held by the case with an unknown age from each lane;
- a branch check in the route that auth already made. It was redundant, so it was removed rather than tested.
