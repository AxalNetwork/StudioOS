## D4653 part 3 — Founder cards show their limits above the button; partner insights drop "views" (#4653)

- **Status:** built by S39 (Claude Code), 2026-11-20. Part 3 of #4653; part 1 is `D4653.md` (#6646), part 2 is `D4653-p2.md` (#7781). No migration, no route, no price or limit change.
- **Source:** the owner's two answers on #4653, 2026-11-20, to the questions `D4653-p2.md` left open.

### The owner's answers

1. **Founder layout: option A.** On `/pricing?for=founder` each plan card lists its limits (startups, interviews and OKRs, introductions a month, the AI allowance) above its button, next to the price, so the three plans compare without scrolling. Options B (Compare table under the cards), C (a shorter hero) and D (another reading of "approval") were not taken.
2. **"Views" goes.** The Operating Partner Pro line "Partner insights: views, leads and conversion" becomes "Partner insights: leads and conversion". Nothing counts a profile view, the reason D676 dropped "Profile views" from the partner page.
