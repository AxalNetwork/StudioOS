### How it opens

- A register row's LP name opens the record, and `?lp=<id>` is its address.
  Closing it (×, Escape, the scrim) drops `?lp=` and keeps `?fund=`.
- An LP's name on a call line (Fund · Calls) links to
  `/funds/lps?fund=<id>&lp=<lpId>`.
- The record is the shared `Sheet` (D1317) at 720px. Below 640px it is a full
  page, the canvas's IFD14C phone sheet.
- The canvas draws FV4 as a page under the LPs zone. The issue asks for a
  drawer over the register, and the drawer keeps the register and its filter
  behind it. Both the content and the `?lp=` address are as drawn.
- An id that is not on the fund's register says so in the same sheet.

### What it shows, and from where

| Block | Source | Fails as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commitment, share, paid to date | the register row the page already read (the same figures as the row) | the register's own failure |
| Called to date | the sum of the LP's owed lines from the ledger. Null when the ledger was cut short or a line has no owed figure. | "Unavailable" while the ledger is unreadable |
| LPA, KYC and its source | the register row: `lpa_signed`, `lpa_signed_at`, and the linked account's `kyc_status`. No linked account reads "No account", never pending. | — |
| Calls and receipts | `GET /api/funds/:id/ledger?lp=<id>`. Each line carries only its own receipts. Outstanding is owed less received, floored at zero. A line marked paid before receipts existed reads "Paid · no receipt recorded" and has no received figure. | Unreadable with Retry |
| Comms log | `GET /api/funds/:id/comms?lp=<id>`. Subject only, never a body. | Unreadable with Retry |
| Statement, distributions, issued statements | `GET /api/funds/:id/lp-report/:lpId`, the LP-portal shape: commitment, paid in, distributions and TVPI. TVPI with nothing paid in, and NAV, read Not recorded. | Unreadable with Retry |

Each per-LP read is its own block. A failed one never blanks the others.

### Log a message

`POST /api/funds/:id/comms` for this LP takes:

- the kinds the Worker accepts (`COMM_KINDS`: call reminder, call notice,
  update, other);
- a subject of up to 200 characters;
- the date it was sent;
- optionally, one of this LP's call lines.

A refusal shows the Worker's sentence, and nothing is assumed logged. On
success the drawer's log and the page's fund-wide log are both read again. It
records a message the GP sent outside the platform; nothing is sent.

### Not built here

- **Edit details** (name, email, commitment): IFD04B. There is no write route
  for it; it is under D6229's backend list.
- **Record receipt and Draft reminder on the record:** both stay on Fund · Calls,
  where the line, the stepper and the draft already live. A line's LP name now
  leads back here.
- **The statement as a PDF:** the drawer links to Reporting, where statements
  are produced. A PDF in the drawer would need the period's narrative and
  snapshot. Without them the renderer marks every page DRAFT, even for an issued
  statement.
