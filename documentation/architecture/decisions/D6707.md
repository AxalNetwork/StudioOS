### 1. What the quote reads

`services/incorporationQuote.ts` `quoteInputs` reads the form from the formation catalogue: HQ reads the current published version, and a branch reads version 1 (D6691's `readerVersion`). From the form it takes:
- its fee lines;
- its jurisdiction's vocabulary;
- `allow_axal_collection`;
- its partner.

When the catalogue cannot answer, the reviewed `STATE_FILING_FEES` table stands in, as before. The quote then has no partner, and Axal may not collect a partner's fee. Each fallback is logged as one line, `{"event":"formation_catalogue_fallback","reader":"itemised_quote",…}`.

**The government line** is the catalogue's line, with its label. `vocabulary.fee_labels[<line key>]`, when set, renames it. "State filing fee" is no longer hard-coded. An amount shows only with its source and checked day, so a fee with no sourced figure stays null ("Not recorded").

**The partner** is the case's accepted `formation` assignment. Before one exists, it is the default partner of D6693: a verified firm with formation coverage on for the country and form, an unexpired credential (`date(valid_until) >= today`) and a signed data agreement. The lowest `priority` wins, and ties go to the oldest row.
- Each service that firm covers for the form gives a line, `partner:<service>`, at its current `founder_price_minor`.
- `cost_minor` is never selected, returned or snapshotted.

### 2. The fields each line gains

D4357's fields are unchanged. Each line adds:

| Field | Values | From |
|---|---|---|
| `payee` | `axal`, `partner`, `government` | The catalogue `payee` name: "Axal" or "Axal VC" is `axal`, "partner" is the case's firm, any other name is the authority it names |
| `payee_name` | The authority's or firm's name, or null | The catalogue, or the firm's `partners.company` (else `name`) |
| `collected_by` | `axal`, `partner`, `government` | The catalogue's `axal` is `axal`, `provider` is `partner`, and `payee` is whoever is paid |
| `timing` | `at_order`, `at_filing`, `yearly`, `monthly` | Recurring lines (catalogue `annual`, a partner cadence, a catalog interval) are `yearly` or `monthly`. Otherwise a line is `at_order` when Axal collects it, and `at_filing` when it does not |
| `billed_by` | The firm's name, or null | Set when the partner collects the line. The screen reads it as "billed by [Partner]" |

**Departure from the issue text.** The issue lists `collected_by` as axal or partner. The catalogue also has `payee`, meaning the founder pays the registry itself. That is how all six seeded forms record their state fee, so it reads `government`. `monthly` is a fourth timing because partner prices may be monthly (D6692).

**The catalogue may also carry:**
- **an Axal line keyed `service_fee`** (payee Axal, sourced). That is Axal's own fee, and the Stripe price is the charge it must add up to. Without one, Axal's fee is the Stripe price, as before.
- **a partner line keyed by a coverage service** (payee `partner`, for example `formation`). It sets that partner line's label and who collects it. It has no amount of its own: the amount is the firm's founder price, or null.

### 3. Totals and the charge

- **`total`** is D4357's: every line in the total, in the quote's currency, with a line in another currency counted as not known. It is unchanged.
- **`totals`** has one entry per currency: the quote's first, then the others in order. Lines in different currencies are never summed (no FX). A GBP government fee beside a USD service fee gives two totals.
- **What a total holds:** the lines that are not optional and are paid once, at order or at filing. Yearly and monthly lines, the registered agent and add-ons are listed apart.
- **`charge`** is `{ currency, amount_cents, lines }`: the lines Axal collects with the order (not optional, `collected_by = axal`, `timing = at_order`). It is null when one of them is unknown or in another currency.

### 4. The rules at order

The two order routes in `routes/legal.ts` (`POST /incorporation/order` and `POST /incorporate/checkout`) call `orderQuoteRefusal`. It runs after the Stripe price is resolved, and before any row is written and before Stripe is called:

1. A partner line collected by Axal, on a form whose `allow_axal_collection` is 0, is refused with 409 `partner_collection_not_allowed`.
2. When a Stripe price is resolved, `charge` must equal it: every Axal line known, in the price's currency, summing to its amount. Otherwise the order is refused with 409 `quote_charge_mismatch`.

On the six legacy forms today, the charge is the Stripe price alone, so every order passes as before. The keyless dev simulation has no resolved price, so only rule 1 applies there.

**Option A, and later B.** Option A is built, and it also serves B once HQ sets `allow_axal_collection` on a form: Axal's catalogue fee plus the partner's founder price must equal the one Stripe price. No subscription is created, and no money moves to a partner or a registry. Paying partners is #6718 (X1).

The GET quote and the case quote refuse rule 1 too, so a disallowed collection is never shown.

### 5. The case snapshot

`POST /api/legal/incorporation/cases/:uid/itemised-quote` (in `routes/legal_incorporation_quote.ts`) quotes a formation case. Who may call it: the case's founder only, meaning its venture's founder or an editor of its company. The case must be open, on the HQ (v2) track, before payment, and on a chosen form that is in the catalogue.

It writes every line to `formation_case_fee_lines` under one new `quote_uid`. A later quote adds rows and never changes earlier ones. Each row carries:
- payee, payee name, collector and timing;
- currency and `amount_minor` (null for "Not recorded");
- the line's optional flag, source, source URL and checked day;
- the firm (for partner lines), the catalogue version, who quoted it and when.

Refusals:

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| 403 | `formation_hq_only` | On a branch |
| 404 | `formation_case_not_found` | The case does not exist or is not the caller's |
| 409 | `formation_case_quote_locked` | The case is past payment or closed |
| 409 | `formation_case_no_form` | No form is chosen, or the form is not in the catalogue |

The table has no project or company column (it hangs off `formation_cases`), so it does not join the venture-space data inventory.

**Client:** `frontend/src/lib/api/incorporationQuote.js`, with the shape note and `quoteCase(uid)`.

### 7. Tests

`incorporation_fee_lines_6707.test.ts` (8 cases) runs on the full migrated schema with the real routers:
- **Parity:** for the six ids, the quote equals the fixture recorded from main `9b92d09389` once the added fields are taken off, and no fallback is logged.
- **Two currencies:** a GBP government line beside a USD fee gives two totals.
- **Mismatch:** an Axal-collected extra line is refused with `quote_charge_mismatch` on both order routes. No order or case is written and Stripe is never called. Without the extra line, both orders go through.
- **Option B:** on a form that allows it, Axal's catalogue fee plus a partner fee collected by Axal passes when the sum equals the price, and is refused when it does not.
- **Collection not allowed:** with `allow_axal_collection = 0`, a partner line collected by Axal is refused on the quote and both order routes. Collected by the partner, the same line is quoted and billed by the firm.
- **No cost:** no `cost_minor` and no cost figure reaches a founder or a partner member, in the quote, the case quote or the snapshot rows.
- **Snapshot:** the case quote snapshots its lines, priced by the case's assigned partner rather than the default one. A second quote adds a snapshot. Another user and a paid case are refused and write nothing.
- **Mapping:** the payee, collector and timing rules, and the charge compared in its own currency.

D4357's, D6691's, D6687's, D586's, D601's and D6693's tests pass unchanged.
