### 1. The company lightbox (CMP2), at `/account/companies/<uid>`

`companyUidFor` reads a segment that is not a tab as a company uid. A tab is never read as a uid. The lightbox opens over Companies, and closing it returns to `/account/companies`. Each card has an Open link to it.

**Reads.** Each fails alone and says so.
- `GET /api/company/:uid` gives the facts (Stage, Website, Created) and the members.
- The card gives Last opened and Cycles.
- `GET /api/account/projects` gives the ventures in the company, and the caller's own ventures in no company, with Move. This read now also runs for a profile with no Projects tab while a lightbox is open.
- `GET /api/company/:uid/invitations` is read for an editor only. It lists the pending invitations, and says when an email was not sent.

**Buttons follow the Worker's `canEdit` and primary-admin rules** (`companyPowers`):
- **An editor** (primary admin, Owner, Admin or Founder): Rename and Invite people.
- **The primary admin:** Transfer primary admin and Archive company. Restore company replaces both once the company is archived.
- **Any member:** Make active (when it is not active) and Leave company.
- **Open Studio and Full company settings** show only for the active company, because those pages read the active company.

### 2. The venture lightbox (CMP4, CMP4b)

It opens from Open on a venture in Projects, and from a venture in the company lightbox.
- **The facts come from the venture's row:** Stage, "Lab week n of 4", Cycles, and "In <company>" or "Not in a company".
- **The team, the caller's seat and the pending invitations** come from `GET /api/projects/:id/members`.

**The Lab lock is the Worker's** `gate_reason` (`evaluateTeamGate`), shown word for word in place of the invitations. Past the lock (CMP4b), the list shows with Revoke, and a link to the venture's page to invite.

**Who sees what:**
- **The owner** gets Move, Transfer ownership, Remove and Move to trash.
- **A seated member who is not the owner** gets Leave venture.

### 3. The dialogs (CMP6–CMP8)

**Shared rules:**
- **One shell.** Every dialog is `ActionDialog`, an in-page `Lightbox` over the lightbox.
- **The words.** They live in `LIFECYCLE` in the model and are said before the act.
- **A refusal** is said inside the dialog, and the dialog stays open.

**The dialogs:**

| | Dialog | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| CMP6 | New company | Says the clash before sending: "You already have a company called X." The check uses the Worker's rule (trimmed, case-insensitive). |
| | Rename | `PATCH /api/company/:uid`. |
| | Make active | The switcher's own `switchTo`. It applies to this device only. |
| | Invite people | Email, role, a title from `GET /api/company/team-vocabulary`, and the authority chips. If `email_sent` is false, the dialog shows the accept link, with Copy. |
| CMP7 | Transfer primary admin | A radio of the other members. `demote` is true unless "Keep me as a primary admin too" is ticked. |
| | Archive | Says what happens and what is kept. |
| | Restore | Brings an archived company back. |
| | Leave, refused | See below. |
| CMP8 | Move | One of the caller's other companies, or out of its company. |
| | Transfer ownership | A radio of the venture's co-founders. |
| | Leave a venture | Removes your own seat. |
| | Move to trash | Names the last day it can be restored: 30 days, `TRASH_DAYS`. |
| | Remove | Removes a team member. |
| | Revoke | Withdraws a pending invitation. |

**Leave is refused for the only primary admin, with the reason.**
- **When the read shows the caller as the only primary admin,** Leave opens on the refusal and sends nothing: "You are the only primary admin of X. Transfer it first."
- **When the read does not show it,** the Worker's `last_primary_admin` answer swaps the Leave dialog for the same refusal.
- **Either way,** its button opens Transfer primary admin.

### 4. Where it departs from the canvas, and why

- **"Keep me as Admin" reads "Keep me as a primary admin too".** The transfer's `demote` removes only the primary-admin flag, and the caller's role is unchanged. The dialog says which of the two outcomes the tick gives.
- **Archive and Move to trash do not say "under Archived".** Archived is #3017's read and is not built. Their words say it can be restored and that Archived is not built yet. Restore is drawn in the lightbox for an archived company.
- **New company has no Stage field.** `useCompanySwitch().create` takes a name only, and it is not in this part's files.
- **The venture's Created and Status facts are not drawn.** `GET /api/account/projects` does not carry them. They are left out rather than drawn as "Not recorded".
- **The venture's invite form is not drawn here.** It stays on the venture's page, which owns the stage gate. CMP4b links to it.
