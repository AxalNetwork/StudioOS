### Addresses and permissions

- Partners retain `/comarketing` and `/comarketing/:pitch` under Offers. Their pitch submission, own-pitch edits, withdrawals and attribution reads keep their existing ownership and plan checks.
- Founders open the existing Published feed at `/grow/partner-network/co-marketing`. Partner network has the door; the module return retains a validated venture query. Legacy Partnerships desk links and its action point there too. This move grants no partner pitch-writing permission.
- The founder branch of `/comarketing` becomes `CoMarketingFounderRedirect`. It replaces history, preserves query/hash, and emits only `redirect:/comarketing`. The existing D2301 `ROLE_REDIRECTS` entry is founder-only: the global address-bar beacon skips it, so a partner's page opening is never counted as a redirect. The counter key includes no request query or hash.
- Investors are refused by the frontend guards, the Offers tab list and a Worker router middleware before all `/api/comarketing/*` handlers and compatibility aliases. The investor sidebar already has no co-marketing row. Anonymous requests still require sign-in. Other unsupported member roles are refused too.
- Admins retain their existing page and HQ board. Every `/admin/*` handler still uses `requireAdmin`, including its compliance write bar. No pitch, attribution, decision or historical investor submission is deleted or reattributed.
