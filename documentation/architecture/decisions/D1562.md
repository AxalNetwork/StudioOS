### 1. Owner decision K: Google Fonts under the OFL only

- `services/decks/brandKitLists.ts` holds `BRAND_FONTS`, a fixed list of 20 Google Fonts families. Each entry names its family, kind (sans, serif, display), licence (`OFL-1.1`) and source.
- A kit's font pair is a heading and a body family, both on the list (`fontPairOf`). Half a pair, or any name off the list, is refused on write and dropped on read. The migration's CHECK holds both or neither.
- Font uploads (K's part B) are out of scope. They need licence-confirmation wording that the owner and counsel would set, so no request field accepts a font file or an unlisted family.
- The licences are as Google Fonts lists them, from memory. They were not fetched in this session (§8).

### 2. Where the kit lives

- The brand kit the studio already read stays the venture's newest live landing page (palette, `font_pairing`, logos, voice).
- Migration 737 adds the deck's own kit over it. `deck_brand_kits` is one row per venture: the font pair, the logo URL, the palette roles as JSON and the image style. `deck_brand_kit_images` is the library: at most 24 images, each a public https address with alt text, at most 3 of them flagged as references.
- A value in the deck kit wins; a value it leaves out falls back to the page's (`mergeKit`).
- **The five palette roles** are Background, Text, Accent, Secondary and Muted text (`ground`, `ink`, `accent`, `secondary`, `muted`). A role nobody recorded is null, never a guessed colour.

### 4. The pure pick (dk-auto AUTO-2's brand-kit precedence)

`brandKitPick(kit, base)` is pure: no clock, no D1, no model. Given a kit, it answers `{ theme, reason, adjustments }`:
- `kind: 'brand'`;
- the font pair as `theme.fonts`, which the slide model now keeps (`sanitizeTheme`, kept only when set, so a theme saved before this hashes as it did);
- the logo in every card's corner (`chrome.logo_corner`) when a logo is recorded;
- the image style as `art_direction.style`, keeping the deck's own art-direction note and seed;
- a deck background colour cleared (a background image stays);
- the deck's own settings kept (size, language, Confidential, footer).

The reason is one sentence, for example "Brand, because your brand kit is recorded: Fraunces and Inter, 5 of 5 palette roles, the logo, Photo images."

The palette roles reach the deck through the brand theme's tokens. **`themeTokens('brand', kit)` now never answers an unreadable pair:**
- after placing the kit's colours, it repairs muted text, the surface, the accent, the text on the accent and the chart colours, each from fixed rules;
- if any pair still fails, the deck keeps its base brand colours and says so in `adjustments`.

`themeProblems` of its answer is always []. A seeded test of 400 random kits holds it.

**The one-click run.** `oneClickModel.themePick` takes the kit's pick first, before the base version's theme, and records its reason. The review shows the reason (`pitch-review-kit-reason`), and Change still offers every theme. `themeToWrite` writes the pick whole: its fonts, logo corner and image style. A kit read without a `pick` (an old Worker) keeps D1604's order.

### 5. The image style feeds the art direction (dk-auto gap d)

`GET /api/decks/:id/images/plan` now reads the merged kit (`brandKitFor`), so every proposed image takes the kit's style. Apply brand also writes the style into `theme.art_direction`, which every image request on the deck inherits (D1544).

### 6. Apply brand, one undo step

`applyBrandToDeck(cards, theme, kit)` is pure. It answers:
- the picked theme;
- every card's own background colour cleared, so the card sits on the brand ground (a background image stays);
- every accent run recoloured to the brand accent where that accent reads at the slide model's 4.5:1, on the card and on the brand ground; otherwise the run loses its colour and keeps its words;
- the cards nothing changed, as the same objects.

The route refuses (422) rather than hand back a deck with a contrast problem. By construction, `problems` is [] on every 200.

In the editor, `BrandKitSheet`'s Apply brand calls `deckBrandKitApi.apply` with the deck on screen. `brandKitModel.applyOps` turns the answer into one `setDoc` op (cards and theme together), so D1515's history gains one step and one Undo puts every card and the theme back. The edit is held until Save, like every other edit. An edit made while the route answered wins: the answer is refused, never merged.
