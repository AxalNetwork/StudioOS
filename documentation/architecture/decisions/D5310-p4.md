## D5310 part 4 — the Network overview checked against the phone (NW8) and dark (NW10) boards (#5310)

Part 4 of #5310, after `D5310.md` (NW1, NW1.2, NW5), `D5310-p2.md` (NW2, NW2.1) and part 3 (NW3.2, NW3.4). Part 1 built the founder overview responsive and dark, and left the two boards that draw those cases to be checked against their own canvas: `design/incoming/network-workspace/NW8-Phone.dc.html` (390 wide) and `NW10-Dark.dc.html` (NW1 with the canvas's dark tokens). This part makes that check. No read, route or Worker change.

### What the check found, and what changed

| Board | Found | Changed |
|---|---|---|
| NW8 | The heading shared its row with Ask and Request introduction, so "Your network" broke over two lines. | Below 600px the hero is one column in the board's order: kicker, heading, description, then the actions, Request introduction filling the row. |
| NW8 | The four section pills wrapped onto two lines. The board draws one **Section** picker. | Below 600px a `<select>` labelled Network section replaces the pills: Overview, then each zone with the count its pill carries. A choice opens its zone the way its pill does, as a lens on a phone (D575, `useLensGo` in `workspaces/lens.jsx`). From 600px up the pills stay and the picker is hidden. |
| NW1, NW8, NW10 | The boards draw Needs attention and Awaiting my action with an amber edge (`.k.att`) and a glyph (◷, !). The build drew all four tiles alike. | `overviewTiles` gives those two tiles a `mark` when their read count is above zero. The tile draws the amber edge and the glyph before its line, so the state is never colour alone. Zero, loading and Could not be read are never marked; a stale count still is. |
| NW1, NW10 | **Accept** on a curated match drew as a plain white button: `.a6-match-actions button` outranked `.a6-primary`. The boards draw it as the primary action. | Accept is violet in both themes. |
| NW10 | Every other surface already reads the dark theme (`.dark` rules from part 1). | Nothing else. |

### Departures from the boards

| Board | Departs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| NW8 | The phone top bar is the shell's (logo, company, notifications, account), not the board's ☰ / Network / Eadwyn row, and **Ask** stays beside Request introduction. | The shell owns the top bar on every page. The board's Eadwyn button is the page's Ask, which stays on the page. |
| NW8 | **Awaiting my decision** is the first group of Introductions in progress, not its own card above Needs attention. | One card holds the four groups on every width (part 1), and the Awaiting my action tile states the count at the top. |
| NW8 | The tiles keep the desktop wording ("Awaiting my action", "people in your relationship book") rather than the board's short phone labels. | One wording on every width; the tiles fit at 390 without truncation. |
| NW10 | The dark palette is the platform's dark theme, not the board's slate tokens. | Every page draws dark from the same theme. |
| NW1, NW8, NW10 | The desktop column layout, the Organizations count and the activity feed are as `D5310.md` records. | Unchanged by this part. |
