## D634 — HQ review: delete a review comment, a billing dispute's page, and sensitive fields masked

**Issue:** #1392, part of #1165 (the Ops canvas, area 7). **Built by:** S07, moved from S15 at the owner's request.
**No migration.**

Three screens from the Ops canvas (`design/canvases/integrated/ops-data.js`):

| Board | Screen | Built into |
|---|---|---|
| Q4 | `hq-comments` | the review comments of `/admin/articles` (`components/hqReview/ReviewComments.jsx`) |
| Q5 | `hq-dispute` | a new address, `/admin/revenue/disputes/:disputeId` (`pages/hq/DisputePage.jsx`), opened from the Disputes rows of HQ · Revenue |
| Q6 | `hq-sensitive` | the KYC and partner-profile review windows of `/admin` (`components/hqReview/SensitiveField.jsx`) |

The words are in `components/hqReview/hqReviewCopy.js`.

### Q6 needed a Worker change, and it came first

D612 logs each read of a KYC record and of a partner profile, and those reads
returned every sensitive value in clear. Masking only in the browser would
have put "each showing is logged" on a page whose values had already left the
server. So:

- **The record reads mask.**
  - `GET /api/kyc/admin/:userId` drops date of birth, ID number, the address
    lines (line 1 and 2, city, region, postcode), and phone from `kyc_data`.
    It returns `kyc_data.masked` in their place.
  - `GET /api/profiling/admin/:email` drops `ein`, from the column and from the
    AI's `extracted_data`, and returns `masked.ein`.
  - The masks are in `services/sensitiveFields.ts`:
    - a mask keeps separators;
    - an ID number, phone or tax ID keeps its last two characters, so a
      reviewer can match a document;
    - a date of birth and an address keep none.
- **One value at a time is shown, and that showing is logged first.**
  - `GET /api/kyc/admin/:userId/sensitive/:field` (`date_of_birth`,
    `id_number`, `address`, `phone`) writes `kyc_field_shown`.
  - `GET /api/profiling/admin/:email/sensitive/ein` writes
    `partner_field_shown`.
  - Each row has the admin's hashed email as `actor`, the admin as `user_id`,
    and details naming the field and the member's id, never the value.
  - The response carries `shown_by` and `shown_at`, and the field prints
    "Shown by … · time · logged".
- **Refusals that write nothing:**
  - a field the member did not submit: 404 `field_not_recorded`;
  - an unknown field: 400 `unknown_field`;
  - a missing member or profile: 404.
- **Plain fields stay plain:** legal name, nationality, country, ID type.
- **The tax ID on the KYC record** reads "Not recorded: the KYC form does not
  ask for a tax ID". The KYC submission has no such field, so there is nothing
  to show.

### Q4 and Q5 needed no new route

- **Q4.** `DELETE /admin/articles/comments/:cid` (D617) already allowed the
  author or a Super Admin, and logged the delete.
  - Its two refusals now carry sentences beside their codes.
  - Resolve stays the first action.
  - Delete asks first: "The author loses it too. This cannot be undone. To keep
    it on record, resolve it instead." Only the confirmation's button sends the
    delete.
  - A comment with no anchor says so.
  - A failed article read is Unreadable with "Try again". It used to be a toast
    over "Select an article".
- **Q5.** `GET /admin/billing/disputes/:id` already returned amount, currency,
  status, reason, due date, charge, payment intent, whether evidence was sent,
  and the submission count.
  - It reads live from Stripe, so the page has two failure states:
    - "Stripe is not configured" (503 `stripe_not_configured`);
    - "Stripe could not be read" (any other failure), with "Try again".
  - A missing due date, reason or reference reads "Not recorded" with a reason.
    A missing amount is not shown as zero.

### Where the build departs from the canvas

- **No customer reference on the dispute.** Stripe's dispute object carries no
  customer; it hangs off the charge, and the route does not read the charge.
- **No date of the last evidence.** Stripe gives a count and a flag, not when
  evidence was last sent.
- **No AI rail on the comments.** No surface is allow-listed for it.
- **No search, filters or pages on the comments.** They are listed inside the
  article's own panel, which is a short list.
- **`/admin/kyc/[member]` is not a new address.** The review stays a window
  over `/admin`, where the KYC queue already lives.
- **Free text is not masked.** The partner's onboarding transcript is the
  partner's own words, so an EIN typed into the chat is not masked there. Only
  structured fields are.
