### What is built

- **Needs your attention** sits between the controls and the board or table on Mine and All deals (`AttentionStrip` in `pages/investor/dealflow/DealFlow.jsx`, rules in `flowModel.attentionOf`). New for you itself draws no strip, since it is that list.
- **Its one item is “N new”**: the deals that reached the investor and are not yet in the flow, counted once per deal from the inbox, linking to New for you with the current settings. DE17's handoff table names the inbox (`deal_flow.ts` … inbox) as the strip's source. With none, the strip says “Nothing new for you.”
- **A failed read is the strip's alone:** Unreadable, “What needs your attention could not be read. That is not the same as nothing needing it.”, and Try again, which re-reads the inbox only. No count is drawn, so never 0; the board, table and “Other deal activity” are unaffected. A way in that the inbox could not read (its `sources[*].unreadable`) is named with Try again, and the count from the rest reads as “there may be more”.
- **View settings** (`ViewSettings` in `FlowControls.jsx`) holds the advanced options in one panel: the saved view (pick, Save view, Delete), Sort, and Columns on the table or Card scopes on the board. The button names the saved view in use (“View settings · Strong team”); “• edited” with Save changes and Save as new stays on the toolbar, because it is a state, not a setting. Every change still writes the address through `settingsHref`, so a link reopens the view.
- **One primary in the header:** unchanged, Set your thesis while the flow is empty (D1424), and none otherwise. The strip and View settings add no primary.
- **44px targets below `md`** (`TAP` in `FlowControls.jsx`): search, Filters, View settings and everything in its panel, the strip's chip and its Try again.

### Left out, with no field behind them

DE01 draws three chips: “3 stale”, “2 new”, “1 waiting on diligence”. Only “new” has a field.

- **Stale.** The flow returns `days_in_stage` but no rule for when a deal is stale, and no read carries a stale flag (D1240 already lists Stale among the flags no read carries). A threshold chosen here would be invented, so the chip is not drawn. It needs a recorded rule: either a firm setting or a `stale` field on `GET /api/deals/flow`.
- **Waiting on diligence.** Nothing on the flow, deal list, score or fit reads records what a deal in Diligence is waiting on (open diligence requests, an NDA, a data-room answer). The board's Diligence column already counts the deals there; counting them again would not be the canvas's meaning. It needs a new read, for example open diligence requests per deal on the flow.

Both are raised on #5899 and named in its PR; neither is built here.

### Departures

- **Sort moved under View settings.** DE01 draws Sort beside Filters on the toolbar and View settings to the right; the issue (#5899) puts Sort, Columns, Card scopes and the saved views under View settings, and this follows the issue. Filters and search stay on the toolbar.
- **The strip is drawn on the table too.** DE02 (the table board) draws no strip; the issue asks for it above the board or the table, and one placement keeps the two views the same page.
- **The chip counts deals, not inbox items.** One deal can reach you two ways (an invitation and a room request); it counts once, as New for you's rows are deals to add.
