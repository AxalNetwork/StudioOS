## D6989 — Spin-Out Lab sign-up: an honest heading and stages, and the chosen starting point carried and stored (#6989)

- **Status:** built by S11 on 2026-11-23 for #6989 (X7 of tracker #6976), after #6984 (X2, PR #8180) and #6990
  (X8, PR #7029).
- **Register P0:** `/spinout-lab` and `/spinout-lab/apply`.

**The sign-up says what it is.** `/register?product=spinout-lab` now shows:
- the heading "Apply to Spin-Out Lab: start by creating your account." and the line "Programme: Spin-Out Lab";
- five labelled stages, from `SPINOUT_LAB_SIGNUP` in `frontend/src/lib/registerCopy.js`:
  1. Create your account.
  2. Confirm your email.
  3. Complete your application.
  4. Axal VC reviews your membership and sends an agreement.
  5. Sign the agreement and your workspace opens.

The order is the one the code allows:
- `POST /api/auth/register` creates the account with role `exploring`;
- confirming the email signs the member in;
- `POST /api/spinout-lab/apply` takes an explorer's application at once (`holdsLicence(user, 'founder') ||
  user.role === 'exploring'`), so the application comes before the review;
- the team reviews the membership and sends the agreement, and once it is signed an admin gives the role that opens
  the workspace (`MEMBERSHIP_STEPS`, D4578).

No step is dropped and no time is promised. Without the product, `/register?lane=founder` keeps the founder heading of
#6983 (X1). The amber box that repeated the programme ("our 28-day cohort") is replaced by the programme line.

**The starting point is carried.** The Lab page's track (form, fit or line, `LAB_TRACKS`) used to stop at the apply
link. Now:
- **Signed out:** `labApplyHref(track)` (`frontend/src/lib/spinoutLab.js`) gives
  `/register?lane=founder&product=spinout-lab&next=/spinout-lab/apply?track=<id>`. The track rides inside `next`, the
  same-origin return path RegisterPage already keeps across sign-up (`pendingNext.js`), and never as a fourth param on
  /register.
- **Signed in:** the link is `/spinout-lab/apply?track=<id>`.
- **An unknown track** gives the plain link.
- **On the application:** the first step gains a "Starting point" choice, preselected from `?track=`. A saved draft
  keeps its own choice; a draft saved without one takes the link's. The applicant can change it or clear it, and it is
  sent only when chosen.

**The starting point is stored.** Migration `202611231545_6989_spinout_application_track.sql` adds the nullable
`spinout_applications.track`, with `CHECK (track IS NULL OR track IN ('form', 'fit', 'line'))`.
- **The apply route:** it validates the value against `LAB_TRACK_IDS`.
  - an unknown value is refused, 400 `track_unknown`, and nothing is stored;
  - a missing or empty value stores NULL;
  - the route's answer includes `track`.
- **Older rows:** they keep NULL, and a reader shows "Not recorded", never a default track.
- **Not yet migrated:** the column is named only when a track is sent, and the read-back retries without it, so a
  database the migration has not reached still takes an application without one. That matches how the route already
  treats migration 315.

**Tests that change with the behaviour.**
- `spinout_lab_intro.test.mjs` asserted "the apply link carries no track, because nothing records one". Something now
  records it, so the test asserts the new rule: only `labApplyHref()` builds such a link, both Lab pages use it, and
  the Worker names the three ids.
- `multi_licence_pages_3704.test.mjs` counts apply links that may carry `?track=`.
- `pricing_selection_4651.test.mjs` reads the heading call with its new argument.

**Not here:**
- per-track milestones (D385 keeps one set);
- redrawing the form (#6956, #6979);
- the Lab page's track sentence (#6990);
- the signed-out redirect at `/spinout-lab/apply` in `App.jsx`, a frozen hub, which still sends a visitor who typed
  the address to the plain register link. Every link the Lab pages draw carries the track.

**Rollback.** Revert the Worker and the frontend. The column is additive and can stay.
