## D6072 — Data room: tell a member investor when a room is shared with them (#6072)

- **Status:** built by S12 for #6072, 2026-11-15.
- **Owner answer (2026-11-15, on #6072):** keep D1101 §5 answer 6, "existing accounts only".
  - A share to an address with no account still creates nothing.
  - No pending invite is built.
  - The scope is the member notification only.
- **Migration:** none. **Screen:** none, so the #6071 canvas is not needed.
- **Route:** `POST /api/data-room/:projectUid/grants` (`cloudflare-worker/src/routes/data_room.ts`).

### 1. Who is told, and when

When the owner shares the room with an address that belongs to an existing account, and that grant opens the room for the investor now, the investor gets one notification through `notify`:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| type | `data_room_shared` |
| category | `deals` |
| title | "A data room was shared with you" |
| body | "<venture> shared its data room with you. Open it in Research › Diligence." |
| link | `/research/diligence/<grant uid>`, the investor's room page (`App.jsx`) |

**"Opens the room now"** means the investor had no open grant before the write. An open grant is one that is `active` and either has no end date or ends in the future. So:

| Case | Notified |
| --- | --- |
| A new grant | yes |
| A revoked grant shared again | yes (the row is reactivated and keeps its uid) |
| An expired grant shared again | yes |
| An open grant shared again | no |
| A change to an open grant's folders | no |

"One notification" means the share is told once, however often the owner presses Share.

### 2. What does not change

- **The answer is the same in every case:** `{ ok: true }` with 201, whether the address has an account or not, and whether or not a notice went out. Share still never says who has an account.
- **No account, no row:** an unknown address writes no grant and sends no notice.
- **Sharing with yourself** writes nothing and tells nobody, as before.
- **A notice is best effort.** It is sent after the grant is written, and a failure is logged, never thrown, so the share always succeeds (as the request and NDA notices already do).

### 3. Tests

**`cloudflare-worker/test/data_room_share_notice_6072.test.ts` (4 cases),** on the D1107 fixture:
- a new share tells the member once, with the title, body, link and category;
- a repeat share and a folder change tell nobody;
- an investor already holding an open grant is not told on a re-share;
- a revoked grant and an expired grant shared again are each told, and the link keeps the reactivated row's uid;
- an unknown address gets the same answer, no grant and no notice, and a share to yourself tells nobody;
- a notice that cannot be written does not fail the share.

**Mutation checks:**
- notifying on every share turns the "once" case red;
- removing the notice turns two cases red.
