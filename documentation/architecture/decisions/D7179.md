### What was built

- **Order (RS-17 A).** Search, sort and rows per page; the Filters panel; active filters with the sort; the count line ("26–50 of 186 companies match · page 2 of 8 · of 2,480 in the directory"); the results; the pager; the selection bar; Directory coverage as a closed disclosure; the source boundary. The coverage card no longer sits above the filters.
- **URL state.** `q`, `sector_label`, `country`, `founded_from`, `founded_to`, `has`, `sort`, `page` and `limit` are read from and written to the URL under the API's own names, defaults left out. Typing and filter changes replace the history entry; a page change pushes one, so Back steps to the previous page. `tab`, `market` and `website` are kept beside them, so the Saved analyses tab and back returns to the same results. A value the menus do not offer (an unknown sort, a size other than 25/50/100, a year that is not digits) falls back to its default and never reaches the Worker. "Copy link to these results" copies the list parameters only.
- **Session state (option A).** In `sessionStorage`, which sign-out already clears (`App.jsx`): the selection, keyed by the member's id; the last results URL with its summary; and the return point (the results URL and the company link opened from it), taken once.
- **Return (RS-17 B).** The profile's "Back to results" names page, filters, sort and selection and links to the last results; the breadcrumb's "Companies" goes to the same URL. Browser Back, Back to results and the breadcrumb all re-read the results from the URL (never a cached copy). Back and Back to results focus the company link that was opened, scroll it into view, and show "Back to your results: page N, M selected" as a polite status; the breadcrumb focuses the count line. A page change moves focus to the count line.
- **Funding (RCD01, RCD01E).** A Funding total column on every sort, with its currency, through `lib/money.js` (`majorToMinor` then `codeAmount`; the directory's `funding_total` is a REAL in major units). Sorted by funding: one `th scope="colgroup"` row per currency, in the Worker's order (currency first, then amount), the rows with no funding last under their own heading, each "Not recorded · Sorted last", and the note that amounts are ordered only within one currency.
- **Year range (RCD01D).** Four digits from 1000 to 2100, From not after To, checked before anything is sent. A reversed range shows its message at once; a half-typed year once the field loses focus. Both fields carry `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`. Nothing is read and the URL keeps the last valid filters, whose results stay on screen with "still Founded 2016 – 2024" and a Last valid results chip.
- **One link per row (RS-17 E).** The `role="link"` row with a click handler, which nested a second link, is gone: each row has one native company link and a separate checkbox.
- **States (RCD05).** First read: a skeleton and no count. A later read keeps the rows, `aria-busy`, "Updating…". True zero: "No matches", the filters to remove one by one, "Search all sectors and countries for …", Clear all filters, and which "Only companies with" filter narrows most, from the whole-directory facet counts. Unavailable (`role="alert"`) with Retry. Refused (401, 403) is its own state, not a failed read. Missing fields are the Not recorded chip.
- **Phone 390 and tablet 768 (RS-17 C).** Below 1024px (`usePhoneWidth`): cards with a 44px checkbox beside one link, the sector, country, founding year, funding total and the row's number; currency group headings under a funding sort; a Filters button with a count that opens the filter sheet (`PhoneSheet`: Reset all, Show results); a selection bar that opens the selection sheet (Remove per company, Add N to research, Clear selection, Compare N). Targets are 44px below `lg`.
- **Dark (RS-17 D).** Every new surface carries its `dark:` pair.

### The ◇ backend items (D7045)

- **Summary cost, measured.** `listCompanies` prepares six statements per call: the taxonomy overrides, the filtered count, the page, and `directoryShape`'s three whole-directory aggregates (coverage, sector facets, country facets); `country_code` adds the crosswalk's distinct-country read. The test pins this, so a seventh read, or a filter reaching an aggregate, fails. Measured 2026-11-18 on `node:sqlite` with migrations 228, 375, 403 and 405 applied (6,286 companies, the directory's size on `main`; 95 sector labels, 117 countries), 300 runs after 30 warm-up runs each:

  | Read | Median | p95 |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Whole list call, no filter | 4.99 ms | 5.70 ms |
  | Whole list call, Founded 2016–2024, funding sort, page 2 | 5.37 ms | 7.99 ms |
  | Whole list call, `q=tech&country=United Kingdom` | 6.62 ms | 7.78 ms |
  | Coverage aggregate alone | 2.13 ms | 2.98 ms |
  | Sector facets alone | 1.47 ms | 1.71 ms |
  | Country facets alone | 0.54 ms | 0.67 ms |
  | The three aggregates together | 3.89 ms | 5.32 ms |
  | Filtered count and page (Founded 2016–2024, funding sort) | 0.56 ms | 0.65 ms |

  So the summary is about three quarters of a list call's database time, and each call scans the whole table three times (about 18,900 rows read at 6,286 companies) whatever the filter, on top of the filtered reads; the coverage and facets add about 7 KB of JSON. It is recounted on every keystroke's read. This build adds no read to it. Caching it per directory import is the follow-up that would also give the freshness date; it is not built here. Local SQLite, not D1: D1 adds its per-query round trip, so the ratio, not the milliseconds, is the number to carry.
- **Filtered facets: not drawn.** Coverage and facets are the whole directory's under every filter (pinned for country, sector label, none, has, founded, q and country code). The filter panel says "Option counts are of the whole directory (N), not of your results", the coverage disclosure says "not your results", and the tab says its count is the whole directory.
- **Freshness: a dependency.** The list response carries no refresh date (pinned), and the coverage says "Last refreshed: not reported by the directory today". No date is invented.

### Departures from the canvas

- The funding total is written in full through `lib/money.js` ("USD 18,000,000"), not compacted ("USD 18M"): the money layer has no compact form, and D5373 forbids a page's own conversion.
- The Funding total column shows on every sort, beside Last round and Employees; the canvas draws it under the funding sort, where it drops Employees. Groups, the note and "Sorted last" appear only under the funding sort.
- A currency group's heading does not say "continued from page N": the page cannot know whether the previous page ended in the same currency without another read.
- The return point is held in this tab's session, not on the history entry, so Back, Back to results and the breadcrumb restore the same way; the breadcrumb lands on the count line rather than at the top of the page.
- "Opened from a shared link · selections are not shared" (RCD02C) is not shown: a shared link cannot be told from a reload without carrying something in it. The selection is never in the link.
- The selection bar keeps D5157's words and does not list the selected names with "kept in this session"; #7177 owns that bar (the limit line and Review selection). The phone selection sheet lists names only (no sector, country or page): the selection stores uid and name.
- Sort on phone and tablet is a native select beside the Filters button, not a sheet; rows per page sits beside it.
- Refused says the directory refused the read and how to get back, without a Sign in button: the access gate and sign-in return are #7178's.
- The Unavailable card shows no status code or time: the page does not keep the failed response's time.
- The page's type and colours are the app's (`axal-*` tokens and their `dark:` pairs), not the canvas's Research v2 CSS variables.
