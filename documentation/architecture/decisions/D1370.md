## D1370 — Pitch preflight: deterministic checks that block an export only over lost content

- **Status:** asked for by the owner (#3661). Part one was built by S33 in #3670 and merged on 2026-10-29. Its draft decision text was filed by S1 under this number.
- **Builds on:** D898 (the deck block model and its 12 × 6 grid), D901 (the offline HTML export), D903 (worst-case contrast over a background image), D896 (refresh rows) and D900, which is a different thing: the evidence Check in `checkModel.js`.
- **Migration:** none. No Worker change.

### What was decided

1. **Deterministic only.**
   - Preflight gives no score and no "investor ready" verdict.
   - It rewrites nothing and corrects no figure.
   - It reads only, with no fetch and no write.
2. **No false passes.** A check it cannot run honestly reads "Not measured", never Passed (`CHECK_STATE.unmeasured`). Clipping, rendered font size and image upscale come from a real measurement of the card drawn at 1920 × 1080 (`preflightMeasure.js`). Without one, those checks read "Not measured".
3. **Blocking means the export would lose content.** Only four findings block an export:
   - an off-grid block, or two overlapping blocks;
   - more than 24 blocks on a card;
   - measured clipping;
   - an image block with no image.

   Everything else is a warning the founder may dismiss: text density, font size, contrast, upscale, alt text, theme consistency, placeholders and sources. Dismissing a warning never clears a blocking finding (`exportGate`).
4. **A result belongs to one version.** `deckKey` ties it to that version, and `currentResult` hides it after an edit.
5. **The thresholds are constants in `frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/preflightModel.js`:**

   | Constant | Value |
   |---|---|
   | `MIN_FONT_PX` | 24 (about 18 pt at export size) |
   | `CONTRAST_BODY` | 4.5 : 1 |
   | `CONTRAST_LARGE` | 3 : 1, for headings |
   | `CARD_WORDS_MAX` | 110 words |
   | `UPSCALE_MAX` | 1.5 × |
   | `STALE_DAYS` | 120 days, for a stale source |

   Changing one is a change to this decision.
6. **Each finding is a specific fix.** It names its card and block (`{ id, check, severity, card, block, title, detail, fix, dismissible }`), so a later UI can open the affected item.

### Part two (2026-11-02, S21): Check before you send

Built to the owner's boards PF1 to PF5 in `design/incoming/Pitch Deck Studio v2 Page.dc.html`, framed in
`Pitch Deck Studio v2 · Preflight Boards.dc.html`.

1. **Two ways in.** ⋯ gains a fourth section, **Before you send › Check before you send · Free**, between
   This card and the AI items (pd13). Once a check has run, the toolbar carries a chip:
   - "3 to fix · 5 to look at";
   - "✓ All checks passed";
   - "Not checked since your last edit";
   - "Checking · 7 of 12".

   Pressing the chip opens the panel.
2. **Measured, slide by slide, never guessed.** `usePreflight.js` draws each slide off screen at export size:
   - The page is 1920 × 1080, or 1440 × 1080 at 4:3, with `BlockGrid`'s print sizes. It is hidden and inert.
   - Each slide is measured with `measureBlocks`, then `runPreflight` reads the deck as it was when the run started.
   - A slide the page cannot lay out is **"Couldn't check"**. That is its own group and its own count, and never a pass. "All checks passed" is said only when that group is empty.
3. **The panel** is a 480px Sheet, and the full-height page on a phone. Its groups are:
   - To fix;
   - Worth a look;
   - Couldn't check, tagged "Not a pass";
   - Passed, collapsed.

   Each finding row carries:
   - its slide;
   - its block, as "Slide 4 · Title";
   - the model's defect, without its prefix;
   - why it matters, and what to do;
   - a screen-reader name, such as "Slide 4, title, the text is cut off at export size".

   **Show me** selects the card and the block, then closes the panel. It leaves a strip over the slide with
   "Findings · 1 of 8", Previous, Next, and the way back to the check (pf5g).
4. **Dismiss is for warnings only.** It comes with an Undo, and `exportGate` still never clears a blocking finding. Until P1 (#4208) keeps dismissals per version on the Worker, they are held in the open editor, and the panel says so: "Dismissed here only".
5. **"Ask Eadwyn to suggest a fix"** appears on text findings only: cut-off text, density, font size and placeholders. It is greyed, "Not switched on yet.", until P2 (#4210) adds it to D899's card tools as a quoted proposal that is never applied by itself. It is never offered on a figure, a contrast or an image.
6. **After an edit** the result reads **"Out of date"**, with Re-check now. It is never silently re-run. The next run lists "Fixed since the last check".
7. **Share and Export check first.** With the editor on screen, both run the check, or reuse a current result.
   - **Something to fix:** the gate lists it, and "Send anyway" or "Export anyway" is held back. It reads "opens once the N items to fix are fixed". "Fix first" opens the panel.
   - **Warnings only:** the gate offers "Export anyway" beside Fix first.
   - **Nothing to fix and nothing to look at:** the dialog opens at once.
   - **"Couldn't check" alone:** it does not stop the dialog. The chip and the panel still say so.
8. **A reader** (an admin on a founder's deck) sees the findings and Show me, with no Dismiss, Ask or Re-check (pf5d).

### Part three (2026-11-03): on the slide, one slide again, and the acceptance run (#3661)

1. **PF3: the finding on its slide.** Show me now draws the finding under the strip (`PreflightOverlay.jsx`). The slide is drawn as it was checked, at export size (1920 × 1080, or 1440 × 1080 at 4:3, `BlockGrid`'s print sizes), scaled to fit:
   - the block is outlined and numbered: red to fix, amber dashed to look at;
   - cut-off text is drawn as exported: the words past the block's edge are greyed, and the edge is marked on the side the text overflows. That side is read from the block's own box in the layout. With no box, there is no outline: nothing is guessed;
   - a contrast finding shows, beside the slide, **The contrast pair**: the text and background colours, their ratio, and the ratio that reads reliably (pf3b). The model now carries the pair on the finding (`pair: { fg, bg, ratio, min }`), so the card and the finding's line say the same figure.
   - When the deck was edited after the check, the eyebrow says "as checked, before your edit".
2. **pf5c: "Try again" for one slide.** A slide the page could not draw at export size is tried again on its own (`usePreflight.retry`), on the same snapshot, while the result is still current. Once the deck has changed, Try again runs the whole check, because results are only ever for one version. A slide that fails again stays "Couldn't check", never a pass.
3. **The acceptance run, in a real browser** (`scripts/pitch-preflight-acceptance.mjs`, documented in `documentation/operations/PITCH_ACCEPTANCE.md`). The product's own preflight code runs on the deliberately defective synthetic deck (`frontend/test/fixtures/pitch_preflight_defective.mjs`, moved out of the part-one test so a browser bundle can read it). It runs at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, light and dark. On 2026-11-03 all 27 checks passed:
   - the long body on slide 1 measured cut off by the real layout, and export-blocking;
   - every slide drawn;
   - every panel button named;
   - Tab reaching every Show me, in the listed order;
   - each finding a list item with its spoken name;
   - PF3's outline, with its edge marked;
   - the contrast pair;
   - the gate holding Export anyway back.
   
   It exports no file. The fixed deck's export is `pitch_preflight_export_3661`, and the live exports are `scripts/pitch-acceptance-live.mjs`'s. That run cannot reach the host from an agent sandbox, so it stays a manual step.
4. **Phone:** the findings strip puts the finding on its own row above its buttons (pf5g).
