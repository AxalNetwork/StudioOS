### 1. The addresses

| Address | Board | What it draws |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/admin/licences/pricing` | L23 | The catalogue: every market with region, currency, data completeness, last source update (Stale from 12 months), regulatory class, tier and index, recommended annual and initial fee. All four are `guard(['admin'], hqOnly(…))`, as every Licences page is, and the reads are Super Admin: a 403 says
"Super Admin only", never empty. The third segment is the static `pricing`, which React Router ranks above the
`:uid` of `/admin/licences/:uid` and `/admin/licences/:uid/:section`, as `/admin/licences/programs` already is.

### 2. The ledger's Needs HQ (L28)

The four signals of part 1 are four Needs HQ items, each a filter of the table like the legal ones. The page reads
`/signals` once per ledger load and joins it by licence uid (`pricingSignalsRead`); the hot paged ledger read stays
as it is (D5020 §2). The items draw in three rows, Pricing, Legal and Operations. A signal is Reading until the read
answers and Unavailable when it failed or the read said `null`; it is never a 0 and never a "no". The pricing items
fold into Attention; no column is added. The model still reads only the ledger fields the D4819 guard allows: the
signals come in as their own argument, keyed by `l.uid`.

### 3. What is not built here

- **Model editing.** Draft, edit, impact preview per licence and activation (the other half of L25) are not drawn.
  The API exists (D5014); activation needs the write bar and a reason, and the canvas's preview of every licence's
  difference needs a read part 1 does not have. A draft can be previewed across every market from the catalogue.
- **A separate Pricing & Market tab.** The canvas draws it as a tab under Terms. The section stays where D5017 put it,
  on the Terms tab under the core terms, renamed Pricing & Market: a new tab would change `LICENCE_TABS`, which
  the licence navigation tests pin. It now says whether the recommendation shown is the one the terms came from, names the accepted
  terms or "No pricing decision recorded", flags a source older than 12 months, links each market to its sources and
  calculation, and links the Exclusivity block (D5019) instead of drawing it again.
- **Import country data and Record observation.** The canvas's buttons write; the writes exist (D5016) and are not
  drawn on these read screens.
- **The rollout.** Activating v1 in production still waits on the owner's sourced figures and approved fixtures
  (D5016). Nothing here backfills a licence.

### 4. Mutation checks

Each fails `licence_pricing_hq_ui_5020.test.mjs` and passes on the code: an unpriced fee read as 0; stale only after
12 months; a failed signals read counted; pricing left out of Attention; "applied" said of any snapshot; the
no-decision note dropped; Incomplete data leaving provisional markets out; an unknown surcharge called fixed; the
index row drawn for an unpriced market; a 403 drawn as Unavailable.
