## D7118 part 2 — the reflection screens: Today, This week and This month on the Wellness home, and the heart opening Today (#7118)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-21. Frontend only. Part 1, the records, the routes and the client, merged in #7131. The canvas (#7123) landed in `design/incoming/Wellness Member Home Page.dc.html`.
- **Builds on:** D7118 (`/api/wellbeing/reflections`), D7117 (the canvas, boards WLM00 to WLMH), D570 (the Wellbeing home), D699 (the phone Account sheet).

### What is built

- **The home (WLM-01, WLM-01B).** The home opens with "Take a moment to check in", followed by three period cards: Today, This week and This month. Each card shows its date in the member's calendar and its own state: ○ Not yet, ✓ Saved (with the time in the member's zone) or Couldn't be read. If the read fails, the page says "Couldn't load your reflections" with Retry, never "Not yet". The old weekly check-in card no longer writes from the home. Bookings and resources stay below.
- **One period (WLM-03, WLM-03W, WLM-03M) at `/wellbeing?period=today|week|month`.** This is the `?period=` parameter WLM-00 names on the existing route, so no new route is added. Each view has:
  - tabs for the three periods;
  - the editor, every question optional, using the Worker's score keys: day asks Energy, Stress and Sleep plus one line; week asks Stress, Sleep, Support, Decisions and Energy, plus a note and up to 8 topics; month asks the two prompts;
  - the period's private history, newest first, with Edit reflection (which opens that past period) and Delete (which asks first);
  - for the month only, "What you recorded", which counts only what was written;
  - for the week only, earlier check-ins (rows from before D7118), each with its own date and its own Delete;
  - Export, and the retention line.
- **Saving.**
  - Each save writes its own period and nothing else.
  - A save carries the reflection's `base_revision` and one `request_id` per attempt. A retry after a lost answer reuses that id, so the write lands once.
  - A failed save (503 `reflection_protection_unavailable`, `reflection_not_saved`) says what happened and keeps every word on screen.
  - A save refused with 409 `reflection_changed` offers "Reload the newer version".
- **The heart (WLM-02, WLM-02B, WLM-09F).** The popover opens Today directly. It has four states:
  - loading: "Checking today's reflection…";
  - a failed read: "Couldn't load your check-in." with Retry, replacing the old fall-through to "Take a minute";
  - not yet: Energy, Stress, Sleep and one line, with Save today;
  - saved: Edit reflection.

  It always offers "Open full Wellness home →". Escape closes it and returns focus to the heart.
- **The Account sheet (WLM-02P).** The row is renamed Wellness and opens `/wellbeing?period=today` in one tap. A failed read says "Couldn't load your check-in", never "Unreadable".
- **Export.** Export reads `GET /export` with the member's credentials (`wellbeingReflectionsApi.exportFile`, the route part 1 mounted) and saves the JSON file on the device.
