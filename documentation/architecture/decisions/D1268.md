## D1268 — Raise evidence part 7: the investor block live on the deal page, the round call and the diligence room

S22, 2026-10-27, for #3291 (part 7 of #2430). Frontend only. No migration.
Built on D1265's investor read (#3288, merged as #3462) on `main` at `3255b9f5`. Canvas:
`design/incoming/Raise Evidence Page.dc.html` (VR8–VR10, VR18).

### 1. What is built

- **`frontend/src/pages/cycles/evidence/InvestorEvidence.jsx`** (new). It replaces D1261's
  `EvidenceSummary` wait.
  - `InvestorEvidence`: the block, from one read.
  - `EvidenceSummary`: the deal page's Evidence tab, on `GET /api/deals/:id/evidence`.
  - `SharedEvidence`: the diligence room, on `GET /api/data-room/shared/:projectUid/evidence`.
  - It imports no `ui` index, so the round call and the room do not read as drawing the rail.
  - `RaiseEvidence.jsx` re-exports `EvidenceSummary`.
- **`evidenceModel.js`** grows the investor rules: `investorView`, `coverageTile`, `figureOf`,
  `sourceLine`, `recordDay`, `KIND_WORDS`, `refusalOf`. D1261's `WAITS.investor` is retired.
- **The three placements:**
  - The deal page: `/deals/:id/evidence`.
  - An investor's round call: the call read's own `evidence` (`services/roundCalls.ts`). It is
    placed only in `InvestorCallView`; `null` draws nothing.
  - The diligence room: `/research/diligence/:grantUid`, by the room's `project.uid`.

### 2. What each board draws

**VR8, before the NDA:**
- The lock pill and "[Company] shares what backs its claims. Figures, files and quotes open once
  the NDA is signed."
- One tile per area, kind and Source, with its count and the newest record date.
- `investorView` returns only `stage`, `line`, `coverage` and `partial`. A read that carried items
  before the NDA would still draw none.

**VR9, after the NDA:**
- The NDA pill and a tally: "N shared items: X with their figures, Y without."
- Each shared item, by area:
  - its kind in words;
  - its figure, or the read's own reason ("Shared as a summary only.", one investor's money);
  - its source line;
  - the states of the claims it backs.
- An item the read held back is simply absent. No row, count or word says something is hidden.

**VR10, one item:** a `Lightbox` (full screen on a phone) with:
- the area and the Source;
- the source line;
- the figure or its reason;
- the summary and the strength;
- a stale note;
- the claims it backs;
- the sharing line.

**VR18, the phone:** the same block. Its tiles stack in one column and the drawer is full screen.

### 3. The source line and D1269

A bank-derived item (`provider: 'mercury'`) reads "From the company's bank, as of [date]", from
the record's own `observed_on`, never the sync's. D1269 (#3292) records this as D840 §2's
amendment. The read already withholds every balance, line and counterparty (D1265 §3).

### 4. Refusals and failures

- **The deal page:**
  - a founder or staff reader (403 `investor_only`) reads "This is the investor's view. You read your
    evidence in Raise › Evidence.", with the link;
  - another 403 or a 404 says the evidence is not open to them;
  - any other failure is "not the same as none", with Try again.
- **The diligence room:** a refusal draws nothing, since the room already says what is open. A
  failure has Try again.
- **One block failing never blanks the others:** coverage, items and claims each carry their own
  Unreadable.
