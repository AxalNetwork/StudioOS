## D6204 — Reporting obligations carry a due date and days overdue (#6204)

The Reports inbox could not say who is late, or by how much. Its Due column always read "No due date recorded", because `GET /api/positions/kpi-compliance` returned none. Its Overdue filter and Chase all overdue treated every company that had not reported as overdue, even one still inside its time to report.

**Owner answer (2026-11-16, on #6204): per book, default 30.** Each reporting duty uses its own recorded `due_days`; otherwise a firm-wide default of 30 days after the period ends applies. The same answer settles proposal 12's "due day" question.

### What the Worker returns

`kpi-compliance` adds these fields to each company, for the period it measured:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `due_date` | `period_until` plus the term below, in UTC |
| `due_days`, `due_basis` | The term, and where it came from. `reporting_duty` is the duty's own `due_days`; `firm_default` is 30 (`REPORT_DUE_DEFAULT_DAYS`). |
| `days_overdue`, `overdue` | Days past `due_date` by the Worker's date, and only for a company that has not reported. The due day itself is not late. |
| `due_reason` | Why there is no due date, when there is none. |

Which duty applies:
- The term is read from `reporting_duties` rows that are `active`, have `due_rule = 'after_end'` and match the cadence measured.
- They must also be in the caller's book: the duties the caller holds (`holder_user_id`), or every duty for an admin's whole book.
- Several duties on one company take the earliest due day (`MIN(due_days)`).

Top-level fields:
- `overdue_count` and `overdue_project_ids`: the overdue set.
- `due_default_days`: 30.
- `due_read`: `ok` or `unreadable`.

If the duties cannot be read, no due date is claimed and no company is overdue. The reason reads "The reporting duties could not be read". The firm default is never filled in for a failed read.

Nothing is stored. As with `reporting_periods`, lateness is worked out on read.

### What the pages draw

- **Reports (`/portfolio/updates`).**
  - Each row's Due cell shows "Due 14 Nov 2026", or "Overdue by 11 days" over its due day. With no due date it shows Not recorded with the Worker's reason.
  - The Overdue filter and Chase all overdue use the Worker's overdue set (`row.overdue`), not every company that has not reported.
  - The board's Due date tile reads "N overdue" or "None overdue", with the rule.
  - The per-row Chase stays on every company that has not reported.
- **Positions and the Overview's attention list.** The Reporting chip reads "Overdue by N days" (the `Overdue` state: an icon and a word, drawn as late). A company that has not reported and is not yet due stays "Not received".
- **The Overview's Reporting card** adds an Overdue tile: the Worker's count, or Not recorded when the read carries no due data. It is never 0 for an unknown.
