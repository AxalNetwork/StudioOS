## D1353 — LinkedIn part 4: Refer & Earn's "Import from LinkedIn" works end to end

S11, 2026-10-28, for #3568 (part 4 of #3564). No migration and no Worker change. Basis: `main` at
`daed664fd1`.

### 1. What was broken

- **"Import from LinkedIn" imported nothing.** For a member signed in with LinkedIn, it showed a toast
  pointing at another button. For a member who wasn't, it asked the Worker to start sign-in and read
  `start.url`, but the Worker answers `authorize_url`, so the sign-in never opened either.
- **Upload CSV dropped people silently.** Every connection whose email LinkedIn left out was
  dropped without a word. A quoted name with a comma ("Cara, Jr") also lost its quotes while lines
  were joined, so its columns shifted and that contact's email was lost too.

### 2. The guided sheet (`frontend/src/pages/referrals/LinkedInImportSheet.jsx`)

"Import from LinkedIn" opens the shared right Sheet (D1317) at 520px, a full-height page under
640px.

1. **Download your connections from LinkedIn.** It links LinkedIn's data-export page
   (`https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/download-my-data`), with the path: Settings → Data
   privacy → Get a copy of your data → Connections.
2. **Drop or choose `Connections.csv`.** The file is read in the browser, up to 2 MB.
3. **The preview.**
   - How many have an email and can be invited.
   - How many have none: "LinkedIn didn't include an email for these; invite them by sharing your
     link on LinkedIn."
   - A file with no email column says so.

"Add N to your invite list" hands over only the rows with an email. The page puts them on its invite
list, each with its personalised link, as Upload CSV always did. Nothing is sent until the member
presses Invite on a contact.

**Signing in with LinkedIn is no step here.** LinkedIn gives no connections over its API (D1350
§1), so the export is the import. The sign-in stays on the connector page and Settings.

### 3. The page (`ReferralsPage.jsx`)

- The dead-end toast and the sign-in call are gone.
- Upload CSV uses the same counting reader, and its toast says how many had no email.
- **The `localStorage` hand-off stays.** It is not the OAuth return's: the connector page's CSV
  import (`pages/account/useConnectFlow.js`) writes it so Refer & Earn can send those invites
  without a second upload.

### 4. The reader (`frontend/src/lib/linkedinCsv.js`)

- `readLinkedInCsv(text)` returns `{ rows, no_email, header }`.
- `parseLinkedInCsv` still returns the rows, so the connector page is unchanged.
- Both parsers keep a quoted field's quotes until the field splitter, so a quoted comma, a doubled
  quote or a line break inside quotes reads as one field.
