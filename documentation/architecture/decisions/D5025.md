## D5025 — Package term states, change review and proration quotes (#5025)

Part 1 of #5025. It builds on D5024 part 2: accepted package offers are
immutable, so a renewal, upgrade, downgrade or change is always a new offer
that HQ accepts through D5024's route. This part adds the reads HQ needs
before accepting one. It writes nothing: no offer, grant, invoice or
adjustment changes. No migration.

### Term states

An accepted offer is a term from `starts_on` to `ends_on`, both days
included, read on the UTC day:

- `scheduled`: the term has not started.
- `active`: the term covers the day.
- `renewal_pending`: active, and a later draft or offered offer for the same
  licence exists.
- `superseded`: a later accepted term for the licence has started.
- `ended`: the term is over and no later accepted term has started.

The state is derived, not stored, so it can never fall out of step with the
dates.

### Change review

`services/packageLifecycle.ts` reads a draft or offered offer against the
licence's current term (the active one, else the latest scheduled one):

- Kind: `renewal` for the same package with the same programs, `upgrade`
  for a strict superset of programs, `downgrade` for a strict subset,
  `change` otherwise.
- Programs held, added and ending, and those the licence holds today as a
  standalone grant (no accepted package item names it) that the offer would
  bundle.
- The old accepted price against the new price (the accepted price if the
  offer has one, else the recommendation), with the difference only when the
  currency is the same.
- The country index movement between the two frozen recommendations
  (`country_index_ppm`); null when either is not recorded.
- When the change would take effect: the day after the current term ends,
  the offer's own start, and whether that start is inside the current term.
- For a downgrade or change, the open cohorts, claims and workflows of an
  ending program read `not_recorded`: no shared store names them yet, so the
  review says so instead of answering an empty list.

### Proration quote

With `effective_on`, the review adds an actual-day quote over the current
term: the remaining days from `effective_on` to the end, both included, over
the term's days. The unused share of the old annual fee is a credit and the
same share of the new fee a debit, each floored to whole cents with BigInt,
and the five-line trace is returned with the answer. A day outside the term
or a currency change is refused (`effective_outside_term`).

### Not decided here

`financial_rule` reads `not_decided`. The owner decides on #5025:

1. whether a mid-term change takes effect at the next term only, or
   immediately with proration;
2. which billing record carries a credit or debit;
3. the notice period for a downgrade.

Until then the quote is a quote, and acceptance stays D5024's.
