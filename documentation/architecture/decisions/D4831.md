## D4831 — Practice Engagements: PX-03's chips and cards, and the six-tab engagement sheet (#4831)

- **Status:** part 1 of 2, built 2026-11-14 by S16. It covers boards PX-03, PX-03b, PX-03c and PX-03d of the Practice v2 canvas (D4874, `design/incoming/Practice v2 Page.dc.html`).
- **Part 2:** the shared Client Sheet (PX-04). It closes #4831.
- **No Worker change, no migration, no new client method.**

### 1. The chips (PX-03)

- **The row:** `practice/engagements` takes the canvas's row, `Active · Renewal due · Proposed · Ended · All`, each with its count (`Active 2`).
  - It replaces PR2's `All · Signed · Renewal due · Ended · By client`.
  - `profile_zone_filters.test.mjs` now reads the row from the v2 canvas (`v2Zones`), as it does for Delivery and Earnings.
- **Active** is signed or renewal due: the set the Active tile counts, so the chip and the tile always agree.
- **Counts:** each chip narrows the board and is counted over the whole record (`chipRows`, `chipCounts` in `engagementBoard.js`). An unknown key shows everything, never nothing.
- **By client is gone.** It was a sort, not a filter, and the v2 board draws no sort.

### 2. The card (PX-03)

Each card shows the client, its lane as a pill, the scope line, a price, the date line, the scope, an activity line and **Open engagement →**.

- **Price.** The store keeps an amount per cycle and no billing period, so the card reads "$3,000 per cycle", not the canvas's "/ month".
  - Per call reads "per call".
  - Equity with no amount reads "Equity".
  - Any other missing amount reads "Price not recorded", never "$0".
- **Activity: "2 sessions · 3 deliverables".**
  - **Sessions** are the bookings by the engagement's linked client account that are pending, confirmed or completed. A cancelled or declined request was not a session.
  - A client with no linked account reads "Sessions need a linked client": nothing joins a booking to a name.
  - **Deliverables** are the ones filed under the engagement (`engagement_id`).
  - A source whose read failed says "could not be read". Neither half is ever drawn as 0.
- **One new read, tolerated:** `listMyAdvisorDeliverables`, beside the bookings read that was already there. Neither feeds the totals or the lanes, and the board stands if either fails. `advisor_practice_pr2.test.mjs` names it.

### 3. The engagement sheet (PX-03b to PX-03d)

`EngagementSheet.jsx` is a 548px `Sheet` (full height on a phone, as `Sheet` does). It has six tabs in canvas order: Overview · Scope · Sessions · Delivery · Agreement · Activity. The tabs are a `tablist` that the arrow keys move through.

- **Data:** the sheet draws the board's own rows, so a card and its sheet cannot disagree. Its only read is the engagement's history (`GET /me/engagements/:id/events`), for Activity and Overview's Latest activity.
- **Overview:**
  - Client, with "Founder account linked" or "Name only, no account linked".
  - State, Started, Term ends, Price, Cycles, Latest activity.
  - For an ended engagement, **Why it ended**.
- **Outcome (PX-03c).** On a signed or renewal-due engagement, the sheet offers the canvas's required choice: Renewed, Did not renew, Ended for another reason, then **Record outcome**.
  - It sends the same renewal decision as the board's form, with D5316's `end_reason` and the cycle count the row showed.
  - **Departure:** the canvas draws the choice on an engagement that has already ended. D5316 makes the Worker refuse an ending without a reason, so the choice comes at the moment of ending instead.
  - A row that ended before the reason existed reads "Not recorded". Nothing can record a reason after the fact, and the sheet does not guess one.
- **The #4684 warning.** When work under this engagement was already sent, Overview says "This engagement has work previously sent to J. Park. Changing the client does not transfer historical deliveries."
  - It names each sent version's own recipient.
  - An unsent version is not counted, even when it names someone.
  - When the deliverables could not be read, it says that instead of staying silent.
- **Scope:** scope line, in scope, not in scope (amber), shape, price and term end. It points to Correct terms, because signed terms change only as a recorded correction (D4715).
- **Sessions and Delivery:** the rows behind the card's activity line, each with a link to its zone.
- **Agreement.** Nothing links a signed agreement to an engagement, so the tab says "No agreement is linked to this engagement" and links to Contracts (`/advisor/advisory/contracts`), where the advisor's agreements still live.
  - It never shows an agreement matched by the client's name.
  - **Departure:** PX-03d draws the envelope, signers, View PDF and Audit trail. Showing them needs a stored link from engagement to agreement. That is backend work, not part of this package.
- **Refusals:** a refused write shows in the sheet, where it was made, not behind it.
