## D1027 — Cap table part 12: the investor's read-only Cap table panel (CT15), on the deal's Terms tab and the company page

- **Status:** filed by S21 as #2864, part 12 of Cap table (#2432, D1015 §6). Built by S22.
- **Depends on:**
  - part 4, who sees what (D1019), and the record-based views (D1130);
  - the deal page's Terms tab (D1153).
- **Migration:** none.
- **Canvas:** CT15, CT15o and CT15n of `design/incoming/Raise Cap Table Page.dc.html`. The deal page around the panel is DEAL's canvas (DL10, DL24).

### 1. The panel

`frontend/src/pages/raise/captable/InvestorCapTablePanel.jsx` draws the panel, and `investorPanelModel.js` holds its rules as pure functions.

**The header.**
- "Cap table · [venture]".
- "As of [date] · shared at [level] level by the founder". The date is the day of the read, and the view is the record at that moment.

**The read.** It is D1019's `GET /api/captable/access/:projectId`, made once. The Worker logs the open. The panel draws exactly what the investor's level returns, through the same `sharedView` the other cap-table reads use.

| Level | What is drawn |
|---|---|
| Investor (CT15) | See below |
| Own position (CT15o) | "Your position: [shares] shares ([group]) · [%] fully diluted", in lavender, and the group bar. No other holder is named. |
| Summary | The group bar and the rounds line |
| Nothing recorded | The Worker's sentence, never an empty table |

**At Investor level** the panel draws:
- the group bar;
- the positions, with the pool and the other holders as one line each, and the line "Investor level: the pool is one line, and who holds options is not shown.";
- "Your position" in lavender, but only on a row the Worker marks (§2);
- the recorded instruments;
- rounds and pricing, or "Rounds and pricing are not recorded on the cap table yet." when the record holds none;
- why the payout at exit values is not shown. The founder's exit values live in saved scenarios, and since D1130 a shared cap table leaves scenarios out. The panel says so instead of drawing a waterfall the level no longer returns.

**The group bar** is built only from what the level sent:
- for Own position and Summary, the group split;
- for Investor and Full, the positions' own percentages.

A share that is not recorded leaves the bar empty, with the Worker's reason, never 0%. A bar that does not reach 100% says why.

**Not shared (CT15n).** The panel shows "The founder has not shared the cap table with you." and "Ask [venture] for access. The founder answers on their Access tab."
- **"Ask for access"** opens an in-page dialog saying what asking does. Nothing is sent until it is confirmed.
- **After a sent ask,** or one the Worker already holds from the last day, the panel says so. Any other refusal stays in the dialog, in the Worker's words.

**A failed read** is "could not be read" with Try again. It is never "not shared".

### 2. Two Worker changes (`routes/captable_access.ts`, `services/capTableAccessRecord.ts`)

D1019 drew no ask route, and its Investor view could not tell the caller which row was theirs.

**`POST /api/captable/access/:projectId/ask`.**
- **Who:** an investor with a relationship to the venture (`investorProjectIds`: dealroom, introduction or converted watchlist), whom the founder has not shared with.
- **Once a day,** held by the `activity_logs` row it writes (`captable_access_asked`).
- **The founder is told** (in the app, linking `/raise/cap-table/access?project_id=`). They answer by choosing a level on their Access tab (D1026).
- **Nothing is granted here.**
- **Refusals:**

  | Case | Answer |
  |---|---|
  | An investor with no relationship, or another venture | 404, the same as no venture |
  | One already shared with | 409 `already_shared` |
  | A second ask within a day | 409 `already_asked` |
  | A non-investor or a support session | 403 |

**"Your position".** When a grant names the caller's holder (`holder_ref`), the Investor and Full views mark that holder's rows `mine: true`, and no other row. It tells the caller only which rows are their own. A grant naming no holder marks nothing.

The client gains `capTableAccessApi.ask`.

### 3. Where it is mounted

| Place | What is drawn |
|---|---|
| The deal page's Terms tab (`/deals/:dealId/terms`, DL10, DL24) | The panel, for an investor, replacing D1153's slot. Staff and the founder keep a slot saying investors read it here, at the level the founder shares, and the founder keeps it in Raise › Cap table. |
| The company page's Terms (`/portfolio/companies/:uid/terms`) | The panel, under the duties' wait, which stays Reporting part 5's |

The investor never opens `/raise/cap-table`.
