## D8279 — Show the decision roster on the Commit card (#8279)

The Commit decision card uses `roster.eligible` and `roster.to_vote` from the existing commit-room read (D6143). It appends `· you` to To vote when the signed-in user is a roster member whose `vote` is `null`. A cast vote, abstention or recusal clears that cue. This follows DX27 A in the Closing workspace canvas, board DCL29a, and completes the decision-card handoff from D6866 part 2.

If the decision has no roster, both figures read Not recorded and the card shows the reason from the read. If the read omits the roster, both figures read Unavailable. A recorded zero remains visible. Meeting invitees still appear only as invitees.
