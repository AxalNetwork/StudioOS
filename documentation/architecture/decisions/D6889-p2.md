## D6889 part 2 — Record position from a closing line, with the Portfolio form filled in (#6889)

Split out of #6889 as #7938 (owner answer A on #6889, 2026-11-20). D6889 part 1
showed each received line of a closed closing as In Portfolio or Not in
Portfolio, by the position's own `closing_line_uid`, and listed the canvas's
Record position button (DX23 A, D6158) as not built, because no Portfolio form
took a closing line.

### Decision

1. **The Worker (`POST /api/positions`, D1215).** When the body names a
   `closing_line_uid`, the line must be a line of a closing on that venture
   (as before, `404 closing_line_not_found`) and must be received
   (`409 closing_line_not_received`). One position per line: the insert is
   `INSERT … SELECT … WHERE NOT EXISTS` a position with that line, so two saves
   at once cannot both land; the one that loses answers
   `409 closing_line_already_recorded` with the `position_uid` that holds the
   line. A position that names no line is unchanged and never refused as a
   duplicate. No migration: the column (612) and its index already exist, and
   a unique index was not added because positions recorded before this rule
   could already share a line.
2. **The model (`closingWorkspaceModel.js`, `positionPrefill`).** Each received
   line that is Not in Portfolio carries a prefill built only from the closing
   read: the venture (`project_id`), the closing's `round_label` as the round,
   the amount received, the day its receiving evidence was accepted (or
   recorded), and the line's uid. The form records dollars, so a line in
   another currency hands no amount; its amount and currency are shown as a
   label for the Admin to convert. A missing round or day stays empty, never
   a guess. A closing with no venture offers no prefill.
3. **The closing workspace.** In the after-close Record in Portfolio table, an
   Admin's line that is Not in Portfolio has **Record position** (a 44px
   target on a phone). It opens `/portfolio/positions` with the prefill and the
   closing's address. The founder reads the label only, as in part 1.
4. **The Portfolio form (`PortfolioPositionsPage.jsx`).** The existing Record
   Round form, which already took a prefill from an IC decision, takes the
   closing line's too: it fills the fields, says which line and amount the
   position is for, sends `closing_line_uid`, and on save returns to the
   closing (only an in-app path is followed), where the line reads In
   Portfolio. A refused save shows the Worker's sentence, as every save on the
   page does.
5. **Matching stays the link.** A line is never matched to a position by
   venture name or amount; only `closing_line_uid` links them (D6889).

### Not in scope

The after-close totals and the cap-table card (D6866 part 2), and the
founder's access to `/deals/closing` (#5912).
