### 1. The Worker (`services/capRelTabs.ts`, routes in `cycle_raise.ts`)

Every route takes the module's gate: the venture's founder or an accepted co-founder. Staff are refused, as D1191 decided.

| Route | What it answers |
|---|---|
| `GET /capital-relationships/activity` | Every `raise.capital-relationships` log row for the venture, newest first, 25 a page. Each row has who wrote it (Founder, Co-founder, or Eadwyn), what, the investor, and its source. It filters by text, kind and investor. No email is returned. |
| `GET /capital-relationships/materials` | Per investor who has not passed: deck links made for them (`pitch_deck_share_tokens.prospect_id`), the data room's opens by their Axal account, and a sent Call for Capital they received. |
| `GET /capital-relationships/proposals` | The caller's own open proposals and drafts on this venture's investors. |
| `POST /capital-relationships/proposals` | One metered run over a logged note: the named step, or else the investor's newest step with a note. |
| `POST /capital-relationships/proposals/:uid/accept` | A person accepts one proposal, as written or edited. |
| `POST /capital-relationships/drafts` | One metered run: a follow-up, a thank-you after a pass, or an intro request. |

**Activity's sources:**
- **Typed:** a person's write.
- **Document · CSV:** an Import.
- **Proposed by Eadwyn:** a run, or a person accepting what it proposed.

An answer through a call, a deal-room commitment and a Mercury match write no log row here. Their stores record them, and Commitments lists each with its date. The read says so (`not_here`).

**Materials sent:**
- An investor's account is the verified Axal investor with the prospect's email, as Calls finds them (`roundCalls.ts`).
- The data room's column is for the room's owner alone (D1102). A co-founder reads "The data room's owner sees this".
- Nothing recorded reads "Not sent", and no account reads "Not on Axal". Neither is ever a zero.

**Eadwyn proposes; a person accepts (#2457 owner question 7).**
- Runs are `rail_write`, recorded on the page `/raise/capital-relationships` (`AI_SURFACE.raiseCapitalRelationships`, D552) and charged to the member who pressed. They are kept as `ai_rail_proposals` (D780).
- **A note's proposals are screened:**
  - each must quote the note word for word;
  - a next step must name no figure, and a past due date is dropped;
  - a move goes only to Contacted, Meeting or Diligence. Committed asks the person what was committed, and Passed is the person's call, so Eadwyn proposes neither, and an edit to them is refused (400).
- Each proposal carries an excerpt of its note so the panel can show it.
- **Accept** writes through the one prospect writer:
  - a next step through `setProspectNextStep`, keeping the investor's warm path;
  - a move through `updateProspect`.
  
  Then the proposal is decided, so a second press finds it gone (404). The write and the acceptance are logged.
- **A draft** is for the founder to send from their own email, and Axal never sends it.
  - A draft that names a figure is dropped (422 `draft_dropped`). Eadwyn never writes an amount.
  - A thank-you is only for an investor who passed. An intro names who is asked.
  - No email address goes to the model.

**Round and closes (CRL10):**
- `PUT /api/contacts/raise-round` takes `currency`, three capital letters, as #2457 owner question 5's one currency per venture.
- The round's changes and a close's addition or change are logged for Activity, by field name, never a figure.

**A stage move's log** now names both stages: "Investor edited: stage Meeting → Diligence".

### 2. The pages (`frontend/src/pages/raise/caprel/`)

- **Match (CRL7), `CapRelMatch.jsx`:**
  - D1190's match read, with the Fit, Sector, Geography, Fund size and warm-path filters, search, sort and pages.
  - Each fund shows its Fit and why ("Their focus … Your sector …"), its size or "Not recorded", and its warm path.
  - Investors on Axal is a count, never names.
  - Add to pipeline opens Add investor with the fund filled in, tracked by its directory id.
  - Your network lists the warm paths. "Ask for an intro" has Eadwyn draft the request; a fund not yet in the pipeline is added first.
- **Outreach (CRL8), `CapRelOutreach.jsx`:**
  - **Follow-ups:** Overdue, Today, This week and No next step. A passed investor is never due.
  - **Done, Snooze and Draft a message:** each opens a dialog that says what changes. Snooze moves the step a week from today, or from its own date when that is later.
  - **Drafts:** Copy, "Open in my email" (a `mailto:` link), Edit (Save my version) and Dismiss, each through a dialog.
  - **Materials sent:** the read above.
  - **Updates to prospects:** shared from Grow, or recorded and never emailed. "Share an update" opens Grow's share sheet (`/grow/update`), as owner question 6 decided.
- **Commitments (CRL9, CRL10), `CapRelCommitments.jsx`:**
  - "Committed against the target": counted statuses solid, Indicated and Soft hatched and marked not counted, and "Status not recorded" named apart and never in the bar.
  - Every commitment, each line under its investor with its status, source and date. Only the investor's strongest line counts.
  - A source that did not answer says so.
  - The closes, with New close and a state change, each in a dialog. Mercury's matched credits. Pro-rata.
  - Round settings (name, target, currency, close date, notes).
  - "Commitments become signed only in the closing room."
- **Eadwyn's proposals (CRL11), `CapRelProposals.jsx`:**
  - Opened from Pipeline ("Eadwyn's proposals"), or from an investor's lightbox ("Ask Eadwyn"), which runs on the press, once.
  - Proposals are grouped under their note, each beside the words it came from.
  - Accept, Edit and Dismiss each say what changes before the second press. Accept all takes at most 50.
- **Activity (CRL12), `CapRelActivity.jsx`:**
  - When, who, what and the source, with search and the kind and investor filters, 25 a page.
  - Export saves the rows on screen as a CSV in the browser.
- **Pipeline's Next step dialog** now sends the investor's warm path, which the writer otherwise cleared. That was a D1192 bug.

Every new colour has its `dark:` pair. On a phone, each tab is one column, and the wide tables scroll inside their card.

### 3. What is not counted, and never zero

- A commitment with no status is "Status not recorded" and never counted.
- A missing amount, size or total reads "Not recorded", never $0.

### 5. Not built here, and why

- **"Not for us" on Match:** hiding a fund from a venture's results needs a store of its own, and this part has no migration.
- **Match's "Stage they invest at":** the fund directory records no stage (D1190).
- **A pattern across investors' feedback (CRL11):** no read groups feedback by theme across investors. The panel says "Not read here yet".
- **Commitments' "Close or tranche" and "Route" columns:** a commitment records neither until a closing exists (CAPITAL, #2424). The table says so.
- **Mercury's unmatched credits** ("Match", "Not this round") stay on Overview's Mercury card (D840). Commitments lists the matched ones.
