## D1012 — Payments part 4: Catalogue complete — Needs you from the checks, every column, the product lightbox at its own address, and the states (#2831)

- **Status:** S07, 2026-10-25. Part 4 of Payments (#2386), from S21's integration (D1008, #2809).
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Payments Page.dc.html`: PAY1 (Catalogue), PAY2 and PAY2c (the product), PAY8a and PAY8b (empty), PAY9 (loading), PAY10 and PAY10b (errors), PAY11 (no permission).
- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** none. Every field drawn here is one parts 2 and 3 already return (D1010, D1011), plus the promo-code mirror's existing read.
- **Builds on:** D1008 (the page and its reads), D1010 (the checks, "shows on", the AI allowance per price, members per plan), D1011 (the change log read), D1014 (the phone form, the dark colours, `PayDialog`), D940 (the HQ-only card).

### 1. Needs you from the checks (PAY1)

- **The checks come from the catalogue read.** When it answers, part 2's `checks` say everything about the catalogue and the keys, and the page shows each check's own sentence as written. Blockers come first, then warnings, then the webhook's items.
- **One action each:**
  - a check that names a product reads "Review [product]" and opens that product (§3);
  - a key check (`secret_key_missing`, `publishable_key_missing`, `publishable_key_unknown`, `key_mode_mismatch`) reads "Open Stripe connection";
  - the webhook's items (no endpoint of ours, missing events, a disabled endpoint) read "Fix in Stripe connection". Part 2's checks do not judge the webhook, so these still come from the webhook read.
- **When the catalogue read fails,** the keys are judged from the config and summary reads as before (D1008), and a line says "The catalogue’s checks could not be read, so they were not run. This is not a claim that nothing needs you."
- **The heading is "Needs you · n".** Needs you waits until all four reads have settled, so it never draws a half list.
- "Not read here yet: D1008 part 2" is gone.

### 2. The table (PAY1)

- **Columns:** Product, Kind, Prices, AI allowance, Shows on, For, Status.
  - **Product:** the name links to the product's address. The line under it is the kind in words and the product ID.
  - **Kind:** Plan, One-time or Token pack.
  - **Prices:** the active prices; a one-time price now reads "499.00 USD · one time". An active plan with a yearly price shows "Yearly saves N%" above its prices. An active plan without one shows "No yearly price".
  - **AI allowance:** active plans only, from D1010's `allowance_microusd`, never computed here.
    - The monthly price's allowance reads "$5.00 / month", with "$4.25 on yearly" under it.
    - Anything else is "—", with the reason as its tooltip ("Only active plans include an AI allowance.", "An archived price gives no AI allowance.", "An AI allowance is counted in USD only.", …).
    - A read that carried no allowance says "Not recorded". It is never a zero.
  - **Shows on:** each surface from D1010's `shows_on`, in words: "/pricing", "Products page", "Products page · Profile bundles", "/incorporate", "/pricing · token packs". The Worker's sentence is the tooltip. A product that reaches no one is "—". A read without the field says "Not recorded".
  - **For:** the audience categories. A token pack reads "Every member".
  - **Status:** "✓ Active" or "– Archived". A product a check names carries the amber "● Needs you" under its status; that is the only issue marker, as the canvas draws it.
- **Archived rows: "Date not recorded".** The canvas's "Archived on [date]" has no source: the mirror keeps no archive date.
- **Groups:**
  - "Plans", "One-time · Incorporation, Sessions, Add-ons" and "Token packs · n · read only" (with "Managed on AI allowances →") hold the active products.
  - Every archived product sits in its own "Archived" group, last.
  - A group header counts its rows across every page.
- **The toolbar:**
  - search, from two characters, as before;
  - the kind chips with their counts;
  - **Filters ▾**, an in-page panel: Status (active and archived, active only, archived only), Shows on (any surface), and Needs you only. The button counts the filters that are on, "Filters · 1", as PAY12a draws it;
  - **Sort:** Name, A to Z (the canvas's); Name, Z to A; Needs you first.
  - The canvas draws neither menu open, so these options are this part's.
- **The pager:** "1–25 of n", rows per page 25, 50 or 100, and previous and next.
- **The row's ⋯ menu is not drawn.** Its Open is the product's link. Add a price, Change a price, Rename and Archive product are part 5's dialogs (#2832).
- **On a phone** (PAY12a, D1014), each card is now a link to the product's address.

### 3. The product lightbox (PAY2) at its own address

- **The address:** `/admin/platform/payments/products/[id]` is a route behind `hqOnly`, like the tabs. It opens over Catalogue (`tabOf` reads it as the Catalogue tab). Closing it returns to `/admin/platform/payments`. On a phone it is a bottom sheet (`PayDialog`, D1014).
- **The sections, in the canvas's order:**
  - **The header:** the name, "Plan · [the plan's label]" (or One-time, or Token pack), and "✓ Active" or "– Archived". Add a price and Change a price are drawn disabled, saying "Opens with D1008 part 5 (#2832). Until then, use the editor on Catalogue." A token pack has neither and says "Managed on AI allowances →".
  - **Where members see it:** each surface from `shows_on`, as a ↗ link to the public page, with the Worker's sentence. An archived product says members do not see it anywhere.
  - **Prices:**
    - columns Interval, Amount, AI allowance a month, Nickname and Status. Active prices come first, then archived ones;
    - the interval in words: Monthly, Yearly, "Every 3 months", One time;
    - "Yearly saves N%, computed from the two prices." when D1010 computed a saving;
    - price IDs only under Technical details.
  - **Members on this plan** (plans only), from `GET /api/admin/catalog/members` (D1010 §3):
    - "37 monthly · 12 yearly", the paid members on this product's prices;
    - the other live statuses as their own line;
    - the family's rows recorded before prices were stored, said apart and never counted under a price;
    - then "Archiving a price stops new sales. Members already on it keep their price."
  - **Promo codes that apply,** from the promo mirror (`GET /api/admin/promos`):
    - the codes checkout would take now (on, not expired, not used up), using the same rule as `services/promos` `promoState`;
    - each of those whose product list is empty (every product) or names this product;
    - a code whose product list could not be read is counted apart;
    - then "Open Promo codes".
  - **Checks:** this product's checks as amber lines (PAY2c), or "✓ No issues.".
  - **Recent changes:** the change log's three newest rows for this product (`?product=…&limit=3`, D1011), as "18 Oct 2026 · [who] · [action]". "Open the change log" opens the Change log tab already filtered to the product: `ChangeLog.jsx` now reads `?product=prod_…` from the address, and ignores anything that is not a product ID.
  - **Technical details** (PAY2c): the product ID, the plan key, every metadata key and value, and each price's ID.
- **Its reads:** the three are made only when the lightbox opens. Members is read for a plan only. Each read answers alone:
  - a failed one says "Could not be read" with Try again;
  - each names what it is not a claim of: that nobody is on the plan, that no code applies, or that the history is empty;
  - the catalogue's sections still draw.
- **An address naming no product** says so, with the ID under Technical details. While the catalogue reads, it shows Reading; when the catalogue fails, it says so with Try again.

### 4. The states

- **PAY8a, not connected:** the config read's mode, or the catalogue's account mode, is `unconfigured`. Catalogue is then one card: "Stripe is not connected on this platform yet." and "HQ adds the account’s keys at deploy. Until then nothing can be sold, and this page shows no products." There are no tiles and no table. A failed read is never taken for this.
- **PAY8b, no products yet:** "No products yet." and "Make the first one, or sync products you made in the Stripe dashboard.", with New product and Sync from Stripe drawn disabled until part 5. The tiles still draw.
- **PAY9, reading:** the table's real headers with eight skeleton rows. Needs you waits.
- **PAY10b, the catalogue failed:** "We could not load the catalogue." and "The rest of Payments still works.", with Try again. The tiles still draw, and it is never drawn as "no products". PAY10's failed Stripe tile was already D1008's. PAY10's refused-write toast is part 5's.
- **PAY11, a studio admin:** `hqOnly`'s card (D940) with Payments' words and doors, from `pages/hq/hqNoticeModel.js`:
  - "HQ sets what members pay. You are signed in as a studio admin, so this page stays closed rather than opening and refusing each action.";
  - "Go to Studio" and "See the public pricing page";
  - other HQ pages keep "Go to Studio" and "My licence".
