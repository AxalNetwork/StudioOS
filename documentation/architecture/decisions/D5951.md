## D5951 — Revenue ledger: a guarded HQ route to reconcile `financial_events` rows (#5951)

- **Status:** built by S05 for #5951, 2026-11-15. Part of #5038, for its acceptance box "Refund/dispute/payment events have a canonical reconciliation path".
- **Basis:** D5299 §3 says: "No route calls it yet; a later slice gives it one."
- **Migration:** none. The D5299 migration (`202611100929_financial_events.sql`) already holds the state, the actor, the time, the note and the immutability trigger.
- **Route:** `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/admin_financial_events.ts`, mounted by its own `mount` export at `/api/admin/financial-events`.

### 1. The reads and the writes

Both are Super Admin only (`requireSuperAdmin`), on HQ.

**`GET /api/admin/financial-events?state=&kind=&currency=&from=&to=&cursor=&limit=`:**
- **Order and paging:** newest first, paged by `cursor` (the last id read), 50 a page by default and at most 100.
- **Filters:**
  - reconciliation state;
  - kind;
  - currency, as a three-letter code;
  - the day the row occurred, with `from` and `to` as inclusive `YYYY-MM-DD` days. They are compared through `datetime()`, because the stored timestamps are ISO (D124).
- **Validation:** an invalid filter answers 400.
- **What each row holds:**
  - its refs;
  - its attribution, with `unattributed` naming the dimensions that are NULL (`unattributedFields`);
  - its amounts in its own currency. A missing amount is null, never 0, and nothing is summed across rows.
  - its reconciliation: state, time, who and note.
- **The allowed moves:** the response carries `RECONCILIATION_MOVES`, so a caller can offer only those.

**`POST /api/admin/financial-events/:uid/reconcile` with `{ from, to, note? }`:**
- **States:** `from` and `to` must be states; otherwise it answers 400.
- **Moves:** only a move in `RECONCILIATION_MOVES` is accepted, anything else is 400. `matched` and `excluded` are final.
- **Notes:**
  - a note is required for `discrepancy` and `excluded` (400 `note_required`);
  - a note is at most 500 characters.
- **An unknown `uid`** answers 404.
- **How the change is made:** through `setReconciliationState`, compare-and-set on the state the caller read.
- **A stale `from`** changes nothing and answers 409 `state_changed`, carrying the state the row holds now.
- **The actor and the time** are stored by that function, in `reconciled_by_user_id` and `reconciled_at`.
- **The audit:** a successful move is recorded through `logAdminAction` as `financial_event_reconciled`, in `admin_audit_log` and `activity_logs`. A refusal writes nothing.

### 2. Nothing else changes

- **The body's other fields are ignored.** The route reads only `from`, `to` and `note`.
- **The only UPDATE is the service's.** D5299's trigger refuses any change to another column, and its delete trigger keeps every row. The test checks both.

### 3. Open question: step-up

#5038 does not decide whether a reconciliation needs a TOTP step-up, as HQ's refund route does (`requireSuperAdminWriteBar`, D224).

- **Built without it:** the elevation alone (`requireSuperAdmin`), as the issue allows.
- **Why that may be enough:** a reconciliation moves no money and is fully audited.
- **The PR asks the owner.** If the answer is yes, the change is the POST's one guard.

### 4. Not built here

- **No frontend method:** there is no screen. #5038 asks for none, and the "Reconcile…" on the Revenue v2 canvas is a different action. A screen would need its own design brief.
- **No change** to the writer, the triggers or `setReconciliationState`.

### 5. Tests

**`cloudflare-worker/test/financial_events_reconcile_5951.test.ts` (5 cases),** on the real D5299 migration:
- the mount, and Super Admin only (a plain admin and a founder are refused);
- the list:
  - its order;
  - each filter, with `to` inclusive of the whole day;
  - the paging;
  - unattributed fields named;
  - a missing amount as null;
  - 400 for bad filters;
- an allowed move stores the actor, the time and the note, and is audited;
- refusals:
  - a stale `from` is 409 with the state now;
  - a final state and an unknown state are 400;
  - a missing note is 400 for both `discrepancy` and `excluded`;
  - an over-long note is 400;
  - an unknown row is 404;
  - nothing moved, and refusals are not audited;
- extra body fields change no column, and the trigger refuses a direct edit or delete.

**Mutation checks:**
- removing the note check turns the refusals case red;
- removing the GET's Super Admin guard turns the access case red.
