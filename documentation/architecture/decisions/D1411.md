## D1411 — Storage: article uploads, covers and generated images count toward the author's plan (#3894)

- **Status:** built by S17, 2026-10-31. Closes #3894.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `07a47f34a7`; D1409 §6's "UNVERIFIED: storage" line, and the #3694 canvas intake (D1408). D1409 released this number; #3894 reassigned it.
- **Migration:** none. No row records an article object's size, so the sizes are read from R2's own listing. A size column was not needed.
- **Code:**
  - `cloudflare-worker/src/services/storageUsage.ts`:
    - the `articles` area (`readArticleArea`), counted in `readStorageUsage` and `storageUsedBytes` (the upload guard);
    - HQ's per-owner totals (`readArticleOwners`, in `readStorageByOwner`);
    - `forgetArticleStorage`.
  - `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/articles.ts` (cover, body upload) and `routes/article_assist.ts` (generate, discard) call `forgetArticleStorage` after they write or delete under `articles/<id>/`.
  - `routes/article_assist.ts`: the image press weighs the author's plan, not the presser's.
  - `frontend/src/pages/account/usageModel.js` (`STORAGE_AREAS`: "Articles") and `storagePageModel.js` (`AREA_PLACE`: "your articles", `/articles/draft`).

### 1. Whose plan

An article's objects count to its **author** (`articles.author_user_id`). That holds whoever uploaded or pressed.

- **Generated images:** the bytes live in the author's article, so they count to the author, as S21 recommended on #3894. The charge for the run stays with the presser (D1409).
- **The press's guard:** `assertStorageRoom` names the author: `{ userId: author }`. D1409.md §4 is updated.
- **Who can press:** only the author or an admin (`loadOwned`). Staff writes pass the guard (D1170), so in practice the guard weighs the author's own press. The two owners differ only for an admin, who passes either way.
- **The refusal:** an author's refusal reads `counts_to: 'you'`.

An article is the member's own, tied to no company. Its bytes sit in the usage read's personal row ("files tied to no company").

### 2. What counts

Every object under `articles/<id>/` of each of the member's articles counts:

- body uploads (`img-<uuid>.<ext>`, `POST /api/articles/:id/image`);
- generated images (`img-<uuid>.<ext>`, D1409 part 2), including ones proposed and not yet placed;
- covers (`cover-<uuid>.<ext>`, `POST /api/articles/:id/cover`).

These do not count:

- an older cover still under `news/`;
- an object whose article is no longer on record.

The area's kind is `other`. Its label is "Articles"; the largest-files list names each object by its article's title, and Open goes to `/articles/draft`.

### 3. How it is read

- **One member** (`readArticleArea`):
  - one `SELECT id, title FROM articles WHERE author_user_id = ?`, at most 200 articles (`ARTICLE_AREA_MAX`);
  - then one R2 `list` per article, with the prefix `articles/<id>/` and its trailing slash, so article 5 never lists 55's objects;
  - at most 10 pages of 1,000 objects per article.
  - A member with no article makes no R2 call.
- **HQ's totals** (`readArticleOwners`): one listing of the whole `articles/` prefix (at most 100 pages), each object counted to its article's author.
- **Cache:** each article's listing is kept for a minute in the isolate. The routes that write there forget it (`forgetArticleStorage`), so an upload, cover, generation or discard is read afresh in the same isolate. Another isolate can read a listing up to a minute old. This is the only cached area: the others are D1 reads.

### 4. Unknown, never 0

Past either bound, without the `FILES` binding, or on any failed list, the area is unknown. That makes the whole total unknown, as for any unreadable area (D1169), never an invented empty store. With the total unknown, the guard passes the write as `unreadable` (D1170): a read failure of ours never refuses a member's file. A member with no article reads 0 files, 0 bytes, known, even without the binding.

### 5. Not changed here

- `POST /api/articles/:id/image` and `/cover` still write without `assertStorageRoom`. Their bytes now count to the author's total, and they are refused by nothing.
- Weighing them before the write is a one-line change in each route. It is left for the owner to decide, since it would newly refuse an author's upload.
