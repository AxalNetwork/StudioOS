### 2. As built

**The reads.** Each answers alone, and one failing costs only its own blocks.

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/admin/partner-profiles/counts` | The four tiles, the To review tab's count, the Drafts note and the weekly chart |
| `GET /api/admin/partner-profiles` with the tab's choices | The queue and the All profiles table: rows, totals and pages |
| The same list, unfiltered, newest first, 100 a page (the summary) | The status chips and the All profiles tab's count (the first page's `totals` cover every profile), the filter choices (`options`), and on All profiles the categories chart, which pages through every row up to 1,000 |

- **The list is logged by reference** for each read (D625). So the page asks again only when a choice changes or on Refresh, never on a timer.
- **The categories chart** counts the newest 1,000 profiles, and says so, if a studio ever holds more.

**To review (PRF1).**
- **The four tiles:**
  - **Waiting:** the count, with "oldest [n] days".
  - **Past 72 hours:** the count, amber when above 0, with the bands "[n] under 24h · [n] under 72h".
  - **Verified in 30 days:** "Not recorded", with the Worker's reason (part 2).
  - **Agreements out for signature:** the profiles whose first agreement's envelope is out (D993), with "[n] not delivered" when any are, else "sent from this page". A signing store that cannot be read says so with the Worker's reason.

  A measured 0 shows as 0 (PRF7). A failed read says "Could not be read" on each tile.
- **The queue** is the waiting profiles (`status=pending`, so Drafts are out), oldest first.
  - **Columns:** Waiting, Person, Source, Onboarding category (with Founder track and incorporation for a founder), Entity (with its type), Account and Identity check.
  - **Waiting** is a pill: "45m", "20h" or "4d", amber from 72 hours exactly. No stamp reads "—", never "0h".
  - **Source** names each of D990's derived sources: Onboarding chat, Partner invitation, Eadwyn questions. A profile with none says "None recorded".
  - **Account** is the role, "Explorer" with the Exploring review's suggested role, or "No account yet".
  - **Identity check** is KYC & KYB's word for its state. With no account it is a dash whose tip says so.
  - **Tools:** search from two characters; Source, Onboarding category and Founder track filters; the sort (Waiting longest, Newest, Name); "1–25 of n" and pages of 25.
- **The Drafts note** under the queue: "Drafts are not in the queue: [n] profiles have nothing to review yet." It links to All profiles on the Drafts chip (`/admin/partner-profiles/all?status=draft`).
- **The states:**
  - **Filtered, no match:** "No waiting profile matches", never "Nothing waiting".
  - **Nothing waiting (PRF7a):** "Nothing waiting. Every profile has a decision.", with a link to All profiles.
  - **No profile ever (PRF7b):** "No partner profiles yet." in place of the queue.

**All profiles (PRF2).**
- **"Profiles captured and decided, per week":**
  - twelve weeks of captured profiles from the counts read, as D893's grouped columns, with the current week hatched and "so far";
  - Verified and Rejected are drawn as a dashed "not recorded" mark on each week, with the Worker's reason above the chart, until part 2 records decision dates;
  - "View as table" shows the same rows, with "not recorded" in words.
- **"Onboarding categories":**
  - Founder is split into Spin-Out, Strategic Scale and track not given; every other category is one bar;
  - "No category captured" is a dashed line with its count, never a bar;
  - a bar, or a Founder segment, filters the list below;
  - it can also be viewed as a table.
- **Status chips:** All, Waiting, Verified, Rejected and Drafts, each counting every profile. A status the Worker should never store is counted apart, as Not recorded under All. Picking Drafts says what a Draft is.
- **Filters:**
  - Source, Onboarding category, Founder track, Entity type, Agreement, Has an account and Identity check;
  - Captured from and to;
  - "Not recorded" as a choice where D990 offers it.

  **Reviewed by** is drawn and disabled, with part 2's reason; D990 refuses it until then. **Clear filters** shows once anything is set.
- **Sort:** Newest (the default), Waiting longest and Name. Reviewed waits for part 2, as D990 refuses it.
- **The table:** Person, Status (Draft its own pill), Source, Onboarding category, Entity, Agreement, Reviewed and Captured.
  - **Reviewed** reads "Not recorded" on a verified or rejected profile, with part 2's reason as its tip, and "—" on one not decided.
  - **Agreement** is the agreement, with its envelope under it (D993): "Out for signature since 3 Oct", "Signed 5 Oct", "Not delivered" (amber), "Declined" or "Voided". No agreement reads "Not sent", and an envelope is never shown without one.
  - **Captured** is the day, in UTC as D1 stores it.

**Both tabs.**
- **Loading (PRF8):** the header, tabs and tools draw at once. Tab counts are grey bars, tiles are skeletons, and the list shows eight skeleton rows.
- **A failed list (PRF9a):** "We could not load partner profiles. This is not a sign that there are none." with Try again, and Details showing the HTTP status and the Worker's code. The tiles and charts still answer.
- **A failed record read** is said in the page, as D989 drew it.
- **Dark (PRF12):** every block carries its dark classes. The amber pills (Waiting, the age past 72 hours) keep their fill.

**The chart part.** `ui/Charts.jsx`'s `GroupedColumns` draws a `null` value as a short dashed mark on the baseline, never a zero-height bar. It is its own commit, as the house rule asks; `ChartTable` already wrote "not recorded".
