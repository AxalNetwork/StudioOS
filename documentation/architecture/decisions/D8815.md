- **Status:** built 2026-11-27 by S34. Part 3a of #2403 (the Worker only). Part 1 is #5290 (the shared client), part 2 is #6915 (D2403, the connection). Not deployed, not live verified.
- **No canvas.** This part adds no screen and no frontend API method, as TikTok 2a (#5863) did not. The screen issue adds them, after the X1-X5 export; nothing here waits for it.
- **Owner answers it builds on** (#2403, 2026-11-16): (2) the founders app, with a per-founder monthly cap in StudioOS; (7) sync on demand only; (8) mentions stay out.
- **Migration:** `202611270444_8815_x_grow_facts.sql` (`x_grow_links`, `x_grow_audience_facts`, `x_grow_post_facts`, `x_founder_usage`). HQ's own X (migration 068) holds `x_accounts` and `x_posts`; no name is reused.
- **Precedent:** TikTok 2a (D5863) for the shape: one account per venture, the first sync links it, every landing through `connector_fed_items`, `parts.from_<provider>` on Distribution and Revenue and traction, delete through the ledger, a purge on disconnect.

### 1. What lands

| Store | Module | Figures | Ledger kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| `x_grow_audience_facts` | Revenue and traction | `followers`, `following`, `post_count`, one row per venture per sync day (`UNIQUE (project_id, snapshot_date)`) | `x_audience_snapshot` |
| `x_grow_post_facts` | Distribution | each of the founder's own posts' `impressions`, `likes`, `reposts`, `replies`, `quotes`, `bookmarks` and `posted_on` (`UNIQUE (project_id, post_id)`) | `x_post` |

**Never stored:** post text, the handle, the display name, or any other user's id. Nothing is written to `project_metrics` or `custom_kpi_values`.

**The reads** (`parts.from_x`, `services/x/growFeeds.ts`):
- **Revenue and traction:** the follower count with its change since the previous snapshot, and per month (the newest six) followers at the month's last snapshot, posts, impressions and engagement. The note: "shown beside revenue and never as revenue".
- **Distribution:** the same, plus the newest 20 posts, each with its own figures and engagement.
- **Engagement is computed** (likes + reposts + replies + quotes + bookmarks) and says so (`engagement_note`). A post missing any of the five has no engagement figure. A month's total leaves out a post X gave no figure for, and says how many: "1 post had no engagement from X; the total leaves it out". A month whose every post lacks the figure is `null` with its reason. Never 0.
- **States:** `team_only`, `not_linked`, `not_recorded` (linked, nothing landed), `ok`, `unreadable` (the rest of the module still loads).
- **Team only** (#2403's table): `isTeamViewer` (the founder or a member linked to the venture's company). An admin, partner, advisor or investor who reads the venture gets `team_only` and the sentence, as Mercury's figures do.
- `can_sync` is true for the member whose X serves the venture (anyone on the team before a link), writing, not in a support session. `can_delete` is any writer.

### 2. The sync

**`POST /api/cycles/grow/x/sync { project }`**:
1. the caller's own active X connection (`x_not_connected`, 409), switched on for the venture's company (`company_not_enabled`, 403); a support session is refused first (403);
2. the founder's monthly budget: none left is **429 `x_monthly_cap_reached`**, before the link, the token refresh or any call to X;
3. the link (`venture_served`, `serves_other_venture`, 409);
4. one `users/me` read, then the founder's newest posts, at most `POST_PAGE_BUDGET` (2) pages of 100, reposts excluded;
5. `last_synced_at`, and `logGrowWrite` with counts only: "Grow synced from X: 1 snapshot, 3 posts, 0 kept deleted".

The answer carries `counts` and `reads: { used, left, cap }`.

- **One failed read** leaves the other's landing intact and the sync is "Partly synced" (`x_grow_links.partly_synced`, shown as `link.partly_synced`). **Both failing** answers the read's refusal: `rate_limited` (429), `sign_in_again` (409: a 401, a refused or missing refresh, an unreadable connection), `x_busy` (503: another request holds the refresh lease), `x_unreadable` (502). X's body never reaches the founder (the shared client's rule).
- **`DELETE /api/cycles/grow/x/facts/:id?project=&kind=audience|post`**, any writer. The ledger row keeps no `local_id`, so the figure stays deleted through every later sync.

**Departure from #2403's Backend section, on purpose:**
- **Per-post rows, not stored monthly totals.** #2403 sketched one `x_grow_facts` table of `(kind, period, value)`. #8815's acceptance asks for "per-post impressions and engagement as facts", and a stored month total would keep counting a post the founder deleted. The month figures are computed when read, from the posts that are there.
- **The newest 200 posts each sync, not "since the last sync".** An impression count keeps growing for days; a post read only once would freeze at its first figure. Re-reading the newest 200 costs the same as #2403's cap of 200 and keeps recent posts current. A post older than the newest 200 is not re-read, and its last figures stay.
- **No link or profile clicks.** They are `non_public_metrics`, which X gives only for posts of the last 30 days (#2403, Metrics). Asking for them on an older post is an error whose shape is not confirmed, and one bad field would fail the page. They wait for the owner's first live sync to settle it. `xFeeds.ts`'s Distribution sentence no longer promises them.

### 3. The monthly read budget (owner decision 2)

`services/x/budget.ts`, table `x_founder_usage (user_id, period YYYY-MM, reads)`.

- **Counted in what X bills for**: one resource per user a read returns, one per post a page returns (#2403's pay-per-use table, **UNVERIFIED**).
- **`X_FOUNDER_MONTHLY_READS = 1000`** per founder per UTC month. That is about five full syncs, or one a week with room to spare. It is a constant, not a setting; see the owner question below.
- **Reserved before every billed call.** Each read (`services/x/reads.ts`) reserves what it may be billed for before it is sent, by a compare-and-set on the month's row: 1 for `users/me`, and up to 100 for a page. When fewer than 100 are left, the page asks X for only what is left (`max_results`). When fewer than X's minimum page of 5 are left, the read is refused. A reservation that loses every race is a refusal, never a call without one.
- **Refunded after.** A page refunds the posts it did not return; a failed call refunds everything.
- **A spent month mid-sync** stops it "Partly synced" with `counts.capped: true` and "monthly reads used" in the log line. Whatever landed stays.
- **Per founder, not per connection.** Disconnect deletes the facts and never the budget row, so reconnecting does not reset a spent month.

### 4. Disconnect

`providers/x.ts` `disconnect` still revokes both tokens best effort. It then calls `purgeXConnection`, one batch that deletes the connection's `x_audience_snapshot` and `x_post` ledger rows, its facts and its link. The route deletes the row whether or not X answered (D2403), so the purge does not wait on the revoke either. This differs from TikTok, where a failed revoke keeps everything.

### 5. Registry, page, ledger

- **`integrations/xFeeds.ts`:** `audience` and `posts` are `built: true`, decision D8815. `channel` (the proposal) stays `false` for its own part, as TikTok's #5864 and Facebook's #5860 were. `calls` are relative to `https://api.x.com/2`, as `reads.ts` names them (`/users/me`, `/users/:id/tweets`).
- **The registry's** `pulls` and `feeds` follow the built entries (unchanged code). **The connector page** has the two read tools and says what lands where, that Grow figures are the team's only, that a deleted figure stays deleted, that each sync counts against the monthly reads, and that disconnecting removes them.
- **`FED_LEDGER_PROVIDERS` gains `x`** (D2403 said part 3), so "Now fed by" counts what landed per module, not Not recorded.
- **`services/xClient.ts` gains `xApiGet`**, one authenticated GET under `api.x.com/2` with the client's breaker, 429, 401 and deadline handling. `services/x/reads.ts` is the only file that names the founder's Grow paths. `connector_pages_d753` reads X's tools from `reads.ts`, not from `xClient.ts`, whose writes are HQ's own posting.

### 6. UNVERIFIED

Nothing here was checked against X. Every one of these is a constant in `services/x/reads.ts` or `budget.ts`:
- the `public_metrics` names `followers_count`, `following_count`, `tweet_count`, `impression_count`, `like_count`, `retweet_count`, `reply_count`, `quote_count` and `bookmark_count`;
- `exclude=retweets`;
- `max_results` between 5 and 100;
- `meta.next_token` with `pagination_token`;
- billing per resource returned.

The owner's first live sync checks them, and records `verified_on` on the connector page.

### Owner question

**The budget's number.** 1,000 reads a founder a month is chosen here, not given. At #2403's prices that is $1 at the owned-read rate, or $5 at $0.005 a post read (**UNVERIFIED**). **Recommended default: keep 1,000** until the first month's usage page shows the real cost. Changing it is one constant.
