- The sizing sheet is V18 plus eight inputs, each with unit, currency, reference year and a Source line (an attached observation, a market source, or the word Assumption with a note). Results come only from the Worker: a preview marked "Not saved", "How it was computed" in words, and the limit that set Obtainable. Prices share a line only on identical terms; otherwise "Not comparable" with the Worker's reason. Nothing is converted, annualised, averaged or totalled; withheld rows are a count only (RST-23).

Would pay records and service-engagement readings sit in their own blocks (RST-24).
- The report is assembled by the Worker in twelve parts; "Take version [3]" freezes it, with Markdown and CSV per numbered version; withheld values are named with the reason; Reviewed by you, Verdict and Needs review are three words never mixed (RST-25).
