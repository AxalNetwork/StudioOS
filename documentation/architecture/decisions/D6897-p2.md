### What a PDF is

The canvas gives both share sheets an **Output** choice: Link or PDF.

- A **link** can be stopped and its opens are counted (D6864).
- A **PDF** is a copy. Once downloaded, nobody can stop it and its opens are not counted. The sheet says so before and after the download, and so does Shared links: "A PDF is a copy: it cannot be stopped and its opens are not counted."

A PDF has no expiry and no view limit. The Worker refuses those fields on a PDF (`invalid`), and the client sends neither.

### The content is the link's content

The Worker builds exactly what a link would freeze. For the pack that is `packBody` and the same projection. For the snapshot it is `readinessBody`: seven areas, no overall score, no Product-market fit.

The pack's label, reader and NDA checks are now one function, `packChoice`, shared by the link and the PDF. So a PDF after an NDA needs the same confirmation and date, and an empty pack is refused the same way. Private items, unconsented quotes, bank lines and investors' names or money never reach either.

### Recorded, not stored

`raise_share_pdf_copies` records that a copy left. Each row holds:
- the output and the label;
- the reader, and the NDA day;
- the item count and the content hash;
- who downloaded it, and when.

It holds no token and no copy of the content. The PDF itself is drawn in the browser from what the Worker returned; there is no Worker PDF route, as with D693's memo. Each copy is also logged in the cycle activity (`evidence_pack_pdf_copied`, `readiness_snapshot_pdf_copied`).

The copy is recorded before it is drawn. If the browser cannot draw it, the sheet says so, and the copy stays listed: what the Worker returned never reached a reader.

### Shared links

`listPackLinks` returns `copies` beside `links`, newest first and capped at 100 (`copies_capped`). The page lists them under **PDF copies · n**. Each row shows:
- the label;
- what it shares, e.g. "Evidence pack · 6 items · after NDA" or "Readiness snapshot · 7 areas";
- "Downloaded" and the day.

A copy has no state, no views and no Revoke, and the revoke route answers 404 for a copy's uid.

### The A4 page

The page is A4 portrait: 794 × 1123 in the board's design px. It shows, top to bottom:
1. the venture and what the page is;
2. the date, and that the page does not update;
3. for the pack, the reader; for the snapshot, the founder's covering line;
4. the table;
5. a foot.

The wording is the reader pages' own:
- "Not scored yet", never 0;
- "Team is assessed by a person";
- a figure only where the founder shared it after an NDA, otherwise "Value withheld:" with its reason;
- a Mismatch is never hidden, and the founder's note sits beside it.

A long table continues onto a new page with its header row repeated, so no row is ever clipped. Every page ends with a footer: what the page is, the day it was made, "Page n of m" and "a copy: it does not update". jsPDF is imported on demand, so it stays out of the initial bundle.
