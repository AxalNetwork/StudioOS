## D4633 — Deals: the commitment shown is the selected firm's, a late answer changes nothing, and Ask means one figure (#4633)

- **Status:** Built for #4633, 2026-11-07. Worker and frontend.
- **Builds on:** D467 (a transfer carries its firm), D1153 (the investor's own commitment card), D1155 (who reads which commitment), D1149 (a missing figure stays missing).
- **No migration.**
- **Package 3 of 5** of the Deals follow-up. It is built beside #4632, which owns the persisted transitions; the two share `routes/deals.ts` but no handler.

### What was decided

1. **One firm context for an investor's commitments.**
   - `GET /api/deals/:id/commitments` resolves the firm exactly as committing and withdrawing do: the verified active firm, else the investor's only firm.
   - An investor in several firms who has chosen none gets 400 `firm_required`. The read never guesses.
   - Each row names its firm (`company_id`, `company_name`) and says whether it belongs to the context (`in_context`, `firm_context`).
   - The card says whom the commitment is held for: "Held for Alder Ventures." or "Held in your own name."
   - The key strip reads the same row.
2. **Historical commitments with no firm (`company_id` NULL) follow a documented policy.**
   - They belong to the investor, but to no firm.
   - In the no-firm context they are the investor's own and can be acted on, as before.
   - When a firm is selected they are listed apart, marked `legacy`, with "Recorded before commitments named a firm. Ask the studio to assign it to a firm; until then it cannot be changed or withdrawn here." No action is offered on them.
   - Before this change they were the live row only by accident, because the read took the first live row whatever its firm.
3. **Answers are scoped to the route and the firm they were asked for.**
   - **DealRoomPage.** Its reads (the deal, the stage, the commitments) carry the deal id they started for and a serial number. An answer for a deal the address no longer names, or one superseded by a retry, lands nowhere. Nothing is drawn, an action least of all, while the loaded deal is not the one in the address.
   - **The Commitment tab.** It reads again when the deal or the selected firm changes, and drops an earlier answer. `myCommitment` treats a read for another deal as still loading.
   - **The tabs' shared `useRead`.** It drops an answer from a superseded read.
4. **Ask is the fundraising target.**
   - `roundAsk(deal)` (`components/deals/dealMoney.js`) is `target_raise`. It falls back to `amount` only for deals drafted before the target existed. When neither is recorded the Ask is null, never 0.
   - The Worker's `progress_pct` and the deal-flow `ask` already used this order.
   - The Studio lens (which read `amount` first), the Deal Flow table (which read `amount` only), the flow, the key strip, the proposed terms, the round card and the closing desk now all read `roundAsk`. Editing the target therefore moves every one of them together.
