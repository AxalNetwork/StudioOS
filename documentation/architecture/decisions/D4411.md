## D4411 — Recording consent and Quotes OK (#4411)

The interview list used Consent for permission to quote, while Record a
session records permission to record. These remain separate permissions.

The Customer research list reads recording consent from its existing latest
session join (highest session id within the interview and venture). Both the
page and count use the same join and bound recording-consent predicate. Rows
carry `recording_consent: { status, method }`; the existing `consent` row key
continues to come only from `quote_consent` and is labelled Quotes OK.

Recording statuses are Given, Declined and Not asked. A missing session or an
unrecorded status is Not asked. Given includes its known method in words; an
unknown method is omitted rather than guessed. A method by itself never
implies permission. The existing chat-reply method remains readable. Both
table values are pills with words as well as colour, and their filters are
independent and can be combined. The quote filter keeps Agreed · deck-eligible.

The interview record uses the same recording normalization and shows two
explicit facts. Its existing four facts become five to retain Captured, ICP
fit and Need-to-have. The record contract accepts exactly four or five facts;
other records keep their four. No permissions or quote eligibility are written
or changed. No schema, migration, new route, binding or production write.

Validation covers the real SQLite list/count queries, latest-session and
venture scope, independent and combined filters, pagination, record facts,
the four form methods, missing/unknown values, and the table's column mapping.
