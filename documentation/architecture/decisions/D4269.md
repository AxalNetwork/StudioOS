### 1. Teleprompter (ps5)

Present gains a fourth view beside Presenter, Audience and Rehearse: **Teleprompter** (`PitchPresent.jsx` `Teleprompter`, the pure rules in `teleprompterModel.js`).

- **What it draws:**
  - "Present · Teleprompter", Present's own timer and **End** (back to the studio);
  - **On screen**: the current card, small;
  - the card's per-card budget (D1581), unchanged;
  - **This card**: the card's script (`slide.notes`), one paragraph per line, in large type. A card with no script reads "No notes on this card.";
  - **Next · 8 · Business model**, with the next card's first script line (its title alone when it has none), or "This is the last card."
- **The controls:**
  - **Size A− / A+:** from 20 to 72 px in steps of 4, starting at 36. The buttons stop at each end.
  - **Speed:** Off, Slow, Steady, Fast (0, 14, 26, 42 px a second). The script moves up at that pace and starts again at the top on each card.
  - **Mirror:** flips the words left to right, for a reflecting glass.
- **What is kept:** Size and Speed are the viewer's own, kept in this browser (`localStorage`, key `pitch.teleprompter.v1`). Every read and write is wrapped, so a private window or a blocked store just uses the defaults. Mirror is for this screen only and is not kept.
- **The keys** still move the cards, as in the other views.
- **Opening it:** `?view=teleprompter` opens Present on the teleprompter. P2a's **Open teleprompter** now links there (`teleprompterHref`), where it used to open the presenter view.

### 2. The phone remote (ps5b)

On a phone, `?remote=1` is the phone remote (`PhoneRemote`).

- **What it draws:** "Phone remote", the timer, the card ("7 · Traction") and its script, the next line, and **‹ Back** and **Next card ›**, each a 44 px target. Without `remote=1` a phone is the viewer it was.
- **The steps:** Back and Next card move a whole card, as D1581's remote steps do (`remoteMove`), never one entrance at a time. They stop at the first and the last card.
- **Card numbers** read as the boards print them ("7", not Present's padded "07").

### 3. What this does not do

- **It writes nothing to the Worker.** The only read is the version (`GET /api/decks/:id`), as before.
- **It does not drive the presenting screen.** D1581's pairing routes exist (`/api/decks/:id/remote/pair`, `/remote/join`, `/remote/step`), and Present already obeys their steps. But their pairing screen is still needs-design on #3828, and this issue writes nothing to the Worker. So the phone remote moves its own view.
  - Pairing it with the screen is the next step once #3828's pairing screen is drawn: a code entered on the phone, then each Back and Next card also sent as a `step`.
