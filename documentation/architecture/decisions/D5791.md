## D5791 — Investors play the walkthrough attached to their share link (#5791)

- **Status:** built by S08 on 2026-11-13 (#5791). The follow-up D1590 (#3837) and D4599 part 2 (#4599, PR #5742) left open: "the share viewer playing an attached walkthrough".
- **Number:** D5791, the issue's own number (D4106).

### The routes (`cloudflare-worker/src/routes/deck_share_walkthrough.ts`, self-mounted at `/api/decks`, before `decks`)

- `GET /api/decks/share/:token/walkthrough` returns `{ attached, takes }`. Each take is `{ uid, card, card_uid, take, duration_ms, media_mime, captions }`. Nothing attached is `{ attached: false, takes: [] }`, a 200, so the viewer draws nothing.
- `GET /api/decks/share/:token/walkthrough/:uid/media` streams the take's file. It answers a `Range` request with 206, because Safari plays a video only from a server that does. Every answer is `private, no-store` and `nosniff`.
- Both routes run the same checks, in this order. Each check refuses before the next one reads anything:
  1. **The token:** signed, unexpired and scoped to a deck version. A bad token is 403, an expired one 410 `link_closed`, and a bad scope 400.
  2. **The link:** live, so not withdrawn and not run out, and bound to this browser when forwarding is off (`bindShareViewer`, `shareLinkLive`). Otherwise 403 `forwarding_off` or 410 `link_closed`.
  3. **The reader's consent.** These are the share read's gates, in its order. First, a published link's email gate (`emailGatePassed`, 403 `email_required`). Then the link's NDA, which the signed-in reader must have accepted on this link (`ndaGate`, 403 `nda_required` with the gate's answer).
  4. **The attachment:** a `deck_recording_links` row for exactly this link and this version.
  5. **The switch:** `deck:walkthrough`, read for the walkthrough's maker (below). Off answers 404 `not_switched_on`.
  6. **The takes:** the maker's newest take of each card the link sends. A card hidden from the version, or an appendix card on a link without sources, plays nothing (`shareSlides`). This is the same card rule as the share read, so a take never plays for a card the investor cannot see.
- **The media route** serves a take only when it is one of step 6's takes. Any other uid answers 404 `not_found` without reading R2. That covers an older take, another member's take, a take of another version, and a take on a link it is not attached to.
- **What a take carries.** It carries its card, its number, its length, its type and its captions, and nothing of the member: no caption model, no charge, no size and no date (`sharedWalkthroughOf` in `services/decks/recordings.ts`). Captions that were not recorded are `null`. Each take names its card by uid (`card_uid`), so a link made from a variant places it on the right card. `card` is the Worker's number for the card, hidden cards included, as D1590 counts it.
- **Views.** Neither route uses a view or writes analytics.

### How the switch resolves for an anonymous viewer

An investor reading a link has no member, so they have no flags of their own. The routes read `flagsFor(env, maker)`, where the maker is the `user_id` on the link's `deck_recording_links` row.

- The member routes (`deck_recordings.ts`) read `deck:walkthrough` for the maker. So an attached walkthrough plays only while its maker could still record it.
- HQ's global row is one of the rows the maker's read consults. So switching it off in HQ › Decks stops every share link at once, as #5791 needs ("until it is built, the owner keeps `deck:walkthrough` off").
- A role or user row for the maker also stops their links.
- No existing share read resolved a deck flag before this one, so there was no precedent to follow.

### Rate limit

A new bucket in `middleware/rateLimit.ts`, `deck_share_walkthrough`, matches `GET` on the two paths (`DECK_SHARE_WALKTHROUGH`). It allows 120 a minute per IP and fails open, like the other public reads. A video asks for its file in ranges as it plays and seeks, so the limit sits above the 60 a minute of the other public reads and below the general 200 a minute per IP.

### The contract

`cloudflare-worker/src/contracts/deckShareWalkthrough.schema.json`, with a row in the folder's README.

- It is not a `CONTRACTS` row. `CONTRACTS` lists deck v2 adapter methods swapped to `through(…)`, and `deck_contracts_d1511` test 3 fails a row with no such method.
- The share viewer calls its own client, as its other share reads do (`deckShareRead`, `deckMediaApi.click`, `deckPublishApi.gateEmail`).
- `deck_share_walkthrough_d5791.test.ts` checks the route's reply against the schema, both attached and not attached.

### The viewer

`frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/ShareWalkthrough.jsx` and `shareWalkthroughModel.js` read `deckShareWalkthroughApi` (`frontend/src/lib/api/deckShareWalkthrough.js`). `PitchDeckPrintPage.jsx` draws them in share mode, in each of its three share layouts: the card view, the template view and the plain cards.

- **Watch the walkthrough** is drawn only when the link has a take of a card on the page. A line under it gives the number of cards and the total length.
- **The player** plays each card's take. The label shows the card's number as the page numbers it, and its title. The caption at the playhead sits under the video, or "Captions not recorded for this take." Previous card and Next card move between takes, and a take that ends moves on to the next one.
- **Nothing attached draws nothing.** So does HQ's switch being off, because the investor cannot act on it.
- **A failed read** says "The walkthrough could not be read." with Try again, and never the raw error. A take the browser cannot play says "This card's take could not be played."
- **No design canvas** covers this panel. It follows the share viewer's existing sections (`CardVoices`, `FigureSources`): a mono label, a bordered card and the same buttons, at every width.

### Tests

`cloudflare-worker/test/deck_share_walkthrough_d5791.test.ts` (7 cases, on the migrated schema) covers:
- the attached read, which matches the contract, carries nothing of the member and uses no view;
- hidden and appendix cards;
- nothing attached;
- an NDA link that is signed out or not accepted, then accepted;
- a published link's email gate;
- withdrawn and expired links, with their media;
- another version's link, another member's take, an older take and an unattached link;
- the switch off globally and for the maker alone;
- whole and ranged media, and a 416;
- the bucket's pattern.

`frontend/test/share_walkthrough_d5791.test.mjs` (4 cases) covers:
- the model;
- playing card by card with captions from the share media path;
- nothing attached and the switch off drawing nothing;
- a failed read with Try again and no raw text;
- the page's three placements.

Mutations, each caught by a failing test:
- **Worker:** the sent-card filter dropped; the whole `takeView` sent; the attachment read without its link id; the NDA check removed; the email gate removed; `shareLinkLive` skipped; media served by uid alone; the takes read without `user_id`; the flag check removed; the flag read for no one; `Range` ignored; the bucket's pattern unanchored.
- **Viewer:** `attached` ignored; the takes' order used instead of the link's; every failure treated as off; off treated as unreadable; Watch drawn without a take; a take's end not moving on; the member's media route used; the unreadable branch dropped; a page placement removed.
