- **Builds on:** D1188 (the tool), D1192 (Overview's blocks, CRL1), D5079 (a refusal is not a failed read), D5080 (Needs you says Partial when commitments are unread), D5076 (the zero state's first action is the page's one primary), D5081 part 1 (`owned`, a module's own primary).

### What was wrong

`/raise/capital-relationships` drew the tool's Overview (the round at a glance, Needs you, the pipeline by stage, follow-ups) and then CycleModulePage's generic layer under it: "Capital relationships · at a glance" (the Worker's This venture card and its pipeline counts), the Open the investor pipeline door and an Investors records table, a second set of stage counts. The header's primary action was Raise's global Build your deck. On first use the blocks drew a zero total and an empty stage chart. Two blocks shipped implementation-stage copy: "Against 30 days ago: not recorded. The pipeline’s stage history is not read here yet." and "The next meetings, recent activity, and fit · Not read here yet", although Activity and Match are tabs of their own.

### The decision

1. **The tool owns its page.** CycleRoute passes `owned` on Overview (`capRelOwnsHome`: the module page, no record, no lightbox), so the page draws its header, the tool's tab bar and Overview's blocks, and the module's own parts (receipt matches, From Mercury, From Notion, Evidence), and nothing generic, on a desktop or a phone. The URL is unchanged. The generic card and records table duplicate Overview's stages and Pipeline, so neither is kept.
2. **One pair of reads.** The prospects and commitments reads moved to `capRelHomeRead.js` (`useProspects`, `useCommitments`, `useCapRelHome`), a small file so the tool's step stays lazy (D4574). CycleRoute holds them through `defs/raise.jsx` (HUBS.md: the frozen route gains no import specifier) and hands them to Overview (`home`), so the header's action and the blocks read once. `CapRelPipeline.jsx` re-exports `useProspects`.
3. **The primary action is the module's own** (`homeAction`, RA-14's row: Add investor · Find investors · Follow up). On Overview: follow-ups overdue or due today → **Follow up (n)**, opening Pipeline; no investor yet but money recorded → **Find investors** (Match); otherwise **Add investor**, opening Pipeline's own Add investor (`?open=add`). None while the prospects are unread, failed or refused, for a viewer the module read's capability map does not let work the tool (`capRelStanding(...).works`, D5074), and on first use. On a phone the bar carries no action, so Overview draws it full width. On the tabs, `raise.actions['capital-relationships']` replaces Build your deck with Add investor for a viewer who works the tool; on Pipeline the tab's own Add investor is the action and the header has none.
4. **First use collapses to its set-up actions** (`isFirstUse`, `setupActions`, RA-22 B). The prospects read answered with no investor and the commitments read with no amount (nothing counted, nothing listed, no source unread) is first use: "Start your investor pipeline", one line, and Find investors · Add investor · Import pipeline (the last two open Pipeline's own dialogs, `?open=add|import`). A reader sees the line "No investor is recorded for this venture yet." and no action. A read still loading, failed or refused is never first use; until the commitments read answers, one placeholder is drawn, not the blocks.
5. **No implementation-stage copy.** The "not read here yet" stage-history line and the "next meetings, recent activity, and fit" block are removed; Follow-ups takes the row.

### What the boards draw that no read backs (reconciled against main)

Not built here, and nothing is made up in their place:

- **RA-22 A's tile strip** (Target raise, Committed, Active investors, In diligence, Follow-ups due). Each figure is already on Overview from the reads it has: the round block (counted of target, D1190), the pipeline by stage (Diligence) and Follow-ups. A tile strip beside the round block would draw the same figures twice on one page, the duplication this issue removes. Restyling the blocks into RA-22 A's strip is a follow-up of the Capital relationships redesign, not this part.
- **The round's name in the purpose line** ("for [Seed round]"). The header keeps the tool's line (`LINE`); the commitments read carries `round.name`, but the header is drawn before it answers and an unnamed round would read oddly. Follow-up with the strip.
- **Overview as "Pipeline"** (RA-22 A marks Pipeline as the current tab and draws no Overview tab). The tool's seven tabs (D1188) keep Overview; folding it into Pipeline is RA-12's navigation, part 5.
- **The investor sheet (RA-22 D)**: Overview · Timeline · Materials · Engagement · Notes · Commitment is the investor record's own design, not this page's; D1317's sheet is unchanged.
- **Calls under Capital relationships** (RA-14's rule). Calls is already a tab here; removing the top-level Raise module is RA-12, part 5.
