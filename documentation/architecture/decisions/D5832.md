### 1. What stays private

The pilot's company name, its people, its figures and any invitation stay off
GitHub. On #5824 record only: the provider chosen, the region, the basis
options seen, which reports returned, and pass/fail per check below.

### 2. Comparison

| Question | QuickBooks Online (Intuit) | Xero |
|---|---|---|
| What a connection reaches | One company (`realmId`) per authorization. | One or more organisations (tenants) per authorization; the user picks them at consent. |
| OAuth scope for reports | `com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting`: broad read **and write** of the whole ledger; there is no report-only scope. | Granular, read-only report scopes for apps created on or after 2 March 2026 (`accounting.reports.balancesheet.read`, `accounting.reports.profitandloss.read`; exact strings **to confirm**, one source spells the first with a plural). |
| Reports in D5833's shape | `BalanceSheet` and `ProfitAndLoss`; the response header states the basis and currency; group codes mark bank, income, expenses and net income. | `BalanceSheet` and `ProfitAndLoss`; the response states neither basis nor currency, so both come from the request and the organisation (D5833 records `unknown` when the request does not say). |
| Cash and accrual | `accounting_method=Cash` or `Accrual` per request (parameter **to confirm** in Intuit's reference); the company's default is in its Preferences. | Cash basis through `paymentsOnly=true` on the P&L (**to confirm**). |
| Cost at pilot size | Builder tier is free: 500,000 CorePlus calls a month, and reports are CorePlus calls. Past the allowance Builder returns errors, not charges. Silver and above are paid. | Starter tier is free with 5 connections; Core (50 connections) is AUD 35 a month; Plus and above require app certification (all **to confirm** on Xero's pricing page). |
| Before production | Every app connected to a production company must complete Intuit's app assessment and compliance questionnaire, listed or not. | Within Starter's 5 connections, no certification is reported; certification starts at Plus (**to confirm**). |
| Use terms | Intuit's developer terms apply; nothing found that names founder reporting as restricted (**to confirm** by reading them). | Xero's terms (in force for every developer from 2 March 2026) prohibit using API data to **train** AI or ML models. Whether reading figures into Eadwyn's answers at request time is allowed is read differently by third parties: **the owner must read Xero's §11 and its Financial Services appendix** before Xero data reaches Eadwyn. |
| Rate limits | About 500 requests a minute and 10 concurrent per company (third-party figure). | Daily per-organisation limits that differ by tier (sources disagree). |

### 3. What either pilot must show on the real ledger

1. The founder or finance operator connects, and the connection reaches only
   the chosen entity.
2. A balance sheet at a month end and a P&L for the venture's own fiscal
   period both return, in the entity's currency.
3. Both bases are offered, or the one that is not is recorded.
4. The bank total, income total and net income lines are present and map to
   D5833's kinds; anything the adapter cannot place is recorded as `other`,
   not guessed.
5. A resync with no ledger change is `unchanged`; a correction made in the
   ledger appears as the next revision.
6. Disconnecting stops new snapshots and keeps the pinned ones.

### 4. Recommendation

**A — QuickBooks first, Xero kept as the alternate,** unless the willing pilot
venture keeps its books in Xero, in which case **B**. QuickBooks' terms raise
no open question about Eadwyn reading the figures, its free tier covers a
pilot, and its reports state their own basis and currency. Its cost is a
broad read-write scope that Axal must use read-only, and Intuit's production
assessment before the pilot can connect a real company. Xero's narrower
read-only scopes are better, but the AI terms question has to be settled by
the owner before any Xero figure reaches Eadwyn.

### 4a. Owner decision (2026-11-14)

**A — QuickBooks first; Xero stays the alternate.** The willing pilot
venture's books are in QuickBooks Online, so the "unless Xero" exception in §4
does not apply. The venture is named privately, not here. The Xero terms read
in §5 is no longer on the pilot's path; it stays a step for when a Xero
venture asks to connect.
