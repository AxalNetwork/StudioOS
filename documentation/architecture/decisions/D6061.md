## D6061 — Pitch Studio v2 PS-07: Share is one investor at a time, with what they receive beside it (#6061)

Build 7 of 9 of D4839 (`design/incoming/Pitch Studio v2 Page.dc.html`, boards PS06, PS06G, PS06C; phone PS08g; dark PS09B). No Worker change: `deck_investor_links.ts`, `deck_share_actions.ts` and the one link writer stay as they are.

### What is built

- **Share is the investor-first page.** `ShareDialog.jsx` keeps its three openers (the editor's Share, Decks' Share this version, Share & analytics' New link) and becomes the PS-07 page: 1 · Investor, 2 · What they get, 3 · Access, then the content check and Create link for [name]. Every option the dialog offered is kept: the investor (a prospect or a typed name), version or variant, the six "What is included" switches, the frozen data line and what is never on a link, the NDA, expiry (7, 14, 30 days, Never), Download, Forwarding, the fixed watermark, Time per slide and the view limit. The approval and cards-not-Done confirm (D1570, D1579), the check on the link's own content (D4598) and the link shown once with Publish (D1564) are unchanged.
- **"What [name] will receive" is built from the link's own data.** `shareModel.recipientPreview` takes the form's state (what `shareBody` writes) and the cards `POST /api/decks/:id/preflight/link-cards` answers for the chosen variant and "What is included", the selection `effectiveSlidesFor` serves the link. It draws the viewer's header and frozen note, the NDA step when the NDA is on, the first card (the studio's card renderer, `DeckPreview`) with the watermark the writer will store under it, the card count, Download and the expiry day, and Sees / Can / Cannot. Before the read answers it says "Reading the cards…", and a failed read says the cards could not be read: never 0.
- **Revoke and expiry per investor.** The chosen investor's own links on this version (`linksForInvestor`, by prospect id or by the typed name) show in part 1 as "Live until 2026-11-22", "Withdrawn 2026-11-02", "Expired …" or "Used up", with their views. The links table (`InvestorLinksTable.jsx`) moves behind "Investor links (n)" and gains a Status column (`linkStatusLine`); Withdraw stays on every live row. The table hands its one read to the page (`onLinks`), so the count and part 1 need no second read.
- **The walkthrough reads its switch.** `walkthroughModel.walkthroughOption` reads `GET /api/decks/:id/recordings` as it answers: reading, could not be read (Try again), HQ's `deck:walkthrough` off ("Not switched on yet.", the adapter's words, never "Coming later"), nothing recorded (Record ›), or "Attach your recorded pitch · n slides". Attach is the founder's choice, off by default; once the link exists the page calls `PUT /api/decks/:id/recordings/links/:shareId` and says whether it attached. A failed attach leaves the link standing and says to attach it from Investor links.

### Departures from the canvas

- The page is still the Share lightbox (1180px wide; full screen on a phone), not a tab body under Share & analytics: it is opened from three places, and the tab's own layout (`PitchStudio.jsx`, PS-08's analytics) is outside this issue.
- The investor is a select of prospects plus "or type a name", not a search combobox; "from Capital relationships" is a line under it. The prospect's email is not shown: the form-options read sends name and firm only.
- The NDA row names no NDA template or version ("Standard mutual NDA from Legal · v3"): nothing the page reads records which NDA text a link uses.
- Forwarding's line is what the Worker does (the link opens only in the first browser that opens it), not "only for her email".
- The approval hold (PS06G) appears when Create link is pressed, as D1570's confirm, not as a status line drawn before: the approval is read at that moment so it is never stale. The footer line before it reports the check on the link's own content.
- The preview's browser bar shows the share address without its token: the token exists only once the link is made. The preview draws the first card only.
- "View limit" is kept although the canvas does not draw it, because the dialog offered it.
- PS06C's Withdraw, Expired and Creating failed states are the table's confirm, the Status column and the existing failure line; "Send a new link" on an expired row is not drawn.
- Below 1024px the preview folds under its summary line (PS08g); the form is one column.
