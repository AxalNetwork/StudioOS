### 2. The list and the overview (DDL1, #2385 item 24)

- **`GET /api/dd/cases`** searches, filters, sorts and pages on the server.
  - **Search** (`q`): the subject's name or the case id. `%` and `_` are characters, not wildcards.
  - **Filters:** `subject_type`, `status`, `stage`, `lead`, `template`, `risk_band` (`none` = not scored yet), `decision` (`none` = not recorded), `past_target=1`.
  - **Sort:** `opened` (default), `target` (no date last), `activity`, `flags`, `name`.
  - **Pages:** `page` and `page_size`, at most 200. With none of these it answers as before, the newest 200, and adds `total`.
  - **Each row** carries: its stage, checklist total and worked, open flags with the worst severity, requests waiting, and the lead's name.
- **The stages:**
  - **not started:** `open`;
  - **in review:** `in_review`;
  - **ready for sign-off:** open or in review, with a verdict on every section;
  - **decided:** `completed`;
  - **archived.**
- **One fixed statement.** Every filter is a bound optional value (`? IS NULL OR …`), so the SQL text never changes with the request and nothing is interpolated. The list, its count and the overview read the same predicate (`CASE_FILTER_SQL`), which also carries who may see which case: an Admin sees all; anyone else sees what they opened, lead or review. This replaced the one `sql.unsafe` the list had, so the guard's count of raw-interpolated strings fell from 498 to 497.
- **`GET /api/dd/overview`** covers the same cases. It answers 503 when it cannot read them, never an empty desk. It returns:
  - `stages`, with decided in the last 90 days;
  - `severe_flags`: open High or Critical flags, and across how many open cases;
  - `requests`: waiting on subjects. `past_due` is null, because requests carry no due date until part 4 (#3263);
  - `ages`: open cases by days open, split before target, past target and no target date;
  - `decisions`: the last 12 months by decision, plus archived without one. A month before the first recorded decision says `recorded: false`, never zero.

### 3. My reviews and the searches (DDL2, DDL9, DDL12)

- **`GET /api/dd/my-reviews`** returns the caller's own sections. Each row has:
  - the case and the section;
  - the role, invited and accepted dates, the NDA, and conflicts;
  - the items still pending and the verdict;
  - whether the invitation link has expired.
- **`GET /api/dd/subjects?type=&q=`** searches by name and returns 20.
  - An investor sees only startups they share a deal with: invited, or committed. That is the relationship `POST /cases` already checks (D1149).
  - An advisor is refused: an advisor never opens a case.
- **`GET /api/dd/people?q=&page=`** searches reviewers by name, 20 to a page with a total, among Admins, partners, investors and advisors.
  - An email is returned for an Admin only (#2385 item 17).
  - `GET /experts` now drops the email for anyone but an Admin too.

### 4. The team (DDL9, #2385 item 18)

- **Observer.** Assign takes `role: reviewer | observer`. An observer reads the section and never records its verdict or works its checklist. An observer is not the section's assignee.
- **Expiry.** An invitation link lasts 14 days. Assigning again resends it and starts the 14 days again. An expired link answers 410 `invite_expired`. A reviewer who has already accepted keeps their access.
- **Conflicts.** `POST …/sections/:id/conflicts { declared, note }` comes from the invited person only. A declaration needs words, is encrypted, and tells the lead in the bell. The case read gives the note to the deal team and to the reviewer themself.
- **Remove.** `DELETE …/sections/:id/reviewers/:userId`, by the case lead, the opener or an Admin.
  - The reviewer row goes, so their link and read go with it.
  - A verdict they recorded stays signed in their name (D466).
  - An unfinished section goes back to pending.

### 5. Templates (DDL13, #2385 item 23)

- **A template is one depth at one version.** For every catalog section it holds a weight (above 0, at most 5), whether the section blocks, and the catalog items it seeds. Titles and item words stay the catalog's: a template chooses, it does not rewrite (`checkTemplateBody`).
- **Version 0 is the built-in catalog.**
- **`POST /api/dd/templates`** is `requireSuperAdmin`. It saves the next version, and it applies to new cases only. Each version is its own row and is never overwritten, so the store is its own record (`dd_audit_log` is per case).
- **A case keeps its version** (`template_id`). It opens with that version's weights, blocking and items. A case opened from the catalog is stamped with D1246's blocking list.
- **`POST /cases/:uid/widen { depth }`** goes Lite → Standard → Deep and never narrower. It is the lead's or an Admin's, on an open case. It adds the deeper template's items (`INSERT OR IGNORE`) and is audited.

### 6. Platform checks (DDL8)

The case read gives the deal team `platform_checks`, read only. A section reviewer gets none.

| Check | Read from | For |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | the subject account's `kyc_status` and its review date | every case |
| Business | the account's company `company_kyb_records.status` | partner and investor cases |
| Partner profile | `partner_profiles.admin_status`, with its reference | partner cases |

- **Links** go to `/admin/kyc` and `/admin/partner-profiles/:ref`, for an Admin only. The canvas's per-person KYC addresses do not exist yet.
- **Failures** are per check: an unreadable check says so, and a missing record says so.
