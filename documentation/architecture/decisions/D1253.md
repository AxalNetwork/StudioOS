### 3. What is drawn today

- **The panel** draws the eleven scopes in order, each "Not read here yet", with How scoring works.
  - An investor also sees that the weighting waits.
  - A founder sees that their view, sharing and trajectory wait. The sharing line says that until sharing is stored, no investor sees a score of theirs.
- **The strip** is drawn unread: an outlined strip whose text is "Scorecard: Not read here yet", never eleven zero bars.
  - Its rule is in the model:
    - height is the value, 20px at 100;
    - violet opacity is the confidence (High 100%, Medium 60%, Low 30%);
    - "Not enough recorded" is a dashed hairline;
    - "Not expected yet" is a muted dot;
    - "Locked" outlines the whole strip.
  - Part 4 feeds it real scopes.
- **How scoring works** draws model v1 from the model: scopes, grades, confidence and bands, and what is never an input. It says model v1 is not published, so no venture carries a score from it today.
- **HQ › Scoring** shows three waits: model versions, country indices and the fairness check.

### 5. Owner decisions

#2428's eleven questions take their recommended answers until the owner says otherwise:
1. An investor sees bands and confidence once on the deal (invited or committed), and input values after a pairwise NDA.
2. A per-venture consent switch, on when the venture is listed, with a preview.
3. There is no platform total; each investor sorts by their own weighting.
4. The country indices are World Bank governance indicators plus the FATF and EU lists, after a licence check. Until the import (part 6), both scopes are facts only.
5. Values count only as the co-founders' alignment band, with every founder's consent.
6. Experience counts only as corroboration of a skill.
7. The DD band counts only for a closed case with real findings.
8. The Lab scorer stays for practice. Screening and venture risk read the scorecard. No score sets a status.
9. Reviews may adjust a scope by up to 15 points, with a reason. The founder is told.
10. Advisors read only, when the founder shares.
11. Traction and Financial say "Not expected yet" before Build.
