## D4817 part 3 — PF-03 Performance: NAV at each quarter-end as one series, chart and table, with its methodology (#4817)

Part of #5311 (PF-03): the third board of the Portfolio v2 canvas (`design/incoming/Portfolio v2 Page.dc.html`, boards PF-03 "A · Chart" and PF-03b "B · Table view", with addendum PFA07), drawn in the frame PF-01 built (D4817 part 1). Frontend only, against today's Worker reads. No route, Worker or schema change.

**What is built.** `frontend/src/pages/investor/InvestorPortfolioPerformance.jsx` is the investor's Performance. `PortfolioWorkspace` returns it for an investor at `/portfolio/performance`, as it does the other investor boards, so the address and every link to it still resolve. It draws in `PortfolioFrame` with `tab="performance"`: the document title is `Performance · Portfolio · Axal VC` and the board carries its own `WorkerRail`. An admin's Performance tab is unchanged (`PortfolioPerformancePage`). The page holds, in the canvas's order:
- **The performance summary**: NAV with mark coverage, TVPI, DPI, RVPI, IRR, invested, unrealised and realised, each with its definition, and the basis strip. It is the Overview's own part, moved to `PortfolioParts` as `PerformanceSummary`. Here, its Methodology link goes to the panel on the same page.
- **NAV at each quarter-end**, as a Chart or a Table of one series, switched by a two-button group (`aria-pressed`).
  - **One read per quarter-end:** `GET /api/positions/analytics?as_of=<quarter-end>`. D4644 cuts the flows and the marks at that date and echoes it as `as_of`.
  - **Where the series runs:** from the first quarter of the earliest cheque's year (`vintage` on `/api/positions`) to the last completed quarter that `kpi-compliance` names (`period_until`, with `period_complete`). That is the Worker's date, never the browser clock. It keeps at most the eight newest quarter-ends and says how many earlier ones are not shown.
  - **Each quarter-end is read, committed and retried on its own** (`usePortfolioReads`, keyed by date). A failed one leaves the others standing.
- **The methodology panel**: TVPI, DPI, RVPI, IRR, NAV, paid-in, distributed, mark coverage, quarter-ends and basis. Each says what `portfolioMetrics.ts` and the analytics route compute today.
- **Vintage cohorts** (addendum PFA07: "Vintage cohorts remain here"): companies, invested, carrying value with the at-cost count, and carrying ÷ invested, grouped by the first cheque's year.
- **Company history and other tools**: doors to Positions (each company's mark history, PF-02b) and to Exit Waterfall (`/portfolio/waterfall`, addendum PFA07's secondary utility).

**One series, two views.** `quarterSeriesOf` builds the list of points once, and the chart and the table both draw that list, so they cannot show different points.
- The chart is one image (`role="img"`). Its text alternative names every quarter-end in order with its value or its gap.
- Under the plot, each quarter-end has a column with its value (`$11.1m`) or the word for its gap.
- The bars are drawn with recharts, the library the old Performance page already used. `QuarterEndBars` is loaded lazily, once the plot has a measured width.
- `chartRowsOf` gives a gap the value null, so recharts draws no bar for it: never a 0, and never a line drawn across it.
- The table is the accessible equivalent: one row per quarter-end with NAV, paid-in, distributed, TVPI and mark coverage. A gap row says why.

**How each point reads.**
- **Drawn**: the read answered for that date with a NAV. Its paid-in, distributed (a measured $0 stays $0), TVPI and mark coverage come from the same read.
- **Not recorded**, with its reason. The read answered and holds no position on or before that date (`position_count: 0`), or it returned no NAV.
- **Unreadable**: the read failed, or it answered without echoing the date asked (`as_of` is not that quarter-end). A read that does not speak for the date is not drawn as that quarter. The row offers a Retry for that quarter-end alone, and a line under the chart offers Retry for every failed quarter-end.
- **The change line** ("NAV up $1,450,000 from 30 Jun 2026 to 30 Sep 2026") compares the two newest quarter-ends only when both were read. It is never measured across a gap.
- **When there is no series:**
  - An empty book says so and reads no quarter-end.
  - A reporting read that names no completed quarter reads Not recorded.
  - A failed positions or reporting read makes the series Unreadable, says which source it needs, and offers a Retry for that source. The summary beside it stands.

**Departures from the canvas, and why.**
- **The chart's bars are drawn by recharts**, not the canvas's CSS bars, because the page already used it.
  - Gaps are drawn as dashed, hatched empty slots, labelled Not recorded or Unreadable.
  - The canvas has no gap, since its sample reads every quarter.
- **The column labels are `Q3 2026`**, where the canvas has dates. The full date is in the table and the text alternative.
- **The view switch is two buttons with `aria-pressed`**, where the canvas has a radiogroup. Two buttons need no arrow-key handling to be correct.
- **The methodology sits beside the series only at 1500px and wider.** The canvas sets them 7:5 side by side, but on a 1440px screen the app's sidebar and the rail leave the series card about 470px wide. At that width the six-column table scrolled inside its card. Below 1500px, the methodology is drawn under the series at full width.
- **The canvas's sample shows four quarter-ends.** The build reads up to the eight newest completed ones from the first cheque's year, one read each.
- **The methodology panel's footnote** in the canvas says "Definitions follow #4644 once it lands". D4644 has landed and no issue number goes in member copy (#5365 F-09), so the build says the definitions describe what the platform computes today.
- **Vintage cohorts show carrying value ÷ invested, not TVPI.**
  - Addendum PFA07's cohort table has Distributed and TVPI.
  - The positions read carries no distributions per company, and reading each company would be one read per company.
  - So the build labels the multiple for what it is and says why. It shows no Distributed column rather than an invented one.
- **The old page's company table and mark-history slide-over are not on PF-03.** Positions (PF-02) holds the register and each company's mark history, and Performance links there. PFA07 moves company history to Financials (PF-06), which is not built yet.
- **No "Up $X since 30 Jun" under the hero NAV.** The hero is the latest book, not a quarter-end, so the change is stated on the series card, between two quarter-end reads.
- **The local navigation carries no counts**, as in PF-01.

**Not drawn, because no read supplies it.** D4817's "Backend required" items stay with #4642, #4644, #4645 and #4647. This board also needs none of the following, and it invents none of them:
- a per-company distributions read, for cohort TVPI;
- a read time on any of these reads;
- net-of-fees returns.

Each reads as absent here; none is estimated.
