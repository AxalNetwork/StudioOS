### Deadline disposition

The existing hosted job ceiling is 45 minutes. A newly issued hosted receipt
now ends at the earlier of its original start-plus-work envelope and 45 minutes
from generation creation. This safely narrows the previous 50-minute envelope
without extending native timeouts. With the current ten-minute pickup allowance,
a last-minute pickup has at most 35 minutes left in that receipt. Dispatch,
startup and repeated observations never extend it. This is a maximum lease,
not a promise that setup plus 40 minutes of tests fits. S09 still owns deriving
actual work/cleanup allowances from the remaining absolute deadline.

For observation delay, the coordinator accepts a successful current, unfenced
attempt only with coherent whole-job completion evidence within the original
lease. The independent GitHub reader requires both exact Run-step and exact-job
completion timestamps, with step completion no later than job completion; the
job timestamp includes the later cleanup steps. The policy requires completion
at or after acceptance and start, strictly before receipt expiry, and no later
than the observation clock. Missing, malformed, future or expired completion is
not success. A later poll can report an earlier completed job, but no success can
revive a fenced or old generation, nor extend the overall request deadline.

This chooses verified completion-time treatment for observation delay. It does
not invent a measured finish reserve. Candidate work ending exactly at expiry,
cleanup ending outside the lease, a slow synchronous staging operation and an
unconfirmed native shutdown remain refusal/unknown conditions. Historical hosted
queue observations are context, not a pickup SLA.

### Result and cleanup disposition

A failed `Run admitted Worker shard` wrapper no longer proves failed tests: the
wrapper also covers preparation, watchdog and cleanup. Without independent
precision it stays unknown. The new optional `CapacityExecutionResultReader`
is a trusted-code port, with no positive production implementation or request,
environment, candidate-artifact or deployment injection. `CapacityControl`
exposes only a protected composition seam for the eventual reviewed supervisor.
The reader must bound its own operations and independently verify the result.

Its output binds every receipt field and the exact independently observed job,
a nonfuture observation at most 30 seconds old, explicit test truth, typed
execution outcome and cleanup confirmation. Mismatched/malformed evidence
refuses. Valid timeout or cleanup-failure evidence does not manufacture a test
failure. A proved test failure immediately fails the aggregate and denies all
sibling receipts. Its durable negative flag survives restart and later success
reports. If cleanup is unconfirmed, the attempt remains nonterminal and keeps
its included-compute reservation. Only independently confirmed completed cleanup
can settle that failed attempt and release the slot. The internal flag is not
part of the executor receipt contract.

Normal exact successful run/job/Run-step observations can establish success
with coherent completion timestamps. Failed or cancelled wrappers require
independent cleanup and test truth before an infrastructure fallback. Unknown
termination/test truth keeps the execution fenced, never replaced or green.
The transport fixtures explicitly inject simulated supervisor facts; no fixture
callback is a live reader or native proof.

### Cancellation and fallback disposition

The existing complete-fallback guard is retained: cancellation must be observed
and cleanup confirmed, then the original hosted pickup + work + control-margin
reservation must still fit the request deadline. With the current constants,
confirmation at request minute 35 permits one hosted generation; 1 ms later
refuses it. The new 45-minute hosted cap does not relax this 55-minute reservation.
A cancellation request/acknowledgement, missing observation or expiry of the
two-minute cancellation-state timer is not termination. There is no second
hosted fallback. Source, plan and parent ownership stay bound across generations.

The two-minute timer is a reconciliation state budget, not a guarantee that
native teardown finishes in two minutes. S09 retains watchdog placement,
synchronous stage cancellation, container cleanup and Run-step/job composition.
The authority-side typed reader and any no-overage eligibility projection still
need reviewed integration with S09. No new provider/billing proof is supplied.
The included cap and standard-hosted floor stay enforced; unknown cleanup cannot
reopen an included slot even when negative test truth is already known.
