## D8259 — Company creation offers the filings its jurisdiction's pack suggests, with Add to my filings and Not now (#8259)

- **Status:** built for #8259 (slot S17), 2026-11-22. Builds board BD28 A and B of the Company Creation addendum (D6035), with the brief's Q3 at option A: Axal's packs are offered to founders. BD28 C, the reminder control, stays placement only (D6038 §3).
- **Builds on:** D6038 (Company creation's own page and its per-block reads, #8174, stacked here until it lands), D417 (the US packs, `entityObligations.ts` `US_PACKS` and `packObligations`), the founder calendar's ruleset (`compliance.ts` `STANDARD_EVENTS`), D690 (the filing write, `POST /api/cycles/build/formation-steps`), D4600 (a filing whose proof upload failed is kept), D695 (an Add opens from its `/new` address).
- **Migration:** `202611222115_8259_suggested_filing_dismissals.sql`, one new table. **New route:** `POST /api/cycles/build/company-creation/suggested-filings/:key/dismiss`. **New client method:** `cyclesWrite.build.dismissSuggestedFiling`.

### 1. The read (Worker)

`routes/cycle_build.ts` `company()` gains one more page fact, `suggestions`, from `services/suggestedFilings.ts`, on the module page only (`opts.pageFacts`), beside `entity`, `elections` and `papers`. The overview reads none of it. It fails on its own: a store that cannot be read says `unreadable`, never "no suggestions", and the filings and other blocks still read (BD29).

**Which pack.** The incorporation order's `jurisdiction_id` when it is `us_de_ccorp` or `us_de_llc`; else the company record's `jurisdiction` and `legal_form` (`US-DE` with `c_corp` or `llc`, as `incorporationCompany.ts` writes them). An entity record alone does not say C-Corp or LLC, so it names no pack. Every other jurisdiction has no pack: the read is `no_pack`, with the jurisdiction it saw and the sentence BD28 B draws, and offers nothing.

**Each suggestion.** One per rule of the pack, keyed `<pack>:<event_type>` (the key `entity_obligations.pack_key` gives a seeded obligation): the rule's title, Build's filing kind (Franchise tax, Annual report, else Other), Federal for the rule filed with the IRS (Form 1120; every other rule's jurisdiction is chosen in the form), how often it recurs, and its due date from `packObligations`. A fixed date takes its next occurrence. A rule counted from formation uses the order's `filed_on` once filed, else the company's, else the entity's `incorporation_date`. With none of them recorded it is offered with no date and says why; no date is invented.

**What is left out.** A suggestion the venture already has: a Build filing or an entity obligation with the same title (trimmed, any case; a filing added from a suggestion keeps the suggestion's title), or an obligation Axal seeded with the same pack key. And a suggestion the venture hid with Not now. The read counts both (`already_added`, `dismissed`) and lists neither.

### 2. The writes

**Add to my filings creates nothing on its own.** It opens the existing Add filing form at its own address, `/build/company-creation/new?form=suggested`, with the suggestion's name, kind, jurisdiction and due date filled in (`forms.js` `suggestedFilingValues` reads only those, and only in their own shapes). The founder checks the values and saves the filing through the same write, the same idempotency key and the same proof upload and retry as Add filing (`buildFiling.submit`, D4600). The only difference is a Filing name field, sent as the filing's `title`, which the route already accepted. Nothing is filed with any authority.

That form is never a button. It sits in `forms.js` `ADDRESS_FORMS`, not `FORMS`, so `formsFor` stays the module's Add buttons and its one Add, Add filing, is unchanged. `CycleRoute` adds it to the open forms only while `?form=` names it (`addressFormFor`), and the Add buttons skip any `addressOnly` form.

**Not now** is `POST …/suggested-filings/:key/dismiss?project=<id>` (`routes/build_suggested_filings.ts`, mounted ahead of Build's own router). The gate is Build's write gate (`canWrite`: the venture's founders, an admin). The key must name a rule one of the packs holds, else 404 `suggestion_not_found`. The row goes into `company_filing_suggestion_dismissals` (UNIQUE `project_id`, `suggestion_key`) with one `activity_logs` row, `build_suggested_filing_dismissed`, in the same batch. A second Not now writes nothing and answers `already_dismissed: true`. A dismissal stays: no read or later filing brings it back. There is no undo route; see §5.

Refusals: 400 `project_required`, 404 `project_not_found`, 403 `forbidden`, 404 `suggestion_not_found`, each with its sentence. All SQL is bound.

### 3. The page (frontend)

`CompanyCreationHome.jsx` draws **Suggested for your US Delaware C-Corp** (or LLC) between the 83(b) tracker and the filings table, as BD28 A places it. The line reads "N suggestions · from Axal's … pack". Each row shows its name, its due date with days left (or why there is none) and how often it recurs, then Add to my filings and Not now. The block closes with "Adding one creates a Build filing you own, with its due date. Nothing is filed. 'Not now' hides the suggestion for this venture."

- **BD28 B:** with no pack, "Suggested for <jurisdiction>", "No suggested filings for <jurisdiction> yet" and the reason.
- **Nothing left** (all added or hidden): no block.
- **Could not be read:** "Suggested filings could not be read" with Try again, on this block only.
- **A viewer with no Add** (read only, founder preview without write): the list, without its buttons.
- **No company yet:** Incorporate first stays the one state (D6038, BD26), so no suggestions are drawn.

The words come from `companyCreationModel.js` `suggestionsView` and `suggestionHref`, so desktop and phone say the same; every colour has its `dark:` pair; its buttons are 44px targets on a phone and 36px from 640px up (BD30).
