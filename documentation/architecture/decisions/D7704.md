### Why

The sizing page did the arithmetic in the browser (`SpinoutLabMarketPage.jsx`, `recalcMarket`). A blank or zero serviceable share became 14%, a blank win rate 8%, SOM was multiplied by 2.2, a missing TAM fell back to the venture's earlier TAM, and runway and sales capacity were never read. The figures and the assumptions were saved in two requests, so one could land without the other.

### What changed

**The formula (version 2, `services/marketSizing.ts`).** Pure functions, no store.

- TAM is the override when one is entered (zero included). An override needs a reason. Otherwise TAM is eligible accounts × annual revenue per account. Both values are kept.
- SAM is TAM × serviceable share.
- SOM is the lower of SAM × win rate and deals per month × selling months × annual revenue per account × retention share. Selling months are the horizon months minus one sales cycle, never below 0.
- There is no default, no multiplier and no fall-through to the earlier TAM.
- Required: serviceable share, win rate, deals per month, horizon months, sales cycle months, retention share, annual revenue per account, and eligible accounts unless TAM is overridden. A missing input is refused with `missing_<field>` (`missing_sam_pct`), naming the field in `field`. A malformed, negative or out-of-range input is refused with `invalid_<field>`.
- A sales cycle as long as the horizon gives SOM 0, and `notes` says so. SOM never exceeds SAM.
- The result gives the formula in words with this version's numbers, both SOM limits and which limit set SOM (`som_limit`: `win_rate`, `capacity` or `both`).
- Units: the TAM override is in millions of USD, as the drawer labels it. Every stored figure is whole USD, rounded once from unrounded arithmetic. Currency is USD only.
- Number grammar: blank is not entered, and `0` is zero. A value is an optional `$`, digits with optional comma thousands groups, an optional decimal part, then optionally `%` (percentages only) or a unit in words after a space or a slash. So `41,200 firms` is 41200 and `$18,000 / year` is 18000. A scale word (`k`, `million`, `bn`), a second number or anything else is refused by field name and never cut short. A percentage is 0 to 100.

**The store (migration 202611191932).**

- `market_sizing_versions` holds one row per save: `uid`, `project_id` (`ON DELETE CASCADE`), `version_number` (unique per project), `formula_version`, `scenario` (`base`), `computed_tam`, `tam_override` with `tam_override_reason` (both or neither), `tam`, `sam`, `som` (checked `som ≤ sam ≤ tam`), `som_limit`, `currency` (`USD`), `reference_year`, `horizon_months`, `author_id` (`ON DELETE SET NULL`) and `created_at`.
- `market_sizing_version_inputs` holds one row per input of a version: `input_key`, `value` (the number the formula used), `typed_text` (as typed), `unit`, `label` (`entered` or `assumption`) and `note`. Its source columns stay empty until #7725.
- Both tables are append-only, as `market_figures` is. An UPDATE trigger refuses any rewrite, and only a reference may go NULL: a version's author, or an input's source. There is no delete trigger, because `services/projectTrash.ts` hard-deletes a project and relies on the cascade.
- `market_figures.sizing_version_id` (nullable) names the version that saved a row. Its own trigger lets it only go NULL, because D6011's trigger names its columns and does not cover it.
- `project_market_assumptions` gains `horizon_months`, `sales_cycle_months`, `retention_pct` and `tam_override_reason`, as typed text like the rest of the row. They are mirrored in `ASSUMPTION_COLUMNS`, `loadAssumptions` and the lazy table definition, with `runSchemaBootstrap` adding them to an older dev table. None of them matches a never-fill rule (`services/fills/neverFill.ts`).

**The routes (`/api/market-sizing`, gate `canEditProjectData`).** Every route checks the gate before it reads or writes anything about the venture.

- `POST /projects/:projectId/preview` computes and stores nothing.
- `POST /projects/:projectId/versions` computes, then writes one `DB.batch`: the version, its inputs, `projects.tam/.sam/.som`, a `market_figures` row for each of the three figures carrying the version id, and the assumptions row. The figure rows are written on every save, so all three current rows name one version. A failure of any statement leaves every table as it was (`500 sizing_not_saved`). Two saves in the same moment collide on the version number (`409 sizing_version_conflict`).
  - `market_figure_source` is read as `PUT /api/projects/:id` reads it and resolved by `resolveMarketProvenance` before anything is written. A save that names no method records `bottom_up`, or `typed` when TAM was overridden.
  - An admin's save on a venture the admin neither owns nor belongs to is logged with `logAdminAction` (`market_figures_set`, with the version uid).
- `GET /projects/:projectId/versions` lists the versions newest first. `GET /projects/:projectId/versions/:uid` returns one version with its inputs and `recomputes`: whether the version's stored figures follow from its own input rows under formula version 2. Both say `current`: whether the version's three figures still equal the project's. A version uid of another project reads as `404 sizing_version_not_found`.
- `PUT /api/projects/:id` still accepts typed figures, and its rows carry no version. `marketFigureHistory` gives each row's `sizing_version` (the version number, or null).

### What this reverses

- Migration 247's "one row per project, no versioned table". The assumptions row stays the drawer's current state; the history is the versions.
- The page's deliberate two-request save (figures, then assumptions). A versioned save is one batch.
- D6011's "the figures are saved even when their rows are not", for the sizing save. `PUT /api/projects/:id` keeps that behaviour for typed figures.
- The page's ×2.2 on SOM, and its 14% and 8% defaults. The page itself moves to these routes in #7706.

### Open questions for the owner (built as A)

1. **SOM.** **A (built):** the lower of SAM × win rate and deals per month × selling months left in the horizon after one sales cycle × annual revenue per account × retention share. B: SAM × win rate only, with capacity shown as a check. C: a formula the owner names.
2. **Figures saved under the old arithmetic.** **A (built):** never rewritten. Their history rows carry no version, so a page can say they were saved before formula version 2 (they may include ×2.2). B: recalculated by a migration.
3. **Inputs stored with a unit** ('41,200 firms', '$18,000 / year'). **A (built):** read a well-formed figure, keep the text, and refuse anything else by name. B: refuse them all, and the founder retypes the number.
4. **Runway (months).** **A (built):** not an input. It stays on the assumptions row as context. B: the horizon may not exceed it.
