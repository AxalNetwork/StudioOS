## D4483 — Campaign details, monthly spend sources and creative text (#4483)

Extends D4447 (#4447, PR #4454) inside Grow › Distribution. Campaigns stay
Draft until an explicit PATCH activates them. No timer or start date activates
a plan. Paused may resume or end; Ended is final.

The detail read adds the creator's recorded name (never email), creation time
and same-venture linked content/drafts. Drafts have no title column, so their
recorded body title/text or linked calendar title supplies the label; absence
stays null. Lists accept literal, case-insensitive name search and status;
status counts cover all unarchived plans before either filter.

Audience kind is optional for existing plans. ICP and segment require a
same-venture segment; none clears it. Channels carry Daily/Lifetime, nullable
whole-cent amount and validated start/end days. These are plans, not ad jobs.

Spend uses the campaign's inclusive start/end months. For each channel/month,
a measured Mercury channel-spend fact wins over manual channel spend. Facts
are filtered to the venture, distribution module, channel-spend kind and channel
uid. A Mercury null is not measured spend and cannot erase manual evidence.
No recorded months means null with a reason, never zero. A failed source read
makes spend Unreadable. Per-month source labels preserve Mercury/manual
provenance; a channel with both is mixed. Budget-used percent is null with
missing spend/budget or a zero denominator. Campaign totals require measured
spend for every linked channel; channel dates do not silently change the
campaign-wide attribution window.

One venture-scoped contacts scan supplies leads, demos (qualified or active)
and customers (active) by normalized UTM campaign. Malformed JSON is ignored;
a missing tag or failed scan gives null with a reason. These read-only figures
never write revenue, MRR, traction or Raise stores.

Creatives are plain founder-entered or accepted text in a separate
`grow_campaign_creatives` table, one placement/field per campaign. They never
run, send, publish or spend. Existing venture reader/writer gates apply.
Ended/archived plans refuse creative writes, including concurrent finalization;
upsert and actor audit share one atomic batch. The UTC-stamped migration's
ALTERs apply once through the ledger.

Verification: mounted Grow routes over baseline plus all migrations, the D4447
regressions, source-precedence/isolation/null/state/funnel mutation checks,
and repository build/drift/schema/documentation checks. See the PR for results.
