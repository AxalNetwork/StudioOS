## D6195 — An investor's route preference, stored on their commitment and shown on Terms, Commitment and Choose the route (#6195)

**Status:** built (2026-11-16) by S14. The owner answered the open question on #6195 the same day: **a preference only.** An investor may record a route preference when committing and change it while the commitment is Pending. The closing line's real route is the one the studio sets, and nothing here changes it. No preference reads "No preference recorded", never Direct.

### What changed

- **One column.** Migration `202611161505_6195_commitment_route_preference.sql` adds `commitments.route_preference`: `direct`, `syndicate`, `fund` or NULL, enforced by a CHECK.
  - It sits on the commitment row, which is held per firm (D4633), so each firm's commitment has its own preference.
  - Additive, with no backfill: every existing commitment reads as none recorded.
- **The Worker** (`routes/deals.ts`).
  - `POST /:id/commitments` accepts `route_preference`. Sent as one of the three keys, it is stored. Sent as null or `''`, it is cleared. Left out of the body, it is kept, so a change of amount alone never wipes it.
  - Any other value is refused with 400 `invalid_route_preference` and the sentence "A route preference is direct, syndicate, fund or none. Nothing was changed."
  - A Confirmed commitment still changes only by withdrawing it (409, unchanged), so its preference stands.
  - The answer returns the stored `route_preference`.
  - `GET /:id/commitments` returns it on the investor's own rows and on the staff read (Admin, founder, Operating Partner), beside the amount those readers already see.
- **The closing desk** (`services/closings.ts`). Each closing line carries its commitment's `route_preference`, through a LEFT JOIN on `commitment_id`. It is null for a prospect line. `route` is untouched: it starts null and is set only by `PUT /:uid/lines/:lineUid/route`.
- **The frontend.**
  - `CommitModal` (`DealRoomPage.jsx`) asks "How you would like to invest (optional)", with Direct, Through a syndicate, Through a fund and No preference recorded. A new commitment starts on No preference recorded; a change starts from the stored one.
  - Terms (`DealTerms`) marks the investor's card "✓ Your preference", or says "No preference recorded. You can record one when you commit." A failed read is said as unreadable, never as none.
  - Commitment (`DealCommitment`) shows "How you would like to invest" with the preference or "No preference recorded".
  - Terms, Commitment and the dialog each carry the canvas sentence "This is your preference. The studio sets the route in the closing room." (`ROUTE_NOTE`, which replaces "No preference is recorded here yet.").
  - Choose the route (`ClosingDialogs.jsx`) shows the Admin "The investor's preference: Through a fund. A preference only; the route is the one you set.", or "The investor recorded no route preference." The radio group still starts from the line's own route.

### Why the preference does not map to a route

The closing desk has four routes (Direct, Syndicate (SPV), Axal VC fund, Third-party fund). The investor's three choices do not map one to one: "Through a fund" could be either fund route. Preselecting from the preference would make it more than a preference, so the dialog only says it.
