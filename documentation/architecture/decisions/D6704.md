## D6704 — Recorded formation comparisons and choices (#6704)

Uses owner decisions 3A/10A/4A (#6681) and keeps decision 15 (#6682) as a
separate production launch gate. No provider, model, production-data or flag
activation action is part of this change. #8232 currently prevents publication.

`formationRules` is pure: answers plus a published version's ordered rule data
and forms produce the same bytes. A claimed written US investor requirement
also needs its recorded document reference. Boolean answers must be booleans. It requires recorded founder residence,
operating countries and management country; it does not infer management from
an address. The payload of a `formation_rules` recommend row is
`{scenario, country, form_key, when, reasons, what_would_change_it}`. Scenario
is 1–13; country is the management country; `when` is an exact-match object
of the documented boolean intake flags or founder_state. Unknown conditions
cannot match. Catalogue editors own publication and factual content. This
change seeds no comparison rules, country facts, scenarios or counsel text.
Missing comparison coverage refuses with `comparison_not_recorded`.

The procedural safeguards constrain published data: home jurisdiction first,
Estonia and the two exceptional US forms only by explicit opening (Delaware
LLC also for Delaware residents), written US investor requirements add the
Delaware corporation, no cost input influences selection. Complex circumstances
require a specialist route; otherwise a foreign founder's Delaware corporation
remains simple under 10A. Scenario 1 cannot map a non-Delaware resident to a
Delaware LLC. US home-state LLC remains an information item. Opening an
available form reserves one of at most three slots. Orderable is catalogue
information, not permission to pay: existing order gates remain separate.

Only formation requirements are projected from facts, with their status,
source and check date. Fees, tax data and index rankings are never projected.
Unsafe comparison text refuses. The only notice is the existing D4421 constant;
its key/version/hash and the full output are retained with the answers UID and
catalogue version. No proposed legal text is authored here.

Three owner-scoped endpoints compare and record choices; memo authoring is
Super Admin with fresh TOTP/step-up, recording for the accepting partner under
4A. All endpoints remain HQ-only. The global formation_options flag is explicitly
off by migration; missing means off, unlike generic default-on flags. Failed
reads stay failures. No role/user override or cache can activate this launch gate.

Suggestions, choices and memos cannot be updated or deleted. New choices append.
Stale answer sets and revision conflicts refuse. First entry to chosen uses the
existing transition service with choice statements in its transaction. Re-choice
already at chosen changes only revision and records a new choice/event atomically.
Every extra write requires this request's event token: a competing request's next
revision cannot authorize an append. D1 batches abort or roll back as documented
in https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/worker-api/d1-database/#batch .
A memo names its author, firm, licensing body, country, date, document, answers
set and the assigned partner's acknowledgement; only HQ may attest it. A specialist
choice must name that case's memo and current partner. The gate refuses self
confirmation and forbids waiver for 6/7/8/9/12 and payments/health/fund/flip/spinout.
For 4/5/10/11/13 a recorded counsel-sourced structure_waiver_template fact and
current approved template-body review are both required. No approval is seeded.

No new UI or frontend recommendation change (#6708), people workflow (#6705),
payment or partner-account flow is implemented. Help impact: no member-facing
workflow is switched on; future founder UI and launch documentation remain with
their named tasks. Local fixtures are synthetic and establish no real-world
country, professional, counsel or partner approval.
