## D701 — On a phone, Studio is Eadwyn

Issue #1663, part of D689 (the owner's request of 2026-10-14 for mobile versions of every screen). The boards are S1m–S5m of `design/incoming/Studio · Mobile.dc.html` (SM) and `design/incoming/Studio Mobile Page.dc.html` (SMP). It shipped as two PRs. The first (#1671) added the Worker read, one breakpoint, the tab in the URL and the ticket route, at every width. The second draws the phone page: the bar, the drawer, the Conversations list, Tasks, My tickets and the ticket card.

### What was decided

1. **Studio on a phone is the Studio Mobile Page canvas** (epic D-i), not Cycle Overview's One Next Thing `/studio`, which no board uses (D-k). At 1024px and up `/studio` stays D564's page.
2. **One breakpoint, 1024px** (D-l). Eadwyn's chat switched at 768px (`innerWidth >= 768`) while the shell switches at 1024px (`PHONE_QUERY`), so from 768px to 1023px a phone shell held the desktop chat. `useIsDesktop` now lives in `components/advisor/eadwynPhone.jsx` and returns `!usePhoneWidth()` (D699), so one hook follows the query. A server render is desktop. It applies wherever `PersonalAdvisor` mounts: the five Studio homes, `/exploring` and `/advisory`. Those pages and the other roles keep today's phone layout (the panel tabs above the card), now below 1024px.
3. **The tab lives in the URL, at every width.** `?tab=tasks` opens Tasks and `?tab=tickets` My tickets; no `tab`, `?tab=conversations` or any other value opens Conversation. While the founder's home passes `tab`, every tab change goes through `onTab`: the card's tabs, the fullscreen view's tabs and Start on a task, which returns to Conversation. `onTab` replaces the URL on the path it is on (so an open ticket form stays open), removes `tab` for Conversation and keeps every other parameter. `project_id` follows every Studio link this task writes, because it picks the venture the page reads: without it a founder with several ventures would fall back to their first one.
4. **Open a ticket is a route, `/studio/tickets/new`**, guarded `['founder']` beside `/studio`. `labRoles` would admit an admin in any view mode and a Lab member of any role; with `['founder']` anyone else is sent to their default page, which is `/studio` for every Studio role, and an admin viewing as a founder passes. Only the founder's Studio links to it: Suggest a task (`?type=task`), the collapsed interview row's Open a ticket and the drawer. The form writes through `POST /api/tickets` as today. Leaving it (×, Cancel, or a filed ticket after its "has been filed" message) replaces the URL: `/studio?tab=tasks` when it was opened on Task, `/studio` otherwise, each keeping `project_id`. The chat header's own Open a ticket keeps its local toggle; with the route open it closes the route.
5. **No default ticket type.** A `?type=` other than `task`, `bug` or `feature` opens the form with no type, as D544 and D606 require. The canvas's Bug-on chip is a sample state and is not copied.
6. **One new Worker read, `GET /api/eadwyn/conversations/mine`** (spec below).
7. **The collapsed interview row stands for the conversation.** `StudioInterview` draws it only while `tab` is Conversation and no ticket form is open; `const collapsed = …` is unchanged. On the founder's Studio the row's Open a ticket opens the route and reopens the chat under it, so the "has been filed" message stays in view once the form closes. The other four homes pass none of the new props and keep `setReopened('ticket')`.
8. **With no props the chat keeps its own state**, as on the other four homes, `/exploring` and `/advisory`.
9. **The phone page** (`pages/founder/StudioPhone.jsx`). Below 1024px `FounderStudioHome` renders `StudioPhone` in place of `FounderStudioBody` (decided with `usePhoneWidth`), handing either the one `StudioInterview` element it builds. No zone, tile or header of D564's page is drawn there.
   - **The bar** (SMP:17-18): `eadwyn-ai.png` at 34px, "Eadwyn AI" at 19px/800, the tab's label (Conversations, Tasks or My tickets) and the 40px "Open the Eadwyn menu" button.
   - **The gutter.** The page cancels the shell's `p-4 md:p-6` with matching negative margins, as D699's More page does, and uses the canvas's 22px.
   - **No advisor.** The page says "The guided founder interview is not available in this environment." `AdvisorUnavailable`'s "Your Studio records remain below." would be untrue where nothing is below (D-s).
   - **The chat's `phone` frame.** `PersonalAdvisor` draws no header and no panel tabs: the drawer and `?tab=` hold them. Conversations is the thread under its label, then the list (`belowThread`), in the page's one scroll, with "Currently:", the option chips, the text box, the mic, Skip and send pinned above the tab bar. Tasks and My tickets are drawn the canvas's way.
10. **The thread's label** is "<day> · <venture>": "Today" when the last message is from the viewer's today, else its date, and the day alone with no venture. The history read's `created_at` is kept on each message for it; a message sent in this tab counts as now. Every stored time is read as UTC (`toUtcInstant`), so "Today" and every date are the viewer's local day. Month names are written out ("12 Sep"), since en-GB `Intl` writes "Sept". With the interview complete, D324's collapsed row stands in for the thread, and the list follows it.
11. **The Conversations list** comes from `GET /api/eadwyn/conversations/mine`, drawn with D699's `PhoneRows`.
    - A rail row reads "<date> · <n> messages", with "Today" when it was updated today.
    - The profiling row reads "<date> · <answered> of <total> answered" with "<pct>%"; with no total it is the date alone, never 0%.
    - A half the Worker could not read is one line ("Your Eadwyn conversations could not be read."), and the list is never called empty while a half is missing.
    - **Rows open read-only.** The active profiling row is the thread, so it scrolls there. Every other row opens its transcript in D699's `PhoneSheet`: `user` and `assistant` messages only, no Send. A refusal from the advisor gate (423) shows the Worker's own message. The rail read is today's and still closes a stranded send.
12. **Tasks** (S2m): "Focus a section" over the same section stats as the desktop chips; the widget's own buckets drawn through `PhoneBuckets` (Proposed's "You’re all caught up!", Completed's three newest and "Show all <n>"), each card linking its own page; Community tasks; My suggestions ("Sent <date>" with Pending review, "Approved <date>" with what the grant paid, "Declined · <reason>"); and the foot "Suggest a task".
13. **My tickets** (S3m): "Credit balance" ("<n> credits", "US$1 each, spent on Perks", Perks), withheld and never 0 when unreadable; then each ticket as "<Type> · <date>" with D542's bounty words or D604's review words, linking `/help/tickets/:id`.
14. **The ticket card** (S4m, `PhoneTicketCard`): "New ticket", the "Title" and "What happened, or what you need" fields, Task / Bug / Feature and Low / Medium / High / Urgent as radio groups, "File ticket" waiting for a title, and Cancel. Its checks and messages are the desktop form's, and it files through `POST /api/tickets`. `AdvisorTicketPanel` returns it on any phone, before its desktop form, which keeps its selects and `MyBugReports`; the card leaves `MyBugReports` out, since My tickets is one menu away.
15. **The drawer** (`pages/founder/EadwynMenu.jsx`, S5m): a modal dialog labelled "Eadwyn menu" at `z-50` (over the tab bar, under #1660's panel and the Lightbox), with the scrim, the 286px panel, × and 48px rows, the current one violet with `aria-current="page"`. Escape is `useEscapeClose(onClose, { active: open })`; the focus trap and scroll lock are `ui/lightboxFocus.js`'s. Its rows are links to Open a ticket, Conversations, Tasks, My tickets and Archetype card; You, Your venture, What others see and Visibility wait behind `PROFILE_ROWS_LIVE = false`, which #1664 sets with their routes. The four Eadwyn rows, and the three venture profile rows once live, keep the page's `project_id`. `EadwynMenu` is exported for #1664's profile pages.

### The read

`GET /api/eadwyn/conversations/mine`, in `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/eadwyn.ts`, registered right after the per-workspace list and before `GET /conversations/:uid`. After it, "mine" would reach `loadConversation`, fail `UID_RE` and answer 404.

- **Caller only.** `requireAuth`; every query is `WHERE user_id = ?` with the caller. No id and no query parameter is read.
- **Rail rows:** the caller's 20 newest `eadwyn_conversations` (`ORDER BY updated_at DESC, id DESC`), each with a count of its `eadwyn_messages`.
- **One profiling row:** the row `/api/advisor/start` resumes (the newest `state = 'active'` by id), else the newest of any state (the row `/progress` reads). Listing every row would repeat "Profiling questions" once per restart. Its count is its `user` and `assistant` messages; `tool` and `system` are not shown.
- **Merge:** by `updated_at` descending, read as UTC; on a tie the rail row first, then the higher id. The rail rows never push the profiling row out.
- **Each half on its own.** A query that throws leaves that half out and names it in `unreadable`, so the page can say that half could not be read, never "no conversations".
- **Writes nothing.** `releaseStranded` is not called; a stranded send stays `sending` until its own conversation is opened.

| Field | Rail row | Profiling row |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | `uid` | `uid` |
| `kind` | `'eadwyn'` | `'profiling'` |
| `title` | `title`, or "<workspace> conversation" when empty | "Profiling questions" |
| `workspace` | `workspace` | `null` |
| `updated_at` | `updated_at` | `updated_at` |
| `messages` | count of `eadwyn_messages` | count of its `user` and `assistant` `advisor_messages` |
| `answered`, `total` | `null` | `answered_count`, `total_questions` |
| `active` | `false` | `state = 'active'` |

The response is `{ conversations: [...], unreadable: [] }`, where `unreadable` holds `'eadwyn'`, `'profiling'` or both. With the rail's chat unmounted since 2026-10-10, most founders' lists hold only their profiling conversation; that is the truth, and the list draws it. The client method is `eadwynApi.conversationsMine()` in `frontend/src/lib/api/eadwyn.js`. No migration and no other `/api/*` route is added.

### Departures from the canvas

- No default ticket type (decision 5).
- The ticket card, the drawer's Eadwyn rows, its three venture profile rows and every Studio link written here keep the page's `project_id` (decisions 3 and 15).
- One profiling row in the conversations list, not one per restart.
- Transcripts open read-only, with no Send (decision 11).
- Community tasks are kept above My suggestions: dropping them would lose the only way into a community task on a phone.
- No "Open Admin →": each card links its own page.
- D542's ticket words, with "Fixed: 150 credits" rather than "+150", and no reason and no "#n" on a row: both live only in the ticket's comments, which the row's hint says.
- The composer keeps the option chips and Skip, which the canvas does not draw; without them a choice question could not be answered, nor a question skipped, on a phone.
- The four profile rows are held back until their routes exist (decision 15).
- The segmented control the canvas builds (SMP:119) is never drawn; the drawer changes the tab.
