## D779 — The investor's, advisor's and operating partner's options fill, draft and place through the run path, and nothing is written without the member's Accept or Save

- **Status:** decided by S1, 2026-10-19 (#2104; task `X2` of D720's plan, #1874 §9). Built in parts of at most 30 option rows, one PR each. **Part 1 (§1) is the investor's 15 options. Part 2 (§2) is the advisor's 14 and the operating partner's 9 on the same components.** **Part 3 (§3) is the operating partner's 19 other rows**, every one a zone draft band, and with it every draft band of the three profiles names its own option to the rail.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `803e524`, with D777 part 1 (#2139) merged in. SPA paths are relative to `frontend/src/`, Worker paths to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Stacks on:** D777 part 1 (#2139), the never-fill registry; D765's run path (`RunOptions.rail`, `railFor`); D770's checked drafts (`CHECKING`, Accept only once a run is checked); D772's inventory (`lib/railOptions/investor.js`).

### The rule, per handler (D720 §9)

| Handler | What the option may do | What writes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `draft` | Draft on the member's press, into the band | The run writes the draft (`research_zone_drafts`). Accept stamps it, or first writes the record through the surface's own `accept` hook. Nothing else. |
| `form` | Fill the open form or panel | The answer is kept as unsaved history. The member's Save is the only write that keeps it. |
| `upload` | Place what the upload yields, one proposal at a time | The member's own document is saved. Any placement is a proposal, accepted on its own (D777 part 3). |

### 1. Part 1: the investor's options

Every row of `lib/railOptions/investor.js`, 15 options. The option keys:

- **Drafts (11):** `draft:stale-deal-notes`, `draft:screening-memo`, `draft:ic-memo`, `draft:closing-cover-note`, `draft:multiple-narrative`, `draft:updates-read`, `draft:support-summary`, `draft:lp-reminder`, `draft:session-brief`, `draft:thin-evidence`, `draft:index-gaps`.
- **Forms (2):** `ask:evidence`, `ask:question`.
- **Uploads (2):** `upload:document`, `reindex:document`.

**What the code already does, and this part pins:**

1. **Drafts.** Each option is a `ZoneDraft` band.
   - It runs only on the member's press, through `POST /api/research/drafts`, which carries the request's rail page and option (D765).
   - While the run is out, the band shows "Eadwyn is checking this draft." and offers no Accept (D770).
   - The run writes the draft and nothing else. Accept stamps it.
   - None of the investor's eleven surfaces has an `accept` hook. The only surface that has one, `research/funds`, is not an investor option.
2. **Forms.** "Find evidence" (Research's desk) and "Ask" call `POST /api/research/ask` on the press, with `rail` set.
   - The answer fills the open panel or thread.
   - It is stored in `research_ask_answers` as history, with `saved` at its default of 0.
   - Only the member's Save (`PATCH /api/research/ask/answers/:uid`) marks it saved. The desk has no Save of its own: its answer joins the Ask thread, where Save is.
3. **Uploads.** "Add to library" and "Re-index" save the member's own document.
   - They write `research_documents` and queue its embedding (`embed_entity`), and nothing else.
   - Indexing extracts nothing into a field, so there is nothing to place. Saving is not assistance, as D765 §1 holds for transcription.

**No code changed for part 1.** Each of the 15 already met its rule. What part 1 adds is `frontend/test/rail_options_run_d779.test.mjs`:

- It mounts each draft band with a stubbed run and checks: nothing runs on mount; the press runs once; the check is shown and Accept is withheld while the run is out; the stubbed draft is shown; and Accept is the only further write.
- It reads the Worker's handlers for what each route writes.
- It holds the part to the inventory. A row added to `investor.js` fails it, and becomes a later part's decision.

### 2. Part 2: the advisor's options, and the partner's on the same components

**Rows (23).** Every row of `lib/railOptions/advisor.js` (14), and the nine rows of `partner.js` that the operating partner reaches through the advisor's own components: the Studio interview (`components/advisor/PersonalAdvisor.jsx`) and Research. A shared row has the advisor's handler, file and marker, so one rule covers both. The option keys:

- **Advisor (14):**
  - `answer:interview`, `explain:question`, `transcribe:voice-answer` (Home);
  - `draft:terms` (Opportunities);
  - `draft:period-narrative` (Earnings);
  - `ask:library`, `draft:session-brief` (Ask);
  - `draft:checkpoint-brief` (Client prep);
  - `analyze:competitors`, `rerun:analysis`, `refresh:sources` (Companies);
  - `upload:document`, `reindex:document`, `draft:index-gaps` (Library).
- **Operating partner (9):**
  - `answer:interview`, `explain:question`, `transcribe:voice-answer` (Home);
  - `ask:library`, `draft:session-brief` (Ask);
  - `draft:checkpoint-brief` (Client prep);
  - `upload:document`, `reindex:document`, `draft:index-gaps` (Library).

**What the code does, and this part pins:**

1. **Draft bands.** `draft:terms`, `draft:session-brief`, `draft:checkpoint-brief` and `draft:index-gaps` behave as §1's drafts.
   - The band runs only on the member's press.
   - It shows the check and withholds Accept while the run is out.
   - The run writes the draft and nothing else, and Accept stamps it.
   - None of `practice/opportunities`, `research/ask`, `research/client-prep` or `research/library` has an `accept` hook.
2. **Earnings' narrative** (`draft:period-narrative`) is a draft the page holds.
   - The press calls `POST /api/ai/workspace/explain` with the page's coverage lines and keeps the text in the editor.
   - That route's one write is the audit of a named branch, and Earnings never sends a branch.
   - Accept (`PUT /api/advisors/me/period-notes/:key`) is the only write.
3. **The interview** fills its own thread.
   - **Answer** (`POST /api/advisor/tool/auto`) picks a tool and returns a call to action that the member presses. The tools only read.
   - **Explain** (`POST /api/advisor/explain`) streams into the tutor panel.
   - Both keep the turn as conversation history (`advisor_messages`, `advisor_turn_audit`, `advisor_conversations`, `activity_logs`). Neither records an answer or routes a write.
   - **The voice answer** (`POST /api/advisor/transcribe`) writes nothing. Its text fills the composer, and the member sends it.
4. **Ask** is §1's form: the answer is history, unsaved until the member's Save.
5. **The competitor analysis** (`analyze:competitors`, `rerun:analysis`, `refresh:sources`) runs on the member's press. It writes only its own analysis (`competitor_analyses`, `_candidates`, `_sources`, `_signals`, `_analysis_outputs`), the way a draft run writes its draft.
   - Nothing reaches a project or another module. A competitor reaches Validate only through a fill proposal (`_competitor_writes.ts`).
   - Re-run keeps the member's hand-added competitors (`keep_manual: true`).
6. **Uploads** are §1's: the member's own document is saved and indexed, and nothing is placed.

**One fix.** Earnings filed the note's `source` (migration 242) wrongly both ways:
- a first draft accepted as written was stored `'edited'`, because `drafting` is already false when Accept is pressed;
- a filed `'ai'` note the advisor then changed stayed `'ai'`.

`periodNoteSource` now compares the text filed with the model's last text: unchanged is `'ai'`; a changed model draft, or a re-filed `'ai'` or `'edited'` note, is `'edited'`; anything else is `'advisor'`.

**Open for the owner.** The competitor analysis is a `form` in D773's inventory, but its results are written on the press, with no Save. Re-run and Refresh replace the run's own output, and they reset the edited flag on the summary the member may have changed. This part pins what the code does and changes nothing there. Whether the analysis should wait for a Save, or Re-run should warn about an edited summary, is a decision for the owner.

**Tests:** `frontend/test/rail_options_run_d779_part2.test.mjs`.
- Each band is mounted with a stubbed run, through `frontend/test/_zone_draft_run.mjs`, now shared with part 1.
- Each route's writes are read from the Worker's source.
- The part is held to both inventories: a row added to either fails it.

### 3. Part 3: the operating partner's own boards, and the option every band names

**Rows (19).** The rows of `lib/railOptions/partner.js` that part 2 did not take. Every one is a zone draft band on a partner board:
- `draft:operating-brief` (Home);
- `draft:proposal`, `draft:win-loss-read`, `draft:counter`, `draft:renewal-risk`, `draft:quarter-narrative` (Pipeline);
- `draft:board-read`, `draft:chases`, `draft:capacity-findings`, `draft:weekly-reports`, `draft:renewal-risk` (Delivery);
- `draft:catalog-read`, `draft:expiry-consequences`, `draft:attribution-read`, `draft:consent-requests`, `draft:pass-reasons` (Offers);
- `draft:orphan-reassignment`, `suggest:paths`, `draft:single-contact-exposure` (Network).

The three parts together cover all 57 rows of the investor's, the advisor's and the operating partner's inventories.

**What the code already did, and this part pins.** Each band is §1's draft:
- the run happens on the press;
- the check is shown, and Accept is withheld while the run is out;
- the run writes the draft and nothing else, and Accept stamps it.

No partner surface has an `accept` hook, and no band passes `onAccepted`.

**The one fix: the option a band names.** A run carries the option its band names (`X-Rail-Option`, D765), and `run()` takes the option's models from its row of the rail matrix (D764). ZoneDraft names `railOption` when a band passes one, and D770's `draft:<surface>` when it does not.

The matrix holds each option under its inventory key. The investor's bands passed theirs (D772), but most of the advisor's and partner's did not:
- 15 of the partner's own boards passed no key, so their runs named `draft:pipeline/leads` and the like;
- Catalog and Perk deals passed none either;
- Relationships and Introductions passed the founder's keys;
- in part 2's rows, Opportunities and Client prep passed none.

None of those keys is in the matrix, so each run lost its option row.

Each band now names its inventory key:
- **one-profile boards** (the partner's Pipeline, Delivery, Offers, Organizations and Home, and the advisor's Opportunities) pass it as a literal, as the investor's do;
- **boards more than one profile renders** (`ServiceCatalogPage`, `PerksPage`, `ClientPrepZone`, `RelationshipsPage`, `IntroductionsPanel`) use D772's lookup, `railOptionFor(railPage, { file, control })`, as `AskZone` and `LibraryZone` do. The founder's Relationships and Introductions keep the founder's keys. A board no inventory lists keeps `draft:<surface>`.

**Tests:** `frontend/test/rail_options_run_d779_part3.test.mjs`.
- Each of the 19 bands is mounted with a stubbed run (`_zone_draft_run.mjs`).
- Each page and surface is read for its band and its Accept.
- For every draft band of the three profiles (37), it checks the key the band names on its page, and that the matrix holds an option row for that key.
