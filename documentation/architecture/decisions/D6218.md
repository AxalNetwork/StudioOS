### When a notice is raised

Health snapshots are written only by the recompute routes:
`POST /api/portfolio/health/recompute` and `/recompute/:uid`. No scheduled job
writes them. The notice hangs off that write, in `upsertSnapshot`, after a
snapshot is written.

A notice is raised only when both of these are true:

- the new snapshot is red, which is "Attention", D5129's word;
- the company's previous snapshot exists and is not red.

So no notice is raised for:

- a company's first snapshot, which is the same rule as #6210's "changed at";
- a company still red on a later recompute;
- a recovery to Watch or Healthy.

A recovery followed by a new fall is a new change, and notifies again.

A health read that fails writes no snapshot (D4645), so it raises nothing. A
read that fails inside the alert also raises nothing. That error is logged,
and the recompute answers exactly as before.

### Who is told

Only the company's holders are told, by the Portfolio positions rule
(`investorHeldProjectIds`, D1057):

- **Candidates:** the seat owners with a confirmed commitment on one of the
  company's deals, plus the active members of their seats.
- **Who is kept:** only a candidate whose held set includes the company. That
  needs an investor licence, a relationship and a confirmed commitment.

These are not told:

- a related investor with no confirmed commitment;
- a pending commitment;
- another company's holder;
- a founder;
- an inactive seat.

### Once

`portfolio_attention_alerts` holds one row per company, holder and change,
keyed on `changed_on`, the date of the first red snapshot. A notice goes out
only when its row is new. So re-writing the same snapshot never re-notifies,
and neither does a re-run on the same day.

The row is written whether or not the member's settings let the notice
through. A member who had the type switched off is not notified later about
that same change.

### The notice

- **Type:** `portfolio_needs_attention`, in category `scoring`, on the in-app and
  email channels. Push follows in-app.
- **Title:** "<Company> needs attention".
- **Body:** the evidence and its date. For example: "Health turned to Attention
  on 2026-11-08: score 31, from Watch (58) on 2026-11-01. Source: Health."
- **Link:** `/portfolio/companies/:uid`.
- **Settings:** Settings › Notifications has a row for the type, "A company I
  hold needs attention", unlocked on every channel. `notify()`'s one rule
  (D4615) applies the member's switch.

### Not built: the other two triggers

The issue names two other triggers: a missed report, and a runway under six
months. Neither is a health-snapshot change. The health score reads scoring,
activity and tickets, not reports or runway, so neither can be seen at the
recompute. Each needs its own trigger, and they are left to the owner's
choice:

- **A (my pick):** a daily scheduled check for both, filed as its own task. It
  raises the same notice type with its own evidence line.
- **B:** recompute health on a schedule, and add both as health inputs.
- **C:** do not notify for them. They stay in the Overview queue only.
