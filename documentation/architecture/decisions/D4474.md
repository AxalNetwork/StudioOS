## D4474 — Studio and Spin-Out Lab show one block per held licence (#4474)

Part 3 of #3704, on part 1 (D4472, `licences` on `/api/me`, merged as #4504) and part 2 (D4473, `heldLicences` and `holds()` in `App.jsx`, merged as #5006). It also sits on #5092 (D4622 part 2), which made the four role Studio homes lazy, each reading its own sources. #3704 has no decision file of its own, so this part's record is here. The owner's rule 3: a section two licences share (Studio, Spin-Out Lab) appears once, and its page shows each held licence's part as its own block. There is still no licence switcher and no "active" licence (rules 1 and 6).

### What was built

**Who gets the block pages.** `frontend/src/lib/multiLicencePages.js` is pure. `multiLicenceSet` returns the held licences in licence order (Founder, Investor, Advisor, Operating Partner) when a signed-in member on their own account holds two or more. It returns `null` otherwise:
- for an admin, with or without a "View as" preview;
- for an impersonation;
- for an exploring account;
- for a signed-out visitor.

`App.jsx` computes `multiLicence` from it, beside `heldLicences`. Every session it returns `null` for keeps today's page.

**Studio.** `pages/studio/MultiLicenceStudio.jsx` draws a "Studio" h1, then one block per held licence, titled "Founder", "Investor", "Advisor" or "Operating Partner". Each block is that licence's existing home from `pages/StudioHomesLazy.jsx`, unforked, with `embedded`:
- **Founder:** the home, then My tasks, as on a Founder's own Studio.
- **Investor:** handed the dashboard summary and the deal lifecycle, which `Dashboard` reads for it on a single Studio. Here the page reads them once, and only when Investor is held, through the existing `api.getDashboard` and `api.investorLifecycle`. `previewing` is false, because this is the member's own account. A summary read that fails reaches the home as a fixed sentence, never the error's message.
- **Advisor and Operating Partner:** the home alone, since each reads its own sources.

The first-login tour and Product testing are drawn once for the page, not once per block. With one held licence the component renders `Dashboard`, exactly as today. The route sends a single-licence member straight to `Dashboard` in any case.

**Spin-Out Lab.** `pages/spinout/MultiLicenceSpinoutLab.jsx` draws at most two blocks, in licence order (`spinoutBlocks`):
- **The Lab block** (`SpinoutLabPage`) appears once when Founder, Advisor or Operating Partner is held. Its title names the licences it serves ("Founder", "Advisor · Operating Partner"), and it sits at the place of the first of them.
- **The Investor block** (`SpinoutLabInvestorPage`) appears when Investor is held.

So Founder + Investor reads Lab, then Investor, and Investor + Advisor reads Investor, then Lab. Both pages stay lazy. `SpinoutLabPage` mounts, and so reads founder-scoped Lab state, only inside the Lab block. The route comment keeps that reason. With one licence the component renders today's page. On the route, `multiLicence` comes first in the ternary. An admin preview, an impersonation and an exploring account keep the `effectiveRole === 'investor'` ternary, and a signed-out visitor still gets `SpinoutLabPage`.

**Blocks.** Both pages draw through `pages/studio/LicenceBlocks.jsx`. Each block is a section with an h2 title and nothing else in its header, then its own error boundary and its own Suspense:
- one licence's failure or slow chunk never blanks the others;
- a failed block reads "This part of the page could not be shown. The other parts are not affected.", with Try again, which re-mounts the block;
- the boundary logs the block's key only.

**`embedded`.** It is added only where a home assumes it owns the page:
- the Investor, Advisor and Operating Partner homes drop their own "Studio" h1;
- the Operating Partner home draws a `<section>` in place of its `<main>`;
- below 1024px the Founder home draws the chat above its body, in place of `StudioPhone`'s sticky bar and negative gutter.

Nothing else in any home changes.

**One AIRail.** No home on `/studio` mounts a rail of its own (owner answer G on #3698). The block pages add none either, so the page keeps the one rail the shell draws for `/studio`, keyed for the primary licence.

### Departures from the issue text, and why

- **The issue's line numbers are stale.** The routes were found by searching `App.jsx`, at `path="/spinout-lab"` and `path="/studio"` on 6ac1f0a8af.
- **The Studio blocks render the homes, not `Dashboard` per block.** `Dashboard` decides `previewing` by `user.role !== <licence>`. For a member whose primary role is another licence, it would show their own Investor, Advisor or Partner part as a withheld admin preview. It also reads the deal lifecycle only for `user.role === 'investor'`. The block page therefore draws the homes directly and reads what `Dashboard` would have handed the Investor home.
- **The Google sign-in notice is not drawn on the block page.** It appears once, after a fresh Google signup. A fresh account holds one licence, and the single-licence path is `Dashboard`, which still shows it.

### Not decided here, and UNVERIFIED

- **`GET /api/dashboard` computes its investor fields from `user.role` alone** (`cloudflare-worker/src/routes/dashboard.ts`). On a block page where Investor is not the primary licence, the summary therefore carries no deal-flow total or match average. The Investor home draws those two figures as "Not recorded", with its own reason, never 0. Reading them for a held, non-primary Investor licence is a Worker change for #4623, the final multi-licence integration.
- **`SpinoutLabPage` passes `investorView={user?.role === 'investor'}`** to its program overview. A member whose primary role is Investor and who also holds Advisor sees the Lab block's overview without the Apply call to action, as an Investor sees it today. The page is embedded unchanged, as the issue asks.
- **Not checked in a browser.** The block pages were rendered in the Node test document with stub homes; the real homes inside a block were not. Phone width was not checked by eye.
