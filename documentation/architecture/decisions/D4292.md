## D4292 — Commission on subscriptions: one per paid invoice, twelve invoices, held 30 days, clawed back, milestone bonuses (#4292)

- **Status:** built by Claude Code for slot S26, 2026-11-03. Worker only. Not deployed and not checked live.
- **Part of:** #4289 (P3, D-f). It reads P2's (#4291) checkout metadata and P1's (#4290) terms. Neither is on `main` yet; see §6.
- **Migration:** `202611031345_influencer_subscription_commission.sql`.
- **Code:** `services/influencerCommission.ts`; wired in `routes/billing.ts` (webhook) and `index.ts` (the daily sweep).

### 1. What earns

`invoice.paid` calls `onInvoicePaid`. It writes one commission when all of these hold:

- **Attribution.** The invoice's subscription metadata names `referrer_user_id` and the member's `user_id`, and they are different people. `handle` and `channel` are recorded with it. These are P2's keys, read exactly as named. The metadata comes from the invoice (`subscription_details`, or `parent.subscription_details` on newer API versions), and from the subscription only when the invoice carries none.
- **Software only.** The price's product, read from the `stripe_products` mirror through `planOf`, is a founder, investor, advisor or partner plan.
  - Never LP access, deals, funds, or anything without a plan.
  - Market intelligence is not one of the four families the owner named, so it earns nothing until the owner says otherwise.
- **Terms.** The referrer holds active signed terms with the `subscription_invoice` trigger. The share is that trigger's `percentage_bps`; terms that set a flat amount instead pay it per invoice. No rate is in code (D1050).
- **The first twelve invoices** (D4289 §1.1: invoices, not months). The count is the member's paid invoices with a net amount above 0 on that subscription: a free or fully discounted invoice is not one of the twelve. Invoice 13 earns nothing.
- **No stacking.** A payment that already earned a purchase commission (`source_type` `purchase`, on its PaymentIntent) earns no influencer commission. The purchase path refuses the reverse case the same way (`paymentEarnedInfluencerCommission`), whichever event arrives first.

### 2. The amount

- The amount is the invoice's `amount_paid` (already net of discount) less its tax, times the share. Tax is read from `tax`, `total_taxes` or `total_tax_amounts`, whichever the API version sends.
- **Currency.** The commission is in the invoice's currency. The terms' caps (`cap_per_intro_cents`, `overall_cap_cents`) are in the terms' currency, so they bind only an invoice in that currency. They are measured against what the terms paid in that currency, never a sum across currencies.

### 3. Once per invoice

- The row is `commissions` with `source_type` `subscription_invoice` and `source_id` the invoice id, so the existing UNIQUE `(user_id, source_type, source_id)` holds.
- The side table `influencer_commission_invoices` records, per invoice:
  - which of the twelve it was;
  - its charge and PaymentIntent, for refunds and disputes;
  - its net amount and plan;
  - the handle and channel;
  - when it was paid and when it becomes payable;
  - any cancellation, and when the subscription ended.
- `invoice_id` is UNIQUE there too. A replayed webhook, however late, returns `replayed` and writes nothing.

### 4. The hold

- A commission is written `pending`, with `commissions.payable_at` 30 days after the invoice's `status_transitions.paid_at`.
- The daily sweep (05:30 UTC, `sweepPendingCommissions`) moves due rows to `accrued`, the status the payout request already claims.
- `status` has no CHECK in the baseline, so `pending` needed no rebuild. Its readers:
  - the payout request and its claim read `accrued` only, so a held commission can never be paid out;
  - `/api/network/commissions/me` gains `held_cents` per currency;
  - the terms cap counts pending rows as paid, the safe direction.

### 5. Refunds, disputes and bonuses

**Inside the hold, a refund or a dispute cancels the commission whole.** This holds even for a partial refund: D4289's rule is "a refund or dispute inside the hold cancels it". The row reads `reversed`, with `reversed_amount_cents` equal to its amount, and the side table records `cancel_reason`.

**After the hold:**
- A refund reverses its share through `clawbackReferralCommissionForRefund`, as the admin refund does: proportional and cumulative.
  - The function now takes `sourceType` and `sourceId`, so a subscription commission is found by its invoice id.
  - A purchase commission is found by its PaymentIntent, as before.
- An open dispute leaves the commission standing. A dispute closed as `lost` reverses all of it, and one closed as `won` changes nothing.

The webhook calls these first in `charge.refunded` and `charge.dispute.*`. They throw on a database failure, so Stripe retries, and they act only on a charge that paid such an invoice.

**Bonuses:**
- **Who counts.** A paid customer is a referred member with one of these commissions:
  - made payable by the sweep (`accrued`, or `paid` since);
  - not wholly reversed;
  - on a subscription that has not ended. `customer.subscription.deleted` stamps `subscription_ended_at`.
- **The milestones.** At 10, 25, 50 and 100 customers, the sweep pays the terms' flat `influencer_bonus_<n>` trigger once each (`source_type` `influencer_bonus`, `source_id` the milestone).
  - The bonus is payable at once, since it counts only members already past their hold.
  - Terms without the trigger pay no bonus for that milestone: no amount is typed in code.
  - At 250, HQ's Super Admins are notified once to agree a custom partnership.
  - `influencer_milestones` keeps each milestone reached, once.
- **Triggers.** `FIRING_RULES` gains `subscription_invoice` and `influencer_bonus_10`, `_25`, `_50` and `_100`, so HQ can put them in terms. The terms editor's list (`referralPartnersModel.js` `FIRING_TRIGGERS`, held equal by `admin_programs_d963`) gains the same five; the bonuses are flat only, each with its own reason (`flatWhy`).

### 6. Before P1 and P2 merge

- **Attribution.** The gate is the referrer's active signed terms, not P1's `influencers.status`. When P1 lands, pausing or ending an influencer must end or suspend their terms, or this part must also read `influencers.status = 'active'`. That is a one-line change in `onInvoicePaid`.
- **Who has terms.** Terms here are D958's `referral_partner_terms` on a `referral_partners` row; P1 adds the `influencers` segment. Nothing in this part depends on the segment.
- **Duration.** The twelve-invoice duration and the 30-day hold are program rules in code (`INVOICES_PER_MEMBER`, `HOLD_DAYS`). If a Strategic Partner's signed terms are to set their own duration, the terms need a column for it, and `invoice_number` has no upper CHECK for that reason.

### Tests

**`cloudflare-worker/test/influencer_commission_d4292.test.ts`, 11 tests.** Each is given `now`, or uses invoices paid on fixed dates far from any clock. They cover:
- invoice, then pending, then payable at 30 days, net of tax;
- a replay, including a late replay of invoice 1;
- invoices 1 to 12, and 13 earning nothing;
- no attribution, self-referral, no terms, a zero invoice, a deal price, an unknown product and market intelligence, none of which earn;
- the invoice currency, and caps that bind only their own currency;
- a flat amount per invoice, when the terms set one;
- no stacking, both ways;
- a refund inside the hold (a partial one cancels it all) and after it (proportional, then full);
- a dispute inside the hold, then won, then lost;
- the 10-customer bonus firing once, with an ended subscription and the hold respected;
- no bonus without a trigger, and the custom milestone notifying HQ once;
- the Stripe webhook end to end: `invoice.paid` twice, `charge.refunded`, a lost dispute, a refund inside the hold, and `customer.subscription.deleted`.

**`partner_earnings_d572`** gains a case: a pending commission is held, outside the balance, and no payout can claim it.
