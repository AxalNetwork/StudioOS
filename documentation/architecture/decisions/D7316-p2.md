## D7316 part 2 — Primary-register company discovery identities (#7316)

### Decision and scope

Add a separate, offline compiler for company records supplied directly by the
Norwegian BRREG, Finnish PRH and Estonian RIK registers. The registers bind a
legal identity to its reported homepage and provide legal-form and current
status evidence. The compiler derives admission from those native fields; it
does not generate primary-review approvals for Wikidata candidates.

The existing [D7316 decision](D7316.md), Wikidata review requirement and worldwide
acceptance gate remain unchanged. This part records the primary-register route
and preserves the earlier history, including the 11,000 bulk rows under a
quality hold. Those old held rows remain unchanged and do not count toward the
usable-company target.

The applied 2026-11-19 batch contains 25,500 new company identities: Estonia
8,000, Finland 8,750 and Norway 8,750. It is a three-country European cohort.
Although the largest country contributes 34.31%, it does **not** satisfy the
original 50-country and non-European regional minima. The compiler reports
those failures separately from the current 25,000-usable-company goal. A
completed proposal is not a production import receipt. The dated
[application audit](../../audits/RESEARCH_COMPANIES_REGISTERS_2026-11-19.md)
records the frozen batch and the separate production receipt, verified at
`2026-11-19T01:13:09.982Z`.

### Primary evidence and retained holds

`scripts/import-register-companies.mjs` consumes explicit candidate JSON, source
manifest files, native primary-record JSONL projections, an existing identity
snapshot, an as-of date and the same-day baseline observation timestamp. It
opens no database and performs no network requests.

Each source manifest identifies the official source URL, registry, licence,
retrieval timestamp, projection filename and its exact byte SHA-256, plus the
upstream snapshot hashes. Each candidate pins its complete native projection
record using recursively key-sorted compact JSON and UTF-8 SHA-256. Projection
records retain the source fields needed for admission and omit unrelated
personal/contact details. The compiler independently derives the legal name,
identifier, form, status, homepage, native activity and supported formation
year, then requires the candidate to match. A hash pins the captured bytes; it
does not independently authenticate the upstream publisher or check a live site.

The quality-hold ledger is mandatory, including when explicitly empty for a
source with no known holds. This run uses the recovered **314-entry** ledger;
its exact file hash is retained in the batch audit. A prior hold or exclusion
matches by domain, normalized Unicode name plus country, or registry plus
registration identifier. A new registry UID cannot clear a recorded website or
entity-scope hold. The ledger must not be replaced with a blanket approval or
silently omitted on replay. The entry count describes this run, not a fixed
minimum encoded in the compiler.

The entire supplied primary ledger is indexed before candidate selection.
Every identity in a shared-domain or same-name/country group is held, including
when the other identity was omitted from the shortlist. Conflicting records
for one registry identifier are held. Remaining candidates are deduplicated
against every observed directory row by source UID, normalized hostname and
Unicode name/country identity. Existing taxonomy names, regions and exact manual
overrides remain authoritative.

### Source-specific admission

| Register | Required native evidence |
| --- | --- |
| BRREG | `AS` or `ASA`; current Business Register entry and Norwegian country evidence; explicit false bankruptcy/liquidation flags; no recorded reconstruction, foreign insolvency or deletion; recent annual accounts or recent source-reported formation. |
| PRH | One current legal name and accepted company form; valid business status and current Trade Register entry; no unresolved special situation; a current qualifying tax/employer registration and source-listed homepage. Accepted YRMU forms are `16` limited company, `17` public limited company, `80` European company, `23` public limited insurance company and `24` limited insurance company. Partnerships `5` and `13` are excluded. |
| RIK | `OÜ`/code `5` or `AS`/code `1`, with consistent current names/forms and no special subtype or deletion; registered status; retained registry-card notes; native annual-report periods showing recent activity or recent first registration. Status `R` alone is insufficient. |

For RIK, unknown or unresolved card notes hold the company. The only permitted
current routine note is the exact sentence, after whitespace normalization:
`Kanne on tehtud äriregistri seaduse § 70 lõike 1 alusel.` Historical notes must
have a valid end date before the as-of date; unknown or future dates cannot hide
an adverse note. Redacted nonroutine notes retain their source hashes and remain
held. Activity recency is derived from native report periods, not a supplied
summary assertion.

Across these registers, absent/unknown activities and the conservative property,
financial or head-office activity exclusions remain held. The website
must be a usable HTTP(S) URL with one unambiguous registered host. Credentials,
IP/private hosts, social/directory profiles, malformed DNS names and concatenated
or encoded second URLs are rejected. These are identity and URL-structure
checks; current domain ownership, reachability, trading activity and investment
suitability are not asserted.

Native sector labels are retained without invented translations. Only BRREG's
explicit `stiftelsesdato` can supply a supported formation year. PRH and RIK
registration dates do not become founding years. Funding, valuation, revenue,
employee and diligence metrics are not inferred or populated.

Source terms are [BRREG NLOD 2.0](https://data.brreg.no/enhetsregisteret/api/dokumentasjon/en/index.html),
[PRH open data](https://avoindata.prh.fi/en/info/swagger-ui) and
[RIK open data](https://avaandmed.ariregister.rik.ee/en), with PRH/RIK under
CC-BY-4.0. Retain source and licence attribution and disclose normalization.
The [PRH YRMU codebook](https://avoindata.prh.fi/opendata-ytj-api/v3/description?code=YRMU&lang=en)
supplies the legal-form meanings above.

### SQL ownership and reproducible proposal

Verified source state is private behind an opaque frozen handle. Nested source
objects are frozen before they can appear in returned audit metadata. SQL
helpers accept only the exact unchanged objects admitted by this compiler;
copied audit rows, forged evidence maps or post-admission edits cannot emit SQL.

The proposal is insert-only and uses bounded statements below 90,000 UTF-8
bytes. Runtime UID, host and exact name/country guards supplement the broader
offline Unicode deduplication, so refresh the complete baseline immediately
before application. Field-level `company_sources` evidence is inserted only
when every inserted directory identity/provenance field, including nullable
sector and founding year, agrees exactly. Evidence uses
`source_type='official_registry'`, `confidence='medium'` and `verified_at=NULL`;
it does not certify current website ownership. Existing rows, metrics and
evidence are preserved, including on replay.

```sh
node --experimental-strip-types --no-warnings \
  --import ./cloudflare-worker/test/_ts-loader.mjs \
  scripts/import-register-companies.mjs \
  --candidates /path/to/selected-candidates.json \
  --source-manifests /path/to/brreg/source-manifests.json,/path/to/prh/source-manifests.json,/path/to/estonia/source-manifests.json \
  --quality-holds /path/to/consolidated-company-quality-holds.json \
  --existing /path/to/existing-snapshot.json \
  --as-of 2026-11-19 \
  --baseline-observed-at 2026-11-19T00:42:04.721Z \
  --out /path/to/proposed-import.sql \
  --statements /path/to/statements.json \
  --report /path/to/batch-report.json \
  --audit /path/to/source-audit.json
```

Inputs and outputs must be distinct, including resolved primary-ledger paths.
An existing SQL proposal with different bytes cannot be overwritten. Inspect
the source audit, rejected rows, original worldwide-gate failures, complete
inserted-UID plan and exact-source rehearsal before application. Application
and production receipts remain separate from compilation.

The distributed audit bundle omits the full pre-existing directory snapshot.
It retains that snapshot's pinned hash and the bounded aggregate comparison
proof. Recompiling the exact historical batch requires a separately authorized
matching baseline; the included public primary ledgers do not by themselves
reproduce historical deduplication against existing rows.
