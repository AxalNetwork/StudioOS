### 1. One helper, not two copies

Admin › Insights (D893) and this overview count the same accounts.

**The move.** Insights' queries moved to `services/accountCounts.ts` unchanged:
- the grouped pass over live accounts;
- deactivated accounts;
- sign-ups by week;
- last activity.

`adminInsights.ts` imports them. Its LP count is now `lpTotal`, which sums the per-profile LP count. An account holds one profile, so the total is the same distinct count.

**What the pass adds.** It now also sums three things on the same rows:
- email not verified;
- email not verified for more than 7 days;
- active in the last 30 days.

Insights ignores the new sums. Its 18 tests and the export's 13 pass unchanged.

**The rules,** in one place:

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| A live account | `is_active = 1`; a deactivated one is counted apart |
| A profile | `users.role` as stored |
| New in 30 days | `created_at` in the last 30 days |
| An LP | an approved `lp_applications` row |
| Active in 30 days | `users.last_active_at` in the last 30 days |
| Never seen | no stamp at all; counted apart, never as idle |

Active, dormant (Insights' block) and never seen add up to the live total.

### 2. The read

`GET /api/admin/accounts/overview` is `requireAdmin`. It returns `kpis`, `profiles`, `lps`, `product_testers`, `readiness`, `explorers`, `newest`, `attention` and `definitions`.

Each block is one of:
- `{ available: true, … }`;
- `{ available: false, unreadable: true, reason }` when its store failed;
- `{ available: false, recorded: false, reason }` when nothing records it.

One failed block never takes another with it, and a failure is never a zero. Each kind of "needs attention" is its own statement:
- if one kind fails, the list says `partial`, and that kind's `by_kind` entry is unreadable;
- if every kind fails, the list is unreadable, never an empty "nothing needs you".

**The four figures**

| Figure | Counts |
|---|---|
| Accounts | live accounts, new in 30 days, deactivated |
| Active in the last 30 days | the count of the total, with never seen and dormant beside it |
| Email not verified | the count, and those created more than 7 days ago |
| Waiting on you | Explorers ready for a profile, plus open LP applications (`pending`, `in_review`, `needs_follow_up`, the LP queue's own open states) |

Tester applications in "Waiting on you" are `recorded: false` until D1302's store lands. While either part is unreadable, the total is unreadable too.

**A widget per profile**
- The profiles are Founders (`founder`), Investors (`investor`), Advisors (`advisor`), Operating Partners (`partner`) and Explorers (`exploring`).
- Each widget has its count, new in 30 days, and eight Monday weeks of sign-ups, the current week to date. The weeks are Insights' growth chart, deactivated accounts included.
- `admins` and `other` complete the mix.

**LPs**
- LPs are live accounts with an approved LP application, by their own profile, as Insights counts them.
- The block also carries the open applications.
- **An LP record in a fund (`limited_partners.user_id`) is not counted.** That table is a fund's ledger of commitments, written by whoever administers the fund, and a row need not carry an account. Counting it would make an LP of anyone a fund lists, without the review an application gets. It would also make this count disagree with Insights'. If the owner wants fund records counted, both reads change together in `accountCounts.ts`.

**Product testers** are `recorded: false` with their reason until D1302's store lands.

**Readiness** gives five steps, for all live accounts and for each profile:
1. Email verified.
2. The Terms accepted: a `legal_acceptances` row for `tos_v1`, the activation funnel's key. If that table cannot be read, this step is `null` with `terms_reason`, and the other steps still read.
3. Identity check approved.
4. A profile at least 75% complete. The canvas says "at least 75%", so 75 counts. Insights' completeness bands put 75 in 51–75; the bands stay as they are, and this step uses the canvas's own threshold.
5. Active in the last 30 days.

**The Explorer split** reads the Exploring board's own states (`admin_exploring.ts`): live Explorers whose binding agreement is not sent, sent and not signed, or signed (`completed`) and ready for a profile. An agreement whose envelope row is missing, or belongs to another account, counts as sent and not signed. The three parts always add up to the Explorers.

**Newest accounts and Needs attention** list five rows each. A row carries the account's id, name, profile and country, never its email, so the page can open `/admin/accounts/all/<id>`.

Needs attention has four kinds, oldest wait first. A wait with no recorded start goes last. Each kind's `by_kind` count covers every account in that kind, not only the five shown.

| Kind | Who | Waiting since |
|---|---|---|
| `email_unverified` | email unverified for more than 7 days | the account's creation |
| `identity_waiting` | identity check `pending` | `kyc_submitted_at` |
| `explorer_signed` | Explorer with a signed agreement | `completed_at` |
| `lp_application` | an open LP application | its earliest open application |

Two kinds the canvas draws are named in `not_recorded`:
- a Product tester application waits on D1302;
- "limited access for 60 days" cannot be read, because `users.access_level` keeps the level, not when it was set.

**Other administrators.** An administrator's record is the Super Admin's (D1299), so another admin appears in Newest and Needs attention only for a Super Admin. Counts include admins, as Insights' do.

### 3. What is not here

- **Per-profile facts.** The canvas's facts under each widget are not in this read. That covers Spin-Out Lab, incorporated, paid investor plan, proof of capital, mentor cohort, partner verified and invitations. They are the boards' parts, D1304 on.
- **Licence seats.** They are the licence read's.
- **Scope.** The read answers for this deployment's own accounts (`scope` is `hq` or the branch code), as Insights does without `scope=`. A Super Admin's view of a subsidiary is not part of D1301.
