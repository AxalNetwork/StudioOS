## D9017 — Capital call line: a "bank reversed" state a person resolves (#9017)

D8534's owner answer (b), 2026-11-25: a reconciled receipt whose bank line fails or is restated keeps today's `needs_review` flag, and the call line gains a visible "bank reversed" state that a person resolves. There is no automatic reversal. This builds that state.

### What is recorded, and when

- **The table.** `capital_call_bank_reversals` (migration `202611272146_9017_capital_call_bank_reversals.sql`): one row per receipt whose bank line came back changed. It names the fund, the call line, the receipt, the match and the Mercury transaction, the cause (`failed`, `cancelled` or `restated`), the bank's status and cents as read, the receipt's cents, `detected_at`, and the resolution (`resolved_at`, `resolved_by`, `resolution_note`). Money is integer cents.
- **The writer.** `services/mercury/store.ts` `upsertTransaction`. When a stored line changes and now reads a voided status (`VOIDED_STATUSES`) or a different amount, `services/capitalCallBankReversals.ts` `recordStatement` inserts the reversal for the receipt the line's live capital-call match holds (`confirmed`, or already `needs_review` from an earlier change). It runs in one `batch` with the transaction update and the existing flag of the match and the Not ours mark, so a reversal cannot be lost between them.
- **Once per receipt.** `receipt_id` is unique, and the insert is `ON CONFLICT (receipt_id) DO NOTHING`. A replayed sync is `unchanged` and writes nothing; a second change to the same line (failed, then restated) finds the row and adds nothing.
- **Kept.** Two triggers refuse a `DELETE` and any change to what was detected, and refuse every change once the row is resolved. A `CHECK` keeps the three resolution columns together and the note at 10 to 1,000 characters.

### What it changes

Nothing in the money. The receipt (append-only since migration 312), the line's `paid` state, the LP's paid-in, the fund's deployed capital, DPI and TVPI stand, both at detection and at resolution. The journey asserts this on every read it makes (`money()` in `fund_reconciliation_journey_d8534.test.ts`).

Rejecting the `needs_review` match does not touch the row (`rejectMatch` is unchanged). The state stays until the resolve act.

### Who sees it

- **The GP's call read** (`GET /api/funds/:id/capital-calls/:callId`): each line carries `bank_reversed` (`{ detected_at, resolved_at }`, or `null`), and the call carries `bank_reversals` (`{ status: 'ready', rows }`, open ones first, each with its cause, cents, who resolved it and the note) or `{ status: 'unreadable', rows: null }`. Read apart from the call like the receipts, comms and notices (D6238): when it fails, the lines carry no field, never a `null` that would say the bank reversed nothing.
- **The investor's portal** (`GET /api/funds/lp-portal`): each of the caller's own lines carries `bank_reversed` (`{ detected_at, resolved_at }` or `null`), read for the caller's own line ids only, so no investor sees another's. A failed read is `bank_reversals_recorded: false` with no field on the lines.
- **The UI.** On the GP's call page (`pages/investor/BankReversals.jsx`, in `InvestorFundCalls.jsx`), a "Bank reversed · n open" block above the lines lists each reversal with Resolve, and a Bank reversed pill sits beside the line's state in the table and on the 390px card. On the investor's My commitment card (`SpinoutLabLpCommitment.jsx`), an open reversal is a notice under the line, and a resolved one says when it was resolved. Both have light and dark colours; the phone layout uses the call page's existing cards and 44px targets.

No canvas draws this state (the issue: "The display waits on a canvas"). The block reuses the call page's existing list, pill and form styles and the card's existing notice style; a later canvas may restyle it without changing the payloads.

### Who resolves it

`POST /api/funds/:id/capital-calls/bank-reversals/:uid/resolve`, `{ note }`.

- `requireFundGp`: the GP of record or an Admin. Anyone else on the fund is the gate's 402 (below the institutional tier) or 404 (not this fund's GP).
- `assertFundAct(c, gp, 'capital_call.bank_reversal')`, a new `admin`-tier act: a support session is refused (`support_session_refused`), whoever it acts as, and a frozen Admin gets the D135 423. It sits with recording a receipt, not with issuing a call, because it moves no money.
- A note of 10 to 1,000 characters is required (`note_required`). A reversal on another fund, or none, is `bank_reversal_not_found` (404). A second resolve is `bank_reversal_resolved` (409) and carries the resolution as recorded.
- **Audit.** The row keeps who, when and the note, and cannot be rewritten. In the same batch, one `activity_logs` row (`capital_call_bank_reversal_resolved`, with the fund and the reversal uid, no note text) lands only when the row changed, for the GP and the Admin alike. An Admin's act also writes `fund_admin.capital_call.bank_reversal` through `auditFundAct` (D714 decision 4).
- The client method is `fundCallsApi.resolveBankReversal` in `frontend/src/lib/api/fundCalls.js`.

### Not done here

- `fundUnmatchedCount` still drops a failed line (D8534 finding 2). The reversal row is now what shows the gap on the GP's side; counting it on the cash summary is a separate change.
- A bank line that goes back to a settled status after a reversal is not un-reversed by the sync. A person resolves it, and says so in the note.
