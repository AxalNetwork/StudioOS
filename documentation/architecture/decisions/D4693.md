## D4693 — The Pipeline's overview and zones share one scope, one set of definitions, one score (#4693)

This is package 4 of 6 of the `/pipeline` audit. It lands the parts of that
package that need no product decision; the rest is listed under "Not done".

### The failure

- **Company.** `/quotes/me` and `/quotes/analytics` narrow to the member's
  selected company (`(company_id = ? OR company_id IS NULL)`). The zones under
  `/partner/pipeline` (proposals, negotiations, retainers) and their writes
  filtered by partner only, so a firm saw one company's win rate beside another's
  proposals.
- **Win rate.** Analytics counted won ÷ (won + lost). Proposals counted a quote
  as *decided* when it had a `decided_at` stamp, so a withdrawn quote that
  carried one went into the denominator, and rounded to a whole percent where
  Analytics keeps one decimal.
- **Retainers.** The recurring total added every retainer with an amount, ended
  ones included. The board printed the raw amount under a "Monthly" heading, so
  a quarterly retainer showed three times its monthly share.
- **Negotiations.** The board counted every tracked row as "open" and printed a
  `status` field the row does not have; the Worker counted "not closed"; a
  negotiation left in an open stage on an accepted quote was live on one screen
  and not on the other.
- **Traction score.** Four capped parts summed to 110 on a number printed as
  "/100". `Number()` read `true` as 1, `null` and `''` as 0, and a negative user
  count or a retention of 400 was stored. A metric left out scored like a metric
  recorded as zero.
- **"Send counter"** accepted a draft, which stamps `accepted_at` and "nothing
  else — nothing is sent" (`ZoneDraft`), under a label that promised delivery.

### What changed

One rules module, `services/partnerPipelineRules.ts`, used by the overview and
every zone:

| Rule | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| `quoteOutcome` | `accepted` won, `rejected` lost, `submitted` open, `withdrawn` withdrawn, anything else unknown and counted nowhere. A `decided_at` stamp alone decides nothing. |
| `winRate` | won ÷ (won + lost), one decimal, null with none decided. `winRateBasis` is the one sentence that says what it counted. `analysePipeline`, the by-shape and by-quarter series and `/proposals` all call them. |
| `isActiveRetainer` | not cancelled, and `ended_at` absent or after today. A scheduled end is not an end yet. |
| `monthlyCents` | monthly as it is, quarterly ÷ 3, no amount or an unknown cadence null (never assumed monthly). |
| `negotiationIsOpen` / `negotiationLabel` | open = not `closed` **and** the quote still waiting; the label follows the quote (Won, Lost, Withdrawn) before the stage. |
| `companyBinds` | the two binds for `(? IS NULL OR x.company_id IS NULL OR x.company_id = ?)`. |

- **Scope.** `actingPartner` resolves the verified selected company once
  (`activeCompanyFor`; an admin's oversight view stays wide) and hands it to the
  three zone reads and to every write that loads a quote, an engagement or a term
  (`requireOwnQuote`, `requireOwnEngagement`, `ownTerm`). Another company's row is
  a 404, like any row that is not yours. Nothing selected means every company, and
  a row with no company is visible under any selection, as on the overview.
- **Responses say what they counted.** `/proposals`: `win_rate_basis`,
  `withdrawn_count`. `/negotiations`: `open_count`, `tracked_count`,
  `count_basis`, and per row `open` and `label`. `/retainers`: `active_count`,
  `ended_count`, a `mrr_basis` that names the active count and the ended ones left
  out, and per retainer `monthly_cents` and `active`. `retainer_count` keeps its
  meaning (every retainer on record).
- **Traction** (`services/tractionScore.ts`). A metric is a finite number or a
  plain decimal string; each has a unit and a range (percentages 0–100, growth not
  below −100, NPS −100..100, counts and money not negative) and out of range is
  refused, not clamped. Formula **v2** caps users 35, revenue 35, engagement 15,
  growth 15, which add up to 100. A part nobody recorded is `null` and named in
  `components_missing`; it is not scored as zero.
- **Versioned.** A new snapshot stores `_traction_formula: 2` beside its metrics
  (the two screens that list metric chips skip keys that start with `_`). A
  snapshot without it is formula 1, a 0–110 scale, and keeps its score. The
  decision-gate review states the formula and scale it read
  (`traction score 72/110 (formula v1)`). The 70 and 40 thresholds are unchanged.
- **"Send counter"** is "Save counter draft", and the page says nothing is sent.
  No delivery workflow is built; that needs a recipient, a channel and a delivery
  state, which is the owner's call.

### Not done, and why

- **The overview endpoints still carry their own copy of the company predicate**
  (`needs.ts`: `/quotes/me`, `/quotes/analytics`, the engagements read). The
  zones and the writes now use the shared helper; moving the overview onto it is
  a separate, mechanical change with its own risk.
- **Leads are not company-scoped.** They are open marketplace needs, which have
  no company on the firm's side.
- **No formula-version column.** Adding one means a migration on a table with
  several competing shapes (`scripts/sqlite-table-collisions-baseline.json`). The
  version rides in the snapshot's stored JSON instead; a column can replace it.
- **Old snapshots are not rescored.** Their stored number keeps formula 1's
  meaning; nothing is rewritten.
- **Not every summary has a basis line** (Leads, for one).
- **Not verified here:** an authenticated run against production data. The tests
  use synthetic partners, quotes and retainers.

Worker and 10 frontend mutations were each caught.
