### What the canvas decides

- **L0 is the production state:** “No licences have been issued” and Issue first licence lead. Search, filters and sort are hidden at zero; territory coverage is one line (27 available · 0 held · 0 suspended); seat utilisation reads Not recorded in operator language (no `U1`).
- **Populated ledger:** counts plus a Needs HQ summary whose items are filters; nine columns with one primary state per cell; rows deep-link to `/admin/licences/:uid/:section`.
- **Licence detail:** identity header, attention strip, sections Overview · Terms · Agreement · Access · Operations · History. Overview keeps licence, agreement, commercial, access, branch, billing and requests as separate facts, plus a Sources card (HQ ledger vs branch freshness).
- **Terms** are a contractual schedule; Amend terms starts a session with before → after values, Review N changes, downstream effects and a required reason; a conflict state when another admin changed the record.
- **Agreement:** the executed agreement leads; ledger terms, draft, sent and executed are distinct; readiness preflight; the frozen document is shown before Send for signature.
- **Operations:** requests, compliance, billing, and branch sync as first-class state with Retry sync and Reconcile deployment.
- **One consequence sheet** (what happens, what else, reason, can it be undone) for activate, suspend, reinstate, terminate, admins, invoices, send, deploy and sync. Partial success is distinct from completion.
- **New licence** is a six-step issuance; rules (overlap, suspension, release) appear at the step where they apply.
- **“Program grants”** is renamed “Program access by licence”. Eadwyn is a compact launcher.

### Backend required

- Terms conflict: **met by #4923 (D4694, #4694).** Both terms writes name the revision they read (`expected_revision`: `terms_revision` for `PATCH /:uid/terms`, `licence_terms.revision` for `PUT /:uid/terms`) and refuse a stale one with 409 `terms_conflict`, `current_revision` and `current`. Build part 2 adds who and when to that 409 (below).
- Branch sync: **partly met by #4929 (D4686, #4686), checked in part 3.** `licence_sync_state` holds, per licence, the last revision HQ numbered (`hq_revision`) and the last one a branch accepted in answer to a push (`branch_revision`, `last_success_at`), and `licence_push_pending` holds a failed push's revision and failure code. `GET /api/admin/licences/:uid` returns them as `sync`. What is still missing is a read of the branch itself: HQ never asks a branch what it holds, so "branch revision" is the branch's answer to HQ's last push, and a branch on an older build that answers `{ applied: true }` is recorded as holding the revision HQ sent (D4686). The canvas's "France branch responded 4 min ago" needs that read, or a branch heartbeat. Partial success and History's branch outcome depend on it.

### Build, part 2 (2026-11-08): Terms read, amendment and conflict

It builds boards L05 to L07 at `/admin/licences/:uid/terms`. The Worker change is small and comes first. There is no migration.

**Backend check.** D4694 (#4923) had already done what "Backend required" asked for the terms conflict. Each point was checked against the code:

- **(a) The Terms reads return the revision the write needs.** `GET /api/admin/licences/:uid` spreads the `territory_licences` row (`byUid` then `hydrate`), so it carries `terms_revision`. `GET /:uid/terms` returns `revision`. A licence with no schedule row reads revision 0.
- **(b) The 409 gives the frontend enough to show the conflict.** Both writes answered `terms_conflict` with `current_revision` and `current`, the core terms or the schedule's terms and sections. The answer did not say who made the change or when. That is the one gap, and it is closed below.
- **(c) The frontend sends `expected_revision`.** `buildCoreSave` (core terms) and `buildSave` (schedule) already sent it, and the new section sends it through them.

**Worker: who and when on the 409.** Both 409s now carry `changed_by` (the account's name) and `changed_at` (`LicenceTermsConflict` in `services/licenceContracts.ts`; the helpers are in `services/licenceTerms.ts`).

- **Schedule.** The schedule's `UPDATE` already writes `updated_by_user_id` and `updated_at` in the statement that bumps `revision`. The 409 reads them from the row, joined to the account's name.
- **Core terms.** `territory_licences` keeps no "last terms writer", so no row can answer. Its two writers record the revision they produced instead: the PATCH's `terms_changed` event and a renewal's `renewed` event now carry `terms_revision` in `detail_json`. The 409 names the actor and time of the event whose `terms_revision` equals `current_revision`.
- **Never a guess.** The PATCH logs its event after the branch push. If the winning write has not logged yet, or wrote before this change, no event names the revision, and both fields are null. The latest terms event is never offered in its place, because it may be an older change than the one that won. The screen then says who and when are not recorded.
- No new revision scheme, no schema change, no migration. Event details that already existed are unchanged apart from the added key.

**What was built (frontend).**

- **L05, Terms read.** `licences/TermsWorkspace.jsx` replaces step 4's `TermsEditor` on the Terms tab. It is read-only by default and headed "Read-only · current ledger terms", with Amend terms as its one action.
  - **Groups.** The terms are grouped in the schedule's own order: Parties, Territory and exclusivity, Term and renewal, Fees and revenue share, Deal terms, Seats, Brand and platform, Compliance and reporting.
  - **Rows.** Each group holds the core terms (fee, currency, term, shares, dates), D797's schedule terms, and the licence facts the agreement names (licensee, signatory, countries, seat counts). Each row shows its contract section: HQ's override, else the canvas default. A licence with no schedule row reads the defaults too.
  - **Data states.** A term nobody recorded reads Not recorded. A recorded fee of 0 reads "EUR 0". A licence with no currency reads Not recorded, never EUR. A successful read of no countries reads None. A failed schedule read reads Unreadable, with "The terms schedule could not be read. This is not a sign that no terms are recorded." and Retry. The core terms still read when the schedule cannot.
- **L06, the amendment session.**
  - **Starting.** Amend terms opens a banner: "Amendment in progress · started HH:MM by you · nothing changes until you review and apply". The amendable rows become fields with their labels attached.
  - **Changed fields.** Each changed field shows before → after under it, struck and marked, and in words for a screen reader ("Changed from … to …"). Colour is never the only signal.
  - **Review.** The button is "Review N changes", disabled with "No changes to review" at zero. There is no Save button. Review checks the fields first. An error names its field, is tied to its input (`aria-describedby`, `aria-invalid`), and focus moves to the first one.
  - **The review panel.** It lists each change as − before / + after, then "What else this affects", then the reason. The reason is labelled "Reason · required, recorded in History" and is tied to its error. Apply without a reason sends nothing.
  - **Discard.** Discard with changes asks in the page ("Discard N changes? Nothing has been saved."). There is no browser dialog.
  - **Fixed facts.** The licence facts are not amended here. In the session each one offers "Changed on its own tab", which opens Territory, Seats or Contract. A schedule that could not be read cannot be amended: its rows say so, and the core terms can still be amended.
- **What else this affects.** It lists only what the reads can know (`amendmentEffects`):
  - **Ledger:** always. The terms change on apply, and History records each change with the reason.
  - **Branch revision:** from the licence's deployment. "The branch at host is sent a new revision; if it does not land, HQ retries it", or "No branch is deployed, so no branch revision is sent". If the deployment registry could not be read, it says "Not known".
  - **Agreement:** from `GET /:uid/contract`, read when the session starts. Only a live contract counts; a voided or superseded one does not.
    - An executed (`signed`) agreement will no longer match, and a replacement agreement is needed. It is prepared on the Contract tab.
    - An agreement out for signature will no longer match.
    - A draft does not update itself, so issue it again.
    - With no agreement: none falls out of step.
    - An unreadable read says "Not known" with Try again. While it loads, it says Reading, never "none".
- **Apply.** Apply sends only what changed. The core terms go first, through `PATCH /:uid/terms` with `terms_revision`. The schedule goes second, through `PUT /:uid/terms` with its `revision`. Both carry the same reason.
  - **The outcome follows D4706.** An apply whose branch push did not land is partial ("applied to HQ's ledger; branch has not received them yet"), never Done. A timeout is unknown: the section re-reads, and the session's revision makes a second press safe, because a write that had landed turns the second into a conflict, not a duplicate.
- **L07, the conflict.**
  - **The alert.** A 409 opens an alert: "These terms changed while you were editing." It says who and when, only as the 409 carries them ("M. Okafor changed them at 8 Nov 2026, 14:24 UTC."; otherwise "Who changed them, and when, is not recorded yet."). Then: "Nothing of yours was saved."
  - **The table.** It reads Term · When you started · Now · Your change. It holds every term that moved on the server or that you changed. A server-side change is marked "changed since you started" in words.
  - **Nothing is lost.** Every typed value and the reason are kept.
  - **The two choices.** "Discard my changes", or "Reapply my N changes on the current terms". Reapply moves the session onto the 409's current values and revision, types your values again on top, and opens the review. Nothing is sent until Apply. A value of yours that now equals the current one is no longer a change.
  - **Partial.** If the core write committed and the schedule's was refused, the outcome is partial: "Your core terms were applied; your schedule changes were not saved." Only the schedule's changes are left to reapply.

**Departures from the canvas.**

- **No section number on core terms.** The fee, currency, term, shares and dates have no contract-section store, so their § column is "—", read as "No contract section recorded". The canvas shows §4.1 against the fee.
- **Contract § references are not amended in the session.** They are edited on the Terms schedule tab, as before. That tab stays, with its own form and D4694's conflict handling, until the detail slice (L03, L04) folds the tabs into the canvas's six sections. - **Reapply reviews first.** "Reapply my N changes on the current terms" opens the review on the current terms instead of writing at once, so the operator sees the diff against what is now stored before applying.
- **The amendment is a snapshot.** D4694's editor folded a refreshed licence into the open form: untouched fields followed the server, and a "Changed elsewhere" line warned about edited ones. The session instead keeps the values it started from, and a change made elsewhere is refused at Apply and shown in the conflict view. That is the canvas's L07. `reconcileCoreForm` stays in `lib/licenceCoreTermsForm.js` with its tests, unused by the page.

### Build, part 3 (2026-11-09): licence detail and Overview

It builds boards L03 (Overview, active and healthy) and L04 (Overview with operational problems), the identity header and the canvas's section tabs. It is frontend only: every fact has a read on main already, so there is no Worker change and no migration.

**Backend check.** Each fact was checked against the read it comes from:

- **Licence, Commercial, renewal, territory, type:** `GET /api/admin/licences/:uid` (the row, hydrated).
- **Agreement:** `GET /:uid/contract`, the licence agreement (`licence_contracts`). The package and program agreements of D5048 are a separate store and wait for #5051's canvas.
- **Branch:** the same licence read's `deployment` and `sync` (#4686), and `GET /:uid/deployment` for the provisioning phase (D4706).
- **Billing:** `GET /:uid/fee-invoices` (D802).
- **Requests:** `GET /:uid/requests?limit=1`, which counts every open request and returns the oldest first (#4700).
- **Access:** `GET /:uid/admins`, and the seats on the licence read.

D4819's "Backend required" line for branch sync was corrected above: #4929 built the per-licence revisions and the push acknowledgement after the canvas was drawn. A branch-side read is still missing.

**What was built.**

- **Identity header** (`licences/LicenceIdentity.jsx`). It shows the studio's name and legal entity, the kind sentence, then seven facts, each with its own state:
  - Reference: Not recorded when blank.
  - Territory: the countries, or None.
  - Type: Axal subsidiary or White-label, or Not recorded.
  - Status: the word, with a shape.
  - Renewal: the date with "in Nd" or "Nd overdue", or Not recorded.
  - Executed agreement: "Executed · signed <date>", "Not executed · draft" or "out for signature", None issued, or Unavailable with Try again.
  - Branch sync: Synced · revision N, Update pending, Out of sync, Deployment failed, No branch deployed, Not recorded, or Unavailable with Try again.

  The agreement and branch lines are the Overview's own facts (`identityFacts`), so the header and the Overview cannot disagree. The lifecycle actions (Reinstate, Suspend, Renew, Terminate) sit top right, as the canvas places More. The legal entity and the kind sentence stay under the name.
- **Needs HQ strip** (L04). It is drawn under the header only when something needs HQ, as "N items need HQ · …". The items are:
  - requests waiting;
  - an annual invoice overdue;
  - the branch has not received the current revision;
  - the branch deployment failed;
  - no administrator on a running licence;
  - a renewal within 90 days, or past its date.

  A fact whose read failed is named on the strip ("Whether anything needs HQ is not fully known: Billing could not be read"), so an empty strip never stands for "nothing needs HQ" when HQ could not look.
- **Section tabs.** The first row is the canvas's six sections: Overview, Terms, Agreement, Access, Operations, History. It is a `role="tablist"` with `aria-selected`, roving `tabIndex`, and arrow keys, Home and End (`tabKeyMove`). A section that holds more than one of today's tabs draws them as a second tablist under it, with the same keys. Both rows are URL-backed through part 1's `?tab=`. A section opens on its first tab, and `/admin/licences/:uid/:section` opens that section. The default is now Overview, where it was Entity.
- **The Overview** (`licences/LicenceOverview.jsx`, model `licenceOverviewModel.js`). It has seven fact cards and a Sources card. On a phone they stack, with Licence, Agreement and Requests first.
  - **Licence:** the status, since the start date, the suspension note, or the steps before activation.
  - **Agreement:** Executed, Out for signature, Draft (with unfilled fields), None issued, or Unavailable. Whether today's terms still match the executed agreement reads "Not instrumented". A contract records its template and hash, not the terms revision it was issued from, and `terms_changed` events are also written by administrator changes, so their times cannot answer it.
  - **Commercial:** the annual fee per year with its currency, and the revenue share; the token split as the Licensee's share. A term nobody set reads Not recorded. A recorded 0 fee reads €0.00. A fee with no currency is never shown as EUR.
  - **Access:** the number of administrators, or No administrator, which is attention only on an active or suspended licence. Then the seats licensed, or "Seats not recorded" when no seat row exists, and utilisation not recorded.
  - **Branch:** the deployment phase first (Deployment failed, Deploying, Partly provisioned, outcome unknown). Then, for a linked branch, the sync record:
    - Update pending, with the failure sentence, "branch on 142 of 143" and the last attempt;
    - Out of sync, when HQ numbered a revision the branch has not accepted and nothing is queued;
    - Synced · revision N, with when the branch accepted it;
    - Not recorded, when no revision has been numbered yet.
