## D6705 — Private people, capital and screening in formation cases (#6705)

The founder who owns the case can maintain the people and capital before payment. Each write checks the case revision and records its actor in the same transaction. Ownership is recorded in basis points. Partner-specific consent can be withdrawn after payment. Named people without an account have a founder-supplied private email used solely for their notice. Read responses omit it. Notices require approved copy and a positive email transport receipt. Editing a person invalidates prior notices and screening coverage. Partner assignment, membership, notice, screening and sharing permission must all be checked before disclosure. Counsel wording and non-US launch approval remain pending.

Sanctions screening uses the same OFAC, EU and UK HMT lists and scoring as account screening. A match waits for HQ. Only the assigned HQ admin or a Super Admin with fresh step-up may decide it. Reasons belong to the private screening record. Founder responses may report “We are checking your details”.

### Retention and erasure

Counsel owns retention periods, lawful bases and legal holds under #5510 and #5511. No duration or automatic purge is inferred.

| Table | Private data and erasure handling for review |
| --- | --- |
| formation_case_people | Limit collection to formation identity, applicable countries, roles' ownership, partner verification reference and notice routing. Erase notice addresses and identifiers when the approved basis ends. Identity documents are excluded. |
| formation_case_person_roles | Case roles follow the person's retention decision. Remove dependent role rows on authorized person erasure. |
| formation_case_capital | Amounts, currency, shares and a case-scoped evidence reference follow the case retention decision. The document store owns the underlying document. |
| formation_case_consents | Retain partner, scope, actor, wording hash and withdrawal evidence subject to counsel's consent-proof schedule. Withdrawal immediately prevents further sharing on that consent. |
| formation_case_screenings | Restrict match details, reviewer and reason to HQ. Resolve screening and case holds before erasure and apply counsel's compliance-record schedule. |

The Worker notice reader requires the form's recorded counsel fact `named_person_notice_template`. The active template must contain the partner substitution and have a legal approval for its exact body hash and country. A missing approval returns `awaiting_counsel`. No wording is seeded. The receipt records provider acceptance for a particular person revision and partner. A linked account supplies its email, and an unlinked person's private notice address comes from the founder. Optional birth date is used only for a natural person's screening.

The v2 case transition checks details before quote/payment and re-screens before paid/submitted. The #6710 offer and partner read implementations must call the same screening and partner-specific sharing gates. Withdrawing permission remains available after payment. Removing a participant omits it from current requirements and clears its routing address while preserving any prior review evidence.
