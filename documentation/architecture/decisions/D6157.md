## D6157 — One closing overview read for /deals/closing, with the caller's closing room (#6157)

The Closing zone (DE08 A) drew its per-deal rows from four lists and one checklist read per deal, joined in the browser, and never read the closing record (`services/closings.ts`), so an investor's own closing line was not shown. `GET /api/deals/closing-overview` answers the row in one scoped read. It is `routes/deal_closing_overview.ts`, mounted ahead of `deals.ts` so its `GET /:id` does not read `closing-overview` as an id.

### Which deals

Funded deals (`dealStage` calls `funded` closing) on a venture that is not deleted, narrowed by the deal audience rule (`readableWorkspaceDealIds`: deal staff, the venture's founder, an investor invited to or committed on the deal), less every withdrawn or unreleased preparation (`hiddenDealIds('transactional')`, D5454). A caller with no such deal gets `{ deals: [] }`. If the deal record itself cannot be read the read is refused (503 `closing_overview_unreadable`): without it there are no rows to draw.

### Each part reuses the read that already scopes it

| Part | Read | Recorded shape |
|---|---|---|
| `checklist` | `closingChecklistForDeal` (now exported from `deals.ts`) | `done` of `total`; a skipped item counts neither way |
| `envelopes` | the esign list's columns under `esignEnvelopeScope` | `executed` (only `completed`) of `total`; `signatures_outstanding` on `sent` paper; `uncounted` for paper that records no recipient |
| `conditions` | `openConditionsOnDeal` (D467) | `open`: the number the caller may see; `held`: any open condition, theirs or another firm's |
| `transfer` | `deal_transfers` on a deal the caller already reads | `recorded`, `count` |
| `closing` | `listClosings`, then `readClosing` | `uid`, `status`, `reach`, `own_lines` (the caller's own lines and their status only) |

Each part is `{ state: 'recorded', ... }`, `{ state: 'unrecorded', reason }` (no checklist, no paper, no closing) or `{ state: 'unreadable', reason }`. A part that fails costs only that part; it is never 0. Another firm's open condition holds the deal without a number, as `transfer-gate` says.

### What the scopes mean for an investor

An investor's envelopes are the ones they created, are about them, or are asked to sign (`esignEnvelopeScope`), so `executed` counts their own paper, not the round's. Their closing part is the closing they hold a line on, with their own lines only (`readClosing`'s `own` reach); a closing they hold no line on reads `unrecorded`. Both are the scopes the existing reads already apply; this read adds no wider view.

### Not here

The Closing zone keeps its four reads until the #4815 rebuild switches `closingListModel.js` to this one (`dealClosingOverviewApi.read()`). No schema change.
