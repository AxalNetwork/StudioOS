## D6245 — Fund performance is an LP's and a GP's read, in the Fund chrome (#6245)

Part of the 2026-11-14 coordinator audit (#5128). The owner chose **C** on #6245: `/funds/performance` is both an LP's read of the funds they are in and a GP's read of the funds they run. The GP rows come from #6246 (the Worker's GP-of-record read); this decision is the page.

### What was wrong

- No link in the app pointed to `/funds/performance`.
- With the Institutional tier, `InvestorWorkspacePage` returned the page bare (`embedded`), so it had no title, crumb, zone row or rail.
- The page asked for the Institutional tier. `GET /api/fund-performance` asks only for the Investor licence (D627), so an LP the Worker serves was shown "Run your own fund".

### The rule

- **The gate is the Worker's.** `App.jsx`: `holds('investor')` draws the page. A non-admin without the licence gets `<FundPerformancePage locked />`, a locked notice that never reads the API and never shows an empty list. An admin outside investor view still forwards to Fund administration (D1160, `FundOpsRedirect`). No tier is asked.
- **The page draws its own Fund chrome.** That is `ZoneHeader` (family `i6`) with the crumb Fund ‹ Performance, the h1 "Fund performance" and a refresh button, then the `WorkerRail` for the Fund workspace. The figures are `FundPerformanceView` as before (D627).
- **The zone row is shown only to an Institutional reader.** The Fund zones (LPs, Calls, Accounting, Reporting) are GP operations and would only show an LP the locked notice. So `ZoneHeader` takes `nav={false}`: the crumb stays and the row goes.
- **The inbound link.** The Fund landing's summary has "Open fund performance" (`link-fund-performance`). The fund page's link is #6247.
- `railPages.js` keeps its one alias row for the path (key Investor · Fund), with its note updated. `RENDER_NOT_YET` loses `Investor /funds/performance`: the route now draws one rail with its key.

### Not changed

- The Worker read and the figures; the GP rows are #6246.
- The admin console's NAV & performance tab (D1164).
- The visual redesign, which waits on the design brief (D6244).
