### 1. The page (STG1)

The page has two reads, and each fails on its own:
- **The storage read** draws every figure.
- **The public plans read** names the plan on its pill and lists the plans with more room.
  - Its rows are the catalogue's (`planCatalogue.ts` and `storagePlans.ts`), with a sold plan's monthly price where the mirror holds one.
  - If it fails, the pill names no plan, and nothing else changes.

**The blocks, in the canvas's order:**
- **Your storage:**
  - the plan pill and the state pill;
  - "X of Y", with what is left or how far over;
  - the bar;
  - the legend;
  - "Your plan includes Y…";
  - the files whose size is not recorded yet, counted and said;
  - Compare plans.
- **By kind:** bars, and "By kind · as a table" with files, size and share of what is used.
- **By company:** the sidebar note, then each company with its ventures (the first one open), and "Not tied to a company" last.
- **Largest files:** the ten, each with its name, kind, company, size and day.
  - Filters by Kind and Company, and sorts by Size or Added.
  - Each row has Open, which goes to where the file lives (`AREA_PLACE`).
  - Imports have no page, and say so.
  - A list the Worker marks incomplete says that a larger file may be missing.
- **What counts and Does not count,** byte for byte, with "1 GB = 1,024 MB."

**Sizes are binary,** as the catalogue counts them (`storageSize`). No allowance figure is typed anywhere in the page, the model or the test: the test reads `storagePlan` and `publicCatalogue` from the Worker's source.

**The bar** is only ever drawn with an allowance:
- Each kind's share of the plan is drawn to scale.
- It has ticks at 80% and 95%.
- From 80% on, it is amber past the tick.
- When full, it has a red edge.
- Over the plan, the scale becomes what is used. The plan's end is marked inside the bar, and the overrun is hatched past it. **Nothing is clipped,** and the progress value stops at 100.

### 2. Near, full and over (STG2, STG3, STG5, STG16)

The state is the Worker's (`state`, D1169 §3). The words are the canvas's:

| State | Pill | Notice |
|---|---|---|
| `near_80` | Near your limit | You have used 80% of your storage. Uploads keep working. |
| `near_95` | Almost full | Your storage is almost full. At 100%, new uploads pause. |
| `full` | Storage full | Storage full. New uploads are paused. Everything you stored stays, and you can still open and download it. Then: A recording already in progress finishes and is kept. |
| `over` | Over your plan | Your plan is [plan]. You are using X of Y. Nothing was deleted. New uploads resume once you are under Y. |

**Over the plan, the largest files come first,** as STG5 draws.

**The canvas's over notice names the day the plan changed.** No read returns that day, so the notice names the plan without the day.

### 4. STG4: the one refusal, where it happens

D1170's `storage_full` refusal has one shape. `storageRefusal(error)` turns its figures into one sentence and two links:

- **Room left:** "This file needs X. You have Y left on your plan." It has two links: Open Storage and Compare plans.
- **Full:** "Storage full. Nothing new can be stored until you free up space or change plan. Everything you stored stays."
- **A file that counts to the company owner:** "This file needs X. It counts to the company owner’s plan, which has Y left." The full case has its own sentence. It has no links, because that plan is not the member's to open or change.

`StorageRefusal.jsx` draws it the same in all three places:
- **The data room upload:** the file reads "✕ Not added", with the block under it. The queue's short word is "Refused: no room left on the storage plan". The size limit keeps its own words.
- **The message composer:** the block, with "Send without the file". The file stays chosen until it is removed.
- **Record a Session:** the block, with "Start returns once there is room. A recording already in progress finishes and is kept." It is drawn in place of `startRefusal`'s text.

**The canvas's wording differs slightly.** It writes "Needs X. Y left on your plan." in the composer, and a recording's own sentence for Record a Session. The issue asks for one message that is the same everywhere, so the three share the data room's sentence.
