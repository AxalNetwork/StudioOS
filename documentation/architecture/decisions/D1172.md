### 1. Pricing: the Storage group (STG18–STG19)

**Every Compare plans table ends with a Storage group,** marked New. Its rows:
- **Storage:** each card's allowance;
- **Best suited for:** on Founder and Investor only (#2449 owner question 11). It is set in words, not in mono.

**Where the figures come from.** They are the public read's, `GET /api/public/plans-and-packs`. Each card names its storage plan key (`storage` in `PLAN_CARDS`). The cell reads, in this order:
1. that plan's row in `plans`, from `storage_bytes`, `storage_reason`, `best_suited` and `best_suited_reason`;
2. else the plan's entry in `catalogue`, from `storage` and `best_suited_for`. Institutional has no product, so it has no row.

**When there is no figure:**
- **Advisory Firm and Partner Firm** have no catalogue plan, so they read "Negotiated" (#2449 owner question 2).
- **A missing figure** reads "Not recorded", never 0.

**The unit** is binary, as the catalogue counts it: 1 GB = 1,024 MB. A decimal gigabyte reads 953.7 MB.

**No byte count lives in `sitePricing.js`;** a test scans it for one. The phone's one-plan table ends with the same group.

**The LP band (STG18b, STG19)** gains its storage line, marked New, from `lp_access`'s row:
- "[10 GB] personal storage."
- "Fund documents are excluded."

A read without the figure says "Storage with LP Access is not recorded yet."

### 2. HQ › Platform › Storage (STG20)

**The page** is at `/admin/platform/storage`. It is `hqOnly` and sits on the Platform strip after AI usage, in both `HQ_STRIPS` and the shell's literal strip. It reads D1170's `GET /api/admin/platform/storage` and draws:
- **Six tiles:**
  - Total stored, across the members with files;
  - Members with files (staff not counted);
  - Near the limit (80% or more);
  - Storage full;
  - Over their plan;
  - Size not recorded (being measured, not in the total).
- **Stored by plan:** a column per plan row, stacked by kind (Other hatched). Chart or Table.
- **Members by state, per plan:** a bar per plan row. Chart or Table. A row with no members says so, and a negotiated row says its members are not measured.
- **By plan:** plan, allowance, members, used in total, median used, near the limit, full, over their plan. Sorted by Used in total, or the owner's table order. The canvas's foot line follows.

**What a studio admin sees (STG14a).** `hqOnly`'s card names the page ("Platform storage is for HQ. Studio admins see their own storage in Account.") and leads to Your own storage (`/account/usage/storage`). A signed-out visitor goes to sign-in, as on every guarded page.

**No member or file is named.** The read carries none. The page draws only the fields it names; a test puts a person's name on the read and finds it nowhere on the page. There is no Eadwyn rail: the boards draw none.

### 3. The Worker: the owner's table by price

**STG20 groups plans as the owner's table on #2449 does,** by price (D1166 §5, question 1):
- Free;
- $20 a month: Validate + Build, Advisor Pro, Operating Partner Pro;
- $50 a month: Investor Plus;
- $100 a month: Raise + Grow;
- $150 a month: Investor Pro;
- LP Access;
- Negotiated: Institutional.

**`STORAGE_TIERS` in `storagePlans.ts`** holds that grouping, beside the allowances. It holds no figure: each tier's allowance is its plans', and a test proves they share one. Staff hold no plan and are in no tier.

**HQ's read gains `by_tier`.** Per tier:
- the plan names, from `planCatalogue`;
- members, and members with files;
- bytes in all, by kind, and the median member's;
- members by state.

It also gains `totals.members_with_files`, which leaves staff out.

**What cannot be read:** D1170's rule holds.
- **An unreadable area** leaves the members and blanks the bytes and states, with the reason.
- **Unreadable plans** blank `by_tier`.

The payload still has no name, email, id, uid or file name.

D1170's overall `by_state` and `by_plan` are unchanged.
