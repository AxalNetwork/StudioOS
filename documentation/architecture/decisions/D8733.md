### What the walkthrough found

- `homeFeed()` lists blocked and at-risk work first, then due milestones, capacity, renewals and seats, and returns `out.slice(0, 6)` with no word that more exist. Six blocked clients hide the due milestone and the renewal.
- Each action goes to `/delivery/health`, `/delivery/board`, `/delivery/capacity` or `/pipeline/retainers` without naming an item, and `partnerTiles` links "Open the feed" to `/delivery/health` although the feed also holds capacity and renewals.
- The targets cannot all take an item: `RetainersZone.jsx` keeps its sheet in component state with no URL parameter, `CapacityZone.jsx` has no person focus, and `BoardZone.jsx` opens nothing and says nothing for a key outside the loaded rows (including past the first 200).
- At 390 × 844 the feed started around 993 CSS px down. The populated feed was reviewed in source; the preview withheld private data.

### What the canvas decides

- The Partner Studio frame stays as shipped (masthead with Customise, firm line, Eadwyn, locked profile band, five firm tiles, operating brief). Only the firm feed and the pages it opens change.
- Every row names its client and opens that item: Blocked opens the engagement's Sheet on Board, Due opens Health narrowed to the engagement, over cap opens Capacity narrowed to the person, and a renewal opens that retainer's sheet. Each destination shows Back to Studio · Firm feed.
- The summary keeps six rows but reserves one for each kind present. Kind chips above the rows count every alert, and the disclosure names the ones hidden.
- Show all expands in place, grouped by kind, with each destination printed, and can be filtered to one kind. "Open the feed" goes.
- On return the view is kept and focus goes back to the action used. If the item has left the feed, focus goes to the alert now in its place and a status line says why.
- A removed engagement or one of another firm, a person no longer on the roster and a removed retainer each get their own fallback.
- A failed read shows its kind as Unavailable, never 0, with Partial · 2 of 3 reads and the existing retry. Module states, phone 390 steps 1 to 6, keyboard order (11 tab stops) and the heading outline are drawn.
