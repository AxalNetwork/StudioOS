## D1374 — Meet's own refusal is a block: the bot reads the live Workspace refusal page

- **Status:** a fix, from a live page the owner captured on 2026-10-29. Built by S21.
- **Builds on:**
  - D886: the Meet adapter;
  - D1373: names an unread page.
- **Migration:** none.

### What happened

After D1373, a run on the owner's Meet call failed as `platform_blocked@meet_page_unread`. The owner opened the same link in a private window, as a guest who is not signed in, and Meet showed this page:

- "You can't join this video call";
- a Return to home screen button;
- "Your meeting is safe. No one can join a meeting unless invited or admitted by the host."

The meeting is hosted by a Google Workspace account whose Meet policy turns away anyone who is not signed in to Google, before they can ask to join. The bot is such a guest. The host never sees a request, so the bot can never be admitted.

The adapter had two faults that turned this into `unread`:

- **The classifier needed two lines.** It took "blocked" only together with an "Only people in this organization can join" line, and this page has no such line.
- **The match needed plain spaces.** The `blocked` hook matched the words only with ordinary spaces between them.

### What changed

- **`classify`:** Meet's refusal alone is `platform_blocked`.
- **The `blocked` hook:** it matches its words across any whitespace, including non-breaking spaces. It also matches the page's "No one can join a meeting unless invited or admitted" line.
- **The fixture** gains the observed page as `blocked_live`. It is reconstructed from the owner's screenshot, not recorded.

A run refused this way now ends as `platform_blocked@meet_prejoin_result:platform_blocked`.

### Not changed

The bot still joins as a guest who is not signed in. A meeting whose Workspace refuses such guests cannot take it. The ways in are the owner's to choose:

- the host organisation's Meet policy;
- a signed-in account (#3678);
- Google's own transcript import (D871).

### Verified

- **Bot suite:** 128 of 128 pass on real Chromium.
- **Mutation check:** restoring the two-line rule fails both new tests.
