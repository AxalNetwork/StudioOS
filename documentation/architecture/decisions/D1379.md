## D1379 — What Google's OAuth verification reads on axal.vc

- **Status:** asked for by the owner on 2026-10-29, after Google's verification of the Meet scopes (D1376) came back non-compliant. Built by S21.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1376: Axal creates the Meet call (`meetings.space.created`);
  - D757: Meet transcript import (`meetings.space.readonly`);
  - D680: the legal pages' layout.
- **Migration:** none.

### What happened

Google's verification of the app "Axal VC" returned three findings:

1. **"Your privacy policy page at https://axal.vc/privacy does not have sufficient content."** The policy did not mention Google data at all. Google requires a policy to say which Google user data the app reads, how it uses, stores and shares that data, and to carry the Limited Use statement.
2. **"Your homepage is behind a login page."**
3. **"Your homepage does not explain the purpose of your app."**

For findings 2 and 3, the homepage is a single-page app. Before its script runs, the page read only "Loading…". Its fallback title and description named "Global Venture Partner Network", not the app name Google shows on the consent screen.

### What changed

- **The Privacy Policy gains section 09, "Google user data"** (`PrivacyPage.jsx`). Contact moves to section 10, and the date is now October 29, 2026. The section says:
  - what each Google product is used for (Calendar, Meet, Drive and Sheets, Gmail send only, and the profile);
  - Google's Limited Use sentence, linked to the Google API Services User Data Policy;
  - no sale, advertising or third-party profiling, and no training of generalized AI or machine-learning models;
  - when the data is transferred, and when staff may read it;
  - storage: tokens are encrypted, and imported transcripts stay with their interview;
  - deletion: disconnecting deletes the stored tokens and revokes access. Access can also be removed at Google's permissions page, or deletion asked for at privacy@axal.vc.

  Each claim was checked against the code. Gmail is used only through `gmail.send`; `gmail.readonly` is a legacy grant the app offers to remove and never reads. Disconnecting deletes `google_oauth_tokens` and calls Google's revoke endpoint.
- **The homepage shell** (`frontend/index.html`, which every route serves before the app boots) now shows:
  - the heading "Axal VC";
  - one sentence on what the app does;
  - links to the privacy policy and terms;
  - "Loading…" under them.

  React replaces it once the app starts. The fallback `<title>` and description name Axal VC.
- **The prerendered homepage description** (`ogRegistry.js`, `/`) says what the app does. The title was already "From idea to funded company — Axal VC StudioOS".

### Not changed

- **Brand copy:** `brand/gvpn.ts` and the landing page's own copy are untouched.
- **Scopes:** unchanged. Both Meet scopes stay. `meetings.space.created` covers only calls Axal created. `meetings.space.readonly` is still needed to import transcripts of calls made in Google Calendar (D757).

### Owner steps that no code can do

In the Google Cloud console's verification form, the owner still has to:
- write the scope justifications;
- record the demo video;
- appeal the homepage findings, once this has deployed.

Until Google verifies the scopes, the owner and any test users can still grant them. Google shows the "unverified app" screen; choose **Advanced → Go to Axal VC**.

  - the new title;
  - the homepage description.
