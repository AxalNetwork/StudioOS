## D4817 part 4 — PF-04 Reports: the reporting obligations inbox and the report detail (#4817)

Part of #5311 (PF-04): the fourth board of the Portfolio v2 canvas (`design/incoming/Portfolio v2 Page.dc.html`, boards PF-04 "A · Obligations" and PF-04b "B · Report detail", with the PF-10 phone layout), drawn in the frame PF-01 built (D4817 part 1). Frontend only, against today's Worker reads. No route, Worker or schema change.

**What is built.** `frontend/src/pages/investor/InvestorPortfolioUpdates.jsx` is rebuilt in place. It keeps its file, its mount through `PortfolioWorkspace` and its address, `/portfolio/updates`, so every link to it still resolves. It draws in `PortfolioFrame` with `tab="updates"`: the local navigation marks Reports current, the document title is `Reports · Portfolio · Axal VC`, and the board carries its one `WorkerRail` (#5130's structure, the same props as before). An admin's Reports tab is unchanged (`PortfolioUpdatesPage`). The page holds, in the canvas's order:
- **Four tiles**: Received (`n of m` for the measured period), Not received, Due date (Not recorded, with why) and Source handling.
- **The reporting obligations inbox** (PF-04 A, "who owes what"). One row per company in `kpi-compliance`'s population (the companies held in the caller's book) for the period the Worker measured, the last completed one unless a period is asked for. Companies that have not reported come first, then by name. Its columns, in the board's order:
  - **Company**, a link to `/portfolio/companies/:uid`;
  - **Period**, in words (`Q3 2026`);
  - **Due**: "No due date recorded". The cadence read carries no due date, so the row says so and no days overdue are computed. `days_since_report` is not turned into lateness;
  - **Received**: the day the last update arrived (`last_reported_at`), or Never reported. Under it, the latest readable update's title and its first KPI values, each formatted by its unit (#5365 F-07);
  - **Status**: the shared `ObligationStatus` (compact), an icon and a word (Received, Due, Not received, Not recorded, Unreadable), never colour alone;
  - **Missing**: the period's update when none arrived; otherwise each required KPI the update leaves out or blank, measured only against an update that speaks for the period, by the Worker's own rule (`periodCounts`: the same label, or a month inside the quarter). An update for another period reads "Not measured";
  - **Last chase**: the newest chase from the chase log, with the count; Not chased; or Unreadable;
  - **Report detail**, and **Chase** where the company has not reported.
- **The toolbar** stays the zone's: This period, Overdue and Rules; Chase all overdue, Edit rules (unbuilt, as before) and Export of the rows shown. Rules swaps the inbox for the KPI rule set, kept as it was (unreadable, empty and populated stay three states).
- **The report detail** (PF-04 B), in the shared Sheet, for one company's latest founder update:
  - what was received: title, period (named "not the period measured" when it is not), submitted date, version and source;
  - each field with its value, source and date: the update's sourced figures first (a figure the founders left out reads Not recorded with their reason), then each value typed into the KPI form that no figure covers ("Typed in the update"; a blank is Not recorded, never 0);
  - the required KPI set against it, each Carried or Missing;
  - the health snapshot beside it, with its date, said to be the latest snapshot and not a reading of the report;
  - the update as written;
  - its history: the submission and each chase, newest first;
  - a door to the company's Reports (`/portfolio/companies/:uid/reports`).
- **Duties under signed documents** (`PortfolioReports`, RPT9, `/api/reporting/portfolio`) keep their own section below, unchanged, with their own due dates, states and holding routes.
- **Kept from the old page:** the Eadwyn band (`ZoneDraft`, `draft:updates-read`), the "How updates are handled" card and the boundary line.

**Each source is read on its own** (`usePortfolioReads`): reporting (`kpi-compliance`), founder updates (`portfolio-updates/latest`), positions, the chase log (`portfolio/chases`) and health. A failed one labels its own cells or panel Unreadable with a Retry for that source alone, and a line under the navigation names it. A failed reporting read is never an empty inbox, a failed chase log never "Not chased", and a failed updates read never "no update" in the detail. A Worker `detail` never reaches the page.

**The two company Reports actions stay as they are.** #5311 holds "the two Reports actions" (Ask for this report, Waive this period) for #4817's addendum. The addendum canvas has landed (`Portfolio v2 · Addendum.dc.html`, PFA03 to PFA05 and PFA10, merged 2026-11-12), but it marks itself "owner review required", D4817 says #5311 builds the approved frame once the owner approves it, and no approval is recorded on #4817 as of 2026-11-13. So PF-04 draws neither action. Both stay on the company's Reports tab (`CompanyReports.jsx`), unchanged, with their confirmations. The inbox's signed-duty rows and the report detail link there. The chase, per row and Chase all overdue, keeps today's write, set and messages.

**Departures from the canvas, and why.**
- **No due date and no "11 days overdue".** The cadence read records no due date for a period, so Due reads "No due date recorded" with its reason, and Status says Not received rather than a count of days. Duties under signed documents do carry due dates; they stay in their own section.
- **The detail opens in the shared Sheet**, not a 520px column beside the table, so it works the same on a phone.
- **The detail is the founder update** the obligation is measured from (`portfolio_updates`). Reports sent under signed documents are read in the company's Reports reader, which already shows each figure's source.
- **No "Ask for runway…" in the detail.** Asking is one of the two actions held for the addendum; the detail links to the company's Reports instead of the canvas's "Open company".
- **No health implication computed from the report** ("Watch (MRR down 9% QoQ)"). The detail shows the latest health snapshot with its date and says it is not a reading of the report.
- **No "Reporting settings" link.** The KPI set is read-only for an investor and shown by the Rules view.
- **Last chase shows the count of chases** rather than "2nd chase".
- **The holding route is not on the inbox** (an addendum gap). The signed-duty section shows each holding.
- **The tiles replace IP2's strip**: Runway alerts left, since the Overview's queue and Positions' attention rules carry runway.
- **The local navigation carries no counts**, as in PF-01.

**Not drawn, because no read supplies it.** D4817's "Backend required" items stay with #4642, #4644, #4645 and #4647. This board also needs none of the following, and it invents none of them: a due date on the cadence obligation; a chase delivery outcome; a per-report health implication. Each reads as absent; none is estimated.
