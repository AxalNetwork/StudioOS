## D1334 — Investor Plus is sold, between Investor Basic and Investor Pro

- **Status:** decided by the owner on 2026-10-27 (pricing answer: "Investor Plus is $50 a month"); built by S21 on #3540, 2026-10-28.
- **Migration:** none. `users.investor_tier` is TEXT with no CHECK, so `plus` needs no schema change.
- **Replaces:** the "Investor Plus is not sold" rule of D786, D1008, D1013 and D1050. Plus was on /pricing, but no `investor_tier` named it.
- **No price is typed.** The $50 is Stripe's. It is read from the D1 mirror, as every plan price is (D1050).

### 1. Why this had to be code before it was a price

Before this change, the webhook mapped any investor product that was not `institutional` to `professional`:

- `checkout.session.completed` did it with a `: 'professional'` fallback;
- the subscription mirror did it by naming only the two tiers.

A Stripe product with `investor_tier=plus`, created before this code, would have handed out Investor Pro for $50.

### 2. The tier ladder

`InvestorTier` is `free | plus | professional | institutional`. The ranks are free 0, plus 1, professional 2 and institutional 3 (`middleware/requireInvestorTier.ts`, and `TIER_RANK` in `frontend/src/sidebarConfig.js`).

**Plus passes no Professional gate.** Every `requireInvestorTier('professional')` and `requireInvestorTierExcept('professional')` keeps Pro's features from Plus. These include:

- `/api/dd`, `/api/pipeline`, `/api/deals` and `/api/ic`;
- `deal_views.ts`, the calendar, and the archetype entitlement.

The Institutional gates are unchanged.

The tier names live in `services/investorTiers.ts`, a module with no imports. `paidInvestorTier(raw)` returns `plus`, `professional` or `institutional`, or null for anything else. Every reader of Stripe metadata uses it:

- `routes/billing.ts`: the checkout, the swap's `resolveScopePrice`, `checkout.session.completed`, and the subscription mirror;
- `routes/billing_checkout.ts`;
- `services/planSubscription.ts`;
- `services/aiCredits.ts` (`planOf`);
- `services/catalog.ts` (the audience and `validateProductMetadata`);
- `services/promos.ts`;
- `services/moneyRails.ts`.

**An unknown tier grants nothing.**
- **Checkout completed:** logs and writes nothing.
- **Subscription mirror:** takes the status-only branch.
- **Swap:** refuses the price.

### 3. Checkout

- `investor_plus_monthly` and `investor_plus_yearly` are the plan keys. The owner's approved prices are $50 a month, or $510 a year (15% off). `scripts/plan-investor-prices.mjs` plans both, once the Plus product exists.
- A tier with no plan key at the asked interval gets 400 `invalid_interval`. It never falls back to another tier's price.
- The inline checkout (`/api/billing/checkout/*`, D1051) sells Plus through the catalogue entry. The entry is `investor:plus`, with `matches: { investor_tier: 'plus' }` and `sellable: true`. `NOT_SOLD.plus` is gone.

### 4. What Plus unlocks, and its caps

The /pricing card's lines (`frontend/src/lib/sitePricing.js`) are unchanged:
- the research workspace;
- watchlists and opportunity tracking;
- AI-assisted research;
- personal notes;
- deal comparisons;
- standard limits.

Every cap sits between Basic and Pro:

| Cap | Basic | **Plus** | Pro | Institutional | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introductions a quarter (`INVESTOR_QUOTAS.intros_per_quarter`) | 3 | **10** | 25 | 100 | `middleware/requireInvestorTier.ts`, read by `routes/introductions.ts` |
| Deal rooms (`INVESTOR_QUOTAS.dealroom_max`) | 1 | **2** | 5 | unlimited | the same; `routes/deals.ts` |
| Colleague seats | 0 | **0** | 0 | 4 | the same |
| Introductions a member may accept each month (`monthlyAllowanceFor`) | 3 | **5** | 10 | 25 | `services/introductions.ts` |
| AI licence key | `investor:free` | **`investor:plus`** | `investor:professional` | `investor:institutional` | `services/aiBudget.ts` `AI_LICENCE_KEYS` |
| Storage | 1 GB | **50 GB** (unchanged, D1167) | 250 GB | negotiated | `services/storagePlans.ts` |

- **Market Intelligence full lens** (`util/marketIntelTier.ts`): any paid investor tier, so Plus is included. A founder's lowest paid tier has the full lens too. Market Intelligence exports remain Pro.
- **AI allowance:** 25% of the price, computed from the Stripe price (D783), as for every plan.

### 5. The upgrade a refusal names

`nextInvestorTier`: Free → Plus → Professional → Institutional.
- The deal-room quota, a full deal room's 402 and an introduction quota's 402 name the next tier up.
- Free's `upgrade_to` is now `plus`.
- `frontend/src/components/checkout/checkoutEntry.js`'s ladder offers Plus from Free and Pro from Plus.
- The paywall shows only the plans that unlock what was asked for. A Pro feature never offers Plus.

### 6. Stripe, after this merges

The account production uses is the Stripe sandbox. The steps:

1. In HQ › Payments › New product, choose Investor › Investor Plus. The dialog now creates it with `investor_tier=plus` and logs the change.
2. Add the prices: run `node scripts/plan-investor-prices.mjs --catalog <export>` for the $50 monthly and $510 yearly price bodies, or add them by hand.
3. Press **Sync from Stripe**.

Until the product is mirrored, /pricing shows the Plus card with no price, and the checkout sheet says no price is on sale.

**Never create the product before this code is deployed.**
