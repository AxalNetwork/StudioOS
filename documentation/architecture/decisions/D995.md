### 1. Where it lives

  - Review on To review, and Open on All profiles, go to the address and remember the tab;
  - opening the address cold draws it over To review.

**Each read answers alone.** A failed history or agreements read says "Could not be read" with Try again in its block. A failed profile read says so in the lightbox, with no decision. A missing reference says "No partner profile has this address."

### 3. The dialogs (PRF5, `profiles/ProfileDialogs.jsx`)

Each dialog says what it changes, who is told and what is recorded before anything is written. A refusal is said in the dialog, in the Worker's words, and the dialog stays open. There is never a browser pop-up.
- **A, Verify and send,** and **B, Verify:** the agreement's name and recipient, "They are told by email and in their activity.", that verifying does not change their profile, and that the decision is recorded. It goes through the verify route (D991, D992), with the notes and the provider.
- **C, Reject:** a reason of 10 to 1,000 characters that the member reads, with its count. Reject stays disabled until it is long enough.
- **D, Reopen:** D991's Reopen, with an optional reason. Nobody is told. It moved here from the page (D1127).
- **F, Unbind** (a Super Admin's): it names the account the address now belongs to, and needs a reason of 10 or more characters. `request()` asks for the fresh sign-in check and retries (D993's write bar). It is recorded in Governance.

### 4. The send results (PRF6)

- **Sent** ("Verified. The agreement is out for signature, sent to …") and **Rejected:** the next waiting profile in the queue opens, and the page says what changed. The list keeps its filters.
- **Not delivered:** the result stays in the record with the signing link and Copy.
- **Not ready** (D758's `esign_refusal`): the result stays in the record, in the Worker's words, with Open in Contracts.
- **An existing envelope:** says no second one was sent.
- **The plan check:** a DocuSign send the plan does not allow (402 `tier_required`, D992) shows "DocuSign needs the Raise + Grow plan. Choose StudioOS signing, or upgrade first. Nothing was changed." in the dialog. Nothing is saved.
- **A failed member notice** (`member_notified: null`, D991) adds "The notice to them could not be sent: tell them yourself."

### 5. The Worker

`GET /api/admin/partner-profiles/:ref` adds `unbind: { eligible, reason, holder }`.
- **The rule** is `POST /:ref/unbind`'s own: bound, and the address now belongs to another account (matched without case) or to none.
- **So** the page offers Unbind only when the write would take it.
