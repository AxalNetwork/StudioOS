### What is built

An investor's Investment tab (`/deals/:id/investment`, and `/deals/:id/commitment`) is four cards in DE09's order, seven and five columns wide on a desktop and one column on a phone:

- **Your commitment.** The amount, its state (Pending or Confirmed) beside the title, when it was recorded, the firm it is held for, the note, and who reads the note: the note line is said with a note and without one. **Change amount…** is offered on a Pending commitment on an active deal, and **Withdraw…** on any live one, because that is where the Worker takes them: `POST /api/deals/:id/commitments` changes a Pending row on an active deal and refuses a Confirmed one (409), and `POST /api/deals/:id/commitments/:uid/withdrawal` withdraws a Pending or Confirmed row whatever the deal's status. With no commitment, Commit on an open deal, or why the deal takes none.
- **Investment route.** Direct, Through a syndicate and Through a fund, with the investor's stored preference (`commitments.route_preference`, D6195) marked by a filled dot, a border, "Your preference" and `aria-current`. A preference of null marks no route and says "No preference recorded. No route is selected." — never Direct. Under it, D6195's sentence: the route is a preference, and the studio sets it in the closing room.
- **Round.** From `GET /api/deals/:id/round`: the confirmed total of the round's target with a bar, what is pending, and the count of investors with a live commitment, with the line that other investors are counted, never named, and their amounts are not shown. That read returns a total and a count only, so there is nothing else to hide. Disclosure is #4631's and is not changed.
- **Closing status.** From `GET /api/deals/:id/stage`, read for the selected firm. The step's word as a chip, the next step in a sentence and one primary action that follows the step: Terms ready → Review terms (the Terms tab); Committed → Review signing and Signed → Review funding (the closing room); Funded → View company reports. Before Terms the card says closing has not started and which step the investor is on, and offers nothing; a pass offers nothing. "Open the closing room" stays beside the primary action, unless the primary action is the closing room.

### A read that failed is never "none"

Each card reads alone. A commitments read that failed says so with Try again in Your commitment, and the route card draws no route at all (three unmarked routes would read as "no preference"); neither offers Commit. A stage read that failed says so in Closing status, never "Not started". A withdrawal reads all three again.
