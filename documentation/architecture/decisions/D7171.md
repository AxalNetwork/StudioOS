### Backend (the ◇ items of D7036)

- **Coverage for every market, not the page.** The D6284 fields (`countries`, `source_count`, `newest_source_at`, `sources`) are derived for the whole directory in the same fixed number of queries as before, so filters, totals and counts are right past 100 markets. `sources` is still listed for the page only, capped at 20.
- **Filters before paging.** `country` (two-letter codes, repeatable or comma-joined, any of), `fresh` (`30`, `90` = 31 to 90 days, `older` = Stale past 90 days, `none`), `src` (`named`: any evidence naming the market; `sector`: sector-level only; `none`) and `sector` (tracked sector names, used by the investor's "Show only markets that match"). A market whose countries are Not recorded never matches a country. An unreadable value is a 400 with a code (`invalid_country`, `invalid_fresh`, `invalid_src`), never dropped: an ignored filter would answer the whole directory under a filtered heading.
- **Counts per option** (`facets`), each over the markets the *other* filters leave, with the markets whose countries are Not recorded counted apart. `total` is the filtered count; `directory_total` the whole directory.
- **`q` matches the tracked sector too**; each item says `matched_on` (`name` or `sector`) and the answer counts both.
- **Coverage summary** (`summary`) over the filtered markets: freshness bands, countries recorded and the two Not recorded reasons apart, mapped and unmapped, per tracked sector its markets, sector-level sources, newest sector-level source and markets named by evidence, and the taxonomy's own counts summed (`supplied_total`, labelled, never a size). No size, growth or ranking.
- **Optional items, both built:** `sort=newest_source` (undated last either way) and `sources_by_kind` per market (count and newest date for sector-level and for evidence naming it, over the full list, not the capped one).
- Freshness is measured against the Worker's clock at the read, returned as `read_at`, so the page's ages agree with the bands.

### Frontend

- Two tabs, **Market directory** and **Listed companies**, kept in the URL (`tab=listed`). Government ownership is a line in the coverage note, Not recorded with its reason, not a tab.
- `pages/research/MarketDirectory.jsx` draws the directory: "Search markets or sectors", Country (multi-select with counts), Source freshness, Source type, Sort, View (Table, Cards, Analysis) and the "Group by tracked sector" toggle (Q2 A, `group=1`); active filters as chips with Clear filters; the result line as a polite live region; the five columns with Not recorded and the Worker's reason; the expanded row; Cards; Analysis from `summary`; paging at 25 a page. The page asks for one page and never re-filters or re-sorts rows.
- `pages/research/marketDirectoryQuery.js` keeps `tab`, `q`, `country`, `fresh`, `src`, `sort`, `view`, `group`, `page`, `open` and `thesis` in the URL. A market's link to its sector page carries the view in `from` with the row open; `MarketsBack.jsx` ("‹ Back to Markets · “energy” · Germany") rebuilds it through the same reader, so it can only be `/research/markets`; the directory then focuses the open row's expand button. `SectorProfile.jsx` keeps `from` through its market-to-sector redirect.
- **The thesis (RMD-08).** For an investor, `GET /api/investor-profile/me` is read and drawn beside the results (stacked above them below `lg`): set, no thesis, and unreadable ("not a claim that nothing matches", with Retry). Q1 A: a market matches only through the published crosswalk `THESIS_CROSSWALK` (profile sector → tracked sectors); keywords and geographies never match. "✓ Matches your thesis sectors" appears only on a real match, with the profile sectors it came through. The match count is over the filtered directory, from `summary.by_sector`.
- Readings are hidden for investors (RS-17, #6273 Q2): the card and its form are not drawn and the list is not read.
- Listed companies: search, Source and Sort over the Worker's existing `q`, `source` and `sort`, kept in the URL (`lq`, `lsrc`, `lsort`).
- Phone: below `md` the table becomes the cards; filter menus open as a bottom sheet with 44px rows. Dark: every surface has its `dark:` pair.
