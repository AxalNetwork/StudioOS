### 2. The header and the key strip (DL1)

**The header.**
- **What it shows:** one h1, the back link, the sector, the status, and the lead partner when one is recorded.
- **Its buttons:**
  - Invite Investors, for an admin on an open deal;
  - Commit Capital, for an investor (its dialogs are part 5's);
  - Pass, while the investor is at Invited.

**The key strip** has four tiles:
1. The round, and its instrument.
2. What is committed, with a bar when the share of the round is known.
3. Your commitment, for an investor (a pending or confirmed one; a withdrawn one is not yours any more). Staff and the founder see Investors instead: the distinct investors with a live commitment.
4. When the round closes.

Every figure the deal read lacks is "Not recorded" with its reason, never 0.

Two reads stand behind the third tile:
- **Commitments:** the strip reads `GET /api/deals/:id/commitments`. That read is already shaped per role (#2647): an investor gets only their own rows, and a partner gets the total only. So a partner's Investors tile is "Not recorded", with the reason.
- **Unreadable:** if that read fails, the tile is "Unreadable", never "none".

### 3. The stage track (DL2)

**The read.** It comes from part 2's `GET /api/deals/:id/stage`, for investors only. Founders and staff have no track, as in DL16 and DL17.

**The steps.**
- **The eight steps:** Invited, Reviewing, Diligence, IC, Terms, Committed, Signed, Funded.
- **What each step is:** steps before your step are done; your step is now; the rest are ahead.
- **A firm with no IC record:** its IC step reads "Not recorded" once you are past it (DL2e–g). The step you are on is never "Not recorded".
- **"You passed" and "Passed by the studio":** no step is drawn as current or done. The Worker does not say how far you got.
- **No stage at all:** the Worker's reason is shown instead.

**Dates.** The read carries none, so a step says Done, Now or a dash, and no date is invented.

**Exactly one next action.** It is the Worker's `next_action`, drawn once:
- a step that opens another page is a link;
- "I'm interested" is a button. It opens an in-page dialog saying what it tells the founder, and only then posts the answer.

Pass asks in the same way.

**On a phone** (DL21a), the track is a list and the next action sits fixed at the foot.

**A failed stage read** shows "Unreadable" with Try again, and draws no next action. The header, key strip, tabs and Overview all still render.

### 4. Overview (DL1)

The investor's Overview has these cards:

| Card | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| The scorecard strip (DL24a) | A labelled slot until SCORING's per-deal read exists (#2428). The one score read today, `/scoring/scores/:projectId`, is founder, partner and admin only. |
| What's new since you last looked | Part 2's `whats-new`, in words, with its date or "Date not recorded" and its unavailable sources |
| Terms at a glance | The deal: instrument, cap, minimum, carry, fee |
| The round | The deal: committed of target, with "Other investors' names and amounts are not shown." |
| Your next step | The same one next action as the track |
| The deck | A labelled slot. A deck opens through its share link today (D1150). |
| About this deal | The deal fields the old grid showed |

**The syndicates** stay under Overview.

**What's new advances your marker on every read** (D1150). So `dealVisit.js` reads it once per deal per visit and keeps the answer through tab changes and remounts. It is never prefetched and never read again on focus. A failed read advanced nothing, so it is dropped and read afresh.

### 5. Notes and Activity (DL14)

**Notes** reads part 2's store.
- **Who reads:** every member of your firm.
- **Writing a note:** it is written from the form, with an optional tag (Thesis, Risk, Question, Decision) and checked before it is sent.
- **Who edits:** only a note's author edits or deletes it. Each opens an in-page dialog saying what changes.
- **No notes yet:** the tab says so (DL18c).
- **Eadwyn's draft summary:** AUTOFILL's proposal (#2431). No deal-summary read exists on the Worker, so it is a slot saying the draft arrives as a proposal and nothing lands until you save it.

**Activity, a Worker change.** `GET /api/deals/:id/activity` used to name every investor and their amount to anyone who could open the deal. It now follows the commitments read:

| Reader | What they see |
|---|---|
| An admin, and the deal's founder | Every investor, by name and amount |
| Anyone else | Their own rows as "You …"; another investor's commitment as "An investor committed", with no name or amount |

**Other investors' invitations** are never shown to anyone outside the first row. Each row carries `mine`, for the page's All/Mine filter.

**Where Activity shows.** The founder and staff read the same list on their Activity tab.

### 6. The states (DL18–DL22)

| State | What is drawn |
|---|---|
| DL19 loading | The tab row draws at once; the key strip's tiles read |
| DL20b, the deal read failed | "This deal could not be read. That is not the same as it not existing." with Try again |
| A 404 | "This deal is not here." |
| DL22a, a 403 | "You're not on this deal." Nothing private is read. |
| DL22b, the free plan | Part 1's card (D1149), unchanged. A plan-limited deal makes no stage, notes, what's-new or commitments read. |
| DL22c, an Advisor | "Deal pages are for investors." |
| DL22d, an Explorer | "Deal pages open once you set up an investor profile." |

**Advisors and Explorers.** `App.jsx` now admits these two roles to the two deal routes, so they reach these words instead of being bounced. The page reads nothing for them.

**Dark (DL23)** comes from `dark:` counterparts on every class. The frame uses the axal tokens and no hex value.

**No browser pop-up anywhere on the page.** The Documents tab's "Download Data Room" button, which called `alert()` on failure, is gone: its export has been retired (410) since #2647.
