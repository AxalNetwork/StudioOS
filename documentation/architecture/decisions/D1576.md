## D1576 — Entrances and transitions: one order for Present and the PowerPoint, and reduced motion respected (#3823)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-03 by S16 for #3823, part of #3716 (deck builder v2, Phase E, task E3).
- **Migration:** none. **Charge:** none.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1533 (B1, the area model);
  - D1574 (E1, the structured PowerPoint writer);
  - D1604 (S1's editor chrome: BlockTools, ⋯, Present).

### 1. Scope

No board in the final canvas draws animation. S1 kept the issue in scope with no new board (#3823, 2026-11-02). The controls sit on boards that already exist:
- **The entrance** is in pd3's BlockTools. It shows only while one area is selected.
- **The transition** is under ⋯ › This card (pd13). It is never a toolbar button.
- **Playback** is in pd8's Present.

### 2. The model (`services/decks/motion.ts`)

- **An area's entrance:** `block.entrance = { effect, order? }`.
  - The effect is `fade`, `rise`, `zoom` or `wipe`.
  - `order` runs from 1 to 24 and puts an area earlier or later.
- **A card's transition:** `card.transition`, one of `fade`, `push` or `wipe`. When it is absent there is none.
- **Kept only when set,** so a card saved before E3 hashes as it did.
- **A value outside the list:** a save refuses it (`motionProblems`, in `modelProblems`), and a read drops it.

### 3. One order

`entranceSequence(card)` gives the order:
- areas with an `order` first, by that number;
- then the rest in reading order, top to bottom and then start to end.

The browser mirrors it in `motionModel.entranceSequence`, and `motion_d1576` holds the two equal.

### 4. Present (`PitchPresent.jsx`)

- **→** reveals the next area in the order. Once all are shown, it moves to the next card.
- **←** hides the last area revealed. With none revealed, it goes back to the previous card with all of its areas shown.
- **Home, End and a remote's card number** land on a card with nothing revealed yet.
- **Not yet revealed:** an area keeps its place on the grid but is not drawn (`visibility: hidden`, `aria-hidden`).
- **Timing:** each entrance plays once, on the step that reveals it, over `MOTION_MS` (500 ms). The card's transition plays as the card comes up.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion`:** the steps stay the same, but each area appears at once with no movement, and no transition plays.
- **The NEXT preview, the phone viewer and Rehearse** show each card whole.

### 5. The PowerPoint (`pptxEditable.ts`)

- **One click per entrance.** Each card's entrances are written as PowerPoint's main sequence (`p:timing`), one click each, in `entranceSequence` order.
- **The target.** Each click effect targets the area's own shape, which is the first shape the writer drew for it.
- **PowerPoint's own presets:**
  - Fade is 10;
  - Wipe is 22;
  - Ascend (`rise`) is 42;
  - Zoom is 53.

  Each sets the shape visible, then draws it in over `MOTION_MS`.
- **The transition** is `p:transition`: `<p:fade/>`, `<p:push dir="u"/>` or `<p:wipe dir="r"/>`.
- **An area with no shape** is skipped, both here and in Present. An example is an empty text block.
- **Google Slides** imports the same file. Slides keeps the transitions and reads the click effects as far as Slides supports them.
