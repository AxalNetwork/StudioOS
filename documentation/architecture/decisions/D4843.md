### Part 1 — Visibility to OF-05

**The question first.** The zone opens on "Where does our work come from?" under the line "Engagements and won value by the surface that brought them in." It has one primary action, + Add surface.

**Missing attribution is an action.** When engagements name no surface, an amber notice reads "4 engagements have no source recorded. They aren't counted against any surface below." Its Review attribution link goes to the attribution list on the same page, where each engagement's source is set. Review is offered only when there is an active surface to attribute to. Without one, the notice says to add a surface.

**One table**, in OF-05's columns:

| Column | From | When the read has none |
|---|---|---|
| Source | the surface's name; Retired when it is no longer in use | — |
| Kind | the surface's kind, in words | — |
| Engagements | the engagements that name it | 0 is a real zero: attribution is a join |
| Won | the agreed price of those engagements | Not recorded, never $0 |
| Views | none: no impression pipeline exists | Not measured ⓘ |
| Trend | none: no read carries a time series | Not recorded ⓘ |

- **Order:** rows are ranked by engagements, then by name.
- **Caption:** the table's caption counts the book: "14 engagements · 10 attributed". Attributed means engagements that name a surface.
- **Footnote:** "Won = the agreed price of attributed engagements. Conversion isn't shown because views aren't measured."

### Not measured ⓘ

`NotMeasured` is new in `partner/kit.jsx` and is shared with parts 2 and 3.

- **What it shows:** grey words, and an ⓘ button that discloses the reason on request (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`).
- **The reason:** each Views cell gives the read's own `views_note` when it carries one, or else the product's reason.
- **Rule:** it follows the canvas spec's rule for a figure the product cannot state: "never a zero, never a paragraph on the page".
- **Phone target:** on a phone the ⓘ is a 44px target.

### What left the page

The po3 artboard's composition is superseded by OF-05. These parts went:

  - Leads, Not recorded;

**Kept unchanged:**

- surface editing (now "Manage surfaces");

- the toolbar's chips and Export;

### Phone (OF-09)

- **Rows as cards:** on a phone the table becomes one labelled card per surface: name, kind, Engagements, Won and Views.
- **Order and controls:** the notice and the primary action come first. Every control is at least 44px.

### Not built here

- **Trend.** It needs engagements counted over time, and no read carries a time series (the canvas's own backend note). Each row reads Not recorded ⓘ.
- **Views and conversion.** There is no impression pipeline, so neither is ever computed.
