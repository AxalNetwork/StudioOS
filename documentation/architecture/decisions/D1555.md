## D1555 — Consistency rules across the deck's cards (deck v2 C7)

- **Status:** built by S24, 2026-11-01 (#3802, deck v2 C7, part of #3716). Claimed at the owner's request after S21 released it.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `0b3f116d9c`.
- **Scope:** S1's final canvas revision on #3802 (2026-11-01). The Wording half of "Check claims" stays with #3808 C13.
- **Migration:** none. Nothing is stored.

### Why

D1264 checks each figure against the venture's pinned evidence. It never compares the deck with itself: a deck could say $1.2M ARR on one card and $1.5M on another, or ask for $2M and spend $1.8M, and nothing would notice. pd25's "Consistency · free" is the free half of Check claims, and the automated run (AUTO-5) needs the same rules as code checks on every pass.

### What was decided

1. **Five rules, one pure function.** `deckConsistency(slides)` (`services/deckConsistency.ts`, re-exported from `services/deckCheck.ts`) returns `{ state: 'ok' | 'unreadable', findings: [{ key, rule, message, cards }] }`:

   | Rule | Fails when |
   |---|---|
   | `one_value` | the deck states one metric with values that differ by more than D1264's 2% |
   | `use_of_funds` | the use of funds (a stated total, or the allocation lines of the one use-of-funds card) does not match the raise; or the allocation's shares do not add up to 100% (±1 point) |
   | `runway` | runway is not cash ÷ monthly burn (±1 month, or ±10% of a long runway) |
   | `round` | post-money is not pre-money plus the raise; or the dilution is not raise ÷ post-money (±1 point) |
   | `forecast` | the forecast's year 1 does not match the current ARR |

   - **Inputs:** it takes slides and nothing else. No database, no model, no charge.
   - **Unreadable:** slides that are not a list are `unreadable`, never "consistent".
2. **Where a figure comes from.**
   - **A bound metric block** is read as its slot (`grow.arr`, `raise.round_target`, `raise.pre_money`, `raise.post_money`, `raise.dilution_pct`, `raise.projection_revenue_12m` as year 1, …). A fund-section slot is a share.
   - **Any other text** is read by fixed patterns that need the figure's words beside the number: D1264's `parseStatedFigures`, plus cash, pre-money, post-money, a plain valuation, dilution, year 1 and use of funds. A bare number is never a figure.
   - **What is never read:** speaker notes, image fields and bindings.
   - **Forward-looking lines:** a line that speaks of the future ("projected", "forecast", "by 2027", "target", "will") states no current figure. It can still state year 1, so a forecast never contradicts today's ARR.
3. **One report per contradiction.**
   - **Duplicates:** a metric the deck states twice is reported once, by `one_value`, and the rules that need it skip it. A wrong pair is not counted twice.
   - **Valuations:** a plain "valuation" (neither pre- nor post-money) passes if either reading agrees.
4. **Served in the check's own reply, not a new route.** `GET` and `POST /api/decks/:id/check` (D900) carry `consistency` for this version's own slides.
   - **The founder only:** like the claims' figures, the findings quote the deck's figures, so they are the founder's. A reader who is not the founder gets `consistency: null` (D900's admin reads states with no figure).
   - **Free:** no model and no charge, so it answers with AI off (pd9e).
   - **No new client module:** `deckCheckApi.read` already carries it. `deckV2Adapter.consistency` is swapped (`SWAPPED` now `['grid', 'consistency']`) and answers the read's `consistency`.
5. **The panel** (`CheckPanel.jsx`, reached from ⋯ › Check claims; no new panel, no toolbar button) draws "Consistency · free" under the figures:
   - **Each finding:** its message and its cards ("cards 3 and 5"), with one "Go to card ›" per card. Go to card selects that card and closes the sheet (`PitchEditor` → `CheckSheet` → `onGoToCard`).
   - **Failed:** "Could not be read." with Try again, which runs the check again.
   - **None:** "Every figure is linked and consistent."
   - **A reader who is not the founder** sees no section.
