- **The row** is D944's pool row. The CTE (`POOL_CTE`) and its JSON (`ITEM_JSON`) are now shared by the list and this read, so the drawer can never disagree with the queue. D944's suite passes unchanged against the refactor.
- **Beside it, each block answers alone:**
  - what the founder was told and asked (migration 315);

### 4. The dialogs (SL4)

Every change is an in-page dialog that says what it changes. The internal reason and anything the founder reads are separate fields.

| Dialog | Write | What it says and asks |
|---|---|---|
| A Approve | `POST …/:id/decide` `{status:'approved', reason, confirm_over_places}` | When the founder's workspace opens; "[n] of [places] places after this." Over the places, or when the Worker refuses `over_places`, a checkbox must be ticked; only `true` is sent. |
| B Waitlist | `…/decide` `{status:'waitlisted', reason}` | They stay in the pool; if still undecided at the start, they move to the next cohort. |
| C Decline | `…/decide` `{status:'rejected', reason, applicant_note?, applicant_asks?}` | They can apply again for the next cohort. Reason (never shown), note to the founder, up to three lines to work on. A decision saved without its note says so. |
| D Move | D945's `POST …/:id/move` | Open cohorts only, not its own, with the window; reason; the over-places confirm on refusal; whether the founder was told. |
| E Schedule an interview | `POST …/:id/interview` | Date, time in ET (converted to UTC across DST), length, place or link, note. "Move" on a set interview opens the same dialog; Cancel has its own. |
| F Run below the minimum | `POST …/cycles/:id/force-proceed` | "[n] approved, minimum [m]. Without this, the cohort moves to [next] about 12 hours before it starts." |
| G Admit without an application | D945's `POST …/applications/admit` | Find the founder, choose an open cohort, a reason; the over-places confirm on refusal. |

Refusals are said in the dialog in the page's words (`APPLICATION_REFUSALS`), never as a code.
