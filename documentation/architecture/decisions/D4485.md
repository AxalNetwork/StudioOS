## D4485 — Distribution overview reads (#4485)

Part of #3722, using D4447's existing campaign store and #4483's campaign-reader additions. This foundation does not introduce a second campaign store, a migration, an ad/social metric estimate, a sending action or a revenue write.

The overview service reads venture-scoped contacts whose captured UTM tag matches an unarchived campaign of the same venture in the current UTC month. Malformed or non-string tags do not match. Replies count inbound contact replies of that venture in the same month. Reach and clicks remain null with the exact missing-source reason from the task. Real zero counts remain distinguishable from missing data.

Active campaigns come from the existing `campaigns` reader. Monetary KPI values and budgets are in cents; cost per lead may retain fractional cents until display formatting. Spend consumes #4483's per-channel/month provenance, retaining Mercury over Manual and counting a shared channel/month once. It never falls back to a source-less aggregate from the older reader. Budget is unknown if any active campaign lacks its budget. The owner's answer A selects current-month spend divided by current-month leads for cost per lead. Its source chips describe only that month's spend plus the lead source. Missing monthly spend or zero/unreadable leads yield null, never a mixed-period ratio. Spend vs budget retains the campaign reader's full date windows. Shared channel/month facts are reused rather than introducing a second spend store.

The week accepts a real calendar date or ISO week key; its UTC Monday is inclusive and the following Monday exclusive. Only planned or scheduled content appears, with a channel from the same venture. Sequence steps remain a later task. Failed blocks preserve null and a fixed Unreadable reason while independent blocks remain available. Database exception text is never returned.
