## D4485 — Distribution Overview (#4485)

Part of #3722, using D4447's campaign store, #4483's campaign/spend reader and #4484's shared Distribution shell. No second campaign store, migration, ad/social estimate, sending action or revenue write is introduced.

`GET /api/cycles/grow/distribution/overview?project=&week=` is mounted in the existing Grow router before parameter routes and uses its venture read gate. The client method lives beside campaigns in cycleWrites. Venture writers alone receive update-ask identities; only a founder writer gets the Add to Prospects action. The action reuses the existing POST /api/contacts with customer audience and source `update ask`. Without a recorded email, the row links to Prospects. Contact validation is unchanged.

Leads count this venture's campaign-tagged contacts in the current UTC month. Malformed/non-string tags and other ventures' tags do not match. Replies count this venture's inbound contact replies in that month. Reach and Clicks remain null with the specified missing-source reason. A recorded zero remains distinct from absent data.

Monetary values are cents; CPL can retain fractional cents until formatting. Spend reuses the campaign reader's channel/month provenance, retaining Mercury over Manual and counting a shared channel/month once. The owner's answer A selects current-month spend divided by current-month leads for CPL. Its chips describe that month's spend and the lead source. Missing monthly spend, unreadable leads or zero leads yield null. Spend vs budget retains active campaigns' full date windows. An absent budget stays unknown.

The week accepts a real date or ISO week. UTC Monday is inclusive; the following Monday exclusive. Planned/scheduled content is project-scoped and joined only to that venture's channel. The UI highlights the viewer's real local day. Sequence steps remain the sequence task's work.

Update-ask replies come only from the latest submitted/sent update of the venture, and only customer asks. The read is independently fallible. Private names and email are withheld from nonwriters. Personal connection status is read only for the signed-in user. LinkedIn profile sign-in and manually entered channel plans do not establish a marketing connection. Unsupported LinkedIn ads, X, Meta ads, Google Ads and personal Newsletter/Resend connections say Not offered yet. WhatsApp capture reports the actual personal link state and explicitly does not claim outbound marketing capability. No credentials or underlying connection error text are returned.

OverviewTab renders DS1's six KPI cards, three-column active campaigns, seven-day week and right-side channels/replies. At phone widths KPI cards use two columns, the main/right columns stack, campaigns use one column, and the week scrolls within the page. The existing channels table, draft band, content calendar, Mercury/Notion and tool doors remain below the cards. Update-ask replies move from the legacy full-width card into DS1's side card. Loading uses Skeleton; unreadable blocks offer Try again without hiding independent blocks. Campaign totals distinguish no campaigns from no active campaigns.

S1 approved DS1 in #3722 (https://github.com/AxalNetwork/StudioOS/issues/3722#issuecomment-6638696416). Its detailed #4485 contract supplies the layout; no app sidebar or company switcher is copied. Departures from sample content reflect actual stores: absent reach/clicks, unsupported marketing apps and sequence steps, real dates, real campaign totals and nullable results. No sample figure or connection status is copied.

Validation uses migrated SQLite for project/month/week boundaries, identity and connection isolation, Mercury precedence and independent failures. Rendering/action tests cover null figures, source chips, actual dates, budget states, retries and the existing contact-create payload. Assertions are checked with deliberate mutations and restored passing runs. Desktop (1600px) and phone (390px) browser checks cover light/dark layouts, column counts, page overflow and runtime errors. No production data is read or written.
