## D6222 — An investor's private notes on a portfolio company (#6222)

- **Status:** built. Migration, Worker route, client module and the company workspace's Overview.
- **Builds on:** D1061 §5 (What changed, and the placeholder that waited for a notes store), D4817 part 6 (the company workspace, PF-06), D1058 (reporting duties: who is owed reports), D1057 (who holds a company), D1150 (`deal_notes`).

### What it decides

**A small store, keyed by author and company.** Migration `202611171211_6222_investor_company_notes.sql` adds `investor_company_notes`: `uid`, `author_user_id`, `project_id`, `body` (1 to 10,000 characters after trimming), an optional `source`, and `created_at` and `updated_at`. `source` is NULL for a note typed by hand, or `what_changed` for one saved from Eadwyn's What changed card, with the two reports it compared (`from_report_uid`, `to_report_uid`). The CHECKs hold "both or neither" and "no report uids without a source".

**Only the author reads or writes a note.** `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/portfolio_notes.ts`, mounted at `/api/portfolio-notes`:

| Method and path | What it does |
|---|---|
| `GET /companies/:projectUid` | the caller's own notes on one company, newest first, with `can_write` and `write_refusal` |
| `POST /companies/:projectUid` | a new note; answers with the stored row |
| `PUT /:uid` | edits the body of the caller's own note |
| `DELETE /:uid` | deletes the caller's own note |

Every statement is scoped by `author_user_id = caller`. Another investor's note, or any note to the founder, an admin or an advisor, is the same 404 `note_not_found` as one that does not exist. No response carries a user id or a name. The writes are the author's own, so no admin audit row is written; `deal_notes` writes one to `activity_logs`, and this store does not, because that row would tell staff which companies an investor keeps notes on.

**Which company: the caller holds it, or it owes them reports.** The list and the create first admit the caller to the company, and anyone else gets 404 `not_found` (the reporting read's answer for "no such company"):

- **Owed reports:** a `reporting_duties` row for the company whose `holder_user_id` is the caller. This is exactly the admission of `GET /api/reporting/companies/:projectUid` (`routes/reporting.ts`, `ownDuties`), the read the company page uses for the reports owed to the caller.
- **Holds:** `investorHeldProjectIds` (`routes/_investorProjectScope.ts`) in the caller's active company: a confirmed commitment on a deal of that company, inside the investor's relationships. This is the holder scope `GET /api/positions` uses for the page's "your positions" read.

`investorHeldProjectIds` answers null for an operator ("every company"). Here null admits no one: a holding is never inferred from a role, so an admin who holds nothing gets 404. A GP of record who reaches a company only through a fund they run (`positionScope`'s `gpOnly`) is not admitted either: that is the fund's book, not the person's holding. Edit and delete check the author only, so a note stays the author's to change or remove if the holding later ends.

**A support session reads; it cannot write.** It reads the notes as the investor would (the reporting reads do the same), and each write is 403 `support_session_refused` (`isSupportSession`, `services/fundAdminAccess.ts`). The list says so with `can_write: false` and the sentence, and the page draws the notes read only.

**What changed names its reports.** A note saved from What changed carries the uids of the two reports it compared, the earlier and the last (`whatChangedSource`, `portfolioReportsModel.js`). The Worker checks that both are sent reports of this company with the caller among their investor recipients (`update_recipients`), or refuses 409 `source_not_received`. When fewer than two reports arrived, What changed lists only late periods and the note is saved with no source.

**At most 500 notes per author per company.** The insert holds the cap in its own statement (`too_many_notes`, 409).

### Why not reuse `deal_notes`

`deal_notes` (migration 600, `routes/deal_context.ts`, D1150) is keyed by **deal**, not by company: a portfolio company can have several deals, and a holding reached through reporting duties need not name a deal at all. Its privacy rule is also different. A `deal_notes` row with a `company_id` (the investor's firm) is read by every member of that firm; only a note with no firm is the author's alone. #6222 asks for a note no one but the author ever sees. Its admission also differs: `deal_notes` needs the investor licence and a visible deal (`visibleDeal`), not a holding or a reporting duty. This store copies its shape (uid, author, a 1–10,000 character body, created and updated times, the author-only edit and delete) and its "another author's note is a 404" rule, and drops the firm.

`account_note_saves` (migration 639) is not a notes store. It records who last saved an account's internal admin notes, when, and their length; the notes themselves live in `users.admin_notes`. It is an admin's record about an account and has nothing to share with an investor's notes.

### The page

The company workspace's Overview (`PortfolioCompanyPage.jsx`) reads the notes once (`useCompanyNotes`, `frontend/src/pages/portfolio/CompanyNotes.jsx`, client `frontend/src/lib/api/portfolioNotes.js`).

- **What changed** (`FiguresTab` in `CompanyReports.jsx`) drops the sentence "Saving this as your own note waits for a notes store; nothing here is saved or sent." In its place is **Save as my note**, drawn only while the notes read answered and the caller may write. It saves the card's lines, each with its source and periods, as one note. A failed save hands the text to the Notes box with the sentence for the refusal's code, so nothing typed or shown is lost.
- **Your notes** lists the caller's notes newest first. Each note shows "Written <day>" and, once edited, "edited <day>", and "from What changed" where that is its source. Add, Edit and Delete are on the card; Delete asks in the page first, with no browser pop-up. A failed add or edit keeps the text with the sentence. The read's states are loading, none yet, the list, and Unreadable with a Retry. An unreadable read is never drawn as "no notes", and What changed offers no save into it.
- `NOTE_SENTENCES` keeps one sentence per refusal code the router can give (`NOTE_REFUSALS`). The frontend test reads the router's table and fails on a code with no sentence.
