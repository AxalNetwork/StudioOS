### 2. The canvas settles

1. **Accept and Made are two steps.** Accept tells the asker yes. Made is recorded when the introducer sends the intro from their mailbox. "Not now" is Declined, with an optional note the asker reads.
2. **Two notes on an ask.** The reason, which is required and read by the teammate, is the existing `note`. The context for the person is separate, optional, and only the teammate can forward it.
3. **An outcome is what someone recorded.** Until then the log reads "Not recorded", never "no reply". The canvas's "No reply" is a recorded outcome.
4. **Axal never contacts the person.** No route sends anything to them. The intro goes from the introducer's own mailbox through the Email composer.
5. **Nothing hints at a hidden relationship.** A teammate who did not share is absent from the strip, the log and the counts (#3572's rule, D1356 §3).
