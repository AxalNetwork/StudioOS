## D4689 — Expertise lists page, every row stays reachable, and every figure is the whole list (#4689)

- **Status:** built by S13 on #4689 (Expertise P2, audit of 2026-11-06).
- **Builds on:** D1597 (Visibility), the `/me/thinking` shelf (#3871) and the Services and Proof stores (migrations 203 and 204).
- **Migration:** none.

### The question

Four Expertise reads stopped at a bare `LIMIT`: services and proof at 200, the Thinking shelf and the booking links behind Visibility at 100. Row 201 (or 101) could not be reached. Every figure taken from the rows that came back described the first page while looking like the whole list. Visibility was worse: its links stopped at 100 but its bookings did not, so a booking on link 101 matched no link it had read and was counted as "Booked without a link".

### What was decided

**Each list is a page, and the page says where it sits.**
- `GET /api/advisors/me/services`, `/me/proof`, `/me/thinking` and `/me/visibility` take `?limit=&offset=` (`services/listPage.ts`).
- `limit` is clamped to 1 and the list's old cap (200, 200, 100, 100), which is also the default; `offset` is 0 or more. A request without either answers exactly the first page it always did.
- Each answer carries `page` (Visibility: `links`) as `{ offset, limit, total, next_offset }`. `total` is a `COUNT(*)` of the whole list, read by its own query. `next_offset` is null on the last page.
- **Ordering ends in a unique column** (`id`), so a row can neither repeat nor fall between two pages when timestamps tie.

**Every figure is read over the whole list, never off the page.**
- Services: `counts: { total, live, draft, priced, unpriced }`.
- Proof: `counts: { total, attested, self_stated, awaiting }`. `attested` follows `proofDto`'s rule, a consent given and not withdrawn. `awaiting` is a claim with an ask nobody has answered or withdrawn.
- Thinking: `counts` keeps its shape (`published`, `drafts`, `best_reach`, `total_reach`, `talk_reach`), and is now read by one aggregate over every piece. The most-opened piece is the best reach even when it is on page 2.
- Visibility: `requests: { total, by_status }` is every booking in the window, on any link or none.

**Attribution is not paged.** Visibility matches each booking to its link in SQL (`LEFT JOIN advisor_booking_links … AND l.advisor_id = b.advisor_id`), against every link the advisor owns. A booking on a link outside the page shown counts in `requests` and on that link's own page, and **never** under `no_link`. `no_link` is still only a booking that names no link, or one that is not this advisor's.

**A failed read is never a zero.**
- A count query that fails fails the whole list read (500), as the page read did.
- On Visibility, a failed link read makes `surfaces`, `links` and `requests` all null with `unreadable: true`, while `writing` still answers.
- On the screen, a failed "Show more" keeps the rows already read and says so beside the button.

**Opens are times opened, never people.**
- `articles.views` is incremented on every published read, so one person opening a piece twice counts twice.
- The Thinking zone, the board footnote and Visibility's `OPENS_NOTE` now say "opens" or "times opened". They had said "how many people opened it". Nothing new is measured: no profile view, impression or external readership figure is added.

**The screens.**
- `pages/advisor/expertise/pages.js` is the paged list: `usePagedList` reads the first page, appends `next_offset` on "Show more", and merges rows by key. The line "Showing N of total" is drawn only while rows remain.
- The four zones read through it. Their tiles read `counts`, and Visibility adds "N requests in the last 30 days, across every link".
- The Expertise board's summaries read `counts` or `requests`, and its sections' `total` reads the page facts. An answer with no counts shows no figure rather than one counted off the page.

### Not in scope

- `practice/OpportunitiesZone.jsx` still reads the first page of services to choose one for a proposal, as before.
- Exports from the zones' toolbars export the rows on screen.
