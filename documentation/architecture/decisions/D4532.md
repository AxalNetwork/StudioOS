### 1. One command, on the runtime's own rules

`scripts/check-skill-library.mjs` runs in `npm run test:guards` and exits 1 on any error. Each finding is one line naming the file, the slug, the field or case, the rule, and what to do.

The rules live in `services/agents/skillCatalogue.ts`. Every field rule calls the runtime's own validators, so CI and the seed cannot disagree:
- a definition goes through `readLibrarySkill` and `checkDraft`;
- a case goes through `checkCase`;
- `checkLibrary` runs both;
- `draftHash` gives content identity.

The file adds only the checks a single draft cannot make:
- each definition has its case and each case has its definition;
- the generated module equals the generator's own `render()` of the files. The command compares the two and never writes the module.
- a case's records are reads that its skill lists in `tools`;
- the first taxonomy tag gives a radar area.

**Errors** (CI fails):
- `slug_invalid`, `duplicate_slug`, `case_missing`, `case_orphan`, `case_malformed`;
- every `checkLibrary` code;
- `tool_source_unknown`, `area_unknown`;
- `case_skill_mismatch`, `case_output_mismatch`;
- `record_key_unknown`, `record_key_outside_tools`;
- `generated_stale`, `generated_order`, `generated_without_source`, `source_not_generated`.

**Warnings** are reported for a reviewer to look at and are never refused:
- `front_key_unknown`, `case_key_unknown`, `expect_key_unknown`: a field the importer or the judge ignores, such as today's one `refuse_when`;
- `tool_not_library_form`;
- `roles_broad` (3 roles or more) and `tags_broad` (7 tags or more);
- `duplicate_case_content`.

On the committed library: 182 skills, 182 cases, 0 errors, 13 warnings. The test does not pin these totals.

### 2. Content identity

A case's identity is `<slug>#<first 12 hex of sha256(input, expect)>`. It is derived from what `checkCase` keeps, and it serves until #4588 gives cases an explicit id. A skill's identity is its slug and its `draftHash`. The manifest lists each skill with its hash and its case identities.

**Since D5336 (#5336).** A case whose source gives `case_id` and `revision` (#4588's scenarios) is identified by them, key `<case_id>:r<revision>`; a golden case keeps the identity above. Both come from one helper, `services/agents/skillCaseIdentity.ts`, which also holds #4531's `caseSha`. The source side now hashes the checked case, as this section always said: before, it hashed the raw file, so `customer-interview-summary` (whose file carries `refuse_when`) read as `case_drift` against its own seeded row. A source case counts as present in the store when a live case has its identity and its content. The gate also runs the scenario rules, fails two cases of one identity (`case_identity_duplicate`) and a skill over the case cap (`cases_max`), and reports scenario coverage per skill.

### 3. Reconciliation reports and never repairs

`persistedManifest(env)` reads HQ-scope skills and their live cases from D1 and writes nothing. Company skills are outside it.

`reconcile(source, persisted)` compares the two by identity and hash, never by count. Each slug gets a class and a disposition:

| Class | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| `exact` | none |
| `source_only` | seed (H2's seed adds it as a draft) |
| `persisted_only`, `content_drift`, `case_drift`, `missing_case` | report |
| `duplicate_identity`, `invalid_reference` | fail |

Nothing renames, overwrites or deletes a draft or a case. A drifted draft is someone's edit, and it waits for a person.

The command reconciles only when given `--persisted <file>`: a manifest produced from a local or test D1, or from a separately authorized read-only preflight. Without one, the summary says reconciliation was `not_run`. Ordinary CI holds no production credential.

### 4. What the summary never claims

`--json <path>` writes a machine-readable summary. It always reports `model_evaluation: not_run`. CI runs no model and spends nothing. A case that passes these checks is well-formed, which does not mean it passed.
