## D5029 — One money layer: a `*_cents` column is a count of the currency's own minor units (#5029)

#5029 (slot S29, taken at the owner's request, P0). Revenue read and wrote money as hundredths in every currency: `Number(cents) / 100` to show, `Math.round(amount * 100)` to save a refund, a paid or disputed total and a promo ceiling, and `maximumFractionDigits: 0` that hid the cents of €1,234.56. StudioOS accepts the whole ISO 4217 list, which has currencies with no minor unit (JPY, KRW) and with three (KWD, BHD). A yen refund of 2,500 was sent as 250,000.

### Decision

1. **The layer is `frontend/src/lib/money.js`**, pure and import-free so the Worker imports the same file. It holds the ISO 4217 code list and each currency's exponent (0 for BIF, CLP, DJF, GNF, ISK, JPY, KMF, KRW, PYG, RWF, UGX, VND, VUV, XAF, XOF, XPF; 3 for BHD, IQD, JOD, KWD, LYD, OMR, TND; 2 for the rest), and:
   - `parseMinor(text, code)`: major-unit text to integer minor units, built from the digits, never through a float (`19.99 * 100` is 1998.9999999999998). More decimals than the currency has is refused, not rounded (`10.005` EUR). Thousands commas only in plain 3-digit grouping; `1.234,56` is refused rather than guessed. A value past the largest safe integer is refused.
   - `formatMinor`, `formatMicro` (the token ledger's micro-USD), `toMajorString`, `toMinorSafe`, `exponentOf`, `currencyCode`.
   - A missing or unlisted currency returns `null` or a refusal. It is never read as EUR or USD.
   `services/currencies.ts` now takes its list from this file, so there is one list.
2. **Revenue.** The refund, paid, dispute and ceiling forms and every rendering use it. The number boxes became decimal text boxes (`step="0.01"` was wrong for JPY and KWD). The statement and ceiling payloads take the row's currency; a statement with none is refused. Where a figure has no usable currency it is shown as the stored count of minor units with the currency named, not as euros.
3. **The refund form cannot know the currency**: it is given a Stripe id. It sends `amount_major` and `POST /admin/billing/refund` converts it in the charge's own currency, read from Stripe. `amount` (Stripe minor units, as before) still works; both at once is refused. A charge whose currency could not be read, or is not a listed one, is refused (`currency_unknown`) with nothing refunded. At Stripe's boundary two of its own rules apply: a three-decimal currency is refunded in multiples of ten of its minor unit, and ISK and UGX (no decimals in ISO 4217) are sent as Stripe's two-decimal amounts.
4. **Worker writes.** A statement draw and a ceiling write refuse a currency that is not a listed one (`currency_unknown`, `bad_currency`) instead of defaulting to `EUR`; `totals_by_currency` buckets such a statement as `UNRECORDED`, never EUR; paid, disputed and ceiling figures must be safe integers; a licence fee invoice refuses an unlisted currency at issue.
5. **Adjacent licence-fee surfaces.** The licence fee invoice paper prints at the currency's decimals through `formatMinorMoney` (a yen fee was printed `¥12,000.00`); the licence terms form types and shows a fee in the licence's currency, and a stored fee on a licence with no usable currency is shown as its count, cannot be edited, and is not cleared by an untouched save; the licence page's `money()` follows the currency.

### Not changed, on purpose

- `fundNotices.formatMoney` stays two-decimal: billing-alert emails format Stripe's own amounts with it, where ISK and UGX arrive two-decimal, and an exponent-aware version would regress those. `formatMinorMoney` is the new function for records held in our own minor units. Fund notices (`formatCents`) are dollars by construction.
- `formatEventPrice` (D332) is kept as it was. It still divides by 100 and defaults to USD; event tickets are outside this issue.
- Other pages that divide by 100 (checkout, packs, deal room, spin-out ledger, partner settlements, Mercury spend and others) are not Revenue or licence-fee surfaces and are not migrated. The check that no financial UI introduces a manual conversion covers the Revenue page, the licence page and the licence terms form; each of the others is a follow-up.
- The column names (`*_cents`) are unchanged.

### Verified

`money_5029` (14: EUR, USD, JPY, KRW, KWD, BHD, exact round trips, too many decimals, zero, the safe-integer boundary, every listed currency), `revenue_money_5029` (12), `billing_refund_currency_5029` (5, the conversion at Stripe's boundary), `hq_statements` (4 new), `licence_fee_invoices_d802` (1 new). Mutation checked: the old `Math.round(x * 100)` back in a payload, in the refund route and in the terms form; the EUR guess back in `money()`, the draw and the totals; the ceiling currency accepted on shape alone; `Number.isInteger` for `isSafeInteger`; the invoice paper on the two-decimal formatter; the invoice currency check removed; each Stripe-boundary rule removed.
