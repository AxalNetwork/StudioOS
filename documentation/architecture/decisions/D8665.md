### What the audit found

- The headings ran into the next paragraph because `ReadingArea` wraps the Markdown in `prose prose-sm`, and neither `package.json` nor `index.css` loads a typography plugin, so `prose` styles nothing.
- The outline stacked titles one word per line because `CourseOutline.jsx` (`lengthsSent`) tests whether the `duration_seconds` key exists; the course read always sends it, even as null, so every row says "Length not recorded" inside an 18rem column.
- The double purple Mark complete at 390 is `LessonPage` drawing `MarkCompleteButton` in the header while `LessonFooter` draws another, fixed below 640px.
- `lessonFooterModel.js` `lessonPositionLine` divides by the course-wide total but names the module ("Lesson 2 of 10 in Module 1").

### What the canvas decides

- Lesson 1 "Give every important claim a traceable basis" sets Outcome, Attempt first, Worked explanation, Feedback and transfer, and Source each with its own glyph, word and spacing; every one stays a real `h2`.
- One Mark complete, after the text, at every width; Previous and Next are named by lesson title.
- Next is navigation only: after Next, lesson 1 is still Not completed and the count is still 0 of 7 required (LRD02, LRD04D).
- The position line carries both denominators: "Lesson 2 of 10 in the course · 2 of 5 in Module 1".
- The desktop outline is 340px wide with no length column, so full titles, the current lesson, optional status and completion stay legible; reading time shows "Not recorded" once rather than on every row.
- On phones the outline is a disclosure that moves focus to the current lesson and returns it on close; the fixed bar is navigation only and the reading keeps bottom padding so the last paragraph and focused controls stay visible.
- Loading, text unavailable, course unavailable, no text yet, not available, save failed, offline and no such lesson follow Platform States (LRD05, Synthetic); dark mode on LRD07 and LRD07B; long-title wrapping on LRD08.
