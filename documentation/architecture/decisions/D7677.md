### What the canvas decides

- Readiness is five separate facts: profile completeness, identity verification, due-diligence review, eligibility for this request and legal agreement. Aggregate readiness is ordered worst first, and each fact keeps its own date (NRD-00, NRD-01).
- Requirement rows are grouped Needs you, With a reviewer, Done and Optional, across 11 row statuses. Requirement names, reasons and validity periods are labelled illustrative (policy v0.3) until the policy owner supplies them.
- Readiness depends on capacity: founder, investor and advisor differ (NRD-02). Switching capacity previews what newly applies, what stops applying (kept, not deleted) and what stays the same, and keeps the draft (NRD-03).
- The profile form shows a title conflict the requester can correct but not approve, and an Eadwyn description draft quotes its cost first (NRD-04). The Trust Center identity check is reused as status and date only; entity verification is out of scope and reads Refused (NRD-05).
- "What Axal will share" stands beside what stays private, and confirming sends nothing (NRD-06). Submit is blocked by named items; the receipt says "Nobody has been contacted" and repeats on a second click (NRD-07). Readiness that expires after submission flags the case for the operator and does not cancel it.
- Reviewers get a queue and a case view with a requester-visible explanation kept apart from the internal note, and evidence opening is logged (NRD-08). Other members, another investor and a support session see only what NRD-09's matrix allows; a decline is seen only by the requester.
- States, tablet 768, phone 390, dark and the frame index with keyboard order and announcements are drawn (NRD-10 to NRD-14).
