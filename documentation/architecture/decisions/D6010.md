## D6010 — Validate market intelligence opens market sizing to every venture founder (#6010)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-14. Interim work under the #4837 addendum (proposal 0): the V2 canvas does not draw this page. Builds to no board; the "Not recorded" wording is V13 / VA13's in `design/incoming/Validate v2 · Boards.dc.html`.
- **No migration.** No new Worker route and no new client method.

### The problem

The Market intelligence card said "Add the total market, with its source" and linked back to `/validate/market-intelligence`, which has no control for it. The control is the sizing tool, `SpinoutLabMarketPage`, and its only address was `/spinout-lab/market`: guarded by `labRoles(['admin'])`, and inside the page refused to anyone whose Lab is not active. A founder outside the Lab had no way to do the step the card named.

### What changed

1. **A Validate door.** `frontend/src/routes/validateMarketSizing.jsx` adds `/validate/market-intelligence/sizing?project=<id>`, behind the same gate as `/validate/:slug` (`labRoles(['admin', 'founder'])`). It renders the sizing page with `from="validate"`. A new route module, not a line in `App.jsx` (D528, #4105); the static path outranks `/validate/:slug/:id`. `/spinout-lab/market` and its Lab gate are unchanged.
2. **The page, from Validate** (`SpinoutLabMarketPage.jsx`):
   - It sizes the venture in the query (`?project=` or `?project_id=`, digits only, `ventureFromSearch`) and only that one. A venture missing from the caller's own project list shows "This venture is not one you can size". It never falls back to the Lab's venture (`pickMarketProject`). A failed project list is the page's error state, not "not yours".
   - It reads no Lab state and skips the "Spin-Out Lab isn't active" gate. There is no Lab week: nothing is locked by one, and the Week 1, Week 2 and Week 3 copy is not drawn. The title is "Market sizing" and its subtitle is the card's caption. The back control returns to Market intelligence for the same venture.
   - `markMilestone` already does nothing for an account outside the Lab, so a save never fails on the Lab's milestone.
3. **Size the market** sits beside "Open the competitor tracker" on the module (`defs/validate.jsx`, `tools['market-intelligence']`, now two links). `CycleModulePage` and `PhoneModulePage` flatten a `tool` that is a list.
4. **The card's next step is the sizing door** (`services/cycleValidate.ts`, `sizingHref`). This covers all four of `marketCard`'s steps: the total, serviceable or obtainable market, tracking competitors, and the positioning map. Each one is done on the sizing page: the figure drawer, the competitor board, and "Generate positioning", which stores the positioning mark.
5. **The stored checks under the table** (`pages/cycles/ValidateMarketReadiness.jsx`). It shows the positioning mark, the assumptions mark and the sources list from `GET /api/cycles/validate/market-readiness`. An absent mark or an empty list reads "Not recorded", never 0. A failed read says it could not be read and claims nothing. A source's link is drawn only for an `http(s)` address. The Notion sources part stays below it.

### The write gate (checked, unchanged)

Every write the sizing page makes already admits a founder of the venture whether or not they are in the Lab, and refuses partners and investors:

- `PUT /api/projects/:id` (the three figures): an admin, or an owner (the `founder` licence or an active Lab member, matching `founder_id`), or an accepted `owner`/`cofounder` member. Investor-only accounts are never editors. Advisors and partners without an owner or co-founder seat are refused.
- `GET`/`PUT /api/projects/:projectId/market-assumptions`: `loadProjectForAssumptions` → `canEditProjectData`, the same rule.
- `PUT /api/cycles/validate/market-marks/:item`, `POST` and `DELETE /api/cycles/validate/market-sources`: `canWrite`, which admits an admin or the venture's own founder (`ownsVenture`) only.
- `GET /api/cycles/validate/market-readiness`: `canReadBoard` (the founder, admins, partners). An investor reaches nothing.

`cloudflare-worker/test/validate_market_sizing_6010.test.ts` checks this on the real schema. A founder outside the Lab saves all five writes for their own venture. A partner seated on the venture as an advisor, an investor and another venture's founder are refused all five with 403, and no row changes.
