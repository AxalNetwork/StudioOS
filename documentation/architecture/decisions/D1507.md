## D1507 — Share scoping for every Grid element (deck v2 A14)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-01 (#3758, deck v2 A14, part of #3716).
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `bdf59aa0df`.
- **Scope:** S1's final canvas revision on #3758 (2026-11-01). Per-investor variants (D4) stay out.
- **Migration:** none.

### Why

D902's include rules judged a card's *fields*: a quote without consent, a bank key, a band. The Grid's *blocks* went out on a link whatever they held. A one-click deck goes straight from Accept to Share, so cap-table totals, team photos, interview quotes and CRM logos it pulled in must stay off every link until the founder includes them or the venture's records allow them.

### 1. The gate of each block

`services/decks/shareInclude.ts` `blockKept(block, include, gates)` judges every block on the share read (`decks.ts` `GET /api/decks/share/:token`) and on the investor's grant read (`deck_grants.ts`).

A block that names its element (`block.element`, an id of `elementModels.ELEMENTS`, the one catalogue of #3754) is judged by `ELEMENT_GATES`. A block that names none is judged by its kind (`BLOCK_KIND_GATES`). An element id the table does not hold is left out.

| Gate | Elements and kinds | Goes out when |
|---|---|---|
| `none` | text, images, icons, shapes, diagrams, timeline, competition, positioning, media; the text, graphic, image, video, audio and link kinds | always |
| `figures` | chart, metric, table, typed table, market size, projections, market and projection charts, use of funds, round terms; the chart, metric and table kinds | `include.figures` |
| `notes` | speaker notes | `include.notes` |
| `team_photos` | team grid, team bios | `include.team_photos` (new, off by default) |
| `cap_table` | cap table | `include.cap_table` (new, off by default); a table then keeps its header and its "Total" rows only, a metric goes as it is, any other shape is left out |
| `consent` | interview quote | its `session_uid` is a session the venture recorded consent for whose interview agreed to be quoted |
| `permission` | logo wall, and any image or graphic bound to a `hubspot.*` source (#3880) | its `logo_key` has a recorded permission |

Whatever the gate, a block bound anywhere (its own binding, a chart series, a table cell) to a bank slot or an internal score never goes out, and one bound to a band needs `include.bands`.

### 2. Consent and permission are the Worker's facts

`shareGatesFor(env, projectId)` reads the quote consents at share time with the same rule as D1556's `quotableLines`: `consent_status = 'given'` with a `consent_at`, `quote_consent = 1`, this venture only. A block's own fields name what to look up (`session_uid`, `logo_key`). They are never a permission by themselves, and they are cut from the block before it is sent.

No store records a logo's permission to show yet (#3781). Until one does, `logo_permissions` is empty, so no logo wall or CRM logo goes out on a link. That is the safe default the canvas's pd9r copy describes ("[Customer name] hasn't given permission to show their logo.").

### 3. What the block keeps

`slideModel.sanitizeBlocks` keeps `element` (a lower-case id), `session_uid` and `logo_key` (letters, digits and `_.:-`, at most 80) on a saved block. Ill-formed values are dropped.

### 4. The share dialog

`frontend/src/pages/raise/pitch/shareModel.js` adds two include rows, "Cap-table totals" and "Team photos", both off in `DEFAULT_INCLUDE`. The existing `ShareDialog.jsx` draws them. `include` on `POST /api/decks/:id/share` and on a grant accepts the two keys. A link made before this reads both as off.

When #3812's per-investor row editor lands (#4003), the same two switches go there.

### 5. Checks

`cloudflare-worker/test/share_scoping_d1507.test.ts`, 12 tests:
- every element and every block kind has a gate, and no gate names a missing element;
- one test per gate (figures, notes, team photos, cap table, consent, permission), and bank, score and band bindings in all three places;
- `shareSlides` runs the gates and cuts the references;
- the slide model keeps well-formed references only;
- `shareGatesFor` against a real SQLite schema.

Mutations: 25 of 25 caught, among them each gate opened, each binding place skipped, the unknown-element default, each consent clause, the reference cut, a default switched on, and gate rows removed.

`pitch_share_d902` (frontend) and `deck_grants_d1587` now expect the two new keys.
