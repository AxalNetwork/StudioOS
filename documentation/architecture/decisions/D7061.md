### What the canvas decides

- My relationships (private to you, not tied to a startup) and Leads from your pages are two lists. Leads carry a scope strip: startup, source pages, the server's total and how many are loaded.
- A signup with no logged reply reads No interaction recorded, not Going cold. Going cold needs a logged reply older than 60 days. Needs attention means an overdue follow-up only.
- Each label is explained by its source. There is no strength score; activity count and date replace Strong, Working and Thin.
- Switching startups while a page is loading drops the late answer for the old startup; list, total, next page, filters and export follow the new one.
- When only one page is loaded, every figure says its unit ("of 200 loaded") and Export asks: all, the rows shown, or the rows loaded.
- Sections fail on their own: leads failed while My relationships works, notes failed while follow-ups work, firm book versus roster. A failed read says Could not be read, never 0.
- Loading, first use, filtered-empty, failed next page, Stale, refresh with rows kept and Try again are drawn.
