## D4152 — Settings, simplified: one sidebar entry and one settings page per scope (#4152)

- **Status:** canvas intake and the frontend integration, by S21, 2026-11-02, on S1's decision in #4152 (the owner's direction). It updates D1497 (the sidebar block) and D1413 (the settings head).
- **Canvases:** the owner's two artifacts for #4152, decoded by `scripts/read-canvas.mjs`:

  - `design/incoming/Settings · Boards.dc.html`, the board grid ST1–ST6, which imports the page by name.
- **Migration:** none. The logo upload is Worker task G1 (#4201).

### 1. What changed

| # | Decision | Built as |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One sidebar entry, "Settings".** | `SettingsScopeBlock` draws one link: a gear, "Settings", and the scope under it. The scope reads "Acme", "Acme › Pilot app", or a standalone project's name (`settingsEntry` in `lib/settingsScope.js`). It opens the scope's settings page and is lit on any of its tabs (`settingsActive`). Collapsed, it is the gear alone, named "Settings · <scope>". D1497's rows (Team, Company settings with General and Legal, Projects) are gone from the sidebar; their pages and `scopeFor`'s entries are unchanged. |
| 2 | **Billing leaves the block.** | The Billing row and its "pending owner" chip are removed. Billing is in the Account menu, after Trust Center, and on a phone's account sheet. |
| 3 | **Portfolio is a place, not a setting.** | `withPortfolioRow` puts `/account/portfolio` in the main navigation right after Studio. An investor already has a "Portfolio" row (their holdings, `/portfolio`), so theirs reads "Companies": two rows never share a name. A shell with no Studio row (the HQ shell) keeps Portfolio at the foot. |
| 4 | **One settings page per scope, with tabs.** | A company shows **Team · General · Legal · Projects** on every tab (`SettingsHead`). There is one header: the mark, the name and a "Settings" eyebrow, with no ID, no internal path and no back-link banner. Team opens the company's Team page (D1497). A project keeps D1497's Project settings (Team · General, no Legal). |
| 5 | **One General form.** | `CompanyGeneralForm` has name, stage, website, LinkedIn, regions (chips) and description, with one "Save changes" that appears when something changed, "Saved · just now", and "Your changes were not saved. Try again." on failure. It sends only what changed through `PATCH /api/company/:uid`, for any company the member belongs to, not only the one active here. The read-only General card and the old page's duplicate header and profile card are no longer drawn. |
| 6 | **No explanations of how the app works.** | Gone: the "legal entity" sentence, the "Coming later" default-currency row and the internal `/c/<uid>` address. A role is shown only where it limits: "Only Owners, Admins and Founders of Acme can edit these settings." |
| 7 | **Logo is an upload.** | The field is hidden until the upload exists: **G1 #4201** (S08, ready). |

### 2. Choices to check with the owner

- **Growth details stay in the one form**, as a fifth section: revenue range, employee count, current products and expansion goals. The canvas draws four sections. Dropping these would take away a field investors' matching reads. Remove the section if the owner wants the canvas exactly.
- **A partner's Firm Settings** (D395) stay on `/account/portfolio/settings`, with a link to their company's settings. Before, they sat above the old page.
- **The "Leave without saving?" prompt** guards a reload or a closed tab (`beforeunload`). In-app navigation does not ask yet, because the app's router has no blocker.
