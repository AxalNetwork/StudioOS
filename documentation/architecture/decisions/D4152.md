### 2. Choices to check with the owner

- **Growth details stay in the one form**, as a fifth section: revenue range, employee count, current products and expansion goals. The canvas draws four sections. Dropping these would take away a field investors' matching reads. Remove the section if the owner wants the canvas exactly.
- **A partner's Firm Settings** (D395) stay on `/account/portfolio/settings`, with a link to their company's settings. Before, they sat above the old page.
- **The "Leave without saving?" prompt** guards a reload or a closed tab (`beforeunload`). In-app navigation does not ask yet, because the app's router has no blocker.

### 4. The logo, built (#4201, 2026-11-03)

G1 is on `main` in two PRs.

- **The store and routes** came in #4213: R2 `COMPANY_LOGOS`, and `POST`, `DELETE` and `GET /api/company/:uid/logo`. The full note is in the route map's Company Settings row.
- **The rest:**
  - **The tile (ST2) on General.** It shows the hint "PNG or SVG, square, up to 2 MB. Drop a file on the tile.", Replace (Upload when there is none), Remove, and Undo. A dropped file is taken too.
  - **Checked before it is held.** A picked file must be a PNG or SVG, not empty, and at most 2 MB. Shape and SVG safety stay the Worker's to judge.
  - **Saved with the form (ST2b, ST2c).** A picked or removed logo is held until the form's one Save, which sends the logo first and then the fields' PATCH. A logo alone counts as one unsaved change and sends no empty PATCH. Discard drops it, and the preview shows what will be saved.
  - **The `logo_change` company event.** Each upload, replace and remove writes one, with `before`/`after` `{ logo: 'stored' | 'outside' | null }`, never a URL or a key. Migration `202611031619_company_logo_change_event.sql` widens the CHECK by rebuilding the table, as 757 did.
- **Pending invitees** still cannot read a stored logo: members only, per S1's answer on #4201.
- **Tests:** `company_logo_4201` (events added) and `company_logo_tile_4201` (new). Mutations, each caught:
  - no event on remove;
  - outside and stored logos not told apart;
  - a URL in the event;
  - the size limit;
  - Remove still showing the logo;
  - a logo not counted as a change;
  - an empty PATCH.
