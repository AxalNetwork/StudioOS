## D1049 — Checkout part 1: the fixes

- **Status:** S20, 2026-10-25 (#2900; part 1 of #2451, from S21's integration D1048 in #2852). No migration.
- **Basis:** D1048's map of today's checkout. Its three fixes are the paywall's `alert()`, the Basic mark on a paying Advisor or Operating Partner, and the webhook event list.

### 1. The paywall's refusal is a line in the paywall

`frontend/src/components/PaywallModal.jsx` used to call `alert()` with the Worker's message when a checkout failed, which could be a raw sentence such as "No active Stripe price for tier=growth interval=month". Now:

- the refusal is an amber line inside the paywall (`role="alert"`, `data-testid="paywall-refusal"`), above the plans;
- it is cleared each time the paywall opens or a checkout is tried.

Every refusal says money did not move: nothing is charged before the member reaches Stripe's own page. The words come from `checkoutRefusal(err)` in `frontend/src/lib/pricingModel.js`, by the error's code, and never show a code or the Worker's message:

| When | The line |
|---|---|
| `catalog_price_missing`, `stripe_not_configured` | Checkout is not open for this plan yet. You were not charged. |
| `investor_only` | This plan is for investor accounts. You were not charged. |
| a timeout, or a 5xx | Checkout could not be reached. You were not charged. Try again. |
| anything else, or an answer with no checkout address | Checkout could not start. You were not charged. Try again. |

An answer with no `url` used to do nothing. It opened no checkout, so it is now a refusal too.

### 2. A paying Advisor or Operating Partner is on Pro

`heldCard` (`pricingModel.js`) used to return `free` for an Advisor on the Advisor tab and for an Operating Partner on its tab, whatever they paid. Their plan lives in `account_subscriptions` (`services/accountPlans.ts`), not on the session, so the pricing page now reads it:

- **Who reads it.** `PricingBody` reads `GET /api/billing/plan/status` (`api.planStatus()`, an existing route) only when `needsPlanStatus(user, role, tab)`: a signed-in Advisor on the Advisor tab, or an Operating Partner on theirs. Nobody else makes the read.
- **How the card is chosen.** `heldCard(user, role, tab, planRead)` gives:
  - `pro` when the read holds a plan in the tab's group (`advisor` or `partner`) with a status of `active`, `trialing` or `past_due`. That is `HOLDS_PLAN`, the Billing panel's own rule for "subscribed".
  - `free` when the read holds none.
  - Nothing, while the read is loading or when it failed. A failed read is not Basic.
- **D1048's sheet.** With the mark right, the sheet can say "You already pay for a plan in this group" to such a member, rather than opening an intent the Worker refuses.
- **The read no longer reports a failure as free.** `readAccountSubscription` used to catch a D1 error and answer `null`, so `/plan/status` reported a paying member as `free`. It now throws. `/plan/status` answers 503 `{ error: 'unreadable' }`, and the page marks no plan. `/plan/status` is the function's only caller.

Founders and Investors are unchanged: they still read their tier from the session.

### 3. `invoice.payment_action_required` is in `REQUIRED_EVENTS`

`cloudflare-worker/src/routes/admin_stripe.ts` adds `invoice.payment_action_required` to `REQUIRED_EVENTS`, the events the Stripe console registers on the webhook endpoint and reports as missing.

Part 4 (D1052, #2903) handles it in its renewal alerts. It is subscribed now so part 4 receives it from its first deploy. Until then the webhook's `default:` ignores it.

#2393's guard (`secret_consoles_d223.test.ts`) required the registered set to equal the handled cases exactly. It now reads as the issue states it: every handled event is required (handled ⊆ required). An event required ahead of its handler is allowed only when it is named in the test's `AHEAD_OF_HANDLER` list, so nothing can be subscribed by accident. Once part 4 adds the `case`, the event is both handled and required, and the entry in `AHEAD_OF_HANDLER` stays harmless until someone removes it.
