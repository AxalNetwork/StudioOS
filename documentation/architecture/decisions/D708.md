## D708 — A registry of each entity's bank accounts, encrypted at rest, and one resolver that gives every notice the payee's own payment instructions or refuses

Issue: #1773. S1 decided every open point on 2026-10-17, under the owner's standing rule.

### Why

- **No store.** Before this, `cloudflare-worker/sql` and `cloudflare-worker/src` held no bank details for any entity: no `account_number`, `routing_number`, `bank_account` or `wire_instructions`, and no mention of Mercury.
- **A capital call carried no payment instructions.** `issueFundCall`'s only notice was an `activity_logs` row with an amount. Four paths created call lines, and none checked that the payee could take the money, so a GP could call Fund I's LPs while Fund I had no account.
- **The owner's word, 2026-10-17.** Mercury, at its partner bank Column N.A., carries the 2% management fee, which the Fund pays to Axal VC GP LLC. It also holds all the money of Axal VC Holdings LLC, Axal VC GP LLC and Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP. A notice that asks someone to wire money must print the payee's own account, or say why it cannot.

### What was decided

1. **A registry keyed on the four Axal entities.** Migration 472's `entity_bank_accounts` keys each row on the `AxalEntityKey` of `services/legalDocFormat.ts`, not on `entities.id`. Each row holds:
   - a label;
   - the bank's name and address, and the beneficiary's name and address;
   - the kind (`checking` or `savings`) and the last four digits;
   - the bank's public identifiers in plain text (ABA, alternate ABA, SWIFT, intermediary SWIFT);
   - its purposes, and its status (`active` or `retired`).
2. **The full number is stored only encrypted**, through `encryptColumn(env, 'entity_bank_accounts', 'account_number_enc', uid, number)`. The uid is minted before the insert, so the ciphertext is bound to its row, and a CHECK holds the `v1.` prefix. It is never plaintext, `cryptoBox` or KV, and never a Worker secret: it follows D579's EIN store.
3. **Payment instructions never change in place.** Triggers refuse three things:
   - a DELETE;
   - an UPDATE of the number, the identifiers, the bank, the beneficiary, the entity, the kind, `uid`, `recorded_by` or `created_at`;
   - any UPDATE of a retired row.

   Only the label and the purposes are edited. To change an account, retire it and add another, which leaves two rows and two audit entries.
4. **Purposes, each with one fixed payee.**
   - `capital_contribution` → `fund`, taking the money kind `capital_contribution`.
   - `gp_commitment` → `fund`, taking `gp_commitment`.
   - `management_fee` → `gp`, taking `management_fee`.
   - `platform_licence_fee` → `holdings`, taking `licence_fee_management_admin` and `licence_fee_fund_admin`.

   A write refuses:
   - a purpose on a savings account (`purpose_on_savings`);
   - a purpose paid to another entity (`purpose_wrong_entity`);
   - a purpose another active account of the same entity carries (`purpose_taken`).

   An account may carry none: Management takes its revenue through Stripe.
5. **Super Admin only, HQ only.**
   - Reads need `requireSuperAdmin`, which also refuses on a branch.
   - Each write and the reveal first refuse an admin without the elevation with 403 `{ code: 'super_admin_required', detail: 'Super admin required' }`, before any TOTP or step-up demand, so a plain Admin is never asked for a code it cannot use. Then they take `requireSuperAdminWriteBar`.
   - The console's check stays inline, in `superAdminWriter` and `requireSuperAdmin`. Since D714's PR 3a, its act table carries rows for these routes (`bank_account.see`, `bank_account.change`, `bank_account.reveal`), and its tests hold each handler to the call it makes.
   - The slug lock is D714's `fund.slug_fund_i` act (PR 3a): `fundPatchAct` names it, and `assertFundAct` refuses it to anyone without the elevation, a fund's own GP of record and every caller on a branch included. The Fund I hold on `POST /:id/lps` runs after its `capital_call.issue` check. Both still run before any write.
6. **A reveal is one number, recorded first.** The reveal writes a row to the append-only `entity_bank_account_reveals` before it decrypts. If that write fails, it answers 503 `reveal_unrecorded` and gives no number. `logAdminAction` then records the uid, the entity and the last four only; it never throws, so it cannot be the gate. The console's reveal takes a reason of 1 to 200 characters, stored as `context_ref`.
7. **Nothing seeds a number.** No migration, script or fixture holds a real account number.
8. **One resolver,** `services/payInstructions.ts`.
   - `payInstructionsFor(env, payee, purpose)` returns the payee's one active checking account that carries the purpose. It gives the masked instructions and the warning, never the number.
   - `revealPayInstructions` writes the reveal row, then decrypts. Only `routes/funds.ts`, for the LP's own line, imports it. The console reveals one account by its uid through `revealAccount`, which writes the same ledger first.
   - The resolver re-checks what the writes block, because a row can be changed by hand. Its refusals are below.
9. **Fund I calls need the Fund's account.** Fund I is the `vc_funds` row with slug `spinout-fund-i` (`fundPayeeOf`). Each of these refuses with the resolver's code while `payInstructionsFor('fund', 'capital_contribution')` refuses, and writes nothing:
   - `POST /api/funds/:id/capital-call` answers 409;
   - `POST /api/funds/:id/lps` with `first_call_cents` answers 409 before the LP is created;
   - the legacy `POST /api/capital/calls` and `POST /api/capital/capitalCall` answer 409;
   - the `capital_call_notice` job writes `capital_call_held` for the issuer and ends without throwing.

   Other funds are not Axal entities: their calls issue as before and never read the registry.
10. **One warning** goes with every set of instructions, masked or full: "Before you send money, call us to confirm these details on a phone number you already hold for Axal, never one given in an email or on this page. Axal never changes its payment instructions by email."
11. **A reference on every capital-call line:** `AXAL-CC-<fund id>-L<limited_partners.id>-<line id>`, computed, never stored.
12. **The fund brief stops naming First Republic.** `spinoutFundModel.js` says "Mercury (Column N.A.) · account not yet opened". S1 drops the suffix once the Fund's account exists.
13. **Fund I's slug is locked.** A `PATCH /api/funds/:id` that moves `spinout-fund-i` onto or off a fund needs the Super Admin's write bar. Anyone without the elevation gets 403 `super_admin_required` first, the fund's own GP and a plain Admin included, and nothing changes.
14. **No canvas, by decision.** The console reuses an existing console's list and form, and Wire details is one block in the call row.
    Restyled to the canvas's board L11 by D717 (#1782, PR 3 step 1).

### Refusal codes

| Code | Status | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `payee_not_entitled` | 409 | The purpose is paid to another entity. |
| `fund_account_missing` | 409 | A Fund purpose, and Fund I has no active checking account for it. |
| `no_active_account` | 409 | Any other payee has no active account for the purpose. |
| `holder_not_payee` | 409 | The account's entity, or its beneficiary name, is not the payee's legal name. |
| `savings_account` | 409 | The match is a savings account. |
| `ambiguous_account` | 409 | Two active accounts carry the purpose. |
| `account_unreadable` | 409 | The ciphertext does not decrypt, for example after a key change. |
| `reveal_unrecorded` | 503 | The reveal row could not be written, so no number is given. |
| `cipher_key_missing` | 503 | `KEK_PII` is missing or short in production; nothing is stored. |
| `instructions_immutable` | 409 | A PATCH named a field other than `label` or `purposes`. |
| `account_retired` | 409 | A change to a retired account. |
| `super_admin_required` | 403 | A write, a reveal or a Fund I slug move by anyone without the elevation. |
| `support_session_refused` | 403 | A support session asked for an LP's wire details. |
| `line_already_paid` | 409 | Wire details for a paid line. |

The write validation codes are `entity_invalid`, `label_invalid`, `bank_name_invalid`, `bank_address_invalid`, `beneficiary_invalid`, `beneficiary_address_invalid`, `beneficiary_not_entity`, `account_kind_invalid`, `account_number_invalid`, `account_number_mismatch`, `routing_number_invalid`, `routing_number_alt_invalid`, `swift_invalid`, `intermediary_swift_invalid`, `purposes_invalid`, `purpose_unknown`, `purpose_on_savings`, `purpose_wrong_entity` and `purpose_taken`. No refusal echoes what was typed.

### What it changed

- **Worker.**
  - New: migration 472, `services/entityBankAccounts.ts`, `services/payInstructions.ts` and `routes/admin_bank_accounts.ts`. The router is mounted at `/api/admin/bank-accounts` beside `/api/admin/stripe`, under the recovery cool-off.
  - `middleware/rateLimit.ts` gains the fail-closed `bank_accounts` bucket: 10 a minute per user, for every non-GET under the prefix and the LP's GET. It exports `BANK_ACCOUNT_WRITES` and `LP_PAY_INSTRUCTIONS`.
  - `issueFundCall` returns `held` and writes nothing. The holds above are in `routes/funds.ts`, `routes/capital.ts` and `services/queueWorker.ts`.
  - The preview gains `payment_instructions`.
  - `/lp-portal` gains `pay_instructions` on each unpaid Fund I line.
  - New route: `GET /api/funds/:id/capital-calls/lines/:lineId/pay-instructions`.
- **Frontend.**
  - `lib/api/bankAccounts.js`.
  - `pages/admin/consoles/BankAccountsConsole.jsx` on the `bank-accounts` tab, after Payments on HQ · Platform. A revealed number shows for 30 seconds, in component state only.
  - Wire details on the LP's My commitment card.
  - The fund brief's banking line.

### What stays not built

- No email or notification carries payment instructions, masked or full. When counsel writes D706's `capital_call_notice` stub, its wire block comes from `payInstructionsFor`, never the template body.
- The platform moves no money and calls no Mercury API. #1775 adds a read-only client.
- #1774 adds `AXAL-MF-…` references, and #1775 matches bank credits by amount and date window, never by reference.
