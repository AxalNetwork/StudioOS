### 1. The type scale

- **The baseline.** Every line sits on the 8 px baseline. `lineHeight(size, leading)` rounds a line up to the next multiple of 8, the Grid's `BASELINE` (`structures.ts`), and `textCapacity` counts lines at that height. Sizes, weights and the 22 px floor are unchanged.
- **The kicker.** The scale gains `kicker`: 24 px, weight 600, tracked 0.08. It is the short line above a cover title. `caption` stays.
- **`TYPE_STEPS`.** Each role's sizes, largest first, all within the scale and never under 22 px. Heading, for example, steps 64, 56, 48.

### 2. Step-down fit, balanced wrap, no one-word title lines

**`fitText(range, role, text)`.** It tries the role's own size, then each smaller step:
- At each size it wraps the text balanced (`wrapBalanced`): the same number of lines as a greedy wrap, at the narrowest measure that keeps them, so the lines come out even.
- A heading or display line never ends on one word when the line above can give it a second.
- The text fits at the first size where it sits in its range and stays under its density cap (`DENSITY_MAX`, unchanged).

**`fitProblems`.** It now reports an overflow only when even the smallest step overflows, and its message names that size. A title line of one word that the balance could not mend is reported as `single_word_line`.

The renderers do not read the type scale today. Fit governs what generation accepts and what the fit report and the rubric count. The editor's drawing at the stepped size is the visual-regression suite's to check (#3762).

### 4. The chart rules table (dk-auto AUTO-3)

`CHART_RULES` and `chartRuleFor` in `visuals.ts`. Code reads a figure's shape; a model never picks. Each type is one of `CHART_TYPES`.

| Shape | Chart |
|---|---|
| a time series of 4 points or more | `line` |
| a time series of 2 or 3 points | `columns` |
| parts of a whole | `percent_bar` (a stacked bar) |
| categories compared | `ranked_bars` (horizontal bars) |
| TAM, SAM and SOM | `nested_bars`, drawn as circles when #3787 lands |
| one value | `metric_tiles` (a big number) |
| funnel stages | `funnel` |

**Projections.** A projection keeps its type, is drawn dashed and is labelled "Projected".

**`chartTypeFor`.** It reads the table for a bound series. Its answers are unchanged: a weekly count is still columns.

### 5. Art on any image area

`imagePlan` now proposes art for any empty image block, whatever the card, and names those blocks (`areas`). A words-only card in `ART_CARDS` can still take one. It remains a proposal: nothing is generated until the founder asks.

### 6. Words written to their areas (dk-auto gap b), and fix-or-drop

- **The budget.** `wordBudget` is what a card's heading and sentence can hold. It is read from the areas its layout gives them, at the smallest step of the scale, capped at 120 and 400 characters.
- **The build run's message** gives each card `headline_max_chars` and `body_max_chars` and asks the words to keep within them. The quote reads the same message.
- **Fix-or-drop** (`fixOrDrop`). What still does not fit is fixed or its block dropped, and never moved to the notes:
  - words are shortened at a sentence, then at a clause (`shortenToFit`);
  - a heading that cannot be shortened becomes the card's title;
  - a sentence that cannot be shortened is dropped;
  - an unreadable chart or diagram, or a figure too long for its tile, is dropped. The card's bound figures stay in its fields.
- **What `/generate` still refuses.** `cards_unsound` 500 now comes only from the slide model's own checks. A fit problem is always fixed or dropped first.
- **Departure.** "Shorten and retry inside the same quote" is done by code, not by a second model run. A second run would charge again, outside the quote.

### 7. The rubric

`services/decks/rubric.ts`, `deckRubric`. It scores out of 100 and names every deduction:

| Check | Deduction |
|---|---|
| fit | −15 per block that overflows even at its smallest step |
| one-word title lines | −4 each |
| a card without a heading | −8 each |
| a chart type the deck cannot draw | −10 each |
| fewer distinct layouts than a third of the cards | −10 |
| gaps | −2 per Not recorded line, at most −20 |

`placeholders` counts what the one-click bar wants at zero.

**The gate is 70** on the three sample ventures: the full, sparse and conflicting fixtures in `test/fixtures/pitchStoryFixtures.ts`. All three pass with no fit problem left, and a full venture's deck has no placeholder.
