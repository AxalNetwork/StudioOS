## D6104 — Worked-out KPIs: ARR and burn multiple (#6104)

**Decided by the owner, 2026-11-15.**
- Build ARR and burn multiple now.
- CAC payback waits until acquisition spend per period is stored.
- Lifetime value is not built.

Both are derived KPIs. They refuse a hand entry, name their inputs, and read
Not recorded, saying which input is missing, rather than show a value from
fewer inputs than the formula needs.

**Formulas.**
- **ARR = 12 × MRR.** It uses the MRR row as Revenue and traction reads it:
  Stripe from the last 35 days, else the latest hand entry. It keeps MRR's
  source and date, and its 30-day change is 12 × MRR's. With no MRR it reads
  "Not recorded: needs MRR". A measured zero MRR gives a zero ARR.
- **Burn multiple = net burn ÷ net new ARR** for the revenue ledger's latest
  month.
  - Net new ARR = 12 × (new + expansion + unsplit − churned MRR), from that
    month's ledger movements (D664). Unsplit counts, because it is growth the
    ledger could not split between new and expansion.
  - It reads Not recorded when:
    - the ledger has fewer than two billings, or a gap;
    - no burn is recorded for that same month (burn from another month is
      never paired);
    - burn is zero or negative;
    - net new ARR is zero or negative.
  - Burn is US dollars (`project_metrics`); the ledger is cents and USD only.
    Rounded to two decimals and shown with "×".

**Where they appear.**
- **The KPI table and record** (`services/growKpis.ts` `kpiRows`): two new
  rows after Runway. Their record's history is empty: neither has a stored
  series.
- **Form options** (`services/records/grow.ts`): both are drawn disabled with
  why ("worked out"), like Runway. `POST /api/grow/kpis` refuses them.
- **The report builder** (`services/reportFigures.ts`):
  - ARR falls back to 12 × the period's MRR, marked computed, when no ARR is
    stored in the period.
  - A new `burn_multiple` figure (unit `multiple`) uses the period's net burn
    and the ledger's net new ARR in that burn's month.
  - A burn from Mercury makes it team only, as Runway already is.
- **The frontend** formats ARR as dollars and burn multiple as "×"
  (`adapters/grow.js`, `reporting/builderModel.js`).

- `report_figures_d1059.test.ts` tests the ARR fallback, the burn multiple

- `grow_writes_d691.test.ts` tests both hand-entry refusals.
