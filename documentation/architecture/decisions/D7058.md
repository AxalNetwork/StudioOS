### What the canvas decides

- Add person opens one sheet from two places: My relationships and step 2 of the first-run overview.
- The form says first that saving adds the person to your own contacts and that nobody is contacted. Name is required; organization, title, email and LinkedIn profile are optional.
- Fields are checked in the page, and a server field error lands on its field. Saving locks the form once; a failed save keeps every entry; a support session is refused.
- A save opens the Person Sheet on the named person, and the list keeps its search and filters.
- A matching LinkedIn profile or email opens the duplicate review: what was just added ("Added by you"), the existing record, why they matched, and Merge into existing, Keep both or Dismiss.
- A name alone never matches: two people with the same display name open no review.
- Add person, Import LinkedIn Connections.csv, Invite to Axal and an investor's tie request are four separate journeys.
