### 1. The decision

Deals, one deal and Referrals stop reading D923's capped lists and fan-out, and read the Worker's own lists and records:
- **The Deals list** is D926's `GET /api/admin/partners/deals`, with its filters, sort, pages and exact `total`.
- **One deal** is D928's `GET …/deals/:id`.
- **The Terminate dialog** states D928's preview.
- **Referrals** is D928's `GET …/redemptions`.

So these three surfaces carry no Guard L ("within the newest 200") and no Guard F ("1 of 2 deals did not answer"). The Overview, the tab counts and Needs you keep D923's reads until part 4's overview read.

### 2. As built

The files are under `frontend/src/pages/admin/partners/`. The derivations live in a new pure module, `dealsReferralsModel.js`, and the reads in `useDealsReferrals.js`. Each read goes through the desk's `useRead`, so each block draws its own state, and a failure costs that block only.

1. **C5 "Terms ending"** (Deals, above the list).
   - **The read:** the active deals, soonest end first (`status=active&sort=ends_soonest&limit=100`).
   - **The chart:** one bar per deal whose stored end falls within −20…+120 days of today, both edges included. Each bar runs from the stored start to the stored end, both parsed as UTC. A start before the window is clipped to its edge.
   - **Today and tones:** a Today line; amber at 30 days or less, violet after.
   - **Past the end:** an active deal past its end reads "Term ended, not yet closed", never negative days.
   - **The count:** at most twelve bars, soonest end first, under "Showing [n] of [m] active deals", where `m` is the Worker's exact total.
   - **What it leaves out, said:** a deal with no end date, and a first page that stops inside the window.
   - **Chart or table:** "View as table" lists each deal, its end date and days left.
   - **The part:** C5 had no chart part, so `ui/Charts.jsx` gains `TermBars` (`type: 'terms'`) under D915's rule. It uses the one palette and draws a Today line.
2. **The Deals list** on D926's parameters:
   - search; the six state chips (none is `status=all`, every state); type; ends within; has redemptions; the four sorts in D926's words (`ends_soonest`, `newest`, `most_redemptions`, `partner`);
   - 25 a page with `offset`, and the pager's exact "26–50 of 60";
   - the search reaches the Worker after 250 ms without typing;
   - the partner before signing comes with the row (D926 §3);
   - Due diligence still comes from R9, and the lapsed cross-check from R1;
   - a failed list says so with Try again, and never "No partner deals yet".
3. **One deal**, from D928's read, so a deal past any list's cap opens.
   - **Absent or failed:** a 404 reads "There is no deal with this address." A failure reads "This deal could not be read", with Try again inside the dialog, and a cool-off reads as paused. An address that is no deal id makes no read.
   - **Terms:** from the stored offer. An unreadable one says so.
   - **Redemptions:** D928's uncapped `redemptions_total`.
   - **Invitation:** D928's own join, so the name and address show whatever R1 read. One that is no longer stored reads "Not recorded".
   - **Agreement:** from D928's envelope block, with the signing link's expiry when it is out.
   - **Due diligence:**
     - the case D928 matched (Open, In review, Completed or Archived) and its KYB section, in its stored words (Waiting, Assigned, In review, Completed, Blocked);
     - "Cleared" waits for #2385's rule, so a completed section reads "Completed";
     - two candidate cases are both named, and neither is picked;
     - before activation it reads "Opened when the deal is activated".
   - **Obligations:** each one's own state (Satisfied, Outstanding in amber, Waived, Expired), with "Not required" or its expiry. A key never seeded reads "Not recorded · Never opened for this member", never "Satisfied". Before signing, none.
   - **History:** each event and its day. A termination says by whom and why; an activation re-run says by whom and its outcome.
   - **"Signed, deal not activated":** shown for an awaiting deal whose envelope is completed, or whose activation failed. Its banner opens **Re-run activation** (D925's `POST …/activate`) in a dialog, which says before the write:
     - that it runs the activation again;
     - that it makes the deal active, grants the tiers and issues the code, and that a paid plan is kept;
     - that a failure changes nothing.

     A 409 or 503 refusal, or the result, stays in the dialog.
4. **Terminate (PI8c)** states D928's preview, read as the dialog opens:
   - **The partner:** "The tiers this deal granted [partner] end now", or that they no longer hold it.
   - **The members:** "[n] members lose the grant this deal gave them" (one: "1 member loses …"; none: "No member loses …").
   - **Who keeps theirs:** how many other members who registered keep their plans.
   - **A preview that could not be read:** says so with Try again, and draws no number.
   - **A deal no longer active:** Terminate stays off.
5. **Referrals** on D928's cross-deal read. Each block reads alone:
   - **"Redemptions, all time" and "Last 90 days":** the read's exact `total` (one row asked for).
   - **"Rev-share windows open" and "Closing within 30 days":** every open window (`window_within=365`), read page after page.
   - **C6 "Rev-share windows closing":** from the same set. It is D893's `HBars` on a 365-day axis, one amber or violet fill per bar, "member · partner", soonest first, at most twelve, under "Showing [n] of [m] open windows".
   - **Ended deals and O-WIN:** a window on an ended deal is not counted, D923's rule, until the owner answers O-WIN. C6 says how many it leaves out.
   - **C4, full width:** the Overview's own C4 from part 4's overview read (D927, D929), so the two tabs draw the same twelve weeks.
   - **Every redemption:** the Worker's filters (search, attribution, partner by account, window closing within, from, to, `?deal=`), its three sorts (newest, window closing soonest, partner A–Z), and its pages of 25 with the exact total. Each row names the member's role.
   - **A whole set** is read at 100 a page, up to 20 pages. Past that, each figure drawn from it reads "[n]+" and says it was counted over the first 2,000.
6. **PI11d on a phone:**
   - Deals is cards (name, state, deal type, days left), a sort select, and "Filters · [n]".
   - The filter sheet (`PhoneSheet`) holds four state groups (Active, Awaiting, Offer chosen, Ended; Ended is term over, terminated and voided), the deal types and ends within.
   - "Clear", and "Show [n] deals", where `n` is the Worker's count for what the sheet holds before it is applied.

### 3. Departures, for review

- **Referrals' sorts are the Worker's three.** "Oldest registered" and "Member A–Z" are gone: D928 has no sort for them, and a client-side sort of one page would mislead.
- **The window filter counts open windows on ended deals too.** D928's `window_within` does not read the deal's state. The table says so under the filter, and an ended deal's row reads "Deal ended". The tiles and C6 still count active deals only.
- **The partner filter is by account.** Its options are the partner accounts that hold a deal in the six lists, because D928 filters on `pd.user_id`. A partner before signing has no account to filter on.
- **"Founder Pro" is still never drawn as a plan** (O-TIER): tiers go through `tiersText`.
- **D923's tests change where its behaviour changed:**
  - its Worker stub answers D926's `total` and D928's three reads;
  - the Terminate, record-lookup, cool-off and pre-signing-name tests read the single deal;
  - the Guard F Referrals test and the C2 R1-refresh test, which described the old lookup, are replaced by this part's tests.
- **The trust-score batch (R10) is no longer read.** One deal's obligations come from D928, which never seeds them.
