## D585 — The formation milestone is recorded by the Worker, at payment

**`incorporation_completed` is recorded when the founder's incorporation
order is paid, and only from the server's order status.** That is the owner's
answer A on #1212, "at payment, as today". Recording it graduates the founder:
it issues the certificate and pays D541's graduation bonus. Slot S08, issue
#1212, 2026-10-06, from #1009 ("Milestone timing"; D362). No migration.

### What was true

The milestone could be recorded three ways, and only one of them was tied to
a payment.
- **The Lab Incorporate page** recorded it from the browser
  (`markMilestone`), once `GET /api/legal/incorporate/orders` showed a paid
  order.
- **`POST /api/spinout-lab/milestone`** accepted it from any client call.
- **Eadwyn's answer to the entity question** (`writeRouter.ts`,
  `founder.captable.entity`) recorded it and issued the certificate with no
  order at all.

### What changed

- **`services/labIncorporationMilestone.ts` `syncIncorporationMilestone`.**
  It records the milestone only when the user has an order at or past
  payment (`paid`, `packet_processing`, `packet_ready` or `filed`). It then
  pays the milestone credit, issues the certificate and pays the graduation
  bonus, all through the existing functions.
  - The cohort calendar and a frozen sprint gate it, as they gate
    `POST /milestone`.
  - It never throws, and it returns what happened.
- **Where it runs.**
  - **The payment webhook.** `recordPaidIncorporation` (Checkout) and
    `recordPaidIncorporationFromPaymentIntent` call it the moment an order
    moves from `pending_payment` to `paid`.
  - **`GET /api/spinout-lab/state`.** A paid order not yet recorded is caught
    up here: an order paid before this change, or one paid while week 4 was
    still locked.
  - **`POST /milestone` with `incorporation_completed`.** It now only syncs.
    Without a paid order it answers 409 `incorporation_not_paid`.
  - **Eadwyn's entity answer** now syncs too, so answering the question no
    longer graduates anyone.
- **The Lab Incorporate page** no longer records the milestone. After
  payment it re-reads the Lab state. The note under Pay says payment
  graduates the founder: "the certificate is issued when the payment is
  confirmed, not when the state approves the filing". The open-decision
  sentence is gone.

**Exactly once.** The milestone row is unique per user, the credit grants are
keyed per milestone, and the certificate is issued once. A replayed webhook
finds the order already past `pending_payment` and calls nothing.

**#998's question 1** also asked whether the milestone waits for `filed`. This
answers that half: it does not. Who builds and files the packet is still
#998's question.

### Verified

`cloudflare-worker/test/lab_incorporation_milestone_d585.test.ts` has 6 tests,
on node:sqlite with the baseline's Lab, certificate and ledger tables. They
cover:
- the Checkout and PaymentIntent webhooks recording the milestone, the
  certificate and both credits once, and a replay recording nothing new;
- an unpaid order recording nothing by any path;
- a client call refused without a paid order;
- the state read catching up a paid order;
- another user's order not counting;
- Eadwyn's path and the page no longer recording it.

`lab_milestone_credits_d541`'s two graduation tests now create a paid order
first. 9 of 9 mutations were caught.
