## D889 — An Operating Partner reaches a venture only through its team; an update reaches only the audience its founder chose

- **Status:** ruled by S1 for #2438 (option A, 2026-10-24); built by S07.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `54bec7e8`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Stacks on:**
  - Task #1's membership layer (`services/projectAccess.ts`, migration 119);
  - D654's share sheet (`audience_json`, `update_recipients`, migration 437).
- **Migration:** none.

### 1. The decision

`partner` is the Operating Partner profile: an outside role an admin approves after sign-up, not studio staff. It is now treated like any other user.

- **Venture data.** A partner reaches a venture's data only through an accepted `project_members` row, with that row's role:
  - an `owner` or `cofounder` row can edit;
  - an `advisor` row can read.

  An engagement alone grants nothing.
- **Roster and deletion.** These stay with the venture's founder-owner and admins only.
- **Updates.** A submitted update reaches a reader other than its founder and an admin only when both hold:
  - **the relationship:** an investor can see the company; a partner is an accepted member of the venture;
  - **the grant:** the founder switched that audience on (`audience_json`, an explicit JSON `true`), or the reader is a recorded recipient of that audience (`update_recipients`).

  A missing, malformed or partial grant is no grant.
- **Updates with no audience recorded.** These were made before the share sheet, and they stay with their founder and admins until the founder shares them again.
- **Admins** are unchanged everywhere.

### 2. What changed

**`services/projectAccess.ts`**
- `canAccessProject`: only an admin skips the membership check. A partner falls through to the owner and membership checks like anyone else.
- `isProjectManager`: the founder-owner or an admin.
- `evaluateTeamGate`: only an admin bypasses the stage gate.

**`routes/projects.ts`**

| Route or helper | A partner now |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /` (list) | lists the ventures they own or are an accepted member of, like a founder |
| `PUT /:id` | edits data as an accepted owner or co-founder; never the staff fields (`stage`, `status`, `playbook_week`, admin only) |
| `DELETE /:id` | is refused; only the founder-owner (role `founder`) or an admin deletes |
| `POST /:id/advance-week` | is refused |
| Demo Day deck data | as an accepted owner or co-founder |
| `canEditProjectData` (the assumptions routes) | as an accepted owner or co-founder |
| `GET /:id/members` | views as a member; `can_manage` false |
| `POST /:id/members`, `POST /:id/invitations`, `POST /:id/invitations/:invId/revoke`, `DELETE /:id/members/:userId` | is refused (`requireRosterManager`) |

**`routes/portfolio_updates.ts`**
- The inbox (`GET /`) and the detail (`GET /:uid`) apply §1's relationship and grant to every non-admin reader on the investor side.
- `readerProjectIds` gives the relationship.
- The grant is one bound SQL fragment per audience. The detail checks it with a fully written-out query.
- `GET /:uid/page` already answered recorded recipients only, and is unchanged.
- Authoring (`canShareUpdate`) already excluded partners, and is unchanged.

**`routes/founder_profile.ts`**
- The founder's "preview as partners" reads as a partner with no tie, which now opens nothing.
- Its note now says "Partners cannot open your venture in the app unless you add them to its team." It no longer claims they read every figure.

### 5. Verified

`cloudflare-worker/test/partner_access_d889.test.ts` has 11 tests over real routes and in-memory SQLite. Nine fail on `main` and pass with this change. They cover:
- the predicates, for each partner kind and each control role;
- delete, refused to every partner, an investor and another founder;
- `PUT /:id`, refused to an untied or advisor partner; a co-founder partner edits data but never the staff fields;
- every roster route, refused to every partner;
- the list, and advance-week;
- the update feed: investors' grant against team-only, legacy, malformed, non-boolean and silent audiences; a recorded recipient; an audience other than investor; the relationship; a partner's membership and team grant; drafts.

Seven existing suites encoded the old rule, and now encode D889:
- `grow_writes_d691`, `cycle_records_grow_d691` and `mercury_grow_d840`: an untied partner is refused, and a co-founder partner writes;
- `profile_pages_d702`: the partners preview and its note;
- `projects_company_scope`: the list;
- `investorLifecycle.authz`: the grant is read;
- `investorPortfolioScope`: the shared scope, now in `readerProjectIds`.

19 mutations were caught, each one either putting back a partner bypass or weakening an audience check.
