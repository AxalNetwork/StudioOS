### 2. Instruments (CT3, CT3b)

- **The table is the record's SAFEs and notes,** each with every canvas column: Holder, Amount, Cap, Discount, Basis, MFN, Interest · maturity, Signed, Document, Status and Source.
  - Amounts and caps are in the record's currency, from cents. A discount is from basis points.
  - A missing figure reads "Not recorded", never 0% or $0.
  - A SAFE's interest column is a dash with the tooltip "Notes only".
  - A pre-money SAFE carries "! Check" and the pre-money note.
  - A row with no document reads "! No document".
  - The source is the record's word: Mercury, Carta, Signed, Document or Typed.
- **Status:** an instrument is Converted when a recorded `conversion` row names it (`related_uid`), and Outstanding otherwise. A correction that names it, or a void conversion, converts nothing.
- **The toolbar:** Type, Basis (including "Basis not recorded") and Status filters, with Outstanding the default; four sorts, with Largest first the default; and a search by holder.
- **"Convert at":** two choices, Modelled, and nothing is saved.
  - **A next-round price** needs the price per share and the amount raised. It becomes one round whose pre-money is the price times Today's share count, so the engine's round price is the price typed.
    - Today's share count is the sum of the engine's start: holders, options and the unallocated pool.
    - The amount raised is asked for because the engine needs it for "% after the round". It is never assumed.
  - **A scenario** (the model or a draft variant) sends its own first round, as it holds it, top-up payer included.
- **The modelled columns** are the engine's own numbers (`conversions`): conversion price, a sentence saying which price won, shares and % after the round. Nothing is re-derived.
  - An outstanding instrument the engine did not convert reads its reason, in the Worker's `not_modelled` sentence where there is one.
  - **The chart** draws one bar per instrument, scaled to the largest. An instrument that cannot convert is a dashed hairline marked "Cannot convert", with the line under the chart.
- **The red over-100% notice** comes from part 3's check: "These post-money caps promise [n]% of the company. They cannot all be honoured. Check the terms." It shows before any conversion (from Today's check on the record's instruments), and over the converted table (from the round's check). The table is never rescaled: it shows the engine's numbers.
- **SAFEs sent through StudioOS** are no longer a block of their own. Once recorded, they are record rows linked to their issuance (D1017), and they read their terms from it.

### 3. The instrument drawer (CT4)

- **Address:** `/raise/cap-table/instrument/<uid>`, drawn over Instruments. It is one route in `App.jsx`, reusing `capTableProposal` (the tool's own drawer routes) with the `:instrument` parameter. CycleRoute passes it as `instrument`.
- **Terms as recorded:** each term with its source and the document. The record keeps one source per row, so every term carries that row's source.
- **How it converts:** the model's planned round, run from Today, as part 3's steps in mono:
  - "Cap price = cap ÷ shares = price". The share count is the cap divided by the engine's cap price, so the line reads as the arithmetic it is.
  - "Discount price = round price × (1 − discount) = price".
  - "Lower price wins: … shares … % after the round".
  - A note's accrued interest.
  - With no planned round it says so, and never invents one.
- **MFN:** "yes", "no" or "not recorded". For an MFN instrument, each later SAFE from Today's `mfn` read: Better cap, No change, or "Not recorded" when the later cap is not recorded.
- **At an exit before conversion:** "Cash back [amount], or common at the cap, whichever pays more." For a note it says it is not modelled.

### 4. Option pool (CT5, CT5c)

- **The tiles:** Authorized, Granted, Vested, Exercised, Available.
  - They read the record's pool classes. Exercised is "Not recorded" until an exercise is on record, because no exercise rows is not the same as none exercised.
  - Vested is the plan's total.
  - With no pool on the record, the tiles read the plan's pools and say so (entered on StudioOS, or Carta's).
  - A tile with nothing stored is dashed and reads "Not recorded".
  - A total over a partly recorded column is unknown, never a partial sum.
- **The burn-down by month:** from the record's grants, cancellations and exercises in its pools, over at most the last 24 months.
  - It draws granted and available, plus exercised once any is recorded.
  - The model's target pool is a dotted line, labelled Modelled.
  - Its summary is the chart's accessible label.
- **The grants:** from the venture's plan, each named, priced and dated from the record's grant for the same holder. Each row shows vesting terms, vested shares, exercise, and the document or "No document".
  - The toolbar has a search, a role and a sort.
  - The foot ("Vested [n] adds up the [n] rows") shows only when every row records it.
- **CT5c:** when the plan's grants are Carta's, they are a read-only list with "Edit in Carta" (opening Carta), and no table.
- **409A:** the status in the Worker's words, the FMV, and each trigger as a warning or as clear.
  - "Record a valuation" and "Record an event" are dialogs (`CapTableDialog`). They send the Worker's own fields and replace the simulator's door.
  - An incomplete form names what is missing and sends nothing.
- **Top up the pool** opens a scenario (Draft variants) and records nothing.
