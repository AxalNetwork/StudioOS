### 1. What Story reads, and what it writes

| | Route | Through |
|---|---|---|
| Read | `GET /api/cycles/raise/narrative/:version?project=` (D908) | `narrativeApi.read`, in the new `lib/api/narrative.js` |
| Read | `GET /api/decks/sources?project_id=` (D896) | `useDeckSources`, PITCH's hook |

- **Story no longer reads the version's record.** Check still does.
- **Each part can fail alone.** The sections, the bindings and the registry each say so in their own words when they cannot be read. None is drawn as empty, and the page never shows a value without its record.
- **The Worker logs every write.** It writes one `raise.investor-narrative` row per `PUT` (`narrative_section_saved`), naming the version and the section and never the words, a figure or an investor.

### 2. NAR2, Story

- **The sections rail.** It lists the ten sections in the Worker's order (`storyModel.js` `SECTIONS`; a guard test reads `services/narrative/store.ts` and fails if the two drift). Each section has a dot:
  - `Empty`: no words and no figure;
  - `Stale`: a figure it binds has moved, or is gone;
  - `Written`: otherwise.
- **Keyboard.** Up and Down choose a section. Alt+Up and Alt+Down move the chosen section. A new place is a draft change, saved with Save version as `position`.
- **The writing surface.** One section at a time, with its limit (600, 2000, 4000 or 300 characters, as D908 has them). Under the editor:
  - "Reads as" draws the words with every typed number as a claim;
  - "Figures in this section" draws the bound figures;
  - the audience note is labelled with the version's investor type.
  A section whose words are the deck's says so ("These are the deck's words for v2.").
- **Save version** opens a dialog that says what it changes:
  - one line per section, such as "Problem: new words, new place.";
  - when a deck section is among them, that saving changes this version's deck wording and runs its check again;
  - that other versions and the live deck do not change.

  Confirm sends one `PUT` per changed section, in order. Only what changed is sent, and a cleared field is sent as `null`, never `""`. When one save fails, the dialog says "Save failed. Your text is kept here; nothing after the failed section was saved.", the draft stays, and no later section is sent (NAR17d).
- **Version 0 is the live deck.** It is read only: no editor, no Insert, no Save, and D908's sentence.
- **One-liner, investor type and what the version checks against** stay on the version's own page, which Story links to.

### 3. Bound figures and typed numbers

- **A figure is bound or a claim, never a bare number.**
- **A bound figure** is one of the section's D908 bindings. It is drawn from the registry's item now, with its value, its Source ("From Stripe", "From Validate › Customer research"), its date and its grade, in D896's own wording. Against the registry, a figure is:
  - `normal`: the item's `snapshot_hash` equals the binding's;
  - `stale`: the hash differs, so the record changed since it was inserted. The chip says "Changed since 30 Sep";
  - `missing`: the registry holds no value. The chip says "Not recorded on StudioOS" and where to add it;
  - `unknown`, drawn as missing: the registry or that item could not be read. The chip names no value and no internal key.
- **A typed number** is underlined, with the pill "Founder's figure" (owner question 3, read as A) until #2430's claim check gives it a state. Money, percentages, counts and decimals are claims. A digit inside a word ("B2B", "Q3") is not.
- **Departure from the canvas.** NAR2 draws a bound figure inside the sentence. D908 keeps a section's bindings as an ordered list beside its words, not as a token inside them, because most sections' words are the deck's own rows and a token would print in the deck. So the chips sit under the words they belong to, in their order. Putting a figure inside the sentence needs a placement the deck can also render; that is the owner's call, and outside this part.

### 4. NAR3, Insert a figure

- **What it lists.** The registry's items grouped by area (Validate, Build, Raise, Grow, Team), with a search.
  - A recorded item has Insert.
  - A `not_recorded` item is a dashed row that says "Not recorded on StudioOS · Add it in [module] ›".
  - An `unreadable` item says "Could not be read".
  - Withheld items are not this reader's, and Scoring is a band a deck includes on purpose, so neither is listed.
- **What Insert does.** It adds `{ source_key, record_ref, as_of, grade, snapshot_hash }`, D908's binding, to the section's draft.
- **When Insert is refused.** A figure already in the section, the twenty-figure limit, and an item with no grade are each refused with the reason.
- **No write until Save version.** Inserting changes nothing until then. A section's bindings are sent only when they changed and were readable: a write replaces the whole set, so unreadable bindings are never sent.

### 5. NAR21, the components (`StoryComponents.jsx`)

- `BoundFigure`, in normal, stale and missing.
- `ClaimPill`, with its five states: Backed, Founder's figure, Mismatch, Not backed and Stale.
- `SectionDot`: Empty, Written, Stale and Mismatch.
- `SourceKind`: the five sources (Connector, Document, Recorded, Signed, Typed).
- `GradeRamp`: Measured, Reviewed, Sourced, Stated and Not recorded, using PITCH's `GradeDot`.
- `TypedClaim`.

Each has its dark pair (NAR20). **A component reaches a page only with a read behind it:**
- only "Founder's figure" among the claim pills is drawn on Story;
- the Mismatch dot is not drawn on Story, because no read states a mismatch per section until #2430.
