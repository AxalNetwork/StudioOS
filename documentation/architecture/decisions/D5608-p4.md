## D5608 part 4 — The overhead target, restated: no regression above about 8%, from the hosted runs (#5608)

- **Status:** decided 2026-11-27 by the owner (answer B to Q1 on #5608), recorded by S39. This part changes no code. It replaces done-when item 2 and closes #5608.
- **Builds on:** D5608-p3 §4 (the paired hosted medians and why they cannot isolate a 5% effect), D5608-p2 (the CI wiring), D5608 (the tracer).

### The decision

Done-when item 2 was "measured overhead is 5% wall or less, with medians in D5608". D5608-p3 §4 showed that the hosted runs cannot measure the landed off-thread tracer to within 5%. Even with no tracer in either run, the queue-versus-PR gap moved by 5 to 10 points from causes other than the tracer: queue load, the lag between a PR run and its queue run, the plan change of 2026-11-18, and the candidates ahead in the queue.

The owner chose B: accept the bound the existing runs do establish, and restate the target, rather than dispatch paired traced and untraced runs.

**Restated item 2:** across the paired hosted runs in D5608-p3 §4 (60 merged PRs, 2026-11-24 22:24Z to 2026-11-25 22:53Z), no test family runs more than about 8% slower with the tracer on.

| Family (sum of parts) | Paired median, traced queue vs untraced PR | 95% interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| frontend (2 parts) | +0.75% | −3.0% to +7.8% |
| worker (6 parts) | −5.75% | −9.3% to +2.6% |
| rest | +2.99% | −2.2% to +5.0% |

Every upper bound is under +8%. **Item 2 is met as restated.**

The 5% figure is not dropped as a goal. It is no longer an acceptance gate, because nothing on hand can measure it. The local paired runs of 2026-11-13 (+1.9% frontend, +2.9% Worker, D5608-p2) stay on record as evidence for the earlier synchronous-hook tracer only.

### If the bound is ever in doubt

The paired measurement in D5608-p3 §4 ("The exact gap") still holds as the way to measure the 5% figure: at least three interleaved traced and untraced pairs on one commit, on one runner class or one idle machine. A session that runs it records the result in a later part. Nothing waits on it.
