## D4729 — Paid features follow each licence's own plan (#4729)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-07.
- **Builds on:** D4472 (#4472, merged as #4504), D594 (archetype unlock), D683 (founder plans), D1334 (investor tiers), D4422 (agent plan offer).
- **Migration:** none. Every plan already has a per-member store (below).

### The owner's answer

On 2026-11-06 the owner answered S1's question on [#4472](https://github.com/AxalNetwork/StudioOS/issues/4472#issuecomment-6697053594) with **A**. Its text:

> **A (S1's pick):** each licence's paid features check that licence's own stored plan. A granted licence with no plan of its own stays on that licence's free tier. No member gets a paid feature without that licence's plan.

### The rule

`services/licencePlans.ts` is the rule, in one place. `users.role` is the primary licence and the only source of Admin; `user.licences` is the held set.

- **Founder gates** (`userMeetsTier`, `ensureTier`, `requireTier`, `requireTierForMutations`): Admin passes. A caller who **holds Founder** is checked on the Founder plan only; no other held licence bypasses it. A caller who does not hold Founder keeps today's bypass for `partner`, `investor` and `advisor` primaries.
- **Investor gates** (`userMeetsInvestorTier`, `ensureInvestorTier`, `requireInvestorTier`, `requireInvestorTierExcept`): Admin passes. A caller who **holds Investor** is checked on the Investor plan only. This replaces the `user.role !== 'investor'` pass-through and the Partner/Advisor bypass for that member. A caller who does not hold Investor keeps today's pass-through.
- **Interview cap** (`interviewCapRefusal`): applies to a member who holds Founder (read from `account_licences`), not only to a Founder primary.
- `licencePlanTier(env, user, licence)` is the stored tier of that licence's own plan: Founder from the `subscription_*` columns; Investor from the `investor_*` columns with an expired `trialing` reading Free and a seat reading its primary's plan; Advisor and Partner from `account_subscriptions` where `plan_group` is the licence and the status is live. It reads `'free'` for a held licence with no stored plan and `null` for a licence not held. `licenceMeetsTier` compares it with a required tier.
- The gates are synchronous, so they read the caller's own row through the same pure helpers (`founderPlanTier`, `investorPlanTier`). Two small differences are kept on purpose: an Investor **seat** reads its own row in a gate (the copy made at acceptance; `licencePlanTier` reads the primary's), and a `trialing` row with **no** recorded end keeps its tier in a gate (`billing_grace_bound` pins it) while the archetype unlock requires a recorded end.
- `modelOffers.ts` and `archetypeEntitlement.ts` moved onto these helpers with behaviour unchanged.

A member holding one licence sees no change: every case is pinned by `licence_plans_4729.test.ts`. A licence grant never creates a plan row.

### Advisor and Partner

D594's archetype unlock for Advisor and Partner is that licence's own free-tier right, so it stays. `account_subscriptions` holds **one** row per member (`user_id` is its primary key), so one account cannot store both an Advisor and a Partner plan. That cannot happen while buying a second licence is deferred (`services/planCheckout.ts`, `other_profile`). **Whoever enables second-licence purchase must first make that store per licence.**

### The account read

`GET /api/account/licences` returns `plan: { key, name } | null` on each `held[]` entry, from the plan catalogue: the licence's `:free` entry when it is held with no paid plan, `null` only when no plan can be recorded. A client draws `null` as "Not recorded", never 0.

### Refusals

A refusal names the plan of the licence whose feature was refused (`tierUpsell` or `investorUpsell`), in the existing fixed shape (`error`, `required`, `message`, `plan`, `checkout_path`); none carries exception text.
