## D665 — Record a session from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok: StudioOS as an MCP connector that feeds Validate

**Issue:** #1495 (tracking, S1). **Decided by:** the owner's request and S1, 2026-10-09, under the
standing rule to decide using the recommendations. **Extends:** D649 (Connectors) and D656
(Record a session).

### What was decided

**StudioOS becomes a connector inside the founder's own AI assistant.**
- A founder adds StudioOS to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok, all four at the same URL, `https://axal.vc/api/mcp`.
- In that assistant, the founder can:
  - prepare an interview from their Validate context;
  - run the interview or paste its transcript;
  - ask for a summary.
- The assistant then submits the interview. It lands as an interview session plus proposals for every Validate module: customer research, pain themes, the ICP, hypotheses, demand signals, competitors and market facts.
- **Nothing enters a module until the founder accepts it** on the Interview Session page's review step (D660).

**One server, four assistants.** Each of the four documents a way to add a remote MCP server by URL:
- ChatGPT's developer mode;
- Claude's custom connectors;
- Gemini's custom apps;
- Grok's custom connectors.

Availability differs by plan and country, so each build task re-checks the vendor's own docs. The page's setup steps come from those docs.

### Why

**The founder's assistant is where much of the interview work already happens:** the guide, the notes and the summary.

**Making StudioOS the server, rather than calling four vendors' APIs:**
- **It keeps credentials out of StudioOS.** The founder's assistant signs in with OAuth, and the founder never types a key in.
- **It keeps StudioOS's own model runs on Workers AI,** as D656 decided.

**Making every write a proposal keeps D656's rule:** nothing an interview produces enters a module without the founder's click.

### Architecture
**The server.**
- It runs inside the `studioos` Worker at `/api/mcp`.
- It is stateless Streamable HTTP, through `createMcpHandler` (Cloudflare `agents`), with no Durable Object.
- `wrangler.toml`'s `main` is unchanged.

**OAuth 2.1, through `@cloudflare/workers-oauth-provider`.**
- It covers dynamic client registration, PKCE, and authorization-server and protected-resource metadata.
- **Its grants live in a new `OAUTH_KV` namespace.**
- **The two `/.well-known/oauth-*` paths join `run_worker_first`.** No apex route is added.

**Consent goes through the SPA**, because the SPA signs requests with a bearer token:
- the Worker parks the authorize request for 10 minutes;
- the signed-in founder approves it on `/connect/assistant`, seeing the client's name and its redirect host, and ticking the companies it may use;
- nothing is auto-approved.

**Tools:**
- **Read tools** (the granted companies only, and never an interviewee's name):
  - `list_companies`;
  - `get_validate_context`;
  - `get_interview_guide`, computed by code.
- **Write tools:**
  - `submit_interview`, refused with 409 unless consent is `given`;
  - `propose_placements`, where each quote must be verbatim in the stored transcript;
  - `structure_with_studioos`, D658's own run, against the D640 budget.
- No tool writes a module row, deletes anything, or reads another company.

**Grants:** `/account/connectors` lists each grant with its client, companies and last use. Revoking one takes effect at once.

**Audit and limits:**
- every call writes an `activity_logs` row with no transcript text;
- each grant is rate-limited;
- access tokens last 1 hour, and refresh tokens expire after 30 days unused.

**Flag:** `MCP_SERVER_ENABLED`, off in production until S1's adversarial review of the OAuth flow, the scopes and transcript prompt-injection.

### The rules every task keeps
- **An assistant's output is a proposal labelled with that assistant, never a fact.** Its name is self-declared, so its redirect host is shown with it.
- **Transcript text is data.** A placement's quote is checked against the stored transcript.
- **The Worker checks consent.** It covers the founder's assistant processing the interview.
- **A missing value is "Not recorded", never 0.**
