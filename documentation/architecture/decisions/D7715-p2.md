### What the pass checks (6 tests × 4 combinations = 24, all passing)

1. **Import, written.** The builder's body is chosen through the file chooser. The preview shows 3 countries, 5 of at most 500 rows, every field counted, 5 rows and each country's revision. Import is off while the reason is under 3 characters. One POST is sent, carrying the typed reason, and the catalogue is read once more. The new revisions (14, 9, 5) replace the old ones, and the note says the catalogue was read again at the pinned minute. The same file chosen again says it was already sent, and Import stays off.
2. **Import, 207.** The answer says it was partial and lists EE and IE with their rows and both revisions. It says to rebuild, and the sheet offers no Import button. The same file chosen again cannot be sent: one POST in all.
3. **Import, refused.** A 400 `invalid_import` shows the Worker's sentence, and its row, counted from 1 with its country. Import is off and the reason is kept. After the sheet closes, the catalogue and the check have not been read again, there is no note, and the page's text is unchanged. Only the screen-reader live region now says the refusal. A 502 on the same file is said as no answer: there is no Send again and the page is not re-read until "Read the catalogue again". After that the file is spent.
4. **Record observation, written.** The sheet says "You are changing revision 9 of Denmark, read at 09:40 UTC". One POST is sent with the field, the value 41,250 (read with its grouping), the status, source, reference, as-of date, reason and `expected_revision: 9`. The country is read once more and shows revision 10. The new row is first in the history, focused, and carries "Source B sample · edition 4" and 2026-10-01.
5. **Record observation, refused.** A 409 `profile_conflict` keeps the sheet and the typed values. It shows the Worker's sentence with "It is at revision 10 now", and the page is not re-read. "Reload Denmark" brings revision 10 into the sheet. A 400 `invalid_observation` then puts each problem under its field: `aria-invalid`, the accessible description, and focus on Source, the first field in error. The second POST names revision 10, and no history row is added.
6. **Keyboard.** Enter on each opener opens its sheet with focus inside, and eight Tabs stay inside. Escape, and Enter on Close, give focus back to the opener.

Throughout, axe (WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2, A and AA, every impact, not only serious) finds nothing on the catalogue, on the country, or on each sheet in every state the screenshots show. At 390, neither the page nor the open sheet is wider than the window (`scrollWidth ≤ clientWidth`).

### Defects the pass found and fixed

1. **Scrolling tables a keyboard could not reach** (axe `scrollable-region-focusable`, serious, WCAG 2.1.1). On the country, the Profile inputs and Observation history tables scroll sideways inside their cards at 1280. The same applies to the snapshot and trace tables, and to the sheets' tables at 390. Each scroll box now takes Tab focus, is a named region ("…, scrolls sideways") and shows a focus ring. This covers the four on the country, the sheets' shared `Table` and the catalogue's desktop table.
2. **The primary button's dark hover failed contrast** (axe `color-contrast`, serious, WCAG 1.4.3). In dark mode, `dark:hover:bg-violet-500` held white text at about 4.2:1. The pass caught it with the pointer still on the button after a press: Import, Review, Back to the catalogue and Record observation. The dark hover now darkens to violet-700, as the light hover does to violet-800.
