### What the canvas decides

- Each row names severity, slide, the element by its label and value (MRR [$34.2k], Paying customers [38]) and the source problem, and each Show and Dismiss button has its own accessible name.
- The same problem on several elements groups under one heading per slide and problem, with a row per element. Finding ids do not change, so dismissals keep working. Stale refresh rows map to their tiles.
- Show on slide selects the tile; the inspector re-binds it, keeping the value and adding the source date [20 Nov 2026] in the draft; Back to the check re-checks the draft, shows "Fixed since the last check" and puts focus on the next row.
- Every check says whether it ran on the draft or a saved version. Export v7 checks the saved version, shows no blockers and three named warnings, exports only after they are confirmed, notes how the draft differs and offers "Show in your draft".
- The re-checked draft is saved as v8, and Export v8 shows "Nothing to fix" only when nothing at all is found. The headline rules and what is announced are fixed per result (PTF13).
- Blockers drawn: a title cut off at export size and two overlapping blocks. States: checking, a slide that couldn't be checked, version unreadable with Try again, out of date, read-only and not checked.
- The recipient's tile source line is drawn for v7 today, v7 proposed and v8. The phone (390 × 844) runs six steps; PTF14 fixes keyboard order, headings and live regions.
