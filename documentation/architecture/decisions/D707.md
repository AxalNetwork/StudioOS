## D707 — Each Axal entity's role and payment rail in code: Stripe carries only Management's platform revenue, Mercury every fund and intercompany flow, and the two platform licences come from Holdings

Issue #1772. On 2026-10-17 the owner said what each Axal entity does and which rail carries its money:
- **Stripe**, on Axal VC Management LLC's account, takes payments.
- **Mercury** carries:
  - the 2% management fee, which the Fund pays to Axal VC GP LLC;
  - all the money of Axal VC Holdings LLC, Axal VC GP LLC and Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP.

Before this decision the code recorded none of it:
- Stripe had one account, and no Stripe object said whose revenue it was.
- Fund money was on no rail.
- The roles lived only in prose that was out of date, and said Holdings licenses its IP to Management alone.

This decision writes each role, and each money kind's payer, payee and rail, into the Worker, and makes it refuse any Stripe charge of fund or intercompany money. #1773 (bank accounts), #1774 (management fee) and #1775 (Mercury, read-only) build on the same map.

### Decisions

1. **Roles, exactly as the owner said.** `services/moneyRails.ts`'s `ENTITY_ROLES` holds one role per entity, and builds the LLC sentences from `AXAL_ENTITIES`' names:
   - **Holdings:** "Axal VC Holdings LLC licenses the software IP platform to Axal VC Management LLC for management administration, and to Axal VC GP LLC for fund administration."
   - **Management:** "Axal VC Management LLC operates between the different partners as Managing Partner under the Spin-Out Lab, and manages the other operating partners across the platform."
   - **GP:** "Axal VC GP LLC is the General Partner that manages the Fund's investors, and the founders getting funding through Axal VC Spin-Out Lab Fund I LP."
   - **Fund:** unchanged, `frontend/src/brand/gvpn.ts`'s "Limited partnership pooling LP capital. Managed exclusively by Axal VC GP LLC as general partner."

   `gvpn.ts`, `LEGAL_ENTITIES.md` and `LEGAL_REVIEW.md` repeat the LLC sentences word for word. Whose Stripe account takes payments is a rail fact, kept in the rails map. *Why:* one source lets a test hold the code and the prose to the same words.
2. **One rails map in the Worker.** `MONEY_KINDS` gives each money kind its payer, payee, rail and group:

   | Kind | Payer → payee | Rail | Group |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | `platform_plan` (plans and subscriptions) | customer → management | Stripe | platform |
   | `investor_tier` (investor tiers) | customer → management | Stripe | platform |
   | `partner_service` (partner and operating-partner services) | customer → management | Stripe | platform |
   | `marketplace` (marketplace and perks) | customer → management | Stripe | platform |
   | `capital_contribution` | lp → fund | Mercury | fund |
   | `distribution` | fund → lp | Mercury | fund |
   | `fund_expense` | fund → vendor | Mercury | fund |
   | `management_fee` (2%) | fund → gp | Mercury | fund |
   | `gp_commitment` | gp → fund | Mercury | fund |
   | `licence_fee_management_admin` | management → holdings | Mercury | intercompany |
   | `licence_fee_fund_admin` | gp → holdings | Mercury | intercompany |

   - Stripe carries only Management's platform revenue, and Mercury carries every fund flow. An unknown kind is refused, never guessed.
   - Today's `metadata[kind]` values map to kinds in `MONEY_KIND_FOR_STRIPE_METADATA`:
     - `mi`, `tier` and `plan` → `platform_plan`;
     - `investor` → `investor_tier`;
     - `advisor_session` → `partner_service`;
     - `alacarte`, `intro_credits`, `cart_order`, `incorporation`, `event_ticket` and `expert_booking` → `marketplace`.
   - Catalog prices go through `moneyKindForCatalogPrice`:
     - a recurring price is `investor_tier` when its product's `metadata.investor_tier` is `professional` or `institutional` (as `services/catalog.ts` tests), else `platform_plan`;
     - a one-time price is `marketplace`.
   - The two Connect destination charges (expert bookings, advisor sessions) stay Management's, as merchant of record.

   *Why:* the owner's rails, in one map that #1773 to #1775 also name.
3. **A guard on Stripe.**
   - `stripeCall` (`routes/billing.ts`) takes a `moneyKind`. It refuses, before the key is read or any request leaves the Worker:
     - a `POST` to a create path with no kind (`money_kind_required`);
     - an unknown kind (`money_kind_unknown`);
     - a fund or intercompany kind (`money_kind_not_on_stripe`).
   - The create paths are `/checkout/sessions`, `/subscriptions`, `/subscription_items`, `/subscription_schedules`, `/invoices`, `/invoiceitems`, `/quotes`, `/payment_intents`, `/payment_links` and `/charges`. Each is compared with any query string and trailing slash removed.
   - An accepted kind is stamped as `metadata[money_kind]`, and as `subscription_data[metadata][money_kind]` beside a propagated `subscription_data[metadata][kind]`.
   - On any other call `moneyKind` is optional, and when given it is checked and stamped alike. The two updates that re-stamp `metadata[kind]` pass it, so `money_kind` never disagrees with `kind`.
   - A charge needs a priced product. `POST /api/payments/intent` refuses a body with no `price_id` (`money_kind_required`) before `ensurePaymentsCustomer` can create a Stripe customer. Its raw-amount branch, `MAX_RAW_AMOUNT`, the body's `amount` and `currency`, and `AxalCheckout`'s unused `amount` and `currency` props are gone. No page passed them.
   - A test proves that no fund module imports Stripe code, directly or through another module.

   *Why:* every platform write already passes through `stripeCall`, and a source scan stops a new one going around it.
4. **The two platform licences are catalog stubs.** Migration 471 adds:
   - `platform_ip_licence_management_admin`, "Platform IP Licence Agreement — Management Administration" (Holdings → Management);
   - `platform_ip_licence_fund_admin`, "Platform IP Licence Agreement — Fund Administration" (Holdings → GP).

   Both are in the `gp` layer, the only internal one ("Internal Management (GP Level)"), as body-less stubs.
   - `axalEntityKeyForDoc` routes both to Holdings by name (`HOLDINGS_PARTY_SLUGS_D707`), and the operating-partner agreements to Management (`MANAGEMENT_PARTY_SLUGS_D707`: `partner_msa_v1`, `partner_services`, `partner_equity`, `partner_revshare`, `partner_custom`, `partner_nda_nonsolicit`). Both checks run before the regex fallback, in the Worker and its frontend mirror.
   - `partner_capital` is left out, because a capital partnership is an investor relationship.
   - Counsel writes the bodies.
   - `intercompany_ip_services_v1.md` stays as it is, and nothing issues it: its §3.1 grants Management an exclusive licence, which contradicts decision 1.

   *Why:* the slug is the join key for envelopes and usage counts (084); the body is counsel's.
5. **The management fee is paid to Axal VC GP LLC**, the owner's model. Migration 468's rows do not change. *Why:* the owner places the fee with the GP, and a legal body is counsel's to change.

### `GET /api/admin/money-rails`

`routes/admin_money_rails.ts`, behind `requireAdmin`, is mounted before the `/api/admin` catch-all. It answers:
- `entities`: each entity's key, name and role;
- `kinds`: the `MONEY_KINDS` rows;
- `stripe`: `{ account_holder: 'management', mode }`, where `mode` is `stripeMode`'s.

It answers no key and no account number. There is no frontend method.

### Left for counsel

None of these is changed here, because each body is counsel's:
- **`investment_management_agreement_v1.md`** makes Axal VC Management LLC the "Manager" and has the Fund pay it the fee. The live `mgmt_company` body does the same. Both contradict decision 5, and the IMA stays a stub.
- **`LEGAL_REVIEW.md`'s red flag 2** treated Management as the fund's adviser under Advisers Act §203(l). The GP now receives the fee, so counsel says which entity is the adviser.
- **`intercompany_ip_services_v1.md`'s exclusive grant** to Management leaves no room for the GP's fund-administration licence. The two Platform IP Licence stubs replace it.
- **`capital_partnership`** (`services/partnerDeals.ts`) still goes out as Management's `partner_msa_v1` (`routes/partner_onboarding.ts`).
- **The partner revenue share "of management fees"** (`partnerDeals.ts`) comes out of a fee the GP now receives.

### Unchanged on purpose

- **`requireFundGp`'s Institutional-tier gate** (`services/fundGpAccess.ts`) stays. A third-party GP's subscription is `investor_tier`, and Axal VC GP LLC's own use is the fund-administration licence.
- **The `investors_lps` audience** (`services/catalog.ts`) sells services, never capital.
- **Holdings' ownership of Management and the GP** stays "to confirm" (`LEGAL_REVIEW.md` §F).
- **The webhook and what any purchase fulfils** do not change.

### Stale lines corrected

- `types.ts` said `routes/funds.ts` uses Stripe. It now says who calls Stripe (`stripeCall` and its callers, never fund code), and that `util/paymentMode.ts` allows the dev fallback only outside production with no key.
