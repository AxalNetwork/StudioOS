## D4819 part 12 — Operations: requests and compliance, and billing (L12, L13) (#4819)

Built for #5923, after parts 1 to 3 in `D4819.md`. The part number is the board number (L12, with L13 beside it): several #4819 slices are open at once, and a part named by its board cannot collide with a sibling's part 4. It is frontend only, with no Worker change and no migration. Package and base-licence billing (addendum boards L29 to L32) belongs to #5026, not here.

### Backend check

Each fact was checked against the read it comes from, on main as it stands:

- **Studio requests:** `GET /api/admin/licences/:uid/requests` (`services/licenceLedgerReads.ts`, #4700). It returns **open** requests only, oldest first, with `raised_by_name`, `created_at` and the response band.
- **A decision:** `PATCH /api/admin/escalations/:uid` returns the decided row with `status`, `answered_by_name` and `answered_at`. It is the same write the Support queue makes (`EscalationDecision`), with its required reason.
- **Compliance:** `GET /api/admin/licences/:uid/notices` (D135, D136). It gives each notice's status, `respond_by`, `responded_at`, `reviewed_at` and `froze_at`. An unreadable table arrives as `notices_available: false`.
- **Invoices:** `GET /api/admin/licences/:uid/fee-invoices` (D802). It gives each invoice's state, `void_note` and `pdf_url`; a void invoice has no PDF. The void write (`PATCH …/fee-invoices/:invoiceUid`) refuses a reason under 10 characters and logs `licence_fee_invoice_void` to the admin audit log.

### What the canvas asks that the record cannot give yet

- **"Who decided, and when", for a request decided earlier.** The licence's requests read lists open requests only, so a request decided before the page was opened is not on it. A request decided **on this page** shows who decided it and when, from the row the decision returned. The card says that earlier decisions are on Support. Showing them here needs the read to also return answered and declined rows, which is a Worker change.
- **"Voiding is recorded in History".** Licence History reads `licence_events`, and an invoice void is not one of those events. It is in the admin audit log. The foot says the audit log, not History.
- **Approve.** The escalation board has two decided states, `answered` and `declined`. Approve records `answered` with its reason, and Decline records `declined`. "Approved" is the word this page shows for an `answered` request.

### What was built

- **Operations sub-tabs.** They are now Branch and domain, Requests and compliance, and Billing. The URL keys (`deploy`, `notices`, `fee-invoices`) are unchanged, so links already made still open them.
- **L12, Studio requests** (`LicenceRequestsPanel.jsx`, `layout="section"`):
  - The card holds a table of Request, From, Age, and Decline… / Approve….
  - Age is in days or hours from `created_at`. An unreadable time is Not recorded, never 0.
  - Decline… and Approve… open the Support queue's own decision panel on that outcome (`EscalationDecision`'s new `initialOutcome`). The reason is required.
  - A decided request moves to "Decided here", with "Approved by <name> · <UTC minute>".
  - The heading's state is one of: N waiting; None, only when the read counted none and the branch was looked up; Partly unavailable; or Unavailable with Try again.
  - On this tab the side panel steps aside. Every other tab keeps it, unchanged.
- **L12, Compliance** (`NoticesEditor.jsx`):
  - The heading counts outstanding notices. Outstanding means every status except accepted and withdrawn.
  - Each row carries its state in words, such as "Open · waiting on the administrator", "Overdue · account frozen" or "Answered · waiting on HQ's review". It also shows the addressee, the deadline and the freeze clock, and outstanding rows are outlined. So an outstanding notice is seen without opening it.
  - The notice's text, the answer and HQ's note sit under Open notice.
  - An unreadable store says so with Try again.
  - Issuing, Accept and Reject are unchanged.
- **L13, Billing** (`LicenceFeeInvoices.jsx`):
  - The card is "Annual licence-fee invoices", with columns Invoice (the reference, with its issue date), Licence year, Amount, Due, Received and State.
  - State is Overdue with its days past due, Issued, Paid or Void. A void invoice gives its reason.
  - PDF is offered where the invoice has one.
  - Void… opens a labelled, required reason.
  - The foot says what a void keeps. It also says that an issued invoice keeps its fee and that an amended fee applies to the next invoice issued, since `figures_json` freezes the amount at issue.
  - A failed read says unknown, not none, with Try again.
- **Overview.** The Requests fact now opens Requests and compliance. Billing already opened Billing.
- **Each group fails on its own.** Requests, compliance and invoices are three reads with three states. Try again re-reads only its own store.
