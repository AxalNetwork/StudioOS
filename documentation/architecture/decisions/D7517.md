## D7517 — Expertise in a browser: axe at 390, 768 and 1440, light and dark, and the phone section row (#7517)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-18. Split from #4828 by the owner's answer (A) on 2026-11-18; D4828 left "an axe pass at 390, 768 and 1440" as a follow-up.
- **Builds on:** D4828 (the Expertise states, the phone section row and More), D4819 part 5933 (the fixtures-in-a-browser pattern), D4803 and D4814 (the `text-axal-faint` to `text-axal-muted` swap inside a scanned frame).
- **No migration, no Worker route, no client method, no new dependency.** `@axe-core/playwright` and `@playwright/test` were already in `frontend/package.json`.

### The decision

One spec, `frontend/tests/e2e/expertise_axe_7517.spec.js`, in the pattern of `licences_console_5933.spec.js`: it serves the built SPA (`docs/`) from its own local server with the Workers SPA fallback, signs in a fixture advisor, answers every `/api/*` read with invented fixtures in the Worker's shapes (`advisorDto`, `serviceDto`, `proofDto`, the thinking shelf, `advisorVisibility`), aborts every other origin, closes WebSockets and pins the clock. It proves the pages in a browser, not the Worker's answers.

It covers `/expertise` (the Overview board) and the five zones, Profile, Services, Proof, Thinking and Visibility:

1. **axe, 36 page states.** Each of the six pages at 390, 768 and 1440 wide, in light and dark: no axe violation of any impact under the `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a`, `wcag21aa` and `wcag22aa` rules, no page wider than its window, every Expertise read answered and no write made. The More panel, open, is scanned too.
2. **The phone section row at 390.** Overview · Profile · Services · Proof · More, in that order and nothing else, each at least 44 × 44 px and inside the window, the current zone marked `aria-current="page"`, Thinking and Visibility hidden from the row (light and dark).
3. **More at 390.** Closed by default; opened by keyboard, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` name its panel; Thinking and Visibility are 44 px rows with their routes; Tab reaches the first, Enter opens Thinking, the panel closes and More is named "More: Thinking"; opened again, Thinking is current; Visibility opens from its row.
4. **From 1024 px** there is no More and all six items are pills.

**What is scanned.** Expertise's own column: the block WorkspaceShell draws with the h1, the section row and the body (`div:has(> nav[aria-label="Expertise sections"])`), less its crumb row, plus any open dialog. The app around it is not scanned here, as in the Portfolio, Security and Fund Admin passes. No axe rule is disabled.

**CI.** The `visual` job of `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs it after the Licences console step, on the docs/ it builds and its pinned Chromium, with `--retries=0 --trace off`. Locally: `npm run build` at the root, then `cd frontend && npx --no-install playwright test -c playwright.config.js tests/e2e/expertise_axe_7517.spec.js --trace off`.

### Fixed in Expertise's files

- **Contrast, light.** Every `text-axal-faint` (`#a1a1aa`, 2.3:1 to 2.6:1 on white and on the ground) in `pages/advisor/expertise/` is `text-axal-muted` (`#6b6577`, about 5:1): tile labels and captions, form field labels, the profile preview's terms, list meta lines, the Visibility notes. 53 classes in eight files. The token itself is unchanged.
- **Contrast, dark.** Profile's completeness figure was `text-emerald-700` on the dark card (3.3:1); it takes `dark:text-emerald-300`.
