## D5019 part 2 — The operating licence shows its minimum, and every exclusivity change is held to its audit trail (#5019)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-13 (#5019), after verifying #5113, #5140 and #5142 against main. Part 1 is `D5019.md`.
- **Code:** `routes/admin_licence_exclusivity.ts` (the read), `pages/admin/licences/exclusivityModel.js` and
  `Exclusivity.jsx`.
- **Tests:** `cloudflare-worker/test/licence_exclusivity_5019.test.ts`, `frontend/test/licence_exclusivity_5019.test.mjs`.

### What the verification found

Every acceptance criterion of #5019 had code and a test on main. Two gaps remained against the issue's scope:

1. **The UX section asks for "Annual fee / royalty / minimum" under Operating licence.** The panel showed the fee and
   the royalty only.
2. **"Every exclusivity change is revision-safe and audited."** The test asserted the audit row of a direct set
   (`licence_exclusivity_set`). Nothing asserted the audit of a review opened or resolved, or the `terms_changed`
   licence events. Removing any of those writes left the suite green.

### Decision

- **The minimum is the terms schedule's Minimum Guarantee (§4.4, `licence_terms.minimum_guarantee_cents`).** It is
  the contractual minimum the agreement states. D5017 left the pricing recommendation's minimum royalty without a
  core-term column, recorded on the pricing decision only, so it is not the licence's minimum and is not shown as one.
- The read gains `operating.minimum_guarantee_cents`. It is read in the same call that already reads the schedule's
  "Exclusive territory":
  - `null` when the schedule records none, never 0;
  - `'unreadable'` when the schedule could not be read.
- The panel's Operating licence line reads: "Annual fee … · royalty … · minimum guarantee …". The minimum shows
  Not recorded or Unreadable when it has no figure.
- **The audit is now tested.** The tests assert:
  - each set writes a `terms_changed` licence event with its revision;
  - each review opened writes an audit row, whether a miss opened it or HQ did by hand;
  - each resolution writes an audit row with its action and reason, and a licence event with the state before and
    after.

### Not changed

No new column and no migration. Nothing writes the Minimum Guarantee from this panel: it is edited on the Terms tab, as
before.
