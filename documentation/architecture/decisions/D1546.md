## D1546 — Compliance labels: the forward-looking footnote, proposed round terms, Confidential, and the guarantee-language block (deck v2 B14)

- **Status:** built by S24, 2026-11-01 (#3790, deck v2 B14, part of #3716). Claimed at the owner's request after S21 released it.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `e33a9e15af`.
- **Scope:** S1's final canvas revision on #3790 (PICK, pd6, pd3, pd8b, pd9r).
- **Migration:** none.
- **Owner decision T (compliance defaults) is open.** This is built to the interim rule, footnote on and the wording a placeholder. Every default and every sentence lives in `services/decks/compliance.ts` (the Worker) and `complianceModel.js` (the frontend's one mirror, held equal by a test), so the answer is a one-line change in each.

### Why

A projection could leave the studio without its forward-looking footnote. The HTML file, the .odp file and the editable PowerPoint drew no such line, and the editor's label lived only on charts. Generated words could promise a return. Unapproved round terms were shown as plain figures. Nothing marked a deck Confidential.

### What was decided

1. **One rule** (`services/decks/compliance.ts`, pure).
   - **A card shows a projection** when any binding on it is graded Projected, or names a `raise.projection_*` slot (D1520), or when a chart on it has a point after today. The last is the editor's `projectionOf` rule, now on the Worker too.
     - **Its footnote** is D1520's `PROJECTION_FOOTNOTE`, shown while `compliance_fls` is on.
   - **A card shows proposed round terms** when it binds pre-money, post-money, dilution or the instrument, and counsel has not approved them (the grade is not Reviewed).
     - **Its line** is "Round terms: Proposed, subject to counsel."
   - **Confidential** is on unless the deck turns it off.
   - **`deckCompliance(slides, theme)`** gives `{ confidential, footnotes: [lines per card] }`.
2. **The flags live in the version's theme**, on by default. `sanitizeTheme` keeps `compliance_fls: false` or `confidential: false` only when a flag is turned off, so a deck saved before this decision hashes as it did.
3. **Every format the Worker renders draws it.**
   - **The writers:** the HTML file (`offlineHtml.ts`), the .odp file (`odp.ts`, D1603) and the editable PowerPoint behind Google Slides (`pptxEditable.ts`, D904). Each puts the card's lines above its footer and Confidential beside its page number.
   - **The reads:** `publicDeckOf` adds `compliance` to every reader's read, which covers the share page, the print page and the print-export the PDF and PowerPoint photograph. The export route now reads the row whole (`SELECT *`), so it sees the version's theme.
4. **The reader pages** draw the same lines with `CardCompliance.jsx`:
   - the print page (`StudioPrintCard`, `PitchDeckPrintPage`), from the read's `compliance`;
   - Present, which reads the founder's own deck, through the mirror (`complianceModel.chromeOf`).

   A chart that BlockGrid already labels as a projection keeps BlockGrid's own note (S1's `PROJECTION_NOTE`), so a card never carries the footnote twice. The editor's label is S1's and is not redrawn.
5. **The guarantee-language block.**
   - **What it matches:** `GUARANTEE_PATTERNS` is fixed patterns, never a model:
     - a guaranteed return, profit, exit or growth;
     - returns "are guaranteed";
     - risk-free or no-risk;
     - "can't lose";
     - a sure or certain bet or return;
     - "will certainly return…";
     - "investors will make 5x".
   - **What it does:** `withoutGuarantees` drops each sentence that carries one.
   - **Where it runs:**
     - New deck's outline and build wording (`deck_new.ts`), and the one-card rebuild. A dropped sentence counts in the reply's `dropped`.
     - The card assist (`deck_assist.ts`). Words that only promised a return propose nothing, and the reply is 422 `guarantee_language`, charged for the run.
   - **Separate from "Check wording":** this is the free code block, not the charged check (#3802/#3808).
6. **The editor (pd3's chrome strip):** the Theme sheet has a **Confidential** switch, on by default. It writes `confidential: false` into the theme, held until a version is saved. A theme picked afterwards keeps the setting (`keepSettings`).
7. **Export ownership with #3787:** #3790 owns the footnote on the export side. #3787's chart kinds carry it automatically through a Projected binding or a point past today (comment on #3787).

### Not in this decision

- **Page number and Footer text switches** (pd3). Page numbers are already drawn in every format, and the footer is the plan's (D683: the Axal footer stays on the free plan), so a switch would contradict the plan gate. Left for S1's batch, or an owner call.
- **Logo corner** (pd3, off by default) and **public views**: not touched. Nothing here makes any view public.
- **Counsel's final wording:** owner decision T.
