## D4495 — Distribution's Newsletter tab: the not-connected state first, on Beehiiv (#4495)

- **Status:** built by S14, 2026-11-07 (#4495, part of #3722). No route, client, migration or API call.
- **Owner answer G (2026-11-06, recorded on #3722):** (a) Beehiiv sends a founder's newsletter, through the connector tracked in #2407. Axal-hosted lists and a Mailchimp or Resend connector are not built.
- **Designs:** DS6 is primary; MK6b gives the not-connected card and MK6 the later connected tab.

### 1. What ships now

`frontend/src/pages/cycles/distribution/NewsletterTab.jsx`, mounted by `CycleRoute.jsx` for `tab="newsletter"` in place of Coming soon. While no founder provider exists it shows only:

- **The not-connected card (MK6b):** "Connect Beehiiv to send your newsletter." and "Your subscribers stay in your Beehiiv account.", with **Connect Beehiiv disabled** and the visible reason **"Not offered yet"**. The reason is the button's `aria-describedby`, not a tooltip.
- **The Results card (DS6 right):** Opens, Clicks and Unsubscribes, each **"Not recorded"**, with "Recorded after the first send." Never a 0.

No lists, no editor, no subscribers and no send controls are drawn, so none can be invented. The tab makes **no request**, so it needs no Worker route. It follows the shared phone and tablet layout (`useDistributionLayout`): one column under 1024px, results beside the card on a desktop. Dark classes and a 44px Connect target are on the new component.

### 2. Never Axal's house newsletter

`routes/newsletter.ts` (D810, `/api/newsletter`) is Axal's own newsletter. A founder's newsletter never reads or writes its tables, and the test asserts the tab sends no request at all.

### 3. The later scope, once the Beehiiv connector exists (#2407)

Each item gets its own mounted Worker route before any client method, and its own test:

1. **Lists and opt-in proof** read from Beehiiv: List, Subscribers and the opt-in source per subscriber (double opt-in, form, imported with consent recorded), with a loading skeleton and an empty state.
2. **The editor:** subject and sections (Intro, What shipped, Customer story "with consent", Call to action, + Add a section), with the Ask section draft through the drafting task (kind `newsletter`). A newsletter section is a "safe" proposal for Accept all safe (#4488); sending and scheduling never are. Every issue carries Beehiiv's one-click unsubscribe link.
3. **Send a test and Schedule…**, each through a pressed confirm. Nothing sends or schedules on its own.
4. **Results read from Beehiiv:** Opens, Clicks and Unsubscribes, each "Not recorded" with "Until Beehiiv reports it" until the provider reports them.

Connect Beehiiv is enabled when the connector's own connect flow is on main; until then it stays disabled with "Not offered yet".
