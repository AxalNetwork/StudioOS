### 2. The verified rule, checked every time

`isVerifiedInvestor` applies D653's rule to a user row. Every SQL site writes the same rule out as literal text, never interpolated: `(lower(u.role) = 'investor' AND u.is_active = 1 AND u.kyc_status = 'approved')`. The test counts the sites.

- **On a give:** only a verified investor can be given a cohort (400 `not_verified`), as PG11d's "VERIFIED ONLY" says.
- **On every list:** the assignable list offers only verified investors; the tiles count only verified holders.
- **On every read:** the investor's routes read the user again and refuse 403 `not_verified`.
- **Afterwards:** an investor who stops being verified keeps the grant, reads nothing, and the grant reads `waits_for_verification`. A deactivated account is refused at sign-in (401).

### 3. The store (migration 564)

- **`investor_cohort_access`, one row per grant, not per pair.**
  - The unique index covers only the open grant (`WHERE ended_at IS NULL`). An ended grant keeps its dates, and a new grant is a new row. 206's mentor table overwrote the earlier dates on a re-assign (map F5).
  - `ends_at` is PG11d's End date, the last day of access (inclusive).
  - A grant past its last day is closed as `end_reason = 'expired'`, by the nightly sweep and before any give or end.
  - `scope_json` holds what the grant opens.
  - `nda_template_slug`, `nda_envelope_uuid`, `nda_sent_at` and `nda_signed_at` hold the cohort NDA.
- **`investor_cohort_notices`:** the delivery ledger, one row per notice sent, as 367's.
- **`investor_cohort_optouts`:** one row per hide. The unique index covers only the hide in force, so hiding again while hidden changes nothing and keeps the first date. 367's re-hide overwrote it (map F6). Showing again keeps the row with its `withdrawn_at`.
- **`investor_cohort_intro_requests`:** one request per grant and founder, decided once (`decided_at`, `decision` accepted or declined).

**The program grant gate does not apply.** Investor cohorts is not in `ENFORCED_PROGRAMS` (`services/programEnforcement.ts`), because no branch holds it today. Which studio holds the program is #2376 and part 3 (#2789).

### 4. The admin's routes (`/api/admin/investor-cohorts`)

- **Gates:** every route takes `requireAdmin`. The three writes then take `requireBranchNotSuspended` (S-GATE), checked in `branch_suspended_freeze.test.ts`'s order list.
- **The audit:** each write records `logAdminAction` with the cohort and the grant's uid, so Programs' Activity tab lists it under Investor cohort access, naming the cohort and never the investor:
  - `investor_cohort.granted`;
  - `investor_cohort.ended`;
  - `investor_cohort.nda_sent`.

**`GET /assignable?q=&cycle_id=&limit=&offset=`**
- Verified investors, by name or fund (`investor_profiles.firm_name`), paged with a total.
- No email address.
- With `cycle_id`, each row has `has_open_grant`, so PG11d can mark it.

**`GET /?q=&cycle_id=&nda=&state=&limit=&offset=`**
- The grants, newest first, paged with a total.
- Searched by investor or fund, with an escaped `LIKE` (`util/likeSearch.ts`).
- Filtered by cohort, NDA state (`signed`, `sent`, `not_sent`) and access state (`active`, `ended`, `waits_for_verification`, `waits_for_nda`).
- Each row has:
  - the investor (with their email, for the admin) and their fund;
  - `verified_now`;
  - the cohort and its label;
  - both states and the scope;
  - the note;
  - `intro_requests` and `intros_accepted`;
  - `granted_at`, `ends_at`, `ended_at` and `end_reason`.

**`GET /summary`:** PG11's tiles.
- `investors_with_access`: distinct verified people with an open grant.
- `cohorts_open`.
- `founders_hidden`: a count, never names. It is `null` when it cannot be read, never 0.
- `intro_requests`, `intros_accepted` and `intros_pending`.
- `nda_template`: `published`, `missing` or `unreadable`.

**`POST /`:** `{ investor_user_id, cohort_cycle_id, ends_at, scope?, note? }`.
- Refusals:
  - 400 `invalid_ids`;
  - 404 `user_not_found`;
  - 400 `not_verified`;
  - 404 `cycle_not_found`;
  - 400 `invalid_end_date`;
  - 400 `scope_unavailable` (pitch deck, Demo Day) or `invalid_scope`;
  - 400 `note_too_long` (1,000 characters);
  - 409 `already_active` while the pair has an open grant. That writes nothing and sends nothing.
- It answers 201 with the grant, `founders_notified` (`null` when the sweep failed, never 0) and `nda_template`.

**`POST /:uid/end`:** ends an open grant and records who and when (`end_reason = 'admin'`).
- An ended grant answers 409 `already_ended` and keeps its first end date.
- Its founders are told.

**`POST /:uid/nda`:** sends the cohort NDA (§5).

### 5. The cohort NDA (O-NDA B)

- **The template.** A grant names HQ's template `investor_cohort_nda_v1`.
  - **No such template is published, and its wording is UNVERIFIED.** The owner or counsel supplies it, and HQ writes it in the legal template library (`requireHqAuthoring`).
  - Until then `POST /:uid/nda` answers 409 `nda_template_missing`, and every grant reads `waits_for_nda`.
- **The send.** Once the template is published, the route:
  - fills `{{investor_name}}`, `{{firm_name}}`, `{{cohort}}` and `{{ends_at}}`;
  - sends it to the investor through the native e-sign flow, as document type `investor_cohort_nda`;
  - records the envelope on the grant.
- **Its refusals:**
  - 409 `grant_ended`;
  - 400 `not_verified`;
  - 409 `already_signed`;
  - 409 `already_sent`, while the envelope is neither voided nor declined;
  - 409 `nda_template_unfilled`;
  - 503 `nda_template_unreadable`;
  - 502 `envelope_send_failed`.
- **The signature.** `routes/esign.ts`'s completion block calls `onCohortNdaEnvelopeCompleted`, which sets `nda_signed_at` once on the grant that names the envelope. A second completion changes nothing.
  - The DocuSign poller has no hook, because this send is native only.

### 6. What the investor reads (O-SEE A), at `/api/investor-cohorts`

Every investor route checks, every time:
- an active account, verified now (403 `not_verified`);
- an open grant whose last day has not passed (403 `not_assigned`);
- the grant's NDA signed (403 `nda_required`).

**The routes:**
- **`GET /me`** lists the investor's open grants, with their state, NDA state and scope.
- **`GET /me/:cycleId/ventures`** lists each founder in the cohort, by name, with:
  - `venture` when the scope has `summary`: the founder's newest live project's name, tagline, sector and stage, or `null` when none is recorded;
  - `weeks` when the scope has `weekly_progress`: each week's status and deliverables done and required;
  - `intro`: this investor's request to them and its state, or `null`.

**What the read leaves out:**
- It selects its own columns and does not reuse `maskFounderForInvestor`.
- It serves no email address of any kind, and no figure beyond the summary.
- A founder who hid from this investor is left out.
- Before it returns, the notice sweep runs for the grant, so a founder is told no later than the first read.

**Cohort membership** is `company_week_status`, as the mentor reads have it (`routes/advisors.ts`), until O-FND names one definition.
- A founder with no week row yet (Week 1 not opened) is not in the cohort here (map F2).
- PG11d's founder count waits for O-FND.

### 7. Founders: told, hiding, and introductions

**Notices** (`syncInvestorCohortNotices`):
- It runs on a give, on an end, before an investor's read, when a founder opens their access list or shows themselves again, and nightly.
- It first closes grants past their last day.
- It tells each founder in the cohort when a grant starts and when it ends, including at `ends_at`.
- A start is owed only while the investor is verified now, which also checks the account state. The mentor sweep checks the role only (map F1).
- It is never owed while the founder is hidden from that investor.
- The ledger's `NOT EXISTS` keeps rows already sent out of the window, so a small limit never stalls.

**The founder's routes,** mirroring the mentor ones:
- **`GET /founder/access`:** the investors who hold or held a grant on the founder's cohorts. `can_see` is true only for an active grant, signed and verified, that they have not hidden from.
- **`PUT /founder/access/:investorUserId`,** body `{ visible }`.
  - It answers 404 `investor_not_found` for an investor with no grant on their cohorts.
  - Showing again sends a start notice now owed.

**Introductions (O-INT B):**
- **The investor asks:** `POST /me/:cycleId/intros` with `{ founder_user_id, message? }`.
  - It takes the same three gates as the read.
  - It answers 404 `founder_not_in_cohort` for a founder outside the cohort or hidden from this investor, who cannot be asked.
  - It answers 409 `already_requested` for a second request to the same founder on the same grant.
  - The founder is told.
- **The founder reads them:** `GET /founder/intros`.
- **The founder decides once:** `POST /founder/intros/:uid/decide` with `{ accepted }`.
  - It answers 404 `intro_not_found` for a request made to someone else.
  - It answers 409 `already_decided` for a second decision.
  - The investor is told the decision.
- **Contact goes one way.** After the founder accepts, their own list shows the investor's email address, and the founder writes first. The investor never receives the founder's address.
