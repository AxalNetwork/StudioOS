## D1383 — A bot transcript is no longer refused because Nova's timestamps run slightly past a chunk

- **Status:** a fix, found live on 2026-10-29 by the owner. Built by S21.
- **Builds on:** D886, which assembles the bot's per-chunk transcripts into one timeline.
- **Migration:** none.

### What happened

Session 48 was the owner's Zoom call, with one participant who joined and left during it. Eadwyn AI recorded it: 26 chunks, about 5 minutes, and per-participant audio. Nova transcribed all 26 chunks.

Assembly then failed with "Processing stopped unexpectedly". `botStagedPassages` refused any segment time later than its chunk's duration, and Nova pads a segment's end past the audio it heard:
- chunk 15: 2,340 ms long, segment ending at 2,560 ms;
- chunk 17: 30,000 ms long, segment ending at 30,765 ms.

One padded end failed the whole transcript as `bot_stage_invalid`. The check was read only from segment times, not from the transcript text.

### What changed

- **`botStagedPassages`** (`services/meetingBotAssembly.ts`) accepts a time up to `STAGE_SLACK_MS` (2 s) past the chunk and reads it as the chunk's own end, so the time stays on the call's clock.
- **Still refused:** a time further out than that, an end before its start, or a non-text segment.
- **Session 48 can be finished.** Its chunk transcripts are staged already, so pressing Try again assembles them without transcribing (or charging) again.

### Verified

- **Assembly test** (`meeting_bot_assembly_d886`):
  - the live case (2,560 ms on a 2,340 ms chunk) lands at the chunk's end, on the call's clock;
  - 3,001 ms on a 1,000 ms chunk is still refused.
- **Mutation check:** the file's mutations were retargeted at the new lines, with one more for the clamp. 47 of 47 pass, and every mutation is caught.
- **Neighbouring tests:** 210 of 210 meeting-bot and processing Worker tests pass. `tsc` reports no errors.
