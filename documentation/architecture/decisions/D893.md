### 1. The address

- **The page lives at `/admin/insights`,** under `guard(['admin'])` and never `hqOnly`. It is the plain Admin's own shell, as D286's held landings were.
- **The old address redirects.** `/admin/held/insights` goes through `HeldInsightsRedirect`, which keeps the query and the hash and adds `?moved=held-insights`. The page then shows #2375's notice once, under the title: "Insights has a new address. Update your bookmark." Dismiss removes `moved`.
- **The redirect is counted** as `redirect:/admin/held/insights` (#2301). The reachability guard treats a route whose whole element is a `*Redirect` component as a redirect, as `redirect_hits_2301.test.mjs` already did.
- **The other four held landings keep their addresses** until their own issues move them.

### 2. What is read, and from where

Every figure comes from a read that already exists. Each read answers alone, and its failure costs only the blocks it feeds. Try again re-reads that one read.

| Block or tile | Read | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts, New this week, Who your accounts are, Growth, Open support tickets, AI spend this month | the Studio glance, `GET /api/admin/studio/glance` | any admin |
| Active last week, and its change on the week before | the analytics overview, `GET /api/monitoring/analytics/overview`, read for last week and the week before | any admin |
| Still active after sign-up | the retention cohorts, `GET /api/monitoring/analytics/cohorts` | any admin |
| Activity, Activation, the Activated tile | HQ analytics, `GET /api/admin/hq/analytics?range=8w`, the HQ-held line | Super Admin only |

### 3. What waits, and how it says so

- **Nine figures have no read yet:**
  - the plan mix;
  - countries;
  - identity checks;
  - profile completeness;
  - dormant accounts;
  - the LP and Product tester counts;
  - AI spend by profile, and the sign-up split by profile;
  - weekly activity and the activation funnel, for a plain Admin.
- **Each of these blocks draws "Not recorded"** with one sentence naming what is missing (`WAITS_FOR_READ` in `insightsModel.js`). None draws a sample, and none draws a zero.
- **The filters are drawn and disabled.** Range (Quarter, Year), profile, country and "Compare with previous period" wait for the read. One line under the bar says so: "Filtering by range, profile and country arrives with the Insights read."
- **#2660 builds the read and switches the page to it.** Its response keys map one to one onto the model's blocks.
