## D7700 — Selected-document passage citations for SAFE qualification (#7700)

SAFE qualification now reads instrument terms from the run's selected data-room document, matching the owner-selected reader carried onto main by #7668. Every one of the five scenarios advances to revision 2, the pack to revision 2, and the golden case to version 2. Complete versioned documents have the real reader's identity, digest, ordered passages, coverage and null continuation shape. Cap-table facts stay typed; the conditional case keeps only financing-status facts in raise.records. No instrument term remains in a typed raise record.

Expected answers and citations are re-derived as data_room_passage:<uid>:<seq> references. Every assertion has a targeted incorrect output, including removing every occurrence of a required citation without breaking the other assertions first. The synthetic complete instrument text explicitly says no maturity date is stated; its cited answer says Not recorded. No typed-null exception or inference from a truncated excerpt is needed.

The evaluator recognizes a stable passage only inside the selected read document, with matching file UID/ref, passage reference/sequence and nonempty text. Arbitrary mentions, malformed tags, unknown references, missing or duplicate citations fail. Dotted record paths and the citation cap remain supported. Syntactically valid passage-reference digits do not count as an invented maturity value; a real 24-month value beside the reference is still refused.

The evaluator advances from on-main version 4 to version 5, invalidating stored evidence under changed citation semantics. The slice-1 compatibility battery selects actual version-1 golden cases; the rewritten version-2 SAFE golden is independently qualified with its stronger citation assertions. No stored production evaluation row is rewritten, no model is called and no paid evaluation is run.

Focused coverage includes native reader shape, revisions, complete missing-maturity evidence, revised golden qualification, selected-file binding, malformed/unknown citation refusal and legacy number/structure/claim rules. The canonical scenario suite checks every correct output and each targeted incorrect output; the library and case-identity gates validate generated manifests and immutable case identity. Generated modules and docs/ are not committed.
