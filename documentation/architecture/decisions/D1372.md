## D1372 — Start on a pasted Meet link answers in seconds, and sends Eadwyn AI when the call is not on the Calendar

- **Status:** a fix, found live on 2026-10-29 by the owner. Built by S21.
- **Builds on:**
  - D871: attaching a Meet call from the member's Calendar;
  - D1360: Eadwyn AI joins by default;
  - D1362: one Start for a call.
- **Migration:** none.

### What happened

The owner pasted a Meet link into Record a Session and pressed Start Call. After 30 s the dialog said "The server did not respond within 30s", and nothing was saved: no interview, no session, no bot run.

With Google connected, Start saves a Meet call for import first (D871's attach). The attach looks the link's code up in the member's primary Calendar, and the lookup had three faults:

- **The window was too wide.** It covered 30 days back to a year ahead, with recurring meetings expanded.
- **It read too much.** That was up to 10 pages of 250 events.
- **Nothing timed out.** None of the Calendar reads had a timeout.

A call started from Meet itself (`meet.google.com/new`) is on no Calendar event, so the lookup read every page and ran past the browser's 30 s. Even when it finished, the answer was `meeting_not_in_calendar`, a refusal, although Eadwyn AI could have joined that link.

### What changed

- **The Calendar lookup** (`services/channels/googleMeet.ts`):
  - **The day either side of now first.** A call being recorded now costs one read.
  - **The wide window only on a miss,** and never past `ATTACH_SEARCH_BUDGET_MS` (10 s) in all. Past the budget it answers `meet_unreachable` (503, retryable), never a hang.
  - **A timeout on every Calendar read** (`CALENDAR_FETCH_TIMEOUT_MS`, 8 s), through `calendarFetch`. It covers each place that calls it.
- **Record a Session's Start** (`RecordLightbox.jsx`): when Eadwyn AI can join and the attach is refused only because the call cannot be imported, Start sends the bot to the pasted link instead. That is the same session and `sendBot` path as a link Axal cannot import. The codes that do this:
  - `meeting_not_in_calendar`;
  - `meeting_not_hosted`;
  - `meeting_no_time`;
  - `meet_unreachable`.
- **What still refuses:**
  - **A timeout:** the attach may have been saved and sent its own bot, and a second session would send a second.
  - **`meeting_link_reused`.**
  - **A call where the bot cannot join.**

### Verified

- **Worker tests** (`meet_attach_speakers_d871`):
  - a call on today's Calendar is found in one read of at most two days, and the read carries a timeout;
  - a code on no event is refused with `meet_unreachable` within the budget, after at most 5 slow reads (it used to take up to 20), and nothing is stored.
- **Frontend tests** (`record_call_create_meeting_d1346`):
  - each of the four codes sends the bot and opens the meeting with no refusal;
  - a timeout, a reused link and a bot that cannot join still refuse and close the tab.
- **Mutation check:** both halves were reverted in turn, and each failed its new tests.
