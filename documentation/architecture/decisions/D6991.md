- **Status:** built by Claude Code for slot S13, 2026-11-17. Not deployed.
- **Part of:** #6976 (the owner's public website audit, plan key X9). Register P0, `/methodology` and `/spinout-lab`.

- **Migration:** none. **Worker:** none.

### Before

Three vocabularies named the same four weeks:

| Surface | Names |
|---|---|
| Homepage band (`LAB_WEEKS` in `LandingPage.jsx`) | Idea & Customer, Solution & Roadmap, Validate & Team, Incorporate & Capital |
| Lab page, brief, admitted welcome (`PIPELINE_PHASES`) | Validate, Build, Pitch, Fund |
| Methodology Lab section (`LAB_WEEKS` in `siteMethod.js`) | Validate, Build, Raise, Grow, with formation in week 2 |

### After

- `PIPELINE_PHASES` in `lib/spinoutLab.js` carries the F1 names and a one-line `desc` each. `LAB_WEEKS` is derived from it (`n`, `name`, `desc`, `days`). It is the only place a week is named.
- These surfaces import the list: the homepage band, the Lab page's gates (`LabGates`), the admitted welcome in `SpinoutLabPage.jsx`, the brief's gate grid and the methodology Lab section. The events card names no week; it says "a four-week programme" from `COHORT_WEEKS`, and it stays as it is.
- **Methodology.**
  - The four stages keep Validate, Build, Raise, Grow, under the eyebrow "The company lifecycle · four stages".
  - The Lab section draws the Lab's four weeks with their lines, and says "Its four weeks are the Lab's, not the four lifecycle stages above."
  - It no longer says "Each week maps to one stage" or "through all four stages".
  - `siteMethod.js` keeps only the canvas's four week colours (`LAB_WEEK_TONES`).
- **Formation.**
  - It is in no week. `LAB_FORMATION` reads: "Formation depends on your starting point. Wherever it falls, the Lab checks incorporation and the founder equity filing before its last gate opens."
  - The Lab page prints it once under the four gates, with the chosen jurisdiction's two lines (`formationItemsFor`). The brief prints it once under its gate grid.
  - The second sentence is there because the Worker still checks formation at the end of week 4 (`MILESTONES` week 4 requires `incorporation_completed`). The page says when formation is checked without placing it in a week.
- **Week 4 items.** The two jurisdiction lines (incorporation, the equity filing) move out of week 4's items. `pipelineItemsFor(phase)` now returns a week's items as they are.

### Not changed

- **Signed-in surfaces.** The workspace (`SpinoutLabWorkspace.jsx`, which still says "Idea & Customer" … "Incorporate & Capital"), the Week 4 compliance and 83(b) pages, and `spinoutInvestorContent.js` are not public pages this issue names. They are left for a follow-up.
- **Demo Day** (#6994, X12) and programme content.
- **The scoring wording in decision F.** That is X8.
