### What the canvas decides

- **Overview cockpit**, plus a first-use path; Opportunities (inbox, empty); Engagements (board, sheet, required outcome on an ended signed engagement, Agreement tab); Client Sheet; Sessions with scheduling setup; Delivery (versions, add version, send confirmation); Earnings (ledger, unsaved-note guard).
- **Clients and Contracts are absorbed** into the Client Sheet and the engagement’s Agreement tab, in Advisor emerald.
- **Honest reads:** a failed read says “could not be read · Try again” and never becomes 0; one unreadable source does not blank the page. Implementation history goes under a disclosure.
- **Eadwyn is task-first;** models, pricing, usage and top-up sit under AI settings. The admin preview is a one-line banner.

### Backend required

- An engagement end reason: “Did not renew” vs “Ended for another reason” (`outcome` records active, renewed or ended only).
- A deliverable Ready state (versions record sent, opened and signed off only).
- Included pricing (billing state is unpriced, billed, collected or written off; Free is a recorded $0).
