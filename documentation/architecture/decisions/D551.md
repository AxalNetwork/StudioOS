## D551 — A founder's contact gets a strength and an organisation, and the Network desk drafts

**The founder Network desk (A6 of `Founder Workspaces Canvas.dc.html`) draws
a strength per contact, an Organizations lens, follow-ups and the last note,
and two proposal bands. None had a store or a route behind it. A contact now
carries a `strength` and an `organization`. The contact list carries its
follow-up rollup. The desk's bands draft over founder surfaces of their own.**
Issue #1144, slot S07. Migration 386.

**What was true on main.**
- `contacts` had no strength and no organisation column. The desk printed "Not
  scored". The Organizations zone grouped nobody, because `organizationOf`
  read an `organization` field no contact carried.
- `contact_tasks` and `contact_replies` existed (`routes/contacts.ts`), but
  `GET /api/contacts` returned no rollup of them.
- `network/relationships` and `network/introductions` in `DRAFT_SURFACES`
  read a partner's book (`partner_book_contacts`), not a founder's contacts,
  so the desk drew no switch (D424's `none`).

**What changed.**
- **Migration 386** adds two columns to `contacts`:
  - `strength`: one of strong, warm or cool, as the canvas draws them. NULL
    means nobody has set it, and the pages read that as "Not recorded", never
    as cool.
  - `organization`: free text, nullable.
  `ensureSchema` self-heals both on an existing table (the 128/166 pattern).
  #1023's IN3 is to read the same column on the investor side.
- **`PUT /api/contacts/:uid`** sets both, for the contact's owner only
  (`loadOwned`: another founder's contact is 403). An absent key keeps the
  value, and null or a blank clears it. A strength outside the set is a 400
  naming `strength`, and nothing is written.
- **`GET /api/contacts`** adds three fields per contact, in the same one
  statement:
  - `next_task_due`: the earliest due date among open tasks; a blank date is
    not a date;
  - `open_tasks`: the count of open tasks;
  - `last_reply_at`: when the latest reply was logged.
- **Two founder draft surfaces**, scoped like every founder surface:
  - `network/re-engagement` drafts over the caller's contacts going cold on
    one project (`founderProject`): more than 60 days since the last activity,
    the pages' own definition. Coldest comes first, with what each person last
    wrote, inbound only. It never sends an email address to the model.
  - `network/intro-email` drafts over the caller's own accepted introduction
    propositions and the reasons each match recorded. A `scope_key` names one,
    and it must be the caller's.
- **The pages.**
  - **The desk** shows each contact's strength and next follow-up. It draws
    the rail's switch and both bands; the re-engagement band is scoped to the
    coldest contact's project. "Who should meet whom" reads Not recorded,
    with the reason that no store pairs two of a founder's contacts.
  - **`/network/relationships`** edits strength and organisation per row, and
    shows the follow-up and last note. Its Strong stat counts the contacts
    scored strong.
  - **`/network/organizations`** groups the set field and names each group's
    strongest tie.
