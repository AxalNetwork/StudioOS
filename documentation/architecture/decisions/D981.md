## D981 — Program grants at `/admin/licences/programs`, and the studio's Programs tab at `/admin/my-licence/programs`: the shell, the studios against the programs, and no grant until the store exists (#2376)

- **Status:** decided and built by S21, 2026-10-24 (#2376, under #2711), in draft PR #2742 beside D936, D937 and D972.
- **Canvas:** the owner's "Program grants", GR1–GR15 with lettered boards (artifacts `9bU81t2zZxckmAmhrKenc6`, `DSX8rakWCr4VBkoRddA3yU`). It is committed as `design/incoming/Program Grants Page.dc.html` and `Program Grants · Boards.dc.html`.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `cc35435fd5`. The map is `documentation/audits/PROGRAM_GRANTS_MAP_2026-10-24.md`.
- **Migration:** none here. Parts 1, 3 and 5 take 553, 554 and 555.
- **Why:** the owner asked for "a feature for the Super Admin, to moderate what program can the HQ grant to other Admin's (subsidiaries), under what terms", and for it to "reflect in the Licenses section of Admin" (#2365, quoted on #2376). Nothing stores a grant today: no table, no route, no request kind. This decision is the integration: the two addresses, the header, the strip, the tab, and what can be drawn truthfully without a store. The rest is seven slot tasks (§6).

### 1. Where it lives

| Address | Who | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `/admin/licences/programs` | Super Admin, `guard(['admin'], hqOnly(…))` as the ledger | Program grants (GR1): the grid of studios against programs |
| `/admin/my-licence/programs` | a licence's Administrator, `guard(['admin'], …)` as the other licence tabs | The Programs tab (GR7), the licence page's third, after Terms |

- **The addresses are #2375's route map.** #2376 also proposes `/admin/licences/programs/requests`, `/defaults`, `/history` and `/[licence-ref]` (GR3, GR5, GR6). None is added here: a tab is not added before its feature (D953's rule). Part 6 adds them.
- **No old address moves,** so nothing joins the redirect list (#2319).
- **The header is D936's:** crumb "Licences › Program grants", one h1, the pill "HQ only", "Updated" and a refresh, and the canvas's line "Which programs each studio may run, and on what terms. Studios ask; HQ decides."
- **The strip.** HQ's Licences row gains a strip, Ledger (`/admin/licences`) and Program grants, first in `HQ_STRIPS`. `stripFor` lights it on both.
- **The rail** is `AdminBoardFrame`, workspace "Licences", with the note "Eadwyn reads the grants on this page. It changes nothing." The studio's tab sits inside the licence page's own frame (`MyLicencePage.jsx`), as its other tabs do.
- **The sidebars.** Both addresses are rail aliases of their Licences rows (`railPages.js`): the Administrator's Licences and HQ's Licences.

### 2. What is read, and from where

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/admin/licences` (`requireSuperAdmin`): every licence with its reference, brand, kind, status and territories | The studios tile; one grid row per studio |

- **That is the only read.** Nothing reads a program list or a grant (the map, §A). The programs are the canvas's nine, in its order and groups, kept in `fe/lib/programGrants.js` on the program keys #2375 and D953 use, so part 1's store keys on the same strings.
- **A row is a licence that is not a draft and not terminated.** A draft holds no territory yet; a terminated one holds none any more. A suspended row says "Program changes are frozen until HQ lifts the suspension." and a pending one "Grants start when the licence is active." (GR1).
- **A failed ledger read** says "Program grants could not be read. That is not the same as none." with Try again, and the tile says "Unreadable". An empty ledger says to issue a licence first.
- **The studio's tab reads nothing yet.** `GET /api/licence/mine/programs` is part 3.

### 3. What each page draws today

- **The fixed facts are drawn as the canvas states them,** because no store can change them: Product testing is "HQ only" (the studio reads "Runs at HQ"), and Investor cohort access and AI-native Venture are "Not ready to grant" (the studio reads "Not offered yet").
- **Every other cell says "Not recorded",** never "Not granted": not granting is a decision HQ has not recorded either. The cell's title and the note under the grid say the grant store arrives with part 1.
- **HQ's page (GR1).**
  - The studios tile ("[n] active · [n] suspended · [n] pending activation").
  - Grants in force, Requests waiting and Ending in 90 days: "Not read here yet", each saying which part brings it.
  - The grid: the studio column (name, reference, territories, kind, status, the row note) and nine program columns under six groups.
  - Studios holding each program and Requests by month: "Not read here yet".
- **The studio's tab (GR7):** the rule "HQ decides which programs your studio runs.", the nine programs with their state, and the partner deal types card reading "Not recorded" (part 5). The licence page's none-state adds "On HQ-held accounts, HQ runs every program." (GR11 C).
- **Not drawn now, and why:** GR2's dark and GR15's phone with each part; GR3's one studio, GR4's grant lightbox, GR5's requests, GR6's default terms and GR10's four dialogs (parts 1, 2, 6); GR8's terms lightbox and GR9's request sheet (parts 2, 3, 7); GR12–GR14's states beyond each block's own; every chart (part 6). Nothing on either page writes.

### 4. Who sees what

The Super Admin alone reads HQ's page: `hqOnly` shows the HQ-only notice to an Admin without the elevation, as the ledger does, and the read is `requireSuperAdmin`. A licence's Administrator sees the Programs tab of their own licence, read only; asking for a program is a request (part 2), never a write here.

### 5. Owner decisions, and what S21 read from the canvas

#2376 put six questions to the owner. No comment on #2376 answers them; the approved canvas answers four.

- **Read as answered by the canvas:**
  - **3. Is a grant valid before its schedule is signed?** Only after, with a switch: GR4's term "Starts only when the schedule is signed: On", and GR10 A's sentence "It starts when the program schedule is signed."
  - **4. Do partner deal types belong here (#2370)?** Yes: GR3's "Partner deal types this studio may offer", and GR7's locked card "HQ sets these."
  - **6. Can Product testing be granted?** No: GR6 keeps it at HQ, and the program picker lists it as one that "cannot be picked: HQ only, or not ready to grant."
  - **1. Does a grant limit an Administrator on HQ-held accounts?** No, only what a branch runs: GR11 C, "Your account is not linked to a licence. On HQ-held accounts, HQ runs every program." The owner may correct this reading; part 4 rests on it.
- **Still the owner's:**
  - **2.** Is the program fee billed on the annual fee invoice or on the statements? GR4 states "Program fee to HQ [€2,000] a year" and not where it is billed. Part 1 stores the fee; billing waits for the answer.
  - **5.** Do Community features or the advisor network become grantable (#2367, #2368, #2369)? Not drawn. D972 §5 carries the same question.
