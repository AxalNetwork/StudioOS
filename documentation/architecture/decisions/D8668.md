### What the audit found

- `/learning/me` showed five separate empty histories and a Certificates card: `MyLearning.jsx` draws one section card per list from three reads, plus a static Certificates card that only says certificates are not issued. Nothing in the main content leads to a course.
- The plan offered only a study block and sent the learner elsewhere for course content: `LearningPlanPage.jsx` does not reuse the PlanPicker that `PlanSheet.jsx` already has. The study-block dialog was correct (labelled, Escape returns focus to Add a study block).
- Programs appear only on the Learning home (`MyPrograms.jsx`); the admin page `/learning/programs` correctly offers an HQ ticket when nothing is assigned.

### What the canvas decides

- My learning is one workspace with History, Plan and Programs tabs, and a fourth tab, Assigned to you, for admins. It keeps the Axal VC shell and the collapsed rail.
- A learner whose three reads all succeed and are empty sees one first-use card with four courses and Browse all courses, never five empty cards. The Certificates card is removed (LPL01, compared with production on LPL01B).
- A failed read is its own state: a banner names the unavailable section, the section stays in place with Try again, and the page makes no claim of inactivity. Loading, all unavailable, offline (Stale), no percent, an unknown status and an open attempt are drawn (LPL02, LPL02B).
- A returning learner's History shows Next for you with its reason ("3 of 7 required lessons complete"), Continue lesson from `resume`, submitted work, results against the pass mark, and completed courses, each only when the record carries it (LPL03).
- Every plan row carries an origin label (From course, Study block · yours, Webinar · its own time, Exam · time reserved) and one next action. Choosing a course, scheduling it and returning to the plan marks and focuses the new rows with a status message (LPL04 to LPL06D).
- The study-block dialog keeps its labels and focus return; its focus order is numbered, with validation, saving and refusal states (LPL07, LPL07B).
- Each program is marked Added by HQ or You joined; Open to join is a separate list, and HQ contact is offered for joining and leaving (LPL08). Assigned to you with nothing assigned offers Ask HQ: open a ticket, as observed (LPL09, LPL09B).
