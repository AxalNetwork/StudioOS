### 1. Where it lives

- **The Models tab of `/pricing`** (`?for=models`), in `pages/PricingPage.jsx`. The hero, the tabs, the model tables and the FAQ stay as they are.
- **The order is the canvas's (CRP1):** the "Your AI tokens" band, then Token packs, then the model tables. Token packs used to come after the tables.
- **Anchors:** the band is `#add-tokens` and Token packs is `#token-packs`, so D1288 can point `ADD_TOKENS_PATH` at `/pricing?for=models#add-tokens`.

### 2. The band (CRP1, CRP11, CRP14)

- **Who:** a signed-in member only. A visitor sees no band and the page makes no balance read (CRP12).
- **Read:** `GET /api/ai-budget/me` (`aiBudgetApi.mine`), on the Models tab only.
- **Draws:**
  - the eyebrow "Token balance", the balance to the cent (`usageMoney`), and "Tokens never expire.";
  - "Reading…" while it reads;
  - a failed read: "Your token balance couldn't be read. That's not the same as having none.", with Try again. Never `$0.00`;
  - `ai_paused`: "AI is paused on this account. Contact support. You can still add tokens: runs are paused, not purchases.";
  - Add tokens, the one primary button. It goes to Token packs on the same page until D1287 opens the sheet in place.
- **Not drawn yet (D1286):** the plan's allowance and its reset, the allowance-then-tokens bar, the storage line, the auto-reload line, and each profile's wording (CRP13).

### 3. Token packs (CRP1, CRP6, CRP10, CRP11, CRP12)

- **Read:** `GET /api/public/plans-and-packs`, the read the plan tabs use, through `packCards`.
- **Each pack:** "[$N] of AI tokens", its price, and its offers (a bonus with its end, first purchase only).
- **One Buy per pack.** A member's Buy opens `/checkout?token_pack=<uid>`, D755's pack checkout. It used to open `/pricing/products#token-packs`, whose own Buy then opened the checkout (#2452's broken item 3).
- **A visitor** gets "Sign in to buy", which signs in and comes back to `/pricing?for=models#token-packs`.
- **States:**
  - loading: three cards reading "Reading…";
  - a failed read: "Token packs couldn't be read just now." with Try again, never an empty list (#2452's broken item 1: the strip used to draw nothing);
  - nothing on sale: "Top-up isn't available yet." (`TOPUP_UNAVAILABLE`).
- **Footnote:** "Sold by Axal VC Management LLC. Paid securely through Stripe. VAT, where it applies, is added at checkout and is not part of the tokens."
- **The copy** is in `lib/sitePricing.js` (`PACKS`, `TOKENS_BAND`). It says "AI tokens", never "credits" (D793).

### 4. What is read, and what is not computed

| Read | Used for |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/ai-budget/me` | the balance, `ai_paused` |
| `GET /api/public/plans-and-packs` | the packs, their prices and offers |

Nothing is computed in the browser beyond formatting. The balance is the Worker's, rounded to the cent by `usageMoney`. The pack price and offer are `packCards`'.

### 5. Owner questions

#2452's eight owner questions have no answer on the issue. The canvas draws the recommended answer to each, so the integration takes them:

1. "Founder pack $20, $100" and the rest are plan prices, not packs.
2. The words are "AI tokens", "Add tokens", "Token packs" (D793 stands).
3. Pack sizes are HQ's, set on HQ › AI allowances; the canvas draws placeholders.
4. No promo codes on packs (D755 §5): "Promo codes don't apply to token packs. Offers show on the pack."
5. Add tokens lives on the Models tab, and every Add tokens opens the same sheet (D1287, D1288).
6. Auto-reload's state shows in the sheet; it is set on Billing behind its step-up (D794).
7. Tokens never expire.
8. Admins and Super Admins may buy for their own runs, under the same limits.

An answer that differs reopens the matching slot task, not this integration.
