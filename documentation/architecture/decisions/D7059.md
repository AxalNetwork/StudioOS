### What the canvas decides

- An overdue follow-up on the Overview opens its own contact and task, even on page 2 of the book; the task shows Selected and takes focus.
- A follow-up queue opens on Overdue with the overview's total, oldest due first, and has Due today, Due later, No due date, All open and Done in 30 days. It pages past 20.
- The Person Sheet is held by contact id, so finishing the last open task under "Has open follow-up" keeps the sheet and both drafts open, with Undo.
- Every exit with pending edits (✕, Escape, backdrop, navigation, Back, another person, Request introduction) asks one question: Save, Keep editing or Discard, naming both drafts.
- Saves ignore repeat taps, keep text typed during the save, and cover failure, offline, done in another tab and a person no longer in the book.
- History beyond the newest 200 shows its range and loads older notes and done follow-ups on their own.
- On return, filter, page and scroll are kept, focus moves to the next row and the change is announced.
- History labels say added by you, matched by an import, note written and follow-up completed.
