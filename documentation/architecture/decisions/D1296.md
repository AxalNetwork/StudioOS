- **Status:** built in slot S04, 2026-10-27 (#3392, part of #2383). Not deployed or live verified.
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Content queue · Boards.dc.html` and `Content Queue Page.dc.html` (ART7, ART8, ART13c), integrated by D1290.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1290, the addresses and the shared header;
  - D1291, the queue read by date range and its Publications chips;
  - D484, the schedule call and its UTC storage.
- **Migration:** none.
- **Worker:** no change. The page reads `GET /api/admin/articles/queue` (`stage=calendar` with `from` and `to`, and `stage=ready`) and writes `POST /api/admin/articles/:id/schedule`, both already served.

### 1. Where it lives

- **The address is D1290's:** `/admin/articles/calendar`. `ArticlesQueuePage.jsx` draws `contentQueue/Calendar.jsx` there.
- **The wait is gone:** `WAITS.calendar` is removed from `contentQueueModel.js`.
- **The rules are pure,** in `contentQueue/calendarModel.js`.

### 2. Month (ART7) and week (ART8)

- **Month:** six weeks of seven days, Monday first, with days outside the month shaded and today marked.
  - It reads the shown days plus one day either side, because a time near midnight can fall on a different UTC day than the reader's.
  - Six weeks is 44 days with that margin, inside D1291's 92-day limit.
- **Week:** the seven days by hour. The rows are 08:00 to 18:00, widened to any hour a chip sits in.
- **Times are the reader's.** A chip sits on the day it goes out in the reader's own zone (the browser's) and shows its time there. The header says "Times in <zone>".
  - The schedule dialog shows the same instant in UTC, so two admins in two zones agree.
  - Across a clock change each chip keeps its own local time: 08:00 UTC reads 09:00 in Lisbon before 25 October and 08:00 after.
- **The chips:**
  - **Scheduled:** the time and title, with Move….
  - **Published:** ✓ and the title. It opens the article's review and does not move.
  - **Publications issued:** read only, behind the "Show Publications issued" switch.

  Two scheduled at the same minute note "2 at 09:00".
- **On a phone** the month is an agenda list of the days that hold something. Every target is 44px.
- **Beside the grid,** Ready to publish lists approved articles with no time, each with Schedule….

### 3. Move… and Schedule…

- **Move… on every scheduled chip, and Schedule… on every ready article, open the schedule dialog (ART6 E).** Nothing is written until the dialog's button is pressed.
- **The dialog has:**
  - the date and time in the reader's zone;
  - the same instant in UTC;
  - the time now set, when moving;
  - "Also going out that day" for the other scheduled articles that day;
  - the rule "in the future, up to a year ahead". The button stays off until a time meets it.
- **It sends D484's call:** `schedule(id, iso)`, the same one the review panel makes.
- **Dragging is not built.** The issue makes it optional, never the only way, and Move… covers every chip.

### 4. States

- **Nothing in the shown days (ART13c):** "Nothing scheduled in October 2026.", with "Approved articles wait on the right until you give them a time."
- **A month with past articles but none scheduled:** the grid is drawn, under the line "Nothing is scheduled in … yet. What went out is shown."
- **A failed read:** "The calendar could not be read. That is not the same as nothing scheduled.", with Try again. It is never drawn as empty.
- **Publications unread:** that block says so, and the articles still draw.
- **Ready to publish** fails or empties on its own.
