## D1164 — Fund admin part 7: NAV & performance with its charts, positions and mark dialogs (FND10–FND12)

- **Status:** filed by S21 for S1 as #3071, part 7 of Fund admin (#2388). Built by S26. Frontend only. No migration. No Worker change.
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Fund Admin Console Page.dc.html`, boards FND10, FND11 (dark), FND10b (a fund with no marks) and FND12 (the dialogs).
- **Builds on:**
  - D1157, the tab at `/admin/fund-admin/performance`;
  - D1158, the positions read and the quarterly history;
  - D1159, `fund.valuation` in `FUND_ACTS` and its audited writes;
  - D1160, the shared header and the fund filter.

### 1. What it reads

| Read | For |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/fund-performance` (D627) | The tiles of the chosen fund, and Funds side by side |
| `GET /api/fund-performance/:id/history?step=quarter` (D1158) | The two quarterly charts, and IRR's "since" date |
| `GET /api/fund-performance/:id/positions` (D1158) | The positions chart, the positions table, and NAV's "latest marks of N positions" |

- **The per-fund reads run only when a fund is chosen** in the filter (`?fund=`).
- **Each block reads alone.** A read that fails is Unreadable with Retry, and says what it is not evidence of. The other blocks still answer.
- **The fund filter is honoured here.** NAV & performance offers All funds and also reads the chosen fund. So the header no longer says "This tab shows every fund…" on it (`HONOURS_FILTER` in `fundAdminModel.js`). Every other tab is unchanged.

### 2. What it draws

All of it is in `PerformanceTab.jsx`, from pure functions in `performanceModel.js`. It uses `parts.jsx` (`Section`, `Panel`, `Tiles`, `Table`) and the theme's tokens, with a dark pair for every class (FND11). `Table` stacks below `sm`, and the charts size to their box on a phone.

1. **The muted NAV line:** "NAV is investments only: the latest dated mark of each position. Cash, liabilities, fees and carry are not included. Paid-in means capital called."
2. **Seven tiles:** NAV ("latest marks of N positions"), Paid-in ("capital called"), Distributed, DPI, RVPI, TVPI and IRR ("since [first call]").
   - A figure the Worker could not compute reads **Not recorded**, with its reason under it and on hover. TVPI, for example, reads "TVPI needs a complete NAV."
   - A measured 0.00x is a zero.
3. **Paid-in, distributed and NAV by quarter**, and **TVPI and DPI by quarter**, drawn with `QuarterLines`, a new part in `ui/Charts.jsx`.
   - **A missing quarter:** a quarter with no NAV (or no TVPI, which needs one) is a **dashed bridge** from the quarter before to the quarter after, with no dot. It is never a dip to 0.
   - **The foot** names the quarter: "Q3 2025 has no complete NAV: a position had no mark. The dashed line bridges it; it is not a dip to zero." The multiples chart adds "TVPI needs a complete NAV, so a quarter without one is bridged. DPI does not."
   - **The notes** are computed: "NAV passed paid-in in [quarter] and NAV stands at [amount] in [quarter]." and "TVPI went from a to b."
4. **Positions: cost and latest mark**, drawn with `PairBars`, a new part in `ui/Charts.jsx`.
   - A position with no mark is a dashed **"No mark"** bar, never its cost.
   - The note says how many positions have no mark, or which position's mark is the highest multiple of its cost.
5. **Every chart has "View as table"** (`ChartTable` with the chart's own table), and an `alt` sentence on a `role="img"` wrapper. A missing figure reads Not recorded in the table.
6. **The positions table:** Company, Round, Cost (the invested dollars), Latest mark, Mark date, Source, Recorded by, and the acts.
   - Search by company or round; newest mark first.
   - A position with no mark reads "No mark", with the Worker's reason on hover.
7. **Funds side by side** (all funds): NAV, Paid-in, Distributed, DPI, RVPI, TVPI and IRR, each Not recorded with its reason where missing. With All funds chosen, the page says "Pick a fund above to see its quarters, its positions and its marks." and makes no per-fund read.
8. **A fund with no position**, or no quarter yet, says so in place of the block (FND10b).

### 3. The dialogs (FND12)

**Both acts are `fund.valuation`.** That is D1159's Admin-tier act in `FUND_ACTS`, drawn through `FundActControl`, so a viewer the act table refuses reads its sentence, never a disabled button. Each act opens an in-page `Lightbox`, and nothing is written without one. A refusal stays in the dialog and changes nothing. A success closes it and reads the tab again.

**Record a mark** writes `POST /api/fund-performance/positions/:id/valuations`. It is checked before the call (`checkMark`):
- **Valuation date:** a real date, **not in the future** (today is allowed). A future date is refused with "The valuation date cannot be in the future." and no call is made.
- **Value:** dollars as typed (`150000`, `150,000.00`, `$150000.5`), turned into integer cents. 0 is allowed, as a write-off.
- **Source:** required, 1 to 500 characters.
- **The dialog's line:** "Marks are dated and kept. A new mark does not change an old one. LPs see NAV in issued reports only."

**Move to another fund** writes `PUT /api/fund-performance/positions/:id/fund`.
- The choices are each administered fund and "Firm-level (no fund)", with the current fund marked "Where it counts now".
- Move is off until another fund is chosen.
- **The dialog's line:** "Changes which fund's NAV this position counts in."

### 4. Departures, for review

- **The positions table's Fund, Source and Sort filters are not drawn.** The table is one fund's, so a Fund filter would choose nothing new. Search by company or round, and newest mark first, are drawn.
- **Funds side by side shows under a chosen fund too,** as FND10 draws it. With All funds it is the tab's main block.
- **`FundPerformanceView` stays** for the investor's `/funds/performance` page. The console no longer draws it.

### 5. Tests

**`frontend/test/fund_admin_performance_d1164.test.mjs`** has 10 cases, with figures worked out in its comments.
- **The model:**
  - the tiles (a missing TVPI reads its reason; a measured 0.00x stays);
  - the quarterly series keep a missing NAV as null, with the gap foot, the notes and the tables;
  - positions: an unmarked one is never its cost, invested dollars stay dollars, newest first, search;
  - side by side with reasons;
  - `checkMark`: a future date refused, today allowed, an impossible date, 0 allowed, three decimals refused, a blank source and 501 characters refused;
  - the move options;
  - the filter note on this tab and on another.
- **Mounted, on stubbed APIs:**
  - one fund: the tiles, the per-fund reads with the chosen fund, two dashed bridges (NAV and TVPI), every chart's View as table, the positions rows, and side by side;
  - Record a mark: a future date refused before any call, then sent with its body and the tab read again;
  - Move: the chosen fund sent, firm-level as null, a refusal kept in the dialog;
  - a viewer refused `fund.valuation` reads its sentence;
  - All funds: the prompt and no per-fund read;
  - a failed history leaves the tiles and positions.
- **The source:** in-page dialogs, no browser pop-up, the charts from `ui/Charts.jsx`, the act named.

**19 of 19 mutations killed.** The first try at "the gap foot dropped" was a no-op and was replaced by one that drops it.
