## D683 — Founder plans as two cycle groups: Validate + Build, Raise + Grow

Issue: #1574. Built in #1573. Number assigned by S1, 2026-10-13.

### Why

The owner's website canvas priced the founder plans as a four-step cycle ladder (Validate, + Build, + Raise, + Grow) that billing never had. What billing sold was Free, Growth ($79) and Studio ($249), whose lines mixed the cycles: Growth already carried Raise and Grow work (the pitch deck, cap-table writes, co-marketing).

The owner, 2026-10-13: "Put them into two groups, of products, Validate + Build 20$, Grow + Validate 100$, decide what the free plan should include". S1 reads the second group as **Raise + Grow**. It keeps everything in Validate + Build, as the canvas's ladder ("each step keeps everything below it") and `TIER_RANK` both already do.

### What was decided

The tier ids stay `growth` and `studio`. Stripe product metadata (`tier`), `users.subscription_tier`, the webhook mirror and every `userMeetsTier` call keep working unchanged. What a member reads changes:

| Plan | Tier id | Monthly | What it opens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | `free` | $0 | 1 startup; start Validate (5 interviews, 3 OKRs, `FREE_TIER_LIMITS`); apply to the Spin-Out Lab; public profile and referral link; accept up to 3 proposed introductions a month |
| Validate + Build | `growth` | $20 | Unlimited startups, interviews and OKRs; venture-readiness scoring runs; company data lookups; the full archetype card; wellbeing sessions; accept up to 10 proposed introductions a month |
| Raise + Grow | `studio` | $100 | Everything in Validate + Build; the pitch deck (generate, positioning, share, premium templates, own branding); cap-table writes; advisor booking; co-marketing; compliance events; everything `STUDIO_PREFIXES` already gated (capital, funds, liquidity, legal cap, co-founder, KYC, insights, journal, watchlist, partner office hours); accept up to 25 proposed introductions a month |

- **Free is a decision, not a leftover.** It is what lets a founder start: one idea into Validate, an application to the Lab, and a public profile investors can find. Everything it opens was already free; only the words are new.
- **Five gates move from `growth` to `studio`,** because they are Raise or Grow work:
  - `decks.ts`: generate, positioning, share, and the premium-method checks, with `branding.ts` and the frontend template list (`required_tier`);
  - `captable.ts` writes;
  - `advisors.ts` booking;
  - `comarketing.ts` pitches;
  - `compliance.ts` events.
  
  Scoring runs, company lookups, project, interview and OKR limits, wellbeing views, the archetype card and integrations keep their tier.
- **Annual equivalents** keep the 20% the old ladder used: $16 and $80.
- **AI use is not in the plan.** It is D629's allowance, then tokens, the same on every plan today.

### Where the names and prices live

- `requireTier.ts`: `PLAN_NAME`, `PLAN_PRICE_LABEL` and `TIER_COPY`. `tierUpsell()` now sends `plan.name`, so a 402 names "Validate + Build" rather than a capitalised tier id.
- `wellbeing.ts` and `archetypeEntitlement.ts` read `tierUpsell()` instead of typing a price.
- `PaywallModal.jsx` `TIER_PLANS`: the catalogue `/pricing`, the doors and Settings read.
- `data/pricing.js`: Free's lines and the annual equivalents.
- `archetypeLock.js` prefers the plan name the Worker sends.
- The sidebar's lock label, Settings › Billing, and the billing emails name the plans.

### Introductions in the plans

The plan lines count Network Introductions credits (`services/introductions.ts` `monthlyAllowanceFor`): the platform proposes a warm introduction, and accepting it spends a credit. A founder gets 3, 10 or 25 a month by tier, and packs can be bought. This is not the investor's request path (`trust.ts`), which reads no plan (`for_founders_promise_accuracy.test.mjs`).
