## D6363 — The reviewer's alert job starts only when there is an alert to open or close (#6363)

- **Status:** changed 2026-11-14 by S01.
- **What happened:** seventeen of twenty-five open PRs showed a red X while main was green. The mark was `claude review (alert)`, cancelled, not failed. The job holds one repository-wide concurrency group, `github-health-review-blocked`, so that two runs never open the same alert issue at once. GitHub keeps one running and one pending job per group; a newer pending job cancels the older pending one. With many PRs pushing together, the alert jobs cancelled each other.
- **Decision:** keep the group, and stop entering it for nothing. A new job, `claude review (alert needed?)`, runs outside the group with `issues: read`. The alert job starts only when it says so: on a blocked outcome (`budget_refused`, `configuration_error`, or anything unknown), or on an ok outcome (`reviewed`, `duplicate`) while the issue `[alert] the PR reviewer is not reviewing` is open and needs closing. When the issue list cannot be read, the answer is "needed", so an alert is never lost to the gate.
- **Not chosen:** a group per pull request. It would also end the cancellations, but two PRs could then open or close the alert at the same moment and post to Slack twice. The reconciliation in `scripts/health-alert-episodes.mjs` would repair the issue, not the second post.
- **Effect:** on a normal day the alert job is skipped on every PR. It still runs, serialised, when the reviewer stops reviewing and when it recovers.
- **Pinned by:** `frontend/test/github_alerts.test.mjs` (the gate has no concurrency, read-only permission, the reporter's exact title, and both fail-open rules).
