### 1. The amendment to D840 §2

D840 §2 says "A balance is never shown to anyone else." #2430's owner question 1 took
answer (A) (D1261 §5): **founders may share derived bank figures after the NDA**.

**What is allowed:** four derived figures, the kinds `BANK_FIGURES` in
`cloudflare-worker/src/services/raiseEvidence.ts` names, and no others. Each one carries its own value.

| Kind | What it is | Unit the read carries |
|---|---|---|
| `bank-runway` | Runway in months, derived from the company's bank | `months` |
| `bank-revenue` | Monthly revenue credits, or revenue growth | `cents`, or `percent` for growth |
| `mapped-bank-spend` | Bank spend the founder mapped to a use-of-funds section | `cents` |
| `kpi-snapshot` (from Mercury) | A dated KPI such as net burn, where the bank is its source | the KPI's own unit |

**How each one is shared:**
- **One by one, by the founder.** Every bank-derived item starts Private (owner question 5;
  `private_reason: 'bank'`). Only the founder moves it to Summary or After NDA, per item. Eadwyn
  never does, and no figure is shared in bulk.
- **After the NDA only.** Before it, an investor sees coverage alone: kind, count, Source and
  newest date, with no value (owner question 2; D1265 §2).
- **Labelled by its source and the record's own date.** The investor block draws "From the
  company's bank, as of [date]" from the item's `observed_on`, never the sync's
  (`sourceLine`, `frontend/src/pages/cycles/evidence/evidenceModel.js`, D1268).
- **Re-checked at every read.** The source is re-read with the accepting founder as the bank's
  owner. A changed or vanished figure leaves the item out (D1265 §3, the fingerprint).

**What stays as D840 §2 says, for everyone but the founder:**
- **Never a balance, an account, a bank line or a counterparty.** Any bank-derived kind that is not
  one of the four above (a bank statement's lines, an accepted SAFE, a confirmed receipt) has its
  value withheld even after the NDA. The item says "A bank line is never shared; the item says it
  is recorded." (`valueWithheld`).
- **One investor's money is never shown to another.** That covers round-call responses, typed
  commitments, confirmed receipts and accepted SAFEs (`ONE_INVESTOR`).
- **Proof of funds stays D752's** dated "at least" statement.
- **Account numbers, logs and errors stay as D840 §2 says.** Account numbers show their last four
  only. No Mercury figure is written to a log, an error or a comment.

D840.md §2 now points here at the line it amends.

### 3. SCORING's hand-off

`evidencePack(env, projectId, { audience: 'scoring' })` (D1265 §5) is the one read SCORING
takes from the evidence pack. It holds:
- **Shared items only.** It returns items shared beyond Private (owner question 6) and current
  at read: the fingerprint check and the withheld values are the investor read's.
- **Per item:** `backs`, the input keys it supports; its Source label for SCORING's
  `SOURCE_GRADE`; and its value only where an investor after the NDA would see it.
- **The claims:** the states of the claims linked to those items.
- **`pack_hash`:** a SHA-256 of exactly what was read, so a stored scorecard can name the pack it
  used. A SCORING read is not an investor open and logs nothing.

**The state today:** D1255's engine (`services/ventureScoring/`, #3278) does not call
`evidencePack` yet. Its collectors read each store directly; only `mercuryRunway` comes from
`raiseEvidence.ts`. The hand-off is the contract a later SCORING part wires.

**When it is wired, these rules hold:**
- **No unshared input.** An input the pack backs must not be read from a Private item or from a
  store behind one.
- **The grade is SCORING's, not the pack's.** It comes from the item's Source label through
  `SOURCE_GRADE`; strength never sets a grade.
- **The hash is stored.** The pack's hash is stored with the scorecard row that used it.
