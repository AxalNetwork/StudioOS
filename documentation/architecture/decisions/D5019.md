### 1. Two independent concepts

The **operating licence** keeps its territory in `licence_territories` and its fee and royalty in `territory_licences`.
None of these is read or written by anything here; the first test compares the licence row before and after.

**Exclusivity** is `licence_exclusivity_terms`, with no fee column. A licence with no exclusivity row is
non-exclusive. The read says "Paying the licence fee grants none".

### 2. Versioned and frozen

Each change is a new row with the next `revision`. The previous row is marked `superseded_at` in the same batch, and
the write is a D4805 `runDecision` guarded on the revision the writer read. A stale write answers
`409 exclusivity_conflict` and writes nothing. A partial unique index keeps one current version per licence.

A trigger refuses any other update to a version, and any delete. Every write is audited twice: in `admin_audit_log`
(`logAdminAction`), and as a `terms_changed` licence event with `source: 'exclusivity'`.

### 3. The scope model

- **Scope types:**
  - `non_exclusive`;
  - `country_exclusive` (whole countries);
  - `regional_exclusive` (named regions);
  - `master_franchise`.
- **Territory:** `{ countries: ISO2[], regions: region ids }`. No scope assumes a sovereign country.
- **`territory_regions`:** HQ's named sub-territories. Each is a `subnational` region or a `metro` in one country, or a
  `multi_country` region. A region may sit inside a parent region, within the parent's countries. Managed at
  `GET|POST /api/admin/territory-regions`.
- **Structures:** a master franchise lists the operating licences it contains (`child_licences_json`). A child names
  its master (`parent_licence_uid`).

### 4. Overlap rules (`overlapProblems`)

- **Operating-licence overlap is unchanged.** `licence_territories`' one-holder-per-country index (migration 187)
  still refuses two operating licences on one country, and the Territory step still refuses it first.
- **An operating licence's exclusivity lies inside its own operating territory.** A scope reaching outside it is
  refused (`409 territory_overlap`, naming the countries).
- **A master franchise may cover a country another licence operates in only when it lists that licence as a child.**
  This is the one way a parent scope may contain child operating scopes. A listed child must operate inside the
  master's reach and may belong to only one master.
- **A child's parent must be a master in force that lists it.** The child's reach must lie inside the master's.
- **Two exclusive scopes may not cover common ground.** That covers:
  - a whole country against the same country, or against a region in it;
  - a region against itself, or against a region that contains it, or one it contains;
  - a multi-country region, which counts as each of its countries.

  The one exception is a master and a child it lists, in either direction: they are one structure, not competing
  rights.

  Unknown region ids are refused.

**Why the master lists its children.** If the child declared its parent first, the child would need the master's scope
to already contain it, while the master would need its children modelled before it could cover their countries.
Neither could be written first. With the list, HQ writes the master, naming the children, and then each child names
its master.

### 5. Development commitments, and actuals kept apart

- **Targets:** `targets_json` holds one entry per year, each with a period and optional minimums for revenue
  (cents), cohorts, active members and network. A minimum left out is null: no commitment, never 0.
- **Actuals:** `licence_performance_observations` is append-only. Each row is a metric, a period, a value and a
  source. A correction is a new row that names the one it supersedes. A measurement therefore never overwrites a
  target.
- **Progress per year (`progressOf`):**
  - `met`, `missed` or `in_progress`;
  - `not_recorded` when nothing was measured, never 0 and never missed;
  - `no_target`.

### 6. A miss opens a review; nothing revokes itself

When an observation shows a completed year missing its target, one batch writes the next version with
`state = at_risk` (scope and territory unchanged) and opens a `licence_exclusivity_reviews` row
(`trigger = target_missed`). There is one open review per licence. HQ can also open one by hand.

The review is closed by one action:

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| `continue` | Back to in force; the miss stays on record. |
| `cure_period` | Stays at risk, with `cure_until` (days given, or the recorded cure period; none is invented). |
| `reduce_territory` | A strict subset of the current scope, re-checked against the overlap rules. |
| `convert_non_exclusive` | Released: the licence keeps operating, but not exclusively. |
| `end_exclusivity` | Ended today; the operating licence is unchanged. |

Each action works the same way:

1. `POST …/reviews/:rid/preview` returns the consequence in a sentence and the version it would write, and stores
   nothing.
2. `POST …/resolve` needs `acknowledge_consequence: true`, a reason and the expected revision.
3. It writes that version, closes the review and records the consequence on the review, all in one guarded batch.

A trigger keeps a closed review closed.

### 7. The agreement

A version marked `contractual` (the default) is part of the agreement. Two effects follow:

- **Merge fields.** It fills the `{{exclusivity.*}}` merge fields when a contract is instantiated:
  - scope, territory, start and end, review date and cure period;
  - each year's period and minimums.

  A minimum that is not set stays unfilled, never 0.
- **Changes.** Changing a contractual exclusivity on a licence that has an agreement needs
  `acknowledge_agreement_mismatch: true` (`409 agreement_ack_required`). The executed agreement is never edited.

The read reports:

- `agreement.state: 'mismatch'` while a contractual version is newer than the current contract. HQ clears it by
  instantiating the replacement on the Contract tab (D4671).
- Whether the terms schedule's own "Exclusive territory" (§2.1) agrees with this record. A disagreement is reported,
  never fixed by itself.

### 8. HQ screen

The Territory tab gains a "Pricing & market · territory" section with four parts:

- **The operating licence:** its fee, royalty and territory.
- **Exclusivity:** national, regional, non-exclusive or master. It shows the state and version, the parent or the
  children, and the cure date.
- **Development commitments:** Year 1 and Year 2 targets with measured progress or "Not recorded", the next review,
  each year's status, and a form to record a measured figure.
- **An open review:** the five actions, each one previewed before it can be taken, with a reason.

Editing writes a new version. It shows the agreement acknowledgement when one is needed.
