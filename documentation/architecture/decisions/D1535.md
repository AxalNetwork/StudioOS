## D1535 — Templates v1: the store, the pick and "Save as my template" (#3782, deck v2 B6)

- **Status:** built by Claude Code for slot S25, 2026-11-01. Not deployed and not checked live.
- **Numbering:** #3782 assigned D1535 and migration 725; both were free on main.
- **Builds on:** #3876's H14 contract (`deck_template/1`), D767's proposals (`pitch_deck_proposals`), the deck rubric, New deck's seven audiences and D1604's Gallery (`TemplatesGallery.jsx`).

### 1. The store (migration 725)

`deck_templates` holds:
- `uid`;
- `scope` (`hq` or `user`) and `owner_id`;
- the Gallery's metadata as columns: `name`, `description`, `stage`, `audience_tags` (a JSON list of the seven audiences), `deck_audience`, `style`, `swatch` and `card_count`;
- `structure_json` (the H14 object), `theme_json` and `provenance_json`.

### 2. What a template may hold (`services/decks/templates.ts`)

`templateProblems` is H14's mechanical check:
- the contract and uid;
- the enums, with New deck's seven audiences;
- `card_count` equal to the cards;
- catalogue cards on their `ROLE_LAYOUTS`;
- no metric value, no chart points, no image URL, no video, audio or link block, and no model id;
- every visible string passing `numberIn`;
- every bracket a placeholder;
- provenance with its origin and the attestation that nothing was copied.

A seed that fails it is never written. A deck saved by a member that fails it is refused (422 `not_a_template`).

### 3. The routes (`routes/deck_templates.ts`, mounted before `decks`)

- **List.** By stage, audience (the seven) and style.
- **Mine.**
- **One template.**
- **Apply.** It writes a proposal through D767's `proposeFill`; nothing is a version before Accept.
- **Save as my template.** `stripDeck` keeps each card's layout, structure, areas, background and decor, and each block's kind, grid, style, chart type and binding key. It replaces every text with a placeholder for its style, nulls every metric value and image, and drops chart points, notes and alt text. The theme is kept, and the scope is the caller's own.
- **Pick.** It is described in §5.

### 4. Q5, the label clash: apply has its own route

Apply writes `method_id` `tpl:<uid>`. v1's `POST /api/decks/apply-method`, its 13 method ids and its "Use this template" label stay as they are. Whether the 24 templates replace the 13 methods in the Gallery or sit beside them is #3876's seeding step and S1's Gallery to decide; the store allows either.

### 5. The deterministic pick (dk-auto AUTO-2)

`GET /api/decks/templates/pick?stage=&audience=&sector=` returns `{ template_id, reason }`.
- It searches, in order:
  1. the stage's templates tagged with the audience;
  2. the stage's templates;
  3. the audience's templates;
  4. any template.
- Within a tier, the templates are sorted by uid, and a hash of the three inputs picks one.
- The same inputs always give the same template and the reason, for example "Picked Field Letter because it matches its stage and audience (Pre-seed · Strategic)."
- It is null only when the gallery is empty.

### 6. The seed: #3876's content, not yet here

HQ's templates are `services/decks/templateSeeds.ts`, written into the table on the first read (`INSERT OR IGNORE` by uid).
- #3876 holds the 24 templates' content: the prompts, the QA, the designer's review (`provenance.reviewed_by`) and the rubric scores. It is blocked on this store.
- So the seed list is empty here, and the Gallery lists no HQ template until #3876 adds them.
- The test that every seed passes its contract and scores ≥ 80 on the rubric runs now on a synthetic fixture, and on each seed as #3876 adds it.

### 7. The client and the Gallery

- **The client.** `lib/api/deckTemplates.js` is re-exported by one line in `api.js`. The adapter's `templates`, `myTemplates` and `saveAsTemplate` are swapped, with their D1511 contracts.
- **The Gallery.** S1's `TemplatesGallery.jsx` does not call them yet. Until S1 wires its filters and Yours, it keeps them greyed with the seam's words (`NOT_WIRED`), so no chip looks live and does nothing.
