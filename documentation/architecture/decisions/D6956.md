### Boards (40 frames in 15 sections)
- **SGN-00** Journey and rules: first view to completion or retry for Email (link, passkey, code), Apple and Google.
- **SGN-01** Desktop 1440: one compact card over the scene with “Sign in to Axal VC”, then Continue with Email, Continue with Apple and Continue with Google, and one quiet Create an account link. Also the protected-destination example (“Sign in to continue to Grow”).
- **SGN-02** Phone 390 sheet: rising on arrival, at rest, closed to the artwork with a Sign in button that reopens it, reduced motion, and the protected destination.
- **SGN-03** Email method chooser: Email me a sign-in link, Use a passkey and Use an authenticator code. Opening it sends nothing. With no WebAuthn the passkey row is left out.
- **SGN-04** Sign-in link: address, conditional Turnstile, send, invalid address, sending, sent with the 60 s resend cooldown, change address, the expired-link retry, Turnstile that could not load, rate limits, and a send the queue refused.
- **SGN-05** Authenticator code: a labelled 6-digit field with Paste (a recovery code works there too), Turnstile, the recovery link, a wrong code, no authenticator yet, and no Turnstile.
- **SGN-06** Passkey: no address needed. The card waits while the browser shows its own prompt (drawn as a dashed placeholder). Cancelled, not recognised and expired each lead to Try passkey again.
- **SGN-07** Google: its own window or a redirect in this tab; signing in, cancelled, a different account, an address Google can't prove, a blocked window, not configured, rate limited.
- **SGN-08** Apple ◇: handoff and return, signed in, cancelled, failed, and a private relay address that matches no account.
- **SGN-09** Signed in, then offline, account inactive and a security check.
- **SGN-10** Phone 390 journeys (8 frames). **SGN-11** Phone 320 with the keyboard open: the field and its button stay above the keyboard.
- **SGN-12** Motion and reduced motion. **SGN-13** Focus order, return focus, keyboard, Escape and Back, accessible names, live status, safe areas and keyboard scrolling.
- **SGN-14** Handoff: tokens and components to reuse, engineering, owner decisions, frontend-only work.

### Checked against `main` (e245cd0df9)
- `pages/LoginPage.jsx`: one card holds the email field, Turnstile, Email me a sign-in link, Google, a “Passkey or authenticator code” disclosure and the recovery note, with a separate signup card (`AuthShell showApplyCard`). For a kept destination the heading is “Sign in to continue to Grow” (`lib/pendingNext.js`, #4579).
- `components/auth/AuthShell.jsx`: the background, the header, `AuthCard` and the `authV2` tokens (purple `#7c3aed`, ink `#241f38`, muted `#6b6577`, hair `#e8e6ee`, the 10 px mono label). The boards reuse all of them.
- `routes/auth.ts` magic link: a 15-minute link that works once, rate limits of 5 per 5 min per IP and 3 per 15 min per address, and a 502 when the email queue refuses. `/magic/verify` returns errors as `?magic_error=`. The page sets the 60 s resend cooldown. **The server does not check Turnstile on `magic/start`**; only the page does.
- `routes/auth.ts` `/login`: checks Turnstile and fails closed, then checks the TOTP code, then tries it as a recovery code.
- `routes/auth_passkey.ts`: sign-in options are issued with or without an address (discoverable passkeys).
- `routes/auth_google.ts`: `/config`, `/start` (redirect) and `/popup` (Google's window, D722), with the error codes drawn on SGN-07.
- `routes/auth_recover.ts`: the existing `/auth/recover` page and its layers.
- Apple: there is no Apple sign-in route or button anywhere on main.
