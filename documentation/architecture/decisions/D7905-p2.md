## D7905 part 2 — Input staging, cancellation and recovery (#7905)

Review of #7972 found two integration gaps. This part closes both.

**Input staging.** The listener writes the plan `0600` and checks out under
`UMask=0077`, so the distinct candidate UID 10000 could not read either bound
input. Before `docker create`, the trusted host runs
`setfacl -R -P -m u:10000:rX` on the source tree and `setfacl -m u:10000:r` on
the plan. That grants one UID read and traverse on exactly those two inputs.
`-P` never follows a candidate symlink to its target. The mounts stay
read-only, and nothing else of the listener's becomes readable; inside the
container the bind starts at the inputs, so their parents' modes do not apply.
`verifyIsolation` now also runs a readability probe as the candidate inside
the container, so an unreadable input fails isolation instead of failing the
tests. Readiness requires `/usr/bin/setfacl` (package `acl`) on the host.

**Cancellation.** `run` installs SIGINT/SIGTERM handling before the container
path. The candidate exec is asynchronous, so a signal or the deadline acts
mid-run: it kills the engine client, and exact-ID removal in `finally` stops
the processes inside. Cancelled exits 130, timed out 124, failed 1.

**Recovery after supervisor loss.** The exact container ID and owner are
written to `container-owner.json` in the run's own temp directory before
`start`. The record is removed once cleanup succeeds and kept if it fails. The
workflow's `if: always()` cleanup step removes a recorded container only
while the engine still reports that ID, owner label and fixed name; a missing
container counts as gone, and any other mismatch refuses but still removes
the run's files. A host that dies entirely is bounded by the container's
fixed lifetime.

**Authority composition.** `executor-authority.mjs` composes #7973's
`createReceiptReader` with the #7906 catalog resolver. URL and audience come
from repository variables, never inputs or receipts. Without them, or without
the reader module, no reader is returned and admission keeps refusing at
`receipt_authority_unconfigured`. The executor job grants no `id-token`, so
the reader cannot mint a token yet. That grant, and showing that no host-side
candidate step can reach the OIDC request variables, belong to enablement
(#7904, #7909).

Still required before enablement: the reviewed pinned image and the live
credential-denial canary on the actual node.
