### 1. Access stays as `dd.ts` decides it

Nothing here widens a read:
- **My reviews** is the caller's own sections, from `GET /api/dd/my-reviews` (D1249).
- **The reviewer's panel** draws only from a case read the Worker scoped to the viewer's sections (`case.scoped`). A deal team's read draws none of it.
- **The subject** reads only `GET /api/dd/requests/mine`, never the case.
- **Each act goes to its own route:** the NDA to `POST …/sections/:id/nda`, conflicts to `POST …/conflicts`, the answer to `PATCH /requests/:id/respond`. Each is an in-page dialog or form that says what it changes, and a refusal is shown in the Worker's words.

### 2. My reviews (DDL12a)

- **Where:** `/admin/due-diligence/my-reviews` and `/due-diligence/my-reviews`.
- **Each row:**
  - the case (name and short id) and the section, with Observer when the role is observer;
  - when the caller was invited;
  - the NDA: "On file" or "NDA missing";
  - the items pending, "Not recorded" when the read did not count them, never 0;
  - the verdict, or "No verdict yet";
  - "Invitation expired. Ask the case lead to resend it." when the link has run out.
- **Open section** opens the case on the caller's own base with the section in focus (`?section=`). A member never gets an `/admin/…` link.
- **States:** loading, unreadable (with Retry, "not a claim that you have none") and empty.
- **Layout:** a table from `md` up, cards below.

### 3. The reviewer's view (DDL12b, DDL18c)

On a scoped case, above the tabs:
- **The banner:** "You're reviewing [section] as an invited expert (or as an observer). You see your section only."
- **For each of their sections, the steps:**
  1. **Sign the NDA:** signed with its date, or Upload signed NDA, a dialog that sends the file.
  2. **Declare conflicts:** "I have no conflict" or "I have an interest to declare". A declaration needs words; the dialog refuses an empty one before sending anything.
  3. **Work the checklist and record your verdict:** "n of m items still pending", or the verdict once recorded. An observer reads the section instead and does neither.
- **The current step** is the first not done.
- **The Subject card** says the legal name and email are hidden from section reviewers.
- **After the NDA or a declaration,** the case is read again.

### 4. The subject's requests (DDL15)

**The Worker.** `GET /api/dd/requests/mine` gains two fields:
- **`project_uid`:** the venture the request is addressed to, for a startup case. It is the caller's own venture, since the route keeps only requests addressed to them. "Pick a file from your data room" reads it, and the data-room routes check ownership again.
- **`file_names`:** the names of the files already matched to the request, so the subject sees what they sent.

Neither is case material: no case uid, score, sections or findings. The test checks that, and that another account sees neither.

**The page:**
- Each request's asked and due dates. "Due 20 Oct 2026 · 4 days late" appears only while it waits on the subject; a request with no due date is never late.
- Chips filter by Waiting on you, Sent and Reviewed, each with its count.
- **The answer form** takes a note, a link or a file:
  - "Pick a file from your data room" opens a bottom sheet listing the venture's files by name;
  - a request made of a founder, investor or advisor has no data room, and says to paste a link instead;
  - an empty answer, or a link that is not http(s), is refused before sending.

### 5. The Diligence row (owner question 1)

- **Who gets it:** a partner, investor or advisor, only when they lead or review a case. `useDiligenceInvolvement` reads `GET /api/dd/cases?page_size=1`, which holds only cases the caller opened, leads or reviews. One case is a yes.
- **The read:**
  - it is made once per account;
  - a failed read draws no row, so the row never leads to a refusal;
  - a founder, an Admin or a Super Admin makes no read: Admins reach Due diligence through Approvals, and the subject never sees the case.
- **The row:** `withDiligenceRow` adds it after Research in the role's one flat group, never twice.
