### 1. What was built

**Part 2's list fields, drawn (check first: confirmed).** D991 returns `reviewed_at`, `reviewed_by { user_id, name }` and `changed_after_review_at` on every row, and takes `reviewed_by=` and `sort=reviewed`. D994 drew none of them: it was built before D991 merged. Now:
- **Reviewed** reads "3 Oct by Ada Admin". A verdict given before review dates began (24 Oct 2026, migration 557) reads Not recorded, with that reason as its tip. A profile with no verdict reads "—".
- **The changed dot** is under the status pill, amber: "● Changed after verified" (or rejected). Its tip gives the day the answers were written again (owner decision 3).
- **The Reviewed by filter** is enabled. Its choices are the admins the Worker names, then Not recorded. The list's `options` now carry `reviewed_by`.
- **The Reviewed sort** is on All profiles.
- The sentences saying these "wait for part 2" are gone.

**An Explorer's binding agreement (confirmed).** D990's list carried the Explorer's suggested role but not the Exploring board's binding agreement.
- **The Worker:** the list row's `account.binding` is `{ state, sent_at, signed_at }` for an Explorer, and null for any other account. It comes from `user_role_review.binding_sent_at` and the envelope `binding_envelope_id` names (migration 147). The envelope is signed once it is `completed`.
- **The page:** PRF1's Account cell says "binding agreement sent 2 Oct", "binding agreement not sent", or "binding agreement signed 4 Oct". No day is drawn when none was stored.

**Rows per page and Columns (confirmed).**
- **Rows per page 25 · 50 · 100** sits beside the pager on both tabs. A change starts again at page 1.
- **"Columns ▾"** on All profiles hides Source, Onboarding category, Entity, Agreement, Reviewed or Captured. Person and Status always show.
- The choice is kept in this browser only, a per-viewer convenience. It survives a failed storage read.

**Export and the menus (confirmed).**
- **The Worker:** `GET /api/admin/partner-profiles/export`.
  - **What it answers:** the list's own filters and sort, or `refs` (at most 100) within them. It returns a CSV of what the page draws: reference, name, email, status, source, category, track, entity and type, agreement and its envelope, account, binding agreement, identity check, reviewed at and by, changed after review, and captured.
  - **What it never holds:** the tax ID, a note or the chat.
  - **Formulas:** a cell that starts like a formula is written as text.
  - **Unknown values:** an empty cell, never a zero.
  - **Limits:** at most 5,000 profiles. A larger view is refused 413 `export_too_large` with its count, never cut short.
  - **Logging:** each export is logged (`partner_profile_export`, by count, never by address). If the log cannot be written, nothing is handed out (503 `export_not_logged`).
- **The header's More menu:** Export this view (the tab's filters and sort, every page), Open Approvals, and Open the Exploring board (`/admin/exploring`).
- **All profiles' rows:**
  - each row has a checkbox, and the header box chooses the page;
  - "n selected" offers Export selected and Clear selection;
  - the choice clears when the list is read again.
- **Each row's "⋯"** holds Open, Reopen for review and Export. Reopen shows only on a verdict.
- **Reopen opens PRF5d's dialog:**
  - "Put [name]'s profile back in the queue?", "The verdict is kept in its history.";
  - an optional reason;
  - "Nobody is told. Reopening is recorded in the profile's history under your name.".

  It calls D991's `POST /:ref/reopen`. A refusal is said in the dialog, in the Worker's words, and the dialog stays open.
- **Outcomes are said in the page:** "Exported 3 profiles. The export is recorded under your name.", "Back in the queue.", or the Worker's sentence.
