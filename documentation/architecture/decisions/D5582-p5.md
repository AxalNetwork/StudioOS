## D5582 part 5 — A frozen deck export pins the media it embeds (#5582)

Part 4 (D5582-p4) froze a deck's approved head into a D5585 manifest and left out the D5583 media a slide embeds. This
part closes acceptance item 2 for decks: a frozen export pins the exact approved deck revision **and** the exact media
revisions it includes, and a later edit of either needs a renewed approval. It extends part 4's
`POST /api/admin/workgraph/drafting-assets/:assetUid/freeze`, behind the same gates (an HQ preview deployment,
`WORK_GRAPH_DRAFTING=preview`, a Super Admin, the write bar and the verified company). No new route, table, column,
binding or switch. No migration was needed: the manifest already pins components and records their edges.

**How a deck names a media version.** A card carries `workgraph_media: { asset_uid, revision_uid }` on an image block,
on its background or anywhere else in the card. `services/workGraphDraftingMedia.ts` reads these refs from the deck's
units, the same units whose digest the person approved, so the pinned media are exactly what that approval covered.
Changing a ref is a deck edit: the domain row no longer reads as the head, the freeze is refused `stale_revision`, and a
person re-adopts it (part 4). A ref with any other shape, or one revision named under two assets, is
`embedded_media_malformed`. Two revisions of one asset are `embedded_media_ambiguous`, because a manifest pins one
revision per asset. More than 31 is `embedded_media_too_many` (the manifest's 32 components, less the deck).

**What is checked before anything is cut.** One D1 read covers every ref, and R2 is asked for each object's head only
after every ref passes. Bytes are never read.

| Code | Status | When |
|---|---|---|
| `embedded_media_not_found` | 404 | no such revision, or one of another tenant, company or project (a company-wide asset of the deck's company is allowed). All read the same, and no foreign object is asked of R2 |
| `embedded_media_revoked` | 409 | the revision is offline (D5581's flag), an applied D5585 takedown covers that revision, or its author's account is revoked (`users.is_active = 0`) |
| `embedded_media_unreadable` | 409 | not an `image`, `brand` or `model_3d` asset; tombstoned; a live `bytes_missing`, `bytes_deleted` or `residency_mismatch` flag; not WorkGraph-held at its typed key; or R2 has no object there, or one whose size, checksum or digest metadata differs |
| `embedded_media_stale` | 409 | the revision is no longer its asset's accepted head |

Each refusal records the freeze audit row with its code and ids, and writes nothing else.

**Pinned beside the deck.** Each media revision becomes a component of the same `cutManifest` call, with role
`embedded_media`, at its digest (the SHA-256 checksum of its bytes). The manifest body pins it and records a `component`
edge, and the answer lists each pin: asset, revision, kind, digest, byte length, media profile, object class and the
deck units that embed it. One image embedded twice is one pin. The audit row lists `asset:revision:digest` for each.
D5585 still applies its audience rule and the media's `marketing` profile, so their rights and claims attestations
remain publication's gate.

**A media edit needs renewed approval.** A new master or a selected variant moves the media asset's head. Through the
existing machinery, with no new code:

- The advance's impact walk names the frozen manifest.
- D5585's status reads the pinned media `reapproval_required` (`superseded`), which refuses its publication.
- The same deck cannot be frozen again (`embedded_media_stale`) until it embeds the new revision and a person adopts
  that deck version, which records a new approval of a new digest.

**Idempotent.** A retry under the same key replays the same manifest, digest and pins, and writes nothing. The same
key with different pins is D5585's `idempotency_conflict`.

**Coordination with #5583.** `workGraphMedia.ts` and `workGraphBlobs.ts` are unchanged. This part reads the media
version identity (`work_graph_revisions`, `work_graph_assets`, flags) and uses the blob service's exported `blobKey`,
`parseBlobKey` and `isWorkGraphBlobSource` helpers. Signed streamed delivery does not touch it.

**Not in this part.**

- Offers and templates embed no media, so their freeze is unchanged.
- An external-editor media revision (D5579) cannot be frozen: it is `embedded_media_unreadable`, because WorkGraph
  cannot check those bytes.
- A licence revoked or expired inside a revision's provenance is not re-read here. The recipe keeps no revocation date,
  so D5585's rights attestation and takedowns remain the gate.
- Taking a pinned revision offline, or an author's revocation after the freeze, does not change the frozen manifest's
  status. Takedowns already show there.
- Nothing writes `workgraph_media` into a deck yet. The deck editor's embed action, the frozen-export screen and the
  adopt/confirm actions wait on the design brief #8833. There is no client method.

Tests: `cloudflare-worker/test/work_graph_drafting_media_5582.test.ts`, on migrated D1 with the in-memory R2. Seven
tests cover:

- two agents' variants of two images, a person's picks, and a freeze that pins exactly those revisions and digests;
- a media edit after the freeze: the impact, `reapproval_required`, the refused re-freeze, then a re-adopted deck that
  freezes the new revision;
- an idempotent re-freeze and the conflicting key;
- media of another tenant, company or project refused as absent, with a company-wide image allowed;
- offline, taken-down and revoked-author media, and a refused takedown that revokes nothing;
- missing and swapped bytes, a tombstone, a non-media asset, an external-editor source, and malformed and ambiguous refs;
- ref extraction across units, and a deck without media.

Part 4's suite is unchanged and passes. Mutation check: 24 mutations of the service and the freeze route, 24 caught.
