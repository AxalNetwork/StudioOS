## D8731 part 2 — Investor Studio's Funds and Usage & quota actions open the heading they name (#8731)

Part 1 is `D8731.md`: the walkthrough, the canvas (`design/incoming/Studio Home Investor Page.dc.html`, boards SIV00 to SIV20) and the owner's questions. This part builds it.

- **Status:** built 2026-11-27. Frontend only; no Worker route, migration or API method changes.
- **Owner answers (2026-11-27):** option A on every open question. Q1: no commitment opens Axal VC Fund › Fund and terms. Q2: Usage & quota is "Compare investor plans" → Pricing › Investor. The fund-name question had no lettered options; see **Left out**.

Run my fund (`/funds`) is no destination of either card. The Funds card counts `lp_holdings` only; when Fund is open to the member (the same Institutional gate `App.jsx` uses for `fundUnlocked`) the card says "Only your commitments as an LP are counted here. A fund you run is under Fund." The no-commitment card always says it.

Every card link and the Needs you link carry `FROM_STUDIO` (`{ from: 'studio' }`) as router state (`StudioModuleTile` passes `spec.linkState`).

### Usage & quota reads

`InvestorQuotaBars.jsx` no longer falls back to default caps with 0 used. `quotaView(billing, intros)` takes each figure from the read that has it (tier from either, intros from either, deal rooms from the billing read only) and leaves it null otherwise; `QuotaBarsView` draws a null as the Unreadable chip. The billing read failing with the intro read answering adds "Your plan status could not be read · Try again"; both failing draws "Usage & quota could not be read · Try again" (role alert) and no bar. Try again refetches both. The compact card (Studio's tile, its only caller) drops the inner "Compare plans" link; the full card keeps it. A billing answer without `dealroom_used` is Unreadable, no longer 0.

### Destinations

- `SpinoutLabLpCommitment.jsx`: "My commitment" is an `h2` (`#my-commitment-heading`, `tabindex=-1`, `data-section-heading`) and the section is labelled by it. Unreadable keeps "Your LP position … This is not a claim that you hold no commitment." with Retry.
- `FundOverviewSection.jsx`: "Fund and terms" is a visible `h2` (`#fund-heading`, same attributes) with "The fund brief, open to every investor"; the section is labelled by it.
- `SpinoutLabLpWorkspacePage.jsx`: after the hash scroll, `focusSectionHeading(target)` focuses that heading with `preventScroll`.
- `StudioBackLink.jsx` (new): on a phone, when the router state says a Studio card opened the page, "‹ Studio" (44 px) is drawn first, before the h1, on Axal VC Fund (`InvestorWorkspacePage.jsx`) and Pricing (`PricingPage.jsx` `MemberPricing`). It goes one step back through history, so Studio returns where it was left; with no history it links to `/studio`. At desktop the browser's Back does it, and nothing is drawn.
- The authorization checks are unchanged: the LP workspace route guard, `/pricing`'s guard, the Worker's reads, and the preview, which withholds both cards and draws no link.

### Departures from the canvas

- **Usage & quota while loading keeps its link.** SIV-7 D draws "Loading quota records…" with no action. The tile cannot see the bars' own loading state without lifting their reads into the home, and the destination needs no read (SIV-11 says the same of the unreadable state), so the link stays.
- **Phone means below 1024 px.** "‹ Studio" follows the app's `PHONE_QUERY`, as every phone header does, so it also shows on a tablet; the canvas draws 390.
- **The Funds unavailable line** is SIV-17's "Not a claim that you hold no commitment."; SIV-2 E's second sentence, "No link until the read answers.", describes the board and is not drawn. Likewise SIV-7 C's "No bar is drawn, so nothing reads as 0 used."
- **The GP line on a linked card** shows when Fund is open (Institutional, in good standing), the gate `App.jsx` uses; nothing on the client says whether the member actually runs a fund, so an Institutional LP with no fund of their own also sees it.
- **The phone card links** are made 44 px tall by a rule scoped to the two tiles (`investorHomeModules.css`), not by changing `.sm-link` for every role's Studio.
- **"Linked to this account"** is not drawn: the canvas shows it only with the ◇ claimed-row count from `lp-portal`, which does not exist ("Without it, the line is not shown").

- **The private member-state clicks** await #8688; sidebar labels are #8683.
