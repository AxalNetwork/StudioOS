## D4416 — Portfolio belongs in the Account menu (#4416)

The owner's decision on 2026-11-04 amends D4152 §3 (decision row 3). The companies and projects Portfolio at `/account/portfolio` appears in the Account menu immediately above Billing, including the phone Account sheet. It is no longer injected after Studio or linked at the sidebar foot, in expanded, collapsed or HQ shells.

The investor holdings Portfolio at `/portfolio` remains in the role navigation. The phone company sheet retains its existing Portfolio link. Settings scope, routes and permissions do not change. No Worker change or migration is needed.
