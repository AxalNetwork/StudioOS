### 1. The rules (`components/scoring/scorecard/scorecardModel.js`)

Nothing here computes a score. The model turns what the Worker returns into what the canvas draws.

- **`scopeOf`:** one scope as the Worker sends it (`scope`, `state`, `band`, `confidence_word`, and after the NDA `value`, `coverage` and `inputs`).
  - A scope that is not `scored` carries no value, band or confidence, even if a figure reached the page.
  - The Worker's `not_read` (never stored) is "Not scored yet". "Not read here yet" stays for a block the page does not read.
- **`scopesOf`:** always the eleven scopes in their one order. A scope the Worker did not send is unread, never empty.
- **Coverage:** counted from the inputs when the read carries them: the inputs recorded, out of those that apply. A personal input that is recorded counts. Without inputs, the Worker's share is shown as a percentage.
- **`inputOf`:** what an input shows.
  - `value`: a recorded figure, its points, words, grade, date and weight.
  - `counted`: a personal input. A band, "Counted, not shown", never its figure or words.
  - `missing`: Not recorded, which lowers confidence, with its reason.
  - `out`: does not apply, or not expected yet, with its reason.
  - `unreadable`.
- **`gradeMix`:** counts the grades behind a scope's inputs. A missing input counts as Not recorded. Counting is not scoring.
- **`panelOf`:** the deal read as one state: `loading`, `not_on_deal`, `no_access`, `not_found`, `unreadable`, `locked`, `masked` or `open`. One scope unreadable is listed, and the rest stand.
- **`stripOf`:** one row of the batch.
  - `closed`: the batch left the deal out, because the reader may not read it.
  - `locked`: the founder's switch is off.
  - `unreadable`.
  - `open`: the eleven scopes.
- **`batches`:** at most 200 deal ids a call, D1256's limit.

**The strip's bar.** The batch sends a band and a confidence word, never a number (D1256 §1). So with no value, a bar's height is its band's middle: Strong 85, Fair 55 and Weak 20, drawn 17, 11 and 4 pixels tall (`BAND_HEIGHT`). A bar never claims a value the reader was not sent.

What draws no bar at all:
- not enough recorded (a dashed line);
- not expected yet (a dot);
- could not be read (a hollow mark);
- not scored (a hairline);
- a locked or closed strip (an outline).

None of them is a bar of zero.

### 2. The panel (`Scorecard.jsx`, SC1)

`VentureScorecard` takes `dealId` and `ndaHref`. The deal page's Scorecard tab passes both; the NDA door is the deal's Documents tab, as DL4–DL6 already use.

- **The header:**
  - the strip;
  - Model v1.0, or the live version the read names;
  - when the scorecard was last computed (UTC);
  - How scoring works.
- **Eleven scopes at a glance:** each value on 0–100. Opacity is confidence. Not enough recorded is a dashed line.
- **Scopes:** one row per scope.
  - The columns: band (Risk in risk words), value, a 0–100 bar, confidence (three dots and the word), coverage and the grade mix.
  - A review in force sits under the scope's name. The computed value is unchanged.
- **What this rests on:** the inputs across the eleven scopes, by grade, with each grade's meaning.
- **"Your weighting"** keeps D1253's wait for an investor; it is part 5's (SC7).
- **The canvas's six-month sparkline and Eadwyn's "Questions to ask"** are not drawn: no store holds either. The trajectory is part 5's (SC12).

### 3. The drawers (SC2–SC5, SC22)

A scope opens in the shared `Lightbox` (D685): in-page, Escape and the backdrop close it, focus is held inside, and on a phone it is a bottom sheet (`phone="sheet"`, SC19b). It reads nothing and changes nothing.

- **The head:** band, value, confidence and coverage. Risk says that a higher value means lower risk. A scope with no value says why.
- **A review in force,** with "The computed value stays as it is."
- **Each input:**
  - its value and points;
  - its words;
  - its grade, with Stale when it is past its window;
  - as of, and its weight;
  - for staff, how many records it was read from.
- **The other kinds of input:**
  - a missing input reads "Not recorded · lowers confidence" with its reason;
  - one that does not apply is muted, with its reason;
  - a personal input is a band and "Counted, not shown".
- **Product-market fit (SC22)** groups its inputs as D1143 does: Who you heard from, The problem, Claims and experiments, Demand and pull.
- **Add review (SC5, SC10)** is part 5's dialog. Staff see reviews here, read only.
- **Masked rows open nothing:** before the NDA the read carries no input.

### 4. The states (SC15–SC18)

| State | What the panel draws |
|---|---|
| Loading (SC15) | The panel's shape, "Reading…", no figure |
| One scope unreadable (SC16) | That row: "Traction could not be read. The other scopes are current." and Try again, which reads again; every other scope stands |
| Off the deal (SC17, `403 not_on_deal`) | "This scorecard opens when this venture is in your deal flow." |
| No access (SC17, Explorer, `403 no_access`) | "Scorecards are for investors, founders and studio staff." and Go to your home |
| Locked | "Scorecard locked" and the Worker's reason |
| Unreadable | Unreadable with Retry; "That is not a score of zero." |
| Masked before the NDA (SC18) | The NDA banner and Sign NDA (the Documents tab); each row's band and confidence with the value locked "After the NDA"; no chart, no grade mix, no input; What opens after the NDA |

None of them draws a scope row, a strip or a number.

### 5. The strip (SC6, DL24a)

- **Deal flow's table** (`DealFlow.jsx`): after the deals load, `useScopeStrips` sends one `POST /batch` for their ids, 200 a call.
  - Each row's `DealStrip`: an open strip; an outline saying "Scorecard locked: not open to you" for a deal the batch left out; the founder's reason when it is locked; or "Could not be read".
  - The batch failing says so once under the table, with Retry.
  - The table's key replaces D1253's wait.
- **The deal's Overview** (`DealOverview.jsx`, DL24a): `DealScoreStrip` reads the batch for the one deal and links to the Scorecard tab.
  - A deal the reader may not read says when it opens.
  - A founder, whom the batch refuses, is pointed to Raise › Scorecard.
  - `dealFrameModel.SCORE_SLOT` is gone.
- **Hover and focus:** each strip is focusable, and reads as text ("Team: Fair, medium confidence; …"). A locked strip says nothing per scope.
