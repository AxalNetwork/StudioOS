## D4820 — Admin › Community v2: attention first, honest empty states, four authoritative KPIs (#4820)

- **Status:** canvas landed 2026-11-07 (`design/incoming/Community v2 Page.dc.html`, `design/incoming/Community v2 · Boards.dc.html`, boards CM01 to CM12e). Not built. The build is a separate task.
- **Builds on:** D930, D931, D932, D933 (the live overview and its reads), D918 (Next up).
- **Migration:** none.

### The question

The live overview is honest (unreadable is not zero, privacy-withheld is distinct from zero), but it leads with eight measures and renders a full analytics wall even when Community is empty. The browser audit of 2026-11-07 found the empty state's message sitting above zero funnels, twelve "none" event types and twelve weeks of zero bookings.

### What the canvas decides

**Four authoritative KPIs:** Waiting on you · Upcoming events · Event registrations · Job applications. Active members is not promoted: it is not recorded. Show-up rate moves into Events, circles and the roster into Network, and wellbeing experts into Wellbeing.

**The decision queue sits directly under the KPIs** ("Needs your attention"), ranked overdue first, each row opening Approvals. When nothing waits it collapses to one line: "Nothing needs your attention · Approvals are currently clear."

**Insight before chart.** Every chart card leads with one plain sentence. The current week is drawn hollow and labelled in progress; an unrecorded week is a gap marked `n/r`, never a zero.

**One View details (analytics lightbox) and one Manage → (console) per domain.** Consoles is renamed Manage Community and compressed into six destination rows.

**Not recorded metrics** collapse into one Additional insights row that expands to explain each.

**Empty and first-activity states.**
- A genuinely empty Community replaces most of the analytical body with Build your community (Create an event / Create a circle), the clear-queue line, Manage Community and one Additional insights line.
- With first activity, each module shows what it has or the next action; charts wait for meaningful variation.

**Partial data:** one unreadable source changes only its own block (the KPI, the card, the chart series, the participation basis), each saying so.

**Phone:** its own hierarchy. 2 × 2 KPIs, the queue, the next event, an activity summary with a View chart button, secondary analytics as disclosure rows. Detail lightboxes become full-screen pages.

### Data

- Every figure maps to `GET /api/admin/community/overview` and `/activity` (D931, D932); each block answers alone.
- Show-up is attended ÷ registered over events that ended in the period. Registrations for upcoming events are shown separately so the totals add up.
- Check-ins stay privacy-thresholded ranges; no mood, stress or check-in answers and no member names.

### What the canvas does not decide

Sample figures are illustrative. The threshold for "enough activity for a trend" (drawn as two weeks with activity) is a product call to confirm.
