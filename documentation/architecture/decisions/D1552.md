### 2. What it checks

`services/decks/critique.ts` is pure and runs on every card except the appendix:

| Check | How | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Density | a text block longer than its role's `DENSITY_MAX` (a paragraph 280, a heading 70, …) | Shorten |
| Fit | a text within that which still overflows its area at its smallest step (D1517's `fitText`) | Shorten |
| Contrast | the theme's ink on its ground below 4.5:1, once for the deck; unread is no finding. Today every theme passes by construction (`themeTokens` keeps a brand's colours only at 4.5:1 or more), so this is a guard | Change it in Theme |
| Image-style drift | a picture drawn in another style than the deck's art direction (`deck_images.style`) | Match deck style, on the picture |
| Vision (DK9) | each rendered card, read as at most three layout notes (crowding, alignment, hierarchy, contrast, legibility) | none: noted |

Density is read before fit because `textCapacity` caps every area at the role's most, so a text over it never fits either. Calling it density names the real problem: it says too much, not that the box is small.

**The vision pass.** DK9 is "Slide design feedback" in `ai/modelUseCases.ts`, D760's catalogue through the "Vision and checks" group. `CRITIC_MODELS` is read from that job (its default, alternative, deeper, then its other choices, each one HQ offers); no model id is written in the critic. It runs `rail_vision` on each card, rendered by the deck's own print page through the `BROWSER` binding (1280 × 720, at most 20 cards, three at a time, under a two-minute deadline). It runs only on a model of another family than the deck's writer (#3804's rule, `criticModelFor`). The writer is Body's element model (D1503). The rule holds for the model that answered too, so after a fallback to the writer's family the answer is not shown. Without the binding, or without a model of another family, the pass does not run and the reply says why. An answer that cannot be read is no finding, never "clean".

### 3. The score

`critiqueScore` is rubric v0 less what the critic adds: density 3 each, contrast 10, vision 4 each (at most 20), style 3 each (at most 15). Fit adds nothing, because the rubric already takes 15 for each overflow. With no added finding the score is the rubric's. Each card also gets its own score.

### 4. Quote, run, proposals

- **`POST /api/decks/:id/critique/quote`** prices the blocks to shorten (one `rail_write` run each, `deck:shorten`) and the vision pass (one `rail_vision` run a card), and runs nothing. The ⋯ dialog shows "Holds up to $x · you pay what it uses". With no block to shorten the price is null, never 0. With AI off it answers `{ ai: false, reason, message }`, and the free checks still run.
- **`POST /api/decks/:id/critique`** runs the free checks, the vision pass, then one Shorten per block. Each shortening is checked before it is kept:
  - it may name no figure, link or address the text did not (`wordingProblem`);
  - it must fit its area. It is never cut to fit, because words cut mid-sentence are not a fix.

  A shortening that passes is kept as a rail proposal on `deck:<id>` (`deck:shorten`), with the payload a card's Shorten leaves (`deck_id`, `card`, `card_uid`, `block_id`, `action: 'shorten'`, `before`, `after`, plus `source: 'critique'`). So the Ask's "Waiting for you" sheet (`DeckAsk.jsx`, `cardEdit`) offers it with Accept and Accept all, and nothing is on a card until Accept. Anything else is "Left for you", with why. The reply carries:
  - the score now, and the score once the fixes are accepted;
  - "What we fixed";
  - each finding (never the words a fix works from, nor a model id);
  - the vision pass's families, or why it did not run;
  - what it charged.
- **Who.** The deck's writers only (`projectOwned` with write). Each run holds and settles on its own, as every press on the deck does, and the quote is their sum. An AI-off or budget refusal stops the remaining runs.

### 5. AUTO-5: the critic inside the one-click run

`autoCritique(slides, deps)` is the service the one-click run (#3768) calls in-run:
- it scores the deck;
- while the score is under `QUALITY_THRESHOLD` (75, as suggested on the issue), it shortens what crowds or overflows;
- it scores again, at most `MAX_PASSES` (2) times;
- it returns the fixed slides, the final score, whether it met the threshold, "What we fixed", and every finding still on the deck, met or not.

The caller passes its own `shorten` (under the run's one AUTO-1 hold), the price of one fix, and what the critic may spend in all. A fix runs only while it fits in what is left, and each is counted at no more than its quoted price, so a pass never spends past the quote. Nothing is saved or changed in place: the run places the slides as its proposal. Each pass works from the words the last one left.

### Not built here

- **Image-style drift corrected in the run.** It is found and reported, with "Match deck style" on the picture (D1544's edit). Redrawing inside AUTO-5 waits for #3768's run, which holds the image budget.
- **The vision pass inside AUTO-5.** The service takes the findings a caller adds. Rendering a card inside a generation run waits for #3768.
- **A quality threshold the owner sets.** It is `QUALITY_THRESHOLD`, 75, a constant until the owner names a setting.
