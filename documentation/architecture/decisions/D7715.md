### Decisions

1. **The import sends a body once.** The sheet reads the builder's body in the browser and keeps nothing. Before anything is sent it refuses only what the Worker refuses whole: not that body, 0 or more than 500 rows, a country with no revision named. Each row's fields are the Worker's to judge, in its words. Once the Worker answered with a write (200 or 207), or no answer came (no status, or a 5xx), the page holds the file's text and will not send it again: a 207 names the countries that changed since the file was built and says to read the catalogue again and rebuild. The memory is the page's, in memory; after a reload a resent body meets the revisions it already moved, so the Worker answers 207 and writes nothing for those countries.
2. **A refusal wrote nothing and is said in the Worker's words.** Import: the sentence, its rows renumbered from 1 as in the file with their country, or the countries with no revision; the reason is kept; the file is chosen again before anything is sent (no Send again). Record: the sentence on top, each field's problem under its field (`aria-describedby`), focus on the first field in error, the typed values kept. A stale revision (409) says the revision now and offers to read the country again; the sheet then names the new revision.
3. **A failed send changes nothing on the page.** Only a write reads the page again: the catalogue and the check after an import, the market and the check after a record or a status change, with the new history row first and focused.
4. **The expected revision is the read's.** Record observation and Change a field's status send `profile.revision` from the country read on screen; the sheet says "You are changing revision N of Country".
5. **Provisional and Not recorded are the sheet's second mode (Q9 A).** Provisional only where a value exists and is not already provisional, never for a rank; Not recorded where a value exists, after a confirm step listing what is cleared and saying the history is kept; a Not recorded field offers only Record a value.
6. **A value to check is flagged from the #7714 read (Q8 A).** On the country, each input row says "matches history" or what the finding is (no history row, differs and how, the frozen snapshots holding it) with Record observation… on that field; on the catalogue, a chip and a Check column, only when something is found. Nothing found says the countries and fields checked; a failed check is Unavailable, never "all consistent".

### Departures from the canvas

- The catalogue keeps the D5020 columns (regulatory class, tier and index, initial fee) and adds Revision, and Check when there is something to check; the canvas draws a shorter table.
- The country's inputs keep the D5020 three rows (the rank on the score row, the reference with the source); the canvas draws four rows with a Reference column.
- The Import reason is filled from the reason the builder wrote, to be read and kept or changed; Import stays off until the reason is 3 to 500 characters.
- "Revision now" after an import is the revision read plus the rows written, what the Worker wrote, not the re-read catalogue's.
- When no answer came back to a record or status change, the sheet says "No answer came back from the Worker", not "Nothing was saved": a lost answer is not known to have saved nothing. A 5xx is treated as no answer.
- Phone 390 is the same sheet as a bottom sheet (`phone="sheet"`), scrolling; the canvas's separate "Rows in the file" sheet (LCD-06 B) and the "Reference and reason ›" disclosure (LCD-06 D) are not built.
