## D1151 — Deal Team, Market and Traction reads

Issue #3056, part of #2421. S20, s31 Codex. No migration, new binding or
production data access. Production implementation is the Worker; no page layout
changes in this part.

`GET /api/deals/:id/team`, `/market` and `/traction` mount before `deals.ts`.
The D1149 `userMayReadDeal` rule runs first: staff and partners keep the existing deal reader,
a founder reads their own venture and an investor needs an invitation or
commitment. A room request alone grants nothing. Deleted projects are refused.
Every response is private/no-store. The NDA-exempt roles are explicitly admin
and a founder reading their own venture. Partners retain their canonical deal
read grant, but that grant does not waive the NDA for these new sections. Tests
assert the full response for admins/owners and the mask for partners before the
NDA, followed by their unmasked response after signing. Every other admitted
role, including investors and partners, needs the canonical
founder-to-investor pairwise NDA, active and unexpired. Invalid expiry fails
closed. Before that, the response contains only mask metadata and project name,
sector, stage and headline; it never queries the section's stores. Query strings
cannot grant access. Errors use fixed words, never database messages or figures.

The company-switcher scope guard records this route in its existing deliberate
exceptions: the read is keyed to an explicitly selected deal through D1149, not
a founder-wide venture list. Its founder-ID predicates resolve team membership
and the NDA party; they are not an active-company listing scope. Switching
companies does not choose or grant a deal. The exception keeps the guard's
existing stale-entry assertions and the canonical access tests intact.

Team includes active founder accounts and accepted, non-removed project members,
subject to their profile visibility and public name/background preferences.
Private profiles, invited members and inactive accounts are omitted. Public
background exposes only prior role/company and dates. Archetypes need an
explicit published flag and the latest snapshot for that person's persona;
private traits, individual skills and values never leave the route. The team
coverage projection follows the eight radar axes and its self/peer blend,
returning only coarse bands of the team mean (covered at 3/5, partial at 1.5/5,
otherwise gap), where two visible members with published background contribute
to that axis. Exact maximums could identify one person's skill; neither a raw
score nor a per-person band is returned. Hiding background removes that member
from the aggregate. Corrupt career history is Unreadable for that person's field
only; other people and coverage remain readable. Corrupt publication preferences
withhold only that person and mark publication Unreadable, preserving the other
profiles and their coverage rather than defaulting the corrupt person public. Missing or single-person axes are null with a reason, not zero.
SQL returns only aggregates, not individual skill rows. This deliberately avoids
the radar helper's read-side schema bootstrap. Full-time dates and published
founder-market-fit evidence have no recorded source here and stay Not recorded.

The requested team values band stays null/Not recorded: main has no recorded
consent to share a cofounder team's compatibility band with an investor. Archetype
publication is not such consent. This does not read private values to manufacture
a band. The SCORING sharing store in #3279 is a separate, unfinished dependency;
this part neither invents its schema nor implements its writes.

Market reads stored TAM/SAM/SOM, general market sources, non-archived customer
segments (who pays), recorded company markets and founder-kept competitors.
Stopped competitors and unaccepted AI candidates are omitted. Existing TAM/SAM/SOM
columns have no figure-level provenance: their source is null with the reason
"No source recorded for this figure". A general market-source URL is never
represented as the source of a specific number. Stored zero remains zero; absent
values remain null. Company markets are not presented as customer geographic
revenue distribution or a geographic risk score. Incorporation addresses, KYB,
private interview content and personal customer records are not read. Each
market block fails independently as Unreadable/Try again.

Traction uses submitted portfolio updates with an explicit all-investors boolean
or an investor recipient grant to this caller. The NDA does not create a sharing
grant. This same safe projection applies to staff/founders; no private-snapshot
bypass. Only the update's whitelisted, sourced figures leave the read: MRR,
growth, customers, burn and runway. No legacy unsourced KPIs, bank balances,
private metrics snapshots, financial models or Mercury calls. Genuine zero is
preserved. The newest shared submission per period is authoritative, including
an empty revision. A rolling twelve-calendar-month history (UTC) uses Not
reported with an empty figures list for missing/unshared months. ARR and NRR
stay null with reasons: MRR is never annualised and no supported shared NRR store
exists. Raw shared metric sources/as-of dates are preserved by the canonical
`updateFigures` projection.

Owner decisions relayed on #3056 permit bands/confidence after invite/commit and
inputs after the NDA; coverage may be visible before the NDA. This task's stricter
explicit test contract masks all three sections before the NDA. Investor evidence
items will join when PIPE-RAISE's investor read lands, as the owner directed;
these endpoints report that dependency rather than exposing a founder-only read.

The D527 `dealInsightsApi` client forwards each section without inventing defaults.
The later UI part can consume recorded, Not recorded, Not reported, masked and
Unreadable states without treating a failed block as an empty venture.
