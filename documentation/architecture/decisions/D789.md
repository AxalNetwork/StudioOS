## D789 — Usage overview and Cost console

Issue #2214, part of D785 (#2210). Canvas: `design/incoming/Usage Page.dc.html`, B1 (`screen: 'usage'`) and B2 (`screen: 'cost'`).

### Behaviour

- `/account/usage` is the six-card overview: spend this month, token balance, daily model cost, input/output tokens, billing/limits and recent invoices. Each card links to Cost, Billing or Limits. The account navigation has Overview, Cost and Limits.
- `/account/usage/cost` reads D787 by UTC range and group. Range totals (charge and D805's token/audio/image costs) cover all models. Model selection narrows the chart and model table; page/option tables and CSV explicitly remain all-model views. Reset restores month-to-date, Model and All models. CSV uses the existing authenticated download.
- Charts use the exact `days[].by_model` segments. Empty days remain on the axis. Hover, keyboard focus or tap reveals a day's labelled model amounts. Input/output lines show recorded counts, break on missing values, and label the last day's values. `frontend/src/lib/modelColours.js` is the single colour map, ready for D791's HQ chart. Canvas models retain their seven colours; other IDs hash stably into that categorical palette. Labels accompany every colour.
- Loading, failed reads, missing fields and empty reads remain distinct. Partial charge sums, missing output counts, other-model cost and the 10,000-run cap are stated. No company identifier is added to a read.
- The same cards stack on a phone; the Cost table becomes labelled two-column rows. Every surface carries dark-mode foreground/background/border classes.

### Nothing removed

The old allowance (including paused/held states), top-up purchase state/history, 30-day days, where-it-went Page/Task/Charged views, model counters, rates, last-run receipt and storage all move into Cost's open detailed section. Their existing components and tests stay. The overview's old `#days`, `#where`, `#models`, `#last-run` and `#storage` links forward to the same Cost anchor. Billing and Limits keep their own components and ownership.

### Dependencies and visible gaps

- D787, D788, D794, D805 and D807 are on main.
- **D806 (#2245) is still unimplemented.** Its endpoint/client are owned by S20. Recent invoices has a renderer for its documented rows, safe HTTPS invoice links and authenticated order PDF downloads, but does not request an unmounted endpoint or substitute the incomplete orders list. It visibly states why the combined list is Not recorded. The final API wiring remains on #2214; this PR is Part of, not Closes.
- D787's `groups` return runs, tokens and charges but no group cost or non-token units. Those cells say Not recorded with a reason. Model rows and range totals retain the available fields. Group charge completeness is not returned either, so no claim of complete grouped charges is made.
- The canvas stays in incoming: Billing, Limits and HQ have separate tasks, and the invoice dependency is pending.
