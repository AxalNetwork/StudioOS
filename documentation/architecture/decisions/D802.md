## D802 — HQ bills a licensee: annual licence-fee invoices with a PDF, then a revenue share per stream and the platform-licence royalty

- **Status:** filed by S1 for the owner (2026-10-20) as #2238, part of D795 (#2228). Built by S26. **A money path: S1 reviews it for security before the owner merges.**
- **Canvas:** the Licences canvas (`design/incoming/Licences Page.dc.html`), boards L1, L2 and L4.
- **Built in two parts**, as the issue allows:
  - **This PR: the annual licence-fee invoices** (the issue's part 3) and HQ's console list (part 4).
  - **Next: the per-stream shares and the royalty stream** (parts 1 and 2). These read D797's terms columns (#2230, PR #2242), which are not on `main`. They come after #2230 merges.

### 1. The decision

HQ invoices each territory licensee's annual fee, one invoice per licence year:
- **The amount and currency** are the ledger's `annual_fee_cents` and `currency`.
- **The period** runs from the licence's anniversary.
- **The PDF** is drawn as D716's invoice is.
- **Payment** is recorded by hand, as a statement's is (D.8).
- **The Administrator** reads their own invoices beside their statements.

### 2. As built

1. **Migration 513, `licence_fee_invoices`.** It has the issue's columns, and four more:
   - `reference`, the payment reference, stored so the list and the PDF agree;
   - `figures_json` and `figures_sha256`, the invoice as printed and its digest, so tomorrow's PDF is today's even after the fee is re-termed;
   - `void_note`, the reason for a void.

   A unique index on `(licence_uid, period_start)` keeps one invoice per year. A voided invoice still holds its year.
2. **The licence year** (`licenceYear`, `services/licenceFeeInvoices.ts`):
   - It runs from the anniversary of `territory_licences.starts_on` to the day before the next one.
   - 29 February falls on the 28th in a common year.
   - The invoice covers the year that holds the day it is issued. Before the licence starts, it covers the first year, invoiced in advance.
   - The label is `2026–27`.
   - The reference is `AXAL-LIC-<licence_ref>-2026-27`, through D798's `licenceFeeReference`.
3. **Issuing** (`POST /api/admin/licences/:uid/fee-invoices`):
   - **Auth:** Super Admin with a TOTP session and a recent step-up (`requireSuperAdminWriteBar`).
   - **Refusals** (409):
     - `invoice_exists` (with its uid) while the year is invoiced;
     - `no_annual_fee`;
     - `no_start_date`;
     - `licence_not_billable`, for any status other than active or suspended;
     - the payment refusal, when no Holdings account carries `territory_licence_fee`. An invoice is never issued without payment instructions.
   - **The due date** is D797's `payment_terms_days` after issue, defaulting to 30 days (`paymentTermsDays`); D797 is not on `main` yet.
   - **Audit:** `licence_fee_invoice_issued` in the admin audit log.
4. **Recording** (`PATCH …/:invoiceUid`), with the write bar, as on a statement:
   - **A payment:**
     - `paid_cents` is the running total received, never above the amount;
     - `paid_note` is the bank's reference;
     - the status is `paid` at the full amount, else `issued`.
   - **A void:** `{ status: 'void', void_note }`. It needs nothing paid and a reason of 10 characters or more.
   - **After a void**, nothing more is recorded on the invoice.
   - **Audit:** each write is audited (`licence_fee_invoice_payment`, `licence_fee_invoice_void`).
5. **The PDF.** The generated-document type is `territory_licence_fee_invoice` (`fundNotices.territoryLicenceInvoice`).
   - **The paper:** D716's invoice paper (`noticePaperInput`, `paperModel`, `renderLegalPdf`), drawn from the frozen figures and stamped as issued.
   - **The sender:**
     - Holdings, the platform IP's licensor until D797's `licensor_ip_entity` records another;
     - another licensor is refused with D798's `licensorRefusal`;
     - the addressee is the licensee's legal entity.
   - **The figures:** invoice date, licence, licence year, amount due (in the licence's currency) and due date.
   - **The payment block:** D798's `territory_licence_fee` purpose, masked to the last four.
   - **The type** joins `NOTICE_TYPES` and the Templates catalog with a specimen. The generic `/api/fund-notices` routes refuse it (409 `issued_from_licence`): an invoice is issued only from its licence, which holds one per year.
6. **The Administrator's read:**
   - `GET /api/licence/mine/statements` (D798) gains `fee_invoices`, newest year first, without HQ's notes. A failed read costs only the invoices, with `fee_invoices_reason`.
   - `GET /api/licence/mine/fee-invoices/:uid/pdf` prints one of their own licence's invoices; another licence's is 404.
   - Both are scoped through `licence_admins`.
7. **HQ's console:** the "Annual fee invoices" tab on a licence (`pages/admin/LicenceFeeInvoices.jsx`, words in `licenceFeeInvoicesModel.js`, calls in `lib/api/licenceFeeInvoices.js`). It has:
   - the fee and the anniversary;
   - Issue this licence year's invoice;
   - each invoice's year, reference, dates, amount, what was received and status;
   - the PDF, Record payment (the total received and a note) and Void (with a reason).

   The controls are plain; D801 (#2234) restyles them from board L9 if needed.

### 3. Departures, for review

- **Parts 1 and 2 wait on D797** (#2230). The issue allows building the invoices first.
- **Four columns beyond the issue's list:** `reference`, `figures_json`, `figures_sha256` and `void_note` (§2.1).
- **Migration 513 is out of order on disk.** Main reached 516 (D812) before this task; 513 was this task's and is free on every branch.
- **Recording takes the write bar.** The issue says "as statements do", and statements take plain Super Admin. A recorded payment is a money write, so this asks for the step-up.
- **Only the current licence year** (or the first, before the start) is invoiced. A later year waits for its anniversary; an earlier one cannot be invoiced from here.
- **Two guards widened:**
  - D716's specimen guard skips this type, which the generic preview refuses; `licence_fee_invoices_d802.test.ts` holds its specimen to a real invoice.
  - The type counts in D711's and D758's guards rise by one.
