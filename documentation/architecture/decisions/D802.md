## D802 — HQ bills a licensee: a revenue share per stream, the platform-licence royalty, and annual licence-fee invoices with a PDF

- **Status:** decided by S1 for the owner, 2026-10-20 (#2238; part of D795, #2228). From the Licences canvas (`design/incoming/Licences Page.dc.html`, boards L1, L2 and L4). A money path: S1 reviews it for security before the owner merges.
- **Basis:** D797's branch (#2242), which this stacks on, over `main`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.

### 1. A share per stream (`services/statements.ts`)

- `drawStatement(streams, shares, currency)` takes either one rate (the H5 draw, unchanged: rounded once over the total) or `ShareTerms`: `fallback_bps` (the ledger's `territory_licences.revenue_share_bps`) and D797's `share_subscriptions_bps`, `share_licence_fees_bps`, `share_token_margin_bps` and `royalty_platform_licence_bps`.
- With per-stream terms, **each stream is rounded once, on its own gross at its own rate**, and the statement owes the sum. A stream's share is a term of its own, so its cent is settled where its rate is.
- A null stream share falls back to the ledger's, and the row records it: `share_bps`, `share_source` (`stream` or `fallback`) and `owed_cents` travel in `streams_json`. `other` has no share of its own and always falls back.
- `subsidiary_statements.revenue_share_bps` keeps the fallback, so the column means what it always meant: the ledger's share at the draw.
- **The draw route reads the schedule** (`routes/admin_statements.ts`). A licence with no schedule draws every stream at the fallback and records no royalty. A schedule that cannot be read refuses (503 `terms_unreadable`): drawing at the fallback then would bill a rate the terms may have replaced, without saying so.
- The H5 refusals stand: no draw for a licence that is not active or has no ledger share, none past draft, and `recorded_above_owed` when a redraw would leave more paid and disputed than owed.

### 2. The royalty stream

- `platform_licence_royalties` (`ROYALTY_STREAM`) is **derived**: its gross is the licence-fees stream's gross, owed at `royalty_platform_licence_bps` (`§4.7`). It is not a stream a branch reports, so it is never in a usage report and never in `STATEMENT_STREAMS`.
- **Not recorded is not zero.** With no royalty in the terms, the row reads "Not recorded: …", has `share_bps: null` and `share_source: 'not_recorded'`, and owes 0. It is not counted in `unreported`: that counts figures a branch failed to send, and this is a term HQ never set.
- With a royalty but no usable licence-fees figure, the row is unavailable with a reason and owes nothing; the licence-fees stream is already counted as unreported once.
- The statement's `gross_cents` does not add the royalty row, which would count the licence fees twice.

### 3. Annual licence-fee invoices (migration 513)

- **`licence_fee_invoices`:** `uid`, `licence_uid`, `period_label`, `period_start`, `period_end`, `amount_cents` (> 0), `currency`, `issued_at`, `due_at`, `status` (`issued`, `paid`, `void`), `paid_cents`, `paid_at`, `paid_note`, `void_reason`, `issued_by_user_id`, `recorded_by_user_id`. A partial unique index on `(licence_uid, period_start) WHERE status <> 'void'` holds one live invoice per fee year, so two concurrent issues cannot both land.
- **The fee year** (`feePeriod`) runs from an anniversary of `starts_on` to the day before the next, labelled "2026–27". A 29 February start falls on 28 February in a common year. A date before the start gives the first year.
- **Issuing** (`POST /api/admin/licences/:uid/fee-invoices`, Super Admin with a TOTP session and a recent step-up):
  - the amount and currency are the ledger's `annual_fee_cents` and `currency`, copied at issue and never recomputed;
  - the period is the fee year holding today, or the one `period_start` names (it must be an anniversary);
  - it is refused while a live invoice holds that year (`period_invoiced`), and for a licence that is not active, has no annual fee or has no start date;
  - due `payment_terms_days` after issue, 30 when the terms record none; a schedule that cannot be read refuses rather than guessing.
- **Recording** (`PATCH …/:invoiceUid`, the same bar) is HQ-entered, as statements are (D.8). `paid_cents` is the total received, a whole number of minor units, never above the amount, with a note; the full amount marks it paid. Voiding needs a reason of ten characters or more and no payment on it. A void invoice takes nothing more.
- **Every issue, payment and void writes one audit row** (`logAdminAction`: `licence_fee_invoice_issued`, `_paid`, `_voided`). `licence_events` is not used: its event CHECK would need a table rebuild for new names, and these are ledger entries, not changes to the licence.
- The routes are their own file (`routes/admin_licence_fee_invoices.ts`), mounted at `/api/admin/licences` before `admin_licences.ts`.

### 4. The PDF (`territory_licence_fee_invoice`, `services/licenceFeeInvoice.ts`)

- D716's invoice layout, built as its own paper input rather than an eighth fund-notice type: a territory licensee is not a fund party, and the fund-notice store, issue flow and LP reads must not learn to hold one.
- **The sender** is D797's `licensor_ip_entity`, Holdings when not recorded.
- **The payment block** is D798's `territory_licence_fee` purpose, masked to the last four digits (`paymentBlock`). A licensor that is not that purpose's payee prints D798's refusal sentence (`licensorRefusal`) and no account, so an invoice never pairs one entity's name with another's bank.
- **The reference** is `AXAL-LIC-<licence_ref>-<period>`, with the period's dash as an ASCII hyphen ("2026-27"), because a bank's reference field is not where an en dash survives.
- Rendered on demand through Browser Run with no fallback, as D716's notices are: 503 when it cannot be drawn, never a lesser page. A void invoice is not printed (409).
- It is the eleventh entry in the Templates catalog's "Generated by the platform" group (`GENERATED_DOCUMENTS`), with a bracketed specimen that reads no account.

### 5. The Administrator's read

- `GET /api/licence/mine/statements` adds `fee_invoices` (newest first, each with `pdf_url` and `payment_reference`) and `fee_invoices_reason`. It reads through the caller's own licence (`licence_admins`), never a licence named in the request. A read that fails is `null` with its reason, never an empty list.
- `GET /api/licence/mine/fee-invoices/:uid/pdf` prints one of them; another licence's is 404.

### 6. HQ's console

- "Annual fee invoices" on `/admin/licences/:uid` (`pages/admin/LicenceFeeInvoicesPanel.jsx`, `lib/api/licenceFeeInvoices.js`): each invoice's period, date, amount, status and PDF; Issue (naming the fee year, disabled with the reason when it is taken); Record payment and Void. Plain controls; D801 may restyle them from board L9.
- Amounts are typed as text and read into minor units without a float (`parseMinor`); anything inexact is refused, never rounded.
