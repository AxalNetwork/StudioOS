## D6039 part 2 — Company-level compliance events as a filing reminder source, one reminder per pack item (#6039)

Part 1 is `D6039.md` (the reminder dates, recipients, ledger and the 05:35 UTC
sweep). This part adds the third source S1 asked for on 2026-11-15 and S35
handed off from #6713 on 2026-11-22 (`D6713.md`).

**The source.** A `compliance_events` row with no project (`project_id IS
NULL`, a `company_id`) whose `completion_status` is `pending` is eligible on
the same two days as a filing: 14 days before its due date and on it. A
completed or snoozed event is not reminded. Project-level compliance events
keep their own reminders (`growDaily.ts`, the days set on the obligation) and
are not read here.

**The recipients.** A company has no venture founders, so a company event goes
to the company's active admins: a seat whose role is Owner, Admin or Founder,
or the primary admin. These are the people who may change and close a company
compliance task (D1614). A plain member and an inactive user get nothing. The
link opens the company's compliance tasks.

**One reminder per item.** A registered v2 case with a venture writes each
pack item as a formation step and, when dated, also as a company event, both
carrying the same `pack_item_key` (case/form/item). The step is the venture's
record of the item, so an event whose key is on a formation step of a live
venture is left out, even when the step is done. The database holds the same
rule: a step or event with a pack item key first claims (`pack_item_key`,
recipient, due date, reminder day) in `filing_reminder_pack_claims`, naming the
source row, and in the same D1 batch writes its ledger row only while that claim
names it. A reminder is sent only when the ledger row is new. So an item that
arrives from both sources, a rerun and a concurrent run each send it once.

**The schema is additive (the owner, 2026-11-26).**
`202611260037_6039_filing_reminder_company_claims.sql` creates two tables with
`IF NOT EXISTS` and changes nothing else. `filing_reminder_deliveries` keeps
main's shape, and steps and obligations keep writing there.
`filing_reminder_event_deliveries` is the company events' ledger, keyed
(`event_id`, recipient, due date, reminder day), with `company_id`,
`pack_item_key` and `sent_at`. `filing_reminder_pack_claims` is the
cross-source claim. An earlier draft rebuilt `filing_reminder_deliveries` in
place. The release-window rehearsal (D8532) classed its DROP as manual recovery
with no forward fix, so it never merged.

**The pack flag.** `formationPack` reports `reminders: true` for a dated item
when the read names a venture or a company, so a registered company-only case's
evidence no longer says it has no reminder. `D6713.md`'s "Company-only items
return reminders:false until #6039 reads compliance_events" ends here.

**Not built.** "Add to calendar" beside a filing's reminder (drawn in #6038's
brief) is separate scope. A company event's own record page does not yet list
the reminders sent for it; the filing record's Reminders section reads venture
filings only.
