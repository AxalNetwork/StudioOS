**Issues #1107 and #1108, slot S01, 2026-10-06.** Worker first. There is no
migration and one new route, `GET /api/community-tasks/rewards`.

The owner approved the Eadwyn Tickets canvas on 2026-10-06
(`https://claude.ai/artifact/7sLCsbcdBAqK8XuaYVtzcA`). It was drawn from the
prompt on #1107. It is a board, `Eadwyn Tickets.dc.html`, and the component
its artboards import, `Eadwyn Tickets Screen.dc.html`. Both are committed in
`design/canvases/integrated/`, since all four of its screens are built here:

### What was true on main

- **D544** (#1107) built the review store and its Super Admin routes. **D545**
  (#1108) built the members' task list. Both pages waited on this design.
- **A list that could undercount.** The review lists returned the oldest 200
  waiting and a `count` of what came back, so a queue past the 200th would
  show a short number.
- **No issue number on tasks.** A suggested task carried its GitHub link but
  not its issue number, though a feature request carried both.
- **No paid amount on suggestions.** A member's suggestions said "approved"
  without what the grant paid.
- **No member-readable rule amounts.** No member could read the reward
  amounts the canvas states (`credit_reward_rules` is the Super Admin's).
- **A preset type in the chat.** The chat's ticket form preset the type to
  `task`. D544 made the Worker refuse a missing type, and D604 gave the Help
  Center's form no default; the chat's form still sent `task` for anyone who
  did not change it.

### The change: the Worker

- **The review lists say how many are waiting.**
  - `GET /api/admin/ticket-reviews/features` and `/tasks` return `total`, a
    count of everything waiting, beside `count`, what the page holds.
  - A task now carries `github_issue_number`.
- **Each approved suggestion carries `reward`.** This is the credits its grant
  actually paid: the ledger row D544's approval writes, keyed `task:<id>`,
  read the way D542 reads a bounty.
  - `revoked` says the grant was taken back.
  - `credits: null` says nothing was paid (the rule was off, or the grant was
    refused).
  - `credits_unreadable` says the ledger could not be read, and the list is
    still served.
  - A pending or declined suggestion carries `null`.
- **`GET /api/community-tasks/rewards`** gives every signed-in member the four
  amounts the panels state: `task_approved`, `bug_high`, `bug_medium` and
  `bug_low`. Each is `{ credits, active }`, or `null` when the table has no
  such rule.
  - The feature rules are left out, since feature requests earn nothing
    (#1102 Q2 A).
  - A failed read is a 500 refusal (`rewards_unreadable`), never zeros.

### The change: T1 and T1b, `/hq/review` (`pages/hq/ReviewQueuePage.jsx`)

- **Who and where.** The page is the Super Admin's (`hqOnly`, as D544's routes
  are). The sidebar's Support row lights on it, and HQ Support links to it.
- **The tabs:**
  - Feature requests, and Suggested tasks (`?tab=tasks`);
  - each tab counts the number waiting;
  - the count is withheld while its list loads or cannot be read, never 0.
- **The toolbar:**
  - a title search, and Priority and Date filters;
  - "1–25 of [N]", where N is the number waiting, or the number matching once
    a filter is on;
  - when more wait than the 200 loaded, a line says so.
- **Order.** The most pressing come first, oldest first within a priority, as
  the canvas draws them.
- **An item shows:**
  - its title, priority and type, and its text;
  - who asked, with the date;
  - its GitHub issue, or "Not on the GitHub tracker".
- **A task also has:**
  - the "Publish to" audience (D544's six, in the Worker's order);
  - "Approve · +N credits", with N read from the credit rules. When the rule is
    off, absent or unreadable, the button says only "Approve", and a line under
    it says why.
- **Approve and Decline open the canvas's confirm panels on the item they
  decide.**
  - Decline says the user sees the reason.
  - Neither panel sends until the reason is long enough.
- **After a decision,** the card shows what the route did, and the tab's
  count drops:
  - the GitHub label, or why there is none;
  - whether the requester was told;
  - for a task, the credits paid, or the refusal in the grant service's own
    words.
- **A 409** says someone decided it first, and asks for a reload.
- **The rail.** The page carries HQ's Worker AI rail, like every HQ page.

### The change: T2 and T3, the Eadwyn chat (`components/advisor/EadwynPanels.jsx`)

- **Three tabs head the chat's panel:** Conversation, Tasks and My tickets.
  - **Beside the chat,** on a desktop and in fullscreen, they head the rail.
    Conversation keeps the rail it always had: the interview's sections and
    queue.
  - **On a phone,** they sit above the card. Conversation is the chat itself,
    as the canvas hides the chat column there.
- **Tasks (T2):**
  - the approved community tasks, with Start;
  - the member's suggestions, each with its state: Pending review,
    "Approved · +N credits" (what the grant paid), "credits taken back", or
    Declined with the reason;
  - older suggestions, one request away;
  - "Suggest a task", which opens the ticket form on Task;
  - "No tasks yet" only for an empty list; a list that could not be read says
    so.
- **My tickets (T3):**
  - the balance, from `/perks/mine`, linking to Perks. One that could not be
    read is withheld, never 0.
  - the five most recently active tickets, each with type, priority, and
    bounty (D542's words) or review (D604's badge);
  - a link to all tickets;
  - what a fixed bug earns by tier, from the rules.
- **The chat's ticket form has no default type.** It opens on Task only from
  "Suggest a task". Its submit stops, naming the field, while no type is
  picked.

### Where the build departs from the canvas, and why

- **Approve asks for a reason.** The Worker records a typed reason of at least
  ten characters with every decision, approvals included (D544). The approve
  panel says the reason is recorded and not sent to the requester.
- **"Asked by" is a name and an email, not a link.** No page exists for one
  account; the canvas's `/admin/accounts/[id]` does not exist.
- **Start puts the task to Eadwyn.** It writes "Help me with …" into the
  composer, which the chat reads as a request for an explanation, and the
  member sends it. The canvas's `/tasks/[id]` does not exist, and no store
  records who started a task.
- **A duplicate's original and a not-a-bug reason are in the ticket's
  replies.** The ticket list carries neither, so the row says so and links to
  the ticket. Reading every ticket's GitHub thread for the list was not worth
  one call per row.
- **The bounty note is by tier, not by priority.** The canvas says "Urgent and
  High bugs earn +150"; the tier is set by whoever grades the fix (D542), not
  by the ticket's priority. The amounts are the rules' today, never code's.
- **The review page carries the Worker AI rail,** which the canvas does not
  draw, as every HQ page does.
