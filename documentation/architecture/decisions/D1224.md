### 2. The five steps, as the canvas draws them

The sheet's eyebrow says "New call · step n of 5", and its title is the step's name.

**CLS4, the round.**
- Name, Target amount with Currency USD, Instrument (SAFE, Convertible note, Equity), the instrument's own key terms, Minimum cheque, Target close, and "Terms in plain words".
- **Each seeded field says where it came from** until the founder changes it (`seedChips`):
  - "From your round settings": the active round's target and close;
  - "From your round planner": a planner amount;
  - "From your recorded terms": `term_seeds`.
- The minimum cheque says "Typed by you. Eadwyn never fills an amount, a term, a cap, a discount or a minimum cheque."
- Save draft writes the draft and links its address. Next saves and moves on.

**CLS5, materials and audience.**
- **Pitch deck:** the linked version, "frozen when sent", with "Change version".
- **Data room:** "Investors see it only after an NDA and your grant", a link switch, and "Open Data room ›".
- **From your pipeline:**
  - every verified prospect with "Verified on Axal" and a checkbox;
  - every prospect not on Axal (D1222's `not_on_axal`), greyed, its checkbox disabled, with "Not on Axal" and "Invite to Axal" (§3).
- **Matching investors** are a switch, "Also invite investors whose published focus fits", with "[n] matching investors · stage · sector · cheque range" and "Their names stay hidden from you until they respond."

**CLS6, preview.**
- **What investors see:** "A private round call from [venture]", with Instrument, Target, Key terms, Minimum and Closes, then the plain-words terms and "Deck v[n] · data room after an NDA · indicate an amount · ask a question".
- **Readiness:** "[n] of [m] ready", each missing item with Open, and "You can send with items missing. Investors see what is there."
- **Counsel review, in its four states** (`counselState`):

  | State | Says | Button |
  |---|---|---|
  | Approved | "Approved for these terms on [date]." | none |
  | Requested | "Requested [date]. Waiting for counsel." | none |
  | Cleared | "Your changes cleared the approval." | Request again |
  | Not requested | "Not requested." | Request counsel review |

  The Worker clears counsel's approval on any saved change to the terms (`saveDraft`). The sheet says Cleared after such a save, and so does the read when the last request was for other terms.

**CLS7, send and its result.**
- "Send [round] to [n] investors?" and "[a] chosen from your pipeline and [b] matching investors."
- Each item missing from the call has its own "Send anyway without it". Send the call is offered only when counsel approved these terms and every missing item is ticked (`canSend`).
- "What happens": each chosen investor is told on Axal; matching investors stay nameless until they respond; and counsel's approval date.
- **The result:** "Sent. [n] investors were told on Axal. [k] could not be told." with "See who", which opens the call, and "Investors are told inside Axal. No email is sent."
- A count the Worker did not return is never read as 0: the result says Axal could not read it.

**A refusal keeps the entry.** At every step a refused write stays on that step with the Worker's sentence, and keeps the fields, the chosen investors and the acknowledgements.

### 3. Departures from the canvas

- **The plain-words terms are not Eadwyn's.**
  - **What the canvas shows:** a "Proposed by Eadwyn · from the fields above" card.
  - **Why it is not built:** no Worker route proposes call terms, and the house rule is Worker first.
  - **What the sheet does instead:** it drafts the words from the fields themselves (`plainTerms`) and says "Drafted from the fields above". The draft restates only figures the founder typed or a record filled, and is null until every one is set. Accept writes the words and nothing else (`acceptPlainTerms`), so no money term is ever proposed (#2431 `money_term`).
  - **The follow-up:** an Eadwyn wording needs that route.
- **"Invite to Axal" is disabled.**
  - **Why:** no route invites a pipeline prospect.
  - **What it says:** "Invite to Axal: not offered here yet. Axal has no route that invites a pipeline investor; they can sign up and verify, then be chosen."
  - **The follow-up:** a route.
- **"Could not be told" names no one.** The send answers counts only, so "See who" opens the call, whose recipient drawer is part 4's (#3224).
- **"Open full preview"** is not drawn. The investor's page is part 5's.
- **Counsel review sits on the Preview step** as the canvas places it. The old sheet had it on Send.
