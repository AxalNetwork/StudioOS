### 1. What the inbox asks for

`GET /api/work-graph/releases/pending` keeps `pending`, the publish asks of part 10, unchanged. It gains
`renderPending`: the manifests asking this person for a render approval, newest first, at most the read's limit.

A manifest of the scope asks when all of these hold:

- the person did not author it;
- it pins a piece of a render kind (video, image or brand) whose revision is still its asset's head, so a render can
  still build on it;
- the piece is under a profile the person can decide (an authority they hold now approves it);
- the person has no live `render` decision for that manifest and profile (their latest render row is absent or a
  withdrawal);
- no applied takedown covers it.

Each item carries:

- `manifestUid`, `lineAssetUid`, `digest`, `audience` and `authorUserId`;
- `pieces`: the render pieces, each with asset, revision, kind, role and profiles;
- `profiles`: the profiles to decide;
- `authorities`: the person's authorities that approve each profile.

It never carries a body. The authors are named in `people`, only for people linked to the company, as part 11 names
the publish asks' authors.

**Published manifests ask too.** A publish ask ends once the line's pointer names the manifest. A render approval is
about spend, not publication, so a published manifest whose render piece is still the head keeps asking.

### 2. How it is answered

On the existing route, `POST /manifests/:manifest/decisions`, with `purpose: 'render'`:

- **Approve:** needs `render_ceiling_microusd`, otherwise `approval_incomplete` (part 15).
- **Reject or request changes:** answers the ask.
- **Withdraw:** asks again.

A publish decision does not answer a render ask.

Once approved, `renderApprovalFor` (part 15) finds the approval when #5586's admission is asked to admit a render
built on that piece.

### 3. Why not a lane of the HQ Approvals board

The Approvals board (`approvalLaneRegistry.ts`) is the studio's and HQ's own queue, read by admins (D5879). A render
approval is a company member's decision under a release authority (D5585-p8), which is the reviewer's inbox of part
10. A lane there would put another company's release decisions in front of studio staff who hold no release
authority. So the ask goes where the decision is made.

### 4. Cost

Each read adds at most three statements:

1. the manifests (at most `REVIEW_LIMITS.scan`);
2. their render pieces' current heads, in one statement;
3. the person's live render decisions.

Then one takedown read per manifest that would ask. A read with no authority, or with no render piece in view, stops
after the first statement or none.
