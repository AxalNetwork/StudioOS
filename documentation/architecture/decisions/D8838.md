## D8838 — Deals › Closing: the investor's Open my closing room (#8838)

The D6158 canvas (`design/incoming/Deals v2 Closing Workspace Page.dc.html`,
section DX22, board DCL24; dark DX28 B) gives the investor one primary action
in the closing workspace: Open my closing room. D6866 and D6866 part 2 left it
unbuilt because "its room has no route". That was out of date: the room is
the deal page's Closing tab, `/deals/:dealId/closing`, which renders
`ClosingRoom.jsx` for every role and whose `SUB_PAGES.closing` already admits
an investor. This decision builds the action on that route. It is frontend
only: no route, Worker or schema change.

**Files.**
- `frontend/src/pages/investor/deals/closingWorkspaceModel.js`:
  `myClosingRoomHref({ role, userId, line, dealId })`.
- `ClosingWorkspace.jsx`: `OpenMyClosingRoom`, drawn under the Your line row.
- Test: `frontend/test/deals_closing_my_room_8838.test.mjs`.

### Who sees it

- **Only an investor, and only on their own line.** The line's
  `investor_user_id` must be the signed-in user's id. The closing read already
  gives an investor their own lines only (D1215), so this is a second check,
  not the only one. A line with no investor user, or no signed-in id, draws
  nothing.
- **Never an Admin or an Operating Partner.** Neither has a Your line row, and
  the helper refuses any role but `investor` even if one were drawn.
- **The target** is `tabHref(dealId, 'closing')` from `pages/dealPageModel.js`,
  so the action and the deal page agree on the path. A deal id that is not a
  positive integer draws nothing rather than a link to `/deals/`.

### Where this departs from the canvas

- **Placement.** DCL24 puts the button at the right of the workspace header.
  Here it sits under the Your line figure, because it is drawn only when the
  investor has a line of their own; an investor without one has no room to
  open. On a phone (390px) it is full width with a 44px target; from `sm` it
  is its own width, and 32px tall from `md`.
- **The room's look.** The room it opens is `ClosingRoom.jsx` as D1213 and
  D1218 built it. Its Deals v2 restyle to the #6188 canvas
  (`design/incoming/Deals v2 Terms and Closing Room Page.dc.html` and its
  Boards) is not part of this decision.

### Verified

`deals_closing_my_room_8838.test.mjs`: the helper's cases, the rendered link,
and the workspace mounted with stubbed reads for an investor on their own
line, an investor on another's line, an Admin and an Operating Partner.
Mutation-checked: 12 mutations, 11 caught, 1 equivalent (dropping the
investor gate on the Your line row; the helper still refuses every other
role).
