### 1. Each tab, three ways

Each tab reads its own section once. The answer is drawn as one of:

- **Masked:** the viewer has no NDA on file with the founder (D1151). The tab shows only the company's name, sector, stage and headline, says "This opens once you sign the NDA", and links to Documents, where the NDA is signed (D1153). No section is drawn.
- **Unreadable:** the read failed. "… could not be read. That is not the same as none." with Retry, which reads again.
- **Open:** every block carries its own state, `recorded`, `not_recorded` or `unreadable`, and is drawn from it alone. **One failed block never blanks the tab.**

Staff and the venture's own founder are NDA-exempt in the Worker. A founder has no Team, Market or Traction tab on the deal page (D1148), so this reaches staff and investors.

### 2. Team (DL4)

- **Each person as they published themselves:** name, initials, role in words, archetype or "Not published", prior roles "as published" or "Not published". Prior roles the read could not parse say so for that person only.
- **Full time since and founder-market fit** are the read's "Not recorded" with its reason. Nothing is invented.
- **Coverage is a band per skill axis:** Covered, Partial or Gap. It is **never a number**, because the read returns only coarse bands of the team mean. An axis with fewer than two published members is "Not recorded" with the read's reason. The gaps are named in one line ("Gaps: Finance.").
- **The co-founders' values band** is the read's "Not recorded": there is no recorded consent to share it.
- **Advisors on this company** is "Not recorded": no per-deal advisor read exists.
- **Unreadable publication choices** are said in one line: those members are not shown, which is not the same as none.

### 3. Market (DL5)

- **TAM, SAM and SOM:** the figure in dollars, with its source chip, or the read's reason for none ("No source recorded for this figure"). A missing figure is "Not recorded". A recorded `0` stays `$0`.
- **Who pays** (customer segments), **Where** (the company's recorded markets, and the read's line that customer geography is not recorded), **Market sources** and **Competitors** (name, why it matters, price and depth position; a missing cell is "Not recorded").
- **A market source is a link only when it is `http` or `https`.** Its card says a source backs the market overall, never one figure above it (D1151).

### 4. Traction (DL6)

- **One line says how many of the last twelve months the founder shared.** A month not reported is a gap, never a zero.
- **The tiles:** MRR, Growth, Net burn, Runway and Paying accounts, each the latest shared figure with its source ("Typed · the founder's own figure" for a typed one) and "As of" date. A figure not shared in twelve months is "Not recorded". ARR and Net revenue retention are the read's own "Not recorded" ("MRR is not annualised").
- **Three charts:** MRR by month (a line), Net burn by month (bars), Paying accounts by month (a line).
  - **A month not reported is a gap, never a point at 0.** The line breaks, and a single missing month between two reported ones is bridged by a dashed line labelled "Not reported". A bar chart leaves the month empty, marked "–".
  - A month that reports some figures but not this one is a gap in this series.
  - **A recorded 0 stays 0.**
  - Each chart has a one-sentence summary for screen readers and a "View as table" with each month's figure, or "Not reported", and its source.
  - The canvas's dotted previous year is not drawn: the read returns twelve months.
- If the history block fails, the tiles and charts are replaced by "could not be read". The evidence slot and the scorecard rows stay.
