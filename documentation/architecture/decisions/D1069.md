### 1. What the plan reads

| Read | Drawn as |
|---|---|
| D1065 `GET /api/cycles/build/plan` | the strip, every module's items, the brief, the verdict states |
| D1071 `GET /plan/word` (founders only) | Eadwyn's wording, in the cards |
| D1067 `GET /plan/since` | VB13's new evidence |
| the Validate overview (D1063) | "What Validate proved" |

- **Only the plan read can blank the board.**
  - A failed plan read is "The plan could not be read. This is not a claim that there is nothing to build."
  - A 404 is VB20a: "This plan belongs to the venture's founders."
  - A failed wording or "since" read is its own block. The plan still draws.
- **An admin never asks for the wording.** It is a founder's (D1071 §1), and its route answers an admin 404.
- **The plan read now carries the verdict.**
  - `planVerdict` is in `services/verdict.ts`, the only file that reads the Verdict's tables (D693). It reads the scope's newest unsuperseded decision: its word, state, day and evidence count, and never its reasoning or a name.
  - `latestDecision` is unchanged, because D1065 hashes what it answers.
  - A partner's read has no `verdict` key and no `decided_at` (D693 decision 7). Their shape is as D1065 left it.
- **A plan prepared before this has `prepared_at`.** It is `build_plans.created_at`, which migration 581 already wrote. `decided_at` is the proposal's `updated_at` once it is decided, and null while it is proposed.

### 2. What is drawn

- **VB1's strip:**
  - the Spin-Out Lab pill (VB20b, from `user.spinout_lab_active`);
  - "From your Verdict: Proceed · 12 Oct · 6 pieces of evidence", for founders only, and only for a given Proceed or Pivot;
  - "Prepared 12 Oct, 14:05 UTC · rules only, no AI cost";
  - the read's own counts: proposed, accepted, dismissed. A withdrawn item is none of them.
  - Update the plan and Pause the plan.
- **Each module** shows "Name · N proposed" and "Accept all in Name". Its items are drawn in this order: proposals, then decided, dismissed and withdrawn.
  - **A proposal card:**
    - its kind and status pills, the title, and what it says (a description, a severity, a test's method and target, "From your commitment");
    - the "Because" chips, each with its source word (Recorded, Typed, Document). Signals are counted into one chip, never listed;
    - "Lands in Module › Place", D1066 §1's table. A commitment lands in Next. A door lands nothing; it links to Connect.
    - Accept, Edit and Dismiss.
  - **An accepted item** is one line, "Accepted 12 Oct · Solution design › Features", with Open to its module.
  - **A dismissed item** is struck through, "it will not be proposed again", with no Open.
  - **A withdrawn item** reads "A newer decision replaced this plan." and takes no action.
  - **A module the read answered with nothing** says so, after a real read.
- **The brief** (Solution design) shows For, Problem, Promise and Replaces from D1065's brief. It is the plan's snapshot, not a Build record, so it takes no Accept.
- **VB3's states:** Proposed; Proposed and worded by Eadwyn; Edited and accepted; Accepted; Dismissed; Withdrawn. A write the module refused shows "Solution design could not save this: [the Worker's sentence]" with Try again, and the item stays Proposed.
- **VB4, Edit and accept.** The item opens in its kind's own fields, D1066 §2's list and values (Impact is `added_value`). The evidence is shown and cannot be removed.
  - Only the fields changed are sent, so an untouched item reads accepted, not edited.
  - A blank title is refused before sending. A refusal stays in the dialog.
- **VB5, Eadwyn's wording in the cards.** The worded title is in lavender, under "Eadwyn's wording · not saved", with the rule's title beneath.
  - Keep wording on an item is its Save (D1066's PATCH with the wording's id). The brief's goes to the wording row.
  - D1071's panel keeps the press, the cost, the refusals and the Lovable prompt. Standalone it still lists the rows; on the plan the cards carry them.
- **VB2a.** A given, current Proceed or Pivot's Verdict record declares a `plan-build` link (`/build/plan?project_id=N`, with `&scope=h-<id>` for one claim), only for the venture's founders (`ownsVenture`).
  - The record draws it once, as the lavender panel: "Plan the build from this evidence. N proposals are ready."
  - N is the plan read's own count. There is no count when that read fails, and none when no plan exists.
  - A withheld decision, a Stop, a superseded decision and an admin get no link.
- **A one-claim plan.** The plan's address keeps `scope=h-<id>` (D1067 §1), and every read and write passes it. A link that leaves the plan keeps only the venture.
- **VB13.** "4 new pieces of evidence since this plan, 1 contradicting.", with "No accepted item is changed by itself. Open it to decide." and Update the plan.
  - A plan prepared before D1067 counts nothing. It says the evidence changed and that how many is not recorded, never 0.
- **VB14:**
  - **Withheld:** "Your verdict is withheld: 2 of 5 pieces of evidence. You can still plan, but Raise will show the plan as resting on withheld evidence." With no plan yet, it offers Plan anyway.
  - **Stop:** "Your verdict is Stop. The plan is paused. Accepted items stay. Record a new decision to resume." The page shows no Accept, Update, Pause, Resume or wording, and accepted items stay listed.
  - **A plan the founder paused:** the page says so, with Resume the plan.
- **VB16, an admin:** the "Read only" pill, "Founders accept their plan. You can read it." and "If the owner allows admins to accept, Accept appears here." The page shows no Accept, Edit, Dismiss, Accept all, wording, tools, Open, or "From your Verdict".
- **VB17, nothing accepted:** with no plan, the founder is offered Prepare the plan. If D1065 answers `no_evidence`, the page reads "Nothing to plan from yet." with Record a session and Open Validate.
- **VB18, loading:** the header and tabs are drawn at once, the strip reads "Reading…", and three cards read. No count is shown before it is read.
- **VB21, phone:** each module is an accordion, the first open. Every button is 44px.

**Every change is said in an in-page dialog before it is made:** Dismiss, Accept all, Pause, Resume, Update the plan, Prepare and Plan anyway, and Edit and accept. Accept itself is the card's own press, as the canvas draws it. Nothing lands until Accept.

### 3. Not built here, and why

- **VB19a's "one module could not be read":** the plan is one read, so a module cannot fail alone. A failed wording or "since" read is drawn as its own block instead.
- **VB13's per-item "Its evidence changed":** no read says which accepted item's evidence changed. D1067's `/since` is the plan's whole count, so only that is drawn.
- **VB3's "Undo · 10 s":** D1066 has no undo for Dismiss, and a dismissed item is never proposed again. The dialog says so before it is done.
- **VB2b's "From your plan · N" on the Build overview's tiles:** the door stays as D1063 drew it.
