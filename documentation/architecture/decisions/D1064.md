## D1064 — Validate → Build part 1: Lab founders reach Build, a trashed venture takes no decision, Build's creates return their own id, a must-have's activity under Prototype / MVP (#2934)

- **Status:** built by S26 for #2934 (part 1 of Validate → Build, #2426), 2026-10-25. Migration: none.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `c5a11ba261`.

### 1. Spin-Out Lab founders reach Build

Validate's routes take `labRoles(['admin', 'founder'])`; Build's cycle routes took `guard(['admin', 'founder'])`, so an active Lab member whose account role is not `founder` was sent home from Build (owner question 8, the canvas's VB20).

- `/build` (its overview branch), `/build/connect`, `/build/:slug`, `/build/:slug/:id` and `/build/:slug/new` now take `labRoles(['admin', 'founder'])`. `/build/plan` and `/build/plan/progress` are `/build/:slug` and `/build/:slug/:id`; no route of their own exists.
- `founderBuildLanding` admits an active Lab member who is not an admin. Without it `/build` sent them to `/build/this-week`, which still refuses them. `?mode=workspace` keeps asking for the editor, and an admin keeps the editor, as before.
- The Worker already admits a Lab founder on their own venture (`ownsVenture`); nothing changes there.
- Build's other pages (`/build/this-week`, `/build/board`, `/build/team`, `/build/marketplace`) are not cycle pages and keep their guards.

### 2. A trashed venture takes no decision

`routes/cycle_verdict.ts` and `routes/cycle_validate.ts` read the venture with `deleted_at IS NULL`, as Build's lookups do. A trashed venture's Verdict, and every Validate read and write through that lookup, is "Project not found" (404), for its founder and for an admin.

### 3. Build's creates return their own id

`POST /features`, `/must-haves` and `/roadmap` read the new id with `SELECT MAX(id)` after their batch, outside it. A GitHub sync that added a feature or item in between handed the founder that row.

- The three writers are statement builders in `services/buildCreates.ts` (`featureCreateStatements`, `mustHaveCreateStatements`, `roadmapCreateStatements`), each INSERT first with `RETURNING id`. The route answers with `createdId(results)`, the row the batch wrote. Part 3's Accept can put the same statements in its own batch.
- `feedbackLink` moved with them; the roadmap PATCH imports it.
- **The statements after the INSERT keep naming the new row as the venture's newest:** the must-have's links, the "Waits on" edge, the feedback link and the activity line. Inside the batch that is exact. D1 runs a batch as one transaction, so no other write lands between its statements, and the newest row is the one just inserted. The race was only the read after the batch, and that read is gone.

### 4. A must-have's activity line

`build_must_have_created` is filed under `build.prototype-mvp`, the module whose burn-up counts must-haves, not `build.solution-design`. A feature stays under Solution design.
