## D4709 — Delivery histories: complete, paged, kept as sent, and corrected with attribution (#4709)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-16 by S09. Delivery audit package 5 of 6. The audit was pinned to `d85951ca90`, and every finding was re-checked against main `de604fc930`.
- **Migration:** `202611152339_4709_delivery_history.sql`:
  - adds `engagement_status_reports.blockers_at_send` and a trigger that freezes it once the report is sent;
  - adds `engagement_status_report_amendments` and `engagement_deliverable_corrections`, both append-only (two triggers each).
- **Code:**
  - `cloudflare-worker/src/services/deliveryHistory.ts` (input checks, keyset cursors, the at-send record);
  - `routes/partner_delivery.ts`;
  - `frontend/src/lib/api/partnerDeliveryHistory.js`;
  - `frontend/src/pages/partner/delivery/historyPages.jsx`, plus the Board, Health, Deliverables and Status reports zones.

### 1. A report's period is filtered before any limit

`GET /engagements/:id/report-draft/:period` read the 50 newest sends and then kept the ones in the period. On a busy engagement, composing an earlier month found nothing it had shipped. The period is now filtered in SQL, with no limit: `substr(sent_at, 1, 7) = ?`, the same test the page made with `startsWith`.

### 2. Every list is paged, with stable order

| Read | Order (the last key breaks ties) | Page | Cursor |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/board`, `/health` | `created_at DESC, id DESC` | 200 | `[created_at, id]` |
| `/deliverables` | sent first, `sent_at DESC, id DESC`, then unsent by `id DESC` | 300 | `[unsent, sent_at, id]` |
| `/status-reports` | `period DESC, id DESC` | 200 | `[period, id]` |

**The cursor:**
- Each list takes `?cursor=` and `?limit=`. The limit runs from 1 to the page size, and leaving it out means the full page.
- Each list answers `page: { limit, returned, total, next_cursor, complete }`.
- The cursor is opaque base64url JSON. One of the wrong shape is refused with `400 bad_cursor`, and a limit out of range with `400 bad_limit`. Neither is ever treated as the first page.

**Page size.** The default page is the old fixed limit. So a book that fitted on one page still comes back whole, and a client that never sends a cursor sees no change until its history grows past one page.

**How the keyset compares.** The deliverables and status-report keysets compare the raw stored text, in the same order the `ORDER BY` sorts it. So the boundary is exact whatever date format a row holds: no gap and no repeat.

### 3. The figures are the whole book's

**Board and Health.** These read every engagement, because their figures need all of them:
- needs attention;
- unrated;
- at risk and drift;
- lowest utilisation;
- the satisfaction average.

The page is then cut from those same rows.

**Deliverables.** The log adds a narrow whole-book read of the columns its figures need:
- sent;
- unopened;
- Not recorded;
- the median days to open;
- the new `signed_off_count`, `shipped_31d_count` and `shipped_31d_clients`.

**Status reports.** These count drafts, sent, sent this cycle, blocked and client-blocked over every report.

**On the page.** The figures are labelled as the whole book's. When the page is focused on one engagement, they count that engagement's loaded rows, and the pager says how many of the book that is.

### 4. A sent report shows the blockers it was sent with

**Before this change**, the list attached today's open blockers to every report, sent ones included. A report sent in March looked blocked on something raised in June.

**At the send.** The send now writes `blockers_at_send` in the same `UPDATE`: a JSON list of the engagement's open blockers (side, summary, date raised) at that moment. The trigger freezes it.

**What the list returns for each report:**
- **A draft:** `blockers`, the open blockers now, with `blockers_source: 'live'`.
- **A sent report:** the blockers at its send, with `'at_send'`. If it was sent before this record existed, or the record cannot be read, it gets `[]` with `'not_recorded'` and a note. That is never "nothing blocked", and today's blockers are never passed off as the report's.
- **Every report:** `current_blockers` holds the open blockers now, labelled as now.

**The strip** counts each report by its own blockers. `blockers_not_recorded_count` says how many sent reports have none on record.

**Reports already sent are not backfilled.** What was open then was never written down, and it is not inferred (the Not recorded rule).

### 5. A sent deliverable is corrected, never silently rewritten

**A correction needs a reason.** Once a deliverable is sent, a `PATCH` that changes its title, version, link or sent date (unsending included) needs `correction_reason`. Without one it answers `409 deliverable_sent`, and nothing changes.

**What a correction keeps.** It writes one row to `engagement_deliverable_corrections` in the same batch as the update:
- the revision it replaced;
- the four values that revision held;
- the reason, who made it and when.

**Racing writes.** The correction is inserted only while the row is still at the revision that was read, so a write that loses a race leaves no correction behind.

**Edits with no change.** An edit that changes none of the four fields needs no reason and records nothing.

**Delete.** A sent deliverable is never deleted (`409 deliverable_sent`). The delete repeats `sent_at IS NULL`, so a send that lands in between keeps the row.

**Where corrections appear.** The log and the `PATCH` answer return each deliverable's `corrections`, oldest first, with the corrector's name.

**Why not versions.** A sent deliverable could instead have become a list of immutable versions. That would be a second deliverable model beside `engagement_deliverables`, and the client opens (#5314) are keyed on the row. A new version of a deliverable is still logged as a new deliverable. A correction is for a mistake in what was recorded about the one that was sent.

### 6. Corrections to a sent report are amendments

**A sent report stays frozen** (D4682's triggers).

**The amendment route.** `POST /status-reports/:id/amendments { note }` adds an amendment, up to 2,000 characters, recording who wrote it and when. It leaves the report itself untouched.

**Refusals:**
- a draft (`409 report_draft`, because a draft is edited, not amended);
- an empty note (`400 empty_amendment`);
- a note that is not text (`400 bad_input`);
- another firm's report (404).

**Where amendments appear.** The list returns each report's amendments.

**Auth inventory.** The route is in the auth inventory as unreviewed (`record_without_ownership_check`), like its siblings. The check is `ownChild`, which the inventory does not recognise.

### 7. Inputs are checked before they are stored

**Dates** are checked by `dateInput`. They apply to a deliverable's sent date, a milestone's due and completed dates, a seat's granted date, and the date a satisfaction score was said.
- **Accepted:** `2026-11-15`, an ISO date and time, or blank (which clears the field).
- **Refused** with `400 bad_date` and one fixed sentence: anything else, including impossible dates, a non-string, or a list.

**Links** are checked by `linkInput`. A deliverable link must be http or https, have a host, carry no credentials and be at most 600 characters. Otherwise it is refused with `400 bad_link`. The link is the one a client is handed to open, so `javascript:`, `data:` and `file:` links are never stored.

**Titles and versions** must be text (`400 bad_input`).

**Where the checks run.** They run on the fields a request sends, before the merge. So a value stored before the checks never blocks an edit to another field.

### 8. The states on the page

`HistoryPager` (`historyPages.jsx`) draws four states under each list. The state copy follows the canvas (`design/incoming/Delivery v2 Page.dc.html`, "Partial data" and "Section could not be read"):

| State | What the page shows |
|---|---|
| Complete | Nothing. Past one page, "All N shown". |
| Paginated | "Showing 300 of 412 deliverables", with Load more. |
| Loading more | The rows stay, and the button reads Loading more…. |
| Partial | A later page failed. The shown rows stay, with "The rest of the deliverables could not be read · The 300 shown are current; 112 more are not shown yet", and Retry. |

**Other states:**
- An unreadable first page is still the zone's own "This did not load" state.
- An empty book is still the zone's own first-use state.

**Pages after a refresh.** Pages loaded after the first start again whenever the zone reads its first page again (after a save). They are joined by key, so a row that moved between two reads is shown once.

**On a sent row:**
- **Deliverables:**
  - its corrections show under the row;
  - its editor asks "Why is it being corrected?" before Save works;
  - Delete is replaced by "Sent deliverables are records, not deletable rows."
- **Status reports:**
  - the card shows "Open when it was sent" (or Not recorded) and "Open now, after this report";
  - the card shows its amendments, with "Add an amendment";
  - the Report feed's Blocked column reads the blockers as sent, marked "when sent" or "now".

### Not done here

- **The engagement pickers.** The Capacity, Deliverables and Status reports zones read Health's first page for their pickers (the newest 200 engagements). A firm with more engagements than that would not find its oldest ones in a picker.
- **Board and Health chip counts.** Apart from All, which is the book's total, these count the loaded rows.
- **Pages after a save.** A save that refreshes the zone starts the pager again from the first page.

### Tests

- **Period filtering:** the period is found behind more than 50 newer sends, and its first and last days are inside it.
- **Paging:** pages for deliverables, Board, Health and status reports with tied timestamps, with no gap and no repeat, including a boundary one row past the limit. The figures on every page are the book's.
- **Bad page requests:** cursors (wrong shape, wrong length, a sent flag that is not 0 or 1) and limits that are refused.
- **Sent reports:** blockers as sent against now; Not recorded for a legacy, garbled or non-list record; nothing open at the send recorded as nothing.
- **Amendments:** attributed amendments; the report byte-for-byte unchanged; append-only.
- **Deliverable corrections:**
  - a reason is required, and the replaced values, revision and who are kept;
  - a sent deliverable is never deleted;
  - append-only;
  - a correction racing another edit records only the write that landed.
