### 1. What the founder does

**⋯ › This card › AI voice-over** opens a sheet (`VoiceSheet.jsx`). It is never in the header or the AIRail, and it works in three steps:
1. **The quote**, read when the sheet opens: "AI voice · melotts · holds $0.0004 until it is made". A balance that cannot cover the hold, AI off, or the kill switch say so, and Make stays off.
2. **Make the voice-over** speaks the card's script (its speaker notes, at most 2,000 characters). The clip then plays in the sheet, tagged **AI voice** and **Not saved**.
3. **Accept** saves a new version whose card carries the voice-over, and the editor reads it back as Restore does. **Discard** deletes it, and nothing is saved.

The sheet runs nothing, not even the quote, in three cases:
- the card has no script ("Write its script first");
- the editor has unsaved changes ("Save your version first", since Accept writes a version);
- the viewer is a reader.

### 2. The Worker (`routes/deck_voice.ts`)

| Route | What it does |
|---|---|
| `POST /api/decks/:id/cards/:card/voice/quote` | `quoteRun` on DK10 with MeloTTS named, and the balance check. |
| `POST /api/decks/:id/cards/:card/voice` | Quote, balance, the owner's daily ceiling (D1510), then `run()`. The clip goes into R2 as pending, and one `ai_rail_proposals` row (option `deck:voice`, kind `proposal`) holds its key. Returns the proposal and a link to hear it. A support session is refused. |
| `POST /api/decks/:id/voice/:proposal/accept` | One new version, with the opened version as the base (an older one answers 409). The card gets `voice: { key, model, label: 'AI voice', chars }`; the proposal is accepted. |
| `POST /api/decks/:id/voice/:proposal/discard` | The clip leaves R2 first, then the proposal is discarded. A clip R2 refuses to delete keeps its row. |
| `GET /api/decks/:id/voice` | The model (for the rail) and a link for each card of the version that carries a voice-over (for Present). |
| `GET /api/decks/voice/:token` | The clip, for a token the Worker signed for that one clip, valid for an hour. |

- **Who:** the deck's founders (`projectOwned` with `write`).
- **The model:** `VOICE_MODEL` is MeloTTS, named on the run and checked by `speechChoice` against DK10's own list. There is no model list here. Aura comes in its own change, after owner decision P and the partner switch.
- **Charging:** the run is metered as every run is. Its usage row sits on the rail option `deck:voice`.

### 3. Storage and retention (owner's Q1 answer)

- **One writer:** `services/decks/cardAudio.ts` `putCardAudio` stores every per-card clip under `deck-audio/<project>/<uid>.mp3`. It checks the venture's storage room first, and is listed in the R2 writer inventory (`storage_room_d1170.test.ts` SITES). `kind` is `ai_voice`.
- **Retention:** `documentation/operations/DATA_RETENTION.md` gives per-card audio one row, `deck-audio/`.
  - Proposed: the clip is the member's through the proposal row.
  - Accepted: the deck version names it.
  - Discard removes it.
- **Undecided clips:** the daily cleanup (`models/jobs.ts`) runs `sweepPendingVoice`. A clip still proposed after **seven days** is deleted and its proposal withdrawn.
- **Orphaned clips:** the same cleanup runs `sweepOrphanAudio`. A clip that no version of its venture and no proposal names is deleted once it is eight days old; this covers a deck, venture or account that was deleted. A clip an older version names is kept, so Restore brings it back.
- **#3837's walkthrough takes** were built before this writer (D1590, `deck-recordings/`, with their own table and row). Moving them onto `putCardAudio` is a change to that route and its tests, left for its own issue. Until then the two kinds of per-card audio have two rows, and the voice-over's row is the one the owner's answer names.

### 4. The key never leaves the venture

- **The card's field:** `slideModel.voiceOf` keeps a voice only when its key is a `deck-audio/` MP3 key and its model is a catalogue-shaped id, and it always writes the label "AI voice".
- **Outside readers:** `publicDeckOf` gives every reader (share, print, embed) the label and the model, and never the key.
- **The share read:** after every gate of the link has passed (NDA, email, forwarding, the view claim), it sends each voiced card's `voice.url`, a link signed for one clip and an hour (`withVoiceLinks`).
  - A card naming another venture's clip gets no link (`keyOfProject`).
  - The player plays only a signed `deck-audio/` key. A share token, any other key, a forged signature or an expired link all answer 404.

### 5. Where it plays, always labelled "AI voice"

- **Present:** the presenter view, under the notes (`VoicePlayer`, "AI VOICE").
- **The share viewer:** `ShareViewerParts.jsx` `CardVoices`, one player per voiced card ("Card 1 · Kestrel" and an AI voice tag), on both share layouts.
- **The rail:** the **Voice** group (`deckRailGroups.js`) shows the one voice the Worker names (`GET …/voice`) and refuses a pick with "One voice for now. Other voices come later."
