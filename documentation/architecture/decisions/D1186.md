### 2. Cash (BNK3, BNK4)

**One read, one figure.**
- Cash reads `GET /api/admin/mercury/accounts` once. That read feeds the tiles, the figures in the chart's legend and the Balances table.
- The Balances table is Fund admin › Banking's own `Accounts`. It takes the shared read as `reading` (the prop D1185 gave it) and, from this decision, a `label`. Without `reading`, it reads for itself as before.
- So the Fund's cash on Cash is the figure Fund admin draws, by construction. A test mounts both and compares them.

**The four tiles.**

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Cash across entities | The sum of Current over the registry's accounts (Mercury's `registered`) that have a Current. Its line reads "Registered accounts, n of m read · as of <latest read time>" |
| Available | The same sum over Available, with "Excludes pending debits" |
| Unmatched receipts | Every unmatched credit, counted (see below) |
| Accounts without a balance | How many registered accounts Mercury sent no Current for, each named by entity and masked ending |

- An account Mercury reads that is not in the registry is in none of these sums. In the canvas, that is the card account.
- With no registered account, or no Current for any of them, a tile is "Not recorded" with its reason. It is never $0.00.

**Unmatched receipts.**
- This closes finding 9 of the map: the tile counted a sample before.
- For each connected entity, Cash reads `GET /api/admin/mercury/transactions?match=none&direction=credit` and takes its `total`. That is the count of every such credit, not the page of five the registry draws.
- An entity that is not connected is named "not connected" and is not read.
- If one connected entity's read fails, the tile is "Unreadable" and names the entity. It never shows the smaller sum.

**Cash by entity.**
- **The read:** `/balances` with `range` 30d, 90d or 12m; the default is 90d. Changing the range reads again.
- **What is drawn:** end-of-day Current of the registered accounts, stacked by entity.
- **A gap:**
  - `/balances` sends no point for a day with no snapshot.
  - A day on which any charted account has no snapshot, or a snapshot with no Current, is a gap for the whole stack. It is drawn as a shaded band marked "Not recorded" and is never a fall to 0.
  - A recorded zero is still a zero.
  - The foot counts the days not recorded.
- **An entity with no stored balance in the range** is left out of the stack and named in the foot. Its legend swatch is hollow.
- **The legend** gives each entity today's Current from the shared accounts read. An entity whose accounts are not all known reads "Not recorded" instead of a partial sum.
- **Chart or Table:** the Table view lists each day, and a gap's cells read "Not recorded".

**Money in and out by month.**
- **The read:** `/monthly`, read once for every entity. The All / Holdings / Management / GP / Fund I chips filter that answer on the page and read nothing again.
- **The bars:** money in sits above the axis and money out below it. The current month is hatched and labelled "to date".
- **A month with no posted line** is absent from the read. It is drawn as "Not recorded", never as an empty bar at 0.
- **Under All,** a month sums the entities that have it, and the row names the ones that do not.
- **The foot:** moves between Axal's own accounts are not counted. That is D1183's rule.

**Also on Cash:** "Reconcile receipts" opens Fund admin › Banking › Reconciliation. No matching happens here.

### 3. History (BNK5, BNK8b)

**The read and its columns.**
- Part 1's read, 25 rows a page, newest first.
- The columns are When, Event, Entity, Account, By and Detail.
- **Account** is the Worker's `account_masked` ("••••4821") or a dash. Nothing else is ever drawn.

**Event words** come from the stored action:

| Action | Event |
|---|---|
| Reveal | Reveal |
| `bank_account_added` | Added |
| `bank_account_updated` | Changed |
| `bank_account_retired` | Retired |
| `mercury_connected` | Mercury connected |
| `mercury_token_replaced` | Token replaced |
| `mercury_disconnected` | Mercury disconnected |

- `bank_account_updated` is one action for both relabels and payment changes. So History says "Changed", not the canvas's "Relabelled" or "Payments changed".

**What a row's detail shows.**

| Row | Detail |
|---|---|
| An admin's reveal | "Full reason", which opens to the recorded reason (the read's `context_ref`, digits already redacted by D1182) and "The number was shown for 30 seconds." |
| An LP's reveal | "An LP revealed their own capital-call line (line #n)." |
| A row whose stored detail could not be read | It says so; its action, time and person stay as recorded |
| A missing name, entity or label | "Name not recorded", "Not recorded" or "Account label not recorded" |

**Search and filters.**
- **The filters:** a search (sent as `q`, at most 120 characters), and Event (`kind`), Entity and Dates (`range`: all, 7d, 30d or 90d).
- **The canvas's "Who" filter** is not drawn. The read takes an actor id, but no list of people to choose from exists.

**Pages.**
- Page 1 sends no snapshot and keeps the one it is given.
- Every later page sends that snapshot back, so a reveal recorded while you page does not shift the rows.
- A new search or filter starts again from page 1 with a new snapshot.

### 4. The states (BNK8–BNK11)

| Board | What is drawn |
|---|---|
| BNK8b, a history with no events | "Nothing has been revealed or changed yet. Every reveal and change will be listed here." A filtered history with no rows says "No event matches this search and these filters." instead; it is not the same claim |
| BNK8a, no account at all | Accounts' (part 4's). On Cash, the Balances table says no account is recorded, and the tiles say Not recorded |
| BNK9, loading | Every block shows its own loading rows; a tile reads "Reading…" |
| BNK10a and BNK10b, error and partial | Each block reads alone and fails alone, with "could not be read" and Try again, and the rest of the tab stays. A 503 `audit_unavailable` reads "Nothing was read, because its audit row could not be written." A history refused for its filters (400) says so |
| BNK11a, an Admin who is not the Super Admin | The canvas's card: "This page belongs to the Super Admin", with Open Fund admin › Banking. Cash and History read nothing for them |
| BNK11b, a member | Never reaches the page: the route is `guard(['admin'])` |

**BNK10c, paused after an account recovery, is not drawn.** No Worker route answers a pause after recovery. That board waits for one.

**Dark (BNK4)** comes from `dark:` counterparts on every class. The charts draw with `fill-current` and `stroke-current` over token and palette text classes, and no hex value.

### 5. Read-only

Every request on these two tabs is a GET. Each Mercury and history read writes its own audit row on the Worker before it answers (D1182, D1183). No account number, token or figure is logged.
