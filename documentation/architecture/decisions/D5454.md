## D5454 — Private deal preparation: lifecycle, access, projections and the reader inventory (#5454)

- **Status:** contract version 1, built 2026-11-15 by S37 as ADP-01 of #5453. Part of #5454: owner questions Q1–Q4 below are open.
- **Code:** `cloudflare-worker/src/services/dealPreparation.ts` (contract, capability, projections, issuer pair, queue read, reader guard, legacy classifier).
- **Migration:** `cloudflare-worker/sql/migrations/202611150148_5454_deal_preparations.sql` (two new tables, two triggers, five indexes; additive, re-runnable, nothing backfilled).
- **Tests:** `cloudflare-worker/test/deal_preparation_5454.test.ts`; guard `scripts/check-deal-preparation-readers.mjs` (in `npm run test:guards`).
- **Disabled:** draft creation, editing, submission, review, publication and withdrawal. `PREPARATION_MUTATIONS_ENABLED` is false for each; no route writes a preparation. ADP-02 owns create/edit/submit, ADP-04 review/publish/withdraw.
- **No production change** is made or authorised: no backfill, no production query, no deploy, no publication, invitation or permission change.

### 1. Identity: one deal, a company-backed project scope

- A preparation is one `deal_preparations` row. It names the **issuing company** (`company_id`) **and** its **project scope** (`project_id`), both required (owner, 2026-11-10). Only a company-backed project may become a formal deal; a project with `projects.company_id IS NULL` is refused (`project_has_no_company`). The pair is checked on the server by `resolveIssuerPair`: the company must be the project's own `projects.company_id` (migration 189), the project live, the company present and not archived. The caller's active company, an investor firm (`commitments.company_id`), a broker or GP operator never substitutes for it.
- Every content revision (`deal_preparation_revisions`) pins `company_id` and `project_id` as they stood when it was written. A later project move never rewrites a revision, a contract or a commitment: `issuerDrift` reports `moved` or `detached`, which invalidates a working approval and asks for reconciliation (ADP-04), never a silent transfer.
- **The structural rule (CHECK `dp_private_owns_no_deal`).** A preparation whose origin is `preparation` has no `deal_id` until a revision is released. A never-released draft therefore owns **no `deals` row**, so none of the existing `deals` readers can list, count, index, export, notify or feed it to an AI. ADP-04's release allocates the canonical deal identity in the same batch that sets `released_revision_id` (CHECK `dp_release_has_deal`). Existing deal ids, commitment ids and investor deep links never change (owner question Q2 confirms this choice).
- A preparation of an **existing** deal is `origin = 'legacy_attach'` and must name it (CHECK `dp_legacy_names_deal`). One preparation per deal (`idx_deal_preparations_deal`, unique where `deal_id` is set).
- `deals.company_id` is not added: project-keyed tables inherit company context (migration 189); the pinned issuer lives on the preparation and its revisions.

### 2. Three independent dimensions

| Dimension | Values | Stored | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preparation (editorial review of a working revision) | `draft`, `in_review`, `changes_requested`, `approved` | `deal_preparations.preparation_state` | ADP-02 (submit), ADP-04 (review) |
| Publication (distribution of a released revision) | `unreleased`, `published`, `withdrawn`, `archived` | `deal_preparations.publication_state` | ADP-04 only |
| Round stage | `applied`, `scored`, `active`, `funded`, `rejected` | `deals.status` (unchanged) | `services/dealTransitions.ts`, unchanged policy |

- Revision pointers are separate columns: `working_revision_id`, `submitted_revision_id`, `approved_revision_id`, `released_revision_id`. A published r1 and a private working r2 coexist; opening r2 neither hides nor mutates r1. `lock_version` is the optimistic-concurrency counter ADP-02 checks.
- `published`, `withdrawn` and `archived` require a released revision (CHECK `dp_distribution_needs_release`).
- Creating or publishing a preparation never implies money received, an IC approval, a signature or a stage change. A stage move, a score refresh or a CRM pull never writes `deal_preparations` (`AC4` tests) and never rewrites the deal's **editorial columns** (`EDITORIAL_DEAL_COLUMNS`: amount, target raise, minimum check, valuation cap, carry, management fee, instrument, SPV jurisdiction, closing deadline, website, description) once a preparation manages the deal: `PUT /api/deals/:id` answers 409 `preparation_managed_terms` with nothing written, and `runSystemMove` drops those fields and still moves the stage.
- Revisions are immutable (triggers `deal_preparation_revisions_no_update` / `_no_delete`); content is valid JSON of at most 64 KiB; `content_sha256` is the content's digest. Retention: revisions are kept for the life of the preparation; deleting a preparation is not in this contract.

### 3. Classification and the reader rule

`classifyPreparation(row)` is the one reading of a deal's preparation metadata, mirrored in SQL by the hidden-set queries (every comparison uses `IS`, so a NULL or unknown value cannot fall out of a `NOT`):

| Class | When | Discovery readers (lists, counts, search, feeds, notifications, exports, AI) | Transactional readers (by id, related party) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `legacy` | no preparation row; or `legacy_attach` + `unreleased` + no release | today's rules | today's rules |
| `listed` | released + `published` | shown | shown |
| `unlisted_released` | released + `withdrawn`/`archived` | deal staff, the venture's owner and already-related investors only | shown to whoever the route already admits |
| `private` | anything else, unknown and missing values included | never | 404, as a deal that does not exist, for every viewer including admins |

**Missing or unknown metadata is never permission to publish.** A database without the migration has no preparations, so nothing is private there (`isMissingPreparationTable`); any other read error fails the request closed. The hidden set is bounded (`HIDDEN_SET_MAX` = 5000 ids); past it the read refuses rather than truncating a privacy filter.

### 4. Capabilities

Explicit names: `read`, `create`, `edit`, `submit`, `review`, `publish`, `withdraw`. `preparationCapabilities(env, user, row, ctx)`:

- Authority is the platform admin (`role = 'admin'`, MFA-gated by `requireAdmin` at the route), outside a support session, on the row's own deployment (`scope_code` = `branchOf(env) || 'hq'`; each branch has its own database, so there is no cross-tenant lookup or HQ-to-branch fan-out).
- A frozen admin (D135, `adminWritesFrozen`) keeps `read` and loses every write.
- Nothing else grants anything: not the partner licence (partner-wide pipeline read/move access is not preparation access), not the investor or founder licence, not a secondary licence, not preview mode.
- Mutations are additionally gated by `PREPARATION_MUTATIONS_ENABLED`; today the most anyone holds is `read`. The distinct-reviewer and publisher matrix is ADP-04's policy gate (#5458); missing policy blocks publication, not drafting.

### 5. Named projections

`project(name, prep, revision)` copies allowlisted keys only; nothing is serialised whole and trimmed afterwards. Each projection reads exactly one revision pointer and returns null for any other revision.

| Projection | Audience | Revision | Content keys |
|---|---|---|---|
| `authoring` | the authoring admin | working | every `REVISION_CONTENT_KEYS` key |
| `review` | the reviewer | submitted | every key but `internal_notes` |
| `marketplace_teaser` | an eligible marketplace reader | released, `listed` only | title, summary, sector, instrument, currency |
| `investor` | an invited or NDA-authorised investor | released, `listed` only | offering material and terms; no internal notes, no audience manifest |

Live transactional facts (commitments, transfers, closing state) are not part of a projection; they stay on their own permission-checked routes. The NDA rule stays `services/dealAccess.ts`'s.

### 6. Operational bounds

- Indexes: queue `(scope_code, preparation_state, updated_at DESC, id DESC)`, publication `(scope_code, publication_state, updated_at DESC, id DESC)`, issuer `(company_id, project_id)`, deal (unique, partial), revisions `(preparation_id, revision_no DESC)`.
- `readPreparationQueue`: keyset pagination on `(updated_at DESC, id DESC)`, at most 100 a page, the total counted over the same filtered population, exactly two bound statements a page (`QUEUE_QUERY_COUNT`), metadata only (no revision content, so a queue page carries no title or amount).
- Reader guard cost: one indexed statement per guarded list or count request, one per by-id request (`/api/deals/:id/*` middleware). No per-row lookups, no external or AI call on a list.
- Logs carry counts and outcome codes only.

### 7. Expand / contract, mixed versions, rollback

1. **Expand (this PR):** tables and indexes; guards on every discovery reader that could see a prepared deal row; creation and release disabled. Mixed version: a build without this PR reads no new table and, by the structural rule, cannot see a never-released draft. Rows only appear once ADP-02 enables creation.
2. **ADP-02** may enable `create`/`edit`/`submit` only while `scripts/check-deal-preparation-readers.mjs` passes and the CHECKs above stand. Drafts still own no deal row, so even a rollback to a pre-D5454 build does not list one.
3. **ADP-04** may enable `publish`/`withdraw` only after every inventory row marked **R4** below is guarded for `unlisted_released`. From the first withdrawal on, the **oldest safe rollback build is the first build containing this PR's guards plus those R4 guards**: an older build would re-list a withdrawn release as an ordinary deal. Do not roll back past it.
4. No applied-migration edits, no runtime DDL (`ensure*Schema` is not used for these tables).

### 8. Legacy deals

No deal is backfilled, none is marked reviewed or approved, no approval date is invented, and no visibility changes in this PR. The read-only plan is `LEGACY_INVENTORY_SQL`: one row of **counts** per class, no id, name, title or amount. It is to be run only on a copy, with the owner's approval; this code never runs it on production. `classifyLegacyDeal` gives each fixture its outcome:

| Fixture | Outcome | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Visible, no relationship | `legacy_visible_unreviewed` | Unchanged (marketplace as today), marked unreviewed; **owner question Q1** |
| Invited | `legacy_transactional_preserved` | Existing access survives as is; no review recorded |
| Funded / signed / committed | `legacy_transactional_preserved` | Same; signed documents, commitments and transfers untouched |
| Unknown exposure (unrecognised status) | `legacy_unknown_exposure` | Owner review; nothing inferred |
| Archived (deleted venture) | `legacy_archived` | Stays out of every reader, history kept |
| No issuer company | `legacy_no_issuer` | Cannot become a preparation without a reviewed company link; **owner question Q3** |
| Already managed | `preparation_managed` | Classified by `classifyPreparation` |

Attaching a preparation to a legacy deal (`legacy_attach`, `unreleased`) changes nothing a reader sees until a reviewed release.

### 10. Owner questions

- **Q1 — legacy visible deals (no invitation, commitment or signature).** A: keep today's marketplace visibility, marked `legacy_visible_unreviewed`, until the owner reviews each through a `legacy_attach` preparation (no change now). B: hide them from discovery until reviewed, keeping transactional access. C: keep only `active` ones listed and hide `applied`/`scored` intake deals from investor discovery. **Pick: A** — it changes no access without a reviewed mapping.
- **Q2 — the structural boundary.** A: a never-released preparation owns no `deals` row; release allocates the deal id (encoded in CHECK `dp_private_owns_no_deal`). B: create the `deals` row at draft creation and rely on a guard in every reader (~50 files, several owned by open PRs). **Pick: A** — a forgotten reader cannot leak a row that does not exist.
- **Q3 — legacy deals whose project has no company.** A: leave their transactional history as is and refuse a preparation until a reviewed company link exists (no placeholder company). B: allow a `legacy_attach` preparation with the company left for review. **Pick: A.**
- **Q4 — today's creators (`POST /api/deals`, `POST /api/deals/draft`, intake, CRM import).** A: leave them unchanged here; ADP-02 routes admin creation through a preparation and retires `/draft`. B: refuse a no-company project in `/draft` now. **Pick: A** — B changes a live admin flow outside this package.
