## D5582 part 4 — Drafting registry bootstrap, re-adoption and frozen-export pinning (#5582)

Part 3 (D5582-p3) let a Super Admin draft against an existing WorkGraph asset, but left "initial asset
registration/adoption over HTTP" and the frozen export to later work. Its stale refusal told the caller to adopt the
domain's current revision, and no route did that. This part adds that registry as a second router,
`/api/admin/workgraph/drafting-assets` (`routes/admin_workgraph_drafting_assets.ts`), behind part 3's gates: an HQ
preview deployment, `WORK_GRAPH_DRAFTING=preview`, a Super Admin, the write bar on every POST, and the caller's
verified selected company. Templates also need counsel (`counsel_members`). No switch is turned on by this change.

**One asset per domain object.** `services/workGraphDraftingRegistry.ts` maps a domain id to the object an asset
stands for, and to that object's current row: a deck to its project and that project's `is_current` deck, a template to
its active `legal_template_versions` row, an offer to its quote and the quote's latest `quote_versions` row. The asset's
retry key is derived from the object (`drafting-bootstrap:<kind>:<object>`), never supplied by the caller. A retry, or
another Super Admin naming the same object, reaches the same asset, and a concurrent bootstrap converges on one asset
through the store's `UNIQUE (tenant_id, company_id, idempotency_key)`. The caller names the tenant, as the D5585 release
routes do. A company WorkGraph already holds under another tenant is refused `wrong_tenant`.

**Bootstrap reads before it writes.** The current row is read through part 3's `readDraftingDomain`, the same canonical
reader the drafting routes use, before any asset exists. A record of another company reads as absent (404). The asset is
registered with `registerDraftedAsset`, so its policy is always `human_publication`. The row is adopted as the first
head with `adoptCurrent`, where the Super Admin is the person approving those exact units. Nothing else is written:
no deck, proposal, template, research draft or quote version.

**Re-adoption renews the approval.** `POST /:assetUid/adopt` carries the head the caller saw. A different head is
refused `stale_revision` before any revision is written. An unchanged domain row answers `adopted: false`. Otherwise
the current row becomes the head through `advanceHead`'s compare-and-swap, with the person's approval of its digest. The
answer carries `invalidatedApprovals`, and every manifest that pinned the old head now reads `reapproval_required`
(D5585's profiles).

**Frozen export pins the approved head and nothing newer.** `POST /:assetUid/freeze` carries the head and the digest the
person approved and names a release line of the same company. It refuses `stale_revision` or `stale_approval` when
either has moved. It also refuses `stale_revision` while the domain row no longer reads as the head
(`currentDraftingDomain`): a deck saved, a template republished or a quote revised outside WorkGraph does not move a
WorkGraph head by itself, and an export cut then would pin text that is no longer the domain's. Otherwise it calls
D5585's `cutManifest` with one component at that exact revision and digest. That service still applies the audience
rule, kind profiles, accepted-head check and coverage of a full package. A line of another company is 404
`release_line_not_found`. Publishing, approvals, attestations, renders and delivery remain D5585's routes and gates.
Nothing here publishes or signs. A signed agreement or envelope has no drafting adapter (D5582-p3), so neither can be
frozen through this route.

Every write records an admin audit row (`workgraph_drafting_bootstrap`, `_adopt`, `_freeze`) beside WorkGraph's own
events, which keep the actor, digest and D1 cost counters. Turning the flag off removes the entry point and keeps every
asset, revision and manifest.

**Not in this part.** Release lines are still created only by D5585's service; no HTTP route here makes one. Media a
deck slide embeds (D5583 media assets) is not pinned beside the deck; the manifest pins the deck's units revision only.
Automatic invocation of adopt or confirm from each domain screen (Keep, template publication, the partner's quote
save) and the visual frozen-export screen have no approved canvas. They need a Claude Design brief before any frontend
work. There is no client method.

Tests: `cloudflare-worker/test/work_graph_drafting_registry_5582.test.ts`, on migrated D1 with both drafting routers
mounted at their real prefixes. They cover default-off, production and branch refusals; the Super Admin, write-bar and
company gates; one asset per object across retries and Super Admins; foreign records, tenants, kinds and caller-named
scope fields; counsel; re-adoption and stale heads; and freeze pinning, the refusal after a domain edit, the stale old
export and the audience rule. Mutation check: 23 mutations, 23 caught, 0 equivalent.
