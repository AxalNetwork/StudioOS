## D687 — Connectors draw each service's own mark, coded once in `ui/BrandIcon.jsx`, with initials only where no mark is cleared

Status: Decided. Date: 2026-10-14. Slot: S02. Issue: #1646. Decided by S1 under the owner's standing rule. This record is the build's.

Follow-up to D649 (#1461, the Connectors epic) and D686 §5.4 (#1597). The owner asked for coded social-media icons on the connectors.

### Decisions

1. **"Coded" means inline SVG in one module.** Every mark is an SVG path string in `frontend/src/ui/BrandIcon.jsx`, drawn by one `BrandIcon` component.
   - There is no new dependency (no simple-icons, react-icons or icon font), no `<img>` from any host, and no `.svg` file.
   - So a member's connector list never reaches a logo CDN, and the test loader can read every mark.
2. **Sources.**
   - Simple Icons 16.35.0 (CC0-1.0) is used where it has the brand.
   - Elsewhere the vendor's own brand page is used, under its terms, when that page allows the mark to identify an integration or share target.
   - Each entry records its source (URL and version, or the date the page was read), its licence and its guidelines.
   - The module's header comment is the short notice.
3. **One map, by key.**
   - Lookups go by `p.key`, never by `p.icon` or a display name, and use `Object.hasOwn`.
   - Every key the app draws is in exactly one of three tables:
     - `BRAND_MARKS`: a mark;
     - `BRAND_GENERIC`: a lucide glyph;
     - `BRAND_MONOGRAM`: the initials tile on purpose, with a `reason`.
   - `BRAND_ALIAS` folds four keys onto their brands.
   - A key nobody has classified draws the initials tile, and the guard test fails until it is classified.
4. **Colour.**
   - Light mode draws the brand's own colour on a white tile.
   - Dark mode draws the same colour when it keeps 3:1 against `#1e2939` (Tailwind 4's gray-800), and white otherwise. That is GitHub, X, TikTok, Zoom (`#0B5CFF`, 2.79:1) and Signal (`#3B45FD`, 2.41:1).
   - A multi-colour mark keeps its fills and always sits on white.
   - `tone="current"` draws `currentColor` with no tile, for surfaces that paint their own background.
5. **Size.**
   - The default is 34px with a 9px radius and an 18px mark, the canvas tile (`Connectors Page.dc.html:74`).
   - Sheet headers and rows use 24px with a 14px mark.
   - `variant="bare"` draws the mark alone at its text's size.
6. **Accessibility.**
   - A mark is decoration (`aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"`, no `<title>`) beside a visible name, never the only label.
   - Mark-only controls keep their `aria-label` and `title`.
7. **Assistants.** On `/connect/assistant`, the mark keys only off `verified_assistant`, which the Worker derives from the redirect URI (`mcp/assistantIdentity.ts`).
   - The mark is drawn on the identity line, never on the self-declared `client_name`.
   - Grant rows and `AssistantBadge` draw no mark until the Worker sends a verified identity for them.
8. **Trademark.**
   - A mark identifies the service the member connects or shares to (nominative use), and nothing else.
   - Each mark is drawn unaltered: in its own colour, or the monochrome or white version its guidelines document.
   - It is never cropped, outlined, rotated or restyled, never larger than its neighbours, and never placed to suggest endorsement.
   - Simple Icons' CC0 covers the path data only. Its clause 4(a) waives no trademark, and its DISCLAIMER says single icons may carry other terms, so each brand's guidelines still apply.
9. **Frontend only.** `GET /api/connectors/available` already sends `key`. No Worker change and no migration.

#### Monograms (the initials tile, on purpose)

Simple Icons 16.35.0 has none of these marks, so each was to follow the vendor's own page. Here is what happened:
- **Microsoft's page was read** (2026-10-14).
- **No other vendor page was reachable.** The build environment's egress policy refused every other vendor page that day, Google's Sign in with Google branding guidelines included. So no permission to use those marks could be read or recorded, and the initials stand until it is.

| Key | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| `google` | The four-colour G is to come from Google's Sign in with Google branding guidelines (https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines). That page was not reachable on 2026-10-14. Simple Icons 16.35.0 has only a monochrome G, which this decision does not use. **Open: read the page and record the G from it.** Until then, the sign-in buttons keep their current G, which step 11 replaces (second PR). |
| `microsoft` | Microsoft's Trademark and Brand Guidelines (https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/intellectualproperty/trademarks/usage/general, read 2026-10-14) say: "our logos, app and product icons, illustrations, photographs, videos, and designs can never be used without an express license". | `teams` | The same Microsoft guidelines, which also name app and product icons. |
| `salesforce`, `slack`, `docusign`, `carta`, `affinity`, `linkedin`, `chatgpt`, `grok` | Not in Simple Icons 16.35.0. The vendor's brand page could not be read from the build environment on 2026-10-14, so no permission to use the mark is recorded. |

### Where the marks are drawn (first PR, "Part of #1646")

- **Account › Connectors:**
  - the Yours rows and the Discover cards (the monogram moved in unchanged);
  - the Telegram Community card;
  - the connector sheet header (24px);
  - `Row`'s optional leading icon;
  - the GitHub sync sheet headers;
  - the HubSpot and Salesforce modals;
  - the LinkedIn CSV import, which draws LinkedIn's recorded monogram. The hand-copied `Linkedin` glyph, which dropped `className`, and its wrong comment are gone.
- **The channel cards** (Telegram, WhatsApp, Zoom and Google Meet). Google Meet draws its own mark, though it connects through Google.
- **The assistant setup cards.**
- **Build's Connect a tool rows** (24px).
- **Interviews:**
  - `ProviderTag` and `ChannelOriginBadge` draw a bare 14px mark with `fallback="none"`, so `other` never draws the assistant `Bot`;
  - the badge's key is the channel the server recorded;
  - Schedule a call's "Call on" chips draw a bare 16px mark.
- **Calendar's provider cards**, both managed-key panels (24px), and the GitHub sync console. The console drops lucide's `GitBranch as Github`.
- **The ✓ "Verified by Axal"** gains `role="img"`.
- **`SOCIAL_LINKS`** entries carry a `key`.

The second PR covers steps 10 and 11: the Record a session chips (after #1644), and replacing every hand-copied mark (the footer, share buttons, profiles, Referrals and the Google G) with `BrandIcon`.

### Departures

- **D651's rule "Logos are initials on the house lavender"** (`D651.md`) is replaced. Initials now appear only for keys in `BRAND_MONOGRAM`, each with its reason.
- **The canvas's brand-coloured initials tiles** (`Connectors Page.dc.html:73-75`) are not followed. A mark on a neutral tile replaces them.
- **The registry's `icon` field** (`cloudflare-worker/src/integrations/registry.ts`) is read by no frontend file. Two of its values, `Github` and `Figma`, are not lucide-react 1.52.0 icons. It is left alone: dropping or reusing it is a Worker change.

### Verified

`frontend/test/brand_icons_d687.test.mjs`:
1. **Every key is classified.**

6. **Rendering:** `aria-hidden`, `focusable="false"`, no `<title>` and `data-provider`; the tile's and the monogram's `dark:` classes; and the sizes.

9. **Only `AdminPage.jsx` imports `GitBranch as Github`** (out of scope, #1439). The rest of check 9 ships with the second PR.
