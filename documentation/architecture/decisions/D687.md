## D687 — Connectors draw each service's own mark, coded once in `ui/BrandIcon.jsx`, with initials only where no mark is cleared

Status: Decided. Date: 2026-10-14. Slot: S02. Issue: #1646. Decided by S1 under the owner's standing rule. This record is the build's.

Follow-up to D649 (#1461, the Connectors epic) and D686 §5.4 (#1597). The owner asked for coded social-media icons on the connectors.

### Decisions

1. **"Coded" means inline SVG in one module.** Every mark is an SVG path string in `frontend/src/ui/BrandIcon.jsx`, drawn by one `BrandIcon` component.
   - There is no new dependency (no simple-icons, react-icons or icon font), no `<img>` from any host, and no `.svg` file.
   - So a member's connector list never reaches a logo CDN, and the test loader can read every mark.
2. **Sources.**
   - Simple Icons 16.35.0 (CC0-1.0) is used where it has the brand.
   - Elsewhere the vendor's own brand page is used, under its terms, when that page allows the mark to identify an integration or share target.
   - Each entry records its source (URL and version, or the date the page was read), its licence and its guidelines.
   - The module's header comment is the short notice.
3. **One map, by key.**
   - Lookups go by `p.key`, never by `p.icon` or a display name, and use `Object.hasOwn`.
   - Every key the app draws is in exactly one of three tables:
     - `BRAND_MARKS`: a mark;
     - `BRAND_GENERIC`: a lucide glyph;
     - `BRAND_MONOGRAM`: the initials tile on purpose, with a `reason`.
   - `BRAND_ALIAS` folds four keys onto their brands.
   - A key nobody has classified draws the initials tile, and the guard test fails until it is classified.
4. **Colour.**
   - Light mode draws the brand's own colour on a white tile.
   - Dark mode draws the same colour when it keeps 3:1 against `#1e2939` (Tailwind 4's gray-800), and white otherwise. That is GitHub, X, TikTok, Zoom (`#0B5CFF`, 2.79:1) and Signal (`#3B45FD`, 2.41:1).
   - A multi-colour mark keeps its fills and always sits on white.
   - `tone="current"` draws `currentColor` with no tile, for surfaces that paint their own background.
5. **Size.**
   - The default is 34px with a 9px radius and an 18px mark, the canvas tile (`Connectors Page.dc.html:74`).
   - Sheet headers and rows use 24px with a 14px mark.
   - `variant="bare"` draws the mark alone at its text's size.
   - D753 (2026-10-18): the connector page's header tile is 56px, 48px on a phone. Cards keep 34px and sheet heads 24px.
6. **Accessibility.**
   - A mark is decoration (`aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"`, no `<title>`) beside a visible name, never the only label.
   - Mark-only controls keep their `aria-label` and `title`.
7. **Assistants.** On `/connect/assistant`, the mark keys only off `verified_assistant`, which the Worker derives from the redirect URI (`mcp/assistantIdentity.ts`).
   - The mark is drawn on the identity line, never on the self-declared `client_name`.
   - Grant rows and `AssistantBadge` draw no mark until the Worker sends a verified identity for them.
8. **Trademark.**
   - A mark identifies the service the member connects or shares to (nominative use), and nothing else.
   - Each mark is drawn unaltered: in its own colour, or the monochrome or white version its guidelines document.
   - It is never cropped, outlined, rotated or restyled, never larger than its neighbours, and never placed to suggest endorsement.
   - Simple Icons' CC0 covers the path data only. Its clause 4(a) waives no trademark, and its DISCLAIMER says single icons may carry other terms, so each brand's guidelines still apply.
9. **Frontend only.** `GET /api/connectors/available` already sends `key`. No Worker change and no migration.

The current inventory is 25 brand marks (18 Simple Icons plus these seven),

with no runtime XML parsing, HTML injection, image host or new dependency.

Single-colour black/blue marks use currentColor and the recorded light/dark
palette. Multi-colour assets retain their fills and a white backing. React
useId namespaces every gradient, filter, clip path and local href so repeated
Google icons on a page never share definitions.

and an exact Git blob hash in each entry. SVGL paths are covered by its MIT
notice, included in the module; the LinkedIn mirror is CC0-1.0. Those source
licences do not grant trademark rights; the linked vendor guidelines govern
