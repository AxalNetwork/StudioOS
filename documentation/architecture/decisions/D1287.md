### 2. Add tokens (CRP2)

- **The packs:** radio rows from `GET /api/ai-budget/top-up`, in HQ's order, each with its offers ("+10% bonus until 1 Nov", "First purchase only"). A line the checkout cannot sell is left out.
- **The states:**
  - reading;
  - "Token packs couldn't be read just now." with Try again;
  - "Top-up isn't available yet." when nothing is on sale.
- **The balance line:** "Your balance: $3.40 → $30.90 once the payment is confirmed."
  - The before-figure is `/me`'s balance.
  - The after-figure adds the pack's tokens and bonus for this line only. It is the one sum the page draws: the canvas asks for it, and the ledger decides the real balance.
  - With no balance read, the line names what the pack adds instead.
- **The totals:** the pack, its bonus, "VAT, where it applies" and Due today.
  - Before Pay no order exists, so VAT reads "Worked out when you pay", never $0.00. Due today is the pack's price, since a pack is sold at face value (D755 §5).
  - Once the intent answers, VAT and Due today are its own figures.
- **Lines:**
  - "Promo codes don't apply to token packs. Offers show on the pack."
  - "Paid securely through Stripe. Axal VC never stores your card details. Sold by Axal VC Management LLC."
- **The payment column:** D1053's card form (a deferred Payment Element, Apple Pay first), in payment mode. Saved cards are not offered for a pack (§6).
- **Pay:**
  - It sends `POST /api/orders/intent` exactly one pack line (`intentBody`), then confirms the order's PaymentIntent with the card.
  - Nothing is created before Pay.
  - One primary button per view.

### 3. After Pay (CRP3, CRP4)

`GET /api/orders/:ref` is read until its `ai_topup` is credited, refunded or disputed. Only the verified webhook credits a pack (D755 §6), so:

- **Waiting:** "Payment received. Adding $25.00 to your balance… This usually takes a few seconds."
- **Overdue** (paid over an hour ago, not credited; `purchaseState`): "Your tokens are on their way." It says the payment is recorded, gives "Open Usage ›" and "You won't be charged again for this order." There is no retry.
- **Accepted, only on `credited`:**
  - "Tokens added." with the balance counting up from the old figure to the order's new one.
  - With reduced motion, a hidden tab or no old figure, the final value shows at once.
  - Screen readers hear only the final figure, in a status line.
  - Then the bonus line, the receipt (paid, the order, "Receipt sent to your email.") and "Back to Models" / "See history" / "Close". `from` names the page for a refused run ("Back to [page]").
- **Refunded and disputed** say so and point to Usage.
- **An order that does not answer** stays on "Payment received.", never Accepted.

### 4. Refusals (CRP5)

- **A pack rule** the Worker refuses is drawn on the pack's row, with Pay disabled and "Choose another pack to continue." Choosing another pack enables Pay. The rules (D755 §5, D790, D849):
  - first purchase only;
  - today's order limit;
  - the balance ceiling;
  - the hold;
  - not on sale;
  - a pack alone;
  - a promo;
  - a support session.
- **The sentences:** the Worker's own sentence wins when it sends one (D258). Otherwise `PACK_RULES` gives the canvas's, keyed by the code, never by the message. D1289 gives each code a sentence on the Worker.
- **The hold** `/top-up` reports (`on_hold`) blocks Pay before anything is sent.
- **A card decline** is one of D1053's five families of words (`declineFamily`), with Try again, and says you were not charged.

### 5. Auto-reload's state (CRP7)

- **What it shows:** off, on (the pack, the threshold, the card's last four), or turned off by a decline or another reason (D794's words). Each links to Billing. It is never set here.
- **The read:** `GET /api/ai-budget/auto-reload` asks for a fresh authenticator code. The sheet does not open the step-up prompt over a payment.
  - The new `noStepUp` request option throws the 403 instead.
  - The line then says "Auto-reload's setting is shown on Billing." with "Open Billing ›".

### 6. History (CRP8)

- **The read:** `GET /api/ai-budget/history` (20 rows), with "Show AI runs" (`runs=1`). The rows are Usage's own (`usageModel.topUpRows`).
- **The layout:** a table from `sm` up, a list on a phone.
- **States:**
  - empty: "No top-ups yet." with "Packs you buy, bonuses and refunds will be listed here.";
  - unreadable: says so with Try again.
- **"See all on Usage ›"** goes to `/account/usage#top-ups`.
- **The canvas's "Balance after" column is not drawn.** The history read does not carry it.
