## D8251 — Grow Markets: Eadwyn's sourced proposals on a market, decided by the founder (#8251, GR-24)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-22 as the store, the reads and the decision; **the run that produces proposals is not built** (D6120 listed it as a separate piece). Until it calls `recordMarketProposal`, every market reads "Eadwyn has no open proposal for this market". Follows D6119 and D6120 (GR-24 was not built there).
- **Migration:** `202611222112_8251_market_proposals.sql` — `market_proposals` (uid, market, kind `score | plan | comparison`, JSON payload and sources, state `proposed | accepted | dismissed`, who and when decided), cascading with its market. Additive.

### What this decides

1. **Nothing reaches a market without the founder's Accept.** Storing, listing and reading a proposal change no market field. `recordMarketProposal` is a service function for Eadwyn's run, not a route: no founder action creates a proposal.
2. **Kinds, validated before they are stored.** A score names one of the four parts, a whole number 0 to 10, and at least one source (a label, and an https address if it links). A plan is 1 to 12 steps, each with its text and an optional due day. A comparison is written text. Keys outside the kind are dropped.
3. **Accept writes through the existing paths.** A score goes through `updateMarket` (the Edit market path, so its own checks and its activity line apply) with the score and its sources as the source text; a plan goes through `addEntryStep`, one step each; a comparison has nothing to write and is only marked accepted. Dismiss writes nothing.
4. **Decided once.** Accept and Dismiss claim the proposal with a conditional update; a second decision is 409 and writes nothing. If the write fails, the proposal goes back to open so Accept can be retried. A plan that failed part-way may have added its first steps already; Accept again adds them again, so the founder removes the duplicates (a known edge, not guarded).
5. **Where the founder acts.** The routes are the record's actions, `accept-proposal` and `dismiss-proposal` (body `{ proposal }`), under `/api/cycles/grow/modules/market-expansion/records/:uid/actions/:key` like the entry-plan actions; the list is `parts.market.proposals` on the record read. They are declared for writers only: a reader sees the cards and no buttons, and a post is refused (403). A proposal of another market or venture is 404.
6. **A failed read** is `proposals: null` with `proposals_state: 'unreadable'` and reads Unavailable, never "no proposal".
7. **The page.** `MarketRecord.jsx` draws "Eadwyn proposes": one card per open proposal with its proposed and current score and its sources beside them, a plan's steps, or the comparison text, and Accept and Dismiss for a writer.
