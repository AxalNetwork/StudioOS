### 1. Proof: result cards, outcome first

`/offers/proof` is now OF-03's grid of result cards, two across at desktop and one on a phone. The po4 artboard's four tiles and its "Outcome shelf" are removed, because OF-03 draws neither.

**Each card, in the issue's order:**
1. the result (`outcome_note`, or the title when no result is written);
2. the client (`founder_name`) and the work (the title);
3. the consent label;
4. who agreed and when, then the engagement it is linked to;
5. whether it is published.

**Behind "View proof":** edit, delete, the consent record (D4672's rows) and the ask. System detail never leads the card.

**The labels (OF03c)** each have an icon, a word and one line, and never rely on colour alone. They come from `proofCards.js`, which reads `consentState` in `ProofZone.jsx` and the rows behind it:

| Label | When |
|---|---|
| ✓ Client approved | a confirmed consent about the claim as it reads now |
| … Approval requested | an open ask about the current wording |
| – Self-stated | nobody asked, or an ask lapsed (its line says so) |
| ✕ Withdrawn | a client who had approved took it back |
| ✕ Declined | a client said no without ever approving |
| ↺ Superseded | an approval was for an earlier wording |

**Superseded needs no backend.** OF03c marks it "Backend required". D4672's consent rows already carry `state: 'superseded'` when the claim changed after the answer, so the card reads it.

**Outlines (OF-10).** A client-approved card has a solid green outline, and a self-stated or superseded card a dashed one.

**The chips are OF-03's five:** All, Client approved, Approval requested, Self-stated and Withdrawn.
- Each carries this firm's count over the page of items.
- Each selects on the card's own label, so the chip, the label and the export agree.
- Superseded counts under Self-stated, and Declined under Withdrawn.

**Second action:**
- Client approved: Manage consent.
- Approval requested: Show request link.
- Self-stated and Superseded: Request client approval, which opens the ask.
- Withdrawn and Declined: View history.

**Empty state (OF03b):** "Show founders why they should hire you", with one action, Add your first proof.

### 2. Audience fit: one table, and a sheet of business questions

`/offers/audience-fit` is now OF-04's rank note and one rules table. The po5 tiles, the "Fit profiles" instrument and the "Who we are not for" card are removed.

**The rank note** is the canvas's sentence: "Pipeline uses these rules to rank and explain your leads. They never reject a founder on their own." The longer account opens from "How rules affect ranking", and it reads the Worker's own `enforcement_note`.

**The table** has the columns Rule, Who, Strength, What we say and Point them to.
- **Rule** is Great fit (a profile) or Not a fit, with its kind beneath.
- **Who** is the sector or capability. A budget floor reads "Projects below $25,000".
- **A rule with no sentence** reads "No reply yet", and its action is the primary Add reply.
- **On a phone** the same rows stack as cards, so no table is wider than the screen.

**The sheet (OF04b)** is the shared `ui/Sheet.jsx`, used both to add and to edit a rule. Its questions come in the canvas's order:
1. Who is a great fit? (Client type or sector)
2. How strong is this fit?
3. What should we say to them? On an exclusion this reads "When someone isn't a fit, what should we say?"
4. Can we point them somewhere else? (optional)

The three other rule kinds sit under "Advanced rule types", disclosed rather than leading. Choosing one changes the questions. Delete sits on the left, away from Save.

**Empty state:** "Tell Pipeline who your firm is for … Nothing is declined for you", with one action, Add your first fit rule.

### Departures from the canvas

| Board | Departs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| OF-03 | "Shown across Axal" and "Shown as your claim" read "Published." or "Not published." | No public profile or page publishes proof. The po4 build refused the same promise. |
| OF-03 | Resend request reads "Show request link" | No resend exists. An open request's link stays readable on the record (D4672), so the card shows it. |
| OF03c | A sixth label, Declined | A client who never approved and said no is not a withdrawal. "Withdrawn" would say they once approved. |
| OF-03 | The client line is the founder's name | The proof read has no company name. `founder_name` comes from the engagement's founder. |
| OF-04, OF04b | Strength reads Best fit, Qualified and Weak intent, not Strong, Good and Possible | These are the stored words. Pipeline's scoring receipts and this zone's chips use them, so renaming them on one screen would make the two disagree. |
| OF04b | Minimum engagement is the "Below a minimum engagement" kind under Advanced, not a field beside the sector | A budget floor is its own rule in `partner_fit_rules`. One Save writes one rule, so a failed save can never leave half a rule behind. |
| OF04b | The reply question follows the kind | On a great-fit profile the sentence is what to say to them, as OF-04's own "What we say" column shows. On an exclusion it is the pass reason. |
| OF-04 | The po5 chips (All, Best fit, Qualified, Weak) stay | OF-04 draws none. They select profiles by strength, and they narrow the table and the export together (#4696). |
| OF-04, OF-03 | The draft band, the Pass reasons modal and the stated limits stay below the content | They are working features outside the canvas frame. #4844 owns the shared layout. |
| OF-08 | A failed read is the shared ZoneBody card ("This did not load", Try again), not "Proof couldn't be loaded." | The card is shared by every zone. #4844 standardises the states. |
| OF-09 | No dark board | The canvas draws none. Every light class has its `dark:` pair. |
