## D6272 — Ask's Clear history hides and restores; the Ask and Diligence notes stop citing closed #1023 (#6272)

**Status:** built (2026-11-15). The owner's answer to the Clear history question came on #6274 (question 4, option **B**): hide, do not delete.

### What changed

- **Clear history is a live action for the investor.**
  - `POST /api/research/ask/history/clear` stamps `cleared_at` on every one of the caller's Ask sessions that is not already cleared.
  - `POST /api/research/ask/history/restore` sets it back to NULL.
  - Both statements are owner-scoped and take no id from the request. The audit log records only the count.
  - Nothing is deleted, so an answer a memo cites stays traceable.
- **The history reads respect it.**
  - `scope=all` and `scope=session` leave out answers in cleared sessions, and so do their whole-scope totals.
  - A new `scope=cleared` reads them back, paged like every other scope.
  - Every "latest session" read skips a cleared session, so the next question starts a fresh thread. A question sent with a cleared session's uid is not appended to it.
  - Each scope is still a written-out statement, with no SQL fragment spliced in.
- **Saved and Archived are unchanged.** They are the reader's own dispositions, chosen one answer at a time, so clearing the history does not hide them.
- **The investor's Ask filters.**
  - `Cited in a memo` is retired: no store can select it, because a citation names a passage and never a memo.
  - `Show cleared` is added. It shows the cleared answers with **Restore to history** beside them.
- **The Diligence notes now say where a room is asked for today.**
  - The `New request` and `Attach to deal` notes, and the `StatedLimit` on the zone and on a room, point to the deal itself: Documents → Ask for access (`POST /api/deals/:id/room-access/request`).
  - They no longer say they wait on "the deal-request routes in research.ts (slot S02)". This is interim wording until the room-list build replaces these controls.
