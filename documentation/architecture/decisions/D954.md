## D954 — Programs part 1: the program registry and playbook store (migration 560), the catalogue read with its per-studio form, Learning's weekly and learner reads, mentor sessions, and each program's activity (#2787)

- **Status:** S11, 2026-10-24. Part 1 of Programs (#2371, D953 §6). The owner moved the task from S10 to S11.
- **Basis:** `origin/main` at `6daf1a3173`. Worker paths are relative to `cloudflare-worker/src/`.
- **Migration:** 560, `560_program_registry.sql`. S1 reserved it in D953 §6.
- **Why:** D953's integration (#2784) draws the nine programs from a frontend constant and counts from capped lists. It reads "Not recorded" or "Not read here yet" for these:
  - Programs running and People taking part;
  - Credits paid out;
  - sessions booked with a mentor;
  - Learning's enrolments per week and PG12d's dates;
  - every Activity tab.

  This part gives each of those a read. It touches no frontend file. Parts 8, 9 and 10 draw what it adds, and add the clients.

### 1. The registry (`programs`)

- **Columns.** One row per program:
  - `key` (unique), `grp`, `name`, `purpose`, `who`, `join_method`, `status` and `position`, with `created_at` and `updated_at`;
  - `grp` is one of the canvas's six groups (dc:358);
  - `status` is `live`, `partly_built`, `not_built`, `proposed` or `paused`, the canvas's five words (dc:322);
  - a NULL `purpose`, `who` or `join_method` reads "Not recorded".
- **The seed** is the nine programs, keyed as D953 lists them. The words are D953 §3's card table, with its four corrected purposes.
  - AI-native Venture's purpose, Who and Join stay NULL until OD-AIN.
  - Referral partners' Who and Join stay NULL until part 5.
  - No bracketed canvas sample is stored. A test reads every stored string for a `[`.
- **The frontend constant.** #2784's constant was not on its branch when this landed, so the seed is D953 §3's text as that decision states it. If the constant ships different words, the "Copy after the seed" rule in D953 §6 applies: a later migration changes a row only where it still holds this seed.
- **`PATCH /api/admin/programs/:key`** takes `requireSuperAdmin`, and edits purpose, who, join and status.
  - A field that is not sent is left alone, and `null` clears one.
  - `paused` is refused with 400 `pause_not_decided`, because what Pause enforces is OD-PAUSE's.
  - Each save calls `logAdminAction` as `program.registry.update`, with the key and the fields.
- **An unknown key** answers 404 `unknown_program` on every `/:key` route.
- **Refusals keep the D278 body,** `error`, `message` and `detail`, through `refuse()`. They also carry `code`, which is what the catalogue's 400s and 403s (`bad_weeks`, `bad_scope`, `hq_only`) answer, as Insights does. So a page can key every refusal here on `code`.

### 2. The playbook store

- **The tables.**
  - `program_playbook_sections`: `program_key`, `version`, `section_key`, `position`, `title`, `body`, `computed`, `template_keys`, `edited_at` and `edited_by_user_id`. Version 0 is the draft.
  - `program_playbook_versions`: `program_key`, `version`, `published_at` and `published_by_user_id`.
- **One column beyond the issue: `computed`.** The issue says a section the page computes, such as Mentor's cycle, keeps only its heading. It also says a NULL body reads "Not set". A computed section would then read "Not set" for a body the page draws from live data, so `computed = 1` marks it. Its body stays NULL, and an edit that sends one is 400 `computed_section`.
- **The seed.** Version 1 is seeded for every program D953 §3 writes How it runs for:
  - Mentor cohort access, with eleven sections;
  - Refer & Earn, Perks, Learning and Referral partners;
  - Investor cohort access, as the design, with D953's sentence on what is not decided.

  The text is copied section by section from D953 §3, and from the canvas where D953 says "the canvas's". The draft is seeded as a copy of version 1, so an unedited draft does not differ.
  - AI-native Venture gets a draft of its seven sections (dc:458), all unset, and no published version until OD-AIN.
  - Spin-Out Lab's page is #2374's and Product testing's is #2382's, so neither has a playbook.
  - Mentor's "Terms and contracts" reads "No contract template is linked to this program yet.", with no template, because nothing links one (D953 §3).
- **`GET /api/admin/programs/:key`** takes `requireAdmin`.
  - It returns the registry row and the latest published version, with its number, date and sections.
  - A Super Admin also gets the draft: its sections, its latest `edited_at`, and `differs`, which compares the draft's content with what is published. That is PG5s's "Version [3] · edited [2 Oct]".
  - An Admin never gets the draft.
- **`PUT /api/admin/programs/:key/playbook/sections/:section`** takes `requireSuperAdmin`, and edits a draft section's title, body or `template_keys`.
  - An unknown section is 404 `unknown_section`.
  - Each template slug must be an active template in the contracts library's store (`listTemplates`, the list `GET /api/admin/contracts/templates/store` serves), or the save is 400 `unknown_template`, naming the slug.
- **`POST /api/admin/programs/:key/playbook/publish`** takes `requireSuperAdmin`, and makes the draft the next version.
  - It answers 409 `nothing_to_publish` when the draft is what is published.
  - The version row and its sections go in one batch. The version number is unique per program, so two publishes racing get 409 `publish_conflict` rather than both landing.
- **Audit.** Both writes call `logAdminAction`, as `program.playbook.edit` and `program.playbook.publish`.
- **On a branch,** `requireSuperAdmin` already refuses every write (`auth.ts`, D106).

### 3. The catalogue read, `GET /api/admin/programs?weeks=&scope=`

`routes/admin_programs.ts` serves it. It is mounted at `/api/admin/programs` beside Insights, before the `/api/admin` catch-all. `services/programCatalogue.ts` computes it.

- **The gate and the period.**
  - It takes `requireAdmin`.
  - `weeks` is 4, 12 or 52, and 12 when absent, the canvas's choice. Anything else is 400 `bad_weeks`.
  - Weeks are Monday weeks in UTC, from `weekAxis`, and the period before comes from `previousAxis`.
- **Counts only.** No name, email or account id leaves it. The strings that travel are program keys, statuses, groups, the registry's own words, weeks and months.
- **Each block answers alone,** in one of four shapes. A failed block never reads 0, and never takes another with it (PG18a).
  - `{ available: true, … }`: the block was read.
  - `{ available: false, unreadable: true, reason }`: the read failed. A page draws it as "Could not be read".
  - `{ recorded: false, reason }`: no store holds it.
  - `{ available: false, pending: true, reason }`: it is stored, but waits on another part's read. A page draws it as "Not read here yet", with the reason. It never reads 0, and never `unreadable`.
- **Pending today.** Part 4 (#2790) is not on this Worker, so each figure read through its services answers `pending`, with a reason naming the service. Those figures are:
  - Perks' claims and Perk claims per week (`services/perkClaims.ts`);
  - Strategic referral access, as a queue and in Refer & Earn's Needs you (the queue service in `services/referralSubmissions.ts`).

  When part 4 lands, it replaces these with its reads.

#### `programs`

Each registry row, in `position` order, with its card `figures` and its `needs_you`, each a block of its own:

| Program | Figures | Needs you |
|---|---|---|
| Spin-Out Lab | cohort applications waiting | cohort applications plus pending hosts |
| Mentor cohort access | `mentors` (S-CNT), `coverage` (S-COV) | cohorts with no mentor, plus open rows whose holder is no longer an Advisor |
| Refer & Earn | open referrals, with `waiting_on_member` | the same count, and `strategic_requests` (pending, part 4) |
| Perks | `live` (no cap), `claims` (pending, part 4) | listings `in_review` |
| Learning | published programs, active enrolments in them, the join methods present, in the viewer's scope | none |
| Product testing | for a Super Admin only: open programs, and the applications to them not withdrawn | none |
| AI-native Venture, Investor cohort access, Referral partners | none | none |

- **S-CNT** counts distinct people on active rows whose current role is Advisor.
  - A deactivated account still counts, and the block carries `DEACTIVATED_NOT_LEFT_OUT`, the line "Deactivated accounts are not left out yet."
  - Part 7 (#2793) is not on main, so it adds `users.is_active = 0` to this block and drops the reason when it serves account state.
- **S-COV** covers every cycle that is scheduled or running and not postponed, with no cap. Covered means at least one active row held by a current Advisor.
- **Live perks** use `GET /api/perks/`'s rule: `live`, and not past `ends_at`, with no cap.
- **Learning's scope** is an Admin's assigned programs, as `/programs/mine` scopes them, or every program for a Super Admin. It counts enrolments, not people.
- **Product testing's applications** are counted on open programs only, because the card reads "[n] open · [m] applied".

#### `tiles`

- **`running`:** the registry rows by status. **Programs running is the `live` count.**
- **`people` (S-PPL):** distinct accounts that took part in the period, and in the period before, for the delta. It is one `UNION` over the stores, so a person in two programs counts once:
  - an assignment active at any point in the period, from `assigned_at` and `unassigned_at`, or `is_active` for an open row;
  - an enrolment in a published Learning program active at any point in the period, from `enrolled_at`, and `updated_at` for a removed row;
  - a Refer & Earn submission made in the period;
  - a perk claimed in the period, read from `perk_claims` directly.

  The block names the programs it covers (`covers`). The Lab is left out and named until O-FND and #2374 say who a cohort's founders are.
  - **Perk claimants are read directly, not through part 4's service.** Part 4's reads are claims counted by listing, week and price type. None of them gives the distinct claimants a cross-program count needs. A claimant is a person, not a figure part 4 owns.
  - **The tile is deployment-wide** for an Admin too, because it counts people, and no name leaves it.
  - **Re-assignment.** A re-assignment overwrites a mentor row's dates (N16), so an assignment ended and re-opened reads as open since its latest date, until part 7's history.
- **`waiting`:** the total of the queues below that this Worker reads (`covers`: cohort, referrals, perks and hosts), and the oldest among them.
  - Strategic referral access is left out and named (`left_out`), as D953 §2 leaves it out.
  - A covered queue whose read fails makes the total unreadable, because a total that misses a queue is short.
- **`credits`:** item 4's totals over the period.

#### `waiting`

One block per queue, each with `count`, `oldest_hours`, and the three bands of S1-BANDS (`approvalSources.ts`'s `SLA_DUE_SOON_HOURS` and `SLA_PAST_HOURS`): under 24 hours, 24 to 72, and 72 or more.

| Queue | Rows | Aged |
|---|---|---|
| Cohort applications | `cohort_applicants` pending (the lane's SQL) | since applied |
| Refer & Earn reviews | the lane's three statuses, with `more_info_needed` counted apart as `waiting_on_member` | since submitted |
| Strategic referral access | pending (part 4) | — |
| Perk listings | `in_review` | since last edit (`updated_at`) |
| Office-hours hosts | `lab_host_applications` pending, every capacity | since applied |

- **Exact counts.** These are exact, so part 8 can drop "200+", "within the newest 500" and "at least [n]" (Guard L).
- **No recorded age.** A stamp that will not parse is counted as `age_not_recorded`, never as 0 hours.

#### `joins_weekly`

One block per series, per week of the axis, each with its total and the period before's total:
- Referrals submitted (`referral_submissions.created_at`).
- Learning enrolments, from item 6's events, in the viewer's scope.

### 4. Credits (O-MONEY)

- **A Super Admin** gets `credits_monthly`: each month the period touches, summed in SQL from `perk_credit_ledger`.
  - **Granted by source:**
    - `lab` (`lab:*`);
    - `bounty` (`isBountyRule`, in SQL with `substr`);
    - `manual` (granted by HQ);
    - `referral` (the `referral:` rule keys);
    - `other_rule`;
    - `no_rule`, a grant from before migration 381, on a line of its own, so the total is never short.
  - **Spent.**
  - **Deducted:** the `admin_adjust` lines, which are deductions and revokes.
  - **Refunded:** the `refund` lines.

  Deducted and refunded are each summed apart, and never netted into a grant or a spend.
- **Referrals.** While `credit_reward_rules` holds no `referral:` rule, `referral` is NULL in each month and the block's `referral` is `{ recorded: false }`, with "Referral rewards are not paid in perk credits yet." Part 12 seeds the rules.
- **Partial months.** The first and last months are marked `partial` when the period covers only part of them.
- **The tile** is the period's totals of the same figures.
- **An Admin** gets both blocks as `pending`, with no figure and "HQ reads these figures.", which is D953 §2's "Not read here yet".

### 5. The per-studio form

- **`scope`** works as on Insights.
  - No scope, or this deployment's own (`ownScope`), reads this deployment.
  - Another scope, from a plain Admin or from anyone on a branch, is 403 `hq_only` before any read.
  - A malformed scope is 400 `bad_scope`.
- **A Super Admin on HQ** gets that subsidiary's answer through the fan-out: `branchScopedPrograms`, shaped like `branchScopedInsights`.
  - The branch serves `programs()` on its RPC (`rpc/index.ts`, `branchPrograms` in `rpc/branchOps.ts`). It runs this read over its own D1 as HQ's Super Admin reads it, with `weeks` as the only parameter that crosses.
  - `RPC_SURFACE` gains `programs` after `insights`.
- **Counts only, on both sides.** Both sides pass the answer through `countsOnly`, which drops every key that names a person or a record, `name` included. So a branch answers program keys and counts, and HQ adds the names from its own registry.
- **A branch that does not answer** gives every block unreadable with its reason, never 0.
- **The studios listed** are `GET /api/admin/insights/scopes`'s, and the label comes from that registry.

### 6. Learning: enrolments per week (`learning_enrolment_events`)

- **The table.** A re-enrolment overwrote `enrolled_at`, so a learner's first date was lost. 560 adds `learning_enrolment_events`: `program_id`, `user_id`, `kind` (`enrolled` or `removed`), `by_user_id` and `at`.
- **The writes,** each in the same batch as the change it records:
  - a self-enrolment writes `enrolled`;
  - the Super Admin's enrol writes `enrolled` only when the row becomes active, never for a learner already active;
  - a removal writes `removed` only when it removes someone.
- **The backfill.** One `enrolled` event per existing row at its `enrolled_at`, and one `removed` event per removed row at its `updated_at`, with no `by_user_id`.
- **Re-enrolments before 560 are not recovered.** A re-enrolment made before 560 has already overwritten its first `enrolled_at`. So the weekly read counts it once, at its later date, and counts a re-enrolment again only from 560 on.
- **`GET /api/learning/programs/mine/enrolments?weeks=4|12|52`** has `/programs/mine`'s gate and scope. It returns the `enrolled` events per program and per week, and the period's total.
  - A learner removed later still counts in the week they enrolled.
  - A re-enrolment counts again.
  - A bad `weeks` is 400 `bad_weeks`, in Learning's refusal shape.

### 7. Learning: the learner record's dates and kinds

- **Each `completionFor` cell** gains the date its status was reached:
  - a course or an item: `completed_at`. A course done through its required lessons takes the latest of their `completed_at`;
  - an exam: `passed_at`, its first passing attempt's `submitted_at`;
  - an exercise: its latest submission's `submitted_at` and `reviewed_at`.
- **Each learner** gains `last_activity_at`. It is the latest `learning_progress.updated_at`, submission or attempt over this program's contents only: its items and courses, the courses' lessons (required or not), its exercises and its exams. Activity elsewhere does not count.
- **`describeContents`** returns an item's `kind`, `video` or `reading`, and null for other kinds.
- **Served** on the two learner reads as before: name and uid, never an email. Which statuses count as Done is OD9's, in part 9.

### 8. Learning: the assigned-admin count

`GET /api/learning/programs/mine` gives each program `admins`, its count of `learning_program_admins` rows, as the Super Admin's list counts them. It is never names.

### 9. Sessions booked with a mentor, `GET /api/admin/programs/mentor-cohorts/sessions`

- **Gate and cycles.** It takes `requireAdmin`, and reads each of R7's twelve newest cycles.
- **The rule.** The figure is the bookings with any mentor assigned to that cycle whose slot starts inside the cycle's window.
  - A booking is `advisor_bookings` joined to `advisor_office_hour_slots`. The slot's `advisor_id` is the mentor's `advisors` row, not their user id.
  - It counts `status != 'cancelled'`, as `telegramAggregator.ts` counts sessions booked, on a slot with `is_cancelled = 0`.
  - An ended assignment still counts its window, because the sessions happened while it was open.
- **It counts by mentor and window,** not by the founder's cohort, because a cohort's founders wait on O-FND.
- **Counts only:** each cycle's month, window, status, mentors and sessions.

### 10. Each program's activity, `GET /api/admin/programs/:key/activity?before=&limit=`

- **Paging.** It takes `requireAdmin`. It reads newest first, at most 50 rows a page, with a cursor (`next_before`, sent back as `before`).
  - The cursor is the row's time, source and id, so rows at the same second are never skipped.
  - A bad cursor is 400 `bad_cursor`, and a bad limit is 400 `bad_limit`.
- **The rows** are the `admin_audit_log` rows in the program's own action list (`PROGRAM_ACTIVITY_ACTIONS` in `services/programActivity.ts`), plus the registry and playbook writes on that program, matched on its key.
  - Learning's list is its seven `learning.program.*` actions.
  - A row whose details are not JSON is still listed, and names nothing.
- **Refer & Earn** also reads the `referral_submission_events` rows its admin review writes: an admin's, never the referrer's own submission or context. So the review needs no audit row.
- **Each row** has `at`, `kind`, `by` and `concerns`.
  - `by` is `hq` when the admin holds the Super Admin elevation today, and `admin` otherwise. It is never a name or an email.
  - **`by` reads today's elevation.** It is read from `super_admins` now, not at the time of the write, because the audit row does not record it.
  - `concerns` names a program by its key, a Learning program by its name, or a referral by its uid, category and status.
- **On Learning,** an Admin reads only the rows for programs assigned to them.
- **A write that is not audited yet** is named, as `not_audited: { recorded: false, reason }`, never as an empty list:
  - Perks' review, until part 4 (#2790);
  - Mentor's assign and end, until part 7 (#2793).

  A part that writes audit rows a program's Activity reads adds its action names to that program's list and removes its `NOT_AUDITED_YET` line. That is part 4 for Perks and Refer & Earn, part 5 for Referral partners, part 6 for Investor cohort access and part 7 for Mentor. None of those parts is on main yet.
