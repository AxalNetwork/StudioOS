## D5043 — HQ Team: a cross-branch move whose destination invitation did not land can be finished (#5043)

- **Status:** Built for #5043, 2026-11-09. Package 4 of the HQ Team audit (P1).
- **Builds on:** D121 (the move: source closes, destination invites, two facts), D133 (an administrator is unbound, not moved), D163 (mirror against the end that failed), D259 (a branch user id is never an HQ id in audit), D262, D441 (`invite_link` only when the mail did not leave).
- **Two migrations:** `202611091210_hq_account_moves.sql` (HQ's record of each move) and `202611091211_branch_invitation_move_ref.sql` (`branch_invitations.move_ref`, so the destination keeps one invitation per move).

### Built (2026-11-09)

**The recovery contract.** A move now has an operation, and recovery takes that operation and nothing else.

1. **Recorded before the source is called.** `POST /api/admin/branches/:code/accounts/:userId/move` writes an `hq_account_moves` row (`mvop_…`, status `started`) before `moveAccountOut`. A move HQ cannot record is refused 503 `move_not_recorded` with nothing done on either branch. A source refusal marks the row `source_refused`.
2. **The proof is the source's answer.** Only after `moveAccountOut` returns does the row get `moved_out_at` and the target's email, name and role *as the source reported them*, and move to `inviting`. The invitation then lands (`invited`, with `invite_uid`, `invite_email_sent`, `invite_email_reason`) or fails (`invite_failed`, with `last_error_code`: `destination_unreachable`, `destination_refused`, `destination_active` or `destination_not_bound`). The move's reply adds `operation: { uid, recoverable }`, `invited.stored`, `invited.code` on failure, and `invited.invite_link` when the destination's mail did not leave (the branch already returned it; HQ's route dropped it).
3. **`POST /api/admin/account-moves/:uid/retry-invitation`, no body.** The move's own bar in the move's order (TOTP, recent step-up, Super Admin) and the recovery cool-off (added to `COOL_OFF_ROUTES`). Then:
   - 400 `bad_operation` for a malformed id, 404 `move_not_found` for an unknown one;
   - 409 `move_out_not_proven` when the row has no `moved_out_at` (the source refused, or never answered): inviting someone whose account may still be live would give them two homes;
   - **idempotent:** a row already `invited` answers what landed with `retried: false` and calls no branch;
   - **one at a time:** a conditional UPDATE claims the row (`invite_failed`, or an `inviting` claim older than two minutes, treated as abandoned); a second press gets 409 `retry_in_progress`;
   - the invitation is built from the **row**: email, name, role, source, destination, reason. The handler never reads a request body, so it cannot invite an arbitrary person onto a branch. No general "invite to branch" route exists;
   - the outcome is recorded first and audited after (`hq_account_move_invite_retried`, every attempt and refusal past the not-found check, with `operation`, `branch_user_id` and the outcome). `logAdminAction` never throws, so an audit store that is down does not undo a landed invitation;
   - "stored" and "sent" stay two facts in the reply: `invited.stored`, `invited.email_sent`, `invited.reason`, and `invited.invite_link` only when the mail did not leave.
4. **The destination keeps one invitation per move.** `inviteAccount` takes `move_ref` (the operation uid) and looks it up **before** its active-account refusal. A pending invitation whose mail left is answered again unchanged (`reused: true`, no new token, so the link in their inbox still works). A pending one whose mail did not leave gets a new token and a new send, because its only link went out in an answer HQ never heard or did not keep. An accepted one is the move finished (`reused`, `accepted`), not a conflict. With no match, an active account with that email is refused as before, and HQ reports it as 409 `destination_active`: "the destination account became active independently".
5. **`GET /api/admin/account-moves`** (Super Admin) lists what HQ Team still has to finish: moves whose source closed the account and whose invitation failed or is in flight, plus invitations that landed without their email in the last 14 days (the invitation's life), so the manual next step survives a refresh.

**HQ Team.**
- `AccountMoveRecovery.jsx` draws **Moves to finish** above the roster (HQ view only), from the read above. Each card names the account, `from → to`, says the account is closed on the source and that moving them again would be refused, carries the records note, and offers **Retry invitation**, which sends the operation id alone. Nothing is drawn when there are none; a failed read is Unreadable with Retry, never "none".
- On a branch hit, once the move answers, the hit shows the same outcome card in place of its controls: Move is never drawn again for that account.
- After a retry, `accountMoveModel.js` draws three separate lines: invitation created or not; email sent, not sent (with the branch's reason) or not reported; and, when it was not sent, the manual next step with the link if the branch handed it over. No line says the person was contacted or told. A refusal shows the Worker's sentence; a retry whose answer never arrived says the outcome is not known and offers "Read the moves again".

### Departures and limits
- **HQ stores the moved person's email** on the move row, because the retry must send what the source recorded and the source is not asked again. It is shown only to the Super Admin, on the open-moves read.
- **The link is not kept at HQ.** It is shown once, in the reply that created the invitation. After a refresh, an invitation whose mail did not leave says so and names the person to reach, but cannot show the link again; re-issuing it is the destination's.
- **An in-session move is not added to Moves to finish until the next read** (a refresh or a page reload key), so one move is never drawn twice; the hit's own card carries the retry until then.
- **A move whose `moved_out_at` could not be written** (HQ's database failed between the two legs) replies `operation.recoverable: false`, and the card says Retry invitation is not offered for it.
