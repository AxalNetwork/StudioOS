## D9037 — The public-route axe gate runs in CI on the built SPA, with no exceptions (#9037)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-27 (S31). Under #8436.
- **Builds on:** D8537, the local Worker in `frontend/tests/e2e/_helpers.js` and the `visual` job. D8537.md cites `axe-top-routes.spec.js` as the gate its journeys match; until now no workflow ran that spec.
- **No migration, no Worker route, no client method, no new dependency.**

### The decision

`frontend/tests/e2e/axe-top-routes.spec.js` runs in `ci.yml` job `visual` on the PR's own build, with `--retries=0 --trace off`. It uses `startLocalWorker()` to serve `docs/`, signed out. Off-origin requests are aborted and the service worker is blocked.

- **Ten routes, each at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, light and dark (40 tests):** `/`, `/login`, `/register`, `/auth/recover`, `/about`, `/pricing`, `/contact`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/help`. `/forgot-password`, `/legal/terms`, `/legal/privacy` and `/docs` were never routes; each fell to NotFoundPage. They are replaced by the routes that do exist.
- **Dark** means the visitor's own stored Appearance (`axal_appearance_v1`) plus the browser's colour scheme. A page that pins light for itself (`useForcedLightTheme`) stays light, as it does for that visitor.
- **A route must be a page.** It must answer 200 and must not render "Page not found". The `PLAYWRIGHT_BASE_URL` skip and the ≥ 400 skip are gone: a route that drifts to 404 fails.
- **No exceptions.** The gate fails on any serious or critical WCAG 2.0/2.1 A or AA violation. No axe rule is disabled and no element is excused. The named `KNOWN` list in D8537 (G3) belongs to `public_journey.spec.js` and its signed-in pages, and this gate does not use it.

### What the first run found, and the fixes

34 of the 40 tests failed:

- **24: the public header's logo link** (`label-content-name-mismatch`, every PublicNav page). It was named "Axal VC home" and showed "Axal VC" plus the release badge ("alpha"). It is now named by `logoHomeName()` in `AxalLogo.jsx` ("Axal VC alpha home"). `AxalLogo` also puts a space between the wordmark and the badge, so the link's text reads "Axal VC alpha", not "Axal VCalpha". This is the element D8537 G3 listed to be filed.
- **2: `/help`**, the "N guides are public" line under the search box. It was gray-500 on the violet-50 hero, 4.4:1, and is now gray-600.
- **2: `/auth/recover` in dark**, "Back to sign in" and the lead line. They were gray-600 on the gray-900 page, 2.3:1. Every gray-500/600 line on the page now has a dark variant.
- **8: the spec itself.** It waited for an `h1`, but `/register` and `/auth/recover` head with an `h2`. It now waits for the page's own first heading. A missing `h1` is not a WCAG A/AA failure that axe reports here, so this gate does not judge it.

### Mutation note

Removing only the `<label>` on register's Email field is not caught: axe takes the field's placeholder as its name, and that is not a WCAG A/AA failure. Removing the label and the placeholder together fails `label` on all four `/register` tests.
