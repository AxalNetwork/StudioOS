### 2. The boards

**TG1 Connect Telegram: D863's six steps.**
- **The steps:** Link with a code, Bot added in Telegram Business, Bot on, Replies allowed, First chat in, Consent notice ready.
- **Each step's state:** Done, To do, or Waiting (blocked by an earlier step). A step still to do carries the Worker's own sentence for what to change in Telegram.
- **What is pressed here.** The steps are done in Telegram, so only one thing is pressed on this page: **Link with a code**, while the account is not linked and no link is already in progress.
  - Its dialog says what it makes and that nothing is linked until both sides confirm.
  - It then shows the code and the bot's `t.me` link. Any other link is not offered.
- **Check again** reads the steps again.
- **The Premium prerequisite** reads "Not checked by Axal". D863 records that Axal cannot see a member's Premium, so the page never says it is held.
- **The side cards** say Axal never asks for the Telegram login code, and link to Send to Axal.

**TG2 Chats inbox.** WhatsApp's shared inbox, opening on All (the canvas's filter), so both channels share one list.
- Telegram rows read consent in Telegram's words:

  | State | Shown as |
  | --- | --- |
  | `not_asked` | Text in · notes held |
  | `awaiting` | Asked · notes held |
  | `given` | Given |
  | `declined` | Declined |
  | `withdrawn` | Stopped |

  The text enters an assigned session at once (D684); notes wait for the contact's own YES (D756 §1.5).
- A WhatsApp row in the same list keeps WhatsApp's words.

**TG3 Session transcript.** WhatsApp's transcript, with Original · Translation · Both and Translation pending.
- **Deleted.** A message the contact deleted in Telegram is a tombstone, "Deleted in Telegram. Nothing of it is kept.", and none of its text is shown. Its segment reads `[Deleted at the source]`, D866's marker.
- **Edited.** A message the contact edited is marked "Edited in Telegram". Its segment's model is D866's `channel:telegram:change:…`.
- **The consent banner** reads Telegram's line: notes sent before consent were held, then kept, and "stop" ends keeping at once.

**TG4 Analytics.** WhatsApp's figures and trend, read with `channel: telegram`.
- Every figure carries its Source chip, or Not recorded with the Worker's reason.
- See §3 for the split.

**TG5 Proposals.** WhatsApp's proposals. A prototype ask reads "Build candidate" (the canvas's word for Telegram; WhatsApp keeps "For Build").

**TG6 Send to Axal.**
- How to forward a voice note or a recording to the Axal bot.
- The held capture, "Waiting for your answer", with the bot's own question and its three answers. They are the words of `telegramCapture.ts` `CAPTURE_COPY`, and a test holds the two to the same text. Until the founder answers, nothing is downloaded or transcribed.
- The saved state, which links to Customer research.

**TG7 MCP access.**
- What Eadwyn can read.
- What the founder's own AI clients can read through Axal's MCP: the chat list without message text, and session insights read-only.
- What is never read: text from anyone who has not agreed, phone numbers in full, the Telegram account or login.
- "Axal never asks for your Telegram login code."

**TG-M Phone.** Every target is at least 44px tall, the rows stack and the dialogs are bottom sheets, as D862's are.

**TG-S States.**
- **Empty:** "Telegram is not linked yet", with Link with a code.
- **Loading** and **Error:** "could not be read. That is not the same as having none", with Try again. A 401 or 403 is **No permission**.
- **Canary-only:** Telegram is offered but not open to this founder (`available: false` during `TELEGRAM_CHANNEL = canary`), and no connection exists. A founder already connected keeps their chats.

### 3. Departures from the canvas

- **No "Ask to join" on canary-only.** No route takes a request to join the canary.
- **The venture view does not split chats by channel.** The analytics read takes one channel at a time, and Telegram's page reads Telegram's. The canvas's WhatsApp-and-Telegram split would need a read per channel and a second trend; it is not drawn.
- **Round videos are not shown as their own rows.** A round video is refused while `TELEGRAM_VIDEO_NOTES` is off (`telegram.ts`), and a transcribed note's segment carries its speech model, not its media. So a note that was transcribed reads "voice note", and a refused one never reaches the session.
- **TG6 lists no forwarded recordings.** No Worker read lists Telegram captures (WhatsApp has `GET /channels/whatsapp/captures`). A saved one is a session in Customer research. The held and saved cards describe the states, and the question and answers are the bot's own.
- **TG7 lists no connected AI clients.** No read lists a founder's MCP clients; the board says so rather than drawing invented ones.
- **The inbox row's last message, language and note count** are not shown, as in D862 §3: `ThreadView` carries none of them.

### 4. Security and privacy

- **Consent first (D756):**
  - text enters only an assigned session;
  - notes wait for the contact's YES;
  - a forwarded recording waits for the founder's answer.
- **Phone numbers:** a contact name that is a phone number is masked.
- **The link code** is shown only to the founder who pressed for it; the `t.me` link is the only link offered.
- **Scope:** every read is the Worker's, scoped to the venture's founder. Nothing here widens it.
