### 2. Diligence (DL7, DL7n)

**The case.**
- **Where it comes from:** the investor's own case list (`GET /api/dd/cases?subject_type=project`, which the Worker scopes to cases you opened, lead or review). The newest case on this company that is not archived is drawn.
- **The card:** the stage, the risk band (or "Risk not scored yet"), items worked of the checklist with a bar, open flags, who opened it and when, and your own section.
- **Opening it:** "Open the case" goes to `/admin/due-diligence/:uid`, the case's own page.
- **A list cut short:** if the list stopped at its page before reaching a case on this company, it says so, never "no case".

**Requests to the founder.**
- They come from the case read (`GET /api/dd/cases/:uid`), each with one of these:
  - "Waiting [n] days", with D1248's due date when one is set;
  - "Answered" or "Reviewed", with its date;
  - "Withdrawn [date]", for a request D1248 withdrew, which never counts as waiting.
- The Worker returns requests to the case's owner and lead only. A section reviewer reads that the requests are the owner's, never "none sent".

**No case yet (DL7n).** "Open a diligence case" opens a dialog that says what a case holds. It then opens one on this company through the investor's scoped path (`POST /api/dd/cases`, `subject_type: project`).

**Reference calls** are today's `DealReferencesTab`.

**Eadwyn's questions** are a labelled slot: no read drafts them for a deal.

### 3. Documents and data room (DL8, DL8a–c, DL9)

**The three access states**, from `GET /api/deals/:id/room-access` (§6) and, once you hold a grant, `GET /api/data-room/shared/:projectUid`:

| State | What it says |
|---|---|
| Not opened to you | Ask for access. If you already asked, it says when and that the founder decides. A declined request shows its reason. |
| Behind an NDA | "You have access. [n] files are behind an NDA.", with Sign the NDA. If the NDA is already out for signature, it says so instead. |
| NDA on file | "You have access and an NDA on file until [date]." |

**Behind an NDA, a withheld file is counted, never named.**
- The room read returns only the files you may open, and `withheld_behind_nda` as a number (`data_room.ts` `investorView`).
- The page lists exactly the read's files. The count reads "[n] files need your NDA · Their names show once you sign."
- The room is never read without a grant.

**Files.** Folders with their counts, search, and Open. Open asks for the one-time download link (`POST /api/data-room/shared/:projectUid/files/:uid/download`), whose use the founder sees.

**The dialogs (DL9).**
- **Ask for access:** an optional message of up to 2,000 characters, sent as D1104's deal-page request.
- **Sign the NDA:** starts the three-way NDA (`POST /api/trust/intro/request`). The result stays in the dialog: already on file, or out for signature, with your own signing link when the Worker returns it.

**Deal paper.** The envelopes on this deal you are party to (`GET /api/legal/esign?deal_id=`, whose scope is the e-sign read's own), each with its state.

**The deck** is a slot, as on Overview (PITCH #2434).

### 4. Terms (DL10)

**Proposed terms**, each "From the deal":
- instrument, round size, valuation cap, minimum, carry, management fee, vehicle jurisdiction and closing date;
- a field the deal leaves empty reads "Not recorded · The deal does not record it.";
- discount, pro rata, information rights and board seat have no field: "Not recorded · No field on the deal holds it yet." None is ever drawn as 0.

**The legal paper and the cap table panel** are slots:
- no read ties a template to a deal;
- the panel is CAPTABLE part 12's (#2864), as S21's note on #3058 says.

**The hand-off:** a link to the closing room, which holds your line and review-and-sign.

**How you invest (an investor):**
- the three routes, drawn as information;
- "The studio sets the route in the closing room. No preference is recorded here yet.", because no store keeps a route preference;
- Continue to signing opens the closing room.

### 5. Commitment and closing (DL11, DL12, DL13)

**Your commitment** comes from `GET /api/deals/:id/commitments` (an investor's own rows):
- the amount, the status and when it was recorded;
- your note, with who reads it: "You, your firm, the founder and the studio read this note." The canvas's "only you and your firm" is not today's truth: the Worker returns the note to the founder and to admins;
- earlier withdrawn ones, as history.

**Change amount** (pending only) and **Commit** open today's commit dialog. A confirmed commitment changes only by withdrawing it.

**Withdraw (DL12)** opens a dialog with a required reason of up to 500 characters. Nothing is sent until a reason is written. It goes to the reasoned withdrawal (§6).

**From your Mercury** is `FromMercury` with `section="commit"`.

**The closing room** is a labelled card with its door (D1213).

**The round** (`GET /api/deals/:id/round`, §6):
- the confirmed total of the round's target, with its bar;
- what is pending;
- "[n] investors have a live commitment";
- "Other investors are counted, never named, and their amounts are not shown."

**Syndicates (DL13).** Today's `DealSyndicates`, with its drawer and Close, under "The syndicates you lead or belong to". DL13's "a syndicate you are not in" is not drawn: `GET /api/networkfx/syndicates` lists only syndicates you lead or belong to, and the page says so.

### 6. The Worker: `routes/deal_workroom.ts`

**Why a new file.** `routes/deals.ts` and `routes/data_room.ts` belonged to open PRs when this was built (#3272, and #3296, since merged), so neither is edited. The new router is mounted at `/api/deals` before `deals`, as `dealContext` is. The Professional gate on `/api/deals/*` covers it unchanged.

**Who may call it.** An Investor only: anyone else gets 403 `investor_only`. It also needs a deal DEAL's rule opens to them (`investorHasDealRelationship`). A deal you are not on answers 404, as one that does not exist.

| Route | Does |
|---|---|
| `GET /:id/room-access` | `{ project_uid, founder_user_id, granted: { expires_at } \| null, nda: { status: on_file \| out_for_signature, valid_until } \| null, request }`. The NDA is the venture founder's, as `projectNdaActive` reads it. |
| `POST /:id/room-access/request` | `{ message? }` (at most 2,000 characters) through D1104's `dealPageRequestStatement`: one open request per investor and venture. With a live grant: 409 `already_granted`. |
| `GET /:id/round` | `{ investors, confirmed_total, pending_total, currency }`. Distinct investors with a pending or confirmed commitment; no name, note or single amount. |
| `POST /:id/commitments/:uid/withdrawal` | `{ reason }`, required, at most 500 characters. One batch does three things in order. |

**The withdrawal's batch:**
1. The conditional withdrawal of your own live commitment, under the firm it is held under.
2. An `activity_logs` row `deal_commitment_withdrawn` with the reason, written only when step 1 changed a row (`changes() > 0`).
3. The deal's confirmed total.

A commitment that is not yours, or is already withdrawn, is 404 and writes nothing.

**Logs** hold outcomes only; a message or a reason is stored, never logged.

**`deals.ts`'s own `POST /:id/commitments/:uid/withdraw`** still takes no reason. The page no longer calls it. Requiring a reason there, or retiring it, waits for #3272 to leave the file.
