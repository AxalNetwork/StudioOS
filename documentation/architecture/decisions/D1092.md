### 1. Customer research: a Doc becomes an interview's notes

The founder chooses a Doc for Customer research (part 2's Picker) and maps it to one of the venture's interviews: `PUT /api/connectors/google-workspace/files/:uid/mapping` with `{ interview_id }`. Part 2's route took a mapping for Sheets and Forms only. It now also takes one for a **Customer research Doc**; a Doc in any other module still refuses.

**The sync:**
- reads `files.get` (its `modifiedTime`), then `documents.get`;
- turns the Doc into segments: paragraphs and table cells in order, one per line;
  - a line that opens with a short label and a colon ("Me: …", "[Customer 4]: …") is that speaker's;
  - any other line is "Notes";
  - bounded at 200,000 characters and 2,000 lines;
- opens a `text` session with `interviewIntake.openSession(…, 'google_docs', 'text')`, then `importTranscript`. Structure and Ask read it as they read a pasted chat.

**The venture is re-checked:**
- `interviewIntake` re-checks the member and the venture on every write (`writableInterview`, `writableSession`);
- the feed also refuses an interview on another of the member's own ventures, since a file is chosen per venture.

**When the Doc changes,** the next sync replaces that same session's text; an unchanged `modifiedTime` reads nothing more.

**The badge.** A session made this way carries `submitted_via 'google_docs'`. The frontend draws it as "From Google Docs", never as an assistant's. `google_docs` is no channel connection: it has no secrets, webhook or job, and `availability()` does not offer it.

### 2. Demand signals: a Form's qualifying responses wait to be confirmed

**The mapping (GW5)** is `{ question_id, answers: [1–20], kind }`, with `kind` one of `DEMAND_KINDS` (default `waitlist`).

**`POST /api/cycles/validate/google/forms/:uid/preview`** returns, and stores nothing:
- the Form's questions with their offered choices;
- for a mapping, how many responses it read and how many qualify: "18 responses would be proposed as demand signals to confirm".

**The sync:**
- checks the mapped question still exists; otherwise the file reads `mapping_question_missing`;
- reads `forms.responses.list` 100 a page, up to 500 responses;
- writes each new qualifying response through `writeDemandSignal`:
  - `status 'to_confirm'`, `source_kind 'google_forms'`;
  - the response's submit date;
  - the note "From Google Forms: “<form name>”".

**No respondent identity.** A response is read for three things only: its id, its date and its answer to the mapped question. `respondentEmail` and every other answer are never read, stored, returned or logged. The test puts an email, a name and free text in another answer, then scans every table, every log line and every reply.

**Counted only once confirmed.** The Demand card counts no `to_confirm` signal and asks the founder to confirm them, as it does today.

### 3. Experiments: confirmed signals offered as evidence

**Each sync** offers every confirmed or counted Google Forms signal once, as a `google_workspace_proposals` row:
- module `experiments`, kind `signal_evidence`;
- source key `signal:<id>`, fingerprint `signal-<id>`.

The same sync **withdraws** a pending offer whose signal went back to `to_confirm` or is gone.

**Accept** (`POST /google/proposals/:id/accept`, `{ hypothesis_id, direction?, strength? }`):
- needs the hypothesis the founder picks;
- refuses a signal no longer confirmed (409);
- links it through `addEvidence` (`kind 'signal'`, `ref_id signal-<id>`, `supports` by default), the writer the Experiments page uses.

**Dismiss** stays dismissed. A decided offer is never offered again: its source key is unique.

### 4. Once, and deleted stays deleted

**Every landed item has a `connector_fed_items` row:**
- a Doc is keyed `<drive file id>:<venture>`;
- a response is keyed `<drive file id>:<venture>:<response id>`.

Part 2's Stop using unlinks rows by that prefix.

**A key is claimed before anything lands,** so two syncs at once land it once. A row whose `local_id` is NULL was deleted or stopped: it is never landed again, and the Doc is not even read.

**`DELETE /google/facts/:id`** (the ledger id) removes:
- a signal, with its evidence links and its pending offer;
- or a session (`deleteSession`, after `writableSession`).

From Google then counts it under `deleted`.

### 5. The routes and the read

| Route | What it does |
|---|---|
| `POST /api/cycles/validate/google/sync?project=` | Lands the chosen Docs and Forms, offers evidence. Answers counts: imported, unchanged, unmapped, unreadable, signals proposed, offers made and withdrawn, `partly`. |
| `POST /google/forms/:uid/preview?project=` | GW5's preview. Stores nothing. |
| `POST /google/proposals/:id/accept?project=` | Links the signal to the hypothesis picked. |
| `POST /google/proposals/:id/dismiss?project=` | Dismisses the offer for good. |
| `DELETE /google/facts/:id?project=` | Deletes a landed signal or session; the next sync leaves it deleted. |

**The gate** is part 2's:
- the member's own Workspace connection, enabled for the venture's company;
- founders and their team only, never an admin or a support session.

**Logs:** each change logs with Validate's write log, counts only.

**Limits:**
- 20 files and 40 Google reads per sync;
- a 429 or the read budget stops it as `partly`, and never calls the file unreadable;
- an unreadable file says why in `last_error` (a code).

**`parts.from_google`** is on Customer research, Demand signals and Experiments:
- the viewer's chosen files, with state and mapping;
- what landed, labelled with its source;
- pending offers;
- the deleted count.

A partner or a support session reads `team_only`; a venture whose company is not allowed reads `not_connected`. A failed read is `unreadable` for this part only: the module's records still read.
