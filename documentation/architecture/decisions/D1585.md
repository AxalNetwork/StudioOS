## D1585 — The brief for an investor meeting, with no model run (#3832)

- **Status:** built 2026-11-02. Deck v2 F6, part of #3716.
- **Spec:** the issue's final canvas revision (S1, 2026-11-01) and S1's release note (2026-11-02). The feature lives on the investor meeting in the Calendar, as "Prep"; no deck studio board draws it.
- **Builds on:**
  - D1190 (#3124): an investor meeting on the founder's calendar (`services/calendar/investorMeetings.ts`);
  - D1565 (#3812): the deck links made for a prospect;
  - D1568 (#3815): the follow-up signals of their views (`raiseSignals.ts` `signalsForLinks`);
  - D913 and D1582: the objections, with each one's count for one prospect (`of_prospect`).
- **Migration:** none.
- **Deferred:** `objections_extract`, which would read a meeting bot's transcript. The meeting bot is not deployed.

### What was decided

- **One read, no model.** `GET /api/cycles/raise/prospects/:id/brief?project=&event=` (`services/prospectBrief.ts`) answers from rows the venture already holds. Nothing is quoted, held or charged.
- **Who may read it:** the same rule as every Capital relationships path (`scope`, D1191): the venture's founder and accepted co-founder editors. Staff are pointed at the reasoned support read (`support_read_required`). A prospect of another venture answers 404.
- **What it holds:**
  - `prospect`: name, firm, stage, warm path, next step and its due day. Never their email.
  - `meeting`: the meeting asked for (`event`, the calendar item's step), else the next meeting or call with a start, else null. An `event` that is not this prospect's meeting or call is ignored.
  - `last_met`: the latest meeting or call before now, else null.
  - `timeline`: the five latest logged steps, newest first.
  - `deck`: each link made for them on this venture's decks, newest first (at most 10), with its views, read seconds, whether it was withdrawn, and its signals in words ("Opened", "Revisited after 48 h", "Reached the ask").
  - `objections`: what they raised first (`theirs`, ordered by how often they said it), then up to three the venture heard only from others (`elsewhere`).
  - `basis`: one sentence saying where it was read from and that no model ran.
- **A part that cannot be read says so.** `timeline`, `deck` and `objections` each answer `{ state: 'unreadable' }` on a failed read, and the rest of the brief still answers. An unreadable part is never drawn as "none".
- **The shape is a contract.** F3's ranking (#3829) and F9's `raise.prospect_brief` (#3835) read this object, so fields are added, never renamed or dropped.
- **"Prep" in the Calendar.** An investor meeting now carries `prospect_id` (`investorMeetings.ts`), so the item drawer shows a Prep section (`CalendarPrep.jsx`, words in `calendarPrepModel.js`). The brief is read only when the founder presses Prep, not when the drawer opens. A cancelled meeting, or one with no prospect, shows no Prep.
- **Client:** `prospectBriefApi.get(projectId, prospectId, eventId?)` in its own module (`frontend/src/lib/api/prospectBrief.js`, D527).
