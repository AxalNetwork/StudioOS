### 1. Where it lives

- **The parts** are in `segments/SegmentWork.jsx`; their rules are pure, in `segments/segmentWorkModel.js`.
- **The tabs** of `/admin/persona-segments` are Overview, `/unclassified`, `/activity` and, for HQ's Super Admin, `/suggestions`. The two new routes sit in `App.jsx` before `:key`, and `tabOf` reads the path from `TABS`, so neither word is ever taken for a segment key.
- **The old Unclassified** (the persona list capped at 500) is gone, and with it Guard L's note on this tab: the list is now D1002's `/members?segment=none`, paged with a total.

### 2. Unclassified (SEG5)

- **Why they have no segment:** bars for not started, started but not finished, and not offered, from D1002's reasons. A reason the store could not read is "Not recorded", never 0. Pressing a bar filters the list to it.
- **The list:** search (from two characters), profile, chat state and active in 30 days, sorted by joined, paged. Each row shows the name, email, profile, joined, last active, the chat state and when the member was last asked.
- **Ask to finish their profile:** opens a dialog first. It says how many will be asked and skips anyone asked within 30 days (D1002's rule), so the button reads "Send to N". The outcome is said in words.
- **Place in a segment…:** SEG4's shared Move dialog.
- **A failed read** says so with Try again. It is never drawn as "Every member has a segment".

### 3. Edit segment (SEG6), HQ's Super Admin only

- **A lightbox** on the segment page's header ("Edit segment"): name, status, who they are (280 characters) and the needs, one per line.
- **Save changes** opens a confirm step listing each change in words before anything is written.
- **The PATCH** carries only what changed and the version that was read, so a change made meanwhile is refused, not overwritten. A kept need keeps its id and what answers it; a new need starts with nothing answering it.
- **An Admin** sees "Suggest a change" instead, on the segment page and on the page header.

### 4. Suggest a change and Suggestions (SEG7)

- **Suggest a change:** the segment, the kind (who they are, a need, an offer, a new segment, other) and the change in words (500 characters). A new segment sends no segment key. The dialog says HQ answers on the Activity tab.
- **Suggestions** (`/suggestions`) is HQ's Super Admin's: each row says what, for which segment, from whom and from which studio ("HQ-held" for HQ's own), with Accept and Decline.
  - **Accept** on an edit opens Edit segment filled in with the suggestion, after the segment's current version is read. On a new segment it says to use New segment (D1128).
  - **Decline** needs a line (at most 200 characters), which the admin reads as "Declined by HQ: ‘…’".
- **An Admin** opening `/suggestions` is refused in words. Their own suggestions and HQ's answers appear under the Activity tab.

### 5. The page-wide Activity (SEG8)

D1002's activity read without a segment: each move reads "X moved M from A to B", sign-ups and edits as D1005 draws them. `ActivityTab` is D1005's, exported and shared.

### 6. Departures from the canvas

- **Unclassified's profile filter is a select,** not chips with counts: D1002's read gives no per-profile counts for members without a segment.
- **An Admin's suggestions** are a list under Activity, not interleaved with its rows, because they come from D1003's read, not D1002's.
- **Accept on a new segment** points to New segment rather than creating one in place.
