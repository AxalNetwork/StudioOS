## D5814 — Meeting bot: the owner's diagnostic covers Teams, with a notice stage (#5814)

- **Status:** part 4 of 5 of #3709 (D1451), built by S10 on #5814, 2026-11-28, after #5812 (D5812) merged the packaged Teams driver. Built and tested on fixtures only. No Teams meeting, deployed container or production resource was used; the first live Teams diagnostic is the owner's, on #1008, as part of #5816.
- **Migration:** none. **Switch:** `MEETING_BOT_TEAMS` stays `"off"` everywhere. The diagnostic is Super Admin only and runs whatever the switch says; its config row reports the switch.
- **Builds on:** #3708 (the staged diagnostic), D4857 (cumulative snapshots; ran apart from delivered), D5812 (the Playwright Teams driver; capture after admission and the notice), D1451 (the Teams link hosts), D5815 (the deployment matrix: preview never binds production's bot host).

### 1. Why

`startDiagnosis` refused every Teams link with `400 diagnose_platform`, the bot host and the runtime refused a Teams `/diagnose`, and the container's Teams egress list named two hosts, one of them not a link host. An owner running the Teams pilot could not tell which Cloudflare or Teams boundary failed.

### 2. What was decided

1. **Teams runs the Meet stages with the Teams adapter.** `runtimeDiagnose` builds `TeamsAdapter` on `playwrightTeamsPage` for a Teams link, the same driver a run uses (D5812). `/diagnose` accepts `teams` on the Worker, the bot host (`worker/container.ts`) and the runtime (`src/runtime.ts`).
2. **A `notice` stage, Teams only, between admission and audio.** Teams' page capture is gated on the recording notice (D5812). So the check posts the same published notice a run posts (`meetingBotNotice()`, no venture name), up to `NOTICE_TRIES` times, and subscribes to audio only after it is posted. A notice that fails is `notice: fail notice_failed` and the audio stage is skipped: nothing is heard. This is the one place a diagnostic posts a notice. Meet and Zoom are unchanged: their container never reports `notice`, and the Worker writes `notice: skipped not_used_by_platform` itself.
3. **Admission is always read on Teams.** A guest admitted straight into the call still goes through `admitted()`, because that read is what starts the page's capture. Without it a direct admission read as `no_frames` on the packaged page.
4. **Egress is the three link hosts:** `teams.microsoft.com`, `teams.cloud.microsoft`, `teams.live.com`.
5. **Work or personal link.** The Worker's config row says `teams_switch`, `link_kind` (`work`, `personal`) and `link_host`; the container's join row says `link_kind`. The link is never stored.
6. **Codes from the adapter's screen, never Teams' words** (`teamsJoinStop`): `teams_captcha`, `teams_anonymous_blocked`, `teams_bot_blocked`, `teams_not_found`, `meeting_ended`, `not_admitted` (`step: teams_denied`), `teams_join_timed_out` (stuck on the launcher or pre-join), `teams_page_unread` (no hook matches), `teams_link_unread`, `teams_navigation_failed`. Each has a fix in `DIAGNOSE_FIXES`.
7. **Elapsed time.** The HQ read gives each container row `elapsed_s`: whole seconds from the start to when the container ran it, never below zero. Public evidence is then stage, code, counts, elapsed time and the build revision (`site.facts.version`) only.

### 3. What stays as it was

- Access: start needs the Super Admin write bar (TOTP and a recent step-up); a read or the screenshot needs the Super Admin who started it. The screenshot stays in `FILES` under `meeting-bot-diagnostics/`, beside its record. A member, another admin, or another Super Admin reads nothing.
- Preview: the preview site binds no `MEETING_BOT_CONTAINER` (D5815's `site_binds_its_own_bot_host` row), so a preview diagnostic stops at `site: binding_missing`. Its command and callbacks are signed with keys derived from the preview secret, which a production image and a production Worker both refuse.

### 4. Outcomes and where they go

| Outcome on the console | Reads as | Pilot rubric (#5816) | Production alerting (#5674) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A passing canary | no stop; `notice_posted`, `chunk_uploaded` | the pre-flight before each live path | none |
| Denied | `admission` `not_admitted`, `step: teams_denied` | "denied/withdrawn consent" | none: an owner choice |
| Lobby timeout | `admission` `admission_timed_out` | "CAPTCHA/lobby timeout" | none |
| Tenant refusal | `join` `teams_captcha`, `teams_anonymous_blocked`, `teams_bot_blocked` | "guests disabled, bot blocked" | none: tenant policy, part 3's member reason |
| Notice failed | `notice` `notice_failed`, audio skipped | "posts the notice before capture" | a run's `notice_failed` |
| No audio | `audio` `no_frames` | "unavailable audio" | a run's capture failure |
| Startup failure | `site`, `container` or `browser` | before any Teams host | Container readiness |
