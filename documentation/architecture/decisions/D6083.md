## D6083 — Use of funds: Add a hire and the section sheet inside the tool (#6083)

- **Status:** Built, 2026-11-14 (Worker route, frontend). No migration.
- **Canvas:** `design/incoming/Raise Use Of Funds Page.dc.html`: UF4 (the section sheet), UF6 (Add a hire), UF21c (the section as a bottom sheet on a phone).
- **Builds on:** D1176 (the plan's stores and routes), D1177 (the tool's tabs), D5375 (a hire's own revision), D1174 (Mercury actuals are the founder's alone).
- **Must land before:** #5081, which removes the generic section record page.

### What was wrong

The tool could not add a hire or show a section by itself. The Hires table said "A hire is added on its section's page". Every section name linked the generic record page (`recordHref('raise', 'use-of-funds', key)`), where the record drawer's Add hire called `addSectionHire` in `services/raiseWriters.ts`.

### Add a hire goes through the store, not `addSectionHire`

`POST /api/cycles/raise/use-of-funds/hires` calls a new `createHire` in `services/useOfFunds/store.ts`, next to `saveHire`. It does not call `addSectionHire`, for three reasons:

1. **The same field rules as an edit.** The body is `{ role, category_key, start_month, annual_cents, currency }`. Each field is read by the readers `saveHire` uses: `key`, `text`, `month`, `number` and `currency`. Anything else is `400 invalid_plan`, including `expected_revision`, a `source` or a `project_id`. That is the store's rule that a human-only route takes no provenance from the client. `addSectionHire` has its own looser rules. It defaults a blank month, ignores unknown fields, and refuses a zero cost that `saveHire` accepts.
2. **The same audit as an edit.** The hire and its `activity_logs` row (`raise.use-of-funds`, `uof_hire_created`, keyed on the hire's uid, "Studio admin:" for an admin) are one batch. The plan's History already reads that entity type. `addSectionHire` logs `hire_added` on the cycle-write log, keyed on the section.
3. **The revision rule from D5375.** A new hire starts at `revision` 0, and the route answers 201 with that revision, so the next edit names it. A create carries no `expected_revision`, because nobody can have read the hire yet. Sending one is refused rather than ignored.

`addSectionHire` and the record drawer's Add hire are not changed. They go when #5081 removes the record page.

The access rule is the mount's own (D1176): the venture's founder or an admin, never a support session, and the project in the active company. An empty cost stays `null`, never 0. The plan, its section's hire cost and payroll read the new row with no further change, because they already read `use_of_funds_hires`.

### Add a hire in the tool (UF6)

There is an **Add a hire** button beside **Add milestone** on Milestones and hires, and another in the section sheet's Hires block. The dialog has role, section, starts, cost a year and currency, as the canvas draws it. It is a centred `Lightbox`, a bottom sheet on a phone, like Add milestone.

From the sheet, the section is already chosen. From the tab, the section filter is used when one is set. The cost is read in the chosen currency's own minor units (`parseMinor`), never through USD's. The currency list is the plan's currency, then USD, EUR and GBP. On a refusal the dialog stays open with the reason. When the hire saves, the plan is read again, so the hire shows in Hires, in its section's cost and in payroll.

### The section sheet (UF4, UF21c) and its address

A section name opens the section sheet over the tab that holds it. The shared `Sheet` (D1317) is a right-side sheet, and a bottom sheet on a phone (`phone="sheet"`, UF21c). The names that open it are:
- Home's section table;
- Allocation's table, and its phone list's `›`;
- the Section column of Milestones and hires.

The sheet has its own address: `?section=<key>` on the tab that opened it, next to the venture. So it survives a reload, Back closes it, and a link can open it. A tab link drops it, because `tabSearch` keeps only the venture. An unknown key opens nothing. The section's record id under the module stays its key, so `/raise/use-of-funds/product` still draws the record page until #5081.

The sheet shows the canvas's five blocks:
- **The section:** its share of the raise, its source, its amount, and a month over the target runway.
- **Milestones in this section:** by month, each with its cost or Not costed.
- **Hires:** each hire's start, cost a year and cost a month, then "Hires cost X a year of this section's Y". When the hires cost more than the section holds, it shows the same amber warning Allocation shows, and Allocation's own warning stays.
- **Plan vs actual:** the planned share against Mercury's measured share for the last 3 months.
- **Feeds:** the pitch deck's Ask slide, and whether it is in sync.

**D1174 is kept.** The actual share is the founder's alone. The sheet asks for the module read only when the viewer may see Mercury figures (`viewer.mercury`). Anyone else sees "Founder only" and "Mercury figures are visible to the founder only", and no bank figure. The Worker's module read already withholds them (D1174), so this is a second check, not the only one.

### Not done here

- The canvas's Feeds block also draws Fundraising readiness' Use of funds check. The plan read carries no readiness figure, so the sheet does not show one.
- The phone sheet overlaps #5075 (Use of funds hires on a phone). This change draws the section sheet as a bottom sheet and Add a hire as a bottom-sheet dialog. #5075's hire list on a phone is not built here.
