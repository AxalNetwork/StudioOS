## D7706 — Versioned market sizing on the existing page (#7706)

The existing Lab and Validate page delegates preview and Save to the D7704
Worker client. It performs no TAM/SAM/SOM sizing arithmetic, reconstructs no
missing percentages from saved figures, and makes no legacy two-request save.
Inputs (including zero and blank), method and source travel together. Cards
change only after the version save succeeds; milestones and review marks follow.
Failed saves retain the drawer, edits and saved cards. Field refusals mark their
named inputs. A preview has its own state and only its latest response is shown.

Zero is defined and drawn as $0; share text retains 0%. A typed zero CAGR reads
0%, and 24% appears only for blank CAGR with its default label. Method cards show
only a recomputable saved version whose three figures equal the project, with
its number, date, Worker formula and SOM limit. History adds the version number
beside a row that supplies it. Old/unmatched figures show no invented formula.

This is a narrow change within the existing Sheet and its footer/test ids.
V18's Save, Try again, failed-save sentence and Total / Serviceable / Obtainable
preview rows are retained. Departures from V18 follow the 19 November audit and
D7704: capacity participates, horizon/sales-cycle/retention are inputs, and SOM
is the lower of the win-rate and capacity limits. Runway is outside the figures;
category, geography, target year, segment filter and methodology are recorded
context. An override reason is exposed beside TAM because the Worker requires
one for an explicit override, including zero. Eadwyn fills/marks/reload remain.

No API hub, client method, migration or production data is changed. Test, build,
review and manual desktop/390px light/dark evidence are recorded separately on
the PR; synthetic fixtures are not evidence of deployment.
