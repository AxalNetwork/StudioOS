### When it runs

Every day at 05:45 UTC, from the every-minute trigger that already runs the
daily steps (`* * * * *` in `wrangler.toml`). No cron expression was added:
like Reporting's daily step at 05:40, the block gates on the tick's scheduled
minute (D239). It runs after Reporting's step on purpose. It runs on every
tier, because a branch's holders and companies are its own rows.

### What raises a notice

Both rules are the Portfolio Overview's own Needs-attention lines
(`evidenceFor` in `frontend/src/pages/portfolio/portfolioModel.js`), read
from the stored rows. The notice uses no figure the platform does not
record.

**Missed report.** All of these must hold:

- the company is in the holder's reporting population, which means a
  `portfolio_positions` row for it exists, as in `kpi-compliance`;
- no submitted update counts for the last completed quarter (`periodCounts`,
  D1057). A month inside the quarter counts, and a draft does not;
- the due day has passed (`reportDue`, D6204). The due day itself is not
  late.

The due day is worked out per holder, as `GET /api/positions/kpi-compliance`
works it out for that holder. The first of these that exists sets it:

1. the holder's own active `after_end` quarterly duty on the company. With
   several duties, the earliest due day is used;
2. the own rule set of a fund that holds the company, when the holder runs
   that fund as GP of record and holds the institutional tier. With an own set
   among them, the earliest of the company's sets is used;
3. the firm default of 30 days after the period ends.

The cadence is quarterly, the cadence the Overview reads. This is the
Overview's "Report … overdue by N days" line.

**Short runway.** The newest submitted founder update for the company that
the holder may read carries a runway figure under six months. "May read"
is the investor grant of D889: the holder is a recorded investor recipient,
or the update is shared with all investors.

- The figure is `runway_months`, else `runway`, taken through `updateFigures`
  as `/latest` serves it.
- Only the newest readable update is read. If it says nothing about runway,
  an older figure is not used, which is what the Overview does.
- Six months is not under six.

This is the Overview's "Runway" line.

### Who is told

The holders of D6218: the seat owners with a confirmed commitment, plus the
active members of their seats. The check takes every seat owner with a
confirmed commitment as a candidate and lets `investorHeldProjectIds` decide
what each holds, so it reads no `deals` row itself (D5454's inventory is
unchanged). Each is told only about the companies that
`investorHeldProjectIds` (D1057) gives them. The check finds them holder by
holder rather than company by company. It is the same rule, but each
holder's holdings are read once per run.

A seat member is told about a missed report under their own duty term, as
`kpi-compliance` reads it for them. They are told about a short runway only
when the update is shared with them.

### Once

`portfolio_attention_checks` holds one row for each combination of company,
holder, reason and evidence:

- for a missed report, the evidence is the period, such as `2026-Q3`;
- for a short runway, the evidence is the update's uid.

A notice goes out only when its row is new. So a second run the same day
raises nothing. Neither does a later day that finds the same evidence. A new
period that is missed is new evidence, and so is a new update that is still
under six months. Each notifies once.

The row is written whether or not the member's settings let the notice
through, as in D6218.

Nothing is cleared. A notice is an inbox row. When a report arrives, or the
runway goes back to six months or more, the check simply raises nothing more.

### The notice

- **Type:** `portfolio_needs_attention`, the type of D6218, in category
  `scoring`, on the in-app and email channels. The member's switch for it in
  Settings › Notifications ("A company I hold needs attention") turns it off.
  No settings row was added.
- **Title:** "<Company> needs attention".
- **Body, missed report:** "Report for 2026-Q3 overdue by 28 days: due
  2026-10-30, last reported 2026-07-10. Source: Reporting." When the company
  has never reported, the body says "never reported".
- **Body, short runway:** "Runway of 4 months in the founder update for
  2026-Q4, submitted 2026-10-05: under six months. Source: Founder updates."
- **Link:** `/portfolio/companies/:uid`.
- **Payload:** the reason, plus its evidence:
  - for a missed report: the period, the due day, the days overdue, the
    basis and the last report;
  - for a short runway: the update's uid, the figure, the period and the
    submission date.

### A source it cannot read

A source that fails to read raises nothing for the part it covers. It is
never read as "nothing missed".

- **Submitted updates:** no report is measured, and no runway is read.
- **A holder's duty or fund terms:** no due day is claimed for that holder,
  so no missed report is raised for them. Their runway is still read.
- **A holder's updates:** no runway is raised for them.

Each failure is named in the result's `unreadable` list, with the holder's
id. The cron then fails the task (`portfolio_attention_unreadable`), so the
run's `cron_run_history` row reads `partial` (D4703, D8533). The task does not
read as quietly done.

If the holders or the companies cannot be read at all, the run throws. The
cron fails the task with the error's class.

### Where it differs from the page, and why

- **No active company.** A request narrows a GP's managed funds, and an
  investor's holdings, to the active company. A scheduled check has no
  request, so it reads the whole book of each holder, as D6218 does. It
  reads the funds the holder runs as GP of record under any company.
- **No required-KPIs read.** `kpi-compliance` answers 503 when the firm's
  required-KPI rules cannot be read. The check does not read them, because a
  missed report does not depend on them.

**Imports (CI fix).** The check reads an update's figures through `services/portfolioUpdateFigures.ts`, not `routes/portfolio_updates.ts`: the route's import graph reaches member bank data (`mercuryPersonal`, `mercurySpend`), and D752's `no cron path reaches member bank data` refuses any cron entry that does. `updateFigures` moved there unchanged and the route re-exports it, so its other callers keep their import. The new `portfolio_attention_checks` table is in the VentureSpace data inventory (D8530) as `refuses`, like `portfolio_attention_alerts`: its `project_id` references projects with no action, so a hard delete fails while a row exists (Q4).
