### 1. The decision

Billing (`/account/billing`) is laid out as the canvas draws it:

1. **Token balance** and **Included with your plan**, side by side.
2. **Buy tokens**: one card per pack on sale, with the footnote.
3. **The plan and Payment details** in one column, **Auto-reload** beside them.
4. **Orders**, as before.

On a phone each row stacks into one column, and the pack cards are two across.

### 2. As built

Frontend only; no Worker route or migration. Every card reads a route that already exists, and a read that fails draws Unreadable with a sentence saying what it does not claim, never a guessed figure.

| Card | Reads | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Token balance | `GET /api/ai-budget/me` | `balance_microusd` as "$X AI tokens"; Buy tokens scrolls to `#buy-tokens` |
| Included with your plan | `GET /api/ai-budget/me` | what is left of `plan.monthly_microusd` after `month_spend_microusd`, of what, and the reset (the 1st of next month, UTC), with a bar. No plan, or a plan that includes nothing: "No plan: top up as you go." |
| Buy tokens | `GET /api/ai-budget/top-up` | each pack's amount; "+N% bonus, ends 12 Nov" only when the Worker sends a live `bonus_microusd`; "First purchase only"; Buy links `/checkout?token_pack=<uid>` (D755). The page never takes a payment itself |
| Payment details | `GET /api/billing/overview` | the default card (else the first): brand, last four, expiry. "Manage in Stripe ↗" opens the same customer portal as Manage billing, and is disabled with no billing customer. The page never collects card data |
| Auto-reload (D794) | adds `GET /api/ai-budget/limit` | "Stops at your monthly spend limit of $X." with Open limits; with no limit, "No spend limit: reloads continue each time your balance runs low."; when on and the last reload was stopped by the limit, a Paused pill and "Paused: this month's spend limit is reached. Resumes 1 Nov." in place of On. A failed limit read leaves the line out |

### 5. Part 2 (#2270): S1's security review

S1's security reviews of #2266 (D792) and #2253 (D805) found two faults, which merged unfixed on 2026-10-20 and are fixed here.

1. **Payment details read one Stripe customer and managed another.**
   - **The fault:** `useBillingReads` called `billingOverview()` with no scope, so the Worker read the general customer (`users.stripe_customer_id`). An investor's "Manage in Stripe" opens `investorPortal`, on `investor_stripe_customer_id`. An investor saw one customer's card and managed the other's.
   - **The fix:** `useBillingReads({ role })` reads `billingOverview(overviewScopeFor(role))`. That is `'investor'` exactly when `billingSourceFor(role)` opens `investorPortal`; every other role reads with no scope and opens `tierPortal`, both on the general customer.
   - `BillingConsole` is keyed by the role, so a role that changes re-reads on the new customer.
   - `PaymentDetailsCard` takes the same `billingApi` as the reads.
2. **A pack-order count that failed hid the spend limit.**
   - **The fault:** `GET /api/ai-budget/limit` read D805's `pack_orders` in the same `try` as the limit. If `ai_topup_purchases` could not be read, the whole read answered 503, and the Limits page and the Auto-reload line lost the limit too.
   - **The fix:** the count is read on its own. When it fails, the route answers 200 with the limit and allowance, `pack_orders: null` and `pack_orders_reason: 'pack_orders_unreadable'`, and logs the failure's message. A limit that cannot be read is still 503.

**Verified:**
- `frontend/test/billing_customer_scope_d792.test.mjs`: each role's overview scope and portal are on one customer.
- `cloudflare-worker/test/ai_spend_limit_pack_orders_d792.test.ts`: the count reading, failing (200, null with its reason, logged once) and the limit failing (503).
- Stripe is stubbed; no fixture holds account details.
- Nine mutations, each killed: the scope dropped or set to `'founder'`, the portal taken off the injected API, the role not passed or not keyed, the count failure answered 503, a non-null `pack_orders`, the log dropped, and a limit failure not refused.
