## D653 — Founder round calls behind a disabled feature flag

Issue #1465; migration 436. This is distinct from the GP/LP capital_calls domain.

### Contract
ROUND_CALLS_ENABLED defaults off, including production. An enabled flow is private:
investor role, active account, approved KYC and recipient entitlement are required.
Matched investors remain anonymous to the founder until a response. Opening a call
is not a response. Chosen investors are already known pipeline contacts.

Amounts use integer cents in new stores. Existing Raise amounts are dollars; the
pipeline adapter converts explicitly. Unknown evidence is not replaced by zero.
Readiness is a seven-item derivation of actual round/project/material records; it
does not depend on #1476's future readiness schema. Missing readiness can be
acknowledged; counsel approval cannot be bypassed.

Counsel approval is server-held and bound to a SHA-256 fingerprint of the current
draft. Editing the draft clears approval. Materials are versioned deck references
and data-room references; existing grant and NDA checks remain authoritative.
