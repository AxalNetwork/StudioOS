### Where the cohort lives

The cohort lives in the `user` scope of `feature_flags`, with no new table and no migration.

- **Add:** writes one enabled row per person and key. A `user` row wins over the role's and everyone's (`flagsOf`), so HQ can switch a feature off for everyone and on for the cohort.
- **The cohort:** the people with such a row on for a beta key.
- **The keys:**
  - K2's deck features (`deck:rehearsal`, `deck:pptx_import`, `deck:logo_wall`);
  - `grid_generate`;
  - `element:<id>` for each grid element; `element:*` in a write means every element.
  
  Any other key, a model's included, is refused 400 `invalid_feature`.

### The routes (`routes/admin_deck_beta.ts`, `/api/admin/deck-beta`)

The module mounts itself (#4105). It is HQ admins only (`requireHqAdmin`; a branch is refused 403).

- **`GET /`** lists the cohort, paged (25), filtered by `q` (name or company), `feature` and `added_by`. Each row has:
  - `user: { uid, name }`;
  - `company`, by name;
  - `features`;
  - `added_by: { uid, name }`;
  - `added_at`.
  
  It also returns the keys with their labels.
- **`GET /people?q=`** finds members by name for the sheet: name, company and role. It needs at least 2 characters and returns at most 10 rows.
- **`POST /`** takes `{ user_uids[], features[], reason }`.
  - The reason must be at least 10 characters (400 `reason_required`).
  - Each person must be an active member (404 `person_not_found`, and then nothing is written).
  - It writes one admin audit row per person (`deck_beta_add`) with the keys and the reason.
- **`DELETE /:userUid`** takes `{ reason }`.
  - It deletes the person's rows for the beta keys.
  - It clears the same keys from their beta column (`user_settings.feature_flags`, which answers for `grid_generate` and `element:*` when no row does), and keeps their other settings. They fall back to the role's and everyone's setting.
  - It writes one audit row (`deck_beta_remove`).
  - **What they already made stays:** their decks, the logo walls on slides, their rehearsals. No content table is touched.

Neither read carries an email.

### The page

HQ › Decks › Beta cohort (HD3, HD3b) is live: `BUILD.K3.live = true`, with its client in `lib/api/adminDeckBeta.js`.

- **The table:** name and company, features by label (the elements counted), and who added each person and when.
- **Add people:** search by name (from 2 characters), tick people, tick features ("All grid elements" stands for `element:*`), and give the reason. Add stays disabled until all three are there.
- **Remove…:** asks for the reason and says what stays.
