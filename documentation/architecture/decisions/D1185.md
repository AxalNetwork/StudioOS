### 1. Accounts (BNK1)

- **Where each payment goes** comes first. It is drawn from the registry read's `coverage`:
  - "N of 5 can print", and the warning when any purpose is refused, naming Fund I when the Fund has no account;
  - each purpose: paid here, with the entity, the account's label and `••••NNNN`, or Refused with "A notice reads: …" in the resolver's own sentence;
  - **Why a notice refuses**, folded: the four reasons, each with the Worker's `refusals` sentence. A coverage refusal of `ambiguous_account` adds a fifth.
- **One card per entity**, in the order Holdings, Management, GP, Fund I. Each card shows:
  - its short name and legal name, the connection pill, Connect or Replace (the Mercury sheet, D880), and "Add an account for <entity>";
  - **each active account's row:** the label and kind, the ending, the payments, the Balance (D1184's `mercury.current`, with when it was read), whether Mercury reads it, the state, and the row's ⋯ menu (Open, Relabel, Change payments, Reveal, Retire);
  - **the retired accounts folded:** "Show N retired accounts", each with its date and reason;
  - **the accounts Mercury reads that the registry does not hold:** "Mercury also reads ••••9012 …", its balance, "Not in the registry" and "Add it to the registry";
  - the per-entity coverage lines, or "<entity> takes no payment through the registry."
- **The join is D1184's, never the cached `registered` flag.**
  - A registry account's Mercury cell reads Matched, Not seen in Mercury, More than one in Mercury, Not connected, or Not read.
  - "Not in the registry" is Mercury's stored accounts (`GET /api/admin/mercury/accounts`) whose entity and ending no active registry account holds.
- **Never a zero for an unknown.**
  - A balance with no figure is "Not recorded" with its reason (Mercury sent none; no account with this ending; more than one).
  - A failed read is "Could not be read". A measured zero stays $0.00. A retired account has none ("—").
- **Each read is its own block.** A failed Mercury accounts read draws Unreadable with Try again; the registry, the balances and the cards stay.
- **The header** is the canvas's: "The registry of each entity's bank accounts. Notices and invoices print their payment instructions from here.", with the HQ only pill and Add an account.
- **The loading words** are "Reading the bank accounts…", not the console's.

### 2. One account (BNK2, BNK2s)

- **The address** is `/admin/platform/bank-accounts/account/:uid`, behind the same `guard(['admin'], hqOnly(…))`. `tabOf` reads it as Accounts, so the lightbox is drawn as a centred lightbox over Accounts.
- **The row's label and Open both go there.** Close and Esc return to Accounts. An address the registry does not hold says "No such account".
- **It draws, from the registry read Accounts already holds:**
  - the payment instructions (Not recorded for an empty field), "Added <day> · by <name>" from D1182's `recorded_by_name`, never an id;
  - the payments it takes;
  - **How a notice prints it**, from D1184's `notice_preview`: each purpose's masked instructions with the Worker's warning, or "A notice reads: …";
  - In Mercury: Current, Available and when they were read.
- **Its own three reads, each a block that fails alone with Try again:**
  - the 90-day balance series (`GET /api/admin/mercury/balances?account=`, D1183), as a line broken where a day is not recorded;
  - the latest five Mercury lines (`GET /api/admin/mercury/transactions?account=`), each Matched, Proposed, Unmatched, Not a receipt or Not ours, and the door to Fund admin › Banking;
  - this account's rows of History (`GET /api/admin/bank-accounts/history`, filtered to its `account_uid`), with "See all in History".
- **A savings account (BNK2s)** has no notice preview and reads no series.
- **An account Mercury does not match** reads no lines, and says so.

### 3. The dialogs (BNK6)

**Every change opens an in-page dialog that says what it changes before it is sent.** The inline forms under Manage are gone. There is never a browser pop-up.

| Dialog | What it says, and what it sends |
|---|---|
| Add an account | The entity, the beneficiary read only, the bank, the kind, the **Label** (prefilled from the kind, editable, 1 to 60), the number twice, and the entity's own payments. `POST /api/admin/bank-accounts` |
| Add from Mercury | "Mercury reads ••••9012 for Holdings… It must end in 9012." Save waits until the typed number ends in those four. It sends `mercury_account_uid`, never an ending; the Worker checks the entity, ending and kind (D1184) |
| Relabel | "Only the label changes. Notices do not print it." Unchanged or empty cannot be sent. `PATCH {label}` |
| Change payments | Each change in words before it is saved. "Adding Management fee: management-fee invoices will print ••••3318." "Removing Platform licence fee: licence-fee invoices refuse to print until another Holdings checking account takes it." A purpose another account takes is refused here, as the Worker would refuse it. `PATCH {purposes}` |
| Retire | A required reason (1 to 200). When the account takes payments: "This account takes … After you retire it, those invoices refuse to print until another account takes them." `POST /retire` |
| Reveal | A required reason (1 to 200), "Your reason is recorded before the number is shown." `POST /reveal`, then the number in the row and the lightbox for 30 seconds with Hide now and "Reason recorded at HH:MM UTC". It is held in component state only |

- **The step-up (BNK6 F)** is `request()`'s own. When the Worker answers 403 `step_up_required`, the global authenticator dialog asks, and the write is sent again once.
- **A cancelled code** reads "The authenticator code was not entered, so nothing changed." inside the dialog.
- **Any other refusal** stays in its dialog, in the Worker's sentence.

### 4. The entity card states (BNK7)

The line under a card's title, from `entityRows`:
- **connected:** "Last synced <time>." or "Not synced yet.";
- **needs attention:** the error in words ("Mercury refused the token. Only the Super Admin can replace it.");
- **not connected:** "Connect Mercury to see this entity's balances. The registry works without it."

### 5. The rail

- **Bank accounts docks the rail** with BNK1's sentence: "Eadwyn can see which entities have an account and which payments are refused. It never sees an account number, a balance or a transaction."
- **The AIRail canvas draws no Bank accounts board.** So the key is `off('/admin/platform/bank-accounts', 'Super Admin · Platform · Bank accounts')`, beside Payments in `railPages.js`, and the parity test still counts the canvas alone.
- **`BankAccountsRail`** (`BankAccountsConsole.jsx`) is the `AdminBoardFrame` around the whole page. `ConsolePage` is unchanged: `consoleBoard` still names only canvas boards, so it draws Bank accounts alone and one frame wraps it.

### 6. Not built here, and why

- **The canvas's "Explain the refused payments" rail button and its Can see / Never sees cards.** No model handler exists for this page; the sentence above says the same.
- **The reveal's countdown ring.** The number shows for 30 seconds with Hide now; a live second count is part 5's states.
- **Sync now, Disconnect and Replace token in the card's ⋯ menu.** They stay on the Mercury sheet, which Connect and Replace open. **The sync-refused toast** is the sheet's.
- **"Mercury names this organisation …"** needs the org name on the card. The sheet shows it (D880).
- **Cash and History** are part 5 (#3115). **The phone (BNK12)** is part 6 (#3116).
- **Unmatched receipts** stay as D717 drew them, until part 5.
