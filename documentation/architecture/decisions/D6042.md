### 1. The problem

Build has six modules. Product roadmap and Operating systems had Eadwyn drafts (D424, D510); Founding team and Company creation had none, and the rail inventory listed both pages as quiet ("Its tool is the cap table, which runs no model"; "Its legal and e-signature doors run no model"). #5077 gave every module its own asks, but an ask only fills Ask's composer: neither module had a draft that reads its own record.

### 2. The two surfaces

Both follow `build/roadmap`: a POST behind a button, `founderProject` resolving the one venture the caller owns (a scope key that names another venture, or junk, or two ventures and no key, answers `409 nothing_to_draft` before any model runs), material read only from the venture's own stores, and an instruction that forbids anything outside them.

| Surface | Module | Reads | The draft |
|---|---|---|---|
| `build/founding` | Founding team | `cap_table_holders` with a founder role, `cap_table_vesting` joined on holder and user, the latest cofounder agreement's status — the module's own read (`cycle_build.ts` `founding()`) | Checks the split (the total is computed in the material and read back, never redistributed or rounded off) and each founder's vesting, then lists every item marked NOT RECORDED by founder. |
| `build/company` | Company creation | The latest paid formation order (`incorporations`), `formation_steps`, the linked `entities` row and its open `entity_obligations` — the stores the module's table combines (`company()`) | Explains the formation state (which steps are recorded done and which are not), lists the open filings with their timing, and names the one marked NEXT DUE. |

- **The arithmetic is the material's.** The founders' total, each filing's "overdue by N days" or "due in N days" against today, and the next one due (the open filing with the earliest recorded date, which puts overdue first and then due soon, as `company()`'s next action ranks them) are computed in `buildRecordDrafts.ts`. The model reads figures; it never adds or counts days.
- **A gap is a fact.** Every missing value is spelled NOT RECORDED in the material: ownership, vesting, its cliff and length, the agreement, an order's dates, a step's due date, an entity. A missing percentage is never read as zero, so a split with one is "cannot be totalled", not a lower total. An undated open filing is never named next.
- **Nothing outside the record.** The founding instruction forbids saying what the split or vesting should be and citing typical cliffs or norms; the company instruction forbids any filing requirement, deadline, fee or jurisdiction rule the records do not carry, and legal advice. Investors, option holders and the option pool are not read, and the material says so.
- **Nothing to draft.** No founder on the cap table, or no order, step or entity, returns `[]`. A pending-payment order is not an order (as in `company()`).
- **Accept keeps the wording only.** As for `build/roadmap` and `build/kpi`, Accept stamps the stored `research_zone_drafts` row (scoped to the venture); it writes no cap-table, vesting or filing record. Each band's foot says so.
