## D6908 — Ownership & rights: the investor's own pro rata offer and the link to the live round (#6908)

- **Status:** Worker part built 2026-11-16 (`Part of #6908`). The Pro rata row on Ownership & rights waits on #6868, which creates `frontend/src/pages/portfolio/OwnershipRights.jsx`.
- **Builds on:**
  - D366: the round manager's `raise_rounds` and `raise_pro_rata`, and its null-not-zero rule;
  - D6228: owner answer A, "investors read their own pro rata offer";
  - D6868, which files this issue.

### The problem

PRM18 draws the Pro rata row's "What you can do" as "Series A is live · offer to you: up to $310,000 · respond by 30 Nov 2026", with **Open the round →**.

`raise_pro_rata` records a holder's prior stake and their answer (`taking_amount`, `state`). The entitlement is computed per request from the stake and the round size, and the founder's round manager shows it. Nothing records the offer actually made to a holder, nor the day to answer by. The issue forbids computing an offer from the ownership %, so a store was needed.

### What is built

**Store.** Migration `202611161255_6908_pro_rata_offer.sql` adds to `raise_pro_rata`:
- `offer_cents`: positive, in the row's `currency`, or NULL for "Offer not recorded";
- `respond_by`: a day, or NULL.

**The founder records it.** `routes/contacts.ts`, the round manager's existing owner-only routes:
- `POST /api/contacts/raise-pro-rata` and `PUT /api/contacts/raise-pro-rata/:id` take `offer_amount` (in the round's units) and `respond_by` (YYYY-MM-DD).
- An amount that is not positive, or a day that is not one, is 400.
- `''` or null clears either field. A PUT that does not name them keeps them.

**The investor reads it.** `GET /api/portfolio-pro-rata/:projectUid` (`routes/portfolio_pro_rata.ts`, new):
- **Who:** an investor (the licence, not an admin), for a company they hold through a confirmed commitment. This is the held scope of Positions and of D6205's mark requests. Not held and unknown answer alike: 403 `not_held`.
- **No live round:** the company has no `active` round on the round manager. The read is `{ live_round: null, offer: null }`, and the row shows no action.
- **A live round:** `live_round` gives its uid, name and close date, plus `href`:
  - `/deals/<id>` of the company's active deal on Axal. A deal the D5454 preparation guard hides from discovery (a withdrawn or archived listing) is never linked; the route is classified `guarded` in D5454's inventory;
  - or null, with `href_not_recorded`, when the round has no deal page on Axal. A page is never guessed.
- **The offer:** only the caller's own row, matched by their account email without case. It carries the amount in cents, currency, respond-by day, state, what they are taking and when they answered.
- **No offer recorded:** whether there is no row or no amount, `offer` is null and `offer_not_recorded` reads "Offer not recorded". The round is still linked.

The answer never carries another holder's row, name or figure. It never carries the caller's prior stake, nor a figure computed from it.

**Client.** `frontend/src/lib/api/portfolioProRata.js`, `portfolioProRataApi.get(projectUid)`.

### Choices made here

- **"Live" is the round manager's active round.** It is the round the offer rows belong to (`raise_pro_rata.round_id`), and there is at most one per company (`uq_raise_rounds_active`).
- **"Open the round" is the company's active deal on Axal.** An investor has no page for the founder's round manager. A round that is not run as a deal on Axal reads "This round has no deal page on Axal."
- **The holder is matched by email.** That is how the round manager identifies a holder (`uq_raise_pro_rata_holder` on `round_id, holder_email`), so an investor whose cap-table email differs from their account email reads "Offer not recorded" until the founder corrects the row.

### Not built here

- **The Pro rata row on Ownership & rights** (`OwnershipRights.jsx`). That file is created by #6868, which is in progress on S07. The row follows once it lands, in the next PR on #6908.
- **The round manager's own screen.** It gains no field for the offer in this part. The founder records it through the existing route. The input on the round manager's holder sheet follows with the row.
