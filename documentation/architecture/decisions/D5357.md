## D5357 — Deal-flow cells distinguish an unreadable flow from absence (#5357)

Status: accepted, 2026-11-10.

Days in stage and owner are facts supplied by the investor's deal-flow read. When that read fails, a missing value is unknown: it must not be described as “Not on your deals.” The shared Days and Owner cells therefore accept an explicit `flowUnknown` state. A missing value in that state says “Your deal flow could not be read.” A successful read keeps the existing distinctions: a venture outside the flow says “Not on your deals,” while a venture in the flow with no stage start or owner says that the value is not recorded or unassigned.

The comparison page can remain useful after its flow read fails because its deal-list, score and thesis-fit reads are independent. It passes the failed-flow state to its Days cells while retaining those independently read facts. The board, table and peek render only after a successful flow read, so their existing calls do not claim an unknown state and require no change.

This is presentation-only. It adds no route, write, permission, billing rule, store or migration.
