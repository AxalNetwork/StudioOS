## D6216 — Recipient-scoped founder asks and promises (#6216)

Reports reads asks for the selected latest item through a read that does not
record an open. An investor must have a current holding and a recorded
investor delivery. A reservation on a versioned delivery does not grant
access. The response returns answer status and the caller's own promise
state without naming another investor.

A promise opens the existing Value-add form with the company fixed, kind
editable, and the ask text as the starting summary. Hours and outcome stay
blank. Confirmation writes one support entry per recorder and ask. The
unique index makes a retry return the original row, and the existing
lifecycle leaves its ask link intact. Linked notes appear only to their
recorder in the support ledger, and are excluded from LP reporting drafts.

The founder-facing promise summary, naming other recipients, recording an
open from Report detail, and routing a capital promise into a round call
remain pending the decisions in D6215. The existing sent-update page keeps
its separate open and reply behavior.

Integration with main's D6203 Value-add lifecycle keeps the ask-linked row's
recorder-only scope together with `state=open` (promised and in-progress).
The ask-prefilled form retains its fixed promise state and explicit confirmation,
while the new optional due date is initialized, editable and submitted. Combined
regressions exercise private in-progress asks in the open view and the locked
promise form with its due date; four targeted mutations are caught.
