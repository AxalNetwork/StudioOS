### 1. What the page reads

`useEventsDesk` adds two reads when it draws one event: the summary and the history. Each is made alone, and both count towards "Updated".

The page still never reads `GET /api/events/:id/roster`, which carries names and emails. Every figure on it is a count.

The summary answers block by block. Each block is `{ available: true, … }` or `{ available: false, unreadable: true, reason }`. `summaryBlock(read, key)` turns one block into `loading`, `ready` or `unreadable`, and a block the answer lacks is unreadable, never zero. Each card draws only its own block, so one failed block fails one card (EV15a) and the rest still draw. A failed summary read fails every summary card but not the event, its gate, its tiles from the analytics row or its agenda.

### 2. What it draws

- **The header.**
  - The type pill comes from part 1's twelve types (`lib/eventTypes.js`); a type with no word draws no pill.
  - The format word comes from `location_kind`: In person, Online or Hybrid. It leads the place line.
  - The host line reads "Hosted by [name] · [role] · [venture]". A missing part is left out, a host with no name is "A member", and an event with no host is "Host not recorded". "Hosted by the studio team" waits for O-STU.
- **The second row of tiles.**
  - Paid tickets: the count, and the amount per currency. An amount no row stored is said ("+ 1 not recorded"), never summed as 0.
  - Free seats: "From audience rules or free invitations".
  - Invitations: the number sent, and how many were accepted.
- **Seats taken over time (chart A).**
  - It draws the running total of current seat holders by the day each registered, so its label says so: "By the date each current seat holder registered. Cancellations and promotions are not dated."
  - It has a dashed capacity line (none when there is no cap) and an amber Today marker. The marker sits at the series' right edge when today is after the last registration day, and is not drawn before the first.
  - Seat holders with no registration date are counted in a line under the chart, not drawn.
  - The chart is `RunningTotal`, a new part in `ui/Charts.jsx` beside D893's.
- **The funnel (chart B).**
  - Registered → Confirmed → Attended.
  - Registered is every current seat: registered, confirmed and attended. Confirmed is confirmed plus attended, because an attendee was confirmed first; the card says so.
  - Attended is "Not held yet" (or "Not held" for a cancelled event), drawn as a dashed bar, until the event has ended. It is never 0 before then. Once someone has attended, the count is drawn.
  - Waitlisted, Cancelled and Declined sit beside it as figures, not steps.
- **Who registered.** Seated registrations by profile, in the order Founder, Investor, Advisor, Operating Partner, Explorer, Admin, Guest (no account), Profile not recorded. Founder and Investor always show; the others only when non-zero. Then by how the seat was got: Signed up, Invitation, Free seat (D915's order). "Added by an admin" is Not recorded, with part 3's reason.
- **Invitations.**
  - By status: Pending, Accepted, Declined, Revoked.
  - By source: Sent by the host or an admin (`manual`), Official partners (`auto_partner`), Invested LPs (`auto_lp`), and "Another source" only when part 3 counted one.
  - The canvas's Investors, Founders and Host's contacts are dropped: nothing mints an invitation from them (map A, EV7).
- **Payments.** Price, Paid (count and amount) and Payment pending. Refunds are "Not recorded", with the canvas's line (O-REF): HQ refunds a ticket's payment in Revenue › Billing.
- **Reminders.**
  - The 24-hour and 1-hour reminders, each due from the event's start in the event's zone.
  - "Sent to n" comes from the summary. Otherwise the line is "Not sent yet · due …" before the due time, "Not sent · was due …" after it, and "Not sent: the event is cancelled" for a cancelled event.
- **History.**
  - Part 3's items, newest first, then Created last. D915's "Not read here yet" line is gone.
  - Actors are "the host" or "an admin", never a name. A send-back carries its reason, an edit its fields, a capacity override the new cap.
  - Part 2's `changed_after_approval_at` is the amber row: "The host changed the event after it was approved".

### 3. The dialogs

- **Cancel (EV10)** adds "Note to registrants · optional".
  - The note reaches registrants with an account, as part 2 sends it. The line now says so ("…are told in StudioOS, with your note.").
  - The paid box counts from the summary: "3 paid tickets ($120) are not refunded automatically. HQ refunds them in Revenue › Billing." With no paid ticket there is no box.
  - While the summary is still loading, or could not be read, a paid event keeps the box and says which ("still being counted" or "could not be counted here"). Cancelling still needs the box ticked.
  - The "cannot be undone" caveat stays dropped: part 2's 409 stops an approve reviving a cancelled event.
- **Unpublish (EV11a)** adds "Note to the host · optional", and the body ends "The host is told, with your note."
- Both notes are trimmed and capped at `NOTE_MAX` (1,000, the Worker's own cap). A blank note sends `{}`, not an empty note. `adminEvents.cancel(id, note)` and `adminEvents.unpublish(id, note)` take the note as an optional second argument.
