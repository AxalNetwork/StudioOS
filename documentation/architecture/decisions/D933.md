### 2. What is drawn

Every block is `ready`, `loading`, `unreadable` or `unrecorded`. Each of D931's blocks answers alone through `blockOf`: a block that says it could not be read keeps its own sentence, and a block that is absent is unreadable, never zero.

- **The period control:** 4, 12 and 52 weeks, as a radio group with arrow keys, in the URL as `?weeks=`. It sets every period figure. The default is 12, and any other value reads as 12. One line under it says what the period covers: Monday weeks in UTC, this week so far.
- **Tiles:**
  - **Waiting on you** keeps the glance's count. It gains "oldest [n days]", the older of the two queues' oldest waits (D931's `queue`), and the amber dot when either queue has anything Overdue, past 72 hours. Its note says how many jobs were submitted before waits were recorded, and how many events wait from creation. A queue that could not be read gives no age and says so.
  - **Event registrations** and **Job applications**, each with its delta on the same number of weeks before: "+50% on the 12 weeks before", "same as …", or "none in …" when the period before had none, so no percentage is made from a zero.
  - **Show-up rate:** attended over seats taken for the events that ended in the period. With no ended event, or no seat taken, it is a dash with its reason, never 0%.
  - **Wellbeing experts:** "[v] verified of [n]", where n is the listed experts (verified and unverified). Hidden and inactive are named under it, and sample experts are never counted (D931).
- **Community activity by week** (CM1 row 3): event registrations, job applications and expert bookings on the read's own weeks, in violet, sky and amber. The current week is a hollow point, and a week the block does not carry is a gap in the line. "View as table" is CM7, where the current week's row says "so far". A series that could not be read is left out and named. Above it is one sentence on the biggest change, the series that moved most against the weeks before, written from the numbers.
- **Events card:**
  - **The funnel:** the events that ended in the period, Registered (their seats taken) then Attended with its share. D918 counts every seat taken together, so **Confirmed** says it is not counted on its own (D931). Waitlisted sits beside the funnel, not as a step: the seats waiting on upcoming events.
  - **Events by type:** published or waiting, starting in the period, over the twelve stored types. Types with none are behind "Show all · [n] types with none".
  - Next up is D930's.
- **Who takes part:** registrations and applications stacked by profile (founder, investor, advisor, operating partner, explorer, guest, other), with its table and a sentence naming the largest share. One part that failed is left out and named. Registrations from accounts that are gone are counted apart.
- **Wellbeing card:**
  - experts by state as a 100% bar: verified, unverified, inactive and hidden;
  - bookings by week, the current week hatched;
  - the check-in band, "[a] to [b] founders checked in". Under seven founders, or whenever the read says withheld, it shows the canvas's sentence: "Too few check-ins to show. We show this only when seven or more founders check in."
  - No mood or stress figure is drawn, and no answer is read.
- **Needs a decision:** each row says "waiting [n days]", and Overdue with the amber dot past 72 hours.
  - An event waits from `submitted_at`, or from `created_at`, marked "since created", when it was queued before submission was stamped (D918's rule).
  - A job waits from `submitted_at` (migration 550). One submitted before that says "wait not recorded", is never Overdue, and lists after every dated row.
  - Exactly 72 hours is not Overdue.
- **Recent activity** (CM1 row 7): D932's ten newest rows, with All, Events, Jobs, Circles, Wellbeing and Admin actions as chips. "See all activity" opens D934's CM6. A source that failed is named, and its rows are absent.
- **The consoles strip:** the Wellbeing door shows "[n] to verify".
- **The rail** names the new coverage. Its one unavailable figure now is Confirmed registrations on their own.
