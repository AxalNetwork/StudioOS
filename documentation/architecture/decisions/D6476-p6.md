## D6476 part 6 — At most three keeper attempts per PR, then a definitive hand-back (#6476, #8077)

- **Status:** built by S19 on 2026-11-22 for #8077 (audit section 15, P1), after #8057 (D6476-p5, `bot:keeper-tried`).
- **Builds on:** D6474 (the branch keeper), D6476-p5 (the keeper's own hand-back label).

**The problem.** A keeper hand-back used to last only until the session pushed. A PR whose session kept pushing
without resolving the conflict could be tried, labelled, dispatched to the resolver and tried again with no end.

**The rule.** A pull request gets at most `KEEPER_MAX_ATTEMPTS = 3` keeper attempts, whatever their outcome: merged,
update-branch, refused or conflicts. The third attempt is the last. No later sweep, push or dispatch plans a fourth.

**How attempts are counted** (`keeperAttempts()` in `scripts/landing-bot/keeper-plan.mjs`). The count is the larger
of two figures:
- the App's own `bot:keeper-tried` label events plus the App's commits that carry the `Landing-Bot-Kind: keeper`
  trailer. Events and commits by anyone else are not counted;
- the `Keeper attempt k of 3: <outcome>` lines in the App's sticky comment.

GitHub writes no new `labeled` event when the label is already on the PR, so the label events alone undercount.
The sticky lines are the durable record. Taking the larger figure means neither record can lower the count. If the
events, the commits or the sticky comment cannot be read, the count is unknown and nothing is planned for that PR.

**The sticky comment.** Every outcome that is an attempt writes its line (`composeSticky()`). Earlier lines are
kept. A rerun of attempt `k` replaces its own line rather than adding a second one. The third attempt ends with:
"That was the last of 3 keeper attempts: the keeper hands this pull request back to its session for good and never
tries it again."

**The last attempt** (`conflictOutcome()`). A conflict on attempt 3 is labelled `conflict`, `bot:keeper-tried` and
`needs-session`, and the resolver is not dispatched. On attempts 1 and 2 the resolver is asked, as before.

**The ejection handler.** `decide()` in `ejection-rules.mjs` gains one branch: on a conflicting entry whose keeper
attempts are at the cap, it labels `ejected` for the owner. It neither asks the keeper nor waits for it. An unknown
count (`null`) changes nothing. `ejection.mjs` supplies the count from the label events it already reads, the
commits it already reads, and the sticky comment. That caller is one line outside the files #8077 lists, and it is
named in the PR.

**Unchanged:**
- `rules.mjs` `attempts()`: its two-keeper-commit limit is still checked;
- what the keeper does inside an attempt (#6474, #6475);
- the daily and per-sweep caps, the breakers, `bot:off` and every landing gate;
- the keeper and the handler staying behind their switches.

A plan file without an attempt number, written by an older plan stage, is treated as the last attempt, so it can
never open a fourth.

**Rollback.** Revert the PR. With the cap gone, a hand-back again lasts only until the next push. No data or
settings change.

**Tests.** `frontend/test/landing_bot_keeper_8077.test.mjs` covers:
- fixtures with 0, 2 (in each record type) and 3 prior attempts;
- a fourth refused after a push and on a dispatch;
- only the App's records counting, and an unread record planning nothing;
- the sticky lines: kept, one per attempt, and the final line;
- the conflict outcome on attempts 1 to 3;
- the ejection branch.

The fixture in `landing_bot_keeper_6474.test.mjs` gains the two new record fields. The mutation check caught 17
of 17 mutations.
