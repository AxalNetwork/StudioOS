### What was built

- **The map** (`frontend/src/programs/`): one file per program in `catalog/<key>.js` (registry key → canonical page → committed PDF, availability in words, name, purpose, audience, edition, slide count, link preview, index card line) and one in `content/<key>.js` (sections 1 to 4, the Next step band, a fixed availability panel when there is no live read, and the slides). `registry.jsx` collects both folders with `import.meta.glob`; `collect.js` checks every entry and refuses a key that is not its file's name, a PDF path that is not `/program-briefs/axal-vc-<key>-v<n>.pdf`, an unknown availability word, a non-same-origin canonical page, and two programs sharing a file or a page. Adding a program adds files there and edits nothing shared; the recipe is the folder's README.
- **The kit** (PGS00): `kit.jsx` and `programs.css` draw the availability label (glyph and words), Download PDF (an ink-outlined `<a href download>` to the committed file), Print this page (a separate `<button>`), the dark availability panel, the hero with the one h1, "On this page", the five sections in their fixed order, the journey (horizontal at 1440, stacked at 1060 and narrower), and the Next step band with one violet primary and the PDF apart under "Take it with you".
- **`/spinout-lab/brief`** (PGS01 to PGS03) is the presentation. The availability panel reads the public `GET /api/spinout-lab/brief` and draws its five states: open (cohort, start, apply-by and places as read, in the zone the route names), closed (no Apply on the page), unavailable (the shared `Unreadable`, never "closed", Apply to the next cohort kept), a blank field ("Not recorded", never 0) and loading (a skeleton in the card's shape). The four-page print document of D141 is retired with `lib/spinoutBrief.js`; its "Save as PDF" called `window.print()`.
- **The PDF** (PGS04 to PGS06): `frontend/public/program-briefs/axal-vc-spinout-lab-v1.pdf`, five 16:9 pages at 13.333 × 7.5 in, built by `scripts/build-program-briefs.mjs` from the map entry and the content through `deck.js` and `deck.css` with Playwright's Chromium. Text is text, the faces are the legal paper's subsets (Public Sans 400 and 700, Bricolage Grotesque 800, Space Grotesk 700 for the wordmark), embedded; the PDF is tagged with an outline, its links are live, and the cover art is re-encoded as JPEG so the file stays under the 500 KB LFS gate (265 KB). `--gray` writes the grayscale print variant beside it, not committed. It reads nothing live.
- **`/programs` and `/programs/:key`** (PGS08): `routes/programs.jsx`, public and in `isPublicPath`. The index lists only programs with a map entry; the canvas's two labelled example cards are not published. `/programs/spinout-lab` resolves to `/spinout-lab/brief`, keeping the query and hash; an unknown key gets "We could not find that programme." with `noindex`.
- **Link previews:** `/programs` is an `ogRegistry.js` prefix entry; each program's canonical page is prerendered from its map entry (`scripts/lib/programCatalog.mjs`, read by `prerender-og.mjs` and `validate-og-tags.mjs`). `usePageMeta` and `ogTagsFor` take a robots override for the missing-programme page. The sitemap gains `/programs` and `/spinout-lab/brief`.
- **Admin entry points** (PGS07), derived from the map in `AdminPresentationLinks.jsx`: on /admin/programs a mapped program's name (desktop row, card and phone row) links its presentation, and its "Open" reads "Manage program" ("Manage" on a phone row), going where it went. "View presentation" leads the Lab's header (`AdminLab.jsx`) and every `ProgramFrame` header of a mapped program. Programs with no map entry are unchanged.
- **/events** (PGS08C): "See all programs →" beside the Programs heading.

- The index shows the Spin-Out Lab card only; the invitation-only and proposed example cards wait for their programs' own map entries.
