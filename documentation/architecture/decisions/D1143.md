### 1. The engine: `cloudflare-worker/src/services/scoring/pmf.ts`

Pure: it reads no store and writes none.

- **`PMF_MODEL_V1`** is data, frozen. It holds:
  - the version (`1.0`);
  - the ten inputs, each with its weight (10, 5, 10, 10, 20, 15, 10, 5, 5, 10; they sum to 100), its rule to 0–100, its words, its freshness window and its Validate module;
  - the confidence floor (0.25), the confidence words (High 0.70, Medium 0.45, Low 0.25) and the bands (Strong 70, Fair 40, Weak);
  - the freshness windows: 365 days for records, 90 for a connector figure.
- **Four rule kinds:**
  - `count`: n ÷ full, capped. ICP interviews are full at 20.
  - `share`: (n ÷ of) ÷ full, capped. No denominator gives no figure, never 0.
  - `steps`: paying demand 1 → 40, 2–4 → 70, 5 or more → 100.
  - `marks`: product pull averages each part recorded ÷ its mark.
- **`GRADES`** is SCORING's table: Measured 1.0, Reviewed 0.9, Sourced 0.7, Stated 0.4, Not recorded 0.
- **`computePmf(inputs, model, today)`:**
  - **An input's state:** an input that does not apply is `not_expected`; one with no records is `not_recorded`; any other is `recorded`, a 0 included.
  - **Value:** the weighted mean over the recorded inputs, with the weights renormalised over them. A Not recorded input is out of the value, never 0.
  - **Coverage:** recorded inputs ÷ inputs that apply.
  - **Confidence:** Σ (weight × grade) ÷ Σ weight over the inputs that apply. A stale input's grade is halved; its value is kept.
  - **Below the floor:** `state: 'not_enough'`, with no value, band, confidence word or contributions. The page reads it as "Not enough recorded".
  - **Each input's result:** its value, grade, grade factor, stale flag, contribution in points, words, reason, source, `as_of`, the records it left out before the pivot, and its module.
- **`scorePmf(inputs, model, today)`** adds the next steps (§3).

### 2. The collector: `cloudflare-worker/src/services/scoring/pmfCollector.ts`

**What it is.** `collectPmfInputs(env, projectId, today)` returns:
- the ten inputs' facts;
- the Verdict to show, never to score: the current given venture decision, else the latest withheld one, with its evidence count;
- the day of the latest Pivot.

**How it reads.** Every statement is bound.
- **It reads no interviewee name or quote.**
  - The interview read selects the fit, segment, stage, dates and pain phrases, plus a count of transcribed sessions.
  - `claimsOf` gets rows whose name, role, company, notes and claims are null.
  - No buyer, customer, experiment title or result, claim text or decision reasoning is selected.
  - A test spies on every statement and on the result.
- **It reuses Validate's rules:** `isHeld`, `heldDate`, `themesOf`, `claimsOf` (the founder board's verdict), `severityLevel`, the pain group model, and the demand card's stores (both waitlist stores; deposits and pilots from `revenue_entries`).

| Input | Reads | Recorded when | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICP interviews | held interviews, fit strong or partial | any held interview | session transcript (transcribed, structured, reviewed) Measured, else Sourced; mean over the ICP interviews |
| ICP coverage | held with a fit and a segment ÷ held | any held interview | as above, over held |
| Pain frequency | top theme's mentions among ICP interviews ÷ ICP interviews | an ICP interview names a pain | as above |
| Pain severity | judged Severe or Real ÷ judged | any judged interview | as above, over judged; `unjudged` counted for its step |
| Claims supported | Σ (Supported × strongest: measured 1, pattern 0.75, else 0.5) ÷ open claims | any open claim | the held interviews' grade × the share of claims with a verdict |
| Paying demand | paid deposits and pilots, plus confirmed or counted letters of intent and pre-sales with an amount | any demand signal or deposit or pilot | verified Measured, supported Sourced, typed Stated; a letter with a file Sourced, else Stated |
| Would pay | confirmed would-pay ÷ ICP interviews | an ICP interview and any demand signal | session Measured, interview Sourced, typed Stated |
| Waitlist | customer sign-ups + typed waitlist signals ÷ the bar | a bar is set | platform Measured, typed Stated |
| Experiment outcomes | done experiments linked as evidence: those whose every link supports ÷ all | any done, linked experiment | Stated (typed) |
| Product pull | — | never yet (#2409) | applies once a non-prerelease release is published |

**Words.** Each input carries its figure in words ("3 of 5 held interviews with people who fit, 2 from recorded sessions"; "0 of 2 demand signals paid or promised with an amount"), its source table and row count, and its newest record's day as `as_of`. A Not recorded input says why ("No interview held yet", "No bar set"). Product pull before a release reads "Not expected yet".

**Verdict's tables are read through `verdict.ts`** (D693's test 10 holds that no other file names them). It gains two reads, `latestVenturePivotDay` and `ventureVerdictHeader`, and the collector calls them. Neither selects the reasoning or a name.

**The pivot window.** The latest venture Pivot is a `validation_decisions` row with no `hypothesis_id`, `decision = 'pivot'` and `state = 'given'`.
- Only records dated after its day count: interviews by their held day, signals by `signal_date`, payments by `received_on`, sign-ups by `created_at`, experiments by `updated_at`.
- A superseded Pivot still bounds the window, because it happened. A withheld Pivot or a claim's Pivot does not.
- A record with no date is kept: it cannot be placed before the Pivot.

### 3. The next steps

**One fixed rule per input** (`STEP_RULES`). Each rule lists the steps it can ask for, in order, and each step carries the input as it would read once the step is done: the best case, so its value gain is an "up to". `scorePmf` prices each step against the score and keeps the first that moves the value, the confidence or the coverage. An input gives one step at most.

**Ranking.** Steps are ranked by value gain, then by confidence change, then by whether coverage moves.

**What each step carries:**
- "Up to +N";
- a confidence word change ("Medium → High");
- a coverage change ("8 → 9 of 9");
- its module, linked by `stepHref` with `?project=`.

**The rules, in #2433's and the Validate cards' words:**
- **ICP interviews:**
  - "Interview k more people who fit your ICP" (k up to 5, to the mark of 20);
  - "Log your first interviews with people who fit your ICP";
  - "Record your next interview as a session" (Record a session).
- **ICP coverage:** "Record the fit and the segment on N interviews".
- **Pain frequency:**
  - "Hear "[theme]" from N more ICP interviews" (to 60%);
  - "Log the pains each ICP interview names" (Customer research).
- **Pain severity:** "Judge the severity on N interviews".
- **Claims:**
  - "Record the ICP fit on N interviews behind H2" (Customer research);
  - "Link evidence to H3";
  - "Back H1 with measured evidence";
  - "Write the claim you most need to be true".
- **Paying demand:**
  - "Confirm the N signals waiting";
  - "Attach the letter to raise confidence" (the collector gives the grade with the files attached);
  - "Record a paid pilot, a pre-sale or a letter of intent".
- **Would pay:**
  - "Confirm the N would-pay signals waiting";
  - "Ask the next ICP interviews whether they would pay".
- **Waitlist:**
  - "Set the bar for waitlist sign-ups";
  - "Grow the waitlist to your bar of N".
- **Experiment outcomes:** "Close an experiment and link its result as evidence".
- **Product pull:** none until PostHog's metrics are read (#2409).

Code writes every step. Eadwyn writes none.

### 4. Departures, for review

- **Claims are divided by every open claim, not only those with a verdict.** #2433's input table says "Σ ÷ open claims", and its sample (2 Supported at pattern of 5 reads 30) only works that way. Its grade column says a claim with no verdict "is left out of the value"; here that claim adds nothing to the sum. It also lowers confidence: the grade is scaled by the share of claims with a verdict.
- **An input's grade word is the word nearest its mean grade.** For example, 0.9 reads "Reviewed" for a mix of Measured and Sourced interviews. The number is what sets confidence; the word is for the chip.
- **"Would pay" is Not recorded until the venture records a demand signal of any kind.** An ICP interview alone does not make a recorded 0, because the Demand module holds nothing yet.
- **"A product is live" is a published, non-prerelease release.** The other half of #2433's rule, "a PostHog metric tracked", waits for #2409. Until then a live product's pull reads Not recorded, "PostHog metrics are not read yet (#2409)", and lowers confidence.
- **Records on the Pivot's own day are left out.** The rule counts records "dated after it", and a day cannot be split.
- **Nothing is stored or mounted yet.** Part 3 stores the result in SCORING's scorecard store on a changed inputs hash, and mounts `parts.pmf`, the history and Re-score's `pmf: true`.

### 5. Tests

**`cloudflare-worker/test/pmf_engine.test.ts`** has 14 cases. The engine runs on hand-written facts, the collector on SQLite (the baseline's tables and migrations 311, 438, 440, 460, 464 and 499), and every expected figure is worked out in the comments.
- **The model:** v1's data and its rules at their edges.
- **The score:**
  - Not recorded is never 0, and the weights renormalise (62, against a recorded 0's 20);
  - the floor ("Not enough recorded" at 0.19; scored at exactly 0.25);
  - stale grades halved, at 365 and 90 days;
  - product pull Not expected yet, against live and not recorded.
- **The steps:**
  - ranked by gain (+10 before a confidence step; +18 first once two signals wait);
  - a first proposal with no gain giving way to the next;
  - a coverage gain;
  - one test of every input's rule;
  - grades averaged.
- **The collector:**
  - Northwind's ten inputs, each worked by hand, and the score from them: 53, Fair, 0.63 Medium, 9 of 9, with "Link evidence to H1" first at up to +22;
  - no name, note, quote, buyer, customer or experiment text read or returned;
  - a Pivot dropping earlier records, with each input's count left out, while a withheld Pivot and a claim's Pivot drop nothing;
  - claims at 25 with the grade scaled to 0.233;
  - Not recorded with its reason against a recorded 0;
  - a prerelease or unpublished release not counted as a live product.

**35 of 35 mutations killed.** The first try of `ceil` → `floor` in pain frequency's step survived, because 60% of 10 is whole. A case at 7 interviews now catches it.
