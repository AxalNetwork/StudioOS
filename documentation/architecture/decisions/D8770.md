### Built (2026-11-27, against `main` at `d26a93a25`)

Built to option A for Q1 to Q3, as the canvas draws them; Q4 (brand kit) is not touched by this slice.

- **One layout, scaled.** `TemplateSlide.jsx` draws every preview card (gallery cover, aside, Preview all slides, full size) with `BlockGrid` at 1920 × 1080 (`size="print"`, the page the PDF and PowerPoint photograph; the canvas's 960 × 540 halves to it) and scales the frame with `transform`, so the line breaks are the same at every width. The rules are `templatePreviewModel.js`: `previewScale`, and `tooSmall` (a 64 px heading under 9 px on screen, below 270 px wide) which captions "Too small to read here" with Open full size.
- **The template's face.** `previewFace` reads the store's theme (ground, ink, accent, hairline, chart colours, display, body and mono faces with the theme's fallbacks); `useThemeFonts` loads the faces from Google Fonts, only plain family names reaching the URL. `BlockView` draws headings and figures in `colours.display` when a face names one, so Present, print and the editor, whose faces name none, are unchanged.
- **Example and Blank structure.** `templateExamples.js` is the example store: one fictional company, [Larder Lane], keyed by card and layout, so it covers all 24 templates (Q2 A). Cards reach the browser through `sanitizeSlides`, which drops `card`, so a card is matched by its catalogue title (`cardKeyOf`). The Example draws over deep copies, marks each card "Example · fictional figures" and a "sample, fictional" source line on the slide itself, and drops the record bindings of what it fills. The aside, Preview all slides and full size switch between the two; the Example opens first (Q1 A).
- **The example never reaches a draft.** Use this template still posts only `{ project_id }` to `/templates/:uid/apply`, which builds the proposal from the stored structure on the Worker; nothing but the preview imports the example store. The Use this template dialog now lists what carries over (structure, placeholders) and what does not (the example, art, and the theme and type, because Keep still takes the current version's theme, D898).
- **Charts.** `PreviewChart` plots a series with ticks from 0, its unit, the year once and the last value, each series in its own panel; 2 points are two figures with "2 points · shown as figures", 1 point one figure with its month, none a dashed frame saying where the figures come from. A comparison is a table with a glyph and a word, use of funds one bar of shares that sum to its total, a roadmap its milestones.
- **Missing media.** An image or photo with no picture is a labelled dashed frame ("Screenshot · not added yet").
- **Full size.** `TemplateFullSize` has Previous, Next, ← → and Read this slide: the takeaway, the chart's data as a table, the source and what the blank structure holds.
- **Overflow, measured.** Each card measures its areas in the real DOM after the fonts load (`preflightMeasure.measureBlocks`); an area that runs past its box captions "Text runs past its area" with Open full size. Measured over all 24 templates, the blank structure clipped in 11 cards, every one an `image_text` heading in a one-row area (Kitchen Table's "[What the model does; what a person keeps]" among them), at full size as well as in thumbnails. `templateLibrary.ts` now gives that heading rows 1 and 2 and the paragraph rows 3 to 6; the seed upsert carries it to stored HQ templates.
- **Kitchen Table's Product card** is `image_text` with a screenshot area (Q3 A).
- **Tests.** `frontend/test/template_preview_8770.test.mjs` (unit and server render) and `frontend/tests/visual/template_composition_8770.spec.mjs` (the real app and Worker in Chromium: no area of any card of the 24 templates overflows in either view, read both from the preview's own flag and straight from the DOM; Kitchen Table at 1440 and 390 px; a planted long headline is caught; the apply body and the proposal carry no example). The spec writes screenshots for review; it compares no baseline, because baselines are drawn only in CI's pinned container.
