### What the walkthrough found

- Kitchen Table's traction card read "Line chart", "[MRR]", "[Paying customers]": the preview goes `DeckPreview.jsx` → `PitchPresent.jsx` `Card` → `BlockGrid.jsx`, and without `renderChart` a chart block is drawn as "{type} chart" plus its series names. Only the editor (`GridStage.jsx`) plots charts.
- The Solution headline stopped after "[What the model" while the accessibility tree held the whole phrase: `BlockGrid.jsx` re-lays small headings at 15 px inside overflow-hidden areas, and in the 306 px aside the one-row heading area of `image_text` clips it.
- The serif editorial look was hard to see: grid cards are drawn on #fbfaf7 in Outfit with the fixed `FACE_COLOURS`, and the theme is read only for its language. The theme is also lost after apply, because Keep takes the replaced version's theme (D898), `THEME_LABELS` lists no template themes, and the HTML export draws Public Sans and Space Grotesk on #fbfaf7.
- `preflightMeasure.js` measures real DOM overflow at 1920 × 1080 for saved decks only; no template is ever measured.

### What the canvas decides

- Every card is laid out once at 960 × 540 in the template's own theme (for Kitchen Table: cream, Lora, Inter, IBM Plex Mono, accent #2F5D50) and scaled as one picture, so a thumbnail never re-wraps or clips. When a reduced card cannot show its content, it offers Open full size.
- The preview has two views. Example shows a clearly marked fictional company, [Larder Lane]; Blank structure shows main's placeholders. Only the blank structure becomes a deck, and Use this template says what carries over and that the example never does.
- The gallery aside adds what the template sets and the Example / Blank structure switch, and draws main's traction card beside the proposed one.
- Full size at 16:9 has Previous, Next and "Read this slide": the takeaway, the chart's data as a table, the source, and what the blank structure holds instead.
- Five example slides carry one takeaway each: editorial cover, product screenshot, competitive comparison, traction chart, use of funds ([$540k] + [$360k] + [$180k] + [$120k] = [$1.2M]). Type, grid, margins and chart labelling are specified (PTP11, PTP12).
- Charts are plotted in the preview and step down for 2, 1 or 0 points and for Not recorded; long headlines, a missing image and missing photos have drawn fallbacks (PTP14, PTP15).
- After Keep, the editor, Present at 16:9 and every other surface draw the same card with the template's tokens (PTP06 to PTP08). Step 5, main's shared review, is unchanged.
- States: loading, store unavailable, confirmed empty, preview failure with Try again, example unavailable and read-only. The phone (390 × 844) previews in four numbered steps.
