## D652 — Connectors in Eadwyn: per-conversation switches, reads that name their source, nothing sent without Send

**Issue:** #1464, part of #1461 (D649). **Built by:** S09, in two PRs: #1481
(conversations, switches, Send and Cancel) and the follow-up that closes #1464
(the readers, and Send held to every check `/push` makes). **Migration:**
444 (`444_eadwyn_conversations.sql`), which the tests load directly. The
follow-up adds none. **Canvas:** `Connectors Page.dc.html`, screens `rail`
and `railList`, landed with #1468.

### What was true

- **The rail could only read its page back.** `ui/WorkerRail.jsx` had one
  button, "Read this page back" (`POST /api/ai/workspace/explain`), and a model
  picker. It had no question box, no "+", and no conversation.
- **A run took no tools.** The only record of one was its row in
  `ai_usage_logs`.
- **Every write to a connector ran at once.** `POST /api/integrations/:uid/push`
  and `/action/:name` ran on the request; nothing waited for a confirm.

### The owner's call

The issue's switches "belong to one conversation", and a conversation did not
exist. Of three options on #1464 the owner chose **B**: conversations are
stored, with their switches, so a reload keeps them.

**Retention: a conversation is kept until the member deletes it from the
rail.** That is the only way one ends. Migration 444 declares
`ON DELETE CASCADE` from `users`, but no account is ever deleted: there is no
`DELETE FROM users` anywhere (`services/dsrRequests.ts`, on what `fulfilled`
means), so that cascade is a backstop nothing triggers today. A pending
action can no longer be sent 24 hours after it was proposed.

### What is true now

**`/api/eadwyn`** (`routes/eadwyn.ts`) is the caller's own conversations.
Every read is `WHERE user_id = ?`, and someone else's uid answers 404.

| Route | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /connectors` | The "+" menu's list: one row per connected provider, with the reason it cannot be used (`Sign-in expired`, `Needs attention`, `Not finished connecting`, `Disconnected`) or `''`. |
| `GET\|POST /conversations` | List the member's conversations on a workspace, or start one with every switch off. |
| `GET\|DELETE /conversations/:uid` | Read a conversation with its messages and actions, or delete it with them. |
| `PUT /conversations/:uid/connectors` | Set the switches as a whole set: known registry keys only, sorted, at most 12. |
| `POST /conversations/:uid/ask` | One run, only while the conversation's company is still the member's active one (`company_changed` otherwise). |
| `POST /actions/:uid/confirm`, `/cancel` | The member's Send and Cancel. |

**The three rules**, each a function in `services/eadwynConnectors.ts`:

1. **Read gate: `readableConnectors`.** A run reads a connector only if the
   conversation switched it on and the member's connection to it is active.
   The route asks the readers for that list and nothing else (`readForRun`).
   Past the gate, each reader (`services/eadwynReaders.ts`, below) reads only
   when the connector is switched on for the run's company.
   Every answer stores the connectors it read as `{ key, name, as_of }`, and
   the rail prints them as "From Stripe · 12 min ago". The run's receipt
   carries the same list as `usage.connectors_read`.
2. **No write inside a run.** The model answers in one JSON object, and a
   proposed write is a `send` inside it. `validateSend` keeps a proposal only
   when all of these hold:
   - its connector is readable in this run;
   - the provider can push;
   - the payload names exactly one known target (`SEND_TARGETS`: HubSpot and
     Salesforce, `project_id` or `deal_id`) with a positive integer id.

   The route then checks that the target project is the member's to write
   (`canAccessProject`, write) and is in the conversation's company
   (`projectInActiveCompany`): `targetAllowed`. It then asks the provider's
   `preview` what its `push` would send (below). A proposal that passes all
   of it becomes a row in `eadwyn_pending_actions` and is never a call. A
   reply that is not the format is a plain answer with no send.
3. **What the card shows is what goes out.** At proposal time the Worker
   resolves the content: the provider's `preview` (`ProviderImpl.preview`,
   HubSpot and Salesforce) returns the record it lands on ("New HubSpot
   company "Acme"") and every field with its value, including the contact a
   new company or account brings with it. `preview` and `push` call the same
   builder (`dealContent`, `companyContent`, `opportunityContent`,
   `accountContent`), so they cannot disagree;
   `eadwyn_push_preview_d652.test.ts` compares them field by field against the
   request bodies a real push sends. The row stores one envelope,
   `{"send": <payload>, "content": <preview>}` (`actionEnvelope`), and
   `payloadHash` (SHA-256) seals all of it. The model's `summary` is stored
   apart and the card labels it "Eadwyn's words"; it is never the description
   of what is sent.

   **Confirm** reads no request body, and before it sends anything it checks,
   in this order:
   - the row is the member's, still `pending`, under 24 hours old
     (`action_not_pending`, `action_expired`);
   - the envelope re-hashes to the stored hash (`action_changed`) and has
     content (`content_changed` for a row from before content was stored);
   - the role may manage integrations (`ensureRole`, as `/push` checks);
   - the connection is active and its provider can push and preview
     (`connector_unavailable`, `push_not_supported`);
   - the conversation's company is still the member's active one
     (`company_changed`);
   - **the same guard `/push` runs**, `pushScopeRefusal` in
     `routes/integrations.ts`: the connector is switched on for the active
     company and the project is in an enabled company (`company_not_enabled`,
     `project_not_enabled`, 403). `/push` and confirm call this one function;
     neither keeps its own copy;
   - **the target again**, `targetAllowed` run now rather than trusted from
     the proposal (`target_not_allowed`, 403);
   - **the content again**: `preview` rebuilt now must equal the stored
     content byte for byte, or confirm refuses `content_changed` and the
     member asks for a fresh card.

   Then it claims the row (`pending` → `sending`, and still under 24 hours, in
   the same conditional UPDATE), so a double click sends once, and calls the
   provider's `push` with the stored payload. Only a `push` that throws marks
   the row `failed`; once the push has returned, nothing records it as failed.

   **The way out of `sending`.** A Worker that dies between the claim and the
   result leaves the row `sending`. When the conversation is next read, a row
   still `sending` ten minutes after its claim is closed as `failed`, saying
   it is not known whether the update arrived. It is never sent again, which
   could send twice.

   Send and Cancel are both logged to `integration_logs` (`eadwyn_send` ok or
   error, `eadwyn_cancel`), with the payload redacted as every log there is.

   **What the hash does not do.** A plain SHA-256 stored in the same row shows
   that the stored bytes did not drift between proposal and Send. Anyone able
   to write D1 directly can rewrite both, so it is not tamper evidence; that
   would need an HMAC under a Worker secret, which this decision does not add.

   **A switch turned off after the card was shown does not cancel it.** Send
   is the member's explicit click on a card they saw; the company, project,
   target and content checks above still all run.

**The rail.** `ui/EadwynComposer.jsx` sits under the read-back block. It has
the question box and a "+" with "Add a file" (a text file up to 8,000
characters, sent with the next question) and "Connectors ›". The connectors
list draws each connection with a switch; one that needs attention is greyed,
with its reason, and cannot be switched. "Browse connectors" and "Manage
connectors" open `/account/connectors`, the Connectors section D651 (#1463)
built. An answer shows its source lines, and a proposed write
is a card, "Ready to send to HubSpot", with the record, each field and its
value, the model's line marked "Eadwyn's words", and Send and Cancel. The
Research rail's note says the composer answers and sends nothing without a
click; it used to say the rail answers no questions. The rail remembers which conversation is open per workspace,
and nothing else.

### The readers (on #1462's company scope)

`CONNECTOR_READERS` in `services/eadwynReaders.ts` has one read-only function
per connector that writes project data (`writesProjectData`): HubSpot,
Salesforce, Stripe and GitHub. Each reads what the connection already
brought into StudioOS, from D1; none calls the provider.

| Connector | What a run reads |
| --- | --- |
| HubSpot | Deals with a HubSpot id (not Affinity's `affinity:` ids, which share the column): project, stage, amount, last update. |
| Salesforce | Deals with a Salesforce opportunity id, the same lines. |
| Stripe | Each project's latest Stripe snapshot (`project_metrics`, `source = 'stripe'`): MRR, ARR, churn, paying customers. |
| GitHub | What its ledger (`connector_fed_items`, D655) counts per project and module. |

**Company scope, on every run.** A run happens only while the conversation's
company is still the member's active one, resolved against
`user_company_links` at the time of the run. Each reader then asks
`companyEnabled` for that company, which re-reads the member's link and the
connector's switch for it, and reads only that company's projects that are
not deleted. A connector switched off for the company, or a company the member
left, reads nothing.

**No write in a reader.** The test scans `eadwynReaders.ts` and
`eadwynConnectors.ts` for `.push(`, `.sync(`, `.action(` and any SQL write.

Calendly, Carta, DocuSign, Affinity and the rest land personal or unscoped
rows and have no reader yet. Switched on, they are not read and no source
line names them.

### Verified

- `cloudflare-worker/test/eadwyn_connectors_d652.test.ts` (40 tests) runs on a
  real SQLite schema built from migration 444.
  - **A disabled or broken connector is never read**, at the gate and through
    the route: its reader is not called and its data never reaches the model.
  - **A run cannot write.** A proposed send leaves the provider's `push`
    uncalled, and a provider push appears in confirm and nowhere else in the
    route.
  - **Confirm sends exactly the stored payload,** whatever the body says.
  - **Confirm refuses** a changed payload, a second confirm, two racing
    confirms, an expired action, and another member's action.
  - Cancel sends nothing and is logged.
  - **Confirm refuses** a company switched off for the connector, a project in
    a company not switched on, a target that moved to another founder,
    content that changed since the card, a card with no content, a company
    the member left, and a role that may not manage integrations.
  - A push that happened is never recorded as failed; a row stranded in
    `sending` is closed as not known and never re-sent.
  - Each reader reads only the active company's live projects, and nothing
    when the connector is switched off for the company or the member left it.
- `cloudflare-worker/test/eadwyn_push_preview_d652.test.ts` (6 tests) runs the
  real HubSpot and Salesforce providers against a stubbed network and checks
  that `preview` lists exactly the fields each `push` sent.
- `frontend/test/eadwyn_composer_d652.test.mjs` (12 tests) covers:
  - the menu, the greyed rows and their reasons;
  - the source line and its freshness;
  - the card, its fields, "Eadwyn's words", and its states;
  - a confirm with no body.
- Every new assertion was mutation-checked: each mutant fails at least one test.
