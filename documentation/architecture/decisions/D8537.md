### The decision

One spec, `frontend/tests/e2e/public_journey.spec.js`, runs three journeys at 1440 × 900 and at 390 × 844 on the built SPA:

1. **Landing → Pricing → a plan → sign-up → the plan's checkout.** Landing, Founders, "See the token packs" (Pricing), the Founder tab, Validate + Build, the register form, the email link from the outbox, and back on `/pricing` with that plan's checkout sheet open, the ask spent from the address and from storage. The account is new (`exploring`).
2. **Landing → a Grow action → sign-in → that action.** "Share an update" (`/grow/update`), opened signed out from a shared link, goes to "Sign in to continue to Grow"; the email-link steps; back on Share an update.
3. **A lost session → the email link → the same page.** A founder on Share an update whose session ends on the server is sent to `/login?next=…&ended=1` ("Your session ended. Sign in again to go back to Grow."), asks for a new email link and lands on the same page.

On every page of each journey: no sideways scroll; axe finds no serious or critical WCAG 2.0/2.1 A or AA violation beyond the named list below (as `axe-top-routes.spec.js` gates); the sign-in steps put focus on their heading; a refused field (register's name and email, sign-in's email) is `aria-invalid`, names its message with `aria-describedby`, and the message is `role="alert"` text. One failed read per journey: the Pricing catalogue (the page says "Prices unavailable. Try again." and Try again reads it), the sign-in link send (the reason is read out and the same button sends again), and Share an update's own read on Next (the reason is shown, the form stays on step 1, Next again moves on).

**The local Worker, its controlled sign-in and its outbox** are in `frontend/tests/e2e/_helpers.js`: `startLocalWorker({ handle })` serves `docs/` with the SPA fallback and answers `/api/auth/register`, `/api/auth/magic/start`, `/api/auth/magic/verify`, `/api/auth/me`, `/api/auth/logout` and `/api/onboarding/progress` over in-memory accounts, the way `cloudflare-worker/src/routes/auth.ts` does for the email link. The link goes to `outbox`, never to an email; `linkFor(email)` reads it, `addAccount` seeds an account and `endSessions(email)` ends one on the server. `controlledBrowser(context, origin, consentVersion)` decides cookie consent and stands in for Turnstile, and aborts other origins. A spec's own answers come from `handle`. The spec's first test reads `auth.ts` and fails if the Worker's email-link landing (`/onboarding/chat` or `/dashboard`, then `?magic=ok`) moves, because the harness copies it. #8529 and #8527 can use the same helper for their two accounts (two `addAccount` calls, two contexts on one `origin`). It proves what the SPA does with the Worker's answers, not the answers themselves.

**CI.** The `visual` job of `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs it after the Expertise step, on the `docs/` it builds, with `--retries=0 --trace off`. That job does not run on a draft. Locally: `npm run build` at the root, then `cd frontend && npx --no-install playwright test -c playwright.config.js tests/e2e/public_journey.spec.js --trace off`.

### Two return-destination bugs the spec found, fixed here

- **The email link signed nobody in on a fresh browser.** `/api/auth/magic/verify` sets the httpOnly cookie and 302s to `/dashboard?magic=ok`. The SPA holds the user in localStorage and only probes `/api/auth/me` without one for Google's `?google=ok`, so a browser with nothing cached went from `/dashboard` to the sign-in page, signed in on the server but not in the tab, and the kept `?next=` never ran. `useAuthSync.jsx` now treats `magic=ok` as the same marker. The live magic-link probe (`check-magic-link-live.mjs`) checks the Worker's redirect, not the SPA after it, which is why this was not seen.
- **A new account lost the plan it chose.** Back from the email link, RequireAuth redirected to the kept `/pricing?…&checkout=` and marked the redirect spent. React Router runs that navigation as a transition, and the `/me` answer re-rendered the page being left (`/studio`) first; reading the redirect as spent, `/studio`'s role guard sent the `exploring` account to `/exploring` and then the licence picker. The redirect is now keyed on the history entry it was issued from (`location.key`, `pendingNext.js`): a re-render of that entry keeps redirecting to the target, and every other entry reads it spent, as before. #4651's browser test signs in a seeded founder, whom `/studio` admits, so it could not see this. `frontend/test/pending_next_redirect_8537.test.mjs` holds both without a browser.
