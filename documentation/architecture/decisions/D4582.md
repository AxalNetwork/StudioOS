## D4582 — Sign-in carries its own head on every arrival, and its indexing policy is written down (#4582)

#4582 (slot S12). The 6 November 2026 audit followed `axal.vc/grow` into `axal.vc/login` and found the homepage's title, description, canonical and social URL still in the head. A direct load of `/login` was fine, and so was the registration page, so the fault was in how the head is set, not sitewide.

Two drafts recorded this. #5002 merged first and is the indexing policy below. This branch keeps that policy and adds the check that a cold prerender and a client-applied head are the same tags, read from the top-level `<head>` only.

### Cause

Metadata has two writers. The build prerenders each registered route's head from `frontend/src/lib/ogRegistry.js` (`scripts/prerender-og.mjs`), so a cold load of `/login` is correct. A client-side move writes nothing: `/grow` is answered with the SPA shell (the homepage head), the protected route redirects to `/login` inside the app, and no document is fetched, so the shell's head stays. `usePageMeta` is the client-side writer. It also never touched the canonical link, so even a page that did call it kept the shell's canonical.

### Decision

1. **One resolver, two writers, same values.** `AuthScreen` (the wrapper of both `/login` and `/register`) calls `usePageMeta({ path, analytics: false })`, which resolves through the same `ogTagsFor()` the prerender uses. A client arrival and a cold load advertise identical values. `analytics: false` keeps these pages as they were: they never sent a pageview beacon. The pages themselves do not call the hook again: a second call on the same mount restores the previous head in the wrong order when the screen unmounts. This builds on #3705's registry and policy rather than adding a second system; token links and signed-in deep links keep the policy from #3705 (the Worker card injector is untouched).
2. **The canonical link and robots directive are part of the head.** `applyHeadTags(doc, tags)` writes one route's head onto a document and returns the function that puts it back. `applyPageMeta(tags)` is that writer on `document`. Both set `<link rel="canonical">`, creating one if the shell has none, and `<meta name="robots">` only when the route has a directive. Cleanup puts back what it replaced and removes what it added. A route with no directive leaves the shell's robots tag alone.
3. **The indexing policy for sign-in is explicit.** `/login` is `noindex, follow`: a form is a way in, not a search result. It is declared once in the registry (`robots`) and carried three ways, each checked on its own: the prerendered `<meta name="robots">`, the client-applied tag, and an `X-Robots-Tag` header for the path in `frontend/public/_headers`. `/register` is a shared, indexed entry point and carries no robots directive, as do the homepage and every other route. Both paths stay in `sitemap.xml` and are allowed by `robots.txt`; the noindex on `/login` is the exception to that listing, not a reason to drop the listing.
4. **Checked on the top-level head only.** The test reads the real prerendered HTML for `/login` and `/register` and compares it with `applyHeadTags` run on the homepage shell. It reads only tags inside `<head>`, never an embedded verification iframe's document.
5. **Measured live.** `scripts/check-spa-live.mjs` reads `/login`'s `X-Robots-Tag`, canonical and title on every host after a deploy, from the top-level document.

### Verified

`frontend/test/auth_route_meta_4582.test.mjs`: the registry values; arrival by client navigation replacing the homepage head and leaving restoring it; a shell with no canonical; prerender and header wiring; the screen's call; the cold load and the client head agreeing, including robots. Mutation checked on #5002: removing the canonical write fails two of them.

Not changed: no route's own title or description, the Worker card injector, the private/generic card policy, or any migration.
