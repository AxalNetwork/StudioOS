## D4582 — Sign-in carries its own head on every arrival, and its indexing policy is written down (#4582)

#4582 (slot S12, taken at the owner's request). The 6 November 2026 audit followed `axal.vc/grow` into `axal.vc/login` and found the homepage's title, description, canonical and social URL still in the head. A direct load of `/login` was fine, and so was the registration page, so the fault was in how the head is set, not sitewide.

### Cause

Metadata has two writers. The build prerenders each registered route's head from `frontend/src/lib/ogRegistry.js` (`scripts/prerender-og.mjs`), so a cold load of `/login` is correct. A client-side move writes nothing: `/grow` is answered with the SPA shell (the homepage head), the protected route redirects to `/login` inside the app, and no document is fetched, so the shell's head stays. `usePageMeta` is the client-side writer, and neither `LoginPage` nor `RegisterPage` called it. It also never touched the canonical link, so even a page that did call it kept the shell's canonical.

### Decision

1. **One resolver, two writers, same values.** `AuthScreen` (the wrapper of both `/login` and `/register`) calls `usePageMeta({ path, analytics: false })`, which resolves through the same `ogTagsFor()` the prerender uses. A client arrival and a cold load advertise identical values. `analytics: false` keeps these pages as they were: they never sent a pageview beacon. This builds on #3705's registry and policy rather than adding a second system; token links and signed-in deep links keep the policy from #3705 (the Worker card injector is untouched).
2. **The canonical link and robots directive are part of the head.** `applyPageMeta(tags)` (pure of React, so a test can drive it against a fake document) now sets `<link rel="canonical">`, creating one if the shell has none, and `<meta name="robots">` when the route has a directive. Its cleanup puts back what it replaced and removes what it added.
3. **The indexing policy for sign-in is explicit.** `/login` is `noindex, follow`: a form is a way in, not a search result. It is declared once in the registry (`robots`) and carried three ways, each checked on its own: the prerendered `<meta name="robots">`, the client-applied tag, and an `X-Robots-Tag` header for the path in `frontend/public/_headers`. `/register` is a shared, indexed entry point and is unchanged, as are the homepage and every other route (none gets a robots tag).
4. **Measured live.** `scripts/check-spa-live.mjs` now reads `/login`'s `X-Robots-Tag`, canonical and title on every host after a deploy, from the top-level document, not from any embedded verification frame.

### Verified

`frontend/test/auth_route_meta_4582.test.mjs`: the registry values; arrival by client navigation replacing the homepage head and leaving restoring it; a shell with no canonical; prerender and header wiring; the screen's call. Mutation checked: removing the canonical write fails two of them. The real prerender output for `/login`, `/register` and `/` was read, and `prerender-og --check` is stable.

Not changed: no route's own title or description, the Worker card injector, the private/generic card policy, or any migration.
