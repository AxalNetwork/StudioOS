## D1231 — Business verification cases and the KYB decision path

Issue #3239, part of #2384. Slot S32 · Codex. Migration 616.

### Model

`kyb_cases` is a review record beside the original business, rather than a
widening or rebuild of `company_kyb_records`. Its stable external reference is
`company-<company_profiles.id>`, `account-<corporate_profiles.user_id>`,
`import-<kyc_partner_imports.id>` or `studio-<kyb_licensed_studios.id>`.
The read views discover later member submissions automatically. One source
reference has one case. Companies and account entities never share a verdict.
Corporate investor entities and other account firms are distinguished by role.
HQ's own entities and funds are excluded.

Licensed studios are new explicit records, one per existing territory licence.
Only a Super Admin on HQ can create, read or decide them. Ordinary case access
requires an Admin in the case's deployment. Another administrator's legal entity or verified owner link
requires Super Admin, including list, showing and document access. Each decision also requires a recent step-up and a studio
that is not suspended. No cross-studio database or impersonation access is added.

A staff member cannot decide their own entity or introduce a link to themselves.

A case stores the exact internal source revision. It includes legal details as
well as the timestamp, so even two edits in the same second invalidate a
verdict. It is internal sensitive case data, never a response, audit detail or
error. Staff-recorded owners/directors apply only to the revision they reviewed.
Non-decision edits clear verdict metadata and cannot revive a rejection or
stale owner links when a source has been resubmitted.
Member-supplied owner JSON cannot establish an account link; staff explicitly
record those links. Every owner owning 25% or more needs approved KYC or a
required explanation before approval, alongside explicit registry/ownership
confirmations and recorded owner disclosure. The final SQL checks KYC again
inside the conditional update. Single-case reads also bind the indexed source id; Approvals binds its scope
through the source’s shared bindings hook in both query executors. Case changes use a version comparison; their
history and activity record commit atomically with them. Company writes also
update migration 220's member-facing projection in that transaction; the member
Trust Center therefore sees the same verdict. The revision is then recaptured
so that projection updates do not invalidate the decision they just recorded.

### API and privacy

`routes/kyb_admin.ts` mounts at `/api/kyc/businesses` before the people router.
It supplies the paged, oldest-first queue (search, status, kind and jurisdiction),
a business case, recorded owners and their KYC, directors, documents, registry,
read-only account-linked due-diligence sections, and history. Independent case
blocks return `available: false` when unreadable, or `recorded: false` with a
reason when missing. A failed queue is 503, never an empty successful response.

Writes: `PUT /:id/owners`; `PATCH /:id/approve`, `/reject`,
`/request-documents`, `/dismiss` and `/link-account`; `POST /licensed-studios`
and `/:id/documents`. Linking is limited to a local account and resets an import
to review. Dismiss applies only to an unlinked import and keeps its history.
The client is `frontend/src/lib/api/kyb.js` (`kybApi`).

Registration numbers, addresses and dates of birth leave masked. Search accepts
a full registration number only; it cannot probe substrings of a masked value. Clear reads
use `GET /:id/sensitive/:field` (owner date of birth also takes `?index=`),
writing `kyc_field_shown` before returning a value. Tax data is last-four only;
this API does not decrypt tax identifiers. Director emails and raw JSON never
leave through the case read. Request/error text, revisions, document bytes and
R2 keys are absent from logs. Activity rows contain case references, actions
and outcomes, plus the field name for a showing.

Documents are limited to PDF/JPEG/PNG/WebP and 6 MB, stored only in private R2
under the case's studio prefix. `GET /:id/documents/:documentId` checks both
case ownership and the existing Cloudflare Access middleware, and saves the
view audit before streaming. No public or signed object URL is generated.
A failed upload transaction removes its object. Rejection first tombstones all
documents in the decision transaction, then deletes bytes. A storage failure
returns a fixed refusal; repeating rejection retries cleanup. Tombstoned bytes
cannot be streamed during that retry window.

### Trust and Approvals

The nightly sync reads a real approved account case, or a linked import when
there is no corporate profile. It copies the expiry recorded at approval
(currently 24 months), never extends it on the next sweep, and reopens an
obligation when a verdict is rejected, expires, disappears or its source
changes. Satisfaction backed by other evidence is preserved. The sweep reads
the business view once, rather than once per obligation. Company approvals do not satisfy a shareholder's account obligation.
An unreadable KYB reconciliation fails with a fixed error rather than reporting
an empty successful sweep. The obsolete missing-column debt and the tests that
pinned the inert reconciler have been replaced by runnable assertions.

Approvals reads the same waiting business view, scoped to the deployment,
and reports KYC & KYB › Businesses plus `decision_path: /admin/kyc/businesses`
as the decision place. The lane's frontend
door awaits #3242's business route; current main has no such route and this
Worker task does not edit the frontend files that #3242 owns. Licensed studios remain
on the Super Admin's business queue. No member notice is sent by this work;
the lane honestly continues to say Nobody yet. The business-page frontend is
#3242. No screen or people-case implementation is included here.

Review follow-up: member company reads use the derived case status so expiry and changed legal details are visible immediately. Company submitters and members cannot decide their own company case. Corporate profile creation stays Not started until an explicit obligation review request or staff case exists; pending_review imports are explicit review requests. Obligation state is excluded from the source fingerprint so reconciliation cannot invalidate its own decision.

Concurrent review: the case returns an opaque HMAC review_token. Every case write requires that displayed token in If-Match; the client never refreshes it silently. A changed source, status or case version returns 409, and each final SQL update rechecks the source revision within the transaction. Dismissed imports reject all writes. Staff cases without a member submission stay in the case store; projection updates never create a member submitter. Member reads include these staff cases. Migration 616 remaps KYB reviewer assignments and history from legacy record ids, staging negative ids to avoid collisions and retaining orphan history under negative archival keys.

The shared Approvals inbox excludes administrator-linked business identities and staff owner links; those cases remain in the Super Admin Businesses queue. This keeps generic lane reads and assignments from revealing a case the caller cannot open.
