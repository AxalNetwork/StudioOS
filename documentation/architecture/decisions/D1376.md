## D1376 — A Meet call started in Record a Session is a space Axal creates: Open, and transcribing itself

- **Status:** asked for by the owner on 2026-10-29 ("so let's try that route"). Built by S21.
- **Builds on:**
  - D871: attaching a Meet call;
  - D886: the meeting bot;
  - D1346: "Create the meeting for me";
  - D1360: Eadwyn AI joins by default;
  - D1362: one Start;
  - D1374: the bot reads Meet's refusal.
- **Migration:** 656 (`meet_created_spaces`, additive).

### Why

The bot joins as a guest who is not signed in. On the owner's axal.vc Workspace, Meet turned away every such guest with "You can't join this video call". That happened even with the admin console's Domain setting set to all users, including those not signed in, and the meeting access type set to Open. The Zoom bot works end to end.

A meeting made through a Calendar event takes the account's defaults, and Axal cannot change them. A meeting made through the Meet API takes the settings Axal gives it.

### What changed

- **Consent:**
  - The Meet permission (`POST /api/calendar/google/meet-transcripts/connect`) now asks for `meetings.space.created` beside `meetings.space.readonly`.
  - `meetings.space.created` reaches only the spaces Axal itself created.
  - The callback still requires only the transcript scope. A member who leaves the new box unticked keeps today's behaviour.
- **`POST /api/founder/validate/meetings/instant`, Google Meet:**
  - With the new scope, Axal creates the call through `spaces.create` (`createOpenSpace`). The settings:
    - `accessType: OPEN`: anyone with the link joins without knocking, within what the member's Workspace admin allows guests.
    - `artifactConfig.transcriptionConfig.autoGenerationType: ON`: Google transcribes the call itself.
  - An account whose plan refuses the artifact setting (400 or 403) gets the Open space alone. A personal Gmail account is one such case.
  - The space and its slot are recorded in `meet_created_spaces`.
  - The reply adds `open: true` and `auto_transcribe`.
  - Without the scope, or when the Meet API refuses to create, the Calendar event is made as before.
- **Attaching the call (D871):**
  - An attach by Meet code looks in `meet_created_spaces` first: the member's own rows only, from the last 30 days.
  - So a call Axal created is saved without a Calendar read. It is saved as the member's own call, in its slot, with `external_ref` `spaces:<id>`.
  - The sweep then finds its transcript by meeting code, as for any Meet call.
  - D1360 sends Eadwyn AI as before.

### What this does and does not solve

- **The founder does nothing in Google.** Start Call creates the Open call, opens it and sends the bot. When the call ends, Google's own transcript is imported if the bot could not get in, or alongside the recording if it could.
- **A Workspace admin who forbids every guest still keeps the bot out.** No meeting setting overrides that. The automatic transcript is the path for those calls, on plans that have Meet transcripts.
- **A pasted link to someone else's meeting is unchanged.** It depends on that host's settings.

### Owner steps (Google Cloud)

The Google Cloud project behind `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` needs:

1. **The Google Meet REST API enabled.**
2. **`meetings.space.created` added to the OAuth consent screen's scopes.** It is a sensitive scope, so a published app may need Google's verification for it.

The founder then presses **Allow transcript import** once in Account › Connectors. Google asks for both permissions.

### Verified

- **Worker tests:**
  - `record_call_create_meeting_d1346`: the space is created Open and transcribing, with no Calendar event, and is recorded with its slot. An account that refuses the artifact setting gets Open alone. Without the scope, the Calendar event is made as before.
  - `meet_attach_speakers_d871`: an Axal-created space attaches with no Calendar read, as the member's own call. Its transcript is looked up by code. Another member's space with the same code is not theirs.
- **Mutation check:** without the space lookup, the attach test fails; with `TRUSTED` in place of `OPEN`, both creation tests fail.
- **Neighbouring tests:** the 31 Google, Meet, calendar and connector test files pass, 598 of 598, and `tsc` passes.
