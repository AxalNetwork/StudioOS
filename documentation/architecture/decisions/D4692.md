## D4692 — The slot list says what the booking would say (#4692)

Follow-up to D4695, which enforces the advisor's availability rules at the
booking itself. #4692 asked that what a founder is *shown* agree with what the
booking will *do*; D4695 left the list as it was.

### The failure

`GET /api/advisors/:uid/slots` listed every slot that was not cancelled or
blocked. A slot inside the advisor's minimum notice, inside a blackout, too
close to a session the advisor already holds, or in a week already at the
paid cap was offered, and the founder learned otherwise only from the 409 at
the click.

### The rule

The list asks the same `ruleRefusal` the booking asks, for every slot in one
pass (rules read once, the advisor's held sessions read once, the week's paid
sessions read once). Each item gains two fields:

- `unavailable_code`: `null`, or `min_notice` / `blackout` / `buffer`. These
  hold for any session type, so they are stated per slot.
- `paid_cap_reached`: `true` when a paid session cannot be booked in that
  slot's week. A free or included session can still be booked, so this is a
  separate flag and not a refusal of the slot.

The slot stays in the list: a founder can see that a time exists and why it is
not offered. The booking remains the decision; the list is advice, read at one
instant, and a slot that reads open can still be refused by the INSERT.

### Not done

- The frontend does not yet grey out or explain these slots (no screen in this
  PR reads the new fields).
- Recording who confirmed, completed, cancelled or no-showed a booking, and
  the sessions zone's reload offer on a 409, are not in this PR; they are the
  rest of #4692 and stay open there.
