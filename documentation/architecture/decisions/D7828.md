## D7828 — Sourced business facts for a company, and what is supported for each research job (#7828)

**Store.** `research_company_facts` (migration `202611232026_7828_research_company_facts.sql`) holds one row per fact, keyed on the company's uid and a fact key that is stable within that company. Each fact has an attribute from a closed list of twelve (product, capability, use case, buyer, customer segment, markets served, business model, delivery model, pricing model, partner programme, integration listing, contact or application path), a value, a statement type (company statement, registry fact, third-party observation, analyst interpretation), a source link, the publisher as the source names it, a passage capped at 400 characters or the structured field it was read from, `published_at`, `retrieved_at` and `reviewed_at`, a state (found, not_found, not_offered, source_unavailable, outdated) and links to a conflicting fact, the fact it replaces and the fact that replaced it. The closed lists, the found and absence rules and the page-link rule for a contact path are CHECK constraints. A trigger refuses any UPDATE except marking a fact outdated and linking its replacement once: value, passage and source are never edited, so a later citation stays true. The uid carries no foreign key, as D7743's store does not, because an importer re-inserts directory rows. The schema migration writes no rows.

`company_sources` stays the source of identity fields and is neither read for nor written by this store. A pricing-model fact is a category only; a price with an amount is #7723's. Nothing is inferred from a sector label or a classification code.

**Vocabulary.** `services/researchEvidenceVocabulary.ts` exports the statement types, the states, the three date names and the words "Checked by Axal on [date]" for `reviewed_at`. Its header says it is not the observation vocabulary: an observation's status and its four states are #7716's, and neither list imports the other. #7839 and #7841 reuse it. Fund statements (#7743) keep their own columns.

**Writer.** `scripts/import-company-facts.mjs` compiles a committed reviewed manifest into a migration, as D7843's importer does, with `--check`. The reviewer and the review date come from the manifest; the manifest is public, so an email address or phone number anywhere is refused. Refused at compile time, each with its code, and the manifest whole with no file written: a fact without a source (`source_missing`), an attribute, state or statement type outside the lists, a found fact with no value or no passage or field, an absence with a value, a contact path that is not a page link (`contact_path_not_page_link`) or that is a person's name or profile, an email address or a phone number (`contact_path_person_name`, `contact_path_email`, `contact_path_phone`), an amount in a pricing-model value (`pricing_model_amount`), and a changed value under a key an earlier compile used. "Markets served" keeps the source's words, and where they name one country the compiler adds the code from `mapCompanyCountry` (the taxonomy crosswalk, D533).

At apply time a fact is written only where its company uid is in `REACHABLE_COMPANIES` (D7819), and a replay writes nothing (`UNIQUE (company_uid, fact_key)`, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`). **Skipped facts after deploy:** every row records `manifest_name`, and the compiled file's header gives the manifest's fact count and lists each fact; the skipped count is that number less `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM research_company_facts WHERE manifest_name = '<name>'`, an aggregate read. The store is empty in production until a reviewed manifest is compiled and deployed; the first production manifest is separate work.

**Reads.** `routes/research_company_facts.ts` mounts ahead of `research.ts`:

- `GET /api/research/company-directory/:uid/facts` returns current and superseded facts apart, a conflict marked on both sides, a corrected fact as outdated with its replacement's key, each fact's licence from the publisher reference of D7810 when its publisher has a row there, and each attribute's answer. The four states and "no row" are five distinct answers; no row reads `not_recorded` with "Needs research".
- `GET /api/research/company-directory/:uid/completeness` answers four jobs. Lookup is read from the served record (name, website, country). Competitor research needs product, buyer or customer segment, use case and markets served. Partner discovery needs capability, customer segment, markets served and one of partner programme, integration listing or contact path. Each field is supported with its evidence or not recorded with "Needs research"; a checked absence is listed under the field. Investment-firm discovery is listed and answers "Not assessed in this read", naming organisation roles and the fund statement store (#7743), read through #7748 and #7749. No number and no score.

Both check the Companies rule (`admitsCompanies` and `refuseCompanies` of D7178, imported, not edited) before the record is read, then read it through `readCompanyByUid` (D7819): an absent, excluded or withdrawn record answers 404 with no facts. A member read returns the review date and never the reviewer or the manifest name. A failed read is a 503 (`company_facts_unreadable`), never an empty list.

**Registration with #7824.** #7824 was not on main when this branch was cut or when its PR opened, so the reads call the existing Companies rule, and #7824 registers them in its enumeration test and access matrix when it lands, as its scope says.

**Not here.** The strict search by attribute with its three counts, and its page (#7859); the profile's facts section (#7849); the first production manifest.
