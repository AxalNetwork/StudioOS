### 2. The routes

| Route | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `PATCH /api/dd/cases/:uid` | an Admin or the case lead | Changes the lead, target IC date, round, cheque or linked deal. `{ archive: true, archived_reason }` archives the case. |
| `POST /api/dd/cases/:uid/decision` | an Admin or the case lead | Records `{ decision, reason }`; the reason is required. Sets `status = 'completed'`. |
| `GET /api/dd/cases/:uid/memo` | an Admin, the lead, the opener | Returns the memo, or `{ memo: null }`. |
| `PUT /api/dd/cases/:uid/memo` | an Admin or the case lead | Saves summary, verified, on trust and conditions, at most 8,000 characters each. |
| `GET /api/dd/cases/:uid/reports` | who may generate a report: an Admin, or a partner who leads, opened or reviews the case | Every report: format, date, who generated it, the band at the time, and whether it was stored. |
| `POST /api/dd/cases/:uid/reports/:id/download` | the same, with a TOTP session and a recent step-up | Returns a fresh five-minute link. A report that was never stored answers 410. |
| `POST /api/dd/cases/:uid/ic` | an Admin or the case lead, who may use IC | A draft IC record with the case attached, the startup as its project and the linked deal. |

- **The gate.** Every write here belongs to an Admin or the case lead. A section reviewer never has it. Neither does an opener who has handed the lead on, though the opener keeps the read they had.
- **An archived case takes no further change.** The decision, the memo and every `PATCH` answer 409.
- **The lead.** Only an Admin, a partner or an investor can be made lead. An advisor never leads, as an advisor never opens a case. The new lead is told in the bell.
- **Every write records a `dd_audit_log` row.** The details name the decision, or which fields changed, never the figures.

### 3. The decision, and what drains

- **Recording a decision** sets `status = 'completed'`. The Approvals lane counts `open` and `in_review` (`approvalSources.ts`), so a decided case leaves it, as an archived one does.
  - A decision can be recorded again: "Change decision…" on the canvas. Each change is audited with the decision it replaced.
- **Proceed is refused** while any blocking section carries an open High or Critical flag. The answer is 409 `blocking_flag_open`, with the canvas's sentence ("A blocking section still has an open High flag. Record Proceed with conditions, or close the flag first.") and the sections' ids. Two things count as an open flag (`blockingSectionsWithOpenSevereFlags`):
  - a finding with no `resolved_at`;
  - a checklist item still flagged or failed at High or Critical.
  Part 3 (#3262) adds flags raised by hand and a flag's state. That state should feed the same function.
- **Reviewers are told** the decision in the bell, never its reason. The case read gives a section reviewer the decision, but not the reason, the cheque, the linked deal or the IC record.
- **A scan no longer reopens a case.** The scan's status write moved `in_review` onto any case. It now moves only an open one, so a decided case does not go back onto the lane.

### 4. Blocking sections

- **The rule is the template's** until part 5 (#3264) stores templates. It comes from the canvas's template board (DDL13): Corporate & Legal Structure, Financial Health & Runway, and Compliance / AML / Sanctions (`BLOCKING_SECTION_KEYS`).
- **A case stamps it on its sections at open** (`stampNewCase`, called by both openers: `POST /api/dd/cases` and the partner-deal seed). A later change to the list never rewrites a case that is already open.
- **Migration 620 applies the same rule to existing sections.** It is a template rule, not a recorded fact, so this is not inventing data.

### 5. Send to IC

- **It follows a decision to go ahead.** It needs Proceed or Proceed with conditions; Do not proceed and Withdrawn never go to IC. A case goes once; a second send answers 409 `already_sent`.
- **The record is written in `ic.ts`** (`createIcRecordForCase`), with the same company scope and project check as `POST /api/ic`. It adds IC's own gate (`canUseIc`): an advisor never reaches IC.
- **The case read carries `ic_record` through `icDecisionScope`.** A lead who cannot open the record in IC does not learn its uid from the case either. This adds one `${scope.sql}` to `scripts/sql-prepare-baseline.json` (`scope.sql @ routes/ic.ts`). It is the module's own scope fragment, with its values bound, like the five other `scope.sql` sites there.

### 6. The case lead's verdict (#2385 item 19)

The case lead records a verdict on any section without a reviewer row or an NDA upload, as an Admin does. They run the case and already read all of it. A section reviewer still records only on their own section.
