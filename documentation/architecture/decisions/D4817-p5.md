## D4817 part 5 — PF-05 Value-add: commitments promised and delivered, introductions, and what the ledger does not record (#4817)

Part of #5311 (PF-05): the fifth board of the Portfolio v2 canvas (`design/incoming/Portfolio v2 Page.dc.html`, board PF-05, with addendum PFA08 for Log support and Deliver/Withdraw, and PFA12 for the tablet rows). **What is built.** `frontend/src/pages/investor/InvestorPortfolioValueAdd.jsx` is rebuilt in place. `PortfolioWorkspace` still returns it for an investor at `/portfolio/value-add`, so the address and every link to it resolve. It draws in `PortfolioFrame` with `tab="value-add"`. The document title is `Value-add · Portfolio · Axal VC`, and the board carries its own `WorkerRail`. The admin's Value-add tab is unchanged (`PortfolioGrowthPage`). `/portfolio/growth` stays retired (#2297). The page holds, in the canvas's order:
- **The figure row:**
  - **Open commitments**: `totals.promised`.
  - **In progress**: Not recorded.
  - **Delivered**: `totals.delivered`, with how many companies in the book have an entry.
  - **Introductions made**: the ledger's delivered `intro` entries.
  - **Overdue commitments**: Not recorded.
  - **No support recorded**: `untouched`, with the companies named.
  Each figure uses the shared `FinancialMetric`. The hours recorded and the untimed count sit under the row. A line says what the ledger does not record.
- **Commitments**, one table:
  - Company, as the link to `/portfolio/companies/:uid`, with the kind of support.

  The toolbar keeps the zone's four chips (All, Delivered, Outstanding, By company) and three ops (Log support, Export, Per-company view). By company swaps in the per-company rollup, which names every company in the book, the ones with nothing recorded included.
- **Introductions**: the ledger's `intro` entries, each with its company, state, dates and "Meeting resulting: Not recorded", and the reason.
- **The Eadwyn summary band** (`ZoneDraft`, `portfolio/value-add`, rail option `draft:support-summary`), unchanged.

**The one store, and what it does not record.** Every row is a `portfolio_support_entries` entry, read by `GET /api/portfolio-support`. The ledger records Promised, Delivered and Withdrawn, the dates of each, the hours, the outcome a member wrote, who recorded it, and since D4642 its amendments. Four things are not recorded, and none of them is estimated or drawn as 0:
- In progress and Cancelled. The ledger has no such state.
- A due date, so Overdue commitments.
- The meeting that came of an introduction. The ledger records work, not outcomes.
- An introduction requested through the quarterly quota (`investor_introductions`). That is access to a company, not support given to one. It is not read here, as IP3 decided.
These stay with #4642 (D4817's "Backend required").

**How each part reads** (`frontend/src/pages/portfolio/valueAddModel.js`, pure and clock-free):
- **The ledger read** goes through `usePortfolioReads` and its shape check (`ledgerShape`). A failed read reads Unreadable, with "That is not a claim that no support was given" and a Retry. The figures, the commitments and the introductions each say so, and none draws an empty ledger or a 0.
- **The figures have their own read** on the Worker, an aggregate over the whole scope. When that read fails, the Worker sends `totals: null` and `page_state: 'unreadable'`.
  - `totalsOf` returns null, and the figures read Unreadable with a Retry.
  - The entries beside them still stand.
  - What the ledger never records stays Not recorded, not Unreadable.
- **Introductions are counted only over a read that holds every entry** (`holdsWholeLedger`): the first page, no state filter, and as many rows as `totals.entries`.
  - The ledger can be longer than one read. It returns 400 rows at a time.
  - When the read does not hold every entry, the figure reads Unavailable, and the card says it holds the newest N of M.
- **A filtered or later page** (Outstanding, Delivered, Next) is its own read, `?state=` and `?offset=`. It is keyed by the view and retried on its own. Its failure leaves the figures and the introductions standing.
- **One entry's amendment history** is read on demand. A failed read is Unreadable with its own Retry, never "No amendment is recorded".
- **Hours:**
  - `null` is Not recorded, and a measured 0 stays `0 h`.
  - A ledger whose entries are all untimed reads Not recorded hours.
  - A company with no timed entry reads Not recorded hours.
- **Owner:** the ledger read carries `recorded_by` (a member id), not a name.
  - The page says **You** when it is the signed-in member, and **Another member** otherwise, naming where the name is: the entry's history.
  - It says Not recorded when no member is recorded.
- **Dates:**
  - A delivered entry with no `delivered_at` reads "Delivery date not recorded". It never borrows the creation date.

**Writes** (all already mounted: `POST /api/portfolio-support`, `PATCH /api/portfolio-support/:uid`, `GET /:uid/history`). D4642's guards are kept: the write names the record and the revision it read, and one save is pending at a time.
- **Mark delivered… and Withdraw…** confirm in place (PFA08). The confirmation names the entry and its company, and says the act is final.
  - Nothing is sent until the member confirms.
  - A failed save keeps the confirmation and its context, says it did not save, and offers "Try saving again".
  - A conflict (`changed_since_open`) reads the ledger again and says the other member's change is shown.
  - A terminal refusal names the entry's company.
- **Log support** (PFA08): every field is labelled. A missing company or description is tied to its field (`aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`).
  - A failed save keeps every entry, says "This did not save. Your entries are kept." and offers "Try saving again".
  - A blank hours field is sent as absent, never as 0.
  - Ask (D1595) still fills "What happened" once a company is chosen.

**Departures from the canvas, and why.**
- **"Delivered this quarter" reads Delivered, over every entry.** The support read carries no as-of date, and the page does not read the browser clock. A quarter would need the Worker's date from another source, which this board does not read.
- **The Created column is "Recorded".** `created_at` is when the entry was recorded. The promise date is shown under it when one is recorded.
- **Owner is You or Another member, not a name.** The ledger read carries no name. The name is in the entry's history.
- **"Overdue · promised by 31 Oct" in the canvas's sample** needs a due date the ledger does not record. Overdue reads Not recorded.
- **Introductions get their own card** under the commitments. The canvas folds them into the table's Next / linked column. The table keeps "Meeting resulting: Not recorded" there too, and the card gives the introductions alone, with the reason meetings are not counted.
- **Withdraw… is offered beside Mark delivered….** The canvas shows only Mark delivered…. Withdrawn is a state the ledger records (PFA08: "Supported states Promised · Delivered · Withdrawn").
- **The confirmation is in the row, not a Sheet.** It keeps the entry beside the table, so the context stays on screen on a phone. PFA08 asks for the entry, the company and the effect. It does not ask for a dialog.
- **The local navigation carries no counts**, as in PF-01 ("4 open" in the canvas).
