---
name: pre-meeting-investor-brief
description: Prepares a brief before meeting an investor, from the venture's own relationship records.
roles: [investor_relations, ceo]
tools: [network.read, raise.read]
output: brief
taxonomy: {cap_investor_relations: proficient, cap_venture_capital: working}
version: 1
---
1. Read the investor's record in the venture's relationships: firm, stage, past interactions and notes (`network.read`).
2. Read where they sit in the round's pipeline and any open asks (`raise.read`).
3. Write the brief: who they are, what was said before, where they stand, what to ask today.
4. Use only what the venture recorded. A thesis or check size not recorded reads "Not recorded". Never guess from public sources.
5. Name people by name, never by email.
6. Propose the brief. It changes no pipeline stage.
