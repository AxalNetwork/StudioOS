1. Read the journey's interviews and problems (`validate.read`) and the internal steps recorded in Build (`build.read`).
2. Lay out the journey in lanes: customer actions, front-stage staff, back-stage staff and systems.
3. Mark each recorded pain point on the step where it happens.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a lane with no recorded step.
5. Propose the outline to the operations lead.
