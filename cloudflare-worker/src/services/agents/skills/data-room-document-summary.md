1. Read the one document named and its metadata (`data_room.read`).
2. Say what kind of document it is, who the parties are and its date. A field the document does not state is "Not recorded".
3. List the five points a reader needs, each with a short quote and its page.
4. Never give legal or tax advice. Flag clauses a person should read in full.
5. Propose the summary beside the document. It changes no file and shares nothing.
