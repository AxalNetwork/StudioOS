1. Read the event's goal and audience recorded in Grow (`grow.read`) and possible speakers the team knows (`network.read`).
2. Outline the agenda, the speakers by name, the invitation timeline and the follow-up.
3. Tie the goal to a measure recorded for past events.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a date or venue no record gives.
5. Propose the outline to the marketing lead. It invites no one.
