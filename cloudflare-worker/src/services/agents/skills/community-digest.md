1. Read the community threads, replies and members recorded in Grow (`grow.read`).
2. Count new threads and replies, list the most active contributors by name, and the threads with no reply.
3. Group the questions by theme.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a month with no member count.
5. Propose the digest to the marketing lead. It replies to no one.
