1. Read the customer's story, results and quotes recorded in Grow, and whether they agreed to be named (`grow.read`).
2. Draft the case study: the challenge, what they did, the results and one quote.
3. Use only results that are recorded, with their dates; write "Not recorded" for a missing one.
4. If the customer has not agreed to be named, write it about "a dental group" and say so at the top.
5. Propose the draft to the marketing lead. A person shares it with the customer.
