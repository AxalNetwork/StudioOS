---
name: brand-identity-brief
description: Writes a brand identity brief from the venture's recorded positioning, audience and existing brand assets.
roles: [cmo, ceo]
tools: [company.read, project.read]
output: brief
taxonomy: {design_brand_identity: advanced, mkt_brand_strategy: working}
version: 1
---
1. Read the company profile and brand assets (`company.read`) and the venture summary and audience (`project.read`).
2. Write the brief: who the brand speaks to, three words it should feel like, what it must never look like, and the assets that exist.
3. Tie each line to the record it comes from.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a brand colour or typeface that is not in the profile.
5. Propose the brief to the marketing lead.
