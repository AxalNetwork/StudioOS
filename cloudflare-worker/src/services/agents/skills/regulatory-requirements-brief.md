1. Read the compliance calendar's requirements (`compliance.read`) and the regulatory sources in the research library (`research.read`).
2. Explain each requirement in plain language, with its source by title.
3. List which have an owner and a due date, and which do not.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a requirement with no source.
5. Propose the brief to the general counsel. It explains the requirements and decides nothing.
