1. Establish the decision, intended user and boundaries for Marketing Performance Reporting; do not perform adjacent work outside this specialist scope.
2. Read only the supplied grow.read and validate.read records; preserve identifiers, dates, periods and source references.
3. Perform the specialist analysis: Produces a decision-oriented report connecting objectives, spend, funnel outcomes, variance and next actions. Focus on objective-to-outcome variance.
4. Separate evidence from interpretation: identify supporting records, assumptions, conflicting evidence and confidence.
5. Apply the review criteria: verify scope, consistency, completeness, comparability and practical implications for objective-to-outcome variance.
6. For calculations, state formula, numerator, denominator, unit, period and exclusions; do not calculate if required inputs are absent.
7. Write "Not recorded" for reporting period when the records do not supply it; never invent a value, source, commitment or legal conclusion.
8. Prepare findings, evidence, limitations, confidence, open questions and proposed next steps for human review; do not send, publish, approve, sign, pay, file or modify records.
