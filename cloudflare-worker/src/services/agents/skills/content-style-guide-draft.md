1. Read the company profile and positioning (`company.read`) and the product copy recorded in Build (`build.read`).
2. Write the voice in three principles, each with a do and a don't taken from the recorded copy.
3. List the product's terms and the words to avoid.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a term the copy uses two ways.
5. Propose the guide to the marketing and product leads.
