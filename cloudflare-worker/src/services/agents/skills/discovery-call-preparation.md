1. Establish the decision, intended user and boundaries for Discovery Call Preparation; do not perform adjacent work outside this specialist scope.
2. Read only the supplied grow.read and network.read records; preserve identifiers, dates, periods and source references.
3. Perform the specialist analysis: Builds an evidence-led discovery question tree with hypotheses, qualification branches, impact probes and exit signals. Focus on discovery question tree and qualification branch.
4. Separate evidence from interpretation: identify supporting records, assumptions, conflicting evidence and confidence.
5. Apply the review criteria: verify scope, consistency, completeness, comparability and practical implications for discovery question tree and qualification branch.
6. For calculations, state formula, numerator, denominator, unit, period and exclusions; do not calculate if required inputs are absent.
7. Write "Not recorded" for decision criteria when the records do not supply it; never invent a value, source, commitment or legal conclusion.
8. Prepare findings, evidence, limitations, confidence, open questions and proposed next steps for human review; do not send, publish, approve, sign, pay, file or modify records.
