roles: [sales]

output: draft
taxonomy: {gtm_account_management: proficient, gtm_enterprise_sales: working}

1. Read the account's usage and revenue (`grow.read`) and the people the team knows there, by name (`network.read`).
2. Write the plan: where they use the product, where they could, who decides, and the open requests.
3. List three next steps, each with an owner on the team.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a contract date no record gives.
5. Propose the plan to the sales lead.
