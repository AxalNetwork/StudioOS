name: prd-first-draft
description: Writes a first product requirements draft from a roadmap item, its research notes and the problems it answers.
roles: [cpo, cto]
tools: [build.read, validate.read]
output: draft
taxonomy: {product_requirements: proficient, product_ux_strategy: working}
version: 1
---
1. Read the roadmap item and its notes (`build.read`) and the validation problems it answers (`validate.read`).
2. Draft the requirements: the problem, who it is for, what success looks like, what is out of scope, and open questions.
3. Tie each requirement to the problem or note it comes from.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a success measure no record gives.
5. Propose the draft to the product lead and the engineering lead.
