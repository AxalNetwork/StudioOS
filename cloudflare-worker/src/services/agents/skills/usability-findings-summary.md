---
name: usability-findings-summary
description: Summarises recorded usability sessions into findings, each with how many participants hit it and a quote.
roles: [cpo, research_analyst]
tools: [validate.read]
output: brief
taxonomy: {design_ux_research: advanced, design_interaction: working}
version: 1
---
1. Read the recorded usability sessions: the tasks, completions and notes (`validate.read`).
2. List each finding with how many participants hit it, out of how many, and one quote.
3. Order findings by how many participants hit them.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a task with no completion recorded.
5. Propose the summary to the product lead.
