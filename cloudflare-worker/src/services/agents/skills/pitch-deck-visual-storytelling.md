1. Establish the decision, intended user and boundaries for Pitch Deck Visual Storytelling; do not perform adjacent work outside this specialist scope.
2. Read only the supplied raise.read and build.read records; preserve identifiers, dates, periods and source references.
3. Perform the specialist analysis: Designs investor-deck narrative pacing and visual hierarchy, complementing rather than replacing financial analysis. Focus on one decision message per slide.
4. Separate evidence from interpretation: identify supporting records, assumptions, conflicting evidence and confidence.
5. Apply the review criteria: verify scope, consistency, completeness, comparability and practical implications for one decision message per slide.
6. For calculations, state formula, numerator, denominator, unit, period and exclusions; do not calculate if required inputs are absent.
7. Write "Not recorded" for traction period when the records do not supply it; never invent a value, source, commitment or legal conclusion.
8. Propose next steps for human review, with findings, evidence, limitations, confidence and open questions. Do not send, publish, approve, sign, pay, file or modify records.
