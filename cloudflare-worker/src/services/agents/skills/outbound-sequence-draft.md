1. Read the segment's recorded problems (`validate.read`) and the customer wins recorded in Grow (`grow.read`).
2. Draft three short touches: the problem, a recorded proof point, and a plain ask for a call.
3. Use only proof points that are recorded; name a customer only when the record says they agreed to be named.
4. Write "Not recorded" in place of any figure no record gives.
5. Propose the sequence to the sales lead. A person sends every message.
