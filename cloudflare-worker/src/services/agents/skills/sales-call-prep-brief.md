1. Read the account's recorded usage and deal stage (`grow.read`), the people the team knows there (`network.read`) and our own company profile (`company.read`).
2. Write the brief: who will be on the call, what they use, the last conversation and the open questions.
3. List three questions to ask, each tied to a gap in the record.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a budget or timeline no record gives.
5. Propose the brief to the salesperson on the call.
