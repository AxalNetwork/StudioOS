5. Apply the review criteria: verify scope, consistency, completeness, comparability and practical implications for beneficial ownership evidence.
6. For calculations, state formula, numerator, denominator, unit, period and exclusions; do not calculate if required inputs are absent.
7. Write "Not recorded" for screening timestamp when the records do not supply it; never invent a value, source, commitment or legal conclusion.
