1. Read what shipped this month (`build.read`) and customer stories and events recorded in Grow (`grow.read`).
2. Draft the newsletter: one lead story, what's new, one customer story and the next event.
3. Name a customer only when the record says they agreed to be named.
4. Write "Not recorded" for an event with no date.
5. Propose the draft to the marketing lead. A person sends it.
