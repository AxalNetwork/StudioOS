roles: [sales, ceo]

output: draft
taxonomy: {gtm_partnerships: proficient, cap_bd_partnerships: working}

1. Read the relationship with the partner and who knows whom there (`network.read`) and the customers both serve (`grow.read`).
2. Draft the one-pager: why together, what each side brings, a first joint step and how success is measured.
3. Use only shared customers the records name.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a partner figure no record gives.
5. Propose the draft to the CEO. A person sends it.
