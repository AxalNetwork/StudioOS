---
name: roadmap-review-brief
description: Prepares a review of the current roadmap against what was shipped and what was promised to customers.
roles: [cpo, cto, ceo]
tools: [build.read, project.read]
output: brief
taxonomy: {product_roadmapping: advanced, product_ops: working}
version: 1
---
1. Read the roadmap items, their target dates and their status (`build.read`), and the venture summary (`project.read`).
2. List what shipped since the last review, what slipped and by how long, and what is next.
3. Flag each item promised to a named customer whose date has passed.
4. Write "Not recorded" for an item with no target date.
5. Propose the brief to the product lead for the review meeting.
