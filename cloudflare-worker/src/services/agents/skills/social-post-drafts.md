1. Read the launches recorded in Build (`build.read`) and articles in the research library (`research.read`).
2. Draft five posts, each with one idea, a plain call to action and a source.
3. Keep each under 280 characters and use no figure the records do not give; write "Not recorded" in its place.
4. Suggest an order for the week.
5. Propose the drafts to the marketing lead. A person schedules and posts them.
