---
name: seo-keyword-brief
description: Builds a keyword brief from the recorded search terms, pages and competitors, grouped by the intent behind them.
roles: [cmo]
tools: [grow.read, research.read]
output: brief
taxonomy: {mkt_seo: proficient, mkt_analytics: working}
version: 1
---
1. Read the search terms and landing pages recorded in Grow (`grow.read`) and competitor notes (`research.read`).
2. Group the terms by intent: learn, compare, buy.
3. For each group, name the page that answers it, or say no page does.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a term with no recorded volume.
5. Propose the brief to the marketing lead.
