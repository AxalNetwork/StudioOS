roles: [ceo, sales, research_analyst]
tools: [research.read, validate.read, compliance.read]
output: brief
taxonomy: {gtm_market_entry: advanced, legal_regulatory: aware}

---
1. Read the research on the target market (`research.read`), demand signals recorded in validation (`validate.read`) and the compliance requirements recorded (`compliance.read`).
2. Write what is known about the market, the evidence of demand, the requirements to meet and the open questions.
3. Cite each line to its source by title.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a market size or requirement no record gives.
5. Propose the brief to the CEO. It decides nothing about entering.
