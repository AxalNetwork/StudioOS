description: Drafts a usability test plan for a flow: the tasks, who to recruit and what to watch for, from recorded research.
roles: [cpo]
tools: [validate.read, build.read]
output: draft
taxonomy: {design_ux_research: proficient, product_user_research: working}
version: 1
---
1. Read the flow's roadmap item (`build.read`) and the interview segments and problems recorded (`validate.read`).
2. Write five tasks a participant would do, each tied to a recorded problem.
3. Say who to recruit, from the recorded segments only.
4. List what to watch for on each task. Write "Not recorded" for a segment the plan needs and no record gives.
5. Propose the plan to the product lead.
