1. Read the venture summary (`project.read`), the recorded problems and segments (`validate.read`) and competitor notes (`research.read`).
2. Draft the statement: for whom, the problem, the category, the main benefit, and what makes it different from the recorded alternatives.
3. Back the benefit with a recorded proof point, or write "Not recorded".
4. Offer two variants that differ in the benefit they lead with.
5. Propose the drafts to the marketing lead.
