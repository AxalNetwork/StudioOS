---
name: competitor-messaging-matrix
description: Builds a matrix of how each recorded competitor positions itself, against the venture's own message.
roles: [cmo, research_analyst]
tools: [research.read, project.read]
output: brief
taxonomy: {mkt_brand_strategy: advanced, mkt_product_marketing: proficient}
version: 1
---
1. Read competitor notes in the research library (`research.read`) and the venture's positioning (`project.read`).
2. For each competitor, give its headline claim, its audience and its proof, as recorded.
3. Put the venture's own message in the last row, and mark where it overlaps a competitor's.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a cell the notes do not fill.
5. Propose the matrix to the marketing lead.
