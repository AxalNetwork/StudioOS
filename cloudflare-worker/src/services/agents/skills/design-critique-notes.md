---
name: design-critique-notes
description: Writes structured critique notes on a design recorded in Build, against its stated goal and the problems it answers.
roles: [cpo]
tools: [build.read, validate.read]
output: brief
taxonomy: {design_product: advanced, design_visual: working}
version: 1
---
1. Read the design's goal and notes (`build.read`) and the problems it answers (`validate.read`).
2. Write what works, what does not and what to try, each tied to the goal or a problem.
3. Separate questions of taste from questions the research answers.
4. Write "Not recorded" for a goal the design does not state.
5. Propose the notes to the product lead. It approves no design.
