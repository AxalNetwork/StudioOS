# Advisor Program Terms

These Advisor Program Terms (the "Terms") govern participation as an
advisor on the platform operated by **Axal VC Management LLC**, 16192
Coastal Hwy, Lewes, DE 19958, United States (the "Company"). They
supplement the Terms of Service; on conflict regarding the advisor
program, these Terms control.

## 1. The Program

The program lets qualified members ("Advisors") maintain a public
advisor profile, publish bookable office-hours sessions, set session
types and pricing, and provide advisory services to founders and
companies on the platform ("Clients").

## 2. Independent Status

Advisors are independent members of the network — not employees,
agents, partners, or representatives of the Company. Advisors control
the substance of their advice and their availability. The Company does
not supervise, direct, or vouch for any advice, and nothing an Advisor
provides through the platform is advice of the Company.

## 3. Sessions, Pricing, and Payment

3.1 Advisors set their own session types, durations, and prices.
Prices are displayed to Clients before booking.

3.2 Paid sessions are charged at booking through the platform's
payment processor. The Company deducts a platform service fee of
{{advisor_program.take_rate}} of the session price; the remainder
settles to the Advisor's connected payout account on the schedule
shown in the Advisor's earnings dashboard.

3.3 Advisors must maintain a payout account with the platform's
payment processor (including its identity checks) to receive funds.
The Company is not responsible for processor delays or account
limitations imposed by the processor.

3.4 **Cancellations.** Sessions canceled by the Client with less than
{{advisor_program.cancel_window}} notice may be charged per the
Advisor's stated cancellation policy; sessions canceled by the Advisor
are refunded in full. No-shows are treated per the displayed policy of
the session type.

3.5 **Off-platform circumvention.** For relationships originated
through the platform, taking sessions off-platform to avoid the
service fee during the term and for twelve (12) months after is a
material breach.

## 4. Engagements Beyond Sessions

Longer advisory engagements (retainers, equity-for-advice
arrangements) are between the Advisor and the Client. The platform's
template library (including the FAST-style Venture Share Agreement)
and e-signature tools may be used to document them. The Company is not
a party to those agreements.

## 5. Conduct and Compliance

Advisors shall: provide services professionally and within their
actual expertise; keep Client confidential information confidential;
disclose conflicts of interest to affected Clients; and not provide
services requiring a license they do not hold (including legal, tax,
audit, or investment-adviser services where licensure is required).
Advisors shall not solicit investments or transaction-based
compensation through the program.

## 6. Reviews and Profile

Completed sessions may be reviewed by both parties. Reviews must be
honest and comply with the Terms of Service. The Company may feature,
order, or moderate profiles and reviews to maintain marketplace
quality, and may suspend profiles for policy violations.

## 7. Taxes

Advisors are responsible for their own taxes. Where required, the
Company or its processor will collect tax information (e.g., Form
W-9/W-8) and issue information returns (e.g., Form 1099) for amounts
settled through the platform.

## 8. Term; Termination

Either party may end participation at any time; booked sessions must
be completed or refunded. Sections 3.5, 5, 7, and 9 survive.

## 9. General

The Terms of Service's disclaimers, limitations of liability,
governing-law, and dispute provisions apply to these Terms. The
Company may update these Terms on notice; continued participation
after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
