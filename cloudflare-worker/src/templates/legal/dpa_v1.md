# Data Processing Addendum

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of
Service or other written agreement (the "Agreement") between **Axal VC
Management LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its
registered address at 16192 Coastal Hwy, Lewes, DE 19958, United
States (the "Company"), and the counterparty accepting the Agreement
("Customer"), and applies to the extent the Company processes Personal
Data on Customer's behalf in providing the Service.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified
  or identifiable natural person processed by the Company on
  Customer's behalf under the Agreement.
- **"Data Protection Laws"** means all laws applicable to the
  processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, including, where
  applicable, the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR")
  and the California Consumer Privacy Act ("CCPA").
- **"Sub-processor"** means a third party engaged by the Company to
  process Personal Data on Customer's behalf.
- "Controller", "Processor", "Data Subject", "Processing", and
  "Supervisory Authority" have the meanings given in Data Protection
  Laws.

## 2. Roles and Scope

2.1 As between the parties, Customer is the Controller (or a Processor
acting for another Controller) and the Company is a Processor of
Personal Data submitted to the Service by or for Customer.

2.2 For data the Company collects for its own purposes (account
registration, billing, platform security, product analytics, and
network features described in the Privacy Policy), the Company acts as
an independent Controller, and that processing is governed by the
Privacy Policy rather than this DPA.

## 3. Processing Instructions

The Company shall process Personal Data only (a) to provide, secure,
and improve the Service; (b) as documented in the Agreement and this
DPA; and (c) per Customer's reasonable written instructions, unless
required otherwise by law (in which case the Company will notify
Customer unless legally prohibited).

## 4. Confidentiality

The Company ensures that personnel authorized to process Personal Data
are bound by written or statutory confidentiality obligations and
access Personal Data strictly on a need-to-know basis. Administrative
access is further governed by the Company's internal Data Access
Acknowledgment.

## 5. Security

The Company implements appropriate technical and organizational
measures, including: encryption of Personal Data in transit (TLS) and
at rest; identity-verified admin access with step-up authentication
for sensitive operations; private object storage with signed,
single-use download links for uploaded documents; audit logging of
signature and document events; and role-based access controls
throughout the Service.

## 6. Sub-processors

6.1 Customer generally authorizes the Sub-processors listed in the
Sub-processor Schedule below. The Company will provide notice of new
Sub-processors (via the Service or email) at least ten (10) days
before granting access to Personal Data; Customer may object on
reasonable data-protection grounds, in which case the parties will
work in good faith toward a resolution.

6.2 The Company remains responsible for its Sub-processors'
performance to the same standard this DPA requires of the Company.

## 7. Data Subject Requests

Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Company will
assist Customer by appropriate technical and organizational measures
in responding to Data Subject requests (access, rectification,
erasure, restriction, portability, objection). If a Data Subject
contacts the Company directly regarding Customer's data, the Company
will promptly redirect the request to Customer.

## 8. Personal Data Breach

The Company will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming
aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer's Personal Data,
and will provide information reasonably required for Customer to meet
its own notification obligations, including the nature of the breach,
categories and approximate volumes affected, likely consequences, and
measures taken or proposed.

## 9. International Transfers

Where Personal Data protected by GDPR is transferred to a country
without an adequacy decision, the parties rely on the European
Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2:
Controller-to-Processor), which are incorporated by reference, with
the Company as data importer and Customer as data exporter, and the
technical measures in Section 5 as Annex II.

## 10. Audit and Deletion

10.1 On reasonable written request (no more than once per year absent
a Supervisory Authority requirement or a Personal Data Breach), the
Company will make available information reasonably necessary to
demonstrate compliance with this DPA.

10.2 Upon termination of the Agreement, the Company will delete or
return Customer's Personal Data within ninety (90) days, except where
retention is required by law or the data resides in encrypted backups
scheduled for routine destruction.

## 11. Order of Precedence; Liability

In case of conflict, this DPA prevails over the Agreement with respect
to Personal Data processing. Each party's liability under this DPA is
subject to the limitations of liability in the Agreement.
