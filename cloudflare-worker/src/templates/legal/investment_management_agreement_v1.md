# Investment Management Agreement

This Investment Management Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered
into as of {{agreement.effective_date}} by and between:

- **{{fund.legal_name}}**, a Delaware limited partnership (the
  "Fund"), acting through its general partner, **Axal VC GP LLC**, a
  Delaware limited liability company (the "General Partner"); and
- **Axal VC Management LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company
  with its registered address at 16192 Coastal Hwy, Lewes, DE 19958,
  United States (the "Manager").

## 1. Appointment

The Fund appoints the Manager, and the Manager accepts appointment, as
investment manager of the Fund, with authority delegated by the
General Partner to manage the Fund's investment program subject to the
Fund's limited partnership agreement (the "LPA"), its investment
strategy, and this Agreement. Where this Agreement conflicts with the
LPA, the LPA controls.

## 2. Services

The Manager shall provide: sourcing and evaluation of investment
opportunities; due diligence; negotiation and execution support for
investments and dispositions (subject to General Partner approval
where the LPA requires); portfolio monitoring and reporting;
valuation support; investor reporting assistance; and day-to-day
administration of the investment program, in each case with the care a
prudent professional manager would exercise.

## 3. Authority; Limits

3.1 The Manager acts under the supervision of the General Partner. The
General Partner retains authority over: admission of limited partners;
capital calls and distributions; investment-committee approvals as set
out in the Fund's IC charter; and any matter the LPA reserves to the
General Partner.

3.2 The Manager has no authority to bind the Fund except as expressly
delegated in writing.

## 4. Compensation

4.1 **Management fee.** The Fund shall pay the Manager the management
fee set out in the LPA ({{fund.management_fee}} per annum of
{{fund.fee_base}}, subject to the step-downs and offsets stated
there), payable {{fund.fee_frequency}}.

4.2 **Carried interest** is allocated to the General Partner under the
LPA and its carried-interest allocation agreement, not under this
Agreement.

4.3 **Expenses.** The Manager bears its own overhead (salaries, rent,
general operations). The Fund bears fund expenses as defined in the
LPA (organizational costs, transaction costs, audit, legal, and
similar).

## 5. Exclusivity; Other Activities

The Manager may provide services to other clients, funds, and the
Axal platform, provided it allocates opportunities in good faith
consistent with the LPA's allocation provisions and discloses material
conflicts to the General Partner.

## 6. Standard of Care; Indemnification

The Manager shall not be liable to the Fund except for conduct
constituting fraud, willful misconduct, gross negligence, or material
breach of this Agreement. The Fund shall indemnify the Manager and its
members, officers, and agents to the extent, and subject to the
limitations, set out in the LPA.

## 7. Books, Records, and Confidentiality

The Manager shall keep proper records of its services, available to
the General Partner and the Fund's auditors on reasonable notice, and
shall hold Fund and portfolio-company information in confidence,
subject to disclosures required by law or permitted by the LPA.

## 8. Regulatory Status

The Manager represents that it either (a) qualifies for an exemption
from registration under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 (including
the venture-capital-fund adviser exemption) and will make any required
exempt-reporting-adviser filings, or (b) is duly registered where
required. The Manager will notify the General Partner of any change in
its regulatory status.

## 9. Term and Termination

This Agreement continues until the Fund's dissolution unless
terminated earlier: (a) by either party on ninety (90) days' written
notice; (b) immediately by the General Partner for the Manager's
fraud, willful misconduct, or gross negligence; or (c) automatically
if the LPA so requires upon a removal event. Accrued fees survive
termination.

## 10. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware,
without regard to conflicts principles. It may be executed
electronically and in counterparts. Amendments must be in writing
signed by both parties and, where the LPA requires, approved as the
LPA provides.
