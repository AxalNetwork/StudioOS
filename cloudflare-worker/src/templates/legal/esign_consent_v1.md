# Consent to Electronic Signatures and Records

This disclosure is provided by **Axal VC Management LLC**, 16192
Coastal Hwy, Lewes, DE 19958, United States (the "Company"), in
accordance with the U.S. Electronic Signatures in Global and National
Commerce Act ("ESIGN") and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act
("UETA").

## 1. Your Consent

By checking the consent box and proceeding, you agree that:

- Your electronic signature — including a drawn signature, a typed
  name, or a click adopting a signature — is the legal equivalent of
  your handwritten signature, and you intend to be bound by documents
  you sign electronically on the Service;
- All agreements, disclosures, notices, and other records relating to
  documents you sign on the Service may be provided to you
  electronically, by display in the Service or delivery to the email
  address associated with the signing request; and
- An electronic record of each signed document, including its audit
  trail (signer identity, timestamps, and events), will be retained by
  the Company and available to the parties to the document.

## 2. Hardware and Software Requirements

To sign and retain records electronically you need: a device with a
current web browser supporting TLS; a valid email address; and the
ability to view and store PDF files. By consenting, you confirm you
can access documents in these formats.

## 3. Paper Copies

You may request a paper copy of any document you signed electronically
by writing to legal@axal.vc or to the Company's address above. A
reasonable reproduction fee may apply. Signed documents remain
downloadable from the Service while your account is active.

## 4. Withdrawing Consent

You may withdraw your consent to receive records electronically for
FUTURE documents by written notice to legal@axal.vc. Withdrawal does
not affect the validity of documents signed before it takes effect,
and may mean the Company cannot offer you products whose workflows
require electronic execution.

## 5. Keeping Your Email Current

You are responsible for maintaining a current email address with the
Company. Signing requests and executed copies are sent to the address
provided by the requesting party or associated with your account.

## 6. Validity

You agree not to contest the validity or enforceability of a document
solely because it was signed or delivered electronically.
