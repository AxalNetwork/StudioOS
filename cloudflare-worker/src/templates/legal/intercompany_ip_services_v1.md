# Intercompany IP License and Services Agreement

This Intercompany IP License and Services Agreement (this "Agreement")
is entered into as of {{agreement.effective_date}} by and between:

- **Axal VC Holdings LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company
  ("Holdings"); and
- **Axal VC Management LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company
  ("Management"),

each with a registered address at 16192 Coastal Hwy, Lewes, DE 19958,
United States.

## 1. Background

Holdings owns or shall own the intellectual property comprising the
Axal platform — including the StudioOS software, source code, product
designs, the "Axal", "Axal VC", and "StudioOS" names and marks, domain
names, content, and associated goodwill (collectively, the "Licensed
IP"). Management operates the platform and contracts with its members,
customers, and partners. The parties wish to record the ownership,
license, and operating arrangement between them.

## 2. Assignment of Existing IP

To the extent Management (or Joseph Gabriel Guillaume Lauzier acting
for the enterprise) holds title to any Licensed IP as of the effective
date, that title is hereby assigned to Holdings, and each party shall
execute any documents reasonably needed to perfect the assignment.

## 3. License

3.1 Holdings grants Management an exclusive, worldwide, royalty-
bearing license to use, host, operate, modify, and commercialize the
Licensed IP to run the Axal platform and related services, including
the right to sublicense to hosting providers and to end users through
the platform's terms of service.

3.2 **Improvements.** All improvements, modifications, and derivative
works of the Licensed IP created by or for Management are assigned to,
and owned by, Holdings upon creation, and are included in the license
above.

3.3 **Quality control.** Management shall use the marks in a manner
consistent with the quality standards Holdings reasonably prescribes,
and shall not challenge Holdings' ownership of the Licensed IP.

## 4. License Fee

Management shall pay Holdings a license fee of
{{intercompany.license_fee}} (e.g., a percentage of Management's net
platform revenue, settled {{intercompany.fee_frequency}}), as the
parties may adjust in writing on arm's-length terms consistent with
applicable transfer-pricing principles.

## 5. Services

5.1 Management shall provide, at its cost, the operation of the
platform: engineering, hosting administration, support, billing,
compliance operations, and day-to-day management of customer and
partner relationships.

5.2 Holdings' role is limited to ownership and strategic oversight of
the Licensed IP; it conducts no customer-facing operations and is not
a party to Management's customer or partner contracts.

## 6. Liability Separation

Each party shall maintain its own books, accounts, and observe LLC
formalities. Nothing in this Agreement makes either party the agent of
the other, and neither party assumes the debts or obligations of the
other. Management shall indemnify Holdings against claims arising out
of the operation of the platform; Holdings shall indemnify Management
against claims that the Licensed IP, as delivered by Holdings,
infringes third-party rights.

## 7. Term and Termination

This Agreement continues until terminated by mutual written consent or
by either party on one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice.
Sections 2, 3.2, and 6 survive termination. On termination, Management
shall cease use of the Licensed IP except as needed for an orderly
wind-down not exceeding ninety (90) days.

## 8. General

Delaware law governs. This Agreement may be executed electronically
and in counterparts, and together with its schedules is the entire
agreement of the parties on its subject.
