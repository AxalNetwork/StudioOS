TokenLightDark

Two rules an implementer must not flatten. The accent moves indigo-600 → indigo-400, because at low luminance indigo-600 and the Founder product's violet-600 converge and the licences stop reading apart. And the seam's cyan-600 → teal-300 on a desaturated ground, because cyan darkens to mud against near-black — the one hue here that cannot be lightness-flipped. Contrast floor stays 4.5:1 under 18.66px, which is why muted is #615c6e on light and #a6a2b8 on dark, not a fainter grey.
