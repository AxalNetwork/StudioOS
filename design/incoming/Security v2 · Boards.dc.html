Security Command Center

The console isn’t simplified by removing evidence. It separates triage, investigation, action and explanation into seven workspaces: Overview, Events, Access, Privacy, Trust, AI Safety and Resilience. Overview leads with Action required (the failed restore drill and 0 of 2 admins with MFA), then status by area — with no synthetic score. Events uses human labels, keeps the raw IDs in a drawer, and lets you load older rows. Every destructive action goes through Scope → Review → Result, names its exact scope (HQ-held accounts, never “all tenants”), says whether you are affected and shows the server’s per-item outcome. A shared 13-state vocabulary replaces “Not recorded”. Eadwyn opens on security prompts, and model and pricing details fold away.
