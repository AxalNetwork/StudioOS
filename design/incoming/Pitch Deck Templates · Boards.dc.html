Pitch deck templates

All 24 templates, six per stage: Pre-seed TPL-01 to 06, Seed 07 to 12, Series A 13 to 18 and Series B+ 19 to 24. Each board shows the title card large, the template’s swatch, type, decor, chrome, art, tone and what sets it apart, then every card in order on the 12 × 6 grid. Slides show placeholders only: every figure reads “Not recorded” until it is bound to a record. The Pre-seed and Seed templates (TPL-01 to 12) are written for AI-native companies: their placeholders ask for the model’s task, the person in the loop, what is learned from corrections, gross margin after compute, and where the moat is.
