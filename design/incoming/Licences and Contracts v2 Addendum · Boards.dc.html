This addendum extends the landed Licences v2 (L00–L22, D4819) and Contracts v2 (CT-01–CT-16, D4811) canvases; it does not fork them. Same shell, tokens, chips, tables and section tabs. It adds country pricing (#5020), Axal VC packages (#5026), agreement readiness and Program access (#5051), user licences (#5060) and the Agreements tab’s licence context (#5050 §5). Fields that no Worker read returns yet carry a dashed “pending read” tag and are listed on L40. Nothing reprices, renews, grants or sends itself: prepare, review and apply or send are separate steps a person takes, each with a recorded reason. One agreement is one record, opened from Licences and from Contracts. Not recorded and Unavailable are never drawn as 0. The Eadwyn rail (#5130) is not redrawn.
