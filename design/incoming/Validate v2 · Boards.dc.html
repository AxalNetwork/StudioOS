Validate — what did we learn, what is uncertain, what needs review, what next

Validate is organised around learning, not modules: Talk → Learn → Test → Decide. The overview shows the objective, exactly one next action with its reason, the evidence counted with what each number means, and a short Needs attention queue. Then active hypotheses and recent meaningful changes, with the modules as one tab row on desktop and one selector on smaller screens. Supports, Contradicts and Unknown are words with glyphs, never colour alone. Relationships are never confused with sources. Planned experiments are not evidence, and a done experiment without a result reads Result needed. Eadwyn proposes and the founder accepts, edits or discards, with the original evidence always visible. The decision is the founder’s: three equal choices, none pre-selected, with the evidence it rests on. The Eadwyn rail keeps Ask, suggestions to review and the AI toggle; models and money sit behind AI settings.
