Axal VC · Network Introductions — any member asks Axal to introduce them to a person, who may be outside Axal, and an operator owns the handoff

A general introduction service at /network/introductions for founders, investors and advisors alike. The requester names a person (found in the directory, entered by hand as a provisional target, or still to be identified at a known organization), says why, picks the capacity they ask in, previews what Axal will share and submits. Submitting asks Axal to coordinate; it sends nothing to the target. An authorized Axal operator takes the case from a queue, confirms the target, checks the requester’s readiness summary, the contact channel, its freshness and any suppression, and contacts the target only through a separate review-and-contact step. Request lifecycle, target response, contact attempt and introduction are four fields with four events. These boards continue the landed desk, curated match, quarterly ask and teammate request canvases (D7063–D7066) and the Network v2 investor addendum (#6256): the shell, header, four links, request sheet, record Sheet, state words and the one Eadwyn rail (#5130). Team requests, curated matches, quarterly asks and Offer one keep their names and permissions. Keys carry the NIE prefix.
