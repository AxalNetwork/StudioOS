Delivery — one dependable place to run active client work

/delivery lands on Board, the command centre. Rows lead with client, then next action and why, then health, progress, owner, mode and value. Health is On track, At risk, Blocked or Not rated, and Not rated is never green. One engagement sheet travels across all five zones. The no-engagement workspace is one upstream-aware state (Pipeline → Proposal → Engagement → Delivery) instead of five zero cards. Deliverables never claim a client opened anything: read status is unavailable. Capacity makes its time basis explicit and treats an embedded seat as a commitment, not just workload. Status reports are anchored to client and period, with sent reports as read-only records. Health explains every status and separates what you set from what is calculated. Eadwyn stays collapsed and secondary.
