New licence — the five steps around L21: legal entity, commercial terms, administrators, agreement and the issue review

This addendum continues Licences v2 (L00–L22, D4819) and its Licences and Contracts addendum (L23–L40), numbering on from L41; it does not fork them. It uses L21’s step frame exactly: “Issue a licence”, “Step n of 6 · ”, the 240px step list and one step card with Back and Continue at the bottom right. Step 1 creates the licence as a Draft in the ledger; every later step saves to that draft; only Issue makes it Active. Nothing issues, sends or grants by itself: money terms, the brand kit and appointing an administrator each ask for an authenticator code first, and a pending signature never blocks issuing. Every value has a Worker route on main, so there are no ◇ backend tags; amber ⚑ tags mark the four owner questions, drawn as option A. None, Not recorded and Unavailable are never drawn as 0. The Eadwyn rail (#5130) is the shared rail: it proposes, and fills or saves nothing.
