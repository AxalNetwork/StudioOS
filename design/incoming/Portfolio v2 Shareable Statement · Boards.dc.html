Portfolio statement — one PDF of the book as of a quarter-end, that an investor downloads and hands on

Continues the Portfolio v2 canvas (PF-01 to PF-12, D4817) with one board, PF-13, frames PF13a to PF13i: same PortfolioFrame (Account › Portfolio, Overview · Positions · Reports · Performance · Value-add, the rail slot), FinancialMetric and BasisStrip parts, type and colours. A Statement action on Overview and Performance opens a sheet: choose a completed quarter-end, check the preview, download. The PDF is US Letter portrait in the layout of the fund LP report (quarterlyReportPdf.js), never its net figures: Summary, Positions, NAV at quarter-ends, Cohorts, Methodology. Every figure says what it counts and for which date, and every page says Gross of fees and carry; unmarked positions carry at cost and say so. IRR is a rate, −100% Total loss, or Unavailable with its reason, never 0%. Zero, Not recorded and Unreadable never look alike, and a read that fails produces no statement, except one quarter-end point, drawn as a Not read gap. Pills carry a word and a glyph so the PDF reads in greyscale. Eadwyn only proposes a covering note; nothing is added, sent or kept by itself. The #3704 shell stays. Violet dashed ◇ tags mark backend work (positions cut at the date, net basis); amber ⚑ tags mark the four owner picks, drawn as option A. Tags are annotations, not product copy.
