Platform States — one vocabulary every redesigned surface cites

How the platform shows data that is present, absent, failed, refused, partial or old. Six value states (Value, None, Not recorded, Not applicable, Unavailable, Refused), three modifiers (Stale, Partial, Provisional) and Loading as a transient skeleton. Missing data reads “Not recorded”, never 0; a failed read is never drawn as zero, as an empty list or as Not recorded; nothing is made up to fill a gap. Built on the v2 chip shape and tokens, mapped to the Worker’s OperationalRead contract (D4858), and drawn as a redraw of ui/Honesty.jsx. Drawn to pick A on Question 2: the screen says “Unavailable” for a failed read (ST-11 shows B and C).
