Axal VC · My learning — History, Plan and Programs as one personal workspace

My learning (/learning/me, /learning/plan, proposed /learning/my-programs, and /learning/programs for admins) for a signed-in member, reached from the Account menu inside the Axal VC shell. History shows one purposeful first-use card with a direct way to choose a course when every read succeeds and holds nothing; a failed read is shown as Unavailable beside what was read, never as inactivity. A returning learner sees the next lesson, scheduled study, submitted work and results only where the record has them. Plan rows say where each item comes from (a course, your own study block, a webinar, an exam) and what to do next; a course is chosen and scheduled without leaving the plan, and the standalone study-block dialog keeps its labels and returns focus on Escape. Programs keeps membership (added by HQ, or you joined) apart from self-enrolment (open to join), and HQ contact stays where the role needs it. States follow the Platform States vocabulary (Unavailable on screen). Violet dashed ◇ tags mark data or backend work; amber ⚑ tags mark owner choices drawn as option A.
