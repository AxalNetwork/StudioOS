Axal VC · Deals — private preparation for company, SPV and fund deals, reviewed versions, explicit publication

Deals (/admin/deals): a workspace where an authorized administrator, or a GP, scout, broker or delegated Fund admin within their own scope, prepares a deal privately. Every deal names a real issuing company and one of its projects; a project without a company keeps research only. Creation starts from the transaction: direct company financing, SPV raise, fund raise or fund deployment; debt and secondaries stay unavailable behind their gates. The administrator saves and resumes it, assembles evidence and terms, gets an exact version reviewed, previews the permitted investor view and explicitly publishes it. Investors keep /deals and /deals/:dealId. Working draft, release and round stage are three separate indicators, and the version being edited, reviewed or previewed is always labelled. States follow the Platform States vocabulary (Unavailable on screen). Violet dashed ◇ tags mark what depends on ADP-01 to ADP-04; amber ⚑ tags mark proposed policy pending owner sign-off. New deals are USD only.
