Bank accounts

HQ’s registry of the four entities’ bank accounts, joined to each entity’s read-only Mercury. Every notice and invoice prints its payment instructions from here, or refuses and says why. Nothing on these boards moves money. Account numbers show as their last four; a full number appears only through Reveal, for 30 seconds. Balances are as of 21 Oct 2026, 08:00. The account drawer is drawn as a centred lightbox.
