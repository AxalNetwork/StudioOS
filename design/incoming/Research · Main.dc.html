What the six dimensions read

Each index blends several public sources. These are the records behind each one.

Demand

Search interest, app-store activity, product launches and community discussion.

Supply

New apps and products, open-source activity, patents and company filings.

Capital

Funding filings and public finance series.

Talent

Labour statistics and developer questions.

Research

Papers, citations, code releases and patents.

Sentiment

News tone and community discussion.

Sources per dimension come from the market intelligence registry; a sector page lists them by name.
