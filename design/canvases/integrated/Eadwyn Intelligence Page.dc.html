03 · Language

It answers in the way you speak.

When you talk with Eadwyn, it finds the formulation best adapted to your language: the language you write in, the terms your profile uses, and the level of detail you ask for. Voice notes are transcribed, and replies can be read back aloud.

Peux-tu m'expliquer mon score de préparation ?
Votre score s'appuie sur trois éléments : les entretiens clients, la table de capitalisation et le deck. Le point le plus faible est le deck.
Summarise it for my co-founder in English.
04 · Persona

It learns who you are from the work, not a form.

Eadwyn builds your persona from the conversations you have with it and the work you do in the platform. It is the canonical profile the other tools read: matching, scoring, and the order of your backlog. Each part shows how sure it is, and what would make it surer.

06 · Architecture

An open, federated model, grown by everyone who contributes.

Eadwyn is built as a federated model. Nodes learn locally and keep their data where it lives; only model updates travel. Updates are merged, reviewed, and published as a versioned checkpoint, which serves the answers you see.
