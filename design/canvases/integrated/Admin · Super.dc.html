H31Licence record — the domain strip, read-only (every tenant)status and one emergency action · HQ does not configure a hostname
A correction to the earlier draft: domain configuration is not an HQ feature. Super Admin stays on axal.vc and app.axal.vc, and binds nothing else. What HQ keeps is a read-only strip on each licence — Axal subsidiary and white-label alike, because both may bind a host of their own — plus one emergency action, Detach, for a collision, abuse, or DNS that was abandoned.
