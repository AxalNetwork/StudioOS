The firm

We are early because that is where the outcome is decided.

Axal VC is a Geneva-based venture firm investing at pre-seed in technology companies with durable, systemic problems to solve. We built an operating platform because writing checks earlier only works if you can see more.
Mission
Make the earliest stage of company-building legible — to founders first, and to capital second.
Founders are asked to make irreversible decisions — equity splits, entity structure, IP assignment, pricing — usually before anyone has told them what good looks like. Most of the damage in a company's first year is structural, not strategic.
We put those decisions inside a system with defaults, evidence requirements, and expert review. The founder keeps every decision. They just stop making them blind.

Axal VC

Build with clarity. Move with evidence.

We do not believe speed and rigor are opposites. Speed matters because uncertainty compounds. Rigor matters because capital, attention, and trust are limited. The answer is not to slow down until everything is known. It is to move quickly toward the information that changes what should happen next.
