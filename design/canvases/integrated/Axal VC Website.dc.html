Founder overview
The firm

We are early because that is where the outcome is decided.

Axal VC is a Geneva-based venture firm investing at pre-seed in technology companies with durable, systemic problems to solve. We built an operating platform because writing checks earlier only works if you can see more.
Mission
Make the earliest stage of company-building legible — to founders first, and to capital second.
Founders are asked to make irreversible decisions — equity splits, entity structure, IP assignment, pricing — usually before anyone has told them what good looks like. Most of the damage in a company's first year is structural, not strategic.
We put those decisions inside a system with defaults, evidence requirements, and expert review. The founder keeps every decision. They just stop making them blind.

Why this exists

Building alone is not the hard part. Building without a reference is.

You can now generate a product, a landing page, and a pitch deck in a weekend. What you cannot generate is knowing whether any of it is worth building — or whether the equity split you just agreed to will survive a term sheet.
AI collapsed the cost of producing artifacts. It did not collapse the cost of being wrong. Founders now move faster in directions nobody has pressure-tested, and the gap between "shipped something" and "built a company" has never been wider.
The Lab supplies the reference: what good discovery looks like, what a defensible price is, what a clean cap table is, what an investor will actually ask — enforced week by week, with people who have done it reviewing your work.
The 28 days

Four weeks. Each one gated on evidence, not attendance.

You do not advance because a week ended. You advance because the deliverables exist and hold up to review.
