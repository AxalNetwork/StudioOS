How the twenty-eight days run
Four weeks, each ending at a gate that opens on evidence rather than attendance. Every track passes the same four gates; what fills each week comes from the track — a Form founder's formation week is a Find-fit founder's interview week. Companies advance only on completion. Cohorts are calendar months, numbered from May 2026; applications close seven days before the cohort starts, at 23:59 Delaware time, computed rather than fixed.
