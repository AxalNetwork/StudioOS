#12 - Invitation & lane continuity through signup
What & Why
Per the signup friction audit (PR #135), the invitation's promise breaks after signup: ?next= return paths are honored by /login but ignored by /register (AcceptInvitePage sends new users to /register?next=… and it's dropped) and lost by the Google-signup callback, so invited users finish the gauntlet and land in a generic AI interview instead of the thing they were invited to. The ?lane= intent (e.g. "Open LP Account") is stored client-side only and never sent to the server — everyone is created as partner and the 14-day investor trial wired to role === 'investor' is unreachable from the public funnel, even though the register endpoint already accepts role. The forced full-screen onboarding chat then holds users hostage with no skip. This is audit fixes 4, 8, 9 plus the partner-wizard name prefill.
Done looks like
* A user who clicks a project/team invitation and registers lands on that invitation after signup — through the email-verification round-trip, the Google OAuth path, and the onboarding gate
* Registering via "Open LP Account" / ?lane=investor creates an investor account and activates the investor trial; other lanes map to their roles
* The onboarding chat has a visible "Skip for now" that drops the user on the dashboard (partial answers persisted; a later nag is fine); invited users are routed to their next target before the chat gate
* The partner wizard no longer asks for the full legal name the admin already entered — it prefills and asks to confirm
* Invitation emails that land on /register prefill the email field
Out of scope
* Magic-link UI and TOTP policy (previous tasks — but the next threading must survive whatever entry flow those tasks shipped)
* Redesigning the onboarding chat content or the admin-review process
* New invitation types
Steps
1. Thread next through register — Read and validate the return path on register, persist it across the email round-trip alongside the existing intent storage, and redirect to it once the user is signed in (before the onboarding gate)
2. Google path continuity — Carry the redirect/invite context through the Google signup callback instead of hard-routing new signups to the chat
3. Wire lane→role — Send the role derived from the lane in the register payload so investor trials and role routing engage; keep server-side validation authoritative
4. Skippable onboarding — Add "Skip for now" to the onboarding chat gate, persist partial answers, and soften the "admin will review" copy per the audit
5. Partner wizard prefill — Prefill the legal-name question from the invite's recipient name with a confirm step; add ?email= prefill to invitation links that target register
