Objective
  Right rail of the advisor card shows three buckets with priority
  ordering. Drives the user's sense of "what should I do next."

Buckets (computed by the state machine in Prompt CB)
  Proposed   — top 5 unanswered questions for this user, ordered by
                priority. Empty progress bar (gray) per item.
  Pending    — questions partially answered (e.g. a textarea draft
                saved but not committed; or a multi-part question
                like "Add 3 OKRs" where 1/3 are done). Progress bar
                shows %. Sorted by completion % descending then priority.
  Completed  — last 10 answered questions, newest first. Filled bar
                (green).

UI (frontend/src/components/advisor/ProgressRail.jsx)
  Header: "YOUR PROGRESS"
  Three collapsible sections with counts. Sticky on scroll.
  Each item card: section icon, label, progress bar, importance pill,
  click → focuses the chat on that question.
  Hover shows: explainer text + doc-anchor "Learn more →".

  When a section maps to a page (e.g. "Projects · Customer Discovery"),
  show a small "Open page →" link beside the count.

  Empty states:
    Proposed empty: "You're caught up. New tasks show up here as you
    progress." with a small confetti SVG.
    Pending empty: "Nothing in progress."
    Completed empty: "Answers you provide will appear here."

Data source
  GET /api/advisor/queue (returns the three buckets, already
  priority-ordered).
  Polls on advisor turn commit, plus subscribes to a Durable Object
  channel `advisor-progress:{user_id}` for push updates when side-
  effects (milestone unlock, tier change) re-order the queue.

Spin-Out Lab variant
  When users.spinout_lab_active = 1, the rail header shows the week
  badge (e.g. "Week 2 of 4") and a per-week milestone strip above the
  three buckets. Each milestone card has its own progress bar and a
  "what's missing" tooltip.

Acceptance
  - Three buckets render with real counts; refresh-safe.
  - Clicking a Proposed item focuses the chat on that question.
  - Completing a question moves it to Completed and pushes the next
    Proposed item up; animation < 500ms.
  - Free-tier user with a Studio-only question pending sees it in
    Proposed with a small lock pill and "Upgrade to unlock" CTA.
