#18 - Support Consolidation & Account Trim
What & Why
Investor audit items ⑥ + ⑨ + ⑩ plus item ⑫'s mock-settlement label. The Support group is 8 pages where ~4 workspaces would do: the LP holdings table renders identically in Funds and Liquidity; TVPI/DPI is computed live in Funds but hand-authored in LP Reporting; capital calls appear in both Funds and Capital; Reserve Allocation and Exit Waterfall are two views of the same simulator engine and scenarios table. The Account group is an 11-item bucket with items that have no investor purpose, and the well-built tier system is experienced only as lock icons with invisible consumption. Consolidate Support into four coherent workspaces, trim Account, and make quota/paywalls legible.
Done looks like
* Support is four workspaces (sidebar rows stay, deep-linking to tabs): Portfolio (Health · Updates · Positions), Fund Ops (Funds admin · LP Reporting · capital calls), My LP Portal (canonical holdings · reports · calls), and Fund Modeling (Reserve Allocation + Exit Waterfall merged). Liquidity reads holdings from the canonical source; mock settlement is labeled "simulation".
* One canonical holdings table and one capital-call store back these views (no duplicate/divergent copies).
* The investor Account group is trimmed to account-relevant items (Advisors, Jobs, Articles, Partners removed from the investor nav).
* Locked items show a blurred preview instead of a lock icon, and a quota card appears beside every paywall/402.
Out of scope
* The home quota bars (deal-desk task).
Steps
1. Merge Fund Modeling first (Reserve Allocation + Exit Waterfall over the shared simulator/scenarios), then consolidate Portfolio (Health/Updates/Positions) and Fund Ops, and stand up the canonical holdings table + single capital-call store that Funds/Liquidity/LP portal all read.
2. Point Liquidity at the canonical holdings source and label mock settlement as simulation; assemble the My LP Portal view over the canonical holdings/reports/calls.
3. Trim the investor Account group in the sidebar config (remove non-investor items) and add locked-tab blurred previews plus a quota card beside each 402/paywall.
Constraints
* Depends on the diligence→commit→ledger task for the canonical position/capital record shape and the DD de-admin nav changes (both touch positions/capital and the sidebar config).
* Update BOTH changelogs for user-facing changes; pass npm run test:drift before merge.
