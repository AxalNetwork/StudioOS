#29 - Advisor Research Section
What & Why
Build the Advisor profile's Research section — an external-intelligence and knowledge workspace covering Market, Companies, Documents, AI Research, and News. Structurally this mirrors the already-built investor Research section, so the investor Research pages are the primary reference; the goal is an advisor-scoped version replacing the shell stubs.
Done looks like
* Market reads like an intelligence workspace: Industries, Sectors, Trends, Macro analysis, and Geography as tabs, with detail panels.
* Companies covers a Startup database, Unicorn database, Public companies, an Exit database, and Funding rounds, filterable and searchable, with a company detail panel.
* Documents is a searchable document library: Pitch decks, Investment memos, PDFs, Contracts, and Research papers, with search/filter and a detail panel.
* AI Research offers clearly-labelled placeholder AI surfaces: AI Search, AI Analyst, Comparable Companies, Market Maps, and Company Reports — every AI surface is visibly marked as sample output.
* News is a categorized feed: Industry, Portfolio, Competitor, Funding, Acquisitions, and IPO news with category filtering.
* Every tab, filter, and detail panel renders with mock data — no blank views and no console errors.
Out of scope
* Real backend/API wiring and live market/news/document feeds — mock data only.
* The App shell, sidebar, and routing (delivered by the Advisor shell foundation task).
* Network and Advisory sections (separate tasks).
Steps
1. Market — Build the Industries/Sectors/Trends/Macro/Geography tabs with detail panels.
2. Companies — Build the company/exit/funding-round databases with filters, search, and a detail panel.
3. Documents — Build the searchable document library with type/source filters and a detail panel.
4. AI Research — Build AI Search, AI Analyst, Comparables, Market Maps, and Company Reports as clearly-labelled sample surfaces.
5. News — Build the categorized news feed with category filtering.
6. Mock data — Seed a dedicated advisor Research data module with realistic, deterministic placeholder records, structured to be swapped for live data later. Reuse the investor Research data shape where it fits.
7. Verify — Click through every tab, filter, and detail panel across all five pages; confirm no blank states or console errors.
Architectural constraints
* Reuse the shared section kit (components/section) and the investor Research pages as structural references; keep advisor code under its own pages/advisor/research/ and data/advisor/ files.
* AI surfaces must be clearly labelled as sample/placeholder output.
* Deterministic mock data only: demo "today" is 2026-07-11; no Date.now, Math.random, or zero-argument new Date().
