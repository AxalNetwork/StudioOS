Objective
  Use anonymised advisor data to build new MI sections that founders
  and investors can use to position themselves. All extractions
  happen server-side; no raw user content leaves /api/market-intel.
  k-anonymity ≥ 5 enforced on every cell.

New sections (each lives as a tab on MarketIntelPage.jsx)

  1. Axal Investor Signals
       Aggregated investor thesis fields → sector heatmap, average
       ticket, top thesis n-grams. (Already specced in Prompt E;
       wire the advisor data into it now.)

  2. Founder Sentiment
       From every advisor answer flagged as `sentiment_eligible`
       (free-text fields like problem urgency, customer pain, blocker
       reasons), run bge-base-en-v1.5 → 768-dim embedding +
       llama-3.3-70b classifier returning {valence: -1..1, energy:
       0..1, topic_tag}.
       Aggregate per sector and per week.
       Surface chart: rolling 30-day sentiment line + top blockers
       word cloud + top excitements.

  3. Technology Adoption Lifecycle (TALC) positioning
       Question bank asks each founder where they think they are on
       the TALC (Innovators / Early Adopters / Early Majority / Late
       Majority / Laggards) and where their product/sector is.
       Investors answer where they prefer to deploy.
       Output: stacked bar of where users self-position by sector;
       gap chart of "founder belief vs investor preference"
       (chasm-crossing readiness).

  4. Demand & Supply Atlas
       Founders' service needs (mentors, lawyers, designers, GTM,
       data engineering) vs Operating Partners' offered services
       and Investors' connected service partners. Output: two-sided
       heatmap per sector with shortage / surplus pills.

  5. Founder-Investor Fit
       Cosine similarity between founder thesis embeddings (from
       advisor answers) and investor thesis embeddings.
       Per founder: top-5 matching active investors (de-identified
       until NDA per Prompt Y).
       Per investor: top-N deals matching thesis.

  6. Partner Marketplace Pulse
       From Operating Partner offer bank: average rate cards,
       capacity by skill, distribution of compensation models
       (cash/equity/revshare).
       From founder needs: time-to-fill, success rate of intros.

  7. Sector Heat (composite)
       Combines: external sources (Crunchbase, EDGAR, Trends, etc.)
       × internal signals (founder activity counts, investor
       inquiries per sector). Single composite "Axal Sector Heat"
       index per sector + sub-sector.

  8. Sentiment Geography
       Map view: per-country sentiment + activity count.

  9. Capital Velocity
       From investor advisor answers (deployment target, follow-on
       reserve, current deployment pace) + actual deal flow on
       platform — a per-quarter velocity index.

Pipelines
  cloudflare-worker/src/services/market_intel/extractors/
    sentiment.ts                  bge + llama-3.3-70b classifier
    talc_position.ts              parses TALC self-position questions
    thesis_embedding.ts           bge embeddings for founder+investor thesis
    demand_supply.ts              normalises needs vs offers
    fit_match.ts                  cosine + threshold
    sector_heat.ts                composite formula

  Each extractor runs:
    - on every advisor_answer insert via a queue job (idempotent),
    - and as a nightly batch reconciliation cron.

Storage
  market_intel_signals (id, source='advisor', metric_key, sector,
                        geo, persona, value, payload_json,
                        snapshot_ts)
  market_intel_aggregates (sector, metric_key, granularity, ts,
                           value, n, ci_lower, ci_upper)
  market_intel_embeddings (entity_type, entity_id, vector BLOB,
                            ts)
  Aggregates are computed nightly; reads always go to the
  aggregates table (fast).

Endpoints
  GET /api/market-intel/sentiment?sector=&window=
  GET /api/market-intel/talc?sector=
  GET /api/market-intel/demand-supply?sector=
  GET /api/market-intel/fit/founder/:project_id
  GET /api/market-intel/fit/investor/me
  GET /api/market-intel/partner-pulse
  GET /api/market-intel/sector-heat
  GET /api/market-intel/sentiment-geo
  GET /api/market-intel/capital-velocity

Privacy
  - Suppress cells with n < 5.
  - Investor identities never disclosed without signed pairwise NDA.
  - Free-text excerpts shown in dashboards are paraphrased by
    llama-3.3-70b, never raw user text. Paraphrases stored in
    market_intel_snippets with origin redacted.
  - Per-user opt-out toggle (Settings > Privacy → "Contribute my
    advisor answers to anonymised Market Intelligence", default on).

Acceptance
  - Each new MI tab renders with real (anonymised) numbers after the
    first nightly aggregation.
  - Sentiment + TALC charts update within minutes of new advisor
    answers.
  - Founder Fit returns matching investors only after a signed NDA
    is in place; otherwise hashed identifiers.
  - Opt-out toggle removes a user's contribution within 24h.
