I have the current founder sidebar in front of me from `frontend/src/sidebarConfig.js` (the `founder` block, 8 groups). No code changes were requested here — this is the audit. It's grounded in the actual config.

## 1. Current problems

- **Build is a dumping ground and mixes three lifecycle stages.** Today's Build holds Projects, Pipeline Board, Studio Ops, Spin-Outs, Brand & Landing, **Pitch Deck**, Financial Model, **Customer Discovery**, Roadmap, Metrics, **Cap Table** — that's execution + validation (Discovery) + fundraising (Deck/Model/Cap Table) jammed together.
- **Three execution surfaces for one flow.** Projects (`/projects`), Pipeline Board (`/pipeline`), and Roadmap (`/build/roadmap`) are three views of the same execution pipeline with no single home.
- **A parallel portal.** "Founder Portal" (`/founder`) re-exposes the whole nav — same duplication problem the investor "Investor Portal" had.
- **Legal is sprawling and US-forced.** Legal & Capital, Incorporate, Co-Founder Agreement, **83(b) Tracker**, Compliance Calendar, and Trust Center are six separate items; 83(b) (US-only) is shown to every founder, and Co-Founder Agreement is a standalone top-level item.
- **A "Capital" group that isn't founder-core.** Liquidity & Exits, Payouts, Portfolio Health are late-stage/investor-flavored; Portfolio Health duplicates what Metrics should own.
- **"Network" is a grab-bag.** Service Catalogue, Needs Board, Refer & Earn, Relationships, Network Effects, Calendar, Events span Validate, Launch, More, and utility — no coherent stage.
- **Trust Center is filed under Legal**, Wellbeing is its own one-item group, Discover (games) sits in Home, and Articles is buried in Account.
- **Missing raise workflows** (see §3): no investor-updates home, no data room, no fundraising CRM.

## 2. Recommended sidebar structure

**Home:** Dashboard only *(fold Founder Portal in; move Discover to More).*

### Build — execution & venture construction
- **Execution** — merge **Projects + Pipeline Board + Roadmap** into one execution hub (list/board/timeline views).
- **Studio Ops** (`/studio-ops`)
- **Metrics** (`/build/metrics`) — **absorb Portfolio Health** (`/portfolio/health`) as the founder's own company-health view.
- **Brand & Landing** (`/build/brand`) — construction of brand assets (the public *release* lives in Launch).
- **Spin-Outs** (`/spinouts`)
- *Moved out:* Customer Discovery → Validate; Pitch Deck / Financial Model / Cap Table → Raise.

### Validate — discovery, team, signal
- **Customer Discovery** (`/build/discovery`) — moved here from Build (it's validation, not construction).
- **Needs Board** (`/needs`) + **Service Catalogue** (`/services`) — the marketplace pair.
- **AI Advisory Suite** (`/advisory`)
- **Find a Mentor** (`/mentors`) · **Find a Co-founder** (`/cofounder`)
- **Network** — merge **Relationships + Network Effects** into one module (they're the same relationship graph).

### Raise — fundraising readiness, legal, equity
- **Pitch Deck** (`/build/deck`) · **Financial Model** (`/build/financials`) · **Cap Table** (`/build/captable`) — moved from Build; these *are* the raise toolkit.
- **Legal & Capital** (`/legal-capital`) — the legal hub; **absorb Co-Founder Agreement** and **Compliance Calendar**.
- **Incorporate** (`/incorporate`) — keep, but nest **83(b) Tracker conditionally** (US founders only) rather than as a peer item.
- **Investor Updates** — home for the founder side of Company Updates (`/portfolio/updates`, the feature just shipped). Keeping investors warm is a raise activity. *(relocate + surface)*
- **Data Room** *(add, see §3)* · **Investor CRM** *(add, see §3)*

### Launch — go-to-market activation
- **Events** (`/my/events`)
- **Co-Marketing** (`/comarketing`) — GTM activation, currently not surfaced in the founder nav.
- **Articles** (`/articles/draft`) — moved from Account; treated as launch/publishing activation, not a general blog.

### More — secondary / low-frequency
- **Refer & Earn** (`/refer`) · **Founder Wellbeing** (`/wellbeing`) · **Documentation** (`/docs`) · **Discover** (`/play`)
- **Liquidity & Exits** (`/liquidity`) · **Payouts** (`/payouts`) — real but late-stage for most founders.
- **Calendar** (`/calendar`) — utility.

### Account — trust, support, settings, utility
- **Trust Center** (`/trust`) — moved from Legal (it's account/security, not raise-critical).
- **Identity / KYC** (`/kyc`) · **Support** (`/tickets`) · **Settings** (`/settings`) · **Activity Log** (`/activity`) · **My Profile** (`/profile`).

This takes the founder nav from **8 groups / ~35 items** to **Home + 6 stages / ~15 primary items**, with each feature owning exactly one home.

## 6. Final recommendation

Adopt the **Build → Validate → Raise → Launch → More → Account** sidebar. The single biggest fix is making **Build execution-only** — pulling Customer Discovery into Validate and Pitch Deck / Financial Model / Cap Table into Raise — and collapsing **Projects + Pipeline + Roadmap into one Execution hub**. Consolidate all legal into **Legal & Capital** (Co-Founder Agreement + Compliance folded in, 83(b) conditional) so non-US founders aren't dragged through US-specific steps, and move **Trust Center to Account**. That yields a nav that reads exactly like a founder's journey from building to launching, with each feature living in one place.
