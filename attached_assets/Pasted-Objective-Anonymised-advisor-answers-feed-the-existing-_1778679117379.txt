Objective
  Anonymised advisor answers feed the existing Market Intelligence
  sections:
    - Axal Investor Signals
    - Founder Sentiment
    - TALC Positioning
    - Demand & Supply Atlas
    - Founder–Investor Fit
    - Partner Marketplace Pulse
    - Sector Heat
    - Sentiment Geography
    - Capital Velocity
  These remain ADDITIVE to the existing external-source pipeline
  (Crunchbase, EDGAR, GDELT, USPTO, etc.). Never replace external data.

Extractor pipeline (cloudflare-worker/src/services/market_intel/extractors/)
  advisor_sentiment.ts
    On every advisor_answer commit where question.sentiment_eligible
    is true, enqueue a job:
      embed value with @cf/baai/bge-base-en-v1.5
      classify valence/energy/topic with @cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-fp8-fast
      → market_intel_signals row { source='advisor', metric_key='sentiment',
                                    sector, geo, persona, value: {valence, energy},
                                    snapshot_ts }
  advisor_talc.ts
    On every answer where question.talc_eligible:
      Parse the chosen TALC bucket → market_intel_signals row.
  advisor_thesis_embedding.ts
    Investor thesis answers + founder one-liner answers → bge embeddings
    stored in market_intel_embeddings for similarity matching (Founder
    ↔ Investor Fit).
  advisor_demand_supply.ts
    Founder "need" answers + partner "offer" answers → bilateral signal.
  advisor_capital_velocity.ts
    Investor deployment target / current deployment pace →
    quarterly velocity index.
  advisor_partner_pulse.ts
    Partner rate cards / capacity / comp preferences → marketplace pulse.

Privacy
  - k-anonymity ≥ 5 enforced on every aggregate.
  - No row-level pass-through. Free-text excerpts paraphrased by the
    LLM before display; raw text never leaves /api/market-intel.
  - User opt-out in Settings > Privacy ("Contribute my advisor
    answers to anonymised Market Intelligence") — default ON.
  - Opt-out removes contributions within 6 hours (next aggregation run).
  - Investor identities masked behind hashed IDs until a signed
    pairwise NDA exists.

Aggregator
  Nightly cron computes market_intel_aggregates rows for every MI
  section, granularity {1d, 7d, 30d, 90d}, with n, ci_lower, ci_upper.
  Suppress cells with n<5 at read time, not write time, so retroactive
  opt-out is correct.

Endpoints (cloudflare-worker/src/routes/market_intel.ts — extend)
  All these already exist (Prompt AK): sentiment, talc,
  demand-supply, fit, partner-pulse, sector-heat, capital-velocity,
  sentiment-geo. Wire them to read the new advisor-sourced rows as a
  second `source_kind` line alongside external sources.

Acceptance
  - Every MI tab now shows TWO data series where applicable: "external"
    and "community (advisor)".
  - Aggregates update within hours of new advisor answers (cron) or
  minutes if the user explicitly triggers a refresh (admin only).
  - n<5 cells render "Insufficient data" (no zero ghosts).
  - Opt-out flow verified by removing a test user and confirming
    their data no longer appears in next aggregation.
