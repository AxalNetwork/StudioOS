P1System — AI rail · license boundary · the seam
Amber, where Founder is violet, Investor indigo and Advisor emerald. Four licenses, four axioms: build a company · deploy capital · sell expertise · deliver services and operational capacity. One license covers two delivery modes — a firm that ships projects, and an operator who embeds inside the client. That second mode is what forces a new seam idea: an embedded operator can hold founder-granted, scoped access to the client's own objects, and both sides need to see exactly how much.

The grant chip — new, and specific to operator mode
A project engagement receives deliverables. An embedded operator works inside the client's own tools — their board, their metrics — which means the founder grants scoped access to specific objects, and can revoke it. That is a different relationship from a data-room grant, so it gets its own chip rather than reusing the seam marker.
Granted by Verwood · Board, KPIs · revocable
What the operator sees on their own Delivery page: the scope named, the granter named, and the fact it can be withdrawn stated rather than implied.
Rin Takahashi · Board, KPIs · revoke
The same grant as the founder sees it, in their product: a person, a scope, and a revoke action. One fact, two renderings — never a silent standing permission.
Scope is enumerated, never a role name. "Operator access" would be a permission nobody audits; "Board, KPIs" is one a founder can reason about — and the chip shows the same two objects on both sides, so neither party can believe a different scope than the other.
