Build the Spin-Out Lab as a 4-week guided sprint. It's the default experience for new
founders who don't yet have a company incorporated. Detection happens at onboarding
("Tell me about yourself") — if the user picks "I have an idea / pre-incorporation",
their account is flagged as `spinout_lab_active` and the sidebar collapses to the
sprint view.

Schema (add to cloudflare-worker/sql/schema.sql + a migration):
  ALTER TABLE users ADD COLUMN spinout_lab_active INTEGER DEFAULT 0;
  ALTER TABLE users ADD COLUMN spinout_lab_week INTEGER DEFAULT 1;     -- 1..4
  ALTER TABLE users ADD COLUMN spinout_lab_started_at DATETIME;
  CREATE TABLE spinout_lab_milestones (
    id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY AUTOINCREMENT,
    user_id INTEGER NOT NULL,
    week INTEGER NOT NULL,
    milestone_key TEXT NOT NULL,
    completed_at DATETIME,
    UNIQUE(user_id, milestone_key)
  );

Week structure (each week unlocks features in the sidebar; previous weeks remain
unlocked):

  WEEK 1 — Idea & Problem Validation
    Unlocked features: Projects (create the venture record), Customer Discovery,
                       Market Intelligence
    Milestone to advance: project created + ≥3 customer discovery interviews logged
    Explainer copy:
      Projects: "Your venture's home base. Capture the problem, target customer, and
                 hypothesis you're testing this week."
      Customer Discovery: "Talk to 5+ potential customers. Log every conversation —
                           pain points, quotes, and whether your solution resonates."
      Market Intelligence: "Size your market and study competitors before you build.
                            TAM/SAM/SOM and a landscape map."

  WEEK 2 — Solution Design & Roadmap
    Unlocked features: Roadmap (OKRs + Kanban), Brand Builder, Pitch Deck
    Milestone: ≥3 OKRs created, brand basics filled (name, tagline, colors), and a
               first deck draft saved
    Explainer copy:
      Roadmap: "Turn your idea into 90-day objectives and weekly deliverables.
                Drag tasks across To-Do → In Progress → Done."
      Brand Builder: "Pick a name, generate a logo, and lock in your brand voice."
      Pitch Deck: "A 10-slide investor deck builder, pre-filled from your project
                   data. Export to PDF when ready."

  WEEK 3 — Validation & Co-founder
    Unlocked features: Co-founder Match, Mentors, Office Hours, Scoring
    Milestone: scoring run completed (≥40), at least one mentor meeting booked OR
               co-founder request sent
    Explainer copy:
      Co-founder Match: "Find a technical or business co-founder with complementary
                         skills, vetted by us."
      Mentors: "Browse mentors by sector and book 30-min sessions to pressure-test
               your plan."
      Office Hours: "Drop into open partner office hours for live feedback —
                     no booking needed."
      Scoring: "Get an objective venture readiness score across team, market, problem
                fit, and traction. Use it to find your weakest area."

  WEEK 4 — Incorporation & Capital
    Unlocked features: Incorporate, Cap Table, Section 83(b), Cofounder Agreement,
                       Capital, Compliance, KYC
    Milestone: company incorporated (Incorporate flow finished)
    Explainer copy:
      Incorporate: "Form your Delaware C-Corp in one click. We handle filings,
                    EIN, and your founders' stock issuance."
      Cap Table: "Issue founder stock and track equity. Built-in vesting schedules
                  and option-pool modelling."
      Section 83(b): "File your 83(b) election within 30 days of stock issuance to
                      avoid a tax disaster. We auto-generate the form."
      Cofounder Agreement: "Lock down equity splits, vesting, IP assignment, and
                            decision rights before money complicates things."
      Capital: "Open a fundraise with the right investors for your stage and
               sector."
      Compliance + KYC: "Verify your identity, satisfy AML, and stay audit-ready
                         for future investors."

After Week 4 completes (incorporation done) → set users.spinout_lab_active = 0,
set users.is_incorporated = 1, and reveal the full sidebar (the existing
"existing founder" view).

Implementation:
1. Worker route cloudflare-worker/src/routes/spinout_lab.ts:
     GET  /api/spinout-lab/state         → { active, week, days_remaining,
                                             milestones, unlocked_features }
     POST /api/spinout-lab/start         → activates lab, sets week=1, started_at
     POST /api/spinout-lab/milestone     → { milestone_key } marks done; auto-advances
                                            week when that week's milestones are met
     POST /api/spinout-lab/exit          → marks incorporated, deactivates lab
2. Frontend lib/api.js: add `spinoutLab.state()`, `.start()`, `.complete(key)`,
   `.exit()`.
3. Frontend onboarding: if user picks "I have an idea / pre-incorporation" on the
   "Tell me about yourself" / OnboardingFounderPage step, call spinoutLab.start().
4. App.jsx sidebar: when `user.spinout_lab_active === 1`, render a NEW
   <SpinoutLabSidebar /> component that:
   - Shows the 4-week progress bar at the top.
   - Lists ONLY the features unlocked for this user's current week (and any earlier
     weeks).
   - Each item shows a small (i) tooltip with the explainer copy above.
   - Hides every other sidebar section.
5. SpinoutLabPage.jsx becomes the dashboard for active sprints (today's milestones,
   day counter, "I finished this milestone" buttons).
6. Each milestone-eligible action elsewhere (e.g. project created, OKR created,
   incorporation completed) calls spinoutLab.complete(milestone_key) on success.

Acceptance:
- A brand-new founder picks "I have an idea" in onboarding → lands on SpinoutLab
  dashboard with Week 1 features only in the sidebar.
- Creating a project + 3 interviews unlocks Week 2 sidebar items.
- Completing all 4 weeks (incorporating) → sidebar switches to the standard founder
  layout, spinout_lab_active becomes 0.
- Explainer tooltips render for new founders on every unlocked sidebar item.
