# Axal VC — Investor Sidebar Audit & Lifecycle Regroup

## Context

The investor side of axal.vc has grown to **28 sidebar items across 7 groups**
(`frontend/src/sidebarConfig.js`, `SIDEBAR_GROUPS.investor`, lines 255–300).
The grouping (Home / Pipeline / Intelligence / Portfolio / Network / Legal &
Compliance / Account) does not follow how investors actually work, several
features show the same underlying data in multiple places, and one item
(Investor Portal) is a second front-end for the whole app. The user wants the
investor navigation re-architected around the **investment lifecycle**
(Sourcing → Diligence → Commit → Support), made minimal, with every feature
having exactly one home.

**Chosen scope:** deliver the audit **and** rewrite `sidebarConfig.js` into the
new lifecycle groups using existing pages (no new/merged pages built in this
pass — page-level merges are recorded as follow-ups).

---

## 1. Current problems

1. **Grouping isn't lifecycle-based.** Home / Pipeline / Intelligence /
   Portfolio / Network / Legal & Compliance / Account is a feature dump, not a
   workflow. 28 items in 7 accordions is cognitive overload.
2. **Duplicate deal surfaces.** Deal Flow (`/deals`), Pipeline Board
   (`/pipeline`), Watchlist (`/watchlist`), AI Matches (`/matches`) and
   Projects (`/projects`) are five views of the **same deal universe** with no
   canonical "one home." Deal Flow vs Pipeline Board is literally list-vs-board
   of the same records.
3. **A parallel portal.** "Investor Portal" (`/partner-portal`) re-exposes
   dashboard, deal flow, scoring, portfolio and capital — a second way to reach
   everything already in the sidebar. Pure duplication.
4. **Fund-finance sprawl.** Capital, Funds, Liquidity & Exits, Portfolio
   Health, Reserve Allocation, Exit Waterfall = **six** separate items for what
   is one financial workspace.
5. **Compliance bucket conflates four jobs.** "Legal & Compliance" mixes deal
   diligence (Due Diligence), investor self-KYC (Identity Verification),
   account security (Trust Center) and deal legals (Legal & Capital) — four
   unrelated jobs-to-be-done in one drawer.
6. **Score/risk data scattered.** Scoring surfaces in Scoring, Portfolio
   Health, Risk Matrix, Pipeline and Deals; risk surfaces in Risk Matrix,
   Portfolio Health and Trust — no canonical owner, so numbers can disagree.
7. **Mislabeled Home.** "Discover" (`/play`, gamified assessments) sits in the
   Home group; it's education, not a daily investor surface.
8. **Missing core workflows.** No IC/investment-decision record, no LP
   reporting, no portfolio-company update inbox, no portfolio cap-table /
   ownership view (see §3).
9. **Cloned-from-partner residue.** The investor nav mirrors the partner nav;
   items only meaningful to partners were carried over or left as URL-only
   orphans.

---

## 2. Recommended sidebar structure

Six groups: **Home**, **Sourcing**, **Diligence**, **Commit**, **Support**,
**More**. Home + Sourcing open by default (existing `defaultOpenGroups`
behavior); More is a collapsed overflow drawer. Every feature below has exactly
one home; cross-stage needs are met by **read-only badges/deep-links inside
pages**, not by repeating the item in the sidebar.

### Home
- **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`) — the single overview/home.
- *Removed here:* "Investor Portal" (`/partner-portal`) — fold into Dashboard
  (redirect `/partner-portal` → `/dashboard`). *Removed:* "Discover" (moved to
  More).

### Sourcing — find & capture deals
- **Deal Flow** (`/deals`) — **primary home for all deal records.**
- **Pipeline Board** (`/pipeline`) — board view of the *same* deals.
  *Merge (follow-up):* collapse into Deal Flow as a list/board toggle so there
  is one deal surface.
- **AI Matches** (`/matches`) — inbound/discovery, co-invest, referral quality.
- **Watchlist & Journal** (`/watchlist`) — personal tracked subset + conviction
  journal + anti-portfolio. Primary home for conviction tracking (starts at
  sourcing).
- *Removed:* "Projects" (`/projects`) — redundant with Deal Flow for investors;
  keep it founder-side only.

### Diligence — validate founder, market & risk
- **Scoring Engine** (`/scoring`) — quantitative framework; **owns Deal Memo
  generation** (no separate item).
- **Due Diligence** (`/admin/due-diligence`) — structured case/checklist
  tracker; IC-prep material.
- **Market Intelligence** (`/market-intel`) — market/macro validation.
- **Risk Matrix** (`/portfolio/risk-matrix`) — **primary home for risk.**
  It literally answers "validate risk," so it lives in Diligence even though it
  renders across the portfolio; Portfolio Health (Support) shows a *read-only*
  risk badge that deep-links here.
