Objective
  Build ONE pitch-deck template — fixed structure, hardcoded slides —
  that represents what every founder produces during the Axal 30-day
  Spin-Out Lab, ready to present to investors and Axal-network partners.

  Template id           axal_spinout_demoday
  Template label        Axal 30-day Spin-Out Lab — Demo Day
  Audience              Axal-network investors + partners
  Slide count           14 (fixed; do NOT make it adaptive)
  File                  frontend/src/decks/templates/axal_spinout_demoday_app.tsx
  Style                 Single self-contained React + TypeScript + Tailwind +
                        Framer Motion app. Same shape as the other 7 templates
                        in frontend/src/decks/templates/*. SVG-only graphics,
                        16:9, keyboard nav, dot pagination, inline contentEditable
                        editing, structuredClone state, default export the
                        deck shell + a SAMPLE_DATA constant.

Why 14
  Four weeks of curriculum = four narrative acts. Each act ships 2–4
  slides depending on what the Lab actually produces. Plus a cover, an
  Axal-signal closing card, and an "appendix" pointer for the six Market
  Intelligence dashboards. Total: 14. Do not parameterise.

Slide inventory (in this exact order)

  Cover
    01  Cover — venture identity + cohort badge
        Pulled from   projects.name, projects.description (tagline),
                      projects.sector, projects.stage, plus a static
                      "Axal Spin-Out Lab · cohort <Month YYYY>" eyebrow

  Act I — Week 1 · Idea & Customer
    02  Problem
        Pulled from   projects.problem_statement (heroes the slide),
                      discovery_interviews.pains_json (top 3 pains,
                      aggregated across interviews, ordered by frequency)
    03  Validation — five customers, in their own words
        Pulled from   discovery_interviews rows (name, date, notes excerpt);
                      requires ≥ 5 logged interviews. Render up to 5
                      quote cards with date + interviewee role.
    04  Market — TAM / SAM with citations
        Pulled from   projects.tam, projects.sam, projects.som
                      (som derived if missing); plus
                      advisor_answers.raw_value WHERE question_id LIKE
                      'founder.market.%' for citation URLs. SVG nested
                      circles + a citations strip.

  Act II — Week 2 · Solution & Roadmap
    05  Solution — what we built, what it replaces
        Pulled from   projects.solution + projects.why_now.
                      Two-column "Before → After" if both exist; else
                      a single solution statement.
    06  90-day Roadmap — OKRs
        Pulled from   roadmap_okrs (objective + key_results_json,
                      grouped by quarter). Render as a 3-column board
                      (Now / Next / Later) based on kanban_status.
                      Requires ≥ 3 OKRs per Lab spec.
    07  Brand — voice, palette, one-liner
        Pulled from   projects.tagline, advisor_answers WHERE
                      question_id LIKE 'founder.brand.%'.
                      Render the one-liner big, plus a tiny palette
                      strip if brand_palette_json is present.

  Act III — Week 3 · Validate & Team
    08  Venture Readiness — internal score
        Pulled from   score_snapshots (latest, is_sandbox = 0). Show
                      total_score / tier, plus the 6 sub-scores
                      (market, team, product, capital, fit,
                      distribution) as small bars. ai_notes drives
                      the "What this tells us" paragraph.
    09  Team — founders + cofounder + advisors
        Pulled from   captable_holders WHERE kind IN ('founder','team'),
                      plus advisor_answers WHERE question_id LIKE
                      'founder.team.advisor%'. If cofounder track was
                      "going solo", surface that decision honestly.
    10  Mentor cadence + Axal network
        Pulled from   spinout_lab_milestones WHERE milestone_key =
                      'mentor_meeting_booked'. Render a short list of
                      booked sessions + a static "Axal Network" strip
                      listing the five operator-partner categories:
                      Legal · Design · Recruiting · Technical DD · Finance.

  Act IV — Week 4 · Incorporate & Capital
    11  Incorporated + cap table
        Pulled from   captable_holders (all rows), aggregated to %
                      ownership. Static rail confirming: Delaware C-Corp
                      default, 4-yr vesting / 1-yr cliff, 83(b) filed
                      in window (drives from spinout_lab_milestones
                      WHERE milestone_key = 'incorporation_completed').
    12  Fundraise ask
        Pulled from   projects.funding_needed, projects.use_of_funds,
                      financial_models.inputs_json (runway_months,
                      instrument). Render the ask big (e.g. "$1.5M"),
                      use-of-funds as a stacked bar, and a 12-month
                      milestones list from roadmap_okrs WHERE
                      kanban_status = 'next'.

  Closing
    13  The Axal signal — what 30 days produced
        Static structure + dynamic content:
        Pulled from   spinout_lab_milestones (count by week × completed_at)
                      + score_snapshots.tier. Render the 4-week timeline
                      with each milestone checked off, plus the static
                      Axal trust badges (three-way NDA, KYC investor-side,
                      alumni community for life).
    14  Contact + appendix pointer
        Pulled from   users.email (primary founder), users.full_name.
        Static rail   lists the six Market Intelligence dashboards
                      available to interested investors on request:
                      Sector compass · Investor signals · TALC positioning ·
                      Demand & supply atlas · Founder ↔ investor fit ·
                      Capital velocity. CTA: "Request access to the
                      diligence dashboards →".

Visual variants — founder preference
  Expose a `variant` prop on the deck shell that accepts:
    'editorial'      Source Serif Pro display, warm cream paper, generous
                     whitespace, magazine-quality hierarchy
    'product_first'  Inter sans, white paper, BrowserChrome-wrapped SVG
                     mockups on every Act II slide, dashboard energy
    'data_dense'     IBM Plex Sans + JetBrains Mono, white paper,
                     Recharts on slides 4, 6, 8, 11, 12
    'manifesto'      Playfair Display, ink-black paper, one big idea per
                     slide, cinematic motion (550ms cubic-bezier)

  Implement variants as a single token bundle (palette + font stack +
  per-slide layout switch) — NOT four separate decks. Each slide reads
  the variant from context and picks the right layout block.
  Default variant: 'editorial'.
