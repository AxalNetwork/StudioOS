#10 - CF — Market Intelligence "Platform Personas" tab

What & Why

Add a Platform Personas section to Market Intelligence that shows the anonymised composition of platform users — useful for founders ("what kind of investors are here?"), investors ("what kind of founders show up?"), and admins (executive overview). Depends on CE because the underlying advisor signals must be flowing before persona aggregates are meaningful.

Done looks like

New "Platform Personas" tab visible in the Market Intelligence dropdown
All 8 charts render with real data once n ≥ 5 per cell:
Role distribution donut (Founders Spin-Out vs Existing, Investors Free/Pro/Inst, Mentors, Operating Partners by deal type)
Sector × Role heatmap (founder sectors vs investor sectors, matches/mismatches highlighted)
Stage focus stacked bar
Geo distribution pie (country/region)
Activity composite (median session minutes per role, top-feature usage per role)
Spin-Out Lab funnel (count per week + completion rate)
New signups trend (weekly, broken down by role)
Active investor pipeline coverage (weighted by ticket × deals watched)
Cached results refresh within 5s; fresh aggregation within 30s
Tier gating enforced: Free sees role donut + sector heatmap; Growth / Investor Pro see all charts; Studio / Institutional get CSV+PDF export and weekly digest
Free-tier teaser shows blurred charts with an upgrade CTA
Out of scope

The advisor extractor work (CE, must already be merged)
New advisor questions (CH)
