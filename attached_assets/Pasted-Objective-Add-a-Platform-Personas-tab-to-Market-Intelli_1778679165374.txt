Objective
  Add a "Platform Personas" tab to Market Intelligence showing the
  composition of platform users (anonymised, aggregated). Useful for
  founders ("what kind of investors are here?"), investors ("what
  kind of founders show up?"), and admins (executive overview).

Charts on the tab
  1. Role distribution donut: % Founders (Spin-Out vs Existing), %
     Investors (Free/Pro/Inst), % Mentors, % Operating Partners by
     deal type.
  2. Sector × Role heatmap: founder sectors vs investor sectors;
     highlight matches and mismatches.
  3. Stage focus stacked bar: which stage each persona group prefers.
  4. Geo distribution: country / region pie.
  5. Activity composite: median session minutes per role, top-feature
     usage per role.
  6. Spin-Out Lab funnel: count of founders at each week + completion
     rate.
  7. New signups trend: weekly chart, broken down by role.
  8. Active investor pipeline coverage: weighted by ticket × deals
     watched.

Endpoint
  GET /api/market-intel/platform-personas?from=&to=
    → all of the above as JSON, k-anonymity ≥ 5 applied.

Frontend
  frontend/src/pages/MarketIntelPage.jsx adds tab "Platform Personas"
  alongside the existing ones. Uses recharts for visualisations.

Tier gating
  Free tier: high-level composition only (role donut, sector heatmap).
  Growth / Investor Pro: all charts.
  Studio / Institutional: + CSV / PDF export, weekly digest.

Acceptance
  - Tab renders all 8 charts with real data once n ≥ 5 per cell.
  - Refresh within 5s for cached results, 30s for fresh aggregation.
  - Export buttons working on the right tiers.
