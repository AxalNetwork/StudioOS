#7 - AT-2 — MI Frontend Tabs (Advisor-derived sections)
What & Why
Render the 9 new Market Intelligence sections from AT-1's endpoints as tabs on MarketIntelPage.jsx. Founders use these to position themselves; investors use them for sourcing and sizing.

Done looks like
9 new tabs added to MarketIntelPage.jsx: Axal Investor Signals, Founder Sentiment, TALC Positioning, Demand & Supply Atlas, Founder-Investor Fit, Partner Marketplace Pulse, Sector Heat, Sentiment Geography, Capital Velocity.
Each tab renders within p95 800ms after data load (reads pre-aggregated cells).
Founder Sentiment shows rolling 30-day line + top-blockers word cloud + top-excitements.
TALC tab shows stacked bar of self-positioning by sector + chasm-readiness gap chart.
Demand & Supply: two-sided heatmap per sector with shortage/surplus pills.
Founder-Investor Fit: founder view shows top-5 investors (de-identified until NDA); investor view shows top-N matching deals.
Partner Pulse: rate-card averages, capacity by skill, comp-model distribution.
Sector Heat: single composite index per sector + sub-sector with sparkline.
Sentiment Geography: map view (per-country sentiment + activity).
Capital Velocity: per-quarter velocity index chart.
Empty/low-n states: when AT-1 returns suppressed cells, UI shows "Not enough data yet" with k-anonymity threshold copy.
Settings → Privacy toggle "Contribute my advisor answers to anonymised Market Intelligence" wired to users.mi_contribution_optout.
Out of scope
Backend extractors / storage / endpoints (AT-1).
Publication exports (AU).
New chart libraries — reuse what MarketIntelPage.jsx already uses.
Steps
Tab scaffolding — Add 9 tabs to MarketIntelPage.jsx with lazy data loaders calling AT-1's endpoints.
Sentiment tab — Line chart + word cloud + excitement list; date-range + sector filters.
TALC tab — Stacked bar (self-position) + gap chart (founder belief vs investor preference).
Demand & Supply tab — Two-sided heatmap with shortage/surplus pill chips.
Founder-Investor Fit tab — Persona-aware view (founder vs investor); NDA-gated identifier reveal.
Partner Pulse tab — Rate-card stats + skill capacity + comp-model donut.
Sector Heat tab — Composite index table with sparkline per sector.
Sentiment Geography tab — Map (reuse existing geo viz if present; otherwise a heat-coded country list).
Capital Velocity tab — Per-quarter line chart with deployment-target overlay.
Privacy toggle UI — Wire Settings → Privacy section.
Empty/suppressed state — Reusable <MIInsufficientData /> component for n<5 cells.
