Don't listen — Replit is oversimplifying and would have you delete useful external sources.

Reality is **both**, not advisor-only:

| Source class | Examples | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| External | USPTO/EPO patents, arXiv, Papers With Code, GitHub Trending, Product Hunt, AWS Marketplace, App stores, Crunchbase, EDGAR, GDELT, Google Trends, World Bank, OECD | Objective, large-sample market data the advisor can never replicate |
| Personal Advisor (anonymised) | Sector sentiment, TALC self-positions, founder needs, investor thesis embeddings, partner offers | Community/internal signal — small sample, subjective, fresh |

If you make Personal Advisor the single source of truth:

- **Sample size collapses.** A new platform with 50 founders cannot replace public datasets. Patent counts and arXiv volume don't come from advisor chats.
- **Selection bias.** Only your users answer; the market does not.
- **Subjectivity.** Self-reported TAM/MRR/positioning are exactly what the anti-cheat work (Prompt T) is trying to flag — you'd be feeding biased numbers into "market truth".
- **You'd lose the Citations & Methodology tab.** External URLs are the only thing that gives an investor confidence in the data.
- **Founder Lens / Investor Lens already need cross-checks.** A founder's self-positioned TALC needs to be validated against external adoption signals (arXiv volume, GitHub stars, app-store ranks).

What's actually correct (matches my earlier Prompt AT):

- **Sector Compass · Founder Lens · Investor Lens · Geography Lens · Citations** → keep external sources as primary. Advisor data overlays as a second series ("internal signal").
- **Sentiment · TALC positioning · Demand & Supply · Founder-Investor Fit · Partner Pulse · Capital Velocity** → these *are* the advisor-derived sections. They didn't exist before; advisor is their natural source.
- **Axal Investor Signals** → mix of investor-profile advisor answers + Stripe-confirmed deployment data, with k-anonymity ≥ 5.
