## Quickstart

### 1. Create your account
Go to **axal.vc/register**, enter your email, and pass the Cloudflare Turnstile check. We'll send a verification link via email.

### 2. Verify your email
Click the link within 24 hours. The link is single-use and SHA-256-hashed at rest, so it cannot be replayed. If it expires, request a new one — you can resend up to 3 times per hour.

### 3. Set up TOTP (passwordless 2FA)
After verification, scan the QR code with **Google Authenticator**, **1Password**, **Authy**, or any TOTP-compliant app. We do not use passwords — every login requires a fresh 6-digit code from your authenticator. Save the recovery codes shown after setup somewhere safe.

### 4. Pick your role
On first login, you'll be routed to one of three portals based on the role assigned to your account:

- **Founder Portal** — you're applying or running a spin-out venture
- **Investor Portal** — you're a Limited Partner (LP) or co-investor
- **Partner Portal** — you're a service provider (legal, accounting, design, GTM, etc.)

Admins (studio operators) see all three plus the Admin Console and can switch between views via the violet **Portal Switcher** bar at the top.

### 5. Complete your profile
Each portal asks 4–8 onboarding questions — tailored to your role. Your answers populate scoring inputs (founders), LP commitment terms (investors), or service catalog entries (partners).

You're in. Bookmark **axal.vc/dashboard** and continue below.

## How StudioOS works

StudioOS is built around **seven engines** that handle the full lifecycle of venture creation:

1. **Intelligence Engine** — Market pulse, sector signals, competitive intelligence, studio benchmarks, conviction picks.
2. **Scoring & Diligence Engine** — Our proprietary 100-point scoring algorithm + automated diligence checks (legal, technical, financial).
3. **AI Advisory Suite** — On-demand AI advisor for strategy, GTM, fundraising, hiring; financial planner with burn/runway projections; diligence checker.
4. **Deal & Match Engine** — Deal flow CRM (applied → scored → active → funded), partner matchmaking, referral system.
5. **Legal & Compliance Engine** — 18 templates spanning GP governance, fund formation (LPA, PPM, Subscription, Management Co.), portfolio execution (SAFE, term sheets, SPAs, bylaws, equity splits, IP, voting rights), and compliance (Form ADV, AML/KYC, 83(b)).
6. **Operations & Support Hub** — Ticketing (auto-syncs to GitHub Issues), activity & audit log, real-time pipeline updates via WebSockets.
7. **Capital & Investment Engine** — Capital calls, LP investor portal, fund management (`vc_funds`), distributions ledger, TVPI/DPI tracking, secondary liquidity marketplace.

Every action you take in StudioOS is recorded in the **activity log** (audit-grade), encrypted at rest, and gated by a strict role-based access control (RBAC) model. The platform runs on FastAPI (canonical API) with a Cloudflare Workers edge layer for WebSockets, caching, and queue draining.

## For Founders

You're here to spin out a company in 30 days. Here's the entire flow.

### Submitting your venture
Open **Founder Portal → Submit Idea**. The multi-step form captures:

- Problem & market hypothesis
- TAM/SAM estimates and urgency signals
- Team (founders, expertise, prior execution)
- Product plan (MVP timeline, technical complexity, dependencies)
- Capital plan (cost to MVP, time to revenue, burn risk)
- Strategic fit with Axal's thesis
- Distribution & go-to-market

Submitting auto-triggers scoring within seconds. You don't need to wait for a human reviewer to see the result.

### The 100-point score
Your score is broken down across six dimensions:

| Dimension | Max points | What we measure |
|---|---|---|
| Market | 25 | TAM/SAM (10), Urgency (10), Trend (5) |
| Team | 20 | Expertise (8), Execution (8), Network (4) |
| Product | 15 | MVP time (7), Complexity (5), Dependencies (3) |
| Capital | 15 | Cost to MVP (7), Time to revenue (5), Burn risk (3) |
| Strategic Fit | 15 | Alignment with thesis (10), Synergy (5) |
| Distribution | 10 | Channels (5), Virality (5) |

**Outcomes:**

- **≥85 → Tier 1 (Immediate Spinout)** — moved to BUILD stage, mentor & legal docs auto-staged
- **70–84 → Tier 2 (Conditional)** — needs additional diligence; you can iterate on weak dimensions and re-submit
- **<70 → Rejected** — feedback delivered with the lowest-scoring dimensions explained

You can re-submit after material changes; we keep all historical `score_snapshots` for audit purposes.

### The 30-day playbook
Tier 1 ventures enter the 4-week Builder Kit:

- **Week 1 — Brand & narrative.** Logo, brand kit, pitch deck (AI-assisted), one-pager, domain procurement.
- **Week 2 — Legal & financial.** Incorporation wizard (Delaware C-Corp, Swiss SA, or your jurisdiction), cap table setup, founder vesting (4-year, 1-year cliff default), 83(b) elections, IP assignment.
- **Week 3 — Product & GTM.** MVP scoping, hiring plan, GTM strategy, first-customer outreach templates.
- **Week 4 — Fundraising.** SAFE templates, data room, term sheet, pitch practice with AI advisor, investor list.

Progress is tracked on your **Project Detail** page with a 4-week tracker. The pipeline updates in real-time over WebSockets — your mentors and the studio team see your status without you sending status updates.

### AI Advisory
At any point you can ask the **AI Advisor** strategy questions (GTM, fundraising tactics, product trade-offs, hiring), run a **Financial Plan** (burn/runway/projection scenarios), or trigger a **Diligence Check** (validates your scoring inputs, legal docs, team, financials before investor presentations). The advisor uses OpenAI by default; if it's unavailable, deterministic templates fall back so you always get a response.

### Legal templates
Founders get free use of these during their spin-out (gated to the Spin-Out tier):

- SAFE (post-money, MFN, valuation cap)
- Term sheet
- Stock Purchase Agreement
- Bylaws
- Founder equity split & vesting agreement
- IP assignment & confidentiality
- Voting rights
- 83(b) election letter

All templates are AI-personalized to your venture and reviewable in the **Legal** module. **Important:** templates are starting points, not legal advice. Your spin-out includes a partner attorney review before signing.

### Liquidity (post-spin-out)
Once your company is independent, you can list founder/team shares on the **Secondary Marketplace** under Liquidity. Listings get an AI-generated fair-value badge (Llama 3.1 8B + cents-clamped valuation). Buyer matching is anonymized — no emails or exact capital amounts are shipped to the AI; only role, sector, and capital band.

### Refer & Earn
Bring another founder into the studio: send them your invite link from **Refer & Earn**. If they're accepted into a Tier 1 spin-out, you earn equity bonuses or cash referral fees per the program terms. CSV import available for bulk outreach (≤500 rows, 1 MB).

## For Investors & LPs

The Investor Portal is your single pane of glass for everything happening across Axal funds.

### LP Portal
Once your LPA is signed and your commitment recorded in `limited_partners`, you'll see:

- **Commitment summary** — total commitment, called capital, uncalled capital, contributions to date.
- **Distributions ledger** — every distribution received, with `fund_distributions` records for audit. Distributions are calculated atomically per liquidity event with idempotency (no double-pay even on retried jobs).
- **TVPI / DPI charts** — live total-value-to-paid-in and distributions-to-paid-in, computed from your fund's NAV + realized distributions.
- **Capital call history** — pro-rata notices with payment status, delivered via Gmail when issued.

### Signing your LPA
LPAs are AI-generated (Llama 3.1 8B with deterministic fallback) and viewable inline. Click **Sign LPA** → typed-name signature → click-through authentication. We do not store drawn-signature image base64 (data-minimization policy); the legal record is your typed name + click-through with timestamp + audit log entry. Signed LPAs are downloadable via short-lived (5-minute) HMAC-signed URLs — never persisted in browser cache.

### Capital calls
When the GP issues a capital call, you receive:

1. An email notice with pro-rata amount and wire instructions
2. A push notification in your dashboard
3. An entry on your **Capital Calls** tab with status: pending → wired → cleared

The system updates `vc_funds.deployed_capital` automatically on call execution.

### Co-Invest & SPVs
Some deals offer co-invest opportunities for select LPs. When a co-invest is opened on a portfolio company, you'll see it in the **Co-Invest** tab with: deal terms, allocation, deadline, and one-click commit. Each commit creates an SPV-scoped subscription with its own dataroom, reserve model, and waterfall simulation.

### Reporting
LP reports (quarterly + annual) are generated automatically and available in the **Reports** tab. PDFs are pulled from short-lived signed URLs — no inline rendering of legal-grade documents. Export to your fund administrator with one click (CSV + PDF bundle).

### Prospect LP access
If you're evaluating Axal funds before committing, the **Prospect LP** tier ($499/mo) gives you read-only access to current deal flow, market intel, conviction signals, and studio benchmarks for evaluation purposes — without exposure to PII or signed legal docs.

## For Partners (Service Providers)

Partners are the ecosystem of legal, accounting, design, GTM, recruiting, and technical service providers that founders engage during and after spin-out. The Partner Portal is your demand-discovery and engagement workspace.

### Partner Directory
Your public profile in the directory shows: company name, services offered, sectors, geography, ratings/reviews, and case studies. Verified partners get a checkmark badge; Premium partners get top-of-list placement and category sponsorship.

### Live Needs Board (RFP system)
This is the highest-value module for partners. When founders or studio admins post a need ("need a fractional CFO for a Seed-stage SaaS, $5–10k/mo budget, US/EU"), eligible partners see it in real-time. Verified and Premium partners can submit proposals; Listed partners see anonymized previews only.

Proposals include scope, pricing, timeline, and case studies. Founders pick a winner; the system creates an **Engagement** record that tracks the lifecycle (kickoff → milestones → completion → review).

### Service Catalog
Define your standardized service packages (e.g., "Fractional CFO — Seed", "Brand sprint — 2 weeks", "Term sheet review — flat fee"). Founders can purchase these directly via Stripe; the platform takes a referral fee per the partner agreement.

### Demand Heatmap
Premium partners see anonymized demand signals across sectors and stages: which kinds of services are most-requested this month, where pricing is rising, where supply is thin. Use this to expand into adjacent service categories.

### Office Hours
Premium partners can publish recurring office-hour slots; founders book directly via the integrated calendar. Calls happen over the partner's preferred meeting tool (Google Meet, Zoom, Whereby). Outcomes feed back into the engagement lifecycle.

### Insight Feed & Co-Marketing
Publish thought-leadership content; the studio amplifies the best pieces to its founder base. Premium partners get co-marketing slots — webinars, joint briefings, sponsored guides.

## Account & Billing

### Managing your subscription
Go to **Settings → Billing**. Click **Manage subscription** to open the **Stripe Customer Portal** in a new tab. From there you can:

- Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel
- Update payment method
- Change billing address (drives VAT calculation)
- Download invoices (last 24 months)
- Apply promo codes

Plan changes take effect immediately on upgrade (prorated charge); downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

### Invoices & receipts
Every successful payment generates a Stripe-hosted invoice (PDF + HTML view), automatically emailed to your billing email and stored in the Customer Portal. We do not store credit card data — Stripe handles PCI scope end-to-end.

### Failed payments
We retry failed charges 3 times over 7 days (Stripe Smart Retries). If all retries fail, the subscription enters `past_due` status and gated features are revoked after a 3-day grace period. Restore by updating your payment method.

### Cancellation
Cancel anytime via the Customer Portal. You retain access through the end of your paid period. After that:

- **Founders (Alumni)** revert to a read-only Apply tier; submitted projects are preserved.
- **Investors (Co-Invest / Prospect)** lose access to gated dashboards but retain LP Portal access if Committed.
- **Partners (Verified / Premium)** drop to Listed tier; profile remains live but loses badge and RFP access.
- **Studio License** customers receive a final data export (D1 dump + signed-URL bundle) within 30 days; access is then revoked.

We do not delete your account on cancellation. To fully delete, see [Security & Privacy → Data deletion](#data-deletion).

### Refunds
Monthly plans are non-refundable past the 14-day satisfaction window. Annual plans are pro-rata refundable within 30 days. Studio License contracts follow the terms negotiated with your CSM.

## Security & Privacy

Security is not a feature — it's the substrate. Here's what we do and what you should do.

### Authentication
- **TOTP-only** (no passwords). Your authenticator app is your password.
- Verification tokens SHA-256-hashed at rest with 24-hour expiry.
- Resend-verification rate-limited to 3/hour per email.
- JWT session tokens (24-hour expiry) signed with `JWT_SECRET`.
- Cloudflare Turnstile bot protection on registration.

### Perimeter (Cloudflare Zero Trust)
Backoffice routes (`/api/admin/*`, `/api/monitoring/*`, `/api/infra/*`) sit behind **Cloudflare Access**. Every request must include a valid `Cf-Access-Jwt-Assertion` header — verified via RS256 against the team JWKS — *before* it reaches the application's role check. This is layered defense: even a leaked admin JWT can't bypass the perimeter.

### Sensitive data access policy
Four resource categories with strict access predicates (defined in `services/access_policy.py`):

| Resource | Who may view un-masked |
|---|---|
| Contracts (Document) | Admin, Partner, owning Founder |
| `signed_ip` (legal proof IP address) | Admin only |
| `signed_by` (signer email) | Admin, the signer themselves, doc owner |
| Personal contact info (email, phone) | Admin, the subject themselves, co-members on the same company (email only) |
| Company member roster | Admin, members of that company |

These predicates are the only path to unmasked data — there are no ad-hoc role checks scattered through the codebase.

### Data minimization
- Drawn-signature images (base64 data URLs) are **not** shipped or stored. Legal record = typed name + click-through + timestamp.
- Contract bodies never round-trip in JSON responses. Wire shape = file pointer + 5-minute HMAC-signed URL only.
- Search snippets for legal documents are neutral (`{doc_type} • {status} • deal #{id}`) — never first-280-chars of the body.
- Drawn signatures, full contract bodies, and signer IPs are admin-only.

### Encryption at rest
- Integration secrets (API keys, webhook secrets) Fernet-encrypted with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (200k iterations) keys derived from `JWT_SECRET`.
- Database backups encrypted at the storage layer (Cloudflare D1 / managed Postgres).

### Audit log
Every API call is recorded in `activity_logs` with latency, status code, endpoint, method, and user. 30-day retention default; longer with Studio Enterprise.

### Data deletion
You have the right to request full deletion of your account and associated data under GDPR, CCPA, and Swiss data protection law. To exercise this right, email **privacy@axal.vc** from your account email. We will:

1. Acknowledge within 7 days
2. Complete deletion within 30 days
3. Send confirmation with the list of data categories deleted

Some records (signed contracts, capital call records, distribution records) are preserved in compliance with financial recordkeeping obligations (typically 7 years) — these are detailed in our Privacy Policy.

### Security disclosure
Found a vulnerability? Report to **security@axal.vc** with a clear repro. We respond within 48 hours and follow Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD). Hall of fame on request.

## API & Integrations

### Available integrations
StudioOS integrates with major studio tooling out of the box:

- **HubSpot** — push deal pipeline + founder records to HubSpot CRM
- **Salesforce** — same as HubSpot, for enterprise studios
- **Sumsub** — KYC/AML onboarding for LPs
- **Stripe Atlas** — incorporation handoff during Week 2
- **Cooley GO** — legal template alignment with Cooley's library
- **PitchBook** — market intelligence enrichment
- **Custom** — bring your own webhook + API key

Connect at **Integrations** (admin or partner role only). Secrets stored Fernet-encrypted; previews shown masked. Webhook signatures validated via HMAC-SHA256 (`X-Axal-Signature` header).

### API access
Every entitlement-gated route is also accessible programmatically:

- **Base URL**: `https://studioos.guillaumelauzier.workers.dev/api` (edge) or your origin
- **Auth**: `Authorization: Bearer <jwt>` header on every request
- **Rate limits**: same buckets as the UI (60 req/min user, 10 req/min AI, 1000 req/min global)
- **Idempotency**: `Idempotency-Key` header on `POST` to prevent dup-execution

API documentation (OpenAPI) at `/api/docs` (FastAPI auto-generated). For the Cloudflare Worker surface, see `cloudflare-worker/SETUP.md`.

### Webhooks
Subscribe to events at **Settings → Webhooks**:

- `deal.scored` (when scoring completes)
- `deal.stage_advanced`
- `capital_call.issued`
- `distribution.executed`
- `rfp.posted`
- `engagement.completed`
- `subscription.changed`

Payloads signed via HMAC-SHA256 with your webhook secret. Retries: 3 attempts with exponential backoff (1m, 5m, 30m).

### Jekyll Bridge (advanced)
For studios with a public marketing site that needs to surface authenticated portfolio data, the Jekyll Bridge mounts a frontend script that handles JWT auth, session management, impersonation, and role-based data loading. Set `JEKYLL_ORIGIN` to lock CORS to your domain. See **API Bridge** page (admin only).

## Glossary

- **AML/KYC** — Anti-Money-Laundering / Know-Your-Customer checks; mandatory for LP onboarding.
- **Capital call** — Notice from the GP requesting LPs wire a portion of their commitment.
- **Carry / Carried interest** — The GP's share of profits, typically 20% above a hurdle rate.
- **D1** — Cloudflare's SQLite-at-the-edge database, our edge persistence layer.
- **DPI** — Distributions to Paid-In; ratio of cash returned to LPs vs. cash called.
- **Durable Object** — Cloudflare's stateful primitive used for WebSocket fan-out (PipelineRoom, OnboardingChat).
- **Entitlement** — A computed boolean flag indicating whether a user can access a gated feature, derived from their subscription tier.
- **GP** — General Partner; the studio operating entity (Axal Management, LLC for Axal VC).
- **LP** — Limited Partner; an investor in the fund.
- **LPA** — Limited Partnership Agreement; the master fund document.
- **PPM** — Private Placement Memorandum.
- **RBAC** — Role-Based Access Control.
- **RFP** — Request For Proposal; how founders solicit partner services.
- **SAFE** — Simple Agreement for Future Equity; standard early-stage fundraising instrument.
- **Spin-out** — A startup that exits the studio as an independent company.
- **SPV** — Special Purpose Vehicle; a fund structure for a single deal or co-invest.
- **Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Rejected** — Scoring outcomes (≥85 / 70–84 / <70).
- **TOTP** — Time-based One-Time Password; our 2FA method (no passwords).
- **TVPI** — Total Value to Paid-In; ratio of fund NAV + distributions vs. cash called.
- **Waterfall** — The distribution priority among LPs and the GP.
