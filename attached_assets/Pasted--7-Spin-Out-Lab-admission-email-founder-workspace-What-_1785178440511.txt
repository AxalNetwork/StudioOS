#7 - Spin-Out Lab admission email & founder workspace
What & Why
When a founder is admitted to a new Spin-Out Lab cohort, they should get a congratulations email with a link to https://axal.vc/spinout-lab. Clicking it (while logged in) shows a congratulations screen; from there they start Week 1 in a full workspace — matching the uploaded design (Screenshot_2026-07-27_at_12.49.39_PM and the earlier Spin-Out Lab Workspace handoff): program timeline across the 4 weeks, active-week deliverables checklist, and the full grid of tool feature cards (Startups, Customer Discovery, Market Intel, Profiling, Roadmap, Brand & Landing Pages, Pitch Deck Builder, Studio Ops, Scoring, Advisors, Office Hours, Co-founder Match, MI Investor Signals, Revenue, Incorporate, Cap Table, Co-founder Agreement, Capital) with Active/Unlocked states and working links. Everything stays on /spinout-lab and keeps the left sidebar.
Today there is no "admitted" state — users self-start the lab (spinout_lab_active flag, POST /api/spinout_lab/start in the Worker), and the product=spinout-lab registration intent is not persisted. An email template registry + queued Gmail send pipeline already exists in the Worker.
Done looks like
* An admin action admits a founder to the next cohort; the founder receives a branded "You're in" email linking to axal.vc/spinout-lab
* An admitted (not yet started) founder who visits /spinout-lab while logged in sees a congratulations screen; clicking "Start Week 1" activates the lab
* Active founders see the workspace from the design on /spinout-lab: header with cohort/week/day, 4-week program timeline with per-week status and tool chips, active-week deliverables list wired to real milestones, and the full tool-card grid with Active/Unlocked-by-week states, each card opening its working feature page
* The Spin-Out Lab left sidebar remains on the page
* Logged-out visitors clicking the email link land on login and return to /spinout-lab afterwards
Out of scope
* Building an applications/review pipeline (admission is a simple admin action + flag)
* New feature pages — cards link to the existing tool routes only
* Changes to the public/marketing /spinout-lab view shipped earlier
Steps
1. Admission state & admin trigger — Add an admitted flag (and cohort label) to users in the Worker schema; add an admin endpoint/UI action to admit a founder to the next cohort.
2. Admission email — Add a spinout_admitted template to the Worker email registry and send it on admit with a deep link to /spinout-lab; persist the spinout-lab registration intent so applicants are identifiable.
3. Congratulations screen — On /spinout-lab, show a celebration screen for admitted-but-not-started founders that starts Week 1 (calls the existing start endpoint) on click; redirect unauthenticated visitors through login back to /spinout-lab.
4. Founder workspace UI — Rebuild the active-founder view of /spinout-lab to the workspace design: program timeline, active-week deliverables tied to the existing milestone model, and the complete tool-card grid with week-based Active/Unlocked states linking to existing feature routes; keep the SpinoutLabSidebar.
5. Verify & ship — E2E test admitted/active/logged-out flows, then rebuild docs and redeploy the Worker in lockstep.
