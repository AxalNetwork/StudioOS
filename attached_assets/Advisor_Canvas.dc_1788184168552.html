Two rules an implementer must not flatten. The accent is emerald-700 wherever it carries text, because white on emerald-600 measures 3.77:1 against 5.70:1 for the Founder's violet-600 — the same numbered step is not the same contrast across hues, so the accent had to move rather than be inherited.
