#17 - Wire Diligence → Commit → Ledger
What & Why
Investor audit items ⑤ + ⑧ (Important). The Source→Exit funnel stages don't hand off: a DD case can't attach to an IC decision, an IC "invest" verdict creates no capital record, and the thesis-before-the-vote journal isn't connected to the IC vote. Meanwhile Due Diligence lives at an /admin/* URL the investor can only peek into, and Market Intelligence is one page with 21 tabs. Wire the chain and give diligence an investor-facing home.
Done looks like
* Due Diligence has an investor-facing home (no /admin framing); an investor can open a DD case from a deal they're in, and open reviewer items feed the lifecycle module's next-actions.
* A DD case can be attached to an IC decision (dd_case_id); an IC "invest" outcome prompts a pre-filled position/LP (ledger) record; each IC vote auto-drafts a journal entry.
* Market Intelligence's 21 tabs are regrouped into 4–5 lenses with an overview and one "insight → action" verb per lens.
* "Generate deal memo → open IC decision" is the primary CTA on the scoring surface.
Out of scope
* The Support-group consolidation and the single canonical holdings/capital store (Support task, which depends on the ledger record shape defined here).
Steps
1. De-admin Due Diligence: add an investor-facing DD route/page reusing the existing case system so an investor can open/continue a case from a deal, and feed open reviewer items into the lifecycle next-actions.
2. Wire the funnel hand-offs: add dd_case_id to IC decisions; on an IC "invest" outcome pre-fill a position/LP ledger record; auto-draft a journal entry per IC vote; and promote "Generate deal memo → IC" as the primary scoring CTA.
3. Regroup Market Intelligence's tabs into 4–5 lenses with an overview and a single insight→action verb per lens.
Constraints
* Adding a new top-level app route (e.g. investor /due-diligence) requires TWO patterns (exact + /*) in BOTH the top-level [[routes]] block AND [[env.production.routes]] in wrangler.toml (see the apex-routing invariant in replit.md).
* New migration (e.g. IC dd_case_id) is a cloudflare-worker/sql/migrations/NNN_*.sql in numeric order.
* Coordinates with the permissions/scoring task on ScoringPage.jsx, and depends on the fixed watchlist/journal contract for the journal auto-entry.
* Update BOTH changelogs for user-facing changes; pass npm run test:drift before merge.
