  1. Hero
     "Spin-Out Lab — 30 days from idea to incorporated"
     "Four weeks. Four milestones. Personal Advisor at every step.
      Ends with a Delaware C-Corp (or your jurisdiction equivalent),
      a vesting cap table, 83(b) on file, a pitch deck, and a
      venture-readiness score."

  2. The 4-week playbook
     Week 1 — Idea & Customer
       What you do: define the problem, ICP, market sizing seed,
                    talk to ≥5 customers, log every interview.
       What unlocks: Projects, Customer Discovery, Market
                    Intelligence (read-only), Personal Advisor
                    (spinout persona, week 1 question bank).
       You leave with: 1 Project record, ≥5 logged interviews,
                       sized TAM/SAM with citations.

     Week 2 — Solution & Roadmap
       What you do: scope the MVP, set 90-day OKRs, draft brand
                    v1, draft pitch deck v1.
       What unlocks: Roadmap (OKRs + Kanban), Brand Builder,
                    Pitch Deck Builder, Studio Ops cadence.
       You leave with: 3+ OKRs, brand basics, deck v1.

     Week 3 — Validate & Team
       What you do: run your first venture-readiness score, match
                    with mentors, decide co-founder track,
                    pressure-test scoring weak points.
       What unlocks: Diligence & Scoring Engine, Mentors, Office
                    Hours, Co-founder Match, MI investor signals.
       You leave with: a score (with evidence-confidence %), a
                    mentor cadence, a co-founder lead OR a clear
                    "going solo" plan.

     Week 4 — Incorporate & Capital
       What you do: incorporate the entity, issue founder stock
                    with vesting, file 83(b), sign cofounder
                    agreement, lock the fundraise ask.
       What unlocks: Incorporate, Cap Table, Section 83(b),
                    Cofounder Agreement, Capital, Compliance, KYC,
                    LP / partner introductions on Axal's network.
       You leave with: incorporated entity, signed cap table with
                    vesting, 83(b) filed within window, fundraise
                    plan + ask, three warm investor introductions.

  3. What you get
     - Personal Advisor (Workers AI · Llama 3.3 70B FP8 via Axal's
       dedicated AI Gateway) walking you through every page; never
       a blank screen.
     - Founder → Investor introductions, gated by a three-way NDA
       (Founder + Investor + Axal as intermediary). Three warm
       intros minimum during Week 4 for qualified founders.
     - Mentor track. Operators in your sector match by expertise,
       availability, language, time zone, rating.
     - Services-for-equity / services-for-fee partners. Legal,
       design, recruiting, technical DD — pre-vetted, Axal-network
       rates.
     - Market intelligence access. Sector heat, investor signals,
       sentiment, TALC positioning, demand & supply atlas, capital
       velocity — refreshed nightly from external sources +
       anonymised platform data.
     - Document automation. Incorporation packet, 83(b), cofounder
       agreement, SAFE template, NDAs. Generated by the platform,
       e-signed in-product.
     - Alumni community for life. Quarterly updates, alumni-only
       network, co-marketing slots.
     - For accepted ventures: equity-for-platform option under a
       separately negotiated partnership.

  4. What we look for
     Honest. Not every applicant is a fit.
       Strong signals:
         - domain expertise (years in the target sector)
         - customer access (warm intros to ICP from day 1)
         - commitment level (full-time, not nights-and-weekends)
         - lived insight (a non-obvious view about what's broken)
         - coachability (you adjust when evidence contradicts you)
         - founder + market fit (right person for this specific
           problem, not just "any startup")
       Filters:
         - sector fit: AI · Blockchain · Quantum · Digital
           Infrastructure · Frontier Software
         - geography we can support
         - founder ≥ 18
         - no sanctions / PEP / bad-actor disqualifications
       Reasons we say no: too generic a thesis, no customer access,
       part-time only with no plan to flip, conflicts of interest,
       sector mismatch.

  5. Network
     Inside the Lab you tap:
       - operating partners (legal, GTM, design, recruiting, data,
         technical DD, finance)
       - investor signals from Axal's investor pipeline (anonymised
         until pairwise NDA signed)
       - mentor pool with expertise tags and availability calendars
       - co-marketing partners across the network for distribution
       - alumni founders who graduated previous cohorts

  6. Market data
     Each founder receives:
       - sector compass (best sub-sectors given your profile)
       - investor signals (live aggregate of investor thesis +
         deployment patterns, k-anonymity ≥ 5)
       - TALC positioning (where the market is on the technology-
         adoption lifecycle, where founder thinks they are, gap
         analysis)
       - demand & supply atlas (needs vs. offers across the network)
       - founder ↔ investor fit (embeddings-based thesis match)
       - capital velocity (deployment pace per stage per sector)

  7. Pricing
     Free during the 30-day sprint. After graduation: standard
     Founder tiers (Free / Growth / Studio). Services partners are
     separately priced.

  8. FAQ
     - "I already have a co-founder." → Week 3 still useful for
       scoring + investor exposure.
     - "I missed a milestone." → You stay at the current week
       until you complete it. Advisor will list what's missing.
     - "Do you take equity?" → Only under a separately negotiated
       partnership / spin-out agreement. Never automatically.
     - "Can I bring an existing project?" → Yes; fast-forward
       through weeks you've already completed.
     - "What jurisdictions?" → Delaware C-Corp default; LLC, UK
       Ltd, French SAS, German GmbH supported with partner
       counsel.
