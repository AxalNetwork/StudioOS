#28 - Advisor Advisory Section
What & Why
Build the Advisory section — the heart of the Advisor profile — covering the full advisory lifecycle from opportunity to delivery to contracts. This is the genuinely new section (no investor equivalent), replacing the shell stubs with tab-organized workspaces and realistic placeholder data.
Done looks like
* Opportunities reads like a pipeline workspace with stages as tabs — Leads, Discovery Calls, Proposals, Won, Lost — surfacing the spec's fields per stage: lead pipeline/status/source, service interest, opportunity value, qualification score, notes; discovery call schedule/participants/company context/challenges/goals/needs assessment/budget/timeline/AI summary/next steps; proposal builder (scope, services, deliverables, pricing, versions, approval status); won (client/engagement/contract creation, onboarding); lost (reason, competitor, budget, timing, follow-up date). Records open a detail panel.
* Clients reads like an account workspace: Client List (active/past/strategic, status, value, revenue), company profiles (overview, industry, business model, products, markets, stage, funding, revenue, challenges, goals), engagement history/timeline (first engagement, contracts, meetings, deliverables, milestones, decisions, renewals), and health (engagement score, satisfaction, goal progress, responsiveness, renewal probability, expansion opportunities).
* Engagements reads like a delivery workspace with the five delivery models as tabs — Projects, Retainers, Advisory Boards, Fractional Roles, Workshops — each showing its spec fields (scope/objectives/timeline/tasks/milestones/deliverables/progress; monthly scope/hours/fee/usage/renewal/capacity; board membership/meetings/agendas/recommendations/decisions/materials; role definition/responsibilities/time allocation/reporting/performance; workshop type/agenda/participants/materials/outcomes/feedback).
* Delivery reads like meeting notes and artifacts: Sessions (meetings, notes, decisions, action items, follow-ups, recordings, AI summaries) and Deliverables (reports, strategy decks, financial models, research, templates, playbooks).
* Contracts reads like document and billing management: Proposals, Statements of Work, Advisory Agreements, NDAs, Invoices, Renewals — with statuses and a detail panel.
* Every tab, filter, and detail panel renders with mock data — no blank views and no console errors.
Out of scope
* Real backend/API wiring, document generation/e-sign, and real billing — mock data only.
* The App shell, sidebar, and routing (delivered by the Advisor shell foundation task).
* Network and Research sections (separate tasks).
Steps
1. Opportunities — Build the pipeline workspace with stage tabs and the spec's per-stage fields, plus an opportunity detail panel.
2. Clients — Build the account workspace: client list with segments/status/value, company profile, engagement history/timeline, and a health scorecard.
3. Engagements — Build the delivery-models workspace with a tab per model and its spec fields; records open a detail panel.
4. Delivery — Build the sessions and deliverables/artifacts views (notes, decisions, action items, AI summaries; reports, decks, models, templates, playbooks).
5. Contracts — Build the commercial-documents workspace (proposals, SOWs, agreements, NDAs, invoices, renewals) with statuses and a detail panel.
6. Mock data — Seed a dedicated advisor Advisory data module with realistic, deterministic placeholder records, structured to be swapped for live data later.
7. Verify — Click through every tab, filter, and detail panel across all five pages; confirm no blank states or console errors.
Architectural constraints
* Reuse the shared section kit (components/section); keep advisor code under its own pages/advisor/advisory/ and data/advisor/ files.
* Deterministic mock data only: demo "today" is 2026-07-11; no Date.now, Math.random, or zero-argument new Date().
