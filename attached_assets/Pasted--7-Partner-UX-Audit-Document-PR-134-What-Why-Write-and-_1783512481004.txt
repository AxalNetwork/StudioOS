Audit content summary (expand to full prose in the deliverable)
Executive summary — six systemic problems
1. Partners land on the wrong desk — twice. ROLE_DEFAULT_PATH (App.jsx:219) sends partners to /partner-portal → PartnerPortal.jsx, titled "Partner / Investor Portal" with tabs Deals / Capital Calls / LP Investors / Portfolio — an LP-investor console with almost nothing for a service partner. Sidebar Home says Studio (/studio → founder-identical Dashboard, whose dashboard.ts partner payload is never rendered). Two homes; neither is a service-partner workspace.
2. One economy, three marketplaces, two schemas. My Services (/services → ServiceCatalogPage → services.ts offerings + service_engagements), Needs Board (/needs → NeedsBoardPage → needs.ts full RFP economy), and Marketplace (/marketplace → MarketplacePage → catalog.ts providers/inquiries/messages/provider-reviews). Two conflicting CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS service_offerings definitions: schema.sql:724 (owner_user_id/summary/price_usd) vs t13_t14_t15.sql:377 (partner_id/description/price_min/price_max) — whichever ran first on production won.
3. The drift disease, again. ServiceCatalogPage renders o.description/o.partner_name; services.ts serializes summary/owner_user_id (services.ts:19-46) — descriptions render blank; create form Description silently dropped (POST reads summary, services.ts:83). PartnerOfficeHoursPage broken identically to the advisor page: sends start_at/duration_min where partner_office_hours.ts requires starts_at/ends_at — slot creation 400s; status vocabulary mismatch makes bookings unconfirmable; no founder-facing page renders partner slots.
4. Win-work lifecycle buried in one page, ending in a stub. needs.ts implements need → RFP → quote → engagement with gated transitions, delivery notes, reviews — but the whole partner workflow lives in the 910-line NeedsBoardPage; no "My engagements" home or pipeline view. Terminal step is fake: invoice writes invoice_id = 'stub-…' (needs.ts:431), while wellbeing-experts has full Stripe Connect one door over.
5. Front door is an invite token; side door mislabels people. Real partner onboarding is the partner_onboarding.ts invite-token wizard writing partner_profiles + partner_deals. Persona onboarding funnels every "Operator / Advisor" signup into the partner role (personas.ts:178-181) with no setup path — dumped on the LP portal.
6. Bright spots point the way. Demand Insights (PartnerInsightsPage) is real aggregation; the partner sidebar is the best-curated of the four personas. The failure isn't the IA — surfaces behind it are fragmented, drifted, and unpaid.
Section-by-section recommendations
1. Home — Partner Desk on /studio with role-conditional composition (lifecycle rail + matching open needs + quotes awaiting + active engagements + insights digest); /partner-portal stays for investor-type partners and is dropped from ROLE_DEFAULT_PATH for service partners.
2. Marketplace consolidation (core recommendation) — needs.ts RFP economy becomes canonical; services.ts offerings fold in as storefront listings (migrate one true service_offerings shape, reconcile double-CREATE); catalog.ts retires into needs-inquiries. My Services = storefront CRUD; Needs Board = demand only; new Engagements tab inside existing workspace; FounderMarketplacePage keeps its tabs on the canonical backend.
3. Office hours — Same contract fix as advisor audit ① (PartnerOfficeHoursPage / partner_office_hours.ts); then unify with the advisor engine (parameterized service, advisor audit ⑥); add founder-facing discovery on PublicPartnerProfilePage and Team/Network.
4. Money — Adopt wellbeing Stripe Connect rails for engagement invoicing (replace stub): quote → invoice → payment intent → payout; Earnings panel on Partner Desk. Refer & Earn stays separate.
5. Onboarding — Self-serve "Offer services" path: persona onboarding flows into first-run setup (profile + first offering + categories) instead of the LP portal; invite-token deal wizard remains for equity/deal partners; fix "Operator / Advisor"→partner mislabel jointly with advisor audit ③.
6. Leads & network — Partner-audience landing signups get a real destination (intro queue on Partner Desk / Marketplace inquiry) instead of dead-ending in /network; Contacts hub gains "Promote to partner inquiry".
